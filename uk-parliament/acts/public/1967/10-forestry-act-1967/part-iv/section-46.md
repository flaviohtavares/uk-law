## 46. Appropriate legislative authority's (affected by repeal, see note) power to make byelaws. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^c-2112b2cd8ce8b2d014c79bce78487524][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^c377518]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this and the next following sections, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate legislative authority[^c-d4248d47314cf858de3a3d2e78c4e3e5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] may make byelaws with respect to any land which is under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] the[^c-92aaeba2d6cee9b336c6312faf84f1aa] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] management or control *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] of the appropriate forestry authority[^c-d733af2230a53da3c18b985be02458ff] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] and to which the public have, or may be permitted to have, access. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate legislative authority's[^c-6f667cff3eab21aaf55fe46e72515280] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] byelaws may be such as appear to them to be necessary— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) for the preservation of any trees or timber on the land, or of any property of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority[^c-2f1dd01cab7338065e60a09d4f981a18] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) for prohibiting or regulating any act or thing tending to injury or disfigurement of the land or its amenities; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (c) without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, for regulating the reasonable use of the land by the public for the purposes of exercise and recreation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(3) Byelaws under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) shall not take away or injuriously affect any estate, interest, right of common or other right of a profitable or beneficial nature in, over or affecting any land, except with the consent of the person entitled thereto; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) shall not apply to a common which is subject to a scheme or regulation made in pursuance of the Metropolitan Commons Acts 1866 to 1898, or the Inclosure Acts 1845 to 1882, or the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c377519]Commons Act 1899. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(4) Byelaws under this section shall be made by statutory instrument and a draft of a statutory instrument containing any such byelaws *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] with respect to land in England[^c377520] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-dec57b229a5c51cc177e7ff3b80867c2]... shall be laid before Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(4A)[^c-a3c98970c2caf00a66d81e8663bbb637] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4B)[^c-a3c98970c2caf00a66d81e8663bbb637] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4C)[^c-048d13688373ec971e4ac9c26323ec0e] A draft of any statutory instrument containing byelaws under this section with respect to land in Wales must be laid before the National Assembly for Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(5) If anyone fails to comply with, or acts in contravention of, any byelaw made under this section he shall be guilty of an offence and be liable on summary conviction as follows:— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) in the case of an offence against byelaws made by the Commissioners with respect to the New Forest, he shall be liable to a fine not exceeding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] level 2 on the standard scale[^c377522] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7],
- (b) in a case not falling within the foregoing paragraph, he shall be liable to a fine not exceeding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] level 2 on the standard scale *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (c) in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] the case of a continuing offence falling within either of the foregoing paragraphs[^c377524] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] he shall be liable to a further fine not exceeding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] 50p[^c377525] for each day upon which the offence continues. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
