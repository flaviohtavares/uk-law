## 1. The Forestry Commission. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Forestry Commissioners constituted under the Forestry Acts 1919 to 1945 shall continue in existence and are in this Act referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]**the Commissioners *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]**”.

(1A)[^key-4788bf72bcc17e8926d6dcfec0ce29f6] In this Act, “the appropriate forestry authority” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) in relation to England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-3807246843d03b9cba3c250fcda92aba]..., the Commissioners; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) in relation to Wales, the Natural Resources Body for Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority[^key-0316c3773ef361fc2c3c6d8e39b07aa2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] shall be charged with the general duty of promoting the interests of forestry, the development of afforestation and the production and supply of timber and other forest products *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-6811fb707d0a7566b41a173878e171e9]...[^c377193] in England and in Wales[^key-3c04c263db1dec05b12aba4ec37a63ed] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] and in that behalf shall have the powers and duties conferred or imposed on them by this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2A)[^key-7f887f2014be81d469e4da026d973353] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority's[^key-3088e25f4e49f75446688f45dd4cfa67] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] general duty includes that of promoting the establishment and maintenance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-f9484e64500e531f1d35b6714a362fe9]... in England and in Wales[^key-987ff613e309c2ec9096c7efa29769f4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] of adequate reserves of growing trees. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(3A)[^c377195] In discharging their functions under the Forestry Acts 1967 to 1979 the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority[^key-e542f42c601741612077cbe0ecfd0059] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] shall, so far as may be consistent with the proper discharge of those functions, endeavour to achieve a reasonable balance between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) the development of afforestation, the management of forests and the production and supply of timber *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-d72757ff55ed2f3f4fa26edc4c938efa]... *(Extent: S)*[^key-c1dab77bef326ad1fa1bc8081eb02a60] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) the conservation and enhancement of natural beauty and the conservation of flora, fauna and geological or physiographical features of special interest. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(3B)[^key-1cf41849611f744d63ddd1bcc0d6db79] In subsection (3A) “functions” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) in relation to the Commissioners, functions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-5724256266b8e53bdacbde49bfac3149]...;
- (b) in relation to the Natural Resources Body for Wales, functions under this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(4) The Commissioners shall, in exercising their functions under this Act, and also in exercising their powers under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c377196]Plant Health Act 1967 (which enables them to make orders for the control of timber pests and diseases), comply with such directions as may be given to them by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a)[^c377197] the Ministers in relation to the exercise of their functions as regards England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-baa523a28c2b33795f28e6ec9a2a11e1]...; [^key-47d5271f25f752cc1416728432aecd3d] ...
- (b)[^key-47d5271f25f752cc1416728432aecd3d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-04526c68daa84cf1d08b7295e919867b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
