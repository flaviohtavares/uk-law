# Forestry Act 1967, section 3A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/10
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3A. Use of land in connection with renewable electricity projects (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e5ce84af2c61a45eef7c9193ddcc86da][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Commissioners may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) use, or enter into arrangements in connection with the use of, English forestry land for the generation, storage, transmission or supply of renewable electricity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) sell, or enter into other arrangements concerning, renewable electricity generated, stored or supplied on, or transmitted across, English forestry land; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (c) use, or enter into arrangements in connection with the use of, English forestry land for activity that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (i) is intended to satisfy, or enable the satisfaction of, a relevant condition of development (whether existing or contemplated), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] otherwise arises in connection with such a condition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1)(c), a relevant condition of development is a condition of development that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) relates to renewable electricity development located wholly or partly on English forestry land, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) is intended to benefit the natural environment of land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(3) The exercise of the powers in subsection (1) for either of the following purposes is to be taken to be consistent with the Commissioners’ general duty under section 1(2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) facilitating or promoting the use of renewable electricity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) obtaining funds for meeting the expenses referred to in section 41(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(4) When the Commissioners are considering the exercise of those powers, section 1(3A) applies as if it included a further paragraph referring to those purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(5) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- “**condition of development *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (a) a condition to which planning permission is subject, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (b) a requirement contained in an order granting development consent under the Planning Act 2008 (development consent for nationally significant infrastructure); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- “**English forestry land *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]**” means land placed at the disposal of the Commissioners under this Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- “**natural environment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]**”, in relation to land, includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (a) its plants, animals and other living organisms, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (b) their habitats, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (c) its geological features; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- “**renewable electricity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]**” means electricity produced from sources other than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (a) coal, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (b) lignite, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (c) peat, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (d) natural gas, within the meaning of the Energy Act 1976, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (e) crude liquid petroleum, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (f) petroleum products, within the meaning of the Energy Act 1976, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (g) any substance produced directly or indirectly from a substance within paragraphs (a) to (f), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (h) energy derived from any substance within paragraphs (a) to (g), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (i) nuclear fuel; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- “**renewable electricity development *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]**” means development the main purpose of which is the generation, storage, transmission or supply of renewable electricity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(6) References in this section to the storage of electricity are to the storage of energy converted from electricity for the purpose of its reconversion into electricity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
