# Forestry Act 1967, section 7A

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 7A. Incidental powers of Commissioners (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-14e143e5d6f0f397f2b03c397fec25f3][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purpose of the exercise of their functions under this Act so far as relating to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-500c368f135062dc20c20387dcb6efb8]... land in England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-9ee3dccdb45da782dca82ed1a64c6d75]..., or for the purpose of the exercise of their functions under section 8(1)(b) of this Act, the Commissioners may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) form, or participate in the forming of, a body corporate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) invest in a body corporate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (c) provide loans; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (d) establish a charitable trust; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (e) act, or appoint a person to act, as an officer of a body corporate or as a trustee of a charitable trust. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2) The powers contained in subsection (1)(b) and (c) shall be exercisable only with Treasury approval. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
