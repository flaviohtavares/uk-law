## 39. Power of Minister to acquire and dispose of land. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^c377408]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] as regards[^c377409] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] England or Wales[^key-da83ff777fd2bd627172e2e0cbec86f3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-c9c1dac759ef64b01439fadb7d309bfe]... may acquire (by purchase, lease or exchange) land which in his *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-70cd70180148abc2ce87ad4c84f81152]... opinion is suitable for afforestation or for purposes connected with forestry, together with any other land which must necessarily be acquired therewith, and may place any land acquired by him *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-774c1cd644c8efa50655881b36f95578]... under this section at the disposal of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority[^key-5f97132353c51bf8f39a6cc02c051ab1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7].

(2)[^c377412] Subject to subsection (2A) below, the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-4dc78e413620b2aa4a5df67bd9dc9b57]... may dispose for any purpose of land acquired by him *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-ce2252d8786b293c03419540221c421d]... under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2A) Subsection (2) above shall not apply in relation to land acquired under this section which is in the Forest of Dean; but the Minister may sell any such land if in his opinion it is not needed, or ought not to be used, for the purpose of afforestation or any purpose connected with forestry, and may exchange any such land for other land more suitable for either of the said purposes and may pay or receive money for equality of exchange. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(3) The Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-3f9246d069862a376f8ac9517225cde6]... shall have power, in the case of land acquired by him *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-3cfebf85dfc640e2b6eaa5e3b78713d8]... under this section,— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) to manage and use the land for such purposes as he thinks *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-e09415c69ad88788657b2e33ac1fedc8]... fit (this power to include that of erecting buildings and other works on the land) where it is not for the time being placed at the disposal of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] under subsection (1) above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) to let the land, or grant any interest or right in or over it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(4)[^c377418] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-ec0492025fa468f693b4cf7f13b42fb1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Schedule 4 to this Act shall have effect as respects the procedure applicable where the Minister acquires *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-0aa3c9eb172212d53982d34f0e770b8f]... land under this section, except in a case of compulsory purchase in accordance with the following section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
