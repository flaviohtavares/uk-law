## 40. Compulsory purchase of land. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c377461][^c377462][^c377463][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^c377460]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this section, the power of the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-dcdbfd0a59254e219b69d61cf5017ff6]... to acquire land by purchase under section 39 above includes a power of compulsory purchase. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2) The following descriptions of land shall not be subject to compulsory purchase under this Act:— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) land which is the site of an ancient monument or other object of archaeological interest; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) land which forms part of a park, garden or pleasure ground or which forms part of the home farm attached to, and usually occupied with, a mansion house or is otherwise required for the amenity or convenience of a dwelling-house; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (c) land which is the property of a local authority, that is to say,— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (i) in England or Wales, the council of a county, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c377465]... , county district or rural parish, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] the Greater London Council[^c377466] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] the Common Council of the City of London or the council of a London borough; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-f495ff59efeedce176820e78755ec291] ...
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f495ff59efeedce176820e78755ec291][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) land which has been acquired for the purpose of their undertaking by statutory undertakers, that is to say persons authorised by an enactment, or by an order or scheme made under an enactment, to construct, work or carry on a railway, canal, inland navigation, dock, harbour, tramway, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c377468]... , [^c377469]... , water[^c377470] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] or other public undertaking. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (e)[^c377471] land held or used by a universal service provider (within the meaning of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] Part 3 of the Postal Services Act 2011[^key-fdde57e7285b03a190fc483daa886337] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]) for any purpose in connection with the provision of a universal postal service (within the meaning of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] that Part[^key-fa635d08fc2ea1683e53fae5636f3d55] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]).

(3) Land shall not be subject to compulsory purchase under this Act if a forestry dedication covenant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-98100c06a696e4ba2afa406af82c2bbe]... is in force with respect to it and it is being used and managed in accordance with the provisions and conditions of a plan of operations approved by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority[^key-80ddd0027361e89828c69bbd71a3192d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]; and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) any question arising under this subsection whether there has been a breach of any of the provisions and conditions of a plan of operations shall be referred for determination as follows, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
  - (i) in a case relating to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] England or Wales[^key-3b1fb885c98e72030ebcd3df0883cf66] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7], by an arbitrator appointerd by the President of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-f6b94b3a02a4299a31211596a46a898e] ...
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f6b94b3a02a4299a31211596a46a898e][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) such a breach shall not be treated as having occurred by virtue of any act or omission capable of remedy unless there has been default in remedying it within a reasonable time after notice given by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] requiring it to be remedied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(4)[^c377472] The power of compulsory purchase under this Act shall not be excercisable in relation to land held inalienably by the National Trust or by the National Trust for Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(5) The Minister’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-5f0849a41373f961930587986ce7938c]... power of compulsory purchase under this Act shall be exercisable by means of a compulsory purchase order, and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) the order shall be made in accordance with Part I of Schedule 5 to this Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) Part II of that Schedule shall apply with respect to the validity and date of operation of the order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (c) Part III of that Schedule shall apply with respect to the procedure for acquiring land by compulsory purchase. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(6) If the said power of compulsory purchase is exercised in relation to land in respect of which an advance by way of grant has been made by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]—

- (a) under section 4 of this Act; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) under section 3(3) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c377474]Forestry Act 1919 at any time after the 26th March 1945, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

not being in either case an advance made more than thirty years before the date of the service of the notice to treat in the exercise of that power, the amount of the compensation for the compulsory purchase shall be reduced by the amount of the advance with compound interest thereon (calculated from the date of the advance to the date on which the compensation is paid) at the rate of £3 per cent. per annum with yearly rests: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

Provided that, in the case of compensation for the compulsory purchase of one of several interests in such land, the amount of the reduction in the case of each interest purchased shall be equal to a part of the advance and interest proportionate to the value of that interest as compared with the value of the land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
