## 38. Composition (affected by repeal, see note) etc. (affected by repeal, see note) of advisory committees. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(1)[^c-fd80b052bb4292e6f5b6e2ddfe8f9ae0] [^M_F_4b2e28d7-0604-4b52-9489-5024c534f4d4]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A) The chairman and other members of each regional advisory committee shall be appointed by the appropriate forestry authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(1B) A chairman or member appointed under subsection *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^M_F_63779e44-73b2-439b-c244-ce6d5d43ce3d]... (1A) shall hold and vacate office in accordance with the terms of the instrument by which they are appointed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2)[^M_F_b83562e9-ff5a-42ab-f621-dcf483330c7a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Each regional advisory committee shall consist of not less than seven nor more than *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] twelve[^c377397] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] members, and of those members (other than the chairman) not less than four shall be persons appointed by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority[^c-1e9b3f65780b554eb689e6a81632491b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] after consultation with organisations appearing to them to represent the interests of owners of woodlands and timber merchants respectively and organisations concerned with the study and promotion of forestry. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(4) [^M_F_cd5d22aa-06f0-4052-e576-7d88b1e4f561]... The appropriate forestry authority may pay to the members of a regional advisory committee,[^c-6fb5cf14a76c795a204cfbc56711d1c6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] such allowances as they may with the consent of the Treasury determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(5)[^c-e0122abc4b4eb851c1d92df15d6f5e21] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
