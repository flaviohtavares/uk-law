## 37. Advisory committees (affected by repeal, see note).[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^key-0671bedf9ce8246cb7ad03c96fff1b28][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-9c8928f19c9d97727905e57460985adb] For the purposes of advising the appropriate forestry authority as to the performance of their functions under section 1(3) and Part II of this Act, and such other functions as the appropriate forestry authority may from time to time determine— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a)[^M_F_c8403386-e574-4200-b29d-66494c911517] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the appropriate forestry authority shall continue to maintain a regional advisory committee for each conservancy (within the meaning of Part II of this Act) in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] England and Wales[^key-fe3b7f658ce1bbc4d0deeb360d1c94ac] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7].

(2)[^M_F_63ec2aa7-6c71-43e5-ec7f-25bc181359b9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority[^key-5c5595e301ee8e10f800527eba825ecd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) in considering whether to refuse a felling licence under Part II of this Act, or to grant it unconditionally or subject to any conditions; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) in considering whether to give felling directions under the said Part II, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

take into account any advice tendered by the regional advisory committee for the conservancy in which are growing the trees to which the felling licence applied for, or the directions proposed to be given, relate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
