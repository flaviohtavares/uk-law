# General Rate Act 1967, section 110

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 110. Inquiries.

The Minister may direct any inquiries to be held by his I inspectors which he might have directed to be so held under section 61(1) of the [^m00061]Rating and Valuation Act 1925 if this Act had not been passed.
