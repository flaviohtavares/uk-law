# General Rate Act 1967, section 111

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 111. Interest in municipal property not to disqualify.

The interest of any council in any property of which I they or the corporation for which they act are owners or occupiers shall not disqualify the council or any member thereof for acting under this Act in relation to that property.
