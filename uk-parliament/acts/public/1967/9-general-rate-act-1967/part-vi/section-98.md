# General Rate Act 1967, section 98

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 98. Statement of case on application for warrant.

The justices may state a case under the [^m00052]Magistrates' Courts Act 1952 when called upon to issue a warrant of distress under this Part of this Act.
