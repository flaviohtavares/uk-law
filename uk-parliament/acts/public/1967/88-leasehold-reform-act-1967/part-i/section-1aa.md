## 1AA. Additional right to enfranchisement only in case of houses whose rent exceeds applicable limit under section 4.[^c8292581]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882]

- (a) section 1(1) above would apply in the case of the tenant of a house but for the fact that the tenancy is not a tenancy at a low rent, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882]
- (b) the tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882] [^c8292591]. . . is not an excluded tenancy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882]

this Part of this Act shall have effect to confer on the tenant the same right to acquire the freehold of the house and premises as would be conferred by section 1(1) above if it were a tenancy at a low rent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882]

(2)[^c8292601] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) A tenancy is an excluded tenancy for the purposes of subsection (1) above if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882]

- (a) the house which the tenant occupies under the tenancy is in an area designated for the purposes of this provision as a rural area by order made by the Secretary of State, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882]
- (b) the freehold of that house is owned together with adjoining land which is not occupied for residential purposes and has been owned together with such land since *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882] 1st April 1997 (the date on which section 106 of the Housing Act 1996 came into force)[^c8292611] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882]
- (c)[^c8292621] the tenancy either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882]
  - (i) was granted on or before that date, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882] was granted after that date, but on or before the coming into force of section 141 of the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002, for a term of years certain not exceeding thirty-five years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882]

(4) Where this Part of this Act applies as if there were a single tenancy of property comprised in two or more separate tenancies, then, if each of the separate tenancies falls within subsection (2) above, this section shall apply as if the single tenancy did so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882]

(5) The power to make an order under subsection (3) above shall be exercisable by statutory instrument which shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0c6aeff5b5dc328df663069d7fa09882]
