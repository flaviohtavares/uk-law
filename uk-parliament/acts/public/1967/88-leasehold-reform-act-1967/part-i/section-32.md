## 32. Saving for National Trust.

*Extent: E+W*

This Part of this Act shall not prejudice the operation of section 21 of the [^c8301191]National Trust Act 1907, and accordingly a person shall not be entitled under this Part of this Act to acquire the freehold of property if an interest in the property is under that section vested inalienably in the National Trust for Places of Historic Interest or Natural Beauty.
