# Leasehold Reform Act 1967, section 4A

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4A. Alternative rent limits for purposes of section 1A(2).[^c8293211]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3] For the purposes of section 1A(2) above a tenancy of any property falls within this subsection if either no rent was payable under it in respect of the property during the initial year or the aggregate amount of rent so payable during that year did not exceed the following amount, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]

- (a) where the tenancy was entered into before 1st April 1963, two-thirds of the letting value of the property (on the same terms) on the date of the commencement of the tenancy; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]
- (b) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]
  - (i) the tenancy was entered into either on or after 1st April 1963 but before 1st April 1990, or on or after 1st April 1990 in pursuance of a contract made before that date, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c8293221][^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3] the property had a rateable value other than nil at the date of commencement of the tenancy or else at any time before 1st April 1990, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]

  two-thirds of the rateable value of the property on the relevant date; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]
- (c) in any other case, £1,000 if the property is in Greater London or £250 if elsewhere. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1) above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]

- (a) “**the initial year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]**”, in relation to any tenancy, means the period of one year beginning with the date of the commencement of the tenancy; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]
- (b)[^c8293231] “**the relevant date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]**” means the date of the commencement of the tenancy or, if the property did not have a rateable value, or had a rateable value of nil, on that date, the date on which it first had a rateable value other than nil; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]
- (c) paragraphs (b) and (c) of section 4(1) above shall apply as they apply for the purposes of section 4(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]

and it is hereby declared that in subsection (1) above the reference to the letting value of any property is to be construed in like manner as the reference in similar terms which appears in the proviso to section 4(1) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]

(3) Section 1(7) above applies to any amount referred to in subsection (1)(c) above as it applies to the amount referred to in subsection (1)(a)(ii) of that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-942576191943b45cc3381b6c61288fc3]
