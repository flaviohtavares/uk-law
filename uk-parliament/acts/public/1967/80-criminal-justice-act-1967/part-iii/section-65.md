# Criminal Justice Act 1967, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/80
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65. Abolition of corporal punishment in prison.

*Extent: E+W*

Corporal punishment shall not be inflicted in any prison or other institution to which the [^c7284711]Prison Act 1952 applies, and accordingly section 18 of that Act shall cease to have effect.
