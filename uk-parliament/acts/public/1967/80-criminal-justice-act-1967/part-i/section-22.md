## 22. Extension of power of High Court to grant, or vary conditions of, bail.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c7282281] Where

- (a)[^c-f220b9a58dc4d88414f951b11c771c38] a magistrates’ court[^c7282291] withholds bail in criminal proceedings or imposes conditions in granting bail in criminal proceedings, and[^c-4173cde3c9b7822eb6c70de3c422f579]
- (b) it does so where an application to the court to state a case for the opinion of the High Court is made,

the High Court may [^c-62e7d1d72fc823df33d644d49c39c46f] ... grant bail or vary the conditions.

(1A)[^c-6838fb66bd7f4d33b91154dfc16fcabc] Where a magistrates' court withholds bail in extradition proceedings or imposes conditions in granting bail in extradition proceedings, the High Court may grant bail or vary the conditions.

(2) Where the High Court grants a person bail under this section it may direct him to appear at a time and place which the magistrates’ court could have directed and the recognizance of any surety shall be conditioned accordingly.

(3) Subsections [^c7282321]... (4) and (6) of section 37 of the [^c7282331]Criminal Justice Act 1948 (ancillary provisions as to persons granted[^c7282341] to bail by the High Court under that section and the currency of sentence in the case of persons so admitted) shall apply in relation to the powers conferred by this section and persons granted bail in pursuance of those powers as it applies in relation to the powers conferred by that section and persons granted bail in pursuance of those powers [^c-5f33dd5d5f653ce153024651e03aee14]...

(4) In this section [^c7282391]... ...[^c7282411][^c7282401]“bail in criminal proceedings”, “extradition proceedings”[^c-0027e3719583f6b23561257b97df8c0d] and “vary” shall have the same meanings as they have in the [^c7282421]Bail Act 1976.

(5) The powers conferred on the High Court by this section shall be in substitution for the powers so conferred by paragraphs (*a*), (*b*) and (*c*) of section 37(1) of the [^c7282431]Criminal Justice Act 1948, but except as aforesaid this section shall not prejudice any powers of the High Court to admit or direct the admission of persons to bail.
