## 25. Restriction on issue of search warrants under Obscene Publications Act 1959.

*Extent: E+W*

A justice of the peace shall not issue a warrant under section 3(1) of the [^c7282461]Obscene Publications Act 1959 (search for and seizure of obscene articles) except on an information laid by or on behalf of the Director of Public Prosecutions or by a constable.
