# Countryside (Scotland) Act 1967, section 31

## 31. Compulsory powers for creation of public paths.[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where it appears to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c] a general or district planning[^c1423561] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c] authority that there is need for a public path over land in their area and they are satisfied that, having regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c]

- (a) the extent to which the path would add to the convenience or enjoyment of a substantial section of the public, or to the convenience of persons resident in the area, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c]
- (b) the effect which the creation of the path would have on the rights of persons interested in the land, account being taken of the provisions as to compensation contained in section 37 below, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c]

it is expedient that the path should be created, the authority, if it appears to them impracticable to create the path by means of a public path creation agreement, may by order (in this Act referred to as a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c]**public path creation order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c]**”) made by them and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c] , subject to paragraph 2(1A) of Schedule 3 of this Act,[^c1423562] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c] submitted to and confirmed by the Secretary of State create a public path. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c]

(2) A right of way created by a public path creation order may be either unconditional or subject to such limitations or conditions as may be specified in the order, and the Secretary of State may *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c] , in a case where his confirmation of the order is required, confirm it[^c1423563] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c] subject to such modifications or conditions as he thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c]

(3) A public path creation order shall be in such form as may be prescribed and shall contain a map, on such scale as may be prescribed, defining the land over which a public path is thereby created. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c]

(4) The provisions in that behalf of Schedule 3 to this Act shall apply to the making, confirmation, validity and date of operation of public path creation orders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b80ba32c7b6c2f8b27279173e71b8c]
