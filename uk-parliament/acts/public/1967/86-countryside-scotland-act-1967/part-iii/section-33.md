## 33. Making up and maintenance of public paths.[^M_F_25a0cded-512d-46a1-ce97-e50a2676505c]

*Extent: S*

(1) On the creation of a public path in pursuance of a public path creation agreement, or on the coming into operation of a public path creation order or public path diversion order, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_25a0cded-512d-46a1-ce97-e50a2676505c] the general or district planning[^c1423568] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_25a0cded-512d-46a1-ce97-e50a2676505c] authority shall carry out such work as appears to them to be necessary to bring it into a fit condition for use by the public as a public path in conformity with the terms and conditions of the said agreement or order, as the case may be, and shall maintain it in such condition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_25a0cded-512d-46a1-ce97-e50a2676505c]
