## 34. Closure of public paths.[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where it appears to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9] a general or district planning[^c1423570] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9] authority as respects a public path in their area that it is expedient that the path should be closed on the ground that the path is not needed for public use, the authority may by order (in this Act referred to as a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9]**public path extinguishment order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9]**”) made by them and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9] , subject to paragraph 2(1A) of Schedule 3 to this Act,[^c1423571] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9] submitted to and confirmed by the Secretary of State extinguish the right of way over the path. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9]

(2) The Secretary of State shall not confirm a public path extinguishment order unless he is satisfied that it is expedient so to do having regard to the extent to which it appears to him that the path would, apart from the order, be likely to be used by the public, and having regard to the effect which the extinguishment of the right of way would have as respects land served by the path, account being taken of the provisions as to compensation contained in section 37 below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9]

(3) A public path extinguishment order shall be in such form as may be prescribed and shall contain a map, on such scale as may be prescribed, defining the land over which the right of way is thereby extinguished. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9]

(4) Schedule 3 to this Act shall have effect as to the making, confirmation, validity and date of operation of public path extinguishment orders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9]

(5) Where in accordance with regulations made under paragraph 3 of the said Schedule proceedings preliminary to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9] making or[^c1423572] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9] confirmation of a public path extinguishment order are taken concurrently with proceedings preliminary to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9] making or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9] confirmation of a public path creation order or of a public path diversion order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9] [^c1423574]... then, in considering— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9]

- (a) under subsection (1) above whether the path to which the public path extinguishment order relates is needed for public use, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9]
- (b) under subsection (2) above to what extent that path would apart from the order be likely to be used by the public, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9]

the local planning authority or the Secretary of State, as the case may be, may have regard to the extent to which the public path creation order or the public path diversion order would provide an alternative path. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9]

(6) For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2) above, any temporary circumstances preventing or diminishing the use of a path by the public shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33545c14f59e79842f14f70c189773f9]
