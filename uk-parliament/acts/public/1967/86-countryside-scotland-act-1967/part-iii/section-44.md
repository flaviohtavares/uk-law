# Countryside (Scotland) Act 1967, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2019-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/86
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Pasturing of bulls.

*Extent: S*

(1) No person shall, being the occupier of any field or enclosure through which there is a public right of way, permit any bull to be at large in such field or enclosure:

Provided that this section shall not apply to any bull which—

- (a) does not exceed the age of 10 months; or
- (b) is not of a recognised dairy breed and is at large in any field or enclosure in which cows or heifers are also at large.

(2) A person who fails to comply with the foregoing subsection shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale[^c1423654].
