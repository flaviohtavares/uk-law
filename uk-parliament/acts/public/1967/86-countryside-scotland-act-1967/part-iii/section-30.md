# Countryside (Scotland) Act 1967, section 30

## 30. Creation of public paths by agreement.[^c-19f750f0ddb6dccef606360c54a0ac8b]

*Extent: S*

(1) A general or district planning[^c1423560] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-19f750f0ddb6dccef606360c54a0ac8b] authority shall have power to enter into an agreement with any person having the necessary power in that behalf for the creation by that person of a public path over land in their area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-19f750f0ddb6dccef606360c54a0ac8b]

(2) An agreement made under the foregoing subsection (in this Act referred to as a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-19f750f0ddb6dccef606360c54a0ac8b]**public path creation agreement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-19f750f0ddb6dccef606360c54a0ac8b]**”) shall be on such terms as to payment or otherwise as may be specified in the agreement, and may, if it is so agreed, provide for the creation of the public path subject to limitations or conditions affecting any public right of way thereover. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-19f750f0ddb6dccef606360c54a0ac8b]

(3) In this Part of this Act “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-19f750f0ddb6dccef606360c54a0ac8b]**public path *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-19f750f0ddb6dccef606360c54a0ac8b]**” means a way which is a footpath or bridleway or a combination of those. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-19f750f0ddb6dccef606360c54a0ac8b]
