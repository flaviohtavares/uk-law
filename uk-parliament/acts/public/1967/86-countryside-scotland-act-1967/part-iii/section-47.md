# Countryside (Scotland) Act 1967, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2019-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/86
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Interpretation of Part III.

*Extent: S*

In this Part of this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**bridleway**” means a way over which the public have the following, but no other, rights of way, that is to say, a right of way on foot and a right of way on horseback or leading a horse, with or without a right to drive animals of any description along that way;
- “**footpath**” means a way over which the public have the following, but no other, rights of way, that is to say, a right of way on foot with or without a right of way on pedal cycles;
- “**horse**” includes pony, ass and mule, and “**horseback**” shall be construed accordingly;
- “**public path**” has the meaning assigned to it in section 30 of this Act ;and[^c1423673]
- “ **public road** ” has the same meaning as in the [^c1423674] Roads (Scotland) Act 1984.

  and references to a right of way do not include references to access rights within the meaning of section 1 of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 2)[^key-f22fe4bb06f82e9382e569f099d022c7]
