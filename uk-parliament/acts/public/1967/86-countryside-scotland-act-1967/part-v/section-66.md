# Countryside (Scotland) Act 1967, section 66

## 66. Conservation of natural beauty.[^c1423984][^c1423985][^c1423983]

*Extent: S*

In the excersise of their functions relating to land under any enactment every Minister, government department and public body shall have regard to the desirability of conserving the natural heritage of Scotland within the meaning of the Natural Heritage (Scotland) Act 1991[^c1423986].
