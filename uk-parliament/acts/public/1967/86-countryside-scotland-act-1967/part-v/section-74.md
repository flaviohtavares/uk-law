# Countryside (Scotland) Act 1967, section 74

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2019-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/86
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Expenses.

*Extent: S*

(1) The Secretary of State may pay such sums in respect of the expenses of Scottish Natural Heritage[^c1424049] as he may, with the consent of the Treasury, determine.

(2) There shall be paid out of moneys provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenses incurred by the Secretary of State under this Act; and
- (b) any increase in the sums payable out of moneys so provided under any enactment relating to local government in Scotland [^c-a86d30e3418f54688884f4a126c28a58]....
