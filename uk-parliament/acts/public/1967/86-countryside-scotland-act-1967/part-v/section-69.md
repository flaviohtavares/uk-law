# Countryside (Scotland) Act 1967, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2019-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/86
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Acquisition, appropriation, disposal, etc. of land.

*Extent: S*

(1) The [^c1424002]Acquisition of Land (Authorisation Procedure) (Scotland) Act 1947 shall apply in relation to the compulsory acquisition of land under this Act as if this Act had been in force immediately before the commencement of that Act, and in relation to such acquisition of land—

- (a) by the Secretary of State, as if section 1(1)(b) of that Act included a reference to the Secretary of State, and
- (b) by Scottish Natural Heritage[^c1424003] or by a local planning authority, as if it or[^c1424004] they were a local authority within the meaning of that Act.

(2) No land may be compulsorily acquired under this Act unless the acquisition is authorised by the Secretary of State.

(3) For the purposes of this Act a general or district planning[^c1424005] authority shall have the powers conferred on a local authority by sections 70, 71, 73 and 74 and section 85 of the[^c1424006] [^c1424007]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 (acquisition, appropriation and disposal of land and gifts of land) and Sections 70(3) and 211 of that Act (application of Lands Clauses Acts etc. and enforcement of duty) shall apply to a general or district planning[^c1424009] authority as they apply to a local authority.
