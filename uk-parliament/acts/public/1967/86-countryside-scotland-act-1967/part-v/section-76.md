# Countryside (Scotland) Act 1967, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2019-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/86
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Inquiries and service of notices.

*Extent: S*

(1) section 265 of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997[^c1424075](which provides for the holding by a Minister of local inquiries) shall apply for the purposes of this Act.

(2) Section 271 of the Act of 1997[^c1424076] (which provides for the services of notices and other documents) shall apply to notices and other documents required or authorised to be served or given under this Act:

Provided that this subsection shall not apply to the service of any notice required or authorised to be served under Schedule 1 to the [^c1424077]Acquisition of Land (Authorisation Procedure) (Scotland) Act 1947 as applied by this Act.
