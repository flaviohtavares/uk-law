# Countryside (Scotland) Act 1967, section 57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2019-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/86
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Supplementary provisions as to byelaws.

*Extent: S*

(1) Sections 202[^c1423848][^c1423847] to 204 of the Local Government (Scotland) Act [^c1423850]1973 (which relate to the procedure for making byelaws, penalties and the proof of byelaws in legal proceedings) shall apply to byelaws made by Scottish Natural Heritage[^c1423851] or a local planning authority or an Electricity Board under section 54, . . . [^c1423852] or section 64 of this Act as if Scottish Natural Heritage or authority or Board were a local authority within the meaning of the said Act of 1973.

(2) In relation to byelaws made under the said section 54 . . . [^c1423852] or the said section 64 the confirming authority for the purposes of section 202 of the said Act of 1973 shall be the Secretary of State.
