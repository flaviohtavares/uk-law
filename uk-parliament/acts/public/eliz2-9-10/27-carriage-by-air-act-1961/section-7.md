# Carriage by Air Act 1961, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/9-10/27

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Power to exclude aircraft in use for military purposes.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Her Majesty may from time to time by Order in Council direct that this section shall apply, or shall cease to apply, to the United Kingdom or any other State specified in the Order.

(2) The the applicable provisions[^c12407281] to this Act shall not apply to the carriage of persons, cargo and baggage for the military authorities of a State to which this section applies in aircraft registered in that State if the whole capacity of the aircraft has been reserved by or on behalf of those authorities.
