# Carriage by Air Act 1961, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/9-10/27

## 6. Contributory negligence.[^c-680ee16b48d757f39c3bd8682fba4551][^c-8ee85266dd5afd54a0b4317ad45f990d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c12407221] It is hereby declared that for the purposes of the provisions mentioned in subsection (2)[^c12407231] the [^c12407241]Law Reform (Contributory Negligence) Act 1945 (including that Act as applied to Scotland), and section two of the [^c12407251]Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 1948, are provisions of the law of the United Kingdom under which a court may exonerate the carrier wholly or partly from his liability.

(2)[^c12407261] The provisions are—

- (a) Article 21 of the Convention;
- (b) Article 21 of the Convention as amended; and
- (c) Article 20 of the Montreal Convention.
