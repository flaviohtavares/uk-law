# Carriage by Air Act 1961, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/9-10/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Fatal accidents.[^c-29e6c28da1c84cd52ba0226a3888059a][^c-29948c002b67d0d08f5cc2b1cf43c047][^c12406311]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

References in section one of the [^c12406321]Fatal Accidents Act, 1846, as it applies in England and Wales, and in Article 3(1) of the Fatal Accidents (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^c12406331] to a wrongful act, neglect or default shall include references to any occurrence which gives rise to a liability under —[^c12406341]

- (a) Article 17 of the Convention;
- (b) Article 17 of the Convention as amended; or
- (c) Article 17.1 of the Montreal Convention.
