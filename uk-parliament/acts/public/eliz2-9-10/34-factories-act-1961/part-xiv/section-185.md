# Factories Act 1961, section 185

> Text as at 2016-07-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 185. Short title, commencement and extent.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Factories Act 1961.

(2) This Act shall come into force on the first day of April, nineteen hundred and sixty-two.

(3) This Act, except subsections (1) and (2) of section seventy-seven [^c14259301]. . ., does not extend to Northern Ireland.
