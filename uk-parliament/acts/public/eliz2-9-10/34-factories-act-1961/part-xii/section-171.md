# Factories Act 1961, section 171

> Text as at 2016-07-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 171. Application of the Arbitration (Scotland) Act 2010 or (Extent: S) the Arbitration Act 1996.[^c14254461][^M_F_1be30dd5-e601-49df-daac-f4c5ef956e38]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing in any of sections 1 to 15 of and schedule 1 to the Arbitration (Scotland) Act 2010 or *(Extent: S)*[^M_F_643b251d-8109-469e-8ada-ec3f2fa93be0] Part I of the Arbitration Act 1996 applies *(Extent: S)*[^M_F_d7b80c89-4293-46d0-fa33-01304b672c46] to proceedings under this Act except in so far as those provisions *(Extent: S)*[^M_F_4b9a320e-977f-427e-f882-703e22922df1] may be applied by regulations made under this Act.
