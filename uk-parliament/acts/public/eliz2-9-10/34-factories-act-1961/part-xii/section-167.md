# Factories Act 1961, section 167

> Text as at 2016-07-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 167. Proceedings for offences in respect of the employment of children.

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of any proceedings under this Act in respect of the employment of children in contravention of . . . [^c14254391]section one hundred and thirty six of the[^c14254401] [^c14254411]Education (Scotland) Act 1962 or section one of the [^c14254421]Employment of Women, Young Persons, and Children Act 1920 or any other enactment prohibiting the employment of children which is incorporated with this Act, references in this Part of this Act to young persons shall be construed as including references to children within the meaning of any such enactment.
