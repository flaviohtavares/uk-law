# Rating and Valuation Act 1961

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1996-11-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

Rating and Valuation Act 1961 [^c660986](repealed 1.11.1996)

1961 c.45

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

## Part I — General Provisions as to Valuation and Rating

### 1. Rating of industrial and freight-transport hereditaments.[^c660988]

No reduction in rateable value shall be made under section sixty-eight of the [^c660989]Local Government Act, 1929 (which provided for relief from rates in respect of industrial and freight-transport hereditaments) for the purposes of valuation lists coming into force after the passing of this Act.
