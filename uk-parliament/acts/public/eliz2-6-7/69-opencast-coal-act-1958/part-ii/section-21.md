# Opencast Coal Act 1958, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2025-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/6-7/69

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 21. Terminal compensation.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where a compulsory rights order comprises the whole or part of a holding to which this section applies, the provisions of this and the two next following sections shall have effect as to compensation payable in respect of that holding by the person who immediately before the end of the period of occupation is the person entitled to the rights conferred by the order.[^c6618201].

(2) Subsection (2) of section seventeen of this Act shall have effect in relation to this section as it has effect in relation to that section, and references to a holding to which this section applies shall be construed accordingly.

(3) Compensation payable in respect of a holding under the provisions referred to in subsection (1) of this section shall consist of either or both of the following, that is to say—

- (a) compensation by way of payment of cost of works, and
- (b) compensation by reference to the diminution in value of the holding.
