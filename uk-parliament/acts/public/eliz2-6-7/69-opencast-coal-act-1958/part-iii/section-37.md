# Opencast Coal Act 1958, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2025-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/6-7/69

## 37. Consequential adjustments between landlords and tenants and in respect of mortgages and mining leases and orders.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The provisions of the Seventh Schedule to this Act shall have effect as to matters arising between landlords and tenants, or (in England and Wales) between mortgagees and mortgagors, or in respect of mining leases or orders conferring working rights, as mentioned in that Schedule, in consequence of the coming into operation of a compulsory rights order or the occupation or use of land in the exercise of rights conferred by such an order.
