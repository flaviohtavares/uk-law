# Gambling Act 2005, section 251

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 251. Territorial application[^c-2dbafb65ab28009afa9934bf8bf8cd82]

*Extent: E+W+S*

This Part applies—

- (a) to anything done in relation to a gaming machine which (or any part of which) is situated in Great Britain, and
- (b) to anything done in Great Britain in relation to a gaming machine (irrespective of where it is situated).
