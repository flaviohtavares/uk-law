# Gambling Act 2005, section 133

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133. Multiple licences[^key-de65743326e00c12772e9b03fed797cc][^key-b91b4450fb503a3b47fb51f92755c42a][^key-b91b4450fb503a3b47fb51f92755c42a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Commission may not issue a personal licence to an individual who already holds one.

(2) But a personal licence may authorise the performance of more than one function.
