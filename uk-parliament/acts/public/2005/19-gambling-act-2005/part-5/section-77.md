## 77. Individual condition imposed by Commission[^key-1f9fe6daec42db66698deada2968c40a][^key-db6a924b6c60dc1ce719f4a0f0ef546f][^key-e1f6e585bac667309e250f830e864ec8][^key-db6a924b6c60dc1ce719f4a0f0ef546f][^key-e1f6e585bac667309e250f830e864ec8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the Commission issues an operating licence it may attach a condition to the licence.
