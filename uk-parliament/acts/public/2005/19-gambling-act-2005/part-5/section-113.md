# Gambling Act 2005, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 113. Surrender[^c-237596af92331d7d5d5f6288d2446ecf][^c-db6a924b6c60dc1ce719f4a0f0ef546f][^c-b9f85ccab040ea25b265b6e7e1841ca7][^c-e1f6e585bac667309e250f830e864ec8][^c-db6a924b6c60dc1ce719f4a0f0ef546f][^c-e1f6e585bac667309e250f830e864ec8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An operating licence shall cease to have effect if the licensee—

- (a) notifies the Commission of his intention to surrender the licence, and
- (b) gives the Commission either—
  - (i) the licence, or
  - (ii) a written statement explaining why it is not reasonably practicable to produce the licence.
