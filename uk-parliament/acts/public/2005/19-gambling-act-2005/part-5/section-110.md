>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Indefinite duration[^key-db6a924b6c60dc1ce719f4a0f0ef546f][^key-cbe6f215f0f3070b9aa23c07460e4886][^key-805c809a165b568135a6522ebc4fa089][^key-e1f6e585bac667309e250f830e864ec8][^key-21b89f77920d0c9de093933618794e89][^key-db6a924b6c60dc1ce719f4a0f0ef546f][^key-e1f6e585bac667309e250f830e864ec8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An operating licence shall continue to have effect unless and until it ceases to have effect in accordance with—

- (a) a determination under section 111, or
- (b) section 113, 114, 115, 118 or 119.
