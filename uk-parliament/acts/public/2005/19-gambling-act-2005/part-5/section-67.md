# Gambling Act 2005, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 67. Remote gambling[^c-e9d5bb4b74e5ad9e06d00441ae7cf319][^c-db6a924b6c60dc1ce719f4a0f0ef546f][^c-deab32ea2fb9129eb0dd09fb35f729b9][^c-e1f6e585bac667309e250f830e864ec8][^c-24789c5949bf878c3c5aa50f856f6656][^c-db6a924b6c60dc1ce719f4a0f0ef546f][^c-e1f6e585bac667309e250f830e864ec8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An operating licence is a “**remote operating licence**” if it authorises activity to be carried on—

- (a) in respect of remote gambling, or
- (b) by means of remote communication.

(2)[^c-0d7d9f5309ec6b229660426557b4bc27] A remote operating licence may not also authorise activity which is neither—

- (a) in respect of remote gambling, nor
- (b) carried on by means of remote communication.

(3)[^c-0d7d9f5309ec6b229660426557b4bc27] An operating licence must state whether it is a remote operating licence or not.

(4)[^c-0d0842e510d3ad6e9d95d0bef0af0cd1] The power of the Commission to issue a remote operating licence to the holder of a licence suspended or revoked pursuant to a direction given under Schedule 27 to the Finance Act 2014 is subject to paragraph 7 of that Schedule (requirement for HMRC's consent).
