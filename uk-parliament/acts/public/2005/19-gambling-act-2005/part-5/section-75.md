# Gambling Act 2005, section 75

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. General conditions imposed by Commission[^c-db6a924b6c60dc1ce719f4a0f0ef546f][^c-e1f6e585bac667309e250f830e864ec8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-4561385dee07d5cafe2802f4d1cfd258] The Commission may specify conditions to be attached to—

- (a) each operating licence, or
- (b) each operating licence falling within a specified class.

(2)[^c-4561385dee07d5cafe2802f4d1cfd258][^c-2e841ab5319cdee09b9586076233d8e1] For the purposes of subsection (1)(b) a class may be defined wholly or partly by reference to—

- (a) the nature of the licensed activities;
- (b) the circumstances in which the licensed activities are carried on;
- (c) the nature or circumstances of the licensee or of another person involved or likely to be involved in the conduct of the licensed activities.

(3)[^c-428a77b77722726041499f015fc14a9a] Where the Commission issues an operating licence it shall attach to the licence any condition specified under subsection (1) as a condition to be attached to operating licences of a class within which the licence falls.
