# Gambling Act 2005, section 352

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 352. Data protection[^key-4c285947d3a85043e00343e7740073e7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-1202760faeaebfe9ba56296fb70732f0] Nothing in this Act authorises a disclosure which contravenes the data protection legislation[^key-55fed12c6a025509416e4305ffb214a4] .

(2)[^key-c2e4fb6864cf3bbf7a375c1038e1ef2d] In this section, “**the data protection legislation**” has the same meaning as in the Data Protection Act 2018 (see section 3 of that Act).
