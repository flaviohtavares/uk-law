# Gambling Act 2005, section 356

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 356. Amendments and repeals

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-d313fa6952bae88fbf629d802b338358][^key-0defff2e7f26962b50fbdfef9549d91b][^key-c7c7659d01a4a62d0221b348652782d7][^key-4c24937a554b73e4b020e7c138f3667a][^key-33901d60329f65b7f1422238979d3e33] Schedule 16 (minor and consequential amendments) shall have effect.

(2)[^key-d313fa6952bae88fbf629d802b338358][^key-0defff2e7f26962b50fbdfef9549d91b][^key-c7c7659d01a4a62d0221b348652782d7][^key-4c24937a554b73e4b020e7c138f3667a][^key-33901d60329f65b7f1422238979d3e33] An amendment in that Schedule shall have the same extent as the enactment amended (or as the relevant portion of the enactment amended).

(3) The following shall cease to have effect—

- (a)[^key-83aa670ee6cbab11ffacc3a312caa15e] the Gaming Act 1710 (c. 19),
- (b)[^key-83aa670ee6cbab11ffacc3a312caa15e] the Gaming Act 1738 (c. 28),
- (c)[^key-83aa670ee6cbab11ffacc3a312caa15e] the Gaming Act 1835 (c. 41),
- (d)[^key-83aa670ee6cbab11ffacc3a312caa15e] the Gaming Act 1845 (c. 109),
- (e)[^key-83aa670ee6cbab11ffacc3a312caa15e] the Gaming Act 1892 (c. 9),
- (f)[^key-4419395b43358e641980cf62207b501b] the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 (c. 2),
- (g)[^key-89f1c987b533033ecc35fcc788d94a7b] the Gaming Act 1968 (c. 65),
- (h)[^key-83aa670ee6cbab11ffacc3a312caa15e] the Lotteries Act 1975 (c. 58), and
- (i)[^key-f65b003bd6615c29cfdd551658b26ef8] the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976 (c. 32).

(4)[^key-9bb48d72465f17025fd916686cef8ea3][^key-fe424431ef76d7f3df612dd22ed8c2cb][^key-ce5b5bfcd20a8e268982a6d1ffce704c][^key-b6264f3884ceee9a22d9c3cb67bb0a9a][^key-39e52707ebd2208ff9114fd080e2983e][^key-33901d60329f65b7f1422238979d3e33] The enactments listed in Schedule 17 are hereby repealed to the extent specified.

(5)[^key-9bb48d72465f17025fd916686cef8ea3][^key-fe424431ef76d7f3df612dd22ed8c2cb][^key-ce5b5bfcd20a8e268982a6d1ffce704c][^key-b6264f3884ceee9a22d9c3cb67bb0a9a][^key-39e52707ebd2208ff9114fd080e2983e][^key-33901d60329f65b7f1422238979d3e33] A repeal in that Schedule shall have the same extent as the provision repealed (subject to the Note to that Schedule).
