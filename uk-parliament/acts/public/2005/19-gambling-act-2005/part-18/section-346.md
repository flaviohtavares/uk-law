# Gambling Act 2005, section 346

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 346. Prosecution by licensing authority

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A licensing authority may institute criminal proceedings in respect of an offence under any of the following provisions of this Act—

- (a)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] section 37,
- (b)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] section 185,
- (c)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] section 186,
- (d)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] section 229,
- (e)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] section 242,
- (f)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] section 258,
- (g)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] section 259,
- (h)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] section 260,
- (i)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] section 261,
- (j)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] section 262,
- (k)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] section 326,
- (l)[^key-e7121861b8f7edecfd8cb67c7396d823] section 342,
- (m)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] paragraph 20 of Schedule 10,
- (n)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] paragraph 10 of Schedule 13, and
- (o)[^key-febda44a6f80e6e9badc80a2c0fd7d0f] paragraph 20 of Schedule 14.

(2)[^key-e7121861b8f7edecfd8cb67c7396d823] This section is without prejudice to section 222 of the Local Government Act 1972 (c. 70) (power of local authorities to prosecute or defend legal proceedings).

(3)[^key-e7121861b8f7edecfd8cb67c7396d823] This section shall not apply to an authority in Scotland.
