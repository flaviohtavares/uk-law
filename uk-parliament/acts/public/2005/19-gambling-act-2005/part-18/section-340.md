# Gambling Act 2005, section 340

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 340. Foreign betting[^key-2c8a03098e6860c354667fcfc8af0b17]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 9 to 9B of the Betting and Gaming Duties Act 1981 (c. 63) (prohibitions, for protection of the revenue, in relation to foreign betting) shall cease to have effect.
