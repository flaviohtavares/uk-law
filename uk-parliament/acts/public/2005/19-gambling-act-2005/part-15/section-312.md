# Gambling Act 2005, section 312

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 312. Clubs

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-60389d3bf8dedbbbfb9dfe1e62d7ff27] A constable or enforcement officer may enter premises to which this section applies for a purpose specified in subsection (3).

(2)[^key-60389d3bf8dedbbbfb9dfe1e62d7ff27] This section applies to premises which a constable or enforcement officer reasonably believes to be used by a members' club, a commercial club or a miners' welfare institute.

(3)[^key-60389d3bf8dedbbbfb9dfe1e62d7ff27] The purposes mentioned in subsection (1) are—

- (a) to determine whether gaming is taking place on the premises or is about to take place on the premises,
- (b) to determine whether any gaming that is taking place or is about to take place on the premises is in accordance with—
  - (i) section 269,
  - (ii) a club gaming permit, or
  - (iii) a club machine permit.

(4)[^key-2f7969252991227fe288951e2332243e][^key-db5c05207631fa6449d60faa0f5bbee9] An authorised local authority officer may enter premises in respect of which an application has been made for a club gaming permit or a club machine permit for a purpose connected with the consideration of the application.
