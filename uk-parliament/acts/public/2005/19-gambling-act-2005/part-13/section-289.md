# Gambling Act 2005, section 289

## 289. Prize gaming permits

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-ff9ef73bf6bb68d2cf3a2de3d4e39bc3] A person does not commit an offence under section 33 or 37 by providing facilities for prize gaming if—

- (a) the gaming satisfies the conditions specified in section 293, and
- (b) the facilities are provided in accordance with a prize gaming permit.

(2)[^key-b2a5c266d51c37145fa513b270698f8f] A prize gaming permit is a permit issued by a licensing authority authorising a person to provide facilities for gaming with prizes on specified premises.

(3)[^key-df5f7492234074f50bf5efef232cb2d7][^key-550a91a9760e4839ea5fd7fc1e8edeaa][^key-fb6b49f965105f6cf7281585cca15937] Schedule 14 makes further provision about prize gaming permits.
