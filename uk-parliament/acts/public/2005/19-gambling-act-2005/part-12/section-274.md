# Gambling Act 2005, section 274

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 274. Procedure, &c.[^c-bc89f997820403b4b0cb7a8f87200288][^c-7381d6d69a582d690246b893104d5543]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-1c998e8521c462a73afcb05609637036] Schedule 12 makes further provision about club gaming permits and club machine permits.

(2)[^c-8799bcac9dd50a059f0de52c4e2cd0db][^c-453d6da354688bdc8996d7e81bce15b5][^c-35ad3f62fb1426f7892c5be54a535d0d] Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to Scotland if the applicant for or holder of the permit in question is the holder of [^c-3320bf0db65dad8b9e2bae28f1b8aee0]... a relevant Scottish licence or if he falls within such other description as may be specified in regulations under section 285(1).
