# Gambling Act 2005, section 266

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 266. Members' club[^c-37551f992d23ca58e5738ecb4468b897][^c-14a0616b984793e0e2ca5d44bb1fca9e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this Act members' club means a club—

- (a) which is established and conducted wholly or mainly for purposes other than the provision of facilities for gaming (subject to subsection (2)),
- (b) which is established and conducted for the benefit of its members (and which is not otherwise established or conducted as a commercial enterprise),
- (c) which is not established with the purpose of functioning only for a limited period of time, and
- (d) which has at least 25 individual members.

(2) A club is a members' club for the purposes of this Act despite subsection (1)(a) if—

- (a) it is established or conducted wholly or mainly for the purpose of the provision of facilities for gaming of a prescribed kind, and
- (b) facilities are not provided for any other kind of gaming in the course of the club's activities.
