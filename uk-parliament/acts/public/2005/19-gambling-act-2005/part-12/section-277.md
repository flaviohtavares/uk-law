# Gambling Act 2005, section 277

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 277. Alcohol licence[^c-103d26235573d117531871c923b2b753][^c-1be20be6c66eba91f3046fc3ccbe3238]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Act—

- (a) “**alcohol licence**” means a premises licence under Part 3 of the Licensing Act 2003 (c. 17),
- (b) “**on-premises alcohol licence**” means a premises licence under that Part which authorises the supply of alcohol for consumption on the licensed premises, and
- (c)[^c-4048c67a43df1c99d19f36e5b904b3b3] “relevant Scottish licence” means a premises licence issued under the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005, except where such a licence only applies to the sale of alcohol for consumption off the premises.
