# Gambling Act 2005, section 146

## 146. Tribunal Procedure Rules[^c-8964271973acba110b0e6f2330da5437][^c-155116b7063f04f5289ee8610811f48d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-9ab801d93cf7ea5445f569ea7b73d350] Tribunal Procedure Rules may make provision—

- (a) regulating the exercise of a right of appeal under this Act to the Tribunal;
- (b) for the suspension by the Tribunal of decisions of the Commission (whether or not they have already taken effect).

(2)[^c-081a96a879a438d830ef0d45ecf5c763] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In the case of proceedings before the Tribunal under this Act, Tribunal Procedure Rules may[^c-335f30656947b422efbd740bd3cf1e0d] provide—

- (a) that a person commits an offence if without reasonable excuse he fails to comply with a requirement of a specified kind imposed by or in accordance with the rules, and
- (b) that a person guilty of the offence shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
