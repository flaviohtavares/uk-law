# Gambling Act 2005, section 145

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2025-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 145. Stay pending appeal[^c-f133650ec07836e77472942a0a0f75ff]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A decision or other action under Part 5 or 6 shall have no effect while an appeal under section 141—

- (a) could be brought (ignoring the possibility of an appeal out of time under Tribunal Procedure Rules[^c-b6c36da992a44fdc79f6bc55719214d3] ), or
- (b) has been brought and has not yet been either finally determined or abandoned.

(2) But when making a decision or taking other action under Part 5 or 6 the Commission may direct that subsection (1) shall not apply.
