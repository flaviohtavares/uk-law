# Mental Capacity Act 2005, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Mental Health Act matters

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Nothing in this Act authorises anyone—

- (a) to give a patient medical treatment for mental disorder, or
- (b) to consent to a patient's being given medical treatment for mental disorder,

if, at the time when it is proposed to treat the patient, his treatment is regulated by Part 4 of the Mental Health Act.

(1A)[^c2090769] Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to any form of treatment to which section 58A of that Act (electro-convulsive therapy, etc.) applies if the patient comes within subsection (7) of that section (informal patient under 18 who cannot give consent).

(1B)[^c2090769][^c2090771] Section 5 does not apply to an act to which section 64B of the Mental Health Act applies (treatment of community patients not recalled to hospital).

(2)[^c2090771] “**Medical treatment**”, “**mental disorder**” and “**patient**” have the same meaning as in that Act.
