# Mental Capacity Act 2005, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Exceptions[^c2092484][^c2091356]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c2091362] The duty imposed by section 37(3), 38(3) or (4) , 39(4) or (5), 39A(3), 39C(3) or 39D(2)[^c2091363][^c2091363] does not apply where there is—

- (a) a person nominated by P (in whatever manner) as a person to be consulted on matters to which that duty relates,
- (b) a donee of a lasting power of attorney created by P who is authorised to make decisions in relation to those matters, or
- (c) a deputy appointed by the court for P with power to make decisions in relation to those matters.

(2)[^c2091365] A person appointed under Part 10 of Schedule A1 to be P's representative is not, by virtue of that appointment, a person nominated by P as a person to be consulted in matters to which a duty mentioned in subsection (1) relates.[^c2091365][^c2091356]
