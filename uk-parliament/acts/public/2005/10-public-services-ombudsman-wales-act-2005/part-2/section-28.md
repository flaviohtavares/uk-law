# Public Services Ombudsman (Wales) Act 2005, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2019-07-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/10
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Listed authorities

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The persons specified in Schedule 3 are listed authorities for the purposes of this Act.

(2) The Welsh Ministers[^c20337961] may by order amend Schedule 3 by—

- (a) adding a person;
- (b) omitting a person;
- (c) changing the description of a person.

(3) An order under subsection (2) adding a person to Schedule 3 may provide for this Act to apply to the person with the modifications specified in the order.

(4) Before making an order under subsection (2), the Welsh Ministers must consult the Ombudsman and any other persons it thinks appropriate.

(4A)[^c20337991] No order is to be made under subsection (2) unless a draft of the statutory instrument containing it has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

(5) Sections 29 and 30 contain further restrictions on the power in subsection (2).
