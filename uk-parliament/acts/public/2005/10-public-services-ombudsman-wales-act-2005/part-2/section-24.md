## 24. Special reports relating to the Welsh Assembly Government etc.[^c20337821]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if a special report is made in a case where the complaint was made in respect of the Welsh Assembly Government or the National Assembly for Wales Commission[^c20337841].

(2) The relevant person[^c20337861] must—

- (a) lay a copy of the report before the Assembly, [^c20337801]. . .
- (b) [^c20337801]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^c20337891] In subsection (2) “**the relevant person**” means—

- (a) if the complaint was made in respect of the Welsh Assembly Government, the First Minister for Wales, and
- (b) if the complaint was made in respect of the National Assembly for Wales Commission, a member of that Commission.
