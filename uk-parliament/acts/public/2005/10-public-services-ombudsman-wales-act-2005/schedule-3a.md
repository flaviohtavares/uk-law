## SCHEDULE 3A — Excluded Matters: Part 2A[^key-9d564b2f76a31188977ae81d64bfee69]

*Extent: E+W*

**1.** The commencement or conduct of proceedings before a court of competent jurisdiction.

**2.** Action taken in respect of appointments or removals, pay, discipline, superannuation or other personnel matters.
