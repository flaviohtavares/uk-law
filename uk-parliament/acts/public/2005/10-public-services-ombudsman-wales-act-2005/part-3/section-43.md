# Public Services Ombudsman (Wales) Act 2005, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2019-07-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/10
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Consequential, transitional provisions etc.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers[^c20338391] may by order make—

- (a) such consequential, incidental or supplemental provision, and
- (b) such transitory, transitional or saving provision,

as they think[^c20569271] necessary or expedient for the purposes of, in consequence of, or for giving full effect to, any provision of this Act.

(2) An order under subsection (1) may in particular amend, repeal or revoke any enactment other than one contained in an Act passed in a Session after that in which this Act is passed.

(3) The amendments that may be made by virtue of subsection (2) are in addition to those made by or under any other provision of this Act.

(4)[^c20338411] No order is to be made under subsection (1) unless a draft of the statutory instrument containing it has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.
