## 34R. Meaning of “care home” and “care home provider”[^c-cc96a2831599042c9f50396815a8ed9b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies for the purposes of this Act.

(2) “**Care home**” means premises at which a care home service, within the meaning of Part 1 of the Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016, is provided wholly or mainly to persons aged 18 or over;[^c-e001eb37dac418e836118cbe137a1cc5]

(3) “**Care home provider**” means a person who is a service provider of a care home service within the meaning of Part 1 of that Act where the service is provided wholly or mainly to persons aged 18 or over[^c-05f09bf9f82465ee721e49d263d7d1ab] .

(4) Action is to be treated as action taken by a care home provider if it is taken by—

- (a) a person employed by that provider,
- (b) a person acting on behalf of that provider, or
- (c) a person to whom that provider has delegated any functions.

(5) Action is also to be treated as action taken by a care home provider if—

- (a) that provider provides, by means of an arrangement with another person, accommodation, nursing or care in a care home in Wales for an individual because of the individual's vulnerability or need,[^c-7eb4fef1a24e88a7e29cbb3c4b7146ef] and
- (b) the action is taken by or on behalf of the other person in carrying out the arrangement.

(6)[^c-a54da0be3ccbaa6c332d7aa67ba3ce9f] “**Care**” has the same meaning as in Part 1 of the Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016.
