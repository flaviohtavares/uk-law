# Commissioners for Revenue and Customs Act 2005, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-08-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/11

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Data protection, &c.[^key-262f5300b6e2ef2c5829f4575ea32444]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-7b1b4bb5ba3d692257acb03aee8d5810] Nothing in sections 17 to 21 authorises the making of a disclosure which—

- (a) contravenes the data protection legislation[^key-9e84886f4efcaf92b147208c7e39e4ea] , or
- (b) is prohibited by any of Parts 1 to 7 or Chapter 1 of Part 9 of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016[^key-1187a68a80f9248cff366a44fd704d36] .

(2)[^key-da1761746417e49e40bae7a3128069f7] In this section, “**the data protection legislation**” has the same meaning as in the Data Protection Act 2018 (see section 3 of that Act).
