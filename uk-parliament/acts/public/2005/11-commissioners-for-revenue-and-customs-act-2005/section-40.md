# Commissioners for Revenue and Customs Act 2005, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-08-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/11

## 40. Confidentiality[^c-7594abcb9420b87e83e9b19bf8215070][^c-090bc8e1818e592787bbe39da7e49219]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Crown Prosecution Service[^c-e6b7d07bca9fca864cd00a500cb5f90c] may not disclose information which—

- (a) is held by the Service in connection with any of the Director of Public Prosecution’s functions[^c-3161a144fbb29bce455e150a307103a4], [^c-42c831e7d34bb89f4254752ce3c5bf99]...
- (b) relates to a person whose identity is specified in the disclosure or can be deduced from it, and[^c-bbd25a9eb72a7a9040e87657e31c5030]
- (c) was disclosed to the Director of Public Prosecutions by Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs for use in connection with a Revenue and Customs function of the Director of Public Prosecutions.

(2) But subsection (1)—

- (a) does not apply to a disclosure which—
  - (i) is made for the purposes of a function of the Director of Public Prosecutions[^c-a65399e9724b4b34ec573fde95efda99], and
  - (ii) does not contravene any restriction imposed by the Director of Public Prosecutions[^c-579feb9888f48091dfce91ff3c477fd3],
- (b) does not apply to a disclosure made to Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs in connection with a function of the Revenue and Customs (within the meaning of section 25),
- (c) does not apply to a disclosure made for the purposes of a criminal investigation or criminal proceedings (whether or not within the United Kingdom),
- (ca)[^c-2c988ae4e34846600db88651219d78e5] does not apply to a disclosure made for the purposes of—
  - (i) the exercise of any functions of the prosecutor under Parts 2, 3 and 4 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (c. 29),
  - (ii) the exercise of any functions of the National Crime Agency[^c-4fa15d481bdf969a4b72c512b58e77a5] under that Act,
  - (iii) the exercise of any functions of [^c-af93ef76de16316a821227a44b9d144d] ... the Director of the Serious Fraud Office, the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland or the Scottish Ministers under, or in relation to, Part 5 or 8 of that Act,
  - (iv) the exercise of any functions of an officer of Revenue and Customs, an accredited financial investigator[^c-c0d6c0a55921bac43cb6e94c62d661a7] or a constable under Chapter 3 of Part 5 of that Act, or
  - (v) investigations or proceedings outside the United Kingdom which have led or may lead to the making of an external order within the meaning of section 447 of that Act,
- (cb)[^c-d83c51d005fa614f01e76e28d183f618] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) does not apply to a disclosure which in the opinion of the Director of Public Prosecutions[^c-8cf74220d450df6574dddbaf16e03e43] is desirable for the purpose of safeguarding national security,
- (e) does not apply to a disclosure made in pursuance of an order of a court,
- (ea)[^c-8d2936fb731b513d3678454d8170d08f] does not apply to a disclosure made with the consent of the Commissioners (which may be general or specific),
- (f) does not apply to a disclosure made with the consent of each person to whom the information relates, and
- (g) is subject to any other enactment.

(3) A person commits an offence if he contravenes subsection (1).

(4) Subsection (3) does not apply to the disclosure of information about internal administrative arrangements of the Crown Prosecution Service[^c-b84407ed4f788ca2387a3e6a2361fe4b] (whether relating to a member of the Service[^c-71f9edbd44ea09de4ee17ef773b4c496] or to another person).

(5) It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under this section of disclosing information to prove that he reasonably believed—

- (a) that the disclosure was lawful, or
- (b) that the information had already and lawfully been made available to the public.

(6) In this section a reference to the Crown Prosecution Service[^c-e2d035ec841d7fd34eff40c1d1319870] includes a reference to—

- (za)[^c-dfb1533362f036aaf9a983489827c869] former members of the Crown Prosecution Service,
- (zb) persons who hold or have held appointment under section 5 of the Prosecution of Offences Act 1985,
- (a) former members of the Revenue and Customs Prosecutions Office[^c-13cc3d1aa62498d308277cd1f904e69b], and
- (b) persons who [^c-b97b19948e503d82f8d071f9ebe75713]... have held appointment under section 38.

(6A)[^c-bdf58d7fdf0ab7b33e01c58a54213848] In this section “Revenue and Customs function of the Director of Public Prosecutions” means—

- (a) a function of the Director of Public Prosecutions under section 3(2)(ab), (bb) or (ee) of the Prosecution of Offences Act 1985, or
- (b) a function of the Director of Public Prosecutions under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 that relates to a function of the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs or an officer of Revenue and Customs.

(7) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, to a fine or to both, or
- (b) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4330972fffeace58aaf4783792481001] the general limit in a magistrates’ court *(Extent: E+W)*, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both.

(8) A prosecution for an offence under this section may be instituted in England and Wales only by or with the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions.[^c-7d389df88f7fb3a3e78181f78ead650c]

(9) A prosecution for an offence under this section may be instituted in Northern Ireland only—

- (a) by the Commissioners, or
- (b) with the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland.

(10) In the application of this section to Scotland or Northern Ireland the reference in subsection (7)(b) to 12 months shall be taken as a reference to six months.

(10A)[^c-65fbdf321850ced18b56083c35e8b355] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(11) In subsection (2) the reference to an enactment does not include—

- (a) an Act of the Scottish Parliament or an instrument made under such an Act,
- (aa)[^c-823c08d35d3a9fe517acb7e1b4cefc3c] an Act of the National Assembly for Wales or an instrument made under such an Act, or
- (b) an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly or an instrument made under such an Act.
