# Commissioners for Revenue and Customs Act 2005, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-08-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Rewards[^key-2cfc2d616a694ce6d874e7a14547d5c1][^key-724108cbb8bf27d31bf75e35b4cf496a][^key-649dea7ba32fee267775e1d31c1a6ae9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commissioners may pay a reward to a person in return for a service which relates to a function of—

- (a) the Commissioners, or
- (b) an officer of Revenue and Customs.
