*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person commits an offence if he contravenes section 18(1) or (2A)[^c-992ea66b460c2dbb642ea0bc5a712bda] [^c-38aed8c6bd958cf4083d287f245aef59]... or 20(9) by disclosing revenue and customs information relating to a person whose identity—

- (a) is specified in the disclosure, or
- (b) can be deduced from it.

(2) In subsection (1) “**revenue and customs information relating to a person**” means information about, acquired as a result of, or held in connection with the exercise of a function of the Revenue and Customs (within the meaning given by section 18(4)(c)) in respect of the person; but it does not include information about internal administrative arrangements of Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs (whether relating to Commissioners, officers or others).

(3)[^c-fc32d0c17f7346c3c89e55a44cf35a92] It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under this section of disclosing information to prove that he reasonably believed—

- (a) that the disclosure was lawful, or
- (b) that the information had already and lawfully been made available to the public.

(4)[^c-fc32d0c17f7346c3c89e55a44cf35a92][^c-d82a947b5c3ee3b6e4c4e87bd9ea9c1f][^c-0fd444aaad223c0d944ea33e5f681422][^c-8095db28ce2050f49b7300133254f213][^c-8382ede4c14cda45d5a01531c22c29c5][^c-16f04b4a700b40afd98569530c5d4275][^c-92875463bc04dd2e1a3b616aabd94b9c][^c-b4d7d936d23b1fda67b797ef318f92cc][^c-8b0a028999293c52867e956033db1d6b][^c-311016d4fa8fc8bc0084937f3c52da9c][^c-2b3eb08fd3b3559a3de34e17794c7e23][^c-38c4cb20bfed1cb23670ace613915657][^c-28fdff8612cad47721f5b875d5c302f2][^c-a82e2f9e39d887b5ec81edd6cb390a85] A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, to a fine or to both, or
- (b) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4cd129cbe7c567fce0910f52100c3420] the general limit in a magistrates’ court *(Extent: E+W)*, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both.

(5)[^c-d82a947b5c3ee3b6e4c4e87bd9ea9c1f][^c-0fd444aaad223c0d944ea33e5f681422][^c-8095db28ce2050f49b7300133254f213][^c-8382ede4c14cda45d5a01531c22c29c5][^c-16f04b4a700b40afd98569530c5d4275][^c-92875463bc04dd2e1a3b616aabd94b9c][^c-b4d7d936d23b1fda67b797ef318f92cc][^c-8b0a028999293c52867e956033db1d6b][^c-311016d4fa8fc8bc0084937f3c52da9c][^c-2b3eb08fd3b3559a3de34e17794c7e23][^c-38c4cb20bfed1cb23670ace613915657][^c-28fdff8612cad47721f5b875d5c302f2][^c-a82e2f9e39d887b5ec81edd6cb390a85] A prosecution for an offence under this section may be instituted in England and Wales only by or with the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions.[^c-60b7c04ef1b9d41d8f7954edbce43ff2]

(6)[^c-d82a947b5c3ee3b6e4c4e87bd9ea9c1f][^c-0fd444aaad223c0d944ea33e5f681422][^c-8095db28ce2050f49b7300133254f213][^c-8382ede4c14cda45d5a01531c22c29c5][^c-16f04b4a700b40afd98569530c5d4275][^c-92875463bc04dd2e1a3b616aabd94b9c][^c-b4d7d936d23b1fda67b797ef318f92cc][^c-8b0a028999293c52867e956033db1d6b][^c-311016d4fa8fc8bc0084937f3c52da9c][^c-38c4cb20bfed1cb23670ace613915657][^c-28fdff8612cad47721f5b875d5c302f2][^c-a82e2f9e39d887b5ec81edd6cb390a85] A prosecution for an offence under this section may be instituted in Northern Ireland only—

- (a) by the Commissioners, or
- (b) with the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland.

(7)[^c-136f9989d6bbcda5e2ae5afa71824324][^c-d82a947b5c3ee3b6e4c4e87bd9ea9c1f][^c-0fd444aaad223c0d944ea33e5f681422][^c-8095db28ce2050f49b7300133254f213][^c-8382ede4c14cda45d5a01531c22c29c5][^c-16f04b4a700b40afd98569530c5d4275][^c-92875463bc04dd2e1a3b616aabd94b9c][^c-b4d7d936d23b1fda67b797ef318f92cc][^c-8b0a028999293c52867e956033db1d6b][^c-311016d4fa8fc8bc0084937f3c52da9c][^c-38c4cb20bfed1cb23670ace613915657][^c-28fdff8612cad47721f5b875d5c302f2][^c-a82e2f9e39d887b5ec81edd6cb390a85] In the application of this section to Scotland or Northern Ireland the reference in subsection (4)(b) to 12 months shall be taken as a reference to six months.

(8) This section is without prejudice to the pursuit of any remedy or the taking of any action in relation to a contravention of section 18(1) or (2A)[^c-cc4016275556b760670c3b042a62e97d] [^c-4e65e99a1765e67ae234f4f77de6cb78]... or 20(9) (whether or not this section applies to the contravention).
