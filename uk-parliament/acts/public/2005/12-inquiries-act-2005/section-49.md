> Text as at 2024-01-01.

## 49. Repeals and revocations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Tribunals of Inquiry (Evidence) Act 1921 (c. 7) is repealed.

(2) The provisions set out in Schedule 3 are repealed or revoked to the extent specified.
