# Railways Act 2005, section 54

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-05-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 54. Further amendments of the 1993 Act

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-813c4a37ff008fa8171d384ba61dc1e2] In sections 118 and 119 of the 1993 Act (powers in emergency and security powers etc.), at the end of subsection (11), in each case, insert “ with “**railway**” having its wider meaning for the purposes of this section. ”

(2)[^key-813c4a37ff008fa8171d384ba61dc1e2] After section 119(5) of that Act insert—

> (5A) The Secretary of State may give an instruction under this section for the protection of a relevant asset that is wholly in Scotland, or of persons or property on or in such an asset, only if—
>
> - (a) the asset would be a relevant asset even if railway did not have its wider meaning for the purposes of this section; or
> - (b) the instruction is given in the interests of national security.
>
> (5B) In subsection (5A) the reference to an instruction given in the interests of national security includes a reference to any instruction given for the purpose of ensuring that protection against terrorism is provided to the asset, persons or property in question.

(3)[^key-813c4a37ff008fa8171d384ba61dc1e2] In section 119(11) of that Act, after the definition of “specified” insert—

> “**terrorism**” has the same meaning as in the Terrorism Act 2000 (c. 11) (see section 1 of that Act);

.

(4)[^key-26983e2d0fe15c0fb7240409e0879360][^key-44a9b01137324942ebc1276636e2b6b7][^key-29f4995418cf8408dcfe01f16debcb76][^key-0d57c479359f778215f7f2ee03aecf3a] Schedule 11 (which makes further miscellaneous minor and consequential amendments of the 1993 Act) has effect.
