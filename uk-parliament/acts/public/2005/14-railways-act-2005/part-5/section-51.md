# Railways Act 2005, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-05-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 51. ORR to assist and advise national authorities

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) It shall be the duty of the Office of Rail and Road[^key-6f79dddea2a4e1d1e17ffe9da5677049] to comply with every reasonable requirement of the Secretary of State—

- (a)[^key-b8d1b57b21124569b94b38321dcfbaee] to provide him with information or advice about a matter connected with a function or other activity of his in relation to railways or railway services;
- (b)[^key-b9387cc503dc3bcea066934869899e42] to provide him with information or advice about a matter relevant to the railway safety purposes; or
- (c)[^key-b8d1b57b21124569b94b38321dcfbaee] otherwise to provide him with assistance in relation to a matter that is connected with such a function or activity or is relevant to those purposes.

(2)[^key-d6ad855c5efa86902d2eb29c61421b9b] It shall be the duty of the Office of Rail and Road to comply with every reasonable requirement of the Scottish Ministers—

- (a) to provide them with information or advice about a matter connected with a function or other activity of theirs in relation to railways or railway services; or
- (b) otherwise to provide them with assistance in relation to a matter that is connected with such a function or activity.

(3)[^key-b8d1b57b21124569b94b38321dcfbaee][^key-74329203e1cb9e3758147cf129194ccb] It shall be the duty of the Office of Rail and Road to comply with every reasonable requirement of the Welsh Ministers—

- (a) to provide them with information or advice about a matter connected with a function or other activity of theirs in relation to railways or railway services; or
- (b) otherwise to provide them with assistance in relation to a matter that is connected with such a function or activity.

(4)[^key-b8d1b57b21124569b94b38321dcfbaee] References in this section to the functions of a person in relation to railways or railway services include references, in particular, to all that person's functions under Part 1 of the 1993 Act, Part 4 of the 2000 Act or this Act.

(5)[^key-b9387cc503dc3bcea066934869899e42] In this section “**railway safety purposes**” has the same meaning as in Schedule 3.
