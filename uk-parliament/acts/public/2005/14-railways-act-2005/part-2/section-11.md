# Railways Act 2005, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-05-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 11. Notification of assistance from Welsh Ministers for freight services[^key-4b7e1f821076687fdf02b51a65a13414][^key-86fe65dd53e7f9f8b4368e316e9453fe]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section applies if the Welsh Ministers make or modify a scheme setting out how they propose to exercise their powers[^key-9430080341394c22731d3ea562514186] under section 10 for the purpose of securing the provision, improvement or development of—

- (a) services for the carriage of goods by railway; or
- (b) facilities for or in connection with—
  - (i) the carriage of goods by railway; or
  - (ii) the loading or unloading of goods carried or intended to be carried by railway.

(2)[^key-a610f0a01626959128a18ca6286bb9f5] This section also applies if the Welsh Ministers make or modify a determination of the criteria that they will apply in exercising their functions under such a scheme.

(3) The Welsh Ministers[^key-2a2b15fca69ca96e1a66d10e599eb26f] must send a copy of the scheme or determination, or (as the case may be) of the scheme or determination as modified, to the Secretary of State.

(4) In this section—

- “**facilities**” includes track, rolling stock, depots, access roads and equipment; and
- “**railway**” has its wider meaning.
