# Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. “Appropriate person”

*Extent: E+W*

In section 29 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 (c. 43), after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) “**Appropriate person**” means—
>
> - (a) in relation to England, the Secretary of State;
> - (b) in relation to Wales, the National Assembly for Wales.
