# Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 47. Abolition of requirement to contract out waste disposal functions[^key-37dba12d283bef34c7ca797405dc6b42][^key-60eece2a13bfee741b7ae55e8a1bd286]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 32 of and Schedule 2 to the Environmental Protection Act 1990 (c. 43) (power to require local authorities to transfer waste disposal functions etc to specially formed companies) shall cease to have effect.
