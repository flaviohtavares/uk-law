# Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 53. Supplementary enforcement powers[^key-df5c74748f64d372290271f0b27ffce9][^key-cb5f033146ce876f72ec39bed75c3ce1]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 108 of the Environment Act 1995 (c. 25) (powers of enforcing authorities etc), in subsection (15), in the definition of “**pollution control functions**” in relation to a waste collection authority, for “conferred on it by section 59” substitute “ conferred or imposed on it by or under Part 2 ”.
