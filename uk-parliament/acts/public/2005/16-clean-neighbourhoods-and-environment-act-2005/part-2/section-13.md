# Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Guidance[^c-ca274cc7aa4d70f1760a48dc6a406ef4][^c-5f26dc06fa92e2af49de793ce56be507][^c-621bff8d838560e8ab41dafd785c0b76]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Refuse Disposal (Amenity) Act 1978 (c. 3), after section 4 insert—

> **4A. Guidance**
>
> Any authority on whom functions are conferred under section 3 or 4 above must, in exercising those functions, have regard to any guidance given to the authority for the purpose by the appropriate person.
