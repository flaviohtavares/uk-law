# Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005, section 98

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 98. Sections 95A, 96 and 97: supplementary[^key-72d0d6b90f3d984138e2ad89f72d938d][^key-8e51c45be8b7f8fcaf0d7b1f08f5533c][^key-86a272396669785a5a25bae0ebc458e6][^key-0d58f39f843e8a56932fefc885351361]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In section 97[^key-1820f990c80a8b51e1b132f4ee1dd829], “**appropriate person**” means—

- (a) the Secretary of State, in relation to England;
- (b) the National Assembly for Wales, in relation to Wales.

(2) The powers to make regulations conferred by sections 95A,[^key-986d6694ae35a4a4adacfd5a6c754524] 96 and 97 include—

- (a) power to make different provision for different purposes (including different provision for different authorities or different descriptions of authority);
- (b) power to make consequential, supplementary, incidental and transitional provision and savings.

(3) Regulations under sections 95A,[^key-065cbc744fb519e0a3d7bc55a617b05f] 96 and 97 must be made by statutory instrument.

(4) The Secretary of State may not make a statutory instrument containing regulations under section 95A[^key-d98d60fc9441ba7831d3649149728397] or 97 unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.
