# Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005, section 105

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/16

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 105. Offences relating to pollution etc: penalties on conviction

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In paragraph 25 of Schedule 1 to the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999 (c. 24) (purposes for which regulations may be made under section 2: offences), in sub-paragraph (2)(a)—

- (a) in paragraph (i) for “six months” substitute “ 12 months ”;
- (b) in paragraph (ii) for “£20,000” substitute “ £50,000 ”.

(2) Subsection (1)(a)[^c-0087989102bf3f4569f137d2d202b83b] does not have effect in relation to regulations under section 2 of the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999 so far as relating to offences committed before 2 May 2022[^c-d183231d9d73f6e3e8423b13b01089be].
