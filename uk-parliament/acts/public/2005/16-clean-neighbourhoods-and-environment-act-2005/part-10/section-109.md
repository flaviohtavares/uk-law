# Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005, section 109

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 109. Money

*Extent: E+W*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred by the Secretary of State under this Act;
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other Act.
