# Education Act 2005, section 121

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 121. Parliamentary control of subordinate legislation

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any statutory instrument containing regulations or an order made by the Secretary of State under any provision of this Act is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to—

- (a) regulations to which subsection(2A) or[^c-386c49fd48067ee5212fcd9ae248f40d] (3) applies, or
- (b) an order made under section 125 (commencement).

(2A)[^c-8ea6cfaaf8f2a437898f6fe57543a786] This subsection applies to regulations under section 5(4A) (power to prescribe schools exempt from inspection), apart from the first regulations to be made under that subsection.

(2B) A statutory instrument which contains (whether alone or with other provisions) regulations to which subsection (2A) applies may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

(3) A statutory instrument which contains (whether alone or with other provisions) regulations made by the Secretary of State under section 124 which amend or repeal any part of the text of an Act may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.
