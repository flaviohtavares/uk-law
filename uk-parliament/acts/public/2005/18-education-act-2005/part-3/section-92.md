## 92. Joint exercise of functions

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State[^key-0fda7d74fba412935c97358bc2d00dbd] [^key-919a75bd84290096dde64c09331c3a5a]... and any other relevant funding body may exercise any of their functions jointly.

(2) In subsection (1) “**other relevant funding body**” means [^key-c75e3fe647b9f9a3e6e09581c35b7d8f]... the Office for Students[^key-46f7817fec8591c69a498a8949115349] [^key-2ca773a33863808f41d4690b174f410b]... [^key-c48a698ccd98bc4a1e04b8220d2724fc]... [^key-e544415d13422fa9133ed75187af2831]... .

(2A)[^key-06c4c18bcbc8bedf1a28e7e38ffa5145] The reference in subsection (1) to the functions of the Secretary of State is to the functions of the Secretary of State relating to training for members of the school workforce.
