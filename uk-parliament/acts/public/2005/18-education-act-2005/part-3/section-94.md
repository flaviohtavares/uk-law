## 94. Provision of information[^c-e86cd070db1130b8f0ecaac9f9bb673a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-6d78a67eef5e62855b65a382afba3c03] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-98793e60127a20cd2a5ffe9138b36ac9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The persons and bodies mentioned in subsection (4) must—

- (a) give the Secretary of State such information as the Secretary of State may require for the purpose of the exercise of the Secretary of State's functions relating to training for members of the school workforce;
- (b)[^c-54a23c430ce215aac9bd6572a3327d28] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The persons and bodies referred to in subsection (3) are—

- (a) a person receiving, or who has received or applied for [^c-514b51ce4e218750253ea9ccbfe2b866]... financial assistance from the Secretary of State under section 14 of the Education Act 2002;
- (b) a local authority.
