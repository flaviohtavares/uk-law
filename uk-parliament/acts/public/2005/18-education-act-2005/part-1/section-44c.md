## 44C. Report after area inspection on schools with sixth forms requiring significant improvement[^c-50057023474852ace1a59148f3fef9ab]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if in the course of an area inspection under section 63 of the Tertiary Education and Research (Wales) Act 2022[^c-2ad48b94d0ed92c0517c37978d2d1512] the Chief Inspector forms the opinion that a school requires significant improvement in relation to its sixth form.

(2) The Chief Inspector must make a report about the school stating that opinion.

(3) The report is to be treated for the purposes of this Part as if it were a report of an inspection of the school under section 28.
