# Education Act 2005, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Repeal of School Inspections Act 1996[^c19261721]

*Extent: E+W*

The School Inspections Act 1996 (c. 57) (which is superseded by the provisions of this Part) shall cease to have effect.
