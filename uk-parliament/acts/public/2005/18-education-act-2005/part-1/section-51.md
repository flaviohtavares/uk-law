# Education Act 2005, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/18

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Power of local authority to inspect maintained school for specific purpose[^c19818361][^c-94ed0295e29907765ea29be5dd3e5cb4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where—

- (a) for the purpose of enabling them to exercise any education[^c-6695c766f7a420241be3802c26b2dcca] function of theirs, a local authority require information about any matter in connection with a school which is maintained by them, and
- (b) it is not reasonably practicable for them to obtain the information in any other manner,

they may cause an inspection of the school to be made by one or more of their officers for the purpose of obtaining the information.

(2) An officer of a local authority inspecting a school under this section has at all reasonable times a right of entry to the premises of the school.
