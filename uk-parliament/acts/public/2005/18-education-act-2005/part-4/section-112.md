# Education Act 2005, section 112

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/18

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Power to provide that function of determining eligibility remains with local authority[^c-94ed0295e29907765ea29be5dd3e5cb4]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 512A of the Education Act 1996 (c. 56) (transfer of functions under section 512 of that Act to governing bodies), in subsection (4), for paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) if the duty corresponds to the one mentioned in subsection (2)(b) or (c)—
>   - (i) the order may provide that, notwithstanding the other provisions of the order, the function of determining whether pupils at the school fall within section 512ZB(4) is to be exercisable by the local authority, and
>   - (ii) section 533(3) shall not apply to school lunches or milk provided by the governing body in pursuance of the order.
