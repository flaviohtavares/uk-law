# Education Act 2005, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. local authority targets: England[^c-94ed0295e29907765ea29be5dd3e5cb4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations require local authorities[^c-a2173b6f678e4942b640e80d5cd628b4] in England to set annual targets in respect of the educational performance—

- (a) of pupils at schools maintained by them, and
- (b) of any persons of compulsory school age (whether or not pupils at such schools) who are or have been looked after by them.

(2) Regulations under this section may in particular—

- (a) specify the matters in respect of which targets are required to be set;
- (b) require proposed targets to be notified to the Secretary of State by a prescribed time;
- (c) authorise the Secretary of State—
  - (i) to modify any proposed target notified to him, or
  - (ii) to require a local authority to modify the proposed targets and to notify him of the proposed targets as modified;
- (d) require the targets to be set by a prescribed time;
- (e) require local authorities to publish, in such manner as may be specified in the regulations, any targets which they set.

(3) In subsection (1), “**looked after**” is to be read in accordance with section 22 of the Children Act 1989 (c. 41).
