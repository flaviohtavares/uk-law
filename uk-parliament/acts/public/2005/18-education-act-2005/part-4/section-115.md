# Education Act 2005, section 115

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 115. Power of governing body to make alternative provision for excluded pupils[^c19266731]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 29 of the 2002 Act (additional functions of governing body), in subsection (3) (power to require pupils to attend at other places for purpose of education) for “pupils in attendance at the school” substitute “ registered pupils ”.
