## 869. Penalties , interest and (affected by repeal, see note) VAT (affected by repeal, see note) surcharges (affected by repeal, see note) and interest: non-trades etc.[^c-dd278a19a927dd2c2e4db90b4c841082][^c-dd278a19a927dd2c2e4db90b4c841082][^c-dd278a19a927dd2c2e4db90b4c841082]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the purpose of calculating profits or other income charged to income tax.

(2) For this purpose “**profits or other income**” does not include the profits of—

- (a) a trade, profession, or vocation, or
- (b) a property business,

but see subsection (6).

(3) No deduction is allowed for any penalty or interest mentioned in the first column of the following table.

(4) This is the table—

| *Penalty or interest* | *Description of tax, levy or duty* |
| --- | --- |
| Interest under any provision of Part 9 of TMA 1970 | Income tax, capital gains tax and corporation tax |
| Interest required to be paid by regulations made under section 71 of FA 2004 (construction industry) |  |
| Penalty under any of sections 60 to 70 of VATA 1994 | Value added tax |
| Interest under section 101 of FA 2009 in respect of an amount of value added tax[^c-3e98f73be0773bee1bc0bd2444c92992] |  |
| Penalty under any of sections 8 to 11 of FA 1994 | Excise duties |
| Penalty under any of paragraphs 12 to 19 of Schedule 7 to FA 1994 | Insurance premium tax |
| Interest under section 60(8) of[^c-29483e062d412cf25966da497d4fc308] FA 1994 or paragraph 21 to Schedule 7 to FA 1994 |  |
| Penalty under any provision of Part 5 of Schedule 5 to FA 1996 | Landfill tax |
| Interest under section 56(5) of, or paragraph 26 or 27 of Schedule 5 to,[^c-c23598b25dce0bc0e2a1a74c38ce8369] FA 1996 |  |
| Penalty under any provision of Schedule 6 to FA 2000 | Climate change levy |
| Interest under any of paragraphs 70, 81 to 85 , 109 and 123(6)[^c-e6e5fa816303a652b7046bdc454f725f] of that Schedule |  |
| Penalty under any provision of Part 2 of FA 2001 | Aggregates levy |
| Interest under section 42(6) of, or[^c-da4f78381419573587d51583e94baa08] any of paragraphs 5 to 9 of Schedule 5 to, paragraph 6 of Schedule 8 to and paragraph 5 of Schedule 10 to,[^c-3545cc826a8bff33d14fadc2fb3751e1] FA 2001 |  |
| Penalty under section 25 or 26 of FA 2003 | Customs duties[^c-f712b8cfb0f0a1c20d4ba0d8a03a9460] |
| Penalty under any provision of Part 4 of FA 2003 | Stamp duty land tax |
| Interest under any provision of that Part |  |
| Penalty under Schedule 24 to[^c-d05a9a3ab71495348ca344f2cba22273] FA 2007 | Various taxes and excise duties |
| Penalty under Schedule 41 to FA 2008[^c-d259b8e9e6ddacbcf6bceb365662ef96] | Various taxes and excise duties |

(5) No deduction is allowed for any surcharge under section 59 of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4cdf204401ca0e76ea52a24864418685] VATA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4cdf204401ca0e76ea52a24864418685] 1994.

(6) Provision corresponding to that made by this section is made by—

- (a) section 54 (in relation to trades, professions and vocations), and
- (b) sections 272 and 272ZA[^c-eee02ba7193c8258a8a0062faba28972] (in relation to property businesses).
