## 839. Annual payments payable out of relevant foreign income

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In calculating the amount of relevant foreign income to be charged to income tax for a tax year, a deduction is to be allowed for an annual payment other than interest if it meets conditions A, B1 or B2 and C[^c-3eaf2f7cc3ce04a4edfb3d44f90ed118] .

(2) Condition A is that the payment is payable out of the relevant foreign income.

(3) Condition B1[^c-5328ebbf31ef5012d9e32a82d78d8717] is that, had the payment arisen in the United Kingdom, it would have been chargeable to income tax under one of the following provisions [^c-f07c91c105808d7b9c192df820a9fabf]...—

- [^c-4e302663e49faaacb63a889fe3d764d7]...
- section 579 (charge to tax on royalties and other income from intellectual property),
- Chapter 4 of Part 5 (certain telecommunication rights: non-trading income), [^c-2d224c3765660646c5a97fac39bd187d]...
- Chapter 7 of Part 5 (annual payments not otherwise charged).
- , or regulation 15 of the Unauthorised Unit Trusts (Tax) Regulations 2013.[^c-065b79e98e8ce0cba787896b1f2949a5]

(3A) Condition B2 is that, had the payment arisen in the United Kingdom it would have been—

- (a) required to be brought into account under Part 5 of CTA 2009 (loan relationships) as a non-trading credit, or
- (b) chargeable to corporation tax under Chapter 7 of Part 10 of that Act (annual payments not otherwise charged) or regulation 15 of the Unauthorised Unit Trusts (Tax) Regulations 2013[^c-73d4be66ab5eeb35ca7f7293797cc9e5] .

(4) Condition C is that the payment is made to a non-UK resident.

(5) Subsection (1) does not apply if—

- (a) the relevant foreign income is received in the United Kingdom, or
- (b) it is charged for the tax year in accordance with section 832 (relevant foreign income charged on remittance basis).
