# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 382

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 382. Contents of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter—

- (a) imposes a charge to income tax on dividends and other distributions of UK resident companies (see section 383),
- (b) treats dividends as paid in some circumstances (see sections 386 to 391), [^c-721c03bb26ec815a387403eb4c4fa394]...
- (c) makes special provision where the charge is in respect of shares awarded under a Schedule 2[^c-1259d871544f1377530b5c0386e6066d] share incentive plan (see sections 392 to 396), and[^c-bf4e3e9b5be2325106660eb42d83642c]
- (d) treats distributions as made in some circumstances (see section 396A).

(2) This Chapter also makes provision about [^c-96c47fb3c103dc2231bb994ad506d9a7]... tax being treated as paid and reliefs available in respect of certain distributions which applies whether or not the distributions are otherwise dealt with under this Chapter (see sections 399[^c-565ebfe76b0750178d40cfc007a6b887] to 401).

(3) For exemptions from the charge under this Chapter, see in particular—

- Chapter 3 of Part 6 (income from individual investment plans),
- Chapter 5 of that Part (venture capital trust dividends),
- section 770 (amounts applied by SIP trustees acquiring dividend shares or retained for reinvestment), and
- section 498 of ITEPA 2003 (no charge on shares ceasing to be subject to SIP in certain circumstances).

(4) In this Chapter “**dividends**” does not include income treated as arising under section 410 (stock dividends).
