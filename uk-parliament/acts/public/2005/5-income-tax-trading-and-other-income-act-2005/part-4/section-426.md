## 426. Annuity payments received after deduction of tax

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In accordance with section 848 of ITA 2007 a sum representing income tax deducted under section 901 of that Act[^c19742881] from an annuity payment within this Chapter is treated as income tax paid by the recipient [^c19742901]. . . .
