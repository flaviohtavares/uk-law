# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 372

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

## 372. Building society dividends[^key-100f5793f340462e2715bbaaba3196c1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any dividend paid by a building society is treated as interest for the purposes of this Act.

(2) In this section “**dividend**”includes any distribution (whether or not described as a dividend)[^c19742261] .
