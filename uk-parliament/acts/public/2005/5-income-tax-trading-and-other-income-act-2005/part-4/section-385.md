# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 385

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 385. Person liable

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The person liable for any tax charged under this Chapter is—

- (a) the person to whom the distribution is made or is treated as made (see Part 6 of ICTA and sections 386(3) , 389(3) and 396A[^key-ce1f182a9720bd9eb5f4da09da66ab7b] ), or
- (b) the person receiving or entitled to the distribution.

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to—

- section 393(4) (later charge where cash dividends retained in SIPs are paid over), and
- section 394(4) (distribution when dividend shares cease to be subject to SIP).
