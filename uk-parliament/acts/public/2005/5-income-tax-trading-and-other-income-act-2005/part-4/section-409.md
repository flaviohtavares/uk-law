# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 409

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 409. Charge to tax on stock dividend income

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Income tax is charged on stock dividend income.

(2) In this Chapter “**stock dividend income**” means the income that is treated as arising under section 410.
