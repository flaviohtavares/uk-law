# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 367

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 367. Priority between Chapters within Part 4[^c19268401]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any income, so far as it falls within Chapter 2 (interest) and Chapter 8 (profits from deeply discounted securities), is dealt with under Chapter 8.

(2) Any income, so far as it falls within Chapter 3 (dividends etc. from UK resident companies etc.) and another Chapter, is dealt with under Chapter 3 (but this is subject to subsection (3)).

(3) Any income, so far as it falls within—

- (a) Chapter 2 (interest) as a result of section 372 (building society dividends) , 378A (offshore fund distributions)[^c-22aaa92b5f7cd3e2aaf3f8191a8dcff4] or 379 ( payments by registered societies or certain co-operatives[^c-a02478dcf6882f5d10a3735e023d5905] ), and
- (b) Chapter 3 or Chapter 4 (or both)[^c-3ff3016ccde7f96684f849b0eabd834b],

is dealt with under Chapter 2.
