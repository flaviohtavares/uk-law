# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 460

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 460. Minor definitions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter “**share**”, in the case of a share in a company, means any share under which an entitlement to receive distributions may arise, but does not include a share in a building society.

(2) In this Chapter “**tax advantage**” has the meaning given by section 1139 of CTA 2010[^key-585d8812bd3367c1e8df5f7109a637c1].

(3) In this Chapter “**market value**” has the same meaning as in TCGA 1992 (see sections 272 to 274 of that Act), except as provided in section 450 [^c19743121]. . . (market value of strips etc.).
