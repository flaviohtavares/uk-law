## 459. Transfer of assets abroad

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if profits are taken to arise on the disposal of a deeply discounted security by a person resident [^c-c6a26ca9cce1494ac19b5c47e079276a]... outside the United Kingdom (“A”).

(2) For the purpose of determining whether a[^c-964347f56e1c408114bfd96f3aab0d11] UK resident individual is liable for income tax in respect of the profits, Chapter 2 of Part 13 of ITA 2007 (transfer of assets abroad) has[^c19743071] effect as if the profits, when arising, constituted income becoming payable to A.

(3) For this purpose it does not matter if A is not liable to income tax under this Chapter because of section 458 (non-UK resident trustees).
