# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 412

## 412. Cash equivalent of share capital

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-c2d60fac6d1c3eb3f2e8f4f5930bc347][^key-3e11ee044236d7b923900ad0b45c4b0d] The cash equivalent of share capital issued as mentioned in section 410(1)(a) is the amount of the cash dividend alternative (see section 414A(2))[^key-41654e0c4aba7613c612245536cd9da1].

(2)[^key-c2d60fac6d1c3eb3f2e8f4f5930bc347][^key-3e11ee044236d7b923900ad0b45c4b0d] But if the difference between the cash dividend alternative and the share capital's market value equals or exceeds 15% of that market value—

- (a) subsection (1) does not apply, and
- (b) the cash equivalent of the share capital is its market value.

(3) The cash equivalent of share capital issued as mentioned in section 410(1)(b)[^key-27ba6c82fd48baf3784c5cb998b82389] is its market value.

(4)[^key-c2d60fac6d1c3eb3f2e8f4f5930bc347][^key-3e11ee044236d7b923900ad0b45c4b0d] For the purposes of this section, market value is determined—

- (a) in the case of listed share capital, on the date of first dealing, and
- (b) in the case of other share capital, on the earliest date on which the company is required to issue it.

(5)[^key-c2d60fac6d1c3eb3f2e8f4f5930bc347][^key-3e11ee044236d7b923900ad0b45c4b0d] In this section—

- “**listed**” means listed in the Stock Exchange Daily Official List, and
- “**market value**” has the same meaning as in sections 272(1) and (3) and 273(3) of TCGA 1992.
