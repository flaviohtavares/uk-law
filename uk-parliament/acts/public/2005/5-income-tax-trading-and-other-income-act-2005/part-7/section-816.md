## 816. Alternative calculation of profits: trading income

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if the individual's qualifying care[^c-a0268db8fc4592b72ff46650e0741869] receipts for the tax year are the receipts of a trade.

(2) The profits of the trade for the tax year are—

- (a) the individual's total qualifying care[^c-5ebbb9c2bf46f62538cfca401532943e] receipts for the tax year, less
- (b) the individual's limit for the tax year.
