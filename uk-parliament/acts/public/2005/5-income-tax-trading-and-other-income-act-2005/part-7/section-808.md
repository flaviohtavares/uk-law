## 808. The individual's limit

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Chapter an individual's limit for a tax year is the total of—

- (a) the fixed amount for the tax year or, if section 809 or 810 applies, the individual's share of that amount, and
- (b) each amount per adult or[^key-c60d5ded68388be7ba890ac45079367f] child for the individual for the tax year (see section 811).

(2) For the purposes of this Chapter the fixed amount for a tax year is £20,440[^key-b400d31cf6370696ed7c295df788f832].
