# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 815

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

## 815. Alternative calculation of profits: introduction

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 816 and 817 (which provide for an alternative method of calculating profits) apply if—

- (a) an individual qualifies for qualifying care[^c-33e6b0e90b1a1fc68e1f32d4822842c1] relief for a tax year,
- (b) the individual's total qualifying care[^c-9e4f6f873c87ef911c40358f737127b9] receipts for the tax year exceed the individual's limit for the tax year,
- (c) sections 822 and 823 do not apply (accounting date for trade not 5th April), and
- (d) an election by the individual has effect to apply the alternative method of calculating profits for the tax year (see sections 818 and 819).
