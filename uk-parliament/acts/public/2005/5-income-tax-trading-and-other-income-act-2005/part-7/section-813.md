## 813. Full qualifying care relief: trading income[^key-31d4a070cd9f3bec9c91d9a87f51611f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if the individual's qualifying care[^key-0282daab86780dfa69962d3020373a26] receipts for the tax year would otherwise be brought into account in calculating the profits of a trade.

(2) The profits or losses of the trade for the tax year are treated as nil.
