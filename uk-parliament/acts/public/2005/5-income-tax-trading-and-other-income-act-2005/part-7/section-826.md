## 826. Excluded capital expenditure[^key-ee24af62f87b39761b4caacfc38ebb8b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Capital expenditure (“**excluded capital expenditure**”) does not constitute qualifying expenditure for the purposes of CAA 2001 if it is—

- (a) incurred by a relevant individual in a relevant chargeable period, and
- (b) incurred on the provision of plant or machinery wholly or partly for the provision of qualifying care[^key-850ed7d8389e15b4f30fa2bf97ad1fae] by the individual.
