# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 684

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 684. Income charged

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Tax is charged under this Chapter on the full amount of the annual payments arising in the tax year.

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to Part 8 (foreign income: special rules).

(3) The amount charged under this Chapter in the case of certain payments made by trustees in the exercise of a discretion is subject to section 494 of ITA 2007[^c19753731](grossing up of discretionary payments from trusts).
