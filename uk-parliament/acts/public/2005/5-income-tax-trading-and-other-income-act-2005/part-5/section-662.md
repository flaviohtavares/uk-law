# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 662

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

## 662. Basic amount of estate income: discretionary interests

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The basic amount of estate income relating to a person's discretionary interest in the whole or part of the residue of an estate for a tax year is the total of the payments made in the tax year in exercise of the discretion in favour of the person.
