# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 578

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 578. Contents of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter imposes charges to income tax under—

- (a) section 579 (royalties and other income from intellectual property),
- (b) section 583 (income from disposals of know-how), and
- (c) section 587 (income from sales of patent rights).

(2) For an exemption[^key-f9f29838f86c7582f4b0c31f5153c0ca] from the charge under section 579, see, in particular, section[^key-376566ff383ad1efd668ee9af0521c05] 727 (certain annual payments by individuals) [^key-4f371418943b837b3d4e0eef9982faaf]....

(3) This Chapter also provides for relief from income tax on patent income (see section 600).
