# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 686

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 686. Payments received after deduction of tax

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In accordance with section 848 of ITA 2007, a sum representing income tax deducted under Chapter 6 of Part 15 of that Act[^c19755391] from an annual payment within this Chapter is treated as income tax paid by the recipient [^c19755411]. . .

(2) See also section 494(3) of ITA 2007[^c19755421](sum treated as deducted from payments made under a discretionary trust treated as income tax paid by the person to whom the payment is made or the settlor).
