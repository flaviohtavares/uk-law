# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 689

## 689. Person liable[^c-c84463eb2d556cda74ff95f8139012fa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The person liable for any tax charged under this Chapter is the person receiving or entitled to the income.
