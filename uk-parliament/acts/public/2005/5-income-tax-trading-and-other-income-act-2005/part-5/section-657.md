# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 657

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 657. Income charged: foreign estates

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the case of a foreign estate, the charge to tax under section 649 is a charge[^c-3c31bed4987ff5503e2766b66e8ee550] on the full amount of estate income treated as arising in the tax year.

(2) That amount depends on whether the estate income arising in the tax year is paid from sums within section 680(4)[^c-59570ec89900707bf5075bc61a540fc3] (sums treated as bearing income tax).

(3) So far as the estate income is paid from such sums, that amount is the basic amount of that income for the tax year grossed up by reference to the applicable rate [^M_F_eb2b28df-50a1-434a-971d-e2b7e302cdce]... (see section 663).

(4) That gross amount is treated as having borne income tax at that rate.

(5) So far as the estate income is not paid from sums within section 680(4), the amount of estate income treated as arising in the tax year is the basic amount of that income for that year.
