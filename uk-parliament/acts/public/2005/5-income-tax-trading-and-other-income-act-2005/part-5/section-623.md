## 623. Calculation of income

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the purpose of calculating the liability to tax of an individual[^key-3033bb288f3f00f33d68e04ed8976409] charged under this Chapter.

(2) The same deductions and reliefs are allowed as would have been allowed if the amount treated as the individual's[^key-b75a8ad08424934a2b46c70486a32c5d] income as a result of this Chapter had been received by the individual as income.
