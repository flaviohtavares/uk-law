## 622. Person liable

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The person liable for any tax charged under this Chapter is the settlor , but this is subject to[^key-d20419760d4d3d5f1e6e9ae6fdec0ccd] section 643A (under which a close member of the settlor’s family may instead be liable)[^key-0833225e1d1a5fa348b6767025004803].
