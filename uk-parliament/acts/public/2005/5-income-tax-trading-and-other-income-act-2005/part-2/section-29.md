# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Interest

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purpose of calculating the profits of a trade, interest is an item of a revenue nature, whatever the nature of the loan.
