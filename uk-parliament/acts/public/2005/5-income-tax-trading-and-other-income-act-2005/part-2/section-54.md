## 54. Penalties , interest and (affected by repeal, see note) VAT (affected by repeal, see note) surcharges (affected by repeal, see note) and interest[^key-809b4e03b1766726db1f0da3ee3ccdd2][^key-809b4e03b1766726db1f0da3ee3ccdd2][^key-809b4e03b1766726db1f0da3ee3ccdd2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In calculating the profits of a trade, no deduction is allowed for any penalty or interest mentioned in the first column of the following table.

(2) This is the table—

| *Penalty or interest* | *Description of tax, levy or duty* |
| --- | --- |
| Interest under any provision of Part 9 of TMA 1970 | Income tax, capital gains tax and corporation tax |
| Interest under section 101 of[^key-2dbf408b0dfb56385a5cb98316a96da9] FA 2009 in connection with sums required to be deducted under section 61 of FA 2004 (construction industry) |  |
| Penalty under any of sections 60 to 70 of VATA 1994 | Value added tax |
| Interest under section 101 of FA 2009 in respect of an amount of value added tax[^key-59d42b48cab6cf7604638b615d9994ab] |  |
| Penalty under any of sections 8 to 11 of FA 1994 | Excise duties |
| Penalty under any of paragraphs 12 to 19 of Schedule 7 to FA 1994 | Insurance premium tax |
| Interest under section 60(8) of[^key-9f183674b521ebb08a230c3b3669c6d1] FA 1994 or paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to FA 1994 |  |
| Penalty under any provision of Part 5 of Schedule 5 to FA 1996 | Landfill tax |
| Interest under section 56(5) of, or paragraph 26 or 27 of Schedule 5 to,[^key-1220376b787aa8402063e0fc2ccb909f] FA 1996 |  |
| Penalty under any provision of Schedule 6 to FA 2000 | Climate change levy |
| Interest under any of paragraphs 70, 81 to 85 , 109 and 123(6)[^key-880319ba1cda2e41f31c30a64ebbc5c1] of that Schedule |  |
| Penalty under any provision of Part 2 of FA 2001 | Aggregates levy |
| Interest under section 42(6) of, or[^key-bbfea7cc771b500eb950733607b40c46] any of paragraphs 5 to 9 of Schedule 5 to, paragraph 6 of Schedule 8 to and paragraph 5 of Schedule 10 to,[^key-fce9c2d045d449dab4bacdd2ca9a56de] FA 2001 |  |
| Penalty under section 25 or 26 of FA 2003 | Customs duties[^key-bef132ec08962627cdb4e40aaa350046] |
| Penalty under any provision of Part 4 of FA 2003 | Stamp duty land tax |
| Interest under any provision of that Part |  |
| Penalty under Schedule 24 to[^key-76e0adc95f111558124344781b89ad68] FA 2007 | Various taxes and excise duties |
| Penalty under Schedule 41 to FA 2008[^key-35298ac4afd9933689bd53cb46c9d513] | Various taxes and excise duties |
| Penalty under Schedule 16 to F([^key-e3164671e275edbc2f4452356eb18b0a]No. 2)A 2017 | Various taxes |

(3) In calculating the profits of a trade, no deduction is allowed for any surcharge under section 59 of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ce0cf75848dd26825f6ffe2b18f9af09] VATA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ce0cf75848dd26825f6ffe2b18f9af09] 1994.
