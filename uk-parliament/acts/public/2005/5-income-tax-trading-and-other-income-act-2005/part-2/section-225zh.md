# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 225ZH

## 225ZH. Chapter not to apply where cash basis used[^key-780b60c3c5dc94e80c459fb99e74941d][^key-63626370b916f5445aaacd1e1f80434f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Chapter applies in calculating the profits of a trade on the cash basis.
