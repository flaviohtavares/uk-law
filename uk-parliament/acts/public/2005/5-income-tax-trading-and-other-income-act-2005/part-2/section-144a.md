# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 144A

## 144A. Chapter not to apply where cash basis used[^c-b8a446f3fa4b572239ef9d556ffa7e5d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Chapter applies in calculating the profits of a trade on the cash basis.
