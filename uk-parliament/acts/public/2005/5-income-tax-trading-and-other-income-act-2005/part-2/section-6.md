# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Territorial scope of charge to tax

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Profits of a trade arising to a UK resident are chargeable to tax under this Chapter wherever the trade is carried on.

(1A)[^key-f0e7b9854ef02366bd72d95d0cd4ccb1] Profits of a trade of dealing in or developing UK land arising to a non-UK resident are chargeable to tax under this Chapter wherever the trade is carried on.

(2) Profits of a trade other than a trade of dealing in or developing[^key-eaaf6a3ca6f13cca4ef7c4199137d077] UK land arising to a non-UK resident are chargeable to tax under this Chapter only if they arise—

- (a) from a trade carried on wholly in the United Kingdom, or
- (b) in the case of a trade carried on partly in the United Kingdom and partly elsewhere, from the part of the trade carried on in the United Kingdom.

(2A)[^key-b04c14318d64659937b97276ca106382] If the tax year is a split year as respects a UK resident individual, this section has effect as if, for the overseas part of that year, the individual were non-UK resident.

(3) This section applies to professions and vocations as it applies to trades.
