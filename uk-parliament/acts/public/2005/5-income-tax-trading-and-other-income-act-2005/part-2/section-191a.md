# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 191A

## 191A. Chapter not to apply where cash basis used[^key-ca900047a09a2ceceb975d7cadb1e957]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Chapter applies in calculating the profits of a trade on the cash basis.
