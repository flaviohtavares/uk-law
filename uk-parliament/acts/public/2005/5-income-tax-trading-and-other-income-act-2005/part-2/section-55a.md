## 55A. Expenditure on integral features[^c-453b5a179fe0632cb166e60177d04167]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-ed9238400ccdd3c429bb9f3f7e307a11] Section 33A(3) of CAA 2001 provides that no deduction is allowed in respect of certain expenditure on an integral feature of a building or structure (within the meaning of that section).

(2)[^c-dd0161f47a065aec1ea344e9726a616c] But section 33A(3) of CAA 2001 does not apply in calculating the profits of a trade on the cash basis.
