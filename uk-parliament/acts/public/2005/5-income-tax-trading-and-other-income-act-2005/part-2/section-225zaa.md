# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 225ZAA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

## 225ZAA. Chapter not to apply where cash basis used[^c-adb0bb5d3e20b5be3de4b41bd6666162][^c-2f6ef58ac1b32d27aaebea07e3c91b2e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Chapter applies in calculating the profits of a trade on the cash basis.
