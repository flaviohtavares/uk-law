# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 227A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 227A. Application of Chapter where cash basis used[^c-4c6d49941a89239117076e06293af6a0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies if—

- (a)[^c-1be13c2230af0d99f6ded0f88a43a6c3] the cash basis does apply in relation to a trade for a tax year but does not apply in relation to the trade for the following tax year.
- (b)[^c-dcb9488a0ada5ad2af87bae276595cb7] the cash basis does not apply in relation to a trade for a tax year but does apply in relation to the trade for the following tax year.

(2) But this Chapter does not apply to income which is charged in accordance with section 832.

(3)[^c-5c8b929510b38e83c57e1f6aa67ee8f3] This section is subject to section 227C (application of Chapter where section 227B applies).
