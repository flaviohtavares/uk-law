# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

## 25. Generally accepted accounting practice

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The profits of a trade to which the cash basis does not apply[^c-a7e642d7a3a780ffcae001018d198340] must be calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting practice, subject to any adjustment required or authorised by law in calculating profits for income tax purposes.

(2) This does not—

- (a) require a person to comply with the requirements of the Companies Act 2006 or subordinate legislation made under that Act[^c-76f80305f207067ed521c5fa0793bd42] except as to the basis of calculation, or
- (b) impose any requirements as to audit or disclosure.

(3)[^c-8c53f73e79c283486aaa48425c0cf32a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) This section does not affect provisions of the Income Tax Acts relating to the calculation of the profits of Lloyd's underwriters.
