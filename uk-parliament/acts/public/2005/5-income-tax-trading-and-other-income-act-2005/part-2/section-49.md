## 49. Car ... hire: supplementary[^key-cf9da4748768384955ebc176a89c57cb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 48 “**car [^key-8db2ff1c4e6731080f17abfd377ea1ed]...**” means a mechanically propelled road vehicle other than [^key-efd4889645e8c531c8faf3fcb6a61617]...—

- (za)[^key-f48c4e8ca8c89ae4c5180d0df4706b0d] a motor cycle (within the meaning of section 185(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1988),
- (a) a vehicle[^key-e17ba5a4cfb4be7356b4d5c665c2376f] of a construction primarily suited for the conveyance of goods or burden of any description, or
- (b) a vehicle[^key-ff0418e9256390db78a48f12fb7fcb72] of a type not commonly used as a private vehicle and unsuitable for such use.

(1A)[^key-eb3f92dfa32cdc81ec8fecedc3da2f51] In section 48—

- “**a car that has low CO2 emissions**” has the same meaning as in section 104AA of CAA 2001 (special rate expenditure: main rate car);
- “**electrically propelled**” has the meaning given in section 268B of that Act.

(2) In section 48 “**a qualifying hire car [^key-3c69be865003f52dd748474ba8d7c3cb]...**” means a car [^key-3c69be865003f52dd748474ba8d7c3cb]... which—

- (a) is hired under a hire-purchase agreement [^key-429c7760147ca543789de6e32654d670]... under which there is no option to purchase,
- (b) is hired under a hire-purchase agreement under which there is an option to purchase exercisable on the payment of a sum equal to not more than 1% of the retail price of the car [^key-3c69be865003f52dd748474ba8d7c3cb]... when new, or
- (c)[^key-4a610718921ab00fbfc209eb5216aeaa] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^key-c026ef403a2fc3fdc7f3a7857cd55ced] is leased under a long-funding lease (within the meaning of section 70G of CAA 2001).

(3)[^key-de4d0df532a8c69f26bc41544736514c] For this purpose “**hire-purchase agreement**” has the meaning given by section 998A of ITA 2007.

(6) In this section [^key-ee7aa74f31ec99e5eec9aaabb14f0def]... “**new**” means unused and not second-hand.
