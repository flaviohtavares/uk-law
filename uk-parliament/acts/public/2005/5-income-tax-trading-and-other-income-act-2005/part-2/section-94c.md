# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 94C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94C. Provisions not applicable to certain firms[^c-69d7e1ea0086cad3ff3671c5bb07a6a8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of this Chapter do not apply in calculating the profits of a trade carried on by a firm for a period if one or more of the persons who have been partners in the firm at any time during the period was not an individual at that time.
