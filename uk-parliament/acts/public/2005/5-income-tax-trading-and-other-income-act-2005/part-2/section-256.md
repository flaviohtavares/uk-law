## 256. Treatment of post-cessation receipts

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) an individual has permanently ceased to carry on a trade, and
- (b) the income arising to the individual from the trade was [^c19740471]. . . relevant UK earnings within section 189(2)(b) of FA 2004.

(2) Any post-cessation receipts arising to the individual from the trade are similarly [^c19740471]. . . relevant UK earnings.
