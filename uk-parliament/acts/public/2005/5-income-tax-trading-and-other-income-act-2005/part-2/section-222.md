# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 222

## 222. Circumstances in which claim for two-year averaging may be made[^c-f9fde68f9ac9fc282e1c080db0af02a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An averaging claim may be made under this section[^c-b6232b5d24b4f3b2d1130e56c0d0aa80] in relation to two consecutive tax years in which a taxpayer is or has been carrying on the qualifying trade, profession or vocation if—

- (a) the relevant profits of one of the tax years are less than 75% of the relevant profits of the other tax year, or
- (b) the relevant profits of one (but not both) of the tax years are nil.

(2)[^c-eed8794d1fef3b4c5df4e2b85584dedc] The earlier of the two years to which an averaging claim under this section relates may be a tax year in relation to which an averaging claim under this section or section 222A has already been made.

(3) An averaging claim may not be made under this section[^c-320a67227d6fa1d8763ecbc73b2d057a] in relation to a tax year if an averaging claim has already been made under this section or section 222A[^c-da82fb75fe51e0d2a5e81e209f79f9d6] in relation to a later tax year in respect of the trade, profession or vocation.

(4) An averaging claim may not be made under this section[^c-c751b09a74abaeee7064983759a2a179] in relation to the tax year in which—

- (a) the taxpayer starts, or permanently ceases, to carry on the trade, profession or vocation, or
- (b) in the case of a trade, profession or vocation within section 221(2)(c), it begins or ceases to be a qualifying trade, profession or vocation.

(5) An averaging claim under this section[^c-5e2d414e2da5fd19675ac6edaead5332] must be made on or before the first anniversary of the normal self-assessment filing date for the second of the tax years to which the claim relates.

(6) But see section 225(4) (extended time limit if profits adjusted for some other reason).
