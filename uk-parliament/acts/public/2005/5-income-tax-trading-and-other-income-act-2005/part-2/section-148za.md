# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 148ZA

## 148ZA. Chapter not to apply where cash basis used[^key-1c04340cf236d927625b4545c1331fc2][^c19154671]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Chapter applies in calculating the profits of a trade on the cash basis.
