# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 259

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

## 259. Meaning of “statutory insolvency arrangement”[^c19115071]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “**statutory insolvency arrangement**” means—

- (a) a voluntary arrangement that has taken effect under or as a result of the Insolvency Act 1986, the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 or schedule 4 to the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016[^key-74d5dc89d7aa61fe5604e7aeb48f03f1],
- (b)[^key-a87515a4bf92cb67ae95f63f37317b93] a compromise or arrangement that has taken effect under Part 26 or 26A[^key-c997d1481c84a3d08282a81eb3edefb1] of the Companies Act 2006,
- (c) any arrangement or compromise of a kind corresponding to any of those mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b) that has taken effect under or by virtue of the law of a country or territory outside the United Kingdom.
