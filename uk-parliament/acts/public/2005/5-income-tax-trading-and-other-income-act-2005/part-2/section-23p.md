# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 23P

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23P. Carried interest: anti-avoidance[^c-3b3b89fd758a500a76833fb17c4c24b0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In determining whether section 23I applies in relation to an individual, no regard is to be had to any arrangements the main purpose, or one of the main purposes, of which is to secure that that section does not to any extent apply in relation to—

- (a) the individual, or
- (b) the individual and one or more other individuals.

(2) In determining whether an individual falls within paragraph (a) of section 23I(1), no regard is to be had to any arrangements the main purpose, or one of the main purposes, of which is to secure that the individual falls within that paragraph.
