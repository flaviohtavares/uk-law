# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 130A

## 130A. Chapter not to apply where cash basis used[^key-4e03294d44fef5b8f91596c9fadc9134]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Chapter applies in calculating the profits of a trade on the cash basis.
