# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 114

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Initial cost of herd and value of herd

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In calculating the profits of the trade, no deduction is allowed for the initial cost of the herd.

(2) In calculating the profits of the trade, the value of the herd is not brought into account.
