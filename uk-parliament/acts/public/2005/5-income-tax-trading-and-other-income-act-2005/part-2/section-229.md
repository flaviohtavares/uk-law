# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 229

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 229. Income charged

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Tax is charged under this Chapter on the full amount of any adjustment income arising in the tax year.

(2) This is subject to—

- (a) sections 237 to 239B[^c-27c4df47353929313498494333388cf8] (which provide for spreading of adjustment income), and
- (b) Part 8 (foreign income: special rules).
