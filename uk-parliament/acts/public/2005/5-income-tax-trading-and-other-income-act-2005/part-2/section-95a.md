> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

## 95A. Application of Chapter to the cash basis[^key-51ef5be59c216c7a0d1522a1f9578443]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-1a34af6df0c82d1abd816e9be6de03f1] For rules about receipts that apply only for the purpose of calculating profits on the cash basis, see the following—

- [^key-a7c5976a4e575a9df665deaa640bc8be]...
- section 97A (cash basis: value of trading stock on cessation of trade),
- section 97B (cash basis: value of work in progress on cessation of profession or vocation).

(2)[^key-76d89b1fb4818c3ec2390b0704eb7d35] Section 96A makes provision about capital receipts in certain cases where the profits of a trade are calculated on the cash basis or have previously been calculated on the cash basis (and see also section 96B).
