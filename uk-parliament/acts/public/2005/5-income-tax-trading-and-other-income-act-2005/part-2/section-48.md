## 48. Car ... hire[^key-ca31256aaf9b68ebaae24f51c4722d23]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if, in calculating the profits of a trade, a deduction is allowed for expenses incurred on the hiring of a car which is not—[^key-e50f76ca5c96a145b3f409105c5ff30d]

- (a) a car that is first registered before 1 March 2001,
- (b) a car that has low CO<sub>2</sub> emissions,
- (c) a car that is electrically propelled, or
- (d) a qualifying hire car.

(2) The amount of the deduction which would otherwise be allowable is reduced by 15%[^key-6d48fb576ff1f1431942a5402b90bbfb].

(3) Subsection (4) applies if a deduction is reduced as a result of subsection (2), or a corresponding provision,[^key-8d334c7fab9cd289665026ff47337421] and subsequently—

- (a) there is a rebate (however described) of the hire charges, or
- (b) a debt in respect of any of the hire charges is released otherwise than as part of a statutory insolvency arrangement.

(4) The amount that, as a result of the rebate or release—

- (a) is brought into account as a receipt of the trade [^key-15c599c358319828b58796ad1f652178]..., or
- (b) is treated as a post-cessation receipt under section 249 (debts released after cessation),

is reduced by 15%[^key-5de887c76a958cbd906ea62bfa8e9d44] .

(4A)[^key-7bc51f688727a4d659856c97d28fbdb0] In this section “**corresponding provision**” means—

- (a) section 56(2) of CTA 2009 (car [^key-200a3892941d91d7301a4c251381334c]... hire: trade profits and property income), or[^key-3cf4049336577cd41fe055928020efac]
- (b) section 1251(2) of CTA 2009 (car [^key-200a3892941d91d7301a4c251381334c]... hire: expenses of management), including as applied by section 82(4) of FA 2012.[^key-4d42022c1c28c831204c79984ed81c7c] [^key-74dfa6ae6785fa6129282efa45d983dd]...
- (c)[^key-74dfa6ae6785fa6129282efa45d983dd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-f9c22dfc26fa6123e7fd932d638aa2b2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
