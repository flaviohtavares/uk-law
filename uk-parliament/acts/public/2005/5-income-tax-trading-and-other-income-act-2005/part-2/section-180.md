# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 180

## 180. Cost to buyer of stock valued on sale basis of valuation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the purpose of calculating the profits of the trade, profession or vocation[^c-4bb689c93e7d392675dc2a4f0f98c540] carried on by the buyer of trading stock.

(2) If the value of the stock is determined in accordance with—

- (a) section 175(3) or sections 176 to 178 (sale basis of valuation), or
- (b) section 164(3) or sections 165 to 167 of CTA 2009[^c-a0d7dffc92d270e631ff244958c4bc36] (corresponding corporation tax rules),

the cost of the stock to the buyer is taken to be the value as so determined.
