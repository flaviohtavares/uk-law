# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 240B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

## 240B. “Entering the cash basis”[^key-0bc9184a8f38bc9b05cf24d7792ebfd4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Chapter a person carrying on a trade enters the cash basis for a tax year if—

- (a) the cash basis applies[^key-7d90ae0062f596b87f9fac0f8e2d1e2c] in relation to the trade for the tax year, and
- (b) immediately before the beginning of [^key-a3ef19d2d7db84f865bd060688949353]... the tax year, the cash basis does not apply[^key-e3793e0e56715f6135d90ce1139502e4] in relation to the trade.
