# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 331

## 331. Income charged

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-af03088fec6d2debb6a52e51ccb77f19] Tax is charged under this Chapter on the full amount of any adjustment income arising in the tax year.

(2)[^key-fdae36015c0123c752ca7739d06f0359] This is subject to section 334A (spreading on leaving cash basis and related election).
