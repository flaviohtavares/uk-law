# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 281A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 281A. Sums to which sections 277 to 281 do not apply[^key-e3b379e29528b522a3c07f02a5386dcb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if a grant of a lease constitutes a disposal of an asset for the purposes of section 809BZA(2)(b) or 809BZF(2)(a) of ITA 2007 (disposals under finance arrangements).

(2) Sections 277 to 281 do not apply in relation to a premium paid in respect of the grant.
