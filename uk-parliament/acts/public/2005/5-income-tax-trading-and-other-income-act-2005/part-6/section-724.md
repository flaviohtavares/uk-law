## 724. Regulations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs[^c19150151] may by regulations—

- (a) prescribe the procedure to be used in giving effect to sections 717 to 722[^key-bc4114e42d1e3babe474aff57fe366a9] and this section where no provision is made in those provisions,
- (b) apply any provision of the Income Tax Acts, with or without modifications, for the purposes of those provisions or the regulations,
- (c) prescribe tables of mortality for the purposes of sections 720(4) and 721(4).
