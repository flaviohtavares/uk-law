# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 706

## 706. Withdrawal and variation of certifications and connected requirements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commissioners[^key-75bd7b01840e5eaa9a31760743d15f40] may—

- (a) withdraw the requirements specified under section 705(1)(b) for any description of arrangements and any certification made by reference to those requirements, or
- (b) vary those requirements and withdraw any certification made by reference to them.

(2) The withdrawal, or variation and withdrawal, is only effective if the Commissioners[^key-b7101afe5565de32e7ae97cd4c8617b4] —

- (a) specify the date on which it is to take effect, and
- (b) give notice of it [^key-0646c2b4ad032329ea62fe6ce2587cba]... at least 15 days[^key-3af0a15b0dbdc1f5b5503eaf0b5e7c14] before that date to the provider authorised under section 707 to enter into contracts under the arrangement concerned.

(3) The withdrawal, or variation and withdrawal, does not affect

- (a)[^key-e1161e5d5aac4484c8b83ddc9477c321] the operation of the arrangement concerned before that date,
- (b) contracts made under that arrangement before that date, or
- (c) where the notice so provides, contracts which are of a description specified in the notice and are made under that arrangement after that date.
