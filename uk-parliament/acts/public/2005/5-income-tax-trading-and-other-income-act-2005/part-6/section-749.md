## 749. Repayment interest, and interest paid under repayment supplements[^c-69200e843fbbdff7ba0cf957e8f479fc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No liability to income tax arises in respect of interest paid under—

- (a) section 824 of ICTA (repayment supplements: individuals and others), [^c-fd96c083f1bc137f845090ed8802d6fb]...
- (b) section 283 of TCGA 1992 (repayment supplements), or[^c-cb7d16ecce1306f044bb30b28c27c419]
- (c) section 102 of FA 2009 (repayment interest).
