## 708. Withdrawal and variation of authorisations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commissioners[^key-0798a9c94c155ca3e7076b62a4ad0c6e] may withdraw the authorisation of a provider or vary it by imposing, varying or removing conditions.

(2) The withdrawal or variation is only effective if the Commissioners[^key-168b2dc49a4d21f93432ba2c86e4c3c1] —

- (a) specify the date on which it is to take effect, and
- (b) except in the case of a variation removing all conditions, give notice of it [^key-ce5556d29a931421c5f0a27bb50c8f0f]... to the provider at least 28 days before that date.

(3) The withdrawal or variation does not affect contracts made before that date.

(4) The fact that a provider has had its authorisation withdrawn or varied does not affect the later exercise by the Commissioners of their[^key-eba378c8933737b22750d7c17aa8f12e] powers under section 707 or this section as respects the provider.
