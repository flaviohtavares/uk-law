# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 780

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5

## 780. Disabled person's vehicle maintenance grant

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No liability to income tax arises in respect of a disabled person's vehicle maintenance grant.

(2) For this purpose a “**disabled person's vehicle maintenance grant**” means a grant to any person owning a vehicle that is made under—

- (a)[^c19124331] paragraph 10 of Schedule 1 to the National Health Service Act 2006 or paragraph 10 of Schedule 1 to National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006
- (b) section 46(3) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 (c. 29), or
- (c) Article 30 of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 (S.I. 1972/1265 (N.I. 14)).
