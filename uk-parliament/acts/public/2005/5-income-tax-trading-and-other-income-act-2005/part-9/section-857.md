# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 857

## 857. Partners to whom the remittance basis applies[^c-b782c0172ad18922b41a9cacb3af5ea4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) a firm carries on a trade wholly or partly outside the United Kingdom,
- (b) the control and management of the trade is outside the United Kingdom, and
- (c)[^c-e92440de4acf4200235531fbe5e95465] section 809B, 809D or 809E of ITA 2007 (remittance basis) applies to a partner for a tax year.

(2) The partner's share of the profits of the trade arising in the United Kingdom is determined in accordance with sections 849 to 851[^c-80330ac381a84845bdd898fda2a2a2d2].

(3) The partner's share of the profits of the trade arising outside the United Kingdom is treated as relevant foreign income [^c-101cde6aa94778e914031ddd28559d1b]....
