# Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005, section 860

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 860. Adjustment income

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A change in the persons carrying on a trade from one period of account to the next does not prevent Chapter 17 of Part 2 (adjustment income) applying in relation to the trade so long as a person carrying on the trade immediately before the change continues to carry on the trade immediately after the change.

(1A)[^c-0b1b66cabe7d84ac02c31d0813224cfe] A change in the persons carrying on a property business from one period of account to the next does not prevent Chapter 7 of Part 3 (adjustment income) applying in relation to the property business so long as a person carrying on the property business immediately before the change continues to carry on the property business immediately after the change.

(2) A change in the persons carrying on a trade does not constitute the permanent cessation of the trade for the purposes of Chapter 17 of Part 2 so long as a person carrying on the trade immediately before the change continues to carry on the trade immediately after the change.

(3) In the case of a trade or property business[^c-dee8250abe5aaa4d3e764147ebd6e77c] carried on by a firm the amount of any adjustment under Chapter 17 of Part 2 , or Chapter 7 of Part 3,[^c-50a9b36aad45c68e99e163c8846049d8] is calculated as if the firm were a UK resident individual.

(4) Each partner's share of any amount of adjustment income is determined according to the firm's profit-sharing arrangements for the 12 months ending immediately before the date on which the new basis was adopted.

(5) Any election under Chapter 17 of Part 2 , or under section 239B as applied to property businesses by section 334A,[^c-8a680fe738413ce5a308be4ef824faab] must be made jointly by all the persons who have been members of the firm in that 12 month period.

(6) For the purposes of this section—

- (a) “**adjustment income**” and “**change of basis**” have the same meaning as in Chapter 17 of Part 2, or Chapter 7 of Part 3 (as the case requires)[^c-f9d6157825a605bf77a2f3044ad647af]
- (b) “**profit-sharing arrangements**” means the rights of the partners to share in the profits of the trade or property business (as the case requires)[^c-8cdd9a5beae542332bfa366c12175879], and
- (c) references to the date on which a new basis was adopted are to the first day of the first period of account for which the new basis was adopted.

(7) Sections 849 to 851[^c-a5a1c2415f976211a6e0cb8e957c3f62] do not apply so far as this section applies.
