# Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005, section 161

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/15

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 161. Abolition of Royal Parks Constabulary

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-90c34dd977b2fe0f4d1404d33bc3a6fe] The Royal Parks Constabulary is abolished.

(2)[^key-c730125089d1c6e8d632776e82f14ab6][^key-a1d7acca266142a79bbef02a3e704e54] Every relevant person shall cease to be a park constable on the appointed day.

(3)[^key-c730125089d1c6e8d632776e82f14ab6][^key-a1d7acca266142a79bbef02a3e704e54] Subsection (2) is not to be taken as terminating the Crown employment of any relevant person.

(4)[^key-c730125089d1c6e8d632776e82f14ab6][^key-a1d7acca266142a79bbef02a3e704e54] In this section, section 162 and Schedule 13—

- “**appointed day**” means such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint for the purposes of this section,
- “**Crown employment**” has the same meaning as in the Employment Rights Act 1996 (c. 18), and
- “**relevant person**” means a person who immediately before the appointed day is serving as a park constable with the Royal Parks Constabulary.

(5)[^key-c730125089d1c6e8d632776e82f14ab6][^key-52526b1601639c9c22627078a6738a50] Schedule 13 (which provides for transfers to the Metropolitan Police Authority and makes amendments) has effect.
