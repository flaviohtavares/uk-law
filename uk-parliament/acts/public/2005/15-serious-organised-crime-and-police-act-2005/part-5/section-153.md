## 153. Disclosure of information about insurance status of vehicles[^c-823ec6eaf0646641400dcb170152f6a4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision for and in connection with requiring MIIC to make available relevant vehicle insurance information to the Secretary of State for processing[^c-49085bcae84b4296929096df3a01ee9a] with a view to making the processed information available for use by constables.

(2) “**Relevant vehicle insurance information**” means information relating to vehicles the use of which has been (but no longer is) insured under a policy of insurance [^c-efc7a3b98f1963d28f913fe3ba29d04a]... complying with the requirements of Part 6 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 (c. 52).

(3) The regulations may in particular—

- (a) require all relevant vehicle insurance information or any particular description of such information to be made available to the Secretary of State[^c-319e6f980d650a3b144c7bb24c737158],
- (b) determine the purposes for which information processed from such information by the Secretary of State may be made available for use by constables, and
- (c) determine the circumstances in which any of the processed information which has been made available for use by constables may be further disclosed by them.

(4) In this section—

- “**information**” means information held in any form,
- “**MIIC**” means the Motor Insurers' Information Centre (a company limited by guarantee and incorporated under the Companies Act 1985 (c. 6) on 8th December 1998), and
