## 74 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Assistance by defendant: review of sentence[^c-27580857fdd1afa744dace70136106ef][^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] This section applies if *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] in Northern Ireland[^c-52ab53ff00cb0a0988376d519559baef] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] the Crown Court has passed a sentence on a person in respect of an offence, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] the person falls within subsection (2). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] A person falls within this subsection if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] he receives a discounted sentence in consequence of his having offered in pursuance of a written agreement to give assistance to the prosecutor or investigator of an offence but he knowingly fails to any extent to give assistance in accordance with the agreement; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] he receives a discounted sentence in consequence of his having offered in pursuance of a written agreement to give assistance to the prosecutor or investigator of an offence and, having given the assistance in accordance with the agreement, in pursuance of another written agreement gives or offers to give further assistance; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] he receives a sentence which is not discounted but in pursuance of a written agreement he subsequently gives or offers to give assistance to the prosecutor or investigator of an offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] A specified prosecutor may at any time refer the case back to the court by which the sentence was passed if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] the person is still serving his sentence, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] the specified prosecutor thinks it is in the interests of justice to do so. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] A case so referred must, if possible, be heard by the judge who passed the sentence to which the referral relates. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] If the court is satisfied that a person who falls within subsection (2)(a) knowingly failed to give the assistance it may substitute for the sentence to which the referral relates such greater sentence (not exceeding that which it would have passed but for the agreement to give assistance) as it thinks appropriate. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] In a case of a person who falls within subsection (2)(b) or (c) the court may— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] take into account the extent and nature of the assistance given or offered; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] substitute for the sentence to which the referral relates such lesser sentence as it thinks appropriate. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] Any part of the sentence to which the referral relates which the person has already served must be taken into account in determining when a greater or lesser sentence imposed by subsection (5) or (6) has been served. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(8 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] A person in respect of whom a reference is made under this section and the specified prosecutor may with the leave of the Court of Appeal appeal to the Court of Appeal against the decision of the Crown Court. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(9 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4fdecb19dd0fe5a9a7200f31135da6d2][^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(10 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] A discounted sentence is a sentence passed in pursuance of section 73 or subsection (6) above. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(11 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] References— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] to a written agreement are to an agreement made in writing with a specified prosecutor; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] to a specified prosecutor must be construed in accordance with section 71. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(12 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] In relation to any proceedings under this section, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2a6e34a40a78c99d6576000b6b8e66e2][^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] [^c-802c504154cd02d078ce41418324c03e]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] the Department of Justice in Northern Ireland may, in relation to proceedings in Northern Ireland, make an order containing provision corresponding to any provision in the Criminal Appeal (Northern Ireland) Act 1980 (subject to any specified modifications). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(13 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] A person does not fall within subsection (2) if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] he was convicted of an offence for which the sentence is fixed by law, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] he did not plead guilty to the offence for which he was sentenced. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(14 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ededbc97e6d8f47de32542f17856f19f][^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
