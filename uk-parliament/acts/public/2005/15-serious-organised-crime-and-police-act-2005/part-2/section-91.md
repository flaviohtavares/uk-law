# Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005, section 91

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Transitional provision[^c-4b5b12c624e1e9d0fc6f8e600323082a][^c-d9e5adba43736c71931becbc70028864]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] This section applies to arrangements which were, at any time before the commencement of section 82, made by a protection provider, or any person acting with his authority, for the purpose of protecting a person of a description specified in Schedule 5. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] If the following three conditions are satisfied, the arrangements are to be treated as having been made by the protection provider under section 82(1). *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] The first condition is that the protection provider could have made the arrangements under section 82(1) had it been in force at the time when the arrangements were made. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(4 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] The second condition is that the arrangements were in operation immediately before the commencement of section 82. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(5 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] The third condition is that the protection provider determines that it is appropriate to treat the arrangements as having been made under section 82(1). *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(6 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] A determination under subsection (5) may be made at any time before the end of the period of six months beginning with the day on which section 82 comes into force. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(7 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] A protection provider must make a record of a determination under subsection (5). *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(8 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] Subsection (9) applies if— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] at any time before the commencement of section 82, arrangements were made by a person specified in subsection (11), or any person acting with the authority of such a person, for the purpose of protecting a person of a description specified in Schedule 5, and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] functions in relation to the arrangements are, at any time before the end of the period of six months mentioned in subsection (6), exercisable by a protection provider. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(9 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] The provision made by subsections (1) to (7) applies in relation to the arrangements as if they had been made by the protection provider. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(10 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] Accordingly, if the three conditions mentioned in subsections (3) to (5) are satisfied in relation to the arrangements, they are to be treated, by virtue of subsection (2), as having been made by the protection provider under section 82(1). *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(11 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] The persons specified in this subsection are— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] the Director General of the National Criminal Intelligence Service; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] the Director General of the National Crime Squad; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] any of the Commissioners of Her Majesty's Customs and Excise. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]
