# Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005, section 95

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Enforcement of overseas forfeiture orders[^c-4eda3473a182c29257b5e28afe4850db]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 9 of the Criminal Justice (International Co-operation) Act 1990 (c. 5) (enforcement of overseas forfeiture orders), for subsection (6) (offences to which section applies) substitute—

> (6) This section applies to any offence that corresponds to or is similar to—
>
> - (a) an offence under the law of England and Wales;
> - (b) an offence under the law of Scotland; or
> - (c) an offence under the law of Northern Ireland.
