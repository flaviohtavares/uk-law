## 73 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Assistance by defendant: reduction in sentence[^key-9488508d3f5863c9f02ea17a1f9f2f23][^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] This section applies *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] in Northern Ireland[^key-90b4589d95e812355b149426d28d665f] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] if a defendant— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] following a plea of guilty is either convicted of an offence in proceedings in the Crown Court or is committed to the Crown Court for sentence, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] has, pursuant to a written agreement made with a specified prosecutor, assisted or offered to assist the investigator or prosecutor in relation to that or any other offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] In determining what sentence to pass on the defendant the court may take into account the extent and nature of the assistance given or offered. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] If the court passes a sentence which is less than it would have passed but for the assistance given or offered, it must state in open court— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] that it has passed a lesser sentence than it would otherwise have passed, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] what the greater sentence would have been. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] Subsection (3) does not apply if the court thinks that it would not be in the public interest to disclose that the sentence has been discounted; but in such a case the court must give written notice of the matters specified in paragraphs (a) and (b) of subsection (3) to both the prosecutor and the defendant. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] Nothing in any enactment which— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] requires that a minimum sentence is passed in respect of any offence or an offence of any description or by reference to the circumstances of any offender (whether or not the enactment also permits the court to pass a lesser sentence in particular circumstances), or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] in the case of a sentence which is fixed by law, requires the court to take into account certain matters for the purposes of making an order which determines or has the effect of determining the minimum period of imprisonment which the offender must serve (whether or not the enactment also permits the court to fix a lesser period in particular circumstances), *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

affects the power of a court to act under subsection (2). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] If, in determining what sentence to pass on the defendant, the court takes into account the extent and nature of the assistance given or offered as mentioned in subsection (2), that does not prevent the court from also taking account of any other matter which it is entitled by virtue of any other enactment to take account of for the purposes of determining— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] the sentence, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] in the case of a sentence which is fixed by law, any minimum period of imprisonment which an offender must serve. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-feecee93f9270c7ca7dce0b7a46a613d][^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(8 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] In this section— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] a reference to a sentence includes, in the case of a sentence which is fixed by law, a reference to the minimum period an offender is required to serve, and a reference to a lesser sentence must be construed accordingly; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] a reference to imprisonment includes a reference to any other custodial sentence within the meaning of *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] [^key-15f0535ad07955e1a768e358ef42bddf]... Article 2 of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 ( *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]S.I. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] 1996/ 3160). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(9 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] An agreement with a specified prosecutor may provide for assistance to be given to that prosecutor or to any other prosecutor. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(10 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] References to a specified prosecutor must be construed in accordance with section 71. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
