- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] “ *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]**the arrangements *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]**” are arrangements which are treated as having been made by a protection provider by virtue of section 91(2), and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] “ *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]**the relevant date *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]**” is the date of the record made by the protection provider, in relation to the arrangements, in pursuance of section 91(7). *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] A person does not commit an offence under section 86(1) by disclosing information relating to the arrangements unless the information is disclosed on or after the relevant date. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] But it is immaterial whether the information relates to something done in connection with the arrangements before or on or after the relevant date. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(4 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] A person does not commit an offence under section 88(1) or (2) by disclosing information relating to a person who assumed a new identity in pursuance of the arrangements unless the information is disclosed on or after the relevant date. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(5 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] But it is immaterial whether the person assumed a new identity before or on or after the relevant date. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(6 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] Section 90 applies in relation to a false or misleading representation relating to a person who assumed a new identity in pursuance of the arrangements only if the false or misleading representation is made on or after the relevant date. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]

(7 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292] But it is immaterial whether the person assumed a new identity before or on or after the relevant date. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8843bbb10bbd616c7b16bcc3d1daf292]
