## 75 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Proceedings under section 74: exclusion of public[^c-eeb6de619655af21b65b9a5dcf8625b1][^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] This section applies to— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] any proceedings relating to a reference made under section 74(3), and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] any other proceedings arising in consequence of such proceedings. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] The court in which the proceedings will be or are being heard may make such order as it thinks appropriate— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] to exclude from the proceedings any person who does not fall within subsection (4); *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] to give such directions as it thinks appropriate prohibiting the publication of any matter relating to the proceedings (including the fact that the reference has been made). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] An order under subsection (2) may be made only to the extent that the court thinks— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] that it is necessary to do so to protect the safety of any person, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] that it is in the interests of justice. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] The following persons fall within this subsection— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] a member or officer of the court; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] a party to the proceedings; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] counsel or a solicitor for a party to the proceedings; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] a person otherwise directly concerned with the proceedings. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] This section does not affect any other power which the court has by virtue of any rule of law or other enactment— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] to exclude any person from proceedings, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c] to restrict the publication of any matter relating to proceedings. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7e34ab8d29da54b984f1f5149e1265c]
