## SCHEDULE 14 — Amendments of Part 5 of Police Act 1997

*Extent: E+W*

Section 163

**1.**[^c-f9a2faa9a0ec2be1e7bdd4404b2add9c][^c-40b058b8a9a6372c87eb079d2fde73b5][^c-e3ee145d743ccdc0f792135c913bdca2] Part 5 of the Police Act 1997 (c. 50) (certificates of criminal records etc.) is amended as follows.

**2.**[^c-f0ba4d1715dd6e85d76a7ac175bf5927][^c-38894d0326a6a24787d975ece7ad715f][^c-2f6a017163e1730d3f5c072d590b07d5] In section 114(3) for “Section 113(3) to (5)” substitute “ Sections 113A(3) to (6) and 113C to 113F ”.

**3.**[^c-58cf19d9a0074c443508a73ac5a2b6b3][^c-2bc9b8e03eabd5700be86da6064a1f6f][^M_I_25cfe682-7321-48a2-f5aa-0653c94cf321] In section 116—

- (a) in the application to Scotland of subsection (2)(b) for “to which subsection (3) or (4) of section 115 applies” substitute “ of such description as may be prescribed ”;
- (b) in subsection (3) for “Section 115(6) to (10)” substitute “ Sections 113B(3) to (11) and 113C to 113F ”.

**4.**[^c-3555840daf2f54fbc6dd8bf027f8656a][^c-bde691ef1ec17483d875c58e519eda15] In section 119—

- (a) in subsection (1A) for “section 113(3A) or (3C) or (3EA) or (3EC)” substitute “ section 113C(3) or 113D(3) ”;
- (b) in subsection (2) for “115” substitute “ 113B ”.

**5.**[^c-a285fd5d9ef06caccc7b532bc9eab2fd] In section 119A(2) for the words from “under” to “adults)” substitute “ in a list mentioned in section 113C(3) or 113D(3) ”.

**6.**[^c-51b2a16230bec92e7b7dfa296c8b714c][^M_I_70231d4c-ec66-45b6-dc7e-c966cb5ad61a][^c-9840988d18dddc1b55f42a9c862add0f] In section 120—

- (a) in subsection (3)(b) for “113 or 115” substitute “ 113A or 113B ”;
- (b) in subsection (5)(b) for “113 or 115” substitute “ 113A or 113B ”;
- (c) in subsection (7) for “113” substitute “ 113A ”.

**7.**[^c-20a8858b0b14c3932df00e3cf2e4fdcc][^c-4eab6d73c135ff77222ba080dabb9bbd][^c-c4c28885436203567dedd318dff85ac1] In section 120ZA(4)(b) for “113 or 115” substitute “ 113A or 113B ”.

**8.**[^c-15938a538458ab0e9ae56c88ce2f982f][^c-61239fb2eba7c25d7f6fe711948a1667][^c-141ea19319ac4aba08e40f5570f511ea] In section 120A (as inserted by section 134(1) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (c. 16))—

- (a) in subsection (3)(b) for “section 113(3A) or (3C) or (3EA) or (3EC)” substitute “ section 113C(3) or 113D(3) ”;
- (b) in subsection (5) for “113” substitute “ 113A ”.

**9.**[^c-84a6f4f244133c9540d3f06dc217a13f] In section 120B (as inserted by section 70 of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 7))—

- (a) in subsection (3)(a) for “113” substitute “ 113A ”;
- (b) in subsection (3)(b) for “113(3C)” substitute “ 113C(3) or 113D(3) ”;
- (c) after subsection (6) (as inserted by section 165(2) of this Act) insert—

  > (7) In the case of such a body the reference in subsection (5) to a police authority must be construed as a reference to such body as is prescribed.

**10.**[^c-9913be7768bf7af5a6e2e5b309dc0cb1][^c-3234aa96feba29f863b8878c4958addd] In section 121 for “under section 114(2), 115(4) or (10), 116(2), 122(1) or (2) or 125” substitute “ in relation to the making of regulations or orders ”.

**11.**[^c-911a31ee5f0282d0ec2b81bf31913888][^c-5d9464e6c743ea75ad789b598b6db84b][^c-26ffd8ab0a151af169eaa50cd7c70164] In section 122(3) and (4)(b) for “113 or 115” substitute “ 113A or 113B ”.

**12.**[^c-105d2ef6ae76531b9b7cd8adecc4c653][^c-9d7a4c1984f997718088292d12c368c7][^c-a8034addb1f52fe7f1dbaa1837476e52] In section 124—

- (a) in subsections (1), (2), (3), (4) and (6) for “113 or 115” substitute “ 113A or 113B ”;
- (b) in subsection (5) for “115(8)” substitute “ 113B(5) ”;
- (c) in subsection (6)(e) for “113” substitute “ 113A ”.

**Not yet in force.**

**13.** In section 124B—

- (a) in subsection (1) for “113” substitute “ 113A ”;
- (b) in subsection (3) for “113(5)” substitute “ 113A(6) ”.

**14.**[^c-a54be5a496988b002860273e5abc1314][^c-e55e06000581f72f3bf85dde284bb980][^c-9a79bdb1cac57ab0f827f8c6b7f10cd6] In section 125, at the end add—

> (6) If the power mentioned in subsection (1) is exercised by the Scottish Ministers, the reference in subsection (3) to each House of Parliament must be construed as a reference to the Scottish Parliament.
