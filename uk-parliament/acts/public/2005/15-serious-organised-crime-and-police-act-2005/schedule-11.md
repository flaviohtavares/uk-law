## SCHEDULE 11 — Investigations into conduct of police officers: accelerated procedure in special cases

*Extent: E+W*

Section 159

**1.**[^c-30fa2b37b7695c3e815f2fc950d7bf5a] Schedule 3 to the Police Reform Act 2002 (c. 30) is amended as follows.

  > until—
