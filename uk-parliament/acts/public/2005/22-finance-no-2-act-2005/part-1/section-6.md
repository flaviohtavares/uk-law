# Finance (No. 2) Act 2005, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-03-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Disclosure of value added tax avoidance schemes[^c1274153]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-980150a66d9f9b9eb279b7b9b735bd2c] Schedule 1 (which contains amendments of Schedule 11A to VATA 1994) has effect.

(2) Subsection (1) and Schedule 1 shall come into force on such day as the Treasury may by order made by statutory instrument appoint.

(3) An order under subsection (2) may—

- (a) appoint different days for different purposes, and
- (b) contain transitional provisions and savings.
