## 76. Selection of Lords Justices of Appeal

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Sections 77 to 79[^c-ceedc1aeab8fb26518d86a90fbdf55fb] apply to a recommendation for appointment as a Lord Justice of Appeal.

(2) Any such recommendation must be made in accordance with those sections and section 94C and regulations made under it[^c-e5c7c0c85dc4c0cafc1b384d896241ad].
