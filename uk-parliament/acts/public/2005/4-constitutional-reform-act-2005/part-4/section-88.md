# Constitutional Reform Act 2005, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/4

## 88. Selection process

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On receiving a request the Commission must—

- (a) determine the selection process to be applied,
- (b) apply the selection process, and
- (c) make a selection accordingly.

(2)[^c-e3c213c3b63ec06e7f3c3a53709332c7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-f111b0956fcaa560a56b3e0ad51be5b8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) One person only may be selected for each recommendation , appointment or pool membership[^c-b2c41bf8b59fe69c07e0ae8e7d50f202] to which a request relates.

(5) Subsection (4) applies to selection under this section and to selection under regulations under section 94C[^c-648518bcb08dd36e1be4c6b4acaf3b1e].
