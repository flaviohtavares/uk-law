# Constitutional Reform Act 2005, section 119

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 119. Delegation of functions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Lord Chief Justice may nominate a judicial office holder (as defined in section 109(4)) to exercise any of his functions under the relevant sections.

(2) The relevant sections are—

- (a) section 108(3) to (7);
- (b) section 111(2);
- (c) section 112;
- (d) section 116(3)(b).
