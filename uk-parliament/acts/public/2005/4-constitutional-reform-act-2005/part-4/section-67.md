> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/4

## 67. Selection of Lord Chief Justice and Heads of Division

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Sections 68 to 70[^key-232e0b86c71cf0d39cfb4446ebd279ac] apply to a recommendation for an appointment to one of the following offices—

- (a) Lord Chief Justice;
- (b) Master of the Rolls;
- (c) President of the Queen's Bench Division;
- (d) President of the Family Division;
- (e) President of the Business and Property Division[^key-24d7f045e9567424aa1c1444bfb4f61d].

(2) Any such recommendation must be made in accordance with those sections and section 94C and regulations made under it[^key-ac3657c9965a599ff1ecc91931b757d7].
