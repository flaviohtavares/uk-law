# Constitutional Reform Act 2005, section 75A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75A. Sections 75B to 75G apply where request made for selection[^c19676991]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 75B applies[^c-786d07e8d135767b2909cc38b5ed0bd7] where the Lord Chancellor makes a request to the Commission under paragraph 2(5) of Schedule 1 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (request for person to be selected for recommendation for appointment to the office of Senior President of Tribunals).

(2) That section is[^c-19f5535e243cc61edf11f9c17b0a9ea6] subject to section 95 (withdrawal and modification of requests).
