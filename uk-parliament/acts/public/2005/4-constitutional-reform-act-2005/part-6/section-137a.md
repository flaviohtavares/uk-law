> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 137A. Encouragement of diversity[^c-b18fccbd769febefc55c24ed9d422af8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each of the Lord Chancellor and the Lord Chief Justice of England and Wales must take such steps as that office-holder considers appropriate for the purpose of encouraging judicial diversity.
