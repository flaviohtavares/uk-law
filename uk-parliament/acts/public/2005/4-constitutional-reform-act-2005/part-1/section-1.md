# Constitutional Reform Act 2005, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. The rule of law

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Act does not adversely affect—

- (a) the existing constitutional principle of the rule of law, or
- (b) the Lord Chancellor's existing constitutional role in relation to that principle.
