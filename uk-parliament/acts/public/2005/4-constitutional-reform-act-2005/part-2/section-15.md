# Constitutional Reform Act 2005, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Other functions of the Lord Chancellor and organisation of the courts[^c18896081]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 4 provides for—

- (a) the transfer of functions to or from the Lord Chancellor,
- (b) the modification of other functions of the Lord Chancellor,
- (c) the modification of enactments relating to those functions, and
- (d) the modification of enactments relating to the organisation of the courts.

(2)[^key-475d43c372a27ae80085764ebf6cd0f2] Schedule 5 makes similar provision about functions under legislation relating to Northern Ireland.
