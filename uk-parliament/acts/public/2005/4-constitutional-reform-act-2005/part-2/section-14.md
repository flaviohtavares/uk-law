> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2005/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Transfer of appointment functions to Her Majesty[^c18923121]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 3 provides for—

- (a) Her Majesty instead of the Lord Chancellor to make appointments to certain offices, and
- (b) the modification of enactments relating to those offices.
