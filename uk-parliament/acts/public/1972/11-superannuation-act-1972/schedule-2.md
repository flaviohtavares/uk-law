# Superannuation Act 1972, SCHEDULE 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/11

## SCHEDULE 2 — The Existing Civil Service Superannuation Provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 2.

### Part I

#### Enactments

The Pensions Commutation Acts 1871 to 1882, in so far as they apply to persons to whom section 1 of this Act applies.

Section 281 of the [^c8548471]Government of India Act 1935.

Section 26 of the [^c8548481]Agriculture Act 1937.

Section 18 of the [^c8548491]Old Age and Widows’ Pensions Act 1940.

Part II of the Schedule to the [^c8548501]Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1944.

Section 6 of the [^c8548511]Food and Drugs (Milk and Dairies) Act 1944.

Section 6(2)(d)(i), (ii) and (iii) of the [^c8548521]Commonwealth Telegraphs Act 1949.

Section 53 of the [^c8548531]Superannuation Act 1949.

So much of section 2(7) of the [^c8548541]Supreme Court Officers (Pensions) Act 1954 as provides for such employments of such persons as are referred to therein to be treated as having been employment in the civil service of the State.

The [^c8548551]Superannuation Act 1965, except sections 38, 39, 39A, 42(1), 93, 95, 96, 97, 102, 104(2) and 106, Schedule 9, paragraphs 10 and 11 of Schedule 10 and Schedule 11.

Section 1 of the [^c8548561]Superannuation (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1967.

Section 45 of the [^c8548571]Post Office Act 1969.

### Part II

#### Instruments

The following, in so far as they are in force immediately before the commencement of this Act:—

- Any rules, regulations, orders, schemes or warrants made, or having effect as if made, under an enactment listed in Part I above, except any regulations made under section 6(2) of the Commonwealth Telegraphs Act 1949 in so far as they apply to persons who have ceased to be employed in the civil service of the State.
- Article 6 of the [^c8548581]Government of Ireland (Miscellaneous Adaptations) (Northern Ireland) Order 1923.
- The National Insurance (Modification of the [^c8548591]Superannuation Acts) Regulations 1948.
- The National Insurance (Modification of the [^c8548601]Superannuation Acts) (Amendment) Regulations 1949.
- The National Insurance (Modification of the [^c8548611]Superannuation Acts) Regulations 1960.
- The National Insurance (Modification of the [^c8548621]Superannuation Acts) Regulations 1961.
- The [^c8548631]Widows’, Children’s and Dependants’ Pensions (India and Pakistan) Rules 1965, in so far as they apply to persons who at the commencement of this Act are serving in employment in the civil service of the State.
- The National Insurance (Modification of the [^c8548641]Superannuation Acts) (Amendment) Regulations 1965.
- The National Insurance (Modification of the [^c8548651]Superannuation Acts) (Amendment) Regulations 1971.
