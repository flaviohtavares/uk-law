# Superannuation Act 1972, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Certain enactments relating to superannuation of Forestry Commissioners and to declarations required from recipients of certain pensions to cease to have effect.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c8520431] Paragraph 11 of Schedule 1 to the [^c8520441]Forestry Act 1967 (which authorizes the grant in certain circumstances of superannuation benefits to a person who retires from the office of Forestry Commissioner while under the age of 60 without renewal of public employment and who is not entitled to a pension by virtue of other provisions of that Schedule) shall cease to have effect.

(2)[^c8520431] Section 6 of the [^c8520461]Appropriation Act 1957 (which prohibits the receipt of an ypayment out of maneys provided by Parliament for half-pay or navy, army, air-force or civil non-effective services unless the prescribed declararion has been made by the recipient) shall cease to have effect.
