## 2. Constitution of principal councils in England.[^c-157ac2f88588af18c07d59bc94f2ba7c]

*Extent: E*

(1)[^c15088121][^c19883981][^c19884111][^c19884171][^c22561501][^c-b217ae988e8b60e4b7e4cb289a558f08][^c-52788bca59965c991df8f485b74ec1f3][^c-cf857e15a256b44a47b7fe7c8d1696c2][^c-82265546b53734aab671d96e73a199f1][^c-03dfdc0289deb993881913862735fbdf][^c-6ac9e3ac873ef305e10445707408f7ad][^c-a1e82955ad8fb2119d1d6db26c7be814][^c-d06ac868623302ead82c037c276dc4e9] For every non-metropolitan[^c15088131] county there shall be a council consisting of a chairman and councillors and the council shall have all such functions as are vested in them by this Act or otherwise.

(2)[^c15088141][^c19884151][^c19884131][^c19884211][^c-d9c523a2b80d80da326d391efbcd3f5d][^c-ec365b287cb84c338c9c3b66d68bf234][^c-87b0f7fe999651626560d28e566b3e9e][^c-c2978be19c0338ccd633184299bd32c3][^c-bb1ba93221e430bbba26bac6e6d05730][^c-1d91605e4508eff9d64a834d713712eb][^c-9919d21e2d3f8cc587123f635f377a2d][^c-691801697501ca1a56ccb3530c16897b] For every district there shall be a council consisting of a chairman and councillors and the council shall have all such functions as are vested in them by this Act or otherwise.

(2A)[^c-157ac2f88588af18c07d59bc94f2ba7c][^c15088151] Where a council mentioned in subsection (1) or (2) above are operating executive arrangements which involve a mayor and cabinet executive [^c-581877e74d447e88d0cf9070b2f22e0b]..., the council shall consist of an elected mayor, a chairman and councillors.

(2B)[^c19628561] In such a case, a reference in this Act to a member of a council is a reference to—

- (a) the elected mayor of the council,
- (b) the chairman of the council, or
- (c) a councillor of the council.

(3)[^c20519811][^c20519671][^c20519791][^c20519871][^c20519701][^c-6035d54ab8b11a23b3b6b1e1f2185e48][^c-1be889fcbe2c9daed98c6e21ad6070bd] Each council mentioned in subsection (1) or (2) above shall be a body corporate by the name “The County Council” or “The District Council”, as the case may be, with the addition of the name of the particular county or district.
