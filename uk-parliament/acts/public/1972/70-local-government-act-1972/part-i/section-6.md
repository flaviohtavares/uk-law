# Local Government Act 1972, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/70
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. Term of office and retirement of councillors.

*Extent: E*

(1) Councillors for a principal area shall be elected by the local government electors for that area in accordance with this Act and Part I of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^c15088281].

(2) For the purposes of the election of councillors—

- (a)[^c19628621][^c20515501] every non-metropolitan[^c15088291] county shall be divided into electoral divisions, each returning (subject to paragraph 3 of Schedule 3 to this Act and subject to[^c15088301] section 56(8) of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009[^c20514441] and section 12(4) of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007[^c19628751]) one councillor;
- (b)[^c19628811] every metropolitan district shall be divided into wards, each returning such number of councillors as may be provided as mentioned in subsection (3) below;
- (c) every non-metropolitan district shall be divided into wards, each returning such number of councillors as may be provided as mentioned in subsection (3) below[^c15088311];

and there shall be a separate election for each electoral division or ward.

(3)[^c19628841] The number of councillors referred to in subsection (2)(b) or (c) above may be provided—

- (a) under or by virtue of the provisions of section 7 below;
- (b) by an order under Part 2 of the Local Government Act 1992 (c. 19) or Part 3 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009[^c20514471];
- (c) by an order under section 14 of the Local Government and Rating Act 1997 (c. 29);
- (d) by an order under Part 1 of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007.
