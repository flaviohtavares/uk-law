>

## 90. Term of office of persons filling casual vacancies.[^c15096431][^c15096441][^key-fe80ba9eec17fcf69dabc06f04dc3a08][^key-5a37d998d036901a96137356204b1653][^key-e3f07d9eccf075d63bd36d76cc792d30][^key-1551ddf38c187376cac27b875c328132][^key-5a0b3fba4c43364fb4776cd4586ee41e]

*Extent: E+W*

A person elected or appointed under the foregoing provisions of this Act in England or Wales or under Part[^c15096451] [^c15096461]. . . IV of the Local Government Act 1985 to fill any casual vacancy [^c18846001]. . . shall hold office until the date upon which the person in whose place he is elected or appointed would regularly have retired, and he shall then retire.
