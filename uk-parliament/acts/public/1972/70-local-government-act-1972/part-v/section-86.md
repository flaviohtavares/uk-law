## 86. Declaration by local authority of vacancy in office in certain cases.[^c15095991][^c-3a3d3e0c9ee7635f391e7c6139b7efb2][^c-52d4fc90f36beb4634e97f88dc89b0fb][^M_C_25394b7b-d782-4a2d-a582-2f06ca3af857][^c-e12f8ab95bd60ceeff30a06687f7f1d1][^c-e815347b0554c0f1513c1e6a703874b9][^c-e3f07d9eccf075d63bd36d76cc792d30][^c-1551ddf38c187376cac27b875c328132][^c-5a0b3fba4c43364fb4776cd4586ee41e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c15096001] Where a member of a local authority—

- (a) ceases to be qualified to be a member of the authority; or
- (b)[^c15096011] becomes disqualified for being a member of the authority otherwise than under[^c-1df409ac61ca24bbdce07668eff84673] section 80A(1)(c) of this Act,[^c-1f414a35f16ced41d79c7bbfa02ed833] section 79 of the Local Government Act 2000 or section 34 of the Localism Act 2011 or by virtue of, a conviction or a breach of any provision of Part II of the [^c15096051]Representation of the People Act 1983[^c15096041] , or by virtue of an order under section 30 of the Elections Act 2022[^c-333cc9aaf0cd7597072ab86c3f621366]; or
- (c) ceases to be a member of the authority by reason of failure to attend meetings of the authority;

the authority shall, except in any case in which a declaration has been made by the High Court under this Part of this Act, forthwith declare his office to be vacant.

(2)[^c15096071][^c15096081][^c-cf8de3ec52e94439e99c694fb1ab6f5f][^c15096061] In this section “**local authority**” includes a joint authority , an economic prosperity board[^c20510951] , a combined authority and a combined county authority[^c-eefa59fcd341a4f48d8335c3d15cf997] [^c15096091] ...
