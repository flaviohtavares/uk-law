## 99. Meetings and proceedings of local authorities.[^c15099841][^c15099851][^c15099861][^c15099871][^c18887941][^c-5d790444f1f5aef9f762d21cb08ab500][^c-8f491805d920f8d59c1a1e644fe25133][^c-c70af0f2698e177c396571314533820c][^c-dbc220d8f865f255b44988cb6e9da752][^c-3eadbdf5a232c4ed45275b0ea6af6f97][^c-5bccae45f4b2e1634e3c76449f238261][^c-18b4efe1f57f4299a1c8a373fd7be980]

*Extent: E+W*

The provisions of Schedule 12 to this Act shall have effect with respect to the meetings and proceedings of local authorities , joint authorities,[^c15099881] economic prosperity boards, combined authorities,[^c20512291] combined county authorities,[^c-fec86d1b5963833304d640df93e590c5] [^c-f81cff3762506290aaa5c5a20aa6157e].... [^c15099931]... and their committees, parish meetings and their committees and community meetings.
