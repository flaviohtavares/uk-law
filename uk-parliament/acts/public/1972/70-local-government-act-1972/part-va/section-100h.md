# Local Government Act 1972, section 100H

## 100H. Supplemental provisions and offences.[^c15100401][^c15100411][^c15100421][^c15100391][^c15100081]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A document directed by any provision of this Part to be open to inspection shall be so open at all reasonable hours and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b65f962dbdaf5523d631a7d6643ea093]

- (a) in the case of a document open to inspection by virtue of section 100D(1) above, upon payment of such reasonable fee as may be required for the facility; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b65f962dbdaf5523d631a7d6643ea093]
- (b) in any other case, without payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b65f962dbdaf5523d631a7d6643ea093]

section 100G to be open to inspection is to be open to inspection at all reasonable hours and without payment.

(2) In relation to a principal council in England, *(Extent: W)*[^c-17017a6e733be51ad5ac62eacbb21ebb] Where a document is open to inspection by a person under any provision of this Part *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-75f4e97aab8a4178609e9650fa41a1de] section 100G, the person may, subject to subsection (3) below—

- (a) make copies of or extracts from the document, or
- (b)[^c15100431] require the person having custody of the document to supply to him a photographic copy of or of extracts from the document,

upon payment of such reasonable fee as may be required for the facility.

*Extent: W*

(2A)[^c-671b56e893ed29a7a4e039ce1e3cb0aa] In relation to a principal council in Wales, where a document is open to inspection by a person under any provision of this Part the person may, subject to subsection (3) below—

- (a) make copies of the document or parts of the document, or
- (b) require the person having custody of the document to provide a copy of the document or of parts of the document,

upon payment of such reasonable fee as may be required for the facility.

(3)[^c15100441] Subsections (2), (2A) and (6A) do *(Extent: W)*[^c-acf011b2f9ccdbe3660567a2981b7c9d] not require or authorise the doing of any such act which infringes the copyright in any work except that, where the owner of the copyright is a principal council, nothing done in pursuance of those subsections *(Extent: W)*[^c-24cbe778da87a872bcfa76436518f4df] shall constitute an infringement of the copyright.

(3A)[^c-4e4d06db838273ab4969e1bd8e3b436e] Provisions in this Part which require the publication or supply of documents do not require or authorise the doing of any act which infringes the copyright in any work except that, where the owner of the copyright is a principal council, nothing done in pursuance of those provisions shall constitute an infringement of the copyright.

*Extent: W*

(3A)[^c-7293ebc0de1e119b26c797a4d721f9a4] Provisions in this Part which require the publication of documents by a principal council in Wales do not require or authorise the doing of any act which infringes the copyright in any work except that, where the owner of the copyright is the council, nothing done in pursuance of those provisions constitutes an infringement of the copyright.

(4) If, without reasonable excuse, a person having the custody of a document which is required by section 100B(1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8d996b3642c3fbe7d986c5e3919a82ae] , 100C(1) or 100EA(2)[^c19641291] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8d996b3642c3fbe7d986c5e3919a82ae] above to be open to inspection by the public— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8d996b3642c3fbe7d986c5e3919a82ae]

- (a) intentionally obstructs any person exercising a right conferred by this Part to inspect, or to make a copy of or extracts from, the document, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8d996b3642c3fbe7d986c5e3919a82ae]
- (b) refuses to furnish copies to any person entitled to obtain them under any provision of this Part, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8d996b3642c3fbe7d986c5e3919a82ae]

he shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8d996b3642c3fbe7d986c5e3919a82ae]

(5)[^c15100451] Where any accessible document for a meeting to which this subsection applies—[^c-bbaf9ddbd02736dec084d14f8173437f]

- (a) is supplied to, or open to inspection by, a member of the public, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bbaf9ddbd02736dec084d14f8173437f] [^c-07e2dcc84d923ecad40c34a7ba43f590]...

*Extent: W*

- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-19986204400ceb1ab997dac2b84cbb78][^c-bbaf9ddbd02736dec084d14f8173437f] is published electronically by a principal council in Wales, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bbaf9ddbd02736dec084d14f8173437f]
- (b) is supplied for the benefit of any newspaper, in pursuance of section 100B(7) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bbaf9ddbd02736dec084d14f8173437f] or 100BA(9) *(Extent: W)*[^c-24dd88954eac5f593c34c03f49aeb9b5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bbaf9ddbd02736dec084d14f8173437f] above, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bbaf9ddbd02736dec084d14f8173437f]

is published electronically or supplied to a member of the public in accordance with this Part,

the publication thereby of any defamatory matter contained in the document shall be privileged unless the publication is proved to be made with malice.

(6) Subsection (5) above applies to any meeting of a principal council and any meeting of a committee or sub-committee of a principal council; and, for the purposes of that subsection, the “**accessible documents**”; for a meeting are the following—

- (a) any copy of the agenda or of any item included in the agenda for the meeting;
- (b) any such further statements or particulars for the purpose of indicating the nature of any item included in the agenda as are mentioned in section 100B(7)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4658a44d5e00e038a10c51eddfec4930]*b*) or 100BA(9)(b) *(Extent: W)*[^c-5722bea919dbf35d2a2730423a508777] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4658a44d5e00e038a10c51eddfec4930] above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4658a44d5e00e038a10c51eddfec4930]
- (c) any copy of a document relating to such an item which is supplied for the benefit of a newspaper in pursuance of section 100B(7)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5cf9ea544eca02783aa98c418a9b0701]*c*) or 100BA(9)(c) *(Extent: W)*[^c-577d61ef18a9d7fd392b6429dbeb7d54] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5cf9ea544eca02783aa98c418a9b0701] above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5cf9ea544eca02783aa98c418a9b0701]
- (d) any copy of the whole or part of a report for the meeting;
- (e) any copy of the whole or part of any background papers for a report for the meeting, within the meaning of section 100D above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-17767047aa713bac190130d07a7135f9]

*Extent: W*

- (f)[^c-5ca6d4960cef2d1f639fcf72eeab9607] the note required to be published by a principal council in Wales under section 100C(1C).

*Extent: W*

(6A)[^c-501e8630e037f0d24c5d76dab0fe2ffe] A principal council in Wales must put in place facilities for members of the public who would otherwise not be able to do so, to access—

- (a) notices or other documents required to be published electronically under sections 100A(6)(aa), 100BA(1), 100C(1A) and (1C) and 100D(1)(c), and
- (b) documents required to remain accessible electronically under sections 100C(1A) and 100D(2A)(a).

(7) The rights conferred by this Part to inspect or otherwise access[^c-e590c8c26c6d79514eb56a6a38e5ceac], copy and be furnished with documents are in addition, and without prejudice, to any such rights conferred by or under any other enactment.

*Extent: W*

(8)[^c-806991fb86902be645439fbc1c3b71a5] A principal council in Wales must have regard to any guidance issued by the Welsh Ministers about the exercise of its functions relating to the publication, provision and inspection of documents under this Part.
