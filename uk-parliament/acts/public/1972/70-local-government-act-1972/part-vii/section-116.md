# Local Government Act 1972, section 116

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 116. Members of local authorities in England not to be appointed as officers.[^c15107331][^c15107341][^c15107351][^c15107361][^c18607091][^c18607101][^c18858411][^c18891431][^c20521031][^c-8fafe89064f4c009f1abc0056e24a654][^c-af878fb062a04d44ef26509b608fc944][^c-4b4f2336644906072c47e23d5c0cdefa][^c-ddabf4eb92251a76ef623d4f0b3eeead][^c-e69457f593e374ee53b843e47588f01d]

*Extent: E+W*

A person shall, so long as he is, and for twelve months after he ceases to be, a member of a local authority in England[^c-5750eab8c0aa8e9a8f17897d10f42f50], be disqualified for being appointed or elected[^c15107371] by that authority to any paid office, other than to the office of chairman or vice-chairman or, in the case of a local authority[^c15107381] in England which are operating executive arrangements which involve a leader and cabinet executive, the office of executive leader or member of the executive. . ..
