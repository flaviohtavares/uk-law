## 144. Power to encourage visitors and provide conference and other facilities.[^c15110451][^c18865141][^key-afcbbf78fb4eda0e9282a6ffb63bde1c][^key-bf820e01259360d6f6f713d96d1d67d6][^key-1f1832de044cd0222a125a3d3d46f245][^key-790a39edf28c206923df7f3315c79192][^key-940e2776d7e1efd9bebd5058c3f6e1bf][^key-697540b84683c2e07be929ec49691092][^key-fee56827e0ac031f20b02a1751f999b1][^key-dd834b616f11efb16418d650f3cbbf0c][^key-463cb0f8a194e608d9d2d09372e95ef9][^key-9ac40c346b7068d42464839a6f300cd0][^M_C_053ee307-accb-42d8-f80c-d57be51d38e2][^key-ea9f0b72af165e8828a45699f70de9f3][^key-11486f577613ccc2fc42cedc5ca7e898][^key-8cf5cf585046b80acada834e30e72b6a][^key-167d225d26e476196788865509c717ab][^key-a03e3f9886c0465084f033f171005c84][^key-afdde7d2a1e0f5dd8d2c1d393da397a4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority , combined authority or combined county authority[^key-0b53aa1dbb807075fccb33c675033461] may (either alone or jointly with any other person or body)—

- (a) encourage persons, by advertisement or otherwise, to visit their area for recreation, for health purposes, or to hold conferences, trade fairs and exhibitions in their area; and
- (b) provide, or encourage any other person or body to provide, facilities for [^c15110461]..., conferences, trade fairs and exhibitions or improve, or encourage any other person or body to improve, any existing facilities for those purposes.

(2) Without prejudice to subsection (1) above, a local authority , combined authority or combined county authority[^key-973d32645d8a14567c05129285e95a8e] may contribute to any organisation approved by the Secretary of State for the purposes of this subsection and established for the purpose of encouraging persons to visit the United Kingdom or any part thereof.

(3)[^c15110471] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c15110481] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) In this section “**local authority**” includes the Common Council.
