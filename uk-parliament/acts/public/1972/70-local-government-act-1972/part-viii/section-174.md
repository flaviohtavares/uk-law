## 174. Travelling allowance and subsistence allowance.[^c15113251][^c18646701][^c18646711][^c18646721][^c15113261][^c15113271][^c18865781][^c18865861][^c20521221]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c19913481][^c18646731][^c15113281][^c-d46db075dc1bc461327ee692cdd1c78f][^c-adf43756585b2f78ac0a66ded5db916d][^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] Subject to subsections (2) and (3) below, a member of a body to which this section applies shall be entitled to receive payments by way of travelling allowance or subsistence allowance where expenditure on travelling (whether inside or outside the United Kingdom) or, as the case may be, on subsistence is necessarily incurred by him for the purpose of enabling him to perform any approved duty as a member of that body, being payments at rates determined by that body, but not exceeding, in the case of travel *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] or subsistence[^c15113291] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] for the purpose of an approved duty within the United Kingdom, such rates as may be specified by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(2) A member of a parish or community council shall not be entitled to any payment under this section in respect of the performance as such a member of an approved duty within the parish or community or, in the case of a parish or community grouped under a common parish or community council, the area of the group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
