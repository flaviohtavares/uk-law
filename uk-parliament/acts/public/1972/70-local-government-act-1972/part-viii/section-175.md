# Local Government Act 1972, section 175

## 175. Allowances for attending conferences and meetings.[^c15113471][^c18830291][^c18865831][^c18865881][^c20521231]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] The following bodies, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

- (a) any local authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
- (b) any other body to which this section applies and which has power by virtue of any enactment to send representatives to any conference or meeting to which this section applies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

may pay any member of the authority or other body attending any such conference or meeting such *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] allowances in the nature of an attendance allowance and an allowance for travelling and subsistence, as they think fit.[^c15113481] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(1A) Payments made under subsection (1) above shall be of such reasonable amounts as the body in question may determine in a particular case or class of case but shall not exceed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

- (a) in the case of payments of an allowance in the nature of an attendance allowance, such amounts as may be specified in or determined under regulations made by the Secretary of State; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
- (b) in the case of payments of an allowance in the nature of an allowance for travel and subsistence in respect of a conference or meeting held in the United Kingdom, such amounts as may be specified under section 174 above for the corresponding allowance under that section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

and regulations made by the Secretary of State may make it a condition of any payment mentioned in paragraph (a) above that, in the financial year to which the payment would relate, the aggregate amount which the body in question has paid or is already liable to pay in respect of any prescribed allowance or allowances does not exceed such maximum amount as may be specified in or determined under the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(2) Where a body mentioned in subsection (1)(b) above has power under any enactment other than this Act or any instrument under such an enactment to pay expenses incurred in attending a conference or meeting to which this section applies, the amount payable under that enactment or instrument shall not exceed the amount which would be payable in respect of the attendance under that subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(3) In relation to a local authority this section applies to a conference or meeting held inside or outside the United Kingdom and convened by any person or body (other than a person or body convening it in the course of a trade or business or a body the objects of which are wholly or partly political) for the purpose of discussing matters which in their opinion relate to the interests of their area or any part of it or the interests of the inhabitants of their area or any part of it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(3B)[^c15113501][^c22561581][^c-6145b0f97cad075abd941592ab2217b9][^c15113491] In relation to[^c15113511] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] an economic prosperity board, a combined authority[^c-44c611c00c1a93a0ea146c4bc2f8bfc2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] , a combined county authority[^c-249969169861bc15b6a75022b7c70f68] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] body which is a joint board, joint authority or other combined body all the members of which are representatives of local authorities[^c15113531] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] this section applies to a conference or meeting held and convened as mentioned in subsection (3) above for the purpose of discussing matters which in the body’s opinion relate— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

- (a) to the functions of the body; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
- (b) to any functions of local authorities in which the body has an interest. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(4) In relation to any other body to which this section applies this section applies to a conference or meeting convened by one or more such bodies or by an association of such bodies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
