## 173A. Right to opt for financial loss allowance.[^c15113111][^c18830271][^c20521211][^c15113101]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] If a councillor gives notice in writing to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] parish or community council[^c15113121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] of which he is a member that he wishes to receive financial loss allowance, he shall be entitled, subject to and in accordance with the following provisions of this section, to receive that allowance instead of any payment by way of attendance allowance to which he would otherwise be entitled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(2) A notice under this section is referred to in this section as a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]**financial loss allowance notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]**”.

(3)[^c15113131][^c15113141] If a councillor gives a financial loss allowance notice to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] parish or community council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] not later than the end of the period of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] one month beginning with the day of[^c15113161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] his election as a member of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] council[^c15113171] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]then, subject to subsection (4A) below[^c15113181] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7], he shall be entitled to receive financial loss allowance for the performance of any approved duty since his election, whether performed before or after the giving of the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(4)[^c15113191] If a councillor gives a financial loss allowance notice to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] parish or community council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] otherwise than in accordance with subsection (3) above, then, subject to subsection (4A) below, he shall be entitled to receive financial loss allowance for the performance of any approved duty after the end of the period of one month beginning with the day on which the notice is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(4A) If a councillor who has given a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] parish or community council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] a financial loss allowance notice gives them notice in writing that he withdraws that notice, it shall not have effect in relation to any duty performed after the day on which the notice of withdrawal is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
