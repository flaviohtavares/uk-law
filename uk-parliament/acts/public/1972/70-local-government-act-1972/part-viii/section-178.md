## 178. Regulations as to allowances.[^c19913871][^c19913861][^c15114501][^c20521291][^c-cbee53fa0dc134738427fed35e492651][^c-b54980bba66fe3f8f7e61af8b3074b56][^c-896d913145abfa8508c72d1b47204c08][^c-4277d89bd230c7c47bc71cf49ce27dc4][^c-8be4abc544560874d600612ab557415b][^c-de078fc33555d81d61225bf68a6dd0df][^c-426f78b2953a9100b8553e6b570c4ed2][^c-d7e85cd456cf6bb16d509dc98446a667][^c-e6ce7b09e9df21fc32485f5b435ed77a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] The Secretary of State may make regulations as to the manner in which sections 173 to 176 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] [^c15114511] . . . above are to be administered, and in particular, and without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing provision, may make regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

- (a) providing for the avoidance of duplication in payments under those sections, or between payments under any of those sections and any other Act, and for the determination of the body or bodies by whom any payments under those sections are to be made, and, where such payments are to be made by more than one body, for the apportionment between those bodies of the sums payable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
- (b) specifying the forms to be used and the particulars to be provided for the purpose of claiming payments under those sections; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
- (c) providing for the publication by a body to which sections 173 to 175 above apply, in the minutes of that body or otherwise, of details of such payments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(2) A statutory instrument containing regulations under section 173 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] or 177[^c15114521] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] above or this section shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
