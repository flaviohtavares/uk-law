## 177. Provisions supplementary to sections 173 to 176.[^c15114281][^c15114291][^c15114301][^c20521251]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c22561591][^c15114311][^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] [^c15114321] ... sections 174 and 175 above apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

- (a) to the bodies specified in section 21(1) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989, except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
  - (i) the Common Council; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] a body established pursuant to an order under section 67 of the Local Government Act 1985 (successors to residuary bodies); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] without prejudice to section 265 below, the Council of the Isles of Scilly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
- (b)[^c15114331][^c-7499fca75b5c5135c17e7f5be2bdc45c] to any prescribed body on which a body to which those sections apply by virtue of paragraph (a) above is represented; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
- (c) to any parish or community council. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(1A)[^c17823401] Subsection (1) has effect without prejudice to the operation of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

- (a) regulations made by virtue of section 94(5C) or 95(3B) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 (allowances for admission appeal panels); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
- (b)[^c18893701] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (ba (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-06115ffe7ff66f54f301972638c10948][^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] regulations made by virtue of section 51A(8) of the Education Act 2002 (allowances for exclusion review panels: England); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

- (c) regulations made by virtue of section 52(6) of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] that Act (allowances for exclusion appeal panels: Wales); and[^c-5bca5986d3983c35f8812286d0fabb17] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7].

(2)[^c15114351] In sections 173 to 176 above “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]**approved duty *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]**”, in relation to a member of a body, means such duties as may be specified in or determined under regulations made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(2A)[^c15114361] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) For the purposes of sections 173 to 176 above *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] [^c15114371] ... a member of a committee or sub-committee of a local authority or other body mentioned in subsection (1) above shall be deemed to be a member of that body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(4) Section 94(5) above shall apply in relation to a member of any body mentioned in subsection (1) above to whom it would not otherwise apply as it applies in relation to a member of a local authority; and no other enactment or instrument shall prevent a member of any such body from taking part in the consideration or determination of any allowance or other payment under any of the provisions of sections 173 to 176 above *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] or under any scheme made by virtue of section 18 of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^c15114381] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7].
