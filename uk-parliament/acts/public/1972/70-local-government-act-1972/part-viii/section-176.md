## 176. Payment of expenses of official and courtesy visits, etc.[^c15113671][^c18865891][^c20521241][^c-91d612e23a00f3657de2f8a4ef0621ff]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] Subject to subsection (2) below, a local authority may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

- (a)[^c18831041] defray any travelling or other expenses reasonably incurred by or on behalf of any members in making official and courtesy visits, whether inside or outside the United Kingdom, on behalf of the authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]
- (b) defray any expenses incurred in the reception and entertainment by way of official courtesy of distinguished persons visiting the area of the authority and persons representative of or connected with local government or other public services whether inside or outside the United Kingdom and in the supply of information to any such persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(2)[^c18646781][^c18831061] In the case of a visit within the United Kingdom, the amount defrayed under this section by a local authority in respect of the expenses of any member of the authority in making a visit within the United Kingdom shall not exceed the payments which he would have been entitled to receive by way of travelling allowance or subsistence allowance under section 174 above if the making of the visit had been an approved duty of that member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]

(3)[^c15113691][^c15113701][^c-32378681eb18348f4a7a75fbb0f41601][^c15113681] In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]**local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7]**” includes a joint authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] an economic prosperity board,[^c20510791] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] a combined authority and a combined county authority[^c-e19bfac22fba91e22ca1376e52918291] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ec185dba7ec102d181001cd7bb585ce7] [^c-71676ddaa5e6afbd1536a40d2651c68d]...[^c-613bf44ef8148072188900ef5c871ab0].... . .
