# Local Government Act 1972, section 232

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 232. Public notices.[^c15122831][^c15122841][^c18831191][^c-9d90cc99a3b0f57d40d7f12dde67444f][^c-dc2e2ebbedbb253febd1785ce6673744][^c-0294539edf3ec19c7bfb9032f6d89c3c][^c-925be5426bebaad7c6d9363bb8b25110][^c-d0f9fddd27a0ac59f52d26ce34ac6572][^c-e34659e19bc6e241f972f28b7d08476e][^c-d9cbf94e15d95ad3a5a9f68c358c0bce]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Save as otherwise expressly provided, a public notice required to be given by a local authority shall be given—

- (a) by posting the notice in some conspicuous place or places within the area of the local authority; [^c-563f35e9c35d6ccc28f5b6d832820b29]...
- (b) in such other manner, if any, as appears to the local authority to be desirable for giving publicity to the notice.

; and *(Extent: W)*[^c-c36a8393d9a115ff893101615387bc01]

*Extent: W*

- (c) where the local authority is a local authority in Wales, by publishing it electronically.

(1ZA)[^c-6424b4e3c14dc1e1417b4f2be35cc722] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^c15122861][^c15122871][^c-a56ec42729750c3d3fa4bcf0f66b24d5][^c15122851] In subsection (1) above “**local authority**” includes a corporate joint committee,[^M_F_549b79e1-afbd-4f3b-fbb3-c70c1f597110] a joint authority, an economic prosperity board, a combined authority[^c20513281] [^c-99babfbfd9267558a40daed0fd57b278]... ,[^c-785ef6984a80052e33ace91fe42f03dd] a combined county authority,[^c-3d2e768b2f39f89b550518d2f7f9aa1b] a fire and rescue authority created by an order under section 4A of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004,[^c-7ca86f16c74dce71cae514ad171f2fba] a police and crime commissioner and the Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime [^M_F_0eee34cd-5c1f-40f9-803c-2ffb9e7e888d]... [^c15122921]....

(1B)[^M_F_0d17a1b9-228e-414d-cbf3-858dedf6d9f2] In the application of subsection (1) to a corporate joint committee, the reference to the corporate joint committee’s area is to be read as a reference to the area specified as the corporate joint committee’s area in regulations under Part 5 of the Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021 establishing the corporate joint committee.

(2) This section shall apply to a public notice required to be given by the chairman of a parish meeting as it applies to public notices required to be given by a parish council.

*Extent: W*

(3)[^c-eeb459f4a6eac4fac8a00aa8c561c286] The Welsh Ministers may by regulations make further or different provision about the manner of giving a public notice required to be given by a local authority in Wales.

(4 (Extent: W)) Regulations under subsection (3) may also make provision about the manner of giving a public notice required to be given by— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) a National Park authority for a National Park in Wales; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) a fire and rescue authority constituted by a scheme under section 2 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004, or a scheme to which section 4 of that Act applies, for an area in Wales. *(Extent: W)*

(5 (Extent: W)) Regulations under subsection (3) may— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) make different provision for different purposes; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) include supplementary, incidental, consequential, transitional, transitory or saving provision (including provision amending, modifying, repealing or revoking any enactment (including this Act and the Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021)). *(Extent: W)*

(6 (Extent: W)) A statutory instrument containing regulations under subsection (3) must not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of Senedd Cymru. *(Extent: W)*
