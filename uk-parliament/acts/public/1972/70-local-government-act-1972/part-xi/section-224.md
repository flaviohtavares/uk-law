# Local Government Act 1972, section 224

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/70
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 224. Arrangements by principal councils for custody of documents.[^c15119721][^c15119731][^c15119741][^c18866851][^key-9d90cc99a3b0f57d40d7f12dde67444f][^key-18500c6ad0795505aedb5514f2233e28][^key-930db1d778a0188133987be3d1935d9e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c15119751] Without prejudice to the powers of the custos rotulorum to give directions as to the documents of any county, a principal council shall make proper arrangements with respect to any documents which belong to or are in the custody of the council or any of their officers.

(2)[^c15119771][^c15119781][^key-4873087d73a0ba92077a2f81e707f7f2][^c15119761] This section applies to a joint authority , economic prosperity board[^c20512681] , combined authority or combined county authority[^key-b96363a16130ff46a5808d26d04f29e9] [^c15119791] ... as it applies to a principal council.

(3)[^c15119801] In subsection (1) above “**county**”, in relation to Wales, means a preserved county.
