# Local Government Act 1972, section 237

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/70
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 237. Offences against byelaws.[^c15125261][^c15125271][^c15125281][^c15125291][^c15125301][^c15125311][^c18867641][^c22561641][^key-0fe5e87c8489a76081a3d89d700d3091][^key-f6dcb37cea839681f0458be1344651f9]

*Extent: E+W*

Byelaws to which section 236 above applies and byelaws of a class prescribed by regulations under section 236A[^c19657521] may provide that persons contravening the byelaws shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding such sum as may be fixed by the enactment conferring the power to make the byelaws, or, if no sum is so fixed, the sum of £20, and in the case of a continuing offence a further fine not exceeding such sum as may be fixed as aforesaid, or, if no sum is so fixed, the sum of £5 for each day during which the offence continues after conviction thereof.
