## 223. Appearance of local authorities in legal proceedings.[^c15119491][^c15119501][^c15119511][^c18866841][^c18886411][^c19655661][^key-dd4d144b112db32587c87e25b322a04d][^key-765a1b5c4e9ae7e212dfc1d66ba2f39d][^key-268ed5f5fc01f9388b226337275b9536][^key-9b87618271f77c12eb9c52ca217e8b03][^key-b75fbc54cbcffaa6c2f38fae27bfe987][^key-57c2bd3aa8c6f399f33462bcf3052753]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any member or officer of a local authority who is authorised by that authority to prosecute or defend on their behalf, or to appear on their behalf in, proceedings before a magistrates’ court shall be entitled to prosecute or defend or to appear in any such proceedings, and, to conduct any such proceedings.[^c19655681]

(2)[^c15119541][^c15119551][^key-2f0c2f65835a1933635b55e6c97b93dc][^key-5a4bb3336f3ecbdbd2b08b3b90744667][^key-0898547ee27ecf00537fd991e945ca96] In this section “**local authority**” includes the Common Council , a corporate joint committee[^M_F_c3564eb5-4a23-4066-d558-67120f68f8b8] , a joint authority,[^c15119561] an economic prosperity board, a combined authority,[^c20510421] a combined county authority,[^key-7a6b26238619c5a5bd3d6743feee1088] a fire and rescue authority created by an order under section 4A of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004,[^key-0ae8896d1345ede0ccef23fb0080b8d0] [^key-f3bf424ae689422097a70ff098768976]... the Greater London Authority[^c15119571] and[^c15119581] , a[^c15119591] police and crime commissioner and the Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime[^key-2c7c7987a96addeea18448e2d36222f1] [^M_F_27a0810a-956b-4a1c-ae9a-2ae2975a7b84]...
