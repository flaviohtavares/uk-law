## SCHEDULE 19 — AMENDMENTS OF ENACTMENTS RELATING TO ROAD TRAFFIC AND ABANDONED VEHICLES

*Extent: E+W*

Section 186.

### PART I — THE ROAD TRAFFIC ACT 1972[^c15135461]

**1.**[^c15135471] In section 31(7), for the words “county borough or county district” there shall be substituted the word “ county ”.

**2.**[^c15135481] In section 33(4), for the words “of a borough or of an urban district” there shall be substituted the words “ or of a London borough ”.

**3.**[^c15135491] In section 35(5), in paragraph (a) for the words from “county borough” in the first place where they occur to the end of the paragraph there shall be substituted the words “ London borough ”, and the words “and in this subsection “county borough” includes a London borough” shall be omitted.

**4.**[^c15135501] In section 38, subsection (3) shall be omitted and in subsection (5)(a) for the words “a borough or an urban district” there shall be substituted the words “ or London borough ”.

**5.**[^c15135511] In section 43(3) for the words “of a borough, of an urban district” there shall be substituted the words “ of a district or London borough ”.

**6.**[^c15135521] In section 196(1) in the definition of “highway authority”, the words “the council of a county borough, the council of a non-county borough or an urban district” shall be omitted.
