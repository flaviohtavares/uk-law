# Local Government Act 1972, SCHEDULE 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/70
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 18 — AMENDMENTS OF TOWN DEVELOPMENT ACT 1952[^c15135371][^c15135381]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 185.

**1.**[^c15135391] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**2.**[^c15135401] In section 4, in subsection (1) for the words “county borough of county district” there shall be substituted the words “county or district or the Greater London Council”.

**3.**[^c15135411] In section 7, for paragraphs (a) to (c) there shall be substituted the following paragraphs:—

> - (a) the council of a district which is not a receiving district;
> - (b) the Greater London Council;
> - (c) the council of a county, or;

and in paragraph (d) after the words “1945” there shall be inserted the words “under section 181 of the Local Government Act 1972”.

**4.**[^c15135421] In section 8(1), [^c15135431]. . . in paragraph (c) for the words “county borough” in each place where they occur, there shall be substituted the word “district”.

**5.**[^c15135441] In section 10(3), for the words “county borough or county district” there shall be substituted the words “county or district or the Greater London Council”.

**6.**[^c15135451] In section 11, for the words from “of the county” to “it is carried out” there shall be substituted the words “of a county” and for the words “by the council of a county borough” there shall be substituted the words “other district council”.
