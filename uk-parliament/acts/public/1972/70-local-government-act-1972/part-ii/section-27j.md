# Local Government Act 1972, section 27J

## 27J (Extent: W.). Orders dissolving a group of communities (Extent: W.)[^c-3a8b2feef40892a0788d380e05ad377f]

*Extent: E+W.*

(1 (Extent: W.)) This section applies where the council for a group of communities applies to the principal council within whose area the communities lie for an order dissolving the group. *(Extent: W.)*

(2 (Extent: W.)) The principal council must consider whether is it satisfied that— *(Extent: W.)*

- (a (Extent: W.)) the conditions in section 27I are met; and *(Extent: W.)*
- (b (Extent: W.)) any relevant requirements of Schedule 12 have been met. *(Extent: W.)*

(3 (Extent: W.)) If the council is so satisfied, the council must make the order applied for (but this is subject to subsection (4)). *(Extent: W.)*

(4 (Extent: W.)) The order shall make such provision as appears to the principal council to be necessary for the election of a community council for any of the communities in the group in accordance with this Act and Part I of the Representation of the People Act 1983. *(Extent: W.)*
