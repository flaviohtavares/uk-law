## 27M (Extent: W.). Power of Welsh Ministers to alter voting thresholds in connection with organisation of community councils (Extent: W.)[^c-11a9690044064f75a4da2f43a5f1f8ee]

*Extent: E+W.*

(1 (Extent: W.)) The Welsh Ministers may by order amend the following provisions of this Act— *(Extent: W.)*

- (a (Extent: W.)) section 27A(3) and (6); *(Extent: W.)*
- (b (Extent: W.)) section 27C(3) and (6); *(Extent: W.)*
- (c (Extent: W.)) section 27E(3) and (6); *(Extent: W.)*
- (d (Extent: W.)) section 27G(3), (4), (6) and (7); *(Extent: W.)*
- (e (Extent: W.)) section 27I(3) and (6); *(Extent: W.)*
- (f (Extent: W.)) section 27K(3) and (6). *(Extent: W.)*

(2 (Extent: W.)) That power includes power to amend provision previously made by an order under subsection (1). *(Extent: W.)*

(3 (Extent: W.)) No order may be made under subsection (1) unless the Welsh Ministers have carried out such consultation as they consider appropriate with the following— *(Extent: W.)*

- (a (Extent: W.)) principal councils in Wales or a body representative of such councils; and *(Extent: W.)*
- (b (Extent: W.)) community councils in Wales or a body representative of such councils. *(Extent: W.)*

(4 (Extent: W.)) The power of the Welsh Ministers to make an order under subsection (1) is exercisable by statutory instrument. *(Extent: W.)*

(5 (Extent: W.)) A statutory instrument which contains an order under subsection (1) may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the National Assembly for Wales. *(Extent: W.)*
