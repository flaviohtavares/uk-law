# Local Government Act 1972, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/70
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. Term of office and retirement of councillors.

*Extent: W*

(1) Councillors for a principal area shall be elected by the local government electors for that area in accordance with this Act , Part 1[^key-7f7e92b45e57d4e9823c43f2069b12f3] of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^c15088981] , and Part 1 of the Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021[^key-192aa0dcd292309b042519bde8205ffa].

(2)[^c15088991] For the purpose of the election of councillors, every principal area in Wales shall be divided into electoral wards[^key-764bb60eed55b80350ab7162764f1252], each returning such number of councillors as may be provided by an order under paragraph 2 of Schedule 5 to this Act or under or by virtue of the provisions of Part IV of this Act or Part 3 of the[^key-3bfd079223dbc1e858481e42492447fc] Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru etc. Act 2013[^key-0dc4d4c865cb4a87ea1b40d2bd0fcfab] , or by regulations under paragraph 9 or 10 of Schedule 1 to the Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021[^key-ab5e0e939b3674f14f4e99ead6f13efc].

(3) There shall be a separate election for each electoral ward[^key-67dffd2e3c37fc9d0a81357c6022416d].
