# Local Government Act 1972, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/70
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Provision supplementary to sections 27A to 27L (Extent: W).[^c15089401][^c-c830218b4cbb7056a8ca814c710e55ed]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An order made by a principal[^c15089411] council under section 27B, 27D, 27F, 27H, 27J or 27L[^c-e17ed20ef62abee3494a5b29b3c23b20] above may contain such incidental, consequential, transitional or supplementary provision as may appear to the principal council to be necessary or proper for the purposes or in consequence of the order or for giving full effect thereto, and may include provision with respect to the transfer and management or custody of property (whether real or personal) and the transfer of rights and liabilities.

(2) Where any such order is made, section 44 of the[^c-651b87d64efa77e06a22b6bd5b03ec12] Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru etc. Act 2013[^c-6a513123c737d00fb1c42a49e8299239] is to apply as if the order were made under Part 3 of that Act.

(3)[^c15089441] Two copies of every such order shall be sent to the Secretary of State.
