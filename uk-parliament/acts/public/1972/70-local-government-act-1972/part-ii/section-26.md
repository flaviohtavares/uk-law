# Local Government Act 1972, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/70
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 26. Elections of councillors.[^c15089041]

*Extent: W*

(1) The ordinary elections of councillors of the new principal councils shall take place in 2022[^c-075ee371778555f575bbeba2116deae8] and in every fifth[^c-429a605242e1e35dbf4682285fb32257] year after 2022.

(2) The term of office of every such councillor shall be five[^c-b9fbe1ecc64b4963556dece120f98707] years.

(3) On the fourth day after any such ordinary election—

- (a) the persons who were councillors immediately before the election shall retire; and
- (b) the newly elected councillors shall assume office.
