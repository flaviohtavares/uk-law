# Local Government Act 1972, section 27D

## 27D (Extent: W.). Orders dissolving separate community councils for communities (Extent: W.)[^key-f1ae539771c6d4b896e80257a1d8a45f]

*Extent: E+W.*

(1 (Extent: W.)) This section applies where a community meeting of a community which has a separate council applies to the principal council within whose area it lies for an order dissolving the council for the community. *(Extent: W.)*

(2 (Extent: W.)) The principal council must consider whether it is satisfied that— *(Extent: W.)*

- (a (Extent: W.)) the conditions in section 27C are met; and *(Extent: W.)*
- (b (Extent: W.)) any relevant requirements of Schedule 12 have been met. *(Extent: W.)*

(3 (Extent: W.)) If the council is so satisfied, the council must make the order applied for. *(Extent: W.)*
