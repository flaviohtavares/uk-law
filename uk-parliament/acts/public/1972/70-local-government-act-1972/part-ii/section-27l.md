## 27L (Extent: W.). Orders separating a community from a group of communities (Extent: W.)[^c-dcf7f4348e9cad19f3df5e242b69f8b3]

*Extent: E+W.*

(1 (Extent: W.)) This section applies where a community meeting of a community included in a group of communities applies to the principal council within whose area the community lies for an order separating the community from the group. *(Extent: W.)*

(2 (Extent: W.)) The principal council must consider whether is it satisfied that— *(Extent: W.)*

- (a (Extent: W.)) the conditions in section 27K are met; and *(Extent: W.)*
- (b (Extent: W.)) any relevant requirements of Schedule 12 have been met. *(Extent: W.)*

(3 (Extent: W.)) If the council is so satisfied, the council must make the order applied for (but this is subject to subsection (4)). *(Extent: W.)*

(4 (Extent: W.)) The order shall make such provision as appears to the principal council to be necessary for the election of a community council for the community in accordance with this Act and Part I of the Representation of the People Act 1983. *(Extent: W.)*
