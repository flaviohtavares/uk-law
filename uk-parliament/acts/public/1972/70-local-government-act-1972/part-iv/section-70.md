# Local Government Act 1972, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/70

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 70. Restriction on promotion of Bills for changing local government areas, etc.[^c15091671][^c15091681][^c15091691][^c-8768b12e3da7ac5474879ae4305a3f47]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c15091701] No[^c15091721][^c15091711] local authority in England *(Extent: W)*[^c-373ce63e0df3b8a4eec83cc380fda930], joint authority[^c19640381] , economic prosperity board[^c20510821] , combined authority or combined county authority[^c-98935ab019cfc21894f2d7bef16baf1a][^c15091731]... shall have power to promote a bill for forming or abolishing any local government area [^c15091741]... or for altering, or altering the status or electoral arrangements of, any local government area [^c15091741]. . .

(2)[^c-eb93d3433ddb7b6d22f0e52aa35afab2] Subsection (1) above shall have effect as if the reference to a joint authority included a reference to the London Fire Commissioner.

(3)[^c15091771] No local authority in England *(Extent: W)*[^c-0f1b9243fffb1815d7df34b05198ce83], joint authority[^c19640391] , economic prosperity board , combined authority or combined county authority[^c-9d8afac9a806bbb5c53bde06305d9ebf] shall have power to promote a Bill for forming, altering or abolishing executive arrangements [^c-ecbeb9b1926004740a51adf90eeb0855]..., or for altering arrangements for electing an elected mayor.
