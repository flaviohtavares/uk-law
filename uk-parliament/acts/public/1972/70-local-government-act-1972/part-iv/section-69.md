## 69 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Variation and revocation of orders under Part IV, etc.[^c15091391][^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] The power conferred by section 266 below to vary and revoke orders under this Act shall, in the case of orders under this Part of this Act, apply only in relation to any supplementary provision contained in any such order, and an order varying or revoking any such provision shall only be made after compliance with subsections (2) and (3) below. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] The Secretary of State or *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] Welsh principal[^c15091401] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] council proposing to make any such varying or revoking order shall prepare a draft of the order, shall send copies of the draft to such local or public authorities as appear to him or them to be concerned, and shall give public notice, in such manner as appears to him or them sufficient for informing persons likely to be concerned, that the draft has been prepared, that a copy of the draft is available for inspection at a place specified in the notice and that representations with respect to the draft may be made to him or them within two months of the publication of the notice. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] The Secretary of State or *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] Welsh principal *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] council shall consider any representations duly made with respect to the draft and may, if he or they think fit, make an order either in the form of the draft or subject to modifications. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded]

(4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] The Secretary of State or a *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] Welsh principal[^c15091421] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] council may cause a local inquiry to be held with respect to the draft and section 250(2), (3) and (5) below shall apply in relation to an inquiry held under this subsection by a *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] [^c15091431]. . . council with the substitution for references to a Minister of references to the council. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded]

(5 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] Any supplementary provision contained in an order made by a Minister of the Crown under any of the following enactments (being enactments making provision corresponding to some or all of the foregoing provisions of this Part of this Act), that is to say— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] section 46 of the *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] [^c15091441]Local Government Act 1929; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded]

- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] section 6 of the 1963 Act; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded]
- (e (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] any enactment repealed by the 1933 Act and corresponding to any enactment in the said Part VI; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded]

may be varied or revoked by an order made by the Secretary of State, and subsections (2) to (4) above shall apply in relation to any such order as they apply in relation to orders varying or revoking orders under this Part of this Act. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded]

(6 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] Any supplementary provision contained in an order made under any of the enactments mentioned in subsection (5) above by *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] any predecessor of a Welsh principal council[^c15091461] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] may be varied or revoked in relation to any *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] Welsh principal area[^c15091471] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] to which or part of which that provision relates by an order made by the council of *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded] that area[^c15091481] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded], and subsections (2) to (4) above shall apply with all necessary modifications in relation to any such order as they apply in relation to orders varying or revoking orders under this Part of this Act. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6b4233beee2b0fe04e07ee444d4dded]
