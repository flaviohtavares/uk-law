## 78. Supplementary.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part of this Act—

- “ *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9047775972e327b0e112a8f0542ba112]**electoral arrangements *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9047775972e327b0e112a8f0542ba112]**” means— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9047775972e327b0e112a8f0542ba112]
  - (a) in relation to a principal area, the number of councillors of the council for that area, the number and boundaries of the electoral areas into which that area is for the time being divided for the purpose of the election of councillors, the number of councillors to be elected for any electoral area in that principal area and the name of any electoral area; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9047775972e327b0e112a8f0542ba112]
  - (b) in relation to a parish or community council or a common parish or community council, the number of councillors, the question whether the parish or community or any parish or community, as the case may be, should or should not be or continue to be divided into wards for the purpose of the election of councillors, the number and boundaries of any such wards, the number of councillors to be elected for any such ward or in the case of a common parish or community council for each parish or community and the name of any such ward; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9047775972e327b0e112a8f0542ba112]
  - (c) [^c15092381]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9047775972e327b0e112a8f0542ba112]
- “**local government area**” includes the City, the Inner Temple and the Middle Temple;
- [^c18845781]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. . .
- “**substantive change**” has the meaning assigned to it by section 54(1)(e)[^c15092371] above.

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30a09939d1be351b2b35954abb371a57] In considering the electoral arrangements for local government areas for the purposes of this Part of this Act, the Secretary of State, *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30a09939d1be351b2b35954abb371a57] the Welsh Commission[^c15092391] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30a09939d1be351b2b35954abb371a57] and every *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30a09939d1be351b2b35954abb371a57] Welsh principal council and[^c15092401] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30a09939d1be351b2b35954abb371a57] district council shall so far as is reasonably practicable comply with the rules set out *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30a09939d1be351b2b35954abb371a57] in Schedule 11 to this Act[^c15092411] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30a09939d1be351b2b35954abb371a57]. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30a09939d1be351b2b35954abb371a57]
