# Local Government Act 1972, section 274

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/70
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 274. Short title and extent.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Local Government Act 1972.

(2) [^c18868001]. . . this Act shall not extend to Scotland.

(3) [^c15129821]. . ., this Act shall not extend to Northern Ireland.
