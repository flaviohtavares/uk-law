# Local Government Act 1972, section 266

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/70

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 266. Orders, rules and regulations.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any power to make orders, rules or regulations conferred on any Minister or the Treasury by any provision of this Act other than section 261 above *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccdf4c75750d61b2ba31f501474da8fb] other than section 128A(1) or paragraph 3 of Schedule 3 or paragraph 2 of Schedule 5 to this Act shall be exercisable by statutory instrument.

(2) Any power to make an Order in Council or other order under any provision of this Act shall include power to make an order varying or revoking any order previously made under that provision.
