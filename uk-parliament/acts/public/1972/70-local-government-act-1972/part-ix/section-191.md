# Local Government Act 1972, section 191

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1972/70

## 191. Ordnance survey.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In its application outside Greater London, the [^c15116231]Ordnance Survey Act 1841 (in this section referred to as “**the 1841 Act**”) shall have effect subject to the following modifications.

(2) An application under section 1 of the 1841 Act shall be sent to the proper officer of either a county council or a district council (or, in Wales, a principal council)[^c15116241] and, where such an application is made, the function of appointing a person to assist in examining, ascertaining and marking out reputed boundaries shall be exercisable by the council to whose proper officer the application was sent.

(3)[^c15116251] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) References, in whatever terms, in the 1841 Act—

- (a) to the justices by whom a person is appointed under section 1 of that Act shall be construed as references to the county council or the district council, as the case may require or, in Wales, the principal council), and
- (b) to the clerk of the peace for a county shall be construed as references to the proper officer of the county council or the district council as the case may require (or, in Wales, the principal council).

(5) [^c18866551]. . . References in that Act to a county include references to any preserved county or[^c15116281] local government area within the meaning of this Act.
