## 202. Public transport in passenger transport areas.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c15117101] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) [^c15117111]. . ., Part II of the [^c15117121]Transport Act 1968 shall have effect in its application to England and Wales subject to the amendments specified in Part I of Schedule 24 to this Act, being amendments—

- (a) to assimilate in certain respects the provisions of the said Part II to those of Part II of the [^c15117131]Transport (London) Act 1969;
- (b) to make further provision with respect to the control of a Passenger Transport Executive by the Passenger Transport Authority; and
- (c) to remove, or to transfer to the Passenger Transport Authority, certain functions originally conferred on the Secretary of State.

(3) [^c15117141]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) Expressions used in this section have the same meanings as in the [^c15117191]Transport Act 1968.
