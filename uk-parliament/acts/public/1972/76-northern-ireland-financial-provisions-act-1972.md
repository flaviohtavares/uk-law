# Northern Ireland (Financial Provisions) Act 1972

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1993-11-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Northern Ireland (Financial Provisions) Act 1972 [^c824261](repealed 5.11.1993)

1972 c.76

**No longer in force (ended 1993-11-05).**

## 1. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c824262]

## 2. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c824263]

## 3. Laying of certain Northern Ireland accounts and reports.

There shall be laid before the House of Commons of the Parliament of the United Kingdom copies of such of the accounts and reports required by section 10, 11, 19, 21 or 22 of the [^c824264]Exchequer and Audit Act (Northern Ireland) 1921 to be laid before (or presented or made to) the House of Commons of the Parliament of Northern Ireland as relate to a financial year ending after the coming into force of section 1 of the [^c824265]Northern Ireland (Temporary Provisions) Act 1972 and before the expiry of that section.

## 4. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Financial Provisions) Act 1972.
