# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, section 262B

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 262B. \[Formulation and publication of proposals for preservation and enhancement of conservation areas.[^c14937831]

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

(1) It shall be the duty of a planning authority to formulate and publish, from time to time[^c14937841] proposals for the preservation and enhancement of any parts of their district which are conservation areas.

(2) Proposals under this section shall be submitted for consideration to a public meeting in the area to which they relate; and the planning authority shall have regard to any views concerning the proposals expressed by persons attending the meeting.
