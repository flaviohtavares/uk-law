# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, section 277

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 277. Transitional provisions, savings and repeals.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

(1) The transitional provisions and savings contained in Schedule 22 to this Act shall have effect.

(2) Subject to the provisions of that Schedule, the enactments specified in Schedule 23 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.

(3) The inclusion in this Act of any express savings, transitional provision or amendment shall not be taken as prejudicing the operation of sections 16(1) and 17(2)(a) of the[^c14946941] [^c14946951]Interpretation Act 1978 (which relate to the effect of repeals).
