# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, section 281

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 281. Citations and extent.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

(1) This Act may be cited as the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972.

(2) The [^c14947081]Town and Country Planning (Amendment) Act 1972 and this Act may be cited together as the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Acts 1972.

(3) This Act, except so far as it provides for Joint Planning Inquiry Commissions. . . [^c14947091], extends to Scotland only.
