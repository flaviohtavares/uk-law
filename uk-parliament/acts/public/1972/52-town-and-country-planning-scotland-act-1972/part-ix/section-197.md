# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, section 197

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 197. No withdrawal of constructive notice to treat.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

Without prejudice to the provisions of section 187(1) of this Act, the power conferred by section 39 of the [^c14931261]Land Compensation (Scotland) Act 1963 to withdraw a notice to treat shall not be exercisable in the case of a notice to treat which is deemed to have been served by virtue of any of the provisions of this Part of this Act.
