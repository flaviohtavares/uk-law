# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, section 205A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 205A. Further procedure in anticipation of planning permission etc.[^c14933081]

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

(1) Where—

- (a) a planning authority would, if planning permission for any development had been granted under Part III of this Act, have power to make an order under section 198A of this Act authorising the stopping-up or diversion of a road[^c14933091] in order to enable that development to be carried out; [^c14933101]
- (b)

[^c14933101]then, notwithstanding that such permission has not been granted, the [^c14933121] authority may, in the circumstances specified in subsections (2) to (4) of this section, publish notice of the draft of such an order in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 18 to this Act; but nothing in the said schedule shall be construed as authorising the [^c14933121] authority to make the order in anticipation of such permission.

(2) The [^c14933121] authority may publish such a notice as aforesaid where the [^c14933121] development is the subject of an application for planning permission.

(3) The [^c14933121] authority may publish such a notice as aforesaid where—

- (a) the [^c14933121] development is to be carried out by a local authority or[^c14933181], statutory undertakers [^c14933191] and requires, by virtue of an enactment, the authorisation of a government department; and
- (b) the developers have made an application to the department for that authorisation and also requested a direction under section 37 of this Act, [^c14933191], that planning permission be deemed to be granted for that development.

(4) The planning authority may publish such a notice as aforesaid where they have begun to take such steps, in accordance with regulations made by virtue of section 256 of this Act, as are requisite in order to enable them to obtain planning permission for the [^c14933121] development.
