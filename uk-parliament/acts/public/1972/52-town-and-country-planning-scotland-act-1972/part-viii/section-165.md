# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, section 165

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 165. Compensation for restrictions on advertising.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

Where, for the purpose of complying with any regulations made under section 61 of this Act, works are carried out by any person—

- (a) for removing an advertisement which was being displayed on 16th August 1948; or
- (b) for discontinuing the use for the display of advertisements of a site used for that purpose on that date,

that person shall, on a claim made to the. . . [^c14928261] planning authority within the time and in the manner prescribed by regulations under this Act, be entitled to recover from that authority compensation in respect of any expenses reasonably incurred by him in that behalf.
