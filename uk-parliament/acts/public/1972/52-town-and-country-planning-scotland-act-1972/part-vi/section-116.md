# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, section 116

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 116. Management etc. of listed buildings acquired by. . . planning authority or Secretary of State.[^c14926421]

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

(1) Where a. . . [^c14926421] planning authority acquire any building or other land under section 104(1) or 109(1)(b) of this Act, they may make such arrangements as to its management, use or disposal as they consider appropriate for the purpose of its preservation.

(2) Where the Secretary of State acquires any building or other land under section 104(2) of this Act, subsection (3) of section 5 of the [^c14926441]Historic Buildings and Ancient Monuments Act 1953 (management, custody and disposal), except so much of it as refers to subsection (4) of that section, shall apply in relation thereto as it applies in relation to property acquired under that section.
