# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, SCHEDULE 21

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 21 — Consequential Amendments

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

Sections 18 and 276.

### Part I — The Land Compensation (Scotland) Act 1963 (c. 51)[^c14950231]

In the Land Compensation (Scotland) Act 1963 any reference to an area defined in the current development plan as an area of comprehensive development shall be construed as a reference to an action area for which a local plan is in force or to a comprehensive development area as the case may be.[^c14950241].
