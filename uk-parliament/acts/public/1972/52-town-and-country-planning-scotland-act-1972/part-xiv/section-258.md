# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, section 258

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 258. Special provisions as to statutory undertakers who are. . . planning authorities.[^c14937431]

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

In relation to statutory undertakers who are. . . [^c14937431] planning authorities, section 230 of this Act and the provisions specified in subsection (2) of that section shall have effect subject to such exceptions and modifications as may be prescribed by regulations made under this Act.
