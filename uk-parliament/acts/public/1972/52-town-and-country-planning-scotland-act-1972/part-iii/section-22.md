# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, section 22

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Form and content of applications.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

.0. . .[^c14914921] Any application to a. . . [^c14914931] planning authority for planning permission shall be made in such manner as may be prescribed by regulations under this Act or by a development order[^c14914941], and shall include such particulars, and be verified by such evidence, as may be required by—[^c14914951]

- (a) the regulations; or
- (b) the development order; or
- (c) directions given by the planning authority under the said regulations or the said development order.
