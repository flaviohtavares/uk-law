# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, SCHEDULE 20

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 20 — Enactments Exempted from Section 273(6) of this Act

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

Section 273(6).

### 1.

Section 32(1) of the [^c14950191]Public Health (Scotland) Act 1897.

### 2.

Section 158 of the [^c14950201]Burgh Police (Scotland) Act 1892, as extended by section 104(2)(h) of the [^c14950211]Burgh Police (Scotland) Act 1903.

### 3. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c14950221]

### 4.

Any enactment making such provision as might by virtue of any Act of Parliament have been made in relation to the area to which the order applies by means of a byelaw, order or regulation not requiring confirmation by Parliament.

### 5.

Any enactment which has been previously excluded or modified by a development order, and any enactment having substantially the same effect as any such enactment.
