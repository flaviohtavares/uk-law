# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, section 83A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 83A. Expressions used in connection with enforcement.[^c14922551]

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

(1) For the purposes of this Act—

- (a) carrying out development without the required planning permission; or
- (b) failing to comply with any condition or limitation subject to which planning permission has been granted,

constitutes a breach of planning control.

(2) For the purposes of this Act—

- (a) the issue of an enforcement notice (defined in section 84 of this Act); or
- (b) the service of a breach of condition notice (defined in section 87AA of this Act),

constitutes taking enforcement action.

(3) In this Part of this Act “**planning permission**” includes planning permission under Part III of the [^c14922571]Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1947.
