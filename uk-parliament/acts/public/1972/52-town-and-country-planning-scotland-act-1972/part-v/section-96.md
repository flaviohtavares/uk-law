# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, section 96

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 96. Enforcement by, or by direction of, the Secretary of State.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

(1) If it appears to the Secretary of State, after consultation with the. . . [^c14925041] planning authority, to be expedient that a listed building enforcement notice should be served in respect of any land, he. . . [^c14925051] may himself serve such a notice; and any notice so served by the Secretary of State shall have the like effect as a notice served by the. . . [^c14925041] planning authority.

(2) In relation to a listed building enforcement notice served by the Secretary of State, the provisions of sections 94(3) and 95 of this Act shall apply as if for any reference therein to the. . . [^c14925041] planning authority there were substituted a reference to the Secretary of State.
