# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972, section 91

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 91. Grant of certificate by Secretary of State on referred application or appeal against refusal.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

(1) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c14924431]

(2) Where an application is made to a. . . [^c14924441] planning authority for a certificate under section 90 or 90A[^c14924451] and is refused, or is refused in part, the applicant may by notice under this subsection appeal to the Secretary of State; and on any such appeal the Secretary of State shall—

- (a) if and so far as he is satisfied that the authority’s refusal is not well-founded, grant to the appellant an established use certificate accordingly or, as the case may be, modify the certificate granted by the authority on the application; and
- (b) if and so far as he is satisfied that the authority’s refusal is well-founded, dismiss the appeal.

(3)[^c14924461] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Before determining an. . . [^c14924471] appeal under this section the Secretary of State shall, if either the. . . [^c14924471] appellant. . . [^c14924471] or the. . . [^c14924441] planning authority so desire, afford to each of them an opportunity of appearing before, and being heard by, a person appointed by the Secretary of State for the purpose.

(5)[^c14924461] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Subject to section 279 of this Act, Schedule 7 to this Act applies to appeals under this section.

(7)[^c14924521] Where the Secretary of State or a person appointed by him under Schedule 7 to this Act to determine an appeal grants a certificate under section 90 or 90A of this Act, the Secretary of State or that person shall give notice to the planning authority of that fact.
