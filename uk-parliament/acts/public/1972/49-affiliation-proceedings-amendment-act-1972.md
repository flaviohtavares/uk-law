Affiliation Proceedings (Amendment) Act 1972

1972 c. 49

An Act to amend the law relating to proceedings for an affiliation order or for the variation, revocation or revival of such an order, and for connected purposes.

## 4. Short title, extent and commencement.[^c868958]

(1) This Act may be cited as the Affiliation Proceedings (Amendment) Act 1972.

(2) This Act shall not extend to Scotland or [^c868959]. . . to Northern Ireland.

(3) This Act shall come into force at the expiration of the period of three months beginning with the day on which it is passed.
