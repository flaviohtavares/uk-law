# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 141

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 141. Expenses and receipts

(1) Any administrative expenses incurred by the Secretary of State in consequence of any provisions of this Part of this Act shall be defrayed out of moneys provided by Parliament.

(2) Any sums received on account of fees payable by virtue of any provision of this Part of this Act shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund.
