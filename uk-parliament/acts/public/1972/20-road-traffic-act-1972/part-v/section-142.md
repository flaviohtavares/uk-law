# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 142

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 142. Regulations for purposes of Part V

The Secretary of State may make regulations for any purpose for which provision is by this Part of this Act authorised to be made by regulations, and in the said Part " regulations " means regulations made under this section.
