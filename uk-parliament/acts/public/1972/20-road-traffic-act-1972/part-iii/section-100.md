# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 100

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 100. Arrest without warrant of persons driving while disqualified

A constable in uniform may arrest without warrant any person driving or attempting to drive a motor vehicle on a road whom he has reasonable cause to suspect of being disqualified.
