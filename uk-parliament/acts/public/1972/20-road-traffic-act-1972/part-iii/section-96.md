# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 96

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 96. Disqualification of persons under age

A person who under section 4 of this Act is prohibited by reason of his age from driving a motor vehicle or a motor vehicle of any class is disqualified for holding or obtaining a licence other than a licence authorising him to drive such motor vehicles, if any, as he is not by the said section 4 forbidden to drive.
