# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 186

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 186. General power to hold inquiries

Without prejudice to any other provision of this Act, the Secretary of State may hold inquiries for the purposes of this Act.
