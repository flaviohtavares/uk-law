# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 195

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 195. Interpretation of statutory references to carriages

A motor vehicle or trailer shall be deemed to be a carriage within the meaning of any Act of Parliament, whether a public general Act or a local Act, and of any rule, regulation or byelaw made under any Act of Parliament, and if used as a carriage of any particular class shall for the purpose of any enactment relating to carriages of any particular class be deemed to be a carriage of that class.
