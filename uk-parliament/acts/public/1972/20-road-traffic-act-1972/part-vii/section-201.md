# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 201

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 201. Provisions as to Thames embankment

Section 41 of the [^m00101]Thames Embankment Act 1862 shall not apply to motor tractors, heavy motor cars, motor cars, motor cycles or invalid carriages, but save as aforesaid nothing in this Act shall affect the provisions of that section.
