# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 202

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 202. Protection of public interests

It is hereby declared that nothing in Part IV of this Act is to be treated as conferring on the holder of a licence granted under that Part any right to the continuance of any benefits arising from, or from a licence granted under, that Part, or from any conditions attached to any such licence.
