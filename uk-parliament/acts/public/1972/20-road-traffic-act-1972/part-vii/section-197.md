# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 197

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 197. Effect of certain references to Scottish local authorities

Where any powers and duties are by this Act conferred or imposed, in relation to Scotland, on county councils and on the town councils of certain burghs only, all other burghs shall, for the purposes of those powers and duties, be deemed to be within the county.
