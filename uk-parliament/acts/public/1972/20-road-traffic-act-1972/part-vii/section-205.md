# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 205

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 205. General repeals, revocations, savings and transitional provisions

(1) The enactments specified in Part I of Schedule 9 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Part of that Schedule, and the orders specified in Part II of that Schedule are hereby revoked to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.

(2) The saving and transitional provisions contained in Schedule 10 to this Act shall have effect.
