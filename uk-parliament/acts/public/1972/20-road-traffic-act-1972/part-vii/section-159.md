# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 159

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 159. Power of police to stop vehicles

A person driving a motor vehicle on a road and a person riding a cycle, not being a motor vehicle, on a road shall stop the same on being so required by a constable in uniform, and if he fails to do so he shall be guilty of an offence.
