# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 178

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 178. Penalty for breach of regulations

If a person acts in contravention of or fails to comply with any regulations made by the Secretary of State under this Act (other than regulations made under section 20, 43, 54, including that section as applied by section 55, or 133 thereof) and contravention thereof, or failure to comply therewith, is not made an offence under any other provision of this Act, he shall for each offence be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding £20.
