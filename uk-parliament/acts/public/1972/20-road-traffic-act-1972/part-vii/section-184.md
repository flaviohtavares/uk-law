# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 184

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 184. Jurisdiction of courts of summary jurisdiction in Scotland for certain offences

An offence under this Act, or any regulations made under this Act, for which the maximum penalty does not exceed £50 (other than an offence under section 54 (including that section as applied by section 55(3)), 61, 136 or 137 of this Act or an offence involving discretionary disqualification within the meaning of Part III of this Act) may be prosecuted in any court of summary jurisdiction within the meaning of the [^m00087]Summary Jurisdiction (Scotland) Act 1954 having jurisdiction in the place where the offence was committed.
