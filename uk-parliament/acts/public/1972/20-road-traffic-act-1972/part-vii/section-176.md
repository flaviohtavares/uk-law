# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 176

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 176. Penalty for aiding, abetting, etc. commission of offences

As respects Scotland, a person who aids, abets, counsels, procures or incites any other person to commit an offence against the provisions of this Act or any regulations made thereunder shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to the same punishment as might be imposed on conviction of the first-mentioned offence.
