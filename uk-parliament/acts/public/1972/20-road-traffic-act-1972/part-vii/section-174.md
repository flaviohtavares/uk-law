# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 174

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 174. Personation of, or of person employed by, authorised examiner

A person shall be guilty of an- offence if, with intent to deceive, he falsely represents himself to be, or to be employed by, a person authorised by the Secretary of State for the purposes of section 43 of this Act.
