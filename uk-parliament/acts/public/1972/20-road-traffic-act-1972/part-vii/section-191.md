# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 191

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 191. Articulated vehicles

A vehicle so constructed that it can be divided into two parts both of which are vehicles and one of which is a motor vehicle shall (when not so divided) be treated for the purposes of this Act as that motor vehicle with the other part attached as a trailer.
