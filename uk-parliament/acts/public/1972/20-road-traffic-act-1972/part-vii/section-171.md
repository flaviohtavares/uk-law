# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 171

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 171. Issue of false documents

A person shall be guilty of an offence who issues any such document as is referred to in paragraph (a) or (b) of section 170(6) of this Act, or a test certificate or manufacturer's certificate (within the meaning of Part II of this Act) if the document or certificate so issued is to his knowledge false in a material particular.
