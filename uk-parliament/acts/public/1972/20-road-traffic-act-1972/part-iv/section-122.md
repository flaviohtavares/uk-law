# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 122

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 122. Transitional provisions as to certain heavy goods vehicle drivers' licences

The provisions of Schedule 5 to this Act shall have effect in connection with the coming into operation of section 112 of this Act.
