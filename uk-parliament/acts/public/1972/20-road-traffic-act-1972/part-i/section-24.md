# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Leaving vehicles in dangerous positions

If a person in charge of a vehicle causes or permits the vehicle or a trailer drawn thereby to remain at rest on a road in such a position or in such condition or in such circumstances as to be likely to cause danger to other persons using the road, he shall be guilty of an offence.
