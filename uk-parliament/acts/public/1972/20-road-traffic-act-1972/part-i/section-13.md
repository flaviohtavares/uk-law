# Road Traffic Act 1972, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Person liable to be charged with offence under ss.5, 6 or 9 not liable to be charged with certain other offences

A person liable to be charged with an offence under section 5, 6 or 9 of this Act shall not be liable to be charged—

- (a) under section 12 of the [^m00006]Licensing Act 1872, with the offence of being drunk while in charge, on a highway or other public place, of a carriage, or
- (b) under section 70 of the [^m00007]Licensing (Scotland) Act 1903, with the offence of being drunk while in charge, in a street or other place, of a carriage.
