# Post Office Act 1969, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2013-06-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1969/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Rights and liabilities, &c., arising out of Acts authorising the acquisition of land or the execution of works.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any provision of the Sites Acts, the [^c11854531]Post Office Extension Act 1865, the [^c11854541]Manchester Post Office Act 1876, the [^c11854551]Post Office (London) Railway Act 1913, the [^c11854561]Post Office (Site and Railway) Act 1954, the [^c11854571]Post Office Works Act 1959 or the [^c11854581]Post Office Subway Act 1966 which is in force immediately before the appointed day and contains references to the Postmaster General shall, so far as necessary for preserving its effect on and after that day, have effect as from that day as if those references were references to the Post Office.

(2) As from the appointed day, any rights or powers vested in, or exercisable by, the Post Office under the enactments relating to telegraphs in respect of works of any kind whatsoever executed under those enactments shall extend to, and may be exercised by it in respect of, the portions of the tube constructed by the Pneumatic Despatch Company, Limited, that vest in the Post Office by virtue of section 16 of this Act.

(3)[^c11854591] Section 20 of the [^c11854601]Bristol Cattle Market Act 1930 (which empowers the Postmaster General, amongst others, to enter into and carry out agreements with reference to any of the matters referred to in that Act or the agreements mentioned in that section) shall, as from the appointed day, have effect with the substitution, for the reference to the Postmaster General, of a reference to the Post Office.

(4) Section 16 of the [^c11854611]City of London (Various Powers) Act 1950 (which provides for the continuance of the making, by the Postmaster General, the Mayor and commonalty and citizens of the city of London acting by the common council and the Mercers’ Company of certain periodic payments to the incumbents and clerks of certain benefices in the city, being payments originally required to be made under enactments repealed by that Act) shall, as from the appointed day, have effect as if, in Schedule 1 to that Act, for references to the Postmaster General, there were substituted references to the Post Office; and the rights of indemnity in favour of the Postmaster General that are saved by the proviso to section 18 of that Act shall, as from that day, enure for the benefit of the Post Office and the charges on property to secure the payment of money payable to the Postmaster General in respect of indemnities so saved which are themselves also saved by that proviso shall, as from that day, have effect accordingly.

(5) The reference in subsection (1) above to the Sites Acts is a reference to the Acts passed during the years 1885 to 1938 (both inclusive) the short title of each of which consists of the words “The Post Office (Sites) Act” followed by the year in which it was passed.
