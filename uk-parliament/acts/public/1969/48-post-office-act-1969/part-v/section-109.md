## 109. Power of the Treasury to raise money under the auspices of the Director of Savings.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The power conferred by section 12 of the [^c11859201]National Loans Act 1968 on the Treasury to raise money shall extend to raising money under the auspices of the Director of Savings and, in particular, by the issue, under his auspices, of national savings certificates; [^c11859211]
