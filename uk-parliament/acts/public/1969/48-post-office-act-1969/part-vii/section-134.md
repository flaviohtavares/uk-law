## 134. Issue by local authorities in Great Britain of dog and game licences.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c] As from the appointed day, licences *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c] for dogs[^c11859761] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c] shall be issued *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c] in Great Britain *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c] by the councils having power to levy the duties *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c] thereon and so, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c] in England and Wales, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c] shall licences *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c] for dealing in, or killing, game. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c] A council *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c] in Great Britain[^c11859801] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c] having power to levy the duties on any such licences as aforesaid may employ persons to act as its agents for the purpose of the issue of the licences and may pay to persons employed so to act such reasonable remuneration as it thinks fit. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2529dd63f58b6020db5f064a1dd358c]
