## 135. Remuneration of the Post Office for issuing dog and game licences in England and Wales.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf] Any sums falling to be paid by the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf] Secretary of State[^c11859921] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf] to *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf] the Post Office company[^c11859931] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf]in pursuance of any agreement to which he, it and a council in England or Wales are parties, being— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf] an agreement providing for the issue by *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf] the Post Office company *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf], on behalf of the council, of licences of all or any of the following kinds, namely, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf] licences for dogs,[^c11859951] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf] licences for dealing in game and licences for killing game, and the payment by that Minister to *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf] the Post Office company *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf] of sums in consideration of its issuing the licences; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf] [^c11859971]. . . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b916d4a27bf22532342ad4cc042afbf]
