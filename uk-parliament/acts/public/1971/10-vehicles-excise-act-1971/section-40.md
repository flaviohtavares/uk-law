# Vehicles (Excise) Act 1971, section 40

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-09-01).**

(1) This Act may be cited as the Vehicles (Excise) Act 1971.

(2)[^c8887401] This Act except section 39(2) and (3) and this section shall come into force on the transfer date.

(3)[^c8887411] This Act extends to Northern Ireland.
