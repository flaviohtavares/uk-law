# Vehicles (Excise) Act 1971, section 35

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Application of fines, penalties and forfeitures.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-09-01).**

(1) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c8886731]

(2) All fines imposed and penalties [^c8886741]. . .recovered under or in pursuance of this Act in England and Wales otherwise than by or before a magistrates’ court, and all fines imposed, and penalties [^c8886741]. . . recovered, under or in pursuance of this Act in Scotland or Northern Ireland[^c8886761], shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund.

(3) Section 151 of the[^c8886771] [^c8886781]Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 (which relates to the application of penalties) shall not apply to penalties recovered under or in pursuance of this Act.
