# Vehicles (Excise) Act 1971, SCHEDULE 2

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Annual Rates of Duty on Hackney Carriages

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-09-01).**

### Part I

#### 1.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The annual rate of duty applicable to a hackney carriage . . . [^c8887651] shall, subject to paragraph 3 below, be the rate specified . . . [^c8887651]in relation to its seating capacity in the Table in[^c8887671] Part II of this Schedule.

#### 2.

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of this Schedule, the seating capacity of a vehicle shall be determined in accordance with regulations under this Act.

#### 3. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c8887681]

#### 4.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Duty shall not be chargeable by virtue of this Schedule in respect of a vehicle chargeable with duty by virtue of Schedule 1 to this Act.

#### 5. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c8887701]

### Part II[^c8887771]

| Seating capacity | Rate of duty |
| --- | --- |
|  | £ |
| Under 9 | 130[^c8887781] |
| 9 to 16 | 150[^c8887791] |
| 17 to 35 | 200 |
| 36 to 60 | 300 |
| Over 60 | 450 |
