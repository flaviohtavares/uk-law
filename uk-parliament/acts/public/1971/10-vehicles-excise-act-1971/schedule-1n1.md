# Vehicles (Excise) Act 1971, SCHEDULE 1

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Annual Rates of Duty on Certain Vehicles not Exceeding 450 kg. in Weight Unladen[^c8887421]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-09-01).**

Section 1.

### Part I

#### 1.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to paragraph 2 below, the annual rate of duty applicable to a mechanically propelled vehicle of a description specified in the first column of Part II of this Schedule, being a vehicle which does not exceed 450 kilograms[^c8887431] in weight unladen, shall be the rate specified in relation to vehicles of that description in the second column of the said Part II.

#### 4.[^c8887511]

In its application to Northern Ireland, this Part of this Schedule shall have effect as if—

- (a)[^c8887521] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) in paragraph 3, in the definition of “weight unladen”, for “section 190(2) of the Road Traffic Act 1988” there were substituted “Article 2(3) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981”.

### Part II[^c8887591]

| Description of vehicle | Rate of duty |
| --- | --- |
|  | £ |
| 1\. Bicycles of which the cylinder capacity of the engine does not exceed 150 cubic centimetres | 15.00 |
| 2\. Bicycles of which the cylinder capacity of the engine exceeds 150 cubic centimetres but does not exceed 250 cubic centimetres | 35.00[^c8887601] |
| 3\. Bicycles not included above | 55.00[^c8887611] |
| [^c8887621]4. Tricycles of which the cylinder capacity of the engine does not exceed 150 cubic centimetres | 15.00 |
| [^c8887621]5. Tricycles not included above | 55.00[^c8887641] |
