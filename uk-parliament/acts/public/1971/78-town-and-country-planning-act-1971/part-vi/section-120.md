# Town and Country Planning Act 1971, section 120

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 120. Acquisition of land for purposes of exchange

Without prejudice to the generality of the powers conferred by the preceding provisions of this Part of this Act, any power of a local authority to acquire land thereunder, whether compulsorily or by agreement, shall include power to acquire land required for giving in exchange—

- (a) for land appropriated under section 121 of this Act; or
- (b) for Green Belt land, within the meaning of the [^m00034]Green Belt (London and Home Counties) Act 1938, appropriated in accordance with that Act for any purpose specified in a development plan.
