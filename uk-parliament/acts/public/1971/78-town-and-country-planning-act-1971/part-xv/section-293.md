# Town and Country Planning Act 1971, section 293

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 293. Saving for Interpretation Act 1889 s.38

The inclusion in this Act of any express savings, transitional provision or amendment shall not be taken as prejudicing the operation of section 38 of the [^m00125]Interpretation Act 1889 (which relates to the effect of repeals).
