# Town and Country Planning Act 1971, section 295

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 295. Short title and extent

(1) This Act may be cited as the Town and Country Planning Act 1971.

(2) This Act, except so far as it relates to the [^m00127]House of Commons Disqualification Act 1957 or (by Schedule 23) amends any enactment which extends to Scotland or Northern Ireland, extends to England and Wales only.
