# Town and Country Planning Act 1971, section 249

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 249. Special provisions as to orders subject to special parliamentary procedure

(1) Where an order under section 209, 211, 212 or 235 of this Act is subject to special parliamentary procedure, then—

- (a) if the order is confirmed by Act of Parliament under section 6 of the [^m00098]Statutory Orders (Special Procedure) Act 1945, the provisions of sections 242 and 244 of this Act shall not apply to the order;
- (b) in any other case, section 244 of this Act shall have effect in relation to the order as if, in subsection (1) of that section, for the reference to the date therein mentioned there were substituted a reference to the date on which the order becomes operative under section 6 of the said Act of 1945.

(2) Where by virtue of Part XI of this Act any such action as is mentioned in section 248 of this Act is required to be embodied in an order, and that order is subject to special parliamentary procedure, then—

- (a) if the order in which the action is embodied is confirmed by Act of Parliament under section 6 of the said Act of 1945, the provisions of sections 242 and 245 of this Act shall not apply;
- (b) in any other case, the provisions of section 245 of this Act shall apply with the substitution, for any reference to the date on which the action is taken, of a reference to the date on which the order becomes operative under section 6 of the said Act of 1945.
