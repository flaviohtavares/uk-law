# Town and Country Planning Act 1971, section 200

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 200. Death of claimant after service of blight notice

(1) In relation to any time after the death of a person who has served a blight notice, the provisions mentioned in subsection (2) of this section shall apply as if any reference therein to the claimant were a reference to the claimant's personal representatives.

(2) The said provisions are sections 194(11, 195(1) and 196(3) of this Act.
