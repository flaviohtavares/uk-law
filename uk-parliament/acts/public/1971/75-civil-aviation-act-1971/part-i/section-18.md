# Civil Aviation Act 1971, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Application of enactments relating to statutory undertakers etc.

(1) The provisions of Schedule 5 to this Act shall have effect as respects the application of the enactments mentioned in that Schedule (which relate to statutory undertakers, statutory undertakings and related matters).

(2) For the purposes of the law relating to rating the undertaking of the Authority shall be treated as not being a public utility undertaking.
