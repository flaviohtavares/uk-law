## 31. Expenses.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be defrayed out of moneys provided by Parliament any expenses incurred by the Lord Chancellor under Schedule 5 to this Act or[^c9591471] by a Secretary of State under or by virtue of this Act—

- (a) by way of administrative expenses [^c9591481]. . .; or
- (b) in connection with the removal of any person from the United Kingdom under Schedule 2 or 3 to this Act or the departure with him of his dependants, or his or their maintenance pending departure; or
