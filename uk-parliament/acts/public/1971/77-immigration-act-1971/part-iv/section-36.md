# Immigration Act 1971, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1971/77
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Power to extend to Islands.[^c9593181][^c-5d4cad91b9dc65dddbff0e8ee8fe679e][^c-b28ad8d3e5ee27bd1ad73e30b3f798ec][^c-7fb0613df2be93d69d4f35495e1d964e][^c-b97536000995828a47c4969e471807b9][^c9593171]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Her Majesty may by Order in Council direct that any of the provisions of this Act shall extend, with such exceptions, adaptations and modifications, if any, as may be specified in the Order, to any of the Islands; and any Order in Council under this subsection may be varied or revoked by a further Order in Council.
