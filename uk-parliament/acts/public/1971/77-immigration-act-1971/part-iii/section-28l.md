## 28L. Interpretation of Part III.[^c-5a988c4ca2a9354185024e11cc2a88df][^c9591311][^c-d66e253535507db4fe9d2195c8a81d09]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19706661][^c9591321] Subject to subsection (1A)[^c-f0de637390e22ad2b3030353abaa116b] in this Part, “premises” and “items subject to legal privilege” have the same meaning—

- (a) in relation to England and Wales, as in the [^c9591331]Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984;
- (b) in relation to Northern Ireland, as in the [^c9591341]Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989; and
- (c) in relation to Scotland, as in section 412 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002[^c9591351].

(1A)[^c-385b1a24b77929d939f922dd4254d461] In this Part “premises” also includes any artificial island, installation or structure (including one in the territorial sea adjacent to the United Kingdom).

(2)[^c9591361] In this Part “**business premises**” means premises (or any part of premises) not used as a dwelling.

(3) In this Part “**employee records**” means records which show an employee’s—

- (a) name,
- (b) date of birth,
- (c) address,
- (d) length of service,
- (e) rate of pay, or
- (f) nationality or citizenship.

(4) The Secretary of State may by order amend section 28CA(3)(a) to reflect a change in nomenclature.

(5) An order under subsection (4)—

- (a) must be made by statutory instrument, and
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
