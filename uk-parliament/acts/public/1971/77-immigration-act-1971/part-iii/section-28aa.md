# Immigration Act 1971, section 28AA

## 28AA. Arrest with warrant[^c-5a988c4ca2a9354185024e11cc2a88df][^c9590811][^c-d66e253535507db4fe9d2195c8a81d09]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if on an application by an immigration officer a justice of the peace is satisfied that there are reasonable grounds for suspecting that a person has committed an offence under—section 24(1)(d)[^c-be5ac53bf66ca305fd1fa24b2366e3f4]

(2) The justice of the peace may grant a warrant authorising any immigration officer to arrest the person.

(3) In the application of this section to Scotland a reference to a justice of the peace shall be treated as a reference to the sheriff or a justice of the peace.
