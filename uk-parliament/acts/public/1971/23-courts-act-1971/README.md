# Courts Act 1971

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1971/23

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W*

Courts Act 1971

1971 c.23

An Act to make further provision as respects the Senior Courts[^c2088058][^c2088058] and county courts, judges and juries, to establish a Crown Court as part of the Senior Courts[^c2088058] to try indictments and exercise certain other jurisdiction, to abolish courts of assize and certain other courts and to deal with their jurisdiction and other consequential matters, and to amend in other respects the law about courts and court proceedings.

\[12th May 1971\] [^c847699][^c847700]
