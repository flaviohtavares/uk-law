# Courts Act 1971, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1971/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Advisory committees.

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State[^c2070488][^c2070488] may constitute, on a permanent or temporary basis, one or more advisory committees to advise him on such questions relating to the Senior Courts[^c2088062][^c2088062] and the county court[^key-82bf54f5ade7ff7e500e5fdff300dc5e] as he may from time to time refer to them, and shall appoint the members of any such committee with regard to their knowledge of the work of the courts and social conditions.
