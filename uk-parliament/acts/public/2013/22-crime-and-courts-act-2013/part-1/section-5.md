# Crime and Courts Act 2013, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2013/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Relationships between NCA and other agencies: tasking etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-7cee22cacc2ea1e0e4507621474d424d] Any of the following persons may perform a task if the Director General requests the person to perform it—

- (a) the chief officer of a UK police force;
- (b) a UK law enforcement agency.

(2)[^key-7cee22cacc2ea1e0e4507621474d424d] A request under subsection (1)—

- (a) may be made only if the Director General considers that performance of the task would assist the NCA to exercise functions;
- (b) must explain how performance of the requested task would so assist the exercise of functions.

(3)[^key-7cee22cacc2ea1e0e4507621474d424d] The Director General may perform a task if any of the following persons requests the Director General to perform it—

- (a) the chief officer of a UK police force;
- (b) a UK law enforcement agency.

(4)[^key-7cee22cacc2ea1e0e4507621474d424d] A request under subsection (3)—

- (a) may be made only if the person making it considers that performance of the task would assist that person — or, in a case where that person is the chief officer of a police force, would assist that person or police force — to exercise functions;
- (b) must explain how performance of the requested task would so assist the exercise of functions.

(5)[^key-7cee22cacc2ea1e0e4507621474d424d] The Director General may direct any of the following persons to perform a task specified in the direction—

- (a) the chief officer of an England and Wales police force;
- (b) the Chief Constable of the British Transport Police.
- (c)[^key-1f91dc638465918159057f32ee33b0f8] the Director of the Serious Fraud Office.

(6)[^key-7cee22cacc2ea1e0e4507621474d424d] The Director General may give a direction under subsection (5) only if the Director General considers that—

- (a) performance of the task would assist the NCA to exercise functions;
- (b) it is expedient for the directed person to perform that task; and
- (c) satisfactory arrangements cannot be made, or cannot be made in time, under subsection (1).

(7)[^key-7cee22cacc2ea1e0e4507621474d424d] A person given a direction under this section must comply with it.

(8)[^key-7cee22cacc2ea1e0e4507621474d424d] If a person is requested or directed under this section to perform a task, the person may comply with that request or direction by securing that the task is performed by another person.

(9)[^key-7cee22cacc2ea1e0e4507621474d424d] The Director General may give a direction under this section to the Chief Constable of the British Transport Police only if the Secretary of State consents.

(10)[^key-a7047eb7e15f1df603bc902647d906a9][^key-f373308ff169cd76f27cfcdd23833017] Schedule 3 (relationships between NCA and other agencies) has effect.

(11)[^key-7cee22cacc2ea1e0e4507621474d424d] This section has effect subject to Part 5 (payment for tasks etc) of Schedule 3.

(12)[^key-7cee22cacc2ea1e0e4507621474d424d] Paragraph 33 of Schedule 3 gives the Secretary of State power to amend this section.
