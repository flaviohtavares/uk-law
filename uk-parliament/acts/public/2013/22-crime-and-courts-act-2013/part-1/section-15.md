# Crime and Courts Act 2013, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2013/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Abolition of SOCA and NPIA

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-c8eacaaf6bc7b21377bef4101c937fac] The Serious Organised Crime Agency is abolished.

(2)[^key-c8eacaaf6bc7b21377bef4101c937fac] The National Policing Improvement Agency is abolished.

(3)[^key-e63f7f4d06efeada7702d50173e84f9e][^key-98b87a77f36c016850c37cb9ba9f460f][^key-554031c901155405fa03e7da83dcfee2] Schedule 8 (abolition of SOCA and NPIA) has effect.
