## 44. Dealing non-custodially with offenders[^key-c6ed6f5847b29b81fa2f0f0a1ee0c5a6][^key-a4ae4c111054b0914f7c01beec56f537][^key-447941276f484b8688351206e94b0c03][^key-6a2c431548cbea6887901c2ff79495ec][^M_I_6cd4f619-994f-4601-b33c-881c1bdf6865][^key-e197debb40cf69ef0743aa71b559a2be]

Schedule 16 (which makes provision about community orders, restorative justice, community requirements in suspended sentence orders, compensation orders and fines etc) has effect.
