## SCHEDULE 42 — Climate change levy: supplies subject to carbon price support rates etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 200

### PART 1 — Earlier provision not to have effect

**1.** (1) On and after 26 March 2013, Schedule 6 to FA 2000 (climate change levy) has effect as if neither—

- (a) Schedule 20 to FA 2011, nor
- (b) Parts 1 and 2 of Schedule 32 to FA 2012,

had ever been enacted.

(2) Accordingly—

- (a) in FA 2011, section 78 and Schedule 20 are omitted, and
- (b) in FA 2012, Parts 1 and 2 of Schedule 32 are omitted.

(3) The amendments made by sub-paragraph (2) are treated as having come into force on 26 March 2013.

>

>

>
