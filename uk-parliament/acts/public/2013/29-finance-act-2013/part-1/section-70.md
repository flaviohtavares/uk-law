# Finance Act 2013, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2013/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. First-year allowance to be available for ships and railway assets

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 46(2) of CAA 2001 (general exclusions from first-year allowance), omit—

- (a) general exclusion 3 (ships), and
- (b) general exclusion 4 (railway assets),

and the italicised headings preceding them.

(2) The amendments made by this section have effect for expenditure incurred on or after 1 April 2013.
