## 33. Change in company ownership: shell companies

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 13—

- (a) inserts into Part 14 of CTA 2010 (change in company ownership) a new Chapter 5A (shell companies: restrictions on relief), and
- (b) makes consequential provision.
