## 7. Temporary increase in annual investment allowance

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In relation to expenditure incurred during the period beginning with 1 January 2013 and ending with the specified date[^c-871570a76c76e73a5f077c39a982f754] , section 51A of CAA 2001 (entitlement to annual investment allowance) has effect as if in subsection (5) for “£25,000” there were substituted “ £250,000 ”.

(1A)[^c-6488821b031edb251adc74484d623d2c] The specified date is —

- (a) for the purposes of corporation tax, 31 March 2014, and
- (b) for the purposes of income tax, 5 April 2014.

(2) Schedule 1 contains provision about chargeable periods which straddle 1 January 2013 [^c-66964fe2766fc16b7baac02e6ab8aaeb]....
