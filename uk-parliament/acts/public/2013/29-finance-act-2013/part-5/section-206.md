## 206. General anti-abuse rule[^c-09d7592ad45bc844679080b915f91632]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part has effect for the purpose of counteracting tax advantages arising from tax arrangements that are abusive.

(2) The rules of this Part are collectively to be known as “**the general anti-abuse rule**”.

(3)[^c-08b59dd79d1ae8bc3781d00b1159b569] The general anti-abuse rule applies to the following taxes—

- (a) income tax,
- (b) corporation tax, including any amount chargeable as if it were corporation tax or treated as if it were corporation tax,
- (c) capital gains tax,
- (d) petroleum revenue tax,
- (da)[^c-b8d7b33e98a2ad27075726956db36520] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (db)[^c-27be70597b6b48282806a33ea4baea76] apprenticeship levy,
- (e) inheritance tax,
- (f) stamp duty land tax, and
- (g) annual tax on enveloped dwellings.
- (h)[^c-d9175c39135ae388bbbbb9f414419dd2] multinational top-up tax.
- (i)[^c-a2ede2211b4fd12d511f404dce11f8c9] domestic top-up tax.
