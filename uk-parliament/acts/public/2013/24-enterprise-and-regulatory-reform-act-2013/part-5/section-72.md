# Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013, section 72

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-08-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2013/24

## 72. Abolition of Agricultural Wages Board and related English bodies

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-a99602223b78449e4647cfd7d6c8deb4] The Agricultural Wages Board for England and Wales is abolished.

(2)[^key-db1f5fa20ab68e1b3ff3ad25d767703e] Every agricultural wages committee for an area in England is abolished.

(3)[^key-ed91fc5b984ff12ef20b392c9cd70637] Every agricultural dwelling-house advisory committee for an area in England is abolished.

(4)[^key-2010e7f6d022c95bf12b9cee67175518][^key-4dc73e9a543160700683c2c123756e66][^key-23cf4747fc2f31ee7093048243739b79][^key-413633293f8ee8580e288d415d6c5241][^key-a56c7ba8cdf11245033178da6a59140f][^key-032ad976a38e8a6536212f22233814d9][^key-1a76076e6a7d57e65bdc812d5e12a084][^key-65988ebf502d4e6b8aca90dada8c2197] Schedule 20 (abolition of Agricultural Wages Board and related English bodies: consequential provision) has effect.
