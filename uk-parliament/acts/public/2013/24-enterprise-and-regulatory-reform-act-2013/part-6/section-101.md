# Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-08-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2013/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Financial provision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There is to be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred under or by virtue of this Act by the Secretary of State or the Competition and Markets Authority, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
