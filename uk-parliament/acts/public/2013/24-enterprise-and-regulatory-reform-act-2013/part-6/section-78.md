## 78. Penalties under provision implementing Directive on term of protection

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Paragraph 1(1)(d) of Schedule 2 to the European Communities Act 1972 (limitation on criminal penalties) does not apply for the purposes of provision under section 2(2) of that Act implementing [Directive 2011/77/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2011/0077) amending Directive [2006/116/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0116) on the term of protection of copyright and certain related rights.
