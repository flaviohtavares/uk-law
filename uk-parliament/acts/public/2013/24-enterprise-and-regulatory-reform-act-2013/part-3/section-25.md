# Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-08-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2013/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. The Competition and Markets Authority

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-b4e1ce4287488b776d5a82bfd4e67971] There is to be a body corporate known as the Competition and Markets Authority.

(2)[^c-b4e1ce4287488b776d5a82bfd4e67971] In this Part that body is referred to as “**the CMA**”.

(3)[^c-ee5eee74181a3f4425edb01e7ab188ec][^c-d838e7d3988363eba0a1542d099e61cd] The CMA must seek to promote competition, both within and outside the United Kingdom, for the benefit of consumers.

(4)[^c-b4e1ce4287488b776d5a82bfd4e67971] Schedule 4 (which makes provision about the CMA) has effect.

(5)[^c-e1c763d7857d067f6d32df8b5e5c2296] In making any decision, or otherwise taking action, for the purposes of any of its functions within Schedule 4A the CMA must have regard to the need for making a decision, or taking action, as soon as reasonably practicable.
