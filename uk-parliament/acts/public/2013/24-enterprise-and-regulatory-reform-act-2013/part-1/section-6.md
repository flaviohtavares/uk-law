## 6. Documents to be laid before Parliament

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies if—

- (a) [^c-3361eb252cef811a8f531d437402144f]... copies of the UK Green Investment Bank's annual accounts and reports are, in accordance with section 437 of the Companies Act 2006, laid before it in general meeting, and
- (b) as at the date of the meeting, the Crown holds shares in a[^c-36d087a3741fe274ba1dda1e68778343] UK Green Investment Bank company.

(2) The Secretary of State must, as soon as practicable after the meeting, lay a copy of the annual accounts and reports before Parliament.
