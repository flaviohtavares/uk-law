# Electoral Registration and Administration Act 2013

> Text as at 2024-09-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Electoral Registration and Administration Act 2013

2013 c. 6

An Act to make provision about the registration of electors and the administration and conduct of elections; and to amend section 3(2)(a) of the Parliamentary Constituencies Act 1986.

\[31st January 2013\]

Be it enacted by the Queen's most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—[^key-dddcad0eebe07f4bb25209ec446f25c1][^key-9ed5d53fd83b6e3a31d635f4afbf0fc7][^key-cee000e4fb26ce1e36f446a972b223e7][^key-d3d5edbda0781fada4d5ab2b392de6c9]
