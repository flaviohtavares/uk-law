# Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013, section 111

> Text as at 2025-08-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 111. Financial market and cash infrastructure administration[^c-b43a4858736c3e782356dac65967b61c][^c-4ed1adb1f49756428d9d113872a91224]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part—

- (a) provides for a procedure to be known as FMI administration, [^c-ff6de9ed2cb5960f77293d1450804137]...
- (b) restricts the powers of persons other than the Bank of England in relation to the insolvency of infrastructure companies, and[^c-680202928df71148a196a66de1bde781]
- (c) confers power on the Bank of England to participate in proceedings under Part 26A of the Companies Act 2006 (arrangements and reconstructions: companies in financial difficulty).
