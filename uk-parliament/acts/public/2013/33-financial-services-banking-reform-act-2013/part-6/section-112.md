# Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013, section 112

> Text as at 2025-08-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Interpretation: infrastructure companies[^c-70979b5370c20aa8abec7cda16e9be3a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “**infrastructure company**” has the meaning given by this section.

(2) “**Infrastructure company**” means a company which is—

- (a) the operator of a recognised [^c-41aa8c9414f6f719d8ce5ad13f3b9d83]... payment system, other than an operator excluded by subsection (3),
- (aa)[^c-f3ac6905eca864f80af69b70b05b2ce4] a recognised DSA service provider,
- (b)[^c-e6635be400504884571a83f8bf35e4ef] a recognised CSD [^c-a7d11d4c45451ed4f6dedcd85050d250]..., [^c-b1df5a5cd6248a134f273aea29970a11]...
- (ba)[^c-883de0de087adc816d81b7c0e9780f1c] a person recognised for the purposes of Part 5A of the Banking Act 2009 (wholesale cash distribution) as having systemic significance, or
- (c) a company designated by the Treasury under subsection (4).

(3) But a company is not an infrastructure company if it is a recognised central counterparty, as defined by section 285 of FSMA 2000.

(4) The Treasury may by order designate a company for the purposes of subsection (2)(c) if—

- (a) the company provides services to a person falling within subsection (2)(a), (b) or (ba)[^c-355dea63502060ea0da6dfa3df5cf9ab], and
- (b) the Treasury are satisfied that an interruption in the provision of those services would have a serious adverse effect on the effective operation of the recognised [^c-49abcff9f913a77a493a4fe03da57917]... payment system or securities settlement system in question or, in the case of a person falling within subsection (2)(ba), a relevant function[^c-07cd03836d93edd6bde6fb6ebe0d057c].

(5) An order under subsection (4) must specify the recognised [^c-a2c1e7d6df06ed1cec82d4a2b77ac729]... payment system or securities settlement system in connection with which the company is designated.

(6) Before designating a company under subsection (4), the Treasury must consult—

- (a) the company to be designated,
- (b) the person within subsection (2)(a), (b) or (ba)[^c-c05f2ab87fc2d89f4547cc187752e492] to whom the company provides services,
- (c) the Bank of England,
- (d) if the company is a PRA-authorised person, the PRA and the FCA, and
- (e) if the company is an authorised person other than a PRA-authorised person, the FCA.

(7)[^c-12f62440a02ebd8f3d80bdf6e67f0966] In subsection (4)(b), “**relevant function**” means a function performed by the person in relation to wholesale cash distribution.
