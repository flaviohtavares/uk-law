# Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013, section 45

> Text as at 2025-08-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Procedure[^c-af2f4debe754415b2495109f9051bdeb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before making a designation order in respect of a payment system the Treasury must—

- (a) consult the Payment Systems Regulator and, if the system is a recognised [^c-a3066f3a6d612c1cb422db5e519abe7e]... payment system, the Bank of England,
- (b) notify the operator of the system, and
- (c) consider any representations made.

(2) In considering whether to make a designation order in respect of a payment system, the Treasury may rely on information provided by—

- (a) the Bank of England,
- (b) the FCA,
- (c) the PRA, or
- (d) the Payment Systems Regulator.
