# Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013, section 104B

> Text as at 2025-08-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 104B. Duty of Regulator to review generally applicable requirements[^key-4f6d1291d9ec5d3cfde30af3d579d268]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Payment Systems Regulator must keep under review generally any generally applicable requirements.
