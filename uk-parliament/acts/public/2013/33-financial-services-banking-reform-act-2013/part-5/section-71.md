# Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013, section 71

> Text as at 2025-08-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Meaning of “compliance failure”[^c-a6d0ae0d2a2bc5d2552669b8c7074bda]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “**compliance failure**” means a failure by a participant in a regulated payment system to—

- (a) comply with a direction given under section 54, [^c-bc95589ba418d3d3fa06bf085aaade73]...
- (b) comply with a requirement imposed under section 55 or 56 ; or[^c-406bea63a27034c8e67946083076f705]
- (c) comply with rules made by the Regulator under paragraph 9A of Schedule 4.
