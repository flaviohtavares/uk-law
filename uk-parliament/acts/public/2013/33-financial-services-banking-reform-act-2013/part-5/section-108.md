## 108. Relationship with Part 8 of the Payment Services Regulations 2017[^key-1bf2a1ce653821c1c54e5254b29ff902][^key-153db09439e920bbc318f22f4122013f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Payment Systems Regulator may not exercise any power under sections 54 to 58[^key-0615a37a4b7e0fbdf485cebbd384c22f] for the purposes of enabling a person to obtain or maintain access to, or participation in, a payment system in circumstances in which regulation 103 (prohibition on restrictive rules on access to payment systems) or 104 (indirect access to designated payment systems) of the Payment Services Regulations 2017 applies in relation to access to, or participation in, the payment system by the person.[^key-0654575c003a00c83db1bfa326215559]
