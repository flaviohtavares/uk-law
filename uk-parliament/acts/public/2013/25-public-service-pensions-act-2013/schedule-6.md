# Public Service Pensions Act 2013, SCHEDULE 6

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 6 — Existing injury and compensation schemes

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 19

### Civil servants

**1.**[^key-c1867e1c4075351efbda6ad111e60056] A scheme under section 1 of the Superannuation Act 1972.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits and compensation benefits

### Judiciary

**2.**[^key-55d63481afdda59b7a3b2a2c11eb2304] A scheme under paragraph 7A of Schedule 10 to the Rent Act 1977.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits and compensation benefits

**3.**[^key-3cf67a572c53cebd4599101c2848135a] A scheme constituted by or made under Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the Judicial Pensions Act 1981.

**4.**[^key-f48fbc4c3592fb24972076d37bd101ee] A scheme constituted by section 11(b) of the Judicial Pensions Act 1981.

### Local government workers

**5.**[^key-429f94904c0cb12c657e1b7d46fc36ff] Regulations under section 7 of the Superannuation Act 1972.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits

### Teachers

**6.**[^key-de04b7622b2b0d83df5c692335afb4c4] Regulations under section 9 of the Superannuation Act 1972.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits

### Health service workers

**7.**[^key-9a7fd2293de56b86817bd13649b345ab] Regulations under section 10 of the Superannuation Act 1972.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits

### Fire and rescue workers

**8.**[^key-2520df68206fb07a4d7679babb865fc4] A scheme under section 34 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits and compensation benefits

### Members of police forces

**9.**[^key-86c6ab0d8885c80fe43e4bf1cc301ab1] Regulations under section 1 of the Police Pensions Act 1976.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits

**10.**[^key-88140ef87c3da636798b756648a46064] A scheme under section 48 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 (asp 8).

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits and compensation benefits

### Armed forces

**11.**[^key-8ac1fa03eef198172ecf8af4d2ddb07d] The scheme constituted by the Royal Warrant of 19 December 1949 (see Army Order 151 of 1949).

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits

**12.**[^key-93bf777b1efd5586db883ac997c01373] An Order in Council under section 3 of the Naval and Marine Pay and Pensions Act 1865.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits

**13.**[^key-bc9d58234b0c84ec02e04661c815ffa7] An order under section 2 of the Pensions and Yeomanry Pay Act 1884.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits

**14.**[^key-1b81e15e50f7320b8539c4bdb375c8a8] An order under section 2 of the Air Force (Constitution) Act 1917.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits

**15.**[^key-14a18540abcccbc1caff3ed18d62331f] An order or regulations under section 4 of the Reserve Forces Act 1996 containing provision made under section 8 of that Act.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits and compensation benefits

**16.**[^key-56e45ca9251f96d8b2fd590e0c10624c] (1) A scheme under section 1(1) of the Armed Forces (Pensions and Compensation) Act 2004.

*Specified benefits*: injury benefits and compensation benefits

(2) For the purposes of sub-paragraph (1), “**compensation benefits**” includes benefits by way of payments for resettlement or retraining.

**17.**[^key-9b16d5fa375dd3f232a5db35a46d114c] A scheme under section 1(2) of the Armed Forces (Pensions and Compensation) Act 2004.

### Compensation schemes for loss of office etc

**18.**[^key-00d29c99de2ea4b9fbb0926871a44809] Regulations under section 24 of the Superannuation Act 1972.
