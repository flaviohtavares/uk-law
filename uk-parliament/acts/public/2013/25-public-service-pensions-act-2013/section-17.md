# Public Service Pensions Act 2013, section 17

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Regulatory oversight

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-98b2346aae0c3525ff312cb93c9e184d][^c-9f776892fb9e70c65ecfbd31dde9a6b7] Schedule 4 contains provision relating to the regulation of schemes under section 1, new public body pension schemes and connected schemes.

(2)[^c-1cae582a8c82b0e32eeb2411554f2c75] The Secretary of State may by order make—

- (a) provision consequential on Schedule 4, and
- (b) further provision for, or in connection with, the regulation of public service pension schemes within the meaning of the Pensions Act 2004 (as amended by that Schedule).

(3)[^c-1cae582a8c82b0e32eeb2411554f2c75] The provision referred to in subsection (2) includes provision made by amending any legislation (including this Act).

(4)[^c-1cae582a8c82b0e32eeb2411554f2c75] An order under this section may make different provision for different purposes.

(5)[^c-1cae582a8c82b0e32eeb2411554f2c75] An order under this section is subject to—

- (a) the affirmative procedure, if it amends primary legislation, and
- (b) the negative procedure, in any other case.
