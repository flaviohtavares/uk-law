# Public Service Pensions Act 2013, section 14

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Information about benefits

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-f902d991f1e7bd535086a02eb42f8b78] Scheme regulations must require the scheme manager for a scheme under section 1 which is a defined benefits scheme to provide benefit information statements to each person in pensionable service under the scheme in accordance with this section.

(2)[^key-7a07f5264c229727bfef4b85aad209b7][^key-641c2c4e2ba119426ff4f68f525c8867] A benefit information statement must include—

- (a) a description of the benefits earned by the person in respect of his or her pensionable service, and
- (b) such other information as Treasury directions may specify.

(3)[^key-7a07f5264c229727bfef4b85aad209b7][^key-641c2c4e2ba119426ff4f68f525c8867] The information included in a benefit information statement must comply with such requirements as Treasury directions may specify.

(4)[^key-f902d991f1e7bd535086a02eb42f8b78] A benefit information statement must be provided—

- (a) no later than the relevant date, and
- (b) at least once in each year ending with the anniversary of that date.

(5)[^key-f902d991f1e7bd535086a02eb42f8b78] The relevant date is the last day of the period of 17 months beginning with the day on which scheme regulations establishing the scheme come into force.

(6)[^key-7a07f5264c229727bfef4b85aad209b7][^key-641c2c4e2ba119426ff4f68f525c8867] A benefit information statement must be provided in such manner as Treasury directions may specify.
