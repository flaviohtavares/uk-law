# Energy Act 2013, section 127

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2013/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Rights apart from Part 4[^key-5bc67b61572aae0d4c436a7c5c556430]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Nothing in this Part affects any other rights of the Secretary of State in relation to the government pipe-line and storage system (whether conferred under another enactment, by agreement or otherwise, and whether or not existing upon the coming into force of this section).

(2) For the purposes of sections 120, 121 and 125, it is immaterial whether a right corresponding to a right conferred by the section was exercisable by the Secretary of State before the coming into force of the section.
