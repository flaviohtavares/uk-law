# Energy Act 2013, section 108

## 108. Reporting requirements of Secretary of State[^c-bac73ac2e1bc3f7e039f2eed9551f065]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) As soon as reasonably practicable after the end of the financial year, the Secretary of State must make a report to each House of Parliament on the use of the Secretary of State's powers under this Part during the year.

(2) The Secretary of State must lay a copy of any such report before Parliament.
