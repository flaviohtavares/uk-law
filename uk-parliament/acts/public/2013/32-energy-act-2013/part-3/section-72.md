# Energy Act 2013, section 72

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2013/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Nuclear safeguards purposes[^c-3a6084c874b6a1121a08eb807d16f50d][^c-fc6ca3bef9a0999fe5b65cc44624b6e4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, the “**nuclear safeguards purposes**” means the purposes of—

- (a) ensuring compliance with nuclear safeguards regulations (see section 76A),
- (b) ensuring compliance by the United Kingdom or, as the case may be, enabling or facilitating compliance by a Minister of the Crown, with a relevant international agreement, and
- (c) the development of any future obligations relating to nuclear safeguards.
