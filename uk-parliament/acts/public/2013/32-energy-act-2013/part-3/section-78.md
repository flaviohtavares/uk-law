# Energy Act 2013, section 78

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Principal function[^key-867da63a9bfdca137813828b8d03c0b8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The ONR must do whatever it considers appropriate for the ONR's purposes.

(2) That includes, so far as it considers appropriate, assisting and encouraging others to further those purposes.
