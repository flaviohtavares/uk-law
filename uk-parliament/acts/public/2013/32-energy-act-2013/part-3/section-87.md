# Energy Act 2013, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2013/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Provision of information[^key-127381f416500563e4ab84c898df7e27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The ONR must make such arrangements as it considers appropriate for providing information that it holds that is relevant to the ONR's purposes.

(2) Arrangements that may be made under subsection (1) are arrangements of any description, including arrangements—

- (a) for providing information to any person or category of persons (whether or not concerned with matters relevant to the ONR's purposes);
- (b) for providing information on request or on the ONR's initiative;
- (c) for providing only such information as the ONR considers appropriate.

(3) This section is subject to section 94.
