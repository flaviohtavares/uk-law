# Energy Act 2013, section 118

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2013/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Review of Part 3

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) As soon as reasonably practicable after the end of the period of 7 years[^c-2c6f50ae7a4dc28663df6427e0724144] beginning with the day on which section 77 comes into force, the Secretary of State must carry out a review of the provisions of this Part.

(2) The Secretary of State must set out the conclusions of the review in a report.

(3) The report must, in particular—

- (a) set out the objectives of the provisions of this Part,
- (b) assess the extent to which those objectives have been achieved, and
- (c) assess whether those objectives remain appropriate and, if so, the extent to which those objectives could be achieved in a way that imposes less regulation.

(4) The Secretary of State must lay the report before Parliament.
