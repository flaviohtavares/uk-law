# Energy Act 2013, section 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2013/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Enforcement of relevant statutory provisions[^c-b214a7a1807898aa50c887d07d40a110]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The ONR must make adequate arrangements for the enforcement of the relevant statutory provisions.

(2) In this Part, “**relevant statutory provisions**” means—

- (a) the provisions of this Part, nuclear regulations and nuclear safeguards regulations;[^c-8a37030c84f437d8b398097ccaf1cd1c]
- (b) the provisions made by or under the following sections of the Nuclear Installations Act 1965, so far as they have effect in England and Wales or Scotland—
  - section 1;
  - sections 3 to 5;[^c-a7f22e2e40732810cf247e6b66810a2c]
  - section 6, so far as it relates to sites in respect of which nuclear site licences have been granted;
  - section 22;
  - section 24A; and
- (c) the provisions of the Nuclear Safeguards Act 2000.
