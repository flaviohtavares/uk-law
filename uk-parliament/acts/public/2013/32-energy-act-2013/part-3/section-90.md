# Energy Act 2013, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2013/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Arrangements with government departments etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-7782d5d17e592b646758cdd6a6961b22] If the condition in subsection (2) is met, the ONR may enter into an agreement with a Minister of the Crown, a government department or a public authority for the ONR to perform any function exercisable by the Minister, department or authority.

(2)[^key-7782d5d17e592b646758cdd6a6961b22] The condition is that—

- (a) the function is—
  - (i) a function of the Health and Safety Executive of investigating or making a special report under section 14 of the 1974 Act, or
  - (ii) a function of the Office of Rail and Road *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^key-317ef66baa6c365d8745e7312ba0865e] of investigating or making a special report under paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 to the Railways Act 2005, or
- (b) the Secretary of State considers that the function in question can appropriately be performed by the ONR.

(3)[^key-7782d5d17e592b646758cdd6a6961b22] The functions to which an agreement under subsection (1) may relate—

- (a) in the case of an agreement with a Minister of the Crown, include a function not conferred by an enactment;
- (b) do not include any power to make regulations or other instruments of a legislative character.

(4)[^key-7782d5d17e592b646758cdd6a6961b22] An agreement under subsection (1) may provide for functions to be performed with or without payment.

(5)[^key-1b4a95ecb55222fbe4b9f60c27410122] The ONR may provide services or facilities, with or without payment, otherwise than for the ONR's purposes, to a government department or public authority in connection with the exercise of that department's or authority's functions.
