## 102. General duty of employees at work in relation to requirements imposed on others[^c-6e22f991b051b4d9b71b7760bdbd86e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Every employee, while at work, must co-operate with any person (whether or not the employer) on whom a requirement is imposed by or under any of the relevant statutory provisions[^c-d69a086c94cb212bdea6ed70908d5e9f] so far as necessary to enable the requirement to be complied with.

(2) Failure to comply with the duty in subsection (1) is an offence.

(3) A person who commits an offence under subsection (2) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction—
  - (i) to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months (in England and Wales or Scotland) or 6 months (in Northern Ireland),
  - (ii) to a fine (in England and Wales) or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (in Scotland or Northern Ireland), or
  - (iii) to both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment—
  - (i) to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years,
  - (ii) to a fine, or
  - (iii) to both.

(4)[^c-ff9c0086e9d8d2a41651f9f8388225b5] In the application of this section in England and Wales, the reference in subsection (3)(a)(i) to 12 months is to be read as a reference to the general limit in a magistrates’ court (or to 6 months in relation to an offence committed before 2 May 2022).

(5) In this section—

- (a) “**employee**” and “**employer**” have the same meanings as in Part 1 of the 1974 Act (see section 53(1) of that Act), [^c-82b15b12979a8026446ba5386cc96d32]...
