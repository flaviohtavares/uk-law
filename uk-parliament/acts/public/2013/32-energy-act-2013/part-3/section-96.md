## 96. Co-operation between ONR and Health and Safety Executive[^key-04b055b71abae1ecc915a99799d74367]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Health and Safety Executive and the ONR must enter into and maintain arrangements with each other for securing co-operation and the exchange of information in connection with the carrying out of any of their functions.

(2) The Health and Safety Executive and the ONR must—

- (a) review the arrangements from time to time, and
- (b) revise them when they consider it appropriate to do so.
