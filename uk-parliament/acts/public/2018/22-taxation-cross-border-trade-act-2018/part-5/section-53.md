# Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Meaning of “excise duty”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “**excise duty**” means any excise duty under—

- (a)[^c-94c61149efd3184e96dc07d322b3be4a] Part 2 of the Finance (No. 2) Act 2023 (alcohol duty),
- (b) the Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979, or
- (c) the Tobacco Products Duty Act 1979.
