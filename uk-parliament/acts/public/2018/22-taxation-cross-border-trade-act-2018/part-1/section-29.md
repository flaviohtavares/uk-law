# Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Consequential amendments[^c23439311][^c-5eec86624bfcfaf8e8ebea5f2cb64a6c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-6785c2e6c3d5caaab4f58018bb476d9e] Schedule 7 contains amendments consequential on the provision made by this Part.

(2) The amendments made by that Schedule include amendments dealing with—

- (a) reviews or appeals of decisions in relation to import duty (see sections 13A to 16 of, and Schedule 5 to, the Finance Act 1994), and
- (b) penalties in relation to breaches of requirements in relation to import duty (see Part 3 of the Finance Act 2003).
