# Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Charge to import duty[^c23428731][^c-e712ce7ad301568f792cea864c0b602e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-d6d5e4df658eb85fd1fcec685d6cec45] A duty of customs (to be known as “**import duty**”) is charged in accordance with provision made by or under this Part by reference to the importation of chargeable goods into the United Kingdom.

(2)[^c-cf0c5065f13a91fbabd965b3f4dcca06] Sections 30A and 30B make provision about the application of this Part to goods imported into the United Kingdom as a result of their entry into Northern Ireland.
