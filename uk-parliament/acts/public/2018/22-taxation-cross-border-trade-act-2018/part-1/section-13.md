# Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Dumping of goods, foreign subsidies and increases in imports[^key-57900cac1df13b64f17d6b13e498541d][^key-e56479350f1e3c7a6eaae6be27b7d06f][^key-1d8605de9d27b7c25fb7be3cf109acfa][^key-bf06c60818863d542eeb77879ce03116]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Functions relating to import duty are conferred on the Trade Remedies Authority (“the TRA”) by—

- (a) Schedule 4 (dumping and foreign subsidies causing injury to UK industry), [^key-33b5ebf95e6365e6b5d41e51fc25940c]...
- (b) Schedule 5 (increased imports causing serious injury to UK producers) , and[^key-0151d5878661acae51740d2aeabba725]
- (c) Schedule 5A (increase in imports as a result of free trade agreement causing serious injury to UK producers).

(2) If the Secretary of State decides[^key-842d6536cfa879072bd43ccbd321fa1c] under provision made by or under Schedule 4, 5 or 5A[^key-655456a3acae18edeb7fde0e594e4b77] that an additional amount of import duty should be applicable to goods, the Secretary of State must by public notice make provision giving effect to the decision[^key-5d8c269994f8050b4b1c09321349d741].

(3)[^key-2e154b14f186d4162687deeca4f56cca] If the Secretary of State decides[^key-33c00053a42a70e27e256a8a5e2834e3] under provision made by or under Schedule 5 or 5A[^key-8a609c2253f7de951aa1411b543df8f8] that goods should be subject to a provisional tariff rate quota or a tariff rate quota, the Secretary of State must by public notice make provision for determining the amount of import duty applicable to the goods in order to give effect to the decision[^key-42f71c765359eab2c56229b5cb3931b8].

(4) If the Secretary of State decides[^key-c8124dc9080fc595f5b182d326a407b1] under provision made by or under Schedule 4, 5 or 5A[^key-c28002b5a95fb12055b37bd58b70f5b3] that—

- (a) the application of an additional amount of import duty to goods under this section should be suspended, varied or revoked, or
- (b) the application of a quota to which goods are subject under this section should be suspended, varied or revoked,

the Secretary of State must by public notice make provision giving effect to the decision[^key-21388429caf28b9f06407ab1d0734419].

(5) The Secretary of State may make regulations containing any provision that the Secretary of State considers appropriate for the purposes of subsections (3) and (4)(b); and section 11(3)(a) to (d), (4) and (5) apply to regulations under this subsection as they apply to regulations under section 11(3).
