# Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Chargeable goods[^c23428741][^c-4bb1e550dc9f0a5ea2ef5236d5c94ea9][^c-ab61295ef87cff841db75c1f5db0598d][^c-cf8a2ad0f2fcff5eecfa7c7f4bf625d8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-d96e21e9203b4c486557815255d0cd95] Goods are “**chargeable goods**” for the purposes of this Part unless they are domestic goods.

(2)[^c-4289dae01fe0a2a10c28a82bb8fcdd21] But subsection (1) is subject to section 30A(4) (importation of goods: Northern Ireland).
