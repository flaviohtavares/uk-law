# Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Sections 44 to 48: interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In sections 44 to 48—

- “**excise duty**” means any excise duty under—
  - (a) Part 2 of the Finance (No. 2) Act 2023 (alcohol duty),[^key-1038e4067fb7bf4d34a53d377ff58f03]
  - (b) the Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979, [^key-f094e347fd3217a58e8c7ee9ba9fc8ac]...
  - (c) the Tobacco Products Duty Act 1979,or[^key-96043d987b21819bb2386346f733b18f]
  - (d) Part 4 of the Finance Act 2026 (vaping products duty)
- “**HMRC Commissioners**” means the Commissioners for Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs.
