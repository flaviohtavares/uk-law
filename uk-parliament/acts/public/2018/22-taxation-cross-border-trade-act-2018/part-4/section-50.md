## 50. Excise duty amendments connected with withdrawal from EU[^c-26aabea918853b61bbfec17983c51c60]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 9 makes amendments of enactments relating to excise duty in consequence of the provision made by this Part or otherwise in connection with the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the EU (including some amendments also relating to duties of customs).
