## 43. Other VAT amendments connected with withdrawal from EU[^key-308e562b39132c8494669fce82c39f71][^key-7fa811a1d503f8524e4ed298e3761d61]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 8 makes amendments of the Value Added Tax Act 1994, and other enactments relating to VAT, in consequence of the provision made by this Part or otherwise in connection with the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the EU.
