# Finance Act 2018, section 25

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Education Authority of Northern Ireland

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In CTA 2010, after section 987A insert—

> **Education Authority of Northern Ireland**
>
> **987B. Education Authority of Northern Ireland**
>
> The Education Authority of Northern Ireland is not liable to corporation tax.

(2) The amendment made by this section is to be treated as having come into force on 1 April 2015.
