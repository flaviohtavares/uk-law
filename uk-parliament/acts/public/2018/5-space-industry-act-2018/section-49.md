# Space Industry Act 2018, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-02-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Amendment and revocation of orders

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-6c2e547c3ef4b2e9f77ae821680e6892] A power to make an order that is conferred by section 39 or by paragraph 4 or 5 of Schedule 9 includes a power exercisable in the same manner and subject to the same conditions or limitations—

- (a) to amend the order, or
- (b) to revoke the order.

(2)[^c-6c2e547c3ef4b2e9f77ae821680e6892] The power to make an order that is conferred by section 41(1) includes a power—

- (a) to amend the order to shorten, or remove, a period specified as required by subsection (3) of that section;
- (b) otherwise to amend the order;
- (c) to revoke the order.

(3)[^c-6c2e547c3ef4b2e9f77ae821680e6892] Immediately after making an order under the power specified in subsection (2)(a) or (c) the Secretary of State must serve on the relevant persons—

- (a) a copy of the order, and
- (b) a notice explaining the effect of the order and stating when it became operative.

(4)[^c-3881e262048a77d5456d43196bb7a07a] “The relevant persons” are—

- (a) where the original order is a land order—
  - (i) every owner, lessee and occupier of any of the land;
  - (ii) every local authority within whose area any of the land is situated;
- (b) where the original order is not a land order—
  - (i) any person who the Secretary of State thinks is particularly well able to bring the new order to the attention of those likely to be affected by it;
  - (ii) every person who was served with a copy of a notice, under paragraph 8(1)(b) of Schedule 6, in respect of the original order.

(5)[^c-6c2e547c3ef4b2e9f77ae821680e6892] An order made under the power specified in subsection (2)(a) or (c) becomes operative immediately after it is made.

(6)[^c-6c2e547c3ef4b2e9f77ae821680e6892] The power specified in subsection (2)(b) is exercisable in the same manner and subject to the same conditions or limitations as the power to make the original order.
