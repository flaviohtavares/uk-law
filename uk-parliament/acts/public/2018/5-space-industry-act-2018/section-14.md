# Space Industry Act 2018, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-02-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Licences granted for specified periods

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-334bb0615bec6391e43a26d6b252799b] A licence under this Act may be granted for a period specified in the licence.

(2)[^c-334bb0615bec6391e43a26d6b252799b] A licence under this Act granted for a specified period may be renewed by the regulator, on the application of the licensee, for a further specified period.

(3)[^c-bcc99dfbcad0ad23bad14d414c4982c0] If the regulator is not the Secretary of State, the regulator must consult the Secretary of State before renewing a licence under this Act.

(4)[^c-1b6be9bb8891e747d85b5668ff3c9d82] Sections 8 to 13, and other provisions of this Act about licences, apply in relation to the renewal of a licence as they apply in relation to the grant of a licence.
