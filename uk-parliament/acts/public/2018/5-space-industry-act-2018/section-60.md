# Space Industry Act 2018, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-02-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Appeals[^key-eb53cfc7cf4e8c6e0ccd9ac25b9f6ccc][^key-fc2c79e586345d34726c3c2fb81003c2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 10, which makes provision for—

- (a) appeals against decisions under this Act and under the Outer Space Act 1986, and
- (b) the establishment of panels to consider such appeals,

has effect.
