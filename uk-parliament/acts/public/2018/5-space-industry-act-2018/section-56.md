# Space Industry Act 2018, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-02-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Defences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-5bf5ae28a48318a0affb5cd8d963e3e1] It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under a provision of this Act to show that the person exercised all due diligence and took all reasonable precautions to avoid committing the offence.

(2)[^key-5bf5ae28a48318a0affb5cd8d963e3e1] A person is taken to have shown that the person exercised all due diligence and took all reasonable precautions to avoid committing the offence if—

- (a) sufficient evidence of the fact is adduced to raise an issue with respect to it, and
- (b) the contrary is not proved beyond reasonable doubt.

(3)[^key-5bf5ae28a48318a0affb5cd8d963e3e1] Subsection (1) does not apply to an offence under—

- (a) section 3(7),
- (b) section 7(9),
- (c) section 18(6),
- (d) section 32(9),
- (e) section 33(8),
- (f) section 41(5),
- (g) section 42(5)
- (h) section 66(5),
- (i) Schedule 4, or
- (j) paragraph 9(5) of Schedule 9.

(4)[^key-86f368770e722bf9828e1c071033b6c8] Regulations under this Act that create offences may provide for defences in relation to those offences.
