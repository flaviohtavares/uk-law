# Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018, section 1

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. The single financial guidance body

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-fa4c746c39c4d0bca03ce9195cfdbded] A body corporate with functions relating to financial guidance is established (the “single financial guidance body”).

(2)[^key-fa4c746c39c4d0bca03ce9195cfdbded] Schedule 1 makes further provision about the single financial guidance body.

(3)[^key-fa4c746c39c4d0bca03ce9195cfdbded] The name of the new body is to be determined by regulations made by the Secretary of State.

(4)[^key-fa4c746c39c4d0bca03ce9195cfdbded] The regulations may—

- (a) amend any provision of this Part, or of any Act amended by this Part, so as to replace the words “single financial guidance body” with the name of the body;
- (b) make incidental, supplementary and consequential provision.

(5)[^key-fa4c746c39c4d0bca03ce9195cfdbded] The power to make regulations under subsection (3) is exercisable by statutory instrument; and an instrument containing such regulations is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(6)[^key-51602fda8e6e88e3d2b3b19c675b005f] The consumer financial education body is dissolved.

(7)[^key-fa4c746c39c4d0bca03ce9195cfdbded] Schedule 2 makes provision about schemes for the transfer of staff, property, rights and liabilities—

- (a) from the Secretary of State and the Pensions Advisory Service Limited to the single financial guidance body;
- (b) from the consumer financial education body to the single financial guidance body and the devolved authorities.
