# Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018, section 8

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Guidance and directions from the Secretary of State[^key-4f5a1a9c21abd831d1e97004fcd13ec7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may issue guidance and give directions to the single financial guidance body about the exercise of its functions.

(2) The Secretary of State must publish any directions that are given to the single financial guidance body.

(3) The single financial guidance body must have regard to guidance, and comply with directions, given to it by the Secretary of State.
