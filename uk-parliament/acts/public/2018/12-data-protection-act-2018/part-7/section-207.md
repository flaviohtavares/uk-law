# Data Protection Act 2018, section 207

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 207. Territorial application of this Act[^c-b43bb1f6638b5994d92beeecf2ab27a2][^M_I_1dd6f40b-6c26-4df4-ac0b-b87a0c97b98d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act applies only to processing of personal data described in subsections (1A) and (2)[^c-8073dd715b8ba844756ab6ffb34393c2].

(1A)[^c-813ee7d39f52ef91dc62338317d85979] In the case of the processing of personal data to which Part 2 (the UK GDPR) applies, it applies to the types of such processing to which the UK GDPR applies by virtue of Article 3 of the UK GDPR.

(2) In the case of the processing of personal data to which Part 2 does not apply, it applies where such processing is carried out[^c-dd19920218f3766a11863bfd8609d509] in the context of the activities of an establishment of a controller or processor in the United Kingdom, whether or not the processing takes place in the United Kingdom.

(3)[^c-f4bf067041e8f9f8848359b6b4e202b0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Subsections (1), (1A) and (2)[^c-459def90d3d948fe23eed3889127cc51] have effect subject to any provision in or made under section 120 providing for the Commission[^c-d1e7c46c5bffa0cfd1dd8e0a17e92cff] to carry out functions in relation to other processing of personal data.

(5) Section 3(14)(c) does not apply to the reference to the processing of personal data in subsection (2).

(6)[^c-f5f2be2d52fde50a90249c3696ee9e5c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) In this section, references to a person who has an establishment in the United Kingdom include the following—

- (a) an individual who is ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom,
- (b) a body incorporated under the law of the United Kingdom or a part of the United Kingdom,
- (c) a partnership or other unincorporated association formed under the law of the United Kingdom or a part of the United Kingdom, and
- (d) a person not within paragraph (a), (b) or (c) who maintains, and carries on activities through, an office, branch or agency or other stable arrangements in the United Kingdom,
