# Data Protection Act 2018, section 200

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 200. Guidance about PACE codes of practice[^M_I_e449d474-a66a-41e2-eb50-e0b9d3cc4cf2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commission[^key-8c3fe8d42867d31162f7346089510b19] must produce and publish guidance about how the Commission proposes to perform the duty under section 67(9) of the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (duty to have regard to codes of practice under that Act when investigating offences and charging offenders) in connection with offences under this Act.

(2) The Commission—

- (a) may alter or replace the guidance, and
- (b) must publish any altered or replacement guidance.

(3) The Commission must consult the Secretary of State before publishing guidance under this section (including any altered or replacement guidance).

(4) The Commission must arrange for guidance under this section (including any altered or replacement guidance) to be laid before Parliament.
