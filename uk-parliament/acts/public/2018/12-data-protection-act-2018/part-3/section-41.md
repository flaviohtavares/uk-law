# Data Protection Act 2018, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Safeguards: archiving[^M_I_e27ca271-6da8-48a7-c4bb-7fd1cdf1d86a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies in relation to the processing of personal data for a law enforcement purpose where the processing is carried out[^c-c283492444369915cc8328a5db21ac24]—

- (a) for archiving purposes in the public interest,
- (b) for scientific or historical research purposes, or
- (c) for statistical purposes.

(2) The processing is not permitted if—

- (a) it is carried out for the purposes of, or in connection with, measures or decisions with respect to a particular data subject, or
- (b) it is likely to cause substantial damage or substantial distress to a data subject.
