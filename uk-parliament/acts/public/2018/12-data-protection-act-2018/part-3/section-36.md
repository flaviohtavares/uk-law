# Data Protection Act 2018, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. The second data protection principle[^M_I_77433870-fe51-4301-8ff2-9116afefa523]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The second data protection principle is that—

- (a) the law enforcement purpose for which personal data is collected (whether from the data subject or otherwise)[^c-332de4f04a0a242ded4037391f956f33] must be specified, explicit and legitimate, and
- (b) personal data so collected must not be processed by or on behalf of a controller[^c-d90f8aa7af43aed9d99961342328f905] in a manner that is incompatible with the purpose for which the controller collected it[^c-c27fc7f3663f78e184ed53b5cb3fa95a].

(2) Paragraph (b) of the second data protection principle is subject to subsections (3) and (4).

(3) Personal data collected for a law enforcement purpose may be processed for any other law enforcement purpose (whether by the controller that collected the data or by another controller) provided that—

- (a) the controller is authorised by law to process the data for the other purpose, and
- (b) the processing is necessary and proportionate to that other purpose.

(4) Personal data collected for any of the law enforcement purposes may not be processed for a purpose that is not a law enforcement purpose unless the processing is authorised by law.
