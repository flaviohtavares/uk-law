# Data Protection Act 2018, section 74B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74B. Transfers approved by regulations: monitoring[^c-e848a3d8004e9d924b205afb95f2dc17][^c-e2348e78adbd3029a227efae614695f4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-fa7ac0a67482002a01f5620160e274c2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-fa7ac0a67482002a01f5620160e274c2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The Secretary of State must, on an ongoing basis, monitor developments in third countries and international organisations that could affect decisions to make regulations giving approval under section 74AA[^c-60de89cf3b214460c2075bf102f6e8d5] or to amend or revoke such regulations.

(4) Where the Secretary of State becomes aware that the data protection test is no longer met in relation to transfers approved, or of a description approved, in regulations under section 74AA,[^c-37f0e7baab56b10e415c83cab5bddb11] the Secretary of State must, to the extent necessary, amend or revoke the regulations.

(5) Where regulations under section 74AA[^c-e0af39578686eeb0c305a1a1b1364dea] are amended or revoked in accordance with subsection (4), the Secretary of State must enter into consultations with the third country or international organisation concerned with a view to improving the protection provided to data subjects with regard to the processing of personal data in the country or by the organisation[^c-e99058667b9492995c12419ae6af932c].

(6) The Secretary of State must publish—

- (a) a list of the third countries [^c-53107efacf22ac248514ba40c62a4ee5]... and international organisations, and the descriptions of such countries [^c-ff454fa84136ab51d3f46aa48bc8ca42]... and organisations, which are for the time being approved by regulations under section 74AA as places or persons to which personal data may be transferred[^c-67aec105c02309b3dcc27b6e0cf1c250], and
- (b) a list of the third countries [^c-c42e79588cd90b927e90e98fb9ba2512]... and international organisations, and the descriptions of such countries [^c-e3d2ddb984625a2f569456c91752d449]... and organisations, which have been but are no longer approved by[^c-c830a9cae371d430b44b2aa6a4bf2b0f] such regulations.

(7) In the case of regulations under section 74AA which approve only certain transfers to a third country or international organisation that are[^c-0c4dc81c9c250f7500da1b0dc37fb7e1] specified or described in the regulations (in accordance with section 74AA(4)(b))[^c-a3956bf127b4dff3e08e52e3a45b9de4]—

- (a)[^c-c30e1b55c0964dff6ce0025bc68780b2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the lists published under subsection (6) must specify or describe the relevant transfers.
