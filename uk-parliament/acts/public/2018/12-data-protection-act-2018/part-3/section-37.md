# Data Protection Act 2018, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. The third data protection principle[^M_I_fd61ef18-6fb5-4eb5-8aa6-e67333347875]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The third data protection principle is that personal data processed for any of the law enforcement purposes must be adequate, relevant and not excessive in relation to the purpose for which it is processed.
