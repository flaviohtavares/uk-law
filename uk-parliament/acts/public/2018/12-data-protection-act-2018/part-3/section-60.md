# Data Protection Act 2018, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Processing under the authority of the controller or processor[^M_I_bf3711a7-f203-49fa-ba50-dc4a9245ae9a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A processor, and any person acting under the authority of a controller or processor, who has access to personal data may not process the data except—

- (a) on instructions from the controller, or
- (b) to comply with a legal obligation.
