# Data Protection Act 2018, section 124

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 124. Data protection and journalism code[^M_I_85c6af85-3c5b-4fbd-c7b8-108ae565afaa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commission[^key-f6cb8cc65962701e206d88297a9cbc91] must prepare a code of practice which contains—

- (a) practical guidance in relation to the processing of personal data for the purposes of journalism in accordance with the requirements of the data protection legislation, and
- (b) such other guidance as the Commission considers appropriate to promote good practice in the processing of personal data for the purposes of journalism.

(2) Where a code under this section is in force, the Commission may prepare amendments of the code or a replacement code.

(3) Before preparing a code or amendments under this section, the Commission must consult such of the following as the Commission considers appropriate—

- (a) trade associations;
- (b) data subjects;
- (c) persons who appear to the Commission to represent the interests of data subjects.

(4) A code under this section may include transitional provision or savings.

(5) In this section—

- “**good practice in the processing of personal data for the purposes of journalism**” means such practice in the processing of personal data for those purposes as appears to the Commission[^key-24b348d5e5c343b1a905bb7df9cb5322] to be desirable having regard to—
  - (a) the interests of data subjects and others [^key-008659743a491eb703127dd80e785478]..., and
  - (b) the special importance of the public interest in the freedom of expression and information;

    and includes compliance with the requirements of the data protection legislation;[^key-3cb3866fa4cb74bba6a6aa92e8ffe358]
- “**trade association**” includes a body representing controllers or processors.
