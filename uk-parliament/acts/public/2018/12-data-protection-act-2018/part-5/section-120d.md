# Data Protection Act 2018, section 120D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120D. Duty to consult other regulators[^c-e8b323b611736c350bc88b75920a0a2e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commission[^c-7daf001e2a7725a3f0befb179e7f9f33] must, at such times as the Commission considers appropriate, consult the persons mentioned in subsection (2) about how the manner in which the Commission exercises functions under the data protection legislation may affect economic growth, innovation and competition.

(2) The persons are—

- (a) such persons exercising regulatory functions as the Commission considers appropriate;
- (b) such other persons as the Commission considers appropriate.

(3) In this section, “**regulatory function**” has the meaning given by section 111 of the Deregulation Act 2015.
