# Data Protection Act 2018, section 131

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Disclosure of information to the Commission[^M_I_536accb5-c56e-4c05-9321-92e7d2337856][^key-3b33403857dbc61220ee36e1d681e187]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No enactment or rule of law prohibiting or restricting the disclosure of information precludes a person from providing the Commission[^key-18eb2338ebab8c44a067c7ecd77f0844] with information necessary for the discharge of the Commission's functions.

(2) But this section does not authorise the making of a disclosure which is prohibited by any of Parts 1 to 7 or Chapter 1 of Part 9 of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016.

(3) Until the repeal of Part 1 of the Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000 by paragraphs 45 and 54 of Schedule 10 to the Investigatory Powers Act 2016 is fully in force, subsection (2) has effect as if it included a reference to that Part.
