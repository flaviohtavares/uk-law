# Data Protection Act 2018, section 120A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120A. Principal objective[^key-e8b323b611736c350bc88b75920a0a2e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It is the principal objective of the Commission[^key-2214ab3d817f8c3fc88e176cdebc2e01], in carrying out functions under the data protection legislation—

- (a) to secure an appropriate level of protection for personal data, having regard to the interests of data subjects, controllers and others and matters of general public interest, and
- (b) to promote public trust and confidence in the processing of personal data.
