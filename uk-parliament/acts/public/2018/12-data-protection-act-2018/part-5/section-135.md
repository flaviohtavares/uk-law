# Data Protection Act 2018, section 135

## 135. Manifestly unfounded or excessive requests by data subjects etc[^M_I_9278b394-0992-4b27-bae4-d00400b4d831]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^key-081d12fd19e15895b2c24def7373caac] This section makes provision about cases in which a request made to the Commission[^key-b0e00a2e3f324ba8728b6f92d15b81e0], to which the Commission is required or authorised to respond under the data protection legislation, is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

(1) [^key-9ce829069ec9d3018f75efc1d11eddd0]... The Commission[^key-ecdb474b0aa3af024e92ab1a6a993fa2] may—

- (a) charge a reasonable fee for dealing with the request, or
- (b) refuse to act on the request.

(1A)[^key-eb5829a0c87d7e2a3d3519615683d044] In subsection (1)—

- (a) the reference in paragraph (a) to charging a reasonable fee is, in a case in which section 134 is relevant, a reference to doing so under that section, and
- (b) paragraph (b) is not to be read as implying anything about whether the Commission[^key-ece417ec9bf84a78042f2e7e45d76d71] may refuse to act on requests that are neither manifestly unfounded nor excessive.

(2) An example of a request that may be excessive is one that merely repeats the substance of previous requests.

(3) In any proceedings where there is an issue as to whether a request described in subsection A1[^key-3af973959f9df0279172807036d2ff65] is manifestly unfounded or excessive, it is for the Commission[^key-9d087aab962c399b76c184522e6e6f2d] to show that it is.

(4)[^key-de4e64996aab70646e626d9af11ff8da] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-c2c5d116ce6a43c27f2a73f15736bc95] Article 57(3) of the UK GDPR (performance of Commission's[^key-90896aeb9d8b506a5a5acd2b35361147] tasks generally to be free of charge for data subject) has effect subject to this section.
