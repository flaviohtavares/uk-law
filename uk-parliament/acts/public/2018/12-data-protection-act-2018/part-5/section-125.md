# Data Protection Act 2018, section 125

## 125. Approval of codes prepared under sections 121 to 124A[^c23287301][^c-36b37c6d1c4d4736d1dab263576fcebd][^c-7c26211f16d823ce5f8c9572b2d5bcfa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) When a code is prepared under section 121, 122, 123 , 124 or 124A[^c-6262e574d77f3ed97e165e248a2abadd]—

- (a) the Commission[^c-487799fad700214bfac2f72c70622055] must submit the final version to the Secretary of State, and
- (b) the Secretary of State must lay the code before Parliament.

(2)[^c-fc33832f82a4075d15e37423f9a081a3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) If, within the 40-day period, either House of Parliament resolves not to approve a code prepared under section 121, 122, 123 , 124 or 124A[^c-39f62cafffb590c7ab1e4cceea210f8b], the Commission[^c-6a0aeb0726d7741cac9acc455b1603b6] must not issue the code.

(4) If no such resolution is made within that period—

- (a) the Commission[^c-a3c5df74dcf946d161c83432701d4583] must issue the code, and
- (b) the code comes into force at the end of the period of 21 days beginning with the day on which it is issued.

(5)[^c-a6dd67613d93c269ece96506ae442a40] If the Commission[^c-8bc8a64d4fec6d59729a82db70ba9f4a] is prevented by subsection (3) from issuing a code that is not a replacement code, the Commission must prepare another version of the code.

(6) Nothing in subsection (3) prevents another version of the code being laid before Parliament.

(7) In this section, “**the 40-day period**” means—

- (a) if the code is laid before both Houses of Parliament on the same day, the period of 40 days beginning with that day, or
- (b) if the code is laid before the Houses of Parliament on different days, the period of 40 days beginning with the later of those days.

(8) In calculating the 40-day period, no account is to be taken of any whole days that fall within a[^c-31bb776982600aed74cbcf435538a957] period during which Parliament is dissolved or prorogued or during which both Houses of Parliament are adjourned for more than 4 days.

(9) This section, other than subsection (5)[^c-4fe4b7529553a13b08d627389944bf71], applies in relation to amendments prepared under section 121, 122, 123 , 124 or 124A[^c-c24fe2bf46c2f469339fd6168916f31d] as it applies in relation to codes prepared under those sections.
