## 117. Competence in relation to courts etc[^M_I_ab2d64c7-b166-45cf-842d-36ad8f908e2c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Act or the UK GDPR[^key-6fa02887b56cbb4386bbc62d8c2621c4] permits or requires the Commission[^key-586e5bc6535d488f10f696f1d557992f] to exercise functions in relation to the processing of personal data by—

- (a) an individual acting in a judicial capacity, or
- (b) a court or tribunal acting in its judicial capacity [^key-4670484b4cf22739c3b6dc134a6750aa]...
