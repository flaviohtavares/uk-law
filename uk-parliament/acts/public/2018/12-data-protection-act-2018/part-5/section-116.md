# Data Protection Act 2018, section 116

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Other general functions[^M_I_7313d5f1-d03c-4749-c489-1b35781632bf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^c-f36da07bc6957dc9cf0e7e6dcb8769d0] The Commission[^c-2ec4f2a6b6f3224bced50825fad9378f] is responsible for monitoring the application of Part 3 of this Act, in order to protect the fundamental rights and freedoms of individuals in relation to processing by a competent authority for any of the law enforcement purposes (as defined in Part 3) and to facilitate the free flow of personal data.

(1) The Commission[^c-837c57188968f4eae44b22d30aa5c72b] —

- (a)[^c-ff84a0a5b654d268377d223457dbe16d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) is to [^c-7db54b1f6e6bd6360c9da55e9a479e58]... the designated authority in the United Kingdom for the purposes of Article 13 of the Data Protection Convention.

(2) Schedule 13 confers general functions on the Commission[^c-b2e4a1b20185fed22935c76f5e88da5a] in connection with processing to which the UK GDPR[^c-aa86426e70dd097305ad06632c463ad0] does not apply (and see also sections 120A and 120B[^c-afeb5da48b3d2139dbfdcca63b14ba34]).

(3) This section and Schedule 13 are without prejudice to other functions conferred on the Commission[^c-99aa40903d762f7a3024e8da9e37522f], whether by this Act or otherwise.
