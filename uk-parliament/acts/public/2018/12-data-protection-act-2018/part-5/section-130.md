# Data Protection Act 2018, section 130

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 130. Records of national security certificates[^M_I_a875bc54-5a88-4079-b1a7-42f2b60fce36]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A Minister of the Crown who issues a certificate under section 27, 79 or 111 must send a copy of the certificate to the Commission[^key-c351c856bc3b09af5187bf958997f3fb].

(2) If the Commission receives a copy of a certificate under subsection (1), the Commission must publish a record of the certificate.

(3) The record must contain—

- (a) the name of the Minister who issued the certificate,
- (b) the date on which the certificate was issued, and
- (c) subject to subsection (4), the text of the certificate.

(4) The Commission[^key-d0d5fe8f3ee93b458d38ef69acde4c41] must not publish the text, or a part of the text, of the certificate if—

- (a) the Minister determines that publishing the text or that part of the text—
  - (i) would be against the interests of national security,
  - (ii) would be contrary to the public interest, or
  - (iii) might jeopardise the safety of any person, and
- (b) the Minister has notified the Commission of that determination.

(5) The Commission must keep the record of the certificate available to the public while the certificate is in force.

(6) If a Minister of the Crown revokes a certificate issued under section 27, 79 or 111, the Minister must notify the Commission.
