# Data Protection Act 2018, section 120C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120C. Strategy[^key-e8b323b611736c350bc88b75920a0a2e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commission[^key-65d1d46087d455321d1e5644a59af758] must prepare a strategy for carrying out the Commission's functions under the data protection legislation in accordance with the Commission's duties under—

- (a) sections 120A and 120B,
- (b) section 108 of the Deregulation Act 2015 (exercise of regulatory functions: economic growth), and
- (c) section 21 of the Legislative and Regulatory Reform Act 2006 (exercise of regulatory functions: principles).

(2) The Commission must—

- (a) review the strategy from time to time, and
- (b) revise the strategy as appropriate.

(3) The Commission must publish the strategy and any revised strategy.
