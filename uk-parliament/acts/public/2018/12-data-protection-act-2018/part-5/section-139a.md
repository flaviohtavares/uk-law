# Data Protection Act 2018, section 139A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 139A. Analysis of performance[^key-2ee236ebdbefaa20152eb5e389c3f15f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commission[^M_F_183bbab4-84eb-4766-965d-90e661914dc2] must prepare and publish an analysis of the Commissioner’s performance using key performance indicators.

(2) The analysis must be prepared and published at least annually.

(3) In this section, “**key performance indicators**” means factors by reference to which the Commission's[^M_F_0e072ec3-c12b-4405-de4c-fbdce7673e71] performance can be measured most effectively.
