# Data Protection Act 2018, section 139

## 139. Reporting to Parliament[^M_I_82c6bc0d-d6c8-4662-fcf6-6abbc290f7b3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commission[^key-e9b55413287307a323398536f1dba994] must—

- (a) produce a general report on the carrying out of the Commission's functions annually,
- (b) arrange for it to be laid before Parliament, and
- (c) publish it.

(1A)[^key-853556fb17545841a06d58d5f0af9994] In connection with the Commission's[^key-dc4ca2cbc81e1a1113eaa5bbea46e3eb] functions under the data protection legislation, the report must contain (among other things)—

- (a) a review of what the Commission has done during the reporting period to comply with the duties under—
  - (i) sections 120A and 120B,
  - (ii) section 108 of the Deregulation Act 2015, and
  - (iii) section 21 of the Legislative and Regulatory Reform Act 2006,

  including a review of the operation of the strategy prepared and published under section 120C;
- (b) a review of what the Commission has done during the reporting period to comply with the duty under section 120D.

(1B) In subsection (1A), “**the reporting period**” means the period to which the report relates.

(2)[^key-bedf5d2451f593d3f59d1740c5bd6e9c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^key-343856c646dbefbbbdfd8912476af912] The report under this section may include the annual report under section 161A.

(3) The Commission[^key-be6452eba9bbeb08cd147347e8e0f768] may produce other reports relating to the carrying out of the Commission's functions and arrange for them to be laid before Parliament.
