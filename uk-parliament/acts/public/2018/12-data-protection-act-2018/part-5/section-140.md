# Data Protection Act 2018, section 140

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 140. Publication by the Commission[^M_I_821aad00-1f4a-48bb-c29c-4b0444a499d7][^key-296dfd56ab057022ce5bf8719977507f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A duty under this Act for the Commission to publish a document is a duty for the Commission to publish it, or to arrange for it to be published, in such form and manner as the Commission considers appropriate.
