# Data Protection Act 2018, section 124C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12

## 124C. Impact assessments for codes of practice[^key-a45d5a7b4c8252c5c648eaeb70d56554]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a code is prepared under section 121, 122, 123, 124 or 124A, the Commission[^key-aa33782ad627e52457e0caf303ad0365] must carry out and publish an assessment of—

- (a) who would be likely to be affected by the code, and
- (b) the effect the code would be likely to have on them.

(2) This section applies in relation to amendments prepared under section 121, 122, 123, 124 or 124A as it applies in relation to codes prepared under those sections.
