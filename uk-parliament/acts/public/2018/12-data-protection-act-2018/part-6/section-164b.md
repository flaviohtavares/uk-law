# Data Protection Act 2018, section 164B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 164B. Controllers to notify the Commission of the number of complaints[^key-6e07ab4cb80ed81cfca1cbdc5f2f54bb][^key-9683c6b91df3b89dbb02dd20152102eb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations require a controller to notify the Commission[^key-3d7b81d70711cc64f7016d09e3df6619] of the number of complaints made to the controller under section 164A in periods specified or described in the regulations.

(2) Regulations under this section may provide that a controller is required to make a notification to the Commission in respect of a period only in circumstances specified in the regulations.

(3) Regulations under this section may include—

- (a) provision about a matter listed in subsection (4), or
- (b) provision conferring power on the Commission[^key-708e43947d13b67580c6acb9555224ce] to determine those matters.

(4) The matters are—

- (a) the form and manner in which a notification must be made,
- (b) the time at which, or period within which, a notification must be made, and
- (c) how the number of complaints made to a controller during a period is to be calculated.

(5) Regulations under this section are subject to the negative resolution procedure.
