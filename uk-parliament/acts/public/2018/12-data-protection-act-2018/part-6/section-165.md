## 165. Complaints by data subjects to the Commission[^M_I_25166470-99f6-4e01-e8f1-2ad0e67c766a][^key-9274c1a40869575904b799dca52918ec][^key-fd12715b324f2c9cb18d25bd2aa1d55d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-2daed435768192983f704d07048748b0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) A data subject may make a complaint to the Commission[^key-8bbb517b0f92fc90c401d359f3bcba94] if the data subject considers that, in connection with personal data relating to him or her, there is an infringement of the UK GDPR or[^key-ac41d640c37ff8169190d854e62d464b] Part 3 or 4 of this Act.

(3) The Commission must facilitate the making of complaints under subsection (2) by taking steps such as providing a complaint form which can be completed electronically and by other means.

(4) If the Commission receives a complaint under subsection (2), the Commission must—

- (a) take appropriate steps to respond to the complaint,
- (b) inform the complainant of the outcome of the complaint,
- (c) inform the complainant of the rights under section 166, and
- (d) if asked to do so by the complainant, provide the complainant with further information about how to pursue the complaint.

(5) The reference in subsection (4)(a) to taking appropriate steps in response to a complaint includes—

- (a) investigating the subject matter of the complaint, to the extent appropriate, and
- (b) informing the complainant about progress on the complaint, including about whether further investigation or co-ordination with a[^key-068079f0d354b65a415f2cdd6d742510] foreign designated authority is necessary.
