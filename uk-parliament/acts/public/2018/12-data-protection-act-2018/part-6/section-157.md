# Data Protection Act 2018, section 157

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 157. Maximum amount of penalty[^M_I_8ae9281f-1097-4ee9-f76f-cd8d2525eaf6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In relation to an infringement of a provision of the UK GDPR[^c-9ef9aecb69c6cba490e1936f1f0fb107], the maximum amount of the penalty that may be imposed by a penalty notice is—

- (a) the amount specified in Article 83 of the UK GDPR, or
- (b) if an amount is not specified there, the standard maximum amount.

(2) In relation to an infringement of a provision of Part 3 of this Act, the maximum amount of the penalty that may be imposed by a penalty notice is—

- (a) in relation to a failure to comply with section 35, 36, 37, 38(1), 39(1), 40, 44, 45, 46, 47, 48, 50B, 50C,[^c-a9e9900b37646b445f84ef39a0d76cc0] 52, 53, 73, [^c-690c58c64b417a3b6f1d897287ce2cc0]... 75, 76, 77 or 78, the higher maximum amount, and
- (b) otherwise, the standard maximum amount.

(3) In relation to an infringement of a provision of Part 4 of this Act, the maximum amount of the penalty that may be imposed by a penalty notice is—

- (a) in relation to a failure to comply with section 86, 87, 88, 89, 90, 91, 93, 94, 100 or 109, the higher maximum amount, and
- (b) otherwise, the standard maximum amount.

(4) In relation to a failure to comply with an information notice, an assessment notice , an interview notice[^c-2686f9acbc03b702d067ea3f8d19b78f] or an enforcement notice, the maximum amount of the penalty that may be imposed by a penalty notice is the higher maximum amount.

(4A)[^c-176437c9bc37e19d99e8d60b0503ea3c] In relation to an infringement of section 164A or of regulations under section 164B, the maximum amount of the penalty that may be imposed by a penalty notice is the standard maximum amount.

(5) The “higher maximum amount” is—

- (a) in the case of an undertaking, £17,500,000[^c-ea99f9fb002f73f760fea222fdb9ef92] or 4% of the undertaking's total annual worldwide turnover in the preceding financial year, whichever is higher, or
- (b) in any other case, £17,500,000.

(6) The “standard maximum amount” is—

- (a) in the case of an undertaking, £8,700,000[^c-394e213ab391d7a2301095c644dcd821] or 2% of the undertaking's total annual worldwide turnover in the preceding financial year, whichever is higher, or
- (b) in any other case, £8,700,000.
