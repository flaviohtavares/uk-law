# Data Protection Act 2018, section 168

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 168. Compensation for contravention of the UK GDPR[^M_I_d2cfddfb-2465-4e45-adcb-81429283652b][^key-7391cf8e2ee42e601b02ca8e23000688]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Article 82 of the UK GDPR[^key-8ddd7db9cbd1f85a5d9da877704c4c39] (right to compensation for material or non-material damage), “**non-material damage**” includes distress.

(2) Subsection (3) applies where—

- (a) in accordance with rules of court, proceedings under Article 82 of the UK GDPR[^key-5f188da75fed9d0a5af86af0820ced84] are brought by a representative body on behalf of a person, and
- (b) a court orders the payment of compensation.

(3) The court may make an order providing for the compensation to be paid on behalf of the person to—

- (a) the representative body, or
- (b) such other person as the court thinks fit.
