# Data Protection Act 2018, section 159

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 159. Amount of penalties: supplementary[^c23269151][^M_I_95a2e458-7d97-4c0c-d3e1-9597b82c7433]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of Article 83 of the UK GDPR[^key-522cc2ffd4fbefd6bc331626b37d8649] and section 157, the Secretary of State may by regulations—

- (a) provide that a person of a description specified in the regulations is or is not an undertaking, and
- (b) make provision about how an undertaking's turnover is to be determined.

(2) For the purposes of Article 83 of the UK GDPR[^key-9a9736041c34fdc367335955850c35ae], section 157 and section 158, the Secretary of State may by regulations provide that a period is or is not a financial year.

(3) Regulations under this section are subject to the affirmative resolution procedure.
