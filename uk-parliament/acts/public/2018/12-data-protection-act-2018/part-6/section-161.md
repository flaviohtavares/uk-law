# Data Protection Act 2018, section 161

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 161. Approval of first guidance about regulatory action[^key-2b9674d7bfd3049679cc97dc1b298995]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) When the first guidance is produced under section 160(1)—

- (a) the Commission[^key-d9f6a3cbcda0cda614f106b6fc3c70a9] must submit the final version to the Secretary of State, and
- (b) the Secretary of State must lay the guidance before Parliament.

(2) If, within the 40-day period, either House of Parliament resolves not to approve the guidance—

- (a) the Commission[^key-3d619e65d9e2d5ee65de63e8b0eabba0] must not issue the guidance, and
- (b) the Commission must produce another version of the guidance (and this section applies to that version).

(3) If, within the 40-day period, no such resolution is made—

- (a) the Commission[^key-16ecb626e4ffc21ebd7c7d60e998dda2] must issue the guidance, and
- (b) the guidance comes into force at the end of the period of 21 days beginning with the day on which it is issued.

(4) Nothing in subsection (2)(a) prevents another version of the guidance being laid before Parliament.

(5) In this section, “**the 40-day period**” means—

- (a) if the guidance is laid before both Houses of Parliament on the same day, the period of 40 days beginning with that day, or
- (b) if the guidance is laid before the Houses of Parliament on different days, the period of 40 days beginning with the later of those days.

(6) In calculating the 40-day period, no account is to be taken of any whole days that fall within a[^key-067b411a6f252d978f5363bb7f8fd473] period during which Parliament is dissolved or prorogued or during which both Houses of Parliament are adjourned for more than 4 days.
