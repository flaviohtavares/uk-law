# Data Protection Act 2018, section 145

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 145. Information orders[^c-9702e78daee1441e8db8fb0bd641a1d1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if, on an application by the Commission[^c-1c2bb0ca6c03fb49b003b58c76789698], a court is satisfied that a person has failed to comply with a requirement of an information notice.

(2) The court may make an order requiring the person to provide to the Commission some or all of the following—

- (a) information or documents[^M_F_2c5847ba-4fc3-4d86-9631-30fb0ddb34a8] referred to in the information notice;
- (b) other information or documents[^M_F_46b707e2-c0e1-42c5-a4a7-eb6050d6e114] which the court is satisfied the Commission requires, having regard to the statement included in the notice in accordance with section 142(2)(b).

(3) The order—

- (a) may specify the form in which the information or documents[^M_F_2e6eca07-b3a0-4017-d7ee-0a83257d643a] must be provided,
- (b) must specify the time at which, or the period within which, the information or documents[^M_F_41ef45cc-49c8-4f7d-a94b-ab30ac26c3ec] must be provided, and
- (c) may specify the place where the information or documents[^M_F_35955677-8063-4739-e6ff-1c9ba27ebd85] must be provided.
