# Data Protection Act 2018, section 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Processing to which this Part applies[^M_I_790341d6-a730-4825-e4e6-b3e84cad95a1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^c-2f056c8fbea11fd72923190c3a60a7e8] This Part—

- (a) applies to processing of personal data by an intelligence service, and
- (b) applies to processing of personal data by a qualifying competent authority where the processing is the subject of a designation notice that is for the time being in force (see sections 82A to 82E).

(1) This Part applies only[^c-d1152f324a931564f92cc8544265fbed] to—

- (a) processing[^c-ec6f56978250d6a8ac23a54c34201ea0] of personal data wholly or partly by automated means, and
- (b) processing[^c-bbf1a58d25121030163f2ecc32091b86] otherwise than by automated means of personal data which forms part of a filing system or is intended to form part of a filing system.

(2) In this Part, “**intelligence service**” means—

- (a) the Security Service;
- (b) the Secret Intelligence Service;
- (c) the Government Communications Headquarters.

(2A)[^c-dcdb72650fa4d8b42926e24c738770ee] In this Part—

- “**competent authority**” has the same meaning as in Part 3;
- “**qualifying competent authority**” means a competent authority specified or described in regulations made by the Secretary of State.

(3) A reference in this Part to the processing of personal data is to processing to which this Part applies.

(4)[^c-47b298b6510458494f665aeeee02e734] Regulations under this section are subject to the affirmative resolution procedure.
