# Data Protection Act 2018, section 110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. National security[^c-28f473b60938488229022a08cd48e4ad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A provision mentioned in subsection (2) does not apply to personal data to which this Part applies if exemption from the provision is required for the purpose of safeguarding national security.

(2) The provisions are—

- (a) Chapter 2 of this Part[^c-1851ffdfb55a1ae4837371dce443325b] (the data protection principles), except section 86(1)(a) and (2) and Schedules 9 and 10;
- (b) Chapter 3 of this Part[^c-4d9aa0606c413206cb1320a83695979d] (rights of data subjects);
- (c) in Chapter 4 of this Part[^c-94d96ee0fcb3bd0d69d2d1921830ed5c], section 108 (communication of a personal data breach to the Commission[^c-e19c05a0c3f96ede3e7d0feb5cff9083]);
- (d) in Part 5—
  - (i) section 119 (inspection in accordance with international obligations);
  - (ii) in Schedule 13 (other general functions of the Commission[^c-dfa708b4287c65b4276eaf390e6af120]), paragraphs 1(a) and (g) and 2;
- (e) in Part 6—
  - (i) sections 142 to 154 and Schedule 15 (Commission's[^c-0cc2c563bc8714c65d2d58154d5d1002] notices and powers of entry and inspection);
  - (ii) sections 170 to 173 (offences relating to personal data);
  - (iii) sections 174 to 176 (provision relating to the special purposes).
