# Data Protection Act 2018, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. General obligations of the controller[^c-74ae32c1534b7fb225df9f0b0a326da8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each controller must implement appropriate measures—

- (a) to ensure, and
- (b) to be able to demonstrate, in particular to the Commission[^c-1fa3a01bfea3b864f21dcc3177b11bfb],

that the processing of personal data complies with the requirements of this Part.
