## 2. Protection of personal data[^M_I_da01c4c2-f825-40ab-f69c-0d6e773451b9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The UK GDPR[^c-e7856245e743d7a5de4a1d03a5a413ed] and this Act protect individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, in particular by—

- (a) requiring personal data to be processed lawfully and fairly, on the basis of the data subject's consent or another specified basis,
- (b) conferring rights on the data subject to obtain information about the processing of personal data and to require inaccurate personal data to be rectified, and
- (c) conferring functions on the Commission, giving it[^c-7e3e5f1ca48599b2905972ea9c92249f] responsibility for monitoring and enforcing their provisions.
