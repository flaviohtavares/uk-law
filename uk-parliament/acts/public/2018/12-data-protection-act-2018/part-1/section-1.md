# Data Protection Act 2018, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Overview[^M_I_881dda53-b717-425e-8142-f7976a8174ac]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act makes provision about the processing of personal data.

(2) Most processing of personal data is subject to the UK GDPR[^key-0980e055af9390b4571e201b8f8aafaa].

(3) Part 2 supplements the UK GDPR[^key-29e112b63328c8a746536836db1c3966].

(4) Part 3 makes provision about the processing of personal data by competent authorities for law enforcement purposes [^key-9a8be2cf53d7c39ed62f2d07d32d1c20]....

(5) Part 4 makes provision about the processing of personal data by the intelligence services (and certain processing carried out by competent authorities jointly with the intelligence services)[^key-494dd52ba56482beabc25e56147b27ce].

(6) Part 5 makes provision about the Information Commission[^key-8f5d714ca917fa486b29b58fb4e7af0e].

(7) Part 6 makes provision about the enforcement of the data protection legislation.

(8) Part 7 makes supplementary provision, including provision about the application of this Act to the Crown and to Parliament.
