## 4. Processing to which this Part applies[^M_I_16855a45-e444-4356-a88e-7307bc6bca55]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part is relevant to most processing of personal data.

(2) This Part[^c-8a3b5bb5e99ea65efdafbe742d8645aa]—

- (a) applies to the types of processing of personal data to which the UK GDPR[^c-c7ea9a9c87ca4e89d73dbd273cfa8393] applies by virtue of Article 2 of the UK GDPR, and
- (b) supplements, and must be read with, the UK GDPR.
