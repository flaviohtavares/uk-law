## 5. Definitions[^M_I_02957c04-4ec2-4b55-f033-8049c2809d96][^c-aee4aa208301e598dbb8564e8e9b226b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Terms used in [^c-2fbe32aca2b996b3d860ba43d5ed4d2a]... this Part and in the UK GDPR[^c-7e8a96925239e5ff10b4c1f795f37f75] have the same meaning in this Part as[^c-1a7fa63e5530837c3583f0e5c7220882] they have in the UK GDPR.

(2) In subsection (1), the reference to a term's meaning in the UK GDPR[^c-22c9cb2cd2037b308003142aeeee5b32] is to its meaning in the UK GDPR read with any provision of this Part[^c-4166d151c2161e3cfa1baed97addc41e] which modifies the term's meaning for the purposes of the UK GDPR.

(3) Subsection (1) is subject to any provision in this Part[^c-a2c39c1b8f50d64d3eab992537ab337c] which provides expressly for the term to have a different meaning and to section 204.

(4)[^c-908fd5d459d7a579cab7061f3e5f0f93] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c-908fd5d459d7a579cab7061f3e5f0f93] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c-908fd5d459d7a579cab7061f3e5f0f93] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) A reference in [^c-7acebc72466e110599be1c7c05f2d8a7]... this Part to the processing of personal data is to processing to which this Part[^c-4d10128808e45c569543c313b90f3f68] applies.

(8) Sections 3 and 205 include definitions of other expressions used in this Part.
