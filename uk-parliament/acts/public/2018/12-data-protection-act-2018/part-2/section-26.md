## 26. National security and defence exemption[^M_I_b67c4109-933e-4919-ceba-5e061b4d1a4c][^key-cdd8c10a5150531bb30b7690485fc5fc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A provision of the UK GDPR[^key-36a3e256a527d89c5a740d38dbd8973e] or this Act mentioned in subsection (2) does not apply to personal data to which the UK GDPR[^key-6119956a88aea2b8d3d486d7acad1a04] applies if exemption from the provision is required for—

- (a) the purpose of safeguarding national security, or
- (b) defence purposes.

(2) The provisions are—

- (a) Chapter II of the UK GDPR[^key-24c67fb416c38423b577d923c55b3ec9] (principles) except for—
  - (i) Article 5(1)(a) (lawful, fair and transparent processing), so far as it requires processing of personal data to be lawful;
  - (ii) Article 6 (lawfulness of processing);
  - (iii) Article 9 (processing of special categories of personal data);
- (b) Chapter III of the UK GDPR[^key-e2d548a4783586924138a4f64263651f] (rights of data subjects);
- (c) in Chapter IV of the UK GDPR[^key-0228d23a866d3b67264125f2ef6b7ee5]—
  - (i) Article 33 (notification of personal data breach to the Commission[^key-657228c91c8e204623d31631361ce50b]);
  - (ii) Article 34 (communication of personal data breach to the data subject);
- (d) Chapter V of the UK GDPR[^key-816d2aa6bc1c3851c4bfbfc17229dd0e] (transfers of personal data to third countries or international organisations);
- (e) in Chapter VI of the UK GDPR[^key-324729d85d46bf435833df74eab03372]—
  - (i) Article 57(1)(a) and (h) ( Commission's[^key-35c7fcb5e70091acaf9cc96fa12994e3] duties to monitor and enforce the UK GDPR and to conduct investigations);
  - (ii) Article 58 (investigative, corrective, authorisation and advisory powers of Commission);
- (f) Chapter VIII of the UK GDPR[^key-1c030dd28010faaf2bbe31a050893ce4] (remedies, liabilities and penalties) except for—
  - (ai)[^key-339f5d00d9c5075aca5286b3a4de857f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (i) Article 83 (general conditions for imposing administrative fines);
  - (ii) Article 84 (penalties);
- (fa)[^key-b1051ad1ae2b860c2886c33cde88988e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g) in Part 5 of this Act—
  - (i) in section 115 (general functions of the Commission[^key-b29ea313ec3eaaea9b6f7d655d3640a4]), subsections (3) and (8);
  - (ii) in section 115, subsection (9), so far as it relates to Article 58(2)(i) of the UK GDPR[^key-26e53c4365c637973f201bac1a9d893e];
  - (iii) section 119 (inspection in accordance with international obligations);
  - (iv)[^key-cb40895550fff4b1df945a592f3d2928] section 119A (standard clauses for transfers to third countries);
- (h) in Part 6 of this Act—
  - (i) sections 142 to 154 and Schedule 15 ( Commission[^key-400b29786969de766b1706cd05b81d0e] notices and powers of entry and inspection);
  - (ii) sections 170 to 173 (offences relating to personal data);
- (i) in Part 7 of this Act, section 187 (representation of data subjects).
