## 16. Power to make further exemptions etc by regulations[^c23266361][^M_I_5d97a9f6-9446-4bef-ef73-af80493561f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following powers to make provision altering the application of the UK GDPR[^key-1e4185b59d1be365db23794ea0ee1651] may be exercised by way of regulations made by the Secretary of State under this section—

- (a) the power in Article 6(3) [^key-57bc77e64fe7acec0922ea3c97dedc76]... to lay down a legal basis containing specific provisions to adapt the application of rules of the UK GDPR[^key-4d6b908812e073f2b90981ecb1756d66] where processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation, for the performance of a task in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority;
- (b) the power in Article 23(1) to make provision[^key-41f1fed7dde177dcbd4959f74da05f9b] restricting the scope of the obligations and rights mentioned in that Article where necessary and proportionate to safeguard certain objectives of general public interest;
- (c) the power in Article 85(2) to provide for exemptions or derogations from certain Chapters of the UK GDPR[^key-7716f17e15cfe1f6e1c380c980402f84] where necessary to reconcile the protection of personal data with the freedom of expression and information.

(2) Regulations under this section may—

- (a) amend Schedules 2 to 4—
  - (i) by adding or varying provisions, and
  - (ii) by omitting provisions added by regulations under this section, [^key-3d17c501d2052e26fccb827029f0a864]...
- (b) consequentially amend section 15 , and[^key-53926fd489411a896de84bf3547618a6]
- (c) consequentially amend the UK GDPR by adding, varying or omitting a reference to section 15, Schedule 2, 3 or 4, this section or regulations under this section.

(3) Regulations under this section are subject to the affirmative resolution procedure.
