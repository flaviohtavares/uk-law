# Data Protection Act 2018, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/12

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Exemptions etc[^M_I_391b74dc-ff38-40f0-dbeb-d4d0a0c497ae]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedules 2, 3 and 4 make provision for exemptions from, and restrictions and adaptations of the application of, rules of the UK GDPR[^key-7790bea0d0dc4a0926d24673942c24b3].

(2) In Schedule 2—

- (a) Part 1 makes provision adapting or restricting the application of rules contained in Articles 13 to 21 and 34 of the UK GDPR[^key-e292a19194c3f4fab612e8e3dcf45f87] in specified circumstances (of a kind described in[^key-391aec4a4ec0685cabdd1aa28a8ba785] Article 6(3) and Article 23(1) of the UK GDPR)[^key-21f4d7f3e477eb190524128d785be4e6];
- (b) Part 2 makes provision restricting the application of rules contained in Articles 13 to 21 and 34 of the UK GDPR[^key-f2da6f3a2b23afc935273439d0cf1789] in specified circumstances (of a kind described in[^key-493b6699ab43b773b99b03d2551ed8b8] Article 23(1) of the UK GDPR)[^key-7ef94efaaff0ee10936b4c95ef7a9bcb];
- (c) Part 3 makes provision restricting the application of Article 15 of the UK GDPR[^key-ab5b474d3b7afb2f028fbe0f93ce632c] where this is necessary to protect the rights of others (of a kind described in[^key-e6d674ace935bcf1083b558a35b32428] Article 23(1) of the UK GDPR)[^key-1d0aacbc6a3f57fb9614a5fc01346883];
- (d) Part 4 makes provision restricting the application of rules contained in Articles 13 to 15 of the UK GDPR[^key-e19cbfe5436f8c558c28e7bfce051537] in specified circumstances (of a kind described in[^key-3ea752fe762439ea500ec02ee2749c5a] Article 23(1) of the UK GDPR)[^key-ef6078ac24fc77609c82e05a80eb24d7];
- (e) Part 5 makes provision containing exemptions or derogations from Chapters II, III, IV and V of the UK GDPR[^key-52ed2b8cb72fb84acd6e2db1c86a4a19] for reasons relating to freedom of expression (of a kind described in Article 85(2) of the UK GDPR)[^key-10250dcd0017993ac3d096de2e88503b];
- (f) Part 6 makes provision containing derogations from rights contained in Articles 15, 16, 18, 19, 20 and 21 of the UK GDPR[^key-eb8139f6b70ca4dec02be30470479741] for scientific or historical research purposes, statistical purposes and archiving purposes [^key-7bf18aacb7021b4679ff9e76c5cec185]....

(3) Schedule 3 makes provision restricting the application of rules contained in Articles 13 to 21 of the UK GDPR[^key-83e7b4be5485db05a2217193e409052e] to health, social work, education and child abuse data (of a kind described in[^key-d3e1d574f1063ebf020fe69e09894006] Article 23(1) of the UK GDPR)[^key-664f0d0fd099ec05107c1fc9f6ba7bbc].

(4) Schedule 4 makes provision restricting the application of rules contained in Articles 13 to 21 of the UK GDPR[^key-624b7f167b9be0eda00270c5e40aba6b] to information the disclosure of which is prohibited or restricted by an enactment (of a kind described in[^key-7ce231d6f630571ef964323847674614] Article 23(1) of the UK GDPR)[^key-3f969b23e46516434ae2e89ddc69df68].

(4A)[^key-3c842d85c1197ff1b42f60c711f26783] In connection with the manual unstructured processing of personal data held by an FOI public authority, see Chapter 3 of this Part (sections 21, 24 and 25).

(5) In connection with the safeguarding of national security and with defence, see Chapter 3 of this Part (sections 26 to 28)[^key-fcf261b8f1782b32726129a6c66bdebd].
