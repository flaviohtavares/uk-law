# Data Protection Act 2018, section 8

## 8. Lawfulness of processing: public interest etc[^M_I_823d7c60-4c49-4046-d084-63e3c878f220]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 6(1) of the UK GDPR[^key-5fa4e1b4e3138284c1e0c09af0505f2a] (lawfulness of processing), the reference in point (e) to processing of personal data that is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of [^key-47eb0702215830c476b0ed47eb46e7dd]... official authority includes processing of personal data that is necessary for—

- (a) the administration of justice,
- (b) the exercise of a function of either House of Parliament,
- (c) the exercise of a function conferred on a person by an enactment or rule of law,
- (d) the exercise of a function of the Crown, a Minister of the Crown or a government department, or
- (e) an activity that supports or promotes democratic engagement.
