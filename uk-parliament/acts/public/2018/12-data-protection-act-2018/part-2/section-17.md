# Data Protection Act 2018, section 17

## 17. Accreditation of certification providers[^M_I_001184dc-ddbe-45f4-bebc-3ba7048d50bc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Accreditation of a person as a certification provider is only valid when carried out by—

- (a) the Commission[^key-0a3de8d61f436011cbc7cc49da4e1d96], or
- (b) the UK national accreditation body[^key-847feaddc6148bc37c46c1a3e5b8905c].

(2) The Commission[^key-8591ce8790c946c5c58981f37429dfbe] may only accredit a person as a certification provider where the Commission—

- (a) has published a statement that the Commission will carry out such accreditation, and
- (b) has not published a notice withdrawing that statement.

(3) The UK national accreditation body[^key-358700da09bd3b3db589d842d7dd9ab7] may only accredit a person as a certification provider where the Commission—

- (a) has published a statement that the body may carry out such accreditation, and
- (b) has not published a notice withdrawing that statement.

(4) The publication of a notice under subsection (2)(b) or (3)(b) does not affect the validity of any accreditation carried out before its publication.

(5) Schedule 5 makes provision about reviews of, and appeals from, a decision relating to accreditation of a person as a certification provider.

(6) The UK national accreditation body[^key-c7f86e0fd7e97d7dc7de053dc830c822] may charge a reasonable fee in connection with, or incidental to, the carrying out of the body's functions under this section, Schedule 5 and Article 43 of the UK GDPR[^key-3c2ca8b8a39fe6a1c43d2d0d0aa52508].

(7) The UK national accreditation body[^key-835ab8f4363d0fb83f0c4cf209c3a562] must provide the Secretary of State with such information relating to its functions under this section, Schedule 5 and Article 43 of the UK GDPR[^key-0261d887a09ac80ed5671762eabc2d89] as the Secretary of State may reasonably require.

(8) In this section—

- “**certification provider**” means a person who issues certification for the purposes of Article 42 of the UK GDPR[^key-b2e90f163831a820a96c66b0fee0479c];
- “**the UK national accreditation body[^key-9cd6eafc08f53ed26f3cab27f3bd17ed]**” means the UK national accreditation body for the purposes of Article 4(1) of Regulation [(EC) No 765/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/765) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 July 2008 setting out the requirements for accreditation and market surveillance relating to the marketing of products and repealing Regulation [(EEC) No 339/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1993/339).
