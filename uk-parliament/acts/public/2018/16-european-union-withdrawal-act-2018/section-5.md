## 5. Exceptions to savings and incorporation[^key-941b201a8b3f20e501e7597851226cf9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^key-d1a0a46fb8e2d0e4b37043dd03b742c5] The principle of the supremacy of EU law is not part of domestic law.

This applies after the end of 2023, in relation to any enactment or rule of law (whenever passed or made).

(A2)[^key-ba24e0a722fc7a3c2e93c13ce2c21a97][^key-ce60633f17c8dade0b0fd905b2317156] Any provision of assimilated direct[^key-ba86ffc3e947dc618470172bc7ae7da4] legislation—

- (a) must, so far as possible, be read and given effect in a way which is compatible with all domestic enactments, and
- (b) is subject to all domestic enactments, so far as it is incompatible with them.

(A3) Subsection (A2) is subject to—

- (a) sections 183A and[^key-3e782cb39d615c0e1e527d50c866aaf5] 186 of the Data Protection Act 2018 (protection of prohibitions, restrictions and data subject’s rights)[^key-e6152fb0b2ad966b4645418244df8242] (and see also section 183B(3) of that Act)[^key-45b2db73d9c7616cef8123e20f5f2619];
- (b) regulations under section 7(1) of the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023.

(A4)[^key-7b27c2d04514b3c9582063661c6f8ec1] No general principle of EU law is part of domestic law after the end of 2023.

(5)[^key-272c60c21298796a5d2578524de0c86c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
