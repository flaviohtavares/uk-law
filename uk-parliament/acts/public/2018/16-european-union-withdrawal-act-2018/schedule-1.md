## SCHEDULE 1 — Further provision about exceptions to savings and incorporation[^key-e1697ad4adc8129d48bd3118398f8168]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 5(6)

### Challenges to validity of assimilated law[^key-6df5f1190f170309fe42d8e46c99d32e]

**1.**[^c23327481] (1)[^key-0ed8a96a3d7951ff01071eae8dfef5fc][^key-a67cee20e7ca5099200cb1c899ed32f7] There is no right in domestic law on or after IP completion day to challenge any assimilated law on the basis that, immediately before IP completion day, an EU instrument was invalid.

(2) Sub-paragraph (1) does not apply so far as—

- (a)[^key-0ed8a96a3d7951ff01071eae8dfef5fc] the European Court has decided before IP completion day that the instrument is invalid, or
- (b)[^key-1761fd0f8cd7d6bcec9f469c3cd365f8] the challenge is of a kind described, or provided for, in regulations made by a Minister of the Crown.

(3) Regulations under sub-paragraph (2)(b) may (among other things) provide for a challenge which would otherwise have been against an EU institution to be against a public authority in the United Kingdom.

### Rule in Francovich

**4.**[^key-a5ffff771f1fb15920a19842bf691537] There is no right in domestic law on or after IP completion day to damages in accordance with the rule in *Francovich*.

### Interpretation

**5.**[^key-476e4c4823e0a97299c69ab04e81135b] (1) References in section 5 and this Schedule to the principle of the supremacy of EU law, the Charter of Fundamental Rights, any general principle of EU law or the rule in *Francovich* are to be read as references to that principle, Charter or rule so far as it would otherwise continue to be, or form part of, domestic law on or after IP completion day by virtue of section 2, 3, 4 or 6(3) or (6) and otherwise in accordance with this Act[^key-75065d76f53caea2db4a55392649b61d].
