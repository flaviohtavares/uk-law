#### Existing ambulatory references to assimilated direct legislation[^c-3c047dd837103d22e4c5125ebfbdca84]

**1.**[^c-803927beb9651b5b3ab68550f4d7fee8] (1) Any reference so far as it[^c-f51d29a389026a9fa3b2bc349d4c3856], immediately before IP completion day[^c-f2e0bbeeb130d1642aa47f5d3b69a8ca]—
