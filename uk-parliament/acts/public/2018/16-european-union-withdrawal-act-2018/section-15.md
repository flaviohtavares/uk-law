## 15. Publication and rules of evidence[^c23327451]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-35f3d20965b56421df89629d02e6015d] Part 1 of Schedule 5 (which makes provision for the publication by the Queen's Printer of copies of retained direct EU legislation and related information) has effect.

(2)[^c-577e1f84574cfabba6503cab06f6de88] Part 2 of Schedule 5 (which makes provision about rules of evidence) has effect.
