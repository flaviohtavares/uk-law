# European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2025-08-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2018/16

## 7. Status of assimilated law[^M_I_74cbf84f-328f-4d92-9459-2f64ba135510][^M_C_4e661e04-0c44-4744-d373-8e5294dd903c][^key-3ae71ba588364c16f7a8f9ae06dd137c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-62c4c4d497acc329edce3e1d288c1273] Anything which—

- (a) was, immediately before exit day, primary legislation of a particular kind, subordinate legislation of a particular kind or another enactment of a particular kind, and
- (b) continues to be domestic law on and after exit day by virtue of section 1A(2) or 1B(2)[^key-960a58851e0c3ce9520f4bdae9175d0d],

continues to be domestic law as an enactment of the same kind.

(1A)[^key-0b91753634e260fe5ef62c767cf20c2b] Anything which—

- (a) was, immediately before IP completion day, primary legislation of a particular kind, subordinate legislation of a particular kind or another enactment of a particular kind, and
- (b) continues to be domestic law on and after IP completion day by virtue of section 2,

continues to be domestic law as an enactment of the same kind.

(4A)[^key-7a960534d65fd7fb0462776215cce7af] Assimilated direct[^key-ee14569403aef350e9813add659a09ec] legislation [^key-d18bb3a71ca2f8d82c103e919aa23719]... may only be modified by—

- (a) primary legislation, or
- (b) subordinate legislation so far as it is made under a power which permits such a modification by virtue of—
  - (i) paragraph 3, 8(3), 11A, 11B or 12(3) of Schedule 8,
  - (ii) any other provision made by or under this Act,
  - (iii) any provision made by or under an Act of Parliament passed before, and in the same Session as, this Act, or
  - (iv) any provision made on or after the passing of this Act by or under primary legislation.

(5)[^key-a18ce32300af300b871670bbc5fdfaa2] For other provisions about the status of assimilated[^key-d61d7c4ae1c714de35ad343bba19c8e3] law, see—

- (a) section 5 (A1) to (A3)[^key-fe28199ebd42a43fab5bd4f9f44d0a11] and (7)[^key-33eee758db6edf9d993c8a3e744020f8] (status of assimilated law in relation to other enactments or rules of law),
- (b) section 6 (status of assimilated[^key-884bc6dfbae68ce94f01ebd43a9d0f26] case law [^key-7769128871b989978d5260aa6549ecc3]...),
- (ba)[^key-b8899f34c9b40b6ed863d620bd261d0e] section 7C (status of case law of European Court etc. in relation to assimilated law which is relevant separation agreement law),
- (c) section 15(2) and Part 2 of Schedule 5 (status of assimilated law for the purposes of the rules of evidence),
- (d)[^key-90d8094d7fabed53d1bb7d530c108730] paragraph 16 of Schedule 8 (information about Scottish instruments which amend or revoke subordinate legislation under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972),
- (e) paragraphs 19 and 20 of that Schedule (status of certain assimilated direct[^key-784f7f8159a9e8e39f34f5a9de6bf3f3] legislation for the purposes of the Interpretation Act 1978), and
- (f) paragraph 30 of that Schedule (status of assimilated direct[^key-d262829cc5065b9a5adc7c925d6c0005] legislation for the purposes of the Human Rights Act 1998).

(6)[^key-faa6d8321755a62e0fdfa45b2def720d][^key-62c4c4d497acc329edce3e1d288c1273] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
