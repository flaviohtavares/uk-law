# Finance Act 2026, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (118).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Minor corrections

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) [IHTA 1984](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/51/contents) is amended in accordance with subsections (2) and (3).

(2) In section 267ZD (further provision about elections under section 267ZC), in [subsection (8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/51/section/267D), for “a lifetime election” substitute “an election under section 267ZC”.

(3) In [section 157](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/51/section/157) (non-residents’ bank accounts), in [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/51/section/157), in each of [paragraphs (c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/51/section/157) and [(d)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/51/section/157), omit “not”.

(4) The amendment made by subsection (2) is treated as having come into force on 6 April 2025.

(5) The amendment made by subsection (3) is treated as having come into force on 26 November 2025.

(6) [Section 157](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/51/section/157)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/51/section/157) of [IHTA 1984](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/51/contents) is treated as having had effect for the period beginning with 6 April 2025 and ending with 26 November 2025 with the omission of its [paragraphs (c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/51/section/157) and [(d)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/51/section/157) (and the insertion of “or” after paragraph (a)).
