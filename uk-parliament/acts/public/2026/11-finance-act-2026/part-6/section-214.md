## 214. Amendments to existing legislation: removal of privilege exemption

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In [section 316C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/section/316C) of [FA 2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/contents) (disclosure of tax avoidance schemes)—

- (a) omit [subsection (4A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/section/316C);
- (b) in [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/section/316C)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/section/316C)—
  - (i) the words from “make representations” to the end become sub-paragraph (i);
  - (ii) after that sub-paragraph insert

    > , and
    >
    > - (ii) where section 209(2) of FA 2026 applies, provide a declaration made under that subsection substantiating those representations.

    .

(2) In [paragraph 36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/schedule/17/paragraph/36) of [Schedule 17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/schedule/17) to [F(No.2)A 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/contents) (disclosure of tax avoidance schemes: VAT and other indirect taxes)—

- (a) omit [sub-paragraph (4A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/schedule/17/paragraph/36);
- (b) in [sub-paragraph (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/schedule/17/paragraph/36)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/schedule/17/paragraph/36)—
  - (i) the words from “make representations” to the end become sub-paragraph (i);
  - (ii) after that sub-paragraph insert

    > , and
    >
    > - (ii) where section 209(2) of FA 2026 applies, provide a declaration made under that subsection substantiating those representations.

    .

(3) In [section 86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/section/86) of [FA 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/contents) (publication of information about tax avoidance schemes)—

- (a) omit [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/section/86)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/section/86) (and the “or” before it);
