# Finance Act 2026, section 218

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (118).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 218. Consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In [paragraph 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/26/schedule/34/paragraph/5) of [Schedule 34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/26/schedule/34) to [FA 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/26/contents) (promoters of tax avoidance schemes: threshold conditions)—

- (a) in [sub-paragraph (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/26/schedule/34/paragraph/5)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/26/schedule/34/paragraph/5), for “the tribunal” substitute “an authorised officer”;
- (b) in [sub-paragraph (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/26/schedule/34/paragraph/5)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/26/schedule/34/paragraph/5)—
  - (i) at the beginning insert “on appeal,”;
  - (ii) for “section 118(2) of TMA 1970” substitute “section 315D(1) of FA 2004”;
- (c) for sub-paragraph (6) substitute—

  > (6) For the purposes of this paragraph—
  >
  > - “**appeal period**” means—
  >   - (a) the period during which an appeal could be brought against the determination of an authorised officer or the tribunal, as applicable, or
  >   - (b) where an appeal mentioned in paragraph (a) has been brought, the period during which that appeal has not been finally determined, withdrawn or otherwise disposed of;
  > - “**authorised officer**” means an officer of Revenue and Customs who is, or is a member of a class of officers who are, authorised by HMRC for the purposes of the provision concerned.

  .

(2) In [paragraph 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/14/schedule/13/paragraph/5) of [Schedule 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/14/schedule/13) to [FA 2020](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/14/contents) (joint and several liability of company directors), for sub-paragraph (6)(a) substitute—

> - (a) sections 315 and 315A of FA 2004 (penalties for non-disclosure of tax avoidance schemes);

.

(3) In [section 132A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/132A)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/132A) of the [Social Security Administration Act 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/contents) (disclosure of contributions avoidance arrangements)—

- (a) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/132A), at the end insert “or to a penalty under that Part”;
- (b) in [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/132A)—
  - (i) for “section 98C of the Taxes Management Act 1970 (penalties for failure to comply with Part 7 of the Finance Act 2004) and any other” substitute “any”;
  - (ii) for “that section” substitute “Part 7 of the Finance Act 2004”.

(4) In [FA 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/contents)—

- (a) in [section 90](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/section/90)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/section/90) (freezing orders: interpretation etc)—
  - (i) omit [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/section/90);
  - (ii) omit [paragraph (d)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/section/90);
- (b) in [Schedule 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/schedule/13) (penalties for facilitating avoidance schemes involving non-resident promoters), in [paragraph 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/schedule/13/paragraph/1)[(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/schedule/13/paragraph/1), for paragraph (a) substitute—

  > - (a) section 315 or 315A of FA 2004 (disclosure of tax avoidance schemes);
