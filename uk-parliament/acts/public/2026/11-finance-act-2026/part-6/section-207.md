## 207. Application of provisions of [TMA 1970](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/9/contents)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of [TMA 1970](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/9/contents) apply for the purposes of this Chapter as they apply for the purposes of the Taxes Acts—

- (a) [section 108](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/9/section/108) (responsibility of officers);
- (b) [section 114](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/9/section/114) (want of form);
- (c) [section 115](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/9/section/115) (delivery and service of documents).
