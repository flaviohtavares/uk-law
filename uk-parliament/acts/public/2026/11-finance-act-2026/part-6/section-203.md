# Finance Act 2026, section 203

## 203. Assessment etc of penalties: application of [Schedule 36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/schedule/36) to [FA 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/contents)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following paragraphs of [Schedule 36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/schedule/36) to [FA 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/contents) apply as set out below.

(2) Paragraph 46 (assessment of penalty) applies to—

- (a) a penalty under sections 194 (failure to comply) and 195 (concealing information) as it applies to a penalty under paragraphs 39 and 40 of the Schedule;
- (b) a penalty under section 196 (inaccurate information) as it applies to a penalty under paragraph 40A of the Schedule.

(3) Paragraph 51B(2) and (3) (assessment of a penalty for disclosure) apply to a penalty under section 197 (disclosure of a notice) as they apply to a penalty under [paragraph 51B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/schedule/36/paragraph/51B)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/schedule/36/paragraph/51B) of [the Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/schedule/36).

(4) Paragraph 49 (enforcement of penalty) applies to a penalty under sections 194 to 197 as it applies to a penalty under [paragraphs 39](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/schedule/36/paragraph/39), [40](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/schedule/36/paragraph/40) and [40A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/schedule/36/paragraph/40A) of [the Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/schedule/36) and, in the case of a penalty under section 197 (disclosure of a notice), the reference to notification under paragraph 46 of the Schedule is to be read as a reference to notification under paragraph 51B(2)(b) of the Schedule).

(5) Paragraph 51 (enforcement of a tax-related penalty) applies to a penalty under section 198 (penalties based on monies received) as it applies to a penalty under [paragraph 50](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/schedule/36/paragraph/50) of [the Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9/schedule/36).
