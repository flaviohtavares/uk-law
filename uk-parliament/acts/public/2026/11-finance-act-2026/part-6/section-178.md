# Finance Act 2026, section 178

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (118).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 178. Anti-avoidance enactments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter, an “**anti-avoidance enactment**” means—

- (a) [Part 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/part/7) of [FA 2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/contents) (disclosure of tax avoidance schemes);
- (b) [Part 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/26/part/5) of [FA 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/26/contents) (promoters of tax avoidance schemes);
- (c) [Schedule 16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/schedule/16) to [F(No.2)A 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/contents) (penalties for enablers of defeated tax avoidance);
- (d) [Schedule 17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/schedule/17) to [F(No.2)A 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/contents) (disclosure of tax avoidance schemes: VAT and other indirect taxes);
- (e) Chapter 1 of this Part (prohibition on promotion of certain tax avoidance arrangements).

(2) And a reference to taking action under an anti-avoidance enactment includes a reference to taking action under—

- (a) [sections 8ZF](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/46/section/8ZF) and [8ZG](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/46/section/8ZG) of the [Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/46/contents) (disqualification for promoting tax avoidance);
- (b) [Schedule 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/14/schedule/13) to [FA 2020](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/14/contents) (joint and several liability);
- (c) [section 85](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/section/85) of [FA 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/contents) (winding up petitions by an officer of HMRC);
- (d) [section 86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/section/86) of [FA 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/contents) (publication by HMRC of information about tax avoidance schemes);
- (e) [sections 87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/section/87), [88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/section/88) and [89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/section/89) of [FA 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/contents) (freezing orders etc);
- (f) [Schedule 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/schedule/13) to [FA 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/3/contents) (penalties for facilitating avoidance schemes involving non-resident promoters);
- (g) Chapter 2 of this Part (promoter action notices).
