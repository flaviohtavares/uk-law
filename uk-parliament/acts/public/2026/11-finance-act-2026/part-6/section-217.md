## 217. Removal of time limits on publication by HMRC

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In [Part 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/part/7) of [FA 2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/contents) (disclosure of tax avoidance schemes), in [section 316C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/section/316C) (publication by HMRC), omit [subsections (6A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/section/316C) and [(6B)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/section/316C).

(2) In [Part 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/schedule/17/part/1) of [Schedule 17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/schedule/17) to [F(No.2)A 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/contents) (disclosure of tax avoidance schemes: VAT and other indirect taxes), in [paragraph 36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/schedule/17/paragraph/36) (publication by HMRC), omit [sub-paragraphs (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/schedule/17/paragraph/36) and [(8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/32/schedule/17/paragraph/36).
