# Finance Act 2026, section 127

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (118).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Failure to comply with this Part etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Failure to comply with a provision or requirement specified in subsection (2) is conduct which attracts a penalty under [section 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/9/section/9) of [FA 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/9/contents).

(2) The following provisions and requirements are specified for the purpose of subsection (1)—

- (a) provision contained in this Part or regulations made under this Part;
- (b) provision contained in regulations made under [section 45](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/22/section/45) of [TCTA 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/22/contents) insofar as it applies in relation to vaping products duty;
- (c) any requirements imposed under such provision.
