# Finance Act 2026, section 118

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (118).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Excise duty point and payment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Vaping products duty is to be paid, and the amount chargeable is to be determined and become due, in accordance with provision made by or under—

- (a) [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/48/section/1) of [F(No.2)A 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/48/contents) (powers to fix excise duty point);
- (b) [section 45](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/22/section/45) of [TCTA 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/22/contents) (general power for excise duty purposes etc).
