# Finance Act 2026, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (118).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/11

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Freezing starting rate limit for savings for tax years 2026-27 to 2030-31

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the tax years 2026-27, 2027-28, 2028-29, 2029-30 and 2030-31, the amount specified in [section 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3/section/12)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3/section/12) of [ITA 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3/contents) (the starting rate limit for savings) is “£5,000”.

(2) Accordingly, [section 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3/section/21) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3/contents) (indexation) does not apply in relation to the starting rate limit for savings for any of those tax years.
