# Finance Act 2026, section 106

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 106. Pre-consumer plastic[^M_I_8f253e60-64a1-4aed-d462-9eee25986f8a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 49 of FA 2021 (meaning of “**plastic**” and “**recycled plastic**”)—

- (a) in subsection (4), in the opening words, omit “pre-consumer plastic or”;
- (b) omit subsection (5).
