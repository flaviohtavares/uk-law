## 100. Rent reviews and arrangements for new tenancies[^key-be13c1f3d1a34eddc757f53c879cc43b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) After section 54 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 insert—

> **54A. Rent reviews and arrangements for new tenancies**
>
> (1) Schedule 7A makes provision about rent reviews.
>
> (2) Schedule 7B makes provision about terms relating to rent in arrangements which require a new tenancy to be granted or taken.

(2) Schedule 37 inserts the new Schedules 7A and 7B into the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 and otherwise amends that Act.
