## 88. Extension of general power of competence to English National Park authorities and the Broads Authority[^key-f757741fa8698e17ad87f56e51d36277]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 33 amends the Localism Act 2011 to extend the general power of competence to English National Park authorities and the Broads Authority.
