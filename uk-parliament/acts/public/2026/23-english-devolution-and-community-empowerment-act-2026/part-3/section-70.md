# English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026, section 70

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. “National standard” and “regulated licence”[^key-1f172fb543052ba0653ea23e1a81c4ef]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A “national standard” is a standard prescribed by the Secretary of State under any of sections 71 to 73.
