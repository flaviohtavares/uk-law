## 21. Extension of general power of competence to strategic authorities[^c-21c7780fbf8e9a090d01804385ed5223][^c-635cc1bee74eeef52336dca7572a41d1]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 6 amends the Localism Act 2011 to extend the general power of competence to combined authorities and CCAs and their mayors.
