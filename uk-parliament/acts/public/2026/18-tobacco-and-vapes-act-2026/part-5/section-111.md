## 111. Power to amend legislation[^c-61819c416f60368f0962d049414f1f40]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The consequential provision that may be made by regulations under this Part by virtue of section 172(1)(a) includes provision amending, repealing or revoking any legislation (whenever passed or made).
