## 113. Enforcement of 2016 Regulations[^key-428215ddbc24f58e7c150cc4762412dd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 15 to the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 (consumer protection enactments), in Part 1—

- (a) in the second part of the Table (secondary legislation) at the appropriate place insert—

  > |  |  |  |
  > | --- | --- | --- |
  > | The Tobacco and Related Products Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/507](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/507)) | - (1) All public designated enforcers, other than—<br>  - (a) the Department for the Economy in Northern Ireland;<br>  - (b) the Department of Health in Northern Ireland;<br>  - (c) the Department for Infrastructure in Northern Ireland;<br>  - (d) an enforcement authority within the meaning of section 120(15) of the Communications Act 2003 (regulation of premium rate services);<br>  - (e) the Maritime and Coastguard Agency;<br>  - (f) the Office for the Traffic Commissioner;<br>  - (g) the Secretary of State.<br>- (2) All private enforcers. |  |

  ;
- (b) in the fifth part of the Table (saved legislation) omit the entry for the Tobacco Products (Manufacture, Presentation and Sale) (Safety) Regulations 2002 ([S.I. 2002/3041](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/3041)).
