# Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026, section 105

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 105. Matters dealt with by 2016 Regulations[^c-f7fd28972d1b70736043aa18ba387342]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision—

- (a) that is similar to or that corresponds to any provision of the Tobacco and Related Products Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/507](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/507)), other than Part 7 of those Regulations, or
- (b) that applies a provision of those Regulations, with or without modifications, to any of the following to which it would not otherwise apply—
  - (i) tobacco products,
  - (ii) tobacco related devices,
  - (iii) herbal smoking products,
  - (iv) cigarette papers,
  - (v) filters,
  - (vi) vaping products, or
  - (vii) nicotine products,

so far as that provision could not otherwise be made under this Part.

(2) The reference in subsection (1) to the Tobacco and Related Products Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/507](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/507)) is to that instrument as it has effect on the day on which this Act is passed.

(3) Regulations under this section are subject to the affirmative resolution procedure.
