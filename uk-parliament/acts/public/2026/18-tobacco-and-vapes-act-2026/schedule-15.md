# Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026, Schedule 15

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Schedule 15 — Part 3: consequential amendments commencing on 1 January 2027

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Section 90

### 1. Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 ([S.I. 1978/1907 (N.I. 26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1978/1907)[^key-0ee65a86ea7dc36b69d6624ba6214ef8]

In the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 omit Articles 5 and 6.

### 2. Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 ([S.I. 1991/2872 (N.I. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1991/2872))[^key-54f993e8e659eff45298c0cc9ef998a0]

The Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 is revoked.

### 3. Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2003[^key-6f8cfb3a4a4aa00adfeda37cbd33d4db]

(1) Schedule 2A to the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2003 is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraph 7 and the italic heading before it.

(3) In paragraph 8—

- (a) omit sub-paragraph (2);
- (b) in sub-paragraphs (3), (4) and (5) omit “or (2)”.

### 4. Smoking (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 ([S.I. 2006/2957 (N.I. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2006/2957))[^key-2ff817a7c34617ea7e78e9cac4fc8a9c]

The Smoking (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 is amended as follows.

**5.**[^key-24058f949424ac71fc5f49691fc812e8] In Article 1, in paragraph (3)(h), for “14 to” substitute “15 and”.

**6.**[^key-61f841c5cf1973263543af33dbf34515] Omit Article 14 (power to change age of sale for tobacco, etc).

**7.**[^key-d08e11159579e14ed76ed2bebffa2bee] In Article 15, in paragraph (3)(a) omit “or 14”.

### 8. Tobacco Retailers Act (Northern Ireland) [2014 (c. 4 (N.I.))](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2014/4)[^key-61abd19d76774c210472378b1a173d58]

The Tobacco Retailers Act (Northern Ireland) 2014 is amended as follows.

**9.**[^key-e0c8b16ffc6443034bcfee742a8bdce8] In section 1, in subsection (4) omit paragraph (c).

**10.**[^key-34db0c1da19ed3df375cfb496d87bdb6] In section 11, in subsection (1)(a) omit sub-paragraph (iii).

**11.**[^key-3e84bc1f640b3c5ea2f8bb56deb9b367] In section 12, in subsection (1) omit paragraph (c).

**12.**[^key-808adcb257ca7417285fd0fa7deeb042] Omit section 18.

### 13. Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024[^key-ccb6d87229498115b58240877257327f]

In Schedule 15 to the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, in Part 1, in the Table omit the entry relating to Article 5 of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 ([S.I. 1991/2872 (N.I. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1991/2872)).
