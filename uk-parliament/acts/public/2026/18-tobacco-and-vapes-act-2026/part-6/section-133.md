# Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026, section 133

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133. Extension of provisions about audiovisual and radio broadcasting[^key-db0e31667547800a1aadce7592f3eee4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Schedule 16 contains—

- (a) amendments to extend certain provisions of the Communications Act 2003 to all vaping products and to herbal smoking products, cigarette papers and nicotine products, and
- (b) other amendments to that Act made in consequence of this Part.
