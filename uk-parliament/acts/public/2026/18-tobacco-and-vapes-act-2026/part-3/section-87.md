## 87. Extension of retailer register[^c-5d120672eb91a0fa206bfe26fb89cee2][^c-5469b55620744c3f305abb91e6464d31]

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 10 amends the Tobacco Retailers Act (Northern Ireland) [2014 (c. 4 (N.I.))](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2014/4) to extend certain provisions about the registration of tobacco retailers so that they apply in relation to retailers of vaping products and nicotine products.
