## Schedule 13 — Licensing of retail sales of tobacco products etc: consequential amendments

(4) In the heading, after “Offences” insert “in connection with restricted premises orders and restricted sales orders”.

**7.**[^key-48428552887f4f5d6af41036f8df34ba] In the italic heading before [section 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2014/4/section/11), for “Enforcement” substitute “General enforcement”.

- (a) after “section” insert “4B or”;
- (b) omit “(1), (2), (3),”.

**10.**[^key-d0548a9242a5660ed353846fbf67d3f3] (1) [Section 16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2014/4/section/16) is amended as follows.
