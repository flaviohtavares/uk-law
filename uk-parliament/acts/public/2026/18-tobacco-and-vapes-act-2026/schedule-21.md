# Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026, Schedule 21

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Schedule 21 — Consequential amendments: general

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Section 167

### 1. Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002[^key-bf6181ce9543d9c429c87a933dc2af94]

The Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002 is repealed.

### 2. Communications Act 2003[^key-3fdbf1e91bdab4f8891026a72f0095eb]

In Schedule 17 to the Communications Act 2003 omit paragraph 173 and the italic heading before it.

### 3. Regulatory Enforcement and Sanctions Act 2008[^key-e2017f7a524cea47c9634d89374baa82]

In Schedule 3 to the Regulatory Enforcement and Sanctions Act 2008 omit the reference to the Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002.

### 4. Health Act 2009[^key-18cb0b6897267553bb74d36bf7b6f5ba]

The Health Act 2009 is amended as follows.

(6) In subsection (6) omit paragraphs (b) and (c).

(7) In subsection (7) omit paragraphs (b) and (c).

**7.**[^key-868310ed1368cdab8b1e7f5e285a9ae0] Omit Schedule 4.

**10.**[^key-51a552109002c20015cc136c57a4346c] Omit section 2.

**11.**[^key-dd01979d1370ae4185c59e2c8e83ea57] In section 3 omit subsection (5).

### 12. Consumer Rights Act 2015[^key-403e7acf91969d1acda64540ece83d6c]

In Schedule 5 to the Consumer Rights Act 2015, in the entry relating to the Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026 (inserted by section 32 of this Act), after “section 32” insert “or 135”.

### 13. Tobacco and Related Products Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/507](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/507))[^key-d818267aebe3d65c004e04325809fdd1]

The Tobacco and Related Products Regulations 2016 are amended as follows.

**16.**[^key-5241493c9c2cc795dddbb2b7798e46c7] In regulation 48 omit paragraph (e).

**17.**[^key-854f5d419b5344eec4b9ee3e6fd2a285] (1) Regulation 50 is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraphs (2) to (4).

(3) In paragraph (5) omit “or (2)”.

(4) In paragraph (7) omit “or (2)”.

**18.**[^key-265b526c8f4e2a71834b4b0c05dc56ca] In regulation 53 omit paragraph (5).

**19.**[^key-6c0b2bb6b05ad6a9a5eeae9c29a5912c] Omit Schedule 1.

### 20. Health (Tobacco, Nicotine etc. and Care) (Scotland) Act [2016 (asp 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2016/14)[^key-b42a5b658722a02602a5cfe9a61ae722]

The Health (Tobacco, Nicotine etc. and Care) (Scotland) Act 2016 is amended as follows.

### 23. Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024[^key-6b95b3c8ee5c975052d0c74db187542f]

In Schedule 15 to the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, in Part 1, in the Table omit the entry relating to the Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002.

### 24. Media Act 2024[^key-03f28c0651322cef10b13e2cc01cf0df]

The Media Act 2024 is amended as follows.

**25.**[^key-f54c89517dab17d2f35405480e17b303] In Schedule 2 omit paragraph 21 and the italic heading before it.
