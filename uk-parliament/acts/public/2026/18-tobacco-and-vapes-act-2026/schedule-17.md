# Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026, Schedule 17

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Schedule 17 — Amendments consequential on sections 142 to 146

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Section 147

**1.**[^key-3a82d354207fc665618d50f725592b78] The Health Act 2006 is amended as follows.

**2.**[^key-b3c09d83a79d90482899cc4551880cec] (1) Section 2 is amended as follows.

(2) For subsection (2) substitute—

> (2) Premises in England are smoke-free if they are workplaces; they are smoke-free all the time.

(3) In subsection (3), for “used as a place of work mentioned in subsection (2)” substitute “a workplace”.

(4) For subsections (7) and (8) substitute—

> (7) In this Part “**workplace**” means—
>
> - (a) a place of work used by more than one person (even if the persons who work there do so at different times, or only intermittently), or
> - (b) a place of work where members of the public might attend for the purpose of seeking or receiving goods or services from the person or persons working there (even if members of the public are not always present).

**3.**[^key-a1dd32fd1baf53cbabedda5ff8b9356e] (1) Section 9 (fixed penalties) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1), for “or 7(2)” substitute “7(2), 8C(1), 8E(5), 8H(1) or 8J(6)”.

(3) In subsection (1A), after “section 8(4)” insert “8D(3), 8I(3)”.

**4.**[^key-bf26ab3282023f8edee5152409b01150] In section 10 (enforcement), in subsections (1), (3) and (5), for “Chapter”, in each place it occurs, substitute “Part”.

**5.**[^key-3e7b5b18fe67cfff291eb44dc9516567] In section 11 (obstruction etc of officers), in subsections (1) and (2), for “Chapter” substitute “Part”.

**6.**[^key-be6ebb0d462571e2fdba2efb12e87b73] (1) Section 12 (interpretation) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1), for “Chapter” substitute “Part”.

(3) At the appropriate places insert—

> - “**heated tobacco device**” means a device that heats tobacco to generate a vapour or an aerosol for the purpose of inhalation through a mouthpiece (whether or not it can also heat other matter to produce a vapour or aerosol);

;

> - “**heated tobacco-free place**”: see section 8G;

;

> - “**heated tobacco-free vehicle**”: see section 8G;

;

> - “**open to the public**”: premises and places are open to the public if the public or a section of the public has access to them, whether by invitation or not, and whether on payment or not;

;

> - “**performance**” includes—
>   - (a) the performance of a play or a performance given in connection with the making of a film or television programme, and
>   - (b) a rehearsal;

;

> - “**relevant vape**” has the meaning given by section 8C(6);

;

> - “**vape**” has the meaning given by section 50 of the Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026;

;

> - “**vape-free place**”: see section 8B;

;

> - “**vape-free vehicle**”: see section 8B;

;

> - “**vaporises**” has the meaning given by section 50 of the Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026;

;

> - “**work**” includes voluntary work;

;

> - “**workplace**” has the meaning given by section 2(7).

.

(4) In subsection (3), for “Chapter” substitute “Part”.

**7.**[^key-59aec725d7514cbb1f14081922ac1b0d] In Schedule 2 (powers of entry etc), in paragraphs, 2(a), 3, 4 and 6(1)(a) omit “Chapter 1 of”.
