# Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Ban on manufacture of snus etc[^key-c6b452408af83e7655b19686823d0b9b]

*Extent: S*

After section 9 of the Tobacco and Primary Medical Services (Scotland) Act [2010 (asp 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/3) insert—

> **Snus etc**
>
> **9A. Ban on manufacture of snus etc**
>
> (1) It is an offence to manufacture a relevant oral tobacco product.
>
> (2) In subsection (1) “**relevant oral tobacco product**” means a tobacco product that—
>
> - (a) is intended for oral use,
> - (b) is not intended to be inhaled or chewed, and
> - (c) consists wholly or partly of tobacco in powder or particulate form.
>
> (3) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable—
>
> - (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months, or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both;
> - (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years, or a fine, or both.
