## 68. Alignment of definition of “vaping product”[^key-7c57d19d7b8d7cd1ca5da4927221c610]

*Extent: S*

Schedule 8 contains provision for the purposes of aligning the definition of “vaping product” in the Tobacco and Primary Medical Services (Scotland) Act [2010 (asp 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/3) with that used elsewhere in this Act.
