# Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/18

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Fixed penalties in Wales: use of proceeds[^key-62afb3f003392f6f8a31a88c4a226dbf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any sums received by a local weights and measures authority in Wales in pursuance of a fixed penalty notice must be used in connection with their functions under or under regulations made under—

- (a) this Act;
- (b) the Tobacco and Related Products Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/507](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/507));
- (c) Part 3 of the Public Health (Wales) Act 2017.

(2) In this section “**fixed penalty notice**” means a fixed penalty notice given under section 40.
