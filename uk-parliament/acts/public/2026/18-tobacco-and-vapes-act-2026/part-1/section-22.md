# Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026, section 22

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Repeal of register of retailers of tobacco and nicotine products in Wales etc[^c-0dccc1522e7a6a6770ab1c346a6964c4]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In the Public Health (Wales) Act [2017 (anaw 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2017/2), in Part 3 (tobacco and nicotine products) omit Chapter 2 (retailers of tobacco and nicotine products).
