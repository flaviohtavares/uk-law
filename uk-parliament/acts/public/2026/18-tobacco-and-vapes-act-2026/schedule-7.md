# Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026, Schedule 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Schedule 7 — Part 1: consequential amendments commencing on 1 January 2027

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Section 44

### 1. Children and Young Persons Act 1933[^c-d41a1d60f3930bd5224ec36e6b7b84e0]

In the Children and Young Persons Act 1933 omit section 7.

### 2. Protection of Children (Tobacco) Act 1986[^c-3ed751ca46237b957bcff30a611ae4c7]

The Protection of Children (Tobacco) Act 1986 is repealed.

### 3. Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991[^c-2d8a6bfd2370715c210d5503c9e4fdb1]

The Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991 is repealed.

### 4. Local Government etc (Scotland) Act 1994[^c-e610a9f8d896d49acdfb8250cb32504f]

In Schedule 13 to the Local Government etc (Scotland) Act 1994 omit paragraph 169 and the italic heading before that paragraph.

### 5. Police Reform Act 2002[^c-28544936e2551bf05186d9ad61afc9cf]

In Schedule 5 to the Police Reform Act 2002 omit paragraph 6 and the italic heading before that paragraph.

### 6. Courts Act 2003[^c-ef2807845eae36c7d483f3b824cb1dbe]

In Schedule 8 to the Courts Act 2003 omit paragraph 72.

### 7. Regulatory Enforcement and Sanctions Act 2008[^c-51c720486cd0fe1c4fe401de0e59861d]

The Regulatory Enforcement and Sanctions Act 2008 is amended as follows.

**8.**[^c-f5feaa8b1818cf4482b70bfec15608dc] In Schedule 3 omit the reference to the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991.

**9.**[^c-839367b60e7f1f897ea7ecf6697046a0] (1) Schedule 6 is amended as follows.

(2) Omit the reference to section 7(1) of the Children and Young Persons Act 1933.

(3) Omit the reference to the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991.

### 10. Tobacco and Primary Medical Services (Scotland) Act [2010 (asp 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/3)[^c-9bd2120240cc7029ec05dd8437465c7c]

In Schedule 2 to the Tobacco and Primary Medical Services (Scotland) Act [2010 (asp 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/3) omit paragraph 2 and the italic heading before that paragraph.

### 11. Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011[^c-a2c7e8b86fbe55429acb17d837864c56]

In Schedule 16 to the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011 omit paragraph 67.

### 12. Children and Families Act 2014[^c-da62a547edd8fd3b1211ba7586614472]

In the Children and Families Act 2014 omit section 91.

### 13. Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024[^c-439fc8b29ce3d735ce8abff6e5bf12a0]

In Schedule 15 to the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, in Part 1, in the Table omit the entries relating to—

- (a) section 7(1) and (2) of the Children and Young Persons Act 1933;
- (b) section 4 of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991.
