## Schedule 11 — Sexual offences against children under 16: consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Section 81

### 1. Firearms Act [1968 (c.27)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27)[^key-78f0fe407431520c436d55e3fadf292d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) [Paragraph 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/schedule/1/paragraph/6) of [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/schedule/1) to the [Firearms Act 1968](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/contents) (offences to which section 17(2) applies) is amended as follows.

(5) In [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/44/schedule/15/part/2) of [Schedule 15](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/44/schedule/15) (specified sexual offences for purposes of sections 244ZA and 325) after paragraph 109 insert—
