# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 185

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 185. Power to take additional sample[^c-c6279a7778f8732e6045a4ab31c7a7c5]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) [Section 63B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B) of the [Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/contents) (testing for presence of drugs) is amended as follows.

(2) In [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B), at the end insert—

> - (d) either a sample has not been taken from the person under this section during the period of the person’s detention or the additional sample condition is met.

(3) In [subsections (1A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B) and [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B), for “sample to be taken” substitute “taking of samples in accordance with this section”.

(4) Before [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B) insert—

> (4C) The additional sample condition is that one sample (only) has been taken from the person under this section during the period of the person’s detention but—
>
> - (a) it was not suitable for the same means of analysis, or
> - (b) it proved insufficient.

(5) In [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B)—

- (a) in the words before [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B), after “must” insert “, if no sample has been taken from the person under this section during the period of the person’s detention”;
- (b) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B), for “to do so” substitute “to give any sample which may be taken under this section”.

(6) In [subsection (5B)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B)—

- (a) in the words before [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B), omit the words from “no” to “but”;
- (b) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B), for “that period” substitute “the same continuous period of detention”.
