# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 186

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 186. Removal of power to continue detention[^c-38fe7c5d6f3ee1861785cb06c028fff8]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The [Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 37](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/37) (duties of custody officer before charge) omit [subsections (8A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/37) and [(8B)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/37).

(3) In [section 38](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/38) (duties of custody officer after charge)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/38)—
  - (i) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/38), omit [sub-paragraph (iiia)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/38);
  - (ii) in [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/38), in [sub-paragraph (i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/38) omit the words from “(but” to “age)”;
- (b) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/38) omit the words from “but” to the end;
- (c) in [subsection (6A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/38), omit the definition of “minimum age”.
