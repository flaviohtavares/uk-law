# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 183

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/20

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 183. Testing of persons in police detention for presence of controlled drugs[^key-c62e7eb32cd2f48accef4fa47b20f0ae]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The [Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/contents) is amended as set out in subsections (2) to (4) .

(2) In [section 63B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B) (testing for presence of Class A drugs), for “**Class A**” (in each place it appears, including the heading) substitute “controlled”.

(3) In [section 63C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63C) (testing for presence of Class A drugs: supplementary)—

- (a) in the heading, for “Class A” substitute “controlled”;
- (b) for [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63C) substitute—

  > (6) In [section 63B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B)—
  >
  > - (a) “**misuse**” has the same meaning as in the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1971](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/38/contents);
  > - (b) “**specified controlled drug**” means a controlled drug (within the meaning of the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1971](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/38/contents)) specified in regulations made by the Secretary of State by statutory instrument;
  > - (c) “**trigger offence**” means an offence specified in Schedule 2B.
  >
  > (7) In Schedule 2B, “**specified controlled drug**” has the same meaning as in section 63B.
  >
  > (8) The Secretary of State may by regulations made by statutory instrument amend Schedule 2B.
  >
  > (9) Regulations under this section—
  >
  > - (a) may make different provision for different purposes or different areas, and
  > - (b) may make transitional, transitory or saving provision.
  >
  > (10) A statutory instrument containing regulations under subsection (8) (whether alone or with other provision) may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.
  >
  > (11) Any other statutory instrument containing regulations under this section is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(4) After [Schedule 2A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/schedule/2A) insert the Schedule set out in Schedule 20 to this Act.

(5) In [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/63/schedule/1) to the [Bail Act 1976](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/63/contents) (persons entitled to bail: supplementary provisions), in [Part 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/63/schedule/1/part/1) (defendants accused or convicted of imprisonable offences), in paragraph 6B(1)(b)(i), for “Class A” substitute “controlled”.

(6) In the [Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/43/contents)—

- (a) in [section 70](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/43/section/70) (interpretation, etc)—
  - (i) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/43/section/70), omit the definition of “trigger offence”;
  - (ii) omit [subsections (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/43/section/70) and [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/43/section/70);
- (b) in [section 76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/43/section/76) (subordinate legislation), in subsection (5)(a), for “, 57(5) or 70(2)” substitute “or 57(5)”;
- (c) omit [Schedule 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/43/schedule/6) (trigger offences).
