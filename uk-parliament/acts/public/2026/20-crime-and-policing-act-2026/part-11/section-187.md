# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 187

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 187. Removal of notification conditions[^c-52b428fc759de35cb38104c9708cd530]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In [section 63B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B) of the [Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/contents) (testing for presence of drugs)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B) omit paragraph (c);
- (b) omit [subsections (4A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B) and [(4B)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B);
- (c) in [subsection (10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63B), omit the definition of “relevant chief officer”.

(2) In [section 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/7) of the [Drugs Act 2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/contents) (testing for presence of Class A drugs), omit [subsections (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/7), [(13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/7) and [(14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/7).

(3) In [section 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/9) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/contents) (initial assessment)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/9)—
  - (i) at the end of paragraph (b) insert “and”;
  - (ii) after [paragraph (c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/9), for “, and” substitute “.”;
  - (iii) omit [paragraph (d)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/9);
- (b) omit [subsections (5) to (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/9/subsection/5).

(4) In [section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/10) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/contents) (follow-up assessment)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/10)—
  - (i) at the end of paragraph (a) insert “and”;
  - (ii) after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/10), for “, and” substitute “.”;
  - (iii) omit [paragraph (c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/10);
- (b) omit [subsections (6) to (8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/10/subsection/6).
