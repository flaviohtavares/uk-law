# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 184

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 184. Assessment of misuse of controlled drugs[^c-ceca8286ab5c5065a52d1006b8a862e7]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The [Drugs Act 2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/9) (initial assessment following testing for presence of Class A drugs), for “**Class A**” (in each place it appears, including the heading) substitute “controlled”.

(3) In [section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/10) (follow-up assessment), in [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/10), for “Class A” substitute “controlled”.

(4) In [section 16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/16) (samples submitted for further analysis), in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/16)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/16), for “Class A” substitute “controlled”.

(5) In [section 17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/17) (relationship with the Bail Act 1976 etc)—

- (a) for [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/17) substitute—

  > (1) Subsection (1A) applies if a requirement is imposed on a person by virtue of section 9(2) or 10(2) and at any time before the person has fully complied with the requirement—
  >
  > - (a) the person is charged with the related offence, and
  > - (b) a court imposes on the person a condition of bail under [section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/63/section/3)[(6D)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/63/section/3) of the [Bail Act 1976](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/63/contents) (duty to impose condition to undergo relevant Class A drug assessment etc).
  >
  > (1A) For the purposes of the requirement—
  >
  > - (a) section 9(3)(a) or 10(4) applies as if for “specified controlled drug” there were substituted “specified controlled drug that is not a Class A drug”, and
  > - (b) accordingly, the required initial or follow-up assessment (and any care plan within the meaning of section 10(4)) is in respect of the person’s dependency upon or propensity to misuse any specified controlled drug that is not a Class A drug only.

  ;
- (b) in [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/17)—
  - (i) after “subsection (1)” insert “and (1A)”;
  - (ii) for “**ceases to have effect**” (in each place it appears) substitute “is modified by subsection (1A)”.

(6) In [section 19](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/19) (interpretation)—

- (a) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/19), after “drug”” insert “, “controlled drug””;
- (b) for [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/17/section/19) substitute—

  > (3) “**Specified controlled drug**” means a controlled drug specified in regulations under [section 63C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/63C)(6)(b) of PACE.
