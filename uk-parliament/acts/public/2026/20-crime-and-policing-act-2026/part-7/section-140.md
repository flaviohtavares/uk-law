## 140. Safeguarding vulnerable groups: regulated activity (Northern Ireland)[^c-b60ad6591b100ef9e0b0e2e9725c451e][^c-801ec679d0a6ffdfad58a0a74ddaf56b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2) to the [Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups (Northern Ireland) Order 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/contents) ([S.I. 2007/1351 (N.I. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351)) (regulated activity) is amended as follows.

(2) In [paragraph 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/1) omit—

- (a) in [sub-paragraph (2A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/1)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/1), the words “(disregarding paragraph 2(3A) and (3B)(b))”;
- (b) in [sub-paragraph (2B)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/1)—
  - (i) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/1)[(ii)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/1), the words “(disregarding paragraph 2(3A) and (3B)(b))”;
  - (ii) [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/1) and the “or” immediately before it;
- (c) [sub-paragraph (2C)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/1);
- (d) [sub-paragraph (13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/1).

(3) In [paragraph 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/2) omit—

- (a) [sub-paragraph (3A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/2);
- (b) in [sub-paragraph (3B)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/2), [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/2) and the “and” immediately before it;
- (c) [sub-paragraph (3C)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/2).

(4) Omit [paragraph 5A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2007/1351/schedule/2/paragraph/5A) and the italic heading before it.
