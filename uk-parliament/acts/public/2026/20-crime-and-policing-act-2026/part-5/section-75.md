# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 75

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Possession of advice or guidance about creating etc CSA images: England and Wales and Northern Ireland[^c-3a475d727b2b487b66d644e52cec4ba5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) [Section 69](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/69) of the [Serious Crime Act 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/contents) (possession of paedophile manual) is amended as follows.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(2) In [subsections (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/69) and [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/69)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/69)[(ii)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/69) after “sexually” insert “or creating CSA images”.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(3) After [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/69) insert—

> (2A) In this section “**abusing children sexually or creating CSA images**” means—
>
> - (a) in England and Wales, doing anything that constitutes—
>   - (i) an offence under [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/37/section/1) of the [Protection of Children Act 1978](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/37/contents),
>   - (ii) an offence under [Part 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/part/1) of the [Sexual Offences Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/contents) against a person under the age of 16, or
>   - (iii) an offence under [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/section/2) of the [Modern Slavery Act 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/contents) (human trafficking) against a person under the age of 16 that is committed with a view to exploitation that consists of or includes behaviour within [section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/section/3)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/section/3) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/contents) (sexual exploitation),
>
>   or doing anything outside England and Wales that would constitute such an offence if done in England and Wales;
> - (b) in Northern Ireland, doing anything that constitutes—
>   - (i) an offence under [Article 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1978/1047/article/3) of the [Protection of Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1978](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1978/1047/contents) ([S.I. 1978/1047 (N.I. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1978/1047)),
>   - (ii) an offence under [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/part/2), [3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/part/3) or [4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/part/4) of the [Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/contents) ([S.I. 2008/1769 (N.I. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769)) against a person under the age of 16, or
>   - (iii) an offence under [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2015/2/section/2) of the [Human Trafficking and Exploitation (Criminal Justice and Support for Victims) Act (Northern Ireland) 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2015/2/contents) against a person under the age of 16 that is committed with a view to exploitation that consists of or includes behaviour within [section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2015/2/section/3)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2015/2/section/3) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2015/2/contents) (sexual exploitation),
>
>   or doing anything outside Northern Ireland that would constitute such an offence if done in Northern Ireland;
>
> or creating (anywhere) a prohibited image of a child within the meaning of [section 62](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25/section/62) of the [Coroners and Justice Act 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25/contents).

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(4) In [subsection (8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/69) omit the definition of “abusing children sexually”.

(5) In [Schedule 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/3) to the [Sexual Offences Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/contents), in [paragraph 93A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/3/paragraph/93A)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/3/paragraph/93A) (service offences) for “35B” substitute “35C”.

*Extent: E+W*

(6) In [Schedule 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/schedule/4) to the [Modern Slavery Act 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/contents) (offences to which defence in section 45 does not apply), for paragraph 35A (offences under the Serious Crime Act 2015) substitute—

> **35A.** An offence under any of the following provisions of the [Serious Crime Act 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/contents)—
>
> - [section 69](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/69) (possession of paedophile manual)
> - [section 75A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/75A) (strangulation or suffocation).
