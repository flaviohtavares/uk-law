# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 80

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Section 77: supplementary provision[^c-1b3c8c72eff49d38a3b210772c0f4b0b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) [Schedule 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/3) to the [Sexual Offences Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/contents) (sexual offences for purposes of Part 2 of that Act) is amended as follows.

(2) After [paragraph 35C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/3/paragraph/35C) insert—

> **35D.** An offence under section 77 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (online facilitation of child sexual exploitation and abuse), if the offender is or has been sentenced in respect of the offence to imprisonment for a term of at least 12 months.

(3) After [paragraph 59ZL](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/3/paragraph/59ZL) insert—

> **59ZM.** An offence under section 77 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (online facilitation of child sexual exploitation and abuse), if the offender is or has been sentenced in respect of the offence to imprisonment for a term of at least 12 months.

(4) After [paragraph 92Y](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/3/paragraph/92Y) insert—

> **92Z.** An offence under section 77 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (online facilitation of child sexual exploitation and abuse), if the offender is or has been sentenced in respect of the offence to imprisonment for a term of at least 12 months.

(5) In [paragraph 93A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/3/paragraph/93A) (service offences), in [sub-paragraph (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/3/paragraph/93A), for “**35C**” (inserted by section 75) substitute “35D”.

*Extent: E+W*

(6) In Schedule 4 to the Modern Slavery Act 2015 (offences to which defence in section 45 does not apply), in paragraph 36D (inserted by section 48), after the entry for section 68 (inserted by section 68) insert—

> - section 77 (online facilitation of child sexual exploitation and abuse)

.
