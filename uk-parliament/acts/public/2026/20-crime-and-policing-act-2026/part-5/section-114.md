## 114. Sexual activity with an animal[^c-a3ddf7fc108ecb80900b6f9d9ce52a1a][^c-af6f902c1363a39d13360324bf6e73dc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The [Sexual Offences Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/contents) is amended in accordance with subsections (2) to (5).

*Extent: E+W*

(2) For [section 69](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/69) (intercourse with an animal) substitute—

> **69. Sexual activity with an animal**
>
> (1) A person commits an offence if—
>
> - (a) the person intentionally touches an animal (whether living or dead),
> - (b) the person knows that, or is reckless as to whether, that is what is touched, and
> - (c) the touching is sexual.
>
> (2) For the purposes of this section, touching is sexual if a reasonable person would consider that—
>
> - (a) because of its nature it may be sexual, and
> - (b) because of its circumstances or the purpose of any person in relation to it (or both) it is sexual.
>
> (3) A person who commits an offence under this section is liable—
>
> - (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court or a fine (or both);
> - (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years.

*Extent: E+W*

(3) In [section 78](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/78) (meaning of “**sexual**”), after “66D” insert “, 69”.

*Extent: E+W*

(4) In [section 79](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/79) (Part 1: general interpretation) omit subsection (10).

(5) In [paragraphs 35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/3/paragraph/35) and [92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/3/paragraph/92) of [Schedule 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/3) (sexual offences that make offender subject to notification requirements), for “intercourse” substitute “sexual activity”.

*Extent: E+W*

(6) In the following provisions, for “intercourse” substitute “sexual activity”—

- (a) [paragraph 151](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/44/schedule/15/paragraph/151) of [Schedule 15](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/44/schedule/15) to the [Criminal Justice Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/44/contents);
- (b) [paragraph 38](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/18/paragraph/38)[(az)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/18/paragraph/38) of [Schedule 18](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/schedule/18) to the Sentencing Code.
