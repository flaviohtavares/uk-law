# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 126

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126. Power of entry and search[^c-3009b7fe24b905cdd563e5fe421d873a]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The [Sexual Offences Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In the [section 96A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/96A) inserted by [section 80](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2006/10/section/80) of the [Police, Public Order and Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2006/10/contents) (police powers of entry to and examination of relevant offender’s home address)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2006/10/section/80), for “a senior” substitute “an appropriate”;
- (b) in [subsection (11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2006/10/section/80)—
  - (i) after “section—” insert—

    > - “**appropriate police officer**”, in relation to an application for a warrant under subsection (1), means a constable of the Police Service of Scotland authorised to make the application by a constable of the Police Service of Scotland who is of the rank of inspector or above;

    ;
  - (ii) omit the definition of “senior police officer”.

(3) In [section 96B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/96B) (power of entry and search of relevant offender’s home address)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/96B), for “a senior” substitute “an appropriate”;
- (b) in [subsection (10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/96B)—
  - (i) before the definition of “the relevant force” insert—

    > - “**appropriate police officer**”, in relation to an application for a warrant under subsection (1), means a constable authorised to make the application by a constable of the rank of inspector or above;

    ;
  - (ii) omit the definition of “senior police officer”.
