## 101. Taking down intimate image content: consequential amendments[^c-d2a5000531980a3d142e2c32e9701d6b][^c-cca1a29a7fba91de8b2e6dbbaa22ed5b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The [Online Safety Act 2023](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/10) (regulated user-to-user services: safety duties about illegal content)—

- (a) in [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/10) for “and (3)” substitute “to (3A)”;
- (b) in [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/10)—
  - (i) the words from “each paragraph” to the end become [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/10);
  - (ii) at the end of [that paragraph](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/10) insert

    > , and
    >
    > - (b) subsection (3A).

    ;
- (c) in [subsection (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/10) for “subsection (2) or (3)” substitute “subsections (2) to (3A)”.

(3) In [section 23](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/23)[(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/23) (record-keeping and review duties) for “or (3)”, in the first place it occurs, substitute “, (3) or (3A)”.

(4) In [section 27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/27) (regulated search services: safety duties about illegal content)—

- (a) in [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/27) for “and (3)” substitute “to (3A)”;
- (b) in [subsection (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/27) for “subsection (2) or (3)” substitute “subsections (2) to (3A)”.

(5) In [section 34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/34)[(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/34) (record-keeping and review duties), for “or (3)”, in the first place it occurs, substitute “, (3) or (3A)”.

(6) In [section 59](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/59)[(14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/59) (meaning of “illegal content” etc) for “and “priority illegal content”” substitute “, “priority illegal content” and “intimate image content””.

(7) In [section 71](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/71)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/71)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/71)[(i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/71) (duty not to take down content except in accordance with terms of service: exceptions) for “or (3)” substitute “, (3) or (3A)”.

(8) In [section 136](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/136)[(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/136) (confirmation decisions: proactive technology)—

- (a) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/136) for “or (3)” substitute “, (3) or (3A)”;
- (b) in [paragraph (c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/136) for “or (3)” substitute “, (3) or (3A)”.

(9) In [section 237](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/237) (index of defined terms) at the appropriate place insert—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | intimate image content (in Part 3) | section 59 |

.

(10) In [Schedule 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/schedule/4) (codes of practice)—

- (a) in [paragraph 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/schedule/4/paragraph/9)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/schedule/4/paragraph/9) for “or (3)” substitute “, (3) or (3A)”;
- (b) in [paragraph 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/schedule/4/paragraph/9)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/schedule/4/paragraph/9) for “or (3)” substitute “, (3) or (3A)”;
- (c) in [paragraph 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/schedule/4/paragraph/13)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/schedule/4/paragraph/13)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/schedule/4/paragraph/13) for “or (3)” substitute “, (3) or (3A)”;
- (d) in [paragraph 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/schedule/4/paragraph/13)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/schedule/4/paragraph/13)[(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/schedule/4/paragraph/13) for “or (3)” substitute “, (3) or (3A)”.
