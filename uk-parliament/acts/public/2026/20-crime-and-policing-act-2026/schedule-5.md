## Schedule 5 — Sound moderators etc: exemptions

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 44

### 1. Firearms Act 1968[^c-a7daa91ab0278602f007b6838ccc47c5][^c-c4d07b38927b55da622e5085ce45808b]

(1) The [Firearms Act 1968](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/7)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/7)—

- (a) the words from “a firearm” to the end become paragraph (a);
- (b) at the end insert—

  > - (b) a relevant accessory.

(3) In [section 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/8)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/8) for the words from “, have” to “ammunition” substitute—

  > - (a) have in their possession, purchase or acquire, a firearm or ammunition, or
  > - (b) have a relevant accessory in their possession,

  ;
- (b) in [subsection (1A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/8)—
  - (i) after “ammunition”, in the first place it occurs, insert “, or the possession of a relevant accessory,”;
  - (ii) for “or ammunition”, in the second place it occurs, substitute “, ammunition or relevant accessory”;
  - (iii) after “of, or” insert “firearm or ammunition is”.

(4) In [section 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/9)(1) for “or ammunition” substitute “, ammunition or a relevant accessory”.

(5) In [section 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/11)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/11) for “or ammunition”—
  - (i) in the first place it occurs, substitute “, ammunition or a relevant accessory”;
  - (ii) in the second and third places it occurs, substitute “, ammunition or relevant accessory”;
- (b) in [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/11)—
  - (i) omit the “and” at the end of [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/11);
  - (ii) at the end of [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/11) insert

    > and
    >
    > - (c) a relevant accessory,

    .

(6) In [section 11A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/11A) at the end insert—

> (7) Where a rifle is borrowed on any premises in reliance on subsection (1), the borrower may, without holding a certificate under this Act, borrow and have in their possession on those premises a relevant accessory for the period for which the rifle is borrowed.

(7) In [section 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/12)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/12) after “firearm” insert “or relevant accessory”.

(8) In [section 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/13)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/13)—

- (a) in paragraph (a)—
  - (i) for “or ammunition”, in the first place it occurs, substitute “, ammunition or a relevant accessory”;
  - (ii) for “or ammunition therefor” substitute “, or ammunition or a relevant accessory therefor,”;
- (b) in [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/13)—
  - (i) for “or ammunition”, in the first place it occurs, substitute “, or ammunition or a relevant accessory”;
  - (ii) for “or ammunition”, in the second place it occurs, substitute “, or ammunition or relevant accessory”;
  - (iii) omit the “and” at the end;
- (c) after [paragraph (c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/27/section/13) insert—

  > - (d) having obtained a permit under paragraph (c), remove any relevant accessory from or to a ship, aircraft or aerodrome, to or from the place specified in the permit.

### 2. Firearms (Amendment) Act 1988[^c-7f35d54c87c0d723be330e8642217fab][^c-3c37fdf6e9909df0fae4b1c017b80662]

(1) The [Firearms (Amendment) Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/45/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In section 15—

- (a) in subsection (1) for “and ammunition” substitute “, ammunition and a relevant accessory”;
- (b) in subsection (2) for “or ammunition” substitute “, ammunition or relevant accessories”.

(3) In [section 16A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/45/section/16A)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/45/section/16A) for “and ammunition” substitute “, ammunition and a relevant accessory”.

(4) In [section 16B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/45/section/16B)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/45/section/16B) for “and ammunition” substitute “, ammunition and a relevant accessory”.

(5) In [section 17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/45/section/17) after subsection (1) insert—

> (1ZA) The holder of a visitor’s firearm permit may, without holding a firearm certificate, have in their possession a relevant accessory.”

(6) In section 25(4) (interpretation), for “or ammunition” substitute “, ammunition or relevant accessory”.

(7) In [paragraph 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/45/schedule/paragraph/1) of [the Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/45/schedule), in sub-paragraphs (2)(a), (3) and (4) for “and ammunition” substitute “, ammunition and relevant accessories”.
