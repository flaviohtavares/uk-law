# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 213

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 213. Consequential amendments[^c-9fcd9f4b468d9ecb99af5e87097ad4a9][^c-e4790e7b857285cd76be4c4ec7698274]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the [Police Act 1996](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/16/contents) omit—

- (a) [sections 88C to 88E](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/16/section/88C) (effect of inclusion in police barred list);
- (b) [section 88K](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/16/section/88K) (effect of inclusion in police advisory list).

(2) The [Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/13/contents) is amended as follows.

(3) In [section 42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/13/section/42)[(3AA)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/13/section/42) (person on police barred list not eligible for appointment as Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis)—

- (a) the words from “the police” to the end become [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/13/section/42);
- (b) after [that paragraph](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/13/section/42) insert—

  > - (b) the British Transport Police barred list (within the meaning of section 211 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026;
  > - (c) the Civil Nuclear Constabulary barred list (within the meaning of that section);
  > - (d) the Ministry of Defence Police barred list (within the meaning of that section);
  > - (e) the National Crime Agency barred list (within the meaning of that section);
  > - (f) the Scottish police barred list maintained under [section 59A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2012/8/section/59A) of the [Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 (asp 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2012/8/contents).

(4) In [section 43](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/13/section/43)[(3B)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/13/section/43) (person on police barred list not eligible for appointment as Deputy Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis)—

- (a) the words from “the police” to the end become [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/13/section/43);
- (b) after [that paragraph](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/13/section/43) insert—

  > - (b) the British Transport Police barred list (within the meaning of section 211 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026;
  > - (c) the Civil Nuclear Constabulary barred list (within the meaning of that section);
  > - (d) the Ministry of Defence Police barred list (within the meaning of that section);
  > - (e) the National Crime Agency barred list (within the meaning of that section);
  > - (f) the Scottish police barred list maintained under [section 59A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2012/8/section/59A) of the [Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 (asp 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2012/8/contents).
