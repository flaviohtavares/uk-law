# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 33

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Remote sale or letting of knives etc: Scotland[^key-e2e989d02d1f191625f8656e73dfbc83]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) [Section 141C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/141C) of the [Criminal Justice Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/contents) (remote sales and lettings of knives) is amended as follows.

(2) For [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/141C) substitute—

> (5) Condition A is that, before the sale or letting on hire—
>
> - (a) the accused either—
>   - (i) obtained from the recipient a copy of an identity document issued to the recipient in a physical form and a photograph of the recipient, or
>   - (ii) took such steps to verify the recipient’s age as may be prescribed in regulations made by the Scottish Ministers, and
> - (b) on the basis of that copy and photograph or those steps, a reasonable person would have been satisfied that the recipient was aged 18 or over.
>
> (5A) For the purposes of subsection (5) an “**identity document**” means—
>
> - (a) a United Kingdom passport (within the meaning of the [Immigration Act 1971](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/77/contents));
> - (b) a passport issued by or on behalf of the authorities of a country or territory outside the United Kingdom or by or on behalf of an international organisation;
> - (c) a licence to drive a motor vehicle—
>   - (i) granted under Part 3 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 or [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1981/154/part/2) of the [Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1981/154/contents) ([S.I. 1981/154 (N.I. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1981/154)), or
>   - (ii) issued in the form of a photocard by a member State;
> - (d) any other document specified in regulations made by the Scottish Ministers.

(3) In [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/141C)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/141C) for “a person aged 18 or over” substitute “the recipient”.

(4) In [subsection (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/141C) for “a person aged 18 or over” substitute “the recipient”.

(5) In [subsection (9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/141C) omit “or a person acting on behalf of the recipient” in both places it occurs.

(6) After [subsection (10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/141C) insert—

> (11) Regulations made by the Scottish Ministers under subsection (5)(a)(ii) are subject to the affirmative procedure.
>
> (12) Regulations made by the Scottish Ministers under subsection (5A)(d) are subject to the negative procedure.
>
> (13) For the meaning of “affirmative procedure” and “negative procedure” see [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/10/part/2) of the [Interpretation and Legislative Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (asp 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/10/contents).

(7) In section 141A(4B) of the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (sale of knives to persons under 18: Scotland) for paragraphs (a) to (b) substitute—

> - (a) a United Kingdom passport (within the meaning of the [Immigration Act 1971](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/77/contents));
> - (aa) a passport issued by or on behalf of the authorities of a country or territory outside the United Kingdom or by or on behalf of an international organisation;
> - (b) a licence to drive a motor vehicle—
>   - (i) granted under Part 3 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 or [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1981/154/part/2) of the [Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1981/154/contents) ([S.I. 1981/154 (N.I. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1981/154)), or
>   - (ii) issued in the form of a photocard by a member State;

.
