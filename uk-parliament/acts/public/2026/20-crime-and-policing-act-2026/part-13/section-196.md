## 196. Notification requirements for child cruelty offenders: interpretation[^c-c68207239581c96f33a1bf32b19df44f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In section 193(2), a reference to a conviction includes a reference to a finding of a magistrates’ court, where the court makes an order under [section 37](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/37)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/37) of the [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents), that the accused did the act charged.

(2) The following provisions do not apply for the purposes of section 193(2)—

- (a) section 82(2) of the Sentencing Code, and
- (b) [section 187](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/section/187)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/section/187) of the [Armed Forces Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/contents),

(conviction with absolute or conditional discharge deemed not to be a conviction).

(3) A reference in Schedule 24 to an offence (“offence A”) includes—

- (a) an attempt or conspiracy to commit offence A;
- (b) an offence under Part 2 of the Serious Crime Act 2007 (encouraging or assisting crime) in relation to which offence A is the offence (or one of the offences) which the person believed would be committed;
- (c) aiding, abetting, counselling or procuring the commission of offence A.

(4) References in Schedule 24 to a person’s age are to the person’s age at the time of the offence.

(5) References in Schedule 24 to imprisonment include—

- (a) a period of detention which a person is liable to serve under a detention and training order;
- (b) a sentence of detention in a young offender institution;
- (c) a sentence of detention under section 250 of the Sentencing Code or [section 209](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/section/209) of the [Armed Forces Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/contents);
- (d) a sentence of custody for life under section 272 of the Sentencing Code (including one passed as a result of [section 210A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/section/210A) of the [Armed Forces Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/contents));
- (e) an extended sentence under section 254 of the Sentencing Code (including one passed as a result of [section 221A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/section/221A) of the [Armed Forces Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/contents)).
