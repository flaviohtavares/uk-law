## Schedule 2 — Closure of premises by registered social housing provider

*Extent: E+W*

Section 5

**1.**[^c-f7498c84dd7d23b3ebcfb3ecea7c19d6][^c-eb99aba80ed3a4fa3736466bdc9eddcf] The [Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/contents) is amended as follows.

**2.**[^c-bafece0b34ded8c3b7c492745d64542d][^c-f4eb18c508aa4de78dbf0272369b2bc0] In [section 76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/76) (power to issue closure notices)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/76)—
  - (i) omit “or”, in the first place it appears;
  - (ii) after “authority,” insert “or a registered social housing provider (“RSH provider”),”;
- (b) after [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/76) insert—

  > (1A) An RSH provider may only issue a closure notice in respect of premises owned or managed by that provider.

  ;
- (c) in [subsection (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/76)—
  - (i) for “or local authority” substitute “, local authority or RSH provider”;
  - (ii) for “or authority” substitute “, authority or provider”.

**3.**[^c-67ce4c5a300b91892d21d2c99eaabe03][^c-05f00aafc7a8a0922505670a007e67a0] In [section 77](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/77) (duration of closure notices)—

- (a) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/77)—
  - (i) omit “or” at the end of [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/77);
  - (ii) after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/77) insert

    > , or
    >
    > - (c) if, in the case of a notice issued by an RSH provider, the notice is signed by an individual who is part of the provider’s senior management.

    ;
- (b) in [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/77)—
  - (i) omit “or” at the end of [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/77);
  - (ii) after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/77) insert

    > , or
    >
    > - (c) if, in the case of a notice issued by an RSH provider, the RSH provider issues an extension notice signed by an individual who is part of the provider’s senior management.

    ;
- (c) after [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/77) insert—

  > (7) For the purposes of this section, an individual is part of an RSH provider’s senior management if the individual plays a significant role in—
  >
  > - (a) the making of decisions about how the whole or a substantial part of the activities of the provider which relate to social housing are to be managed or organised, or
  > - (b) the management or organisation of the whole or a substantial part of such activities.

**4.**[^c-81fea0bd0c9774096696bdf2b0cc26d8][^c-a688cd22a396b5fd761125de393a21be] In [section 78](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/78) (cancellation or variation of closure notices)—

- (a) in [subsections (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/78), [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/78), [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/78) and [(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/78), for “officer or authority” substitute “person”;
- (b) after [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/78), insert—

  > (4A) A cancellation notice or a variation notice that relates to a closure notice which was—
  >
  > - (a) issued by an RSH provider, and
  > - (b) signed as mentioned in section 77(2)(c),
  >
  > must be signed by the person who signed the closure notice (or, if that person is not available, by another person who could have signed the closure notice).

  ;
- (c) in [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/78)—
  - (i) after “local authority” insert “or RSH provider”;
  - (ii) after “section 77(4)(b)” insert “or (c) (as the case may be)”;
- (d) in [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/78), after [paragraph (c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/78) insert—

  > - (d) in the case of a closure notice issued by an RSH provider, that provider.

**5.**[^c-b532a84681e915cc761f6b8e459a5f54][^c-c656ca908e48842cd0b77d09617b67f9] In [section 79](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/79) (service of notices)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/79), after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/79) insert

  > ;
  >
  > - (c) a representative of the provider that issued the notice, in the case of a notice issued by an RSH provider.

  ;
- (b) in [subsections (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/79), [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/79) and [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/79), omit “local authority”;
- (c) after [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/79) insert—

  > (6) In this section “**representative**”, in relation to an RSH provider, means—
  >
  > - (a) an employee of the provider, or
  > - (b) a person, or employee of a person, acting on behalf of the provider.

**6.**[^c-aaec3b8308a22042e142559d4175ad1d][^c-93eb72e52ffe0447a0dc78e1fd2da547] In [section 80](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/80) (power of court to make closure orders), in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/80), after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/80) insert—

> - (c) by the provider that issued the closure notice, if the notice was issued by an RSH provider.

**7.**[^c-c18b4b41d08d064ac34b97c3450b505a][^c-00abd9f85c662d61f27f2ea5fbf1eff2] In [section 82](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/82) (extension of closure orders)—

- (a) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/82), after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/82) insert—

  > - (c) where the closure order was made on the application of an RSH provider, that provider.

  ;
- (b) in [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/82)—
  - (i) for “or local authority” substitute “, local authority or RSH provider”;
  - (ii) for “the appropriate consultee has been consulted” substitute “appropriate consultation”;
  - (iii) after “application” insert “has been carried out”;
- (c) for [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/82) substitute—

  > (4) An appropriate consultation about the intention to make the application is carried out if the following are consulted—
  >
  > - (a) the local authority and any relevant RSH provider, in the case of an application by a police officer;
  > - (b) the chief officer of police for the area in which the premises are situated and any relevant RSH provider, in the case of an application by a local authority;
  > - (c) the local authority and the chief officer of police for the area in which the premises are situated, in the case of an application by an RSH provider.
  >
  > An RSH provider is “relevant” if it owns or manages the premises.

**8.**[^c-963f744082205f9cd3c723790fcb5d80][^c-2424a6b2d67ebfe82f8022773e601182] In [section 83](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/83) (discharge of closure orders)—

- (a) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/83), after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/83) insert—

  > - (ba) the provider that applied for the closure order, where the order was made on the application of an RSH provider;

  ;
- (b) after [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/83) insert—

  > (6A) Where—
  >
  > - (a) the order in question was made on the application of an RSH provider, and
  > - (b) a person other than that provider makes an application under this section for the discharge of the order,
  >
  > the justice may issue a summons directed to that provider requiring it to appear before the magistrates’ court to respond to the application.
  >
  > (6B) If a summons is issued under subsection (6A), a notice stating the date, time and place of the hearing of the application must be served on—
  >
  > - (a) the provider mentioned in that subsection;
  > - (b) the persons mentioned in subsection (2)(c) and (d) (other than the complainant).

**9.**[^c-d59bff653c986ec11dc4979d22d7febd][^c-da2160ae3a0ddbf495ffe673617ebffd] In [section 84](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/84) (appeals), after [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/84) insert—

> - (c) a decision (under section 81) not to order the continuation in force of a closure notice issued by that provider.

**13.**[^c-2d666f044188f99a4d8d97e5fc28f48b][^c-9c37a1dbcdd98afe48ebc7c58d2a9d21] In [section 89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/89) (exemption from liability)—

**15.**[^c-92a868486a68b11409fcfa113138f125][^c-621f4f0cddec5427bc43acc49013b9fb] In [section 92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/92) (interpretation)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/92), insert at the appropriate place—

  > - “**registered social housing provider**” (or “RSH provider”) means—
  >   - (a) in relation to England, a registered provider of social housing;
  >   - (b) in relation to Wales, a Welsh body registered as a social landlord under [section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/section/3) of the [Housing Act 1996](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/contents);

  ;
- (b) after [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/92), insert—

  > (4) For the purposes of this Chapter, an RSH provider owns premises if—
  >
  > - (a) the provider is a person (other than a mortgagee not in possession) entitled to dispose of the fee simple in the premises, whether in possession or in reversion, or
  > - (b) the provider is a person who holds or is entitled to the rents and profits of the premises under a lease that (when granted) was for a term of at least 3 years.
