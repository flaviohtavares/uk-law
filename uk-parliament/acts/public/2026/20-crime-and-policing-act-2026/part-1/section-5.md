## 5. Closure of premises by registered social housing provider[^c-6841042d34b18c710681a73a673274ab][^c-34770492648fc841b7891359259ee31e]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 2 amends [Chapter 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/part/4/chapter/3) of [Part 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/part/4) of the [Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/contents) so as to enable registered social housing providers to close premises that they own or manage which are associated with nuisance and disorder.
