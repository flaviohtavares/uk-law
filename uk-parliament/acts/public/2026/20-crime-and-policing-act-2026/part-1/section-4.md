# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Fixed penalty notices[^key-af850160e2961b2877054bfdf3644dfd][^key-9d75db4fccaa926ff10c83ca87a860cd]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In [Schedule 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/30/schedule/5) to the [Police Reform Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/30/contents) (powers exercisable by accredited persons), in [paragraph 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/30/schedule/5/paragraph/1)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/30/schedule/5/paragraph/1), after [paragraph (ac)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/30/schedule/5/paragraph/1) insert—

> - (ad) the power of an authorised person to issue a fixed penalty notice under [section 52](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/52) of the [Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/contents) (fixed penalty notices in respect of failure to comply with community protection notice);
> - (ae) the power of a constable or an authorised person to issue a fixed penalty notice under [section 68](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/68) of the [Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/contents) (fixed penalty notices in respect of offences relating to public spaces protection orders and expedited orders);

.

(2) The [Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/contents) is amended as set out in subsections (3) to (6).

(3) In [section 52](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/52) (fixed penalty notices), in [subsection (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/52), for “£100” substitute “£500”.

(4) In section 56 (guidance)—

- (a) at the beginning insert—

  > (A1) The Secretary of State must issue—
  >
  > - (a) guidance to chief officers of police about the issue of fixed penalty notices under section 52 by officers under their direction or control, and
  > - (b) guidance to local authorities about the issue of fixed penalty notices under section 52 by local authorities and persons designated under section 53(1)(c).

  ;
- (b) in subsection (1)(a), after “those officers’” insert “other”;
- (c) in subsection (1)(b)—
  - (i) after “their” insert “other”;
  - (ii) for “those” substitute “other functions”;
- (d) after subsection (3) insert—

  > (4) The Secretary of State must comply with the duty in subsection (A1)(a) and (b) within 6 months of the day on which the Crime and Policing Act 2026 is passed.

(5) In [section 68](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/68) (fixed penalty notices), in [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12/section/68), for “£100” substitute “£500”.

(6) In section 73 (guidance)—

- (a) at the beginning insert—

  > (A1) The Secretary of State must issue guidance to local authorities about the issue of fixed penalty notices under section 68 by persons authorised by local authorities under that section.

  ;
- (b) in subsection (1)(a), for “those” substitute “other functions”;
- (c) after subsection (3) insert—

  > (4) The Secretary of State must comply with the duty in subsection (A1) within 6 months of the day on which the Crime and Policing Act 2026 is passed.
