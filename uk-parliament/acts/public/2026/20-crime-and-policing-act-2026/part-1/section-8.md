# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-11-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8. Seizure of motor vehicles used in manner causing alarm, distress or annoyance[^c-bbccf5f98ac408d285a47ea4144b15b9][^c-f9149ce719c99ea6923333504ad1b2c1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In [section 59](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/30/section/59) of the [Police Reform Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/30/contents) (vehicles used in manner causing alarm, distress or annoyance) omit subsections (4) and (5) (powers of seizure etc exercisable only if warning given, subject to certain exceptions).

(2) The amendment made by subsection (1) does not apply in relation to the use of a motor vehicle on any occasion before the coming into force of this section.
