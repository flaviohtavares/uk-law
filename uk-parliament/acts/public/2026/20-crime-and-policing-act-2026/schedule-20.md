# Crime and Policing Act 2026, Schedule 20

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Schedule 20 — Drug testing in police detention: trigger offences[^c-1cae7a2a9ad730504a750f957f323ad1]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Section 183

This is the Schedule to be inserted after [Schedule 2A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/schedule/2A) to the [Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/contents)—

> **Schedule 2B — Testing for controlled drugs: trigger offences**
>
> Section 63C
>
> **1. Common law offences**
>
> Common assault.
>
> **2.** Battery.
>
> **3. Offences against the Person Act 1861**
>
> Offences under the following provisions of the Offences against the Person Act 1861—
>
> - (a) section 16 (threats to kill);
> - (b) section 18 (wounding with intent to cause grievous bodily harm);
> - (c) section 20 (malicious wounding);
> - (d) section 47 (assault occasioning actual bodily harm).
>
> **4. Children and Young Persons Act 1933**
>
> An offence under section 1 of the Children and Young Persons Act 1933 (cruelty to persons under sixteen).
>
> **5. Prevention of Crime Act 1953**
>
> An offence under section 1 of the Prevention of Crime Act 1953 (carrying offensive weapon in public place).
>
> **6. Restriction of Offensive Weapons Act 1959**
>
> An offence under section 1(1A) of the Restriction of Offensive Weapons Act 1959 (possession of flick knife, flick gun or gravity knife).
>
> **7. Theft Act 1968, etc**
>
> Offences under the following provisions of the [Theft Act 1968](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/60/contents)—
>
> - (a) [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/60/section/1) (theft);
> - (b) [section 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/60/section/8) (robbery);
> - (c) [section 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/60/section/9) (burglary);
> - (d) [section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/60/section/10) (aggravated burglary);
> - (e) [section 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/60/section/12) (taking motor vehicle or other conveyance without authority);
> - (f) [section 12A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/60/section/12A) (aggravated vehicle-taking);
> - (g) [section 22](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/60/section/22) (handling stolen goods);
> - (h) [section 25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/60/section/25) (going equipped for stealing, etc).
>
> **8.** An offence under [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/47/section/1)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/47/section/1) of the [Criminal Attempts Act 1981](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/47/contents), if committed in respect of an offence under any of the following provisions of the Theft Act 1968—
>
> - (a) section 1 (theft);
> - (b) section 8 (robbery);
> - (c) section 9 (burglary);
> - (d) section 22 (handling stolen goods).
>
> **9. Misuse of Drugs Act 1971**
>
> Offences under the following provisions of the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1971](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/38/contents), if committed in respect of a specified controlled drug—
>
> - (a) [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/38/section/4) (restriction of production and supply of controlled drugs);
> - (b) [section 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/38/section/5)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/38/section/5) (possession of controlled drug);
> - (c) [section 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/38/section/5)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/38/section/5) (possession of controlled drug with intent to supply).
>
> **10. Criminal Damage Act 1971**
>
> Offences under the following provisions of the [Criminal Damage Act 1971](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/48/contents), other than an offence of arson—
>
> - (a) [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/48/section/1)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/48/section/1) (destroying or damaging property);
> - (b) [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/48/section/1)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/48/section/1) (destroying or damaging property with intent to endanger life, etc).
>
> **11. Public Order Act 1986**
>
> Offences under the following provisions of the [Public Order Act 1986](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/64/contents)—
>
> - (a) [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/64/section/2) (violent disorder);
> - (b) [section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/64/section/3) (affray);
> - (c) [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/64/section/4) (fear or provocation of violence);
> - (d) [section 4A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/64/section/4A) (intentional harassment, alarm or distress);
> - (e) [section 4B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/64/section/4B) (intentional harassment, alarm or distress on account of sex);
> - (f) [section 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/64/section/5) (harassment).
>
> **12. Criminal Justice Act 1988**
>
> Offences under the following provisions of the [Criminal Justice Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/contents)—
>
> - (a) [section 139](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/139) (having article with blade or point in public place);
> - (b) [section 139A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/139A)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/139A) (having article with blade or point on education premises);
> - (c) [section 139A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/139A)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/139A) (having offensive weapon on education premises);
> - (d) [section 141](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/141)[(1A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/141) (possession of offensive weapon in private).
>
> **13. Road Traffic Act 1988**
>
> Offences under the following provisions of the [Road Traffic Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/52/contents)—
>
> - (a) [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/52/section/4)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/52/section/4) (driving when unfit through drink or drugs);
> - (b) [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/52/section/4)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/52/section/4) (being in charge when unfit through drink or drugs).
>
> **14. Football (Offences) Act 1991**
>
> Offences under the following provisions of the [Football (Offences) Act 1991](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/19/contents)—
>
> - (a) [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/19/section/2) (throwing of missiles);
> - (b) [section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/19/section/3) (indecent or racialist chanting);
> - (c) [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/19/section/4) (going onto the playing area).
>
> **15. Protection from Harassment Act 1997**
>
> Offences under the following provisions of the [Protection from Harassment Act 1997](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/40/contents)—
>
> - (a) [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/40/section/2) (harassment);
> - (b) [section 2A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/40/section/2A) (stalking);
> - (c) [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/40/section/4) (putting people in fear of violence);
> - (d) [section 4A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/40/section/4A) (stalking involving fear of violence or serious alarm or distress).
>
> **16. Crime and Disorder Act 1998**
>
> An offence under [section 32](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/37/section/32) of the [Crime and Disorder Act 1998](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/37/contents) (racially or religiously aggravated harassment, etc).
>
> **17. Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001**
>
> An offence under [section 42A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16/section/42A) of the [Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16/contents) (harassment etc of a person in their home).
>
> **18. Sexual Offences Act 2003**
>
> Offences under the following provisions of the [Sexual Offences Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/contents)—
>
> - (a) [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/1) (rape);
> - (b) [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/2) (assault by penetration);
> - (c) [section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/3) (sexual assault);
> - (d) [section 61](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/61) (administering a substance with intent).
>
> **19. Serious Crime Act 2015**
>
> Offences under the following provisions of the [Serious Crime Act 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/contents)—
>
> - (a) [section 75A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/75A) (strangulation or suffocation);
> - (b) [section 76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/76) (controlling or coercive behaviour).
