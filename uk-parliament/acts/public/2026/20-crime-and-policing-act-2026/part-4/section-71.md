# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 71

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Protections for witnesses, and lifestyle offences[^key-8c0fb5dc038bd0580c477915659c95a6]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In Part 2 of the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999—

- (a) in section 17(4A) after paragraph (b) insert—

  > - (ba) an offence under section 48 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (child criminal exploitation);
  > - (bb) an offence under section 65 of that Act (controlling another’s home for criminal purposes);
  > - (bc) an offence under section 68 of that Act (causing internal concealment of item for criminal purpose);

  ;
- (b) in section 33(6) after paragraph (d) insert—

  > - (e) an offence under section 48 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (child criminal exploitation);
  > - (f) an offence under section 65 of that Act (controlling another’s home for criminal purposes);
  > - (g) an offence under section 68 of that Act (causing internal concealment of item for criminal purpose);

  ;
- (c) in section 35(3) after paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (aa) an offence under section 48 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (child criminal exploitation);
  > - (ab) an offence under section 65 of that Act (controlling another’s home for criminal purposes);
  > - (ac) an offence under section 68 of that Act (causing internal concealment of item for criminal purpose);

  .

*Extent: N.I.*

(2) In Part 2 of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 ([S.I. 1999/2789 (N.I. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1999/2789)) (special measures directions for vulnerable witnesses etc)—

- (a) in Article 5(5) after sub-paragraph (d) insert—

  > - (e) an offence under section 48 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (child criminal exploitation);
  > - (f) an offence under section 65 of that Act (controlling another’s home for criminal purposes).

  ;
- (b) in Article 21(5) for “a slavery or human trafficking offence,” substitute

  > —
  >
  > - (a) a slavery or human trafficking offence,
  > - (b) an offence under section 48 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (child criminal exploitation), or
  > - (c) an offence under section 65 of that Act (controlling another’s home for criminal purposes),

  .

*Extent: N.I.*

(3) In Article 23 of that Order—

- (a) in paragraph (3) after sub-paragraph (ce) insert—

  > - (cf) an offence under section 48 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (child criminal exploitation);
  > - (cg) an offence under section 65 of that Act (controlling another’s home for criminal purposes);

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (4)(a) for the words from the beginning to “(ce)” substitute “except in a case mentioned in sub-paragraph (b)”.

*Extent: N.I.*

(4) In the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002—

- (a) in Schedule 2 (lifestyle offences: England and Wales), after paragraph 3A insert—

  > **3B. Exploitation of vulnerable persons**
  >
  > (1) An offence under section 48 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (child criminal exploitation).
  >
  > (2) An offence under section 65 of that Act (controlling another’s home for criminal purposes).
  >
  > (3) An offence under section 68 of that Act (causing internal concealment of item for criminal purpose).

  ;
- (b) in Schedule 5 (lifestyle offences: Northern Ireland), after paragraph 3A insert—

  > **3B. Exploitation of vulnerable persons**
  >
  > (1) An offence under section 48 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (child criminal exploitation).
  >
  > (2) An offence under section 65 of that Act (controlling another’s home for criminal purposes).
