# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 233

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 233. Offence of wearing or displaying articles in support of proscribed organisation[^key-e01745cdcf769da0abede1677fe97c08][^key-e31df2951b3cd728e1086fda85557657]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) [Section 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13) of the [Terrorism Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/contents) (offence of wearing or displaying articles in support of proscribed organisation) is amended as follows.

(2) After [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13) insert—

> (1ZA) A person commits an offence if, on relevant premises, the person—
>
> - (a) wears an item of clothing, or
> - (b) wears, carries or displays an article,
>
> in such a way or in such circumstances as to arouse reasonable suspicion that the person is a member or supporter of a proscribed organisation.

(3) In [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13)—

- (a) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13) after “(1)” insert “or (1ZA)”;
- (b) in [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13)—
  - (i) the words from “the evidence” to the end become [sub-paragraph (i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13);
  - (ii) after [that sub-paragraph](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13) insert

    > , or
    >
    > - (ii) the item or article continuing to be displayed.

(4) After [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13) insert—

> (7) An item of clothing or other article seized by a constable under subsection (4) may be destroyed.
>
> (8) In subsection (1ZA) “**relevant premises**” means any land and buildings used for the purposes of, or in connection with—
>
> - (a) a prison within the meaning of the Prison Act 1952;
> - (b) a young offender institution within the meaning of section 43(1) of that Act;
> - (c) a secure training centre within the meaning of section 43(1) of that Act;
> - (d) approved premises within the meaning of [section 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/21/section/13) of the [Offender Management Act 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/21/contents);
> - (e) a prison within the meaning of the [Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/45/contents);
> - (f) a young offenders institution within the meaning of [section 19](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/45/section/19)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/45/section/19) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/45/contents);
> - (g) a prison within the meaning of the [Prison Act (Northern Ireland) 1953 (c. 18 (N.I.))](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/apni/1953/18/contents);
> - (h) a young offenders centre within the meaning of [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/apni/1968/29/section/2) of the [Treatment of Offenders Act (Northern Ireland) 1968 (c. 29 (N.I.))](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/apni/1968/29/contents);
> - (i) a juvenile justice centre within the meaning of [Article 51](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1998/1504/section/51) of the [Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1998/1504/contents) ([S.I. 1998/1504 (N.I. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1998/1504));
> - (j) service custody premises within the meaning of [section 300](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/section/300) of the [Armed Forces Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/contents);
> - (k) a removal centre within the meaning of [section 147](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/33/section/147) of the [Immigration and Asylum Act 1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/33/contents).

(5) In the Armed Forces Act 2006, after section 93ZD (inserted by section 172) insert—

> **93ZE. Powers of seizure equivalent to powers under [section 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13) of the [Terrorism Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/contents)**
>
> (1) This section applies in relation to an offence under section 42 as respects which the corresponding offence under the law of England and Wales is an offence under [section 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13)(1ZA) of the [Terrorism Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/contents) (wearing or displaying articles in support of proscribed organisation).
>
> (2) [Section 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13)[(4) to (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13/subsection/4) of the [Terrorism Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/contents) (powers of seizure) applies in relation to the offence under section 42 as it applies to an offence under [section 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13)(1ZA) of the [Terrorism Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/contents), but reading references to a constable as references to a service policeman.
