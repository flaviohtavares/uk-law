## 236. Length of terrorism sentence with fixed licence period: Northern Ireland[^key-fe4eb0c959a7902ea78347bcc6982768]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In Article 7 of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1216 (N.I. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1216))—

- (a) in paragraph (2) omit “Articles 13A, 14 and 15A and”;
- (b) in paragraph (3) before sub-paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (za) Articles 13A and 14 of this Order;

  .

(2) The amendments made by this section apply in relation to convictions occurring on or after the day on which this section comes into force.
