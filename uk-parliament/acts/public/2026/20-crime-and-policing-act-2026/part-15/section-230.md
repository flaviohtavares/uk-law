# Crime and Policing Act 2026, section 230

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 230. Applications[^c-2dc0d956c8b7130c0a1f79e03936f39f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) An application under this Chapter is to be made—

- (a) by complaint, where the application is made to a youth court or other magistrates’ court;
- (b) in accordance with rules of court, in any other case.

(2) The following do not apply to a complaint under this Chapter—

- (a) section 127 of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 (time limit for complaints etc);
- (b) Article 78(1) of the Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 ([S.I. 1981/1675 (N.I. 26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1981/1675)) (time limit for complaints).

(3) In Schedule 1 to the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act [2014 (asp 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2014/17) (civil proceedings etc in which summary sheriff has competence), after paragraph 12 insert—

> **13. Youth diversion orders**
>
> Proceedings for or in relation to a youth diversion order under section 215 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026.
