# Crime and Policing Act 2026, Schedule 10

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Schedule 10 — Online facilitation of child sexual exploitation and abuse: specified offences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Section 77

### Part 1 — England and Wales

**1.**[^c-5d188d2d207b53917a12bebee811a9bb] An offence under any of—

- (a) [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/37/section/1) of the [Protection of Children Act 1978](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/37/contents) (taking etc indecent photograph of child);
- (b) [section 160](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/section/160) of the [Criminal Justice Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/33/contents) (possession of indecent photograph of child);
- (c) the following provisions of the [Sexual Offences Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/contents)—
  - (i) [sections 5 to 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/5) (rape and other offences against children under 13);
  - (ii) sections 8A to 8C (rape and other offences against children under 16);
  - (iii) [sections 9 to 15A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/9) (other child sex offences);
  - (iv) [sections 16 to 19](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/16) (abuse of position of trust);
  - (v) [sections 25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/25) and [26](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/26) (familial child sex offences);
  - (vi) [section 46A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/46A) (child sexual abuse image-generators);
  - (vii) [sections 47 to 50](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/47) (sexual exploitation of children);
- (d) [section 62](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25/section/62) of the [Coroners and Justice Act 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25/contents) (possession of prohibited image of child);
- (e) [section 69](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/69) of the [Serious Crime Act 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/contents) (possession of paedophile manual).

**2.**[^c-85da961aa0a7cdad2b4abda25e91f71b] An offence under any of the following provisions of the [Sexual Offences Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/contents) where the victim, or intended victim, was aged under 18—

- (a) [sections 1 to 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/1) (rape, assault and causing sexual activity without consent);
- (b) [sections 30 to 41](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/30) (sexual offences relating to persons with mental disorder);
- (c) [sections 61 to 63](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/61) (preparatory offences);
- (d) [sections 66 to 66B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/66), [67](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/67) and [67A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42/section/67A) (exposure and voyeurism).

**3.**[^c-000604e203b237bae2262ecb10294d6b] An offence under [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/section/2) of the [Modern Slavery Act 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/contents) (human trafficking) against a person aged under 18, committed with a view to exploitation that consists of or includes behaviour within [section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/section/3)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/section/3) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/contents) (sexual exploitation).

**4.**[^c-f2aa2d0828d51657418206889c40e86f] An inchoate offence (within the meaning given by section 398 of the Sentencing Code) in relation to an offence specified in paragraph 1, 2 or 3.

### Part 2 — Scotland

**5.**[^c-577ea273aca04d92a9b8c32c0183a832] An offence under any of—

- (a) [sections 51](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/45/section/51), [51A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/45/section/51A), [52](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/45/section/52), 52A and 52D of the [Civic Government (Scotland) Act 1982](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/45/contents) (obscene material, extreme pornography, indecent photographs of children and child sexual abuse image-generators);
- (b) sections 9 and 10 of the Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995 (permitting girl to use premises for intercourse and seduction, prostitution, etc., of girl under 16);
- (c) [sections 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/9/section/1) and [9 to 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/9/section/9) of the [Protection of Children and Prevention of Sexual Offences (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/9/contents) (meeting a child following certain preliminary contact, sexual services of children and child pornography);
- (d) the following provisions of the [Sexual Offences (Scotland) Act 2009 (asp 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2009/9/contents)—
  - (i) [Part 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2009/9/part/4) (children);
  - (ii) [section 42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2009/9/section/42) (sexual abuse of trust).

**6.**[^c-1c0f790d131aba5a032a1684150cc4c0] An offence under any of the following provisions of the Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995 where the victim, or intended victim, was aged under 18—

- (a) section 1 (incest);
- (b) section 2 (intercourse with step-child);
- (c) section 7 (procuring).

**7.**[^c-b946e2b7ec3eae85f746214ddacc8455] An offence under any of the following provisions of the [Sexual Offences (Scotland) Act 2009 (asp 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2009/9/contents) where the victim, or intended victim, was aged under 18—

- (a) [Part 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2009/9/part/1) (rape etc);
- (b) [section 46](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2009/9/section/46) (sexual abuse of trust of a mentally disordered person).

**8.**[^c-a84e6258fd8b7992bcbe04849f01bb8a] An offence under section 1 of the Human Trafficking and Exploitation (Scotland) Act [2015 (asp 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2015/12) (human trafficking) against a person aged under 18, committed with a view to exploitation that consists of or includes behaviour within section 3(3) to (5) of that Act (prostitution and sexual exploitation).

**9.**[^c-de64d59d1ef8949faa406d3a963f9427] Attempting or conspiring to commit an offence specified in paragraph 5, 6, 7 or 8.

**10.**[^c-43be6c79122ab6dec3317f845c4dc792] Inciting a person to commit an offence specified in paragraph 5, 6, 7 or 8.

**11.**[^c-bbacd55599e2ed3a25aa352111a191c8] Aiding, abetting, counselling or procuring the commission of an offence specified in paragraph 5, 6, 7 or 8, or being involved art and part in the commission of such an offence.

### Part 3 — Northern Ireland

**12.**[^c-74b99ab16ab2812604e4eb11568127d3] An offence under any of—

- (a) [Article 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1978/1047/article/3) of the [Protection of Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1978](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1978/1047/contents) ([S.I. 1978/1047 (N.I. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1978/1047)) (indecent photographs of children);
- (b) [Article 15](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1988/1847/article/15) of the [Criminal Justice (Evidence, Etc.) (Northern Ireland) Order 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1988/1847/contents) ([S.I. 1988/1847 (N.I. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1988/1847)) (possession of indecent photographs of children);
- (c) the following provisions of the [Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/contents) ([S.I. 2008/1769 (N.I. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769))—
  - (i) [Articles 12 to 15](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/article/12) (rape and other offences against children under 13);
  - (ii) [Articles 16 to 22E](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/article/16) (child sex offences);
  - (iii) [Articles 23 to 26](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/article/23) (abuse of position of trust);
  - (iv) [Articles 32](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/article/32) and [33](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/article/33) (familial child sex offences);
  - (v) [Articles 37 to 40](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/article/37) (sexual offences against children);
  - (vi) Article 42A (child sexual abuse image-generators);
- (d) [section 62](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25/section/62) of the [Coroners and Justice Act 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25/contents) (possession of prohibited image of child);
- (e) [section 69](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/section/69) of the [Serious Crime Act 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9/contents) (possession of paedophile manual).

**13.**[^c-6d5d7d9bc4fddad9ccf2504ef8fbf1f1] An offence under any of the following provisions of the [Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/contents) where the victim, or intended victim, was aged under 18—

- (a) [Articles 5 to 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/article/) (rape, assault and causing sexual activity without consent);
- (b) [Part 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/part/4) (sexual offences against a person with a mental disorder);
- (c) [Articles 65 to 67](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/article/6) (preparatory offences);
- (d) [Articles 70 to 71B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/article/70) (exposure and voyeurism);
- (e) [Article 72A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2008/1769/article/72A) (sending etc an unwanted sexual image).

**14.**[^c-7dc29820e55a799b87a4b39d93f564f3] An offence under [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2015/2/section/2) of the [Human Trafficking and Exploitation (Criminal Justice and Support for Victims) Act (Northern Ireland) 2015 (c. 2 (N.I.))](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2015/2/contents) (human trafficking) against a person aged under 18, committed with a view to exploitation that consists of or includes behaviour within [section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2015/2/section/3)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2015/2/section/3) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2015/2/contents) (sexual exploitation).

**15.**[^c-657d6d815cc06fb794d6534eb62cd9e8] An attempt or conspiracy to commit an offence specified in paragraph 12, 13 or 14.

**16.**[^c-570bd7071e00bdba61d59edf2dab721a] An offence under [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/27/part/2) of the [Serious Crime Act 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/27/contents) in relation to an offence specified in paragraph 12, 13 or 14.
