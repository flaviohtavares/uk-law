## 169. Harassment of a person in their home[^c-ed9fae900679879207782e8975dd2655][^c-6ee4a54a54e02fa2fa618e9425987b2a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The [Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16/section/42) (police directions stopping harassment etc of a person in their home), in subsection [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16/section/42)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16/section/42)—

- (a) omit the “or” at the end of [sub-paragraph (i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16/section/42);
- (b) after [sub-paragraph (ii)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16/section/42) insert—

  > - (iii) that they should not have done something they were entitled or required to do; or
  > - (iv) that they should have done something they were not under any obligation to do;

  .

(3) In section 42A (offence of harassment etc of a person in their home), in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16/section/42A)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16/section/42A)—

- (a) omit the “or” at the end of [sub-paragraph (i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16/section/42A);
- (b) after [sub-paragraph (ii)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16/section/42A) insert—

  > - (iii) that they should not have done something they were entitled or required to do; or
  > - (iv) that they should have done something they were not under any obligation to do;

  .
