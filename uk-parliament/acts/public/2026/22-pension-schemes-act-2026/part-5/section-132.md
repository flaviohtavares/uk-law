# Pension Schemes Act 2026, section 132

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 132. Extent[^c-bff61d755f35498640c6e1fed37eca15]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject as follows, this Act extends to England and Wales and Scotland only.

(2) Sections 105 to 108 extend to Northern Ireland only.

(3) Chapter 3 of Part 4 extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(4) Section 128 extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(5) Any amendment, repeal or revocation made by this Act has the same extent as the provision amended, repealed or revoked.
