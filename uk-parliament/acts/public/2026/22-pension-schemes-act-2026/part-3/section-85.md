# Pension Schemes Act 2026, section 85

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Provision of information by responsible body to trustees[^key-dfabb07573ff555cdfc14332d3b8ca1b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The responsible body of an operating superfund must provide the trustees of the superfund scheme with whatever information relating to the superfund the trustees may reasonably request to enable the trustees to comply with any legislation relating to pensions that applies to them in respect of the superfund scheme.

(2) [Section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/10) of the [Pensions Act 1995](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/contents) (civil penalties) applies to the responsible body if it fails to comply with subsection (1).
