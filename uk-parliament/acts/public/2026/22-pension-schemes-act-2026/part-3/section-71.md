# Pension Schemes Act 2026, section 71

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 71. Duty to monitor financial thresholds[^c-85257804bfa6e701b9f00525d5ebcff5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The responsible body of an operating superfund must ensure that the superfund has adequate policies and procedures in place for monitoring whether each financial threshold is met.

(2) [Section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/10) of the [Pensions Act 1995](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/contents) (civil penalties) applies to the responsible body if it breaches subsection (1).

(3) See also Chapter 5 (events of concern) for the consequences of a financial threshold ceasing to be met.
