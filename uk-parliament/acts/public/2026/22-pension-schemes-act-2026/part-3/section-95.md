# Pension Schemes Act 2026, section 95

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 95. Release of capital buffer treated as reducing employer debt[^c-5120b3f0245d303e27dbb5ce075d903c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

Where some or all of the capital buffer is released in consequence of a debt falling due to the trustees of the superfund scheme under [section 75](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/75) of the [Pensions Act 1995](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/contents), the debt due under that section is treated as reduced by the value of the assets released (as calculated in accordance with regulations under section 89(9)).
