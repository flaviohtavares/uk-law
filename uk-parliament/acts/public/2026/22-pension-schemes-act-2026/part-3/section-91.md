## 91. Directions to pause payments or transfers of liabilities: supplementary provision[^c-b7919e9c600d51942aa23d4ac44c3675]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section applies to a direction under section 90(1)(d) (a “pause direction”).

(2) The Regulator may make a pause direction only if satisfied that doing so is reasonably necessary to protect the interests of members of the superfund scheme.

(3) A pause direction may make different provision for different purposes.

(4) The Regulator must cancel a pause direction if no longer satisfied as described in subsection (2).

(5) A pause direction, so far as not already cancelled, ceases to have effect when the period of concern to which it relates comes to an end.

(6) A payment that would have fallen due but for a pause direction falls due when the pause direction ceases to have effect.

(7) A pause direction within section 90(1)(d)(ii) (no transfers of liabilities) does not affect an order or provision falling within [section 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/30/section/28)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/30/section/28) of the [Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/30/contents) (pension sharing orders or provisions).

(8) The Secretary of State may by regulations modify any provision of [Part 4ZA](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/part/4ZA) of the [Pension Schemes Act 1993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/contents) (transfer rights etc) in its application to a superfund scheme in relation to which a pause direction has effect containing provision within section 90(1)(d)(ii) (no transfer of liabilities).

(9) Regulations under subsection (8) are subject to the affirmative procedure.
