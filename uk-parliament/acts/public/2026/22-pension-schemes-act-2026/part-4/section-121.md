# Pension Schemes Act 2026, section 121

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 121. Alienation or forfeiture of occupational pension[^c-3ec92405e173179b05ef466ac547ff22]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The [Pensions Act 1995](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/contents) is amended in accordance with subsections (2) and (3).

(2) In [section 91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/91) (inalienability of occupational pension)—

- (a) in [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/91), in the words after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/91)—
  - (i) for “there is a dispute as to its amount” substitute “a dispute has arisen as to the amount of the monetary obligation in question”;
  - (ii) for the words from “the obligation in question” to the end substitute “one of the following conditions is met.”;
- (b) after [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/91) insert—

  > (6A) The conditions mentioned in subsection (6) are—
  >
  > - (a) that the dispute has been resolved by the parties to it;
  > - (b) that the Pensions Ombudsman has made a determination under [Part 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/part/10) of the [Pension Schemes Act 1993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/contents)or [Part 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49/part/10) of the [Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49/contents) (investigations) as to the amount of the monetary obligation in question;
  > - (c) that the monetary obligation in question has become enforceable—
  >   - (i) under an order of a competent court, or
  >   - (ii) in consequence of an award of an arbitrator or, in Scotland, an arbiter to be appointed (failing agreement between the parties) by the sheriff.

(3) In [section 93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/93) (forfeiture by reference to obligation to employer), in [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/93)—

- (a) for “there is a dispute” substitute “a dispute has arisen”;
- (b) for the words from “the obligation has become” to the end substitute

  > —
  >
  > - (a) the dispute has been resolved by the parties to it,
  > - (b) the Pensions Ombudsman has made a determination under [Part 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/part/10) of the [Pension Schemes Act 1993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/contents)or [Part 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49/part/10) of the [Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49/contents) (investigations) as to the amount of the monetary obligation in question, or
  > - (c) the monetary obligation in question has become enforceable—
  >   - (i) under an order of a competent court, or
  >   - (ii) in consequence of an award of an arbitrator or, in Scotland, an arbiter to be appointed (failing agreement between the parties) by the sheriff.

(4) The [Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1995/3213/contents) is amended in accordance with subsections (5) and (6).

(5) In [Article 89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1995/3213/article/89) (inalienability of occupational pension)—

- (a) in [paragraph (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1995/3213/article/89), in the words after [sub-paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1995/3213/article/89)—
  - (i) for “there is a dispute as to its amount” substitute “a dispute has arisen as to the amount of the monetary obligation in question”;
  - (ii) for the words from “the obligation in question” to the end substitute “one of the following conditions is met.”;
- (b) after [paragraph (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1995/3213/article/89) insert—

  > (6A) The conditions mentioned in paragraph (6) are—
  >
  > - (a) that the dispute has been resolved by the parties to it;
  > - (b) that the Pensions Ombudsman has made a determination under [Part 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49/part/10) of the [Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49/contents) or [Part 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/part/10) of the [Pension Schemes Act 1993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/contents) (investigations) as to the amount of the monetary obligation in question;
  > - (c) that the monetary obligation in question has become enforceable—
  >   - (i) under an order of a competent court, or
  >   - (ii) in consequence of an award of an arbitrator.

(6) In [Article 91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1995/3213/article/91) (forfeiture by reference to obligation to employer), in [paragraph (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1995/3213/article/91)—

- (a) for “there is a dispute” substitute “a dispute has arisen”;
- (b) for the words from “the obligation has become” to the end substitute

  > —
  >
  > - (a) the dispute has been resolved by the parties to it,
  > - (b) the Pensions Ombudsman has made a determination under [Part 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49/part/10) of the [Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49/contents) or [Part 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/part/10) of the [Pension Schemes Act 1993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/contents) (investigations) as to the amount of the monetary obligation in question, or
  > - (c) the monetary obligation in question has become enforceable—
  >   - (i) under an order of a competent court, or
  >   - (ii) in consequence of an award of an arbitrator.
