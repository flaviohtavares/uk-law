# Pension Schemes Act 2026, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2026/22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Interpretation[^c-1f9e0cfd1b1e802a028adb1ea4c9e60d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**active member**” has the meaning given by section 124(1) of the Pensions Act 1995;
- “**deferred member**” has the meaning given by section 124(1) of the Pensions Act 1995;
- “**enactment**” includes—
  - (a) an enactment comprised in subordinate legislation (within the meaning given by [section 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/30/section/21) of the [Interpretation Act 1978](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/30/contents)),
  - (b) an enactment comprised in, or in an instrument made under, a Measure or Act of Senedd Cymru,
  - (c) an enactment comprised in, or in an instrument made under, an Act of the Scottish Parliament,
  - (d) an enactment comprised in, or in an instrument made under, Northern Ireland legislation;
- “**member**” has the meaning given by [section 124](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/124)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/124) of the [Pensions Act 1995](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/contents);
- “**money purchase benefits**” has the meaning given by [section 181](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/section/181) of the [Pension Schemes Act 1993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/contents);
- “**new public scheme**” has the meaning given by section 112(1);
- “**occupational pension scheme**” has the meaning given by [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/section/1) of the [Pension Schemes Act 1993](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48/contents);
- “**pension credit right**” has the meaning given by [section 124](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/124)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/section/124) of the [Pensions Act 1995](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26/contents);
- “**qualifying person**” has the meaning given by section 112(1);
- “**the trustee company**” means AWE Pension Trustees Ltd.
