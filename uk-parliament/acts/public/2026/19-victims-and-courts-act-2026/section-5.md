# Victims and Courts Act 2026, section 5

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Sections 3 and 4: consequential amendments[^key-07819907bb98f62614f15a35add3dfea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The [Children Act 1989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/contents) is amended in accordance with subsections (2) to (8) .

(2) In [section 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/8)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/8), after “10A” insert “, 10C or 10E”.

(3) In [section 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/9)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/9), [(6A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/9) and [(8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/9) (as amended or inserted by section 18 of the Victims and Prisoners Act 2024), after “10A” insert “, 10C or 10E”.

(4) In [section 10A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/10A) (inserted by section 18 of the Victims and Prisoners Act 2024)—

- (a) for the heading substitute “Duty to make prohibited steps order where one parent kills another”;
- (b) after [subsection (9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/10A) insert—

  > (10) A reference in this Act to an order under this section includes, so far as the context permits, an order varying or discharging it.

(5) In [section 10B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/10B), in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/10B), after “made” insert “(if any)”.

(6) In [section 33](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/33)[(3A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/33) (inserted by section 18 of the Victims and Prisoners Act 2024)—

- (a) after “10A” insert “, 10C or 10E”;
- (b) for “parent”, in both places it occurs, substitute “person”.

(7) In [section 91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/91)—

- (a) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/91), after “**10A**” (inserted by [section 18](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/21/section/18) of the [Victims and Prisoners Act 2024](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/21/contents)) insert “, 10C or 10E”;
- (b) after [subsection (14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/91) insert—

  > (14A) The reference in subsection (14) and section 91A(5)(a)(iii) to the disposal of an application includes an application under the following provisions—
  >
  > - section 10B,
  > - section 10D,
  > - section 10F, or
  > - section 10G.

(8) In [section 104](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/104)[(3AZA)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/104) (inserted by section 18 of the Victims and Prisoners Act 2024), for “section 10B(6)” substitute—

> -
>   - section 10B(6),
>   - section 10C(11),
>   - section 10D(4),
>   - section 10F(7), or
>   - section 10G(4).

(9) In [section 50](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/19/section/50)[(2A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/19/section/50) of the [Criminal Appeal Act 1968](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/19/contents) (inserted by section 18 of the Victims and Prisoners Act 2024), after “10A” insert “, 10C or 10E”.

(10) In [section 379](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/379) of the Sentencing Code (other behaviour orders etc), after [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/379) insert—

> (1A) See [sections 10A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/10A), 10C and 10E of the [Children Act 1989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/contents) for circumstances in which the Crown Court may be required to make a prohibited steps order when dealing with an offender.
