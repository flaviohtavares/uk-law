# Victims and Courts Act 2026, section 12

> Text as at 2026-08-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Private prosecutions: regulations about costs payable out of central funds[^c-9de525897a4a30a5396ac065e375b449]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The [Prosecution of Offences Act 1985](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/17) (prosecution costs)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/17)—
  - (i) in the words before [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/17), for “subsections (2) and (2A)” substitute “subsection (2)”;
  - (ii) for the words after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/17) substitute “make an order in favour of the prosecutor for a payment to be made out of central funds in respect of the prosecutor’s expenses.”;
- (b) after [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/17) insert—

  > (2ZA) An order under this section is, subject to the following provisions of this section, to be for the payment out of central funds to the prosecutor of such amount as the court considers reasonably sufficient to compensate the prosecutor for any expenses properly incurred by them in the proceedings.

  ;
- (c) in [subsection (2A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/17), for “subsection (1)” substitute “subsection (2ZA)”;
- (d) after [subsection (2A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/17) insert—

  > (2AA) Subsections (2ZA) and (2A) have effect subject to regulations under section 20(1A)(d).

(3) In [section 20](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/20) (regulations)—

- (a) in [subsection (1B)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/20) omit [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/20) together with the final “and”, and
- (b) after subsection (4) insert—

  > (4A) Before making regulations under subsection (1A) that relate to costs orders under section 17, the Lord Chancellor must—
  >
  > - (a) consult—
  >   - (i) the Law Society,
  >   - (ii) the General Council of the Bar, and
  >   - (iii) such other persons as the Lord Chancellor considers appropriate, and
  > - (b) publish an impact assessment in respect of the regulations.
