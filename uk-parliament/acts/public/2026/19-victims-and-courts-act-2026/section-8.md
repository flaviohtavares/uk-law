# Victims and Courts Act 2026, section 8

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Commissioner’s power to act in individual cases relevant to public policy[^key-36c6f7bbd0563fdf308f1f0e812fd76a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) [Section 51](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/28/section/51) of the [Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/28/contents) (restrictions on exercise of victims’ commissioner’s functions) is amended as follows.

(2) The existing text becomes [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/28/section/51).

(3) In [that subsection](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/28/section/51), in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/28/section/51), at the end insert “, except as mentioned in subsection (2)”.

(4) After [that subsection](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/28/section/51) insert—

> (2) Where the Commissioner considers that—
>
> - (a) the case of a particular victim or witness raises issues of public policy of relevance to other victims or witnesses, and
> - (b) the exercise of a function of the Commissioner in relation to that case is likely to promote the interests of other victims or witnesses in relation to those issues of public policy,
>
> the Commissioner may exercise that function for that purpose.

(5) The amendments made by this section have effect in relation to a person who is a victim or witness regardless of when the conduct in relation to which the person is a victim or witness occurred.

(6) In subsection (5) “victim” and “witness” have the meaning given by section 52 of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004.
