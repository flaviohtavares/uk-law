# Victims and Courts Act 2026, section 11

> Text as at 2026-08-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Appointment of Crown Prosecutors[^c-83db81f8523dbb46b2fc3b8f57604db8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The [Prosecution of Offences Act 1985](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/1) (the Crown Prosecution Service)—

- (a) in [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/1) omit “who has a general qualification (within the meaning of section 71 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990)”;
- (b) after [subsection (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/1) insert—

  > (8) Nothing in this section confers on a person designated for the purposes of subsection (3) who does not have a general qualification (within the meaning given by section 71 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990)—
  >
  > - (a) any rights of audience, or
  > - (b) any right to conduct litigation,
  >
  > for the purposes of [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/29/part/3) of the [Legal Services Act 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/29/contents) (reserved legal activities).
  >
  > (9) The following provisions of the Legal Services Act 2007 accordingly do not apply to such a person—
  >
  > - (a) paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 3 (exemption for persons with statutory rights of audience), and
  > - (b) paragraph 2(3) of that Schedule (exemption for persons with statutory right to conduct litigation).

(3) In [section 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/5) (conduct of prosecutions on behalf of the Crown Prosecution Service)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/5) omit “but who has a general qualification (within the meaning of section 71 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990)”;
- (b) after [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/23/section/5) insert—

  > (3) Nothing in this section confers on a person appointed under subsection (1) who does not have a general qualification (within the meaning given by section 71 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990)—
  >
  > - (a) any rights of audience, or
  > - (b) any right to conduct litigation,
  >
  > for the purposes of [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/29/part/3) of the [Legal Services Act 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/29/contents) (reserved legal activities).
  >
  > (4) The following provisions of the Legal Services Act 2007 accordingly do not apply to such a person—
  >
  > - (a) paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 3 (exemption for persons with statutory rights of audience), and
  > - (b) paragraph 2(3) of that Schedule (exemption for persons with statutory right to conduct litigation).
