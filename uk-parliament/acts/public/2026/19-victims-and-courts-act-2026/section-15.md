# Victims and Courts Act 2026, section 15

> Text as at 2026-08-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Terms of imprisonment for certain offences on summary conviction[^key-3dfec2f5711a376b8558187ded2067c8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In each of the following provisions for “6 months” substitute “the general limit in a magistrates’ court”—

- [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/3/section/1)[(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/3/section/1)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/3/section/1) of the [Prevention of Social Housing Fraud Act 2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/3/contents) (unlawful sub-letting: secure tenancies);
- [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/3/section/2)[(7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/3/section/2)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/3/section/2) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/3/contents) (unlawful sub-letting: assured tenancies and secure contracts);
- [section 30](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/section/30)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/section/30)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/section/30) of the [Modern Slavery Act 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/30/contents) (breach of certain orders or requirements);
- [section 339](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/section/339)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/section/339)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/section/339) of the [Sentencing Code](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/contents) (breach of criminal behaviour order);
- section 354(4)(a) of the Sentencing Code (breach of sexual harm prevention order);
- section 363(2)(a) of the Sentencing Code (breach of restraining order).

(2) An amendment made by subsection (1) has effect only in relation to an offence for which a person is convicted on or after the day on which this section comes into force.
