# Victims and Courts Act 2026, section 17

> Text as at 2026-08-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Extent[^key-313296dfa5366799394d32ecb9c8abb5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An amendment or repeal made by this Act has the same extent within the United Kingdom as the provision amended or repealed.

(2) [Subsections (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/section/384) and [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/section/384) of [section 384](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/section/384) of the [Armed Forces Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/contents) (extent outside the United Kingdom) apply to the amendments made by section 2 as those subsections apply to the provisions of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52/contents).

(3) Except as mentioned above, this Act extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
