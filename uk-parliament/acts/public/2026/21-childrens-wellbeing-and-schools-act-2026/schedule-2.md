# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, Schedule 2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Schedule 2 — Children not in school: consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Section 42

### 1. Children Act 1989[^c-a8c050eee235e66804639a896fbe01aa]

(1) The [Children Act 1989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/36) (education supervision orders), in [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/36)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/36), for “437” substitute “436I”.

(3) In [section 91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/91) (effect and duration of care orders etc), in [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/91), for “437” substitute “436I”.

(4) In [Schedule 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/schedule/3) (supervision orders), in [paragraph 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/schedule/3/paragraph/13)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/schedule/3/paragraph/13)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/schedule/3/paragraph/13)[(i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/schedule/3/paragraph/13) and [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/schedule/3/paragraph/13)[(i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/schedule/3/paragraph/13), for “437” substitute “436I”.

### 2. Education Act 1996[^c-6f73c153e29b40975dd985890775962b]

(1) The [Education Act 1996](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit [sections 437 to 443](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/437) (school attendance orders) and the italic headings before [sections 437](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/437) and [443](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/443).

(3) Before [section 444](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/444) insert—

> Offence of failure to secure regular attendance at school of registered pupil

.

(4) Before [section 445](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/445) insert—

> Offences: general

.

(5) In [sections 445](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/445)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/445), [446](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/446) and [447](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/447)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/447) and [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/447)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/447), for “443” substitute “436Q”.

(6) After [section 447](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/447) insert—

> **Interpretation of Chapter**
>
> **447A. Interpretation of Chapter 2**
>
> In this Chapter—
>
> - “**maintained school**” means any community, foundation or voluntary school or any community or foundation special school not established in a hospital;
> - “**school nomination notice**” means a notice under section 436L.

(7) In [section 580](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/580) (index), in the second column of the entry relating to “school attendance order”, for “section 437(3)” substitute “section 436I”.

### 3. School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^c-55bf1703bd5cb06863fc73dd57a39522]

In [section 86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/31/section/86) of the [School Standards and Framework Act 1998](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/31/contents) (parental preferences), in [subsection (8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/31/section/86)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/31/section/86), for “438(4) or 440(2)” substitute “436L(4) or 436O(2)”.

### 4. Sentencing Act 2020[^c-dce83956c26223f9bd4ef83796591df2]

In [section 369](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/section/369) of the [Sentencing Act 2020](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/contents) (parenting order in respect of certain offences under the Education Act 1996)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/section/369)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/section/369), for “443” substitute “436Q”;
- (b) in [subsections (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/section/369), [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/section/369) and [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/section/369)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/section/369), for “443” substitute “436Q”.
