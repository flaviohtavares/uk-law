# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 75

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Power to make consequential provision: Scotland[^c-a587630f6d45a067558a9f480e7947ea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations make provision that is consequential on section 13 (use of accommodation for deprivation of liberty).

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may contain only provision which would be within the legislative competence of the Scottish Parliament if it were contained in an Act of that Parliament.

(3) Regulations under this section may amend, repeal or revoke provision made by or under—

- (a) an Act of the Scottish Parliament passed before this Act, or
- (b) an Act passed or made before, or in the same session of Parliament as, this Act.

(4) For provision about instruments containing regulations under this section, see [section 27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/10/section/27) of the [Interpretation and Legislative Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (asp 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/10/contents) (Scottish statutory instruments).

(5) Except as provided by subsection (6), regulations made under this section are subject to the negative procedure (see [section 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/10/section/28) of the [Interpretation and Legislative Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (asp 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/10/contents)).

(6) Regulations made under this section that amend, repeal or revoke provision made by or under an Act of the Scottish Parliament, or an Act, are subject to the affirmative procedure (see [section 29](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/10/section/29) of the [Interpretation and Legislative Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (asp 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/10/contents)).

(7) The power to make regulations under this section includes power to make—

- (a) supplementary, incidental, transitional or saving provision;
- (b) different provision for different purposes or areas.
