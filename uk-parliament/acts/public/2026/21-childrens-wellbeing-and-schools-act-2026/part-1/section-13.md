# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 13

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Use of accommodation for deprivation of liberty[^key-18bef87f2609397046e9548e2aab0a9a]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) [Section 25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25) of the [Children Act 1989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/contents) (use of accommodation for restricting liberty) is amended as follows.

(2) In the following places, for “restricting” substitute “depriving children of their”—

- (a) the heading of the section;
- (b) [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25).

(3) After [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25) insert—

> (1A) Subject to the following provisions of this section, a child who is being looked after by a local authority in England or Wales may not, whilst being kept in relevant accommodation in England, be deprived of their liberty in that accommodation unless it appears—
>
> - (a) that—
>   - (i) the child has a history of absconding and is likely to abscond from any other description of accommodation, and
>   - (ii) if the child absconds, the child is likely to suffer significant harm, or
> - (b) that if the child is kept in any other description of accommodation the child is likely to injure themselves or other persons.
>
> (1B) References in this section to “relevant accommodation” are references to accommodation that—
>
> - (a) is provided for the purposes of the care and treatment of children, and
> - (b) is capable of being used (in whole or in part), in connection with the provision of such care and treatment, for the purpose of depriving children of their liberty.

(4) In [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25)—

- (a) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25)—
  - (i) in [sub-paragraph (i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25) after “Scotland” insert “, or be deprived of their liberty in relevant accommodation in England,”;
  - (ii) in [sub-paragraph (ii)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25) after “Scotland” insert “or be deprived of their liberty in relevant accommodation in England”;
- (b) in [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25) after “Scotland” insert “, or to be deprived of their liberty in relevant accommodation in England,”.

(5) In [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25)—

- (a) after “section” insert “in respect of a child being kept in secure accommodation”;
- (b) for “a child” substitute “the child”.

(6) After [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25) insert—

> (5ZA) Subsections (3) to (5) apply in respect of depriving a child of their liberty in relevant accommodation as they apply in respect of the keeping of a child in secure accommodation.

(7) In [subsection (5A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25), for “restrict the child’s” substitute “deprive the child of their”.

(8) In [subsection (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25)—

- (a) in [paragraph (c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25) after “Scotland” insert “or be deprived of their liberty in relevant accommodation in England”;
- (b) in [paragraph (d)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25) after “secure accommodation” insert “or be deprived of their liberty in relevant accommodation”.

(9) After [subsection (8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/25) insert—

> (8ZA) For the purposes of this section—
>
> - (a) references in subsection (1) to any other description of accommodation do not include relevant accommodation;
> - (b) references in subsection (1A) to any other description of accommodation do not include secure accommodation.

(10) In [section 104](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/104) of the [Children Act 1989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/contents) (regulations and orders)—

- (a) in [subsection (3A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/104), after “(3B)” insert “, (3BZA)”;
- (b) after [subsection (3B)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/104) insert—

  > (3BZA) Regulations fall within this subsection if they are regulations made in the exercise of the power conferred by section 25(2) or (7).

(11) In [section 93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/36/section/93) of the [Children (Scotland) Act 1995](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/36/contents) (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “secure accommodation”, omit [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/36/section/93);
- (b) after that definition insert—

  > - “**secure accommodation**”, in relation to England, means secure accommodation within the meaning of section 25 of the Children Act 1989 or relevant accommodation within the meaning of that section;

  .

(12) In [section 202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2011/1/section/202)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2011/1/section/202) of the [Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (asp 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2011/1/contents) (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “secure accommodation”, omit [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2011/1/section/202);
- (b) after that definition insert—

  > - “**secure accommodation**”, in relation to England, means secure accommodation within the meaning of section 25 of the Children Act 1989 or relevant accommodation within the meaning of that section,

  .
