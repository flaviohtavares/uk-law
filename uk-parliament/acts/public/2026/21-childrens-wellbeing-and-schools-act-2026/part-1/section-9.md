# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 9

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Care leavers not to be regarded as becoming homeless intentionally[^c-57005b155fe0dbd98ff5a97c12aa1431][^c-26f29fe07103a54a45722b74ceb72518]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In [section 191](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/section/191) of the [Housing Act 1996](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/contents) (becoming homeless intentionally)—

- (a) after [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/section/191) insert—

  > (1ZA) But a person does not become homeless intentionally in a case described in any of subsections (1A) to (1C).

  ;
- (b) in [subsection (1A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/section/191), for the words before paragraph (a) substitute “The first case is where—”;
- (c) after [subsection (1A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/section/191) insert—

  > (1B) The second case is where the person is a relevant child within the meaning given by [section 23A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/23A)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/23A) of the [Children Act 1989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/contents).
  >
  > (1C) The third case is where the person is a former relevant child within the meaning given by [section 23C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/23C)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/23C) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/contents) and aged under 25.

  ;
- (d) in [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/section/191), in the words before paragraph (a), after “person” insert “, other than a person described in subsection (1B) or (1C),”.

(2) The amendments made by this section do not apply in relation to an application of a kind mentioned in [section 183](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/section/183)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/section/183) of the [Housing Act 1996](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/contents) made before the date on which this section comes into force, except where the local housing authority deciding the application has not yet decided the matters set out in [section 184](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/section/184)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/section/184)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/section/184) and [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/section/184) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/52/contents).
