# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 2

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Inclusion of childcare and education agencies in safeguarding arrangements[^key-4a5588e00b840d1e7275389556eef23a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [Section 16E](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/31/section/16E) of the [Children Act 2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/31/contents) (local arrangements for safeguarding and promoting welfare of children) is amended as follows.

(2) In [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/31/section/16E)—

- (a) omit the “and” after [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/31/section/16E);
- (b) after [that paragraph](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/31/section/16E) insert—

  > - (aa) those relevant agencies which are designated childcare or education agencies, and

  ;
- (c) for [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/31/section/16E) substitute—

  > - (b) any other relevant agencies that the safeguarding partners consider appropriate,

  .

(3) In [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/31/section/16E), after “partners” insert “and the relevant agencies mentioned in subsection (1)(aa)”.

(4) After [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/31/section/16E) insert—

> (2A) For the purposes of this section, a relevant agency is a “designated childcare or education agency” if it—
>
> - (a) has functions relating to the provision of childcare or education (or both), and
> - (b) is designated as such by regulations made by the Secretary of State.

(5) In [section 66](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/31/section/66)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/31/section/66) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/31/contents) (instruments subject to affirmative procedure), for “16E(3)” substitute “16E(2A)(b) or (3)”.
