# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 25

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Corporate parenting duty: collaborative working[^c-58c3443e3966268dcea0bf4504f4a59f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Relevant authorities and local authorities in England must, so far as reasonably practicable, collaborate with each other when performing their corporate parenting duty where they consider that doing so would safeguard or promote the wellbeing of looked-after children or relevant young people.

(2) In subsection (1), “**corporate parenting duty**” means—

- (a) in the case of a relevant authority, the duty under section 23(1);
- (b) in the case of a local authority in England, the duty under [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/16/section/1)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/16/section/1) of the [Children and Social Work Act 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/16/contents).

(3) Collaboration under subsection (1) may in particular include—

- (a) sharing information;
- (b) providing advice or assistance;
- (c) co-ordinating activities (and seeking to prevent unnecessary duplication).

(4) Subsection (1) is not to be read as requiring or authorising a disclosure of information which is prohibited by any of [Parts 1 to 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/25/part/1) or [Chapter 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/25/part/9/chapter/1) of [Part 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/25/part/9) of the [Investigatory Powers Act 2016](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/25/contents).

(5) In this section—

- “**local authority in England**” has the same meaning as in [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/16/section/1) of the [Children and Social Work Act 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/16/contents);
- “**relevant authority**”, “**looked-after children**” and “**relevant young people**” have the same meaning as in section 23.

(6) In [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/16/section/1) of the [Children and Social Work Act 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/16/contents), after [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/16/section/1) insert—

> (5) See also section 25 of the Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, which requires local authorities in England to collaborate with other bodies in performing their respective corporate parenting duties.
