# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 22

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Ill-treatment or wilful neglect of children[^c-b697b1e39bd4783b3b59fdff8d8859a3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The [Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 20](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/20) (ill-treatment or wilful neglect: care worker offence)—

- (a) in [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/20)—
  - (i) omit “or” after [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/20);
  - (ii) after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/20) insert

    > or
    >
    > - (c) care or support for a child aged 16 or 17 at a regulated establishment,

    ;
- (b) after [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/20) insert—

  > (6A) “**Regulated establishment**” means—
  >
  > - (a) a children’s home in England as defined by [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/14/section/1) of the [Care Standards Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/14/contents);
  > - (b) a residential family centre in England as defined by [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/14/section/4) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/14/contents);
  > - (c) an establishment in England providing accommodation in respect of which requirements under [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/14/part/2) of the [Care Standards Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/14/contents) are applied by virtue of regulations under [section 42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/14/section/42) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/14/contents) (power to extend application of Part 2 of that Act);
  > - (d) youth detention accommodation in England as defined by [section 248](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/section/248)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/section/248) of the [Sentencing Act 2020](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/17/contents);
  > - (e) a place in Wales at which a care home service or a residential family centre service, as defined by [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/2/schedule/1) to the [Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016 (anaw 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/2/contents), is provided;
  > - (f) a place in Wales at which accommodation is provided to disabled children and which is notified to the Welsh Ministers in accordance with regulations under [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/2/section/2) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/2/contents);
  > - (g) youth detention accommodation in Wales as defined by [section 188](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2014/4/section/188)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2014/4/section/188) of the [Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (anaw 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2014/4/contents).

  ;
- (c) in [subsection (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/20) after “social care” insert “, or care or support at a regulated establishment,”.

(3) In [section 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/21) (ill-treatment or wilful neglect: care provider offence)—

- (a) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/21)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/21), for the words after “the provision of” to the end substitute “regulated care, or”;
- (b) after [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/21) insert—

  > (2A) “**Regulated care**” means—
  >
  > - (a) health care for an adult or child, other than excluded health care,
  > - (b) social care for an adult, or
  > - (c) care or support for a child provided at a regulated establishment.

  ;
- (c) in [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/21)—
  - (i) in [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/21) for “health care or social care as part of health care or social care” substitute “regulated care as part of such care”;
  - (ii) in the words after [that paragraph](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/21) for “health care or social care”, at both places where those words appear, substitute “regulated care”;
- (d) in [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/21) for “health care or social care” substitute “regulated care”;
- (e) in [subsection (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/21)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/21), for “health care or social care” substitute “regulated care”;
- (f) in [subsection (8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/21), for “health care or social care” substitute “regulated care”;
- (g) in [subsection (9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/21), in the second definition, after ““health care”” insert “, “regulated establishment””.

(4) In [section 25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/25) (care provider offence: liability for ancillary and other offences)—

- (a) in [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/25)—
  - (i) omit “or” after [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/25);
  - (ii) after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/25) insert

    > , or
    >
    > - (c) the provision of care or support for a child at a regulated establishment.

    ;
- (b) in [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2/section/25), in the second definition—
  - (i) before ““health care”” insert ““child”,”;
  - (ii) after ““health care”” insert “, “regulated establishment””.
