# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 47

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 47. Deregistration by agreement[^c-b09c18bd4a0f53bcafa81a87439ea452][^c-3698fa299f4c69cfd0e4a2f2b66fff0f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The [Education and Skills Act 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 100](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/100) (power to deregister), after [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/100) insert—

> (1A) The Secretary of State may also remove a registered institution from the register if the proprietor of the institution requests, or agrees to, its removal in writing.

(3) In [section 100](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/100)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/100), in the words before [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/100), for “The Secretary of State’s decision” substitute “A decision by the Secretary of State under subsection (1)”.

(4) In [section 124](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/124)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/124)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/124) (appeal against decision to deregister under section 100) for “100” substitute “100(1)”.
