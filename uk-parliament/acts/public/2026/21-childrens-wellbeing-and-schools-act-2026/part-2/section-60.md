> Extent: England, Wales.

## 60. Application of pay and conditions orders to education action zones[^c-e771e58a0f11a5ef8f4e81d9f79eee78]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [Section 128](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/128) of the [Education Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) (application of section 122 orders to teachers at schools forming part of education action zones) is repealed.

(2) In [section 210](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/210)[(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/210) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) (order and regulations)—

- (a) at the end of paragraph (b) insert “or”;
- (b) omit paragraph (c) (together with the final “or”).
