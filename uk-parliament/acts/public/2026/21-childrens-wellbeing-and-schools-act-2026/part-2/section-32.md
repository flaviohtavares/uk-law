# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 32

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Free school lunches: eligibility[^c-bc712daccce8c16806d14b83aae8323e][^c-123c1b84ac67aac26dfc8d3995eb34ec]

*Extent: E+W*

In [section 512ZB](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/512ZB) of the [Education Act 1996](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/contents) (free school lunches and milk)—

- (a) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/512ZB)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/512ZB), for “subsection (4) or (4A) (or both)” substitute “subsection (4), (4A) or (4D)”;
- (b) after [subsection (4C)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/512ZB) insert—

  > (4D) A person is within this subsection if—
  >
  > - (a) they are a registered pupil at a maintained school or pupil referral unit in England,
  > - (b) they or their parent is in receipt of universal credit, and
  > - (c) they are not within subsection (4);
  >
  > and the meaning of “maintained school” given by subsection (4C) applies for the purposes of this subsection.
