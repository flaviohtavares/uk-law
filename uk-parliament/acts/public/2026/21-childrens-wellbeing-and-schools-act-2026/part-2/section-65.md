# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 65

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65. Amendments to invitation process for establishment of new schools[^c-c4d010f646a6e4575b2cba75c8941b2a][^c-df023c677196373b21fa15d0f1124dca]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/part/2) of the [Education and Inspections Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/contents) (establishment, discontinuance or alteration of schools) is amended in accordance with subsections (2) to (4).

(2) Omit [section 6A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/6A) (requirement to seek proposals for establishment of new Academies).

(3) In [section 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7) (invitation for proposals for establishment of new schools)—

- (a) for [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7) substitute—

  > (1) This section applies where a local authority in England think that a new school, other than a maintained nursery school, should be established in their area.
  >
  > (1A) The local authority must (subject to subsection (2A)) publish a notice under this section inviting proposals from persons other than local authorities for the establishment of a new school falling within subsection (2).

  ;
- (b) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7)—
  - (i) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7), omit the words from “other than” to the end of the paragraph (including the “or”), and
  - (ii) at the end of [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7) insert

    > , or
    >
    > - (c) an alternative provision Academy,
    >
    > other than a school providing education suitable only to the requirements of persons above compulsory school age.

    ;
- (c) after [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7) insert—

  > (2A) A local authority are not required to publish a notice under this section (but may do so) if—
  >
  > - (a) the school that the local authority think should be established falls within section 10(2A), or
  > - (b) proposals have been published under section 10(2B) by other persons and those proposals have not yet been determined.

  ;
- (d) in [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7), at the end of [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7) insert

  > , and
  >
  > - (aa) may publish under this section proposals of their own for the establishment of—
  >   - (i) a new community, community special, foundation or foundation special school, other than one providing education suitable only to the requirements of persons above compulsory school age, or
  >   - (ii) a new pupil referral unit.

  ;
- (e) in [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7)—
  - (i) omit the “and” after [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7), and
  - (ii) after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7) insert

    > , and
    >
    > - (c) the information which proposals within subsection (5)(aa) must contain.

    .

(4) In [section 7A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7A) (withdrawal of notices under section 7)—

- (a) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7A)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7A), omit “, with the consent of the Secretary of State”;
- (b) after [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7A) insert—

  > (3) If the local authority withdraw the notice in accordance with subsection (2)(a) or (b), the local authority must notify the Secretary of State.

(5) In [section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/10) of the [Academies Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/contents) (consultation: new and expanded educational institutions), in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/10)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/10) omit the words from “other” to “authority),”.
