# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 48

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Imposition of relevant restrictions[^c-9c68ae34266aaaea761173ee46d75fc3]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The [Education and Skills Act 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 105](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/105)—

- (a) in the heading, for “Power to deregister” substitute “Powers in relation to”;
- (b) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/105)—
  - (i) for the words before [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/105) substitute “This section applies where—”;
  - (ii) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/105), for “the” substitute “an independent educational”;
- (c) after [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/105) insert—

  > (1A) The Secretary of State may—
  >
  > - (a) impose a relevant restriction on the proprietor of the institution (see section 117), or
  > - (b) remove the institution from the register.

  ;
- (d) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/105), omit “to remove it from the register”;
- (e) in [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/105)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/105), after “124” insert “or 125”.

(3) In [section 117](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/117) (meaning of “**relevant restriction**”), in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/117)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/117), after “section” insert “105(1A)(a) or”.

(4) In [section 118](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/118) (relevant restrictions imposed by Secretary of State)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/118), before “116(1)(a)” insert “105(1A)(a) or”;
- (b) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/118), omit “not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale”.

(5) In [section 125](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/125) (appeal by proprietor against other decisions of Secretary of State), in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/125)[(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/section/125), after “section” insert “105(1A)(a) or”.
