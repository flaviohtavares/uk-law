# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 67

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Establishment of pupil referral units[^key-aa8e1c8fedecdd724aedcb77f58d4517][^key-b9d6879a1e0ed74f51d498df40d83c34]

*Extent: E+W*

In [section 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/28) of the [Education and Inspections Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/contents) (restriction on establishment, alteration or discontinuance of schools), after [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/28) insert—

> (5) A pupil referral unit may not be established except in pursuance of proposals falling to be implemented under this Part.
