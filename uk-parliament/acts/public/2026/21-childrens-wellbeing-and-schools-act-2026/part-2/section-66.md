# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 66

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 66. Certain proposals to establish new schools: publication requirements etc[^c-8ed2744777c6e743d2507bac72f06256][^c-23e6807149650e232111cc840b8e11c9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/part/2) of the [Education and Inspections Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/contents) (establishment, discontinuance or alteration of schools) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/10) (publication of proposals with consent of Secretary of State)—

- (a) for the heading substitute “Proposals other than proposals pursuant to a section 7 notice: publication etc”;
- (b) for [subsections (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/10) and [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/10) substitute—

  > (1) Where a local authority in England propose to establish a new maintained nursery school, they must publish their proposals under this section.
  >
  > (2) Where a local authority in England propose to establish a new school within subsection (2A), they may publish their proposals under this section if there is for the time being no notice under section 7 pursuant to which (by virtue of section 7(5)(aa)) they could publish such proposals.
  >
  > (2A) A school is within this subsection if it is—
  >
  > - (a) a community, community special, foundation or foundation special school which—
  >   - (i) is not to be one providing education suitable only to the requirements of persons above compulsory school age, and
  >   - (ii) is to replace one or more maintained schools, or
  > - (b) a pupil referral unit which is to replace one or more pupil referral units.
  >
  > (2B) Where any persons other than a local authority (“proposers”) propose to establish a new foundation, voluntary or foundation special school in England, they may publish their proposals under this section if there is for the time being no notice under section 7 pursuant to which such proposals could be made.

  ;
- (c) in [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/10)—
  - (i) for “(2)” substitute “(2B)”,
  - (ii) the words from “the proposers” to the end become [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/10), and
  - (iii) at the end of [that paragraph](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/10) insert

    > , and
    >
    > - (b) the local authority must take such action to publicise the proposals as may be prescribed.

(3) Omit [section 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/11) (publication of proposals to establish maintained schools: special cases).

(4) In [section 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/12) (establishment of school as federated school), in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/12)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/12), for “7, 10 or 11” substitute “7 or 10”.

(5) In [section 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/13) (schools established outside area of relevant local authority), in [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/13), omit “or 11”.
