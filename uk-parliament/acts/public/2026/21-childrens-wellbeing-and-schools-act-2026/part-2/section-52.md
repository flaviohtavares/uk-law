## 52. Teacher misconduct[^key-8a287d2c4ccc61ad033ec4ac0de28ceb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The [Education Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 141A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/141A) (teachers to whom misconduct regime applies)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/141A)—
  - (i) in the words before [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/141A), after “is” insert “or has (at any time) been”;
  - (ii) after [paragraph (ba)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/141A) insert—

    > - (bb) an independent educational institution in England that is not a school,
    > - (bc) an institution in England within the further education sector,
    > - (bd) a special post-16 institution in England,
    > - (be) an independent training provider,
    > - (bf) an online education provider (see section 141AA),

    ;
- (b) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/141A), after the definition of “children’s home” insert—

  > - “**independent educational institution**” has the same meaning as in [Chapter 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/part/4/chapter/1) of [Part 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/part/4) of the [Education and Skills Act 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/25/contents);
  > - “**independent training provider**” has the same meaning as in [sections 1 to 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/21/section/1) of the [Skills and Post-16 Education Act 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/21/contents) (see section 4 of that Act);
  > - “**special post-16 institution**” has the same meaning as in [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6/part/3) of the [Children and Families Act 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6/contents) (see section 83 of that Act);

  .

(3) After [section 141A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/141A) insert—

> **141AA. Meaning of “online education provider”**
>
> (1) An online education provider is an institution that meets the following conditions.
>
> (2) The first condition is that it is—
>
> - (a) a company registered under the [Companies Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46/contents) which has its registered office for the purposes of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46/contents) in England, or
> - (b) a charity registered in accordance with [section 30](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/25/section/30) of the [Charities Act 2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/25/contents) which has its address in England for the purposes of registration by the Charity Commission.
>
> (3) The second condition is that it provides education to at least one student who lives in England and who—
>
> - (a) is of compulsory school age,
> - (b) is over compulsory school age but is under the age of 19, or
> - (c) is aged 19 or over and has an EHC plan that specifies that the student should be in full-time education.
>
> (4) The third condition is that at least one of the students mentioned in subsection (3) receives all or the majority of their education from the institution.
>
> (5) The fourth condition is that it is set up to deliver all or the majority of the education that it provides online.
>
> (6) The Secretary of State may by regulations amend this section so as to add a new condition or remove or change a condition for the time being specified.

(4) In [section 141B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/141B) (investigation of disciplinary cases by the Secretary of State)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/141B)—

  - (ii) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/141B), for “may be” substitute “may (at any time) have been”;

  > (3A) For the purposes of subsection (1)(a) or (b) it is irrelevant whether the conduct occurred, or the offence was committed, at a time when the person was employed or engaged to carry out teaching work or at some other time.

(5) In [section 141D](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/141D)[(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/141D) (supply of information following dismissal, resignation etc: definitions)—

- (c) after the definition of “services” insert—
