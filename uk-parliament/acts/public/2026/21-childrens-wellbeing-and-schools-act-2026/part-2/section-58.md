# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 58

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Repeal of duty to make Academy order in relation to school causing concern[^key-a08c861608a106b99af970744338826f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The [Academies Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/4) (Academy orders)—

- (a) omit [subsection (A1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/4);
- (b) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/4)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/4), omit “other than by virtue of section 61 or 62 of EIA 2006”.

(3) In [section 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/5)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/5) (consultation about conversion: schools not eligible for intervention), for “section 4(A1) or (1)(b)” substitute “section 4(1)(b)”.

(4) Omit [section 5A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/5A) (consultation about identity of Academy sponsor).

(5) In [section 5B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/5B)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/5B) (duty to facilitate conversion), for “section 4(A1) or (1)(b)” substitute “section 4(1)(b)”.

(6) In [section 5C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/5C)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/5C) (power to give directions to do with conversion), for “section 4(A1) or (1)(b)” substitute “section 4(1)(b)”.

(7) In [section 5D](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/5D) (power to revoke Academy orders)—

- (a) in the heading, for “section 4(A1) or (1)(b)” substitute “section 4(1)(b)”;
- (b) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/5D), for “section 4(A1) or (1)(b)” substitute “section 4(1)(b)”.

(8) In the [Education and Adoption Act 2016](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/6/contents), omit [sections 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/6/section/7) and [9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/6/section/9).

(9) The amendments made by this section are to be disregarded in a case where, immediately before the day on which this section comes into force—

- (a) an order under [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/4)[(A1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/4) of the [Academies Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/contents) has effect in respect of a school, and
- (b) the school has not been converted into an Academy in pursuance of the order (and for this purpose “**converted into an Academy**” is to be read in accordance with [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/4)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/4) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/contents)).
