# Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 55

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 55. Academy schools: educational provision for improving behaviour[^c-e667fa802fe6003de688bf95e57a07b8][^c-1789f93067b0f5c17e7272cbf9f039f1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the [Education Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents), in [section 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) (power of governing body in England: educational provision for improving behaviour), after [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) insert—

> (5) Regulations may make provision for this section and regulations made under it to apply, with prescribed modifications, in relation to Academy schools.

(2) In the [Education Act 1996](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/contents), in [section 444ZA](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/444ZA) (application of section 444 to alternative educational provision), in [subsection (1D)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/444ZA)—

- (a) in the words before paragraph (a), for “or a pupil referral unit” substitute “, a pupil referral unit or an Academy”;
- (b) after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/444ZA) insert—

  > For provision about the application of [sections 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) and [51A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/51A) of the [Education Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) in relation to Academies, see [sections 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A)(5) and [51A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/51A)[(12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/51A) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents).

(3) The provision that may be made in relation to Academy schools by the first regulations under [section 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A)(5) of the [Education Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) includes provision in relation to any relevant requirement imposed before those regulations come into force.

(4) In relation to an Academy school, the requirement referred to in [section 444ZA](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/444ZA)[(1B)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/444ZA) of the [Education Act 1996](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/contents) includes a reference to a relevant requirement imposed before the first regulations under [section 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A)(5) of the [Education Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) come into force.

(5) In subsections (3) and (4) “**relevant requirement**”, in relation to an Academy school, means a requirement imposed by the proprietor of the school on a registered pupil to attend any place outside the school premises for the purpose of receiving educational provision which is intended to improve the behaviour of the pupil.
