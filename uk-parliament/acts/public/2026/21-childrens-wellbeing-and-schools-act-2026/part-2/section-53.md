## 53. School teachers’ qualifications and induction[^key-0e6ff190ad908a394b5d99eaf83cde89]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The [Education Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 133](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/133) (requirement to be qualified), in [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/133)—

- (a) omit “or” after [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/133);
- (b) after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/133) insert

  > , or
  >
  > - (c) Academies of a description specified by the Secretary of State in regulations under this section.

(3) In [section 135A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/135A)[(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/135A) (requirement to serve induction period: teachers in England), at the end of the definition of “relevant school” insert “or Academies of a description specified by the Secretary of State in regulations under this section.”
