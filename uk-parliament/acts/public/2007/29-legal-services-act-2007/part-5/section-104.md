# Legal Services Act 2007, section 104

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 104. Prevention of regulatory conflict: accounts rules[^c-7033359ad0e82f8b562dcd19ef799800]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a licensed body carries on an activity through a solicitor, the rules made under paragraph 20 of Schedule 11 apply instead of those made under sections 32 to 34 of the Solicitors Act 1974 (c. 47).

(2) Where a licensed body carries on an activity through a licensed conveyancer or licensed CLC practitioner[^c-511304236388aaf8d7a9e7610a4f431c], the rules made under paragraph 20 of Schedule 11 apply instead of those made under sections 22 and 23 of the Administration of Justice Act 1985 (c. 61).

(3)[^c-0a7e07f651ad4c410037e40ebee8fdfd] In this section “ **licensed CLC practitioner** ” means a person, other than a licensed conveyancer, who holds a licence under section 53 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990.
