# Legal Services Act 2007, section 86

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 86. Modification of licence

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-8aa4dbed70beaa6031d9d420180f99c6] A licensing authority may modify the terms of a licence granted by it—

- (a) if the licensed body applies to the licensing authority, in accordance with its licensing rules, for it to do so;
- (b) in such other circumstances as may be specified in its licensing rules.

(2) If a licensed body is a body to which section 106 applies, the licensing authority may modify the terms of its licence in accordance with sections 106 and 107.

(3)[^c-8aa4dbed70beaa6031d9d420180f99c6] A licensing authority modifies the terms of a licensed body's licence by giving the licensed body notice in writing of the modifications; and the modifications have effect from the time the licensing authority gives the licensed body the notice or such later time as may be specified in the notice.

(4)[^c-8aa4dbed70beaa6031d9d420180f99c6] The licensing authority's power under this section is subject to—

- (a) section 85(3) and (4), and
- (b) licensing rules made under paragraph 6 of Schedule 11.
