# Legal Services Act 2007, section 128

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 128. Parties[^c20330391]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-301603a3c8a8e5ce1ba15e31367f1cce] The respondent is within this section if, at the relevant time, the respondent was an authorised person in relation to an activity which was a reserved legal activity (whether or not the act or omission relates to a reserved legal activity).

(2)[^c-301603a3c8a8e5ce1ba15e31367f1cce] The complainant (“C”) is within this section if C—

- (a) meets the first and second conditions, and
- (b) is not excluded by subsection (5).

(3) The first condition is that C is—

- (a)[^c-301603a3c8a8e5ce1ba15e31367f1cce] an individual, or
- (b) a person (other than an individual) of a description prescribed by order made by the Lord Chancellor in accordance with a recommendation made under section 130.

(4) The second condition is that—

- (a)[^c-301603a3c8a8e5ce1ba15e31367f1cce] the services to which the complaint relates were provided by the respondent to C;
- (b)[^c-301603a3c8a8e5ce1ba15e31367f1cce] the services to which the complaint relates were provided by the respondent to an authorised person who procured them on C's behalf;
- (c)[^c-301603a3c8a8e5ce1ba15e31367f1cce] the services to which the complaint relates were provided by the respondent—
  - (i) in the respondent's capacity as a personal representative or trustee, or
  - (ii) to a person acting as a personal representative or trustee,

  and C is a beneficiary of the estate or trust in question; or
- (d) C satisfies such other conditions, in relation to the services to which the complaint relates, as may be prescribed by order made by the Lord Chancellor in accordance with a recommendation made under section 130.

(5) C is excluded if, at the relevant time—

- (a)[^c-301603a3c8a8e5ce1ba15e31367f1cce] C was an authorised person in relation to an activity which was a reserved legal activity and the services to which the complaint relates were procured by C on behalf of another person,
- (b)[^c-301603a3c8a8e5ce1ba15e31367f1cce] C was a public body or was acting on behalf of such a body in relation to the services to which the complaint relates, or
- (c) C was a person prescribed, or of a description prescribed, as excluded by order made by the Lord Chancellor in accordance with a recommendation made under section 130.

(6)[^c-301603a3c8a8e5ce1ba15e31367f1cce] In subsection (4)(b) “**authorised person**” means an authorised person in relation to any activity which is a reserved legal activity.

(7)[^c-301603a3c8a8e5ce1ba15e31367f1cce] In this section—

- “**public body**” means any government department, local authority or other body constituted for purposes of the public services, local government or the administration of justice;
- “**relevant time**”, in relation to a complaint, means the time when the act or omission to which the complaint relates took place.
