# Legal Services Act 2007, section 159

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 159. Legal Services Complaints Commissioner and Legal Services Ombudsman

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-5604bb0fdb0d0b67b226cb853a93d770][^key-ab0a373752d50d1596817aced1d58c34] The offices of Legal Services Complaints Commissioner and Legal Services Ombudsman are abolished.

(2) Accordingly—

- (a)[^key-b44b1af7ca54888004786a1759262df0] sections 51 and 52 of, and Schedule 8 to, the Access to Justice Act 1999 (c. 22), and
- (b)[^key-5604bb0fdb0d0b67b226cb853a93d770][^key-480bda0b452c37e5f5da5a57b6471b76][^key-ab0a373752d50d1596817aced1d58c34] sections 21 to 26 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (c. 41),

are repealed.
