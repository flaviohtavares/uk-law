# Legal Services Act 2007, section 181

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 181. Unqualified person not to pretend to be a barrister

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It is an offence for a person who is not a barrister—

- (a) wilfully to pretend to be a barrister, or
- (b) with the intention of implying falsely that that person is a barrister to take or use any name, title or description.

(2) A person who is guilty of an offence under subsection (1) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-bcad4fb1ebf3ac012f234af88d67c814] or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both), and
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or a fine (or both).

(3) In relation to an offence under subsection (1) committed before 2 May 2022[^c-56c331e0fa5aca35a4df6fc84974764d], the reference in subsection (2)(a) to the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-c9d6cd9457f8ce9d08f73a2bd0af384f] is to be read as a reference to 6 months.
