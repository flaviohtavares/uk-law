# Legal Services Act 2007, section 180

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 180. Functions of the Tribunal[^c20062331]

*Extent: E+W*

Sections 69 and 70 (modification of functions of approved regulators) apply in relation to the Tribunal as they apply in relation to an approved regulator, but as if—

- (a) for section 69(3) (purpose for which modifying order may be made) there were substituted—

  > (3) The Board may make a recommendation under this section only with a view to an order being made which enables the Tribunal to carry out its role more effectively or efficiently.

  , and
- (b) subsections (4), (5) and (7) of that section were omitted.
