## 8. Limited class of applicants for making of orders[^c-dc9b269878ca9000f46b2f218b2a27da]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1)[^c-59e8a745263e4ce91a1f63610cb95c9f][^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f] A serious crime prevention order may be made only on an application by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]

- (a) in the case of an order in England and Wales— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
  - (i) the Director of Public Prosecutions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f6efd710357b9ef05e36bb3de89504a5][^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .; [^c-142cdc6777af1baede8830d98f33d3c8]...
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f] the Director of the Serious Fraud Office; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f] or[^c-99ce9aabba408d3434247585b23ef837] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f] subject to subsection (2), a chief officer of police; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-70fb1ad0ecfa1b52c5582718c94673f3][^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f] in the case of an order in Scotland *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f] —[^c-b767d394c5f4dbaae64905cc5fc3d07e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
  - (i) the Lord Advocate; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f] subject to subsection (2), the chief officer of police; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
- (b) in the case of an order in Northern Ireland *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f] —[^c-c566ab25e89ed29784e38f09df96573f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
  - (i) the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f] subject to subsection (2), the chief officer of police. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]

(2)[^c-b1c2b8b967387f04042da24d60fa26b4] A chief officer of police may make an application for a serious crime prevention order only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]

- (a) it is an application for an order under section 1 that is terrorism-related (see section 8A); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
- (b) the chief officer has consulted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
  - (i) the Director of Public Prosecutions, in the case of an order in England and Wales; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f] the Lord Advocate, in the case of an order in Scotland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f] the Director for Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland, in the case of an order in Northern Ireland; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
- (c) in the case of an application by the chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland, it is made to the Court of Session (and not to the sheriff). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]

(3) In this section “chief officer of police”— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]

- (a) in relation to England and Wales, means the chief officer of police of a police force in England and Wales; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
- (b) in relation to Scotland, means the chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]
- (c) in relation to Northern Ireland, means the Chief Constable of the Police Service of Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80494b5e6d6aff7642f1c3656557f6f]

(1) A serious crime prevention order may be made by the High Court in England and Wales—

- (a) only on an application by—
  - (i) the Director of Public Prosecutions,
  - (ii) the Director of the Serious Fraud Office,
  - (iii) the Director General of the National Crime Agency,
  - (iv) the Commissioners for His Majesty’s Revenue and Customs,
  - (v) the chief officer of police of a police force in England and Wales,
  - (vi) the Chief Constable of the British Transport Police Force, or
  - (vii) the Chief Constable of the Ministry of Defence Police, and
- (b) in the case of an application by a person listed in paragraph (a)(iii) to (vii), only if the person has consulted the Director of Public Prosecutions.

(2) A serious crime prevention order may be made by the Crown Court in England and Wales—

- (a) only on an application by—
  - (i) the Director of Public Prosecutions,
  - (ii) the Director of the Serious Fraud Office, or
  - (iii) the chief officer of police of a police force in England and Wales, and
- (b) in the case of an application by the chief officer of police of such a police force, only if—
  - (i) it is an application for an order under section 19 or 19A that is terrorism-related (see section 8A), and
  - (ii) the chief officer has consulted the Director of Public Prosecutions.

(3) A serious crime prevention order may be made by a court or sheriff in Scotland—

- (a) only on an application by—
  - (i) the Lord Advocate, or
  - (ii) the chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland, and
- (b) in the case of an application by the chief constable, only if—
  - (i) it is an application for an order under section 1 that is terrorism-related (see section 8A),
  - (ii) the chief constable has consulted the Lord Advocate, and
  - (iii) it is an application made to the Court of Session (and not to the sheriff).

(4) A serious crime prevention order may be made by a court in Northern Ireland—

- (a) only on an application by—
  - (i) the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland, or
  - (ii) the Chief Constable of the Police Service of Northern Ireland, and
- (b) in the case of an application by the Chief Constable, only if—
  - (i) it is an application for an order under section 1 that is terrorism-related (see section 8A), and
  - (ii) the Chief Constable has consulted the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland.
