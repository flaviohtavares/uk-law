# Serious Crime Act 2007, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (182).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/27

## 68. Disclosure of information to prevent fraud

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-ecf9e318b077f0bc14ce4c97d6e66715] A public authority may, for the purposes of preventing fraud or a particular kind of fraud, disclose information as a member of a specified anti-fraud organisation or otherwise in accordance with any arrangements made by such an organisation.

(2)[^c-ecf9e318b077f0bc14ce4c97d6e66715] The information—

- (a) may be information of any kind; and
- (b) may be disclosed to the specified anti-fraud organisation, any members of it or any other person to whom disclosure is permitted by the arrangements concerned.

(3)[^c-ecf9e318b077f0bc14ce4c97d6e66715] Disclosure under this section does not breach—

- (a) any obligation of confidence owed by the public authority disclosing the information; or
- (b) any other restriction on the disclosure of information (however imposed).

(4)[^c-ecf9e318b077f0bc14ce4c97d6e66715] But nothing in this section authorises any disclosure of information which—

- (a) contravenes the data protection legislation[^c-39ff4cf98c6920905d8878fa825eaef0]; or
- (b) is prohibited by any of Parts 1 to 7 or Chapter 1 of Part 9 of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016[^c-5912e050e08071dcbb04f795eb7aa264].

(5)[^c-141798b798aa27363dfafa98455ef9c0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c-dd053e2945581b7a637c2605e6b423c6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-ecf9e318b077f0bc14ce4c97d6e66715] This section does not limit the circumstances in which information may be disclosed apart from this section.

(8)[^c-60e584f16a900d8834e29eeddfef7f9f] In this section—

- “**an anti-fraud organisation**” means any unincorporated association, body corporate or other person which enables or facilitates any sharing of information to prevent fraud or a particular kind of fraud or which has any of these functions as its purpose or one of its purposes;
- “[^c-94d48f2d3b5742b5148c5c314e9a568a] **the data protection legislation**” has the same meaning as in the Data Protection Act 2018 (see section 3 of that Act).
- “**information**” includes documents;
- “**public authority**” means any public authority within the meaning of section 6 of the Human Rights Act 1998 (c. 42) (acts of public authorities); and
- “**specified**” means specified by an order made by the Secretary of State.
