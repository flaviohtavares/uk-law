## 67. Course of conduct[^c-ca42d1a2e31a9431b6cc46276e954308][^c-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2][^c-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

A reference in this Part to an act includes a reference to a course of conduct, and a reference to doing an act is to be read accordingly.
