# Serious Crime Act 2007, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (182).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Prosecution of offences triable by reason of Schedule 4[^c-42a81e21ffc10023abda5c9be5b18e8c][^c-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2][^c-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1)[^M_F_9a6b91cf-40bb-4044-f2d2-61e97cbfd529] No proceedings for an offence triable by reason of any provision of Schedule 4 may be instituted—

- (a) in England and Wales, except by, or with the consent of, the Attorney General; or
- (b) in Northern Ireland, except by, or with the consent of, the Advocate General for Northern Ireland.

(2)[^c-3f1f22b16de06c9b74cde5c3f3741dc0] Subsection (1) does not apply to an offence under this Part to which section 10 of the Bribery Act 2010 applies by virtue of section 54(1) and (2) below (encouraging or assisting bribery).
