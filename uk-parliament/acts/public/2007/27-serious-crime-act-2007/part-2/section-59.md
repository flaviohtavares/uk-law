## 59. Abolition of common law replaced by this Part[^key-67d5940ba088c1a81e92f6c4e38f372f][^key-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2][^key-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2]

The common law offence of inciting the commission of another offence is abolished.
