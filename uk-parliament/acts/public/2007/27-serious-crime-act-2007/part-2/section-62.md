# Serious Crime Act 2007, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (182).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. No individual liability in respect of corporate manslaughter[^c-abc0582ca1fd6573a8335be409dc972b][^c-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2][^c-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

In section 18 of the Corporate Manslaughter and Corporate Homicide Act 2007 (c. 19) (no individual liability for offences under that Act) after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) An individual cannot be guilty of an offence under Part 2 of the Serious Crime Act 2007 (encouraging or assisting crime) by reference to an offence of corporate manslaughter.
