## 64. Encouraging or assisting the commission of an offence[^key-8c56b0e96544133c93659996dfa27938][^key-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2][^key-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2]

A reference in this Part to encouraging or assisting the commission of an offence is to be read in accordance with section 47.
