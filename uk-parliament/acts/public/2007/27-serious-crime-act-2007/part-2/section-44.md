# Serious Crime Act 2007, section 44

## 44. Intentionally encouraging or assisting an offence[^key-f91e52cab11b7a5a6f6063359a5c63ec][^key-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2][^key-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) A person commits an offence if—

- (a) he does an act capable of encouraging or assisting the commission of an offence; and
- (b) he intends to encourage or assist its commission.

(2) But he is not to be taken to have intended to encourage or assist the commission of an offence merely because such encouragement or assistance was a foreseeable consequence of his act.
