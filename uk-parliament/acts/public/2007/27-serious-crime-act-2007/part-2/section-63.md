# Serious Crime Act 2007, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (182).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Consequential amendments: Part 2[^c-42af3f8f404d5c294aabdd22df55a31e][^c-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2][^c-8fac712985455041598b9ce15117ddd2]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) In the provisions listed in Part 1 of Schedule 6, any reference however expressed to (or to conduct amounting to) the offence abolished by section 59 has effect as a reference to (or to conduct amounting to) the offences under this Part.

(2) Part 2 of Schedule 6 contains other minor and consequential amendments.

(3) The Secretary of State may by order amend Part 1 of Schedule 6 as it applies to England and Wales[^c-fde91fc5ae6fa46a32571160e438c584] by adding or removing a provision.

(4)[^c-3ff3809a398c11ab8a8b929bec1ca793] The Department of Justice in Northern Ireland may by order amend Part 1 of Schedule 6 as it applies to Northern Ireland by adding or removing a provision.
