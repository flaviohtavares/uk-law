# Justice and Security (Northern Ireland) Act 2007, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-06-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/6

## 47. Altering title of resident magistrate

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 102 of the Courts Act 2003 (c. 39) (power to alter judicial titles: Northern Ireland) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)(a), after “county courts” insert “ or magistrates' courts ”.

(3) In subsection (2)—

- (a) after the entry for “Deputy judge of the county court” insert— “ Deputy resident magistrate ”;
- (b) after the entry for “Presiding judge for the county courts” insert— “ Presiding resident magistrate ”;
- (c) at the end of the list insert— “ Resident magistrate ”.
