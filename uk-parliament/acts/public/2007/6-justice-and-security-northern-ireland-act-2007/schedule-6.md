# Justice and Security (Northern Ireland) Act 2007, SCHEDULE 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-06-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/6

## SCHEDULE 6 — Private Security Industry: Interim Arrangements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 48

### Interpretation

**1.** In this Schedule “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2085469]**security services *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2085469]**” means the services of one or more individuals as security guards (whether or not provided together with other services relating to the protection of property or persons).[^c2085469] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2085469]

**2.** In this Schedule “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083884]**licence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083884]**” means a licence under this Schedule.[^c2083884] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083884]

**3.** In this Schedule “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083885]**proscribed organisation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083885]**” has the meaning given by section 3 of the Terrorism Act 2000 (c. 11).[^c2083885] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083885]

### Unlicensed services: offences

**4.** A person commits an offence if he provides or offers to provide security services for reward unless he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083886]

- (a) holds a licence, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083886]
- (b) acts on behalf of someone who holds a licence.[^c2083886] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083886]

**5.** (1)[^c2083887] A person commits an offence if he publishes or causes to be published an advertisement for the provision for reward of security services by a person who does not hold a licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083887]

(2) It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under this paragraph to prove— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083887]

- (a) that his business is (or includes) publishing advertisements or arranging for their publication, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083887]
- (b) that he received the advertisement for publication in the ordinary course of business, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083887]
- (c) that he reasonably believed that the person mentioned in the advertisement as the provider of security services held a licence.[^c2083887] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083887]

**6.** (1)[^c2083888] A person commits an offence if he pays money, in respect of the provision of security services, to a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083888]

- (a) does not hold a licence, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083888]
- (b) is not acting on behalf of someone who holds a licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083888]

(2) It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under this paragraph to prove that he reasonably believed that the person to whom he paid the money— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083888]

- (a) held a licence, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083888]
- (b) was acting on behalf of someone who held a licence.[^c2083888] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083888]

**7.** (1)[^c2083889] A person guilty of an offence under paragraph 4 or 5 shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083889]

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years, to a fine or to both, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083889]
- (b) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083889]

(2) A person guilty of an offence under paragraph 6 is liable on summary conviction to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083889]

- (a) imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083889]
- (b) a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083889]
- (c) both.[^c2083889] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083889]

### Application for licence

**8.** (1)[^c2083890] An application for a licence shall be made to the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]

- (a) in such manner and form as he may specify, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]
- (b) accompanied by such information as he may specify. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]

(2) The Secretary of State may specify information only if it concerns— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]

- (a) the applicant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]
- (b) a business involving the provision of security services for reward which is, was or is proposed to be carried on by the applicant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]
- (c) a person whom the applicant employs or proposes to employ as a security guard, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]
- (d) a partner or proposed partner of the applicant (where the applicant is an individual), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]
- (e) a member or proposed member of the applicant (where the applicant is a partnership), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]
- (f) an officer or proposed officer of the applicant (where the applicant is a body corporate). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]

(3) A person commits an offence if in connection with an application for a licence he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]

- (a) makes a statement which he knows to be false or misleading in a material particular, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]
- (b) recklessly makes a statement which is false or misleading in a material particular. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]

(4) A person guilty of an offence under sub-paragraph (3) shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, to a fine or to both, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]
- (b) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]

(5) For the purposes of this paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]

- (a) a reference to employment or proposed employment by an applicant for a licence shall, where the applicant is a partnership or a member of a partnership, be construed as a reference to employment or proposed employment by the partnership or by any of its partners, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]
- (b) “**officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]**” includes a director, manager or secretary, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]
- (c) a person in accordance with whose directions or instructions the directors of a body corporate are accustomed to act shall be treated as an officer of that body, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]
- (d) the reference to directions or instructions in paragraph (c) does not include a reference to advice given in a professional capacity.[^c2083890] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083890]

### Issue of licence

**9.** (1)[^c2083891] The Secretary of State shall grant an application for a licence unless satisfied that any of Conditions 1 to 4 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]

(2) Condition 1 for the refusal of a licence is that a proscribed organisation, or an organisation which appears to the Secretary of State to be closely associated with a proscribed organisation, would be likely to benefit from the licence (whether or not a condition were imposed under paragraph 10). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]

(3) Condition 2 for the refusal of a licence is that there are reasonable grounds to suspect that any of the following is engaged in criminal activity— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]

- (a) a business involving the provision for reward of security services which is, was or is proposed to be carried on by the applicant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]
- (b) a person whom the applicant employs or proposes to employ as a security guard, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]
- (c) a partner or proposed partner of the applicant (where the applicant is an individual), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]
- (d) a member or proposed member of the applicant (where the applicant is a partnership), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]
- (e) an officer or proposed officer of the applicant (where the applicant is a body corporate). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]

(4) Condition 3 for the refusal of a licence is that the applicant has persistently failed to comply with the requirements of this Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]

(5) Condition 4 for the refusal of a licence is that the applicant has failed to comply with a condition imposed under paragraph 10. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]

(6) In Condition 1 a reference to a benefit is a reference to any benefit— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]

- (a) whether direct or indirect, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]
- (b) whether financial or not. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]

(7) Paragraph 8(5) shall have effect for the purposes of Condition 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]

(8) In Condition 3 the reference to this Schedule includes a reference to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]

- (a) Part V of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991 (c. 24) (private security services), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]
- (b) Part V of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1996 (c. 22) (private security services), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]
- (c) Schedule 13 to the Terrorism Act 2000 (c. 11) (Northern Ireland: private security services).[^c2083891] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083891]

### Conditions of licence

**10.** (1)[^c2083892] The Secretary of State may on granting a licence impose a condition if satisfied that it is necessary in order to prevent any of the persons listed in sub-paragraph (2) from benefiting from the licence (within the meaning of paragraph 9(6)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083892]

(2) Those persons are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083892]

- (a) a proscribed organisation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083892]
- (b) an organisation which appears to the Secretary of State to be closely associated with a proscribed organisation, from benefiting from the licence, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083892]
- (c) a person who engages in criminal activity.[^c2083892] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083892]

### Refusal of licence

**11.** If the Secretary of State refuses an application for a licence he shall notify the applicant.[^c2083893] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083893]

### Duration of licence

**12.** (1)[^c2083894] A licence— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083894]

- (a) shall come into force at the beginning of the day on which it is issued, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083894]
- (b) subject to sub-paragraph (2), shall expire at the end of the period of 12 months beginning with that day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083894]

(2) Where a licence is issued to a person who already holds a licence, the new licence shall expire at the end of the period of 12 months beginning with the day after the day on which the current licence expires. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083894]

(3) The Secretary of State may by order substitute a period exceeding 12 months for the period for the time being specified in sub-paragraphs (1)(b) and (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083894]

(4) An order under sub-paragraph (3)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083894]

- (a) may include incidental or transitional provision, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083894]
- (b) shall be made by statutory instrument, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083894]
- (c) shall be laid before Parliament.[^c2083894] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083894]

### Revocation of licence

**13.** (1)[^c2083895] The Secretary of State may revoke a licence if satisfied that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083895]

- (a) a proscribed organisation, or an organisation which appears to the Secretary of State to be closely associated with a proscribed organisation, would be likely to benefit from the licence remaining in force, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083895]
- (b) there are reasonable grounds to suspect that any of the persons listed in paragraph 9(3) (taking a reference to the applicant as a reference to the holder of the licence) is engaged in criminal activity, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083895]
- (c) the holder of the licence has persistently failed to comply with the requirements of this Schedule, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083895]
- (d) the holder of the licence has failed to comply with a condition imposed under paragraph 10. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083895]

(2) The Secretary of State shall not revoke a licence unless the holder— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083895]

- (a) has been notified of the Secretary of State's intention to revoke the licence, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083895]
- (b) has been given a reasonable opportunity to make representations to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083895]

(3) If the Secretary of State revokes a licence he shall notify the holder immediately. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083895]

(4) Paragraph 9(6) and (8) shall apply for the purposes of this paragraph.[^c2083895] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083895]

### Appeal

**14.** The applicant for a licence may appeal to the High Court if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083896]

- (a) the application is refused, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083896]
- (b) a condition is imposed on the grant of a licence, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083896]
- (c) the licence is revoked.[^c2083896] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083896]

**15.** (1)[^c2083897] Where an appeal is brought under paragraph 14, the Secretary of State may issue a certificate that the decision to which the appeal relates— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]

- (a) was taken for a purpose specified in sub-paragraph (2), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]
- (b) was justified by that purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]

(2) Those purposes are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]

- (a) preventing benefit from accruing to an organisation which was proscribed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]
- (b) preventing benefit from accruing to an organisation which appeared to the Secretary of State to be closely associated with an organisation which was proscribed, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]
- (c) preventing benefit from accruing to a person who was engaged in criminal activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]

(3) If he intends to rely on a certificate under this paragraph the Secretary of State shall notify the appellant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]

(4) Where the appellant is notified of the Secretary of State's intention to rely on a certificate under this paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]

- (a) he may appeal against the certificate to the Tribunal established under section 91 of the Northern Ireland Act 1998 (c. 47), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]
- (b) sections 90(3) and (4), 91(2) to (9) and 92 of that Act (effect of appeal, procedure, and further appeal) shall apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]

(5) Rules made under section 91 or 92 of that Act which are in force immediately before this paragraph comes into force shall have effect in relation to a certificate under this paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]

- (a) with any necessary modifications, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]
- (b) subject to any later rules made by virtue of sub-paragraph (4)(b).[^c2083897] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083897]

### Change of personnel

**16.** Paragraphs 17 and 18 apply to a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083899]

- (a) holds a licence, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083899]
- (b) has made an application for a licence which has not yet been determined.[^c2083899] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083899]

**17.** (1)[^c2083903] If a person to whom this paragraph applies proposes to employ a security guard about whom information was not given under paragraph 8, he shall give the Secretary of State such information about the security guard as the Secretary of State may specify. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083903]

(2) The information shall be given not less than 14 days before the employment is to begin. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083903]

(3) For the purposes of this paragraph the provisions of paragraph 8(5) shall have effect in relation to a holder of or an applicant for a licence as they have effect for the purposes of paragraph 8 in relation to an applicant.[^c2083903] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083903]

**18.** (1)[^c2083904] A person to whom this paragraph applies shall give the Secretary of State such information about a relevant change of personnel as the Secretary of State may specify. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083904]

(2) The information shall be given— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083904]

- (a) not less than 14 days before the change, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083904]
- (b) if that is not reasonably practicable, as soon as is reasonably practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083904]

(3) A relevant change of personnel is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083904]

- (a) where the application for the licence was made by a partnership or a member of a partnership, a change in the members of the partnership, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083904]
- (b) where the application for the licence was made by a body corporate, a change in the officers of the body (within the meaning of paragraph 8). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083904]

(4) But a change of personnel is not relevant if it was mentioned in the information given under paragraph 8.[^c2083904] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083904]

**19.** (1)[^c2083905] A person commits an offence if he fails to comply with paragraph 17 or 18. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083905]

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this paragraph shall be liable on summary conviction to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083905]

- (a) imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083905]
- (b) a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083905]
- (c) both.[^c2083905] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083905]

### Records

**20.** (1)[^c2083906] A constable may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]

- (a) enter premises where a business involving the provision of security services is being carried on, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]
- (b) require records kept there of a person employed as a security guard to be produced for the constable's inspection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]

(2) A constable exercising the power under this paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]

- (a) shall identify himself to a person appearing to be in charge of the premises, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]
- (b) if the constable is not in uniform, shall produce to that person documentary evidence that he is a constable, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]
- (c) may use reasonable force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]

(3) A person commits an offence if he fails to comply with a requirement imposed under sub-paragraph (1)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]

(4) But it is a defence for a person charged with an offence under sub-paragraph (3) to show that he had a reasonable excuse for his failure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]

(5) A person guilty of an offence under sub-paragraph (3) shall be liable on summary conviction to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]

- (a) imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]
- (b) a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]
- (c) both.[^c2083906] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083906]

**21.** (1)[^c2083907] A person who provides for reward security services commits an offence if he makes or keeps a record of a person employed by him as a security guard which he knows to be false or misleading in a material particular. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083907]

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this paragraph shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083907]

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, to a fine or to both, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083907]
- (b) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both.[^c2083907] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083907]

### Offence: body corporate

**22.** (1)[^c2083911] This paragraph applies where an offence under this Schedule committed by a body corporate is proved— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083911]

- (a) to have been committed with the consent or connivance of an officer of the body corporate, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083911]
- (b) to be attributable to neglect on the part of an officer of the body corporate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083911]

(2) The officer, as well as the body corporate, shall be guilty of the offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083911]

(3) In this paragraph “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083911]**officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083911]**” includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083911]

- (a) a director, manager or secretary, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083911]
- (b) a person purporting to act as a director, manager or secretary, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083911]
- (c) a member of a body corporate the affairs of which are managed by its members.[^c2083911] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083911]

### Notice

**23.** (1)[^c2083912] A notice under this Schedule must be in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]

(2) Information required to be given to the Secretary of State under this Schedule— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]

- (a) must be in writing, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]
- (b) may be sent to him by post. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]

(3) The Secretary of State may serve a notice under this Schedule on an individual— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]

- (a) by delivering it to him, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]
- (b) by sending it by post addressed to him at his usual or last-known place of residence or business, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]
- (c) by leaving it for him there. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]

(4) The Secretary of State may serve a notice under this Schedule on a partnership— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]

- (a) by sending it by post to a partner, or to a person having the control or management of the partnership business, at the principal office of the partnership, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]
- (b) by addressing it to a partner or to a person mentioned in paragraph (a) and leaving it at that office. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]

(5) The Secretary of State may serve a notice under this Schedule on a body corporate— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]

- (a) by sending it by post to the secretary or clerk of the body at its registered or principal office, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]
- (b) by addressing it to the secretary or clerk of the body and leaving it at that office. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]

(6) The Secretary of State may serve a notice under this Schedule on any person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]

- (a) by delivering it to his solicitor, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]
- (b) by sending it by post to his solicitor at his solicitor's office, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]
- (c) by leaving it for his solicitor there. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2083912]
