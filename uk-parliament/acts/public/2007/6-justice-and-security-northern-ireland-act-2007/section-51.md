# Justice and Security (Northern Ireland) Act 2007, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-06-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Financial provisions

*Extent: N.I.*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) expenditure of the Secretary of State in connection with this Act, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in sums payable out of money provided by Parliament under another enactment.
