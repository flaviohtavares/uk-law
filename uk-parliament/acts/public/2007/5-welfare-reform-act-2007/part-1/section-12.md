# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 12. Work-focused interviews[^c-2f0b0e97cc51e8ffa803c19fc451cb9d][^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323][^c-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1][^c-e07ff833df3a12a5801c6eea3b2c5c40] Regulations may make provision for or in connection with imposing on a person who is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) entitled to an employment and support allowance, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) not a member of the support group, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323] or a lone parent of a child under the age of one[^c-a06db2c2465d43d73331183d74aaf7fb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

a requirement to take part in one or more work-focused interviews as a condition of continuing to be entitled to the full amount payable to him in respect of the allowance apart from the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(2) Regulations under this section may, in particular, make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] prescribing circumstances in which such a person is subject to a requirement to take part in one or more work-focused interviews; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] for notifying such a person of any such requirement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] prescribing the work-focused interviews in which a person who is subject to such a requirement is required to take part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (d)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] for determining, in relation to work-focused interviews under the regulations, when and how the interview is to be conducted and, if it is to be conducted face to face, where it is to take place; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (e)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] for notifying persons who are required under the regulations to take part in a work-focused interview of what is determined in respect of the matters mentioned in paragraph (d); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (f)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] prescribing circumstances in which a person who is a party to a work-focused interview under the regulations is to be regarded as having, or not having, taken part in it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (g)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] for securing that the appropriate consequence follows if a person who is required under the regulations to take part in a work-focused interview— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
  - (i) fails to take part in the interview, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323] does not, within a prescribed period, show that he had good cause for that failure; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (h)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] prescribing matters which are, or are not, to be taken into account in determining whether a person had good cause for any failure to comply with the regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (i)[^c-4a8a99687f25fb094be30199b243b1a0] prescribing circumstances in which a person is, or is not, to be regarded as having good cause for any such failure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(3)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] For the purposes of subsection (2)(g), the appropriate consequence of a failure falling within that provision is that the amount payable to the person in question in respect of an employment and support allowance is reduced in accordance with regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(4)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] Regulations under subsection (3) may, in particular, make provision for determining— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) the amount by which an allowance is to be reduced, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) when the reduction is to start, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) how long it is to continue, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

and may include provision prescribing circumstances in which the amount of the reduction is to be nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(5)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] Regulations under this section shall include provision for a requirement to take part in one or more work-focused interviews to cease to have effect if the person subject to the requirement becomes a member of the support group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(6)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] Regulations under this section may include provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) that in such circumstances as the regulations may prescribe a requirement to take part in a work-focused interview that would otherwise apply to a person by virtue of such regulations is not to apply, or is to be treated as not having applied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) that in such circumstances as the regulations may prescribe such a requirement is not to apply until a prescribed time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) that in such circumstances as the regulations may prescribe matters mentioned in subsection (2)(d) may be redetermined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(7)[^c-d34b1d236eae9c5023e1071de694e092][^c-8445ca694c9a664ab8ba0785632387b1] In this section, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]**work-focused interview *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]**” means an interview by the Secretary of State conducted for such purposes connected with getting the person interviewed into work, or keeping him in work, as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
