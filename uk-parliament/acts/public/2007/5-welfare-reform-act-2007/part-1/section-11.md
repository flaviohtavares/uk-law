## 11. Work-focused health-related assessments (affected by repeal, see note)Work-related requirements (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323][^c-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0][^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323][^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72][^c-c0fcead2a289aaf17fa23eaf8bc0df2d] Regulations may make provision for or in connection with imposing on a person who is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) entitled to an employment and support allowance, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) not a member of the support group, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

a requirement to take part in one or more work-focused health-related assessments as a condition of continuing to be entitled to the full amount payable to him in respect of the allowance apart from the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(2) Regulations under this section may, in particular, make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72] prescribing circumstances in which such a person is subject to a requirement to take part in one or more work-focused health-related assessments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72] for notifying such a person of any such requirement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72] prescribing the work-focused health-related assessments in which a person who is subject to such a requirement is required to take part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (d)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72] for the determination, and notification, of the time and place of any such assessment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (e)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72] prescribing circumstances in which a person attending such an assessment is to be regarded as having, or not having, taken part in it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (f)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72] for securing that the appropriate consequence follows if a person who is required under the regulations to take part in a work-focused health-related assessment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
  - (i) fails to take part in the assessment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323] does not, within a prescribed period, show that he had good cause for that failure; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (g)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72] prescribing matters which are, or are not, to be taken into account in determining whether a person had good cause for any failure to comply with the regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (h)[^c-53d4465e668673970de4ba077759ea3b] prescribing circumstances in which a person is, or is not, to be regarded as having good cause for any such failure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(3)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72] For the purposes of subsection (2)(f), the appropriate consequence of a failure falling within that provision is that the amount payable to the person in question in respect of an employment and support allowance is reduced in accordance with regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(4)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72] Regulations under subsection (3) may, in particular, make provision for determining— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) the amount by which an allowance is to be reduced, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) when the reduction is to start, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) how long it is to continue, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

and may include provision prescribing circumstances in which the amount of the reduction is to be nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(5)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72] Regulations under this section shall include provision for a requirement to take part in one or more work-focused health-related assessments to cease to have effect if the person subject to the requirement becomes a member of the support group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(6) Regulations under this section may include provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72] that in such circumstances as the regulations may prescribe a requirement to take part in a work-focused health-related assessment that would otherwise apply to a person by virtue of such regulations is not to apply, or is to be treated as not having applied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b)[^c-53d4465e668673970de4ba077759ea3b] that in such circumstances as the regulations may prescribe such a requirement is not to apply until a prescribed time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c)[^c-53d4465e668673970de4ba077759ea3b] that in such circumstances as the regulations may prescribe the time and place of a work-focused health-related assessment in which a person is required by regulations under this section to take part may be redetermined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(7) In this section, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]**work-focused health-related assessment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]**” means an assessment by a health care professional approved by the Secretary of State which is carried out for the purpose of assessing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a)[^c-53d4465e668673970de4ba077759ea3b] the extent to which a person still has capability for work, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b)[^c-53d4465e668673970de4ba077759ea3b] the extent to which his capability for work may be improved by the taking of steps in relation to his physical or mental condition, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c)[^c-74f8059ab06854610028b6e7be54026c][^c-ce0304f9ebb41ad1a728b1c1f9adef72] such other matters relating to his physical or mental condition and the likelihood of his obtaining or remaining in work or being able to do so, as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(8)[^c-53d4465e668673970de4ba077759ea3b] In subsection (7), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]**health care professional *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) a registered medical practitioner, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) a registered nurse, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) an occupational therapist or physiotherapist registered with a regulatory body established by an Order in Council under section 60 of the Health Act 1999 (c. 8), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (d) a member of such other profession regulated by a body mentioned in section 25(3) of the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002 (c. 17) as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(1) The following provisions of this Part provide for the Secretary of State to impose work-related requirements with which persons entitled to an employment and support allowance must comply for the purposes of this Part.

(2) In this Part “**work-related requirement**” means—

- (a) a work-focused interview requirement (see section 11B);
- (b) a work preparation requirement (see section 11C).

(3) The work-related requirements which may be imposed on a person depend on which of the following groups the person falls into—

- (a) persons subject to no work-related requirements (see section 11D);
- (b) persons subject to work-focused interview requirement only (see section 11E);
- (c) persons subject to work-focused interview and work preparation requirements (see section 11F).
