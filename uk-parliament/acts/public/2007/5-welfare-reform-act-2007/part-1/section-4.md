## 4. Amount of income-related allowance[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff][^c-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-74c970a8bf7c529912660eabfea7750a] In the case of an income-related allowance, the amount payable in respect of a claimant shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]

- (a) if he has no income, the applicable amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]
- (b) if he has an income, the amount by which the applicable amount exceeds his income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]

(2) Subject to subsection (3), the applicable amount for the purposes of subsection (1) shall be calculated by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]

- (a)[^c-35c45465d8f1bfd074d44e77c556eb93][^c-f6a8f6316ad51da6a6a3af0494a99fb9] taking such amount, or the aggregate of such amounts, as may be prescribed, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]
- (b)[^c-74c970a8bf7c529912660eabfea7750a] if in the claimant's case the conditions of entitlement to the support component *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff] [^c-4e073e11132e3e910175b8c6de1f05eb]... are satisfied, adding the amount of that component. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]

(3)[^c-35c45465d8f1bfd074d44e77c556eb93][^c-f6a8f6316ad51da6a6a3af0494a99fb9] Regulations may provide that, in prescribed cases, the applicable amount for the purposes of subsection (1) shall be nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]

(4)[^c-74c970a8bf7c529912660eabfea7750a] The conditions of entitlement to the support component are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]

- (a)[^c-623fb1a64e31e876964e68f704207376] that the assessment phase has ended, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]
- (b) that the claimant has limited capability for work-related activity, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]
- (c) that such other conditions as may be prescribed are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]

(6) Regulations may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]

- (a)[^c-35c45465d8f1bfd074d44e77c556eb93][^c-f6a8f6316ad51da6a6a3af0494a99fb9] prescribe circumstances in which paragraph (a) of subsection (4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff] [^c-58286bee58f78a9731d877ce101cfb1b]... is not to apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa58e5532683b2e3e4168fdc6726f1ff]
