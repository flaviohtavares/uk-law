# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Work-related activity[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323][^key-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-efdc511e87869d99afa5bfa05190230d] Regulations may make provision for or in connection with imposing on a person who is subject to a requirement imposed under section 12(1) a requirement to undertake work-related activity in accordance with regulations as a condition of continuing to be entitled to the full amount payable to him in respect of an employment and support allowance apart from the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(2)[^key-efdc511e87869d99afa5bfa05190230d] Regulations under this section may, in particular, make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) prescribing circumstances in which such a person is subject to a requirement to undertake work-related activity in accordance with regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) for notifying such a person of any such requirement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) prescribing the time or times at which a person who is subject to such a requirement is required to undertake work-related activity and the amount of work-related activity he is required at any time to undertake; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (d) prescribing circumstances in which a person who is subject to such a requirement is, or is not, to be regarded as undertaking work-related activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (e) for securing that the appropriate consequence follows if a person who is subject to such a requirement— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
  - (i) fails to comply with the regulations, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323] does not, within a prescribed period, show that he had good cause for that failure; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (f) prescribing the evidence which a person who is subject to such a requirement needs to provide in order to show that he has complied with the regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (g) prescribing matters which are, or are not, to be taken into account in determining whether a person has complied with the regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (h) prescribing matters which are, or are not, to be taken into account in determining whether a person had good cause for any failure to comply with the regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (i) prescribing circumstances in which a person is, or is not, to be regarded as having good cause for any such failure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(3)[^key-efdc511e87869d99afa5bfa05190230d] For the purposes of subsection (2)(e), the appropriate consequence of a failure falling within that provision is that the amount payable to the person in question in respect of an employment and support allowance is to be reduced in accordance with regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(4)[^key-efdc511e87869d99afa5bfa05190230d] Regulations under subsection (3) may, in particular, make provision for determining— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) the amount by which an allowance is to be reduced, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) when the reduction is to start, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) how long it is to continue, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

and may include provision prescribing circumstances in which the amount of the reduction is to be nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(5)[^key-efdc511e87869d99afa5bfa05190230d] Regulations under this section shall include provision for a requirement to undertake work-related activity in accordance with regulations to cease to have effect if the person subject to the requirement becomes a member of the support group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(6)[^key-efdc511e87869d99afa5bfa05190230d] Regulations under this section may include provision that in such circumstances as the regulations may provide a person's obligation under the regulations to undertake work-related activity at a particular time is not to apply, or is to be treated as not having applied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(7)[^key-754c3fe19a7f1754efa4278692b0c4e5] In this Part, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]**work-related activity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]**”, in relation to a person, means activity which makes it more likely that the person will obtain or remain in work or be able to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(8)[^key-a19ccc83ed081d84f8a82d9ae28e3a80] The reference to activity in subsection (7) includes work experience or a work placement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
