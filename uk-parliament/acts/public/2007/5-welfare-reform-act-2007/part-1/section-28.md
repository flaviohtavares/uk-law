# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Consequential amendments relating to Part 1[^key-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-4e385335352cc9e6ab010df0826a6402][^key-88d86d8528b10b3b12eb8eefdd80467a][^key-0690e6e8f622bfc38f6c06d7f5822b15] Schedule 3 (which makes amendments consequential on this Part) has effect.

(2)[^key-c69ae6e30f7079ba9b57f342b6f4c79c][^key-2aa7be951d440cf41e95efee3a4b043f] Regulations may make provision consequential on this Part amending, repealing or revoking any provision of—

- (a) an Act passed on or before the last day of the Session in which this Act is passed, or
- (b) an instrument made under an Act before the passing of this Act.

(3)[^key-6d845112eb23b4ed7d90f3f1162f68ef] In subsection (2), “**Act**” includes an Act of the Scottish Parliament.
