# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14. Action plans in connection with work-focused interviews[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323][^c-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-b67d01613c1ba5d820040b5e8a80d56c][^c-e0cd2040421e34559000b1f80b0c095d] The Secretary of State shall in prescribed circumstances provide a person subject to a requirement imposed under section 12(1) with a document prepared for such purposes as may be prescribed (in this section referred to as an action plan). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(2) Regulations may make provision about— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a)[^c-b67d01613c1ba5d820040b5e8a80d56c][^c-e0cd2040421e34559000b1f80b0c095d] the form of action plans; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b)[^c-b67d01613c1ba5d820040b5e8a80d56c][^c-e0cd2040421e34559000b1f80b0c095d] the content of action plans; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c)[^c-2625f8f6251aac59feb02579660d1234] the review and updating of action plans. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(3) Regulations under this section may, in particular, make provision for action plans which are provided to a person who is subject under section 13 to a requirement to undertake work-related activity to contain particulars of activity which, if undertaken, would enable the requirement to be met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(4) Regulations may make provision for reconsideration of an action plan at the request of the person to whom the plan is provided and may, in particular, make provision about— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) the circumstances in which reconsideration may be requested; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) the period within which any reconsideration must take place; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) the matters to which regard must be had when deciding on reconsideration whether the plan should be changed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (d) notification of the decision on reconsideration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (e) the giving of directions for the purpose of giving effect to the decision on reconsideration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
