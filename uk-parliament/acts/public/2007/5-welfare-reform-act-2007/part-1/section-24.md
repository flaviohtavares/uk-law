## 24. Interpretation of Part 1[^c-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-692ef5fbd76354fdf6a3fa342fc6a9f9][^c-075f19a450c53a70f060aa782ccb9cf8] In this Part—

- “[^c-45b72d4f0a65f3de5a4b14862e00d0da]**child**” means a person under the age of 16;
- “**claimant**” means a person who has claimed an employment and support allowance;
- “[^c-79d324667a45c9da19b1dd61b1ed3d69]**contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-79d324667a45c9da19b1dd61b1ed3d69]**” has the meaning given by section 1(7); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-79d324667a45c9da19b1dd61b1ed3d69]
- “employment” and “employed” have the meanings prescribed for the purposes of this Part;
- “**entitled**”, in relation to an employment and support allowance, is to be construed in accordance with—
  - (a) the provisions of this Act,
  - (b) section 1 of the Administration Act (entitlement dependent on making of claim), and
  - (c) section 27 of the Social Security Act 1998 (c. 14) (restrictions on entitlement in certain cases of error);
- “**income-related allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-79d324667a45c9da19b1dd61b1ed3d69]**” has the meaning given by section 1(7); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-79d324667a45c9da19b1dd61b1ed3d69]
- “**income support *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-79d324667a45c9da19b1dd61b1ed3d69]**” means income support under section 124 of the Contributions and Benefits Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-79d324667a45c9da19b1dd61b1ed3d69]
- “**limited capability for work**” shall be construed in accordance with section 1(4);
- “**limited capability for work-related activity**” shall be construed in accordance with section 2(5);
- “**period of limited capability for work**” has the meaning prescribed for the purposes of this Part;
- “**prescribed**” means specified in, or determined in accordance with, regulations;
- “**regulations**” means regulations made by the Secretary of State;
- “**single person**” means an individual who is not a member of a couple (within the meaning of Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012);
- “**week**” means a period of 7 days beginning with a Sunday or such other period of 7 days as may be prescribed;
- “work” has such meaning as may be prescribed;
- “**work-focused interview requirement**” has the meaning given by section 11B;
- “**work preparation requirement**” has the meaning given by section 11C;
- “[^c-fe2935cb092b0e7c6e130629207d4485]**work-related activity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fe2935cb092b0e7c6e130629207d4485]**” has the meaning given by section 13(7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fe2935cb092b0e7c6e130629207d4485]
- “**work-related activity**”, in relation to a person, means activity which makes it more likely that the person will obtain or remain in work or be able to do so;

  “**work-related requirement**” has the meaning given by section 11;

(2)[^c-ae40789137b470176bfa012a3a7a1c39] For the purposes of this Part, the assessment phase, in relation to a claimant, is the period—

- (a)[^c-d3bb83d8a52e56ad77ebd5497565e371] beginning, subject to subsection (3), with the first day of the period for which he is entitled to an employment and support allowance, and
- (b)[^c-379f5fd5f5bfd5afd3d1b874c99fac81][^c-075f19a450c53a70f060aa782ccb9cf8] ending with such day as may be prescribed.

(3)[^c-379f5fd5f5bfd5afd3d1b874c99fac81][^c-075f19a450c53a70f060aa782ccb9cf8] Regulations may prescribe circumstances in which the assessment phase is to begin with such day as may be prescribed.

(3A)[^c-489c860be99017df5cf313705be3b31a][^c-f563eb0ec4dd9ce3c9f53ec9c7afd82e] For the purposes of this Part, a person is a lone parent if the person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-489c860be99017df5cf313705be3b31a]

- (a) is not a member of a couple (within the meaning given by section 137(1) of the Contributions and Benefits Act), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-489c860be99017df5cf313705be3b31a]
- (b) is responsible for, and a member of the same household as, a person under the age of 16. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-489c860be99017df5cf313705be3b31a]

(3B) For the purposes of subsection (3A)(b) regulations may make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-489c860be99017df5cf313705be3b31a]

- (a) as to circumstances in which one person is to be treated as responsible or not responsible for another; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-489c860be99017df5cf313705be3b31a]
- (b) as to circumstances in which persons are to be treated as being or not being members of the same household. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-489c860be99017df5cf313705be3b31a]

(4)[^c-d3bb83d8a52e56ad77ebd5497565e371] For the purposes of this Part, a person is a member of the support group if he is a person in respect of whom it is determined that he has, or is to be treated as having, limited capability for work-related activity.
