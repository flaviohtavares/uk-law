# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 1B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1B. Further entitlement after time-limiting[^c-e0292818a3035aa7a8ad81a861e7476b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where a person's entitlement to a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0861065368b67bf272fa0df6f78d7ee9] an employment and support allowance has ceased as a result of section 1A (1) or (4) but—

- (a) the person has not at any subsequent time ceased to have (or to be treated as having) limited capability for work,
- (b) the person satisfies the basic conditions, and
- (c) the person has (or is treated as having) limited capability for work-related activity,

the claimant is entitled to an employment and support allowance by virtue of this section.

(2)[^c-efe36ed16c1585b92883156792f7b01d] An employment and support allowance entitlement to which is based on this section is to be regarded as a contributory allowance for the purposes of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-efe36ed16c1585b92883156792f7b01d]
