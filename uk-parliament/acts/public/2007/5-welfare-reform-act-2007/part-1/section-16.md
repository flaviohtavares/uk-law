## 16. Contracting out[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323][^c-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] The following functions of the Secretary of State may be exercised by, or by employees of, such person (if any) as the Secretary of State may authorise for the purpose, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) conducting interviews under section 12; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) providing documents under section 14; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) giving, varying or revoking directions under section 15. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (d)[^c-8bf257dce0ae820fe8671b9e87dbd7eb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^c-8bf257dce0ae820fe8671b9e87dbd7eb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^c-8bf257dce0ae820fe8671b9e87dbd7eb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Regulations may provide for any of the following functions of the Secretary of State to be exercisable by, or by employees of, such person (if any) as the Secretary of State may authorise for the purpose— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a)[^c-8062f786bbbfc86f183ee984cfd20d96][^c-5f27c5e1b14db13121fbc36a77c04672] any function under regulations under any of sections 11 to 15 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323] [^c-ea0d4422884b6519f75e177bbb5a13f1]..., except the making of a decision to which subsection (3) applies (an “excluded decision”); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] the function under section 9(1) of the Social Security Act 1998 (c. 14) (revision of decisions), so far as relating to decisions, except excluded decisions, that relate to any matter arising under such regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] the function under section 10(1) of that Act (superseding of decisions), so far as relating to decisions, except excluded decisions, of the Secretary of State that relate to any matter arising under such regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (d)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] any function under Chapter 2 of Part 1 of that Act (social security decisions), except section 25(2) and (3) (decisions involving issues that arise on appeal in other cases), which relates to the exercise of any of the functions falling within paragraphs (a) to (c). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(3)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] This subsection applies to the following decisions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) a decision about whether a person has failed to comply with a requirement imposed by regulations under section 11, 12 or 13 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323] [^c-5054b417122fef15616f116cc583ee99]...;
- (b) a decision about whether a person had good cause for failure to comply with such a requirement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) a decision about reduction of an employment and support allowance in consequence of failure to comply with such a requirement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(4)[^c-8062f786bbbfc86f183ee984cfd20d96][^c-5f27c5e1b14db13121fbc36a77c04672] Regulations under subsection (2) may provide that a function to which that subsection applies may be exercised— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) either wholly or to such extent as the regulations may provide, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) either generally or in such cases or areas as the regulations may provide, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) either unconditionally or subject to the fulfilment of such conditions as the regulations may provide. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(5)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] An authorisation given by virtue of subsection (1), or by virtue of regulations under subsection (2), may authorise the exercise of the function concerned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) either wholly or to such extent as may be specified in the authorisation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) either generally or in such cases or areas as may be so specified, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) either unconditionally or subject to the fulfilment of such conditions as may be so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(6)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] In the case of an authorisation given by virtue of regulations under subsection (2), subsection (5) is subject to the provisions of the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(7)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] An authorisation given by virtue of subsection (1), or by virtue of regulations under subsection (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) may specify its duration, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) may be revoked at any time by the Secretary of State, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) shall not prevent the Secretary of State or any other person from exercising the function to which the authorisation relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(8)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] Where a person is authorised to exercise any function by virtue of subsection (1), or by virtue of regulations under subsection (2), anything done or omitted to be done by or in relation to him (or an employee of his) in, or in connection with, the exercise or purported exercise of the function shall be treated for all purposes as done or omitted to be done by or in relation to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(9)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] Subsection (8) shall not apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) for the purposes of so much of any contract made between the authorised person and the Secretary of State as relates to the exercise of the function, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) for the purposes of any criminal proceedings brought in respect of anything done or omitted to be done by the authorised person (or an employee of his). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(10)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] Any decision which a person authorised to exercise any function by virtue of subsection (1), or by virtue of regulations under subsection (2), makes in exercise of the function shall have effect as a decision of the Secretary of State under section 8 of the Social Security Act 1998 (c. 14). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(11)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) a person is authorised to exercise any function by virtue of subsection (1), or by virtue of regulations under subsection (2), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) the authorisation is revoked at a time when a relevant contract is subsisting, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

the authorised person shall be entitled to treat the relevant contract as repudiated by the Secretary of State (and not as frustrated by reason of the revocation). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(12)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] In subsection (11), the reference to a relevant contract is to so much of any contract made between the authorised person and the Secretary of State as relates to the exercise of the function. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(13)[^c-9f57f8830ff39c274906ad3547e3dc92] In this section, references to functions of the Secretary of State under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) an enactment contained in, or in regulations under, this Part, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) an enactment contained in Chapter 2 of Part 1 of the Social Security Act 1998, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

include a reference to any function which the Secretary of State has by virtue of the application in relation to that enactment of section 8(1)(c) of that Act (decisions under certain enactments to be made by the Secretary of State). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
