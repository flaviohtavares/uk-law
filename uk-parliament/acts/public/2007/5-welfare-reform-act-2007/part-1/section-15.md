# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5

## 15. Directions about work-related activity[^key-6803088872ba98b7f3aea4403e8aca41][^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323][^key-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-6d71f0093e47103156df89531eee8fe8] In prescribed circumstances, the Secretary of State may by direction given to a person subject to a requirement imposed under section 13(1) provide that the activity specified in the direction is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) to be the only activity which, in the person’s case, is to be regarded as being work-related activity; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) to be regarded, in the person’s case, as not being work-related activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(1A) But a direction under subsection (1) may not specify medical or surgical treatment as the only activity which, in any person’s case, is to be regarded as being work-related activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(2) A direction under subsection (1) given to any person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

- (a) must be reasonable, having regard to the person’s circumstances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (b) must be given to the person by being included in an action plan provided to the person under section 14; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
- (c) may be varied or revoked by a subsequent direction under subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

(3) Where a direction under subsection (1) varies or revokes a previous direction, it may provide for the variation or revocation to have effect from a time before the giving of the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]
