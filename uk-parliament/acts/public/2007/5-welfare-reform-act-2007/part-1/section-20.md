# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5

## 20. Relationship with statutory payments[^c-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-f20fcc712f17be0e125fbb89f1fc6e78] A person is not entitled to an employment and support allowance in respect of a day if, for the purposes of statutory sick pay, that day—

- (a) is a day of incapacity for work in relation to a contract of service, and
- (b) falls within a period of entitlement (whether or not it is a qualifying day).

(2)[^c-c04d0684d1d062032d04d8a5a2095c7c][^c-60f718dbc0401a41b183bde55b1be473][^c-6ee491f2ab2ca9fadb656c2a73bf11c4] Except as regulations may provide, a woman who is entitled to statutory maternity pay is not entitled to a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-98a1da4ee12d9bdde098ad20aa25dd73] an employment and support allowance in respect of a day that falls within the maternity pay period.

(3)[^c-c04d0684d1d062032d04d8a5a2095c7c][^c-60f718dbc0401a41b183bde55b1be473] Regulations may provide that—

- (a) an amount equal to a woman's statutory maternity pay for a period shall be deducted from a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fab7d3a0791d999b0cc1774ad22826da] an employment and support allowance in respect of the same period,
- (b) a woman shall only be entitled to a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fab7d3a0791d999b0cc1774ad22826da] an employment and support allowance if there is a balance after the deduction, and
- (c) if there is such a balance, a woman shall be entitled to a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fab7d3a0791d999b0cc1774ad22826da] an employment and support allowance at a weekly rate equal to it.

(4)[^c-c04d0684d1d062032d04d8a5a2095c7c][^c-60f718dbc0401a41b183bde55b1be473][^c-09613af293275760fda2e9fed3db0ea0] Except as regulations may provide, a person who is entitled to statutory adoption pay is not entitled to a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4a842d883b610438c51240ae22593b0a] an employment and support allowance in respect of a day that falls within the adoption pay period.

(5)[^c-c04d0684d1d062032d04d8a5a2095c7c][^c-60f718dbc0401a41b183bde55b1be473] Regulations may provide that—

- (a) an amount equal to a person's statutory adoption pay for a period shall be deducted from a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c88d96c880f34f9d5cd70e3dfe743032] an employment and support allowance in respect of the same period,
- (b) a person shall only be entitled to a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c88d96c880f34f9d5cd70e3dfe743032] an employment and support allowance if there is a balance after the deduction, and
- (c) if there is such a balance, a person shall be entitled to a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c88d96c880f34f9d5cd70e3dfe743032] an employment and support allowance at a weekly rate equal to it.

(6)[^c-c04d0684d1d062032d04d8a5a2095c7c][^c-60f718dbc0401a41b183bde55b1be473][^c-5bb0a0d07cf151efc4070ce5a96b5054] Except as regulations may provide, a person who is entitled to statutory shared parental pay[^c-1603bc48950caf3602226194103178f8] is not entitled to a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-02b16573feaaf87bdc9fb79cd616d2de] an employment and support allowance in respect of a day that falls within a period in respect of which statutory shared parental pay is payable[^c-c8b0674d394f113fccb6e30ad768f93d].

(7)[^c-c04d0684d1d062032d04d8a5a2095c7c][^c-60f718dbc0401a41b183bde55b1be473] Regulations may provide that—

- (a) an amount equal to a person's statutory shared parental pay for a period[^c-1ff9d5777a1bb24e75ec0f86f48e4545] shall be deducted from a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8cbe7b2227de3a5b50abb5d65a6bb032] an employment and support allowance in respect of the same period,
- (b) a person shall only be entitled to a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8cbe7b2227de3a5b50abb5d65a6bb032] an employment and support allowance if there is a balance after the deduction, and
- (c) if there is such a balance, a person shall be entitled to a contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8cbe7b2227de3a5b50abb5d65a6bb032] an employment and support allowance at a weekly rate equal to it.

(7A)[^c-943258cdacd3127b38fd6446b23835b7] Except as regulations may provide, a person who is entitled to statutory neonatal care pay is not entitled to an employment and support allowance in respect of a day that falls within a period in respect of which statutory neonatal care pay is payable.

(7B) Regulations may provide that—

- (a) an amount equal to a person’s statutory neonatal care pay for a period shall be deducted from an employment and support allowance in respect of the same period,
- (b) a person shall only be entitled to an employment and support allowance if there is a balance after the deduction, and
- (c) if there is such a balance, a person shall be entitled to an employment and support allowance at a weekly rate equal to it.

(8)[^c-f20fcc712f17be0e125fbb89f1fc6e78] In this section—

- [^c-bada48a79723c60846826a3cf4c3b9e1]...
- “**the adoption pay period**” has the meaning given in section 171ZN(2) of the Contributions and Benefits Act[^c-b5232a5d4be04fcc65341067d7ff4e1e];
- “**the maternity pay period**” has the meaning given in section 165(1) of that Act.
