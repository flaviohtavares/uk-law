# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Employment and support allowance[^c-c97e7ad905407acc9a797f683f50f2b8][^c-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0][^c-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An allowance, to be known as an employment and support allowance, shall be payable in accordance with the provisions of this Part.

(2) Subject to the provisions of this Part, a claimant is entitled to an employment and support allowance if he satisfies the basic conditions and either *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f613a4550f8c8967cd8f2deb64663a02]—

- (a) the first and the second conditions set out in Part 1 of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-42945cf3b66ec88b05ad7641bc96bdc3] Schedule 1 (conditions relating to national insurance) or the third condition set out in that Part of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-42945cf3b66ec88b05ad7641bc96bdc3] that Schedule (condition relating to youth), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63df0be79b8ace9f82e0649358e342ed]
- (b) the conditions set out in Part 2 of that Schedule (conditions relating to financial position). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63df0be79b8ace9f82e0649358e342ed]

(3) The basic conditions are that the claimant—

- (a) has limited capability for work,
- (aa)[^c-669aabd46acbd593b797a3600d8919e4][^c-0a34207442814fca5a5f1df5891682f7] has accepted a claimant commitment,
- (b) is at least 16 years old,
- (c) has not reached pensionable age,
- (d) is in Great Britain, and[^c-261bc11c0f702d8420278a93c6bee947]
- (e) is not entitled to income support, and
- (f) is not entitled to a jobseeker's allowance (and is not a member of a couple who are entitled to a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b68d050f8ae57c7d0f91b3dad9a3b99f].

(3A)[^c-fade5770993323ef0cb1008e187b061c] After the coming into force of this subsection no claim may be made for an employment and support allowance by virtue of the third condition set out in Part 1 of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d778a5960987af0f4d2d2aaccc10958f] Schedule 1 (youth).

(4) For the purposes of this Part, a person has limited capability for work if—

- (a) his capability for work is limited by his physical or mental condition, and
- (b) the limitation is such that it is not reasonable to require him to work.

(5) An employment and support allowance is payable in respect of a week.

(6) In subsection (3)—

- “[^c-2b829f5af8d5624ef146330945f4488a]**joint-claim jobseeker's allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2b829f5af8d5624ef146330945f4488a]**” means a jobseeker's allowance entitlement to which arises by virtue of section 1(2B) of the Jobseekers Act 1995 (c. 18); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2b829f5af8d5624ef146330945f4488a]
- “**pensionable age**” has the meaning given by the rules in paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Pensions Act 1995 (c. 26).

(6A)[^c-9b44a82aa4ecbd6d5a4927715131b65f][^c-f53f077b7e49441b6464a4e86a8ff182] In subsection (3)(f), in relation to a contributory allowance, the reference to a couple entitled to a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance does not include a couple so entitled by virtue of regulations under paragraph 8A of Schedule 1 to the Jobseekers Act 1995. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b44a82aa4ecbd6d5a4927715131b65f]

(7) In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b44a82aa4ecbd6d5a4927715131b65f]

- “**contributory allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b44a82aa4ecbd6d5a4927715131b65f]**” means an employment and support allowance entitlement to which is based on subsection (2)(a) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b44a82aa4ecbd6d5a4927715131b65f] (and see section 1B(2))[^c-d502e4ede2bcb45ea542bec92f3f8a91] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b44a82aa4ecbd6d5a4927715131b65f];
- “**income-related allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b44a82aa4ecbd6d5a4927715131b65f]**” means an employment and support allowance entitlement to which is based on subsection (2)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b44a82aa4ecbd6d5a4927715131b65f]
