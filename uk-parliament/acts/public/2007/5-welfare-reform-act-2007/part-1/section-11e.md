# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 11E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11E. Persons subject to work-focused interview requirement only[^c-c7ff5ca6cfc7ac0256f01590ed666323]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person falls within this section if—

- (a) the person is a single person responsible for a child who is aged at least 1 and is under a prescribed age (which may not be less than 3), or
- (b) the person is of a prescribed description.

(2) The Secretary of State may, subject to this Part, impose a work-focused interview requirement on a person entitled to an employment and support allowance who falls within this section.

(3) The Secretary of State may not impose a work preparation requirement on a person falling within this section (and, where a person falls within this section, a work preparation requirement previously applying to the person ceases to have effect).
