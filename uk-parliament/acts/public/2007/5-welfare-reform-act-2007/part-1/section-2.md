## 2. Amount of contributory (affected by repeal, see note) allowance[^key-a930b4f5a9da12f15855a11f7d9602d0][^key-309efab56bb5a531ea6631eb47041949]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In the case of a contributory allowance, the amount payable *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65d0867e5db4c1e6b4430a3abab8f648] The amount payable by way of an employment and support allowance in respect of a claimant shall be calculated by—

- (a)[^key-4321c85d3b348beadf2b695f0317013b][^key-23fd453768af20b3a2dbee7141e3d223] taking such amount as may be prescribed,
- (b)[^key-c96624e800be4f0f666fdae24be29f84] if in his case the conditions of entitlement to the support component [^key-58c88872a832064d16ba2655db943fbe]... are satisfied, adding the amount of that component, and
- (c)[^key-4321c85d3b348beadf2b695f0317013b][^key-23fd453768af20b3a2dbee7141e3d223] making prescribed deductions in respect of any payments to which section 3 applies.

(2)[^key-c96624e800be4f0f666fdae24be29f84] The conditions of entitlement to the support component are—

- (a)[^key-99a2cac272892b1813725e27e36ba41c][^key-87007a6da49f9f7cebe4ffa25d077c5b] that the assessment phase has ended,
- (b) that the claimant has limited capability for work-related activity, and
- (c) that such other conditions as may be prescribed are satisfied.

(3) [^key-826b5035510faa732c7fa684aa6c1c5f]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Regulations may—

- (a)[^key-4321c85d3b348beadf2b695f0317013b][^key-23fd453768af20b3a2dbee7141e3d223] prescribe circumstances in which paragraph (a) of subsection (2) [^key-68df886d616eb38e310964dbedf2a64e]... is not to apply;
- (b)[^key-c96624e800be4f0f666fdae24be29f84] prescribe circumstances in which entitlement under subsection (2) [^key-38f0c9607b258fe4368d476f5cd89507]... is to be backdated;
- (c)[^key-4321c85d3b348beadf2b695f0317013b][^key-23fd453768af20b3a2dbee7141e3d223] make provision about the amount of the component under subsection (2) [^key-7931b952cb3362b29959c6a3ebc3d8f9]....

(5) For the purposes of this Part, a person has limited capability for work-related activity if—

- (a) his capability for work-related activity is limited by his physical or mental condition, and
- (b) the limitation is such that it is not reasonable to require him to undertake such activity.
