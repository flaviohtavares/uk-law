# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 16A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5

## 16A. Hardship payments[^c-9669e76881bb0c75f7f4fda192286fa0][^c-78b57750744e99e87ce105ee3b0a3fe2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Regulations may make provision for the making of payments (“hardship payments”) by way of an employment and support allowance to a person where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9669e76881bb0c75f7f4fda192286fa0]

- (a) the amount otherwise payable to the person in respect of an employment and support allowance is reduced by virtue of regulations under section 11(3), 12(3) or 13(3), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9669e76881bb0c75f7f4fda192286fa0]
- (b) the person is or will be in hardship. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9669e76881bb0c75f7f4fda192286fa0]

(2) Regulations under this section may in particular make provision as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9669e76881bb0c75f7f4fda192286fa0]

- (a) circumstances in which a person is to be treated as being or not being in hardship; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9669e76881bb0c75f7f4fda192286fa0]
- (b) matters to be taken into account in determining whether a person is or will be in hardship; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9669e76881bb0c75f7f4fda192286fa0]
- (c) requirements or conditions to be met by a person in order to receive hardship payments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9669e76881bb0c75f7f4fda192286fa0]
- (d) the amount or rate of hardship payments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9669e76881bb0c75f7f4fda192286fa0]
- (e) the period for which hardship payments may be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9669e76881bb0c75f7f4fda192286fa0]
