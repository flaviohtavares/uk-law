## 65. General interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act—

- “**Administration Act**” means the Social Security Administration Act 1992 (c. 5);
- “**Contributions and Benefits Act**” means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (c. 4).
