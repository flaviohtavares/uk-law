# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Local authority powers to investigate benefit fraud[^c-e62d54f080fbbf38de9363578de0f8d8][^c-b3e5c7e01106f1b595d4258841082fe5][^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 110A of the Administration Act (authorisation of investigations by authorities administering housing benefit or council tax benefit) is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d]

(2) In subsection (1) for “any one or more of the purposes mentioned in subsection (2) below” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d] “ a relevant purpose ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d].

(3) After subsection (1) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d]

> (1A) Each of the following is a relevant purpose— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d]
>
> - (a) a purpose mentioned in subsection (2) below; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d]
> - (b) a purpose mentioned in section 109A(2)(a), (c) or (d). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d]
>
> (1B) If the Secretary of State prescribes conditions for the purposes of this section, an authority must not proceed under this section for a purpose mentioned in section 109A(2)(a), (c) or (d) unless any such condition is satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d]
>
> (1C) An authorisation made for a purpose mentioned in section 109A(2)(a), (c) or (d)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d]
>
> - (a) is subject to such restrictions as may be prescribed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d]
> - (b) is not valid in such circumstances as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d]

(4) In subsection (2) for “Those purposes” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d] “ The purposes in this subsection ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d].

(5) In subsection (8), after paragraph (c) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d] “ but paragraphs (a) and (b) above do not apply in any case where the relevant purpose is as mentioned in subsection (1A)(b) above. ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7bda4d2dd611ac4700be621325d5f86d]
