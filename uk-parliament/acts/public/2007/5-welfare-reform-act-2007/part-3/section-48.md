# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 48

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Local authority functions relating to benefit: information

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1)[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb] Section 122C of the Administration Act (supply of information to authorities administering benefit) is amended as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]

- (a) in subsection (2) at the end insert *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb] “ or for the purposes of anything the authority is permitted to do in relation to any other benefit by virtue of section 110A or 116A above ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb];
- (b) in subsection (3)(a) for “offences relating to housing benefit or council tax benefit” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb] “ benefit offences (within the meaning of Part 6 above) ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb].

(2) Section 122D of that Act (supply of information by authorities administering benefit) is amended as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]

- (a) in subsection (1), for “benefit administration information” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb] “ relevant benefit information ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb];
- (b) in subsection (2A), after “subsection (2)” insert *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb] “ , in addition to any other purpose for which the information may be used, ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb];
- (c) for subsection (4) substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]

  > (4) In subsection (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]**relevant benefit information *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]**”, in relation to an authority or other person, means any information which is relevant to the exercise of any function relating to a relevant social security benefit by the authority or other person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]

  ;
- (d) in subsection (5), in each place where it occurs for “housing benefit or council tax benefit” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb] “ any relevant social security benefit ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb];
- (e) in subsection (6), after the definition of “private pensions policy” insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]

  > “**relevant social security benefit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]**” has the same meaning as in section 121DA above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]

  .

(3) Section 122E of that Act (supply of information between authorities administering benefit) is amended as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]

- (a) in subsection (1) for “benefit administration information” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb] “ relevant benefit information ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb];
- (b) in subsection (2)(a) for “offences relating to housing benefit or council tax benefit” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb] “ benefit offences (within the meaning of Part 6 above) ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb];
- (c) for subsection (6) substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]

  > (6) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]**relevant benefit information *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]**”, in relation to an authority or other person, means any information which is relevant to the exercise of any function relating to a relevant social security benefit (within the meaning of section 121DA above) by the authority or other person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36e7ef893c687e4f8ae81da0d958dafb]

(4) In section 126A of that Act (power to require information from landlords and agents), in subsection (8) for “benefit administration information” substitute “ relevant benefit information ”.
