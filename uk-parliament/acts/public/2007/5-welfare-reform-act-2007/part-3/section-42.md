# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Information relating to certain benefits[^c-a3a2f4ade7e5e791f5aa5e9708fad001][^c-7a8e1631e4df4aea6981ce645882476a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09] Information falling within subsection (3) may be supplied by the person who holds it to a person falling within subsection (4) for purposes connected with the application of grant paid under a relevant enactment towards expenditure incurred by the recipient of the grant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

- (a) in providing, or contributing to the provision of, welfare services, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (b) in connection with such welfare services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

(2) Information falling within subsection (3) which is held for a prescribed purpose by a person falling within any of paragraphs (c) to (h) of subsection (4) may be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

- (a) used by that person for another prescribed purpose; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (b) provided to another such person for use in relation to the same or another prescribed purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

(3) The information is any information which is held by a person falling within subsection (4) relating to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

- (a) income support; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (b) income-based jobseeker's allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (c) income-related employment and support allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (d) state pension credit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (e) housing benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (f) welfare services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

(4) The persons are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

- (a) the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (b) a person providing services to the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (c) an authority administering housing benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (d) a person authorised to exercise any function of such an authority relating to housing benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (e) a person providing to such an authority services relating to housing benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (f) a local authority to which any grant is or will be paid as mentioned in subsection (1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (g) a person authorised to exercise any function of such an authority relating to the grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (h) a person providing to such an authority services relating to any such function. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

(5) Information which is supplied under subsection (1) to an authority or other person falling within subsection (4)(f), (g) or (h) may be supplied by the authority or person to a person who provides qualifying welfare services for purposes connected with the provision of those services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

(6) A person provides qualifying welfare services if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

- (a) he provides welfare services, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (b) a local authority contribute or will contribute to the expenditure incurred by him in providing those services, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (c) that contribution is or will be derived (in whole or in part) from any grant which is or will be paid to the authority as mentioned in subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

(7) A relevant enactment is an enactment specified by order made by the Secretary of State; and the power to make an order under this subsection is exercisable by statutory instrument subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

(8) In subsection (2) a prescribed purpose is a purpose relating to housing benefit or welfare services which is prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State by statutory instrument subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

(9) The power to make an order or regulations under this section includes power— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

- (a) to make different provision for different purposes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- (b) to make such incidental, supplementary, consequential, transitional or saving provision as the Secretary of State thinks necessary or expedient. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

(10) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

- “**income-based jobseeker's allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]**” has the same meaning as in the Jobseekers Act 1995 (c. 18); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- “**income-related employment and support allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]**” means an income-related allowance under Part 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- “**local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
  - (a) in relation to England, a county council, a district council, a London borough council, the Common Council of the City of London or the Council of the Isles of Scilly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
  - (b) in relation to Wales, a county council or a county borough council; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
- “**welfare services *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]**” includes services which provide support, assistance, advice or counselling to individuals with particular needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]

(11) In the Local Government Act 2000 (c. 22), sections 94 (disclosure of information) and 95 (unauthorised disclosure of information) are omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cef097f4aa09f43566ff83313ddbca09]
