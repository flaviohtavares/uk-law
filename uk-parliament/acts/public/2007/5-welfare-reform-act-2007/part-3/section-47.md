# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. Local authority powers to prosecute benefit fraud[^c-f45d6bd033f6948bed9cd29475231ecc][^c-9a0b856965711c1c49dfc4095bab205f][^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

After section 116 of the Administration Act (legal proceedings) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]

> **116A. Local authority powers to prosecute benefit fraud (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]**
>
> (1) This section applies if an authority administering housing benefit or council tax benefit has power to bring proceedings for a benefit offence relating to that benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
>
> (2) The authority may bring proceedings for a benefit offence relating to any other relevant social security benefit unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
>
> - (a) the proceedings relate to any benefit or circumstances or any description of benefit or circumstances which the Secretary of State prescribes for the purposes of this paragraph, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
> - (b) the Secretary of State has directed that the authority must not bring the proceedings, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
>
> and a direction under paragraph (b) may relate to a particular authority or description of authority or to particular proceedings or any description of proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
>
> (3) If the Secretary of State prescribes conditions for the purposes of this section, an authority must not bring proceedings under this section unless any such condition is satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
>
> (4) The Secretary of State may continue proceedings which have been brought by an authority under this section as if the proceedings had been brought in his name or he may discontinue the proceedings if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
>
> - (a) he makes provision under subsection (2)(a), such that the authority would no longer be entitled to bring the proceedings under this section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
> - (b) he gives a direction under subsection (2)(b) in relation to the proceedings, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
> - (c) a condition prescribed under subsection (3) ceases to be satisfied in relation to the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
>
> (5) In the exercise of its power under subsection (2), a local authority must have regard to the Code for Crown Prosecutors issued by the Director of Public Prosecutions under section 10 of the Prosecution of Offences Act 1985— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
>
> - (a) in determining whether the proceedings should be instituted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
> - (b) in determining what charges should be preferred; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
> - (c) in considering what representations to make to a magistrates' court about mode of trial; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
> - (d) in determining whether to discontinue proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
>
> (6) An authority must not bring proceedings for a benefit offence which does not relate to housing benefit or council tax benefit otherwise than in accordance with this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
>
> (7) In subsection (2), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]**relevant social security benefit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]**” has the same meaning as in section 121DA below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
>
> (8) This section does not apply to Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-82f9af4dc32db1c5d1deff326940dac0]
