# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Unlawful disclosure of certain information[^c-5111d29a205d3473ccff6d6324944b44]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0] A person to whom subsection (2) applies is guilty of an offence if he discloses without lawful authority any information— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]

- (a) which comes to him by virtue of section 42(1), (2) or (5), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]
- (b) which relates to a particular person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]

(2) This subsection applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]

- (a) a person mentioned in section 42(4)(f) to (h); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]
- (b) a person who provides qualifying welfare services (within the meaning of section 42(6)); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]
- (c) a person who is or has been a director, member of the committee of management, manager, secretary or other similar officer of a person mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]
- (d) a person who is or has been an employee of a person mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]

(3) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or a fine or both, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]
- (b) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]

(4) It is not an offence under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]

- (a) to disclose information in the form of a summary or collection of information so framed as not to enable information relating to any particular person to be ascertained from it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]
- (b) to disclose information which has previously been disclosed to the public with lawful authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]

(5) It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under this section to prove that at the time of the alleged offence— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]

- (a) he believed that he was making the disclosure in question with lawful authority and had no reasonable cause to believe otherwise, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]
- (b) he believed that the information in question had previously been disclosed to the public with lawful authority and had no reasonable cause to believe otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]

(6) A disclosure is made with lawful authority if it is so made for the purposes of section 123 of the Administration Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]

(7) This section does not affect that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]

(8) Until the commencement of section 282 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (c. 44) (increase in maximum term that may be imposed on summary conviction of offence triable either way) the reference in subsection (3)(b) to 12 months must be taken to be a reference to 6 months. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-367124bddaceaf344c82ff5f2062d5d0]
