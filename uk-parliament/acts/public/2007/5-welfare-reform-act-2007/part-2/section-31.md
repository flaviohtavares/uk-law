## 31. Loss of housing benefit following eviction for anti-social behaviour, etc.[^c-df1e06c1448fde2dd04d53a7498c0a8a][^c-ff608f6678794d7aab7613b964244159][^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) After section 130A of the Contributions and Benefits Act (inserted by section 30) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

> **130B. Loss of housing benefit following eviction on certain grounds (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]**
>
> (1) If the following conditions are satisfied, then housing benefit is payable in the case of a person (“the former occupier”) subject to subsection (4)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) a court makes a relevant order for possession of a dwelling occupied by him as his home; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) in consequence of the order he ceases to occupy the dwelling; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (c) either of the conditions in subsections (2) and (3) is satisfied; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (d) the conditions for entitlement to housing benefit are or become satisfied with respect to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (2) The condition in this subsection is that the former occupier fails, without good cause, to comply with a warning notice served on him by a relevant local authority in England and Wales after he has ceased to occupy the dwelling. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (3) The condition in this subsection is that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) the former occupier was, after he ceased to occupy the dwelling, required by a relevant local authority in Scotland to take specified action with the aim mentioned in subsection (10), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) the former occupier was warned by the relevant local authority that if he failed to comply with the requirement the amount of housing benefit payable to him would be affected, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (c) the former occupier fails, without good cause, to comply with the requirement, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (d) the relevant local authority recommends that housing benefit be payable to the former occupier subject to subsection (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (4) During the restriction period or such part of it as may be prescribed, one or both of the following applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) the rate of the benefit is reduced in such a manner as may be prescribed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) the benefit is payable only if the circumstances are such as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (5) The restriction period begins with the earliest date on which the conditions set out in subsections (1) to (3) are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (6) That period stops running if the relevant local authority considers that the restriction set out in subsection (4) should no longer apply (whether because the former occupier is taking action to improve his behaviour or for any other reason), but starts running again if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) in England and Wales, the former occupier fails to comply with a further warning notice served on him; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) in Scotland, the condition in subsection (7) is satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (7) The condition is that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) the former occupier fails to comply with a further requirement such as is mentioned in paragraph (a) of subsection (3), having been warned as mentioned in paragraph (b) of that subsection, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) the relevant local authority recommends that the restriction period starts running again. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (8) The restriction period shall not include any period which falls more than five years after the date on which the order for possession was made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (9) A former occupier may not be subject to more than one restriction period in respect of one order for possession. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (10) A relevant local authority is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) in England and Wales, a local authority within the meaning of section 1 of the Local Government Act 2000, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) in Scotland, a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97] (Scotland) Act 1994, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> which provides or may provide services to a former occupier with the aim of ending, or preventing repetition of, the conduct which may lead or has led to the making of a relevant order for possession. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (11) A warning notice is a notice in the prescribed form— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) requiring the former occupier to take specified action with the aim mentioned in subsection (10), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) specifying the time when, or within which, that action must be taken, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (c) warning the former occupier that if he fails to take the action the amount of housing benefit payable to him would be affected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> **130C. Relevant orders for possession (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]**
>
> (1) In section 130B a relevant order for possession is, in England and Wales— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) an order made under section 84 of the Housing Act 1985 (secure tenancies) on Ground 2 set out in Schedule 2 to that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) an order made under section 7 of the Housing Act 1988 (assured tenancies) on Ground 14 set out in Schedule 2 to that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (c) an order made under section 98 of the Rent Act 1977 (protected or statutory tenancies) in the circumstances specified in Case 2 in Schedule 15 to that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (2) In that section a relevant order for possession is, in Scotland— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) an order made under section 16(2) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (secure tenancies) on one of the grounds set out in paragraphs 2 and 7 in Part 1 of Schedule 2 to that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) an order made in accordance with section 18 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1988 (assured tenancies) on Ground 15 in Part 2 of Schedule 5 to that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (c) an order made in accordance with section 11 of the Rent (Scotland) Act 1984 (protected or statutory tenancies) in the circumstances specified in Case 2 in Part 1 of Schedule 2 to that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (3) For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2) it does not matter whether the order is made on the grounds or in the circumstances there mentioned alone or together with other grounds or circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (4) Subsections (5) and (6) apply if the court— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) stays (in Scotland, sists) or suspends the execution of a relevant order for possession, or postpones the date of possession under it, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) imposes a condition (or conditions) on that stay, sist, suspension or postponement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (5) If a condition relates to the behaviour of a person or persons occupying the dwelling, section 130B(4) applies only if the order takes effect as a result of a breach of that condition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (6) Section 130B(4) does not apply if the condition (or, if there is more than one, each of them) relates only to matters other than the behaviour of a person or persons occupying the dwelling. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> **130D. Loss of housing benefit: supplementary (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]**
>
> (1) Regulations may provide that, where housing benefit has been paid subject to the restriction set out in section 130B(4), in prescribed circumstances— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) the former occupier must be paid some or all of the amount of the benefit which, by virtue of that subsection, has not been payable to him, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) such other adjustments must be made as are prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (2) The Secretary of State may by order vary the definition of relevant order for possession by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) adding to or removing from it orders of a specified description; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) specifying circumstances in which it includes orders of a specified description. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (3) Regulations may prescribe— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) matters which are, or are not, to be taken into account in determining whether a person has, or does not have, good cause for failing to take action specified in a warning notice or failing to comply with a requirement such as is mentioned in section 130B(3)(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) circumstances in which a person is, or is not, to be regarded as having, or not having, such good cause. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (4) Expressions used in this section and in section 130B have the meaning given in that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> **130E. Couples (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]**
>
> (1) This section applies where at any time the conditions for entitlement to housing benefit are satisfied with respect to a person who is a member of a couple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (2) Where paragraphs (a) and (b) of section 130B(1) are satisfied in relation to both members of the couple (whether or not in respect of the same dwelling), then for the purposes of subsection (2) or (3) of that section, the failure by one member of the couple to comply with a warning notice or with a requirement such as is mentioned in section 130B(3)(a) must be treated also as a failure by his partner to comply with it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (3) Where paragraph (a) of section 130B(1) is not satisfied in relation to one member of the couple, then subsection (4) of that section does not apply to his partner (even if paragraphs (a), (b) and (c) of section 130B(1) are satisfied in relation to the partner). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (4) References to a person's partner are to the other member of the couple concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> **130F. Information provision (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]**
>
> (1) The Secretary of State may by regulations require— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) a court which makes a relevant order for possession, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) any other person or description of person who the Secretary of State thinks is or may be aware of the making of such an order, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

>
> (2) The Secretary of State may provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) information obtained under subsection (1), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) information which is relevant to the exercise by him of any function relating to housing benefit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> to a relevant local authority, or a person authorised to exercise any function of such an authority relating to services mentioned in section 130B(10), for use in the provision of such services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (3) The Secretary of State may by regulations require— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) a relevant local authority, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) a person authorised to exercise any function of such an authority relating to services mentioned in section 130B(10), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> to supply relevant information held by the authority or other person to, or to a person providing services to, the Secretary of State for use for any purpose relating to the administration of housing benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> (4) The Secretary of State may by regulations require— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

>
> (5) The Secretary of State may by regulations require— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> - (a) an authority administering housing benefit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (b) a person authorised to exercise any function of such an authority relating to such a benefit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (c) a relevant local authority, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
> - (d) a person authorised to exercise any function of such an authority relating to services mentioned in section 130B(10), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
>
> to provide relevant information held by that authority or person to an authority or person mentioned in paragraph (c) or (d) for use in the provision of those services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

>
> (8) “**Relevant order for possession *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]**relevant local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]**” have the same meaning as in section 130B. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
