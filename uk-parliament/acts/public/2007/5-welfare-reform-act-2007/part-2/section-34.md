# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Sections 32 and 33: interpretation[^c-6b82b971d6b3f40b905ceb67bc4a9386][^c-f8d8ae9c038bc344f4aca9848b055d43][^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section has effect for the interpretation of sections 32 and 33. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

(2) The general conditions of entitlement are the conditions governing entitlement to housing benefit or council tax benefit provided for by Part 7 of the Contributions and Benefits Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

(3) The appropriate authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

- (a) in relation to housing benefit is the local authority or housing authority which, immediately before the person concerned ceased to be entitled to the prescribed benefit, funded and administered the housing benefit to which he was entitled; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
- (b) in relation to council tax benefit is the billing authority or, in Scotland, local authority which, immediately before the person concerned ceased to be entitled to the prescribed benefit, funded and administered the council tax benefit to which he was entitled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

(4) The following expressions have the same meaning as in the Administration Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

- (a) billing authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
- (b) housing authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
- (c) local authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

(5) Partner, in relation to a person, is a person who is a member of the same couple (within the meaning of Part 7 of the Contributions and Benefits Act) as that person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

(6) Prescribed means prescribed by regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
