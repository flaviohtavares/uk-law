# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Directions by Secretary of State[^key-db5da7b6752e9cb9da3458cefad318b0][^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 139D of the Administration Act (power to give directions) is amended in accordance with subsections (2) to (8) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

(2) In subsection (1) (reports that trigger the section), for paragraph (c) substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

> - (c) a copy of a report under section 102(1)(b) or (c) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 which to any extent relates to the administration of benefit has been sent to a local authority and the Secretary of State under section 102(2) of that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

.

(3) In subsection (1), after paragraph (c) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

> - (ca (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd] a copy of a report which has been sent to a local authority under section 13A(3) of the Local Government Act 1999 and to the Secretary of State under section 13A(4A) of that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

.

(4) In subsection (2) for “invite” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd] “ require ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd].

(5) After subsection (2) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

> (2A) A requirement under subsection (2) above may specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> - (a) any information or description of information to be provided; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
> - (b) the form and manner in which the information is to be provided. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (2B) The authority must respond to a requirement under subsection (2) above before the end of such period (not less than one month after the day on which the requirement is made) as the Secretary of State specifies in the requirement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (2C) The Secretary of State may extend the period specified under subsection (2B) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

(6) For subsection (3) substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

> (3) After considering— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> - (a) the report, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
> - (b) any proposals made by the authority in response to it, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
> - (c) any other information he thinks is relevant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> the Secretary of State may give directions to the authority under subsection (3A) or (3B) or both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (3A) Directions under this subsection are directions as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> - (a) standards which the authority is to attain in the prevention and detection of fraud relating to benefit or otherwise in the administration of benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
> - (b) the time within which the standards are to be attained. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (3B) Directions under this subsection are directions to take such action as the Secretary of State thinks necessary or expedient for the purpose of improving the authority's exercise of its functions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> - (a) in relation to the prevention and detection of fraud relating to benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
> - (b) otherwise in relation to the administration of benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (3C) A direction under subsection (3B) may specify the time within which anything is to be done. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

(7) In subsection (4), for “subsection (3)” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd] “ subsection (3A) ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd].

(8) After subsection (4) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

> (4A) If the Secretary of State proposes to give a direction under this section he must give the authority to which the direction is to be addressed an opportunity to make representations about the proposed direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (4B) The Secretary of State may specify a period within which representations mentioned in subsection (4A) above must be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (4C) The Secretary of State may extend a period specified under subsection (4B) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (4D) Subsections (4A) to (4C) do not apply if the Secretary of State thinks that it is necessary for a direction to be given as a matter of urgency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (4E) If the Secretary of State acts under subsection (4D) he must give in writing to the authority to which the direction is addressed his reasons for doing so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

(9) After section 139D of that Act insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

> **139DA (affected by repeal, see note). Directions: variation and revocation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd][^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]**
>
> (1) The Secretary of State may at any time in accordance with this section vary or revoke a direction under section 139D above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (2) A direction may be varied or revoked only if the Secretary of State thinks it is necessary to do so— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> - (a) in consequence of representations made by the authority to which the direction is addressed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
> - (b) to rectify an omission or error, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
> - (c) in consequence of a material change in circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (3) The Secretary of State must not vary a direction unless he first— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> - (a) sends a copy of the proposed variation to the authority concerned, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
> - (b) gives the authority his reasons for making the variation, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
> - (c) gives the authority an opportunity to make representations about the proposed variation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (4) The Secretary of State may specify a period of not less than one month within which representations mentioned in subsection (3)(c) above must be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (5) The Secretary of State may extend a period specified under subsection (4) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
