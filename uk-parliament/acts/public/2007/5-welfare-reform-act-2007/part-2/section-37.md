# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 37

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Payment of housing benefit[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 134 of the Administration Act (arrangements for housing benefit), for subsection (2) substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]

> (2) Housing benefit is to be paid in such manner as is prescribed, and regulations may, in particular, provide for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> - (a) a payment or payments by the authority administering the benefit to the person entitled to it (E), to some other person on E's behalf or in respect of a liability which E has, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
> - (b) a reduction in the amount of any payments which E is liable to make to the authority by way of rent, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
> - (c) such a payment or payments and such a reduction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (2A) In any enactment or instrument (whenever passed or made) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]**pay *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]**” in relation to housing benefit includes discharge in any manner prescribed under subsection (2) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
>
> (2B) Subsection (2) above does not affect any power under section 5 above to make provision in relation to the payment of benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b64d8285c8973a8dd7438de786e739bd]
