# Welfare Reform Act 2007, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Section 32: supplemental[^c-86b9c3791589e17ae08ea3624650f6d9][^c-85b232a9d376405e4953f3278f633a5a][^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The administration provisions apply in relation to housing benefit or council tax benefit to which a person is entitled by virtue of subsection (2) of section 32 subject to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

- (a) subsections (4), (5) and (6) of that section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
- (b) any prescribed modifications of those provisions which the Secretary of State thinks are necessary or expedient in connection with such housing benefit or council tax benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

(2) Modifications under subsection (1)(b) may, in particular, provide that housing benefit or council tax benefit to which a person is entitled by virtue of section 32(2) must or may take the form of a payment by the appropriate authority to another local authority in prescribed circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

(3) In this section the administration provisions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

- (a) the Administration Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
- (b) subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978 (c. 30)) made in pursuance of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

(4) The power to make regulations under this section or section 32 is exercisable by the Secretary of State by statutory instrument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

(5) A statutory instrument containing regulations under this section or section 32 is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

(6) Section 175(3) to (7) of the Contributions and Benefits Act (supplemental provision as to regulations) applies in relation to regulations under this section and section 32 above as it applies in relation to regulations under that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

(7) In section 170 of the Administration Act (Social Security Advisory Committee), in subsection (5)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

- (a) in the definition of “the relevant enactments”, after paragraph (ai) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

  > - (aj (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97] sections 32 and 33 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

  ;
- (b) in the definition of “the relevant Northern Ireland enactments”, after paragraph (ai) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

  > - (aj (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97] any provisions in Northern Ireland which correspond to sections 32 and 33 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

  .

(8) For the purposes of any enactment other than a relevant enactment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

- (a) entitlement to housing benefit by virtue of section 32(2) above is to be treated as entitlement under section 130 of the Contributions and Benefits Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
- (b) entitlement to council tax benefit by virtue of section 32(2) above is to be treated as entitlement under section 131 of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

(9) In subsection (8), the relevant enactments are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]

- (a) the administration provisions, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
- (b) Part 7 of the Contributions and Benefits Act, except sections 123 and 134(2) and (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3bea21ff05489d31fd0ba93fb0bee97]
