# Mental Health Act 2007, section 52

> Text as at 2019-05-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Meaning of “1983 Act”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act “**the 1983 Act**” means the Mental Health Act 1983 (c. 20).
