# Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Membership of redress schemes

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-8337f38ab72fa251519246c295e268a1] Schedule 6 (which amends the Estate Agents Act 1979 (c. 38) to make provision for redress schemes dealing with complaints about estate agents) has effect.

(2)[^key-2a613230b0b8e2ea9be4d8f0be7d65ff] In section 3(1) of the Estate Agents Act 1979 (grounds for making prohibition orders), after paragraph (c) insert—

> - (ca) has engaged in estate agency work in relation to residential property in breach of the duty imposed by an order under section 23A(1) below; or

.

(3)[^key-0b62621300ff9c90f9c8a930281745ce] Sections 172 to 174 of the Housing Act 2004 (c. 34) (redress schemes dealing with complaints about the activities of estate agents in relation to home information packs) are omitted.
