# Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. General powers of investigation[^c-16b7862e8325778664d4b0f5105cf13c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The GCCNI[^c-f72ce8cf3a0f264be2d80e0cd09f6a98] may investigate—

- (a) a complaint made by or on behalf of a consumer which appears to the GCCNI[^c-0f1b46f04a42d980c34d3acfabc0b689] to raise one or more issues of general relevance concerning consumer matters that relate to postal services in Northern Ireland.[^c-dcb100272d400652f428e6da7cd594f3]
- (b) any matter which appears to the GCCNI[^c-6a7ec9b79a9ba15f12595193e26d5b44] to be, or to be related to, a problem which affects or may affect consumers of postal services in Northern Ireland.[^c-ff1d37de71d94a2abe38aec7cb5e63ba]

(2) For this purpose, a complaint raises an issue of general relevance if it raises—

- (a) a novel issue which affects or may affect consumers generally or consumers of a particular description, or
- (b) any other issue which has or may have an important effect on consumers generally or consumers of a particular description.
