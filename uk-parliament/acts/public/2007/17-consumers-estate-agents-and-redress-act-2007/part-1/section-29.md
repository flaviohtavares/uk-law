# Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Disclosure of information[^key-a1999a29d9aa72d98c7779e5678441ab][^key-a55ad460b0829a4689bc949c4dfc7f38][^key-6340f2f6a2d82dee9d7592ce09b80e30]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Schedule 14 to the Enterprise Act 2002 (c. 40) (restrictions on disclosure: list of enactments under or by virtue of which information is obtained), at the appropriate place in the list insert— “ Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007. ”

(2) In Schedule 15 to that Act (enactments in relation to which disclosure may be made), at the appropriate place in the list insert— “ Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007. ”

(3) For the purposes of Part 9 of the Enterprise Act 2002 (c. 40) (information) the following information is to be regarded as “specified information” within the meaning of that Part—

- (a) information obtained by Citizens Advice[^key-f9d14620c9c8ef8b253153971a5accef] , Citizens Advice Scotland or Consumer Scotland[^key-3f1b589872b652aecf36435dd2cba7c8] under or by virtue of Part 1 of the the Gas Act 1986 (c. 44), Part 1 of the Electricity Act 1989 (c. 29) , the Utilities Act[^key-64e5427ce47aa1bc7c0d4b607291a2e9] [2000 (c. 27)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/27) or Part 8 of the Energy Act [2023 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/52);
- (b) information obtained by the consumer advocacy bodies[^key-f184d97e52dd34cb03348e4b66a20cde] under or by virtue of the Postal Services Act 2011[^key-66274a91a12072180ac3285f5af26e74].

(3A)[^key-1365ac9b24c9d106c7a95a5c1cc863ec] Citizens Advice and Citizens Advice Scotland are to be treated as public authorities for the purposes of section 238(1) of the Enterprise Act 2002 only so far as regards functions conferred on the body in question under or by virtue of—

- (a) the enactments mentioned in subsection (3), or
- (b) this Act.

(3B) If and so far as a relevant function is exercisable by Citizens Advice , Citizens Advice Scotland or Consumer Scotland[^key-4f56fa43aa96766674659500a3d851b7] it is to be regarded as a function of that body under this Act for the purpose of enabling that body to receive information under section 241(3) of the Enterprise Act 2002 (disclosure to facilitate the exercise of another person’s function).

(3C) “Relevant function” means , in relation to Citizens Advice or Citizens Advice Scotland,[^key-bbbb79908567b0a55637ce5c1d439daf] a function that—

- (a) is not conferred by or under an enactment, and
- (b) corresponds to a function conferred on the GCCNI under section 8, 9, 10, 11 or 19, disregarding for these purposes the limitations relating to postal services in Northern Ireland in sections 1(4) and 11(1).

and, in relation to Consumer Scotland, a function conferred under section 3, 4 or 5 of the Consumer Scotland Act 2020 (asp 11).[^key-e7087f1d38b078922eddb1dba633ea50]

(4) Part 9 of the Enterprise Act 2002 (which among other things restricts the disclosure of certain information) does not limit the information which may be—

- (a) made available by the GCCNI[^key-57eed3d2170fcf366b9e728a2876bb01] under section 8 or 10,
- (b) included in, or made public as part of, a report of the GCCNI[^key-174065216f0eec64a760ee593efaedc4] under any provision of this Part,
- (c) published by the GCCNI[^key-da6c58eb82e6de061185261867f9e200] under section 19(2) [^key-64239ddf82807e6a6415c7a791c10e5d] ...,
- (ca)[^key-f31bb3a0692f547442c93c30fc2350fe] published or made available by Citizens Advice or[^key-1c949e20cde38eba81b04d78f075afd2] Consumer Scotland[^key-1e115c69220154f3603d1034d2254516] under subsection (7) or (8)[^key-982e744c766f8d7213986efed3f524a7] of section 19A,
- (cb) published by Citizens Advice[^key-cab3e1606e041723f14c9e1e08ddbbb9] , Citizens Advice Scotland or Consumer Scotland[^key-dc175c8e59e6eb05d81819758689b6e5] under section 24(8),
- (d) published by an authorised person under section 27(7),
- (e) published by a consumer advocacy body[^key-a5ebfe962096b0795d500e1470820caa] under section 45, or
- (f) published by Citizens Advice or Citizens Advice Scotland[^key-d615a5ec061e0d99ec0ba364820472e3] under section 33DA of the Gas Act 1986 or section 42AA of the Electricity Act 1989.

(5) Before disclosing any specified information by virtue of subsection (4) (other than by publishing it as mentioned in subsection (4)(e) or (f)), the consumer advocacy body in question or the[^key-aa0f84b1a9da9516da5d0376bdb9fe75] authorised person must consult—

- (a) if the information relates to the affairs of an individual, that individual, and
- (b) if the information relates to the business of an undertaking, the person for the time being carrying on the business.

(6) Before disclosing any specified information by virtue of subsection (4) (other than by publishing it as mentioned in subsection (4)(e) or (f)), the consumer advocacy body in question or the[^key-c18b261951be3d41aaa7c819b2ac5d05] authorised person must also have regard to the considerations set out in subsections (2) to (4) of section 244 of the Enterprise Act 2002.

For this purpose, references to “the authority” in those subsections are to be read as references to the consumer advocacy body in question or the the authorised person, as appropriate.

(7) In this section—

- “ **authorised person** ” has the same meaning as in section 27;
- “ **specified information** ” has the meaning given by section 238(1) of the Enterprise Act 2002.
