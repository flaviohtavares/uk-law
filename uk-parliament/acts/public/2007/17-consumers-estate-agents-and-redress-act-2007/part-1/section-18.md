# Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Secretary of State's power to require reports[^c-294327e6249fdc0b7551aa7472173370]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may direct the GCCNI[^c-9f4876e694313a907d8efaf6fce73d1a] to prepare, and submit to the Secretary of State within a specified period, a report in respect of any matter specified in the direction which relates to consumer matters which relate to postal services in Northern Ireland[^c-0713609298f4fc75b2e667deeba4ea71].

(2) The Secretary of State may publish any report submitted under this section.
