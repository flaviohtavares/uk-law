# Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/17

## 16. Investigations relating to public post offices[^key-3b7bb67eec1a30704f9b053ae394dde2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-90c7f82d928d8311a3ffa6380b506243] Without prejudice to the generality of section 11—

- (a) Citizens Advice , Citizens Advice Scotland and Consumer Scotland[^key-d93738ee330ee61f31869ce5839ec0cd] may investigate any matter relating to the number and location of public post offices in England, Wales and Scotland;
- (b) the GCCNI may investigate any matter relating to the number and location of public post offices in Northern Ireland.

(2) In this section “ **public post office** ” has the same meaning as in the Postal Services Act 2000 (c. 26) (see section 125(1)[^key-7d60c0e7cc48123c597602e67faa10f7] of that Act).
