# Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007, section 6A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6A. Exercise of functions of consumer advocacy bodies[^c-b5f8b9ff960a6d0c2f9f7c66c8f570eb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to section 13 (investigation of complaints relating to the disconnection of gas or electricity), nothing in this Part imposes on a consumer advocacy body a duty to exercise any of its functions on behalf of or at the request of a particular consumer.
