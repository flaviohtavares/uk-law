# Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/17

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Reports by the GCCNI[^c-1e18b1e3efe4be5983278f512eee6750][^c-ba1a1ca94f20170005234428305fb61d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The GCCNI[^c-030b7b58df858903a4c0f0247755283f] may prepare a report in relation to any matter falling within the scope of its functions under this Act.[^c-99384aa7df3e4c5244b0b16c9c900dd1]

(2) The GCCNI[^c-b2bea9d609b7b282efe9981282c807c6] may publish any report prepared under this section.
