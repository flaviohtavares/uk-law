# Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. The information function

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The GCCNI[^c-4587c19ef78b96fae0b830bc8d48f1f3] may facilitate the dissemination to consumers of advice and information—

- (a)[^c-55319bf9d9141a399241c9e968c3b592] about the GCCNI[^c-4696d6760a35f5a175b9f5b68d6f09f1] and its functions,
- (b)[^c-ecd94bb48f00236b2224955b595c5102] about consumer matters, and
- (c)[^c-ecd94bb48f00236b2224955b595c5102] about such other matters as may be prescribed by the Secretary of State by order.

(2)[^c-55319bf9d9141a399241c9e968c3b592] In exercising the power conferred by subsection (1) the GCCNI[^c-0d11e07afefc401714330b5c9a62feef] may (among other things)—

- (a) publish or otherwise make available information in any manner the GCCNI[^c-ae76cc30499c7262cba64d9a3d068678] thinks appropriate for the purpose of bringing it to the attention of those likely to be interested;
- (b) support (financially or otherwise), facilitate or co-ordinate the activities of other persons.
