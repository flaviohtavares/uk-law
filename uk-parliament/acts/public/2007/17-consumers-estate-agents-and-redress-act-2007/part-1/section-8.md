# Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/17

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. The representative function[^key-42e7b6f74089a34d43e123ace7d31e13]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The GCCNI[^key-2812d939312d457311ead9b182a78f93] may—

- (a) provide advice and information to persons within subsection (2) about consumer matters,
- (b) make proposals to such persons about consumer matters, and
- (c) represent the views of consumers on consumer matters to such persons.

(2) Those persons are—

- (a) any Minister of the Crown or government department;
- (b) the Scottish Ministers;
- (c) the Welsh Ministers;
- (d) any regulatory body established by or under an enactment;
- (e) the European Commission or any other international organisation;
- (f) any other person whom the GCCNI[^key-c00cb2f00506a4a5de4259f8dd63e671] considers might have an interest in the matter in question.

(3) In this section “ **enactment** ” means—

- (a) an Act of Parliament,
- (b) an Act of the Scottish Parliament,
- (c) a Measure or Act of the National Assembly for Wales, or
- (d) Northern Ireland legislation,

whenever passed or made.
