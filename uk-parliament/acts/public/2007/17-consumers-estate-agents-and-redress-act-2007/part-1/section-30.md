# Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Abolition of “Energywatch” and “Postwatch”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-495738c8a8536075230becc33af45a27] The Gas and Electricity Consumer Council is abolished.

(2)[^key-495738c8a8536075230becc33af45a27] The Consumer Council for Postal Services is abolished.

(3)[^key-495738c8a8536075230becc33af45a27] Subject to any modifications made by this Act—

- (a) the functions of the Gas and Electricity Consumer Council under the Gas Act 1986 (c. 44), the Electricity Act 1989 (c. 29) and the Utilities Act 2000 (c. 27), and
- (b) the functions of the Consumer Council for Postal Services under the Postal Services Act 2000 (c. 26),

are transferred by this section to the Council.

(4) Accordingly—

- (a)[^key-495738c8a8536075230becc33af45a27] in section 66 of the Gas Act 1986 (general interpretation), for the definition of “the Council” substitute—

  > “ **the Council** ” means the National Consumer Council;

  ,
- (b)[^key-495738c8a8536075230becc33af45a27] in section 111(1) of the Electricity Act 1989 (general interpretation), for the definition of “the Council” substitute—

  > “ **the Council** ” means the National Consumer Council;

  ,
- (c)[^key-229b9e7dab58153d4af300857e25dc96][^key-f1bddb0c379d94066231a9fe1f123bb6] in section 125(1) of the Postal Services Act 2000 (interpretation), after the definition of “correspondent” insert—

  > “ **the Council** ” means the National Consumer Council,

  , and
- (d)[^key-3509c7616baa4cdc7b4ccdfb3361be90][^key-f1bddb0c379d94066231a9fe1f123bb6] in section 106(1) of the Utilities Act 2000 (interpretation), for the definition of “Council” substitute—

  > “ **Council** ” means the National Consumer Council;

  .

(5)[^key-495738c8a8536075230becc33af45a27] Schedule 3 contains transitional provisions.
