# Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007, section 15

## 15. Reference of postal matters to OFCOM[^c-f566d23b52d3280fffad0f13cc716a92][^c-593a60c884a86922e3ee5d2aa705d70e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A consumer advocacy body[^c-3fd37023656352d0dafb3e864a6b7ce1] must refer any complaint within section 11(1)(a), or to which section 12(3) applies, to the Office of Communications (“OFCOM”)[^c-95ecfe5087f106ea3f4199547f535d22] if that consumer advocacy body[^c-87a66e36fba2347344160abb31d98df2] considers that—

- (a) the subject matter of the complaint indicates that a regulatory condition imposed under Part 3 of the Postal Services Act 2011[^c-0ca38683f1b965f089c38e9f9e27ed8c] has been contravened,
- (b) the subject matter of the complaint is a referable matter, or
- (c) it is appropriate to do so.

(2) The consumer advocacy bodies[^c-242730550184f0409079891753b13d59] and OFCOM[^c-d969921e74369348fb37516dd2fbc56c] must, from time to time, agree the descriptions of matters which are to be referred to OFCOM and, for the purposes of subsection (1)(b), a matter is a “referable matter” if it is of a description for the time being so agreed.
