# Finance Act 2007, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Charge and rates for 2007-08

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Income tax is charged for the tax year 2007-08; and for that tax year—

- (a) the starting rate is 10%,
- (b) the basic rate is 22%, and
- (c) the higher rate is 40%.
