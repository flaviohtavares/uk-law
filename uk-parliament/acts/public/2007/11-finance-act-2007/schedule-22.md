# Finance Act 2007, SCHEDULE 22

## SCHEDULE 22 — Amendments and repeals consequential on extension of HMRC powers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 84

### Part 1 — Amendments

**1.**[^key-b32afa94f104a762f22ba8cba8cc65bd] In section 20D(1) of TMA 1970, for “sections 20A, 20BA and 20C” substitute “ sections 20A and 20BA ”.

**2.**[^key-acbb399b212b02b90b2a2f5e4f894bfe] In section 67 of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (c. 16) and the heading of that section, for “customs officers” substitute “ officers of Revenue and Customs ”.

### Part 2 — Repeals

**3.**[^key-47bbdbea53f947eeb7305dbd73204fdf] The provisions listed below are omitted.

**4.**[^key-3bfb56ec8940b52dd0a6ae4f83bbea5c] In TMA 1970—

- (a) sections 20C and 20CC (search warrants), and
- (b) in the definition of “tax” in section 118 the word “, 20C”.

**5.**[^key-457da292048b534f1f570ee0621a8d26] In CEMA 1979—

- (a) section 118C(3)(c) (gaming duty), and
- (b) the references to “a gaming duty offence” in section 118C(4)(b) and (5).

**6.**[^key-71eb062b9735186628a44fb6d48b7efd] In BGDA 1981—

- (a) paragraph 16 of Schedule 1 (general betting duty: search warrants),
- (b) paragraph 17 of Schedule 3 (bingo duty: search warrants), and
- (c) paragraph 17 of Schedule 4 (amusement machine licence duty: search warrants).

**7.**[^key-c47d75d9186410462b09894e28271b79] Section 148(4) of FA 1989 (definition of “business” for purposes of section 20C of TMA 1970).

**8.**[^key-9c1f20b833984d3544b927f4680e2c34] In VATA 1994—

- (a) section 72(9) (powers of arrest), and
- (b) paragraph 10(3) to (6) of Schedule 11 (search warrants).

**9.**[^key-8c4ac8e16d8901ba56561c2d0d981592] In Schedule 7 to FA 1994 (insurance premium tax)—

- (a) paragraph 4(2) to (5) (search warrants), and
- (b) paragraph 4(6) and (7) (power of arrest).

**10.**[^key-bff57c4d43ec22fccfa6624a5d6d886b] In Schedule 5 to FA 1996 (landfill tax)—

- (a) paragraph 5 (search warrants), and
- (b) paragraph 6 (power of arrest).

**11.**[^key-1cdf97dc9db82adfef43e778d81b3e02] In Schedule 6 to FA 2000 (climate change levy)—

- (a) paragraph 97 (power of arrest), and
- (b) paragraph 130 (search warrants).

**12.**[^key-92eab80bb1e667aa40acec7ce8d4fd1a] In FA 2001 (aggregates levy)—

- (a) paragraph 6 of Schedule 6 (power of arrest), and
- (b) paragraph 7 of Schedule 7 (search warrants).

**13.**[^key-925e46e55ce5fa211b37781b59b3c23a] (1) In the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001—

- (a) section 57(1)(c) (section 20CC of TMA 1970),
- (b) section 63(2)(e) (section 20C of TMA 1970), and
- (c) section 65(3) (section 20C of TMA 1970).

(2) In Schedule 1 to that Act—

- (a) paragraph 13 (section 20C of TMA 1970),
- (b) paragraph 28 (paragraph 17(2) of Schedule 3 to BGDA 1981),
- (c) paragraph 29 (paragraph 17(2) of Schedule 4 to BDGA 1981),
- (d) paragraph 57 (paragraph 4(3) of Schedule 7 to FA 1994),
- (e) paragraph 58 (paragraph 10(3) of Schedule 11 to VATA 1994),
- (f) paragraph 61 (paragraph 5(2) of Schedule 5 to FA 1996), and
- (g) paragraph 72 (paragraph 130(2) of Schedule 6 to FA 2000).

**14.**[^key-755610aed8b8ffb58ea1ca267efae2ce] Section 36(2) and (3) of the Tax Credits Act 2002 (c. 21) (search warrants).

**15.**[^key-73887fec475a6eed97d37f297faef9b6] Section 323(3)(e) and (f) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (c. 29) (approval of applications under section 20C of TMA 1970).

**16.**[^key-f58b26c5444a870496e1d2ac84017852] Part 7 of Schedule 13 to FA 2003 (stamp duty land tax: search warrants).

**17.**[^key-2a3df1db5d53931a3198ee4f98cb5e26] In CRCA 2005—

- (a) section 13(3)(b) and (c) (Commissioners' functions not delegable to officers), and
- (b) section 14(2)(b) and (c) (non-delegable functions of Commissioners).
