# Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007, section 6

## 6. Local Government Boundary Commission's procedure[^c-b18ae4eaaf45be3ca43475942875a94e][^c-8e5ce2e9f9de5f28184f1e5dbbf56d7c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority must if requested by the Local Government Boundary Commission[^c-50eadd08716ce5d7a58782279159e27f] to do so provide the Commission[^c-60b7f652397f0408042c8e1fda34221e], by such date as the Commission may specify, with any information that the Commission may reasonably require in connection with any of their functions under section 5.

(2) In making a recommendation or alternative proposal under section 5 the Local Government Boundary Commission[^c-cd7d5721f25e38ed08c3e261c64ea580] must have regard to any guidance from the Secretary of State about the exercise of the Commission's[^c-f912cc5c64130577b5445623d9842cd9] functions under that section.

(3) Any recommendation or alternative proposal under section 5 must be made no later than the relevant date.

(4) Before making an alternative proposal under section 5(3)(c) the Local Government Boundary Commission[^c-473fbef39239ff9df03d9952262a7ee0] must—

- (a) publish a draft of the proposal; and
- (b) take such steps as they consider sufficient to secure that persons who may be interested are informed of—
  - (i) the draft proposal; and
  - (ii) the period within which representations about it may be made to the Commission[^c-d3c4cf24f76695868c3f60a8fbb76f0e].

(5) The Local Government Boundary Commission[^c-4f47343cc7ab25ea4c686940d3597e55]—

- (a) must take into account any representations made to them within that period; and
- (b) if they make any proposal to the Secretary of State, must inform any person who made such representations—
  - (i) of the proposal made; and
  - (ii) that representations about the proposal may be made to the Secretary of State until the end of the relevant period.

(6) In subsection (5)(b) “**the relevant period**” means four weeks beginning with the relevant date.

(7) In this section and section 7 “**the relevant date**” means the date specified in the request under section 4(2) (or, if a later date is substituted under section 4(3), the date substituted (or last substituted) under that provision).
