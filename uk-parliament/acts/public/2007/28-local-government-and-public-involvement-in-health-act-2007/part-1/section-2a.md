# Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007, section 2A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2A. Proposals for merger of single tiers of local government[^c-d80f9462c70d9b09544570afcef8ea7a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may invite or direct a relevant principal authority to make a proposal that there should be one or more single tiers of local government for an area which currently consists of—

- (a) the area of that authority, and
- (b) one or more other eligible areas.

(2) A principal authority is a “**relevant principal authority**” for the purposes of subsection (1) if it is the district or county council for an area for which there is currently a single tier of local government.

(3) An “eligible area” is an area for which there is a single tier of local government.

(4) An invitation or direction may—

- (a) be such that the authority may choose which eligible areas (other than the area of that authority) should form the proposed area for the new single tier of local government;
- (b) specify which eligible areas (other than the area of that authority) should form the proposed area for the new single tier of local government.
