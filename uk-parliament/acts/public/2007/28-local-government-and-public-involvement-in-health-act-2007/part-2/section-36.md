# Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Notice to Local Government Boundary Commission for England[^c-92347951a74c473300c23e342fa84483][^c-7c061b3a3822dc02a9e2c65d1eebd348]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A council must comply with this section as soon as practicable after passing a resolution for whole-council elections.

(2) The council must give the Local Government Boundary Commission[^c-866bfca2f49389e745b13d3b60a79a48] notice that it has passed the resolution.

(3)[^c-2bcfd46a643adf2c1d7708bab4d3ca39] In this Chapter, “**Local Government Boundary Commission**” means the Local Government Boundary Commission for England.
