# Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Notice to Local Government Boundary Commission[^key-cafa04ceee7decb9203d2c616db8a08f][^key-f9531e4a5e08f27ffa076dd600c6ee45]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A council must comply with this section as soon as practicable after passing a resolution for elections by halves or a resolution for elections by thirds.

(2) The council must give the Local Government Boundary Commission[^key-734696c83a0b1501cc0c3e92617be23d] notice that it has passed the resolution.
