# Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Local Government Boundary Commission to make order for new electoral scheme[^c-c1f524f24864c65c8162e9ddc62db576][^c-5aad2065e327a868ef5d0f16deb5e46d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the Local Government Boundary Commission[^c-26d4a86ad44263eef2ec21f641c06d9d] receive notice under section 42 that a council has passed a resolution, they must—

- (a) in the case of a resolution for elections by halves, make an order for elections by halves in relation to the council (see sections 45 and 46);
- (b) in the case of a resolution for elections by thirds, make an order for elections by thirds in relation to the council (see sections 47 and 48).

(2)[^c-099a9a926ac351c79b15bbad52dffb76] But the Local Government Boundary Commission must not make the order—

- (a) before it has decided whether or not conduct an electoral review (see section 43(2)), and
- (b) if it has decided to conduct such a review, before the review is concluded.
