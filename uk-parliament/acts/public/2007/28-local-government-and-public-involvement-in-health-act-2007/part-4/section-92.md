# Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007, section 92

## 92. County, district or London borough: consequential recommendations[^key-eb53e972892c878ffe0ebeb7309057ba][^key-acbbab3670b34f48dd6e95b5aee6d190][^key-749a1dc04c73c32d3b35d57477750753][^key-0c53550bd68f3c1a4ee54d44ba90705d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if a community governance review makes recommendations under any other provision of this Chapter.

(2) The review may make recommendations to the Local Government Boundary Commission[^key-c9f1bff112fdbd8d8000f6e26bec59be] as to what related alteration (if any) should be made to the boundaries of the electoral areas of any affected principal council.

(3) The Local Government Boundary Commission[^key-024d58e0689e64f9ad2142805f977a97] may by order give effect to recommendations made under subsection (2).

(4) The Local Government Boundary Commission[^key-429ef8f96588539c96210e62bb56efe1] must notify each relevant principal council of whether or not the Commission have given effect to recommendations made under subsection (2).

(5) If the Local Government Boundary Commission[^key-a0269fe8ebdf792205a37ace0585173e] have given effect to the recommendations, they must also send each relevant principal council two copies of the order under this section.

(6) In this section—

- “**affected principal council**” means any principal council whose area the community governance review relates to (including the council carrying out the review);
- “**related**” means related to the other recommendations made under this Chapter.
- “**relevant principal council**”, in relation to recommendations under subsection (2), means—
  - (a) the principal council that made the recommendations, and
  - (b) if the recommendations are made by a district council for an area for which there is a county council, the county council.
