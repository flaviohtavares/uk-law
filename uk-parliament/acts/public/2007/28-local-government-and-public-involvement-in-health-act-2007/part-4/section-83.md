# Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007, section 83

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. No review being undertaken: duty to respond to petition or application[^c-6214ed9c31b6d3b3496ec8cc4aefafc2][^c-34bd74c656c2a6b27fd596ba8af98bd6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if these conditions are met—

- (a) a principal council is not in the course of undertaking a community governance review;
- (b) the council receives a community governance petition or community governance application[^c-f63cf58055e0dfd01afbc5bb9551c7f6] which relates to the whole or part of the council's area.

(2) The principal council must undertake a community governance review that has terms of reference that allow for the petition or application[^c-2e6d6426d9e97ef2740c7ad6c54e45e2] to be considered.

(3) But the duty in subsection (2) does not apply if—

- (a) the principal council has concluded a previous community governance review within the relevant two-year period, and
- (b) in the council's opinion the petition area or application area[^c-5c12e6f6777f1dbff8bbb25ce0ce359c] covers the whole or a significant part of the area to which the previous review related.

For further provision about this case, see section 85.
