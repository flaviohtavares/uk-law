# Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007, section 183

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 183. Conduct that may be covered by code

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-f65f7534d530efdb8427e738f303ddf7] In section 49 of the Local Government Act 2000 (c. 22) (principles governing conduct of members of relevant authorities), after subsection (2) insert—

> (2A)[^c-33a77eab3dce1738dfd4c1ad491848e5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> (2B)[^c-33a77eab3dce1738dfd4c1ad491848e5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> (2C) An order under subsection (1) may define, for the purposes of the order—
>
> - “official capacity”; and
> - “criminal offence”.
>
> (2D) An order under subsection (2)—
>
> - (a) may specify principles which are to apply to a person at all times;
> - (b) may specify principles which are to apply to a person otherwise than at all times.

(2)[^c-7e9c2838bc688dba4326f4b0e7fd6c13] In section 50 of that Act (model code of conduct), after subsection (4) insert—

> (4A)[^c-988e1486771663165cce20d7daf9e1d5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> (4B)[^c-988e1486771663165cce20d7daf9e1d5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> (4C) A model code of conduct issued under subsection (1) may define for the purposes of the code—
>
> - “official capacity”; and
> - “criminal offence”.
>
> (4D) Provision included under subsection (4A) or (4C) in a model code of conduct—
>
> - (a) must be consistent with the provision for the time being included in an order under section 49(1) by virtue of section 49(2A) or (2C);
> - (b) is to be mandatory except to the extent that it relates to an optional provision;
> - (c) to the extent that it relates to an optional provision, is to be mandatory where that optional provision is incorporated in a code of conduct under section 51.
>
> (4E) A model code of conduct issued under subsection (2) may include—
>
> - (a) provisions which are to apply to a person at all times;
> - (b) provisions which are to apply to a person otherwise than at all times.

(3)[^c-4d062ab82917a4a93fb89cdc00d4503f] In section 51 of that Act (duty of relevant authorities to adopt codes of conduct), after subsection (4) insert—

> (4A)[^c-ef681998778b0496a76bd6194b302b2f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> (4B)[^c-ef681998778b0496a76bd6194b302b2f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> (4C) The provisions which may be included under subsection (4)(c) by a relevant authority in Wales other than a police authority include—
>
> - (a) provisions which are to apply to a person at all times;
> - (b) provisions which are to apply to a person otherwise than at all times.

(4)[^c-86b21b304ca01bde2d44db9a969f7dff] In section 52 of that Act (duty to comply with code of conduct), in each of subsections (1) to (4), omit the words “in performing his functions”.

(5)[^c-86b21b304ca01bde2d44db9a969f7dff] Subsection (6) below applies where immediately before the commencement date a person is a member or co-opted member of a relevant authority and not prevented by any provision of section 52 of that Act from acting as such.

(6)[^c-86b21b304ca01bde2d44db9a969f7dff] The coming into force of this section shall not have the effect of preventing the person from acting as a member or co-opted member of the relevant authority, but—

- (a) he must before the end of the prescribed period give to that authority a written undertaking that he will observe the authority's code of conduct for the time being; and
- (b) if he fails to comply with paragraph (a), he shall cease to be a member or co-opted member at the end of that period.

(7)[^c-3b2beccf5e1428ec8b8c71c2f55a7626] With effect from the commencement date—

- (a) any order under section 49(2) or 50(2) of the Local Government Act 2000 (c. 22), and
- (b) any code of conduct of a relevant authority [^c-981f0abc4961cce6d8bfb9f6b38bbdc2]...,

which is in force immediately before that date shall have effect as if the amendments made by this section had always had effect.

(8)[^c-3b2beccf5e1428ec8b8c71c2f55a7626] Subsection (7) does not affect the operation of any order or code in relation to any time before the commencement date.

(9)[^c-3b2beccf5e1428ec8b8c71c2f55a7626] In this section the following expressions have the same meanings as in Part 3 of the Local Government Act 2000—

- “code of conduct”;
- “co-opted member”;
- “member of a relevant authority”;
- “relevant authority”.

(10)[^c-3b2beccf5e1428ec8b8c71c2f55a7626] References in subsections (6) to (8) to the code of conduct of a relevant authority include, in relation to a relevant authority whose members and co-opted members are subject to mandatory provisions by virtue of section 51(5)(b) of the Local Government Act 2000, those mandatory provisions.

(11)[^c-3b2beccf5e1428ec8b8c71c2f55a7626] In this section—

- “**the commencement date**” means the date this section comes into force;
- “**the prescribed period**” means such period, beginning with the commencement date, as may be prescribed for the purposes of subsection (6) by order made by the Secretary of State.
