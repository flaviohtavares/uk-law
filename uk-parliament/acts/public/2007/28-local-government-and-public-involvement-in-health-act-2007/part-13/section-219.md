# Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007, section 219

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 219. Establishment of the Tribunal[^c-f1b44dde97def33f94e79f25280279e3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-42c529dd4e6fde9c9e90617226265b08][^c-5ee7ff5b043cc93905562b8a1a0f4721] Schedule 15 (which establishes the Valuation Tribunal for England, and makes consequential provision) has effect.

(2)[^c-7250803fd1c78ed7f3dbb54b3b33049d] The existing English tribunals are abolished.

(3)[^c-7250803fd1c78ed7f3dbb54b3b33049d] In this Part “**existing English tribunals**” means the valuation tribunals established in relation to England by regulations under Schedule 11 to the Local Government Finance Act 1988 (c. 41) which are in existence immediately before the transfer of jurisdiction takes place.

(4)[^c-7250803fd1c78ed7f3dbb54b3b33049d] The transfer of jurisdiction is the transfer made by paragraph A2 of Schedule 11 to the Local Government Finance Act 1988 (as inserted by Schedule 15 to this Act).
