## SCHEDULE 6 — Byelaws: further amendments

*Extent: E+W*

Section 135

### Public Health Acts Amendment Act 1907 (c. 53)

**1.**[^c-132abdeb4aefcd8ec760ee3fcc3c8a62] In subsection (4) of section 82 of the Public Health Acts Amendment Act 1907 (byelaws relating to the seashore), omit from “Provided” to the end of the subsection.

### Public Health Act 1936 (c. 49)

**2.**[^c-aca1409c2894e5160b82ab4e1a641c75] In section 231(1) of the Public Health Act 1936 (byelaws with respect to public bathing)—

- (a) in paragraph (b), omit “bathing-machines may be stationed, or”,
- (b) in paragraph (c), omit “bathing-machines,”,
- (c) omit paragraph (d).

### Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984 (c. 22)

**3.**[^c-6599de12d77fa2a16ead36215b7ba8a4] Omit subsections (2) and (3) of section 56 of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984 (byelaws for preventing disease by the occupants or users of tents, vans, etc).

### Police Reform Act 2002 (c. 30)

**4.**[^c-1bfc16aa8ad4c806b1361b38fa77ce1c] (1)[^c-4cbc20149389e2f9b60ecc8876660163] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) In paragraph 1(3)(a) of Schedule 5 to that Act (definition of “relevant fixed penalty offence”) for “sub-paragraph (2)(a) to (c)” substitute “ sub-paragraph (2)(a) to (d) ”.
