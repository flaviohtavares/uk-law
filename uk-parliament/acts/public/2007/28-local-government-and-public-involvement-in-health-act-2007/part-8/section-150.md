## 150. Interaction of benefits inspectors with the Audit Commission[^c-ac6fdd5bf686c1e4687f5798c85b75e7]

*Extent: E+W*

After section 139B of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 (c. 5) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]

> **139BA (affected by repeal, see note). Interaction with Audit Commission (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689][^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]**
>
> (1) A person authorised under section 139A(1) must from time to time, or at such times as the Secretary of State may specify by order, prepare— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
>
> - (a) a document setting out what inspections of English authorities he proposes to carry out (an “inspection programme”); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
> - (b) a document setting out the way in which he proposes to carry out his functions of inspecting and reporting on such authorities (an “inspection framework”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
>
> (2) The person authorised under section 139A(1) must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
>
> - (a) consult the Audit Commission before preparing an inspection programme or an inspection framework; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
> - (b) once an inspection programme or inspection framework is prepared, send a copy of it to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
>   - (i) the Secretary of State; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689] the Audit Commission. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
>
> (3) The Secretary of State may by order specify the form that inspection programmes or inspection frameworks must take. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
>
> (4) A person authorised under section 139A(1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
>
> - (a) must co-operate with the Audit Commission, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
> - (b) may act jointly with the Audit Commission, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
>
> where it is appropriate to do so for the efficient and effective discharge of the person's functions in relation to English authorities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
>
> (5) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
>
> - “**the Audit Commission *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]**” means the Audit Commission for Local Authorities and the National Health Service in England; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
> - “**English authorities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]**” means authorities administering housing benefit or council tax benefit in England; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
> - “**person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]**” does not include the Audit Commission. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36c1e8d066be1759bbb84472a94f6689]
