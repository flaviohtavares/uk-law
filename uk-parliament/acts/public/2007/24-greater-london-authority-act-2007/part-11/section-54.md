# Greater London Authority Act 2007, section 54

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2018-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 54. Directions

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any directions given under this Act must be in writing.

(2) Any power conferred by this Act to give a direction includes power to vary or revoke the direction.
