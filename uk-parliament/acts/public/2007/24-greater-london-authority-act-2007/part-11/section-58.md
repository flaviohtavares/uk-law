## 58. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

In this Act—

- “**the GLA Act 1999**” means the Greater London Authority Act 1999 (c. 29);
- “**PCPA 2004**” means the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 (c. 5);
- “**TCPA 1990**” means the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (c. 8).
