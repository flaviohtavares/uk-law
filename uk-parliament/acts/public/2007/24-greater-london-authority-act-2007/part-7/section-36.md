## 36. Planning contribution under section 46 of PCPA 2004[^key-2e15568ebe72ab09406f8b02b5bb06fb]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 46 of PCPA 2004 (planning contribution), after subsection (7) insert—

> (8) The regulations may include provision for the making of a planning contribution in circumstances where the Mayor of London is the local planning authority by virtue of a direction given under section 2A of the principal Act (applications of potential strategic importance).
