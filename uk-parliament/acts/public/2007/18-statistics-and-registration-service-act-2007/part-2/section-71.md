# Statistics and Registration Service Act 2007, section 71

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Deputy superintendent registrars and registrars[^key-919290716d01732eecdb3a154bb8aeba]

*Extent: E+W*

For section 8 of the Registration Service Act 1953 (deputy superintendent registrars and registrars) substitute—

> **8. Deputy superintendent registrars and registrars**
>
> (1) Subject to the provisions of the local scheme, a council which employs a superintendent registrar or a registrar of births and deaths may appoint one or more fit persons to act as his deputy.
>
> (2) Any person appointed as a deputy under subsection (1) shall have all the powers and duties of a superintendent registrar or a registrar of births and deaths, as the case may be.
