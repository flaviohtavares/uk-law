# Statistics and Registration Service Act 2007, section 55

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Cessation of Office for National Statistics etc[^c-b0b9308714f107069ea9bdabcc3c7987]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following shall cease to function on the commencement of this section—

- (a) the Office for National Statistics;
- (b) the Statistics Commission.
