# Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007, section 124

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 124. Charges by operator of approved scheme

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The operator of an approved scheme may recover its costs by charging debtors or affected creditors (or both).

(2) In this section—

- “**costs**” means the costs which the operator incurs, taking one year with another, in connection with the approved scheme, so far as those costs are reasonable;
- “**debtors**” means—
  - (a) debtors who make requests for debt repayment plans to be arranged in accordance with the approved scheme, and
  - (b) debtors for whom debt repayment plans are arranged in accordance with the approved scheme.
