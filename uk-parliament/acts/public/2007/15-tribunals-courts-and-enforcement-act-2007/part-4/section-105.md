# Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007, section 105

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 105. Application and transitional provision

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Sections 95 to 104 apply in relation to any judgment debt, whether it became payable, or recoverable, before or after the commencement of those sections.

(2) In relation to an offence committed before 2 May 2022[^key-bd024e7e8bd4467e98e344477cb27c6e], the reference in section 102(9)(b) to 12 months is to be read as a reference to 6 months.
