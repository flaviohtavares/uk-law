## 8. Senior President of Tribunals: power to delegate

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-6b648c4ed705669af0afb36ef6b31c5f] The Senior President of Tribunals may delegate any function he has in his capacity as Senior President of Tribunals—

- (a) to any judge, or other member, of the Upper Tribunal or First-tier Tribunal;
- (b) to staff appointed under section 40(1).

(1A)[^key-a533868369af80c80c1223d61811375f] A function under paragraph 1(1) or 2(1) of Schedule 2 may be delegated under subsection (1) only to a Chamber President of a chamber of the Upper Tribunal.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to functions of the Senior President of Tribunals under any of the following—[^key-dcec7d3516e07e31c8fca5152e412ce0]

section 7(7);

section 7(9);

section 29B;[^key-003b5f455704861c9ecb1de75dbbc179]

section 29D;

paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3;

paragraph 7(1) of Schedule 3;

paragraph 2 of Schedule 4;

paragraph 5(1) and (3) of Schedule 4;

paragraph 5(5) to (8) of Schedule 4;

paragraph 5A(2)(a) of Schedule 4;

paragraph 5A(3)(a) of Schedule 4.

paragraph 3 of Schedule 5;

paragraph[^key-53dbf86b6f19400218450408c8d5f7da] 2 of Schedule A1 to the Employment Tribunals Act 1996.

(3)[^key-7bf34f79b9d1e4e8daa34c1eec6bf199] A delegation under subsection (1) is not revoked by the delegator's becoming incapacitated.

(4)[^key-7bf34f79b9d1e4e8daa34c1eec6bf199] Any delegation under subsection (1) that is in force immediately before a person ceases to be Senior President of Tribunals continues in force until varied or revoked by a subsequent holder of the office of Senior President of Tribunals.

(5)[^key-7bf34f79b9d1e4e8daa34c1eec6bf199] The delegation under this section of a function shall not prevent the exercise of the function by the Senior President of Tribunals.
