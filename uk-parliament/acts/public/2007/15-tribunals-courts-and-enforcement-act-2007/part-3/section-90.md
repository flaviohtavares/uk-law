# Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/15

## 90. Regulations[^key-6b48e533ee03408038732a58a406a8f8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part—

- “**prescribed**” means prescribed by regulations;
- “**regulations**” means regulations made by the Lord Chancellor.

(2) The following apply to regulations under this Part.

(3) Any power to make regulations is exercisable by statutory instrument.

(4)[^key-09ae048bc460125d028796b240fd4b7d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) [^key-eda5b5b44c8c9575702aae54af50e565]... a statutory instrument containing regulations is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(6) Regulations may include any of these that the Lord Chancellor considers necessary or expedient—

- (a) supplementary, incidental or consequential provision;
- (b) transitory, transitional or saving provision.

(7) Regulations may make different provision for different cases.
