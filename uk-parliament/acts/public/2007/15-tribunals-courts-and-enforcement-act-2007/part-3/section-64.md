# Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Certificates to act as an enforcement agent

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-4aec5d1a70637e0ef0ea7d42c35fde6f] A certificate may be issued under this section by a judge of the county court.[^c-a90e0208d305569dc6156238892a0c4f]

(2)[^c-0b11c029e66b77841ddebb372b4284e7][^c-aab32d9c3d387b8d1d8126ef7b6f273f] The Lord Chancellor must make regulations about certificates under this section.

(3)[^c-0b11c029e66b77841ddebb372b4284e7][^c-aab32d9c3d387b8d1d8126ef7b6f273f] The regulations may in particular include provision—

- (a) for fees to be charged for applications;
- (b) for certificates to be issued subject to conditions, including the giving of security;
- (c) for certificates to be limited to purposes specified by or under the regulations;
- (d) about complaints against holders of certificates;
- (e) about suspension and cancellation of certificates;
- (f) to modify or supplement Schedule 12 for cases where a certificate is suspended or cancelled or expires;
- (g) requiring courts to make information available relating to certificates.

(4)[^c-0b11c029e66b77841ddebb372b4284e7][^c-aab32d9c3d387b8d1d8126ef7b6f273f] A certificate under section 7 of the Law of Distress Amendment Act 1888 (c. 21) which is in force on the coming into force of this section has effect as a certificate under this section, subject to any provision made by regulations.
