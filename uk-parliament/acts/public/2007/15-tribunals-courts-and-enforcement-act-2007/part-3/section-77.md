# Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007, section 77

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 77. The rent recoverable

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-92b4a5820b83bd9688d8cc65bcd977cd] CRAR is not exercisable except to recover rent that meets each of these conditions—

- (a) it has become due and payable before notice of enforcement is given;
- (b) it is certain, or capable of being calculated with certainty;[^c-6d0429d162ec672428f6b7a6908b2efd]
- (c) it is not excluded from recovery using CRAR by paragraph 4 of Schedule 2 to the Commercial Rent (Coronavirus) Act 2022 (temporary moratorium on enforcement of protected rent debts).

(2)[^c-92b4a5820b83bd9688d8cc65bcd977cd] The amount of any rent recoverable by CRAR is reduced by any permitted deduction.

(3)[^c-92b4a5820b83bd9688d8cc65bcd977cd] CRAR is exercisable only if the net unpaid rent is at least the minimum amount immediately before each of these—

- (a) the time when notice of enforcement is given;
- (b) the first time that goods are taken control of after that notice.

(4)[^c-a9e84ba0592702463b5108bdfbb110ea][^c-bee99a575f068c4757dfaf51a0f890a6] The minimum amount is to be calculated in accordance with regulations.

(5)[^c-92b4a5820b83bd9688d8cc65bcd977cd] The net unpaid rent is the amount of rent that meets the conditions in subsection (1), less—

- (a) any interest or value added tax included in that amount under section 76(1)(a) or (b), and
- (b) any permitted deductions.

(6)[^c-a9e84ba0592702463b5108bdfbb110ea][^c-bee99a575f068c4757dfaf51a0f890a6] Regulations may provide for subsection (5)(a) not to apply in specified cases.

(7)[^c-92b4a5820b83bd9688d8cc65bcd977cd] Permitted deductions, against any rent, are any deduction, recoupment or set-off that the tenant would be entitled to claim (in law or equity) in an action by the landlord for that rent.
