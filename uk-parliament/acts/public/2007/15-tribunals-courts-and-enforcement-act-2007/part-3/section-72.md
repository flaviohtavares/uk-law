# Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007, section 72

## 72. Commercial rent arrears recovery (CRAR)[^c-63857673ef96e95c13e03dd9c5e7c257]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20734901][^c-22508ff36986afb07be254d4459f7fab] A landlord under a lease of commercial premises may use the procedure in Schedule 12 (taking control of goods) to recover from the tenant rent payable under the lease.

(2) A landlord's power under subsection (1) is referred to as CRAR (commercial rent arrears recovery).
