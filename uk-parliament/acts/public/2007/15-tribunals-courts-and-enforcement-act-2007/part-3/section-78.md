# Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007, section 78

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Intervention of the court

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-7fce5988f768c56a38347b92d736f9c6] If notice of enforcement is given in exercise (or purported exercise) of CRAR the court may make either or both of these orders on the application of the tenant—

- (a) an order setting aside the notice;
- (b) an order that no further step may be taken under CRAR, without further order, in relation to the rent claimed.

(2)[^c-d7f81460fae04c57e9892614bb50b615][^c-8194190f14bd80f25512aa598717317f] Regulations may make provision about—

- (a) the further orders that may be made for the purposes of subsection (1)(b);
- (b) grounds of which the court must be satisfied before making an order or further order.

(3)[^c-7fce5988f768c56a38347b92d736f9c6] In this section “**the court**” means the High Court or the county court[^c-f0b24eae2584ea60e94a0d7317e5c185], as rules of court may provide.
