# Income Tax Act 2007, section 721

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 721. Individuals with power to enjoy income as a result of relevant transactions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Income is treated as arising to such an individual as is mentioned in section 720(1) or 720A(1)[^M_F_98cc3b94-5b4c-42ca-c87d-a2d54b08b2d1] in a tax year for income tax purposes if conditions A to C[^c-cfb0b196123c38ec37bcde1df6d60ab2] are met.

(2) Condition A is that the individual has power in the tax year to enjoy income of a person abroad as a result of—

- (a) a relevant transfer,
- (b) one or more associated operations, or
- (c) a relevant transfer and one or more associated operations.

(3) Condition B is that the income of the person abroad[^c-003ada8ed6deda9b15a39a32b1d0a283] would be chargeable to income tax if it were the individual's and received by the individual in the United Kingdom.

(3A)[^c-96a935618e21f7d5ffe3232cc18c2d5f] Condition C is that the individual is UK resident for the tax year.

(3B)[^c-0685bae5da201c7b00cfd7517d44b555][^c-efa611cb3f3368f55c4dd9852939d77b] The amount of the income treated as arising under subsection (1) is equal to the amount of the income of the person abroad (subject to sections 724 and 725).

(3C) Subsection (1) does not apply if—

- (a) the individual is liable for income tax charged on the income of the person abroad by virtue of a charge not contained in this Chapter, and
- (b) all that income tax has been paid.

(4) For the purposes of subsection (2), it does not matter whether the income of the person abroad[^c-889cfba9fd58e4a6bc002553c7998bcb] may be enjoyed immediately or only later.

(5) It does not matter for the purposes of this section—

- (a)[^c-240a66d3cafeabbc27b6968ee40125a2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-9f510b7f0f69b54ff62e3aec60413e9e] whether the individual is UK resident for the tax year in which the relevant transfer is made (if different from the tax year mentioned in subsection (1)), or
- (c) whether the avoiding of liability to income tax is a purpose for which the transfer is effected.

(6) For the circumstances in which an individual is treated as having the power to enjoy income for the purposes of this section, see section 722.
