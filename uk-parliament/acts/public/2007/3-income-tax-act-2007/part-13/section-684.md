## 684. Person liable to counteraction of income tax advantage[^c-cdfbe83cffc90aa4547726f1205c39c7][^c-4d82f86ca38c259c580623b528000db9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to a person (“the party”)[^c-45b7c5bf457a582d28f030beaa7e1d2e] where—

- (a) the person is a party to a transaction in securities or two or more transactions in securities (see subsection (2)),
- (b) the circumstances are covered by section 685 and not excluded by section 686,
- (c) the main purpose, or one of the main purposes, of [^c-f98f47ee164f26e49eeb29b49937e2db]... the transaction in securities, or any of the transactions in securities, is to obtain an income tax advantage, and
- (d) the party or any other person[^c-d697aadbe08de63a6d0a2c5a59c3b805] obtains an income tax advantage in consequence of the transaction or the combined effect of the transactions.

(2) In this Chapter “**transaction in securities**” means a transaction, of whatever description, relating to securities, and includes in particular—

- (a) the purchase, sale or exchange of securities,
- (b) issuing or securing the issue of new securities,
- (c) applying or subscribing for new securities, [^c-5078b93e3f192627bdff668eb9a5b3da]...
- (d) altering or securing the alteration of the rights attached to securities.
- (e)[^c-6d12a5e0661b0e66253400086d950796] a repayment of share capital or share premium, and
- (f) a distribution in respect of securities in a winding up.

(3) Section 687 defines “income tax advantage”.

(4)[^c-8dda42950dd70966c52dac26bdfa1f84] This section is subject to no-counteraction notices issued under section 698A.
