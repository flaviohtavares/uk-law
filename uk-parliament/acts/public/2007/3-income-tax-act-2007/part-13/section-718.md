## 718. Meaning of “person abroad” etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-220dddfe0d57d9f78e2ae6ee01a5c370] In this Chapter “**person abroad**” means a person who is resident outside the United Kingdom.[^key-a75373acf1c7e1d5fd16dca76a1f064d]

(2) For the purposes of this Chapter, the following persons are treated as resident outside the United Kingdom—

- (a)[^key-abe56187e7b35d9c5dd6ad926c4b2cdd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the person treated as non-UK resident[^key-0f2c660376f025a04e77ef9176d4b39a] under section 475(3) (trustees of settlements), and
- (c) persons treated as non-UK resident under section 834(4) (personal representatives).
