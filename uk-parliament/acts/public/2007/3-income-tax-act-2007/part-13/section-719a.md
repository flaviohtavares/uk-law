# Income Tax Act 2007, section 719A

## 719A. Other definitions[^M_F_a1b3604c-0755-471a-f6c4-2d018406e399]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**closely-held company**” means—
  - (a) a close company for the purposes of the Corporation Tax Acts (see Part 10 of CTA 2010), or
  - (b) a company that would be a close company if section 442(a) of CTA 2010 were ignored (non-UK resident company not to be treated as close);
- “**nominee**”, in relation to an individual, means a person—
  - (a) who possesses any rights or powers on behalf of the individual, or
  - (b) who may be required to exercise any rights or powers on the individual’s direction or behalf;
- “**participator**” is to be construed in accordance with section 454 of CTA 2010.
