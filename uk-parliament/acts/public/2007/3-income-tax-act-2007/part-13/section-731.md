## 731. Charge to tax on income treated as arising under section 732

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Income tax is charged on income treated as arising to an individual under section 732 (non-transferors receiving a benefit[^key-8c466fa3c396825a375030e532f5747a] as a result of relevant transactions).

(1A)[^key-39aad531f31280c0f6dbc2b980d9fd05] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1B)[^key-39aad531f31280c0f6dbc2b980d9fd05] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1C)[^key-39aad531f31280c0f6dbc2b980d9fd05] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Tax is charged under this section on the amount of income treated as arising for the tax year.

(2A)[^key-7fac1b614dbf33f1359a84cdfe176d56] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The person liable for any tax charged under this section is the individual to whom the income is treated as arising [^key-0d02d78b4ada681b9fc6d55cd40673ab]...

(4) For exemptions from the charge under this section, see sections 736 to 742[^key-01b314fe462d1ee1731a92857be98d23] (exemptions where no tax avoidance purpose or genuine commercial transaction , etc[^key-01b4e579d68ef813c6c273d57ba7ab91]).
