# Income Tax Act 2007, section 735AC

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 735AC. Transitionally protected income not to be taxed on remittance[^c-da9c2a80c85ab78bc887650ee1ef18fe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 832 of ITTOIA 2005 (relevant foreign income charged on remittance basis) does not apply to transitionally protected income.
