## 809ZH. Overview of Chapter[^c-a137542f054243a0afcb3e80db0cece6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter makes provision for removing entitlement to income tax reliefs, and counteracting income tax advantages, where a person makes a relievable charity donation which is a tainted donation.

(2) See sections 257A and 257B[^c-bd2d045d313c3c723a7b15c4695e7688] of TCGA 1992 and Part 21C of CTA 2010 for the removal of entitlement to other reliefs where a person makes a relievable charity donation which is a tainted donation.
