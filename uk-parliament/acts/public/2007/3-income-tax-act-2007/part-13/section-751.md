## 751. The tribunal’s jurisdiction on appeals[^key-21e62f2ce1640922c34eff12647da795]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On any appeal that is notified to the tribunal, the jurisdiction of the tribunal[^key-ece4a0e519fabc69c655c18e98d89920] includes jurisdiction to affirm or replace any decision taken by an officer of Revenue and Customs in exercise of the officer's functions under—

- (za)[^M_F_953f7fcc-5281-47fd-ca77-db5d6ca2e2c6] section 720A(4) or 727A(4) (whether individual treated as involved in closely-held companies),
- (a) section 737 (exemption: all relevant transactions post-4 December 2005 transactions),
- (b) section 738 (meaning of “commercial transaction”),
- (c) section 739 (exemption: all relevant transactions pre-5 December 2005 transactions),
- (d) section 742 (partial exemption where later associated operations fail conditions),
- (da)[^key-8675eef4eda2a28cd01bc1b91d8f7a8a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) section 743(2) (no duplication of charges: choice of persons in relation to whom income is taken into account).
