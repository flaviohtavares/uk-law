# Income Tax Act 2007, section 498

## 498. Types of income tax for the purposes of section 497

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The types of amount referred to at Step 2 in section 497 are as follows.

*Type 1*

The amount of any tax on income (other than income of a kind mentioned below in relation to Type [^key-d6903c4b7f2c130c689293602db1c6d4]... 3A[^key-2a2069585a6b11b94170ed0980309a4e]) charged at the dividend trust rate or at the trust rate.

*[^key-93fe6f4f5b0bf100f358ad73302d2e94]...*

[^key-93fe6f4f5b0bf100f358ad73302d2e94]...

*Type 3A[^key-fc09c548b0a3bed901ccc0ae94378ab3]*

The amount of tax at the nominal rate on any amount in respect of which—

- (a) the trustees are liable to income tax under section 467 of ITTOIA 2005 (gains from contracts for life insurance etc),
- (b) the trustees are liable to income tax at the trust rate by virtue of section 482 above, and
- (c) tax at the basic rate[^key-60c570df7ff155bc51eab54291ff31a8] is treated as having been paid by virtue of section 530 of ITTOIA 2005 (life insurance).

*[^key-2fd720327a7c792c24ae1cda88b02c0a]...*

[^key-270c1a99e509bf4ec3268e21ec7ae980][^key-c72880a9de9187bdb9ee5b4bbb22fbf7]

*Type 5*

The amount of tax on any income determined in accordance with section 26 of FA 2005 (special tax treatment for trusts for the benefit of vulnerable persons).

(2)[^key-4d1e00314468315a450ca29eb4a381b9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^key-3e71940aeed0b801c617b3b351dfcc69] In relation to Type 3A, the reference to the nominal rate is a reference to a rate equal to the difference between the trust rate and the basic rate[^key-f056327d2c7721f589cbe8d7efe31d70] .

(3) In relation to Types 1 to 4, references to income do not include income the tax on which is reduced in accordance with section 26 of FA 2005.
