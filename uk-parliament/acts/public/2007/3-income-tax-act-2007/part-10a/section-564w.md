## 564W. Diminishing shared ownership arrangements not partnerships[^M_F_d5577bf6-d837-4a0e-c444-635c240de786]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Diminishing shared ownership arrangements are not treated as a partnership for the purposes of the Income Tax Acts.
