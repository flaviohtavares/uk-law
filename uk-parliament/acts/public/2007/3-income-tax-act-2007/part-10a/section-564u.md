# Income Tax Act 2007, section 564U

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 564U. Arrangements not unit trust scheme or offshore fund[^c-da836259ed8c8af04bb84f2af6a0c5a7][^M_F_8ca1c352-5adf-4014-ecb2-7928a7709513]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Investment bond arrangements are not—

- (a) a unit trust scheme for the purposes of section 1007 of this Act, or
- (b) an offshore fund for the purposes of section 354 of TIOPA 2010 so far as relating to income tax.
