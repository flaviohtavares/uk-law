# Income Tax Act 2007, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Repayment: tax paid at greater rate instead of starting rate for savings or savings nil rate[^c-154e1cd73e272cdfcc9ab10cc10edad4][^c-56afddbf84404045f4a6a6c218c59754][^c-44e68f2d9964615ad4ea37cafa970735]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if income tax at a rate greater than the starting rate for savings[^M_F_939672ce-5a24-4adb-eb8e-b3894201f65e] has been paid on income on which income tax is chargeable at the starting rate for savings[^c-25fad1f1aec3289969c0c9771e473fcc].

(1A)[^c-8338dbca313605e161e06e963fa82695] This section also applies if income tax at a rate greater than the savings nil rate has been paid on income on which income tax is chargeable at the savings nil rate.

(2) If a claim is made, any necessary repayment of tax must be made.
