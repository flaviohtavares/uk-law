# Income Tax Act 2007, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. The trust rate , property trust rate, savings trust rate and dividend trust rate[^c-415f166d325da677909e758274db481e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The trust rate is 45%[^c-c9bf23030787ed50250d9d22bff2a9f4].

(1A)[^c-fd9c9db25ee167ec9e798332cb548b3a] The property trust rate is 47%.

(1B) The savings trust rate is 47%.

(2) The dividend trust rate is 39.35%[^c-6d585085e58ffb9acf190c84596581d5].
