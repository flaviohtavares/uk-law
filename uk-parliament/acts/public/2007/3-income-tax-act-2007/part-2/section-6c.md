# Income Tax Act 2007, section 6C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3

## 6C. The default basic, higher and additional rates[^M_F_bf291c2a-c74f-4d3e-e766-662b820c5e59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The default basic rate, default higher rate and default additional rate for a tax year are the rates determined as such by Parliament for the tax year.
