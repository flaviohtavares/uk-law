# Income Tax Act 2007, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Income charged at the default basic rate: non-individuals[^M_F_fae626b7-ded1-4ce3-ed26-666aafdbeb22]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Income tax is charged at the default[^M_F_7800dc7d-d1c6-4edc-898b-dd0031c60722] basic rate on the income of persons other than individuals.

(2) This section is subject to—

- [^c-3459db2b9f878db317f8e5f35ea12a17]...
- section 11CC (income charged at the property basic rate: non-individuals),[^c-1d71c5d49610666e7a34c019085cd99c]
- section 11DA (income charged at the savings basic rate: non-individuals),
- section 14 (income charged at the dividend ordinary rate: other persons),
- Chapters 3 to 5[^c-b0c9b000e2e3e3797e0c1b8ea22184dd] of Part 9 (which provide for some income of trustees to be charged at the property trust rate, the savings trust rate or[^c-dff1533ce9d5a8f53da681a16a178683] the dividend trust rate or at the trust rate), and
- any other provisions of the Income Tax Acts which provide for income of persons other than individuals to be charged at different rates of income tax in some circumstances.
