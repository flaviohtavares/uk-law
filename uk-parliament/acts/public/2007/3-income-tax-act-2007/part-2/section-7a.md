# Income Tax Act 2007, section 7A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3

## 7A. The savings basic, higher and additional rates[^M_F_031c8319-541c-4f40-f20d-3f043d9f32c7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The savings basic rate, savings higher rate and savings additional rate for a tax year are the rates determined as such by Parliament for the tax year.
