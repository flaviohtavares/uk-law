# Income Tax Act 2007, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Additional tax

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the taxpayer is an individual, the provisions referred to at Step 7 of the calculation in section 23 are—

- section 414A(4) read with section 414A(5) (gift aid where devolved basic rate is below basic rate),[^c-31833e1bbb2d637f1bfd42eb758a66a2]
- section 424 (gift aid: charge to tax),
- section 809ZMA (tainted donations: clawback where tainting occurs in later year),[^c-fb80dfe06d803bb6e542cf98d910c7f4]
- section 809ZMB (tainted donations: associated donations)
- section 809ZN (tainted gift aid donations: charge to tax),[^c-79a68eceb4004fc767bcf134bb5f4bca]
- section 809ZO (tainted charity donations by trustees: charge to tax),
- Chapter 8 of Part 10 of ITEPA 2003 (high income child benefit charge),[^c-300d0027ee238068f789be01b8aa361e]
- Chapter 9 of Part 10 of ITEPA 2003 (winter fuel payment charge),[^c-bd59641feb05755f43c6face7dda3807]
- section 192B of FA 2004 (relief at source: excessive relief given),[^c-77e1025cbad37c9f61ef14093e5def8e]
- section 205 of FA 2004 (pension schemes: the short service refund lump sum charge),
- [^c-c56054bca308569b65db975d271755ac]...
- section 206 of FA 2004 (pension schemes: the special lump sum death benefits charge),
- section 208[^c-bd466f79cc827403ec239c898f593c38] of FA 2004 (pension schemes: the unauthorised payments charge),
- section 209[^c-78c584c3b450dc57042b44a0ea5b28dd] of FA 2004 (pension schemes: the unauthorised payments surcharge),
- [^c-eb16703f3309f34a011f871ba125421c]...
- section 227 of FA 2004 (pension schemes: the annual allowance charge), and
- section 244A of FA 2004 (pension schemes: the overseas transfer charge),[^c-1902e1211642084c880e568c74ab1c89]
- section 7 of F(No.2)A 2005 (social security pension lump sum).

(2)[^c-0a24f89d1340bc6b030023166f2ef3c1] If the taxpayer is a trustee, the provisions referred to at Step 7 of the calculation in section 23 are—

- section 496 (discretionary payments by trustees: tax pool adjustment),
- section 809ZMA (tainted donations: clawback where tainting occurs in later year),[^c-a03258135178f85765b06679b00f6fcd]
- section 809ZMB (tainted donations: associated donations)
- section 809ZN (tainted gift aid donations: charge to tax), and
- section 809ZO (tainted charity donations by trustees: charge to tax).
