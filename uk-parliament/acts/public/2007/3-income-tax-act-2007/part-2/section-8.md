# Income Tax Act 2007, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. The dividend nil rate, dividend ordinary rate, dividend upper rate and dividend additional rate[^key-d43df9e231b084d5be9f098a9c4fcd2b][^key-3b5834e143feafeab6f32b33dfbbf148]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^key-be746fa1ef9babf3656539d7ef444409] The dividend nil rate is 0%.

(1) The dividend ordinary rate is 10.75%[^key-9be6ae45a5022f7c4b75d7f56416390a].

(2) The dividend upper rate is 35.75%[^key-ea360ef5c4bf2168e1357984836a6875].

(3)[^key-0a21e6c3365bb41fba7112413f07785a] The dividend additional rate is 39.35%[^key-6403497eab64606d27c0a841effa12ab].
