# Income Tax Act 2007, section 31

## 31. Total income: supplementary

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the purposes of calculating total income.

(2) Income from which a deduction in respect of income tax is to be made (or treated as made) at the basic rate[^c-504c7ae0bba102de39021f905acdc88a] ,[^c-f4b19b527c2bb77b70c26b9f1d2005da] the property basic rate, the savings basic rate,[^c-f79b6b9521f746d0d321e49873e6dc68] the Welsh basic rate or the Scottish basic rate[^c-9af8bfb6c03292e5d597da1491cf3e91] [^c-dbd072a01f3ba603901945b0912e23e9]... in force for a tax year is treated as income of that tax year.

(3)[^c-532dd9d752efd90210fa977135e5e5fd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Subsection (2) applies[^c-937b32a03149025ca5c53d02f4b85526] even if all or part of the income, or the dividend or other distribution, accrued or will accrue in a different tax year.

(5) An assessment that has become final and conclusive for income tax purposes for a tax year is also final and conclusive for the purposes of calculating total income.
