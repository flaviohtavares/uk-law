(3) For other rates at which income tax is charged see—

- (za)[^M_F_76c785d0-c4f2-4135-edf6-8c5a56b2401c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (zb)[^key-780cd0d7a3fa1df17cf5e8cf63dcdb3f] section 6B (the Welsh rates[^key-1718f0fba32823585c7d5ce3f7b755b0]),

- (aa)[^M_F_6fadd0a2-377e-4e65-9611-cc9e077a54b0] section 7A (savings basic, higher and additional rates),

- (c) section 9 (trust rate , property trust rate, savings trust rate[^key-dae3547d3930a33acb5b5889279bcc5e] and dividend trust rate).
