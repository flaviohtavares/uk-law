## 37. Personal allowance for those born before 6 April 1938[^key-d18de6573a9fb992ad1427972a2adb3a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-5a33016d13d57e4b78bc83e79c855272][^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b] An individual who makes a claim is entitled to a personal allowance of £10,660, or (if greater) the section 35 amount, for a tax year if the individual— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b]

- (a) was born before 6 April 1938, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b]
- (b) meets the requirements of section 56 (residence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b] etc *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b]).

(2) If the allowance under subsection (1) is greater than the section 35 amount, for[^key-55595282cb49e63469f22e92980dcdd4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b] an individual whose adjusted net income for the tax year exceeds *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b] £27,700[^key-143844cc09af1ccb92a900d56c4a85d4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b], the allowance under subsection (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b]

- (a) is reduced by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b] an amount equal to half of that excess income[^key-e8b8cbda46e5432979c7dec4ce33da1d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b], but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b]
- (b) is not reduced below *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b] the[^key-f2291151d7f476be730e3ed586ac27c6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b] section 35 amount[^key-2b84a8883890be86741957dd6f26220f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b].

(2A)[^key-82cab1b6b8002f0c77404d869a821b93] In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b]**the section 35 amount *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b]**” means the amount of any allowance to which the individual would be entitled under section 35 for the tax year if the individual had been born after 5 April 1948. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b]

(3) For the meaning of “adjusted net income” see section 58. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8351c12e5fb7e40d8e7bca1f88dd011b]
