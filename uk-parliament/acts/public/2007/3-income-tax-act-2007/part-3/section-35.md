# Income Tax Act 2007, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3

## 35. Personal allowance[^c-fd42d2976fcd13af3489b51c7257a4ef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-ad9936220fddfdead740ad3bfc557eb0][^c-5ffcbafec2bebec33a40e890d41ac19b] An individual who makes a claim is entitled to a personal allowance of £12,570[^M_F_0be72d43-7f4b-49ac-d16c-9db1a8e4d79c] for a tax year if the individual meets the requirements of section 56 (residence[^c-d35bd4a1e3967cc7556a59eae1478f64] etc).

(2)[^c-43da58cfcb819796fa7a59ae58021c4c] For an individual whose adjusted net income exceeds £100,000, the allowance under subsection (1) is reduced by one-half of the excess.

(3) If the amount of any allowance that remains after the operation of subsection (2) would otherwise not be a multiple of £1, it is to be rounded up to the nearest amount which is a multiple of £1.

(4) For the meaning of “adjusted net income” see section 58.
