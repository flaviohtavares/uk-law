# Income Tax Act 2007, section 45

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Marriages before 5 December 2005

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a man—

- (a) makes a claim for a tax year, and
- (b) meets the conditions set out in subsection (2) or the conditions set out in subsection (2A)[^key-b95d79cd5f208cbca2a2591047d1958f],

he is entitled to a tax reduction for the tax year of 10% of the amount specified in subsection (3)(a) [^key-5d6229384f2359b35b24dc505448ef61]... .

(2) The conditions are that—

- (a) for the whole or part of the tax year he is married and his wife is living with him,
- (b) the marriage took place before 5 December 2005 and no election for the new rules to apply is in force for the tax year,
- (c) he or his wife was born before 6 April 1935, and
- (d) he meets the requirements of section 56 (residence etc).

(2A)[^key-963597e9b5e99e05c2d8a1541b10858a] The conditions are that—

- (a) for the whole or part of the tax year he is in a civil partnership and his female civil partner is living with him,
- (b) the civil partnership results from a relevant conversion and no election for the new rules to apply is in force for the tax year,
- (c) he or his civil partner was born before 6 April 1935, and
- (d) he meets the requirements of section 56 (residence etc).

(3) The amount is—

- (a) £11,700[^key-d090603ef25acd0670b065ec89ac2743] if either the man or his wife or civil partner[^key-08376e1daf8039e8a3a6fb533d58f5a0] is aged 75 or over at some time in the tax year [^key-fa3e74a5b45d9f7409f51d52be9ab4ff]...
- (b)[^key-fa3e74a5b45d9f7409f51d52be9ab4ff] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) For a man whose adjusted net income for the tax year exceeds £39,200[^key-468d6b9866f1a4ef9e75ae8284f76673], the amounts specified in subsection (3) are reduced by half the excess[^key-504f25907f51729931f538e616de2ebc].

(5) But subsection (4) does not reduce the amounts specified in subsection (3) below the minimum amount.

(6) For the meaning of “adjusted net income” see section 58.
