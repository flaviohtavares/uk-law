# Income Tax Act 2007, section 925C

## 925C. Actual payments ignored if section 925A ... applies[^key-7920ff4a5607779c7ac914e0c1fae59a][^key-bbda56a5ff6891ced2a6fd064e046a9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If section 925A(2) [^key-3fa548d35b8ffe9eb629f5a69215a413]... applies, any payment actually made under an arrangement which is representative of any income payable on any securities is to be treated for the purposes of sections 918 , 919 and 921[^key-88e6d2098ea3364d757fa48255072815] as if it had not been made.
