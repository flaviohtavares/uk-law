# Income Tax Act 2007, section 878

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 878. Interest paid by banks[^c-faa70043fae13e45c8ebd71a3ba00e3f][^c-743585d475201369cc01099e59cc9986]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The duty to deduct a sum representing income tax under section 874 does not apply to a payment of interest made by a bank if that payment is made in the ordinary course of its business.

(1A)[^c-4066a178d7d8a4268ae87bd0500c8fe8] But that duty does apply to such a payment if it is treated as a payment of yearly interest by virtue of section 874(5A).

(2) Section 991 (meaning of “bank”) applies for the purposes of this section.
