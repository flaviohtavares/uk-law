# Income Tax Act 2007, section 867

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 867. Lloyd's premium trust funds

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An investment is not a relevant investment if it forms part of a premium trust fund of an underwriting or former underwriting member of Lloyd's.

(2) In this section “**premium trust fund**” has the meaning given in section 184 of FA 1993.
