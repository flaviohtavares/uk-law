## 980. Derivative contracts: exception from duties to deduct[^c-cd30a7bca5d4c6619cdad489eac9d258]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Despite the provisions of this Part, a company is not required to deduct a sum representing income tax from a payment made under a derivative contract to which this section applies.

(2) This section applies to a derivative contract if profits and losses arising from it are calculated in accordance with Part 7 of CTA 2009[^c-581b85e48fed3a774f152ab290924230] .
