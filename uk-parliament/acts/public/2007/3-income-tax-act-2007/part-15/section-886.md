## 886. Interest paid by recognised clearing houses etc[^c-03c28b742baa2dfa1f7ec736cddc3d0d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The duty to deduct a sum representing income tax under section 874 does not apply to a payment of interest made by a relevant entity[^c-261a74040d81a63a979321e0ef28a43d] if—

- (a) the relevant entity[^c-bf30faa2b2d63b8ca606fe9cb7783c37] is carrying on business as the provider of a central counterparty clearing service or as a central securities depository[^c-ac06e17b66115d11d7b4b28a8695cf43], and
- (b) the interest is paid in the ordinary course of that business, on margin or other collateral deposited with it by users of the service or as a central securities depository[^c-197f0abfd026d8c82f44054516655043].

(2) The duty to deduct a sum representing income tax under section 874 does not apply to interest treated by virtue of section 607 (treatment of price differences under repos), or paragraph 5 of Schedule 13 to FA 2007 (relief for borrower for finance charges in case of debtor repos and debtor quasi-repos),[^c-38e04be824ae8dd2fdab3c4a6d422143] as paid by a relevant entity[^c-1e3fb3782ce3bf4b38af602ad808c35d] in respect of contracts made by it as the provider of a central counterparty clearing service or as a central securities depository[^c-60af532186aacdf1b6cf7226625d61d5] .

(3) In this section—

- “**central counterparty clearing service**” means the service provided by a relevant entity[^c-9e1acd768dcb9848ba17bec14748bbdc] to the parties to a transaction where there are contracts between each of the parties and the relevant entity (in place of, or as an alternative to, a contract directly between the parties), and
- “relevant entity”, means any of the following (as defined for the purposes of[^c-9d59876f91b0c6adea013b81a753c34c] FISMA 2000 by section 285 of that Act)—
  - (a) a recognised clearing house;
  - (b) a recognised investment exchange;
  - (c) [^c-79ece506040a5aa13fc09bfcbce91cc3]...
  - (d) a third country central counterparty.
  - (e) a recognised CSD;[^c-83c85d8784ebd36fe918e417850fda03]
  - (f) [^c-79ece506040a5aa13fc09bfcbce91cc3]...
  - (g) a third country CSD.
