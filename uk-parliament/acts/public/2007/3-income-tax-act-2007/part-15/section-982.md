# Income Tax Act 2007, section 982

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 982. Income tax is calculated by reference to gross amounts[^c-cd30a7bca5d4c6619cdad489eac9d258]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any provision of this Part requires the deduction from a payment of a sum representing income tax at a specified rate, the rate is to be applied to the gross payment, that is to the payment before deduction of a sum representing income tax under this Part.
