# Income Tax Act 2007, section 850

## 850. Overview of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-962c26c15a5c04a0edce1eee61c346f8] This Chapter has effect for the purposes of section 876 (duty under section 874 to deduct tax from payments of yearly interest: exception for deposit-takers).

(2)[^c-6609efc963ba4a69b632848531952e74] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Sections 853 to 856 set out some basic concepts, so that—

- (a) section 853 defines “deposit-taker” (and section 854 allows the Treasury by order to prescribe persons as deposit-takers),
- (b) section 855 defines “investment” and “deposit”, and
- (c) section 856 explains which investments are relevant investments.

(4) Section 856 is subject to—

- (a) section 857 (which sets out when investments must be treated as relevant and when they may be treated as not relevant), and
- (b) sections 863[^c-338fedc52dd790a60d5287ae7bcfee81] to 870 (which describe various kinds of investment which are not relevant investments).

(5) Sections 872 and[^c-d760800b4438e662e3385fe68d0b918c] 873 contain supplementary provisions.

(6) For the purposes of this Chapter, crediting[^c-14261e9ac41d340b8feea4ee24e342c1] interest counts as paying it.
