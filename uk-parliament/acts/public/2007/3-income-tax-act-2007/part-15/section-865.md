## 865. Qualifying certificates of deposit

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An investment is not a relevant investment if it is a deposit in respect of which a deposit-taker [^key-ccd2dcb46ef2b39163edbba5a271d3d9]... has issued a qualifying certificate of deposit (see section 985).
