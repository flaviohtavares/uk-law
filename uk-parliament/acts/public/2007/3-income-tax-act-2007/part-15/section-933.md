# Income Tax Act 2007, section 933

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 933. UK resident companies[^key-6b283bdf5af8428541bdde995ea32128]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A payment is an excepted payment if the person beneficially entitled to the income in respect of which the payment is made is a UK resident company.
