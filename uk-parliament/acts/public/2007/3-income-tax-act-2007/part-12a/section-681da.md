## 681DA. Application of the Chapter[^key-2d2699c86c35075e7d602f5a9e2ac5a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter applies if—

- (a) condition A is met (see section 681DB), and
- (b) condition B, C, D or E is met (see section 681DC).
