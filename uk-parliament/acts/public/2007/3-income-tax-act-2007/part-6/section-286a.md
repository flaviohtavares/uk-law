# Income Tax Act 2007, section 286A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 286A. The UK permanent establishment requirement[^key-341f9f3682ce38c7ccda03ddd5fef719]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The requirement of this section, at any time on or after the issue of the relevant holding, is that the relevant company has a permanent establishment in the United Kingdom at all times from the issue of the holding to the time in question.
