# Income Tax Act 2007, section 330A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 330A. Nominees[^c-4e0c410c39b3d7391add8d2a396b4c72]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Shares subscribed for, issued to, held by or disposed of for an individual by a nominee are treated for the purposes of this Part as subscribed for, issued to, held by or disposed of by the individual.
