## 307B. Excluded activities: producing coal[^c-f6786fa824408c8bbe706a1372c6f3bb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section supplements section 303(1)(ib).

(2) “**Coal**” has the meaning given by Article 2 of Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1407/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1407) (state aid to coal industry).

(3) The production of coal includes the extraction of it.
