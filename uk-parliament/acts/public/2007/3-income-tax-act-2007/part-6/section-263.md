# Income Tax Act 2007, section 263

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 263. Form and amount of relief

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An individual who—

- (a) is entitled to claim VCT relief for a tax year, and
- (b) claims such relief for the year on any amount,

is entitled to a tax reduction for the year.

(2) The tax reduction is equal to 20%[^c-41b339b2811e293f263365a7743d1692] of the amount in respect of which the claim is made.

(3) The tax reduction is given effect at Step 6 of the calculation in section 23.
