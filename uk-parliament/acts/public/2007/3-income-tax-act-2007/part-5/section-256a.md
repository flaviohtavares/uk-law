# Income Tax Act 2007, section 256A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 256A. Meaning of “the EIS original rate”[^c-13448b3632c0249f57955c332f0f37b7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “**the EIS original rate**”, in relation to EIS relief, means the EIS rate for the tax year for which the EIS relief was obtained.
