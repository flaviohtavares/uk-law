# Income Tax Act 2007, section 172

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 172. Overview of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The general requirements are met in respect of the relevant shares if the requirements of this Chapter are met as to—

- (a) the shares (see section 173),
- (aa)[^c-b82038277c66abb950416d9bfe31af5e] the maximum amount raised annually through risk finance investments[^c-8ddf09214dbd02cbc2c648aaa101355f] (see section 173A),
- (aaa)[^c-1e0fef9a480b347cd0e4c5c3e175b65b] the maximum risk finance investments at the issue date (see section 173AA),
- (aab) the maximum risk finance investments at times during period B (see section 173AB),
- (ab)[^c-31429eb2730182030cf918babc8ffe94] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the purpose of the issue (see section 174),
- (c) the use of the money raised (see section 175),
- (ca)[^c-9d21e913d0036db0e9e1993d2462ca70] the permitted maximum age (see section 175A),
- (d) the minimum period (see section 176),
- (e) no pre-arranged exits (see section 177), and
- (f) no tax avoidance (see section 178), and[^c-cd9ea13033653de1077df1169d8416c2]
- (g) no disqualifying arrangements (see section 178A).
