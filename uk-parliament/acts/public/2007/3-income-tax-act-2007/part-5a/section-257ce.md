# Income Tax Act 2007, section 257CE

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 257CE. The no tax avoidance requirement[^c-86ceadf9ebe4ce15f761ac0cdcdbfb33]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The relevant shares must be issued for genuine commercial reasons, and not as part of a scheme or arrangement the main purpose or one of the main purposes of which is the avoidance of tax.
