# Income Tax Act 2007, section 257G

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3

## 257G. Assessments for the withdrawal or reduction of SEIS relief[^c-86ceadf9ebe4ce15f761ac0cdcdbfb33]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any SEIS relief which has been obtained falls to be withdrawn or reduced under Chapter 6, it must be withdrawn or reduced by the making of an assessment to income tax for the tax year for which the relief was obtained.
