# Income Tax Act 2007, section 614CD

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 614CD. Application of provisions of Chapter 2 for purposes of this Chapter[^key-d193ae9bd44a503c86cdc0fbafb21cef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 614BG to 614BQ apply for the purposes of this Chapter as they apply for the purposes of Chapter 2, but taking the references in sections 614BH(1) and 614BK(1)(a) to section 614BF as references to section 614CC.
