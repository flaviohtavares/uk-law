## 399B. Property partnerships: tax reduction for non-deductible loan interest[^key-3cc000833a30f07932c49d78f6e320da]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsections (2) and (3) apply if for a tax year an individual would be given relief for an amount (“the relievable amount”) by section 383(1) but for section 399A.

(2) The individual is entitled to relief under this section for the tax year in respect of the relievable amount.

(3) The amount of the relief is given by—[^M_F_a785c3a4-b006-4fb6-b7d3-6adeb16959a8]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3)

where PBR[^key-81852b3d8b020beaf031050dabc92a7a] is the property basic rate[^key-b1647b29c292a1c2efe2749fc9544275] of income tax for the year.
