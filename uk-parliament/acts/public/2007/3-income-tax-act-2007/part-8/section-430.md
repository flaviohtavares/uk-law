# Income Tax Act 2007, section 430

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 430. “Charity” to include exempt bodies

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter “**charity**” includes—

- (a) the Trustees of the National Heritage Memorial Fund, and[^key-3b7f60c82c0f57eef2f7d7c22aafc0b5]
- (b) the Historic Buildings and Monuments Commission for England,
- (c)[^key-632de5208da3a46e988f89034be61be0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) a club that is registered as a community amateur sports club for the purposes of Chapter 9 of Part 13 of CTA 2010[^key-80a859e9fe73cd9d662fdae18c927482].

(2) For the purposes of the application of section 414(1) in relation to clubs that are charities as a result of subsection (1)(d) of this section, membership fees are not gifts.
