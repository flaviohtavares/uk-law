# Income Tax Act 2007, section 384B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 384B. Restriction on relief where cash basis applies[^c-7dbaa7703e98f2077accafb8b2ba2748]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Relief is not to be given under this Chapter for a tax year for interest paid by a person on a relevant loan if the partnership to which the loan relates carried on[^c-9229e3824af1aaa1166b3a95537745ba] a[^c-111ac40f2919d5c32d33836913fa213d] UK property business or overseas property business the profits of which[^c-d3be5a9a575397e440ff7151353cd848] are calculated on the cash basis for the tax year (see section 271D of ITTOIA 2005).

(2) A loan is a “relevant loan” if—

- (a) it is a loan to which section 388 applies (loan to buy plant or machinery for partnership use), or
- (b) it is a loan to which section 398 applies (loan to invest in partnership) and which is not used for purchasing a share in a partnership.
