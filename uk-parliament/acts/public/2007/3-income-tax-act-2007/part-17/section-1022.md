# Income Tax Act 2007, section 1022

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1022. Meaning of “debenture”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act “**debenture**” has the meaning given by section 738 of the Companies Act 2006[^c-eadc1fc431f03b0af4774d4526e764db].

(1A)[^c-120024df4c98cca989f2a393c733b7a4] For the meaning of “debenture” in sections 257KB(3) to (5), 257L(4), 257LA(2) and 392(3A)(a), see also sections 257KB(6), 257L(6), 257LA(4) and 392(3B).

(2) This section does not apply for the purposes of sections 559, 560 and 691.
