# Income Tax Act 2007, section 1020

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1020. Claims and elections

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act any reference to a claim or election is to a claim or election in writing or in any form authorised (in relation to the case in question) by directions under section 43E(1) of[^c-4aab8bc6fa83047233060524ff6a871b] TMA 1970.

(2) For further information about claims and elections, see TMA 1970 more generally (but in[^c-546a027a3104d5fd550b9fa1459a5863] particular, section 42(2), (10) and (11) and Schedule 1A).
