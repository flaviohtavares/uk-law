## 1023. Meaning of “double taxation arrangements”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act “**double taxation arrangements**” means arrangements which have effect under section 2(1) of TIOPA 2010[^c-d721002d3a62536866c557c0671d75ce].
