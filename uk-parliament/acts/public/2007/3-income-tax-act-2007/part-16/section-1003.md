# Income Tax Act 2007, section 1003

## 1003. Meaning of “oil and gas exploration and appraisal”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the Income Tax Acts “**oil and gas exploration and appraisal**” means activities carried out for the purpose of—

- (a) searching for petroleum anywhere in an area,
- (b) ascertaining a petroleum-bearing area's extent or characteristics, or
- (c) ascertaining its reserves of petroleum,

so that it may be determined whether the petroleum is suitable for commercial exploitation.

(2) In this section “**petroleum**” has the meaning given by section 1 of the Petroleum Act 1998 (c. 17).
