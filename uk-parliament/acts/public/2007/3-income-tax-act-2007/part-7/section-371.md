# Income Tax Act 2007, section 371

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 371. CITR subsequently found not to have been due

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any CITR has been obtained which is subsequently found not to have been due, the CITR must be withdrawn.
