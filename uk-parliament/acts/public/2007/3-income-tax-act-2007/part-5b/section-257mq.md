## 257MQ. Meaning of “excluded activity”[^c-aaf10c7dc329529b2ca19087f01e69e7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following are excluded activities for the purposes of sections 257JD, 257MJ and 257MP—

- (a) dealing in land, in commodities or futures or in shares, securities or other financial instruments,
- (b) banking, insurance, money-lending, debt-factoring, hire-purchase financing or other financial activities [^c-1de631aae0eca8f77a13bfc9cabef07e]...,
- (ba)[^c-78c62c711480900325c1fe13c31e5bb6] leasing (including letting ships on charter or other assets on hire),
- (bb) receiving royalties or licence fees,
- (bc) operating or managing nursing homes or residential care homes or managing property used as a nursing home or residential care home (see section 257MQA),
- (bd) generating electricity, exporting electricity (see subsection (3)) or making electricity generating capacity available,
- (be) generating heat,
- (bf) generating any form of energy not within paragraph (bd) or (be),
- (bg) producing gas or fuel,
- (c) property development (see section 257MR),
- (d) activities in the fishery and aquaculture sector that is covered by Council Regulation [(EC) No. 104/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2000/104) of 17 December 1999 on the common organisation of the markets in fishery and aquaculture products,
- (e) the primary production of products listed in Annex I to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (agricultural etc products), with the exception of products covered by Council Regulation [EC() No. 104/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2000/104) (fishery and aquaculture products),
- (f)[^c-5fbef5cb05a61c37243bd695c93adce7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g) road freight transport for hire or reward, and
- (h) providing services or facilities for a business carried on by another person (other than a company of which the provider of the services or facilities is a qualifying subsidiary) if—
  - (i) the business consists wholly or as to a substantial part of activities falling within any of paragraphs (a) to (g), and
  - (ii) a controlling interest (see section 257MT) in the business is held by a person who also has a controlling interest in the business carried on by the provider of the services or facilities.

(2)[^c-91633db70f31e1c2ae07442a2b4e889a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-804d686751f43a3fdf45454f4a086632] For the purposes of subsection (1)(bd) electricity is exported if it is exported onto a distribution system or transmission system (within the meaning of section 4 of the Electricity Act 1989).
