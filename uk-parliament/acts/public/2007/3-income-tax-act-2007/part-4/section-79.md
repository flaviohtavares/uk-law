# Income Tax Act 2007, section 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Capital allowances restrictions: supplementary[^c-476db765a6242e6706fb3f2a8e2408b7][^c-99ded692b8a95c9ed0ff31c29d83b29e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If relief is given in a case to which section 75 or 76 applies, the relief is withdrawn by the making of an assessment to income tax under this section.

(2) Expressions which are used—

- (a) in any of sections 75 to 78, and
- (b) in Part 2 of CAA 2001,

have the same meaning in those sections as in that Part.
