## 76. First-year allowances and annual investment allowances: introduction[^key-476db765a6242e6706fb3f2a8e2408b7][^key-99ded692b8a95c9ed0ff31c29d83b29e][^key-2ece3abb032427593c213cd8b2841b0d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sideways relief is not available to an individual for so much of a loss as derives from an annual investment allowance or[^key-ae6b629fd54d2a9b991eff3287e4a4cc] a first-year allowance under Part 2 of CAA 2001 if either section 77 or 78 applies.
