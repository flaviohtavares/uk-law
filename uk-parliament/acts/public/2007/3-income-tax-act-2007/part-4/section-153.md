# Income Tax Act 2007, section 153

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 153. How relief works[^key-db2f9051a0dc0fc5bf1107d6565c3d2e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This section explains how the deductions are to be made. The amount of the loss to be deducted at any step is limited in accordance with section 25(4) and (5).

*Step 1*

Deduct the loss from the relevant[^key-b943d28def85b2665b6a95776a5d8152] miscellaneous income for the loss-making year.

*Step 2*

Deduct from the relevant miscellaneous income for the next tax year the amount of the loss not previously deducted.

*Step 3*

Continue to apply Step 2 in relation to relevant miscellaneous income for subsequent tax years until all the loss is deducted.
