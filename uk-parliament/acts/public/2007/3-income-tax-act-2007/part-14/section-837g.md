# Income Tax Act 2007, section 837G

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3

## 837G. Relationship of Chapter with other income tax provisions[^c-0f2a57111ab50b504b4923bf92945a31]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a winding up receipt arising from the deposit-taking trade is chargeable to income tax under this Chapter it is not chargeable to income tax under any other provision.
