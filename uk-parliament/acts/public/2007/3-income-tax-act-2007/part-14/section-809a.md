## 809A. Overview of Chapter[^c-3c7dd0ba1b37a7977a79cace5dd20e2d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter provides for an alternative basis of charge in the case of individuals who were[^c-fc96d8fe973dbdedd63a59bbeccc5e0f] not domiciled in the United Kingdom in tax years before tax year 2025-26[^c-66e19bd0d8ac168954929d0d71ef0024] [^c-bb3303752a743af6aab7a26d2af1c076]....
