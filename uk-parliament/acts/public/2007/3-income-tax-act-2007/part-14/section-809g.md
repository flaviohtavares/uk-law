# Income Tax Act 2007, section 809G

## 809G. Claim for remittance basis: effect on allowances etc[^key-abca4169b106ffac522a97ab2fcb1821][^key-3280fdca40e2f321a2783a3d140a2863][^key-ed1a58dece43dec27fdb7c130c46ef71][^key-3c7dd0ba1b37a7977a79cace5dd20e2d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if section 809B (claim for remittance basis to apply) applies to an individual for a tax year.

(2) For that year, the individual is not entitled to—

- (a) any allowance under Chapter 2 of Part 3 (personal allowance and blind person's allowance),
- (b) any tax reduction under Chapter 3 of that Part (tax reductions for married couples and civil partners), [^key-ca97002d516c8ddc030210254bcee3e9]...
- (ba)[^key-2fa978de355d4cb58c7df8b60070583e] any tax reduction under Chapter 3A of that Part (transferable tax allowance for married couples and civil partners), or
- (c) any relief under section 457 or 458[^key-4563d90d9f0d381653682528e7dca81f] (payments for life insurance etc).

(3) See also section 1K(6)[^key-caf46ca917a4a33696f0cb2dbee93341] of TCGA 1992 (no annual exempt amount for chargeable gains).
