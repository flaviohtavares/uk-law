# Income Tax Act 2007, section 835D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2007/3
>

## 835D. Income tax chargeable on company's income: application[^key-c848084c0140e7e5ded0f80303c0d1fe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter does not apply in relation to income tax chargeable on income of a company otherwise than as a trustee.
