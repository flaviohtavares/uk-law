## 835S. Interpretation of Chapter[^key-955e45c9a012e5c0d4ce61c6c851f21f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the purposes of this Chapter.

(2) “**Branch or agency**” means any factorship, agency, receivership, branch or management.

(3) “**Investment manager**” and “investment transaction” have the same meanings[^key-81ae281acd0402e72705eec695ce7d33] as in Chapter 1 (see section 827).

(4)[^key-7a5a6775b29b8151dc220eda8d3d4d12] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Provision made in regulations under subsection (4) may, in particular, have effect in relation to the tax year current on the day on which the regulations are made.
