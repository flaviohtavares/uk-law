## 837A. Overview of Chapter[^key-0f2a57111ab50b504b4923bf92945a31]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter provides for the receipts of certain types of company being wound up to be charged to income tax.

(2) For provision charging the receipts of such companies to corporation tax, see Chapter 6 of Part 13 of CTA 2010.
