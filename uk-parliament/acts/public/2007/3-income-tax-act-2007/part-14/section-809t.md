## 809T. Foreign chargeable gains accruing on disposal made otherwise than for full consideration[^key-d22f65c468935a2cf97760c287b43072][^key-ecbfc597f6653475e871ac196657f259][^key-3c7dd0ba1b37a7977a79cace5dd20e2d][^key-2f355ac1a1e66238bd6157cfb3697788]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) foreign chargeable gains accrue to an individual on the disposal of an asset, and
- (b) the individual does not receive consideration for the disposal of an amount at least[^key-06f9e1a1e201efa294156584685ac347] equal to the market value of the asset.

(2) For the purposes of this Chapter treat the asset as deriving from the chargeable gains.
