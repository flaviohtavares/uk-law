## 809B. Claim for remittance basis to apply[^key-0e09b6bf51fffb43b01e74991d2ec599][^key-620423ff67953516e024e1c2471b8cff][^key-3c7dd0ba1b37a7977a79cace5dd20e2d][^key-663aab71536aca4dd3468c65a569bb04]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to an individual for a tax year if [^key-3a2315e20bd6ac7e00b912b315f96a30]...—

- (za)[^key-a46f73df473b6961bb4b55535da231ba] the tax year is the tax year 2024-25 or an earlier tax year,
- (a) the individual[^key-770f79969f719380de10c4a56ed9a919] is UK resident for that year[^key-fc9d36d8664c83a57acdc91e8eedd2e4] ,
- (b) the individual[^key-bd773696e758519f2ede037e24982cc8] is not domiciled in the United Kingdom in that year [^key-f9720856d5a507caeab18540466c7876]..., and
- (c) the individual[^key-d7ca3f86a6a2c49ac819f9c1dd00fd55] makes a claim under this section for that year.

(1A)[^key-38572b775684224675785e326e25d168][^key-df212d4c1d1ffde8dff057423700a430] Section 835BA (deemed domicile) applies for the purposes of subsection (1)(b).

(2)[^key-01192ad80cddb147b74c9c5e3414ff8e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Sections 42 and 43 of TMA 1970 (procedure and time limit for making claims), except section 42(1A) of that Act, apply in relation to a claim under this section as they apply in relation to a claim for relief.
