## Schedule 9 — Part 6: further amendments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 48(2)

**1.**[^key-85b436db8f1b3b8204bd96d377acb027][^key-d1450afd17e6244f73a70fa6e270c469] The Communications Act 2003 is amended as follows.

**2.**[^key-09162070f538bc4b3ee7140a211d3669][^key-9c63ac3d6e7966a8c19adb8b0d4675de] In section 393 (general restrictions on disclosure of information), in subsection (6), in paragraph (a), after “362AW” (inserted by paragraph 2 of Schedule 3) insert “, 362BC(6)”.

**3.**[^key-8dc9044662683dbdcd6516623be2c370][^key-4656282da22c1eeaa1d36dfd20a726d9] In section 394 (service of notifications and other documents), in subsection (2)(a), after “**Part 3A**” (inserted by paragraph 3 of Schedule 3) insert “, Part 3B”.

**4.**[^key-2fdedd2bfb5a3064a9e0cfec04fbc067][^key-24a478577b80854804bcc90e5bc07d89] In section 400 (destination of fees and penalties), in subsection (1), in paragraph (ha) (inserted by paragraph 4 of Schedule 3), after “3A” insert “or 3B”.
