> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2024/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Amendments of broadcasting legislation: UK’s withdrawal from EU[^key-f1c17a0eed5a0caa596a6fd1eddd10e4][^key-ea9fc80c237c9c823eb173025f7bb5d5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 12 contains amendments of the Broadcasting Act 1990, the Broadcasting Act 1996 and the Communications Act 2003 to address failures of retained EU law to operate effectively and other deficiencies arising from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union.
