# Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, section 144

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2024/13

## 144. Service and extra-territoriality of notices under CA 1998 and EA 2002[^key-61ad83ea1b381f24e40e049002c48e8c][^key-137ef528477ba660f3bb4bf896e56b79]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 13 makes provision about—

- (a) the service of documents under Chapter 3 of Part 1 of CA 1998 (investigation and enforcement) and Parts 3 (mergers) and 4 (market studies and market investigations) of EA 2002;
- (b) the extra-territorial application of notices under sections 26 and 40ZD of CA 1998 and sections 109(2) and (3) and 174(4) and (5) of EA 2002.
