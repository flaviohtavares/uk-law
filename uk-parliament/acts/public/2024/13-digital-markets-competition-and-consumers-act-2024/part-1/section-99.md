# Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2024/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Director disqualification[^key-5cbe5b080b14d85286f6a11444573efb][^key-efb6220d4b7311eafc52d967b37aff7f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986, in section 9A (competition disqualification order), in subsection (4)—

- (a) in the words before paragraph (a), for “either” substitute “any”;
- (b) at the end insert—

  > - (e) a requirement imposed by or under Chapter 3 of Part 1 of the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 (undertakings with strategic market status in respect of a digital activity: conduct requirements);
  > - (f) a requirement imposed by or under Chapter 4 of Part 1 of that Act (pro-competition interventions).

(2) In the Company Directors Disqualification (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 ([S.I. 2002/3150 (N.I. 4))](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2002/3150), in Article 13A (competition disqualification order), in paragraph (4)—

- (a) in the words before paragraph (a), for “either” substitute “any”;
- (b) at the end insert—

  > - (e) a requirement imposed by or under Chapter 3 of Part 1 of the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 (undertakings with strategic market status in respect of a digital activity: conduct requirements);
  > - (f) a requirement imposed by or under Chapter 4 of Part 1 of that Act (pro-competition interventions).
