# Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, section 334

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 334. Financial provision[^key-0786f2d2d388d17cdbd6c17c5d3b946e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There is to be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenses incurred by the Secretary of State or the CMA under or by virtue of this Act, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
