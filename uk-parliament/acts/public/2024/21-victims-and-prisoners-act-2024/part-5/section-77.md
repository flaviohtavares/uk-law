# Victims and Prisoners Act 2024, section 77

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-09-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2024/21

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Financial provision[^key-865aeeb10765afcd0f7ff22449a91cb0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There is to be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred under or by virtue of this Act by the Secretary of State, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
