# Victims and Prisoners Act 2024, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-09-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2024/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Meaning of “major incident” etc[^c-d7a66fc834f0afeb2f1ee819ac65f2be]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-c70c74751dab3cceb8429bf03bcab5eb] This Part concerns advocates for victims of major incidents.

(2)[^c-c70c74751dab3cceb8429bf03bcab5eb] In this Part, “**major incident**” means an incident that—

- (a) occurs in England or Wales after this section comes into force,
- (b) causes the death of, or serious harm to, a significant number of individuals, and
- (c) is declared in writing by the Secretary of State to be a major incident for the purposes of this Part.

(3)[^c-c70c74751dab3cceb8429bf03bcab5eb] Before declaring an incident that occurs in Wales to be a major incident, the Secretary of State must consult the Welsh Ministers.

(4)[^c-c70c74751dab3cceb8429bf03bcab5eb] For the purposes of this Part, “**harm**” includes physical, mental or emotional harm.

(5)[^c-c70c74751dab3cceb8429bf03bcab5eb] In this Part, “**victims**”, in relation to a major incident, means—

- (a) individuals who have suffered harm as a direct result of the incident (whether or not that harm is serious harm), and
- (b) close family members or close friends of individuals who have died or suffered serious harm as a direct result of the incident.

(6) In this Part, “**advocate**” means—

- (a)[^c-b1430c13c7af20cb02855ff093b8f46f] the standing advocate appointed under section 35(1);
- (b)[^c-c70c74751dab3cceb8429bf03bcab5eb] an individual appointed as an advocate in respect of a major incident under section 36(1).

(7)[^c-b1430c13c7af20cb02855ff093b8f46f] But a reference in this Part to an advocate appointed in respect of a major incident includes the standing advocate only if the standing advocate has been appointed in respect of that incident under section 36(1).
