# Automated Vehicles Act 2024, section 63

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Powers in respect of persons[^c-d1f8ca2a17df0dc20cfed38464b80549]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) If an inspector considers it necessary for the purposes of an investigation, the inspector may require a person—

- (a) to provide to the inspector, or allow the inspector access to, information, items or material in any manner the inspector considers appropriate;
- (b) not to access, disturb, move or alter anything specified by the inspector.

(2) The Secretary of State may make regulations authorising an inspector to require a person to provide other forms of assistance for the purposes of an investigation.
