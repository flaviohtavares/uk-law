# Automated Vehicles Act 2024, section 61

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Purpose of inspectors[^key-727876f79c3c09d9268e125ab4c45260]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The main purpose of the role of inspector is that of identifying, improving understanding of, and reducing the risks of harm arising from the use of authorised automated vehicles on roads in Great Britain.

(2) It is no part of that purpose to establish blame or liability on the part of any person in relation to a particular incident.

(3) The functions conferred by this Chapter are to be exercised in view of that purpose.
