# Automated Vehicles Act 2024, section 23

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Supplementary provision[^key-77ce60005b0501cc9652bf7a26a61f94]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Where the Secretary of State has issued an information notice or an interview notice to a regulated body, the Secretary of State may by further notice issued to that body—

- (a) cancel the notice, or
- (b) vary it in any way that does not make it more onerous.

(2) For the purposes of the sections from section 16 to this section, a person who has ceased to be a regulated body is to be treated, in relation to anything that occurred when the person was a regulated body, as if the person were still a regulated body.
