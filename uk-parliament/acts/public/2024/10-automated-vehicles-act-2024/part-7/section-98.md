# Automated Vehicles Act 2024, section 98

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2024/10
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 98. Extent[^key-8561228485055cf70482fb82172d7541]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act extends to England and Wales and Scotland, except as provided below.

(2) Sections 54(2) and 93 extend only to England and Wales.

(3) An amendment made by Schedule 2 or 3 has the same extent as the provision amended (and sections 53(3) and 54(4) accordingly extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland).

(4) Paragraph 2 of Schedule 5 extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland (and accordingly so does section 81(4)).

(5) This Part extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
