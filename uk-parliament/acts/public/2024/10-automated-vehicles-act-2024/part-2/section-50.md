# Automated Vehicles Act 2024, section 50

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Power to change or clarify existing traffic legislation[^key-2e973c11322371be6d1a6bc1847a526c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Secretary of State may, by regulations, make provision for the purpose of changing or clarifying whether, how or in what circumstances a relevant enactment applies to the user-in-charge of a vehicle.

(2) A “relevant enactment” is an enactment that—

- (a) was passed or made on or before the relevant day, and
- (b) relates to the driving or use of a vehicle.

(3) Regulations under this section may amend any enactment passed or made on or before the relevant day.
