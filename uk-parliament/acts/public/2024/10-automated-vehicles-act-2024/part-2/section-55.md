# Automated Vehicles Act 2024, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2024/10
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 55. Amendment of tampering offence[^c-349a817073d625a9d9d99c211306e2ad][^c-6d74b79dad1ab36d5c739ec49595034d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 25 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 (tampering with motor vehicles)—

- (a) the existing text becomes subsection (1);
- (b) after that subsection insert—

  > (2) For the purposes of subsection (1), the “mechanism” of a vehicle includes—
  >
  > - (a) any equipment in or on the vehicle designed to allow the motion of the vehicle to be controlled other than by an individual in the vehicle, or to facilitate its being so controlled, and
  > - (b) any software installed in, or other electronic information stored in, any such equipment.
