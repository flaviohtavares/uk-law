# Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024, section 88

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Codes of management practice: extension to estate managers[^c-98123e6c2000fef9a8f074bc15a960df]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 87 of the LRHUDA 1993 (codes of management practice)—

- (a) in subsection (6)(b)(i), before “tenants” insert “owners or”;
- (b) in subsection (8)(b), omit “let on leases”.
