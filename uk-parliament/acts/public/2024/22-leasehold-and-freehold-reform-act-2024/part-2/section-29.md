# Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024, section 29

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Change of non-residential limit on collective enfranchisement claims[^c-990fd33ba506cf3c2e99846e0d81daec]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 4(1)(b) of the LRHUDA 1993 (non-residential limit on collective enfranchisement claims), for “25 per cent.” substitute “50%”.
