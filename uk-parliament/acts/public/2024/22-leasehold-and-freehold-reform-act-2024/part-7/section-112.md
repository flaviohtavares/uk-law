# Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024, section 112

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Meaning of “estate rentcharge”[^c-82294a0bc0684a0e61fce7dba788e86d]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 2(4)(b) of the RA 1977 (meaning of “**estate rentcharge**”), for “or repairs” substitute “, repairs or improvements”.
