# Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024, section 48

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Right to vary long lease to replace rent with peppercorn rent[^c-d719a4bb04cdcadc9290148d548c8901]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Schedule 10 confers on certain leaseholders the right to a variation of their leases so that the whole or part of the rent payable becomes and will remain a peppercorn rent.
