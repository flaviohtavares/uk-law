# Debtors (Scotland) Act 1880

> Text as at 1993-04-05.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

Debtors (Scotland) Act 1880

1880 c.34

An Act to abolish Imprisonment for Debt, and to provide for the better Punishment of Fraudulent Debtors in Scotland; and for other purposes.

\[7th September 1880\][^c570033][^c570034]

## 1. Short title.[^c570035]

*This Act may be cited for all purposes as the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1880.*

## 2. Extent of title.[^c570036]

*This Act shall extend to Scotland only.*

## 3. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c570037]

## Abolition of Imprisonment for Debt

### 4. Abolition of imprisonment for debt, with certain exceptions.[^c570038]

With the exceptions herein-after mentioned, no person shall be apprehended or imprisoned on account of any civil debt.

There shall be excepted from the operation of the above enactment,—

1.[^c570039] Taxes, fines, or penalties due to Her Majesty, and rates and assessments lawfully imposed or to be imposed:

1. Fines imposed for contempt of court or under section 91 of the Court of Session Act 1868.

2. Sums decerned for aliment:

Provided that no person shall be imprisoned in any case excepted from the operation of this section for a longer period than twelve months.[^c570041]

Nothing contained in this Act shall affect or prevent the apprehension or imprisonment of any person under a warrant granted against him as being in meditatione fugæ, or under any decree or obligation ad factum præstandum.
