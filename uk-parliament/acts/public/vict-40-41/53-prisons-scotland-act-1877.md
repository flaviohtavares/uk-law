Prisons (Scotland) Act 1877

1877 c.53

An Act to amend the Law relating to Prisons in Scotland.

\[14th August 1877\]

## Preliminary

### 1. Short title.

This Act shall be cited for all purposes as the Prisons (Scotland) Act 1877.

### Judicial Statistics

#### 66. Prison Commissioners to discharge duties of department of judicial statistics.[^c569752]

The Prison Commissioners shall be the department of judicial statistics under the [^c569753]Judicial Statistics (Scotland) Act 1869, and shall discharge the duties of the said department, and all statutory returns relating to such statistics shall be made to them accordingly . . . [^c569754]
