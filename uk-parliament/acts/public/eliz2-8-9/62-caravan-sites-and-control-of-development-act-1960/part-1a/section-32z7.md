## 32Z7. Guidance[^key-d9e239e079122e71a8984ef526b09fbb]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may, after consulting such persons as they consider appropriate, publish guidance about the operation of this Part.

(2) A local authority must have regard to any guidance published when carrying out its functions under this Part.
