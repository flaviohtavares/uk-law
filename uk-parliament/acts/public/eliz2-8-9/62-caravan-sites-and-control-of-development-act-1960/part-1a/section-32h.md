## 32H. Transfer of Part 1A site licences on death: relevant permanent sites[^key-a3c3d2dfd99d0abedb063324f4625a4f]

*Extent: S*

Where a Part 1A site licence is transferred to a person in accordance with section 10(4), that person must give to the local authority such information as the authority may reasonably require in order to make a determination under section 32L.
