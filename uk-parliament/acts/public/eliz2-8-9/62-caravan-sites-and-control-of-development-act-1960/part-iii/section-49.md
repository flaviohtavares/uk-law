# Caravan Sites and Control of Development Act 1960, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/8-9/62

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 49 (Extent: S). Financial provisions. (Extent: S)[^c-8847684b151be95d5b31cd9e36fd5bf5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

There shall be paid out of monies provided by Parliament— *(Extent: S)*

- (a (Extent: S))[^c6636161] any administrative expenses incurred by the Minister of Housing and Local Government or the Secretary of State in consequence of the passing of this Act, and *(Extent: S)*
- (b (Extent: S)) any increase attributable to the provisions of this Act in the sums payable out of monies so provided under any other enactment. *(Extent: S)*
