## 50 (Extent: S). Short title, interpretation, extent and commencement. (Extent: S)[^key-8847684b151be95d5b31cd9e36fd5bf5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S)) This Act may be cited as the Caravan Sites and Control of Development Act 1960. *(Extent: S)*

(2 (Extent: S)) In this Act “ *(Extent: S)***the Act of 1947 *(Extent: S)***” means the *(Extent: S)* [^c6636171]Town and Country Planning Act 1947. *(Extent: S)*

(3 (Extent: S)) This Act shall not extend to Northern Ireland. *(Extent: S)*

(4 (Extent: S)) This Act shall come into force at the expiration of a period of one month beginning with the date on which it is passed. *(Extent: S)*
