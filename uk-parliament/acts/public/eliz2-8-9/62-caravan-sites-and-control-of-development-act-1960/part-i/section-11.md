# Caravan Sites and Control of Development Act 1960, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/8-9/62

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 11. Duty of licence holder to surrender licence for alteration.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A local authority who have issued a site licence may at any time require the holder to deliver it up so as to enable them to enter in it any alteration of the conditions or other terms of the licence made in pursuance of the provisions of this Part of this Act.

(2) If the holder of a site licence fails without reasonable excuse to comply with a requirement duly made under this section he shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c6634401].
