# Caravan Sites and Control of Development Act 1960, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/8-9/62

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2. Exemptions from licensing requirements.

*Extent: E+W+S*

No site licence shall be required for the use of land as a caravan site in any of the circumstances specified in the First Schedule to this Act and that Schedule shall have effect accordingly.
