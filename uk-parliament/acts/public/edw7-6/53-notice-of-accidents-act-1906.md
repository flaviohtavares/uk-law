## 6. Notice of accidents under 57 & 58 Vict. c.28.[^c578580]

Section one of the Notice of Accidents Act, 1894, shall be read as if the words “cause him to be absent throughout at least one whole day from his ordinary work”were substituted for the words “prevent him on any one of the three working days next after the occurrence of the accident from being employed for five hours on his ordinary work”in subsection (1) of that section.
