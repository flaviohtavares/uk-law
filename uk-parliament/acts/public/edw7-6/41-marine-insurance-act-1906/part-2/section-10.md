# Marine Insurance Act 1906, section 10

> Text as at 2016-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Bottomry.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The lender of money on bottomry or respondentia has an insurable interest in respect of the loan.
