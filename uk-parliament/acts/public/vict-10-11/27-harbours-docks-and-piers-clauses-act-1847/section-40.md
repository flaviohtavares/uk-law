# Harbours, Docks and Piers Clauses Act 1847, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. In case of dispute between collector and master, &c. goods to be weighed or measured.[^c559527][^c559528][^c559529][^c559530][^c559531][^c-313f6a4784ac07890b5cceeb2ee4838b][^c559526]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any difference arise between the collector of the rates and the master of any vessel or the owner of any goods, concerning the weight or quantities of the goods in respect of which any rates are payable, such collector may cause all such goods to be weighed or measured, and, if necessary, may detain the vessel containing such goods until they have been weighed or measured.
