## 35. Master to report arrival of vessel.[^c559493][^c559494][^c559495][^c559496][^c559497][^key-a26453e9dfe6cb9f2074f7f0802fc5fb][^key-e901e5e2ebe4a2a154b9901eace2b01a][^key-5adb65b7d99812bb846bc87b6133a706][^key-20dc4a097938a5b246826dc31c7075f6][^key-f21fcb271c22cda420cf8897e9c03290][^key-9d1164bc15c1663646fd19562179dcd9][^key-313f6a4784ac07890b5cceeb2ee4838b][^key-44ccbc6634be7814443cf217218ce557][^key-504ff4e0418bb44b4e77fc78d64da272][^key-dd1487916ccbb47550f5f49d0f5c1002][^key-2875a5be5f6bf0622be38a948bde4204][^key-0d29b22e43b6eeb270987412800b9416][^key-26ce7840b824b1d2a087a3a0e560e9fb][^c559492]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Within twenty-four hours after the arrival within the limits of the harbour, dock, or pier of any vessel liable to rates, the master of such vessel shall report such arrival to the harbour master; and if he fail to make such report within the time aforesaid he shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c559498].
