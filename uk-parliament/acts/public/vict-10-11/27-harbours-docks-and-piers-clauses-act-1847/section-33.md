## 33. Harbour, dock, and pier free to the public on payment of rate.[^c559482][^c559483][^c559484][^c559485][^key-c1f68e4a1747e238877de98cac4cb826][^key-ab151e2236e143d8b81a6978c933312a][^key-551d7fc15364b58594f6541abb7196bc][^key-e901e5e2ebe4a2a154b9901eace2b01a][^key-20dc4a097938a5b246826dc31c7075f6][^key-f21fcb271c22cda420cf8897e9c03290][^key-9d1164bc15c1663646fd19562179dcd9][^key-313f6a4784ac07890b5cceeb2ee4838b][^key-44ccbc6634be7814443cf217218ce557][^key-504ff4e0418bb44b4e77fc78d64da272][^key-588b9383f5d9af62d54598b466edd869][^key-2875a5be5f6bf0622be38a948bde4204][^key-0bb4b682f451709013dbdb0c016d0bd9][^key-1e5e81d70c298871c27e3bca701a938a][^key-4d9288b4a34a90e0a70f09ec1e34da19][^key-8a7537d3d0054dffadbaea48b7212e46][^key-26ce7840b824b1d2a087a3a0e560e9fb][^c559481]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Upon payment of the rates made payable by this and the special Act, and subject to the other provisions thereof, the harbour, dock, and pier shall be open to all persons for the shipping and unshipping of goods, and the embarking and landing of passengers.
