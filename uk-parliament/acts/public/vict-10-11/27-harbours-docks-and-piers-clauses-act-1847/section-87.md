# Harbours, Docks and Piers Clauses Act 1847, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/27

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. A copy of proposed byelaws to be open to inspection.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For one month at least before any such application for confirmation of any byelaws, a copy of the proposed byelaws shall be kept at the principal office of the undertakers; and all persons may, at all reasonable times, inspect such copy, without fee or reward, and the undertakers shall furnish every person who shall apply for the same with a copy thereof, or of any part thereof, on payment of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bff57cb0e42858757bbd3e7c97aac92b] 2½p[^c559631] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bff57cb0e42858757bbd3e7c97aac92b] for every one hundred words so to be copied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bff57cb0e42858757bbd3e7c97aac92b]
