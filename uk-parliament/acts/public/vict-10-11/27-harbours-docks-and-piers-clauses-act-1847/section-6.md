# Harbours, Docks and Piers Clauses Act 1847, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Construction of harbour, dock, or pier, to be subject to the provisions of this Act and one of the Lands Clauses Consolidation Acts.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where by the special Act the undertakers shall be empowered, for the purpose of constructing the harbour, dock, or pier, to take or use any lands otherwise than with the consent of the owners and occupiers thereof, they shall, in exercising the power so given to them, be subject, if the harbour, dock, or pier be situate in England or Ireland, to the provisions and restrictions contained in this Act and in the [^c559397]Lands Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, and, if the harbour, dock or pier be situated in Scotland, to the provisions and restrictions contained in this and in the [^c559398]Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845; and the undertakers shall make to the owners and occupiers of and all other parties interested in any lands taken or used for the purposes of this or the special Act, or injuriously affected by the construction of the works thereby authorized, full compensation for the value of the lands so taken or used, and for all damage sustained by such owners, occupiers, and other parties by reason of the exercise as regards such lands of the powers vested in the undertakers by this or the special Act, or any Act incorporated therewith; . . . [^c559399] the said Lands Clauses Consolidation Acts . . . [^c559399] shall be applicable to determining the amount of any such compensation, . . . [^c559399]
