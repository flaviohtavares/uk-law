# Harbours, Docks and Piers Clauses Act 1847, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Master of vessel to produce certificate of registry.[^c559500][^c559501][^c559502][^c559503][^c559504][^key-313f6a4784ac07890b5cceeb2ee4838b][^key-e0500153e2204842d9f512c834bd0adb][^key-5a5f682dbc439ef00c863f7fd4bf92b3][^c559499]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The master of every registered vessel shall, on demand, produce the certificate of the registry of such vessel to the collector of rates; and if any such master refuse or neglect to make such production, on demand, he shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding level 2 on the standard scale[^c559505].
