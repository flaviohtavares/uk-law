# Harbours, Docks and Piers Clauses Act 1847, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Combustible matter on quays, &c., to be removed.[^c559612][^c-2409a552059a58f44da45f00817c993c][^c-546fde5889c6cad1de6d48e9b0745b62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Every person being the owner of or having the charge of any tar, pitch, resin, spirituous liquors, turpentine, oil, or other combustible thing, which shall be upon any quay, dock, or wharf belonging to the undertakers, or on the deck of any vessel within the harbour or dock, or at or near the pier, shall cause the same to be removed to a place of safety within two hours after being required so to do by notice in writing, signed by the harbour master, and if he fail so to do shall forfeit a sum not exceeding £2[^c559613] for every hour such combustible thing shall remain in any such place as aforesaid after the expiration of two hours from the service of the said notice.
