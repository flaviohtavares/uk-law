# Harbours, Docks and Piers Clauses Act 1847, section 98

## 98. Penalty on undertakers failing to keep or deposit such copies.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the undertakers fail to keep or deposit, as herein-before mentioned any of the said copies of the special Act, they shall forfeit level 2 on the standard scale[^c559654] for every such offence, and also five pounds for every day afterwards during which such copy shall be not so kept or deposited.
