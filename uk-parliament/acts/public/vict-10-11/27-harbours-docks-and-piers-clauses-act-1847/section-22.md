# Harbours, Docks and Piers Clauses Act 1847, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/27

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Undertakers to hire persons to work cranes.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The undertakers or their lessees shall provide proper servants and labourers for working such cranes at all reasonable times for the use of the public.
