# Harbours, Docks and Piers Clauses Act 1847, section 72

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Power to enter ship and search for and extinguish fires or lights.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The harbour master may enter into any vessel within the harbour or dock, or at or near the pier, to search for any fire or light in or suspected to be in such vessel, contrary to the provisions of this or the special Act, or of any byelaw made in pursuance thereof, and may extinguish the same; and any person who shall obstruct the harbour master in the execution of such duty shall for every such offence be liable to a penalty not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c559616].
