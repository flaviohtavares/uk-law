# Harbours, Docks and Piers Clauses Act 1847, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Vessels to have their sails lowered when entering and navigating dock.[^c-759ebc31601d73c911ca569fd127c40b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before any vessel shall enter into the dock the master of such vessel shall cause her sails to be lowered or furled; and if the master of any vessel shall navigate the same under sail into or in the dock, he shall for every such offence be liable to a penalty not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c559603].
