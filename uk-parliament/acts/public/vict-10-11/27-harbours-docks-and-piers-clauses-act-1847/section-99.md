# Harbours, Docks and Piers Clauses Act 1847, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Nothing in this or the special Act to affect the rights of the crown;

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this or the special Act, or any Act incorporated therewith contained shall extend to alienate, defeat, vary, lessen, abrogate, or prejudice any estate, right, title, interest, prerogative, royalty, jurisdiction, or authority, of or appertaining to the Queen’s most excellent Majesty, nor to abridge, vary, or abrogate any of the powers or authorities by law vested in the Admiralty, or in the Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Customs and Excise[^c559655], or in the Crown Estate Commissioners[^c559656], in relation to the possessions and land revenues of her Majesty in right of her crown, or otherwise howsoever.
