# Harbours, Docks and Piers Clauses Act 1847, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Penalty on shipmasters not complying with directions of the harbour master.[^c559593][^c-f93d084a1066aacae84a4c0889fe9dd5][^c559592]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The master of every vessel within the harbour or dock, or at or near the pier, or within the prescribed limits, if any, shall regulate such vessel according to the directions of the harbour master, made in conformity with this and the special Act; and any master of a vessel who, after notice of any such direction by the harbour master served upon him, shall not forthwith regulate such vessel according to such direction shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding level 2 on the standard scale[^c559594].
