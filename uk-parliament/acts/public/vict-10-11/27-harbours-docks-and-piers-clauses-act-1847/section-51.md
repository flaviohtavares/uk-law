## 51. Appointment of harbour, dock, or pier master.[^c559587][^c-edeceacc3efdc3dd7c504ae880d59481][^c-4a5b8d0c96a3715c1d25bb8c5a557be3][^c-7714b6a239f39bd8b12c72d1580df03f][^c-7f88c9ab5115f6072c6fa8b0c8ffdc3d][^c-6c31d05c184bf4c7b9c9c7e3efe651e4][^c-f93d084a1066aacae84a4c0889fe9dd5][^c-8f25056d16b77d15a6f73a607e2dd982][^c-1e934ac8b16c4d1c5190f38345310ee9][^c-33d3ad7784f2e6106890b7ff6a73db53][^c-0407c67c83127dcb46d27bb4fdb18e81][^c-bf794775284c86b82a47a0e9ee6fdfda][^c-4028102d59155d6c1c311430235e0f50]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The undertakers may appoint such harbour masters as they think necessary, (including in such expression dock masters and pier masters, as herein-before defined,) and from time to time, as often as they think fit, may remove any such harbour master.
