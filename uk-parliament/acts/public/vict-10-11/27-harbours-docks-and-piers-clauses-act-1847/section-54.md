## 54. Penalty on harbour master for misbehaviour.[^key-ea4c84ca24281175cd52043887ee5448][^key-c0449e4832f16d18508ef7fffd659332][^key-422049ee945cd1fab1cba79a2c739c38][^key-99156eef844bb547123d7c3bd014267b][^key-9147c031d84d5a98c5fcd6d1280bd1b2][^key-8074068b41e409bac9000a13d5b3d2dd][^key-6c503a33cc7e59c0a9f3db3fa4048c63][^key-8cff6891558c6bb54742b0ef36d8fbd5][^key-9a3f97fbc7b66eaf19a4c2c246ac0e12][^key-64eb1f648bb90f9977741b09634b088f][^key-246c7134f5a5de632d391146aeb99c39]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any harbour master, or any of his assistants, without reasonable cause, or in an unreasonable or unfair manner, exercise any of the powers or authorities vested in the harbour master by this or the special Act, the person so offending shall for every such offence be liable to a penalty not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c559595].
