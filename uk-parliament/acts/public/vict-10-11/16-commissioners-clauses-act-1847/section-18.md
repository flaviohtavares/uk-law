# Commissioners Clauses Act 1847, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Commissioners eligible to be re-elected.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Every commissioner going out of office by rotation, or otherwise ceasing to be a commissioner, may be re-elected, and after such re-election he shall, with reference to going out by rotation, be considered as a new commissioner.
