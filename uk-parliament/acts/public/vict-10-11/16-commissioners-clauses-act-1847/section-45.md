# Commissioners Clauses Act 1847, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. As to the holding of special meetings.[^c5651301][^key-c932cb2e4a669a8a1e5790c03ad24649][^key-781ec30cf1f4afb82202b0e5ef605061][^key-01774061dfacc4dcb648b8c8ffb72468]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The commissioners may hold special meetings, and any five or more of the commissioners may require a special meeting to be held, but no such meeting shall be held unless two clear days notice thereof at the least be given.
