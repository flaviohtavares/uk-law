# Commissioners Clauses Act 1847, section 111

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 111. Penalty on commissioners failing to keep or deposit such copies.[^c5652151][^key-f2606d12850dd88c889a28fda46ceeb0][^key-3204e8f106a1bbb122d47e7eea5171b4][^key-80ceee3b6a9d40a88f42189844fbbda8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the commissioners fail to keep or deposit, as herein-before mentioned, any of the said copies of the special Act, they shall forfeit level 2 on the standard scale[^c5652161] level 1 on the standard scale[^c5652171] for every such offence, and also five pounds for every day afterwards during which such copy shall be not so kept or deposited.
