# Commissioners Clauses Act 1847, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. No extraordinary business shall be transacted at monthly meetings, unless notice be given.[^c5651281][^key-18374215ffd564826dd75994218c308d][^key-c932cb2e4a669a8a1e5790c03ad24649][^key-781ec30cf1f4afb82202b0e5ef605061][^key-2a19b3cc7b1252a653b17beea6830e27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where any business other than ordinary business is required or intended to be transacted at any monthly meeting, the clerk shall give notice thereof to each of the commissioners, and no such extraordinary business, nor any new rules or regulations, shall be adopted at the monthly meetings, unless due notice thereof have been given at a prior meeting and sent to each commissioner in the manner required for special meetings.
