# Commissioners Clauses Act 1847, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/16

## 7. Same property not to give two qualifications.[^c-18b4a554e391dd466877978394f58439]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The same property shall not at the same time give a qualification as commissioner to one person as the owner, and to another as the occupier thereof.
