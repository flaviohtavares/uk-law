# Commissioners Clauses Act 1847, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Declaration to be taken at the first meeting.[^c5651151][^c-e29e72cc875ae901dd64a395d2f1449d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Every person elected or appointed a commissioner shall, at the meeting of commissioners at which he first attends as a commissioner, make and subscribe the declaration herein required, and any commissioner, whether he himself have made such declaration or not, may administer such declaration.
