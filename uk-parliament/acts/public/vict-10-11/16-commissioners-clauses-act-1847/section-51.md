# Commissioners Clauses Act 1847, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/10-11/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Acts of the commissioners not to be invalidated by reason of vacancies.[^c-c932cb2e4a669a8a1e5790c03ad24649]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No proceeding of the commissioners or of any committee shall be invalidated or be illegal in consequence only of there being any vacancy in the number of commissioners at the time of such proceeding.
