## 74. Proceedings against officers not to discharge sureties.[^c5651561][^c-4d770e8a6ac46d14273921af07a57fae][^c-c4d52245715f88bc14a0966c315f706b][^c-c932cb2e4a669a8a1e5790c03ad24649]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No such proceeding against or dealing with any such officer as aforesaid shall deprive the commissioners of any remedy which they might otherwise have against any surety of such officer.
