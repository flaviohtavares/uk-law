# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 89

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Commissioners may compound for sweeping footways.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The commissioners may compound, for such time as they think fit, with any person liable to sweep or clean any footway under the provisions of this or the special Act, for sweeping and cleaning the same in the manner directed by this or the special Act.
