# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 57

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Notice of intention to lay out new streets to be given to commissioners.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Every person who intends to make or lay out any new street shall give notice thereof to the commissioners, in order that the level of such street may be fixed by the commissioners.
