# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 22

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Management of sewers and other works vested in the commissioners.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

All public sewers and drains within the limits of the special Act, and all sewers and drains in and under the streets, with all the works and materials thereunto belonging, whether made at the time of the passing of the special Act, or at any time thereafter, and whether made at the cost of the commissioners or otherwise, and the entire management of the same, shall vest in and belong to the commissioners.
