# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 107

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 107. Act not to affect nuisances at common law.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Act contained shall be construed to render lawful any act or omission on the part of any person which is, or but for this Act would be, deemed to be a nuisance at common law, nor to exempt any person guilty of nuisance at common law from prosecution or action in respect thereof, according to the forms of proceeding at common law, nor from the consequences upon being convicted thereof.
