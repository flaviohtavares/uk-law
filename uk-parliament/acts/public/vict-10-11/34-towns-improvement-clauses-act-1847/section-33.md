# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 33

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. All sewers, &c. to be covered with traps.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

All sewers and drains within the limits of the special Act, whether public or private, shall be provided by the commissioners or other persons to whom they severally belong with proper traps or other coverings or means of ventilation so as to prevent stench.
