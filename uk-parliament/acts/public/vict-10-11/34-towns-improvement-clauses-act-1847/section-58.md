# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 58

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Levels to be fixed by the surveyor to the commissioners.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The level of every new street shall be fixed under the direction of the surveyor of the commissioners, subject to such right of appeal as hereafter mentioned; and the level so fixed, if not altered on appeal, shall be kept thereafter by every person raising any house or other building in such street.
