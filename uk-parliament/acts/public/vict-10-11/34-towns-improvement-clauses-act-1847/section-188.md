# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 188

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 188. No order of special sessions to be in force pending appeal.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No order of the said justices shall be of any force pending any appeal touching the same subject matter to the Crown Court[^c640083] having jurisdiction to try such appeal, or in opposition to the order of any such court on such appeal.

## Annotations
