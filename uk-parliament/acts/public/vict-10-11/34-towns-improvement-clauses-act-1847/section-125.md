# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 125

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 125. Commissioners may license slaughter-houses, &c.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The commissioners may license such slaughter-houses and knacker’s yards as they from time to time think proper for slaughtering cattle within the limits of the special Act.
