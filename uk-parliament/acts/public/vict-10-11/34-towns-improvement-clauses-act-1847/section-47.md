# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 47

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Management of streets vested in the commissioners.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The management of all the streets which at the passing of the special Act are or which thereafter become public highways, and the pavements and other materials, as well in the footways as carriageways, of such streets, and all buildings, materials, implements, and other things provided for the purposes of the said highways, by the surveyors of highways or by the commissioners, shall belong to the commissioners.
