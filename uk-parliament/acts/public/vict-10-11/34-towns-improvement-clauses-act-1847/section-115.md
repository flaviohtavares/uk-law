# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 115

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. Penalty on letting such cellars as dwelling places.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Every person who lets separately (except as aforesaid) or who knowingly suffers to be occupied for hire, as a dwelling place, any cellar or room within the limits of the special Act, contrary to the provisions of this and the special Act, shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding £25[^c640023][^c640022]level 1 on the standard scale, and a further penalty not exceeding 25p[^c640025] for every day during which such cellar or room is so occupied after conviction of the first offence.
