# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 186

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 186. Parties may appeal to the quarter sessions against a rate.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any person think himself aggrieved by any rate made under the authority of this or the special Act, or by any matters included in or omitted from the same, he may, at any time within one month after the same is made, give notice of his intention to appeal to the Crown Court[^c640080] . . .
