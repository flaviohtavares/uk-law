# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 38

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Notice of buildings and rebuildings to be given to the commissioners.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before beginning to build any new house, or to rebuild any existing house, within the limits of the special Act, the person intending to build or rebuild such house shall give to the commissioners notice thereof in writing, and shall accompany such notice with a plan showing the level at which the foundation of such house is proposed to be laid, by reference to some level ascertained under the direction of the commissioners.
