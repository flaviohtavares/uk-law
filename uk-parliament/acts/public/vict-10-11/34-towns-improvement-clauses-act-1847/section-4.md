# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 4

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Short title of this Act.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In citing this Act in other Acts of Parliament, and in legal instruments, it shall be enough to use the expression “The Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847.”
