# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 215

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 215. Penalty on commissioners failing to keep or deposit such copies.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the commissioners shall fail to keep or deposit, as herein-before mentioned, any of the said copies of the special Act, they shall forfeit £50[^c640105][^c640104]level 2 on the standard scale for every such offence, and also five pounds for every day afterwards during which such copy is not so kept or deposited.
