# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 120

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. For ascertaining price to be paid for gas, in case of dispute.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the commissioners, and the owners of any gasworks authorized by Act of Parliament to supply gas within the limits of the special Act, and with whom the commissioners may be desirous of contracting, shall not agree as to the price to be paid for such supply, then such price shall be settled by arbitration; and for that purpose the clauses of the [^c640030]Lands Clauses Consolidation Act 1845 with respect to the settlement of disputes by arbitration, shall be incorporated with this and the special Act.
