# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 128

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128. Commissioners may make byelaws for regulation of slaughter-houses, &c.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The commissioners shall from time to time, by byelaws, to be made and confirmed in the manner herein-after provided, make regulations for the licensing, registering, and inspection of the said slaughter-houses and knackers’s yards, and preventing cruelty therein, and for keeping the same in a cleanly and proper state, and for removing filth at least once in every twenty-four hours, and requiring them to be provided with a sufficient supply of water, and they may impose pecuniary penalties on persons breaking such byelaws; provided that no such penalty exceed for any one offence the sum of £25[^c640043][^c640042]level 1 on the standard scale, and in the case of a continuing nuisance the sum of 50p[^c640045] for every day during which such nuisance shall be continued after the conviction for the first offence.
