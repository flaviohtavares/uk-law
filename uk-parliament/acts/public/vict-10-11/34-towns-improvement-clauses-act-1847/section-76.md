# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 76

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. The expences to be levied by distress on the owner.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If such owner can be found within the limits of the special Act, and if, on demand of the expences aforesaid, he neglect or refuse to pay the same, then such expences may be levied by distress, and any justice may issue his warrant accordingly.
