# Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, section 32

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Streets may be stopped for repairs.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The commissioners may stop any street, and prevent all persons from passing along and using the same, for a reasonable time during the construction, alteration, repair, or demolition of any sewer or drain in or under such street.
