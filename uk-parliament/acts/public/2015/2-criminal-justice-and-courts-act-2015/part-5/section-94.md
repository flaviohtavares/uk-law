# Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015, section 94

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Financial provision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There is to be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred by a Minister of the Crown under or by virtue of this Act, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
