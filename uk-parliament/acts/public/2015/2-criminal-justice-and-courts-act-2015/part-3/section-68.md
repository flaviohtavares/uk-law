# Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/2

## 68. Upper age limit for jury service to be 75

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-3d01dda038172485120aaf9be0f9a2f7][^c-20d5f59345bc595f96a1e6e4e22fdd68] The Juries Act 1974 is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-99fedbcccb375e61d27c02ac19a8071c] In section 1(1)(a) (qualification for jury service), for the words from “and” to the end substitute “ and aged eighteen or over but under seventy six ”.

(3)[^c-ce6486523474b72cd7cc0a1dbe6873c4] In section 3(1) (electoral register as basis of jury selection), for “less than eighteen or more than seventy years of age” substitute

> —
>
> - (a) aged under eighteen, or
> - (b) aged seventy six or over

.
