# Modern Slavery Act 2015, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/30
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Penalties[^c-66996997ceb6d7e223d5e1dafaa5cb41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person guilty of an offence under section 1 or 2 is liable—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for life;
- (b) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-7dcae225d3840726c66048371a1c175f] or a fine or both.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under section 4 is liable (unless subsection (3) applies)—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 10 years;
- (b) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-c699b0830b2378946f24da56c6f51087] or a fine or both.

(3) Where the offence under section 4 is committed by kidnapping or false imprisonment, a person guilty of that offence is liable, on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for life.

(4) In relation to an offence committed before 2 May 2022[^c-068abda32423cb28aa9efae00c0454ed], the references in subsections (1)(b) and (2)(b) to the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-27a0cab2b75630c038f57c808078ee34] are to be read as references to 6 months.
