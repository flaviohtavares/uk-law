## SCHEDULE 4 — Offences to which defence in section 45 does not apply

- section 4 (soliciting murder)
- section 16 (threats to kill)

**37.**[^c-15577d226ae9975aba1921f5ea20fdfd] (1) An offence of attempting or conspiring to commit an offence listed in this Schedule.
