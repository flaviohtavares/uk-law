## 81. Register of people with significant control[^c-a48c5774e83773f82ffd1f1d26977b5e][^c-31e9325ac2396f78c1325f2b07a3a330][^M_I_a752421f-9715-450e-99ca-6c8407327dee]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 3 amends the Companies Act 2006 to require companies to keep a register of people who have significant control over the company.
