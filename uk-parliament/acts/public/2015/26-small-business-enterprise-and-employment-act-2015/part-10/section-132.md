# Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015, section 132

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-05-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/26

## 132. Creditors not required to prove small debts: individual insolvency

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 9 to the Insolvency Act 1986 (provisions capable of inclusion in individual insolvency rules) after paragraph 18 insert—

> **18A.**
>
> Provision for a creditor who has not proved a small debt to be treated as having done so for purposes relating to the distribution of a bankrupt's estate (and for provisions of, or contained in legislation made under, this Act to apply accordingly).
