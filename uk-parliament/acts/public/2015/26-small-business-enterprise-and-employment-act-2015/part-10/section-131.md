# Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015, section 131

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-05-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Creditors not required to prove small debts: company insolvency

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 8 to the Insolvency Act 1986 (provisions capable of inclusion in company insolvency rules) after paragraph 13 insert—

> **13A.**
>
> Provision for a creditor who has not proved a small debt to be treated as having done so for purposes relating to the distribution of a company's property (and for provisions of, or contained in legislation made under, this Act to apply accordingly).
