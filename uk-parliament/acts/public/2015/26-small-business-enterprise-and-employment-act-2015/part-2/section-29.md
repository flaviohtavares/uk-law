# Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-05-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Section 28(1)(b): interpretation[^key-063e265cc9a5eb443617f3635ef71b66]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the purposes of section 28(1)(b).

(2) “**Qualifying activity**” means any activity carried on—

- (a) by a business for the purposes of the business, or
- (b) by a voluntary or community body for the purposes of the body.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2) the references to a business or a voluntary or community body do not include a business or a voluntary or community body which—

- (a) is controlled by a public authority, or
- (b) is acting on behalf of a public authority in carrying out the activity.

(4) The Secretary of State must publish a statement as to how it is to be determined whether a business or a voluntary or community body is controlled by a public authority.

(5) “**Voluntary or community body**” means any of the following—[^key-2a46e51bc960697caf78d6edb1d681c1]

- (a) a trade union (within the meaning of section 1 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 or Article 3 of the Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 ([S.I. 1992/807 (N.I. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1992/807)));
- (b) an unincorporated body which does not distribute any surplus it makes to its members;
- (c) a charity;
- (d) a company limited by guarantee which does not distribute any surplus it makes to its members;
- (e) a registered society within the meaning given by section 1 of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014;
- (f) a registered society within the meaning given by section 1A of the [Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 1969 (c. 24 (N.I.))](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/apni/1969/24) or a credit union within the meaning of the Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985 ([S.I. 1985/1205 (N.I. 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1985/1205));
- (g) a community interest company;
- (h) a charitable incorporated organisation within the meaning of Part 11 of the Charities Act 2011 or of the [Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (c. 12 (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2008/12));
- (i) a Scottish charitable incorporated organisation within the meaning of Chapter 7 of Part 1 of the [Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/10).
