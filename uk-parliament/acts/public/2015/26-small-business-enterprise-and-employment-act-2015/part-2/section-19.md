# Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-05-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Guidance by the Secretary of State

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-08060180da8d763f8ae0d50b8e2f29b1] The Secretary of State may issue guidance to reviewers as to the exercise of functions under section 17.

(2)[^c-071965f38b3866767b0351c2ab845c98] A reviewer must, in exercising any of those functions, have regard to any guidance for the time being in force under this section.

(3)[^c-08060180da8d763f8ae0d50b8e2f29b1] The Secretary of State must—

- (a) publish any guidance or revised guidance issued under this section, and
- (b) lay any such guidance or revised guidance before Parliament.

(4)[^c-08060180da8d763f8ae0d50b8e2f29b1] In this section “**reviewer**” has the same meaning as in section 17.
