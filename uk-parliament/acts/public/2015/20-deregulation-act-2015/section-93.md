# Deregulation Act 2015, section 93

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 93. Reduction in regulation of providers of social work services[^c-a05d0d04250cafc9f5e6cfa0952ca239]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In the Care Standards Act 2000, omit section 4(10) (which provides for the Act to apply to a provider of social work services as it applies to an agency to which that Act applies).

(2) In consequence of subsection (1)—

- (a) in that Act, omit—
  - (i) section 5(1A)(f), and the “and” before it;
  - (ii) section 30A(6)(f);
  - (iii) section 121(4A);
- (b) in the Children and Young Persons Act 2008, omit section 4.
