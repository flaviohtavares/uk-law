# Deregulation Act 2015, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. Tenancy deposits: non-compliance with requirements (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-84d53b6a712250e3287519c9bd537edf][^c-84d53b6a712250e3287519c9bd537edf]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Chapter 4 of Part 6 of the Housing Act 2004 (Tenancy Deposit Schemes) is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-84d53b6a712250e3287519c9bd537edf]

(2) In section 214 (proceedings relating to tenancy deposits), in subsection (1), after “shorthold tenancy” insert *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-84d53b6a712250e3287519c9bd537edf] “ on or after 6 April 2007 ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-84d53b6a712250e3287519c9bd537edf].

(3) In section 215 (sanctions for non-compliance)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-84d53b6a712250e3287519c9bd537edf]

- (a) for subsection (1) substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-84d53b6a712250e3287519c9bd537edf]

  > (1) Subject to subsection (2A), if (whether before, on or after 6 April 2007) a tenancy deposit has been paid in connection with a shorthold tenancy, no section 21 notice may be given in relation to the tenancy at a time when the deposit is not being held in accordance with an authorised scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-84d53b6a712250e3287519c9bd537edf]
  >
  > (1A) Subject to subsection (2A), if a tenancy deposit has been paid in connection with a shorthold tenancy on or after 6 April 2007, no section 21 notice may be given in relation to the tenancy at a time when section 213(3) has not been complied with in relation to the deposit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-84d53b6a712250e3287519c9bd537edf]

  ;
- (b) in subsection (2A), after “Subsections (1)” insert *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-84d53b6a712250e3287519c9bd537edf] “ , (1A) ”.
