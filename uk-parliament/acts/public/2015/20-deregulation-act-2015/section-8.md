# Deregulation Act 2015, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/20

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 8. Driving instructors[^c22547841]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-bcf8b3c84f5c402b40492d18f92be2be] Schedule 2 makes provision to simplify the regulation of driving instructors by removing the separate system for the registration of disabled instructors.

(2) Part 1 of the Schedule contains amendments of Part 5 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 as amended by Schedule 6 to the Road Safety Act 2006.

(3)[^c-bcf8b3c84f5c402b40492d18f92be2be] Part 2 of the Schedule contains transitory amendments of Part 5 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 which have effect before the commencement of Schedule 6 to the Road Safety Act 2006.

(4)[^c-bcf8b3c84f5c402b40492d18f92be2be] Part 3 of the Schedule contains consequential and related amendments.
