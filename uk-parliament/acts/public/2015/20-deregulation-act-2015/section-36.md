# Deregulation Act 2015, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Time limits in relation to section 21 notices and proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-09693ead7848c19f967d8997d6fc9e5a][^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840][^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 21 of the Housing Act 1988 is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

(2) After subsection (4A) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

> (4B) A notice under subsection (1) or (4) may not be given in relation to an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> - (a) in the case of a tenancy which is not a replacement tenancy, within the period of four months beginning with the day on which the tenancy began, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
> - (b) in the case of a replacement tenancy, within the period of four months beginning with the day on which the original tenancy began. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (4C) Subsection (4B) does not apply where the tenancy has arisen due to section 5(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (4D) Subject to subsection (4E), proceedings for an order for possession under this section in relation to a dwelling-house in England may not be begun after the end of the period of six months beginning with the date on which the notice was given under subsection (1) or (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (4E) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> - (a) a notice under subsection (4) has been given in relation to a dwelling-house in England, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
> - (b) paragraph (b) of that subsection requires the date specified in the notice to be more than two months after the date the notice was given, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> proceedings for an order for possession under this section may not be begun after the end of the period of four months beginning with the date specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

(3) In subsection (6), for “subsection” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840] “ subsections (4B)(b) and ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840].
