# Deregulation Act 2015, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/20

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Further exemptions to section 33 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-283084408d29efe8f47da946255a92ab][^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840][^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subsections (1) and (2) of section 33 do not apply where the condition of the dwelling-house or common parts that gave rise to the service of the relevant notice is due to a breach by the tenant of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

- (a) the duty to use the dwelling-house in a tenant-like manner, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (b) an express term of the tenancy to the same effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

(2) Subsections (1) and (2) of section 33 do not apply where at the time the section 21 notice is given the dwelling-house is genuinely on the market for sale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2), a dwelling-house is not genuinely on the market for sale if, in particular, the landlord intends to sell the landlord's interest in the dwelling-house to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

- (a) a person associated with the landlord, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (b) a business partner of the landlord, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (c) a person associated with a business partner of the landlord, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (d) a business partner of a person associated with the landlord. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

(4) In subsection (3), references to a person who is associated with another person are to be read in accordance with section 178 of the Housing Act 1996. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

(5) For the purposes of subsection (3), a business partner of a person (“P”) is a person who is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

- (a) a director, secretary or other officer of a company of which P is also a director, secretary or other officer, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (b) a director, secretary or other officer of a company in which P has a shareholding or other financial interest, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (c) a person who has a shareholding or other financial interest in a company of which P is a director, secretary or other officer, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (d) an employee of P, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (e) a person by whom P is employed, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (f) a partner of a partnership of which P is also a partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

(6) Subsections (1) and (2) of section 33 do not apply where the landlord is a private registered provider of social housing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

(7) Subsections (1) and (2) of section 33 do not apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

- (a) the dwelling-house is subject to a mortgage granted before the beginning of the tenancy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (b) the mortgagee is entitled to exercise a power of sale conferred on the mortgagee by the mortgage or by section 101 of the Law of Property Act 1925, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (c) at the time the section 21 notice is given the mortgagee requires possession of the dwelling-house for the purpose of disposing of it with vacant possession in exercise of that power. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

(8) In subsection (7)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

- (a) “**mortgage *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]**” includes a charge, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (b) “**mortgagee *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]**” includes a receiver appointed by the mortgagee under the terms of the mortgage or in accordance with the Law of Property Act 1925. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
