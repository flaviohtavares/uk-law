# Deregulation Act 2015, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Prescribed form of section 21 notices (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-409fc61e5c0072573bbd2df37d495be8][^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840][^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 21 of the Housing Act 1988, after subsection (7) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

> (8) The Secretary of State may by regulations made by statutory instrument prescribe the form of a notice under subsection (1) or (4) given in relation to an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (9) A statutory instrument containing regulations made under subsection (8) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
