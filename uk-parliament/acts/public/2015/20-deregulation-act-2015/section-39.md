## 39. Requirement for landlord to provide prescribed information (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-81864090582fb47c65dd73408791e722][^c-7275c2dc22c74cf861eed729bf034a7b][^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840][^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

*Extent: E+W+S*

After section 21A of the Housing Act 1988 insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

> **21B. Requirement for landlord to provide prescribed information (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]**
>
> (1) The Secretary of State may by regulations require information about the rights and responsibilities of a landlord and a tenant under an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England (or any related matters) to be given by a landlord under such a tenancy, or a person acting on behalf of such a landlord, to the tenant under such a tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (2) Regulations under subsection (1) may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> - (a) require the information to be given in the form of a document produced by the Secretary of State or another person, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
> - (b) provide that the document to be given is the version that has effect at the time the requirement applies, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
> - (c) specify cases where the requirement does not apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (3) A notice under subsection (1) or (4) of section 21 may not be given in relation to an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England at a time when the landlord is in breach of a requirement imposed by regulations under subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (4) A statutory instrument containing regulations made under subsection (1) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
