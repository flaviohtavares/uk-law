# Deregulation Act 2015, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 38. Compliance with prescribed legal requirements (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-cefafd80d4f9cff3300152d7426bd655][^c-fa99eb00dede9319080b672a62029e0e][^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840][^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

*Extent: E+W+S*

After section 21 of the Housing Act 1988 insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

> **21A. Compliance with prescribed legal requirements (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]**
>
> (1) A notice under subsection (1) or (4) of section 21 may not be given in relation to an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England at a time when the landlord is in breach of a prescribed requirement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (2) The requirements that may be prescribed are requirements imposed on landlords by any enactment and which relate to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> - (a) the condition of dwelling-houses or their common parts, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
> - (b) the health and safety of occupiers of dwelling-houses, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
> - (c) the energy performance of dwelling-houses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (3) In subsection (2) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]**enactment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]**” includes an enactment contained in subordinate legislation within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (4) For the purposes of subsection (2)(a) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]**common parts *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]**” has the same meaning as in Ground 13 in Part 2 of Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (5) A statutory instrument containing regulations made under this section is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
