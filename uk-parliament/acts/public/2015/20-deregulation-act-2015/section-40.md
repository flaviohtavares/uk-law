## 40. Repayment of rent where tenancy ends before end of a period (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-80a454044e2e371997dcf7d058c420c6][^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840][^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

*Extent: E+W+S*

After section 21B of the Housing Act 1988 insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

> **21C. Repayment of rent where tenancy ends before end of a period (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]**
>
> (1) A tenant under an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England is entitled to a repayment of rent from the landlord where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> - (a) as a result of the service of a notice under section 21 the tenancy is brought to an end before the end of a period of the tenancy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
> - (b) the tenant has paid rent in advance for that period, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
> - (c) the tenant was not in occupation of the dwelling-house for one or more whole days of that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (2) The amount of repayment to which a tenant is entitled under subsection (1) is to be calculated in accordance with the following formula— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/20) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> R is the rent paid for the final period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> D is the number of whole days of the final period for which the tenant was not in occupation of the dwelling-house; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> P is the number of whole days in that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (3) If the repayment of rent described in subsections (1) and (2) has not been made when the court makes an order for possession under section 21, the court must order the landlord to repay the amount of rent to which the tenant is entitled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
>
> (4) Nothing in this section affects any other right of the tenant to a repayment of rent from the landlord. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
