# Deregulation Act 2015, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Application of sections 33 to 40 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-30d4b11f7d2cb227fd3962ea5960b922][^key-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840][^key-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsections (2) and (3), a provision of sections 33 to 40 applies only to an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England granted on or after the day on which the provision comes into force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

(2) Subject to subsection (3), a provision of sections 33 to 40 does not apply to an assured shorthold tenancy that came into being under section 5(2) of the Housing Act 1988 after the commencement of that provision and on the coming to an end of an assured shorthold tenancy that was granted before the commencement of that provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

(3) At the end of the period of three years beginning with the coming into force of a provision of sections 33 to 38 or section 40, that provision also applies to any assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

- (a) which is in existence at that time, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
- (b) to which that provision does not otherwise apply by virtue of subsection (1) or (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
