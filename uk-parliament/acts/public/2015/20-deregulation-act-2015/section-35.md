# Deregulation Act 2015, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Notice to be provided in relation to periodic assured shorthold tenancies (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7e4d4d2f99d88b4dd13bb7bd11c9ed4a][^key-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840][^key-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 21 of the Housing Act 1988 (recovery of possession on termination of shorthold tenancy), after subsection (4) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]

> (4ZA (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840] In the case of a dwelling-house in England, subsection (4)(a) above has effect with the omission of the requirement for the date specified in the notice to be the last day of a period of the tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ba443a201038ad76c217678840dc9840]
