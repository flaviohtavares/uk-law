## 59. Other measures relating to animals, food and the environment[^c22547901][^c-71986d12ff5e4bf3e5a859372fe6f6a7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 13 makes provision about the following matters—

- (a) destructive imported animals;
- (b) the Farriers Registration Council;
- (c)[^c-a4b9aaa8dacce86f9914678afa06e8cd] joint waste authorities;
- (d) air quality assessments;
- (e) noise abatement zones.
