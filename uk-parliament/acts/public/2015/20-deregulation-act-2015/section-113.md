# Deregulation Act 2015, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/20

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 113. Financial provision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There is to be paid out of money provided by Parliament any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
