## 70. Sale of liqueur confectionery to children under 16: abolition of offence[^c-2cc240bf794670dbd8f9a504d942b78f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 148 of the Licensing Act 2003 (sale of liqueur confectionery to children under 16) is repealed.
