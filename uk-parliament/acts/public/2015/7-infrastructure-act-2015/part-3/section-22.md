# Infrastructure Act 2015, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-12-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/7

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Powers of British Transport Police Force[^key-d25b2e6737c328e26741f154c991be6d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In section 100 of the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001 (jurisdiction of transport police)—

- (a) in subsection (2)(b), after “personal injury” insert “ or damage to property ”, and
- (b) omit subsection (3)(a).

(2) In section 172 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 (duty to give information as to identity of driver etc in certain circumstances), in subsection (2)(a), after “chief officer of police” insert “ or the Chief Constable of the British Transport Police Force ”.
