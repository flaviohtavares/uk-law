## 20. Interpretation of Part 1[^c-d944120ea64bf80a7e687f234b0cdabc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**enactment**” includes an enactment comprised in subordinate legislation within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978;
- “**highway**” has the same meaning as in the Highways Act 1980;
- “**local highway authority**” has the same meaning as in that Act;
- “**users of highways**” includes cyclists and pedestrians.
