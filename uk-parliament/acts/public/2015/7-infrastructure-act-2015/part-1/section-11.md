# Infrastructure Act 2015, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-12-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Monitor: compliance and fines[^key-734451d328a17e1c609a8c143ccd4c36]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the Office of Rail and Road[^key-7b210c1d01f269260d3746ca992c28dc] is satisfied that a strategic highways company has contravened or is contravening—

- (a) section 3(6) (compliance with the Road Investment Strategy), or
- (b) section 6(3) (compliance with directions and regard to guidance),

the Office may take one or more of the steps mentioned in subsection (2).

(2) The Office may—

- (a) give notice to the company as to the contravention and the steps the company must take in order to remedy it;
- (b) require the company to pay a fine to the Secretary of State.
