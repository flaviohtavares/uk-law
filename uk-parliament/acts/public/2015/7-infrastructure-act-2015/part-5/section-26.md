## 26. Timing of appointment of examining authority[^c-8ff4d9dfce7221f3c773e53ac2d44095]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 61 of the Planning Act 2008 (decision as to whether application for order granting development consent should be handled by Panel or single appointed person) for subsection (1) substitute—

> (1) Subsection (2) applies where the Secretary of State has accepted an application for an order granting development consent.
