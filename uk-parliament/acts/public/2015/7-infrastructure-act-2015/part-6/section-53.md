# Infrastructure Act 2015, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-12-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 53. Consequential provision

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations make consequential provision in connection with any provision made by or under this Part (other than section 40).

(2) Regulations under this section may amend, repeal, revoke or otherwise modify the application of any enactment (but, in the case of an Act, only if the Act was passed before the end of the Session in which this Act is passed).

(3) In this section “**enactment**” includes an enactment comprised in subordinate legislation within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978.
