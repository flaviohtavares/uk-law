## 42. Obtaining articles for purposes relating to computer misuse[^key-a96fdc7916149620295d3f33019abe3e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 3A of the Computer Misuse Act 1990 (making, supplying or obtaining articles for use in offence under section 1 or 3), in subsection (3), for “article with a view to” substitute

> article—
>
> - (a) intending to use it to commit, or to assist in the commission of, an offence under section 1, 3 or 3ZA, or
> - (b) with a view to

.
