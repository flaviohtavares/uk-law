# Serious Crime Act 2015, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Confiscation investigations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-33ca063f30d5bb641f67e1f839f942e9] In section 341 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, at the end of subsection (1) insert

> , or
>
> - (c) the extent or whereabouts of realisable property available for satisfying a confiscation order made in respect of him.

(2)[^key-33ca063f30d5bb641f67e1f839f942e9] In section 353 of that Act (requirements where production order not available), in subsection (6)(a), after “of his benefit from his criminal conduct” insert “ or of realisable property available for satisfying a confiscation order made in respect of him ”.

(3)[^key-3216ee9dce28de3870edc94f2d89f58d] In section 388 of that Act (requirements where production order not available), in subsection (6)(a), after “of his benefit from his criminal conduct” insert “ or of realisable property available for satisfying a confiscation order made in respect of him ”.
