# Serious Crime Act 2015, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Enforcement receivers[^c-35b084feab7cd1fcff0eb267550d1656]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 51 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (powers of receivers etc), after subsection (8A) insert—

> (8B) Representations that a person is entitled to make by virtue of subsection (8) do not include representations that are inconsistent with a determination made under section 10A, unless—
>
> - (a) the person was not given a reasonable opportunity to make representations when the determination was made and has not appealed against the determination, or
> - (b) it appears to the court that there would be a serious risk of injustice to the person if the court was bound by the determination;
>
> and the determination does not bind the court if paragraph (a) or (b) applies.
