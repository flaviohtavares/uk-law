# Serious Crime Act 2015, section 27

>

## 27. Enforcement receivers[^key-d10b2211700ea551ea5b8747c5892228]

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 199 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (powers of receivers etc), after subsection (8A) insert—

> (8B) Representations that a person is entitled to make by virtue of subsection (8) do not include representations that are inconsistent with a determination made under section 160A, unless—
>
> - (a) the person was not given a reasonable opportunity to make representations when the determination was made and has not appealed against the determination, or
> - (b) it appears to the court that there would be a serious risk of injustice to the person if the court was bound by the determination;
>
> and the determination does not bind the court if paragraph (a) or (b) applies.
