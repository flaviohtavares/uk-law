# Serious Crime Act 2015, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. External orders and investigations: meaning of “obtaining property”[^key-18dafdf6f5f1610d08d8686a30a2aaa9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 447 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (interpretation of Part 11 (co-operation)), after subsection (6) insert—

> (6A) A person who obtains a pecuniary advantage as a result of or in connection with conduct is to be taken to obtain, as a result of or in connection with the conduct, a sum of money equal to the value of the pecuniary advantage.
>
> (6B) References to property or a pecuniary advantage obtained in connection with conduct include references to property or a pecuniary advantage obtained both in that connection and some other.
