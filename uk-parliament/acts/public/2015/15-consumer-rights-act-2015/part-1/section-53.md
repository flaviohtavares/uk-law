# Consumer Rights Act 2015, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Relation to other law on contract terms[^c-db3ce15b6e7223b356181f3494709838][^c-7beddd931a3f9d315c214e0057c1410e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Nothing in this Chapter affects any enactment or rule of law that imposes a stricter duty on the trader.

(2) This Chapter is subject to any other enactment which defines or restricts the rights, duties or liabilities arising in connection with a service of any description.
