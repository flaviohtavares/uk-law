# Consumer Rights Act 2015, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Service to be performed with reasonable care and skill[^key-7c619e2e70b486592d735892ffb3c5bd][^key-64246ed5056fa4c4d13df2d4eb70a295]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Every contract to supply a service is to be treated as including a term that the trader must perform the service with reasonable care and skill.

(2) See section 54 for a consumer's rights if the trader is in breach of a term that this section requires to be treated as included in a contract.
