# Consumer Rights Act 2015, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/15

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Contract terms that may have different meanings[^c-5263732cd284428189e859d9c69362ec]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a term in a consumer contract, or a consumer notice, could have different meanings, the meaning that is most favourable to the consumer is to prevail.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to the construction of a term or a notice in proceedings on an application for an injunction or interdict under paragraph 3 of Schedule 3.
