# Consumer Rights Act 2015, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2015/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Effect of an unfair term on the rest of a contract[^c-d850d8b2c7d17ab4c657837d8550a15f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a term of a consumer contract is not binding on the consumer as a result of this Part, the contract continues, so far as practicable, to have effect in every other respect.
