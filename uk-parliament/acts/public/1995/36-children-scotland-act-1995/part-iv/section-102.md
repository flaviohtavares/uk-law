# Children (Scotland) Act 1995, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2025-09-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/36

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Removal of duty to report on operation of Children Act 1975.

*Extent: S*

Section 105 of the [^c13127821]Children Act 1975 (which among other things provides that every five years there shall be laid before Parliament by the Secretary of State a report on the operation of such sections of that Act as are for the time being in force) shall cease to have effect.
