# Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2025-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/23

## 1. Functions of traffic commissioners.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A traffic commissioner[^key-b3e81071bc848658f4acd0babc58ed25] shall exercise the functions conferred on him by this Act.

(2) In the exercise of his functions under this Act a traffic commissioner shall act under the general directions of, and shall have regard to any guidance given by, the senior traffic commissioner[^key-f60b94fecc4af23329cfd3b8afb6eed3].
