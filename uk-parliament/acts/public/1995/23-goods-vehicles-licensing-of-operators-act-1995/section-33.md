## 33. Transfer of operating centres.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 4 (which makes provision in relation to certain applications for, or for the variation of, heavy goods vehicle[^key-ea37bb75dc9116830c620adb590834c3] licences where the proposed operating centres of the applicant are already specified in a heavy goods vehicle[^key-d5325a4383c12e62c440233f658c627d] licence) shall have effect.
