# Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995, section 13D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2025-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 13D. Further requirement for standard and restricted licences[^c-16e3ec170bd5659cd0a873a5dccc4957][^M_F_0e6abad3-e6bb-448a-8a9d-ff1a35d1baab]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The requirement of this section is that the provision of the facilities and arrangements for maintaining the vehicles in a fit and serviceable condition (see section 13C(4)) is not prejudiced by reason of the applicant’s having insufficient financial resources for that purpose.
