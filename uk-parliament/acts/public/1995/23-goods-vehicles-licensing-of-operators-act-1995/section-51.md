## 51. Time for bringing proceedings.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 6 of the [^c10975071]Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988 (time for bringing summary proceedings for certain offences) shall apply to an offence under section 9(3)(a) or (b), 38 or 39.
