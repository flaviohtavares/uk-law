# Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995, section 56A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2025-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/23

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 56A. Traffic commissioner may have regard to international obligations[^c-f746853a02b0eb5cfccd397dbe6e7190]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In exercising a function under any provision made by or under this Act in relation to a standard licence authorising both national and international transport operations, a traffic commissioner may have regard to any obligation of the United Kingdom as a party to the Trade and Cooperation Agreement that is relevant to the exercise of the function.

(2) In this section, “**the Trade and Cooperation Agreement**” has the meaning given in section 37 of the European Union (Future Relationship) Act 2020.
