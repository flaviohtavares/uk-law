# Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995, section 42

## 42. Meaning of “officer” and powers of police constables.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In sections 40 and 41 “**officer**” means—

- (a) an examiner appointed under section 66A of the [^c10974991]Road Traffic Act 1988, or
- (b) any person authorised for the purposes of sections 40 and 41 by a traffic commissioner[^key-6f8cb113f1d7783d707741b8e229c412] .

(2) The powers conferred by sections 40 and 41 on an officer shall be exercisable also by a police constable.
