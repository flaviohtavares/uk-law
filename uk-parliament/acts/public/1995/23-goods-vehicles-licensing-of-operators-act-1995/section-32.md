# Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2025-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32. Power to attach conditions on review.[^c10974941]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If, after a notice has been served[^key-42a1e4d919b1728adf25b7f5a93af999] under section 30 in respect of a place specified in a heavy goods vehicle[^key-51f371949cd55bd5631f1c6acae07ca8] licence, no direction is given in respect of the place under section 31, a traffic commissioner may direct—

- (a) that conditions (or additional conditions) such as are mentioned in section 21, 22(1)(c) or 23 be attached to the licence;
- (b) that any conditions already attached to the licence under section 21, 22(1)(c) or 23 be varied.

(2) Any conditions attached to the licence under subsection (1)(a) shall relate or, in the case of conditions such as are mentioned in section 22(1)(c), shall only require a traffic commissioner[^key-d990c3c99033f1191a58ca31e4308798] to be informed of events that relate—

- (a) only to the place referred to in subsection (1), or
- (b) only to that place and any other places in respect of which the traffic commissioner giving the direction[^key-979570f1388bec6b1c5b1d862855f287] has power to attach conditions under that subsection.

(3) Any variation under subsection (1)(b) shall be such as imposes new or further restrictions or requirements—

- (a) only in relation to the place referred to in subsection (1), or
- (b) only in relation to that place and any other places in respect of which the traffic commissioner giving the direction[^key-3453888be6d931ee59f6eccf47514d89] has power to attach conditions under that subsection.

(4) Where a traffic commissioner[^key-1d8e3224761cc78b912f0a103be5dcce] gives a direction in respect of a heavy goods vehicle[^key-6d6d901b6b192fd291567aefd4487699] licence under section 31 or subsection (1)(a) above, he may also vary the licence by directing—

- (a) that any heavy goods[^key-39b7bf3635b659bc6b74d4d985620091] vehicle cease to be specified in the licence;
- (b) that any maximum number of heavy goods vehicles[^key-f0a7b682860ab36d00b15b856ee109eb] specified in the licence under section 6 be reduced;
- (c) that a provision relating to heavy goods vehicles[^key-a3cdd0aeef9c36efb31c7d79f8eb10fa] such as is mentioned in section 5(2) be included in the licence;
- (d) that a provision relating to heavy goods vehicles[^key-a12f96de0a9549de97df37808cbaabe4] such as is mentioned in section 6(1)(b) or (2)(b) be included in the licence.

(5) In this Act any reference, in relation to a heavy goods vehicle[^key-2b363a28a38b35199188d7a579d01206] licence, to a condition attached to the licence under section 21, 22 or 23 includes a reference to any condition such as is mentioned in section 21, 22 or (as the case may be) 23 attached to the licence under subsection (1)(a) above.
