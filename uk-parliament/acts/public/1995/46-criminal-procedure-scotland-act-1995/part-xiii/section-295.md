## 295. Legal custody.

*Extent: S*

Without prejudice to section 13 of the Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989 (c. 45)(legal custody of prisoners),[^c18945751] any person required or authorised by or under this Act or any other enactment to be taken to any place, or to be detained or kept in custody is[^c18945771], while being so taken or detained or kept, [^c18945791]. . . in legal custody.
