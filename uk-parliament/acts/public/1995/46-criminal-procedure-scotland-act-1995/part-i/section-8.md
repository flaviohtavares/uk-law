## 8. Sittings of sheriff and JP courts.[^c19783791]

*Extent: S*

(1) Notwithstanding any enactment or rule of law, a sheriff court or a JP court[^c19783801]—

- (a) shall not be required to sit on any Saturday or Sunday or on a day which by virtue of subsection (2) or (3) below is a court holiday; but
- (b) may sit on any day for the disposal of criminal business.

(2) A sheriff principal may in an order made under section 28(1) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^key-4894677f461a2085c31373caa663a3f9] prescribe in respect of criminal business not more than 11[^c20418691] days, other than Saturdays and Sundays, in a calendar year as court holidays in the sheriff courts within his jurisdiction; and may in the like manner prescribe as an additional court holiday any day which has been proclaimed, under section 1(3) of the [^c14467761]Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971, to be a bank holiday either throughout the United Kingdom or in a place or locality in the United Kingdom within his jurisdiction.

(3) A sheriff principal may[^c19783811] prescribe not more than 11 days, other than Saturdays and Sundays, in a calendar year as court holidays in the JP courts[^c19783831] within his jurisdiction; and he may [^c19923651]. . . prescribe as an additional holiday any day which has been proclaimed, under section 1(3) of the said Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971, to be a bank holiday either throughout the United Kingdom or in a place or locality in the United Kingdom within his jurisdiction.

(4) A sheriff principal may in pursuance of subsection (2) or (3) above prescribe different days as court holidays in relation to different sheriff or JP courts[^c19783851] .
