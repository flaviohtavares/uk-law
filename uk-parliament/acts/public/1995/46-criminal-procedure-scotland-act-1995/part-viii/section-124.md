## 124. Finality of proceedings and Secretary of State’s reference.

*Extent: S*

(1) Nothing in this Part or Part XA[^c14470691] of this Act shall affect the prerogative of mercy.

(2) Subject to Part XA and[^c14470701] section 288AA[^c-d5070f55972b03617ae60b908fc53c89] of this Act and paragraph 13(a) of Schedule 6 to the Scotland Act 1998[^c14470711], every interlocutor and sentence pronounced by the High Court under this Part of this Act shall be final and conclusive and not subject to review by any court whatsoever and , except for the purposes of[^c14470721] [^c-58dd7d2325cdaae376fcbac1c7ff924e]... an appeal under section 288AA of this Act or[^c-035547bec18cdbd3129bc5b4bac940ff] paragraph 13(a) of that Schedule, it shall be incompetent to stay or suspend any execution or diligence issuing from the High Court under this Part of this Act.
