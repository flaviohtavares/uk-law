## 108A. Lord Advocate’s appeal against decision not to impose automatic sentence in certain cases.[^c14470301]

*Extent: S*

Where the court has exercised the power conferred by section or or 205B(3)[^c14470311] of this Act, the Lord Advocate may appeal against that decision.
