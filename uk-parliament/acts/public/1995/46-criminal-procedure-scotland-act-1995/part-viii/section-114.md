# Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995, section 114

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-11-02. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/46

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Applications made orally or in writing.

*Extent: S*

Subject to any provision of this Part of this Act or to rules made under section 305 of this Act[^c18926581] to the contrary, any application to the High Court may be made by the appellant or respondent as the case may be or by counsel on his behalf, orally or in writing.
