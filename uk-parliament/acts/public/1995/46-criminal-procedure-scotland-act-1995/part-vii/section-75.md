## 75. Computation of certain periods.

*Extent: S*

Where the last day of any period mentioned in section 66(6), 67(3), 71C(3)[^key-60cc8fadef740355f0b2c2023b3fb3d1] [^c18987291]. . . or 74 of this Act falls on a Saturday, Sunday or court holiday, such period shall extend to and include the next day which is not a Saturday, Sunday or court holiday.
