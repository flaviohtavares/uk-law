## 33. Bail: no fees exigible.

*Extent: S*

No clerks fees, court fees or other fees or expenses shall be exigible from or awarded against an accused in respect of a decision on bail under section 22A above, an[^c14468391] application for bail or of the appeal of such a decision or[^c14468401] application to the High Court.
