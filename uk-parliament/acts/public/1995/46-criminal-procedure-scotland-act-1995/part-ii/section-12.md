## 12. Instructions by Lord Advocate as to reporting of offences.

*Extent: S*

The Lord Advocate may, from time to time, issue instructions to the[^c-dd57cbd0fbded0905ff375724dca0864] chief constable with regard to the reporting, for consideration of the question of prosecution, of offences alleged to have been committed [^c-d31bfe470374045e8865303cb928254e]....
