# Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995, section 19B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-11-02. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19B. Power of constable in obtaining relevant physical data etc.[^c14468141]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c18908541] A constable may use reasonable force in—

- (a) taking any relevant physical data from a person or securing a person’s compliance with a requirement made under section 18(2), 19(2)(a) or 19A(2)(a) of this Act , or under subsection (3)(a) of section 19AA of this Act where that section applies by virtue of subsection (1)(a) of that section[^c19033441];
- (b) exercising any power conferred by section 18(6), 19(2)(b) or 19A(2)(b) of this Act , or under subsection (3)(b) of section 19AA of this Act where that section applies by virtue of subsection (1)(a) of that section[^c19033461].

(2)[^c18908561] A constable may, with the authority of an officer of a rank no lower than inspector, use reasonable force in (himself) exercising any power conferred by section 18(6A), 19(2)(c) or 19A(2)(c) of this Act , or under subsection (3)(c) of section 19AA of this Act where that section applies by virtue of subsection (1)(a) of that section[^c19033481].
