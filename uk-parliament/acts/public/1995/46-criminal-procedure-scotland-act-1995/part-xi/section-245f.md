## 245F. Breach of restriction of liberty order.[^c14474301]

*Extent: S*

(1) If at any time when a restriction of liberty order is in force it appears

- (a)[^M_F_dfad7721-d2a5-4668-f8b7-62a148915821] except in a case to which paragraph (b) below applies,[^c18942651] to the court which made the order or[^c18942771]
- (b)[^c18942791] where a copy of the order was, under section 245A(5)(a)(ii) or 245E(7)(a) of this Act, sent to the clerk of a different court, to that different court (or, if there has been more than one such sending, the different court to which such a copy has most recently been so sent),

that the offender has failed to comply with any of the requirements of the order the court in question[^c18942811] may issue a citation requiring the offender to appear before it[^c18942831] at such time as may be specified in the citation or, if it appears to that court[^c18942851] to be appropriate, it may issue a warrant for the arrest of the offender.

(1A)[^c18943071] The unified citation provisions apply in relation to a citation under this section as they apply in relation to a citation under section 216(3)(a) of this Act.

(2) If it is proved to the satisfaction of that court[^c18942901] that the offender has failed without reasonable excuse to comply with any of the requirements of the order it[^c18942941] may by order—

- (a) without prejudice to the continuance in force of the order, impose a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale;
- (b) vary the restriction of liberty order; or
- (c) revoke that order.

(2A)[^c19837941] For the purposes of subsection (2) above, evidence of one witness shall be sufficient evidence.

(3) A fine imposed under this section in respect of a failure to comply with the requirements of a restriction of liberty order shall be deemed for the purposes of any enactment to be a sum adjudged to be paid by or in respect of a conviction or a penalty imposed on a person summarily convicted.

(4) Where a court[^c18942981] varies a restriction of liberty order under subsection (2) above it may do so in any of the ways mentioned in paragraph (a) of section 245E(2) of this Act.
