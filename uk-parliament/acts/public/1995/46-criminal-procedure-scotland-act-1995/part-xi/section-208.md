## 208. Detention of children convicted on indictment.

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c18913521] Subject to sections 205 and 205ZC(5)[^c-d28c761d2d905fe5a617cb8e4f58412b] of this Act and subsection (3) below[^c18987211] , where a child is convicted on indictment and the court is of the opinion that no other method of dealing with him is appropriate, it may sentence him to be detained for a period which it shall specify in the sentence; and the child shall during that period be liable to be detained in such place (in any part of the United Kingdom)[^c-9c7a238c20db0650f3be2713c458e8f8] and on such conditions as the Secretary of State may , subject to section 208A,[^c-e08183a9d20c611fbb9e0e319d8fa1ef] direct.

(1A)[^c20919871] Where the court imposes a sentence of detention on a child, the court must—

- (a) state its reasons for the opinion that no other method of dealing with the child is appropriate, and
- (b) have those reasons entered in the record of the proceedings.

(2)[^c19030001] Subsections (1) and (1A) above are[^c20919891] subject to—

- (a) section 51A(2) of the Firearms Act 1968 (minimum sentences for certain firearms offences); and
- (b) section 29(9) of the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006 (minimum sentence of detention for certain offences relating to dangerous weapons).

(3)[^c18987251] If the child is under the age of 16 years, the power conferred by subsection (1) above shall not be exercisable in respect of a conviction for an offence under section 9(1) of the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004 (asp 8) or that section as applied by section 234AA(11) of this Act.
