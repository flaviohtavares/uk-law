## 248C. Application of sections 248A and 248B.[^c14474501]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Secretary of State may by order prescribe which courts, or class or classes of courts, may make orders under section 248A or 248B of this Act [^c-1df18ca74056adfc9bffa4fb6d712521]....

(2) An order made under subsection (1) above shall be made by statutory instrument and any such instrument shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
