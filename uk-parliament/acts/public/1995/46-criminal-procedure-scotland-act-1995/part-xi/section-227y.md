## 227Y. Applications to vary, revoke and discharge community payback orders[^c20919581]

*Extent: S*

(1) The appropriate court may, on the application of either of the persons mentioned in subsection (2), vary, revoke or discharge a community payback order in accordance with section 227Z.

(2) Those persons are—

- (a) the offender on whom the order was imposed,
- (b) the responsible officer in relation to the offender.
