## 271Z. Appeal against a refusal to vary or discharge a witness anonymity order[^c-ad57f6d736639648bab48cdff7b984da][^c-f19ec92dcab2752e44bde5d5e8fcf2e4]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) an appeal is brought under section 271V(1)(f) against a refusal to discharge or vary a witness anonymity order, and
- (b) the court hearing the appeal[^c-396ea78e56f0ef21076a7aaa9b637678] determines that the decision of the judge at first instance was wrong in law.

(2) The court hearing the appeal[^c-a624e931ccfd590c88dd915d54797d1a] must discharge the order, or make the variation, as the case requires.

(3) If, in the case of a variation, the court hearing the appeal[^c-fd220a8885c744813101c1dc1a16a1cb] determines that it is appropriate to make an additional variation in view of the provisions of sections 271R and 271S, the court may do so.
