## 271W. Appeal against the making of a witness anonymity order[^key-ad57f6d736639648bab48cdff7b984da][^key-f19ec92dcab2752e44bde5d5e8fcf2e4]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) an appeal is brought under section 271V(1)(a) against the making of a witness anonymity order, and
- (b) the court hearing the appeal[^key-2321c4399852fc4edc01ee3b4ac19dd2] determines that the decision of the judge at first instance was wrong in law.

(2) The court hearing the appeal[^key-78e6774b40d391820f06486dfea4312b] must discharge the order and the trial is to proceed as if the order had not been made.
