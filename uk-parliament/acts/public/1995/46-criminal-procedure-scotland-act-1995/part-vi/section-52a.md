## 52A. Remit of certain mentally disordered persons from district court to sheriff court[^c18911291]

*Extent: S*

Where—

- (a) a person has been charged in a JP court[^c19795101] with an offence punishable by imprisonment; and
- (b) it appears to the court that the person has a mental disorder,

the JP court shall remit the person to the sheriff in the manner provided by section 7(9) and (10) of this Act.
