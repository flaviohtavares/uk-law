# Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995, section 60B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-11-02. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/46

## 60B. Intervention orders[^c18926681]

*Extent: S*

The court may instead of making a compulsion[^c18933001] order [^c19795811]. . . or a guardianship order under section 57(2)(c) or 58(1A) of this Act, make an intervention order(as defined in section 53(1) of the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (asp 4) where it considers that it would be appropriate to do so.
