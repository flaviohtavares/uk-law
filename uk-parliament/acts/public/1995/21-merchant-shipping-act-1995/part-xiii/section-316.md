# Merchant Shipping Act 1995, section 316

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-06-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 316. Short title and commencement.[^c14529651]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Merchant Shipping Act 1995.

(2) This Act shall come into force on 1st January 1996.
