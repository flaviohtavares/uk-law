## 310. Application of Act to hovercraft.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The enactments and instruments with respect to which provision may be made by Order in Council under section 1(1)(h) of the [^c14529421]Hovercraft Act 1968 shall include this Act (except Parts I and II) and any instrument made thereunder.
