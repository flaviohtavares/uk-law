# Merchant Shipping Act 1995, section 110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-11-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/21

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Payments of seamen’s wages.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except as provided by or under Part III or any other enactment, the wages due to a seaman under a crew agreement relating to a United Kingdom fishing vessel shall be paid to him in full.
