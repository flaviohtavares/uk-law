# Merchant Shipping Act 1995, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-11-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/21

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 113. Restriction on assignment of and charge upon wages.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Nothing in section 34 shall affect the operation of—

- (a) the [^c14526411]Attachment of Earnings Act 1971, or
- (b) without prejudice to Article 97(2) of the [^c14526421]Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, Articles 73 to 79 and 97 to 105 of that Order,

in relation to wages due to a person employed in a fishing vessel.

(2) The provisions of the [^c14526431]Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 and the Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 relating to the attachment of wages shall apply in relation to wages due to a person employed in a fishing vessel as they apply in relation to other wages.
