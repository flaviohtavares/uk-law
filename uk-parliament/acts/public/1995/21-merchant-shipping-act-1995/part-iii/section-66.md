# Merchant Shipping Act 1995, section 66

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-06-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/21

## 66. Failure to deliver cancelled or suspended certificate.[^key-fd242e2587628a2bf1bdfcec301cfe3b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a person fails to deliver a certificate as required under section 61, 62 or 63 he shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
