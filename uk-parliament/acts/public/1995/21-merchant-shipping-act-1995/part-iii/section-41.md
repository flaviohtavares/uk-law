# Merchant Shipping Act 1995, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-11-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/21

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Remedies of master for remuneration, disbursements and liabilities.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The master of a ship shall have the same lien for his remuneration, and all disbursements or liabilities properly made or incurred by him on account of the ship, as a seaman has for his wages.
