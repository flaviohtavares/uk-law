# Merchant Shipping Act 1995, section 254

## 254. Referral of questions as to powers between authorities.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If any question arises between a harbour authority or conservancy authority and a general lighthouse authority as to their respective powers under sections 252 and 253 in relation to any place in or near an approach to a harbour or tidal water, that question shall, on the application of either authority, be referred to the Secretary of State for his decision.

(2) Any decision of the Secretary of State under this section shall be final.
