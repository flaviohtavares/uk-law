# Merchant Shipping Act 1995, section 273

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-06-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/21

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 273. Transmission of particulars of certain deaths on ships.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-eb5ef43feb82a419303c606e0d59bc2b] Where—

- (a) an inquest is held into a death or subsection (2) below applies; and[^c-7bbf40045f9d025c2f3d5e1b33d15517]
- (b) it appears to the coroner that the death in question is such as is mentioned in section 108(2) or in that subsection as extended (with or without amendments) by virtue of section 307,

it shall be the duty of the coroner to send to the Registrar General of Shipping and Seamen particulars in respect of the deceased of a kind prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State.

(2)[^c-e853d5170a765f8065f480f7e81f1a57] This subsection applies where—

- (a) in England and Wales, an investigation under Part 1 of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 into a person's death is discontinued under section 4 of that Act (cause of death becoming clear before inquest[^c-dac0f1af9f281b7a5f044142fc7fa22b]); or
- (b) in Northern Ireland, a preliminary investigation is made of a dead body as a result of which the coroner is satisfied that an inquest is unnecessary.
