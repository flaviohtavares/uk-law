# Merchant Shipping Act 1995, section 203

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-06-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 203. Individual transfers of local lighthouses to harbour authorities.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A general lighthouse authority may, at any time, with the consent of the Secretary of State, transfer to a statutory harbour authority[^c14527901] any lighthouse, buoy or beacon held by the general lighthouse authority which—

- (a) is situated in the area of that harbour authority or on land adjacent to that area or any part of it; and
- (b) appears to the general lighthouse authority to be of benefit solely or mainly to ships within, or entering or leaving, that harbour authority’s area.
