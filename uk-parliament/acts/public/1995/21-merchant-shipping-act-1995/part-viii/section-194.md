## 194. Returns and information to Secretary of State.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Every general lighthouse authority and their officers shall give to the Secretary of State all such returns, explanations or information in relation to the lighthouses, buoys or beacons within their area and their management as the Secretary of State requires.
