# Merchant Shipping Act 1995, section 130E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-06-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 130E. Interpretation of Chapter IA.[^c14526641][^c-ac1793e613af0b924f498056774ab9d6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**prescribe**” means prescribe by regulations;
- “**ship, passenger and goods dues**” has the same meaning—
  - (a) in relation to Great Britain, as in the [^c14526651]Harbours Act 1964; and
  - (b) in relation to Northern Ireland, as in the [^c14526661]Harbours Act (Northern Ireland) 1970;
- “**waste reception facilities**” has the meaning given by section 130A(1).
