# Environment Act 1995, section 72

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/25

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. National Park grant.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may make grants to a National Park authority for such purposes, of such amounts and on such terms and conditions as he thinks fit.

(2) Before determining the amount of any grant which he proposes to make to a National Park authority in Wales[^key-290a5e5546592faaf861b3be1ca823ef] under this section, or the purpose for which it is to be made, the Secretary of State shall consult, according to whether the relevant Park is in England or in Wales, either *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-37b39cf532db30b5dfa3ab77c1c76d48] Natural England[^key-eb26738c54d911afc0d09e0c162eadcf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-37b39cf532db30b5dfa3ab77c1c76d48] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-37b39cf532db30b5dfa3ab77c1c76d48] the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^key-2f6949f9d1dbaefee4ab548f57cc4201].

(3) The consent of the Treasury shall be required for the making of a grant under this section.
