# Environment Act 1995, section 100

## 100. Meaning of “drainage” in certain enactments.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In the definition of “**drainage**” in section 113(1) of the [^c14540451]Water Resources Act 1991, after paragraph (c) there shall be added the words

> and
>
> - (d) the carrying on, for any purpose, of any other practice which involves management of the level of water in a watercourse;

.

(2) For the definition of “**drainage**” in section 72(1) of the [^c14540461]Land Drainage Act 1991 there shall be substituted—

> - “**drainage**” includes—
>   - (a) defence against water (including sea water);
>   - (b) irrigation, other than spray irrigation;
>   - (c) warping; and
>   - (d) the carrying on, for any purpose, of any other practice which involves management of the level of water in a watercourse;

.
