## 102. Sea fisheries.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4] The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4] [^c14540491]Sea Fisheries Regulation Act 1966 shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]

(2) In section 2 (constitution of local fisheries committees) in subsection (2) (which includes provision for the members appointed by the Minister to be persons acquainted with the needs and opinions of the fishing interests of that district) after the words “of that district” there shall be added the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4] “ or as being persons having knowledge of, or expertise in, marine environmental matters ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4].

(3) After that subsection there shall be inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]

> (2A) In addition to the members appointed as mentioned in subsection (1) above, a local fisheries committee may appoint such number of persons with knowledge of or expertise in marine environmental matters as it thinks fit as further members of the committee for those occasions on which it is considering any proposed byelaw under section 5 below by virtue of section 5A below, or any proposed amendment or revocation of such a byelaw. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]

(4) At the end of that section there shall be added— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]

> (7) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]**marine environmental matters *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]
>
> - (a) the conservation or enhancement of the natural beauty or amenity of marine or coastal areas (including their geological or physiographical features) or of any features of archaeological or historic interest in such areas; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]
> - (b) the conservation of flora or fauna which are dependent on, or associated with, a marine or coastal environment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]

(5) After section 5 (byelaws for regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4] etc *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4] of sea fisheries) there shall be inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]

> **5A. Byelaws under section 5 for marine environmental purposes. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]**
>
> (1) Any power to make byelaws conferred by section 5 above may be exercised for marine environmental purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]
>
> (2) The power to make byelaws under section 5 above by virtue of this section is in addition to, and not in derogation from, the power to make byelaws under that section otherwise than by virtue of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]
>
> (3) Byelaws under section 5 above by virtue of this section shall be submitted for confirmation under section 7 below— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]
>
> - (a) in the case of a byelaw which is to have effect in England, only after consultation with the Nature Conservancy Council for England; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]
> - (b) in the case of a byelaw which is to have effect in Wales, only after consultation with the Countryside Council for Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]
>
> (4) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]**marine environmental purposes *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]**” means the purposes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]
>
> - (a) of conserving or enhancing the natural beauty or amenity of marine or coastal areas (including their geological or physiographical features) or of any features of archaeological or historic interest in such areas; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]
> - (b) of conserving flora or fauna which are dependent on, or associated with, a marine or coastal environment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4]

(6) In section 8 (power of Minister to revoke byelaws if it appears necessary or desirable for the maintenance or improvement of fisheries) after the words “maintenance or improvement of fisheries” there shall be inserted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4] “ or for marine environmental purposes, within the meaning of section 5A above, ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b6bc9e3c93d5b63b870765a2ae154dd4].
