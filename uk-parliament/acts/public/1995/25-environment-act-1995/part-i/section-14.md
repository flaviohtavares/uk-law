## 14. Regional flood defence committees.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] There shall be committees, known as regional flood defence committees, for the purpose of carrying out the functions which fall to be carried out by such committees by virtue of this Act and the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] 1991 Act *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] Subject to Schedule 4 to this Act (which makes provision for the alteration of the boundaries of and the amalgamation of the areas of regional flood defence committees)— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] there shall be a regional flood defence committee for each of the areas for which there was an old committee immediately before the transfer date; but *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] where under section 165(2) or (3) of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] 1991 Act *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] any function of the Agency falls to be carried out at a place beyond the seaward boundaries of the area of any regional flood defence committee, that place shall be assumed for the purposes of this Act and the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] 1991 Act *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] to be within the area of the regional flood defence committee to whose area the area of sea where that place is situated is adjacent. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] The Agency shall maintain a principal office for the area of each regional flood defence committee. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] In this section “old committee” means a regional flood defence committee for the purposes of section 9 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] 1991 Act *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
