## 18. Composition of local flood defence committees.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] Subject to subsections (2) and (3) below, a local flood defence scheme shall provide that any local flood defence committee to which it relates shall consist of not less than eleven and not more than fifteen members. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] A regional flood defence committee may include in a local flood defence scheme which they submit to the Agency a recommendation that a committee to which the scheme relates should consist of a number of members greater than fifteen; and a scheme so submitted shall be taken to provide for the number of members of a committee if it contains a recommendation under this subsection relating to that committee. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] The power conferred on each of the Ministers by section 17(8) above shall include power to direct that a committee to which a recommendation under subsection (2) above relates shall consist either of the recommended number of members or of some other number of members greater than fifteen. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] A local flood defence committee shall consist of— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] a chairman appointed from among their own members by the regional flood defence committee; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] other members appointed by that committee; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] members appointed, in accordance with and subject to the terms of the local flood defence scheme, by or on behalf of constituent councils. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] The number of members appointed to a local flood defence committee by or on behalf of constituent councils shall be one more than the total number of members appointed by the regional flood defence committee. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] In appointing a person to be a member of a local flood defence committee, the regional flood defence committee shall have regard to the desirability of appointing a person who has experience of, and has shown capacity in, some matter relevant to the functions of the committee to which he is appointed. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] Any person who, immediately before the transfer date is, by virtue of an appointment by an old regional committee or by or on behalf of any council, the chairman or a member of a local flood defence committee which is continued in force by virtue of section 17(2) above shall be treated, on and after that date, for the remainder of the period for which he would, under the terms of his appointment, have held office in relation to the local flood defence committee— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] as if he had been appointed as such under this section by the regional flood defence committee or, as the case may be, by or on behalf of that council; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] in the case of the chairman, as if he were a member of the regional flood defence committee. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(8 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] The councils of every county, county borough, metropolitan district or London borough any part of which is in a local flood defence district shall be the constituent councils for the local flood defence committee for that district, and the Common Council of the City of London shall be a constituent council for the local flood defence committee of any local flood defence district which comprises any part of the City. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(9 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] In this section “old regional committee” means a regional flood defence committee for the purposes of section 9 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] 1991 Act *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
