# Environment Act 1995, section 41A

## 41A. Charges in respect of the EU greenhouse gas emissions trading scheme[^c-231d0c0416ccd93fbbc79b8e86da7ee7][^c-d16530e6dfe93acb845cee6588cb2478][^c-f89872fed6284103696e5e8ab34f91a2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-65cfa8d047ae715cace186c30ee469a0] Each of the charging authorities[^c-de671768acb0a07331b2c606e1640dd5] may require the payment to it of such charges as may from time to time be prescribed—

- (a)[^c-c50feed435ac5dc4d5f623571ea8b7eb] as a means of recovering costs incurred by it in performing functions conferred under or by virtue of—
  - (i) the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2005 (S.I. 2005/925);
  - (ii) the Aviation Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2010 (S.I. 2010/1996);
  - (iii) the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012 (S.I. 2012/3038).
- (b) in respect of—
  - (i) an application to open an account that, under the Registries Regulation 2013[^c-7f96dd47e92195b35e147eb627c26809], is required to be held in the Kyoto Protocol Registry[^c-016f74ae495dee5695a9983244c20b77];
  - (ii) the subsistence of such an account;
  - (iii) the updating of information provided to the Agency in relation to such an account;

and in this section “prescribed” means specified in, or determined under, a scheme (in this section referred to as a “charging scheme”) made under this section by the charging authority[^c-a4c94ae0d6093e47d5f41b55020c8d18] in question.

(1A) A charging scheme may—

- (a) make different provision for different cases, including different provision in relation to different persons, circumstances or localities;
- (b) provide for the times at which, and the manner in which, the charges prescribed by the scheme are to be paid;
- (c) revoke or amend any previous charging scheme;
- (d) contain supplemental, incidental, consequential or transitional provision for the purposes of the scheme.

(1B) A charging scheme must specify, in relation to any charge prescribed by the scheme, the description of person who is liable to pay the charge.

(1C) A charging authority[^c-8ec80f04103e1fdc870c479de5230cbd] may not make a charging scheme unless the provisions of the scheme have been approved by the Secretary of State , the Welsh Ministers[^c-ee041e5ea8f77a1bc0ebf7e32d7f9f82] or the Scottish Ministers under section 42.

(2) If the Agency—

- (a) proposes to prescribe charges under subsection (1)(b)[^c-75f94f532dbc42cabd87d76cb82df122] or to amend any provision for such charges included in a charging scheme, and
- (b) notifies another charging authority[^c-c0a78a6d6e60aef0ce3139f2792251b0] of its proposals,

the Agency and that other charging authority[^c-f113d6960d478610a4328f98a919c169] shall each include in a charging scheme (subject to approval by the Secretary of State , the Welsh Ministers[^c-8eece7968d02ca26f355c62bca9d1c4a] or the Scottish Ministers under section 42[^c-aa032fe5eee51624a4f083a5619414ff]) provision giving effect to the proposals.

(3) If the Agency revises any proposals of which it has given notification under subsection (2) above, and notifies the relevant charging authority[^c-21513b8263d9d2e3bd07d84d4662696a] accordingly, the obligations imposed by that subsection apply in relation to the proposals as revised.

(4) A notification under subsection (2) or (3) above shall include details of the amount of the proposed charges.

(5) The Natural Resources Body for Wales and[^c-c49118349a8e63dac4777d1cb4345c4c] SEPA shall pass on to the Agency any charges under subsection (1)(b)[^c-f499dd1fdf4ed289b28a27373450e588] that they receive[^c-861fc805d8027db5420981fbb8077151].

(6) A charging scheme made by the Agency may require the payment to the Agency of such charges as may from time to time be prescribed in respect of—

- (a)[^c-227136e255f6911e486233dcc9f59b9b] an application to open an account in the Kyoto Protocol Registry[^c-363c5e3cbf60361d1b85cf6fedd060bc], other than one that under the Registries Regulation 2013[^c-40671f93bc46b966f50d5261e1022b0e] is required to be held in that registry;
- (b) the subsistence of such an account;
- (c) the updating of information provided to the Agency in relation to such an account.

(7)[^c-0b13bcf1fa9685d8906895b84e2d0855] In this section—

- “charging authority” means the Agency, the Natural Resources Body for Wales or SEPA;
- “the Registries Regulation 2013” means Commission Regulation (EU) No 389/2013 establishing a Union Registry pursuant to [Directive 2003/87/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2003/0087) of the European Parliament and of the Council, Decisions No [290/2004/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2004/0290) and No [406/2009/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2009/0406) of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Regulations (EU) No 920/2010 and No 1193/2011, as amended from time to time; and
- “the Kyoto Protocol Registry” means the registry administered on behalf of the United Kingdom for the purposes of its obligations as a party to the Kyoto Protocol to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change.[^c-097f5cdb94765cb0532fb04915ab27b4]
