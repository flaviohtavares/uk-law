# Environment Act 1995, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Incidental power of the new Agencies to impose charges.[^c14537341]

*Extent: E+W+S*

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-d95fbfb3c0dca88a9dd92ad9e179a20d] Without prejudice to the generality of its powers by virtue of section 37(1)(a) above and subject to any such express provision with respect to charging by a new Agency as is contained in the preceding provisions of this Chapter or any other enactment, each new Agency shall have power to fix and recover charges for services and facilities provided in the course of carrying out its functions.

*Extent: S*

(2)[^key-3faf4df3db537d05a47414e88527089e] In subsection (1) above, “**enactment**” includes an enactment comprised in, or in an instrument made under, an Act of the Scottish Parliament.
