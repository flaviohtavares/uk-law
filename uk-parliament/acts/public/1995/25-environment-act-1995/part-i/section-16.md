## 16. Change of composition of regional flood defence committee.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] The Agency may, in accordance with the following provisions of this section, from time to time make a determination varying the total number of members of a regional flood defence committee. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] The Agency shall submit any determination under subsection (1) above to the relevant Minister. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] For the purposes of this section— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] the total number of members of a regional flood defence committee shall not be less than eleven; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] any determination by the Agency under subsection (1) above that a regional flood defence committee should consist of more than seventeen members shall be provisional and shall take effect only if the relevant Minister makes an order under subsection (4) below. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] If the Agency submits a provisional determination to the relevant Minister with respect to any regional flood defence committee and he considers that the committee should consist of more than seventeen members, he may by order made by statutory instrument— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] confirm it; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] substitute for the number of members determined by the Agency some other number not less than seventeen. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] Subject to the following provisions of this section, whenever— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] the total number of members of a regional flood defence committee is varied under this section, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] the relevant Minister considers it necessary or expedient to make an order under this subsection, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

the relevant Minister shall by order made by statutory instrument specify the number of members to be appointed to the committee by each of the constituent councils. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] An order under subsection (5) above shall relate— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] where paragraph (a) of that subsection applies, to times after the coming into force of the variation; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] where paragraph (b) of that subsection applies, to such times as are specified in the order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] An order under subsection (5) above shall be so framed that the total number of members appointed under section 15(1)(a) and (b) above is one less than the number of those appointed by or on behalf of constituent councils. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(8 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] For the purpose of determining for the purposes of subsection (5) above the number of persons to be appointed to a regional flood defence committee by or on behalf of each constituent council, the relevant Minister— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] if he considers it to be inappropriate that that council should appoint a member of the committee, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] if he considers that one or more members should be appointed jointly by that council and one or more other constituent councils, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

may include provision to that effect in the order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(9 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] In this section— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- “member”, in relation to a regional flood defence committee, includes the chairman of the committee; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- “the relevant Minister” has the same meaning as in section 15 above. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
