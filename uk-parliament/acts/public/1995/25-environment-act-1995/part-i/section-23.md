## 23. Functions of staff commission.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The functions of the staff commission established under section 12 of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-69807589917c39cc17f2e8e6391dbad8] [^c14536661]Local Government *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-69807589917c39cc17f2e8e6391dbad8] etc. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-69807589917c39cc17f2e8e6391dbad8] (Scotland) Act 1994 shall include— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-69807589917c39cc17f2e8e6391dbad8]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-69807589917c39cc17f2e8e6391dbad8] considering and keeping under review the arrangements for the transfer to *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-69807589917c39cc17f2e8e6391dbad8] SEPA *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-69807589917c39cc17f2e8e6391dbad8], in consequence of this Act or of any scheme made under it, of staff employed by local authorities; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-69807589917c39cc17f2e8e6391dbad8]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-69807589917c39cc17f2e8e6391dbad8] considering such staffing problems arising out of, consequential on or connected with any provision of, or scheme made under, this Act as may be referred to them by the Secretary of State or by any local authority; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-69807589917c39cc17f2e8e6391dbad8]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-69807589917c39cc17f2e8e6391dbad8] advising the Secretary of State as to the steps necessary to safeguard the interests of the staff referred to in paragraph (a) above. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-69807589917c39cc17f2e8e6391dbad8]
