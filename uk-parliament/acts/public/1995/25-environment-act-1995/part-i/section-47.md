# Environment Act 1995, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Grants to the new Agencies.[^c-b4f295453d20723ccf419c28d6802a95]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The appropriate Minister may, in the case of the Agency only[^c14537521] with the approval of the Treasury, make to a new Agency grants of such amounts, and on such terms, as he thinks fit.
