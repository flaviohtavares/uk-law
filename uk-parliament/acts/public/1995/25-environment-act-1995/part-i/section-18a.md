# Environment Act 1995, section 18A

## 18A. Power to revoke local flood defence schemes[^M_F_a1e0a21b-bcb1-4184-b3da-5ecf2dcdd75a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] The Secretary of State may by order made by statutory instrument revoke any local flood defence scheme. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] The power to make an order under this section shall include power to make such supplemental, consequential and transitional provision as the Secretary of State considers appropriate. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] The provision which may be made under subsection (2) above includes provision altering— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] the total number of members of the regional flood defence committee in whose area the local flood defence district created by the scheme was situated; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] the total number of such members to be appointed by the constituent councils of that committee, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

and subsections (7) and (8) of section 16 of this Act shall apply in relation to so much of an order under this section as is made by virtue of this subsection as they apply in relation to an order under subsection (5) of that section. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] Paragraphs 2 to 6 of Schedule 4 to this Act, apart from paragraph 3(3), apply in relation to an order under this section as they apply in relation to an order under that Schedule, reading references there to the relevant Minister as references to the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
