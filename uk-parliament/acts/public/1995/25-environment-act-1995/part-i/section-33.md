## 33. General duties as respects the state of the environment and effects of pollution.[^key-c2b5c23d3c84ee070e0adec60f1f87cb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) SEPA *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-679dd26e550e53681ba22eced4591d9c]’s pollution control powers shall be exercisable for the purpose of preventing or minimising, or remedying or mitigating the effects of, pollution of the environment. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-679dd26e550e53681ba22eced4591d9c]

(2) SEPA shall *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2882025dc3f5d2ccfebe9896d3fd9653] may *(Extent: S)*, for the purpose—

- (a) of facilitating the carrying out of its pollution control *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-20bffc89527353212adc7b04c65b7fe2] functions; or
- (b) of enabling it to form an opinion of the general state of pollution of *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e35a7ace69434a1e2d49e4898e980f29] the environment,

compile information relating to such pollution *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f1fd4ceacb99646cbd491d97f2e64644] the general state of the environment *(Extent: S)* (whether the information is acquired by SEPA carrying out observations or is obtained in any other way).

(3) If required by the Secretary of State to do so, SEPA shall—

- (a) carry out assessments (whether generally or for such particular purpose as may be specified in the requirement) of the effect, or likely effect, on the environment of existing or potential levels of pollution of the environment and report its findings to the Secretary of State; or
- (b) prepare and send to the Secretary of State a report identifying—
  - (i) the options which SEPA considers to be available for preventing or minimising, or remedying or mitigating the effects of, pollution of the environment, whether generally or in cases or circumstances specified in the requirement; and
  - (ii) the costs and benefits of such options as are identified by SEPA pursuant to sub-paragraph (i) above.

(4) SEPA *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24030ab8d76dab7a55a5b498e80cd13c] shall follow developments in technology and techniques for preventing or minimising, or remedying or mitigating the effects of, pollution of the environment. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24030ab8d76dab7a55a5b498e80cd13c]
