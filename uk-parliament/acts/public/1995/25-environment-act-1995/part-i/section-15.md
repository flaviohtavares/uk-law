## 15. Composition of regional flood defence committees.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] Subject to subsection (2) below, a regional flood defence committee shall consist of the following, none of whom shall be a member of the Agency, that is to say— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] a chairman and a number of other members appointed by the relevant Minister; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] two members appointed by the Agency; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] a number of members appointed by or on behalf of the constituent councils. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] Any person who immediately before the transfer date is, by virtue of his appointment— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] by a Minister of the Crown, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] by or on behalf of any council, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] by the National Rivers Authority, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

the chairman or a member of an old committee which, by virtue of section 14 above, is replaced by a new committee shall be treated, on and after that date, for the remainder of the period for which he would, under the terms of his appointment, have held office in relation to the old committee, as if he had been appointed as the chairman or, as the case may be, a member of the new committee, and on the same terms, by that Minister or, as the case may be, by or on behalf of that council or, in the case of a person appointed by the National Rivers Authority, by the Agency. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] Subject to section 16 below and to any order under Schedule 4 to this Act amalgamating the areas of any two or more regional flood defence committees— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] the total number of members of a new committee for any area shall be the same as the total number of members of the old committee for that area immediately before the transfer date; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] the number of members to be appointed to a new committee for any area by or on behalf of each of the constituent councils or, as the case may be, jointly by or on behalf of more than one of them shall be the same as the number of members of the old committee for that area which fell to be so appointed immediately before the transfer date. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] In any case where— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] the appointment of one or more members of a regional flood defence committee is (by virtue of subsection (3) above or an order under section 16(5) below), to be made jointly by more than one constituent council, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] the councils by whom that appointment is to be made are unable to agree on an appointment, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

the member or members in question shall be appointed by the relevant Minister on behalf of those councils. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] In appointing a person to be the chairman or a member of a regional flood defence committee under subsection (1)(a) or (c) or (4) above the relevant Minister or, as the case may be, a constituent council shall have regard to the desirability of appointing a person who has experience of, and has shown capacity in, some matter relevant to the functions of the committee. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] The councils of every county, county borough, metropolitan district or London borough any part of which is in the area of a regional flood defence committee shall be the constituent councils for the regional flood defence committee for that area, and the Common Council of the City of London shall be a constituent council for the regional flood defence committee for any area which comprises any part of the City. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] In this section— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]

- “old committee” has the same meaning as in section 14 above; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- “new committee” means a regional flood defence committee established under section 14 above; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
- “the relevant Minister”— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
  - (a) in relation to the regional flood defence committee for an area the whole or the greater part of which is in Wales, means the Secretary of State; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
  - (b) in relation to any other regional flood defence committee, means *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258] the Minister *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-71ef21c83d50d0dae56188d6e8258258]
