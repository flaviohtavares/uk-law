## 5. General functions with respect to pollution control.[^c14536041]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An appropriate agency's[^c-ad2e37fcda9e7703a01bcb0060e0df00] pollution control powers shall be exercisable for the purpose of preventing or minimising, or remedying or mitigating the effects of, pollution of the environment.

(2) An appropriate agency[^c-7e4653d03dfcb1f8efda32cc1b52bce9] shall, for the purpose—

- (a) of facilitating the carrying out of its pollution control functions, or
- (b) of enabling it to form an opinion of the general state of pollution of the environment,

compile information relating to such pollution (whether the information is acquired by the Agency carrying out observations or is obtained in any other way).

(3) If required by the appropriate national authority[^c-2f48eb07aeca553ba67949c3b223b579] to do so, an appropriate agency[^c-fc7f81f8f37e3296819ecfb9695276fe] shall—

- (a) carry out assessments (whether generally or for such particular purpose as may be specified in the requirement) of the effect, or likely effect, on the environment of existing or potential levels of pollution of the environment and report its findings to the appropriate national authority[^c-a5c2776c9e2c5332a9bf17d42c47c918]; or
- (b) prepare and send to the appropriate national authority[^c-515c1b33f9bfcce93f0f326972a87f2f] a report identifying—
  - (i) the options which the appropriate agency[^c-55b36466e0b931ac3113693843e8ae02] considers to be available for preventing or minimising, or remedying or mitigating the effects of, pollution of the environment, whether generally or in cases or circumstances specified in the requirement; and
  - (ii) the costs and benefits of such options as are identified by the appropriate agency pursuant to sub-paragraph (i) above.

(4) An appropriate agency[^c-fa86ea986def81ac32966db300349f6d] shall follow developments in technology and techniques for preventing or minimising, or remedying or mitigating the effects of, pollution of the environment.

(5) In this section, “**pollution control powers**” and “**pollution control functions**”, in relation to an appropriate agency[^c-58a11370b67165b17de0c8b43302a458], mean respectively its powers or its functions under or by virtue of the following enactments, that is to say—

- (a) the [^c14536051]Alkali, &c, Works Regulation Act 1906;
- (b) Part I of the [^c14536061]Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974;
- (c) Part I of the [^c14536071]Control of Pollution Act 1974;
- (d) the [^c14536081]Control of Pollution (Amendment) Act 1989;
- (e) Parts I,[^c-23b9e4d01d4f15e4c232915b6f0105ae] II and IIA of the 1990 Act (integrated pollution control etc, waste on land and contaminated land);
- (f) Chapter III of Part IV of the [^c14536091]Water Industry Act 1991 (special category effluent);
- (g) Part III and sections 161 to 161D of the 1991 Act (control of pollution of water resources);
- (h) the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcf08af7614e0430141a32f63893502f] [^c14536101]Radioactive Substances Act 1993; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcf08af7614e0430141a32f63893502f]
- (i)[^c-c75c7fec729629da43bf91d2ffbeff0f] regulations under section 2 of the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999;
- (i)[^c14536111] regulations under section 2 of the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999;
- (j) regulations made by virtue of section 2(2) of the [^c14536121]European Communities Act 1972, to the extent that the regulations relate to pollution.

(6)[^c-7f2911948e49c4b56300dc66fd1582c5] But in relation to the Natural Resources Body for Wales, “pollution control powers” and “pollution control functions” do not include powers or functions which—

- (a) were exercisable by the Countryside Council for Wales or the Forestry Commissioners immediately before 1 April 2013; and
- (b) are functions of that Body by virtue of the Natural Resources Body for Wales (Functions) Order 2013.
