## 21. Transfer of functions to SEPA.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) On the transfer date there shall by virtue of this section be transferred to SEPA—

- (a) the functions of river purification authorities, that is to say—
  - (i) heir functions with respect to water resources under or by virtue of Part III of the Rivers (Prevention of Pollution) (Scotland) Act 1951 (in this Part referred to as “the 1951 Act”) and Part II of the Natural Heritage (Scotland) Act 1991; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bd8b01c0ccd33da6b3e93ba9fb2d8d6b]
  - (ii) their functions with respect to water pollution under or by virtue of Part III of the 1951 Act, the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d9e459a6bdf4413f86ccc04bdc699ff] [^c14536551]Rivers (Prevention of Pollution) (Scotland) Act 1965 and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d9e459a6bdf4413f86ccc04bdc699ff] Part II of the [^c14536561]Control of Pollution Act 1974;
  - (iii) their functions as enforcing authority, in relation to releases of substances into the environment, under or by virtue of Part I of the 1990 Act; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5bdd8fb45448f879f5e7e8484e1b26c9]
  - (iv) their functions with respect to flood warning systems under or by virtue of Part VI of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f6450245fc98b6bbbf0a91bef6704978] [^c14536571]Agriculture Act 1970; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f6450245fc98b6bbbf0a91bef6704978]
  - (v) the functions assigned to them by or under any other enactment apart from this Act;
- (b) the functions of waste regulation authorities, that is to say, the functions conferred or imposed on them by or under—
  - (i) the [^c14536581]Control of Pollution (Amendment) Act 1989; or
  - (ii) Part II of the 1990 Act,

  or assigned to them by or under any other enactment apart from this Act;
- (c) the functions of disposal authorities under or by virtue of sections 3 to 10, 16, 17(1)(a) and 17(2)(b) to (d) of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7bab245f27b098f0dd91ab9dc9a37c2] [^c14536591]Control of Pollution Act 1974; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7bab245f27b098f0dd91ab9dc9a37c2]
- (d) the functions of the chief inspector for Scotland constituted under section 16(3) of the 1990 Act, that is to say, the functions conferred or imposed on him by or under Part I of that Act or assigned to him by or under any other enactment apart from this Act; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f26c0d18e685198666c250ee9f93b123]
- (e) the functions of the chief inspector for Scotland appointed under section 4(2)(b) of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2ac13ae9133d3f836f5e74e727d47fa4] [^c14536601]Radioactive Substances Act 1993, that is to say, the functions conferred or imposed on him by or under that Act or assigned to him by or under any other enactment apart from this Act; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2ac13ae9133d3f836f5e74e727d47fa4]
- (f) the functions conferred or imposed by or under the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-da102be32c80f4ce21cbd47359f87329] [^c14536611]Alkali, &c, Works Regulation Act 1906 (in this section referred to as “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-da102be32c80f4ce21cbd47359f87329]**the 1906 Act *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-da102be32c80f4ce21cbd47359f87329]**”) on the chief, or any other, inspector (within the meaning of that Act), so far as exercisable in relation to Scotland; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-da102be32c80f4ce21cbd47359f87329]
- (g)[^c-923a3e5cdc9b8b3acbc5009078ad7cfd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h) the functions of local authorities as enforcing authority, in relation to releases of substances into the air, under or by virtue of Part I of the 1990 Act; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e409424f0ce98ef5dd18175bcbba373]
- (i) the functions of the Secretary of State specified in subsection (2) below.

(2) The functions of the Secretary of State mentioned in subsection (1)(i) above are, so far as exercisable in relation to Scotland—

- (a)[^c-9eb920d9edff889f3fa1bfc576118333] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) his functions under, or under regulations made by virtue of, section 9 of the 1906 Act (registration of works), other than any functions of his as an appellate authority or any function of making regulations;
- (c) his functions under section 19 of the [^c14536631]Clean Air Act 1993 with respect to the creation of smoke control areas by local authorities; and
- (d) his functions under section 30(1) of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-760c14ea1b1e4b978eead35249748321] [^c14536641]Radioactive Substances Act 1993 (power to dispose of radioactive waste). *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-760c14ea1b1e4b978eead35249748321]
