## 81. Functions of the new Agencies.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In discharging its pollution control functions, each new Agency and the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^M_F_56dc7031-cbd8-4192-c881-d1c825b4a152] shall have regard to the strategy.

(2) In this section “**pollution control functions**” [^M_F_6d6f9423-8d64-47a2-915c-36be4d1cfdbd]... means *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0f5dee15cf4a273d1eca669c8c716f07]—

- (a) means *(Extent: S)*[^key-ab365f19c847e3371a5211ad08f89a34] in the case of the Agency or the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^M_F_ff0b7542-a3e8-4e8c-8ca3-46e772cc6c97], the functions conferred on it by or under the enactments specified in section 5(5) above (subject, in the case of[^M_F_cc968db3-5c72-4318-9214-f2266504c6ed] the Body, to section 5(6) above) ; or
- (b) in the case of SEPA, the functions conferred on it by or under the enactments specified in section 33(5) above *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-486e58d4e40adc0e433ea46ba08f7be9] has the same meaning as in section 108(15) below in relation to *(Extent: S)* SEPA *(Extent: S)*.
