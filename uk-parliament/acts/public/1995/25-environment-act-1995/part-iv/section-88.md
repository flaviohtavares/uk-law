# Environment Act 1995, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State may issue guidance to local authorities with respect to, or in connection with, the exercise of any of the powers conferred, or the discharge of any of the duties imposed, on those authorities by or under this Part.

(2) A local authority, in carrying out any of its functions under or by virtue of this Part, shall have regard to any guidance issued by the Secretary of State under this Part.

(3) This section *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-798855c556d5b1f685ec313ceb69c679] Subsections (1) and (2) *(Extent: W)* shall apply in relation to county councils for areas for which there are district councils , relevant public authorities and the Agency[^c-b6cf4c611d3ec6caaf8d0c4bace02d59] as it applies *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffdf97c32aab2c6202dbd77c11756f61] they apply *(Extent: W)* in relation to local authorities.

*Extent: W*

(4)[^c-f0136fb8664d2cdb94ff6c74dc69f613] The Welsh Ministers may issue guidance to relevant Welsh public authorities with respect to, or in connection with, the exercise of any of the powers conferred, or the discharge of any of the duties imposed, on those authorities by section 81B or regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under this Part.

(5 (Extent: W)) A relevant Welsh public authority, in exercising those powers and discharging those duties, must have regard to any guidance issued under subsection (4). *(Extent: W)*
