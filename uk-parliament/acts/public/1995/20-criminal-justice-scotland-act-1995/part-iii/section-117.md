# Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1995, section 117

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117. Minor and consequential amendments and repeals.[^c1524021]

*Extent: S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-04-01).**

(1) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 6 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified, being minor amendments and amendments consequential on the foregoing provisions of this Act.

(2) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 7 to this Act (which include enactments which are spent) are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
