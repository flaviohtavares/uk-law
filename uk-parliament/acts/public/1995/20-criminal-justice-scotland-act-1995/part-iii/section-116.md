# Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1995, section 116

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 116. Expenses.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-04-01).**

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any amount payable—
  - (i) as compensation under section 83 or 92 of this Act; or
  - (ii) under paragraph 6 of Schedule 3 to this Act in respect of remuneration or expenses of administrators;
- (b) any administrative expenses incurred by the Secretary of State or the Lord Advocate under this Act; and
- (c) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other Act.
