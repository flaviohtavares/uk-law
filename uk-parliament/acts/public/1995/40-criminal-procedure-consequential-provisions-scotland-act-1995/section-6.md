# Criminal Procedure (Consequential Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1995, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Repeals.

*Extent: S*

(1) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 5 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.

(2) Without prejudice to section 16(1)(a) of the [^c8315521]Interpretation Act 1978, the repeal by this Act of the provisions of the [^c8315531]Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975 specified in Schedule 6 to this Act shall not revive any rule of law or practice having effect before the coming into force of the [^c8315541]Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1887.
