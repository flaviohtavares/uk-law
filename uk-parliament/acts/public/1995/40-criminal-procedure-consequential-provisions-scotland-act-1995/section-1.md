# Criminal Procedure (Consequential Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1995, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Interpretation.

*Extent: S*

In this Act—

- “**the consolidating Acts**” means the Principal Act, the [^c8315471]Proceeds of Crime (Scotland) Act 1995, the [^c8315481]Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995 and, so far as it reproduces the effect of the repealed enactments, this Act;
- “**the Principal Act**” means the [^c8315491]Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995; and
- “**the repealed enactments**” means the enactments repealed by this Act.
