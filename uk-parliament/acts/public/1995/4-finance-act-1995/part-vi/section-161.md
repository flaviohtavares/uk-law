# Finance Act 1995, section 161

> Text as at 2023-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 161. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act “**the Taxes Act 1988**” means the [^c15673621]Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988.

(2) In Part III of this Act “**the Management Act**” means the [^c15673631]Taxes Management Act 1970.

(3) Part V of this Act shall be construed as one with the [^c15673641]Stamp Act 1891.
