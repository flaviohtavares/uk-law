# Finance Act 1995, section 9

> Text as at 2023-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Road fuel gas: old stock.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 8 of the [^c15669931]Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979 (road fuel gas) subsection (7) (no charge on use of gas if delivered or stocked before 3rd July 1972) shall be omitted.
