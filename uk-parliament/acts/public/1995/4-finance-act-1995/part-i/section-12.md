# Finance Act 1995, section 12

> Text as at 2023-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Pool betting duty.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 7(1) of the [^c15669981]Betting and Gaming Duties Act 1981 (which specifies 37.50 per cent. as the rate of pool betting duty) for “37.50 per cent.” there shall be substituted “ 32.50 per cent. ”

(2) This section shall apply in relation to any pool betting duty the requirement to pay which takes effect on or after 6th May 1995.
