# Finance Act 1995, section 2

> Text as at 2023-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Wine and made-wine: rates.[^c15669781]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the Table of rates of duty in Schedule 1 to the [^c15669791]Alcoholic Liquor Duties Act 1979 (wine and made-wine) there shall be substituted the Table in Schedule 1 to this Act.

(2) This section shall be deemed to have come into force on 1st January 1995.
