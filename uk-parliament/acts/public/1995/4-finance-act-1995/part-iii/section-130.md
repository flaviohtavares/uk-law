# Finance Act 1995, section 130

> Text as at 2023-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 130. Exchange gains and losses: general.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 24 to this Act (which amends the provisions of the [^c15672241]Finance Act 1993 relating to exchange gains and losses and other provisions connected with exchange gains and losses) shall have effect.
