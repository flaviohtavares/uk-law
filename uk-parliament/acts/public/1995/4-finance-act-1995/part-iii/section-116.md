# Finance Act 1995, section 116

> Text as at 2023-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Transitional provisions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The provisions of the Management Act specified in Schedule 21 to this Act shall have effect subject to the transitional provisions contained in that Schedule.

(2) Section 198 of the [^c15672031]Finance Act 1994 (which is superseded by this section) shall cease to have effect.
