# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 18E

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18E. Premises provided otherwise than in course of a Part 2 relationship[^c20840221][^c20684361]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22786551] This Part does not apply in relation to the provision, otherwise than in the course of a Part 2 relationship, of premises by the regulated party to the other party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786551]

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786551]

- (a) “Part 2 relationship” means a relationship during the course of which an act of discrimination against, or harassment of, one party to the relationship by the other party to it is unlawful under sections 4 to 15C; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786551]
- (b) in relation to a Part 2 relationship, “regulated party” means the party whose acts of discrimination, or harassment, are made unlawful by sections 4 to 15C. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786551]
