# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 17A

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17A. Enforcement, remedies and procedure.[^c22786441][^c20516001][^c20678761]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22786431] A complaint by any person that another person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

- (a) has discriminated against him *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431] , or subjected him to harassment,[^c20739151] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431] in a way which is unlawful under this Part, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]
- (b) is, by virtue of section 57 or 58, to be treated as having *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431] done so[^c20739171] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431],

may be presented to an industrial tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

(1A)[^c20739191] Subsection (1) does not apply to a complaint under section 14A(1) or (2) about an act in respect of which an appeal, or proceedings in the nature of an appeal, may be brought under any enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

(1B) Where, on the hearing of a complaint under subsection (1), the complainant proves facts from which the tribunal could, apart from this subsection, conclude in the absence of an adequate explanation that the respondent has acted in a way which is unlawful under this Part, the tribunal shall uphold the complaint unless the respondent proves that he did not so act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

(2) Where an industrial tribunal finds that a complaint presented to it under this section is well-founded, it shall take such of the following steps as it considers just and equitable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

- (a) making a declaration as to the rights of the complainant and the respondent in relation to the matters to which the complaint relates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]
- (b) ordering the respondent to pay compensation to the complainant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]
- (c) recommending that the respondent take, within a specified period, action appearing to the tribunal to be reasonable, in all the circumstances of the case, for the purpose of obviating or reducing the adverse effect on the complainant of any matter to which the complaint relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

(3)[^c20739211] Where a tribunal orders compensation under subsection (2)(b), the amount of the compensation shall be calculated by applying the principles applicable to the calculation of damages in claims in tort for breach of statutory duty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

(4) For the avoidance of doubt it is hereby declared that compensation in respect of discrimination in a way which is unlawful under this Part may include compensation for injury to feelings whether or not it includes compensation under any other head. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

(5) If the respondent to a complaint fails, without reasonable justification, to comply with a recommendation made by an industrial tribunal under subsection (2)(c) the tribunal may, if it thinks it just and equitable to do so— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

- (a) increase the amount of compensation required to be paid to the complainant in respect of the complaint, where an order was made under subsection (2)(b); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]
- (b) make an order under subsection (2)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

(6) Regulations may make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

- (a) for enabling a tribunal, where an amount of compensation falls to be awarded under subsection (2)(b), to include in the award interest on that amount; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]
- (b) specifying, for cases where a tribunal decides that an award is to include an amount in respect of interest, the manner in which and the periods and rate by reference to which the interest is to be determined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

(7)[^c20739231] Regulations may modify the operation of any order made under Article 16 of the Industrial Tribunals (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431] [^c20739251] (power to make provision as to interest on sums payable in pursuance of industrial tribunal decisions) to the extent that it relates to an award of compensation under subsection (2)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]

(8) Part I of Schedule 3 makes further provision about the enforcement of this Part and about procedure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786431]
