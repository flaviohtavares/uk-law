# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 4

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Discrimination against applicants and employees.[^c22783581][^c20656511]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22783571] It is unlawful for an employer to discriminate against a disabled person – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]

- (a) in the arrangements which he makes for the purpose of determining to whom he should offer employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]
- (b) in the terms on which he offers that person employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]
- (c) by refusing to offer, or deliberately not offering, him employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]

(2) It is unlawful for an employer to discriminate against a disabled person whom he employs – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]

- (a) in the terms of employment which he affords him; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]
- (b) in the opportunities which he affords him for promotion, a transfer, training or receiving any other benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]
- (c) by refusing to afford him, or deliberately not affording him, any such opportunity; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]
- (d) by dismissing him, or subjecting him to any other detriment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]

(3) It is also unlawful for an employer, in relation to employment by him, to subject to harassment – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]

- (a) a disabled person whom he employs; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]
- (b) a disabled person who has applied to him for employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]

(4) Subsection (2) does not apply to benefits of any description if the employer is concerned with the provision (whether or not for payment) of benefits of that description to the public, or to a section of the public which includes the employee in question, unless – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]

- (a) that provision differs in a material respect from the provision of the benefits by the employer to his employees; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]
- (b) the provision of the benefits to the employee in question is regulated by his contract of employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]
- (c) the benefits relate to training. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]

(5) The reference in subsection (2)(d) to the dismissal of a person includes a reference – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]

- (a) to the termination of that person’s employment by the expiration of any period (including a period expiring by reference to an event or circumstance), not being a termination immediately after which the employment is renewed on the same terms; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]
- (b) to the termination of that person’s employment by any act of his (including the giving of notice) in circumstances such that he is entitled to terminate it without notice by reason of the conduct of the employer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]

(6) This section applies only in relation to employment at an establishment in Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783571]
