# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 18B

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18B. Reasonable adjustments: supplementary[^c22786881][^c20684521][^c20684361]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

 [^c22786511]

(1) In determining whether it is reasonable for a person to have to take a particular step in order to comply with a duty to make reasonable adjustments, regard shall be had, in particular, to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]

- (a) the extent to which taking the step would prevent the effect in relation to which the duty is imposed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (b) the extent to which it is practicable for him to take the step; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (c) the financial and other costs which would be incurred by him in taking the step and the extent to which taking it would disrupt any of his activities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (d) the extent of his financial and other resources; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (e) the availability to him of financial or other assistance with respect to taking the step; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (f) the nature of his activities and the size of his undertaking; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (g) where the step would be taken in relation to a private household, the extent to which taking it would— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
  - (i) disrupt that household, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22786511] disturb any person residing there. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]

(2) The following are examples of steps which a person may need to take in relation to a disabled person in order to comply with a duty to make reasonable adjustments— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]

- (a) making adjustments to premises; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (b) allocating some of the disabled person’s duties to another person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (c) transferring him to fill an existing vacancy; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (d) altering his hours of working or training; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (e) assigning him to a different place of work or training; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (f) allowing him to be absent during working or training hours for rehabilitation, assessment or treatment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (g) giving, or arranging for, training or mentoring (whether for the disabled person or any other person); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (h) acquiring or modifying equipment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (i) modifying instructions or reference manuals; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (j) modifying procedures for testing or assessment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (k) providing a reader or interpreter; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (l) providing supervision or other support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]

(3) For the purposes of a duty to make reasonable adjustments, where under any binding obligation a person is required to obtain the consent of another person to any alteration of the premises occupied by him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]

- (a) it is always reasonable for him to have to take steps to obtain that consent; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
- (b) it is never reasonable for him to have to make that alteration before that consent is obtained. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]

(4) The steps referred to in subsection (3)(a) shall not be taken to include an application to a court or tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]

(5) In subsection (3), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]**binding obligation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]**” means a legally binding obligation (not contained in a lease (within the meaning of section 18A(3)) in relation to the premises, whether arising from an agreement or otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]

(6) A provision of this Part imposing a duty to make reasonable adjustments applies only for the purpose of determining whether a person has discriminated against a disabled person; and accordingly a breach of any such duty is not actionable as such. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786511]
