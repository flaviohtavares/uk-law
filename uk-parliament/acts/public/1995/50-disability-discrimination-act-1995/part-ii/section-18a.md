# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 18A

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18A. Alterations to premises occupied under leases.[^c20684471][^c20743321][^c20738831][^c20684361]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22786491] This section applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]

- (a) a person to whom a duty to make reasonable adjustments applies[^c20743271] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491](“the occupier”) occupies premises under a lease; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]
- (b) but for this section, the occupier would not be entitled to make a particular alteration to the premises; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]
- (c) the alteration is one which the occupier proposes to make in order to comply with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491] that duty[^c20743291] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491].

(2) Except to the extent to which it expressly so provides, the lease shall have effect by virtue of this subsection as if it provided— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]

- (a) for the occupier to be entitled to make the alteration with the written consent of the lessor; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]
- (b) for the occupier to have to make a written application to the lessor for consent if he wishes to make the alteration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]
- (c) if such an application is made, for the lessor not to withhold his consent unreasonably; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]
- (d) for the lessor to be entitled to make his consent subject to reasonable conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]

(2A)[^c20684481] Except to the extent to which it expressly so provides, any superior lease under which the premises are held shall have effect in relation to the lessor and lessee who are parties to that lease as if it provided— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]

- (i) for the lessee to have to make a written application to the lessor for consent to the alteration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22786491] if such an application is made, for the lessor not to withhold his consent unreasonably; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]
- (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22786491] for the lessor to be entitled to make his consent subject to reasonable conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]

(3) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]

- “**lease *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]**” includes a tenancy, sub-lease or sub-tenancy and an agreement for a lease, tenancy, sub-lease or sub-tenancy; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]
- “**sub-lease *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]**sub-tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]**” have such meaning as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]

(4) If the terms and conditions of a lease— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]

- (a) impose conditions which are to apply if the occupier alters the premises, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]
- (b) entitle the lessor to impose conditions when consenting to the occupier’s altering the premises, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]

the occupier is to be treated for the purposes of subsection (1) as not being entitled to make the alteration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]

(5) Part I of Schedule 4 supplements the provisions of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786491]
