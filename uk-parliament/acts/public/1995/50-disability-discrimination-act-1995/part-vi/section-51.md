# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 51

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Codes of practice prepared by the Council.[^c18601171][^c22795481][^c18601151]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c18601151][^c22795471] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c18601151] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) A failure on the part of any person to observe any provision of a code does not of itself make that person liable to any proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22795471]

(4) A code is admissible in evidence in any proceedings under this Act before an industrial tribunal, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22795471] or a county court[^c18601201] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22795471].

(5) If any provision of a code appears to a tribunal or court to be relevant to any question arising in any proceedings under this Act, it shall be taken into account in determining that question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22795471]

(6) In this section and section 52 “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22795471]**code *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22795471]**” means a code issued by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22795471] a Northern Ireland department[^c18601211] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22795471] under this section and includes a code which has been altered and re-issued. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22795471]
