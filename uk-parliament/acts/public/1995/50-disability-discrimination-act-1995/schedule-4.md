## SCHEDULE 4 — Premises Occupied Under Leases[^c18603881]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 18A(5)[^c20366591] and 27(5) 31ADB[^c20460981].

### Part I — Occupation by EMPLOYER etc[^c20366681][^c20366631]

#### Failure to obtain consent to alteration

**1.**[^c22875351][^c18603891] If any question arises as to whether the occupier has failed to comply with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875331] any duty to make reasonable adjustments[^c20366241] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875331], by failing to make a particular alteration to the premises, any constraint attributable to the fact that he occupies the premises under a lease is to be ignored unless he has applied to the lessor in writing for consent to the making of the alteration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875331]

#### Joining lessors in proceedings under section 17A . . .[^c20366271][^c20458581]

**2.**[^c22875381][^c18603901] (1)[^c22875361] In any proceedings *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361] on a complaint under section 17A[^c20458871] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361], in a case to which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361] section 18A[^c20365371] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361] applies, the complainant or the occupier may ask the tribunal hearing the complaint to direct that the lessor be joined or sisted as a party to the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]

(2) The request shall be granted if it is made before the hearing of the complaint begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]

(3) The tribunal may refuse the request if it is made after the hearing of the complaint begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]

(4) The request may not be granted if it is made after the tribunal has determined the complaint. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]

(5) Where a lessor has been so joined or sisted as a party to the proceedings, the tribunal may determine— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]

- (a) whether the lessor has— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]
  - (i) refused consent to the alteration, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22875361] consented subject to one or more conditions, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]
- (b) if so, whether the refusal or any of the conditions was unreasonable, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]

(6) If, under sub-paragraph (5), the tribunal determines that the refusal or any of the conditions was unreasonable it may take one or more of the following steps— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]

- (a) make such declaration as it considers appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]
- (b) make an order authorising the occupier to make the alteration specified in the order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]
- (c) order the lessor to pay compensation to the complainant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]

(7) An order under sub-paragraph (6)(b) may require the occupier to comply with conditions specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]

(8) Any step taken by the tribunal under sub-paragraph (6) may be in substitution for, or in addition to, any step taken by the tribunal under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361] section 17A(2)[^c20365401] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361].

(9) If the tribunal orders the lessor to pay compensation it may not make an order under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361] section 17A(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361] ordering the occupier to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875361]

#### Regulations

**3.**[^c22875411][^c18603911][^c18603921] Regulations may make provision as to circumstances in which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875391]

- (a) a lessor is to be taken, for the purposes of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875391] section 18A[^c20366461] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875391] and this Part of this Schedule to have— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875391]
  - (i) withheld his consent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875391]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22875391] withheld his consent unreasonably; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875391]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22875391] acted reasonably in withholding his consent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875391]
- (b) a condition subject to which a lessor has given his consent is to be taken to be reasonable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875391]
- (c) a condition subject to which a lessor has given his consent is to be taken to be unreasonable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875391]

#### Sub-leases etc.

**4.**[^c22875441][^c18603931] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision supplementing, or modifying, the provision made by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875421] section 18A[^c20366491] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875421] or any provision made by or under this Part of this Schedule in relation to cases where the occupier occupies premises under a sub-lease or sub-tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875421]

### Part II — Occupation by persons subject to a duty under section 21, 21E or 21H[^c18603951][^c20457811][^c20459971]

#### Failure to obtain consent to alteration

**5.**[^c22875511] If any question arises as to whether the occupier has failed to comply with the section 21 duty *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875491] or the duty imposed under section 21E or 21H[^c20459951] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875491], by failing to make a particular alteration to premises, any constraint attributable to the fact that he occupies the premises under a lease is to be ignored unless he has applied to the lessor in writing for consent to the making of the alteration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875491]

#### Reference to court

**6.**[^c22875561] (1)[^c22875541] If the occupier has applied in writing to the lessor for consent to the alteration and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]

- (a) that consent has been refused, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]
- (b) the lessor has made his consent subject to one or more conditions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]

the occupier or a disabled person who has an interest in the proposed alteration to the premises being made, may refer the matter to a county court or, in Scotland, to the sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]

(2) In the following provisions of this Schedule “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]**court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]**” includes “sheriff”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]

(3) On such a reference the court shall determine whether the lessor’s refusal was unreasonable or (as the case may be) whether the condition is, or any of the conditions are, unreasonable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]

(4) If the court determines— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]

- (a) that the lessor’s refusal was unreasonable, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]
- (b) that the condition is, or any of the conditions are, unreasonable, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]

it may make such declaration as it considers appropriate or an order authorising the occupier to make the alteration specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]

(5) An order under sub-paragraph (4) may require the occupier to comply with conditions specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875541]

#### Joining lessors in proceedings under section 25

**7.**[^c22875531] (1)[^c22875521] In any proceedings on a claim *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521] under section 25 in a case to which section 27 applies, other than a claim presented as a complaint under section 25(8),[^c20459151] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521] the plaintiff, the pursuer or the occupier concerned may ask the court to direct that the lessor be joined or sisted as a party to the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]

(2) The request shall be granted if it is made before the hearing of the claim begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]

(3) The court may refuse the request if it is made after the hearing of the claim begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]

(4) The request may not be granted if it is made after the court has determined the claim. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]

(5) Where a lessor has been so joined or sisted as a party to the proceedings, the court may determine— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]

- (a) whether the lessor has— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]
  - (i) refused consent to the alteration, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22875521] consented subject to one or more conditions, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]
- (b) if so, whether the refusal or any of the conditions was unreasonable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]

(6) If, under sub-paragraph (5), the court determines that the refusal or any of the conditions was unreasonable it may take one or more of the following steps— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]

- (a) make such declaration as it considers appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]
- (b) make an order authorising the occupier to make the alteration specified in the order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]
- (c) order the lessor to pay compensation to the complainant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]

(7) An order under sub-paragraph (6)(b) may require the occupier to comply with conditions specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]

(8) If the court orders the lessor to pay compensation it may not order the occupier to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22875521]

**7A.**[^c22886041] (1) In any proceedings on a complaint under section 25(8) in a case to which section 27 applies, the complainant or the occupier may ask the tribunal hearing the complaint to direct that the lessor be joined as a party to the proceedings.

(2) The request shall be granted if it is made before the hearing of the complaint begins.

(3) The tribunal may refuse the request if it is made after the hearing of the complaint begins.

(4) The request may not be granted if it is made after the tribunal has determined the complaint.

(5) Where a lessor has been so joined as a party to the proceedings, the tribunal may determine—

- (a) whether the lessor has—
  - (i) refused consent to the alteration, or
  - (ii) consented subject to one or more conditions; and
- (b) if so, whether the refusal or any of the conditions was unreasonable.

(6) If, under sub-paragraph (5), the tribunal determines that the refusal or any of the conditions was unreasonable it may take one or more of the following steps—

- (a) make such declaration as it considers appropriate;
- (b) make an order authorising the occupier to make the alteration specified in the order;
- (c) order the lessor to pay compensation to the complainant.

(7) An order under sub-paragraph (6)(b) may require the occupier to comply with conditions specified in the order.

(8) Any step taken by the tribunal under sub-paragraph (6) may be in substitution for, or in addition to, any step taken by the tribunal under section 17A(2).

(9) If the tribunal orders the lessor to pay compensation it may not make an order under section 17A(2) ordering the occupier to do so.
