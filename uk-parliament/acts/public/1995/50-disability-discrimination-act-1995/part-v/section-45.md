# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 45

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Fees.[^c18600731][^c22809301][^c23366561]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22809281] Such fees, payable at such times, as may be prescribed may be charged by the Secretary of State in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809281]

- (a) applications for, and grants of, approval under section 42(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809281]
- (b) applications for, and the issue of, accessibility certificates and approval certificates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809281]
- (c) copies of such certificates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809281]
- (d) reviews and appeals under section 44. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809281]

(2) Any such fees received by the Secretary of State shall be paid by him into the Consolidated Fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809281]

(3) Regulations under subsection (1) may make provision for the repayment of fees, in whole or in part, in such circumstances as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809281]

(4) Before making any regulations under subsection (1) the Secretary of State shall consult such representative organisations as he thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809281]
