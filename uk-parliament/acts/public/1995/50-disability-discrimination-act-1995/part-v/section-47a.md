# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 47A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## (not yet in force: 47A). (not yet in force: Rail vehicle accessibility compliance certificates)[^c22810131][^c20485931]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1))[^c22810111] *(not yet in force: A regulated rail vehicle to which this subsection applies shall not be used for carriage unless a rail vehicle accessibility compliance certificate is in force for the vehicle. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810111])*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Subsection (1) applies to a regulated rail vehicle if the vehicle— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810111])*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: is prescribed; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810111])*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: is of a prescribed class or description. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810111])*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A rail vehicle accessibility compliance certificate is a certificate that the Secretary of State is satisfied that the regulated rail vehicle conforms with those provisions of rail vehicle accessibility regulations with which the vehicle is required to conform. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810111])*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: A rail vehicle accessibility compliance certificate may provide that it is subject to conditions specified in the certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810111])*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Subsection (6) applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810111])*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the Secretary of State refuses an application for the issue of a rail vehicle accessibility compliance certificate for a regulated rail vehicle; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810111])*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: before the end of the prescribed period, the applicant asks the Secretary of State to review the decision and pays any fee fixed under section 47C. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810111])*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: The Secretary of State shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810111])*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: review the decision; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810111])*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in doing so, consider any representations made to him in writing, before the end of the prescribed period, by the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810111])*

## (not yet in force: 47A). (not yet in force: Rail vehicle accessibility compliance certificates)[^c20486061]

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A regulated rail vehicle to which this subsection applies shall not be used for carriage unless a rail vehicle accessibility compliance certificate is in force for the vehicle.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Subsection (1) applies to a regulated rail vehicle if the vehicle—)*
