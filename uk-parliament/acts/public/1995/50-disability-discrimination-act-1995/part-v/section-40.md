# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 40

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. PSV accessibility regulations.[^c18600611][^c22809101][^c23366511]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22809131] The Secretary of State may make regulations (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]**PSV accessibility regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]**”) for the purpose of securing that it is possible for disabled persons— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]

- (a) to get on to and off regulated public service vehicles in safety and without unreasonable difficulty (and, in the case of disabled persons in wheelchairs, to do so while remaining in their wheelchairs); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]
- (b) to be carried in such vehicles in safety and in reasonable comfort. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]

(2) PSV accessibility regulations may, in particular, make provision as to the construction, use and maintenance of regulated public service vehicles including provision as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]

- (a) the fitting of equipment to vehicles; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]
- (b) equipment to be carried by vehicles; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]
- (c) the design of equipment to be fitted to, or carried by, vehicles; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]
- (d) the fitting and use of restraining devices designed to ensure the stability of wheelchairs while vehicles are moving; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]
- (e) the position in which wheelchairs are to be secured while vehicles are moving. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]

(3) Any person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]

- (a) contravenes or fails to comply with any provision of the PSV accessibility regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]
- (b) uses on a road a regulated public service vehicle which does not conform with any provision of the regulations with which it is required to conform, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]
- (c) causes or permits to be used on a road such a regulated public service vehicle, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]

is guilty of an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]

(4) A person who is guilty of such an offence is liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]

(5) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]

- “**public service vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]**” means a vehicle which is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]
  - (a) adapted to carry more than eight passengers; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]
  - (b) a public service vehicle for the purposes of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131] [^c18600631]Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]
- “**regulated public service vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]**” means any public service vehicle to which the PSV accessibility regulations are expressed to apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]

(6) Different provision may be made in regulations under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]

- (a) as respects different classes or descriptions of vehicle; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]
- (b) as respects the same class or description of vehicle in different circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]

(7) Before making any regulations under this section or section 41 or 42 the Secretary of State shall consult the Disabled Persons Transport Advisory Committee and such other representative organisations as he thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809131]
