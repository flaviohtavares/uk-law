# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 37A

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37A. Carrying of assistance dogs in private hire vehicles[^c22504901][^c23366211][^c18600511]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22739501] It is an offence for the operator of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] taxi[^c20372451] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] to fail or refuse to accept a booking for a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]—

- (a) if the booking is requested by or on behalf of a disabled person, or a person who wishes a disabled person to accompany him; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
- (b) the reason for the failure or refusal is that the disabled person will be accompanied by his assistance dog. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]

(2) It is an offence for the operator of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] to make an additional charge for carrying an assistance dog which is accompanying a disabled person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]

(3) It is an offence for the driver of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] to fail or refuse to carry out a booking accepted by the operator of the vehicle— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]

- (a) if the booking was made by or on behalf of a disabled person, or a person who wishes a disabled person to accompany him; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
- (b) the reason for the failure or refusal is that the disabled person is accompanied by his assistance dog. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]

(4) A person who is guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]

(5) If the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] Department of the Environment[^c22774201] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] is satisfied that it is appropriate on medical grounds to issue a certificate of exemption to a driver in respect of subsection (3) it must do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]

(6) In determining whether to issue a certificate of exemption, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] Department of the Environment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] shall, in particular, have regard to the physical characteristics of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] which the applicant drives or those of any kind of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] in relation to which he requires the certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]

(7) A certificate of exemption shall be issued— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]

- (a) with respect to a specified *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] or a specified kind of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
- (b) for such period as may be specified in the certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]

(8) No offence is committed by a driver under subsection (3) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]

- (a) a certificate of exemption issued to him under this section is in force with respect to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
- (b) the prescribed notice is exhibited on the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501] in the prescribed manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]

(9) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]

- “**assistance dog *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]**” means a dog which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
  - (a) has been trained to guide a blind person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
  - (b) has been trained to assist a deaf person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
  - (c) has been trained by a prescribed charity to assist a disabled person who has a disability which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
    - (i) consists of epilepsy; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
    - (ii) otherwise affects his mobility, manual dexterity, physical co-ordination or ability to lift, carry or otherwise move everyday objects; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
- “[^c20372591]**booking *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]**” means a taxi booking within the meaning given by section 57(1) of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
- “[^c20372571]**driver *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]**” means a person who holds a taxi driver's licence granted under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
  - (a) Article 79A of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
  - (b) section 23 of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
- “**licensing authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]**”, in relation to any area of England and Wales, means the authority responsible for licensing private hire vehicles in that area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
- “[^c20372611]**operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]**” means a person who holds an operator's licence granted under section 2 of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
- “[^c22774221] **taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]** ” means a vehicle which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
  - (a) seats not more than 8 passengers in addition to the driver; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
  - (b) is licensed under Article 61 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 to carry passengers for hire (but not to stand or ply for hire). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22739501]
