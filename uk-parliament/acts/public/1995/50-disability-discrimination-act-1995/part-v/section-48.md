# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 48

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Offences by bodies corporate etc.[^c18600801][^c22810741]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22810861] Where an offence under section 40 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810861] or 46 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20486191] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810861] committed by a body corporate is committed with the consent or connivance of, or is attributable to any neglect on the part of, a director, manager, secretary or other similar officer of the body, or a person purporting to act in such a capacity, he as well as the body corporate is guilty of the offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810861]

(2) In subsection (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810861]**director *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810861]**”, in relation to a body corporate whose affairs are managed by its members, means a member of the body corporate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810861]
