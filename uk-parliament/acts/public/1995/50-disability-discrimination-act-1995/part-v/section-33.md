# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 33

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Designated transport facilities.[^c18600421][^c22806411][^c23366171]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1)[^c22806401] In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]**a franchise agreement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]**” means a contract entered into by the operator of a designated transport facility for the provision by the other party to the contract of hire car services— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]

- (a) for members of the public using any part of the transport facility; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]
- (b) which involve vehicles entering any part of that facility. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]

(2) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401] appropriate national authority[^c20439411] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401] may by regulations provide for the application of any taxi provision in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]

- (a) vehicles used for the provision of services under a franchise agreement; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]
- (b) the drivers of such vehicles. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]

(3) Any regulations under subsection (2) may apply any taxi provision with such modifications as the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401] authority making the regulations[^c20439441] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401] considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]

(4) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]

- appropriate national authority means—[^c20439511] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]
  - (a) in relation to transport facilities in England and Wales, the Secretary of State, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]
  - (b) in relation to transport facilities in Scotland, the Scottish Ministers (but see subsection (5)); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]
- “**designated *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]**” means designated for the purposes of this section by an order made by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401] appropriate national authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401];
- “**hire car *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]**” has such meaning as may be *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401] specified by regulations made by the appropriate national authority[^c20439551] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401];
- “**operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]**”, in relation to a transport facility, means any person who is concerned with the management or operation of the facility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]
- “**taxi provision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]**” means any provision of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]
  - (a) this Act, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]
  - (b) regulations made in pursuance of section 20(2A) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401] [^c18600441]Civic Government (Scotland) Act 1982, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]

  which applies in relation to taxis or the drivers of taxis; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]
- “**transport facility *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]**” means any premises which form part of any port, airport, railway station or bus station. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]

(5)[^c20439571] The Secretary of State may, for the purposes mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 (implementation of Community obligations etc. of the United Kingdom), exercise the powers conferred by this section on the Scottish Ministers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806401]
