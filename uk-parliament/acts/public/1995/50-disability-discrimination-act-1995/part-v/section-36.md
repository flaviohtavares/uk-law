# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 36

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Carrying of passengers in wheelchairs.[^c18600481][^c20487811][^c23366191][^c22469211]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22806471] This section imposes duties on the driver of a regulated taxi which has been hired— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

- (a) by or for a disabled person who is in a wheelchair; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
- (b) by a person who wishes such a disabled person to accompany him in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] vehicle[^c20486641] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471].

(1A)[^c20486661] This section also imposes duties on the driver of a designated vehicle other than a regulated taxi if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

- (a) the designated vehicle is being used to provide a local service (within the meaning of section 2 of the Transport Act 1985), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
- (b) a person falling within paragraph (a) or (b) of subsection (1) has indicated to the driver that he wishes to travel on the service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

(2) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

- “**carry *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]**” means carry in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] vehicle[^c20486681] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] concerned; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
- “**the passenger *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]**” means the disabled person concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

(3) The duties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

- (a) to carry the passenger while he remains in his wheelchair; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
- (b) not to make any additional charge for doing so; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
- (c) if the passenger chooses to sit in a passenger seat, to carry the wheelchair; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
- (d) to take such steps as are necessary to ensure that the passenger is carried in safety and in reasonable comfort; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
- (e) to give such assistance as may be reasonably required— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
  - (i) to enable the passenger to get into or out of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471];
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22806471] if the passenger wishes to remain in his wheelchair, to enable him to be conveyed into and out of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] while in his wheelchair; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22806471] to load the passenger’s luggage into or out of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471];
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22806471] if the passenger does not wish to remain in his wheelchair, to load the wheelchair into or out of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471].

(4) Nothing in this section is to be taken to require the driver of any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]—

- (a) except in the case of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] of a prescribed description, to carry more than one person in a wheelchair, or more than one wheelchair, on any one journey; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
- (b) to carry any person in circumstances in which it would otherwise be lawful for him to refuse to carry that person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

(5) A driver of a regulated taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] or designated vehicle[^c20486781] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] who fails to comply with any duty imposed on him by this section is guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

(6) In any proceedings for an offence under this section, it is a defence for the accused to show that, even though at the time of the alleged offence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

- (a)[^c20486801] in the case of a regulated taxi, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

the taxi conformed with those provisions of the taxi accessibility regulations with which it was required to conform, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

- (b)[^c20486841] in the case of a designated vehicle, the vehicle conformed to the accessibility requirements which applied to it, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

it would not have been possible for the wheelchair in question to be carried in safety in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471] vehicle[^c20486861] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471].

(7) If the licensing authority is satisfied that it is appropriate to exempt a person from the duties imposed by this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

- (a) on medical grounds, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
- (b) on the ground that his physical condition makes it impossible or unreasonably difficult for him to comply with the duties imposed on drivers by this section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

it shall issue him with a certificate of exemption. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

(8) A certificate of exemption shall be issued for such period as may be specified in the certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

(9) The driver of a regulated taxi is exempt from the duties imposed by this section if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

- (a) a certificate of exemption issued to him under this section is in force; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
- (b) the prescribed notice of his exemption is exhibited on the taxi in the prescribed manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

(10)[^c20486881] The driver of a designated vehicle is exempt from the duties imposed by this section if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

- (a) a certificate of exemption issued to him under this section is in force; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
- (b) he is carrying the certificate on the vehicle. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

(11) The driver of a designated vehicle who is exempt under subsection (10) must show the certificate, on request, to a person falling within paragraph (a) or (b) of subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

(12)[^c20486901] In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]

- “**designated vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]**” means a vehicle which appears on a list maintained under section 36A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
- “**licensing authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]**” has the meaning given by section 36A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806471]
