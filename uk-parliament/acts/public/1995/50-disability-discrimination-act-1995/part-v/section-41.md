# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 41

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Accessibility certificates.[^c18600641][^c22809171][^c23366521]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22809151] A regulated public service vehicle shall not be used on a road unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809151]

- (a) a vehicle examiner has issued a certificate (an “accessibility certificate”) that such provisions of the PSV accessibility regulations as may be prescribed are satisfied in respect of the vehicle; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809151]
- (b) an approval certificate has been issued under section 42 in respect of the vehicle. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809151]

(2) The Secretary of State may make regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809151]

- (a) with respect to applications for, and the issue of, accessibility certificates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809151]
- (b) providing for the examination of vehicles in respect of which applications have been made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809151]
- (c) with respect to the issue of copies of accessibility certificates in place of certificates which have been lost or destroyed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809151]

(3) If a regulated public service vehicle is used in contravention of this section, the operator of the vehicle is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809151]

(4) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809151]**operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809151]**” has the same meaning as in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809151] [^c18600661]Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809151]
