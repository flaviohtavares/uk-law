# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 32

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Taxi accessibility regulations.[^c23366091][^c22806351][^c23366161]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22806331] The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331] Department of the Environment[^c22806361] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331] may make regulations (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]**taxi accessibility regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]**”) for the purpose of securing that it is possible— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]

- (a) for disabled persons— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
  - (i) to get into and out of taxis in safety; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22806331] to be carried in taxis in safety and in reasonable comfort; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
- (b) for disabled persons in wheelchairs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
  - (i) to be conveyed in safety into and out of taxis while remaining in their wheelchairs; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22806331] to be carried in taxis in safety and in reasonable comfort while remaining in their wheelchairs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]

(2) Taxi accessibility regulations may, in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]

- (a) require any regulated taxi to conform with provisions of the regulations as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
  - (i) the size of any door opening which is for the use of passengers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22806331] the floor area of the passenger compartment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22806331] the amount of headroom in the passenger compartment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22806331] the fitting of restraining devices designed to ensure the stability of a wheelchair while the taxi is moving; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
- (b) require the driver of any regulated taxi which is plying for hire, or which has been hired, to comply with provisions of the regulations as to the carrying of ramps or other devices designed to facilitate the loading and unloading of wheelchairs; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
- (c) require the driver of any regulated taxi in which a disabled person who is in a wheelchair is being carried (while remaining in his wheelchair) to comply with provisions of the regulations as to the position in which the wheelchair is to be secured. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]

(3) The driver of a regulated taxi which is plying for hire, or which has been hired, is guilty of an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]

- (a) he fails to comply with any requirement imposed on him by the regulations; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
- (b) the taxi fails to conform with any provision of the regulations with which it is required to conform. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]

(4) A person who is guilty of such an offence is liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]

(5) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]

- “**passenger compartment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]**” has such meaning as may be prescribed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
- “**regulated taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]**” means any taxi to which the regulations are expressed to apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
- “[^c23366271]**taxi *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]**” means a vehicle which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
  - (a) is licensed under Article 61 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 to stand or ply for hire; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
  - (b) seats not more than 8 passengers in addition to the driver *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806331]
