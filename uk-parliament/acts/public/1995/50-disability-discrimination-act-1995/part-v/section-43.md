# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 43

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Special authorisations.[^c18600691][^c22809231][^c23366541]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22809211] The Secretary of State may by order authorise the use on roads of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809211]

- (a) any regulated public service vehicle of a class or description specified by the order, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809211]
- (b) any regulated public service vehicle which is so specified, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809211]

and nothing in section 40, 41 or 42 prevents the use of any vehicle in accordance with the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809211]

(2) Any such authorisation may be given subject to such restrictions and conditions as may be specified by or under the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809211]

(3) The Secretary of State may by order make provision for the purpose of securing that, subject to such restrictions and conditions as may be specified by or under the order, provisions of the PSV accessibility regulations apply to regulated public service vehicles of a description specified by the order subject to such modifications or exceptions as may be specified by the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809211]
