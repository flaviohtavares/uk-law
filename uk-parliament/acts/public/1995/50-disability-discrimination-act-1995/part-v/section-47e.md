# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 47E

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## (not yet in force: 47E). (not yet in force: Penalty for using rail vehicle that does not conform with accessibility regulations)[^c22810491][^c20485971]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1))[^c22810471] *(not yet in force: Where it appears to the Secretary of State that a regulated rail vehicle does not conform with a provision of rail vehicle accessibility regulations with which the vehicle is required to conform, the Secretary of State may give to the operator of the vehicle a notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: identifying the vehicle, the provision and how the vehicle fails to conform with the provision; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: specifying the improvement deadline. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The improvement deadline specified in a notice under subsection (1) may not be earlier than the end of the prescribed period beginning with the day when the notice is given to the operator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Subsection (4) applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the Secretary of State has given a notice under subsection (1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the improvement deadline specified in the notice has passed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: it appears to the Secretary of State that the vehicle still does not conform with the provision identified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The Secretary of State may give to the operator a further notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: identifying the vehicle, the provision and how the vehicle fails to conform to the provision; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: specifying the final deadline. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The final deadline specified in a notice under subsection (4) may not be earlier than the end of the prescribed period beginning with the day when the notice is given to the operator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: If— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the Secretary of State has given a notice under subsection (4) to the operator of a regulated rail vehicle, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the vehicle is used for carriage at a time after the final deadline when the vehicle does not conform with the provision identified in the notice, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*

*(not yet in force: the Secretary of State may require the operator to pay a penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810471])*

## (not yet in force: 47E). (not yet in force: Penalty for using rail vehicle that does not conform with accessibility regulations)[^c20486101]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Where it appears to the Department for Regional Development that a regulated rail vehicle does not conform with a provision of rail vehicle accessibility regulations with which the vehicle is required to conform, that Department may give to the operator of the vehicle a notice—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: identifying the vehicle, the provision and how the vehicle fails to conform with the provision; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: specifying the improvement deadline.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The improvement deadline specified in a notice under subsection (1) may not be earlier than the end of the prescribed period beginning with the day when the notice is given to the operator.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Subsection (4) applies where—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the Department for Regional Development has given a notice under subsection (1);)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the improvement deadline specified in the notice has passed; and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: it appears to the Department for Regional Development that the vehicle still does not conform with the provision identified in the notice.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The Department for Regional Development may give to the operator a further notice—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: identifying the vehicle, the provision and how the vehicle fails to conform to the provision; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: specifying the final deadline.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The final deadline specified in a notice under subsection (4) may not be earlier than the end of the prescribed period beginning with the day when the notice is given to the operator.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: If—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the Department for Regional Development has given a notice under subsection (4) to the operator of a regulated rail vehicle, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the vehicle is used for carriage at a time after the final deadline when the vehicle does not conform with the provision identified in the notice,)*

*(not yet in force: the Department for Regional Development may require the operator to pay a penalty.)*
