## (not yet in force: 47G). (not yet in force: Sections 47E and 47F: inspection of rail vehicles)[^c20486101]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Where the Department for Regional Development has reasonable grounds for suspecting that a regulated rail vehicle may not conform with provisions of rail vehicle accessibility regulations with which it is required to conform, a person authorised by that Department—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: may inspect the vehicle for conformity with the provisions;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: for the purpose of exercising his power under paragraph (a)—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: may enter premises if he has reasonable grounds for suspecting the vehicle to be at those premises, and)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: may enter the vehicle; and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: for the purpose of exercising his power under paragraph (a) or (b), may require any person to afford such facilities and assistance with respect to matters under that person's control as are necessary to enable the power to be exercised.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Where the Department for Regional Development has given a notice under section 47E(1) or (4), a person authorised by that Department—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: may inspect the vehicle concerned for conformity with the provision specified in the notice;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: for the purpose of exercising his power under paragraph (a)—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: may enter premises if he has reasonable grounds for suspecting the vehicle to be at those premises, and)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: may enter the vehicle; and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: for the purpose of exercising his power under paragraph (a) or (b), may require any person to afford such facilities and assistance with respect to matters under that person's control as are necessary to enable the power to be exercised.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A person exercising power under subsection (1) or (2) shall, if required to do so, produce evidence of his authority to exercise the power.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Where a person obstructs the exercise of power under subsection (1), the Department for Regional Development may, for purposes of section 47E(1) or 47F(1), draw such inferences from the obstruction as appear proper.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Where a person—)*
