# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 44

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Reviews and appeals.[^c18600711][^c22809271][^c23366551]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22809251] Subsection (2) applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809251]

- (a) the Secretary of State refuses an application for the approval of a vehicle under section 42(1); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809251]
- (b) before the end of the prescribed period, the applicant asks the Secretary of State to review the decision and pays any fee fixed under section 45. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809251]

(2) The Secretary of State shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809251]

- (a) review the decision; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809251]
- (b) in doing so, consider any representations made to him in writing, before the end of the prescribed period, by the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809251]

(3) A person applying for an accessibility certificate or an approval certificate may appeal to the Secretary of State against the refusal of a vehicle examiner to issue such a certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809251]

(4) An appeal must be made within the prescribed time and in the prescribed manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809251]

(5) Regulations may make provision as to the procedure to be followed in connection with appeals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809251]

(6) On the determination of an appeal, the Secretary of State may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809251]

- (a) confirm, vary or reverse the decision appealed against; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809251]
- (b) give such directions as he thinks fit to the vehicle examiner for giving effect to his decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809251]
