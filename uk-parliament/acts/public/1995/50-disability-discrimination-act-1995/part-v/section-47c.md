# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 47C

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 47C). (not yet in force: Rail vehicle accessibility compliance certificates: fees)[^c22810211][^c20485931]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1))[^c22810191] *(not yet in force: Such fees, payable at such times, as may be prescribed may be charged by the Secretary of State in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810191])*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: applications for, and the issue of, rail vehicle accessibility compliance certificates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810191])*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: copies of such certificates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810191])*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: reviews under section 47A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810191])*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: referrals of disputes under provision that, in accordance with section 47B(6)(d), is contained in regulations under section 47B(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810191])*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Any such fees received by the Secretary of State shall be paid by him into the Consolidated Fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810191])*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Regulations under subsection (1) may make provision for the repayment of fees, in whole or in part, in such circumstances as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810191])*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Before making any regulations under subsection (1) the Secretary of State shall consult such representative organisations as he thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810191])*

## (not yet in force: 47C). (not yet in force: Rail vehicle accessibility compliance certificates: fees)[^c20486061]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Such fees, payable at such times, as may be prescribed may be charged by the Department for Regional Development in respect of—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: applications for, and the issue of, rail vehicle accessibility compliance certificates;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: copies of such certificates;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: reviews under section 47A;)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: referrals of disputes under provision that, in accordance with section 47B(6)(d), is contained in regulations under section 47B(1).)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Any such fees received by the Department for Regional Development shall be paid by it into the Consolidated Fund.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Regulations under subsection (1) may make provision for the repayment of fees, in whole or in part, in such circumstances as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Before making any regulations under subsection (1) the Department for Regional Development shall consult such representative organisations as it thinks fit.)*
