## 46. Rail vehicle accessibility regulations.[^c18600751][^c22809351]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22809331] The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331] Department of the Environment[^c22421741] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331] may make regulations (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]**rail vehicle accessibility regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]**”) for the purpose of securing that it is possible— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]

- (a) for disabled persons— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
  - (i) to get on to and off regulated rail vehicles in safety and without unreasonable difficulty; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22809331] to be carried in such vehicles in safety and in reasonable comfort; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
- (b) for disabled persons in wheelchairs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
  - (i) to get on to and off such vehicles in safety and without unreasonable difficulty while remaining in their wheelchairs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22809331] to be carried in such vehicles in safety and in reasonable comfort while remaining in their wheelchairs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]

(2) Rail vehicle accessibility regulations may, in particular, make provision as to the construction, use and maintenance of regulated rail vehicles including provision as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]

- (a) the fitting of equipment to vehicles; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
- (b) equipment to be carried by vehicles; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
- (c) the design of equipment to be fitted to, or carried by, vehicles; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
- (d) the use of equipment fitted to, or carried by, vehicles; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
- (e) the toilet facilities to be provided in vehicles; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
- (f) the location and floor area of the wheelchair accommodation to be provided in vehicles; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
- (g) assistance to be given to disabled persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]

(3) [^c20397591]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) [^c20397591]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4A)[^c20397781] The Department for Regional Development shall exercise the power to make rail vehicle accessibility regulations so as to secure that on and after 1st January 2020 every rail vehicle is a regulated rail vehicle, but this does not affect the powers conferred by subsection (5) or section 47(1) or 67(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]

(5) Different provision may be made in rail vehicle accessibility regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]

- (a) as respects different classes or descriptions of rail vehicle; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
- (b) as respects the same class or description of rail vehicle in different circumstances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
- (c) as respects different networks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]

(6) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]

- “**network *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]**” means any permanent way or other means of guiding or supporting rail vehicles or any section of it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
- [^c20397641]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “[^c20397821]**rail vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]**” means a vehicle constructed or adapted to carry passengers by rail; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
- “**regulated rail vehicle *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]**” means any rail vehicle to which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331] provisions of[^c20397701] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331] rail vehicle accessibility regulations are expressed to apply; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
- “**wheelchair accommodation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]**” has such meaning as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809331]
