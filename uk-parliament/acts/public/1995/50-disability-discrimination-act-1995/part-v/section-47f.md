## (not yet in force: 47F). (not yet in force: Penalty for using rail vehicle otherwise than in conformity with accessibility regulations)[^c20486101]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Where it appears to the Department for Regional Development that a regulated rail vehicle has been used for carriage otherwise than in conformity with a provision of rail vehicle accessibility regulations with which use of the vehicle is required to conform, that Department may give to the operator of the vehicle a notice—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: identifying the provision and how it was breached;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: identifying which of the regulated rail vehicles operated by the operator is or are covered by the notice; and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: specifying the improvement deadline.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The improvement deadline specified in a notice under subsection (1) may not be earlier than the end of the prescribed period beginning with the day when the notice is given to the operator.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Subsection (4) applies where—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the Department for Regional Development has given a notice under subsection (1);)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the improvement deadline specified in the notice has passed; and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: it appears to that Department that a vehicle covered by the notice has after that deadline been used for carriage otherwise than in conformity with the provision identified in the notice.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The Department for Regional Development may give to the operator a further notice—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: identifying the provision and how it was breached;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: identifying which of the regulated rail vehicles covered by the notice under subsection (1) is or are covered by the further notice; and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: specifying the final deadline.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The final deadline specified in a notice under subsection (4) may not be earlier than the end of the prescribed period beginning with the day when the notice is given to the operator.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: If—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the Department for Regional Development has given a notice under subsection (4), and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a vehicle covered by the notice is at a time after the final deadline used for carriage otherwise than in conformity with the provision identified in the notice,)*

*(not yet in force: that Department may require the operator of the vehicle to pay a penalty.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of subsection (1), a vehicle is operated by a person if that person is the operator of the vehicle.)*
