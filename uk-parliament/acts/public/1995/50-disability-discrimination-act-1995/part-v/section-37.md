# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 37

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Carrying of guide dogs and hearing dogs.[^c18600491][^c22806541][^c23366201]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22806521] This section imposes duties on the driver of a taxi which has been hired— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

- (a) by or for a disabled person who is accompanied by his guide dog or hearing dog, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]
- (b) by a person who wishes such a disabled person to accompany him in the taxi. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

(2) The disabled person is referred to in this section as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]**the passenger *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]**”.

(3) The duties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

- (a) to carry the passenger’s dog and allow it to remain with the passenger; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]
- (b) not to make any additional charge for doing so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

(4) A driver of a taxi who fails to comply with any duty imposed on him by this section is guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

(5) If the licensing authority is satisfied that it is appropriate on medical grounds to exempt a person from the duties imposed by this section, it shall issue him with a certificate of exemption. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

(6) In determining whether to issue a certificate of exemption, the licensing authority shall, in particular, have regard to the physical characteristics of the taxi which the applicant drives or those of any kind of taxi in relation to which he requires the certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

(7) A certificate of exemption shall be issued— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

- (a) with respect to a specified taxi or a specified kind of taxi; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]
- (b) for such period as may be specified in the certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

(8) The driver of a taxi is exempt from the duties imposed by this section if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

- (a) a certificate of exemption issued to him under this section is in force with respect to the taxi; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]
- (b) the prescribed notice of his exemption is exhibited on the taxi in the prescribed manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

(9) The Secretary of State may, for the purposes of this section, prescribe any other category of dog trained to assist a disabled person who has a disability of a prescribed kind. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

(10) This section applies in relation to any such prescribed category of dog as it applies in relation to guide dogs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

(11) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]

- “**guide dog *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]**” means a dog which has been trained to guide a blind person; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]
- “**hearing dog *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]**” means a dog which has been trained to assist a deaf person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806521]
