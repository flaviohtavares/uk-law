# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 49

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Forgery and false statements.[^c22810981][^c20374151]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22810961] In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]**relevant document *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]

- (a) a certificate of exemption issued under section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961] section 36, 37 or 37A[^c18600821] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961];
- (b) a notice of a kind mentioned in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961] section 36(9)(b), 37(8)(b) or 37A(8)(b)[^c18600831] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961];
- (c) an accessibility certificate; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]
- (d) an approval certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]

(2) A person is guilty of an offence if, with intent to deceive, he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]

- (a) forges, alters or uses a relevant document; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]
- (b) lends a relevant document to any other person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]
- (c) allows a relevant document to be used by any other person; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]
- (d) makes or has in his possession any document which closely resembles a relevant document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]

(3) A person who is guilty of an offence under subsection (2) is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or to a fine or to both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]

(4) A person who knowingly makes a false statement for the purpose of obtaining an accessibility certificate or an approval certificate is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22810961]
