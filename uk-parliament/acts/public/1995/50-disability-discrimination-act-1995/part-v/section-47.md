# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 47

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Exemption from rail vehicle accessibility regulations.[^c18600781][^c22809381]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c20484751][^c22809361] The Department for Regional Development may by order (an “exemption order”)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]

- (a) authorise the use for carriage of a regulated rail vehicle even though the vehicle does not conform with the provisions of rail vehicle accessibility regulations with which it is required to conform; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]
- (b) authorise a regulated rail vehicle to be used for carriage otherwise than in conformity with the provisions of rail vehicle accessibility regulations with which use of the vehicle is required to conform. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]

(1A) Authority under subsection (1)(a) or (b) may be for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]

- (a) any regulated rail vehicle that is specified or is of a specified description; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]
- (b) use in specified circumstances of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]
  - (i) any regulated rail vehicle, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22809361] any regulated rail vehicle that is specified or is of a specified description. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]

(2) Regulations may make provision with respect to exemption orders including, in particular, provision as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]

- (a) the persons by whom applications for exemption orders may be made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]
- (b) the form in which such applications are to be made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]
- (c) information to be supplied in connection with such applications; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]
- (d) the period for which exemption orders are to continue in force; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]
- (e) the revocation of exemption orders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]

(3) After considering any application for an exemption order and consulting *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361] such persons as it[^c23356321] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361] considers appropriate, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361] Department of the Environment[^c23356311] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361] may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]

- (a) make an exemption order in the terms of the application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]
- (b) make an exemption order in such other terms as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361] it[^c23356341] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361] considers appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]
- (c) refuse to make an exemption order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]

(4) An exemption order may be made subject to such restrictions and conditions as may be specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]

(5) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]**specified *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]**” means specified in an exemption order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809361]
