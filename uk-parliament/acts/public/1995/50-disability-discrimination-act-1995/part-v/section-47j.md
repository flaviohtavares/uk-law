## (not yet in force: 47J). (not yet in force: Penalties under sections 47D to 47H: amount, due date and recovery)[^c20486101]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: In this section “)****(not yet in force: penalty)****(not yet in force: ” means a penalty under any of sections 47D to 47H.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The amount of a penalty—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: must not exceed the maximum prescribed for the purposes of this subsection; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: must not exceed 10 per cent of the turnover of the person on whom it is imposed.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of subsection (2)(b), a person's turnover shall be determined in accordance with regulations.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: A penalty must be paid to the Department for Regional Development before the end of the prescribed period.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Any sum payable to the Department for Regional Development as a penalty may be recovered by that Department as a debt due to it.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: In proceedings under subsection (5) for enforcement of a penalty, no question may be raised as to—)*
