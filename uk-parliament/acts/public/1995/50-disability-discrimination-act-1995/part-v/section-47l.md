## (not yet in force: 47L). (not yet in force: Penalties under sections 47D to 47H: appeals)[^c20486101]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A person may appeal to the county court against a penalty imposed on him under any of sections 47D to 47H on the ground that—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: he is not liable to the imposition of a penalty; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the amount of the penalty is too high.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: On an appeal under this section, the county court may—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: allow the appeal and cancel the penalty;)*

- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: dismiss the appeal.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: An appeal under this section shall be a re-hearing of the decision of the Department for Regional Development to impose a penalty, and shall be determined having regard to—)*

- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: any other matters which the county court thinks relevant (which may include matters of which the Department for Regional Development was unaware).)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: An appeal may be brought by a person under this section against a penalty whether or not—)*
