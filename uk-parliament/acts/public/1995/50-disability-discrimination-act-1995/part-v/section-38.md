# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 38

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Appeals Appeal against refusal of exemption certificate[^c22806751][^c18600561][^c20487921][^c23366221][^c20427741]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22806741] Any person who is aggrieved by the refusal of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741] the Department of the Environment[^c23366651] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741] to issue an exemption certificate under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741] section 36, 37 or 37A[^c18600571] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741] may appeal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741] a magistrates'[^c20427491] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741] court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741] , a court of summary jurisdiction[^c22736731] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741]or, in Scotland, the sheriff court[^c20427571] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741] before the end of the period of 28 days beginning with the date of the refusal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741]

(2) On an appeal to it under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741] subsection (1)[^c20427591] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741]this section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741], the court may direct the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741] Department of the Environment[^c23366671] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741] to issue the appropriate certificate of exemption to have effect for such period as may be specified in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741]

(3) [^c20427531]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c20427611] Any person who is aggrieved by the decision of a licensing authority to include a vehicle on a list maintained under section 36A may appeal to a magistrates' court or, in Scotland, the sheriff court before the end of the period of 28 days beginning with the date of the inclusion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741]

(5) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741]**licensing authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741]**” has the meaning given by section 36A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806741]
