# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 42

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Approval certificates.[^c18600671][^c22809201][^c23366531]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22809181] Where the Secretary of State is satisfied that such provisions of the PSV accessibility regulations as may be prescribed for the purposes of section 41 are satisfied in respect of a particular vehicle he may approve the vehicle for the purposes of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]

(2) A vehicle which has been so approved is referred to in this section as a “type vehicle”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]

(3) Subsection (4) applies where a declaration in the prescribed form has been made by an authorised person that a particular vehicle conforms in design, construction and equipment with a type vehicle. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]

(4) A vehicle examiner may, after examining (if he thinks fit) the vehicle to which the declaration applies, issue a certificate in the prescribed form (“an approval certificate”) that it conforms to the type vehicle. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]

(5) The Secretary of State may make regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]

- (a) with respect to applications for, and grants of, approval under subsection (1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]
- (b) with respect to applications for, and the issue of, approval certificates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]
- (c) providing for the examination of vehicles in respect of which applications have been made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]
- (d) with respect to the issue of copies of approval certificates in place of certificates which have been lost or destroyed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]

(6) The Secretary of State may at any time withdraw his approval of a type vehicle. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]

(7) Where an approval is withdrawn— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]

- (a) no further approval certificates shall be issued by reference to the type vehicle; but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]
- (b) any approval certificate issued by reference to the type vehicle before the withdrawal shall continue to have effect for the purposes of section 41. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]

(8) In subsection (3) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]**authorised person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]**” means a person authorised by the Secretary of State for the purposes of that subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22809181]
