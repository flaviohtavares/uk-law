## (not yet in force: 47K). (not yet in force: Penalties under sections 47D to 47H: procedure)[^c20486101]

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: If the Department for Regional Development decides that a person is liable to a penalty, that Department must notify the person of the decision.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A notification under subsection (2) must—)*

- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: include an explanation of the steps that the person may take if he objects to the penalty.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Where a person to whom a notification under subsection (2) is issued objects on the ground that—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: he is not liable to the imposition of a penalty, or)*

*(not yet in force: the person may give a notice of objection to the Department for Regional Development.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: A notice of objection must—)*
