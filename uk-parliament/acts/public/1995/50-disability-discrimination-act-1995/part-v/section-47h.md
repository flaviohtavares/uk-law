## (not yet in force: 47H). (not yet in force: Sections 47E and 47F: supplementary powers)[^c20486101]

*Extent: N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of section 47E, the Department for Regional Development may give notice to a person requiring the person to supply the Department for Regional Development, by a time specified in the notice, with a vehicle number or other identifier for a rail vehicle—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: of which that person is the operator; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: which is described in the notice.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The time specified in a notice given to a person under subsection (1) may not be earlier than the end of 14 days beginning with the day when the notice is given to the person.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: If a person to whom a notice is given under subsection (1) does not comply with the notice by the time specified in the notice, the Department for Regional Development may require the person to pay a penalty.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Where the Department for Regional Development has given a notice to a person under section 47E(1) or (4) or 47F(1) or (4), that Department may request that person to supply that Department, by a time specified in the request, with a statement detailing the steps taken in response to the notice.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The time specified in a request under subsection (4) must—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: if the request relates to a notice under section 47E(1) or 47F(1), be no earlier than the improvement deadline; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: if the request relates to a notice under section 47E(4) or 47F(4), be no earlier than the final deadline.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: Where a request under subsection (4)—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: relates to a notice under section 47E(1) or 47F(1), and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: is not complied with by the time specified in the request,)*

*(not yet in force: the Department for Regional Development may treat section 47E(3)(c) or (as the case may be) section 47F(3)(c) as being satisfied in the case concerned.)*
