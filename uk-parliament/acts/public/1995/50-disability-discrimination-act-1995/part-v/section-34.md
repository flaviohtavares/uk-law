# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 34

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. New licences conditional on compliance with taxi accessibility regulations.[^c22806431][^c23366181]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1)[^c22806421] The Department of the Environment shall not grant a public service vehicle licence under Article 61 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 for a taxi unless the vehicle conforms with those provisions of the taxi accessibility regulations with which it will be required to conform if licensed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806421]

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply if such a licence was in force with respect to the vehicle at any time during the period of 28 days immediately before the day on which the licence is granted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806421]

(3) The Department of the Environment may by order provide for subsection (2) to cease to have effect on such date as may be specified in the order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22806421]
