### Part II — Discrimination in Other Areas

#### Restriction on proceedings for breach of Part III

**5.**[^c22874501] (1)[^c22874481] Except as provided by section 25 no civil or criminal proceedings may be brought against any person in respect of an act merely because the act is unlawful under Part III. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22874481]

#### Period within which proceedings must be brought

**6.**[^c22874531] (1)[^c22874511] A county court or a sheriff court shall not consider a claim under section 25 unless proceedings in respect of the claim are instituted before the end of the period of six months beginning when the act complained of was done. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22874511]

(2)[^c20464791] Where, in relation to proceedings or prospective proceedings under section 25, the dispute concerned is referred for conciliation in pursuance of arrangements under section 27 of the Equality Act 2006 before the end of the period of six months mentioned in sub-paragraph (1), the period allowed by that sub-paragraph shall be extended by three months. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22874511]

(3) A court may consider any claim under section 25 which is out of time if, in all the circumstances of the case, it considers that it is just and equitable to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22874511]

(4) For the purposes of sub-paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22874511]

- (a) where an unlawful act of discrimination is attributable to a term in a contract, that act is to be treated as extending throughout the duration of the contract; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22874511]
- (b) any act extending over a period shall be treated as done at the end of that period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22874511]
- (c) a deliberate omission shall be treated as done when the person in question decided upon it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22874511]

(5) In the absence of evidence establishing the contrary, a person shall be taken for the purposes of this paragraph to decide upon an omission— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22874511]

- (a) when he does an act inconsistent with doing the omitted act; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22874511]
- (b) if he has done no such inconsistent act, when the period expires within which he might reasonably have been expected to do the omitted act if it was to be done. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22874511]

**6B.**[^c22874551] (1) Sub-paragraph (2) applies to a remedy other than—

- (a) damages; or
- (b) a declaration.

(2) In proceedings under section 25, the remedy shall be obtainable in respect of a relevant discriminatory act only if the court is satisfied that—

- (a) no criminal investigation,
- (b) no decision to institute criminal proceedings, and
- (c) no criminal proceedings,

would be prejudiced by the remedy.

(3) In sub-paragraph (2) “**relevant discriminatory act**” means an act—

- (a) which is done, or by virtue of section 57 or 58 is treated as done by a person—
  - (i) in carrying out public investigator functions, or
  - (ii) in carrying out functions as a public prosecutor; and
- (b) which is unlawful by virtue of section 21B(1).
