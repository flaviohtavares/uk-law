# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 61

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Amendment of Disabled Persons (Employment) Act 1944.[^c18602211][^c22812341]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22812301] Section 15 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301] [^c18602231]Disabled Persons (Employment) Act 1944 (which gives the Secretary of State power to make arrangements for the provision of supported employment) is amended as set out in subsections (2) to (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]

(2) In subsection (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]

- (a) for “persons registered as handicapped by disablement” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301] “ disabled persons ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301];
- (b) for “their disablement” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301] “ their disability ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
- (c) for “are not subject to disablement” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301] “ do not have a disability ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301].

(3) In subsection (2), for the words from “any of one or more companies” to “so required and prohibited” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301] “ any company, association or body ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301].

(4) After subsection (2) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]

> (2A) The only kind of company which the Minister himself may form in exercising his powers under this section is a company which is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
>
> - (a) required by its constitution to apply its profits, if any, or other income in promoting its objects; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
> - (b) prohibited by its constitution from paying any dividend to its members. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]

(5) After subsection (5) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]

> (5A) For the purposes of this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
>
> - (a) a person is a disabled person if he is a disabled person for the purposes of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
> - (b) “**disability *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]**” has the same meaning as in that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]

(6) The provisions of section 16 (preference to be given under section 15 of that Act to ex-service men and women) shall become subsection (1) of that section and at the end insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]

> and whose disability is due to that service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
>
> (2) For the purposes of subsection (1) of this section, a disabled person’s disability shall be treated as due to service of a particular kind only in such circumstances as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]

(7) The following provisions of the Act of 1944 shall cease to have effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]

- (a) section 1 (definition of “disabled person”); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
- (b) sections 6 to 8 (the register of disabled persons); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
- (c) sections 9 to 11 (obligations on employers with substantial staffs to employ a quota of registered persons); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
- (d) section 12 (the designated employment scheme for persons registered as handicapped by disablement); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
- (e) section 13 (interpretation of provisions repealed by this Act); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
- (f) section 14 (records to be kept by employers); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
- (g) section 19 (proceedings in relation to offences); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
- (h) section 21 (application as respects place of employment, and nationality). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]

(8) Any provision of subordinate legislation in which “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]**disabled person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]**” is defined by reference to the Act of 1944 shall be construed as if that expression had the same meaning as in this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]

(9) Subsection (8) does not prevent the further amendment of any such provision by subordinate legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812301]
