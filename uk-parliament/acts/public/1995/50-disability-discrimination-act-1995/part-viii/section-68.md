## 68. Interpretation.[^c18602921][^c22814251]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22497171][^c18602941][^c22814231] In this Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

- “**accessibility certificate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means a certificate issued under section 41(1)(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” includes a deliberate omission; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**the Agency *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means the Labour Relations Agency; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**approval certificate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means a certificate issued under section 42(4); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**the Assembly *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means the Northern Ireland Assembly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

- “[^c20844581]**criminal proceedings *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” includes service law proceedings (as defined by section 324(5) of the Armed Forces Act 2006); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**the Department of Economic Development *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means the Department of Economic Development in Northern Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**the Department of the Environment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

- “**employment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means, subject to any prescribed provision, employment under a contract of service or of apprenticeship or a contract personally to do work and related expressions are to be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “[^c20376051]**employment at an establishment in Northern Ireland *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” is to be construed in accordance with subsections (2) to (4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “[^c20378891]**employment services *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” has the meaning given in section 21A(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

- “**government department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means a Northern Ireland department or a department of the Government of the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “[^c20378121]**group insurance arrangement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means an arrangement between an employer and another for the provision by the other of facilities by way of insurance to the employer's employees or to any class of those employees; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “[^c20376091]**Minister of the Crown *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” includes the Treasury and the Defence Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**Northern Ireland department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” includes (except in sections 51 and 52) the head of a Northern Ireland department; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**occupational pension scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” has the same meaning as in the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “[^c20378911]**the Office *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**premises *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**”, includes land of any description; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means prescribed by regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**profession *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” includes any vocation or occupation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**provider of services *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” has the meaning given in section 19(2)(b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

(1A)[^c20378951] In this Act “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**criminal investigation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

- (a) any investigation which a person in carrying out functions to which section 21B(1) applies has a duty to conduct with a view to it being ascertained whether a person should be charged with an offence, or whether a person charged with or prosecuted for an offence is guilty of it; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- (b) any investigation which is conducted by a person in carrying out functions to which section 21B(1) applies and which in the circumstances may lead to a decision by that person to institute criminal proceedings which the person has power to conduct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

(1B) In this Act “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**public investigator functions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” means functions of conducting criminal investigations or charging offenders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

(1C)[^c22497181] In subsections (1A) and (1B)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

- “**offence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” includes *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231] any service offence within the meaning of the Armed Forces Act 2006[^c20844601] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- “**offender *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]**” is to be construed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

(2)[^c20376151] Employment (including employment on board a ship to which subsection (2B) applies or on an aircraft or hovercraft to which subsection (2C) applies) is to be regarded as being employment at an establishment in Northern Ireland if the employee – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

- (a) does his work wholly or partly in Northern Ireland; or, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- (b) does his work wholly outside Northern Ireland and subsection (2A) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

(2A) This subsection applies if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

- (a) the employer has a place of business at an establishment in Northern Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- (b) the work is for the purposes of the business carried on at the establishment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- (c) the employee is ordinarily resident in Northern Ireland – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
  - (i) at the time when he applies for or is offered the employment, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22814231] at any time during the course of the employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

(2B) This subsection applies to a ship if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

- (a) it is registered at a port of registry in Northern Ireland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- (b) it belongs to or is possessed by Her Majesty in right of the Government of the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

(2C) This subsection applies to an aircraft or hovercraft if – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

- (a) it is – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
  - (i) registered in the United Kingdom, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22814231] operated by a person who has his principal place of business, or is ordinarily resident, in Northern Ireland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- (b) it belongs to or is possessed by Her Majesty in right of the Government of the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

(2D) The following are not to be regarded as being employment at an establishment in Northern Ireland – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

- (a) employment on board a ship to which subsection (2B) does not apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]
- (b) employment on an aircraft or hovercraft to which subsection (2C) does not apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231]

(4) Employment of a prescribed kind, or in prescribed circumstances, is to be regarded as not being employment at an establishment in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231] Northern Ireland[^c18602991] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22814231].
