# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 69

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Financial provisions.[^c18603231][^c22855361]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22855341]

- (a) any expenditure incurred by a Minister of the Crown under this Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22855341]
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under or by virtue of any other enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22855341]
