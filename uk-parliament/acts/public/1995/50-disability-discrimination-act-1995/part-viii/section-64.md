# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 64

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Application to Crown etc.[^c22812561]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^c20383891][^c22812541] The following provisions bind the Crown— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

- (a) sections 21B to 21E and Part 5A, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]
- (b) the other provisions of this Act so far as applying for the purposes of provisions mentioned in paragraph (a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

and sections 57 and 58 shall apply for purposes of provisions mentioned in paragraph (a) as if service as a Crown servant were employment by the Crown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

(1) This Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541] , other than the provisions mentioned in paragraphs (a) and (b) of subsection (A1),[^c20384001] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541] applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

- (a) to an act done by or for purposes of a Minister of the Crown or government department, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]
- (b) to an act done on behalf of the Crown by a statutory body, or a person holding a statutory office, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

as it applies to an act done by a private person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

(2) [^c20381861]. . . Part II applies to service— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

- (a) for purposes of a Minister of the Crown or government department, other than service of a person holding a statutory office, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]
- (b) on behalf of the Crown for purposes of a person holding a statutory office or purposes of a statutory body, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

as it applies to employment by a private person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

(2A)[^c20993491] Subsections *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541] (A1) to[^c20993561] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541](2) have effect subject to section 64A and section 64B. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

(2A)[^c20382471] Subsections *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541] (A1) to[^c20384021] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541](2) have effect subject to section 64A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

(3) The provisions of Parts II to IV of the 1947 Act apply to proceedings against the Crown under this Act as they apply to Crown proceedings in England and Wales *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541] [^c20383271]. . . .

(4) The provisions of Part V of the 1947 Act apply to proceedings against the Crown under this Act as they apply to proceedings in Scotland which by virtue of that Part are treated as civil proceedings by or against the Crown; but the proviso to section 44 of that Act (removal of proceedings from the sheriff court to the Court of Session) does not apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

(5) [^c20382491]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) [^c20382491]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) [^c20382581]. . . Part II does not apply to service in any of the naval, military or air forces of the Crown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

(8) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]

- “**the 1947 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]**” means the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541] [^c18602381]Crown Proceedings Act 1947; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]
- [^c20991821]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**Crown proceedings *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]**” means proceedings which, by virtue of section 23 of the 1947 Act, are treated for the purposes of Part II of that Act as civil proceedings by or against the Crown; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]
- [^c20382611]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c20382611]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c20382611]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

- “**service for purposes of a Minister of the Crown or government department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]**” does not include service in any office for the time being mentioned in Schedule 2 (Ministerial offices) to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541] [^c18602441]House of Commons Disqualification Act 1975; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]
- “**statutory body *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]**” means a body set up by or under an enactment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812541]
