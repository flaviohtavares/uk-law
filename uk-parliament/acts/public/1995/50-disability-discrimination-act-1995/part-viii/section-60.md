# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 60

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Appointment by Secretary of State of advisers.[^c22812271]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22812221] The Secretary of State may appoint such persons as he thinks fit to advise or assist him in connection with matters relating to the employment of disabled persons and persons who have had a disability. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]

(2) Persons may be appointed by the Secretary of State to act generally or in relation to a particular area or locality. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]

(3) The Secretary of State may pay to any person appointed under this section such allowances and compensation for loss of earnings as he considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]

(4) The approval of the Treasury is required for any payment under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]

(5) In subsection (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]**employment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]**” includes self-employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]

(6) The Secretary of State may by order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]

- (a) provide for section 17 of, and Schedule 2 to, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221] [^c18602181]Disabled Persons (Employment) Act 1944 (national advisory council and district advisory committees) to cease to have effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]
  - (i) so far as concerns the national advisory council; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22812221] so far as concerns district advisory committees; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]
- (b) repeal that section and Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]

(7) At any time before the coming into force of an order under paragraph (b) of subsection (6), section 17 of the Act of 1944 shall have effect as if in subsection (1), after “disabled persons” in each case there were inserted “, and persons who have had a disability,” and as if at the end of the section there were added— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]

> (3) For the purposes of this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]
>
> - (a) a person is a disabled person if he is a disabled person for the purposes of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221] [^c18602191]Disability Discrimination Act 1995; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]
> - (b) “**disability *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]**” has the same meaning as in that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]

(8) At any time before the coming into force of an order under paragraph (a)(i) or (b) of subsection (6), section 16 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221] [^c18602201]Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970 (which extends the functions of the national advisory council) shall have effect as if after “disabled persons” in each case there were inserted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221] “ , and persons who have had a disability, ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221] and as if at the end of the section there were added— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]

> (2) For the purposes of this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]
>
> - (a) a person is a disabled person if he is a disabled person for the purposes of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]
> - (b) “**disability *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]**” has the same meaning as in that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812221]
