# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 70

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Short title, commencement, extent etc.[^c23376421][^c18603301]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c23376201] This Act may be cited as the Disability Discrimination Act 1995. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

(2) This section (apart from subsections (4), (5) and (7)) comes into force on the passing of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

(2A)[^c20453311] The following provisions of this Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

- (a) section 36 so far as it applies to designated vehicles, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]
- (b) section 36A, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]
- (c) section 38 (which has already been brought in force in England and Wales by an order under subsection (3)) so far as it extends to Scotland, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

come into force 2 months after the passing of the Local Transport Act 2008. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

(3)[^c18603321][^c18603331] The other provisions of this Act come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint and different days may be appointed for different purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

(4) Schedule 6 makes consequential amendments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

(5) The repeals set out in Schedule 7 shall have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

(5A)[^c20452371] Sections 7A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201] 7B and 49G[^c20453021] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201] extend to England and Wales only. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

(5B) Sections 7C and 7D extend to Scotland only. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

(6) Subject to subsections (5A) and (5B), this Act extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland;[^c20452391] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201] but in their application to Northern Ireland the provisions of this Act mentioned in Schedule 8 shall have effect subject to the modifications set out in that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

(7)[^c18603341] In Part II of Schedule 1 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201] [^c18603351]House of Commons Disqualification Act 1975 and in Part II of Schedule 1 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201] [^c18603361]Northern Ireland Assembly Disqualification Act 1975 (bodies whose members are disqualified) in each case insert at the appropriate places— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

> The National Disability Council.[^c18603371] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

> The Northern Ireland Disability Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]

(8) Consultations which are required by any provision of this Act to be held by the Secretary of State may be held by him before the coming into force of that provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376201]
