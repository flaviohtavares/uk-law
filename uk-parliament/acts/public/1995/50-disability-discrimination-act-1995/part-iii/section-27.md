# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 27

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Alterations to premises occupied under leases.[^c22791511][^c18598761][^c18598771][^c20476341]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22791501] This section applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]

- (a) a provider of services *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501] , a public authority (within the meaning given by section 21B) or an association to which section 21F applies[^c20500071] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501](“the occupier”) occupies premises under a lease; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]
- (b) but for this section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501] the occupier[^c20500091] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501] would not be entitled to make a particular alteration to the premises; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]
- (c) the alteration is one which the occupier proposes to make in order to comply with a section 21 duty *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501] or a duty imposed under section 21E or 21H[^c20500111] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501].

(2) Except to the extent to which it expressly so provides, the lease shall have effect by virtue of this subsection as if it provided— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]

- (a) for the occupier to be entitled to make the alteration with the written consent of the lessor; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]
- (b) for the occupier to have to make a written application to the lessor for consent if he wishes to make the alteration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]
- (c) if such an application is made, for the lessor not to withhold his consent unreasonably; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]
- (d) for the lessor to be entitled to make his consent subject to reasonable conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]

(3) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]

- “**lease *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]**” includes a tenancy, sub-lease or sub-tenancy and an agreement for a lease, tenancy, sub-lease or sub-tenancy; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]
- “**sub-lease *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]**sub-tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]**” have such meaning as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]

(4) If the terms and conditions of a lease— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]

- (a) impose conditions which are to apply if the occupier alters the premises, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]
- (b) entitle the lessor to impose conditions when consenting to the occupier’s altering the premises, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]

the occupier is to be treated for the purposes of subsection (1) as not being entitled to make the alteration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]

(5) Part II of Schedule 4 supplements the provisions of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791501]
