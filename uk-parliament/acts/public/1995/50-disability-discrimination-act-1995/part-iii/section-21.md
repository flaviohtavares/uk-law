# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 21

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Duty of providers of services to make adjustments.[^c18598691][^c22786991]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22786971] Where a provider of services has a practice, policy or procedure which makes it impossible or unreasonably difficult for disabled persons to make use of a service which he provides, or is prepared to provide, to other members of the public, it is his duty to take such steps as it is reasonable, in all the circumstances of the case, for him to have to take in order to change that practice, policy or procedure so that it no longer has that effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

(2)[^c18598701][^c20500021] Where a physical feature (for example, one arising from the design or construction of a building or the approach or access to premises) makes it impossible or unreasonably difficult for disabled persons to make use of such a service, it is the duty of the provider of that service to take such steps as it is reasonable, in all the circumstances of the case, for him to have to take in order to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

- (a) remove the feature; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (b) alter it so that it no longer has that effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (c) provide a reasonable means of avoiding the feature; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (d) provide a reasonable alternative method of making the service in question available to disabled persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

(3) Regulations may prescribe— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

- (a) matters which are to be taken into account in determining whether any provision of a kind mentioned in subsection (2)(c) or (d) is reasonable; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (b) categories of providers of services to whom subsection (2) does not apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

(4)[^c18598711][^c20500051] Where an auxiliary aid or service (for example, the provision of information on audio tape or of a sign language interpreter) would— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

- (a) enable disabled persons to make use of a service which a provider of services provides, or is prepared to provide, to members of the public, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (b) facilitate the use by disabled persons of such a service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

it is the duty of the provider of that service to take such steps as it is reasonable, in all the circumstances of the case, for him to have to take in order to provide that auxiliary aid or service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

(5) Regulations may make provision, for the purposes of this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

- (a) as to circumstances in which it is reasonable for a provider of services to have to take steps of a prescribed description; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (b) as to circumstances in which it is not reasonable for a provider of services to have to take steps of a prescribed description; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (c) as to what is to be included within the meaning of “practice, policy or procedure”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (d) as to what is not to be included within the meaning of that expression; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (e) as to things which are to be treated as physical features; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (f) as to things which are not to be treated as such features; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (g) as to things which are to be treated as auxiliary aids or services; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (h) as to things which are not to be treated as auxiliary aids or services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

(6) Nothing in this section requires a provider of services to take any steps which would fundamentally alter the nature of the service in question or the nature of his trade, profession or business. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

(7) Nothing in this section requires a provider of services to take any steps which would cause him to incur expenditure exceeding the prescribed maximum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

(8) Regulations under subsection (7) may provide for the prescribed maximum to be calculated by reference to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

- (a) aggregate amounts of expenditure incurred in relation to different cases; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (b) prescribed periods; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (c) services of a prescribed description; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (d) premises of a prescribed description; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
- (e) such other criteria as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

(9) Regulations may provide, for the purposes of subsection (7), for expenditure incurred by one provider of services to be treated as incurred by another. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]

(10) This section imposes duties only for the purpose of determining whether a provider of services has discriminated against a disabled person; and accordingly a breach of any such duty is not actionable as such. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786971]
