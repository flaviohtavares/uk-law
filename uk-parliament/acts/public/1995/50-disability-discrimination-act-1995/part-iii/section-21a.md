# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 21A

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21A. Employment services[^c22787051][^c20489551]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

 [^c22787031]

(1) In *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031] this Act[^c22225761] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031] , “**employment services *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

- (a) vocational guidance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]
- (b) vocational training; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]
- (c) services to assist a person to obtain or retain employment, or to establish himself as self-employed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

(2) It is unlawful for a provider of employment services, in relation to such services, to subject to harassment a disabled person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

- (a) to whom he is providing such services, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]
- (b) who has requested him to provide such services; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

and section 3B (meaning of “harassment”) applies for the purposes of this subsection as it applies for the purposes of Part II. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

(3) In their application to employment services, the preceding provisions of this Part have effect as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

(4) Section 19 has effect as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

- (a) after subsection (1)(a), there were inserted the following paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

  > - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22787031] in failing to comply with a duty imposed on him by subsection (1) of section 21 in circumstances in which the effect of that failure is to place the disabled person at a substantial disadvantage in comparison with persons who are not disabled in relation to the provision of the service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

  ;
- (b) in subsection (1)(b), for “section 21” there were substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031] “ subsection (2) or (4) of section 21 ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031];
- (c)[^c22225771] in subsection (2), for “sections 20 to 21ZA” there were substituted sections 20 to 21A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

(5) Section 20 has effect as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

- (a) after subsection (1), there were inserted the following subsection— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

  > - (1A) For the purposes of section 19, a provider of services also discriminates against a disabled person if he fails to comply with a duty imposed on him by subsection (1) of section 21 in relation to the disabled person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

  ;
- (b) in subsection (2)(a), for “a section 21 duty imposed” there were substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031] “ a duty imposed by subsection (2) or (4) of section 21 ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031];
- (c) after subsection (3), there were inserted the following subsection— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

  > - (3A) But treatment of a disabled person cannot be justified under subsection (3) if it amounts to direct discrimination falling within section 3A(5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

  .

(6) Section 21 has effect as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

- (a) in subsection (1), for “makes it impossible or unreasonably difficult for disabled persons to make use of” there were substituted places disabled persons at a substantial disadvantage in comparison with persons who are not disabled in relation to the provision of; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]
- (b) after subsection (1), there were inserted the following subsection— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

  > - (1A) In subsection (1), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]**practice, policy or procedure *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]**” includes a provision or criterion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787031]

  .
