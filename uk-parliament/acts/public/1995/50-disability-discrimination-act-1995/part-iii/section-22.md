# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 22

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Discrimination in relation to premises.[^c18598721]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22787221] It is unlawful for a person with power to dispose of any premises to discriminate against a disabled person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

- (a) in the terms on which he offers to dispose of those premises to the disabled person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
- (b) by refusing to dispose of those premises to the disabled person; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
- (c) in his treatment of the disabled person in relation to any list of persons in need of premises of that description. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to a person who owns an estate or interest in the premises and wholly occupies them unless, for the purpose of disposing of the premises, he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

- (a) uses the services of an estate agent, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
- (b) publishes an advertisement or causes an advertisement to be published. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

(3) It is unlawful for a person managing any premises to discriminate against a disabled person occupying those premises— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

- (a) in the way he permits the disabled person to make use of any benefits or facilities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
- (b) by refusing or deliberately omitting to permit the disabled person to make use of any benefits or facilities; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
- (c) by evicting the disabled person, or subjecting him to any other detriment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

(3A)[^c20491951] Regulations may make provision, for purposes of subsection (3)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

- (a) as to who is to be treated as being, or as to who is to be treated as not being, a person who manages premises; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
- (b) as to who is to be treated as being, or as to who is to be treated as not being, a person occupying premises. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

(4) It is unlawful for any person whose licence or consent is required for the disposal of any premises comprised in, or (in Scotland) the subject of, a tenancy to discriminate against a disabled person by withholding his licence or consent for the disposal of the premises to the disabled person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

(5) Subsection (4) applies to tenancies created before as well as after the passing of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

(6) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

- “**advertisement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]**” includes every form of advertisement or notice, whether to the public or not; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
- “**dispose *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]**”, in relation to premises, includes granting a right to occupy the premises, and, in relation to premises comprised in, or (in Scotland) the subject of, a tenancy, includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
  - (a) assigning the tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
  - (b) sub-letting or parting with possession of the premises or any part of the premises; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

  and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]**disposal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]**” shall be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
- “**estate agent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]**” means a person who, by way of profession or trade, provides services for the purpose of finding premises for persons seeking to acquire them or assisting in the disposal of premises; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
- “**tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]**” means a tenancy created— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
  - (a) by a lease or sub-lease, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
  - (b) by an agreement for a lease or sub-lease, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
  - (c) by a tenancy agreement, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
  - (d) in pursuance of any enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

(7) In the case of an act which constitutes discrimination by virtue of section 55, this section also applies to discrimination against a person who is not disabled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]

(8) This section applies only in relation to premises in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787221]
