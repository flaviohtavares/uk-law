# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 20

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Meaning of “discrimination”[^c22042181][^c22786961]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22786951] For the purposes of section 19, a provider of services discriminates against a disabled person if — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

- (a) for a reason which relates to the disabled person's disability, he treats him less favourably than he treats or would treat others to whom that reason does not or would not apply; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]
- (b) he cannot show that the treatment in question is justified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

(2) For the purposes of section 19, a provider of services also discriminates against a disabled person if — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

- (a) he fails to comply with a section 21 duty imposed on him in relation to the disabled person; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]
- (b) he cannot show that his failure to comply with that duty is justified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

(3) For the purposes of this section, treatment is justified only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

- (a) in the opinion of the provider of services, one or more of the conditions mentioned in subsection (4) are satisfied; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]
- (b) it is reasonable, in all the circumstances of the case, for him to hold that opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

(4) The conditions are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

- (a) in any case, the treatment is necessary in order not to endanger the health or safety of any person (which may include that of the disabled person); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]
- (b)[^c22042221][^c22042251] in any case, the disabled person is incapable of entering into an enforceable agreement, or of giving an informed consent, and for that reason the treatment is reasonable in that case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]
- (c) in a case falling within section 19(1)(a), the treatment is necessary because the provider of services would otherwise be unable to provide the service to members of the public; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]
- (d) in a case falling within section 19(1)(c) or (d), the treatment is necessary in order for the provider of services to be able to provide the service to the disabled person or to other members of the public; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]
- (e) in a case falling within section 19(1)(d), the difference in the terms on which the service is provided to the disabled person and those on which it is provided to other members of the public reflects the greater cost to the provider of services in providing the service to the disabled person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

(5) Any increase in the cost of providing a service to a disabled person which results from compliance by a provider of services with a section 21 duty shall be disregarded for the purposes of subsection (4)(e). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

(6) Regulations may make provision, for purposes of this section, as to circumstances in which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

- (a) it is reasonable for a provider of services to hold the opinion mentioned in subsection (3)(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]
- (b) it is not reasonable for a provider of services to hold that opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

(7) Regulations may make provision for subsection (4)(b) not to apply in prescribed circumstances where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

- (a) a person is acting for a disabled person under a power of attorney; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]
- (b) functions conferred by or under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951] the Mental Capacity Act 2005[^c22042211] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951] are exercisable in relation to a disabled person's property or affairs; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]
- (c)[^c22042201] powers are exercisable in relation to a disabled person’s property or affairs in consequence of the appointment, under the law of Scotland, of a guardian, tutor or judicial factor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

(8) Regulations may make provision, for purposes of this section, as to circumstances (other than those mentioned in subsection (4)) in which treatment is to be taken to be justified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]

(9) In subsections (3), (4) and (8) “treatment” includes failure to comply with a section 21 duty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22786951]
