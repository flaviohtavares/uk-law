# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 25

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Enforcement, remedies and procedure.[^c22791401]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22791391] A claim by any person that another person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

- (a) has discriminated against him in a way which is unlawful under this Part; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]
- (b) is by virtue of section 57 or 58 to be treated as having discriminated against him in such a way, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

may be made the subject of civil proceedings in the same way as any other claim in tort *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391] [^c22225801]. . . for breach of statutory duty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

(2) For the avoidance of doubt it is hereby declared that damages in respect of discrimination in a way which is unlawful under this Part may include compensation for injury to feelings whether or not they include compensation under any other head. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

(3) Proceedings in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391] Northern Ireland[^c22225841] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391] shall be brought only in a county court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

(4) [^c22192611]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) The remedies available in such proceedings are those which are available in the High Court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391] [^c22192591]. . . .

(6) Part II of Schedule 3 makes further provision about the enforcement of this Part and about procedure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

(6A)[^c20452121] Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to a claim by a person that another person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

- (a) has discriminated against him in relation to the provision under a group insurance arrangement of facilities by way of insurance; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]
- (b) is by virtue of section 57 or 58 to be treated as having discriminated against him in relation to the provision under such an arrangement of such facilities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

(7)[^c20452161][^c22421911] Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to a claim by a person that another person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

- (a) has discriminated against him in relation to the provision of employment services; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]
- (b) is by virtue of section 57 or 58 to be treated as having discriminated against him in relation to the provision of employment services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

(8) A claim— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

- (a) of the kind referred to in subsection (6A) or (7); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]
- (b) by a person that another— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]
  - (i) has subjected him to harassment in a way which is unlawful under section 21A(2), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22791391] is by virtue of section 57 or 58 to be treated as having subjected him to harassment in such a way, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

  may be presented as a complaint to an industrial tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]

(9) Section 17A(1A) to (7) and paragraphs 3 and 4 of Schedule 3 apply in relation to a complaint under subsection (8) as if it were a complaint under section 17A(1) (and paragraphs 6 to 8 of Schedule 3 do not apply in relation to such a complaint). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791391]
