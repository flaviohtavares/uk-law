# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 21H

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21H. Duty to make adjustments[^c22788361][^c20489891]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22788341] Regulations may make provision imposing on an association to which section 21F applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788341]

- (a) a duty to take steps for a purpose relating to a policy, practice or procedure of the association, or a physical feature, which adversely affects disabled persons who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788341]
  - (i) are, or might wish to become, members or associates of the association, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788341]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22788341] are, or are likely to become, guests of the association; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788341]
- (b) a duty to take steps for the purpose of making an auxiliary aid or service available to any such disabled persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788341]

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may (in particular)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788341]

- (a) make provision as to the cases in which a duty is imposed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788341]
- (b) make provision as to the steps which a duty requires to be taken; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788341]
- (c) make provision as to the purpose for which a duty requires steps to be taken. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788341]

(3) Any duty imposed under this section is imposed only for the purpose of determining whether an association has, for the purposes of section 21F, discriminated against a disabled person; and accordingly a breach of any such duty is not actionable as such. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788341]
