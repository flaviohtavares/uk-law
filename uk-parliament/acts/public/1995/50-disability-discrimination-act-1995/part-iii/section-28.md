# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 28

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Conciliation of disputes.[^c22237861][^c22791541][^c18598831]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22791521] The Commission may make arrangements with any other person for the provision of conciliation services by, or by persons appointed by, that person in relation to disputes arising under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791521]

(2) In deciding what arrangements (if any) to make, the Commission shall have regard to the desirability of securing, so far as reasonably practicable, that conciliation services are available for all disputes arising under this Part which the parties may wish to refer to conciliation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791521]

(3) No member or employee of the Commission may provide conciliation services in relation to disputes arising under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791521]

(4) The Commission shall ensure that any arrangements under this section include appropriate safeguards to prevent the disclosure to members or employees of the Commission of information obtained by a person in connection with the provision of conciliation services in pursuance of the arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791521]

(5) Subsection (4) does not apply to information relating to a dispute which is disclosed with the consent of the parties to that dispute. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791521]

(6) Subsection (4) does not apply to information which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791521]

- (a) is not identifiable with a particular dispute or a particular person; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791521]
- (b) is reasonably required by the Commission for the purpose of monitoring the operation of the arrangements concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791521]

(7) Anything communicated to a person while providing conciliation services in pursuance of any arrangements under this section is not admissible in evidence in any proceedings except with the consent of the person who communicated it to that person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791521]

(8) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791521]

- “the Commission” means the Equality Commission for Northern Ireland; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791521]
- “conciliation services” means advice and assistance provided by a conciliator to the parties to a dispute with a view to promoting its settlement otherwise than through the courts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791521]
