# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 19

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Discrimination in relation to goods, facilities and services.[^c18598521][^c22779281]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22779261] It is unlawful for a provider of services to discriminate against a disabled person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]

- (a) in refusing to provide, or deliberately not providing, to the disabled person any service which he provides, or is prepared to provide, to members of the public; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]
- (b) in failing to comply with any duty imposed on him by section 21 in circumstances in which the effect of that failure is to make it impossible or unreasonably difficult for the disabled person to make use of any such service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]
- (c) in the standard of service which he provides to the disabled person or the manner in which he provides it to him; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]
- (d) in the terms on which he provides a service to the disabled person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]

(2) For the purposes of this section and sections 20 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261] to 21ZA[^c20394741] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]—

- (a) the provision of services includes the provision of any goods or facilities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]
- (b) a person is “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]**a provider of services *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]**” if he is concerned with the provision, in the United Kingdom, of services to the public or to a section of the public; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]
- (c) it is irrelevant whether a service is provided on payment or without payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]

(3) The following are examples of services to which this section and sections 20 and 21 apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]

- (a) access to and use of any place which members of the public are permitted to enter; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]
- (b) access to and use of means of communication; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]
- (c) access to and use of information services; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]
- (d) accommodation in a hotel, boarding house or other similar establishment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]
- (e) facilities by way of banking or insurance or for grants, loans, credit or finance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]
- (f) facilities for entertainment, recreation or refreshment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]
- (g) facilities provided by employment agencies or under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261] sections 1 and 2 of the Employment and Training Act (Northern Ireland) 1950[^c22394981] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261];
- (h) the services of any profession or trade, or any local or other public authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]

(4) In the case of an act which constitutes discrimination by virtue of section 55, this section also applies to discrimination against a person who is not disabled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]

(4A)[^c20395011] Subsection (1) does not apply to anything that is governed by Regulation [(EC) No. 1107/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1107) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 July 2006 concerning the rights of disabled persons and persons with reduced mobility when travelling by air. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]

(5)[^c20394781] Regulations may provide for subsection (1) and section 21(1), (2) and (4) not to apply, or to apply only to a prescribed extent, in relation to a service of a prescribed description. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]

(6)[^c20395191] Nothing in this section or sections 20 to 21A applies to the provision of a service in relation to which discrimination is unlawful under Part III of the 2005 Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22779261]
