# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 26

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Validity and revision of certain agreements.[^c22791491]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22791471] Any term in a contract for the provision of goods, facilities or services or in any other agreement is void so far as it purports to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]

- (a) require a person to do anything which would contravene any provision of, or made under, this Part, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]
- (b) exclude or limit the operation of any provision of this Part, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]
- (c) prevent any person from making a claim under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]

(1A)[^c20452351] Subsection (1) does not apply to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]

- (a) any term in a contract for the provision of employment services; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]
- (b) any term in a contract which is a group insurance arrangement; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]
- (c) a term which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]
  - (i) is in an agreement which is not a contract of either of those kinds, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22791471] relates to the provision of employment services or the provision under a group insurance arrangement of facilities by way of insurance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]

(2) Paragraphs (b) and (c) of subsection (1) do not apply to an agreement settling a claim to which section 25 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]

(3) On the application of any person interested in an agreement to which subsection (1) applies, a county court or a sheriff court may make such order as it thinks just for modifying the agreement to take account of the effect of subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]

(4) No such order shall be made unless all persons affected have been— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]

- (a) given notice of the application; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]
- (b) afforded an opportunity to make representations to the court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]

(5) Subsection (4) applies subject to any rules of court providing for that notice to be dispensed with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]

(6) An order under subsection (3) may include provision as respects any period before the making of the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22791471]
