# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 21F

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21F. Discrimination by private clubs etc.[^c22788181][^c20489891]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22788161] This section applies to any association of persons (however described, whether corporate or unincorporate, and whether or not its activities are carried on for profit) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]

- (a) it has twenty-five or more members; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
- (b) admission to membership is regulated by its constitution and is so conducted that the members do not constitute a section of the public within the meaning of section 19(2); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
- (c) it is not an organisation to which section 13 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]

(2) It is unlawful for an association to which this section applies, in the case of a disabled person who is not a member of the association, to discriminate against him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]

- (a) in the terms on which it is prepared to admit him to membership; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
- (b) by refusing or deliberately omitting to accept his application for membership. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]

(3) It is unlawful for an association to which this section applies, in the case of a disabled person who is a member, or associate, of the association, to discriminate against him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]

- (a) in the way it affords him access to a benefit, facility or service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
- (b) by refusing or deliberately omitting to afford him access to a benefit, facility or service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
- (c) in the case of a member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
  - (i) by depriving him of membership, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22788161] by varying the terms on which he is a member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
- (d) in the case of an associate— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
  - (i) by depriving him of his rights as an associate, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22788161] by varying those rights; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
- (e) in either case, by subjecting him to any other detriment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]

(4) It is unlawful for an association to which this section applies to discriminate against a disabled person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]

- (a) in the way it affords him access to a benefit, facility or service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
- (b) by refusing or deliberately omitting to afford him access to a benefit, facility or service, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
- (c) by subjecting him to any other detriment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]

in his capacity as a guest of the association. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]

(5) It is unlawful for an association to which this section applies to discriminate against a disabled person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]

- (a) in the terms on which it is prepared to invite him, or permit a member or associate to invite him, to be a guest of the association; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
- (b) by refusing or deliberately omitting to invite him to be a guest of the association; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
- (c) by not permitting a member or associate to invite him to be a guest of the association. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]

(6) It is unlawful for an association to which this section applies to discriminate against a disabled person in failing in prescribed circumstances to comply with a duty imposed on it under section 21H. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]

(7) In the case of an act which constitutes discrimination by virtue of section 55, this section also applies to discrimination against a person who is not disabled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788161]
