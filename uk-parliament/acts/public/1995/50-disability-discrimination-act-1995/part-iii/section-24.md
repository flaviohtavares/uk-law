# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 24

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Meaning of “discrimination” .[^c18598731][^c22791381]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22787281] For the purposes of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281] section 22 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22047961] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281] sections 22 and 22A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281], a person (“A”) discriminates against a disabled person if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

- (a) for a reason which relates to the disabled person’s disability, he treats him less favourably than he treats or would treat others to whom that reason does not or would not apply; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (b) he cannot show that the treatment in question is justified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

(2) For the purposes of this section, treatment is justified only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

- (a) in A’s opinion, one or more of the conditions mentioned in subsection (3) are satisfied; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (b) it is reasonable, in all the circumstances of the case, for him to hold that opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

(3) The conditions are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

- (a) in any case, the treatment is necessary in order not to endanger the health or safety of any person (which may include that of the disabled person); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (b)[^c20500861] in any case, the disabled person is incapable of entering into an enforceable agreement, or of giving an informed consent, and for that reason the treatment is reasonable in that case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (c) in a case falling within section 22(3)(a), the treatment is necessary in order for the disabled person or the occupiers of other premises forming part of the building to make use of the benefit or facility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (d) in a case falling within section 22(3)(b), the treatment is necessary in order for the occupiers of other premises forming part of the building to make use of the benefit or facility. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (e)[^c20448851] in a case to which subsection (3A) applies, the terms are less favourable in order to recover costs which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
  - (i) as a result of the disabled person having a disability, are incurred in connection with the disposal of the premises, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22787281] are not costs incurred in connection with taking steps to avoid liability under section 24G(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (f) in a case to which subsection (3B) applies, the disabled person is subjected to the detriment in order to recover costs which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
  - (i) as a result of the disabled person having a disability, are incurred in connection with the management of the premises, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22787281] are not costs incurred in connection with taking steps to avoid liability under section 24A(1) or 24G(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

(3A)[^c20448871] This subsection applies to a case if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

- (a) the case falls within section 22(1)(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (b) the premises are to let; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (c) the person with power to dispose of the premises is a controller of them; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (d) the proposed disposal of the premises would involve the disabled person becoming a person to whom they are let. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

(3B) This subsection applies to a case if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

- (a) the case falls within section 22(3)(c); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (b) the detriment is not eviction; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (c) the premises are let premises; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (d) the person managing the premises is a controller of them; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (e) the disabled person is a person to whom the premises are let or, although not a person to whom they are let, is lawfully under the letting an occupier of them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

(3C) Section 24G(3) and (4) apply for the purposes of subsection (3A) as for those of section 24G; and section 24A(3) and (4) apply for the purposes of subsection (3B) as for those of section 24A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

(4) Regulations may make provision, for purposes of this section, as to circumstances in which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

- (a) it is reasonable for a person to hold the opinion mentioned in subsection 2(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
- (b) it is not reasonable for a person to hold that opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

(4A)[^c20448442] Regulations may make provision for the condition specified in subsection (3)(b) not to apply in prescribed circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]

(5) Regulations may make provision, for purposes of this section, as to circumstances (other than those mentioned in subsection (3)) in which treatment is to be taken to be justified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787281]
