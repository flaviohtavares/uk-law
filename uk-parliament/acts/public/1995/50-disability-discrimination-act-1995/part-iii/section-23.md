# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 23

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Exemption for small dwellings.[^c20492051]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22787261] Where the conditions mentioned in subsection (2) are satisfied, subsection (1), (3) or (as the case may be) (4) of section 22 does not apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]

(2) The conditions are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]

- (a) the relevant occupier resides, and intends to continue to reside, on the premises; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]
- (b) the relevant occupier shares accommodation on the premises with persons who reside on the premises and are not members of his household; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]
- (c) the shared accommodation is not storage accommodation or a means of access; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]
- (d) the premises are small premises. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]

(3) For the purposes of this section, premises are “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]**small premises *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]**” if they fall within subsection (4) or (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]

(4) Premises fall within this subsection if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]

- (a) only the relevant occupier and members of his household reside in the accommodation occupied by him; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]
- (b) the premises comprise, in addition to the accommodation occupied by the relevant occupier, residential accommodation for at least one other household; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]
- (c) the residential accommodation for each other household is let, or available for letting, on a separate tenancy or similar agreement; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]
- (d) there are not normally more than two such other households. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]

(5) Premises fall within this subsection if there is not normally residential accommodation on the premises for more than six persons in addition to the relevant occupier and any members of his household. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]

(6) For the purposes of this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]**the relevant occupier *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]

- (a) in a case falling within section 22(1), the person with power to dispose of the premises, or a near relative of his; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20492022][^c22787261] in a case falling within section 22(3), the person managing the premises, or a near relative of his; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]
- (b) in a case falling within section 22(4), the person whose licence or consent is required for the disposal of the premises, or a near relative of his. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]

(7) For the purposes of this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]

- “**near relative *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]**” means a person’s spouse *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261] or civil partner[^c20506341] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261], partner, parent, child, grandparent, grandchild, or brother or sister (whether of full or half blood or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261] by marriage or civil partnership)[^c20506361] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]
- “[^c20506381]**partner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]**” means the other member of a couple consisting of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]
  - (a) a man and a woman who are not married to each other but are living together as husband and wife, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]
  - (b) two people of the same sex who are not civil partners of each other but are living together as if they were civil partners. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22787261]
