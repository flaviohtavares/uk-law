# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 21G

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21G. Meaning of “discrimination”[^c22788221][^c20489891]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22788191] For the purposes of section 21F, an association discriminates against a disabled person if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

- (a) for a reason which relates to the disabled person’s disability, the association treats him less favourably than it treats or would treat others to whom that reason does not or would not apply; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
- (b) it cannot show that the treatment in question is justified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), treatment is justified only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

- (a) in the opinion of the association, one or more of the conditions mentioned in subsection (3) are satisfied; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
- (b) it is reasonable, in all the circumstances, for it to hold that opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

(3) The conditions are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

- (a) the treatment is necessary in order not to endanger the health or safety of any person (which may include that of the disabled person); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
- (b)[^c20499611] the disabled person is incapable of entering into an enforceable agreement, or giving an informed consent, and for that reason the treatment is reasonable in that case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
- (c) in a case falling within section 21F(2)(a), (3)(a), (c)(ii), (d)(ii) or (e), (4)(a) or (c) or (5)(a), the treatment is necessary in order for the association to be able to afford members, associates or guests of the association, or the disabled person, access to a benefit, facility or service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
- (d) in a case falling within section 21F(2)(b), (3)(b), (c)(i) or (d)(i), (4)(b) or (5)(b) or (c), the treatment is necessary because the association would otherwise be unable to afford members, associates or guests of the association access to a benefit, facility or service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
- (e) in a case falling within section 21F(2)(a), the difference between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
  - (i) the terms on which membership is offered to the disabled person, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22788191] those on which it is offered to other persons, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

  reflects the greater cost to the association of affording the disabled person access to a benefit, facility or service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
- (f) in a case falling within section 21F(3)(a), (c)(ii) or (d)(ii) or (4)(a), the difference between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
  - (i) the association’s treatment of the disabled person, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22788191] its treatment of other members or (as the case may be) other associates or other guests of the association, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

  reflects the greater cost to the association of affording the disabled person access to a benefit, facility or service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
- (g) in a case falling within section 21F(5)(a), the difference between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
  - (i) the terms on which the disabled person is invited, or permitted to be invited, to be a guest of the association, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22788191] those on which other persons are invited, or permitted to be invited, to be guests of the association, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

  reflects the greater cost to the association of affording the disabled person access to a benefit, facility or service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

(4) Any increase in the cost of affording a disabled person access to a benefit, facility or service which results from compliance with a duty under section 21H shall be disregarded for the purposes of subsection (3)(e), (f) and (g). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

(5) Regulations may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

- (a) make provision, for purposes of this section, as to circumstances in which it is, or as to circumstances in which it is not, reasonable for an association to hold the opinion mentioned in subsection (2)(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
- (b) amend or omit a condition specified in subsection (3) or make provision for it not to apply in prescribed circumstances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
- (c) make provision as to circumstances (other than any for the time being mentioned in subsection (3)) in which treatment is to be taken to be justified for the purposes of subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

(6) For the purposes of section 21F, an association also discriminates against a disabled person if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

- (a) it fails to comply with a duty under section 21H imposed on it in relation to the disabled person; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
- (b) it cannot show that its failure to comply with that duty is justified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]

(7) Regulations may make provision as to circumstances in which failure to comply with a duty under section 21H is to be taken to be justified for the purposes of subsection (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22788191]
