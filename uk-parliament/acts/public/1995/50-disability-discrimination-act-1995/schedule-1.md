# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Provisions Supplementing Section 1[^c18603391][^c20474831]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 1(1).

### Impairment

**1.**[^c18603401] (1)[^c23376101] [^c20475051]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Regulations may make provision, for the purposes of this Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376101]

- (a) for conditions of a prescribed description to be treated as amounting to impairments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376101]
- (b) for conditions of a prescribed description to be treated as not amounting to impairments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376101]

(3) Regulations made under sub-paragraph (2) may make provision as to the meaning of “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376101]**condition *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376101]**” for the purposes of those regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23376101]

### Long-term effects

**2.**[^c22866131][^c18603411] (1)[^c22866111] The effect of an impairment is a long-term effect if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866111]

- (a) it has lasted at least 12 months; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866111]
- (b) the period for which it lasts is likely to be at least 12 months; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866111]
- (c) it is likely to last for the rest of the life of the person affected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866111]

(2) Where an impairment ceases to have a substantial adverse effect on a person’s ability to carry out normal day-to-day activities, it is to be treated as continuing to have that effect if that effect is likely to recur. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866111]

(3) For the purposes of sub-paragraph (2), the likelihood of an effect recurring shall be disregarded in prescribed circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866111]

(4) Regulations may prescribe circumstances in which, for the purposes of this Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866111]

- (a) an effect which would not otherwise be a long-term effect is to be treated as such an effect; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866111]
- (b) an effect which would otherwise be a long-term effect is to be treated as not being such an effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866111]

### Severe disfigurement

**3.**[^c22866071][^c18603421] (1)[^c22866041] An impairment which consists of a severe disfigurement is to be treated as having a substantial adverse effect on the ability of the person concerned to carry out normal day-to-day activities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866041]

(2) Regulations may provide that in prescribed circumstances a severe disfigurement is not to be treated as having that effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866041]

(3) Regulations under sub-paragraph (2) may, in particular, make provision with respect to deliberately acquired disfigurements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866041]

### Normal day-to-day activities

**4.**[^c18603431][^c22866031] (1)[^c22866011] An impairment is to be taken to affect the ability of the person concerned to carry out normal day-to-day activities only if it affects one of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]

- (a) mobility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]
- (b) manual dexterity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]
- (c) physical co-ordination; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]
- (d) continence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]
- (e) ability to lift, carry or otherwise move everyday objects; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]
- (f) speech, hearing or eyesight; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]
- (g) memory or ability to concentrate, learn or understand; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]
- (h) perception of the risk of physical danger. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]
- (i)[^c23366571] taking part in normal social interaction; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]
- (j) forming social relationships *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]

(2) Regulations may prescribe— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]

- (a) circumstances in which an impairment which does not have an effect falling within sub-paragraph (1) is to be taken to affect the ability of the person concerned to carry out normal day-to-day activities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]
- (b) circumstances in which an impairment which has an effect falling within sub-paragraph (1) is to be taken not to affect the ability of the person concerned to carry out normal day-to-day activities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866011]

### Substantial adverse effects

**5.**[^c22866161][^c18603441] Regulations may make provision for the purposes of this Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866141]

- (a) for an effect of a prescribed kind on the ability of a person to carry out normal day-to-day activities to be treated as a substantial adverse effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866141]
- (b) for an effect of a prescribed kind on the ability of a person to carry out normal day-to-day activities to be treated as not being a substantial adverse effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866141]

### Effect of medical treatment

**6.**[^c22866191][^c18603451] (1)[^c22866171] An impairment which would be likely to have a substantial adverse effect on the ability of the person concerned to carry out normal day-to-day activities, but for the fact that measures are being taken to treat or correct it, is to be treated as having that effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866171]

(2) In sub-paragraph (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866171]**measures *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866171]**” includes, in particular, medical treatment and the use of a prosthesis or other aid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866171]

(3) Sub-paragraph (1) does not apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866171]

- (a) in relation to the impairment of a person’s sight, to the extent that the impairment is, in his case, correctable by spectacles or contact lenses or in such other ways as may be prescribed; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866171]
- (b) in relation to such other impairments as may be prescribed, in such circumstances as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866171]

### Persons deemed to be disabled

**6A.**[^c22866221][^c20474861] (1)[^c22866201] Subject to sub-paragraph (2), a person who has cancer, HIV infection or multiple sclerosis is to be deemed to have a disability, and hence to be a disabled person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866201]

(2) Regulations may provide for sub-paragraph (1) not to apply in the case of a person who has cancer if he has cancer of a prescribed description. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866201]

(3) A description of cancer prescribed under sub-paragraph (2) may (in particular) be framed by reference to consequences for a person of his having it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866201]

**7.**[^c22866251] (1)[^c22866231] Sub-paragraph (2) applies to any person whose name is, both on 12th January 1995 and on the date when this paragraph comes into force, in the register of disabled persons maintained under section 6 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231] [^c18603461]Disabled Persons (Employment) Act 1944. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]

(2) That person is to be deemed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]

- (a) during the initial period, to have a disability, and hence to be a disabled person; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]
- (b) afterwards, to have had a disability and hence to have been a disabled person during that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]

(3) A certificate of registration shall be conclusive evidence, in relation to the person with respect to whom it was issued, of the matters certified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]

(4) Unless the contrary is shown, any document purporting to be a certificate of registration shall be taken to be such a certificate and to have been validly issued. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]

(5) Regulations may provide for prescribed descriptions of person to be deemed to have disabilities, and hence to be disabled persons, for the purposes of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]

(5A)[^c20474911] The generality of sub-paragraph (5) shall not be taken to be prejudiced by the other provisions of this Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]

(6) Regulations may prescribe circumstances in which a person who has been deemed to be a disabled person by the provisions of sub-paragraph (1) or regulations made under sub-paragraph (5) is to be treated as no longer being deemed to be such a person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]

(7) In this paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]

- “**certificate of registration *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]**” means a certificate issued under regulations made under section 6 of the Act of 1944; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]
- “**initial period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]**” means the period of three years beginning with the date on which this paragraph comes into force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866231]

### Progressive conditions

**8.**[^c22866281][^c18603471] (1)[^c22866261] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866261]

- (a) a person has a progressive condition (such as cancer, multiple sclerosis or muscular dystrophy or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866261] HIV infection[^c20475271] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866261]),
- (b) as a result of that condition, he has an impairment which has (or had) an effect on his ability to carry out normal day-to-day activities, but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866261]
- (c) that effect is not (or was not) a substantial adverse effect, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866261]

he shall be taken to have an impairment which has such a substantial adverse effect if the condition is likely to result in his having such an impairment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866261]

(2) Regulations may make provision, for the purposes of this paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866261]

- (a) for conditions of a prescribed description to be treated as being progressive; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866261]
- (b) for conditions of a prescribed description to be treated as not being progressive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866261]

### Interpretation[^c20475241]

**9.**[^c22866311] In this Schedule, “HIV infection” means infection by a virus capable of causing the Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22866291]
