# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 58

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Liability of employers and principals.[^c22736851][^c22812081]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22812041] Anything done by a person in the course of his employment shall be treated for the purposes of this Act as also done by his employer, whether or not it was done with the employer’s knowledge or approval. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812041]

(2) Anything done by a person as agent for another person with the authority of that other person shall be treated for the purposes of this Act as also done by that other person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812041]

(3) Subsection (2) applies whether the authority was— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812041]

- (a) express or implied; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812041]
- (b) given before or after the act in question was done. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812041]

(4) Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply in relation to an offence under section 57(4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812041]

(5) In proceedings under this Act against any person in respect of an act alleged to have been done by an employee of his, it shall be a defence for that person to prove that he took such steps as were reasonably practicable to prevent the employee from— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812041]

- (a) doing that act; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812041]
- (b) doing, in the course of his employment, acts of that description. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812041]
