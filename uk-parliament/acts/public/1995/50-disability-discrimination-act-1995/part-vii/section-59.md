# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 59

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Statutory authority and national security etc.[^c18602151][^c22812111]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22812091] Nothing in this Act makes unlawful any act done— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]

- (a) in pursuance of any enactment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]
- (b)[^c20381601] in pursuance of any instrument made under any enactment by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]
  - (i) a Minister of the Crown, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22812091] a member of the Scottish Executive, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091] [^c20381731]. . .
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22812091] the National Assembly for Wales *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091] constituted by the Government of Wales Act 1998, or[^c20381781] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22812091] the Welsh Ministers, the First Minister for Wales or the Counsel General to the Welsh Assembly Government. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]

  or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]
- (c) to comply with any condition or requirement— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]
  - (i) imposed by a Minister of the Crown (whether before or after the passing of this Act) by virtue of any enactment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22812091] imposed by a member of the Scottish Executive (whether before or after the coming into force of this sub-paragraph) by virtue of any enactment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091] [^c20381801]. . .
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22812091] imposed by the National Assembly for Wales *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091] constituted by the Government of Wales Act 1998[^c20381821] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091](whether before or after the coming into force of this sub-paragraph) by virtue of any enactment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091] or[^c20381841] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22812091] imposed by the Welsh Ministers, the First Minister for Wales or the Counsel General to the Welsh Assembly Government. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]

(2) In subsection (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]**enactment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]**” includes one passed or made after the date on which this Act is passed and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]**instrument *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]**” includes one made after that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]

(2A)[^c21003281] Nothing in – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]

- (a) Part II, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]
- (b) Part III to the extent that it relates to the provision of employment services, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]

makes unlawful any act done for the purpose of safeguarding national security if the doing of the act was justified by that purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]

(2A)[^c20381521] Nothing in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]

- (a) Part 2 of this Act, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]
- (b) Part 3 of this Act to the extent that it relates to the provision of employment services, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]

makes unlawful any act done for the purpose of safeguarding national security if the doing of the act was justified by that purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]

(3) Nothing in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091] any other provision of[^c20381561] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091] this Act makes unlawful any act done for the purpose of safeguarding national security. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812091]
