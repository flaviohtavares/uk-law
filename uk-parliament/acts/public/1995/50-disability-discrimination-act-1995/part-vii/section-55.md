# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 55

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Victimisation.[^c22811971][^c22811961]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)F[^c22811941] or the purposes of Part II or Part III *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941] (other than sections 24A to 24L)[^c20381211] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941], a person (“A”) discriminates against another person (“B”) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]

- (a) he treats B less favourably than he treats or would treat other persons whose circumstances are the same as B’s; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]
- (b) he does so for a reason mentioned in subsection (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]

(2) The reasons are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]

- (a) B has— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]
  - (i) brought proceedings against A or any other person under this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941] or Part III of the 2005 Order[^c20380591] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941] ; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22811941] given evidence or information in connection with such proceedings brought by any person; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22811941] otherwise done anything under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941] , or by reference to,[^c20381231] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941] this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941] or Part III of the 2005 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941] in relation to A or any other person; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22811941] alleged that A or any other person has (whether or not the allegation so states) contravened this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941] or Part III of the 2005 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941] ; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]
- (b) A believes or suspects that B has done or intends to do any of those things. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]

(3) Where B is a disabled person, or a person who has had a disability, the disability in question shall be disregarded in comparing his circumstances with those of any other person for the purposes of subsection (1)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]

(3A)[^c20380631] For the purposes of Chapter I of Part III of the 2005 Order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]

- (a) references in subsection (2) to B include references to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]
  - (i) a person who is, for the purposes of that Chapter, B's parent; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22811941] a sibling of B; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]
- (b) references in that subsection to this Act or Part III of the 2005 Order are, as respects a person mentioned in sub-paragraph (i) or (ii) of paragraph (a), to be read as references to that Chapter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]

(4) Subsection (1) does not apply to treatment of a person because of an allegation made by him if the allegation was false and not made in good faith. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]

(5)[^c20381201] In the case of an act which constitutes discrimination by virtue of this section, sections 4, 4B, 4D, 4G, 6A, 7A, 13, 14A, 14C *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941] , 15B[^c20381251] and 16A also apply to discrimination against a person who is not disabled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811941]
