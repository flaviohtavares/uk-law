# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 57

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Aiding unlawful acts.[^c22812031]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22812011] A person who knowingly aids another person to do an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812011] unlawful act[^c18602121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812011]act made unlawful by this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812011] is to be treated for the purposes of this Act as himself doing the same kind of unlawful act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812011]

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), an employee or agent for whose act the employer or principal is liable under section 58 (or would be so liable but for section 58(5)) shall be taken to have aided the employer or principal to do the act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812011]

(3) For the purposes of this section, a person does not knowingly aid another to do an unlawful act if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812011]

- (a) he acts in reliance on a statement made to him by that other person that, because of any provision of this Act, the act would not be unlawful; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812011]
- (b) it is reasonable for him to rely on the statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812011]

(4) A person who knowingly or recklessly makes such a statement which is false or misleading in a material respect is guilty of an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812011]

(5) Any person guilty of an offence under subsection (4) shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812011]

(6)[^c18602131] “**Unlawful act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812011]**” means an act made unlawful by any provision of this Act other than a provision contained in Chapter 1 of Part 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812011]
