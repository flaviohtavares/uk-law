# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 59A

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59A. National security[^c22812181][^c20507471]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22812161] Rules of court may make provision for enabling a county court or sheriff court in which a claim is brought in respect of alleged discrimination contrary to this Act (including anything treated by virtue of this Act as amounting to discrimination contrary to this Act), where the court considers it expedient in the interests of national security— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]

- (a) to exclude from all or part of the proceedings— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]
  - (i) the claimant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22812161] the claimant's representatives; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22812161] any assessors; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]
- (b) to permit a claimant or representative who has been excluded to make a statement to the court before the commencement of the proceedings, or the part of the proceedings, from which he is excluded; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]
- (c) to take steps to keep secret all or part of the reasons for the court's decision in the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]

(2) The Attorney General or, in Scotland, the Advocate General for Scotland, may appoint a person to represent the interests of a claimant in, or in any part of, proceedings from which the claimant or his representatives are excluded by virtue of subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]

(3) A person may be appointed under subsection (2) only— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]

- (a) in relation to proceedings in England and Wales, if he has a general qualification (within the meaning of section 71 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (c. 41)), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]
- (b) in relation to proceedings in Scotland, if he is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]
  - (i) an advocate, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22812161] qualified to practice as a solicitor in Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]

(4) A person appointed under subsection (2) shall not be responsible to the person whose interests he is appointed to represent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22812161]
