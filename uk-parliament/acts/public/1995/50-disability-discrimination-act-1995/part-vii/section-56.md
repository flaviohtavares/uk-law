# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 56

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Help for aggrieved persons in obtaining information etc.[^c22812001][^c18602091][^c20381351]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22811981] For the purposes of this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

- (a) a person who considers that he may have been— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]
  - (i) discriminated against in contravention of Part II or III, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22811981] subjected to harassment in contravention of Part II or section 21A(2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

  is referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]**the person aggrieved *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]**”; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]
- (b) a person against whom the person aggrieved may decide to institute, or has instituted, proceedings in respect of such discrimination or harassment is referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]**the respondent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]**”.

(2) With a view to helping the person aggrieved decide whether to institute proceedings and, if he does so, to formulate and present his case in the most effective manner, the Office shall by order prescribe— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

- (a) forms by which the person aggrieved may question the respondent on his reasons for doing any relevant act, or on any other matter which is or may be relevant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]
- (b) forms by which the respondent may if he so wishes reply to any questions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

(3) Where the person aggrieved questions the respondent in accordance with forms prescribed by an order under subsection (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

- (a) the question, and any reply by the respondent (whether in accordance with such an order or not), shall be admissible as evidence in any proceedings under Part II or III; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]
- (b) if it appears to the court or tribunal in any such proceedings— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]
  - (i) that the respondent deliberately, and without reasonable excuse, omitted to reply within the period of eight weeks beginning with the day on which the question was served on him, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c22811981] that the respondent's reply is evasive or equivocal, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

  it may draw any inference which it considers it just and equitable to draw, including an inference that the respondent committed an unlawful act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

(4) The Office may by order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

- (a) prescribe the period within which questions must be duly served in order to be admissible under subsection (3)(a); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]
- (b) prescribe the manner in which a question, and any reply by the respondent, may be duly served. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

(5) County court rules may enable a court entertaining a claim under section 25 to determine, before the date fixed for the hearing of the claim, whether a question or reply is admissible under this section or not. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

(6) In proceedings in respect of a section 21B claim, subsection (3)(b) does not apply in relation to a failure to reply, or a particular reply, if the following conditions are met— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

- (a) that at the time of doing any relevant act, the respondent was carrying out public investigator functions or was a public prosecutor; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]
- (b) that the respondent reasonably believes that a reply or (as the case may be) a different reply would be likely to prejudice any criminal investigation, any decision to institute criminal proceedings or any criminal proceedings or would reveal the reasons behind a decision not to institute, or a decision not to continue, criminal proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

(7) The Office may by regulations provide for this section not to have effect, or to have effect with prescribed modifications, in relation to section 21B claims of a prescribed description. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

(8) This section is without prejudice to any other enactment or rule of law regulating interlocutory and preliminary matters in proceedings before a county court or industrial tribunal, and has effect subject to any enactment or rule of law regulating the admissibility of evidence in such proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]

(9) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]**section 21B claim *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]**” means a claim under section 25 by virtue of section 21B. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22811981]
