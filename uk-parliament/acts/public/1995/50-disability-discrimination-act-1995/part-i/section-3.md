# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 3

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Guidance.[^c18597241][^c22783541]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^c20396441][^c22783521] The Office may issue guidance about matters to be taken into account in determining whether a person is a disabled person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

(1) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (A1), the Office may, in particular,[^c20396461] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521] issue guidance about the matters to be taken into account in determining— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

- (a) whether an impairment has a substantial adverse effect on a person’s ability to carry out normal day-to-day activities; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]
- (b) whether such an impairment has a long-term effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (A1), guidance about the matters mentioned in subsection (1)[^c20396481] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521] may, among other things, give examples of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

- (a) effects which it would be reasonable, in relation to particular activities, to regard for purposes of this Act as substantial adverse effects; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]
- (b) effects which it would not be reasonable, in relation to particular activities, to regard for such purposes as substantial adverse effects; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]
- (c) substantial adverse effects which it would be reasonable to regard, for such purposes, as long-term; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]
- (d) substantial adverse effects which it would not be reasonable to regard, for such purposes, as long-term. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

(3) A tribunal or court determining, for any purpose of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521] or Part III of the 2005 Order[^c20396401] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521] , whether *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521] a person is a disabled person[^c20396501] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521], shall take into account any guidance which appears to it to be relevant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

(4)[^c22151751] In preparing a draft of any guidance, the Department shall consult such persons as it considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

(5) Where the Department proposes to issue any guidance, the Department shall publish a draft of it, consider any representations that are made to the Department about the draft and, if the Department thinks it appropriate, modify its proposals in the light of any of those representations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

(6) If the Department decides to proceed with any proposed guidance, the Department shall lay a draft of it before the Assembly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

(7) If, within the statutory period, the Assembly resolves not to approve the draft, the Department shall take no further steps in relation to the proposed guidance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

(8) If no such resolution is made within the statutory period, the Department shall issue the guidance in the form of its draft. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

(9) The guidance shall come into force on such date as the Department may by order appoint. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

(10) Subsection (7) does not prevent a new draft of the proposed guidance being laid before the Assembly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

(11) The Department may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

- (a) from time to time revise the whole or any part of any guidance and re-issue it ; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]
- (b) by order revoke any guidance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

(12) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]

- “**the Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]**” means the Department of Economic Development; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]
- “**guidance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]**” means guidance issued by the Department under this section and includes guidance which has been revised and re-issued; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]
- “**statutory period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]**” has the meaning assigned to it by section 41(2) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783521]
