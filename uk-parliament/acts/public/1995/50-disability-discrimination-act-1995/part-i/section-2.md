# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 2

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Past disabilities.[^c18597181][^c22783511]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22783491] The provisions of this Part and Parts II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491] , III and VA[^c20385201] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491]and Part III of the 2005 Order[^c20384871] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491] apply in relation to a person who has had a disability as they apply in relation to a person who has that disability. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491]

(2) Those provisions are subject to the modifications made by Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491]

(3) Any regulations or order made under this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491] or Part III of the 2005 Order[^c20384891] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491] may include provision with respect to persons who have had a disability. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491]

(4) In any proceedings under Part II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491] , III or VA[^c20385221] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491] of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491] or Part III of the 2005 Order[^c20384911] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491], the question whether a person had a disability at a particular time (“the relevant time”) shall be determined, for the purposes of this section, as if the provisions of, or made under, this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491] or Part III of the 2005 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491] in force when the act complained of was done had been in force at the relevant time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491]

(5) The relevant time may be a time before the passing of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491] or (as the case may be) the making of the 2005 Order[^c20384941] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22783491].
