# Disability Discrimination Act 1995, section 1

> Text as at 2020-01-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Meaning of “disability” and “disabled person”.[^c18597171][^c20506211]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c22778241] Subject to the provisions of Schedule 1, a person has a disability for the purposes of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22778241] and Part III of the 2005 Order[^c20506311] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22778241] if he has a physical or mental impairment which has a substantial and long-term adverse effect on his ability to carry out normal day-to-day activities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22778241]

(2) In this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22778241] and Part III of the 2005 Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22778241]“**disabled person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22778241]**” means a person who has a disability. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c22778241]
