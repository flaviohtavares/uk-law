# Pensions Act 1995, section 116

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (65).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Breach of regulations.[^c-5802e5a4da557fa204b543eba06802d5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-92f45f3b67df2197252e0f87a4974bbf] Regulations made by virtue of any provision of this Part may provide for the contravention of any provision contained in any such regulations to be an offence under this Part and for the recovery on summary conviction for any such offence of a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.

(2)[^c-0f3682be43fb9e565dad85237afa291a] An offence under any provision of the regulations may be charged by reference to any day or longer period of time; and a person may be convicted of a second or subsequent offence under such a provision by reference to any period of time following the preceding conviction of the offence.

(3)[^c-0f3682be43fb9e565dad85237afa291a] Where by reason of the contravention of any provision contained in regulations made by virtue of this Part—

- (a) a person is convicted of an offence under this Part, or
- (b) a person pays a penalty under section 10 or under provision contained in regulations made by virtue of section 41C[^c-e4bfdf207510b7d8ca1395375bb967bd],

then, in respect of that contravention, he shall not, in a case within paragraph (a), be liable to pay such a penalty or, in a case within paragraph (b), be convicted of such an offence.
