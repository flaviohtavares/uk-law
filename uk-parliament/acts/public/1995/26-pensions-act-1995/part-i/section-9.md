# Pensions Act 1995, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (65).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9. Removal and appointment of trustees: property.[^M_C_108cdf78-1cba-42cf-fb8f-6f1b8a4f3942][^c-165cb123ee03a7669bfc81595e1811c6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the Authority have power under this Part to appoint or remove a trustee or a trustee is removed under section 3A[^c-5958df3b4258c27ceddf888234848daa], they may exercise by order[^c-1864f7b6236e1cd4514b5f14c0fb954f] the same jurisdiction and powers as are exercisable by the High Court or, in relation to a trust scheme subject to the law of Scotland, the Court of Session for vesting any property in, or transferring any property to, trustees in consequence of the appointment or of the removal.
