# Pensions Act 1995, section 51ZA

## 51ZA. Meaning of “the appropriate percentage”[^key-f191ea979d8c2be8dcdfd8160e0820e3][^key-f191ea979d8c2be8dcdfd8160e0820e3][^key-0bed85530da9fc4a86235f0830a5dc3c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purposes of section 51(1)(c) and (2), “**the appropriate percentage**” in relation to an increase in the whole or part of the annual rate of a pension—

- (a) in the case of a category X pension, means the higher revaluation percentage[^key-e54145f03dd80eac3f57a7dc122bcd6b] for the latest revaluation period specified in the order under paragraph 2 of Schedule 3 to the Pension Schemes Act 1993 (revaluation of accrued pension benefits) which is in force at the time of the increase, and
- (b)[^key-d119532b92170bff1103d6b27fcb60d4] in the case of a category Y pension, means the lower revaluation percentage for that period.

(2) In this section “the higher revaluation percentage”, “the lower revaluation percentage”[^key-34aa357f4c89189015595a6023c31c87] and “**the revaluation period**” have the same meaning as in paragraph 2 of Schedule 3 to the Pension Schemes Act 1993.
