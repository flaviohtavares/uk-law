> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 126. Equalisation of pensionable age and of entitlement to certain benefits and increase in pensionable age.[^c-c797847cafd49d3b246bfe77bc54b18d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 4 to this Act, of which—

- (a) Part I has effect to equalise pensionable age for men and women and then to increase it[^c-e4d4a1e5ea322bc15cd690581f1d36c7],
- (b) Part II makes provision for bringing equality for men and women to certain pension and other benefits, and
- (c) Part III makes consequential amendments of enactments,

shall have effect.
