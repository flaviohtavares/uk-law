> **42A. Reduced rates of Class 1 contributions, and rebates. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]**
>
> (1) Subsections (2) and (3) apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> - (a) the earnings paid to or for the benefit of an earner in any tax week are in respect of an employment which is contracted-out employment at the time of the payment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
> - (b) the earner’s service in the employment is service which qualifies him for a pension provided by a money purchase contracted-out scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> (2) The amount of a Class 1 contribution in respect of so much of the earnings paid in that week in respect of that employment as exceeds the current lower earnings limit but not the current upper earnings limit for that week (or the prescribed equivalents if he is paid otherwise than weekly) shall be reduced by an amount equal to the appropriate flat-rate percentage of that part of those earnings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> (3) The Secretary of State shall except in prescribed circumstances or in respect of prescribed periods pay in respect of that earner and that tax week to the trustees or managers of the scheme or, in prescribed circumstances, to a prescribed person the amount by which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> - (a) the appropriate age-related percentage of that part of those earnings, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> exceeds *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> - (b) the appropriate flat-rate percentage of that part of those earnings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> (4) Regulations may make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> - (a) as to the manner in which and time at which or period within which payments under subsection (3) are to be made, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
> - (b) for the adjustment of the amount which would otherwise be payable under that subsection so as to avoid the payment of trivial or fractional amounts, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
> - (c) for earnings to be calculated or estimated in such manner and on such basis as may be prescribed for the purpose of determining whether any, and if so what, payments under subsection (3) are to be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> (5) If the Secretary of State pays an amount under subsection (3) which he is not required to pay or is not required to pay to the person to whom, or in respect of whom, he pays it, he may recover it from any person to whom, or in respect of whom, he paid it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> (6) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> - (a) an earner has ceased to be employed in an employment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
> - (b) earnings are paid to him or for his benefit within the period of six weeks, or such other period as may be prescribed, from the day on which he so ceased, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> that employment shall be treated for the purposes of this section as contracted-out employment at the time when the earnings are paid if it was contracted-out employment in relation to the earner when he was last employed in it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> (7) Subsection (3) of section 41 applies for the purposes of this section as it applies for the purposes of that. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> **42B. Determination and alteration of rates of contributions, and rebates, applicable under section 42A. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]**
>
> (1) The Secretary of State shall at intervals of not more than five years lay before each House of Parliament— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> - (a) a report by the Government Actuary or the Deputy Government Actuary on the percentages which, in his opinion, are required to be specified in an order under this section so as to reflect the cost of providing benefits of an actuarial value equivalent to that of the benefits which, under section 48A, are foregone by or in respect of members of money purchase contracted-out schemes, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
> - (b) a report by the Secretary of State stating what, in view of the report under paragraph (a), he considers those percentages should be, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
> - (c) a draft of an order under subsection (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> (2) An order under this subsection shall have effect in relation to a period of tax years (not exceeding five) and may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> - (a) specify different percentages for primary and secondary Class 1 contributions, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
> - (b) for each of the tax years for which it has effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>   - (i) specify a percentage in respect of all earners which is “the appropriate flat-rate percentage” for the purposes of section 42A, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a] specify different percentages (not being less than the percentage specified by virtue of sub-paragraph (i)) in respect of earners by reference to their ages on the last day of the preceding year (the percentage for each group of earners being “the appropriate age-related percentage” in respect of earners in that group for the purposes of section 42A). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> (3) If the draft of an order under subsection (2) is approved by resolution of each House of Parliament, the Secretary of State shall make the order in the form of the draft. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> (4) An order under subsection (2) shall have effect from the beginning of such tax year as may be specified in the order, not being a tax year earlier than the second after that in which the order is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
>
> (5) Subsection (2) is without prejudice to the generality of section 182 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bd80453218633947005cb483f3d834a]
