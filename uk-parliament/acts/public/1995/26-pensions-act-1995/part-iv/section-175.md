# Pensions Act 1995, section 175

## 175. Parliamentary control of orders and regulations.[^c-0dcf50ddc3f8576a1505cbcc38dc0a0f][^c-1271b72ce534b8ff5c2f9e5de8f828db]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to subsections (2) to (3)[^c-a81cc341019ceb64beb9cd5ad9e381a7], a statutory instrument which contains any regulations or order made under this Act shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(2) A statutory instrument which contains any regulations made by virtue of—

- (a) section 64(4),
- (b) section 78(6),
- (c) section 116(1), [^c-4fe492139f773468a583f3eeaf215792]...
- (d)[^c-4fe492139f773468a583f3eeaf215792] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

or order under section 10(2) must not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

(2A)[^c-565648fc8a4645ab57f9417204a01c2a] A statutory instrument which contains the first regulations made by virtue of section 37(2A),[^c-754a53a0af481f34dcbada93b69ccd9b] 41A or 41C must not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

(2B)[^c-21a960efd0dfde22613ae8c529d278ba] Any provision that may be made by regulations or an order under this Act subject to the procedure described in subsection (1) may instead be made by regulations subject to the procedure described in subsection (2).

(3) Subsection (1) does not apply to an order under section 180.
