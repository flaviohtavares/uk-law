# Pensions Act 1995, section 178

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (65).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 178. Extent.[^key-17bacf4d16c849b2f0986f7f9b098c14]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following provisions, this Act does not extend to Northern Ireland.

(2) Sections [^key-3b05a2169010ec3adf8b1c1c1c4c9e25]... 68(5), [^key-3b05a2169010ec3adf8b1c1c1c4c9e25]... [^key-f7f05b44088666feff25cd31130e4d29]...168, 170(4) to (7), 172 and 179 extend to Northern Ireland.

(3) The amendment by this Act of an enactment which extends to Northern Ireland extends also to Northern Ireland.
