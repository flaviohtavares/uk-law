# Pensions Act 1995, section 176

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 176. Interpretation.[^key-085064ba04d11c156981c103975c1a52]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Act—

- “**enactment**” includes an enactment comprised in subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the [^c2136395]Interpretation Act 1978),
- “**occupational pension scheme**” and “**personal pension scheme**” have the meaning given by section 1 of the [^c2136396]Pension Schemes Act 1993,

and the definition of “enactment” shall apply for the purposes of section 114 as if “**Act**” in section 21(1) of the Interpretation Act 1978 included any enactment.
