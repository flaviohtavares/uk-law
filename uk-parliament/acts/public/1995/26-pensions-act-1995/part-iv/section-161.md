## 161. Repeal of sections 136 to 143 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993.[^c-5801d50b67723a26578e0b34910655e0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Sections 136 to 141 (modification) and 142 and 143 (winding up) of the [^c2136367]Pension Schemes Act 1993 are repealed.
