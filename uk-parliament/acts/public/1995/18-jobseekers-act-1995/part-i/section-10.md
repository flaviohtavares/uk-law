## 10. Variation of jobseeker’s agreement.[^c11297141][^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851] A jobseeker’s agreement may be varied, in the prescribed manner, by agreement between the claimant and any employment officer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]

(2) Any agreement to vary a jobseeker’s agreement shall be in writing and be signed by both parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]

(2A)[^c21720881] Any agreement to vary a jobseeker's agreement may be in electronic form and signed by means of an electronic signature (within the meaning given in section 7(2) of the Electronic Communications Act 2000). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]

(3) A copy of the agreement, as varied, shall be given to the claimant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]

(4) An employment officer shall not agree to a variation of a jobseeker’s agreement, unless, in the officer’s opinion, the conditions mentioned in section 1(2)(a) and (c) would continue to be satisfied with respect to the claimant if he were to comply with, or be treated as complying with, the agreement as proposed to be varied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]

(5) The employment officer may, and if asked to do so by the claimant shall forthwith, refer a proposed variation of a jobseeker’s agreement to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851] the Secretary of State[^c11297151] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851] for him to determine— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]

- (a) whether, if the claimant concerned were to comply with the agreement as proposed to be varied, he would satisfy— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
  - (i) the condition mentioned in section 1(2)(a), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851] the condition mentioned in section 1(2)(c); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
- (b) whether it is reasonable to expect the claimant to have to comply with the agreement as proposed to be varied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]

(6) On a reference under subsection (5) the Secretary of State[^c11297161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]—

- (a) shall, so far as practicable, dispose of it in accordance with this section before the end of the period of 14 days from the date of the reference; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
- (b) shall give such directions as he considers appropriate as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
  - (i) whether the jobseeker’s agreement should be varied, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851] if so, the terms on which the claimant and the employment officer are to enter into an agreement to vary it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
- (c) may bring the jobseeker’s agreement to an end where the claimant fails, within a prescribed period, to comply with a direction given under paragraph (b)(ii); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
- (d) may direct that, if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
  - (i) the jobseeker’s agreement is varied, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851] such conditions as he considers appropriate are satisfied, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]

  the agreement as varied is to be treated as having effect on such date, before it would otherwise have effect, as may be specified in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]

(7) Regulations may provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]

- (a) for such matters as may be prescribed to be taken into account by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851] the Secretary of State[^c11297171] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]in giving a direction under subsection (6)(b) or (d); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
- (b) for such persons as may be prescribed to be notified of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
  - (i) any determination of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851] under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851] any direction given by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851] under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]

(8)[^c11297201] Any determination of an adjudication officer under this section shall be binding. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31a41273-e234-4532-f5a8-6339a738e851]
