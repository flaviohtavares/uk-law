# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 15A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15A. Trade disputes: joint-claim couples.[^c17276701]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] Sections 14 and 15 shall, in relation to a joint-claim couple claiming a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance, apply in accordance with this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(2) Where each member of the couple is prevented by section 14 from being entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance, the couple are not entitled to a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(3) But where only one member of the couple is prevented by that section from being entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance, the couple are not for that reason alone prevented from being entitled to a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(4) Section 15(1) does not have effect in relation to the couple but, except in prescribed circumstances, section 15(2) applies for the purposes of calculating the couple’s entitlement to a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

- (a) a member of the couple, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- (b) any other person who is a member of any family of which the couple are members, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

is, or would be, prevented by section 14 from being entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(5) Where section 15(2) applies in relation to the couple by virtue of subsection (4) above, that provision and section 15(4) apply with the following modifications— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

- (a) references to the claimant are to be taken as references to the couple; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- (b) references to “A" are to the person mentioned in subsection (4)(a) or (b) above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- (c) section 15(2)(b) has effect as if for “where the claimant and A are a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] couple[^c21696411] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]," there were substituted “where A is a member of the couple,"; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- (d) section 15(2)(c)(ii) has effect as if for “of his family" there were substituted “of any family of which the couple are members". *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
