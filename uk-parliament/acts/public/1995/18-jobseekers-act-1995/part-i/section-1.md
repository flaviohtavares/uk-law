# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. The jobseeker’s allowance.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An allowance, to be known as a jobseeker’s allowance, shall be payable in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

(2)[^c21696181][^c21696201][^c21696221][^c21696281][^c21710691] Subject to the provisions of this Act, a claimant is entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance if he—

- (a)[^c11296141][^c11296151][^c-51387f2cdf3c1e06b6c1968ab962a839][^c-cf78d465b49dfa96e8beec064433ee5c] is available for employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-622e65f77c3e0da066aefc7b3df2d5f1]
- (b)[^c11296151] has entered into a jobseeker’s agreement which remains in force; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4052b2d4e10adc927cbf2a513041f38]
- (c)[^c11296151][^c-51387f2cdf3c1e06b6c1968ab962a839][^c-cf78d465b49dfa96e8beec064433ee5c][^c-1b5c4dc8e45cf9c78717ff45a0974c32] is actively seeking employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6abff7e93ed8f2d7b02b07a274234a83]
- (d)[^c11296181] satisfies the conditions set out in section 2;
- (e) is not engaged in remunerative work;
- (f)[^c11296151][^c21710911] does not have limited capability for work;
- (g)[^c11296151] is not receiving relevant education;
- (h)[^c11296151] is under pensionable age; and
- (i)[^c11296151] is in Great Britain.

(2A)[^c11296241] Subject to the provisions of this Act, a claimant who is not a member of a joint-claim couple is entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance if he satisfies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-578bdacf9814c8433002f2a01a8eab28]

- (a) the conditions set out in paragraphs (a) to (c) and (e) to (i) of subsection (2); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-578bdacf9814c8433002f2a01a8eab28]
- (b) the conditions set out in section 3. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-578bdacf9814c8433002f2a01a8eab28]

(2B) Subject to the provisions of this Act, a joint-claim couple are entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-578bdacf9814c8433002f2a01a8eab28]

- (a) a claim for the allowance is made jointly by the couple; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-578bdacf9814c8433002f2a01a8eab28]
- (b) each member of the couple satisfies the conditions set out in paragraphs (a) to (c) and (e) to (i) of subsection (2); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-578bdacf9814c8433002f2a01a8eab28]
- (c) the conditions set out in section 3A are satisfied in relation to the couple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-578bdacf9814c8433002f2a01a8eab28]

(2C) Regulations may prescribe circumstances in which subsection (2A) is to apply to a claimant who is a member of a joint-claim couple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-578bdacf9814c8433002f2a01a8eab28]

(2D) Regulations may, in respect of cases where a person would (but for the regulations) be a member of two or more joint-claim couples, make provision for only one of those couples to be a joint-claim couple; and the provision which may be so made includes provision for the couple which is to be the joint-claim couple to be nominated— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-578bdacf9814c8433002f2a01a8eab28]

- (a) by the persons who are the members of the couples, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-578bdacf9814c8433002f2a01a8eab28]
- (b) in default of one of the couples being so nominated, by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-578bdacf9814c8433002f2a01a8eab28]

(3) A jobseeker’s allowance is payable in respect of a week.

(4) In this Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196]

- “**a contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196]**” means a jobseeker’s allowance entitlement to which is based on the claimant’s satisfying conditions which include those set out in section 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196] [^c11296251]...
- “**an income-based jobseeker’s allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196]**” means a jobseeker’s allowance entitlement to which is based on the claimant’s satisfying conditions which include those set out in section 3 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196] or a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance;[^c11296261] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196]
- “[^c11296271]**a joint-claim couple *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196]**” means a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196] couple[^c21696321] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196] who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196]
  - (a) are not members of any family whose members include a person in respect of whom a member of the couple is entitled to child benefit, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196]
  - (b) are of a prescribed description; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196]
- “**a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196]**” means a jobseeker’s allowance entitlement to which arises by virtue of subsection (2B). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-512efb84f6968fbf9c881a7ef5187196]
