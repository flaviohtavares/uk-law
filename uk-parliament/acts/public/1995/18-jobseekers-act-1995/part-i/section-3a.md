# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 3A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18

## 3A. The conditions for claims by joint-claim couples.[^c11296431]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] The conditions referred to in section 1(2B)(c) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]

- (a) that the income of the joint-claim couple does not exceed the applicable amount (determined in accordance with regulations under section 4) or the couple have no income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (b) that no member of a family of which the couple are members is entitled to income support; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (c) that no member of any such family (other than the couple) is entitled to an income-based jobseeker’s allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^c17542321][^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] that neither member of the couple is entitled to state pension credit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (cd (affected by repeal, see note))[^c21711081][^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] that neither member of the couple is entitled to an income-related employment and support allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (d) that at least one member of the couple has reached the age of 18; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (e) that if only one member of the couple has reached the age of 18, the other member of the couple is a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
  - (i) in respect of whom a direction under section 16 is in force; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c11296441][^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] who has, in prescribed circumstances to be taken into account for a prescribed period, reached the age of 16. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]

(2) Subsections (2) and (4) of section 3 shall apply in relation to a member of the couple to whom subsection (1)(e)(i) or (ii) above applies as they apply in relation to a claimant to whom subsection (1)(f)(ii) or (iii) of that section applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]

(3) In subsection (1)(e)(ii) above “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]**period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]**” shall be construed in accordance with section 3(3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
