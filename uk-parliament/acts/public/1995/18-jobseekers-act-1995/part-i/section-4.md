# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Amount payable by way of a jobseeker’s allowance.[^c11296541]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c11296551] In the case of a contribution-based *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65281001693139b2e2e042df7aec0ad6] jobseeker’s allowance, the amount payable in respect of a claimant (“**his personal rate**”) shall be calculated by—

- (a) determining the age-related amount applicable to him; and
- (b) making prescribed deductions in respect of earnings , pension payments,[^c21710561] PPF payments and FAS payments.

(2) The age-related amount applicable to a claimant, for the purposes of subsection (1)(a), shall be determined in accordance with regulations.

(3) In the case of an income-based jobseeker’s allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdc2ee00aa999674a395817ccd93e788] (other than a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance)[^c11296561] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdc2ee00aa999674a395817ccd93e788], the amount payable shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdc2ee00aa999674a395817ccd93e788]

- (a) if a claimant has no income, the applicable amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdc2ee00aa999674a395817ccd93e788]
- (b) if a claimant has an income, the amount by which the applicable amount exceeds his income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdc2ee00aa999674a395817ccd93e788]

(3A)[^c11296571] In the case of a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance, the amount payable in respect of a joint-claim couple shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdc2ee00aa999674a395817ccd93e788]

- (a) if the couple have no income, the applicable amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdc2ee00aa999674a395817ccd93e788]
- (b) if the couple have an income, the amount by which the applicable amount exceeds the couple’s income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdc2ee00aa999674a395817ccd93e788]

(4) Except in prescribed circumstances, a jobseeker’s allowance shall not be payable where the amount otherwise payable would be less than a prescribed minimum.

(5)[^c11296581] The applicable amount shall be such amount or the aggregate of such amounts as may be determined in accordance with regulations.

(6) Where a claimant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35] is entitled to both a contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance and an income-based jobseeker’s allowance[^c11296591] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35] but has no income, the amount payable *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35] by way of a jobseeker’s allowance[^c11296601] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35] shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

- (a) the applicable amount, if that is greater than his personal rate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]
- (b) his personal rate, if it is not. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

(7) Where the amount payable to a claimant to whom subsection (6) applies is the applicable amount, the amount payable to him by way of a jobseeker’s allowance shall be taken to consist of two elements— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

- (a) one being an amount equal to his personal rate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]
- (b) the other being an amount equal to the excess of the applicable amount over his personal rate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

(8)[^key-b16957877087e37a368f23ed7af9d9e6] Where a claimant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35] is entitled to both a contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance and an income-based jobseeker’s allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35], the amount payable *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35] by way of a jobseeker’s allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35] shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

- (a) the amount by which the applicable amount exceeds his income, if the amount of that excess is greater than his personal rate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]
- (b) his personal rate, if it is not. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

(9)[^key-b16957877087e37a368f23ed7af9d9e6] Where the amount payable to a claimant to whom subsection (8) applies is the amount by which the applicable amount exceeds his income, the amount payable to him by way of a jobseeker’s allowance shall be taken to consist of two elements— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

- (a) one being an amount equal to his personal rate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]
- (b) the other being an amount equal to the amount by which the difference between the applicable amount and his income exceeds his personal rate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

(10)[^key-b16957877087e37a368f23ed7af9d9e6] The element of a jobseeker’s allowance mentioned in subsection (7)(a) and that mentioned in subsection (9)(a) shall be treated, for the purpose of identifying the source of the allowance, as attributable to the claimant’s entitlement to a contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

(11)[^key-b16957877087e37a368f23ed7af9d9e6] The element of a jobseeker’s allowance mentioned in subsection (7)(b) and that mentioned in subsection (9)(b) shall be treated, for the purpose of identifying the source of the allowance, as attributable to the claimant’s entitlement to an income-based jobseeker’s allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

(11A)[^c11296631] In subsections (6) to (11) “claimant" does not include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

- (a) a joint-claim couple, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]
- (b) a member of such a couple (other than a person to whom regulations under section 1(2C) apply); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

but section 4A, which contains corresponding provisions relating to joint-claim couples, applies instead. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5be6ac3c3b5bf151dcbaa6511817b35]

(12) Regulations under subsection (5) may provide that, in prescribed cases, an applicable amount is to be nil.
