# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. The income-based conditions.[^c11296411]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] The conditions referred to in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] section 1(2A)(b)[^c11296421] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] are that the claimant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]

- (a) has an income which does not exceed the applicable amount (determined in accordance with regulations under section 4) or has no income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (b) is not entitled to income support *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91], state pension credit or an income-related employment and support allowance[^c21711041] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91];
- (c) is not a member of a family one of whose members is entitled to income support; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (d) is not a member of a family one of whose members is entitled to an income-based jobseeker’s allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (dd (affected by repeal, see note))[^c17542301][^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] is not a member of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] couple[^c21696341] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] the other member of which is entitled to state pension credit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (de (affected by repeal, see note))[^c21711061][^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] is not a member of a couple the other member of which is entitled to an income-related employment and support allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (e) is not a member of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] couple *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] the other member of which is engaged in remunerative work; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (f) is a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
  - (i) who has reached the age of 18; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] in respect of whom a direction under section 16 is in force; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91] who has, in prescribed circumstances to be taken into account for a prescribed period, reached the age of 16 but not the age of 18. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]

(2) Regulations may provide for one or both of the following conditions to be included in the income-based conditions, in the case of a person to whom subsection (1)(f)(ii) or (iii) applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]

- (a) a condition that the claimant must register for employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (b) a condition that the claimant must register for training. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]

(3) In subsection (1)(f)(iii) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]**period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]**” includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]

- (a) a period of a determinate length; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (b) a period defined by reference to the happening of a future event; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
- (c) a period of a determinate length but subject to earlier determination upon the happening of a future event. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]

(4) Regulations under subsection (2) may, in particular, make provision by reference to persons designated by the Secretary of State for the purpose of the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e5122e7a854ad0dc34f14fb88eeed91]
