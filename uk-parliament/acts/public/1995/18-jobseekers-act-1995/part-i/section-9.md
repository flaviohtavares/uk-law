# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18

## 9. The jobseeker’s agreement.[^c11297051][^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa] An agreement which is entered into by a claimant and an employment officer and which complies with the prescribed requirements in force at the time when the agreement is made is referred to in this Act as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]**a jobseeker’s agreement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]**”.

(2) A jobseeker’s agreement shall have effect only for the purposes of section 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

(3) A jobseeker’s agreement shall be in writing and be signed by both parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

(3A)[^c21720861] The agreement may be in electronic form and signed by means of an electronic signature (within the meaning given in section 7(2) of the Electronic Communications Act 2000). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

(4) A copy of the agreement shall be given to the claimant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

(5) An employment officer shall not enter into a jobseeker’s agreement with a claimant unless, in the officer’s opinion, the conditions mentioned in section 1(2)(a) and (c) would be satisfied with respect to the claimant if he were to comply with, or be treated as complying with, the proposed agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

(6) The employment officer may, and if asked to do so by the claimant shall forthwith, refer a proposed jobseeker’s agreement to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa] the Secretary of State[^c11297061] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa] for him to determine— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

- (a) whether, if the claimant concerned were to comply with the proposed agreement, he would satisfy— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]
  - (i) the condition mentioned in section 1(2)(a), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa] the condition mentioned in section 1(2)(c); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]
- (b) whether it is reasonable to expect the claimant to have to comply with the proposed agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

(7) On a reference under subsection (6) the Secretary of State[^c11297071] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]—

- (a) shall, so far as practicable, dispose of it in accordance with this section before the end of the period of 14 days from the date of the reference; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]
- (b) may give such directions, with respect to the terms on which the employment officer is to enter into a jobseeker’s agreement with the claimant, as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa] the Secretary of State[^c11297081] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa] considers appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]
- (c) may direct that, if such conditions as he considers appropriate are satisfied, the proposed jobseeker’s agreement is to be treated (if entered into) as having effect on such date, before it would otherwise have effect, as may be specified in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

(8) Regulations may provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

- (a) for such matters as may be prescribed to be taken into account by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa] the Secretary of State[^c11297091] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa] in giving a direction under subsection (7)(c); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]
- (b) for such persons as may be prescribed to be notified of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]
  - (i) any determination of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa] the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa] under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa] any direction given by an adjudication officer under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

(9)[^c11297111] Any determination of an adjudication officer under this section shall be binding. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

(10) Regulations may provide that, in prescribed circumstances, a claimant is to be treated as having satisfied the condition mentioned in section 1(2)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

(11) Regulations may provide that, in prescribed circumstances, a jobseeker’s agreement is to be treated as having effect on a date, to be determined in accordance with the regulations, before it would otherwise have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]

(12) Except in such circumstances as may be prescribed, a jobseeker’s agreement entered into by a claimant shall cease to have effect on the coming to an end of an award of a jobseeker’s allowance made to him *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa] or to a joint-claim couple of which he is a member.[^c11297121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa].

(13) In this section and section 10 “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]**employment officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]**” means an officer of the Secretary of State or such other person as may be designated for the purposes of this section by an order made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9e402a6a-a5f5-42e4-d62b-b6129c61b1aa]
