## 20. Exemptions from section 19 and 19A.[^c11297751][^c11297761][^c21712261][^c21792101]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Nothing in section 19, or in regulations under that section, shall be taken to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1] authorise reduction[^c21792121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1] of a jobseeker’s allowance merely because the claimant refuses to seek or accept employment in a situation which is vacant in consequence of a stoppage of work due to a trade dispute. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1]

(2) Section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1] 19A[^c21792161] does not apply, in the circumstances mentioned in subsection *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1] (2)(c) to (g)[^c21792181] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1] of that section, if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1]

- (a) a direction is in force under section 16 with respect to the claimant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1]
- (b) he has acted in such a way as to risk— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1]
  - (i) having that direction revoked under subsection (3)(b) of section 16; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1] having the amount of his jobseeker’s allowance reduced by virtue of section 17, because *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1] the condition mentioned in section 17(3)(b) or (c) is satisfied[^c11297771] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1].

(3) Regulations shall make provision for the purpose of enabling any person of a prescribed description to accept any employed earner’s employment without falling within section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1] 19(2)(b) or (d)[^c21792201] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1] should he leave that employment voluntarily and without *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1] good reason[^c21792221] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1] at any time during a trial period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fcd97f893d631cd143b64f26b0a79ee1]

(4) In such circumstances as may be prescribed, an income-based jobseeker’s allowance shall be *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21792241] payable in respect of[^c11297781] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21792241] a claimant even though section 19 prevents payment of a jobseeker’s allowance to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21792241]

(5) [^c21792241]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Regulations under subsection (4) may, in particular, provide for a jobseeker’s allowance payable by virtue of that subsection to be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21792241]

- (a) payable at a prescribed rate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21792241]
- (b) payable for a prescribed period (which may differ from the period fixed under section 19(2) or (3)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21792241]

(7) In subsection (3), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dab201a5ff6f64c4d643257e35633b10]**trial period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dab201a5ff6f64c4d643257e35633b10]**” has such meaning as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dab201a5ff6f64c4d643257e35633b10]

(8) Regulations may make provision for determining, for the purposes of this section, the day on which a person’s employment is to be regarded as commencing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dab201a5ff6f64c4d643257e35633b10]
