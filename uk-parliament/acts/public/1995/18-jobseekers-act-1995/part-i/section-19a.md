# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 19A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 19A. Other sanctions[^c21787101][^c21712261]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724] The amount of an award of a jobseeker's allowance is to be reduced in accordance with this section in the event of a failure by the claimant which is sanctionable under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(2) It is a failure sanctionable under this section if a claimant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) without a good reason fails to comply with regulations under section 8(1) or (1A); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) without a good reason fails to comply with regulations under section 17A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (c) without a good reason refuses or fails to carry out a jobseeker's direction which was reasonable having regard to his circumstances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (d) without a good reason neglects to avail himself of a reasonable opportunity of a place on a training scheme or employment programme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (e) without a good reason refuses or fails to apply for, or accept if offered, a place on such a scheme or programme which an employment officer has informed him is vacant or about to become vacant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (f) without a good reason gives up a place on such a scheme or programme or fails to attend such a scheme or programme having been given a place on it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (g) through misconduct loses a place on such a scheme or programme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(3) But a failure is not sanctionable under this section if it is also sanctionable under section 19. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(4) Regulations are to provide for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) the amount of a reduction under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) the period for which such a reduction has effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(5) Regulations under subsection (4)(b) may provide that a reduction under this section in relation to any failure is to have effect for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) a period continuing until the claimant meets a compliance condition specified by the Secretary of State, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) a fixed period not exceeding 26 weeks which is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
  - (i) specified in the regulations, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724] determined in any case by the Secretary of State, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (c) a combination of both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(6) In subsection (5)(a) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]**compliance condition *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) a condition that the failure ceases, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) a condition relating to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
  - (i) future compliance with a jobseeker's direction or any requirement imposed under section 8(1) or (1A) or 17A of this Act, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724] future avoidance of the failures referred to in subsection (2)(d) to (g). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(7) A compliance condition specified under subsection (5)(a) may be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) revoked or varied by the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) notified to the claimant in such manner as the Secretary of State may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(8) The period fixed under subsection (5)(b) may in particular depend on either or both of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) the number of failures by the claimant sanctionable under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) the period between such failures. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(9) Regulations may provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) for cases in which no reduction is to be made under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) for a reduction under this section made in relation to an award that is terminated to be applied to any new award made within a prescribed period of the termination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(10) During any period for which the amount of a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance is reduced under this section by virtue of a failure by one of the claimants which is sanctionable under this section, the allowance is payable to the other member of the couple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(11) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) “**jobseeker's direction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]**” means a direction given by an employment officer (in such manner as he thinks fit) with a view to achieving one or both of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
  - (i) assisting the claimant to find employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724] improving the claimant's prospects of being employed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) “training scheme“ and “employment programme” have such meaning as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
