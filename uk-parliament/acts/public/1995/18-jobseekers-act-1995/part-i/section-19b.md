# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 19B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19B. Claimants ceasing to be available for employment etc[^c21787101][^c21712261]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724] Regulations may make provision for reduction of the amount of an award of a jobseeker's allowance other than a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance if the claimant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) was previously entitled to such an allowance or was a member of a couple entitled to a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) ceased to be so entitled by failing to comply with the condition in section 1(2)(a) or (c) (availability for employment and actively seeking employment). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(2) Regulations may make provision for reduction of the amount of a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance if one of the claimants— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) was previously entitled to a jobseeker's allowance other than a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) ceased to be so entitled by failing to comply with the condition in section 1(2)(a) or (c). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(3) Regulations may make provision for reduction of the amount of an award of joint-claim jobseeker's allowance if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) the couple were previously entitled to a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance but ceased to be so entitled by either or both of them failing to comply with the condition in section 1(2)(a) or (c), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) either member of the couple was a member of another couple previously entitled to such an allowance and that couple ceased to be so entitled by that person failing to comply with the condition in section 1(2)(a) or (c). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(4) Regulations are to provide for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) the amount of a reduction under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) the period for which such a reduction has effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(5) The period referred to in subsection (4)(b) must not include any period after the end of the period of 13 weeks beginning with the day on which the claimant's previous entitlement ceased. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(6) Regulations under subsection (4)(b) may in particular provide for the period of a reduction to depend on either or both of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) the number of occasions on which a claimant's entitlement has ceased as specified in subsection (1), (2) or (3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) the period between such occasions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(7) Regulations may provide for a reduction under this section made in relation to an award that is terminated to be applied to any new award made within a prescribed period of the termination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(8) During any period for which the amount of a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance is reduced under this section by virtue of a failure by one of the claimants to comply with the condition in section 1(2)(a) or (c), the allowance is payable to the other member of the couple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
