# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 17B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17B. Section 17A: supplemental[^c21712241]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9] For the purposes of, or in connection with, any scheme within section 17A(1) the Secretary of State may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]

- (a) make arrangements (whether or not with other persons) for the provision of facilities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]
- (b) provide support (by whatever means) for arrangements made by other persons for the provision of facilities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]
- (c) make payments (by way of fees, grants, loans or otherwise) to persons undertaking the provision of facilities under arrangements within paragraph (a) or (b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]
- (d) make payments (by way of grants, loans or otherwise) to persons participating in the scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]
- (e) make payments in respect of incidental expenses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]

(2) For the purposes of, or in connection with, any scheme within section 17A(1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]

- (a) the Scottish Ministers, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]
- (b) the Welsh Ministers, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]

may make payments (by way of fees, grants, loans or otherwise) to persons (including the Secretary of State) undertaking the provision of facilities under arrangements within subsection (1)(a) or (b) if the following condition is met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]

(3) The condition is that the Scottish Ministers or the Welsh Ministers consider that the facilities are capable of supporting the training in Scotland or Wales of persons for employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]

(4) Unless the Scottish Ministers or Welsh Ministers otherwise specify, the payments may be used by the person to whom they are made for the provision of any of the facilities provided under the arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]

(5) In subsections (1) to (4) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]**facilities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]**” includes services, and any reference to the provision of facilities includes the making of payments to persons participating in the scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]

(6) The power of the Secretary of State to make an order under section 26 of the Employment Act 1988 (status of trainees *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9] etc *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]) includes power to make, in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]

- (a) persons participating in any scheme within section 17A(1), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]
- (b) payments received by them by virtue of subsection (1) above, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]

provision corresponding to any provision which (by virtue of section 26(1) or (2) of that Act) may be made in relation to persons using such facilities, and to such payments received by them, as are mentioned in section 26(1) of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-58b02161e77720d6439191fbc2d2d6a9]
