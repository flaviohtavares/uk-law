## 8. Attendance, information and evidence.[^c11296801][^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] Regulations may make provision for requiring a claimant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] (other than a joint-claim couple claiming a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance)[^c11296811] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd]—

- (a) to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] participate in an interview in such manner, time and place[^c21789391] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] an employment officer[^c11296821] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] may specify; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd]
- (b) to provide information and such evidence as may be prescribed as to his circumstances, his availability for employment and the extent to which he is actively seeking employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd]

(1A)[^c11296831] Regulations may make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd]

- (a) for requiring each member of a joint-claim couple claiming a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] participate in an interview in such manner, time and place *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] an employment officer[^c21791661] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] may specify; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd]
- (b) for requiring a member of such a couple to provide information and such evidence as may be prescribed as to his circumstances, his availability for employment and the extent to which he is actively seeking employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd]
- (c) for requiring such a couple to jointly provide information and such evidence as may be prescribed as to the circumstances of each or either member of the couple, the availability for employment of each or either member of the couple and the extent to which each or either member of the couple is actively seeking employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd]
- (d) where any requirement to provide information or evidence is imposed on such a couple by virtue of paragraph (c), for the joint obligation of the couple to be capable of being discharged by the provision of the information or evidence by one member of the couple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd]

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] or (1A)[^c11296841] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] may, in particular *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd] provide for entitlement to a jobseeker's allowance to cease at such time as may be determined in accordance with any such regulations if, when a person fails to comply with such regulations, that person (or, if that person is a member of a joint-claim couple, either member of the couple) does not make prescribed contact with an employment officer within a prescribed period of the failure.[^c21791681] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31dec74f-4b21-4d91-a45a-626128772cfd]
