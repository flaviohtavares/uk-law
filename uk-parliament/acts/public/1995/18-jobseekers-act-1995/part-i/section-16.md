# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Severe hardship.[^c11297351]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] If it appears to the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

- (a) that a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
  - (i) has reached the age of 16 but not the age of 18, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] is not entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance or to income support, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] is registered for training but is not being provided with any training, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- (b) that severe hardship will result to him unless a jobseeker’s allowance is paid to him, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

the Secretary of State may direct that this section is to apply to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(2) A direction may be given so as to have effect for a specified period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(3) The Secretary of State may revoke a direction if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

- (a) it appears to him that there has been a change of circumstances as a result of which failure to receive a jobseeker’s allowance need no longer result in severe hardship to the person concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- (b)[^c11297361] it appears to him that the person concerned has, without *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] a good reason[^c21791821] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]—
  - (i) neglected to avail himself of a reasonable opportunity of a place on a training scheme; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] after a place on such a scheme has been notified to him by an employment officer as vacant or about to become vacant, refused or failed to apply for it or to accept it when offered to him; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- (c) he is satisfied that it was given in ignorance of some material fact or was based on a mistake as to some material fact and considers that, but for that ignorance or mistake, he would not have given the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(4)[^c11297371] In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

- “**employment officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]**” means an officer of the Secretary of State or such other person as may be designated for the purposes of this section by an order made by the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- “**period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]**” includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
  - (a) a period of a determinate length; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
  - (b) a period defined by reference to the happening of a future event; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
  - (c) a period of a determinate length but subject to earlier determination upon the happening of a future event; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- “**training scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]**” has such meaning as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
