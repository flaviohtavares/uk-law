# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 19C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18

## 19C. Hardship payments[^c21787101][^c21712261]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724] Regulations may make provision for the making of payments (“hardship payments”) by way of a jobseeker's allowance to a claimant where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) the amount of the claimant's award is reduced under sections 19 to 19B, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) the claimant is or will be in hardship. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

(2) Regulations under this section may in particular make provision as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]

- (a) circumstances in which a claimant is to be treated as being or not being in hardship; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (b) matters to be taken into account in determining whether a claimant is or will be in hardship; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (c) requirements or conditions to be met by a claimant in order to receive hardship payments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (d) the amount or rate of hardship payments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (e) the period for which hardship payments may be made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
- (f) whether hardship payments are recoverable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aa5c7d6a3ab46128bce71e6a3fa63724]
