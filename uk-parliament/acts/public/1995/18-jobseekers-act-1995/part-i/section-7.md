# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 7

## 7. Actively seeking employment.[^c11296731][^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c] For the purposes of this Act, a person is actively seeking employment in any week if he takes in that week such steps as he can reasonably be expected to have to take in order to have the best prospects of securing employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]

(2) Regulations may make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]

- (a) with respect to steps which it is reasonable, for the purposes of subsection (1), for a person to be expected to have to take in any week; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]
- (b) as to circumstances (for example, his skills, qualifications, abilities and physical or mental limitations) which, in particular, are to be taken into account in determining whether, in relation to any steps taken by a person, the requirements of subsection (1) are satisfied in any week. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]

(3) Regulations may make provision for acts of a person which would otherwise be relevant for purposes of this section to be disregarded in such circumstances (including circumstances constituted by, or connected with, his behaviour or appearance) as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]

(4) Regulations may prescribe circumstances in which, for the purposes of this Act, a person is to be treated as actively seeking employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]

(5) Regulations under subsection (4) may, in particular, provide for a person who is actively seeking employment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]

- (a) only in his usual occupation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]
- (b) only at a level of remuneration not lower than that which he is accustomed to receive, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]
- (c) only in his usual occupation and at a level of remuneration not lower than that which he is accustomed to receive, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]

to be treated, for the permitted period determined in his case for the purposes of section 6(5), as actively seeking employment during that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]

(6) Regulations may provide for this section, and any regulations made under it, to have effect in relation to a person who has reached the age of 16 but not the age of 18 as if “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]**employment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]**” included “training". *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]

(7) Where it has been determined *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c] (“the first determination")[^c11296741] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c] that a person is to be treated, for the purposes of this Act, as actively seeking employment in any week, the question whether he is actively seeking employment in that week may subsequently be determined *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c] under section 9 or 10 of the Social Security Act 1998[^c11296751] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c].

(8) For the purposes of this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]

- “**employment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]**” means employed earner’s employment or, in prescribed circumstances— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]
- (a) self-employed earner’s employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]
- (b) employed earner’s employment and self-employed earner’s employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]

“**employed earner’s employment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]**self-employed earner’s employment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]**” have the same meaning as in the Benefits Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_12203e04-07fb-4878-d06e-58dc8446093c]
