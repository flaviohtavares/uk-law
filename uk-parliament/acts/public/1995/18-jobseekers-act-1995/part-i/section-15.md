# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Effect on other claimants.[^c11297301]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] Except in prescribed circumstances, subsection (2) applies in relation to a claimant for an income-based jobseeker’s allowance where a member of his family (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]**A**”) is, or would be, prevented by section 14 from being entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(2)[^c11297311] For the purposes of calculating the claimant’s entitlement to an income-based jobseeker’s allowance— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

- (a) any portion of the applicable amount which is included in respect of A shall be disregarded for the period for which this subsection applies to the claimant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- (b) where the claimant and A are a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] couple[^c21696371] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab], any portion of the applicable amount which is included in respect of them shall be reduced to one half for the period for which this subsection applies to the claimant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- (c) except so far as regulations provide otherwise, there shall be treated as the claimant’s income— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
  - (i) any amount which becomes, or would on an application duly made become, available to A in relation to that period by way of repayment of income tax deducted from A’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] taxable earnings (as defined by section 10 of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003) under[^c21695171] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] PAYE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] any other payment which the claimant or any member of his family receives or is entitled to obtain because A is without employment for that period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- (d)[^c11297331] any payment by way of a jobseeker’s allowance for that period or any part of it which apart from this paragraph would be made to the claimant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
  - (i) shall not be made, if the weekly rate of payment (“the rate") would be equal to or less than the prescribed sum; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab] shall be at a weekly rate equal to the difference between the rate and the prescribed sum, if the rate would be more than the prescribed sum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(3) Where a reduction under subsection (2)(b) would not produce a sum which is a multiple of 5p, the reduction shall be to the nearest lower sum which is such a multiple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(4) Where A returns to work with the same employer after a period during which subsection (2) applied to the claimant (whether or not his return is before the end of any stoppage of work in relation to which he is, or would be, prevented from being entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance), subsection (2) shall cease to apply to the claimant at the commencement of the day on which A returns to work. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(5) In relation to any period of less than a week, subsection (2) shall have effect subject to such modifications as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(6) Subsections (7) to (9) apply where an order made under section 150 of the Administration Act (annual up-rating of benefits) has the effect of increasing the sum prescribed in regulations made under section 4(5) as the personal allowance for a single person aged not less than 25 (“the personal allowance"). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(7) For the sum prescribed in regulations made under subsection (2)(d) there shall be substituted, from the time when the order comes into force, a sum arrived at by increasing the prescribed sum by the percentage by which the personal allowance has been increased by the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(8) If the sum arrived at under subsection (7) is not a multiple of 50p— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

- (a) any remainder of 25p or less shall be disregarded; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
- (b) any remainder of more than 25p shall be rounded up to the nearest 50p. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(9) The order shall state the sum substituted for the sum prescribed in regulations made under subsection (2)(d). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]

(10) Nothing in subsection (7) prevents the making of further regulations under subsection (2)(d) varying the prescribed sum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a056953c5bc4a74c2520402196b724ab]
