# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 5

## 5. Duration of a contribution-based (affected by repeal, see note) jobseeker’s allowance.[^c11296651][^key-4b40ec4f4b13d4326ee89d433616899a][^key-4db8cb94ce6ea67cfb1653dd4b04b231]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c11296661] The period for which a person is entitled to a contribution-based *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5dab9d75d44b98f780f5c049492e64e7] jobseeker’s allowance shall not exceed, in the aggregate, 182 days in any period for which his entitlement is established by reference (under section 2(1)(b)) to the same two years.

(2) The fact that a person’s entitlement to a contribution-based *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-48cfc5d57ae926786fd75b593a3d4132] jobseeker’s allowance (“his previous entitlement") has ceased as a result of subsection (1), does not prevent his being entitled to a further contribution-based *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-48cfc5d57ae926786fd75b593a3d4132] jobseeker’s allowance if—

- (a) he satisfies the contribution-based conditions; and
- (b) the two years by reference to which he satisfies those conditions includes at least one year which is later than the second of the two years by reference to which his previous entitlement was established.

(3) Regulations may provide that a person who would be entitled to a contribution-based *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5270c5b1c1e82386e3a92b4347a15633] jobseeker’s allowance but for the operation of prescribed provisions of, or made under, this Act shall be treated as if entitled to the allowance for the purposes of this section.
