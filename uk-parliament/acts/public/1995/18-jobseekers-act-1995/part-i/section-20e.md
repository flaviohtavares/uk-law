(2) The following functions of officers of the Secretary of State may be exercised by, or by employees of, such person (if any) as the Secretary of State may authorise for the purpose, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

- (a) specifying places and times, and being contacted, under section 8; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (b) entering into or varying any jobseeker's agreement under section 9 or 10 and referring any proposed agreement or variation to the Secretary of State under section 9 or 10; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (c) giving notifications under section 16 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc] [^c21794131]. . . ;
- (d) [^c21794141]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Regulations may provide for any of the following functions of the Secretary of State to be exercisable by, or by employees of, such person (if any) as the Secretary of State may authorise for the purpose— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

- (a) any function under regulations under section 8, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc] [^c21794151]. . . 17A [^c21794151]. . . [^c21721631]. . . , except the making of an excluded decision (see subsection (4)); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (b) the function under section 9(1) of the 1998 Act (revision of decisions) so far as relating to decisions (other than excluded decisions) that relate to any matter arising under any such regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (c) the function under section 10(1) of the 1998 Act (superseding of decisions) so far as relating to decisions (other than excluded decisions) of the Secretary of State that relate to any matter arising under any such regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (d) any function under Chapter 2 of Part 1 of the 1998 Act (social security decisions), except section 25(2) and (3) (decisions involving issues arising on appeal in other cases), which relates to the exercise of any of the functions within paragraphs (a) to (c). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

(4) Each of the following is an “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]**excluded decision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]**” for the purposes of subsection (3)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

- (a) a decision about whether a person has failed to comply with a requirement imposed by regulations under section 8, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc] [^c21794171]. . . [^c21794231]. . . [^c21721631]. . . ;
- (b) a decision about whether a person had good cause for failure to comply with such a requirement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (c) a decision about not paying or reducing a jobseeker's allowance in consequence of a failure to comply with such a requirement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

(5) Regulations under subsection (3) may provide that a function to which that subsection applies may be exercised— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

- (a) either wholly or to such extent as the regulations may provide, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (b) either generally or in such cases as the regulations may provide, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (c) either unconditionally or subject to the fulfilment of such conditions as the regulations may provide. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

(6) An authorisation given by virtue of any provision made by or under this section may authorise the exercise of the function concerned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

- (a) either wholly or to such extent as may be specified in the authorisation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (b) either generally or in such cases as may be so specified, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (c) either unconditionally or subject to the fulfilment of such conditions as may be so specified; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

but, in the case of an authorisation given by virtue of regulations under subsection (3), this subsection is subject to the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

(7) An authorisation given by virtue of any provision made by or under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

- (a) may specify its duration, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (b) may be revoked at any time by the Secretary of State, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (c) does not prevent the Secretary of State or any other person from exercising the function to which the authorisation relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

(8) Anything done or omitted to be done by or in relation to an authorised person (or an employee of that person) in, or in connection with, the exercise or purported exercise of the function concerned is to be treated for all purposes as done or omitted to be done by or in relation to the Secretary of State or (as the case may be) an officer of the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

(9) But subsection (8) does not apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

- (a) for the purposes of so much of any contract made between the authorised person and the Secretary of State as relates to the exercise of the function, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (b) for the purposes of any criminal proceedings brought in respect of anything done by the authorised person (or an employee of that person). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

(10) Any decision which an authorised person makes in exercise of a function of the Secretary of State has effect as a decision of the Secretary of State under section 8 of the 1998 Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

(11) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

- (a) the authorisation of an authorised person is revoked at any time, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (b) at the time of the revocation so much of any contract made between the authorised person and the Secretary of State as relates to the exercise of the function is subsisting, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

the authorised person is entitled to treat the contract as repudiated by the Secretary of State (and not as frustrated by reason of the revocation). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

(12) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]

- (a) “**the 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]**” means the Social Security Act 1998; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (b) “**authorised person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]**” means a person authorised to exercise any function by virtue of any provision made by or under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
- (c) references to functions of the Secretary of State under any enactment (including one comprised in regulations) include functions which the Secretary of State has by virtue of the application of section 8(1)(c) of the 1998 Act in relation to the enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-53bc0651ca784f8c60877623988b68dc]
