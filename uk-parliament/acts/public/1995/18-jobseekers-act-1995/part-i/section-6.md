# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18
>

## 6. Availability for employment. (affected by repeal, see note) Work-related requirements[^c11296681][^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purposes of this Act, a person is available for employment if he is willing and able to take up immediately any employed earner’s employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to such provisions as may be made by regulations; and those regulations may, in particular, provide that a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

- (a) may restrict his availability for employment in any week in such ways as may be prescribed; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]
- (b) may restrict his availability for employment in any week in such circumstances as may be prescribed (for example, on grounds of conscience, religious conviction or physical or mental condition or because he is caring for another person) and in such ways as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

(3) The following are examples of restrictions for which provision may be made by the regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

- (a) restrictions on the nature of the employment for which a person is available; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]
- (b) restrictions on the periods for which he is available; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]
- (c) restrictions on the terms or conditions of employment for which he is available; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]
- (d) restrictions on the locality or localities within which he is available. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

(4) Regulations may prescribe circumstances in which, for the purposes of this Act, a person is or is not to be treated as available for employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

(5) Regulations under subsection (4) may, in particular, provide for a person who is available for employment— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

- (a) only in his usual occupation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]
- (b) only at a level of remuneration not lower than that which he is accustomed to receive, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]
- (c) only in his usual occupation and at a level of remuneration not lower than that which he is accustomed to receive, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

to be treated, for a permitted period, as available for employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

(6) Where it has been determined *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16] (“the first determination")[^c11296691] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16] that a person is to be treated, for the purposes of this Act, as available for employment in any week, the question whether he is available for employment in that week may be subsequently determined *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16] under section 9 or 10 of the Social Security Act 1998[^c11296701] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16].

(7) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]**permitted period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]**”, in relation to any person, means such period as may be determined in accordance with the regulations made under subsection (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

(8) Regulations under subsection (4) may prescribe, in relation to permitted periods— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

- (a) the day on which any such period is to be regarded as having begun in any case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]
- (b) the shortest and longest periods which may be determined in any case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]
- (c) factors which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16] the Secretary of State[^c11296711] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16] may take into account in determining the period in any case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

(9) For the purposes of this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]**employed earner’s employment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]**” has the same meaning as in the Benefits Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e95fcd64b72026bcb101e69190780b16]

(1) The following provisions of this Act provide for the Secretary of State to impose work-related requirements with which claimants must comply for the purposes of this Act.

(2) In this Act “**work-related requirement**” means—

- (a) a work-focused interview requirement (see section 6B);
- (b) a work preparation requirement (see section 6C);
- (c) a work search requirement (see section 6D);
- (d) a work availability requirement (see section 6E).
