# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Provision for Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An Order in Council under paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to the [^c11298951]Northern Ireland Act 1974 (legislation for Northern Ireland in the interim period) which states that it is made only for purposes corresponding to those of this Act—

- (a) shall not be subject to paragraph 1(4) and (5) of that Schedule (affirmative resolution of both Houses of Parliament); but
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
