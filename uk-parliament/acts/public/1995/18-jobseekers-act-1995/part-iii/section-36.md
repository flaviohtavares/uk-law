# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 36

## 36. Regulations and orders.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any power under this Act to make regulations or orders, other than an order under section 8(3),[^c11298811]9(13), 16(4) or 19(10)(a)[^c11298821], shall be exercisable by statutory instrument.

(1A)[^c21792921] Subsection (1) does not apply to an order under section 35(1) in relation to employment officers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-004afd5762f46c45598684e494752efa]

(2) Any such power may be exercised—

- (a) either in relation to all cases to which it extends, or in relation to those cases subject to specified exceptions, or in relation to any specified cases or classes of case;
- (b) so as to make, as respects the cases in relation to which it is exercised—
  - (i) the full provision to which the power extends or any less provision (whether by way of exception or otherwise),
  - (ii) the same provision for all cases in relation to which it is exercised, or different provision for different cases or different classes of case or different provision as respects the same case or class of case for different purposes of this Act,
  - (iii) any such provision either unconditionally or subject to any specified condition.

(3) Where any such power is expressed to be exercisable for alternative purposes it may be exercised in relation to the same case for any or all of those purposes.

(4) Any such power includes power—

- (a) to make such incidental, supplemental, consequential or transitional provision as appears to the Secretary of State , or (in the case of regulations made by the Treasury) to the Treasury,[^c11298831] to be expedient; and
- (b) to provide for a person to exercise a discretion in dealing with any matter.

(4A)[^c21719951] Without prejudice to the generality of the provisions of this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9e890c4fe503a0887933dc16b4d7d564]

- (a) regulations under section 17A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9e890c4fe503a0887933dc16b4d7d564] [^c21721731]. . . may make different provision for different areas; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9e890c4fe503a0887933dc16b4d7d564]
- (b) regulations under section 17A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9e890c4fe503a0887933dc16b4d7d564] [^c21721731]. . . may make provision which applies only in relation to an area or areas specified in the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9e890c4fe503a0887933dc16b4d7d564]

(5) Any power to make regulations or an order for the purposes of any provision of this Act is without prejudice to any power to make regulations or an order for the purposes of any other provision.
