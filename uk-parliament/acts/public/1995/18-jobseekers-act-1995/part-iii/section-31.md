# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1995/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. Termination of awards.[^c11298161]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Regulations may make provision allowing, in prescribed circumstances, an award of income support to be brought to an end by the Secretary of State[^c11298171] where the person to whom it was made, or where he is a member of a couple[^c21696451] his partner or the couple[^c11298181], will be entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance if the award is brought to an end.

(2) Regulations may make provision allowing, in prescribed circumstances, an award of a jobseeker’s allowance to be brought to an end by the Secretary of State where the person to whom it was made, or where he is a member of a couple his partner, or where the award was made to a couple a member of the couple,[^c11298201] will be entitled to income support if the award is brought to an end.

(3) In this section “**partner**” means the other member of the couple concerned.
