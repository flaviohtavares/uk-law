Jobseekers Act 1995

1995 c. 18

An Act to provide for a jobseeker’s allowance and to make other provision to promote the employment of the unemployed and the assistance of persons without a settled way of life.

\[28th June 1995\]

Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—[^c-1ec928b97ced60e013b7540cdf7d57b7][^c-662f771389c6c6aa048472fc53a0cb9b][^c-02037df71cb4f2ac358c151b880a12c7][^c-421d0d2350aaf92822fc34a5f0224ae7][^c-023febb2a4d2c33cdd9a3033b34f69ae][^c-6116e99940dd35f28a0f55367a4adaa2][^c-06977dfdfd0f56a8c9c48419fd0f1f6d][^c-0a040c35b9eafd574e30b002f8284494][^c-fe0949ac9a62160549b04dbc283fb1ef][^c-ebff2aa3957b5de78e1c64a2a80826c3]
