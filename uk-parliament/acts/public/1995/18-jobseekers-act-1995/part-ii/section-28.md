## 28. Expedited claims for housing benefit and council tax benefit.[^c11298031]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-bbaae658da06c6c2395e7ca7f3124321] This section provides for the making of regulations to enable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bbaae658da06c6c2395e7ca7f3124321]

- (a)[^c11298041] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c11298051] priority to be given to certain persons, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bbaae658da06c6c2395e7ca7f3124321]

with a view to claims for housing or council tax benefit made by or in respect of persons who cease to be entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance or income support being dealt with quickly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bbaae658da06c6c2395e7ca7f3124321]

(2)[^c11298061] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In section 63 of the Administration Act (adjudication of claims for housing benefit or council tax benefit), insert after subsection (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bbaae658da06c6c2395e7ca7f3124321]

> (2A) Regulations may make provision requiring authorities to whom claims for housing benefit or council tax benefit are made by, or in respect of, persons who have been entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance or to income support to give priority, in prescribed circumstances, to those claims over other claims for any such benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bbaae658da06c6c2395e7ca7f3124321]
