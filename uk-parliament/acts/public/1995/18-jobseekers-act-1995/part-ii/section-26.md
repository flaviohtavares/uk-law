# Jobseekers Act 1995, section 26

## 26. The back to work bonus.[^c11297881]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4] Regulations may make provision for the payment, in prescribed circumstances, of sums to or in respect of persons who are or have been entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance or to income support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]

(2) A sum payable under the regulations shall be known as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]**a back to work bonus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]**”.

(3) Subject to section 677 of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003 (which provides for a back to work bonus not to be taxable)[^c21696081] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4], a back to work bonus shall be treated for all purposes as payable by way of a jobseeker’s allowance or (as the case may be) income support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]

(4) The regulations may, in particular, provide for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]

- (a) a back to work bonus to be payable only on the occurrence of a prescribed event; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
- (b) a bonus not to be payable unless a claim is made before the end of the prescribed period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
- (c) the amount of a bonus (subject to any maximum prescribed by virtue of paragraph (g)) to be determined in accordance with the regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
- (d) enabling amounts to be calculated by reference to periods of entitlement to a jobseeker’s allowance and periods of entitlement to income support; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
- (e) treating a bonus as payable wholly by way of income support or wholly by way of a jobseeker’s allowance, in a case where amounts have been calculated in accordance with provision made by virtue of paragraph (d); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
- (f) keeping persons who may be entitled to a bonus informed of the amounts calculated in accordance with any provision of the regulations made by virtue of paragraph (c); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
- (g) the amount of a bonus not to exceed a prescribed maximum; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
- (h) a bonus not to be payable if the amount of the bonus which would otherwise be payable is less than the prescribed minimum; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
- (i) prescribed periods to be disregarded for prescribed purposes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
- (j) a bonus which has been paid to a person to be treated, in prescribed circumstances and for prescribed purposes, as income or capital of his or of any other member of his family; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
- (k) treating the whole or a prescribed part of an amount which has accrued towards a person’s bonus— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
  - (i) as not having accrued towards his bonus; but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4] as having accrued towards the bonus of another person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
- (l) the whole or a prescribed part of a back to work bonus to be payable, in such circumstances as may be prescribed, to such person, other than the person who is or had been entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance or to income support, as may be determined in accordance with the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6173713c310775c9b479ab57ca5d52f4]
