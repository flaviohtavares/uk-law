# Housing Act 1957, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Provisions as to appeals under Part II

(1) The rules made under section ninety-nine of the County Courts Act, 1934, for regulating the procedure and practice as respects appeals to the county court under this Part of this Act shall make provision with respect to an inspection by the judge of the premises to which the appeal relates in any case in which he considers that inspection is desirable.

(2) No appeal shall lie from a decision of the Court of Appeal on an appeal from a county court in proceedings originating in an appeal to the county court under this Part of this Act.
