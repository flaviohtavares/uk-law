# Housing Act 1957, section 167

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 167. Authentication of certificates

Any document purporting to be a certificate of a local authority named therein issued for any of the purposes of this Act and to be signed by the clerk to that authority shall be received in evidence and be deemed to be such a certificate without further proof unless the contrary is shown.
