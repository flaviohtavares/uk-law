# Housing Act 1957, section 152

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 152. Compensation in certain cases of subsidence

Notwithstanding anything in section fifty of the Brine Pumping (Compensation for Subsidence) Act, 1891, a local authority or county council shall be entitled to compensation in accordance with the provisions of that Act in respect of any injury or damage to any houses belonging to them which were provided under a housing scheme towards the losses on which the Minister is liable to contribute under the Housing, Town Planning, &c. Act, 1919.
