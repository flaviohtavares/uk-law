# Housing Act 1957, section 75

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 75. Local authority for demolition of obstructive buildings in London

As respects the administrative county of London other than the City of London the metropolitan borough council shall be the local authority for the purposes of the three last foregoing sections.
