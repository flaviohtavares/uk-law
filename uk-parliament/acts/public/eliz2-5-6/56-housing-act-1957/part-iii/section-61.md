# Housing Act 1957, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Temporary provisions for payments to owner-occupiers and others

The provisions of Part II of the Second Schedule to this Act shall have effect as respects payments to be made in certain cases in respect of houses comprised in a clearance area or re-development area.
