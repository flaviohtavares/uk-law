# Housing Act 1957, section 190

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 190. Consequential amendments

The enactments mentioned in the Tenth Schedule to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule, being amendments consequential on the provisions of this Act.
