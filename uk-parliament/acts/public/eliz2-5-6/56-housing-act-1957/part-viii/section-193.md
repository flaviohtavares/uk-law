# Housing Act 1957, section 193

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 193. Short title, commencement and extent

(1) This Act may be cited as the Housing Act, 1957.

(2) This Act shall come into force on the first day of September, nineteen hundred and fifty-seven.

(3) This Act shall not extend to Scotland or Northern Ireland.
