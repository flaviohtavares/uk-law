# Housing Act 1957, section 141

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 141. Subscriptions by local authorities to local savings committees

A local authority for the purposes of Part V of this Act may, subject to the approval of the Minister, contribute to the expenses of any local savings committee established for their district or any part thereof.
