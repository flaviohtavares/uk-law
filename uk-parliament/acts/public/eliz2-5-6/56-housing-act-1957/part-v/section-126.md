# Housing Act 1957, section 126

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 126. Power of county councils to provide houses for their employees

A county council shall have power to provide houses for persons employed or paid by, or by a statutory committee of, the council, and for that purpose may be authorised to acquire or appropriate land in like manner as a local authority may be authorised to acquire or appropriate land for the purposes of this Part of this Act.
