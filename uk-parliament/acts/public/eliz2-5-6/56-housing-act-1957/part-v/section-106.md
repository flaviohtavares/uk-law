# Housing Act 1957, section 106

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 106. Power of Minister to impose conditions on sale of houses and land

If any house, building, land or dwelling in respect of which a local authority are required to keep a Housing Revenue Account is with the consent of the Minister sold by them or leased by them under either of the two last foregoing sections he may in giving consent impose such conditions as he thinks just.
