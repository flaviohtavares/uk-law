# Agriculture Act 1957

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Agriculture Act 1957 *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7b88b4ad199ccd261ab93b862de25feb]

1957 c.57 *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7b88b4ad199ccd261ab93b862de25feb]

An Act to make further provision for guaranteed prices and assured markets for producers of agricultural produce in the United Kingdom; to authorise the payment of grants in respect of long-term improvements of agricultural land within the United Kingdom and in respect of the costs of certain transactions concerning such land; to make fresh provision for the development of the pig industry in Great Britain (including the production, processing, manufacture and distribution of pig products); to authorise payments into the Exchequer of Northern Ireland on account of expenses incurred for the benefit of producers of agricultural produce in Northern Ireland; and for purposes connected with the matters aforesaid. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7b88b4ad199ccd261ab93b862de25feb]
