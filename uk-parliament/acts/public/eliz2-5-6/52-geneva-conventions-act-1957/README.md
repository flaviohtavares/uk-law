Geneva Conventions Act 1957

1957 c.52

An Act to enable effect to be given to certain international conventions done at Geneva on the twelfth day of August, nineteen hundred and forty-nine, and for purposes connected therewith.

\[31st July 1957\] [^c9659751]

Whereas, with a view to the ratification by Her Majesty of the conventions set out in the Schedules to this Act, it is expedient to make certain amendments in the law:[^c-b14f4be46feb942735a27e681be8e151][^c-188f2d701c32df77f5130abedeff4c7b][^c-4fd7a7f9887d6469c05e5d241456e6e2]
