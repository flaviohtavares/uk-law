# Agriculture (Amendment) Act 1984

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Agriculture (Amendment) Act 1984 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]

1984 c. 20

An Act to enable grants under section 64 of the Agriculture Act 1967 towards fulfilling guarantees of bank loans to be made in relation to a wider range of co-operative marketing businesses; and to extend the powers of obtaining information conferred by subsection (1) of section 1 of the Agricultural Statistics Act 1979 and to repeal subsection (5) of that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]

\[24th May 1984\] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]

Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]

## 1. Amendment of Agriculture Act 1967: “co-operative marketing business”. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]

(1) In section 64 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76] [^c757113]Agriculture Act 1967 (grants towards fulfilling guarantees of bank loans to agriculture or horticulture businesses), after subsection (8) there shall be inserted the following subsection:— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]

> (8A) For the purposes of the definitions in subsection (8) above of “co-operative marketing business” and “co-operative association”, the members of a co-operative association shall be deemed to include the members of any other co-operative association which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]
>
> - (a) is a member of the first mentioned association, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]
> - (b) is deemed to be such a member by virtue of paragraph (a) above or this paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]

.

(2) This section extends to Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]

## 2. Amendments of Agricultural Statistics Act 1979. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In subsection (1) of section 1 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76] [^c757114]Agricultural Statistics Act 1979, for paragraphs (b) and (c) (power to require information relating to the names and addresses of owners and occupiers and to whether land is let and, if so, at what rent) there shall be substituted the following paragraph:— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]

> - (b) any person who is an owner or occupier of the land or any part of it and the terms on which, and arrangements under which, the land or any part of it is owned, occupied, managed or farmed by any person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]

.

(2)[^c-871f403fdb0c0982ea552e7a1ecc1eb9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) This section extends to England and Wales only. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f57310a2177e95cea845787ca3db9d76]
