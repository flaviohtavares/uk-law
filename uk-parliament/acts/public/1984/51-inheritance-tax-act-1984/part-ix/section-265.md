# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 265

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 265. Chargeable transfers affecting more than one property.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the value transferred by a chargeable transfer is determined by reference to the values of more than one property the tax chargeable on the value transferred shall be attributed to the respective values in the proportions which they bear to their aggregate, but subject to section 54B(3) above and to[^c13615031] any provision reducing the amount of tax attributable to the value of any particular property.
