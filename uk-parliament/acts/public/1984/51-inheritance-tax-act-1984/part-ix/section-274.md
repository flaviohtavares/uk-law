# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 274

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 274. Commencement.[^c13615471]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act shall come into force on 1st January 1985, but shall not apply to transfers of value made before that date or to other events before that date on which capital transfer tax is chargeable or would be chargeable but for an exemption, exception or relief.

(2) Subsection (1) above shall have effect subject to section 275 below, to Schedule 7 to this Act and to any other provision to the contrary.

## Annotations

[^c13615471]: Modification: *By* Finance Act 1986 s. 100(1),*on and after* 25*July* 1986*the* 1984*Act may be cited as the Inheritance Tax Act and the tax charged under the* 1984*Act shall be known as inheritance tax.*
