# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 270

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 270. Connected persons.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Act any question whether a person is connected with another shall be determined as, for the purposes of the 1992 Act[^c13615081], it falls to be determined under section 286 of that Act, but as if in that section “**relative**” included uncle, aunt, nephew and niece and “**settlement**”, “**settlor**” and “**trustee**” had the same meanings as in this Act.
