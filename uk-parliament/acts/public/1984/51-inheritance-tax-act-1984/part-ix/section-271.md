# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 271

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 271. Property of corporations sole.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

References in this Act (except section 59) to property to which a person is beneficially entitled do not include references to property to which a person is entitled as a corporation sole.
