# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 164

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 164. Transferor’s expenses.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In determining the value transferred by a transfer of value, expenses incurred by the transferor in making the transfer (but not his liability for inheritance tax[^c13612571])—

- (a) shall, if borne by him, be left out of account;
- (b) shall, if borne by a person benefiting from the transfer, be treated as reducing the value transferred.

## Annotations

[^c13612571]: Textual amendment: *See* Finance Act 1986 s. 100(1)*and* (2)—*for any liability to tax arising on and after* 25*July* 1986*any reference in the legislation to capital transfer tax has effect as a reference to inheritance tax.*
