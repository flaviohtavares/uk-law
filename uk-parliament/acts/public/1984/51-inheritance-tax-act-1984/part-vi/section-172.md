# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 172

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 172. Funeral expenses.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In determining the value of a person’s estate immediately before his death, allowance shall be made for reasonable funeral expenses.
