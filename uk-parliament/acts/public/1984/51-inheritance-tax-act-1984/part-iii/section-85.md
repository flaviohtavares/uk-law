# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 85

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Credit for annual charges under Finance Act 1975.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any tax charged under paragraph 12(2) of Schedule 5 to the [^c13609621]Finance Act 1975 and not already allowed as a credit under paragraph 12(3) of that Schedule or under section 125 of the [^c13609631]Finance Act 1982 or under this section shall be allowed as a credit against tax chargeable under this Chapter (apart from section 79) in respect of the settled property or part concerned.
