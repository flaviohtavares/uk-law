> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47A. Settlement power[^c13608521]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act “**settlement power**” means any power over, or exercisable (whether directly or indirectly) in relation to, settled property or a settlement.
