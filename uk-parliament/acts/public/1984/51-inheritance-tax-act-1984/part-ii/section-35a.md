# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 35A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35A. Variation of undertakings.[^c13608471][^c13608461]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An undertaking given under section 30, 32 or 32A above or paragraph 5 of Schedule 5 to this Act may be varied from time to time by agreement between the Board and the person bound by the undertaking.

(2) Where the tribunal[^c21421451] is satisfied that—

- (a) the Board have made a proposal for the variation of such an undertaking to the person bound by the undertaking,
- (b) that person has failed to agree to the proposed variation within six months after the date on which the proposal was made, and
- (c) it is just and reasonable, in all the circumstances, to require the proposed variation to be made,

the tribunal may direct that the undertaking is to have effect from a specified date[^c21421471] as if the proposed variation had been agreed to by the person bound by the undertaking.

(3) The date specified by the tribunal[^c21421491] must not be less than sixty days after the date of the tribunal's direction[^c21421511].

(4) A direction under this section shall not take effect if, before the date specified by the tribunal[^c21422341], a variation different from that to which the direction relates is agreed between the Board and the person bound by the undertaking.
