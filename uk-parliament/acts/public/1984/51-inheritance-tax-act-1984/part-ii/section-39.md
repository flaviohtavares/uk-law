# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Attribution of value to residuary gifts.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Such part only of the value transferred shall be attributed to gifts of residue or shares in residue as is not attributed under section 38 above to specific gifts.
