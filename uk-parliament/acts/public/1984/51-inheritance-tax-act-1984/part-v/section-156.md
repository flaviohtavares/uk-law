## 156. Apsley House and Chevening Estate.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Act shall not apply in respect of—

- (a) the rights conferred by section 3 of the [^c13612281]Wellington Museum Act 1947, or
- (b) property held on the trusts of the trust instrument set out in the Schedule to the [^c13612291]Chevening Estate Act 1959.
