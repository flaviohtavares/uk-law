# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Charge on transfers.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Inheritance tax[^c13606531] shall be charged on the value transferred by a chargeable transfer.

## Annotations

[^c13606531]: Textual amendment: *See* Finance Act 1986 s. 100(1)*and* (2)—*for any liability to tax arising on and after* 25*July* 1986*any reference in the legislation to capital transfer tax has effect as a reference to inheritance tax.*
