# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51

## 17. Changes in distribution of deceased’s estate, etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

None of the following is a transfer of value—

- (a) a variation or disclaimer to which section 142(1) below applies;
- (b) a transfer to which section 143 below applies;
- (c)[^key-e3ded92b982ea4b214bd3bc1c512413f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) the renunciation of a claim to legitim or rights under section 131 of the Civil Partnership Act 2004[^c19772311] within the period mentioned in section 147(6) below.
