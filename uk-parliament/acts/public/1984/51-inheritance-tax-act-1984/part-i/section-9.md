## 9. Transitional provisions on reduction of tax.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The transitional provisions in Schedule 2 to this Act shall have effect in relation to any enactment by virtue of which tax is reduced by the substitution of a new Table[^c13607171] in Schedule 1.
