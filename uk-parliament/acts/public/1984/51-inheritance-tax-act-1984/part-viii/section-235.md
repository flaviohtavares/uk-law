# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 235

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 235. Interest on overpaid tax.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any repayment of an amount paid in excess of a liability for tax or for interest on tax shall carry interest from the date on which the payment was made until the order for repayment is issued[^c13614411] at the rate applicable under section 178 of the Finance Act 1989[^c21431861].

(2) Interest paid under this section shall not constitute income for any tax purposes.
