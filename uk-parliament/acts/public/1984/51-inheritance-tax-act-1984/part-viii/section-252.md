# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 252

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51

## 252. Effect of award by the tribunal.[^c21422981]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any penalty awarded by the tribunal[^c21423001] shall be recoverable by the Board as a debt due to the Crown.
