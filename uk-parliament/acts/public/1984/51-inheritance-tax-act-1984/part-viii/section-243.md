## 243. Scotland: recovery of tax in sheriff court.[^c13614691]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Scotland, tax and interest on tax may, without prejudice to any other remedy, and if the amount of the tax and interest does not exceed the sum for the time being specified in section 35(1)(a) of the [^c13614701]Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1971, be sued for and recovered in the sheriff court.
