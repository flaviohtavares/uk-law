## 245A. Failure to provide information etc.[^c13614741]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who fails to make a return under section 218 above shall be liable—

- (a) to a penalty not exceeding £300; and
- (b) to a further penalty not exceeding £60 for every day after the day on which the failure has been declared by a court or the tribunal[^c21412831] and before the day on which the return is made.

(1A)[^c13614751] A person who fails to comply with the requirements of section 218A above shall be liable—

- (a) to a penalty not exceeding £100; and
- (b) to a further penalty not exceeding £60 for every day after the day on which the failure has been declared by a court or the tribunal and before the day on which the requirements are complied with.

(1B)[^c19468071] Without prejudice to any penalties under subsection (1A) above, if a person continues to fail to comply with the requirements of section 218A after the anniversary of the end of the period of six months referred to in section 218A(1), he shall be liable to a penalty of an amount not exceeding £3,000.
