# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 260

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 260. Inland Revenue Regulation Act 1890.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 21, 22 and 35 of the [^c13614991]Inland Revenue Regulation Act 1890 (proceedings for fines, etc.) shall not apply in relation to inheritance tax[^c13615001].

[^c13614991]: Marginal citation: [1890 c.21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1890/21).

[^c13615001]: Textual amendment: *See* Finance Act 1986 s. 100(1)*and* (2)—*for any liability to tax arising on and after* 25*July* 1986*any reference in the legislation to capital transfer tax has effect as a reference to inheritance tax.*
