# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 241

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 241. Overpayments.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-1ba303f73c782b7a0d74c4b34a1f2ea9] If it is proved to the satisfaction of the Board that too much tax has been paid on the value transferred by a chargeable transfer or on so much of that value as is attributable to any property, the Board shall repay the excess unless the claim for repayment was made more than 4 years[^c21411821] after the date on which the payment or last payment of the tax was made.

(2) References in this section to tax include references to interest on tax.
