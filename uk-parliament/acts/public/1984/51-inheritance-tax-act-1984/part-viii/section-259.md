# Inheritance Tax Act 1984, section 259

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/51

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 259. Inspection of records.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 16 of the [^c13614951]Stamp Act 1891, section 56 of the [^c13614961]Finance Act 1946 and section 27 of the [^c13614971]Finance (No.2) Act (Northern Ireland) 1946 (inspection of public records and records of unit trusts) shall apply in relation to inheritance tax[^c13614981] as they apply in relation to stamp duties.
