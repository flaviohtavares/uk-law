# Finance Act 1984, section 8

> Text as at 2024-02-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Free zones.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions set out in Part I of Schedule 4 to this Act (which provide for special areas, to be known as free zones, to be designated for customs and excise purposes) shall be inserted in the [^c10406901] Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 after Part VIII as a new Part VIIIA, and that Act shall have effect with the amendments specified in Part II of that Schedule (which also relate to free zones).
