# Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, section 22B

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## (not yet in force: 22B). (not yet in force: Traffic regulation on long distance routes)[^c18863541]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This section applies where the Secretary of State thinks that, because of the use of a long distance route by vehicular traffic, members of the public cannot safely and conveniently—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: enjoy the amenities of any part of the route or of the area through which the route runs;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: take advantage of opportunities for recreation in any part of that area;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: study nature in any part of that area.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The Secretary of State may make an order preventing the use of the route or a specified part of the route—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: by vehicular traffic, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: by vehicular traffic of a specified kind.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: An order under this section may have effect only in relation to a long distance route which is, or in so far as it is, in England.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: An order under this section shall be treated for all purposes as if it were a traffic regulation order made by the Secretary of State in relation to a road for which he is the traffic authority (and, in particular, any provision of this Act about the making or effect of such an order shall apply).)*
