# Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, section 22D

## 22D. Section 22C: supplemental[^c18809031]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An order may be made , and a notice may be issued,[^c-28e4f603675a0a1678ff8333bd175872] by virtue of section 22C only on the recommendation of the chief officer of police for the area to which the order or notice[^c-762acd214888e3bcd02a3fc6e94db01b] relates.

(1A)[^c-1c14436f89568e910f8be7e56e94c920] Any statutory requirement to publish a proposal for, or a notice of, the making of an order does not apply to an order made by virtue of section 22C if the chief officer of police for the area to which the order relates considers that to do so would risk undermining the purpose for which the order is made.

(2) The following shall not apply in relation to an order made , or a notice issued,[^c-7650fe4b3742c1c625c2f5177355c709] by virtue of section 22C—

- (a) section 3,
- (b) section 6(5),
- (c) the words in section 14(4) from “but” to the end,
- (d) section 121B, and
- (e) paragraph 13(1)(a) of Schedule 9.

(3)[^c18811571] Sections 92 and 94 shall apply in relation to an order under section 14 made , or a notice under that section issued,[^c-8efd5505a0903c3932c4406b349fff42] by virtue of section 22C as they apply in relation to an order under section 1 or 6.

(4)[^c18811591] An order made , or a notice issued,[^c-d2067fd1fda9410d4c984e3a29760fbd] by virtue of section 22C, or an authorisation or requirement by virtue of subsection (3) above, may authorise the undertaking of works for the purpose of, or for a purpose ancillary to, another provision of the order, notice,[^c-7ce1a2415bea908650d476f53c949774] authorisation or requirement.

(5)[^c18811611] An order made , or a notice issued,[^c-c59daa4efe098b593aea098d10f34285] by virtue of section 22C may—

- (a) enable a constable to direct that a provision of the order or notice[^c-0da006dc7697a73776dd2dc76dc0d903] shall (to such extent as the constable may specify) be commenced, suspended or revived;
- (b) confer a discretion on a constable;
- (c) make provision conferring a power on a constable in relation to the placing of structures or signs (which may, in particular, apply a provision of this Act with or without modifications).
- (d)[^c-17e6ce519d5142a9dc47ba3149d669a6] enable a constable to authorise a person of a description specified in the order or notice to do anything that the constable could do by virtue of this subsection.
