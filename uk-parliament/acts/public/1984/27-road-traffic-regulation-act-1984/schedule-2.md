# Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, SCHEDULE 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 2[^c19184151][^c19184151]

*Extent: E+W+S*

### 1.

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Prescribing the routes to be followed by traffic from one specified point to another.

### 2.

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Prescribing streets which are not to be used for traffic.

### 3.

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Regulating the relative position in the roadway of traffic of differing speeds or types.

### 4.

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Prescribing the places where vehicles may not turn so as to face in the opposite direction to that in which they were proceeding, or where they may only so turn under prescribed conditions.

### 5.

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Prescribing the conditions subject to which, and the times at which, articles may be loaded on to, or unloaded from, vehicles on streets.

### 6.

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Prescribing the conditions subject to which, and the times at which, vehicles delivering or collecting goods or merchandise, or goods or merchandise of any particular class or classes, may stand in streets.

### 7.

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Prescribing rules as to precedence to be observed as between vehicles proceeding in the same direction, in opposite directions or when crossing.

### 8.

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Making provision as to vehicles when unattended.

### 9.

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Making provision as to places in streets where vehicles may, or may not, wait.

### 10.

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Making provision as to cab ranks, and as to ranks and stopping places of buses and other public conveyances.
