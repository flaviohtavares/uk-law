## 1. Traffic regulation orders outside Greater London.[^c13544131][^c13544141][^c13544151][^c13544161][^c16323921][^c18778731][^c-4bc0edb514cb65f371f71504dcafc111][^c-a3bb8928da54b0e35e727f79c0becbef][^c-4d24872aa7f6e193ebd0f93ece0a2fdc][^M_E_fbea046a-8738-4780-d616-e8b1738180d2][^c-daf4a3e110fc5b598397f47d7cc10221][^c-725ea49afe373d9ff82c67da483b7e4f][^c-725ea49afe373d9ff82c67da483b7e4f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The traffic authority for a road outside Greater London may make an order under this section (referred to in this Act as a “[^c13544171]**traffic regulation order**”) in respect of the road where it appears to the authority making the order that it is expedient to make it—

- (a) for avoiding danger to persons or other traffic using the road or any other road or for preventing the likelihood of any such danger arising, or
- (b) for preventing damage to the road or to any building on or near the road, or
- (c) for facilitating the passage on the road or any other road of any class of traffic (including pedestrians), or
- (d) for preventing the use of the road by vehicular traffic of a kind which, or its use by vehicular traffic in a manner which, is unsuitable having regard to the existing character of the road or adjoining property, or
- (e) (without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (d) above) for preserving the character of the road in a case where it is specially suitable for use by persons on horseback or on foot, or
- (f) for preserving or improving the amenities of the area through which the road runs or[^c13544181]
- (g) for any of the purposes specified in paragraphs (a) to (c) of subsection (1) of section 87 of the Environment Act 1995 (air quality).

(3)[^M_F_b97af4f8-9544-4c79-c642-8d62159d0740] A traffic regulation order made by a local traffic authority may, with the consent of the Secretary of State , a strategic highways company[^c-082ffda48b740aaef3c4fee65a37309f], extend to a road in relation to which he is , it is[^c-e18746e4dea8de81b606e6dce269a742] the traffic authority if the order forms part of a scheme of general traffic control relating to roads of which at least one has a junction with the length of road in question.

(3A)[^c13544231] A local traffic authority may make a traffic regulation order in respect of a road in relation to which the Secretary of State , a strategic highways company[^c-803ff5aadfcd8f1324a346f289c031c8] or the National Assembly for Wales is the traffic authority if—

- (a) the order is required for the provision of relevant bus scheme facilities[^c19975621] or for the taking of relevant bus scheme measures[^c-1d76f6e4f02fa6235ea3e9a111cec434], and
- (b) the Secretary of State or, the National Assembly for Wales, consents.

(3B)[^c-60320f6ededd842f546ec1494c986fa4] In subsection (3A) “ **relevant bus scheme facilities** ” means—

- (za)[^c-be11d78278a578f60af90bc22f510dcd] facilities provided pursuant to an advanced quality partnership scheme under Part 2 of the Transport Act 2000;
- (a) facilities provided pursuant to a quality partnership scheme under that Part[^c-c958851662aa6d4fcbe762f5b113c3f4];
- (aa)[^c-624031e8600bcf563e64254999ba2616] facilities provided in connection with a franchising scheme under that Part (see section 123A(3)(d) of that Act);
- (b) facilities provided pursuant to a quality contract within the meaning of that Part (see section 124(4) and (5) of that Act) or otherwise in connection with a quality contracts scheme under that Part.
- (c)[^c-97a677098267da86ba3f810d1edf0b0d] facilities provided pursuant to an enhanced partnership scheme under that Part.

(3C)[^c-29dc4aae80e804165560b3694e1c9ae2] In subsection (3A) “**relevant bus scheme measures**” means—

- (a) measures taken pursuant to an advanced quality partnership scheme under Part 2 of the Transport Act 2000;
- (b) measures taken pursuant to an enhanced partnership scheme under that Part.
