# Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, section 133

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/27

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 133. Vehicles used for marine salvage.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subsection (3) of section 130 of this Act shall have effect in relation to motor vehicles used for salvage purposes pursuant to Part IX of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^c13565231] as it has effect in relation to vehicles used for naval, military or air force purposes while being driven as mentioned in that subsection.

(2) In this section “**salvage**” means the preservation of a vessel which is wrecked, stranded or in distress, or the lives of persons belonging to, or the cargo or apparel of, such a vessel.
