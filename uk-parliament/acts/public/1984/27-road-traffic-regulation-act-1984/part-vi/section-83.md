# Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, section 83

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/27

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 83. Provisions as to directions under s. 82(2)

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c13559391] A direction under section 82(2) by the[^c13559401] national authority[^c-127c505532cf3ef73d7e627e6a3cbdf0] shall be given by means of an order made by the national authority after giving public notice of the national authority's[^c-84af63c52d95a35a19431aff34137b1a] intention to make an order.

(2)[^c13559411][^c13559421] A direction under section 82(2) by a local traffic authority shall be given by a strategic highways company or[^c-f46a5da78b7ff3ffda87bdd83a0321b5] by means of an order made by the company or[^c-b832f012ef69dd88bae4dd58c21b8c5f] the authority.

(3) Section 68(1)(c) of this Act shall apply to any order made under subsection (2) above.

(4)[^c-105bbfcc927e3f7bd95118f9d4a50dc4] The power of the Welsh Ministers to make an order under subsection (1) is exercisable by statutory instrument.
