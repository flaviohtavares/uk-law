## 70. Default powers of Secretary of State as to traffic signs.[^c13558071]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c13558081] If a strategic highways company,[^key-d6232ca8469aca2d89d9cf28656524fd] a local traffic authority[^c13558091] or an authority to whom section 68 of this Act applies fail to comply with any direction given under section 65(2) or section 69 of this Act, the national authority[^key-c367f33a42c28b134643201b107295a1] may [^key-4eb8409e59f837faf06eec74d9e07604]... carry out the work required by the direction; and the expenses incurred [^key-573b17da7d400190badbc05a95291b59]... in doing so shall be recoverable by the national authority[^key-6807f18fa2baf5c576c2664470a99f98] from the company or[^key-4b89124fa8a59caf7f5d923d07a63046] the authority that failed to comply with the direction[^key-7448496bc2c1a0a9e7bcc7535f618f53] , and, in England or Wales, shall be so recoverable summarily as a civil debt.

(2) Any such direction—

- (a) if relating to a road in England or Wales, shall be enforceable on the application of the Secretary of State by an order of mandamus; or
- (b) if relating to a road in Scotland, shall be enforceable by order of the Court of Session on an application by the Lord Advocate under section 91 of the [^c13558101]Court of Session Act 1868.

(3)[^key-3061b34c0ccc3b28640e56af38fd00f7] In England, where subsection (1) applies in respect of non-compliance with a direction by a traffic authority other than a strategic highways company—

- (a) a strategic highways company may carry out the work required by the direction with the consent of the Secretary of State, and
- (b) the expenses incurred by the company in doing so are recoverable by the company from the authority summarily as a civil debt.
