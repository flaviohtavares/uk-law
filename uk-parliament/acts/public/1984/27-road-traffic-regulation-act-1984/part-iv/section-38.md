## 38. Parking place to be used as bus or coach station.[^c13551451]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where, in pursuance of the powers conferred by section 32 of this Act, a local authority provides a parking place which may be used by public service vehicles, then, subject to Parts I to III and Part V of Schedule 9 to this Act, the local authority may, if they think fit,—

- (a) by order appoint that parking place as a station for, or for a specified class of, public service vehicles;
- (b) in England or Wales by regulation, and in Scotland by order, declare that section 35A(5)[^c13551461] of this Act shall not apply to public service vehicles, either absolutely or to such extent as may be specified in the regulation or order.

(2) A local authority by whom a parking place is appointed under this section as a station for public service vehicles may—

- (a) do all such things as are necessary to adapt the parking place for use as such a station, and in particular provide and maintain waiting rooms, ticket offices and lavatories and other similar accommodation in connection with it;
- (b) make reasonable charges for the use of, or let on hire to any person, any accommodation so provided; and
- (c) make regulations as to the use of any such accommodation.

(3) Subject to subsection (4) below, a local authority shall have power to make such reasonable charges for the use of a parking place (not being part of a road[^c13551471]) as a station for public service vehicles as may be fixed by the local authority.

(4) If the holder of a PSV operator’s licence in respect of any vehicles using the parking place as a station considers that the charges fixed under subsection (3) above are unreasonable, then, in default of agreement between the licence holder and the local authority for a reduction of them, the charges in respect of those vehicles shall be such as may be determined by the appropriate traffic *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8ffa4cc1141fbdc552efba453d29c053] commissioner[^c13551481] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8ffa4cc1141fbdc552efba453d29c053]a traffic commissioner *(Extent: E+W)* *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8ffa4cc1141fbdc552efba453d29c053].

(5) The powers conferred on a local authority by subsections (1) and (2) above shall be in addition to, and not in substitution for, the powers conferred on a local authority by sections 32 and 33(1) of this Act.

(6) The purposes of this section shall be purposes for which a local authority may borrow.

(7) In this section—

- (a) “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-de580b73357ddbd0e89b344f82c544cf]**the appropriate traffic *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-de580b73357ddbd0e89b344f82c544cf] commissioner *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-de580b73357ddbd0e89b344f82c544cf]**” means the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-de580b73357ddbd0e89b344f82c544cf] commissioner *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-de580b73357ddbd0e89b344f82c544cf] for any traffic area constituted for the purposes of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-de580b73357ddbd0e89b344f82c544cf] [^c13551511]Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981 in which the area or any part of the area of the local authority is situated; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-de580b73357ddbd0e89b344f82c544cf]
- (b) “**PSV operator’s licence**” means a PSV operator’s licence granted under the provisions of Part II of the said Act of 1981.
