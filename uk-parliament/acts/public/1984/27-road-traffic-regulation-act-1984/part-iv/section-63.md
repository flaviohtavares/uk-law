# Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 63. Stands and racks for bicycles.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The powers of any authority under this Act to provide parking places shall extend to providing, in roads or elsewhere, stands or racks for, or devices for securing, bicycles or motor cycles[^c13556201].
