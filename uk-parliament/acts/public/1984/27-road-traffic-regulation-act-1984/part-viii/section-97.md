## 97 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Supplementary provisions as to traffic wardens.[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] Neither regulations under section 7 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] [^c13561301]Superannuation Act 1972 nor any local Act scheme within the meaning of section 8 of that Act shall apply to traffic wardens by virtue of section 95 or 96 of this Act; but, in relation to such traffic wardens *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] [^c13561311]... as *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] their employer[^c-920d924af13a1d4a6d9c827b0a64a140] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] may determine, those regulations (or, if the expenses of *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] that employer[^c-a7bc32744e7015a00ab3c38522240219] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] are paid by a local Act authority, the local Act scheme) shall apply, subject to such adaptations, modifications and exceptions as the Sectetary of State may by regulations prescribe. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c13561321][^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] Where traffic wardens are employed by a police authority which is a committee of the council of a county, they shall be treated as employed by the committee as constituted from time to time; but the committee’s employment of traffic wardens shall not subject members of the committee to any personal liability under contract or otherwise *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]
