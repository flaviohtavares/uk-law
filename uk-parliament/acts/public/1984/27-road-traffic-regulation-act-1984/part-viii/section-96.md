## 96 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Additional powers of traffic wardens.[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] An order under section 95(5) of this Act may provide that, for the purposes of any functions which traffic wardens are authorised by the order to discharge, but subject to the provisions of subsection (3) below, references to a constable or police constable in all or any of the enactments specified in subsection (2) below shall include references to a traffic warden. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]

- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4ca87f72a2c2a96d9863cdaf46a619ac][^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]

  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] section 100(3) (which relates to the interim disposal of vehicles removed under section 99); and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] sections 104 and 105 (which relate to the immobilisation of illegally parked vehicles); *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] Any power of a constable for the purposes of the following provisions of *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] the Road Traffic Act 1988, namely, sections 163, 164(1), (2) and (6) and 165[^c13561231] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a], shall be exercisable by a traffic warden under an order made by virtue of subsection (1) above only where— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] the traffic warden is assisting a constable, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] the traffic warden has reasonable cause to believe that an offence has been committed of a description specified in relation to the section in question for the purposes of this paragraph by the order, and, in the case of a power for the purposes of *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] section 165 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^c13561241] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a], the order authorises the use of that power in relation to that offence, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] in the case of a power for the purposes of *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] section 163 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^c13561251] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a], the traffic warden is exercising functions in connection with the control and regulation of traffic (including pedestrians) or stationary vehicles. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c13561261][^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] Where an order has been made pursuant to subsection (2)(bb)(i) above, in section 100(3) of this Act the words “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]**chief officer of the police force to which the constable belongs *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]**” shall be deemed to include a reference to a chief officer of police under whose direction a traffic warden acts. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a] Any order made under section 95(5) of this Act may make different provision for different cases or classes of case, or in respect of different areas. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60c8adeeb8575c0ec8dd7404653b295a]
