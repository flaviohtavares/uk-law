## 22H. Regulations about licences under this Part[^key-f2f131d8d2fca00a16779e13295096ff]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may make regulations about the licences required for persons providing passenger micromobility vehicles or non-passenger micromobility vehicles.

(2) Schedule 3A makes further provision about regulations under this section (including provision for the imposition of financial penalties and fees, and for the creation of criminal offences).
