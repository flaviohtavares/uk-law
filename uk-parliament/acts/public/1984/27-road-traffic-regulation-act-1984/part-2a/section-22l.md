# Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, section 22L

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22L. Regulations[^c-f2f131d8d2fca00a16779e13295096ff]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations under this Part—

- (a) may make different provision for different purposes, including different provision for different types of micromobility vehicle or different types of provision of micromobility vehicles;
- (b) may make consequential, supplementary, incidental, transitional and saving provision.
