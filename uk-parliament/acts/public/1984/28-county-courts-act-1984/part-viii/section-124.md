# County Courts Act 1984, section 124

## 124. Liability of bailiff for neglect to levy execution.[^c4408671]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-10a53e03a0b1871ecd3a8a6b54808ab7] Where the county court issues a warrant of execution, control, possession or delivery and the person to whom it is directed loses the opportunity of executing it[^c-43a0e26afbed3262bf3a5938e90474bf] against goods, loses the opportunity of levying the execution by reason of neglect, connivance or omission, any party aggrieved thereby may complain to the court.[^c-8cade6e20317d65c9b80760e90dd972f]

(2) On any such complaint the court[^c-4387fe7d933fb3aaa1edafedc99a5eed] , if the neglect, connivance or omission is proved to his satisfaction, shall order that person[^c-1485d82389533bda6acb0555adc69a80] to pay such damages as it appears that the complainant has sustained by reason of it, not exceeding in any case the sum for which the warrant was[^c-c4fab363699cd4b477e849692b3b03fb] issued.
