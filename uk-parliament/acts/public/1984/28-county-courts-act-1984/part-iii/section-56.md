# County Courts Act 1984, section 56

## 56. Examination of witnesses abroad.

*Extent: E+W*

The High Court shall have the same power to issue a commission, request or order to examine witnesses abroad for the purpose of proceedings in the county court[^c-971a4049e38f94ce52ff72aa04702e6d] as it has for the purpose of an action or matter in the High Court.
