# County Courts Act 1984, section 58

## 58. Persons who may take affidavits for use in county courts.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An affidavit to be used in the county court[^key-971a4049e38f94ce52ff72aa04702e6d] may be sworn before—

- (a)[^key-b1788ae212e9993411d358936b7f4546] a judge of the county court; or
- (b) any justice of the peace; or
- (c)[^key-b1a8e610324ce998031266eb2ea388bb] an officer of the county court appointed by a judge of the county court for the purpose,

as well as before a commissioner for oaths or any other person[^c4404441] authorised to take affidavits under the Commissioners for Oaths Acts 1889 and 1891 . . . [^c4404451].

(2) An affidavit sworn before any such judge or[^key-30c56c19a9767c721e2624b729f35e0c] officer may be sworn without the payment of any fee.
