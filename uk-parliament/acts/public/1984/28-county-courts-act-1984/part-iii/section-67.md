# County Courts Act 1984, section 67

## 67. Impanelling and swearing of jury.

*Extent: E+W*

Where any proceedings in the county court[^c-0a18b78c1ca86614cf54fb546b788274] are to be tried with a jury, eight jurymen shall be impanelled and sworn as occasion requires to give their verdicts in the proceedings brought before them, and being once sworn need not be re-sworn in each trial.
