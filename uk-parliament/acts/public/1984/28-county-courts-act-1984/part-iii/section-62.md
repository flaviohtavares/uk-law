# County Courts Act 1984, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. General power of judge to determine questions of law and fact.

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to the provisions of this Act and of rules of court[^c4405451], a judge[^c-c6c105d7495b73a514ce2a415688192d] of the county court[^c-971a4049e38f94ce52ff72aa04702e6d] shall be the sole judge in all proceedings brought in the court, and shall determine all questions of fact as well as of law.
