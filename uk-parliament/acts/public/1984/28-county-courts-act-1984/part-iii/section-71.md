# County Courts Act 1984, section 71

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71. Satisfaction of judgments and orders for payment of money.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a judgment is given or an order is made by the county court[^c-971a4049e38f94ce52ff72aa04702e6d] under which a sum of money of any amount is payable, whether by way of satisfaction of the claim or counterclaim in the proceedings or by way of costs or otherwise, the court may, as it thinks fit, order the money to be paid either—

- (a) in one sum, whether forthwith or within such period as the court may fix; or
- (b) by such instalments payable at such times as the court may fix.

(2) If at any time it appears to the satisfaction of the county[^c-f66b3d8bc7475f33c82d70ef2adc007b] court that any party to any proceedings in the court[^c-802accdc70c183db2627b087ffb70f91] is unable from any cause to pay any sum recovered against him (whether by way of satisfaction of the claim or counterclaim in the proceedings or by way of costs or otherwise) or any instalment of such a sum, the court may, in its discretion, suspend or stay any judgment or order given or made in the proceedings for such time and on such terms as the court thinks fit, and so from time to time until it appears that the cause of inability has ceased.

(3)[^c-ed7760938a62ef0796b30f711855bd27] Subsections (1) and (2), so far as relating to costs, apply in relation to the family court as they apply in relation to the county court.
