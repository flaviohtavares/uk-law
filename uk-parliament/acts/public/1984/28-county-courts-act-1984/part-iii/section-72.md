# County Courts Act 1984, section 72

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 72. Set-off in cases of cross judgments in county courts and High Court.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where one person has obtained a judgment or order in the county court[^c-971a4049e38f94ce52ff72aa04702e6d] against another person, and that other person has obtained a judgment or order against the first-mentioned person in the [^c-88220cddfe8d24b338f244e4ea08cb85]... county court or in the High Court, either such person may, in accordance with rules of court, give notice in writing to the court or the several courts as the case may be, and may apply to the court or any of the said courts in accordance with rules of court for leave to set off any sums, including costs, payable under the several judgments or orders.

(2)[^c-b8d033eef332cacc1b603dfaea685872] Upon any such application, the set-off may be allowed in accordance with the practice for the time being in force in the High Court as to the allowance of set-off and in particular in relation to any solicitor’s lien for costs.

(3) Where the cross judgments or orders have not been obtained in the same court, a copy of the order made on any such application shall be sent by the proper officer of the court to which the application is made to the proper officer of the other court.
