# County Courts Act 1984, section 142

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 142. Power to enforce undertakings of solicitors.[^c4409051][^c-808998484148efa834de34c4fd6355e0]

*Extent: E+W*

The county court[^c-d33bf7b8d17db2356c2fbbe5454e1738] shall have the same power to enforce an undertaking given by a solicitor in relation to any proceedings in that court as the High Court has to enforce an undertaking so given in relation to any proceedings in the High Court.
