# County Courts Act 1984, section 135

## 135. Penalty for falsely pretending to act under authority of court.[^key-ee35855c9426a970f7655b41feb55c5b]

*Extent: E+W*

Any person who—

- (a) delivers or causes to be delivered to any other person any paper falsely purporting to be a copy of any summons or other process of the county court[^key-971a4049e38f94ce52ff72aa04702e6d] , knowing it to be false; or
- (b) acts or professes to act under any false colour or pretence of the process or authority of the county court ;

shall be guilty of an offence and shall for each offence be liable on conviction on indictment to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 7 years.
