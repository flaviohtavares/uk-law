# County Courts Act 1984, section 129

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 129. Enforcement of fines.[^c4408741]

*Extent: E+W*

Payment of any fine imposed by the county court[^c-90453defd1917aab335ea06e17385136] under this Act may be enforced upon the order of the court[^c-6671cb4c5ee3e38c2532e9d111f03279] in like manner—

- (a) as payment of a debt adjudged by the court to be paid may be enforced under this Act; or
- (b) as payment of a sum adjudged to be paid by a conviction of a magistrates’ court may be enforced under the [^c4408751]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 (disregarding section 81(1) of that Act).
