# County Courts Act 1984, section 132

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 132. Payment of salaries and expenses.

*Extent: E+W*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) all salaries, remuneration and other sums payable under Part I of this Act or under section 131.
- (b) the expenses of supplying the the county court and its[^c-db3004bcae4f0eea66c485831ca6329b] offices , and the family court and its offices,[^c-be8e83d991a3cfb3bf72fb011b2a465a] with law and office books and stationery and postage stamps;
- (c) expenses incurred in conveying to prison persons committed by the family court or[^c-9dc168efab75a1c914a1c2aa18023e50] the county court[^c-836e33e684e6a7f4a4d07d37612f2cc8] ; and
- (d) all other expenses arising out of any jurisdiction for the time being conferred on the family court or any officer of the family court or[^c-351dbb30a49252d423727addf2aa0783] on the county court or any officer of the county court.
