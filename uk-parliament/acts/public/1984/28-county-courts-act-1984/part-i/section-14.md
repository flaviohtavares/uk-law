## 14. Penalty for assaulting officers.[^c4402331]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If any person assaults an officer of the county court[^c-5126b8903c4c1f8655995aa4d1835aec] while in the execution of his duty, he shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or to a fine of an amount not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, or both; or
- (b) on an order made by the court[^c-6e27c3f7714deba421d2766173100d93] in that behalf, to be committed for a specified period not exceeding 3 months to . . . [^c4402341]prison . . . [^c4402341]or to such a fine as aforesaid, or to be so committed and to such a fine,

and an officer[^c-5bd7dafd59a8406f811b4d51233d75d1] of the court may take the offender into custody, with or without warrant, and bring him before the court.

(2) The judge may at any time revoke an order committing a person to prison under this section and, if he is already in custody, order his discharge.
