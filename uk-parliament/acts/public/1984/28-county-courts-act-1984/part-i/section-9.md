> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/28

## 9. Qualifications for appointment as district judge.[^c20595031][^c4402011]

*Extent: E+W*

No person shall be appointed a district judge[^c4402021], [^c4402031]. . . [^c20595011]. . . unless he satisfies the judicial-appointment eligibility condition on a 5-year basis.[^c20595051]
