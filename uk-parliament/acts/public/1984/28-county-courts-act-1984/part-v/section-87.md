## 87. Indorsement of amount on warrant[^c4407501][^c-a1d3d80032498689cd9dfb7c714e5f52]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In or upon every warrant of control[^c-a65deb87517b9696b7e1b83dafa3aa53] issued from the county court[^c-971a4049e38f94ce52ff72aa04702e6d] against the goods of any person, the court[^c-f251085df01adc5b1b74f056fec85277] shall cause to be inserted or indorsed the total amount to be recovered[^c-ba8610fbf0659c49e46d539a5cfe2254], inclusive of the fee for issuing the warrant but exclusive of the fees for its execution.
