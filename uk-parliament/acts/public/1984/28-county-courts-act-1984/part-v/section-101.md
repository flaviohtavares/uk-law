# County Courts Act 1984, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Interpleader by registrar.[^c4407741]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a claim is made to or in respect of any goods seized in execution under process of the county court[^key-971a4049e38f94ce52ff72aa04702e6d] , or in respect of the proceeds or value of any such goods, the court[^key-c4b4f794b697e6f3a650b2f939ffa154] may, as well before as after any action brought in respect of the claim[^key-a5241078ad18bc3f4681fd10fa170e46] , issue a summons calling before the court the party at whose instance the process issued and the party making the claim.

(2) Upon the issue of the summons, any action brought in the county court or any[^key-44998d5e57bcc07ba1b26099f125fa1f] other court in respect of the claim or of any damage arising out of the execution of the warrant shall be stayed.

(3) On the hearing of the summons, the court[^key-525d2433cd44332f1d97ab825f1356ff] shall adjudicate upon the claim, and shall also adjudicate between the parties or either of them and the person executing the warrant[^key-58f6a519d125fabd1332cfe7a2e938f6] upon any claim to damages arising or capable of arising out of the execution of the warrant [^key-b229b3c0a67fec31c43f9e93e3be17bb]..., and shall make such order in respect of any such claim and the costs of the proceedings as he thinks fit.

(4)[^key-af23ef757690f95312348d66771cb5b2] This section does not apply in the case of goods seized under Schedule 12 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007.
