# County Courts Act 1984, section 85

## 85. Execution of judgments or orders for payment of money.[^key-2828d7799743e9ca09547c63f9ba8dd0][^key-a5152b0530864d08a75761aa4dfaf5e6][^key-a5d22ccde46960281befc4d47daace3d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c4407411] Subject to article 8 of the High Court and County Courts Jurisdiction Order 1991,[^c4407421]any sum of money payable under a judgment or order of the county court[^key-971a4049e38f94ce52ff72aa04702e6d] may be recovered, in case of default or failure of payment, forthwith or at the time or times and in the manner thereby directed, under a warrant under subsection (2).[^key-24d2ac89c7b2137a8c88bd8ab04152fa]

(2)[^c4407411] A judge of the county court,[^key-77cee60d110676f81b5a4d0a841e46a2] on the application of the party prosecuting any such judgment or order, shall issue a warrant of control[^key-84b8fef59676fa0b64fed2ccfc354e47] whereby any person authorised by or on behalf of the Lord Chancellor is[^key-84624828433bc0c7d486b1dd33926c62] empowered to use the procedure in Schedule 12 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (taking control of goods) to recover the money payable under the judgment or order.[^key-d49284f14b403f65c74ced260f57865b]

(2A)[^key-500b2eaa1a75a5890b25334ae9d7f2cf] The person to whom a warrant under subsection (2) must be directed is to be determined in accordance with arrangements made by a person authorised by or on behalf of the Lord Chancellor.

(3)[^key-ba5c3d99170a2058e074ec61a2882c77][^c4407411] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c4407411] It shall be the duty of every constable within his jurisdiction to assist in the execution of every such warrant.
