# County Courts Act 1984, section 92

## 92. Penalty for rescuing goods seized.[^c4407621]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If any person rescues or attempts to rescue any goods seized in execution under process of the county court[^c-971a4049e38f94ce52ff72aa04702e6d] , he shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one month or to a fine of an amount not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale, or both; or
- (b) on an order made by the county court[^c-43dc11b464cd61313aa1efb7c5e26dcc] in that behalf, to be committed for a specified period not exceeding one month to . . . [^c4407631] prison . . . [^c4407631]or to a fine of an amount not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale or to be so committed and to such a fine.

and an officer[^c-8a427c8452f5ac4914f677ca9462ff77] of the court may take the offender into custody, with or without warrant, and bring him before the county court .

(2) A judge of the county court[^c-64771e57032807c98683b8f2d914254d] may at any time revoke an order committing a person to prison under this section and, if he is already in custody, order his discharge.

(3)[^c-3599475fe6a9d4b4817ae8af458beca3] This section does not apply in the case of goods seized under Schedule 12 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007.
