(9) The county court does not[^c-c5578313f2270c9bf305821778aec496] have jurisdiction to determine any claim or question certified by the Secretary of State to be a claim or question which, under the Rhine Navigation Convention, falls to be determined in accordance with the provisions of that Convention; and any proceedings to enforce such a claim which are commenced in the county court[^c-110865f86e602c9ae0e3c0bed5a32757] shall be set aside.

(10) In subsection (9) “**the Rhine Navigation Convention**” means the Convention of the 7th October 1868 as revised by any subsequent Convention.
