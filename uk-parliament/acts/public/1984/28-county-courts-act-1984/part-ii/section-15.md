## 15. General jurisdiction in actions of contract and tort.[^c4402421]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the county court[^c-ce2b235f94190414bec0448041c628c8] shall have jurisdiction to hear and determine any action founded on contract or tort [^c4402431]....

(2) The county court[^c-ac9845c10614c1a3005422e42a93a89c] shall not, except as in this Act provided, have jurisdiction to hear and determine—

- (a)[^c4402441] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) any action in which the title to [^c4402451]... any toll, fair, market or franchise is in question; or
- (c) any action for libel or slander.

(3)[^c4402461] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
