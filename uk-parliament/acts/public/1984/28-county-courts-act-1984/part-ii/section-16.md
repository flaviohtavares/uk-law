## 16. Money recoverable by statute.[^c4402471]

*Extent: E+W*

The county court[^c-ac9845c10614c1a3005422e42a93a89c] shall have jurisdiction to hear and determine an action for the recovery of a sum recoverable by virtue of any enactment for the time being in force, if—

- (a) it is not provided by that or any other enactment that such sums shall only be recoverable in the High Court or shall only be recoverable summarily; [^c4402481] . . .
