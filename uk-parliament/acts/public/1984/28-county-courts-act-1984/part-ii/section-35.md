## 35. Division of causes of action.[^c-c6348a4164870691791de0a5dee91284]

*Extent: E+W*

It shall not be lawful for any plaintiff to divide any cause of action for the purpose of bringing two or more actions the county court[^c-66a3cb7d1938dda163aae449e5d6e9b5].
