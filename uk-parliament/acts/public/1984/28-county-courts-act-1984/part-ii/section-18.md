# County Courts Act 1984, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Jurisdiction by agreement in certain actions.

*Extent: E+W*

If the parties to any action, other than an action which, if commenced in the High Court, would have been assigned to the Business and Property Division[^key-fba15ba5a2091cf3999d4eb4a45fd770] or to the Family Division or have involved the exercise of the High Court’s Admiralty jurisdiction, agree, by a memorandum signed by them or by their respective legal representatives[^c4402521], that the county court[^key-ce2b235f94190414bec0448041c628c8] [^key-55e11cf8436445d39ecf777eba01a088]... shall have jurisdiction in the action, that court shall have jurisdiction to hear and determine the action accordingly.
