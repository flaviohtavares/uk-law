# County Courts Act 1984, section 83

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/28

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Stay of proceedings in case of certiorari or prohibition.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The grant by the High Court of leave to make an application for an order of certiorari or prohibition to the county court[^c-971a4049e38f94ce52ff72aa04702e6d] shall, if the High Court so directs, operate as a stay of the proceedings in question until the determination of the application, or until the High Court otherwise orders.

(2) Where any proceedings are so stayed, [^c-3247a5466bb7abb015e28d8f8c8e81f0]... the county court shall from time to time adjourn the hearing of the proceedings to such day as the court[^c-ecacc9d10d7b33215334f00a1cb818f2] thinks fit.
