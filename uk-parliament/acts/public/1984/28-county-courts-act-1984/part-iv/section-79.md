## 79. Agreement not to appeal.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) No appeal shall lie from any judgment, direction, decision or order of a judge of the county court[^key-cd34f44005fb987c1f16714f6cdbea7a] if, before the judgment, direction, decision or order is given or made, the parties agree, in writing signed by themselves or their legal representatives[^c4407381] or agents, that it shall be final.
