## 113. Notice of order and proof of debts.

*Extent: E+W*

Where an administration order has been made—

- (a) notice of the order—
  - (i) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c4408411]
  - (ii) shall be posted on an appropriate website[^key-a1fdc08f25ea3b37945554418cce5490] , and
  - (iii) shall be sent to every person whose name the debtor has notified to the county court[^key-157f83c17fe537f28bcb53965deb628e] as being a creditor of his or who has proved;
- (b) any creditor of the debtor, on proof of his debt before the county court[^key-04bccc1e39bc17f0a0d9936af559a2eb] , shall be entitled to be scheduled as a creditor of the debtor for the amount of his proof;
- (c) any creditor may object in the prescribed manner to any debt scheduled, or to the manner in which payment is directed to be made by instalments;
- (d) any person who, after the date of the order, becomes a creditor of the debtor shall, on proof of his debt before the county court[^key-3067c37826c908dbd56c6c77590af7f9] , be scheduled as a creditor of the debtor for the amount of his proof, but shall not be entitled to any dividend under the order until the creditors who are scheduled as having been creditors before the date of the order have been paid to the extent provided by the order.
