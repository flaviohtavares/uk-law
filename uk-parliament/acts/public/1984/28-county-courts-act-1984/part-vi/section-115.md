# County Courts Act 1984, section 115

## 115. Execution by registrar.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where it appears to [^c-2a7a95da2a1b6a40961f791c487dd974]... the county court[^c-157f83c17fe537f28bcb53965deb628e] at any time while an administration order is in force that property of the debtor exceeds in value the minimum amount[^c4408421], the court[^c-8db26aa177d3866cbe417bbfa4959618] shall, at the request of any creditor, and without fee, issue execution against the debtor’s goods.

(1A)[^c4408431] In subsection (1) above “**the minimum amount**” means £50 or such other amount as the Lord Chancellor may by order specify instead of that amount or the amount for the time being specified in such an order; and an order under this subsection shall be made by statutory instrument subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(2) Section 89 applies on an execution under this section as it applies on an execution under Part V.
