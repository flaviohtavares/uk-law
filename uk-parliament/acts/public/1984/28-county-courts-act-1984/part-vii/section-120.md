# County Courts Act 1984, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/28

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Prisons to which committals may be made.

*Extent: E+W*

Any person committed to prison by the [^c-a182b543861a34d6d7f0624ca006dc8e]... county court, in pursuance of this or any other Act or of rules of court[^c4408601], shall be committed to such prison as may from time to time be directed in the case of that court by order of the Secretary of State.
