## 14. Application of s. 13 to aerodromes.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf] In relation to aerodromes for the time being vested in or under the control of the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf] [^key-e649fd678dcce14f8e695358b64f808f] ... [^c8861981]. . . , and in relation to persons and aircraft arriving at or departing from any such aerodromes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

- (a) subsection (4) of section 13 above shall have effect as if, for paragraph (a) of that subsection, there were substituted the following paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

  > - (a) may provide for their enforcement and execution by officers designated for that purpose by the Secretary of State, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

  ,
- (b) so much of that subsection as requires the consent of the Commissioners of Customs and Excise shall not apply in relation to aerodromes for the time being vested in or under the control of the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf] [^key-184dad286ecb76735c2c21d9db082123]..., and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]
- (c) subsection (5) of that section shall have effect as if, for paragraph (a) of that subsection, there were substituted the following paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

  > - (a) officers designated in accordance with subsection (4)(a) above (as modified by section 14(1)(a) below, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

  .

(2) In this section, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf] **aerodrome *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]** ” has the meaning given by section 105(1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf] [^c8861991] Civil Aviation Act 1982. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]
