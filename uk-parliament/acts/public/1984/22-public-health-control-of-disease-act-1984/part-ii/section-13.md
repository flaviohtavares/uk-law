# Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-11-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Regulations for control of certain diseases.[^c8861941][^M_C_8f4e1260-02e2-4065-f228-f115865a1a33][^c-0baaabba8a03c6cead01c6028d1eac62]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf] Subject to the provisions of this section, the Secretary of State may, as respects the whole or any part of England and Wales, including coastal waters, make regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

- (a) with a view to the treatment of persons affected with any epidemic, endemic or infectious disease and for preventing the spread of such diseases, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]
- (b) for preventing danger to public health from vessels or aircraft arriving at any place, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]
- (c) for preventing the spread of infection by means of any vessel or aircraft leaving any place, so far as may be necessary or expedient for the purpose of carrying out any treaty, convention, arrangement or engagement with any other country. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1) above, the Secretary of State may by any such regulations apply, with or without modifications, to any disease to which the regulations relate any enactment (including any enactment in this Act) relating to the notification of disease or to notifiable diseases. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

(3) Regulations made under this section may provide for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

- (a) the signals to be displayed by vessels or aircraft having on board any case of epidemic, endemic or infectious disease, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]
- (b) the questions to be answered by masters, pilots and other persons on board any vessel or aircraft as to cases of such disease on board during the voyage or on arrival, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]
- (c) requiring persons alighting from aircraft to answer questions pertaining to their state of health or their contact with infection, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]
- (d) the detention of vessels or aircraft and of persons on board them, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]
- (e) the duties to be performed in cases of such diseases by masters, pilots and other persons on board vessels or aircraft, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

and may authorise the making of charges and provide for the recovery of such charges and of any expenses incurred in disinfection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

(4) Subject to section 14 below, regulations made under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

- (a) shall specify the authorities, whether county councils, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf] county borough councils,[^c8861951] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf] local authorities, port health authorities, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf] [^c-4521c008dc83ef2cc9e4986de6bd5744]... Local Health Boards[^c-9c0f0fdb920cc23c0d2671969872ffe3][^c8861961] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf], Special Health Authorities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf] [^c-8ee47b68a0eaf1aaa21837aeab88ce7d]... , National Health Service trusts or[^c-d5f33779e7da91de25764bc16331a7d4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf] NHS *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf] foundation trusts *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf], by whom they are to be enforced and executed, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]
- (b) may also provide for their enforcement and execution by officers of customs and excise, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

but, in so far as they apply to officers of customs and excise, the regulations shall require the consent of the Commissioners of Customs and Excise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

(5) Subject to section 14 below, the following persons, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

- (a) authorised officers of any such authority as is specified by the regulations in accordance with subsection (4)(a) above, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]
- (b) officers of customs and excise, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

shall have power to enter any premises, vessel or aircraft for the purpose of executing, or superintending the execution of, regulations under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

(6) The power to make regulations under this section shall be exercisable by statutory instrument, which shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]

(7) Any expenses incurred by a county council under this section shall, if the Secretary of State by order so directs, be defrayed as special expenses charged on such part of the county as may be provided by the order, but such an order may be revoked or varied by a subsequent order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5fe1e202e7ec46cbe1679755f539bebf]
