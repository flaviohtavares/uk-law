## 78. Amendments and repeals.

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to section 77 above—

- (a) the Acts specified in Schedule 2 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule, and
- (b) the Acts specified in Schedule 3 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
