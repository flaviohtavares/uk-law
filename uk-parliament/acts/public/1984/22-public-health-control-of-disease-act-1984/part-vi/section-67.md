## 67. Applications to, and appeals from magistrates’ courts.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where this Act or a provision contained in regulations made under this Act[^key-b0ed229f88ad517e58f59a6c48cb7460] provides for any matter to be determined by, or for an application in respect of a matter to be made to, a magistrates’ court, the procedure shall be by way of complaint for an order.

(2) Where a person aggrieved by any order, determination or other decision of a magistrates’ court under a relevant provision of this Act is not by any other enactment authorised to appeal to the Crown Court, he may appeal to the Crown Court.
