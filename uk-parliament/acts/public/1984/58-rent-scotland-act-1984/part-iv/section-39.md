# Rent (Scotland) Act 1984, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/58

## 39. Rectification of rent books in light of determina-tion of recoverable rent.

*Extent: S*

Where, in any proceedings, the recoverable rent of a dwelling-house subject to a regulated tenancy is determined by the First-tier Tribunal[^key-05c5f1d8c30ef2453d4cf8f9868fd74f], then, on the application of the tenant (whether in those or in any subsequent proceedings) the Tribunal[^key-76c5c512c7e6856da2d82b22872d70c8] may call for the production of the rent book or any similar document relating to the dwelling-house and may order the correction of[^key-23fc3ae574f720920fa7243eb966a3f0] any entries showing, or purporting to show, the tenant as being in arrears in respect of any sum which the Tribunal has determined to be irrecoverable.
