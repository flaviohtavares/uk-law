## 59. Increase of rent without notice to quit.[^c982251][^c4469861]

*Extent: S*

Sections 212 and 213 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987[^c982261] (increase of rents of houses belonging to certain authorities without notice of removal) shall apply to a housing association such as is referred to in section 5(4) above or the Housing Corporation as they apply to any authority to which that section applies, . . . [^c982271].
