# Rent (Scotland) Act 1984, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/58
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 55. Tenancies to which sections 55 to 59 apply.[^c982001][^c4469721]

*Extent: S*

This section and sections 56 to 59 below apply to a tenancy where—

- (a) the interest of the landlord under that tenancy belongs to a housing association or to the Housing Corporation, and
- (b) the tenancy would be a protected tenancy but for section 1(1)(a) or 5 above but do not apply, and shall be deemed never to have applied, to a tenancy which is a lease under a shared ownership agreement within the meaning of[^c982011] section 83(3) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (asp 10)[^c982021].,

and in this section and the said sections 56 to 59 “**tenancy**” means, unless the context otherwise requires, a tenancy to which those sections apply.
