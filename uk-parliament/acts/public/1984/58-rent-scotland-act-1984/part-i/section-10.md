# Rent (Scotland) Act 1984, section 10

## 10. Premises with business use.

*Extent: S*

(1) A tenancy of a dwelling-house which consists of or comprises premises which, by virtue of a premises licence issued under the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005, are licensed for the sale of alcohol (within the meaning of section 2 of that Act) for consumption on the premises[^key-8e9ac53de8f1583fa6534c7b64dcafab] shall not be a protected tenancy, nor shall such a dwelling-house be the subject of a statutory tenancy.

(2) A tenancy shall not be a regulated tenancy if it is a tenancy to which the [^c980911]Tenancy of Shops (Scotland) Act 1949 applies (but this provision is without prejudice to the application of any other provision of this Act to a sub-tenancy of any part of the premises comprised in such a tenancy).
