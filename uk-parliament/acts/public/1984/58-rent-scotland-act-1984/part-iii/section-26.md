## 26. Application to Crown.

*Extent: S*

In so far as this Part of this Act requires the taking of proceedings in the First-tier Tribunal[^key-831314f5d877770046a762ad6845d0d3] for the recovery of possession or confers any powers on the Tribunal, it shall be binding on the Crown.
