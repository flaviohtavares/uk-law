# Rent (Scotland) Act 1984, section 102

## 102. Jurisdiction.

*Extent: S*

(A1)[^key-f40af368b1bdc68890df5d5b9bd84914] The First-tier Tribunal has jurisdiction, either in the course of any proceedings relating to a dwelling-house or on an application made for the purpose by the landlord or the tenant, to determine any question as to the application of this Act (other than Part IX) or as to any matter which is or may become material for determining any such question.

(1) The sheriff shall have jurisdiction, either in the course of any proceedings relating to a dwelling-house or on an application made for the purpose by the landlord or the tenant, to determine any question as to the application of Part IX of[^key-e4b950156232ccfce628fea00b44236f] this Act or as to any matter which is or may become material for determining any such question.

(2)[^key-e1fa7dda4a4e7251735f6abe2dbbf08a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) If under any provision of this Act a person takes proceedings in the Court of Session which he could have taken before the First-tier Tribunal[^key-aecde97dc99be32b0c15df55a1d22bcd], he shall not be entitled to recover any expenses.
