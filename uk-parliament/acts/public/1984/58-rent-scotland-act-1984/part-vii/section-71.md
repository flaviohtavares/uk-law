## 71. Notice to quit served after reference of contract to the First-tier Tribunal.[^key-4f8ffd175b2af4713db2791c0c672d21]

*Extent: S*

(1) If, after a Part VII contract has been referred to the First-tier Tribunal[^key-50da9308ade4f85423ceb6844b6eb24e] by the lessee . . . [^c982581] under section 65 or 68 above, a notice to quit the dwelling-house to which the contract relates is served by the lessor on the lessee at any time before the decision of the First-tier Tribunal[^key-f8dff199c55b46f7a035f81934a77311] is given or within the period of six months thereafter, then, subject to subsection (2) and sections 73 and 74 below, the notice shall not take effect before the expiry of that period.

(2) In a case falling within subsection (1) above,—

- (a) the First-tier Tribunal may, if it thinks[^key-267368902bd4bdb28f2bc59f0b23a464] fit, direct that a shorter period shall be substituted for the period of six months specified in that subsection; and
- (b) if the reference to the First-tier Tribunal[^key-65bee3e8e3be571754e8cfc3baaf6564] is withdrawn, the period during which the notice to quit is not to take effect shall end on the expiry of seven days from the withdrawal of the reference.
