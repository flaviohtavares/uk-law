## 68. Reconsideration of rent after registration.

*Extent: S*

Where the rent payable for any dwelling-house has been entered in the register under section 67 above then, subject to section 66(4) above, the lessor or the lessee . . . [^c982551] may refer the case to the First-tier Tribunal[^c-c01b31a6c643a10c293e71be75655da0] for reconsideration of the rent so entered.
