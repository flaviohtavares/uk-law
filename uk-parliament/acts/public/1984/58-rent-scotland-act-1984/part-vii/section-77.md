## 77. Jurisdiction of the First-tier Tribunal.[^c-5f034f058da6715ce47f525cef40d05f]

*Extent: S*

Where a Part VII contract is referred to the First-tier Tribunal[^c-cf8d2132339b239c66b4dc51db955b29] under this Part of this Act and—

- (a) the contract relates to a dwelling-house consisting of or comprising part only of lands and heritages, and
- (b) no apportionment of the rateable value of the lands and heritages has been made under section 7 above,

then, unless the lessor in the course of the proceedings requires that such an apportionment shall be made and, within two weeks of making the requirement, brings proceedings in the First-tier Tribunal[^c-6782e61989f1c331ad7cc4cc6f50155e] for the making of the apportionment, the First-tier Tribunal[^c-4e950cc9d955f612159bb8d871d92e93] shall have jurisdiction to deal with the reference if it appears to it[^c-554bd8408d88e3521cfcbd47bd2b1a19] that, had the apportionment been made, it would have had jurisdiction.
