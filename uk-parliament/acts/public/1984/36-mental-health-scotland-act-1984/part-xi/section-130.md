# Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1984, section 130

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2005-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 130. Short title and commencement.[^key-0a67c56d28da55a6f047fc2edb64008b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Act may be cited as the Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1984 and shall come into force on 30th September 1984.
