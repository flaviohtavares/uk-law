# Dentists Act 1984, section 36E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/24

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36E. Rules relating to the dental care professionals register[^key-1d96b1e0984e692736dfc03f35ac8d7a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-29bc6399c6c10ddede3ba468e540a087] Rules may make provision relating to—

- (a) the form of an application by a person seeking registration under a title in[^key-faa46c7fa04830b4043ec87622c4c058] [^key-a709e08f25b33a504c8e0dd27f643d57]... the dental care professionals register;
- (b) the manner in which an application under paragraph (a) is to be made;
- (c) the evidence and information which an applicant must supply in support of an application under paragraph (a) (other than evidence and information falling within section 36CA(1))[^key-b2b9cc08641c76471100f2fcfedbdce6];
- (d) the details which must be entered in the dental care professionals register; and
- (e) the procedure for the making, alteration and deletion of entries in that register.

(2)[^key-96f8dd638e5068f2ba54622b6e44025f] Subsection (1) is subject to section 36EA*.*
