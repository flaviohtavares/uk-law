# Dentists Act 1984, section 36OA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/24

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36OA. Delegation of the Investigating Committee’s functions under section 36O[^c-e6610645b98e6f7bddd4a299dd6cbb78][^c-ab4407b8a03d50859e6c0a0648ea6539]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Rules may make provision for one or more of the following—

- (a) the registrar;
- (b) any other officer of the Council,

to exercise the functions of the Investigating Committee under section 36O, whether generally or in relation to such classes of case as may be specified in the rules.
