# Dentists Act 1984, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Prohibition on practice of dentistry by laymen.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who is not[^c-958e154c5b1f3ce74f7a7e2841a8282e] a registered dentist or a registered dental care professional[^c-e8555ecf570e6a8790fa9f0b26c2c534] shall not practise or hold himself out, whether directly or by implication, as practising or as being prepared to practise dentistry.

(1A)[^c-fbd27c6903f3e6f19a4d610debbbad88] A specified state professional undertaking an adaptation period specified in relation to the person by the Council under section 15ZA or 36C(4E) does not contravene subsection (1) by practising dentistry or by holding out as regards practising or being prepared to practise dentistry if the practising of dentistry is done as part of the adaptation period or (as the case may be) the holding out relates only to the practising of dentistry as part of the adaptation period.

(2) Any person who acts in contravention of subsection (1) above shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding the fifth level on the standard scale.

(3) Summary proceedings for an offence under this section may be brought within the period of six months beginning with the date on which evidence sufficient in the opinion of the prosecutor to warrant the proceedings came to his knowledge; but no such proceedings shall be brought by virtue of this subsection more than two years after the commission of the offence.

(4) For the purposes of subsection (3) above a certificate signed by or on behalf of the prosecutor and stating the date on which such evidence as is mentioned in that subsection came to his knowledge shall be conclusive evidence of that date, and any certificate purporting to be so signed shall be taken to have been so signed unless the contrary is proved.
