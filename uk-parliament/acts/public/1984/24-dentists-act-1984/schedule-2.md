## SCHEDULE 2 — European Dental Qualifications

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 15(2).

### Part I — Appropriate European Diplomas

**1.**[^M_F_088bad80-9605-4a49-8db5-1fcda5cce71b] (1)[^M_F_e351934e-5877-4516-a6e7-e036d209ffa9] In this Schedule—

- [^c-63b50c08511e658f1ff96867d5f05073]...
- “the reference date”, in relation to a relevant European State, means the date specified in relation to that State in the column entitled “Reference date” in Annex V, point 5.3.2 of the Directive.

(2)[^M_F_8fc54af8-20de-4805-abc7-932b92fd81f5] References in this Schedule to article 34 of the Directive are, in relation to training begun on or before 18 January 2016, to article 34 disregarding the amendments made by [Directive 2013/55/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2013/0055) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 November 2013.

**2.**[^c10222991] Subject to paragraph 3[^c-d41c39bf07fe840d32fdf8b2f399bc92] below, a[^c-36c9f6195cf3bd289440ef898870417f] diploma in dentistry granted in a relevant European State[^M_F_d7e778f5-f5e0-4e16-d5ec-ae7639e5767e] is an appropriate European diploma for the purposes of section 15(2ZA)[^c-0db4cd6614fba8c1edf56b95c125b723] of this Act if it[^c-a3f458a02de7c6fdea244e5c82816515][^M_F_31bc68cb-56ff-4014-99ba-b351593dc3e8] is listed in Annex V, point 5.3.2 of the Directive and[^c-81ab9a2c94733247d71d8aa85f8adac6], it is accompanied, where appropriate, by the certificate listed in relation to that State in the column of Annex V, point 5.3.2 of the Directive entitled “Certificate accompanying the evidence of qualifications”.

**3.**[^c-d3d58d8491e03ed09aa6e4e8d36e402b] A diploma listed in Annex V, point 5.3.2 of the Directive granted in a relevant European State before the reference date, or on or after that date where training of which the diploma is evidence was commenced by the holder before that date, is not an appropriate European diploma for the purposes of section 15(2ZA)[^c-82e16aa7bf2546e335c2e886883c5398][^c-1f4b675ce0953dd00a71764e9025ea02]...
