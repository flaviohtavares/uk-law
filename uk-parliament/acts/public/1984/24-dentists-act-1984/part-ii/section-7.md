# Dentists Act 1984, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Effect of grant of degree or licence in dentistry.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A degree or licence in dentistry granted by a dental authority shall not confer any right or title to be registered under the [^c10221451]Medical Act 1983, nor to assume any name, title or designation implying that the holder of the degree or licence is by law recognised as a practitioner or licentiate in medicine or general surgery.
