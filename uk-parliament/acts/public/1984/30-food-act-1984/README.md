# Food Act 1984

> Text as at 2025-08-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W*

Food Act 1984

1984 c. 30

[^M_F_eec3906e-2c0d-4c58-bcc2-862178c5942e]An Act to consolidate the provisions of the Food and Drugs Acts 1955 to 1982, the Sugar Act 1956, the Food and Drugs (Milk) Act 1970, section 7(3) and (4) of the European Communities Act 1972, section 198 of the Local Government Act 1972 and Part IX of the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1982, and connected provisions.

\[26th June 1984\]

[^c1022365][^c1022366][^c1022367][^c1022368][^c1022369][^c1022370][^c1022371]Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—
