# Food Act 1984, section 69

> Text as at 2025-08-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Crop price.[^c1022461][^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840] If as regards the home-grown beet crop for any year it is made to appear to the Ministers— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840] by the processors of home-grown beet, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840] by a body which is in their opinion substantially representative of the groers of home-grown beet, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840]

that the processors and that body are unable to agree on the prices and other terms and conditions for the purchase of home-grown beet by the processors, the Ministers may determine or designate a person to determine those prices, terms and conditions. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840] Any purchase by processors for which prices, terms and conditions have been so determined, or contract for such a purchase, shall take effect as a purchase or contract for purchase at those prices and on those terms and conditions. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840] In this section *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840] and section 69A[^c1022462] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840] -“home-grown beet” means sugar beet grown in Great Britain; and *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-cdfa90c4d18a2d1e2f576799bac055bd] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840] “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840]**the Ministers *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840]**” means the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f1447caa-c0a5-4742-e19e-f0d9ca791840]
