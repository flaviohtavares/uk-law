# Food Act 1984, section 121

> Text as at 2025-08-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 121. Byelaws.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The confirming authority in respect of byelaws made under this Act by a local authority in England[^c-ff7a9784b6a9aa935417c795ba2638f2] is the Secretary of State.

(1A)[^c-0d7a66f4761a2263e9daa6090e7ce0fb] Subsection (1) does not apply to byelaws of a class prescribed by regulations under section 236A(1) of the Local Government Act 1972.
