# Building Act 1984, section 134

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 134. Commencement.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c929281] The following provisions of this Act—

- (a) sections 12, 13, 31, 38, 42(4) to (6) and 43(3), except so far as they enable regulations to be made,
- (b) sections 20, 33, 42(1) to (3), 43(1) and (2), 44 and 45,
- (c) section 133(2) and Schedule 7 so far as they relate to [^c929291]. . . the [^c929301]Atomic Energy Authority Act 1954, and
- (d) section 50(2) and (3) and paragraph 9 of Schedule 1,

come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint.

(1A)[^c-5ebb4a251b780eb1e809d9b073968839] Except so far as relating to the provisions listed in subsection (1B), the reference in subsection (1) to the Secretary of State is to be read, in relation to Wales, as a reference to the Welsh Ministers.

(1B) The provisions mentioned in subsection (1A) are sections 38, 44, 45 and 133(2) and Schedule 7.

(2) Subject to—

- (a) subsection (1) above, and
- (b) sections [^c929291]. . . 42(7) above,

this Act comes into force on 1st December 1984.
