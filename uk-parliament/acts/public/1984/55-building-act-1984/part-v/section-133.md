## 133. Consequential amendments and repeals.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The enactments specified in Schedule 6 to this Act have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule.

(2) The enactments specified in Schedule 7 to this Act are repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
