# Building Act 1984, section 56

## 56. Recording and furnishing of information.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Every local authority in Wales[^key-635ab34a63dcd3f046a17b7e0076361d] shall keep, in such manner as may be prescribed, a register containing such information as may be prescribed with respect to initial notices amendment notices, notices under section 51C above,[^c927351], public body’s notices , transfer reports[^key-f63c09a8c4316121427c146cb8bbea57] and certificates given to them, including information (where applicable)[^c927361] as to whether such notices , reports[^key-599adea213338c53e7040d17f07af632] or certificates have been accepted or rejected.

(2)[^key-e81fc58fc7d96d59c36e519a1a7aa8ac] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The reference in subsection (1) above to certificates is a reference to plans certificates, final certificates , transfer certificates[^key-29147efaeccf7ef55787f3d64bc5291d] and public body’s final certificates.[^key-ff6f00494f2f3ba63bd4cc6591e4d10f]

(4) Every register kept under this section by a local authority in Wales[^key-1b187b414333122267734e3779406352] shall be available for inspection by the public at all reasonable hours.

(5) Where an initial notice or a public body’s notice has continued in force for any period, the local authority by whom it was accepted may require the registered building control approver[^key-18790d1fcd19b78292725f6dafa84ab9] or public body by whom it was given to furnish them with any information that—

- (a) they would have obtained themselves if during that period their function of enforcing building regulations had continued to be exercisable in relation to the work to which the notice relates[^c927391], and
- (b) they require for the purpose of performing their duty under section 230 of the [^c927401]Local Government Act 1972 (reports and returns),

and that section shall have effect as if during that period that function had continued to be so exercisable.
