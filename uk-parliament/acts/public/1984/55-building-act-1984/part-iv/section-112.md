# Building Act 1984, section 112

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 112. Obstruction

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-1d7d957b29b3386b7264e752ab160950] A person who wilfully obstructs a person acting in the execution of this Act, or of building regulations, or of an order or warrant made or issued under this Act, is in a case for which no other provision is made by this Act, liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3[^c-74d87fb470793c0fd55f24c81abb7e99] on the standard scale.

(2)[^c-0a4a0ce4d81d20518f63f2f2a6412b95] Subsection (1) does not apply where the person obstructed is an authorised officer (within the meaning of section 22 of the Building Safety Act 2022).
