# Building Act 1984, section 98

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 98. Power to require occupier to permit work.

*Extent: E+W*

If, on an application or (as the case may be) complaint[^key-4d722f8dffb77cab3376af6cf6dbadec] made by the owner of premises, it appears to the appropriate court or tribunal[^key-497a3b80462b7df3ee04785e91710bed] that the occupier of those premises prevents the owner from executing any work that he is by or under this Act required to execute, the court or tribunal[^key-8d0a08e635e6076d0a48930894441ce5] may order the occupier to permit the execution of the work.
