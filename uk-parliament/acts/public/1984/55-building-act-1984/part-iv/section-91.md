# Building Act 1984, section 91

## 91. Duties of local authorities and the regulator.[^key-398f569c336745e9c0fca1ca694c38a2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It is the duty of local authorities to carry this Act into execution in their areas, subject to—

- (za)[^key-28d8d6d0fe32b014088d369a3a8c88ab] subsection (3) of this section and regulations under section 91ZD,
- (a) the provisions of this Act relating to certain other authorities or persons,
- (b) the provisions of Part I of the [^c928421]Public Health Act 1936 relating to united districts and joint boards,
- (c) section 151 of the [^c928431]Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 (urban development areas), and
- (d) section 1(3) of the [^c928441]Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984 (port health authorities).

(2) It is the function of local authorities to enforce building regulations in their areas, subject to —[^key-50f7d308c18dd184da6c6e0c6dc45600]

- (a) subsection (3) of this section,
- (b) sections 5(3), 48(1) and 53(2), and
- (c) regulations under section 91ZD.

(3)[^key-03ccae2a97d57c89f7b8d9630627780a] In cases where section 91ZA or 91ZB provides that the regulator is the building control authority, it is the duty of the regulator (and not the local authority in question)—

- (a) to carry Part 1 of this Act into execution, and
- (b) to enforce building regulations.
