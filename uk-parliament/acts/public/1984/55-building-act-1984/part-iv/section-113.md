# Building Act 1984, section 113

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 113. Prosecution of offences.

*Extent: E+W*

Proceedings in respect of an offence created by or under this Act shall not, without the written consent of the Attorney General, be taken by any person other than—

- (a) a party aggrieved, or
- (b) a local authority , the regulator, the Welsh Ministers, the Counsel General to the Welsh Government[^c-db3302d2daa155c37d6fd23106e918e9] or a body whose function it is to enforce the provision in question.
