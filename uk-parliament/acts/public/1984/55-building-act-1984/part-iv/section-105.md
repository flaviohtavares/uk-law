# Building Act 1984, section 105

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/55

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 105. Judge not disqualified by liability to rates.

*Extent: E+W*

A judge of a court or tribunal[^c-f6e8f321e4f97388958d9641a4cbf97c] or a justice of the peace is not disqualified from acting in cases arising under this Act by reason only of his being, as one of several ratepayers, or as one of any other class of persons, liable in common with the others to contribute to, or be benefited by, a rate or fund out of which expenses of a local authority are to be defrayed.
