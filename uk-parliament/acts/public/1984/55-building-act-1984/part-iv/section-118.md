## 118. Variations or revocation of order transferring powers.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the appropriate national authority *(Extent: E)*[^c-167619800e35f9f3e8a3858670eb743f] has made a transfer order under section 116 *(Extent: E)*[^c-3e71eaf02e6ab99babfdf9ab5c6b28ab] above, it *(Extent: E)*[^c-c336af3ab70e1bd6f665bd37f7cbf03e] may at any time by a subsequent order vary or revoke that order, but without prejudice to the validity of anything previously done.

(1A)[^c-ba63ad51efe02b9419e1715644c2763d] The Secretary of State must consult the regulator before making an order under subsection (1).

(2) Where an order is so revoked, the appropriate national authority *(Extent: E)*[^c-6b15cf9db7d802985896fafc6be95cb4] may, either by the revoking order or by a subsequent order, make such provision as appears to it *(Extent: E)*[^c-b10ab0f0e914fd3d48f96086d85b49dc] to be desirable with respect to the transfer, vesting and discharge of any property or liabilities acquired or incurred , by the person to whom the revoked order transferred functions, in discharging those functions. *(Extent: E)*[^c-d07116366c1322d114346bd3aeef8b38]
