## 103. Procedure on appeal or application to appropriate court or tribunal.[^c-560842d7541e5c6c27b3f43b61aa448b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where this Act provides—

- (a) for an appeal to a magistrates’ court against a requirement, refusal or other decision of a local authority, or
- (b) for a matter to be determined by, or for an application in respect of a matter to be made to, a magistrates’ court,

the procedure shall be by way of complaint for an order.

(1A)[^c-8b513ecffc33f0366e6b4b65f4fc1cda] Subsections (2) and (3) apply—

- (a) where this Act provides for an appeal to the tribunal against a requirement, refusal or other decision of a relevant authority, and
- (b) in relation to an appeal of a kind mentioned in subsection (1)(a).

(2) The time within which such an appeal may be brought is 21 days from the date on which notice of the relevant[^c-6f73bdf518103ae13dda1417d4d29d6a] authority’s requirement, refusal or other decision was served upon the person desiring to appeal, and for the purposes of this subsection the making of the application or[^c-d593d3da093ae3d44dc936a21aea71f9] complaint is deemed to be the bringing of the appeal.

(3) In a case where such an appeal lies, the document notifying to the person concerned the relevant[^c-a11b278a5a74d688247089a348d29aa8] authority’s decision in the matter shall state the right of appeal to the appropriate court or tribunal[^c-e581f1a84e69a82fae36276b43f2cd3a] and the time within such an appeal may be brought.

(4)[^c-1a753d6f435abb6b082540af92c41928] In this section “**relevant authority**” means a local authority or the regulator.
