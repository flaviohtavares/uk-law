# Building Act 1984, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 120. Orders.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The power to make an order under section 134(1)[^key-d25239d8b62eed93ae93a32b314f710a] is exercisable by statutory instrument, and different days may be appointed by such an order for different provisions or for different purposes.

(2) An order under section [^key-4cd631bd47e66db9c4dc0e86d21dde84]... 134(1) (a), (b) or (c) below may contain such transitional provisions and savings as appear to the Secretary of State or Welsh Ministers[^key-b1ce6a6829c334615868474b9ef68356] to be necessary or expedient in connection with the provisions thereby brought into force, including such adaptations of those provisions as appear to the Secretary of State or Welsh Ministers[^key-e65a84ade31e5cdf09259401d7557471] necessary or expedient in consequence of the partial operation of this Act (whether before or after the day appointed by the order).
