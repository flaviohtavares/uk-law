# Building Act 1984, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 101. Breaking open of streets.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of any section of this Act that confers powers on any relevant authority[^c-541e0c91dc8ce7d856341bd1865b76c5] to construct, lay or maintain sewers, drains or pipes, section 158 of the Water Industry Act 1991[^c928491][^c928481] (street works) shall apply, with the necessary modifications, as it applies[^c928501] for the purpose of conferring power on a water undertaker or sewerage undertaker to lay a relevant pipe, within the meaning of that section[^c928511].

(2)[^c928521] That section[^c928531] shall also so apply so far as necessary for the purposes of any power to lay or maintain a sewer or drain which is conferred by this Act on a person other than a relevant authority[^c-a26a7060175bb41a2adf6ff4485c24e7].

(3)[^c-9f2f5742028c72c272c4481e781371de] In this section “**relevant authority**” means a local authority or the regulator.
