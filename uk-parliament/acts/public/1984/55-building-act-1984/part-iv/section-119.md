# Building Act 1984, section 119

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 119. Local inquiries.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^M_F_833b1633-6a9c-4f37-80e1-0506fab40046] The appropriate national authority or the regulator[^key-df88562ac95f5a45bae20197b4068cdc] may cause a local inquiry to be held in a case where it[^key-c369a088e3f88117e36b394dee0e2024] is authorised by this Act to determine a difference, to make an order, to give a consent or approval or otherwise to act under such a provision.

(2)[^key-694045caa9984d4f249a840ff308f8cb] Section 250 of the Local Government Act 1972 (power to direct inquiries) applies in relation to a local inquiry caused to be held by the regulator as it applies in relation to one caused to be held by the Secretary of State.
