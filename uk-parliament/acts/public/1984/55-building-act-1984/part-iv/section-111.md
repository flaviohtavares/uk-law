## 111. Arbitration.

*Extent: E+W*

In an arbitration under this Act, the reference shall be to a single arbitrator appointed by agreement between the parties, or in default of agreement by the appropriate national authority[^key-815bd59c0e186f562e2f843e416dfe4f].
