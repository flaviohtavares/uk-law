# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Application of law in relation to offer of shares or debentures of the successor company.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19244201] This section applies where the Secretary of State or a nominee of his offers for sale to the public shares or debentures of the successor company at a time when that company is wholly owned by the Crown; and in this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]**full prospectus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]**” means a prospectus which complies, or is deemed to comply, with the requirements of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201] Schedule 3 to the[^c13620591] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201] [^c13620601]Companies Act 1985 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201] (matters to be specified in prospectus and reports to be set out therein). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]

(2) If the shares or debentures are offered by a full prospectus as respects which the conditions mentioned in subsection (3) below are fulfilled (in this section referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]**the offer prospectus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]**”), any form of application for the shares or debentures may (instead of being issued with a full prospectus) be issued with a notice given by the Secretary of State which includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]

- (a) a brief description of the shares or debentures offered, the terms of the offer, the successor company’s business and its financial position; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]
- (b) an indication of the places in the United Kingdom where copies of the offer prospectus are to be available for inspection by members of the public; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]
- (c) a statement of the effect of subsections (4) and (5) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]

(3) The said conditions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]

- (a) that a copy of the prospectus has been delivered to the registrar of companies in pursuance of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201] section 64(1) of the Companies Act 1985[^c13620611] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]
- (b) that arrangements have been made with a view to securing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]
  - (i) that on or before the date of receipt of the form of application by a member of the public a copy of the prospectus is published in not less than four national newspapers; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19244201] that on that date copies of the prospectus are generally available in the United Kingdom for inspection by members of the public. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]

(4) Where a form of application is issued without a full prospectus but with a notice given by the Secretary of State under subsection (2) above, then, for the purposes of any enactment or any rule of law— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]

- (a) the notice shall be taken to have incorporated the offer prospectus; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]
- (b) any application for the shares or debentures which is made in pursuance of the notice shall be taken to have been made in pursuance of that prospectus. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]

(5) Where a form of application is issued without a full prospectus, neither the form of application nor any document which is issued with it shall be regarded— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]

- (a) as a prospectus for the purposes of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201] Chapter I of Part III of the Companies Act 1985[^c13620621] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201] (prospectus requirements); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]
- (b) as a circular for the purposes of section 14 of the Prevention of Fraud (Investments) Act 1958 or section 13 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201] [^c13620631]Prevention of Fraud (Investments) Act (Northern Ireland) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201] [^c13620641]1940 (circulars relating to investments), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]

but only, where the form of application is issued without a notice given by the Secretary of State under subsection (2) above, for the purpose of determining the liability of persons other than the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244201]
