# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 73

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Interpretation of Part V.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19245271] In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271]

- “**debenture *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271]**” includes debenture stock; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271]
- “**securities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271]**”, in relation to a company, includes shares, debentures, bonds and other securities of the company, whether or not constituting a charge on the assets of the company; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271]
- “**shares *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271]**” includes stock; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271]
- “**subsidiary *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271]**” has *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271] the meaning given by section 736 of[^c13620801] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271] the [^c13620811] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271]Companies Act 1985[^c13620821] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271].

(2) An order under section 60 above nominating any company for the purposes of that section and an order under subsection (1) of that section appointing the transfer date may be varied or revoked by a subsequent order at any time before any property, rights or liabilities vest in any company by virtue of section 60 above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271]

(3) A company shall be regarded for the purposes of this Part as wholly owned by the Crown at any time when all the issued shares in the company are held by or on behalf of the Crown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19245271]
