# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Conversion of certain loans transferred to the successor company.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19243311] The Secretary of State may by order extinguish all or any liabilities of the successor company to the Secretary of State in respect of the principal of such transferred loans as may be specified in the order; and the assets of the National Loans fund shall accordingly be reduced by amounts corresponding to any liabilities so extinguished. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]

(2) As a consequence of the extinguishment by an order under subsection (1) above of any such liabilities, the successor company shall issue such debentures of the company as the Secretary of State may direct— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]

- (a) to the Secretary of State; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]
- (b) to any person entitled to require the issue of the debentures following their initial allotment to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]

(3) The Secretary of State shall not make an order or give a direction under this section at a time when the successor company has ceased to be wholly owned by the Crown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]

(4) Except as may be agreed between the Secretary of State and the successor company— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]

- (a) the aggregate of the principal sums payable under debentures issued in pursuance of this section shall be equal to the aggregate of the sums the liability to repay which is extinguished by the order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]
- (b) the terms as to the payment of the principal sums so payable, and as to the payment of interest thereon, shall be the same as the corresponding terms of the transferred loans specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]

(5) For the purposes of subsection (4) above any express or implied terms of a transferred loan shall be disregarded in so far as they relate to the early discharge of liabilities to make repayments of principal and payments of interest. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]

(6) Subsection (3) and subsections (5) to (7) of section 61 above shall apply for the purposes of this section as they apply for the purposes of that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]

(7) For the avoidance of doubt it is hereby declared that the principal sums payable under debentures issued in pursuance of this section are to be regarded as money lent for the purposes of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311] section 400(9) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988[^c13620301] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311] (write-off of government investment: restriction of tax losses). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]

(8) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]**transferred loan *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]**” means any sum borrowed or treated as borrowed by British Telecommunications the liability to repay which vests in the successor company by virtue of section 60 above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243311]
