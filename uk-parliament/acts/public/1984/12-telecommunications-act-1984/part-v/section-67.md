# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 67

## 67. Temporary restrictions on successor company’s borrowings etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19244091] If articles of association of the successor company confer on the Secretary of State powers exercisable with the consent of the Treasury for, or in connection with, restricting the sums of money which may be borrowed or raised by the group during any period, those powers shall be exercisable in the national interest notwithstanding any rule of law and the provisions of any enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244091]

(2) For the purposes of this section any alteration of the articles of association of the successor company which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244091]

- (a) has the effect of conferring or extending any such power as is mentioned in subsection (1) above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244091]
- (b) is made at a time when that company has ceased to be wholly owned by the Crown, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244091]

shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244091]

(3) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244091]**group *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244091]**” means the successor company and all of its subsidiaries taken together. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19244091]
