# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 64

## 64. Exercise of Secretary of State’s functions through nominees.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c13620361][^c19243961] The Secretary of State may with the consent of the Treasury appoint such person or persons as he thinks fit to act as his nominees for the purposes of section 61, 62 or 63 above *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961] and the Treasury may appoint such person or persons as they think fit to act as their nominees for the purposes of section 63 above[^c13620371] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961]; and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961]

- (a) securities of the successor company may be issued under section 61 or 62 above to any nominee of the Secretary of State appointed for the purposes of that section or to any person entitled to require the issue of the securities following their initial allotment to any such nominee; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961]
- (b) any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961] nominee of the Treasury or the Secretary of State[^c13620381] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961] appointed for the purposes of section 63 above may acquire securities or rights in accordance with that section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961]

in accordance with directions given from time to time *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961] by the Treasury or[^c13620391] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961] by the Secretary of State with the consent of the Treasury. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961]

(2)[^c13620361] Any person holding any securities or rights as a nominee of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961] the Treasury or[^c13620411] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961] the Secretary of State by virtue of subsection (1) above shall hold and deal with them (or any of them) on such terms and in such manner as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961] the Treasury or the Secretary of State with the consent of the Treasury may direct[^c13620421] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243961].
