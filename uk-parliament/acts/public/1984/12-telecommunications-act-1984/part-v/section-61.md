# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Initial Government holding in the successor company.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) As a consequence of the vesting in the successor company by virtue of section 60 above of property, rights and liabilities of British Telecommunications, the successor company shall issue such securities of the company as the Secretary of State may from time to time direct— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561]

- (a) to the Secretary of State; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561]
- (b) to any person entitled to require the issue of the securities following their initial allotment to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561]

(2) The Secretary of State shall not give a direction under subsection (1) above at a time when the successor company has ceased to be wholly owned by the Crown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561]

(3) Securities required to be issued in pursuance of this section shall be issued or allotted at such time or times and on such terms (as to allotment) as the Secretary of State may direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561]

(4) Shares issued in pursuance of this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561]

- (a) shall be of such nominal value as the Secretary of State may direct; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561]
- (b) shall be issued as fully paid and treated for the purposes of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561] Companies Act 1985[^c13620271] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561] as if they had been paid up by virtue of the payment to the successor company of their nominal value in cash. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561]

(5) The Secretary of State may not exercise any power conferred on him by this section, or dispose of any securities issued or of any rights to securities initially allotted to him in pursuance of this section, without the consent of the Treasury. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561]

(6) Any dividends or other sums received by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561] the Treasury or[^c13620281] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561] the Secretary of State in right of or on the disposal of any securities or rights acquired by virtue of this section shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19241561]
