## 65. Target investment limit for Government shareholding.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19243981] As soon as the successor company ceases to be wholly owned by the Crown, the Secretary of State shall by order fix a target investment limit in relation to the shares for the time being held in that company by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] the Treasury or the Secretary of State or their or his nominee[^c13620431] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] by virtue of any provision of this Part (in this section referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]**the Government shareholding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]**”).

(2) The target investment limit shall be expressed as a proportion of the voting rights which are exercisable in all circumstances at general meetings of the successor company (in this section referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]**the ordinary voting rights *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]**”).

(3) The first target investment limit fixed under this section shall be equal to the proportion of the ordinary voting rights which is carried by the Government shareholding at the time when the order fixing the limit is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]

(4)[^c13620441] The Treasury or the Secretary of State[^c13620451] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] may from time to time by order fix a new target investment limit in place of the one previously in force under this section; but— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]

- (a) any new limit must be lower than the one it replaces; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]
- (b) an order under this section may only be revoked by an order fixing a new limit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]

(5) It shall be the duty of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] the Treasury and of[^c13620461] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] the Secretary of State so to exercise— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]

- (a) their[^c13620471] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] powers under section 63 above and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] their *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] power to dispose of any shares held by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] them[^c13620491] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] by virtue of any provision of this Part; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]
- (b) their *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] power to give directions to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] their *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] nominees, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]

as to secure that the Government shareholding does not carry a proportion of the ordinary voting rights exceeding any target investment limit for the time being in force under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]

(6) Notwithstanding subsection (5) above, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] the Treasury or the Secretary of State may take up, or direct any nominee of the Treasury or the Secretary of State[^c13620521] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] to take up, any rights for the time being available to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] the Treasury or the Secretary of State[^c13620531] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981], or to that nominee, as an existing holder of shares or other securities of the successor company or of any subsidiary of the successor company; but if as a result the ordinary voting rights carried by the Government shareholding at any time exceeds the target investment limit it shall be the duty of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] the Treasury or, as the case may be,[^c13620541] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981] the Secretary of State to comply with subsection (5) as soon after that time as is reasonably practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]

(7) For the purposes of this section the temporary suspension of any of the ordinary voting rights shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243981]
