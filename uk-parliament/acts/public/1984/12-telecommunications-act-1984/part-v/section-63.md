## 63. Government investment in securities of the successor company.[^c13620311]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Treasury or, with the consent of the Treasury, the Secretary of State may at any time[^c13620321] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243681], acquire— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243681]

- (a) securities of the successor company or of any subsidiary of the successor company; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243681]
- (b) rights to subscribe for any such securities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243681]

(2) The Secretary of State may not dispose of any securities or rights acquired under this section without the consent of the Treasury. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243681]

(3) Any expenses incurred by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243681] the Treasury or[^c13620331] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243681] the Secretary of State in consequence of the provisions of this section shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243681]

(4) Any dividends or other sums received by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243681] the Treasury or[^c13620341] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243681] the Secretary of State in right of, or on the disposal of, any securities or rights acquired under this section shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19243681]
