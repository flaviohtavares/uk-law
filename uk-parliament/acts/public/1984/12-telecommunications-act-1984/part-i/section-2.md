# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Abolition of British Telecommunications’ exclusive privilege.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

As from such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint for the purposes of Parts II to IV of this Act (in this Act referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221031]**the appointed day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221031]**”), the exclusive privilege of running telecommunication systems conferred on British Telecommunications by section 12 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221031] [^c13616781]British Telecommunications Act 1981 (in this Act referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221031]**the 1981 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221031]**”) shall cease to exist. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221031]
