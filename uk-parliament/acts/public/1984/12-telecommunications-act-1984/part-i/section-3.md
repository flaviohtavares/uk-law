# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. General duties of Secretary of State and Director.[^c13616861]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19221171] The Secretary of State and the Director shall each have a duty to exercise the functions assigned or transferred to him by or under Part II or Part III of this Act in the manner which he considers is best calculated— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]

- (a) to secure that there are provided throughout the United Kingdom, save in so far as the provision thereof is impracticable or not reasonably practicable, such telecommunication services as satisfy all reasonable demands for them including, in particular, emergency services, public call box services, directory information services, maritime services and services in rural areas; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]
- (b) without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (a) above, to secure that any person by whom any such services fall to be provided is able to finance the provision of those services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]

(2) Subject to subsection (1) above, the Secretary of State and the Director shall each have a duty to exercise the functions assigned or transferred to him by or under Part II or Part III of this Act in the manner which he considers is best calculated— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]

- (a) to promote the interests of consumers, purchasers and other users in the United Kingdom (including, in particular, those who are disabled or of pensionable age) in respect of the prices charged for, and the quality and variety of, telecommunication services provided and telecommunication apparatus supplied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]
- (b) to maintain and promote effective competition between persons engaged in commercial activities connected with telecommunications in the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]
- (c) to promote efficiency and economy on the part of such persons; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]
- (d) to promote research into and the development and use of new techniques by such persons; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]
- (e) to encourage major users of telecommunication services whose places of business are outside the United Kingdom to establish places of business in the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]
- (f) to promote the provision of international transit services by persons providing telecommunication services in the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]
- (g) to enable persons providing telecommunications services in the United Kingdom to compete effectively in the provision of such services outside the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]
- (h) to enable persons producing telecommunication apparatus in the United Kingdom to compete effectively in the supply of such apparatus both in and outside the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]

(3) Subsections (1) and (2) above do not apply in relation to anything done— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]

- (a) by the Secretary of State in the interests of national security or relations with the government of a country or territory outside the United Kingdom; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]
- (b) in the exercise of functions assigned or transferred by or under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171] section 50(1) or (2)[^c13616871] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171] below; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]

and subsection (2) above does not apply in relation to anything done in the exercise of functions assigned by section 10(3) or (8) or 52 below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]

(3A)[^c13616881] Subsections (1) and (2) above do not apply in relation to the determination of disputes by the Director under or by virtue of section 27A, 27F, 27G or 27I below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]

(3B)[^c13616891] Subsections (1) and (2) above do not apply in relation to anything done by the Director in the exercise of functions assigned to him by section 50(3) below (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]**Competition Act functions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]**”).

(3C)[^c13616891] The Director may nevertheless, when exercising any Competition Act function, have regard to any matter in respect of which a duty is imposed by subsection (1) or (2) above (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]**a general matter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]**”), if it is a matter to which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171] the Office of Fair Trading[^c13616911] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171] could have regard when exercising that function; but that is not to be taken as implying that, in relation to any of the matters mentioned in subsection (3) or (3A) above, regard may not be had to any general matter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]

(4) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]**international transit service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]**” means a telecommunications service consisting in the conveyance of sounds, visual images or signals which have been conveyed from, and are to be conveyed to, places outside the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221171]
