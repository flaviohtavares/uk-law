# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Meaning of “telecommunication system” and related expressions.[^c13616921][^c13616931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19221261] In this Act “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]**telecommunication system *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]**” means a system for the conveyance, through the agency of electric, magnetic, electro-magnetic, electro-chemical or electro-mechanical energy, of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]

- (a) speech, music and other sounds; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
- (b) visual images; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
- (c) signals serving for the impartation (whether as between persons and persons, things and things or persons and things) of any matter otherwise than in the form of sounds or visual images; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
- (d) signals serving for the actuation or control of machinery or apparatus. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]

(2) For the purposes of this Act telecommunication apparatus which is situated in the United Kingdom and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]

- (a) is connected to and comprised in a telecommunication system; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
- (b) is connected to but not comprised in a telecommunication system which extends beyond the United Kingdom, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]

shall be regarded as a telecommunication system and any person who controls the apparatus shall be regarded as running the system. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]

(3) In this Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]

- “**commercial activities connected with telecommunications *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]**” means any of the following, that is to say, the provision of telecommunication services, the supply or export of telecommunication apparatus and the production or acquisition of such apparatus for supply or export; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
- “**telecommunication apparatus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]**” means (except where the extended definition in Schedule 2 to this Act applies) apparatus constructed or adapted for use— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
  - (a) in transmitting or receiving anything falling within paragraphs (a) to (d) of subsection (1) above which is to be or has been conveyed by means of a telecommunication system; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
  - (b) in conveying, for the purposes of such a system, anything falling within those paragraphs; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
- “**telecommunication service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]**” means any of the following, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
  - (a) a service consisting in the conveyance by means of a telecommunication system of anything falling within paragraphs (a) to (d) of subsection (1) above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
  - (b) a directory information service, that is to say, a service consisting in the provision by means of a telecommunication system of directory information for the purpose of facilitating the use of a service falling within paragraph (a) above and provided by means of that system; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
  - (c) a service consisting in the installation, maintenance, adjustment, repair, alteration, moving, removal or replacement of apparatus which is or is to be connected to a telecommunication system. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]

(4) Subject to subsection (6) below, a telecommunication system is connected to another telecommunication system for the purposes of this Act if it is being used, or is installed or connected for use, in conveying anything falling within paragraphs (a) to (d) of subsection (1) above which is to be or has been conveyed by means of that other system. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]

(5) Subject to subsection (6) below, apparatus is connected to a telecommunication system for the purposes of this Act if it is being used, or is installed or connected for use— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]

- (a) in transmitting or receiving anything falling within paragraphs (a) to (d) of subsection (1) above which is to be or has been conveyed by means of that system; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
- (b) in conveying, for the purposes of that system, anything falling within those paragraphs; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]

and references in this subsection to anything falling within those paragraphs shall include references to energy of any kind mentioned in that subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]

(6) The connection to a telecommunication system of any other telecommunication system or any apparatus shall not be regarded as a connection for the purposes of this Act if that other telecommunication system or that apparatus would not be so connected but for its connection to another telecommunication system. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]

(7) In this section, except subsection (1) above, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]**convey *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]**” includes transmit, switch and receive and cognate expressions shall be construed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221261]
