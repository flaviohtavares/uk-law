# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. General functions.[^c13619131]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19238651] It shall be the duty of the Director, so far as it appears to him practicable from time to time, to keep under review the carrying on both within and outside the United Kingdom of activities connected with telecommunications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238651]

(2) It shall also be the duty of the Director, so far as it appears to him practicable from time to time, to collect information with respect to commercial activities connected with telecommunications carried on in the United Kingdom, and the persons by whom they are carried on, with a view to his becoming aware of, and ascertaining the circumstances relating to, matters with respect to which his functions are exercisable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238651]

(3) The Secretary of State may give general directions indicating— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238651]

- (a) considerations to which the Director should have particular regard in determining the order of priority in which matters are to be brought under review in the performance of his duty under subsection (1) or (2) above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238651]
- (b) considerations to which, in cases where it appears to the Director that any of his functions are exercisable, he should have particular regard in determining whether to exercise those functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238651]

(4) It shall be the duty of the Director, where either he considers it expedient or he is requested by the Secretary of State or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238651] the Office of Fair Trading[^c13619141] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238651] to do so, to give information, advice and assistance to the Secretary of State or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238651] the Office of Fair Trading *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238651] with respect to any matter in respect of which any function of the Director is exercisable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238651]
