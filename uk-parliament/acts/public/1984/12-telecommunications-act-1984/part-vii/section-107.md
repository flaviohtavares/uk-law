# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 107

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 107. Application to territorial waters and the continental shelf etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An Order in Council under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161] section 11 of the Petroleum Act 1998[^c13625321] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161] (application of civil law) may make provision for treating for the purposes of this Act and subordinate legislation made under it— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161]

- (a) any installation in waters to which that section applies and with respect to which provision is made under that section; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161]
- (b) any waters within five hundred metres of such an installation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161]

as if they were situated in such part of the United Kingdom as may be specified in the Order; and different provision may be so made for different purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161]

(2) In section 6 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161] [^c13625331]Continental Shelf Act 1964 (which makes, in relation to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161] [^c13625341]Wireless Telegraphy Act 1949 and regulations made under it, provision corresponding to that made by subsection (1) above) for the words “an area or part” there shall be substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161] “ waters to which that section applies and ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161].

(3)[^c13625351] In relation to any time before the coming into force of the said section 23, subsection (1) above shall have effect as if—

- (a) for the words “section 23 of the Oil and Gas (Enterprise) Act 1982” there were substituted the words “ section 3 of the Continental Shelf Act 1964 ”; and
- (b) for the words “waters to which that section applies and” there were substituted the words “ an area or part ”.

(4) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161]

- “**installation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161]**” includes any floating structure or device maintained on a station by whatever means; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161]
- “**subordinate legislation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161]**” has the same meaning as in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161] [^c13625361]Interpretation Act 1978. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272161]
