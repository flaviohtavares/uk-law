# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 109

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 109. Amendments, transitional provisions and repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 4 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified (being minor amendments or amendments consequential on the preceding provisions of this Act).

(2) The Secretary of State may by order make such consequential modifications of any provision contained in any Act (whether public general or local) passed, or subordinate legislation made, before the coming into force of the relevant provision of this Act as appear to him necessary or expedient in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]

- (a) any reference in that Act or subordinate legislation to British Telecommunications; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]
- (b) any reference in that Act or subordinate legislation to an enactment contained in the Telegraph Acts 1863 to 1916; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]
- (c) any use in that Act or subordinate legislation of terminology used in the Telegraph Acts 1863 to 1916 but not in the telecommunications code contained in Schedule 2 to this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]

(3) The Secretary of State may by order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]

- (a) repeal or amend any enactment in a local Act which appears to him to be unnecessary having regard to the provisions of this Act or to be inconsistent with any provision of this Act; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]
- (b) repeal any enactment in a local telegraph Act which appears to him to be spent or no longer of practical utility. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]

(4) The general transitional provisions and savings contained in Schedule 5 to this Act and the special transitional provisions with respect to patents for inventions and registered designs contained in Schedule 6 to this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272361] shall have effect; but those provisions and savings are without prejudice to sections 16 and 17 of the [^c13625371]Interpretation Act 1978 (effect of repeals).

(5) If it appears to the Secretary of State requisite or expedient to do so in order to secure that telecommunication services provided before the appointed day by means of any existing apparatus continue to be available after the appointed day, he may by order make provision with respect to the terms on which existing apparatus is kept installed on any premises; and such an order may, in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]

- (a) provide for the terms of agreements in pursuance of which existing apparatus is kept installed on any premises to have effect with such modifications as may be specified in the order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]
- (b) impose obligations in relation to existing apparatus on persons who own or use such apparatus or who own interests in, or occupy, premises where such apparatus is kept installed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]
- (c) provide, for the purposes of any provision contained in such an order by virtue of paragraph (a) or (b) above, for such questions arising under the order as are specified in the order, or are of a description so specified, to be referred to, and determined by, the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]

(6)[^c13625381][^c19613921] The enactments mentioned in Schedule 7 to this Act (which include some that are spent or no longer of practical utility) are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]

(7) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]

- “**existing apparatus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]**” means any telecommunication apparatus (within the meaning of Schedule 2 to this Act) which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]
  - (a) was installed on any premises before the appointed day, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]
  - (b) cannot, after the appointed day, be kept installed there by virtue of any right which is or may be conferred by or in accordance with the telecommunications code; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]
- “**local telegraph Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]**” means a local Act which incorporated a telegraph company or the Bill for which was promoted by such a company; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]
- “**subordinate legislation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]**” has the same meaning as in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301] [^c13625391]Interpretation Act 1978. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19272301]
