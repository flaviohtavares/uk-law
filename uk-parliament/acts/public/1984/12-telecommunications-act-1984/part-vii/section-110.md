# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Short title, commencement and extent.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c13625401][^c19275041] This Act may be cited as the Telecommunications Act 1984.

(2)[^c13625411] The following provisions of this Act, namely—

- sections 2 to 4;
- Parts II to IV;
- sections 93 to 95;
- sections 97 to 99;
- sections 101 to 103;
- section 106;
- section 109(1) and Schedule 4;
- section 109(2), (3) and (7);
- Part I of Schedule 5 and section 109(4) so far as relating to that Part; and
- Part I of Schedule 7 and section 109(6) so far as relating to that Part,

shall come into force on the appointed day.

(3)[^c13625421] The following provisions of this Act, namely—

- Part V;
- Part II of Schedule 5 and Schedule 6 and section 109(4) so far as relating to that Part and that Schedule; and
- Part II of Schedule 7 and section 109(6) so far as relating to that Part,

shall come into force on the transfer date.

(4)[^c13625431] Part III of Schedule 7 and section 109(6) so far as relating to that Part shall come into force on the dissolution of British Telecommunications.

(5)[^c13625441] Subject to subsections (2) to (4) above, this Act shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint; and different days may be so appointed for different provisions or for different purposes.

(6) This Act extends to Northern Ireland.
