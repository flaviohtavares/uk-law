# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 97

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Contributions by local authorities towards provision of facilities.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19259001] Where a local authority consider that it would be for the benefit of the whole or any part of their area that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001]

- (a) any additional telecommunication facilities should be provided; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001]
- (b) any existing telecommunication facilities should continue to be provided, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001]

by a public telecommunications operator, whether within or outside the area to be benefited, the authority may undertake to pay to that operator any loss he may sustain by reason of the provision or continued provision of those facilities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001]

(2) In the application of this section to Scotland, nothing in subsection (1) above shall authorise the giving of an undertaking as respects the provision or continued provision of facilities outside the area to be benefited. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001]

(3) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001]**local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001]**”—

- (a) in relation to England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001] [^c13621081]. . ., means a county council, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001] [^c13621091], a district council, a London borough council, the Common Council of the City of London *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001] or[^c13621101] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001], a parish council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001] [^c13621111]. . .;
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c13621121][^c19259001] in relation to Wales, means a county council, a county borough council or a community council; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001]
- (b) in relation to Scotland, means a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001] council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994[^c13621131] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001];
- (c) in relation to Northern Ireland, means a district council. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19259001]
