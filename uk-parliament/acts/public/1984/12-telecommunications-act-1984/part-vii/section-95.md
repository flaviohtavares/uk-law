# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 95

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Orders under the 1973 and 1980 Acts.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c13621051][^c19380501] Where the Office of Fair Trading, the Commission or (as the case may be) the Secretary of State (in this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19380501]**the relevant authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19380501]**”) makes a relevant order, the order may also provide for the revocation or modification of licences granted under section 7 above to such extent as may appear to the relevant authority to be requisite or expedient for the purpose of giving effect to, or taking account of, any provision made by the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19380501]

(2) In subsection (1) above, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19380501]**relevant order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19380501]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19380501]

- (a)[^c19380461] an order under section 75, 83 or 84 of, or paragraph 5, 10 or 11 of Schedule 7 to, the Enterprise Act 2002 where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19380501]
  - (i) one or more than one of the enterprises which have, or may have, ceased to be distinct enterprises was engaged in the carrying on of a commercial activity connected with telecommunications; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19380501]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19380501] one or more than one of the enterprises which will or may cease to be distinct enterprises is engaged in the carrying on of a commercial activity connected with telecommunications; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19380501]
- (b) an order under section 160 or 161 of that Act where the feature, or combination of features, of the market in the United Kingdom for goods or services which prevents, restricts or distorts competition relates to commercial activities connected with telecommunications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19380501]

(3)[^c13621061] Expressions used in subsection (2) above and in Part 3 or (as the case may be) Part 4 of the Enterprise Act 2002 have the same meanings in that subsection as in that Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19380501]
