# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 93

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Grants to promote interests of disabled persons.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19255741] The Secretary of State may, with the approval of the Treasury, make grants for the purpose of defraying or contributing towards— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255741]

- (a) any expenses which may be incurred by any person in supporting research into or the development of apparatus to which this section applies; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255741]
- (b) any fees incurred by any person in respect of the exercise in relation to apparatus to which this section applies of any function conferred by or under section 22 above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255741]

(2) This section applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255741]

- (a) telecommunication apparatus which is constructed for use by disabled persons; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255741]
- (b) telecommunication apparatus which is so constructed as to be capable of being adapted for such use; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255741]
- (c) apparatus by means of which telecommunication apparatus falling within paragraph (b) above may be so adapted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255741]

(3) In making a grant under this section, the Secretary of State may impose such conditions as he thinks fit and may, in particular, impose a condition requiring the repayment of all or any part of the grant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255741]

- (a) if any other condition is not complied with; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255741]
- (b) in such other circumstances as he may specify. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255741]

(4) There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament any sums required by the Secretary of State for making grants under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255741]

(5) Any sums received by the Secretary of State under subsection (3) above shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255741]
