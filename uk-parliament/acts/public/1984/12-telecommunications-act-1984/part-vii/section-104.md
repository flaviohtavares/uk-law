## 104. Orders and schemes.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any power of the Secretary of State to make an order or a scheme under this Act shall be exercisable by statutory instrument subject, except in the case of an order under section [^c19271272]. . . 110(5), [^c19271272]. . . to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(1A)[^c19271412] Section 403 of the Communications Act 2003 (procedure for regulations and orders made by OFCOM) applies to every power of OFCOM to make an order under a provision of this Act.
