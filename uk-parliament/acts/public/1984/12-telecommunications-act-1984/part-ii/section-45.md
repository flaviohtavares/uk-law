# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Disclosure of messages etc.[^c13619021]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19238231] A person engaged in the running of a public telecommunication system who otherwise than in the course of his duty intentionally discloses to any person — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]

- (a) the contents of any message which has been intercepted in the course of its transmission by means of that system; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]
- (b) any information concerning the use made of telecommunication services provided for any other person by means of that system, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]

shall be guilty of an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]

(2)[^c13619031] Subsection (1) above does not apply to any disclosure made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]

- (a) in accordance with the order of any court or for the purposes of any criminal proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]
- (b) in accordance with any warrant, authorisation or notice issued, granted or given under any provision of the Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]
- (c) in compliance with any requirement imposed (apart from that Act) in consequence of the exercise by any person of any statutory power exercisable by him for the purpose of obtaining any document or other information; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]
- (d) in pursuance of any duty under that Act of 2000, or under Part III of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231] [^c13619041]Police Act 1997, to provide information or produce any document to the Interception of Communications Commissioner or to the tribunal established under section 65 of that Act of 2000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]

(3)[^c13619031] In subsection (2) above “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]**criminal proceedings *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]**statutory power *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]**” have the same meanings as in the Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]

(4) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238231]
