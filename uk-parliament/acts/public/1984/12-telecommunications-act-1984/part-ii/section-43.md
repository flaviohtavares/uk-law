# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Improper use of public telecommunication system.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19237991] A person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237991]

- (a) sends, by means of a public telecommunication system, a message or other matter that is grossly offensive or of an indecent, obscene or menacing character; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237991]
- (b) sends by those means, for the purpose of causing annoyance, inconvenience or needless anxiety to another, a message that he knows to be false or persistently makes use for that purpose of a public telecommunication system, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237991]

shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237991] imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale or both[^c13618941] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237991].

(2) Subsection (1) above does not apply to anything done in the course of providing a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237991] programme service (within the meaning of the Broadcasting Act 1990)[^c13618951] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237991].
