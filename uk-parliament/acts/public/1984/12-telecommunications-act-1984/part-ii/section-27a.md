# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 27A

## 27A. Standards of performance in individual cases.[^c13618301][^c13618291]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19227411] The Director may make regulations prescribing, for any designated operator, such standards of performance in connection with the provision of relevant services by that operator as, in his opinion, ought to be achieved in individual cases. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) above may only be made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

- (a) with the consent of the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]
- (b) after consulting— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]
  - (i) the designated operator; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19227411] persons or bodies appearing to the Director to be representative of persons likely to be affected by the regulations; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]
- (c) after arranging for such research as the Director considers appropriate with a view to discovering the views of a representative sample of persons likely to be so affected and considering the results. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

(3) Regulations under this section may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

- (a) prescribe circumstances in which the designated operator is to inform persons of their rights under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]
- (b) prescribe such standards of performance in relation to any duty arising under paragraph (a) above as, in the Director’s opinion, ought to be achieved in all cases; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]
- (c) prescribe circumstances in which the designated operator is to be exempted from any requirements of the regulations or this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

(4) If the designated operator fails to meet a prescribed standard, he shall make to any person who is affected by the failure such compensation as may be determined by or under the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

(5) The making of compensation under this section in respect of any failure to meet a prescribed standard shall not prejudice any other remedy which may be available in respect of the act or omission which constituted that failure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

(6) Any dispute arising under this section or regulations made under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

- (a) may be referred to the Director by either party; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]
- (b) on such a reference, shall be determined by order made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]
  - (i) by the Director; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19227411] by such other person as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

(7) Any person making an order under subsection (6) above shall include in the order his reasons for reaching his decision with respect to the dispute. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

(8) The practice and procedure to be followed in connection with any such determination shall be such as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

(9) An order under subsection (6) above shall be final and shall be enforceable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

- (a) in England and Wales and in Northern Ireland, as if it were a judgment of a county court; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]
- (b) in Scotland, as if it were an extract registered decree arbitral bearing a warrant for execution issued by the sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]

(10) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]**prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]**” means prescribed by regulations under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227411]
