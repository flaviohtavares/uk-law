# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 27G

## 27G. Billing disputes.[^c13618361]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19228901] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision for billing disputes to be referred to the Director for determination in accordance with the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]

(2) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]**billing dispute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]**” means a dispute between a designated operator and a customer concerning the amount of the charge which the operator is entitled to recover from the customer in connection with the provision of any relevant service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]

(3) Regulations under this section may only be made after consulting— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]

- (a) the Director; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]
- (b) persons or bodies appearing to the Secretary of State to be representative of persons likely to be affected by the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]

(4) Regulations under this section may provide that, where a billing dispute is referred to the Director, he may either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]

- (a) determine the dispute, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]
- (b) appoint an arbitrator (or in Scotland an arbiter) to determine it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]

(5) Any person determining any billing dispute in accordance with regulations under this section shall, in such manner as may be specified in the regulations, give his reasons for reaching his decision with respect to the dispute. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]

(6) Regulations under this section may provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]

- (a) that disputes may be referred to the Director under this section only by prescribed persons; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]
- (b) for any determination to be final and enforceable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]
  - (i) in England and Wales and in Northern Ireland, as if it were a judgment of a county court; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19228901] in Scotland, as if it were an extract registered decree arbitral bearing a warrant for execution issued by the sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]

(7) Except in such circumstances (if any) as may be prescribed by regulations under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]

- (a) the Director or an arbitrator (or in Scotland an arbiter) appointed by him shall not determine any billing dispute which is the subject of proceedings before, or with respect to which judgment has been given by, any court; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]
- (b) neither party to any billing dispute which has been referred to the Director for determination in accordance with regulations under this section shall commence proceedings before any court in respect of that dispute pending its determination in accordance with the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]

(8) No designated operator may commence proceedings before any court in respect of any charge in connection with the provision by him of any relevant service unless, not less than 28 days before doing so, the customer concerned was informed by him, in such form and manner as may be prescribed by regulations under this section, of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]

- (a) his intention to commence proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]
- (b) the customer’s rights by virtue of this section; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]
- (c) such other matters (if any) as may be so prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228901]
