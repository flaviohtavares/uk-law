# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 16

## 16. Securing compliance with licence conditions.[^c13618091]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19222481] Subject to subsections (2) and (5) and section 17 below, where the Director is satisfied that a person who is authorised by a licence granted under section 7 above to run a telecommunication system (in this Act referred to as a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]**telecommunications operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]**”) is contravening, or has contravened and is likely again to contravene, any of the conditions of his licence, the Director shall by a final order make such provision as is requisite for the purpose of securing compliance with that condition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

(2) Subject to subsection (5) below, where it appears to the Director— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

- (a) that a telecommunications operator is contravening, or has contravened and is likely again to contravene, any of the conditions of his licence; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]
- (b) that it is requisite that a provisional order be made, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

the Director shall (instead of taking steps towards the making of a final order) by a provisional order make such provision as appears to him requisite for the purpose of securing compliance with that condition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

(3) In determining for the purposes of subsection (2)(b) above whether it is requisite that a provisional order be made, the Director shall have regard, in particular, to the extent to which any person is likely to sustain loss or damage in consequence of anything which, in contravention of the relevant condition, is likely to be done, or omitted to be done, before a final order may be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

(4) Subject to subsection (5) and section 17 below, the Director shall confirm a provisional order with or without modifications if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

- (a) he is satisfied that the telecommunications operator is contravening, or has contravened and is likely again to contravene, any of the conditions of his licence; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]
- (b) the provision made by the order (with any modifications) is requisite for the purpose of securing compliance with that condition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

(5) The duties imposed by subsections (1) to (4) above shall not apply where the Director gives notice that he is satisfied— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

- (a) that the duties imposed on him by section 3 above preclude the making of a final or provisional order or, as the case may be, the confirmation of the provisional order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481] [^c13618101]. . .
- (b) that the contraventions or apprehended contraventions are of a trivial nature; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481] or[^c13618111] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]
- (c)[^c13618111] that the most appropriate way of proceeding is under the Competition Act 1998. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

and a notice under this subsection shall be given by publication in such manner as the Director considers appropriate for the purpose of bringing the matters to which the notice relates to the attention of persons likely to be affected by them and by sending a copy of the notice to the telecommunications operator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

(6) A final or provisional order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

- (a) shall require the telecommunications operator (according to the circumstances of the case) to do, or not to do, such things as are specified in the order or are of a description so specified; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]
- (b) shall take effect at such time, being the earliest practicable time, as is determined by or under the order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]
- (c) may be revoked at any time by the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

(7) In this section and sections 17 to 19 below— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

- “**contravention *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]**”, in relation to any condition of a licence, includes any failure to comply with that condition and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]**contravene *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]**” shall be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]
- “**final order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]**” means an order under this section other than a provisional order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]
- “**provisional order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]**” means an order under this section which, if not previously confirmed under subsection (4) above, will cease to have effect at the end of such period (not exceeding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481] two months[^c13618131] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]) as is determined by or under the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]

(8) References in this section to conditions of a licence do not include references to conditions relating to the application of the telecommunications code. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222481]
