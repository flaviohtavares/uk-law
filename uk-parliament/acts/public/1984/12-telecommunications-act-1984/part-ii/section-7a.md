# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 7A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7A. Licensing procedures[^c13617411]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19221711] Licences granted under section 7 above to particular persons shall be granted on the basis of procedures established by the Secretary of State or the Director, details of which shall be contained in a notice given by him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221711]

(2) The details given in a notice under subsection (1) above in relation to any licences shall include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221711]

- (a) time limits for dealing with applications for the grant of a licence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221711]
- (b) requirements which must be met for the grant of a licence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221711]
- (c) information about the conditions to be included in a licence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221711]
- (d) details of the fees or the method of calculating the fees payable in respect of a licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221711]

(3) Where the person applying for a licence fails to provide any information which the Secretary of State or the Director reasonably requires in order to satisfy himself that the applicant is able to comply with the conditions in the licence the Secretary of State or the Director may refuse to grant the licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221711]

(4) A notice under this section shall be published in such manner as the Secretary of State or the Director considers appropriate for the purpose of bringing the matters to which the notice relates to the attention of those likely to be affected by them, and a reference to such notice shall also be published in the London, Edinburgh and Belfast Gazettes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221711]
