# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Compulsory purchase of land in Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19237821] Subject to subsection (2) below, the Secretary of State may authorise a public telecommunications operator to purchase compulsorily any land in Scotland which is required by the operator for, or in connection with, the establishment or running of the operator’s system or as to which it can reasonably be foreseen that it will be so required; and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821] [^c13618571]Acquisition of Land (Authorisation Procedure) (Scotland) Act 1947 shall apply to any compulsory purchase under this section as if the operator were a local authority within the meaning of that Act and as if this section had been in force immediately before the commencement of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821]

(2) No order shall be made authorising a compulsory purchase under this section except with the consent of the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821]

(3) The power of purchasing land compulsorily under this section includes power to acquire a servitude or other right over land by the creation of a new right. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821]

(4) The following provisions of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821] the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997[^c13618581] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821] shall have effect in relation to land acquired compulsorily by a public telecommunications operator under this section as they have effect in relation to land acquired compulsorily by statutory undertakers under any other enactment, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821]

- (a) section 197 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821] (provisions as to churches and burial grounds); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821]
- (b) section 198 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821] (use and development of land for open spaces); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821]
- (c) sections 224 to 227 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821] (extinguishment of rights of way, and rights as to apparatus, of statutory undertakers). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821]

(5) Where a public telecommunications operator has acquired land under this section, he shall not dispose of that land or of any interest or right in or over it except with the consent of the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237821]
