# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 13A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13A. References under section 13: time limits[^c13617921]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19377131] Every reference under section 13 above shall specify a period (not longer than six months beginning with the date of the reference) within which a report on the reference is to be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377131]

(2) A report of the Commission on a reference under section 13 above shall not have effect (and no action shall be taken in relation to it under section 15 below) unless the report is made before the end of the period specified in the reference or such further period (if any) as may be allowed by the Director under subsection (3) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377131]

(3) The Director may, if he has received representations on the subject from the Commission and is satisfied that there are special reasons why the report cannot be made within the period specified in the reference, extend that period by no more than six months. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377131]

(4) No more than one extension is possible under subsection (3) above in relation to the same reference. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377131]

(5) The Director shall, in the case of an extension made by him under subsection (3) above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377131]

- (a) publish that extension in such manner as he considers appropriate for the purpose of bringing it to the attention of persons likely to be affected by it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377131]
- (b) in the case of a licence granted to a particular person, send to that person a copy of what has been published by him under paragraph (a) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377131]
