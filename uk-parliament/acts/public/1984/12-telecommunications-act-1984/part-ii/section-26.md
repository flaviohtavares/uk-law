# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Grants and loans to persons exercising certain functions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19227341] The Secretary of State may, with the approval of the Treasury, make grants or loans— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227341]

- (a) to persons by whom any matter falls to be determined for the purposes of any requirement imposed in pursuance of subsection (2) or (4) of section 20, 22 or 24 above; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227341]
- (b) to persons appointed under section 25 above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227341]

(2) Any loans under this section shall be repaid to the Secretary of State at such times and by such methods and interest thereon shall be paid to him at such rates and at such times as he may, with the approval of the Treasury, from time to time direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227341]

(3) There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament any sums required by the Secretary of State for making grants or loans under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227341]

(4) Any sums received by the Secretary of State under subsection (2) above shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227341]
