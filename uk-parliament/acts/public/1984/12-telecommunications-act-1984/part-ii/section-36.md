# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Compulsory purchase of land in Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19237851] Where a public telecommunications operator proposes to acquire, otherwise than by agreement, any land in Northern Ireland required by him for, or in connection with, the establishment or running of his system or as to which it can reasonably be foreseen that it will be so required, he may, with the consent of the Director, apply to the Secretary of State for an order vesting that land in him and the Secretary of State shall have power to make such an order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]

(2) The power of acquiring land compulsorily under this section shall include power to acquire, by the creation of a new right, an easement or other right over land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]

(3) Where a public telecommunications operator has acquired any land under this section, he shall not dispose of it except with the consent of the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]

(4) For the purposes of the acquisition of land by means of a vesting order under this section, Schedule 6 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851] [^c13618621]Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972 and Schedule 8 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851] [^c13618631] Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 are hereby incorporated in this Act subject to the modifications specified in subsection (5) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]

(5) The said modifications are as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]

- (a) for any reference in the said Schedule 6 to a council there shall be substituted a reference to the operator; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]
- (b) for any reference in either Schedule to the Department there shall be substituted a reference to the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]
- (c) for any reference in either Schedule to the Act or Order in question there shall be substituted a reference to this Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]
- (d) in paragraph 6(2) of the said Schedule 6, for the words from “the fund” onwards there shall be substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851] “ funds of the operator (in this Schedule referred to as "the compensation fund’) and shall be discharged by payments made by the operator ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]
- (e) in paragraph 12(2) of that Schedule for the words “the clerk of the council” there shall be substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851] “ such person as may be designated for the purposes of this Schedule by the operator ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851].

(6) The enactments for the time being in force relating to the assessment of compensation in respect of land vested in a district council by an order made under Schedule 6 to the Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972 shall, subject to any necessary modifications, apply in relation to any land vested in a public telecommunications operator by an order made under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]

(7) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]**land *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]**” has the meaning assigned to it by section 45(1)(a) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237851]
