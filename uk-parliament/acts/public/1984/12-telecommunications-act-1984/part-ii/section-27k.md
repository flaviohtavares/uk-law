# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 27K

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27K. Enforcement of standards of performance, etc.[^c13618401]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19229851] Sections 16 to 18 above shall apply in relation to a designated operator as if it were a condition of the licence granted to him under section 7 above that he shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]

- (a) achieve the standards of overall performance determined in relation to him under section 27B above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]
- (b) take steps to inform those of his customers to whom he supplies relevant services about— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]
  - (i) those standards, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19229851] the levels of performance which he has achieved as respects those standards, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]

  in accordance with section 27D above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]
- (c) comply with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]
  - (i) the requirements of section 27E above, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19229851] any direction given by the Director under subsection (4) or (6) of that section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]
- (d) comply with any order made under section 27F(3) above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]
- (e) comply with any direction given by the Director under section 27H(6) above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]
- (f) comply with any direction given under section 27I(3) above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]
- (g) comply with the requirements of section 27J above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]

(2) For the purposes of the application by this section of sections 16 to 18 above, any term of a licence granted under section 7 above which has or which might have the effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]

- (a) of excepting a designated operator from liability for a contravention of a condition of that licence, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]
- (b) otherwise restricting any such liability, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]

shall not apply in relation to any contravention of the condition mentioned in subsection (1) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229851]
