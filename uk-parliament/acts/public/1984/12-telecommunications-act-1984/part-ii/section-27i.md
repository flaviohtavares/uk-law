# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 27I

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27I. Complaints about deposits.[^c13618381]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19229661] Any person who is aggrieved by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]

- (a) the decision of a designated operator to require him to pay a deposit before he is provided with a relevant service, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]
- (b) by the amount which he is so required to pay, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]

may refer the matter to the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]

(2) On any such reference the Director, or an arbitrator (or in Scotland an arbiter) appointed by him, shall consider whether the criteria settled under section 27H above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]

- (a) have been applied correctly, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]
- (b) are inappropriate in the particular case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]

(3) If the Director or arbitrator (or arbiter) considers that the criteria have not been correctly applied, or that they are inappropriate in the particular case, he shall, unless the complaint has been withdrawn or it is otherwise inappropriate to proceed, determine— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]

- (a) whether the person concerned is to be required to pay a deposit, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]
- (b) if so, the amount which he is to be required to pay, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]

and give the appropriate direction to the designated operator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]

(4) Any person giving a direction under subsection (3) above shall include in the direction his reasons for reaching his decision with respect to the complaint. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]

(5) The practice and procedure to be followed in connection with a complaint under subsection (1) above shall be determined by the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]

(6) A direction under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]

- (a) may include such incidental, supplemental and consequential provision (including provision requiring either party to pay a sum in respect of the costs or expenses incurred by the person giving the direction) as that person considers appropriate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]
- (b) shall be final and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]
  - (i) in England and Wales and in Northern Ireland enforceable, in so far as it includes such provision as to costs or expenses, as if it were a judgment of a county court; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19229661] in Scotland, enforceable as if it were an extract registered decree arbitral bearing a warrant for execution issued by the sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]

(7) In including in a direction under this section any such provision as to costs or expenses, the person giving the direction shall have regard to the conduct and means of the parties and any other relevant circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229661]
