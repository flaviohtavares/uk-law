# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Register of approved contractors.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19227111] The Director shall keep a register of approvals given under section 20 above at such premises and in such form as he may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227111]

(2) The Director shall cause particulars of every such approval, and of every variation or withdrawal of such an approval, to be entered in the register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227111]

(3) Subsections (4) to (6) of section 19 above shall apply for the purposes of this section as they apply for the purposes of that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227111]
