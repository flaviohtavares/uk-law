# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 15

## 15. Modification of licence conditions following report.[^c13618041][^c13618051]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19222431] Where a report of the Commission on a reference under section 13 above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]

- (a) includes conclusions to the effect that any of the matters specified in the reference operate, or may be expected to operate, against the public interest; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]
- (b) specifies effects adverse to the public interest which those matters have or may be expected to have; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]
- (c) includes conclusions to the effect that those effects could be remedied or prevented by modifications of the conditions of the licence; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]
- (d) specifies modifications by which those effects could be remedied or prevented, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]

the Director shall, subject to the following provisions of this section, make such modifications of the conditions of the licence as appear to him requisite for the purpose of remedying or preventing the adverse effects specified in the report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]

(2) Before making modifications under this section, the Director shall have regard to the modifications specified in the report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]

(3) Before making modifications under this section, the Director shall give notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]

- (a) stating that he proposes to make the modifications and setting out their effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]
- (b) stating the reasons why he proposes to make the modifications; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]
- (c) specifying the time (not being less than 28 days from the date of publication of the notice) within which representations or objections with respect to the proposed modifications may be made, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]

and shall consider any representations or objections which are duly made and not withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]

(4) A notice under subsection (3) above shall be given by publication in such manner as the Director considers appropriate for the purpose of bringing the matters to which the notice relates to the attention of persons likely to be affected by them and, in the case of a licence granted to a particular person, by sending a copy of the notice to that person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]

(5) The Director shall also send a copy of a notice under subsection (3) above to the Secretary of State; and if, within the time specified in the notice, the Secretary of State directs the Director not to make any modification the Director shall comply with the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]

(6) The Secretary of State shall not give a direction under subsection (5) above unless it appears to him requisite or expedient to do so in the interests of national security or relations with the government of a country or territory outside the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222431]
