# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Acquisition of land by agreement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19237951] For the purpose of the acquisition by agreement by a public telecommunications operator of land in England and Wales the provisions of Part I of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237951] [^c13618771]Compulsory Purchase Act 1965 (so far as applicable) other than sections 4 to 8, section 27 and section 31 shall apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237951]

(2) For the purpose of the acquisition by agreement by a public telecommunications operator of land in Scotland, section 109(2) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237951] [^c13618781]Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972 (incorporation of Lands Clauses Acts) shall, with any necessary modifications, apply for the purposes of this Act as it applies for the purposes of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237951]

(3) For the purpose of the acquisition by agreement by a public telecommunications operator of land in Northern Ireland, the Lands Clauses Acts shall be incorporated with this Act except for sections 127 to 133 (sale of superfluous land) and sections 150 and 151 (access to the special Act) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237951] [^c13618791]Lands Clauses Consolidation Act 1845. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237951]
