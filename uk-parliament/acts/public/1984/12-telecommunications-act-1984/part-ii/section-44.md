# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Modification etc. of messages.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19238201] A person engaged in the running of a public telecommunication system who otherwise than in the course of his duty intentionally modifies or interferes with the contents of a message sent by means of that system shall be guilty of an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238201]

(2) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) above shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238201]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238201]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or to a fine or to both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238201]
