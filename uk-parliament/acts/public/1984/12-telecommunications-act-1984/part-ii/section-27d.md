# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 27D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27D. Information to be given to customers about overall performance.[^c13618331]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19228761] Each designated operator shall, in such form and manner and with such frequency as the Director may direct, take steps to inform those of his customers to whom he supplies relevant services of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228761]

- (a) the standards of overall performance determined under section 27B above which are applicable to that operator; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228761]
- (b) that operator’s level of performance as respects each of those standards. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228761]

(2) In giving any such direction, the Director shall not specify a frequency of less than once in every period of twelve months. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228761]
