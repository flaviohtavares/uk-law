# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Approval of contractors.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19227081] Where licences granted under section 7 above include provisions which are framed by reference to the carrying out of relevant operations by persons for the time being approved under this section then, for the purposes of those provisions, persons may be approved under this section in relation to such operations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]

- (a) by the Secretary of State; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]
- (b) with the consent of, or in accordance with a general authorisation given by, the Secretary of State, by the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]

(2) A person applying for an approval under this section may be required by the person to whom the application is made to comply with such requirements as the person to whom the application is made may think appropriate; and those requirements may include a requirement to satisfy some other person with respect to any matter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]

(3) An approval under this section may apply either to a particular person or to persons of a description specified in the approval, and may so apply either in relation to particular relevant operations or in relation to relevant operations of a description so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]

(4) An approval under this section may specify conditions which must be complied with if the approval is to apply, for any purposes specified in the approval, to any person who is so specified or is of a description so specified; and any such condition may impose on the person to whom the approval is given a requirement from time to time to satisfy any person with respect to any matter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]

(5) Nothing in this section shall preclude a person (not being the Secretary of State or the Director) by whom any matter falls to be determined for the purposes of any requirement imposed in pursuance of subsection (2) or (4) above from charging any fee in respect of the carrying out of any test or other assessment made by him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]

(6) Any power conferred by this section to give an approval includes power to vary or withdraw an approval given in exercise of that power. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]

(7) The Secretary of State shall send to the Director— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]

- (a) a copy of every approval given by him under this section; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]
- (b) particulars of every variation or withdrawal of an approval so given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]

(8) The Secretary of State may by order provide for the charging of fees in respect of the exercise of any functions conferred by or under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]

(9) Any sums received by the Secretary of State or the Director under this section shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]

(10) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]**relevant operations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]**” means the installation, maintenance, adjustment, repair, alteration, moving, removal or replacement of apparatus which is or is to be connected to any telecommunication system to which a licence under section 7 above relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227081]
