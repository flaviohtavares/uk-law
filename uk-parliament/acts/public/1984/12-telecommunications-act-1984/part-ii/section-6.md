# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>

## 6. Exceptions to section 5.[^c13616981]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19221471] [^c13616991]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Section 5(1) above is not contravened by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]

- (a) the running of a telecommunication system in the case of which the only agency involved in the conveyance of things thereby conveyed is light and the things thereby conveyed are so conveyed as to be capable of being received or perceived by the eye and without more; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
- (b)[^c13617001] the running by a person of a telecommunication system which is not connected to another telecommunication system and in the case of which all the apparatus comprised therein is situated either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
  - (i) on a single set of premises in single occupation; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19221471] in a vehicle, vessel, aircraft or hovercraft or in two or more vehicles, vessels, aircraft or hovercraft mechanically coupled together; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
- (c) the running by a single individual of a telecommunication system which is not connected to another telecommunication system and in the case of which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
  - (i) all the apparatus comprised therein is under his control; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19221471] everything conveyed by it that falls within paragraphs (a) to (d) of section 4(1) above is conveyed solely for domestic purposes of his; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]

and references in paragraphs (b) and (c) above to another telecommunication system do not include references to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471] a telecommunication system to which subsection (2A) below applies[^c13617011] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471] (whether run by a broadcasting authority or by any other person). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]

(2A)[^c13617021] This subsection applies to a telecommunication system in the case of which every conveyance made by it is either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]

- (a) a transmission, by wireless telegraphy, from a transmitting station for general reception of sounds, visual images or such signals as are mentioned in paragraph (c) of section 4(1) above; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
- (b) a conveyance within a single set of premises of sounds, visual images or such signals which are to be or have been so transmitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]

(3) In the case of a business carried on by a person, section 5(1) above is not contravened by the running, for the purposes of that business, of a telecommunication system which is not connected to another telecommunication system and with respect to which the conditions specified in subsection (4) below are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]

(4) The said conditions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]

- (a) that no person except the person carrying on the business is concerned in the control of the apparatus comprised in the system; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
- (b) that nothing falling within paragraphs (a) to (d) of section 4(1) above is conveyed by the system by way of rendering a service to another; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
- (c) that, in so far as sounds or visual images are conveyed by the system, they are not conveyed for the purpose of their being heard or seen by persons other than the person carrying on the business or any employees of his engaged in the conduct thereof; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
- (d) that in so far as such signals are mentioned in paragraph (c) of section 4(1) above are conveyed by the system, they are not conveyed for the purpose of imparting matter otherwise than to the person carrying on the business, any employees of his engaged in the conduct thereof or things used in the course of the business and controlled by him; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
- (e) that, in so far as such signals as are mentioned in paragraph (d) of section 4(1) above are conveyed by the system, they are not conveyed for the purpose of actuating or controlling machinery or apparatus used otherwise than in the course of the business. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]

(5) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]

- “**broadcasting authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]**” means a person licensed under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471] [^c13617031]Wireless Telegraphy Act 1949 to broadcast programmes for general reception; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
- “**business *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]**” includes a trade, profession or employment and includes any activity carried on by a body of persons, whether corporate or unincorporate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
- “**vessel *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]**” means a vessel of any description used in navigation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
- “**wireless telegraphy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]**” has the same meaning as in the said Act of 1949. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221471]
