# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 27E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27E. Procedures for dealing with complaints.[^c13618341]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19228791] Each designated operator shall establish a procedure for dealing with complaints made by his customers or potential customers in connection with the provision by the designated operator of relevant services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]

(2) No such procedure shall be established, and no modification of such a procedure shall be made, unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]

- (a) the designated operator has consulted persons or bodies appearing to him to be representative of customers for whom he provides relevant services; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]
- (b) the proposed procedure or modification has been approved by the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]

(3) The designated operator shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]

- (a) publicise the procedure in such manner as may be approved by the Director; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]
- (b) send a description of the procedure, free of charge, to any person who asks for one. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]

(4) The Director may give a direction to a designated operator requiring the operator to review his procedure or the manner in which it operates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]

(5) A direction under subsection (4) above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]

- (a) may specify the manner in which the review is to be conducted; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]
- (b) shall require a written report of the review to be made to the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]

(6) Where the Director receives a report under subsection (5)(b) above, he may, after consulting the designated operator, direct him to make such modifications of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]

- (a) the procedure; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]
- (b) the manner in which the procedure operates, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]

as may be specified in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]

(7) Subsection (2) above does not apply to any modification made in compliance with a direction under subsection (6) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228791]
