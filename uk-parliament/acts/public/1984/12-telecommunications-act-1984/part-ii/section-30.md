# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Enforcement provisions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19232251] The Director or a relevant authority shall have power to purchase telecommunication apparatus, and to authorise any of his or their officers to purchase telecommunication apparatus on his or their behalf, for the purpose of ascertaining whether sections 28 and 29 above and orders made under those sections (in this section referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19232251]**the relevant provisions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19232251]**”) are being complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19232251]

(2) The Director shall have power to enforce the relevant provisions and every local weights and measures authority in Great Britain shall have power to enforce those provisions within their area; but nothing in this subsection shall be construed as authorising the Director or a local weights and measures authority to institute proceedings in Scotland for an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19232251]

(3) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19232251]**relevant authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19232251]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19232251]

- (a) in relation to Great Britain, the Secretary of State or a local weights and measures authority on whom a power to enforce the relevant provisions is conferred by subsection (2) above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19232251]
- (b) in relation to Northern Ireland, the Department of Economic Development for Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19232251]
