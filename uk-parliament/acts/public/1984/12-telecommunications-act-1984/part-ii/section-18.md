## 18. Validity and effect of orders.[^c13618231]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c13618241][^c19223541] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c13618241] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Except as provided by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541] section 46B[^c13618261] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541], the validity of a final or provisional order shall not be questioned by any legal proceedings whatever. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]

(4) No criminal proceedings shall, by virtue of the making of a final order or the making or confirmation of a provisional order, lie against any person on the ground that he has committed, or aided, abetted, counselled or procured the commission of, or conspired or attempted to commit, or incited others to commit, any contravention of the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]

(5) The obligation to comply with a final or provisional order is a duty owed to any person who may be affected by a contravention of it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]

(6) Where a duty is owed by virtue of subsection (5) above to any person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]

- (a) any breach of the duty which causes that person to sustain loss or damage; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]
- (b) any act which, by inducing a breach of that duty or interfering with its performance, causes that person to sustain loss or damage and which is done wholly or partly for the purpose of achieving that result, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]

shall be actionable at the suit or instance of that person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]

(7) In any proceedings brought against any person in pursuance of subsection (6)(a) above, it shall be a defence for him to prove that he took all reasonable steps and exercised all due diligence to avoid contavening the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]

(8) Without prejudice to any right which any person may have by virtue of subsection (6)(a) above to bring civil proceedings in respect of any contravention or apprehended contravention of a final or provisional order, compliance with any such order shall be enforceable by civil proceedings by the Director for an injunction or interdict or for any other appropriate relief. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]

(9) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]

- “**act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]**”, in relation to any person, includes any failure to do an act which he is under a duty to do and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]**done *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]**” shall be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]
- “**contravention *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]**”, in relation to a final or provisional order, includes any failure to comply with it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]
- “**the court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]**”—
  - (a) in relation to England and Wales and Northern Ireland, means the High Court; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]
  - (b) in relation to Scotland, means the Court of Session. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19223541]
