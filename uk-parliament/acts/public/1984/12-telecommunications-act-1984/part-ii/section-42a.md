# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 42A

## 42A. Possession or supply of anything for fraudulent purpose in connection with use of telecommunication system.[^c13618881]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19237981] Subsection (2) below applies if a person has in his custody or under his control anything *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981] (other than an unauthorised decoder as defined in section 297A(4) of the[^c13618891] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981] [^c13618901]Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981] which may be used for the purpose of obtaining, or for a purpose connected with the obtaining of, a service to which section 42(1) above applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]

(2) If the person intends— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]

- (a) to use the thing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]
  - (i) to obtain such a service dishonestly, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19237981] for a purpose connected with the dishonest obtaining of such a service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]
- (b) dishonestly to allow the thing to be used to obtain such a service, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]
- (c) to allow the thing to be used for a purpose connected with the dishonest obtaining of such a service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]

he shall be guilty of an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]

(3) Subsection (4) below applies if a person supplies or offers to supply anything *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981] (other than an unauthorised decoder as defined in section 297A(4) of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988)[^c13618911] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]which may be used for the purpose of obtaining, or for a purpose connected with the obtaining of, a service to which section 42(1) above applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]

(4) If the person supplying or offering to supply the thing knows or believes that the person to whom it is supplied or offered intends or intends if it is supplied to him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]

- (a) to use it— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]
  - (i) to obtain such a service dishonestly, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19237981] for a purpose connected with the dishonest obtaining of such a service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]
- (b) dishonestly to allow it to be used to obtain such a service, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]
- (c) to allow it to be used for a purpose connected with the dishonest obtaining of such a service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]

he shall be guilty of an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]

(5) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years or to a fine or to both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]

(6) In this section, references to use of a thing include, in the case of a thing which is used to record any data, use of any of the data. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237981]
