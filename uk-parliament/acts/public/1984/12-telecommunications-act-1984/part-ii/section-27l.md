# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 27L

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27L. Definitions for sections 27A to 27K.[^c13618411]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19229861] For the purposes of sections 27A to 27K above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]

- “**designated operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]**” means any public telecommunications operator designated for the purposes of those sections by order made by the Secretary of State; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
- “**relevant services *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
  - (a) any voice telephony service, telephone rental service, directory service, directory information service or facsimile transmission service provided for occupiers of residential or single line premises; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
  - (b) any public call box service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]

(2) The Secretary of State shall not exercise his power under subsection (1) above to designate a telecommunications operator unless he is satisfied that the operator provides at least 25 per cent. of the voice telephony services supplied within the area in relation to which he is a public telecommunications operator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]

(3) For the purposes of this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]

- “**public call box service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]**” means a service which is provided by a designated operator and which consists of the provision of telecommunication apparatus which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
  - (a) is owned and operated by the designated operator; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
  - (b) gives access to a voice telephony service; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
  - (c) is intended for use by members of the public generally; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
- “**directory service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]**” means a service which consists of the preparation and provision of a list (which may be made available in separate parts and through different media) of customers of a designated operator which is not arranged by reference to a description of the trades, professions or businesses carried on by those customers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
- “**directory information service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]**” has the same meaning as in section 4(3) above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
- “**facsimile transmission service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]**” means a telecommunication service for the transmission of electronic signals by a designated operator, over exchange lines provided by him, for the purposes of making a facsimile of a document; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
- “**hard wired telephone *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]**” means a telephone of a kind which can only be connected to a public telecommunication system by means other than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
  - (a) the insertion of a plug into a socket; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
  - (b) wireless telegraphy; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
- “**single line premises *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]**” means premises which are not residential premises but which are served by a single exchange line provided by the designated operator; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
- “**telephone rental service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]**” means a service consisting in the hiring out of any hard wired telephone which is capable of emitting or receiving signals which have been, or are to be, conveyed by means of a public telecommunication system run by a designated operator; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
- “**voice telephony service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]**” means a telecommunication service for the conveyance of speech over exchange lines provided by the designated operator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19229861]
