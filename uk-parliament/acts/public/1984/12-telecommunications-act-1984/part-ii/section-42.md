# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Fraudulent use of telecommunication system.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19237971] A person who dishonestly obtains a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237971] service to which this subsection applies[^c13618831] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237971] with intent to avoid payment of any charge applicable to the provision of that service shall be guilty of an offence and liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237971]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237971]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237971] five years[^c13618841] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237971] or to a fine or to both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237971]

(2) Subsection (1) above applies to any service (other than a service[^c13618851] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237971] such as is mentioned in section 297(1) of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988)[^c13618861] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237971] which is provided by means of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237971] a telecommunication system the running of which is authorised by a licence granted under section 7 above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237971]
