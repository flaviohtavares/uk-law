# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 27C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27C. Information with respect to levels of performance.[^c13618321]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19228731] The Director shall from time to time collect information with respect to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228731]

- (a) the compensation made by designated operators under section 27A above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228731]
- (b) the levels of overall performance achieved by designated operators in connection with the provision of relevant services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228731]

(2) At such times as the Director may direct, each designated operator shall give the following information to the Director— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228731]

- (a) as respects each standard prescribed by regulations under section 27A above, the number of cases in which compensation was made and the aggregate amount or value of that compensation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228731]
- (b) as respects each standard determined under section 27B above, such information with respect to the level of performance achieved by the operator as may be so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228731]

(3) A designated operator who, without reasonable excuse, fails to do anything required of him by subsection (2) above shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228731]

(4) The Director shall, at least once in every year, arrange for the publication, in such form and in such manner as he considers appropriate, of such of the information collected by or furnished to him under this section as it may appear to him expedient to give to users or potential users of any relevant services provided by designated operators. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228731]

(5) In arranging for the publication of any such information the Director shall have regard to the need for excluding, so far as practicable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228731]

- (a) any matter which relates to the affairs of an individual, where publication of that matter would or might, in the opinion of the Director, seriously and prejudicially affect the interests of that individual; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228731]
- (b) any matter which relates specifically to the affairs of a particular body of persons, whether corporate or unincorporate, where publication of that matter would or might, in the opinion of the Director, seriously and prejudicially affect the interests of that body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228731]
