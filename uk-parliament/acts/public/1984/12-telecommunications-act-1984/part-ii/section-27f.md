# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 27F

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27F. Disputes about discrimination etc. in fixing charges.[^c13618351]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19228861] Any dispute, of a kind to which this section applies, between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

- (a) a person (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]**the customer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]**”) who is, or wishes to be, provided with any relevant service by a designated operator, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]
- (b) that designated operator, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

may be referred to the Director by either party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

(2) This section applies to any dispute as to whether the designated operator— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

- (a) has exercised undue discrimination against the customer in respect of charges applied, or to be applied, in connection with the provision of the service in question; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]
- (b) has shown undue preference to any other person in respect of such charges, to the detriment of the customer; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]
- (c) has applied, or proposes to apply, any charge in connection with the provision of the service in question to the customer which is neither specified in, nor determined in accordance with a method specified in, a notice required by a condition of a kind mentioned in section 8(1)(e) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

(3) Where a dispute is referred to him under this section, the Director, or an arbitrator (or in Scotland an arbiter) appointed by him, shall determine whether the customer’s allegation is well founded and, if it is, make such order as he considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

(4) Any person making an order under subsection (3) above shall include in the order his reasons for reaching his decision with respect to the dispute. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

(5) No act or omission of a designated operator which is permitted by any condition— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

- (a) relating to any of the matters referred to in section 8(1)(d) above, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]
- (b) included in the licence granted to him under section 7 above, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

shall be taken to constitute undue discrimination or undue preference for the purposes of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

(6) The practice and procedure to be followed in connection with a reference under this section shall be determined by the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

(7) An order under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

- (a) may include such incidental, supplemental and consequential provision (including provision requiring either party to pay a sum in respect of the costs or expenses incurred by the person making the order) as that person considers appropriate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]
- (b) shall be final and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]
  - (i) in England and Wales and in Northern Ireland enforceable, in so far as it includes such provision as to costs or expenses, as if it were a judgment of a county court; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19228861] in Scotland, enforceable as if it were an extract registered decree arbitral bearing a warrant for execution issued by the sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]

(8) In including in an order under this section any such provision as to costs or expenses, the person making the order shall have regard to the conduct and means of the parties and any other relevant circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228861]
