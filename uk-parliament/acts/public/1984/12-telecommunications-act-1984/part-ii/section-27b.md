# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 27B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27B. Overall standards of performance.[^c13618311][^c13618291]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19227601] The Director may from time to time— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227601]

- (a) determine such standards of overall performance in connection with the provision of relevant services by the designated operator as, in his opinion, ought to be achieved by that operator; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227601]
- (b) arrange for the publication, in such form and in such manner as he considers appropriate, of the standards so determined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227601]

(2) The Director may only make a determination under subsection (1)(a) above after— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227601]

- (a) consulting the designated operator concerned and persons or bodies appearing to the Director to be representative of persons likely to be affected; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227601]
- (b) arranging for such research as the Director considers appropriate with a view to discovering the views of a representative sample of persons likely to be affected and considering the results. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227601]
