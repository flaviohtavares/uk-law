# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 27H

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27H. Deposits.[^c13618371]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19228911] Each designated operator shall, with the agreement of the Director, settle criteria by reference to which the operator will determine— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228911]

- (a) whether a customer is required to pay a deposit before being provided with any relevant service; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228911]
- (b) if so, the amount which he is required to pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228911]

(2) The criteria may be varied by the designated operator with the consent of the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228911]

(3) Before settling, or varying, the criteria the designated operator shall consult persons or bodies appearing to him to be representative of persons likely to be affected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228911]

(4) Except in such circumstances as may be specified in the criteria, no person who is disabled (as defined by the criteria) shall be required to pay a deposit before being provided with any relevant service by the designated operator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228911]

(5) The designated operator shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228911]

- (a) prepare a summary of the criteria, with the agreement of the Director; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228911]
- (b) publicise it in such manner as may be approved by the Director; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228911]
- (c) send a copy of it, free of charge, to any person who asks for one. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228911]

(6) The Director may, after consulting the designated operator, direct him to vary the criteria as specified in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228911]

(7) Subsection (3) above does not apply to any variation made in compliance with a direction under subsection (6) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228911]
