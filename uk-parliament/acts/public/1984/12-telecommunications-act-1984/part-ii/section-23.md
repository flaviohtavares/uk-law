# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Register of approved apparatus etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19227251] The Director shall keep a register of approvals given and designations made under section 22 above at such premises and in such form as he may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227251]

(2) Subject to subsection (3) below and to any direction given under subsection (4) below, the Director shall cause particulars of every such approval or designation, and of every variation or withdrawal of such an approval or designation, to be entered in the register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227251]

(3) The Director shall not enter in the register particulars of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227251]

- (a) any approval given or designation made by the Secretary of State under section 22 above a copy of which is not sent to the Director under subsection (11) of that section; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227251]
- (b) any variation or withdrawal of an approval so given or a designation so made particulars of which are not so sent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227251]

(4) Subsections (3) to (6) of section 19 above shall apply for the purposes of this section as they apply for the purposes of that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227251]
