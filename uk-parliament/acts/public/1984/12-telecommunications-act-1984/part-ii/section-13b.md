# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 13B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13B. References under section 13: powers of investigation[^c13617931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19377181] The following sections of Part 3 of the Enterprise Act 2002 shall apply, with the modifications mentioned in subsections (2) and (3) below, for the purposes of references under section 13 above as they apply for the purposes of references under that Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]

- (a) section 109 (attendance of witnesses and production of documents etc.); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]
- (b) section 110 (enforcement of powers under section 109: general); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]
- (c) section 111 (penalties); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]
- (d) section 112 (penalties: main procedural requirements); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]
- (e) section 113 (payments and interest by instalments); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]
- (f) section 114 (appeals in relation to penalties); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]
- (g) section 115 (recovery of penalties); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]
- (h) section 116 (statement of policy). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]

(2) Section 110 shall, in its application by virtue of subsection (1) above, have effect as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]

- (a) subsection (2) were omitted; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]
- (b) in subsection (9) the words from “or section” to “section 65(3))” were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]

(3) Section 111(5)(b)(ii) shall, in its application by virtue of subsection (1) above, have effect as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]

- (a) for the words “published (or, in the case of a report under section 50 or 65, given)” there were substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181] “ made ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181];
- (b) for the words “published (or given)”, in both places where they appear, there were substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181] “ made ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]
- (c) the words “by this Part” were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]

(4) Provisions of Part 3 of the Enterprise Act 2002 which have effect for the purposes of sections 109 to 116 of that Act (including, in particular, provisions relating to offences and the making of orders) shall, for the purposes of the application of those sections by virtue of subsection (1) above, have effect in relation to those sections as applied by virtue of that subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]

(5) Accordingly, corresponding provisions of this Act shall not have effect in relation to those sections as applied by virtue of that subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19377181]
