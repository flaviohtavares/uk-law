# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Prohibition on running unlicensed systems.[^c13616971]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19221461] Subject to the provisions of this section and section 6 below, a person who runs a telecommunication system within the United Kingdom shall be guilty of an offence unless he is authorised to run the system by a licence granted under section 7 below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]

(2) Subject to the provisions of this section, a person who runs within the United Kingdom a telecommunication system which he is authorised to run by a licence granted under section 7 below shall be guilty of an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]

- (a) there is connected to the system— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]
  - (i) any other telecommunication system; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19221461] any apparatus, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]

  which is not authorised by the licence to be so connected; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]
- (b) there are provided by means of the system any telecommunication services which are not authorised by the licence to be so provided. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]

(3) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]

(4) Where the commission by any person of an offence under this section is due to the act or default of some other person, that other person shall be guilty of the offence; and a person may be charged with and convicted of the offence by virtue of this subsection whether or not proceedings are taken against the first-mentioned person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]

(5) In any proceedings for an offence under this section it shall, subject to subsection (6) below, be a defence for the person charged to prove that he took all reasonable steps and exercised all due diligence to avoid committing the offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]

(6) Where the defence provided by subsection (5) above involves an allegation that the commission of the offence was due to the act or default of another person, the person charged shall not, without leave of the court, be entitled to rely on that defence unless, within a period ending seven clear days before the hearing, he has served on the prosecutor a notice in writing giving such information identifying or assisting in the identification of that other person as was then in his possession. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]

(7) No proceedings shall be instituted in England and Wales or Northern Ireland in respect of an offence under this section except by or on behalf of the Secretary of State or the Director. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19221461]
