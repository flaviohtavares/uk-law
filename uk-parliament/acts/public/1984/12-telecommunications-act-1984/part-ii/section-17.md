# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Procedural requirements.[^c13618151]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19222501] Before making a final order or confirming a provisional order, the Director shall give notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]

- (a) stating that he proposes to make or confirm the order and setting out its effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]
- (b) stating the relevant condition of the licence and the acts or omissions which, in his opinion, constitute or would constitute contraventions of it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]
- (c) specifying the time (not being less than 28 days from the date of publication of the notice) within which representations or objections to the proposed order or confirmation of the order may be made, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]

and shall consider any representations or objections which are duly made and not withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]

(2) The Director shall not make a final order, or confirm a provisional order, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501] [^c13618161]. . . except with the consent of the telecommunications operator or after complying with the requirements of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501] subsections (3) and (3A)[^c13618171] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501] below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]

(3) The said requirements are that the Director shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]

- (a) give to the telecommunications operator such notice as appears to him requisite of his proposal to make or confirm the order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501] [^c13618181]. . .;
- (b) specify the time (not being less than 28 days from the date of the service of the notice) within which representations or objections to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501] proposal[^c13618191] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501] may be made; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]
- (c) consider any representations or objections which are duly made and not withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]

(3A)[^c13618201] If the Director makes a final order, such order shall be made within two months of the notice given under subsection (3)(a) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]

(4) Before revoking a final order or a provisional order which has been confirmed, the Director shall give notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]

- (a) stating that he proposes to revoke the order and setting out its effect; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]
- (b) specifying the time (not being less than 28 days) from the date of publication of the notice within which representations or objections to the proposed revocation may be made, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]

and shall consider any representations or objections which are duly made and not withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]

(5) A notice under subsection (1) or (4) above shall be given by publication in such manner as the Director considers appropriate for the purpose of bringing the matters to which the notice relates to the attention of persons likely to be affected by them and by sending a copy of the notice to the telecommunications operator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]

(6) As soon as practicable after a final order is made or a provisional order is made or confirmed, the Director shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]

- (a) publish the order in such manner as he considers appropriate for the purpose of bringing the order to the attention of persons likely to be affected by it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]
- (b)[^c13618211] serve a copy of the order on the telecommunications operator within one week of the order being made or confirmed with a statement of the reasons for his decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19222501]
