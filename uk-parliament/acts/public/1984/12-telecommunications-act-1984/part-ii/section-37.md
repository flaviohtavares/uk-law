# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Entry, for exploratory purposes, on land in England and Wales.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19237901] A person nominated by a public telecommunications operator and duly authorised in writing by the Secretary of State may, at any reasonable time, enter upon and survey any land in England and Wales, other than land covered by buildings or used as a garden or pleasure ground, for the purpose of ascertaining whether the land would be suitable for use by the operator for, or in connection with, the establishment or running of the operator’s system. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901]

(2) Sections 324(8), 325(1) to (5), (8) and (9) of the[^c13618641] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901] [^c13618651]Town and Country Planning Act 1990 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901] (which contain supplementary provisions relating to the powers of entry conferred by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901] section 324(1) to (7) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901] thereof) shall have effect in relation to the power conferred by this section as they have effect in relation to the powers conferred by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901] the said section 324 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901], subject however to the following modifications, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901]

- (a) that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901] section 324(8) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901] (which relates to power to search and bore for the purpose of ascertaining the nature of the subsoil or the presence of minerals therein) shall so have effect as if the words “or the presence of minerals therein” were omitted; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901]
- (b) that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901] section 325(1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901] (which requires twenty-four hours’ notice to be given of an intended entry upon occupied land) shall so have effect as if for the words “twenty-four hours” there were substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901] “ twenty-eight days ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901].

(3) Where, in an exercise of the power conferred by this section, any damage is caused to land or to chattels, the operator shall make good the damage or pay to every person interested in the land or chattels compensation in respect of the damage; and where, in consequence of an exercise of that power, any person is disturbed in his enjoyment of any land or chattels, the operator shall pay to that person compensation in respect of the disturbance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901]

(4) Section 118 of the said Act of 1990[^c13618701] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901] (which provides for the determination of disputes as to compensation under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901] Part IV of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901]) shall apply to any question of disputed compensation under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19237901]
