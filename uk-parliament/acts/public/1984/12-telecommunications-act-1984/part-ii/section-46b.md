# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 46B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46B. Appeals[^c19219551][^c19219571][^c13619081]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19238641] This section shall apply to the following decisions of the Secretary of State or the Director under this Part taken on or after 20th December 1999— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]

- (a) a decision to grant or refuse to grant a licence under section 7; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (b) a decision to include within a licence on its grant particular provisions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
  - (i) describing the telecommunication systems authorised to be run under the licence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19238641] describing the connections authorised to be made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19238641] describing the telecommunication services authorised to be provided; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19238641] imposing a condition; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
  - (v) applying the telecommunications code to the licensee subject to such exceptions and conditions as may be included in the licence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (c) a decision with regard to the designation under section 9 of a telecommunication system as a public telecommunication system; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (d) a decision with regard to the modification of any exception or condition included in a licence as mentioned in section 10(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (da (affected by repeal, see note))[^c13619111][^c19238641] a decision with regard to the modification under section 12 of a condition of a licence granted under section 7 above to a particular person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (e) a decision to exercise any power contained in a licence to give a direction or consent or to make any determination; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (f) a decision with regard to the enforcement of conditions in a licence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (g) a decision to revoke a licence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (h) a decision with regard to approval of any person, apparatus or meter; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (j) any other decison (other than a decision made under sections 12 to 15) in respect of which the rights or interests of a person running or wishing to run a telecommunication system under a licence or making or wishing to make any connection or providing or wishing to provide any telecommunication service by means of a telecommunication system licensed under this Act are materially affected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]

(2) Subject to subsection (5) below, a person aggrieved by a decision to which this section applies may appeal against the decision on one or more of the following grounds— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]

- (a) that a material error as to the facts has been made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (b) that there was a material procedural error; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (c) that an error of law has been made; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (d) that there was some other material illegality, including unreasonableness or lack of proportionality. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]

(3) In England and Wales and Northern Ireland, an appeal lies to the High Court, and in Scotland, an appeal lies to the Court of Session. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]

(4) The court determining an appeal may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]

- (a) dismiss the appeal; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (b) quash the decision, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]

and where the court quashes a decision it may refer the matter to the Secretary of State or the Director (as the case may be) with a direction to reconsider it and reach a decision in accordance with the findings of the court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]

(5) No appeal under this section shall be brought unless the leave of the court has been obtained. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]

(6) An appeal under this section shall be brought— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]

- (a) in respect of a decision made under section 16, without unreasonable delay and in any event not later than 42 days from the date on which the Secretary of State or the Director made his decision; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
- (b) in respect of any other decision to which this section applies, without unreasonable delay and in any event not later than three months from the date on which the Secretary of State or the Director made his decision, or within such other period as may be specified by rules of court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]

(7) The effect of a decision to which an appeal under this section relates shall not, except where the court so orders, be suspended in consequence of the bringing of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]

(8) Any proceedings under this section in the Court of Session shall be before the Lord Ordinary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19238641]
