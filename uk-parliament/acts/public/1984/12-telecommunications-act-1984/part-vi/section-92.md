# Telecommunications Act 1984, section 92

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Interpretation of Part VI and minor amendments.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2007-02-08).**

(1) In this Part—

- “**the 1949 Act**” means the [^c13620971]Wireless Telegraphy Act 1949;
- “**the 1967 Act**” means the [^c13620981]Wireless Telegraphy Act 1967; and
- “**wireless telegraphy**”, “**wireless telegraphy apparatus**”, “**emission**” and “**interference**” have the same meanings as in the 1949 Act.

(2) In section 15(4) of the 1949 Act (obstruction of search and seizure powers, etc.)—

- (a) in paragraph (a) the word “ intentionally ” shall be inserted before the word “obstructs”; and
- (b) in paragraph (b) the words “ without reasonable excuse ” shall be inserted before the words “fails or refuses”.

(4) In paragraph 6 of Schedule 2 to the 1949 Act (rules of procedure for appeal tribunal to be made by tribunal) for the words from “by the tribunal” to the end of the paragraph there shall be substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255721] “ by the Secretary of State by statutory instrument. ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19255721].
