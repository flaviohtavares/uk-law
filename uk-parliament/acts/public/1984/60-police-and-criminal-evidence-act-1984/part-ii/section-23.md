# Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, section 23

## 23. Meaning of “premises” etc.[^key-2ace9d2b3bdcbb127c1e9b05e8416b77][^key-451210b39928c21c0f6a237f9407653a][^key-24cc59714766dc9a5225c3524dc2631e]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Act—

- “**premises**” includes any place and, in particular, includes—
  - (a) any vehicle, vessel, aircraft or hovercraft;
  - (b) any offshore installation;
  - (ba) any renewable energy installation;[^c18945231]
  - (c) any tent or movable structure; [^c18946661]. . .
- “**offshore installation**” has the meaning given to it by section 1 of the [^c11315601]Mineral Workings (Offshore Installations) Act 1971.
- “[^c18945621]**renewable energy installation**” has the same meaning as in Chapter 2 of Part 2 of the Energy Act 2004.
