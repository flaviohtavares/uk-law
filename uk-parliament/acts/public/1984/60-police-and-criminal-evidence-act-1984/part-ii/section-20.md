# Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, section 20

## 20. Extension of powers of seizure to computerised information.[^c11315361][^c16921021][^c18904101][^c18904171][^c18904201][^c-9a1ab069ee8c4b769d0dd27ed40616c8][^c-8684bfeaae42c75fa000dae70a6a3d59][^c-bc19e85baacaa69a4ddd5ca9961a1bdb][^c-c7d1b979d5a60e4c97c9c7296c0e5362][^c-39951c5cc074ffc14c53d5a5af8089e7][^c-f5a04b0fe0365aacb28f9018868d4f0a][^c-5473719ba15003f31adeb50e40ef1cc8][^c-5cc724ff3fb94f337e68756ba102a81a][^c-354c497032dc4597a0199732eebf4150][^c11315351]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Every power of seizure which is conferred by an enactment to which this section applies on a constable who has entered premises in the exercise of a power conferred by an enactment shall be construed as including a power to require any information stored in any electronic form[^c11315371] contained in a computer and accessible from the premises to be produced in a form in which it can be taken away and in which it is visible and legible or from which it can readily be produced in a visible and legible form[^c11315381].

(2) This section applies—

- (a) to any enactment contained in an Act passed before this Act;
- (b) to sections 8 and 18 above;
- (c) to paragraph 13 of Schedule 1 to this Act; and
- (d) to any enactment contained in an Act passed after this Act.
