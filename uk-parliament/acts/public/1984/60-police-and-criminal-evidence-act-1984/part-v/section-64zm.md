# Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, section 64ZM

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 64ZM). (not yet in force: Destruction of copies, and notification of destruction)[^c20929631]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: If fingerprints or impressions of footwear are required to be destroyed by virtue of any of sections 64ZB to 64ZJ, any copies of the fingerprints or impressions of footwear must also be destroyed.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: If a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile is required to be destroyed by virtue of any of those sections, no copy may be kept except in a form which does not include information which identifies the person to whom the)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile relates.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: If a person makes a request to the responsible chief officer of police to be notified when anything relating to the person is destroyed under any of sections 64ZA to 64ZJ, the responsible chief officer of police or a person authorised by the chief officer or on the chief officer's behalf must within three months of the request issue the person with a certificate recording the destruction.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: Responsible chief officer of police)****(not yet in force: ” means the chief officer of police for the police area—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: in which the samples, fingerprints or impressions of footwear which have been destroyed were taken, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in the case of a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile which has been destroyed, in which the samples from which the)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile was derived were taken.)*
