# Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, section 64ZB

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 64ZB). (not yet in force: Destruction of data given voluntarily)[^c20929631]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This section applies to—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: fingerprints or impressions of footwear taken in connection with the investigation of an offence with the consent of the person from whom they were taken, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile derived from a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: sample taken in connection with the investigation of an offence with the consent of the person from whom the sample was taken.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Material to which this section applies must be destroyed as soon as it has fulfilled the purpose for which it was taken or derived, unless it is—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: material relating to a person who is convicted of the offence,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: material relating to a person who has previously been convicted of a recordable offence, other than a person who has only one exempt conviction,)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: material in relation to which any of sections 64ZC to 64ZH applies, or)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: material which is not required to be destroyed by virtue of consent given under section 64ZL.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: If material to which this section applies leads to the person to whom the material relates being arrested for or charged with an offence other than the offence under investigation—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the material is not required to be destroyed by virtue of this section, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: sections 64ZD to 64ZH have effect in relation to the material as if the material was taken (or, in the case of a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile, was derived from material taken) in connection with the investigation of the offence in respect of which the person is arrested or charged.)*
