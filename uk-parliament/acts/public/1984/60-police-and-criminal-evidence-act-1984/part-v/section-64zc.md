# Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, section 64ZC

> This provision is not yet in force.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## (not yet in force: 64ZC). (not yet in force: Destruction of data relating to a person subject to a control order)[^c20929631]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This section applies to material falling within subsection (2) relating to a person who—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: has no previous convictions or only one exempt conviction, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: is subject to a control order.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Material falls within this subsection if it is—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: fingerprints taken from the person, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile derived from a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: sample taken from the person.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The material must be destroyed before the end of the period of 2 years beginning with the date on which the person ceases to be subject to a control order.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: This section ceases to have effect in relation to the material if the person is convicted—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: in England and Wales or Northern Ireland of a recordable offence, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in Scotland of an offence which is punishable by imprisonment,)*

*(not yet in force: before the material is required to be destroyed by virtue of this section.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of subsection (1)—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a person has no previous convictions if the person has not previously been convicted—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: in England and Wales or Northern Ireland of a recordable offence, or)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: in Scotland of an offence which is punishable by imprisonment, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: if the person has been previously convicted of a recordable offence in England and Wales or Northern Ireland, the conviction is exempt if it is in respect of a recordable offence other than a qualifying offence, committed when the person is aged under 18.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of that subsection—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a person is to be treated as having been convicted of an offence if—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: he has been given a caution in England and Wales or Northern Ireland in respect of the offence which, at the time of the caution, he has admitted,)* *(not yet in force: and[^c-118d6aeaa5c565677ade4ff504e3c10c])*
  - ((not yet in force: ii))[^c-cc0703e8dd0175a856852eaa109d5c27] *(not yet in force: . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: if a person is convicted of more than one offence arising out of a single course of action, those convictions are to be treated as a single conviction.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: In this section—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: “recordable offence” has, in relation to a conviction in Northern Ireland, the meaning given by Article 2(2) of the Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, and)*
