# Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, section 64ZL

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## (not yet in force: 64ZL). (not yet in force: Retention with consent)[^c20929631]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: If a person consents in writing to the retention of fingerprints, impressions of footwear or a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile to which section 64 applies, other than fingerprints taken under section 61(6A)—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the material is not required to be destroyed in accordance with sections 64ZB to 64ZH, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: section 64ZN(2) does not apply to the material.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: It is immaterial for the purposes of subsection (1) whether the consent is given at, before or after the time when the entitlement to the destruction of the material arises.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Consent given under this section can be withdrawn at any time.)*
