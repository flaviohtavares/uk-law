# Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, section 63M

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/60

## 63M. Retention of section 63D material for purposes of national security[^c-5233504a3e132704cf86bdfb21de097f][^c-7ffa1adec1d7956934e69a046842bbff]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 63D material may be retained for as long as a national security determination made by a[^c-90918174e1c4ae63a7709adf851cac0f] chief officer of police has effect in relation to it.

(2) A national security determination is made if a[^c-c7d80d3f9532f6c1965bed7c303a5085] chief officer of police determines that it is necessary for any section 63D material to be retained for the purposes of national security.

(3) A national security determination—

- (a) must be made in writing,
- (b) has effect for a maximum of 5 years[^c-6e7dffa13dfaf96b860f6f5d1041267b] beginning with the date on which it is made, and
- (c) may be renewed.
