# Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, section 64ZD

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## (not yet in force: 64ZD). (not yet in force: Destruction of data relating to persons not convicted)[^c20929631]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This section applies to material falling within subsection (2) relating to a person who—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: has no previous convictions or only one exempt conviction,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: is arrested for or charged with a recordable offence, and)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: is aged 18 or over at the time of the alleged offence.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Material falls within this subsection if it is—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: fingerprints or impressions of footwear taken from the person in connection with the investigation of the offence, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile derived from a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: sample so taken.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The material must be destroyed—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: in the case of fingerprints or impressions of footwear, before the end of the period of 6 years beginning with the date on which the fingerprints or impressions were taken,)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in the case of a)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: profile, before the end of the period of 6 years beginning with the date on which the)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: sample from which the profile was derived was taken (or, if the profile was derived from more than one)* *(not yet in force: DNA)* *(not yet in force: sample, the date on which the first of those samples was taken).)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: But if, before the material is required to be destroyed by virtue of this section, the person is arrested for or charged with a recordable offence the material may be further retained until the end of the period of 6 years beginning with the date of the arrest or charge.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: This section ceases to have effect in relation to the material if the person is convicted of a recordable offence before the material is required to be destroyed by virtue of this section.)*
