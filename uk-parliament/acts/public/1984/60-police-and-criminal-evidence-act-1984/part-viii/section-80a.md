# Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, section 80A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/60
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80A. Rule where accused’s spouse or civil partner not compellable.[^c11330371][^c11330361][^c19019611]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The failure of the spouse or civil partner[^c19019591] of a person charged in any proceedings to give evidence in the proceedings shall not be made the subject of any comment by the prosecution.
