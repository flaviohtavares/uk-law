# Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, section 114B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/60
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114B. Application of Act to labour abuse prevention officers (affected by repeal, see note) enforcement officers[^c-ad2d87f251fb9c07781451ab9c485d1f][^c-70c917bad297fa4866be993a6156a5ae]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations apply any provision of this Act which relates to investigations of offences conducted by police officers to investigations of labour market offences conducted by labour abuse prevention officers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a278ef892ced63af5d97bb33c5f29cb4]enforcement officers.

(2) The regulations may apply provisions of this Act with any modifications specified in the regulations.

(3)[^c-a9f7c2a2b2bfd23f91a77508412979bb] In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a9f7c2a2b2bfd23f91a77508412979bb]**labour abuse prevention officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a9f7c2a2b2bfd23f91a77508412979bb]**” means an officer of the Gangmasters and Labour Abuse Authority who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a9f7c2a2b2bfd23f91a77508412979bb]

- (a) falls within subsection (4), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a9f7c2a2b2bfd23f91a77508412979bb]
- (b) is authorised (whether generally or specifically) by the Secretary of State for the purposes of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a9f7c2a2b2bfd23f91a77508412979bb]

(4)[^c-647a2e39e40d3e7102dfc32e84c35923] An officer of the Gangmasters and Labour Abuse Authority falls within this subsection if he or she is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-647a2e39e40d3e7102dfc32e84c35923]

- (a) acting for the purposes of the Employment Agencies Act 1973 (see section 8A of that Act), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-647a2e39e40d3e7102dfc32e84c35923]
- (b) acting for the purposes of the National Minimum Wage Act 1998 (see section 13 of that Act), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-647a2e39e40d3e7102dfc32e84c35923]
- (c) acting for the purposes of the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 as an enforcement officer within the meaning of section 15 of that Act, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-647a2e39e40d3e7102dfc32e84c35923]
- (d) acting for the purposes of Part 1 or 2 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015 (see sections 11A and 30A of that Act), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-647a2e39e40d3e7102dfc32e84c35923]
- (e) acting for any other purpose prescribed in regulations made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-647a2e39e40d3e7102dfc32e84c35923]

(5) The investigations for the purposes of which provisions of this Act may be applied by regulations under this section include investigations of offences committed, or suspected of having been committed, before the coming into force of the regulations or of this section.

(6) Regulations under this section are to be made by statutory instrument.

(7) Regulations under this section may make—

- (a) different provision for different purposes;
- (b) provision which applies generally or for particular purposes;
- (c) incidental, supplementary, consequential, transitional or transitory provision or savings.

(8)[^c-40fecb8e9e2bcc94f29e2e7dd50e31da] Regulations under subsection (4)(e) may, in particular, make such provision amending, repealing or revoking any enactment as the Secretary of State considers appropriate in consequence of any provision made by the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-40fecb8e9e2bcc94f29e2e7dd50e31da]

(9)[^c-d4bc3b8a5c2bdcaaed5681a56eb183d6] A statutory instrument containing regulations under subsection (4)(e) may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4bc3b8a5c2bdcaaed5681a56eb183d6]

(10) Any other *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f3b6e6cb84d38cff98f432c584913295] A statutory instrument containing regulations under this section is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(11)[^c-ea98f1190722531b7aeba10cbb6c5f06] In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ea98f1190722531b7aeba10cbb6c5f06]

- “**enactment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ea98f1190722531b7aeba10cbb6c5f06]**” includes an enactment contained in subordinate legislation within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ea98f1190722531b7aeba10cbb6c5f06]
- “**labour market offence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ea98f1190722531b7aeba10cbb6c5f06]**” has the meaning given in section 3 of the Immigration Act 2016. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ea98f1190722531b7aeba10cbb6c5f06]

(11) In this section—

- “**enforcement officer**” has the meaning given by section 90(3) of the Employment Rights Act 2025;
- “**labour market offence**” has the same meaning as in Part 5 of that Act (see section 151(1) of that Act).
