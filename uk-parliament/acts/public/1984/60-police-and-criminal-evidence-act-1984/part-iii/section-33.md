# Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/60
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Execution of warrant not in possession of constable.[^c11321201]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 125 of the [^c11321211]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980—

- (a) in subsection (3), for the words “arrest a person charged with an offence” there shall be substituted the words “which this subsection applies”;
- (b) the following subsection shall be added after that subsection—

  > (4) The warrants to which subsection (3) above applies are—
  >
  > - (a) a warrant to arrest a person in connection with an offence;
  > - (b) without prejudice to paragraph (a) above, a warrant under section 186(3) of the [^c11321221]Army Act 1955, section 186(3) of the [^c11321231]Air Force Act 1955, section 105(3) of the [^c11321241]Naval Discipline Act 1957 or Schedule 5 to the [^c11321251]Reserve Forces Act 1980 (desertion etc.);
  > - (c) a warrant under—
  >   - (i) section 102 or 104 of the [^c11321261]General Rate Act 1967 (insufficiency of distress);
  >   - (ii) section 18(4) of the [^c11321271]Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates’ Courts Act 1978 (protection of parties to marriage and children of family); and
  >   - (iii) section 55, 76, 93 or 97 above.
