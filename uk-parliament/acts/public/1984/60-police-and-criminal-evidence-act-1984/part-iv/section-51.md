# Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/60
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Savings.

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in this Part of this Act shall affect—

- (a) the powers conferred on immigration officers by section 4 of and Schedule 2 to the [^c11323471]Immigration Act 1971 (administrative provisions as to control on entry etc.);
- (b)[^c-7c99981ddcc1b5aeae16bf1872a31beb][^c11323481] the powers conferred by virtue of section 41 or 43B[^c-6ce8f29b5780c16178a508ffac217758] of, or Schedule 7 to, the Terrorism Act 2000 (powers of arrest and detention); [^c-aad8d31cb17657022978a16d88cf6b3e]...
- (ba)[^c-863591a7fc4c8d379c36614cabecdebc] the powers conferred by virtue of Part 1 of Schedule 3 to the Counter-Terrorism and Border Security Act 2019 (powers of detention);
- (bb)[^c-c9c85119ef45d980d0beafcc6d2e720d] the powers conferred by virtue of section 27 of and Schedule 6 to the National Security Act 2023;
- (c) [^c19009601]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c11323521][^c-5ca135cc0eb88abd46b6972a65d5ef77] any right of a person in police detention to apply for a writ of habeas corpus or other prerogative remedy.
