## 118. Compensation where interest in land subject to heritable security.

*Extent: S*

Subsection (4) of section 87 of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997[^c1610847] (provisions as to compensation where an interest in land is subject to a heritable security) shall apply in relation to such compensation as is mentioned in section 71(6), 72(1), 83(8) or 88(2), or as may be provided for in an agreement under section 53(2), of this Act as the said subsection (4) applies in relation to compensation to which the said section 87 applies.
