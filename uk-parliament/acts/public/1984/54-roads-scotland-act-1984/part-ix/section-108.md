## 108. Acquisition of land for provision of picnic site.[^c-a38930988c5127b02f6fd9fcbf27eed5]

*Extent: S*

Subject to section 109 of this Act, the Secretary of State may acquire land required in connection with the provision under section 55 of this Act of a picnic site.
