## 49A. Power to install equipment for detection of traffic offences etc .[^c1610709][^key-eaa36dc656176ccd67003983b1aba846]

*Extent: S*

A roads authority may install and maintain on or near a road structures and equipment for the detection of traffic offences or offences under section 11 of the[^key-e8f7bc5e685e64051506422656425aaf] HGV Road User Levy Act 2013 (using or keeping heavy goods vehicle if HGV road user levy not paid) .
