## 55B. Application of section 20B[^key-1f3d16d3eb0d46aee69790be68c5e27d][^key-29ba69cf2ef16f1728467d9569f53d63]

*Extent: S*

The provisions of sections 20A, 20B and 20D to 20G apply to a project to which section 55A applies as they apply to a project in terms of sections 20A, 20B and 20D to 20G.
