## 143A. “Developments of national significance” etc.: special procedure[^c-93f2b7ed9408181423f4a0edc7b5c08d]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to subsection (4), subsection (3) applies to any[^c-1959a4a40f8fb82896daa6604d570477] —

- (a) [^c-b95502e937f328b2ad782a6610f1c4c5]... order under section 5 of this Act directing that a road proposed to be constructed shall be a trunk road and the construction of the road would constitute a national development;
- (b) [^c-f27f4e175a579a31c6d3bf150f74a782]... scheme under section 7 of this Act (or any instrument confirming such a scheme)[^c-2af40e54876e39a7b0783e381bab8e9b] which authorises the carrying out of work which would constitute a national development; or
- (c) subordinate legislation which[^c-dbf07520697ffa12953f9d95e8f7a470] is the subject of a direction by the Scottish Ministers under this paragraph.

(2) In subsection (1) above, the references to a “national development” are to any development (within the meaning of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997) for the time being designated under section 3A(4)(b) of that Act as a national development.

(3)[^c-eea79def9b26199a784153b4b20c6617] The legislation is subject to the affirmative procedure.

(4) Unless the Scottish Ministers otherwise direct, any subordinate legislation[^c-7458ed2588956812401a71442026f9c9] which revokes, amends or re-enacts any legislation which was subject to the affirmative procedure by virtue[^c-8e97c745428269ee2d02389a817abdf3] of subsection (3) above is not subject to the procedure in that subsection.
