# Roads (Scotland) Act 1984, section 137

## 137. Service of notices.[^c1610916]

*Extent: S*

Section 192 of the [^c1610917]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 (service of notices) shall apply to notices under this Act relating to land as that section applies to notices under that Act relating to premises.
