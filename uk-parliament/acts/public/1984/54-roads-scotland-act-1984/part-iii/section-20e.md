## 20E. Competent authority – avoidance of conflict of interest[^key-01824b2d26321a17af4b99db3cd68399][^key-69eca98abd5db250ac552fea222ab7ee]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers must perform their duties under Part III of this Act in an objective manner and so as not to find themselves in a situation giving rise to a conflict of interest.

(2) Where the Scottish Ministers are considering whether to proceed with a new road construction project they must implement within their organisation of administrative competencies an appropriate separation between conflicting functions when performing their duties under this Part[^key-c723da9c15f21f74dc796df6c2b01b95].
