## 37. Directions as to distribution and transfer of family business and proceedings.

*Extent: E+W*

The President of the Family Division may, with the concurrence of the Lord Chancellor, give directions with respect to the distribution and transfer between the High Court and the family court[^c-a86279c5528cd9af85456eedd177e1ae] of family business and family proceedings.
