# Matrimonial and Family Proceedings Act 1984, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1984/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Short title and extent.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Matrimonial and Family Proceedings Act 1984.

(2) Parts I to III and V and Schedules 2 and 3 extend to England and Wales only, Part IV extends to Scotland only [^c4595921]. . .

(3) Where any enactment amended by Schedule 1 extends to any part of the United Kingdom, the amendment extends to that part.
