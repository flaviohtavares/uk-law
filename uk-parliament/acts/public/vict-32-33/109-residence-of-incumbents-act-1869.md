# Residence of Incumbents Act 1869

> Text as at 2018-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Residence of Incumbents Act 1869

1869 c.109

An Act . . .[^c5673901] for securing uniformity in the law relating to the residence of spiritual persons upon their benefices, and to the penalties and forfeitures consequent on non-residence.[^c-8be2852556c7d3d8d3ec43e8422d8795]

\[11th August 1869\][^c5673911][^c5673921][^c5673931]

## 1. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c5673941]

## 2. What proceedings may be taken for enforcing residence.

*Extent: E*

No proceeding shall be taken against any spiritual person for enforcing the residence of such spiritual person upon his benefice, or by reason of his non-residence on his benefice, except under the powers and provisions contained in the [^c5673971]Pluralities Act 1838 [^c5673981]. . . or of any Act for the time being in force for amending the same, and no penalty or forfeiture shall be incurred in consequence of the non-residence of any spiritual person upon his benefice, save and except such penalties for non-residence as are provided by the Pluralities Act 1838 [^c5673981]. . . or as shall be provided by any Act for the time being in force for amending the same.
