# Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022, section 172

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 172. List of countries[^key-0eb61999bf6f5a0dc9567381b0f44af9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-c1c50e8184d1bf2c4dfb1fac81fdceeb] The Secretary of State may—

- (a) prepare a list of countries and territories outside the United Kingdom in which the Secretary of State considers children are at a high risk of sexual abuse or sexual exploitation from United Kingdom nationals or United Kingdom residents, or
- (b) direct a relevant person to prepare a list of countries and territories outside the United Kingdom in which the relevant person considers children are at a high risk of sexual abuse or sexual exploitation from United Kingdom nationals or United Kingdom residents.

(2)[^key-c1c50e8184d1bf2c4dfb1fac81fdceeb] If a list is prepared by the Secretary of State, the Secretary of State must lay the list before Parliament.

(3)[^key-c1c50e8184d1bf2c4dfb1fac81fdceeb] If a list is prepared by a relevant person—

- (a) the relevant person must submit the list to the Secretary of State, and
- (b) the Secretary of State must lay the list before Parliament.

(4)[^key-c1c50e8184d1bf2c4dfb1fac81fdceeb] As soon as reasonably practicable after a list has been laid before Parliament, the person who prepared the list must publish it.

(5) A list published under subsection (4) has effect for the purposes of—

- (a)[^key-c344a1fe464f4d3bc08de7e2f55ea070] section 346 of the Sentencing Code (exercise of power to make sexual harm prevention order),
- (b)[^key-c344a1fe464f4d3bc08de7e2f55ea070] section 350 of the Sentencing Code (sexual harm prevention orders: variations, renewals and discharges),
- (c)[^key-c344a1fe464f4d3bc08de7e2f55ea070] section 103A of the Sexual Offences Act 2003 (sexual harm prevention orders: applications and grounds),
- (d)[^key-c344a1fe464f4d3bc08de7e2f55ea070] section 103E of that Act (sexual harm prevention orders: variations, renewals and discharges),
- (e)[^key-c344a1fe464f4d3bc08de7e2f55ea070] section 103F of that Act (interim sexual harm prevention orders),
- (f)[^key-c344a1fe464f4d3bc08de7e2f55ea070] section 122A of that Act (sexual risk orders: applications, grounds and effect),
- (g)[^key-c344a1fe464f4d3bc08de7e2f55ea070] section 122D of that Act (sexual risk orders: variations, renewals and discharges),
- (h)[^key-c344a1fe464f4d3bc08de7e2f55ea070] section 122E of that Act (interim sexual risk orders),
- (i)[^key-fe1e0fedaff9957b3050a7a9b0d3e0fd] section 136ZG of that Act (variation of sexual harm prevention order made in Scotland by court in England and Wales),
- (j)[^key-fe1e0fedaff9957b3050a7a9b0d3e0fd] section 136ZH of that Act (variation of sexual offences prevention order or foreign travel order by court in England and Wales), and
- (k)[^key-fe1e0fedaff9957b3050a7a9b0d3e0fd] section 136ZI of that Act (variation of sexual risk order made in Scotland by court in England and Wales).

(6)[^key-b9f3021ff012caeb139a583ca36569ac] If a list has been published, the person who prepared it must keep it under review and may, from time to time, prepare a revised list (but see subsections (7) and (8)).

(7)[^key-b9f3021ff012caeb139a583ca36569ac] If the function under subsection (6) is for the time being exercisable by the Secretary of State, the Secretary of State may direct a relevant person to exercise that function.

(8)[^key-b9f3021ff012caeb139a583ca36569ac] If the function under subsection (6) is for the time being exercisable by a relevant person, the Secretary of State may direct that the function is to be exercisable by another relevant person or by the Secretary of State.

(9)[^key-b9f3021ff012caeb139a583ca36569ac] A list published under this section may at any time be withdrawn by the Secretary of State.

(10)[^key-b9f3021ff012caeb139a583ca36569ac] Subsections (2) to (9) apply to a revised list as they apply to a list prepared under subsection (1).

(11)[^key-c1c50e8184d1bf2c4dfb1fac81fdceeb] In this section—

- “**child**” means a person under 18;
- “**relevant person**” means a person whose statutory functions relate to—
  - (a) the prevention or detection of crime, or
  - (b) other law enforcement purposes;
- “**United Kingdom national**” means—
  - (a) a British citizen, a British overseas territories citizen, a British National (Overseas) or a British Overseas citizen,
  - (b) a person who under the British Nationality Act 1981 is a British subject, or
  - (c) a British protected person within the meaning of that Act;
- “**United Kingdom resident**” means an individual who is resident in the United Kingdom.
