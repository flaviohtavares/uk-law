## 201. Repeal of temporary provision[^key-18d717a2ecf8fd7c36aa0e27ab11695e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-18d717a2ecf8fd7c36aa0e27ab11695e] In the Coronavirus Act 2020, sections 53 and 54 and Schedules 23 and 24 (which make temporary modifications that are superseded by the provision made by section 200) are repealed.

(2)[^key-2d18fdb0be1c6484e5d7252b4f2f4c87] In the Coronavirus Act 2020, section 55 and Schedule 25 (which make temporary modifications that are superseded by the provision made by sections 198 and 199) are repealed.
