# Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Information[^key-13ffdacfe19e17cc478bab868ee23839]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-ed5f21237b96be9dd3eb16040aba759a] A review partner may request a person to provide information specified in the request to the review partner or another review partner.

(2)[^key-ed5f21237b96be9dd3eb16040aba759a] A review partner may make a request to a person under this section only if the conditions in subsections (3) and (4) are satisfied.

(3)[^key-ed5f21237b96be9dd3eb16040aba759a] The condition in this subsection is that the request is made for the purpose of enabling or assisting the performance of functions conferred on a review partner by sections 24 to 28.

(4)[^key-ed5f21237b96be9dd3eb16040aba759a] The condition in this subsection is that the request is made to a person whose functions or activities are considered by the review partner to be such that the person is likely to have information that would enable or assist the performance of functions conferred on a review partner by sections 24 to 28.

(5)[^key-b6718bd69e763e381e277dee71cfee43] The person to whom a request under this section is made must comply with the request.

(6)[^key-b6718bd69e763e381e277dee71cfee43] The review partner that made the request may enforce the duty under subsection (5) against the person by making an application to the High Court or the county court for an injunction.

(7)[^key-ed5f21237b96be9dd3eb16040aba759a] A review partner may provide information to another review partner for the purpose of enabling or assisting the performance of functions under sections 24 to 28.
