# Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/32

## 21. Amendment to the Police and Justice Act 2006[^c-2fd1fcda6aa0361c6910240d9d3f8960][^c-1b968792f2e64a5625ddda9faa9056a0]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 19(11) of the Police and Justice Act 2006 (local authority scrutiny of crime and disorder matters: interpretation), in the definition of “local crime and disorder matter”—

- (a) omit the “or” at the end of paragraph (a), and
- (b) at the end of paragraph (b) insert

  > or
  >
  > - (a) serious violence (within the meaning of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998),

  .
