# Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Code of practice about the extraction of information[^c-7c3b73e2426b682f7d0c132f97dda7e3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-9bdebf42c2887be5031707623841a285] The Secretary of State must prepare a code of practice containing guidance about the exercise of the powers in sections 37(1) and 41(1).

(2)[^c-9bdebf42c2887be5031707623841a285] The code may make different provision for different purposes or areas.

(3)[^c-9bdebf42c2887be5031707623841a285] In preparing the code, the Secretary of State must consult—

- (a) the Information Commission[^c-78557cf3e31be51c05150297f3b27a2e],
- (b) the Scottish Ministers,
- (c) the Department of Justice in Northern Ireland,
- (d) the Commissioner for Victims and Witnesses,
- (e) the Domestic Abuse Commissioner,
- (f) the Commission for Victims and Survivors for Northern Ireland, and
- (g) such other persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

(4)[^c-9bdebf42c2887be5031707623841a285] Subsection (3)(f) does not apply on or after the day appointed under Article 4(4) of the Victims and Survivors (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 ([S.I. 2006/2953 (N.I. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2006/2953)) (power to revoke Article 4).

(5)[^c-9bdebf42c2887be5031707623841a285] After preparing the code, the Secretary of State must lay it before Parliament and publish it.

(6)[^c-9bdebf42c2887be5031707623841a285] The code is to be brought into force by regulations made by statutory instrument.

(7)[^c-9bdebf42c2887be5031707623841a285] A statutory instrument containing regulations under subsection (6) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(8)[^c-72c7ee5c0746799dd502af10de573f51] After the code has come into force the Secretary of State may from time to time revise it.

(9)[^c-72c7ee5c0746799dd502af10de573f51] A failure on the part of an authorised person to act in accordance with the code does not of itself render the person liable to any criminal or civil proceedings.

(10)[^c-72c7ee5c0746799dd502af10de573f51] But the code is admissible in evidence in criminal or civil proceedings and a court may take into account a failure to act in accordance with it in determining a question in the proceedings.

(11)[^c-72c7ee5c0746799dd502af10de573f51] References in subsections (2) to (10) to the code include a revised code, subject to subsection (12).

(12)[^c-72c7ee5c0746799dd502af10de573f51] The duty to consult in subsection (3) does not apply in relation to the preparation of a revised code if the Secretary of State considers that the proposed revisions are insubstantial.
