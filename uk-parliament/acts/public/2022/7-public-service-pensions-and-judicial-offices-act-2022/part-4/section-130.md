# Public Service Pensions and Judicial Offices Act 2022, section 130

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/7

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 130. Extent[^c-b53b09d991d5b634d34ee2147fc17280]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject as follows, this Act extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(2) In Schedule 3 (judicial offices)—

- (a) Part 4 extends to Northern Ireland only;
- (b) Part 5 extends to England and Wales only.

(3) An amendment, repeal or revocation made by this Act has the same extent as the provision amended, repealed or revoked.
