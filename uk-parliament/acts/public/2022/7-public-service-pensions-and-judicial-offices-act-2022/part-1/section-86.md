# Public Service Pensions and Judicial Offices Act 2022, section 86

## 86. Meaning of “local government scheme” etc[^c-d4870dc3558d96ab4a5ab81aabc0e68e][^c-944ee42ee7fad43595a484539b21e500]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter “**local government scheme**” means—

- (a) a local government new scheme, or
- (b) a local government legacy scheme.

(2) In this Chapter “**local government new scheme**” means—

- (a) a scheme under section 1 of PSPA 2013 (schemes for persons in public service) which is a defined benefits scheme for local government workers (within the meaning of that Act), or
- (b) a scheme under section 1 of PSPA(NI) 2014 (schemes for persons in public service) which is a defined benefits scheme for local government workers (within the meaning of that Act).

(3) In this Chapter “**local government legacy scheme**” means—

- (a) an existing scheme (within the meaning of PSPA 2013) mentioned in paragraph 16 or 17 of Schedule 5 to PSPA 2013 (local government workers), or
- (b) an existing scheme (within the meaning of PSPA(NI) 2014) mentioned in paragraph 3 of Schedule 5 to PSPA(NI) 2014 (local government workers).
