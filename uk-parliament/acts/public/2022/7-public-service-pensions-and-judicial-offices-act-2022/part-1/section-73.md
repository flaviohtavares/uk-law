# Public Service Pensions and Judicial Offices Act 2022, section 73

## 73. Meaning of “opted-out service” and “PPA opted-out service”[^key-0262c763995490624da08568a0216e05][^key-33a27232afd1cdae60ba3809b54d79fc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person’s remediable service in a judicial office is “opted-out service” if and to the extent that the service meets the second condition in section 39 by virtue of the application of paragraph (b) of subsection (4) of that section.

(2) A person’s remediable service in a judicial office is “PPA opted-out service” if—

- (a) it is opted-out service, and
- (b) the person has rights under a partnership pension account that are referable to pension contributions made in respect of it.

(3) This section has effect for the purposes of this Chapter.
