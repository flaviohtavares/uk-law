# Public Service Pensions and Judicial Offices Act 2022, section 71

## 71. Meaning of “judicial office” etc[^key-cd7f2c767a9ad3ad4efa12a033dc4a24][^key-8d68f6d5299d55b3c09beda73ff663c1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) “**Judicial office**” means—

- (a) an office specified in an order made under paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 1 to PSPA 2013,
- (b) an office specified in regulation 3(2) of the Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/182](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/182)), or
- (c) an office specified in an order made under paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 1 to PSPA(NI) 2014.

(2) “**Salaried judicial office**” means a judicial office held by a person whose service in that office is remunerated by the payment of salary.

(3) “**Fee-paid judicial office**” means a judicial office held by a person whose service in that office is remunerated by the payment of fees (as opposed to the payment of salary).

(4) This section has effect for the purposes of this Chapter.
