## 28. Scheme rules that prohibit unauthorised payments[^key-e36b8f93320cdb0e5ecfedd3fa339955][^key-416088103da721094107f49833c55a13][^key-5f1db8659b09f0d4cd5f7770ad43c06e][^key-0ebc97570fe64d2ecab31766191c15cc][^key-c5ff820d2faa8a93393417a4e0c7071a][^key-7b7bdbf8f5d72dddd12b9e7d6161d15a][^key-0c6335b4c72b71fd69155f502b782d80][^key-f48529b3f17aac6bb5c59713fc7faabe][^key-cab2de024f984264025a293c38f351f2][^key-e8b2fbde4b68ac3adfb1ec0b69b1c0b0][^key-866288b9b9331ea947ab2f24ff113410][^key-68839b6ac0f25ad4b32302e7bdb6b255][^key-c3b8fa64926d5bf1ca226c7a233b23e2][^key-e031700c4ea8331191852bba05246d27][^key-3540f37415a182fefa08caec7bd80b1e][^key-0ebc97570fe64d2ecab31766191c15cc][^key-7b7bdbf8f5d72dddd12b9e7d6161d15a][^key-f48529b3f17aac6bb5c59713fc7faabe][^key-68839b6ac0f25ad4b32302e7bdb6b255][^key-c3b8fa64926d5bf1ca226c7a233b23e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where—

- (a) a payment from a Chapter 1 scheme is permitted or required to be made under or by virtue of this Chapter,
- (b) the payment, if made, would be an unauthorised payment, and
- (c) a rule of the scheme prohibits the scheme from making unauthorised payments.

(2) The payment may be made only if it falls within a description of payments specified for the purposes of this section in Treasury directions.

(3) In this section “**unauthorised payment**” means a payment that is an unauthorised payment for the purposes of Part 4 of FA 2004 (see section 160(5) of that Act).
