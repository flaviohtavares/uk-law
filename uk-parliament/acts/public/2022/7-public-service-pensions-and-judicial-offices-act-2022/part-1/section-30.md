## 30. Section 61 of the Equality Act 2010 etc[^c-5406f24d8fd37f0d0747e101fabbc90d][^c-e65eac3c53981fe116420108570c1277][^c-3540f37415a182fefa08caec7bd80b1e][^c-5f1db8659b09f0d4cd5f7770ad43c06e][^c-0ebc97570fe64d2ecab31766191c15cc][^c-c5ff820d2faa8a93393417a4e0c7071a][^c-7b7bdbf8f5d72dddd12b9e7d6161d15a][^c-f48529b3f17aac6bb5c59713fc7faabe][^c-cab2de024f984264025a293c38f351f2][^c-e8b2fbde4b68ac3adfb1ec0b69b1c0b0][^c-866288b9b9331ea947ab2f24ff113410][^c-28e5a0759d91121143368e889a2d5e96][^c-68839b6ac0f25ad4b32302e7bdb6b255][^c-c3b8fa64926d5bf1ca226c7a233b23e2][^c-e031700c4ea8331191852bba05246d27][^c-0ebc97570fe64d2ecab31766191c15cc][^c-7b7bdbf8f5d72dddd12b9e7d6161d15a][^c-f48529b3f17aac6bb5c59713fc7faabe][^c-68839b6ac0f25ad4b32302e7bdb6b255][^c-c3b8fa64926d5bf1ca226c7a233b23e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In determining for the purposes of this Chapter whether any service is pensionable service under a particular pension scheme, section 61 of EA 2010 and paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 to EEAR(NI) 2006 (non-discrimination rule) are to be disregarded.

(2) To the extent that section 61 of EA 2010 or paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 to EEAR(NI) 2006 has the effect (apart from this subsection) that any remediable service of a person—

- (a) is not pensionable service under a Chapter 1 new scheme, or
- (b) is pensionable service under a Chapter 1 legacy scheme,

it ceases to have effect (to that extent) immediately before the coming into force of section 2(1).
