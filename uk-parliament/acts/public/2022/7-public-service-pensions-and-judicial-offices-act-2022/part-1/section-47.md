# Public Service Pensions and Judicial Offices Act 2022, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Cases in which 2015 scheme election treated as made[^key-b39e7da9255bc621750b72e88339f104][^key-b82861afd466be58ef35d62d1f9951bd][^key-0b1c3dc4b741d0dd132ca0fd041d0515]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where —

- (a) a person has remediable service in a judicial office which is mixed service, and
- (b) no legacy scheme election or 2015 scheme election in respect of the person is made before the end of the election period.

(2) A 2015 scheme election in respect of the person is treated as having been made.

(3) For the purposes of this section a person’s remediable service in a judicial office is “mixed service” if—

- (a) some of the service is pensionable service under a judicial legacy scheme, and
- (b) some of the service is—
  - (i) pensionable service under a judicial 2015 scheme, or
  - (ii) PPA opted-out service.
