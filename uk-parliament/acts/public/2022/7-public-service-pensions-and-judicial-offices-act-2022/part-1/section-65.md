# Public Service Pensions and Judicial Offices Act 2022, section 65

## 65. Power to delegate[^key-da6041fb386e1e08b51fb6b6832e4f2b][^key-8f32cd01e46d3e2d62ed8443b8464d43][^key-0b1c3dc4b741d0dd132ca0fd041d0515]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Lord Chancellor may delegate to another person any of the Lord Chancellor’s functions under this Chapter.

(2) The Department of Justice in Northern Ireland may delegate to another person any of its functions under this Chapter.
