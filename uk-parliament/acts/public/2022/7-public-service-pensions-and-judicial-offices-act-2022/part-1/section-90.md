# Public Service Pensions and Judicial Offices Act 2022, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Restriction of other schemes[^c-e8e66fb9c15f67213f64d078667e0aea][^c-c791d45de4f8d33d1723542cdd320e3d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No benefits are to be provided under a relevant scheme to or in respect of a person in relation to the person’s service after 31 March 2022.

(2) In subsection (1), “**relevant scheme**” means—

- (a) the Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/182](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/182)),
- (b) the Judicial Pensions Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 ([S.R. (N.I.) 2015 No. 76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2015/76)),
- (c) the scheme constituted by Parts 1 to 11 of the Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/522](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/522)), or
- (d) the pension scheme established for certain employees of the Secret Intelligence Service which came into operation on 1 January 1946 and was amended on 1 September 1957 and 1 July 1964.

(3) In subsection (1), the reference to benefits in relation to a person’s service includes benefits relating to the person’s death in service.
