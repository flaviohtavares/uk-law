# Judicial Review and Courts Act 2022, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-09-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/35
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Provision of information to registrar when investigation discontinued[^c-55c6f6f2921150693cd4d112c05f56f7][^c-528715c3698ea8c418f3a2d5ae954756]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 23 of the Births and Deaths Registration Act 1953 (furnishing of information by coroner in connection with registration of death), after subsection (3) insert—

> (4) Where a senior coroner—
>
> - (a) discontinues an investigation under section 4 of the 2009 Act,
> - (b) authorises the disposal of the body, and
> - (c) sends to the registrar, on request by the registrar, a certificate stating any particulars required by this Act to be registered concerning the death (so far as they have been ascertained at the date of the certificate),
>
> the registrar shall in the prescribed form and manner register the death and those particulars, so far as they are not already registered.
