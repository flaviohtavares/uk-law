# Judicial Review and Courts Act 2022, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-09-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/35

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Saving for existing procedural provisions[^key-24c1a17da32e0a12405a66ea78dbc765][^key-a429b1b3f700fa8b4b832491c454cc82]

*Extent: E+W+S*

No amendment or repeal made by section 34 or 35 or Schedule 5 affects the continued operation of—

- (a) the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013 ([S.I. 2013/1237](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1237)),
- (b) the Employment Appeal Tribunal Rules 1993 ([S.I. 1993/2854](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/2854)), or
- (c) any practice direction made under section 7A of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996 or the regulations referred to in paragraph (a).
