# Nationality and Borders Act 2022, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2026-03-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Removal of asylum seeker to safe country[^key-90087907b442826eb98e5e457da1fd97][^key-0bf298094e094b9ad7a3512ab33fc8ea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 4 makes amendments to—

- (a) section 77 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (no removal while claim for asylum pending), and
- (b) Schedule 3 to the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc) Act 2004 (removal of asylum seeker to safe country).
