# Nationality and Borders Act 2022, section 83

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2026-03-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Financial provision[^c-cbe50fa926ee70dbe187ef79a3b6e640]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following are to be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) expenditure incurred under or by virtue of this Act by a Minister of the Crown, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
