## 48. Alternative test to the income requirement: Scotland[^key-5f62ad8c4435f9893fc4c1e1f4a33805][^key-b614924c1a551e47051c54ccab10214f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 396B(3) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (income requirement for making of unexplained wealth order)—

- (a) after “suspecting” insert

  > —
  >
  > - (a)

  ;
- (b) at the end insert

  > , or
  >
  > - (b) that the property has been obtained through unlawful conduct (within the meaning given by section 242).
