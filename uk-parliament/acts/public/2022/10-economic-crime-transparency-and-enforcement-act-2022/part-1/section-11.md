# Economic Crime (Transparency and Enforcement) Act 2022, section 11

> Text as at 2024-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Transfer of documents to Public Record Office[^c-351ddb96e8d5b4fba04d366f14d930f6][^c-23632728120573ac5d1d66e04512a267]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where an overseas entity has been removed from the list of registered overseas entities for at least two years, the registrar may transfer any records relating to that entity to the Public Record Office.
