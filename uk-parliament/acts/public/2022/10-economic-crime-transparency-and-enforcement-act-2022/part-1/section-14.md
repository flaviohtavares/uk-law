# Economic Crime (Transparency and Enforcement) Act 2022, section 14

> Text as at 2024-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Sections 12 and 13: supplementary[^c-ee08fdaa3853384c612b750a9c359eee][^c-5ff22432de24465a4a7b6262df15c006][^c-7279908be38e95c9ab159d75c6ee7f0d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations make further provision about the giving of notices under section 12 or 13, including provision about the form and content of any such notices and the manner in which they must be given.

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) are subject to the negative resolution procedure.
