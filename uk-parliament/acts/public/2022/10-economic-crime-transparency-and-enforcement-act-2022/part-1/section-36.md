## 36. Meaning of “daily default fine”[^c-adb91fccd59a90111077a116566f39a3][^c-bb020fc92e42d0ae2eb69a87f3a6847c][^c-bd171afa9d907736c59fb0867d4a4205]

Section 1125 of the Companies Act 2006 (meaning of “**daily default fine**”) applies for the[^c-d648cd18e3379c1c6e755069c6a36a51] purpose of any provision made by this Part as it applies for the purposes of provisions of the Companies Acts.
