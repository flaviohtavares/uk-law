# Economic Crime (Transparency and Enforcement) Act 2022, section 19

> Text as at 2024-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Documents to be in English[^c-5f3b618e7ae5e2dfd32d62b9288a3f33][^c-6ce4bce741f53be87410ef80ed122c65][^c-b9a0722c0659df64117a9d813665865c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Documents delivered to the registrar under this Part, or under regulations made under it, must be drawn up and delivered in English.
