# Economic Crime (Transparency and Enforcement) Act 2022, section 12

> Text as at 2024-03-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Identifying registrable beneficial owners (affected by repeal, see note)Duty to take steps to obtain information[^c-fb9c2a3939ac76a95bdbb0f15504de85][^c-8205660c1bf5b58020f25cb2df8a053b][^c-4eb15b25995aec4da4111e8491b8cff6][^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An overseas entity must comply with this section before— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]

- (a) making an application under section 4 for registration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]
- (b) complying with the updating duty under section 7; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]
- (c) making an application under section 9 for removal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]

(2) The overseas entity must take reasonable steps— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]

- (a) to identify any registrable beneficial owners in relation to the entity, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]
- (b) if it identifies any, to obtain, for the purposes of the application under section 4 or 9 or for the purposes of complying with the updating duty under section 7— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]
  - (i) the required information about each registrable beneficial owner, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80] in respect of any registrable beneficial owner who is a trustee, the required information about the trust. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]

(3) The steps that an overseas entity must take include giving an information notice under this section to any person that it knows, or has reasonable cause to believe, is a registrable beneficial owner in relation to the entity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]

(4) An information notice under this section is a notice requiring the person to whom it is given— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]

- (a) to state whether or not the person is a registrable beneficial owner in relation to the overseas entity, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]
- (b) if the person is a registrable beneficial owner, to confirm or correct any of the required information about the person that is specified in the notice and to supply any of the required information that the notice states the overseas entity does not already have, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]
- (c) if the person is a registrable beneficial owner by virtue of being a trustee, to confirm or correct any of the required information about the trust that is specified in the notice and to supply any of the required information about the trust that the notice states the overseas entity does not already have. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]

(5) An information notice under this section must require the person to whom it is given to comply with the notice within the period of one month beginning with the day on which it is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd9b374f5a2fa21796d5441720f6c80]

(1) Before making an application for registration under section 4(1) an overseas entity must take reasonable steps to obtain all of the information that it is required to deliver to the registrar under that section if it is able to obtain it.

(2) Before complying with the updating duty under section 7 an overseas entity must take reasonable steps to obtain all of the information that it is required to deliver to the registrar under that section if it is able to obtain it.

(3) Before making an application for removal under section 9 an overseas entity must take reasonable steps to obtain all of the information that it is required to include in the application if it is able to obtain it.

(4) The steps that an overseas entity must take by virtue of subsection (1), (2) or (3) include giving a notice to any person that it knows, or has reasonable cause to believe, is a registrable beneficial owner in relation to the entity, requiring the person—

- (a) to state whether or not they are such a person, and
- (b) if they are, to provide or confirm information of the kind mentioned in subsection (1), (2) or (3) so far as relating to the person, or a trust of which they are or were a trustee.

(5) The steps that an overseas entity must take by virtue of subsection (2) or (3) also include giving a notice to any person that it knows, or has reasonable cause to believe, has ceased to be a registrable beneficial owner in relation to the entity during the update period (within the meaning of section 7) or relevant period (within the meaning of section 9), requiring the person—

- (a) to state whether or not they are such a person, and
- (b) if they are, to provide or confirm information of the kind mentioned in subsection (2) or (3) so far as relating to the person, or a trust of which they are or were a trustee.

(6) A notice under subsection (4) or (5) must require the person to whom it is given to comply with the notice within the period of one month beginning with the day on which it is given.

(7) A person given a notice under subsection (4) or (5) is not required by that notice to disclose any information in respect of which a claim to legal professional privilege or, in Scotland, confidentiality of communications, could be maintained in legal proceedings.
