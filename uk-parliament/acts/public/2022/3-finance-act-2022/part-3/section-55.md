# Finance Act 2022, section 55

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. UK revenue: amount

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person’s UK revenue—

- (a) is in band A[^key-7de5f79d1e5e3b4a3b6ce540cdc2c54f] for a financial year if the person’s UK revenue for the relevant accounting period is more than £10.2 million but not more than £36 million;
- (b) is in band B[^key-8a1f01e15b6b14700614645f7e605738] for a financial year if the person’s UK revenue for the relevant accounting period is more than £36 million but not more than £500 million;
- (ba)[^key-6e5ad782ebc69574cd13d52918cd99b3] is in band C for a financial year if the person’s UK revenue for the relevant accounting period is more than £500 million but not more than £1 billion;
- (c) is in band D[^key-a7774e02b119e3a81e9409caca361a04] for a financial year if the person’s UK revenue for the relevant accounting period is more than £1 billion.

(2) To determine the “relevant accounting period”, see section 56.

(3) The sums in subsection (1) are to be proportionately adjusted if the relevant accounting period of the person is a period other than 12 months.
