# Finance Act 2022, section 12

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Extension of temporary increase in annual investment allowance (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599][^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 32(1) of FA 2019 (which increases the maximum amount of the annual investment allowance to £1,000,000 until 31 December 2021), for “the period of three years beginning with 1 January 2019” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599] “the period beginning with 1 January 2019 and ending with 31 March 2023” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599].

(2) In consequence of the amendment made by subsection (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599]

- (a) in section 32(2) of that Act, for “1 January 2022” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599] “1 April 2023” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599],
- (b) in paragraph 2 of Schedule 13 to that Act and the heading before that paragraph, for “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599]**1 January 2022 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599]**” (in each place) substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599] “1 April 2023” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599],
- (c) in paragraph 3(3)(b) of that Schedule, for “the period of three years beginning with 1 January 2019” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599] “the period beginning with 1 January 2019 and ending with 31 March 2023” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599]
- (d) in the heading for that Schedule, for “1 January 2022” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599] “1 April 2023” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cc70ae91a8e11698310941d97ba4599].
