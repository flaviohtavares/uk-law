# Finance Act 2022, section 101

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Vehicle CO<sub>2</sub> emissions certificates

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 18 makes provision about certificates in relation to the CO<sub>2</sub> emissions of vehicles for the purposes of—

- (a) section 268C(1) of CAA 2001 (meaning of “**qualifying emissions certificate**”),
- (b) Chapter 6 of Part 3 of ITEPA 2003 (taxable benefits: cars etc), and
- (c) Part 1A of Schedule 1 to VERA 1994 (light passenger vehicles: rates of duty).
