# Subsidy Control Act 2022, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/23

## 51. Nuclear energy[^c-eaf02e0db76edccbc1c4a54868346cf8][^c-e634beeda4b9374c8b03b61c468fdc7f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 13(1) does not apply to a subsidy in relation to nuclear energy.

(2) Section 13(3) does not apply to a subsidy scheme that provides for subsidies in relation to nuclear energy.
