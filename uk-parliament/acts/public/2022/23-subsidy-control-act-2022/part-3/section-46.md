> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/23

## 46. Bank of England monetary policy[^key-319625b19148d18c57539a32963fc8f3][^key-accfa558debd2c0e41761c0e9afb99fb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The subsidy control requirements do not apply to the giving of a subsidy by or on behalf of the Bank of England in pursuit of monetary policy.
