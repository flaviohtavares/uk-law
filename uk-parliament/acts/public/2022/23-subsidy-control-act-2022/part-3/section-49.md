# Subsidy Control Act 2022, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Tax measures[^key-be560239a5fbc4ee1fbea546ae5b1020][^key-422880a13bbd0f07d2e06913575a3deb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The subsidy control requirements do not apply to the giving of a subsidy where the giving of the subsidy is permissible by virtue of Article 413 of the Trade and Cooperation Agreement (taxation).
