## 63. Referrals in relation to subsidy schemes[^key-a7d0ee090f9f139cb8b3cabd74e4804b][^key-b59050911d5bf96898c579d132bb0788]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The preceding provisions of this Chapter relating to subsidies do not apply to a subsidy given under a subsidy scheme.
