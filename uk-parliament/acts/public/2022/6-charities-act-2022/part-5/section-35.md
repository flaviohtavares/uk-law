# Charities Act 2022, section 35

> Text as at 2025-11-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Vesting permanent endowment following a merger[^c-16d70cb5ddb305ca6a618af4c0bde07f]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 306 of the Charities Act 2011 (meaning of “**relevant charity merger**”), in subsection (2)—

- (a)[^c-cbf2601daa2d926a0bfc307e82794d94] in paragraph (a), omit “a”;
- (b)[^c-12d25cc92b5f0480fafffe32a4d201db] omit paragraph (b) and the “and” before it.
