# Charities Act 2022, section 19

> Text as at 2025-11-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Advertising and report requirements for disposition of charity land[^c-9ec10c9e219a74b56aa98fad8aa684b9][^c-745c9191edc1b9b7931b15e2d1fe6deb]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 119 of the Charities Act 2011 (requirements for dispositions other than certain leases)—

- (a) omit subsection (1)(b) (but not the “and” following it);
- (b) in subsection (4), omit—
  - (i) “contain such information, and”;
  - (ii) the “,” after “matters”.
