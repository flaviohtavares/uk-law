# Charities Act 2022, section 5

> Text as at 2025-11-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Orders under section 73 of the Charities Act 2011: parliamentary procedure[^c-2f46e81b717c8611c85fc8a55191e609][^c-673de4c9e094c0be529c19980a31514e]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 73 of the Charities Act 2011 (powers to make schemes altering provision made by Acts, etc)—

- (a) omit subsections (3) and (4);
- (b) in subsection (5), omit “Subject to subsection (6),”;
- (c) omit subsection (6).
