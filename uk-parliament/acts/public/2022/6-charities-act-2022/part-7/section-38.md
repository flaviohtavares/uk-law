# Charities Act 2022, section 38

> Text as at 2025-11-27.

## 38. “Connected person”: illegitimate children[^c-454d073077af176d2d7b442cb2fc77e0][^c-26f248e1697c343e13fe81a2ec38d97e]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 350(1) of the Charities Act 2011 (connected person: meaning of “child”), omit “and an illegitimate child”.
