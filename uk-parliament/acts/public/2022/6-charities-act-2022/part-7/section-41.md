# Charities Act 2022, section 41

> Text as at 2025-11-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Extent, commencement and short title[^c-850d5129bcd7c668684f1cf20b68e37d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Act extends to England and Wales only, subject to subsection (2).

(2) An amendment made by—

- (a) section 24;
- (b) Schedule 1;
- (c) paragraph 12 or 46 of Schedule 2,

has the same extent as the enactment or provision to which it relates.

(3) This section comes into force on the day on which this Act is passed.

(4) The other provisions of this Act come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by regulations made by statutory instrument appoint and different days may be appointed for different purposes.

(5) Regulations under subsection (4) may make consequential, transitional or saving provision.

(6) This Act may be cited as the Charities Act 2022.
