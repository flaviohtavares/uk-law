# Building Safety Act 2022, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/30

## 5. Duty to keep safety and standard of buildings under review[^c-2686d1a7380b95e629eb77f7314ec730][^c-9a34ffb7c6848ca9fcc5c1a425af6df3]

*Extent: E+W*

The regulator must keep under review—

- (a) the safety of people in or about buildings in relation to risks as regards buildings, and
- (b) the standard of buildings.
