# Building Safety Act 2022, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/30
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Higher-risk building work: public bodies[^c-ba35912f8b940f5c664690ab3ea9d47c][^c-a70d67502901a738321ad203a39129ee][^c-1238e8877237d42c3f0b94e425bedf70]

*Extent: E+W*

After section 54 of the Building Act 1984 insert—

> **54A. Public bodies and higher-risk building work**
>
> (1) The appropriate national authority may by regulations make such amendments of the provisions listed in subsection (2) as the authority considers appropriate in connection with higher-risk building work.
>
> (2) The provisions are—
>
> - (a) section 5;
> - (b) section 54;
> - (c) Schedule 4.
