# Building Safety Act 2022, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/30
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Transfer of approved inspectors’ functions to registered building control approvers[^c-7e2ad1525f3c0c66a37e8bf19010c9af][^c-45c3a99d98938f90fac8cbd2ac0f9587][^c-067eca21d829b33f9935a4f66bf690da]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 4 amends the Building Act 1984 so as to—

- (a) transfer the functions of approved inspectors to registered building control approvers, and
- (b) make other provision consequential on section 42.
