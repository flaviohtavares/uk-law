# Health and Care Act 2022, section 36

> Text as at 2025-07-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Abolition of NHS Trust Development Authority[^key-b8457245ba65969057cda25310737a5a][^key-9e53aa49b945073210e41499093e486e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Special Health Authority called the National Health Service Trust Development Authority is abolished.

(2) The following are revoked—

- (a) the National Health Service Trust Development Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Order 2012 ([S.I. 2012/901](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/901));
- (b) the National Health Service Trust Development Authority Regulations 2012 ([S.I. 2012/922](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/922));
- (c) the National Health Service Trust Development Authority (Directions and Miscellaneous Amendments etc.) Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/214](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/214)).

(3) In section 9 of the Mental Health Units (Use of Force) Act 2018 (investigation of deaths or serious injuries), omit paragraph (d).

(4) In section 15 of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 (duty to co-operate with the Domestic Abuse Commissioner), in subsection (7), omit paragraph (e) of the definition of “NHS body in England”.
