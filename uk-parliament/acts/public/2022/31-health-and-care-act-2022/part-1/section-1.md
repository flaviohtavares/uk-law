> Text as at 2025-07-07.

## 1. NHS Commissioning Board renamed NHS England[^key-46542ccaf88b354af693e63cd0b84cc1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-877723493d4a72dd20d21766ca6f313c][^key-08fa0b3d330c24a1abab4dbed3ff48c9] The National Health Service Commissioning Board is renamed NHS England.

(2)[^key-3a78d3aa27832d75ed8edc436b01b2ea] Schedule 1 contains consequential amendments.
