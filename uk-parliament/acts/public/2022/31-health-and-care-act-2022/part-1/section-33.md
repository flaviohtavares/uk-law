# Health and Care Act 2022, section 33

> Text as at 2025-07-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Abolition of Monitor and transfer of functions to NHS England[^c-ffece9192b12f2838a020bb2b2192b94][^c-19d391b94c93e84a479c0f45fdc870fe]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Monitor is abolished.

(2) Schedule 5 contains amendments to transfer Monitor’s functions to NHS England and related amendments.
