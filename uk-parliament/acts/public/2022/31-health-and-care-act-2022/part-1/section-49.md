# Health and Care Act 2022, section 49

> Text as at 2025-07-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Removal of power to appoint trust funds and trustees[^c-9d25ca6fd1c55b0ff684dc97b4d457cb][^c-c282235108b675f555134a73553bd3c8]

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 4 to the National Health Service Act 2006, omit paragraph 10 (power to appoint trustees for an NHS trust) and the italic heading before it.
