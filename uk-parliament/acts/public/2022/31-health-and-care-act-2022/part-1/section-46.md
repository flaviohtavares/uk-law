# Health and Care Act 2022, section 46

> Text as at 2025-07-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Reconfiguration of services: intervention powers[^key-45aecd7b80a506ff4f6458340e5e0d4c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-a550c256634d4901f8467e720606f810] After section 68 of the National Health Service Act 2006 insert—

> **Reconfiguration of NHS services**
>
> **68A. Reconfiguration of NHS services**
>
> Schedule 10A confers intervention powers on the Secretary of State in relation to the reconfiguration of NHS services.

(2)[^key-d26ba92fda457643063946775519ec4b] Schedule 6 inserts into the National Health Service Act 2006 a new Schedule 10A to that Act (intervention powers in relation to the reconfiguration of NHS services).
