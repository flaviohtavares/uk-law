# Health and Care Act 2022, section 88

> Text as at 2025-07-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Meaning of “health” in NHS Act 2006[^c-816de0cae002b2581a79a80058833947][^c-f953c71a46b949c61591189a68edd683]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 275(1) of the National Health Service Act 2006 (interpretation), at the appropriate place insert—

> - “**health**” includes mental health;

.
