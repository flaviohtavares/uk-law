# Health and Care Act 2022, SCHEDULE 12

> Text as at 2025-07-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 12 — Removal of functions relating to competition etc

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 84

### 1. Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986[^key-ef2ba2bdb631be65f757134976f1b131][^key-913518faca198c662827e300d7973b22]

In section 9E of the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986 (disqualification for competition infringements: interpretation), in subsection (2), omit paragraph (f).

### 2. Competition Act 1998[^key-96fe2c531fa1d5c6914c5c4ad27a0736][^key-82d661d937c2fb0d3379bdd5a994af4a]

In section 54 of the Competition Act 1998 (list of regulators), in subsection (1), omit paragraph (h).

### 3. Enterprise Act 2002[^key-1ace4fc083f4b3f91b3990aaee83842f][^key-efc73943bfda3fde22bded2335dbcdcb]

(1) Section 136 of the Enterprise Act 2002 (investigations and reports on market investigation references) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (7), omit paragraph (i).

(3) In subsection (8), omit “, Monitor”.

### 4. Health and Social Care Act 2012[^key-b9e20fa94e0a1c99ad33b3d2e898fb31][^key-970e6b42a576737bc15cca4686353d73]

The Health and Social Care Act 2012 is amended as follows.

**5.**[^key-27e0058005bbd18c26665820c2a00e56][^key-22e5569666fb2200b26082491799415f] In section 74 (competition functions: supplementary), omit subsections (1) to (6).

**6.**[^key-369e7dabe113f40e85c35c587cf81d1e][^key-c74bc19db735b624984bca91dabaac21] In section 288 (Monitor: duty to cooperate with Care Quality Commission), omit subsection (3).

### 7. Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013[^key-2b46484107ddd1d47b87df9795fa223a][^key-6474b096411176d954b26132be9d95f7]

The Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 is amended as follows.

**8.**[^key-3ef1808a8e9e7c1dd33e1b7980a14eeb][^key-bae8172f686c0a5c9b2a24e4530ba288] In Schedule 4 (the Competition and Markets Authority), in paragraph 16(7), omit paragraph (g).

**9.**[^key-2abf2489f48ea1e34514e30e85c1f856][^key-cfb3d4533f216c46b1da4c4929aa3f02] In Schedule 14 (regulators: use of powers under the Competition Act 1998), omit paragraphs 20 to 22.

### 10. Care Act 2014[^key-b609f81a595362cb652d76e343daf862][^key-f2e26ba0d92694977de576f9793ba34e]

In section 92 of the Care Act 2014 (care standards: false or misleading information offence), omit subsections (7) and (8).
