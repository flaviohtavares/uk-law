# Health and Care Act 2022, section 102

> Text as at 2025-07-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Relevant bodies and Special Health Authorities[^key-f3d106f24db25c84b8a121e64d65f768][^key-00fa0fa9e28c7b5713fc7b6aa1192f50]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**relevant body**” means—
  - (a) Health Education England,
  - (b) the Health and Social Care Information Centre,
  - (c) the Health Research Authority,
  - (d) the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority,
  - (e) the Human Tissue Authority, or
  - (f) NHS England;
- “**Special Health Authority**” means a Special Health Authority established under section 28 of the National Health Service Act 2006.
