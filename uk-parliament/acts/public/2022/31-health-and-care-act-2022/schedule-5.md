## SCHEDULE 5 — Abolition of Monitor and transfer of its functions

*Extent: E+W*

Section 33

### 1. General[^key-661b92b2890bfb622dd3f3eb037d44ea][^key-c10aed05d4757c66f355bc7e4a96d7e2]
