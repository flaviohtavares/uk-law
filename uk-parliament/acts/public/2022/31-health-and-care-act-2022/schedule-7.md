## SCHEDULE 7 — NHS trusts in England and removal of power to appoint trustees: consequential amendments

### 1. Value Added Tax Act 1994[^c-7c82068a1109b0ab7bd4f68cb1887d7d][^c-97b31c60c1704f69f69a3cf5ad027707]

.

>
