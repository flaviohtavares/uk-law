# Product Security and Telecommunications Infrastructure Act 2022, section 77

> Text as at 2026-04-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Regulations[^key-25342ad70d151717fa08819c2db5ec45]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any power of the Secretary of State to make regulations under this Act is exercisable by statutory instrument.

(2) Regulations under this Act may—

- (a) make different provision for different purposes;
- (b) contain supplementary, incidental, consequential, transitional or saving provision.

(3) Subsection (2) does not apply to regulations under section 79 (see instead subsection (3) of that section).

(4) Where regulations under this Act are subject to the “negative resolution procedure” the statutory instrument containing the regulations is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(5) Where regulations under this Act are subject to the “affirmative resolution procedure” the regulations may not be made unless a draft of the statutory instrument containing them has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

(6) Any provision that may be included in an instrument under this Act subject to the negative resolution procedure may be made by regulations subject to the affirmative resolution procedure.
