# Product Security and Telecommunications Infrastructure Act 2022, section 75

> Text as at 2026-04-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Meaning of “the electronic communications code”[^key-745e02d08fe11fbcc9c8b8ad6b35644c][^key-f50f9f24f7583ceb13e8a3fcf15267ed]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, “**the electronic communications code**” means the electronic communications code set out in Schedule 3A to the Communications Act 2003.
