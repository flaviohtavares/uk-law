# Product Security and Telecommunications Infrastructure Act 2022, section 70

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Complaints relating to the conduct of operators[^key-e838b9bc5dd229ebd23f05aabef98eba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In paragraph 103 of the electronic communications code (duty for OFCOM to prepare code of practice), in sub-paragraph (1), after paragraph (c) insert—

> - (ca) the handling by operators of complaints relating to the failure of operators to comply with the code of practice;

.
