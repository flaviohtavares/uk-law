# Product Security and Telecommunications Infrastructure Act 2022, section 31

> Text as at 2026-04-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Power to vary or revoke enforcement notices[^key-103d88db4f67099153133fb1bce57680][^key-26d4cbe54967fd6f0f1783fbc6b632f0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may vary or revoke an enforcement notice.

(2) But the Secretary of State may not vary an enforcement notice in order to make it more onerous.
