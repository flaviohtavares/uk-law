## 61. Meaning of “the Commission”[^c-34ab396bae020c9735fb4ded65b1af02][^c-6568d341a539731d0c2c611209b37b86]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “**the Commission**” means the Electoral Commission.
