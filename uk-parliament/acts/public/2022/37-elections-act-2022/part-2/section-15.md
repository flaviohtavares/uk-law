## 15. Voting and candidacy rights of EU citizens[^key-aa4c4457c76257402613f0d7795f708b][^key-5b842588f58372d1f27abf9ff5b3fb02][^key-e56ef59f58533f7ad7d2c2bd2f3d25c2][^key-93808e57c00c1a6b483b6c9a93b0da26]

Schedule 8 makes provision about voting and candidacy rights of EU citizens in relation to local elections in England and certain other elections.
