# Elections Act 2022, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2022/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Criminal proceedings[^key-3f60c700bf0591663fa9b06938ceda4b][^key-c392d372082064db3069d43768036aff]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 to PPERA (the Electoral Commission: incidental powers) is amended as follows.

(2) The existing text becomes sub-paragraph (1).

(3) In sub-paragraph (1)—

- (a) after “may” insert “(subject to sub-paragraph (2))”;
- (b) omit “(except borrow money)”.

(4) After sub-paragraph (1) insert—

> (2) The Commission may not—
>
> - (a) borrow money;
> - (b) institute criminal proceedings in England and Wales or Northern Ireland.
