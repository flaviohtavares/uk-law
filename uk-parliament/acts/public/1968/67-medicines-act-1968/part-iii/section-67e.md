# Medicines Act 1968, section 67E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2025-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/67
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67E. Sections 67A to 67D: “adulteration” and “registrant”[^c-cd94600ee6a0fdb159df7fabfb1af9af][^c-ac11326f7f30261f76088900f64e8f4a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In sections 67A to 67D—

- “adulteration”, in relation to a medicinal product, means the addition of a substance to, or the abstraction of a substance from, the product, so as to affect injuriously its composition (and related expressions are to be construed accordingly);
- “registrant” means—
  - (a) where it is alleged that the offence in question took place in Great Britain, a person who is entered in Part 1, or 2[^c-41eae4799b3e5d41bb409bfb2192be5a] of the register of pharmacists and pharmacy technicians established and maintained under article 19 of the Pharmacy Order 2010 ([SI 2010/231](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/231)); or
  - (b) where it is alleged that the offence in question took place in Northern Ireland, a person registered in the register of pharmaceutical chemists for Northern Ireland [^c-9c1b4cb95831b78b7fb93b22c004b881]... maintained under articles 6 and 9 of the Pharmacy (Northern Ireland) Order 1976 ([SI 1976/1213 (NI 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1976/1213)).
