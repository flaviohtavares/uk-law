## 130. Meaning of “medicinal product" and related expressions.[^c998946]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-a0e9a8e8aa5b240973fd74d25730bcd2] In this Act, “medicinal product” has the meaning given by regulation 2 of the 2012 Regulations.

(3C)[^c-f2ea00618ea30a519ccd89bbbb788117] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c998956]

(9) In this Act “ **administer** ” means administer to a human being [^c-64da9f4b519f9218577cbb818c2de097]..., whether orally, by injection or by introduction into the body in any other way, or by external application, whether by direct contact with the body or not; and any reference in this Act to administering ([^c-d36d6dceb7af5506b98a9133d8193683]...) a substance or article is a reference to administering ([^c-d36d6dceb7af5506b98a9133d8193683]...) it either in its existing state or after it has been dissolved or dispersed in, or diluted or mixed with, some other substance used as a vehicle.
