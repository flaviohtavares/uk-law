# Medicines Act 1968, section 136

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2025-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/67
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 136. Short title, extent and commencement.[^c999123]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Medicines Act 1968.

(2)[^key-44107a4835ecdc408dc166f44b753c84] Except as provided by section 78(5B), this Act extends to Northern Ireland.

(3)[^c999124][^c999125] The following provisions of this Act, that is to say, sections 63 to 65, 77, 85(5), 86(3), 90(2), 93, 97 and 135, shall not come into operation on the passing of this Act but shall come into operation on such day as the Ministers may by order appoint, and different days may be so appointed for, or for different purposes of, any one or more of those provisions (including, in the case of section 135 of this Act, the amendment or repeal of different enactments to which that section is applicable).

(4) Any order made under this section may make such transitional provision as appears to the Ministers to be necessary or expedient in connection with the provisions of this Act which are thereby brought (wholly or in part) into force, including such adaptations of those provisions or any provision of this Act then in force as appear to them to be necessary or expedient in consequence of the partial operation of this Act (whether before, on or after the day appointed by the order).
