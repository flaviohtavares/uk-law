## 91. Offences under Part V, and supplementary provisions.[^c998709][^c998710][^c998708]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-36a07712348226a75cc28bb37bc01d63] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Any regulations made under this Part of this Act may provide that any person who contravenes the regulations, or who contravenes the provisions of [^c-8d36e35ac4e9ad3d21f890d7df1aea95]... section 87(2) of this Act [^c-3250e27d777e442029fd8b8782498ca7]..., shall be guilty of an offence and—

- (a)[^c998712] shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding £400 or such lesser sum as may be specified in the regulations, and
- (b) if the regulations so provide, shall be liable on conviction on indictment to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or to both.

(3) Without prejudice to the application of section 129(5) of this Act, any power to make regulations conferred by section[^c-c2f64dee6bbf2eaffa25c91ad100da8d] 87 of this Act may be exercised so as to impose requirements either in relation to medicinal products generally or in relation to medicinal products of a particular description, or falling within a particular class, specified in the regulations[^c-cc2aed213497a10b376a23408ea578e6]....

(4) In this Part of this Act “**requirements**” includes restrictions.
