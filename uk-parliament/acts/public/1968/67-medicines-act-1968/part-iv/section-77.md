## 77. Annual return of premises to registrar.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Every person who carried on a retail pharmacy business at[^key-c04399a3f42fe7bee244eb7528183be1] or from[^key-d026c31414e893cc035eb98710cb8fe2] premises in Northern Ireland shall, in the month of January in each year, send to the registrar—

- (a) a list of all such[^key-277f264fffc48889bbd7d0e64b01e622] premises at or from[^key-38fa64fbf6b122eade9df883b412a7be] which his business, so far as it consists of the retail sale of medicinal products, is carried on, and
- (b)[^key-1c6445fff780feb46e07172230cd7244] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
