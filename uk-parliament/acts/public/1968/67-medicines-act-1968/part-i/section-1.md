# Medicines Act 1968, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2025-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/67
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Ministers responsible for administration of Act.[^c998118][^key-2df11f8a824f53cf2c6c019c1acc677c][^c998117]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act, “the Ministers” has the meaning given by regulation 6(6) to (8) of the 2012 Regulations (but as if references in that regulation to those Regulations were references to this Act).
