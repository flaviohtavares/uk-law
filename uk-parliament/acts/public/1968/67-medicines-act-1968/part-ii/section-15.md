(3) The [^key-730b1b1e840aee50d39193db1f5c4863] ... Ministers may by order provide that any of the provisions of section 10[^key-28f2174956f3abeb3560641ebfc0e42e] of this Act specified in the order shall cease to have effect, or shall have effect subject to such exceptions or modifications as may be so specified.
