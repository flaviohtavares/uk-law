# New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968, section 9

> Text as at 2011-04-22.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 9. Recording of compulsory purchase orders.

*Extent: S*

As soon as may be after a compulsory purchase order under section 7 or 8 of this Act becomes operative, the acquiring authority shall register it in the Land Register of Scotland or as the case may be[^c733559] record it in the Register of Sasines.
