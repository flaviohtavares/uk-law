# New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968, section 48

> Text as at 2011-04-22.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 48. Saving and transitional provisions, consequential amendments and repeals.

*Extent: S*

(1) The saving and transitional provisions contained in Schedule 9 to this Act shall have effect.

(2)[^c733776] The enactments specified in Schedule 10 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments therein specified, being amendments consequential on the repeals specified in Schedule 11 to this Act.

(3)[^c733776] The enactments specified in Schedule 11 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
