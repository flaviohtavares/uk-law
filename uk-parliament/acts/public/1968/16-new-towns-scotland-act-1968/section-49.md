# New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968, section 49

> Text as at 2011-04-22.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 49. Short title, commencement and extent.

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st April 1968.

(3) This Act shall extend to Scotland only.
