# Transport Act 1968, section 123

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2024-05-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/73
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Power of highway and other authorities to contribute to cost of barriers, etc., at level crossings.[^c15333801]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Contributions to the expenses incurred by the Railways Board or a network owner[^c15333811] in providing lifting or other barriers, lights, signs, or other devices or appliances for the protection or convenience of the public at or near any level crossing where a road is crossed by a railway of the Board , or, as the case may be, by a railway comprised in a transferred network or a new network of that network owner, may be made by—

- (a) the highway authority, if any, for that road; and[^c15333831]
- (b) any local authority, not being the highway authority, in whose area the level crossing is situated.

(2) In this section “**local authority**” means, as respects England and Wales, the council of a county,. . . [^c15333841] London borough or county district[^c15333851]. . . [^c15333861] and the Common Council of the City of London and “network owner", “new network”and “transferred network”shall be given the meanings in section 122(5) above,[^c15333871]and, as respects Scotland, a county council and a town council.

## 123. Power of highway and other authorities to contribute to cost of barriers, etc., at level crossings.

*Extent: S*

(1) Contributions to the expenses incurred by the Railways Board or a network owner[^c15333881] in providing lifting or other barriers, lights, signs, or other devices or appliances for the protection or convenience of the public at or near any level crossing where a road is crossed by a railway of the Board , or, as the case may be, by a railway comprised in a transferred network or a new network of that network owner, may be made by—

- (a) the highway[^c15333901] authority, if any, for that road; and[^c15333911]
- (b) any local authority, not being the highway authority, in whose area the level crossing is situated.

(2) In this section “**local authority**” means, as respects England and Wales, the council of a county,. . . [^c15333921] London borough or county district[^c15333931]. . . [^c15333941] and the Common Council of the City of London and “network owner", “new network" and “transferred network"shall be given the meanings in section 122(5) above,[^c15333951]and, as respects Scotland, a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994[^c15333961].
