# Transport Act 1968, section 158

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2024-05-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/73
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 158. Inquiries.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c15337451] The Minister may hold inquiries for the purposes of his functions under any provision of this Act [^c15337461]. . . as if those purposes were purposes of the [^c15337471]Ministry of Transport Act 1919 and section 20 of that Act shall apply accordingly.

(2) For the purposes of any functions under this Act of the Secretary of State for Scotland or the Secretary of State for Wales, subsection (1) of this section, the said section 20 and section 90(2) to (7) of the Act of 1962 shall have effect as if for any reference therein to the Minister there were substituted a reference to that Secretary of State.
