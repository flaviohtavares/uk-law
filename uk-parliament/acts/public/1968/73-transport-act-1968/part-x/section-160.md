# Transport Act 1968, section 160

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 160. Stamp duty.[^c15339331]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 2012-07-17).**

(1) Nothing in section 12 of the [^c15339341]Finance Act 1895 (which requires Acts to be stamped as conveyances on sale in certain cases) [^c-122e3c7781571fac049cb212017acc7f]... shall be taken as applying to this Act.

(2)[^c15339361] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c15339361] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c15339371] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Stamp duty shall not be chargeable on any instrument which is certified to the Commissioners of Inland Revenue by the appropriate Board or new authority or appropriate Executive for any area which is an integrated transport area or *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-158cacc50b41bfa266996e29f1485a36] a passenger transport area for the purposes of Part II of this Act as having been made or executed in pursuance of Schedule 4 to this Act [^c15339391] ... ; but no such instrument shall be deemed to be duly stamped unless it is stamped with the duty to which it would but for this subsection be liable or it has, in accordance with the provisions of section 12 of the [^c15339401]Stamp Act 1891, been stamped with a particular stamp denoting that it is not chargeable with any duty or that it is duly stamped.

## 160. Stamp duty.[^c15339411]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Nothing in section 12 of the [^c15339421]Finance Act 1895 (which requires Acts to be stamped as conveyances on sale in certain cases) [^c-122e3c7781571fac049cb212017acc7f]... shall be taken as applying to this Act.

(2), (3) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c15339441]

(4) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c15339451]

(5) Stamp duty shall not be chargeable on any instrument which is certified to the Commissioners of Inland Revenue by the appropriate Board or new authority or appropriate Executive established under section 9(1) of this Act as having been made or executed in pursuance of Schedule 4 to this Act [^c15339461]. . .; but no such instrument shall be deemed to be duly stamped unless it is stamped with the duty to which it would but for this subsection be liable or it has, in accordance with the provisions of section 12 of the [^c15339471]Stamp Act 1891, been stamped with a particular stamp denoting that it is not chargeable with any duty or that it is duly stamped.
