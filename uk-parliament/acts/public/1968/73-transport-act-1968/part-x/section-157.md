## 157. Orders and regulations.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any power to make orders or regulations conferred on any Minister by any provision of this Act shall [^c-9321cdf5fbe1577048037a6fac4b16f1]... be exercisable by statutory instrument; and any power to make an order under any provision of this Act shall include power to make such an order varying or revoking any previous order under that provision.
