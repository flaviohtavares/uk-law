## 59. The licensing authority for Part V.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-01-01).**

(1)[^c15320271] The traffic commissioner for any traffic area constituted for the purposes of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981 shall exercise the functions conferred on him by this Part of this Act and is in this Part of this Act referred to as “**the licensing authority.**”

(2) In the exercise of his functions under this Part of this Act. . . [^c15320281] the licensing authority shall act under the general directions of the Minister.
