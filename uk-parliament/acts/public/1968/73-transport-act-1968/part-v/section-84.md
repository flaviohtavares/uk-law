# Transport Act 1968, section 84

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Evidence by certificate.[^c15323901]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-01-01).**

In any proceedings for an offence under this Part of this Act or Schedule 9 thereto a certificate signed by or on behalf of a licensing authority and stating—

- (a) that, on any date, a person was or was not the holder of an operator’s licence,. . . [^c15323911]. . . [^c15323921] granted by the authority;
- (b) the dates of the coming into force and expiration of any such licence. . . [^c15323911] granted by the authority;
- (c) the terms and conditions of any operator’s licence. . . [^c15323911] granted by the authority;
- (d) that a person is by virtue of an order of the authority disqualified from holding or obtaining an operator’s licence,. . . [^c15323911]. . . [^c15323921] licence indefinitely or for a specified period;
- (e) that a direction, having effect indefinitely or for a specified period, has been given by the licensing authority under section 69(6) of this Act in relation to any person;
- (f) that, on any date or during any specified period, any such licence. . . [^c15323911] granted by the authority was of no effect by reason of a direction that it be suspended,

shall be evidence, and in Scotland sufficient evidence, of the facts stated; and a certificate stating any of the matters aforesaid and purporting to be signed by or on behalf of a licensing authority shall be deemed to be so signed unless the contrary is proved.
