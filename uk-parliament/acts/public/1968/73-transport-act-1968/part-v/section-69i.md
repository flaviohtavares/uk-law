# Transport Act 1968, section 69I

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 69I. Assessors.[^c15323361]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-01-01).**

(1) In considering any financial question which appears to him to arise in relation to the exercise of his functions under this Part of this Act or [^c15323371] the Goods Vehicles (Operators’ Licences, Qualifications and Fees) Regulations 1984, the licensing authority may be assisted by an assessor drawn from a panel of persons appointed for that purpose by the Secretary of State.

(2) The Secretary of State shall pay to any such assessor in respect of his services such remuneration as may be determined by the Secretary of State with the consent of the Treasury.
