# Transport Act 1968, section 83

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Falsification of consignment notes and records.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-01-01).**

Any person who makes, or causes to be made, any. . . [^c15323871] document required to be made under section. . . [^c15323871] 81 of this Act which he knows to be false or, with intent to deceive, alters or causes to be altered any such. . . [^c15323871] document shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding £200;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years.
