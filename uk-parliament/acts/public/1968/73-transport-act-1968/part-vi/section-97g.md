# Transport Act 1968, section 97G

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2024-05-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/73
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97G. Downloading and retaining data: offences[^key-e330be0867afc24c41c40e53132dbb3c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A transport undertaking commits an offence if, without reasonable excuse, it fails to comply with section 97D or 97E, or with a requirement imposed under section 97F.

(2) A transport undertaking commits an offence if, without reasonable excuse, it fails to comply with any requirement imposed by article 10 of the Community Drivers’ Hours Regulation or article 11(2)(b) of the Annex to the AETR[^key-a8dc969aa892d52c055d66790b2dde7c] in respect of the retention of data downloaded in accordance with section 97D to 97F.

(3) An offence under this section is punishable on summary conviction with a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
