# Transport Act 1968, section 97AA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2024-05-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/73

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97AA. Forgery, etc of seals on recording equipment[^c15327001]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person who, with intent to deceive, forges, alters or uses any seal on recording equipment installed in, or designed for installation in, a vehicle to which section 97 or 97ZA[^c-0a47e6101a22bd84755da40785b66ca8] of this Act applies, shall be guilty of an offence.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) above shall be liable—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or
- (b) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum.

(3) In the application of this section to England and Wales a person “**forges**” a seal if he makes a false seal in order that it may be used as genuine.
