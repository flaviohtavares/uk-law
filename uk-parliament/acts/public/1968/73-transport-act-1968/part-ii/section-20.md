## 20. Special duty of certain Executives with respect to railway passenger services.[^c-dae5b66085e1d7bc7e0f6b1e5414ffb1][^M_C_2f7e3029-c0fa-4d46-c737-867b0c39d3d5][^c15313951]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c15313971] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) [^c15314001]Without prejudice to their *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c1bce112df5c0a5c45794a7a38bcf1a] duty under section 9A(3)[^c15313981] *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c1bce112df5c0a5c45794a7a38bcf1a] of this Act, *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c1bce112df5c0a5c45794a7a38bcf1a] it shall be the special duty of the Executive for an integrated transport area *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-1a1206fad005489e93b29fbd1d0e30a0] , a combined authority area[^c-cbb9a4b319972890e1e27c740110ab6c] or a passenger transport[^c15313991] area ... —

- (a) to [^c15314011]... keep under review, the railway passenger services provided by passenger service operators (within the meaning of Part I of the Railways Act 1993)[^c15314021] for meeting the needs of persons travelling between places in that area or between such places and places outside that area but within the permitted distance [^c-7ed0aff7d665448f966e809b9f898d16]... and *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6cce563552e7f4c3370f8907c833a12f]
- (b (Extent: E.W.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6cce563552e7f4c3370f8907c833a12f] without prejudice to the general powers of the Executive under section 10 of this Act *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6cce563552e7f4c3370f8907c833a12f] , to enter into such agreements with the Strategic Rail Authority or any wholly-owned subsidiary of the Strategic Rail Authority as the Passenger Transport Authority for that area may approve for securing the provision of such railway passenger services as the Passenger Transport Authority[^c15314031] *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6cce563552e7f4c3370f8907c833a12f] consider it appropriate to secure to meet any public transport requirements within that area[^c15314041] *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6cce563552e7f4c3370f8907c833a12f]. *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6cce563552e7f4c3370f8907c833a12f]

(2A)[^c-81504e473e3bca40742e2d6199b392d8] For the purposes of subsection (2)(a) “**permitted distance**”, in relation to an integrated transport area, a combined authority area , a combined county authority area[^c-885ba1581ccb28cf995788219762f8ac] or a passenger transport area, means the distance of 25 miles from the nearest point on the boundary of that area.

(3) The Strategic Rail Authority[^c15314051] *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7773d1496f2806155cfa5e45c766cd4b] shall furnish the Executive with any information which the Executive may reasonably require for the purposes of the discharge of their functions under subsection (2) of this section. *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7773d1496f2806155cfa5e45c766cd4b]

(4) Without prejudice to the general power of the Executive under section 10(1)(vi) of this Act, any agreement under this section may include provision for the making of payments by the Executive to *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a808c4ac33651d2356649ef051c3472] the Strategic Rail Authority or a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Strategic Rail Authority in respect of the railway passenger services provided[^c15314061] *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a808c4ac33651d2356649ef051c3472] in pursuance of the agreement. *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a808c4ac33651d2356649ef051c3472]

(5) Before entering into any agreement under this section, the Executive shall send a copy of the proposed agreement to the Minister; but a failure to comply with this subsection shall not affect the validity of the agreement. *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a808c4ac33651d2356649ef051c3472]

(6) If any dispute arises between the Executive and *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a808c4ac33651d2356649ef051c3472] the Strategic Rail Authority or any wholly-owned subsidiary of the Strategic Rail Authority[^c15314071] *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a808c4ac33651d2356649ef051c3472] in connection with the provisions of subsection (2) or (3) of this section, either of them may require the dispute to be referred to the Minister for determination, and any agreement under the said subsection (2) may include provision for any dispute in connection with the agreement to be so referred; and where any dispute is referred to the Minister under or by virtue of this subsection, then, subject to subsection (7) of this section, the Minister may give such directions to the Executive and *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a808c4ac33651d2356649ef051c3472] the Strategic Rail Authority or the subsidiary[^c15314081] *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a808c4ac33651d2356649ef051c3472] with respect to the dispute as he thinks fit. *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a808c4ac33651d2356649ef051c3472]

(7) Before giving any directions under subsection (6) of this section to the Executive for a *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a808c4ac33651d2356649ef051c3472] passenger transport *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a808c4ac33651d2356649ef051c3472] area in Scotland or Wales the Minister shall consult with the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a808c4ac33651d2356649ef051c3472]
