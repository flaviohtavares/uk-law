# Town and Country Planning Act 1968, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Removal of need for Minister's consent to certain local authority grants.

The power of a local authority under section 1(1) (b) of the [^m00026]Local Authorities (Historic Buildings) Act 1962 to contribute towards expenses incurred or to be incurred in the repair or maintenance of a building in their area appearing to them to be of architectural or historic interest shall be exercisable without the consent of the Minister.
