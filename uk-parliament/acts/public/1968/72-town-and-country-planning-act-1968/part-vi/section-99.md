# Town and Country Planning Act 1968, section 99

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 99. Exchequer contributions in connection with town development.

In section 2(2) of the [^m00055]Town Development Act 1952 (Exchequer contributions towards specified expenses incurred by the council of a county district in connection with town development), after paragraph (c) there shall be inserted the following paragraph:—

> - (cc) expenses of providing buildings and other works for social, cultural or recreational purposes

.
