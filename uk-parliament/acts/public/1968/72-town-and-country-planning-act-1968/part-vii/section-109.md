# Town and Country Planning Act 1968, section 109

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 109. Short title, citation and extent.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Town and Country Planning Act 1968.

(2) The Town and Country Planning Acts 1962 to 1966 and this Act may be cited as the Town and Country Planning Acts 1962 to 1968.

(3) This Act—

- (a) except so far as it amends the [^m00059]House of Commons Disqualification Act 1957, section 24(9) of the [^m00060]Industrial Development Act 1966 and section 8(3) of the [^m00061]Civic Amenities Act 1967, shall not extend to Scotland ; and
- (b) except so far as it amends the said Act of 1957, shall not extend to Northern Ireland.
