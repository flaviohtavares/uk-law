# Town and Country Planning Act 1968, section 106

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 106. Adaptation, amendment and modification of enactments.

Schedule 9 to this Act shall have effect for adapting and interpreting Acts other than this Act and for making amendments and modifications to such Acts, being minor amendments and amendments consequential on the foregoing provisions of this Act.
