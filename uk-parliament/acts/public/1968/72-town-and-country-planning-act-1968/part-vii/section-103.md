# Town and Country Planning Act 1968, section 103

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 103. Expenses.

There shall be defrayed out of moneys provided by Parliament—

- (a) any sums required for the payment of grants under section 98 above;
- (b) any other expenses of a Minister under this Act; and
- (c) any increase attributable to the provisions of this Act in the sums payable out of moneys so provided under any other enactment.
