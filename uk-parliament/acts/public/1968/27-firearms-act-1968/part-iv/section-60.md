# Firearms Act 1968, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/27

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 60. Short title, commencement and extent.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Firearms Act 1968.

(2) This Act shall come into force on 1st August 1968.

(3) This Act shall not extend to Northern Ireland.
