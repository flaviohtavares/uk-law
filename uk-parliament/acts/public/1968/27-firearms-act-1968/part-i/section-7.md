# Firearms Act 1968, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 7. Police permit.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person who has obtained from the chief officer of police for the area in which he resides a permit for the purpose in the prescribed form may, without holding a certificate under this Act, have in his possession—

- (a)[^key-cd548ab5c199f2838b9f5185b3799b8a] a firearm and ammunition in accordance with the terms of the permit.
- (b)[^key-9648e0a88ba238e308bb2898e6489dc0] a relevant accessory.

(2) It is an offence for a person knowingly or recklessly to make a statement false in any material particular[^c9809381] for the purpose of procuring, whether for himself or for another person, the grant of a permit under this section.
