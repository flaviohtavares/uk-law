## 22. Acquisition and possession of firearms by minors.[^c18189451]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) It is an offence for a person under the age of eighteen to purchase or hire any firearm or ammunition.[^c-d798fa9d9ce958a42fd7fba0c02fdd93]

(1A)[^c-5d76be0500d7db3cc05fa57a2983c384] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) It is an offence for a person under the age of fourteen to have in his possession any firearm or ammunition to which section 1 of this Act applies, except in circumstances where under section 11(1), (3) or (4) of this Act or section 15 of the Firearms (Amendment) Act 1988[^c9810281] he is entitled to have possession of it without holding a firearm certificate.

(3) It is an offence for a person under the age of fifteen to have with him an assembled shot gun except while under the supervision of a person of or over the age of twenty-one, or while the shot gun is so covered with a securely fastened gun cover that it cannot be fired.

(4) Subject to section 23 below, it is an offence for a person under the age of *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-89a4fdad6ff6227a648aedba5e3df735] eighteen[^M_F_2b56e79a-49b8-448d-96cf-ba8026cf718f] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-89a4fdad6ff6227a648aedba5e3df735] to have with him an air weapon or ammunition for an air weapon. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-89a4fdad6ff6227a648aedba5e3df735]
