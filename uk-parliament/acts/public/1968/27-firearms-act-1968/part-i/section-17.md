## 17. Use of firearm to resist arrest.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) It is an offence for a person to make or attempt to make any use whatsoever of a firearm or imitation firearm with intent to resist or prevent the lawful arrest or detention of himself or another person.

(2)[^c9809621] If a person, at the time of his committing or being arrested for an offence specified in Schedule 1 to this Act, has in his possession a firearm or imitation firearm, he shall be guilty of an offence under this subsection unless he shows that he had it in his possession for a lawful object.

(3) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c9809631]

(4) For purposes of this section, the definition of “**firearm**” in section 57(1) of this Act shall apply without paragraphs (b) and (c) of that subsection, and “**imitation firearm**” shall be construed accordingly.

(5) In the application of this section to Scotland, a reference to Schedule 2 to this Act shall be substituted for the reference in subsection (2) to Schedule 1 . . . [^c9809641]
