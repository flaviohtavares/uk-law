## 23 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Exceptions from s. 22(4) (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b][^c18086331][^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b][^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b] It is not an offence under section 22(4) of this Act for a person to have with him an air weapon or ammunition while he is under the supervision of a person of or over the age of twenty-one; but where a person has with him an air weapon on any premises in circumstances where he would be prohibited from having it with him but for this subsection, it is an offence *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b] for the person under whose supervision he is to allow him to use it for firing any missile beyond those premises.[^M_F_f7f6e3fc-855e-4310-cfc7-09c374b3c915] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b]. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b]

(1A (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_c9b6c3f4-036d-4677-c32c-4bca7065dffa][^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b] In proceedings against a person for an offence under subsection (1) it shall be a defence for him to show that the only premises into or across which the missile was fired were premises the occupier of which had consented to the firing of the missile (whether specifically or by way of a general consent). *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b] It is not an offence under section 22(4) *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b] [^M_F_4e442541-9b6a-4473-9a3b-0748587dc70d]... of this Act for a person to have with him an air weapon or ammunition at a time when— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b] being a member of a rifle club or miniature rifle club for the time being approved by the Secretary of State for the purposes of this section or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b] section 15 of the Firearms (Amendment) Act 1988[^M_F_ae606efb-ecc7-401b-a22d-bb3ef8a7ddcc] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b], he is engaged as such a member *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b] [^M_F_12202dfb-1505-437f-b749-67176b47bdcf]... in connection with *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b] target shooting[^M_F_75244edd-4c8e-4713-8723-3ef8d74e6680] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b]; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b] he is using the weapon or ammunition at a shooting gallery where the only firearms used are either air weapons or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b] rifles chambered for .22 rim-fire cartridges[^c-62befad36bd0957ba34caf41c8642ef5] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b]. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_02eb1f94-fa27-42dd-a504-5ae13eba5899][^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b] It is not an offence under section 22(4) of this Act for a person of or over the age of fourteen to have with him an air weapon or ammunition on private premises with the consent of the occupier. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4811b7616a1531b233db0bb698ef236b]
