# Firearms Act 1968, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 13. Equipment for ships and aircraft.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person may, without holding a certificate,—

- (a) have in his possession a firearm , ammunition or a relevant accessory[^c-c7c310ecc22f7e5c8f68328830c60470] on board a ship, or a signalling apparatus , or ammunition or a relevant accessory therefor,[^c-101a7efceb1cb1a5927e23a014542349] on board an aircraft or at an aerodrome, as part of the equipment of the ship, aircraft or aerodrome;
- (b) remove a signalling apparatus , or ammunition or a relevant accessory[^c-c78481dec79a9d3f80d06a2deb55568b] therefor, being part of the equipment of an aircraft, from one aircraft to another at an aerodrome, or from or to an aircraft at an aerodrome to or from a place appointed for the storage thereof in safe custody at that aerodrome, and keep any such apparatus , or ammunition or relevant accessory[^c-42df0db5047eab1fcac0bf29e5f49105] at such a place; [^c-26e8c2e9a3ff11b37d176cfd05e21f4c]...
- (c) if he has obtained from a constable[^c9809531] a permit for the purpose in the prescribed form, remove a firearm from or to a ship, or a signalling apparatus from or to an aircraft or aerodrome, to or from such place and for such purpose as may be specified in the permit.
- (d)[^c-5baf372fbe635285b53889dd117792f6] having obtained a permit under paragraph (c), remove any relevant accessory from or to a ship, aircraft or aerodrome, to or from the place specified in the permit.

(2) It is an offence for a person knowingly or recklessly to make a statement false in any material particular[^c9809541] for the purpose of procuring, either for himself or for another person, the grant of a permit under subsection (1)(c) of this section.
