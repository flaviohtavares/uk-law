## 11. Sports, athletics and other approved activities.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person carrying a firearm , ammunition or a relevant accessory[^key-d08184716e7ec6d94fa3310eda40d9cb] belonging to another person holding a certificate under this Act may, without himself holding such a certificate, have in his possession that firearm , ammunition or relevant accessory[^key-c9f7c7120a494ec8e3ad777784d30d4e] under instructions from, and for the use of, that other person for sporting purposes only ; but where the person carrying the firearm[^key-807c5fd9a34821d517062b4dd412ddb1] , ammunition or relevant accessory is under the age of eighteen, this subsection applies only if the other person is of or over the age of eighteen.

(2) A person of or over the age of eighteen[^key-823da05b8b1c87795a97256a1e452463] may, without holding a certificate, have a firearm in his possession at an athletic meeting for the purpose of starting races at that meeting.

(3)[^c9809471] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3A)[^key-8535ec86b116e0fb1594141c987309e4] Subsection (4) applies to a person at a relevant rifle range or shooting gallery who is not the operator of the range or gallery.

(4) The person may, without holding a certificate, have in their possession—

- (a) a miniature rifle, [^key-8d858530cdffd96e493c9cca8add1b94]...
- (b) ammunition for a miniature rifle, and[^key-2804eeacff18bdcb8b9eed35b3f79be1]
- (c) a relevant accessory,

provided by the operator of the range or gallery.

(4A) In this section—

- “**miniature rifle**” means a rifle chambered for .22 rim-fire cartridges;
- “**operator**” means the person conducting or carrying on the relevant rifle range or shooting gallery;
- “**relevant rifle range or shooting gallery**” means a rifle range or shooting gallery at which no firearms are used other than air weapons or miniature rifles.

(5)[^key-f43f54b23a9402979e1cd431d980e4c5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) A person may, without holding a shot gun certificate, use a shot gun at a time and place approved for shooting at artificial targets by the chief officer of police for the area in which that place is situated.
