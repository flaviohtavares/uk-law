# Firearms Act 1968, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 19. Carrying firearm in a public place.

*Extent: E+W+S*

A person commits an offence if, without lawful authority or reasonable excuse (the proof whereof lies on him) he has with him in a public place

- (a)[^c18081591] a loaded shot gun,
- (b) an air weapon (whether loaded or not),
- (c) any other firearm (whether loaded or not) together with ammunition suitable for use in that firearm, or
- (d) an imitation firearm.
