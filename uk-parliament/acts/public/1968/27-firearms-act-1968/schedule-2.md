# Firearms Act 1968, SCHEDULE 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Offences to which sections 17(2) and 18 apply in Scotland

*Extent: E+W+S*

Sections 17, 18.

### Common Law Offences

**1.** Abduction.

**2.** Administration of drugs with intent to enable or assist the commission of a crime.

**3.** Assault.

**4.** Housebreaking with intent to steal.

**5.** Malicious mischief.

**6.** Mobbing and rioting.

**7.** Perverting the course of justice.

**8.** Prison breaking and breaking into prison to rescue prisoners.

**9.** Rape.

**10.** Robbery.

**11.** Theft.

**12.** Use of threats with intent to extort money or property.

**13.** Wilful fireraising and culpable and reckless fireraising.

### Statutory Offences

**13A.**[^c9816941] Offences against section 57 of the Civil Government (Scotland) Act 1982

**Not yet in force.**

**13B.**[^c9816981] An offence under paragraph 4 of Schedule 11 to the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (assaulting a detainee custody officer).

**14.**[^c9817011] Offences against such of the provisions of section 4 of the [^c9817031]Vagrancy Act 1824 as are extended to Scotland by section 15 of the [^c9817051]Prevention of Crimes Act 1871.

**15.** Offences against the third and fourth paragraphs of section 7 of the [^c9817071]Prevention of Crimes Act 1871.

**16.** Offences against sections 2, 3 or 4 of the [^c9817091]Explosive Substances Act 1883.

**17.**[^c9817111] Offences against section 175 of the [^c9817131]Road Traffic Act 1972.

**18.** Offences against section 90 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012.[^key-7e7a70361a04f606ed0de84f0c637bb6]

### Attempts

**19.** Attempt to commit any of the offences mentioned in this Schedule.
