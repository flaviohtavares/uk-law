# Firearms Act 1968, SCHEDULE 1

## SCHEDULE 1 — Offences to which section 17(2) applies

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 17.

**1.**[^c9816411] Offences under section 1 of the [^c9816431]Criminal Damage Act 1971.

**2.** Offences under any of the following provisions of the Offences Against the [^c9816451]Person Act 1861:—

- sections 20 to 22 (inflicting bodily injury; garrotting; criminal use of stupefying drugs);
- section 30 (laying explosive to building etc. );
- section 32 (endangering railway passengers by tampering with track);
- section 38 (assault with intent to commit felony or resist arrest);
- section 47 (criminal assaults);
- . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c9816471]

**2A.**[^c9816501] Offences under Part I of the Child Abduction Act 1984 (abduction of children).

**3.**[^c9816521] Offences under such of the provisions of section 4 of the Vagrancy Act 1824 as are referred to in and amended by section 15 of the Prevention of Crimes Act 1871 and section 7 of the Penal Servitude Act 1891 (suspected persons and reputed thieves being abroad with criminal intent).

**4.**[^c9816591] Theft, robbery[^c9816611] burglary, blackmail and any offence under section 12(1) (taking of motor vehicle or other conveyance without owner’s consent) of the [^c9816631]Theft Act 1968.

**5.** Offences under section 89(1) of the Police Act 1996[^c9816691] or section 90 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 (assaulting or impeding police)[^key-02aaa51b03e6e2636bbdf8eb633d486b] .

**5A.**[^c9816731] An offence under section 90(1) of the Criminal Justice Act 1991 (assaulting prisoner custody officer).

**5B.**[^c17641261] An offence under section 13(1) of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (assaulting secure training centre custody officer).

**Not yet in force.**

**5C.**[^c9816781] An offence under paragraph 4 of Schedule 11 to the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (assaulting a detainee custody officer).

**6.**[^c18322551] Offences under any of the following provisions of the Sexual Offences Act 2003—

- (a) section 1 (rape);
- (b) section 2 (assault by penetration);
- (c) section 4 (causing a person to engage in sexual activity without consent), where the activity caused involved penetration within subsection (4)(a) to (d) of that section;
- (d) section 5 (rape of a child under 13);
- (e) section 6 (assault of a child under 13 by penetration);
- (f) section 8 (causing or inciting a child under 13 to engage in sexual activity), where an activity involving penetration within subsection (3)(a) to (d) of that section was caused;
- (g) section 30 (sexual activity with a person with a mental disorder impeding choice), where the touching involved penetration within subsection (3)(a) to (d) of that section;
- (h) section 31 (causing or inciting a person, with a mental disorder impeding choice, to engage in sexual activity), where an activity involving penetration within subsection (3)(a) to (d) of that section was caused.

**6A.**[^key-fee740764ac1ded233a857170b2fa224] An offence under paragraph 14 or 24 of Schedule 10 to the Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015 (assaulting secure college custody officer).

**7.** . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c9816831]

**8.** Aiding or abetting the commission of any offence specified in paragraphs 1 to[^c9816851] 6A[^key-894b5c5a9ef59735aa43691801ee5f21] of this Schedule.

**9.** Attempting to commit any offence so specified, . . . [^c9816871]
