## 57. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

- “[^key-22bd257b7e6cff6cd1bcdcf0cba08a8c]**the 2006 Act**” means the Armed Forces Act 2006;
- [^key-90aa4da2d9e5f6c0f6012ab20b045944]...
- [^key-90aa4da2d9e5f6c0f6012ab20b045944]...
- “**appellant**” includes a person who [^key-700d05e56e4c88e46ca9fb61f5984e81]... wishes to appeal under Part II of this Act to the Appeal Court;
- [^key-90aa4da2d9e5f6c0f6012ab20b045944]...
- [^key-90aa4da2d9e5f6c0f6012ab20b045944]...
- [^key-90aa4da2d9e5f6c0f6012ab20b045944]...
- “[^key-24b1178316a77539bbc794f79565ce73]**Director of Service Prosecutions**” has the meaning given by section 374 of the 2006 Act;
- [^key-90aa4da2d9e5f6c0f6012ab20b045944]...
- “**enactment**” includes an enactment of the Parliament of Northern Ireland;
- “[^key-c12f6bd5619b7146fb6beae4f0a58269]**hospital order**” has the meaning given in section 37 of the Mental Health Act 1983;
- “**interim hospital order**” has the meaning given in section 38 of that Act;
- [^key-90aa4da2d9e5f6c0f6012ab20b045944]...
- [^key-90aa4da2d9e5f6c0f6012ab20b045944]...
- “**the Lord Chief Justice**” means the Lord Chief Justice of England;
- [^key-90aa4da2d9e5f6c0f6012ab20b045944]...
- [^key-90aa4da2d9e5f6c0f6012ab20b045944]...
- “**prescribed**” means prescribed by rules of court; [^c7716031]. . .
- “**the registrar**” means the registrar of the Appeal Court. ; and[^c7716041]
- [^key-90aa4da2d9e5f6c0f6012ab20b045944]...
- “**sentence**”, in relation to an offence, includes any order made by a court when dealing with an offender.
- [^key-90aa4da2d9e5f6c0f6012ab20b045944]...

(2)[^key-4f46a2114409b11db87a552cfb162775] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^key-4f46a2114409b11db87a552cfb162775] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2B)[^key-4f46a2114409b11db87a552cfb162775] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In Part III of this Act “**the accused**” means, in relation to an appeal from the Appeal Court to the Supreme Court[^key-f46cad3ef745f36a62bfb57336f3c38f], the person who was the appellant in the Appeal Court.[^key-8f99af9619228f1b7a0a38301f601637]

(4) Any reference in this Act to another enactment is a reference thereto as amended by or under any other enactment, including this Act.

(5) Any reference in this Act to an enactment of the Parliament of Northern Ireland includes a reference to an enactment corresponding thereto and for the time being in force in Northern Ireland.
