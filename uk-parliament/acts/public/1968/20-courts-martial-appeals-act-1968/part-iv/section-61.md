# Courts-Martial (Appeals) Act 1968, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Short title and commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Court Martial Appeals Act 1968[^c-575a7fd10a153533e9b58fa22bfa34d6].

(2)[^c7716361] This Act shall come into force on the day appointed under section 106(5) of the [^c7716371]Criminal Justice Act 1967 for the coming into force of section 98 of that Act.
