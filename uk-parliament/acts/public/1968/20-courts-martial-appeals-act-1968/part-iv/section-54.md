# Courts-Martial (Appeals) Act 1968, section 54

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Saving for prerogative.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-4a5d10dc1642eb7873a296eef7359bce] Nothing in this Act is to be taken as affecting any right of Her Majesty, by virtue of Her Royal prerogative or otherwise, to quash a conviction by the Court Martial[^c-551a84d4517f53a92895877744e091fc], so far as regards the exercise thereof at a time before whichever of the following two events first occurs—

- (a) the receipt by the registrar of an application for leave to appeal under Part II of this Act against the conviction; or
- (b) the receipt by him of particulars of such an application furnished in pursuance of section 9 of this Act.

(2) Nothing in this Act is to be taken as affecting Her Majesty’s Royal prerogative of mercy.
