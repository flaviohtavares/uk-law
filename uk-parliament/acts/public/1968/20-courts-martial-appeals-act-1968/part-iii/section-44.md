# Courts-Martial (Appeals) Act 1968, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/20

## 44. Presence of accused at hearing.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the accused is detained pending an appeal from the Appeal Court to the Supreme Court[^c-e22897b0036b011b8304cb29ff00f844], he shall not be entitled to be present on the hearing of the appeal or of any proceedings preliminary or incidental thereto unless Supreme Court Rules authorise[^c-d6a8c0993006daa27c98270a38b89fbc] him to be present, or the Supreme Court[^c-d1a0b120c1633b6735e8ea9131b685af] or the Appeal Court give him leave to be present.
