# Courts-Martial (Appeals) Act 1968, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Sittings.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purpose of hearing and determining appeals under this Act, or any matter preliminary or incidental to an appeal, the Appeal Court shall be summoned in accordance with directions given in accordance with Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^c-004735762a005a2d1e9f669b6a1026e1].

(2)[^c-9633a6dd20b88827c393ac883fc3a97a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The Appeal Court shall sit at such place as such directions may provide[^c-9b119558f0bf6c1fc9164b008994e93a], whether within or outside the United Kingdom.
