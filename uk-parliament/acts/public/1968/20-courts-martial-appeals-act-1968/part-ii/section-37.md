# Courts-Martial (Appeals) Act 1968, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Provision of record of proceedings of the Court Martial.[^key-9fbd09972b3179f59d86a7af89777680][^key-b3e6cc59a4f484243dc643f5746214a1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the case of every appeal or application for leave to appeal to the Appeal Court, the Judge Advocate General must provide the registrar, in accordance with rules of court, with a record of the proceedings of the Court Martial.
