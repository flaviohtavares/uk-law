## 8. Right of appeal.[^key-2efcf5edfb8021732593198a1de98e67][^key-54f43fba03b596c98701b57f4f2048ec]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-f08708fa241f8869b9587035ab08d3ed] Subject to the provisions of this Act, a person convicted by the Court Martial[^key-73afecda0afbba5c1bdc7fff2f5d6c4f] may, with the leave of the Appeal Court, appeal to the Court —[^c7714201]

- (a) against his conviction; and
- (b) against any sentence (not being a sentence fixed by law) passed on him for the offence for which he was convicted, whether passed on conviction or in subsequent proceedings[^key-9314415bc104f4e0539da08d53ef2f94].

*Extent: E+W*

(1ZA)[^key-cdb7285eedca85ba1c61c9a26f72150d] In subsection (1) above, the reference to a sentence fixed by law does not include a reference to an order made under section 321 of the Sentencing Code[^key-2a12882b9ed305c415e65b0edf7a5c09] in relation to a life sentence (as defined in section 324 of that Code[^key-86bf0453e56658afe80206c3a5e091dd]) that is fixed by law.
