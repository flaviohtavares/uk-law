# Courts-Martial (Appeals) Act 1968, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Power to quash conviction as unsafe.[^c7714331]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c7714341] The Appeal Court—

- (a)[^c-264af092a0d1c92ba30f0345db7cf0fc] shall allow an appeal against conviction by the Court Martial[^c-58ec62687dbb833e1991ebdbbb95dc08] if they think that the conviction is unsafe; and
- (b) shall dismiss such an appeal in any other case.

(2) If the Appeal Court allow an appeal against conviction, they shall quash the conviction.

(3)[^c-ff06f40dc8e97ef3bf5cd29d0fb3e84d] Where the Appeal Court quash a conviction, the appellant is to be treated as if he had been acquitted by the Court Martial; but this does not apply if an order under section 19 authorising the appellant to be retried is made.
