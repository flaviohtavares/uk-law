# Courts-Martial (Appeals) Act 1968, section 14

## 14. Substitution of conviction on different charge otherwise than after guilty plea.[^c-69e702006b21f5280f9bb4eb191b024b][^c-380cb740081ed1108076a61e29205350]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-8d25899ee08d549b705f248eae545c88] This section applies where—

- (a) an appellant has been convicted of an offence to which he did not plead guilty;
- (b) the Court Martial could lawfully have found him guilty of some other offence; and
- (c) it appears to the Appeal Court on an appeal against conviction that the Court Martial must have been satisfied of facts which prove him guilty of that other offence.

(2)[^c-3ae8cd391d984432776978c30450e396] The Appeal Court may, instead of allowing or dismissing the appeal, substitute for the finding of the Court Martial[^c-d9c352739733ea9535f479521b1694d6] a finding of guilty of the other offence, and may pass on the appellant, in substitution for the sentence passed on him by the Court Martial, any sentence that—[^c-eaeebde1b93859232a8fc1f57acd6311]

- (a) they think appropriate;
- (b)[^c-76e42a2ce5e801b29742f2a9b6f1ab2c] is a sentence that the Court Martial would have had power to pass in respect of that other offence; and
- (c) is not more severe than the sentence passed by the Court Martial.
