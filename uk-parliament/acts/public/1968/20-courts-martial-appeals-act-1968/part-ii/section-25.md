## 25. Disposal of appeal under s. 24[^c-53da555844a868886d6b44f66bc5bd1c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to appeals under section 24 of this Act.

(1A)[^c-84251cb7566c9aec1d2325e920013178] The Appeal Court—

- (a) shall allow an appeal against a finding if they think the finding is unsafe; and
- (b) shall dismiss such an appeal in any other case.

(1B) If the Appeal Court allow an appeal against a finding they shall quash the finding.

(2) Where the Appeal Court quash[^c-798201dd176a8ea656d48281c49d1e62] a finding that the appellant is unfit to stand trial—

- (a) the Court may make an order authorising the appellant to be tried[^c-586fbf7611c97b688b49ad28f6b3828a] for the offence with which he was charged; and
- (b)[^c-b41c65a74fcc8546d0c1171b0cfce410][^c-94eb89a2f81b89273335f9397eaa03fc] if such an order is made, section 20 and paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 apply in relation to the case as they apply in relation to a case in which an order under section 19 is made (and references there to conviction, and to related expressions, are to be read accordingly).

(3)[^c-5bc74aeec8cfc0faf34ded55e81b73c4] Where the Appeal Court quash a finding that the defendant did the act or made the omission charged, the appellant is to be treated as if he had been acquitted by the Court Martial; but this does not apply if an order under subsection (2)(a) authorising the appellant to be tried is made.
