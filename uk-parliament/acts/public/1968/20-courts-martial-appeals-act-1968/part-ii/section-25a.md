# Courts-Martial (Appeals) Act 1968, section 25A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25A. Right of appeal against hospital order etc.[^c-d1d14cdc555e0ccf74ef6eb1cc67e095][^c-665f8b664372b62470ca48258f3f5082]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person may, with the leave of the Appeal Court, appeal against the making by the Court Martial of an order within subsection (2) in respect of him.

(2) The orders are—

- (a) a hospital order;
- (b) an interim hospital order;
- (c) a service supervision order (as defined by section 170 of the 2006 Act).
