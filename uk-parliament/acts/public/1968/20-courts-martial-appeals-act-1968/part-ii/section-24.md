## 24. Appeal against finding of unfitness.[^c-bab90a2d7e7e1d233d77bbfeff450c5a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-5928bf5a26f63b7c43465bdca7ee2899] A person found by the Court Martial[^c-f83c79a1c6e7f54c40cfa0bf32109a68] to be unfit to stand trial and to have done the act or made the omission charged against him[^c-0bcb4e5c8ee5e5083604b478b1ae256d] may, with the leave of the Appeal Court, appeal to the Court against either or both of those findings[^c-2853a2fdb0b02d2c8ae9efd0bdfbd122].
