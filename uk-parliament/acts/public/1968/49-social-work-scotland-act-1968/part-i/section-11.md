# Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-01-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/49

> Extent: Scotland.

## 11. Acquisition of land.

*Extent: S*

(1) A local authority may be authorised by the Secretary of State to purchase compulsorily any land, whether situated in or outside their area for the purposes of any of their functions under this Act , Part 2 of the Children (Scotland) Act[^c-324e7f55d857b7ebc309096e3ae7c408] [1995 (c.36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/36) or the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act [2011 (asp 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2011/1).

(2) The [^c9964031]Acquisition of Land (Authorisation Procedure) (Scotland) Act 1947 shall apply in relation to the compulsory purchase of land under this section as if the last foregoing subsection had been in force immediately before the commencement of that Act.
