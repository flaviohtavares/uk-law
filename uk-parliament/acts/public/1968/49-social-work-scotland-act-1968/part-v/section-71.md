# Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968, section 71

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-01-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/49
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Harbouring.[^c9973051][^c9973041]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Any person who knowingly—

- (a) assists or induces or persistently attempts to induce a child so to act as to be liable to be brought back in pursuance of either of the two last foregoing sections, or
- (b) harbours or conceals a child so liable or prevents him from returning to a place or person mentioned in either of those sections,

shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale[^c9973061] or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to both such fine and such imprisonment.
