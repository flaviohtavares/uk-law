# Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-01-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/49
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Power of local authorities to assist persons in need in disposal of produce of their work.

*Extent: S*

Where, by virtue of section 12 of this Act[^c9964381], a local authority make arrangements or provide or secure the provision of facilities for the engagement of persons in need (whether under a contract of service or otherwise) in suitable work, that local authority may assist such persons in disposing of the produce of their work.
