## 13A. Residential accommodation with nursing.[^c-c02a67feffef9a72a5d21e3a475a23de][^c9964571][^c16929661]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c16929671][^c-836444e54a349d6ce748745fbad388e4] Without prejudice to section 12 of this Act, a local authority shall

- (a)[^c9964581] provide and maintain; or[^c9964591]
- (b)[^c9964601] make such arrangements as they consider appropriate and adequate for the provision of

suitable residential accommodation where nursing is provided for persons who appear to them to be in need of such accommodation by reason of infirmity, age, illness or mental disorder, dependency on drugs or alcohol or being substantially handicapped by any deformity or disability.

(2)[^c16929721][^c-7a0dd8b3362aec6289bab8100a0b1b64][^c9964611] [^c16924511] . . . Arrangements made by virtue of subsection (1) above shall be made with a voluntary or other organisation or other person, being an organisation or person providing—

- (a) an independent health care service which is a private psychiatric hospital; or
- (b) a care home service.

(2A)[^c-1e520352fa87e474d4faf8f387cc55fe] In subsection (2)(a) above, “independent health care service” and “private psychiatric hospital” have the same meanings as in section 10F of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act [1978 (c.29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/29).

(2B)[^c-b9c52c79b4d81e07ea9d035e7c75156c] In subsection (2)(b) above, “care home service” has the same meaning as in paragraph   2 of schedule   12 to the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act [2010 (asp 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/8).

(3)[^c16930511][^c-7a0dd8b3362aec6289bab8100a0b1b64] The provisions of section 6 of this Act apply in relation to premises where accommodation is provided for the purposes of this section as they apply in relation to establishments provided for the purposes of this Act.

(4)[^c9964621] No arrangements under subsection (1) above may be given effect to in relation to a person to whom section 115 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (exclusion from benefits) applies solely—

- (a) because he is destitute; or
- (b) because of the physical effects, or anticipated physical effects, of his being destitute.

(5)[^c9964621] Subsections (3) and (5) to (8) of section 95 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, and paragraph 2 of Schedule 8 to that Act, apply for the purposes of subsection (4) above as they apply for the purposes of that section, but for the references in subsections (5) and (7) of that section and in that paragraph to the Secretary of State substitute references to a local authority.
