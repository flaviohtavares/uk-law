# Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968, section 81

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-01-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1968/49
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Provisions as to decrees for aliment.

*Extent: S*

(1) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c9975341]

(2) Where a decree for aliment of a maintainable child is in force[^c9975351], on the application of the local authority concerned, any court of summary jurisdiction having jurisdiction in the place where the person liable under the decree[^c9975361] is for the time being residing may, at any time, order the payments under the decree . . . [^c9975371] to be paid to the local authority who are from time to time entitled under either of the last two foregoing sections to receive contributions in respect of the child.

(3) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c9975381] where an order made under this section in respect to a decree for aliment is in force any sums received under the decree for aliment shall be applied in like manner as if they were contributions received under a contribution order.

(4)

- (a) In this section the local authority concerned means the local authority which may make application for a contribution order in respect of a child under the last foregoing section;
- (b) where the person liable to pay aliment for a child under a decree[^c9975391] is resident in England or Wales or Northern Ireland, subsection (2) of this section shall have effect as if for the reference to a court of summary jurisdiction having jurisdiction in the place where that person is for the time being residing, there were substituted a reference to a court of summary jurisdiction having jurisdiction in any place within the area of the local authority concerned.
