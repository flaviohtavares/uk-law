## SCHEDULE 2 — Amendment of Part IV of the Children and Young Persons (Scotland) Act 1937[^c9979991]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 31.

### Part I

*Extent: S*

#### General Adaptations

**1.**[^c9980001] Any reference to a child or to a young person shall be construed as a reference to a child as defined in section 93(2)(b) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995.

**2.** Any reference to a court of summary jurisdiction or to a juvenile court . . . [^c9980011] shall be construed as a reference to the sheriff sitting summarily.
