## 140. Enforcement of ... recognizances forfeited by Crown Court.[^c-84af9cb0bb02b66f4f5e3734cb200600]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-f5bc1d80a0e90e1178aa5e2cfcd8c6de] Subject to subsection (5) below, [^c-1f4606d3a1d9d053cdee188c3e6657f9]... a recognizance forfeited by the Crown Court shall be treated for the purposes of collection, enforcement and remission of the [^c-cd8a006d6593216867dd6b25a1514974]... sum as having been [^c-95f92d92e8b2d55ed7e3236a9b4951d4]... forfeited—

- (a) by a magistrates’ court specified in an order made by the Crown Court, or
- (b) if no such order is made, by the magistrates’ court by which the offender [^c-74dc254ed703f2fe802403e4ab01af12]... was sent to the Crown Court for trial under section 51 or 51A[^c-e1c4d25e8da351f86137c61e756da165] of the [^c12957341]Crime and Disorder Act 1998,

[^c-a2ed9e808b1280c6713b7aa05fa76f41]...

(2)[^c-f5bc1d80a0e90e1178aa5e2cfcd8c6de] Subsection (3) below applies where a magistrates’ court issues a warrant of commitment on a default in the payment of—

- (a)[^c-51cab1244295707fb4e476dc2f311ef2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a sum due under a recognizance forfeited by the Crown Court.

(3)[^c-f5bc1d80a0e90e1178aa5e2cfcd8c6de] In such a case, the term of imprisonment or detention under section 108 above specified in the warrant of commitment as the term which the offender is liable to serve shall be—

- (a) the term fixed by the Crown Court under section 139(2) above, or
- (b) if that term has been reduced under section 79(2) of the [^c12957351]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 (part payment) or section 85(2) of that Act (remission), that term as so reduced,

[^c-b81e547557e2d1d9e1c1ddd3a19f5024]...

(4)[^c-f5bc1d80a0e90e1178aa5e2cfcd8c6de] Subsections (1) to (3) above shall apply in relation to a [^c-d649ece7c870b6942458439af5c30f61]... recognizance forfeited by the criminal division of the Court of Appeal, or by the Supreme Court[^c-8f026cda60a97a7d19645dcf01896b2f] on appeal from that division, as they apply in relation to a [^c-d649ece7c870b6942458439af5c30f61]... recognizance forfeited by the Crown Court; and references in those subsections to the Crown Court (except the references in subsection (1)(b)) shall be construed accordingly.

(5) A magistrates’ court shall not, under section [^c-dfcea850600dc9436bbccc463abb074b]... 120 of the [^c12957371]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 as applied by subsection (1) above, remit the whole or any part of a [^c-7c4c0468dd5acd7f0a517e1a94aa8466]... sum due under a recognizance forfeited by—

- (a) the Crown Court,
- (b) the criminal division of the Court of Appeal, or
- (c) the Supreme Court on appeal from that division,

without the consent of the Crown Court.

(6) Any [^c-b5da6d4c9da7f2186f65d5efbc97cbdf]... sum the payment of which is enforceable by a magistrates’ court by virtue of this section shall be treated for the purposes of section 38 of the Courts Act 2003 (application of receipts of designated officers)[^c-6725683ba0f320db5e862d3f3009e90f] [^c-1414723b3ae6882725f6670e91e9ffed]... as being due under a recognizance forfeited by a magistrates' court[^c-6358af99913fa27f871ebd095311cd40].
