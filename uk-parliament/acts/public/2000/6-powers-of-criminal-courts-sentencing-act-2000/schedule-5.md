### Breach of order or attendance centre rules (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

**1.** (1) Where an attendance centre order is in force and it appears on information to a justice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] [^M_F_c20da49d-5b1b-4805-d866-1731150fb81d]... that the offender— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) has failed to attend in accordance with the order, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) while attending has committed a breach of rules made under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] section 394(1)(d) or (e) of the Sentencing Code[^key-4a165e445aedb57e53d85225723b7356] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] which cannot be adequately dealt with under those rules, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

the justice may issue a summons requiring the offender to appear at the place and time specified in the summons *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] [^key-6ce97f4a2b94e7c67c5fcdec0648d865]... or, if the information is in writing and on oath, may issue a warrant for the offender’s arrest *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] [^key-dcd057e62de8ff563c2d672272d3f0f1]....

(2)[^key-c9256da7cf197c377411b5754451baee] Any summons or warrant issued under this paragraph shall direct the offender to appear or be brought— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) before a magistrates' court acting for the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] local justice area[^key-59b1e76dcb86438dfc93e2ac9f9dfc9c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] in which the offender resides; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) if it is not known where the offender resides, before a magistrates' court acting for the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] local justice area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] in which is situated the attendance centre which the offender is required to attend by the order or by virtue of an order under paragraph 5(1)(b) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

**2.** (1) If it is proved to the satisfaction of the magistrates’ court before which an offender appears or is brought under paragraph 1 above that he has failed without reasonable excuse to attend as mentioned in sub-paragraph (1)(a) of that paragraph or has committed such a breach of rules as is mentioned in sub-paragraph (1)(b) of that paragraph, that court may deal with him in any one of the following ways— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) it may impose on him a fine not exceeding £1,000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) where the attendance centre order was made by a magistrates’ court, it may deal with him, for the offence in respect of which the order was made, in any way in which he could have been dealt with for that offence by the court which made the order if the order had not been made; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (c) where the order was made by the Crown Court, it may commit him to custody or release him on bail until he can be brought or appear before the Crown Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(2) Any exercise by the court of its power under sub-paragraph (1)(a) above shall be without prejudice to the continuation of the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(3) A fine imposed under sub-paragraph (1)(a) above shall be deemed, for the purposes of any enactment, to be a sum adjudged to be paid by a conviction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(3A)[^key-683c93c801310511e6b2ae9beaf65d33] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) the offender is aged under 18, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) but for this sub-paragraph, the court would impose a fine on the offender under sub-paragraph (1)(a) above, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

section 380 of the Sentencing Code (order for payment by parent or guardian) applies to the fine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(4) Where a magistrates’ court deals with an offender under sub-paragraph (1)(b) above, it shall revoke the attendance centre order if it is still in force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(5) In dealing with an offender under sub-paragraph (1)(b) above, a magistrates’ court— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) shall take into account the extent to which the offender has complied with the requirements of the attendance centre order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) in the case of an offender who has wilfully and persistently failed to comply with those requirements, may impose a custodial sentence notwithstanding anything in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] section 230(2) of the Sentencing Code[^key-b518366336f16b1db578e3e71caa8452] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3].

(5A)[^key-109bbd9e53c448540d99a34e12d037cc] Where a magistrates' court dealing with an offender under sub-paragraph (1)(a) above would not otherwise have the power to amend the order under paragraph 5(1)(b) below (substitution of different attendance centre), that paragraph has effect as if references to an appropriate magistrates' court were references to the court dealing with the offender. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(6) A person sentenced under sub-paragraph (1)(b) above for an offence may appeal to the Crown Court against the sentence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(7) A magistrates’ court which deals with an offender’s case under sub-paragraph (1)(c) above shall send to the Crown Court— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) a certificate signed by a justice of the peace giving particulars of the offender’s failure to attend or, as the case may be, the breach of the rules which he has committed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) such other particulars of the case as may be desirable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

and a certificate purporting to be so signed shall be admissible as evidence of the failure or the breach before the Crown Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

**3.** (1) Where by virtue of paragraph 2(1)(c) above the offender is brought or appears before the Crown Court and it is proved to the satisfaction of the court— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) that he has failed without reasonable excuse to attend as mentioned in paragraph 1(1)(a) above, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) that he has committed such a breach of rules as is mentioned in paragraph 1(1)(b) above, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

that court may deal with him, for the offence in respect of which the order was made, in any way in which it could have dealt with him for that offence if it had not made the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(2) Where the Crown Court deals with an offender under sub-paragraph (1) above, it shall revoke the attendance centre order if it is still in force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(3) In dealing with an offender under sub-paragraph (1) above, the Crown Court— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) shall take into account the extent to which the offender has complied with the requirements of the attendance centre order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) in the case of an offender who has wilfully and persistently failed to comply with those requirements, may impose a custodial sentence notwithstanding anything in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] section 230(2) of the Sentencing Code[^key-6be663ddb2c10838b084731aa8ca3dcc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3].

(4) In proceedings before the Crown Court under this paragraph any question whether there has been a failure to attend or a breach of the rules shall be determined by the court and not by the verdict of a jury. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

### Revocation of order with or without re-sentencing (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

**4.** (1) Where an attendance centre order is in force in respect of an offender, an appropriate court may, on an application made by the offender or by the officer in charge of the relevant attendance centre, revoke the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(2) In sub-paragraph (1) above “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]**an appropriate court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) where the court which made the order was the Crown Court and there is included in the order a direction that the power to revoke the order is reserved to that court, the Crown Court; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) in any other case, either of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
  - (i) a magistrates’ court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] acting in the local justice area[^key-79de199203538336b13ef986a67fea3d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] in which the relevant attendance centre is situated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] the court which made the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(3) Any power conferred by this paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) on a magistrates’ court to revoke an attendance centre order made by such a court, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) on the Crown Court to revoke an attendance centre order made by the Crown Court, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

includes power to deal with the offender, for the offence in respect of which the order was made, in any way in which he could have been dealt with for that offence by the court which made the order if the order had not been made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(4) A person sentenced by a magistrates’ court under sub-paragraph (3) above for an offence may appeal to the Crown Court against the sentence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(5) The proper officer of a court which makes an order under this paragraph revoking an attendance centre order shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) deliver a copy of the revoking order to the offender or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] serve a copy on the offender in accordance with Criminal Procedure Rules[^key-4b3ed030ab9e1850b6a693e46bab6f74] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) deliver or send a copy to the officer in charge of the relevant attendance centre. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(6) In this paragraph “the relevant attendance centre”, in relation to an attendance centre order, means the attendance centre specified in the order or substituted for the attendance centre so specified by an order made by virtue of paragraph 5(1)(b) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(7) In this paragraph “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]**proper officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) in relation to a magistrates’ court, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] designated officer[^key-e6c511094101ddad0d42d58a57626b3e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] for the court; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) in relation to the Crown Court, the appropriate officer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

### Amendment of order (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

**5.** (1) Where an attendance centre order is in force in respect of an offender, an appropriate magistrates’ court may, on an application made by the offender or by the officer in charge of the relevant attendance centre, by order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) vary the day or hour specified in the order for the offender’s first attendance at the relevant attendance centre; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) substitute for the relevant attendance centre an attendance centre which the court is satisfied is reasonably accessible to the offender, having regard to his age, the means of access available to him and any other circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(2) In sub-paragraph (1) above “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]**an appropriate magistrates’ court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) a magistrates’ court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] acting in the local justice area[^key-362aef83641164d01f7ecfe418ccdd1d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] in which the relevant attendance centre is situated; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(3) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] designated officer[^key-fbf4850498485d60d3f0584abc386f94] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3] for a court which makes an order under this paragraph shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

### Orders made on appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

- (a) if it was made on an appeal brought from a magistrates’ court, to have been made by that magistrates’ court; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
- (b) if it was made on an appeal brought from the Crown Court or from the criminal division of the Court of Appeal, to have been made by the Crown Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]

(2) In relation to an attendance centre order made on appeal, paragraphs 2(1)(b) and 4(3) above shall each have effect as if the words “if the order had not been made” were omitted and paragraph 3(1) above shall have effect as if the words “if it had not made the order” were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a9d1b1592e07a85e34e6355c1c1137a3]
