## 61. Breach, revocation and amendment of attendance centre orders.[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 5 to this Act (which makes provision for dealing with failures to comply with attendance centre orders, for revoking such orders with or without the substitution of other sentences and for amending such orders) shall have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]
