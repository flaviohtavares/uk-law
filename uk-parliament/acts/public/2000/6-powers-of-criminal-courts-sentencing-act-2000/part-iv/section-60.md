## 60. Attendance centre orders.[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

- (a)[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a court would have power, but for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b] section 227 of the Sentencing Code (restriction on imposing imprisonment on persons under[^c-8757ef47c126202ddb1a3a93f015af63] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b] 18[^c-d99b8b29f6eb0ea92918e89123b0fbb3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]), to commit a person aged under 21 to prison in default of payment of any sum of money or for failing to do or abstain from doing anything required to be done or left undone, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b] [^c-8f35318878045e6df9a98db33ef7d9f6]...
- (c)[^c-8f35318878045e6df9a98db33ef7d9f6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

the court may, if it has been notified by the Secretary of State that an attendance centre is available for the reception of persons of his description, order him to attend at such a centre, to be specified in the order, for such number of hours as may be so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

(2) An order under subsection (1) above is in this Act referred to as an “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]**attendance centre order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]**”.

(3) The aggregate number of hours for which an attendance centre order may require a person to attend at an attendance centre shall not be less than 12 except where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

- (a) he is aged under 14; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]
- (b) the court is of the opinion that 12 hours would be excessive, having regard to his age or any other circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

(4) The aggregate number of hours shall not exceed 12 except where the court is of the opinion, having regard to all the circumstances, that 12 hours would be inadequate, and in that case— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

- (a) shall not exceed 24 where the person is aged under 16; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]
- (b) shall not exceed 36 where the person is aged *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b] 16 or 17[^c-15430c510192387fc883a26466f1cb40] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b].

(5) A court may make an attendance centre order in respect of a person before a previous attendance centre order made in respect of him has ceased to have effect, and may determine the number of hours to be specified in the order without regard— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

- (a) to the number specified in the previous order; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]
- (b) to the fact that that order is still in effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

(6) An attendance centre order shall not be made unless the court is satisfied that the attendance centre to be specified in it is reasonably accessible to the person concerned, having regard to his age, the means of access available to him and any other circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

(7) The times at which a person is required to attend at an attendance centre shall, as far as practicable, be such as to avoid— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

- (a) any conflict with his religious beliefs or with the requirements of any other *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b] youth community order[^c-fcef2c4e25d87bbfb60e50f28f5e6fd6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b] to which he may be subject; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]
- (b) any interference with the times, if any, at which he normally works or attends school or any other educational establishment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

(8) The first time at which the person is required to attend at an attendance centre shall be a time at which the centre is available for his attendance in accordance with the notification of the Secretary of State, and shall be specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

(9) The subsequent times shall be fixed by the officer in charge of the centre, having regard to the person’s circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

(10) A person shall not be required under this section to attend at an attendance centre on more than one occasion on any day, or for more than three hours on any occasion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

(11) Where a court makes an attendance centre order, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b] designated officer[^c-db32859c0978f06cb4523c9063490c95][^c12954881] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b] for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b] the court shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

- (a) deliver or send a copy of the order to the officer in charge of the attendance centre specified in it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]
- (b) deliver a copy of the order to the person in respect of whom it is made or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b] serve a copy on that person in accordance with Criminal Procedure Rules[^c-7a61bc6f41f8587071e09ae19d447251] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b].

(12) Where a person (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]**the defaulter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]**”) has been ordered to attend at an attendance centre in default of the payment of any sum of money— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]

- (a) on payment of the whole sum to any person authorised to receive it, the attendance centre order shall cease to have effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]
- (b) on payment of a part of the sum to any such person, the total number of hours for which the defaulter is required to attend at the centre shall be reduced proportionately, that is to say by such number of complete hours as bears to the total number the proportion most nearly approximating to, without exceeding, the proportion which the part bears to the whole sum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cff423f11f2794936b2a61204756106b]
