# Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Reference by relevant advisory body.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subsections (2) and (3) apply where—

- (a) the relevant advisory body has given advice under section 26(4) or on being consulted under section 27(6), but
- (b) in any respect, the relevant authority decide not to act in accordance with that advice.

(2) The relevant advisory body may refer the decision—

- (a) in the case of land in England, to the Secretary of State[^c1057029], or
- (b) in the case of land in Wales, to the National Assembly for Wales.

(3) On a reference under this section the Secretary of State[^c1057030] or the National Assembly for Wales may, if he or it thinks fit—

- (a) cancel any direction given by the relevant authority, or
- (b) require the relevant authority to give such direction under section 26 as the Secretary of State or, as the case may be, the Assembly, think fit.

(4) Sections 7 and 8 (and Schedule 3) have effect in relation to a reference under this section as they have effect in relation to an appeal under section 6 [^c1057032]. . ..

(5) In this section—

- [^c1057033]. . .
- “**the relevant advisory body**” has the same meaning as in section 26, except that it does not include the National Assembly for Wales.
