# Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Principal definitions for Part I.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part “**access land**” means any land which—

- (a) is shown as open country on a map in conclusive form issued by the appropriate countryside body for the purposes of this Part,
- (b) is shown on such a map as registered common land,
- (c) is registered common land in any area outside Inner London for which no such map relating to registered common land has been issued,
- (d) is situated more than 600 metres above sea level in any area for which no such map relating to open country has been issued, [^c-2180ccc9a57267a2d28c77d2caca1733]...
- (da)[^c-af3c4f8eeb8c623b77cbeae85df8244e] is coastal margin, or
- (e) is dedicated for the purposes of this Part under section 16,

but does not (in any of those cases) include excepted land or land which is treated by section 15(1) as being accessible to the public apart from this Act.

(2)[^c-a3455ce0c601cfd9d1ca1a6f4bb517f0] In this Part—

- “**access authority**”—
  - (a) in relation to land in a National Park, means the National Park authority, and
  - (b) in relation to any other land, means the local highway authority in whose area the land is situated;
- “**the appropriate countryside body**” means—
  - (a) in relation to England, Natural England[^c-f9dc7432502318038a1e4543fcbf000d] , and
  - (b) in relation to Wales, the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c-0f9c790292b2a02c09bf301375442ee3] ;
- “[^c-93f4c22d83bf9f2dbeec631a0f1230b2]**coastal margin**” means land which is of a description specified by an order under section 3A;
- “**excepted land**” means land which is for the time being of any of the descriptions specified in Part I of Schedule 1, those descriptions having effect subject to Part II of that Schedule;
- “**mountain**” includes, subject to the following definition, any land situated more than 600 metres above sea level;
- “**mountain, moor, heath or down**” does not include land which appears to the appropriate countryside body to consist of improved or semi-improved grassland;
- “**open country**” means land which—
  - (a) appears to the appropriate countryside body to consist wholly or predominantly of mountain, moor, heath or down, and
  - (b) is not registered common land or coastal margin[^c-94b4798476336ba18f3c6ca725cf409f] or land dedicated as coastal margin for the purposes of this Part under section 16 *(Extent: E.)*[^c-9b4c8a45a3906b7dcb1550e6290dd2e3].

(3)[^c-dfc77765aae1d94d54f117c562cefcb1] In this Part “**registered common land**” means—

- (a) land which is registered as common land under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8cd51986cd4aea74e5ec206d4aa2f66f] [^c1057009]Commons Registration Act 1965 (in this section referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8cd51986cd4aea74e5ec206d4aa2f66f]**the 1965 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8cd51986cd4aea74e5ec206d4aa2f66f]**”) (but is not coastal margin)[^c-53f23fd77dc914bfdb3e681c2e44d83c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8cd51986cd4aea74e5ec206d4aa2f66f] or land dedicated as coastal margin for the purposes of this Part under section 16 *(Extent: E)*[^c-e11da1539b959c4cfdb6fb1116d449e3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8cd51986cd4aea74e5ec206d4aa2f66f] and whose registration under that Act has become final, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8cd51986cd4aea74e5ec206d4aa2f66f] land which is registered as common land in a register of common land kept under Part 1 of the Commons Act 2006.
- (b) subject to subsection (4), land which fell within paragraph (a) on the day on which this Act is passed or at any time after that day but has subsequently ceased to be registered as common land under the 1965 Act on the register of common land in which it was included being amended by reason of the land having ceased to be common land within the meaning of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-33c49550fa87cfb73825993b5217d4b7]

(4) Subsection (3)(b) does not apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61444ff754d0774a65d89b845a6e855d]

- (a) the amendment of the register of common land was made in pursuance of an application made before the day on which this Act is passed, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61444ff754d0774a65d89b845a6e855d]
- (b) the land ceased to be common land by reason of the exercise of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61444ff754d0774a65d89b845a6e855d]
  - (i) any power of compulsory purchase, of appropriation or of sale which is conferred by an enactment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61444ff754d0774a65d89b845a6e855d]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-61444ff754d0774a65d89b845a6e855d] any power so conferred under which land may be made common land within the meaning of the 1965 Act in substitution for other land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61444ff754d0774a65d89b845a6e855d]
