# Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Power to extend to coastal land : Wales .[^c-7b19a5beb6f3a251fdfd9b1369d04260]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers[^c-f9f11ef1475aa5b6b3456310fcf57e91] may by order amend the definition of “open country” in section 1(2) so as to include as respects Wales[^c-af0e64ce1809af4c347160c24a152144] a reference to coastal land or to coastal land of any description.

(2) An order under this section may—

- (a) make consequential amendments of other provisions of this Part, and
- (b) modify the provisions of this Part in their application to land which is open country merely because it is coastal land.

(3) In this section “**coastal land**” means—

- (a) the foreshore, and
- (b) land adjacent to the foreshore (including in particular any cliff, bank, barrier, dune, beach or flat which is adjacent to the foreshore).
