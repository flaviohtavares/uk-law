# Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Land management.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The relevant authority may by direction, on an application made by a person interested in any land, exclude or restrict access to that land by virtue of section 2(1) during a specified period, if the authority are satisfied that the exclusion or restriction under this section of access by virtue of section 2(1) to the extent provided by the direction is necessary for the purposes of the management of the land by the applicant. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3197165fd694e2d4f49836b5c71ab2db]

The relevant authority may make a land management direction in relation to any land (“the designated land”). *(Extent: E)*

(1A (Extent: E)) The relevant authority may exercise their powers under subsection (1)— *(Extent: E)*

- (a (Extent: E)) in any case, on the application of any person interested in the designated land, and *(Extent: E)*
- (b (Extent: E)) if the designated land is coastal margin, without any such application having been made. *(Extent: E)*

(1B (Extent: E)) A land management direction is a direction which— *(Extent: E)*

- (a (Extent: E)) where the designated land is coastal margin— *(Extent: E)*
  - (i (Extent: E)) excludes or restricts access to the designated land during a specified period, or *(Extent: E)*
  - (ii (Extent: E)) authorises a specified person to exclude or restrict in the manner specified in the direction access to the designated land, or to such part or parts of that land as the specified person may determine in accordance with the direction, during a specified period, and *(Extent: E)*
- (b (Extent: E)) in any other case, excludes or restricts access to the designated land during a specified period. *(Extent: E)*

(1C (Extent: E)) The relevant authority may not make a land management direction unless— *(Extent: E)*

- (a (Extent: E)) in the case of a direction within subsection (1B)(a), they are satisfied that the exclusion or restriction under this section of access to the designated land to the extent provided for in or by the direction is necessary for the purposes of the management of the designated land or any adjoining land; *(Extent: E)*
- (b (Extent: E)) in the case of a direction within subsection (1B)(b), they are satisfied that the exclusion or restriction under this section of access to the designated land to the extent provided by the direction is necessary for the purposes of the management of the land by the applicant. *(Extent: E)*

(1D (Extent: E)) Before making a land management direction under subsection (1B)(a) in a case where an application has not been made under subsection (1A)(a), the relevant authority must take reasonable steps to consult any person who— *(Extent: E)*

- (a (Extent: E)) holds an estate in fee simple absolute in possession in the designated land (or any part of it), *(Extent: E)*
- (b (Extent: E)) holds a term of years absolute in that land (or any part of it), or *(Extent: E)*
- (c (Extent: E)) is in lawful occupation of that land (or any part of it). *(Extent: E)*

(1E (Extent: E)) Subsection (1D) does not apply if the direction is made in accordance with section 55F(2) of the National Parks and Access to the Countryside *(Extent: E)* Act 1949 (directions set out in report approving proposals for English coastal route). *(Extent: E)*

(1F (Extent: E)) The reference in subsection (1B)(a) to a specified period includes a reference to— *(Extent: E)*

- (a (Extent: E)) a specified period in every calendar year; *(Extent: E)*
- (b (Extent: E)) a period which is to be determined by the relevant authority in accordance with the direction; *(Extent: E)*
- (c (Extent: E)) a period which is to be determined by a specified person in accordance with the direction and, where the direction so requires, notified to the relevant authority by the specified person in accordance with the direction; *(Extent: E)*
- (d (Extent: E)) an indefinite period. *(Extent: E)*

(2) The reference in subsection (1) *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-da3a3b7e34ef56ae9918f179a5880455] subsection (1B)(b) *(Extent: E)* to a specified period includes a reference to—

- (a) a specified period in every calendar year, or
- (b) a period which is to be—
  - (i) determined by the applicant in accordance with the direction, and
  - (ii) notified by him to the relevant authority in accordance with regulations under section 32(1)(d).

(3) In determining whether to any extent the exclusion or restriction under this section of access by virtue of section 2(1) during any period is necessary for the purposes of land management, the relevant authority shall have regard to—

- (a) the existence of the right conferred by section 22,
- (b) the extent to which the applicant has exercised or proposes to exercise that right, and
- (c) the purposes for which he has exercised or proposes to exercise it.

(4) Where an application under this section relates to land which is not access land at the time when the application is made, the relevant authority shall not give a direction under this section unless they are satisfied that it is likely that the land will be access land during all or part of the period to which the application relates.

*Extent: E*

(5)[^c-a8d07eb92bac01ca108eb2ee086174cf] Subsections (3) and (4) do not apply in relation to land management directions under subsection (1B)(a).

(6 (Extent: E)) In this section references to access to land are to access by virtue of section 2(1). *(Extent: E)*
