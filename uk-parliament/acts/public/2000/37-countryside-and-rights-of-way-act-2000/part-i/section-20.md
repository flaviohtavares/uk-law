# Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Codes of conduct and other information.[^c-62b198205928715b9201b88a6f012298][^c-e771778bfb8a4bb42e1dc877ef3dc84f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In relation to England, it shall be the duty of Natural England[^c-0b8008ba52a461bcfe33b15ba6cea98e] to issue, and from time to time revise, a code of conduct for the guidance of persons exercising the right conferred by section 2(1) and of persons interested in access land, and to take such other steps as appear to them expedient for securing—

- (a) that the public are informed of the situation and extent of, and means of access to, access land, [^c-745d133fe7532e636d922dc18e8b89ba]...
- (b) that the public and persons interested in access land are informed of their respective rights and obligations—
  - (i) under this Part, and
  - (ii) with regard to public rights of way on, and nature conservation in relation to, access land, and[^c-c64452213e686cb8a7dec59ddee2bcfb]
- (c) that, in relation to access land which is coastal margin, the public are informed that the right conferred by section 2(1) does not affect any other right of access that may exist in relation to that land.

(1A)[^c-ed26c5405b2c19b4cb231a61f70c48ab] The duty imposed by subsection (1) to issue and revise a code of conduct may be discharged, in relation to access land which is coastal margin, by (or in part by) issuing and revising a separate code relating to such access land only.

(2) In relation to Wales, it shall be the duty of the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c-b27d90bf8a168c3c420fea7f6cce9f00] to issue, and from time to time revise, a code of conduct for the guidance of persons exercising the right conferred by section 2(1) and of persons interested in access land, and to take such other steps as appear to them expedient for securing the results mentioned in paragraphs (a) and (b) of subsection (1).

(3) A code of conduct issued by Natural England[^c-8b68838eddc5dbdbf87d98ae7dfe3072] or the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c-c446950405b8d82242dca51621d60ad5] may include provisions in pursuance of subsection (1) or (2) and in pursuance of section 86(1) of the National Parks and Access to the [^c1057024]Countryside Act 1949.

(4) The powers conferred by subsections (1) and (2) include power to contribute towards expenses incurred by other persons.
