## 79. Payments under agreements under s.16 of 1949 Act or s.15 of 1968 Act.

*Extent: E+W*

In section 50 of the 1981 Act (which makes provision relating to payments under section 16 of the National Parks and Access to the [^c1057079]Countryside Act 1949 or section 15 of the [^c1057080]Countryside Act 1968), in subsection (1)(a), for sub-paragraphs (i) and (ii) and the preceding word “to” there is substituted “ to any person; or ”.
