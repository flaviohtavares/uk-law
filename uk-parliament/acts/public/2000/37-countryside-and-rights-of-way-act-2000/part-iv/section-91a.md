# Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000, section 91A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/37

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91A. Regulations under Part 4[^c-b3805f2d032ea996239a0153128a5c78]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A power to make regulations under this Part—

- (a) is exercisable by statutory instrument;
- (b) includes power to make different provision for different purposes or different areas;
- (c) includes power to make consequential, incidental, supplementary, transitional, transitory or saving provision.

(2) Regulations under this Part are to be made by statutory instrument.

(3) A statutory instrument containing regulations under this Part is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
