## 91. Grants to conservation boards.[^c1057098]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State (as respects England) or the National Assembly for Wales (as respects Wales) may make grants to a conservation board, of such amounts and on such terms and conditions as the Secretary of State or the Assembly thinks fit.

(2)[^c-4fb97fa6fa34c01d9749c8f2c18874ba] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Before determining the amount of any grant which it proposes to make to a conservation board under this section, or the purpose for which the grant is to be made, the National Assembly for Wales shall consult the NRBW[^c-758b15c83a2d6b042a489230d88a8bfc].
