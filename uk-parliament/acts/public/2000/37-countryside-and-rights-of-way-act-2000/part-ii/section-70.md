# Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Minor amendments.[^c1057070]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-dc5f82021dcb89e2c64e811f635686bb][^key-95b583f4559371e12836cdcde3e777e1] In section 66(3) of the 1980 Act (works for safeguarding persons using footpaths)—

- (a) after “footpath” there is inserted “ or bridleway ”, and
- (b) after “barriers,” there is inserted “ posts, ”.

(2) In section 134 of that Act, subsection (5) (which limits the persons who may bring proceedings for failure to restore a public path disturbed by ploughing etc.) is omitted.

(3)[^key-5c2c7d09fd52ae7c81172b77f5ff1903][^key-95b583f4559371e12836cdcde3e777e1] In section 300 of that Act (right of local authorities to use vehicles and appliances on footways and bridleways), in subsection (1) after “verges,” there is inserted “ for preventing or removing obstructions to them or otherwise preventing or abating nuisances or other interferences with them, ”.

(4) In section 21(2)(b) of the [^c1057071]Road Traffic Act 1988 (defence to charge of driving or parking on cycle track for highway authority vehicles), after “verges” there is inserted “ , or the preventing or removing of obstructions to the cycle track or the preventing or abating in any other way of nuisances or other interferences with the cycle track, ”.
