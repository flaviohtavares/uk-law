# Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Vehicular access across common land etc.[^c1057068]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb] This section applies to a way which the owner or occupier (from time to time) of any premises has used as a means of access for vehicles to the premises, if that use of the way— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]

- (a) was an offence under an enactment applying to the land crossed by the way, but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
- (b) would otherwise have been sufficient to create on or after the prescribed date, and to keep in existence, an easement giving a right of way for vehicles. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]

(2) Regulations may provide, as respects a way to which this section applies, for the creation in accordance with the regulations, on the application of the owner of the premises concerned and on compliance by him with prescribed requirements, of an easement subsisting at law for the benefit of the premises and giving a right of way for vehicles over that way. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]

(3) An easement created in accordance with the regulations is subject to any enactment or rule of law which would apply to such an easement granted by the owner of the land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]

(4) The regulations may in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]

- (a) require that, where an application is made after the relevant use of the way has ceased, it is to be made within a specified time, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
- (b) specify grounds on which objections may be made and the procedure to apply to the making of objections, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
- (c) require any matter to be referred to and determined by the Lands Tribunal, and make provision as to procedure and costs, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
- (d) make provision as to the payment of any amount by the owner of the premises concerned to any person or into court and as to the time when any payment is to be made, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
- (e) provide for the determination of any such amount, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
- (f) make provision as to the date on which any easement is created, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
- (g) specify any limitation to which the easement is subject, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
- (h) provide for the easement to include any specified right incidental to the right of way, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
- (i) make different provision for different circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]

(5) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]

- “**enactment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]**” includes an enactment in a local or private Act and a byelaw, regulation or other provision having effect under an enactment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
- “**owner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]**”, in relation to any premises, means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
  - (a) a person, other than a mortgagee not in possession, who is for the time being entitled to dispose of the fee simple of the premises, whether in possession or in reversion, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
  - (b) a tenant under a long lease, within the meaning of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb] [^M_M_cc198905-ff49-45cc-d0f0-dc2db2ad89f8]Landlord and Tenant Act 1987; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
- “**prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]**” means prescribed by regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
- “**regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]**” means regulations made, as respects England, by the Secretary of State and, as respects Wales, by the National Assembly for Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]

(6) Regulations under this section shall be made by statutory instrument, and no such regulations shall be made by the Secretary of State unless a draft has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-39e51f7ea5422131addb5e3cd475a3fb]
