# Representation of the People Act 2000, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2024-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Further amendments about registration.[^c9549381]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedules 1, 2 and 3, which make consequential and connected amendments of—

- (a) the 1983 Act,
- (b) sections 1 to 3 of the Representation of the [^c9549391]People Act 1985 (overseas electors), and
- (c) the [^c9549401]Elected Authorities (Northern Ireland) Act 1989,

respectively, shall have effect.
