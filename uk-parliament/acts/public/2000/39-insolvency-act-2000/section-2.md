# Insolvency Act 2000, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-08-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2. Company voluntary arrangements.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 2 (which—

- (a) amends the provisions about company voluntary arrangements under Part I of the [^c1029506]Insolvency Act 1986, and
- (b) in consequence of Schedule 1 and those amendments, makes amendments of the [^c1029507]Building Societies Act 1986),

is to have effect.
