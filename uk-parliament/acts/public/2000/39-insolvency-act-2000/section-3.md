# Insolvency Act 2000, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-08-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Individual voluntary arrangements.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 3 (which enables the procedure for the approval of individual voluntary arrangements under Part VIII of the [^c1029508]Insolvency Act 1986 to be started without an initial moratorium for the insolvent debtor and makes other amendments of the provisions about individual voluntary arrangements) is to have effect.
