# Finance Act 2000, section 140

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 140. Rate.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 42 of the [^c14055241]Finance Act 1996 (amount of landfill tax), in subsections (1)(a) and (2) for “£10" substitute “ £11 ”.

(2) This section has effect in relation to taxable disposals made, or treated as made, on or after 1st April 2000.
