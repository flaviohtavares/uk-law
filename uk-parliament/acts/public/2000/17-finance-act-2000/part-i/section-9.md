# Finance Act 2000, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Use of rebated heavy oil as fuel.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 1 to the [^c14052791]Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979 (which sets out the categories of excepted vehicle which may use rebated heavy oil as fuel) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit the following provisions—

- (a) paragraph 2(1)(b) (which provides that off-road tractors are excepted vehicles) and the word “or" immediately preceding it, and
- (b) paragraph 2(4) (which defines off-road tractors).

(3) This section shall have effect in relation to the use of rebated heavy oil as fuel on or after 1st May 2000.
