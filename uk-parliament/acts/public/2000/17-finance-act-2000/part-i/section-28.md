## 28. Civil penalties for breach of excise duty requirements.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 9(2)(a) of the [^c14053051]Finance Act 1994 (how to calculate the penalty in cases where provision is made by any enactment for conduct to attract a penalty calculated by reference to an amount of excise duty), for “or any other enactment" substitute “ , or by or under any other enactment, ”.
