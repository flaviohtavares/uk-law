## 29. Correction of reference.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 127 of the [^c14053061]Finance Act 1999 (interest on repayments of customs duty), in subsection (1)(b) for “Council Regulation 2454/93" substitute “ Commission Regulation 2454/93 ”.

This amendment shall be deemed always to have had effect.
