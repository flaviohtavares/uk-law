## 6. Mixing of rebated light oils.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 2A to the [^c14052761]Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979 (mixing of rebated oils) is amended in accordance with Schedule 1 to this Act.

(2) The amendments in that Schedule come into force on the day appointed under section 5(6).
