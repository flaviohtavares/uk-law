# Finance Act 2000, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Rates of gaming duty.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the table in section 11(2) of the [^c14052921]Finance Act 1997 (rates of gaming duty) substitute—

> TABLE
>
> | *Part of gross gaming yield* | *Rate* |
> | --- | --- |
> | The first £470,500 | 2½ per cent. |
> | The next £1,045,500 | 12½ per cent. |
> | The next £1,045,500 | 20 per cent. |
> | The next £1,830,000 | 30 per cent. |
> | The remainder | 40 per cent. |

(2) This section has effect in relation to accounting periods beginning on or after 1st April 2000.
