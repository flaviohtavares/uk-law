# Finance Act 2000, section 155

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/17

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 155. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act “**the Taxes Act 1988**” means the [^c14055401]Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 and “[^key-808f1bd7a64642203e64e333a196dce5]**ITTOIA 2005**” means the Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005 and “[^key-03aa790e73f898fa2bbeb0e9e4b19558]**ITA 2007**” means the Income Tax Act 2007.
