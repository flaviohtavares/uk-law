# Finance Act 2000, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Chargeable gains: non-resident companies and groups etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 29 to this Act has effect.

In that Schedule—

Part I makes provision with respect to the application of the [^c14053961]Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 to companies not resident in the United Kingdom and groups of companies etc,

Part II contains minor and consequential amendments, and

Part III contains transitional provisions.
