# Finance Act 2000, section 54

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Employee share ownership trusts.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No claim for relief under section 229(1) or (3) of the [^c14053271]Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 (roll-over relief where disposal made to employee share ownership trust) may be made in relation to a disposal of shares, or an interest in shares, made on or after 6th April 2001.
