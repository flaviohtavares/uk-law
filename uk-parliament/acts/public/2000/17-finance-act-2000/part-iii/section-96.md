# Finance Act 2000, section 96

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Payments by trustees to non-resident companies.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 96(5) of the [^c14053931]Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 (capital payments by trustees to non-resident company), in the opening words (which refer to the persons by whom the company is controlled), omit “and each of them is then resident or ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom".

(2) This section applies to payments received on or after 21st March 2000.
