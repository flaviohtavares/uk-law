# Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000, section 158

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (131).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 158. Minor and consequential amendments and repeals.[^c10575601]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The minor and consequential amendments specified in Schedule 21 shall have effect.

(2) The enactments specified in Schedule 22 are repealed to the extent specified.

(3) However, none of the repeals—

- (a) of provisions of Part II (other than[^c20066851] those relating to sections 72, 73, 79, 81, 82, 101 to 105, and 108 and Schedule 3[^c20102571]) or III of the Representation of the [^c10575611]People Act 1983 (election campaigns and legal proceedings in respect of elections), or
- (b) of provisions amending any of those provisions,

have effect in relation to local government elections in Scotland.
