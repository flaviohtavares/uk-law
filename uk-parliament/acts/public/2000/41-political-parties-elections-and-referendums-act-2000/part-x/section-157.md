# Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000, section 157

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (131).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 157. Documents for purposes of the Act.[^c10575591]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any application, notice or notification required or authorised to be made or given under this Act must be in writing.

(2) Any document required or authorised to be given or sent under this Act may be sent by post.
