# Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000, section 155

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (131).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 155. Power to vary specified sums or percentages .[^c10575581][^key-054025440ccfec3bf097961abf358377]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Except where subsection (1A) applies,[^key-950a8cd1d4a16ff8b9a4643eb9b4bd2f] the Secretary of State may by order vary any sum for the time being specified in any provision of this Act (other than the sum specified in section 12(8) or 36(5)).

(1A)[^key-c40c0d8e17721870d816c5c6b5c0eb1c] The Scottish Ministers may by order vary any sum for the time being specified in Part 5 or 6 so far as that sum applies in relation to an election the conduct of which is within the legislative competence of the Scottish Parliament.

(2) An order under subsection (1) or (1A) may be made either—[^key-7a9a8737d6ba56b3968ced18d816ff1f]

- (a) where the person making the order[^key-303a463aed3077b2a2572d768ea090c7] considers it expedient to do so in consequence of changes in the value of money, or
- (b) where the order gives effect to a recommendation of the Commission.

(3)[^c20225451] Subsection (4) applies in relation to the sums specified in—

- (a) Part 4;
- (b) Part 4A;
- (c) Schedule 11;
- (ca)[^key-6a38cafd9adfe1c1a3133c917fe188d8] section 95B(6);
- (cb) Schedule 11A;
- (d) Schedule 15;
- (e) Schedule 19A.

(4) In each Parliament, other than a Parliament that is dissolved less than two years after the date of its first sitting, the Secretary of State must either—

- (a) make an order in pursuance of subsection (2)(a), or
- (b) lay before Parliament a statement setting out the Secretary of State's reasons for not doing so.

(4A)[^key-021b45ba2c79b93555a085ce1da3af9d] Subsection (4B) applies in relation to the sums specified in Schedule 11.

(4B) In each session of the Scottish Parliament, other than a session that is dissolved less than two years after the date of its first sitting, the Scottish Ministers must either—

- (a) make an order in pursuance of subsection (2)(a), or
- (b) lay before the Scottish Parliament a statement setting out the Scottish Ministers' reasons for not doing so.

(5)[^key-94d2c3184a28ecdd6af380421fe361ba] The Secretary of State may by order vary any percentage for the time being specified in—

- (a) section 94(5ZA), 94D(4) or 96(2)(aa), or
- (b) paragraph 3(2) or (2A) of Schedule 10.

(6) The Secretary of State may make an order under subsection (5) only if it gives effect to a recommendation of the Commission.
