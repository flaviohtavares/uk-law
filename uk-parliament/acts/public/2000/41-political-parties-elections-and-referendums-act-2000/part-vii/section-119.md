> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (131).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/41

## 119. Control of donations to permitted participants.[^c10574941][^c20061201]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 15 has effect for controlling donations to permitted participants that either are not registered parties or are minor parties.
