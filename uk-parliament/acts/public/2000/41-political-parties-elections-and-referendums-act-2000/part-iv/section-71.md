# Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000, section 71

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (131).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/41

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Control of donations to individuals and members associations.[^c10574381]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 7, which makes provision for controlling donations to individual members of registered parties, associations of such members, and certain elected office holders, shall have effect.
