# Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000, section 54A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 54A). (not yet in force: Declaration as to source of donation)[^c20216261]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Where a person (P) causes an amount exceeding £7,500 to be received by a registered party by way of a donation, a written declaration must be given to the party—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: by P, if P is an individual, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: if not, by an individual authorised by P to make the declaration,)*

*(not yet in force: stating, to the best of the individual's knowledge and belief, whether or not subsection (2) applies to the donation.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: This subsection applies to the donation if—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a person other than P has provided, or is expected to provide, money or any other benefit to P with a view to, or otherwise in connection with, the making of the donation, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the money, or the value of the benefit, is more than £7,500.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Where a declaration under this section contains a statement to the effect that subsection (2) applies to the donation, it must also—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: state whether or not, in the opinion of the person making the declaration—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: subsection (4) of section 54 applies to the donation;)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: subsection (6) of that section applies to it;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: if the person's opinion is that neither of those subsections applies to the donation, give the person's reasons for that opinion.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: A declaration under this section must also state the full name and address of the person by whom it is made and, where subsection (1)(b) applies—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: state that the person is authorised by P to make the declaration;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: describe the person's role or position in relation to P.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: A person who knowingly or recklessly makes a false declaration under this section commits an offence.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: Regulations made by the Secretary of State may make provision as to how the value of a benefit is to be calculated for the purposes of subsection (2).)*
