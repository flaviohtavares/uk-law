# Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000, section 54B

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 54B). (not yet in force: Declaration as to whether residence) (not yet in force: etc) (not yet in force: condition satisfied)[^c20216281]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: An individual making to a registered party a donation in relation to which the condition set out in section 54(2ZA) applies must give to the party a written declaration stating whether or not the individual satisfies that condition.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: A declaration under this section must also state the individual's full name and address.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A person who knowingly or recklessly makes a false declaration under this section commits an offence.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision requiring a declaration under this section to be retained for a specified period.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The requirement in subsection (1) does not apply where, by reason of section 71B(1)(a), the individual by whom the donation would be made is a permissible donor in relation to the donation at the time of its receipt by the party.)*
