## 8. Powers with respect to elections exercisable only on Commission recommendation.[^c10573151]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The function of giving directions under section 52(1) of the Representation of the [^c10573161]People Act 1983 (directions as to discharge of registration duties) shall be exercisable only on, and in accordance with, a recommendation of the Commission.

(2)[^c-67ecd3d48ac87eb992e3090b172de2ff] A function to which this subsection applies shall, unless the person on whom the function is conferred[^c-d42d53e338ec9965898722ac1ab33e32] considers that the exercise of the function is expedient in consequence of changes in the value of money, be exercisable only on, and in accordance with, a recommendation of the Commission.

(3) Subsection (2) applies to the following functions, namely—

- (a) the making of orders under section 76(2A) of that Act (limitation of expenses in connection with elections to the Greater London Authority);
- (b) the making of orders under section 13 of the Government of Wales Act 2006[^c20096551] or section 12 of the [^c10573181]Scotland Act 1998 so far as relating to the matters mentioned in subsection (2)(c) of the section (limitation of expenses in connection with elections to Senedd Cymru[^c-c3d91d48b20ef96bc5f5228ba07b75fb] or Scottish Parliament);
