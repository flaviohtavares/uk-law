# Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (131).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Commission to be consulted on changes to electoral law.[^c10573001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before making an instrument to which this section applies, the authority making the instrument shall consult the Commission.

(2) This section applies to an instrument containing—

- (a)[^key-3f1b311a13e19ab74a7933b5227820a3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-3f1b311a13e19ab74a7933b5227820a3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) an order under section 24(1)(c), (cc) or (e), 25(1)(b), 28(1)(b) or 35(2B) of the Representation of the [^c10573031]People Act 1983 (designations of returning officers and acting returning officers);
- (d) rules under section 36 of that Act (local government elections in England [^key-67d04dfdc7a5a2b20632b21688c23e0f]...);
- (e) regulations under that Act (“**the 1983 Act**”), or under the Representation of the [^c10573041]People Act 1985, in relation to which section 201(2) of the 1983 Act (regulations which may not be made unless a draft of them has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament) has effect;

*Extent: S*

- (eza)[^key-97178e708b8ec3d66759f609c3d25f29] regulations to be made by the Scottish Ministers under section 81(3A)(c) of the Representation of the People Act 1983 (other matters to be included in election expenses return in relation to local government elections in Scotland);

*Extent: S*

- (ezb)[^key-23d64128d2e3f64270959529829e5383] regulations to be made by the Scottish Ministers under paragraph 10(2) of schedule 2A of the Representation of the People Act 1983 (evidence of donor's anonymous registration to accompany statement of relevant donations in relation to local government elections in Scotland);
- (ea)[^key-0f04d400afc1cc10027dfe899d2a7f00] regulations made by virtue of paragraph 7F of Schedule 4 to the Representation of the People Act 2000 (regulations made by the Scottish Ministers about notification of rejected postal votes in relation to local government elections in Scotland);
- (f)[^c20105731] an order under section 13 or 64(3) or regulations under section 13A[^key-ec5607eaae59d02779d2b444ac401f1e] of the Government of Wales Act 2006 (conduct of elections to Senedd Cymru[^key-cff6b4dfa0693c6ed6d436d312036a3a] and of polls held by Welsh Ministers);
- (g)[^key-e45cd27b829410b4c75226f4485b5599] an order under section 12(1) or (6) or regulations under section 12A(1)[^key-96536a1b2cd1d3cd384d78e67b6a9049] of the [^c10573061]Scotland Act 1998 (conduct of elections to the Scottish Parliament);
- (h) an order under section 34(4) of the [^c10573071]Northern Ireland Act 1998 (conduct of elections to the Northern Ireland Assembly);
- (ha)[^key-69a77125340018ef763c50dae1bc22a2] an order under subsection (1)(b) of section 54 of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011 (designations of returning officers for elections of persons as police and crime commissioners in England and Wales);
- (hb) regulations under subsection (2) of that section (functions of returning officers and local returning officers for such elections);
- (hc) an order under section 58 of that Act (conduct of elections of persons as police and crime commissioners in England and Wales);
- (i) an order under section 17A(3) of the [^c10573081]Greater London Authority Act 1999 (free delivery of election addresses at elections to the Greater London Authority).

*Extent: S*

- (j)[^key-e8459f3954327498bad4068e95860c43] an order under section 3(1) of the Local Governance (Scotland) Act 2004.
- (k)[^key-6a048e160611db1fd4397b5190c892f6] regulations under section 9(5) or 18 of the Recall of MPs Act 2015 (wording of the recall petition signing sheet and the conduct of a recall petition etc).

(3) No draft Order shall be laid before Parliament under section 84(4) of the [^c10573091]Northern Ireland Act 1998 (power to make provision with respect to elections in Northern Ireland) except after consultation with the Commission.
