# Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (131).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Interpretation of Part I.[^c10573411]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-c9cca4059e348a9e0cd0782fb86860a6] In this Part “**financial year**”, in relation to the Commission, means a period of 12 months ending with 31st March; but the first financial year of the Commission is the period beginning with the date of the establishment of the Commission and ending with the next 31st March.

(2)[^c-3bc7f8d226cda9a10e6958a3e98d9e3e] In this Part, “**petition officer**” and “**recall petition**” have the same meaning as in the Recall of MPs Act 2015 (see section 22 of that Act).
