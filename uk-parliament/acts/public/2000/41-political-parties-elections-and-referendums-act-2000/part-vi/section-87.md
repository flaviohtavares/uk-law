# Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (131).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Expenditure by third parties which is not controlled expenditure.[^c10574561]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No amount of controlled expenditure shall be regarded as incurred by a third party by virtue of section 85 or 86 in respect of—

- (a)[^key-74f31447ffcefba7c67dd51de2670378] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) any property, services or facilities to the extent that the property, services or facilities is or are used in circumstances in which—
  - (i) an amount of campaign expenditure is to be regarded as incurred by or on behalf of a registered party for the purposes of Part V, [^key-6b624de5dca5c3837192c810d09d773f]...
  - (ii) an amount of expenses falls (in accordance with any enactment) to be included in a return as to election expenses in respect of a candidate or candidates at a particular election, or[^key-9ba9b5a412971ce4f44d7f82a03bbec5]
  - (iii) an amount of expenses falls to be included in a recall petition return within the meaning of Schedule 5 to the Recall of MPs Act 2015 (see paragraph 1 of that Schedule),

  in respect of that use.

(2)[^key-1cf3ba32dbefe835aabb8ab88535af82] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-cb2df5828affa91bf6025954f839f085] The reference in subsection (1)(b)(i) to circumstances in which an amount of campaign expenditure is to be regarded as incurred by or on behalf of a registered party for the purposes of Part 5 does not include circumstances in which an amount of campaign expenditure is treated as incurred by a registered party under section 94F(2).
