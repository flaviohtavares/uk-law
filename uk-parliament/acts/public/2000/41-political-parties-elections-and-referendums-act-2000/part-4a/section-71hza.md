# Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000, section 71HZA

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 71HZA). (not yet in force: Declaration that residence) (not yet in force: etc) (not yet in force: condition is satisfied)[^c20270581][^c20234671][^c20069971]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A registered party must not be a party to a regulated transaction to which this section applies unless the registered party has received a written declaration from each of the other parties to the transaction who is an individual stating that the individual satisfies the condition set out in section 54(2ZA).)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: This section applies to a regulated transaction—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: if the value of the transaction is more than £7,500, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: if the aggregate amount of it and any other relevant benefit or benefits accruing in the same calendar year as the transaction is more than £7,500.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of subsection (2)(b) “relevant benefit” and “accruing” have the meaning given by section 71M(3).)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: A declaration under this section must also state the individual's full name and address.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: A person who knowingly or recklessly makes a false declaration under this section commits an offence.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision requiring a declaration under this section to be retained for a specified period.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: The reference in subsection (1) to each of the other parties to the transaction does not include any individual who, at the time the transaction is entered into, is an authorised participant in relation to it by reason of section 71Z1(1)(a).)*
