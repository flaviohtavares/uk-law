# Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000, section 137

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (131).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 137. Corrupt and illegal practices: election petitions etc.[^c10575191]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Representation of the [^c10575201]People Act 1983 shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in Schedule 17, which in particular modifies the provisions relating to—

- (a) the procedure on election petitions; and
- (b) the consequences of reports by election courts.
