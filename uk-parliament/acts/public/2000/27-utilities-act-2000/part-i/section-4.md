# Utilities Act 2000, section 4

## 4. Forward work programmes.[^c1258975]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Authority [^key-fe9faa006ae77198be98da659e5ec568]... shall, before each financial year, [^key-0a384e12da9cd3d7515abb8d7f89d1fd]... publish a document (the “**forward work programme**”) containing a general description of the projects, other than those comprising routine activities in the exercise of its functions, which it plans to undertake during the year.

(2) That description must include the objectives of each project.

(3) The forward work programme for any year shall also include an estimate of the overall expenditure which the Authority [^key-5585e0b61618cc52257cb3bcd5add288]... expects to incur during the year in the exercise of its functions.

(4) Before publishing the forward work programme for any year, the Authority [^key-96b52309b00314f7ecdcaa1097052b3a]... shall give notice—

- (a) containing a draft of the forward work programme, and
- (b) specifying the time within which representations or objections to the proposals contained in it may be made,

and shall consider any representations or objections which are duly made and not withdrawn.

(5) The notice under subsection (4) must be published by the Authority [^key-56c18d04e30e2ee2cc493960c79155de]... in such manner as it considers appropriate for the purpose of bringing the matters contained in it to the attention of persons likely to be affected by them.

(6) The Authority must send a copy of any notice given by it under subsection (4) to Citizens Advice[^key-eff9a08419d20e17458fb626cab5973f] and Consumer Scotland[^key-6ef3284740c72eba569287d180a956ce].

(7)[^key-ecca483bbfbec810ee41dfa96333f886] In this section—

- (a) references to functions do not include functions under Part 1 of the Energy Act 2023, and
- (b) references to projects do not include projects with regard to the exercise of such functions.
