## 3A. Exercise of designated regulatory functions etc[^c-3a3bde362e4a517aea4732e8e6a54f6f][^c-040c7c9b80823e443c8ef5bc4ab2233c]

(2) The Authority must ensure that members of its staff do not—

- (a) seek or take any instructions that might compromise, or might reasonably be seen to compromise, the Authority ’s independence in relation to its designated regulatory functions[^c-179eb5ea1640faac5eb019ac7896627d]; or
- (b) carry out any other activity, or have any financial or other interest, that might compromise, or might reasonably be seen to compromise, the impartiality of those members of staff.
