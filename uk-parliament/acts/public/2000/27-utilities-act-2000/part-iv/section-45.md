# Utilities Act 2000, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Abolition of tariffs.[^c1259040]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 18 of the 1989 Act (charging by reference to tariffs) shall cease to have effect.
