# Utilities Act 2000, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 89. Repeal of section 10(2) to (5) of Gas Act 1995.[^c1259098]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subsections (2) to (5) of section 10 of the [^c1259099]Gas Act 1995 shall cease to have effect and, accordingly, sections 33A to 33E of the 1986 Act are not liable to expire in accordance with those subsections.
