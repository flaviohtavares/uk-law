# Utilities Act 2000, section 81

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/27

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 81. Standard conditions of gas licences.[^c1259080]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The standard conditions for the purposes of gas licences of any type (that is to say, licences under section 7, section 7A(1) , section 7A(2) or 7AC(1)[^key-7d69a4048c598e13974dafa65b7aa1af] of the 1986 Act) may contain provision—

- (a) for any standard condition included in a licence of that type not to have effect until brought into operation in such manner and in such circumstances as may be specified in or determined under the standard conditions;
- (b) for the effect of any standard condition included in such a licence to be suspended in such manner, and in such circumstances, as may be so specified or determined; or
- (c) for any standard condition included in such a licence which is for the time being suspended to be brought back into operation in such manner and in such circumstances as may be so specified or determined.

(2) Such conditions as may be determined by the Secretary of State before the relevant time[^key-d8da357a46cb81710f13200ed874efc0], and published by him in such manner as he considers appropriate, in relation to gas licences of any type shall, subject to any modifications made[^key-a5d52c778b243362a4ce6e8f57214902] [^key-e30e356d66f2f990793486e30fa3e866]... under Part 1 or section 76[^key-c136a3c4cda9cbb9aa15dc770220b39d] or 98[^key-f079e30e7d880d2a021370909fcdc7e3] of the 1986 Act or , under the Energy Act[^key-d39aec47d7cc57b85cd878c3706f45cf] 2004,[^key-943ae44e3041bd3dfdd9c682d2f7f172] under section 88 or 94 of the Energy Act 2008,[^key-1a7e9e436d2d433f0752eefb9c3cd51a] under the Energy Act 2010[^key-460293004ddbd6c56c8dba24cf0f3bb4] , under Chapter 1 of Part 1 or section 98 of the Energy Act 2011[^key-c4fb7e6163d80a3cde0f38ecb39ff333] , under section 139 of the Energy Act 2013 or under section 1 or 10 of the Domestic Gas and Electricity (Tariff Cap) Act 2018[^key-7e596215bdddfbea945fc3f7e9c6abe4] or under section 6 of the Smart Meters Act 2018[^key-d6f398ee9f8e1327f2557d7d221eabc8] or under or by virtue of section 21 of the Energy Prices Act 2022[^key-520ae19d4dd68f91cbc6eb57fb78dac9] or under[^key-21f02b8ecf3efa7bbfecde6bc62d08e4] sections 89, 136(1) or (4), 169, 245 to 247 of, and paragraph 4 of Schedule 12 to, the Energy Act 2023[^key-59c1eb5347341564fe7f0c637f77c0aa] after the determination under this subsection, be standard conditions for the purposes of licences of that type (in place of the standard conditions which would otherwise be incorporated in licences of that type granted immediately before that commencement).

(2A)[^key-cceaeb093655e4f1514553dfcd1cd3e1] In subsection (2), “**the relevant time**” means—

- (a) where the determination relates to any of the types of licence mentioned in sections 7, 7A(1) or 7A(2) of the 1986 Act, the time when subsection (3) comes into force;
- (b) where the determination relates to the type of licence mentioned in section 7AC of the 1986 Act (licence of a person performing code manager function), 26th October 2030.

(3) In section 8(1) of the 1986 Act (standard conditions of licences to be incorporated in gas licences)—

- (a) the words “and sections 23(2), 26(1A) and 27(2) below” shall be omitted; and
- (b) for “section 8(2) of the Gas Act 1995” there is substituted “ section 81(2) of the Utilities Act 2000 ”.
