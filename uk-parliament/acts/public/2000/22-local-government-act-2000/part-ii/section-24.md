# Local Government Act 2000, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Absence of requirement for political balance.[^c1427870]

*Extent: E+W*

Neither—

- (a) a local authority executive, nor
- (b) a committee of a local authority executive,

is to be regarded as a body to which section 15 of the [^c1427871]Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (duty to allocate seats to political groups) applies.
