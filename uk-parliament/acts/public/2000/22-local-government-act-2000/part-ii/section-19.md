# Local Government Act 2000, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Discharge of functions of and by another local authority.[^c1427825]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers[^key-e1674d3d0fd5f735561e0a72ab76f576] may by regulations make provision for or in connection with enabling an executive of a local authority [^key-5df3a3d672d8a26c0a601b0c62cda7a3]..., or a committee or specified member of such an executive, to arrange for the discharge of any functions which, under executive arrangements, are the responsibility of the executive—

- (a)[^key-19d21857089a84dd0eea3e0397f4b522] by a relevant authority (other than the local authority), or
- (b) by a relevant executive (other than an executive of the local authority)[^key-97d9240c8d731fcddbf5b5d0c142c6c8] or a committee or specified member of such an executive.

(2) The Welsh Ministers[^key-8f457090bf063140788756f820e6994e] may by regulations make provision for or in connection with enabling a relevant authority in Wales[^key-619237a7a18f64c1f7bb4fe06e7956af] to arrange for the discharge of any of their functions by a relevant executive (other than an executive of the relevant authority)[^key-4b16c6dee8d97f9b3c839fc44feafeee] or a committee or specified member of such an executive.

(3) The reference in subsection (2) to the functions of a relevant authority in Wales[^key-244817ca1d6dbaf721ab3e2f72082165], in a case where the authority are operating executive arrangements, is a reference to the functions which, under those arrangements, are not the responsibility of the authority’s executive.

(4) Regulations under subsection (1) or (2) may include provision—

- (a) requiring, in the case of arrangements for the discharge of any functions by a relevant executive[^key-f19025e7425e5b4ce3dc7484a614474a] or a committee or member of such an executive, the approval of the authority of which the executive is part[^key-2a2b48655ce62b39221462a9d5781cdc] to such arrangements,
- (b) which, in the case of arrangements for the discharge of any functions by a relevant authority[^key-eee2712da92b704ee1a3cbffe9d2c472], enables any of those functions to be delegated,
- (c) which, in the case of arrangements for the discharge of any functions by a relevant executive[^key-305578224da947a60eb6916093d7144f] or a committee or member of such an executive, enables any of those functions to be delegated.

(5) The provision which may be made under subsection (4)(b) includes provision which applies or reproduces (with or without modifications) any provisions of section 101(2) to (4) of the [^c1427827]Local Government Act 1972.

(6) The provision which may be made under subsection (4)(c) includes provision which applies or reproduces (with or without modifications) any provisions of section 14(3) to (6), 15(5) to (9) or 16(3) to (5).

(7) Nothing in subsection (4), (5) or (6) affects the generality of the power under subsection (1) or (2).

(8) In this section —[^key-c4251af7e153943c912a6892bfcd1f21]

- "relevant authority” means a local authority within the meaning of section 101 of the Local Government Act 1972;
- “**relevant executive**” means an executive of a local authority under either this Part or Part 1A;
- “specified” means specified in regulations under this section.
