## 30. Operation of different executive arrangements.[^c1427879]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision for or in connection with the operation by a local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78] in Wales[^key-2f634fedbe476ff65f9c8af50426a362] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78] which are operating executive arrangements (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78]**the existing arrangements *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78]**”) of executive arrangements (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78]**the different arrangements *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78]**”) which differ from the existing arrangements in any respect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78]

(2) The provision which may be made by virtue of subsection (1) includes provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78]

- (a) which applies or reproduces (with or without modifications) any provisions of section 25, 26, 27, 28 or 29, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78]
- (b) for or in connection with requiring the consent of an elected mayor under the existing arrangements to the operation of the different arrangements, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78]
- (c) with respect to changes to the existing arrangements as a result of changes to the functions which are the responsibility of an executive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78]

(3) Nothing in subsection (2) affects the generality of the power under subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-00cfbfabd2bc5765c25fdb6e4b31bb78]
