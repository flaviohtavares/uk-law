# Local Government Act 2000, section 33

## 33. Operation of alternative arrangements.[^c1427884]

*Extent: E+W*

(A1)[^c-7f1b8bd63d6fbd5b386d3bc85e97e57d][^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7] In this section references to a local authority are references to a local authority in Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7]

(1) A local authority may not operate alternative arrangements unless permitted or required to do so by virtue of any provision made by or under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7]

(2)[^c1427885][^c1427886][^c-cbb7b07751b624e41fd3f229fe016e45][^c-7b38807f8e0893a942ea776d554b79d3][^c-52c908186f674d33c2afcc513c1afa73] A resolution of a local authority is required in order for the authority to operate alternative arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7]

(3) Subsection (2) of section 29 is to apply for the purposes of this section as it applies for the purposes of that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7]

(4) A local authority which pass a resolution under this section to operate alternative arrangements may not at any subsequent time cease to operate those arrangements unless, by virtue of any provision made under subsection (9) or section 34, 35 or 36, the authority operate executive arrangements in place of those arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7]

(5) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision for or in connection with enabling a local authority to which section 31 applies which are operating executive arrangements to operate alternative arrangements in place of the executive arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7]

(6) The provision which may be made by virtue of subsection (5) includes provision which applies or reproduces (with or without modifications) any provisions of section 25, 26, 27 or 28. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7]

(7) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision for or in connection with enabling a local authority which are operating alternative arrangements to operate alternative arrangements which differ from the existing alternative arrangements in any respect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7]

(8) The provision which may be made by virtue of subsection (7) includes provision which applies or reproduces (with or without modifications) any provisions of section 25 or 26. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7]

(9) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision for or in connection with enabling a local authority which are operating alternative arrangements to operate executive arrangements in place of the alternative arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7]

(10) The provision which may be made by virtue of subsection (9) includes provision which applies or reproduces (with or without modifications) any provisions of section 25, 26, 27, 28 or 29. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7]

(11) Nothing in subsection (6), (8) or (10) affects the generality of the power under subsection (5), (7) or (9) (as the case may be). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d4658a736e6ad305a38a7e66b9218aa7]
