## 48. Interpretation of Part II.[^c1427908]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**alternative arrangements**” has the meaning given by section 32(1),
- “[^c-17c32480a6a197a20aa2db1f60d01c7b]**council manager *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-17c32480a6a197a20aa2db1f60d01c7b]**” has the meaning given by section 11(4)(b), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-17c32480a6a197a20aa2db1f60d01c7b]
- “**elected executive member**” has the meaning given by section 39(4),
- “**elected mayor**” has the meaning given by section 39(1),
- “**electoral area**” has the meaning given by section 203(1) of the [^c1427909]Representation of the People Act 1983,
- “**enactment**” includes an enactment contained in a local Act or comprised in subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the [^c1427910]Interpretation Act 1978),
- “**executive**”, in relation to a local authority, is to be construed in accordance with section 11,
- “**executive arrangements**” has the meaning given by section 10,
- “**executive leader**” has the meaning given by section 11(3)(a)[^c-5939ace886af277b49f909fe9b58fe30],
- “**fall-back proposals**” and “**outline fall-back proposals**” are to be construed in accordance with section 27(1) and (2),
- “**first preference vote**” has the meaning given by section 42(1)(a),
- “**local authority**” means a county council in Wales[^c-f430f5dfdda73c0a9aaa7e4387844186] or a county borough council,
- “**local government elector**” has the meaning given by section 270(1) of the [^c1427911]Local Government Act 1972,
- [^c-c0402ee83decc40df2ec182e29b9d419]...
- “**overview and scrutiny committee**” has the meaning given by section 21(1),
- “**the political balance requirements**” means the provisions made by or under sections 15 to 17 of, and Schedule 1 to, the [^c1427912]Local Government and Housing Act 1989,
- “**second preference vote**” has the meaning given by section 42(1)(b).

(2) Any reference in this Part to the chairman of a local authority—

- (a) is a reference to that person whether or not he is entitled to another style, [^c-c9df5135437eec77fdb0b301f7f4dda8]...
- (b)[^c-e16c48cc7ec23040e424ef421f103a3f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Any reference in this Part to the vice-chairman of a local authority—

- (a) is a reference to that person whether or not he is entitled to another style, [^c-fea104284c0fa2ef20361ba5a31536fc]...
- (b)[^c-70d5748cc668ff3952af95582851fcfd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Any reference in this Part to the discharge of any functions includes a reference to the doing of anything which is calculated to facilitate, or is conducive or incidental to, the discharge of those functions.

(5) Section 101 of the [^c1427915]Local Government Act 1972 does not apply to the function of the passing of a resolution under any provision made by or under this Part.

(6) Any functions conferred on a local authority by virtue of this Part are not to be the responsibility of an executive of the authority under executive arrangements.

(7) Any directions given by the Welsh Ministers[^c-ed5d41a0661921c56adb5c9acd18e09a] under any provision of this Part—

- (a) may be varied or revoked by subsequent directions given by them[^c-1cb507fb0f8dc3938cb242f5175fe178] under that provision, and
- (b) may make different provision for different cases, local authorities or descriptions of local authority.
