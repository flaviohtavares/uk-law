## 100 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Power to make provision about allowances.[^c1428010][^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision with respect to— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] allowances payable to members of a parish council, *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] travelling and subsistence allowances payable to members of such relevant authorities as may be prescribed, *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] allowances payable to members of such relevant authorities as may be prescribed for attending conferences or meetings, *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] the reimbursement of expenses incurred by members of such relevant authorities as may be prescribed. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] For the purposes of this section a member of a committee or sub-committee of a relevant authority is to be treated as a member of the authority. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] The provision which may be made under subsection (1)(b) includes provision with respect to allowances in respect of travel by bicycle or by any other non-motorised form of transport. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]

(4 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] The provision which may be made under this section includes provision which amends or repeals any provisions of sections 173 to 178 of the *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] [^c1428011]Local Government Act 1972. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]

(5 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] Before making any regulations under this section, the Secretary of State must consult such representatives of local government and such other persons (if any) as he considers appropriate. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]

(6 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] In this section— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]

- “ *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]**prescribed *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]**” means prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State, *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]
- “ *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]**relevant authority *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]**” means— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]
  - (a) a body specified in section 21(1) of the *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] [^c1428012]Local Government and Housing Act 1989, *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]
  - (b) a body on which a body falling within paragraph (a) is represented, *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]
  - (c) a parish council. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]

(7 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] In its application to Wales this section has effect as if— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] for any reference to the Secretary of State there were substituted a reference to the National Assembly for Wales, *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1] for any reference to a parish council there were substituted a reference to a community council. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-79732cc99fcda9c67d01f5a0599f1ad1]
