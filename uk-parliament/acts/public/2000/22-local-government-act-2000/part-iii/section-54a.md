# Local Government Act 2000, section 54A

## 54A. Sub-committees of standards committees[^c-bc5b988bebae81ea065629905dab0d42]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A standards committee of a relevant authority may appoint one or more sub-committees for the purpose of discharging any of the committee’s functions, whether or not to the exclusion of the committee.

(1A)[^c-4d2d20bf4fe408c980494e12366e3a98] Subsection (1) does not apply to a standards committee established by a corporate joint committee.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to functions under section [^c-6e3b4c43a67aa0a99ddf06010d319321]...56.

(3) A sub-committee under subsection (1) shall be appointed from among the members of the standards committee by which it is appointed [^c-c271b73504e6795de3ed74fa894dfc0d]....

(4)[^c-3f48a3e68a0150396510ca74c6699546] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) As regards sub-committees appointed under subsection (1) by a standards committee of a relevant authority [^c-f0f727530528be2baf72c830155c8a15]...—

- (a) regulations under section 53(11) may make provision in relation to such sub-committees, and
- (b) section 54(5) and (7) apply in relation to such sub-committees as they apply in relation to standards committees.

(6) Subject to [^c-92a056de3874d03e8ea10a2ed6c333bc]... any provision made by regulations under section 53(11)(a)[^c-bcca84eb292844763d320270dac37662] (as applied by this section)—

- (a) the number of members of a sub-committee under subsection (1), and
- (b) the term of office of those members,

are to be fixed by the standards committee by which the sub-committee is appointed.
