# Local Government Act 2000, section 69C

## 69C (Extent: W). Information, documents, evidence and facilities (Extent: W)[^c-1273902fdb4bd70d7be1bd6a934e4579]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) The Ombudsman may require a person the Ombudsman thinks is able to supply information or produce a document relevant to an investigation under section 69 to do so. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The Ombudsman has the same powers as the High Court in relation to— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) the attendance and examination of witnesses (including the administration of oaths and affirmations and the examination of witnesses abroad), and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the production of documents. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) The Ombudsman may require a person the Ombudsman thinks is able to supply information or produce a document relevant to an investigation to provide any facility the Ombudsman may reasonably require. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) The Ombudsman may require the relevant authority concerned to provide any facility the Ombudsman may reasonably require. *(Extent: W)*

(5 (Extent: W)) Subject to subsection (6), no person may be compelled to give any evidence or produce any document which the person could not be compelled to give or produce in civil proceedings before the High Court. *(Extent: W)*

(6 (Extent: W)) The Crown is not entitled to any privilege in relation to the production of documents or the giving of evidence that would otherwise be allowed by law in legal proceedings. *(Extent: W)*

(7 (Extent: W)) Where an obligation to maintain secrecy or other restriction on the disclosure of information obtained by or supplied to persons in Her Majesty's service has been imposed by an enactment or a rule of law, the obligation or restriction does not apply to the disclosure of information for the purposes of the investigation. *(Extent: W)*
