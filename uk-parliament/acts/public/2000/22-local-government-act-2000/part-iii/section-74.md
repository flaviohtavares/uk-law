## 74 (Extent: W). Law of defamation: absolute privilege (Extent: W)[^key-17bc839e963503e977b437302666e608]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of the law of defamation a publication of a matter is absolutely privileged if— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) the publication is made in the exercise of the functions of the Ombudsman under Chapters 3 and 4 of this Part; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the publication— *(Extent: W)*
  - (i (Extent: W)) is made in communications with the Ombudsman or a person exercising a function of the Ombudsman, and *(Extent: W)*
  - (ii (Extent: W)) is made for the purposes of, or in connection with, the Ombudsman's functions under Chapters 3 and 4 of this Part. *(Extent: W)*
