## 80. Recommendations by ... case tribunals.[^c1427976][^c-680948e544323db3c09da960f0766eee]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where[^c-4d208df37a3c3c7322ef18924405d73c] [^c-02991d1c5ebc342dc2d494099013a733]... a case tribunal has adjudicated on any matter under this Act, it may make recommendations to a relevant authority about any matters relating to—

- (a) the exercise of the authority’s functions,
- (b) the authority’s code of conduct, or
- (c) the authority’s standards committee.

(2) A case tribunal[^c-c13ae240a419219d6e699896c8bde696] must send a copy of any recommendations it makes under subsection (1) to the relevant person.

(3) A relevant authority to whom recommendations are made under subsection (1) must consider the recommendations and, within a period of three months beginning with the day on which the recommendations are received, prepare a report for the Public Services Ombudsman for Wales[^c-2abb64ad11c2fdf838bb8afbf2ed0853] giving details of what action the authority have taken or are proposing to take as a result of the recommendations.

(4) A relevant authority’s function of considering a report under subsection (3) may be discharged only by the authority or by the standards committee of that authority (and accordingly, in the case of a relevant authority to which section 101 of the [^c1427977]Local Government Act 1972 or regulation 13 of the Corporate Joint Committees (General) (No. 2) (Wales) Regulations 2021 (arrangements for discharge of functions)[^c-e9d245801ae811c3a3bd2174354107ac] applies, is not to be a function to which that section or regulation applies[^c-4208be39a6b440444afd40960f44aac9]).

(5) If the Public Services Ombudsman for Wales[^c-5f1aaa6b256630e8872f79c3aea270c2] is not satisfied with the action the relevant authority have taken or propose to take in relation to the recommendations, the Public Services Ombudsman for Wales may require the authority to publish a statement giving details of the recommendations made by the tribunal and of the authority’s reasons for not fully implementing the recommendations.
