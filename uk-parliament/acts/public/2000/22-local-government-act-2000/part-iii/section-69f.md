## 69F (Extent: W). Power of the Welsh Ministers to amend this Chapter (Extent: W)[^key-1273902fdb4bd70d7be1bd6a934e4579]

*Extent: E+W*

The Welsh Ministers may by regulations amend this Chapter to make further or different provision about the exercise of the functions of the Public Services Ombudsman for Wales under section 69. *(Extent: W)*
