## 50. Model code of conduct.[^c1427920][^key-1c90b4816bee5a5e3aed9c25eb764788][^key-8f33abf4380d779a4842bad9f7d9f5f8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-c01d38d7e135ae0815227c9c381fc1d8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The National Assembly for Wales may by order issue a model code as regards the conduct which is expected of members and co-opted members of relevant authorities [^key-25daefdd84b315c2f9b3e15e0321d445]... (also referred to in this Part as a model code of conduct).

(3) The power under subsection [^key-471392e89d2c01284e557ad0b94a7d4c]... (2) to issue a model code of conduct includes power to revise any such model code which has been issued.

(4) A model code of conduct—

- (a) must be consistent with the principles for the time being specified in an order under section [^key-6f15d0ce1f4136ed01625996b3f368fb]... 49(2) [^key-8fac09e216b7457467af31b4effd48ed]...,
- (b) may include provisions which are mandatory, and
- (c) may include provisions which are optional.

(4A)[^key-7a42e21b0e03c8c8aa52cc2003532368] A model code of conduct issued under subsection (1) must provide, as respects each provision of the code which relates to the conduct expected of the persons mentioned in that subsection—

- (a) that the provision applies to a person only when acting in an official capacity; or
- (b) that it applies to a person only when not acting in an official capacity;

but the code may provide as mentioned in paragraph (b) only as respects a provision within subsection (4B).

(4B) A provision is within this subsection if it prohibits particular conduct (or conduct of a particular description) where that conduct would constitute a criminal offence.

(4C)[^key-0a84743458473b1fde70e9ea869c7fc8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4D)[^key-0a84743458473b1fde70e9ea869c7fc8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4E) A model code of conduct issued under subsection (2) may include—

- (a) provisions which are to apply to a person at all times;
- (b) provisions which are to apply to a person otherwise than at all times.

(5) Before making an order under this section, [^key-2ae2ba12239962a833f0b8fcf395fbf5]... the National Assembly for Wales must carry out such consultation as is required, by virtue of section 49, before an order is made under that section.
