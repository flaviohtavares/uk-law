## 69E (Extent: W). Disclosure of information (Extent: W)[^key-1273902fdb4bd70d7be1bd6a934e4579]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) This section applies to information obtained in the exercise of the Ombudsman's functions under this Part by— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) the Ombudsman; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) a member of the Ombudsman's staff or other person acting on the Ombudsman's behalf; *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) a person assisting the Ombudsman. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The information may be disclosed only— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) for the purposes of the Ombudsman's functions under— *(Extent: W)*
  - (i (Extent: W)) Chapter 3 or 4 of this Part; *(Extent: W)*
  - (ii (Extent: W)) Part 3 or 5 of the Public Services Ombudsman (Wales) Act 2019; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) for the purposes of the functions of the Adjudication Panel for Wales, including the functions of its President, Deputy President and tribunals, under Chapter 4 of this Part; *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) for the purposes of criminal proceedings or the investigation of a criminal offence; *(Extent: W)*
- (d (Extent: W)) if the disclosure is made to the Auditor General for Wales for the purposes of the Auditor General's functions under Part 2 of the Public Audit (Wales) Act 2004; *(Extent: W)*
- (e (Extent: W)) if the disclosure is made to the Electoral Commission for the purposes of any of its functions. *(Extent: W)*
