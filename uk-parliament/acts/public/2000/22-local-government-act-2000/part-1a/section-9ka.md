## 9KA. Mayor and cabinet executive: adoption of leader and cabinet executive[^c-d0889913e49d5ad41844633fd2ce02d1][^c-7d6540f29d1520baab596e38e95ad9c0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^c-83aa94d6e84e6bdf2b388a6ebd3fa288] This section applies to a local authority if it is operating a mayor and cabinet executive.

(1) The local authority—

- (a) may vary its executive arrangements so that they provide for a leader and cabinet executive (England), and
- (b) may vary its executive arrangements in such other respects (if any) as it considers appropriate.
