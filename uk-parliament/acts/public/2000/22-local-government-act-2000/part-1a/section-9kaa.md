# Local Government Act 2000, section 9KAA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9KAA. Duty to retain leader and cabinet executive[^key-f0e739791cb2b7333b84aa14debdf60c][^key-d0889913e49d5ad41844633fd2ce02d1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A local authority which is operating a leader and cabinet executive (England) must not cease to operate—

- (a) executive arrangements, or
- (b) a leader and cabinet executive (England).

(2) That duty applies to a local authority whenever it began to operate the leader and cabinet executive (England).
