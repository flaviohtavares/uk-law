## 9HC. Voting at elections of elected mayors[^key-d0889913e49d5ad41844633fd2ce02d1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-44114650b43ba861ac426fd1542de7f9] Each person entitled to vote as an elector at an election for the return of an elected mayor is to have one vote which may be given for a candidate to be the elected mayor.

(2) The elected mayor is to be returned under the simple majority system [^key-1f41d0dabbe8ec7b09b8feeaaefb2caa]....
