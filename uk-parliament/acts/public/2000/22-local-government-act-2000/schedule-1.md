## SCHEDULE 1 — Executive arrangements in Wales: further provision[^c-2ea19cb7b89df6d048152783fdd9be7a]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 23.

### Mayor and cabinet executives

**1.**[^c1428042] (1) This paragraph applies in relation to executive arrangements by a local authority which provide for a mayor and cabinet executive.

(2) Subject to section 11(8) and (8ZA) *(Extent: W)*[^c-720332dbc6b9d70d673c495b5d1e1f14], the executive arrangements must include provision which enables the elected mayor to determine the number of councillors who may be appointed to the executive under section 11(2)(b).

(3) The executive arrangements must include provision which requires the elected mayor to appoint one of the members of the executive to be his deputy (referred to in this paragraph as the deputy mayor).

(4) Subject to sub-paragraph (5), the deputy mayor, unless he resigns as deputy mayor or ceases to be a member of the authority, is to hold office until the end of the term of office of the elected mayor.

(5) The elected mayor may, if he thinks fit, remove the deputy mayor from office.

(6) Where a vacancy occurs in the office of deputy mayor, the elected mayor must appoint another person in his place.

(7) If for any reason the elected mayor is unable to act or the office of elected mayor is vacant, the deputy mayor must act in his place.

(8) If for any reason—

- (a) the elected mayor is unable to act or the office of elected mayor is vacant, and
- (b) the deputy mayor is unable to act or the office of deputy mayor is vacant,

the executive must act in the elected mayor’s place or must arrange for a member of the executive to act in his place.

(9) The[^c-dbc630968f054fbb422cba6368d2a5bb] deputy mayor is entitled to the style of “dirprwy faer”.

### Leader and cabinet executives (Wales)[^c-3d609984f4358c18b913bac73639509f]

**2.**[^c1428043] (1) This paragraph applies in relation to executive arrangements by a local authority which provide for a leader and cabinet executive (Wales)[^c-dff5d1b5ab58d5e4e43402fb8f8c5471].

(2) The executive arrangements may include provision with respect to—

- (a) the election and term of office of the executive leader, and
- (b) the appointment and term of office of members of the executive appointed under section 11(3)(b)(ii).

*Extent: W*

(2A)[^c-726432911452b41bbd207f529ffc0b99] The executive arrangements must include provision under which two or more councillors may be elected by the authority to share office as executive leader; and references in any enactment to an executive leader include executive leaders elected by virtue of that provision.

(3) Subject to section 11(8) and (8ZA) *(Extent: W)*[^c-353452988b64bd130ce23d60286eb230], the executive arrangements must include provision which either—

- (a) enables the authority to determine the number of councillors who may be appointed to the executive under section 11(3)(b), or
- (b) enables the executive leader to determine the number of councillors who may be so appointed.

(4) Section 101 of the [^c1428044]Local Government Act 1972 does not apply to the function of determining the number of councillors under sub-paragraph (3)(a).[^c-edb270ee8aeb4b065f11bfdb52242920]

### Appointment of councillors to executive to share the same position (Extent: W)[^c-fcc64c9065584a28cfdb356f893e1e0d]

**2A (Extent: W).** Executive arrangements by a local authority must include provision under which two or more councillors may be appointed to the executive to share office. *(Extent: W)*

### Voting and quorum where members of executive share their position (Extent: W)

**2B (Extent: W).** (1 (Extent: W)) This paragraph applies where two or more councillors of a local authority are— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) appointed to a mayor and cabinet executive to share office, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) appointed to a leader and cabinet executive (Wales) to share office, or *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) elected to share office as executive leader of a leader and cabinet executive (Wales). *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The members of the executive who share the same office have between them one vote in respect of any matter on which they have a right to vote because they are a member of the executive. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) Where any meeting is attended by more than one of the members who share the same office and those members are attending in their capacity as a member of the executive, they together count only as one person for the purpose of determining whether the meeting is quorate. *(Extent: W)*

### Assistants to the executive (Extent: W)[^c-874991058093c05e9aba5e963cf465a6]

**3A (Extent: W).** (1 (Extent: W)) Executive arrangements by a local authority may make provision for councillors of the authority to be appointed to assist the executive in discharging functions which are the responsibility of the executive. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) Such a councillor is referred to in this Schedule as an assistant to the executive of the authority. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) Assistants to the executive of an authority are to be appointed— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) in the case of an authority operating a mayor and cabinet executive, by the elected mayor; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) in the case of an authority operating a leader and cabinet executive (Wales), by the executive leader or the authority. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) Executive arrangements which make provision for the appointment of assistants to an executive may include provision about— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) the number of assistants that may be appointed, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) their term of office, and *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) their responsibilities. *(Extent: W)*

(5 (Extent: W)) The assistants to the executive of a local authority may not include— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) the chairman and vice-chairman of the authority; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the presiding member and deputy presiding member of the authority (if the authority has a presiding member). *(Extent: W)*

(6 (Extent: W)) An assistant to the executive of an authority is not a member of the executive of the authority. *(Extent: W)*

(7 (Extent: W)) Section 101 of the Local Government Act 1972 (arrangements for discharge of functions by local authorities) does not apply to a local authority's function of making appointments under sub-paragraph (3)(b). *(Extent: W)*

### Procedure

**4.**[^c1428055] Executive arrangements by a local authority may include provision with respect to—

- (a) the quorum, proceedings and location of meetings of the executive,
- (b) the appointment of committees of the executive, and
- (c) the quorum, proceedings and location of meetings of committees of the executive.

**5.**[^c1428056] (1)[^c-d014c55044b85b31145e491c2df60575] An assistant to the executive of a local authority is entitled to attend, and speak at, any meeting of the executive or of a committee of the executive.

(2)[^c-80d35f762e14d941e622d214e02de4a7] A member of a local authority who is neither a member of the authority's executive nor an assistant to the executive[^c-d0711e5c033abddac9526c2e571b3e25] is entitled to attend, and speak at, a meeting of the executive, or of a committee of the executive, which is held in private only if invited to do so.

**6.**[^c1428057] (1) The Welsh Ministers[^c-dca56f8dd173d3eab1c83a7c4a0f108e] may by regulations make provision for or in connection with the appointment of a person (an “**assistant**”) to provide assistance to an elected mayor.

(3) Nothing in sub-paragraph (2) affects the generality of the power under sub-paragraph (1).
