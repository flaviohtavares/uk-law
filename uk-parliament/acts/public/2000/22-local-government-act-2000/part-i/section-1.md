# Local Government Act 2000, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Meaning of “local authority” in Part I.[^c1427784]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-5b7dc28dd8c68bede6e8d36312c02ae6] In this Part “**local authority**” means—

- (a) in relation to England—
  - (i) a county council,
  - (ii) a district council,
  - (iii) a London borough council,
  - (iv) the Common Council of the City of London in its capacity as a local authority,
  - (v) the Council of the Isles of Scilly,
  - (vi)[^c-f4901cbf0bead7cb8fe4c874e895f5c0] an eligible parish council,
- (b) in relation to Wales, a county council or a county borough council or a community council[^c-8ab5623ebae9ca0ae26506597a40626b].

(2)[^c-538d81fdf62e0c6d7e6e92bf68bea782] A parish council is “**eligible**” for the purposes of this Part if the council meets the conditions prescribed by the Secretary of State by order for the purposes of this section.
