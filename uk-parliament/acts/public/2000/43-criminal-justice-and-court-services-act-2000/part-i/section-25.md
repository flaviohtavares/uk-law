> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/43

## 25. Interpretation of Part I.

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- [^key-ddfaaa23687f92bbc440cce6b893a6ff]...
- [^key-d614bad7503acc33552320282864156a]...
- “**by virtue of**” includes by or under,
- “**organisation**” includes a public body and a private or voluntary organisation,
- “**prescribed**” means prescribed by regulations,
- “**regulations**” means—
  - (a) in the case of regulations under section 15, regulations made by the Lord Chancellor, and[^key-c6a7398101a6d94ce2b1fe8e93621c6e]
  - (b) in any other case, regulations made by the Secretary of State.
