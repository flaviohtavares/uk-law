## 73. Parenting orders: responsible officer.

*Extent: E+W*

In section 8(8) of the [^c10579531]Crime and Disorder Act 1998 (parenting orders: persons who may be specified as the responsible officer), after paragraph (b) there is inserted—

> - (bb) a person nominated by a person appointed as chief education officer under section 532 of the [^c10579541]Education Act 1996

.
