# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Expenses.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred by a Minister of the Crown by virtue of this Act,
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other enactment.
