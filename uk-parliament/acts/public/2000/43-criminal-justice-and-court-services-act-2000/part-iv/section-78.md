# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 78

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. General interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act—

- [^key-6cb5ad4bec9e2ecc472ae8d9195a13aa]...
- “**enactment**” includes an enactment contained in subordinate legislation,
- “**functions**” includes powers and duties,
- [^key-1947028f8c2bc327dec0e2368a0c67f1]...
- “**subordinate legislation**” has the same meaning as in the [^c10579571]Interpretation Act 1978.

(2) In this Act, “**enactment**” means an enactment whenever passed or made; but in this Part other than section 77(3)[^key-5dcec9d396a897f27910f347842ec294] it means—

- (a) an Act passed before, or in the same Session as, this Act, and
- (b) subordinate legislation made before the passing of this Act.
