# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 81

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Extent.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following provisions, this Act extends to England and Wales only.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to—

- (a) sections 26 to 33, so far as they relate to the making of orders by, or orders made by, the Court Martial or the Court Martial Appeal Court[^c-e7a9c7d1617a292f5103dd21471b284d],
- (b) section 60, and paragraphs 135 to 138, 142 and 144 to 148 of Schedule 7, so far as they relate to sentences passed by a court-martial,
- (c) section 61 so far as it relates to sentences passed by a court-martial or a Standing Civilian Court,
- (d) section 66 and Schedule 5,
- (e) section 71,
- (f) this Chapter,
- (g) paragraphs 17 and 19 of Schedule 2,
- (h)[^c-907b6c0272c6a79581568b9cce877c18] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (i) paragraph 159 of Schedule 7.

(3) Sections 35, 36 and 41 extend to England and Wales and Northern Ireland.

(4) Section 40 extends to Northern Ireland only.

(5) The amendment or repeal by Schedule 7 or 8 of an enactment extending to Scotland or Northern Ireland extends also to Scotland or, as the case may be, Northern Ireland.

(6) For the purposes of the [^c10579621]Scotland Act 1998, any provision of section 66 and Schedule 5 and, so far as relating to those provisions and extending to Scotland, any provision of this Chapter is to be taken to be a pre-commencement enactment within the meaning of that Act.
