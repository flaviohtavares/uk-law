## 65. Short-term prisoners: release subject to curfew conditions.

*Extent: E+W*

In section 34A of the [^c10579321]Criminal Justice Act 1991 (power to release short-term prisoners on licence), after subsection (2)(d) there is inserted—

> - (da) the prisoner is subject to the notification requirements of Part I of the [^c10579331]Sex Offenders Act 1997;

.
