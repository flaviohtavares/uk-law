# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/43

## 58. Right to bail: relevance of drug misuse.[^c10579101]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 4 of the [^c10579111]Bail Act 1976 (general right to bail), after subsection (8) there is inserted—

> (9) In taking any decisions required by Part I or II of Schedule 1 to this Act, the considerations to which the court is to have regard include, so far as relevant, any misuse of controlled drugs by the defendant (“**controlled drugs**” and “**misuse**” having the same meanings as in the [^c10579121]Misuse of Drugs Act 1971).
