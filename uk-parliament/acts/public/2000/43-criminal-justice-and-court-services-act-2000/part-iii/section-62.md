## 62. Release on licence etc: electronic monitoring conditions.[^c-fc681856ee34a147b85ff24fc45a30a4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where a sentence of imprisonment has been imposed on a person and, by virtue of any enactment—

- (a) the Secretary of State is required to, or may, release the person from prison, and
- (b) the release is required to be, or may be, subject to conditions (whether conditions of a licence or any other conditions, however expressed).

(2)[^M_F_0af6c191-a68e-4ebe-fd23-e9202b1b7b68] The conditions may include electronic monitoring conditions.

(2A) An electronic monitoring condition imposed under this section must include provision for making a person responsible for the monitoring.

(2B) A person may not be made responsible for the monitoring unless the person is of a description specified in an order made by the Secretary of State.

(3)[^c-ddf1dcc598ea15a2767fdd03fa5defbf] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The Secretary of State may make rules about the conditions that may be imposed by virtue of this section.

(5) In this section, “**sentence of imprisonment**” includes—

- (a) a detention and training order,
- (b) a sentence of detention in a young offender institution,
- (c) a sentence of detention under section 90 of the [^c10579281]Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000 or section 259 of the Sentencing Code[^c-d7a3737232328c378da922e26583a91e] (detention at Her Majesty’s pleasure),
- (d) a sentence of detention under section 91 of that Act or section 250[^c-bd5075290e377bdb388e3d4165e5930e] or 252A[^c-05c174bfda70dceba091953d804a375e] of that Code (detention of offenders under 18 convicted of certain serious offences),
- (e) a sentence of custody for life under section 93 or 94 of that Act or section 272 or 275 of that Code (including one passed as a result of section 210A, 217, 218A or 219 of the Armed Forces Act 2006)[^c-31427d10763a7b76b66ef87091f7e9ea],,[^c-f587dc4177669c59d97a9ca8a561b202] [^c-dedacf8ee1046a308712776d9f2ae089]...
- (f) a sentence of detention under section 226 , 226B[^c-92d0088e3362e72e61319baf2824e8a5] or 228 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 or section 254 of the Sentencing Code[^c-f4ef3d7df6bcecdd55915acf3b953815] (including one passed as a result of section 221[^c-a5f7f9323db2e12748ccb6206b1b342d] , 221A[^c-6653ea09f4a835164d4d7541fbaa401b] or 222 of the Armed Forces Act 2006)
- (g)[^c-2b0cc899fa7c11c79b496b19116dd1f0] a sentence of detention under section 209 or 218 of the Armed Forces Act 2006, [^c-f575c3c24051fda4e72036409e109bfa]...
- (ga)[^c-93be6622e73384c4007370d49cd55986] a sentence of detention under section 71A(3) or (4) of the Army Act 1955 or the Air Force Act 1955, or section 43A(3) or (4) of the Naval Discipline Act 1957,
- (h) an order under section 211 of the Armed Forces Act 2006[^c-36d84fd3aaffb03e69b8d50e19329991], , and[^c-3e3fcbdda67c881162e8cfe2c56371a4]
- (i) a custodial order under—
  - (i) section 71AA of the Army Act 1955 or the Air Force Act 1955, or section 43AA of the Naval Discipline Act 1957, or
  - (ii) paragraph 10 of Schedule 5A to the Army Act 1955 or the Air Force Act 1955, or paragraph 10 of Schedule 4A to the Naval Discipline Act 1957,

and “**prison**” shall be construed accordingly.

(5A)[^M_F_94b58c93-4496-4a64-8681-b5de1179dcd5] In this section “**electronic monitoring condition**” means a condition requiring the person to submit to either or both of the following—

- (a) electronic monitoring of the person's compliance with another condition of release, and
- (b) electronic monitoring of the person's whereabouts (other than for the purpose of monitoring compliance with another condition of release).
