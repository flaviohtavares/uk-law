## 64. Release on licence etc: drug testing requirements.[^c10579311][^c-7cf25b8d3ec4ba3bb5472e6954ea8236][^M_C_409875bd-3bd0-46af-bf7c-b64ce1a42555][^c-f6cdedfbcdbafbb0dcd05fd7099adf7e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) the Secretary of State releases from prison a person aged 18 or over on whom a sentence of imprisonment has been imposed [^c-a6aec5107fee328baa603118167b77f1]... , and[^c-a1a59feda1d71924c9cace4698dcee1c]
- (b) the release is subject to conditions (whether conditions of a licence or any other conditions, however expressed), [^c-bd112d1c5046bce0d5df4a779f011055]...
- (c)[^c-afce8c0f9a675e3c7c540765bb35729a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^c-b71e4706a0e9b31f8d853dfa462e7480] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Those conditions[^c-69b289c663b9f481e923f646d41ce0ff] may include the following requirement.

(3) The requirement is that the person must provide, when instructed to do so by an officer of a local probation board , an officer of a provider of probation services[^c-b5bd9d6f24401d1346cf8cfab4f70beb] or a person authorised by the Secretary of State, any sample mentioned in the instruction for the purpose of ascertaining whether he has any specified Class A drug or specified Class B drug[^c-2af2058e8b1caf7d0446b16ee16bc814] in his body.

(4) The function of giving such an instruction is to be exercised in accordance with guidance given from time to time by the Secretary of State; and rules[^c-940691a7660223c14ac712994ade40a3] made by the Secretary of State may regulate the provision of samples in pursuance of such an instruction.

(5) In this section, “**sentence of imprisonment**” includes—

- (a) a detention and training order,
- (b) a sentence of detention in a young offender institution,
- (c) a sentence of detention under section 90 of the Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000 or section 259 of the Sentencing Code[^c-09e77012418585dcc3a149c44ca68bb0] (detention at Her Majesty’s pleasure),
- (d) a sentence of detention under section 91 of that Act or section 250[^c-09e5347c8a4b7389262fbed6ddfd5db2] or 252A[^c-85569dd7f03ae6def865c2266a0c0f01] of that Code (detention of offenders under 18 convicted of certain serious offences),
- (e) a sentence of custody for life under section 93 or 94 of that Act or section 272 or 275 of that Code[^c-95e0d8bade2af13b2de8ecffb3f4f6c3],
- (g)[^c-93e1a75216f1e0cb74226d8ca619ce2b] a sentence of detention under section 209 or 218 of the Armed Forces Act 2006, [^c-4d886bf6b0f70d3da35031daeb26c6c3]...
- (ga)[^c-d870d865d4317c412892f17ee47ff4c9] a sentence of detention under section 71A(3) or (4) of the Army Act 1955 or the Air Force Act 1955, or section 43A(3) or (4) of the Naval Discipline Act 1957,
- (h) an order under section 211 of the Armed Forces Act 2006[^c-d3a75fd1c6befa5f2dda144b8b882d19] , and[^c-16fd1cf601866fafe2a0f9fab8fd184e]
- (i) a custodial order under—
  - (i) section 71AA of the Army Act 1955 or the Air Force Act 1955, or section 43AA of the Naval Discipline Act 1957, or
  - (ii) paragraph 10 of Schedule 5A to the Army Act 1955 or the Air Force Act 1955, or paragraph 10 of Schedule 4A to the Naval Discipline Act 1957,

and “**prison**” shall be construed accordingly.
