# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 59

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Remand centres.

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 43(1) of the [^c10579131]Prison Act 1952 (places of detention provided by Secretary of State), paragraph (a) (remand centres) is to cease to have effect.
