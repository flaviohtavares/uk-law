## 70. Interpretation, etc.[^c10579471]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part—

- “[^key-1225e56f55ac49dc28d3f7359cd3c959]**Class A drug**” and “ **Class B drug**” have the same meaning as in the [^c10579481]Misuse of Drugs Act 1971,
- “**specified**”, in relation to a Class A drug or Class B drug[^key-226775337d6d2cd524947c4f6b87627d], means specified by an order made by the Secretary of State,
- “[^key-f2d91dd90a26e8878e791c358888a265]**trigger offence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f2d91dd90a26e8878e791c358888a265]**” has the meaning given by Schedule 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f2d91dd90a26e8878e791c358888a265]

(2)[^key-69905bc85004dbe4acd8392ed7e351c7] The Secretary of State may by order amend Schedule 6 so as to add, modify or omit any description of offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-69905bc85004dbe4acd8392ed7e351c7]

(3) In this Part (except in section 69), references to release include temporary release.

(4)[^key-c484a22319b813261b17cbe0d9d70dca] In section 163 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c484a22319b813261b17cbe0d9d70dca] [^c10579491]Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000 (general definitions), at the appropriate places there are inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c484a22319b813261b17cbe0d9d70dca]

> “**specified Class A drug *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c484a22319b813261b17cbe0d9d70dca]**” has the same meaning as in Part III of the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c484a22319b813261b17cbe0d9d70dca]

,

> “**trigger offence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c484a22319b813261b17cbe0d9d70dca]**” has the same meaning as in Part III of the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c484a22319b813261b17cbe0d9d70dca]

.
