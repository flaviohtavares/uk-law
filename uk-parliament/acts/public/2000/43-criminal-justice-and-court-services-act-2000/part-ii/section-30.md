# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Sections 28 and 29: supplemental.

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1)[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] In sections 28 and this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- “guardianship order” means a guardianship order within the meaning of the Army Act 1955, the Air Force Act 1955, the Naval Discipline Act 1957 or the Mental Health Act 1983, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- “qualifying sentence” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - ((a)) a sentence of imprisonment for a term of 12 months or more, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - ((b)) a sentence of detention in a young offender institution for a term of 12 months or more, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - ((c)) a sentence of detention during Her Majesty’s pleasure, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - ((d)) a sentence of detention for a period of 12 months or more under section 91 of the M5Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000 (offenders under 18 convicted of certain serious offences), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - ((e)) a detention and training order for a term of 12 months or more, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - ((f)) a sentence of detention for a term of 12 months or more imposed by a court-martial or the Courts-Martial Appeal Court, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - ((g)) a hospital order within the meaning of the Mental Health Act 1983, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - ((h)) a guardianship order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- “relevant order” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - ((a)) an order made by the Crown Court, the Court of Appeal, a court-martial or the Courts-Martial Appeal Court that the individual in question be admitted to hospital, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - ((b)) a guardianship order, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- “senior court” means the Crown Court, the Court of Appeal, a court-martial or the Courts-Martial Appeal Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(2) The reference to detention in paragraph (f) of the above definition of “qualifying sentence” includes a reference to detention by virtue of a custodial order under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a) section 71AA of, or paragraph 10 of Schedule 5A to, the Army Act 1955, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b) in relation to an individual on whom a sentence has been passed, or in relation to whom an order has been made, as mentioned in subsection (2) or (3) of section 28 or 29, references to his sentence are to that sentence or order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
