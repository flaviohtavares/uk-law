# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 35

## 35. Persons disqualified from working with children: offences.[^c-c19b720fce5a60673bf663a6b648fb39]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] An individual who is disqualified from working with children is guilty of an offence if he knowingly applies for, offers to do, accepts or does any work in a regulated position. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] An individual is guilty of an offence if he knowingly— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] offers work in a regulated position to, or procures work in a regulated position for, an individual who is disqualified from working with children, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] fails to remove such an individual from such work. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] It is a defence for an individual charged with an offence under subsection (1) to prove that he did not know, and could not reasonably be expected to know, that he was disqualified from working with children. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] An individual is disqualified from working with children for the purposes of this Part if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] he is included (otherwise than provisionally) in the list kept under section 1 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] [^c10578511]Protection of Children Act 1999 (individuals considered unsuitable to work with children), *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c10578521][^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] he is subject to a direction under section 142 of the Education Act 2002 (prohibition from teaching, &c. given on the grounds that he is unsuitable to work with children, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] he is included, on the grounds that he is unsuitable to work with children, in any list kept by the Secretary of State or the National Assembly for Wales of persons disqualified under section 470 or 471 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] [^c10578531]Education Act 1996, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] he is subject to a disqualification order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c10578541][^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] An individual who is guilty of an offence under this section is liable— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or to both, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years, or to a fine, or to both. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
