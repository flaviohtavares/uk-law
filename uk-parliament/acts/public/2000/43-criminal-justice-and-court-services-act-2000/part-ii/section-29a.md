# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 29A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/43

## 29A. Disqualification at discretion of court: adults and juveniles[^c-9b7ac4623c433aeeb48167d3cfbafb8c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] This section applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a) an individual is convicted of an offence against a child (whether or not committed when he was aged 18 or over), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b) the individual is sentenced by a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] superior court[^c-673c8869acd9797ed7690df20eeb543b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (c) no qualifying sentence is imposed in respect of the conviction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(2) If the court is satisfied, having regard to all the circumstances, that it is likely that the individual will commit a further offence against a child, it may order the individual to be disqualified from working with children. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(3) If the court makes an order under this section, it must state its reasons for doing so and cause those reasons to be included in the record of the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
