# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 34

## 34. Restoration of disqualification order.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] If it appears to a chief officer of police or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] a director of social services of a local authority *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-98ff1f17bae6fc5b6eab27bdbe32fa18] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] a director of children’s services of a local authority in England or a director of social services of a local authority in Wales *(Extent: E+W)* *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] that the conditions set out in subsection (2) are satisfied in the case of an individual, the chief officer or (as the case may be) the director may apply to the High Court for an order under this section to be made in respect of the individual. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] The conditions are that— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] a disqualification order made in respect of the individual is no longer in force, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] the individual has acted in such a way (whether before or after the order ceased to be in force) as to give reasonable cause to believe that an order under this section is necessary to protect children in general, or any children in particular, from serious harm from him. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] An application under this section may be made at any time after the disqualification order ceased to be in force. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] If the High Court is satisfied that the conditions set out in subsection (2) are satisfied, it must order that the disqualification order is to be restored; otherwise it must dismiss the application. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] Where an order is made under this section, section 33 has effect with the following modifications— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] in subsection (3), the reference to the individual being under the age of 18 when he committed the offence against a child is to be read as a reference to his being under that age when the order under this section was made, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] in subsections (3)(a) and (4)(a), references to the relevant date are to be read as references to the date on which the order under this section was made, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] in subsection (5)(a), the reference to the individual’s circumstances changing since the disqualification order was made is to be read as a reference to his circumstances changing since the order under this section was made. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] For the purposes of this section a disqualification order is no longer in force if a direction under section 32(3) has been given in respect of it and it is not restored by virtue of an order under this section. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
