## 26. Meaning of “offence against a child”.[^c10578171]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] For the purposes of this Part, an individual commits an offence against a child if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] he commits any offence mentioned in paragraph 1 of Schedule 4, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] he commits against a child any offence mentioned in paragraph 2 of that Schedule, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] he falls within paragraph 3 of that Schedule, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

and references to being convicted of, or charged with, an offence against a child are to be read accordingly. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] The Secretary of State may by order amend Schedule 4 so as to add, modify or omit any entry. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
