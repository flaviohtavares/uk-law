# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/43

## 27. Equivalent armed forces offences.[^c10578181]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] For the purposes of this Part, an individual is treated as being convicted of or (as the case may be) charged with an offence against a child if he is convicted of or charged with an equivalent armed forces offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] In subsection (1), “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]**equivalent armed forces offence *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]**” means *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] an offence under section 42 of the Armed Forces Act 2006[^c-3a9445d928f251a2996aedf745f26594] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] constituted by an act or omission which— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] is an offence against a child, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] would, if committed in England or Wales, be an offence against a child. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a39214c3f5ef0c6f72e04270cc8eadc1][^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] Section 48 of the Armed Forces Act 2006 (attempts, conspiracy, incitement and aiding and abetting outside England and Wales) applies for the purposes of subsection (2) of this section as if the reference in subsection (3)(b) of that section to any of the following provisions of that Act were a reference to subsection (2) of this section. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
