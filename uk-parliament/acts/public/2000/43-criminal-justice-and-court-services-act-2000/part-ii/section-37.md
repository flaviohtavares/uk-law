# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/43

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Disqualification in Scotland or Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] The Secretary of State may by order provide that section 35 shall apply in relation to an individual falling within subsection (2) as it applies in relation to an individual who is disqualified from working with children. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] An individual falls within this subsection if, under the law of Scotland or Northern Ireland, he is subject to a prohibition or disqualification which, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, corresponds to disqualification (by any of the means mentioned in section 35(4)) from working with children. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
