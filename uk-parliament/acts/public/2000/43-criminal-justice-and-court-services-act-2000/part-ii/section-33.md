(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] An individual may only make an application under section 32 with the leave of the Tribunal. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
