# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 29B

## 29B. Subsequent application for order under section 28 or 29[^key-9b7ac4623c433aeeb48167d3cfbafb8c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a) section 28 applies but the court has neither made an order under that section nor complied with subsection (6) of that section, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b) section 29 applies but the court has not made an order under that section, and it appears to the prosecutor that the court has not considered the making of an order under that section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

the prosecutor may at any time apply to that court for an order under section 28 or 29. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(2) Subject to subsection (3), on an application under subsection (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a) in a case falling within subsection (1)(a), the court— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - (i) must make an order under section 28 unless it is satisfied as mentioned in subsection (5) of that section, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] if it does not make an order under that section, must comply with subsection (6) of that section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b) in a case falling within subsection (1)(b), the court— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - (i) must make an order under section 29 if it is satisfied as mentioned in subsection (4) of that section, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] if it does so, must comply with subsection (5) of that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(3) Subsection (2) does not enable or require an order under section 28 or 29 to be made where the court is satisfied that it had considered the making of an order under that section at the time when it imposed the qualifying sentence or made the relevant order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
