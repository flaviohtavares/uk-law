## 31. Appeals.[^c10578431]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] An individual may appeal against a disqualification order— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] where the first condition mentioned in section 28 or 29 is satisfied in his case, as if the order were a sentence passed on him for the offence of which he has been convicted, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] where the second condition mentioned in section 28 or 29 is satisfied in his case, as if he had been convicted of an offence on indictment and the order were a sentence passed on him for the offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

*Extent: E+W*

- (c)[^key-dae35b03dbeef953378dbfc6c0e59a8d] where an order is made under section 29A, as if the order were a sentence passed on him for the offence of which he has been convicted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] In relation to a disqualification order made by *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] the Court Martial[^key-3518d5b071ba387b44b3d4c19c8c832f] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6], subsection (1)(b) has effect as if the reference to conviction on indictment were a reference to conviction by *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] the Court Martial *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
