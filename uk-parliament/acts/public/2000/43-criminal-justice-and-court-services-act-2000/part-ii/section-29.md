# Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/43

## 29. Disqualification from working with children: juveniles.[^c10578211]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] This section applies where either of the conditions set out below is satisfied in the case of an individual. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] The first condition is that— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] the individual is convicted of an offence against a child committed at a time when the individual was under the age of 18, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] a qualifying sentence is imposed by a *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] superior court[^c-56d84e91416ec7ef2fc3270b5db7038c] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] in respect of the conviction. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] The second condition is that— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] the individual is charged with an offence against a child committed at a time when the individual was under the age of 18, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] a relevant order is made by a *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] superior court[^c-ea94cd8341ae8aec7384378919f9a8ad] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] in respect of the act or omission charged against him as the offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] If the court is satisfied, having regard to all the circumstances, that it is likely that the individual will commit a further offence against a child, it must order the individual to be disqualified from working with children. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6] If the court makes an order under this section, it must state its reasons for doing so and cause those reasons to be included in the record of the proceedings. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1cbfebd68c6adb7ae43e71db2ef75e6]
