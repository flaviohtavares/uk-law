# Consolidated Fund Act 2000

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 2002-07-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Consolidated Fund Act 2000 [^c887081] (REPEALED 8.7.2002)

2000 c. 3

**No longer in force (ended 2002-07-08).**

## 1. Issue out of the Consolidated Fund for the year ending 31st March 1999.

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund of the United Kingdom and apply towards making good the supply granted to Her Majesty for the service of the year ending on 31st March 1999 the sum of £37,847,584.45.

## 2. Issue out of the Consolidated Fund for the year ending 31st March 2000.

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund of the United Kingdom and apply towards making good the supply granted to Her Majesty for the service of the year ending on 31st March 2000 the sum of £2,510,712,000.

## 3. Issue out of the Consolidated Fund for the year ending 31st March 2001.

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund of the United Kingdom and apply towards making good the supply granted to Her Majesty for the service of the year ending on 31st March 2001 the sum of £1,000.

## 4. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Consolidated Fund Act 2000.
