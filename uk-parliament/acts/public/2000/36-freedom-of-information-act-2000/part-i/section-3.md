# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Public authorities.[^c20145451][^c20144651][^key-004b3f6163115d8e900d6401bf32e9de]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act “**public authority**” means—

- (a) subject to section 4(4), any body which, any other person who, or the holder of any office which—
  - (i) is listed in Schedule 1, or
  - (ii) is designated by order under section 5, or
- (b)[^c20201111][^c20144801][^key-cee708cddc045e660b3fc85f7f0147bb] a publicly-owned company as defined by section 6.

(2)[^key-bda873f20fa74954dbb9668caa91dd30] For the purposes of this Act, information is held by a public authority if—

- (a) it is held by the authority, otherwise than on behalf of another person, or
- (b)[^c20201191][^c20144891][^c20144851][^key-26a3cbb09bef065b8cbde03423b3ef21] it is held by another person on behalf of the authority.
