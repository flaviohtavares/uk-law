# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Further power to designate public authorities.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-7a6a830831b9eb51068f1f9255dc854e] The Secretary of State[^c19539771] or the[^key-c02eaace255db1045e2b396654ac01a0] Minister for the Cabinet Office[^key-04b3b8b58c91f5cde28964b7c525ee7f] may by order designate as a public authority for the purposes of this Act any person who is neither listed in Schedule 1 nor capable of being added to that Schedule by an order under section 4(1), but who—

- (a) appears to the Secretary of State or the Minister for the Cabinet Office to exercise functions of a public nature, or
- (b) is providing under a contract made with a public authority any service whose provision is a function of that authority.

(2) An order under this section may designate a specified person or office or persons or offices falling within a specified description.

(3)[^key-7a6a830831b9eb51068f1f9255dc854e] Before making an order under this section, the Secretary of State or the[^key-3d09094cc15b5bb5f5141705d93621ce] Minister for the Cabinet Office[^key-2d1d62e5ea9c91ec361c35e9970f89ed] shall consult every person to whom the order relates, or persons appearing to him to represent such persons.

(4) This section has effect subject to section 80.
