# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Model publication schemes.[^c11340911]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commission[^key-8f5f98044fc3570dfbaf73f49a0f114f] may from time to time approve, in relation to public authorities falling within particular classes, model publication schemes prepared by the Commission[^key-426ea260b8eee407420864b189a28a92] or by other persons.

(2) Where a public authority falling within the class to which an approved model scheme relates adopts such a scheme without modification, no further approval of the Commission[^key-843af76bfceb13218747e2da14f0d293] is required so long as the model scheme remains approved; and where such an authority adopts such a scheme with modifications, the approval of the Commission is required only in relation to the modifications.

(3) The Commission[^key-0923b3a545a10db58ef8ec79456664f0] may, when approving a model publication scheme, provide that its[^key-b850c9504196c57d82941a891d7fd6f9] approval is to expire at the end of a specified period.

(4) Where the Commission[^key-96383a7937b6470a8cd4e9e877a936a0] has approved a model publication scheme, the Commission[^key-2832ba0656866dfef7d4b764e3fa4689] may at any time publish, in such manner as the Commission thinks fit, a notice revoking its[^key-e8f02419fb8e231a853057b9dd95e4df] approval of the scheme as from the end of the period of six months beginning with the day on which the notice is published.

(5) Where the Commission[^key-4f9b43eee800588b5f257665d31fcd18] refuses to approve a proposed model publication scheme on the application of any person, the Commission[^key-f799e587b54451e5281ef2220ddaf572] must give the person who applied for approval of the scheme a statement of the reasons for the[^key-31fdd2a8f2e9996545c35d36db8ea07d] refusal.

(6) Where the Commission[^key-8a79dbd7d543dc88fe349b0df9c662a2] refuses to approve any modifications under subsection (2), the Commission[^key-545c9063ceaf781823bb41a45c77feba] must give the public authority a statement of the reasons for the[^key-6533d09a5961a8781698c321b2d997ea] refusal.

(7) Where the Commission[^key-190a8667f7b65a329629dc9f40a6afd7] revokes its[^key-ef117346b1d2bf37fe5c24aa3f537968] approval of a model publication scheme, the Commission[^key-f44a3fa8afba3458ab88523232e692b9] must include in the notice under subsection (4) a statement of its reasons for doing so.
