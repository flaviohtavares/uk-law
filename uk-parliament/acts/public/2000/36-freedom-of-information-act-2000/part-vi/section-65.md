# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Decisions as to refusal of discretionary disclosure of historical records.[^key-e4bd9be908f32ceccc0c3ae336f02600]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before refusing a request for information relating to information which is contained in a historical record and is exempt information only by virtue of a provision not specified in section 2(3), a public authority shall—

- (a)[^key-e0861cb123acac8db12d41882198ceda] if the historical record is a public record within the meaning of the [^c11342031]Public Records Act 1958, consult the Secretary of State[^key-e8b6de1ef29807982211c374a1e7f659], or
- (b) if the historical record is a public record to which the [^c11342041]Public Records Act (Northern Ireland) 1923 applies, consult the appropriate Northern Ireland Minister.

(2) This section does not apply to information to which section 66 applies.
