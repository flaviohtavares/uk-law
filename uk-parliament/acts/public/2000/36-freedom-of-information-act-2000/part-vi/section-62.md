# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Interpretation of Part VI.[^c11341991][^c-e4bd9be908f32ceccc0c3ae336f02600]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-bcbdb83909bb25f48d8687ee02231551] For the purposes of this Part, a record becomes a “**historical record**” at the end of the period of twenty years[^c-5c2174b6a831a7afb0175b914b60dc28] beginning with the year following that in which it was created.

(2) Where records created at different dates are for administrative purposes kept together in one file or other assembly, all the records in that file or other assembly are to be treated for the purposes of this Part as having been created when the latest of those records was created.

(2A)[^c-50d7580476f972c359f73d36a50edda7] Until the end of the period of 10 years beginning with the commencement of paragraph 4 of Schedule 7 to the Constitutional Reform and Governance Act 2010, subsection (1) has effect subject to any order made under section 46(2) of that Act.

(3) In this Part “**year**” means a calendar year.
