# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Prohibitions on disclosure.[^c19642821]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Information is exempt information if its disclosure (otherwise than under this Act) by the public authority holding it—

- (a) is prohibited by or under any enactment,
- (b) is incompatible with any assimilated[^key-f2000e2321f97844632c732657c13aa3][^key-f1b344241a5e93fb3bc5502b290c81f8] obligation, or
- (c) would constitute or be punishable as a contempt of court.

(2) The duty to confirm or deny does not arise if the confirmation or denial that would have to be given to comply with section 1(1)(a) would (apart from this Act) fall within any of paragraphs (a) to (c) of subsection (1).
