## 76. Disclosure of information between Commission and ombudsmen.[^key-94b56cf90e3385f4f2c9c948ba29bdea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c20206161] The Commission[^key-811793bee7e340e89b52425c432edf7d] may disclose to a person specified in the first column of the Table below any information obtained by, or furnished to, the Commission under or for the purposes of this Act or the data protection legislation[^key-e2f50911150f2ee9c4d8a1ad6d589e76] if it appears to the Commission that the information relates to a matter which could be the subject of an investigation by that person under the enactment specified in relation to that person in the second column of that Table.
