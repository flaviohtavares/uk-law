# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 85

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Expenses.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any increase attributable to this Act in the expenses of the Secretary of State[^c19540121] in respect of the Commission[^key-42ab8decee3c71e93bfabb3cc8a4b47a], the Tribunal or the members of the Tribunal,
- (b) any administrative expenses of the Secretary of State or the[^key-4314378fff9dfdb4ca462f9f8e7ab9db] Minister for the Cabinet Office[^key-059840339089270d4765aba5360fd560] attributable to this Act,
- (c) any other expenses incurred in consequence of this Act by a Minister of the Crown or government department or by either House of Parliament, and
- (d) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums which under any other Act are payable out of money so provided.
