# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 83

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Meaning of “Welsh public authority”.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act “**Welsh public authority**” means—

- (a) any public authority which is listed in Part II, III, IV or VI of Schedule 1 and whose functions are exercisable only or mainly in or as regards Wales, other than an excluded authority, or
- (b)[^c19564311] any public authority which is—
  - (i) a subsidiary of the Welsh Ministers (as defined by section 134(4) of the Government of Wales Act 2006), or
  - (ii) a subsidiary of the Assembly Commission (as defined by section 139(4) of that Act).

(2)[^c-fef10a2566f2a544bbd1d30243ea3697] In paragraph (a) of subsection (1) “**excluded authority**” means a public authority which is designated by the Secretary of State[^c19540081] or the[^c-44e027c6fc394d38a706b8471d4c52bf] Minister for the Cabinet Office[^c-42e4fe72fb1e32f262f2d0933d581c6d] by order as an excluded authority for the purposes of that paragraph.

(3)[^c-fef10a2566f2a544bbd1d30243ea3697] Before making an order under subsection (2), the Secretary of State or the[^c-08bd2c2daec6c0832ccdc18e3af7aad4] Minister for the Cabinet Office[^c-4b715a3d6078a418155f0b7cd1e69bf6] shall consult the First Minister for Wales[^c19564331].
