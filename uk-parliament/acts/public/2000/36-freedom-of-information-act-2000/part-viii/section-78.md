# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 78

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Saving for existing powers.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Act is to be taken to limit the powers of a public authority to disclose information held by it.
