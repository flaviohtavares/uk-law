# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 76A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76A. Disclosure between Commission and Scottish Information Commissioner[^c19576911][^key-f7c7336d57d032c9f0a174eddd8b28c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commission[^key-546f050bc3b46ab332b326e68767425f] may disclose to the Scottish Information Commissioner any information obtained or furnished as mentioned in section 76(1) of this Act if it appears to the Commission[^key-5b92e4e1460d637493edbb558bd1204b] that the information is of the same type that could be obtained by, or furnished to, the Scottish Information Commissioner under or for the purposes of the Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act 2002.
