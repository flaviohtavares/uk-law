# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Determination of appeals.[^c20205851][^key-31975c02a4d27e5f2fb892fc4cacaea8][^key-c5a36b489253ccc80ab75300b2e081d0][^key-9c6440f4fbf067aa3c560aaa43e01dde]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If on an appeal under section 57 the Tribunal considers—

- (a) that the notice against which the appeal is brought is not in accordance with the law, or
- (b) to the extent that the notice involved an exercise of discretion by the Commission[^key-d8737606056a23af7192a0a16641bb2a], that the Commission[^key-5f4495ca33badb1eb18a65fa576d3510] ought to have exercised its[^key-0c0cb8b52a7d0b8a0652aaa099fc0a76] discretion differently,

the Tribunal shall allow the appeal or substitute such other notice as could have been served by the Commission[^key-ef6ef5acb18a5c85e14b12e346f7d7bf]; and in any other case the Tribunal shall dismiss the appeal.

(2) On such an appeal, the Tribunal may review any finding of fact on which the notice in question was based.
