# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36

## 47. General functions of Commission.[^c11341762][^c19555401][^key-95e1b297a24dc5bdd1b62c16b9817bf5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It shall be the duty of the Commission[^key-8ba25a9773769281064c5af97df1b814] to promote the following of good practice by public authorities and, in particular, so to perform its[^key-f1c0dc1c05613796f80e3a684b7592db] functions under this Act as to promote the observance by public authorities of—

- (a) the requirements of this Act, and
- (b) the provisions of the codes of practice under sections 45 and 46.

(2) The Commission[^key-029957b1ca3b1d378038140432b0e4b0] shall arrange for the dissemination in such form and manner as the Commission[^key-e8e5d64883bd0805ebc09877fdef7428] considers appropriate of such information as it may appear to the Commission[^key-e568ae9331b7c9292377a7be9aff90fc] expedient to give to the public—

- (a) about the operation of this Act,
- (b) about good practice, and
- (c) about other matters within the scope of the Commission’s[^key-5f1548e6a413ecd8183d2de0bfe0ac4b] functions under this Act,

and may give advice to any person as to any of those matters.

(3) The Commission[^key-b60f5927635de4631edfed9ea02f8696] may, with the consent of any public authority, assess whether that authority is following good practice.

(4) The Commission[^key-9b3824b09dee705d10d77bed096df231] may charge such sums as it[^key-e2a296c5e363052869dcb62d26d8f028] may [^key-57921819e28db7dd8711f67fa2d729e9]... determine for any relevant[^key-f9578dec0ec1e055c69eb08ae0059f5f] services provided by the Commission under this section.

(4A)[^key-64e2bbb0150824ee957b8ab640bb99f0] In subsection (4) “**relevant services**” means—

- (a) the provision to the same person of more than one copy of any published material where each of the copies of the material is either provided on paper, a portable disk which stores the material electronically or a similar medium,
- (b) the provision of training, or
- (c) the provision of conferences.

(4B)[^key-1f758104b545c627e1db3062b16791b8] The Minister for the Cabinet Office[^key-4d820578d0068b65a3db2ea041bb3c0b] may by order amend subsection (4A).

(4C)[^key-1f758104b545c627e1db3062b16791b8] An order under subsection (4B) may include such transitional or saving provision as the Minister for the Cabinet Office considers appropriate.

(4D)[^key-1f758104b545c627e1db3062b16791b8] The Minister for the Cabinet Office must consult the Commission[^key-cf87c7d77c14d1028dba28e69f6e2917] before making an order under subsection (4B).

(5) The Commission[^key-d852fe0944e27a049685e4ad947c2977] shall from time to time as the Commission[^key-51979f41ea3adbadb34f11099badc597] considers appropriate—

- (a) consult the Keeper of Public Records about the promotion by the Commission of the observance by public authorities of the provisions of the code of practice under section 46 in relation to records which are public records for the purposes of the [^c11341781]Public Records Act 1958, and
- (b) consult the Deputy Keeper of the Records of Northern Ireland about the promotion by the Commission of the observance by public authorities of those provisions in relation to records which are public records for the purposes of the [^c11341791]Public Records Act (Northern Ireland) 1923.

(6) In this section “**good practice**”, in relation to a public authority, means such practice in the discharge of its functions under this Act as appears to the Commission[^key-b5fb1980aa3365734cfa0e1e68c0d3ca] to be desirable, and includes (but is not limited to) compliance with the requirements of this Act and the provisions of the codes of practice under sections 45 and 46.
