# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Recommendations as to good practice.[^c11341801][^c19555421]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If it appears to the Commission[^c-e0ab385d59564d542e77382259e60525] that the practice of a public authority in relation to the exercise of its functions under this Act does not conform with that proposed in the codes of practice under sections 45 and 46, the Commission[^c-0981b81a86fc19766e0b88dd4c466567] may give to the authority a recommendation (in this section referred to as a “**practice recommendation**”) specifying the steps which ought in the Commission’s[^c-f7c8a2791950473344726877c52b529e] opinion to be taken for promoting such conformity.

(2) A practice recommendation must be given in writing and must refer to the particular provisions of the code of practice with which, in the Commission’s[^c-5b609a1e40636d40a40ce27dd8b44f30] opinion, the public authority’s practice does not conform.

(3) Before giving to a public authority other than the Public Record Office a practice recommendation which relates to conformity with the code of practice under section 46 in respect of records which are public records for the purposes of the [^c11341811]Public Records Act 1958, the Commission[^c-861b7ccf500c5eff47e97800ea661bc6] shall consult the Keeper of Public Records.

(4) Before giving to a public authority other than the Public Record Office of Northern Ireland a practice recommendation which relates to conformity with the code of practice under section 46 in respect of records which are public records for the purposes of the Public Records Act (Northern Ireland) 1923, the Commission[^c-a64bd6f0b4cebf33f874261352791ce7] shall consult the Deputy Keeper of the Records of Northern Ireland.
