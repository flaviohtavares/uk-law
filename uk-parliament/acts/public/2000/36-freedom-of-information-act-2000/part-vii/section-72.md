> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Availability under Act disregarded for purpose of exemption.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 34 of the [^c11342191]Data Protection Act 1998 (information available to the public by or under enactment), after the word “enactment” there is inserted “ other than an enactment contained in the Freedom of Information Act 2000 ”.
