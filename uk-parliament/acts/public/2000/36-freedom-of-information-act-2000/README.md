Freedom of Information Act 2000

2000 c. 36

An Act to make provision for the disclosure of information held by public authorities or by persons providing services for them and to amend the Data Protection Act 1998 and the Public Records Act 1958; and for connected purposes.

\[30th November 2000\]

Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—[^c11340551][^c-080473f42b96e519c9ae08ad8bc6e909][^c-2d11eb2608b68c0ea9ff1dbb7142f808][^c-3c97fe226f9834a1e98c94a47c269e47][^c-4b520cf0d8042f3c164a08d2b5a902fc][^c-c19e787a3f466590c6ddd1fd25ece712][^c-a0e0b494dec9b2ae93aa2a8d6815e031][^c-1903a4996e82afbf4fd91d97e8797881][^c-b6ab2b761c85282244afb9ee195a0e77][^c-508317457534f1a4ba808f6e40268007]
