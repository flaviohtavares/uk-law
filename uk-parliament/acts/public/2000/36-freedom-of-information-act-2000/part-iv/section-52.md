# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Enforcement notices.[^c20205661][^c11341841][^c-b03d3134da552b8a16bbebf3021019fa][^c-348f03af3d6f68bda2100e92fa58be8f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the Commission[^c-f9cefde959bdbff87927cdaf5514e543] is satisfied that a public authority has failed to comply with any of the requirements of Part I, the Commission may serve the authority with a notice (in this Act referred to as “**an enforcement notice**”) requiring the authority to take, within such time as may be specified in the notice, such steps as may be so specified for complying with those requirements.

(2) An enforcement notice must contain—

- (a) a statement of the requirement or requirements of Part I with which the Commission[^c-d37103b12126cb3f732511603689ad57] is satisfied that the public authority has failed to comply and the[^c-48dad9904472fe66867a3baeb572085e] reasons for reaching that conclusion, and
- (b) particulars of the right of appeal conferred by section 57.

(3) An enforcement notice must not require any of the provisions of the notice to be complied with before the end of the period within which an appeal can be brought against the notice and, if such an appeal is brought, the notice need not be complied with pending the determination or withdrawal of the appeal.

(4) The Commission[^c-d0672a7a0c1d70bfe7cdfee62c557c83] may cancel an enforcement notice by written notice to the authority on which it was served.

(5) This section has effect subject to section 53.
