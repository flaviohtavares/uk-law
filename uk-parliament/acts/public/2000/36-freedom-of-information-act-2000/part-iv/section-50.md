# Freedom of Information Act 2000, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Application for decision by Commission.[^c20205561][^c11341821][^c-2ee91f9eb17bae19e155b336276dd463][^c-73ed776f29e580abc7fd85994f9abf2c][^c-896e855250541cda12f2d05917f0e35e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any person (in this section referred to as “**the complainant**”) may apply to the Commission[^c-ff6b4516d6b332b52e418e739ea25de2] for a decision whether, in any specified respect, a request for information made by the complainant to a public authority has been dealt with in accordance with the requirements of Part I.

(2) On receiving an application under this section, the Commission[^c-f549a9edacf37884a8fb7dd080864362] shall make a decision unless it appears to the Commission[^c-fb452c4f655d452d7247023732bf273d]—

- (a) that the complainant has not exhausted any complaints procedure which is provided by the public authority in conformity with the code of practice under section 45,
- (b) that there has been undue delay in making the application,
- (c) that the application is frivolous or vexatious, or
- (d) that the application has been withdrawn or abandoned.

(3) Where the Commission[^c-b5f8a3c71b8c13e6466ce9c86d829799] has received an application under this section, the Commission[^c-9041b537a5e6addf01414cc1719db075] shall either—

- (a) notify the complainant that it[^c-f3e603f264ac222acbcc818a01bbe440] has not made any decision under this section as a result of the application and of the[^c-54a60ab96274908f3fc1f0460ed4131f] grounds for not doing so, or
- (b) serve notice of its[^c-4c54e7b60ae7e2553469e28a620ac917] decision (in this Act referred to as a “**decision notice**”) on the complainant and the public authority.

(4) Where the Commission[^c-06705bf04882f3bf083c0d3ca1645cd1] decides that a public authority—

- (a) has failed to communicate information, or to provide confirmation or denial, in a case where it is required to do so by section 1(1), or
- (b) has failed to comply with any of the requirements of sections 11 and 17,

the decision notice must specify the steps which must be taken by the authority for complying with that requirement and the period within which they must be taken.

(5) A decision notice must contain particulars of the right of appeal conferred by section 57.

(6) Where a decision notice requires steps to be taken by the public authority within a specified period, the time specified in the notice must not expire before the end of the period within which an appeal can be brought against the notice and, if such an appeal is brought, no step which is affected by the appeal need be taken pending the determination or withdrawal of the appeal.

(7) This section has effect subject to section 53.
