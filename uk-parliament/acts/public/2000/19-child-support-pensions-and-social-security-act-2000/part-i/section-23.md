# Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act 2000, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2016-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Abolition of the child maintenance bonus.[^c21444471]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 10 of the [^c11420911]Child Support Act 1995 (which provides for the child maintenance bonus) shall cease to have effect.
