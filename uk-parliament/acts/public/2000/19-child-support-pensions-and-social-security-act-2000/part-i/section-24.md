## 24. Periodical reviews.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 3(4) of the [^c11420921]Social Security Act 1998 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1998 (which saved section 16 of the 1991 Act for certain purposes) is revoked; and accordingly that section shall cease to have effect for all purposes.
