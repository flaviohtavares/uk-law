## 56. Miscellaneous amendments and alternative to anti-franking rules.[^c18695961]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 5 (which contains miscellaneous amendments of the [^c11421531]Pension Schemes Act 1993 and the [^c11421541]Pensions Act 1995 and makes provision for an alternative to the anti-franking rules in Part III of that Act of 1993) shall have effect.
