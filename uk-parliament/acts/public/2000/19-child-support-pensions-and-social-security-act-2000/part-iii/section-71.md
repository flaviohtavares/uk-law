# Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act 2000, section 71

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2016-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 71. Recovery of housing benefit.[^c18758691]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For subsection (3) of section 75 of the [^c11421901]Social Security Administration Act 1992 (overpayments of housing benefit) there shall be substituted—

> (3) An amount recoverable under this section shall be recoverable—
>
> - (a) except in such circumstances as may be prescribed, from the person to whom it was paid; and
> - (b) where regulations so provide, from such other person (as well as, or instead of, the person to whom it was paid) as may be prescribed.
