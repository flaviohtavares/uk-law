> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Some defined terms.[^c13164451]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part—

- [^c-6b6d0aab0afbbde66bdae22facf19889]...
- **“local authority in Wales[^c-c1dd5918c70c604c8c039675a42afdd2]**” has the same meaning as in the Education Act 1996 (see section 579(1) of that Act);
- “**the National Assembly**” means the National Assembly for Wales.

(2) In this Part, any reference to the Chief Inspector for Wales is to be read as a reference to Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales or Prif Arolygydd Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru[^c-4ff413fa4d530e2b16a40efc7b83d19f].
