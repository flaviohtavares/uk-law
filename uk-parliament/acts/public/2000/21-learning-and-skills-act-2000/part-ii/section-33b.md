## 33B. Local curricula: Welsh language[^c-09b200202f5599d4298d1b101dd2f764]

*Extent: E+W*

The Commission[^c-5269a0b2ca014c4bb0dac87aa8c9db12] must exercise its functions in relation to local curricula so as to promote access to and availability of courses of study which are taught through the medium of the Welsh language.
