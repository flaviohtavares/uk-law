## 33J. Planning the local curriculum[^c-3ce3f5c2d28b2f0a006de211986c347c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following persons must assist the Commission[^c-ef528ead680a74d64de8d8eb61000a6e] in planning the local curriculum or curricula for a local authority's area—

- (a) the local authority;
- (b) the governing body and head teacher of any maintained school maintained by the authority;
- (c) the governing body and principal of an institution in the authority's area.

(2) In subsection (1), “ **planning the local curriculum or curricula** ” means the process by which the Commission decides[^c-77837720e27063a01667a3d2e3ee5ff7] under section 33A which courses of study to include in the local curriculum or curricula.
