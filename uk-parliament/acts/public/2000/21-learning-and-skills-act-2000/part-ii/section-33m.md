# Learning and Skills Act 2000, section 33M

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33M. Power to amend learning domains[^key-08ad75c262d793055b8b2705c1d26b0f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-8f64273726e6e34c9707d073bdaea70d] The Welsh Ministers may by order—

- (a) amend or omit any paragraph of subsection (3) of section 33A;
- (b) add additional paragraphs to that subsection;
- (c) amend or omit such additional paragraphs.

(2)[^key-2f8239c320a872b426394639e7c23150] The Welsh Ministers must consult the Commission before making an order under subsection (1).
