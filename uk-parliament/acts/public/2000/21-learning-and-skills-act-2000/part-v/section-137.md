# Learning and Skills Act 2000, section 137

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 137. Recreation and social and physical training.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 508 of the [^c13165391]Education Act 1996 (local education authorities: recreation and social and physical training) shall be amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1) for “secondary and further education” substitute “ and secondary education ”.

(3) After subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) A local authority[^c-35207b0165f4bf8683c697e9c46b3e8b] may provide facilities for recreation and social and physical training as part of the facilities for further education provided (whether or not by them) for their area.

(4) In subsection (2) for “For that purpose” substitute “ For the purpose of subsection (1) or (1A) ”.
