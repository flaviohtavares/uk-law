> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/21

## 140. Assessments relating to learning difficulties: Wales[^c13165451][^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69][^key-4f6bcd11093b58282313ff42cfa92680]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subsection (2) applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69]

- (a) a local authority[^key-35207b0165f4bf8683c697e9c46b3e8b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] in Wales[^key-6af087b1ba5117dce7422f8c15b2c2e9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] maintains a statement of special educational needs for a person under section 324 of the Education Act 1996, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69]
- (b) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] Welsh Ministers believe[^key-2e287fdc95bf6ddcf9e5a1ebce606c6c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] that the person will leave school at the end of his last year of compulsory schooling to receive post-16 education or training (within the meaning of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] Part 2[^M_F_bc490d0c-9a0c-4034-f522-655259967085] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] of this Act) or higher education (within the meaning of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] [^c13165461]Education Reform Act 1988). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69]

(2) The Welsh Ministers[^key-3adf328691f522bc1f1d0170abf57456] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] must arrange for an assessment of the person to be conducted at some time during the person’s last year of compulsory schooling. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69]

(3) The Welsh Ministers[^key-18356cf9e535c51d7d65a6ebf9f06291] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] may at any time arrange for an assessment to be conducted of a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69]

- (a) who is in his last year of compulsory schooling or who is over compulsory school age but has not attained the age of 25, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69]
- (b) who appears *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] to the Welsh Ministers[^key-f4503942a2c8ca047efa561f317cf120] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] to have a learning difficulty (within the meaning of section 13), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69]
- (c) who is receiving, or in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] opinion of the Welsh Ministers[^key-91bbbd09089cfbca8821fff157ead17e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] is likely to receive, post-16 education or training (within the meaning of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] Part 2 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] of this Act) or higher education (within the meaning of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] [^c13165471]Education Reform Act 1988). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69]

(4) For the purposes of this section an assessment of a person is an assessment resulting in a written report of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69]

- (a) his educational and training needs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69]
- (b) the provision required to meet them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69]

(5) A local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] in Wales[^key-a395a633dffc68cbe943d0214af58169] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] must send a copy of a statement maintained by it under section 324 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] [^c13165481]Education Act 1996 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69] Welsh Ministers on their request[^key-0afab60e430be07052de9423aa52781a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69].

(5A)[^key-841f9cc551e84ddc1cabea1a54c13d6f] “Local authority in Wales” has the same meaning as in the Education Act 1996 (see section 579(1) of that Act). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a232a2c65ae75ccb11451fb24cd1a69]
