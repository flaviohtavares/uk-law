# Learning and Skills Act 2000, section 138

## 138. Wales: provision of information by public bodies.[^c13165401]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purpose of the provision in Wales of services mentioned in subsection (2), any of the persons or bodies mentioned in subsection (3) may supply information about a young person—

- (a) to a local authority;
- (b) to any other person or body involved in the provision of the services.

(2) The services are—

- (a) services provided in pursuance of section 123 of this Act,
- (b) services provided in pursuance of any of sections 2, 8, 9 and 10 of the [^c13165411]Employment and Training Act 1973 (training and careers services), and
- (c) services wholly or partly funded in pursuance of section 12 of the [^c13165421]Industrial Development Act 1982 (careers in industry).

(3) The persons and bodies are—

- (a) a local authority,
- (b)[^c-bcdca075b3b1c7c0edbe0e2d2f850afb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (ba)[^M_F_4687ad2c-ff16-4a15-919c-588ae0023bbb] a Local Health Board,
- (c) the National Assembly for Wales[^c-3a66a2c617c6c1f1ef01b1692982362a],
- (ca)[^c-6e1bc7bc0744c9ceb3eb5cce4b9e8889] the Commission for Tertiary Education and Research;
- (d) a chief officer of police,
- (e) a probation committee,
- (ea)[^c-21fbbee03845e0438e08fd6526f5a885] a probation trust,
- (eb) a provider of probation services (other than a probation trust or the Secretary of State), in carrying out its statutory functions or activities of a public nature in pursuance of arrangements made under section 3 of the Offender Management Act 2007, and
- (f) a youth offending team.
