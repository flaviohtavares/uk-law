# Learning and Skills Act 2000, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Enforcement[^key-c1a5b924409094dcd292d506ebfdb7f3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section has effect for the purposes of section 96[^M_F_1adfebf6-c324-4414-c2d7-3aeeb39b0aac] [^key-b69a620d17d46d45e9c13f97450864eb]..., and it applies if the Secretary of State is satisfied that—

- (a) a local authority[^key-35207b0165f4bf8683c697e9c46b3e8b] or specified body has failed to comply with section 96(2)(a) or is proposing to do so, or[^key-9dcc548ab078e3d8dacb1cc99c9f2689]
- (b) a local authority or governing body has failed to comply with section 96(3) or is proposing to do so, [^key-3344a98ea433a4ba139e59d6a8a2fa72]...
- (c)[^key-3344a98ea433a4ba139e59d6a8a2fa72] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The Secretary of State may give such directions to the authority or body as he thinks fit.

(3) An authority or body must comply with any directions given to it under this section.

(4) A specified body is a body specified under section 100(1)(c).
