# Learning and Skills Act 2000, section 129

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 129. Supplementary.[^c13165241]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In sections 123 to 128—

- “**local authority**” means a county council or a county borough council,
- “[^c-161897ce829ca6c15f3e5823f65193d6]**Local Health Board**” has the meaning given by section 11 of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006,
- [^c-e9f0cb53ab1da24af7a7b8f9f0e3375f]...
- “**parent**”, in relation to a child, means a person who has parental responsibility for him within the meaning of section 3 of the [^c13165261]Children Act 1989,
- “**probation committee**” means a committee established under section 3 of the [^c13165271]Probation Service Act 1993,
- [^c-306c350ae98bc577f898929b8ffc8525]...
- “**young person**” has the meaning given by section 123(3),
- “**youth offending team**” means a team established under section 39 of the [^c13165291]Crime and Disorder Act 1998, and
- “**youth support services**” has the meaning given by section 123(2).

(2) The power under section 123 shall not relate to services which are provided or to be provided outside Wales.
