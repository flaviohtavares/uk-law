# Trustee Act 2000, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-05-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Existing trusts.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part does not apply in relation to—

- (a) a trust of property which consists of or includes land which (despite section 2 of the [^c1055518]Trusts of Land and Appointment of Trustees Act 1996) is settled land, or
- (b) a trust to which the [^c1055519]Universities and College Estates Act 1925 applies.

(2) Subject to subsection (1), this Part applies in relation to trusts whether created before or after its commencement.
