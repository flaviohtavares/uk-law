# Trustee Act 2000, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-05-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Authorised unit trusts.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Parts II to IV do not apply to trustees of authorised unit trusts.

(2)[^M_X_ed1f46e4-2952-43c1-893f-569915e786a3] “**Authorised unit trust**” means a unit trust scheme in the case of which an order under section 78 of the [^c1055535]Financial Services Act 1986 is in force.
