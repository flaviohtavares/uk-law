# Trustee Act 2000, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-05-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Act—

- “**asset**” includes any right or interest;
- “**charitable trust**” means a trust under which property is held for charitable purposes and “**charitable purposes**” has the meaning given by section 11 of the Charities Act 2011[^c-dc59cef53601515c7b7123bfb871dc18];
- “**custodian trustee**” has the same meaning as in the [^c1055538]Public Trustee Act 1906;
- “**enactment**” includes any provision of a Measure of the Church Assembly or of the General Synod of the Church of England;
- “**exempt charity**” has the same meaning as in the Charities Act 2011[^c-381b5366793c5c54269905c1b5752725] ;
- “**functions**” includes powers and duties;
- “**legal mortgage**” has the same meaning as in the [^c1055540]Law of Property Act 1925;
- “**personal representative**” has the same meaning as in the [^c1055541]Trustee Act 1925;
- “**settled land**” has the same meaning as in the [^c1055542]Settled Land Act 1925;
- “**trust corporation**” has the same meaning as in the [^c1055543]Trustee Act 1925;
- “**trust funds**” means income or capital funds of the trust.

(2) In this Act the expressions listed below are defined or otherwise explained by the provisions indicated—

| custodian | section 17(2) |
| the duty of care | section 1(2) |
| the general power of investment | section 3(2) |
| lay trustee | section 28(6) |
| power of intervention | section 22(4) |
| the standard investment criteria | section 4(3) |
| subordinate legislation | section 6(3) |
| trustee acting in a professional capacity | section 28(5) |
| trust instrument | sections 6(2) and 35(2)(a) |
