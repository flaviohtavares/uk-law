## 216. Assumption of certain functions of the Office of Rail and Road.[^c14882731][^key-3808bee45113681055337238c012f2fe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 17 makes provision for the transfer to the Authority of certain functions of the Office of Rail and Road[^key-86163f1b5efaeca7ec4585e1a95807c8] and of associated property, rights and liabilities.
