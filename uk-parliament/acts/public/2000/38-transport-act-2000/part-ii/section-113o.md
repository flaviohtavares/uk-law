# Transport Act 2000, section 113O

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 113O. Guidance about schemes[^c-5ac2fb72b7756b61b7770f242893b4cc][^c-c05adb8597f2acc398b3fefbd54794ff][^c-2fdf823590288d7b7cd91a6009704f7d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may issue guidance concerning the carrying out by local transport authorities whose areas are in England and local highway authorities[^c-05d5f0e0d85cdb38fca618f84b9f82c6] of their functions under this Part in relation to advanced quality partnership schemes.

(2) Those authorities and councils must have regard to any such guidance.
