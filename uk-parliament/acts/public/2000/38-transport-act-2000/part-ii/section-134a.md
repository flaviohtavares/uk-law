# Transport Act 2000, section 134A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/38

## 134A. Guidance about schemes[^key-c05adb8597f2acc398b3fefbd54794ff][^key-03b7bf52c2928e7c1b7cc37411695304]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The appropriate national authority may issue guidance concerning the performance by local transport authorities of their functions under this Part in relation to quality contracts schemes.

(2) Those authorities must have regard to any such guidance.
