# Transport Act 2000, section 134F

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 134F. Effect of scheme[^c-926b818f80b9729b35c156e0b3bb59e3][^c-2850519bb6ef94e31a825d5132bc4dc8][^c-c05adb8597f2acc398b3fefbd54794ff][^c-e31b873c69e07bc52340dd07efdc0caf]

*Extent: E+W*

During any period in which an advanced ticketing scheme is in operation, operators of local services to which the scheme relates must make and implement the arrangements required by the scheme.
