## 112. Plans and strategies: supplementary.[^c14880791][^key-48922f5e08ab1d88e498c7b9b7466880][^key-2f37502a7b77afc92ab46047e781dbfa][^key-b069c4289f8b15ecb2b733627728edce][^key-f9a8685da116b8b1ca23180938af8fa4][^key-c05adb8597f2acc398b3fefbd54794ff][^key-4f00553ac73c18aa9914c53dc7536991][^key-0bc0873501ff951a62dcebbf35d0f3b0][^key-cd4ef9c00e26b4cb3b2e5822c389fab1][^key-622229e8cffe3b997450d535b289aa47][^key-63131c26ca98554bd7e4a82be39decdd][^key-38655cb458b97a6dd21ff07e03d03432][^key-4d6da790ee3fb92444037d40b72b3801][^key-ba53aa819dfa66b6ca6177e0cd938ee3]

*Extent: E+W*

[^key-f9a8685da116b8b1ca23180938af8fa4]

(1) In carrying out their functions under sections 108 to 109B[^key-21ffd1bbfd1c94e9442d80fcb01b0c32], a local transport authority must have regard to any guidance concerning—

- (a) the content of local transport plans [^key-11d336ff2024ad2fb0dd147890dd58f0]...,
- (b) the preparation of such plans [^key-11d336ff2024ad2fb0dd147890dd58f0]...,
- (c) the alteration and replacement of such plans [^key-11d336ff2024ad2fb0dd147890dd58f0]..., and
- (d) the publication and making available of such plans [^key-11d336ff2024ad2fb0dd147890dd58f0]... as originally made and as altered or replaced,

which is issued from time to time by the Secretary of State (as respects England) or the National Assembly for Wales (as respects Wales).

(2) In developing and implementing[^key-947a999b23732879483b1d4208df7295] their policies under section 108(1) [^key-0a6550bbe5d7aa4b9feb23bdce4e8c6b]..., a local transport authority must have regard to the transport needs of disabled persons (within the meaning of the[^key-712c5a671db98143c7dc3a923e7a7ae3] Equality Act 2010[^key-73b10510cfe33ea73267e35ea12b8385]) and of persons who are elderly or have mobility problems.

(3)[^key-cde0f40cf13bcf35307434a6e095a2cd] Where a local transport authority is a mayoral combined authority, references in this section to the local transport authority are to the mayor acting on behalf of the combined authority.

(4) Where a local transport authority is a mayoral combined county authority, references in this section to the local transport authority are to the mayor acting on behalf of the combined county authority.
