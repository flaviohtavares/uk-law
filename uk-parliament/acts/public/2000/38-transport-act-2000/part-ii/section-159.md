# Transport Act 2000, section 159

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 159. Abolition of financial plans of Passenger Transport Executives.[^c14881841][^c-afa4d55676cdde2fa792f5bd827fd9fe][^c-c05adb8597f2acc398b3fefbd54794ff][^c-ba53aa819dfa66b6ca6177e0cd938ee3]

*Extent: E+W*

[^c-f9a8685da116b8b1ca23180938af8fa4]

Sections 3 to 5 of the [^c14881851]Transport Act 1983 (duty of Passenger Transport Executives to prepare three-year financial plans and determination of revenue grants) shall cease to have effect.
