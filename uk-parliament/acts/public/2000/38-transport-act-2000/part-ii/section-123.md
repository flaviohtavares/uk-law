# Transport Act 2000, section 123

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 123. Guidance about schemes.[^c14880941][^c-5ac2fb72b7756b61b7770f242893b4cc][^c-c05adb8597f2acc398b3fefbd54794ff]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The appropriate national authority may issue guidance concerning the carrying out by local transport authorities and local highway authorities[^c-6b04eaf8f3ecb98f431b6b2be05c48fe] of their functions under this Part in relation to quality partnership schemes.

(2) Those authorities and councils must have regard to any such guidance.
