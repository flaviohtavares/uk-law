## 146. Mandatory concessions: supplementary.[^c14881451][^key-5d4abb4b4c4c4ae281ef2b4f8f91b487][^key-4c15e05157adc6fadfbb9b6f8ffc8c5e][^key-ac8f01c2d4ea3a1d02caefd88f5eee4d][^key-c05adb8597f2acc398b3fefbd54794ff]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-4854323a0b0dbaecbe78455bd1877e0a]

- (a) In this Part—
  - “**disabled person**” means a person who—
    - (a) is blind or partially sighted,
    - (b) is profoundly or severely deaf,
    - (c) is without speech,
    - (d) has a disability, or has suffered an injury, which has a substantial and long-term adverse effect on his ability to walk,
    - (e) does not have arms or has long-term loss of the use of both arms,
    - (f) has a learning disability, that is, a state of arrested or incomplete development of mind which includes significant impairment of intelligence and social functioning, or
    - (g) would, if he applied for the grant of a licence to drive a motor vehicle under Part III of the [^c14881461]Road Traffic Act 1988, have his application refused pursuant to section 92 of that Act (physical fitness) otherwise than on the ground of persistent misuse of drugs or alcohol,
  - “[^key-71559896e3f403618c5db49549b46f20]**elderly person**” means a person who has attained the age of 60 years,
  - “**eligible service**” means—
    - (a) [^c14881481]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
    - (b) [^c14881481]... a bus service of a class specified in an order made by the Secretary of State (as respects England) or the National Assembly for Wales (as respects Wales),
  - [^M_F_ca71134d-692c-4bee-a514-e385dfe6e29f]“**a half-price travel concession**”, in relation to a journey, means reduction of the fare for the journey to not more than one-half of that applicable to an adult who is not entitled to any reduction,
  - “[^key-d9b3f367e68039d43fc34e76266471b0]**London authority**” means a London borough council or the Common Council of the City of London,
  - “**relevant time**” means—
    - (a) any time on a Saturday or Sunday or on any day which is a bank holiday in England and Wales under the [^c14881501]Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971, or
    - (b) a time during the period from 9.30 a.m. to 11 p.m. on any other day, and
  - “**travel concession authority**” means—
    - (a) a council of a non-metropolitan district in England comprised in an area for which there is no county council,[^key-3f9f2823e0ff8018b7c70573b33794dc]
    - (b) a county council in England,[^key-d679ebc75c0e5cccc70727a3dc48895d]
    - (ba) a London authority,[^key-7d224a502e7ec2a2ee10615e70776a49]
    - (c) a Passenger Transport Executive for an integrated transport area[^key-b86af225f12de2201da34617c745852b] in England, [^key-39003800c58dc6cecddcc0833c2855f6]...
    - (cza) a combined authority,[^key-c1d90a763154890b1377ceb415ab2b6e]
    - (czb) a combined county authority,
    - (ca) the Council of the Isles of Scilly, or[^key-aece4eeedb6db8e40a04b0c7b040f5f9]
    - (d) a county council or county borough council in Wales,

(2)[^key-f7a315d99f112b9699931e95ca1ffd3c] A county council or a council of a non-metropolitan district is not a travel concession authority for the purposes of this Part where—

- (a) the council is a constituent council of a combined authority or a combined county authority (and here “**constituent council**” has the meaning given by section 104(11) of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 in relation to a combined authority and section 10(11) of the Levelling-Up and Regeneration Act 2023 in relation to a combined county authority), and
- (b) the combined authority or combined county authority has completed its first full financial year.
