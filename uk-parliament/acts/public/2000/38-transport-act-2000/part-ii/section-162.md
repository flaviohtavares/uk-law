## 162. Interpretation of Part II.[^c14881881][^key-7af3605e8cc67070a3553ab7ded9f2a0][^key-afa4d55676cdde2fa792f5bd827fd9fe]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part—

- “[^key-9f418e73df9b3914ec4d8e15f4298a8a]**advanced quality partnership scheme**” is to be construed in accordance with section 113C(2),
- “[^key-8abf9383afbae502e60a014dfb2d700f]**advanced ticketing scheme**” is to be construed in accordance with section 134C(3),
- “**appropriate national authority**”, in relation to a quality partnership scheme, a quality contracts scheme or a ticketing scheme, means—
  - (a) [^key-e989684f4e9fc04ed2afcf636549dc82]...
  - (b) the National Assembly for Wales, as respects a scheme relating to an area in Wales, or
  - (c) the Secretary of State and the National Assembly for Wales acting jointly, as respects a scheme relating to an area in England and Wales,
- “[^key-9993bef4a1f41fbc11c1ceef0888fe01]**bus services**” means services using public service vehicles,
- “**community bus permit**” means a permit under section 22 of the [^c14881891]Transport Act 1985,
- “**connecting rail or tram service[^key-f9981b68a3eb64af27fa08213a9cf7de]**”—
  - (a) in relation to an advanced ticketing scheme, has the meaning given by section 134C(3), and
  - (b) in relation to a ticketing scheme, has the meaning given in section 135(5),
- “[^key-efc17b537944a35737168ad997d36182]**council in Scotland**” means a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994,
- “**disabled person**” has the meaning given in section 146,
- “[^key-56650868a360910ca0d3ba3b9156c9ec]**effective time**”, in relation to a local service contract, has the meaning given by section 123J(7),
- “**elderly person**” has the meaning given in section 146,
- “**eligible service**” has the meaning given in section 146,
- “enhanced partnership plan” and “enhanced partnership scheme” have the meaning given by section 138A,[^key-6565ff17eef54fe5a47add18068efe49]
- “[^key-a48c0b13b3f4999e8f15704d5748241d]**exempt continuation proposal**” is to be read in accordance with section 131B,
- “**franchising authority**” has the meaning given by section 123A(4),
- “**franchising scheme**” is to be construed in accordance with section 123A(3),
- [^M_F_7110a5df-d820-4961-b325-c4196310a19e]“**half-price travel concession**” has the meaning given in section 146,
- “**interim service**” has the meaning given by section 123O,
- “[^key-c3aa29bea69269f6745d080cbd9eeda9]**local highway authority**” has the meaning given in section 329(1) of the Highways Act 1980,
- “**local service contract**” has the meaning given by section 123A(5),
- “**local transport authority**” has the meaning given in section 108(4),
- “[^key-c6d46113bfb313bf4027043ed2602f40]**local transport policies**” has the meaning given in section 108(5),
- “[^key-ed6fa2912bff6bd83b83df7ab6572347]**London authority**” has the meaning given in section 146,
- “**London transport authority**” means the Greater London Authority, a London borough council or the Common Council of the City of London,
- “[^key-f8a8275d25e1af9b90a606cf31b5ae46]**mayoral CCA**” has the meaning given by section 27(8) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023;
- “**mayoral combined authority**” has the meaning given by section 107A of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009,
- [^key-ab8f73a4997e38493dd700a35af68e2d]...
- “**non-mayoral CCA**” means a combined county authority that is not a mayoral CCA,
- “**non-mayoral combined authority**” means a combined authority that is not a mayoral combined authority,
- “**quality contract**” has the meaning given in section 124(4),
- “**quality contracts scheme**” is to be construed in accordance with section 124(3),
- “**quality partnership scheme**” is to be construed in accordance with section 114(2),
- “**railway**” and “**tramway**” have the meanings given in section 67(1) of the [^c14881901]Transport and Works Act 1992,
- “**relevant time**” has the meaning given in section 146,
- “**scheme sub-area**” has the meaning given by section 123H,
- “**service permit**” has the meaning given by section 123P,
- “**ticketing scheme**” is to be construed in accordance with section 135(3),
- “**traffic regulation order**” means an order under the [^c14881911]Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 or any other enactment (other than this Act) regulating the use of roads or other places by public service vehicles, and
- “**travel concession authority**” has the meaning given in section 146.

(2) In this Part the expressions listed below have the same meaning as in the [^c14881921]Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981—

- “**fares**”,
- “**modification**”,
- “**public service vehicle**”,
- “**PSV operator’s licence**”,
- “**road**”, and
- “**traffic commissioner**”.

(3) In this Part the expressions listed below have the same meaning as in the [^c14881931]Transport Act 1985—

- “**local service**”,
- “**public passenger transport services**”,
- “**stopping place**”, and
- “**traffic area**”.

(4) Where a reference to an authority in any of the following provisions is to an Integrated Transport Authority[^key-4518009d6fa9256823c58d077cc2854a], it is to be construed as a reference to the Passenger Transport Executive for the integrated transport area[^key-001b6df6455466ef979bd2cec7b7f0c9] concerned; and where a reference to authorities in any of those provisions is to one or more Integrated Transport Authorities[^key-cf8faafd8e1f7ed1b9df55455c2cd5ce], it is to be construed as a reference to the Passenger Transport Executive or Executives for the integrated transport area or areas concerned—

- section 114(2)(a),
- section 116(2)(a), (4)(a), (4A)(a) and, in the second place, (4C), *(Extent: E)*[^key-d6c3312bbec95e4a16ded681bbbfc88b]
- section 118(1) and (2),
- [^key-93880e406e9326ed8e9d4defd7c1ef6b]...
- [^key-93880e406e9326ed8e9d4defd7c1ef6b]...
- [^key-93880e406e9326ed8e9d4defd7c1ef6b]...
- [^key-b50af050ab4c039c2c9b49a664d77de5]...
- [^key-93880e406e9326ed8e9d4defd7c1ef6b]...
- [^key-93880e406e9326ed8e9d4defd7c1ef6b]...
- [^key-93880e406e9326ed8e9d4defd7c1ef6b]...
- [^key-93880e406e9326ed8e9d4defd7c1ef6b]...
- section 139(5), and
- section 140.

(4A)[^key-e8539476c2eb06d1398c43be8572e022] Where a reference to an authority in any of the following provisions is to an Integrated Transport Authority, it is to be construed as including a reference to the Passenger Transport Executive for the integrated transport area concerned—

- section 153(2)(a),
- in Schedule 10, paragraph 17(5)(b) and (8).

(5) References in this Part to Integrated Transport Authorities and Passenger Transport Executives[^key-98e4ab3ebe7c8a487930d3499705c865] and to integrated transport areas[^key-ec5dde3b811fc9526ff848a7599b3907] are references respectively to the Integrated Transport Authorities and Passenger Transport Executives, and to integrated transport areas, for the purposes of Part II of the [^c14881941]Transport Act 1968.

(5A)[^key-bf639c5174726f075a7cfd9b515fa739] In this Part “**combined authority**” means a combined authority established under section 103 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009.

(5B)[^key-ab2f77ef2ff2bd90eb2ae8199c0d6ced] In this Part “**combined county authority**” means a combined county authority established under section 9(1) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023.

(6)[^key-a347d37fae2aed23c850ccf5aa78832e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) References in this Part to the operator of a passenger transport service of any description are to be construed in accordance with section 137(7) of the [^c14881951]Transport Act 1985.
