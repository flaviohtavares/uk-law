# Transport Act 2000, section 129

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 129. Effect of scheme.[^c14881011][^c-c05adb8597f2acc398b3fefbd54794ff]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) During any period in which the scheme , or (in the case of a scheme which provides for different provisions to come into operation on different dates) any provision of the scheme,[^c-6b9b104b85ca6358f3035306d43f35a2] is in operation—

- (a) sections 6 to 9 of the [^c14881021]Transport Act 1985 (registration of local services) do not have effect in relation to the area to which the scheme, or that provision, relates[^c-dd01cf34b61f068466d97f620be36581], and
- (b) no local service shall be provided in that area (if there is a stopping place for the service in that area) unless it is provided under a quality contract or is an interim service (see section 132C)[^c-fcb84edeeec643e8db4b51fa2320e775].

(2) But subsection (1) does not apply —[^c-955edb7d94dd05269e652a18d5b7c803]

- (a) so as to prevent the application of sections 6 to 9 of the Transport Act 1985 in relation to any service by virtue or in consequence of section 6B of that Act (application for registration or variation where quality contracts scheme in force),
- (b) so as to prevent the provision of any service registered under section 6 of the Transport Act 1985 by virtue of section 6B of that Act, or
- (c) in relation to services which are excluded from the scheme as a result of any provision of the scheme made in accordance with section 127(4).

(3) Where the exclusion of a local service from the scheme is made subject to conditions as a result of such a provision, those conditions are to be treated, during any period in which the scheme is in operation, as if they were prescribed particulars registered under section 6 of the [^c14881031]Transport Act 1985 of the service concerned.

(4)[^c-d14d23e8a9818ae17cf54f562bf225f0] The authority or authorities must invite tenders in accordance with section 130 not later than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d14d23e8a9818ae17cf54f562bf225f0]

- (a) three months, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d14d23e8a9818ae17cf54f562bf225f0]
- (b) such other period as the appropriate national authority may by order specify, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d14d23e8a9818ae17cf54f562bf225f0]

after the scheme has been made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d14d23e8a9818ae17cf54f562bf225f0]
