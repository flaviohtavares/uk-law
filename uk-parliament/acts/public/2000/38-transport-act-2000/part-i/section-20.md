## 20. Enforcement[^c-2bee5318dd61eb7b7a3f10b2995f3147]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule B1 makes provision for—

- (a) the enforcement of the duties imposed by section 8 and licence conditions, and
- (b) connected appeals.
