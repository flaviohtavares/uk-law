# Transport Act 2000, section 85

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Interpretation of Chapter V.[^c14879951]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Chapter—

- (a)[^c-b0a5d162ceb0a728d78bf2cedeb7c214] the 2002 Act is the Enterprise Act 2002;
- (b) the 1998 Act is the [^c14879971]Competition Act 1998;
- (c)[^c-5efb6df2b5d91081339cf89f1f54297d] the CMA is the Competition and Markets Authority.

(2) For the purposes of this Chapter these expressions have the meanings given by section 40—

- (a) aerodrome;
- (b) licence;
- (c) licence holder.

(3) If an expression is used in this Chapter and also in [^c-99e7fa3f17a55ee2f4c1884a6f632029]... the 1998 Act it has the same meaning in this Chapter as it has in the 1998 Act[^c-61ee1627fe936dbade23cef90e72c491].
