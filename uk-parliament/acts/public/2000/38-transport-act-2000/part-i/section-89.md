# Transport Act 2000, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/38

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Carrying out functions.[^c14880231]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this section the 2002[^c-1c8da1a1d81a8f9e50be1d9e68477084] Act functions are the functions mentioned in subsection (2) of section 86 which, by virtue of that section, are concurrent functions of the CAA and the[^c-be4e1b8314a9dfc62e7e1c167933db3f] CMA.

(2) Before the CMA or the CAA first carries out the 2002 Act functions in relation to a matter it[^c-8b7a44d534f47f1c7a0b7ed981adc948] must consult the other.

(3) If the CMA or the CAA has carried out the 2002 Act functions in relation to a matter the other must not carry out the 2002 Act functions in relation to the matter.

(4) If in carrying out the 2002 Act functions the CAA makes a market investigation reference (under section 131 of the[^c-4c0af1e7e26e62ffea363ce2c40e29e5] 2002 Act), to help the CMA[^c-1d68b880b7782b6a697a33ac9b57f12c] group (constituted under Schedule 4 to the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013) which is to conduct the investigation on the reference the CAA must give to it—

- (a) any information the CAA has which relates to matters within the scope of the investigation and which the CMA[^c-4448b707d04109c7165a21d69d16eb8f] group requests;
- (b) any information the CAA has which relates to matters within the scope of the investigation and which the CAA thinks it would be appropriate for it to give without a request;
- (c) any other help which the CAA is able to give in relation to matters within the scope of the investigation and which the CMA[^c-b9c2ba4c97ea7a4869a2893b6ce7b0f3] group requests.

(5) In carrying out the investigation concerned the CMA[^c-83d2366c5b5abc613a1a56a3bdb73a56] group must take account of any information given under subsection (4).
