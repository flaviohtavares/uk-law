## 180. Joint local licensing schemes.[^c14882221][^c-37e500a8e7eab52221dcd8feb6d784b6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A joint local licensing scheme may cover the whole or any part of the combined area of the licensing authorities.

(2) A joint local licensing scheme may only be made if it appears desirable for the purpose of directly or indirectly facilitating the achievement of local transport policies of the licensing authorities[^c-743222e96555b2aa1a220c7a6ad79703].
