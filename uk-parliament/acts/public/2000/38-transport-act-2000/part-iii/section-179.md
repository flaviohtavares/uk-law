# Transport Act 2000, section 179

## 179. Local licensing schemes.[^c14882211][^c-2218be6cb984928f78199ee46a7ccf89]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local licensing scheme may cover the whole or any part of the area of the licensing authority.

(2) A local licensing scheme may only be made if it appears desirable for the purpose of directly or indirectly facilitating the achievement of local transport policies of the licensing authority[^c-7a77acb71029cfd59f0004e446d74a90].
