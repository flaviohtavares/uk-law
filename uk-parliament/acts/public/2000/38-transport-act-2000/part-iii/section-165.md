# Transport Act 2000, section 165

## 165. Joint local charging schemes.[^c14881991][^c-921d8e913e23c4108bd9478e8f36ece8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A joint local charging scheme may only be made in respect of roads for which any of the charging authorities are the traffic authority.

(2) A joint local charging scheme which has effect wholly outside an integrated transport area[^c-991b5957a96ab88e721b726f772c90a3] , the area of a combined authority or the area of a combined county authority[^c-da1c6b2378dabdcbc60a5bbc477bf22f] may only be made if it appears desirable for the purpose of directly or indirectly facilitating the achievement of local transport policies of the charging authorities[^c-12c6bcaf5758e461c8850be806954cec].

(3)[^c-24d8fbc8c022d903174d35789ddf1d88] A joint local charging scheme which has effect wholly or partly within an integrated transport area , the area of a combined authority or the area of a combined county authority[^c-32b492fe7b087be4208d35c73ebe20d5] may only be made if it appears desirable for the purpose of directly or indirectly facilitating the achievement of local transport policies of—

- (a) the charging authorities, and
- (b) the Integrated Transport Authority for the integrated transport area or (as the case may be) the combined authority[^c-cb5b37817326f087adeb1177d98260f9] or combined county authority[^c-8c3dae17d5914fab557916827a7898c4].

(4) Section 164(4) has effect for the purposes of this section.
