# Transport Act 2000, section 164

## 164. Local charging schemes.[^c14881981][^key-d9e25aded09239ae4c694679a81227f5][^M_C_a79a48df-780c-4556-c0e5-439b1df7dc1c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local charging scheme may only be made in respect of roads for which the charging authority are the traffic authority.

(2) A local charging scheme which has effect wholly outside an integrated transport area[^key-598b772050f82df969291103220a35ea] , the area of a combined authority or the area of a combined county authority[^key-8250cea1fba1548b5ec0581bacc13296] may only be made if it appears desirable for the purpose of directly or indirectly facilitating the achievement of local transport policies of the charging authority[^key-0649126c8c130d8ad216d61ac8c161b2].

(3)[^key-7138a1bafa3e08d1d3537c13f97a38f9] A local charging scheme which has effect wholly within an integrated transport area , the area of a combined authority or the area of a combined county authority[^key-54ed4aa526b80eacfc43d294cddd659f] may only be made if it appears desirable for the purpose of directly or indirectly facilitating the achievement of local transport policies of—

- (a) the charging authority, and
- (b) the Integrated Transport Authority for the integrated transport area or (as the case may be) the combined authority[^key-1bd2bf8890045c17ad66028509d2bcf8] or combined county authority[^key-68e9b405fe23668e326a79edb2f6987b].

(4) For the purposes of this section and sections 165 and 166—

- (a) a charging scheme has effect wholly outside an integrated transport area if none of the roads in respect of which it is made is in such an area;
- (b) any reference to a charging scheme which has effect wholly, or partly, within an integrated transport area is to be read accordingly.
