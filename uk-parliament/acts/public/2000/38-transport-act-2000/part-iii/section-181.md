## 181. Joint local-London licensing schemes.[^c14882231][^c-94bd9fbc9a1f49d43384f444d5350395]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A joint local-London licensing scheme may cover—

- (a) the whole or any part of the area of the non-metropolitan local traffic authority, or combined area of the non-metropolitan local traffic authorities, by which it is made, and
- (b) the whole or any part of any area to which a scheme under Schedule 24 to the [^c14882241]Greater London Authority Act 1999 made by the London traffic authority, or any of the London traffic authorities, by which it is made could apply.

(2) A joint local-London licensing scheme may only be made if it appears desirable for the purpose of directly or indirectly facilitating the achievement of—

- (a) local transport policies[^c-31a4e6c113205bd9f49dc28b40c535f6] of the non-metropolitan local traffic authority, or [^c-ff3d433aefa4f392e19afe16395b62d8]... the non-metropolitan local traffic authorities, by which it is made, and
- (b) policies and proposals set out in the transport strategy prepared and published by the Mayor of London under section 142 of the [^c14882251]Greater London Authority Act 1999.
