# Transport Act 2000, section 169

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/38

## 169. Confirmation of charging schemes.[^c14882061]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A charging scheme under this Part which relates wholly or partly to Wales[^key-c09ad983390db48909f3cee174b80798], other than a trunk road charging scheme, shall not come into force unless the order making it has been submitted to and confirmed by the Welsh Ministers[^key-c8c8303f4ba386634bdd2ba236ce1e3a]; and a variation of such a charging scheme shall not take effect until the order making the variation has been so submitted and confirmed.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply in such circumstances as may be specified in or determined in accordance with regulations made by the Welsh Ministers[^key-b2fc0e1b0bdb182c5e30afebf60b41fb].

(3) A joint local-London charging scheme or joint ITA-London charging scheme[^key-2440d98c7a0e4c36fa613256746e0e9c] shall not come into force unless the order making it has been submitted to and confirmed by the Greater London Authority; and a variation or revocation of such a charging scheme shall not take effect until the order making the variation or revocation has been so submitted and confirmed.

(4) Where confirmation of an order is required by this section, the order may be confirmed with or without modifications.
