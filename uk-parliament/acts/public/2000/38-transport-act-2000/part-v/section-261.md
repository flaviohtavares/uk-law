# Transport Act 2000, section 261

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 261. Increase of fine for breach of obligation to hold operator’s licence.[^c14883431]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In section 2(5) of the [^c14883441]Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995 (obligation to hold operator’s licence: penalty for offence), for “level 4” substitute “ level 5 ”.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to any offence committed before this section comes into force.
