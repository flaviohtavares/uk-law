# Transport Act 2000, section 263

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 263. Addition of specified vehicles to operator’s licence.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 5 of the [^c14883471]Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995 (vehicles authorised to be used under operator’s licence), for subsection (6) substitute—

> (6) A motor vehicle which is not specified in an operator’s licence is not authorised to be used under that licence by virtue of subsection (1) on or after the relevant day[^key-e26fa6ef7e620aa1762d2be3f3978bd1] unless the licence-holder—
>
> - (a) has given to a traffic commissioner[^key-c03bd27c45ffc52e1060d1c1bfdcb62f] a notice in such form and containing such information about the vehicle as is required by a traffic commissioner, and
> - (b) has paid the prescribed fee (if any) to a traffic commissioner.
>
> (6A)[^key-0b0ed41da416ec3bacc5703ebb29f60f] For the purposes of subsection (6) “the relevant day” is the latest of the following days—
>
> - (a) the day on which the vehicle was first in the lawful possession of the licence holder,
> - (b) the day on which the licence came into force,
> - (c) if a day not more than one month after the later of those days is prescribed for the purpose, the day so prescribed.
