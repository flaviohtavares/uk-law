# Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000, section 83

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Short title, commencement and extent.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000.

(2)[^c10569261] The provisions of this Act, other than this section, shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint; and different days may be appointed under this subsection for different purposes.

(3) This Act extends to Northern Ireland.
