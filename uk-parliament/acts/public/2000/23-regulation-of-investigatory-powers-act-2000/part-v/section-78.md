# Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000, section 78

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/23

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Orders, regulations and rules.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to any power of the Secretary of State or the Lord Chancellor[^c-0199b2961aef0d8b19b2872d7566e126] to make any order, regulations or rules under any provision of this Act.

(2) The powers to which this section applies shall be exercisable by statutory instrument.

(3) A statutory instrument which contains any order made in exercise of a power to which this section applies (other than the power to appoint a day under section 83(2)) but which contains neither—

- (a) an order a draft of which has been approved for the purposes of section [^c-85a53b440295b89ec779d6f8ab5aeeba]... [^c-371f26c7cd74f29c5eba8eee5d3343a3]... 28(5), 29(6), 30(7), 32A(7),[^c-e7701e5173a0c8437f6e1217a1a7019b] 35(5), 41(6), 47(2), 66(3), 67(11) , 71(9) or 76A(9)[^c19875801] nor
- (b) the order to which section 35(7) applies,

shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(4) A statutory instrument containing any regulations made in exercise of a power to which this section applies (other than regulations under section 67A(5))[^c-b12fd4c61fa0e06c019d62cb142d67b2] shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(4A)[^c-99256712cb268842098113da2ec6dfab] A statutory instrument containing regulations under section 67A(5) may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

(5) Any order, regulations or rules made in exercise of a power to which this section applies may—

- (a) make different provisions for different cases;
- (b) contain such incidental, supplemental, consequential and transitional provision as the Secretary of State or (as the case may be) the Lord Chancellor[^c-e137dcd406fbe794f508386b8e8be2c4] thinks fit.
