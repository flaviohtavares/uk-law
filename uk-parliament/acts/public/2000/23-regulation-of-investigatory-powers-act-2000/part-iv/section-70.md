# Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Abolition of jurisdiction in relation to complaints.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The provisions set out in subsection (2) (which provide for the investigation etc. of certain complaints) shall not apply in relation to any complaint made after the coming into force of this section.

(2) Those provisions are—

- (a) section 5 of, and Schedules 1 and 2 to, the [^c10568491]Security Service Act 1989 (investigation of complaints about the Security Service made to the Tribunal established under that Act);
- (b) section 9 of, and Schedules 1 and 2 to, the [^c10568501]Intelligence Services Act 1994 (investigation of complaints about the Secret Intelligence Service or GCHQ made to the Tribunal established under that Act); and
- (c) section 102 of, and Schedule 7 to, the [^c10568511]Police Act 1997 (investigation of complaints made to the Surveillance Commissioners).
