# Care Standards Act 2000, section 4

## 4. Other basic definitions.[^c12426031]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section has effect for the purposes of this Act.

(2) “**Residential family centre**” means, subject to subsection (6), any establishment in England[^c-d961b32bdcef865b5828a7969f14c2db] at which—

- (a) accommodation is provided for children and their parents;
- (b) the parents’ capacity to respond to the children’s needs and to safeguard their welfare is monitored or assessed; and
- (c) the parents are given such advice, guidance or counselling as is considered necessary.

- In this subsection “**parent**”, in relation to a child, includes any person who is looking after him.

(3) “**Domiciliary care agency**” means, subject to subsection (6), an undertaking which consists of or includes arranging the provision of personal care in their own homes for persons in England[^c-918d5ded2b49e6af7c43fd0d4f74e911] who by reason of illness, infirmity or disability are unable to provide it for themselves without assistance.

(4) “**Fostering agency**” means, subject to subsection (6)—

- (a) an undertaking which consists of or includes discharging functions of local authorities in England[^c-476d9ade24c4438a73e7f83c818f36ba] in connection with the placing of children with foster parents; or
- (b) a voluntary organisation in England[^c-a3d46fb8be783a95a060262a0ee4466b] which places children with foster parents under section 59(1) of the 1989 Act.

(5)[^M_F_8c54a78f-2faa-42c1-a443-3052d9587dc7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) The definitions in subsections (2) to (5) do not include any description of establishment, undertaking or organisation excepted from those definitions by regulations.

(7) “**Voluntary adoption agency**” means an adoption society within the meaning of the the Adoption and Children Act 2002[^c21088151] which is a voluntary organisation within the meaning of that Act whose principal office is in England[^c-0baa99450e0ced090980d643efbbb7dd].

(7A)[^c21087981] “**Adoption support agency**” means an undertaking in England which is an adoption support agency within[^c-cedca873e1d736601cb0617812e50a97] the meaning given by section 8 of the Adoption and Children Act 2002.

(8) Below in this Act—

- (a)[^c21088211] any reference to a description of establishment is a reference to—
  - (i) a children's home in England[^c-6fb1a196c9ddca3908d6884924509894],
  - (ii) a children's home in England[^c-5c6069e1f1e3462dd4aa79a4e1271514] providing accommodation for the purpose of restricting liberty,
  - (iii) an independent hospital in Wales,
  - (iv) an independent hospital in Wales in which treatment or nursing (or both) are provided for persons liable to be detained under the Mental Health Act 1983,
  - (v) an independent clinic in Wales,
  - (vi)[^c-bacc97a6a094651a79802da89120e63b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (vii) a residential family centre in England[^c-7073bfe17a9714eaa2bae876c43c591b];
- (b) a reference to any establishment is a reference to an establishment of any of those descriptions.

(9)[^c21088231] Below in this Act—

- (a) any reference to a description of agency is a reference to—
  - (i) an independent medical agency in Wales or, where the activities of an independent medical agency are carried on from two or more branches, a branch in Wales of an independent medical agency,
  - (ii)[^c-784072e4beb0aa763c4567d28d4f7f41] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii)[^c-784072e4beb0aa763c4567d28d4f7f41] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iv) a fostering agency or, where the activities of a fostering agency are carried on from two or more branches, a branch of a fostering agency,
  - (v) a voluntary adoption agency, or
  - (vi) an adoption support agency or, where the activities of an adoption support agency are carried on from two or more branches, a branch of an adoption support agency;
- (b) a reference to any agency is a reference to an agency or branch of any of those descriptions.
