# Care Standards Act 2000, section 3

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Care homes in England[^c12426021][^key-9c516c161300b1895ac32be20aabc9bd]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of this Act, an establishment in England[^key-9a7cd1a759ab637b57c14a6a0fc663df] is a care home if it provides accommodation, together with nursing or personal care, for any of the following persons.

(2) They are—

- (a) persons who are or have been ill;
- (b) persons who have or have had a mental disorder;
- (c) persons who are disabled or infirm;
- (d) persons who are or have been dependent on alcohol or drugs.

(3)[^key-0d609023f9089f13d721e3b51e9e2978] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c21150721] And an establishment [^key-b5f4baed4efca08402891e2ca81717da]... is not a care home if it is—

- (a) a hospital (within the meaning of the National Health Service Act 2006); or
- (b) a children's home,

or if it is of a description excepted by regulations.
