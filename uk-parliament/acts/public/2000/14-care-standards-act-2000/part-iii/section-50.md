# Care Standards Act 2000, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Annual returns.[^c12426661]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulations may require a local authority to make to the CIECSS[^key-44458bfb7abfecf41d4c99e6150d4c8c] an annual return containing such information with respect to the exercise by the local authority of relevant functions as may be prescribed.

(2) Provision may be made by the regulations as to the period in respect of which and date by which the return is to be made.
