# Care Standards Act 2000, SCHEDULE 2B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/14

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2B — Persons whose arrangements are subject to review by the Commissioner under section 73.[^c12428731][^c-525a933248fb5fbdf9f2510e27a4f306][^c12428721]

*Extent: E+W*

**1.** The Welsh Ministers, the First Minister for Wales or the Counsel General to the Welsh Assembly Government[^c21129581].

**2.** Any county council or county borough council in Wales.

**3.** Any Health Authority for an area in, or consisting of, Wales.

3A Any Local Health Board for an area in, or consisting of Wales[^c21159471]

**4.** Any National Health Service trust all or most of whose hospitals, establishments and facilities are situated in Wales.

**5.** [^c21129651]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**6.** Any further education corporation within the meaning of section 17(1) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 (c. 13) which conducts an institution in Wales.

**7.** Any higher education corporation within the meaning of section 90(1) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 which conducts an institution in Wales.

**8.**[^c-aeb6355b55c678901562368cbfb48274] Any tertiary education provider (other than one within paragraph 6, 7, 9 or 10) that is registered in a category specified in regulations made for the purposes of section 88 or 105 of the Tertiary Education and Research (Wales) Act 2022.

**9.** Any institution in Wales in relation to which a designation under section 28 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 has effect, which on the date the designation took effect was an institution falling within subsection (2)(a) of that section.

**10.** Any governing body of a school in Wales which is a maintained school within the meaning of section 20(7) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 (c. 31).

**11.**[^c-00d805771553efc94a04c47d1c152a5d] The Commission for Tertiary Education and Research.
