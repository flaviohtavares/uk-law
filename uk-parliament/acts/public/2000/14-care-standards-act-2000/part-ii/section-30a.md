## 30A. Notification of matters relating to persons carrying on or managing certain establishments or agencies[^c21152901]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-e6d34a33bf9b916f2674a1135e704e2a][^key-33d1d09458e2b3b76fadb75e60967219][^key-062fbcc33fbf273c262302271a59d423] This section applies where a person (“P”) is carrying on or managing an establishment or agency in England[^key-dae0d33cf6a01a37d2f2ebee0dbbbfd1] mentioned in subsection (6).

(2) If the CIECSS[^key-8cfa3cc6ecc3140285131333ce2f52f7] —

- (a)[^key-211d6e8d4652f2544a2acbc6c0b8a12b] has decided to adopt a proposal under section 17(4)(a) to cancel the registration of P in respect of the establishment or agency,
- (aa)[^key-211d6e8d4652f2544a2acbc6c0b8a12b][^c21152921] has decided to adopt a proposal under section 17(4)(aa) to suspend the registration of P in respect of the establishment or agency or to extend any such suspension,
- (ab) has given a notice under section 20B to suspend the registration of P in respect of the establishment or agency or to extend any such suspension,
- (b)[^key-f670ebfbda477bcd0581d930f4986962] has brought proceedings against P for a relevant offence which it alleges P committed in relation to the establishment or agency, [^c21152961]..,
- (ba)[^key-fda36731127fe6e9ba3776c0b4631134] has served on P, or a parent undertaking of P, a penalty notice under paragraph 3 of Schedule 1A (monetary penalties);
- (c) has served a notice on P under section 22B [^key-0a4ed5b0d3b001f55d5c45deb5b18e52]...
- (d)[^key-bc19c7ae3e30c7a29b1f7a7b66c141ed] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

it must as soon as practicable notify each local authority in England and Wales of that fact.

(3)[^key-a4e96abefa7a135e4c7ff535f30840c3][^key-33d1d09458e2b3b76fadb75e60967219][^key-03023ba83c31783d1c18759bb0e9b94c] If the CIECSS[^key-dcd2e0f22a6ae7d937ee2d22abc2b0f7] becomes aware of any prescribed circumstances which relate to P it must as soon as practicable notify each local authority in England and Wales of those circumstances.

(4)[^key-ce28df2e154ef36fadabff8990ba4c7c][^key-33d1d09458e2b3b76fadb75e60967219][^key-2a713e71450ccbe7d6f4c5e0a6b281cd] A notification under this section must contain such information as may be prescribed.

(5) A notification under this section may be transmitted to a local authority electronically if—

- (a) the local authority has agreed that notifications may be given to them by being transmitted to an electronic address and in an electronic form specified in the agreement; and
- (b) the notification is a notification to which that agreement applies.

(6) The establishments and agencies are—

- (a) a children's home;
- (b) a residential family centre;
- (c) a fostering agency;
- (d) a voluntary adoption agency;
- (e) an adoption support agency;
- (f)[^key-351da5a5d54b41018a4db9c02fad0007] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) In this section—

- “**electronic address**” includes any number or address used for the purposes of receiving electronic communications;
- “**electronic communication**” means an electronic communication within the meaning of the Electronic Communications Act 2000 the processing of which on receipt is intended to produce writing;
- “**electronically**” means in the form of an electronic communication;
- “**relevant offence**” means an offence under—
  - (a) this Part;
  - (b) regulations under this Part;
  - (c) section 9(4) of the Adoption Act 1976;
  - (d) regulations under section 9 of the Adoption and Children Act 2002;
- “**prescribed**” means prescribed by regulations made—
  - (a) in relation to England, by the Secretary of State;
  - (b) [^key-3fd1e8f3c8b3d075c2a46ed1c6c972bb]...
