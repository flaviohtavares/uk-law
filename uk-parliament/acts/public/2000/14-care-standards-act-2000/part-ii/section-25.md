# Care Standards Act 2000, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Contravention of regulations.[^c12426311]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c21094351][^c-3b05c3d1f26b61db1260671def9226a8][^c-b388f49dc4e1de2aeb27653bebe7817d][^c-18dc859b3baa9db01b3ca778f7c32617] Regulations under this Part may provide that a contravention of or failure to comply with any specified provision of the regulations shall be an offence.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under the regulations shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.

(2A)[^c-5879cb87645f8473622b85ea10637104] Subsection (1) does not apply to regulations under section 30ZK or 30ZL (power to limit profits).
