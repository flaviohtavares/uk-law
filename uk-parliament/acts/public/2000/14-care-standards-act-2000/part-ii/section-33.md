# Care Standards Act 2000, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Annual returns.[^c12426411][^c21097261][^c-75b249567a4c864ee3f851a761478f8c][^c-3d7bce267f53dac554f71667ee3eb887][^c-66f6d151e6f8631cdf9e044591c8c5c1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulations may require the person carrying on an establishment or agency to make an annual return to the registration authority.

(2) Provision may be made by the regulations as to the contents of the return and the period in respect of which and date by which it is to be made.
