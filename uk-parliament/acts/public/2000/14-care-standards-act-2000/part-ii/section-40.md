> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/14

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Temporary extension of meaning of “children’s home”.[^c12426531]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 63(3)(a) of the 1989 Act (meaning of “children’s home”), for “more than three children at any one time” there shall be substituted “ children ”.
