# Care Standards Act 2000, section 72A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72A. Principal aim of the Commissioner[^c12426851][^c12426841]

*Extent: E+W*

The principal aim of the Commissioner in exercising his functions is to safeguard and promote the rights and welfare of children to whom this Part applies.
