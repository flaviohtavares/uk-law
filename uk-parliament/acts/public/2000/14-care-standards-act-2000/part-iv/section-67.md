## 67. Functions of the Secretary of State.[^c12426781][^c-f0ad3e766bdd8964a4fa184320d46bb2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State[^c-566ac01bcfc60309d0c860f9ad8002f5] has the function of—

- (a) ascertaining what training is required by persons who are or wish to become social care workers;
- (b) ascertaining what financial and other assistance is required for promoting such training;
- (c) encouraging the provision of such assistance;
- (d) drawing up occupational standards for social care workers.

(1A)[^c-ebd5f2ae4d746be54c5808ddbec5f060] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-dfa9999dc27f348122c2d08c85c6e8bd] The Secretary of State shall encourage persons to take part in—

- (a)[^c-54a3f430552049643e19b00d1854e927] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) [^c-635c3f1634b80f83155948c38bcf72a6]...courses relevant to the training of persons who are or wish to become social care workers.

(3) If it appears to the Secretary of State[^c-a5bb8cb6526e0617dbf9725fec60c21f] that adequate provision is not being made for training persons who are or wish to become social care workers, the Secretary of State[^c-b1b8eb9a21dd2c7db5dffa7316b04a5d] may provide, or secure the provision of, courses for that purpose.

(4) The Secretary of State[^c-f17002b9d53aa8b88fc64c9bbffd22d6] may, upon such terms and subject to such conditions as he or she[^c-2c2cbffff91a3f926dbb390847b0cb24] considers appropriate—

- (a) make grants, and pay travelling and other allowances, to persons resident in England [^c-2297b3ee95542f8af202ca28c7693383]... in order to secure their training in the work of social care workers;
- (b) make grants to organisations providing training in the work of social care workers.

(5) Any functions of the Secretary of State under this section—

- (a)[^c-9fc8ce060b44a2cb9268a33fbbefb252] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) may be exercised by any person, or by employees of any person, authorised to do so by the Secretary of State.

(6)[^c-b85f136d36bfa8a4c5afde95ca535b5f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) For the purpose of determining—

- (a) the terms and effect of an authorisation under subsection (5)(b) [^c-a89fa5b601995532f44ef15a9529a89f]...; and
- (b) the effect of so much of any contract made between the Secretary of State[^c-0d49a922d6007ac534f9eeb0f76ead39] and the authorised person as relates to the exercise of the function,

Part II of the [^c12426791]Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994 shall have effect as if the authorisation were given by virtue of an order under section 69 of that Act and in subsection (5)(b)[^c-f2cd4cf9344fcf42b38cb7f613046c08] “**employee**” has the same meaning as in that Part.
