# Care Standards Act 2000, section 68

## 68. Appeals to the Tribunal.[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf][^c21129041][^key-3530f343916ed38ffbcbf282a5a2ac17]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An appeal against a decision of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf] the Welsh Council[^key-ffcf620374b576435745b9aab9cf8ee1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf] under this Part in respect of registration shall lie to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf]

(1A)[^c21129081] An appeal shall lie to the Tribunal against a decision of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf] the Welsh Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf] under Part 3 of the General Systems Regulations in respect of an aptitude test, or period of adaptation, in connection with a person's becoming permitted, by virtue of that Part of those Regulations, to have access to, and to pursue, the profession of social worker in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf]

(1B)[^key-9c4b818b761195eba02254f795579495] An appeal shall lie to the Tribunal against a decision of the Welsh Council under Part 3 of the General Systems Regulations in respect of an aptitude test, or period of adaptation, in connection with a person becoming permitted, by virtue of that Part of those Regulations, to have access to, and to pursue, the profession of social care manager in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf]

(1C) An appeal shall lie to the Tribunal against a decision of the Welsh Council under regulation 67 of the General Systems Regulations to send an alert about a person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf]

(2) On an appeal against a decision, the Tribunal may confirm the decision or direct that it shall not have effect *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf] or, in the case of an appeal under subsection (1C), direct that the alert be withdrawn or amended[^key-1e6e6c5f1caabc592a201628fb4280d7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf] .

(3) The Tribunal shall also have power on an appeal against a decision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf]

- (a) to vary any condition for the time being in force in respect of the person to whom the appeal relates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf]
- (b) to direct that any such condition shall cease to have effect; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf]
- (c) to direct that any such condition as it thinks fit shall have effect in respect of that person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-314f693f79afa8c3257bb154b8e84abf]
