# Care Standards Act 2000, section 110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 110. Extension of Part IX to school children during holidays.[^c12427691]

*Extent: E+W*

In paragraph 9(1) of Schedule 8 to the 1989 Act (extension of Part IX to certain school children during holidays), “which is not maintained by a local education authority” shall be omitted.
