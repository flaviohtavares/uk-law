# Care Standards Act 2000, section 112

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Charges for local authority welfare services.[^c12427741]

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 1 to the [^c12427751]Local Authority Social Services Act 1970 (enactments conferring functions assigned to social services committee), there shall be inserted at the appropriate place—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Health and Social Services and Social Security Adjudications Act 1983 (c. 41)<br>Section 17, so far as relating to services provided under the enactments mentioned in subsection (2)(a) to (c) | Charges for local authority welfare services |

.
