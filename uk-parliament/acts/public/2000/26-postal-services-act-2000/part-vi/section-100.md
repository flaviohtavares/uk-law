# Postal Services Act 2000, section 100

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100. Certain exemptions from postage etc.[^c10291901]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-4dce6c256b8eeed131615b7a4267cb06] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-4dce6c256b8eeed131615b7a4267cb06] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-4dce6c256b8eeed131615b7a4267cb06] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Subsection (5) applies where the postage chargeable by a postal operator[^c-033f1857e7c6979c5a10c36dc7c2d545] in connection with the provision of postal services[^c-eb53134cd6570b7e22cc92d6599488fa] on a postal packet consisting of parliamentary proceedings has not been prepaid by the sender or has been insufficiently prepaid by him.

(5) No sum exceeding the amount of the postage or (as the case may be) the deficiency may be recovered by the postal operator[^c-c124ac8474d47b4176706b622adc5323] (whether from the sender or the addressee).

(6) In this section “**parliamentary proceedings**” means proceedings of either House of Parliament, the Scottish Parliament, the National Assembly for Wales or the Northern Ireland Assembly.
