# Postal Services Act 2000, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/26

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Common carriers.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A postal operator[^key-45c5a73e10059bb95182f3b545fee2d8] shall not be regarded as a common carrier so far as he is providing postal services[^key-0974cad53f8b973b8776117a2b80b9e6].
