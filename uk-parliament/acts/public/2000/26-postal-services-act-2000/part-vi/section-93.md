# Postal Services Act 2000, section 93

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Power to modify sections 89 to 92.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by order modify sections 89 to 92.

(2) Before making an order under subsection (1), the Secretary of State shall consult OFCOM[^key-e8f35d81bd00a6e91b0d3631bbda148d], Citizens Advice, Citizens Advice Scotland,[^key-9b51889fae906e8d33c7ff85b92b7c96] Consumer Scotland,[^key-79921ab86664f2b4610a8709f1793ff1] the GCCNI and such other persons as he considers appropriate.
