## 64. Government investment in securities of certain companies.[^c-a644c0555e860495dacd4a702a0ee5f8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Treasury or, with the consent of the Treasury, the Secretary of State may at any time acquire securities of—[^c-8c9969c50724151478d8bcf13a74a23f]

- (a) a Post Office company,
- (b) a Royal Mail company, or
- (c) a company in the same group as a company within paragraph (a) or (b).

(1A)[^c-4c9179025bedd03938cd9a166d9e0140] Securities acquired under this section may be held by a nominee.

(2) The Secretary of State shall not, without the consent of the Treasury, dispose of any securities acquired by him in pursuance of this section.
