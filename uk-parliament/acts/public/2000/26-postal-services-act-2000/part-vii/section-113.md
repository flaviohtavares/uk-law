# Postal Services Act 2000, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 113. Recoupment of losses on wrongly paid money orders.[^c10292121][^c-0eaba5d78ec83fd71d6953652fe87e7e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where—

- (a)[^c10292131] a money order issued by a Post Office company[^c-2d9d60f3ee2f99ea5360c56bcba72c64] or a foreign postal administration has been delivered for collection to a banker, and
- (b) a Post Office company (“[^c-9e2b864585cd2377344bb6e57e657f60]**the company**”) has paid the order to the banker when it should not have done so.

(2) The sum paid may be deducted from sums subsequently falling to be paid by the company[^c-d37ca1b2b6b6dd638f164ccd85d76e40] company to the banker by way of payment of money orders so issued which have been delivered to the banker for collection.
