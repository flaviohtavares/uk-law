# Postal Services Act 2000, section 130

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 130. Commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c10292451] Parts I to VI, sections 101 to 114, sections 116 to 119 (including Schedule 7) and section 127(4) and (6) (including Schedules 8 and 9) shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint; and different days may be appointed for different purposes or different areas.

(2) Section 115 shall come into force at the end of the period of two months beginning with the day on which this Act is passed.
