# Postal Services Act 2000, section 114

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Special arrangements with other countries or territories.[^c10292141][^c-0eaba5d78ec83fd71d6953652fe87e7e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) References in sections 111 (other than subsection (4)), 112 and 113 to money orders issued by a Post Office company[^c-f8c27aaacabea2244c20ce7ad6397051] shall be construed as including references to special money orders issued by that company.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1) special money orders are orders for the payment of money in the United Kingdom which are issued by a Post Office company[^c-1158405e60e3f43b6a00db6c9345005a] in pursuance of an arrangement made with a government or postal administration of any other country or territory for the transmission, by means of orders for the payment of money, of sums of money through post offices under the charge of the company[^c-3336ebd4a9f56074873d8c6e83a06d83] and the postal administration of the other country or territory.

(3) The reference in section 112(2)(b) to money orders shall be construed as including a reference to orders for the payment of money issued in pursuance of an arrangement of the kind mentioned in subsection (2) above by the postal administration of the other country or territory.
