# Terrorism Act 2000, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Temporary legislation.[^c11204521]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following shall cease to have effect—

- (a) the [^c11204531]Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989, and
- (b) the [^c11204541]Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1996.

(2) Schedule 1 (which preserves certain provisions of the 1996 Act, in some cases with amendment, for a transitional period) shall have effect.
