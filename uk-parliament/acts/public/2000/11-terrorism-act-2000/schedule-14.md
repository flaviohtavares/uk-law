## SCHEDULE 14 — Exercise of Officers’ Powers[^c16774431][^key-d6548ca2b538ab91926deb2c68561c5f][^key-db7d0bc466544b69ad6a9aba75c2c0df][^key-1a2f543f665628b4e6049f93019f5d0c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 115.

### General

**1.** In this Schedule an “**officer**” means (subject to paragraph 6A)[^key-70b128b4b6531d68fef47c4e39702ca8] —

- (a) an authorised officer within the meaning given by the[^c11209421] terrorist property[^key-11e1528de554e9612230ab7e331bdbc9] provisions (including when referred to in those provisions as an “enforcement officer” or a “senior officer”)[^key-bb008b822e962740b699c2675e162907] , and
- (b) an examining officer within the meaning of Schedule 7.

  and “[^c11209431]**the terrorist property provisions**” means Schedule 1 to the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001.

**2.** An officer may enter a vehicle (within the meaning of section 121) for the purpose of exercising any of the functions conferred on him by virtue of this Act or the[^c11209441] terrorist property provisions.

**3.** An officer may if necessary use reasonable force for the purpose of exercising a power conferred on him by virtue of this Act (apart from paragraphs 2 and 3 of Schedule 7) or the[^c11209451] terrorist property provisions.

### Information

**4.** (1) Information acquired by an officer may be supplied—

- (a) to the Secretary of State for use in relation to immigration;
- (b) to the Commissioners of Customs and Excise or a customs officer;
- (c) to a constable;
- (d)[^key-cd88ec5cd6e68542e02d71c60eb94d1f] to the National Crime Agency;
- (e) to a person specified by order of the Secretary of State for use of a kind specified in the order.

(2)[^c16744841] Information acquired by a customs officer or an immigration officer may be supplied to an examining officer within the meaning of Schedule 7.

(3)[^key-cab65be8c1ff43f4a7657f4a5743dccb] A person may be specified in an order under this paragraph only if the person exercises public functions (whether or not in the United Kingdom).

### Code of practice

**5.** An officer shall perform functions conferred on him by virtue of this Act or the[^c11209461] terrorist property provisions in accordance with any relevant code of practice in operation under paragraph 6.

**6.**[^c11209481] (1) The Secretary of State shall issue codes of practice about the exercise by officers of functions conferred on them by virtue of this Act or the[^c11209491] terrorist property provisions.

(2) The failure by an officer to observe a provision of a code shall not of itself make him liable to criminal or civil proceedings.

(3) A code—

- (a) shall be admissible in evidence in criminal and civil proceedings, and
- (b) shall be taken into account by a court or tribunal in any case in which it appears to the court or tribunal to be relevant.

(4) The Secretary of State may revise a code and issue the revised code.

**6A.**[^key-f401f383261526b58616a73e493cb74f] In paragraphs 5 and 6, “**officer**” includes a constable, immigration officer or customs officer who—

- (a) has functions under Schedule 7, or
- (b) has functions under Schedule 8 in relation to a person detained under Schedule 7,

otherwise than as an examining officer.

**7.** (1) Before issuing a code of practice the Secretary of State shall—

- (a) publish a draft code,
- (b) consider any representations made to him about the draft, and
- (c) if he thinks it appropriate, modify the draft in the light of any representations made to him.

(2) The Secretary of State shall lay a draft of the code before Parliament.

(3) When the Secretary of State has laid a draft code before Parliament he may bring it into operation by order.

(4) This paragraph has effect in relation to the issue of a revised code as it has effect in relation to the first issue of a code.
