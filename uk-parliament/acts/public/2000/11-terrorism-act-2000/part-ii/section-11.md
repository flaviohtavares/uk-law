# Terrorism Act 2000, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Membership.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person commits an offence if he belongs or professes to belong to a proscribed organisation.

(2) It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under subsection (1) to prove—

- (a) that the organisation was not proscribed on the last (or only) occasion on which he became a member or began to profess to be a member, and
- (b) that he has not taken part in the activities of the organisation at any time while it was proscribed.

(3) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14[^c-2dca1d1a34e378a5514b162bcceec376] years, to a fine or to both, or
- (b) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both.

(4) In subsection (2) “**proscribed**” means proscribed for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) this Act;
- (b) the [^c11204711]Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1996;
- (c) the [^c11204721]Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991;
- (d) the [^c11204731]Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989;
- (e) the [^c11204741]Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984;
- (f) the [^c11204751]Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1978;
- (g) the [^c11204761]Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1976;
- (h) the [^c11204771]Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1974;
- (i) the [^c11204781]Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1973.
