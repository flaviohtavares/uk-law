# Terrorism Act 2000, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Terrorist bombing: jurisdiction.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If—

- (a) a person does anything outside the United Kingdom as an act of terrorism or for the purposes of terrorism, and
- (b) his action would have constituted the commission of one of the offences listed in subsection (2) if it had been done in the United Kingdom,

he shall be guilty of the offence.

(2) The offences referred to in subsection (1)(b) are—

- (a) an offence under section 2, 3 or 5 of the [^c11205421]Explosive Substances Act 1883 (causing explosions, &c.),
- (b) an offence under section 1 of the [^c11205431]Biological Weapons Act 1974 (biological weapons), and
- (c) an offence under section 2 of the [^c11205441]Chemical Weapons Act 1996 (chemical weapons).
