## 55. Weapons training: interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 54—

- “[^c11205341] **biological weapon** ” means a biological agent or toxin (within the meaning of the Biological Weapons Act 1974) in a form capable of use for hostile purposes or anything to which section 1(1)(b) of that Act applies,
- “**chemical weapon**” has the meaning given by section 1 of the [^c11205351]Chemical Weapons Act 1996, and
- “[^c11205361] **radioactive material** ” means radioactive material capable of endangering life or causing harm to human health,
