# Terrorism Act 2000, section 22E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22E. Supplementary[^c-5755f9f543a576eeb7b7ebdcb5c4df5e][^c-fbc7f89c2fb9bd7ffceea9cb9b9f6e14][^c-4b0b43da1ec2889193d968f4e98c6c86]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An[^c-79cef4ddfcdb12b4f08ec06c51fdcb9e] information order does not confer the right to require a person to provide privileged information.

(2) “Privileged information” is information which a person would be entitled to refuse to provide on grounds of legal professional privilege in proceedings in the High Court or, in Scotland, legal privilege as defined by section 412 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002.

(3) Information provided in pursuance of an[^c-9ae047af9f7dfa08dbb59babae842967] information order is not to be taken to breach any restriction on the disclosure of information (however imposed).

(4) An application for an[^c-6bf38b25e02d6f5e9284ae9407f05ef2] information order may be heard and determined in private.

(5) Rules of court may make provision as to the practice and procedure to be followed in connection with proceedings relating to [^c-3435d23c40d118d48d915b48e0b55ef8]... information orders.
