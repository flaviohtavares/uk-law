## 128. Commencement.[^c11206381]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The preceding provisions of this Act, apart from sections 2(1)(b) and (2) and 118 and Schedule 1, shall come into force in accordance with provision made by the Secretary of State by order.
