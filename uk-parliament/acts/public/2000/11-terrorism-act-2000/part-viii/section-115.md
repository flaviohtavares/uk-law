## 115. Officers’ powers.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 14 (which makes provision about the exercise of functions by authorised officers for the purposes of Schedule 1 to the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001 (forfeiture of terrorist property)[^key-e32a2c4cc3dba1387cc58e3ce7927dfb] and examining officers for the purposes of Schedule 7 to this Act (port and border controls)[^key-2512869953e66e69ede69cfb623d1cf0]) shall have effect.
