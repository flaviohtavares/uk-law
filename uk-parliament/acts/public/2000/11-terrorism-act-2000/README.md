Terrorism Act 2000

2000 c. 11

[^c20359551][^c18717901]An Act to make provision about terrorism; and to make temporary provision for Northern Ireland about the prosecution and punishment of certain offences, the preservation of peace and the maintenance of order.

\[20th July 2000\]
