## 112. Expiry and revival.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) [^c16834981]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The Secretary of State may by order provide—

- (a) [^c16834981]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) that a provision of this Part shall cease to have effect;
- (c)[^c16835011][^c16835051] that a provision of this Part which is not in force (whether or not by virtue of this subsection) shall come into force and remain in force for a specified period not exceeding twelve months and ending before 1st August 2007[^c16835031] .

(3) An order under subsection (2) may make provision with respect to a provision of this Part—

- (a) generally,
- (b) only in so far as it concerns powers of members of Her Majesty’s Forces, or
- (c) except in so far as it concerns powers of members of Her Majesty’s Forces.

(4) [^c16834981]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c16835071] Paragraph 37 of Schedule 4 to this Act shall be treated for the purposes of this section as forming part of this Part of this Act.
