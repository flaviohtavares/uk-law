# Terrorism Act 2000, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/11

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Port and border controls.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 7 (port and border controls) shall have effect.

(2) The Secretary of State may by order repeal paragraph 16 of Schedule 7.

(3) The powers conferred by Schedule 7 shall be exercisable notwithstanding the rights conferred by section 1 of the [^c11205281]Immigration Act 1971 (general principles regulating entry into and staying in the United Kingdom).
