# Terrorism Act 2000, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Exercise of power.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The power conferred by an authorisation under section 48 shall be exercised by placing a traffic sign on the road concerned.

(2) A constable exercising the power conferred by an authorisation under section 48 may suspend a parking place.

(3) Where a parking place is suspended under subsection (2), the suspension shall be treated as a restriction imposed by virtue of section 48—

- (a) for the purposes of section 99 of the [^c11205201]Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 (removal of vehicles illegally parked, &c.) and of any regulations in force under that section, and
- (b) for the purposes of Articles 47 and 48 of the [^c11205211]Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 (in relation to Northern Ireland).
