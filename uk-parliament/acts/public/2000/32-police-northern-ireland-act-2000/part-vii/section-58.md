# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000, section 58

## 58. Annual report by Chief Constable to Board.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Chief Constable shall, not later than 3 months after the end of each financial year, submit to the Board a general report on the policing of Northern Ireland during that year.

(2) The Chief Constable shall arrange for a report submitted under this section to be published in such manner as appears to him to be appropriate.

(3) The Chief Constable shall, at the same time as he submits a report to the Board under this section, submit the same report to the Department of Justice[^c-f9a3cc85d2afbc567a44c152e2766dd1].

(4) The Department of Justice[^c-40380c44b78762b7dbbed41c8479411e] shall lay before the Northern Ireland Assembly[^c-4b5cc963dac7cf7ff7cc9187d0db4ace] every report submitted to the Department of Justice[^c-a664339142614a745ee0f3674c681b1d] under subsection (3).

(5)[^c-e0c2333afd73625476f81629c50919ce] Section 41(3) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 applies for the purposes of subsection (4) in relation to the laying of a report as it applies in relation to the laying of a statutory document under an enactment.
