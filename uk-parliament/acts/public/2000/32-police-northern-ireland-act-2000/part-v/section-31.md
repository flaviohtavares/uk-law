# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000, section 31

## 31. Enforcement of duties under section 28.[^c-634d1efce0f6cdf7330b0a6272de61ad]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies if the Department of Justice[^c-19894371e53ef68ab4b78f831b12ea26] has received a report under section 29(4) or (4A)[^c-fbc2bf2b43e00746740e85ccf0713c15] or 30(5) of this Act, or section 41(4) of the 1998 Act, and it[^c-80c4fe1486958afab05f2ffb5eac8962] is satisfied that—

- (a) the Board is failing to comply with the requirements of section 28; or
- (b) it is appropriate to change performance indicators or performance standards contained in a performance plan.

(2) Subject to subsection (3), the Department of Justice[^c-8a3016855cbe122949d3f462601d4247] may direct the Board—

- (a) to take any action which the Department of Justice[^c-cba3175428cd662747da3bb40c207a20] considers necessary or expedient to secure compliance with the requirement of section 28; or
- (b) to make such changes to the performance indicators or performance standards mentioned in subsection (1)(b) as the Department of Justice considers appropriate.

(3)[^c-6702c5d2aaaad71c89a7c02de35a9634] The Department of Justice[^c-ace21ea5d870996af628fc10fc130163] shall have regard to any relevant statement before—

- (a) giving a direction under subsection (2)(a) or (b) following a recommendation contained in a report by virtue of section 29(4)(c), (d) or (e);
- (b) giving a direction under subsection (2)(a) following a recommendation contained in a report by virtue of section 29(4A)(c) or (d).

(4) A relevant statement is a statement submitted to the Department of Justice[^c-a86ecb10ca1f04802780a7c4e10c154c] under section 29(10) before the end of the period mentioned in section 29(8).
