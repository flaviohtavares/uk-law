# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2016-05-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. The local policing plan.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Each district commander shall issue, and may from time to time revise, a plan (“**the local policing plan**”) setting out the proposed arrangements for the policing of the police district.

(2) The local policing plan may relate to matters to which the policing plan also relates, or to other matters, but in any event shall be so framed as to be consistent with the policing plan.

(3) Before issuing or revising a local policing plan, the district commander shall consult the policing and community safety partnership[^key-c7718108399aad750b3cf7f5c29c8590] whose district is, or includes, the police district and take account of any views expressed.

(3A)[^key-87e170a08b29d07b3bc30f69848b18f0] Before issuing or revising a local policing plan for a police district established under section 20(2), the district commander shall also consult the district policing and community safety partnership[^key-2fa3b7edd42f9ff59cd365a99423f1da] and take account of any views expressed.

(4) The district commander shall arrange for the local policing plan issued or revised under this section to be published in such manner as appears to him to be appropriate.
