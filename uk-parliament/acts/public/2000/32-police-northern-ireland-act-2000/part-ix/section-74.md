## 74. Application to police of anti-discrimination legislation.

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 5, which amends in their application to the police and other bodies of constables—

- (a) the [^c1055572]Sex Discrimination (Northern Ireland) Order 1976,
- (b) the [^c1055573]Race Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1997, and
- (c) the [^c1055574]Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998,

shall have effect.
