# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000, section 36A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2016-05-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36A. Fixed-term appointments to the Police Service of Northern Ireland[^key-a5ce6c9bc5533cf928560b8efae06a0e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Chief Constable may appoint a person to the Police Service of Northern Ireland for a fixed term not exceeding three years.

(2) Appointments under subsection (1) shall be in accordance with regulations under section 25 of the 1998 Act.

(3) A person shall not be appointed under subsection (1) to the rank of constable or the rank of a senior officer.

(4) The Department of Justice[^key-7978b9dc0e74b2f6a093d070ed645167] may by order make such modifications as it[^key-9bf993380323796fd383cf6797f3d305] considers necessary or expedient to any provision of the 1998 Act or this Act in its application to persons appointed under subsection (1).

(5) The Department of Justice[^key-373e85b6057ce78defe82e17657c6665] shall consult the Board and the Police Association before making an order under subsection (4).
