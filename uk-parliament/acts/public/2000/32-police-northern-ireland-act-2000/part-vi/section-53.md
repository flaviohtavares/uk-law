# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2016-05-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Guidance as to use of equipment for maintaining or restoring public order.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department of Justice[^key-516e49fce4bb16b671eefdf45821e833] may issue, and from time to time revise, guidance on the use by police officers of equipment designed for use in maintaining or restoring public order.

(2) Before issuing or revising any guidance under this section, the Department of Justice[^key-77075479b87ac7586884a240b72b90d1] shall consult—

- (a) the Board;
- (b) the Chief Constable;
- (c) the Ombudsman; and
- (d) the Police Association.

(3) The Department of Justice[^key-1e420c01d00bc405715067dc1df176f7] shall publish any guidance issued or revised under this section in such manner as it[^key-4332906f4e268af61401ea07d7f27554] thinks appropriate.
