# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2016-05-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. General functions of Chief Constable.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The police shall be under the direction and control of the Chief Constable.

(2)[^key-229a5aa370519b49a8b4a9741d61a664] The Chief Constable shall have regard to the policing plan in discharging his functions.

(3) The Chief Constable shall have regard to any code of practice under section 27 in discharging his functions.

(4) The duty under subsection (3) applies only so far as consistent with the duty under subsection (2).
