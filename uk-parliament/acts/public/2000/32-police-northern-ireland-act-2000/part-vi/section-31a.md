# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000, section 31A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2016-05-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31A. Core policing principles[^key-337c9a17aee8532ba80b3a96d032f6c1]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Police officers and National Crime Agency officers[^key-5c4b43e8bca2b113ca277145a0fdcc61] shall carry out their functions with the aim—

- (a) of securing the support of the local community, and
- (b) of acting in co-operation with the local community.

(2) In carrying out their functions, police officers and National Crime Agency officers[^key-35124ea9d29f2c186ed5b9ededa3c834] shall be guided by the code of ethics under section 52.

(3)[^key-1ab009d0201a9c01d3f27f89deae2b95] Nothing in this section shall have effect in relation to anything done by a National Crime Agency officer outside Northern Ireland.
