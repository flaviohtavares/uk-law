# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 425C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 425C. “Qualifying provision”[^c-f72c1e7ee708e374306e4a453f664799]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-18c868ab50eec53a0011ac3de6c8dadb] In this Act “**qualifying provision**” means a provision of any of the following—

- (a) assimilated direct[^c-fae1737be2f52f53ecb80c3ab4bf4632] legislation;
- (b) technical standards made in accordance with Chapter 2A of Part 9A;
- (ba)[^c-5644a1cb2fa3ae4b14fea73db94cdd48] other subordinate legislation made under assimilated direct[^c-e70a45bd6feda6932d24803bbd3c7a40] legislation;
- (c) subordinate legislation [^c-1db4f1774c450a72adeac0f7670740cd]... made by virtue of regulations made under section 8 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018.

(2)[^c-8eaa57f8c4a057062f90e47e711136d7] In this section, “**subordinate legislation**” has the same meaning as in the Interpretation Act 1978 (see section 21 of that Act).
