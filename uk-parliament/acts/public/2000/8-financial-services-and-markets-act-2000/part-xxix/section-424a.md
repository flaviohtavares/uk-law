# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 424A

## 424A. Investment firm[^c2075979]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act (except as otherwise provided)[^key-c04eaacabfb55f821bb902fd0bb6ab6c], “**investment firm**” has the meaning given in paragraph 2.1A of the markets in financial instruments regulation[^key-ad2d47b949ce8f3d5a27d4ba2e6f3725].

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to subsection (5)[^key-abdeb90b7df6d85da5893a8617415f45].

(3)[^key-90eb8bde463fcd28402c6c35273adac9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-90eb8bde463fcd28402c6c35273adac9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) References in this Act to an “investment firm” do not include references to—

- (a)[^key-c4df6690e18645cfce1aeca0e9b7b481] a person excluded from the definition of “investment firm” in Article 3(1) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 (S.I. 2001/544) by paragraph (a) or (b) of that definition; or
- (b) a firm which has a Part 4A permission to carry on regulated activities as an exempt investment firm within the meaning of regulation 8 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017 (S.I. 2017/701).
