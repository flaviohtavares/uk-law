# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 420

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 420. Parent and subsidiary undertaking.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act, except in relation to an incorporated friendly society, “**parent undertaking**” and “**subsidiary undertaking**” have the same meaning as in the Companies Acts (see section 1162 of, and Schedule 7 to, the Companies Act 2006)[^c2076530].

(2) But—

- (a) “**parent undertaking**” also includes an individual who would be a parent undertaking for the purposes of those provisions if he were taken to be an undertaking (and “**subsidiary undertaking**” is to be read accordingly);
- (b) “**subsidiary undertaking**” also includes, in relation to a body incorporated in or formed under the law of an EEA State [^c-c6ceb8ad2f03ad808a017c84d1b66fae]..., an undertaking which is a subsidiary undertaking within the meaning of any rule of law in force in that State for purposes connected with implementation of the [Directive 2013/34/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2013/0034)[^c-884e19c5180a2c6d08e80525c2d8026b] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on the annual financial statements, consolidated financial statements and related reports of certain types of undertakings, amending [Directive 2006/43/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2006/0043) of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives [78/660/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1978/660) and [83/349/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1983/349) (and “**parent undertaking**” is to be read accordingly).

(3) In this Act “**subsidiary undertaking**”, in relation to an incorporated friendly society, means a body corporate of which the society has control within the meaning of section 13(9)(a) or (aa) of the [^c1709788]Friendly Societies Act 1992 (and “**parent undertaking**” is to be read accordingly).
