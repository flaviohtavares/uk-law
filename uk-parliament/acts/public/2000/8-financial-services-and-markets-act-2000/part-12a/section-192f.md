# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 192F

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 192F. Consultation between regulators[^key-8628c60b9494b1d63957cdd6cbea73ea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before the PRA gives a notice under section 192E(1) or (8)(b), it must consult the FCA.

(2) Before the FCA gives a notice under section 192E(1) or (8)(b) in relation to the parent undertaking of a PRA-authorised person, the FCA must consult the PRA.

(3) Before the FCA[^key-8ec05bc65b945a3d07c364906cefb144] gives a notice under section 192E(1) or (8)(b) in relation to the parent undertaking of a recognised clearing house or a recognised CSD[^key-9f666aadda98151df3aa4adebf825471], it[^key-8a18977e1baa738a770f131672ba14ae] must consult the Bank of England.
