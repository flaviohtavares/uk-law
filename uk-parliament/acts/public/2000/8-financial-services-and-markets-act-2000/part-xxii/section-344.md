## 344. Duty of auditor or actuary resigning etc. to give notice.[^c2077011][^c-19e82d361ca1e0c266c17d9b9dca2d6e][^c-585a4fd487b388829e619ad676ea6686][^c-790e968b747642e289b551636dcdd400][^c-af217cd60b404b3c4803395ec4b2fa29][^c-f7264ba5f159f6445dcc6e60740f3c4e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to an auditor or actuary to whom section 342 applies.

(2) He must without delay notify the appropriate regulator[^c-5dfec9ec8da4a7d3c71462104fef6fdc] if he—

- (a) is removed from office by an authorised person or recognised investment exchange[^c-2716700e1aff6a1598eb9106c0b1809d];
- (b) resigns before the expiry of his term of office with such a person; or
- (c) is not re-appointed by such a person.

(3) If he ceases to be an auditor of, or actuary acting for, such a person, he must without delay notify the appropriate regulator—

- (a) of any matter connected with his so ceasing which he thinks ought to be drawn to the regulator's[^c-1b67ebc821c5d4ed3c62c3c1a5a73508] attention; or
- (b) that there is no such matter.

(4)[^c-38cf8f7ab5c5b9c8a482fc82f96e75b1] In this section “**the appropriate regulator**” means—

- (a) in the case of an auditor of, or an actuary acting for, a PRA-authorised person, the PRA;
- (b) in any other case, the FCA.
