# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 131AE

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131AE. Liability for contraventions of Article 14 or 15 of the market abuse regulation[^key-61863ad5120815a9eb4821ea7c4cf57e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of any enactment a person contravenes Article 14 (prohibition of insider dealing and of unlawful disclosure of inside information) or Article 15 (prohibition of market manipulation) whether the contravention is by that person alone or by that person and one or more other persons jointly or in concert.
