## 131AB. Interpretation[^c-61863ad5120815a9eb4821ea7c4cf57e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-da300b5ef9f61b803e5faeee92b69d50] In this Part—

- [^c-3f7c5a7ba70f9b846f7ccb66787fa7db]...
- “emission allowance”[^c-205907e85c7124e093bf7ed14e846f22] means emission allowance as described in paragraph 11 of Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001[^c-1e61cc3983ea92637a8ee04ad0b4ebd8];
- “[^c-7c2e3704498c52c430fbef3e3a079a62] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-04b3989919b6478a3c2a823e03ffd212]**financial instrument *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-04b3989919b6478a3c2a823e03ffd212]**” means any instrument specified in Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order , read with Part 2 of that Schedule; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-04b3989919b6478a3c2a823e03ffd212]
- “**financial instrument**” means any of the following—
  - (a) an instrument specified in Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, read with Part 2 of that Schedule;
  - (b) a “**qualifying cryptoasset**” as defined by article 88F of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001;
  - (c) a “**related instrument**” as defined by regulation 17(1) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Cryptoassets) Regulations 2026;
- “issuer” has the meaning given in Article 3.1(21) of the market abuse regulation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-62ea9f0f53c185678eb85a658ecd6c5f]
- “**issuer**” means—
  - (a) an issuer defined by Article 3.1(21) of the market abuse regulation, or
  - (b) a “**relevant person**” as defined by regulation 17(4) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Cryptoassets) Regulations 2026; and
- “recognised auction platform” has the meaning given in regulation 1(3) of the Recognised Auction Platform Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/2699](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/2699));
- [^c-940faf92ea527fcabb4779d57edde7d3]...

(2)[^c-9bef830f90d9dd4de7a4e1281bd10eba] The following are supplementary market abuse legislation for the purposes of this Part—

- (a) an EU regulation, originally made under the market abuse regulation, which is assimilated direct[^c-e2e9ddc0ab4c398e7e3b60bbb229c953] legislation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-68832ad162163d0402fd5844b9fc815b]
- (b) subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made under the market abuse regulation on or after IP completion day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a3549e7888cde0659e7d55ea6514f655] day; and
- (c)[^c-51ec8fe2f4759574a86e98194145ee62] Chapter 2 of Part 2 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Cryptoassets) Regulations 2026.
