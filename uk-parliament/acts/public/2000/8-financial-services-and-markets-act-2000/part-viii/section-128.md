# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 128

## 128. Suspension of investigations.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the FCA[^key-26cb191e674edf25be714f7d89730519] considers it desirable or expedient because of the exercise or possible exercise of a relevant power[^key-692002b2c56e853772485d71fd376fdc], it may direct a recognised investment exchange , recognised clearing house or recognised CSD[^key-d214eb339eddc0a25a48a9e76ae85e67]—

- (a) to terminate, suspend or limit the scope of any inquiry which the exchange , clearing house or central securities depository[^key-d34c424f14dd928744dad0bc36ce1453] is conducting under its rules; or
- (b) not to conduct an inquiry which the exchange , clearing house or central securities depository[^key-c38a45733b743f1aaa396279ecdf64e8] proposes to conduct under its rules.

(2) A direction under this section—

- (a) must be given to the exchange , clearing house or central securities depository[^key-e761e13054618b2257d12ae131088df9] concerned by notice in writing; and
- (b) is enforceable, on the application of the FCA, by injunction or, in Scotland, by an order under section 45 of the [^c1709306]Court of Session Act 1988.

(3)[^key-cb277a65f21c7bfd1fc2079854525db6] In this section “relevant power” means the FCA’s power—

- (a) to impose a penalty or publish a statement of censure under section 123;
- (b) to impose a prohibition under section 123A;
- (c) to impose a suspension or restriction under section 123B;
- (d) to appoint a person to conduct an investigation under section 168 in a case falling within subsection (2)(d) of that section; or
- (e) to appoint a person to conduct an investigation under section 169 (investigation etc in support of an overseas regulator) in a case falling within subsection (2A) of that section.
