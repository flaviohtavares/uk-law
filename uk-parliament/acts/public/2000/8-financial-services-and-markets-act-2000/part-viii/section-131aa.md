# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 131AA

## 131AA. Reporting of infringements[^key-61863ad5120815a9eb4821ea7c4cf57e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to employers who—

- (a) provide regulated financial services;
- (b) carry on regulated activities in reliance on the exemption in section 327; or
- (c) are recognised bodies [^key-a7a098e6a37f8d1c82fad7a1f4ca1365]... or third country central counterparties.

(2) Employers must have in place appropriate internal procedures for their employees to report contraventions of the market abuse regulation or any supplementary market abuse legislation[^key-d361869d3100a6bd15e42c7d8211f840].

(3) In this section—

- “employee” and “employer” have the meaning given in section 230 of the Employment Rights Act 1996;
- “recognised body” has the meaning given in section 313;
- “regulated financial services” has the meaning given in section 1H.
