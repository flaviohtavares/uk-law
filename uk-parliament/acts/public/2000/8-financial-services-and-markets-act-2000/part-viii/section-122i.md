# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 122I

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 122I. Power to suspend trading in financial instruments[^key-6fd0432ab398d8bfa3958d489ec58a52]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA may suspend trading of a financial instrument where it considers it necessary for the purpose of the exercise by it of functions under the market abuse regulation or under supplementary market abuse legislation[^key-76889f791e6dd67a267644a27ef0f96f].

(2) If the FCA does so the issuer of the financial instrument may refer the matter to the Tribunal.

(2A)[^key-d3820d6f0b0ab0d7ca474fbadb503d79] But subsection (2) does not apply if the financial instrument is an emission allowance.

(3) The FCA may—

- (a) cancel a suspension under subsection (1); and
- (b) impose such conditions for the cancellation to take effect as it considers appropriate.

(4) The provisions relating to suspension of listing of securities in section 78 (discontinuance or suspension: procedure) apply to a suspension of trading in a financial instrument other than an emission allowance[^key-486e25124009a6813dab9b1f6f1533e4] under subsection (1) and for the purposes of this section—

- (a) the references in section 78 to listing are to be read as references to trading; and
- (b) the references in section 78 to securities are to be read as references to financial instruments.

(4A)[^key-b278868af6673ea04be55bce6644c447] A suspension of trading in a financial instrument that is an emission allowance takes effect—

- (a) immediately, if the FCA states that is the case; or
- (b) on such later date as the FCA specify.

(5) For the meaning of “issuer” in this Part, see section 131AB.
