# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 131AC

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131AC. Meaning of “persons closely associated” in the market abuse regulation[^key-61863ad5120815a9eb4821ea7c4cf57e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Article 3.1(26)(a) (definitions) of the market abuse regulation “partner considered to be equivalent to a spouse” includes a civil partner.

(2) In Article 3.1(26)(b) of the market abuse regulation “dependent child” means a child who—

- (a) is under the age of 18 years;
- (b) is unmarried; and
- (c) does not have a civil partner.

(3) In this section “child” includes a stepchild.
