## 167. Appointment of persons to carry out general investigations.[^c1709358][^c1751109][^c2124680][^key-274824ebeaf791a98e4a797e25520f54][^key-e5d60babf0ba670037f63e6f5b5208a8][^key-53affd4a50507b05961dd2495e1deba5][^key-a77585be34fc728a4c336bf223c5f30a][^key-653480f1705f74d1101001ad88c6fb8a][^key-6aa163907febf3ca9385a611ea1849dd][^key-95eea5e2492a5c73e5b8cdb16e2f500f][^key-c1435aa032f8520e9627a5de8acba854]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-e5d60babf0ba670037f63e6f5b5208a8][^key-653480f1705f74d1101001ad88c6fb8a][^key-6e50533187a23900e1b5ed23af9af792][^key-69cc3f0413270dde800fe6d85101a062]

(1)[^key-16cfd8cbb275069282e226b100f9d875][^key-9bcda5e21ce4a9f3e766032c3ac63bd4] If it appears to an investigating authority[^key-bf84038a6501ae70a19737cf0b0ee4a6] that there is good reason for doing so, the investigating authority may appoint one or more competent persons to conduct an investigation on its behalf into—

- (a) the nature, conduct or state of the business of a person to whom subsection (1A) applies or[^key-e294c7c2ea1efa4a9c3c5ba7126a36af] an appointed representative;
- (b) a particular aspect of that business; or
- (c) the ownership or control of a person to whom subsection (1A) applies[^key-8e3ad2574e492e2be70b95e7c40bd7df].

(1A)[^key-ab6d92591edfa4ffaa507acf262bd4bb] This subsection applies to the following persons—

- (a) a recognised investment exchange;
- (b) an authorised person;
- (c) a relevant parent undertaking of an FCA investment firm;
- (d) a person who provides a service to an FCA investment firm or to a relevant parent undertaking of such a firm.
- (e)[^key-2da95af7b95b9ea36844be6e9ca85dd1] a person on whom a relevant Part 5A requirement is imposed.

(2) If a person appointed under subsection (1) thinks it necessary for the purposes of his investigation, he may also investigate the business of a person who is or has at any relevant time been—

- (a) a member of the group of which the person under investigation (“**A**”) is part; [^key-c86fe67f552a2096641a5e4afa6aa6df]...
- (b) a partnership of which A is a member ; or[^key-57e7d9074c2ade4e712c9c44d58a2a57]
- (c)[^key-321f8984a308dab09148ee61be70728c] where A is an insurance undertaking or reinsurance undertaking[^key-b57b72d2224be5bbcdb85c95fd94520e], a person who provides services to A.

(3) If a person appointed under subsection (1) decides to investigate the business of any person under subsection (2) he must give that person written notice of his decision.

(3A)[^key-038d2124d82dd09a7e525d507794d8b4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4A)[^key-000dc40fda441d39937627a0ec43a505] The power conferred by this section may be exercised in relation to a person who has at any time been an authorised person or a person described in subsection (1A)(c) , (d) or (e)[^key-27416504bec2625041b46da392d16e4e] but only in relation to—

- (a) business carried on at any time when the person was an authorised person or a person described in subsection (1A)(c) , (d) or (e), or
- (b) the ownership or control of the person at such a time.

(4B) The power conferred by this section may be exercised in relation to a person who has at any time been an appointed representative but only in relation to business carried on at any time when the person was an appointed representative.

(5) “**Business**” includes any part of a business even if it does not consist of carrying on regulated activities.

(5A)[^key-ba34e8a8fbc2c9ad9df3f25438dcb79e] “Investigating authority” means—

- (a) in relation to a recognised investment exchange, the Secretary of State or the FCA;
- (b) in relation to an authorised person or former authorised person, the FCA or the PRA;
- (c) in relation to an appointed representative or former appointed representative, the FCA or the PRA.
- (d)[^key-36eca3866cec47ac9afa81b08447c6ee] in relation to a person who is, or has at any time been, a person described in subsection (1A)(c) , (d) or (e)[^key-829c1c4b142de6f66a2b67653f250698] who is not an authorised person, the FCA.

(6)[^c2078002] References in subsection (1) to a recognised investment exchange do not include references to an overseas investment exchange (as defined by section 313(1)).
