# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 177A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 177A. Interpretation of Part 11[^c-a8d67caadc02caa01227ec18b900353f][^c-6743369830a805b8848d192695fe83e3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**FCA investment firm**” has the meaning given in section 143A;
- “**relevant parent undertaking**”, in relation to an FCA investment firm, means an authorised parent undertaking or a non-authorised parent undertaking (as defined in section 143B).
