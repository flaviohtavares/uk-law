## 168. Appointment of persons to carry out investigations in particular cases.[^c1709359][^c1751110][^key-790e1a2688d74988f9ff236d5e5a0544][^key-e5d60babf0ba670037f63e6f5b5208a8][^key-ffa9508e70d23367bb315461c1ed65a7][^key-5bb68221bf7d747f40455481de1026e9][^key-653480f1705f74d1101001ad88c6fb8a][^key-b2897de54781844df547cbd59b2bb0c8][^key-1d45e0b313891a42bc0c8a538007e54b][^key-ab35a5833a0cf23e5f9d964a14bebc28][^key-b1659ef1325db898453d64748424b829][^key-9be5df7446b20188feb940a77285b929]

(1) Subsection (3) applies if it appears to an investigating authority that there are circumstances suggesting that—

- (a)[^key-93fd603cc29d523238e5d795d34c71f3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a person may be guilty of an offence under section 122F,[^key-4345854d07430ea5e453a84d4a28a962] 177, 191F[^key-d246673eaf3aee5b83d143d9df2236c5], 346 or 398(1) [^key-d0005e63334009e63a00f25bda45eb2d]....

(2) Subsection (3) also applies if it appears to an investigating authority that there are circumstances suggesting that—

- (a) an offence under section 24(1) [^key-56f56cbe0da5e93e524c6635594c8321]... or under Part 7 of the Financial Services Act 2012[^key-96cb0203f3df149b55b76c41f5036f22] or under Part V of the [^c1709360]Criminal Justice Act 1993 may have been committed;
- (b) there may have been a breach of the general prohibition;
- (ba)[^key-45d5e36df1217312317f05baa30d6d4f] an authorised person may have contravened section 20 in relation to a credit-related regulated activity;
- (c) there may have been a contravention of section 21 or 238; or
- (d)[^key-858b637f4d98f3da9f240426feb83e22] a person has contravened Article 14 (prohibition of insider dealing and of unlawful disclosure of inside information) or Article 15 (prohibition of market manipulation) of the market abuse regulation.

(3)[^c1709361][^key-456d4e450ab649c57144fc5ec02712ae] The investigating authority may appoint one or more competent persons to conduct an investigation on its behalf.

(4)[^c1751142][^key-34780f0c930e543648f681efe03602ad] Subsection (5) applies if it appears to an investigating authority[^key-2f327dd16aa06ac4007ad2e4b2bf7198] that there are circumstances suggesting that—

- (a) a person may have contravened section 20;
- (b) a person may be guilty of an offence under prescribed regulations relating to money laundering;
- (ba)[^c2079991] a person may be guilty of an offence under Schedule 7 to the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (terrorist financing or money laundering);
- (c)[^c1751150][^key-0aebcbaa3c02a8e9c11eccf92ab213f9] a person[^c2080571] may have contravened a rule made by the investigating authority[^key-c4d17fedeb29c1372441e6fb8d288587];
- (ca)[^key-ee298fd2841385e51bd33f2b6213d8a2] a recognised investment exchange may have contravened the recognition requirements (within the meaning of Part 18) or a rule made by the FCA under section 300H[^key-e5a85eff967d66e324760bee16b7a8b4];
- (d) an individual may not be a fit and proper person to perform functions in relation to a regulated activity carried on by an authorised or exempt person;
- (e) an individual may have performed or agreed to perform a function in breach of a prohibition order;
- (f) a person[^key-79145c759dc69d5f54d86a5f55cc588d] may have failed to comply with section 56(6);
- (g) an authorised person may have failed to comply with section 59(1) or (2);
- (h) a person in relation to whom a regulator[^key-b4379548c10339da4af368f7a704ac8e] has given its approval under section 59 may not be a fit and proper person to perform the function to which that approval relates; [^c2078006]. . .
- (ha)[^c2080577] a person may have performed a controlled function without approval for the purposes of section 63A;
- (i) a person may be guilty of misconduct for the purposes of section 66 ;[^c2078008] [^key-5490d05087324366691ede83e3b9b2fa]...
- (iza)[^key-7fabb386c42ca1f903ff7e20b43ed288] a person has effected, or has purported to effect, a unilateral change under subsection (1) of section 117B (unilateral changes by providers of pension schemes), but any of the provisions mentioned in subsection (3) of that section may have been contravened in relation to it;
- (ia)[^key-f52594d0978545d23618884be1bf376c] a person may have failed to comply with section 143R;
- (ib) an individual may have performed or agreed to perform a function in breach of a Part 9C prohibition order;
- (ic) a person may have failed to comply with section 143S(6);
- (id)[^key-bd52d040a842910de5f0bbe4f45a8b2c] a person may have contravened any provision made by or under the Securitisation Regulations 2024;
- (j) a person may have contravened any provision made by or under this Act for the purpose of implementing the markets in financial instruments directive [^key-1a572bf456ee1624f92e2a5d5783e8fe]...
- (ja)[^key-d171a1d5d2bd2e2696c576cc94cceafe] a person may have contravened—
  - (i) any provision made by or under this Act for the purpose of implementing the alternative investment fund managers directive; [^key-1187f8fd189d0031c7e61778b68f97d2]...
  - (ii)[^key-7f145393de313ee98a561d50d34c3f1e] any provision made by the Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013; [^key-c954ec954d24aa15fddbdbc78b15a683]...
  - (iii)[^key-58d2dcdc8118ae04d9ff0b6ba060570f] any provision made by or under this Act for the purpose of implementing the UCITS Directive; or
  - (iv) any provision made by the Undertakings for Collective Investment in Transferable Securities Regulations 2011; [^key-5af90926f846568aa6d6c5c5c7d44304]....
- (jb)[^key-ccac00055d8b2453eb3320c6d752d652] a person may have contravened—
  - (i) any provision made by or under this Act for the purposes of the market abuse regulation; or
  - (ii) a requirement imposed on that person under sections 122A to 122C, 122G to 122I, 123A or 123B;
- (jc) a person may have been knowingly concerned in the contravention of—
  - (i) a provision of the market abuse regulation other than Article 14 (prohibition of insider dealing) or 15 (prohibition of market manipulation) of that regulation; or
  - (ii) any[^key-77ee258d1aeeb9f4ab3a41b33a327daf] assimilated direct[^key-97e6e6ae92ed3d48adee5aa94732b5c6] legislation originally made under the market abuse regulation or any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made on or after IP completion day under the market abuse regulation; or
- (k)[^key-0d183bc09c147cb50548f1f2844d5677] a person may have contravened a qualifying provision[^key-306dc446df61687dd280b47d52c4f776] that is specified, or of a description specified, for the purposes of this subsection by the Treasury by order.

(4A)[^key-ed5b261478003d11051fe85038e82c4e] Subsection (5) applies if it appears to the investigating authority that there are circumstances suggesting that—

- (a) an individual may not be a fit and proper person to perform functions in relation to an activity carried on by a relevant recognised body;
- (b) an individual may have performed, or agreed to perform, a function in breach of a Part 18 prohibition order;
- (c) a person may have failed to comply with section 309F(1);
- (d) a relevant recognised body may have failed to comply with section 309G(1);
- (e) a person in relation to whom the FCA has given approval under section 309G may not be a fit and proper person to perform the function to which that approval relates;
- (f) a person may have performed a designated senior management function without approval under section 309G (see section 309U(3));
- (g) a person may be guilty of misconduct for the purposes of section 309Z2.

(4B)[^key-b8270cbed6c3a833d29b3c70c7fd6515] Subsection (5) also applies if it appears to the investigating authority that there are circumstances suggesting that a person may have contravened a relevant Part 5A requirement.

(5)[^c1709361][^key-34780f0c930e543648f681efe03602ad][^key-456d4e450ab649c57144fc5ec02712ae] The investigating authority[^key-f272f1495ec1ab621486a423bf4609ef] may appoint one or more competent persons to conduct an investigation on its behalf.

(6)[^key-34780f0c930e543648f681efe03602ad][^key-5f6cdba4d901e681747c1c1509720a25] Investigating authority” means—

- (a) in subsections (1) to (3), the FCA, the PRA or the Secretary of State;
- (b) in subsections (4) and (5), the FCA or the PRA.
- (c)[^key-c7c08095f60750feedc5f7ffacc4daba] in subsections (4A) and (4B)[^key-6b2b73af0e647890c014c36b55227067], the FCA.

(7)[^key-823a3bd1124913c1276d9ceba30dda64] “**Relevant recognised body**” has the same meaning as in Chapter 2A of Part 18 (see section 309A).
