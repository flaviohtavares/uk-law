## 398. Misleading FCA or PRA: residual cases.[^c1709732][^c1751276][^c2077333][^c-950991615d2d2770dde3e3a4a3ec9403][^c-d751e3e2e5e85549c34e1327e4de3663][^c-52f524c776abd86db4126824afa24e7d][^c-ce7e2728217c649da59e3899f873b950][^c-0703f1475dd263687cb32cd5c6839200][^c-2fafd6311a3d7fc08b54a316566d9a24][^c-78cd641ce113f1f1502ac770e7b40bc9][^c-0687ac06e4d8a7f28fb6255e8cc99916][^c-dd03fd3da8b10ba5cb62ae0e0be8f921][^c-e6bc4fadc7d67f1a4d124fbfe938676c][^c-f49b7a0b917d65580aa8cce9018fd2b8][^c-2d0bdfd82fe9f446bec6b71fb7a93bd6][^c-d2db3a6c44d7b3c4090dc3d9ab98a1ea][^c-0ef41b9c8909ddec4fb67ef8b22643d6][^c-1587a2de33c358ba310afc18b067caac][^c-cbce198df87a28f2f5cfc79d2d64b630][^c-05ad45d94649b5f1b798e46460bf8c7c][^c-11a33e968328c2016a043e107682e760]

[^c-8696800328cdb10db7a1f54eaa9d2782][^c-1f949bbfc09751accb07f77f00ef122d][^c-cb7996d996f5ea669b13ee232aac28be][^c-f2beae1a79435a1ec54d7ceff083e39f]

(1)[^c1709733][^c2076469][^c-a0df4b8dba29276f8d67844e98aa5d11][^c-2c037236a3222a00c1d82929f84cd1c3] A person who, in purported compliance with any requirement falling within subsection (1A)[^c-85dc84fb8561795359770bfaff0e40ab] knowingly or recklessly gives a regulator[^c-7410d1080fc2ccc33cc5c8fb19f19320] information which is false or misleading in a material particular is guilty of an offence.

(1A)[^c-1a4b7e825ccfac88ecad781b3f614be7] A requirement falls within this subsection if it is imposed by or under—

- (a) this Act;
- (b)[^c-b88348244d6778cb4af048899c60218b] the Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013;
- (ba)[^c-87d8156500950369d8d6b49200a9efba] the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017 (S.I. 2017/701);
- (c) the Short Selling Regulations 2025[^c-5cfab8ce09b7dada8ea87f6abcee479b];
- (d) Regulation (EU) No 345/2013 of the European Parliament and the Council of 17 April 2013 on European venture capital funds; [^c-4670a160e9e0c7b23c272a42897087f9]...
- (e) Regulation (EU) No 346/2013 of the European Parliament and the Council of 17 April 2013 on European social entrepreneurship funds ;[^c-e66992aab65589c4084fe781b7fdab01] [^c-50d3fe1b8b23d03175b6107a373b85aa]...
- (ea)[^c-23d7c461c539717f03bcbd895d54096a] any EU regulation, originally made under the markets in financial instruments directive, which is assimilated direct[^c-142e7d47dc3c462d8f94e079fda2fa25] legislation;
- (eb)[^c-17b47cf0dd3c09dfd6ed66cedff5b431] any of the following—
  - (i) the markets in financial instruments regulation,
  - (ii) any EU regulation, originally made under the markets in financial instruments regulation, which is assimilated direct[^c-8476440879956c919ff25749e20fdf70] legislation, and
  - (iii) any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made under the markets in financial instruments regulation on or after IP completion day;
- (f)[^c-ca527bec6efb3e2cf664f863125b4f36] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . ;[^c-385829d86eb51d83b9be5577918f987b] [^c-6df3a25b37305136813efe60bdb2f4e1]...
- (g) the market abuse regulation; [^c-44d4b4a093d7c1a74f62a07c83960453]...
- (h)[^c-85485235b2bd51b64ce1ffece29c284f] the EU Benchmarks Regulation 2016 ;[^c-3e20759a3cefb52ce412c6adf607d594] [^c-87581b472a31510831daba1157af5c52]...
- (i)[^c-36c92ea2a8a96bde2170bcb67b4319e5] the MMF Regulation ;[^c-76dbbba6319ede6807b92c0bce9397c1] [^c-a424b48d37d0af2f830dad023cf5bc81]...
- (j)[^c-6c057f49228054cd3c123bace4958ef7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Subsection (1) applies only to a requirement in relation to which no other provision of this Act creates an offence in connection with the giving of information.

(3)[^c1709733][^c2076469] A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine.
