# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 402

## 402. Power of FCA to institute proceedings for certain other offences.[^c1709739][^c-138c9810978bfe98658f21f2360a5d88][^c-891b0e6896b0c0c0f811f9970665c1c0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^c-f27ea322e6f3eccf38d6952f370ae745][^c-c646dcf7d340dd84bebbb8250d8c9f47]

(1)[^c-eedd2de9fa459dafb843a9d79fa7a1bb] Except in Scotland, the FCA[^c-1415a7ff6b554acb87a78b002e510dd7] may institute proceedings for an offence under—

- (a) Part V of the [^c1709740]Criminal Justice Act 1993 (insider dealing); [^c2076713]. . .
- (b) prescribed regulations relating to money laundering. or[^c2076716]
- (c) Schedule 7 to the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (terrorist financing or money laundering).

(2) In exercising its power to institute proceedings for any such offence, the FCA[^c-adce8e1db7d9436b77b3887dac369d4d] must comply with any conditions or restrictions imposed in writing by the Treasury.

(3) Conditions or restrictions may be imposed under subsection (2) in relation to—

- (a) proceedings generally; or
- (b) such proceedings, or categories of proceedings, as the Treasury may direct.
