## 399. Misleading the CMA.[^key-6e8cac37382d703b5433983bac4f2bb7][^key-3053677eae0a6388b91b5347d7b5a475]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 44 of the [^c1709735]Competition Act 1998 (offences connected with the provision of false or misleading information) applies in relation to any function of the Competition and Markets Authority[^key-3586dd1e39368c5aa9fd2046af535838] under this Act as if it were a function under Part I of that Act.
