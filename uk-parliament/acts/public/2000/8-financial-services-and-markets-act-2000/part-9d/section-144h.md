# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 144H

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 144H. Relationship with the capital requirements regulation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) CRR rules may make provision by reference to the capital requirements regulation, to an instrument made under the capital requirements regulation or to Directive 2013/36/EU UK law, as amended from time to time.

(2)[^key-6420734e39edba39c2ee15c1faba8a67] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In this section, “**Directive 2013/36/EU UK law**” means the law of the United Kingdom which was relied on immediately before 1 January 2022[^key-a54cca0e6af7602ad8aa0840a938cc34] to implement the capital requirements directive and its implementing measures as it has effect—

- (a) on IP completion day, in the case of rules made by the PRA or the FCA under this Act, and
- (b) as amended from time to time, in all other cases.
