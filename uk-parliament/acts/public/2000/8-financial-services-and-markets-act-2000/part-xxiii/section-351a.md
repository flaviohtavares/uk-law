# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 351A

## 351A. Disclosure under the UCITS directive[^c2109472]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies in relation to a disclosure made by a person who falls within subsection (2) for the purpose of compliance with requirements set out in rules made by the FCA or the PRA[^key-c6e7ae200b369cf899243fc6db226314] to implement Chapter VIII of the UCITS directive.

(2) The following persons fall within this subsection—

- (a) the auditor of an authorised unit trust scheme or authorised contractual scheme[^key-0dba2f032fa84409b5addf494f171be5] that is a master UCITS;
- (b) the trustee of an authorised unit trust scheme that is a master UCITS;
- (ba)[^key-f614388e65ca2ad8b968923eb0f2ea23] the depositary of an authorised contractual scheme that is a master UCITS;
- (c) the auditor of an authorised unit trust scheme or authorised contractual scheme[^key-3dff61bd5dea6e56fcda1c3313cd5a87] that is a feeder UCITS;
- (d) the trustee of an authorised unit trust scheme that is a feeder UCITS; [^key-ca03a7cae733372a499031060e818ada]...
- (da)[^key-a3ec7eac835b6536a62bc6515d643985] the depositary of an authorised contractual scheme that is a feeder UCITS; or
- (e)[^key-0e245d93622a2af8e2479660f45e768c] a person acting on behalf of a person within any of paragraphs (a) to (da)

(3) A disclosure to which this section applies is not to be taken as a contravention of any duty to which the person making the disclosure is subject.

(4) In this section, “authorised unit trust scheme”, “authorised contractual scheme”,[^key-52cc60c14e830ef85eaffdc961dc3f75] “master UCITS” and “feeder UCITS” have the meaning given in section 237.
