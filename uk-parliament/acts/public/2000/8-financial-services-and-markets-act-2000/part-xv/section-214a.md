# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 214A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 214A). (not yet in force: Contingency funding)[^c2081100]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Treasury may make regulations (“)****(not yet in force: contingency fund regulations)****(not yet in force: ”) permitting the scheme manager to impose levies under section 213 for the purpose of maintaining contingency funds from which possible expenses may be paid.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Contingency fund regulations may make provision about the establishment and management of contingency funds; in particular, the regulations may make provision about—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the number and size of funds;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the circumstances and timing of their establishment;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the classes of person from whom contributions to the funds may be levied;)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: the amount and timing of payments into and out of funds (which may include provision for different levies for different classes of person);)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: refunds;)*
- ((not yet in force: f)) *(not yet in force: the ways in which funds' contents may be invested (including (i) the extent of reliance on section 223A, and (ii) the application of investment income);)*
- ((not yet in force: g)) *(not yet in force: the purposes for which funds may be applied, but only so as to determine whether a fund is to be used (i) for the payment of compensation, (ii) for the purposes of co-operating with a bank liquidator in accordance with section 99 of the Banking Act 2009, or (iii) for contributions under section 214B;)*
- ((not yet in force: h)) *(not yet in force: procedures to be followed in connection with funds, including the keeping of records and the provision of information.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The compensation scheme may include provision about contingency funds provided that it is not inconsistent with contingency fund regulations.)*
