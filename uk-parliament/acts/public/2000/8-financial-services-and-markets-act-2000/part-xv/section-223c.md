# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 223C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 223C. Payments in error[^c2080242]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Payments made by the scheme manager in error may be provided for in setting a levy by virtue of section 213, 214A, 214B , 214E[^key-cea63e65e86e2bda7616d3c9eaff3cc0] or 223B.

(2) This section does not apply to payments made in bad faith.
