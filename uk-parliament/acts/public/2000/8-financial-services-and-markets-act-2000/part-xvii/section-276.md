# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 276

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 276. Procedure when refusing an application.[^c1709567]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the FCA[^c-2002a7ce52dd7d974d24d49e805fed98] proposes to refuse an application made under section 272 it must give the applicant a warning notice.

(2) If the FCA decides to refuse the application—

- (a) it must give the applicant a decision notice; and
- (b) the applicant may refer the matter to the Tribunal.
