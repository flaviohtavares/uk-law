# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 283B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 283B. Reports on derivative instruments[^c2109279]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An authorised person who is the management company in relation to a UK[^c-9f80bb7a60ea25693a8ceb9714f0310e] UCITS must report to the FCA[^c-2002a7ce52dd7d974d24d49e805fed98] at specified intervals of not more than 12 months about any investment in derivative instruments during the specified period to which the report relates.

(2) The report must be in the specified form and contain the specified information.

(3) The FCA must review the regularity and completeness of the information provided by each management company under subsection (1).

(4) In this section, “**specified**” means specified—

- (a)[^c-e09be1ef2f891403c16d808b4ba71f42] in rules 6.12.3 and 6.12.3A, and annex 2R to chapter 6, of the Collective Investment Schemes sourcebook, or
- (b) in UCITS-related direct EU legislation.
