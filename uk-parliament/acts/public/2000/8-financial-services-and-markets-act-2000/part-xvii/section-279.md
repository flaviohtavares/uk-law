# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 279

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 279. Revocation of recognition.[^c1709572]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The FCA[^c-2002a7ce52dd7d974d24d49e805fed98] may [^c-a4d4bde5cb641a7b6a0308b7febb2779]... revoke an order under section 272 if it appears to the FCA—

- (a) that the operator, trustee or depositary of the scheme has contravened a requirement imposed on him by or under this Act;
- (b) that the operator, trustee or depositary of the scheme has, in purported compliance with any such requirement, knowingly or recklessly given the FCA information which is false or misleading in a material particular;
- (c)[^c1709573] [^c-57c7285e4747ca5ed4e7b53ca376a4ac]... that one or more of the requirements for the making of the order are no longer satisfied; or
- (d) that none of paragraphs (a) to (c) applies, but it is undesirable in the interests of the participants or potential participants that the scheme should continue to be recognised.
