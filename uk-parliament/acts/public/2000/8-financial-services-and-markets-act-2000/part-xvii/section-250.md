# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 250

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 250. Modification or waiver of rules.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this section “**rules**” means—

- (a) trust scheme rules; or
- (b) scheme particulars rules.

(2)[^c1709525] The FCA[^c-3e4eb04b65e89eb5f622d8f7cf8e1291] may, on the application or with the consent of any person to whom any rules apply, direct that all or any of the rules—

- (a) are not to apply to him as respects a particular scheme; or
- (b) are to apply to him, as respects a particular scheme, with such modifications as may be specified in the direction.

(3) The FCA may, on the application or with the consent of the manager and trustee of a particular scheme acting jointly, direct that all or any of the rules—

- (a) are not to apply to the scheme; or
- (b) are to apply to the scheme with such modifications as may be specified in the direction.

(4) Section 138A and subsections (1) to (3), (5) and (6) of section 138B[^c-00577b2cd3303ef8312bf388287262c4] have effect in relation to a direction under subsection (2) as they have effect in relation to a direction under section 138A(1)[^c-3cae3ca58ddf1cd4488e3f398d402364] but with the following modifications—

- (a) [^c2078108]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) any reference to the person[^c2078136] is to be read as a reference to the person mentioned in subsection (2); and
- (c) section 138B(3)(c)[^c-750b611f2daaf5c03ae413eba9601bdc] is to be read, in relation to a participant of the scheme, as if the word “commercial” were omitted.

(5) Section 138A and subsections (1) to (3), (5) and (6) of section 138B[^c-7c36170d92da1f2d81e415180e7153c1] have effect in relation to a direction under subsection (3) as they have effect in relation to a direction under section 138A(1)[^c-206a9e56d9169e87480b3a66772dd3b2] but with the following modifications—

- (a) subsection (4)(a) of section 138A[^c-50ff34bc94b3b2fd3f49af639654f919] is to be read as if the words “by the [^c2078140]. . . person” were omitted;
- (b) section 138B(3)(c) and the definition of “immediate group” in section 421ZA as it applies to that section[^c-cd8059f5ecbb7a971b59aae339af591a] are to be read as if references to the [^c2078140]. . . person were references to each of the manager and the trustee of the scheme;
- (c) section 138B(3)(c)[^c-141bdd3960390c87469dc680bddc0c35] is to be read, in relation to a participant of the scheme, as if the word “commercial” were omitted;
- (d) section 138B(5)[^c-a658753e2500d9e94c1d597f4f92285b] is to be read as if the reference to the [^c2078140]. . . person concerned were a reference to the scheme concerned and to its manager and trustee; and
- (e) section 138A(7)[^c-114d2335152df676b15ee420fd50fed3] is to be read as if the reference to the [^c2078140]. . . person were a reference to the manager and trustee of the scheme acting jointly.
