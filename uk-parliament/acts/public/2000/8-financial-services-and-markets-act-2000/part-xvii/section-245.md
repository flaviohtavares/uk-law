# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 245

## 245. Procedure when refusing an application.[^c1709522]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the FCA[^key-3e4eb04b65e89eb5f622d8f7cf8e1291] proposes to refuse an application made under section 242 it must give each of the applicants a warning notice.

(2) If the FCA decides to refuse the application—

- (a) it must give each of the applicants a decision notice; and
- (b) either applicant may refer the matter to the Tribunal.
