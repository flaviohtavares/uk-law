# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 255

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 255. Procedure.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c1709536] If the FCA[^key-225f639a0482e6aafade78ac497b5cbc] proposes to make an order under section 254 revoking an authorisation order (“**a revoking order**”), it must give separate warning notices to the manager and the trustee of the scheme.

(2) If the FCA decides to make a revoking order, it must without delay give each of them a decision notice and either of them may refer the matter to the Tribunal.
