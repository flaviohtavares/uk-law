# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 244

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 244. Determination of applications.[^c1709520][^c1709521]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to subsection (1A),[^c2109392] An application under section 242(1)(a)[^key-123a8b781d93014af97983b928d45ae0] must be determined by the FCA[^key-3e4eb04b65e89eb5f622d8f7cf8e1291] before the end of the period of six months beginning with the date on which it receives the completed application.

(1A)[^c2109394] An application under section 242(1)(a) in respect of a unit trust scheme which is a[^key-3115749942a8f83bab3c9ebf52981584] UCITS, or an application under section 242(1)(b), must be determined by the FCA before the end of two months beginning with the date on which it receives the application.

(2) The FCA may determine an incomplete application if it considers it appropriate to do so; and it must in any event determine such an application within twelve months beginning with the date on which it first receives the application.

(3) The applicant may withdraw his application, by giving the FCA written notice, at any time before the FCA determines it.
