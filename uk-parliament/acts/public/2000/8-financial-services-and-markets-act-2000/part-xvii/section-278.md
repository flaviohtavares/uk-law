## 278. Rules as to scheme particulars.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The FCA[^key-2002a7ce52dd7d974d24d49e805fed98] may make rules imposing duties or liabilities on the operator of a scheme recognised under section [^key-af4ed2e039be42275d4e92306f63cdff]... 272 for purposes corresponding to those for which rules may be made under section 248 in relation to authorised unit trust schemes.
