# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 263

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 263. Amendment of section 716 Companies Act 1985.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 716(1) of the [^c1709546]Companies Act 1985 (prohibition on formation of companies with more than 20 members unless registered under the Act etc.), after “this Act,” insert “ is incorporated by virtue of regulations made under section 262 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 ”.
