# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 280

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 280. Procedure.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c1709574] If the FCA[^c-2002a7ce52dd7d974d24d49e805fed98] proposes to make an order under section 279[^c-bb19e98aae8bf0625436f02425caeaed] revoking a recognition order, it must give a warning notice to the operator and (if any) the trustee or depositary of the scheme.

(2) If the FCA decides to [^c-8e3063af8a22f77f7713e96374e39494]... make an order under that section—

- (a) it must without delay give a decision notice to the operator and (if any) the trustee or depositary of the scheme; and
- (b) the operator or the trustee or depositary may refer the matter to the Tribunal.
