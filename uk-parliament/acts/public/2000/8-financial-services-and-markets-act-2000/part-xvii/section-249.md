# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 249

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 249. Disciplinary measures[^key-5ae23d2ba70c1f8938681cca138d0df8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c2076810][^key-e04dd9b0ba9cfc30e447ea919a0ee23e] If it appears to the FCA[^key-3e4eb04b65e89eb5f622d8f7cf8e1291] that an auditor has failed to comply with a duty imposed on him by trust scheme rules, it may do one or more of the following—[^key-da12168f6bb1ff80450c1c21aa4394ee]

- (a) disqualify the auditor from being the auditor of any authorised unit trust scheme , authorised contractual scheme[^key-ebadb148fbfd1aaf1b5d00ca3b8a09af] or authorised open-ended investment company;
- (b) publish a statement to the effect that it appears to the FCA that the auditor has failed to comply with the duty;
- (c) impose on the auditor a penalty, payable to the FCA, of such amount as the FCA considers appropriate.

(2)[^key-0da6ab957b93fb98e640d97a4ed4087b] Sections 345B to 345E have effect in relation to the taking of action under subsection (1) as they have effect in relation to the taking of action under section 345(2).
