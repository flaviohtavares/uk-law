# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 313A

## 313A. FCA's power to require suspension or removal of financial instruments from trading[^c2077965][^key-79c91d1906a6b308bf6d22f47f417082]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA[^key-8dceeb235c564efe8d05ca68916e2915] may, for the purpose of protecting—

- (a) the interests of investors, or
- (b) the orderly functioning of the financial markets,

require an institution or a class of institutions[^c2080607] to suspend or remove a financial instrument from trading.

(2)[^c2080615] If the FCA exercises the power conferred by subsection (1), the matter may be referred to the Tribunal by—

- (a) the institution or, as the case may be, any institution in the class, or
- (b) the issuer of the financial instrument (if any).

(3) In this section, “**trading**” includes trading otherwise than on a trading venue[^key-1df5eb603c98c37594ef9e3c818d9eaa] .
