## 207. Proposal to take disciplinary measures.[^c1709465][^c1751218][^c-dc7a9ea07b21b84d8e10f10b8cb7a6c8][^c-97c15f88da6006c123913550c828fa23][^c-a8d1c5a10ccb097a9272ad4aace457a9][^c-8db4b12de91eaca59034347131164485][^c-7b69e4cdd84c6f92660f082b9f74eca1][^c-778a8347d711f822b0cba7c3e729c9ae][^c-e8362c0a14e85ecb33e37eaa4a8c4fb1][^c-df60e3d40c500902034d26ceba7806cd][^c-b91177596964cac36bbb25759ed68aa5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^c-8db4b12de91eaca59034347131164485]

(1) If a regulator[^c-838f0cf7144cbd8666b03521238b80dd] proposes—

- (a) to publish a statement in respect of an authorised person (under section 205(1)[^c-ad6f49ede1ddb515e688a4ed7e213459]), [^c2080735]...
- (b) to impose a penalty on an authorised person (under section 206(1)[^c-57c7c63186e3fae5ed5c075db049c8f2]), or[^c2080737]
- (c) to suspend a permission of an authorised person or impose a restriction in relation to the carrying on of a regulated activity by an authorised person (under section 206A),

it must give the authorised person a warning notice.

(1A)[^c-fe7f671bf641b1ec47eef6ec0935b806] If the FCA proposes—

- (a) to publish a statement in respect of a person under section 205(2),
- (b) to impose a penalty on a person under section 206(1A), or
- (c) to impose a prohibition or restriction on a person under section 206B,

it must give the person a warning notice.

(2) A warning notice about a proposal to publish a statement must set out the terms of the statement.

(3) A warning notice about a proposal to impose a penalty, must state the amount of the penalty.

(4)[^c2080739] A warning notice about a proposal to suspend a permission or impose a prohibition or[^c-6533d6de1ce2c6f294cba12739fec1f6] restriction must state the period for which the suspension , prohibition[^c-a51e0efe44d959c01d3eb479e9622906] or restriction is to have effect.
