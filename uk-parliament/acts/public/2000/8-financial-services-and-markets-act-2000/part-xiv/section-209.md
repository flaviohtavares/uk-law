# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 209

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 209. Publication.[^c1751220][^c-dc7a9ea07b21b84d8e10f10b8cb7a6c8][^c-97c15f88da6006c123913550c828fa23][^c-a8d1c5a10ccb097a9272ad4aace457a9][^c-4505fca971bd179e1d17b3b59b3effd9][^c-8440deea05be87b99fcaeb1a62d74cc9][^c-5cb8c6382de87014ef094cd8288f84a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-35724ffb7c7ec6feea480d7b62428c2e] After a statement under section 205(1)[^c-d095026f18f90adbe17fb37d9ee8b779] is published, the regulator concerned[^c-31411db2dabd3c33d3da26f3543f3fba] must send a copy of it to the authorised person and to any person on whom a copy of the decision notice was given under section 393(4).

(2)[^c-3cbf0b782c57f398aefb2c45102d58d3] After a statement under section 205(2) is published, the FCA must send a copy of it to the person to whom the statement relates and to any person to whom a copy of the decision notice was given under section 393(4).
