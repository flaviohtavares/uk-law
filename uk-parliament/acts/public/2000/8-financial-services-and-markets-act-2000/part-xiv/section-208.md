## 208. Decision notice.[^c1709466][^c1751219][^key-dc7a9ea07b21b84d8e10f10b8cb7a6c8][^key-97c15f88da6006c123913550c828fa23][^key-a8d1c5a10ccb097a9272ad4aace457a9][^key-8db4b12de91eaca59034347131164485][^key-db93719423e994fcc2d957bce108f93d][^key-014a402d4b3e5227adb897d8ae3e4ab8][^key-c54458f7cab5053e46876d3dbe0dba49][^key-1479c6b5cf086ce879aa5d3180fb7e55][^key-38313479e76e894520630c6ca29d8563][^key-ebdd8ca57672ea5b4484a7663d3711ad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-382ee182f5efcfdf21efeffe306039b7][^key-a55bd7b2d0ab039e5cbd2822caf75362][^key-8db4b12de91eaca59034347131164485]

(1) If a regulator[^key-629eacda21f5457539df4a1095dc8324] decides—

- (a)[^c1709467] to publish a statement under section 205(1)[^key-7c2601bd7765c6437ff6ec8efd6b0732] (whether or not in the terms proposed), [^c2080741]. . .
- (b)[^c1709467] to impose a penalty under section 206(1)[^key-39ddedaaa1f86dec164da414b7370127] (whether or not of the amount proposed), or[^c2080743]
- (c) to suspend a permission or impose a restriction under section 206A (whether or not in the manner proposed),

it must without delay give the authorised person concerned a decision notice.

(1A)[^key-b1920ce0a02f7f65b145e22345aa3d20] If the FCA decides—

- (a) to publish a statement in respect of a person under section 205(2),
- (b) to impose a penalty on a person under section 206(1A), or
- (c) to impose a prohibition or restriction on a person under section 206B,

it must without delay give the person a decision notice.

(2) In the case of a statement, the decision notice must set out the terms of the statement.

(3) In the case of a penalty, the decision notice must state the amount of the penalty.

(3A)[^c2080747] In the case of a suspension , prohibition[^key-268db8844ea38386af08ae0dd2dc7dea] or restriction, the decision notice must state the period for which the suspension , prohibition or restriction is to have effect.

(4)[^key-0132f5f4fe5b292d76596a84de3042be][^key-ce0b0088dadcac89a6ad82ebb591456e][^key-c54ccbc6a0c3a14975f2bb29d3e94c09] If a regulator[^key-ff4aa88edb8f3b3f10b4ed53e209e013] decides to—

- (a) publish a statement in respect of an authorised person under section 205(1)[^key-336a3517351a7a87626e5051640cfb59], [^c2080749]. . .
- (b) impose a penalty on an authorised person under section 206(1)[^key-e3acd389d25b929cbec864226791e73e], or[^c2080751]
- (c) suspend a permission of an authorised person, or impose a restriction in relation to the carrying on of a regulated activity by an authorised person, under section 206A,

the authorised person may refer the matter to the Tribunal.

(5)[^key-3a7d126b67d04a9be331c828371bca34] If the FCA decides to—

- (a) publish a statement in respect of a person under section 205(2),
- (b) impose a penalty on a person under section 206(1A), or
- (c) impose a prohibition or restriction on a person under section 206B,

the person may refer the matter to the Tribunal.
