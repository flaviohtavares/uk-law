# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 232A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 232A. Scheme operator's duty to provide information to FCA[^c-e97a38974e8d2cce42da64df1e3010de][^c-06e1714df06f71282002a963d7fa4194]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-8a7a27324b5f92636177d5df1db21272] If the scheme operator considers that it has information that, in its opinion, would or might be of assistance to the FCA in advancing one or more of the FCA's operational objectives , or the purpose for which the FCA must exercise its functions under Part 8B (see section[^c-5182e3765dd126b364719ac05575f4e9] 131U(1)),, it must disclose that information to the FCA.

(2)[^c-93ed44479105b4ea641086c88007b056] The reference in subsection (1) to the FCA’s operational objectives includes, in its application as a secondary objective, the competitiveness and growth objective (see section 1EB).
