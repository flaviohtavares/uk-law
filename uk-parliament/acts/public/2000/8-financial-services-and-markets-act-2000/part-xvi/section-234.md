# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 234

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 234. Industry funding.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c1709509][^c2076760][^c-3eba550e559b7e88e74b1c3e1557e659] For the purpose of funding—

- (a) the establishment of the ombudsman scheme (whenever any relevant expense is incurred), and
- (b) its operation in relation to the compulsory jurisdiction,

the FCA[^c-57449dae9a90f91008d4906e8b620caf] may make rules requiring the payment to it or to the scheme operator, by authorised persons or any class of authorised person , any electronic money issuer within the meaning of the Electronic Money Regulations 2011[^c2081010] or any payment service provider within the meaning of the Payment Services Regulations[^c2080419] 2017[^c-8d6c1bd12c84882b8547ba9a984d2e23] of specified amounts (or amounts calculated in a specified way).

(2) “**Specified**” means specified in the rules.
