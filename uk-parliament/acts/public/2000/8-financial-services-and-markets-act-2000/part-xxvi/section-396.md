# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 396

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 396. Statements under section 395: consultation.[^c-26ec0b1c70e94c2a40a8c72e7f5378eb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before issuing a statement of its[^c-a6efa389878fdc85b0472d3ebe8db983] procedure under section 395, the regulator[^c-cf7b412b6985c0b7a05deb2b0bf267f9] must publish a draft of the proposed statement in the way appearing to it[^c-699580cb3f9e45b047d8e5a82eb70a07] to be best calculated to bring the draft[^c-2baddb1e5002d7a52c8eaaa95729412c] to the attention of the public.

(2) The draft must be accompanied by notice that representations about the proposal may be made to the regulator publishing the draft[^c-3a06267779a810f46dfe414ecfe04e16] within a specified time.

(3) Before a regulator issues the proposed statement of its procedure, it[^c-414fc9d86305e7aab07e640b3e3b184c] must have regard to any representations made to it in accordance with subsection (2).

(4) If the regulator issues the proposed statement of its procedure,[^c-b70e5a432630996a162066472f0c8f2b] it must publish an account, in general terms, of—

- (a) the representations made to it in accordance with subsection (2); and
- (b) its response to them.

(5) If the statement of the regulator's procedure differs from the draft published by it[^c-ba8d260c84e92c9b83cf3aa27ec5d9d2] under subsection (1) in a way which is , in its opinion,[^c-5467b6232dba3faf9610d129ca937646] significant, it must[^c-a81ec794632623e816769a0a6335f316] (in addition to complying with subsection (4)) publish details of the difference.

(6) The regulator publishing a draft under subsection (1)[^c-bcc46a9ddcb63b39ede693b9c3205f6c] may charge a reasonable fee for providing a person with a copy of the draft[^c-0c07bafa25276fe58dc6600f50c50990].

(7) This section also applies to a proposal to revise a statement of policy.
