(1)[^c-54ec5efae09bec7f12d456e44e457bc8] In the case of a warning notice falling within subsection (1ZB)—

- (a) neither the regulator giving the notice nor a person to whom it is given or copied may publish the notice,
- (b) a person to whom the notice is given or copied may not publish any details concerning the notice unless the regulator giving the notice has published those details, and
- (c)[^c-dad644e180d2d15fab44a4094cbfec0a] after consulting the persons to whom the notice is given or copied, the regulator giving the notice may publish such information about the matter to which the notice relates as it considers appropriate.

(1ZA) In the case of a warning notice not falling within subsection (1ZB), neither the regulator giving the notice nor a person to whom it is given or copied may publish the notice or any details concerning it.

(1ZB) A warning notice falls within this subsection if it is given under—

- (a) section 63B;
- (b) section 67;
- (c)[^c-256d78544878e0727cd113b61d2da490] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) section 88B;
- (e) section 89K;
- (f) section 89R;
- (g) section 92;
- (h) section 126;
- (i)[^c-3a48c43a2134d138e4bcc6d4a1f8fefa] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (ia)[^c-caab30b8247e09d3b8094938d7289766] section 142N;
- (ib)[^c-f4a8e9cb9cfb22c9105c038e600eb414] section 143T;
- (ic) section 143X;
- (j) section 192L;
- (k) section 207;
- (ka)[^c-c24141a7fa08b803e3d4c1348baf4773] section 309V;
- (kb) section 309Z4;
- (l) section 312G;
- (la)[^c-22a1ba2344170ffd5fa399cd5c535043] section 312S;
- (m) section 345B (whether as a result of section 345(2) or 345A(3) or section 249(1) or 261K(1)[^c-1192352bf0b472683c94a8062f63e5e6]).
- (n)[^c-720b06ab07367abd8b62e270b3f63c08]  regulation 41(1) of the Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024.

(1A)[^c2077820] A person to whom a decision notice is given or copied may not publish the notice or any details concerning it unless the regulator giving the notice[^c-5bb48f5f6db5eb45354ca1cd66904905] has published the notice or those details.

(2) A notice of discontinuance must state that, if the person to whom the notice is given consents, the regulator giving the notice[^c-46434d355d128a50b04f2b6c5f1832b8] may publish such information as it considers appropriate about the matter to which the discontinued proceedings related.

(3) A copy of a notice of discontinuance must be accompanied by a statement that, if the person to whom the notice is copied consents, the regulator giving the notice[^c-4f52a796d914623656fd8c6920f6be76] may publish such information as it considers appropriate about the matter to which the discontinued proceedings related, so far as relevant to that person.

(4)[^c1709718][^c1709719] The regulator giving a decision or final notice[^c-4479a0ee59ff5e053837c6147c478af1] must publish such information about the matter to which the notice[^c-75fae035cb37e243ae189466051982ea] relates as it considers appropriate.

(4A)[^c-41a31d1f14c00573b1792dd97e395f2b] Subsection (4) is subject to sections 391A[^c-c2fd76b60422d055cb1cbf343965bb29] , 391B[^c-2b3a4c8feb2e06dbc7f3af4b4a3cce04] , 391C[^c-5e7557d2171a7469703786946622b79d] 391D, 391E and 391F[^c-6d3866b77f875d3fd2e1c68cfc57de5f].

(5) When a supervisory notice takes effect, the regulator giving the notice[^c-cdc5ad774dc661f75adaab92b8dc5f71] must publish such information about the matter to which the notice relates as it considers appropriate.

(5A)[^c-9980aa643e2bd9162a2c9b420b4ac5c3] Subsection (5) does not apply in relation to a notice given in accordance with section 137S(5) or (8)(a) (but see section 137S(11)).

(6)[^c-58bfd943eb0c2d28677c3420b9187290] The FCA may not publish information under this section if, in its opinion, publication of the information would be—

- (a) unfair to the person with respect to whom the action was taken (or was proposed to be taken),
- (b)[^c-86c2cb4f67527578b5fcfcc19de1af99] prejudicial to the interests of consumers, or
- (c) detrimental to the stability of the UK financial system.

(6A) The PRA may not publish information under this section if, in its opinion, publication of the information would be—

- (a) unfair to the person with respect to whom the action was taken (or was proposed to be taken),
- (b) prejudicial to the safety and soundness of PRA-authorised persons, or
- (c) in a case where section 2C applies, prejudicial to securing the appropriate degree of protection for policyholders.

(7)[^c1709718] Information is to be published under this section in such manner as the regulator[^c-f46e755f21a2bf8c2adfb2606ee1d4b7] considers appropriate.

- (a)[^c2076974] the period during which any person may refer the matter to the Tribunal is still running;
- (b)[^c2076974] the matter has been referred to the Tribunal but has not been dealt with;

- (d)[^c2076974] such an appeal has been brought but has not been determined.

(8A)[^c-a5a5ee4b2d6e82dfa0671ef9823c0187] Where a decision notice or final notice relates to any decision or action under a provision of this Act in relation to the contravention of a CSD requirement[^c-ab2599dac9ae54d79d7c4c990eca5e78], this section has effect subject to Article 62 of the CSD regulation (publication of decisions).

(8AA)[^c-90e4b9b33cc0f0e4919ad7df90ae73db] A “CSD requirement” is a requirement imposed by—

- (a) the CSD regulation,
- (b) any EU regulation, originally made under the CSD regulation, which is assimilated direct[^c-0fbc1940421f538aaffa8dc3f0827578] legislation, or
- (c) any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made under the CSD regulation on or after IP completion day.

(8B)[^c-b7fd170471edf2f5c9f8b44a1f84366d] Where a decision notice or final notice relates to any decision or action under a provision of this Act in relation to the contravention of a market abuse requirement[^c-bacebeaaa89b96f2bf68850198d01d25], this section has effect subject to Article 34 of the market abuse regulation (publication of decisions).

(8BA)[^c-5f39e214e0926d14bfc10ec157fbd091] A “market abuse requirement” is a requirement imposed by—

- (c) any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made under the market abuse regulation on or after IP completion day.
