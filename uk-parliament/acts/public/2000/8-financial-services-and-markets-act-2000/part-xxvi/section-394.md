## 394. Access to FCA or PRA material.[^c1709726][^c2074587][^c2075198][^c-db3ba4dbaf22a053424e165d3f64f184][^c-4ef20efe6435c02a59466aac8df3b352][^c-494f14bcc1662c2df5ede86205d9d940][^c-38303859a7795ca5cf1dbd77c0b83cef][^c-e0292c0ecba857b421a7910a15292944][^c-c164e92b28d67f32c459e1b168c6dc66][^c-0ae867858b56278b63f609d08173b0c3][^c-6427af07cd81177766c115db600bd183][^c-da4721b6a4af5a8004e25b1b7e510acd][^c-9254815a56c927705240298efd51b064][^c-75159879adc661cb50557fdaad4ae566]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a regulator[^c-daff4dbd701977432002641b329a493b] gives a person (“**A**”) a notice to which this section applies, it must—

- (a) allow him access to the material on which it relied in taking the decision which gave rise to the obligation to give the notice;
- (b) allow him access to any secondary material which , in the regulator's opinion,[^c-72cc935574eec8409eea6f614175181b] might undermine that decision.

(2) But the regulator giving the notice[^c-b490bdc50d0094a9eec9754ce985c797] does not have to allow A access to material under subsection (1) if the material is excluded material or it—

- (a) relates to a case involving a person other than A; and
- (b) was taken into account by the regulator giving the notice in A’s case only for purposes of comparison with other cases.

(3) The regulator giving the notice[^c-a5a2b7f5f58188d137efe1f5db3a8400] may refuse access A to particular material which it would otherwise have to allow him access to if, in its opinion, allowing him access to the material—

- (a) would not be in the public interest; or
- (b) would not be fair, having regard to—
  - (i) the likely significance of the material to A in relation to the matter in respect of which he has been given a notice to which this section applies; and
  - (ii) the potential prejudice to the commercial interests of a person other than A which would be caused by the material’s disclosure.

(4) If the regulator giving the notice[^c-3707391ca6f54edbf239c8df32a3a7ee] does not allow A access to material because it is excluded material consisting of a protected item, it must give A written notice of—

- (a) the existence of the protected item; and
- (b) the regulator's[^c-d15e397bce191eb4a4fddb3221547565] decision not to allow him access to it.

(5) If the regulator giving the notice[^c-1eec0bc3c5acbb1f78f96042b99fd020] refuses under subsection (3) to allow A access to material, it must give him written notice of—

- (a) the refusal; and
- (b) the reasons for it.

(6) “**Secondary material**” means material, other than material falling within paragraph (a) of subsection (1) which—

- (a) was considered by the regulator giving the notice[^c-4653d165f42bbed6b0b40f81cd1032d7] in reaching the decision mentioned in that paragraph; or
- (b) was obtained by the regulator giving the notice in connection with the matter to which that notice[^c-470386586bd3bb109cfacd13ce5de21a] relates but which was not considered by it in reaching that decision.

(7) “**Excluded material**” means material which—

- (a)[^c1709727] is material the disclosure of which for the purposes of or in connection with any legal proceedings is prohibited by section 56 of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016[^c-af3cffc20b05a80ee1bea5585b999b21]; or
- (c) is a protected item (as defined in section 413).
