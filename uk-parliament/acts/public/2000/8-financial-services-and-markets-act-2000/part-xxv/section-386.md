# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 386

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 386. Decision notices.[^c1709694][^key-8e3cf7618955c41c737b25fcadd10767][^key-ed28e9bfe8df9d4bc8941477a6f282a0][^key-94a5db8633b87b579a5c6e31a2d0c91f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c1709695] If the regulator[^key-1e50fdecbd12f242cb3c4a1db884ceec] decides to exercise the power under section 384(5), it must give a decision notice to the person in relation to whom the power is exercised.

(2) The decision notice must—

- (a) state the amount that he is to pay or distribute as mentioned in section 384(5);
- (b) identify the person or persons to whom that amount is to be paid or among whom that amount is to be distributed; and
- (c) state the arrangements in accordance with which the payment or distribution is to be made.

(3) If the regulator[^key-8e91a99ae646503e12e9e75ff520b809] decides to exercise the power under section 384(5), the person in relation to whom it is exercised may refer the matter to the Tribunal.
