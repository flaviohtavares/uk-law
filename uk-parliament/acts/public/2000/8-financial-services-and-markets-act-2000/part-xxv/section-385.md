# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 385

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 385. Warning notices.[^c1709693][^key-193222db21f2564cf1c3c337952ef901][^key-ed28e9bfe8df9d4bc8941477a6f282a0][^key-1ea50c760abbd7f8df5034d55ce9c581]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a regulator[^key-f5ffefb0b6645384ef508bbc6746445e] proposes to exercise the power under section 384(5) in relation to a person, it must give him a warning notice.

(2) A warning notice under this section must specify the amount which the regulator[^key-2af708ffb5f9205955163f671ad6f31e] proposes to require the person concerned to pay or distribute as mentioned in section 384(5).
