# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 131M

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 131M. Overview[^c-fc4ab12a6f7e8c559f905cdc2c6661c9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part—

- (a) requires the Treasury to publish a statement of policy concerning cash deposit and withdrawal services,
- (b) enables the Treasury to designate persons involved in the provision of such services, and
- (c) gives the FCA functions in relation to designated persons.
