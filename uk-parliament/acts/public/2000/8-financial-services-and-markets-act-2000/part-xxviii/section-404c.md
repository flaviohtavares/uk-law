# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 404C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 404C. Enforcement[^c-cdfd60fd9105c487216563401b77c128][^c2078023]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions—

- (a) Part 14 (disciplinary measures), and
- (b) so much of this Act as relates to any provision of that Part,

(which , subject to section 415AA(1),[^c-f3e8112acc72678868507f6534515e10] apply only in relation to authorised persons) are also to apply in relation to relevant firms which are not (or are no longer) authorised persons.
