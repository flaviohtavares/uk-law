# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 415A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 415A. Powers under the Act[^c2077312][^key-a7a80238e877fccb497fc1f86843d514]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any power which the FCA, the PRA or the Bank of England[^key-adee8725b757fc4b559539f2bf157e5a] has under any provision of this Act is not limited in any way by any other power which it has under any other provision of this Act.
