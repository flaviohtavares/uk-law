# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 410

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 410. International obligations.[^c1709753]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If it appears to the Treasury that any action proposed to be taken by a relevant person would be incompatible with any[^key-4cd6c2be4b56d951d1c8da628eedb014] international obligations of the United Kingdom, they may direct that person not to take that action.

(2) If it appears to the Treasury that any action which a relevant person has power to take is required for the purpose of implementing any such obligations, they may direct that person to take that action.

(3) A direction under this section—

- (a) may include such supplemental or incidental requirements as the Treasury consider necessary or expedient; and
- (b) is enforceable, on an application made by the Treasury, by injunction or, in Scotland, by an order for specific performance under section 45 of the [^c1709754]Court of Session Act 1988.

(4) “**Relevant person**” means—

- (a)[^key-fc2ad05f06f628c01b41216efe2bf822] the FCA;
- (aa) the PRA;
- (ab) the Bank of England when exercising functions conferred on it by Part 18;
- (b)[^key-4bf97a37f493c6433d05eb69485510b8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) any recognised investment exchange (other than one which is an overseas investment exchange);
- (d) any recognised clearing house (other than one which is an overseas clearing house);
- (da)[^key-9bea7f85e481c5167f6d6a80dd46db90] any recognised CSD;
- (e) a person included in the list maintained under section 301; or
- (f) the scheme operator of the ombudsman scheme.
