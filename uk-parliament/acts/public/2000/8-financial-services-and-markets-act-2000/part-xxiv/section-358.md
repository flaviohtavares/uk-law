## 358. Powers of FCA and PRA to participate in proceedings: trust deeds for creditors in Scotland.[^c-bcef4cba6f9d5d9ea9881b1521037593]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where a trust deed has been granted by or on behalf of a debtor who is an authorised person or recognised investment exchange[^c-8214f141149a4a55b0f7174525480a60].

(2) The trustee must, as soon as practicable after he becomes aware that the debtor is an authorised person or recognised investment exchange[^c-3dabe0c324c001cd177a8996d6daf1ab], send to the appropriate regulator[^c-64a4b3dbb41973712bd270942e1aee7c]—

- (a) in every case, a copy of the trust deed;
- (b) where any other document or information is sent to every creditor known to the trustee in pursuance of section 170 of the 2016[^c-b01f5133d51e573690b4878b81ae53d3] Act, a copy of such document or information.

(3)[^c-70df7f992c813900c8029db15660904a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The appropriate regulator must be given the same notice as the creditors of any meeting of creditors held in relation to the trust deed.

(5) A person appointed for the purpose by the appropriate regulator[^c-dca1a58b223182a0003895d4bdd07448] is entitled to attend and participate in (but not to vote at) any such meeting of creditors as if that regulator[^c-ee2f656cef78bf67e1fc95c0340230d7] were a creditor under the deed.

(6) This section does not affect any right a regulator[^c-c35b59870875b2f02bfc559a7666b985] has as a creditor of a debtor who is an authorised person or recognised investment exchange[^c-82e4c270f64807c84ff8dd4027878331].

(6A)[^c-b38842d2b78ba4536cb6a8ebb100086b] "The appropriate regulator” means—

- (a) in the case of a PRA-authorised person—
  - (i) for the purposes of subsections (2) [^c-0c94c2126485ef82a8c44c3f3eb43662]... and (4), each of the FCA and the PRA, and
  - (ii) for the purposes of subsection (5), the FCA or the PRA;
- (b) in any other case, the FCA.

(7) Expressions used in this section and in the 2016[^c-076a9dc0cd4bfef1b0e2e49eb2e938d2] Act have the same meaning in this section as in that Act.
