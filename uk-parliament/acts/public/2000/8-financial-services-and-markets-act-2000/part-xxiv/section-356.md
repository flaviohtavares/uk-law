# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 356

## 356. Powers of FCA and PRA to participate in proceedings: company voluntary arrangements.[^c1709654][^c2074528][^key-6c726ce774761585e89b01ae30ff8494][^key-1f90dfbee55906b92e509e459a14fcab]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c1709655] Where a voluntary arrangement has effect under Part I of the 1986 Act in respect of a company or insolvent partnership which is an authorised person, or recognised investment exchange, the appropriate regulator[^key-abd56bdbda3308cf14be7afce5f06456] may apply to the court under section 6 or 7 of that Act.

(2)[^c1709656] Where a voluntary arrangement has been approved under Part II of the 1989 Order in respect of a company or insolvent partnership which is an authorised person, or recognised investment exchange, the appropriate regulator[^key-6ed3496a6d469c40b7a9ee4b92171c8f] may apply to the court under Article 19 or 20 of that Order.

(3) If a person other than a regulator[^key-e9d44ef71525bb581294e0ad55dad987] makes an application to the court in relation to the company or insolvent partnership under any[^c1709657] of those provisions, the appropriate regulator[^key-39dacd97489f394e1001e5af2e0c7a38] is entitled to be heard at any hearing relating to the application.

(4)[^key-073585b651fec7967a24331c8f7870e3] "The appropriate regulator” means—

- (a) in the case of a PRA-authorised person—
  - (i) for the purposes of subsections (1) and (2), the FCA or the PRA, and
  - (ii) for the purposes of subsection (3), each of the FCA and the PRA;
- (b) in any other case, the FCA.

(5) If either regulator makes an application to the court under any of those provisions in relation to a PRA-authorised person, the other regulator is entitled to be heard at any hearing relating to the application.
