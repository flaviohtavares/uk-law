## 373. Insolvency practitioner’s duty to report to FCA and PRA.[^c-45861ec5cbdb0f689e1a545c06fe7b63]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If—

- (a) a bankruptcy order or sequestration award is in force in relation to an individual [^c-964999ef80789008b9a67ee63efac313]..., and
- (b) it appears to the insolvency practitioner that the individual is carrying on, or has carried on—[^c-7baf0fb608f98807206df80dfe40f457]
- (i) a regulated activity in contravention of the general prohibition, or
- (ii) a credit-related regulated activity in contravention of section 20,

the insolvency practitioner must report the matter without delay to the FCA and, if the regulated activity concerned is a PRA-regulated activity, to the PRA[^c-2f6b2fc0d5226c781fa98cab6f6c620b].

(1A)[^c-58d0fe0c63918f747207508245172821] Subsection (1) does not apply where—

- (a) the bankruptcy order or sequestration award is in force by virtue of a petition presented by a regulator, and
- (b) the regulator's petition depended on a contravention by the individual of the general prohibition.

(2) “**Bankruptcy order**” means a bankruptcy order under Part IX of the 1986 Act (or Part IX of the 1989 Order).

(3) “**Sequestration award**” means an award of sequestration under section 22 of the 2016[^c-33a2c4e0a3fe5238dec9fde0ed9c2602] Act.

(4) “**Individual**” includes an entity mentioned in section 374(1)(c).
