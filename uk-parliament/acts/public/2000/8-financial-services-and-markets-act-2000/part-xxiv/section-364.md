# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 364

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 364. Receiver’s duty to report to FCA and PRA[^c1709667][^c2074541][^key-09c8f53cd43e59be6172079b88154d11]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If—

- (a) a receiver has been appointed in relation to a company, and
- (b) it appears to the receiver that the company is carrying on, or has carried on, a regulated activity in contravention of the general prohibition or a credit-related regulated activity in contravention of section 20[^key-da7206ada7d343779cd88701b4d13863],

the receiver must report the matter without delay to the FCA and, if the regulated activity concerned is a PRA-regulated activity, to the PRA[^key-50b78b2c86fdb5bae805f18d1024dc84].
