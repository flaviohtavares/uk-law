# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 113. Appointment of actuary in relation to reduction of benefits.[^c1709303]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if an order has been made under section 111(1).

(2) The court making the order may, on the application of either regulator[^c-6df0e5ff342b6bc024b8f12f21c561f7], appoint an independent actuary—

- (a) to investigate the business transferred under the scheme; and
- (b) to report to the regulator which made the application[^c-8503b30fe7bebe8d878645c0c30bff84] on any reduction in the benefits payable under policies entered into by the transferor concerned[^c-75f9d1c53aae4b2689a2fde035857ebc] that, in the opinion of the actuary, ought to be made.

(3)[^c-0fbceaebf578df279b076cbfc5bbaac4] An application under subsection (2) may be made by the PRA only if—

- (a) the transferor concerned or the transferee is a PRA-authorised person, or
- (b) the transferor concerned or the transferee has as a member of its immediate group a PRA-authorised person.
