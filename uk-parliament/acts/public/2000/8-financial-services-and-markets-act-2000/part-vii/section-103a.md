# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 103A

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103A. Meaning of “the appropriate regulator”[^key-f249092e348a344cbd272316f4599d0d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “**the appropriate regulator**” means—

- (a) in relation to a ring-fencing transfer scheme or a scheme (other than a ring-fencing transfer scheme)[^key-a4c0ed68d4557831170fe1342254cda3] in respect of which the transferor concerned[^key-75f9d1c53aae4b2689a2fde035857ebc] is a PRA-authorised person, the PRA;
- (b) in any other case, the FCA.

(2) In this Part, “the transferor concerned”—

- (a) in the case of an insurance business transfer scheme, is to be read in accordance with section 105(2);
- (b) in the case of a banking business transfer scheme, is to be read in accordance with section 106(2);
- (c) in the case of a reclaim fund business transfer scheme, means the reclaim fund to whose business the scheme relates.
- (d)[^key-89c145ef0e31ef7198067e79902dd0ac] in the case of a ring-fencing transfer scheme, means the body to whose business the scheme relates.
