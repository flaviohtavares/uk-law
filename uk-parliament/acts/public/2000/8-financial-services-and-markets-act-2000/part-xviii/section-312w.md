# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 312W

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 312W. Application of provisions of this Act to this Chapter[^c-324dd5e825c1cf2b2115d54b810555ac]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions do not apply for the purposes of this Chapter—

- (a) section 3D (duty to ensure co-ordinated exercise of functions);
- (b) section 3E (memorandum of understanding);
- (c) section 138D (actions for damages).
