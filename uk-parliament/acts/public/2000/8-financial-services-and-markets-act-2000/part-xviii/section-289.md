# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 289

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 289. Applications: supplementary.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) At any time after receiving an application and before determining it, the appropriate regulator[^key-0453b5b89ac8182768c31a116988fee1] may require the applicant to provide such further information as it reasonably considers necessary to enable it to determine the application.

(2) Information which the appropriate regulator[^key-dc526f834b826c2b2aea7dce0e3eefd9] requires in connection with an application must be provided in such form, or verified in such manner, as the appropriate regulator may direct.

(3) Different directions may be given, or requirements imposed, by the appropriate regulator[^key-5730d4d03a1a4f29672a69cd6f128f17] with respect to different applications.

(4)[^key-462e55d98e2f554e926ef959bc9f19d9] In relation to an application under section 288(1), this section does not apply to information which can be required under Article 17 of the EMIR regulation.

(5)[^key-336e4e923405ced225bc3c0f33c9d653] In relation to an application under section 288A, this section applies only in relation to information which the Bank of England may require in connection with recognition requirements which do not derive from any of the following—[^key-6982428367223e5bcec122e5724cc7c6]

- (a) the CSD Regulation;
- (b) any EU regulation, originally made under the CSD regulation, which is assimilated direct[^key-679cf2a5413ae562cd45ae83d2ce594e] legislation;
- (c) any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made under the CSD regulation on or after IP completion day.
