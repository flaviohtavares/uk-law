# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 293A

## 293A. Information: compliance with specified requirements[^key-ab7a3bf32516ff022b05ced2fd51c0ac][^key-84675d29aa3c453ec2a6ac119c2b55fd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-5cecdae25ce912035cc67f57ab23d2d4] The appropriate regulator may require a recognised body to give the appropriate regulator such information as the appropriate regulator reasonably requires in order to satisfy itself that the body is complying with any qualifying provision[^key-8305df4e313b813916f8cfec1925eb35] that is specified, or of a description specified, for the purposes of this section by the Treasury by order.

(2)[^key-b896b882b1c227b03ebf614bfc575949] The Bank of England may require a third country CSD[^key-c87cbf40636a84123e600d2098e075b3] which provides any services referred to in the Annex to the CSD regulation in the United Kingdom to give the Bank reports on those services and statistical information relating to those services, at such times or in respect of such periods as may be specified by the Bank.

(3) A requirement under subsection (2) extends only to information which the Bank may reasonably require for the exercise of its functions under the CSD regulation or , any EU regulation originally made under the CSD regulation which is[^key-5eec087e1fcdfd4d79057afad5eba298] assimilated direct[^key-808c1564b8ca8ccdfa1d5fd5b55eb928] legislation, or any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made under the CSD regulation on or after IP completion day.
