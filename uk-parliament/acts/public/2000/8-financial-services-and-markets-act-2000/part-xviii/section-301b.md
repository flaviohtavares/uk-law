# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 301B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 301B. Requirements for section 301A notices[^c2090267]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)  A section 301A notice must be in such form, include such information and be accompanied by such documents as the FCA[^key-9297ad01567b9238f94e63e25a0d19f1] may reasonably require.

(2) The FCA must publish a list of its requirements as to the form, information and accompanying documents for a section 301A notice.

(3) The FCA may impose different requirements for different cases and may vary or waive requirements in particular cases.
