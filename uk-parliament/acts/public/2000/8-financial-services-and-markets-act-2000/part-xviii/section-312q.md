> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 312Q. Power of censure[^c-324dd5e825c1cf2b2115d54b810555ac]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a relevant regulator considers that a critical third party has contravened a requirement imposed by or under this Chapter the regulator may publish a statement to that effect.
