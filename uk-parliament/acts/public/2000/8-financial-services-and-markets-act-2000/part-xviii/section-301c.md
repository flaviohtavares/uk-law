# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 301C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 301C. Acknowledgment of receipt[^c2090267]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)  The FCA[^c-b5e9c4e4cc2ab780d4f4f8854480fb9a] must acknowledge receipt of a section 301A notice in writing before the end of the second working day following receipt.

(2) If the FCA[^c-6c17243e10e7fa6afac7502d82196245] receives an incomplete section 301A notice it must inform the section 301A notice-giver as soon as reasonably practicable.
