# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 299

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 299. Complaints about recognised bodies.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The appropriate regulator[^c-00f47b64acaf2e8afe0e8f3dc5241a01] must make arrangements for the investigation of any relevant complaint about a recognised body.

(2) “**Relevant complaint**” means a complaint which the appropriate regulator[^c-aa22089d64760327d243ccf8e31937ad] considers is relevant to the question of whether the body concerned should remain a recognised body.
