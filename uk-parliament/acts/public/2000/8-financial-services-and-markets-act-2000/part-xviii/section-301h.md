# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 301H

## 301H. Duration of approval[^c2090267]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)  Approval of an acquisition is effective for such period as the FCA[^c-18926b3d0a04f0f6149f9482dd3c1de9] may specify in writing.

(2) Where the FCA[^c-cc0bec80efb61061de6500b0ceb3bc45] has specified a period under subsection (1), it may extend the period.

(3) Where the FCA[^c-129f8fa6fce44fbcd9f28ee2c3d92faa] has not specified a period, the approval is effective for one year beginning with the date—

- (a) of the notice given under section 301G(3)(a);
- (b) on which the FCA is treated as having given approval under section 301G(5); or
- (c) of a decision on a reference to the Tribunal which results in the person receiving approval.
