# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 31

## 31. Authorised persons.[^c1709140][^c-5ef31be532a4e5f51726e974f332fe80]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following persons are authorised for the purposes of this Act—

- (a) a person who has a Part 4A permission[^c-53e99c1d52b7ff63e2e77270307ce428] to carry on one or more regulated activities;
- (b)[^c-609cba0f0bb6a666dee5932184d63c23] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-ebd49bd48b5d56cce73b064a620d9fc7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) a person who is otherwise authorised by a provision of, or made under, this Act.

(2) In this Act “**authorised person**” means a person who is authorised for the purposes of this Act.
