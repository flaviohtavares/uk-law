## 33. Withdrawal of authorisation ....[^key-5a77b30aa99f25fbb5e9548542c34314]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) an authorised person’s Part 4A permission[^key-c53d8464a367f4430e2c8099e184bbdb] is cancelled; and
- (b) as a result, there is no regulated activity for which he has permission.

(2) The appropriate regulator[^key-0461748008e9629c87f10c9088529af3] must give a direction withdrawing that person’s status as an authorised person.

(2A)[^key-7f785031dcc93d6997fb0a773ae86bb9] In subsection (2) “**the appropriate regulator**” means—

- (a) in the case of a PRA-authorised person, the PRA, and
- (b) in any other case, the FCA.
