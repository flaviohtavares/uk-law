# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 71Y

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71Y. Relevant Part 5A direction: right to refer to Tribunal[^c-1f5c80633c23fc8f7310b3357b648d0e][^c-2175f3032e84c642e79a415782bc91ad][^c-0b37bb1bfd301e02d8c8df3c7e213886]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is aggrieved by the exercise by the FCA of its powers in relation to a relevant Part 5A direction given to that person may refer the matter to the Tribunal.
