# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 97

## 97. Appointment by FCA of persons to carry out investigations.[^c-9927ca5530fa26b483ba2aa61ac1396f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies if it appears to the FCA that there are circumstances suggesting that—

- (a)[^c2076461] there may have been a contravention of—
  - (i) a provision of this Part or of Part 6 rules, [^c-e212aacdf33d4ca8b806849008cbd455]... or[^c-b31873b4c495cc4ff63f78e59a6fbc2b]
  - (ii)[^c-c60eece5b90f12f7a00dc6af497dbaef] any other provision of qualifying transparency legislation, [^c-a633b5b4e0f9d8be512fe3a5fc95334d]...
  - (iii)[^c-02c844898faf006392bcb2ef58dd69b4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a person who was at the material time a director of a person mentioned in section 91(1), [^c-f8c530cf1895cb440e3dfbaf3c2954a4]... [^c-9e001fe9776a22a5034e1587135791ae]... or section 91(1B) (ignoring paragraph (a)(i) of that provision),[^c-ce43530b6544e96dd45f031df85ce2be] has been knowingly concerned in a contravention by that person of—
  - (i) a provision of this Part or of Part 6 rules, [^c-00b58d53f9ce99be90d89e83c338ba48]... or[^c-009e5f5c40d13f928fcd27491c240238]
  - (ii)[^c-b71d7abbcb197a46dbc8d47e2b69111a] any other provision of qualifying transparency legislation, [^c-60a10a9f6b99e7aee736e5956ff84a0e]...
  - (iii)[^c-628b1d73ff278ba6478854ccc27787ee] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (ba)[^c-a298f4fa77ec7bb3943b1ebf1a9fc18c] a person who was at the material time a relevant officer of a person mentioned in section 91(1B) (ignoring paragraphs (a)(ii) and (b) of that provision) has been knowingly concerned in a contravention by that person of qualifying transparency legislation;[^c-60f5c363f56873c54001bcb31764d383]
- (c)[^c2075390] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-07cfc1ffc7ce1d0b042ef17d97c217a1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The FCA may appoint one or more competent persons to conduct an investigation on its behalf.

(3) Part XI applies to an investigation under subsection (2) as if—

- (a) the investigator were appointed under section 167(1);
- (b) references to the investigating authority in relation to him were to the FCA;
- (c)[^c-72ba095e329da38b96179e052e81e75b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) references to an authorised person were references to the person under investigation.

(4)[^c-d663eebde9e393b3b65700f7276a32c7] In this section “relevant officer” has the meaning given by section 91(2B).
