## 73A. Part 6 Rules[^c2075218]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA[^key-52b75077fc1c20cf46020d507394c140] may make rules (“Part 6 rules”) for the purposes of this Part.

(2) Provisions of Part 6 rules expressed to relate to the official list are referred to in this Part as “**listing rules**”.

(3)[^key-97ddfda3d082a1aa8e937156d8ed519c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-f27061a48e784f8e2426dac3816a68b9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-f27061a48e784f8e2426dac3816a68b9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c2076420] Transparency rules and corporate governance rules are not listing rules [^key-0109bf014f4eaee8b3cada660f4dcc48]... [^key-5e87442cf713470cc06671803efa4bc0]..., but are Part 6 rules.
