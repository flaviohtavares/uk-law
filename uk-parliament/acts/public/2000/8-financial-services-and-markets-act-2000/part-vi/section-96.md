# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 96

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Obligations of issuers of listed securities.[^key-8a038deeb92aeb5cacb7a23aea228ce1][^key-c453b188bb516ca67c59792a115f61f7][^key-6050ed300292bfbc0d78cc0cfd17c3e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Listing rules may—

- (a) specify requirements to be complied with by issuers of listed securities; and
- (b) make provision with respect to the action that may be taken by the FCA[^key-9927ca5530fa26b483ba2aa61ac1396f] in the event of non-compliance.

(2) If the rules require an issuer to publish information, they may include provision authorising the FCA to publish it in the event of his failure to do so.

(3) This section applies whenever the listed securities were admitted to the official list.
