# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 89N

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 89N. Right to refer matters to the Tribunal[^key-9927ca5530fa26b483ba2aa61ac1396f][^c2076389]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person—

- (a) to whom a decision notice is given under section 89K (public censure), or
- (b) to whom a notice is given under section 89M (procedure in connection with suspension or prohibition of trading),

may refer the matter to the Tribunal.
