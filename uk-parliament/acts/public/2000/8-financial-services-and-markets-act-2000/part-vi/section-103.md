## 103. Interpretation of this Part[^c2076024][^c2075993]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

 

(1)[^key-9531f58bcd9db33f8eead354551a5fe7] In this Part, save where the context otherwise requires—

- [^key-fc4a40162a857b36997acdb5be2a61ca]...
- [^key-fc4a40162a857b36997acdb5be2a61ca]...
- “**listed securities**” means anything which has been admitted to the official list;
- “**listing**” has the meaning given in section 74(5);
- “**listing particulars**” has the meaning given in section 79(2);
- “**listing rules**” has the meaning given in section 73A;
- “**market operator**” means a person who manages or operates the business of a regulated market;
- [^key-0ed6dcdf654e7689fad6e0de0468f07b]...
- “**the official list**” means the list maintained by the FCA[^key-d7e378a1a75a1a32315f0364ce97e1db] as that list has effect for the time being;
- “**Part 6 rules**” has the meaning given in section 73A;
- [^key-1659eedc6b725c7f65b21cb457f65df3]...
- [^key-0ed6dcdf654e7689fad6e0de0468f07b]...
- [^key-0ed6dcdf654e7689fad6e0de0468f07b]...
- “[^key-816c83d579bcb151d039869788735b4b]**qualifying transparency legislation**” has the meaning given in section 89K(5A);
- “[^key-c1ab62f565e15b1a152c32d21d29fe7f]**regulated market**” (except in section 102B and Schedule 10A) means a UK regulated market, as defined in Article 2(1)(13A) of the markets in financial instruments regulation;
- [^key-0ed6dcdf654e7689fad6e0de0468f07b]...
- “[^c2076376]**the transparency obligations directive**” means Directive [2004/109/ EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/109) of the European Parliament and of the Council relating to the harmonisation of transparency requirements in relation to information about issuers whose securities are admitted to trading on a regulated market as amended by Directive 2010/73/[^key-3f81cfd557175976aa150c3dc5c8d63e]EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 November 2010 and by Directive 2010/78/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 November 2010 and by Directive 2013/50/[^key-4e89b76fd69ace6f90ce831e8f259fd3]EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 October 2013;
- “[^c2076502]**transparency rules**” has the meaning given by section 89A(5);
- “**voteholder information**” has the meaning given by section 89B(3);
- “[^key-2f81e6d50301d47fb12132cf0a91c37d]**working day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2f81e6d50301d47fb12132cf0a91c37d]**” means any day other that a Saturday, a Sunday, Christmas Day, Good Friday or a day which is a bank holiday under the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 (c. 80) in any part of the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2f81e6d50301d47fb12132cf0a91c37d]
