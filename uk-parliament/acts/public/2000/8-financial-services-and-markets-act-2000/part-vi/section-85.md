(3)[^key-9ec849cb565abfeb0edda586cc22dad8] A person who contravenes regulation 12 of the Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024 (prohibition of public offers of relevant securities)[^key-54128308c40711817e83772b6806324e] is guilty of an offence and liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or a fine or both.

(4)[^key-9ec849cb565abfeb0edda586cc22dad8] A contravention of regulation 12 of the Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024[^key-053be751af3a2e08dcd5fdf9e261e5a6] is actionable, at the suit of a person who suffers loss as a result of the contravention, subject to the defences and other incidents applying to actions for breach of statutory duty.
