# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 117A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117A. Pension schemes to which this Part applies[^c-acad4d00c0669c167e0aa25529827af5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies to a pension scheme—

- (a) that is FCA-regulated, and
- (b) in relation to which any of the following conditions is met.

(2) The conditions are—

- (a) that the scheme is an auto-enrolment scheme;
- (b) that the scheme is a workplace personal pension scheme that is not an auto-enrolment scheme;
- (c) that the scheme is a pension scheme of a prescribed description.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2)(a) and (b) a pension scheme is an “auto-enrolment scheme” if any individual is or at any time was an active member of the scheme in consequence of arrangements under [section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/30/section/3)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/30/section/3), [5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/30/section/5)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/30/section/5) or [7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/30/section/7)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/30/section/7) of the [Pensions Act 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/30/contents) or section 3(2), 5(2) or 7(3) of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) [2008 (c. 13 (N.I.))](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2008/13) (arrangements for jobholder to become active member of automatic enrolment scheme).

(4) In subsection (3) “**active member**” means an active member within the meaning of Part 1 of the Pensions Act 2008 (see section 99 of that Act) or Part 1 of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) [2008 (c. 13 (N.I.))](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2008/13) (see section 78 of that Act).

(5) For the purposes of subsection (2)(b) a pension scheme is a “workplace personal pension scheme” if—

- (a) the scheme is a personal pension scheme,
- (b) direct payment arrangements exist, or have at any time existed, in relation to the scheme, and
- (c) contributions have been paid under the arrangements in respect of, or on behalf of, two or more employees.

(6) In subsection (5) “**direct payment arrangements**” means direct payment arrangements within the meaning of section 111A of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 or section 107A of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993.
