## 133A. Proceedings before Tribunal: decision and supervisory notices, etc.[^key-376003dc245cbd980338a6efb2861115][^key-75d9435187a0e944d58fb5f50f48247f][^key-0515628ad4d8601a8f23fe146a2339ed][^key-130a7d2a30dc83fdc0a199d737891447][^key-8a92d8d8d482e6b2b7b424bdc0afbc9e][^key-9a7fae0b7dbb1fdc3987ec18947c90fa][^key-24d51bd40d293ab771ce3d771a359a70][^key-2c15a4717adfaa37130bcf9430dd7cc6][^c2078754]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-8a92d8d8d482e6b2b7b424bdc0afbc9e][^key-9a7fae0b7dbb1fdc3987ec18947c90fa][^key-24d51bd40d293ab771ce3d771a359a70]

(1)[^key-3f92af634f7ef58e9d19f7e773c2cdd5] In determining in accordance with section 133(5)[^key-4d37fe08ce638d8b78b112a42a2f2456] a reference made (whether under this or any other Act) as a result of a decision notice given by a body, the Tribunal may not direct the body to take action which it would[^key-ad17f59922da443d93f5d30e2db9fceb] not, as a result of section 388(2), have had power to take when giving the notice.

(2)[^key-eb622f2f72b70902bc4e6a573b61a519] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-02a88d49f26de13e7ca2c2ee95b53e29] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The action specified in a decision notice must not be taken—[^key-e55d060faf6cc1a8435f04f3b9dbb2e6]

- (a) during the period within which the matter to which the notice relates may be referred to the Tribunal (whether under this or any other Act); and
- (b) if the matter is so referred, until the reference, and any appeal against the Tribunal's determination, has been finally disposed of.

(5) The Tribunal may, on determining a reference (whether made under this or any other Act) in respect of a decision of the FCA or the PRA[^key-a4c02f699678d85b3263beab1d799724], make recommendations as to its[^key-ba923490a6fe3d5951b24248a78b896f] regulating provisions or its procedures.
