## 1M. The FCA's general duty to consult[^key-213c415ae170f27ccc3cbe5ebbb68cce]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The FCA must make and maintain effective arrangements for consulting practitioners and consumers on the extent to which its general policies and practices are consistent with its general duties under section 1B [^key-a7aa6775a04b289e20b7fed354912323]....
