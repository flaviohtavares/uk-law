# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 3Q

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 3Q. Co-operation by FCA ... with Bank of England[^c-213c415ae170f27ccc3cbe5ebbb68cce][^c-274cb7c248cb6b830919d9a85ef64458]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA[^c-afbe0087b233434a6b4d1f63f64a0ee5] must take such steps as it considers appropriate to co-operate with the Bank of England in connection with—

- (a) the pursuit by the Bank of its Financial Stability Objective, and
- (b) the Bank's compliance with its duties under sections 58 and 59 of the Financial Services Act 2012 (duty to notify Treasury of possible need for public funds and of subsequent changes).

(2) Co-operation under subsection (1) may include the sharing of information that the FCA[^c-951e95e9152575adf7d7a6c141525d44] is not prevented from disclosing.
