# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 3A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3A. Meaning of “regulator”[^key-213c415ae170f27ccc3cbe5ebbb68cce]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section has effect for the interpretation of this Act.

(2) The FCA and the PRA are the “**regulators**”, and references to a regulator are to be read accordingly.

(3) Subsection (2) does not affect—

- (a) the meaning of the following expressions—
  - “**home state regulator**”;
  - “**host state regulator**”;
  - “**overseas regulator**”; [^M_F_0595627b-67e6-4f58-f850-707af593f6d3]...
- (b) the meaning of “**the appropriate regulator**” in Part 18 (recognised investment exchanges, clearing houses and CSDs[^M_F_8796e333-2ed1-43f2-bcdb-2bf95d8d5f9c]) or[^M_F_170f9e9e-3b41-4d38-8d19-2bb98a870670]
- (c) the meaning of “**regulator**” in sections 410A and 410B (fees to meet certain expenses of Treasury).
