> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 3C. Duty to follow principles of good governance[^key-213c415ae170f27ccc3cbe5ebbb68cce]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In managing its affairs, the FCA[^key-514937de2b5b8afe6d9ceeef20f6886a] must have regard to such generally accepted principles of good corporate governance as it is reasonable to regard as applicable to it.
