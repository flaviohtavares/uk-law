# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 1I

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 1I. Meaning of “the UK financial system”[^c-213c415ae170f27ccc3cbe5ebbb68cce]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act “**the UK financial system**” means the financial system operating in the United Kingdom and includes—

- (a) financial markets and exchanges,
- (b) regulated activities (including regulated claims management activities)[^c-a6aa2d5326451d2bb4eea4d0b230a944], and
- (c) other activities connected with financial markets and exchanges.
