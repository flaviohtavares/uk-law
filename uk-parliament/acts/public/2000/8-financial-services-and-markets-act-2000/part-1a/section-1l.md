# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 1L

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1L. Supervision, monitoring and enforcement[^c-6f836ebf9dbeda2868ae95e2a121c1e7][^c-3dd408a275ddb26673eebfa7f2c63cfa][^c-213c415ae170f27ccc3cbe5ebbb68cce]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-1fea1d71b6db8f6e7fd7323542a78dfd] The FCA must maintain arrangements for supervising authorised persons.

(2)[^c-fefecb66f9459420788e2e52df25b8f7][^c-0eafd64cc4f3c2a62f9ddfc7b3ca05e8][^c-0facef61739e90e798597be648d9f600][^c-a561fb73f98730d1d5eebbe436d8563c][^c-0602af335735363f1ea3621e01b95f41] The FCA must maintain arrangements designed to enable it to determine whether persons other than authorised persons are complying—

- (a) with requirements imposed on them by or under this Act, in cases where the FCA is the appropriate regulator for the purposes of Part 14 (disciplinary measures), [^c-25580c7d87aa3ab38f35ebecdabbb510]...
- (aa)[^c-c9e49e58d288006c3feafa8cdb50acd5][^c-79e5deca12598b96e146f6723501e5b8] with requirements imposed on them by the Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, or
- (b) with requirements imposed on them by any qualifying provision[^c-84be5b24b124ff5f98be5731c0ab0364] that is specified, or of a description specified, for the purposes of this subsection by the Treasury by order.

(3)[^c-0eafd64cc4f3c2a62f9ddfc7b3ca05e8][^c-0facef61739e90e798597be648d9f600][^c-a561fb73f98730d1d5eebbe436d8563c][^c-0602af335735363f1ea3621e01b95f41] The FCA must also maintain arrangements for enforcing compliance by persons other than authorised persons with relevant requirements, within the meaning of Part 14, in cases where the FCA is the appropriate regulator for the purposes of any provision of that Part.
