## 69. Statement of policy.[^c2077082][^c2077252][^c2077850][^M_C_2b337e52-6bd6-4208-fe46-94c625304de0][^c-c77deb0c9786d59c98a2f3dacdc034bd][^c-0ed34d54789dbaf155d5877ac94ba2b1][^c-6e96838b8f0df9768daa81ac4bad28df][^c-d02330d65e5c0576d3bfff73cf151365][^c-cab1884953bd800928a6d2bc59a79541][^c-2828085989bd424e7c2af009d79327f8][^c-e3e06923881b21d9784dd1885bdfe1c2][^c-97557e614a9fbe966cfe3f5f36beebea][^c-61691cb4b4e8aa2c598f4c072b23c9b4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Each regulator must[^c-28f7e475517d157898647fa643a75362] prepare and issue a statement of its policy with respect to—

- (a)[^c2077953] the imposition of penalties, suspensions, conditions or limitations[^c-7c54d0567face1c0fee9a7916ad418fc] under section 66;
- (b) the amount of penalties under that section; [^c-aae109c62761ce5f4e7435a330abbf6f]...
- (c) the period for which suspensions or conditions[^c-38571ea24763937a8829c4a5b389d27e] under that section are to have effect.; and[^c-e680b8a74a54a7f4c7a2f87df95614b1]
- (d) the period for which approvals under section 59 are to have effect as a result of a limitation under section 66.

(2) A regulator's[^c-5c1f677d613dbdd7943fa82a68da5cb9] policy in determining what the amount of a penalty should be , or what the period for which a suspension or restriction is to have effect should be,[^c2077955] must include having regard to—

- (a) the seriousness of the misconduct in question in relation to the nature of the principle or requirement concerned;
- (b) the extent to which that misconduct was deliberate or reckless; and
- (c) whether the person against whom action is to be taken[^c2077958] is an individual.

(3) A regulator[^c-99efd7d1bd38cb170e53382e37e238bb] may at any time alter or replace a statement issued by it[^c-4e1012bba9b99570cfca44bd481f09fe] under this section.

(4) If a statement issued under this section is altered or replaced by a regulator, the regulator[^c-86141a0c3d0fd43c62fffe9d2a80dca2] must issue the altered or replacement statement.

(5) A regulator[^c-e851a92c9780e5a70cd46da7dee5dca5] must, without delay, give the Treasury a copy of any statement which it publishes under this section.

(6) A statement issued under this section by a regulator[^c-ccf534fbb422fbc622788f89aeb011b5] must be published by the regulator[^c-ceaa1c055c674677ece6dfe4497707e5] in the way appearing to the regulator to be best calculated to bring it to the attention of the public.

(7) The regulator[^c-4e9c87dde151a89e5a531233ef806405] may charge a reasonable fee for providing a person with a copy of the statement.

(8) In exercising, or deciding whether to exercise, its power under section 66 in the case of any particular misconduct, a regulator[^c-20ba00d148a8953e9727de421565a88c] must have regard to any statement of policy published by it[^c-ea9f73094d1db3d2664a4060ac8b6b39] under this section and in force at the time when the misconduct in question occurred.
