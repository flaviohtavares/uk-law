# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 63C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63C. Statement of policy[^key-ef1187ee4264be2b289a78e5ba29004d][^c2077905]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Each regulator[^key-154f218fcaf1752ac741faa46b2d588c] must prepare and issue a statement of its policy with respect to—

- (a) the imposition of penalties under section 63A; and
- (b) the amount of penalties under that section.

(2) Each regulator's[^key-80ef2881fe0f7a174fdfdb9f7a66deff] policy in determining whether a penalty should be imposed, and what the amount of a penalty should be, must include having regard to—

- (a) the conduct of the person on whom the penalty is to be imposed;
- (b) the extent to which the person could reasonably be expected to have known that a controlled function was performed without approval;
- (c) the length of the period during which the person performed a controlled function without approval; and
- (d) whether the person on whom the penalty is to be imposed is an individual.

(3) Each regulator's[^key-50cec56e4096af694788de6aad9c8e32] policy in determining whether a penalty should be imposed on a person must also include having regard to the appropriateness of taking action against the person instead of, or in addition to, taking action against an authorised person.

(4) A statement issued under this section must include an indication of the circumstances in which the regulator that has issued the statement[^key-959a594f98b6ca28e7c21c91603c59ee] would expect to be satisfied that a person could reasonably be expected to have known that the person was performing a controlled function without approval.

(5) A regulator[^key-f45334fdebb7dae424b8a2673a7445af] may at any time alter or replace a statement issued by it[^key-712ea800eb518516af10f3e6b5a1c034] under this section.

(6) If a statement issued under this section is altered or replaced by a regulator, the regulator[^key-0f44a4136485cba92472d89befb301e8] must issue the altered or replaced statement.

(7) A regulator[^key-3df0bd10d06eed13bd8631d5b2cc3a13] must, without delay, give the Treasury a copy of any statement which it publishes under this section.

(8) A statement issued under this section by a regulator[^key-370198ebcded9325d326713a20886ba6] must be published by the regulator[^key-b2ab384582d295bf28fc27ba175abab8] in the way appearing to the regulator to be best calculated to bring it to the attention of the public.

(9) The regulator[^key-9a2e153b2621144d6d6147f4beb76441] may charge a reasonable fee for providing a person with a copy of the statement.

(10)[^key-9f1d367b562822c83909b69ac05e6fc6] In exercising, or deciding whether to exercise, its power under section 63A in the case of any particular person, a regulator[^key-9ae7a0f2731ccde326db4a3a6380df1f] must have regard to any statement of policy published by it[^key-0746722d53fb9a02d09d4a09d1db0077] under this section and in force at a time when the person concerned performed a controlled function without approval.
