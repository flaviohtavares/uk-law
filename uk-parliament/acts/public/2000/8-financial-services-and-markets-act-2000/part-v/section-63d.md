# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 63D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

## 63D. Statement of policy: procedure[^c-1f6a5eb8c6975eebf6aa1c4d87d61161][^c-2f43ee28ac3ed84bcc1adc469bc5bd52][^c-b87a81802a6f80ac9e4a0c6cd3bc571e][^c2077905]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before a regulator issues[^c-8c7fcb03c19eb5a04d401afe5594cc8a] a statement under section 63C, the regulator[^c-153ffe5dc9bb66f8d6b17202da787562] must publish a draft of the proposed statement in the way appearing to the regulator to be best calculated to bring it to the attention of the public.

(2) The draft must be accompanied by notice that representations about the proposal may be made to the regulator[^c-29f59f18ea03d60592589b94a8ef77de] within a specified time.

(3) Before issuing the proposed statement, the regulator[^c-1954dfd7d485170bf2e94dcebb59d014] must have regard to any representations made to it in accordance with subsection (2).

(4) If the regulator[^c-9e25a756f1483da66c0e6563425c1260] issues the proposed statement it must publish an account, in general terms, of—

- (a) the representations made to it in accordance with subsection (2); and
- (b) its response to them.

(5) If the statement differs from the draft published under subsection (1) in a way which is, in the opinion of the regulator[^c-e2773b740459d7ad27f21bd72cbf3cfa], significant, the regulator must (in addition to complying with subsection (4)) publish details of the difference.

(6) A regulator[^c-9c14264de4f44d14e041c02410b63da5] may charge a reasonable fee for providing a person with a copy of a draft published by it[^c-13dc6905face835a043a862626fcecae] under subsection (1).

(7) This section also applies to a proposal to alter or replace a statement.
