# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 66B

## 66B. Misconduct: action by the PRA[^key-2828085989bd424e7c2af009d79327f8][^key-97557e614a9fbe966cfe3f5f36beebea][^key-31b586aaf0c42afa15c2d686eba393b4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of action by the PRA under section 66, a person is guilty of misconduct if any of conditions A to C is met in relation to the person.

(2) Condition A is that—

- (a) the person has at any time failed to comply with rules made by the PRA under section 64A, and
- (b) at that time the person was—
  - (i) an approved person, [^key-c7f035beb545ad2e25a012d6441e6ff3]...
  - (ii) an employee of a [^key-a1c4fe3e294a79172fe92885112b2b27]... PRA-authorised person , or[^key-69a73e1a73acc894330d60eb0733d0a2]
  - (iii) a director of a PRA-authorised person.

(3) Condition B is that—

- (a) the person has at any time been knowingly concerned in a contravention of a relevant requirement by a PRA-authorised person, and
- (b) at that time the person was—
  - (i) an approved person in respect of the performance of a relevant senior management function in relation to the carrying on by the PRA-authorised person of a regulated activity, [^key-d750ae83d928c8e672bb4797bff6194b]...
  - (ii)[^key-8597de527dd1b348e08efdc06011e34a] an employee of the PRA-authorised person , or[^key-327930b9d1c7b3fad0cc66b2278d6499]
  - (iii) a director of the PRA-authorised person.

(4) In this section “**relevant requirement**” means a requirement—

- (a) imposed by or under this Act, [^key-383e35c5096e8f66630e3d3cf6cd9395]...
- (aa)[^key-b02788c56dfddb752c34795770c92f7d] imposed by or under the Securitisation Regulations 2024, or
- (b) imposed by any qualifying provision[^key-b4ff9b959d1d7deb758620706d0a3002] specified, or of a description specified, for the purposes of this subsection by the Treasury by order.

(5) Condition C is that—

- (a) the person has at any time been a senior manager in relation to a [^key-ed5d988ea0d8a92a9e726852faad0ecf]... PRA-authorised person,
- (b) there has at that time been (or continued to be) a contravention of a relevant requirement by the authorised person, [^key-dc96b41f8ac3c3184fc994c57996f45b]...
- (c) the senior manager was at that time responsible for the management of any of the authorised person's activities in relation to which the contravention occurred , and[^key-96bd6b6308f1b7b3bf4e74f3134ab7cd]
- (d) the senior manager did not take such steps as a person in the senior manager's position could reasonably be expected to take to avoid the contravention occurring (or continuing).

(6)[^key-0c32fcabb776b8fde553a087dbbe35a9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) For the purposes of subsection (5)—

- “**senior manager**”, in relation to a [^key-a7be1d9dc685d759a38d099a0db80f4e]... PRA-authorised person, means a person who has approval under section 59 to perform a designated senior management function in relation to the carrying on by the authorised person of a regulated activity;
- [^key-17a319683a5bb452a68fbcffe1bcbedd]...

(8) In this section—

- “approved person”—
  - (a) means a person in relation to whom—
    - (i) the PRA has given its approval under section 59, or
    - (ii) the FCA has given its approval under section 59 in respect of the performance by the person of a relevant senior management function in relation to the carrying on by a PRA-authorised person of a regulated activity, and
  - (b) in relation to an authorised person, means a person in relation to whom approval under section 59 is given on the application of the authorised person;
- “[^key-8d55f7333be27dd4787d4035e4f0495c]**director**”, in relation to an authorised person, has the same meaning as in section 64A;
- “**employee**”, in relation to a person, has the same meaning as in section 64A;
- [^key-86c239e84e3ef15185c40c4ab53a92b4]...
- “**relevant senior management function**” means a function which the PRA is satisfied is a senior management function as defined in section 59ZA (whether or not the function has been designated as such by the FCA).
