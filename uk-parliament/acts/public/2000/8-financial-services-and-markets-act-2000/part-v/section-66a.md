# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 66A

## 66A. Misconduct: action by the FCA[^key-2828085989bd424e7c2af009d79327f8][^key-97557e614a9fbe966cfe3f5f36beebea][^key-094218b6a421272d2124c5c377fe6969][^key-31b586aaf0c42afa15c2d686eba393b4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of action by the FCA under section 66, a person is guilty of misconduct if any of conditions A to C is met in relation to the person.

(2) Condition A is that—

- (a) the person has at any time failed to comply with rules made by the FCA under section 64A, and
- (b) at that time the person was—
  - (i) an approved person, [^key-a18adc41dd1f2bf21e1c02440a95dbc9]...
  - (ii) an employee of an[^M_F_5b2fe026-42a5-421f-de41-060a1e23f05a] authorised person , or[^key-10a1c7215d951d242e4857cba7dcbb69]
  - (iii) a director of an authorised person.

(3) Condition B is that—

- (a) the person has at any time been knowingly concerned in a contravention of a relevant requirement by an authorised person, and
- (b) at that time the person was—
  - (i) an approved person in relation to the authorised person, [^key-62f45dfd0fd44f6442b8fffe48a65186]...
  - (ii) [^key-072a13d621e414b25fb01f3c50407f9f]... an employee of the authorised person , or[^key-f1d15289d9e870c2a14a814aad78366c]
  - (iii) a director of the authorised person.

(4) In this section “**relevant requirement**” means a requirement—

- (a) imposed by or under this Act, [^key-e7538eacb6573b9ec0df8aa2713f9e27]...
- (aa)[^key-3adb5c454887c2d7b243bd802f9f1227] imposed by the Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013 [^key-1d272f4eabe2194814ca21f937a63d0d]...;
- (ab)[^key-c29d422fb63df37a5114ba27920fb7cc] imposed by the Undertakings for Collective Investment in Transferable Securities Regulations 2011, [^key-17a615ffabb49c2d8c95d6a88049f7db]...
- (ac)[^key-8a092e81bfd97a8b18c443d90560230a] imposed by or under the Securitisation Regulations 2024, or
- (b) imposed by any qualifying provision[^key-1a4e155212a767996a35b5796bb797cb] specified, or of a description specified, for the purposes of this subsection by the Treasury by order.

(5) Condition C is that—

- (a) the person has at any time been a senior manager in relation to an[^key-0fc0422acce5f4216f66e3fc9a705b5d] authorised person,
- (b) there has at that time been (or continued to be) a contravention of a relevant requirement by the authorised person, [^key-36a682582a1d6ef0658ea2a363adebfb]...
- (c) the senior manager was at that time responsible for the management of any of the authorised person's activities in relation to which the contravention occurred , and[^key-a4a1e045985a322a06434fb317d12cf9]
- (d) the senior manager did not take such steps as a person in the senior manager's position could reasonably be expected to take to avoid the contravention occurring (or continuing).

(6)[^key-ab77420c1dc17fe26c8c005649e465e4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) For the purposes of subsection (5)—

- “**senior manager**”, in relation to an[^key-72b5e5440838701e43a5bb45b4f6a0ea] authorised person, means a person who has approval under section 59 to perform a designated senior management function in relation to the carrying on by the authorised person of a regulated activity;
- [^key-fa91f7e6f6d13d7db0a760b967346a57]...

(8) In this section—

- “approved person”—
  - (a) means a person in relation to whom an approval is given under section 59, and
  - (b) in relation to an authorised person, means a person in relation to whom such approval is given on the application of the authorised person;
- “[^key-5a83f0809e2e2459c59bfb766f10c59e]**director**”, in relation to an authorised person, has the same meaning as in section 64A;
- “**employee**”, in relation to a person, has the same meaning as in section 64A.
