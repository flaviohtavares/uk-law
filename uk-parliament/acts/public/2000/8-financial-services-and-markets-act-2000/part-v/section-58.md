## 58. Applications relating to prohibitions: procedure and right to refer to Tribunal.[^c1709222][^c1709223][^c2124647][^key-42d43617aeb732b05f2c005fe117aa4c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to an application for the variation or revocation of a prohibition order.

(2) If the appropriate regulator[^key-6e4806b1d416ebb8cf61b6cacbc9cc21] decides to grant the application, it must give the applicant written notice of its decision.

(3) If the appropriate regulator proposes to refuse the application, it must give the applicant a warning notice.

(4) If the appropriate regulator decides to refuse the application, it must give the applicant a decision notice.

(5) If the appropriate regulator gives the applicant a decision notice, he may refer the matter to the Tribunal.

(6)[^key-aff29d0bbe4a9ee07406bdfa71a237ab] The appropriate regulator” means the regulator to which the application is made.
