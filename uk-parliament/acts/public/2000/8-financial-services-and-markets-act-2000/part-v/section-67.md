## 67. Disciplinary measures: procedure and right to refer to Tribunal.[^c1709243][^c2077080][^c2077250][^M_C_2b337e52-6bd6-4208-fe46-94c625304de0][^key-89fd40e0dc4295780bdda6c585f80bde][^key-6e96838b8f0df9768daa81ac4bad28df][^key-d02330d65e5c0576d3bfff73cf151365][^key-cab1884953bd800928a6d2bc59a79541][^key-2828085989bd424e7c2af009d79327f8][^key-e3e06923881b21d9784dd1885bdfe1c2][^key-97557e614a9fbe966cfe3f5f36beebea][^key-61691cb4b4e8aa2c598f4c072b23c9b4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a regulator[^key-21f67b871f196746d31d56f23393d6bf] proposes to take action against a person under section 66, it must give him a warning notice; and if it proposes to take action under subsection (3)(aa)[^c2077939] , (ab) or (ac)[^key-f5a8c7f60aeb07637976f12497a197d5] of that section, it must also give each of the other interested parties a warning notice.

(2) A warning notice about a proposal to impose a penalty must state the amount of the penalty.

(2A)[^c2077941] A warning notice about a proposal—

- (a) to suspend an approval, or
- (b) to impose a condition[^key-52b10dfaaf597228a825968fb7ef6402] in relation to the performance of a function,

must state the period for which the suspension or condition is to have effect.

(2B)[^key-d04a510d8bee9c1af075bf9e6f418d42] A warning notice about a proposal to limit the period for which an approval is to have effect must state the length of that period.

(3) A warning notice about a proposal to publish a statement must set out the terms of the statement.

(4)[^c1709244] If a regulator[^key-5591497639d6fe16501b36bcebbd668b] decides to take action against a person under section 66, it must give him a decision notice; and if it decides to take action under subsection (3)(aa)[^c2077943] , (ab) or (ac)[^key-b92e72c71b2d591177c6e5d8abfaa7ce] of that section, it must also give each of the other interested parties a decision notice.

(5) A decision notice about the imposition of a penalty must state the amount of the penalty.

(5A)[^c2077945] A decision notice about—

- (a) the suspension of an approval, or
- (b) the imposition of a condition[^key-3fc39f21108c594d22b2cf53da27fc1b] in relation to the performance of a function,

must state the period for which the suspension or condition is to have effect.

(5B)[^key-96de185f9fb11cee872c3878469a0d38] A decision notice about limiting the period for which an approval is to have effect must state the length of that period.

(6) A decision notice about the publication of a statement must set out the terms of the statement.

(7) If a regulator[^key-24b6efb8ac16b0a5e966ab2bc01c8994] decides to take action against a person under section 66, he may refer the matter to the Tribunal; and if[^c2077947] the regulator[^key-fb13cbddbdab50476eb7d8be0d272880] decides to take action under section 66(3)(aa) , (ab) or (ac)[^key-22102a7f69cef44f58fa36ba44a40020], each of the other interested parties may also refer the matter to the Tribunal.

(8)[^c2077951] Approval” means an approval given under section 59.

(9) “**Other interested parties**”, in relation to a person (“A”) in relation to whom approval has been given,[^key-69482b16021f070b557fb0d3b2f95574] are—

- (a) the person on whose application the approval was given (“B”); and
- (b) the person by whom A's services are retained, if not B.
