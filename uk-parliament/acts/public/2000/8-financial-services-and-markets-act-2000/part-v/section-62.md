# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 62

## 62. Applications for approval: procedure and right to refer to Tribunal.[^c1709232][^c2077174][^c2124650]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the regulator to which an application is made under section 60 (“an application”) decides to grant the application, it[^key-623ad321f902e9c00328af4ae5621b4a] must give written notice of its decision to each of the interested parties.

(2) If the regulator to which an application is made[^key-9c57151f83e045defef76b2d33c18dd9] proposes to refuse the application[^key-fb80d7d8e845d29ec5fc8e67179b3784] , or to grant the application subject to conditions or for a limited period (or both)[^key-3e9149db0ac6e3405f42f4ab3888dd64], it must give a warning notice to each of the interested parties.

(3) If the regulator to which an application is made decides to refuse the application , or to grant the application subject to conditions or for a limited period (or both)[^key-eb7acd3200ff6e432ca5d8ddb1e48953], it must give a decision notice to each of the interested parties.

(4) If the regulator to which an application is made decides to refuse the application , or to grant the application subject to conditions or for a limited period (or both)[^key-9ef1bef4327b723b0ba227b04be1c3e0], each of the interested parties may refer the matter to the Tribunal.

(5) “The interested parties”, in relation to an application, are—

- (a) the applicant;
- (b) the person in respect of whom the application is made (“**A**”); and
- (c) the person by whom A’s services are to be retained, if not the applicant.
