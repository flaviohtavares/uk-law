# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 191E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 191E. Requirements for notices under section 191D[^c2081646]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)  A notice under section 191D must be in such form, include such information and be accompanied by such documents as the appropriate regulator[^c-8b26b5b58175132d7d9cc8696779786a] may reasonably require.

(2) Each regulator[^c-403a8df4614c60650f2c3aaac494230a] must publish a list of its requirements as to the form, information and accompanying documents for a notice under section 191D.

(3) The appropriate regulator may impose different requirements for different cases and may vary or waive requirements in particular cases.
