# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 191G

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 191G. Interpretation[^c2081646]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)  In this Part—

- “**acquisition**” means the acquisition of control or of an increase in control over a UK authorised person;
- “[^c-78299e8a80330db006197a2a00c2fdba]**the appropriate regulator**” is to be read in accordance with section 178(2A);
- [^c-ec980aae1994f87e45a808cff78f650f]...
- “[^c-78e5ebf6f9f22992fdda4ff5101c3117]**qualifying credit institution**” includes a credit institution which is authorised under the law of Gibraltar relied on immediately before IP completion day to implement the capital requirements directive.
- “**shares**” has the same meaning as in section 422;
- “**UK authorised person**” means an authorised person who—
  - (a) is a body incorporated in, or an unincorporated association formed under the law of, any part of the United Kingdom; and
  - (b) is not a person authorised as a result of paragraph 1 of Schedule 5 , or a person treated as having a Part 4A permission to carry on a regulated activity by virtue of regulation 71 of the Collective Investment Schemes (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^c-eddd41af5bf54c0ef267c99ed724eefb]; and
- “**voting power**” has the same meaning as in section 422.

(2) For the purposes of this Part, a “**working day**” is a day other than—

- (a) a Saturday or a Sunday; or
- (b) a day which is a bank holiday in England and Wales under the [^c2081649]Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971.
