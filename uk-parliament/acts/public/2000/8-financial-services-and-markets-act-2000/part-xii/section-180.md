# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 180

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 180. Acknowledgment of receipt[^c2081682][^c2081646]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)  The appropriate regulator[^c-8b26b5b58175132d7d9cc8696779786a] must acknowledge receipt of a completed section 178 notice in writing before the end of the second working day following receipt.

(2) If the appropriate regulator receives an incomplete section 178 notice it must inform the section 178 notice-giver as soon as reasonably practicable.
