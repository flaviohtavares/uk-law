# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 187

## 187. Approval with conditions[^c2081689][^c2081646]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)  The appropriate regulator[^c-8b26b5b58175132d7d9cc8696779786a] may impose conditions on its approval of an acquisition.

(2)[^c-8468355384a5b2b7b5a760251f95fce5] The appropriate regulator may only impose conditions where—

- (a) if it did not impose those conditions, it would propose to object to the acquisition, [^c-b7f2e4a2df67a9e517bcf5446cc505a9]...
- (aa)[^c-3edd3568354e0085dbf11439231d37f8] it appears to that regulator that it is desirable to impose those conditions in order to advance any of that regulator’s objectives (subject to section 185(2)(c)), or
- (b) it is required to do so by a direction under section 187A(3)(b) or section 187B(3).

(3) The appropriate regulator may not impose conditions requiring a particular level of holding to be acquired.

(4) The appropriate regulator may vary or cancel the conditions.
