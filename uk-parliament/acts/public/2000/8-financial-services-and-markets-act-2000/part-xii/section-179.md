# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 179

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 179. Requirements for section 178 notices[^c2081681][^c2081646]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)  A section 178 notice must be in such form, include such information and be accompanied by such documents as the appropriate regulator[^key-8b26b5b58175132d7d9cc8696779786a] may reasonably require.

(2) Each regulator[^key-fe8136f27a814ebe6ca2fab7f69ee24a] must publish a list of its requirements as to the form, information and accompanying documents for a section 178 notice.

(3) The appropriate regulator may impose different requirements for different cases and may vary or waive requirements in particular cases.
