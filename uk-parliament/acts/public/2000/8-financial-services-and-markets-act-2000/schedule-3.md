#### EEA firm

**5.**[^c1709865] “**EEA firm**” means any of the following if it does not have its relevant office[^c2074606] in the United Kingdom—

- (a) an investment firm (as defined in Article 4.1.1 of the markets in financial instruments directive[^c2075596]) which is authorised (within the meaning of Article 5[^c2075603]) by its home state regulator;
- (b)[^key-11f4687a46b9b6e0170d1e13731b78d7] a credit institution (as defined in Article 4(1)(1) of the capital requirements regulation) which is authorised (within the meaning of Article 8 of the capital requirements directive) by its home state regulator;
- (c)[^key-21dd11c40c97fcd0cb89d5bb59a22271] a financial institution (as defined in Article 4(1)(26) of the capital requirements regulation) which is a subsidiary of the kind mentioned in Article 34 of the capital requirements directive and which fulfils the conditions of that Article;
- (d)[^key-9436d488060cd1375ef89cdaa9cde90e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (da)[^key-9436d488060cd1375ef89cdaa9cde90e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^key-ddb71abdc9ec4627e7d49daf34149c38] an insurance intermediary (as defined in Article 2.1(3) of the insurance distribution directive), an ancillary insurance intermediary (as defined in Article 2.1(4) of that directive) or a reinsurance intermediary (as defined in Article 2.1(5) of that directive), which is registered with its home state regulator under Article 3 of that directive;
- (f)[^c2109504] a management company (as defined in paragraph 11B) which is authorised (within the meaning of Article 6 of the UCITS directive) by its home state regulator;[^key-9ae02686ebb14f3a23686752752a4949] [^key-c037131dee0e2e04a24cea108ad829d3]...
- (g) a person who has received authorisation under Article 18.2 of the emission allowance auctioning regulation;[^key-2cca0f45b12422e4915dad60ce456f67] [^key-ce10305a5cea9706fbbe1a62fcfbd0d3]...
- (h) an AIFM (as defined in Article 4.1(b) of the alternative investment fund managers directive) which is authorised (in accordance with Article 6.1 of that directive) by its home state regulator; or[^key-52be2842a8e722e5f783c4c8baa184d1]
- (i) a mortgage intermediary which is admitted (in accordance with Article 29(1) of the mortgages directive) by its home state regulator to carry out all or part of the credit intermediation activities set out in Article 4(5) of that directive or to provide advisory services (as defined in Article 4(21) of that directive).

**5A.**[^c2075007] In paragraph 5, “**relevant office**” means—

- (a) in relation to a firm falling within sub-paragraph (e) or (i)[^key-80d43416ebbb46b8c64b36d0b12d21b2] of that paragraph which has a registered office, its registered office;
- (aa)[^key-76c172fe90e73eb13e876f24f1dc59b9] in relation to a firm falling within sub-paragraph (h) of that paragraph, its registered office;
- (b) in relation to any other firm, its head office.

#### EEA right

**7.**[^c1709869] “**EEA right**” means the entitlement of a person to establish a branch, or provide services, in an EEA State other than that in which he has his relevant office[^c2075011]—

- (a) in accordance with the Treaty as applied in the EEA; and
- (b) subject to the conditions of the relevant single market directive or, as the case may be, the emission allowance auctioning regulation[^key-631e44b4c3322e77190b578708aadf63].

**7A.**[^c2075013] In paragraph 7, “**relevant office**” means—

- (a) in relation to a person who has a registered office and whose entitlement is subject to the conditions of the insurance distribution directive[^key-aa9f6c8cba060e17b286c131780eb5b4] or the mortgages directive[^key-a4aff7786dbe178e201e1c9af677b857], his registered office;
- (aa)[^key-fde6a9d51f96a6c0d2721c21b66426ee] in relation to a person whose entitlement is subject to the conditions of the alternative investment fund managers directive, its registered office;
- (b) in relation to any other person, his head office.

#### EEA State

**8.**[^c1709870][^c2075566] “**EEA State** ” has the meaning given by Schedule 1 to the Interpretation Act 1978.

#### Home state regulator

**9.**[^c1709871] “**Home state regulator**” means the competent authority (within the meaning of the relevant single market directive or, as the case may be, the emission allowance auctioning regulation[^key-6d36b62eacf8001a205817691a730a14]) of an EEA State (other than the United Kingdom) in relation to the EEA firm concerned.

#### UK firm

**10.**[^c1709872] “**UK firm**” means a person whose relevant office[^c2075015] is in the UK and who has an EEA right to carry on activity in an EEA State other than the United Kingdom.

**10A.**[^c2075017] In paragraph 10, “**relevant office**” means—

- (a) in relation to a firm whose EEA right derives from the insurance distribution directive[^key-e1e6af68063313131a04d3d68c377e1a] and which has a registered office, its registered office;
- (aa)[^key-f25e7d38f6031c07ea3b782a1cc509ff] in relation to a firm whose EEA right derives from the alternative investment fund managers directive, its registered office;
- (b) in relation to any other firm, its head office.

#### UK investment firm[^c2075657]

**10B.** ”UK investment firm” means a UK firm—

- (a) which is an investment firm, and
- (b) whose EEA right derives from the markets in financial instruments directive.

#### Host state regulator

**11.**[^c1709873] “**Host state regulator**” means the competent authority (within the meaning of the relevant single market directive or, as the case may be, the emission allowance auctioning regulation[^key-c80a58eb69c3dd4d307150d44b95616c]) of an EEA State (other than the United Kingdom) in relation to a UK firm’s exercise of EEA rights there.

#### Tied agent[^c2075661]

**11A.** ”Tied agent” has the meaning given in Article 4.1.29[^key-28515027f606b9fbf98b9c0f59204dc8] of the markets in financial instruments directive.

#### Management company[^c2109510]

**11B.** “**Management company**” has the meaning given in Article 2.1(b) of the UCITS directive.
