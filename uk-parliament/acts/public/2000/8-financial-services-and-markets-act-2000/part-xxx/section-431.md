# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 431

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 431. Commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following provisions come into force on the passing of this Act—

- (a) this section;
- (b) sections 428, 430 and 433;
- (c) paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 21.

(2)[^c1709796] The other provisions of this Act come into force on such day as the Treasury may by order appoint; and different days may be appointed for different purposes.
