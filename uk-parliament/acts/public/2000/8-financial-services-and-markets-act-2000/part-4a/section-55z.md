## 55Z. Cancellation of Part 4A permission or permission under section 55NA: procedure[^key-a1cbdcf397d75bda52f42ca575270441][^key-a8053f534ca14682fa8517d44405ca3d][^key-41e0069ff01eda5cdb97bf06c689bed5][^key-467bd90e36c4130bbb182a3c8bc83f99][^key-b03507c0a7312de4f2bd96a84be18317][^key-ff61ac4979a5a23df47a128e88fb1d53]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a regulator proposes to cancel an authorised person's Part 4A permission or permission under section 55NA[^key-41a5dc6ec47dcac185073e7a8578a002] otherwise than at the person's request, it must give the person a warning notice.

(2) If a regulator decides to cancel an authorised person's Part 4A permission or permission under section 55NA[^key-97fcb6d5a91c0b54fc7b4ec984ee36ff] otherwise than at the person's request, it must give the person a decision notice.
