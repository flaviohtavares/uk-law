## 55Z3. Right to refer matters to the Tribunal[^c-77a3d4d2f3e4722cb966b230167fb61f][^c-ac5c835946b3aade84bd8f3429282cc1][^c-8e15e6f990846a4c90ae1358d4c863fe][^c-b03507c0a7312de4f2bd96a84be18317]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^c-77a3d4d2f3e4722cb966b230167fb61f]

(1)[^c-fb411b1343f330864410dfa7744172c3][^c-fb625302f9f5835ca7a8113ab30d2491][^c-98c5f4016c2b00be18ac7e7982d4627d] An applicant who is aggrieved by the determination of an application made under this Part may refer the matter to the Tribunal.

(2)[^c-8dc1f8836c19172052e1c08094bd4872][^c-de7743c8f1165bbc490346fdf6207a87][^c-15aa7c93accb453495435c85aee8d333] An authorised person who is aggrieved by the exercise by either regulator of its own-initiative variation power or its own-initiative requirement power may refer the matter to the Tribunal.

(2A)[^c-1361bf6b6128551cd60d4611bd21231d] An authorised person who is aggrieved by the exercise by the FCA of its power under section 55NA(5)(b) may refer the matter to the Tribunal.

(3)[^c-0889961c306ea97d94c49dc8ee32fb27] Where there is a review under section 55XA(3) of a determination within section 55XA(1), subsection (1) applies only in relation to the determination made on the review.
