# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 55Z4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55Z4. Interpretation of Part 4A[^c-837df3543a06feadd28555f561254a47][^c-b03507c0a7312de4f2bd96a84be18317]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**own-initiative requirement power**”, in relation to the FCA or the PRA, is to be read in accordance with section 55L(4) or 55M(4);
- “**own-initiative variation power**”, in relation to the FCA or the PRA, is to be read in accordance with section 55J(12).
