## 138A. Modification or waiver of rules[^key-fa44424cb9f25e927372ad87ad375cc2][^key-8bf2ae990ae837cbba2d51d134fd8e0e][^key-537ce6a06761a848c7c200e18c09e518][^key-742d90f96e3c1d4f5cbc337f58fc1b46][^key-a4baae7e9796269dc4126fb7e469c581][^key-42c59a581b6f586f05855856c875bb03][^key-250d892cc2ba3e165527ce9dc17ca02d][^M_C_c6408ce6-0268-4570-aee9-43c4fbfe09a0][^key-5d8d11ffe796f707e058cdc0576d0dc4][^key-3409d0b174eb1347683f325a9a2baf5a][^key-cb6f0c788ff618621553f0ca35b3a27c][^key-d3af761239f9e575a79c7d599233f904][^key-7b20c7a7fb801e6a4286735b9cd0aea3][^key-d227c3f18b9d7bad75657c5ff2802205][^key-1b3ba41dad33bf56b3174ac5c2937d9b][^key-173b1b6aff8c0eaa2b0da76424255d81][^key-e7d16eef3cb74b7c7fdce09df565c5b6][^key-612493a4997282f797e866742e3bc32b][^key-e922c816a6b3c56cc617ae59c14f3510]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-5d8d11ffe796f707e058cdc0576d0dc4][^key-cb6f0c788ff618621553f0ca35b3a27c][^key-d3af761239f9e575a79c7d599233f904][^key-7b20c7a7fb801e6a4286735b9cd0aea3][^key-d227c3f18b9d7bad75657c5ff2802205]

(1)[^key-8b8fa47c6afa1be3074fd7235137251b][^key-db0fa4234cc325262a59ec52e03ed292] Either regulator may, on the application or with the consent of a person who is subject to rules made by that regulator, direct that all or any of those rules—

- (a) are not to apply to that person, or
- (b) are to apply to that person with such modifications as may be specified in the direction.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to—

- (za)[^key-d661b53d3d7c8c9db81e0985930ca2c2] rules made by either regulator under section 64A (rules of conduct);
- (a) rules made by either regulator under section 137O (threshold condition code);
- (b) rules made by the FCA under section 247 (trust scheme rules) , section 248 (scheme particulars rules), section 261I (contractual scheme rules) or section 261J (contractual scheme particulars rules)[^key-4005185e310aab4cd089301d74bf1441];
- (c)[^key-ffcf21843af7101b84ab65ee4f9a059b] rules made by the FCA under section 309Z1 (rules of conduct).

(3)[^key-4cde9d621c5f4a1feb08dca85a5357bd][^key-0f300e0dda3ae48e6044b178ce5ad953] An application must be made in such manner as the regulator may direct.

(4)[^key-b0acda74968acd7ee407784922257372][^key-b359713ac158e969943492cbf7a63ed5][^key-4cde9d621c5f4a1feb08dca85a5357bd][^key-8cea4163bab6bf3b1152d32954085bae][^key-9b46aa05e84aa96a263f16bd7af2e0fb][^key-410cdccf7477091171a992a599d81c48][^key-7a1c258df84b87b10606a7e1d73faac0][^key-3bce7f931d63d78b78d06fc778b8341c][^key-0f300e0dda3ae48e6044b178ce5ad953][^key-1e6bdfb8a3dc2f38ac0340d180886662] A regulator may not give a direction unless it is satisfied that—

- (a) compliance by the person with the rules, or with the rules as unmodified, would be unduly burdensome or would not achieve the purpose for which the rules were made, and
- (b) the direction would not adversely affect the advancement of any of the regulator's objectives.

(5)[^key-4cde9d621c5f4a1feb08dca85a5357bd][^key-0f300e0dda3ae48e6044b178ce5ad953] In subsection (4)(b) “**objectives**”, in relation to the FCA, means operational objectives.

(6)[^key-4cde9d621c5f4a1feb08dca85a5357bd][^key-db0fa4234cc325262a59ec52e03ed292][^key-0f300e0dda3ae48e6044b178ce5ad953] A direction may be given subject to conditions.

(7)[^key-4cde9d621c5f4a1feb08dca85a5357bd][^key-db0fa4234cc325262a59ec52e03ed292][^key-0f300e0dda3ae48e6044b178ce5ad953] The regulator may—

- (a) revoke a direction, or
- (b) vary it on the application, or with the consent, of the person to whom it relates.

(8) “**Direction**” means a direction under this section.
