## 140C. Consultation with regulator[^key-e922c816a6b3c56cc617ae59c14f3510]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before giving section 140B advice, the[^key-2d168b16c6b0076b5859791cea6eba27] CMA must consult the regulator to which the advice is to be given.
