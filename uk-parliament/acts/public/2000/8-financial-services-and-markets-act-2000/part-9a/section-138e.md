## 138E. Limits on effect of contravening rules[^M_C_d4d4e5f4-27b0-4c8a-c39f-8553579e2a96][^key-98c8eb2152a047a06d25f226e3b3ada4][^key-900eacff346b904ad28932142590347c][^key-c4fd718ee4648414c753950dc8bd3dc1][^key-21b57312258ba0aa1c8d8ab82c9b72ba][^key-f9240ed3e53e91059f3aa6d30f4042f2][^key-63b58a4b9e0ecfcd456417da30cc0b07][^key-52595f4b420477cd86c7d6c3f6881daa][^key-e922c816a6b3c56cc617ae59c14f3510]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-564bdd1d706171eb82d6fed61a06601c][^key-a3b43149eb08ca27fdf54e15addb4d87][^key-900eacff346b904ad28932142590347c][^key-21b57312258ba0aa1c8d8ab82c9b72ba]

(1) A person is not guilty of an offence by reason of a contravention of a rule made by either regulator.

(2) No such contravention makes any transaction void or unenforceable.

(3) Subsection (2) does not apply in relation to—

- (a) rules made by the FCA under section 137C, [^key-a39eb6c8947f8a8372cc1210797cfee2]...
- (b) product intervention rules made by the FCA under section 137D [^key-44e5832723f746fc3e5bfd320fd5c16b]...[^key-163a35f4b53998475a0af323acc2effb]
- (c) rules made by the FCA under section 137FBB [^key-4b4838c3223dba26a2086a8631878a17]... *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^key-95698bf69d983503cef14b2e2fe0b5d3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

- (d) rules made by the FCA under section 137FD ; or[^key-3d48c7f924020f2ee17af8b97663964f]
- (e)[^key-b1033511bdc90aa5039c6ebf6969280b] designated activity rules imposing under paragraph (2) of regulation 32 of the Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024 (withdrawal rights in connection with public offers of securities) a duty in relation to which provision made under paragraph (4) of that regulation applies.
