# Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2000/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. The general prohibition.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-a62a4f7ac11a3a2f070ab13bd8748614] No person may carry on a regulated activity in the United Kingdom, or purport to do so, unless he is—

- (a) an authorised person; or
- (b)[^key-4721695ae1bf64c4389a7067308964a0] an exempt person.

(2) The prohibition is referred to in this Act as the general prohibition.
