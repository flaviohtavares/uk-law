## 22. Regulated activities[^c1709133][^c-1e860ac0922409d0bcd9d9871a3d63f7][^c-61da2815a105df1a6b1f838430b7d3c6][^c-48b4ee8a7663191f97a13258625a0097][^c-244d478fd9d696a1900cc9743dd9c00d][^c-8d32d450e87c69ca5b9470d90ed92b05][^c-3d95e7518e20909d1336546aee075cb1][^c-72a5f4901e8da297fb513a85c2b37b51][^c-02e8ac83088fe1ecbd1b4fe6f87f86ec][^c-b7c41a5d357afc11456f8e2104693181][^c-1a616b75bdcadfd4cc2027fd4108fb04][^c-8991a725565ba5bc0c2aff2187c53f14][^c-e68bae43b827d41c172cbf0628adc001][^c-2cd44cb347e572414723ddaede186769][^c-c17ee122b6609ff1e164bdadb5d637bd][^c-800681d721f4ac7a79fc04aeebe41910][^c-dcaeb12cdff93e2cf22650e68af51384][^c-f1c7380911ad675703e083738dc96d68][^c-17aaca0fe1dabeaecd885c086e87c41a][^c-dec3dd6db12e3700357a76df4ac560bc][^c-39a661cb657c17922c37d1843100c3a1][^c-6dff696bb88f8662b3d32f652ab24118][^c-99ee070f7801972c431e45558b1fd644]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An activity is a regulated activity for the purposes of this Act if it is an activity of a specified kind which is carried on by way of business and—

- (a) relates to an investment of a specified kind; or
- (b) in the case of an activity of a kind which is also specified for the purposes of this paragraph, is carried on in relation to property of any kind.

(1A)[^c-59aa211298c98ecc4273d7539962d2ac] An activity is also a regulated activity for the purposes of this Act if it is an activity of a specified kind which is carried on by way of business and relates to—

- (a) information about a person's financial standing, [^c-ad12a6c6571f1f04c23314deb4e4446c]... or[^c-7e8ad9a25c4b95e34acb1d95f29aac34]
- (b)[^c-25f7906a9b54105ab7deaa2abc5c1429] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-6178ada7257af5e6bded45ae8e9f8fe4] administering a benchmark.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1B)[^c-534f6061835a6027351114682d6ba7fd] An activity is also a regulated activity for the purposes of this Act if it is an activity of a specified kind which—

- (a) is carried on by way of business in Great Britain, and
- (b) is, or relates to, claims management services.

(2) Schedule 2 makes provision supplementing this section.

(3) Nothing in Schedule 2 limits the powers conferred by subsection (1) *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c38716e11df71910270ca9d8132976c0] or (1A)[^c-7fc4f7a4419702248be1131dad982b3b] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c38716e11df71910270ca9d8132976c0] subsections (1) to (1B) *(Extent: E+W+S)* .

(4) “**Investment**” includes any asset, right or interest (including where an asset, right or interest is, or comprises or represents, a cryptoasset)[^c-da76d8dae3f80129504eed1f0cb30cfe].

(5) “**Specified**” means specified in an order made by the Treasury.

(6)[^c-4ef492f29a480a5888b3c16642d5d4b5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6A)[^c-d5b8c3c0ee5c5630ee75f2a11acfbbcf] For the purposes of subsection (1A)(c), “**benchmark**” has the meaning given by Article 3 of the EU Benchmarks Regulation 2016, and “administering” a benchmark means acting as an administrator of that benchmark within the meaning of that Article.
