# Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, section 37

## 37. General interpretation.

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c10760251] In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**the [^c10760261]Act of 1947**” means the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1947;
- “**the [^c10760271]Act of 1966**” means the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1966;
- “**the [^c10760281]Act of 1973**” means the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973;
- [^c10760291]. . .
- “ **[^c10760301] apportionment note** ” shall be construed in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Schedule 5 to the Local Government Finance Act 1992;
- “[^c-7094e0a5dbb5db2bad1c7ff546bf1b79]**the First-tier Tribunal for Scotland**” means the First-tier Tribunal for Scotland, established by section 1 of the Tribunals (Scotland) Act 2014;
- “**material change of circumstances**” means in relation to any lands and heritages a change of circumstances affecting their value
  - ((a)) and, without prejudice to the foregoing generality, includes any alteration in such lands and heritages . . .[^c-6b8d511bb55cfe5e520b3a2d1555c48d] [^c10760311] any relevant decision of the Lands Valuation Appeal Court or a valuation appeal committee the members of which are drawn from the valuation appeal panel serving[^c10760321] the valuation area in which the lands and heritages are situated , the First-tier Tribunal for Scotland,[^c-eb828414f6e7337f1426b073b2c37fe4] or the Lands Tribunal for Scotland[^c10760331] or the Upper Tribunal for Scotland[^c-04360ef42ba47d74f097003a46625e92] under section 1(3A) of the Lands Tribunal Act 1949, and any decision of that Court, committee or[^c10760341] those Tribunals[^c-97813a8e9fa381b6244449e8d4912763] which alters the . . . [^c10760351] net annual value or rateable value of any comparable lands and heritages;
  - ((b)) but does not include any change—[^c-aefd87878ef7b90522050a9d0e107308]
    - ((i)) in the rent of the lands and heritages (or any other lands and heritages), or
    - ((ii)) in the level of valuations generally or in the value of lands and heritages generally,
- “ **[^c10760361] non-domestic rate** ” shall be construed in accordance with the provisions of section 7A of this Act;
- “ **part residential subjects** ” shall be construed in accordance with the provisions of section 99(1) of the Local Government Finance Act 1992;
- “**prescribed**” [^c-d0b9b5abca519b985e763db7fd6091cc]... means prescribed by an order made by the Secretary of State ,and cognate expressions shall be construed accordingly[^c10760371] ;
- “[^c-ed573c292a4faa3277023a3e4958a194]**Upper Tribunal for Scotland**” means the Upper Tribunal for Scotland, established by section 1 of the Tribunals (Scotland) Act 2014.
- [^c10760381]. . .
- “**the Valuation Acts**” means the [^c10760391]Lands Valuation (Scotland) Act 1854, the Acts amending that Act, and any other enactment relating to valuation[^c10760401];
- “**year**”, except in paragraph 2 of Schedule 4, means the financial year of a local authority;
- “**year of revaluation**” means the year[^c10760411] 2023-24[^c-61b208904fe9d7bcf22a86c38fdbb1de] and each third[^c-ee25a373e742b0aeb45ae634f370d465] year thereafter.

(2) Expressions used in this Act and in the Act of 1973 shall have the same meanings in this Act as in that Act.

(3)[^c10760421] The Secretary of State may by order amend the definition of “ **year of revaluation** ” in subsection (1) above by substituting, for the financial year for the time being specified in that definition by reference to two calendar years, a different financial year so specified.

(4) An order under subsection (3) above shall have no effect until aproved by resolution of each House of Parliament.
