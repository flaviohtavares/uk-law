# Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, section 38

> Extent: Scotland.

## 38. Minor and consequential amendments and repeals.

*Extent: S*

(1) Part I of Schedule 6 to this Act shall have effect for the purpose of making general adaptation of enactments consequential on section 5 of this Act, and of the Valuation Acts and of any enactment having effect by virtue of those Acts consequential on section 18 of this Act, and the enactments specified in Part II of that Schedule shall have effect subject to the modifications and amendments set out in that Part, being modifications and amendments which are consequential on the provisions of this Act and minor amendments.

(2)[^c10760431] The enactments specified in Schedule 7 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
