# Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/30

> Extent: Scotland.

## 36. Expenses.

*Extent: S*

There shall be defrayed out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any administrative expenses incurred by the Secretary of State under this Act;
- (b) any increase attributable to the provisions of this Act in the sums payable out of such money under any other enactment.
