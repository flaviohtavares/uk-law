# Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, section 1A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/30

## 1A. Valuation of shootings and deer forests[^key-0757b39b87fee50db9dc79aec7a00cab]

*Extent: S*

The assessor for each valuation area must, when making up or altering a valuation roll, enter separately any—

- (a) shootings relating to,
- (b) deer forests, in so far as situated in,

that area.
