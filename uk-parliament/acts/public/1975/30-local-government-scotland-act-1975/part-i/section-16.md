## 16. Borrowing and lending by local authorities and certain other bodies and certain of their funds.

*Extent: S*

Schedule 3 to this Act shall . . . [^c10755881] have effect with respect to the powers of local authorities and[^c10755891] joint boards [^c10755901]. . . [^c10755911]. . . to borrow and lend money and with respect to certain of their funds.
