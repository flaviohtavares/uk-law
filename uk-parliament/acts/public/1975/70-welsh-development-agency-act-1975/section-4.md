# Welsh Development Agency Act 1975, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2024-04-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/70
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4. Ancillary powers.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Assembly[^c2091801][^c2091801] shall have power in connection with its functions under this Act[^c2091791][^c2091791]—

- (a) to make such charge for any of its services as it thinks[^c2091793][^c2091793] fit;
- (b) to accept any gift made to it[^c2091795][^c2091795] for the purposes of any such[^c2091797][^c2091797] functions, and subject to the terms of the gift and to the provisions of this Act, to apply it for those purposes;
- (c) to carry out or commission the carrying out of such enquiries, investigations or researches as the Assembly[^c2091801] may deem necessary or expedient for the purposes of such[^c2091799][^c2091799] functions.
