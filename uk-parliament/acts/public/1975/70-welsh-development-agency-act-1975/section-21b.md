# Welsh Development Agency Act 1975, section 21B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2024-04-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/70
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21B. Disposal of land.[^c301521][^c301520][^c301519]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In exercising any power under this Act to dispose of land, the Assembly[^c2091975][^c2091975] shall not dispose of land for a consideration less than the best that can reasonably be obtained except—

- (a) as provided by section 16(7) above; or
- (b) otherwise as it considers appropriate[^c2091973][^c2091973].
