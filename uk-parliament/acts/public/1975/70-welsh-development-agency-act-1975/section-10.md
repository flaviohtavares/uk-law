# Welsh Development Agency Act 1975, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2024-04-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/70
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 10. Services etc. for development of industry.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Assembly may[^c2091829][^c2091829] undertake or assist in the provision of means of access or other services or facilities in or for an area where this appears to it[^c2091831][^c2091831] to be expedient for the purpose of contributing to or supporting the development of businesses[^c301612] in that area.
