# Welsh Development Agency Act 1975, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2024-04-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/70
>

## 5. Assistance to Assembly from public authorities and other persons.[^c2092314][^c2092314]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Assembly[^c2091805][^c2091805] may appoint a local authority, a National Park authority[^c301601], the development corporation of a new town or any other body or person to act as its[^c2091803][^c2091803] agent to carry out the Assembly's[^c2091808][^c2091808] functions mentioned in section 1(3)(*a*) , (da)[^c301602] and (*f*) to (*i*) above or section 21C below[^c301603].

(1A)[^c301604] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) For the purpose of assisting the Assembly[^c2091805] to carry out any of the functions referred to in subsection (1) above, a local authority, a National Park authority or a development corporation, or any other body of a public nature, on being so requested by the Assembly[^c2091811][^c2091811], may place the services of any of their staff at the Assembly's[^c2091808] disposal, on such terms as may be agreed with the Assembly[^c2091805].
