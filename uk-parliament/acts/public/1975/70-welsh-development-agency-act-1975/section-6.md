# Welsh Development Agency Act 1975, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2024-04-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/70
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Power to form committees.[^c2091812]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Assembly may establish such committees for giving advice to the Assembly about the discharge of any of its functions under this Act as it considers appropriate.

(2) The members of any such committee are to be appointed by the Assembly and may be either members of the Assembly or persons who are not members.[^c2091812]
