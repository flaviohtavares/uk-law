# Welsh Development Agency Act 1975, section 13

## 13. Welsh Industrial Development Advisory Board.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Assembly[^c2091835][^c2091835] shall appoint a board to be called the Welsh Industrial Development Advisory Board, to advise it[^c2091837][^c2091837] with respect to the exercise of its[^c2091839][^c2091839] functions under section 7 of the Industrial Development Act 1982[^c301621].

(2) The Board shall consist of a chairman and not less than four nor more than seven other members.

(3) The members of the Board shall include persons who appear to the Assembly[^c2091841][^c2091841] to have wide experience of, and to have shown capacity in, industry, banking, accounting, finance or the organisation or representation of workers.

(4)[^c2091843] If the Board make a recommendation with respect to any matter at the request of the Assembly and the Assembly exercises its functions under section 7 of the Industrial Development Act 1982 contrary to their recommendation, it shall, if the Board so request, publish a statement as to the matter.[^c2091843]
