# Oil Taxation Act 1975, section 11

> Text as at 2019-02-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Application of Provisional Collection of Taxes Act 1968.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 1 of the [^c10743391]Provisional Collection of Taxes Act 1968 shall apply to petroleum revenue tax ; and accordingly, in subsection (1) of that section after the words “income tax” there shall be inserted the words “ petroleum revenue tax ”.
