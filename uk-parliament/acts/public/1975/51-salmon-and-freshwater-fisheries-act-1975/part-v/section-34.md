# Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1975, section 34

> Text as at 2022-06-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. Power to apprehend persons fishing illegally ....[^c10050101][^key-41fd5b7e52be113c83ea86682cf49d15]

*Extent: E+W*

If any person, [^key-ca2db55fc1d7f8a77d035612d2a78ac4]..., takes or kills any fish where the taking or killing constitutes an offence under this Act, or is found on or near any waters with intent to take or kill any fish where the taking or killing would constitute an offence under this Act, or having an instrument prohibited by this Act in his possession for the capture of any fish, where the capture would constitute an offence under this Act[^key-00d277b4551a2ef9b5c970f4a8f91d4d] , a water bailiff or a person appointed by the Minister, with any assistants, may seize him without warrant and put him as soon as may be into the custody of a police officer.
