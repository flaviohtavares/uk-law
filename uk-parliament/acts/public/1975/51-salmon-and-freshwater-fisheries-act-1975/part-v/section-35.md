# Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1975, section 35

> Text as at 2022-06-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Power to require production of fishing licences.[^c10050191]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A water bailiff or other officer of the agency, or any constable[^c10050201], may require any person who is fishing, or whom he reasonably suspects of intending to[^c-c8803c33befe6276d6d1c05be690a868] fish or of having recently[^c-f179959bc712e898749d9f97f33f14bf] fished in a water authority area[^c10050211] in any area, in circumstances where the fishing would require a licence or authorisation under this Act or a licence under section 16 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981,[^c-d267631cea3a75b9177f2de3861d343c] to produce his licence or other authority to fish and to state his name and address.

(1A)[^c10050231] Without prejudice to subsection (1) above, a water bailiff or other officer of the appropriate agency[^c-3585ef0bf645a388c03e291dad3b4d57] who on any occasion finds a person who he has reason to believe is committing, or has on that occasion committed, a fixed penalty offence, within the meaning of section 37A below, may require that person to state his name and address.

(2)[^c-ce17548cbdcd54efb614fb9620650c3f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) If any person required to produce his fishing licence or other authority or to state his name and address fails to do so, he shall be guilty of an offence; but if within seven days after the production of his licence was so required he produces the licence or other authority at the office of the water authority[^c10050261] the appropriate office of the[^c10050281] appropriate agency he shall not be convicted of an offence under this section for failing to produce it.

(4)[^c10050291] In subsection (3) above, “ **the appropriate office of the appropriate agency** ” means—

- (a) in a case where the person requiring the production of the licence or other authority specifies a particular office of the appropriate agency for its production, that office; and
- (b) in any other case, any office of the appropriate agency;

and for the purposes of that subsection where a licence or other authority which any person has been required to produce is sent by post to an office of the appropriate agency that licence or other authority shall be treated as produced by that person at that office.
