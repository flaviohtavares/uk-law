> Text as at 2022-06-28.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 30. Introduction of fish into inland waters.

*Extent: E+W*

A person shall be guilty of an offence if he introduces any fish or spawn of fish into an inland water *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03fe442ee37c9d07c2ef1cfd2f48c701]in Wales[^c-6f4c2a4b875139af5708634988a75926] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03fe442ee37c9d07c2ef1cfd2f48c701], or has in his possession any fish or spawn of fish intending to introduce it into an inland water *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03fe442ee37c9d07c2ef1cfd2f48c701]in Wales *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03fe442ee37c9d07c2ef1cfd2f48c701], unless he first obtains the written *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03fe442ee37c9d07c2ef1cfd2f48c701] consent of the appropriate agency or the inland water[^c-3efb2e060435d1a2df15d26059c3c1db] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03fe442ee37c9d07c2ef1cfd2f48c701] is one which consists exclusively of, or of part of, a fish farm and which, if it discharges into another inland water, does so only through a conduit constructed or adapted for the purpose.[^M_F_d0612c64-abd4-4c85-8c4f-50e40f156bc0] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03fe442ee37c9d07c2ef1cfd2f48c701]
