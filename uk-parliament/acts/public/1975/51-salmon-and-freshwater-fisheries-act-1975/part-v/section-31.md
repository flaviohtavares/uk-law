# Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1975, section 31

> Text as at 2022-06-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. Powers of search etc.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c10050011] Any water bailiff appointed by the appropriate agency and[^c-edab62b4950523aab4abaae5cbd44209] any person appointed by the Minister—

- (a) may examine any dam, fishing weir, fishing mill dam, fixed engine or obstruction, or any artificial watercourse, and for that purpose enter on any land;
- (b)[^c10050021] may examine any instrument or bait which he has reasonable cause to suspect of having been or being used or likely to be used in taking fish [^c-1eae69a07e9e150f55e25d537042d863]... or any container which he has reasonable cause to suspect of having been or being used or likely to be used for holding any such instrument, bait or fish;
- (c)[^c10050021] may stop and search any boat or other vessel used in fishing in the appropriate agency’s area[^c-55e9fa7b998c71fb70907430d88ce4af] or any vessel or vehicle which he has reasonable cause to suspect of containing—
  - (i) fish [^c-55ee5d402ecce7bbd3d66f991fdd6ef6]...;
  - (ii) any such instrument, bait or container as aforesaid;
- (d) may seize any fish (or a sample of any fish)[^c-abea6060ab9c9cc06376a4b2656cb2b5] and any instrument, vessel, vehicle or other thing liable to be forfeited in pursuance of this Act.
- (e)[^c-5a78983a162c1b12a013b76523707920] may disable or destroy any dam, fishing weir, fishing mill dam or fixed engine which he has reasonable cause to suspect of having operated or been used, or of being likely to be used, in contravention of this Act.

(2)[^c10050041] If any person refuses to allow a water bailiff or a person appointed by the Minister to make any entry, search or examination which he is by this section authorised to make, or to seize anything which he is so authorised to seize, or resists or obstructs a water bailiff or person so appointed in any such entry, search, examination or seizure, he shall be guilty of an offence.
