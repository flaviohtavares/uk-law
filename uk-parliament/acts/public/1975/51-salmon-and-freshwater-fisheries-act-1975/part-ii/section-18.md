# Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1975, section 18

> Text as at 2022-06-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Provisons supplementary to Part II.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If any person obstructs a person legally authorised whilst he is doing any act authorised by section 9, 10 or 15 above, he shall be guilty of an offence.

(2)[^c10049551][^c10049541] The appropriate agency[^c-937a5af3d28029acec5d3e9fc3fe8f78] shall not—

- (a) construct, abolish or alter any fish pass, or abolish or alter any free gap, in pursuance of section 10 above, or
- (b) do any work under section 15 above,

unless reasonable notice of its intention to do so (specifying the section in question) has been served on the owner and occupier of the dam, fish pass or free gap, watercourse, mill race, cut, leat, conduit or other channel, with a plan and specification of the proposed work; and the appropriate agency shall take into consideration any objections by the owner or occupier, before doing the proposed work.

(3) If any injury is caused—

- (a) to any dam by reason of the construction, abolition or alteration of a fish pass or the abolition or alteration of a free gap in pursuance of section 10 above; or
- (b) by anything done by the appropriate agency under[^c-2097fd02586f6ba78674483f9be37dda] section 15 above,

any person sustaining any loss as a result may recover from the appropriate agency compensation[^c-5be6f723d972fcf2d58a63eefe8e3efa] for the injury sustained.

(4) The amount of any compensation under section 10 or 15[^c-6129bf7fa4e356bc770ae936ccc7893a] above shall be settled in case of dispute by a single arbitrator appointed by the Minister.

(5) In any case in which the appropriate agency is liable[^c-e823bf3ffc51fe140df8a9290ac34a6f] to pay compensation under this Part of this Act in respect of injury or damage caused by the making or maintaining of any work, compensation shall not be recoverable unless proceedings for its recovery are instituted within two years from the completion of the work.
