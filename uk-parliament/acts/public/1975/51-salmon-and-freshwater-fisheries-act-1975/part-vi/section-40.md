# Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1975, section 40

> Text as at 2022-06-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. River Severn.

*Extent: E+W*

This Act applies to the dams constructed by the Severn Commissioners under the [^c10050621]Severn Navigation Act 1842 and the [^c10050631]Severn Navigation Act 1853 and now vested in Canal & River Trust[^c-902b75235e1027ffc81c845befb29e9c], and to all fish passes in those dams; and it shall accordingly be Canal & River Trust’s[^c-fe0a192fdc0fe698f4ebef8868da96c4] duty, subject to the provisions of this Act and to section 23 of the [^c10050641]Severn Navigation Act 1881 (by virtue of which they have power to stop up the passes with the agreement of the appropriate agency) to maintain[^c-40c61625772488f47cb44bb9f3ecb97f] those passes in an efficient state.
