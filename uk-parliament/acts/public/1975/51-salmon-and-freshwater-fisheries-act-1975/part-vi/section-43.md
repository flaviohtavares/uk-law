# Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1975, section 43

> Text as at 2022-06-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Citation etc.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1975.

(2) Subject to section 39 above and subsection (3) below, this Act extends only to England and Wales.

(3) The following provisions of this Act, namely—

- section 28(1) and (2) above,
- section 39(1) ,(1A)[^c10051091], (1B),(1C)[^c10051101](4) and (5)[^c10051111] above,
- section 42(1) above, so far as it relates to the repeal of section 15 of the [^c10051121]Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1972 and section 18 of the [^c10051131]Water Act 1973,
- paragraph 3 of Schedule 4 below,

extend to Scotland.

(4) This Act shall come into force on 1st August 1975.
