# Social Security Pensions Act 1975, section 64

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Expenses and receipts.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) There shall be paid out of moneys provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenses of the Secretary of State or any other government department under this Act;
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums falling to be paid out of moneys so provided under any other Act.

(2) Section 165(5) of the Administration Act[^c1107693] (reimbursement of expenses out of the National Insurance Fund) shall have effect in relation to the expenses mentioned in paragraph (a) of subsection (1) above as it has effect in relation to the expenses mentioned in section 163(2)(a) of that Act; . . . [^c1107695]

(3) Any contributions equivalent premium[^c1107696] received by the Secretary of State under this Act shall be paid by him into the National Insurance Fund.
