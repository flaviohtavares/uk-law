# Social Security Pensions Act 1975, section 68

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Short title, citation and extent.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Social Security Pensions Act 1975.

(2) The [^c1107736]Social Security Act 1975 and this Act may be cited together as the Social Security Acts 1975.

(3) The following provisions of this Act have the same extent as the [^c1107737]Pensions (Increase) Act 1971—

- (a) sections 59 and 59A[^c1107738];
- (b) paragraph 18 of Schedule 4;
- (c) Schedule 5, so far as it repeals provisions of that Act.

(4) [^c1107739]. . . this Act extends to Northern Ireland so far as it—

- (a)[^c1107740] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c1107740] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) repeals Part III and section 89(3)(c) and (4) of that Act, and Schedules 18 to 20 to that Act;
- (d) by section 65(2) and Part II of Schedule 4 amends the [^c1107742]Social Security (Northern Ireland) Act 1975 and the [^c1107743]Social Security (Consequential Provisions) Act 1975 and reinstates paragraph 1 of Schedule 5 to the [^c1107744]Social Security Benefits Act 1975;
- (e) repeals paragraphs 47 and (in part) 58 of Schedule 2 to the Social Security (Consequential Provisions) Act 1975;
- (f) [^c2154958]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g) [^c2154958]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h) repeals provisions of the [^c1107747]Public Records Act 1958, the [^c1107748]Superannuation Act 1972, the [^c1107749]Parliamentary and other Pensions Act 1972, the [^c1107750]Pensions (Increase) Act 1974 and the [^c1107751]House of Commons Disqualification Act 1975;

but subject to the foregoing provisions of this subsection and to subsection (3) above, this Act does not extend to Northern Ireland.
