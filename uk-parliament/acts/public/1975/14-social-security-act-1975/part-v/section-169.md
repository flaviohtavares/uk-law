# Social Security Act 1975, section 169

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 169. Citation, extent and commencement

(1) This Act may be cited as the Social Security Act 1975.

(2) This Act, except sections 9(3) to (6), 128(4), 142(2) and (5) and Schedules 2 and 17, does not extend to Northern Ireland.

(3) Subject to section 3(5) of the Social Security (Consequential Provisions) Act 1975, this Act comes into force on 6th April 1975.
