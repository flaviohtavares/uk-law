# Social Security Act 1975, SCHEDULE 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 20 — Glossary of Expressions

Section 168(1).

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th><em>Expression</em></th><th><em>Meaning</em></th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>" The 1973 Act "</td><td>The Social Security Act 1973.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Age "</td><td>For purposes of this Act, a person—<br>(a) is over or under a particular age if he has or, as the case may be, has not attained that age;<br>(b) is between two particular ages if he has attained the first but not the second.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Assessed " (in relation to extent of disablement).</td><td>Assessed in accordance with Schedule 8.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Beneficiary "</td><td>In relation to any benefit, the person entitled to that benefit.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Benefit "</td><td>Benefit under this Act or (as respects any period before 6th April 1975) under—<br>(a) the National Insurance Act 1946 or 1965, or<br>(b) the National Insurance (Industrial Injuries) Act 1946 or 1965.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Benefit year "</td><td>See section 13(7).</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Capable of work "</td><td>See section 17(1).</td></tr>
<tr><td rowspan="2">" Child "; " child of family "</td><td>" Child " means a person who would be treated as a child for the purposes of the Family Allowances Act.</td></tr>
<tr><td>A person has, for the purposes of this Act, a family which includes a child or children if that person (not being a child) and a child or children (with or without a wife or husband of that person) would be treated for the purposes of the Family Allowances Act as constituting a family; and references to a child of a person's family are to be construed accordingly.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Claim "</td><td>Construe in accordance with " claimant" (below).</td></tr>
<tr><td rowspan="2">" Claimant " (in relation to contributions under Part I and to benefit under Part II Chapters I to III).</td><td>A person whose right to be excepted from liability to pay, or to have his liability deferred for, or to be credited with, a contribution, is in question; a person who has claimed benefit.</td></tr>
<tr><td>The expression includes, in relation to an award or decision, a beneficiary under the award or affected by the decision.</td></tr>
<tr><td rowspan="2">" Claimant " (in relation to industrial injuries benefit).</td><td>A person who has claimed industrial injuries benefit.</td></tr>
<tr><td>The expression includes—<br>(a) an applicant for a declaration under section 107 that an accident was or was not an industrial accident; and<br>(b) in relation to an award or decision, a beneficiary under the award or affected by the decision.</td></tr>
<tr><td rowspan="2">" Commissioner "</td><td>The Chief National Insurance Commissioner, or any other National Insurance Commissioner.</td></tr>
<tr><td>The expression includes a tribunal of 3 Commissioners constituted under section 116.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Confinement "; " date of confinement "; " confined ".</td><td>See section 23(1).</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Contract of service "</td><td>Any contract of service or apprenticeship, whether written or oral and whether expressed or implied.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Contribution card "</td><td>See section 146(4).</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Current " (in relation to the lower and upper earnings limits under section 4(1)).</td><td>For the time being in force.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Day of incapacity for work ".</td><td rowspan="2">See section 17.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Day of interruption of employment ".</td></tr>
<tr><td>" The deceased " (in relation to industrial death benefit).</td><td>The person in respect of whose death the benefit is claimed or payable.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Disablement questions "</td><td>See section 108.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Earner " and " earnings "</td><td>See section 3.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Earnings factor "</td><td>See section 13.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Employed "</td><td>Construe in accordance with "employment " below (except in the expression " employed earner ").</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Employed earner "</td><td>Construe in accordance with sections 2 and 51 and regulations under those sections.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Employment "</td><td>Includes any trade, business, profession, office or vocation.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Family allowance "</td><td>An allowance under the Family Allowances Act.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" The Family Allowances Act ".</td><td>The Family Allowances Act 1965.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" The former Industrial Injuries Act ".</td><td>The National Insurance (Industrial Injuries) Act 1965.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" The former principal Act "</td><td>The National Insurance Act 1965.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Friendly society "</td><td>A society registered as a friendly society under the Friendly Societies Act 1974—<br>(a) being a society which, as part of its ordinary business, provides benefits during sickness or other infirmity, or in old age, or in widowhood, or for orphans,<br>(b) not being a collecting society within the meaning of the Industrial Assurance Act 1923.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Hovercraft "</td><td>A vehicle which is designed to be supported when in motion wholly or partly by air expelled from the vehicle to form a cushion of which the boundaries include the ground, water or other surface beneath the vehicle.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Incapable of work " and " incapacity for work "</td><td>See sections 17(1), 36(7) and 56(2).</td></tr>
<tr><td rowspan="2">" Incapable of self-support "</td><td>A person is " incapable of self-support" if (but only if) he is incapable of supporting himself by reason of physical or mental infirmity and is likely to remain so incapable for a prolonged period.</td></tr>
<tr><td>He is " permanently incapable of self-support" if (but only if) he is incapable of supporting himself by that reason, and is likely to remain so incapable for the remainder of his life.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Industrial death benefit "</td><td>Industrial injuries benefit in respect of a person's death.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Industrial injuries benefit "</td><td>Benefit under Part II, Chapter IV or V.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" The Inland Revenue "</td><td>The Commissioners of Inland Revenue.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Late husband "</td><td>A reference to a woman's late husband, in relation to a woman who has been more than once married, is to her last husband.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Living with "; " living together ".</td><td>A man and his wife shall not be deemed to be living otherwise than together unless—<br>(a) they are permanently living in separation either by agreement or under an order of a court, or<br>(b) one of them has deserted the other and the separation incident to the desertion has not come to an end.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Local office "</td><td>Any office appointed by the Secretary of State as a local office for the purposes of this Act.</td></tr>
<tr><td rowspan="2">" Lower earnings limit "; " upper earnings limit ".</td><td>References to these limits are to the lower and upper limits referred to in section 4(1).</td></tr>
<tr><td>References to the lower or upper earnings limit of a tax year are to whatever is (or was) for that year the limit in force under section 4(1).</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Medical examination "</td><td>The expression includes bacteriological and radiographical tests and similar investigations; construe "medically examined" accordingly.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Medical practitioner "</td><td>A registered medical practitioner. The expression includes a person outside the United Kingdom who is not a registered medical practitioner, but has qualifications corresponding (in the Secretary of State's opinion) to those of a registered medical practitioner.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Medical treatment "</td><td>Medical, surgical or rehabilitative treatment (including any course of diet or other regimen); references to a person receiving or submitting himself to medical treatment to be construed accordingly.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" The Northern Ireland Department ".</td><td>The Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" The Old Cases Act "</td><td>The Industrial Injuries and Diseases (Old Cases) Act 1975.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Pensionable age "</td><td>In the case of a man, 65; in the case of a woman, 60.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Pneumoconiosis "</td><td>Fibrosis of the lungs due to silica dust, asbestos dust or other dust. The expression includes the condition of the lungs known as dust-reticulation.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Prescribe "</td><td>Prescribe by regulations.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Procedure regulations "</td><td>Regulations under section 115.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Regulations "</td><td>Regulations made by the Secretary of State under this Act.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Relative "</td><td>Includes a person who is a relative by marriage or adoption and a person who would be a relative if some person born illegitimate had been born legitimate (subject however to sections 44(6), 47(1), 66(8) and 72(6) as respects the meaning of " relative " in those sections).</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Relevant accident "</td><td>The accident in respect of which industrial injuries benefit is claimed or payable.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Relevant injury "</td><td>The injury in respect of which industrial injuries benefit is claimed or payable.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Relevant loss of faculty "</td><td>The loss of faculty resulting from the relevant injury.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Residing with "; " residing together ".</td><td>Two persons shall not be deemed to have ceased to reside together by reason of any temporary absence of either or both of them, and in particular by reason—<br>(a) of any such absence at school or while receiving medical treatment as an in-patient in a hospital or similar institution, or<br>(b) of any absence of either or both of them in such circumstances as may be prescribed.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Reserve Pension Fund "</td><td>The Fund established under Part III of the 1973 Act.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Reserve scheme contributions " ; " reserve scheme premiums ".</td><td>The contributions and premiums payable under Part III of the 1973 Act.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" School-leaving age "</td><td>For purposes of this Act, a person has attained (or not attained) school-leaving age if he would be treated as being over (or under) the upper limit of compulsory school age for purposes of the Family Allowances Act (disregarding regulations under section 2 of the Family Allowances and National Insurance Act 1967).</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Self-employed earner "</td><td>Construe in accordance with section 2.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Short-term benefit "</td><td>Unemployment benefit, sickness benefit, maternity grant, maternity allowance and widow's allowance.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Supplementary scheme "</td><td>A scheme under section 158.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Tax week "</td><td>One of the successive periods in a tax year beginning with the first day of that year and every 7th day thereafter; the last day of a tax year (or, in the case of a tax year ending in a leap year, the last 2 days) to be treated accordingly as a separate tax week.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Tax year "</td><td>The 12 months beginning with 6th April in any year.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Trade or business "</td><td>For purposes of this Act, the exercise and performance of the powers and duties of a public or local authority are to be treated as the trade or business of the authority.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Trade union "</td><td>An association of employed earners.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Unemployability supplement ".</td><td>Increase of a disablement pension under section 58.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Upper earnings limit "</td><td>See " lower earnings limit" and " upper earnings limit" (above).</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Up-rating order "</td><td>An order made by the Secretary of State under section 124.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Week "</td><td>A period of 7 days beginning with midnight between Saturday and Sunday, except (in sections 17(1)(d), 20, 30(1) and 45(3)) where the expression is otherwise defined.</td></tr>
<tr><td>" Working life "</td><td>See section 27(2).</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
