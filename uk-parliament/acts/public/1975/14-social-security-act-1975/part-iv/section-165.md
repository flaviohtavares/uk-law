# Social Security Act 1975, section 165

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 165. Deputy Government Actuary

Anything which under any provision of this Act is authorised or required to be done by the Government Actuary may be done instead by the Deputy Government Actuary; and references to the Government Actuary shall be construed accordingly.
