# Social Security Act 1975, section 124

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 124. Power to increase rates of benefit

(1) The Secretary of State may by order increase any of the sums specified in—

- (a) Schedule 4 to this Act;
- (b) Schedule 6 to this Act, paragraphs 3(1)(a)(i) and (ii) (calculation of earnings-related supplement and addition) ; and
- (c) sections 2(6)(c) and 7(2)(b) of the Old Cases Act;
- (d) sections 30(1) and (2), 45(3) and (4), 66(4) and (5) of this Act (earnings rules).

(2) No order shall be made under this section unless a draft of it has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

(3) The Secretary of State shall lay with any draft order under this section a copy of a report by the Government Actuary giving the latter's opinion on the likely effect on the National Insurance Fund of the making of the order.
