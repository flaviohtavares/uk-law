# Social Security Act 1975, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Benefit to be inalienable

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, every assignment of, or charge on, benefit and every agreement to assign or charge benefit shall be void; and, on the bankruptcy of a beneficiary, the benefit shall not pass to any trustee or other person acting on behalf of his creditors.

(2) In the application of this section to Scotland—

- (a) the reference to assignment of benefit shall be read as a reference to its assignation, " assign " being construed accordingly;
- (b) the reference to a beneficiary's bankruptcy shall be read as a reference to the sequestration of his estate or the appointment on his estate of a judicial factor under section 14 of the [^m00010]Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 1913 or section 15 of the [^m00011]Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1958.

(3) In calculating for the purposes of section 5 of the [^m00012]Debtors Act 1869 or section 4 of the [^m00013]Civil Imprisonment (Scotland) Act 1882 the means of any beneficiary, no account shall be taken of any increase of injury benefit or disablement benefit in respect of a child, or of industrial death benefit.
