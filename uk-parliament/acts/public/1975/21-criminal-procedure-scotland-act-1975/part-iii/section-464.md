# Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975, section 464

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 1996-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 464. Short title and commencement. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197][^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(2) Subject to the following provisions of this section, this Act shall come into operation on 16th May 1975. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(3)[^c13064131] Sections 23 and 329 of this Act shall come into operation on such day as Her Majesty may by Order in Council appoint. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(4) Sections 214 and 423 of this Act shall come into operation on such date as the Secretary of State may by order appoint; and any such order shall be made by statutory instrument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(5) A statutory instrument containing an order under subsection (4) of this section shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]
