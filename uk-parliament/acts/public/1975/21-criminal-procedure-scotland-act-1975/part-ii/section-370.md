# Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975, section 370

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 1996-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/21

## 370. Child charged jointly with person who is not a child. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197][^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

*Extent: E+W+S*

When a child has been charged with an offence jointly with a person who is not a child the provisions of sections 366 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] and 367[^c13051801] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] of this Act shall not apply to summary proceedings before the sheriff in respect of the charges. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]
