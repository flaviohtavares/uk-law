# Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975, section 324

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 324. Backing of certain warrants from the Isle of Man. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197][^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-04-01).**

(1) A warrant issued in the Isle of Man for the arrest of a person charged with an offence may, after it has been endorsed by a justice in Scotland, be executed there by the person bringing that warrant, by any person to whom the warrant was originally directed or by any officer of law of the sheriff court district where the warrant has been endorsed as aforesaid in like manner as any such warrant issued in Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(2) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]**endorsed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]**” means endorsed in the like manner as a process to which section 4 of the Summary Jurisdiction (Process) Act 1881 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]
