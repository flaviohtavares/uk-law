# Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975, section 169

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 1996-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/21

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 169. Restrictions on report of proceedings involving person under 16. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197][^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) No newspaper report of any proceedings in a court shall reveal the name, address or school, or include any particulars calculated to lead to the identification, of any person under the age of 16 years concerned in the proceedings, either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

- (a) as being a person against or in respect of whom the proceedings are taken; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]
- (b) as being a witness therein; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

nor shall any picture which is, or includes, a picture of a person under the age of 16 years so concerned in the proceedings be published in any newspaper in a context relevant to the proceedings: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

Provided that, in any case— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

- (i) where the person is concerned in the proceedings as a witness only and no one against whom the proceedings are taken is under the age of 16 years, the foregoing provisions of this subsection shall not apply unless the court so directs; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]
- (ii) the court may at any stage of the proceedings if satisfied that it is in the public interest so to do, direct that the requirements of this section (including such requirements as applied by a direction under paragraph (i) above) shall be dispensed with to such extent as the court may specify; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]
- (iii) the Secretary of State may, after completion of the proceedings, if so satisfied by order dispense with the said requirements to such extent as may be specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(2) This section shall, with the necessary modifications, apply in relation to sound and television *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] programmes included in a programme service (within the meaning of the Broadcasting Act 1990)[^c13038131] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] as it applies in relation to newspapers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(3) A person who publishes matter in contravention of this section shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] level 4 on the standard scale[^c13038141] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197].

(4) In this section, references to a court shall not include a court in England, Wales or Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]
