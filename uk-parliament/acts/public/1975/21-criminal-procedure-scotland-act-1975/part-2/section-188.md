# Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975, section 188

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 1996-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 188. Probation orders relating to persons residing in England. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c13041181][^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197][^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the court by which a probation order is made under section 183 of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] (not being a probation order including a requirement[^c13041191] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] which, while corresponding to a requirement mentioned in paragraph 2 or 3 of Schedule 1A to the[^c13041201] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] [^c13041211]Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973, would if included in a probation order made under that Act fail to accord with a restriction as to days of presentation, participation or attendance mentioned in paragraph 2(4)(a) or (6)(a), or as the case may be 3(3)(a), of that Schedule *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] ) is satisfied that the offender has attained the age of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] 16[^c13041221] years and resides or will reside in England, subsection (2) of the said section shall not apply to the order, but the order shall contain a requirement that he be under the supervision of a probation officer appointed for or assigned to the petty sessions area in which the offender resides or will reside; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] [^c13041231]. . . that area shall be named in the order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] ; and where the order includes a requirement that the probationer perform unpaid work for a number of hours, the number specified shall not exceed one hundred.[^c13041241] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197].

(2) Where a probation order has been made under section 183 of this Act and the court in Scotland by which the order was made or the appropriate court is satisfied *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

- (a)[^c13041251] that the probationer has attained the age of 16 years; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]
- (b) that he proposes to reside, or is residing, in England; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]
- (c) that suitable arrangements for his supervision can be made by the probation committee for the area which contains the petty sessions area in which he resides or will reside *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

, the power of that court to amend the order under Schedule 5 to this Act shall include power to insert the provisions required by subsection (1) of this section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] or to vary any requirement for performance of unpaid work so that such hours as remain to be worked do not exceed one hundred[^c13041261] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]; and the court may so amend the order without summoning the probationer and without his consent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(3) A probation order made or amended by virtue of this section may, notwithstanding section 184(8) of this Act, include a requirement that the probationer shall submit to treatment for his mental condition and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

- (a) subsections (1), (3) and (7) of the said section 184 and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] paragraph 5(3) of Schedule 1A to[^c13041271] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] [^c13041211]Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973 (all of which regulate the making of probation orders which include any such requirement) shall apply to the making of an order which includes any such requirement by virtue of this subsection as they apply to the making of an order which includes any such requirement by virtue of section 184 of this Act and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] paragraph 5 of Schedule 1A to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] the said Act of 1973 respectively; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]
- (b) sub-paragraphs (5) to (7) of paragraph 5 of Schedule 1A to[^c13041301] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] the said Act of 1973 (functions of supervising officer and medical practitioner where such a requirement has been imposed) shall apply in relation to a probationer who is undergoing treatment in England in pursuance of a requirement imposed by virtue of this subsection as they apply in relation to a probationer undergoing such treatment in pursuance of a requirement imposed by virtue of that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(4) Sections 185(1) and 186(1) of this Act shall not apply to any order made or amended under this section; but subject as hereinafter provided the provisions of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

Schedule 2 to the Criminal Justice Act 1991 shall apply to the order—[^c13041311] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

- (a) except in the case mentioned in paragraph (b) below, as if that order were a probation order made under section 2 of the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]
- (b) in the case of an order which contains a requirement such as is mentioned in subsection (5A) of section 183 or 384 of this Act, as if it were a combination order made under section 11 of the said Act of 1991: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

Provided that Part III of that Schedule shall not so apply; and sub-paragraphs (3) and (4) of paragraph 3 of that Schedule shall so apply as if for the first reference in the said sub-paragraph (3) to the Crown Court there were substituted a reference to a court in Scotland and for the other references in those sub-paragraphs to the Crown Court there were substituted references to the court in Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(5) If it appears on information to a justice acting for the petty sessions area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] named in a probation order made or amended under this section that the person to whom the order relates[^c13041321] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] has been convicted by a court in any part of Great Britain of an offence committed during the period specified in the order, he may issue a summons requiring that person to appear, at the place and time specified therein, before the court in Scotland by which the probation order was made or, if the information is in writing and on oath, may issue a warrant for his arrest, directing that person to be brought before the last-mentioned court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(6) If a warrant for the arrest of a probationer issued under section 187 of this Act by a court is executed in England, and the probationer cannot forthwith be brought before that court, the warrant shall have effect as if it directed him to be brought before a magistrates’ court for the place where he is arrested; and the magistrates’ court shall commit him to custody or release him on bail (with or without sureties) until he can be brought or appear before the court in Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(7) The court by which a probation order is made or amended in accordance with the provisions of this section shall send three copies of the order to the clerk to the justices for the petty sessions area named therein, together with such documents and information relating to the case as it considers likely to be of assistance to the court acting for that petty sessions area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]

(8) Where a probation order which is amended under subsection (2) of this section is an order to which the provisions of this Act apply by virtue of section 10 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197] [^c13041211]Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973 (which relates to probation orders under that Act relating to persons residing in Scotland) then, notwithstanding anything in that section or this section, the order shall, as from the date of the amendment, have effect in all respects as if it were an order made under section 2 of that Act in the case of a person residing in England. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8976ff85-885b-4a45-b72f-b8464f87d197]
