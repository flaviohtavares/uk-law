# Children Act 1975, section 50

> Text as at 2013-06-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 50. Payments towards maintenance of children.

*Extent: S*

Without prejudice to any existing powers and duties to make payments in respect of the maintenance of children, where a child under the age of[^c1078097] eighteen[^c1078098] is residing with and being cared for (other than as a foster child) by a person other than a parent of the child, a council constituted under section 2 of the [^c1078099]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1994 may make to that person payments for or towards the maintenance of the child.
