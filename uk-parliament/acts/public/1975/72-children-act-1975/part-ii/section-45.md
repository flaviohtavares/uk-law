# Children Act 1975, section 45

> Text as at 2013-06-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Affiliation order on application by custodian.[^c1078050]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a custodianship order subsists in respect of an illegitimate child, and no affiliation order relating to the child has been made under the [^c1078051]Affiliation Proceedings Act 1957, the custodian of the child may apply to a justice of the peace acting for the petty sessions area in which the child or the child’s mother resides for a summons to be served under section 1 of that Act.

(2) The court shall proceed on the application as on a complaint under that section, but the person entitled to any payments under an affiliation order made on the application shall be the custodian.

(3) An application may not be made under subsection (1)—

- (a) if the custodian is married to the child’s mother, or
- (b) more than three years after the custodianship order was made.
