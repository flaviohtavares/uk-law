# Children Act 1975, section 62

> Text as at 2013-06-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Effect of resolutions under sections 60 and 61.[^c1078149]

*Extent: S*

(1) A resolution under subsection (1) of section 60 shall cease to have effect on the passing of a resolution under subsection (1) of section 61.

(2) Section 6 of the [^c1078150]Children Act 1948 shall have effect in relation to a resolution under subsection (1) of section 60 as it has effect in relation to a resolution under section 2 of that Act.

(3) A resolution under subsection (1) of section 61 shall be deemed to be a resolution under section 2 of the Children Act 1948 except that sections 2(2) to (7) and 4(3) of that Act shall not apply.
