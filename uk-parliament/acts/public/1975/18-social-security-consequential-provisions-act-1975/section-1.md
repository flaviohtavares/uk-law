# Social Security (Consequential Provisions) Act 1975, section 1

> Text as at 2013-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Repeals; amendments of statute book.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In consequence of, and in connection with, the following consolidation Acts, namely—

- the [^c782149]Social Security Act 1975,
- the [^c782150]Industrial Injuries and Diseases (Old Cases) Act 1975,
- the [^c782151]Social Security (Northern Ireland) Act 1975, and
- the [^c782152]Industrial Injuries and Diseases (Northern Ireland Old Cases) Act 1975,

(which Acts are together referred to in this Act as “**the consolidations**”) the following provisions of this section shall have effect.

(2)[^c782153] The enactments specified in Schedule 1 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.

(3)[^c782153] The enactments specified in Schedule 2 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified.

(4) Where this Act amends an enactment of the Parliament of Northern Ireland or of the Northern Ireland Assembly, or an Order made under, or having the same effect as, an enactment of that Parliament or that Assembly, the enactment or Order as amended shall be subject to the [^c782155]Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 in the same way as an enactment of that Parliament or that Assembly is so subject.
