# Employment Protection Act 1975, section 125

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/71
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 125. Minor and consequential amendments, transitional provisions and repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c890472] [^c890473]. . . the enactments specified in Part IV of that Schedule, shall have effect subject to the amendments so specified respectively, being minor amendments and amendments consequential on any provisions of this Act.

(2) The transitional provisions in Schedule 17 to this Act shall have effect.

(3)[^c890474] The enactments specified in Schedule 18 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in column 3 of that Schedule.
