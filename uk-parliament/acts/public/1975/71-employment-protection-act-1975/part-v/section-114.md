# Employment Protection Act 1975, section 114

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1975/71

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Amendments of the Employment Agencies Act 1973.[^c890397]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The [^c890398] Employment Agencies Act 1973 shall have effect subject to the amendments which transfer the licensing functions under that Act from local authorities to the Secretary of State.
