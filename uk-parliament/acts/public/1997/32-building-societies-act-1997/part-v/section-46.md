# Building Societies Act 1997, section 46

> Text as at 2015-03-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Transitional provisions, savings and repeals etc.[^c12278591]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The transitional provisions and savings contained in Schedule 8 to this Act shall have effect; but those provisions and savings are without prejudice to sections 16 and 17 of the [^c12278601]Interpretation Act 1978 (effect of repeals).

(2) The enactments and instruments specified in Schedule 9 to this Act are hereby repealed or revoked to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
