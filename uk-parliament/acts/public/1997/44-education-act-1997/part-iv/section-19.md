## 19. School performance targets.

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] The Welsh Ministers[^key-41f58e2489ab52900b109797625241bd] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] may by regulations make such provision as *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] the Welsh Ministers consider[^key-7b187d8eba8c4eee230a87c1a383466d] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] appropriate for requiring the governing bodies of maintained schools *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] in Wales[^key-bec93737c7b7f2b7678c118a5789ffc4] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] to secure that annual targets are set in respect of the performance of pupils— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] in public examinations or in assessments *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] required by virtue of regulations made under Part 4 of the Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021[^key-c111424c8e70964012db1b535b3f8d56] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a], in the case of pupils of compulsory school age; or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] in public examinations or in connection with the attainment of other external qualifications, in the case of pupils of any age over that age. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] Regulations under this section may require— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] such targets, and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] the past performance of pupils in the particular examinations or assessments, or in connection with the attainment of the particular qualifications, to which such targets relate, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a]

to be published in such manner as is specified in the regulations. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c11259771][^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] In this section “ *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a]**maintained school *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a]**” means— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] a community, foundation or voluntary school, or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a] a community or foundation special school (other than one established in a hospital). *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db34351caa889826654b2d42171141a]
