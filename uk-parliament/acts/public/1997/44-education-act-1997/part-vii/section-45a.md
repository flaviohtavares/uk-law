# Education Act 1997, section 45A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/44
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45A. Guidance as to discharge of duties : schools in England[^key-2d6b727f1c05efb7f0a444253fc768b8][^key-efc3c1bd6eb94dbf1dc552cb674af0a6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The persons responsible for discharging a relevant duty in relation to a school in England falling within section 42A(2)[^key-113ef4163adb1ff19dc411be2633f962] must, in discharging the duty, have regard to any guidance given from time to time by the Secretary of State.

(2) A “**relevant duty**” means a duty under section 42A(1) or (4) or 42B[^key-cede73289bb474a16bd0733b33fcbab3] .
