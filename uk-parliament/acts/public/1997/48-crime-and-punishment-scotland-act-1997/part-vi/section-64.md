# Crime and Punishment (Scotland) Act 1997, section 64

## 64. Interpretation.

*Extent: S*

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**supervised release order**” has the same meaning as in section 209 of the 1995 Act (supervised release orders);
- “**the 1984 Act**” means the [^c441689]Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1984;
- “**the 1986 Act**” means the [^c441690]Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986;
- “**the 1989 Act**” means the [^c441691]Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989;
- “**the 1993 Act**” means the [^c441692]Prisoners and Criminal Proceedings (Scotland) Act 1993;
- “**the 1995 Act**” means the [^c441693]Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995.
