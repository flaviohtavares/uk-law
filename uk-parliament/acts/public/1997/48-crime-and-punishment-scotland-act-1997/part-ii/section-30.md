# Crime and Punishment (Scotland) Act 1997, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2022-05-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Routine evidence.

*Extent: S*

(1) Schedule 9 to the 1995 Act (routine evidence) shall be amended in accordance with subsection (3)[^key-31240a443e99810c2f59a18bec8687ec] below.

(2)[^key-5beed38787a4937abb08c4b7dcfd1f1f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In the entry relating to the [^c441648]Video Recordings Act 1984, for the words in the second and third columns there shall be substituted the words in, respectively, the left and right hand columns below—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | A person authorised to do so by the Secretary of State, being a person who has examined the record maintained in pursuance of arrangements made by the designated authority and in the case of a certificate in terms of—<br>(a) sub-paragraph (a) in column 3, the video work mentioned in that sub-paragraph;<br>(b) sub-paragraph (b) in that column, both video works mentioned in that sub-paragraph. | That the record shows any of the following—<br>(a) in respect of a video work (or part of a video work) contained in a video recording identified by the certificate, that by a date specified no classification certificate had been issued;<br>(b) in respect of a video work which is the subject of a certificate under sub-paragraph (a) above, that the video work differs in a specified way from another video work contained in a video recording identified in the certificate under this sub-paragraph and that, on a date specified, a classification certificate was issued in respect of that other video work;<br>(c) that, by a date specified, no classification certificate had been issued in respect of a video work having a particular title;<br>(d) that, on a date specified, a classification certificate was issued in respect of a video work having a particular title and that a document which is identified in the certificate under this sub-paragraph is a copy of the classification certificate so issued;<br>expressions used in column 2, or in this column, of this entry being construed in accordance with that Act; and in each of sub-paragraphs (a) to (d) above “**specified**” means specified in the certificate under that sub-paragraph. |

.

(4) Section 5 of the [^c441649]Video Recordings Act 1993 shall cease to have effect.

(5) In Schedule 22 of the [^c441650]Environment Act 1995 (minor and consequential amendments), paragraph 35 shall cease to have effect.
