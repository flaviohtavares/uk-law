National Health Service (Private Finance) Act 1997

1997 c. 56

An Act to make provision about the powers of National Health Service trusts to enter into agreements.

\[15th July 1997\]

[^c863777]Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—

## 1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Powers of NHS trusts to enter into agreements.[^c863778][^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd] For the purposes of this section, an agreement is an externally financed development agreement if it is certified as such in writing by the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd] The Secretary of State may give a certificate under this section if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd]

- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd] a person proposes to make a loan to, or provide any other form of finance for, another party in connection with the agreement. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd]

- “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd]**another party *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd]**” means any party to the agreement other than the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd] Board or, as the case may be, the Agency *(Extent: S)*[^key-761f8f012616481eef1c4a05af8cb381] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd] ; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd]

  - (a) works, buildings, plant, equipment or other property; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd] The fact that an agreement made by a *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd] Health Board, a Special Health Board or the Agency *(Extent: S)*[^key-3d207bbe4cf21ccdeb00629549311021] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd] has not been certified under this section does not affect its validity. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5135920c0c5a3cb6b205b99b7b632cd]

## 2. Provision for Northern Ireland.

An Order in Council under paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to the [^c863779]Northern Ireland Act 1974 (legislation for Northern Ireland in the interim period) which contains a statement that it is made only for purposes corresponding to those of section 1—

- (a) shall not be subject to paragraph 1(4) and (5) of that Schedule (affirmative resolution of both Houses of Parliament); but
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

## 3. Short title and extent.

(1) This Act may be cited as the National Health Service (Private Finance) Act 1997.

(2) Section 2 extends to Northern Ireland but section 1 does not.
