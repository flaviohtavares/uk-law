## 105. Appeals by authorising officers: supplementary.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the Investigatory Powers Commissioner[^c-980ba83fbc7f67a8a92bc12ab8319c80] determines an appeal under section 104—

- (a) he shall give notice of his determination—
  - (i) to the authorising officer concerned, and[^c12094961]
  - (ii) to the Judicial Commissioner[^c-e3b2d2cd9d4cf39010a9008c18094013] against whose refusal, decision or determination the appeal was made, [^c12094971]. . .
  - (iii) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) if he dismisses the appeal, he shall make a report of his findings—
  - (i) to the authorising officer concerned,
  - (ii) to the Judicial Commissioner[^c-792087e3856442274caba4e0eb6017db] against whose refusal, decision or determination the appeal was made, and
  - (iii) [^c-3c651913628648c5be88c1dcaf0ff994]... to the Prime Minister and the Scottish Ministers[^c12094981].

(2) Subject to subsection (1)(b), the Investigatory Powers Commissioner shall not give any reasons for a determination under section 104.

(3) Nothing in section 104 shall prevent a designated deputy from exercising the powers conferred by subsection (1) of that section on an authorising officer within paragraph (a) or (c)[^c-d177af5ecf35b5de0a9c27cf336a839f] of section 93(5).
