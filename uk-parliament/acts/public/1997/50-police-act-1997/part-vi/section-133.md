# Police Act 1997, section 133

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133. Rehabilitation of Offenders.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

The following provisions (which restrict the effect of the [^c12097061]Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 and the [^c12097071]Rehabilitation of Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1978) shall cease to have effect—

- (a) section 189 of, and Schedule 14 to, the [^c12097081]Financial Services Act 1986;
- (b) section 95 of the [^c12097091]Banking Act 1987;
- (c) section 39 of the [^c12097101]Osteopaths Act 1993;
- (d) section 19 of the [^c12097111]National Lottery etc. Act 1993;
- (e) section 40 of the [^c12097121]Chiropractors Act 1994.
