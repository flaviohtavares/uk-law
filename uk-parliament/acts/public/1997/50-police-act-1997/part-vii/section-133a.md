> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/50

## 133A. Meaning of “prevention” and “detection”.[^c12097131]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 81(5) of the Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000 (meaning of “**prevention**” and “**detection**”) shall apply for the purposes of this Act as it applies for the purposes of that Act[^key-52dc590898aff6ee13943ae8301333e5].
