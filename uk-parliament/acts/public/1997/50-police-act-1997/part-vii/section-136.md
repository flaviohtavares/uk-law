# Police Act 1997, section 136

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 136. Police: co-operation on implementation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It shall be the duty of police authorities for areas in Great Britain, and the Northern Ireland Policing Board[^c12097181], and their staff to co-operate with each other, and generally to exercise their functions, so as to facilitate the implementation of Parts I and II of this Act and any transfer of property or staff made by an order under section 135.
