# Police Act 1997, section 125B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/50

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 125B. Form of applications[^key-8c22d5bbf27041b64f98c49bd0a64699]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-825f0ab22ff305c8fd1d52e90cfaf09e] DBS *(Extent: E+W)* may determine the form, manner and contents of an application for the purposes of any provision of this Part.

(2) A determination may, in particular, impose requirements about the form or manner in which an electronic application is to be signed or countersigned.

(3)[^key-0cfe0cd3cb68cc83ed4bc1c89faa8c68] In this section “**application**” includes a request under section 116A(1), 120AC(1) or 120AD(2).
