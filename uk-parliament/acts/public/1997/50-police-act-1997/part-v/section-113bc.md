# Police Act 1997, section 113BC

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 113BC. Suitability information: power to amend[^c-e8d8af7461d70dee39938021e21698c8]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by order made by statutory instrument—

- (a) amend section 113BA for the purpose of altering the meaning of suitability information relating to children;
- (b) amend section 113BB for the purpose of altering the meaning of suitability information relating to vulnerable adults ;[^c-10955db03851bbbea6d33cd21ae69f89]
- (c) amend section 120AC(4)(b) in consequence of an order made under paragraph (a) or (b).

*Extent: N.I.*

- (c (Extent: E+W+N.I.))[^c-b869ad012f0b51ad5a4ddbb07f900ee5] amend section 120AC(4)(b) in consequence of an order made under paragraph (a) or (b). *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*

(2) Such an order is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
