# Police Act 1997, section 120AA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/50

## 120AA. Refusal, cancellation or suspension of registration on other grounds[^key-7c39cb5a10d385ea08d0d8290fdc7b76][^key-012c56c7c73adf01b30d4bb813937930][^M_E_ffbfa3b9-b378-42f2-9009-a6f43bd917eb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulations may make provision enabling DBS[^key-49a213a5aa08b48893eda469c96bfdf5] in prescribed cases to refuse to register a person who, in the opinion of DBS, is likely to act as the registered person in relation to[^key-a32558b4d5cb707572ebc39d79e7fe05] fewer applications under this Part in any period of twelve months than a prescribed minimum number.

(2) Subsection (3) applies where a registered person—

- (a) is, in the opinion of DBS[^key-f08cd4b05dbeba73ab65300d147a51cb], no longer likely to wish to act as the registered person in relation to[^key-63b9012bc13ce5f67a0aef32f42f77c1] applications under this Part,
- (b) has, in any period of twelve months during which he was registered, acted as the registered person in relation to[^key-95f9e773f4ac67d02cf8fd45c64a6bd5] fewer applications under this Part than the minimum number specified in respect of him by regulations under subsection (1), or
- (c) has failed to comply with any condition of his registration.

(3) Subject to section 120AB, DBS[^key-33466bc2ec3804d89ed65795d3497917] may—

- (a) suspend that person’s registration for such period not exceeding 6 months as DBS thinks fit, or
- (b) remove that person from the register.

(4)[^key-d31497e93383848d69a7aff278541e85] Subsection (6) applies if an application is made under section 120 by an individual who—

- (a) has previously been a registered person; and
- (b) has been removed from the register (otherwise than at that individual's own request).

(5) Subsection (6) also applies if an application is made under section 120 by a body corporate or unincorporate which—

- (a) has previously been a registered person; and
- (b) has been removed from the register (otherwise than at its own request).

(6) DBS[^key-053573453abae38baada6a18e94fd577] may refuse the application.

## 120AA. Refusal, cancellation or suspension of registration on other grounds[^key-7c39cb5a10d385ea08d0d8290fdc7b76][^M_E_dbd09a64-b42c-4d27-84ba-f217ae01fc3c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations may make provision enabling the Secretary of State in prescribed cases to refuse to register a person who, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, is likely to act as the registered person in relation to[^key-af5afd23fc594c981196fe0ebfacd1c4] fewer applications under this Part in any period of twelve months than a prescribed minimum number.

(2) Subsection (3) applies where a registered person—

- (a) is, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, no longer likely to wish to act as the registered person in relation to[^key-f4b77212b01c24480c27b2cf5f695d0b] applications under this Part,
- (b) has, in any period of twelve months during which he was registered, acted as the registered person in relation to[^key-6f4379f0b1cf607f431943cf7d6c9b74] fewer applications under this Part than the minimum number specified in respect of him by regulations under subsection (1), or
- (c) has failed to comply with any condition of his registration.

(3) Subject to section 120AB, the Secretary of State may—

- (a) suspend that person’s registration for such period not exceeding 6 months as the Secretary of State thinks fit, or
- (b) remove that person from the register.

(4)[^key-187f5b9210eb6ae7508a86d904c0c99f] Subsection (6) applies if an application is made under section 120 by an individual who—

- (a) has previously been a registered person; and
- (b) has been removed from the register (otherwise than at that individual's own request).

(5) Subsection (6) also applies if an application is made under section 120 by a body corporate or unincorporate which—

- (a) has previously been a registered person; and
- (b) has been removed from the register (otherwise than at its own request).

(6) The Department may refuse the application.
