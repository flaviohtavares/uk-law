## 127 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Saving: disclosure of information and records.[^M_I_3801d113-f1ad-47fa-d758-c813cd288fae][^c-9f3795ae92067594f3cc4a19fdd4fad9][^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e]

Nothing in sections 112 to 119 shall be taken to prejudice any power which exists apart from this Act to disclose information or to make records available. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e]
