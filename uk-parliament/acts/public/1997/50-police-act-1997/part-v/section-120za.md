## 120ZA (Extent: E+W). Regulations about registration (Extent: E+W)[^key-4643e5e58f1ec86052ff352afc5bf3a3][^key-4b8e29ff10e793b60d7703f539acae31]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W)) The Secretary of State may by regulations make further provision about registration. *(Extent: E+W)*

(2 (Extent: E+W)) Regulations under this section may in particular make provision for— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) the payment of fees, *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) the information to be included in the register, *(Extent: E+W)*
- (c (Extent: E+W)) the registration of any person to be subject to conditions, *(Extent: E+W)*
- (d (Extent: E+W)) the nomination by— *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (i (Extent: E+W)) a body corporate or unincorporate, or *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (ii (Extent: E+W)) a person appointed to an office by virtue of any enactment, *(Extent: E+W)*

  of the individuals authorised to act for it or, as the case may be, him in relation to the countersigning of applications under this Part *(Extent: E+W)* or the transmitting of applications under section 113A(2A) or 113B(2A)[^key-bec81b77b461c22b8b02649291ecafdb] *(Extent: E+W)*, and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (e (Extent: E+W)) the refusal by *(Extent: E+W)* the Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2291b76018f45178be6dad918a590a5d] *(Extent: E+W)* DBS *(Extent: E+W)* *(Extent: E+W)*, on such grounds as may be specified in or determined under the regulations, to accept or to continue to accept the nomination of a person as so authorised. *(Extent: E+W)*

(3 (Extent: E+W)) The provision which may be made by virtue of subsection (2)(c) includes provision— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) for the registration or continued registration of any person to be subject to prescribed conditions or, if the regulations so provide, such conditions as *(Extent: E+W)* the Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fa9f113d241380fc7659e07c1b1e0dda] *(Extent: E+W)* DBS *(Extent: E+W)* *(Extent: E+W)* thinks fit, and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) for *(Extent: E+W)* the Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fa9f113d241380fc7659e07c1b1e0dda] *(Extent: E+W)* DBS *(Extent: E+W)* *(Extent: E+W)* to vary or revoke those conditions. *(Extent: E+W)*

(4 (Extent: E+W)) The conditions imposed by virtue of subsection (2)(c) may in particular include conditions— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) requiring a registered person, before *(Extent: E+W)* acting as the registered person in relation to[^key-d9a3bbd9fb573de3c18ad4c1e64a7222] *(Extent: E+W)* an application at an individual’s request, to verify the identity of that individual in the prescribed manner, *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) requiring an application under section *(Extent: E+W)* 113A or 113B[^key-f8fb025de3c1cdefc830920cc44d119b] *(Extent: E+W)* to be transmitted by electronic means to *(Extent: E+W)* the Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5f7697553bc83bb64ef042c8fb915747] *(Extent: E+W)* DBS *(Extent: E+W)* *(Extent: E+W)* by the *(Extent: E+W)* person who acts as the registered person in relation to the application[^key-d09da45f8bf4201087973b530a4a45ed] *(Extent: E+W)*, and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (c (Extent: E+W)) requiring a registered person to comply with any code of practice for the time being in force under section 122. *(Extent: E+W)*
