## 120. Registered persons.[^M_E_7a9e1252-a2e1-4e24-82bf-9e45c6f5c628][^key-d49305865ef1a951c60a0e459b72bbc5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Part a registered person is a person who is listed in a register to be maintained by the Secretary of State for the purposes of this Part.

(2) The[^key-6ef79012953c289f3ca2a6a832443c06] Secretary of State shall include in the register any person who—

- (a) applies to him in writing to be registered, and[^key-9bca76b92cf7e9b68429599153ea0898]
- (b) satisfies the conditions in subsections (4) to (6),[^key-ae1bfc511d475a8d9aad3e67da73a845]...
- (c)[^key-ae1bfc511d475a8d9aad3e67da73a845] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^key-a9049350a59bf9fa3996eddb96f9a1f0] Subsection (2) is subject to—

- (a) regulations under section 120ZA,
- (b) section 120A, and
- (c) section 120AA and regulations made under that section.

(3)[^key-050ac8512850373f744400fff71742c3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) A person applying for registration under this section must be—

- (a) a body corporate or unincorporate,
- (b) a person appointed to an office by virtue of any enactment, or
- (c) an individual who employs others in the course of a business.

(4A)[^key-96149aacb695591ab87c7878ed56ca0f] An individual under the age of 18 applying for registration undersubsection (4)(b) or (c) must satisfy the Department that there is good reason for being registered.

(5) A body applying for registration under this section must satisfy the Secretary of State that it—

- (a) is likely to ask exempted questions, or
- (b) is likely to act as the registered person in relation to[^key-1f883d21a25a770694922e0115593a93] applications under section 113A or 113B at the request of bodies or individuals asking exempted questions.

(6) A person, other than a body, applying for registration under this section must satisfy the Secretary of State that he is likely to ask exempted questions.

(7) In this section “**exempted question**” has the same meaning as in section 113A.
