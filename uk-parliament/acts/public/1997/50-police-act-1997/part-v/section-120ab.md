## 120AB. Procedure for cancellation or suspension under section 120AA[^c-176b10f3f936dd42e89302ed00acc422][^c-012c56c7c73adf01b30d4bb813937930]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) Before cancelling or suspending a person’s registration by virtue of section 120AA, the Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0ed726bb5ff55ce02a20ebe940b1e7d0]DBS *(Extent: E+W)* must send him *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-847a5798dcee86e6562c78f4ee531a08]its *(Extent: E+W)* written notice of his intention to do so.

(2) Every such notice must—

- (a) give the Secretary of State’s *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8a1af4feda100406458df7cd150a0a2e]DBS's *(Extent: E+W)* reasons for proposing to cancel or suspend the registration, and
- (b) inform the person concerned of his right under subsection (3) to make representations.

(3) A person who receives such a notice may, within 21 days of service, make representations in writing to the Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbba30dd95d9f9c1691bd6c88935b59a]DBS *(Extent: E+W)* as to why the registration should not be cancelled or suspended.

(4) After considering such representations, the Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-92ada443f28e73143f9509754f3d2892]DBS *(Extent: E+W)* must give the registered person written notice—

- (a) that at the end of a further period of six weeks beginning with the date of service, the person’s registration will be cancelled or suspended, or
- (b) that he *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b3842d4c76fb1cff8b4021f493364dfb]it *(Extent: E+W)* does not propose to take any further action.

(5) If no representations are received within the period mentioned in subsection (3) the Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b5b99f864e3621c2897a37125ce321dd]DBS *(Extent: E+W)* may cancel or suspend the person’s registration at the end of the period mentioned in that subsection.

(6) Subsection (1) does not prevent the Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-60dd77c301490312861a7ff7175dfeb6]DBS *(Extent: E+W)* from imposing on the registered person a lesser sanction than that specified in the notice under that subsection.

(7) Any notice under this section that is required to be given in writing may be given by being transmitted electronically.

(8) This section does not apply where—

- (a) the Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13b1fcb5678ffbe193e57e9433b0334d]DBS *(Extent: E+W)* is satisfied, in the case of a registered person other than a body, that the person has died or is incapable, by reason of physical or mental impairment, of acting as the registered person in relation to[^c-434d6d745381086b7200cadda7c8176c] applications under this Part, or
- (b) the registered person has requested to be removed from the register.

(9) The Secretary of State may by regulations amend subsection (4)(a) by substituting for the period there specified, such other period as may be specified in the regulations.
