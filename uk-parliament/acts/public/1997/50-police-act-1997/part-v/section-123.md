## 123 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Offences: falsification, &c.[^M_I_518b2bbe-9d97-44a2-c2a7-951fe5782e47][^c-220d7ad7ff44e7c04a55b36263d9e933][^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e] A person commits an offence if, with intent to deceive, he— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e] makes a false certificate under this Part, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e] alters a certificate under this Part, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e] uses a certificate under this Part which relates to another person in a way which suggests that it relates to himself, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e]
- (d (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e] allows a certificate under this Part which relates to him to be used by another person in a way which suggests that it relates to that other person. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e] A person commits an offence if he knowingly makes a false statement for the purpose of obtaining, or enabling another person to obtain, a certificate under this Part. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e] A person who is guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, or to both. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-18291a6dd88b7ac8a1b42216c7a67f7e]
