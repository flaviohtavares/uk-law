## 113E. Criminal record certificates: specified children's and adults' lists: urgent cases[^M_C_db4de8dc-b785-4efa-bcea-db4933a2a37e][^M_C_4f8ef368-b8a0-4a6e-9b1c-70a802aa2745][^key-c18b8140a9b9e303d4c11a81c2e78e7b][^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34][^key-537f1cfef743da40a0ea29a8029de155]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies to an application under section 113A or 113B if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]

- (a) it is accompanied by a children's suitability statement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]
- (b) the registered person requests an urgent preliminary response, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]
- (c) the applicant pays in the prescribed manner such additional fee as is prescribed in respect of the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]

(2) The Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d6c9dcb6a1c552a4b39d085feb818a9f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34] DBS *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34] must notify the registered person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]

- (a) if the applicant is not included in a specified children's list, of that fact; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]
- (b) if the applicant is included in such a list, of the details prescribed for the purposes of section 113C(1)(b) above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]
- (c) if the applicant is not subject to a specified children's direction, of that fact; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]
- (d) if the applicant is subject to such a direction, of the grounds on which the direction was given and the details prescribed for the purposes of section 113C(1)(d) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]

(3) Subsection (4) applies to an application under section 113A or 113B if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]

- (a) it is accompanied by an adults' suitability statement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]
- (b) the registered person requests an urgent preliminary response, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]
- (c) the applicant pays in the prescribed manner such additional fee as is prescribed in respect of the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]

(4) The Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-15016a4ed05ce53645bc13a5aa0ac005] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34] DBS *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34] must notify the registered person either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]

- (a) that the applicant is not included in a specified adults' list, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]
- (b) that a criminal record certificate or enhanced criminal record certificate will be issued in due course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]

(5) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]

- “criminal record certificate” has the same meaning as in section 113A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]
- “enhanced criminal record certificate” has the same meaning as in section 113B; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]
- “children's suitability statement” “, specified children's direction” and “specified children's list” have the same meaning as in section 113C; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]
- “adults' suitability statement” and “specified adults' list” have the same meaning as in section 113D. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ddd8afd3295c676d56687e05cb58b34]
