# National Health Service (Primary Care) Act 1997, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2007-03-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Corresponding provision for Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An Order in Council under paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to the [^c12087061]Northern Ireland Act 1974 (legislation for Northern Ireland in the interim period) which contains a statement that it is made only for purposes corresponding to purposes of any of the provisions of this Act—

- (a) shall not be subject to paragraph 1(4) and (5) of that Schedule (affirmative resolution of both Houses of Parliament); but
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(2) This section does not apply in relation to the provisions of sections 32 to 34.
