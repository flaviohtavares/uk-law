# Finance (No. 2) Act 1997, section 18

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Rates for financial year 1997.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The rate at which corporation tax is charged for the financial year 1997 shall be, and shall be deemed always to have been, 31 per cent. (and not 33 per cent. as provided by section 58 of the [^c4155511]Finance Act 1997).

(2)[^key-9c8ffb21d1fb930528166d5ce1db9a6d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) All such adjustments shall be made, whether by way of discharge or repayment of tax or otherwise, as may be required in consequence of the provisions of this section.
