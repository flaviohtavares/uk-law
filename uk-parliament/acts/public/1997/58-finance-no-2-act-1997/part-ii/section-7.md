# Finance (No. 2) Act 1997, section 7

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Rate of duty on spirits.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 5 of the [^c4155401]Alcoholic Liquor Duties Act 1979 (spirits), for “£18.99” there shall be substituted “ £19.56 ”.

(2) This section shall come into force on 1st January 1998.
