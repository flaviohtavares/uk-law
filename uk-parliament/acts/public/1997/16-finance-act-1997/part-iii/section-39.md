## 39. Bad debt relief.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 36 of the [^c14707111]Value Added Tax Act 1994, paragraph (b) of subsection (4) (condition of bad debt relief that property in goods supplied has passed) shall not apply in the case of any claim made under that section in relation to a supply of goods made after the day on which this Act is passed.
