# Finance Act 1997, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Voluntary registration.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For sub-paragraph (2) of paragraph 10 of Schedule 1 to the [^c14707041]Value Added Tax Act 1994 (non-taxable supplies in respect of which a person is entitled to be registered) there shall be substituted the following sub-paragraph—

> (2) A supply is within this sub-paragraph if—
>
> - (a) it is made outside the United Kingdom but would be a taxable supply if made in the United Kingdom; or
> - (b) it is specified for the purposes of subsection (2) of section 26 in an order made under paragraph (c) of that subsection.
