# Finance Act 1997, section 71

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Set-off against franked investment income.[^c14707681]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 242 of the Taxes Act 1988 (set-off of losses against surplus franked investment income) shall have effect, and be deemed always to have had effect, as if at the end of paragraph (c) of subsection (6) (power to carry set-off forward) there were inserted

> and
>
> - (d) in relation to relief given in respect of amounts available to be set against profits under section 83 of the [^c14707691]Finance Act 1996 or paragraph 4 of Schedule 11 to that Act or under section 131(4) of the [^c14707701]Finance Act 1993 (which are provisions relating to deficits on loan relationships, foreign exchange losses and losses on certain financial instruments);

.
