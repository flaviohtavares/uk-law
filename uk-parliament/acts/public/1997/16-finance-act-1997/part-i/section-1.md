# Finance Act 1997, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Rates of duty on spirits and wines of equivalent strength.[^c14706471]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 5 of the [^c14706481]Alcoholic Liquor Duties Act 1979 (spirits), for “£19.78” there shall be substituted “ £18.99 ”.

(2) In Part II of the Table of rates of duty in Schedule 1 to that Act (wine or made-wine of a strength exceeding 22 per cent.), for “19.78” there shall be substituted “ 18.99 ”.

(3) This section shall be deemed to have come into force at 6 o’clock in the evening of 26th November 1996.
