>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Increase in general rate.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Schedule 1 to the [^c14706861]Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994 (annual rates of duty), in paragraph 1(2) (the general rate), for “£140” there shall be substituted “ £145 ”.

(2) This section applies in relation to licences taken out after 26th November 1996.
