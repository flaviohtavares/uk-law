# Finance Act 1997, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Rates of tobacco products duty.[^c14706581]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the Table of rates of duty in Schedule 1 to the [^c14706591]Tobacco Products Duty Act 1979 there shall be substituted—

TABLE

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| 1\. Cigarettes | An amount equal to 21 per cent. of the retail price plus £65.97 per thousand cigarettes. |
| 2\. Cigars | £98.02 per kilogram. |
| 3\. Hand-rolling tobacco | £87.74 per kilogram. |
| 4\. Other smoking tobacco and chewing tobacco | £43.10 per kilogram. |

(2) This section shall be deemed to have come into force at 6 o’clock in the evening of 26th November 1996.
