# Finance Act 1997, section 109

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 109. Nil levy on dwelling-house disposals.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 136 of the [^c14708941]Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 (levy on local authorities in respect of dwelling-house disposals) shall have effect, and be deemed always to have had effect, with the following subsection inserted after subsection (4)—

> (4A) The power of the Secretary of State to determine a formula for the purposes of item D in subsection (3) shall include power to determine that, in such cases as he may determine, item D is to be taken to be equal to item CR.
