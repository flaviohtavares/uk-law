# Justices of the Peace Act 1997, section 51

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Immunity for acts within jurisdiction.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2005-04-01).**

(1)[^c14197571] No action shall lie against any justice of the peace or justices’ clerk in respect of any act or omission of his—

- (a) in the execution of his duty—
  - (i) as such a justice; or
  - (ii) as such a clerk exercising, by virtue of any statutory provision, any of the functions of a single justice; and
- (b) with respect to any matter within his jurisdiction.

(2)[^c14197581] In this section references to a justices’ clerk include any person appointed by a magistrates’ courts committee to assist a justices’ clerk.
