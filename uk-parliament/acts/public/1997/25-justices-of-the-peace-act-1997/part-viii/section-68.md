# Justices of the Peace Act 1997, section 68

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Acts done by justices outside their commission area.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2005-04-01).**

(1) A justice of the peace for any commission area may act as a justice for that area in any commission area which adjoins the commission area for which he is a justice.
