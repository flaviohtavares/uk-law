# Justices of the Peace Act 1997, section 64A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64A. Disqualifying offices

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2005-04-01).**

The person who is the chairman of the Independent Police Complaints Commission an any person who is otherwise a member of that Commission or a member of its staff is disqualified for being appointed or acting as a justice of the peace.
