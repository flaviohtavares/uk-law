# Merchant Shipping and Maritime Security Act 1997, section 25

> Text as at 2015-03-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Amendments of Aviation and Maritime Security Act 1990.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 4 (amendments of Part III of the [^c8633781]Aviation and Maritime Security Act 1990, which relates to the protection of ships and harbour areas against acts of violence) shall have effect.
