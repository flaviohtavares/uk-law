# Telecommunications (Fraud) Act 1997

> Text as at 2003-07-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Telecommunications (Fraud) Act 1997

1997 c. 4

[^c20999791]An Act to amend the Telecommunications Act 1984 to make further provision for the prevention of fraud in connection with use of a telecommunication system.

\[27th February 1997\]

Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—

## 1. Possession or supply of anything for fraudulent purpose in connection with use of telecommunication system.

In the 1984 Act, after section 42 there shall be inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]

> **42A. Possession or supply of anything for fraudulent purpose in connection with use of telecommunication system. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c20999921]**
>
> (1) Subsection (2) below applies if a person has in his custody or under his control anything which may be used for the purpose of obtaining, or for a purpose connected with the obtaining of, a service to which section 42(1) above applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>
> (2) If the person intends— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>
> - (a) to use the thing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>   - (i) to obtain such a service dishonestly, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20999921] for a purpose connected with the dishonest obtaining of such a service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
> - (b) dishonestly to allow the thing to be used to obtain such a service, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
> - (c) to allow the thing to be used for a purpose connected with the dishonest obtaining of such a service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>
> he shall be guilty of an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>
> (3) Subsection (4) below applies if a person supplies or offers to supply anything which may be used for the purpose of obtaining, or for a purpose connected with the obtaining of, a service to which section 42(1) above applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>
> (4) If the person supplying or offering to supply the thing knows or believes that the person to whom it is supplied or offered intends or intends if it is supplied to him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>
> - (a) to use it— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>   - (i) to obtain such a service dishonestly, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20999921] for a purpose connected with the dishonest obtaining of such a service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
> - (b) dishonestly to allow it to be used to obtain such a service, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
> - (c) to allow it to be used for a purpose connected with the dishonest obtaining of such a service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>
> he shall be guilty of an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>
> (5) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>
> - (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
> - (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years or to a fine or to both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]
>
> (6) In this section, references to use of a thing include, in the case of a thing which is used to record any data, use of any of the data. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999921]

## 2. Increased penalty for fraudulent use of telecommunication system.

(1)[^c20999931] In section 42(1)(b) of the 1984 Act (penalty on conviction on indictment for fraudulent use of telecommunication system), for “two years” there shall be substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999931] “ five years ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999931].

(2) Subsection (1) above does not apply to an offence committed before this Act comes into force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999931]

## 3. Short title, interpretation, commencement and extent.

(1)[^c20999941] This Act may be cited as the Telecommunications (Fraud) Act 1997. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999941]

(2) In this Act “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999941]**the 1984 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999941]**” means the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999941] [^c8634731]Telecommunications Act 1984. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999941]

(3) This Act shall come into force at the end of the period of two months beginning with the day on which it is passed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999941]

(4) This Act extends to Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20999941]
