## 6. Restriction on works affecting listed buildings.[^key-037661255e2f132bd4dadd13a2a8cab8][^key-49c3bf57ab9f5cc0d9d64a6ed2407822][^key-b41b613c9450848755b4ce25783281d5][^key-1a73ea9844012a55fc61a96348052e91][^key-0fa908744ad62c67df4d78836a2b734a][^key-8f937845ec9926ca3c24c007e5bdd58c][^key-9a7cc3ea509051ccf4f7151358171c57][^key-1beff6be837c7dc6b2ad2b24c6889f25][^key-0481d14bae1c8a1056cb3179ae30282c]

*Extent: S*

Subject to the following provisions of this Act, no person shall execute or cause to be executed any works for the demolition of a listed building or for its alteration or extension in any manner which would affect its character as a building of special architectural or historic interest, unless the works are authorised.
