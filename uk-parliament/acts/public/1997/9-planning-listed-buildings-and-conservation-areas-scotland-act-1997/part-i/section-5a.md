## 5A. Certificate that building not intended to be listed[^c-7c97be4034299f2b362aabe64a088703]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-e5b8a2f0e73aa7e962ca9ad4702680c8] Historic Environment Scotland[^c-9e81f983277300b50f40fb83d4770f88] may, on the application of any person, issue a certificate stating that it does[^c-88f5cc8a92ce5a94eb6cf733184e149b] not intend to include a building in a list compiled or approved under section 1.

(2) Where Historic Environment Scotland issues[^c-5da9f20b145b978f77b37536dd9bdeef] a certificate under subsection (1) in respect of a building—

- (a) it[^c-2995628d41edffc3e6dbb02149d9fe14] may not for a period of 5 years from the date of issue exercise in relation to the building any of the powers conferred on it by section 1, and
- (b) a planning authority may not for that period—
  - (i) serve a building preservation notice in relation to the building, or
  - (ii) affix such a notice under section 4(1).

(3) A person submitting an application to Historic Environment Scotland[^c-91e5e6256385159382c4596fe973c892] under subsection (1) must, at the same time as submitting it, give notice of the application to the planning authority within whose district the building is situated.
