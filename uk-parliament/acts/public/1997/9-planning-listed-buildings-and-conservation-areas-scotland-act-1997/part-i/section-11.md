# Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) (Scotland) Act 1997, section 11

## 11. Reference of certain applications to Secretary of State.[^c-a5c572c9011e9592a49aaf99057ae264][^c-e5ad2ddbaba8e593a24e3e5080e0e27d][^c-bc1b8fefc8ab228ea3d821647b381ffb][^c-0481d14bae1c8a1056cb3179ae30282c]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-183a4a1563702e055681871aaa97338b] The Secretary of State may give directions requiring applications for listed building consent to be referred to him instead of being dealt with by the planning authority.

(2)[^c-183a4a1563702e055681871aaa97338b] A direction under this section may relate either to a particular application or to applications in respect of such buildings as may be specified in the direction.

(3)[^c-183a4a1563702e055681871aaa97338b] An application in respect of which a direction under this section has effect shall be referred to the Secretary of State accordingly.

(4)[^c-48abb2bd2e7d9155060b0d800fbdb811] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) The decision of the Secretary of State on any application referred to him under this section shall be final.
