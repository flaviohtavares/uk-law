# Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) (Scotland) Act 1997, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2022-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/9
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 61. Designation of conservation areas.

*Extent: S*

(1) Every planning authority shall—

- (a) from time to time determine which parts of their district are areas of special architectural or historic interest the character or appearance of which it is desirable to preserve or enhance, and
- (b) designate such areas as conservation areas.

(2)[^c-ea1eb733c449c63a7de328a6f0a8c486] Historic Environment Scotland[^c-caca00db4a18b4f48c600665edac3194] may from time to time, after consultation with a planning authority, determine that any part of the authority’s district which is not for the time being designated as a conservation area is an area of special architectural or historic interest the character or appearance of which it is desirable to preserve or enhance; and, if Historic Environment Scotland so determines, it[^c-e9c64957f98c92956b9188f064316de4] may designate that part as a conservation area.
