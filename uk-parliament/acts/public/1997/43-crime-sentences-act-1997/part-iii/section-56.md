# Crime (Sentences) Act 1997, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-06-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/43

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Transitional provisions, savings and repeals.[^c11467961]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The transitional provisions and savings contained in Schedule 5 to this Act shall have effect; but nothing in this subsection shall be taken as prejudicing the operation of sections 16 and 17 of the [^c11467971]Interpretation Act 1978 (which relate to the effect of repeals).

(2)[^c-7b75fd1d23733ef2fb35444d82736c0d][^c-fc61744007b599c5bb7f5ec103e81d32] The enactments specified in Schedule 6 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
