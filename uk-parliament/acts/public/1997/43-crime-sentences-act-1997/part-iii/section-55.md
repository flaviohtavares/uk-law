# Crime (Sentences) Act 1997, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-06-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1997/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales; Scotland, Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 55. Minor and consequential amendments.[^c11467681][^c11467691]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 4 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified, being minor amendments and amendments consequential on the provisions of this Act.

(2) For the purposes of any of those enactments as so amended—

- (a)[^c11467701] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a sentence falls to be imposed under subsection (3A) of section 70 of the [^c11467711]Army Act 1955 or the [^c11467721]Air Force Act 1955 or subsection (1A) of section 42 of the [^c11467731]Naval Discipline Act 1957 if it is required by that subsection and[^c11467741] the court-martial is not of the opinion there mentioned.

## 55. Minor and consequential amendments.[^c11467751][^c11467761]

*Extent: S+N.I.*

(1) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 4 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified, being minor amendments and amendments consequential on the provisions of this Act.

(2) For the purposes of any of those enactments as so amended—

- (a)[^c11467771] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a sentence falls to be imposed under subsection (3A) of section 70 of the [^c11467781]Army Act 1955 or the [^c11467791]Air Force Act 1955 or subsection (1A) of section 42 of the [^c11467801]Naval Discipline Act 1957 if it is required by that subsection in any case where the court-martial is not of the opinion there mentioned.
