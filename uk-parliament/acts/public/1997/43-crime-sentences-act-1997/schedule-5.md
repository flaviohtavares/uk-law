### Interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

**13.** In this Schedule—

- “**life prisoner**” has the same meaning as in Chapter II of Part II of this Act;
- “**term of imprisonment**” includes a sentence of detention in a young offender institution or under section 53 of the 1933 Act.
