# Crime (Sentences) Act 1997, section 5

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Appeals where previous convictions set aside.[^c11464761]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2000-08-25).**

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) a sentence has been imposed on any person under subsection (2) of section 2, 3 or 4 above; and
- (b) any previous conviction of his without which that section would not have applied has been subsequently set aside on appeal.

(2) Notwithstanding anything in section 18 of the [^c11464771]Criminal Appeal Act 1968, notice of appeal against the sentence may be given at any time within 28 days from the date on which the previous conviction was set aside.
