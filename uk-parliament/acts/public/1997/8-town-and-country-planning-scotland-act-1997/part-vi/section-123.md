# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997, section 123

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 123. Expressions used in connection with enforcement.[^c-525ea2a5bf0d39daff4aa1bbfa7caff8]

*Extent: S*

(1) For the purposes of this Act—

- (a) carrying out development without the required planning permission, or
- (b) failing to comply with any condition or limitation subject to which planning permission has been granted, or[^c-aee9cb4dfa237f6ef8c02a75d28ef129]
- (c) initiating development without giving notice in accordance with section 27A(1) of this Act, or
- (d) carrying out development without displaying a notice in accordance with section 27C(1) of this Act,

constitutes a breach of planning control.

(2) For the purposes of this Act—

- (a) the issue of an enforcement notice, or
- (b) the service of a breach of condition notice,

under this Part constitutes taking enforcement action as does the issuing of a notice under section 33A[^c-6323d741bc3e6179c63a2d8a7c729140].

(3) In this Part “**planning permission**” includes planning permission under Part III of the 1947 Act and Part III of the 1972 Act.
