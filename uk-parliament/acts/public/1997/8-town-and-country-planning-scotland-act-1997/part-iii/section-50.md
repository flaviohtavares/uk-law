## 50. Alteration of simplified planning zone schemes.[^key-22331c5e9995f289ecb86cbfe0848659]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-4148db2fd018de3007d94ba5feb69946] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) A planning authority may at any time decide―[^key-98767ef5865184ab3ccabce45d45c165]

- (b) to alter a simplified planning zone[^key-2ebbe97bc9cc50d678d7ba35b043e9df] scheme adopted by them, or
- (c) with the consent of the Secretary of State, to alter a scheme approved by him.

(3) Schedule 5 has effect with respect to the [^key-a8f4fa67755042599cfc0787ce807d2e]... alteration of simplified planning zone schemes and other related matters.
