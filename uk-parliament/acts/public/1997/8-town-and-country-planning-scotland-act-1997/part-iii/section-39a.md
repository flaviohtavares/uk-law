## 39A. Declining to determine an application: further provision[^c-5a1ee2d1da704780e80235ba6b9f8912]

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers must publish guidance outlining what constitutes a “similar application” and a “**significant change**” for the purposes of section 39.
