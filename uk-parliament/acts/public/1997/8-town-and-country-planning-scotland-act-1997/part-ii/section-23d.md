## 23D. Meaning of “key agency”[^c-a9a88db2b397a4914f8e8d771f762037]

*Extent: S*

Any reference in a provision of Part 1A or[^c-1231632de3f2990552f697ab49976e59] this Part to a “key agency” is to a person (other than an individual) or an officeholder[^c-a17b87e98ce570fd896b99f6be4b1287] which the Scottish Ministers specify as such for the purposes of that provision by regulations.
