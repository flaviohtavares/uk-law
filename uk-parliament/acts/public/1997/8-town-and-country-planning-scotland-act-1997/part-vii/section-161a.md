# Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997, section 161A

## 161A. Imminent danger to trees: entry to affix copy of tree preservation order[^key-7720a48f56af9e82dd619fa70ddc2b93]

*Extent: S*

(1) If it appears to a planning authority that a tree, group of trees or woodlands—

- (a) on land in their district, and
- (b) in respect of which they have made a tree preservation order,

may be in imminent danger of being cut down, topped, lopped, uprooted, wilfully damaged or wilfully destroyed and accordingly that it is expedient that a copy of the order should be affixed conspicuously to the tree, group of trees or woodlands in question, a person duly authorised in writing by them may enter the land and so affix such a copy.

(2) Subsection (1) is without prejudice to any provision made by virtue of section 161(3)(b) and (4) as respects notice of the making of the order.
