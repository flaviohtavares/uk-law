## 118. No withdrawal of constructive notice to treat.

*Extent: S*

Without prejudice to the provisions of section 107(1) and (2), a notice to treat which is deemed to have been served by virtue of this Chapter may not be withdrawn under section 39 of the [^c950132]Land Compensation (Scotland) Act 1963.
