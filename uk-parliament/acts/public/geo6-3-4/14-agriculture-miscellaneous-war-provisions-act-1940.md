### 15. Mole drainage.[^c590620][^c590619]

(1)[^c590622][^c590621] Where a scheme for the field drainage of any agricultural land or for the cleansing or other improvement of ditches on any such land or for the supply of water to any such land[^c590623] or to any building used in connection with any such land[^c590624]—

- (a) has been submitted . . . [^c590625] to the War Agricultural Executive Committee for the county or county borough in which the land is situated; and
- (b) has been approved for the purposes of this section by that Committee;

the Minister may, out of moneys provided by Parliament, make, towards expenditure incurred by any person in carrying out the scheme, grants of such amounts and subject to such conditions as the Treasury may approve.

(2)[^c590626] In assessing the amount of any compensation payable to a tenant of agricultural land, whether under the [^c590627]Agricultural Holdings Act, 1923, or under custom or agreement, by reason of the improvement of the land by mole drainage works in respect of which a grant has been made under this section, the grant shall be taken into account as if it had been a benefit allowed to the tenant in consideration of his executing the improvement, and the compensation shall be reduced accordingly.

### 22. Interpretation construction, citation and duration of Part III.

(1) In this Part of this Act—

- (a)[^c590629] the expression “**agricultural land**” has the meaning assigned to it by section twenty-nine of the Land Drainage Act, 1930, for the purposes of Part IV of that Act;
- (b) the expression “**dam**” includes a lock, weir or other structure affecting the flow of water in any watercourse.

(2)[^c590630] This Part of this Act shall be construed as one with the Land Drainage Act, 1930, and may be cited together with that Act as the Land Drainage Acts, 1930 and 1940.

### 30. Definitions.

(1) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c590636]

(2) For the purposes of this Part of this Act—

- (a) the expression “**agricultural holding**” means a holding within the meaning of the [^c590637]Agricultural Holdings Act, 1923;
- (b) the expression “**the Minister**” means the Minister of Agriculture and Fisheries.

### 31. Application to Scotland.

This Act shall apply to Scotland subject to the following modifications:—

(1) In this Part of this Act—

- (a) for any reference to the Minister of Agriculture and Fisheries there shall be substituted a reference to the Secretary of State;
- (b) for references to the [^c590638]Agricultural Holdings Act, 1923, and to a contract of tenancy within the meaning of that Act, there shall be respectively substituted references to the [^c590639]Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Acts, 1923 and 1931, and to a lease within the meaning of those Acts;
- (c) for any reference to the War Agricultural Executive Committee for a county or a county borough there shall be substituted a reference to the Agricultural Executive Committee for any area to which the Secretary of State has delegated any of his powers under regulations made under the [^c590640]Emergency Powers (Defence) Act, 1939;
- (d) for any reference to such a council as is mentioned in section twenty-one of the [^c590641]Land Settlement (Facilities) Act, 1919, there shall be substituted a reference to a local authority within the meaning of section twenty-two of the [^c590642]Land Settlement (Scotland) Act, 1919;
- (e) the expression “**owner**” has the like meaning as in the [^c590643]Public Health (Scotland) Act, 1897,
