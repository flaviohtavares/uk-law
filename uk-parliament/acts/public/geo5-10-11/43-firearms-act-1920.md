# Firearms Act 1920

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1995-11-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Firearms Act 1920 [^c581879](repealed 8.11.1995)

1920 c.43

**No longer in force (ended 1995-11-08).**

## 1—15. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c581881]

## 16. Amendment of 60 Geo. 3 & 1 Geo. 4. c. 1.

The power under the Unlawful Drilling Act 1819, of authorising meetings and assemblies of persons for the purpose of training and drilling themselves, or of being trained and drilled to the use of arms, or for the purpose of practising military exercises, manoeuvres, or evolutions, shall be exerciseable by a Secretary of State, or any officer deputed by him for the purpose, instead of by the lieutenant or two justices of the peace of a county.
