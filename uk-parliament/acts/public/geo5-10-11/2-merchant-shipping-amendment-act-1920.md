# Merchant Shipping (Amendment) Act 1920

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1996-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Merchant Shipping (Amendment) Act 1920 [^c581657](repealed 1.1.1996)

1920 c.2

**No longer in force (ended 1996-01-01).**

## 1. Amendment of s. 659 of 57 & 58 Vict. c. 60.

The power of the Secretary of State under section 659 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894 to determine[^c581660] the annual or other sums to be paid out of the General Lighthouse Fund in respect of the establishment of the general lighthouse authorities, shall extend to determining[^c581661] the annual or other sums to be paid out of that fund to members of the general lighthouse authority for England and Wales, and the sums so determined shall have effect notwithstanding anything in any Act limiting the amount thereof.

## 2. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Amendment) Act 1920, and shall be included amongst the Acts which may be cited together as the Merchant Shipping Acts 1894 to 1920.
