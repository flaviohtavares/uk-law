Customs and Inland Revenue Act 1883

1883 c.10

[^M_F_1b2fe6dd-a881-468d-f3db-5de591d41b6e]An Act to grant certain Duties of Customs and Inland Revenue, to alter other Duties, and to amend the Laws relating to Customs and Inland Revenue.

\[31st May 1883\][^c570834][^c570835][^c570836]

## 1. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Customs and Inland Revenue Act, 1883.

### 4. Alteration of date of expiration of certain game licences.[^c570839]

Every licence or certificate to kill game now in force, or to be taken out under the [^c570840]Game Licences Act, 1860, which expires, or would expire, under the provisions of that Act on the fifth day of April, shall expire on the thirty-first day of July, and wherever in such provisions the fifth day of April is mentioned or referred to, the same shall be read as if the thirty-first day of July had been therein inserted in lieu of the said fifth day of April.

### 5. Game licences for short periods.[^c570841]

A licence or certificate to kill game may be taken out under the provisions of the Game Licences Act, 1860, for a continuous period of fourteen days to be specified in such licence or certificate, and there shall be granted and paid thereon the duty of two pounds.[^c570842]
