## 14C. Restrictions on use of rebated biodiesel and bioblend[^key-80b4832c51fd5731529b9f93b6117e59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Rebated biodiesel or bioblend must not be—

- (a) used as fuel other than for an excepted machine[^key-1f491d515cf88a92eb87261f20ebe306],
- (b) used as an additive or extender in any substance other than a substance used as fuel for an excepted machine[^key-367825efa993cf3a5a3d353ca38078d5], [^key-5c4b2d9b0a89fd798d7bc1a150ce9b46]... or[^key-d5dfe5c15708218488bc98a417a1228e]
- (c)[^key-268c233fc7dbe361de7b76ac046ac1a2] taken into the fuel supply of any engine that is not the engine of an excepted machine as fuel or as an additive or extender in any substance used as fuel, or
- (d)[^key-4253f5c5100e5489885e458ce4244fff] (in the case of rebated biodiesel) used as fuel for propelling private pleasure craft or as an additive or extender in any substance so used. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4253f5c5100e5489885e458ce4244fff]

(2) Rebated bioblend that was produced by mixing kerosene and biodiesel must not be—

- (a) used as mentioned in[^key-9355804c40fea1dd40baaa1a6b63dcfc] section 13AA(1),
- (b) used as an additive or extender in any substance so used, or
- (c) taken into the fuel supply of an engine used as mentioned in section 13AA(1)[^key-4e6b205fdee17f0d84981f40285221b6].

(3) Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to a quantity of biodiesel or bioblend if the amount specified in subsection (4) has been paid to the Commissioners, in accordance with regulations, in respect of it.

Q is the quantity (in litres) of the biodiesel or bioblend, and

R is the rate of the rebate under section 14A or 14B at the time of payment.
