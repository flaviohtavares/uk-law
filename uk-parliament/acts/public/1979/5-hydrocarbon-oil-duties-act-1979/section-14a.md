## 14A. Rebate on biodiesel used as fuel for excepted machines[^c-80b4832c51fd5731529b9f93b6117e59][^c-f5103999bc8718cc5edc8c85706009f8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if, at the excise duty point, it is intended that biodiesel on which duty under section 6AA is charged will not be—

- (a) used as fuel other than for an excepted machine[^c-e0dff204f098b155d1618db1f298d80e], [^c-3347a49c578aceae024dbda7c30c8349]...
- (aa)[^c-2e0f97284cb87fd76ace04fe8d9c1d1a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) used as an additive or extender in any substance other than a substance used as fuel for an excepted machine[^c-5bb7f0f5ad11ee1eaa2082efe34f01f0].

(2) A rebate of duty is to be allowed on the biodiesel at a rate of £0.1114[^c-c9ae5ebe41dd958ea9620e7b88eee33a] a litre less than the rate of duty under section 6AA.

(3) In this section “**the excise duty point**” has the same meaning as in section 1 of the Finance (No.2) Act 1992.
