## 27. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act—

- “[^key-ce7ac1ff71735423a5571601df7525cf]**aqua methanol**” has the meaning given by section 2AC above;
- “aviation gasoline” has the meaning given by[^c11250881] section 1(3D)[^key-d252136f979092bd902b5b44e59a4c59];
- “[^c11250891]**bioblend**” has the meaning given by section 6AB(2) above;
- “[^c11250901]**biodiesel**” has the meaning given by section 2AA above;
- “[^key-1178275bcd0e813e90ae9bb00f8bf487]**bioethanol**” has the meaning given by section 2AB above;
- “**bioethanol blend**” has the meaning given by section 6AE(2) above;
- “[^c11250911]**controlled oil**” means hydrocarbon oil in respect of which a rebate has been allowed under section 11(1)(b), (ba) or (c) or 13AA or biodiesel or bioblend in respect of which a rebate has been allowed under section 14A or 14B[^key-ef89a29869e84fda7e331fc89dd69bc6];
- “[^key-ec09fb08775f5b4e3af6a107b2c85bbf]**excepted machine**” means a vehicle, vessel, machine or appliance that is of a description given in Schedule 1A;
- [^key-9c8bf423970703aa704f334b1e1605c8]...
- “[^c11250921]**gas oil**” has the meaning given by section 1(5) above;
- “**heavy oil**” has the meaning given by section 1(4) above;
- [^c11250931]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**hydrocarbon oil**” has the meaning given by section 1(2) above;
- “[^key-1459cde957cdc8d4351184eb8f172818]**kerosene**” has the meaning given by section 1(8);
- “**light oil**” has the meaning given by section 1(3) above;
- “**the Management Act**” means the [^c11250941]Customs and Excise Management Act 1979;
- [^c11250951]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c11250951]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**the prescribed sum**”, in relation to the penalty provided for an offence, means—
  - (a) if the offence was committed in England or Wales[^c11250971], the prescribed sum within the meaning of section 32 of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 (£1,000 or other sum substituted by order under section 143(1) of that Act)[^c11250981];
  - (b) if the offence was committed in Scotland, the prescribed sum within the meaning of subsection (8) of section 225 of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995 (£5,000 or other sum substituted by order under subsection (4) of that section)[^c11250991];
  - (c) if the offence was committed in Northern Ireland, the prescribed sum within the meaning of Article 4 of the Fines and Penalties (Northern Ireland) Order 1984 (£1,000 or other sum substituted by order under Article 17 of that Order);[^c11251001]

- “private pleasure craft” has the meaning given by section 14E;[^key-50e3ff3a623ef99a596c23df14303155]
- “**rebate**” means rebate of duty under section 11 , 13ZA,[^key-5f678460854e39a97c5f600111bb4017] 13AA,[^c11251011] [^key-d5af7210126279199926dbc74dd51131]... 14[^c11251031], 14A, 14B[^key-12e012753a7b146f23e9709eef98558d] or 20AB above, and “**rebated**” has a corresponding meaning;
- “[^c11251041]**refinery**” means any premises which—
  - (a) are approved by the Commissioners for the treatment of hydrocarbon oil; or
  - (b) are approved by them for the production of energy for use in the treatment of hydrocarbon oil at premises approved under paragraph (a) above or in the production of hydrocarbon oil at other premises used for the production of such oil;

  and the Commissioners may approve any premises under paragraph (b) above if it appears to them that more than one-third of the energy will be produced for such use as is mentioned in that paragraph;

- “**road fuel gas**” has the meaning given by section 5 above; [^c11251051]...
- “**road vehicle**” means a vehicle constructed or adapted for use on roads, but does not include any vehicle that is an excepted machine[^key-b0c01b4ea59f2d506fc69dd931011a83]
- [^key-3bcf250e5a64e43b0e4d528f3dc2beb6]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

- [^key-3bcf250e5a64e43b0e4d528f3dc2beb6]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “[^c11251081]**unleaded petrol**” has[^key-31cd7ace9a134426233639be2f35c1a2] the meaning given by section 1(3C) above.[^key-399c49be14bc64ac5c93dbb48a2c05ef]

(1ZA)[^key-575b0e608ce8c6703bd48cec5b83acf6] For the purposes of this Act, a substance is used as fuel for a vehicle , vessel, machine or appliance[^key-8d54a52428dcc430f930cd29902193b4] if (and only if) it is used as fuel for—

- (a) the engine provided for propelling the vehicle or vessel, or, as the case may be, for powering the machine or appliance[^key-93b0d19d9f276ebc4b44e9d8a3ed7bd3],
- (aa)[^key-05f4335d90b22ecace16c9bdbcf95b2b] in relation to an appliance that contains a furnace or boiler for use in a heating system, that furnace or boiler, or
- (b)[^key-5ebad05f29322c0cfe575e9d209f9f07] an engine, furnace or boiler which draws fuel from the same supply as an engine or, as the case may be, furnace or boiler, within paragraph (a) or (aa).

(1ZB) For those purposes, a substance is taken into a vehicle , vessel, machine or appliance[^key-74bbf8321baaaef095fdb9937cbc66fd] as fuel, or as an additive or extender in any fuel, if (and only if) it is taken into the vehicle , vessel, machine or appliance[^key-93c9c7881f9282de90f7ae666ea15537] as part of the supply—

- (a)[^key-8d16cf7613186039c62b49e5a4c92831] from which the engine provided for propelling the vehicle or vessel or, as the case may be, for powering the machine or appliance,[^key-91804c2a9652528bb78f196aa36c41cd] draws fuel , or[^key-744608832560c4b86de7c51c4f6d0501]
- (b) in relation to an appliance that contains a furnace or boiler for use in a heating system, from which the furnace or boiler draws fuel.

(1ZC) For those purposes, the following persons are liable for a substance being taken into a vehicle , vessel, machine or appliance,[^key-7da2c83388bc5f43bbd8b62709c0bb58] or into the fuel supply of an engine , furnace or boiler[^key-c3e54be69e2b0bc82e2026a9ad8ae09f]—

- (a) the person who has charge of the vehicle , vessel, machine or appliance, or of the engine, furnace or boiler[^key-f4d03fed3e4b71a5c7500510ec405aa9] at the time the substance is taken in, and
- (b) the owner of the vehicle , vessel, machine or appliance, or of the engine, furnace or boiler[^key-a6822b44c8d9bb1610f1a71188384edb] at that time (or, if another person is entitled to possession of it at that time, that other person).

(1ZD) Subsection (1ZC) applies in relation to [^key-ed0fab309981f30eaeb726e9d42293a1]... storage tanks as it applies in relation to vehicles , vessels, machines or appliances[^key-35e8ca5db581ff0e121101769370d082].

(1A)[^c11251101] If in the case of any premises which the Commissioners can approve under paragraph (b) of the definition of ’’refinery’’ in subsection (1) above it appears to them appropriate to do so, they may direct that the provisions of this Act (other than that definition) shall apply to them as if, instead of being a refinery, they were other premises used for the production of hydrocarbon oil.

(1B)[^key-ea927953f867788e0383e89b397df895] The Treasury may by order made by statutory instrument amend Schedule 1A[^key-90e7f006f612ff9938afd5fa0f3bb637] to this Act so as to—

- (a) add a class of excepted machine[^key-7d76c0a66aa9b01e4c18461d1562fe58],
- (b) remove a class of excepted machine[^key-176c8bfeffe5f45119542b526eaadd73], or
- (c) redefine a class of excepted machine[^key-c11cb72a8ab3651b06c21b5be39af760].

(1C) Section 2A(2) and (3) above shall apply to an order under subsection (1B).

(2) This Act and the other Acts included in the Customs and Excise Acts 1979 shall be construed as one Act but where a provision of this Act refers to this Act that reference is not to be construed as including a reference to any of the others.

(3) Any expression used in this Act or in any instrument made under this Act to which a meaning is given by any other Act included in the Customs and Excise Acts 1979 has, except where the context otherwise requires, the same meaning in this Act or in any such instrument as in that Act; and for ease of reference the Table below indicates the expressions used in this Act to which a meaning is given by any other such Act—
