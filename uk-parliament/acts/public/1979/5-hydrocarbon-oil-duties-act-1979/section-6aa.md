## 6AA. Excise duty on biodiesel[^c-45697d881fa8112cf7e772e695a9a15f][^c11242991]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A duty of excise shall be charged on the setting aside for a chargeable use by any person, or (where it has not already been charged under this section) on the chargeable use by any person, of biodiesel.

(2) In subsection (1) “**chargeable use**” means use—

- (a) as fuel for any engine, motor or other machinery, [^c-35da957ce6bbd533d306cc24f6d664db]...
- (aa)[^c-409c6a955094ff86107f1069c05db6ca] for heating,
- (b) as an additive or extender in any substance used as mentioned in paragraph (a) or (aa)[^c-05155298322865b5e39a3df25abf106d].
- (c)[^c-9634b03a7c5f4214258ecf7eb72d27f7] for the production of bioblend.

(3) The rate of duty under this section is the same as that in the case of heavy oil[^c-b1f77de01817bf910bc50c742856de45].

(4)[^c-455239db0829d057b14e76d54c03ffb0] See section 14A (biodiesel used other than as fuel for road vehicles) for rebates on duty charged under this section.
