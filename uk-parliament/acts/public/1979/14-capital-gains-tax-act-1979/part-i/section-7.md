# Capital Gains Tax Act 1979, section 7

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Time for payment of tax.[^c1110481]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1992-03-06).**

Capital gains tax assessed on any person in respect of gains accruing in any year shall be payable by that person on or before 1st December following the end of that year[^c1110482], or at the expiration of a period of thirty days beginning with the date of the issue of the notice of assessment, whichever is the later.
