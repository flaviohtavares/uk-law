# Capital Gains Tax Act 1979, section 53

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Gifts in settlement.[^c1110644]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1992-03-06).**

A transfer into[^c1110645] settlement, whether revocable or irrevocable, is a disposal of the entire property thereby becoming settled property notwithstanding that the transferor[^c1110646] has some interest as a beneficiary under the settlement and notwithstanding that he is a trustee, or the sole trustee, of the settlement.
