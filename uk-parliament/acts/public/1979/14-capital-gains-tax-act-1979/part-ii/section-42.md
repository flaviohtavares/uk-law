# Capital Gains Tax Act 1979, section 42

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Expenditure reimbursed out of public money.[^c1110617]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1992-03-06).**

There shall be excluded from the computation under this Chapter any expenditure which has been or is to be met directly or indirectly by the Crown or by any Government, public or local authority whether in the United Kingdom or elsewhere.
