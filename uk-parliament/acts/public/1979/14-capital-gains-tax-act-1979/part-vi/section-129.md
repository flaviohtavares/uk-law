# Capital Gains Tax Act 1979, section 129

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Leases of property other than land.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1992-03-06).**

Schedule 3 to this Act has effect, to the extent specified in paragraph 9 of that Schedule, as respects leases of property other than land.
