# Capital Gains Tax Act 1979, section 76

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Consideration for acquisition of shares under share option schemes.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1992-03-06).**

Section 19(3) above (assets deemed acquired and disposed of at market value) shall not apply in calculating the consideration for the acquisition of shares in pursuance of a share option scheme as defined in Schedule 12 to the [^c1110707]Finance Act 1972.
