# Capital Gains Tax Act 1979, section 91

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Application of Chapter II to quoted options.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1992-03-06).**

The preceding provisions of this Chapter have effect subject to section 139 below (quoted option to be regarded for the purposes of this Chapter as the shares which could be acquired by exercising the option).
