# Justices of the Peace Act 1979, section 72

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 72. Short title, commencement and extent.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-06-19).**

(1) This Act may be cited as the Justices of the Peace Act 1979.

(2) This Act shall come into force at the end of the period of three months beginning with the day on which it is passed.

(3) This Act shall not extend to Scotland or to Northern Ireland.
