# Justices of the Peace Act 1979, section 63A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 63A. Disqualification in case of bankruptcy.[^c1128481]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-06-19).**

(1) A person who is adjudged bankrupt shall be disqualified for being appointed or acting as a justice of the peace.

(2) Where a person is disqualified under this section, the disqualification shall cease—

- (a) on his discharge from bankruptcy; or
- (b) if the bankruptcy order is previously annulled, on the date of its annulment.
