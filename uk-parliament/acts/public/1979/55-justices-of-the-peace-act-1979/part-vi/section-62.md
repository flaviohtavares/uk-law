# Justices of the Peace Act 1979, section 62

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Defaults of justices’ clerks and their staffs.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-06-19).**

(1) The Lord Chancellor[^c1128472] may, if he thinks fit, pay to any person any money due to that person which he has not received because of the default of a justices’ clerk or of any staff of a magistrates’ courts committee[^c1128473].

(2) In this section “**justices’ clerk**” has the same meaning as in section 61 of this Act.
