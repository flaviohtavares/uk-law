# Justices of the Peace Act 1979, section 42

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 42. No petty sessional divisions in the City.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-06-19).**

Nothing in this Act[^c1128225] shall authorise the making of an order under section 23 of this Act for the division of the City into petty sessional divisions.
