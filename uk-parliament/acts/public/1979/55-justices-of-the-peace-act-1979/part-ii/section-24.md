# Justices of the Peace Act 1979, section 24

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Procedure relating to s. 23.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-06-19).**

(1) Before submitting to the Lord Chancellor[^c1128016] a draft order or a report under section 23 of this Act about any area, a magistrates’ courts committee—

- (a) shall consult the council of every[^c1128017] non-metropolitan county unitary district[^c1128019][^c1128018], county borough in Wales[^c1128020] or [^c1128021]. . . London borough which includes all or part of the area[^c1128022] and the magistrates for any existing petty sessional division in the area; and
- (b) in the case of a draft order concerning a non-metropolitan county[^c1128023] in England[^c1128024], after complying with paragraph (a) above, shall send a copy of their proposals to every interested authority and take into consideration any objections made in the prescribed manner and within the prescribed time.

(2) A magistrates’ courts committee submitting to the Lord Chancellor a draft order or a report under section 23 of this Act shall comply with such requirements (if any) as to notice as may be prescribed; and the Lord Chancellor, before making an order under that section about any area otherwise than in accordance with a draft submitted to him by the magistrates’ courts committee, shall send a copy of his proposals to the committee, to the council of every[^c1128027] non-metropolitan county ,[^c1128028] unitary district, county borough in Wales or [^c1128031]. . . London borough which includes all or part of the area[^c1128032], to the magistrates for any existing petty sessional division in the area and, if a non-metropolitan county in England is concerned, to every interested authority.

(3) Before making any order under section 23 of this Act the Lord Chancellor shall take into consideration any objections made in the prescribed manner and within the prescribed time, and may cause a local inquiry to be held.

(4) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c1128035]

(5) Subject to the provisions of Schedule 1 to this Act, the powers conferred by section 23 of this Act shall be in substitution for any other power to create or alter petty sessional divisions [^c1128036]. . ., except powers conferred by any other provision of this Act or by section 69 of the Police and Magistrates’ Courts Act 1994[^c1128037].

(6) For the purposes of this section—

- (a) “**interested authority**”, in relation to any order or draft order concerning a non-metropolitan county[^c1128038] in England, means the council of any district in the county (other than a unitary district)[^c1128040] which is wholly or partly included in the area to which the order or draft order relates; and
- (b) an order shall be deemed to be made in accordance with a draft order if either it is made in terms of the draft order or the departures from the draft order do not, in the opinion of the Lord Chancellor, effect important alterations in the draft order.
