# Justices of the Peace Act 1979, section 1

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Commission areas.[^c1127804]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-06-19).**

Subject to any order made under section 17 of the Local Government Act 1992,[^c1127805]or section 55 of the Local Government (Wales) Act 1994[^c1127806] there shall in England and Wales be a commission of the peace for the following areas [^c1127807]. . ., that is to say—

- (a)[^c1127808] every metropolitan county or relevant area in England;
- (aa)[^c1127809] every preserved county in Wales;
- (b) every London commission area; and
- (c) the City of London.

and in this Act “commission area” means an area for which there is a commission of the peace.[^c1127810]
