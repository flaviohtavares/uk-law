# Justices of the Peace Act 1979, section 5

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. General form of commissions of the peace.[^c1127818]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-06-19).**

(1) The commission of the peace for any commission area shall be a commission under the Great Seal addressed generally, and not by name, to all such persons as may from time to time hold office as justices of the peace for the commission area.

(2) A commission of the peace issued after the commencement of this Act shall be framed so as to take account of the abolition of courts of quarter sessions by section 3 of the [^c1127819]Courts Act 1971.
