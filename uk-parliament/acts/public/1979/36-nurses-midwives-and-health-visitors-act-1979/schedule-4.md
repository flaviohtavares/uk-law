# Nurses, Midwives and Health Visitors Act 1979, SCHEDULE 4

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4[^c12386731]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-06-19).**

### 1.[^c12386741]

**No longer in force (ended 1993-03-29).**

A person is qualified for the purposes of section 20(2) if he is a member of one of the recognised professional bodies.

### 2.[^c12386751]

**No longer in force (ended 1993-03-29).**

Those bodies are—

- the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales;
- the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Scotland;
- the Association of Certified Accountants; and
- the Institute of Chartered Accountants in Ireland.

### 3.[^c12386771]

**No longer in force (ended 1993-03-29).**

A person is also qualified if he is a member of a body of accountants established in the United Kingdom and recognised by the Secretary of State for the purposes of section 389(1)([^c12386781]*a*) of the Companies Act 1985

### 4.[^c12386791]

**No longer in force (ended 1993-03-29).**

A Scottish firm is qualified if each of the partners in it is so.
