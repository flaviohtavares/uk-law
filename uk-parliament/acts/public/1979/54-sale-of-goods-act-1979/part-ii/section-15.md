# Sale of Goods Act 1979, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2015-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Sale by sample.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A contract of sale is a contract for sale by sample where there is an express or implied term to that effect in the contract.

(2) In the case of a contract for sale by sample there is an implied term[^c1108911]—

- (a) that the bulk will correspond with the sample in quality;
- (b)[^c1108912] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) that the goods will be free from any defect, making their quality unsatisfactory[^c1108913], which would not be apparent on reasonable examination of the sample.

(3)[^c1108914] As regards England and Wales and Northern Ireland, the term implied by subsection (2) above is a condition.

(4) Paragraph 7 of Schedule 1 below applies in relation to a contract made before 18 May 1973.

(5)[^key-1c299eca68949cd9fbdca827321ebb3b] This section does not apply to a contract to which Chapter 2 of Part 1 of the Consumer Rights Act 2015 applies (but see the provision made about such contracts in sections 13 and 18 of that Act).
