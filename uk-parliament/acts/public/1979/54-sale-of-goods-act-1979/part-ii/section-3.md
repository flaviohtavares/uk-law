# Sale of Goods Act 1979, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2015-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Capacity to buy and sell.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Capacity to buy and sell is regulated by the general law concerning capacity to contract and to transfer and acquire property.

(2) Where necessaries are sold and delivered to a minor or[^c1108884] to a person who by reason of mental incapacity or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c1886084] drunkenness is incompetent to contract, he must pay a reasonable price for them.

(3) In subsection (2) above “**necessaries**” means goods suitable to the condition in life of the minor or other person concerned and to his actual requirements at the time of the sale and delivery.
