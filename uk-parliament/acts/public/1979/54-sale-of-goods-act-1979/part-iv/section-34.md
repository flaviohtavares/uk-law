# Sale of Goods Act 1979, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2015-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Buyer’s right of examining the goods.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-b2dbc890d7f00cb419b7efd9e0a9d269] [^c1108931]. . .

Unless otherwise agreed, when the seller tenders delivery of goods to the buyer, he is bound on request to afford the buyer a reasonable opportunity of examining the goods for the purpose of ascertaining whether they are in conformity with the contract and, in the case of a contract for sale by sample, of comparing the bulk with the sample.[^c1108932]

(2)[^key-364e2ccb14a64da068076d6d703513ac] Nothing in this section affects the operation of section 22 (time limit for short-term right to reject) of the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
