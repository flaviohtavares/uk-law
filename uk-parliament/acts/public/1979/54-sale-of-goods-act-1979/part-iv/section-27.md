# Sale of Goods Act 1979, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2015-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Duties of seller and buyer.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It is the duty of the seller to deliver the goods, and of the buyer to accept and pay for them, in accordance with the terms of the contract of sale.
