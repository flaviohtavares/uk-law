# Sale of Goods Act 1979, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2015-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Goods must be ascertained.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to section 20A below[^c1108918] Where there is a contract for the sale of unascertained goods no property in the goods is transferred to the buyer unless and until the goods are ascertained.
