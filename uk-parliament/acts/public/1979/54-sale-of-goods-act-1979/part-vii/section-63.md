*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Without prejudice to section 17 of the [^c1108988]Interpretation Act 1978 (repeal and re-enactment), the enactments mentioned in Schedule 2 below have effect subject to the amendments there specified (being amendments consequential on this Act).
