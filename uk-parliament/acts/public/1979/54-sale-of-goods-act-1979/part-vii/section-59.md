# Sale of Goods Act 1979, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2015-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Reasonable time a question of fact.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a reference is made in this Act to a reasonable time the question what is a reasonable time is a question of fact.
