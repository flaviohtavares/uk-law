# Sale of Goods Act 1979, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2015-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Rights etc. enforceable by action.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a right, duty or liability is declared by this Act, it may (unless otherwise provided by this Act) be enforced by action.
