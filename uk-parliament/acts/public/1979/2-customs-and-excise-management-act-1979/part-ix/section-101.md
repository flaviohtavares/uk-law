# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Excise licences.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An excise licence shall be in such form and contain such particulars as the Commissioners may direct and, subject to the provisions of any enactment relating to the licence or trade in question, may be granted by the proper officer on payment of any appropriate duty[^c14777381].

(2) An excise licence for the carrying on of a trade shall be granted in respect of one set of premises only, but a licence for the same trade may be granted to the same person in respect of each of two or more sets of premises.

(3) Where an excise licence trade is carried on at any set of premises by two or more persons in partnership, then, subject to the provisions of any enactment relating to the licence or trade in question, not more than one licence shall be required to be held[^c14777391] by those persons in respect of those premises at any one time[^c14777401].

(4) Without prejudice to any other requirement as to the production of licences contained in the Customs and Excise Acts 1979 or made by regulations under section 45 of the Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018[^c-06dd51fc320d87ccd0181b7fc49b92ce], if any person who is the holder of an excise licence to carry on any trade or to manufacture or sell any goods fails to produce his licence for examination within one month[^c14777411] after being so requested by an officer his failure shall attract a penalty under section 9 of the Finance Act 1994 (civil penalties)..
