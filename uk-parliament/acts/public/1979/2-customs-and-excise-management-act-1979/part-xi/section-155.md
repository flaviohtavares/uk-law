(1) An officer of Revenue and Customs or other person authorised by the Commissioners may conduct criminal proceedings relating to an assigned matter before a court of summary jurisdiction in Scotland or Northern Ireland.[^key-c801dc70b6ad53ee5c2eaf57d87bf225] .

(2) Any person who has been admitted as a solicitor and is employed by the Commissioners may act as a solicitor in any proceedings in England, Wales or Northern Ireland relating to any assigned matter notwithstanding that he does not hold a current practising certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d103d2dfee7cd55fbada31f324b4ef9c]
