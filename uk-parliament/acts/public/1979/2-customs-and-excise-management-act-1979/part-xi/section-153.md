## 153. Proof of certain documents.[^c14781021][^c14781031][^c14781041][^key-1ad2b17938e8c60673b326923a733d5f][^key-790f937949963d7dd43c78938ed6dab1][^key-f7253a1d5ff88a38233e150be2ca6ded][^key-0eef77f51e14fd576db7517f3cc9e247][^key-77f1e7334b871373a56930419ebe9da9][^key-0e300ea821ab8c4c5bdd5b2d79eaf99b][^key-bc997b26180d62fec09ce4d742d25fe6][^key-66d0fe36d867bf7c2d92c9d16bad4c7b][^key-87f61aaa426db7e7472f3e0b6f0a0657][^key-e65f9eb2c07cb79e8c47ec3f15dd49e1][^key-b302c1340d1be17b3ff448c76e1cc6a3][^key-d34434c290cdd6f61f98bae59ea125ed][^key-e6d112ffc8fc528add79d888bdfcb388][^key-b1ae40b1f27f0ec68fe7913834d66e00][^key-f8db2f589ca495d34838df9c64747807][^key-3ea479177f3f122ede413712f4e5fac8][^key-1fdf115dbc22eb3328c5f7d11e17f305][^key-d356bb6af561f63b388e92af1c4d3f65][^key-844c69e68c687c4f64a113cc4c012b17][^c14781011]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-a3c6b24d87c95a0739a46582e53e04b1] Any document purporting to be signed either by one or more of the Commissioners, or by their order, or by any other person with their authority, shall, until the contrary is proved, be deemed to have been so signed and to be made and issued by the Commissioners, and may be proved by the production of a copy thereof purporting to be so signed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a3c6b24d87c95a0739a46582e53e04b1]

(2) Without prejudice to subsection (1) above, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a3c6b24d87c95a0739a46582e53e04b1] [^c14781051]Documentary Evidence Act 1868 shall apply in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a3c6b24d87c95a0739a46582e53e04b1]

- (a) any document issued by the Commissioners; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a3c6b24d87c95a0739a46582e53e04b1]
- (b) any document issued before 1st April 1909, by the Commissioners of Customs or the Commissioners of Customs and the Commissioners of Inland Revenue jointly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a3c6b24d87c95a0739a46582e53e04b1]
- (c) any document issued before that date in relation to the revenue of excise by the Commissioners of Inland Revenue, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a3c6b24d87c95a0739a46582e53e04b1]

as it applies in relation to the documents mentioned in that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a3c6b24d87c95a0739a46582e53e04b1]

(3) That Act shall, as applied by subsection (2) above, have effect as if the persons mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (c) of that subsection were included in the first column of the Schedule to that Act, and any of the Commissioners or any secretary or assistant secretary to the Commissioners were specified in the second column of that Schedule in connection with those persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a3c6b24d87c95a0739a46582e53e04b1]

(4)[^c14781061] A photograph of any document delivered to the Commissioners for any customs or excise purpose and certified by them to be such a photograph shall be admissible in any proceedings, whether civil or criminal, to the same extent as the document itself. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a3c6b24d87c95a0739a46582e53e04b1]
