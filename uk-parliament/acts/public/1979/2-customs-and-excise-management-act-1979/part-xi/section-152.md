The Commissioners may, as they see fit—

- (a)[^key-bc8b124ef931b688a148f56c8cbda75d][^key-7ffab135d3b2efb817844b26148a7e3b][^key-721061e1a9fa341afb45edb2f36cba50] [^key-8c201981b5f85338f2a9fb6ee2fd03da]...compound an offence (whether or not proceedings have been instituted in respect of it) and compound proceedings[^key-06ef5ae4ea5ac49f255e54470c89c876] or for the condemnation of any thing as being forfeited under the customs and excise Acts; or
- (b)[^key-bc8b124ef931b688a148f56c8cbda75d][^key-7ffab135d3b2efb817844b26148a7e3b][^key-721061e1a9fa341afb45edb2f36cba50] restore, subject to such conditions (if any) as they think proper, any thing forfeited or seized under those Acts; or
- (c) after judgment, mitigate or remit any pecuniary penalty imposed under those Acts; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9c8fcb255a0ac37371538faa684fa681]
- (d) order any person who has been imprisoned to be discharged before the expiration of his term of imprisonment, being a person imprisoned for any offence under those Acts or in respect of the non-payment of a penalty or other sum adjudged to be paid or awarded in relation to such an offence or in respect of the default of a sufficient distress to satisfy such a sum; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3a7ebd790bb4162b2024c6995b43c039]

but paragraph (a) above shall not apply to proceedings on indictment in Scotland.
