# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, section 27

## 27. Officers’ powers of boarding.[^c14771341][^c-e69d5ca294a7619853699dce45b84254][^c-b5eae6671c327f3e1d2475a2cd24ed23][^c14771331]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) At any time while a ship is within the limits of a port, or an aircraft is at an aerodrome[^c14771351], or any other vehicle[^c-6ca5b96b00774a81e3b36a0dccd0bdcd][^c14771361] is—

- (a) entering, leaving or about to leave the United Kingdom,
- (b) within the prescribed area,
- (ba)[^c-a9cf24280df8f503e50d51d86781251d] within a railway customs area,
- (c) within the limits of or entering or leaving a port or any land adjacent to a port and occupied wholly or mainly for the purpose of activities carried on at the port,
- (d) at, entering or leaving an aerodrome,
- (e) at, entering or leaving an approved wharf, temporary storage facility,[^c-fcacbba7840c421fa792456e6942a4b1] customs warehouse or free zone, or
- (f) at, entering or leaving any such premises as are mentioned in subsection (1) of section 112 below,

any officer and any other person duly engaged in the prevention of smuggling may board the vehicle[^c-3df3136272bd9379710c4307e89dc086] and remain therein and rummage and search any part thereof.

(1A)[^c-67a224491cb769427c9a4c9a5a02aaad] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The Commissioners may station officers in any ship at any time while it is within the limits of a port, and if the master of any ship neglects or refuses to provide—

- (a) reasonable accommodation below decks for any officer stationed therein; or
- (b) means of safe access to and egress from the ship in accordance with the requirements of any such officer,

the master shall be liable on summary conviction to a penalty of level 2 on the standard scale[^c14771391].
