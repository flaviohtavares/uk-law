# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, section 137A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 137A. Recovery of overpaid excise duty.[^c14779761][^c14779751]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-52f079ad13e21e2fe7b3bfb87b4232f5][^c14779771] Where a person pays to the Commissioners an amount by way of excise duty which is not due to them, the Commissioners are liable to repay that amount.

(2) The Commissioners shall not be required to make any such repayment unless a claim is made to them in such form, and supported by such documentary evidence, as may be prescribed by them by regulations; and regulations under this subsection may make different provision for different cases.

(3)[^c14779781] It is a defence to a claim for repayment that the repayment would unjustly enrich the claimant.

(4)[^c14779791] The Commissioners shall not be liable, on a claim made under this section, to repay any amount paid to them more than 4 years[^key-fd4aa4b51355920b6727fb86fcc0a410] before the making of the claim.

(5) Except as provided by this section the Commissioners are not liable to repay an amount paid to them by way of excise duty by reason of the fact that it was not due to them.

(6)[^c14779801] This section does not apply in a case where the Commissioners are—

- (a) entitled to pay an amount under Part 1 of Schedule 3 to the Finance Act 2001, or
- (b) required to repay an amount under Part 3 of that Schedule.
