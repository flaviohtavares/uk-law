# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/2
>

## 90. Forfeiture of ship , railway vehicle or aircraft unable to account for missing cargo.[^key-f0bd4cf2182ed0794d4f58bab1222255]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a ship has been within the limits of any port in the United Kingdom or the Isle of Man[^c14775511], a railway vehicle has been within the limits of a railway customs area[^key-fe9a70ddf02e08998954dc7227011d83] or an aircraft has been in the United Kingdom or the Isle of Man, with a cargo on board and a substantial part of that cargo is afterwards found in the United Kingdom to be missing, then, if the vehicle operator[^key-06bdd7283179cbf9652770ba609204e2] fails to account therefor to the satisfaction of the Commissioners, the ship, railway vehicle or aircraft[^key-a0fede711e7f67964d99fcd82f976450] shall be liable to forfeiture.
