# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, section 75

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Explosives.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c14774661] No goods which are explosives within the meaning of the Manufacture and Storage of Explosives Regulations 2005 *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-222a60d6c18ab2702e6f826e8bae8884][^key-500b1f79e8f6544229874549f3d8b4d8] the Explosives Regulations 2014 *(Extent: E+W+S)* shall be loaded into any ship or aircraft for exportation, exported by land or shipped for carriage coastwise as cargo, until due entry has been made of the goods in such form and manner and containing such particulars as the Commissioners may direct.

(2) Without prejudice to sections 53 and 60 above, any goods required to be entered under this section which are loaded, exported or shipped as mentioned in subsection (1) above without being entered under this section shall be liable to forfeiture, and the exporter or, as the case may be, shipper shall be liable on summary conviction to a penalty of level 3 on the standard scale[^c14774681].
