# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Forfeiture of ship, aircraft or vehicle constructed, etc. for concealing goods.[^c14775471]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where—

- (a) a ship is or has been in United Kingdom waters[^c14775481]; or
- (b) an aircraft is or has been at any place, whether on land or on water, in the United Kingdom; or
- (c) any other vehicle[^c-89a152ee4aab148ac7bf43c3fd0615a0] is or has been within the limits of any port, railway customs area or aerodrome[^c-64e8499ade4f3a9cdd900bb3f15ad79d] or, while in Northern Ireland, within the prescribed area,

while constructed, adapted, altered or fitted in any manner for the purpose of concealing goods, that ship, aircraft or other vehicle[^c-60c79ec53bbd572bea32ca8c586abdc5] shall be liable to forfeiture.
