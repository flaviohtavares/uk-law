# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, section 59

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Restrictions on putting export goods alongside for loading.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) This section applies to all goods which are required to be exported in accordance with the applicable export provisions[^key-ad937690420e18708b22f8b7f4ab9cf9].

(2) The Commissioners may make regulations—

- (a) prohibiting, as from such date as is specified in the regulations, the putting of any goods to which this section applies alongside any vehicle other than a road vehicle[^key-044db99562d260f0ae7ae0320ccd6d21] for loading for exportation, except under a written authority in that behalf obtained in accordance with, and in such form as is specified in, the regulations; and
- (b) requiring any person putting goods alongside a vehicle other than a road vehicle[^key-a30e0b0edb75535e7d4e8f9a86d2b821] under one or more such authorities to endorse the authority or each of the authorities with such particulars as are specified in the regulations, and to deliver the endorsed authority or authorities, together with a written statement of the number of authorities delivered, to the proper officer within such period as is so specified.

(3) Regulations under subsection (2) above may make different provision for different circumstances.

(4) Without prejudice to section 3 above, subsection (2) above shall apply to the charging of goods into a pipe-line for exportation as it applies to the putting of goods alongside a ship or aircraft for loading for exportation.

(5) The Commissioners may relax any requirement imposed under subsection (2) above as they think fit in relation to any goods.

(6) Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with any regulation under subsection (2) above shall be liable on summary conviction to a penalty of level 3 on the standard scale[^c14773521].

(7)[^c14773531] This section shall not come into force until such day as the Commissioners may appoint by order made by statutory instrument.
