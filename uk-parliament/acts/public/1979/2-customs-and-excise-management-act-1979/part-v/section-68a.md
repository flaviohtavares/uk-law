# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, section 68A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/2

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68A. Offences in relation to agricultural levies.[^c14774511]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Without prejudice to section 11(1) of the Finance Act 1982, if any person is, in relation to any goods, in any way knowingly concerned in any fraudulent evasion or attempt at evasion of any agricultural levy chargeable on the export of the goods, he shall be guilty of an offence and may be detained.

(2)[^c14774521] A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a penalty of the prescribed sum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cab942a7d846126b39e91a0713a17a51] £20,000 or of three times the value of the goods, whichever is the greater, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months, or to both; or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a penalty of any amount, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14[^key-71ce93b21044e80b52ff6cfbc8e8565b] years, or to both.

(3) Any goods in respect of which an offence under this section is committed shall be liable to forfeiture.

(4) In this section “**agricultural levy**” has the same meaning as in section 6 of the European Communities Act 1972 and the provisions of this section apply notwithstanding that any such levy may be payable to the Secretary of State, the Scottish Ministers, the National Assembly for Wales or (in relation to Northern Ireland) the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, as the case may be[^c14774531].
