# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Information, documentation, etc. as to export goods.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commissioners may give directions under this subsection imposing on persons specified in the directions requirements as to the giving of information with respect to, or the furnishing of documents in connection with, goods exported, or intended to be exported, in any such vehicle or container as is specified in the directions, or by such other means, or in accordance with any such commercial procedure, as is so specified.

(2)[^c14773891] [^key-9ad3b12b325913c4e3f300001e960e49]...

This subsection shall not come into force until such day as the Commissioners may appoint by order made by statutory instrument.

(3) The Commissioners may relax any requirement imposed under subsection (1) [^key-1872057639a4c3a35cb99fd3f498ae63]... above as they think fit in relation to any goods.

(4) Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with any direction given under subsection (1) [^key-29e1274ad52569843edf973acc5868f2]... above shall be liable on summary conviction to a penalty of level 3 on the standard scale[^c14773901].
