# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, section 44

## 44. Exclusion of s. 43(1) for importers etc. keeping standing deposits.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the Commissioners so direct, section 43(1) above shall not apply if and so long as the importer or his agent pays to, and keeps deposited with, the Commissioners a sum by way of standing deposit sufficient in their opinion to cover any excise[^key-5a8b493891a59588b7c2e6448fb3575e] duty which may become payable in respect of goods entered by that importer or agent, and if the importer or agent complies with such other conditions as the Commissioners may impose.
