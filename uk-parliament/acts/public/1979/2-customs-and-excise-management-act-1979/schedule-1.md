# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, SCHEDULE 1

## SCHEDULE 1 — Controlled Drugs: Variation of Punishments for Certain Offences under this Act

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 50(5), 68(4) and 170(4).

**1.** Section 50(4), 68(3) and 170(3) of this Act shall have effect in a case where the goods in respect of which the offence referred to in that subsection was committed were a Class A drug , Class B drug or a temporary class drug[^key-e98d12a12f6321c0537a29e362123b7a] as if for the words from shall be liable onwards there were substituted the following words, that is to say—

> shall be liable—
>
> - (a) on summary conviction, to a penalty of the prescribed sum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d4b9b47b7688558f97e8700221930a6]£20,000 or of three times the value of the goods, whichever is the greater, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months, or to both;
> - (b)[^c14782521] on conviction on indictment—
>   - (i) where the goods were a Class A drug, to a penalty of any amount, or to imprisonment for life, or to both; and
>   - (ii) where they were a Class B drug or a temporary class drug[^key-a7b990ec8386eca8657bb1970c321723], to a penalty of any amount, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14 years, or to both.

.

**2.** Section 50(4), 68(3) and 170(3) of this Act shall have effect in a case where the goods in respect of which the offence referred to in that subsection was committed were a Class C drug as if for the words from shall be liable onwards there were substituted the following words, that is to say—

> shall be liable—
>
> - (a) on summary conviction [^c14782531], to a penalty of three times the value of the goods or £500, whichever is the greater, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months, or to both;
> - (b)
> - (c)[^c14782541] on conviction on indictment, to a penalty of any amount, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14 years[^key-f93ab6fc08f4171e30de1b3d4ccfd74f] , or to both.

.

**3.** In this Schedule Class A drug, Class B drug , “Class C drug” and “temporary class drug”[^M_F_3934b1f9-454b-461d-ade0-1ef03dc3a018] have the same meanings as in the [^c14782551]Misuse of Drugs Act 1971.
