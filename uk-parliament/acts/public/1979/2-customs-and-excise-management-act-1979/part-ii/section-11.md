# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/2

## 11. Assistance to be rendered by police, etc.[^c-eb70719e959544f075045438ba87ba8c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It shall be the duty of every constable and every member of Her Majesty’s armed forces or coastguard to assist in the enforcement of the law relating to any assigned matter.
