# Customs and Excise Management Act 1979, section 178

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 178. Citation and commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979.

(2) This Act, the [^c14782471]Customs and Excise Duties (General Reliefs) Act 1979, [^c-319dff24578b023bb0a794e586b12b50]... the [^c14782491]Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979 [^c14782501]... and the [^c14782511]Tobacco Products Duty Act 1979 may be cited together as the Customs and Excise Acts 1979.

(3) This Act shall come into operation on 1st April 1979.
