# Crown Agents Act 1979, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2008-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4[^c1108110]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

### 1.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Procuring movable property of any kind.

### 2.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Making arrangements for and in connection with the conveyance of movable property of any kind to any destination (including arrangements for its insurance and inspection).

### 3.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Obtaining professional advice on projects, and negotiating and making contracts for the preparation of projects and the carrying out of feasibility studies.

### 4.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Acquiring and disposing of land, doing anything in connection with any land, and arranging for anything to be done in connection with any land.

### 5.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Negotiating and making contracts for the design, production or supply of coins, medallions, currency notes, postage stamps and other documents.

### 6.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Selling coins, medallions, currency notes and postage stamps to dealers.

### 7.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Recruiting staff.

### 8.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Making travel and other arrangements for staff and other persons.

### 9.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Paying remuneration, allowances, expenses and pensions.

### 10.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Accepting funds for investment of behalf of the principal and managing the investment of funds so accepted, but only at the risk of, and (subject to section 8(5)) in accordance with general or specific instructions given by, the principal.

### 11.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Making or receiving payments in accordance with standing or other instructions given by the principal.

### 12.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Managing operations relating to the raising and servicing of loans.

### 13.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Administering and managing the provision or utilisation of financial, technical or other assistance of any kind.

### 14.

**No longer in force (ended 1997-03-21).**

Negotiating and making contracts for the assembly, installation, commissioning or maintenance of equipment.
