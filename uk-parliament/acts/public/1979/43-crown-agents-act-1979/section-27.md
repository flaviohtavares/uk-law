# Crown Agents Act 1979, section 27

> Text as at 2008-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Exemptions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Board and every wholly owned subsidiary of the Board shall be exempt from corporation tax.

(2)[^c1108052] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) [^c1108053]The provisions of the Moneylenders Act 1900 to 1927 and the Moneylenders Acts (Northern Ireland) 1900 to 1969 shall not apply—

- (a) to [^c1108054]. . .the Board; or
- (b) to any body corporate the whole of whose issued share capital is held by or on behalf of the Board.
