Crown Agents Act 1979

1979 c.43

An Act to reconstitute as a body corporate, and make other provision with respects to, the Crown Agents for Oversea Governments and Administrations, including the establishment of a Board to realise certain of their assets.

\[4th April 1979\][^c1108000]
