# Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. Advice and superintendence by Secretary of State.[^M_E_43f80880-c914-4779-d24f-1094e9d3dedd]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsection (3A) below[^c250968] the Secretary of State may give advice with reference to the treatment of any ancient monument.

(2)[^c250969][^c-7b536e9301894d6b9cca93523e3f464a][^c-c9d4f3445545588405d0e112b79c3706][^c-d8e1b72804e78608435f42f3247e43c1] Subject to subsection (3A) below the Secretary of State may also, if in his opinion it is advisable, superintend any work in connection with any ancient monument if invited to do so by the owner, and shall superintend any such work, whether required to do so by the owner or not, in connection with any scheduled monument, if in his opinion it is advisable.

(3)[^c250971][^c-841777b6edfe36a6da9a266a238ec8f4][^c-efdf7d1ca4a3209586414243b8f2a0ce][^c-66a2bf149aaa691e04bd600932e9460f] Subject to subsection (3A) below the Secretary of State may make a charge for giving advice and superintendence under this section or may give it free of charge, as he thinks fit.

(3A)[^c250973] As respects a monument situated in England, subsections (1) to (3) above shall apply as if “Commission” were substituted for “Secretary of State”, “their” for “his” (in each place) and “they think” for “he thinks”.

(3B) References to an ancient monument in subsections (1) and (2) above, as amended by subsection (3A) above, shall be construed as if the reference in section 61(12)(b) of this Act to the Secretary of State were to the Commission.

## 25. Advice and superintendence by Secretary of State.[^M_E_234fed62-27e4-462f-e19e-a651e0cb04c1]

*Extent: S*

(1) Historic Environment Scotland[^V_S_206713ed-60f7-5bbc-41cf-5fe3aa3023c1] may give advice with reference to the treatment of any ancient monument.

(2)[^V_S_06128f85-0bb4-ebab-1f13-4f44294777fb] Historic Environment Scotland[^V_S_bc4ca162-d7ee-8dcc-9521-cfcf956860ef] may also, if in its[^V_S_d0cec280-f584-7b8f-7fbd-2a159c63ba30] opinion it is advisable, superintend any work in connection with any ancient monument if invited to do so by the owner, and shall superintend any such work, whether required to do so by the owner or not, in connection with any scheduled monument, if in its opinion it is advisable.

(3)[^V_S_2d4019c1-e8f1-d457-a02f-36581dfc8491] Historic Environment Scotland[^V_S_47e4dded-8756-6576-1934-e428ab2efddc] may make a charge for giving advice and superintendence under this section or may give it free of charge, as it[^V_S_c00ddabc-dfb6-fa97-0c77-015db33cc67d] thinks fit.
