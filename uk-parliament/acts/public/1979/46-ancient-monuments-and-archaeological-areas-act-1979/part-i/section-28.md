# Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979, section 28

## 28. Offence of damaging certain ancient monuments.[^M_E_f132f54c-107b-4d2a-ca42-74285ee20dba][^c250977][^c-a0b64b5b061e00fc21b706434ae439ae][^c-e563ed869421940ebde7ae9316e990bb][^c-bcfdb6f6f7cabe520c43e348f277f7c6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person who without lawful excuse destroys or damages any protected monument [^c-0121f7b9751a24af9515d7bfeb384ac7]...—

- (a) knowing that it is a protected monument; and
- (b) intending to destroy or damage the monument or being reckless as to whether the monument would be destroyed or damaged;

shall be guilty of an offence.

(1A)[^c-924957b73ead6f71a72e225af6ed3317] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) This section applies to anything done by or under the authority of the owner of the monument, other than an act for the execution of excepted works, as it applies to anything done by any other person.

In this subsection “**excepted works**” means works for which scheduled monument consent has been given under this Act (including any consent granted by order under section 3) or for which development consent has been granted[^c-3637f7cb4132bdf86159f38cff096c02].

(3) In this section “**protected monument**” means any scheduled monument and any monument under the ownership or guardianship of the Secretary of State or the Commission[^c250978] or a local authority by virtue of this Act.

(4) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or both; or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or both.

## 28. Offence of damaging certain ancient monuments.[^M_E_c5bb72b8-4516-4798-d375-ad73024feb71][^V_S_b64b9839-5c78-8110-4169-f2e6b1369a35]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person who without reasonable[^V_S_10c55d9b-7171-1036-0aea-0303b5ac2679] excuse destroys or damages any protected monument shall be guilty of an offence if the person[^V_S_29a4c344-7222-9038-9518-88ba306814ca]—

- (a) knew or ought to have known that it was[^V_S_f23be6d5-a911-0699-6acb-7d2c35276e6c] a protected monument; and
- (b) intended[^V_S_07789f6a-e5cd-ab40-725d-16aac2d2db01] to destroy or damage the monument or was[^V_S_789ccd0a-fb51-0f4f-9b99-68a4953c6f6b] reckless as to whether the monument would be destroyed or damaged;

[^V_S_b9e7168c-7718-e734-63e7-079841d609ef]...

(2) This section applies to anything done by or under the authority of the owner of the monument, other than an act for the execution of excepted works, as it applies to anything done by any other person.

In this subsection “**excepted works**” means works for which scheduled monument consent has been given under this Act (including any consent granted by order under section 3) or for which development consent has been granted[^V_S_0372ddc2-9bcb-588e-8a31-d1a33c16c315].

(3) In this section “**protected monument**” means any scheduled monument and any monument under the ownership or guardianship of the Secretary of State or the Commission[^V_S_e3007697-4bcc-5b24-803d-3e0963ae4c50] or a local authority by virtue of this Act.

(4) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding £50,000[^V_S_db3e3763-c597-f266-0ffe-899c4dd8a333] or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or both; or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or both.

(5)[^V_S_3cdaf859-9b14-9f16-62e1-ecdae49075f0] In determining the amount of any fine to be imposed on a person under this section, the court shall in particular have regard to any financial benefit which has accrued or appears likely to accrue to the person in consequence of the offence.
