# Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/46

## 31. Voluntary contributions towards expenditure under Part I.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State , Historic Environment Scotland *(Extent: S)*[^c-f67e77fd382c93dcdb498036ce413ea5] or any local authority may receive voluntary contributions for or towards the cost of any expenditure incurred by them under this Part of this Act (whether in relation to any particular monument or land or otherwise).
