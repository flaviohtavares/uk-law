# Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. General provisions with respect to claims for compensation under this Act.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any claim for compensation under this Act shall be made within the time and in the manner prescribed.

(2) Any question of disputed compensation under this Act shall be referred to and determined by the Upper Tribunal or[^key-2b5b1f87a59b41b5686d4fc5a00ede9d] (in the case of any land situated in Scotland) by the Lands Tribunal for Scotland.

(3) In relation to the determination of any such question, the provisions of section[^key-3979cac6b05e68bd3740eebcc5025479] 4 of the [^c251051]Land Compensation Act 1961 or (as the case may be) of Sections 9 and 11 of the [^c251052]Land Compensation (Scotland) Act 1963 shall apply, but the references in section 4 of the Act of 1961 and section 11 of the Act of 1963 to the acquiring authority shall be construed as references to the authority by whom the compensation claimed is payable under this Act.
