# Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/46

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 59. Prosecution of offences: Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Notwithstanding anything in section 136 of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^c251103], summary proceedings in Scotland for an offence under this Act may be commenced at any time within one year from the date on which evidence sufficient in the opinion of the prosecutor to warrant proceedings came to his knowledge; and a certificate purporting to be signed by the prosecutor stating that date shall be conclusive.
