## 42. Restrictions on use of metal detectors.[^M_E_baa1788f-3db5-4f34-ff08-2326cab280c3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c251032][^c-8b06341764092ef3434d91495aac02ea][^c-46fa721a611dd7c5500581528ef7afd8][^c-d64ad26b5c5d095898601a5d4fe56505] If a person uses a metal detector in a protected place without the written consent of the Commission (in a case of a place situated in England)[^c251033] [^c-432e7535d5bf5a310200546beccd6888]... he shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction or, in Scotland, on conviction before a court of summary jurisdiction, to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale[^c251034].

(2) In this section—

- “**metal detector**” means any device designed or adapted for detecting or locating any metal or mineral in the ground; and
- “**protected place**” means any place which is either—
  - (a) the site of a scheduled monument or of any monument under the ownership or guardianship of the Secretary of State or the Commission[^c251035] or a local authority by virtue of this Act; or
  - (b) situated in an area of archaeological importance.

(3)[^c251036][^c-a6f08cfc2537e32e09396b7ebdd1c9aa][^c-5fc34078ce53b013076320fb758eb338][^c-159eaf9e7cbf854f69f0beedad42d666] If a person without written consent[^c251037] removes any object of archaeological or historical interest which he has discovered by the use of a metal detector in a protected place he shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or on conviction on indictment to a fine.

The reference in this subsection to written consent is to that of the Commission (where the place in question is situated in England)[^c251038] [^c-44d668ea9bb1a7c1a8631b4f68f65c0b]...

(4) A consent granted by [^c-15257afbaff95dbdc1550b739f7ac656]... the Commission[^c251039] for the purposes of this section may be granted either unconditionally or subject to conditions.

(5) If any person—

- (a) in using a metal detector in a protected place in accordance with any consent granted by [^c-6ba710418e372eb29bd26f373fc9ae13]... the Commission for the purposes of this section; or
- (b) in removing or otherwise dealing with any object which he has discovered by the use of a metal detector in a protected place in accordance with any such consent;

fails to comply with any condition attached to the consent, he shall be guilty of an offence and liable, in a case falling within paragraph (a) above, to the penalty provided by subsection (1) above, and in a case falling within paragraph (b) above, to the penalty provided by subsection (3) above.

(6) In any proceedings for an offence under subsection (1) above, it shall be a defence for the accused to prove that he used the metal detector for a purpose other than detecting or locating objects of archaeological or historical interest.

(7) In any proceedings for an offence under subsection (1) or (3) above [^c-b4c6278128235a95437f3b430f59c0a1]..., it shall be a defence for the accused to prove that he had taken all reasonable precautions to find out whether the place where he used the metal detector was a protected place and did not believe that it was.

## 42. Restrictions on use of metal detectors.[^M_E_199da62d-b97d-4fe6-a62f-87e9ac96e2d9]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^V_S_c8d7b941-ca55-02e9-d13f-536b847c679e] If a person uses a metal detector in a protected place without the written consent of Historic Environment Scotland (in the case of a place situated in Scotland),[^V_S_6198f0af-7003-9111-9bcd-539fe0e5abb9] of the Commission (in a case of a place situated in England) or of the Secretary of State (in any other case)[^V_S_b5d5f473-404f-16ec-6384-0cf0de3c4ddd] he shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction or, in Scotland, on conviction before a court of summary jurisdiction, to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale[^V_S_80a5e5c1-b45a-18ac-10dc-87efd41bf262].

(2) In this section—

- “**metal detector**” means any device designed or adapted for detecting or locating any metal or mineral in the ground; and
- “**protected place**” means any place which is either—
  - (a) the site of a scheduled monument or of any monument under the ownership or guardianship of the Secretary of State , Historic Environment Scotland[^V_S_978c37da-9ba3-ab45-6040-fcb55525c4d7] or the Commission[^V_S_78e33645-3e66-50ce-1b85-9f8a8b01593b] or a local authority by virtue of this Act; or
  - (b) situated in an area of archaeological importance.

(3)[^V_S_dfeabac6-b5d2-bd9a-e9e5-606581145bd4] If a person without written consent[^V_S_7ea4ad64-d0a7-8d85-0bbe-83e255c9ed74] removes any object of archaeological or historical interest which he has discovered by the use of a metal detector in a protected place he shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or on conviction on indictment to a fine.

The reference in this subsection to written consent is to that[^V_S_fafdb199-9dba-2ed8-772d-4d73a3f3a31c] of Historic Environment Scotland (where the place in question is situated in Scotland),[^V_S_e0811f25-d930-2f4d-d100-14cc8afe2a79] of the Commission (where the place in question is situated in England) or of the Secretary of State (in any other case)

(4) A consent granted by Historic Environment Scotland,[^V_S_81d4fc05-33de-6af5-fdcf-ad63ba8fe31e] the Secretary of State or the Commission[^V_S_06ff7fd8-3793-74d6-1497-3b771501c1a2] for the purposes of this section may be granted either unconditionally or subject to conditions.

(5) If any person—

- (a) in using a metal detector in a protected place in accordance with any consent granted by Historic Environment Scotland,[^V_S_7dc27ec8-058e-1216-8094-4e44ab418b49] the Secretary of State or the Commission for the purposes of this section; or
- (b) in removing or otherwise dealing with any object which he has discovered by the use of a metal detector in a protected place in accordance with any such consent;

fails to comply with any condition attached to the consent, he shall be guilty of an offence and liable, in a case falling within paragraph (a) above, to the penalty provided by subsection (1) above, and in a case falling within paragraph (b) above, to the penalty provided by subsection (3) above.

(6) In any proceedings for an offence under subsection (1) above, it shall be a defence for the accused to show[^V_S_ce4889f6-270f-7273-08b5-cffcfa1b226e] that he used the metal detector for a purpose other than detecting or locating objects of archaeological or historical interest.

(7) In any proceedings for an offence under subsection (1) or (3) above, it shall be a defence for the accused to show that—[^V_S_d3376f80-9618-96ef-6414-9c4b9f58d926]

- (a) he had taken all reasonable steps to find out whether the place where he used the metal detector was a protected place ; and[^V_S_de999da3-4736-ff79-e3d6-ac200b90627e]
- (b) he did not know and had no reason to believe that that place was a protected place.
