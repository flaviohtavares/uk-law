# Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979, section 49

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 49. Grants to the Architectural Heritage Fund.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State may make grants to the Architectural Heritage Fund[^c251074].

(1A)[^c251075] The Commission may make grants to the Architectural Heritage Fund for the purpose of enabling it to perform its functions in, or in relation to, England.

(2) A grant under this section may be made subject to such conditions as the Secretary of State or the Commission (as the case may be)[^c251076] may think fit to impose.

(3)[^c251077] In this section “the Architectural Heritage Fund” means the institution registered under that name under the Charities Act 2011 *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-57545e725e79bc152884c21c323c2931]
