# Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 64. Transitional provisions, consequential amendments and repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Schedule 3 to this Act shall have effect for the purposes of the transition to the provisions of this Act from the law previously in force.

(2)[^c251386] The enactments specified in Schedule 4 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule,being amendments consequential on the provisions of this Act.

(3)[^c251386] The enactments specified in Schedule 5 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
