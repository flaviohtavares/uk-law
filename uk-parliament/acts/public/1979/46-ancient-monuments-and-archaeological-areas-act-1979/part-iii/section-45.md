# Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. Expenditure on archaeological investigation.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State may undertake, or assist in, or defray or contribute towards the cost of, an archaeological investigation of any land *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cc61867866aa63898d46d2b1bff7dff0] )(other than land in England)[^c251042] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cc61867866aa63898d46d2b1bff7dff0] which he considers may contain an ancient monument or anything else of archaeological or historical interest. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cc61867866aa63898d46d2b1bff7dff0]

(1A)[^c251043] The Commission may undertake, or assist in, or defray or contribute towards the cost of, an archaeological investigation of any land in England which they consider may contain an ancient monument or anything else of archaeological or historical interest; and the reference to an ancient monument in this subsection shall be construed as if the reference in section 61(12)( *b* ) of this Act to the Secretary of State were to the Commission.

(2)[^c251044] Any local authority may undertake, or assist in, or defray or contribute towards the cost of, an archaeological investigation of any land in or in the vicinity of their area, being land which they consider may contain an ancient monument or anything else of archaeological or historical interest.

(3)[^c251044] The Secretary of State *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7538e012f1947384c4c3f54183f2933b] or[^c251046] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7538e012f1947384c4c3f54183f2933b] the Commission or any local authority may publish the results of any archaeological investigation undertaken, assisted, or wholly or partly financed by them under this section in such manner and form as they think fit.

(4) Without prejudice to the application, by virtue of section 53 of this Act, of any other provision of this Act to land which is not within Great Britain, the powers conferred by this section shall be exercisable in relation to any such land which forms part of the sea bed within the seaward limits of United Kingdom territorial waters adjacent to the coast of Great Britain (or, as regards the powers mentioned in subsection (1A) above, England)[^c251047].
