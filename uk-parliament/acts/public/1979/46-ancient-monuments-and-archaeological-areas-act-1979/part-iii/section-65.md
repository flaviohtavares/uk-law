# Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1979/46

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 65. Short title, commencement and extent.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979.

(2)[^c251388] This Act shall come into force on such day as may be appointed by order of the Secretary of State, and different days may be appointed for different purposes; and a reference in any provision of this Act to the commencement of this Act is a reference to the day appointed for the coming into force of that provision.

(3) This Act does not extend to Northern Ireland.
