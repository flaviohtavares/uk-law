## 18. Amendment of Corn Returns Act, 1882. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5645e805a8faaddcc86c15e24d49bceb]

(1) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5645e805a8faaddcc86c15e24d49bceb] [^c591199]Corn Returns Act, 1882, as amended by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5645e805a8faaddcc86c15e24d49bceb] [^c591200]Corn Sales Act, 1921, shall have effect subject to the amendments set out in the Third Schedule to this Act, being amendments made for the purpose of — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5645e805a8faaddcc86c15e24d49bceb]

- (a) removing the limits as to the number of towns to which the said Act is applicable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5645e805a8faaddcc86c15e24d49bceb]
- (b) abolishing the office of inspector of corn returns; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5645e805a8faaddcc86c15e24d49bceb]
- (c) providing for certain other matters of minor importance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5645e805a8faaddcc86c15e24d49bceb]
