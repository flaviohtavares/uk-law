## 38A. Notice of termination in respect of dwelling-houses.[^c-4a2fbc01d2837c2d58f3a30aa6d3de05][^c15187441]

*Extent: S*

Any notice of termination of tenancy or notice of removal given under section 37 or 38 above in respect of a dwelling-house, on or after 2nd of December 1974, shall be in writing and shall contain such information as may be prescribed by virtue of section 112 of the [^c15187451]Rent (Scotland) Act 1984, and Rule 112 of Schedule 1 to this Act shall no longer apply to any such notice under section 37 above.
