# Marriage Act 1949, section 19

> Text as at 2025-05-27.
>
> Extent: England.

## 19. Publication of banns and solemnization of marriage where church injured by war damage.

*Extent: E*

Where an order made by the Church Commissioners under section three of the [^c6279951]Diocesan Reorganisation Committees Measure, 1941, (which enables orders to be made deferring the restoration of churches injured by war damage) is in force as respects any church, banns of matrimony of persons entitled to be married in that church may be published, and marriages of such persons may be solemnized, in such other church, chapel or place of worship within the diocese as the bishop of the diocese shall in writing direct.
