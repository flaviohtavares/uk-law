# Marriage Act 1949, section 25

> Text as at 2025-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Void marriages.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-91f7b7c2ed484554b6a2bdceb6194153] A marriage shall be void in any of the following cases.

(2)[^key-b8b614a89432c2ec01b339229a6fa7a5] Case A is where any persons[^key-5a54d4e562ac3e3ca49077798c252cbe] knowingly and wilfully intermarry according to the rites of the Church of England (otherwise than by special licence)—

- (a) except in the case of a marriage in pursuance of section 26(1)([^c6280151]*dd*) of this Act, in any place other than a church or other building in which banns may be published;
- (b) without banns having been duly published, a common licence having been obtained, or a marriage schedule[^key-9f105f0fa5cb63dbeee352f52ff1b93e] having been duly issued under Part III of this Act [^key-3aa60d5ca833051864b1bd879d1ba472]...; or
- (c) on the authority of a publication of banns which is void by virtue of [^key-12201d003dc8f2c2ea74a61df67ffb78]... subsection (2) of section twelve of this Act, on the authority of a common licence which is void by virtue of subsection (3) of section sixteen of this Act, or on the authority of a marriage schedule which is[^key-7a0fe4fe11d2adfe68c3120b2a929775] void by virtue of subsection (2) of section thirty-three of this Act;
- (d) in the case of a marriage on the authority of a marriage schedule[^key-8a7752b115d91ea618a360504c279b70], in any place other than the church building or other place specified in the[^c6280191] notices of marriage[^c6280181] and (if so specified) in the marriage schedule[^key-0e0c75692c173d16f8bdb531b9c3ad8a] as the place where the marriage is to be solemnized.

(3)[^key-f911d86c083d67d84973c7b134e53d47] Case B is where any persons knowingly and wilfully consent to or acquiesce in the solemnization of a Church of England marriage between them by a person who is not in Holy Orders.

(4) Case C is where any persons of the same sex consent to or acquiesce in the solemnization of a Church of England marriage between them.

(5) In subsections (3) and (4) “**Church of England marriage**” means a marriage according to the rites of the Church of England.
