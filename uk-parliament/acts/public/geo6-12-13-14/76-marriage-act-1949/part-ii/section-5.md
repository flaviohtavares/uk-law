# Marriage Act 1949, section 5

> Text as at 2025-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Methods of authorising marriages.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-51eb31631d84faae3b556c3e4f057e87] A marriage according to the rites of the Church of England may be solemnized—

- (a) after the publication of banns of matrimony;
- (b) on the authority of a special licence of marriage granted by the Archbishop of Canterbury or any other person by virtue of the [^c6279751]Ecclesiastical Licences Act, 1533 (in this Act referred to as a “special licence”);
- (c) on the authority of a licence of marriage (other than a special licence) granted by an ecclesiastical authority having power to grant such a licence (in this Act referred to as a “common licence”); or
- (d) on the authority of a marriage schedule[^c-6e018ceaee2b0da356c45a3cdcad3022] under Part III of this Act.

(2)[^c-17e365b81e02bac58fdb5b5f52c278fa][^c6279771] Subsection (1)(a) of this section shall not apply in relation to the solemnization of any marriage mentioned in subsection (2) of section 1 of this Act.

(3)[^c-f1ef4e1f08db367ea9bace2fcd9204d4] In a case where one or both of the persons whose marriage is to be solemnized is not a relevant national—

- (a)[^c-8d3bf6206300c27d248e11801c669a51] subsection (1)(a) shall not apply unless the banns are published in accordance with section 14 (whether or not the banns are also published otherwise);
- (b)[^c-a2bddedb18cdb745fc39bef709d0025e] subsection (1)(c) shall not apply.
