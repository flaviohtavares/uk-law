# Marriage Act 1949, section 17

> Text as at 2025-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Marriage under marriage schedule.[^c-b42781be51ba52f9fa28ea18eb15312f]

*Extent: E+W*

A marriage according to the rites of the Church of England may be solemnized on the authority of a marriage schedule[^c-3f4c81e8bfa14a33cfdd68f1e87ea449] in force under Part III of this Act in any church or chapel in which banns of matrimony may be published or in the case of a marriage in pursuance of section 26(1)(dd) of this Act the place specified in the[^c6279921] notices of marriage[^c6279931] and (if so specified) in the marriage schedule[^c-660fce24857e475936b551e1fd33b7e2] as the place where the marriage is to be solemnized:

Provided that a marriage shall not be solemnized as aforesaid in any such church or chapel without the consent of the minister thereof or (wherever the marriage is solemnized)[^c6279941] by any person other than a clergyman.
