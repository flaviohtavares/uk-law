# Marriage Act 1949, section 2

> Text as at 2025-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Marriages of persons under eighteen.[^c-1c3bdce29a0294ce6be59ccbfa55d834]

*Extent: E+W*

A marriage solemnized between persons either of whom is under the age of eighteen[^c-6c0a1b354169cdebb204b54f834e9799] shall be void.
