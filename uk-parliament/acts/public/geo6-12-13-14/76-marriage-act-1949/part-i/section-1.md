# Marriage Act 1949, section 1

> Text as at 2025-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Marriages within prohibited degrees.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A marriage solemnized between a person and any person mentioned in the list in Part 1 of Schedule 1[^c-3ae1ba75c727c3eee2329d9b73ddbf54], shall be void.

(2)[^c6279051] Subject to subsection (3) of this section, a marriage solemnized between a person and any person mentioned in the list in Part 2 of Schedule 1[^c-277483ea62ae6b562bfa40242c619351], shall be void.

(3) Any such marriage as is mentioned in subsection (2) of this section shall not be void by reason only of affinity if both the parties to the marriage have attained the age of twenty-one at the time of the marriage and the younger party has not at any time before attaining the age of eighteen been a child of the family in relation to the other party.
