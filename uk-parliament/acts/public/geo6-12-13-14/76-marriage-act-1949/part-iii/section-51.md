## 51. Fees of superintendent registrars for attending marriages in approved premises[^c-eee443844fca27b096765aa8b9e8f415]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-968baa2cc8c011efa9b2fd9d4234e3fe][^c6284491] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^c6284521] In the case of persons married on approved premises in pursuance of section 26(1)(bb) of this Act—

- (a)[^c-11906b2f96bf0d6e63818460b2d342fb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the superintendent registrar in whose presence the persons are married shall be entitled to receive from them a fee of an amount determined in accordance with regulations under section 46A of this Act by the local authority that approved the premises.
