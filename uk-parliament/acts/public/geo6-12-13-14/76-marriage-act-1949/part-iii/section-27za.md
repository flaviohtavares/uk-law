# Marriage Act 1949, section 27ZA

> Text as at 2025-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27ZA. Circumstances in which a notice of marriage is not to be recorded in the marriage register[^key-dbd7a87d7b704aefac40d26987efb2e1][^key-3b8d6b9d92fd69a24d1bb97678e2ddf1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The superintendent registrar shall not record a notice of marriage in the marriage register[^key-d8e616f4f9b49cbde88299450e51a654] in a case where any of the following requirements is applicable but is not complied with—

- (a) a requirement imposed by or under any of the following provisions of this Act—
  - section 27A(2) or (3);
  - section 27A(3A);[^key-29d968085e85c51634c3d8dc9cb16bbf]
  - section 27A(4);
  - section 27B(2);
  - section 27E(3) to (7);
  - section 27E(8);
  - section 28B(1);
  - section 28B(1A);[^key-12eb9dda0467432a883615c0402f1e2c]
  - section 28C(4) or (6);
- (b) the requirement imposed by section 19(2) of the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc) Act 2004.
