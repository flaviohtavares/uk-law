# Marriage Act 1949, section 34

> Text as at 2025-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Marriages normally to be solemnized in registration district in which one party resides.[^c6282071][^c-c898f7a5800b3f42e0c6454cec446a45]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to section 35, a superintendent registrar may not issue a marriage schedule[^c-16353acd40051b8a1a9ba822012e59a5] for the solemnization of a marriage elsewhere than within a registration district in which one of the persons to be married has resided for 7 days immediately before the giving of the notice of marriage.
