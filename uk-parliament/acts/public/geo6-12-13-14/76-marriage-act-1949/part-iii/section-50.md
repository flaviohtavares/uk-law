## 50. Person to whom marriage schedule to be delivered.[^key-930f36f696264c32ed3bac21fd2f2dee]

*Extent: E+W*

(A1)[^key-76902a4d774dbaccaefde5b6ed51b336] This section applies where the parties to a marriage have been issued a marriage schedule under section 31(4).

(1) The parties to the marriage must deliver the marriage schedule to the following person[^key-7363b2340b69a2d6885cbdf384bfca95] :—

- (a)[^key-74eec9a05c767f92e3408d15a14bd1ee] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) if the marriage is to be solemnized in a registered building without the presence of a registrar, the authorised person in whose presence the marriage is to be solemnized;
- (c)[^key-2aba5c72bb2052cfaa2852b0efdedff5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (cc)[^key-c238b8f2a194a606267dcfb1c8ba3e4c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) if the marriage is to be solemnized according to the usages of the Society of Friends, an[^key-8ba64d861b94d7230cc4baa9a19f17e5] officer of that Society for the place where the marriage is to be solemnized;
- (e) if the marriage is to be solemnized according to the usages of persons professing the Jewish religion, the officer of a synagogue by whom the marriage schedule is required to be signed in accordance with section 53C[^key-3cc5e07c9ca4c2f270b4e7523d3bdfc9];
- (f) if the marriage is to be solemnized according to the rites of the Church of England, the officiating clergyman.
