# Marriage Act 1949, section 76A

> Text as at 2025-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 76A. Offence of failing to attend premises in compliance with a section 53D(8) or section 53E(10) notice[^c-bb48480ed0f9eb3a6537f2909777e41d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person who, without reasonable cause, fails to attend premises in compliance with a notice issued under section 53D(8) or section 53E(10) commits an offence.

(2) A person who commits an offence under subsection (1) is liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
