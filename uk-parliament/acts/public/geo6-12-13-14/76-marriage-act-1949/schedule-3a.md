# Marriage Act 1949, SCHEDULE 3A

> Text as at 2025-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 3A — Modifications if proposed marriage referred under section 28H[^c-f2d06e0950bf5aee31a53afa9048786a]

*Extent: E+W*

### Introduction

**1.** (1) These are the modifications subject to which this Act has effect if the superintendent registrar refers a proposed marriage to the Secretary of State.

(2) In this Schedule—

- “**2014 Act**” means the Immigration Act 2014;
- “**referred marriage**” means the proposed marriage referred to the Secretary of State.

### No marriage schedule to be issued until decision about investigation etc[^c-6b7764b5ff492adb60826f5c6e082876]

**2.** (1) The duty under section 31(2) to issue a marriage schedule[^c-02b39da5fc8aa65edd4472fc8e2c8c87] in respect of the referred marriage does not apply unless and until one of the following events occurs.

(2) Event 1 occurs if—

- (a) the Secretary of State gives the superintendent registrar or (as the case may be) superintendent registrars[^c-3673ac42a24899122586f8f76f64050d] the section 48 notice, and
- (b) that notice is of a decision not to investigate whether the referred marriage is a sham.

(3) Event 2 occurs if—

- (a) the relevant statutory period ends, and
- (b) the Secretary of State has not given the superintendent registrar or (as the case may be) superintendent registrars[^c-0b13e556d916aebc4d8542cd5572168b] the section 48 notice.

(4) Event 3 occurs if—

- (a) the Secretary of State gives the superintendent registrar or (as the case may be) superintendent registrars[^c-ed951e84b89345953b3154b46325b73e] the section 48 notice,
- (b) that notice is of a decision to investigate whether the referred marriage is a sham,
- (c) the Secretary of State gives the superintendent registrar or (as the case may be) superintendent registrars[^c-54bc5dfc37803df9465a707e992d9e9b] the section 50 notice, and
- (d) that notice is of a decision that both of the parties to the referred marriage have complied with the investigation.

(5) Event 4 occurs if—

- (a) the 70 day period ends, and
- (b) the Secretary of State has not given the superintendent registrar or (as the case may be) superintendent registrars[^c-0bbd8e7fb4670de639eb05c93633a0db] the section 50 notice.

(6) Event 5 occurs if the Secretary of State gives the superintendent registrar or (as the case may be) superintendent registrars[^c-46c6454054fa25fb6c46f2e0f4daa183] notice that the duty under section 31(2) is applicable.

(7) The Secretary of State may give a notice for that purpose only if—

- (a) the Secretary of State has given the superintendent registrar or (as the case may be) superintendent registrars[^c-5bbf000c9567473fcc94ee1dce7eb89e] the section 48 notice,
- (b) that notice is of a decision to investigate whether the referred marriage is a sham,
- (c) the Secretary of State has given the superintendent registrar or (as the case may be) superintendent registrars[^c-5fd75bf21cc9ec69d548113f668940fb] the section 50 notice, and
- (d) that notice is of a decision that one or both of the parties to the referred marriage have not complied with the investigation.

(8) This paragraph applies in addition to any other requirements applicable to the issue of the marriage schedule[^c-b8d1592fe408b339f463e9d491efe755].

(9) This paragraph is subject to paragraph 4.

(10) In this paragraph—

- “**70 day period**” has the same meaning as in section 50 of the 2014 Act;
- “**relevant statutory period**” has the same meaning as in section 48 of the 2014 Act;
- “**section 48 notice**” means notice under section 48(7) of the 2014 Act;
- “**section 50 notice**” means notice under section 50(7) of the 2014 Act.

### Marriage to be investigated: extension of waiting period to 70 days

**3.** (1) The modifications in this paragraph have effect if the Secretary of State gives the superintendent registrar notice under section 48(7) of the 2014 Act of a decision to investigate whether the referred marriage is a sham.

(2)[^c-6c55586a9381f4ca486365fcfdffef97] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Section 31(4A)(a): the reference to the period of 28 days has effect as a reference to the relevant 70 day period.

(4) Section 31(5A) [^c-09aa055614beaecf75b8b07952475687]...: the reference to the 28 day period has effect as a reference to the relevant 70 day period.

(5) Section 31(5B) does not apply.

(6)[^c-d079aab1bcb987d5fb1f8f687dc8a540] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) In this paragraph “**relevant 70 day period**” means the period—

- (a) beginning the day after notice of the proposed marriage is recorded in the marriage register[^c-bbb3a59d503a6975c11b2f48e4c10181] in accordance with Part 3 of the Marriage Act 1949, [^c-a202ba32cdfa1326d63600ab0e1f429e]... and
- (b) ending at the end of the period of 70 days beginning with that day.

### Effect of reducing statutory period

**4.** (1) This paragraph applies if—

- (a) the Secretary of State gives notice under section 31(5EB) of the grant of an application made under section 31(5A) (reduction of statutory period) in relation to the referred marriage, and
- (b) that notice is given at a time when the duty under section 31(2) to issue a marriage schedule[^c-d0b4e743273890c1022ee9cd4a187102] in respect of the referred marriage has not arisen in accordance with paragraph 2.

(2) The duty under subsection 31(2) to issue a marriage schedule[^c-63000b94dafc54a15705b1e39a4bbce0] in respect of the referred marriage arises on the giving of the notice, subject to any other requirements applicable to the issue of the marriage schedule being met.

(3) But the requirements of paragraph 2 are not applicable in such a case.

(4) The Secretary of State is not prevented from deciding to conduct, conducting, or continuing, an investigation if a marriage schedule[^c-5c64eab67446a519efa5186da2d83cd2] in respect of the referred marriage is issued as mentioned in sub-paragraph (2).

(5) But in such a case, nothing in the 2014 Act requires the Secretary of State to decide whether to conduct, to conduct, or to continue, an investigation.

(6) In this paragraph “**investigation**” means an investigation, conducted following a decision by the Secretary of State under section 48 of the 2014 Act, whether a proposed marriage is a sham.
