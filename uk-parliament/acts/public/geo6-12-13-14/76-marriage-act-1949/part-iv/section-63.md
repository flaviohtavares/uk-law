# Marriage Act 1949, section 63

> Text as at 2025-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Searches in register books.[^c6285141]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Every incumbent, [^c-afbb70d12ec2dbc11e4d4a6c8cd93ed8]... officer of the Society of Friends, secretary of a synagogue and registrar by whom a marriage register book is kept may[^c-4507d62b970a2571bbf9dafc1b573c6e] allow searches to be made in any marriage register book in his keeping[^c-8d67d2e68858f1827d1df601ad68a32a]... [^c-e9e8f84088ec315223d3fc18d229e842]...

(2) The last foregoing subsection shall apply in the case of a registered building for which an authorised person has been appointed with the substitution for the reference to the incumbent of a reference to the person responsible for keeping[^c-17086ee17e06a7d1e456969735c0339c] a marriage register book in accordance with regulations made under section seventy-four of this Act.
