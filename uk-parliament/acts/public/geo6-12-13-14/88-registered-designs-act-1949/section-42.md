## 42. Annual report of registrar.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Comptroller—General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks shall, in his annual report with respect to the execution of the[^c9627] [^c9628]Patents Act 1977, include a report with respect to the execution of this Act as if it formed a part of or was included in that Act.
