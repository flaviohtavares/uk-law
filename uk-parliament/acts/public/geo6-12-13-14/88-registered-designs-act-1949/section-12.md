# Registered Designs Act 1949, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/88
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Use for services of the Crown.[^c9434]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of the First Schedule to this Act shall have effect with respect to the use of registered designs for the services of the Crown and the rights of third parties in respect of such use.
