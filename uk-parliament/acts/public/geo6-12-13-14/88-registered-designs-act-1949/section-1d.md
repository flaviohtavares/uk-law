# Registered Designs Act 1949, section 1D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/88
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1D. Designs contrary to public policy or morality.[^c9305]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A right in a registered design shall not subsist in a design which is contrary to public policy or to accepted principles of morality.
