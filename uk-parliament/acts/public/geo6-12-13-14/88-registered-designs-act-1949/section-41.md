# Registered Designs Act 1949, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/88

## 41. Service of notices, etc., by post.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any notice required or authorised to be given by or under this Act, and any application or other document so authorised or required to be made or filed, may be given, made or filed by post.
