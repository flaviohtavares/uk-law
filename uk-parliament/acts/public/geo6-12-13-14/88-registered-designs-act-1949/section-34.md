# Registered Designs Act 1949, section 34

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Falsification of register, etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any person makes or causes to be made a false entry in the register of designs, or a writing falsely purporting to be a copy of an entry in that register, or produces or tenders or causes to be produced or tendered in evidence any such writing, knowing the entry or writing to be false, he shall be guilty of an offence and liable—[^c9543]

- (a) on conviction on indictment to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or a fine, or both;
- (b) on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both.
