# Registered Designs Act 1949, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/88
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Fees.[^c9624][^c9625]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be paid in respect of the registration of designs and applications therefor, and in respect of other matters relating to designs arising under this Act, such fees as may be prescribed by rules made by the Secretary of State[^c9626] with the consent of the Treasury.
