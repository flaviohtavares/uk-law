# Registered Designs Act 1949, section 3A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/88
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3A. Determination of applications for registration.[^c9315]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject as follows, the registrar shall not refuse to register a design included in an application under this Act[^c-fde5009ac74e5d4a30b465740661f734].

(2) If it appears to the registrar that an application for the registration of a design or designs[^c-29350c9a3af027f76542dfb54ca89bbd] has not been made in accordance with any rules made under this Act, he may refuse to register any design included in it[^c-4cbf26be0f031b24b035dff6282343de].

(3) If it appears to the registrar that the applicant is not under section 3(2) or (3) or 14 entitled to apply for the registration of a design included in the application, he shall refuse to register that design.[^c-e22fcb031b68bec14354401efe6fb3a0]

(4)[^c-6cc0477e99826fa1567e9ff4ba737f0d] If it appears to the registrar that the application for registration includes—

- (a) something which does not fulfil the requirements of section 1(2) of this Act;
- (b) a design that does not fulfil the requirements of section 1C or 1D of this Act; or
- (c) a design to which a ground of refusal mentioned in Schedule A1 to this Act applies,

he shall refuse to register that thing or that design.
