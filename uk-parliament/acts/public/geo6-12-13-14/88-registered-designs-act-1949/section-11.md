# Registered Designs Act 1949, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/88

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Cancellation of registration.[^c1323162]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The registrar may, upon a request made in the prescribed manner by the registered proprietor, cancel the registration of a design.
