# National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949, section 50A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/97
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50A. Application of Part IV of this Act in Wales.[^c641956]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The provisions of this Part of this Act shall, subject to the next following subsection, apply to land in Wales as they apply to land in England.

(2) Where a provision of this Part of this Act confers a function on the Natural England[^c-57e8006b2048c7533efd000b5caadc12] as respects England (or land of any description in England), the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c-c9222bddb34c4f63b5997addab6bef4f] shall have the corresponding function as respects Wales (or land of a similar description in Wales).
