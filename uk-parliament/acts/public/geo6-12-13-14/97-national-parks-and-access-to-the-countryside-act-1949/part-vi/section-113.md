# National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/97
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 113. National Trust land.[^c642485]

*Extent: E+W+S*

No power conferred by Part V or Part VI of this Act to acquire land compulsorily shall be exercisable in respect of land belonging to the National Trust which is held by the Trust inalienably.
