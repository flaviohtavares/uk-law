# National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949, section 86A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/97

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 86A. Information services to be provided by Natural Resources Body for Wales.[^c642194][^c-1ec66adbe3b2ac62a1962e3beb510bd7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The provisions of section eighty-six of this Act shall apply to the Natural Resources Body for Wales in relation to National Parks and other land in Wales as they apply to Natural England[^c-673829518a5315607fc9466e37f62f3c] in relation to National Parks and other land in England.
