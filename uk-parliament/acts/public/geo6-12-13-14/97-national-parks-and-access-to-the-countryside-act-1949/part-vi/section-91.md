# National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949, section 91

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/97

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 91. Default powers of Secretary of State as to certain byelaws.[^c642273]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If a local planning authority, when required by the Secretary of State to make, as respects land or a waterway to which the public are given access by an agreement or order, or in consequence of acquisition, under Part V of this Act, byelaws with respect to any of the matters with respect to which they are empowered by the last foregoing section to make byelaws, do not within three months after being so required comply with the requirement to the satisfaction of the Secretary of State, he may himself make byelaws in relation to the matters, and as respects the land or waterway, in question:

Provided that before making byelaws under this section as respects a National Park or area of outstanding natural beauty in Wales[^c-322250fac87eb6d90a2a87d46312eec7] the Secretary of State shall consult with Natural England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1b82984e277dd7fc281911203921fa6][^c-cd59eb25788b265ea62e04d057568a65](as regards land or waterways in England) or[^c642275] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1b82984e277dd7fc281911203921fa6] the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c-3712e3635e1eb2c4cf555f7bede523a8] (as regards land or waterways in Wales) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1b82984e277dd7fc281911203921fa6].

(2) Any byelaws made by the Secretary of State under this section shall have effect as if they had been made by the local planning authority and confirmed by the Secretary of State, and the provisions of this Act and of any enactment thereby applied shall have effect in relation to the byelaws accordingly.
