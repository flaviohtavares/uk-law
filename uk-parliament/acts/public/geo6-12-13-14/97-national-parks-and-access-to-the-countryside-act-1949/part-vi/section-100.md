# National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949, section 100

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/97

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 100. Payments out of moneys provided by Parliament.[^c642310]

*Extent: E+W+S*

There shall be defrayed out of moneys provided by Parliament—

- (a) the expenses under this Act of the Minister of Town and Country Planning, . . . [^c642311] and the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food[^c642312];
- (b) any increase attributable to the provisions of this Act in the sums payable out of such moneys under Part I or Part II of the [^c642313]Local Government Act 1948.
