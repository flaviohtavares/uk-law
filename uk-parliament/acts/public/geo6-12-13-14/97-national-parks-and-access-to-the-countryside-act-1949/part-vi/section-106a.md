## 106A. Supplementary provisions as to byelaws made by the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^key-6316c69fdaf07db98498f30fee38f52a][^key-256de3451c308a92bcf8398c48f9d8fb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Sections 3 to 19 of the Local Government Byelaws (Wales) Act 2012 shall apply to all byelaws made by the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^key-f4045839b21f05bf61f1f5ec568ae150] under this Act.

(2) The confirming authority for the purposes of section 7 of the 2012 Act is the Welsh Ministers.
