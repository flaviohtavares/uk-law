# National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949, section 103

## 103. General provisions as to acquisition of land.[^c642371]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c642373][^c642372] Any power conferred by this Act on the appropriate conservation body[^key-d539396009002d22ff0a18dc856c6c79] (as defined in section 15A of this Act)[^c642374] or a local authority to acquire land compulsorily shall be exercisable in any particular case on their being authorised so to do by the Secretary of State.

(1A)[^c642375] The [^c642376] Acquisition of Land Act 1981 shall apply to the acquisition of land under this Act, and in relation to the acquisition under this Act of any interest in land the [^c642377] Compulsory Purchase Act 1965 shall apply with any necessary modifications.

(2)[^c642373][^c642378] In relation to the compulsory acquisition of land under this Act by Scottish Natural Heritage[^c642380] or a local authority, the [^c642381] Acquisition of Land (Authorisation Procedure) Act 1946, shall apply as if this Act had been in force immediately before the commencement of that Act and as if in paragraph (a) of subsection (1) of section one thereof, in Part I of the First Schedule thereto and in the Second Schedule thereto references to a local authority included references to Scottish Natural Heritage :

Provided that section two of the said Act (which confers temporary powers for the speedy acquisition of land in urgent cases) shall not apply to any such compulsory acquisition as is mentioned in this subsection.

(3) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c642383]

(4) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c642384]

(5) The following provision shall have effect, in relation to the acquisition of land under this Act, in substitution for section 120 of the[^c642385] [^c642386]Local Government Act 1972 (which provides for the acquisition of land by local authorities by agreement for the purposes of their functions), that is to say, a local authority may with the consent of the Minister by agreement acquire, whether by way of purchase, lease or exchange, any land, whether within or without the area of the local authority, which they require for the purpose of any of their functions under this Act or any other land which they may be authorised under this Act to acquire compulsorily.

(6) In this section, and in any enactment in this Act which confers a power to acquire land compulsorily, the expression “**land**” includes any interest in land; and the provisions of the Lands Clauses Acts incorporated with this Act by virtue of paragraph 1 of the Second Schedule to the[^c642387] [^c642388]Acquisition of Land (Authorisation Procedure) Act 1946, as applied by subsections (2) and (4) of this section, shall apply with the necessary modifications in relation to the compulsory acquisition of any interest in land, being an interest not falling within the definition of “lands” contained in the Lands Clauses Acts.

(7) In the application of this section to Scotland there shall be substituted, for references to the [^c642389]Acquisition of Land (Authorisation Procedure) Act 1946, and to section 120 of the [^c642391]Local Government Act 1972 respectively, references to the [^c642392]Acquisition of Land (Authorisation Procedure) (Scotland) Act 1947, and to section 70 of the[^c642393] [^c642394]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 and in subsection (5) there shall be substituted for the words “whether by way of purchase, lease or exchange” the words “whether by way of purchase, feu, lease or excambion”; and subsection (4) shall not apply.
