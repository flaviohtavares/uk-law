# National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949, section 15A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/97

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15A. Meaning of appropriate conservation body.[^c641852][^c-299c2130cd91ee382b3dab1fd1006e4e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-9bfed4b911206b11767385367458fc95] In this Part of this Act references to the appropriate conservation body are references[^c-a5fd6e9cca3c419f13468747e1e180b8]—

- (a) in relation to land in England, to Natural England[^c-aab809cadbb4d7f02271379cf453dac8] ;
- (b) in relation to land in Scotland, to Scottish Natural Heritage[^c641854] for Scotland; and
- (c) in relation to land in Wales, to the Countryside Council for Wales *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5ae70b4c4e248b42d8f81cd7ae6c5d34]Natural Resources Body for Wales *(Extent: E+W)*

(2)[^c-76b3165b8ab8207ca639567ad9651d9d] In this Part of this Act—

- (a) “ **nature reserve agreement** ” means an agreement under section 7 of the 2006 Act or section 16 of the 2016 Act *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-877fe01f1821cdb561b135f017c739d9] for securing that land which it appears expedient in the national interest should be managed as a nature reserve shall be so managed, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-412ffbdead3f027428a4c8e8f1e89ee2]
- (b) “ **the 2006 Act** ” means the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006.

*Extent: E+W*

- (c)[^c-98d170d1bef352b950b8109c8f8d1cd3] “**the 2016 Act**” means the Environment (Wales) Act 2016.
