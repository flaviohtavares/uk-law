# National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949, section 4A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/97
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4A. Application of Part II of this Act in Wales.[^c641702][^c641701]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The provisions of this Part of this Act shall, subject to the next following subsection, apply to land in Wales as they apply to land in England.

(2) Where a provision of this Part of this Act confers a function on the Natural England[^c-94b6f04f976d5831129b628251b259bb] as respects England (or areas of any description in England), the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c-e149426dcb8c8251970a66380714a02e] shall have the corresponding function as respects Wales (or areas of a similar description in Wales).

(3)[^c-27db2ad2d42b28ddd9169c984c9d63b6] Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to section 11A(1A) or (1B) (duty to further statutory purposes of National Parks in England).
