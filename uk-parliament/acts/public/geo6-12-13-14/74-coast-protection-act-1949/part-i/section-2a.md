> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-05-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/74

## 2A. Coastal erosion risk management authorities[^c-533e9f3f67237e90a561560103179a3f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-1120a0ae30bc7361dbb7ad442f6db34f] The following are “**coastal erosion risk management authorities**” for the purposes of this Act—

- (a) coast protection authorities, [^c-5bc71bd50879085d7c70b54b6caaa531]...
- (b)[^c-456e1d0d6e8d0c2aabb010b61d5613ee] the Environment Agency, in relation to coastal erosion risks in England, and
- (c) the Natural Resources Body for Wales, in relation to coastal erosion risks in Wales.

(2)[^c-a59029935056f52a04054eb8cf10cefa] In this Part, references to the area of a coastal erosion risk management authority are—

- (a) in relation to the Environment Agency, references to England, and
- (b) in relation to the Natural Resources Body for Wales, references to Wales.
