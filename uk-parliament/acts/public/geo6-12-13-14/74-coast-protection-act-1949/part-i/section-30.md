## 30. Transfer and compensation of officers, and superannuation rights.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An order constituting or dissolving a coast protection board . . . [^c6292431], shall make such provision (if any) as the Minister may require as to the transfer, compensation and pension rights of officers and servants of the authorities concerned.

(2) References in the last foregoing subsection to an order constituting a coast protection board . . . [^c6292431] shall be construed . . . [^c6292431] as including references to an order . . . [^c6292431] varying the constitution of such a board . . . [^c6292431]

(3) In this section the expression “**the authorities concerned**” means—

- (a) in relation to an order constituting a coast protection board . . . [^c6292431], the constituent or appointing authorities;
- (b) in relation to an order . . . [^c6292431] varying the constitution of such a board . . . [^c6292431], the board . . . [^c6292431], the constituent or appointing authorities, and any authority which on the variation taking effect will be one of the constituent or appointing authorities;
- (c) in relation to an order . . . [^c6292431] dissolving such a board . . . [^c6292431], the board . . . [^c6292431] and the constituent or appointing authorities;
- (d) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c6292551]
- (4)[^c6292561] This section shall not apply to Scotland.
