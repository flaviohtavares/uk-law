# Coast Protection Act 1949, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-05-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/74

## 24. Provisions as to arbitrations.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In arbitrations as to any question which under this Part of this Act is expressly required to be determined by arbitration, the reference shall be to the appropriate tribunal[^c-9e8d61b07fc0f0f20539703c2de2c340].

(2) Rules 2 to 4 of the Rules set out in section five of the[^c6292291] [^c6292301]Land Compensation Act 1961 (which provides rules for valuation on a compulsory acquisition) shall apply for the purposes of any such arbitration.

(3) For the purposes of any such arbitration, section four of the said Act of 1961 (which relates to costs) shall have effect with the substitution for the references to the acquiring authority of references to the coast protection authority *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8c5a62cbc7a18eab11dcf5c3562c25a2] coastal erosion risk management authority *(Extent: E+W)* .

(4) In subsection (1) “the appropriate tribunal” means—[^c-9feebf181734d66bd62f8a3fa1b1120a]

- (a) in the application of this Act to England and Wales, the Upper Tribunal;
- (b) in the application of this Act to Scotland, the Lands Tribunal for Scotland.
