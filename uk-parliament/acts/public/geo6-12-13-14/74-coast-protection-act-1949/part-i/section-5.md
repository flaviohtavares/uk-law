# Coast Protection Act 1949, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-05-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/74
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Objections to, and approval of, proposals to carry out coast protection work.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act as to works schemes, a coast protection authority *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-434a7f28705104288ce51101310c91ce] coastal erosion risk management authority *(Extent: E+W)* proposing to carry out any coast protection work, other than work of maintenance or repair, shall publish in one or more local newspapers circulating in the area of the authority *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7fc12349b12522bc228feb86e5feec6d] area in which the proposed work is to take place *(Extent: E+W)* and in such other manner (if any) as may be prescribed by regulations made by the Minister or as the Minister may in any particular case direct, such notice of that proposal, including an estimate of the cost of the work and containing a sufficient indication of the effect of subsection (3) of this section, as may be so prescribed or as the Minister may so direct, and shall serve a like notice on any[^c6289451] river authority[^c6289461] or other drainage authority in whose area on the National Rivers Authority and on any internal drainage board in whose district *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c30b516f27a5497b2f0e325cf272bea5]any of the work is to be carried out *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c30b516f27a5497b2f0e325cf272bea5] on the persons listed in subsection (1A) *(Extent: E+W)*.

*Extent: E+W*

(1A)[^c-2fd82501c87b696763c16a823fdde3a3] The notice shall be served—

- (a) on any internal drainage board in whose district any of the work is to be carried out, and
- (b) in the case of work proposed by a coast protection authority, on the appropriate agency[^c-668db1bc1da5f41424f74a68bd80078f].

(2) Where regulations made by the Minister so prescribe or the Minister in any particular case so directs, a like notice shall be served on such other authorities or persons as may be specified in the regulations or direction.

(3) Where a notice has been published by an authority under subsection (1) of this section, any person may serve on the Minister and on the authority *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cfb85277ff9c370541f60796de2a330f] , the authority proposing to carry out the work and, in the case of work proposed by a coast protection authority, the *(Extent: E+W)* appropriate agency[^c-ba1c6b1cc6484ddf9d1ac1d84f8760d6] *(Extent: E+W)*, in the manner and within the period prescribed by regulations made by the Minister, notice of objection to the proposal.

(4) Where notice of objection has been served under the last foregoing subsection and not withdrawn, and the ground of objection is that the proposed work will be detrimental to the protection of any land specified in the notice, or will interfere with the exercise by the objector of his functions under any enactment other than this Act, the Minister shall either cause a local inquiry to be held or shall give to the objector and to all other persons appearing to him to be affected by the proposal an opportunity of being heard by a person appointed by him for the purpose; and after considering the report of the person appointed to hold the inquiry or to hear objections, the Minister shall determine the objection:

[^c6289481]Provided that—

- (a) where the objection is made by a river authority or other drainage authority, fishery board (other than the Tweed Commissioners) or local fisheries committee, or by the harbour authority for a harbour to which the [^c6289501]Fishery Harbours Act 1915 applies, the powers of the Minister under this subsection shall be exercised jointly with the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food[^c6289511];
- (b) where the objection is made by the Tweed Commissioners, the said powers shall be exercised jointly with the Secretary of State; and
- (c)[^c6289521] where the objection is made by any other harbour authority, or by a conservancy authority, navigation authority or highway authority[^c6289531] or navigation authority, or by any of the Boards[^c6289551], the said powers shall be exercised jointly with the the Secretary of State.[^c6289561]

(5) In the case of work proposed by a coast protection authority, *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-558ac966a5ea33f508eda8bcf367d842] As soon as may be after the time for serving notices of objection under subsection (3) of this section has expired the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_fcc4d079-cb97-4636-c3d1-ef6bc91993c6] appropriate agency[^c-83b82f62a826a1a09e291922687f08f2] shall either approve the proposal or direct the authority not to carry out the proposed work or impose such modifications of the proposal or such conditions as to the carrying out of the work as he may think fit, having regard to the determination of objections as aforesaid.

*Extent: E+W*

(5A)[^c-f9c61f8abcf4a863cf83038bad982330] In the case of work proposed by the Environment Agency or the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c-0e8a799b5544452dd6ecde1aa5df02aa], the Agency or[^c-628011e1780574140ec38b370ab07c97] the Body —

- (a) may not begin the work before the time for serving notices of objection has expired and any objections have been determined by the Minister, and
- (b) must have regard to any determination of the Minister in deciding whether to carry out the proposed work, with or without modification.

(6) Nothing in this section shall prevent a coast protection authority *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0abe383812be4fd89277b093a172aed] coastal erosion risk management authority *(Extent: E+W)* from carrying out, without the provisions of this section having been complied with, any coast protection work which appears to them to be urgently necessary for the protection of any land in their area; but where work is so carried out in the area of any[^c6289571] river authority or other drainage authority and the coast protection authority is not a board on which the drainage authority are represented, the coast protection authority shall *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0cb770216ef8d03e764e073e76f22384] the authority shall *(Extent: E+W)* give to the drainage authority, before or as soon as possible after the commencement of the work, notice of the nature of the work.the coast protection authority shall, before or as soon as possible after the commencement of the work, give notice of the nature of the work —

- (a) to the appropriate agency[^c-3c8a1b89e3bb7c9501e8ad50cf2f53c6] where it is not represented on the coast protection authority *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7db9e3bb7c5d8ff2925f6743ae43e5ae] (in the case of work carried out by a coast protection authority) *(Extent: E+W)*; and
- (b) to any internal drainage board which are not so represented and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6c6a199b44158c1c19c475f0bbcc163] in whose district the work is to be or has been carried out.

(7) In the application of this section to Scotland, subsection (4) shall have effect as if in the proviso for the words from “by a river authority” to “any other harbour authority” there were substituted the words “by a harbour authority other than the authority for a harbour principally used by the fishing industry”, and as if for the words “the said powers” there were substituted the words “the powers of the Minister under this subsection”.
