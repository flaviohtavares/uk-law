# Coast Protection Act 1949, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-05-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/74
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Power of coast protection authorities to require information as to ownership of land.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A coast protection authority *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-140de8dfee2ed548bbbb2e5815d46b2e] coastal erosion risk management authority *(Extent: E+W)* may, for the purpose of enabling them to perform any of their functions under this Part of this Act, require the occupier of any land, and any person who either directly or indirectly receives rent in respect of any land, to state in writing the nature of his own interest therein and the name and address of any other person known to him as having an interest therein, whether as freeholder, mortgagee, owner, creditor in a heritable security, lessee or otherwise.

(2) Any person who having been required by a coast protection authority *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d148f0bb4a2d1c0f48cc3632c6e75ac4] coastal erosion risk management authority *(Extent: E+W)* in pursuance of this section to give to them any information fails to give that information or knowingly makes any misstatement in respect thereof shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c6292341].
