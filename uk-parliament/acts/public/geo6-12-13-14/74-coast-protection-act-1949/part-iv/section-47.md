# Coast Protection Act 1949, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-05-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/74

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Savings.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Act or in any order made thereunder shall—

- (a) affect the powers conferred on the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-84b21c3ed6c07cf2f7aaefe4b02491b0] Secretary of State[^c6294111] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-84b21c3ed6c07cf2f7aaefe4b02491b0] under the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-84b21c3ed6c07cf2f7aaefe4b02491b0] [^c6294121]Dockyard Ports Regulation Act 1865; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-84b21c3ed6c07cf2f7aaefe4b02491b0]
- (b) affect any right conferred by or in accordance with[^c6294131] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5fa346fc5cb9d607a93d4d591b9e67f] the electronic communications code on the operator of an electronic communications code network[^key-1f22c0070e8b1dd747938daff7d55fa4] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5fa346fc5cb9d607a93d4d591b9e67f] or any duty of any such operator under that code; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5fa346fc5cb9d607a93d4d591b9e67f]
- (c)[^c6294141] authorise or require any person—
  - (i) to carry out any work of alteration, improvement, repair, maintenance, demolition or removal on any works constructed or maintainable by a river authority[^c6294151] or other drainage authority, or
  - (ii) to carry out any work on land on which the sowing or planting of vegetation is carried out or vegetation is maintained by a river authority or other drainage authority,

  unless the river authority or other authority consents or the work is to be done by, or under a scheme prepared by, a coast protection board on which the river authority or other authority is represented and is to be carried out in the area of that coast protection board;
- (c) authorise or require any person—
  - (i) to carry out any work of alteration, improvement, repair, maintenance, demolition or removal on any works constructed or maintainable by the Environment Agency, the Natural Resources Body for Wales or an[^key-53e3dbe77046bd02f45bdbf342d6d96c] internal drainage board, or
  - (ii) to carry out any work on land on which the sowing or planting of vegetation is carried out or vegetation is maintained by the Environment Agency, the Natural Resources Body for Wales or an[^key-f98e014f8ebea15851ef10bac45d32c3] internal drainage board,

  unless the Environment Agency, the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^key-d6d3a89ee793292d1cebb1b81591d17e] or the internal drainage board consents or the work is done by, or under a scheme prepared by, a coast protection board on which the Environment Agency, the Natural Resources Body for Wales or the internal drainage board is represented[^key-cf4fb11e9c77537a402b22b8efcf488d] and is to be carried out in the area of that coast protection board;
- (d) authorise or require any person to carry out any work or do anything in contravention of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-da5ef2c2e303ea4687ff8b976d59855d] the Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979[^c6294201] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-da5ef2c2e303ea4687ff8b976d59855d]; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-da5ef2c2e303ea4687ff8b976d59855d]
- (e) authorise or require any person to carry out any work of alteration, improvement, repair, maintenance, demolition or removal on any works constructed for the drainage of agricultural land in Scotland, unless the Secretary of State consents.
