# Coast Protection Act 1949, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-05-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/74
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Penalties for offences.[^c6293731][^c6293721]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Save as hereinbefore expressly provided, any person committing an offence under any provision of this Act shall be liable on summary conviction, in the case of a first offence under that provision, to a fine not exceeding ten pounds[^c6293741] level 3 on the standard scale and, in the case of a second or any subsequent offence thereunder, to a fine not exceeding fifty pounds level 3 on the standard scale.
