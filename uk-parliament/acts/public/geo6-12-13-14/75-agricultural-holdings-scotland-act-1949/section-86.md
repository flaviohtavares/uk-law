# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1949, section 86

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 86. Application of Act to Crown land.[^c641599][^c641598]

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-09-25).**

(1)[^c641600] This Act shall apply to land belonging to His majesty in right of the Crown, subject to such modifications as may be prescribed; and for the purposes of this Act the Commissioners of Crown Lands or other the proper officer or body having charge of the land for the time being, or if there is no such officer or body, such person as His Majesty may appoint in writing under the Royal Sign Manual, shall represent His Majesty and shall be deemed to be the landlord.

(2)[^c641600] Without prejudice to the provisions of the foregoing subsection it is hereby declared that the provisions of this Act apply to land notwithstanding that the interest of the landlord or the tenant thereof belongs to a government department or is held on behalf of His Majesty for the purposes of any government department; but in their application to any land belonging, or an interest in which is held as aforesaid, the said provisions shall have effect subject to such modifications as may be prescribed.
