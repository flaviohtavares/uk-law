# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1949, FOURTH SCHEDULE

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## FOURTH SCHEDULE — Market garden improvements for which compensation may be payable

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-09-25).**

Sections 65, 66, 79, 86.

### 1.

Planting of standard or other fruit trees permanently set out.

### 2.

Planting of fruit bushes permanently set out.

### 3.

Planting of strawberry plants.

### 4.

Planting os asparagus, rhubarb, and other vegetable crops which continue productive for two or more years.

### 5.

Erection, alteration or enlargement of buildings for the purpose of the trade or business of a market gardener.
