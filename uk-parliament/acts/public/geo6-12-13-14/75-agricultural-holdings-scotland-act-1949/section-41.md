# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1949, section 41

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 41. Conditions attaching to right to compensation for repairs to buildings.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-09-25).**

Compensation under this Act shall not be payable in respect of any such repairs as are specified in paragraph 29 of the Second Schedule to this Act or in paragraph 29 of the Third Schedule thereto unless, before beginning to execute any such repairs, the tenant gave to the landlord notice in writing under paragraph (29) of the First Schedule to the [^c641540] Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act, 1923, or under paragraph (30) of the First Schedule to the Small Landholders and Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act, 1931, of his intention to execute the repairs, together with particulars thereof, and the landlord failed to exercise the right conferred on him by the said paragraph (29) or the said paragraph (30) to execute the repairs himself within a reasonable time after receiving the notice.
