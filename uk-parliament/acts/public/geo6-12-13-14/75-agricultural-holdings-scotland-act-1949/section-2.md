# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1949, section 2

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2. Restriction on letting agricultural land for less than from year to year.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-09-25).**

(1)[^c641458] Subject to the provisions of this section, where under a lease entered into on or after the first day of November, nineteen hundred and forty-eight, any land is let to a person for use as agricultural land for a shorter period than from year to year, and the circumstances are such that if he were a tenant from year to year he would in respect of that land be the tenant of an agricultural holding, then, unless the letting was approved by the Secretary of State before the lease was entered into, the lease shall take effect, with the necessary modifications, as if it were a lease of the land from year to year:

Provided that this subsection shall not have effect in relation to a lease of land entered into (whether or not the lease expressly so provides) in contemplation of the use of the land only for grazing or mowing during some specified period of the year, or in relation to a lease of land granted by a person whose interest in the land is that of a tenant under a lease which is for a shorter period than from year to year and which has not by virtue of this section taken effect as a lease from year to year.

(2) Any question arising as to the operation of the foregoing subsection in relation to any lease shall be determined by arbitration.
