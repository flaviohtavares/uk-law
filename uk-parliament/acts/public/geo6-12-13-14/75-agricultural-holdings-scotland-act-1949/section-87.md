# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1949, section 87

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 87. Determination of matters relating to holdings of which the Secretary of State is landlord or tenant.[^c641604][^c641603]

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-09-25).**

(1) Any section of this Act under which any matter is referred to the decision of the Secretary of State shall, in its application to an agricultural holding of which the Secretary of State is himself the landlord or the tenant, have effect with the substitution of the Land Court for the Secretary of State, and any provision in any such section for an appeal to an arbiter from the decision of the Secretary of State shall not apply.

(2)[^c641605] The provisions of this Act shall, in their application to any arbitration with regard to an agricultural holding of which the Secretary of State is himself the landlord or the tenant, have effect with the substitution of the Land Court for the Secretary of State.
