# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1949, section 95

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 95. Construction of references in other Acts to holdings as defined by the Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act, 1923.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-09-25).**

(1) References, in whatever terms, in any enactment, other than an enactment contained in this Act, in the [^c641620][^c641621] Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Acts, 1923 and 1931, or in Part I of the Agriculture (Scotland) Act, 1948, to a holding within the meaning of the Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act, 1923, or of the [^c641622] Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Acts, 1923 to 1948, shall be construed as references to an agricultural holding as defined by section one of this Act.

(2) The foregoing subsection shall not apply to an enactment in so far as its operation is material for the purposes of the provisions of the said Acts of 1923 and 1931 or the said Act of 1948 to the extent to which they are excepted from the repeal of enactments effected by this Act.
