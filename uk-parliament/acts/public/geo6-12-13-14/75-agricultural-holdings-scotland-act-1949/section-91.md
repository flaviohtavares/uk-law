# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1949, section 91

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 91. Prohibition of appeal from sheriff substitute.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-09-25).**

Where any jurisdiction committed by this Act to the sheriff is exercised by the sheriff substitute, there shall be no appeal to the sheriff.
