# Energy Act 2023, section 251

> Text as at 2026-09-30.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 251. Energy performance regulations relating to new premises[^key-225c909ee0d885513206f088aca3ef89]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The power to make energy performance regulations is exercisable in relation to new premises.

(2) Accordingly, in section 250 (except in the definition of “premises” in section 250(5))—

- (a) a reference to premises includes new premises;
- (b) a reference to the energy usage and energy efficiency of premises includes the anticipated energy usage and energy efficiency of new premises.

(3) In this section “**new premises**” means premises—

- (a) which are being constructed or adapted, or
- (b) which it is proposed to construct or adapt.
