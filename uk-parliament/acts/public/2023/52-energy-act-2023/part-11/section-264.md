# Energy Act 2023, section 264

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 264. Directions to scheme administrators[^key-e31398d4517232be35c4d7d99e460600]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may give directions to a scheme administrator.

(2) The power to give directions under this section includes a power to vary or revoke the directions.

(3) A scheme administrator must comply with any direction given to it under this section.
