# Energy Act 2023, section 7

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Power to grant licences[^key-bee99b7ee4a1978d72793bf4e7dad72b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The economic regulator may grant a licence authorising a person to carry on—

- (a) activities falling within section 2(2)(a);
- (b) activities falling within section 2(2)(b).

(2) A licence—

- (a) must be in writing;
- (b) is to continue in force for such period as may be specified in or determined by or under the licence, unless previously revoked in accordance with any term of the licence.
