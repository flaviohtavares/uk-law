# Energy Act 2023, section 4

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Territorial scope of prohibition[^key-440adc411335a83ad50b160c047966b0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 2(1) applies to activities in, above or below—

- (a) the territorial sea adjacent to the United Kingdom, or
- (b) waters in a Gas Importation and Storage Zone (within the meaning given by section 1 of the Energy Act 2008),

as it applies to activities in the United Kingdom.
