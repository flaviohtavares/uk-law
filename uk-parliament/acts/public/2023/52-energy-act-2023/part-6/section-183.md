# Energy Act 2023, section 183

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 183. Meaning of “code manager” and “code manager licence”[^c-f09d153708334930fccf128925036c4f][^c-330878f032b46b549f1eded6cf054074]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this Part, “**code manager**”, in relation to a designated document, means the holder of a code manager licence in respect of the document.

(2) In this Part, “**code manager licence**” means a licence under section 7AC of the Gas Act 1986 or section 6(1)(g) of the Electricity Act 1989.

(3) See sections 185 and 186, which contain amendments to the Gas Act 1986 and the Electricity Act 1989 in relation to the licences mentioned in subsection (2).
