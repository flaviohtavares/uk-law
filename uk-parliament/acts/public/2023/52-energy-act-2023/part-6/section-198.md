> Text as at 2026-09-30.

## 198. Regulations under Part 6[^key-2244b0ab22b58744c7b6b648d702a339][^key-fc325b4eec2ec2859186073658f8dbe8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Regulations under this Part are subject to the negative procedure, subject to subsection (2).

(2) Regulations under section 191 are subject to the affirmative procedure.
