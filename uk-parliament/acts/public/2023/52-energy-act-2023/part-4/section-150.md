# Energy Act 2023, section 150

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 150. Appeals[^c-040feb429868a2c3281d33206e52c046]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Scheme regulations that, by virtue of section 148(3), provide that a person is liable to a financial penalty must also provide for a person to have a right of appeal to a court or tribunal against the imposition of the penalty.

(2) Scheme regulations may make provision about appeals against—

- (a) decisions made by the administrator of a low-carbon heat scheme, and
- (b) penalties imposed (other than financial penalties) or enforcement action taken for failure to comply with a requirement imposed by or under a low-carbon heat scheme.

(3) Scheme regulations that make provision by virtue of subsection (2) must specify the court, tribunal or person who is to hear and determine an appeal made by virtue of that subsection.
