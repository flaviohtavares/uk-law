# Energy Act 2023, section 125

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 125. Appeals[^key-e0d581eb5d11e5bc788e09dc122894d5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 8—

- (a) Part 1 contains provision about appeals against decisions by the OGA relating to the preparation of an information and samples plan and appeals against the giving of a notice under section 112, and
- (b) Part 2 contains provision about appeals against the imposition of sanction notices and appeals against the giving of a notice under section 124.
