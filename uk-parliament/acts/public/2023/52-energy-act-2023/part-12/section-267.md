# Energy Act 2023, section 267

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 267. General objective[^key-b5a33ea9c47f83c03fab57f91596108c][^key-14aab415f8660b6d8b6b9de0971723c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The functions of the Secretary of State under this Part must be exercised with a view to—

- (a) ensuring that economic activity in the United Kingdom is not adversely affected by disruptions to core fuel sector activities, and
- (b) reducing the risk of emergencies affecting fuel supplies.
