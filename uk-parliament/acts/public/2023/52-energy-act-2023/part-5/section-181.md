# Energy Act 2023, section 181

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 181. Regulations under Part 5[^c-e5db939d5eddb16f1467fa66dcbffc03]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Regulations under this Part are subject to the negative procedure.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to regulations under paragraph 9 of Schedule 9.
