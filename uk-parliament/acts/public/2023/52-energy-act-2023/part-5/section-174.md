## 174. Transfers[^key-645fd5faf5d0be5a309949ace19d2966]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 9 contains—

- (a) in Part 1, provision about transfer schemes relating to the ISOP;
- (b) in Part 2, related provision about transfers and other provision in connection with the designation of a person as the ISOP.
