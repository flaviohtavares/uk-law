# Energy Act 2023, section 173

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 173. Duty to keep developments in energy sector under review[^c-3f4e2bc80498306623ea806519e5bcde][^c-4e7fc2e53c86e73462d97a79f73db533]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The ISOP must keep under review developments relating to the energy sector that may be relevant to the carrying out of any of the ISOP’s functions.
