# Finance (No. 2) Act 2023, section 325

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 325. Reform of HGV road user levy

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 22 makes provision (including consequential provision) about—

- (a) the charging of HGV road user levy in respect of UK-registered and non-UK registered heavy goods vehicles,
- (b) the register of HGV road user levy paid or due to be paid, and
- (c) the rate of HGV road user levy chargeable in respect of a heavy goods vehicle by reference to the vehicle’s revenue weight.
