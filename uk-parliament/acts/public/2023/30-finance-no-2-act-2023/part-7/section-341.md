# Finance (No. 2) Act 2023, section 341

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 341. Temporary approvals etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 16B of FA 1994 (temporary approvals etc pending review or appeal: process) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (3), for paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) expires—
>   - (i) on the expiry day determined in accordance with subsection (4), or
>   - (ii) if HMRC are satisfied that it is appropriate in all the circumstances, on a later day determined by HMRC, and

.

(3) In subsection (4), for “The day on which a temporary approval expires is” substitute “For the purposes of subsection (3)(b)(i), the expiry day in relation to a temporary approval is”.
