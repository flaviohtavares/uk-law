# Finance (No. 2) Act 2023, section 72

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Exemption: production for personal consumption[^M_I_d564ec1a-cf1a-41e0-e487-9a7958324cb0][^key-cbe2883f5132518432647c619c77b872]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Alcohol duty is not charged on alcoholic products which—

- (a) are produced, in the United Kingdom, by a person who produces alcoholic products only for the person’s own domestic use, and
- (b) are not spirits.
