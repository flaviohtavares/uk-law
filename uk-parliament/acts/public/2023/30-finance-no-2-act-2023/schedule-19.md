> **22A. Revocation in the public interest**
>
> (1) The Secretary of State may decide to revoke the application of an anti-dumping amount or a countervailing amount to goods in the absence of a recommendation from the TRA where the Secretary of State considers that it is in the public interest to do so.

> - (d) is required under section 13 to make provision by public notice to give effect to the decision.
