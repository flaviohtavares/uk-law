> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Standard small profits rate and fraction for financial year 2024

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of Part 3A of CTA 2010, for the financial year 2024—

- (a) the standard small profits rate is 19%, and
- (b) the standard marginal relief fraction is 3/200ths.
