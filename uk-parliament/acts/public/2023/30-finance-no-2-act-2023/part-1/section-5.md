# Finance (No. 2) Act 2023, section 5

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Charge and main rate for financial year 2024

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Corporation tax is charged for the financial year 2024.

(2) The main rate of corporation tax for that year is 25%.
