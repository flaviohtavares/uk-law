# Finance (No. 2) Act 2023, section 4

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Freezing starting rate limit for savings for tax year 2023-24

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the tax year 2023-24 the amount specified in section 12(3) of ITA 2007 (the starting rate limit for savings) is “£5,000”.

(2) Accordingly, section 21 of that Act (indexation) does not apply in relation to the starting rate limit for savings for that tax year.
