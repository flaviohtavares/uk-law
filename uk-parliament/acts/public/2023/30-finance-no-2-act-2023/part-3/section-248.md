# Finance (No. 2) Act 2023, section 248

> Text as at 2026-07-15.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 248. Exclusion of indirect interests held through ultimate parent

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of determining whether an entity or individual[^c-82ce9503f373b75156dfb77101241dbf] has an indirect ownership interest in a member of a multinational group (other than the ultimate parent), ignore any indirect interests arising only as a result of an ownership interest in the ultimate parent.
