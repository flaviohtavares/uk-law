# Finance (No. 2) Act 2023, section 122

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 122. Chargeable persons

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person is chargeable to multinational top-up tax for an accounting period of a multinational group if the group is a qualifying multinational group in that period and—

- (a) the person—
  - (i) is a [^c-827dd43ff6640d6c71333722eda357d0]... member of the multinational group at any time in that period,
  - (ii) is a body corporate or a partnership [^c-a3721dc3266a003329717c5df9ab6a1a]..., and
  - (iii) is located in the United Kingdom, or
- (b) the person is chargeable to tax in respect of an entity that is a [^c-827dd43ff6640d6c71333722eda357d0]... member of the multinational group at any time in that period.

(2) A person is chargeable to tax in respect of a [^c-a56aa63f6dd0c45fd4c0ff4e3ca9cd91]... member of a multinational group if—

- (a) the profits of the [^c-a56aa63f6dd0c45fd4c0ff4e3ca9cd91]... member would, on the relevant assumptions, be the profits of the person for the purposes of income tax or corporation tax,
- (b) the [^c-a56aa63f6dd0c45fd4c0ff4e3ca9cd91]... member is located in the United Kingdom, and
- (c) the [^c-a56aa63f6dd0c45fd4c0ff4e3ca9cd91]... member is not—
  - (i) a body corporate, or
  - (ii) a partnership [^c-f8c631bde7cd1deb80a97eca1722a941]....

(3) The relevant assumptions are—

- (a) that the [^c-14717058ea1c75f86b5f3707aac82e45]... member has profits that are chargeable to income tax or corporation tax, and
- (b) that the person is resident in the United Kingdom for the purposes of that tax.

(5)[^c-ac40f8b205f2520d4e962c7de46c979e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c-ac40f8b205f2520d4e962c7de46c979e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) Where more than one person is chargeable to tax in relation to the same [^c-ee3249c8f884fdfc387d580f8230738f]... member of a qualifying multinational group as a result of the application of subsection (2), each of those persons is jointly and severally liable to multinational top-up tax.
