# Finance (No. 2) Act 2023, section 153

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 153. Exclusion of certain insurance reserve movement expense

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The underlying profits of a member of a multinational group that is an insurance company are to be adjusted so as to exclude any expense resulting from the movement of its insurance reserves where the movement is economically matched by excluded dividends falling within section 141(2)(b)[^c-35affbcf8f7a31cce12832ad911ffa50] (ignoring the extent to which those dividends also reflect any investment management fees).

(2) The underlying profits of a member of a multinational group that is an insurance company are to be adjusted so as to exclude any expense resulting from the movement of its insurance reserves where the movement is economically matched by an excluded equity gain or loss.
