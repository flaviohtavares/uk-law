# Finance (No. 2) Act 2023, section 238

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 238. Tax transparency of entities

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An entity is regarded as tax transparent in a territory to the extent that[^c-e3bd8e71b7286a853b58eb70b3bf4def] the territory treats the income, expenditure, profits or[^c-169d660aa0229ffbee993cf73cb67d7b] losses of the entity, for the purposes of covered taxes, as the income, expenditure, profits or losses of the direct owner of the entity in proportion to its interest in the entity.
