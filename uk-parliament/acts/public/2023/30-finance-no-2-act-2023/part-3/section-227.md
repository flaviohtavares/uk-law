# Finance (No. 2) Act 2023, section 227

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 227. Application of Part to joint venture groups

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies to a joint venture group as it applies to a multinational group, but in their application by virtue of this subsection[^key-ce69afa3afd42443902683e9e6fbf4a8] Chapters 3 to 6 and 8 of this Part , this Chapter other than this section and section 226[^key-db95ee69fde4364c2a79b41c745f319e] and Schedules 16 and 16A[^key-5138ab9297861a20724ea0ed54a88b30] have effect[^key-0b912db138d720a4981ae9562c41d37b] as if—

- (a) references to the ultimate parent were to the joint venture parent of that group,
- (b) references to a member of a multinational group were to the members of the joint venture group, and.
- (c) references to the filing member were to the filing member of each respective multinational[^key-4ca3308622cf8c703933ec203683fe70] group whose ultimate parent holds at least 50% of the ownership interests in the joint venture parent.

(2) For the purposes of the other provisions of this Part (in its application by virtue of subsection (1)),[^key-b0cfa3c49752ecbb6ffe49b550acbfed] the members of the joint venture group are treated as members of each multinational group[^key-1aac2f3a1c5500672322c603f0541ae0] whose ultimate parent directly or indirectly holds at least 50% of the ownership interests in the joint venture parent.

(3) But (in the application of this Part by virtue of subsection (1))[^key-67e1317180a711fcf5be725b7af26f99] no member of the joint venture group is to be regarded as an intermediate parent member or a partially owned parent member of the joint venture[^key-fe113a117c1682e2acdb7a40cb61985b] group.
