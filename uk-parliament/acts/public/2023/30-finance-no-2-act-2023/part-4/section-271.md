# Finance (No. 2) Act 2023, section 271

> Text as at 2026-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 271. Election to make one member of a group liable for amounts charged

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where multiple members of a group are chargeable to domestic top-up tax in an accounting period, the filing member of the group may elect that only one member of the group specified in the election (the “elected[^c-631d12d2247db4c88aac28360f8adacb] member”) is to be liable to pay domestic top-up tax in that period.

(2) Where an election under this section is made—

- (a) no member of the group other than the elected[^c-95cf259c43c0569d8a5f10e368985374] member is required to pay any amount by way of domestic top-up tax, and
- (b) the elected member must pay any amount by way of domestic top-up tax any other member of the group would have been required to pay if the election had not been made.

(3) Subsection (2) does not apply if the elected[^c-421ee89738ddd4f7e07f7ee0914abfc7] member has not consented to the election.

(4) Paragraph 2 of Schedule 15 (annual elections) applies to an election under this section, and has effect for that purpose as if references to an information return or overseas return notification were to a self-assessment return or below-threshold notification.
