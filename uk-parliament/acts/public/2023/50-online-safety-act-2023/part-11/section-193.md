# Online Safety Act 2023, section 193

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 193. OFCOM’s guidance about illegal content judgements[^key-c14ed0625f17d13a112dc5d61e6c8cdd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-c14ed0625f17d13a112dc5d61e6c8cdd] OFCOM must produce guidance for providers of Part 3 services about the matters dealt with in section 192 so far as relating to illegal content judgements.

(2) “**Illegal content judgements**” means—

- (a)[^key-c14ed0625f17d13a112dc5d61e6c8cdd] judgements of a kind mentioned in subsection (4) of that section, and
- (b) judgements by providers about whether news publisher content amounts to a relevant offence (see section 18(5) and (10)).

(3)[^key-c14ed0625f17d13a112dc5d61e6c8cdd] Before producing the guidance (including revised or replacement guidance), OFCOM must consult such persons as they consider appropriate.

(4)[^key-c14ed0625f17d13a112dc5d61e6c8cdd] OFCOM must publish the guidance (and any revised or replacement guidance).
