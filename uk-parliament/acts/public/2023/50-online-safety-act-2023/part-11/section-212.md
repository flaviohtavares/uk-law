## 212. Repeals: Digital Economy Act 2017[^c-ced08207dcf4115e4f4b0e3feb8e14ac]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Digital Economy Act 2017 is amended as follows.

(2) Omit—

- (a) Part 3 (online pornography), and
- (b) section 119(10) and (11) (power to extend that Part to the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man).

(3) Omit section 103 (code of practice for providers of online social media platforms).
