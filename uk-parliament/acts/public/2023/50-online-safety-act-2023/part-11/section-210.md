# Online Safety Act 2023, section 210

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 210. Repeal of Part 4B of the Communications Act[^c-6163bfe1e41056e014e66cdec81a5de4][^c-a00cfcfc48ec81f28c94a78f181edde1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the Communications Act, omit Part 4B (video-sharing platform services).

(2) In the Audiovisual Media Services Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/1062](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1062)), omit Part 4 (which inserts Part 4B into the Communications Act).

(3) In this Act, omit—

- (a) section 209, and
- (b) Schedule 16.

(4) In the Audiovisual Media Services (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/1536](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1536)), omit regulation 4.
