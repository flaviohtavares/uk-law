## 223. Power to make consequential provision[^key-6c3e4984be71092ede48cf9bc1314420]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision that is consequential on this Act or regulations under this Act.

(2) The regulations may—

- (a) amend or repeal provision made by the Communications Act;
- (b) amend or revoke provision made under that Act.

(3) The regulations may make transitional, transitory or saving provision.
