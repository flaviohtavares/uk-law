# Online Safety Act 2023, section 189

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 189. Repeals in connection with offences under sections 179 and 181[^c-eb11711206f40291ae5e95427612457d][^c-f8f53342d4b6d17638885f755ca61c31]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) Section 127(2)(a) and (b) of the Communications Act (false messages) is repealed so far as it extends to England and Wales and Northern Ireland.

(2) The following provisions of the Malicious Communications Act 1988 are repealed—

- (a) section 1(1)(a)(ii),
- (b) section 1(1)(a)(iii), and
- (c) section 1(2).

(3) The following provisions of the Malicious Communications (Northern Ireland) Order 1988 ([S.I. 1988/1849 (N.I. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1988/1849)) are repealed—

- (a) Article 3(1)(a)(ii),
- (b) Article 3(1)(a)(iii), and
- (c) Article 3(2).
