# Online Safety Act 2023, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-11-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Scope of duties about regulated provider pornographic content[^c-3f6f7b3f348c584fd604673e79c17f1b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-76f821c735b62b3e96edd025628a9a3f] A provider of an internet service within subsection (2) must comply with the duties set out in section 81 in relation to the service.

(2)[^c-76f821c735b62b3e96edd025628a9a3f] An internet service is within this subsection if—

- (a) regulated provider pornographic content is published or displayed on the service,
- (b) the service is not exempt, and
- (c) the service has links with the United Kingdom.

(3)[^c-76f821c735b62b3e96edd025628a9a3f] A service is “**exempt**” for the purposes of this Part if it is —

- (a) a user-to-user service or a search service of a description that is exempt as provided for by Schedule 1, or
- (b) an internet service of a kind described in Schedule 9.

(4)[^c-3f6f7b3f348c584fd604673e79c17f1b] A service “**has links with the United Kingdom**” for the purposes of this Part if either of the following conditions is met in relation to the service—

- (a) the service has a significant number of United Kingdom users, or
- (b) United Kingdom users form one of the target markets for the service (or the only target market).

(5)[^c-76f821c735b62b3e96edd025628a9a3f] This Part does not apply in relation to a part of a regulated service if—

- (a) in the case of a Part 3 service, the conditions in paragraph 7(2) of Schedule 1 (internal business service conditions) are met in relation to that part;
- (b) in the case of an internet service other than a Part 3 service, the conditions in paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 9 (internal business service conditions) are met in relation to that part.

(6)[^c-76f821c735b62b3e96edd025628a9a3f] This Part does not apply in relation to a part of a regulated service if that part is an on-demand programme service or a non-UK on-demand programme service that is a Tier 1 service[^c-2b3ccb991ac9dc7931b5221f180b10d7] [^c-17c66a6518e14da8677718b586e0332f]....

(6A)[^c-e803c5f0380a1a19a4e43930823bb3da] In subsection (6), “**on-demand programme service**”, “**non-UK on-demand programme service**” and “**Tier 1 service**” have the same meaning as in the Communications Act (see sections 368A, 368AA and 368HA of that Act).

(7)[^c-76f821c735b62b3e96edd025628a9a3f] If a person is the provider of more than one internet service within subsection (2), the duties set out in section 81 apply in relation to each such service.

(8)[^c-76f821c735b62b3e96edd025628a9a3f] The duties set out in section 81 extend only to the design, operation and use of an internet service in the United Kingdom.
