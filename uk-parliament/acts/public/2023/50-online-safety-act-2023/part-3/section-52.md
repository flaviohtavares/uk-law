# Online Safety Act 2023, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. OFCOM’s guidance about certain duties in Part 3[^key-7597fd911096fea618d18431ad51906a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-22cb88888d3b29363aa04d20eb612ad0] OFCOM must produce guidance for providers of Category 1 services to assist them in complying with their duties set out in section 14 (assessments related to the adult user empowerment duty set out in section 15(2)).

(2)[^key-7597fd911096fea618d18431ad51906a][^key-22cb88888d3b29363aa04d20eb612ad0] OFCOM must produce guidance for providers of Category 1 services to assist them in complying with their duties set out in section 18 (news publisher content).

(3)[^key-7597fd911096fea618d18431ad51906a] OFCOM must produce guidance for providers of Part 3 services to assist them in complying with—

- (a) their duties set out in section 23 or 34, except the duty set out in section 23(10) or 34(9) (record-keeping and review), and
- (b) their duties set out in section 36 (children’s access assessments).

(4)[^key-7597fd911096fea618d18431ad51906a] Before producing guidance under subsection (1) or (3) (including revised or replacement guidance), OFCOM must consult the Information Commission[^key-3ed58e54c90cbce005b97643d728edd1].

(5) OFCOM must publish guidance under this section (and any revised or replacement guidance).
