# Online Safety Act 2023, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-11-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. OFCOM’s guidance: content that is harmful to children and user empowerment[^c-cd8715288fe419dbcf2f88e2ba669fbd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-cd8715288fe419dbcf2f88e2ba669fbd] OFCOM must produce guidance for providers of Part 3 services which contains examples of content or kinds of content that OFCOM consider to be, or consider not to be—

- (a) primary priority content that is harmful to children, or
- (b) priority content that is harmful to children.

(2)[^c-039e115cbc39f52a25be1340ba3f03a4] OFCOM must produce guidance for providers of Category 1 services which contains examples of content or kinds of content that OFCOM consider to be, or consider not to be, content to which section 15(2) applies (see section 16).

(3)[^c-cd8715288fe419dbcf2f88e2ba669fbd] Before producing any guidance under this section (including revised or replacement guidance), OFCOM must consult such persons as they consider appropriate.

(4)[^c-cd8715288fe419dbcf2f88e2ba669fbd] OFCOM must publish guidance under this section (and any revised or replacement guidance).
