# Online Safety Act 2023, section 73

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. OFCOM’s guidance about duties set out in sections 71 and 72[^key-ab10a471f2b308b06837f45f487cc9e4][^key-09b5cd79b0a1cd435763bce11219d517]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) OFCOM must produce guidance for providers of Category 1 services to assist them in complying with their duties set out in sections 71 and 72(3) to (7).

(2) OFCOM must publish the guidance (and any revised or replacement guidance).
