# Online Safety Act 2023, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-11-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. NCA: information sharing[^c-0d4a5923c7d21163735a1bf1b2f87ea8][^c-6d66607432ef18749f560dfc740ca6d7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 16 of the Crime and Courts Act 2013 (interpretation of Part 1), in subsection (1), in the definition of “permitted purpose”, after paragraph (o) insert—

> - (oa) the exercise of any function of OFCOM (the Office of Communications) under the Online Safety Act 2023;

.
