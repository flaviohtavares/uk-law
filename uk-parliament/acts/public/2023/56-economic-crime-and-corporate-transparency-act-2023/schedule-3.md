# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 3 — Disclosure of information: consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 94

### 1. Companies Act 2006[^c-51a7940d1e21f066bbd93cfb7834d6de][^c-7a2e7d51826ce3bd18f0d771667ce563]

The Companies Act 2006 is amended as follows.

**2.**[^c-fa91502520b6dbfed0df53f600715f1d][^c-ba7bc791482604875a5a3ac93d5b4740] In section 242 (protected information: restriction on disclosure by registrar), in subsection (3)—

- (a) omit the “or” at the end of paragraph (a);
- (b) at the end of paragraph (b) insert

  > , or
  >
  > - (c) as permitted by section 1110F (general powers of disclosure by the registrar).

**3.**[^c-9103df9d119946f2a48be570959fb026][^c-cfbf348380435ece6bfd92daeea4b945] (1) Section 243 (permitted disclosure by the registrar) is amended as follows.

(2) For subsection (2) substitute—

> (2) The registrar may disclose protected information to a credit reference agency.

(3) In subsection (7), omit—

- (a) the definition of “public authority”;
- (b) the “and” before that definition.

### 4. Economic Crime (Transparency and Enforcement) Act 2022[^c-b866fff8a408ac39622e95984552c443][^c-573246f86d9d761a13917472350f1aea]

The Economic Crime (Transparency and Enforcement) Act 2022 is amended as follows.

**5.**[^c-7ac99967f8df099d4aa2c08966d354bb][^c-8491ab8d88d6468ad6634e78f0406ab0] In section 40 (sharing of information by HMRC), in subsection (1), omit “or the registrar”.

**6.**[^c-dde552dee9d4163f1702ec5742df59ae][^c-524f1c038177b738d321c605df8c256b] In section 44 (interpretation), in subsection (1), at the end of the definition of “document”, insert “and references to delivering a document are to be read in accordance with section 1114(1)(b) of the Companies Act 2006”.
