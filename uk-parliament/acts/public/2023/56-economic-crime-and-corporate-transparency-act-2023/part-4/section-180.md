# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 180

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 180. Cryptoassets: civil recovery[^key-9209d8c6063952ddd4e5802c33f0a512][^key-b6c8dc40a388db4cfa7c63b93be8d06d][^key-0b57859bf44820c0aef65b2723df8857][^key-a8c4ca91fe97a6772581f1c186805c4c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 9 amends the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 to make provision for a civil recovery regime in relation to cryptoassets.

(2) It also contains related amendments.
