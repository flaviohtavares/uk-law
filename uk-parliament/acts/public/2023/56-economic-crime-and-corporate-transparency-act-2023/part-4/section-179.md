# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 179

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 179. Cryptoassets: confiscation orders[^c-8eed268d9b045c0112cdb8403a5489a1][^c-21d5a71ea10bb357d47ce9ca2c01d31b][^c-c5d67fed80f0be04357d61a4390b8fdd][^c-052159e23dd01ac37f9cf27b603b02b3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 8 amends the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 to make provision in connection with cryptoassets and confiscation orders under Parts 2, 3 and 4 of that Act.
