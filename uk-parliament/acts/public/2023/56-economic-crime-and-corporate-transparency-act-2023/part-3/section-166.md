## 166. Material unavailable for public inspection: verification information[^c-6716f33a495238f98bc79f77f3124e92]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 16 of the Economic Crime (Transparency and Enforcement) Act 2022 (verification of registrable beneficial owners and managing officers), in subsection (2), after paragraph (c) insert—

> - (d) requiring the registrar not to make available for public inspection certain information delivered to the registrar by virtue of the regulations;
