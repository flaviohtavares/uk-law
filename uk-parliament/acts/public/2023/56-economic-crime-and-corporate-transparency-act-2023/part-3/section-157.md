# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 157

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 157. Required information about overseas entities: address information[^key-1e832150c3c9cb0b152d4dbc4a4a3136][^key-57f3c71bec4f4f657d962140afdf048f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the following provisions of Schedule 1 to the Economic Crime (Transparency and Enforcement) Act 2022 (which refer to an entity’s registered or principal office) omit “registered or”—

- paragraph 2(1)(c);
- paragraph 5(1)(b);
- paragraph 6(1)(d);
- paragraph 7(1)(b).
