# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 10

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Names containing computer code[^key-b2d05787b2dd2e14b68271394412692d][^key-31dacc85f8283f97d7d5d060c2c693d7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Companies Act 2006 is amended as follows.

(2) After section 57 insert—

> **Computer code**
>
> **57A. Names containing computer code**
>
> A company must not be registered under this Act by a name that, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, consists of or includes computer code.

(3) In section 1047 (registered name of overseas company), in subsection (4), after paragraph (ba) insert—

> - (bb) section 57A (names containing computer code);

.
