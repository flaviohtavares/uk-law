# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 60

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Duty to confirm lawful purposes[^key-8f566c993d0a9a03730bba97e0b6ff9b][^key-7cd7976496185d14c158e6ba973196c5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After section 853B of the Companies Act 2006 insert—

> **853BA. Duty to confirm lawful purpose**
>
> Where a company makes a confirmation statement it must at the same time deliver to the registrar a statement that the intended future activities of the company are lawful.
