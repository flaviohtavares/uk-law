# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 58

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Removal of option to abridge Companies Act accounts[^c-9f494c63a0c79d0aab09d02cbb08d9b0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 1 to the Small Companies and Groups (Accounts and Directors’ Report) Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/409](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/409)) (Companies Act individual accounts) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1(3), omit “Subject to paragraph 1A”.

(3) Omit paragraph 1A (abridged accounts).

(4) In paragraph 1B(2), omit “, otherwise than pursuant to paragraph 1A(2),”.

(5) In paragraph 1C, omit—

- (a) “abridgment or”;
- (b) “1A or”.
