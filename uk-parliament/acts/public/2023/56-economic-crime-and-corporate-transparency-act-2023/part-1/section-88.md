# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 88

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Inspection of the register: general[^key-eaddc375284831e3ec27360c3f5a0826][^key-3eede166eafdd764f68ed0ebcc58833e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1085 of the Companies Act 2006 (inspection of the register), for subsection (3) substitute—

> (3) This section has effect subject to—
>
> - sections 64(6A), 67(1A), 73(7), 75(4A), 76(5B), 76A(9) and 76B(9) (which confer powers to suppress a company’s name that it has been directed or ordered to change);
> - section 1084(1A) (records relating to dissolved companies etc);
> - section 1087 (material not available for public inspection).
