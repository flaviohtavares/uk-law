# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 61

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Duty to notify a change in company’s principal business activities[^key-403e5bf7ce296f9172860dce5dc5248e][^key-f677a290162533bf6d675bc1e7f9e72e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 853C of the Companies Act 2006 (duty to notify a change in company’s principal business activities), after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) This section also applies where—
>
> - (a) a company makes its first confirmation statement, and
> - (b) by the time of its incorporation, the company’s principal business activities had changed from those specified in the statement under section 9(5)(c).
