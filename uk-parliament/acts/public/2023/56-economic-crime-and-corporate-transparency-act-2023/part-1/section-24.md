# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 24

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Use of name giving misleading indication of activities[^key-fb17b14994d4461ea302692f43a14b19][^key-989ec065e38953f087003965772d1a4e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1198 of the Companies Act 2006 (misleading indication of activities), in subsection (1), for “be likely to cause harm to the public” substitute “pose a risk of harm to the public in the United Kingdom or elsewhere”.
