# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 90

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Material not available for public inspection[^key-ebb7b04dbf40acb165969a46a897cc90][^key-9158893a50481e33015a3c22213f3995]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1087 of the Companies Act 2006 (material not available for public inspection), in subsection (1), after paragraph (j) insert—

> - (ja) any record of the information contained in a document (or part of a document) mentioned in any of the previous paragraphs of this subsection.
