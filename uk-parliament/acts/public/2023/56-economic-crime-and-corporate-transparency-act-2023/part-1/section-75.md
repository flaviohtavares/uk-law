# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 75

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Delivery of documents by electronic means[^key-f522315f56269aa3cab64f8379c0f908]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-80ffd04a06df956cab91536bcdc6c286] The Companies Act 2006 is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-80ffd04a06df956cab91536bcdc6c286] In section 1068 (registrar’s requirements as to form, authentication and manner of delivery)—

- (a) after subsection (4) insert—

  > (4A) Any requirements under subsection (4)(b) to (d) must be imposed by means of registrar’s rules.

  ;
- (b) omit subsections (5) to (6A).

(3)[^key-41cd0664260f523dcd9eff21e11ac662] Omit section 1069 (power to require delivery by electronic means).

(4)[^key-41cd0664260f523dcd9eff21e11ac662] In section 1072 (requirements for proper delivery), in subsection (1)(b), omit “section 1069 (power to require delivery by electronic means),”.
