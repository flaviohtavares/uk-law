# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 148

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 148. Service on a limited partnership[^c-b247308c9a3b6b9f8d14fad5d9976102]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After section 36 of the Limited Partnerships Act 1907 (inserted by section 147 of this Act) insert—

> **Service of documents**
>
> **37. Service of documents on limited partnership**
>
> A document may be served on a limited partnership by leaving it at, or sending it by post to, the limited partnership’s registered office.
