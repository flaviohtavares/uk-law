# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 127

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Confirmation statements: Scottish partnerships[^key-4f631a8e0ddcf530d731d58ecc83070c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 37 of the Scottish Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/694](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/694)) (review period), for paragraphs (4) and (5) substitute—

> (4) For the purpose of making a confirmation statement, an eligible Scottish partnership is entitled to assume that information that has been delivered to the registrar has been properly delivered unless the registrar has notified the eligible Scottish partnership otherwise.
