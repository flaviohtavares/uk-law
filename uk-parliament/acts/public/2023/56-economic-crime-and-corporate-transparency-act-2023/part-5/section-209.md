# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, section 209

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 209. Regulators of legal services: objective relating to economic crime[^key-2c4785a66a2c7ee37c0c7479abe82db0][^key-d65fb6f1019ab56b3a2a45f1887aab8d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 1 of the Legal Services Act 2007 (regulatory objectives) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1), after paragraph (h) insert—

> - (i) promoting the prevention and detection of economic crime.

(3) After subsection (4) insert—

> (5) In subsection (1)(i) “**economic crime**” has the meaning given by section 193(1) of the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023

.
