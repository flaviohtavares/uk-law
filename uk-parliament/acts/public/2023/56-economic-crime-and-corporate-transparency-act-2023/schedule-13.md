# Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023, SCHEDULE 13

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 13 — Failure to prevent fraud: fraud offences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 199

### 1. Common law offences[^c-e9fa7e8ecabd156d7056dc95f7454954][^c-4ccfd23c7a43ee21a6bc2257fd6b9c10]

Cheating the public revenue.

**2.**[^c-7fdf8982b95732d1f5a19b85fd676374][^c-796e82c8681913ebaa6b2a393f9553a6] In Scotland, the following offences at common law—

- (a) fraud;
- (b) uttering;
- (c) embezzlement.

### 3. Statutory offences[^c-d22dfb4d72449140766bf5356a5370a2][^c-e8c0590995c31f2f4a55396689f8610e]

An offence under any of the following provisions of the Theft Act 1968—

- (a) section 17 (false accounting);
- (b) section 19 (false statements by company directors etc).

**4.**[^c-efb33b1dcea3080f58b738983229ed89][^c-d2d48f64607607911824c5fe9fcb48ce] An offence under any of the following provisions of the Theft Act (Northern Ireland) 1969—

- (a) section 17 (false accounting);
- (b) section 18 (false statements by company directors etc).

**5.**[^c-8a62c745f53e063778573c563e82a01f][^c-e4d322907748655b32f2ee3e51ac04be] An offence under section 993 of the Companies Act 2006 (fraudulent trading).

**6.**[^c-87f00ee07eaeb6bf260c9a418a15e956][^c-a9bac9ed5bc2fcafad8f177b3ba40b01] An offence under any of the following provisions of the Fraud Act 2006—

- (a) section 1 (fraud);
- (b) section 9 (participating in fraudulent business carried on by sole trader);
- (c) section 11 (obtaining services dishonestly).
