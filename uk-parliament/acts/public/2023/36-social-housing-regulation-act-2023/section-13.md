> Text as at 2024-04-01.

## 13. Limited liability partnerships[^key-c345cff917372dda1ca7f36fd64d0fff][^key-ac8af27077b56ec0a8b130b7d9abb9d8][^key-50145e38a2b46790397b347e336a9e24]

Schedule 1 amends Part 2 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 and Chapter 5 of Part 4 of the Housing and Planning Act 2016 to make provision about limited liability partnerships.
