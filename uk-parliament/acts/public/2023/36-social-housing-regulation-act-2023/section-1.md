# Social Housing (Regulation) Act 2023, section 1

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Fundamental objectives[^c-56a2eb8689f0dca76a3c9b73bf01198c][^c-1a4ce28a8b17163148968569bfca0a20]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 92K of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (fundamental objectives), in subsection (3)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), after “well-managed” insert “, safe, energy efficient”;
- (b) in paragraph (c), omit the final “and”;
- (c) after paragraph (c) insert—

  > - (ca) to ensure that registered providers act in a transparent manner in relation to their tenants of social housing, and

  .
