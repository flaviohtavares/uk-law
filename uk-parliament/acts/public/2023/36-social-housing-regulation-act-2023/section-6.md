> Text as at 2024-04-01.

## 6. Meaning of “English body”[^c-87169b6fbfbf01ba4897eda872d7ad0d][^c-b6b2a27e216763ced3e6a8a266df15e4]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 79 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (English bodies), in subsection (1)—

- (a) after paragraph (c) insert

  > and
  >
  > - (f) a limited liability partnership which has its registered office in England.

  ;
- (b) omit paragraphs (d) and (e).
