# Social Housing (Regulation) Act 2023, section 7

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Registration criteria[^key-1a015f6f1701ddbb82b9802b61fbcefb][^key-e3376f4eb96af25aecaac4c20976de3c]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 112 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (eligibility for voluntary registration), in subsection (3)—

- (a) in the opening words omit “as to”;
- (b) in paragraph (a), at the beginning insert “as to”;
- (c) in paragraph (b)—
  - (i) at the beginning insert “as to”;
  - (ii) omit “and”;
- (d) in paragraph (c)—
  - (i) at the beginning insert “as to”;
  - (ii) at the end insert

    > , and
    >
    > - (d) to secure that the body would meet, on registration, standards set by the regulator under sections 193, 194, 194A and 194C.
