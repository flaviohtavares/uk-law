# Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023, section 138

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 138. Power to designate Homes and Communities Agency as a charging authority[^c-30d21787fcae3f0a03106f2afced1fc1]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 14 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (contents of designation orders), after subsection (6) insert—

> (6A) The order may provide that where the HCA is the local planning authority for the whole or any part of the designated area it is to be a charging authority under section 204B(3)(b) of the Planning Act 2008 (Infrastructure Levy) for the whole or any part of that area—
>
> - (a) for all or specified purposes,
> - (b) in relation to all or specified kinds of development, and
> - (c) in place of any person or body who would otherwise be the charging authority for that area, for those purposes, and in relation to those kinds of development.
