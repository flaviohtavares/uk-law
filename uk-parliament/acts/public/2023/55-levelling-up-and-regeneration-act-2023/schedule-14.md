## Schedule 14 — Existing environmental assessment legislation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 167(1)

### Part 1 — United Kingdom and England and Wales[^key-3e0d041dd52cd1be586dac906009dcf1]

#### United Kingdom and England and Wales

- Schedule 3 to the Harbours Act 1964 (procedure for making harbour revision and empowerment orders) so far as relating to environmental impact assessments;
- Part 5A of the Highways Act 1980 (environmental impact assessments);
- Sections 13A to 13D of the Transport and Works Act 1992 (environmental impact assessments);
- The Offshore Petroleum Production and Pipe-lines (Assessment of Environmental Effects) Regulations 1999 ([S.I. 1999/360](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/360));
- The Public Gas Transporter Pipe-line Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 1999 ([S.I. 1999/1672](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1672));
- The Environmental Impact Assessment (Land Drainage Improvement Works) Regulations 1999 ([S.I. 1999/1783](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1783));
- The Environmental Impact Assessment (Forestry) (England and Wales) Regulations 1999 ([S.I. 1999/2228](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/2228));
- The Nuclear Reactors (Environmental Impact Assessment for Decommissioning) Regulations 1999 ([S.I. 1999/2892](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/2892));
- The Pipe-line Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2000 ([S.I. 2000/1928](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/1928));
- The Water Resources (Environmental Impact Assessment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2003 ([S.I. 2003/164](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/164));
- The Environmental Assessment of Plans and Programmes Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/1633](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1633));
- The Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure)(England and Wales) Rules 2006 ([S.I. 2006/1466](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/1466)) so far as dealing with environmental matters;
- The Environmental Impact Assessment (Agriculture) (England) (No. 2) Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/2522](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/2522));
- The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2007 ([S.I. 2007/1518](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1518));
- The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/571](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/571));
- The Infrastructure Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/572](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/572));
- The Electricity Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/580](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/580));
- The Offshore Oil and Gas Exploration, Production, Unloading and Storage (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/1497](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1497)).

### Part 2 — Scotland[^key-e8a406b020fe52058503f02d965bc734]

#### Scotland

- Schedule 3 to the Harbours Act 1964 so far as relating to environmental impact assessments in Scotland;
- Sections 20A to 20G, 22A, 22B and 55A to 55D of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984 (environmental assessment of certain road construction and improvement projects);
- The Environmental Assessment (Scotland) Act 2005;
- The Transport and Works (Scotland) Act 2007;
- The Transport and Works (Scotland) Act 2007 (Applications and Objections Procedure) Rules 2007;
- The Electricity Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017 ([S.S.I. 2017/101](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2017/101));
- The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017 ([S.S.I. 2017/102](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2017/102));
- The Forestry (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017 ([S.S.I. 2017/113](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2017/113));
- The Agriculture, Land Drainage and Irrigation Projects (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017 ([S.S.I. 2017/114](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2017/114));
- The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017 ([S.S.I. 2017/115](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2017/115)).

- The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/58](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/58));
- The Environmental Impact Assessment (Agriculture) (Wales) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/565](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/565));
- The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/567](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/567)).
