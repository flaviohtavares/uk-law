# Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023, Schedule 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (97).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## Schedule 21 — Provision to be included in terms of tenancy further to contract under section 204

*Extent: E+W*

Section 206

**1.**[^c-6e1509d772ca9480863b4671e8fccf03][^c-ef8044f290d7eca4a5b0066ee50c950f] Provision about what obligations (if any) the landlord is to have with respect to the maintenance or repair of anything outside the premises that enables or facilitates the use of the premises.

**2.**[^c-5c35fcdf3906891a47ef97720ac3f0af][^c-433dd67a922d5ddd2e02359cce79d503] Provision about what obligations (if any) the landlord is to have with respect to the supply of water, energy or telecommunications services to the premises.

**3.**[^c-7f541501c97c1e909610251d34fc6b5b][^c-483aed9af3c562bc06017a1dd6547d1e] Provision requiring the tenant to keep the premises in repair.

**4.**[^c-6d73e0bc9b14e8e56764aa2c9c0d5d62][^c-84e49e8356284a0d0dc083926ea84cfe] Provision about—

- (a) what works and alterations the tenant can or cannot carry out, with or without the consent of the landlord, and
- (b) (if applicable) the giving or withholding of such consent by the landlord.

**5.**[^c-080326ee589e1d05c1b5179ecd2b65b7][^c-c4679e3d9bc85313db5629f72c62c912] Provision requiring the tenant to insure the premises (if they are not otherwise insured).

**6.**[^c-64338a768707fb7ceb6026f996525b3a][^c-8df85dac1827f1d5ba7133f9657df613] Provision enabling the landlord to recover from the tenant costs reasonably incurred by or on behalf of the landlord in connection with the premises.

**7.**[^c-0e8ab7bafbc97703fe5b0ece579135bf][^c-94ef24244ea60b3725f357cfe5e4e939] Provision about circumstances in which the tenant can or cannot—

- (a) assign the tenancy,
- (b) sub-let the premises, or
- (c) otherwise allow another person to possess or occupy the premises.

**8.**[^c-265cd393d3474c162213c462e26e7cbb][^c-5ae2b249b98e81b95d76e4d25c4007b8] Provision for, and in connection with, the giving of a deposit by the tenant to secure the performance of the tenant’s obligations.

**9.**[^c-e87f8912f936db84ef2b1c8a09662ca7][^c-0968c2ab5793fb5ad5965feaff02dbb0] Provision about the circumstances in which the landlord can re-enter the premises following a breach of the tenant’s obligations.

**10.**[^c-2f86b84671db8171883e65059a410270][^c-72fcc3f85d9570ec40128ca698d6e3c3] Provision requiring the tenant to deliver up the premises with vacant possession at the end of the tenancy.
