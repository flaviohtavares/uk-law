> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (97).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/55

## 116. Duration of temporary stop notices[^c-b38697077841694edc5ab5229c5fc352][^c-cd725086b8d9f4923ae31c24ec197b36]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 171E of TCPA 1990 (temporary stop notices) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (7)(a), for “period of 28 days” substitute “relevant period”.

(3) After subsection (7) insert—

> (8) In subsection (7)(a), “**relevant period**” means—
>
> - (a) in the case of a notice issued by a local planning authority in England, 56 days;
> - (b) in the case of a notice issued by a local planning authority in Wales, 28 days.
