## 108. Street votes: modifications of the Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017[^c-24cfa65575a16de4a881ccaae2678259]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision modifying the application of the Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/517](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/517)) in relation to the grant of planning permission by a street vote development order.
