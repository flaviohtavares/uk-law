# Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023, section 129

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (97).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Hazardous substances consent: connected applications to the Secretary of State[^c-d00ab6d2f6dc1a0f592fc330b56fc1d9][^c-f3e4a3cfe53bff3acae8d2af0388e93f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 62A of TCPA 1990 (when application may be made directly to the Secretary of State), in subsection (3)(a)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (i) omit “or”;
- (b) after that sub-paragraph insert—

  > - (ia) an application for hazardous substances consent under the Planning (Hazardous Substances) Act 1990, or

  .
