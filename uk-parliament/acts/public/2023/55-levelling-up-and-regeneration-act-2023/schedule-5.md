# Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023, Schedule 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (97).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/55

## Schedule 5 — Alteration of street names: consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W*

section 81

### 1. Public Health Acts Amendment Act 1907[^c-7342a8267665f2ab4eb627c11949093a][^c-8697876439c3dfccda0c5130138218b9]

In section 21 of the Public Health Acts Amendment Act 1907 (power to alter names of streets), at the end insert—

> This section does not apply in relation to a street or part of a street in England.

### 2. Public Health Act 1925[^c-ab3e2ef8f8100384a36a9cf0795ef099][^c-520770637959f152f35865ddc3cc97ca]

In section 18 of the Public Health Act 1925 (alteration of name of street), after subsection (4) insert—

> (4A) In its application in relation to a street, or part of a street, in England, this section applies as if, in subsection (1), the words “may alter the name of any street, or part of a street, or” were omitted.
>
> See section 81 of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023 for provision about altering street names in England.

### 3. London Building Acts (Amendment) Act 1939[^c-57ac605c551fe569c5602a9c0caa88ff][^c-ca693bb7dfe3580578a912794fb2a9f7]

In section 6 (assigning of names to streets etc), after subsection (3) insert—

> (4) In the case of an order under subsection (1) to which section 81(5) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023 (requirement to demonstrate necessary support before street name altered) applies, subsections (2) and (3) do not apply.

### 4. Local Government Act 1972[^c-73025a3d0c2d424322238a80290e641c][^c-aedf3341bd4ee639cf0f76279e644b5c]

In Part 2 of Schedule 14 to the Local Government Act 1972 (amendments and modifications of Public Health Acts etc), in paragraph 26(c) for “sections 21 and” substitute “section”.
