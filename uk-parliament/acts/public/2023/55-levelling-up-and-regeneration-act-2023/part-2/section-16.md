# Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (97).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Funding[^c-c1f5259441bee98bf2ae0d51f2c489f5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision—

- (a) for the costs of a CCA to be met by its constituent councils, and
- (b) about the basis on which the amount payable by each constituent council is to be determined.

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may be made in relation to an existing CCA[^c-f94e7a5b9d952ab6457d3c39aad2281a] only with the consent of—

- (a) the constituent councils, and
- (b) [^c-efd3770b349c34c758453906671ef4c7]... the CCA.

(2A)[^c-fb977f740550985879cd54edd165682e] Subsection (2) does not apply where a proposal to make the regulations has been submitted to the Secretary of State in accordance with section 47 (proposal for changes to existing arrangements relating to CCA - locally led).

(3) Subsection (1) is subject to regulations under section 13(1) (CCA membership).
