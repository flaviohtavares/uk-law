# Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023, section 73

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (97).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/55

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Power to transfer etc public authority functions to certain local authorities[^c-58869858f0da81224c7dda9bfb490b84]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 17 of the Cities and Local Government Devolution Act 2016 (procedure for making regulations under section 16)—

- (a) in subsection (1)(b), for “the exercise of statutory functions” substitute “the economic, social and environmental well-being of some or all of the people who live or work”, and
- (b) before subsection (5) insert—

  > (4C) The requirement in subsection (1)(b) does not apply to the making of regulations under section 16 where subsection (4A) applies to those regulations.
