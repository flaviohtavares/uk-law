## 62. Consent to conferral of police and crime commissioner functions on mayor[^c-7ad072ea1049e959e3c61c8e5f66ac24]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 107F of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 (functions of mayors: policing) is amended as follows.

(2) For subsection (4) substitute—

> (4) An order under subsection (1) may be made in relation to an existing mayoral combined authority only with the consent of the mayor of the authority.

(3) Omit subsection (9).
