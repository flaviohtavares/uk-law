# Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023, section 69

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Combined authorities and combined county authorities: power to borrow[^key-05d75991400098453ccea08a33aaddd3]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 23 of the Local Government Act 2003 (meaning of “**local authority**” for the purposes of Part 1), after subsection (10) insert—

> (10A) If a draft of a statutory instrument containing regulations under subsection (5) or (8A) would, apart from this subsection, be treated for the purposes of the standing orders of either House of Parliament as a hybrid instrument, it is to proceed in that House as if it were not such an instrument.
