## 48C. Relevant consenting authorities in relation to regulations under section 26[^key-0a73aaa5b028033872b81f54c15f14e5]

*Extent: E+W*

The “**relevant consenting authorities**” in relation to regulations under section 26 means—

- (a) a majority of the constituent councils, and
- (b) if the CCA to be abolished is a mayoral CCA, the mayor for the area of the authority.
