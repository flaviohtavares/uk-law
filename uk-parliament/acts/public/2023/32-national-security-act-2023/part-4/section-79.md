# National Security Act 2023, section 79

## 79. Publication and disclosure of information[^key-451ca31b5b9fc3a698f97b84af507aef][^key-8b6b5863e5dbf4b2d4ad30383fb6c861]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision about—

- (a) publication of information provided to the Secretary of State under section 74 or 75;
- (b) the disclosure of information provided to the Secretary of State under either of those sections.

(2) The power under subsection (1)(a) includes in particular power to make provision about a description of information or material which is not to be published.
