# National Security Act 2023, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-07-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Power to exclude the public from proceedings[^key-bbb1ba191baa92e24d2d06ea38c0cc55][^key-3e452df0a5a79a3eb9b84e537dbb15b4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If it is necessary in the interests of national security, a court may exclude the public from—

- (a) any part of proceedings for an offence under this Part, or
- (b) any part of proceedings relating to section 69A of the Sentencing Act 2020 (as inserted by section 19) or to section 20 or 21,

except for the passing of sentence.
