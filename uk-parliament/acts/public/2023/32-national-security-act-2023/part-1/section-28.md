## 28. Use of reasonable force[^key-b9f94f2ecbb0a8ffc2fe8e9bae329fd0][^key-2e585a449604c53583f756eb8323e5ad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A power conferred on a constable by virtue of this Part—

- (a) is additional to powers which the constable has at common law or by virtue of any other enactment, and
- (b) is not to be taken as affecting those powers.

(2) A constable may if necessary use reasonable force for the purpose of exercising a power conferred on the constable by virtue of this Part.
