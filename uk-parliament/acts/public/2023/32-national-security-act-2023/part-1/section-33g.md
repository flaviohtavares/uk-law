## 33G. Proceedings under the Human Rights Act 1998[^c-f8c57c901b82ed01e2f3393b1d86e588]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commission is the appropriate tribunal for the purposes of section 7 of the Human Rights Act 1998 in relation to any proceedings which call into question a decision made by the Secretary of State in relation to an application made under section 33C.
