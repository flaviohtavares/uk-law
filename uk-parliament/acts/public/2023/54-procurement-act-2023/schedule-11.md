## SCHEDULE 11 — Repeals and revocations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 119

### 1. Primary legislation[^c-c741504f4abd3c4a101eaad494794c5c][^c-fd80d3310684fca1008e00e924d9d42c]

In Schedule 7B to the Government of Wales Act 2006 (general restrictions on devolved competence)—

- (a) paragraph 9(9)(d) (as inserted by the Trade (Australia and New Zealand) Act 2023), and
- (b) paragraph 11(6)(b)(x) (as inserted by the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023).

**2.**[^c-4b9f970d3e610feb6ab0c05259ee3241][^c-d54c1099792588c2fdb672985401489b] Sections 39 and 40 of the Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015.

**3.**[^c-1b11f4f0971f7050affbf668a598017b][^c-7d684301ae9a98a4fd06adc7242fc7fa] The Trade (Australia and New Zealand) Act 2023.

### 4. Subordinate legislation[^c-45a99f8c8b6e260b192f3f18f63c7a0b][^c-653c3ab00eb46800cca24549db4713fb]

The Defence and Security Public Contracts Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/1848](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1848)).

**5.**[^c-3ed6317bca3169bf25e565ae40f2c7d8][^c-84ca0645041bf9721add4a6a7e3d2266] The Public Contracts Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/102](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/102)).

**6.**[^c-6cdb940846b0c0a797644adae66de5df][^c-66b434fc1680a19564ae8244ee406f8c] The Concession Contracts Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/273](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/273)).

**7.**[^c-d4bb3508a9c855720227c18fbc11d96c][^c-9421386f1a72a8371db79b1377c5f0f0] The Utilities Contracts Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/274](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/274)).
