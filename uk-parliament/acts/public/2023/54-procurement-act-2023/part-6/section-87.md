# Procurement Act 2023, section 87

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Regulated below-threshold contracts: notices[^key-00ffdbd77555fd900a4422640d619c18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-e9185f55a9d9e626dd95e99e4def3bcc] A contracting authority may not advertise for the purpose of inviting tenders in relation to the award of a notifiable below-threshold contract without first publishing a below-threshold tender notice.

(2)[^key-e9185f55a9d9e626dd95e99e4def3bcc] Subsection (1) does not apply if a contracting authority advertises only for the purpose of inviting tenders from particular or pre-selected suppliers.

(3)[^key-e9185f55a9d9e626dd95e99e4def3bcc] As soon as reasonably practicable after entering into a notifiable below-threshold contract, a contracting authority must publish a contract details notice.

(4)[^key-e9185f55a9d9e626dd95e99e4def3bcc] A “notifiable below-threshold contract” is a regulated below-threshold contract with an estimated value of—

- (a)[^M_F_1baad92b-8376-49e5-a459-e5c2a3dff3b1] in the case of a contract to be awarded by—
  - (i) a central government authority that is not a devolved Welsh authority, not less than £12,000;
  - (ii) a central government authority that is a devolved Welsh authority, not less than £30,000, or
- (b) otherwise, not less than £30,000.

(5)[^key-e9185f55a9d9e626dd95e99e4def3bcc] A “below-threshold tender notice” is a notice setting out—

- (a) that the contracting authority intends to award a contract, and
- (b) any other information specified in regulations under section 95.

(6)[^key-e9185f55a9d9e626dd95e99e4def3bcc] Any time limits provided for in a below-threshold tender notice must be—

- (a) reasonable, and
- (b) the same for each supplier.

(7)[^key-950b1b6bf35759996a86b6fc398987ea] A Minister of the Crown or the Welsh Ministers may by regulations amend this section for the purpose of amending the financial thresholds.
