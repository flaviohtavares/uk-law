# Procurement Act 2023, section 33

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Reserving contracts to public service mutuals[^key-a56210da03405c83a34b18682f3e5c25]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-18f82b5119e21c04404cd7896905dbf8] This section applies in relation to the award of a public contract under section 19 if the contract—

- (a) is for reservable light touch services, and
- (b) has a maximum term of five years or less.

(2)[^key-18f82b5119e21c04404cd7896905dbf8] A competitive flexible procedure may provide for suppliers that are not qualifying public service mutuals to be excluded from participating in, or progressing as part of, the procedure.

(3)[^key-18f82b5119e21c04404cd7896905dbf8] Subsection (4) applies in relation to the award of a public contract under section 19 if the competitive flexible procedure provides for suppliers to be excluded as set out in subsection (2).

(4)[^key-18f82b5119e21c04404cd7896905dbf8] In assessing tenders under section 19, a contracting authority must disregard any tender from a supplier that is not a qualifying public service mutual.

(5)[^key-18f82b5119e21c04404cd7896905dbf8] A “**qualifying public service mutual**” means a public service mutual that has not entered into a comparable contract during the period of three years ending with the day on which the contract referred to in subsection (1) is awarded.

(6)[^key-18f82b5119e21c04404cd7896905dbf8] A “**public service mutual**” means a body that—

- (a) operates for the purpose of delivering public services and mainly for the purpose of delivering one or more reservable light touch services,
- (b) is run on a not-for-profit basis or provides for the distribution of profits only to members, and
- (c) is under the management and control of its employees.

(7)[^key-e35c7dce10629b9250e6eb54e84370ea] In this section—

- “**comparable contract**” means a contract that was—
  - (a) a contract for the same kind of services,
  - (b) awarded by the same contracting authority, and
  - (c) awarded in reliance on this section;
- “**reservable light touch services**” means services of a kind specified in regulations under subsection (8).

(8)[^key-e35c7dce10629b9250e6eb54e84370ea] An appropriate authority may by regulations specify services of a kind specified in regulations of the authority under section 9 (light touch contracts).
