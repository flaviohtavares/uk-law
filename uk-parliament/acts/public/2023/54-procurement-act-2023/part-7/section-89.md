# Procurement Act 2023, section 89

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Treaty state suppliers[^key-3eabed00e45c79b47358658ca11fa6d4][^key-19559613e2403f8ed550bc1110016af4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-61c2e2a55b4ecf060348a21661949d99] In this Act, a “**treaty state supplier**” means a supplier that is entitled to the benefits of an international agreement specified in Schedule 9.

(2)[^key-2bb5e6cc8837fc7ae8f50b1d4404d23a] But a supplier is a treaty state supplier only to the extent that it is entitled to the benefits of an international agreement specified in Schedule 9 in relation to the procurement being—

- (a) carried out, or
- (b) challenged.

(3)[^key-61c2e2a55b4ecf060348a21661949d99] An appropriate authority may by regulations amend Schedule 9 for the purpose of—

- (a) specifying an international agreement to which the United Kingdom is a signatory, or
- (b) removing, or amending a reference to, an international agreement.

(4)[^key-61c2e2a55b4ecf060348a21661949d99] In subsection (3)(a), the reference to being a signatory to an international agreement includes a reference to having—

- (a) exchanged instruments, where the exchange constitutes the agreement;
- (b) acceded to the agreement.

(5)[^key-2bb5e6cc8837fc7ae8f50b1d4404d23a] In this Part a reference to a supplier being entitled to the benefits of a treaty includes a reference to a supplier being entitled by virtue of the place of origin of goods, services or works supplied.

(6)[^key-2bb5e6cc8837fc7ae8f50b1d4404d23a] In this Act—

- (a) a reference to a treaty state supplier does not include a reference to a supplier that is entitled to the benefits of an international agreement only by reference to the United Kingdom being party to that agreement;
- (b) a reference to a state or territory being party to an agreement includes a reference to a state or territory being part of an organisation of states or territories that is party to an agreement.
