# Procurement Act 2023, section 83F

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83F. Power of Scottish Ministers to amend this Part[^c-8f0cd20fd1e65d18dfb4381a150f7a6a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Scottish Ministers may by regulations modify section 83A, 83B or 83E in consequence of a modification of Scottish procurement legislation.
