# Procurement Act 2023, section 3

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Public contracts[^c-920d748e40d4a7b1a3a019dbaea7ded5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-aac3523d3ab9fb2b207a3c209672b0be] A “public contract” is a contract of a kind specified in subsection (2), (3) or (4).

(2)[^c-b505b4742c6143a4379a2eec38f1d66f] Any contract for the supply, for pecuniary interest, of goods, services or works to a contracting authority which—

- (a) has an estimated value of not less than the threshold amount for the type of contract, and
- (b) is not an exempted contract.

(3)[^c-b505b4742c6143a4379a2eec38f1d66f] Any framework which—

- (a) has an estimated value of not less than the threshold amount for the type of contract, and
- (b) is not an exempted contract.

(4)[^c-b505b4742c6143a4379a2eec38f1d66f] Any concession contract which—

- (a) has an estimated value of not less than the threshold amount for the type of contract, and
- (b) is not an exempted contract.

(5)[^c-aac3523d3ab9fb2b207a3c209672b0be] Schedule 1 sets out the threshold amounts.

(6)[^c-aac3523d3ab9fb2b207a3c209672b0be] Schedule 2 sets out contracts that are exempted contracts for the purposes of this Act.
