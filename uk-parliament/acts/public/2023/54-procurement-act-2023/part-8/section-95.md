# Procurement Act 2023, section 95

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Notices, documents and information: regulations and online system[^c-aaaa3745104e9640e9082df80911dbba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-8291fdfe3492f60e74a7a34e1c32ff67] An appropriate authority may by regulations make provision about—

- (a) the form and content of notices, documents or other information to be published or provided under this Act;
- (b) how such notices or documents are, or information is, to be published, provided or revised.

(2)[^c-8291fdfe3492f60e74a7a34e1c32ff67] Regulations under subsection (1) may for example—

- (a) require a notice or document to contain specified information;
- (b) require publication on a specified online system.

(3)[^c-8291fdfe3492f60e74a7a34e1c32ff67] Regulations under subsection (1) may—

- (a) make different provision for different kinds of notice, document or information;
- (b) make different provision for the same kind of notice, document or information for different purposes.

See also section 122(3).

(4)[^c-46d00195b73c53e7fb621569121c027b] A Minister of the Crown must make arrangements to establish and operate an online system for the purpose of publishing notices, documents and other information under this Act.

(5)[^c-46d00195b73c53e7fb621569121c027b] An online system established or operated under subsection (4) must—

- (a) make notices, documents and other information published under this Act available free of charge, and
- (b) be accessible to people with disabilities.
