# Procurement Act 2023, section 97

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Information relating to a procurement[^key-d2f53f98f08075ee9b7ba354241d15e2][^key-1cc73b74e5dc39e3031a2b71e398092a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An appropriate authority may by regulations make provision requiring certain information to be shared in a particular way, including through a specified online system.

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may require a contracting authority to—

- (a) share information in a particular way, or
- (b) take steps to ensure that suppliers participating in a procurement share information in a particular way.

(3) In this section, “**information**” means information shared under, or for a purpose relating to, this Act.
