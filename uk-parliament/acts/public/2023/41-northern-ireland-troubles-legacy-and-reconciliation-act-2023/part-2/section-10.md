# Northern Ireland Troubles (Legacy and Reconciliation) Act 2023, section 10

> Text as at 2024-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Requests for reviews of other harmful conduct forming part of the Troubles[^key-88359b9d2ac3047ef606ff997e238d8a][^key-ae08e4e5c35aabd2a33d7dba4c896464]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person may request a review of other harmful conduct forming part of the Troubles if that conduct caused that person to suffer serious physical or mental harm.

(2) The Secretary of State may request a review of other harmful conduct forming part of the Troubles (whether or not it caused any person to suffer serious physical or mental harm).

(3) A request under this section may not be made after the end of the fifth year of the period of operation of the ICRIR.
