## 59. Application of provisions to regulatory functions under this Act[^key-a210a58a7fe2faf1afda12314cf0e56e][^key-84f9e9c5ceeaa32cba76633d64c4aaea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) FSMA 2000 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 1A (the FCA), in subsection (6) after paragraph (czb) insert—

> - (czc) the Financial Services and Markets Act 2023,

.

(3) In section 2AB (functions of the PRA), in subsection (3) after paragraph (c) insert—

> - (ca) the Financial Services and Markets Act 2023,

.
