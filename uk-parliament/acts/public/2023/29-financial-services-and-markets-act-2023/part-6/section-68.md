# Financial Services and Markets Act 2023, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-07-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Power under FSMA 2000 to make transitional provisions[^key-f3cf9fe1ee34370b0827c20783e8ff94][^key-981d17a25a9c1498d39be9e046e9c6fe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) FSMA 2000 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 427 (transitional provisions)—

- (a) in subsection (2)(a) to (c), for “the Authority”, in each place, substitute “a regulator”;
- (b) in subsection (2)(f), for “the Authority’s” substitute “the FCA’s”;
- (c) in subsection (3)(a), for “the Authority” substitute “a regulator”.

(3) In Schedule 17A, after paragraph 31A (inserted by section 50), insert—

> **31B. Transitional provisions**
>
> Section 427 (transitional provisions), so far as it relates to an order under section 426 which makes provision in connection with this Part of this Act, applies in relation to the Bank.
