# Financial Services and Markets Act 2023, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-07-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2023/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Regulatory principles[^c-3c16a148b1e1a9c1aecbdf2a2b31f4a2][^c-81e3976cde08215a3ffc6acb5f67f255][^c-775a4b1f57c4c20a8ee8c60cef111876]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 3B of FSMA 2000 (regulatory principles to be applied by both regulators), in subsection (1) for paragraph (c) substitute—

> - (c) the need to contribute towards achieving compliance by the Secretary of State with section 1 of the Climate Change Act 2008 (UK net zero emissions target) and section 5 of the Environment Act 2021 (environmental targets) where each regulator considers the exercise of its functions to be relevant to the making of such a contribution;

.
