## 2. Transitional amendments[^c-7d9d3e63e6ca3161dce46f7c0322c76f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-09fb7ed8ffbdab601f050ecac19f3362][^c-23d05a5dd6a24f676d5ca6ca88d3d2cf][^c-ef9553fef6ce676df5b6bcc821a953b6][^c-3f0ad2167f23fd4eb2201d322f0af474][^c-7eb752917d4f18622bcb141bebaa2b22][^c-0c4d4c801904955106bf6bea8f1d86f7][^c-d9301ed95ee3a14bc0fd8ddbafc16f87][^c-36c66ca940d50966792c7d5f293a8e59][^c-5bda9a357eba200805566d6a0ef7da46][^c-d7f3f0ae6a7e1ecdef5782fa74077ac4][^c-6bee3763910d5c24ad703233c7e8b110] Schedule 2 amends particular legislation referred to in Schedule 1 in relation to the transitional period.

(2)[^c-b5aea18e1ac7021fc3dc128a417af702] In this Chapter “**the transitional period**”, in relation to any legislation, means the period ending with the revocation of that legislation.

(3)[^c-b5aea18e1ac7021fc3dc128a417af702] The amendments in Schedule 2 do not restrict the power in section 3 to modify legislation as amended by that Schedule.
