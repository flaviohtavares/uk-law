Parliamentary Witnesses Act 1858

1858 c.78

An Act to enable the Committees of both Houses of Parliament to administer Oaths to Witnesses in certain Cases.

\[2nd August 1858\][^c562996][^c562997]

## 1. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c562998]

## 2. Any committee of House of Lords may administer oath.

Any committee of the House of Lords may administer an oath to the witnesses examined before such committee.
