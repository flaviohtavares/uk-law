## 87. Actions on lost negotiable instruments.

In case of any action founded upon a . . . [^c561295] negotiable instrument, it shall be lawful for the court or a judge to order that the loss of such instrument shall not be set up, provided an indemnity is given, to the satisfaction of the court or judge, or a master, against the claims of any other person upon such negotiable instrument.

## 106. Short title of Act.

In citing this Act in any instrument, document, or proceeding, it shall be sufficient to use the expression, “The Common Law Procedure Act 1854.”

## 107. Act not to extend to Ireland or Scotland.
