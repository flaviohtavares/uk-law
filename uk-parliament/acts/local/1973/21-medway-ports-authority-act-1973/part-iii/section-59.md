# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Allowances and donations

The Authority may give donations, contributions or subscriptions to such public institutions or charities as the Authority in their discretion may think fit.
