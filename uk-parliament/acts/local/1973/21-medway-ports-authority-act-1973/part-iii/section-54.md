# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 54

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 54. Reserve fund

The Authority may continue and maintain a reserve fund and may determine the moneys to be carried to the credit of that fund, the management thereof and the application for the purposes of the Authority of the moneys comprised therein.
