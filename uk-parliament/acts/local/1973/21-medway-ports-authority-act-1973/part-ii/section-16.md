# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Returns and statistics

The Authority shall give the Secretary of State for the Environment such returns, statistics and information with respect to the exercise of their powers as he may require.
