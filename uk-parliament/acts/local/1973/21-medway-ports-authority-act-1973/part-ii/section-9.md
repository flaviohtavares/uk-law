# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Authority to be local lighthouse authority

The Authority shall be a local lighthouse authority for the purposes of the Merchant Shipping Act, 1894.
