# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Survey of tidal works

The Secretary of State may at any time if he deems it expedient order a survey and examination of a tidal work or of the site upon which the Authority propose to construct a tidal work, and any expenditure incurred by the Secretary of State in any such survey and examination shall be recoverable from the Authority as a simple contract debt.
