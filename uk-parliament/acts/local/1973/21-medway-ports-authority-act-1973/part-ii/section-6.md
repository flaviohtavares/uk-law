# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Jurisdiction of Authority

The Authority and the harbour master shall exercise jurisdiction within the limits of the port and, subject to the provisions of this Act, within the Medway approach area.
