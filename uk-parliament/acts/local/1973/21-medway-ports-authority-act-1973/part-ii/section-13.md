# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Power to hire out plant

The Authority may let upon hire (with or without the services of their employees) on such terms as they think fit any vessel, plant, equipment or other property belonging to them.
