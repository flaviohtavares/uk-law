# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Hydrographic surveys within the Medway approach area

(1) The Authority may carry out such surveys of the bed of the river Thames within the Medway approach area as they consider necessary or desirable.

(2) The Authority shall publish all surveys made by them under this section.
