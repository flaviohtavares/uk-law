# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Power to acquire undertakings

The Authority may acquire by agreement an undertaking providing or intended to provide services or facilities of a kind which the Authority are themselves authorised to provide.
