# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Claims for repayment of charges

Any person claiming the return of the whole or any part of any charges paid to the Authority shall make his claim, and produce all documents and give all information required by the Authority in proof thereof, within three years from the time of payment; and in default thereof the claim shall cease to be enforceable.
