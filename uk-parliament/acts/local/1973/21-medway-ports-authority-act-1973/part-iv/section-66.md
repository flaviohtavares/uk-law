# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Payment of charges on warehoused goods

The owner or person having the charge of any goods warehoused, placed, stored or yarded in a warehouse, transit shed or area, store or yard of the Authority shall, before the removal of those goods from the warehouse, transit shed or area, store or yard and at such date or dates as shall be fixed by the Authority, pay such charges as shall be then due and payable on those goods.
