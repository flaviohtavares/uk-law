# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Deposit for charges

The Authority may, if they think fit, require any person liable, or to become liable, to pay charges to the Authority to deposit with their collector, or to guarantee, such sum as, in the opinion of the Authority, is reasonable' having regard to the probable amount of the charges.
