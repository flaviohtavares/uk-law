# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Short and collective titles

(1) This Act may be cited as the Medway Ports Authority Act 1973.

(2) The Medway Ports Reorganisation Scheme, 1968, and this Act may be cited together as the Medway Ports Authority Scheme and Act 1968 and 1973.
