## 2. Division of Act into Parts

This Act is divided into Parts as follows:—

Part I.—Preliminary. Part II.—Duties and general powers of the Authority. Part III.—Financial. Part IV.—Charges. Part V.—Goods. Part VI.—Byelaws. Part VII.—Miscellaneous and general.
