# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 104

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 104. Saving for Chatham Sun Pier

Nothing in this Act shall affect the provisions of the Chatham Pier Orders, 1863 to 1902.
