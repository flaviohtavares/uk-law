# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 95

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 95. Saving for Trinity House

Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or derogate from any of the rights or privileges or the jurisdiction or authority of Trinity House.
