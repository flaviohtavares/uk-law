# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 100

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 100. Saving for port health authority

Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect the rights, powers and duties of the corporation of the city of London as port health authority under section 41 of the London Government Act, 1963.
