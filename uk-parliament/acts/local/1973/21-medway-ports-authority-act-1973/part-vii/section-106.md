# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 106

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 106. Saving for Coast Protection Act, 1949

Nothing in this Act shall affect the application to any operation of sections 34 to 36 of the Coast Protection Act, 1949.
