# Medway Ports Authority Act 1973, section 110

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 110. Costs of Act

All costs, charges and expenses preliminary to and of and incidental to the preparing for, obtaining and passing of this Act or otherwise in relation thereto shall be paid by the Authority.
