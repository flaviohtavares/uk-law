# Standard Life Assurance Company Act 1991, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Companies Clauses Acts not to apply

The [^m00008]Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845 shall not apply to the Company or its undertaking.
