# Greenham and Crookham Commons Act 2002, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Saving for areas of special scientific interest

Nothing in this Act shall prejudice the operation of sections 28 to 33 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act [1981 (c. 69)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/69) or any other enactment relating to areas of special scientific interest under sections 28 and 29 of that Act.
