# London Local Authorities Act 2007, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2007-09-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukla/2007/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Lapsing of street trading licence under Act of 1990[^c-9d4459e1a488569b7d03dc1707c23c6a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In subsection (4)(b) of section 29 (further provisions relating to grant, renewal or revocation of street trading licences) of the Act of 1990, for “the next following subsection” there is substituted “section 30 (Part III appeals) of this Act”.

(2) After the said section 29, the following section is inserted—

> **29A. Lapsing of licence in certain cases**
>
> In the case of a street trading licence which authorises street trading on land which falls within paragraph (b) in the definition of “street” in subsection (1) of section 21 (interpretation of Part III) of this Act, the licence shall lapse if—
>
> - (a) the consent is discontinued by the person who gave it, or a successor in title of that person, and the council is provided with written notice of the discontinuation of the consent by the person who discontinues it; or
> - (b) the holder of the licence no longer is the owner of the land in question,
>
> as the case may be.
