# London Local Authorities Act 2007, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Keeling Schedule

Part III of the Act of 1990, as amended by the [London Local Authorities Act 1994 (c. xii)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukla/1994/12), the [London Local Authorities Act 2004 (c. i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukla/2004/1) and this Act, is set out in Schedule 3 to this Act.
