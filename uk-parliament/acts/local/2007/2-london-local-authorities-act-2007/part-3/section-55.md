## 55. Employment of assistants under Act of 1999[^c-830842bc6aaab852156889ab6de37a29]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 26 (employment of assistants) of the Act of 1999 is amended as follows.

(2) After “street trading licence” (where those words first appear), the words “or a temporary licence” are inserted.

(3) For “street trading licence” where those words appear for the second time, substitute “licence”.
