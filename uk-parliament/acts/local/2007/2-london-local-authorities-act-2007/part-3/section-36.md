# London Local Authorities Act 2007, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Interpretation of Chapter 2

In this Chapter—

- “the Act of 1990” means the [London Local Authorities Act 1990 (c. vii)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukla/1990/7);
- “the Act of 1999” means the [City of Westminster Act 1999 (c. i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukla/1999/1).
