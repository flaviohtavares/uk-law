## 35. Near beer premises[^c-4886aae594a57c4b6def4153a5ab0e3e]

*Extent: E+W*

On the day that section 33 (hostess bars) of this Act comes into force in a borough, Part IV (near beer premises) of the [London Local Authorities Act 1995 (c. x)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukla/1995/10) shall cease to have effect as respects that borough.
