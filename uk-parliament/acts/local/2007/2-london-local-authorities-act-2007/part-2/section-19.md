## 19. Placing of receptacles for household waste[^c-35fa0c2c481dcca3b5145a09f71e4a56]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 46 of the 1990 Act (receptacles for household waste) shall apply in the area of a borough council as if, in subsection (4), the following paragraph were inserted after paragraph (b)—

> - (ba) the placing of the receptacles for the purpose of avoiding nuisance or detriment to the amenities of the area;

.
