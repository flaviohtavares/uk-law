## 78. Provision of information to authorised officers[^c-2697360137146bb4fe4d272f2e85af30]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 26 (provision of information to authorised officers) of the [London Local Authorities Act 2004 (c. i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukla/2004/1) is amended by the addition of the following subsection after subsection (3)—

> (4) In this section “authorised officer” includes any person who is authorised in writing by a borough council to enforce any enactment which gives rise to a criminal offence.
>
> No such person shall require any further authorisation under this Act for the purposes of this section.
