> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Fixed penalty offences

Schedule 2 to the London Local Authorities Act 2004 is amended by the insertion, in the appropriate place, of the following entry into the table of offences in respect of which a fixed penalty notice can be given under section 15 (fixed penalty offences) of that Act—

> |  |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | 14 | [London Local Authorities Act 2007 (c. ii)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukla/2007/2) | 26(5) | Failure to comply with requirement at civic amenity site |
