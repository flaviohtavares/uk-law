# London Docklands Development Corporation Act 1994, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Status of Corporation

The Corporation shall not be a navigation authority, harbour authority or conservancy authority for the purposes of the [^m00015]Water Resources Act 1991 or the [^m00016]Land Drainage Act 1991 or any byelaw made under either of those Acts and section 23 of the Land Drainage Act 1991 shall apply to any works carried out or maintained under or in pursuance of this Act or any byelaw made under this Act.
