# London Docklands Development Corporation Act 1994, SCHEDULE 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Description of jetties

Sections 2 and 3.

**1.** Greenland pier and associated jetties.

**2.** Blackwall pier.

**3.** Two jetties on either side of entrance of lock leading to King George V Dock.

**4.** Two jetties on either side of site of former entrance to Albert Basin.

**5.** Jetty on north side of Gallion’s Yacht Lock entrance.

**6.** Cory’s jetty.
