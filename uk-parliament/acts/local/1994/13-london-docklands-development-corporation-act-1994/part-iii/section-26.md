# London Docklands Development Corporation Act 1994, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Saving for fire authority

Notwithstanding the proviso to section 2 (1) of the [^m00013]Petroleum (Consolidation) Act 1928, the local authority empowered to grant petroleum-spirit licences under that Act in the designated areas (other than the jetties) shall be the fire authority.
