# London Docklands Development Corporation Act 1994, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Saving for London City Airport

It shall be the duty of the Corporation, in exercising any power conferred on it by or under this Act, to have due regard to the safe operation of London City Airport and in particular to any requirements made by the Civil Aviation Authority in respect of the airport.
