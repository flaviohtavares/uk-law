# London Docklands Development Corporation Act 1994, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Control of craft in designated areas, etc

The provisions of Schedule 4 to this Act for the control of craft in the designated areas, and related matters, shall have effect.
