# London Docklands Development Corporation Act 1994, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Duty to regulate and manage

It shall be the duty of the Corporation, so far as is reasonably practicable, to regulate and manage the designated areas in accordance with the provisions of this Act and any byelaw made under section 9 (Byelaws) of this Act.
