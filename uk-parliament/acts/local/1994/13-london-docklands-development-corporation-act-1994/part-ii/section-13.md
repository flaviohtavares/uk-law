# London Docklands Development Corporation Act 1994, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Regulation of placing things on waterside

(1) Any person who, without lawful authority, places any structure or object so as to obstruct the free passage over any part of the waterside to which the public have access shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale and in addition, in the case of a continuing offence, a daily fine not exceeding one-tenth of that level.

(2) Nothing in subsection (1) above shall apply to anything done on any land by or with the consent of the occupier of the land.
