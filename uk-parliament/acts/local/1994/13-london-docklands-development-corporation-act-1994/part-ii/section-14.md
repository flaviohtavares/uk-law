# London Docklands Development Corporation Act 1994, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. As to section 161 of Water Resources Act 1991

Section 161 of the [^m00008]Water Resources Act 1991 (anti-pollution works and operations) shall have effect in relation to the designated areas as though references to the Authority included references to the Corporation.
