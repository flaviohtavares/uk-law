# London Docklands Development Corporation Act 1994, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Obstruction of officers

Any person who—

- (a) intentionally obstructs the manager or any other officer of the Corporation acting in pursuance of this Act, or of any byelaw made under or by virtue of this Act; or
- (b) without reasonable excuse fails to comply with a requirement properly made by any such person so acting; or
- (c) without reasonable cause fails to give to any such person so acting any information which he may reasonably require for the purpose of the performance of his functions; or
- (d) in giving such information as aforesaid makes a statement which he knows to be false;

shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
