# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 17

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Debts due to and by the Two Companies respectively to be paid to and by them.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding such Repeal, all Persons who shall immediately before the Commencement of this Act owe any Sum of Money to the Two Companies respectively, or to any Person on their respective Behalf, shall pay the same, with all Interest (if any) due and payable or accruing for the same, to the Two Companies respectively; and all Debts and Monies which immediately before the Commencement of this Act are due or owing by or recoverable from the Two Companies respectively, or for the Payment of which the Two Companies respectively are or but for this Act would be liable, shall be paid, with all Interest (if any) due and payable or accruing for the same, by or be recoverable from the Two Companies respectively.
