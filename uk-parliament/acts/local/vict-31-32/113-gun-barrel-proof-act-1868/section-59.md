# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 59

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Guardians to report to Yearly Meetings.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

At every Yearly Meeting of the *Birmingham* Gun Trade the Guardians shall present a Report of their Transactions during the preceding Year, and shall annex to their Report a tabular classified Statement of the Barrels sent to be proved at the *Birmingham* Proof House and every Branch Proof House of the Guardians, the Number of Barrels proved and marked as proved, the Number of Barrels found faulty and the Character of the Faults, and shall annex to their Report the Accounts for the preceding Year, and the Report of the Auditors thereon.
