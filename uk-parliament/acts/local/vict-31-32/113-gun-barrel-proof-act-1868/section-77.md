# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 77

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Chairman of Guardians.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

So long as the Proof Master is a Guardian he shall be the Chairman of the Guardians, but otherwise the Chairman of the Guardians shall from Time to Time be elected by the Guardians in accordance with the [^c1923381]Commissioners Clauses Act 1847.
