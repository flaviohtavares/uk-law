# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 24

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Bankrupts not to be Guardians.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any Bankrupt who has not obtained his Order of Discharge, or a Person not qualified as required by this Act, shall not be capable of being or continuing a Guardian.
