# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 45

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Time for making Objections.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Notice of Objection shall be in the Form in Schedule A., Part II., to this Act annexed, and shall be signed by the Objector, and shall be given within Fourteen Days next after the Third *Monday* in *March* in the Year in which the Revision is to be made.
