# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 85

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Accounts to be made up to 31st December in every Year.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Accountant shall make up the Accounts to the Thirty-first of *December* in every Year, and shall submit the same and all the necessary Books and Vouchers to the Auditors on or before the Thirty-first Day of *January* following, and shall render to the Auditors all such Assistance as they shall require for the Explanation, Verification, and Audit of the Accounts.
