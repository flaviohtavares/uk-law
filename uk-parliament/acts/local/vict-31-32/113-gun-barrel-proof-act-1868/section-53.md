# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 53

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. First and other yearly Meetings of Birmingham Gun Trade.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A Yearly Meeting of the Members of the *Birmingham* Gun Trade shall be held on the First *Tuesday* in the Month of *May* in every Year after the Commencement of this Act, and the First Yearly Meeting shall be held on the First *Tuesday* in the Month of *May* One thousand eight hundred and sixty-nine.
