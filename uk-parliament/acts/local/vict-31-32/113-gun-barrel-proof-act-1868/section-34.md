# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 34

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Election as elected Guardians of persons ceasing to be official Guardians.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Every Person ceasing to be an official Guardian may, if qualified, be elected an elected Guardian, and with respect to his making and subscribing the Declaration by this Act required he shall on being so elected be deemed to have newly become an elected Guardian.
