# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 33

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Vacancy on elected Guardians becoming official Guardians.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

When any Person, being an elected Guardian, becomes entitled to be an official Guardian, his Office as elected Guardian shall thereupon become vacant, and with respect to his making and subscribing the Declaration by this Act required he shall be deemed to have newly become a Guardian.
