# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 3

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Reserving Power for Crown to establish public Proof Houses.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Act shall not in any way interfere with the Power of Her Majesty, Her Heirs and Successors, from Time to Time to establish public Proof Houses in such Places and under such Regulations as to the Care and Management thereof as to Her Majesty, Her Heirs and Successors, may seem fit.
