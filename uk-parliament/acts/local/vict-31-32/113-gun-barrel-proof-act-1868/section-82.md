# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 82

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Remuneration to Auditors.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each Auditor shall be paid such reasonable Remuneration as the Meeting electing him shall determine, but not exceeding Twenty Pounds *per Annum*, and both Auditors shall attend at every Yearly Meeting of the *Birmingham* Gun Trade to give thereat all necessary Explanations of the Accounts.
