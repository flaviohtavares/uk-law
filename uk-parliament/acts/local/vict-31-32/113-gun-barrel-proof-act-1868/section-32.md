# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 32

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Elected Guardians failing to act to cease to be elected Guardians.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Every elected Guardian who for Three Months after his Election fails to make and subscribe such Declaration, or who for Six Months in succession is absent from all Meetings, or who by Notice in Writing left at the *Birmingham* Proof House resigns his Appointment, shall cease to be an elected Guardian.
