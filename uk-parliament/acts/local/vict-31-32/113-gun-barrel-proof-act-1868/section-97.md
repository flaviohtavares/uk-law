# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 97

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Proof Master of Gunmakers Company responsible.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Proof Master of the Gunmakers Company, or such Officer of that Company as that Company shall from Time to Time appoint to act in that Behalf, shall be responsible for the Execution by himself or his Assistants of the several Duties with respect to the receiving, proving, marking as proved, and delivering of Barrels by this Act imposed on the Gunmakers Company.
