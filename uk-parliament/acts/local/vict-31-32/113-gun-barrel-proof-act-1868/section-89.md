# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 89

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Power to provide Branch Proof Houses.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Two Companies respectively from Time to Time may provide and maintain in proper and convenient Places[^c1923383]... so many Branch Proof Houses as they respectively think proper, and in accordance with this Act may maintain and use every Branch Proof House so provided, and the Works and Conveniences thereof, and may from Time to Time discontinue every such Branch Proof House, and revive every discontinued Branch Proof House, as often as they think fit.
