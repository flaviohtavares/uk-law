# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 46

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Printed Copies of Forms in Schedule A. to be kept.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Guardians shall cause to be printed, and always keep ready for Use, Copies of each of the Forms in Parts I. and II. of Schedule A. to this Act annexed, at the Foot whereof shall be printed the Sections of this Act relating to the Qualification required for Members of the *Birmingham* Gun Trade; and a Copy of either of such printed Forms shall be delivered to any Person applying at the *Birmingham* Proof House for the same, upon his paying to the Officer of the Guardians appointed to have Charge of such Forms the Sum of 5p[^c1923376].
