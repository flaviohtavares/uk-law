> Text as at 1996-07-15.

## 6. 8 & 9 Vict. c. 18. and 23 & 24 Vict. c. 106. incorporated.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

“**The Lands Clauses Consolidation Act 1845,**” and “**The Lands Clauses Consolidation Acts Amendment Act 1860,**” are incorporated with this Act, but not so as to authorize the purchasing of any Lands otherwise than by Agreement.
