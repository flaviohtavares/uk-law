# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 12

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Guardians to continue incorporated.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding such Repeal, but subject to the Provisions of this Act, the Guardians shall be and continue incorporated by the Name of “The Guardians of the *Birmingham* Proof House,” and by that Name shall be One Body Corporate, with perpetual Succession and a Common Seal.
