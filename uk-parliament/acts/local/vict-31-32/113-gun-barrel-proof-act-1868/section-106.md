# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 106

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 106. Regulations to be made by Gunmakers Company for proving Barrels.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Two Companies respectively[^c1923399] from Time to Time may make and establish such Rules and Regulations as appear to them necessary for receiving at their respective[^c1923400] Proof House, and any Branch Proof House provided by them respectively[^c1923401], Barrels for Proof, and for proving there, and marking as proved, the same, according to the Provisions of this Act, and for re-delivering the same when so proved and marked.
