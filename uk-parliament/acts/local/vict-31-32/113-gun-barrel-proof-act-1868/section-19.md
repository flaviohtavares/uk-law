# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 19

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Books to be Evidence.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding such Repeal, all Books, Registers of Foreign Proof Marks, and other Registers, Certificates, Writings, and Documents by the recited Act respectively directed or authorized to be kept or made, and which if this Act were not passed would be receivable in Evidence, shall be admitted as Evidence in all Courts of Law and Equity and elsewhere accordingly.
