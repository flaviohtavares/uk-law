# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 70

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Powers of Guardians and ordinary Business of Meetings of Guardians.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Guardians shall exercise and carry into effect all the Powers and Provisions of this Act, except as to such Matters as by this Act are directed to be otherwise transacted, or as relate to the Gunmakers Company; and at any Meeting of the Guardians, not being a Special Meeting, any of the ordinary Business of the Guardians may be transacted, such ordinary Business including the Supply of occasional Vacancies in the Office of elected Guardian, the appointing and removing of the inferior Officers of the Guardians, the inquiring into the Conduct of Persons employed by the Guardians to execute any Works or perform any Services, and into the State and Progress of such Works, and generally the giving from Time to Time of any Directions necessary for superintending, enforcing, or carrying into effect the Purposes of this Act.
