# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 47

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Revision of Register by Registry Board.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Register of Members of the *Birmingham* Gun Trade for the Time being in force, and all new Claims, and all Objections to Names on the Register for the Time being in force, and to new Claims, shall from Time to Time be revised and decided upon by a Board to be called the Registry Board.
