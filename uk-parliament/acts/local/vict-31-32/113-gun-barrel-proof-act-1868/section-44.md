# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 44

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Objections to Register and Claims.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any Person whose Name shall be on the Register for the Time being in force may object to any other Person as not entitled to be upon the new Register, and whether or not such last-mentioned Person be on the Register for the Time being in force: Provided always, that Notice in Writing of every such Objection shall be given to the Law Clerk and also to the Person objected to, which Notice shall be sent, if by Post Post-paid, to the Law Clerk at the *Birmingham* Proof House, a Duplicate thereof being sent to the Person objected to, addressed to him at the Premises in respect of which he is registered or claims to be registered.
