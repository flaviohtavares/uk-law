# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 67

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Postponing Meetings for Fast Days, &c.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Whenever the Day fixed by or under this Act for the holding of any Meeting of the Guardians or of the *Birmingham* Gun Trade, or for the sitting of the Registry Board, shall be a Day appointed for a General Fast or Thanksgiving, then the Meeting shall be held on the next following Day, not being one of such Days or *Sunday*.
