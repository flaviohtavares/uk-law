# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 66

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. First and other Annual Meetings of Guardians.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Guardians shall hold their First Annual Meeting on the last *Tuesday* in the Month of *April* in the Year One thousand eight hundred and sixty-nine, and shall hold their Second and subsequent Annual Meetings respectively on the last *Tuesday* in the Month of *April* in every subsequent Year, all such Annual Meetings to be held at the Hour of Eleven in the Forenoon.
