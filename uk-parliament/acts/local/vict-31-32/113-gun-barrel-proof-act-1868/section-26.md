# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 26

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. But Shareholders in Companies not disqualified.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Provided always, that a Person being a Shareholder in or Member of any Company established by or under Act of Parliament shall not be prevented from being or acting as a Guardian by reason of any Contract entered into between the Guardians and such established Company, but any such Guardian, being a Shareholder in or Member of such established Company, shall not vote on any Question relating to the Execution of this Act in which such established Company is interested.
