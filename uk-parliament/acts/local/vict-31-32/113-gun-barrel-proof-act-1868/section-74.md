# Gun Barrel Proof Act 1868, section 74

> Text as at 1996-07-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Power for Guardians to resolve that Proof Master shall be a salaried Officer, and not a Guardian.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Provided always, that the Guardians may by Resolution adopted at a Special Meeting resolve that the Proof Master shall not be a Guardian, but shall be a salaried Officer, who shall devote the whole of his Time to the Duties of his Office, and shall not be concerned directly or indirectly (except as a Shareholder in or Member of any Company) in any Trade or Business.
