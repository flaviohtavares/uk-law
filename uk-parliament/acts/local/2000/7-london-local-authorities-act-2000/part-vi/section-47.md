# London Local Authorities Act 2000, section 47

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 47. Compensation to servants of fire authority

Section 31 (Compensation to persons in Council’s service on abolition of office) of the [^m00033]London County Council (General Powers) Act 1921 shall (with any necessary modifications) apply to the London Fire and Civil Defence Authority as it applies in relation to a London borough council.
