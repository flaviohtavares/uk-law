# London Local Authorities Act 2000, section 48

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 48. Bus lanes

Section 4 (Penalty charge notices under Part II) of and Schedule 1 (which makes provision relating to the enforcement of the provisions relating to bus lanes) to the [^m00034]London Local Authorities Act 1996 are amended in accordance with Schedule 2 to this Act.
