# London Local Authorities Act 2000, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Door supervisors

In its application to the area of a participating council, section 29 (Interpretation of Part V) of the [^m00022]London Local Authorities Act 1995 is amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph (c) of the definition of “licensed premises” the words “or licensed” are left out;
- (b) in the definition of “door supervisor”—
  - (i) before the words “to maintain order” the words “any person employed” are inserted; and
  - (ii) at the end the words “but, in respect of premises in respect of which there is in force for the time being a justices' on-licence within the meaning of section 1(2) of the [^m00023]Licensing Act 1964 does not include the holder of that licence” are inserted.
