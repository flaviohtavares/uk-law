# London Local Authorities Act 2000, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Revocation of licence

The council may revoke a licence on any of the following grounds:—

- (a) that there has been a breach of the conditions of the licence;
- (b) that undue interference with, or inconvenience to, or risk to the safety of persons using the street, or other streets within the vicinity of the street, has been caused as a result of the busking;
- (c) that nuisance has been caused as a result of the busking to occupiers of property in or in the vicinity of the street in respect of which the licence was granted.
