# London Local Authorities Act 2000, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Power to prescribe standard terms, conditions and restrictions under Part V

(1) The council may make regulations prescribing standard conditions applicable to all licences.

(2) Where the council have made regulations under this section, every licence granted by them shall be deemed to have been so granted subject to the standard conditions except so far as they are expressly excluded or amended in any particular case.

(3) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1) above, the standard conditions applied shall include a condition requiring the licence holder to carry his licence with him when busking.
