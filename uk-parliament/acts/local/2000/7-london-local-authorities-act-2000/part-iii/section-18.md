# London Local Authorities Act 2000, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Enforcement of waste control enactments

The following sections of the [^m00012]Control of Pollution Act 1974 shall have effect as if references therein to that Act included references to the waste control enactments—

- (a) section 91 (Rights of entry and inspection, etc.); and
- (b) section 92 (Provisions supplementary to s. 91).
