# The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (Revocation) (No. 29) Order 1986

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1986 No. 359

OFFSHORE INSTALLATIONS

The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (Revocation) (No. 29) Order 1986

Made

26th February 1986

Coming into Operation

28th February 1986

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 21(1) of the Oil and Gas (Enterprise) Act 1982[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (Revocation) (No. 29) Order 1986 and shall come into operation on 28th February 1986.

**2.** The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 19) Order 1986[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Alick Buchanan-Smith,

Minister of State,

Department of Energy.

26th February 1986.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This Note is not part of the Order.)

This Order revokes the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 19) Order 1986. The installation known as Western Pacesetter IV which was protected by the safety zone established by that Order has been removed and accordingly that Order is no longer required.
