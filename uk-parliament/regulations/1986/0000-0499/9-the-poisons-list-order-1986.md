# The Poisons List Order 1986

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1986 No. 9

POISONS

The Poisons List Order 1986

Made

9th January 1986

Laid before Parliament

21st January 1986

Coming into Operation

1st April 1986

In pursuance of section 2 of the Poisons Act 1972 and on the recommendation of the Poisons Board, I hereby make the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Poisons List Order 1986 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1986.

**2.** The Poisons List[^f00001] shall be amended and varied as follows, that is to say, in Part I, after the item beginning with the word “Lead” there shall be inserted the item “Magnesium phosphide”.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

9th January 1986

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Poisons List by adding magnesium phosphide to Part I.
