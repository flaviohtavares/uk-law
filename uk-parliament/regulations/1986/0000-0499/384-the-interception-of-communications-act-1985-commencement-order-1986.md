# The Interception of Communications Act 1985 (Commencement) Order 1986

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1986 No. 384 (C.12)

POST OFFICE TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Interception of Communications Act 1985 (Commencement) Order 1986

Made

26th February 1986

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 12(2) of the Interception of Communications Act 1985, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Interception of Communications Act 1985 (Commencement) Order 1986.

**2.** The Interception of Communications Act 1985 shall come into force on 10th April 1986.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

26th February 1986

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 10 April 1986 the provisions of the Interception of Communications Act 1985.
