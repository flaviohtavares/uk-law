# The Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Designation of Countries) Order 1986

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1986 No. 271

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Designation of Countries) Order 1986

Made

18th February 1986

Laid before Parliament

21st February 1986

Coming into Operation

13th March 1986

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 8 of the Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Designation of Countries) Order 1986 and shall come into operation on 13th March 1986.

**2.** The countries specified in the Schedule hereto are hereby designated as parties to the European Convention on the Suppression of Terrorism signed at Strasbourg on 27th January 1977.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

18th February 1986

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

- Belgium
- The Netherlands
- Portugal
- Switzerland

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates Belgium, the Netherlands, Portugal and Switzerland for the purposes of the Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 so that they become convention countries within the meaning of the Act.

The four countries are parties to the European Convention on the suppression of Terrorism. The following countries have now been designated for the purposes of the Act: Austria, Belgium, Cyprus, Denmark, Federal Republic of Germany (and Berlin (West)), Iceland, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland and Turkey.
