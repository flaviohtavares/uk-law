## L12. Notice of decisions[^key-ca873e768d76c57e28dde80aa19adb19]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The relevant body shall give notice in writing of any decision relating to any benefit under this Part to the person affected and shall inform him about his right of appeal under regulation N8.
