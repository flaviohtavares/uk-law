# The Local Government Superannuation Regulations 1986, section L1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (340).** Text as at 1986-03-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## L1. Interpretation[^key-a97b102d3a6d2ea088b7b9dbe149888d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Part, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**benefits**” means any allowance or lump sum payable under this Part;
- “**relevant body**” has the meaning given by regulation L7; and
- “**relevant employment**” has the meaning given by regulation L2(2).
