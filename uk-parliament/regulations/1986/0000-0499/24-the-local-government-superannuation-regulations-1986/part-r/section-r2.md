# The Local Government Superannuation Regulations 1986, section R2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (340).** Text as at 1986-03-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## R2. Records to be kept by authorities[^key-9c2ceb12d8bd4ac4528d0a061d940244]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A scheduled body are to keep, in such form as they think fit, a record of—

- (a) the name of, and
- (b) all their decisions under regulation N2 in relation to,

each of their pensionable employees.

(2) An administering authority are to keep, in such form as they think fit, a record of—

- (a) the name of, and
- (b) all their decisions under regulation N3 or N4 in relation to,

every pensionable employee in relation to whom they are the appropriate administering authority.
