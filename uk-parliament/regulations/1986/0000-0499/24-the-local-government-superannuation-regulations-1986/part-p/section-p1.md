# The Local Government Superannuation Regulations 1986, section P1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (340).** Text as at 1986-03-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## P1. Superannuation funds[^key-b6ca80b63bf0ca014054d803448b751d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The superannuation funds maintained immediately before 1st March 1986 under the Local Government Superannuation Regulations 1974 to 1984[^f00073]—

- (a) by every county council,
- (b) by the Greater London Council,
- (c) by the Common Council,
- (d) by every London borough council, and
- (e) by the Severn-Trent Water Authority,

shall be maintained by those bodies for the purposes of these regulations.
