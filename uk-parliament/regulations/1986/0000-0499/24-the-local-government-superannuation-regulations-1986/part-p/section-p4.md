# The Local Government Superannuation Regulations 1986, section P4

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## P4. Accounts and audit[^c-fca5c7ee2ca5f2f17adb57c25926cd4f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

As soon as may be after any audit of their superannuation fund an administering authority shall send each body whose employees contribute to the fund copies of the revenue account and balance sheet of the fund and of any report by the auditor.
