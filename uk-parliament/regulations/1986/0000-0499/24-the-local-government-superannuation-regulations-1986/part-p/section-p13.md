# The Local Government Superannuation Regulations 1986, section P13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (340).** Text as at 1986-03-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## P13. Provision as to protection of pensions[^c-29093033eeff213cc37ab310c2a66ce9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any increase in a pension which is required by virtue of sections 41A to 41C (protection of pensions) of the Pensions Act[^f00081] shall be paid out of the appropriate superannuation fund.
