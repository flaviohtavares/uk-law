# The Local Government Superannuation Regulations 1986, section G7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (340).** Text as at 1986-03-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## G7. Former clerks of the peace etc.[^key-331bb698804ddb3160932f862020b9c5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person—

- (a) to whom paragraph 13(2) of Schedule 10 to the Courts Act 1971[^f00055] (“paragraph 13(2)”) applied, and
- (b) who immediately before 1st March 1986 was under regulation J10 of the 1974 regulations contributing to the appropriate superannuation fund the amount specified in paragraph 13(2) (“the paragraph 13(2) amount”),

may, so long as he continues in the employment of the same body, continue to contribute the paragraph 13(2) amount.

(2) For the purpose of calculating the amount of any benefit, a person shall be treated as having received, for any period for which he has, under regulation J10 of the 1974 regulations or this regulation, contributed the paragraph 13(2) amount, the remuneration which he would have received but for the reduction by virtue of which paragraph 13(2) became applicable to him.
