## G1. Employees of magistrates' courts committees[^c-b4bc8dcb1a6b61ec58892e5b27fa9f2d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In their application to a person employed by a magistrates' courts committee these regulations have effect—

- (a) in any case, subject to the modifications in paragraphs 1 to 6, and
- (b) in the case of a justices' clerk (outside the inner London area), subject also to the modifications in paragraphs 7 and 8,

of Part I of Schedule 15.
