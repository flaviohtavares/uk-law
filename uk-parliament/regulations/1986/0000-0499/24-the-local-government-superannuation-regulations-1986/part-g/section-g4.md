## G4. Coroners[^c-6438e74ad5f226f316c1a8bd5b139d41]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In their application to a coroner who is by virtue of regulation B1(14) a pensionable employee these regulations have effect subject to the modifications in Part IV of Schedule 15.
