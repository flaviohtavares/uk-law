# The Local Government Superannuation Regulations 1986, section G2

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## G2. Certain employees of the committee of magistrates for the inner London area[^c-bef590177e26d0534dc9c27c9d14aa0c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In their application to a person who is a pensionable employee by virtue of regulation B1(13) these regulations have effect—

- (a) in any case, subject to the modifications in paragraphs 1 and 2, and
- (b) in the case of a justices' clerk (inner London area), subject also to the modifications in paragraphs 3 and 4,

of Part II of Schedule 15.
