# The Local Government Superannuation Regulations 1986, section N6

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## N6. Decisions by administering authorities as to benefits[^key-822bee2b8eaa536700f257bb38f9acb9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where a person is entitled to a benefit which is or may become payable out of their superannuation fund, an administering authority are to decide the amount of the benefit.

(2) The amount of a benefit is to be decided as soon as is reasonably practicable after the person becomes entitled to it.

(3) For the purposes of this regulation “**benefit**” includes a return of contributions and a benefit specified in regulation F6(12) or (16).
