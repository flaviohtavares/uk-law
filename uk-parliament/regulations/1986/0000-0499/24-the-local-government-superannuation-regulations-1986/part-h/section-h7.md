# The Local Government Superannuation Regulations 1986, section H7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (340).** Text as at 1986-03-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## H7. Gratuities etc. granted by former local authorities[^c-7c87dcdf8cfccf6535b52b12bdae6fef]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Notwithstanding the revocation of the 1974 regulations—

- (a) an authority who became responsible under regulation J11(1) of those regulations for the continued payment of a gratuity or allowance remain so responsible, and
- (b) an authority who became liable under regulation J1 IA of those regulations to contribute towards such continued payment remain so liable, and every agreement and every determination under that regulation continues to have effect,

as if the 1974 regulations had not been revoked.
