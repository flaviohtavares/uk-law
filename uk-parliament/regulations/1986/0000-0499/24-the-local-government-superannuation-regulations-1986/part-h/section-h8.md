# The Local Government Superannuation Regulations 1986, section H8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (340).** Text as at 1986-03-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## H8. Policy schemes[^key-14557d09b6313b9a795c9be744866056]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any agreement or trust deed which immediately before 1st March 1986 had effect as mentioned in regulation J12 of the 1974 regulations continues so to have effect, and any policy of insurance held by a scheduled body immediately before that date for the purposes mentioned in that regulation shall continue to be held by them for those purposes.
