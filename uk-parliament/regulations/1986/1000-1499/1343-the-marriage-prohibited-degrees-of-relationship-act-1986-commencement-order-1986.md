# The Marriage (Prohibited Degrees of Relationship) Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1986

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1986 No. 1343 (c. 48)

MARRIAGE

The Marriage (Prohibited Degrees of Relationship) Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1986

Made

30th July 1986

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 6(5) of the Marriage (Prohibited Degrees of Relationship) Act 1986, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Marriage (Prohibited Degrees of Relationship) Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1986.

**2.** The Marriage (Prohibited Degrees of Relationship) Act 1986 shall come into force on 1st November 1986.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

30th July 1986

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the Marriage (Prohibited Degrees of Relationship) Act 1986 into force on 1st November 1986.
