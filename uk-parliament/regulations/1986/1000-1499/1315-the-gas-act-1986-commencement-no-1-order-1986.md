# The Gas Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1986

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1986 No. 1315 (c.43)

GAS

The Gas Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1986

Made

28th July 1986

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 68(5) of the Gas Act 1986, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Gas Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1986.

**2.** (1) Sections 1, 65 and 68 of and Schedule 1 to, the Gas Act 1986 shall come into force on 18th August 1986.

(2) Sections 2, 63 and 64 of, and Schedule 2 to, that Act shall come into force on 23rd August 1986.

Peter Walker

Secretary of State for Energy

28th July 1986

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation the following provisions of the Gas Act 1986, namely—

- (a) on 18th August 1986, section 1 and Schedule 1 (the Director General of Gas Supply) and sections 65 (financial provisions) and 68 (short title, commencement and extent); and
- (b) on 23rd August 1986, section 2 and Schedule 2 (the Gas Consumers' Council) and sections 63 (restrictions on use of certain information) and 64provisions as to orders).
