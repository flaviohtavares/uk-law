# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 1

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**1.** These Regulations consolidate (with amendments) the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1978 as amended and the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Track Laying Vehicles) Regulations 1955 as amended. The Statutory Instruments specified in Schedule 1 are revoked and re-enacted in a new presentation with more extensive use of tabulations, more logical arrangement of material and, as far as possible, simplified language.
