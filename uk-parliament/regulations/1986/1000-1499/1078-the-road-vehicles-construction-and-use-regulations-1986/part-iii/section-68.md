# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 68

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 68. Plates—agricultural trailed appliances[^key-4b6cff5c24f284a00542a84457fb578b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Save as provided in paragraph (3) below, every wheeled agricultural trailed appliance manufactured on or after 1st December 1985 shall be equipped with a plate affixed to the vehicle in a conspicuous and readily accessible position and which is clearly and indelibly marked with the particulars specified in paragraph (2) below.

(2) Those particulars are—

- (a) the name of the manufacturer of the appliance;
- (b) the year in which the appliance was manufactured;
- (c) the maximum gross weight;
- (d) the unladen weight; and
- (e) the maximum load which would be imposed by the appliance on the drawing vehicle.

(3) In the case of a towed roller consisting of several separate rollers used in combination, a single plate shall satisfy the requirement specified in paragraph (2) above.
