# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 71

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 71. Marking of weights on certain vehicles[^key-da72f3ba5b440f96e1629df8ea668277]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This regulation applies to a vehicle (other than an agricultural motor vehicle which is either a track-laying vehicle not exceeding 3050 kg in unladen weight or a wheeled vehicle) which is—

- (a) a locomotive;
- (b) a motor tractor;
- (c) a bus[^key-1c62681c05af007b0b390f44d2eb1bcc] which is registered under the 1971 Act (or any enactment repealed thereby) [^key-5df768de4b21df4b8b20a192956689f9]...; or
- (d) an unbraked wheeled trailer, other than one mentioned in regulation 16(3)(b), (bb), (bc), (c), (d), (e), (f), or (g)[^key-4ee2c8f1dd1342864d514d5964a08d1a].

(2) There shall be plainly marked in a conspicuous place on the outside of a vehicle to which this regulation applies, on its near side—

- (a) if it is a vehicle falling in paragraph (1)*(a)*, *(b)*, or *(c)*, its unladen weight; and
- (b) if it is a vehicle falling in paragraph (1)*(d)*, its maximum gross weight.
