# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, SCHEDULE 7

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 7 — FIRE EXTINGUISHING APPARATUS AND FIRST AID EQUIPMENT FOR MINIBUSES

*Extent: E+W+S*

### PART I — FIRE EXTINGUISHING APPARATUS[^key-c20cd04f1c6d164e2083b6328259fa58]

(see regulation 42)

A fire extinguisher which complies in all respects with the specification for portable fire extinguishers issued by the British Standards Institution numbered BS 5423: 1977 or BS 5423: 1980 or BS 5423:1987 and which\_

- (a) has a minimum test fire rating of 8A or 21B, and
- (b) contains water or foam or contains, and is marked to indicate that it contains, halon 1211 or halon 1301.

### PART II — FIRST AID EQUIPMENT[^key-49fb072b485731345a4cb72ab11f0ad5]

(see regulation 43)

- (i) Ten antiseptic wipes, foil packed;
- (ii) One conforming disposable bandage (not less than 7.5 cm wide);
- (iii) Two triangular bandages;
- (iv) One packet of 24 assorted adhesive dressings;
- (v) Three large sterile unmedicated ambulance dressings (not less than 15.0 cm × 20.0 cm);
- (vi) Two sterile eye pads, with attachments;
- (vii) Twelve assorted safety pins; and
- (viii) One pair of rustless blunt-ended scissors.
