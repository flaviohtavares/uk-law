# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 105

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 105. Opening of doors[^c-75e8ddf437518aa1540c87da98d6456a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

No person shall open, or cause or permit to be opened, any door of a vehicle on a road so as to injure or endanger any person.
