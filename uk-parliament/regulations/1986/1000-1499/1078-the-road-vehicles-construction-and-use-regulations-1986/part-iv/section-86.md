# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 86

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 86. Distance between motor vehicles and trailers[^key-79132da533d6d69f24cfa07c4980fccb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where a trailer is attached to the vehicle immediately in front of it solely by means of a rope or chain, the distance between the trailer and that vehicle shall not in any case exceed 4.5 m, and shall not exceed 1.5 m unless the rope or chain is made clearly visible to any other person using the road within a reasonable distance from either side.

(2) For the purpose of determining the said distance any part of either vehicle designed primarily for use as a means of attaching the one vehicle to the other and any fitting designed for use in connection with any such part shall be disregarded.
