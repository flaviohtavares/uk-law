# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 106

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 106. Reversing[^key-e3ff13dcc4460d9379d66a4eea0a5ffe]

*Extent: E+W+S*

No person shall drive, or cause or permit to be driven, a motor vehicle backwards on a road further than may be requisite for the safety or reasonable convenience of the occupants of the vehicle or other traffic, unless it is a road roller or is engaged in the construction, maintenance or repair of the road.
