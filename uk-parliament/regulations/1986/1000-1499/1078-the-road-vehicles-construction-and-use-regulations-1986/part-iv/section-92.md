# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 92

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 92. Attachment of sidecars[^key-0d915c59b07ab4da92374b054e86f05b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Every sidecar fitted to a motor cycle shall be so attached that the wheel thereof is not wholly outside the space between transverse planes passing through the extreme projecting points at the front and at the rear of the motor cycle.
