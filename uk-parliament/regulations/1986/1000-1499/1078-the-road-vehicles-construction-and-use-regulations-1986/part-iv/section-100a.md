# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 100A

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**100A.**[^c-0b36d8a16163ec0d560675495449297f] (1) No person shall use, or cause or permit to be used, on a road a vehicle displaying the rectangular plate described in the definition of “low platform trailer” in the Table in regulation 3(2) or anything resembling such a plate at a speed exceeding 40 mph.

(2) No person shall use, or cause or permit to be used on a road a vehicle displaying the rectangular plate described in Schedule 13 (Plate for restricted speed vehicle) or anything resembling such a plate at a speed exceeding 50 mph.
