# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 53

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 53. Mascots[^key-5179f97f2bab27cddf08f4686da18753]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), no mascot, emblem or other ornamental object shall be carried by a motor vehicle first used on or after 1st October 1937 in any position where it is likely to strike any person with whom the vehicle may collide unless the mascot is not liable to cause injury to such person by reason of any projection thereon.

(2) Instead of complying with the requirements of paragraph (1) a vehicle may comply with Community Directive 74/483 or 79/488 or ECE Regulation 26.01.
