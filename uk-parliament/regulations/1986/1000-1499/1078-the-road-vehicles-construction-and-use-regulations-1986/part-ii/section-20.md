# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 20

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 20. General requirement as to wheels and tracks[^c-5d5272e51e98f594b243ad26de1aaa6b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Every motor cycle and invalid carriage shall be a wheeled vehicle, and every other motor vehicle and every trailer shall be either a wheeled vehicle or a track-laying vehicle.
