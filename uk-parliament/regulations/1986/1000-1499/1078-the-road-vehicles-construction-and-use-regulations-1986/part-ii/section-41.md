# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 41

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 41. Minibuses[^c-c06bb05e7fb9818341cb4123a55263c1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The requirements specified in Schedule 6 shall apply to every minibus first used on or after 1st April 1988 except a vehicle—

- (a) manufactured by Land Rover U.K. Limited and known as the Land Rover; or
- (b) constructed or adapted for the secure transport of prisoners.
