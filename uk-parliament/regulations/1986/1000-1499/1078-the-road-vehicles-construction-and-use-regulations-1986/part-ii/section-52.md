# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 52

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52. Maintenance of sideguards[^c-3d70a0812e4e0df745fbbf40a7f8cde0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Every sideguard fitted to a vehicle in compliance with the requirements of regulation 51 shall at all times when the vehicle is on a road be maintained free from any obvious defect which would be likely to affect adversely its effectiveness.
