# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 30

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**30.**[^key-6a73b98183ebb679705ddd8165745df5] (1) Every motor vehicle shall be so designed and constructed that the driver thereof while controlling the vehicle can at all times have a full view of the road and traffic ahead of the motor vehicle.

(2) Instead of complying with the requirement of paragraph (1) a vehicle may comply with Community Directive 77/649, 81/643 , 88/366, 90/630[^key-d4d050360810fb94e548487fdf08f0b1] or, in the case of an agricultural motor vehicle, 79/1073.

(3) All glass or other transparent material fitted to a motor vehicle shall be maintained in such condition that it does not obscure the vision of the driver while the vehicle is being driven on a road.
