# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986, section 4

> Text as at 2026-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**4.** In regulation 7 the maximum permitted length of motor vehicles having at least 4 wheels is increased from 11m to 12m in accordance with Community Directive 85/3, and, in consequence, the previous turning circle requirements for articulated buses are dropped.
