# The Friendly Societies Act 1984 (Isle of Man) Order 1986

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1986 No. 768

FRIENDLY SOCIETIES

The Friendly Societies Act 1984 (Isle of Man) Order 1986

Made

29th April 1986

Coming into Operation

29th May 1986

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 29th day of April 1986

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(3) of the Friendly Societies Act 1984, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Friendly Societies Act 1984 (Isle of Man) Order 1986 and shall come into operation on 29th May 1986.

**2.** It is hereby directed that sections 1 and 2 of the Friendly Societies Act 1984 shall extend to the Isle of Man with the adaptations and modifications specified in the Schedule to this Order.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — ADAPTATIONS AND MODIFICATIONS TO PROVISIONS OF THE FRIENDLY SOCIETIES ACT 1984 AS EXTENDED TO THE ISLE OF MAN

Article 2

**1.** (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2) below, any reference to an enactment shall be construed as a reference to that enactment as it has effect in the Isle of Man.

(2) References to the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1970 are to that Act as it has effect in the United Kingdom.

**2.** In section 1(4), for the words “subsections (3)(a) and (3A)” there shall be substituted the words “subsection (3)”.

**3.** (1) In section 2, subsection (4) shall be omitted.

(2) In section 2(7), for the words “(2) to (4)” there shall be substituted the words “(2) and (3)”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order extends sections 1 and 2 of the Friendly Societies Act 1984 to the Isle of Man, with the adaptations and modifications specified in the Schedule to the Order. Those provisions modify the effect of certain provisions of the Friendly Societies Act 1974 (c.46), which extends to the Isle of Man subject to the adaptations and modifications specified in the Friendly Societies (Isle of Man) Order 1975 (S.I. 1975/420).
