# The Companies (Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 1986

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1986 No. 2097

COMPANIES

The Companies (Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 1986

Made

1st December 1986

Coming into Force

29th December 1986

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 469(1), 470(3), 481(1)(b) and (2), 482(1), 485, 486, 495(2)(a) and (b), 496(1)(e), 497(2), 498(3) and 744 of the Companies Act 1985, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following regulations:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These regulations may be cited as the Companies (Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 1986 and shall come into force on 29th December 1986.

(2) In these regulations—

- “**the Act**” means the Companies Act 1985; and
- “**the principal regulations**” means the Companies (Forms) Regulations 1985.

## 2. Revocation and Savings

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) below, the following provisions of the principal regulations are hereby revoked.

- (a) Regulation 7(2);
- (b) in Regulation 7(3), the words “or (2)”;
- (c) in Schedule 3, Form Nos 469(Scot), 470(Scot), 478(Scot), 481(Scot), 482(Scot), 495(2)(a), 495(3)a, 495(3)b, and 497;
- (d) in Part I of Schedule 4, the words “482”; and
- (e) in Part II of Schedule 4, the words “495(3)a and 495(3)b”.

(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) above, the provisions of the principal regulations specified in that paragraph shall continue to apply in relation to any receiver appointed under section 467 of the Act before 29th December 1986 or to any receiver or manager appointed before that date to whom Part XIX of the Act applies.

Andrew Duguid

An Under Secretary

Department of Trade and Industry

1st December 1986

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These regulations revoke certain provisions in the Companies (Forms) Regulations 1985 relating to receivers or to receivers and managers.

The revocations do not apply in relation to any receiver appointed under section 467 of the Companies Act 1985 (Receivers (Scotland) before 29th December 1986 or to any receiver or manager appointed before that date to whom Part XIX of that Act applies (Receivers and Managers (England and Wales)).

These Regulations are consequential upon the coming into force on 29th December 1986 of the Insolvency Act 1986 (c. 45) and the subordinate legislation made thereunder applying to receivers or to receivers and managers appointed on or after that date, namely the Insolvency Rules 1986 (S.I. 1986/1925), the Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986 (S.I. 1986/1915) and the Receivers (Scotland) Regulations 1986 (S.I. 1986/1917).
