# The Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act (Commencement) Order 1986

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1986 No. 2088 (c. 81)

ANIMALS

The Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act (Commencement) Order 1986

Made

1st December 1986

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 30(3) of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act (Commencement) Order 1986.

(2) This Order does not extend to Northern Ireland.

**2.** The provisions of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986 mentioned in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st January 1987.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

1st December 1986

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order, which does not extend to Northern Ireland, brings into force on 1st January 1987 the whole of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986 apart from sections 7 and 10(3) and Schedule 2 (which relates to designated breeding and supplying establishments), and section 29 (which concerns the Act's application to Northern Ireland).
