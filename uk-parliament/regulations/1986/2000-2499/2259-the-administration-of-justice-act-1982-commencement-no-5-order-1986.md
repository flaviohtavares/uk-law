# The Administration of Justice Act 1982 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1986

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1986 No. 2259 (c.89)

SUPREME COURT OF ENGLAND AND WALES COUNTY COURTS

The Administration of Justice Act 1982 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1986

Made

15th December 1986

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 76(1) of the Administration of Justice Act 1982, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Administration of Justice Act 1982 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1986.

**2.** Sections 38 to 47 and section 75, so far as it relates to the County Courts Act 1959, of the Administration of Justice Act 1982 shall come into force on 2nd January 1987.

Hailsham of St Marylebone, C

Dated 15th December 1986

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the following provisions of the Administration of Justice Act 1982 into force on 2nd January 1987:

(a) Sections 38 to 47 (provision for the administration and investment of funds in court);

(b) Section 75 (repeals and revocations), as it relates to the County Courts Act 1959.
