# The British Council and Commonwealth Institute Superannuation Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1986

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1986 No. 1860 (C.66)

PENSIONS

The British Council and Commonwealth Institute Superannuation Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1986

Made

30th October 1986

In pursuance of section 3(2) of the British Council and Commonwealth Institute Superannuation Act 1986, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the British Council and Commonwealth Institute Superannuation Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1986.

**2.** The British Council and Commonwealth Institute Superannuation Act 1986 shall come into force in relation to the British Council on 10th November 1986.

Geoffrey Howe

Her Majesty's Principal Secretary of State for Foreign and Commonwealth Affairs

30th October 1986

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the British Council and Commonwealth Institute Superannuation Act 1986 into force on 10th November 1986 in relation to the British Council only.
