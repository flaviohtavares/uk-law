## 7.27. Court records[^key-b0e022ece1dbe38feb86e0f752a27fb9]

*Extent: E+W*

The court shall keep records of all insolvency proceedings, and shall cause to be entered in the records the taking of any step in the proceedings, and such decisions of the court in relation thereto, as the court thinks fit.
