## 7.3A. Application under section 176A(5) to disapply section 176A[^c-5b5e3767d2fbcdf63398e6757abd7db5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application under section 176A(5) shall be accompanied by an affidavit prepared and sworn by the liquidator, administrator or receiver.

(2) The affidavit shall state—

- (a) the type of insolvency proceedings in which the application arises;
- (b) a summary of the financial position of the company;
- (c) the information substantiating the applicant’s view that the cost of making a distribution to unsecured creditors would be disproportionate to the benefits; and
- (d) whether any other insolvency practitioner is acting in relation to the company and if so his address.
