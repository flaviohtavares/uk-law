## 7.46. Service of notices following appointment[^key-96e91cb42e42b4bed6d2b6c04e5bf6a7]

*Extent: E+W*

Any notice served on, or sent to, a person appointed under Rule 7.44 has the same effect as if it had been served on, or given to, the incapacitated person.
