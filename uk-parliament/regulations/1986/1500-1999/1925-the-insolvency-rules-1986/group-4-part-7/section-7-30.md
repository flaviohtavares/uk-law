## 7.30. File of court proceedings[^c-f86fd831366dea6035639b9c0d8e943d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In respect of all insolvency proceedings, the court shall open and maintain a file for each case; and (subject to directions of the registrar) all documents relating to such proceedings shall be placed on the relevant file.

(2) No proceedings shall be filed in the Central Office of the High Court.
