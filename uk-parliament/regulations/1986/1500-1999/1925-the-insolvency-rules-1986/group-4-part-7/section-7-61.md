# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 7.61

## 7.61. Office copies of documents[^key-1ead1fe82ac90723f62dace43aac30c2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any person who has under the Rules the right to inspect the court file of insolvency proceedings may require the court to provide him with an office copy of any document from the file.

(2) A person's rights under this Rule may be exercised on his behalf by his solicitor.

(3) An office copy provided by the court under this Rule shall be in such form as the registrar thinks appropriate, and shall bear the court's seal.
