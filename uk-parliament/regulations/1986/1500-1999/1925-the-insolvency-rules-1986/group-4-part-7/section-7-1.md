# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 7.1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1414).** Text as at 2008-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/1925
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7.1. Preliminary[^c-f03131e9dd6c6672384d31fcce41dc68]

*Extent: E+W*

This Chapter applies to any application made to the court under the Act or Rules except [^c-f2ef1ed9ba2d0cec6c25b09d87b22119]...—

- (a) an application for[^c-ecd65e8aa406c75e2860d1001cd8f655] an administration order under Part II,
- (b) a petition for[^c-d15b61cf2cc5f6702524dba1c6c90b36] a winding-up order under Part IV, or
- (c) a petition for[^c-5c880c47e197dd6fed46cb0bdb98cc30] a bankruptcy order under Part IX

of the Act.
