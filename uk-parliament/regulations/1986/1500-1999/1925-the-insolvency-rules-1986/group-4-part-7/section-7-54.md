## 7.54. Insolvency practitioner's solicitor[^c-b107266d572939bc788faba2990eb20c]

*Extent: E+W*

Where in any proceedings the attendance of the responsible insolvency practitioner's solicitor is required, whether in court or in chambers, the insolvency practitioner himself need not attend, unless directed by the court.
