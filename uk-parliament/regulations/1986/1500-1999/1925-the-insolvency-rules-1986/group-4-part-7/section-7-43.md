# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 7.43

## 7.43. Introductory[^c-339b2309f6854d4cf92c1e2d7009d285]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Rules in this Chapter apply where in insolvency proceedings it appears to the court that a person affected by the proceedings is one who lacks capacity within the meaning of the Mental Capacity Act 2005 to manage and administer his property and affairs[^c-ebf19ace186cf4a26bfa0ccf96abb275] either—

- (a) by reason of lacking capacity within the meaning of the Mental Capacity Act 2005[^c-124edf074e0cd1211869acaf2493a5a8], or
- (b) due to physical affliction or disability.

(2) The person concerned is referred to as “the incapacitated person”.
