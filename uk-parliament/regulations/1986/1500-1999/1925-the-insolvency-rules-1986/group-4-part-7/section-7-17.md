## 7.17. Remuneration[^c-6a4cbf26c9e4c58d0be1a40e662c0de6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The remuneration of a shorthand writer appointed in insolvency proceedings shall be paid by the party at whose instance the appointment was made, or out of the insolvent estate, or otherwise, as the court may direct.

(2)[^c-1728817e11441cc16ed0fe77a7a41b08] Any question arising as to the rates of remuneration payable under this Rule shall be determined by the court in its discretion
