## 7.41. Costs and expenses of witnesses[^c-c5eaab65272188f4ce7782597f0c6150]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Except as directed by the court, no allowance as a witness in any examination or other proceedings before the court shall be made to the bankrupt or an officer of the insolvent company to which the proceedings relate.

(2) A person presenting any petition in insolvency proceedings shall not be regarded as a witness on the hearing of the petition, but the costs officer may allow his expenses of travelling and subsistence.
