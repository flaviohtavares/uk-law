## 7.52. Right of audience[^key-c473339960ea79c71e41473f4887a255]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Official receivers and deputy official receivers have right of audience in insolvency proceedings, whether in the High Court or a county court.

(2) Subject as above, rights of audience in insolvency proceedings are the same as obtained before the coming into force of the Rules.
