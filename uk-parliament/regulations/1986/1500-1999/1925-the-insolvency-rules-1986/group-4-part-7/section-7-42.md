## 7.42. Final costs certificate[^c-c5eaab65272188f4ce7782597f0c6150]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A final costs certificate of the costs officer is final and conclusive as to all matters which have not been objected to in the manner provided for under the rules of the court.

(2) Where it is proved to the satisfaction of a costs officer that a final costs certificate has been lost or destroyed, he may issue a duplicate.
