# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 7.4A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1414).** Text as at 2008-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/1925

## 7.4A. Notice of application under section 176A(5)[^key-6adf90bee2b84258497523e2bb4139e1]

*Extent: E+W*

An application under section 176A(5) may be made without the application being served upon or notice being given to any other party, save that notice of the application shall be given to any other insolvency practitioner who acts as such in relation to the company including any member State liquidator.
