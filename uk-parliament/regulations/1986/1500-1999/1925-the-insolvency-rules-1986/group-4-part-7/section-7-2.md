# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 7.2

## 7.2. Interpretation[^c-64c7514261bbf730da14c36ab41a083a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [^m00200] In this Chapter, except in so far as the context otherwise requires—

- “**originating application**” means an application to the court which is not an application in pending proceedings before the court; and
- “**ordinary application**” means any other application to the court.

(2) [^m00201] Every application shall be in the form appropriate to the application concerned.
