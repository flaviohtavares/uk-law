## 7.45. Affidavit in support of application[^c-3f9e3fbaa02ae6ae521c7571be3241bc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Except where made by the official receiver, an application under Rule 7.44(3) shall be supported by an affidavit of a registered medical practitioner as to the mental or physical condition of the incapacitated person.

(2) In the excepted case, a report made by the official receiver is sufficient.
