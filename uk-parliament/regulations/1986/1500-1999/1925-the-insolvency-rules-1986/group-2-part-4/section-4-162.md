## 4.162. Removal[^c-f75ffbdce9afe37c0a10f2ffa3410b88]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A creditor member of the committee may be removed by resolution at a meeting of creditors; and a contributory member may be removed by a resolution of a meeting of contributories.

(2) In either case, 14 days' notice must be given of the intention to move the resolution.
