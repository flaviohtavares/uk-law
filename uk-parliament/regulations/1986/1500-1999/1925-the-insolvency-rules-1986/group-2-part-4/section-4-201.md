## 4.201. Costs not to fall on official receiver[^key-c6880ab1766eb3ec22711908791d0599]

*Extent: E+W*

The official receiver is not personally liable for any costs incurred by a person in respect of an application to set aside or vary his act or decision in settling the list of contributories, or varying or adding to the list; and the liquidator (if other than the official receiver) is not so liable unless the court makes an order to that effect.
