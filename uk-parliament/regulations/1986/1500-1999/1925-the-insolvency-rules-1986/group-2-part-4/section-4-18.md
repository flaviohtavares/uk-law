## 4.18. Affidavit in opposition[^key-a211b59f060f01b4eeba24e8a472f7a6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the company intends to oppose the petition, its affidavit in opposition shall be filed in court not less than 7 days before the date fixed for the hearing.

(2) A copy of the affidavit shall be sent by the company to the petitioner, forthwith after filing.
