## 4.92. Payments of a periodical nature[^key-82e6cb1ea8ce65229682998d7d6773b5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the case of rent and other payments of a periodical nature, the creditor may prove for any amounts due and unpaid up to the date when the company went into liquidation or, if the liquidation was immediately preceded by an administration, up to the date that the company entered administration[^key-a2c971961d780849f52176ab58c01f7c].

(2) Where at that date any payment was accruing due, the creditor may prove for so much as would have fallen due at that date, if accruing from day to day.
