## 4.151. Preliminary[^c-72737a3ae34423d327743d14aa87552f]

*Extent: E+W*

(NO CVL APPLICATION)

For the purposes of this Chapter—

- (a) an “**insolvent winding up**” is where the company is being wound up on grounds which include inability to pay its debts, and
- (b) a “**solvent winding up**” is where the company is being wound up on grounds which do not include that one.
