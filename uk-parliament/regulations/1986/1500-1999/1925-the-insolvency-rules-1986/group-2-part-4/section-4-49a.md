# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 4.49A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1414).** Text as at 2008-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/1925

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4.49A. Further information where liquidation follows administration[^key-7da60287d4822458f3b2d7bf257a3b57]

*Extent: E+W*

Where under section 140 the court appoints as the company’s liquidator a person who was formerly its administrator or a person is appointed as liquidator upon the registration of a notice under paragraph 83(3) of Schedule B1 to the Act[^key-cf42e25d78a77a259a1022f2766c29f1] and that person becomes aware of creditors not formerly known to him in his capacity as administrator, he shall send to those creditors a copy of any statement or report sent by him to creditors under Rule 2.33[^key-1b1e9c026f5a47c81cb67e78c8b01e3e], so noted as to indicate that it is being sent under this Rule.
