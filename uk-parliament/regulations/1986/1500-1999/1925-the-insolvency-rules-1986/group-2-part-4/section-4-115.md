## 4.115. Court's power to regulate meetings under Rules 4.113, 4.114-CVL[^key-4c18fe65c4689ff22626216a569bf307]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a meeting under Rule 4.113 or 4.114-CVL is to be held, or is proposed to be summoned, the court may, on the application of any creditor, give directions as to the mode of summoning it, the sending out and return of forms of proxy, the conduct of the meeting, and any other matter which appears to the court to require regulation or control under this Rule.
