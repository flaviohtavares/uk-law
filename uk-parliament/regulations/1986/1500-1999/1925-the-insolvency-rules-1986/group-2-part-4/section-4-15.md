## 4.15. Leave for petitioner to withdraw[^c-bfd37feba4c3947afe02a852c379ff4f]

*Extent: E+W*

If at least 5 days before the hearing the petitioner, on an ex parte application, satisfies the court that—

- (a) the petition has not been advertised, and
- (b) no notices (whether in support or in opposition) have been received by him with reference to the petition, and
- (c) the company consents to an order being made under this Rule,

[^m00038]the court may order that the petitioner has leave to withdraw the petition on such terms as to costs as the parties may agree.
