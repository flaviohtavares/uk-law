# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 4.13

## 4.13. Persons entitled to copy of petition[^key-321af603b6c806ef161a56191531ff02]

*Extent: E+W*

Every director, contributory or creditor of the company is entitled to be furnished by the solicitor for the petitioner (or by the petitioner himself, if acting in person) with a copy of the petition within 2 days after requiring it, on payment of the appropriate fee.
