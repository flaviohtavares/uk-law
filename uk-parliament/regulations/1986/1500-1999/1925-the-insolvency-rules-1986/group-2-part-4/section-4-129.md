## 4.129. Recourse of liquidator to meeting of creditors[^key-a24bad48186a4f81bff2615329be9e45]

*Extent: E+W*

If the liquidator's remuneration has been fixed by the liquidation committee, and he considers the rate or amount to be insufficient, he may request that it be increased by resolution of the creditors.
