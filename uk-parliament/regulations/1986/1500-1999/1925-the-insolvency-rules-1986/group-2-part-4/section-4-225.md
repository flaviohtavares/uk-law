## 4.225. Procedure following appeal under s.203(4) or 205(4)[^c-c77330c9da98a64e4fd7f8739625438f]

*Extent: E+W*

[^m00119] Following an appeal under section 203(4) or 205(4) (against a decision of the Secretary of State under the applicable section) the court shall send two sealed copies of its order to the person in whose favour the appeal was determined; and that party shall send one of the copies to the registrar of companies to comply with section 203(5) or, as the case may be, 205(6).
