## 4.205. Making and enforcement of the call[^key-7fe06b1bd7985cf44bde716f33c63cd9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [^m00109] Notice of the call shall be given to each of the contributories concerned, and shall specify—

- (a) the amount or balance due from him in respect of it, and
- (b) whether the call is made with the sanction of the court or the liquidation committee.

(2) [^m00110] Payment of the amount due from any contributory may be enforced by order of the court.
