# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 4.173

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4.173. Preliminary[^c-3f6040a5d5fa144725c0171ce10eed44]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Rules in this Chapter apply where—

- (a) the winding-up order has been made by the court upon an application under paragraph 79 of Schedule B1 to the Act[^c-9ed35f2fc61f3f6aa281dfe7e8009308], and
- (b) the court makes an order under section 140(1) of the Act appointing as liquidator the person who was previously the administrator.

(2) In this Chapter, “**insolvent winding up**”, “**solvent winding up**”, “**creditor member**” and “**contributory member**” mean the same as in Chapter 12.
