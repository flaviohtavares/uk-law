## 4.81. New liquidator appointed[^key-14506f6cbfceb8023f5df10e7cb6a4b7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a new liquidator is appointed in place of another, the former liquidator shall transmit to him all proofs which he has received, together with an itemised list of them.

(2) The new liquidator shall sign the list by way of receipt for the proofs, and return it to his predecessor.
