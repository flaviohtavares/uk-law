## 4.68-CVL. Chairman's discretion to allow vote[^c-a1e4aff81848511882e60c3d7f0c4d17]

*Extent: E+W*

At a creditors' meeting, the chairman may allow a creditor to vote, notwithstanding that he has failed to comply with Rule 4.67(1)(a), if satisfied that the failure was due to circumstances beyond the creditor's control.
