## 4.147. Vacation of office on making of winding-up order[^key-53947b8b5719a48db430e7a75eec1c66]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the liquidator vacates office in consequence of the court making a winding-up order against the company, Rule 4.144 applies as regards his obtaining his release, as if he had been removed by the court.
