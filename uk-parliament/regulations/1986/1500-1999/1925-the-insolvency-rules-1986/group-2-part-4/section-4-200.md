# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 4.200

## 4.200. Variation of, or addition to, the list[^c-253a589414ecc99d84de7371e2f94834]

*Extent: E+W*

The liquidator may from time to time vary or add to the list of contributories as previously settled by him, but subject in all respects to the preceding Rules in this Chapter.
