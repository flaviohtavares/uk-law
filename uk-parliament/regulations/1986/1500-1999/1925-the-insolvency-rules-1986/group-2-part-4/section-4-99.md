# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 4.99

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4.99. Realisation of security by creditor[^key-35b1d4ea55eb6b5cce0548efb315c9e8]

*Extent: E+W*

If a creditor who has valued his security subsequently realises it (whether or not at the instance of the liquidator)—

- (a) the net amount realised shall be substituted for the value previously put by the creditor on the security, and
- (b) that amount shall be treated in all respects as an amended valuation made by him.
