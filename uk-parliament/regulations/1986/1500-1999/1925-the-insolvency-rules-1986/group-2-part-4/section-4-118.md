## 4.118. Advertisement of removal[^key-b6d80b29f1895dd8b79f40fad765bbf9]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a new liquidator is appointed in place of one removed, the former shall, in giving notice of his appointment, state that his predecessor has been removed and (if it be the case) that he has been given his release.
