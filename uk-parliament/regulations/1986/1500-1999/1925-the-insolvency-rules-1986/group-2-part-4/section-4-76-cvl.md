# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 4.76-CVL

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4.76-CVL. Particulars of creditor's claim[^c-d071d7aaabc0a4216e2b123a38f090ae]

*Extent: E+W*

The liquidator, or the convener or chairman of any meeting, may, if he thinks it necessary for the purpose of clarifying or substantiating the whole or any part of a creditor's claim made in his proof, call for details of any matter specified in paragraphs (a) to (h) of Rule 4.75(1), or for the production to him of such documentary or other evidence as he may require.
