## 4.24. Application of Rules in Chapter 3[^c-6455b00b78ab3c1c64b1a71546c60ed1]

*Extent: E+W*

The following Rules in Chapter 3 apply, with the necessary modifications—

- Rule 4.16 (notice of appearance);
- Rule 4.17 (list of appearances);
- Rule 4.20 (notice and settling of winding-up order); [^c-c1467cca454a00397c0ad3094a9231c5]...
- Rule 4.21 (transmission and advertisement of order) ; and[^c-2f3b69ec68f07eb52c2964b845b8997b]
- Rule 4.21A (expenses of voluntary arrangement).
