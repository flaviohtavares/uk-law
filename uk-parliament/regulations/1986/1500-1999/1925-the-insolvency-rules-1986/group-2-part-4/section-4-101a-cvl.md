# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 4.101A-CVL

## 4.101A-CVL. Power to fill vacancy in office of liquidator[^M_F_5f28b8fb-90e4-40ec-ed8c-db675bc72f75]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a vacancy in the office of liquidator occurs in the manner mentioned in section 104 a meeting of creditors to fill the vacancy may be convened by any creditor or, if there were more liquidators than one, by the continuing liquidators.
