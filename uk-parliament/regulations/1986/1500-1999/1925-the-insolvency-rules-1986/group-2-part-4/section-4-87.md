# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 4.87

## 4.87. Negotiable instruments, etc[^key-642e9e518157155f5923d8f9ac5baffa]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless the liquidator allows, a proof in respect of money owed on a bill of exchange, promissory note, cheque or other negotiable instrument or security cannot be admitted unless there is produced the instrument or security itself or a copy of it, certified by the creditor or his authorised representative to be a true copy.
