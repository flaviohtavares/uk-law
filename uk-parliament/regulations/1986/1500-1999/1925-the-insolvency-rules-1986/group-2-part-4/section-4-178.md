## 4.178. Application of Chapter 12[^c-37fc820499928fecc6750262cd50ecb4]

*Extent: E+W*

Except as provided above in this Chapter, Rules 4.155 to 4.172A[^c-5b83414bbd625a363905d3681cff289f] in Chapter 12 apply to the liquidation committee following the issue of the liquidator's certificate under Rule 4.176, as if it had been established under section 141.
