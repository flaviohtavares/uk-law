# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 4.226

## 4.226. Preliminary[^key-c0745a7463c6e2bd5dd9cf0a0116f892]

*Extent: E+W*

The Rules in this Chapter—

- (a) relate to the leave required under section 216 (restriction on reuse of name of company in insolvent liquidation) for a person to act as mentioned in section 216(3) in relation to a company with a prohibited name, [^key-23542ac0cfe00fd874f8ab28478eed1b]...
- (b) prescribe the cases excepted from that provision, that is to say, those in which a person to whom the section applies may so act without that leave , and[^key-0a7d6ab9be15a65e5b4aa80eccfe75d8]
- (c) apply to all windings up to which section 216 applies, whether or not the winding up commenced before the coming into force of the Rules.
