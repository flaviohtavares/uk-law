## 4.136-CVL. Vacation of office on making of winding-up order[^key-81ce87e45f3ac58836168e2dd77e3fdb]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the liquidator vacates office in consequence of the court making a winding-up order against the company, Rule 4.122-CVL applies as regards his obtaining his release, as if he had been removed by the court.
