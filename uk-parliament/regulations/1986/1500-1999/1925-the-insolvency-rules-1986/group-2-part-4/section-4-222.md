## 4.222. Procedure for return[^key-a98a0343b1a281477d3a261448322257]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The liquidator shall inform each person to whom a return is made of the rate of return per share, and whether it is expected that any further return will be made.

(2) Any payments made by the liquidator by way of the return may be sent by post, unless for any reason another method of making the payment has been agreed with the payee.
