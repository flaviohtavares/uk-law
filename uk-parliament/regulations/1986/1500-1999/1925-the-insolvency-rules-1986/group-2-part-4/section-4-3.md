# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 4.3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1414).** Text as at 2008-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/1925

## 4.3. Time-limits[^c-2a6d46cdf357f826b1036e205e75f36a]

*Extent: E+W*

Where by any provision of the Act or the Rules about winding up, the time for doing anything is limited, the court may extend the time, either before or after it has expired, on such terms, if any, as it thinks fit.
