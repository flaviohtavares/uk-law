## 1.44. Withdrawal of nominee’s consent to act[^c-a24294a6c677ad37333268cfc6787cfd]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the nominee withdraws his consent to act he shall, pursuant to paragraph 25(5) of Schedule A1 to the Act, forthwith give notice of his withdrawal and the reason for withdrawing his consent to act to—

- (a) [^m00016] the registrar of companies;
- (b) [^m00017] the court;
- (c) the company; and
- (d) any creditor of the company of whose claim he is aware.
