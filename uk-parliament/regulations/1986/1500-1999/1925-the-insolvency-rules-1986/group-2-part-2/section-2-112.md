## 2.112. Applications for extension of administration[^key-3cd429780944af0d9d340d0898769129]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application to court for an extension of administration shall be accompanied by a progress report for the period since the last progress report (if any) or the date the company entered administration.

(2) When the administrator requests an extension of the period of the administration by consent of creditors, his request shall be accompanied by a progress report for the period since the last progress report (if any) or the date the company entered administration.

(3) The administrator shall use the notice of extension of period of administration in [^m00031]Form 2.31B in all circumstances where he is required to give such notice.
