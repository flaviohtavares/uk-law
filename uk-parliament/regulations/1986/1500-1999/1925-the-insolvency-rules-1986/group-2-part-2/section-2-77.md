## 2.77. Admission and rejection of proofs for dividend[^c-3cd429780944af0d9d340d0898769129]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A proof may be admitted for dividend either for the whole amount claimed by the creditor, or for part of that amount.

(2) If the administrator rejects a proof in whole or in part, he shall prepare a written statement of his reasons for doing so, and send it as soon as reasonably practicable to the creditor.
