## 2.87. Payments of a periodical nature[^key-3cd429780944af0d9d340d0898769129]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the case of rent and other payments of a periodical nature, the creditor may prove for any amounts due and unpaid up to the date when the company entered administration or, if the administration was immediately preceded by a winding up, up to the date that the company went into liquidation[^key-38614fce0e8202fb1149d31e836819d0].

(2) Where at that date any payment was accruing due, the creditor may prove for so much as would have fallen due at that date, if accruing from day to day.
