## 2.127. Notification and advertisement of appointment of joint administrator[^c-3cd429780944af0d9d340d0898769129]

*Extent: E+W*

Where, after an initial appointment has been made, an additional person or persons are to be appointed as joint administrator the same Rules shall apply in respect of giving notice of and advertising the appointment as in the case of the initial appointment, subject to Rule 2.128.

**2.128.** The replacement or additional administrator shall send notice of the appointment in [^m00040]Form 2.40B to the registrar of companies.
