## 2.94. Realisation of security by creditor[^key-3cd429780944af0d9d340d0898769129]

*Extent: E+W*

If a creditor who has valued his security subsequently realises it (whether or not at the instance of the administrator)—

- (a) the net amount realised shall be substituted for the value previously put by the creditor on the security; and
- (b) that amount shall be treated in all respects as an amended valuation made by him.
