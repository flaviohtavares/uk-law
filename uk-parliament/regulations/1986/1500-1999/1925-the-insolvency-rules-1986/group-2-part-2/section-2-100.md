# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 2.100

## 2.100. Notice of no dividend, or no further dividend[^c-3cd429780944af0d9d340d0898769129]

*Extent: E+W*

If the administrator gives notice to creditors that he is unable to declare any dividend or (as the case may be) any further dividend, the notice shall contain a statement to the effect either—

- (a) that no funds have been realised; or
- (b) that the funds realised have already been distributed or used or allocated for defraying the expenses of administration.
