# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 2.7

## 2.7. Notice to officers charged with execution of writs or other process , etc[^key-3cd429780944af0d9d340d0898769129][^key-db98a1163f996d28e36bae96ad7c819e]

*Extent: E+W*

The applicant shall as soon as reasonably practicable after filing the application give notice of its being made to—

- (a) any enforcement officer[^key-c43c2b58296a44473edf80eda488f9a5] or other officer who to his knowledge is charged with an execution or other legal process against the company or its property; and
- (b) any person who to his knowledge has distrained against the company or its property.
