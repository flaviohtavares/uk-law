# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 13.4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1414).** Text as at 2008-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/1925

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13.4. Notice, etc. to solicitors[^key-b1b7efcebdd2f96d22eb723b40abf2eb]

*Extent: E+W*

Where under the Act or the Rules a notice or other document is required or authorised to be given to a person, it may, if he has indicated that his solicitor is authorised to accept service on his behalf, be given instead to the solicitor.
