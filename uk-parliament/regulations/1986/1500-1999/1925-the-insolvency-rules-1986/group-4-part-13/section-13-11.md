# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 13.11

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13.11. “The appropriate fee”[^c-88b5b387b12570613b29e110dbde2166]

*Extent: E+W*

“**The appropriate fee**” means—

- (a) in Rule 6.192(2) (payor under income payments order entitled to clerical etc. costs) or Rule 6.193C(4) (payor under income payments agreement entitled to clerical etc costs)[^c-f890333c51a84e09def5b63606210749], 50 pence; and
- (b) in other cases, 15 pence per A4 or A5 page, and 30 pence per A3 page.
