# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 6.110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1414).** Text as at 2008-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/1925

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6.110. Discounts[^key-ea1291d120c4cdb458531bf9af197bea]

*Extent: E+W*

There shall in every case be deducted from the claim all trade and other discounts which would have been available to the bankrupt but for his bankruptcy, except any discount for immediate, early or cash settlement.
