## 6.104. Admission and rejection of proofs for dividend[^c-08c116aeba63664019cbd21d0a8a6d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A proof may be admitted for dividend either for the whole amount claimed by the creditor, or for part of that amount.

(2) If the trustee rejects a proof in whole or in part, he shall prepare a written statement of his reasons for doing so, and send it forthwith to the creditor.
