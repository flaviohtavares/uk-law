## 6.41. Statement of affairs[^c-eafbed656e2eb93dd89b929f63ee79dc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [^m00143] The petition shall be accompanied by a statement of the debtor's affairs, verified by affidavit.

(2) Section B of Chapter 5 below applies with respect to the statement of affairs.
