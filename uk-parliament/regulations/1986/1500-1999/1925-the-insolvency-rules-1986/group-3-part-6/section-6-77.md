# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 6.77

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6.77. General rule as to reporting[^c-059ee1d52af7171b6fdd6138aeb3c32f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court may, on the official receiver's application, relieve him of any duty imposed on him by this Chapter of the Rules, or authorise him to carry out the duty in a way other than there required.

(2) In considering whether to act as above, the court shall have regard to the cost of carrying out the duty, to the amount of the funds available in the estate, and to the extent of the interest of creditors or any particular class of them.
