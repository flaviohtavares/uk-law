## 6.103. Transmission of proofs to trustee[^c-eb216ffcead72b9d4ae1f7c58d1fc234]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a trustee is appointed, the official receiver shall forthwith transmit to him all the proofs which he has so far received, together with an itemised list of them.

(2) The trustee shall sign the list by way of receipt for the proofs, and return it to the official receiver.

(3) From then on, all proofs of debt shall be sent to the trustee and retained by him.
