# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 6.237E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1414).** Text as at 2008-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/1925

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6.237E. Interpretation[^c-78c7b777330ac8bace13f129695e3ff7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In Rules 6.237 and 6.237A, “**registered land**” has the same meaning as in section 132(1) of the Land Registration Act 2002.

(2) In Rules 6.237A and 6.237D, “**individual register**” has the same meaning as in the Land Registration Rules 2003.
