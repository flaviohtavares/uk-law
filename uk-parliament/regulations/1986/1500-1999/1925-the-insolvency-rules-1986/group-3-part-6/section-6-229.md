## 6.229. Presentation of petition[^key-745cbc9bf5523f2330303d7bc7184f9c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [^m00199] In criminal bankruptcy, the petition under section 264(1)(d) shall be presented to the High Court, and accordingly Rule 6.9 in Chapter 2 (court in which other petitions to be presented) does not apply.

(2) This does not affect the High Court's power to order that the proceedings be transferred.
