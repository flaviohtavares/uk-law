## 6.22. Amendment of petition[^c-18d8248dcedfefd0eab55578e6362402]

*Extent: E+W*

With the leave of the court (given on such terms, if any, as the court thinks fit to impose), the petition may be amended at any time after presentation by the omission of any creditor or any debt.
