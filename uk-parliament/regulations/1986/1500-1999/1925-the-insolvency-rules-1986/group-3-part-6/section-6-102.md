## 6.102. Proof of licensed moneylender[^c-beb5e8d1eddf7910c19c828fa08b504f]

*Extent: E+W*

A proof of debt in respect of a moneylending transaction made before 27th January 1980, where the creditor was at the time of the transaction a licensed moneylender, shall have endorsed on or annexed to it a statement setting out in detail the particulars mentioned in section 9(2) of the Moneylenders Act 1927.
