# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 6.108

## 6.108. Negotiable instruments, etc[^key-c3f11a23338e93aa081028e6948b6e88]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless the trustee allows, a proof in respect of money owed on a bill of exchange, promissory note, cheque or other negotiable instrument or security cannot be admitted unless there is produced the instrument or security itself or a copy of it, certified by the creditor or his authorised representative to be a true copy.
