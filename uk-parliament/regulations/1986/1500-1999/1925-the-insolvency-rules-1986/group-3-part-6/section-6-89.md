## 6.89. Chairman of meeting as proxy-holder[^key-4aec3711f4ce70057b5ba0b7a31fe6ec]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the chairman at a meeting holds a proxy for a creditor, which requires him to vote for a particular resolution, and no other person proposes that resolution—

- (a) he shall himself propose it, unless he considers that there is good reason for not doing so, and
- (b) if he does not propose it, he shall forthwith after the meeting notify his principal of the reason why not.
