## 6.27. Vacating registration on dismissal of petition[^key-6d828dc0a681dc640a47014adf896357]

*Extent: E+W*

[^m00136] If the petition is dismissed or withdrawn by leave of the court, an order shall be made at the same time permitting vacation of the registration of the petition as a pending action; and the court shall send to the debtor two sealed copies of the order.
