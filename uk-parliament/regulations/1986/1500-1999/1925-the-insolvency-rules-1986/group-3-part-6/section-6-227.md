## 6.227. Delivery up to later trustee[^c-d0f34f0b06c176aca6a7aa2e09db96be]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing trustee shall, as and when requested by the trustee for the purposes of the later bankruptcy, deliver up to the latter all such property and money as is in his custody or under his control in pursuance of Rule 6.226.
