## 6.249. Acceptance of the bankruptcy restrictions undertaking[^key-f1680a6cfbb1fcae58930e797b64ba80]

*Extent: E+W*

A bankruptcy restrictions undertaking signed by the bankrupt shall be deemed to have been accepted by the Secretary of State for the purposes of paragraph 9 of Schedule 4A of the Act when the undertaking is signed by the Secretary of State.
