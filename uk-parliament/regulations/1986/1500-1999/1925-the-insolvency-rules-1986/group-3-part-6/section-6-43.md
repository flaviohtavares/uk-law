## 6.43. Notice to Chief Land Registrar[^c-e86085276ada10deddcefb808501df8f]

*Extent: E+W*

[^m00144] When the petition is filed, the court shall forthwith send to the Chief Land Registrar notice of the petition, for registration in the register of pending actions.
