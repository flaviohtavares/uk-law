## 6.221. Deferment of issue of order pending appeal[^c-11bf7e45d50d48ba2008905bd39d268e]

*Extent: E+W*

An order made by the court on an application by the bankrupt for discharge under section 280 shall not be issued or gazetted until the time allowed for appealing has expired or, if an appeal is entered, until the appeal has been determined.
