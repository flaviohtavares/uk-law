## 6.233. Meetings under the Rules[^c-b2b3764a03af01104d5f36569ad313d5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following Rules in Chapter 6 of this Part do not apply in criminal bankruptcy—

- Rules 6.79 and 6.80 (first meeting of creditors, and business thereat);
- Rule 6.82(2) (the chairman, if other than the official receiver);
- Rule 6.88(2) and (3) (resolution for appointment of trustee).

(2) Rule 6.97 (supply of forms for proof of debts) does not apply.
