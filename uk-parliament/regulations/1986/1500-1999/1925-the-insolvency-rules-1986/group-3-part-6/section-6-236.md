## 6.236. Consolidation of petitions[^key-66fa786aae2e6b00dae8ee270c2988c5]

*Extent: E+W*

Where two or more bankruptcy petitions are presented against the same debtor, the court may order the consolidation of the proceedings, on such terms as it thinks fit.
