# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 6.88

## 6.88. Resolutions[^c-87c63720af4f8e7a0ea115d72fb48af7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject as follows,[^c-875a20fe8db31d9adf8314cded1bd9f2] at a meeting of creditors, a resolution is passed when a majority (in value) of those present and voting, in person or by proxy, have voted in favour of the resolution.

(2) In the case of a resolution for the appointment of a trustee—

- (a) if on any vote there are two nominees for appointment, the person who obtains the most support is appointed , provided that such support represents a majority in value of all those present (in person or by proxy) at the meeting and entitled to vote[^c-5492d695c990477aa483d71875f5d394];
- (b) if there are three or more nominees, and one of them has a clear majority over both or all the others together, that one is appointed; and
- (c) in any other case the chairman shall continue to take votes (disregarding at each vote any nominee who has withdrawn and, if no nominee has withdrawn, the nominee who obtained the least support last time), until a clear majority is obtained for any one nominee.

(3) The chairman may at any time put to the meeting a resolution for the joint appointment of any two or more nominees.

(4) Where a resolution is proposed which affects a person in respect of his remuneration or conduct as trustee, or as proposed or former trustee, the vote of that person, and of any partner or employee of his, shall not be reckoned in the majority required for passing the resolution.

This paragraph applies with respect to a vote given by a person (whether personally or on his behalf by a proxy-holder)[^c-5163c0544126809929c50b69936fc881] either as creditor or as proxy-holder[^c-2532726392e7fc5d27a42c7754ad3f93] for a creditor (but subject to Rule 8.6 in Part 8 of the Rules).
