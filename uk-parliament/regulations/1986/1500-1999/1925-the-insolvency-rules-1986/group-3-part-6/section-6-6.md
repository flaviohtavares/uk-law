## 6.6. Preliminary[^c-dda56a00604e04f20e8ced7e4a044909]

*Extent: E+W*

[^m00125] The Rules in this Chapter relate to a creditor's petition, and the making of a bankruptcy order thereon; and in those Rules “**the debt**” means ,except where the context otherwise requires, the debt (or debts) in respect of which the petition is presented.

Those Rules also apply to a petition under section 264(1)(c) (supervisor of, or person bound by, voluntary arrangement), with any necessary modifications.
