## 6.13. Notice to Chief Land Registrar[^key-6cae94ba4f280b7ffd08de4053f0e666]

*Extent: E+W*

[^m00129] When the petition is filed, the court shall forthwith send to the Chief Land Registrar notice of the petition together with a request that it may be registered in the register of pending actions.
