## 6.196. Custody of documents[^key-f9e98af303632edae71eb156f9df7fe8]

*Extent: E+W*

Where in compliance with an order under section 369 original documents are produced, and not copies, any person who, by order of the court under section 369(2) (authorised disclosure to persons with right of inspection), has them in his possession or custody is responsible to the court for their safe keeping and return as and when directed.
