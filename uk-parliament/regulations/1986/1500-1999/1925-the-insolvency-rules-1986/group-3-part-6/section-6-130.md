# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 6.130

## 6.130. Court's power to regulate meeting under Rule 6.129[^c-e6e60bfecc3bc6768eb3458183bcb7ff]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a meeting under Rule 6.129 is to be held, or is proposed to be summoned, the court may on the application of any creditor give directions as to the mode of summoning it, the sending out and return of forms of proxy, the conduct of the meeting, and any other matter which appears to the court to require regulation or control.
