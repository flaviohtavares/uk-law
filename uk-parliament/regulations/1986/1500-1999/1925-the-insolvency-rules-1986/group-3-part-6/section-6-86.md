## 6.86. Venue of meetings[^c-ca98a9c20d523f6634c8231d8b687dc5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In fixing the venue for a meeting of creditors, the person summoning the meeting shall have regard to the convenience of the creditors.

(2) Meetings shall in all cases be summoned for commencement between the hours of 10.00 and 16.00 hours on a business day, unless the court otherwise directs.

(3) [^m00157] With every notice summoning a creditors' meeting there shall be sent out forms of proxy.
