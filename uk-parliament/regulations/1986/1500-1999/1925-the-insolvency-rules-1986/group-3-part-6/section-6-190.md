## 6.190. Action to follow making of order[^key-7a2f58a9a58a65001b9e9dcac14a0fb2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [^m00189] Where the court makes an income payments order, a sealed copy of the order shall, forthwith after it is made, be sent by the trustee to the bankrupt.

(2) If the order is made under section 310(3)(b), a sealed copy of the order shall also be sent by the trustee to the person to whom the order is directed.
