## 6.85. Notice of meetings by advertisement only[^key-63ca940a8adddeb6791f4e52b927214d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the case of any meeting to be held under the Act or the Rules, the court may order that notice of it be given by public advertisement, and not by individual notice to the persons concerned.

(2) In considering whether to act under this Rule, the court shall have regard to the cost of public advertisement, to the amount of the funds available in the estate, and to the extent of the interest of creditors or any particular class of them.
