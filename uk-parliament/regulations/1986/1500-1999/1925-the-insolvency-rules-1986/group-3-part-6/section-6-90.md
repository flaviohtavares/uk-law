## 6.90. Suspension of meeting[^key-89abe4e864d6948ae04f9fc2639094b2]

*Extent: E+W*

Once only in the course of any meeting, the chairman may, in his discretion and without an adjournment, declare the meeting suspended for any period up to one hour.
