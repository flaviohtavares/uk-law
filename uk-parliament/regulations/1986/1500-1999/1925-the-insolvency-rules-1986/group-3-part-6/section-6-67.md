## 6.67. Preliminary[^c-4877c5d2a63b7fc723b4c8ed185e817e]

*Extent: E+W*

The Rules in this Section apply with respect to the statement of affairs required in the case of a person petitioning for a bankruptcy order to be made against him, and the further disclosure which is required of him in that case.
