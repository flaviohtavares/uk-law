## 6.26. Non-appearance of creditor[^c-037e0603a35226b131ed54b1f2bfa77f]

*Extent: E+W*

If the petitioning creditor fails to appear on the hearing of the petition, no subsequent petition against the same debtor, either alone or jointly with any other person, shall be presented by the same creditor in respect of the same debt, without the leave of the court to which the previous petition was presented.
