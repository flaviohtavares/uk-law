## 6.58. Preliminary[^c-e8fec63c7fc419882a5b8020c524a026]

*Extent: E+W*

The Rules in this Section apply with respect to the statement of affairs required by section 288(1) to be submitted by the bankrupt, following a bankruptcy order made on a creditor's petition, and the further and other disclosure which is required of him in that case.
