## 6.202. Expenses of getting in property for the estate[^key-dda3738fa57102c8bc9e5a923279232f]

*Extent: E+W*

Any expenses incurred by the trustee in acquiring title to after-acquired property shall be paid out of the estate, in the prescribed order of priority.
