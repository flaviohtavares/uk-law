## 6.228. Existing trustee's expenses[^key-48d071f136d528b524b8c18e9a82a483]

*Extent: E+W*

Any expenses incurred by the existing trustee in compliance with section 335(1) and this Chapter of the Rules shall be defrayed out of, and are a charge on, all such property and money as is referred to in section 334(3), whether in the hands of the existing trustee or of the trustee for the purposes of the later bankruptcy.
