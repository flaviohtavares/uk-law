## 6.112. Payments of a periodical nature[^key-76b0cc89d55abfba4aeaea33395241cf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the case of rent and other payments of a periodical nature, the creditor may prove for any amounts due and unpaid up to the date of the bankruptcy order.

(2) Where at that date any payment was accruing due, the creditor may prove for so much as would have fallen due at that date, if accruing from day to day.
