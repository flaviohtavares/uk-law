## 6.181. Duty to keep court informed[^key-c6df6e47fdcd3e1e8bab3d773022f593]

*Extent: E+W*

The trustee shall notify the court from time to time as to the persons to whom he has sent or given copies of the notice of disclaimer under the two preceding Rules, giving their names and addresses, and the nature of their respective interests.
