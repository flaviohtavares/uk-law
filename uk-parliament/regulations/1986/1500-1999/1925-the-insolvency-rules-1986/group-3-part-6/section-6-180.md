## 6.180. Additional notices[^c-d83a54c89fbb4e47d874a5f8a7a8c892]

*Extent: E+W*

The trustee disclaiming property may, without prejudice to his obligations under sections 315 to 319 and Rules 6.178 and 6.179, at any time give notice of the disclaimer to any persons who in his opinion ought, in the public interest or otherwise, to be informed of it.
