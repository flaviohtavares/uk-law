## 6.187. Purchase of replacement property[^c-f8cfddc79d23e91d4665939775c193a8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A purchase of replacement property under section 308(3) may be made either before or after the realisation by the trustee of the value of the property vesting in him under the section.

(2) The trustee is under no obligation, by virtue of the section, to apply funds to the purchase of a replacement for property vested in him, unless and until he has sufficient funds in the estate for that purpose.
