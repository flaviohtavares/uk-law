## 6.128. Leave to resign granted by the court[^c-5e1ee1495cbb00ce1b811525e2eaaa64]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [^m00166] If at a creditors' meeting summoned to accept the trustee's resignation it is resolved that it be not accepted, the court may, on the trustee's application, make an order giving him leave to resign.

(2) The court's order under this Rule may include such provision as it thinks fit with respect to matters arising in connection with the resignation, and shall determine the date from which the trustee's release is effective.

(3) The court shall send two sealed copies of the order to the trustee, who shall send one of the copies forthwith to the official receiver.

(4) [^m00167] On sending notice of his resignation to the court, as required by section 298(7), the trustee shall send a copy of it to the official receiver.
