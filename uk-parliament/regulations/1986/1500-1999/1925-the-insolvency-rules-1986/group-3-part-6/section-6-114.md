## 6.114. Debt payable at future time[^c-0faee0d9afb02e48d30717690164c13c]

*Extent: E+W*

A creditor may prove for a debt of which payment was not yet due at the date of the bankruptcy order, but subject to Rule 11.13 in Part 11 of the Rules (adjustment of dividend where payment made before time).
