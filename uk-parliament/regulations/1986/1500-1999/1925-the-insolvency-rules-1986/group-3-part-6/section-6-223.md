## 6.223. Bankrupt's debts surviving discharge[^key-4253d50e3b6f705413d8eff6fbc347dd]

*Extent: E+W*

Discharge does not release the bankrupt from any obligation arising under a confiscation order made under section 1 of the Drug Trafficking Offences Act 1986 or section 1 of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1987[^key-aa442466660839514b272ae9c8f1273b] or section 71 of the Criminal Justice Act 1988[^key-a0a703a4320b619c287bd91e1c69fdc0] or under Parts 2, 3 or 4 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002.[^key-025cc29122471387a8ce2f38a3bc8444]
