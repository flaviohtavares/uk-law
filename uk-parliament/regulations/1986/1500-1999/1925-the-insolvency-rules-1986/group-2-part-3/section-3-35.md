## 3.35. Vacation of office[^key-f9ff31949049fe00e77375d4bccb0f39]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The administrative receiver, on vacating office on completion of the receivership, or in consequence of his ceasing to be qualified as an insolvency practitioner, shall forthwith give notice of his doing so—

- (a)[^key-bdf2f30ba74be0c344b4ca45d419678d] to the company or, if it is in liquidation, the liquidator, and
- (b) [^key-f39ea892a1b22624a42166c74af93b4b]... to the members of the creditors' committee (if any).

(2) Where the receiver's office is vacated, the notice to the registrar of companies which is required by section 45(4) may be given by means of an indorsement on the notice required by section 405(2) of the Companies Act (notice for the purposes of the register of charges).
