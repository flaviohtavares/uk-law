## 3.19. The chairman at meetings[^key-818fba3acfc423eb7463822db628982c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to Rule 3.28(3), the chairman at any meeting of the creditors' committee shall be the administrative receiver, or a person nominated by him in writing to act.

(2) A person so nominated must be either—

- (a) one who is qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the company, or
- (b) an employee of the receiver or his firm who is experienced in insolvency matters.
