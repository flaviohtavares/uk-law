## 3.34. Receiver deceased[^key-659834d7062698a1e6996f3dea3a6ae0]

*Extent: E+W*

If the administrative receiver dies, the person by whom he was appointed shall, forthwith on his becoming aware of the death, give notice of it to—

- (a) [^m00031] the registrar of companies, [^key-6411a5c0a0bd8e59742448eac2c7b249]...
- (b) the company or, if it is in liquidation, the liquidator , and[^key-2df90b14687e30257b9a380809ea511a]
- (c) in any case, to the members of the creditors' committee (if any).
