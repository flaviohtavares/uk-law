# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 5.60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1414).** Text as at 2008-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/1925
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**5.60.**[^key-8d65eb9d3bcd19d0fe8fff34f33d3479][^M_F_b0164211-5029-4fd7-ab4d-0575a9715ee0] (1) In an order under section 261(2)(a), 261(2)(b) or 263D(3) the court shall include provision permitting vacation of the registration of the bankruptcy petition as a pending action, and of the bankruptcy order, in the register of writs and orders affecting land.

(2) The court shall as soon as reasonably practicable give notice of the making of the order to the Secretary of State.

(3) The former bankrupt may, in writing, within 28 days of the date of the order,[^key-c51dea749efa62f20a72ff9d7572330b] require the Secretary of State to give notice of the making of the order—

- (a) in the Gazette;
- (b) in any newspaper in which the bankruptcy order was advertised; or
- (c) in both.

(4)[^key-57ca647dc4f383f5014ea3d6dace8d44] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Where the former bankrupt has died, or is a person incapable of managing his affairs (within the meaning of Chapter 7 in Part 7 of the Rules), the references to him in paragraph (3) is[^key-178a905ef265fbf380c63094fbc2d2ce] to be read as referring to his personal representative or, as the case may be, a person appointed by the court to represent or act for him.
