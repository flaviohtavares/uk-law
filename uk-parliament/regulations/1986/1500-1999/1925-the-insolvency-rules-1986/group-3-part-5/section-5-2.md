# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 5.2

## 5.2. Preparation of proposal[^M_F_b0164211-5029-4fd7-ab4d-0575a9715ee0]

*Extent: E+W*

The debtor shall prepare for the intended nominee a proposal on which (with or without amendments to be made under Rule 5.3(3) below) to make his report to the court under section 256 or section 256A.
