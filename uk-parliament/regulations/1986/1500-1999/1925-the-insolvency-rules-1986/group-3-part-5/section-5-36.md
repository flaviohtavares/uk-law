## 5.36. Interpretation[^key-8d65eb9d3bcd19d0fe8fff34f33d3479][^M_F_b0164211-5029-4fd7-ab4d-0575a9715ee0]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter—

- “**voluntary arrangement**” means an individual voluntary arrangement under section 263A;
- “**proposal**” means the document setting out the terms of the voluntary arrangement which the debtor is proposing.
