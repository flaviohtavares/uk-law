# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 9.1

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9.1. Preliminary[^key-23995f209822ee1893f308bb07f44e04]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Rules in this Part relate to applications to the court for an order under—

- (a) [^m00212]section 236 (inquiry into company's dealings when it is, or is alleged to be, insolvent), or
- (b) [^m00213]section 366 (inquiry in bankruptcy, with respect to the bankrupt's dealings).

(2) The following definitions apply—

- (a) the person in respect of whom an order is applied for is “**the respondent**”;
- (b) “**the applicable section**” is section 236 or section 366, according to whether the affairs of a company or those of a bankrupt or (where the application under section 366 is made by virtue of section 368 a debtor are in question;
- (c) the company or, as the case may be, the bankrupt or debtor concerned is “**the insolvent**”.
