# The Insolvency Rules 1986, section 12.2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1414).** Text as at 2008-03-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1986/1925
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12.2. Costs, expenses, etc[^key-3da8d2c250dbbb21765db24b9d3cbfa8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-7c6bd448ce58c5fdb509cd9ff2ac2c89] All fees, costs, charges and other expenses incurred in the course of winding up , administration[^key-ba41668d61adacd1b82c7700b0172333] or bankruptcy proceedings are to be regarded as expenses of the winding up or the administration or, as the case may be, of the bankruptcy.

(2)[^key-f436709cce74537e32244abb3a44a0bd] The costs associated with the prescribed part shall be paid out of the prescribed part.
