## 12.11. General provisions as to service[^key-bdab5865a9d8e30380cb4bbda6a580c2]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to Rule 12.10 and Rule 12.12[^key-4c88c482e610fd2436f13b05278ea8fe], CPR Part 6 (service of documents) applies as regards any matter relating to the service of documents and the giving of notice in insolvency proceedings.
