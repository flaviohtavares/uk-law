## 12.15. Right to copy documents[^c-5c394a91462186c1356fe7b9ee0162a0]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the Act or the[^c-d9f3f351fceec35a018c4c9522ce59b9] Rules confer a right for any person to inspect documents, the right includes that of taking copies of those documents, on payment—

- (a) in the case of documents on the court's file of proceedings, of the fee chargeable under any order made under section 92 of the Courts Act 2003[^c-dfe2ac022cf6397123fe61656da4c44b], and
- (b) otherwise, of the appropriate fee.
