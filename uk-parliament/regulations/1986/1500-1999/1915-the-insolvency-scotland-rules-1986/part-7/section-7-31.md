# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 7.31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.31. Fees, expenses, etc.

All fees, costs, charges and other expenses incurred in the course of insolvency proceedings are to be regarded as expenses of those proceedings.
