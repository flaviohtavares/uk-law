# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 7.30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.30. Forms for use in insolvency proceedings

The forms contained in Schedule 5, with such variations as circumstances require, are the forms to be used for the purposes of the provisions of the Act or the Rules which are referred to in those forms.
