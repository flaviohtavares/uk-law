# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 7.2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.2. Summoning of meetings

(1) In fixing the date, time and place for a meeting, the person summoning the meeting (“the convenor”) shall have regard to the convenience of the persons who are to attend.

(2) Meetings shall in all cases be summoned for commencement between 10.00 and 16.00 hours on a business day, unless the court otherwise directs.
