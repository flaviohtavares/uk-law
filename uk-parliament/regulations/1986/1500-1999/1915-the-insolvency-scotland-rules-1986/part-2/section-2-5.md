# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 2.5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.5. Form of the statement of affairs

(1) The statement of affairs shall be in the form required by Rule 7.30 and Schedule 5.[^m00012]

(2) The administrator shall insert any statement of affairs submitted to him in the sederunt book.
