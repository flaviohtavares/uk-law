# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 2.4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.4. Notice requiring statement of affairs

(1) This Rule and Rules 2.5 and 2.6 apply where the administrator decides to require a statement as to the affairs of the company to be made out and submitted to him in accordance with section 22.

(2) The administrator shall send to each of the persons upon whom he decides to make such a requirement under section 22, a notice in the form required by Rule 7.30 and Schedule 5 requiring him to make out and submit a statement of affairs.[^m00011]

(3) Any person to whom a notice is sent under this Rule is referred to in this Chapter as “**a deponent**”.
