# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 4.47

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.47. Quorum

A meeting of the committee is duly constituted if due notice of it has been given to all the members, and at least 2 creditor members or, in the case of a committee of contributories, 2 contributory members are present or represented.
