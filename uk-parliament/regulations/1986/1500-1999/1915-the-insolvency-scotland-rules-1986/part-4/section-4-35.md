# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 4.35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.35. Creditors' claim that remuneration is excessive

(1) If the liquidator's remuneration has been fixed by the liquidation committee or by the creditors, any creditor or creditors of the company representing in value at least 25 per cent of the creditors may apply to the court for an order that the liquidator's remuneration be reduced, on the grounds that it is, in all the circumstances, excessive.

(2) If the court considers the appliction to be well-founded, it shall make an order fixing the remuneration at a reduced amount or rate.

(3) Unless the court orders otherwise, the expenses of the application shall be paid by the applicant, and are not payable as an expense of the liquidation.
