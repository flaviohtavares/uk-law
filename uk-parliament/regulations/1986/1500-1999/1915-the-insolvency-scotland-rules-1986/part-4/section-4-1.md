# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 4.1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.1. Appointment of provisional liquidator

An application to the court for the appointment of a provisional liquidator under section 135 may be made by the petitioner in the winding up, or by a creditor of the company, or by a contributory, or by the company itself, or by any person who under any enactment would be entitled to present a petition for the winding up of the company.
