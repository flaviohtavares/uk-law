# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 4.77

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.77. Dissolution after winding up

Where the court makes an order under section 204(5) or 205(5), the person on whose application the order was made shall deliver to the registrar of companies a copy of the order.[^m00065]
