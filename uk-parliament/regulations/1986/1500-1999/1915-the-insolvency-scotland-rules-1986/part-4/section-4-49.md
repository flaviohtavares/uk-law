# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 4.49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.49. Resignation

A member of the liquidation committee may resign by notice in writing delivered to the liquidator.
