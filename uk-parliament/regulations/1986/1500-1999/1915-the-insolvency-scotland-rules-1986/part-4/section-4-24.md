# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 4.24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.24. Procedure on liquidator's removal

(1) Where the creditors have resolved that the liquidator be removed, the chairman of the creditors' meeting shall forthwith -

- (a) if, at the meeting, another liquidator was not appointed, send a certificate of the liquidator's removal to the court and to the registrar of companies, and[^m00034][^m00035]
- (b) otherwise, deliver the certificate to the new liquidator, who shall forthwith send it to the court and to the registrar of companies.[^m00036][^m00037]

(2) The liquidator's removal is effective as from such date as the meeting of the creditors shall determine, and this shall be stated in the certificate of removal.
