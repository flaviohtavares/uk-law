# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 4.37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.37. Loss of qualification as insolvency practitioner

(1) This Rule applies where the liquidator vacates office on ceasing to be qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the company.

(2) He shall forthwith give notice of his doing so to the court and to the registrar of companies.[^m00052]

(3) Rule 4.25(2) and (3) apply as regards the liquidator obtaining his release, as if he had been removed by the court.
