# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 4.51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.51. Removal

A creditor member of the committee may be removed by resolution at a meeting of creditors; and a contributory member may be removed by a resolution of a meeting of contributories.
