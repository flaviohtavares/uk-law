# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 4.27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.27. Advertisement of removal

Where a new liquidator is appointed in place of the one removed, Rules 4.19 to 4.21 shall apply to the appointment of the new liquidator except that the notice to be given by the new liquidator under Rule 4.19(4) shall also state -[^m00044]

- (a) that his predecessor as liquidator has been removed; and
- (b) whether his predecessor has been released.
