# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 4.33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.33. Recourse of liquidator to meeting of creditors

If the liquidator's remuneration has been fixed by the liquidation committee and he considers the amount to be insufficient, he may request that it be increased by resolution of the creditors.
