# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Application of Part 4

The provisions of Part 4, which are specified in Schedule 2, shall apply in relation to a members' voluntary winding up of a company as they apply in a winding up by the court, subject to the modifications specified in Schedule 2 and to any other necessary modifications.
