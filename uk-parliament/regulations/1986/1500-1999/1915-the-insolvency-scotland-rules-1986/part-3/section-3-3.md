# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 3.3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.3. Expenses of statement of affairs

(1) A deponent who makes up and submits to the receiver a statement of affairs shall be allowed and be paid by the receiver, as an expense of the receivership, any expenses incurred by the deponent in so doing which the receiver considers to be reasonable.

(2) Any decision by the receiver under this Rule is subject to appeal to the court.

(3) Nothing in this Rule relieves a deponent from any obligation to make up and submit a statement of affairs, or to provide information to the receiver.
