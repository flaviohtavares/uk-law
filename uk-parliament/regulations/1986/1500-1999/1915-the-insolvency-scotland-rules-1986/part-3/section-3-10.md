# The Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986, section 3.10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.10. Receiver deceased

If the receiver dies, the holder of the floating charge by virtue of which he was appointed shall, forthwith on his becoming aware of the death, give notice of it to -[^m00024]

- (a) the registrar of companies,
- (b) the members of the creditors' committee (if any),
- (c) the company or, if it is in liquidation, the liquidator,
- (d) the holder of any other floating charge and any receiver appointed by him.
