# The Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1972 (Commencement) Order 1972

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1972 No. 1260 (C.31)

AGRICULTURE

The Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1972 (Commencement) Order 1972

Made

14th August 1972

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, acting jointly, in pursuance of section 27(3) of the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1972 and all their other enabling powers, hereby make the following order:—

**1.** This order may be cited as the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1972 (Commencement) Order 1972.

**2.** Section 18 of the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1972 (which section amends the power to obtain agricultural statistics by means of notices), and Schedule 6 to that Act so far as it relates to sections 77 and 78 of the Agriculture Act 1947, shall come into operation on 15th August 1972.

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 10th August 1972.

J.M.L. Prior

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Gordon Campbell

Secretary of State for Scotland

14th August 1972
