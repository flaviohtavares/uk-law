# The Road Traffic (Foreign Vehicles) Act 1972 Commencement Order 1972

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1972 No. 1018 (C.17)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic (Foreign Vehicles) Act 1972 Commencement Order 1972

Made

4th July 1972

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of his powers under section 8(2) of the Road Traffic (Foreign Vehicles) Act 1972 and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** The Road Traffic (Foreign Vehicles) Act 1972 shall come into operation on 31st July 1972.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Foreign Vehicles) Act 1972 Commencement Order 1972.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

S.M.A. Banister

An Under Secretary in the Department of the Environment

4th July 1972

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation, on 31st July 1972, the Road Traffic (Foreign Vehicles) Act 1972 which makes provision, in relation to foreign goods vehicles and foreign public service vehicles, for securing the observance of certain statutory provisions relating to road traffic.
