# The War Pensions (Mercantile Marine) (Amendment) Scheme 1972

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1972 No. 1434

PENSIONS

The War Pensions (Mercantile Marine) (Amendment) Scheme 1972

Made

21st September 1972

Coming into Operation

2nd October 1972

The Secretary of State for Social Services, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 3, 4 and 7 of the Pensions (Navy, Army, Air Force and Mercantile Marine) Act 1939, as amended by the Pensions (Mercantile Marine) Act 1942, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Scheme:—

## 1. Citation, interpretation and commencement

This Scheme, which may be cited as the War Pensions (Mercantile Marine) (Amendment) Scheme 1972, amends the War Pensions (Mercantile Marine) Scheme 1964[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “**the principal Scheme**”), and shall come into operation on 2nd October 1972.

## 2. Revocation of Article 20 of the principal Scheme

Article 20 of the principal Scheme (awards on death) is hereby revoked.

Keith Joseph

Secretary of State for Social Services

19th September 1972

We consent.

P.L. Hawkins

V.H. Goodhew

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty's Treasury

21st September 1972

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Scheme amends the War Pensions (Mercantile Marine) Scheme 1964 so as to remove a restriction under which no award could be made in respect of the death of a member of the Merchant Navy, occurring more than 7 years after the date of the relevant qualifying injury or end of detention, unless he had been in receipt of a pension in respect of disablement.
