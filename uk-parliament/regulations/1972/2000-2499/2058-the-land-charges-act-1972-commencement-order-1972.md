# The Land Charges Act 1972 (Commencement) Order 1972

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1972 No. 2058 (C.47)

LAND CHARGES

The Land Charges Act 1972 (Commencement) Order 1972

Made

21st December 1972

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 19(2) of the Land Charges Act 1972, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Land Charges Act 1972 (Commencement) Order 1972.

**2.** The Land Charges Act 1972 shall come into operation on 29th January 1973.

Hailsham of St. Marylebone, C

Dated 21st December 1972

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the Land Charges Act 1972 into operation on 29th January 1973.
