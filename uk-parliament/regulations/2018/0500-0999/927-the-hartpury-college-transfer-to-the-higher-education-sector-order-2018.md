# The Hartpury College (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 927

Education, England

The Hartpury College (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2018

Made

1st August 2018

Laid before Parliament

8th August 2018

Coming into force

13th September 2018

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 122A(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001].

In accordance with section 122A(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988, it appears to the Secretary of State that the full-time equivalent enrolment number of the institution conducted by Hartpury College for courses of higher education exceeds 55 per cent of its total full-time equivalent enrolment number.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Hartpury College (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2018 and comes into force on 13th September 2018.

(2) This Order applies in relation to England.

## 2. Transfer to the higher education sector

On 13th September 2018, Hartpury College[^f00002] is transferred to the higher education sector.

Sam Gyimah

Minister of State

Department for Education

1st August 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, made under section 122A of the Education Reform Act 1988, provides for the transfer of Hartpury College from the further education sector to the higher education sector on 13th September 2018.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations. The instrument has no impact on the public sector. The Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
