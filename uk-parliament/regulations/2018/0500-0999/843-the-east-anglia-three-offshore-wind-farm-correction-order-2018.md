# The East Anglia THREE Offshore Wind Farm (Correction) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 843

Infrastructure Planning

The East Anglia THREE Offshore Wind Farm (Correction) Order 2018

Made

12th July 2018

Coming into force

13th July 2018

The East Anglia THREE Offshore Wind Farm Order 2017 (“the 2017 Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of the Act[^f00003].

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period, as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00004] for the correction of errors and omissions in the 2017 Order.

The Secretary of State has informed Suffolk Coastal District Council and Mid Suffolk District Council, as the relevant local planning authorities for the area in which the land to which the 2017 Order relates is situated, that the request has been received in accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraphs 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the East Anglia THREE Offshore Wind Farm (Correction) Order 2018 and comes into force on 13th July 2018.

## 2. Corrections

(1) The East Anglia THREE Offshore Wind Farm Order 2017 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

Gareth Leigh

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning

Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

12th July 2018

(This note is not part of the Order)
