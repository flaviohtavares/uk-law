# The Statute Law (Repeals) Measure 2018 (Commencement) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 718 (C. 55)

Ecclesiastical Law, England

The Statute Law (Repeals) Measure 2018 (Commencement) Order 2018

Made

13th June 2018

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2 of the Statute Law (Repeals) Measure 2018[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Statute Law (Repeals) Measure 2018 (Commencement) Order 2018.

## 2. Commencement on 1st July 2018

Section 1 of, and the Schedule to, the Statute Law (Repeals) Measure 2018 come into force on 1st July 2018.

\+Justin Cantuar:

Archbishop of Canterbury

\+Sentamu Ebor:

Archbishop of York

13th June 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the substantive provisions of the Statute Law (Repeals) Measure 2018.

Article 2 provides for section 1 and the Schedule to come into force on 1st July 2018. Section 2 came into force on Royal Assent, in accordance with that section.
