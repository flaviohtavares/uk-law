# The Registration (Entries of Overseas Births and Deaths) (Amendment) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2017/1279](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1279) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

This Order in Council is made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 41(4)(a) of the British Nationality Act 1981[^f00001].

Accordingly, Her Majesty is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to make the following Order in Council:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Registration (Entries of Overseas Births and Deaths) (Amendment) Order 2018 and comes into force on 22nd May 2018.

(2) In this Order “the 1982 Order” means the Registration (Entries of Overseas Births and Deaths) Order 1982[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the 1982 Order

The 1982 Order is amended in accordance with articles 3 and 4.

## 3. Amendment of Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the 1982 Order

In Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the 1982 Order[^f00003], omit—

> - The Registration of Overseas Births and Deaths Regulations 2014 ([S.I. 2014/511](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/511))[^f00004].

## 4. Amendment of Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the 1982 Order

In Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the 1982 Order, at the end insert—

> - The Registration of Overseas Births and Deaths Regulations 2014 ([S.I. 2014/511](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/511)).

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Registration (Entries of Overseas Births and Deaths) Order 1982 ([S.I. 1982/1526](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1982/1526)) (“the 1982 Order”). The 1982 Order applies provisions of enactments relating to the registration of births and deaths in the United Kingdom to certified copies of birth and death entries sent for deposit with the Registrars General in the United Kingdom.

Articles 3 to 4 of this Order make amendments to Part 1 of Schedule 1 and Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the 1982 Order. This corrects a typographical error made in The Registration (Entries of Overseas Births and Deaths) (Amendment) Order 2017 ([S.I. 2017/1279](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1279)).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
