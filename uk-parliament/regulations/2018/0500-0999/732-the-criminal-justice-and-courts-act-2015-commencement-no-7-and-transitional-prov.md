# The Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015 (Commencement No. 7 and Transitional Provision) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 732 (C. 57)

Immigration

Supreme Court Of The United Kingdom

The Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015 (Commencement No. 7 and Transitional Provision) Order 2018

Made

7th June 2018

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 95(1) and (6)(b) of the Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015[^f00001], makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015 (Commencement No. 7 and Transitional Provision) Order 2018.

(2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015.

## 2. Commencement of section 6 of the Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015

Section 66 of the Act (appeals from the Special Immigration Appeals Commission to the Supreme Court) comes into force on the twenty-first day after the day on which this Order is made.

## 3. Transitional provision

The amendments made by section 66 of the Act do not apply in relation to a final determination of the Special Immigration Appeals Commission made before the twenty-first day after the day on which this Order is made.

Lucy Frazer

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

7th June 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the seventh commencement order made under the Criminal Justice and Courts Act [2015 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2) (“the Act”).

Article 2 brings into force section 66 of the Act, which amends the Special Immigration Appeals Commission Act [1997 (c. 68)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/68) to make provision for appeals (known as “leapfrog” appeals) from final determinations of the Special Immigration Appeals Commission (“the Commission”) to the Supreme Court if certain conditions are met.

Article 3 provides that amendments made by section 66 of the Act do not apply to a final determination made by the Commission before section 66 comes into force, so that only final determinations made on or after that date may be the subject of a “leapfrog” appeal.
