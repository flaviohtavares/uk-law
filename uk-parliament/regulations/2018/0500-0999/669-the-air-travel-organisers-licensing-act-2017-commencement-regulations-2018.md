# The Air Travel Organisers’ Licensing Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 669 (C. 53)

Civil Aviation

The Air Travel Organisers’ Licensing Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2018

Made

4th June 2018

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 4(1) of the Air Travel Organisers’ Licensing Act 2017[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Air Travel Organisers’ Licensing Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2018.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 1st July 2018

Section 3 of the Air Travel Organisers’ Licensing Act 2017 comes into force on 1st July 2018.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Sugg of Coldharbour

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

4th June 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force section 3 of the Air Travel Organisers’ Licensing Act [2017 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/33), the only provision of that Act not commenced on Royal Assent. Section 3 will come into force on 1st July 2018.
