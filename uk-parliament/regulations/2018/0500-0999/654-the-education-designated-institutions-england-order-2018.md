# The Education (Designated Institutions) (England) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 654

Education, England

The Education (Designated Institutions) (England) Order 2018

Made

23rd May 2018

Laid before Parliament

1st June 2018

Coming into force

22nd June 2018

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 129(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Education (Designated Institutions) (England) Order 2018 and comes into force on 22nd June 2018.

(2) This Order applies in relation to England.

## 2. Designation of institution

Leeds College of Music[^f00002], being an institution which appears to the Secretary of State to fall within section 129(2) of the Education Reform Act 1988, is designated as an institution eligible to receive support from funds administered by a higher education funding council[^f00003].

Sam Gyimah

Minister of State

Department for Education

23rd May 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates Leeds College of Music as an institution eligible to receive support from funds administered by a higher education funding council.

An impact assessment has not been produced for the Order because it has no impact on businesses, civil society organisations or the public sector. The Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk .
