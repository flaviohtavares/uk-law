# The Armed Forces (Flexible Working) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 799 (C. 61)

Defence

The Armed Forces (Flexible Working) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2018

Made

29th June 2018

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 3(3) of the Armed Forces (Flexible Working) Act 2018[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Armed Forces (Flexible Working) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2018.

## 2. Commencement

Sections 1 (regular forces: part-time service and geographic restrictions) and 2 (consequential amendments) of the Armed Forces (Flexible Working) Act 2018 come into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Tobias Ellwood

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Defence

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force sections 1 and 2 of the Armed Forces (Flexible Working) Act [2018 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/2) (“the 2018 Act”) on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made. Section 3 of the 2018 Act (the only other provision of that Act) came into force on the day on which the Act was passed.

Section 1 of the 2018 Act amends section 329 of the Armed Forces Act [2006 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52) (“the 2006 Act”). Section 329 of the 2006 Act enables the Defence Council to make regulations with respect to the terms and conditions of enlistment and service of persons enlisting, or who have enlisted, in the regular armed forces. The amendments made by section 1 of the 2018 Act enable those regulations to make provision for members of the regular armed forces to serve on a part-time basis and to serve subject to geographic restrictions.

Section 2 of the 2018 Act makes amendments consequential on the introduction of part-time service in the regular armed forces.
