# The Education (Postgraduate Doctoral Degree Loans and the Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) (Amendment) (No. 2) etc.) Regulations 2018, section 23

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Amendment of regulation 15

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulation 15 (timing of repayments: general) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1) [^c22944201], omit “master's”.

(3) For paragraph (2ZA) [^c22944211], substitute —

> (2ZA) Subject to paragraph (2C) a postgraduate degree loan borrower is not required to repay –
>
> - (a) any part of a postgraduate master's degree loan, before the start of the following tax year commencing on 6 April after the borrower ceases to be eligible for a loan under Part 1 of the 2016 Master's Degree Regulations or Part 4 of the 2017 Postgraduate Wales Regulations, whether by reason of having completed that course or otherwise;
> - (b) any part of a postgraduate doctoral degree loan, until the earlier of—
>   - (i) the start of the following tax year commencing on 6 April after the borrower ceases to be eligible for a loan under Part 1 of the 2018 Doctoral Degree Regulations, whether by reason of having completed that course or otherwise; or
>   - (ii) the start of the following tax year commencing on 6 April after the fourth anniversary of the course start date.

.

(4) For paragraph (2C) [^c22944221], substitute –

> A borrower is not required to repay any part of the postgraduate degree loan under paragraph (2ZA) before 6 April 2019.

.

(5) In paragraph (7) [^c22944231], omit “master's”.
