# The National Grid (Richborough Connection Project) (Correction) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 572

Infrastructure Planning

The National Grid (Richborough Connection Project) (Correction) Order 2018

Made

4th May 2018

Coming into force

8th May 2018

The National Grid (Richborough Connection Project) Development Consent Order 2017 (“the Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of the Act[^f00003].

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period, as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00004] for the correction of errors and omissions in the Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed Canterbury City Council, Thanet District Council and Dover District Council, as the local planning authorities for the area in which the land to which the Order relates is situated, that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraphs 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the National Grid (Richborough Connection Project) (Correction) Order 2018 and comes into force on 8th May 2018.

## 2. Corrections

Corrections to be made to the National Grid (Richborough Connection Project) Development Consent Order 2017 are set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy,

Gareth Leigh

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning

Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

4th May 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects correctable errors in the National Grid (Richborough Connection Project) Development Consent Order 2017 ([S.I. 2017 No. 817](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/817)), a development consent order made under the Planning Act [2008 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/29), following a request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
