# The Silvertown Tunnel Order 2018, section 19

> Text as at 2019-02-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 19. Compulsory acquisition of land

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) TfL may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised development, or to facilitate it, or as is incidental to it.

(2) This article is subject to article 22(2) (compulsory acquisition of rights), article 27(2) (acquisition of subsoil, etc., only) and article 29 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development).
