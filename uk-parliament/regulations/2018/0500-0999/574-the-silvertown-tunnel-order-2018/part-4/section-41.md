# The Silvertown Tunnel Order 2018, section 41

> Text as at 2019-02-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 41. Local legislation relating to the Blackwall Tunnel

*Extent: E+W+S*

The provisions of the Thames Tunnel (Blackwall) Act 1887 [^c22957571] and the London County Council (Tunnel and Improvements) Act 1938 [^c22957581], and any other local enactment relating to the Blackwall Tunnel area, have effect subject to the provisions of this Order.
