# The Silvertown Tunnel Order 2018, section 4

> Text as at 2019-02-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4. Development consent granted by the Order

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order, including the requirements in Schedule 2 (requirements), TfL is granted development consent for the authorised development.

(2) Any enactment applying to land within, adjoining or sharing a common boundary with the Order limits (other than land comprising part of the river Thames outside of the Order limits) has effect subject to the provisions of this Order.
