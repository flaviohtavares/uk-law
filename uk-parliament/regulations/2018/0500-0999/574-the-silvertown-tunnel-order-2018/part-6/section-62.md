# The Silvertown Tunnel Order 2018, section 62

> Text as at 2019-02-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 62. Deemed marine licence

*Extent: E+W+S*

The marine licence set out in Schedule 12 (deemed marine licence) is deemed to have been issued under Part 4 of the 2009 Act for the licensed activities set out in Part 1, and subject to the licence conditions set out in Part 2, of that licence.
