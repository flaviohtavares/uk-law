# The Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2018, section 57

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 57. Publication of tariffs

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Authority must—

- (a) by 15th March, 15th June, 15th September and 15th December in each year (“the tariff publication date”) publish in a table the initial tariffs applicable where a tariff start date falls within the tariff period immediately following the relevant tariff publication date; and
- (b) on or before 1st April each year, publish in a table on its website the subsequent tariffs applicable for the period commencing on 1st April of that year and ending with the 31st March of the following year.
