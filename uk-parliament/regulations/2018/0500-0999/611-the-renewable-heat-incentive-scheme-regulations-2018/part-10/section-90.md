# The Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2018, section 90

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 90. Additional information

*Extent: E+W+S*

On request from the Secretary of State, the Authority must provide to the Secretary of State in such manner and form and by such date as the Secretary of State may request such additional information as the Authority may hold in relation to the performance of its functions under these Regulations.
