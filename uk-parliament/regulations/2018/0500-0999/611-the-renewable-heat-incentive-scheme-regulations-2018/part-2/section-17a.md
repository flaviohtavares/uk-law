# The Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2018, section 17A

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 17A. Environmental permits and declaration of compliance[^key-d442c4b5b4e97c2d14b79e0584f4f1ee]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The requirements referred to in regulation 4(1)(b)(iii) are that, where an application for accreditation is made on or after 1st October 2018—

- (a) any necessary environmental permits are held in relation to the plant; and
- (b) a declaration is made that the plant complies, and will continue to comply, with all local and national laws including those relating to the protection of the environment.
