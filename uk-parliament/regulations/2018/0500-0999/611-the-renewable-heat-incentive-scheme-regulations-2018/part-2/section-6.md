# The Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2018, section 6

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. RHI emission certificates

*Extent: E+W+S*

An RHI emission certificate is a document that meets the following requirements—

- (a) the document must be issued by a testing laboratory;
- (b) where the information contained in the document is based on testing carried out on or after 24th September 2013, the testing laboratory that issued the document must be accredited to BS EN ISO/IEC 17025:2017 or a subsequent version of that standard[^key-7f8a183c0f8f962499a59395a25af835] at the time of testing; and
- (c) the document must contain the information set out in Schedule 1.
