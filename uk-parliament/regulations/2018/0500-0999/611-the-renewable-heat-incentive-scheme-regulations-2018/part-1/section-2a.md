# The Renewable Heat Incentive Scheme Regulations 2018, section 2A

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2A. Approval of subsequent versions of standards[^c-727e948be352f06527040022c798c72b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State may, in relation to a standard specified in a provision of these Regulations, approve for the purpose of that provision an issue or version of that standard which—

- (a) is published on or after 1st April 2022; and
- (b) supersedes the standard specified in that provision.
