> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6.1. Grounds for resignation[^c-3ad252d96f620f905b1b213ec66eca93]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The housing administrator may resign—

- (a) on grounds of ill health;
- (b) because of the intention to cease to practise as an insolvency practitioner; or
- (c) because the further discharge of the duties of housing administrator is prevented or made impractical by—
  - (i) a conflict of interest; or
  - (ii) a change of personal circumstances.

(2) The housing administrator may, with the permission of the court, resign on other grounds.
