> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6.8. Appointment of a replacement or additional housing administrator[^c-e7cd278d7c823f640ad9f97729a9d3aa]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a replacement housing administrator is appointed or an additional housing administrator is appointed to act jointly or concurrently—

- (a) rule 3.1 applies;
- (b) all documents must clearly identify the appointment as of a replacement housing administrator or an additional housing administrator appointed to act jointly or concurrently.
