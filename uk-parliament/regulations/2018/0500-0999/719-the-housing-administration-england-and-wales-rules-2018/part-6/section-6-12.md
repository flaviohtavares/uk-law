## 6.12. Remuneration of former housing administrator[^c-c32d1bcf9ed1572210c324e9562013fc]

*Extent: E+W*

If a housing administrator has ceased to act as such for any reason, the housing administrator (or the housing administrator’s estate, as the case may be) is to be entitled to any amounts outstanding on the basis of the services which that housing administrator provided in accordance with rule 6.10.
