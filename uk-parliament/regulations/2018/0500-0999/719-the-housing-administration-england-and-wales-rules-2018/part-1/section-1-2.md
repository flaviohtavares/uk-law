## 1.2. Application[^key-6e312c474f09b05aa98f6d5d27fbe763]

*Extent: E+W*

These Rules apply in relation to registered providers in respect of which the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction to make a housing administration order under Chapter 5 of Part 4 of the Housing and Planning Act 2016.
