## 5.42. Non payment of dividend[^c-b62ccd173871920e22ab0afedbb8c2f5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) No action lies against a housing administrator for payment of a dividend.

(2) However, if the housing administrator refuses to pay a dividend the court may, if it thinks just, order the housing administrator to pay it and also to pay, out of the housing administrator’s own money—

- (a) interest on the dividend, at the rate for the time being specified in section 17 of the Judgments Act 1838, from the time when it was withheld; and
- (b) the costs of the proceedings in which the order to pay is made.
