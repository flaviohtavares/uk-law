## 5.7. Admission and rejection of proofs for dividend[^c-6f15435f6055f0c5d5ceeba1cddad81d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The housing administrator may admit or reject a proof for dividend (in whole or in part).

(2) If the housing administrator rejects a proof in whole or in part, the housing administrator must deliver to the creditor a statement of the reasons for doing so, as soon as reasonably practicable.
