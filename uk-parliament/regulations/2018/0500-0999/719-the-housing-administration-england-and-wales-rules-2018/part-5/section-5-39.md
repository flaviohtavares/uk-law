# The Housing Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2018, section 5.39

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5.39. Disqualification from dividend[^c-b00576c8ea19b34933fbbe444d39e758]

*Extent: E+W*

If a creditor contravenes any provision of the Act or these Rules relating to the valuation of securities, the court may, on the application of the housing administrator, order that the creditor be wholly or partly disqualified from participation in any dividend.
