## 5.9. Housing administrator not liable for costs under rule 5.8[^key-bebaf085984d174a91f6fb0aaec3e9eb]

*Extent: E+W*

The housing administrator is not personally liable for costs incurred by any person in respect of an application under rule 5.8 unless the court otherwise orders.
