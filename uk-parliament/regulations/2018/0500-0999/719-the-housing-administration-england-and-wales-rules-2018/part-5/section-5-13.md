## 5.13. Division of unsold assets[^c-6aa5d1ed38f1b1286b50eefb66cc1814]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies to any property which, from its peculiar nature or other special circumstances, cannot be readily or advantageously sold.

(2) The housing administrator may with agreement of the creditors divide the property in its existing form among the registered provider’s creditors according to its estimated value.
