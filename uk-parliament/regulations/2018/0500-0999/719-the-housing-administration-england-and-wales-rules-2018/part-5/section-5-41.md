## 5.41. Debt payable at future time[^key-5ac93f80fe8cff97a5f76d7f744baaa8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a creditor has proved for a debt of which payment is not due at the date of the declaration of a dividend, the creditor is entitled to the dividend equally with other creditors, but subject as follows.

(2) For the purpose of the dividend (and no other purpose) the amount of the creditor’s admitted proof must be discounted by applying the following formula—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/719)

where—

- (a) “*X*” is the value of the admitted proof; and
- (b) “*n*” is the period beginning with the relevant date and ending with the date on which the payment of the creditor’s debt would otherwise be due, expressed in years (part of a year being expressed as a decimal fraction of a year).
