## 5.25. Application of Chapter to particular class of creditors and to distributions[^key-b76bccb821280a24dc5c4eec779f0379]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Chapter applies where the housing administrator makes, or proposes to make, a distribution to any class of creditors other than secured creditors.

(2) Where the distribution is to a particular class of creditors in a housing administration, a reference in this Chapter to creditors is a reference to that class of creditors only.
