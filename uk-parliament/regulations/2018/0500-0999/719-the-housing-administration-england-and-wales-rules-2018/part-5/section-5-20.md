## 5.20. Discounts[^c-9bd989e61c55aa73a73685433da15caf]

*Extent: E+W*

All trade and other discounts (except a discount for immediate or early settlement) which would have been available to the registered provider or the debtor but for the housing administration proceedings must be deducted from the claim.
