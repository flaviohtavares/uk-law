# The Housing Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2018, section 5.36

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5.36. Provisions as to dividends[^c-94d0e54266a51c2f7a48a3c88f8b31bc]

*Extent: E+W*

In the calculation and distribution of a dividend, the housing administrator must make provision for—

- (a) any debts which are the subject of claims which have not yet been determined; and
- (b) disputed proofs and claims.
