# The Housing Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2018, section 7.35

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7.35. Offence in relation to inspection of documents[^c-e75ef806e42166ab6a161051299c8a72]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It is an offence for a person who does not have a right under these Rules to inspect a relevant document falsely to claim to be a creditor, a member of a company or other registered provider or a contributory of a company with the intention of gaining sight of the document.

(2) A relevant document is one which is on the court file or held by the housing administrator or any other person and which a member of a company or other registered provider or a contributory of a company has the right to inspect under these Rules.

(3) A person guilty of an offence under this rule is liable to imprisonment or a fine, or both as set out in Schedule 1.
