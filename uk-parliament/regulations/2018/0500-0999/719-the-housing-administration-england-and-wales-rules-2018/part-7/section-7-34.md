## 7.34. Charges for copies of documents provided by the housing administrator[^c-592669616aaa42c1a8add80e1849ba0e]

*Extent: E+W*

Except where prohibited by these Rules, a housing administrator is entitled to require the payment of the standard fee for copies of documents requested by a creditor, member or contributory.
