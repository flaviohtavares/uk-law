## 7.30. Electronic delivery by housing administrators[^key-f61fbffb3d952c091a22389efa8e5d69]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a housing administrator delivers a document by electronic means, the document must contain—

- (a) or be accompanied by, a statement that the recipient may request a hard copy of the document; and
- (b) a telephone number, email address and postal address which may be used to make that request.

(2) A housing administrator must deliver a hard copy of the document to the recipient within five business days of receipt of a request.

(3) A housing administrator must not require the person requesting a hard copy to pay a fee for supplying it.
