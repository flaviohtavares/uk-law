## 7.23. Delivery of documents to joint housing administrators[^key-7ce692fa15cbfa9fd1a9385a489f0372]

*Extent: E+W*

Where joint housing administrators have been appointed, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to all of them.
