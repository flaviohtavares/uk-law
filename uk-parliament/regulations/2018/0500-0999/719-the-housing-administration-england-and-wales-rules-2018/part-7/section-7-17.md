## 7.17. Standard contents of notices relating to court proceedings or orders[^c-3a1e4b3393ddf57d5dd68f24ecd2bd4c]

*Extent: E+W*

A notice relating to court proceedings must also identify those proceedings and if the notice relates to a court order state—

- (a) the nature of the order; and
- (b) the date of the order.
