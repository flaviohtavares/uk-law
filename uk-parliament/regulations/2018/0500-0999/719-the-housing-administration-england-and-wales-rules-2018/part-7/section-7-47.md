## 7.47. Non-Gazette notices – other provisions[^c-250c20597fbf5290e673ac4c2924867b]

*Extent: E+W*

Information which this Chapter requires to be stated in a notice must be included in an advertisement of that notice in a way that is clear and comprehensible.
