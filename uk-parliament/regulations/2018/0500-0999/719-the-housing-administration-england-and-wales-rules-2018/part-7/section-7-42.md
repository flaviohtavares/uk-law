## 7.42. Contents of notices to be gazetted under the Act or Rules[^c-901934387c1b9d96c5fe517d7476c6d1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the Act or these Rules require or permit a notice to be gazetted the notice must also contain the standard contents set out in this Chapter in addition to any content specifically required by the Act or any other provision of these Rules.

(2) Information which this Chapter requires to be included in a Gazette notice may be omitted if it is not reasonably practicable to obtain it.
