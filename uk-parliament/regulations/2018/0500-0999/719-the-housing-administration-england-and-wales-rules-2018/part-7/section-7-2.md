## 7.2. Notices or statements in writing[^c-cd2354de4a448f01e38cd5d5acf22b0d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A notice or statement must be in writing unless the Act or these Rules provide otherwise.

(2) A document in electronic form must be capable of being—

- (a) read by the recipient in electronic form; and
- (b) reproduced by the recipient in hard-copy form.
