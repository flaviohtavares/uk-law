# The Housing Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2018, section 7.36

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7.36. Right to list of creditors[^c-8d811ffd53eba937c5f18896d696f6fc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A creditor has the right to require the housing administrator to provide a list of the names and addresses of the creditors and the amounts of their respective debts.

(2) The housing administrator on being required to provide such a list—

- (a) must deliver it to the person requiring the list as soon as reasonably practicable; and
- (b) may charge the standard fee for copies for a hard copy.

(3) The housing administrator may omit the name and address of a creditor if the housing administrator thinks its disclosure would be prejudicial to the conduct of the proceedings or might reasonably be expected to lead to violence against any person.

(4) In such a case the list must include—

- (a) the amount of that creditor’s debt; and
- (b) a statement that the name and the address of the creditor has been omitted for that debt.
