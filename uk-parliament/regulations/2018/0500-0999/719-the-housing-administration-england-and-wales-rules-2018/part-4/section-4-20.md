## 4.20. Further information and disclosure[^key-78e546b242ced09d3b4d41d27ec6d8dc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A party to housing administration proceedings may apply to the court for an order—

- (a) that in accordance with CPR Part 18 (further information) another party—
  - (i) clarify a matter that is in dispute in the proceedings, or
  - (ii) give additional information in relation to such a matter; or
- (b) for disclosure from any person in accordance with CPR Part 31 (disclosure and inspection of documents), save where Chapter 7 of Part 3 applies.

(2) An application under this rule may be made without notice to any other party.
