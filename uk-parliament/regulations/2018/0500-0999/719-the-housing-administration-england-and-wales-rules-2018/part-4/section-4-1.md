# The Housing Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2018, section 4.1

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4.1. Court rules and practice to apply[^c-b055d3aad6df80252a3f9f4164ed286c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The provisions of the CPR (including any related Practice Directions) apply for the purposes of housing administration with any necessary modifications, except so far as disapplied by or inconsistent with these Rules.

(2) Housing administration proceedings must be allocated to the multi-track, for which CPR Part 29 makes provision, and accordingly those provisions of the CPR which provide for directions questionnaires and track allocation do not apply.

(3) CPR Part 32 applies to a false statement in a document verified by a statement of truth made under these Rules as it applies to a false statement in a document verified by a statement of truth made under CPR Part 22.
