## 4.42. Procedure on appeal[^c-0cb46cee610eac7e66f3034c57a76c3b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An appeal against a decision at first instance may be brought only with the permission of the court which made the decision or of the court which has jurisdiction to hear the appeal.

(2) An appellant must file an appellant’s notice within 21 days after the date of the decision of the court that the appellant wishes to appeal.
