## 4.6. Fixing the venue[^c-6737c885101272ee291d9fce9edc866e]

*Extent: E+W*

When an application is filed the court must fix a venue for it to be heard unless—

- (a) it considers that it is not appropriate to do so;
- (b) the rule under which the application is brought provides otherwise; or
- (c) the case is one to which rule 4.10 applies.
