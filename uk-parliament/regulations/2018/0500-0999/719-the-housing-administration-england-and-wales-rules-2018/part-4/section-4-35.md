## 4.35. Costs and expenses of witnesses[^c-c8ffe209868a3da7ff8f3e2d53cc6d18]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Except as directed by the court no allowance as a witness in any examination or other proceedings before the court may be made to a relevant officer of the registered provider to which the proceedings relate.

(2) A person making any application in housing administration proceedings is not to be regarded as a witness on the hearing of the application, but a costs officer may allow their expenses of travelling and subsistence.
