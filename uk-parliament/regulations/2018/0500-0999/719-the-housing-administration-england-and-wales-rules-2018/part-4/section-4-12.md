## 4.12. Application under section 176A(5) to disapply section 176A[^c-4c3026104572e4ce8864b3264f6f7225]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application under section 176A(5) of the Act must be accompanied by a witness statement of the housing administrator.

(2) The witness statement must state—

- (a) that the application arises in housing administration proceedings;
- (b) a summary of the financial position of the registered provider; and
- (c) the information substantiating the housing administrator’s view that the cost of making a distribution to unsecured creditors would be disproportionate to the benefits.
