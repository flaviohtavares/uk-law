## 3.5. Filing of statement of affairs[^c-766f76cace7b2c5025b6c79ed41bef22]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The housing administrator must as soon as is reasonably practicable deliver to the relevant registry a copy of—

- (a) the statement of affairs; and
- (b) any statement of concurrence.

(2) However, the housing administrator must not deliver to the relevant registry with the statement of affairs any schedule required by rule 3.3(5)(b).

(3) The requirement to deliver the statement of affairs is subject to any order of the court made under rule 3.31(2).
