# The Housing Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2018, section 3.15

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3.15. Final progress report[^c-a3f597f7807e114b27d5e54a3b532a1d]

*Extent: E+W*

“Final progress report” means a progress report which must contain an account of the housing administrator’s administration including—

- (a) a summary of the housing administrator’s proposals;
- (b) any major amendments to, or deviations from, those proposals;
- (c) the steps taken during the administration;
- (d) a statement as to the amount paid to unsecured creditors by virtue of section 176A of the Act; and
- (e) the outcome.
