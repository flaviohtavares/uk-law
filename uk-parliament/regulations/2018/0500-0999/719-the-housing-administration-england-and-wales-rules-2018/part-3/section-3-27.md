## 3.27. Creditors’ decisions[^c-6bd28fecd2f004ca1afef2ba0ca71d42]

*Extent: E+W*

The housing administrator may, and in the circumstances set out in rule 3.28 must, seek a decision from the registered provider’s creditors under paragraph 62 of Schedule B1.
