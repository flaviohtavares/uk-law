## 3.17. Application of Chapter 5 to meetings of the registered provider[^c-0b4dbccc75a57e7069f61da649c01f5f]

*Extent: E+W*

This Chapter applies where the housing administrator calls a meeting of members under paragraph 62 of Schedule B1[^f00017].
