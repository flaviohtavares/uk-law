## 3.36. Expenses[^key-0eca8c023d15a6e82f64321594edca01]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) All fees, costs, charges and other expenses incurred in the course of the housing administration are to be treated as expenses of the housing administration.

(2) The expenses associated with the prescribed part must be paid out of the prescribed part.

(3) The cost of the security required by section 390(3) of the Act for the proper performance of a housing administrator’s functions is an expense of the housing administration.

(4) For the purposes of paragraph 99(3) of Schedule B1, the former housing administrator’s remuneration and expenses comprise all the items in rule 3.37.
