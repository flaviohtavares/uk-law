## 3.26. Application of Chapter 6 to creditors’ decisions[^c-8f19e720b04a084ed6d14d1970efc46e]

*Extent: E+W*

This Chapter applies where the housing administrator seeks a decision from creditors in accordance with paragraph 62 of Schedule B1.
