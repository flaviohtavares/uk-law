# The Housing Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2018, section 3.7

> Text as at 2026-10-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3.7. Expenses of statement of affairs[^c-d94cfc35a71f8ab9f263d2bd300af185]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The expenses of a relevant person which the housing administrator considers to have been reasonably incurred in the making of a statement of affairs or a statement of concurrence must be paid by the housing administrator as an expense of the housing administration.

(2) A decision by the housing administrator that expenses were not reasonably incurred (and are therefore not payable as an expense of the housing administration) may be appealed to the court.
