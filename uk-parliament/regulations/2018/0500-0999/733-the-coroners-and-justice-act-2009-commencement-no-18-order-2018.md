# The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 18) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 733 (C. 58)

Criminal Law, England And Wales

Criminal Law, Scotland

The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 18) Order 2018

Made

14th June 2018

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 182(5) of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 18) Order 2018.

## 2. Commencement of provisions of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009

The following provisions of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 come into force on 16th July 2018 —

- (a) section 177(1) so far as it relates to the provisions specified in sub-paragraph (c);
- (b) paragraphs 2(3) and 3 of Schedule 16 (extension of driving disqualification); and
- (c) paragraphs 90(1) and (2) and (6) to (9) and 91 of Schedule 21 (minor and consequential amendments).

Rory Stewart

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

14th June 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order commences various provisions of Schedules 16 and 21 to the Coroners and Justice Act [2009 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25). The provisions in Schedule 16 provide for a person to be disqualified for the appropriate extension period from holding or obtaining a driving licence where a court in Scotland imposes a sentence of imprisonment and orders a person to be disqualified. In that case the court must add an extension period to the disqualification period to take into account the time spent in custody. The provisions in Schedule 21 make various minor and consequential amendments to the Road Traffic Offenders Act [1988 (c. 53)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/53) which set out the circumstances when the extension period generated by provisions in Schedule 16 are to be disregarded.
