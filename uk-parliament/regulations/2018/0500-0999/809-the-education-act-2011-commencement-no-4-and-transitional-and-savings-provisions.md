# The Education Act 2011 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional and Savings Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 809

Education, England And Wales

The Education Act 2011 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional and Savings Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2018

Made

2nd July 2018

Coming into force

9th July 2018

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 82(3) and (7) of the Education Act 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Education Act 2011 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional and Savings Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2018.

(2) This Order comes into force on 9th July 2018.

## 2. Revocation

Article 5 of the Education Act 2011 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional and Savings Provisions) Order 2012[^f00002] is revoked.

Agnew

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Education

2nd July 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes article 5 of the Education Act 2011 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional and Savings Provisions) Order 2012 ([S.I. 2012/1087](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1087)).

Article 5 related to the commencement of an amendment to section 19 of the Education Act [2002 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32). It paused the effect of the amendment until the governing body or local authority of a maintained school decided to vary the instrument of government. Further to regulations 2(1) and (3) of the School Governance (Constitution and Federations) (England) (Amendment) Regulations ([S.I. 2014/1257](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/1257)), no such transitional arrangement was to persist beyond 1 September 2015. The provision is therefore revoked.
