# The Freedom of Information (Additional Public Authorities) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 173

Freedom Of Information

The Freedom of Information (Additional Public Authorities) Order 2018

Made

8th February 2018

Laid before Parliament

14th February 2018

Coming into force

1st May 2018

The Minister for the Cabinet Office makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4(1) and (6) of the Freedom of Information Act 2000[^f00001] and now concurrently vested in the Minster for the Cabinet Office.

The Minister for the Cabinet Office has consulted the Welsh Ministers in accordance with section 4(7) of that Act[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Freedom of Information (Additional Public Authorities) Order 2018.

(2) This Order comes into force on 1st May 2018

(3) In this Order, “the Act” means the Freedom of Information Act 2000.

## 2. Additional public authorities

In Part 6 of Schedule 1 to the Act (other public bodies and offices: general) insert, at the appropriate place in each case, the entries listed in the Schedule to this Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The obligations under the Freedom of Information Act [2000 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/36) (“the Act”) apply to public authorities. Section 3(1) provides that a “public authority” includes a body or office which is listed in Schedule 1 to the Act. The Minister for the Cabinet Office may add bodies and office holders to Schedule 1 by an order under section 4(1), provided that the conditions in section 4(2) and (3) are satisfied. By section 4(6), an order under section 4(1) may relate to a specified person or office or to persons or offices falling within a specified description.

Article 2(1) of this Order adds the bodies and office holders listed in the Schedule to this Order to Part 6 of Schedule 1 to the Act.

An Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
