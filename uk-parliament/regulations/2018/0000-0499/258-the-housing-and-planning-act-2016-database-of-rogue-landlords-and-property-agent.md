# The Housing and Planning Act 2016 (Database of Rogue Landlords and Property Agents) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Laid before Parliament

1st March 2018

Coming into force

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 33 of the Housing and Planning Act 2016[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Housing and Planning Act 2016 (Database of Rogue Landlords and Property Agents) Regulations 2018.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 6th April 2018.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “the Act” means the Housing and Planning Act 2016;
- “database” means the database established under section 28 of the Act; and
- “relevant housing” means housing[^f00002] owned, let or managed by a person for the purposes of–
  - (a) letting housing in England;
  - (b) engaging in English letting agency work[^f00003], or
  - (c) engaging in English property management work[^f00004].

## 3. Information to be included in the database

(1) A person’s entry in the database must include the information described in Part 1 of the table.

(2) If the entry is made under section 29 of the Act (banning orders made), the database must include—

- (a) the information mentioned in paragraph (1), and
- (b) the information described in Part 2 of the table.

(3) If the entry is made under section 30(1) of the Act (convictions for banning order offence), the database must include—

- (a) the information mentioned in paragraph (1), and
- (b) the information described in Part 3 of the table.

(4) If the entry is made under section 30(2) of the Act (financial penalties in respect of banning order offences), the database must include—

- (a) the information mentioned in paragraph (1), and
- (b) the information described in Part 4 of the table.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Chapter 3 of Part 2 of the Housing and Planning Act [2016 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/22) (“the Act”), which applies to England only makes provision for the Secretary of State to establish and operate a database of rogue landlords and property agents. Section 33 of the Act confers power on the Secretary of State to make provision about the information that must be included in a person’s entry in the database.

Regulation 3 describes the information that must be included in an entry in the database.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
