Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 1 of the United Nations Act 1946[^f00001] and section 77(4) of the International Criminal Court Act 2001[^f00002], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order enables the United Kingdom to cooperate with the International Residual Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals in the investigation and prosecution of persons accused of Mechanism crimes and the punishment of persons convicted of such crimes. The Mechanism was established by United Nations Security Council resolution 1966 (2010) to complete the tasks of the international criminal tribunals for the former Yugoslavia and Rwanda.
