# The United Nations (International Residual Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals) Order 2018, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Proceedings to determine the ownership of property

Where the Secretary of State receives a request from the Mechanism to determine the ownership of property or the proceeds of property, the Secretary of State, or in relation to Scotland the Scottish Ministers, may make an application to the court for a decision as to the ownership of the property or proceeds.
