# The Education (Student Support) (Revocation, Amendment and Saving Provision) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

3.00 p.m. on 27th March 2018

Laid before Parliament

5.00 p.m. on 27th March 2018

Coming into force

28th March 2018

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 22 and 42(6) of the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Support) (Revocation, Amendment and Saving Provision) Regulations 2018.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 28th March 2018.

(3) In these Regulations—

- (a) “dental profession subject” means dental hygiene, dental therapy and dental hygiene and dental therapy;
- (b) “postgraduate pre-registration course” means an education and training programme leading to a graduate master’s degree or a postgraduate diploma which is a condition of inclusion in the register (or, as the case may be, the relevant part or parts of the register) maintained by—
  - (i) the Health and Care Professions Council[^f00002], for allied health profession subjects other than dental profession subjects; or
  - (ii) the Nursing and Midwifery Council[^f00003], for midwifery or nursing.

## 2. Revocation of the Education (Student Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

Subject to regulation 3, the Education (Student Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2018[^f00004] are revoked.

## 3. Saving provision

Regulations 2, and 5 to 16, are of no effect in relation to a person who, before the date on which these Regulations come into force, has submitted an application under the Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011[^f00005] to the Secretary of State for support in relation to a postgraduate pre-registration course or a dental profession course and that application has been received.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Education (Student Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2018 ([S.I. 2018/136](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/136)) and amend the Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/1986](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986)), in order to return the latter regulations to the state they were in before the former regulations were made. In doing so, regulation 3 of these Regulations includes a saving provision for persons who have already made an application for support in respect of a dental profession subject or postgraduate pre-registration course, and whose applications have been received by the Secretary of State by the time these Regulations come into force. The Department will lay new regulations to replace the revoked the Education (Student Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2018.
