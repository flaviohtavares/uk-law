# The Immigration Act 2016 (Commencement No. 7 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [SI 2017/1241](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1241) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2018 No. 31

Immigration

The Immigration Act 2016 (Commencement No. 7 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

Made

11th January 2018

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 92(1) of, and paragraph 13(1) of Schedule 10 to, the Immigration Act 2016[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration Act 2016 (Commencement No. 7 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2018.

## 2. Amendment to the Immigration Act 2016 (Commencement No. 7 and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2017

In the Schedule to the Immigration Act 2016 (Commencement No. 7 and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2017[^f00002], for the words “20 November 2017” on each occasion that they appear substitute “15th January 2018”.

Caroline Nokes

Minister of State

Home Office

11th January 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Schedule to the Immigration Act 2016 (Commencement No. 7 and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2017 to correct an erroneous date.
