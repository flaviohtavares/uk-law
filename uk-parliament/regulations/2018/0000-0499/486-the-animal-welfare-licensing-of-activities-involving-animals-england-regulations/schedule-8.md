# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (England) Regulations 2018, SCHEDULE 8

> Text as at 2021-09-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 8 — Persons who may not apply for a licence

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 11

**1.** A person who has at any time held a licence which was revoked under regulation 15 of these Regulations.

**2.** A person who has at any time held a licence which was revoked under regulation 17 of the Animal Welfare (Breeding of Dogs) (Wales) Regulations 2014 [^c22941501].

**3.** A person who has at any time held a licence which was revoked under regulation 13 of the Welfare of Wild Animals in Travelling Circuses (England) Regulations 2012 [^c22941511].

**4.** A person who is disqualified under section 33 of the Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 [^c22941521].

**5.** A person who has at any time held a licence which was revoked under regulation 12 of the Welfare of Racing Greyhounds Regulations 2010 [^c22941531].

**6.** A person who is disqualified under section 34 of the Act.

**7.** A person who is disqualified under section 40(1) and (2) of the Animal Health and Welfare (Scotland) Act 2006 [^c22941541].

**8.** A person who is disqualified under section 4(1) of the Dangerous Dogs Act 1991 [^c22941551].

**9.** A person who is disqualified under Article 33A of the Dogs (Northern Ireland) Order 1983 [^c22941561].

**10.** A person who is disqualified under section 6(2) of the Dangerous Wild Animals Act 1976 [^c22941571] from keeping a dangerous wild animal.

**11.** A person who is disqualified under section 3(3) of the Breeding of Dogs Act 1973 [^c22941581] from keeping a breeding establishment for dogs.

**12.** A person who is disqualified under section 4(3) of the Riding Establishments Act 1964 [^c22941591] from keeping a riding establishment.

**13.** A person who is disqualified under section 3(3) of the Animal Boarding Establishments Act 1963 [^c22941601] from keeping a boarding establishment for animals.

**14.** A person who is disqualified under section 5(3) of the Pet Animals Act 1951 [^c22941611] from keeping a pet shop.

**15.** A person who is disqualified under section 1(1) of the Protection of Animals (Amendment) Act 1954 [^c22941621] from having custody of an animal.

**16.** A person who is disqualified under section 4(2) of the Performing Animals (Regulation) Act 1925 [^c22941631].

**17.** A person who is disqualified under section 3 of the Protection of Animals Act 1911 [^c22941641] from the ownership of an animal.
