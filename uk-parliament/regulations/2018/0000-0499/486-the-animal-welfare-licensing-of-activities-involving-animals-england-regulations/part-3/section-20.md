# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (England) Regulations 2018, section 20

> Text as at 2021-09-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Offences

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It is an offence for a person, without lawful authority or excuse—

- (a) to breach a licence condition;
- (b) to fail to comply with regulation 7 or 19.

(2) A person who commits an offence under paragraph (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine.
