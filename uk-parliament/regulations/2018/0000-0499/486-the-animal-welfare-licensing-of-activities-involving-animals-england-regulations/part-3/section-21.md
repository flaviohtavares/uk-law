# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (England) Regulations 2018, section 21

> Text as at 2021-09-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. Powers of entry

*Extent: E+W*

Breach of a licence condition must be treated as a relevant offence for the purposes of section 23 of the Act (entry and search under warrant in connection with offences).
