# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (England) Regulations 2018, section 19

> Text as at 2021-09-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Obstruction of inspectors

*Extent: E+W*

A person must not intentionally obstruct an inspector appointed for the purposes of the enforcement of these Regulations in the exercise of any powers conferred by or under the Act.
