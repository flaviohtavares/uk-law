# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (England) Regulations 2018, SCHEDULE 10

> Text as at 2021-09-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 10 — Revocations and consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 26

### 1. Performing Animals Rules 1925

In rule 2 of the Performing Animals Rules 1925 [^c22941801], for the first indented paragraph substitute “ In Wales:– The City of Cardiff. ”

### 2. Sale of Dogs (Identification Tag) Regulations 1999

The Sale of Dogs (Identification Tag) Regulations 1999 [^c22941811] are revoked.

### 3. Breeding of Dogs (Licensing Records) Regulations 1999

The Breeding of Dogs (Licensing Records) Regulations 1999 [^c22941821] are revoked.
