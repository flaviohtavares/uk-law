# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (England) Regulations 2018, section 9

> Text as at 2021-09-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Variation of a licence on the application, or with the consent, of a licence holder

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority may at any time vary a licence—

- (a) on the application in writing of the licence holder, or
- (b) on its own initiative, with the consent in writing of the licence holder.
