# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (England) Regulations 2018, section 6

> Text as at 2021-09-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Power to take samples from animals

*Extent: E+W*

An inspector may, for the purposes of ensuring the licence conditions are being complied with, take samples for laboratory testing from any animals on premises occupied by an operator.
