# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (England) Regulations 2018, section 7

> Text as at 2021-09-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Duty to assist in the taking of samples from animals

*Extent: E+W*

An operator must comply with any reasonable request of an inspector to facilitate the identification and examination of an animal and the taking of samples in accordance with regulation 6 and, in particular, must arrange the suitable restraint of an animal if so requested by an inspector.
