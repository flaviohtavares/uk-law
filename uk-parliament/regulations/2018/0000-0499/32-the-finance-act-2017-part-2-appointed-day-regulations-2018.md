# The Finance Act 2017, Part 2 (Appointed Day) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 32 (C. 3)

Soft Drinks Industry Levy

The Finance Act 2017, Part 2 (Appointed Day) Regulations 2018

Made

12th January 2018

The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 61(1) and (3) of the Finance Act 2017[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Finance Act 2017, Part 2 (Appointed Day) Regulations 2018.

## 2. Appointed day

Part 2 of the Finance Act 2017 comes into force on 13th January 2018, but only for the purpose of making any regulations under that Part.

Justin Holliday

Melissa Tatton

Two of the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs

12th January 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Regulations provide that Part 2 of the Finance Act [2017 (c. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/10), which introduces soft drinks industry levy, is commenced on 13th January 2018, but only for the purpose of making regulations under that Part.

A Tax Information and Impact Note has not been prepared for the Regulations as they give effect to previously announced policy and are appointed day regulations.
