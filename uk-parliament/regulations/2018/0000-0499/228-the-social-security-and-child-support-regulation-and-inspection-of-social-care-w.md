The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 150(1)(a) of the Government of Wales Act 2006 [^f00001].

In accordance with section 176(1)(a) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00002], the Secretary of State has consulted, in relation to regulations 9 and 10, with organisations appearing to the Secretary of State to be representative of the authorities concerned.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-445cadc83813918f2eb95a6513de846c]

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security and Child Support (Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016) (Consequential Provision) Regulations 2018 and come into force on 2nd April 2018.

## 2. Amendment of the Income Support (General) Regulations 1987[^c-c5505a7179f9fd0218e04a65c907acd1]

(1) The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987[^f00003] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation), in paragraph (1), in the definition of “care home”—

- (a) omit “and Wales”;
- (b) after “Care Standards Act 2000,” insert “in Wales means a care home service within the meaning of Part 1 of the Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016[^f00004] which is provided wholly or mainly to persons aged 18 or over”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend social security and child support regulations.

In particular, they make changes which are consequential on the coming into force for certain purposes on 2nd April 2018 of Part 1 of the Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act [2016 (anaw 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/2). That Act creates a new system for the regulation of care homes in Wales.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on business or civil society organisations.
