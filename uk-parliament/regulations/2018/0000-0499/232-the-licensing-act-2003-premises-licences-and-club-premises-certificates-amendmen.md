# The Licensing Act 2003 (Premises licences and club premises certificates) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct errors in [SI 2005/42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/42) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2018 No. 232

Licences And Licensing, England And Wales

The Licensing Act 2003 (Premises licences and club premises certificates) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

Made

22nd February 2018

Laid before Parliament

27th February 2018

Coming into force

23rd March 2018

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 54 of the Licensing Act 2003[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Licensing Act 2003 (Premises licences and club premises certificates) (Amendment) Regulations 2018 and come into force on 23rd March 2018.

## 2. Amendment to the Licensing Act 2003 (Premises licences and club premises certificates) Regulations 2005

(1) The Licensing Act 2003 (Premises licences and club premises certificates) Regulations 2005[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) For the form in Schedule 5[^f00003] (Application to vary a premises licence to specify an individual as designated premises supervisor under the Licensing Act 2003) substitute the form in the Schedule to these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Licensing Act 2003 (Premises licences and club premises certificates) Regulations 2005 ([SI 2005/42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/42)) so as to substitute a new application form to be used to vary a premises licence to specify an individual as a designated premises supervisor.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
