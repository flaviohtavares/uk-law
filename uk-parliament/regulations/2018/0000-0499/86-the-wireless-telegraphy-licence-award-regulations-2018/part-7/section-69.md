# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 69

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Assignment of numbered 3.4 GHz lots by bidding

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except where regulation 67 applies, the particular numbered 3.4 GHz lots that are to be assigned to the winning bidder or bidders for the 3.4 GHz lots shall be determined by bidding in the assignment stage round in accordance with the procedure set out in regulations 70, 71 and 77 to 84.
