# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 90

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Notification of results of the assignment stage

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After the determination of the winning assignment stage bids, and the determination of the additional price for each winning assignment stage bid, OFCOM shall notify each winning bidder of—

- (a) the 2.3 GHz and 3.4 GHz assignment stage options for which the bidder made winning assignment stage bids; and
- (b) the additional price determined for each winning assignment stage bid made by the bidder.
