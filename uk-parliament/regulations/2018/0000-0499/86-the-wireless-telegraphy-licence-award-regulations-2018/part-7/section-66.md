# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 66

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Assignment of numbered 2.3 GHz lots without bidding

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where there is only one winning bidder for all of the 2.3 GHz lots, OFCOM shall assign all of the 2.3 GHz lots to that winning bidder.
