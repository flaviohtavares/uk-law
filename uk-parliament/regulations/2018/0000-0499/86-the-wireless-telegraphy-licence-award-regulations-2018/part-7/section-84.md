# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 84

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Determination of 3.4 GHz additional prices

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In respect of each winning 3.4 GHz assignment stage bid, OFCOM shall determine an amount in whole pounds (“3.4 GHz additional price”) which shall be payable by the relevant winning bidder in accordance with Schedule 6.
