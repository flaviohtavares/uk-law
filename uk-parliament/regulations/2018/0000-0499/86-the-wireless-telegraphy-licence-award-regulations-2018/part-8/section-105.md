# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 105

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 105. Notification of the total auction sum payable by winning bidders to OFCOM

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Following the determination of the total auction sum payable by each winning bidder to OFCOM, OFCOM shall notify each winning bidder of the total auction sum payable by that winning bidder to OFCOM.
