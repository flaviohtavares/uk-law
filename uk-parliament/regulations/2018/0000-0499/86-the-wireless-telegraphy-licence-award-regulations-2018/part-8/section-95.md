# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 95

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 2.3 GHz withdrawn lot licence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 2.3 GHz withdrawn lot licence shall be an amount equal to the sum of—

- (a) that bidder's total 2.3 GHz base price B; and
- (b) except where that bidder also won 2.3 GHz lots in accordance with regulation 38, that bidder's 2.3 GHz additional price.
