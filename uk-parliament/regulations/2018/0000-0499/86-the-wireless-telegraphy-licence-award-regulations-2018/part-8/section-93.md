# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 93

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Licences which may be granted under this award process

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The licences which may be granted to a bidder under this award process are—

- (a) for a bidder which won 2.3 GHz lots in accordance with regulation 38, a 2.3 GHz licence;
- (b) for a bidder which won 2.3 GHz lots in accordance with regulation 39, a 2.3 GHz withdrawn lot licence;
- (c) for a bidder which won 3.4 GHz lots in accordance with regulation 38, a 3.4 GHz licence;
- (d) for a bidder which won 3.4 GHz lots in accordance with regulation 40, a 3.4 GHz withdrawn lot licence; and
- (e) where the pre-existing licence holder is a bidder and did apply for a replacement licence, a replacement licence.
