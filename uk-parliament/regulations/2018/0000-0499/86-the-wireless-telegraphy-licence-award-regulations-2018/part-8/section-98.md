# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 98

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 98. Licence fee payable by the pre-existing licence holder for a replacement licence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The licence fee payable by the pre-existing licence holder for a replacement licence shall be—

- (a) where the pre-existing licence holder does not win any additional 3.4 GHz lots in the principal stage, the amount which is the pre-existing licence holder's 3.4 GHz additional price; or
- (b) where the pre-existing licence holder won additional 3.4 GHz lots in the principal stage, zero pounds.
