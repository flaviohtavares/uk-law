# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 110

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Notification to bidders following the grant of licences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Following the grant of licences and the refund (if any) of deposits, OFCOM shall notify each winning bidder of the names of all of the persons to whom licences were granted, and, in relation to each of them—

- (a) the frequencies in respect of which the licences were granted; and
- (b) the licence fees paid, indicating the total base prices and the additional prices.
