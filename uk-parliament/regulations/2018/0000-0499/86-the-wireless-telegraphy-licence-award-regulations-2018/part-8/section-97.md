# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 97

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 3.4 GHz withdrawn lot licence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The licence fee payable by a winning bidder for a 3.4 GHz withdrawn lot licence shall be an amount equal to the sum of—

- (a) that bidder's total 3.4 GHz base price B; and
- (b) except where that bidder also won 3.4 GHz lots in accordance with regulation 38, that bidder's 3.4 GHz additional price.
