# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 29

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Round prices for the first principal stage round

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the first principal stage round—

- (a) the round price for each 2.3 GHz lot shall be ten million pounds; and
- (b) the round price for each 3.4 GHz lot shall be one million pounds.
