# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 46

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. 3.4 GHz minimum requirement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a bidder has specified a 3.4 GHz minimum requirement, the number of principal stage bids for 3.4 GHz lots that bidder specifies on a principal stage form (if any) must be at least its 3.4 GHz minimum requirement.
