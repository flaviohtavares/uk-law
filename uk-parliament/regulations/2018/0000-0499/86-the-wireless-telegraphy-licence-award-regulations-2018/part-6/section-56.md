# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 56

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Publication of information at the end of each day during the principal stage

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After the end of the latest principal stage round to take place on any given day, OFCOM shall publish on their website, in relation to that latest principal stage round—

- (a) the round price for—
  - (i) each 2.3 GHz lot; and
  - (ii) each 3.4 GHz lot;
- (b) for each lot type separately, the amount in MHz which is the smallest strictly positive whole multiple of twenty which is greater than the excess demand.
