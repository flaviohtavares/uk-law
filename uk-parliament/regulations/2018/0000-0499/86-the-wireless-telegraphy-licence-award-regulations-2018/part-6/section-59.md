# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 59

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Submission of principal stage form to OFCOM

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A bidder must submit the completed principal stage form to OFCOM by using the electronic auction system, except as provided for in regulation 60.

(2) The completed principal stage form must be received by OFCOM by the time notified by OFCOM under regulation 54(a) as the end of the principal stage round.
