# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 1

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement, and extent

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018 and shall come into force on 31st<sup></sup>January 2018.

(2) These Regulations shall not extend to the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man.
