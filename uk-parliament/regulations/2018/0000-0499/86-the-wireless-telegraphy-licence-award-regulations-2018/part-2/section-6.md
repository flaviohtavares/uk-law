# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2018, section 6

> Text as at 2018-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. 3.4 GHz minimum requirement rule

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The 3.4 GHz minimum requirement which must be specified by the applicant in the document referred to in regulation 4(3)(a)(i) shall not be greater than four.
