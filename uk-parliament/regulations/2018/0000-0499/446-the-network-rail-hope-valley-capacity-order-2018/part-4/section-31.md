# The Network Rail (Hope Valley Capacity) Order 2018, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Planning permission

Planning permission which is deemed by a direction under section 90(2A)[^f00060] (development with government authorisation) of the 1990 Act to be granted in relation to the authorised works is to be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)(a) (cases in which land is to be treated as operational land of the purposes of that Act) of that Act.

## Annotations

[^f00060]: Footnote: Section 90(2A) was inserted by section 16(1) of the Transport and Works Act (c. 42).
