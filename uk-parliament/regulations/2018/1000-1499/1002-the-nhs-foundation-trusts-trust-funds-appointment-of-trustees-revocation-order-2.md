# The NHS Foundation Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Revocation Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 1002

National Health Service, England

The NHS Foundation Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Revocation Order 2018

Made

13th September 2018

Coming into force

1st October 2018

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 51(1) and (2), 64(5) and (6) and 273(1) of, and paragraph 10 of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the NHS Foundation Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Revocation Order 2018 and comes into force on 1st October 2018.

## 2. Revocations

The instruments specified in the Schedule (which relate to the appointment of trustees for NHS foundation trusts by the Secretary of State) are revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care.

O’Shaughnessy

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Health and Social Care

13th September 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes nine statutory instruments relating to the appointment of trustees for NHS foundation trusts by the Secretary of State.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
