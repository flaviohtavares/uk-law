# The Eggborough Gas Fired Generating Station (Correction) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 1312

Infrastructure Planning

The Eggborough Gas Fired Generating Station (Correction) Order 2018

Made

6th December 2018

Coming into force

7th December 2018

The Eggborough Gas Fired Generating Station Order 2018 (“the Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of the Act[^f00003].

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period, as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00004] for the correction of errors in the Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed North Yorkshire County Council and Selby District Council, as the relevant local planning authorities[^f00005], that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Eggborough Gas Fired Generating Station (Correction) Order 2018.

(2) This Order comes into force on 7th December 2018.

## 2. Corrections

(1) The Eggborough Gas Fired Generating Station Order 2018 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

Gareth Leigh

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning

Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors in the Eggborough Gas Fired Generating Station Order 2018 ([S.I. 2018/1020](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/1020)), a development consent order made under the Planning Act [2008 (c.29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/29), following a request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
