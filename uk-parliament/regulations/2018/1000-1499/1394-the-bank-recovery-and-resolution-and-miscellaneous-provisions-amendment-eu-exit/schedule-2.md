# The Bank Recovery and Resolution and Miscellaneous Provisions (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, SCHEDULE 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1394
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Amendments of other primary legislation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 3

### 1. Amendments of the Insolvency Act 1986[^c23521441]

Schedule 6 to the Insolvency Act 1986 (preferential debts) is amended in accordance with paragraphs 2 and 3.

**2.**[^c23521461] In paragraph 15BB [^c23491051]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a)—
  - (i) for “non-EEA” substitute “ non-UK ”; and
  - (ii) for “an EEA state” substitute “ the United Kingdom ”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “an EEA” substitute “ a UK ”.

**3.**[^c23521471] In paragraph 15C [^c23491061]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (3) for paragraph (b) substitute—

  > - (b) any micro, small and medium-sized enterprise, as defined with regard to the annual turnover criterion referred to in Article 2(1) of the Annex to Commission recommendation 2003/361/EC.

  .
- (b) in sub-paragraph (4)—
  - (i) for paragraphs (b) and (c) substitute—

    > - (b) “**non-UK branch**” means a branch, as defined in Article 4.1(17) of the capital requirements regulation, which is established outside the United Kingdom;
    > - (c) “**UK branch**” means a branch, as so defined, which is established in the United Kingdom,

    ;
  - (ii) at the end, insert “ , as it forms part of retained EU law[^c-c5a89d8d0fa4d97d3012064157090ebc]”.

### 4. Amendments of the Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013[^c23521481]

Paragraph 5 of Schedule 7 to the Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013 (provision that may be made by a financial market infrastructure transfer scheme) is amended as follows—

- (a) at the end of sub-paragraph (2)(e), insert the words “ (including under legislation of the European Union) ”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (2)(f), omit “, EU instrument”.
