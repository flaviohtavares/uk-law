# The Bank Recovery and Resolution and Miscellaneous Provisions (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1394
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Amendments of other secondary legislation[^c23518411]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 4 amends—

- (a) the Banking Act 2009 (Third Party Compensation Arrangements for Partial Property Transfers) Regulations 2009 [^c23490961],
- (b) the Banking Act 2009 (Restriction of Partial Property Transfers) Order 2009 [^c23490971],
- (c) the Banking Act 2009 (Restriction of Partial Property Transfers) (Recognised Central Counterparties) Order 2014 [^c23490981],
- (d) the Banking Act 2009 (Banking Group Companies) Order 2014 [^c23490991],
- (e) the Bank Recovery and Resolution Order 2014 [^c23491001],
- (f) the Banking Act 2009 (Mandatory Compensation Arrangements Following Bail-in) Regulations 2014 [^c23491011],
- (g) the Banking Act 2009 (Restriction of Special Bail-in Provision, etc.) Order 2014 [^c23491021],
- (h) the Building Societies (Bail-in) Order 2014 [^c23491031] and
- (i) the Bank Recovery and Resolution Order 2016 [^c23491041].
