# The Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 1045 (C. 81)

Financial Services

The Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2018

Made

5th October 2018

The Treasury make the following Regulations, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 37(5) and (9)(b)(ii) of the Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2018.

## 2. Commencement

The following provisions of the Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018 come into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made—

- (a) Section 27(1) to (14) (transfer to FCA of regulation of claims management services); and
- (b) Schedule 4 (regulation of claims management services: transfer schemes).

Paul Maynard

Rebecca Harris

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

5th October 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulation)

Regulation 2 of these Regulations commences section 27(1) to (14) of and Schedule 4 to the Financial Guidance and Claims Act [2018 (c.10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/10) on the day after the day these Regulations are made.
