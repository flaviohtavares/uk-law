# The Customs Transit Procedures (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 2

> Text as at 2026-06-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Common transit procedure[^c-00ca9c20ab79c323f7781711c6a9f563]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 1 has effect for the purpose of the United Kingdom giving effect to the Convention done at Interlaken on 20th May 1987 on a common transit procedure [^c23714591], as most recently amended by Decision No 1/2017 of the EU-EFTA Joint Committee on common transit [^c23714601].

(2) That Schedule must be interpreted and applied consistently with, and so as to give proper effect to, that Convention in relation to goods moving to, from or within the United Kingdom subject to the common transit procedure.
