# The Customs Transit Procedures (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 3

> Text as at 2026-06-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. TIR transit procedure[^c-522fd9012bb9cf6add6f3a437f0c5489]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 2 has effect for the purpose of the United Kingdom giving effect to the Customs Convention on the International Transport of Goods subject to cover of TIR Carnets done at Geneva on 14th November 1975, as most recently amended on 1st October 2009 [^c23714611].

(2) That Schedule must be interpreted and applied consistently with, and so as to give proper effect to, that Convention in relation to goods moving to, from or within the United Kingdom subject to the TIR transit procedure.
