# The EEA Passport Rights (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 35

## 35. Application of regulation 34[^c-ec7994749e834d5079fc7e8b53683f00]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 34 applies to a person who—

- (a) is authorised, under the law of the person’s home state, to carry on an activity which is regulated by the person’s home state regulator, [^c-139f2ae2e6d237d50ae9bb9e41033d96]...
- (b) satisfies the conditions in regulation 36, 37, 38 or 39, and[^c-3acfc6eb07bdbae542627a05c0d1c30d]
- (c) is not a Gibraltar firm.

(2) If a person satisfies the conditions in regulation 37, 38 or 39, a regulator may direct that, nevertheless, regulation 34 does not apply to the person.

(3) Regulation 34 applies to a person for the period determined in accordance with regulation 41.
