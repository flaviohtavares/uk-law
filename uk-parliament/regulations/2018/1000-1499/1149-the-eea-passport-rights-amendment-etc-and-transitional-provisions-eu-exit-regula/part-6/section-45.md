# The EEA Passport Rights (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1149
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Requirements imposed by a home state regulator[^key-ec7994749e834d5079fc7e8b53683f00]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in respect of a person to whom regulation 28 or 34 applies by virtue of the conditions in regulation 30 or 36.

(2) A requirement imposed by a home state regulator and which has effect immediately before IP completion day[^key-4a80eadd0275c27e244fa2a3e181ff22] continues to have effect on and after that day as if it were imposed by a regulator under—

- (a) section 55L (imposition of requirements by FCA) of the 2000 Act, or
- (b) section 55M (imposition of requirements by PRA) of that Act.

(3) The requirement only has effect in so far as it could have been imposed by a regulator under those sections.
