# The EEA Passport Rights (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1149
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Application of regulation 47[^c-b5f78a3d74a4b31a6b80bf62ba4b642c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 47 applies to a person who—

- (a) is authorised, under the law of the person’s home state, to carry on an activity which is regulated by the person’s home state regulator, and
- (b) satisfies the conditions in regulation 49, 49A,[^c-9ccd0dd776f759994c4fa30b93b26a7e] 50 or 51.

(2) Regulation 47 applies to such a person for the period determined in accordance with regulation 66.
