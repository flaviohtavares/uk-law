# The EEA Passport Rights (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1149
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Exemption from the general prohibition under Part 2 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^c-0a7dd45c545aa8b1271a7e22ea647ff1][^c-b5f78a3d74a4b31a6b80bf62ba4b642c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person to whom this regulation applies is an exempt person for the purposes of section 19(1)(b) of the 2000 Act (the general prohibition).

(2) The exemption has effect only in respect of a regulated activity specified (by reference to regulations 49 to 51) in regulation 52.
