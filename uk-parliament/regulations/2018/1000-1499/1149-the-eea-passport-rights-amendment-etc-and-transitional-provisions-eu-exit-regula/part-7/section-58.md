# The EEA Passport Rights (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1149
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Restriction on financial promotion[^key-b5f78a3d74a4b31a6b80bf62ba4b642c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In respect of a person to whom regulation 47 applies, section 21 of the 2000 Act (restrictions on financial promotion) has effect as if—

- (a)[^key-0a56fb385833122a9f2d39a2a473cc99] for subsection (2) there were substituted—

  > (2) But subsection (1) does not apply if A is a person to whom regulation 47 of the EEA Passport Rights (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018 applies.
- (b) after that subsection there were inserted—

  > (2A) Subsection (2)[^key-c3375e18abe2933e9e597423d4b8234e]... only applies to a person in so far as the communication is necessary for the performance of a pre-existing contract (within the meaning of regulation 68 of the EEA Passport Rights (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018).
