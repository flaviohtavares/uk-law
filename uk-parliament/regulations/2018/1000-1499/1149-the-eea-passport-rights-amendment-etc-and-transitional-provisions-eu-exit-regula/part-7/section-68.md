# The EEA Passport Rights (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 68

## 68. Interpretation of Part 7[^key-b5f78a3d74a4b31a6b80bf62ba4b642c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part—

- “contract of insurance” has the same meaning as in article 3(1) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001;
- “establishment” means a branch;
- “the FCA” means the Financial Conduct Authority;
- “home state regulator” has the meaning given by regulation 21;
- “payment service” has the same meaning as in regulation 2 of the Payment Services Regulations 2017;[^key-9da749c9a3108c0c8ecf1e4cf438ef35]
- “the PRA” means the Prudential Regulation Authority;
- “pre-existing contract”—
  - (a) in the case of a person who satisfies the conditions in regulation 49, means a contract entered into before IP completion day[^key-5a5db1516cf8fec2c9e38c728f9df146];
  - (b) in the case of a person who satisfies the conditions in regulation 50 or 51, has the same meaning, in respect of the person, as in Part 6;
- “regulated activity” has the same meaning as in section 22 of the 2000 Act;
- “regulator” means the FCA or the PRA;
- “relevant day” means—
  - (a) in the case of a person who satisfies the conditions in regulation 49, IP completion day;
  - (b) in the case of a person who satisfies the conditions in regulation 50, the earlier of—
    - (i) the day on which regulation 28 first applies to the person, or
    - (ii) where applicable, the day on which regulation 47 first applies to the person;
  - (c) in the case of a person who satisfies the conditions in regulation 51, the earlier of—
    - (i) the day on which regulation 34 first applies to the person, or
    - (ii) where applicable, the day on which regulation 47 first applies to the person.
