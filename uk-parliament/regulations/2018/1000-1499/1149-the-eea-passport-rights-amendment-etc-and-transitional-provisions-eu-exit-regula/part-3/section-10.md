# The EEA Passport Rights (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 10

## 10. Conditions to be satisfied for regulation 8 to apply

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The conditions are that, immediately before IP completion day[^c-63fa236e6a9227f9e988b642fd61a283], the person—

- (a) is authorised to carry on a regulated activity in the United Kingdom by virtue of section 31(1)(b) or (c) of the 2000 Act, [^c-d628fa8373c75c4006d0e528acb30bb2]...
- (b) is not authorised to carry on a regulated activity by virtue of section 31(1)(a) of that Act,and[^c-3d96f798bb9d17221299854a3208c42b]
- (c) is not a Gibraltar firm.
