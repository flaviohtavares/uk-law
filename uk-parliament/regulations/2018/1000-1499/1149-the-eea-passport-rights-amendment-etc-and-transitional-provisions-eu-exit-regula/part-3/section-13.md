## 13. Conditions to be satisfied for regulation 11 to apply

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The conditions are that, immediately before IP completion day[^key-1560d09a5c3db09be9942a490dc7da2c], the person—

- (a) is authorised to carry on a regulated activity in the United Kingdom by virtue of section 31(1)(b) or (c) of the 2000 Act, [^key-be0948c31f713b8e874466effecc3b38]...
- (b) is also authorised to carry on a regulated activity by virtue of section 31(1)(a) of that Act, and[^key-6f8f32076189b390e7e70c68e69f2d06]
- (c) is not a Gibraltar firm.
