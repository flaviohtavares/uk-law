# The Coroners Allowances, Fees and Expenses (Amendment) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct an error in [S.I. 2013/1615](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1615) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2018 No. 1127

Coroners, England And Wales

The Coroners Allowances, Fees and Expenses (Amendment) Regulations 2018

Made

26th October 2018

Laid before Parliament

1st November 2018

Coming into force

1st December 2018

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 9 of Schedule 7 to the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^f00001].

The Lord Chief Justice agrees to the making of these Regulations pursuant to paragraph 11(2) of Schedule 7 to that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Coroners Allowances, Fees and Expenses (Amendment) Regulations 2018 and come into force on 1st December 2018.

## 2. Amendment to the Coroners Allowances, Fees and Expenses Regulations 2013

In regulation 17(1) (indemnity) of the Coroners Allowances, Fees and Expenses Regulations 2013[^f00002], for sub-paragraph (c), substitute—

> - (c) damages awarded against the coroner, or costs ordered to be paid by the coroner, in such proceedings;

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct an error in the Coroners Allowances, Fees and Expenses Regulations 2013 ([S.I. 2013/1615](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1615)) by including an express provision to indemnify coroners against awards or orders for costs made against them in the exercise of their duties under the Coroners and Justice Act [2009 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
