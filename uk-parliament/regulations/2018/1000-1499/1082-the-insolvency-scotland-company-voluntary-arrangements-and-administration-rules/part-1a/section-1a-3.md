## 1A.3. Obtaining a moratorium by lodging document at court (section A3)[^c-d58e60afb0a7d7ff3160780727f1b3be]

*Extent: S*

An application for a moratorium under section A3 is made by lodging at court the relevant documents listed in section A6 (referred to in these Rules as “the relevant documents”).
