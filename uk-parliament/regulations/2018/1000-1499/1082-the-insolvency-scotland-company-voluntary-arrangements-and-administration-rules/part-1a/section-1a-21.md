# The Insolvency (Scotland) (Company Voluntary Arrangements and Administration) Rules 2018, section 1A.21

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1A.21. Debts that are to be disregarded for the purpose of section A38(1)(d) of the Act[^c-d58e60afb0a7d7ff3160780727f1b3be]

*Extent: S*

For the purpose of deciding whether to bring a moratorium to an end under section A38(1)(d) the monitor must disregard—

- (a) any debts that the monitor has reasonable grounds for thinking are likely to be—
  - (i) paid; or
  - (ii) compounded to the satisfaction of the creditor;

  within 5 business days of the decision; and
- (b) any debts in respect of which the creditor has agreed to defer payment until a time that is later than the decision.
