# The Insolvency (Scotland) (Company Voluntary Arrangements and Administration) Rules 2018, section 1A.7

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1A.7. Moratorium application (sections A4 and A5)[^c-d58e60afb0a7d7ff3160780727f1b3be]

*Extent: S*

An application for a moratorium under section A4 or A5 must—

- (a) state the date on which the application is lodged; and
- (b) be lodged with the court together with the relevant documents.
