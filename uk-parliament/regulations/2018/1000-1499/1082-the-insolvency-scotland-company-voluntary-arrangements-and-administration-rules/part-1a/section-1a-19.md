# The Insolvency (Scotland) (Company Voluntary Arrangements and Administration) Rules 2018, section 1A.19

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1A.19. Notification by directors to the monitor of insolvency proceedings (section A24)[^c-d58e60afb0a7d7ff3160780727f1b3be]

*Extent: S*

Notice by the directors of certain insolvency proceedings under section A24 must be delivered within the period of—

- (a) in the case of a notice under subsection (1), 3 business days ending with the day on which any of the steps mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (c) of that subsection are taken; and
- (b) in the case of a notice under subsection (2), 3 business days beginning with the day on which the duty to give the notice arises.
