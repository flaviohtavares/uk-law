# The Insolvency (Scotland) (Company Voluntary Arrangements and Administration) Rules 2018, section 1A.30

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1A.30. Secured debts[^c-d58e60afb0a7d7ff3160780727f1b3be]

*Extent: S*

(1) In calculating the amount of a secured creditor’s claim for the purposes of a decision procedure under section A12 of the Act, the value of the debt for voting purposes is its full value without deduction of the value of the security.

(2) In calculating the amount of a secured creditor’s claim for the purposes of a decision procedure under section A44(4)(c) of the Act, the secured creditor is to deduct the value of any security as estimated by the secured creditor.
