# The Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food) (England) (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Exiting The European Union, England

Animals, England

The Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food) (England) (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018

Sift requirements satisfied

13th November 2018

Made

26th November 2018

Laid before Parliament

27th November 2018

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001].

The requirements of paragraph 3(2) of Schedule 7 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 (relating to the appropriate Parliamentary procedure for these Regulations) have been satisfied.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food) (England) (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018 and come into force on exit day.

## 2. Amendments to the Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food) (England) Regulations 2006

(1) The Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food) (England) Regulations 2006[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 6, omit “and other member States”.

(3) In Part 3, in the heading, omit “Community”.

(4) In regulation 10—

- (a) in the heading, for “Community” substitute “Experts involved in”;
- (b) in the words before paragraph (a), omit “to be carried out by Commission experts pursuant to Article 45”.

(5) Omit regulation 12.

(6) In regulation 14(1)(d), omit “or 12”.

(7) In regulation 17(1)(c), for “, 10 or 12” substitute “or 10”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of these Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of powers conferred by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address failures of retained EU law to operate effectively and other deficiencies (in particular under paragraphs (a), (d) and (g) of section 8(2)) arising from the withdrawal of the UK from the European Union.

These Regulations make amendments to the Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food) (England) Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/3472](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/3472)).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
