# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Disclosure of an appointment[^c-07b624783eb696ac0bf4f3d54d4206a8][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a person (“P”) appoints another person (“A”) to act on P's behalf as a Customs agent, A must disclose that agency in each temporary storage declaration and Customs declaration [^c23522121] which is made by A as agent for P.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply—

- (a) to an appointment of a person as a Customs agent which is treated as disclosed by regulation 81(2)(a); or
- (b) where a Customs declaration is made by conduct as provided by section 4 of chapter 2 of Part 4 of these Regulations.
