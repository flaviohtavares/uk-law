# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Free-circulation procedure: miscellaneous goods[^key-9e402307fae50759a338f32ea9a5ea5e][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An individual may make a Customs declaration for the free-circulation procedure orally in respect of the goods listed in Part A (miscellaneous goods) of the Oral or By conduct list, if at the time of import full relief[^key-ec982bffa9c34788db21f41c06be3285] from import duty is available in respect of the goods to that individual or the person on whose behalf the declaration is made by virtue of the case described in section 8 (agriculture and animals) or section 37 (returned goods relief) of the UK Reliefs document[^key-c9185a86d5aa7982153b5b714cbfa830] .
