# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 26B

## 26B. Free-circulation procedure: pleasure craft[^key-7b49729f91d2e35e9a7cedae8bfe1cc2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), an individual who is a qualifying traveller may make a Customs declaration for the free-circulation procedure by the conduct described in paragraph (3) in respect of goods which are a pleasure craft, if at the time of import full relief from import duty is available in respect of the goods to that individual or the person on whose behalf the declaration is made by virtue of the case described in section 37 of the UK Reliefs document (returned goods relief)[^key-25ddf21cdcc154709faffd0238c5d407].

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply where the goods are liable on import to value added tax.

(3) The conduct referred to in paragraph (1) is where the individual enters the limits of a port in the United Kingdom in the pleasure craft.
