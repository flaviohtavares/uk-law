# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Definition: simplified Customs declaration process[^c23523671][^key-9bcbb868dffa21bc700b2c4ebfe95917][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) “**Simplified Customs declaration process**” means the process in relation to Customs declarations provided by regulation 32 such that the requirements made by or under Part 1 of the Act in relation to Customs declarations which would otherwise apply are disapplied or simplified.

(2) The simplified Customs declaration process does not apply to a Customs declaration made for a transit procedure or[^key-4c15d5a6c177aa609b44a1670a9d1167] in any case specified in a notice published by HMRC[^key-3da83d869f4b0b8506eab6026b3acfb1].
