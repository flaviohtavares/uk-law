# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 29A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29A. Definitions: transitional EIDR simplified Customs declaration process[^c-10ebacbadda1c14d60e4e6409310af1b][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) “Transitional EIDR simplified Customs declaration process” means the process in relation to Customs declarations provided by regulation 29C such that the requirements made by or under Part 1 of the Act in relation to Customs declarations which would otherwise apply are disapplied or simplified.

(2) “Transitional EIDR procedure” has the meaning given in regulation 29D(1).
