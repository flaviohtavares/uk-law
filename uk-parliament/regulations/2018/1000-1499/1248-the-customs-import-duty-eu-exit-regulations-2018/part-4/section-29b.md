# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 29B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29B. Persons eligible to complete Part 1 of the transitional EIDR simplified Customs declaration process[^key-ed63330e26fef2b9a9582e6ca4d8b045][^key-10ebacbadda1c14d60e4e6409310af1b][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person is an “**eligible person**” for the purposes of this section if the person—

- (a) is treated as such in relation to regulation 29C(1)(a) by regulation 85A(2) (authorisation automatically granted); or
- (b) is approved as an eligible person in relation to regulation 29C(1)(a) following an application under regulation 85A(8) (application for authorisation following revocation).
