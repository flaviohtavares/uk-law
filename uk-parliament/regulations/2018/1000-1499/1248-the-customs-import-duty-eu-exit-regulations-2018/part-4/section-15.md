# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Eligibility of persons to make Customs declarations: UK establishment[^c-36830438f8307658058dbac55394d814][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^c-359c9282c52ab00f5b21599bc816dfb8][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person eligible [^c23521681] to make a Customs declaration in respect of chargeable goods [^c23521691] may not do so unless the person is established in the United Kingdom.

(2) The requirement provided by paragraph (1) does not apply to—

- (a) a person who declares chargeable goods for a special Customs procedure [^c23521701] except a customs warehouse procedure[^c-858781d1d6e554c2aebc1d2b0f60c72d];
- (b) a Customs agent [^c23521711] acting in that capacity; or
- (c) a person who makes a Customs declaration of a type described in sections 2 to 4 of this chapter.
