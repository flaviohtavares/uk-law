# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 26D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248

## 26D. Free-circulation procedure: goods imported at other listed locations[^c-8e3e7313952fb585e59ce7a71ef7a196]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An individual may make a Customs declaration for the free-circulation procedure by the conduct described in paragraph (2) in respect of the goods listed in Part A (miscellaneous goods) of the Oral or By conduct list if—

- (a) the goods are unaccompanied chargeable goods;
- (b) the goods are imported at an other listed location; and
- (c) at the time of import full relief[^c-f4a68ec5f5fc8862b767ea6d6ada902a] from import duty is available in respect of the goods to that individual or the person on whose behalf the declaration is made by virtue of the case described in section 8 (agriculture and animals) or section 37 (returned goods relief) of the UK Reliefs document[^c-e376ba7153e09b0414b684624b0a1974].

(2) The conduct referred to in paragraph (1) is that described in regulation 25(3B).
