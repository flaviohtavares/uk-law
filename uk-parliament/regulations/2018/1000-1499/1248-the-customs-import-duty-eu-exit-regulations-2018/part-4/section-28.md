# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 28

## 28. Customs declarations made orally: notification and acceptance[^c-b89a3cc5a6a98e3907eb21e7cebe0f14][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in respect of chargeable goods where a Customs declaration is made orally as provided by section 2 of this chapter.

(2) The following are treated as occurring when an HMRC officer gives the information described in regulation 17(3)(b)—

- (a) notification of importation of the goods; and
- (b) acceptance [^c23521781] of the Customs declaration and notification of its acceptance to the declarant.
