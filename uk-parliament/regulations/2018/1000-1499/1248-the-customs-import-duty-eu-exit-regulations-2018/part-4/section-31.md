# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 31

## 31. Persons authorised to use the simplified Customs declaration process[^c23523681][^key-5f4e274f2e5363a574d01fefdf399792][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person (“an authorised declarant”) may use the simplified Customs declaration process if authorised to do so by HMRC.

(2) Subject to paragraph (4)[^key-8474a9475bbd26477eba6d354e65687d], the eligibility criteria to be so authorised are that [^key-239450a02966ce2790aa37d5ae5be7b8]...—

- (a) the person[^key-d3308dd4319182def3f922f82126af72] meets the criteria which apply to be approved as an authorised economic operator as provided by regulation 93(1)(c)[^key-a4cb272b4355d409b29911b821e9703d] , (d), (f) and (g)[^key-26cc854be4c65463f711b20ffe8600a8], as if reference to suitability to be an authorised economic operator were a reference to suitability to be an authorised declarant;
- (b) the person[^key-c2f2daf3208f7456b45c23fd34f8062c] demonstrates to an HMRC officer that appropriate procedures are in place such that the person can use the simplified Customs declaration process competently; and[^key-4f2cc98b47edbab7cbf722d97baa550b]
- (c)[^key-082619863819143ce70fe0102bd6b3a2] the person—
  - (i) has in place, as appropriate to the authorisation sought, a single guarantee or comprehensive guarantee; or
  - (ii) is approved to defer payment of a liability to import duty in accordance with regulation 43.
- (d)[^key-0ce00940f36f7dbc4aac2ba9f43bb760] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) HMRC may publish a notice setting out appropriate procedures for the purposes of paragraph (2)(b).

(4) A person who is an authorised economic operator may be authorised as an authorised declarant even if the person does not meet the eligibility criterion in paragraph (2)(b).

(4A)[^key-5046ec7813505c3fb58137054942a296] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) An authorisation may be given in respect of—

- (a) a Customs declaration to be made which is identified in the authorisation; or
- (b) Customs declarations of a type, and to be made within a period, which are identified in the authorisation.

(6) The simplified Customs declaration process must be used in compliance with any conditions contained in the authorisation.

(7) The simplified Customs declaration process may not be used by a Customs agent [^c23521821] in respect of a Customs declaration where—

- (a)[^key-0efd15b24a669926887f68b6e2c3ed5d] the principal of the agent is an authorised declarant;
- (b) the agent is not an authorised declarant; and
- (c) the agent intends to act as an indirect agent on behalf of the principal in making the Customs declaration.

(8)[^key-d672656fe82ba71d56a66953a3bb839b] A Customs agent may use the simplified Customs declaration process if—

- (a) the agent is an authorised declarant;
- (b) the agent intends to act as a direct agent on behalf of the principal of the agent in making the Customs declaration; and
- (c)[^key-bf13aa72cc3d4651b3770bf36984686c] the principal is established in the United Kingdom.
