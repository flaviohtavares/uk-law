# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 149

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 149. Goods declared to a customs procedure[^c-44cfeea86bd40e0090916863ad09f7bb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where before IP completion day[^c-1084a6b2e5a45e0316d6953220252f7b] a declaration is made to HMRC in respect of the goods to a customs procedure and—

- (a) before IP completion day the declaration has not been accepted by HMRC; or
- (b) if the declaration has been accepted before IP completion day, the goods have not been released before IP completion day by HMRC to the procedure.

(2) The EUCL continues to have effect in relation to the goods on and after IP completion day.
