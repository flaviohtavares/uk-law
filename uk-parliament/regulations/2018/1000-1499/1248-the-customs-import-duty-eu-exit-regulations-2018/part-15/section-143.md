# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 143

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 143. Interpretation[^key-818f980f446ccc0b9a0efa55c36e1d68]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, the following expressions [^c23522641] have the same meaning as they do for the purposes of the UCC—

- “Common Customs Tariff” [^c23522651];
- “customs procedure”, and “accepted”, “discharged” and “released” and cognate expressions as used in relation to a customs procedure [^c23522661];
- “customs declaration”, except where reference is made to such a declaration provided by or under the Act;
- “customs supervision”;
- “customs territory of the Union”;
- “decision taken upon application” [^c23522671];
- [^key-fa92d0191976709e46d3cf2aee7e5a35]...
- “special procedure” and the following types of special procedure: “end-use procedure”, “outward processing procedure”, “storage procedure” and “transit procedure”[^c23522681];
- “temporary storage”;
- “temporary storage declaration”, except where reference is made to a such declaration provided under the Act;
- [^key-ba8cd7d78a8da47cd5ba2dedd47a3e2c]...
- “Union goods”; and
- “verification” [^c23522701].
