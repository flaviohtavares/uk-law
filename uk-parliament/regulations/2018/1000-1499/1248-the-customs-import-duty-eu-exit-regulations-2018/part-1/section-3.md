# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 3

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Establishment and notices and notifications[^c23523401][^key-7371661061c4d566b985e585c7dd18b0][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In these Regulations, a person is established in the United Kingdom—

- (a) in the case of an individual, where the individual is resident in the United Kingdom; and
- (b) in any other case, where the person—
  - (i) has a registered office in the United Kingdom; or
  - (ii) has a permanent place in the United Kingdom from which the person carries out activities for which the person is constituted to perform.

(2)[^key-c2b3e66ee83bd2b1f6549317ada3a842] In these Regulations, a notice or notification means one made in writing and a requirement to notify is to be read accordingly.

(3)[^key-c2b3e66ee83bd2b1f6549317ada3a842] A notice published by HMRC under these Regulations may make different provision for different cases or different purposes.
