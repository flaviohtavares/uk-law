# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 129

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Interpretation[^key-8657b79b4d8cb03b9b7e72694524e72c][^key-e9539de1f3adb87e890ff5ce9b4caf15][^key-d6ee7542387b5041523a2599d06622cd][^key-dedcbd1d51ab5125253ef0462db92d28][^key-d6ee7542387b5041523a2599d06622cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**RoRo listed location**” has the meaning given by regulation 130(1);
- “**RoRo vehicle**” means a wheeled motorised vehicle or a non-motorised wheeled trailer which may be attached to such a vehicle, which is driven onto, and off, a train or vessel which conveys the vehicle or trailer on board and where the vehicle or trailer is used for the purposes of freight transport.
