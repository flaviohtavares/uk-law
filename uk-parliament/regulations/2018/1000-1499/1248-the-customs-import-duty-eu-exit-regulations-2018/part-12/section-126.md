# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 126

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126. Method 6 valuation[^key-34b351430993d62ae42e4dbaa35a97d1][^key-3b215733b0bd1af66993893b5fdf4612][^key-3b215733b0bd1af66993893b5fdf4612]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

“**Method 6 valuation**” means the valuation of chargeable goods presented to Customs on import which is the value determined by applying—

- (a) such of the elements of valuation used in any other method of valuation in this chapter; and
- (b) the principles for the valuation of goods adopted by the WTO in the Agreement on Implementation of Article VII of the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade 1994 [^c23522521],

as are reasonable to apply in order to determine the value of the chargeable goods immediately before discharge of those goods from the free-circulation procedure[^key-34cd44849090cd5b50d50298044fdace].
