# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 110

## 110. Provision of evidence[^key-138e6131f3aab3e39ff086c41ef7a62f][^key-3b215733b0bd1af66993893b5fdf4612][^key-3b215733b0bd1af66993893b5fdf4612]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

HMRC may publish a notice specifying the type of evidence to be provided to an HMRC officer which supports—

- (a) the use of one Method instead of another; or
- (b) the determination of a valuation.
