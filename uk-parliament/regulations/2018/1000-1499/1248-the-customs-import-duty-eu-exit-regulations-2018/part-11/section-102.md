## 102. Retention of domestic status[^key-dfa214ec2b966ef1d5c4d0509217e59e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-0667dbf70c7f31487061f6fc9b8d66cf] Goods exported from the United Kingdom as a result of their removal from Great Britain[^key-0e4e1d50052a54d20e80fbc059736d5a] in accordance with the applicable export provisions [^c23522321] retain their status as domestic goods [^c23522331] if the goods—

- (a) merely pass through places outside Great Britain[^key-c8676d7a2559dc2c0fd6cb69173fbe70] before arriving at their ultimate destination in Great Britain, [^key-1cdc588115a77bf66b67420ce52edce5]...
- (b)[^key-0223fbda8511c311c89c026d980cfa36] are itemised in a travel document issued in Great Britain[^key-bf7c93c48293a815ab5a1699f7b944a1] which specifies the destination of the goods, and
- (c)[^key-abfe24d4e38f650cb0d63c0afa43f471] are goods in respect of which an exit summary declaration is required and has been made.

(2) An “**exit summary declaration**” has the same meaning as it has in Article 5(10) of the UCC.
