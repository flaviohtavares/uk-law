# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 103

## 103. Goods presumed not to be domestic goods[^key-3a84e3fcf3e1927d45860205ba9c99b6][^key-c6cef333cd93aa3aa9aba76b05ff38db]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Goods to which this paragraph applies are presumed not to be domestic goods unless the contrary is shown.

(2) Paragraph (1) applies to—

- (a) goods that are in Great Britain because they have been imported; and
- (b) goods that have been removed from a free zone otherwise than in accordance with regulation 18F of the special procedures regulations.
