# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Interpretation[^c-131e5fb6364c6df6a3d63e8cca5d75d8][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part, a person is “involved in a breach of a relevant Customs obligation” if that person is within one of paragraphs (a) to (d) of section 6(4) of the Act.
