# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 137

## 137. Examination and taking samples of goods[^key-1e34feeb08bbc0b7acdfc208cd1668fd][^key-60e2efcc2f85928a995ae98dd1574c9c][^key-60e2efcc2f85928a995ae98dd1574c9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where a person makes , in respect of goods, a Customs declaration, an export declaration or a declaration for an outward processing procedure[^key-b521e9f66615c1c6d0272190855d5ab9] and a requirement exists—

- (a) to examine and take account of the goods under section 159(1) of CEMA 1979;
- (b) to take[^key-f4195512cd94529c63bbc8ab0f07df36] samples of the goods under section 160(1) of CEMA 1979; or
- (c) to examine or take[^key-ee64edb579e2dfb5c39c6763b52a624c] samples of the goods in order that an HMRC officer may provide a ruling further to section 24 of the Act.

(2) HMRC may charge the person making the declaration a fee in relation to, as appropriate, the examination, account or taking of samples—

- (a) undertaken on behalf of HMRC by a person other than an HMRC officer or proper officer; or
- (b) undertaken by an HMRC officer or proper officer—
  - (i) in the case described in paragraph (1)(c); or
  - (ii) if the person has failed to comply with directions given further to section 30 of CEMA 1979 in relation to the goods which are the subject of the declaration.
