# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 138

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. Destruction of goods[^key-1ad46a0da4277c92ac56f3d5101ded03][^key-60e2efcc2f85928a995ae98dd1574c9c][^key-60e2efcc2f85928a995ae98dd1574c9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where goods seized by HMRC are destroyed.

(2) HMRC may charge the person who was in possession or control of the goods before they were seized (other than an HMRC officer or proper officer) or the owner of the goods a fee in relation to the destruction of the goods carried out on behalf of HMRC by a person other than an HMRC officer or proper officer.
