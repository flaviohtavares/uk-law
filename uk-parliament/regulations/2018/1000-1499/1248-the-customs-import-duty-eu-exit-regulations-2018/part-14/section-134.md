# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 134

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 134. Requested attendance at premises or vehicles on a Sunday or other non-working days[^key-0b212dcc3137526c0a28290d8fc93624][^key-60e2efcc2f85928a995ae98dd1574c9c][^key-60e2efcc2f85928a995ae98dd1574c9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a person requests attendance by an HMRC officer or proper officer at premises or a vehicle because—

- (a) the person is required—
  - (i) to make a report in respect of a vehicle under section 35 of CEMA 1979 [^c23522551]; or
  - (ii) to deliver an account in respect of a ship under section 36 of CEMA 1979 [^c23522561]; or
- (b) the attendance is required in connection with goods the export of which is required to be made[^key-26d14c76f53fc8d331d964e00a76eb9c] in accordance with the applicable export provisions [^c23522571] or an outward processing procedure[^key-1f648505b97094b89e771a340e4ec2e1],

HMRC may charge that person a fee in relation to the attendance but only where the attendance is requested to occur on a day which is not a working day but treating a Saturday as a working day for the these purposes.

(2) In this regulation, “**vehicle**” has the same meaning as it has in section 1(1) of CEMA 1979 [^c23522581].
