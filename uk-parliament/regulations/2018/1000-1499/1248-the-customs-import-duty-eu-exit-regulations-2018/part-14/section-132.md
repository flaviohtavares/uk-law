# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 132

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 132. Interpretation[^c-a526f0b3d73e37e53feba60f7e3b3076][^c-60e2efcc2f85928a995ae98dd1574c9c][^c-60e2efcc2f85928a995ae98dd1574c9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, “**proper officer**” means a person engaged by the orders or with the concurrence of the HMRC Commissioners who acts with, or in place of, an HMRC officer in the exercise of a function of HMRC described in regulation 139 ; and “[^c-a44ad326a4938da9ae19f53f7c886259]**export declaration**” has the meaning given in regulation 2 of the Customs (Export) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (“CEEER 2019”).
