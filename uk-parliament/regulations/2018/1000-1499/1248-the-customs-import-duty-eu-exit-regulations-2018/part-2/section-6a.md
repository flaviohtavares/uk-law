# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 6A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6A. No requirement to present goods on import: goods that are not unloaded[^c-4821e0462e512196ebde0d71d09cc73a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There is no requirement to present goods to Customs on import where the goods are not unloaded from the vessel, train or aircraft on which they are imported.
