Interpretation[^c23756471][^c-af000790c60bdc871688be5adf23232e][^c-d5654f280b65066860d88d4791270b82][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^c-4aaf4a35e88bf27ccd8bba579ef4d81b][^c-cd5fe9bf8d67d48ab1e10afb04708c15][^c-d5654f280b65066860d88d4791270b82][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^c-4aaf4a35e88bf27ccd8bba579ef4d81b][^c-cd5fe9bf8d67d48ab1e10afb04708c15]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**charges**” means fees under Part 14 of these Regulations, interest payable for late payment of import duty and penalties under Part 3 of the Finance Act 2003 [^c23522221];
- [^c-b83a08a6f537bb39e25bebb905cf18d9]...
- “**specified amount**” has the meaning given in regulation 98.
