# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 87

## 87. HMRC to consider whether an approval falls to be determined[^c23756401][^key-b73179001ccaf1eb17cbf1ac9f4429ad][^key-ee3beab34d2a16480972ab423209d8b6][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-7eaa0e454d0dfc9d6d36e66017ba0303][^key-f440e9ad7acba804821d27367fcc7ef6][^key-f2a8faf6add7c21b8d81d28c22cf36ef][^key-98bc577f7299799a744a1ede2b4c5159][^key-ee3beab34d2a16480972ab423209d8b6][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-7eaa0e454d0dfc9d6d36e66017ba0303][^key-f2a8faf6add7c21b8d81d28c22cf36ef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

By no later than 30 days after the date on which an application, or purported application, for approval is received by HMRC, HMRC must notify the person making the application or purported application that —

- (a) the application is one that falls to be determined; or
- (b) the purported application does not fall to be determined because, for the reasons set out in the notification,—
  - (i) the purported application is not made in accordance with regulation 85; or
  - (ii) the purported application is made contrary to regulation 86.
