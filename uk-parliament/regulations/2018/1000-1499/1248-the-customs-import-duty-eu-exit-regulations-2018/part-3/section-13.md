# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Breach of handling requirement[^key-5cd19f9952e107a2f3f28794da36af2b][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where—

- (a) goods are in the possession or control of[^key-ede402ed2c2c4a753094a3ba7a1c02af] an approved person; and
- (b) the approved person fails to comply with paragraph (1), (3) or (4) of regulation 12 in respect of the goods,

the temporary storage declaration in respect of the goods is to be treated as withdrawn and regulation 11(2) applies.
