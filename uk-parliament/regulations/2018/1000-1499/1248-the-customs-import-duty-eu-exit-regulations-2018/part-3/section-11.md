# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Temporary storage declarations: failure to make and withdrawal[^c-752c93f22da11cece8fe0a29489a9a82][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^c-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies to goods imported into the United Kingdom in respect of which a temporary storage declaration—

- (a) is not made as required by regulation 8(2); or
- (b) is treated as withdrawn as provided by regulation 10.

(2) The goods are to be treated—

- (a) as imported but not presented to Customs on import; and
- (b) in consequence as goods to which section 5(1) of the Act [^c23521641] applies.
