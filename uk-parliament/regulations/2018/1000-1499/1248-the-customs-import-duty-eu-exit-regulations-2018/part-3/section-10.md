# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 10

>

## 10. Withdrawal of temporary storage declarations[^key-0d4f0f4948c1b57410efcda215dba813][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A temporary storage declaration is treated as withdrawn if the goods in respect of which the declaration is made are not presented to Customs within 30 days of the date on which the declaration is made.
