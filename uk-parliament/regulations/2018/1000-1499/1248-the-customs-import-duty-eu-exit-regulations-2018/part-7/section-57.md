# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Applicants[^key-500087ab3f55f80829b969e30fb1945f][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-9887b5ec47b0e7ee20527f9cfeaa88dc][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An application for remission of duty may be made by a person who is liable to pay the import duty.

(2) An application for repayment of duty may be made by a person who has paid some or all of the payment to which the application relates.
