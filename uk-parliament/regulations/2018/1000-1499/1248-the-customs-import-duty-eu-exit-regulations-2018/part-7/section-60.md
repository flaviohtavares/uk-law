# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Meaning of “application”[^key-4a5f97c0bbe4ea48b11fcf56bc55a97e][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-9887b5ec47b0e7ee20527f9cfeaa88dc][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this chapter and the following chapters of this Part, “**application**” means an application for remission or repayment of duty, or both, as the case may be.
