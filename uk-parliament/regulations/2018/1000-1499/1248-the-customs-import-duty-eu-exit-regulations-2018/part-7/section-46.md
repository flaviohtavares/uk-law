# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1248
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Interpretation[^key-aab05cedd7f85563aee94e193f966765][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**application for remission of duty**” has the meaning given by regulation 54(3);
- “**application for repayment of duty**” has the meaning given by regulation 55(3);
- “**reduced duty case**” has the meaning given by regulation 47; and
- “**remit**” means to discharge a liability to import duty.
