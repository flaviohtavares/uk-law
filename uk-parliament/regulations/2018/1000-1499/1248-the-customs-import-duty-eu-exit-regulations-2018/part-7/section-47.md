# The Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 47

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Reduced duty cases[^key-ba93369ee4817c6d8acd9f4ea85f3252][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, a “reduced duty case” is a case described in this chapter and the amount of import duty in respect of which a remission or repayment may be made further to an application under chapter 3 is the amount stated in each case.
