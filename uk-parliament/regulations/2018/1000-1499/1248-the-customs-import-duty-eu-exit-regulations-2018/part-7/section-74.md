## 74. No applications for remission of duty[^key-6d03aef08b94a9b49dc496e56068624b][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62][^key-302606d159bff3b39c5b1dbf0584ce62]

No application for remission of duty is to be made in respect of the cases described in regulations 75 to 78.
