# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 145

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 145.[^c23537091]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where an SE has converted on IP completion day[^c-6385e688eed28144e1cf7ddc79604ea2] to a UK Societas, that UK Societas is required to comply with the provisions in Part 6 (trading disclosures) of the Company, Limited Liability Partnership and Business (Names and Trading Disclosures) Regulations 2015 [^c23529121] within the period of three months beginning on IP completion day.
