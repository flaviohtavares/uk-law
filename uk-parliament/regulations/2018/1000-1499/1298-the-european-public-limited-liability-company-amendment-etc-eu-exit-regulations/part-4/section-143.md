# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 143

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 143.[^c23537071]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the amendments made by regulation 28 would (apart from this paragraph) require a UK Societas's register of supervisory organ members to contain additional particulars in respect of an existing corporate member, the UK Societas need not comply with that requirement until three months after IP completion day[^c-02e0737bdf28f33266ad5db7e4020bf1].

(2) In this paragraph, “**existing corporate member**” means a body corporate, or a firm that is a legal person under the law by which it is governed, whose particulars were contained in the UK Societas's register of supervisory organ members immediately before IP completion day[^c-e9fbe8b396d4527f59b47ba9c2d287f3].
