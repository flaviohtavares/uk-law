# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 148

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 148.[^c23537121]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Despite the amendments and revocations made by these Regulations, regulations 3 and 7 to 18 of the pre-exit 2009 GB Regulations continue, on and after IP completion day[^key-92113df1f9fa621cf92beeefd09b03d6], to have effect in relation to a special negotiating body reconvened under regulation 17 (decision not to open, or to terminate, negotiations) of the 2009 GB Regulations, as appropriate and practicable and subject to the modifications in regulation 149.
