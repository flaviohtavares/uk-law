# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 154

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154.[^c23537181]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulations 155 to 159A[^key-e2919f4b9c8dfa7d57b83673dc096b90]—

- “**the pre-exit 2009 NI Regulations**” means the European Public Limited-Liability Company (Employee Involvement) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2009, as they had effect immediately before IP completion day[^key-ea975ebe51e410b0751416d2ed9d674d];
- “**the 2009 NI Regulations**” means the European Public Limited-Liability Company (Employee Involvement) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2009;
- “**SE**” means a European Public Limited-Liability Company (or Societas Europaea) within the meaning of the EC Regulation, as it had effect immediately before IP completion day.
