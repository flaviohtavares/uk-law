# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 142

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 142.[^c23537061]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) To the extent that enactments or rules of law applied to a dissolved SE immediately before IP completion day[^c-d626624643429f84e74d32b67d016005], by virtue of applying to a public limited-liability company pursuant to the EC Regulation, they continue to apply to a dissolved SE after IP completion day.

(2) If an SE dissolved before IP completion day[^c-fbd77cccd1f8e5ecf61161862ea57b08], and accordingly did not convert to a UK Societas under the EC Regulation, but after IP completion day is restored to the register, upon restoration it is deemed to have become a UK Societas on IP completion day.
