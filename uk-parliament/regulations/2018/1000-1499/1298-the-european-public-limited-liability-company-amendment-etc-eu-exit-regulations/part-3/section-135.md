# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 135

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 135.[^c23536991]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 66—

- (a) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) for “an SE” substitute “ a UK Societas ”;
  - (ii) omit from “governed by” to the end of the paragraph;
- (b) in paragraph 2, for “an SE” substitute “ a UK Societas ”;
- (c) in paragraph 3, for “SE” substitute “ UK Societas ”;
- (d) in paragraph 4, for “the manner laid down in each Member State's law in accordance with Article 3 of Directive [68/151/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1968/0151)” substitute “ in accordance with regulation 86 of the 2004 Regulations ”;
- (e) in paragraph 5, for “the national provisions adopted in implementation of Article 10 of Directive [78/855/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0855), by a judicial or administrative authority in the Member State to which the SE being converted into a public limited liability company is subject” substitute “ section 909 of the Companies Act 2006 (Expert's report (merger)) [^c23529081] ”;
- (f) in paragraph 6—
  - (i) for “SE” substitute “ UK Societas ”;
  - (ii) for “the provisions of national law adopted in implementation of Article 7 of Directive [78/855/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0855)” substitute “ section 907 of the Companies Act 2006 (approval of members of merging companies) ”.
