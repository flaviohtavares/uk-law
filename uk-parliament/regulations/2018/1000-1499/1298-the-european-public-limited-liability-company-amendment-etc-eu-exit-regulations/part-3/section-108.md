# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 108

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108.[^c23536031]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 13—

- (a) for “an SE” substitute “ a UK Societas ”;
- (b) for “the laws of the Member State in which the SE has its registered office in accordance with Directive [68/151/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1968/0151)” substitute “ the 2004 Regulations ”.
