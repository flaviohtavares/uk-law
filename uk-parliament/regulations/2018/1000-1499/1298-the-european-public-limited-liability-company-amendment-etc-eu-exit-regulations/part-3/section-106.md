# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 106

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 106.[^c23536011]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 11—

- (a) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) for “an SE” substitute “ a UK Societas ”;
  - (ii) for “SE” substitute “ UK Societas ”;
- (b) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) for “SEs” substitute “ UK Societates ”;
  - (ii) for “SE” substitute “ UK Societas ”;
- (c) in paragraph 3—
  - (i) for “a Member State” substitute “ England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland ”;
  - (ii) for “the date of entry into force of this Regulation” substitute “IP completion day[^c-36a92ed2eb70a18f50eef45e98954900]”;
  - (iii) for “SE” substitute “ UK Societas ”.
