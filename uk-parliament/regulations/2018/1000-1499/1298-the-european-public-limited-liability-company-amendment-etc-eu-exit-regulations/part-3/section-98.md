# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 98

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 98.[^c23535931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 1—

- (a) omit paragraph 1;
- (b) in paragraphs 2 and 3, for “an SE” substitute “ a UK Societas ”;
- (c) for paragraph 4 substitute—

  > **4.**
  >
  > Employee involvement in a UK Societas shall be governed by the provisions of—
  >
  > - (a) for England and Wales and for Scotland, the 2009 Employee Involvement (GB) Regulations; or
  > - (b) for Northern Ireland, the 2009 Employee Involvement (NI) Regulations.

  .
