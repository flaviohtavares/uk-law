# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 33

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33.[^c23533651]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 80E (putting a member of the supervisory organ's address on the public record) [^c23528811]—

- (a) for “an SE” substitute “ a UK Societas ”;
- (b) for “SE's”, in both places, substitute “UK Societas's”.
