# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 68

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68.[^c23989231]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 4 (application of these regulations)—

- (a) for paragraph (1) substitute—

  > (1) These Regulations apply in respect of a UK Societas.

  ;
- (b) omit paragraph (2);
- (c) in paragraph (3), for sub-paragraph (c) substitute—

  > - (d) an employee of an SE or a representative of such an employee.

  .
