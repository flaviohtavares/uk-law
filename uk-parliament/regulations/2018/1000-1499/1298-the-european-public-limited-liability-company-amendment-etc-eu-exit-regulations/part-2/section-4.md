# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 4

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4.[^c23533361]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 3 (interpretation) [^c23528641]—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) in the definition of “SE”—
    - (aa) after “EC Regulation [^c23528651]” insert “ , as it had effect immediately before IP completion day[^c-98b3381b27e27a455cf3f2347caa912e], ”;
    - (bb) omit “which is to be, or is,”;
  - (ii) after the definition of “SE”, insert—

    > “**UK Societas**” means an SE on or after IP completion day.

    ;
- (b) in paragraphs (3) and (4)—
  - (i) omit “or the EC Directive”;
  - (ii) omit “or Directive”.
