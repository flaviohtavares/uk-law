# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 28

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28.[^c23533601]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 80A (particulars of members to be registered under regulation 79: corporate members and firms) [^c23528761]—

- (a) for “an SE's” substitute “a UK Societas's”;
- (b) for paragraph (c) substitute—

  > - (c) in the case of a limited company that is a UK-registered company, the registered number;

  .
