# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 18

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18.[^c23533501]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 67 (SEs subject to law on public limited liability companies as regard the expression of their capital (Article 67(1)))—

- (a) in the heading, for “SEs” substitute “ UK Societates ”;
- (b) for “an SE” substitute “ a UK Societas ”.
