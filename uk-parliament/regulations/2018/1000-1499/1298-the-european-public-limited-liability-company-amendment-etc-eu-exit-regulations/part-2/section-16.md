# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 16

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16.[^c23533481]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 65 (timing of the first general meeting of an SE (Article 54(1)))—

- (a) in both the heading and the text of the regulation, for “an SE” substitute “ a UK Societas ”;
- (b) for “an SE's” substitute “ its ”;
- (c) after “incorporation”, insert “ as an SE ”.
