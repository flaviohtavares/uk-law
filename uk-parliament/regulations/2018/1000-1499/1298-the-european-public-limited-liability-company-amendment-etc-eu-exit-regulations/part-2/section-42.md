# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 42

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42.[^c23988981]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 87 (registration under the 2006 Act) [^c23528881]—

- (a) in paragraph (3)—
  - (i) for “an SE” substitute “ a UK Societas ”;
  - (ii) for the remaining occurrence of “SE” substitute “ UK Societas ”;
- (b) in paragraph (4), for “SE” substitute “ UK Societas ”.
