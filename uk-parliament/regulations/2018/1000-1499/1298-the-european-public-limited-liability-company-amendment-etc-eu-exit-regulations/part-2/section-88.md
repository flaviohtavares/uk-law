# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 88

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88.[^c23989431]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2 (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “SE”, for “which is to be, or is, registered in the United Kingdom” substitute “ , as it had effect immediately before IP completion day[^c-ac99b1688d4d171f252602416db9effc], which was registered in the United Kingdom ”;
- (b) after the definition of “SE” insert—

  > “**UK Societas**” means an SE which on IP completion day[^c-5f86ec91a33071d54400a384236b00d3] converted to a United Kingdom Societas within the meaning of Council Regulation 2157/2001/EC of 8 October 2001 on the Statute of a European Company.

  .
