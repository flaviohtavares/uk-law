# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 46

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46.[^c23989011]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 2 (provisions of the 2006 Act applying to the registration of SEs) [^c23528921]—

- (a) in the heading, for “SEs” substitute “ UK Societates ”;
- (b) in paragraphs 1, 2, 3 and 4, for “an SE”, in each place it occurs, substitute “ a UK Societas ”.
