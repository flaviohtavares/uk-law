# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 31

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31.[^c23533631]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 80D (protected information: restriction on use or disclosure by SE) [^c23528791]—

- (a) in the heading, for “SE” substitute “ UK Societas ”;
- (b) for “an SE” substitute “ a UK Societas ”.
