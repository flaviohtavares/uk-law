# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 23

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23.[^c23533551]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 76 (enforcement of obligation to amend statutes in conflict with arrangements for employee involvement) [^c23528721]—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a), for “an SE” substitute “ a UK Societas ”;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (b), for “SE” substitute “ UK Societas ”;
- (b) in sub-paragraphs (2)(a)-(c), for “an SE”, in each place it occurs, substitute “ a UK Societas ”.
