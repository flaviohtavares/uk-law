# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 35

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35.[^c23988911]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 82 (notification of amendments to statutes and insolvency events (Articles 59(3) and 65)) [^c23528831]—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “SE” substitute “ UK Societas ”;
- (b) in paragraph (1A), for “SE's”, substitute “UK Societas's”;
- (c) in paragraph (2), for “SE” substitute “ UK Societas ”.
