# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 14

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14.[^c23533461]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 59 (power of the management or administrative organ of an SE to amend statutes where in conflict with employee involvement arrangements (Article 12(4)))—

- (a) in the heading, for “an SE” substitute “ a UK Societas ”;
- (b) for “SE” substitute “ UK Societas ”.
