# The European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 27

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27.[^c23533591]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 80 (particulars of members to be registered under regulation 79: individuals) [^c23528751]—

- (a) in paragraph (1), in the words before sub-paragraph (a), for “an SE's” substitute “a UK Societas's”;
- (b) in paragraph (5), for “The SE's” substitute “The UK Societas's”.
