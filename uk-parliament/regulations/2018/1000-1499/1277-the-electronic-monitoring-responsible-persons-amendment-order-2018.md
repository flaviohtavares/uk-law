# The Electronic Monitoring (Responsible Persons) (Amendment) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 1277

Criminal Law, England And Wales

The Electronic Monitoring (Responsible Persons) (Amendment) Order 2018

Made

26th November 2018

Laid before Parliament

3rd December 2018

Coming into force

10th January 2019

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 62(2B) and 76(3) of the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000[^f00001], makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Electronic Monitoring (Responsible Persons) (Amendment) Order 2018 and comes into force on 10th January 2019.

## 2. Amendment to the Electronic Monitoring (Responsible Persons) Order 2018

(1) Article 3(a) of the Electronic Monitoring (Responsible Persons) Order 2018[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) For sub-paragraph (ii), substitute—

> - (ii) Alcohol Monitoring Systems Limited, Lancashire Gate, 21 Tiviot Dale, Stockport, Cheshire, SK1 1TD (Company Number 07993509).
> - (iii) Buddi Limited, Talbot House, 17 Church Street, Rickmansworth, Hertfordshire, WD3 1DE (Company Number 05308826).

.

Rory Stewart

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

26th November 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Electronic Monitoring (Responsible Persons) Order 2018 ([S.I. 2018/212](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/212)) which lists the persons that may be made responsible for the monitoring of individuals under section 62 (Release on licence etc.: electronic monitoring conditions) of the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act [2000 (c. 43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/43), as required under section 62(2B) of that Act.

Article 2(a)(ii) of this Order amends the details of one of the existing persons listed, and Article 2(a)(iii) adds a further person that may be made responsible.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
