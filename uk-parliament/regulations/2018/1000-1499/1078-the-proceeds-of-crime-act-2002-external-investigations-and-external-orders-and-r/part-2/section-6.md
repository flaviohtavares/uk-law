# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations and External Orders and Requests) (Amendment) Order 2018, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Action on receipt of request in relation to an external investigation

In article 4(1) and (2) (action on receipt of request in relation to an external investigation), for “or a relevant Director” substitute “, a relevant Director, or (in England and Wales) the FCA, the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs or an enforcement authority”.
