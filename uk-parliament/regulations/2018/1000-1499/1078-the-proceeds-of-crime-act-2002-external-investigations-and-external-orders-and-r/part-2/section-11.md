# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations and External Orders and Requests) (Amendment) Order 2018, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Amendments consequential on article 10

(1) In article 12 (supplementary), in paragraph (4)—

- (a) for “an NCA officer” substitute “an officer of a description mentioned in paragraph (a), (b) or (c) of the meaning of appropriate officer (see article 2 (interpretation))”; and
- (b) for “need not be by the same NCA officer” substitute “may be made by a different officer of the same description”.

(2) In article 13 (search and seizure warrants), in paragraph (5), after “NCA officer” insert “, FCA officer (in England and Wales), officer of Revenue and Customs (in England and Wales)”.

(3) In article 14 (requirements where production order not available), in paragraph (8), after “NCA officer” insert “, FCA officer (in England and Wales), officer of Revenue and Customs (in England and Wales)”.

(4) In article 21 (supplementary), in paragraph (4)—

- (a) for “an NCA officer” substitute “an officer of a description mentioned in paragraph (a), (b) or (c) of the meaning of appropriate officer (see article 2 (interpretation))”; and
- (b) for “need not be by the same NCA officer” substitute “may be made by a different officer of the same description”.

(5) In article 28 (supplementary), in paragraph (4)—

- (a) for “an NCA officer” substitute “an officer of a description mentioned in paragraph (a), (b) or (c) of the meaning of appropriate officer (see article 2 (interpretation))”; and
- (b) for “need not be by the same NCA officer” substitute “may be made by a different officer of the same description”;

(6) In article 34 (supplementary), in paragraph (3)—

- (a) for “an NCA officer” substitute “an officer of a description mentioned in paragraph (a), (b) or (c) of the meaning of appropriate officer (see article 2 (interpretation))”; and
- (b) for “need not be by the same NCA officer” substitute “may be made by a different officer of the same description”.
