# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations and External Orders and Requests) (Amendment) Order 2018, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Extension of powers to the Serious Fraud Office: definition of “appropriate officer”

In article 55 (other interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “appropriate officer”—
  - (i) in paragraph (b), omit “or”; and
  - (ii) after paragraph (b), insert—

    > - (ba) an SFO officer; or

    ;
- (b) after the definition of “relevant seizure power”, insert—

  > “SFO officer” means a member of staff of the Serious Fraud Office.

  .
