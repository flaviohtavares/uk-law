# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations and External Orders and Requests) (Amendment) Order 2018, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Scope of the 2014 Order

In article 3 (scope of the Order)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), after “the Crown Court” insert “, or by enabling an enforcement authority to obtain unexplained wealth orders and interim freezing orders from the High Court in England and Wales.”;
- (b) omit paragraph (4).
