# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations and External Orders and Requests) (Amendment) Order 2018, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Action on receipt of request in relation to an external investigation

In article 4(1) and (2) (action on receipt of request in relation to an external investigation), after “Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs” in each place where it occurs insert “, or (in relation to unexplained wealth orders and interim freezing orders) an enforcement authority,”.
