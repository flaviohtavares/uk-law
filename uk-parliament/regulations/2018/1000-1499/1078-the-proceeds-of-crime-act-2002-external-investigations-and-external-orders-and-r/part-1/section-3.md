# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations and External Orders and Requests) (Amendment) Order 2018, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the 2005 Order” means the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005[^f00002];
- “the 2013 Order” means the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013[^f00003];
- “the 2014 Order” means the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2014[^f00004].
