# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations and External Orders and Requests) (Amendment) Order 2018, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations and External Orders and Requests) (Amendment) Order 2018.

(2) This Order comes into force on 12th November 2018 except for articles 27, 28, 30 and 31 which come into force on 12th November 2019.
