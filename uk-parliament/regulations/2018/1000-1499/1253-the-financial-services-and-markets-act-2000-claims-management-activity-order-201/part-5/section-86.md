# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Claims Management Activity) Order 2018, section 86

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 86. Interpretation[^key-69dd686a35c7d3d928278de0c7728a44][^key-261433b9a123fee15c94058a51923657]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Part—

- “the 2006 Act” means the Compensation Act 2006;
- “the 2012 Act” means the Financial Services Act 2012[^f00122];
- “the 2018 Act” means the Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018; and
- “the OLC”, “the FOS” and “the Regulator” have the meanings given in paragraph 2 of Schedule 4 to the 2018 Act.
