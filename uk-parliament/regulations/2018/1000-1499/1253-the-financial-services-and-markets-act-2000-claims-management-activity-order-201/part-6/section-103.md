# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Claims Management Activity) Order 2018, section 103

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 103. Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 (Referral Fees) Regulations 2013[^key-bc2143ff9e2fef038111f95ee5de9199][^key-a1286d2284bff66bb81068b500435a0a]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Schedule to the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 (Referral Fees) Regulations 2013[^f00143] (meaning of referral fees authorised person), in paragraph 3 insert at the end—

> - (h) a regulated claims management activity (within the meaning given by section 417(1) of the 2000 Act (definitions)).

.
