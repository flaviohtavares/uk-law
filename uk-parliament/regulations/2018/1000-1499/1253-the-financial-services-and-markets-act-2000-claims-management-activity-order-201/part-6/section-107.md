## 107. Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^key-b2206f72a09b00621979bb29ef573c57][^key-fbcd9443f46f7c5335de31d90819c190]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 58AA of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 has effect in relation to any damages-based agreement made before 1st April 2019 without the amendment made by article 90.
