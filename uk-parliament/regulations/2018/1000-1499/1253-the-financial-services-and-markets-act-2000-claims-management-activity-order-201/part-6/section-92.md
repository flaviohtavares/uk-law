> Text as at 2026-04-06.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 92. Enterprise Act 2002[^key-37b7dfd8b3755e5815d2fa912d11e476][^key-a98172e30a93748234e4fa62828926c7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In the Enterprise Act 2002[^f00129]—

- (a) in Schedule 14, omit “Compensation Act [2006 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/29).”[^f00130]; and
- (b) in Schedule 15 omit “Compensation Act [2006 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/29).”.
