# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Claims Management Activity) Order 2018, section 98

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 98. Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013[^c-0a247a639ba929ff9425eb8c4b63e199][^c-d3968fe8977817df91e0b255f39b4251]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013[^f00137] is amended as follows.

(2) Omit section 139 (power to impose penalties on persons providing claims management services).

(3) Omit section 140 (recovery of expenditure incurred by Office for Legal Complaints).

(4) In section 147(2) (extent) omit paragraphs (b) and (c).
