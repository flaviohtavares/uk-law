## 96. Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012[^c-8adbe942025ea6260a3442f24cc72087][^c-b5845d4cba8c45bc1841c0606e69b6de]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012[^f00135], in section 59 (regulators and regulated persons), in each table omit the entry relating to a person specified in the first column as “the Claims Management Regulator”.
