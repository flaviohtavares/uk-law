# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Claims Management Activity) Order 2018, section 112

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 112. Damages-Based Agreements Regulations 2013[^c-71e71f1fb2d039dd8176537164de56b3][^c-eab0e274f42d7a4ae2753de1ae49497f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Damages-Based Agreements Regulations 2013 continue to have effect in relation to any damages-based agreement made before 1st April 2019 without the amendment made by article 102.
