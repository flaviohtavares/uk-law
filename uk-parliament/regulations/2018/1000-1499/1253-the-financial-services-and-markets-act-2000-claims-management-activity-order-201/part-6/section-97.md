## 97. Crime and Courts Act 2013[^key-0c891f74ec28f3914931e87c45dc520e][^key-e7764c9e454d0adc8eb49aa47084ae0b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In Schedule 9 to the Crime and Courts Act 2013[^f00136] (single county court in England and Wales), in the list of provisions in paragraph 52(2) omit the entry “Compensation Act 2006: section 8(2),”.
