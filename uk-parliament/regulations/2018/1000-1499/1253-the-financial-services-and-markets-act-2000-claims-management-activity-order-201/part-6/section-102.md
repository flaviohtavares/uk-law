# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Claims Management Activity) Order 2018, section 102

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 102. Damages-Based Agreements Regulations 2013[^c-bafef334a8348927e9306c39da386ef9][^c-f3e1468b3ea1bcfefb0cdd836e7ace19]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Damages-Based Agreements Regulations 2013[^f00142], in regulation 1(2) (interpretation), in the definition of “client”, for “section 4(2)(b) of the Compensation Act 2006” substitute “section 419A of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
