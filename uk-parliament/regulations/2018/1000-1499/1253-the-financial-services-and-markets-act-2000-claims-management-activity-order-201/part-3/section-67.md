# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Claims Management Activity) Order 2018, section 67

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 67. Continuation of references made by the Regulator to the First-tier Tribunal[^c-64f50091eda418b37265848ada5e234e][^c-62706ca7e8d87e6016777c30df3386e8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This article applies to any reference of a complaint or question (“the relevant reference”) which—

- (a) was made by the Regulator to the First-tier Tribunal under section 13(2) of the 2006 Act; and
- (b) has not resulted, on or before 1st April 2019, in any decision or action taken by that tribunal under section 13(3) of that Act.

(2) The FCA may continue to refer the complaint or question which is the substance of the relevant reference, and is for that purpose substituted for the Regulator as a party to the reference.
