# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Claims Management Activity) Order 2018, section 78

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 78. Record-keeping and reporting requirements relating to relevant complaints[^c-6338171ac26c7558fbafd63eec3d82fc][^c-ec90d8ff0c66afe2197263885ae4cb22]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The FCA may make rules applying to 2000 Act authorised persons with respect to the keeping of records and the making of reports in relation to relevant complaints.
