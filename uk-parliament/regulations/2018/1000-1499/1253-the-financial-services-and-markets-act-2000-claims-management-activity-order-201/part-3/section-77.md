# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Claims Management Activity) Order 2018, section 77

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 77. Privilege[^c-503b778c530ba56ec940d9aa199c7798][^c-0c1b1747e9bd7334c35b10c80ba8d06c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Paragraph 11 of Schedule 17 to the 2000 Act[^f00113] (privilege) applies to proceedings in relation to a relevant complaint, and is to be read for those purposes as if the reference to the compulsory jurisdiction included a reference to the jurisdiction of the new scheme enacted by this Chapter.
