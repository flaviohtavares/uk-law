# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Claims Management Activity) Order 2018, section 65

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 65. Continuation of appeals commenced before 1st April 2019[^key-c8fea9e711266a0c18a655201de87cae][^key-c862461aafbef058325e85024cada95b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This article applies where, on or before 1st April 2019—

- (a) a person has submitted notice of an appeal to the First-tier Tribunal under section 13 of the 2006 Act (appeals and references to Tribunal) against a decision of the Regulator (“the relevant decision”); and
- (b) the appeal has not been determined.

(2) The appeal is to continue as if the FCA had made the relevant decision, and for that purpose the FCA is substituted for the Regulator as a party to the appeal.
