# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Claims Management Activity) Order 2018, section 59

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 59. Continuation of injunction applications made by the Regulator[^key-8fd9889a33833ce630518430f0f8a2f8][^key-f2101536519ccf342149893f97bd97df]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This article applies to an application for an injunction (“a relevant application”) which—

- (a) was made by the Regulator under section 8(1) of the 2006 Act)[^f00098]; and
- (b) on 1st April 2019, has not been determined by the court.

(2) The FCA may continue the relevant application and is for that purpose substituted for the Regulator as a party to that application.
