# The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variation) (No. 3) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 1220

Libraries

The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variation) (No. 3) Order 2018

Made

21st November 2018

Laid before Parliament

28th November 2018

Coming into force

20th December 2018

The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982[^f00001] (“the Scheme”) was brought into force on 14<sup>th</sup> June 1982;

The Scheme has been varied[^f00002];

The Secretary of State, after consultation with the representatives of authors and library authorities and of others who appear likely to be affected, has further varied the Scheme;

Accordingly the Secretary of State, in whom the power conferred by section 3(7) of the Public Lending Right Act 1979[^f00003] is now vested[^f00004], makes the following Order in exercise of that power:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variation) (No. 3) Order 2018.

## 2. Commencement of variation

The variation of the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 whereby article 46(1)(a) was varied by substituting “8.52p” for “8.20p”, comes into force on 20th December 2018.

Michael Ellis

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport

21st November 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 20th December 2018 a variation of the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (“the Scheme”) made by the Secretary of State.

The Scheme provides for payments to be made to authors based on the number of times their books are lent out by public libraries. The effect of the variation is to increase the rate per loan set out in article 46(1)(a) of the Scheme from 8.20p to 8.52p. The rate per loan is the sum attributable to each qualifying loan for the purpose of calculating the amount payable in respect of loans of a particular book by public libraries.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
