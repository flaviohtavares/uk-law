# The Air Quality (Miscellaneous Amendment and Revocation of Retained Direct EU Legislation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Revocations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 25(1)

### 1. Decisions[^c-c165be961bd001b0af1fa6c9af3f45a2]

Decision [2002/529/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2002/529) concerning a questionnaire for Member States reports on the implementation of Directive [1999/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1999/13) on the limitation of emissions of volatile organic compounds due to the use of organic solvents in certain activities and installations.

**2.**[^c-d75591ed9e5813c522d5d4aae7a9c78e] Decision [2006/61/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2006/61) on the conclusion, on behalf of the European Community, of the UN-ECE Protocol on Pollutant Release and Transfer Registers.

**3.**[^c-324176dfb44ecf3a939fee1f66312c55] Decision 2010/205/EU concerning the reporting questionnaire relating to Regulation [(EC) No. 166/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/166) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the establishment of a European Pollutant Release and Transfer Register.

**4.**[^c-6498e5dca6b51dc2e1af609f2475f2e8] Decision [2010/681/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2010/681) concerning a questionnaire for Member States reports on the implementation of Directive [1999/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1999/13) on the limitation of emissions of volatile organic compounds due to the use of organic solvents in certain activities and installations during the period 2011-2013.

**5.**[^c-c8e0a0b7c8ae60b01d91d3fc34103e1f] Decision 2010/693/EU establishing a common format for the second report of Member States on the implementation of Directive [2004/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/42) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the limitation of emissions of volatile organic compounds.

**6.**[^c-8cf26e0608ae636f812c9df34efb9d48] Decision 2011/C 146/03 establishing a forum for the exchange of information pursuant to Article 13 of the Directive 2010/75/EU on industrial emissions.

**7.**[^c-229a5d6131e7450145d8003157ff1f97] Decision 2012/119/EU laying down rules concerning guidance on the collection of data and on the drawing up of BAT reference documents and on their quality assurance referred to in Directive 2010/75/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on industrial emissions.

**8.**[^c-cdcf08707411304fd55beaf73552079d] Decision 2013/761/EU on the notification by the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland of a transitional national plan referred to in Article 32 of Directive 2010/75/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on industrial emissions.

**9.**[^c-cac43038473eadf3d94939e6dd949892] Decision C(2015) 9317 on the notification by the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland of a transitional national plan referred to in Article 32 of Directive 2010/75/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on industrial emissions.
