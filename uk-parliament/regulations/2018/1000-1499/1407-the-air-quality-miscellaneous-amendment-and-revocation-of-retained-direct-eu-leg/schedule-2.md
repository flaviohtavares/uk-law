# The Air Quality (Miscellaneous Amendment and Revocation of Retained Direct EU Legislation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Removal of references

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 25(2)

### 1. EEA agreement references[^key-ee933cad5735d05add26406f3c7458aa]

Decision 2010/205/EU concerning the reporting questionnaire relating to Regulation [(EC) No 166/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/166) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the establishment of a European Pollutant Release and Transfer Register.

**2.**[^key-f6ecab3bed9b5f74e3e7088f9ac9f840] Decision 2010/681/EU concerning a questionnaire for Member States reports on the implementation of Council Directive [1999/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1999/13) on the limitation of emissions of volatile organic compounds due to the use of organic solvents in certain activities and installations during the period 2011-2013.

**3.**[^key-658a13faa6224cded0f3677afd0605bd] Decision 2012/119/EU laying down rules concerning guidance on the collection of data and on the drawing up of BAT reference documents and on their quality assurance referred to in Directive 2010/75/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on industrial emissions.

**4.**[^key-d53214d1e3ea74a87195d11855d66a35] Decision 2012/795/EU establishing the type, format and frequency of information to be made available by Member States for the purposes of reporting on the implementation of Directive 2010/75/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on industrial emissions.
