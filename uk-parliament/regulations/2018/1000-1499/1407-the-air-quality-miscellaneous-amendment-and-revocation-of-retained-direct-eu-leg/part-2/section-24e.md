# The Air Quality (Miscellaneous Amendment and Revocation of Retained Direct EU Legislation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 24E

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24E. Decision (EU) 2020/2009 establishing the best available techniques (BAT) conclusions, under [Directive 2010/75/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2010/0075) on industrial emissions, for surface treatment using organic solvents including preservation of wood and wood products with chemicals[^M_I_961c2ccc-3419-4a0f-a771-4a3aa6a38994][^c-fad4a0f1f934fd08ca70ebcde83f280e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Decision (EU) 2020/2009 establishing the best available techniques (BAT) conclusions, under [Directive 2010/75/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2010/0075) on industrial emissions, for surface treatment using organic solvents including preservation of wood and wood products with chemicals is amended as follows.

(2) For Article 2 substitute—

> **Article 2 — Application and Interpretation**
>
> **1.** The BAT conclusions referred to in Article 1 apply in the United Kingdom.
>
> **2.** In this Decision—
>
> - “BAT conclusions” has the meaning given in Article 2 of [Decision 2012/134/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2012/0134) (“[Decision 2012/134/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2012/0134)”) establishing the best available techniques (BAT) conclusions under [Directive 2010/75/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2010/0075) of the European Parliament and of the Council on industrial emissions for the manufacture of glass;
> - “best available techniques” has the meaning given in Article 2 of [Decision 2012/134/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2012/0134).
>
> **3.** For the purpose of this Decision a reference to a provision of a Directive is to be read as a reference to that provision in so far as it has been transposed into the law of any part of the United Kingdom.

.
