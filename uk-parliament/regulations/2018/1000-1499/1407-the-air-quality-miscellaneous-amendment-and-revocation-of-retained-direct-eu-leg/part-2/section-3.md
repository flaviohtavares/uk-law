# The Air Quality (Miscellaneous Amendment and Revocation of Retained Direct EU Legislation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 3

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Decision [2004/279/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2004/279) concerning guidance for implementation of Directive [2002/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/3) of the European Parliament and of the Council relating to ozone in ambient air[^c-7341b557d093cf84e501505f74381aa5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Decision [2004/279/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2004/279) concerning guidance for implementation of Directive [2002/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/3) of the European Parliament and of the Council relating to ozone in ambient air is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) from the words “The guidance with” to “shall be as” substitute “Appropriate authorities must have regard to the guidance”;
  - (ii) after “this Decision” insert “ , in so far as relevant to the area in question ”;
- (b) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) for “Member States shall” substitute “ an appropriate authority must ”;
  - (ii) omit “in accordance with Article 7(3) of Directive [2002/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/3)”;
- (c) in paragraph 3—
  - (i) at the beginning insert “ When developing and implementing a short-term action plan, an appropriate authority must consider ”;
  - (ii) omit the words from “accordance with Article” to “set out in”.

(3) For Article 2, substitute—

> **2.**
>
> (1) In this Decision—
>
> - “**appropriate authority**” means—
>   - (a) in relation to England, the Secretary of State;
>   - (b) in relation to Wales, the Welsh Ministers;
>   - (c) in relation to Scotland, the Scottish Ministers;
>   - (d) in relation to Northern Ireland, the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs;
> - “**short-term action plan**” means—
>   - (a) in relation to England, a plan drawn up in accordance with regulation 27(3) of the Air Quality Standards Regulations 2010 [^c23497561];
>   - (b) in relation to Wales, a plan drawn up in accordance with regulation 21(3) of the Air Quality Standards (Wales) Regulations 2010 [^c23497571];
>   - (c) in relation to Scotland, a plan drawn up in accordance with regulation 25(3) of the Air Quality Standards (Scotland) Regulations 2010 [^c23497581];
>   - (d) in relation to Northern Ireland, a plan drawn up in accordance with regulation 26(3) of the Air Quality Standards Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 [^c23497591].

.

(4) In Annex 1—

- (a) in the heading—
  - (i) omit “BY MEMBER STATES”;
  - (ii) omit “IN ACCORDANCE WITH ARTICLE 7 OF DIRECTIVE [2002/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/3)”;
- (b) omit the first paragraph that begins “Article 7 of Directive”;
- (c) in the second paragraph, omit “With regard to EU long-term policy”;
- (d) in paragraph 3 (Short-term versus long-term measures), omit from “all over the EU” to the end of that sub-paragraph.

(5) In Annex 3—

- (a) in the heading, omit “ACCORDING TO ARTICLE 9(3) OF DIRECTIVE [2002/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/3)”;
- (b) in the first paragraph—
  - (i) for “Member States” substitute “ Appropriate authorities ”;
  - (ii) omit “by Article 9(3) of Directive [2002/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/3)”;
  - (iii) omit the second sentence;
  - (iv) in the third sentence—
    - (aa) omit “Annex VI of Directive [2002/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/3) further states that”;
    - (bb) omit “such”;
- (c) in point 1.1 (Recommendations for the location of the mandatory measuring station), for “Each Member State” substitute “ Appropriate authorities ”.
