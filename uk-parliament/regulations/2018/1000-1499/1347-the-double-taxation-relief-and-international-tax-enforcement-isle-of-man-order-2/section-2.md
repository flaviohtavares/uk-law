# The Double Taxation Relief and International Tax Enforcement (Isle of Man) Order 2018, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Double taxation and international tax arrangements to have effect

It is declared that—

- (a) the arrangements specified in the Schedule have been made with the Government of the Isle of Man—
  - (i) with a view to affording relief from double taxation in relation to capital gains tax, corporation tax and income tax and taxes of a similar character imposed by the Government of the Isle of Man, and
  - (ii) for the purpose of assisting international tax enforcement; and
- (b) it is expedient that those arrangements should have effect.
