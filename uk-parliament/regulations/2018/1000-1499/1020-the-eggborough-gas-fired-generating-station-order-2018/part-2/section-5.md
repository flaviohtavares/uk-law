# The Eggborough Gas Fired Generating Station Order 2018, section 5

> Text as at 2018-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Operation of authorised development

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker is hereby authorised to use and operate the generating station comprised in the authorised development.

(2) This article does not relieve the undertaker of any requirement to obtain any permit or licence or any obligation under any other legislation that may be required from time to time to authorise the operation of a generating station.
