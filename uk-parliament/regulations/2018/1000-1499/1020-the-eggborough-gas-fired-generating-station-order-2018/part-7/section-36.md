# The Eggborough Gas Fired Generating Station Order 2018, section 36

> Text as at 2018-12-07.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Operational land for purposes of the 1990 Act

*Extent: E+W*

Development consent granted by this Order is to be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)(a) of the 1990 Act (cases in which land is to be treated as operational land).
