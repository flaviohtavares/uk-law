# The Government of Wales Act 2006 (Variation of Borrowing Power) Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 1266

Constitutional Law

Devolution, Wales

Public Finance And Accountability

The Government of Wales Act 2006 (Variation of Borrowing Power) Order 2018

Made

29th November 2018

Coming into force

1st December 2018

The Secretary of State, with the consent of the Treasury, makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 121(4) of the Government of Wales Act 2006[^f00001].

In accordance with section 121(5) of that Act[^f00002] a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by a resolution of the House of Commons.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Government of Wales Act 2006 (Variation of Borrowing Power) Order 2018 and comes into force on 1st December 2018.

## 2. Amendment to section 121 of the Government of Wales Act 2006

In section 121(1A) of the Government of Wales Act 2006[^f00003], after the word “loan”, insert “or by the issue of bonds (but not bonds transferable by delivery)”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends section 121(1A) of the Government of Wales Act [2006 (c.32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/32). Section 121(1A) provides that the Welsh Ministers may, with the approval of the Treasury, borrow by way of loan sums required by them for the purpose of meeting capital expenditure. The Order provides that, in addition to being able to borrow by loan, the Welsh Ministers can also issue bonds (other than bonds transferable by delivery).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no negative impact on the costs of the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen. Further information is available from the Office of the Secretary of State for Wales, Gwydyr House, Whitehall, London, SW1A 2NP and on https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/office-of-the-secretary-of-state-for-wales. An Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside the instrument at www.legislation.gov.uk.
