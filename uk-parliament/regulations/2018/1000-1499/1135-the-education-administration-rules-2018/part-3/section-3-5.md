# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 3.5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.5. Filing of statement of affairs

(1) The education administrator must as soon as is reasonably practicable deliver to the registrar of companies a copy of—

- (a) any statement of affairs; and
- (b) any statement of concurrence.

(2) However, the education administrator must not deliver to the registrar of companies with the statement of affairs any schedule required by rule 3.3(5)(b).

(3) The requirement to deliver the statement of affairs is subject to any order of the court made under rule 3.31(2).
