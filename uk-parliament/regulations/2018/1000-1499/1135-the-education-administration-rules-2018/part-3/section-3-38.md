# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 3.38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.38. Application for an order ending education administration

An application to the court under paragraph 79 of Schedule B1 for an order ending an education administration must be accompanied by—

- (a) a progress report for the period since—
  - (i) the last progress report (if any), or
  - (ii) if there has been no previous progress report, the date on which the further education body entered education administration; and
- (b) a statement indicating what the education administrator thinks should be the next steps for the further education body (if applicable).
