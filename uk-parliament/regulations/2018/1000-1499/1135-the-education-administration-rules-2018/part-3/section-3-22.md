# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 3.22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.22. Chair of the meeting

The chair of a meeting of the members of the further education body must be either the education administrator or a person nominated by the education administrator to act as chair.
