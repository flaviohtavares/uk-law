# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 3.30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.30. Application of Chapter 7

This Chapter applies to the disclosure of information which would be likely to prejudice the conduct of the education administration or might reasonably be expected to lead to violence against any person.
