# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 3.26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.26. Application of this Chapter 6

This Chapter applies where the education administrator seeks a decision from the creditors of the further education body in accordance with paragraph 62 of Schedule B1.
