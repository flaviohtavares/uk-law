# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 3.17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.17. Application of this Chapter

This Chapter applies where the education administrator calls a meeting of members of the further education body under paragraph 62 of Schedule B1[^f00019].

## Annotations

[^f00019]: Footnote: Paragraph 62 was amended by paragraph 10 of Schedule 9 to the Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015.
