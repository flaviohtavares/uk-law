# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 3.15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.15. Final progress report

“Final progress report” means a progress report which must contain an account of the education administrator’s administration including—

- (a) a summary of the education administrator’s proposals;
- (b) any major amendments to, or deviations from, those proposals;
- (c) the steps taken during the education administration;
- (d) a statement as to the amount paid to unsecured creditors by virtue of section 176A of the Act; and
- (e) the outcome of the education administration.
