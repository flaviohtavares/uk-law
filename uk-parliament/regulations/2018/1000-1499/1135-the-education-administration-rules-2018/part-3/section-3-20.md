# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 3.20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.20. Remote attendance: notification requirements

When a meeting is to be summoned and held in accordance with section 246A(3) of the Act, the education administrator must notify all those to whom notice of the meeting is being given of—

- (a) the ability of a person claiming to be an excluded person to request an indication in accordance with rule 3.24;
- (b) the ability of a person within rule 3.25(1) to make a complaint in accordance with that rule; and
- (c) the period within which such a request or complaint must be made.
