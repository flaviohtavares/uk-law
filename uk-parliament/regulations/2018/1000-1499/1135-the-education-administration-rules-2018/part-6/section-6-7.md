# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 6.7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.7. Application to replace

(1) Where an application to court is made under paragraph 91(1) of Schedule B1 to appoint a replacement education administrator, the application must be accompanied by the proposed replacement education administrator’s consent to act.

(2) A copy of the application must be delivered to—

- (a) whichever of the appropriate national authority or the education administrator is not the applicant; and
- (b) to those persons set out at rule 2.5(3).

(3) Rules 2.9, 2.10 and 2.11 apply to an application made under paragraph 91(1) of Schedule B1 as they apply to an application for an education administration order.
