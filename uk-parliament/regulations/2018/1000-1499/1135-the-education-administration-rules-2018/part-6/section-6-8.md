# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 6.8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.8. Appointment of a replacement or additional education administrator

Where a replacement education administrator is appointed or an additional education administrator is appointed to act jointly or concurrently—

- (a) rule 3.1 applies;
- (b) all documents must clearly identify the appointment as of a replacement education administrator or an additional education administrator appointed to act jointly or concurrently.
