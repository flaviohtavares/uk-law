# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.34. Charges for copies of documents provided by the education administrator

Except where prohibited by these Rules, an education administrator is entitled to require the payment of the standard fee for copies of documents requested by a creditor, member or contributory.
