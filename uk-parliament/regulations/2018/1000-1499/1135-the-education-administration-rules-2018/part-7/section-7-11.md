# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.11. Standard contents of documents relating to other documents

A document relating to another document must also state—

- (a) the nature of the other document;
- (b) the date of the other document; and
- (c) where the other document relates to a period of time, the period of time to which it relates.
