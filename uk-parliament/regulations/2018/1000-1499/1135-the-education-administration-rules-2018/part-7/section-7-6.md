# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.6. Variations from prescribed contents

Where a rule sets out the required contents of a document, the document may depart from the required contents if—

- (a) the circumstances require such a departure (including where the requirement is not applicable in the particular case); or
- (b) the departure (whether or not intentional) is immaterial.
