# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.21. Application of Chapter

(1) This Chapter applies where a document is required under the Act or these Rules to be delivered, filed, forwarded, furnished, given, sent or submitted in respect of education administration proceedings unless the Act, a rule or an order of the court makes different provision, including one requiring service of the document.

(2) However, in respect of delivery of a document to the registrar of companies—

- (a) subject to sub-paragraph (b) only the following rules in this Chapter apply: rules 7.24, 7.25, 7.26 and 7.28; and
- (b) where a document is to be delivered to the registrar of companies, the registrar’s rules under section 1117 of the Companies Act 2006 apply to determine the date when any document is received by the registrar of companies.
