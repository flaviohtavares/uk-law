# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.26. Personal delivery of documents

A document is delivered if it is personally delivered in accordance with the rules for personal service in CPR Part 6.
