# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.12. Standard contents of documents relating to court orders

A document relating to a court order must also specify—

- (a) the nature of the order; and
- (b) the date of the order.
