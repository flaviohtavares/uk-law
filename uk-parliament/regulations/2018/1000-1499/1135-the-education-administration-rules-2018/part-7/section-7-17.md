# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.17. Standard contents of notices relating to court proceedings or orders

A notice relating to court proceedings must also identify those proceedings and if the notice relates to a court order state—

- (a) the nature of the order; and
- (b) the date of the order.
