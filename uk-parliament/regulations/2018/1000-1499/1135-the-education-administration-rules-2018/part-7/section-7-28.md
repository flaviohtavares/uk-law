# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.28. Electronic delivery of notices to enforcement officers

Where anything in the Act or these Rules provides for the delivery of a notice to an enforcement officer or enforcement agent, it may be delivered by electronic means to a person who has been authorised to receive such a notice on behalf of a specified enforcement officer or enforcement agent or on behalf of enforcement officers or enforcement agents generally.
