# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.22. Delivery of documents to authorised recipients

Where under the Act or these Rules a document is to be delivered to a person (other than being served on that person), it may be delivered instead to any other person authorised in writing to accept delivery on behalf of the first-mentioned person.
