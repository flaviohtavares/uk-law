# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.7. Standard contents for documents delivered to the registrar of companies

(1) Where the Act or these Rules require a document to be delivered to the registrar of companies the document must contain the standard contents set out in this Chapter (in addition to any content specifically required by the Act or any other provision of these Rules).

(2) A document of more than one type must satisfy the requirements which apply to each.

(3) However requirements as to the contents of a document which is to be delivered to another person at the same time as the registrar of companies may be satisfied by delivering to that other person a copy of the document delivered to the registrar of companies.
