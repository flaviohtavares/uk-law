# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.46. Non-Gazette notices relating to a further education body

A notice relating to a further education body must also identify the further education body and state—

- (a) its registered office of the further education body;
- (b) any name under which it was registered in the 12 months before the date of the education administration proceedings; and
- (c) any name or style under which—
  - (i) the further education body carried on business, and
  - (ii) any debt owed to a creditor was incurred.
