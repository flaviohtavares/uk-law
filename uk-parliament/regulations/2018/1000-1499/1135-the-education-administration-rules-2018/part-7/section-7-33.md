# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.33. Right to copy documents

Where the Act or these Rules give a person the right to inspect documents, that person has a right to be supplied on request with copies of those documents on payment of the standard fee for copies.
