# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 7.23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7.23. Delivery of documents to joint education administrators

Where joint education administrators have been appointed, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to all of them.
