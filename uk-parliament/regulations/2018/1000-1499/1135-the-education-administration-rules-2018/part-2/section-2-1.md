# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 2.1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.1. Commencement of education administration proceedings

An application[^f00015] for an education administration order must be commenced in the High Court.

## Annotations

[^f00015]: Footnote: Section 18 of the Technical and Further Education Act provides that an education administration order may only be made on the application of the appropriate national authority (the Secretary of State or the Welsh Ministers).
