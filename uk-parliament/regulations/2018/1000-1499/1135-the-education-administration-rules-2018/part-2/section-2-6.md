# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 2.6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.6. Notice to enforcement agents charged with distress or other legal process, etc.

The applicant must as soon as reasonably practicable after filing the application deliver a notice of its being made to—

- (a) any enforcement agent or other officer who to the knowledge of the applicant is charged with distress or other legal process against the further education body or its property; and
- (b) any person who to the knowledge of the applicant has distrained against the further education body or its property.
