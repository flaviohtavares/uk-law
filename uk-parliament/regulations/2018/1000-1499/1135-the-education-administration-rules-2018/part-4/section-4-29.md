# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.29. Procedure where detailed assessment is required

(1) A person whose costs in education administration proceedings are required to be decided by detailed assessment must, on receipt of a notice given by the education administrator under rule 4.28(2), commence detailed assessment proceedings in accordance with CPR Part 47.

(2) If that person does not commence such proceedings within 3 months of being required to do so under rule 4.28(2), or within such further time as the court, on application, may permit, the education administrator may deal with the administration without regard to any claim for costs by that person, whose claim is forfeited by such failure to commence proceedings.

(3) Where in any such case such a claim for costs lies additionally against an education administrator in their personal capacity, that claim is also forfeited by such failure to commence proceedings.
