# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.35. Orders enforcing compliance

(1) The court may, on an application by the education administrator, make such orders as it thinks necessary for the enforcement of obligations falling on any person in accordance with—

- (a) paragraph 47 of Schedule B1; or
- (b) section 235[^f00024] of the Act.

(2) An order of the court under this rule may provide that all the costs of and incidental to the application for it are to be borne by the person against whom the order is made.

## Annotations

[^f00024]: Footnote: Section 235 was amended by paragraph 24 of Schedule 17 to the Enterprise Act 2002.
