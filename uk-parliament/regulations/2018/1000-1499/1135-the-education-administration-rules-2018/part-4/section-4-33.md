# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.33. Costs and expenses of witnesses

(1) Except as directed by the court no allowance as a witness in any examination or other proceedings before the court may be made to a relevant officer of the further education body to which the proceedings relate.

(2) A person making any application in education administration proceedings is not to be regarded as a witness on the hearing of the application, but a costs officer may allow their expenses of travelling and subsistence.
