# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.1. Court rules and practice to apply

(1) The provisions of the CPR (including any related Practice Directions) apply for the purposes of education administration with any necessary modifications, except so far as disapplied by or inconsistent with these Rules.

(2) Education administration proceedings must be allocated to the multi-track, for which CPR Part 29 makes provision, and accordingly those provisions of the CPR which provide for directions questionnaires and track allocation do not apply.

(3) CPR Part 32 applies to a false statement in a document verified by a statement of truth made under these Rules as it applies to a false statement in a document verified by a statement of truth made under CPR Part 22.
