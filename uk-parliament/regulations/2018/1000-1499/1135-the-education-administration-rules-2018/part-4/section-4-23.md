# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.23. Applications for transfer

Where an application to transfer the proceedings is made by the education administrator, that application must be accompanied by a report by the education administrator which must—

- (a) set out the reasons for the transfer; and
- (b) include a statement that the appropriate national authority either has consented to the transfer or was given at least 14 days’ notice of the education administrator’s application.
