# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.38. Appeals and reviews of in education administration proceedings

(1) A court which has jurisdiction in relation to education administration proceedings may review, rescind or vary any order made by it in the exercise of that jurisdiction.

(2) An appeal made in the exercise of the court’s jurisdiction in relation to education administration proceedings lies—

- (a) from the decision of an Insolvency and Companies Court Judge sitting in the High Court, to a High Court Judge; and
- (b) from a decision of a High Court Judge, to the Civil Division of the Court of Appeal.

(3) Any application for the rescission of a winding-up order must be made within five business days after the date on which the order was made.

(4) In this rule—

- “Civil Division of the Court of Appeal” means the division of the Court of Appeal established by section 3(1) of the Senior Courts Act 1981[^f00026];
- “High Court Judge” means a judge listed in section 4(1) of the Senior Courts Act 1981[^f00027]; and
- “Insolvency and Companies Court Judge of the High Court” means a person appointed to the office of Insolvency and Companies Court Judge under section 89(1) of the Senior Courts Act 1981[^f00028].
