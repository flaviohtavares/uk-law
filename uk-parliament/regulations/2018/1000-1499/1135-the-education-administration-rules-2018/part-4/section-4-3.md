# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.3. Preliminary

This Chapter applies to all applications to a court made within education administration proceedings except an application for an education administration order.
