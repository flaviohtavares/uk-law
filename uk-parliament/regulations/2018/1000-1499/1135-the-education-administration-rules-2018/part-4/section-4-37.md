# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.37. Application of CPR

CPR Part 52[^f00025] applies to appeals under this Chapter as varied by any applicable Practice Direction.
