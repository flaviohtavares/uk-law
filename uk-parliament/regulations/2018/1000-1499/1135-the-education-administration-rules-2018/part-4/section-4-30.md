# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.30. Costs paid otherwise than out of the assets of the further education body

Where the amount of costs is decided by detailed assessment under an order of the court directing that those costs are to be paid otherwise than out of the assets of the further education body, the costs officer must note on the final costs certificate by whom, or the manner in which, the costs are to be paid.
