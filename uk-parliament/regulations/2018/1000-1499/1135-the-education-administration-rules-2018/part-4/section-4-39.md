# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.39. Procedure on appeal

(1) An appeal against a decision of a judge of the High Court may be brought only with the permission of the High Court or the Court of Appeal.

(2) An appellant must file an appeal notice[^f00029] within 21 days after the date of the decision of the court that the appellant wishes to appeal.

## Annotations

[^f00029]: Footnote: “Appeal notice” is defined in CPR 52.1(3)(f).
