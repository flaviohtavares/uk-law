# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.12. Application under section 176A(5) to disapply section 176A

(1) An application under section 176A(5) of the Act must be accompanied by a witness statement of the education administrator.

(2) The witness statement must—

- (a) state that the application arises in education administration proceedings;
- (b) contain a summary of the financial position of the further education body; and
- (c) contain the information substantiating the education administrator’s view that the cost of making a distribution to unsecured creditors would be disproportionate to the benefits.
