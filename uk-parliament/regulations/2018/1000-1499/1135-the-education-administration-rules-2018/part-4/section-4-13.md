# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.13. Notice of an application under section 176A(5)

An application under section 176A(5) may be made without the application being served upon, or notification to, any other party.
