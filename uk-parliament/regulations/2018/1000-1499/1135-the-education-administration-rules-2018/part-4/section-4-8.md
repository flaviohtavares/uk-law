# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.8. Hearing in urgent case

(1) Where the case is urgent, the court may (without prejudice to its general power to extend or abridge time limits) hear the application immediately with or without notification to, or the attendance of, the other parties.

(2) The application may be heard on such terms providing for the filing or service of documents, notification, or the carrying out of other formalities as the court thinks just.
