# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 4.34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.34. Final costs certificate

(1) A final costs certificate of the costs officer is final and conclusive as to all matters which have not been objected to in the manner provided for under the rules of the court.

(2) Where it is demonstrated to the satisfaction of a costs officer that a final costs certificate has been lost or destroyed, the costs officer may issue a duplicate.
