# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 5.10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.10. Withdrawal or variation of proof

(1) A creditor may withdraw a proof at any time by delivering a written notice to the education administrator.

(2) The amount claimed by a creditor’s proof may be varied at any time by agreement between the creditor and the education administrator.
