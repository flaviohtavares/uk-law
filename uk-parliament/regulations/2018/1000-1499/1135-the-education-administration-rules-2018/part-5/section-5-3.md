# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 5.3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.3. Proving a debt

(1) A creditor wishing to recover a debt must submit a proof to the education administrator unless this rule or an order of the court provides otherwise.

(2) A creditor is deemed to have proved for the purposes of determination and payment of a dividend but not otherwise where—

- (a) the debt is a small debt;
- (b) a notice has been delivered to the creditor of notice of intention to declare a dividend or make a distribution under rule 5.26 which complies with rule 5.27 and 5.28; and
- (c) the creditor has not advised the education administrator that the debt is incorrect or not owed in response to the notice.
