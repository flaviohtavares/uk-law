# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 5.25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.25. Application of Chapter to particular class of creditors and to distributions

(1) This Chapter applies where the education administrator makes, or proposes to make, a distribution to any class of creditors other than secured creditors.

(2) Where the distribution is to a particular class of creditors in an education administration, a reference in this Chapter to creditors is a reference to that class of creditors only.
