# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 5.12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.12. Debts of further education bodies to rank equally

Debts of further education bodies other than preferential debts rank equally between themselves and, after preferential debts, must be paid in full unless the assets are insufficient for meeting them, in which case they abate in equal proportions between themselves.
