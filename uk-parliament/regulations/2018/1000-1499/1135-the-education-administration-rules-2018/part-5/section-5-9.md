# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 5.9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.9. Education administrator not liable for costs under rule 5.8

The education administrator is not personally liable for costs incurred by any person in respect of an application under rule 5.8 unless the court otherwise orders.
