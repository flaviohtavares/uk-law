# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 5.36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.36. Provisions as to dividends

In the calculation and distribution of a dividend, the education administrator must make provision for—

- (a) any debts which are the subject of claims which have not yet been determined; and
- (b) any disputed proofs and claims.
