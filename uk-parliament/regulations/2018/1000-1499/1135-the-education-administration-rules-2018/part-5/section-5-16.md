# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 5.16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.16. Secured creditor: surrender for non-disclosure

(1) If a secured creditor fails to disclose a security in a proof, the secured creditor must surrender that security for the general benefit of creditors, unless the court, on application by the secured creditor, relieves the secured creditor from the effect of this rule on the ground that the omission was inadvertent or the result of honest mistake.

(2) If the court grants that relief, it may require or allow the creditor’s proof to be amended, on such terms as may be just.
