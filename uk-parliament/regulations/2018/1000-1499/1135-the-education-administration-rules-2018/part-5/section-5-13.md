# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 5.13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.13. Division of unsold assets

(1) This rule applies to any property which, from its peculiar nature or other special circumstances, cannot be readily or advantageously sold.

(2) The education administrator may with agreement of the creditors divide the property in its existing form among the further education body’s creditors according to its estimated value.
