# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 5.43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.43. Reporting distribution of property to creditors under rule 5.13

(1) This rule applies where there has been a distribution of property to creditors under rule 5.13.

(2) In any account or summary of receipts and payments which is required to be included in a notice or report prepared under a rule listed in paragraph (3), the education administrator must—

- (a) state the estimated value of the property divided among the creditors of the further education body during the period to which the account or summary relates; and
- (b) provide details of the basis of the valuation as a note to the account or summary of receipts and payments.

(3) Paragraph (2) applies to the following—

- (a) rule 3.13 (progress reports); and
- (b) rule 6.2 (notice of intention to resign).
