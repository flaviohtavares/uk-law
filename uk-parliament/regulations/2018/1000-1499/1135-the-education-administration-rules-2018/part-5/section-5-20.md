# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 5.20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.20. Discounts

All trade and other discounts (except a discount for immediate or early settlement) which would have been available to the further education body or the debtor but for the education administration proceedings must be deducted from the claim.
