# The Education Administration Rules 2018, section 5.7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.7. Admission and rejection of proofs for dividend

(1) The education administrator may admit or reject a proof for dividend (in whole or in part).

(2) If the education administrator rejects a proof in whole or in part, the education administrator must deliver to the creditor a statement of the reasons for doing so, as soon as reasonably practicable.
