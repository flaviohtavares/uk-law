# The Pensions Act 2004 (Code of Practice) (Authorisation and Supervision of Master Trusts) Appointed Day Order 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 1097

Pensions

The Pensions Act 2004 (Code of Practice) (Authorisation and Supervision of Master Trusts) Appointed Day Order 2018

Made

17th October 2018

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 91(9) of the Pensions Act 2004[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Act 2004 (Code of Practice) (Authorisation and Supervision of Master Trusts) Appointed Day Order 2018.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into effect of the Pensions Regulator’s Code of Practice No. 15: Authorisation and supervision of master trusts is 18th October 2018.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Guy Opperman

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Work and Pensions

17th October 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 18th October 2018 as the day for the coming into effect of the Pensions Regulator’s Code of Practice No. 15: Authorisation and supervision of master trusts.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no direct impact on business, the public sector or civil society organisations.
