# The Human Fertilisation and Embryology (Parental Orders) Regulations 2018, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2018-12-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1412
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Revocation and savings[^key-edfdd17a53cdfe21cdf3e8cef5e62ed5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Human Fertilisation and Embryology (Parental Orders) Regulations 2010[^f00007] (“the 2010 Regulations”) are revoked.

(2) But the 2010 Regulations still apply to—

- (a) an application for a parental order made under section 54 of the 2008 Act made before the coming into force of these Regulations; and
- (b) an order made under section 30 of the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 1990[^f00008].
