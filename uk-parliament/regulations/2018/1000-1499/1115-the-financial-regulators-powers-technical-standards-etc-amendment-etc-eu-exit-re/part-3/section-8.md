# The Financial Regulators' Powers (Technical Standards etc.) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 8

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Amendment of the Banking Act 2009

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 244 (Immunity) of the Banking Act 2009 [^c23418661], in subsection (2)(c), after “Markets Act 2000” insert “ , of its functions under or as a result of regulations made under section 8 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 ”.
