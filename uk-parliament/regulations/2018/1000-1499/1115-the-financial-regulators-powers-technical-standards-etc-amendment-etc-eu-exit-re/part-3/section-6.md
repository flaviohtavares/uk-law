# The Financial Regulators' Powers (Technical Standards etc.) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2018/1115
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Amendment of the Bank of England Act 1998

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 6A to the Bank of England Act 1998 [^c23418581], in paragraph 17(9)—

- (a) after paragraph (f), insert—

  > - (fa) making technical standards in accordance with Chapter 2A of Part 9A of that Act [^c23418591];
- (b) after paragraph (g), insert—

  > - (h) making EU Exit instruments under the Financial Regulators' Powers (Technical Standards) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018.

  .

## Annotations

[^c23418581]: Marginal citation: [1998 c.11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/11). Schedule 6A was inserted by the [Bank of England and Financial Services Act 2016 (c.14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/14).

[^c23418591]: Marginal citation: Chapter 2A is inserted into Part 9A of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 by regulation 7(8) of these Regulations.
