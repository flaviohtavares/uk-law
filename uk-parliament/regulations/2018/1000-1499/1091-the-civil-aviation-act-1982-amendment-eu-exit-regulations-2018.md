# The Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 1091

Exiting The European Union

Civil Aviation

The Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018

Sift requirements satisfied

5th September 2018

Made

15th October 2018

Laid before Parliament

18th October 2018

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The requirements of paragraph 17(2) of Schedule 7 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001] (relating to the appropriate Parliamentary procedure for these Regulations) have been satisfied.

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 23(1) and (2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018 and they come into force on exit day.

## 2. Amendment of the Civil Aviation Act 1982

In section 105(1) (general interpretation) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00002], in the definition of “enactment”, after “includes” insert “any retained direct EU legislation and”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers in section 23(1) and (2) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) (“the 2018 Act”).

These Regulations amend the definition of “enactment” in section 105(1) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982 (“the 1982 Act”) to include retained direct EU legislation, in line with amendments made by the 2018 Act to the definition of “enactment” in Schedule 1 to the Interpretation Act [1978 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/30). The 2018 Act also adds a definition of “retained direct EU legislation” into that Schedule.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
