*(This note is not part of the Order)*

This Order amends legislative references in the Air Navigation (Single European Sky) (Penalties) Order 2009 (S.I. 2009/1735) that are out of date.

References to Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1794/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1794) (“**the 2006 Regulation**”) are replaced by references to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 391/2013 (“**the 2013 Regulation**”) which supersedes it. Articles 4 and 5 of this Order replace the articles referenced in the 2006 Regulation with the corresponding article references in the 2013 Regulation which supersede them.

A reference to the Air Navigation Order 2005 (S.I. 2005/1970) is replaced by a reference to the Air Navigation Order 2016 (S.I. 2016/765) which supersedes it.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no significant impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is forseen.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been prepared for this instrument and is available alongside this instrument on the UK Legislation website www.legislation.gov.uk.
