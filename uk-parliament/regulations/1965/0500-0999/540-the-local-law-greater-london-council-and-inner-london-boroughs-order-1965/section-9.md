# The Local Law (Greater London Council and Inner London Boroughs) Order 1965, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**9.** Any byelaws made before 1st April 1965 under an enactment specified in Schedule 1 or 2 and in force in an area comprised in an inner London borough shall continue to have effect in such area as if made by the authority substituted in references by that schedule and by article 4 of this order and references in such byelaws to any authority shall in such application be construed as references to the authority so substituted.
