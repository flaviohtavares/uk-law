# The Local Law (Greater London Council and Inner London Boroughs) Order 1965, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**4.** (1) Save as otherwise expressly provided in Schedule 1 or unless the context otherwise requires, in the enactments specified in Schedule 2 there shall be substituted for any reference to an area, authority or body described in the first column of the following Table a reference to the area, authority or body described in respect thereof in the second column.

TABLE

| Existing area, authority or body | Substituted area, authority or body |
| --- | --- |
| The administrative county of London | Greater London |
| The county of London | Greater London except the City of London, the Inner Temple and the Middle Temple |
| The London County Council<br>The Metropolitan Board of Works | The Greater London Council |
| A metropolitan borough | Each inner London borough which includes the area of the metropolitan borough or any part thereof |
| The council of a metropolitan borough | The council or corporation as may be appropriate of each inner London borough which includes the area of the metropolitan borough or any part thereof |
| A gas company or undertaking | The Area Board within the meaning of the Gas Act 1948 for the supply of gas to the area |
| An electricity company or undertaking | An Electricity Board within the meaning of the Electricity Act 1947 as amended by the Electricity Act 1957 |
| A company or undertaking supplying water in an existing area | A company or undertaking supplying water in the substituted area |
| A railway company or undertaking; or the London Passenger Transport Board or any company or undertaking which was or whose functions were transferred to or vested in that Board by the London Passenger Transport Act 1933; or the British Transport Commission or any company or undertaking other than the London Passenger Transport Board which was or whose functions were transferred to or vested in the British Transport Commission by or under the Transport Act 1947 | The appropriate Board to or in whom the property, rights, liabilities and functions of the British Transport Commission are transferred or vested by or under the Transport Act 1962 |
| A company whose undertaking was transferred to or vested in the Port of London Authority by the Port of London Act 1908; or the Conservators of the River Thames in respect of the River Thames below the landward limit of the Port of London | The Port of London Authority |

(2) Save as otherwise expressly provided in Schedule 1 or unless the context otherwise requires, any functions exercisable in an area mentioned in the first column of the Table set out in paragraph (1) of this article shall be exercisable throughout the area specified in respect thereof in the second column.
