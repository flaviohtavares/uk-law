# The Gambia Independence Order 1965, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Emergency Powers Orders in Council 1939 to 1964

The Emergency Powers Orders in Council 1939[^f00003] to 1964[^f00004] shall cease to have effect as part of the law of The Gambia on 18th August 1966 or such earlier date as Parliament may prescribe:

Provided that sections 6(2)(d) and 8 of the Emergency Powers Order in Council 1939 shall cease to have effect as part of the law of The Gambia as from the commencement of this Order.
