# The Gambia Independence Order 1965, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Establishment of Constitution

Subject to the provisions of this Order, the Constitution set out in Schedule 3 to this Order (hereinafter referred to as “**the Constitution**”) shall come into effect in The Gambia at the commencement of this Order.
