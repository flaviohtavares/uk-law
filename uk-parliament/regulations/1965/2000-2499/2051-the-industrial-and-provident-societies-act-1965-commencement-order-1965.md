# The Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965 (Commencement) Order 1965

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1965 No. 2051 (C. 23)

INDUSTRIAL AND PROVIDENT SOCIETIES

The Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965 (Commencement) Order 1965

Made

3rd December 1965

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 3rd day of December 1965

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas by subsection (3) of section 78 of the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965, it is provided that that Act shall come into operation on such day as Her Majesty may by Order in Council appoint:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in pursuance of the said subsection (3), is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** The Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965 shall come into operation on 1st January 1966.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965 (Commencement) Order 1965.

W. G. Agnew
