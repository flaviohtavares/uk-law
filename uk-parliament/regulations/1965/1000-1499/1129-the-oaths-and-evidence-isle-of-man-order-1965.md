# The Oaths and Evidence (Isle of Man) Order 1965

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1965 No. 1129

OATHS

The Oaths and Evidence (Isle of Man) Order 1965

Made

14th May 1965

Coming into Operation

1st June 1965

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of May 1965

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of the power conferred upon Her by section 7(3) of the Oaths and Evidence (Overseas Authorities and Countries) Act 1963, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to other, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** Sections 1, 2, 4 and 6 of the Oaths and Evidence (Overseas Authorities and Countries) Act 1963 shall extend to the Isle of Man subject to the modifications specified in the Schedule to this Order.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the Oaths and Evidence (Isle of Man) Order 1965 and shall come into operation on 1st June 1965.

W. G. Agnew

## SCHEDULE — MODIFICATIONS IN THE APPLICATION OF THE OATHS AND EVIDENCE (OVERSEAS AUTHORITIES AND COUNTRIES) ACT 1963 TO THE ISLE OF MAN

**1.** In section 1 for the words “United Kingdom” there shall be substituted the words “Isle of Man”.

**2.** For section 4(2) there shall be substituted the following subsection:—

> (2) Where the said Act of 1856 is so extended, section 1(4) of the Perjury Act 1952 (an Act of Tynwald) shall apply accordingly.

.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order extends to the Isle of Man certain provisions of the Oaths and Evidence (Overseas Authorities and Countries) Act 1963 which provide for the administration of oaths, the doing of notarial acts and the taking of evidence for the purpose of international tribunals.
