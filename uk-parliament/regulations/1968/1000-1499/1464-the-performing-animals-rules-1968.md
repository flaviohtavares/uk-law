# The Performing Animals Rules 1968

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1968 No. 1464

ANIMALS

The Performing Animals Rules 1968

Made

11th September 1968

Coming into Operation

1st October 1968

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (2) of section 5 of the Performing Animals (Regulation) Act 1925, and by subsection (3) of that section as amended by the Miscellaneous Fees (Variation) Order 1968[^f00001] and the Miscellaneous Fees (Variation) (Scotland) Order 1968[^f00002], I hereby make the following Rules:—

## Fees (section 1(2))

**1.** For Rule 9 of the Performing Animals Rules 1925[^f00003] there shall be substituted the following Rule:—

> **9.** The following fees shall be payable to the local authority:—
>
> For each registration\&dotfill;£3.
>
> For the issue of a copy of a certificate of registration under Rule 7\&dotfill;5s.

.

**2.** These Rules may be cited as the Performing Animals Rules 1968 and shall come into operation on 1st October 1968.

James Callaghan

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office,Whitehall

11th September 1968

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules raise the fee for the registration of exhibitors and trainers of performing animals from one guinea to £3 and abolish the shilling fee for inspections of registers and copies of certificates of registration.
