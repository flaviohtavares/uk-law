# The Children and Young Persons Act 1963 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1968

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1968 No. 1727 (C. 20)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Children and Young Persons Act 1963 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1968

Made

30th October 1968

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 65(6) of the Children and Young Persons Act 1963, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** The remaining provisions of the Children and Young Persons Act 1963, which have not previously come into operation and are specified in the Schedule hereto (which relate to the employment of children and young persons) shall come into operation on 3rd March 1969 except that for the purposes of applying for licences under section 37 of the Act, and granting such licences to have effect on and after the said date, these provisions shall come into operation on 2nd December 1968.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the Children and Young Persons Act 1963 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1968.

James Callaghan

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

Whitehall

30th October 1968

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force the remaining provisions of the Children and Young Persons Act 1963; the provisions brought into force are set out in the Schedule. These provisions will come into force on 3rd March 1969 except that for the purposes of applying for licences under section 37 and granting such licences to have effect on and after 3rd March 1969, these provisions will come into force on 2nd December 1968.
