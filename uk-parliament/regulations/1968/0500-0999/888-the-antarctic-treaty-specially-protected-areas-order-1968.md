# The Antarctic Treaty (Specially Protected Areas) Order 1968

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1968 No. 888

ANTARCTICA

The Antarctic Treaty (Specially Protected Areas) Order 1968

Made

7th June 1968

Laid before Parliament

13th June 1968

Coming into Operation

1st July 1968

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 7th day of June 1968

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 7(2)(b) of the Antarctic Treaty Act 1967. is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** For the purposes of the Antarctic Treaty Act 1967, the areas specified in the Schedule to this Order, being certain of the areas listed in Annex B to Schedule 2 to the said Act, are designated as Specially Protected Areas.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the Antarctic Treaty (Specially Protected Areas) Order 1968 and shall come into operation on 1st July 1968.

W. G. Agnew

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates as Specially Protected Areas for the purposes of the Antarctic Treaty Act 1967 certain of the areas in the Antarctic which have been recommended for inclusion in Annex B to the Agreed Measures for the Conservation of Antarctic Fauna and Flora (Schedule 2 to the Act) in pursuance of Article IX(1) of the Antarctic Treaty.
