# The Community Bus (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 2252

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Community Bus (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made

31st August 2004

Laid before Parliament

9th September 2004

Coming into force

1st October 2004

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 52(1) and 60(1)(e) of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00001], and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 61(2) of the Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Community Bus (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 1st October 2004.

## 2. Amendments

(1) The Community Bus Regulations 1986[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 4(1), substitute “£44” for “£39”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Tony McNulty

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Transport

31st August 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the fee for a community bus permit granted under section 22 of the Transport Act 1985 from £39 to £44.

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business is available from the Licensing, Roadworthiness and Insurance Division of the Department for Transport, Zone 5/20, Southside, 105 Victoria Street, London SW1P 4DR.
