# Local Government (Best Value Authorities) (Power to Trade) (England) (Amendment) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 2307

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

Local Government (Best Value Authorities) (Power to Trade) (England) (Amendment) Order 2004

Made

7th September 2004

Laid before Parliament

10th September 2004

Coming into force

1st October 2004

The First Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 95, 96 and 123 of the Local Government Act 2003[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Best Value Authorities) (Power to Trade) (England) (Amendment) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 1st October 2004.

## 2. Amendment of the Local Government (Best Value Authorities) (Power to Trade) (England) Order 2004

(1) The Local Government (Best Value Authorities) (Power to Trade) (England) Order 2004[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 1 (application)—

- (a) in paragraph (2), after the words “paragraph (3)” insert “and article 4”;
- (b) in paragraph (3) –
  - (i) for the words “This Order” substitute “Subject to article 4, this Order”;
  - (ii) for sub-paragraph (c) to the end of the article substitute –

    > - (c) the Council of the Isles of Scilly;
    >
    >   when acting in its capacity as a fire and rescue authority.

    .

(3) In article 2(1) (power to trade in function-related activities), after the words “paragraphs (2) and (3)” insert “and article 4”.

(4) After article 3 (transitional arrangements), add the following article—

> **4. Transitional arrangements in respect of fire and rescue authorities**
>
> (1) Subject to paragraph (2), this article applies to the fire and rescue authorities specified in column 1 of the Schedule.
>
> (2) This article shall cease to have effect on 30th September 2007 in relation to a fire and rescue authority specified in the Schedule.
>
> (3) The fire and rescue authorities to which this article applies—
>
> - (a) shall be treated as authorities to which this Order applies, irrespective of whether an order under section 99(4) of the Local Government Act 2003 has been made in relation to those authorities; and
> - (b) are authorised to provide for a commercial purpose only the services specified in the corresponding entry in column 2 of the Schedule.
>
> (4) A fire and rescue authority which exercises the power conferred by paragraph (3)(b) does not have to comply with article 2(2).

.

(5) At the end, insert the Schedule as set out in the Schedule to this Order.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Local Government (Best Value Authorities) (Power to Trade) (England) Order 2004 (“the principal Order”) authorises certain local authorities to trade in any of their ordinary functions subject to certain conditions. Under section 95(4) of the Local Government Act 2003 the power to trade must be exercised through a company.

This Order amends the principal Order to provide transitional arrangements in respect of fire and rescue authorities, specified in the Schedule to the Order (which have been trading in certain functions under the Fire Services Act 1947). That Act is repealed by the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 with effect from 1st October 2004. The effect of the amendments made by this Order is to allow those authorities to continue trading in those functions. The [Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 (Commencement) (England and Scotland) Order 2004 S.I. 2004/ 2304 (C.100)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2304) also makes savings for the Fire Services Act 1947, which is repealed by the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004, in relation to trading activities carried out on or before, or contracted to be provided on or before, 31st March 2005.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business, voluntary bodies or public authorities.
