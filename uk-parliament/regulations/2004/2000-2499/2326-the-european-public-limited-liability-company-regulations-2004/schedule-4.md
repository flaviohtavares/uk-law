# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 4 — Modifications of the Companies Acts etc[^c-7dc2b999d72d4bcbf8f8204308b660ed]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulations 85 and 88

**1.**[^c-65463306758b0b0d9d02f80f58809364] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### Modifications applying on or after registration

**2.**[^c-3b42d02a2cffcf1951a99e560bb2d287] A reference to a company’s incorporation shall be construed as a reference to the registration of the documents delivered with the application for registration under regulation 85.

**3.** A reference to documents delivered under the[^c-e3ff278a3a1046b5ad0c72ccd6ef569e] 2006 Act shall be taken to include a reference to documents delivered under regulation 85.

**4.** (1) A reference to a company’s certificate of incorporation shall be construed as a reference to the certificate given under regulation 87(3).

(2) A requirement for the registrar of companies to issue a certificate of incorporation to a company shall—

- (a) be construed as a requirement to issue a certificate of registration similar to the certificate under regulation 87(3), and
- (b) apply with such other modifications as the registrar considers necessary in consequence of paragraph (a).

**5.**[^c-4ad7beb4d1141688c6dca4858d582157] The converting UK Societas[^c-2deeca50a6b82a6d93226ada7c770e30] is treated as if it had been formed (as well as registered) under the 2006 Act.

#### 6. Effect of registration

Section 16 of the[^c-fced5b6c2fe7b0d48a52461c91f12a7e] 2006 Act (effect of registration) shall not apply.

**7.** Section 112(1) of the[^c-ed6cce5236cc1594d4586027a2a94fb7] 2006 Act (definition of “member”) shall not apply.

**8.**[^c-1320459d712d14148d984a3e62ffcc35] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

#### 9. Certificate as to share capital

The following provisions shall not apply—

- (a) section 761 of[^c-ba9e6cdd4a3b9d1bae85afcf76133734] the 2006 Act[^c-6fe08b881fa49568c02097c1712c8cf4] (public company share capital requirements), [^c-17388a20d4d50d8fb3a6259bc0381b97]...
- (b) section 122(1)(b) of the Insolvency Act 1986 (winding up by the court: lack of certificate under section 761 of the 2006 Act).
- (c)[^c-b0f7a5969e6ad9bc429f715e9c2b0692] Article 102(1)(b) of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 (which corresponds to section 122(1)(b) of the Insolvency Act 1986).

#### 10.[^c-24181214502dfb4d547f82ebba7f2884]

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

#### 11. Accounting Reference Date

No modification made under this Schedule shall affect the determination of the accounting reference date of a converting UK Societas[^c-73413f65048d1da74b619d3342438aa0] by the application of section 391(4) of[^c-ec7b5c095d0929f5e25a5a9d4d6b0f31] the 2006 Act[^c-b2e365ece3644c68dbb7399e62b1c429], by virtue of Article 61 of the EC Regulation, or of regulation 83 prior to the registration of the converting UK Societas under regulation 87.
