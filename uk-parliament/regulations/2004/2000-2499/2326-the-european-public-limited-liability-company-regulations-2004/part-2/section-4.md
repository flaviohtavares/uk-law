# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 4

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4. The registrar

*Extent: E+W+S*

The registrar has the functions conferred by this Part in relation to the registration, or the deletion of the registration, of a UK Societas[^c-a4463c796f79de4bbe489b519dd8b1f8].
