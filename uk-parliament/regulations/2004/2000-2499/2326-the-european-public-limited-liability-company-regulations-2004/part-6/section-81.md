# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 81

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 81. The UK Societas as a body corporate[^c-446c62cfd2feaa9b70379d450d5c0fbe]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where—

- (a) any enactment is applied in the manner described in regulation 78(2); or
- (b) any enactment applies to a UK Societas[^c-3fa1d4eed4462ca1b3f6444825f41ced] otherwise than in the manner described in regulation 78(2)

and those enactments are expressed to apply to, or in respect of, a body corporate, a UK Societas[^c-a97ba1f10a997511e7cae9e14c37cae2], [^c-a97ba1f10a997511e7cae9e14c37cae2]... shall be treated for the purposes of the application of those enactments as if it were a body corporate.

(2) Nothing in this regulation has the effect of constituting a UK Societas[^c-015594060824864c465d9368fef353af] as a body corporate incorporated in, or formed under the law of, the United Kingdom (or any part of the United Kingdom)[^c-56ece61a018f19642dc58dfb9f47af4a].
