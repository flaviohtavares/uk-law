# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 80D

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 80D. Protected information: restriction on use or disclosure by UK Societas[^key-9407573c3d891a811140881c94b47e9a][^key-ad60293a35a1420c17a4e78678dae03c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In the application of section 241(1)(b) of the 2006 Act in relation to a UK Societas[^key-47a7b17767e65b94ff6a9a61eb75af86], the reference to any requirement of the Companies Acts includes a reference to any requirement of regulation 80C.
