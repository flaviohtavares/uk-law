# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 77

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 77. Records of an SE transferred under Article 8 of the EC Regulation (as it had effect immediately before IP completion day[^key-d83280f4f7beaaa0e94ce4635c8bfb80]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-92a702a043f6f02b675da089ee709c69] Where the registration of an SE is deleted under regulation 12A(5) following a transfer of its registered office to another Member State, the records of that SE kept by the registrar must continue to be kept by her for a period of twenty years following such a deletion.

(2) Where the registration of an SE is deleted, the application and documents delivered to the registrar[^key-a3e273c599065cd28fd80a92db2555ee] under regulation 11 of these Regulations, as they had effect at the time of delivery[^key-dfaa3b4061b689cf90af14f5f704aace], together with a copy of the certificate issued under Article 8(8) of the EC Regulation, as it had effect at the time of issue,[^key-a0f44db10535a8e80d5127efecd1ec32] shall be deemed to be documents to be retained by the registrar under regulation 13 and the provisions of these Regulations apply accordingly.
