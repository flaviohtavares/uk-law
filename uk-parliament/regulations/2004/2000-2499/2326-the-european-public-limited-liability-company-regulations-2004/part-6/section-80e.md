# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 80E

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 80E. Putting a member of the supervisory organ’s address on the public record[^c-9407573c3d891a811140881c94b47e9a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In the application of section 246 of the 2006 Act (putting a director’s usual residential address on the public record) in relation to a member of the supervisory organ of a UK Societas[^c-dee91bab05ba6063301d91ea75d425bc]—

- (a) the references in subsections (3)(a) and (4)(a) to the company’s register of directors are references to the UK Societas's[^c-ccc6ab9ec0ec1096dd4f5ce545c5a703] register of SO members, and
- (b) the reference in subsection (3)(b) to the company’s register of directors’ residential addresses is a reference to the UK Societas's register of SO members’ residential addresses.

(2) Paragraph (1) is without prejudice to the generality of regulation 78.
