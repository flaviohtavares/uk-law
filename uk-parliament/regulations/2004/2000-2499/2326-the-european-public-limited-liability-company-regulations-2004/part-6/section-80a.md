# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 80A

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 80A. Particulars of members to be registered under regulation 79: corporate members and firms[^key-9407573c3d891a811140881c94b47e9a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A UK Societas's[^key-e3f0744eef2232a8f657c06812eebaa1] register of SO members must contain the following particulars in the case of a body corporate, or a firm that is a legal person under the law by which it is governed,—

- (a) corporate or firm name;
- (b) registered or principal office;
- (c) in the case of a limited company that is a UK-registered company, the registered number;[^key-6546bbd69bbd4ec00b29d0c74e2bb35c]
- (d) in any other case, particulars of—
  - (i) the legal form of the company or firm and the law by which it is governed, and
  - (ii) if applicable, the register in which it is entered (including details of the state) and its registration number in that register.
