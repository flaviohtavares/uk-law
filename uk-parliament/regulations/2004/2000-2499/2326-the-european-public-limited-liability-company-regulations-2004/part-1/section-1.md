# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 1

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 8th October 2004.

(3) These Regulations extend to the whole of the United Kingdom[^key-4377a7dcec2a8b9336e1525fd5de0a66].
