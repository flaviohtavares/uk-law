# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 2

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2. ... EC Regulation[^c-9a8f104415c8fed06387f75153503e49]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In these Regulations—

- [^c-304e43bacb8042c1fdabe822e37e2828]...
- “**the EC Regulation**” means Council Regulation 2157/2001/EC of 8 October 2001 on the Statute for a European Company [^c5399261];

and references to numbered Articles are, unless otherwise specified, references to Articles in the EC Regulation.
