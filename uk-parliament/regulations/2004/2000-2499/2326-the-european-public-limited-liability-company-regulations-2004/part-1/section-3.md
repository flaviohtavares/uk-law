# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 3

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In these Regulations—

- [^key-4e0504990a4129c385b715f8a136b402]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- the “**1996 Act**” means the Employment Rights Act 1996 [^c5399281];
- the “[^key-62cde1cbcdfb0fe764b86117174f26e3]2006 Act” means the Companies Act 2006;
- “[^key-a325bbed27a45e49d468167bad23564f]**the Companies Acts**” has the meaning given by section 2 of the[^key-63b1f94f3c920a3dcc08d1b3a5662e8b] 2006 Act;
- “**SE**” means a European Public Limited-Liability Company (or Societas Europaea) within the meaning of the EC Regulation , as it had effect immediately before IP completion day,[^key-89d5d68063ad6b380c7cce604ca8f6db] and, except as provided in these Regulations, means an SE [^key-c215baecdf2ec4491289614c724b9000]... registered in the United Kingdom[^key-26ef7723ba99316882ac135a3ea117ea].
- “[^key-1eb2675bd413d7d7a2623bc89bf2f477]**UK Societas**” means an SE on or after IP completion day.

(2) Except as otherwise provided in these Regulations, words and expressions defined for the purposes of the Companies Acts have the same meaning in these Regulations[^key-8e1d99c069061573364e234d8fafb8a6].

(3) Except as otherwise provided in these Regulations, words and expressions which are used in the EC Regulation [^key-f2985957050702a6bca57a416b59a501]... have the same meaning as they have in that Regulation[^key-5d6573ef3fdb0948b61eb50c4af9acc3]....

(4) Where a word or expression is both defined as mentioned[^key-94abe2e329e205d265aa5291ac1b99dc] in paragraph (2) and used in the EC Regulation [^key-c1556911e8199f2d26e9f725db1073f7]..., it has the meaning it has in that Regulation [^key-e3ea2b18704182adbb98364cafc6ad88]... except as otherwise provided in these Regulations.
