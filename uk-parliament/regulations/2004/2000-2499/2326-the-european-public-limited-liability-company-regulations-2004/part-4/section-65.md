# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 65

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 65. Timing of the first general meeting of a UK Societas (Article 54(1))[^c-b6607bb7ef616d0ece05daf83caf5288]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The first general meeting of a UK Societas[^c-e9eb0dc973687dc4ebdecc187237e8cd] may be held at any time in the 18 months following its[^c-fd07301d880f190f98e9fca4904674ba] incorporation as an SE[^c-a43c1771cdc20e07d0a3eed833972d7a].
