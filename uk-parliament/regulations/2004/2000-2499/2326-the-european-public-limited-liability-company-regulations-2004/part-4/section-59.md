# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 59

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 59. Power of the management or administrative organ of a UK Societas to amend statutes where in conflict with employee involvement arrangements (Article 12(4))[^key-a11be35b54ab3062fcd985d027d1acd0][^key-faba5b578cef5788a341cf50444a90c0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where there is a conflict between the arrangements for employee involvement and the existing statutes the management or administrative organ of the UK Societas[^key-93e39184e34396c86413b7a8203701e0] may amend the statutes to the extent necessary to resolve the conflict without any further decision from the general shareholders meeting.
