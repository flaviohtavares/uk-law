# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 67

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 67. UK Societates subject to law on public limited liability companies as regard the expression of their capital (Article 67(1))[^c-1cac7f92f03fad9ace47093e900bf789][^c-5716b3d8698384353723cfff1353fb80]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A UK Societas[^c-19759a8e7e2278d3ca6095acb540b09d] shall be subject to the provisions of the enactments and rules of law applying to a public company as regards the expression of its capital.
