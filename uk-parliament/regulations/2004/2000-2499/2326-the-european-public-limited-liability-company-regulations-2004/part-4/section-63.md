# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 63

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 63. Members of the supervisory organ to be entitled to require the management organ to provide certain information (Article 41(3))

*Extent: E+W+S*

Each member of the supervisory organ is entitled to require the management organ to provide to that member information of a kind which the supervisory organ needs to exercise supervision in accordance with Article 40(1).
