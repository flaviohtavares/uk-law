# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 62

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 62. Minimum number of members of the supervisory organ (Article 40(3))[^key-a1231bf7382e3e466b33843bb480d1de]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The minimum number of the members of the supervisory organ of a UK Societas[^key-b7cd2adc58fec90b0792a73d36fdbcd1] is two.
