# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 66

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 66. Proportion of shareholders of a UK Societas who may require one or more additional items to be put on the agenda of any general meeting (Article 56)[^c-fe21b2e285aae9971e32b82636af78b7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The proportion of the shareholders of a UK Societas[^c-573319f34126af5220c562d68794a72a] who may require one or more additional items put on the agenda of any general meeting is to be the holders of at least 5% of the UK Societas's[^c-2f0740af440c23bb1f4d894be0de1064] subscribed capital.
