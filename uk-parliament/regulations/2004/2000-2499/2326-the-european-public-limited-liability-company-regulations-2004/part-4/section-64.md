# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 64

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 64. Minimum number of members of an administrative organ (Article 43(2))[^c-cb9cb2979171eed41464e9cd5f46c3ec]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The minimum number of the members of the administrative organ of a UK Societas[^c-a495bb410822561206a0757846f62b30] is two.
