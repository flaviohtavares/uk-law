# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 61

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 61. Minimum number of members of the management organ (Article 39(4))[^c-fdfc15149ca9cfa3bf3659600b83c1ea]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The minimum number of the members of the management organ of a UK Societas[^c-00eafffca063972a8f09dc478075a3bf] is two.
