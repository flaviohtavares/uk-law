# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 89

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 89. Records of a converting SE

*Extent: E+W+S*

The records of a converting UK Societas[^key-ea349b9e5d4c71318f184608d7493770], when the converting UK Societas has been registered as a public company limited by shares under the provisions of this Part, relating to any period before its registration as a public company shall be treated for the purposes of the Companies Acts[^key-ae4c52bd22a9a1b69e740115dd7ee96c] as if they were records of that public company.
