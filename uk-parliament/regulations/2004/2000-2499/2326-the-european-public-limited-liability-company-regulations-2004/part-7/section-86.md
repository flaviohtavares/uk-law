# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 86

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 86. Publication of draft terms of conversion[^key-ffb6879557d71a35207c90e40884241f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-46da903f122b46bad131ff8337296f4c] Where under Article 66(4) draft terms of conversion are required to be publicised there shall be delivered to the registrar a notice, together with a copy of the draft terms,[^key-2c90317cbff36fc69994eee67e132878] and the registrar shall cause to be published in the Gazette notice of the receipt by her of the copy of the draft terms.

(2)[^key-64ce3618d37c6835c3bf523eacc24ec1] A notice under paragraph (1) must contain the following particulars—

- (a) the UK Societas's[^key-3cb94ddf7e900209886ee5e4f287e58b] name and registered number; and
- (b) the proposed name of the public company.
