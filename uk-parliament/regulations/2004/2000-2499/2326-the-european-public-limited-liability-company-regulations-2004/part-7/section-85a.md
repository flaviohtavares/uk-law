# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 85A

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 85A. Statement of compliance[^key-ce5b9bcfdda29fbbba584b1600c46fc7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The statement of compliance required to be delivered with an application for registration under regulation 85 is a statement that all the requirements of these Regulations and the EC Regulation in respect of the conversion of a UK Societas[^key-4d153f0e64e3c9d493a036720cd7d562] into a public company (including as to registration) have been complied with.

(2) The registrar may accept the statement of compliance as sufficient evidence of compliance.
