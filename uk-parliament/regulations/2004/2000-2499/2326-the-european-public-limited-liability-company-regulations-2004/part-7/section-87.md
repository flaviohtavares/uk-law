# The European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations 2004, section 87

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 87. Registration under the 2006 Act[^c-de88610fb1d7c9f53c20ccc5d7209219]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-7cc1fb46fbabb493647af195e3f6cd35] As from the date on which the application for registration is delivered to the registrar under regulation 85, section 14 of the 2006 Act (registration) shall apply in relation to the documents delivered with the application for registration as if—

- (a) they have been delivered under section 9 of that Act (registration documents), and
- (b) the requirements of that Act in respect of registration had been complied with.

(2)[^c-3b81d514ab4bf0f8b01b921759c3af11] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) On registration of the documents referred to in paragraph (1)[^c-f803edd9248a4ec7ca207d6472e42bac] the registrar shall give a certificate—

- (a) that the converting UK Societas[^c-cb29f6976790a326508930a89f79b07d] is incorporated and retains the legal personality it had when a UK Societas[^c-375080ed0f1f5dc1ef1e933ec35e22bf];
- (b)[^c-35c6e927d1b139663a7674ec37291408] that those documents are registered under the 2006 Act; and
- (c) that it is a public company limited by shares.

(4) The certificate is conclusive evidence—

- (a) that the requirements of the[^c-79f481cf0d9fe60d8be68b7dd4bda1d3] 2006 Act in respect of registration [^c-c829d96ae5fb0eba599183285647e2a8]... have been complied with, and
- (b) that on and after the registration the converting UK Societas[^c-919188b85fee4f3d9e41f45a06aa3d5e] is a public company limited by shares.
