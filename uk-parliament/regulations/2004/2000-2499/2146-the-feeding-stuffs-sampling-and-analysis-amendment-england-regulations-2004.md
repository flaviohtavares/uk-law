# The Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 2146

AGRICULTURE, ENGLAND

The Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2004

Made

12th August 2004

Laid before Parliament

23rd August 2004

Coming into force

13th September 2004

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 66(1), 77(4), 78(6) and 84 of the Agriculture Act 1970[^f00001] (as read with regulation 14 of the Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000[^f00002] and articles 2 and 6 of the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Dissolution) Order 2002[^f00003]), after consultation as required by section 84(1) of that Act and by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00004], and being a Minister designated[^f00005] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00006] in relation to the Common Agricultural Policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that last mentioned section (in so far as these Regulations cannot be made under the powers of the Agriculture Act 1970 specified above), makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Title, application and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2004, shall apply in relation to England only and shall come into force on 13<sup>th</sup> September 2004.

## 2. Amendment of the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1999

(1) The Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1999[^f00007] shall be amended, insofar as they apply in relation to England, in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) For paragraph (3)(b)(ii) of regulation 6 (application of methods of analysis) there shall be substituted the following provision —

> - (ii) the procedure set out in Commission Directive [2003/126/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0126) on the analytical method for the determination of constituents of animal origin for the official control of feedingstuffs[^f00008] shall be used.

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Stephen Ladyman

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

12th August 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

**1.** These Regulations, which apply in relation to England only, further amend the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1999 (S.I. [1999/1663](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1663), as already amended), which extend to the whole of Great Britain.

**2.** These Regulations implement Commission Directive [2003/126/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0126) on the analytical method for the determination of constituents of animal origin for the official control of feedingstuffs (OJ No. L339, 24.12.2003, p.78).

**3.** These Regulations amend the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1999 by providing that where a microscopic examination is carried out for specified purposes, the procedure set out in Commission Directive [2003/126/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0126) shall be used (*regulation 2(2)*).

**4.** A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business has been prepared and placed in the Library of each House of Parliament together with a Transposition Note setting out how Commission Directive [2003/126/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0126) is transposed into domestic law by these Regulations. Copies may be obtained from the Chemical Contaminants and Animal Feed Division of the Food Standards Agency, Aviation House, 125 Kingsway, London, WC2B 6NH.
