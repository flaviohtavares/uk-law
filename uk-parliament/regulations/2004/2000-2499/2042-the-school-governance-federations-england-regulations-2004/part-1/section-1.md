# The School Governance (Federations) (England) Regulations 2004, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the School Governance (Federations) (England) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 30<sup>th</sup> August 2004.

(2) These Regulations apply only in relation to England.
