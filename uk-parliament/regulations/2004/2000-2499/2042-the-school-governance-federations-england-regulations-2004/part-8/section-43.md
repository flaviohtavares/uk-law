# The School Governance (Federations) (England) Regulations 2004, section 43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 43. Availability of amounts representing budget share

Subject to any provision made by or under a scheme made under section 48(1) of the 1998 Act, the temporary governing body of each school may spend any sum made available by the authority under section 50(1) of that Act[^f00025] to the governing body of the federation in respect of each school, as they think fit for any purposes of that school.
