# The School Governance (Federations) (England) Regulations 2004, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Incorporation of governing body of a school leaving a federation

On the date of dissolution the temporary governing body of each federated school shall be incorporated as the governing body of each de-federated school under the name given in the school’s instrument of government.
