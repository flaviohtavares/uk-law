# The School Governance (Federations) (England) Regulations 2004, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Incorporation of governing body of a school leaving a federation

On the de-federation date the temporary governing body of the de-federated school shall be incorporated as the governing body of that school under the name given in the school’s instrument of government.
