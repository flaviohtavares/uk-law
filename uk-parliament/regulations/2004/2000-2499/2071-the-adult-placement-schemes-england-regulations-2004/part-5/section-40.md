# The Adult Placement Schemes (England) Regulations 2004, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Application of Part 2 of the Act to persons carrying on or managing an adult placement scheme

(1) A person who carries on or manages an adult placement scheme is hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 42(1) of the Act.

(2) Part 2 of the Act, in so far as not already applied by the Care Standards Act 2000 (Extension of Part 2 to Adult Placement Schemes) (England) Regulations 2004[^f00006] shall apply to persons prescribed by paragraph (1) with the modifications specified in Schedule 5.
