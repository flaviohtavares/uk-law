# The Adult Placement Schemes (England) Regulations 2004, SCHEDULE 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 9 — AMENDMENT OF THE DOMICILIARY CARE AGENCIES REGULATIONS 2002

Regulation 44

**1.** For regulation 3 of the Domiciliary Care Agencies Regulations 2002 (excepted undertakings)[^f00022], substitute—

> **3.** For the purposes of the Act, an undertaking is excepted from the definition of “domiciliary care agency” in section 4(3) of the Act if—
>
> - (a) the undertaking is carried on by an individual who—
>   - (i) carries it on otherwise than in partnership with others;
>   - (ii) is not employed by an organisation to carry it on; and
>   - (iii) does not employ any other person for the purpose of the undertaking; or
> - (b) it is an adult placement scheme as defined in regulation 2 of the Adult Placement Schemes (England) Regulations 2004.

.
