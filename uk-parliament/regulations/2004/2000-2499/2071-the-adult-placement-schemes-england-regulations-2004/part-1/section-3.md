# The Adult Placement Schemes (England) Regulations 2004, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Specification of registration authority

For the purposes of these Regulations, the CSCI is specified as the registration authority[^f00005].

## Annotations

[^f00005]: Footnote: *See* section 42(5) of the Act as inserted by section 147 of, and paragraph 25 of Schedule 9 to, the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act [2003 (c. 43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/43) (“the 2003 Act”). The CSCI was established by section 42 of that Act. See also section 5B of the Act, as inserted by section 104 of the 2003 Act.
