# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 3) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 2032

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 3) Order 2004

Made

27th July 2004

Coming into force

1st September 2004

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 27th day of July 2004

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 3) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 1st September 2004.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 1st September 2004.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council
