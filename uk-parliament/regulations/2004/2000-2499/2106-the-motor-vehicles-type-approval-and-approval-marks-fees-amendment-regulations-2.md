The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 61(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001], and the powers conferred by section 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00002] with the consent of the Treasury, and of powers conferred by the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00003] and section 128 of the Finance Act 1990[^f00004], and in relation to the exercise of the powers conferred by section 61(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic Act 1988, after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with the provisions of section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval and Approval Marks) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 1st October 2004.

## 2. Preliminary

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval and Approval Marks) (Fees) Regulations 1999[^f00005] shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

## 3. Amendment to Regulation 3 (Interpretation)

In regulation 3(2) for the definition of “Framework Directive” there shall be substituted—

> - “Framework Directive” means Council Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156)[^f00006] as amended by amendments up to and including those effected by Commission Directive [2004/78/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0078)[^f00007];

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval and Approval Marks) (Fees) Regulations 1999 (“the 1999 Regulations”) which prescribe the fees payable for the examination of vehicles and vehicle parts, the examination of complete vehicles and the issue of documents in connection with the type approval (both European and National) of vehicles and vehicle parts.

*Regulation 3* replaces the existing definition of “Framework Directive” in regulation 3(2) of the 1999 Regulations. The new definition encompasses the further amendments that have been made to Council Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156) (on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to the type-approval of motor vehicles and their trailers) by Directives [2003/97/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0097) (OJ No. L25, 29.1.2004, p.1), [2004/03/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0003) (OJ No. L49, 19.2.2004, p.36) and [2004/78/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0078) (OJ No. L153, 30.4.2004, p.100).

The other changes relate to the fee amounts. *Regulations 4, 5* and *the Schedule* provide for an average increase of 2% (over the amounts set out in The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval and Approval Marks) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 ([S.I. 2003/2258](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2258)) in respect of the fees payable. This is with the exception of fee no. 60026 set out in part III of new Schedule 1, which remains unchanged at £25.

*Regulations 6, 7* and *8* amend Schedule 5 to the 1999 Regulations so as to take account of new or amended ECE Regulations and Community Instruments. (The terms “ECE Regulations” and “Community Instruments” are defined in regulation 3(2) of the 1999 Regulations.)

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business beyond an increase in fees in line with inflation.

Copies of the ECE Regulations and Community Instruments referred to in these Regulations may be obtained from the Stationery Office.
