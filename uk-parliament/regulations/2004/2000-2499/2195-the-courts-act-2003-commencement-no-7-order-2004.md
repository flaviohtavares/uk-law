# The Courts Act 2003 (Commencement No.7) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 2195 (C. 98 )

MAGISTRATES' COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Courts Act 2003 (Commencement No.7) Order 2004

Made

24th August 2004

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 110 of the Courts Act 2003 [^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Courts Act 2003 (Commencement No.7) Order 2004.

**2.** Section 97 (collection of fines and discharge of fines by unpaid work) of, and Schedule 6 (discharge of fines by unpaid work) to, the Courts Act 2003 shall come into force on 21<sup>st</sup> September 2004, in so far as they are not already in force.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Filkin

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department for Constitutional Affairs

24th August 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the provisions mentioned in article 2 into force on 21<sup>st</sup> September 2004, in so far as they have not already been commenced.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The following provisions of the Courts Act 2003 have been, or are to be, brought into force by commencement order made before the date of this Order:
