# The Magistrates' Courts Warrants (Specification of Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1835

MAGISTRATES' COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Magistrates' Courts Warrants (Specification of Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2004

Made

14th July 2004

Laid before Parliament

23rd July 2004

Coming into force

1st September 2004

The Lord Chancellor and the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 125A(3) of the Magistrates' Court Act 1980[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Magistrates' Courts Warrants (Specification of Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> September 2004.

## 2. Amendment of the Magistrates' Courts Warrants (Specification of Provisions) Order 2000

(1) The Magistrates' Courts Warrants (Specification of Provisions) Order 2000[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) For paragraphs(a) and (b) in article 2 there shall be substituted the following paragraphs–

> - (a) paragraph 7(2) of Schedule 7 to the Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000[^f00003];
> - (aa) section 7(1) of the Bail Act 1976[^f00004];
> - (b) in the Magistrates' Court Act 1980–
>   - (i) section 1;
>   - (ii) section 13;
>   - (iii) section 76;
>   - (iv) section 83(1) and (2);
>   - (v) section 86(4);
>   - (vi) section 93(5);
>   - (vii) section 97;
>   - (viii) section 97A[^f00005];
>   - (ix) section 136;

(3) For paragraph (d) in article 2 there shall be substituted the following paragraph–

> - (d) paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 to the Crime and Disorder Act 1998[^f00006].

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Christopher Leslie

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Constitutional Affairs

13th July 2004

Paul Goggins

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

14th July 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

Sections 125A to 125D of the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980, as inserted by sections 92, 93, 94 and 96 of the Access to Justice Act 1999, make provision for the manner of enforcement of warrants under statutory provisions to be specified by order. This Order amends the Magistrates' Court Warrants (Specification of Provisions) Order 2000 which specified provisions.
