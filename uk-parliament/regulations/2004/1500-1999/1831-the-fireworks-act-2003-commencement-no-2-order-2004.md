# The Fireworks Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1831 (C. 75)

FIREWORKS

The Fireworks Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004

Made

14th July 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 18(1) of the Fireworks Act 2003[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Fireworks Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Fireworks Act 2003.

## 2. Provisions of the Act commenced by this Order

15th July 2004 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the provisions of the Act listed in the first column of the Schedule to this Order for the purposes set out in the second column.

Gerry Sutcliffe,

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations, Competition and Consumers,

Department of Trade and Industry

14th July 2004
