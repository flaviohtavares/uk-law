# The Road Transport (International Passenger Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1882

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Road Transport (International Passenger Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made

14th July2004

Laid before Parliament

6th August 2004

Coming into force

30th August 2004

The Secretary of State for Transport, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the international carriage of passengers by road, in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00003], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Transport (International Passenger Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 30th August 2004.

## 2. Amendments

(1) The Road Transport (International Passenger Services) Regulations 1984[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1)(g) substitute “Secretary of State for Transport” for “Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions”.

(3) In regulation 14(2A) substitute “£11” for “£10”.

(4) In regulation 14(3) substitute “£160” for “£140”.

(5) In regulation 14(3A) substitute “£163” for “£144.20”.

(6) In regulation 14(4) substitute “£34” for “£30”.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

David Jamieson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

6th July2004

We consent to the making of these Regulations

Derek Twigg

Nick Ainger

Two of the Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

14th July 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase some of the fees that are payable under the Road Transport (International Passenger Transport Services) Regulations 1984 (“the principal Regulations”). These Regulations also change the definition of “Secretary of State” in regulation 2(1)(g) of the principal Regulations to the Secretary of State for Transport.

The changes are as follows:

|  | OLD FEE | NEW FEE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Certified copy of a regular service authorisation, special regular service authorisation, shuttle service authorisation or authorisation under regulation 14(2) of the principal Regulations | £10 | £11 |
| Application for a special regular service authorisation or an international passenger transport authorisation | £140 | £160 |
| Application for a regular service authorisation or shuttle service authorisation | £144.20 | £163 |
| Regular or special regular authorisation – fee per year of validity of the authorisation | £30 | £34 |

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business is available from the Licensing, Roadworthiness and Insurance Division of the Department for Transport, Zone 5/20, Southside, 105 Victoria Street, London SW1P 4DR.
