# The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provisions) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No.1629(C. 64 )

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provisions) Order 2004

Made

25th June 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 330(4)(b) and 336(3) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No.4 and Saving Provisions) Order 2004.

(2) In this Order “the 2003 Act” means the Criminal Justice Act 2003.

**2.** (1) The provisions of the 2003 Act listed in paragraph (2) shall come into force on 3<sup>rd</sup> July 2004.

(2) The provisions of the 2003 Act referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) sections 22 to 24, 26 and 27 (conditional cautions), except sub-paragraphs (b) and (d) to (g) in the entry relating to the “relevant prosecutor” in section 27;
- (b) section 28 (charging or release of persons in police detention) in so far as it relates to the provision referred to in sub-paragraph (c); and
- (c) paragraph 3 of Schedule 2 (charging or release of persons in police detention) to the extent that it inserts section 37B(9)(a) of the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984[^f00002].

**3.** (1) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), the provisions of the 2003 Act listed in paragraph (2) shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> September 2004.

(2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) section 313 (extension of investigations by Criminal Cases Review Commission in England and Wales);
- (b) section 314 (extension of investigations by Criminal Cases Review Commission in Northern Ireland);
- (c) section 316 (power to substitute conviction of alternative offence on appeal in England and Wales);
- (d) section 317 (power to substitute conviction of alternative offence on appeal in Northern Ireland);
- (e) section 318 (substitution of conviction on different charge on appeal from court-martial);
- (f) section 331 (further minor and consequential amendments ) in so far as it relates to the provisions referred to in sub-paragraph (g);
- (g) in Schedule 36 (further minor and consequential amendments), paragraphs 86, 89 and 97.

(3) A direction under section 23A of the Criminal Appeal Act 1968[^f00003] may not be made in relation to an application for leave to appeal made before the commencement of section 313 of the 2003 Act.

(4) A direction under section 25A of the Criminal Appeal (Northern Ireland) Act 1980[^f00004] may not be made in relation to an application for leave to appeal made before the commencement of section 314 of the 2003 Act.

Scotland of Asthal

Minister of State

Home Office

25th June 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Criminal Justice Act [2003 (c. 44)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/44) which are listed in articles 2 and 3 on 3<sup>rd</sup> July and 1<sup>st</sup> September 2004 respectively. Article 3(3) and (4) are saving provisions, needed to ensure that the amendments to the Criminal Appeal Act 1968 and Criminal Appeal (Northern Ireland) Act 1980 apply to new cases only.
