# The Immigration (Provision of Physical Data) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1834

IMMIGRATION

The Immigration (Provision of Physical Data) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2004

Made

14th July 2004

Coming into force

regulations 1 and 2(a)

1st August 2004

regulation 2(b)

1st September 2004

Whereas a draft of these Regulations has been laid before and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament;

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 126(1) of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration (Provision of Physical Data) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2004.

(2) These Regulations, except regulation 2(b), shall come into force on 1st August 2004 and regulation 2(b) shall come into force on 1st September 2004.

**2.** The Schedule to the Immigration (Provision of Physical Data) Regulations 2003[^f00002] is amended as follows:

- (a) before “Sri Lanka”, there is inserted “Rwanda”; and
- (b) after “Ethopia”, there is inserted “Kenya”.

Des Browne

Minister of State

Home Office

14th July 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Schedule to the Immigration (Provision of Physical Data) Regulations 2003, as amended by the Immigration (Provision of Physical Data) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 (taken together “the 2003 Regulations”) by adding two new countries. The Schedule lists countries in which an application for entry clearance is an “application” as defined in the 2003 Regulations. The effect on an application for entry clearance being an “application” under the 2003 Regulations is that it is required to be accompanied by a record of the applicant’s fingerprints. Regulation 2(a) adds Rwanda to the list and regulation 2(b) adds Kenya
