# The Exempt Charities Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1995

CHARITIES

The Exempt Charities Order 2004

Made

27th July 2004

Coming into force

31st July 2004

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 27th day of July 2004

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of paragraph (c) of Schedule 2 to the Charities Act 1993[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Exempt Charities Order 2004 and shall come into force on 31st July 2004.

**2.** Roehampton University is hereby declared to be an exempt charity for the purposes of the Charities Act 1993.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order declares Roehampton University to be an exempt charity within the meaning of the Charities Act 1993 with effect from 31st July 2004. An exempt charity is not required to be registered with the Charity Commission and the Commissioners cannot exercise any powers under the Act in relation to an exempt charity except at the charity’s request.
