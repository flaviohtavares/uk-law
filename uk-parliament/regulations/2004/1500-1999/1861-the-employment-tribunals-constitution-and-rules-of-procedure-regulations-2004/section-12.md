# The Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Modification of section 4 of the Employment Tribunals Act (national security proceedings)

(1) For the purposes of national security proceedings section 4 of the Employment Tribunals Act shall be modified as follows.

(2) In section 4(1)(a), for the words “in accordance with regulations made under section 1(1)” substitute the words “in accordance with regulation 11(a) of the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004”.

(3) In section 4(1)(b), for the words “in accordance with regulations so made” substitute the words “in accordance with regulation 11(b) of those Regulations”.

(4) In section 4(5), for the words “in accordance with Regulations made under section 1(1)” substitute the words “in accordance with regulation 10(a) of the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004”.
