# Freedom of Information (Additional Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct errors in [SI 2004/938](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/938) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2004 No. 1870

FREEDOM OF INFORMATION

Freedom of Information (Additional Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order 2004

Made

13th July 2004

Laid before Parliament

20th July 2004

Coming into force

10th August 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 4(1) and 4(6) of the Freedom of Information Act 2000[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Freedom of Information (Additional Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 10th August 2004.

**2.** Article 2 of the Freedom of Information (Additional Public Authorities) Order 2004[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

(1) the words “at the appropriate place in each case” shall be deleted and replaced with “at paragraph 35A”.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

Filkin

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Constitutional Affairs

13th July 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

S.I. [2004/938](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/938) added a number of public authorities to Schedule 1 to the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (“the Act”). Article 2 and Schedule 1 to that Order added a reference to a local fisheries committee for a sea fisheries district established under section 1 of the Sea Fisheries Regulation Act 1966[^f00003] to Part II of Schedule 1 to the Act “at the appropriate place”. The bodies and offices in Part II of Schedule 1 to the Act, however, are listed in numbered paragraphs. The reference to “the appropriate place” should, therefore, be amended to place the entry at the next sequential paragraph number.
