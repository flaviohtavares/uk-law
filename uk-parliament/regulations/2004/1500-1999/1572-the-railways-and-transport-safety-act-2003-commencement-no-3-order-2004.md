# The Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1572 (C.63 )

TRANSPORT

The Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2004

Made

18th June 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 120(1) of the Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2004.

(2) In this Order, “the 2003 Act” means the Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003.

## 2. Commencement of provisions

The following provisions of the 2003 Act shall come into force on 19th June 2004—

- (a) sub-section (1) of section 34 (Compulsory police services agreement); and
- (b) section 74 (Regulations and orders).
