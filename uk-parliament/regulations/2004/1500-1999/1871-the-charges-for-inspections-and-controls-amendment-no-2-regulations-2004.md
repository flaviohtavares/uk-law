# The Charges for Inspections and Controls (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2004

The powers conferred by sections 45 and 48(1)(b) and (c) of the Food Safety Act 1990 [^f00001] are now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002].

The Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs has carried out the consultation required by section 48(4) of that Act and had regard, in accordance with section 48(4A) of that Act, to any relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency.

She has carried out the consultation required by Article 9 of Regulation (EC) No.178/2002 of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003].

Accordingly, in exercise of those powers, she makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Charges for Inspections and Controls (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2004 and come into force on 25th July 2004.

## 2. Revocation and amendment of previous instruments

(1) The Charges for Inspections and Controls (Amendment) Regulations 2004[^f00004] are revoked.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Charges for Inspections and Controls Regulations 1997 which, as amended, continue to give effect to Article 2 of Council Directive [85/73/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1985/0073) on the financing of veterinary inspections and controls covered by Directives [89/662/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0662), [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425), [90/675/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0675) and [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496) (OJ No.L32, 5.2.85, p14); that Directive was last amended by Council Directive [97/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0079) (OJ No.L24, 30.1.98, p31). Article 2, as read with Article 5, requires member States to ensure that fees are collected to cover costs occasioned by inspections and controls provided for by Council Directive [96/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0023) on measures to monitor certain substances and residues thereof in live animals and animal products and repealing Directives [85/358/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1985/0358) and [86/469/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1986/0469) and Decisions [89/187/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1989/0187) and [91/664/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1991/0664) (OJ No.L125, 23.5.96, p10).

Regulation 2 corrects an error, introduced by the Charges for Inspections and Controls (Amendment) Regulations 2004, relating to the egg inspection charge per case of 360 eggs. It does so by revoking those Regulations from before their coming into force date, and amending the 1997 Regulations with effect from that date, with the table of charges corrected so as to change the egg inspection charge from £0.083 (identified in the Explanatory Note to that instrument as replacing £0.088) to £0.0083, the correct previous figure being £0.0088.

The inspections and controls to which the fees apply relate to checks for residues of veterinary medicines and other substances in food. The changes (in comparison with the 1997 regulations before the previous amendment), ranging approximately from a reduction of 21% to an increase of 45%, are itemised in the table below - other sums are the same as before:

| Type of creature or product | Current sum | Replacement sum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bovine | £0.3666 | £0.4143 |
| Sheep | £0.0184 | £0.0267 |
| Poultry | £1.0461 | £0.8297 |
| Salmon | £1.30 | £1.69 |
| Brown trout, rainbow trout | £1.10 | £1.43 |
| Milk | £0.0154 | £0.0177 |
| Eggs | £0.0088 | £0.0083 |

The full regulatory impact assessment of the effect of the revoked instrument on the costs of business, available from the Veterinary Medicines Directorate, Woodham Lane, New Haw, Addlestone, Surrey KT15 3LS with copies placed in the Library of each House of Parliament, is also applicable to this instrument.
