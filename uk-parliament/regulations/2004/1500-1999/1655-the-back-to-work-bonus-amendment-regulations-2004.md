# The Back to Work Bonus (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1655

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Back to Work Bonus (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made

28th June 2004

Laid before Parliament

5th July 2004

Coming into force

25th October 2004

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 26, 35(1) and 36(2) to (5) of the Jobseekers Act 1995[^f00001] and after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that the proposals in regards of these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Back to Work Bonus (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 25th October 2004, immediately after the Social Security (Back to Work Bonus and Lone Parent Run-on) (Amendment and Revocation) Regulations 2003[^f00003].

## 2. Amendment of the Social Security (Back to Work Bonus and Lone Parent Run-on) (Amendment and Revocation) Regulations 2003.

In regulation 10 of the Social Security (Back to Work Bonus and Lone Parent Run-on) (Amendment and Revocation) Regulations 2003—

- (a) in paragraph (1) for “Subject to paragraph (2)” substitute “Subject to the amendments made by paragraphs (2) and (3)”;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(d), after “regulation 23(6) of the Back to Work Bonus Regulations” add “, or who makes a claim for a bonus after 28th January 2005 within the appropriate specified period”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Jane Kennedy

Minister of State,

Department for Work and Pensions

28th June 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Social Security (Back to Work Bonus and Lone Parent Run-on) (Amendment and Revocation) Regulations 2003 (S.I. [2003/1589](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/1589)) to provide for an extended period of transitional protection for claimants who comply with the requirements contained in the Back to Work Bonus (No. 2) Regulations 1996 (S.I. [1996/2570](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2570)) on or before 28th January 2005.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for the instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
