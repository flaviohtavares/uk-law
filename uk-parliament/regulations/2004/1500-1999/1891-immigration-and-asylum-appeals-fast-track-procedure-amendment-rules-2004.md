# Immigration and Asylum Appeals (Fast Track Procedure) (Amendment) Rules 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1891 (L. 10)

IMMIGRATION

Immigration and Asylum Appeals (Fast Track Procedure) (Amendment) Rules 2004

Made

at 18.55, 20th July 2004

Laid before Parliament

20th July 2004

Coming into force

20th July 2004

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 106(1) – (3) and 112(3) of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002[^f00001], after consulting with the Council on Tribunals in accordance with section 8 of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00002], makes the following Rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Immigration and Asylum Appeals (Fast Track Procedure) (Amendment) Rules 2004 and shall come into force on 20th July 2004.

## 2. Amendment to the Immigration and Asylum Appeals (Fast Track Procedure) Rules 2003

In the Schedule to the Immigration and Asylum Appeals (Fast Track Procedure) Rules 2003[^f00003], before “Immigration Removal Centre, Harmondsworth, Middlesex” insert—

> - Campsfield House Immigration Removal Centre, Kidlington, Oxon
> - Colnbrook House Immigration Removal Centre, Harmondsworth, Middlesex

.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

David Lammy

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Constitutional Affairs

Dated 20th July 2004

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Immigration and Asylum Appeals (Fast Track Procedure) Rules 2003 by inserting into the Schedule to those Rules additional removal centres at Kidlington and Harmondsworth so that the fast track procedure can apply to a party who is detained at one of those centres.
