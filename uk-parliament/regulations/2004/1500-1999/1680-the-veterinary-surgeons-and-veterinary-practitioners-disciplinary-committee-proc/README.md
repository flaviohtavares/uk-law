# The Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Disciplinary Committee) (Procedure and Evidence) Rules Order of Council 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1680

VETERINARY SURGEONS

The Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Disciplinary Committee) (Procedure and Evidence) Rules Order of Council 2004

Made

25th May 2004

Coming into force

1st June 2004

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 25th day of May 2004

By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Whereas the Council of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons, in pursuance of paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 2 to the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966[^f00001] (“the Act”), has made the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Disciplinary Committee) (Procedure and Evidence) Rules 2003 (“the Rules”), as set out in the Schedule to this Order, and whereas, by virtue of section 25(1) of the Act such Rules do not have effect unless approved by the Privy Council: now, therefore, Their Lordships, having taken the Rules into consideration, hereby order as follows:

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order approves rules made by the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons governing the procedure and evidence in cases coming before the College’s Disciplinary Committee.

The rules replace rules approved in 1967.
