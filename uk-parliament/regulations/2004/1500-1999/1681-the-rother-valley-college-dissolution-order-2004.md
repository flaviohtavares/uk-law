# The Rother Valley College (Dissolution) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1681

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Rother Valley College (Dissolution) Order 2004

Made

1st July 2004

Laid before Parliament

8th July 2004

Coming into force

1st August 2004

Whereas the Secretary of State for Education and Skills has received a proposal from the Learning and Skills Council for England, made in accordance with section 51 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001] (“the Act”), for the dissolution under section 27 of the Act of the further education corporation known as Rother Valley College[^f00002] (“the corporation”);

Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27 of the Act, the Secretary of State, after consulting the corporation and with the consent of the further education corporation known as Rotherham College of Arts and Technology[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Rother Valley College (Dissolution) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 1st August 2004.

**2.** On 1st August 2004 the corporation shall be dissolved and all of its property, rights and liabilities shall be transferred to Rotherham College of Arts and Technology, being a body corporate established for purposes which include the provision of educational facilities or services.

**3.** Section 26(2), (3) and (4) of the Act shall apply to any person employed by the corporation immediately before 1st August 2004 as if the references in that section —

- (a) to a person to whom that section applies were to a person so employed;
- (b) to the operative date were to 1st August 2004;
- (c) to the transferor were to the corporation; and
- (d) to the corporation were to Rotherham College of Arts and Technology.

Alan Johnson

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

1st July 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves with effect from 1st August 2004 the further education corporation established to conduct Rother Valley College. It provides for the transfer of its property, rights and liabilities to Rotherham College of Arts and Technology and secures the rights of its employees by applying section 26(2) to (4) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992.
