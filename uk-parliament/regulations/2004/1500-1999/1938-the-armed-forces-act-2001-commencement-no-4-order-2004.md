# The Armed Forces Act 2001 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1938 (C. 83 )

DEFENCE

The Armed Forces Act 2001 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2004

Made

21st July 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 39(2) of the Armed Forces Act 2001[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 2001 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2004.

**2.** Section 20 of the Armed Forces Act 2001 shall come into force on 22nd July 2004.

Ivor Caplin

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State Ministry of Defence

21st July 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 20 of the Armed Forces Act 2001. The Armed Forces Discipline Act [2000 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/4) amended the Army Act [1955 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1955/18), the Air Force Act [1955 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1955/19) and the Naval Discipline Act [1957 (c. 53)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1957/53) so as to establish summary appeal courts to hear appeals from the armed forces' systems of summary discipline. Section 20 of the 2001 Act enables the Secretary of State to provide by order that warrant officers are to be eligible for membership of those courts.
