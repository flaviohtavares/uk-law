# The Chiropractors Act 1994 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1521(C. 62 )

CHIROPRACTORS

The Chiropractors Act 1994 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2004

Made

21st June 2004

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 44(3) of the Chiropractors Act 1994[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Chiropractors Act 1994 (Commencement No.7) Order 2004.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 17 of the Chiropractors Act 1994 (post registration training) is 1<sup>st</sup> July 2004.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

John Hutton

Minister of State,

Department of Health

21st June 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on 1<sup>st</sup> July 2004, section 17 of the Chiropractors Act 1994 which gives the General Chiropractic Council power to make rules requiring registered chiropractors to undertake post registration training.

(This note is not part of the Order)
