# The M11 London to Cambridge Motorway (Stansted Airport Spur Roads and Connecting Roads at Birchanger) Scheme 1985 Revocation Scheme 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 446

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The M11 London to Cambridge Motorway (Stansted Airport Spur Roads and Connecting Roads at Birchanger) Scheme 1985 Revocation Scheme 2004

Made

20th February 2004

Coming into force

15th March 2004

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this scheme in exercise of powers conferred by sections 16, 17, 19 and 326 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

**1.** This scheme may be cited as the M11 London to Cambridge Motorway (Stansted Airport Spur Roads and Connecting Roads at Birchanger) Scheme 1985 Revocation Scheme 2004 and shall come into force on 15th March 2004.

**2.** The M11 London to Cambridge Motorway (Stansted Airport Spur Roads and Connecting Roads at Birchanger) Scheme 1985[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

P Wallwork

A Divisional Director Highways Agency

20th February 2004
