# The Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 83 (C.3)

NORTHERN IRELAND

CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

The Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004

Made

6th January 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 12(2) of the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004.

**2.** Sections 2 to 11 of the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003 shall come into force on 7th January 2004.

Paul Murphy

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

6th January 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 2 to 11 of the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act [2003 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/25) on 7th January 2004, when the whole of this Act will be in force.

(This note is not part of the Order)
