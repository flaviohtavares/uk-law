# The Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 76

MAGISTRATES' COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made

13th January 2004

Laid before Parliament

16th January 2004

Coming into force

6th February 2004

The Lord Chancellor in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 30B of the Justices of the Peace Act 1997[^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 6th February 2004.

## 2. Amendment to the Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Constitution) Regulations 1999

In paragraph 4 of Schedule 4 (Rates for Travelling and Subsistence Allowance) to the Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Constitution) Regulations 1999[^f00002] for “6.9p” there is substituted “20p”.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Christopher Leslie

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Constitutional Affairs

13th January 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations amend:

The Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Constitution) Regulations 1999. The effect of the amendments is to increase the rates for travel by bicycle payable to members of the Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority.

The payment rate is increased from 6.9p per mile to 20p per mile with effect from 6th February 2004.
