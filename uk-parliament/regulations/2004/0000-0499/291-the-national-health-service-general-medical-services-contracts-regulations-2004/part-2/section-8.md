# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2004, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Prescribed period under section 28D(1)(bc) of the Act

The period prescribed for the purposes of section 28D(1)(bc) of the Act (persons with whom agreements may be made)[^f00050] is six months.

## Annotations

[^f00050]: Footnote: Section 28D(1)(bc) was inserted into the Act by section 177(2) of the 2003 Act.
