# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2004, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Conditions: general

Subject to the provisions of any order made by the Secretary of State under section 176 of the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 (general medical services: transitional)[^f00041], a Primary Care Trust may only enter into a contract if the conditions set out in regulations 4 and 5 are met.
