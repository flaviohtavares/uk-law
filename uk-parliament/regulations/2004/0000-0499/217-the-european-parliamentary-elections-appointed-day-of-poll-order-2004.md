# The European Parliamentary Elections (Appointed Day of Poll) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 217

REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE

The European Parliamentary Elections (Appointed Day of Poll) Order 2004

Made

29th January 2004

Laid before Parliament

3rd February 2004

Coming into force

9th February 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 4 of the European Parliamentary Elections Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the European Parliamentary Elections (Appointed Day of Poll) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 9th February 2004.

**2.** The poll at the next general election of Members of the European Parliament shall be held on 10th June 2004.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Christopher Leslie

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Constitutional Affairs

29th January 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 10th June 2004 as the day of the poll for the next general election of MEPs.
