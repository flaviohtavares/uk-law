# The Network Rail (West Coast Main Line) Order 2004, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Saving for regulated rights etc.

Nothing in this Order shall affect any estate, right or interest granted by Network Rail in respect of a railway facility which is subject to an access contract within the meaning of Part I of the Railways Act 1993[^f00027].
