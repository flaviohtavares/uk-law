# The Greater London Authority Elections (Amendment) Rules 2004, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Rules may be cited as the Greater London Authority Elections (Amendment) Rules 2004 and shall come into force on 1st March 2004.

(2) In these Rules “the GLA Elections Rules” means the Greater London Authority Elections (No. 2) Rules 2000[^f00003].
