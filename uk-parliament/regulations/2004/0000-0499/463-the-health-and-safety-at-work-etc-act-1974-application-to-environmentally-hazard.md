# The Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 463

HEALTH AND SAFETY

The Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made

26th February 2004

Laid before Parliament

3rd March 2004

Coming into force

25th March 2004

The Secretary of State, being the Minister designated[^f00001] for the purpose of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to the transport of dangerous or environmentally hazardous goods by road, rail or water, in the exercise of the powers conferred upon him by the said section 2(2)[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Safety etc. Act 1974 (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 25th March 2004.

(2) These Regulations do not extend to Northern Ireland.

## 2. Amendment to the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) Regulations 2002

(1) The Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) Regulations 2002[^f00004] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation), in the definition of “the Directives”—

- (a) after sub-paragraph (f) insert—

  > (fa) Directive [1999/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0045) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the approximation of the laws, regulations and administrative provisions of the Member States relating to the classification, packaging and labelling of dangerous preparations[^f00005];

  ;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (h) insert—

  > (ha) Directive [2000/18/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0018) of the European Parliament and of the Council on minimum examination requirements for safety advisers for the transport of dangerous goods by road, rail or inland waterway[^f00006];

  ;
- (c) at the end of sub-paragraphs (k) and (l) omit the word “and”;
- (d) after sub-paragraph (l) insert—

  > (m) Commission Directive [2001/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0060) adapting to technical progress Directive [1999/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0045) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the approximation of the laws, regulations and administrative provisions of the Member States relating to the classification, packaging and labelling of dangerous preparations[^f00007];
  >
  > (n) Commission Directive [2003/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0028) adapting for the fourth time to technical progress Council Directive [94/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0055) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States with regard to the transport of dangerous goods by road[^f00008]; and
  >
  > (o) Commission Directive [2003/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0029) adapting for the fourth time to technical progress Council Directive [96/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0049) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States with regard to the transport of dangerous goods by rail[^f00009]; and

  .

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

David Jamieson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

26th February 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) Regulations 2002 (“the 2002 Regulations”) extend the reference to dangerous substances in section 1(1)(c) of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (“the 1974 Act”) to include environmentally hazardous substances. The purpose of the 2002 Regulations is to enable regulations to be made under section 15 of the 1974 Act to implement the Directives referred to in regulation 2 of the 2002 Regulations.

These Regulations amend regulation 2 of the 2002 Regulations to add references to Directives [1999/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0045) and [2000/18/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0018) and Commission Directives [2003/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0028) and [2003/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0029) so that Regulations can be made under section 15 of the 1974 Act to implement these Directives.
