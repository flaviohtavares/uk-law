(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, made under section 50(3) of the International Criminal Court Act 2001, set out the Elements of Crimes adopted by the Assembly of States Parties on 9th September 2002 in accordance with Article 9(1) of the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court. In accordance with section 50(2) of the Act these shall be taken into account by a court considering offences under domestic law in Part 5 of the Act. These Regulations revoke and replace The International Criminal Court Act 2001 (Elements of Crimes) Regulations 2004 which were defective.
