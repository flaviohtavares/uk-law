# The Education (School Performance Targets) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [SI 2004/2858](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2858) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2004 No. 3323

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (School Performance Targets) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made

14th December 2004

Laid before Parliament

21st December 2004

Coming into force

22nd December 2004

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by sections 19 and 54(3) of the Education Act 1997[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (School Performance Targets) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 22nd December 2004.

(2) These Regulations apply only in relation to schools in England.

## 2. Amendment of Regulations

The Education (School Performance Targets) (England) Regulations[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with regulation 3.

**3.** At the end of regulation 4(2) insert the words “in connection with the performance of the relevant group of third key stage pupils in NC tests or teacher assessments to be administered at or near the end of the following school year”.

Stephen Twigg

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Education & Skills

14th December 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made to correct a defect in the Education (School Performance Targets) (England) Regulations 2004 ([SI 2004/2858](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2858)).

These Regulations amend the earlier Regulations by inserting words erroneously omitted from regulation 4(2). The words make clear that targets in respect of pupils taking the Key Stage 3 National Curriculum tests and teacher assessments are to be set by schools in the school year before the pupils are due to take the tests.

These Regulations will be issued free of charge to all known recipients of S.I. [2004/2858](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2858).
