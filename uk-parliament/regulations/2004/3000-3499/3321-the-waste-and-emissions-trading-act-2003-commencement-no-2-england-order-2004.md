# The Waste and Emissions Trading Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) (England) Order 2004

> Text as at 2005-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

2004 No. 3321 (C.153)

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION, ENGLAND

The Waste and Emissions Trading Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) (England) Order 2004

Made

13th December 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 40(7)(a) of the Waste and Emissions Trading Act 2003[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Waste And Emissions Trading Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) (England) Order 2004.

## 2. Commencement

As regards England, section 35(a) of the Waste and Emissions Trading Act 2003[^f00002] shall come into force on 1st January 2005.

Elliott Morley

Minister of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

13th December 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st January 2005 the provisions of section 35(a) of the Waste and Emissions Trading Act 2003 (“the Act”) in relation to England.

Part 1 of the Act makes provision, among other things, for waste management in England and for miscellaneous and supplementary matters.

Section 35(a) repeals section 49 of the Environmental Protection Act [1990 (c. 43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43) (collection authorities' waste recycling plans).

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
