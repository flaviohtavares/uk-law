# The Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004 (Commencement) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 3235 (C.143)

CHRISTMAS DAY TRADING

The Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004 (Commencement) Order 2004

Made

8th December 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 6(3) of the Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004 (Commencement) Order 2004.

**2.** 9th December 2004 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004.

Gerry Sutcliffe,

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations, Postal Services and Consumers

Department of Trade and Industry

8th December 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 9th December 2004 the Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004.
