# The Freedom of Information Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 3122 (C. 131)

FREEDOM OF INFORMATION

The Freedom of Information Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2004

Made

26th November 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 87(3) of the Freedom of Information Act 2000[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2004[^f00002].

(2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Freedom of Information Act 2000.

**2.** The provisions of the Act shall, in so far as they are not already in force, come into force on 1st January 2005.

Falconer of Thoroton

Secretary of State

Department for Constitutional Affairs

26th November 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st January 2005 those provisions of the Freedom of Information Act 2000 that are not already in force.

(This note is not part of the Order)
