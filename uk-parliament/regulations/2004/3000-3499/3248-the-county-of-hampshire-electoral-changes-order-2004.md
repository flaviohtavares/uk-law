# The County of Hampshire (Electoral Changes) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 3248

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The County of Hampshire (Electoral Changes) Order 2004

Made

9th December 2004

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2) and 1(3)

Whereas the Boundary Committee for England[^f00001], acting pursuant to section 15(4) of the Local Government Act 1992[^f00002], has submitted to the Electoral Commission[^f00003] recommendations dated July 2004 on its review of the county of Hampshire:

And whereas the Electoral Commission have decided to give effect, with modifications, to those recommendations:

And whereas a period of not less than six weeks has expired since the receipt of those recommendations:

Now, therefore, the Electoral Commission, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 17[^f00004] and 26[^f00005] of the Local Government Act 1992, and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the County of Hampshire (Electoral Changes) Order 2004.

(2) This Order, with the exception of articles 4 and 5, shall come into force –

- (a) for the purpose of proceedings preliminary or relating to any election to be held on the ordinary day of election of councillors in 2005, on the day after that on which it is made;
- (b) for all other purposes, on the ordinary day of election of councillors in 2005.

(3) Articles 4 and 5 shall come into force –

- (a) for the purpose of proceedings preliminary or relating to the election of a parish councillor for the parishes of Bishopstoke and Crondall to be held on the ordinary day of elections in 2007, on 15th October 2006;
- (b) for all other purposes, on the ordinary day of elections in 2007.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order –

- “county” means the county of Hampshire;
- “existing”, in relation to an electoral division or ward, means the electoral division or ward as it exists on the date this Order is made;
- references to wards, unless the context otherwise requires, are references to district, borough and city wards in the county[^f00006];
- any reference to the map is a reference to the map marked “Map referred to in the County of Hampshire (Electoral Changes) Order 2004”, of which prints are available for inspection at –
  - (a) the principal office of the Electoral Commission; and
  - (b) the offices of Hampshire County Council; and

  any reference to a numbered sheet is a reference to the sheet of the map which bears that number.

## 3. Electoral divisions of the county of Hampshire

(1) The existing electoral divisions of the county[^f00007] shall be abolished.

(2) The county shall be divided into seventy-five electoral divisions which shall bear the names set out in column (1) of Schedule 1.

(3) The electoral divisions shall comprise the areas described in column (2) of that Schedule by reference to wards, parishes, or parish wards or, where an area is not so described, the area designated on the map by reference to the name of the electoral division and demarcated by red lines; and the number of councillors to be elected for each electoral division shall be the number specified in respect of the electoral division in column (3) of that Schedule.

(4) Where a boundary is shown on the map as running along a road, railway line, footway, watercourse or similar geographical feature, it shall be treated as running along the centre line of the feature.

## 4. Wards of the parish of Bishopstoke

(1) The existing wards of the parish of Bishopstoke shall be abolished.

(2) The parish shall be divided into three parish wards which shall bear the names Bishopstoke Central, Bishopstoke East, and Bishopstoke North West; and the wards shall comprise the areas designated on sheet 3 by reference to the name of the ward and demarcated by orange lines.

(3) The number of councillors to be elected for the Bishopstoke East parish ward shall be six, and for each of the Bishopstoke Central and Bishopstoke North West parish wards shall be four.

## 5. Wards of the parish of Crondall

The parish of Crondall shall be divided into two parish wards which shall bear the names set out in column (1) of Schedule 2; each parish ward shall comprise the area of the electoral division specified in respect of the parish ward in column (2) of that Schedule, and the number of councillors to be elected for each parish ward shall be the number specified in respect of the parish ward in column (3) of that Schedule.

## 6. Maps

Hampshire County Council shall make a print of the map marked “Map referred to in the County of Hampshire (Electoral Changes) Order 2004” available for inspection at its offices by any member of the public at any reasonable time.

## 7. Electoral registers

The Electoral Registration Officer[^f00008] for each district, borough or city in the county shall make such rearrangement of, or adaptation of, the register of local government electors as may be necessary for the purposes of, and in consequence of, this Order.

## 8. Revocation

The County of Hampshire (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1981[^f00009] is revoked, save for article 5.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order gives effect, with modifications, to recommendations by the Boundary Committee for England, a committee of the Electoral Commission, for electoral changes in the county of Hampshire.

The modifications concern the names of Bedhampton and Leigh Park, Bordon, Whitehill and Lindford, Bridgemary, Hamble, Petersfield Butser, Petersfield Hangers, South Waterside, and Yateley East, Blackwater and Ancells electoral divisions, and the boundary between Basingstoke North West, and Calleva and Kingsclere electoral divisions.

The changes have effect in relation to local government elections to be held on and after the ordinary day of election of councillors in 2005.

Article 3 abolishes the existing electoral divisions of the county and provides for the creation of 75 new electoral divisions. That article and Schedule 1 also make provision for the names and areas of, and numbers of councillors for, the new electoral divisions.

Articles 4 and 5 make electoral changes in the parishes of Bishopstoke and Crondall.

Article 7 obliges the Electoral Registration Officer in each district, borough or city in the county of Hampshire to make any necessary amendments to the electoral register to reflect the new electoral arrangements.

Article 8 revokes the County of Hampshire (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1981, with the exception of article 5.

The areas of the new electoral divisions and of the new parish wards of the parish of Bishopstoke are demarcated on the map described in article 2. Prints of the map may be inspected at all reasonable times at the offices of Hampshire County Council and at the principal office of the Electoral Commission at Trevelyan House, Great Peter Street, London SW1P 2HW.
