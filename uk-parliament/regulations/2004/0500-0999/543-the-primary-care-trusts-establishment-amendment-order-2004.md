# The Primary Care Trusts (Establishment) Amendment Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made to correct errors in certain Statutory Instruments which establish Primary Care Trusts and which are listed in column 1 of the Schedule and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of those Statutory Instruments.

2004 No.543

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Primary Care Trusts (Establishment) Amendment Order 2004

Made

29th February 2004

Coming into force

10th March 2004

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 16A(1) and (2) and 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Primary Care Trusts (Establishment) Amendment Order 2004 and shall come into force on 10th March 2004.

(2) This Order applies to England.

## 2. Amendment of Primary Care Trust Establishment Orders

The Schedule (area of trust) to each of the Primary Care Trust Establishment Orders listed in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order shall be amended as specified in the corresponding entry in column 2.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Hutton

Minister of State

Department of Health

29th February 2004
