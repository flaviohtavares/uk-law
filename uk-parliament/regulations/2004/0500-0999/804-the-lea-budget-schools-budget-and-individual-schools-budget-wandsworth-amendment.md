# The LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (Wandsworth) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 804

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (Wandsworth) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made

18th March 2004

Laid before Parliament

18th March 2004

Coming into force

31st March 2004

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 45A and 138(7) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement, and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (Wandsworth) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 31st March 2004.

(2) These Regulations amend the LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (England) Regulations 2003 [^f00002] (“the Regulations”) in relation to Wandsworth London Borough Council, and apply in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2004.

## 2. Amendment

In respect of Wandsworth London Borough Council the words “plus £316,000” are added after the formula in paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Regulations.

David Miliband

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

18th March 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (England) Regulations 2003 (which were amended for other purposes by [SI 2004/659](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/659)) in respect of Wandsworth London Borough Council to add a cash figure to the calculation required to be made in respect of the limit on the expenditure which may be deducted from the schools budget.
