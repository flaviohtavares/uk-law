# The General Medical Services and Personal Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions Order 2004, section 107

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 107. Continuing validity of forms

Notwithstanding the amendments made by paragraphs 1(3) and 5 of Schedule 1 to the form of certificates set out in Part 2 of Schedule 2 to the Social Security (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1976[^f00085] and in Part 2 of the Schedule to the Statutory Maternity Pay (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1987[^f00086], a form which complies with those Regulations as in force on 31st March 2004 shall continue to be valid.
