# The General Medical Services and Personal Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions Order 2004, section 109

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 109. Transitory interpretation of references to general medical services contracts

(1) For as long as default contracts entered into pursuant to section 176(3) of the 2003 Act (general medical services: transitional) exist, any reference to a general medical services contract or to a contract under section 28Q of the 1977 Act in the enactments listed in paragraph (2) shall be deemed to include a reference to a default contract.

(2) The enactments referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) the 1977 Act, sections 3(4)(b)[^f00087], 26(2)(b) and (4)(aa)[^f00088], 28D(1)(bc)(i)[^f00089], 45A(3), (4) and (11)[^f00090], 54(1)(c)[^f00091], 72(5)(d)[^f00092];
- (b) the Community Health Councils (Access to Information) Act 1988[^f00093], section 1(6), in the paragraph 6B inserted into Schedule 12A to the Local Government Act 1972[^f00094];
- (c) the Access to Health Records Act 1990[^f00095], section 1(2)(a);
- (d) the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992[^f00096], section 279(2);
- (e) the Health Service Commissioners Act 1993, section 2A(1)(a) and (2)(a)[^f00097];
- (f) the Employment Rights Act 1996, section 43K(1)(ba)[^f00098];
- (g) the Health and Social Care Act 2001, Schedule 1, paragraph 11(a)[^f00099].
