# The General Medical Services and Personal Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions Order 2004, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Terms of general medical services contracts, default contracts and personal medical services agreements

Unless the contract or agreement is of a type or nature to which a particular article does not apply—

- (a) a general medical services contract shall include or be deemed to include terms which have the effect specified in articles 74, 76, 78, 79, 81, 82, 84, 86, 87, 88, 91 and 92;
- (b) a default contract shall include or be deemed to include terms which have the effect specified in articles 74, 76, 78, 79, 81, 84, 86 and 92;
- (c) an agreement shall include or be deemed to include terms which have the effect specified in articles 75, 77, 78, 80, 83, 85 and 89.
