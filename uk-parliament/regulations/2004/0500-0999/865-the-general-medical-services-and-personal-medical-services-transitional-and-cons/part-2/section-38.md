# The General Medical Services and Personal Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions Order 2004, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Entry on to medical performers list of persons approved under regulation 18A or 18B of the 1992 Regulations

Where a Primary Care Trust—

- (a) had, on 31st March 2004, approved a medical practitioner under regulation 18A or 18B of the 1992 Regulations[^f00054] but had not yet entered his name on its medical list in accordance with regulation 18F(1) of those Regulations[^f00055]; and
- (b) intends to enter into a default contract or general medical services contract with two or more individuals practising in partnership one of whom is the person so approved,

it shall add that person’s name to its medical performers list unless he is already on the medical performers list of another Primary Care Trust and is not withdrawing from that list.
