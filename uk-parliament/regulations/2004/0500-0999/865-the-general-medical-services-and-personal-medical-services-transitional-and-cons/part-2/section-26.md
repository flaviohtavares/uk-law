# The General Medical Services and Personal Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions Order 2004, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Inquiries about prescriptions and referrals

Where, on or before 31st March 2004, a relevant medical practitioner had received an inquiry about prescriptions or referrals from a Primary Care Trust under paragraph 49 of Schedule 2 to the 1992 Regulations[^f00044] but had not yet responded to that inquiry, the succeeding contractor shall respond as if the inquiry had been made under—

- (a) the term of its general medical services contract which gives effect to paragraph 79 of Schedule 6 to the 2004 Regulations; or
- (b) the equivalent term of its default contract.
