# The General Medical Services and Personal Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions Order 2004, section 70

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 70. Assignment of patients

Where, on 31st March 2004, a person had applied to a Primary Care Trust for assignment to a medical practitioner under regulation 4 of the Choice Regulations[^f00070] but that application had not yet been determined, the Primary Care Trust shall—

- (a) require a contractor to assign the person to a medical practitioner who performs personal medical services under a transitional agreement in accordance with article 64(1);
- (b) assign the patient in accordance with the terms of the contract, to—
  - (i) a default contractor,
  - (ii) a general medical services contractor, or
  - (iii) a person who is a party to a personal medical services agreement; or
- (c) otherwise make arrangements for the applicant to be provided with essential services (or their equivalent) in the area of the Primary Care Trust.
