# The General Medical Services and Personal Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions Order 2004, section 112

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 112. Saving of section 279 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992

Notwithstanding the coming into force of the amendments to the definition of worker in section 279 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992[^f00104] (health service practitioners) made by paragraph 59 of Schedule 11 to the 2003 Act[^f00105], in relation to any complaint arising in respect of a matter which occurred before 1st April 2004, section 279 shall have effect as if those amendments had not been brought into force.

## Annotations

[^f00104]: Footnote: [1992 c. 52](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/52). Section 279 was amended by the Health Authorities Act [1995 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/17), Schedule 1, paragraph 122, the National Health Service (Primary Care) Act [1997 (c. 46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/46), Schedule 2, paragraph 67, the Health and Social Care Act [2001 (c. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/15), Schedule 5, paragraph 9, the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act [2002 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/17), Schedule 2, paragraph 60 and Schedule 3, paragraph 13 and the 2003 Act, Schedule 11, paragraph 59.

[^f00105]: Footnote: Paragraph 59 of Schedule 11 was commenced on 1st April 2004 by article 5(2)(v) of the 2003 Act commencement order, subject to the transitional provision in article 7(9) of that Order.
