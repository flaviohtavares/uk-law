# The General Medical Services and Personal Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions Order 2004, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Dispute resolution

(1) Any dispute relating to a pilot scheme that has been referred to the Secretary of State under section 4 of the 1990 Act[^f00068] ( NHS contracts) and which has not been determined on or before 31st March 2004 shall continue to be dealt with in accordance with the National Health Service Contracts (Dispute Resolution) Regulations 1996[^f00069].

(2) Where a direction as to payment is made (whether before or after 31st March 2004) under section 4(7) of the 1990 Act against, or in favour of, a provider of services under a transitional agreement or a pilot scheme, the direction is enforceable in a county court (if the court so orders) as if it were a judgement or order of that court.
