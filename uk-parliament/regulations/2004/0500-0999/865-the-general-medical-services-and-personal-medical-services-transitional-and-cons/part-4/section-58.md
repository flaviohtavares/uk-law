# The General Medical Services and Personal Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions Order 2004, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Existing pilot schemes

Any pilot scheme under which personal medical services were being provided on 31st March 2004 shall, on the coming into force of section 178(1) of the 2003 Act (abolition of pilot schemes) in relation to personal medical services, be deemed to have been made under section 28C of the 1977 Act[^f00063] and accordingly shall not cease to have effect merely because of the repeal of Part 1 of the 1997 Act in relation to personal medical services.
