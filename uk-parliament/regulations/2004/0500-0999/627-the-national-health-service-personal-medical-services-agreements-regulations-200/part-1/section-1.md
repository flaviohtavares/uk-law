# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2004, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 1st April 2004.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to England only[^f00003].

## Annotations

[^f00003]: Footnote: As regards Wales, the functions of the Secretary of State under sections 28D, 28E and 126(4) of the 1977 Act and section 4 of the 1990 Act were transferred to the National Assembly for Wales under S.I. [1999/672](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/672), article 2 and Schedule 1, as amended by the 1999 Act, section 66(5) and as read with section 40(1) of the 2002 Act.
