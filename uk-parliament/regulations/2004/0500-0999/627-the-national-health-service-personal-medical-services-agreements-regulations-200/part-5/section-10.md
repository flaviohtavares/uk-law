# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2004, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Health service contract

If the contractor is to be regarded as a health service body, the agreement must state that the agreement is an NHS contract.
