# The Travel Documents (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 579

FEES AND CHARGES

The Travel Documents (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made

4th March 2004

Laid before Parliament

10th March 2004

Coming into force

1st April 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 27 and 166 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00001] and section 56 of the Finance Act 1973[^f00002], and with the approval of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Travel Documents (Fees) Amendment Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 1st April 2004.

**2.** The Travel Documents (Fees) Regulations 1999[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting the sum of £42 for the sum of £28 in regulation 3(1).

Beverley Hughes

Minister of State

Home Office

2nd March 2004

We approve,

Nick Ainger

John Heppell

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

4th March 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Travel Documents (Fees) Regulations 1999 so as to increase the fee for a Convention travel document or a document of identity from £28 to £42. This increase is in line with the increase in the fee for a standard adult passport which came into force on 2nd October 2003.

Convention travel documents are issued in accordance with either Article 28 of the Convention relating to the Status of Refugees adopted on 28th July 1951 or Article 28 of the Convention relating to the Status of Stateless Persons adopted on 28th September 1954. Documents of identity are issued at the discretion of the Secretary of State to any foreign national in the United Kingdom and enable the holder to make one journey out of the United Kingdom.
