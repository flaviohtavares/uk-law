# The Police Authorities (Best Value) Performance Indicators Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 644

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

POLICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Police Authorities (Best Value) Performance Indicators Order 2004

Made

7th March 2004

Laid before Parliament

11th March 2004

Coming into force

1st April 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 4(1)(a) of the Local Government Act 1999[^f00001] (having regard to the definition in section 1(1)(d)), and having consulted, in accordance with section 4(3) of that Act, persons appearing to him to represent police authorities and such other persons as he thought fit, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Police Authorities (Best Value) Performance Indicators Order 2004 and shall come into force on 1st April 2004.

## 2. Performance indicators

The performance indicators by reference to which the performance of each police authority in England and Wales can be measured in exercising their functions are hereby specified in—

- (a) Schedule 1 (citizen focus);
- (b) Schedule 2 (reducing crime);
- (c) Schedule 3 (investigating crime);
- (d) Schedule 4 (promoting public safety); and
- (e) Schedule 5 (resource use).

## 3. Amendment of the Local Government (Best Value) Performance Indicators and Performance Standards Order 2003

For article 2 of the Local Government (Best Value) Performance Indicators and Performance Standards Order 2003[^f00002] there is substituted the following article—

> **2. Application**
>
> This Order applies—
>
> - (a) in relation to England, to all best value authorities except authorities falling within section 1(1)(d) of the Act (police authorities); and
> - (b) in relation to Wales, only to authorities falling within section 1(1)(e) of the Act (fire authorities).

## 4. Revocations

(1) The following Orders are hereby revoked—

- (a) the Police Authorities (Best Value) Performance Indicators Order 2003[^f00003]; and
- (b) the Police Authorities (Best Value) Performance Indicators (Amendment) Order 2003[^f00004].

Hazel Blears

Minister of State

Home Office

(This note is not part of the Order)

Part I of the Local Government Act 1999 imposes requirements on police authorities, and other authorities, to secure continuous improvement in the way in which their functions are exercised, having regard to a combination of economy, efficiency and effectiveness. Section 4(1) of that Act confers a power of the Secretary of State to specify by Order best value performance indicators applicable to local authorities, police authorities and other authorities.

Article 2 of this Order specifies that the performance indicators set out in Schedules 1 to 5 of this Order are to be the indicators by which a police authority’s performance can be measured.

Article 3 of this Order removes police authorities in England and Wales from the list of best value authorities to which the Local Government (Best Value) Performance Indicators and Performance Standards Order 2003 (S.I. [2003/530](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/530)) applies.

Article 4 of this Order revokes the Police Authorities (Best Value) Performance Indicators Order 2003 and the Police Authorities (Best Value) Performance Indicators (Amendment) Order 2003.
