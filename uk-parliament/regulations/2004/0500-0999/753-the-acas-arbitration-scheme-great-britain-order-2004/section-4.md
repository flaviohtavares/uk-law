# The ACAS Arbitration Scheme (Great Britain) Order 2004, section 4

> Text as at 2014-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4. Application of Part I of the Arbitration Act 1996[^c-aa9b998d97cbe0d1d33107f59fea96d1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The provisions of Part I of the Arbitration Act 1996[^f00004] referred to in the Schedule at paragraphs 52EW, 110EW, 183EW, 187EW, 194EW, 200EW, 205EW, 209EW, 212EW, 217EW, 223EW and 224EW and shown in italics shall, as modified in those paragraphs, apply to English/Welsh arbitrations conducted in accordance with the Scheme.
