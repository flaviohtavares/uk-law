# The Docklands Light Railway (Woolwich Arsenal Extension) Order 2004, section 52

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Certification of plans, etc.

*Extent: E+W*

DLRL shall, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the book of reference, the deposited plans, the deposited sections and the open space and exchange land plan to the Secretary of State for certification that they are true copies of, respectively, the book of reference, the deposited plans, the deposited sections and the open space and exchange land plan referred to in this Order, and a document so certified shall be admissible in any proceedings, as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
