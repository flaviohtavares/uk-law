Whereas the School Teachers' Review Body[^f00001] have made a report following the reference to them of certain matters;

And whereas the Secretary of State has consulted, in accordance with section 126 of the Education Act 2002[^f00002], such associations of local education authorities, such bodies representing the interests of governors of foundation, voluntary and foundation special schools and such bodies representing school teachers as appeared to him to be concerned:

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 122(1), 123(1) and (4), 124(1) and (3), 125 and 210(7) of the Education Act 2002, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 1st April 2004, except for—

- (a) article 2(dd) and Part 1 of the Schedule, which shall come into force on 1st April 2005; and
- (b) article 2(ee) and Part 2 of the Schedule, which shall come into force on 1st September 2005.

(2) This Order applies to school teachers within the meaning of section 122 of the Education Act 2002 in England and Wales.

  > **41A.1. Determination of applicable pay scale**
  >
  > A teacher who normally works in the London Area but who is temporarily employed elsewhere shall continue to be paid in accordance with the pay scale applicable to his normal area of employment.
  >
  > **41A.2.** A teacher who on 31st March 2004 was being paid in accordance with the Inner London pay scale or who was receiving a London Area allowance shall continue to be paid in accordance with the applicable London Area pay scale notwithstanding that he would not otherwise be entitled to it under the provisions of this Document, for so long as he remains in the post that he held on that date.
  >
  > **41A.3.1.** A teacher who moves out of the Inner London Area may continue to be paid in accordance with the Inner London pay scale at the rate being paid before he moved for so long as he continues to be employed as a teacher in a post in which his salary is paid by the same authority.
  >
  > **41A.3.2.** A teacher who moves out of the Outer London Area, to anywhere but the Inner London Area, may continue to be paid on the Outer London Area scale at the rate being paid before he moved for so long as he continues to be employed as a teacher in a post in which his salary is paid by the same authority.
  >
  > **41A.3.3.** A teacher who moves out of the Fringe Area, to anywhere but the Inner London Area or the Outer London Area, may continue to be paid on the Fringe Area scale at the rate being paid before he moved for so long as he continues to be employed as a teacher in a post in which his salary is paid by the same authority.

This Order amends the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2003 (“the Document”) by up-rating the amounts of salary and certain allowances and by replacing the Outer London and Fringe Area allowances with new Outer London and Fringe Area pay scales. New tables that include provision for the Outer London and Fringe Areas replace the following—

- (a) the leadership group pay spine set out in paragraph 4.4 of the Document;
- (b) the head teachers' groups' pay ranges set out in paragraph 6.3 of the Document;
- (c) the pay scale for classroom teachers set out in paragraph 15.3 of the Document;
- (d) the pay scale for post-threshold teachers set out in paragraph 17.2 of the Document;
- (e) the pay spine for advanced skills teacher set out in paragraph 28.1 of the Document; and
- (f) the pay scale for unqualified teachers set out in paragraph 36.1 of the Document.

The value of the special educational needs allowances set out in paragraph 25.1 of the Document is increased.

The first increase takes effect from 1st April 2004. Subsequent increases, for which the Schedule makes provision, take effect from 1st April 2005 and 1st September 2005.

The Order abolishes recruitment and retention allowances (formerly paid under paragraph 24 of the Document, now omitted) and amends the basis on which recruitment and retention incentives and benefits (collectively referred to as “allowances”) can be awarded under paragraph 48 of the Document, so that such allowances can only be awarded either as a ‘one-off’ or for a fixed period of up to three years. Recruitment incentives cannot be renewed. Fixed term allowances awarded prior to the date of this Order are preserved until the long stop date of 31st March 2007. Permanent allowances or ones that were awarded for as long as the teacher remained in a particular post cease to have effect from 1st April 2004.

Management allowances must henceforth be awarded for a fixed period not exceeding one year, although allowances awarded prior to 1st April may be retained for the period for which they were originally awarded even if that was for a fixed period exceeding a year or while the teacher continues in the same post.

The eligibility criteria for fast track assessment in Annex 3 are amended to enable teachers who are entitled to be paid at point M6 on the main pay scale to be included in the scheme.
