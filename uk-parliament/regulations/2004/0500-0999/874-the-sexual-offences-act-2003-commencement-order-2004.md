# The Sexual Offences Act 2003 (Commencement) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 874 (C.38)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Sexual Offences Act 2003 (Commencement) Order 2004

Made

23rd March 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 141(1) of the Sexual Offences Act 2003 [^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Sexual Offences Act 2003 (Commencement) Order 2004.

(2) This Order does not extend to Scotland.

## 2. Commencement

The Sexual Offences Act 2003 shall come into force on 1st May 2004 in so far as it is not already in force.

Paul Goggins

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State Home Office

23rd March 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the provisions of the Sexual Offences Act 2003 that extend to England, Wales and Northern Ireland into force on 1st May 2004 in so far as they are not already in force. Sections 138 and 141 to 143 came into force on Royal Assent.
