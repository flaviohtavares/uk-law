# The Police Reform Act 2002 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 636 (C.23)

POLICE

The Police Reform Act 2002 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2004

Made

8th March 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 108(2) of the Police Reform Act 2002[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Police Reform Act 2002 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2004.

**2.** Section 79 of the Police Reform Act 2002 shall come into force on 9th March 2004.

Ivor Caplin

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State Ministry of Defence

8th March 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 79 of the Police Reform Act 2002 on 9th March 2004.

(This note is not part of the Order)
