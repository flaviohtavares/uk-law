# The Tribunals and Inquiries (Dairy Produce Quota Tribunal) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 594

TRIBUNALS AND INQUIRIES, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Tribunals and Inquiries (Dairy Produce Quota Tribunal) Order 2004

Made

4th March 2004

Laid before Parliament

9th March 2004

Coming into force

31st March 2004

The Lord Chancellor and the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 13(1) and 13(5)(a) of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00001] hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Tribunals and Inquiries (Dairy Produce Quota Tribunal) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 31st March 2004.

**2.** In Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992, for paragraph 13 substitute—

> **13.** The Dairy Produce Quota Tribunal for England and Wales reconstituted under regulation 7(1)(a) of the Dairy Produce Quotas (General Provisions) Regulations 2002[^f00002].

.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Filkin

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Constitutional Affairs

25th February 2004

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

Whitty

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

4th March 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Dairy Produce Quotas (General Provisions) Regulations 2002 provide in particular for the reconstitution of and set out the procedure of, the Dairy Produce Quota Tribunals (regulation 7 and the Schedule).

This Order brings the Dairy Produce Quota Tribunal for England and Wales, under the supervision of the Council on Tribunals.
