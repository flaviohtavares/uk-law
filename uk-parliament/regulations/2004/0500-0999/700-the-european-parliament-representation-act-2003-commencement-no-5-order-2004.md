# The European Parliament (Representation) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 700 (C. 30)

REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE

The European Parliament (Representation) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2004

Made

10th March 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 28(3) and (4) of the European Parliament (Representation) Act 2003[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the European Parliament (Representation) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2004.

## 2. Commencement of provisions in European Parliament (Representation) Act 2003

The following provisions of the European Parliament (Representation) Act 2003 shall come into force on 10th March 2004—

- (a) in the United Kingdom and Gibraltar, section 19 (so far as not already in force and except as it relates to section 8 of the European Parliamentary Elections Act 2002[^f00002]);
- (b) in Gibraltar—
  - (i) Part 1 (so far as not already in force); and
  - (ii) section 28(2) so far as it relates to the provisions brought into force by paragraph (a) and subparagraph (i).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs

Christopher Leslie

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Constitutional Affairs

10th March 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 10th March 2004 the extension of new sections 1A of and Schedule 1A to the European Parliamentary Elections Act 2002 to Gibraltar. It also commences, for the purposes of Gibraltar, the remaining provisions of Part 1 of the European Parliament (Representation) Act 2003 that relate to changes in the total number of United Kingdom MEPs.
