# The Good Laboratory Practice (Codification Amendments Etc.) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 994

HEALTH AND SAFETY

The Good Laboratory Practice (Codification Amendments Etc.) Regulations 2004

Made

30th March 2004

Laid before Parliament

6th April 2004

Coming into force

27th April 2004

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to good laboratory practice, in exercise of the powers conferred by the said section 2(2)[^f00003], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Good Laboratory Practice (Codification Amendments Etc.) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 27th April 2004.

## 2. Amendment of the Good Laboratory Practice Regulations 1999

The Good Laboratory Practice Regulations 1999[^f00004] are amended as follows:—

- (a) in regulation 2 (interpretation), in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) for the definition of “principles of good laboratory practice” there is substituted the following definition—

    > - “principles of good laboratory practice” means—
    >   - (a) the principles of good laboratory practice set out in Schedule 1, which are based on the Good Laboratory Practice Principles set out in Section II of Annex I to the European Parliament and Council Directive [2004/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0010)[^f00005] on the harmonisation of laws, regulations and administrative provisions relating to the application of the principles of good laboratory practice and the verification of their applications for tests on chemical substances; read with
    >   - (b) the revised guidance for the conduct of test facility inspections and study audits set out in Schedule 2, which is based on part of the Revised Guidance for the Conduct of Test Facility Inspections and Study Audits in Annex I to the European Parliament and Council Directive [2004/9/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0009)[^f00006] on the inspection and verification of good laboratory practice;

    ;
  - (ii) in the definition of “regulatory studies”, at the start of paragraph (c) there is inserted “in respect of which”;
- (b) in regulation 5 (prospective membership of the United Kingdom good laboratory practice compliance programme), in paragraph (4) the word “that” is omitted;
- (c) in regulation 9 (powers of entry etc.), in paragraph (6) for “the Annex to Council Directive [88/320/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0320) on the inspection and verification of good laboratory practice (GLP), as amended by Commission Directive [1999/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0012) adapting to technical progress for the second time the annex to Council Directive [88/320/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0320)” there is substituted “Annex I to the European Parliament and Council Directive [2004/9/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0009)”;
- (d) in Schedule 1 (good laboratory practice principles), in the heading for “(BASED ON SECTION II OF THE ANNEX TO COUNCIL DIRECTIVE [87/18/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0018), AS AMENDED BY COMMISSION DIRECTIVE [1999/11/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0011))” there is substituted “(BASED ON SECTION II OF ANNEX I TO THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND COUNCIL DIRECTIVE [2004/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0010))”;
- (e) in Schedule 2 (revised guidance for the conduct of test facility inspections and study audits), in the heading for “(BASED ON PART OF PART B OF THE ANNEX TO COUNCIL DIRECTIVE [88/320/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0320), AS AMENDED BY COMMISSION DIRECTIVE [1999/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0012))” there is substituted “(BASED ON PART OF PART B OF ANNEX I TO THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND COUNCIL DIRECTIVE [2004/9/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0009))”.

## 3. Amendment of the Medicines and Healthcare Products Regulatory Agency Trading Fund Order 2003

In Schedule 1 (funded operations) of the Medicines and Healthcare Products Regulatory Agency Trading Fund Order 2003[^f00007] for “Council Directive [87/18/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0018) of 18th December 1986 as amended and Council Directive [88/320/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0320) of 9th June 1988 as amended” there is substituted “the European Parliament and Council Directive [2004/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0010) of 11th February 2004 and the European Parliament and Council Directive [2004/9/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0009) of 11th February 2004”.

## 4. Amendment of the Cosmetic Products (Safety) Regulations 2003

In regulation 9 (product information) of the Cosmetic Products (Safety) Regulations 2003[^f00008], in paragraph (2) for “Article 1 of Council Directive [87/18/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0018)” there is substituted “Article 1 of the European Parliament and Council Directive [2004/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0010)”.

## 5. Amendment of the Notification of New Substances Regulations 1993

In regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Notification of New Substances Regulations 1993[^f00009], in paragraph (1) in the definition of “principles of good laboratory practice” for “the Good Laboratory Practice Regulations 1997” there is substituted “the Good Laboratory Practice Regulations 1999”.

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations make consequential amendments to the references in the Good Laboratory Practice Regulations 1999, the Medicines and Healthcare Products Regulatory Agency Trading Fund Order 2003 and the Cosmetic Products (Safety) Regulations 2003 following the adoption of the European Parliament and Council Directives [2004/9/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0009) and [2004/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0010). Those Codification Directives repealed and re-enacted Council Directive [87/18/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0018) as amended by Commission Directive [1999/11/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0011) and Council Directive [88/320/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0320) as amended by Commission Directive [1999/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0012).

The Notification of New Substances Regulations 1993 are amended to update the definition of the “principles of good laboratory practice”.

In addition these Regulations amend the Good Laboratory Practice Regulations 1999 definition of “regulatory study” to improve the clarity of the wording of the definition and make a minor amendment to Regulation 5(4) to correct a drafting error.

A Transposition Note in respect of Directives [2004/9/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0009) and [2004/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0010) has been placed in the libraries of both Houses of Parliament, and copies can be obtained from the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency, Room 16-107, Market Towers, 1 Nine Elms Lane, London SW8 5NQ. These Regulations do not impose any cost on business.
