# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 588

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2004

Made

4th March 2004

Coming into force

31st March 2004

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 9(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement, application and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 31 March 2004.

(2) This Order applies to England.

## 2. Originating capital of NHS trusts

The originating capital of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule shall be of the amount specified in the corresponding entry in column 2 of the Schedule.

John Reid

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State,

Department of Health

1st March 2004

We consent,

John Heppell

Nick Ainger

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

4th March 2004
