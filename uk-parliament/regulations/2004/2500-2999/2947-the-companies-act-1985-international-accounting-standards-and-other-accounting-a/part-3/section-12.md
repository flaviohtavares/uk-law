# The Companies Act 1985 (International Accounting Standards and Other Accounting Amendments) Regulations 2004, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2004-11-12. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2004/2947
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 12. Participating interest no longer required to be subsidiary undertaking[^c-da93fa67368a274e7bcc0496f04d83e7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In section 258(4) of the 1985 Act (parent and subsidiary undertakings)[^f00011]–

- (a) omit “ it has a participating interest in the undertaking and”, and
- (b) for paragraph (a) substitute–

  > - (a) it has the power to exercise, or actually exercises, dominant influence or control over it, or

  .

(2) The modification of section 258(4) in paragraph (1) also applies for the purposes of—

- (a) the Building Societies Act 1986[^f00012], and
- (b) the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^f00013].
