# The Companies Act 1985 (International Accounting Standards and Other Accounting Amendments) Regulations 2004, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2004-11-12. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2004/2947
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 11. Summary financial statements[^key-7e67823f5b18d4af549332c5fe217053]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the heading before section 251 of the 1985 Act (provision of summary financial statement to shareholders)[^f00010] substitute “ *Summary financial statement*”.

(2) In subsection (1) of that section–

- (a) for the words from “A public company” to “listed” substitute “A company”, and
- (b) omit the definitions of “listed” and “the official list”.
