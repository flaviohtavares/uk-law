# The Companies Act 1985 (International Accounting Standards and Other Accounting Amendments) Regulations 2004, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2004-11-12. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2004/2947
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14. Amendment of Schedules to the 1985 Act[^c-bf2ceef53402c7d124ff19633d8fdd80]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Schedule 4 to the 1985 Act (form and content of company accounts)[^f00017] is amended in accordance with Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

(2) Schedule 8 to the 1985 Act (form and content of accounts prepared by small companies)[^f00018] is amended in accordance with Schedule 3 to these Regulations.

(3) Schedule 8A to the 1985 Act (form and content of abbreviated accounts of small companies delivered to registrar)[^f00019] is amended in accordance with Schedule 4 to these Regulations.

(4) Schedule 9 to the 1985 Act (form and content of accounts of banking companies and groups)[^f00020] is amended in accordance with Schedule 5 to these Regulations.

(5) Schedule 9A to the 1985 Act (form and content of accounts of insurance companies and groups)[^f00021] is amended in accordance with Schedule 6 to these Regulations.
