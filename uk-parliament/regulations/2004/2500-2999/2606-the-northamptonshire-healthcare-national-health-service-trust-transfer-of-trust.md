# The Northamptonshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 2606

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Northamptonshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2004

Made

5th October 2004

Laid before Parliament

12th October 2004

Coming into force

2nd November 2004

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 92(1), 96(1) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to proposed changes in the arrangements for the administration of the Northamptonshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust[^f00002] and having consulted that Trust and the Daventry and South Northamptonshire Primary Care Trust[^f00003] in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Northamptonshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 2<sup>nd</sup> November 2004.

(2) In this Order—

- “the old Trust” means the Northamptonshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust;
- “the new Trust” means the Daventry and South Northamptonshire Primary Care Trust; and
- “the trust property” means the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the old Trust and the new Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 30th July 2004 and on behalf of the new Trust on 17th July 2004.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

(1) On the date that this Order comes into force the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property, shall be transferred from the old Trust to the new Trust.

(2) In any instrument relating to the trust property, any reference to the trustees of the trust property or, as the case may be, the old Trust shall be construed as a reference to the trustees of the new Trust or, as the case may be, the new Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Stephen Ladyman

Parliamentary under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

5th October 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Daventry and South Northamptonshire Primary Care Trust, trust property held by the Northamptonshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust and makes related provision. A copy of the schedule of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
