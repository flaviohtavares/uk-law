# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 4) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 2672

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 4) Order 2004

Made

13th October 2004

Coming into force

18th October 2004

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 13th day of October 2004

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No.4) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 18<sup>th</sup> October 2004.

**2.** David White is hereby appointed one of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of School in England with effect from 18<sup>th</sup> October 2004.

A.K.Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints David White to be one of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England.
