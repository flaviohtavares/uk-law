# The A435 Trunk Road (Alcester to Gorcott Hill) De-Trunking Order 1993 (Revocation) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 2675

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The A435 Trunk Road (Alcester to Gorcott Hill) De-Trunking Order 1993 (Revocation) Order 2004

Made

13th October 2004

Coming into force

10th November 2004

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A435 Trunk Road (Alcester to Gorcott Hill) De-Trunking Order 1993 (Revocation) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 10th November 2004.

**2.** The A435 Trunk Road (Alcester to Gorcott Hill) De-Trunking Order 1993 is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Graham Bowskill

A Divisional Director, Highways Agency

13th October 2004
