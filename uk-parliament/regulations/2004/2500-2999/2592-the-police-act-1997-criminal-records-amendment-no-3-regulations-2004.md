# The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 2592

POLICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2004

Made

4th October 2004

Laid before Parliament

7th October 2004

Coming into force

1st November 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 125(1) and (5) of the Police Act 1997[^f00001], and having regard to the meaning of “prescribed” in section 119(3) of that Act[^f00002],hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> November 2004.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Fees payable to police authorities for information provided to the Secretary of State

For Schedule 3 to the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) Regulations 2002[^f00003] there shall be substituted the Schedule to these Regulations.

Fiona Mactaggart

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

4th October 2004

These Regulations amend the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) Regulations 2002 by inserting a new Schedule 3 setting out revised fees payable to the police for each request for information under sections 115, 116 or 120A(4) of the Police Act 1997. The amendments have effect from 1<sup>st</sup> November 2004.
