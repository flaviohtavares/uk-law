# The Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 Commencement (No. 4) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No.2626 (C. 114 )

PUBLIC HEALTH

SOCIAL CARE

The Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 Commencement (No. 4) Order 2004

Made

9th October 2004

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 199(1) and (3) of the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 Commencement (No. 4) Order 2004.

## 2. Appointed day for provisions relating to appointments to certain health and social care bodies

19th October 2004 is the appointed day for the coming into force of sections 187 and 188 of, and Schedule 12 to, the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Rosie Winterton

Minister of State,

Department of Health

9th October 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the fourth commencement order under the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 (“the 2003 Act”).

Article 2 commences on 19th October 2004 sections 187 and 188 of, and Schedule 12 to, the 2003 Act: these provisions relate to the delegation of appointments functions by the Secretary of State and the Privy Council in relation to bodies that have functions concerning health, social care or the regulation of professions associated with health or social care.

(This note is not part of the Order)
