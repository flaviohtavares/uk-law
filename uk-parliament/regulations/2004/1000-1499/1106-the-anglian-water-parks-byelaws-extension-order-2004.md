# The Anglian Water Parks Byelaws (Extension) Order 2004

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2004-04-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2004/1106
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2004 No. 1106

WATER INDUSTRY, ENGLAND

The Anglian Water Parks Byelaws (Extension) Order 2004

Made

5th April 2004

Coming into force

13th April 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 157(5) of the Water Industry Act 1991[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Anglian Water Parks Byelaws (Extension) Order 2004, and shall come into force on 13th April 2004.

## 2. Extension of byelaws

The Anglian Water Services Ltd Water Parks Byelaws 1993[^f00002] shall continue to have effect for a period of one year beginning with 20th April 2004.

Signed on behalf of the Secretary of State,

John Roberts

A Senior Civil Servant,

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

5th April 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the Anglian Water Services Ltd Water Parks Byelaws 1993 (the “Byelaws”) to continue to have effect for a period of one year beginning with 20th April 2004.

The Byelaws continue to have effect until that date by virtue of the Anglian Water Parks Byelaws (Extension) Order 2003 ([S.I 1993/757](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/757)).
