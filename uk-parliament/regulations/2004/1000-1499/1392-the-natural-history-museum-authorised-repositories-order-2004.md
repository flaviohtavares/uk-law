# The Natural History Museum (Authorised Repositories) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1392

MUSEUMS AND GALLERIES

The Natural History Museum (Authorised Repositories) Order 2004

Made

17th May 2004

Laid before Parliament

19th May 2004

Coming into force

11th June 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(2) of the British Museum Act 1963[^f00001] and now vested in her[^f00002], with the agreement of the Trustees of the Natural History Museum, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Natural History Museum (Authorised Repositories) Order 2004 and shall come in to force on 11th June 2004.

**2.** For paragraph 4 of Part 2 of Schedule 3 to the British Museum Act 1963[^f00003] there shall be substituted the following paragraph:—

> **4.** The site in Wandsworth known as 75 Kimber Road, London SW18 4NX excluding the site known as 249 to 251 Merton Road, London SW18 5EB.

.

Estelle Morris

Minister for the Arts

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

17th May 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order alters the description of the Natural History Museum’s authorised repository in Wandsworth.
