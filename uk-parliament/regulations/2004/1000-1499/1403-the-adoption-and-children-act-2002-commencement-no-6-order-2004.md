# The Adoption and Children Act 2002 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1403 (C.56)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Adoption and Children Act 2002 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2004

Made

20th May 2004

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 148(1) of the Adoption and Children Act 2002[^f00001], after consultation with the National Assembly for Wales[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order —

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Adoption and Children Act 2002 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2004.

## 2. Appointed Day

21st May 2004 is the appointed day for the coming into force of section 118 (review of cases of looked after children) of the Adoption and Children Act 2002.

Margaret Hodge

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

20th May 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the sixth Commencement Order made under the Adoption and Children Act 2002 (“the Act”).

Article 2 brings into force on 21st May 2004 section 118 of the Act. That section amends section 26 of the Children Act 1989 (review of cases of looked after children) to provide that regulations may be made to require a local authority to review the care plan of a looked after child.
