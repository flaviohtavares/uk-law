# The Borough of Rochdale (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1073

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The Borough of Rochdale (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2004

Made

6th April 2004

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

The Electoral Commission[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 17[^f00002] and 26[^f00003] of the Local Government Act 1992, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Borough of Rochdale (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2004.

(2) This Order shall come into force on the day after that on which it is made.

## 2. Amendment to the 2004 Order

(1) The Borough of Rochdale (Electoral Changes) Order 2004[^f00004] shall be amended as follows:

(2) In article 2 “any reference to the map is a reference to the map marked “Revised (2004) Map referred to in the Borough of Rochdale (Electoral Changes) Order 2004”, of which prints are available” shall be substituted for the words from “any reference to the map” to “of which prints are available”.

(3) In article 5 “the map marked “Revised (2004) Map referred to in the Borough of Rochdale (Electoral Changes) Order 2004” available for inspection” shall be substituted for the words from “the map marked” to “available for inspection”.

Sealed with the seal of the Electoral Commission on the 6th day of April 2004

Sam Younger

Chairman of the Commission

6th April 2004

Jacqui Dixon

Secretary to the Commission

6th April 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order substitutes a new map for that originally referred to in the Borough of Rochdale (Electoral Changes) Order 2004, in order to give full effect to recommendations by the Boundary Committee for England, a committee of the Electoral Commission, for electoral changes in the borough of Rochdale.
