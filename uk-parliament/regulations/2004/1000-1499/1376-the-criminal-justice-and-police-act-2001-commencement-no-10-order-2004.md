# The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1376 (C. 55 )

CRIMINAL LAW

POLICE

The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2004

Made

15th May 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 138(2) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2004.

**2.** The following provisions of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> June 2004–

- (a) section 64 (meaning of “appropriate judicial authority”) to the extent not already in force; and
- (b) paragraphs 42 and 67 of Schedule 1 (powers of seizure).

**3.** Paragraph 35 of Schedule 1 of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 shall come into force on 8<sup>th</sup> October 2004.

Home Office

Hazel Blears

Minister of State

15th May 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1<sup>st</sup> June 2004 and 8<sup>th</sup> October 2004 the provisions set out in articles 2 and respectively.
