# The Yeovil Schools (School Day and School Year Regulations) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1191

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Yeovil Schools (School Day and School Year Regulations) Order 2004

Made

22nd April 2004

Laid before Parliament

29th April 2004

Coming into force

1st June 2004

Whereas the governing bodies of the schools listed in the Schedule (“the schools”) have, in accordance with section 4(2) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001] (“the Act”), consulted the local education authority which maintains the schools and such other persons as appear to them to be appropriate, having regard to guidance given by the Secretary of State;

Whereas the Secretary of State has, in accordance with section 2(3) of the Act, consulted Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Schools in England;

Whereas it is the opinion of the Secretary of State, having regard to the matters set out in section 1(2) of the Act, that the implementation by the governing bodies of the schools of the provisions of this Order may contribute to the raising of educational standards achieved by children in England;

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 2 (1) of the Act, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills, on the application of the governing bodies of the schools, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Yeovil Schools (School Day and School Year Regulations) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 1st June 2004.

**2.** In this Order the “School Day and School Year Regulations” means the Education (School Day and School Year) (England) Regulations 1999[^f00002].

## 3. Relaxation of the School Day and School Year Regulations

In regulation 3(2) of the School Day and School Year Regulations, in respect of its application to the governing bodies of the schools listed in the Schedule—

- (a) in relation to the school year [^f00003] 2003-2004, the number “378” is substituted for the number “380”; and
- (b) in relation to the school year 2004-2005, the number “376” is substituted for the number “380”.

## 4. Time limit

This Order shall have effect until 31st August 2005.

David Miliband

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

22nd April 2004
