# Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1030

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2004

Made

30th March 2004

Laid before Parliament

6th April 2004

Coming into force

1st August 2004

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(2) and (7) of and paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Education (Student Loans) Act 1990[^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 1st August 2004.

**2.** These Regulations do not apply in or as regards Scotland [^f00002].

## 3. Amendments

The Education (Student Loans) Regulations 1998[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

**4.** In regulation 6(1) to (4), for each of the figures in the first column below, wherever it appears, there shall be substituted the corresponding figure in the second column below:

| First Column (£) | Second Column (£) |
| --- | --- |
| 1,100 | 1,125 |
| 1,765 | 1,810 |
| 1,500 | 1,535 |
| 2,420 | 2,480 |
| 1,430 | 1,465 |
| 1,960 | 2,005 |

Alan Johnson

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

30th March 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations which come into force on 1st August 2004 amend the Education (Student Loans) Regulations 1998. The 1998 Regulations govern mortgage style repayment loans which were made under the Education (Student Loans) Act 1990. For the most part, the loans were made to students who started their courses before 1st August 1998.

Regulation 4 increases the maximum amounts that may be lent to students in relation to an academic year in line with inflation.
