# The Channel Tunnel (Alcoholic Liquor and Tobacco Products)(Amendment) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1004

CHANNEL TUNNEL

The Channel Tunnel (Alcoholic Liquor and Tobacco Products)(Amendment) Order 2004

Made

1st April 2004

Laid before Parliament

1st April 2004

Coming into force

1st May 2004

The Commissioners of Customs and Excise, it appearing to them necessary or expedient to do so, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 11(1), 11(2), 11(3), 13(1), 13(2), 34(1), 34(2) and 34(4) of the Channel Tunnel Act 1987[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Channel Tunnel (Alcoholic Liquor and Tobacco Products) (Amendment) Order 2004 and comes into force on 1st May 2004.

**2.** The Channel Tunnel (Alcoholic Liquor and Tobacco Products) Order 2003[^f00002] is amended as follows.

**3.** In article 2—

- (a) after “The following Regulations” insert “and Order”,
- (b) after paragraph (e) insert —

  > - (f) the Excise Duty Points (Etc.)(New Member States) Regulations 2004[^f00003];
  > - (g) the Customs and Excise Duties (Travellers' Allowances and Personal Reliefs)(New Member States) Order 2004[^f00004].

  .

**4.** In the Schedule, after paragraph 12 insert—

> **12A. The Excise Duty Points (Etc.)(New Member States) Regulations 2004 and the Customs and Excise Duties (Travellers' Allowances and Personal Reliefs)(New Member States) Order 2004**
>
> In regulations 3(2) and 6 of the Excise Duty Points (Etc.)(New Member States) Regulations 2004, and article 3 of the Customs and Excise Duties (Travellers' Allowances and Personal Reliefs)(New Member States) Order 2004, regard each reference to the words “United Kingdom” as being followed by “or a control zone to which article 5(2)(a) of the Channel Tunnel (Customs and Excise) Order 1990[^f00005] refers.

Michael Eland

Commissioner of Customs and Excise

New King’s Beam House 22 Upper Ground London SE1 9PJ

1st April 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Excise Duty Points (Etc.)(New Member States) Regulations 2004 (S.I. [2004/1003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1003)) and the Customs and Excise Duties (Travellers' Allowances and Personal Reliefs)(New Member States) Order 2004 (S.I. [2004/1002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1002)) create new arrangements for individuals who acquire, for their own use, certain duty-paid tobacco products in the Czech Republic, Estonia, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Slovakia or Slovenia and transport them to the United Kingdom.

This Order comes into force on 1st May 2004. It amends the Channel Tunnel (Alcoholic Liquor and Tobacco Products) Order 2003 (S.I. [2003/2758](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2758)) to enable the new arrangements to apply to relevant tobacco products taken into a shuttle train control zone in France.
