# The European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officer’s Charges) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1405

REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE

NORTHERN IRELAND

The European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officer’s Charges) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004

Made

17th May 2004

Coming into force

18th May 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by regulation 16(1) of the European Parliamentary Elections (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2004[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order with the consent of the Treasury:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officer’s Charges) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(2) This Order extends to Northern Ireland only.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “election” means a European Parliamentary election;
- “register of electors” has the meaning given in regulation 2 of the 2004 Regulations; and
- “the 2004 Regulations” means the European Parliamentary Elections (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2004.

## 3. Revocation

The European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officer’s Charges) (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## 4. Expenses in respect of which a returning officer at a European Parliamentary election in Northern Ireland may recover his charges

(1) For the purposes of regulation 16(1)(a) of the 2004 Regulations (which provides that a returning officer is entitled to recover his charges in respect of expenses properly incurred in connection with a European Parliamentary election if the expenses are of a kind specified in an order made under that provision and the charges are reasonable)—

- (a) expenses of the kind described in paragraphs 1(1), 2(1), 3(1), 4(1), 5(1) and 6 of Part A of the Schedule to this Order; and
- (b) expenses of a kind described in Part B of the Schedule to this Order,

are hereby specified.

(2) The maximum recoverable amounts at a contested election in respect of the expenses specified in Part A of the Schedule are hereby specified as the amounts set out in the right-hand side column of that Part in respect of each entry set out opposite thereto in the left-hand column of that Part.

(3) The maximum recoverable amount in respect of the expenses specified in Part A of the Schedule at an uncontested election is hereby specified as £1314.33.

**7.** Expenses in the provision of voting compartments and any other furniture necessary for polling stations and the provision in any place of furniture and equipment necessary for the verification and the count.

**13.** Expenses in connection with the storage of ballot boxes, polling screens, count furniture and equipment.

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under section 6(4) of the European Parliamentary Elections Act [2002 (c. 24)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/24) the returning officer for Northern Ireland is the Chief Electoral Officer. This Order, which extends only to Northern Ireland, provides for the payment of the returning officer at European Parliamentary elections. Under regulation 4 of the European Parliamentary Elections (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2004 (S.I. [2004/1267](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1267)) (“2004 Regulations”), the Chief Electoral Officer may appoint deputies and clerks to discharge, or assist him in, his functions in relation to an election.

Under regulation 16(1) of the 2004 Regulations the returning officer at a European Parliamentary election is entitled to recover his charges in respect of his expenses properly incurred in connection with such an election if the expenses are of a kind specified in any order under that provision and the charges in respect of them are reasonable.

Part A and B of the Schedule to this Order specify the kinds of expenses incurred by the returning officer for or in connection with a European Parliamentary election in respect of which he is entitled to recover his charges, together (in the case of Part A) with the maximum recoverable amounts in respect of those charges.

This Order increases the maximum recoverable amounts from those which were specified in the European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officer’s Charges) (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 (S.I. [1999/1342](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1342)), which is revoked by this Order.
