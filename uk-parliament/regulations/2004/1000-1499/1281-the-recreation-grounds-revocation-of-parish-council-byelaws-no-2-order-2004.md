# The Recreation Grounds (Revocation of Parish Council Byelaws) (No. 2) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1281

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Recreation Grounds (Revocation of Parish Council Byelaws) (No. 2) Order 2004

Made

6th May 2004

Laid before Parliament

13th May 2004

Coming into force

4th June 2004

Whereas it appears to the Secretary of State that the byelaw specified in article 2 of this Order has been substantially superseded by an instrument which applies to the area to which the byelaw applies:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 262(8)(d) of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order;

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Recreation Grounds (Revocation of Parish Council Byelaws) (No. 2) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 4th June 2004.

## 2. Revocation

Byelaw No. 4 in a series of byelaws made by the Parish Council of Milnthorpe on 13th of October 1930[^f00002] and allowed by the Minister of Health on 2nd December 1930 with respect to Milnthorpe Village Green is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the First Secretary of State

Phil Hope

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Office of the Deputy Prime Minister

6th May 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes a byelaw which was made in exercise of the powers conferred on parish councils by section 8(1)(d) of the Local Government Act 1894 (56 and 57 Vict. c.73). The relevant part of that section was repealed by section 272(1) of, and Schedule 30 to, the Local Government Act 1972. The byelaw continued in force notwithstanding the repeal, by virtue of section 262(1) of the 1972 Act. The byelaw is revoked as being obsolete.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, as it has no impact on the costs of businesses, charities or voluntary bodies.
