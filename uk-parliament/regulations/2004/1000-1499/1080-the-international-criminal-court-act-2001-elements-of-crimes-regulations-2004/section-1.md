# The International Criminal Court Act 2001 (Elements of Crimes) Regulations 2004, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the International Criminal Court Act 2001 (Elements of Crimes) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on .

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Northern Ireland.

(3) In so far as these Regulations may relate to proceedings in a service court these Regulations extend to any place at which those proceedings are held.
