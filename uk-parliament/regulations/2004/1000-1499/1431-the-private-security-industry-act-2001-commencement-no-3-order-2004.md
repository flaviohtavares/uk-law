# The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1431 (C. 57)

SECURITY INDUSTRY, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2004

Made

22nd May 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 26(2) of the Private Security Industry Act 2001[^f00001], having consulted the Security Industry Authority in accordance with section 24(4) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2004.

(2) In this Order “the 2001 Act” means the Private Security Industry Act 2001.

**2.** Section 3(2)(a) to (i) of the 2001 Act (conduct prohibited without a licence) shall come into force on 24th May 2004 in the police area[^f00002] of Hampshire.

**3.** Section 3(2)(a) to (i) of the 2001 Act (conduct prohibited without a licence) shall come into force on 1st June 2004 in the police areas of—

- (a) Avon and Somerset;
- (b) Devon and Cornwall;
- (c) Dorset;
- (d) Gloucestershire; and
- (e) Wiltshire.

**4.** Section 3(1) of the 2001 Act (conduct prohibited without a licence) shall come into force on 4th June 2004 in the police area of Hampshire.

Hazel Blears

Minister of State

Home Office

22nd May 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 3(2)(a) to (i) of the Private Security Act 2001 into force on 24th May 2004 in the Hampshire police area and on 1st June 2004 in the Avon and Somerset, Devon and Cornwall, Dorset, Gloucestershire and Wiltshire police areas.

Section 3(1) of the Act is brought into force in the Hampshire police area on 4th June 2004.
