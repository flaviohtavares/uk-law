## 14. Request for authorisation to conduct a clinical trial[^c-33aca170aa74ebae9a80eea7b0736056]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The sponsor must make a request for authorisation to conduct a clinical trial to the licensing authority in accordance with the procedure set out in regulation 16.

(2) Where the trial is a notifiable trial, the sponsor must indicate that it is a notifiable trial in accordance with regulation 16(2)(b).
