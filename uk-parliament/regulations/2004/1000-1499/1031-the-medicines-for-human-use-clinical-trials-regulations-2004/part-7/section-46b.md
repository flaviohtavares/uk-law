## 46B. Labelling of certain POC investigational medicinal products[^key-04348250e8a50fb39db69e7d4ef41919]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 46 does not apply to a POC investigational medicinal product that is to be administered in its entirety immediately after manufacture.
