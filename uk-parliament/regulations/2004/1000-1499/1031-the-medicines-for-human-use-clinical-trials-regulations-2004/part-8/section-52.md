# The Medicines for Human Use (Clinical Trials) Regulations 2004, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2004/1031

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Penalties

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person guilty of an offence under these Regulations shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or to both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or to both.
