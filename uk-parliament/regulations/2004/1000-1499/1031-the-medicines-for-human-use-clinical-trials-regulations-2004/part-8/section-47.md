# The Medicines for Human Use (Clinical Trials) Regulations 2004, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2004/1031
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Application of enforcement provisions of the 2012 Regulations[^key-d550dca41f33c2f7fcef330808e546f7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-0553c75e660e657ed755c6dd82e6c858] Regulations 2, 8(1), 322, 323(1), 324(1), 325 to 330, 332 to 339, 343 and Schedule 31 of the 2012 Regulations (“those provisions”) shall apply for the purposes of these Regulations as they apply for the purposes of the 2012 Regulations, but with the modifications specified in Schedule 9, and any reference in those provisions to the 2012 Regulations includes a reference to these Regulations.

(2) In those provisions as applying by virtue of paragraph (1), a reference to any part of those provisions or a part of any of them is a reference to the provision or part as so applying.

(3)[^key-dfead9f5d35f81bd12cf57e65b6504d1] In those provisions as applying by virtue of paragraph (1), any reference to, or relating to, a requirement, a power, a function, a right, a duty, an entitlement, or a protection shall be read as a reference to, or relating to, that requirement, power, function, right, duty, entitlement, or protection as applied by this regulation.
