# The Medicines for Human Use (Clinical Trials) Regulations 2004, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2004/1031
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Clinical trials conducted in countries other than the United Kingdom[^key-05f2abbd5b189bb84dabeea27967a687]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a clinical trial is being conducted at a trial site in another country[^key-b81066de22a0d6523b361257af1bbb3f] in addition to sites in the United Kingdom, the sponsor of that trial shall ensure that all suspected unexpected serious adverse reactions occurring at that site are reported as soon as possible to the licensing authority, and in any event—[^key-1d68b3f204a804d2d02ec9de0afc6b10]

- (a) in the case of a reaction that is fatal or life-threatening, within 7 days beginning with the day after the sponsor was first aware of the reaction; or
- (b) in any other case, within 15 days beginning with the day after the sponsor is first aware of the reaction.
