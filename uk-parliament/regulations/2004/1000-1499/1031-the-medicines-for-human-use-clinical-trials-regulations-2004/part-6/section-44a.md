## 44A. Variation of MM (IMP) master file[^c-4f3049dc7f4d786791c761400a7d1034]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to regulation 45(5), the holder of a manufacturing authorisation (MM) may amend the information in the MM (IMP) master file provided in accordance with paragraph 10(a), (c) and (h) to (k) of Schedule 6 and paragraph 14E(a) of Schedule 7 without applying to the licensing authority for a variation under regulation 44.
