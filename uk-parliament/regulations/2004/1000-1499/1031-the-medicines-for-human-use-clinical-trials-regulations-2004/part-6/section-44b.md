## 44B. Variation of POC (IMP) master file[^key-4f3049dc7f4d786791c761400a7d1034]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to regulation 45(6), the holder of a manufacturing authorisation (POC) may amend the information in the POC (IMP) master file provided in accordance with paragraph 11(a), (b) and (g) to (i) of Schedule 6 and paragraph 14M(a) of Schedule 7 without applying to the licensing authority for a variation under regulation 44.
