## 3B. Investigator in a clinical trial[^c-7c4fd5bed65b9c9e7f86a804f7418d59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The investigator, in relation to a clinical trial, shall be a health care professional who is appropriately trained to undertake that role in a clinical trial.
