# The Medicines for Human Use (Clinical Trials) Regulations 2004, section 29A

## 29A. Notification of serious breaches[^c-68080a4a2100351944f486ea9f7379df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The sponsor of a clinical trial shall notify the licensing authority in writing of any serious breach of—

- (a) the conditions and principles of good clinical practice in connection with that trial; or
- (b) the protocol relating to that trial, as modified[^c-db181803d59c7a477b0efca865f007b2] from time to time in accordance with regulations 20 to 22C[^c-70276c8af9a53c19eb67ded94df79b9d],

within 7 days of becoming aware of that breach.

(2) For the purposes of this regulation, a “serious breach” is a breach which is likely to effect to a significant degree—

- (a) the safety or physical or mental integrity of the participants[^c-6e1382ff583fecb8afd30801ab78322f] of the trial; or
- (b) the scientific value of the trial.
