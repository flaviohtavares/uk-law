# The Medicines for Human Use (Clinical Trials) Regulations 2004, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2004/1031
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Conduct of trial in accordance with clinical trial authorisation etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to regulation 30, no person shall conduct a clinical trial otherwise than in accordance with—

- (a) the protocol relating to that trial, as may be modified[^key-0195c94b556d1bd6672278655fe64485] from time to time in accordance with regulations 20 to 22C[^key-2d5b6b0183d016475042a68bb108eea4];
- (b) the terms of—
  - (i) the request for authorisation to conduct that trial,
  - (ii) the application for an ethics committee opinion in relation to that trial, and
  - (iii) any particulars or documents, other than the protocol, accompanying that request or that application,

  as may be modified[^key-c153569476e8bdf203bdf679bd6088cd] from time to time in accordance with regulations 20 to 22C[^key-bced1a196ee2eb51d085a401884a1d86]; and
- (c) any conditions imposed [^key-ced9c5251f0c30dc1b844d04776e92c0]... under regulation 18(3), 18B(3), 22A(4), 22C(3)[^key-2b5cb48c2bca0b133390c270e8d0eec1] or Schedule 5.
