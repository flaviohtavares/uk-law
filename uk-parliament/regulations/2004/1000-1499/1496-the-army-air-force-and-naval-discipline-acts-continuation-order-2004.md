# The Army, Air Force and Naval Discipline Acts (Continuation) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1496

DEFENCE

The Army, Air Force and Naval Discipline Acts (Continuation) Order 2004

Made

10th June 2004

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of June 2004

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas a draft of this Order has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the Armed Forces Act 2001[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Army, Air Force and Naval Discipline Acts (Continuation) Order 2004.

**2.** The Army Act 1955[^f00002], the Air Force Act 1955[^f00003] and the Naval Discipline Act 1957[^f00004] shall continue in force for a period of twelve months beginning on the day immediately following 31st August 2004 (which is the date on which those Acts would otherwise expire).

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 1(1) of the Armed Forces Act 2001 provides that the Army Act 1955, the Air Force Act 1955 and the Naval Discipline Act 1957 (“the Service Discipline Acts”) shall expire on 31st August 2002 unless continued in force in accordance with that section.

The Army, Air Force and Naval Discipline Acts (Continuation) Orders 2002 and 2003 (S.I. [2002/1820](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1820) and S.I. [2003/1869](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/1869) respectively) have each provided for the continuation of the Service Discipline Acts for a period of 12 months. Under the 2003 Order, the Service Discipline Acts shall continue in force until 31st August 2004.

This Order enables the Service Discipline Acts to continue in force for a further period of 12 months beyond 31st August 2004.
