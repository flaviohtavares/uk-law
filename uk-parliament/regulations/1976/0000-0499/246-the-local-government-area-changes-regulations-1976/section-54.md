# The Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976, section 54

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 54. Valuation of water hereditaments of statutory water undertakings

Where new valuation lists are to come into force on the appointed day, then, in the application of Schedule 4 to the General Rate Act 1967 as amended by the Rating (Water hereditaments) Order 1975[^f00008] the changes of areas made by the order shall be deemed to have been operative at all material times.
