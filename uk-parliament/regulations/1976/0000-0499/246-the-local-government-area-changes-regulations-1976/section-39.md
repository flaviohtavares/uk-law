# The Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Land registration

Where an area is transferred from a district in which registration of title to land under the Land Registration Acts 1925 to 1971[^f00007] is compulsory on sale to another such district such registration shall continue to be compulsory.
