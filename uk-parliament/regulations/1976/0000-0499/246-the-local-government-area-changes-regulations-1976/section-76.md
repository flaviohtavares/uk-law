# The Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976, section 76

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 76. Saving for extensions of service

Any extension of service under regulation L.15 of the Local Government Superannuation Regulations 1974 effective on the appointed day in relation to an officer transferred by regulation 70 or under regulation 72 shall continue to have effect as if it had been made by the authority to whose employment he has been transferred as aforesaid.
