# The Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Legal proceedings

All legal proceedings pending at the appointed day shall be amended in such manner as may be necessary or proper in consequence of these regulations and the order.
