# The Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Rate support grants

In any calculations of the rate support grants for the year beginning on the appointed day the changes of areas made by the order shall be deemed to have been made at the date by reference to which, for the purposes of paragraph 9(1) of Part III of Schedule 2 to the Local Government Act 1974 the effective rateable values therein referred to are to be generally determined for that year.
