# The Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Schemes for the allocation of officers for transfer—Other authorities

A scheme for the allocation of officers (other than the officers specified in regulation 67(7)) for transfer may be made by any authority to whom regulation 67 does not apply but who are affected by the order.
