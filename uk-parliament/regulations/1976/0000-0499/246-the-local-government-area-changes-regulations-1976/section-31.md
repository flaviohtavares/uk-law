# The Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Registration service

Any reference in any scheme made under section 14 of the Registration Service Act 1953 or article 4 of the Local Government (Registration Service) Order 1973[^f00004] to a relevant area which is altered by the order shall be read as a reference to such area as altered.

Any such reference to a relevant area which is abolished by the order shall cease to have effect.

In this regulation, “**relevant area**” means a county, district, London borough, parish or community, the City, the Inner Temple or the Middle Temple, or any ward of a district, London borough, parish or community or the City.
