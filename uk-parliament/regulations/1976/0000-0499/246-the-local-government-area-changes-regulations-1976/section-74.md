# The Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Saving for training arrangements

Where any officer transferred by regulation 70 or under regulation 72 is undergoing training under arrangements which have not been discharged before the appointed day, those arrangements shall continue to apply with the substitution, for the authority in whose employment the officer was prior to the commencement of the training, of the authority to whose employment he has been transferred as aforesaid.
