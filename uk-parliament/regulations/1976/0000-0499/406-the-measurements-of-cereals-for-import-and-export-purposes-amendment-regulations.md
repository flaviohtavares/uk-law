# The Measurements of Cereals for Import and Export Purposes (Amendment) Regulations 1976

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1976 No. 406

AGRICULTURE

The Measurements of Cereals for Import and Export Purposes (Amendment) Regulations 1976

Made

12th March 1976

Laid before Parliament

22nd March 1976

Coming into Operation

12th April 1976

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, being Ministers designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to measurements of cereals for import and export purposes, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by the said section 2(2) and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following regulations:—

**1.** These regulations may be cited as the Measurements of Cereals for Import and Export Purposes (Amendment) Regulations 1976 and shall come into operation on 12th April 1976.

**2.** The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply to the interpretation of these regulations as it applies to the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

**3.** In the definition of “the Community provisions” in regulation 2(1) of the Measurements of Cereals for Import and Export Purposes Regulations 1975[^f00002] the words “and any instrument replacing, supplementing or further amending that directive” are hereby revoked.

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 10th March 1976.

Frederick Peart

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

William Ross

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

12th March 1976

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Measurements of Cereals for Import and Export Purposes Regulations 1975 provided for the adoption for the purpose of trade in cereals between the United Kingdom and other Member States of the European Economic Community of the unit of measurement known as the EEC standard mass per storage volume as defined in “the Community provisions”. For this purpose “the Community provisions” were defined as “Council Directive No. 71/347EEC on the approximation of the laws of Member States relating to the measuring of the standard mass per storage volume of grain, as amended[^f00003], *and any instrument replacing, supplementing or further amending that directive*” These Regulations revoke the italicised words in that definition.
