# The Restrictive Practices Court Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1976

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1976 No. 1896 (C.45)

RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES

The Restrictive Practices Court Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1976

Made

10th November 1976

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 12(3) of the Restrictive Practices Court Act 1976, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Restrictive Practices Court Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1976.

**2.** The Restrictive Practices Court Act 1976 shall come into operation on 15th December 1976.

Elwyn-Jones, C

Dated 10th November 1976

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation the Restrictive Practices Court Act 1976.
