# The Industrial Training (Transfer of the Activities of Establishments) (No. 2) Order 1976

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1976 No. 1635

INDUSTRIAL TRAINING

The Industrial Training (Transfer of the Activities of Establishments) (No. 2) Order 1976

Made

1st October 1976

Coming into Operation

for all purposes of Article 3

8th November 1976

for all purposes of Article 4

1st January 1977

for all other purposes

1st April 1977

The Secretary of State, on the recommendation of the Manpower Services Commission, and in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9A(2) of the Industrial Training Act 1964, as inserted by section 6 of, and paragraph 13 of Part I of Schedule 2 to, the Employment and Training Act 1973, hereby makes the following Order:—

## Citation and commencement

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Industrial Training (Transfer of the Activities of Establishments) (No. 2) Order 1976.

(2) The provisions of this Order shall come into operation:—

- (a) for all purposes of Article 3 on 8th November 1976;
- (b) for all purposes of Article 4 on 1st January 1977;
- (c) for all other purposes on 1st April 1977.

## Interpretation

**2.** (1) In this Order unless the context otherwise requires “**the Act**” means the Industrial Training Act 1964, as amended by the Employment and Training Act 1973.

(2) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply to the interpretation of this Order as it applies to the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

## Transfer of Establishments' Activities

**3.** The activities carried on at each establishment specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 to this Order, being an establishment of an employer in the industry of an industrial training board, shall, instead of being included for the purposes of the Act in the industry of the industrial training board established by the industrial training order specified in column 2 of that Schedule, be included for those purposes in the industry of the industrial training board established by the industrial training order specified in column 3 of that Schedule.

**4.** The activities carried on at each establishment specified in column 1 of Schedule 2 to this Order, being an establishment of an employer in the industry of an industrial training board, shall, instead of being included for the purposes of the Act in the industry of the industrial training board established by the industrial training order specified in column 2 of that Schedule, be included for those purposes in the industry of the industrial training board established by the industrial training order specified in column 3 of that Schedule.

**5.** The activities carried on at the establishment specified in column 1 of Schedule 3 to this Order, being an establishment of an employer in the industry of an industrial training board, shall, instead of being included for the purposes of the Act in the industry of the industrial training board established by the industrial training order specified in column 2 of that Schedule, be included for those purposes in the industry of the industrial training board established by the industrial training order specified in column 3 of that Schedule.

Signed by order of the Secretary of State.

John Golding

Joint Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Employment
