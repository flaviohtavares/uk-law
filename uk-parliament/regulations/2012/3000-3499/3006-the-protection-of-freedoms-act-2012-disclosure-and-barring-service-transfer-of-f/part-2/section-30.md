# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Consequential amendments to the Family Procedure Rules 2010

(1) The Family Procedure Rules 2010[^f00065] are amended as follows.

(2) In rule 2.3(1)[^f00066] in the definition of “professional acting in furtherance of the protection of children” in sub-paragraph (e)[^f00067]—

- (a) for “Independent Safeguarding Authority” substitute “Disclosure and Barring Service”; and
- (b) for “section 1 of the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006” substitute “section 87(1) of the Protection of Freedoms Act 2012”.
