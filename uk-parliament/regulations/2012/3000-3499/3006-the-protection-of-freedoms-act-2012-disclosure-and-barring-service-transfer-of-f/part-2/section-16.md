# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Consequential amendment to the Data Protection Act 1998

In entry 4 in the table in section 56(6) of the Data Protection Act 1998[^f00058] for “Independent Safeguarding Authority” substitute “Disclosure and Barring Service”.

## Annotations

[^f00058]: Footnote: [1998 c. 29](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/29). Entry 4 was inserted by section 63(1) of, and paragraph 15 of Schedule 9 to, the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 and amended by [S.I. 2007/1351](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1351) and section 81(3)(i) of the Policing and Crime Act 2009.
