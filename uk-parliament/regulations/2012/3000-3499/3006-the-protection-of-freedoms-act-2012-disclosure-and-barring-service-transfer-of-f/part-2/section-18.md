# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Consequential amendments to the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 (Exceptions) Order 1975

The Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 (Exceptions) Order 1975[^f00060] is amended as follows.

**19.** In article 3(a)(ii) for “40, 43 or 44” substitute “38, 40 or 43”.

**20.** (1) Part II of Schedule 1 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 38—

- (a) for “Independent Barring Board” substitute “Disclosure and Barring Service”;
- (b) after “Service” insert “, and any other work in the Disclosure and Barring Service”.

(3) Omit paragraph 44.
