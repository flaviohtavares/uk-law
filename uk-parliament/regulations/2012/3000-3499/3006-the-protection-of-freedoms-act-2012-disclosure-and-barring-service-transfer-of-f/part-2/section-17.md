# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Consequential amendment to the Freedom of Information Act 2000

In Part 6 of Schedule 1 to the Freedom of Information Act 2000[^f00059] omit “The Independent Safeguarding Authority”.

## Annotations

[^f00059]: Footnote: [2000 c. 36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/36). The entry in relation to the Independent Safeguarding Authority was inserted by [S.I. 2011/1041](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1041).
