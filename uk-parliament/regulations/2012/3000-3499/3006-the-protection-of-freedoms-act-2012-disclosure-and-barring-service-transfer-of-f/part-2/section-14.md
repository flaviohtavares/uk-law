# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Consequential amendment to the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967

In Schedule 2 to the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967[^f00056] omit “Independent Safeguarding Authority”.
