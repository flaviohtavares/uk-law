# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Consequential amendments to the 2008 Transitional Provisions Order

In the following provisions of the 2008 Transitional Provisions Order for “IBB” substitute “DBS”—

- (a) article 2(2), (4) and (5);
- (b) article 3(2);
- (c) article 4(2), (4) and (5).

**12.** In the following provisions of the 2008 Transitional Provisions Order for “IBB” substitute “IBB, ISA or DBS, as the case may be”—

- (a) article 2(1)(c);
- (b) article 3(1)(c);
- (c) article 4(1)(c).
