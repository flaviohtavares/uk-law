# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Consequential amendment to the Superannuation Act 1972

In Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00057] omit “Employment by the Independent Safeguarding Authority”.

## Annotations

[^f00057]: Footnote: [1972 c. 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/11). The entry in relation to the Independent Barring Board was inserted by paragraph 3(4) of Schedule 1 to the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 and amended by section 81(3)(a) of the Policing and Crime Act 2009.
