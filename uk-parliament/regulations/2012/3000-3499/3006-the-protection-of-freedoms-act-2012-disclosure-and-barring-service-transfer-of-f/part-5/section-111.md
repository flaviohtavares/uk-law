# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 111

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 111. Dissolution of ISA

ISA is dissolved immediately after its functions are transferred to DBS by article 2 of this Order.

**112.** Section 1 of, and Schedules 1 and 2 to, the 2006 Act cease to have effect.
