# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 76

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 76. Consequential amendments to the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) Regulations 2002

The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) Regulations 2002[^f00105] are amended as follows.

**77.** In regulation 5A[^f00106] omit paragraph (z).

**78.** In regulation 11(1), (1)(b), (2), (2A)[^f00107], (3) and (4) for “Secretary of State” substitute “Disclosure and Barring Service”.
