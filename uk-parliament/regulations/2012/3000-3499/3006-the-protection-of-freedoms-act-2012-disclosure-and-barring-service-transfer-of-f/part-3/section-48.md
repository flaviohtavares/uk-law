# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 48

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**48.** In the following provisions of the 2006 Act for “the Secretary of State” and “The Secretary of State”, in each place where it occurs, substitute “DBS”—

- (a) section 30A(1), (6) and (7)[^f00087];
- (b) section 30B(1), (2), (5), (9) and (10)[^f00088];
- (c) section 33(3), (3A), (3D), (4) and (5)[^f00089];
- (d) section 43(1), (2), (5A), (5B), (5C), and (5F)[^f00090];
- (e) section 47(7);
- (f) section 48(1), (2), (2A), (3), (4), (5) and (8)[^f00091];
- (g) section 49(1), (2), (2A), (3), (4), (5) and (8)[^f00092];
- (h) section 50(5)[^f00093];
- (i) paragraphs 19(7) and 22 of Schedule 3.
