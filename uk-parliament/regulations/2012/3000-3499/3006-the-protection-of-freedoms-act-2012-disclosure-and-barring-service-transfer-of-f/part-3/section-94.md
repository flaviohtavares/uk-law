# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 94

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 94. Consequential amendments to the Legislative and Regulatory Reform (Regulatory Functions) Order 2007

In Part 1 of the Schedule to the Legislative and Regulatory Reform (Regulatory Functions) Order 2007[^f00112] for “Criminal Records Bureau” substitute “Disclosure and Barring Service”.
