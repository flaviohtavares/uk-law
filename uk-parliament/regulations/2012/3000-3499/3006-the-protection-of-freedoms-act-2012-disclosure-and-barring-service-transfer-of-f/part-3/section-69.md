# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Consequential amendments to the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 (Exceptions) Order 1975

The Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 (Exceptions) Order 1975 is amended as follows.

**70.** In article 3(l)[^f00102] for “Secretary of State” substitute “Disclosure and Barring Service”.

**71.** In Part II of Schedule 1 omit paragraph 44[^f00103].
