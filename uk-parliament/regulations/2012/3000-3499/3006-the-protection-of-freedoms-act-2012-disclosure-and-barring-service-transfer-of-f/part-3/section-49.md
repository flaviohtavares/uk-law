# The Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Disclosure and Barring Service Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**49.** In the following provisions of the 2007 Order for “the Secretary of State” and “The Secretary of State”, in each place where it occurs, substitute “DBS”—

- (a) Article 32A(1), (6) and (7)[^f00094];
- (b) Article 32B(1), (2), (5), (9) and (10)[^f00095];
- (c) Article 35(3), (3A), (3D), (4) and (5)[^f00096];
- (d) Article 45(1), (2), (5A), (5B), (5C), and (5F)[^f00097];
- (e) Article 49(7);
- (f) Article 50(1), (2), (2A), (3), (4), (5) and (8)[^f00098];
- (g) Article 51(1), (2), (2A), (3), (4), (5) and (8)[^f00099];
- (h) Article 52(5)[^f00100];
- (i) paragraph 22 of Schedule 1.
