# The Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000 (Estimates and Accounts) (Amendment) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 3135

Government Resources And Accounts

The Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000 (Estimates and Accounts) (Amendment) Order 2012

Made

18th December 2012

Laid before Parliament

19th December 2012

Coming into force

29th January 2013

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4A(3) and (4) of the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000[^f00001], make the following Order.

In accordance with section 4A(8) of that Act the Treasury have consulted, where they think it appropriate, the Scottish Ministers, the Department for Finance and Personnel for Northern Ireland and the Welsh Ministers.

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000 (Estimates and Accounts) (Amendment) Order 2012.

(2) This Order comes into force on 29th January 2013.

(3) In this Order a reference to a table is a reference to column 2 of a table in the Schedule to the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000 (Estimates and Accounts) Order 2012[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment to designations

(1) The Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000 (Estimates and Accounts) Order 2012 is amended as follows.

(2) In each table mentioned in Schedule 1, insert at the appropriate place the bodies which are listed for that table.

(3) In each table mentioned in Schedule 2, omit the bodies which are listed for that table.

(4) In Table 2 (Department of Health)—

- (a) for “The Information Centre” substitute “The Health and Social Care Information Centre”, and
- (b) for “NHS Commissioning Board” substitute “The National Health Service Commissioning Board”.

(5) In Table 4 (Department for Transport)—

- (a) for “British Transport Police Authority (sSRA)” substitute “British Transport Police Authority”, and
- (b) for “Passengers’ Council” substitute “Passenger Focus”.

(6) In Table 6 (Department for Business, Innovation and Skills), for “RCUK Shared Service Centre” substitute “RCUK Shared Services Centre Limited”.

(7) In Table 15 (Department for Culture, Media and Sport)—

- (a) for “National Museums and Galleries on Merseyside” substitute “National Museums Liverpool”, and
- (b) for “Science Museum” substitute “Science Museum Group”.

(8) In Table 17 (Northern Ireland Office), for “Northern Ireland Parades Commission” substitute “Parades Commission for Northern Ireland”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000 (Estimates and Accounts) Order 2012 ([S.I. 2012/717](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/717)) (“the principal Order”), which designates specified central government bodies in relation to named government departments for the purpose of those departments’ supply estimates and resource accounts. The effect of the amendments is that additional bodies are designated, the names of some bodies are changed and some bodies are no longer designated.

The designations have effect for the financial year which ends on 31st March 2013.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument because no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
