# The Charitable Incorporated Organisations (General) Regulations 2012, section 14

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Constitution not to restrict ability to dispose of property[^key-b26a6581a3f7ad9db6e86f0359391557]

*Extent: E+W*

A CIO’s constitution must not include any restriction of the power in section 216 of the 2011 Act (powers of CIO)[^f00005] that would deprive the CIO of its ability to dispose of its property.
