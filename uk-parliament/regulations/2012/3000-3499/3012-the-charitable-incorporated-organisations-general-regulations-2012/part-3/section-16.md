# The Charitable Incorporated Organisations (General) Regulations 2012, section 16

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Date of resolution amending constitution[^c-8913548f52a2c6614ece93671f204421]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), if a resolution under section 224 of the 2011 Act to amend a CIO’s constitution is passed otherwise than at a general meeting it is treated as having been passed on the date on which the last member agreed to it.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply if a provision in the CIO’s constitution treats such a resolution as having been passed on a date later than the date on which the last member agreed to it.
