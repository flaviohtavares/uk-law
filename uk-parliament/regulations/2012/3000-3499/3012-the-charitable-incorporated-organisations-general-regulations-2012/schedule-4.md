# The Charitable Incorporated Organisations (General) Regulations 2012, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 4 — CONSEQUENTIAL AMENDMENTS

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 63

### 1. Amendment of the Charities (Exception from Registration and Accounts) Regulations 1965[^key-a86747c8cac2af4707042cf941ea8b31]

After sub-paragraph (c) of regulation 2 of the Charities (Exception from Registration and Accounts) Regulations 1965[^f00017] insert—

> or
>
> - (d) a charitable incorporated organisation.

.

### 2. Amendment of the Charities (Exception of Universities from Registration) Regulations 1966[^key-b100c27f4741f69e3ff52ad5a5f4f5c3]

In regulation 2 of the Charities (Exception of Universities from Registration) Regulations 1966[^f00018], after “Charities Act 1960” insert “or a charitable incorporated organisation”.

### 3. Amendment of the Charities (Misleading Names) Regulations 1992[^key-2df0c74b7a46545db77820075318bc23]

In the Schedule to the Charities (Misleading Names) Regulations 1992[^f00019]—

- (a) after “Building society” insert “Charitable incorporated organisation”; and
- (b) after “Royalty” insert “Sefydliad elusennol corfforedig”.

### 4. Amendment of the Charities (Exception from Registration) Regulations 1996[^key-69dd6a277f30d421b274a2092431f0ab]

(1) The Charities (Exception from Registration) Regulations 1996[^f00020] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 4—

- (a) at the start of paragraph (2), insert “Subject to paragraph (4),”; and
- (b) after paragraph (3), insert—

  > (4) This regulation shall not apply to a charity which is constituted as a charitable incorporated organisation.

  .

(3) In regulation 5—

- (a) at the start of paragraph (2), insert “Subject to paragraph (3),”; and
- (b) after paragraph (2), insert—

  > (3) This regulation shall not apply to a charity which is constituted as a charitable incorporated organisation.

  .

### 5. Amendment of the Charities (Exception from Registration) Regulations 2008[^key-3f8eb31b76302a58754dcedaf916069d]

(1) The Charities (Exception from Registration) Regulations 2008[^f00021] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2—

- (a) at the start of paragraph (2), insert “Subject to paragraph (4),”; and
- (b) after paragraph (3), insert—

  > (4) This regulation does not apply to a charity which is constituted as a charitable incorporated organisation.

  .

### 6. Amendment of the Charities (Exception from Registration) Regulations 2010[^key-209cceb207f6197670f250238cd2e6b0]

(1) The Charities (Exception from Registration) Regulations 2010[^f00022] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 after “Subject to” insert “regulation 4 and to”.

(3) In regulation 3 after “Subject to” insert “regulation 4 and to”.

(4) After regulation 3 insert—

> (4) These Regulations do not apply to a charity which is constituted as a charitable incorporated organisation.

.
