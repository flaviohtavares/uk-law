# The Charitable Incorporated Organisations (General) Regulations 2012, section 19

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. CIO contracts[^key-cefe506f48bd9816b271105744e39a97]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A contract may be made—

- (a) by a CIO, by writing under its common seal; or
- (b) on behalf of a CIO, by a person acting under its authority, express or implied.

(2) Any formalities required by law in the case of a contract made by an individual also apply, unless a contrary intention appears, to a contract made by or on behalf of a CIO.
