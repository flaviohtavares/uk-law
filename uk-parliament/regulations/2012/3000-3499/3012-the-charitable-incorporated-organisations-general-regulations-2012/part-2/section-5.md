# The Charitable Incorporated Organisations (General) Regulations 2012, section 5

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. CIO not to be exempt[^c-044098ec3f43e96cd590b5f437d4e512]

*Extent: E+W*

No application may be made for a CIO to be constituted and registered where the resulting charity would be an exempt charity.
