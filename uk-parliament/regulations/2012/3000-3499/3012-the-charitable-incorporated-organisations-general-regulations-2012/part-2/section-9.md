# The Charitable Incorporated Organisations (General) Regulations 2012, section 9

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Applications for amalgamation: information to be included[^c-b58e91dfd951cd893b32c0ef3a492349]

*Extent: E+W*

In a case where two or more CIOs make an application for amalgamation and the proposed constitution of the new CIO includes provision for entrenchment, the old CIOs must specify that fact in the application.
