# The Charitable Incorporated Organisations (General) Regulations 2012, section 50

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50. Sending or supplying documents or information[^key-3a0e9e1027e99c76e6d00c9c4c8fa637]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Documents or information to be sent or supplied to a CIO must be sent or supplied in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 2.

(2) Documents or information to be sent or supplied by a CIO must be sent or supplied in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 3.

(3) The provisions referred to in Schedule 3 apply (and those referred to in Schedule 2 do not apply) in relation to a document or information that is to be sent or supplied by one CIO to another.
