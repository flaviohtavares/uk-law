# The Charitable Incorporated Organisations (General) Regulations 2012, section 52

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Member’s right to hard copy[^c-9c112c67bb7277c73ff5ff8a147082d6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a member of a CIO (“M”) has received a document or information from the CIO otherwise than in hard copy form, M is entitled to require the CIO to send M a version of the document or information in hard copy form.

(2) The CIO—

- (a) must send the document or information in hard copy form within 21 days of receipt of M’s request; and
- (b) may not make a charge for providing the document or information in that form.
