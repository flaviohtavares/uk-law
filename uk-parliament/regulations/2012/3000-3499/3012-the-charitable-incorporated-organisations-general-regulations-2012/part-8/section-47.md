# The Charitable Incorporated Organisations (General) Regulations 2012, section 47

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 47. Service of documents on CIO[^key-2fc10fc37f433a99210ff76442b63a8b]

*Extent: E+W*

A document may be served on a CIO by leaving it at, or sending it by post to, the CIO’s principal office as it appears on the register of charities.
