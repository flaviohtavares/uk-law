# The Charitable Incorporated Organisations (General) Regulations 2012, section 29

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Register of members to be evidence[^key-07a0f58a767c4f9899182301be2a636a]

*Extent: E+W*

A CIO’s register of members is to be prima facie evidence of any matters which are by these Regulations required to be inserted in it.
