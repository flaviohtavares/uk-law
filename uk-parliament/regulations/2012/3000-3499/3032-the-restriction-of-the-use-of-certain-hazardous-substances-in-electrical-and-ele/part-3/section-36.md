# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 36

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 36. Market surveillance powers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the market surveillance authority considers that there may be a breach of these Regulations it may—

- (a) exercise the powers set out in Schedule 2 (test purchases, powers of entry etc and warrants); and
- (b) take the actions set out in Schedule 3 (compliance, enforcement and recall notices).
