# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 42

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 42. Time limit for prosecution of offences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3f40d9a56002e9d5ef4c3faaa6513201] In England and Wales an information relating to an offence that is triable by a magistrates’ court may be so tried if it is laid within twelve months after the date on which evidence sufficient in the opinion of the prosecutor to justify the proceedings comes to the knowledge of the prosecutor. *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3f40d9a56002e9d5ef4c3faaa6513201]

(2 (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-57a7303effa881e68e3557d5beafa5c8] In Scotland *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-57a7303effa881e68e3557d5beafa5c8]

- (a (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-57a7303effa881e68e3557d5beafa5c8] summary proceedings for an offence may be commenced before the end of twelve months from the date on which evidence sufficient in the Lord Advocate’s opinion to justify the proceedings came to the Lord Advocate’s knowledge, and *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-57a7303effa881e68e3557d5beafa5c8]
- (b (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-57a7303effa881e68e3557d5beafa5c8] section 136(3) of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995 (time limit for certain offences) *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-57a7303effa881e68e3557d5beafa5c8][^f00012] applies for the purpose of this paragraph as it applies for the purpose of that section. *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-57a7303effa881e68e3557d5beafa5c8]

(3) In Northern Ireland summary proceedings for an offence may be instituted within twelve months after the date on which evidence sufficient in the opinion of the prosecutor to justify proceedings comes to the knowledge of the prosecutor.

(4) No proceedings are to be brought more than three years after the commission of the offence.

(5) For the purposes of this regulation a certificate of the prosecutor (or in Scotland, the Lord Advocate) *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4a837ad649c9dc6f1016428e49eb24bc] as to the date on which such evidence as is referred to above came to their knowledge is conclusive evidence.
