# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 39

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Penalties

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who is guilty of an offence under—

- (a) regulation 37(1)(a);
- (b) regulation 37(2)(a); or
- (c) regulation 37(3)(a),

is liable on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum and on conviction on indictment, to a fine.

(2) A person who is guilty of—

- (a) any other offence under regulation 37; or
- (b) an offence under regulation 38,

is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
