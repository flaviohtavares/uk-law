# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 15

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 15. Duty to keep technical documentation and EU (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note) declaration of conformity[^key-cbc6f6624d1dec0452e5b843f8ea6aa4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A manufacturer must keep the technical documentation and the EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d29eb2f5086582c7b54ab199d8a956b0] declaration of conformity for EEE available for inspection by the market surveillance authority for a period of ten years from the day on which the EEE was placed on the market.
