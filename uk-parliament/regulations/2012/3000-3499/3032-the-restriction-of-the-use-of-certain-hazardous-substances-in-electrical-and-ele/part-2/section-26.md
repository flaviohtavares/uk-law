# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 26

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 26. Non-compliant EEE[^M_E_03633852-a2c7-4a39-fe12-1ee8a45e7184]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If an importer has reason to believe that EEE which the importer was intending to place on the market does not comply with the requirements of regulation 3, the importer must inform the manufacturer and the market surveillance authority of the non-compliance.

(2) An importer who has placed EEE on the market and has reason to believe that any provision of these Regulations has not been complied with in relation to the EEE must immediately—

- (a) take the corrective measures which are necessary to ensure that the provision is complied with in relation to the EEE, withdraw the EEE or recall it, if appropriate; and
- (b) provide the market surveillance authority [^key-1fa1a2b6bd2fe89f75ffcd29086062da]... with information about the non-compliance and any corrective measures taken in accordance with sub-paragraph (a).

## 26. Non-compliant EEE[^M_E_ae52ae5e-bba9-43dd-bcc1-4c35139bb0cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If an importer has reason to believe that EEE which the importer was intending to place on the market does not comply with the requirements of regulation 3, the importer must inform the manufacturer and the market surveillance authority of the non-compliance.

(2) An importer who has placed EEE on the market and has reason to believe that any provision of these Regulations has not been complied with in relation to the EEE must immediately—

- (a) take the corrective measures which are necessary to ensure that the provision is complied with in relation to the EEE, withdraw the EEE or recall it, if appropriate; and
- (b) provide the market surveillance authority and the competent national authorities of any other relevant states[^key-b165f57baba8ade248d851d7cbc834ea] in which they made the EEE available with information about the non-compliance and any corrective measures taken in accordance with sub-paragraph (a).
