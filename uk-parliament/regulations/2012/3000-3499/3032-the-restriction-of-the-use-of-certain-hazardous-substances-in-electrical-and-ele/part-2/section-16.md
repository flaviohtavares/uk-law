# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 16

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 16. EEE to bear UK marking[^M_E_73b6392d-a053-428a-b3bd-e68a8a7d8f3d][^c-6f0adef836a47de1daa25c1be4554319]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The UK[^c-8998cc8fd9504c25916acd0a2750867a] marking which a manufacturer must affix under regulation 13 must be affixed visibly, legibly and indelibly.

(2)[^c-1dea8dfd67f8e09685eea675d36e134a] The UK marking must be affixed to—

- (a) the EEE;
- (b) a data plate affixed to the EEE; or
- (c) where paragraph (2A) applies—
  - (i) a label affixed to the EEE; or
  - (ii) a document accompanying the EEE.

(2A) This paragraph applies to EEE that is placed on the market within a period of seven years[^c-0252cac5555428aa98a86a20c25645c0] beginning with IP completion day.

(3) Where due to the nature of the EEE it is not possible or not warranted for the UK marking to be affixed in accordance with paragraph (2), the manufacturer must instead affix the UK marking to—

- (a) the packaging of the EEE; and
- (b) any documents that accompany the EEE.

## 16. EEE to bear CE marking[^M_E_ae52ae5e-bba9-43dd-bcc1-4c35139bb0cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The CE marking which a manufacturer must affix under regulation 13 must be affixed visibly, legibly and indelibly.

(2) The CE marking must be affixed to—

- (a) the EEE; or
- (b) a data plate affixed to the EEE.

(3) Where due to the nature of the EEE it is not possible or not warranted for the CE marking to be affixed in accordance with paragraph (2), the manufacturer must instead affix the CE marking to—

- (a) the packaging of the EEE; and
- (b) any documents that accompany the EEE.
