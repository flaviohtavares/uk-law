# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 17

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 17. Compliance procedures for series production[^M_E_12bd3148-b436-4395-8735-7e256a689558]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A manufacturer of EEE which is manufactured by means of series production must ensure that procedures are in place to ensure that any EEE so manufactured complies with the requirements of regulation 3.

(2) In doing so, the manufacturer must take adequate account of—

- (a) any changes in the design or characteristics of the EEE; and
- (b) any changes to any designated standards[^c-f3a153c7b327c718e6ec9bdd1a0fa859] or technical specifications referred to in the [^c-92d2a7c9ba102fcfad11b3650b186ed4]... declaration of conformity drawn up in relation to the EEE.

(3) In this regulation “technical specification” means a document that prescribes technical requirements to be fulfilled by a product, process or service.

## 17. Compliance procedures for series production[^M_E_ae52ae5e-bba9-43dd-bcc1-4c35139bb0cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A manufacturer of EEE which is manufactured by means of series production must ensure that procedures are in place to ensure that any EEE so manufactured complies with the requirements of regulation 3.

(2) In doing so, the manufacturer must take adequate account of—

- (a) any changes in the design or characteristics of the EEE; and
- (b) any changes to any harmonised standards or technical specifications referred to in the EU declaration of conformity drawn up in relation to the EEE.

(3) In this regulation “technical specification” means a document that prescribes technical requirements to be fulfilled by a product, process or service.
