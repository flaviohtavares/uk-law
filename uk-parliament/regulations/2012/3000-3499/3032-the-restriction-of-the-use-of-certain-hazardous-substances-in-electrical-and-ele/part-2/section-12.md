# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 12

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 12. Conformity assessment procedure and drawing up of technical documentation[^M_E_507ae91c-1655-4517-b174-a276366a7530]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A manufacturer must—

- (a) draw up technical documentation; and
- (b) carry out, and comply with their obligations under, the internal production control procedure,

in relation to the EEE, in line with Part 4 of Schedule 1[^c-77acbc14aa5a05cdad73172980faca22].

(2) Where other applicable assimilated[^c-3be9d26c0ea879d6563808092d1745b2][^c-b9e3b41df3ced00e9afed783fe3d14c5] law requires the EEE to be subject to a conformity assessment procedure which is at least as stringent as that required under paragraph (1), compliance with the requirements of regulation 3 may be demonstrated within the context of that procedure and a single set of technical documentation may be drawn up.

## 12. Conformity assessment procedure and drawing up of technical documentation[^M_E_ae52ae5e-bba9-43dd-bcc1-4c35139bb0cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A manufacturer must—

- (a) draw up technical documentation; and
- (b) carry out, and comply with their obligations under, the internal production control procedure,

in relation to the EEE, in line with Module A of Annex II to Decision [768/2008/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2008/0768) of the European Parliament and of the Council on a common framework for the marketing of products and repealing Council Decision [93/465/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/1993/465).

(2) Where other applicable EU legislation or legislation giving effect to EU legislation requires the EEE to be subject to a conformity assessment procedure which is at least as stringent as that required under paragraph (1), compliance with the requirements of regulation 3 may be demonstrated within the context of that procedure and a single set of technical documentation may be drawn up.
