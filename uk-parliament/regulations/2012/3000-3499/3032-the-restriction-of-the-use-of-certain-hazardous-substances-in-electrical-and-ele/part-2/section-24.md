# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 24

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 24. Information identifying importers[^M_E_8846504f-d003-4374-f66f-3b6a49269ca0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An importer must ensure that the following information is marked on the EEE—

- (a) the importer’s name, registered trade name or registered trade mark; and
- (b) an address at which the importer can be contacted.

(2)[^c-5374ce312070087627c5dc0e6d94baa2] Paragraph (1) does not apply where—

- (a) either—
  - (i) it is not possible to set out the information referred to in paragraph (1) on the EEE; or
  - (ii) the importer has imported the EEE from the EU and places it on the market within the period of seven years[^c-f72a088783b3185f5afdb878118d4cf2] beginning with IP completion day; and
- (b) before placing the EEE on the market, the importer sets out the information referred to in paragraph (1) on the packaging of the EEE or in a document accompanying the EEE.

(3) Where the importer complies with other applicable assimilated[^c-f18e44db895c20ec0e47444e1eb863a3][^c-9ca76fbe48047e784c20c5d5607865bb] law, containing provisions for the affixing of the importer’s name and address which are at least as stringent as those set out in this regulation, it is sufficient to satisfy this regulation.

## 24. Information identifying importers[^M_E_ae52ae5e-bba9-43dd-bcc1-4c35139bb0cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An importer must ensure that the following information is marked on the EEE—

- (a) the importer’s name, registered trade name or registered trade mark; and
- (b) an address at which the importer can be contacted.

(2) Where it is not possible to mark the information on the EEE the information may instead be marked on the packaging of the EEE or in a document accompanying the EEE.

(3) Where the importer complies with other applicable EU legislation, or legislation giving effect to EU legislation, containing provisions for the affixing of the importer’s name and address which are at least as stringent as those set out in this regulation, it is sufficient to satisfy this regulation.
