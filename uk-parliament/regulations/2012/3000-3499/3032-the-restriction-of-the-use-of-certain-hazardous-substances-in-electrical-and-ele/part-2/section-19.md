# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 19

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 19. Register of EEE

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A manufacturer must keep a register of any EEE placed on the market that the manufacturer has manufactured, or had designed and manufactured,—

- (a) in relation to which any provision of these Regulations has not been complied with; or
- (b) which has been recalled,

and keep distributors informed of these matters.
