# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 28

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Duty in certain circumstances to comply with manufacturers’ duties in place of importers’ duties

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An importer who places EEE on the market under the importer’s name or trademark must comply with all of the duties imposed by these Regulations on manufacturers, and in such a case, a reference to the manufacturer in these Regulations is to be taken as being a reference to the importer.

(2) Such an importer is not required to comply with the duties imposed by these Regulations on importers.
