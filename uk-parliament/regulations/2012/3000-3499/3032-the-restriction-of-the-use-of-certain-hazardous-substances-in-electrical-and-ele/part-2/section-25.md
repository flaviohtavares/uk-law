# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 25

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Monitoring of EEE

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An importer must keep a register of any EEE which they have placed on the market—

- (a) in relation to which any provision of these Regulations has not been complied with; or
- (b) any EEE which has been recalled,

and keep distributors informed of these matters.
