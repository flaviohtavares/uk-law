# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 13

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 13. ... Declaration of conformity and UK marking[^M_E_660b4364-0495-44d5-e29a-7f8ce6eee52a][^c-58c9ea4f3f31c57bae704963dcabe988][^c-1c75b137896437f0cf4313480491bd03]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the compliance of the EEE with the requirements of regulation 3 has been demonstrated by the procedure referred to in regulation 12, a manufacturer must—

- (a) draw up a[^c-5807225c5799104005ca5cd9ff0500d0] declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 14; and
- (b) affix the UK[^c-63e2d18055f00db2a22463447d4fd72d] marking in relation to the EEE in accordance with regulation 16.

## 13. EU declaration of conformity and CE marking[^M_E_ae52ae5e-bba9-43dd-bcc1-4c35139bb0cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the compliance of the EEE with the requirements of regulation 3 has been demonstrated by the procedure referred to in regulation 12, a manufacturer must—

- (a) draw up an EU declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 14; and
- (b) affix the CE marking in relation to the EEE in accordance with regulation 16.
