# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 3

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 3. Restriction on the use of certain hazardous substances in EEE[^M_E_d7d182d3-db6a-4d8c-f112-de3bdbabfe66]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) and (4) EEE placed on the market must not contain the substances listed in Schedule A1[^key-923d3f3e8e64c51784b638cdb71eae25].

(2) The presence of those substances in quantities no greater than the maximum concentration value by weight in homogeneous materials as specified in Schedule A1[^key-279fa147067fc676c6add1e7d43b4004], is allowed.

(3) In paragraph (2) “homogeneous material” means one material of uniform composition throughout or a material, consisting of a combination of materials, that cannot be disjointed or separated into different materials by mechanical actions such as unscrewing, cutting, crushing, grinding and abrasive processes.

(4) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to the applications listed in Schedule A2[^key-2087e56f9179e53cb54cecefa547c581].

## 3. Restriction on the use of certain hazardous substances in EEE[^M_E_ae52ae5e-bba9-43dd-bcc1-4c35139bb0cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) and (4) EEE placed on the market must not contain the substances listed in Annex II to the Directive, as amended from time to time before or after IP completion day[^V_NI_69c5c142-a02e-3476-6ffd-f30b462d554d].

(2) The presence of those substances in quantities no greater than the maximum concentration value by weight in homogeneous materials as specified in that Annex, as so amended, is allowed.

(3) In paragraph (2) “homogeneous material” means one material of uniform composition throughout or a material, consisting of a combination of materials, that cannot be disjointed or separated into different materials by mechanical actions such as unscrewing, cutting, crushing, grinding and abrasive processes.

(4) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to the applications listed in Annex III and IV to the Directive, as amended from time to time before or after IP completion day[^V_NI_366c0e10-bcd9-24c2-3207-833c276e5a62].
