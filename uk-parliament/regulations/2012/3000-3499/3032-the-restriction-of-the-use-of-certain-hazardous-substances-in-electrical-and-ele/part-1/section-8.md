# The Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012, section 8

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 8. Existing legislation[^M_E_3bd67135-1013-4969-b5c7-96e8755aa050]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing in these Regulations affects the application of assimilated[^c-712a89ff1924ffdba2ceb29d4e71cb25][^c-8b5be0764d05fe8cc25aadabd924f615] law as regards requirements in relation to—

- (a) safety and health;
- (b) chemicals, in particular as set out in Regulation [(EC) No 1907/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1907) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals[^f00010]; and
- (c) specific assimilated[^c-1271253cb79f3451bdab83a31ca0ab85] law on waste management.

## 8. Existing legislation[^M_E_ae52ae5e-bba9-43dd-bcc1-4c35139bb0cf]

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations affects the application of existing EU legislation or legislation giving effect to EU legislation as regards requirements in relation to—

- (a) safety and health;
- (b) chemicals, in particular as set out in Regulation [(EC) No 1907/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1907) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals; and
- (c) specific EU waste management legislation.
