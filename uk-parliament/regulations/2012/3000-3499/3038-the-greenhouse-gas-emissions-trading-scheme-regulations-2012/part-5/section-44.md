## 44. Power to determine reportable emissions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A power of the regulator to make a conservative estimate of emissions in accordance with Article 70 of the Monitoring and Reporting Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 2018[^c-30b30b98dfa58fbba556322bb899e71b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] (a “determination of emissions”) may also be exercised where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) an operator fails to comply with the requirement to submit— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (i) a surrender report in accordance with paragraph 4(1)(a) and (b) of Schedule 4; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] a revocation report in accordance with paragraph 5(1)(a) and (b) of Schedule 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) an operator has failed to satisfy the regulator as required pursuant to paragraph 2(3)(c) of Schedule 4 or paragraph 3(8)(c) of Schedule 5; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] or[^c-5e1c7b728ce55a58677406d1745d011d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (c)[^c-fcd1f3529475bfa505265cc881f7a5fe] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) the regulator considers that such a determination is necessary for the purpose of imposing, or considering whether to impose, a penalty under Part 7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) In the case referred to in paragraph (1)(b), in making the determination the regulator may substitute an emissions factor of greater than zero for the factor reported in respect of the bioliquids concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) The regulator may make a determination of emissions where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) the operator has failed to submit a report as required pursuant to paragraph 3(8)(b) of Schedule 5; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the regulator has reason to believe that the report submitted is incorrect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4) A determination of emissions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) must be notified to the operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-5fee73c11500ad753249b7c0c00399fb]... concerned; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) except where paragraph (5B) applies to that determination,[^c-8117e55dce5cb45bf20e4ee3e86fc01b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] is to be treated as determining all of the reportable emissions from the installation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-7eadc1985780611e64f695c66596ffe1]... for the period to which the determination relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5) A notice under paragraph (4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] or under paragraph (5C)(a)[^c-02c2f2c35bfcd290294efc5af811fc6c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]—

- (a) except where it relates to an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] Article 27[^c-0420f1ab68385a7c2a238c3c487d6c00] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] installation, must be served on the registry administrator (and is in that case to be regarded as an instruction to the registry administrator for the purposes of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] Article 31(6) of the Registries Regulation 2019[^c-bb3fd8bf96ab2996aec55f57a9788c06] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) must, where required by Article 70(2) of the Monitoring and Reporting Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 2018[^c-4d919834d09cd37e4fa00de3b8d94191] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc], specify the corrections that are required to the verified report mentioned *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-df2098f389c772faf2fc432503696739]... in paragraph 2(3)(b) of Schedule 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5A)[^c-18af2047cfdab77ceec3648527d952e5] Paragraph (5B) applies where – *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) a determination of emissions has been notified to the operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-83620596d39b8790d03fd1b7f2d04411]... under paragraph (4); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the regulator is satisfied that there is a defect in the estimate of the reportable emissions in that determination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5B) The regulator must withdraw the determination and make a further determination of emissions (the “rectified determination”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5C) A rectified determination— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) must be notified to the operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-55166daee3481ecd2e47078240caa67d]... concerned; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) is to be treated as determining all of the reportable emissions from the installation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-53b58ba67f1c406ddc140cf88766d823]... for the period to which the determination relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5D) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) any rectified determination is notified to the operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-9d1265b07ccd7829cfe9c08ee7d6036f]... concerned; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the regulator is satisfied that there is a defect in the estimate of the reportable emissions in that determination, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

the regulator must withdraw the rectified determination and make one or more further rectified determinations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(6) A regulator who makes a determination of emissions under the Monitoring and Reporting Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 2018[^c-db68f8a226968b3be1312b74a6bc40a3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc], or by virtue of this regulation, may recover the cost of doing so from the operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-d38f4d41ca60cc5f7ed3428b27d6b52f]... concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
