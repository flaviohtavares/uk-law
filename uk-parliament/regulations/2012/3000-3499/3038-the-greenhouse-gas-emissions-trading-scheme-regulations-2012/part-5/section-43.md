# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 43

## 43. Enforcement notices (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the regulator considers that a person (“P”) has contravened, is contravening, or is likely to contravene a relevant provision, the regulator may serve a notice (“enforcement notice”) on P. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) For the purpose of paragraph (1), a “relevant provision” is any provision of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the Monitoring and Reporting Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 2018[^c-07b2491b72aebe64cae1f45ae163ee67] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc];
- (c) a permit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-9ec4c5e7790edb5a9f16b34547b2d146]...
- (d)[^c-9ec4c5e7790edb5a9f16b34547b2d146] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) An enforcement notice must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) state the regulator's view under paragraph (1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) specify the matters constituting the contravention or making a contravention likely; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (c) specify the steps that must be taken to remedy the contravention or to ensure that the likely contravention does not occur; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (d) specify the period within which those steps must be taken. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4) P must comply with the requirements of the notice within the period so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5) The regulator may withdraw an enforcement notice at any time by further notice served on P. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
