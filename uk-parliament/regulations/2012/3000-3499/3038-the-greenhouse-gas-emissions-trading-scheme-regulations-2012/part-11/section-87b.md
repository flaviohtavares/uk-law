# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 87B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3038

## 87B. Unreported emissions arising before 2013 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-92edbe325fba917ae9aadd34481c9ad0][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-9b3f72289fffd55b493bd22af19709b6] Where paragraph (2) applies, a person (“P”) is not liable to an excess emissions penalty for a failure to surrender allowances in respect of those reportable emissions in a relevant year (“Y”) that exceed P's verified annual reportable emissions for that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) This paragraph applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) the regulator becomes aware that P's reportable emissions in Y exceed P's verified annual reportable emissions in respect of that year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) P failed to surrender a number of allowances equal to the unreported emissions by 30th April in the year following Y. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) Where paragraph (2) applies, P is liable to the civil penalty of the sterling equivalent of 20 Euros for each allowance that P failed to surrender by 30th April in the year following Y in respect of the unreported emissions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4) Regulation 51(1) above applies to a penalty under paragraph (3) as it applies to a penalty under Part 7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) “allowance” includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (i) where the excess emission penalty would arise under the 2010 Regulations, an aviation allowance; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] within the limits allowed by regulation 27A of the 2005 Regulations or regulation 26 of the 2010 Regulations, a project credit as defined by regulation 27 of the 2010 Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b)[^key-3902663345c9f6c09cbe30605f99b72b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) “excess emissions penalty” means the penalty under regulation 39 of the 2005 Regulations or regulation 38(1)(a) of the 2010 Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (d)[^key-c49969f38715f1d6e705041b9d358983] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) “relevant year” means a calendar year prior to 2013; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (ea (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e63a529c50daad9ab581ca4f7b6cc5c0][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] “**verified annual reportable emissions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (i) reportable emissions that are verified pursuant to regulation 10 of the 2005 Regulations or regulation 21 of the 2010 Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] reportable emissions that are determined by the regulator under regulation 30 of the 2005 Regulations or regulation 22 of the 2010 Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (f) “unreported emissions” means the emissions mentioned in paragraph (1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (g) “sterling equivalent” has the meaning given in regulation 54(7) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
