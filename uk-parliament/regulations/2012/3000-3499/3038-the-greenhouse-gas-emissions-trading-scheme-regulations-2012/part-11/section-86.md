# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 86

## 86. Savings and transitional provisions: the 2005 Regulations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Notwithstanding the revocations made by regulation 85, the following provisions of the 2005 Regulations (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**the relevant provisions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**”) continue to have effect to the extent specified below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

("1A)[^key-944a869b7e925bc7c0c521800e2b8348] the relevant provisions have effect as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) in regulation 2(1) the definition of “regulator” was amended as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (i) omit “and Wales”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] after sub-paragraph (i) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

    > - (ia (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] in relation to an installation (other than an offshore installation) which is (or will be) situated in Wales, the Natural Resources Body for Wales; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

    ;
- (b) regulation 35(5) was amended as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a) omit “and Wales”; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] in sub-paragraph (b) after “in relation to” insert “Wales,”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) Part 1 and Schedule 1 have effect for the purpose of the relevant provisions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) Regulations 16 and 17 have effect for the purposes of making an application for the surrender of a permit, or the service of a notice of revocation in respect of a failure to make such an application, where the circumstances giving rise to the requirement to make the application occurred before 1st January 2013. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4) Regulation 18(3) to (5) has effect in relation to the charging schemes referred to in that regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5) Regulation 22 (other than paragraph (2)) has effect for the purpose of allowing an operator to make an application for an allocation from the new entrant reserve (as defined by regulation 2 of the 2005 Regulations). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(6) But for the purpose of paragraph (5) above, the reference in regulation 22(22) to regulation 15(1) of the 2005 Regulations is to be read as a reference to regulation 12(1) of these Regulations (and the reference to the proposed transferee is accordingly to be read as a reference to the new operator). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(7) Subject to paragraph (8) below, regulation 26 has effect for all purposes relating to the registry referred to in Article 3(2) of the Registries Regulation 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(8) In regulation 26— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) paragraphs (2) to (5) do not have effect; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) paragraph (8) has effect as if the references to Articles 18, 20(4) and 27(5) were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(9) The following provisions have effect in so far as they relate to any activities carried out, or emissions arising, prior to 1st January 2013— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) regulation 27A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) Part 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(10) Subject to paragraph (11) below, Part 5 and Schedules 2 to 4 have effect in relation to any appeal brought against a decision or notice specified in regulation 32(1) to (5) of the 2005 Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(11) Regulation 32(4) has effect as if the reference to the appropriate authority were a reference to the First-tier tribunal (and the reference to the appropriate authority in regulation 32(7) is to be construed accordingly). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(12) Regulation 35 has effect in so far as it relates to functions carried out before 1st January 2013 or under the relevant provisions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(13) Regulation 36 has effect in so far as it relates to a civil penalty in respect of emissions arising before 1st January 2013. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(14) Regulation 37 has effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(15) Paragraph (1)(c) to (f) of regulation 38 has effect in so far as it relates to the relevant provisions, but where the conduct giving rise to the offence occurs after 31st December 2012 the following civil penalties apply *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] (subject to the regulator’s discretion under regulation 51 above)[^key-64a3f81c626405f5cc168350ef160a84] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] instead of the offences under that paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) the penalty in regulation 59 above applies instead of the offence of failing to making an application to surrender a permit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the penalties in regulation 53 above apply instead of the offence of failing to comply with a notice under regulation 22(13)(a) (and for that purpose the condition of the notice is deemed to be a condition falling within paragraph (1)(c) of regulation 53); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (c) the penalties in regulation 68 above apply instead of the offence of failing to comply with an enforcement notice; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (d) the penalty in regulation 70 above applies instead of an offence under paragraph (1)(f) of regulation 38. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(16) Subject to paragraph (17) below, regulation 38(2) and (3) has effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(17) No prosecution may be brought in respect of an offence under regulation 38(1)(a) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) the conduct that gave rise to the offence continues after 31st December 2012; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the person who has committed the offence will be liable to a civil penalty under regulation 52 above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(18) Subject to regulation 87B below, regulations[^key-b42dc416c8cf06df4f55de1d2b5d4e02] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 39 to 41 have effect in relation to a failure to surrender allowances in respect of emissions arising before 1st January 2013, and regulation 40 has effect in relation to an understatement of such emissions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(19) Parts 8 to 10 have effect in so far as they relate to functions carried out, or powers exercised, under the relevant provisions or as national administrator under the Registries Regulation 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
