# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 2

## 2. Duty to review these Regulations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] DAERA[^key-76cd1d115b196b06af26af66987df856] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] must from time to time— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) carry out a review of these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) set out the conclusions of the review in a report; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (c) publish the report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) In carrying out the review the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] DAERA[^key-2654447c8cb704033b68895fd0dccafa] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] must, so far as is reasonable, have regard to how the Directive, and measures adopted under it by the European Commission, are implemented in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^key-5431caebe09c9742d5eb17af731c5e2a]... member States. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) The report must in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) set out the objectives intended to be achieved by the regulatory system established by these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) assess the extent to which those objectives are achieved; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (c) assess whether those objectives remain appropriate and, if so, the extent to which they could be achieved with a system that imposes less regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4) The first report under this regulation must be published before the end of the period of five years beginning with the day on which these Regulations come into force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5) Reports under this regulation are afterwards to be published at intervals not exceeding five years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
