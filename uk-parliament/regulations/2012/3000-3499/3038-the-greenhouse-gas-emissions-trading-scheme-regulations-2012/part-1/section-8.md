## 8. Commission Regulations: designations[^c-33d205c6acd7073d768d90306a9f461e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) DAERA[^c-ddf957568fccd8d58b20b6da095c5f49] is the national administrator designated by the United Kingdom for the purposes of the Registries Regulation 2019[^c-84d5daeed71f7e5099f55c72d0db86a2], and in these Regulations is referred to in that capacity as the “registry administrator”.

(2)[^c-14ccfbc073548cb0ee90b4f36264379b] Subject to paragraph (3), the regulator is the competent authority designated by the United Kingdom for the purposes of the Registries Regulation 2019 (other than for Articles 22(3) and 30(7))[^c-132bc8a14e82ba79d624b29b4e197459].

(3)[^c-b34774a24773ac5d114725789d5e3ce6] DAERA is the competent authority so designated for the purposes of—

- (a) Article 28(1);
- (b) Article 30(8);
- (c) Article 30(11); and
- (d) Article 66(1).

(6)[^c-a0613547e937d195b779b58e1d6fad99] The regulator is the competent authority designated by the United Kingdom for the purposes of the Monitoring and Reporting Regulation 2018.
