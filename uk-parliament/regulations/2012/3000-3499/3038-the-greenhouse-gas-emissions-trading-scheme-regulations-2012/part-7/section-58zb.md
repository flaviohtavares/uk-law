# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 58ZB

## 58ZB (affected by repeal, see note). Carrying out regulated activity without the necessary permit (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-3843a008e5e6972fd132d01afe051dfd][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the regulator is satisfied that the operator of an Article 27a installation has— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) exceeded the maximum amount in any scheme year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) failed to notify the regulator under paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 5A, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

the operator of the installation (“P”) is subject to the civil penalty in paragraph (2) in respect of any penalty year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the civil penalty is A + (B x C), where— A is the estimated amount of the costs avoided by P in any penalty year as a result of carrying out a regulated activity without the necessary permit; B is the estimated amount of reportable emissions from the installation in the period during which a regulated activity was carried out without the necessary permit; C is the carbon price for that penalty year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) In imposing the penalty under paragraph (2), the regulator may increase the amount determined under that sub-paragraph by a percentage designed to ensure that the penalty exceeds the amount of any economic benefit that P has obtained as a result of carrying out a regulated activity without the necessary permit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4) The authority must exercise powers under regulation 40 of the Northern Ireland Regulations to give the regulator directions as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) the estimation by the regulator of A and B in paragraph (2); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the exercise of the regulator's powers under paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) “**maximum amount *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**” has the meaning given in paragraph 1(a) of Schedule 5A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) “**necessary permit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**” means the Article 27 installation emissions permit or the greenhouse gas emissions permit which P would have been required to comply with under paragraph 5(3) or (4) of Schedule 5A if P had not failed to notify the regulator under paragraph 4(1) of that Schedule; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (c) “**penalty year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**” means any scheme year during which P would have been required to comply with the conditions of the necessary permit, if P had not failed to notify the regulator under paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 5A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
