## 56. Failure to pay a penalty for exceeding an emissions target for an (affected by repeal, see note) Article 27 (affected by repeal, see note) installation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-f654f2e565177c992325a5c7a3107c2d][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An operator of an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] Article 27 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] installation is liable to the civil penalties in paragraph (2) where the operator fails to pay a penalty imposed under regulation 55 by the date specified in the penalty notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) The civil penalties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) 10% of the penalty imposed under regulation 55; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) £150 for each day that the operator fails to pay that penalty following service of a penalty notice in respect of the penalty under sub-paragraph (a), up to a maximum of £13,500. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
