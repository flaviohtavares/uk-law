## 53. Failure to comply with a condition of a permit (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An operator is liable to the civil penalties in paragraph (3) where the operator fails to comply (or comply on time) with a condition of a permit included pursuant to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) paragraph 2(1)(e)(ii) or (iv) of Schedule 4 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^key-01ea97a788d6b16d848e9f51bf7ee50f]...;
- (b) paragraph 3(1)(g), (h) or (i) of Schedule 5; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (c) regulation 10 of the 2005 Regulations, other than regulation 10(3) and (4) (or such a condition as modified by virtue of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] regulation 88[^key-9242fdde4e8bf820654c286993f32f62] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] of these Regulations). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) However, an operator is not liable to those civil penalties where the failure to comply gives rise to a penalty under regulation 57. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) The civil penalties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) £20,000[^key-3e4d7e3ae6a30a86d65658afca95ecc2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) £500[^key-ba5ab39deca1428c6d9758b6ec460461] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] for each day that the operator fails to comply with the condition following service of a penalty notice, up to a maximum of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] £45,000[^key-97f2bcdc4b1b5c87435cc5c974f27997] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc].
