# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 69

## 69. Failure to comply with an information notice (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person (“P”) is liable to the civil penalties in paragraph (2) where P fails to comply (or to comply on time) with the requirements of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] a notice[^key-5cda0dbb5fed6a6b55079beb4e5d5e6e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] served under regulation 45(2) (an “information notice”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) The civil penalties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) £5,000[^key-4a3b59abdf7ac7a955391ad91360a255] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) £500[^key-c0623230529edcf09a38aab9c00a256d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] for each day that P fails to comply with the requirements of the information notice, following service of a penalty notice, up to a maximum of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] £45,000[^key-61716955110e1232bb22ad38d3e855c0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc].
