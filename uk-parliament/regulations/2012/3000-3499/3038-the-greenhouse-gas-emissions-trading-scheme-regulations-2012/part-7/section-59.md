# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3038

## 59. Failure to surrender a permit

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where an operator fails to make an application to surrender a permit, contrary to regulation 13(1) and (2), the operator is liable to a civil penalty of £5,000.
