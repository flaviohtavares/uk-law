# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3038

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Carbon Price (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In respect of the scheme year beginning with 1st January 2013, the carbon price is £6.70. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) From the scheme year beginning with 1st January 2021, and for each subsequent scheme year, DAERA[^key-d41c97e011de4249fe7835ccdaa9c012] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] must determine a price as the carbon price for that year, based on the sterling equivalent of the average end of day settlement price (in Euro per tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent) of the December futures contracts for that scheme year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) For that purpose— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- “**average end of day settlement price *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**” means the average over the 12 months ending with the relevant date; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- “**futures contract *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**” means the futures contract as traded on the single largest carbon market exchange (as determined by volume of sales in the 12 months ending with the relevant date); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- “**sterling equivalent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**” means the sterling equivalent converted by reference to the Bank of England annual average spot exchange rate for the 12 months ending with the relevant date; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- “**the relevant date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**”, in relation to the year for which the carbon price is set, is 11th November in the preceding year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4) DAERA[^key-368d481c3678a3064f4c8c45a7e5f353] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] must publish a determination made under paragraph (2) one month before the beginning the scheme year in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
