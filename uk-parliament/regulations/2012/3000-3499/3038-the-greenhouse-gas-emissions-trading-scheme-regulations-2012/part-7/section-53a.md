# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 53A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3038

## 53A. Failure to transfer or surrender allowances where underreporting discovered after transfer (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0a5ec9c3c3338392a43f36cd5763616b][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person is liable to a civil penalty where the person fails— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) to effect a transfer (or to effect a transfer on time) of allowances, contrary to regulation 12A(3) (transfer of permits: underreporting discovered after transfer); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) to surrender (or to surrender on time) allowances, contrary to regulation 12A(4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) The civil penalty is the sterling equivalent of 20 Euros for each allowance that the person failed to transfer or surrender. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
