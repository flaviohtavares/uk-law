# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3038
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**carbon price**”, in relation to a tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent, is the price referred to in regulation 49;
- “**penalty notice**” means a notice served under regulation 50(1);
- “**additional penalty notice**” means a notice served under regulation 50(3).
