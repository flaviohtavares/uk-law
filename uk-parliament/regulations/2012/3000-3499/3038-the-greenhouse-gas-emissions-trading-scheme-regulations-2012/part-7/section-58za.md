# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 58ZA

## 58ZA (affected by repeal, see note). Exceeding the maximum amount for an Article 27a installation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3843a008e5e6972fd132d01afe051dfd][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The operator of an Article 27a installation is liable to the civil penalty in sub-paragraph (2) where in any scheme year the installation exceeds the maximum amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) The civil penalty is (A – B) x C, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

A is the reportable emissions arising in the scheme year; B is the maximum amount; C is the carbon price for that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) In this regulation, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**maximum amount *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**” has the meaning given in paragraph 1(a) of Schedule 5A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
