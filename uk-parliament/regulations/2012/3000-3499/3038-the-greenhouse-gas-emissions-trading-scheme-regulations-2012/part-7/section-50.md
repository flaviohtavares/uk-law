# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3038
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Penalty notices (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the regulator is satisfied that a person (“P”) is liable to a civil penalty under this Part the regulator must (subject to regulation 51) serve a notice on P. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) The penalty notice must specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) the regulation under which that liability arises; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the amount of the civil penalty due; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (c) where appropriate, how that amount is calculated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (d) whether or not P may be liable to a civil penalty in accordance with regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 53(3)(b), 56(2)(b)[^key-03c39f706d6b7b0db1933295530cad48] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc], 68(2)(b), or 69(2)(b) (an “additional daily penalty”); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (e) if P will not be liable to an additional daily penalty, the date by which the penalty for which P is liable must be paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) Subject to regulation 51 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^key-5c6a65b30e93bd1f17f0266deb122620]..., where the regulator is satisfied that P is liable to an additional daily penalty the regulator must, when the amount of that additional daily penalty can be determined, serve a notice on P specifying— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) the total amount of the civil penalties due; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the date by which that amount must be paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4)[^key-587d1c13bde18db4df5450fc98e3bb1b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) A civil penalty imposed by a penalty notice or an additional penalty notice must be paid to the regulator by the date specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(6) Any such civil penalty is recoverable by the regulator— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) as a civil debt; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^key-99c0383f4012b4228f4dbcc310bd4c0d]...
- (b)[^key-99c0383f4012b4228f4dbcc310bd4c0d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) the regulator must, as soon as is reasonably practicable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) give notice to the authority of the service of any penalty notice or additional penalty notice; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) pass to the authority any civil penalty that has been paid to the regulator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
