## SCHEDULE 12 (affected by repeal, see note) — Appeals (Northern Ireland) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 78(4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

**1.** (1) A person who wishes to appeal to the Planning Appeals Commission (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**the appeals commission *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**”) under regulation 73(1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] or 74(1)[^c-71b992a895fb48267511b043182c191d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] against a decision of the chief inspector *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] or registry administrator[^c-c04c57258c68fde87a730366ba7d0206] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] must give to the appeals commission written notice of the appeal together with a statement of the grounds of appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) The appeals commission must as soon as is reasonably practicable send to the regulator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] or registry administrator[^c-960b2c2ba37b9e7a0c5c6dee4b93ad94] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] a copy of that notice and that statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) An appellant may withdraw an appeal by notifying the appeals commission; and the appeals commission must as soon as is reasonably practicable notify the regulator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] or registry administrator[^c-f30df03a30b546b7e897a7302a992541] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] of that withdrawal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

**2.** Notice of appeal in accordance with paragraph 1 is to be given before the expiry of the period of 47 days beginning with the date on which the decision takes effect.

**3.** (1) The appeals commission must determine the appeal and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] section 204(1), (3) and (4) of the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 applies[^c-484565ae75937f93663cdb23e1bb1ac7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] in relation to the determination of the appeal as they apply in relation to the determination of an appeal under that Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) The appeals commission must determine the process for determining appeals taking into account any requests of either party to the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

**4.** An appeal under this Schedule must be accompanied by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] any fee for the appeal prescribed in regulations made under section 223(7)(b) of the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011; and for that purpose section 223(7)(b) has effect as if a reference to an appeal under that Act[^c-d57d7d2087ab72d2e72cec86eccc32e4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] included a reference to an appeal under these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
