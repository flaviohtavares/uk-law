# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 14

## 14. Revocation of permits (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The regulator— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) may at any time revoke a permit by serving on the operator a notice to that effect (a “revocation notice”), and in particular may do so if the operator has failed to pay a fee for the subsistence of the permit; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b)[^c-44971df55019b45c6e02f4bb181b1bd5] must do so where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (i) the regulator becomes aware that the operator has failed to comply with regulation 13(1) and (2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] a greenhouse gas emissions permit is held by the operator of an Article 27a installation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] an Article 27 installation emissions permit is held by the operator of an Article 27a installation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) A revocation notice takes effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) 28 days after the date on which it is served; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) if a later date is specified in the notice, on that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) Paragraph 5 of Schedule 4 makes further provision about the revocation of permits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
