# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 12A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3038

## 12A. Transfer of permits: underreporting discovered after transfer (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8af9d1cebf46a09eb4d889879496eaf4][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) after the transfer of a greenhouse gas emissions permit under regulation 12 takes effect, the regulator becomes aware, following a determination of reportable emissions under regulation 44, of an error in a report submitted for a scheme year by the transferring operator under the monitoring and reporting requirements of the permit; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) as a result of the error, the transferring operator failed to comply with the surrender requirements of the permit in respect of the scheme year to which the error relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) The regulator must give notice to the transferring operator of the error as soon as reasonably practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) The transferring operator must, within the period of one month beginning with the date the transferring operator received the notice, effect a transfer to the new operator of allowances equal to the reportable emissions in respect of which, as a result of the error, the transferring operator failed to comply with the surrender condition of the permit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4) The new operator must surrender the allowances within the period of one month beginning with the date of the transfer of the allowances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5) In paragraph (1), the reference to the transfer of a permit under regulation 12 includes a reference to an application for a transfer of a permit to which effect is given by a variation of the new operator’s existing permit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(6) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) “transferring operator” means the person who was the current operator for the purposes of regulation 12; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) “new operator” has the same meaning as in regulation 12. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
