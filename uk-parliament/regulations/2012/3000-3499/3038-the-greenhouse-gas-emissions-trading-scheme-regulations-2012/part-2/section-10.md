# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3038

## 10. Applications for and grant of permits (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The operator of an installation (other than an excluded installation) may apply to the regulator for a greenhouse gas emissions permit to carry out a regulated activity at the installation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) The operator of an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] Article 27[^c-1a2eed05c48baa1df52ec8c2648d823b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] installation may apply to the regulator for an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] Article 27 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] installation emissions permit to carry out a regulated activity at the installation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) However, an application may not be made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) under paragraph (1) or (2) where a permit has already been granted in respect of the installation and continues to have effect; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) under paragraph (2) where an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] Article 27[^c-1a4898417767dfc538a54a6f1ecaeffa] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] installation emissions permit has been granted in respect of the installation and has been surrendered or revoked. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3A)[^c-1b45290bb1f935c8751405973c99f0ac] An Article 27 installation emissions permit that is granted for the purposes of the first allocation period continues to have effect for the purposes of the second allocation period if the installation is deemed to be approved by the European Commission under the first subparagraph of Article 27(2) of the Directive in relation to that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4) Following an application under paragraph (1) or (2), the permit must be granted if the regulator is satisfied that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) the application is duly made, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) at the time that the permit is granted (or, if later, has effect) the applicant will be capable of monitoring and reporting emissions from the installation in accordance with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (i) the monitoring and reporting requirements of the greenhouse gas emissions permit, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] the monitoring and reporting conditions of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] Article 27 installation emissions[^c-1cd983b94db4672f01932c77f2fe988e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] permit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

but must otherwise be refused. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5) A permit may be granted under this regulation in respect of more than one installation on the same site, provided that they are operated by the same operator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(6) Paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 makes further provision about applications for permits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(7) Paragraph 2 of Schedule 4 makes provision about the contents of greenhouse gas emissions permits, and paragraph 3 of Schedule 5 makes provision about the contents of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] Article 27[^c-7ee5c0127ffa969d82e23bffe3b862c6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] installation emissions permits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
