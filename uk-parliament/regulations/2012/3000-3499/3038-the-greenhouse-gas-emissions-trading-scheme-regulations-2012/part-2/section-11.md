## 11. Review, variation and consolidation of permits (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-abe94cf2552707a69320fc1981f6b02c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The regulator may, by giving notice to the operator, vary a permit at any time and may in particular make any variation of the permit that the regulator considers necessary in consequence of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a)[^c-c7e91f5220801946ddb8740e9c8c2d73] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) any report made by the operator under Article 69 of the Monitoring and Reporting Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 2018[^c-6197dd6492b0af6a0db40c58819a500a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (c) any notification as mentioned in paragraph 2(7)(b) of Schedule 4 (notification of planned changes in operation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] etc *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]).

(3) The regulator may by giving notice to the operator vary a permit where the operator— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) applies to the regulator for such a variation pursuant to a provision of the permit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) has failed to comply with a requirement of the permit to apply for such a variation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4) The regulator may by giving notice to the operator vary a permit in order to comply with regulator's duty under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 88(2) or (4)[^c-d6874a8851aed9c0ab56209e105479e7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) any of the following provisions of Schedule 5— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (i) paragraph 2(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] paragraph 3(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] paragraph 6(5) or (6); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] paragraph 7(4)(b), (6)(b) or (7)(b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (v) paragraph 8(6) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] or 8(6A)[^c-edf017cb0f99f1a175ca2200fe982c65] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc].

(5) A notice given under paragraph (2), (3)(b) or (4) may specify a period within which a fee for the variation of the permit must be paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(6) The regulator may by giving notice to the operator replace a permit with a consolidated permit applying to the same regulated activities, and containing the same or equivalent provisions, in the following circumstances— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) where the permit has been varied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) where there is more than one permit applying to installations on the same site operated by the same operator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
