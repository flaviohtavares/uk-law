# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3038
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Requirement for permit to carry out regulated activities (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-dd231e6bb6ae8742dceba0680471d7c1] No person may carry out a regulated activity at an installation except to the extent authorised by a permit held by the operator of the installation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2)[^key-77bc630ac97b9b69558ad298807f5f47] Paragraph (1) does not apply in relation to a person carrying out a regulated activity at an Article 27a installation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
