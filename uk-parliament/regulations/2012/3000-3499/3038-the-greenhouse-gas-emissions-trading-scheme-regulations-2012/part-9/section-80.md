- (a) an operator who carried out no regulated activity in the year to which the data would relate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^key-5232a6dd718e4f92e4e2e6888c4d462d]...

- (b) marking the emissions as verified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4) The operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^key-133df4857b6fb3eb965713f462b3e77d]... is responsible for complying with the requirement under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] Article 14(1)[^key-56d889a07e3041a67c62dc1a913bb7be] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] to provide information to the registry administrator and request the opening of a registry account. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(5) In complying with the requirement mentioned in paragraph (4), the operator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^key-ee30f8e3badfcc3d9f410fa76374e0f4]... must provide such evidence of identity and address as may be required by the registry administrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(6) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) an operator fails to comply with regulation 41, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) a UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] aircraft operator fails to comply with regulation 42, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

the registry administrator must set the relevant registry account to blocked status until the compliance status figure for the installation or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] aircraft operator, calculated in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] Article 37[^key-556582c95f82f13af26be9177c8dc67c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc], is greater than or equal to zero. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(7) This paragraph applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) an operator is required to submit a report to the regulator by the terms of a notice of surrender or a revocation notice; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the operator— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (i) fails to submit the report to the regulator within the time specified in the report; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] submits an incomplete report to the regulator within the time so specified; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] submits within the time so specified a report to the regulator that cannot be verified in whole or in part in accordance with the monitoring and reporting requirements for the installation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(8) Where paragraph (7) applies, the registry administrator must set the relevant operator holding account to blocked status until— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) the report has been prepared and verified in accordance with the requirements of paragraph 4(1)(b) or 5(1)(b) of Schedule 4 and has been submitted to the regulator; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the regulator has notified, in accordance with regulation 44(4)(a), a determination of the reportable emissions referred to in paragraph 4(1)(a) or 5(1)(a) of that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(10) Where the registry administrator sets a registry account to blocked status pursuant to paragraph (6), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] or (8)[^key-c9ab433642aa5386d9a7651e96d54f21] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] it must notify the account holder specifying the reason why, and the period during which, the relevant registry account will be blocked. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(12) The registry administrator must, as soon as is reasonably practicable, notify the European Commission in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] Article 52(2) or 55(2)[^key-6a6de222b1ed7ed7ed5cb5d93bcb5342] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] of any changes to the allocation table or aviation allocation table (other than those falling within paragraph (11)(a)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(13) This paragraph applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) a notice of surrender or a revocation notice has been given and has taken effect; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the operator is unable to comply with the requirement to surrender allowances imposed by that notice by the date specified in the notice, due to the suspension of access to the relevant registry account by the registry administrator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^key-dadcb81d0fd2b6f2cbd45971a0829acc]....

(14) Where paragraph (13) applies, the registry administrator must, if so requested by the operator, surrender the number of allowances specified in the notice of surrender or the revocation notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(15) The registry administrator or the KP registry administrator may refuse to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) open any account in the Union Registry or the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] Registry, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) approve an authorised representative or an additional authorised representative in relation to such an account, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

where it is *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] not[^key-50271bed8b7f7f701db9f2add4a353c6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] satisfied that the proposed account holder, authorised representative or additional authorised representative is *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^key-92a671b894ecb5ea26a585798ea36c5f]... a fit and proper person to hold such an account or, as the case may be, act as such a representative. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(16) The registry administrator may *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] decide that national public holidays in Northern Ireland are not working days under Article 35(8)[^key-66dd5b37337d3b2abd05fc8d1035b096] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc], provided that the decision to do so is published by the registry administrator by 1st December in the previous scheme year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
