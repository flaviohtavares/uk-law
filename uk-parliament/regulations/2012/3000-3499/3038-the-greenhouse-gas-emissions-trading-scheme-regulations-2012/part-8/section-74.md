## 74. Rights of appeal: registries (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who is aggrieved by a decision of the registry administrator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] or KP registry administrator[^c-7d8dfc9cf6fa2c2d4a648b326ed6c24a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] referred to in a provision of the Registries Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 2019[^c-6a572acd1b3fef2a63cc2fca4b38cfc3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] mentioned in paragraph (2) may exercise the right to object given by that provision by appealing to the appeal body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2)[^c-851c168a87fe7310c57e9b3fb4f78847] Those provisions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) Article 19(4); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) Article 21(6); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (c) Article 22(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (d) Article 28(5); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (e) Article 30(7); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (f) Article 80(8). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3)[^c-3fb5f8f4b24e8a5da4d1c6f4434c8a7e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
