# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 77

## 77. Determination of an appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In determining an appeal under regulation 73(1) the appeal body may, subject to paragraph (3)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) affirm the decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) quash the decision or vary any of its terms; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (c) substitute a deemed refusal with a decision of the appeal body; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (d) give directions to the regulator as to the exercise of the regulator's functions under these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2)[^key-c3d926868b12d2ebed8fca2e1e2d789d] In determining an appeal under regulation 74, the appeal body may give directions to the registry administrator or the KP registry administrator as to the exercise of their functions under the Registries Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 2019[^key-149b78ee747b78e456f95d937c786601] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc].

(3) The appeal body may not make a determination that would result in a decision which could not otherwise have been made under these Regulations or under the Registries Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 2019[^key-1431a0ba704e74e2a8f136657b250b1c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc].
