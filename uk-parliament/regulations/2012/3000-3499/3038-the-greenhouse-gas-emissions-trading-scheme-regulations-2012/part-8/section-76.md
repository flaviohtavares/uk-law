## 76. Effect of an appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] (2) and (3)[^c-5e2bb4c27a37750eaf324afa2c930f5a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc], the bringing of an appeal under regulation 73 suspends the effect of the decision pending the final determination or withdrawal of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) The bringing of an appeal does not suspend the effect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) a decision refusing an application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) a deemed refusal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (c) a notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] or notification[^c-94a1be118fac08929b7544bd054c7371] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (i) regulation 11(2), (3)(b) or (4); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4437ded12bac819643dc4d3bdf3cdc8e][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] regulation 43(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] paragraph 8(1) or (4) of Schedule 5; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-b1e642e9e2a140b6d8e9b82ddb91adc8]...
  - (v)[^c-c68efc6c48ffa7d3f210706283cf63ba] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (vi (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-87ffbb3b4e806825624d27a686ed292c][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Where (following an application for a permit or for the transfer of a permit) a permit has been granted or varied, the bringing of an appeal against the provisions of the permit or the terms of the variation does not suspend the effect of those provisions or terms. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4)[^c-8203af1713392bfc3b9f8b04a281d231] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) The bringing of an appeal against a determination of emissions under regulation 44(4) suspends the effect of the decision only for the purpose of assessing whether there has been compliance with regulation 41 or 42. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(6) The bringing of an appeal under regulation 74 does not suspend the effect of the decision pending the final determination or withdrawal of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
