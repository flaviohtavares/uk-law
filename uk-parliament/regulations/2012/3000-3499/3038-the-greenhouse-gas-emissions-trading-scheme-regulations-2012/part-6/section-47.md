## 47. National security (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No information may be published— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a)[^c-0570727c44ee8e50a813aa09f293f39a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) by the regulator under regulation 71, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

if, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, the publication of that information would be contrary to the interests of national security. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the Secretary of State may give to the regulator directions specifying information which may not be published under regulation 71. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) The regulator must notify the Secretary of State of any information which is excluded from publication in accordance with directions under paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
