## 45. Provision of information (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-e340f258186368eedaaefc17d104e9ea]... may, by notice served on a regulator (“R”), require R to furnish such information about the discharge of R's functions as the authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-e340f258186368eedaaefc17d104e9ea]... may require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) For the purposes mentioned in paragraph (4), an authority, the Secretary of State, the registry administrator, the KP registry administrator or a regulator (a “relevant body”) may, by notice served on any person, require that person (“P”) to furnish such information as is specified in the notice, in such form and within such period following service of the notice or at such time as is so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) The information which P may be required to furnish by a notice under paragraph (2) includes information, which, although it is not in P's possession or would not otherwise come into P's possession, is information which it is reasonable to require P to compile for the purpose of complying with the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(4) The purposes referred to in paragraph (2) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) the discharge of the relevant body's functions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-6b43b4f9e528465b355d65b1f1570df2]...
- (b)[^c-6b43b4f9e528465b355d65b1f1570df2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c-15db697aebbfb7dc729e6b321d1df69e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) In this regulation, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**functions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**” means functions under or by virtue of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) the Monitoring and Reporting Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 2018[^c-943195fd069a672b9cff96f581f55a30] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc];
- (c) the Verification Regulation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (d) the Registries Regulation 2010; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c-b01075ff6a1b3753edcb501fffe09400]...
- (e) the Registries Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 2013[^c-22682779fe42d0925dd545bbe191ecab] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]; or[^c-468f4b40be7f9576a2b78cbce130ae8b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (ea (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] the Registries Regulation 2019 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
