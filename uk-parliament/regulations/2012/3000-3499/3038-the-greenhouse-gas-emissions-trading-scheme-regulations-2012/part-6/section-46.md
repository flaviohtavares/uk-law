# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 46

## 46. Disclosure of information (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) a relevant body (within the meaning of regulation 45(2)) must not disclose or publish any information provided to the relevant body under these Regulations except where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- (a) disclosure or publication is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (i) required in these Regulations or otherwise by law; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] necessary for the performance of the relevant body's functions (as defined by regulation 45(6)); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb340735e63409d6744fd8baec2e5ebc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] made with the consent of the person by or on behalf of whom the information was provided; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0e85eafcedc8863102fa276b2c510df4][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] necessary for the performance of the Environment Agency’s functions in England under the Emissions Performance Standard Regulations 2015; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1aaada1cbccd3acf3c519c9342eedb61][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] necessary for the performance of the NRBW’s functions in Wales under the Emissions Performance Standard (Enforcement) (Wales) Regulations 2015. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

  *Extent: N.I.*

  - (v)[^c-4a208be9e8c9e3ffb86495e6105d300d] necessary for the performance of the chief inspector’s functions in Northern Ireland under the Emissions Performance Standard Monitoring and Enforcement Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- (b) disclosure is between one relevant body and another. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) The Secretary of State may use any information held or obtained for the purposes of these Regulations, and may share such information with other government bodies, for the purpose of preparing and publishing national energy and emissions statistics, including the preparation and publication of a national inventory. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(3) For the purpose of paragraph (2), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**national inventory *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**” means the estimation, under Article 4(1)(a) of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c21480301], of anthropogenic emissions of greenhouse gases by sources and removals of all greenhouse gases by sinks not controlled by the Montreal Protocol. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
