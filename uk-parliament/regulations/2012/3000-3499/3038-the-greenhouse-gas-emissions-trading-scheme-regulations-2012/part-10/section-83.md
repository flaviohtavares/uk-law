# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Regulations 2012, section 83

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3038

## 83. Consequences of non-payment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The regulator is not required to perform a function for which a fee is payable in relation to a person who has not paid a fee which that person is liable to pay.
