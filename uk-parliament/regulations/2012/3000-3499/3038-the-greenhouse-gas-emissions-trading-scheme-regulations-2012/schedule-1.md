## SCHEDULE 1 (affected by repeal, see note) — Application to the Crown (affected by repeal, see note) etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc][^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 4 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

### 1. Crown application

Subject to paragraphs 2 and 3, these Regulations bind the Crown.

### 2. Entry to Crown premises (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(1) If the Secretary of State considers that in the interests of national security particular powers of entry must not be used in relation to particular Crown premises, the Secretary of State may certify that those powers must not be used in relation to those premises. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

(2) In this paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

- “**Crown premises *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**” means premises held or used by or on behalf of the Crown; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]
- “**power of entry *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]**” means a power of entry exercisable under section 108 of the Environment Act 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] [^c21480361] or regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] 30[^c-787aae43460981fdfec033c9c1e6d37c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc] of the Northern Ireland Regulations, in relation to a function under these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ff0f761e51ae43a5920475cc265851cc]

### 3. Service on certain Crown operators

(1) This paragraph applies in relation to an installation operated by a person acting on behalf of—

- (a) the Royal Household;
- (b) the Duchy of Lancaster; or
- (c) the Duke of Cornwall or other possessor of the Duchy of Cornwall.

(2) In relation to the serving or giving of notices or other documents under these Regulations, the following person must be treated as the operator—

- (a) in relation to sub-paragraph (1)(a), the Keeper of the Privy Purse;
- (b) in relation to sub-paragraph (1)(b), the person appointed by the Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster;
- (c) in relation to sub-paragraph (1)(c), the person appointed by the Duke of Cornwall or other possessor of the Duchy of Cornwall.
