# The Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012, section 29

## 29. General

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part makes provision in relation to the making and withdrawal of determinations under section 9 of the Act about Licensed Work.

(2) In this Part, “**Licensed Work**” means the provision of any of the following forms of civil legal services—

- (a) family help (higher); or
- (b) legal representation that is not Controlled Work or Special Case Work.
