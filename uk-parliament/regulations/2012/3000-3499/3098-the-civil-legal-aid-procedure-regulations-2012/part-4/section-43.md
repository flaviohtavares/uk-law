# The Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-06-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3098
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Reasons

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the Director has—

- (a) determined that the services which are the subject of the application are not civil legal services described in Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Act;
- (b) determined that an individual does not qualify for particular services;
- (c) determined that an individual does qualify but not on the terms requested in the application (whether because of a limitation or condition to which the determination has been made subject or otherwise);
- (d) amended a limitation or condition to which the determination is subject; or
- (e) withdrawn a determination,

the Director must notify the individual.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), in the circumstances[^c-cef9186c4a14ba40d5f2c6e69a3eb237] described in paragraph (1), the Director must send to the individual and provider or proposed provider—

- (a) written notice of the determination, amendment or withdrawal of a determination in a form specified by the Lord Chancellor;
- (b) written reasons for the determination, amendment or withdrawal of a determination; and
- (c) notice of the right to a review of the determination, amendment or withdrawal of a determination,

and may provide information about alternative ways of obtaining or funding civil legal services.

(3)[^c-d897a48bd8fad214269b01c77073cfff] Paragraph (2) does not apply in respect of a provider or proposed provider if they have carried out any of the actions described in paragraph (1).
