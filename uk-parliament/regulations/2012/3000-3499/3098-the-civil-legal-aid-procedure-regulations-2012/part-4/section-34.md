## 34. Supporting documents: protection of children

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application for civil legal services described in paragraph 13 of Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Act must include evidence that the child who is or would be the subject of the order to which the application relates is at risk of abuse from an individual (“B”) other than the applicant for civil legal services (“A”) against which risk of abuse the order is to provide protection.

(2) For the purpose of paragraph (1), evidence of the risk of abuse must be provided in one or more of the forms that is described in Schedule 2[^key-341bdbb7d59f0baa630f78b675d61f9b].
