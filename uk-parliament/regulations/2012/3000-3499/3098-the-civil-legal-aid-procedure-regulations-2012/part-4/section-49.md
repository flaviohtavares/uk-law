# The Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-06-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3098

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Notice to third parties

*Extent: E+W*

Where the Director withdraws a determination and the proceedings to which the determination related have not been concluded, the provider must give notice of the withdrawal in a form specified by the Lord Chancellor to—

- (a) the court or tribunal; and
- (b) any parties to the proceedings who were given notice of the determination under regulation 38.
