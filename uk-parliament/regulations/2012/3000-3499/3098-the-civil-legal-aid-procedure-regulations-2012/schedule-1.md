# The Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012, SCHEDULE1

## SCHEDULE 1 — Supporting documents: domestic abuse[^c-7749704c52d39ebb2bc28bced081f573][^c-43e6ab179d4856ae0d50fd39b0d42464]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 33 (2)

**1.** Evidence that B has been arrested for a relevant domestic abuse[^c-a11a16cf5ed5659f6ae5d64b7e61fb18] offence.

**2.** A relevant police caution for a domestic abuse[^c-f242fbe1f579f8f2e7879c63d8480787] offence.

**3.** Evidence of relevant criminal proceedings for a domestic abuse[^c-04491e76b0399ebbfc945d75d2b7e803] offence which have not concluded.

**4.** A relevant conviction for a domestic abuse[^c-bd5a0e55b606cb36c09e9904f8330908] offence.

**5.** Evidence of a court order binding over B in connection with a domestic abuse[^c-8a90440ee0dda443668ee1ef94f0bfed] offence.

**6.** A domestic violence protection notice issued under section 24 of the Crime and Security Act 2010 against B.

**7.** A relevant protective injunction.

**8.** An undertaking given in England and Wales under section 46 or 63E of the Family Law Act 1996 (or given in Scotland or Northern Ireland in place of a protective injunction) by B provided that a cross-undertaking relating to domestic abuse[^c-c4e17a51595e6ba9c8ef5989c3c3e4dc] was not given by A.

**9.** A copy of a finding of fact, made in proceedings in the United Kingdom, that there has been domestic abuse[^c-4ee837fd44b32f886d4ea6e1c9d6df4d] by B.

**10.** An expert report produced as evidence in proceedings in the United Kingdom for the benefit of a court or tribunal confirming that a person with whom B is or was in a family relationship, was assessed as being, or at risk of being, a victim of domestic abuse[^c-29950ef905dd6db1ea1267649f4edcf5] by B.

**11.** A letter or report from an appropriate health professional confirming that that professional, or another appropriate health professional—

- (a) has examined A in person , by telephone or by video conferencing[^c-17a6936b5f3ca5549d159712497effb7]; and
- (b) in the reasonable professional judgement of the author or the examining appropriate health professional A has, or has had, injuries or a condition consistent with being a victim of domestic abuse[^c-d4a4abc9a0c3030ecf07ce9f7c4950da].

**12.** A letter or report from—

- (a) the appropriate health professional who made the referral described below;
- (b) an appropriate health professional who has access to the medical records of A; or
- (c) the person to whom the referral described below was made;

confirming that there was a referral by an appropriate health professional of A to a person who provides specialist support or assistance for victims of, or those at risk of, domestic abuse[^c-2de4f2577ad847e719c8858d41a81632].

**13.** A letter from any person who is a member of a multi-agency risk assessment conference (or other suitable local safeguarding forum) confirming that A, or a person with whom A is in a family relationship, is or has been at risk of harm from domestic abuse[^c-b26a2488cbc61f1fc6bd9a13148497e4] by B.

**14.** A letter from an independent domestic abuse[^c-c959b0b7acb60cc7d6350aa19ec00096] advisor confirming that they are providing , or have provided,[^c-5679e6c0375258e6c180d05d2bc3c2db] support to A.

**15.** A letter from an independent sexual violence advisor confirming that they are providing , or have provided,[^c-7c35910124f4e37fcfd818e4665a8409] support to A relating to sexual violence by B.

**16.** A letter from an officer employed by a local authority or housing association (or their equivalent in Scotland or Northern Ireland) for the purpose of supporting tenants containing—

- (a) a statement to the effect that, in their reasonable professional judgment, a person with whom B is or has been in a family relationship is, or is at risk of being, a victim of domestic abuse[^c-87aa30556031344e1753468b0a0a81e4] by B;
- (b) a description of the specific matters relied upon to support that judgment; and
- (c) a description of the support they provided to the victim of domestic abuse[^c-4d9585e1e898509dc312555fdaf9ad09] or the person at risk of domestic abuse by B.

**17.** (1) A letter from an organisation providing domestic abuse[^c-634fe04d206df34a8a82fee9a694937e] support services.

(2) The letter must confirm that it—

- (a) is situated in the United Kingdom[^c-136ac93a7014f574d58f535cecd23584];
- (b) has been operating for an uninterrupted period of six months or more; and
- (c) provided A with support in relation to A’s needs as a victim, or person at risk, of domestic abuse[^c-d34a5d6d5ed3273f6f89d5aab4c945ce].

(3) The letter must contain—

- (a) a statement to the effect that, in the reasonable professional judgment of the author of the letter, A is, or is at risk of being, a victim of domestic abuse[^c-6f3912a312a8935150d9c70af6be4248];
- (b) a description of the specific matters relied upon to support that judgment;
- (c) a description of the support provided to A; and
- (d) a statement of the reasons why A needed that support.

**18.** A letter or report from an organisation providing domestic abuse[^c-f5d563bc69e3a29f4acc8d2ec2f5d4db] support services in the United Kingdom confirming—

- (a) that a person with whom B is or was in a family relationship was refused admission to a refuge;
- (b) the date on which they were refused admission to the refuge; and
- (c) they sought admission to the refuge because of allegations of domestic abuse by B.

**19.** A letter from a public authority confirming that a person with whom B is or was in a family relationship, was assessed as being, or at risk of being, a victim of domestic abuse[^c-7d6049cb73e4bfe52d08ab3bf5f50cef] by B (or a copy of that assessment).

**20.** A letter from the Secretary of State for the Home Department confirming that A has been granted leave to enter or[^c-9ba900130d4665f2dd5c29a823c21257] leave to remain in the United Kingdom as a victim of[^c-3c6804bf559a832c0b7d6408b1c8c021] domestic abuse[^c-3bf7aa0011f8397ea2752ae0dd8607b9].

**21.** Evidence which the Director is satisfied demonstrates that A has been, or is at risk of being, the victim of domestic abuse[^c-048461595af6989299457695d3ba6136] by B in the form of abuse which relates to economic[^c-21c72282b3d96472316c22c5d747d77d] matters.

**22.** For the purpose of this Schedule—

- “A” means the applicant for civil legal services;
- “appropriate health professional” means—
  - (a) a medical practitioner licensed to practise by the General Medical Council; or
  - (b) a health professional who is registered to practise in the United Kingdom by—
    - (i) the Nursing and Midwifery Council;
    - (ii) the General Dental Council; [^c-95b7a081b1257b1077bf16c6029b1332]...
  - (c) a paramedic, practitioner psychologist, radiographer or social worker registered to practise in the United Kingdom by the Health and Care Professions Council; or[^c-5c21d170c6474bcde3eb075a0753a7b7]
  - (d) a medical practitioner, nurse, midwife, dental practitioner, paramedic, practitioner psychologist, radiographer or social worker registered and licensed to practise in the country in which they provide a letter or report, or examine A under this Schedule;[^c-1a970f4c8447e4d2e569fe391773494d]
- “B” means the individual with whom A was in a family relationship giving rise to the need for the civil legal services that are the subject of the application;
- “ domestic abuse[^c-ec466a40274384032bbfc85632fb8a94] offence” has the meaning given in the document published by the Lord Chancellor for that purpose under section 2 of the Act;
- “expert report” means a report by a person qualified to give expert advice on all or most of the matters that are the subject of the report;
- “housing association” has the same meaning as in subsection 1(1) of the Housing Associations Act 1985;
- [^c-50d308043d93c37341f2ebdc3d77c3b5]...
- “local authority” means a county council, a district council, a London borough council or a parish council but, in relation to Wales, means a county council, county borough council or community council;
- “protective injunction” means an order made by the court—
  - (a) in respect of persons who are in a family relationship with each other, containing any of the following provisions—
    - (i) protecting a person from harm, intimidation, threats or harassment;
    - (ii) protecting a person from being forced into a marriage or from any attempt to be forced into a marriage;
    - (iii) prohibiting a person from contacting, or communicating with, another;
    - (iv) concerning entry or access to, or the use or occupation of, property;
  - (b) for the protection from female genital mutilation under paragraph 1 or 18 of Schedule 2 to the Female Genital Mutilation Act 2003; or
  - (c) in respect of a violent offender within the meaning of section 98 of the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008;

  but does not include an order made without notice to the respondent that was subsequently set aside by the court;
- “public authority” has the same meaning as in section 6 of the Human Rights Act 1998;
- “refuge” means—
  - (a) a refuge established for the purpose of providing accommodation for victims of, or those at risk of, domestic abuse; or
  - (b) a residential home established and maintained by a public body for any other purpose that also provides accommodation to the victims of, or those at risk of, domestic abuse;
- “relevant” means that the evidence—
  - (a) identifies a person with whom B is or was in a family relationship as being, or at risk of being, the victim of domestic abuse; or
  - (b) is-
    - (i) in a form described in paragraphs 1 to 4 of this Schedule;
    - (ii) identifies B as the person arrested for, cautioned with, charged with, or convicted of the domestic abuse offence; and
    - (iii) relates to a domestic abuse offence which does not identify the victim.
