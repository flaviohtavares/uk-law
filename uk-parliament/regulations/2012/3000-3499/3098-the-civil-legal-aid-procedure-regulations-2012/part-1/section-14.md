# The Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-06-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3098

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Housing Loss Prevention Advice Service[^key-eea92036b12a7659e3903554d1c980bf]

*Extent: E+W*

These Regulations do not apply to civil legal services which are—

- (a) provided under the Housing Loss Prevention Advice Service[^key-37b5a156d7afcd0fd34b49808414c990] (as described in the Specification to the 2024[^key-6e4c4f0e62e64dc3380413ad4d61d0b1] Standard Civil Contract); [^key-0e51c86e795f93c79e56c9303fa0f3da]...
- (b) required by the Specification to the 2024[^key-9310eeee1a92010e01c54088e84d6a2b] Standard Civil Contract to be treated as services described in sub-paragraph (a) [^key-c398582123f14729b58f42ecc029bfa6]... ; or[^key-83c2ad2b2ec48ff123e2a1991d71ac6a]
- (c) legal help or help at court provided under paragraph 33(1) or (1A) of Schedule 1 to the Act to an individual who has a hearing which has been listed at a court in relation to the possession of that individual’s home.
