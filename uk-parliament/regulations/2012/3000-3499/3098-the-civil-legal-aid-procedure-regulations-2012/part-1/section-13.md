# The Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012, section 13

## 13. Disregarding determinations

*Extent: E+W*

The Director may disregard a determination about whether an individual qualifies for civil legal services under Part 1 of the Act if the determination was made—

- (a) by a person to whom the Director has delegated that function; and
- (b) in response to an application made otherwise than in accordance with these Regulations.
