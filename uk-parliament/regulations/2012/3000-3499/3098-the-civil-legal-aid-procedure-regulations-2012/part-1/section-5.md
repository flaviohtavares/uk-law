# The Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-06-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3098
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Help at court

*Extent: E+W*

“**Help at court**” means the provision of any of the following civil legal services at a particular hearing—

- (a) instructing an advocate;
- (b) preparing to provide advocacy; or
- (c) advocacy.
