# The Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012, section 7

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Family mediation

*Extent: E+W*

“**Family mediation**” means the provision of any of the following civil legal services in a family dispute—

- (a) an assessment by a mediator of whether, in light of all the circumstances, a case is suitable for mediation; or
- (b) acting as a mediator.
