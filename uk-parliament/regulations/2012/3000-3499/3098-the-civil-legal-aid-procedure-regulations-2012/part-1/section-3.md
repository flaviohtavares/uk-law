# The Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-06-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3098
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Form of civil legal services

*Extent: E+W*

“**Form of civil legal services**” means—

- (a) legal help;
- (b) help at court;
- (c) family help;
- (d) family mediation;
- (e) help with family mediation;
- (f) legal representation; and
- (g) other legal services,

which are further defined in regulations 4 to 10.
