## 12. Delegation

*Extent: E+W*

Except as provided in regulation 39(3), a function of the Lord Chancellor or Director under these Regulations may be exercised by, or by an employee of, a person authorised for that purpose by the Lord Chancellor or Director respectively [^c21489501].

## Annotations

[^c21489501]: Marginal citation: Section 6 of the Act makes provision for authorisations given for the purpose of section 5 of the Act or regulations under that section.
