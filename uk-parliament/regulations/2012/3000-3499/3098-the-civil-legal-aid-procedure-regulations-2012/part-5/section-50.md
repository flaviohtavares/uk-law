# The Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012, section 50

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50. General

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part makes provision about the making and withdrawal of determinations under section 9 of the Act about emergency representation.

(2) Except as specifically provided in this Part, determinations about emergency representation must be made and withdrawn in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations which would have applied had the application not been made on an urgent basis.

(3) Regulation 42(3) does not apply in relation to emergency representation.
