# The Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-06-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3098
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. The application

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An individual may make an application for emergency representation by such method (including by fax, telephone or e-mail) as the Director has agreed to accept given the urgency of the particular circumstances.

(2) The application must specify whether the emergency representation is to be provided as Licensed Work or under an individual case contract.
