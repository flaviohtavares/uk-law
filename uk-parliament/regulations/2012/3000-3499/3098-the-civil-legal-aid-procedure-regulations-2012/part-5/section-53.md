# The Civil Legal Aid (Procedure) Regulations 2012, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-06-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/3098
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 53. Appeal

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An individual may not appeal—

- (a) against a determination that the individual does not qualify for emergency representation made on the basis of limited information and documents; or
- (b) the withdrawal of a determination about emergency representation on the basis only of the expiry of a time limit.
