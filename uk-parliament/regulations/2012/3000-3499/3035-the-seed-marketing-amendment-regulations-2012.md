# The Seed Marketing (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> Text as at 2012-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

2012 No. 3035

Seeds, England

The Seed Marketing (Amendment) Regulations 2012

Made

3rd December 2012

Laid before Parliament

10th December 2012

Coming into force

31st December 2012

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers in sections 16(1), (1A), (2), (3), (5) and 36 of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001], after consultation in accordance with section 16(1) of that Act with representatives of such interests as appear to be concerned, and in exercise of the powers in paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002].

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003] and it appears to the Secretary of State that it is expedient for the reference in these Regulations to Council Decision [2003/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0017) on the equivalence of field inspections carried out in third countries on seed-producing crops and on the equivalence of seed produced in third countries[^f00004] to be construed as a reference to that Decision as amended from time to time.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Seed Marketing (Amendment) Regulations 2012 and come into force on 31st December 2012.

## 2. Amendment of the Seed Marketing Regulations 2011

The Seed Marketing Regulations 2011[^f00005] are amended as follows.

## 3. Regulation 3 (interpretation of other terms)

In regulation 3(2)—

- (a) at the end of sub-paragraph (g), omit “and”;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (h), insert—

  > and
  >
  > - (i) Council Decision [2003/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0017) on the equivalence of field inspections carried out in third countries on seed-producing crops and on the equivalence of seed produced in third countries,

  ; and
- (c) for “those Directives”, substitute “those instruments”.

## 4. Licences for temporary experiments

After regulation 21, insert—

> **21A. Licences for temporary experiments**
>
> The Secretary of State may by licence exempt any person or class of person from compliance with any provision of these Regulations for the purposes of a temporary experiment organised under—
>
> - (a) Article 19 of Council Directive [2002/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0054) on the marketing of beet seed[^f00006];
> - (b) Article 13a of Council Directive [66/402/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1966/0402) on the marketing of cereal seed[^f00007];
> - (c) Article 13a of Council Directive [66/401/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1966/0401) on the marketing of fodder plant seed[^f00008];
> - (d) Article 16 of Council Directive [2002/57/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0057) on the marketing of seed of oil and fibre plants[^f00009]; or
> - (e) Article 33 of Council Directive [2002/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0055) on the marketing of vegetable seed[^f00010].

.

## 5. Schedule 2 (certification requirements)

In paragraph 24 of Schedule 2, after “peas,” insert “lucerne (*Medicago sativa*),”.

## 6. Schedule 3 (labelling and loose sales)

In paragraph 9(1)(g) of Schedule 3, after “granulated” insert “pesticides”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Seed Marketing Regulations 2011 (S.I 2011/463) (“the principal Regulations”).

Regulation 3 adds Council Decision [2003/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0017) on the equivalence of field inspections carried out in third countries on seed-producing crops and on the equivalence of seed produced in third countries (OJ No L8, 14.1.2003, p. 10) to the list of EU instruments in respect of which references in the principal Regulations are to be construed as references to those instruments as amended from time to time.

Regulation 4 allows the Secretary of State to license exemptions for temporary experiments organised under any of the five European Directives on the marketing of, respectively, beet seed, cereal seed, fodder plant seed, seed of oil and fibre plants and vegetable seed. Other provisions of these Directives are already implemented (in relation to England) by the principal Regulations.

Regulation 5 amends the definition of certified seed to include lucerne (*Medicago sativa*) amongst the seeds to which it does not apply and regulation 6 amends one of the requirements as regards the content of an official label on a mixture of seed.

An impact assessment has not been produced as no impact, or no significant impact, on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
