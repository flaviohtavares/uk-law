# The Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986 Amendment Regulations 2012, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Personal licences

(1) Section 3 (prohibition of unlicensed procedures) is amended as follows.

(2) In the words before paragraph (a) after “shall” insert “personally”.

(3) In paragraph (c) omit “the personal licence and”.

**8.** (1) Section 4[^f00007] (personal licences) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) for “to apply specified regulated procedures” substitute “personally to apply regulated procedures of specified descriptions”;
- (b) omit “at a specified place or specified places”.

(3) In subsection (3)—

- (a) for paragraph (a) substitute—

  > - (a) is for the time being specified in a relevant section 2C licence by virtue of section 2C(5)(d);

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (b) for the words from “biological” to “**the**” (in the second place it occurs) substitute “education,”;
- (c) omit the words after paragraph (b).

(4) After subsection (3) insert—

> (3A) For the purposes of subsection (3)(a), a section 2C licence is “relevant” if it authorises the holder to carry on an undertaking which involves the applying of regulated procedures to protected animals.

(5) In subsection (4A)(a)—

- (a) omit “(including instruction in a relevant scientific discipline)”;
- (b) for “to be specified in the licence” substitute “that the licence would qualify the person to apply”.
