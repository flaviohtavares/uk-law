Made

3rd December 2012

Laid before Parliament

*7*th December 2012

Coming into force

1st January 2013

The Secretary of State is designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Union and makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes (Amendment) Regulations 2012 and come into force on 1st January 2013.

## 2. Amendment of the Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes Regulations 2010

(1) The Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes Regulations 2010[^f00003] are amended as follows.

(2) For regulation 8 substitute—

> **8. Additional modulation**
>
> The rates in the third column of the Schedule are the voluntary modulation referred to in Article 1 of Council Regulation [(EC) No 378/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0378) laying down rules for voluntary modulation of direct payments provided for in Regulation [(EC) No 1782/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1782)[^f00004] for the period 2009 to 2012 and the voluntary adjustment referred to in Article 10b of Council Regulation [(EC) No 73/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0073)[^f00005] for 2013[^f00006].

.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes Regulations 2010 ([S.I. 2010/540](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/540)) (“the principal Regulations”) which make provision in England for the administration of Council Regulation [(EC) No 73/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0073) (OJ No L 30, 31.1.2009, p 16), and other associated EU instruments, in relation to direct support schemes under the common agricultural policy.

Regulation 2 amends the principal Regulations to provide for additional modulation deductions which will be made from direct payments to farmers for 2013.

Regulation 3 revokes the Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes (Reductions from Payments) (England) Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/169](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/169)).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
