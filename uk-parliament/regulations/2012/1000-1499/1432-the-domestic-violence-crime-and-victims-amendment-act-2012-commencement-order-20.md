# The Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims (Amendment) Act 2012 (Commencement) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 1432 (C. 54)

Criminal Law

The Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims (Amendment) Act 2012 (Commencement) Order 2012

Made

28th May 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4(2) of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims (Amendment) Act 2012[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims (Amendment) Act 2012 (Commencement) Order 2012.

## 2. Commencement

The Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims (Amendment) Act 2012 comes into force on 2nd July 2012.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

Crispin Blunt

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

28th May 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 2nd July 2012 the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims (Amendment) Act [2012 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/4).
