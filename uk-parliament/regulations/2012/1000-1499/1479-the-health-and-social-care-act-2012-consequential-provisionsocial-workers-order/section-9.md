# The Health and Social Care Act 2012 (Consequential Provision—Social Workers) Order 2012, section 9

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Amendment of the Health Professions Council (Registration Appeals) Rules Order of Council 2003[^key-163fc19f1e9ab54c8461bda49cf5da0b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Schedule to the Health Professions Council (Registration Appeals) Rules Order of Council 2003[^f00010] (the Health Professions Council (Registration Appeals) Rules 2003) is amended as follows.

(2) In rule 2 (interpretation), in the definition of “Order”, for “Health Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001”.

(3) In rule 14 (conduct of hearing), in paragraph (1), for “health professional” substitute “appellant”.
