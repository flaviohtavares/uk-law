# The Health and Social Care Act 2012 (Consequential Provision—Social Workers) Order 2012, section 3

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Amendment of Health Professions (Parts of and Entries in the Register) Order of Council 2003[^key-f0a54f140188969ca9ee1f6a87f6c686]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Health Professions (Parts of and Entries in the Register) Order of Council 2003[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 1(2) (interpretation), in the definition of “the Order”, for “Health Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001”.

(3) In Schedule 1 (parts of the register), in the table, after the final entries in columns 1 and 2 add—

- (a) in column 1, “Part 16–Social Workers”, and
- (b) in column 2, “Social Worker”.
