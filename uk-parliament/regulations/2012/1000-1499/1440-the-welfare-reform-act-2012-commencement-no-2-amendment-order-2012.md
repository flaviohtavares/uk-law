# The Welfare Reform Act 2012 (Commencement No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument corrects an error in [S.I.2012/1246](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1246) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2012 No. 1440 (C. 55)

Social Security

The Welfare Reform Act 2012 (Commencement No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2012

Made

31st May 2012

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 150(3) of the Welfare Reform Act 2012[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform Act 2012 (Commencement No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2012.

## 2. Amendment of appointed day

In article 2(2) of the Welfare Reform Act 2012 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2012[^f00002], for “sections 122 to 125” substitute “sections 122, 123 and 125”

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Freud,

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department for Work and Pensions

31st May 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the second Commencement Order made under the Welfare Reform Act [2012 (c.5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/5) (“the Act”) so as to prevent section 124 of the Act coming into force on 6th June 2012. Section 124 provides for offences of tax credit fraud up to the value of £20,000 to be tried summarily only.
