# The Environmental Protection Act 1990 (Amendment of Fixed Penalty Amount) (England) Order 2012

> Text as at 2012-05-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2012 No. 1150

Environmental Protection, England

The Environmental Protection Act 1990 (Amendment of Fixed Penalty Amount) (England) Order 2012

Made

21st April 2012

Laid before Parliament

10th May 2012

Coming into force

30th May 2012

The Secretary of State, as the appropriate person in relation to England for the purposes of exercising the power conferred by section 47ZB(6) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00001], makes the following Order in exercise of that power:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Environmental Protection Act 1990 (Amendment of Fixed Penalty Amount) (England) Order 2012; and
- (b) comes into force on 30th May 2012.

## 2. Amendment

In section 47ZB of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 (amount of fixed penalty under section 47ZA), in subsection (2)(b) after “specified” insert—

> —
>
> - (i) is £60 in the case of a fixed penalty relating to the alleged commission in England of an offence under section 46, and
> - (ii) in any other case

.

Taylor of Holbeach

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

21st April 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This instrument amends section 47ZB(2)(b) of the Environmental Protection Act [1990 (c. 43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43) to reduce the amount payable for a fixed penalty given under section 47ZA from £100 to £60 in the case of a fixed penalty relating to the alleged commission in England of an offence under section 46 (receptacles for household waste) (article 2). In any other case the amount remains at £100.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
