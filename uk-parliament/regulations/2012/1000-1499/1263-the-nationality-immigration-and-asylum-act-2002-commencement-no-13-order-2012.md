# The Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 1263 (C. 44)

Immigration

The Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2012

Made

10th May 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 162(1) and (6) of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002[^f00001].

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2012.

**2.** (1) To the extent they are not already in force for the purposes mentioned in paragraph (3), the provisions of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 mentioned in paragraph (2) shall come into force for those purposes on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

(2) The provisions are—

- (a) section 125 (carriers’ liability) so far as it relates to the provisions specified in sub-paragraph (b); and
- (b) in Schedule 8, paragraphs 1 to 11 and 16(1) and (3).

(3) Those purposes are for the purposes of clandestine entrants (within the meaning of section 32(1) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00002]) who pass, or attempt to pass, through immigration control concealed in a vehicle.

Damian Green

Minister of State

Home Office

10th May 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

To the extent they are not already in force for the purposes mentioned in article 2(3), this Order brings into force the provisions of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 mentioned in article 2(2) for those purposes on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been prepared by the Home Office to accompany this Order and is available on www.legislation.gov.uk.
