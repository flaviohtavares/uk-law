## 24. Procedures of governing bodies of federations[^c-8c78c9b69c184f9b66c4bab6f2d603dd]

*Extent: E+W*

The Roles and Procedures Regulations[^c-8985f795898ede7f2b8aa66770eab267] apply to the procedure of governing bodies of federations subject to the modifications in Schedule 6.
