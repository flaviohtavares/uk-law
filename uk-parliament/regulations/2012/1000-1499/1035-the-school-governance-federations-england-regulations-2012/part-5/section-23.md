## 23. Qualifications, tenure of office and instruments of government[^c-cf0fef8ea29970873064b5a6e377fd88]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 16 to 31 of, and Schedule 4 (Qualifications and disqualifications) to, the Constitution Regulations 2012 apply to the governing body of a federation and its members subject to the modifications in Schedule 5.
