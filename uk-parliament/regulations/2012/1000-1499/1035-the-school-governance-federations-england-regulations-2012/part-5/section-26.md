> Text as at 2017-05-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Charitable status of federations[^c-388875974783655287c62395eb629f2c]

*Extent: E+W*

The governing body of a federation which includes a foundation, voluntary or foundation special school is a charity but no governing body of a federation which includes only community, community special or maintained nursery schools is a charity.
