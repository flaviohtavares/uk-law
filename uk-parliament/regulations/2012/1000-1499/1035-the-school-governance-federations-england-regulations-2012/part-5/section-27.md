# The School Governance (Federations) (England) Regulations 2012, section 27

> Text as at 2017-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Information for the governing body of a federation[^key-5ba672b06557572c96bd7f391c31df7f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Immediately before the federation date, the governing body of a school which is to become a federated school must prepare, for the purpose of assisting the governing body of the federation, a written report on the action which they have taken in the discharge of their functions relating to the school.

(2) All minutes and papers of a governing body of a school which is to become a federated school on the federation date, including the report prepared under paragraph (1), must be made available to the governing body of the federation.
