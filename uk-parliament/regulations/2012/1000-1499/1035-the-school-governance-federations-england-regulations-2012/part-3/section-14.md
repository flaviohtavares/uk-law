# The School Governance (Federations) (England) Regulations 2012, section 14

> Text as at 2017-05-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Parent governor[^c-098cc18e39a98f2dbb58d7c747b14f27]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In these Regulations “parent governor” means—

- (a) a person who—
  - (i) is elected as a governor [^c-e225b0c5ec171a9672f251a02bbb314c]... in accordance with paragraphs 3 to 7 of Schedule 2 by—
    - (aa) parents of registered pupils[^f00015] at a[^c-9d939bab71aa1e90883302f63870a235] federated school, or
    - (bb) parents of children for whom educational or other provision is made on the premises of a[^c-2a00ff0abe55af2c413fb6a548170526] federated school when it is a maintained nursery school (including any such provision made by the governing body under section 27[^f00016] of EA 2002); and
  - (ii) is such a parent at the time of the election; or
- (b) a person appointed as a parent governor [^c-4d06d2e92e780e106dc92d79d4a92503]... in accordance with paragraphs 8 to 11[^c-ba4baa68d99cd0cba5af7ed9587f1b84] of Schedule 2.

(2) Schedule 2 applies to the election and appointment of parent governors.

(3) A person is disqualified from election or appointment as a parent governor [^c-c551a492aeee1e756236dafc4610fcaa]... if the person is—

- (a) an elected member of the local authority; or
- (b) paid to work at the federation or a federated school for more than 500 hours in any consecutive twelve month period.

(4) A person (“P”) is not disqualified from continuing to hold office as a parent governor because P ceases to be a parent of a registered pupil at a federated school or to fulfil any of the requirements set out in paragraphs 9 and 10 of Schedule 2 (as the case may be).
