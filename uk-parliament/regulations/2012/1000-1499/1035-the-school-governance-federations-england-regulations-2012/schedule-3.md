# The School Governance (Federations) (England) Regulations 2012, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2017-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 3 — Election of staff governors

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 13

**1.**[^c-3cbf10ad4a1872f9ec180ce27a5df8e6] Subject to paragraphs 2 and 3, the governing body of the federation must make all necessary arrangements for the election of staff governors.

**2.**[^c-ec5d0a167cdefa66eb119916d2b1e7a6] The duty imposed by paragraph 1 does not confer power to impose any requirements as to the minimum number of votes required to be cast for a candidate to be elected.

**3.**[^c-bbb2dcde117b6b873ba2bc3d7306acd4] Any election which is contested must be held by ballot.
