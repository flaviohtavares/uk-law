# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 26

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Valid primary bids[^c-c413c0a760e52806b35a60f6b9ffe70c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A primary bid is a valid primary bid if the bidder complies with the provisions of regulations 25 and 54 in respect of that primary bid.
