# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 96

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Notice to bidders before the assignment stage round[^c-be7c2d521d8bb0d4792d59eee5288203]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before the start of the assignment stage round, OFCOM shall notify each bidder that may make an assignment stage bid in the assignment stage round of—

- (a) the assignment stage options that are available to that bidder as determined in accordance with regulation 89;
- (b) the date on which, and the times on that date within which, the assignment stage round will take place.
