# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 108

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108. Determination of the licence fee[^key-7a35f40710c7fdd74b493f8a6ff3e921]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The licence fee payable by a winning bidder shall be an amount equal to the total of—

- (a) the base price determined by OFCOM under regulation 68; and
- (b) the additional price for each winning assignment stage bid made by the bidder determined by OFCOM under regulation 106.
