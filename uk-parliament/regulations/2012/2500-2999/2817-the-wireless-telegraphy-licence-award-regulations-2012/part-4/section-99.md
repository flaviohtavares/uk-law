# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 99

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Extension to assignment stage round[^key-e7505d15e823cfb10365a640080bafda]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where—

- (a) OFCOM have not received a completed assignment stage bid form from a bidder by the time notified to bidders under regulation 96(b) as the end of the assignment stage round, and
- (b) paragraph (2) applies,

the time by which the bidder’s completed assignment stage bid form must be received by OFCOM shall be the time sixty minutes after the time notified to bidders under regulation 96(b) as the end of the assignment stage round.

(2) This paragraph applies where OFCOM are satisfied that circumstances existed which were beyond the bidder’s control which had the effect that the bidder could not submit its assignment stage bid form so that it was received by OFCOM on time.
