# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 73

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Assignment of frequencies in respect of the 800 MHz coverage obligation lot[^key-e65c1acc20fdb7e7db6896bd72e57a88]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the 800 MHz coverage obligation lot was included in a winning principal stage bid, OFCOM shall assign to the bidder that submitted that winning principal stage bid the frequencies corresponding to that lot.
