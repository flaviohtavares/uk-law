# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 42

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Supplementary bid selection[^key-3f67244a9009dacf8c1fd30c28c314b7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A bid made during the supplementary bids round (“supplementary bid”) shall be a bid for a selection of lots which matches a permissible bid selection for that bidder.
