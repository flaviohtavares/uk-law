# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 40

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Bidders’ eligibility limit for second and subsequent primary bid rounds[^key-a4acbe2824fe1b54a0cc1217accdab6b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before the second primary bid round and before each subsequent primary bid round OFCOM shall determine an eligibility limit for each bidder for that primary bid round in accordance with regulation 41.
