# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 97

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Submission of assignment stage bid form to OFCOM[^key-50176ed0069acb1119f8ade6d9188c1c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A bidder must submit the completed assignment stage bid form to OFCOM by using the electronic auction system, except as provided for in regulation 98.

(2) The completed assignment stage bid form must be received by OFCOM by the time notified to bidders under regulation 96(b) as the end of the assignment stage round, except as provided for in regulation 99.
