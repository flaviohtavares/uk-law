# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 89

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Preparing an assignment stage option list for each bidder[^c-9913dc6ab741b6c41df27533845a61d9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) OFCOM shall determine which assignment stage options shall be included in the assignment stage option list for each bidder which is eligible to bid.

(2) If the bidder is eligible to bid under regulation 88(1)(a), the bidder’s assignment stage options shall include each possible block of numbered 800 MHz paired frequency lots which meets the requirements of regulation 90 in relation to that bidder’s winning principal stage bid.

(3) If the bidder is eligible to bid under regulation 88(1)(b), the bidder’s assignment stage options shall include each possible block of numbered 2.6 GHz paired frequency lots which meets the requirements of regulation 91 in relation to that bidder’s winning principal stage bid.

(4) If the bidder is eligible to bid under regulation 88(1)(c), the bidder’s assignment stage options shall include each possible block of numbered 2.6 GHz individual frequency lots which meets the requirements of regulation 92 in relation to that bidder’s winning principal stage bid.
