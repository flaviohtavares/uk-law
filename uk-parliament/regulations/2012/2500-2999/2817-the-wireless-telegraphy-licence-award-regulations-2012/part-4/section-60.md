# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 60

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Notice to be given to bidders before supplementary bids round[^c-99e09d1f91a41347c016e843a373a127]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before the start of the supplementary bids round, OFCOM shall notify each bidder of—

- (a) the date on which, and the times on that date within which, the supplementary bids round will take place;
- (b) the deadline (which shall be after the end of the supplementary bids round) by when bidders must pay a further sum in pounds as a deposit calculated in accordance with regulation 66; and
- (c) if applicable, the proportion referred to in regulation 66(1)(a).
