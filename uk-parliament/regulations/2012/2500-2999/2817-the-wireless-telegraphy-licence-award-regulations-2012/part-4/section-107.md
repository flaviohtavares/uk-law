# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 107

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 107. Notification of results of assignment stage[^key-76009c31c81bac1928cf11b8069e12f5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Following the determination of the winning assignment stage bids, and the determination of the additional price for each winning assignment stage bid, OFCOM shall notify each winning bidder of—

- (a) the assignment stage options for which the bidder made winning assignment stage bids;
- (b) the additional price for each winning assignment stage bid made by the bidder.
