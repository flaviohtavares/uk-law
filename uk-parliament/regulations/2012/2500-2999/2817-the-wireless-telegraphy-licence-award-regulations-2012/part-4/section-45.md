# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 45

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Notice to be given to bidders before the opt-in round[^c-4d7091f092018a80c1405c131c0d1d49]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before the start of the opt-in round, OFCOM shall notify each bidder of the date on which, and the times on that date within which, the opt-in round will take place.
