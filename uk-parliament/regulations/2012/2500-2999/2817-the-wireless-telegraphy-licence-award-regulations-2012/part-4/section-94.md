# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Award) Regulations 2012, section 94

> Text as at 2012-11-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Assignment stage bid deposit[^key-e3f524a14120e2ebe65ec79d1d3aad4b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Each bidder that makes an assignment stage bid must, by a deadline specified by OFCOM (which shall be after the end of the assignment stage round), pay into OFCOM’s bank account by a same day electronic transfer, with accompanying information which identifies the bidder, a further sum in pounds as a deposit so that the total amount the bidder has on deposit is an amount that is not less than the amount (“*d*”) calculated in accordance with the formula set out in paragraph (2).

(2) The formula is—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/2817)

where—

- (a) “*b*” is the amount of the base price of the bidder’s winning principal stage bid (determined in accordance with regulation 68);
- (b) *“a<sub>1</sub>*” is the amount of the highest assignment stage bid made by the bidder for an assignment stage option in relation to numbered 800 MHz paired frequency lots;
- (c) *“a<sub>2</sub>*” is the amount of the highest assignment stage bid made by the bidder for an assignment stage option in relation to numbered 2.6 GHz paired frequency lots;
- (d) *“a<sub>3</sub>”* is the amount of the highest assignment stage bid made by the bidder for an assignment stage option in relation to numbered 2.6 GHz individual frequency lots.
