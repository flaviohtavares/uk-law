# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes (Prescribed Requirements) (England) Regulations 2012, section 13

> Text as at 2026-02-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Persons subject to immigration control[^key-8ad66bd8c2c53b76b8a3899d8575a52d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Persons[^key-3430f283eb9a41903a0ba1fa89285ce1] subject to immigration control are a class of person prescribed for the purposes of paragraph 2(9)(b) of Schedule 1A to the 1992 Act and which must not be included in an authority’s scheme.

(1A)[^key-345443f796ded299d17f7db9caafff40] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) “Person subject to immigration control” has the same meaning as in section 115(9) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999.
