# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes (Prescribed Requirements) (England) Regulations 2012, section 14

> Text as at 2026-02-13.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Provision for pensioners[^key-b6b03337720c838dc344cbbd22050df1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A scheme must make provision in respect of pensioners.

(2) Schedules 1 to 6, which contain those matters that must be included in a scheme in respect of pensioners, have effect.
