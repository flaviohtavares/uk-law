# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes (Default Scheme) (England) Regulations 2012, section 1

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application[^c-18fbc5ff49f853d489f5ab96d19788f0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Council Tax Reduction Schemes (Default Scheme) (England) Regulations 2012 and come into force on 18th December 2012.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to billing authorities in England.
