# The National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012, section 10

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Services for prisoners and other detainees

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a person is detained in a prison or in other accommodation described in paragraph (2), NHS England[^key-c31464ebf700c58b40bc343c24a900bf] must arrange, to such extent as it considers necessary to meet all reasonable requirements, for the provision to that person as part of the health service of—

- (a)[^key-a0e3390d646bcb593640c18398717d02] community services (including mandatory dental services and sedation services);
- (b) secondary care services; and
- (c) the services specified in Schedule 4.

(2) The other accommodation referred to in paragraph (1) is—

- (a) a court;
- (b) a secure children's home [^key-e51c5d53ac5064e701ef113cf5043aea]...;
- (c) a secure training centre [^key-5dcb250519d5fbf1f40639252ada4cfc]...;
- (d) an immigration removal centre specified in the first column of Table 2 in Schedule 3 from the date specified in the corresponding entry in the second column of that Table; and
- (e) a young offender institution [^key-85e57a41495c3bdeffdd77b5f4d5996d]....

(3) In this regulation, “**court**” means any court in which criminal proceedings against a person are heard.
