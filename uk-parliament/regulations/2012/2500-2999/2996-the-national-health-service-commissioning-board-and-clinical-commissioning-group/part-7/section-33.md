# The National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012, section 33

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**health care intervention**” includes the use of a medicine or medical device, diagnostic technique, surgical procedure or other therapeutic intervention;
- “**NICE**” means—
  - (a) until the coming into force of section 232 of the 2012 Act [^c21468021], the National Institute for Health and Clinical Excellence [^c21468031]; and
  - (b) from the coming into force of that section, the National Institute for Health and Care Excellence;
- “**relevant NICE recommendations**” means—
  - (a) any directions given by the Secretary of State as to the application of sums paid to a Primary Care Trust under section 228 of the 2006 Act (public funding by Primary Care Trusts) in relation to a health care intervention recommended by NICE; and
  - (b) from the coming into force of section 237(8) of the 2012 Act (NICE advice, guidance, information and recommendations), recommendations specified, or recommendations of a description specified, in regulations made under that section where the relevant body—
    - (i) is specified in such regulations as required to comply with the recommendation, or
    - (ii) is a health and social care body of a description specified in such regulations as a health and social care body that is required to comply with the recommendation.

## Annotations

[^c21468021]: Marginal citation: [Section 232](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/2996/section/232) of the 2012 Act establishes a body corporate to be known as the National Institute for Health and Care Excellence.

[^c21468031]: Marginal citation: The National Institute for Health and Clinical Excellence is a Special Health Authority established by [S.I. 1999/220](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/220), as amended by [S.I. 1999/2219](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/2219), 2002/1760, 2005/497 and 2012/476. It is abolished by section 248 of the 2012 Act.
