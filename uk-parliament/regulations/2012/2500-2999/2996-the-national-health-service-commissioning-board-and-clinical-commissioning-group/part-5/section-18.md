# The National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012, section 18

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Consultation by NHS England[^c-c31464ebf700c58b40bc343c24a900bf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) NHS England must consult the persons specified in paragraph (2)—

- (a) before exercising its functions under regulation 17(1) and (2) for the first time; and
- (b) before revising—
  - (i) terms and conditions it has drafted pursuant to regulation 17(1), or
  - (ii) a model commissioning contract it has drafted pursuant to regulation 17(2),

  in a way which would, in the opinion of NHS England , result in a substantial change to those terms and conditions or that contract (as the case may be).

(2) The persons specified for the purposes of paragraph (1) are—

- (a) the Care Quality Commission [^c21467771];
- (b) integrated care boards[^c-c9e790b8be2bf585774066cdc13bd366] ;
- (c) Healthwatch England [^c21467781];
- (d)[^c-41dda8e9ae7b3ad9bd0ef37323f8db5f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) the Secretary of State; and
- (f) such other persons as NHS England considers it is appropriate to consult.
