# The National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012, section 50

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50. Duty to have regard to guidance

*Extent: E+W*

In carrying out its duties under regulations 45 and 48, a relevant body must have regard to the document entitled Referral to treatment consultant-led waiting times: rules suite (October 2022)[^key-040fae4e59833c02a40fbfefd3b6c17b].
