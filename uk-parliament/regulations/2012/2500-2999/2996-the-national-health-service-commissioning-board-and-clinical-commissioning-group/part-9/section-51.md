# The National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012, section 51

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Duty to notify

*Extent: E+W*

Where—

- (a) a person meets the conditions in regulation 47(2) and (3);
- (b) NHS England[^c-c31464ebf700c58b40bc343c24a900bf] has responsibility for that person in respect of the health care service to be provided on referral; and
- (c) an integrated care board[^c-c9e790b8be2bf585774066cdc13bd366] receives notification from that person, or a person acting lawfully on that person's behalf, that they—
  - (i) have not commenced appropriate treatment; or
  - (ii) will not commence appropriate treatment,

  within 18 weeks beginning with the start date,

that integrated care board must notify NHS England in writing of that information.
