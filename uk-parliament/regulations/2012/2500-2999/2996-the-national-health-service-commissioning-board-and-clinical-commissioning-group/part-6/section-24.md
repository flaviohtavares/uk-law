# The National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012, section 24

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Appointment and term of appointment

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to regulation 25 (disqualification for appointment), the integrated care board[^key-c9e790b8be2bf585774066cdc13bd366] members and social services authority members must be appointed by NHS England[^key-c31464ebf700c58b40bc343c24a900bf] following nomination by an integrated care board or a social services authority in respect of which they are to be appointed.

(2) An integrated care board must—

- (a) when requested to do so by NHS England, provide its nomination pursuant to paragraph (1) as soon as is reasonably practicable; and
- (b) ensure that integrated care board members are, so far as reasonably practicable, available to participate in review panels.

(3) Subject to regulation 27 (termination of appointment), the term of appointment of a chair, an integrated care board or a social services authority member is to be such period, not exceeding three years, as NHS England specifies on making the appointment.

(4) Subject to regulation 25 (disqualification for appointment), a chair, integrated care board member or social services authority member is to be eligible for reappointment on the termination of the period of that chair or member's term of appointment.

(5) NHS England must pay to a chair such remuneration and expenses as appear to it to be reasonable.
