# The National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012, section 26

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Cessation of disqualification

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a person is disqualified under paragraph 5 of Schedule 5—

- (a) that person may, after the second anniversary of the day on which they were dismissed, apply in writing to NHS England[^c-c31464ebf700c58b40bc343c24a900bf] to remove the disqualification for appointment as a chair, integrated care board[^c-c9e790b8be2bf585774066cdc13bd366] member or social services authority member; and
- (b) NHS England may decide that the disqualification is removed.

(2) Where NHS England refuses an application to remove a disqualification, no further application may be made by that person to NHS England until the second anniversary of the day of the refusal and this paragraph applies to any subsequent application.

(3) Where a person is disqualified under paragraph 6 of Schedule 5, the disqualification is to cease on the second anniversary of the termination of the person's appointment, or at the end of such longer period as may have been specified on termination.
