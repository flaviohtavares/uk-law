# The National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012, section 25

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Disqualification for appointment

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person is disqualified for appointment as a chair if that person is—

- (a) the chair, a member (other than a member of an NHS foundation trust), a director, a governor or an employee of an NHS body;
- (b) the chair or a member of the governing body of an integrated care board[^key-c9e790b8be2bf585774066cdc13bd366] ; or
- (c) an elected member or employee of a social services authority in England and Wales or of an equivalent body in Scotland or Northern Ireland.

(2) A person is disqualified for appointment as an integrated care board member or social services authority member if that person is—

- (a) the chair, the chief executive, a non-executive director or a non-officer member of an NHS body (other than a member of an NHS foundation trust);
- (b) the chair or a member of the governing body of an integrated care board; or
- (c) an elected member of a social services authority in England and Wales or of an equivalent body in Scotland or Northern Ireland.

(3) Persons of the description set out in Schedule 5 are, subject to regulation 26 (cessation of disqualification), disqualified for appointment as a chair, integrated care board member or social services authority member.
