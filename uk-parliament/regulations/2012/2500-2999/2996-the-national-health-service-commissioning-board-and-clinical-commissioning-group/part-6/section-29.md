# The National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012, section 29

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Persons in receipt of flat rate payments immediately before the relevant date

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where, immediately before the relevant date, a Primary Care Trust was making a flat rate payment in respect of any person pursuant to the National Health Service (Nursing Care in Residential Accommodation) (England) Directions 2007 [^c21467951], paragraph (2) applies.

(2) The relevant body which has responsibility for a person falling within paragraph (1) must [^key-cc1888da83400a897585002ad2732114]... pay to a registered person for the relevant premises the flat rate payment in respect of the person falling within paragraph (1) [^key-cc1888da83400a897585002ad2732114]... unless or until that person—

- (a) has their need for nursing care assessed on or after the relevant date and it is determined that that person no longer has any need for nursing care;
- (b) is no longer resident in the relevant premises;
- (c) becomes eligible for NHS Continuing Healthcare pursuant to this Part; or
- (d) dies.
