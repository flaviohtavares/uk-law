# The National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012, section 41

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Persons to whom the duties as to choice do not apply

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 39(1) does[^c-9e949092b4d79b106a9fe3e461597365] not apply in relation to any person who is—

- (a) detained under the 1983 Act;
- (b) detained in or on temporary release from prison or other accommodation described in regulation 10(2)[^c-ce07cc88fc036acbbaa3cf6c1e228a20]; or
- (c) serving as a member of the armed forces.
