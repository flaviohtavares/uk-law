# The National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012, section 40

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Services to which the duties as to choice do not apply

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulation 39(1) does not apply to the following services—

- (a)[^c-d4542f19c0e4a96580eeb803bc7db5b3] services subject to the duty in regulation 52(1) (duty to make arrangements to diagnose or rule out cancer); or
- (b) maternity services; [^c-a7c51bf946bba2be298e5bf193c86b7f]...
- (c)[^c-c8f1a95535266e4a815b2d21de75f014] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Regulation 39(1) does[^c-6dd0e0a162902913d73c91297624bf0a] not apply to any service where it is necessary to provide urgent care.
