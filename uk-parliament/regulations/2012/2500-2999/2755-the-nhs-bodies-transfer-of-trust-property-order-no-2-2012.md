# The NHS Bodies (Transfer of Trust Property) Order (No. 2) 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 2755

National Health Service, England

The NHS Bodies (Transfer of Trust Property) Order (No. 2) 2012

Made

1st November 2012

Laid before Parliament

6th November 2012

Coming into force

17th December 2012

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 213, 217(2) and 272(7) and (8) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001], having regard to changes in the functions of NHS bodies other than NHS foundation trusts.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the NHS Bodies (Transfer of Trust Property) Order (No. 2) 2012 and comes into force on 17th December 2012.

(2) In this Order—

- “the relevant new Trust” means in relation to any relevant old Trust listed in column 1 of the Schedule (which Schedule has effect), the body which is listed adjacent to it in column 2 of that Schedule;
- “the relevant old Trust” means a body listed in column 1 of the Schedule;
- “the trust property” means the property specified in a schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the relevant old Trust and the relevant new Trust and signed on behalf of—
  - (a) the relevant old Trust on the date specified in column 3 of the Schedule; and
  - (b) the relevant new Trust on the date specified in column 4 of the Schedule.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

(1) On the date that this Order comes into force the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property, shall be transferred from the relevant old Trust to the relevant new Trust.

(2) Following the transfers in paragraph (1), any reference in any instrument relating to the trust property, to the trustees of the trust property or to the relevant old Trust shall be construed as a reference to the relevant new Trust, its property or its trustees (as appropriate).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Earl Howe

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the relevant new NHS bodies, property held on trust by the relevant old NHS bodies and makes related provision. Copies of the schedules of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen
