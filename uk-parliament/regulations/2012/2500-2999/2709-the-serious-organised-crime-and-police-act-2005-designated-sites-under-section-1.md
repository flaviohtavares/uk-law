# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of an omission in [SI 2012/1769](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1769) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2012 No. 2709

Criminal Law, England And Wales

The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2012

Made

26th October 2012

Laid before Parliament

31st October 2012

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(1)

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 128(2) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2012 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it has been laid before Parliament.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales only.

**2.** (1) The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) Order 2007[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2(1)(g), for “the sites described in paragraphs (2) to (11)” substitute “the sites described in paragraphs (2) to (12)”.

Theresa May

Secretary of State

Home Office

26th October 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) Order 2007 to ensure that article 2(1)(g) covers the site mentioned in paragraph (12) of that article. The consequence of this is that the site is clearly designated under section 128 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 so that if a person enters on it as a trespasser, he will commit an offence under section 128(1) of that Act. Article 2(12) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) Order 2007 was inserted into that Order by the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) (Amendment) Order 2012.
