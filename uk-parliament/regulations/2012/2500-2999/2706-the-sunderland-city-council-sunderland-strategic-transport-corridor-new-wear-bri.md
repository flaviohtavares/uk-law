## THE SCHEDULE — Highways Act 1980, Section 106(3)

### The Sunderland City Council (Sunderland Strategic Transport Corridor – New Wear Bridge) Scheme 2009

The Council of the City of Sunderland (“the Council”) make this Scheme in exercise of their powers under Section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf:-

**1.** The Council are authorised to construct over the navigable waters of the River Wear the bridge specified in the Schedule to this Scheme as part of the highway which they are proposing to construct/improve between the Wessington Way/Castletown Way roundabout and the Woodbine Terrace/Pallion New Road junction in the City of Sunderland.

**2.** This Scheme may be cited as The Sunderland City Council (Sunderland Strategic Transport Corridor – New Wear Bridge) Scheme 2009.

Given under the seal of the Council on the 4th day of November 2009

THE SEAL of THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF SUNDERLAND was hereunto affixed in the presence of:-

R C Rayner

Authorised Signatory

THE SCHEDULE TO THE SCHEME

PLAN AND SPECIFICATIONS OF THE NEW WEAR BRIDGE

The location and general design of the Bridge are shown on the plans numbered 8132/100 and 8132/101 marked The Sunderland City Council (Sunderland Strategic Transport Corridor – New Wear Bridge) Scheme 2009.

- **POINTS OF COMMENCEMENT AND TERMINATION**

  From a point on the South Bank of the River Wear 155 metres east of the centre of the roundabout junction between A1231 Wessington Way and Castletown Way located on the north bank.

  To a point on the North Bank of the River Wear 30 metres east of the centre of the roundabout junction between A1231 Wessington Way and Castletown Way.
- **SPAN**

  A double span of 336 metres overall.
- **HEADWAY**

  Not less than 12.9 metres above the mean high water spring tide level (MHWS) of 2.5 metres AOD.
- **WATERWAY**

  A minimum clear navigable waterway of 30 metres.
- **OVERALL DIMENSIONS**

  The Bridge will be 25.2 metres wide overall and will carry a dual 7.3 metre wide carriageway with a 1.8 metre central reserve with a footway of 2 metres width on the east side, separated from the carriageway by a 1 metre median, and a footway and cycleway of 4 metres width on the west side of the structure, separated from the carriageway by a 1 metre median.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This instrument confirms a scheme to construct over the navigable waters of the River Wear a bridge as part of the highway which is to be constructed/improved between the Wessington Way/Castletown Way roundabout and the Woodbine Terrace/Pallion New Road junction in the City of Sunderland.

The Secretary of State has modified this scheme as follows: Under “SPAN” the word “plan” has been amended to “span”.
