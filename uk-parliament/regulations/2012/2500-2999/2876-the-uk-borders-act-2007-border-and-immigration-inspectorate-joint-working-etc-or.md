# The UK Borders Act 2007 (Border and Immigration Inspectorate)(Joint Working etc.) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 2876

Immigration

The UK Borders Act 2007 (Border and Immigration Inspectorate)(Joint Working etc.) Order 2012

Made

19th November 2012

Laid before Parliament

19th November 2012

Coming into force

11th December 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 52(2), 52(3) and 55(1) of the UK Borders Act 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, Commencement and Interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the UK Borders Act 2007 (Border and Immigration Inspectorate)(Joint Working etc.) Order 2012 and shall come into force on 11th December 2012.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the UK Borders Act 2007.

## 2. Persons with whom the Chief Inspector may act jointly

The following persons shall be prescribed persons for the purposes of section 52(2) of the Act—

- (a) Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Prisons;
- (b) Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Constabulary;
- (c) the Independent Police Complaints Commissioner;
- (d) the Police Complaints Commissioner for Scotland;
- (e) the Parliamentary Commissioner for Administration; and
- (f) the Health Service Commissioner for England.

## 3. Persons that the Chief Inspector may assist

The following persons shall be prescribed persons for the purposes of section 52(3) of the Act—

- (a) Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Prisons;
- (b) Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Constabulary;
- (c) the Independent Police Complaints Commissioner;
- (d) the Police Complaints Commissioner for Scotland;
- (e) the Parliamentary Commissioner for Administration; and
- (f) the Health Service Commissioner for England.

Mark Harper

Minister of State

Home Office

19th November 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes the persons with whom the Chief Inspector of the UK Border Agency may act jointly under section 52(2) of the UK Borders Act 2007, in so far as the Chief Inspector thinks it consistent with the efficient and effective performance of the functions under section 48.

This Order also prescribes the persons that the Chief Inspector may assist under section 52(3) of the UK Borders Act 2007.
