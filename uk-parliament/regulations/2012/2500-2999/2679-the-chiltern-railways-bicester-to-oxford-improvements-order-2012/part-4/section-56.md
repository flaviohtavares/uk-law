# The Chiltern Railways (Bicester to Oxford Improvements) Order 2012, section 56

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 56. No double recovery[^key-84fd6078816f0fec903f571e44472e3b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law.
