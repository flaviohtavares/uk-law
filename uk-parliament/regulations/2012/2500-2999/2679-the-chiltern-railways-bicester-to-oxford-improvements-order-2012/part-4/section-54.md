# The Chiltern Railways (Bicester to Oxford Improvements) Order 2012, section 54

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 54. Certification of plans etc.[^key-2c25a89a43bb4c604565d2f59c002df6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Company must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the book of reference, the deposited plans and the deposited sections to the Secretary of State for certification that they are, respectively, true copies of the book of reference, the deposited plans and the deposited sections referred to in this Order; and a document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
