# The Chiltern Railways (Bicester to Oxford Improvements) Order 2012, section 31

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. Limitation of powers[^key-7c44ca469c7995860433b5fd3b390ab4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This article applies to the land numbered 04036, 04037, 04038, 04039, 04041, 04042, 04043, 04044, 04045, 04046, 04047, 04048, 04049, 04050, 04052, 04053, 04055, 04056, 04a001, 04a002 and 18009 in the district of Cherwell shown on the deposited plans.

(2) The Company’s powers under article 20 (protective works to buildings, roads or apparatus of a statutory undertaker), article 21 (power to survey and investigate land), article 22 (power to acquire land), article 25 (power to acquire new rights), article 26 (power to acquire airspace only), article 28 (rights under or over streets), article 29 (temporary use of land for construction of works) and article 30 (temporary use of land for maintenance of works) do not apply in respect of the land referred to in paragraph (1).
