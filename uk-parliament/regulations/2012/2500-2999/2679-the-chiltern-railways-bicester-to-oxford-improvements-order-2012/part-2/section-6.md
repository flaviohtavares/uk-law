# The Chiltern Railways (Bicester to Oxford Improvements) Order 2012, section 6

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. Power to deviate[^c-8cf5c2411b15669eab7880efcec79bcb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In constructing or maintaining any of the scheduled works, the Company may—

- (a) deviate laterally from the lines or situations shown on the deposited plans to the extent of the limits of deviation for that work; and
- (b) deviate vertically from the levels shown on the deposited sections—
  - (i) to any extent not exceeding 3 metres upwards; or
  - (ii) to any extent downwards as may be found to be necessary or convenient.
