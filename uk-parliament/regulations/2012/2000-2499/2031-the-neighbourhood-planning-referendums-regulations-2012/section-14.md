## 14. Advertisements

*Extent: E+W*

The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 2007 [^c21341061] have effect in relation to the display on any site in a referendum area of an advertisement relating specifically to the referendum as they have effect in relation to the display of an advertisement relating specifically to a local government election.
