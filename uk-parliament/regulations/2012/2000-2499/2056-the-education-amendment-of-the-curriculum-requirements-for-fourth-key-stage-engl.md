# The Education (Amendment of the Curriculum Requirements for Fourth Key Stage) (England) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 2056

Education, England

The Education (Amendment of the Curriculum Requirements for Fourth Key Stage) (England) Order 2012

Made

6th August 2012

Coming into force

1st September 2012

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 86 of the Education Act 2002[^f00001].

In accordance with section 210(3) of that Act a draft of this instrument was laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (Amendment of the Curriculum Requirements for Fourth Key Stage) (England) Order 2012 and comes into force on 1st September 2012.

## 2. Amendment of section 85 of the Education Act 2002

In section 85 of the Education Act 2002[^f00002] (curriculum requirements for fourth key stage)—

- (a) omit subsection (5)(a);
- (b) in subsection (9) omit “work-related learning or”; and
- (c) in subsection (10) omit the definition of “work-related learning”.

Nick Gibb

Minister of State

Department for Education

6th August 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends section 85 of the Education Act [2002 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32) so as to remove work-related learning from the requirements for the National Curriculum for England for the fourth key stage.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private sector or civil society organisations is foreseen. The impact on the public sector is minimal.
