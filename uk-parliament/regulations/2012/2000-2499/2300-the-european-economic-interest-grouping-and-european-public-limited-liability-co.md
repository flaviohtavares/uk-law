# The European Economic Interest Grouping and European Public Limited-Liability Company (Fees) Revocation Regulations 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 2300

Fees And Charges

The European Economic Interest Grouping and European Public Limited-Liability Company (Fees) Revocation Regulations 2012

Made

6th September 2012

Laid before Parliament

7th September 2012

Coming into force

1st October 2012

The Secretary of State, with the consent of the Treasury, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the European Economic Interest Grouping and European Public Limited-Liability Company (Fees) Revocation Regulations 2012 and come into force on 1st October 2012.

## 2. Revocation

The European Economic Interest Grouping and European Public Limited-Liability Company (Fees) Revocation Regulations 2009[^f00002] are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the European Economic Interest Grouping and European Public Limited-Liability Company (Fees) Revocation Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/2492](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2492)).

A Regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument.
