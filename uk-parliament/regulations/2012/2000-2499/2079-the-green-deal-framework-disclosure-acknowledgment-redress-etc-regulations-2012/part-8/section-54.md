# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 54

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 54. Enforcement undertakings[^key-3620b99683c93c89eca1f490233b2297]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where, in accordance with chapter 3, the Secretary of State would otherwise impose on an authorised person a sanction described in regulation 53(c) to (f) for a breach of the relevant requirements, the Secretary of State may instead accept from that person an enforcement undertaking.

(2) An enforcement undertaking must comply with regulation 82.
