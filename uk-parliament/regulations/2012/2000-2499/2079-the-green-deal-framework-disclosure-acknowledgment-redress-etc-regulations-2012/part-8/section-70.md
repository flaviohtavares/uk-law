# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 70

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 70. Cancellation in special circumstances – failure by relevant person to take a consumer credit modifying step[^c-58a2c88d11b3441c5c106bdb71dc2f27]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the Secretary of State is satisfied there has been a failure to take a consumer credit modifying step, the Secretary of State may impose cancellation on the relevant person.
