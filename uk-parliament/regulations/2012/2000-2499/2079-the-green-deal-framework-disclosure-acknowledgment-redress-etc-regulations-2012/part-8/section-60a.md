# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 60A

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 60A. Complaints – breach of the relevant requirements by a green deal certification body[^key-8de05123ceb92ce103b782817bcff983]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A complaint in respect of a breach of the relevant requirements by a green deal certification body may be made to the Secretary of State by—

- (a) the improver;
- (b) the bill payer; or
- (c) an owner or occupier who has arranged for a qualifying assessment of a property to be carried out by a green deal assessor.
