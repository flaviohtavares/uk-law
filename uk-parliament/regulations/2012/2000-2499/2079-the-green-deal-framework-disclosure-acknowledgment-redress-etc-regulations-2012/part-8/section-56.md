# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 56

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 56. Eligible complaints – breach of the disclosure and acknowledgment provisions[^c-02f40421c6f0f07777437cf1b647ed2e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An eligible complaint in respect of a breach of the disclosure and acknowledgment provisions is a complaint—

- (a) by the recipient to the green deal provider; and
- (b) which the provider is required to handle under the code of practice.
