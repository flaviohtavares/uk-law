# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 61

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 61. Breach of the consent provision[^key-396e1f0e1b408ffa541f178954bbd4ee]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A breach of the consent provision occurs where the improver has failed to obtain a necessary permission or consent to the installation of an improvement at a property under a green deal plan.
