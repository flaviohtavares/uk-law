> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 76. Recovery of compensation and financial penalties[^key-3b38e53e12f4ccfdd5175ce2c121d074]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Compensation may be recovered by the relevant person as a debt.

(2) A financial penalty may be recovered by the Secretary of State as a debt.
