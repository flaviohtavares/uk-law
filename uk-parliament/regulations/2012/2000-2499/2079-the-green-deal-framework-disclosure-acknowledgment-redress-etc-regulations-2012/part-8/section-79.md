# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 79

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 79. Proportionality requirement[^c-1715d1c06d37eae5cda20c1afcfe10e0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any sanction imposed under this chapter must be proportionate to the breach in relation to which it is imposed.
