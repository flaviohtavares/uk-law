# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 52

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52. No sanctions without receipt of complaints or information[^c-ef354b6f9da3b9226a96b81bcf75ddb3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (4), the Secretary of State[^c-1319c76fb143000d0fb5daa5a9edfdb1] must not impose a sanction under this Part unless—

- (a) an eligible complaint is referred to the Secretary of State in accordance with regulation 59;
- (b) a complaint is made to the Secretary of State under regulation 60 or 60A[^c-57a7984f2332706e3161051d2606230c]; or
- (c) information is received by the Secretary of State from—
  - (i) a person specified in paragraph (3); or
  - (ii) an authorised person,

in respect of a matter described in paragraph (2).

(2) A matter referred to in paragraph (1) is any of the following—

- (a) an improver has breached the consent provision;
- (b) a notifier has breached the disclosure and acknowledgment provisions;
- (c) an authorised person has breached the relevant requirements;
- (d) a relevant person has failed to take a consumer credit modifying step.

(3) The persons specified for the purpose of paragraph (1)(c) are—

- (a) the Financial Conduct Authority[^c-df01db5f8795fb5584775b0bc0238eab];
- (b) the ombudsman;
- (c)[^c-e6108910aef6fdfdc8c6c21cc211698e] the green deal accreditation body;
- (d) a local weights and measures authority.
- (e)[^c-dcd31357d02fabdee6e18ae7b4d7d39c] the Competition and Markets Authority.

(4)[^c-1fd3648bc67211eb58591986c5432a0e] Paragraph (1) does not apply where the Secretary of State is satisfied that an authorised person has breached a requirement to provide information to the Secretary of State under regulation 86.
