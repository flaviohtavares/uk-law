# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 57

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 57. Eligible complaints – breach of the relevant requirements by the green deal provider or the green deal installer[^c-e5c1bd44c270b6e5b4e342414fb3e4c0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An eligible complaint in respect of a breach of the relevant requirements by the green deal provider or the green deal installer is a complaint—

- (a) by the improver or the bill payer;
- (b) to the green deal provider; and
- (c) which the green deal provider is required to handle under the code of practice.
