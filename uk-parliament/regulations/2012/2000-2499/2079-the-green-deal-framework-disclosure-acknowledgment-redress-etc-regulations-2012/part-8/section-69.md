# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 69

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 69. Sanctions for breaches of the relevant requirements by green deal assessors and green deal certification bodies[^c-f4a51f4f222bfe230defaf8a7d595afd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the Secretary of State is satisfied that there is a breach of the relevant requirements by a green deal assessor or a green deal certification body the Secretary of State may impose suspension or withdrawal on, as applicable, the assessor or certification body.
