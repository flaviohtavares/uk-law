# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 58

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 58. Eligible complaints – failure to take consumer credit modifying step[^c-d13b6d2a4e3f60da0c4f2c7468656cbd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An eligible complaint in respect of a failure to take a consumer credit modifying step is a complaint—

- (a) by the bill payer to the green deal provider; and
- (b) which the provider is required to handle under the code of practice.
