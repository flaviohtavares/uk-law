# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 83

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 83. Failure to comply with an enforcement undertaking[^key-8515546cd4f43476bbb484c07c2e4573]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a person fails to comply with an enforcement undertaking, the Secretary of State may impose one or both of the following—

- (a) the sanction which may otherwise have been imposed on that person had the enforcement undertaking not been given;
- (b) in respect of the failure to comply with the enforcement undertaking,—
  - (i) withdrawal; or
  - (ii) except where the person is a green deal provider, suspension.
