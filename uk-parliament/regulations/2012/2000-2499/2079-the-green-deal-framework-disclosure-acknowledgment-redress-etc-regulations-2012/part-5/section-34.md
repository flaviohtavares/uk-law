# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 34

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. No restriction on change of gas or electricity supplier[^key-554a4e43cb053cea4408773775853982]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The energy plan must not restrict a bill payer from changing gas or electricity supplier.
