# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 31

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. Payment period not to exceed savings period[^key-f0795d549d94f1b48c41efd6856a4d58]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The energy plan must not provide for the payment period to end after the date on which the savings period is expected to end.
