> Text as at 2021-10-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29. Conditions to be met for energy plans to be green deal plans[^c-37bbda91b2aecdd7c9b41363f427cb65]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An energy plan is not a green deal plan unless the conditions in regulations 30 to 36 are met.
