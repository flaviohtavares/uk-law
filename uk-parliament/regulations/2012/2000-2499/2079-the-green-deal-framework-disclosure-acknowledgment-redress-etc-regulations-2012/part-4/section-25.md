# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 25

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. Notifications concerning consumer credit[^c-57a405853e56fb4568fa1ac62f30a177]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A green deal provider authorised in relation to domestic properties must notify the Secretary of State as soon as is reasonably practicable if—

- (a)[^c-095dbe95ccc3b13b09307f3997e9da79] it ceases to hold a consumer credit permission, or
- (b) it becomes a person who, by virtue of section 19 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, is required to have a consumer credit permission.
