# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Registers

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulations 2(1), 9(2), 11(2),12(3), 13(2), 14(3), 15(2), 16(4),17(4), 19(1), 20, 21 and 80

### SECTION 1 — All green deal certification bodies and green deal participants

**1.**[^key-b6e00513feabeb41ffdae30e42fb58db] Name, address and contact details.

**2.**[^key-6ee498a12c4766ef0638f345e3c5a1fc] Any trading name.

**3.**[^key-f099b020af566a1a97218009a7a40b37] For a company, the company registration number and names of the directors.

**4.**[^key-ac65799fd80671ec7fb7be46b03b606c] Green deal registration number.

**5.**[^key-bfb0bc0bf9e2d226b43e8eb464b94291] Date of entry on the register.

**6.**[^key-35169a4ec1f5d582de78d28e8183e5e3] Whether the person’s authorisation is suspended or withdrawn and—

- (a) if it is suspended, the period of the suspension;
- (b) if it is withdrawn, when the withdrawal of authorisation began.

**7.**[^key-794607fddf65854870aeccdb9a90fcfc] Any periods when the person’s authorisation has been suspended or withdrawn other than a suspension or withdrawal of authorisation to which paragraph 6 applies.

**8.**[^key-b8ee06385eb7092d648c09bd8a2ec31d] The reasons for any suspension or withdrawal of authorisation.

### SECTION 2 — Green deal assessor certification bodies

**9.**[^key-aca246b9dede39df8d54c3cb4f397503] Date of accreditation to certify persons as able to act as a green deal assessor.

**10.**[^key-aec33cad29da0a0808800fb94173d2b4] Certificate of accreditation number.

**11.**[^key-33633b82084fc1fae6230f4b78acbe41] Name of accrediting body.

### SECTION 3 — Green deal assessors

**12.**[^key-affeab08f63a9edffca683fb31ab38b3] Date of certification to act as a green deal assessor.

**13.**[^key-1c3e93cfe34e9f91e2d12d2713997459] Certificate number.

**14.**[^key-8a3475cc42e586d50369dfbae262518b] Name of certifying body.

### SECTION 4 — Green deal installer certification bodies

**15.**[^key-2ff2449b5f1da3e0d7811bc53b8a4cc1] Date of accreditation to certify persons as able to act as a green deal installer.

**16.**[^key-8399a621d86bc859ffbf0a870ffdc0a1] Scope of accreditation.

**17.**[^key-2e58e0e2cfec94aad93dd44efb277e72] Certificate of accreditation number.

**18.**[^key-7f68a771930470b848a8018f3c8ce3f7] Name of accrediting body.

### SECTION 5 — Green deal installers

**19.**[^key-a795b63030f6a4ba0700753e4ce598d3] Date of certification to act as a green deal installer.

**20.**[^key-953a6442e97866532a01dc2cc1415c68] Scope of certification and energy efficiency improvements certified to install.

**21.**[^key-f0447572988b4a0ef7aad118e2347932] Certificate number.

**22.**[^key-7e164778a0439f209a213512a4da28bc] Name of certifying body.

### SECTION 6 — Green deal providers

**23.**[^key-4706cb423c57678f14c4b2c44cc567e9] Whether authorisation is granted in relation to—

- (a) non-domestic properties only, or
- (b) domestic and non-domestic properties,

and the date of authorisation.

**24.**[^key-89af5256a76ecc77318315d336b0f341] For authorisation in relation to domestic properties—

- (a)[^key-5b247d783b7bf03352993e214408afa5] whether the person has a consumer credit permission and the activities for which he has permission, or
- (b) where the person is not required to have a consumer credit permission, an entry on the register to that effect.
