# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 48

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 48. Acknowledgment in circumstances described in regulation 47[^key-cc781389b9260bb70013291251bdc23c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

(1) Where this regulation applies, E must secure that F acknowledges in writing[^f00034] that, as the bill payer at the property, F will be—

- (a) liable to pay green deal instalments; and
- (b) bound by the terms of the green deal plan which bind a bill payer[^f00035].

(2) The acknowledgment required by paragraph (1) must be given before F starts to occupy the property under the tenancy or licence agreement.
