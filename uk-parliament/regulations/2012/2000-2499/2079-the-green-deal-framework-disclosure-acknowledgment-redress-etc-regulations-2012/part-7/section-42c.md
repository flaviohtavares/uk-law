# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 42C

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42C. Updating of disclosure document where the green deal provider is no longer authorised[^key-f8f60f70bca526dee54a3c9b3d2b10be]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

Where, at the time the requirement under regulation 42A(1) arises, the person named as the green deal provider under the green deal plan is not authorised as such a provider, any reference to the green deal provider in regulations 42A and 42B is to be read instead as a reference to the person who is for the time being entitled under the plan to receive the green deal instalments.
