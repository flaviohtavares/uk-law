# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 3

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Notices[^key-1c0f841fce66f3a450c4fb704c12e5f7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A notice under these Regulations—

- (a) must be in writing; and
- (b) may be transmitted by electronic means unless the recipient has indicated unwillingness to accept notices in that way.
