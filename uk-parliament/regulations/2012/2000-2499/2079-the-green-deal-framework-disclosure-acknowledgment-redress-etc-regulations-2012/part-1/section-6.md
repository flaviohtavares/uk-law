# The Green Deal Framework (Disclosure, Acknowledgment, Redress etc.) Regulations 2012, section 6

> Text as at 2021-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. The bill payer where there is no supply of electricity to a green deal property[^c-c141238922db26a3661b501f4f775cc4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In any period where no electricity is supplied to a green deal property, the bill payer for the purposes of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Act[^f00022] and these Regulations is—

- (a) the relevant title holder, unless that person has let the whole of the property under a registrable lease; or
- (b) the tenant of the property under a registrable lease who has not further let the whole of the property under a registrable lease.
