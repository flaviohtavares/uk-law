# The Ipswich Barrier Order 2012, section 55

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 55. No double recovery[^c-7512dd3e9056c8f68ec2df3286c4bedd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law.
