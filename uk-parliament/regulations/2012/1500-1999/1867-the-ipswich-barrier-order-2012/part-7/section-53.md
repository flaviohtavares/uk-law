# The Ipswich Barrier Order 2012, section 53

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 53. Certification of plans etc.[^c-d739410505bc0941b35a59bf24d4cb1d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Agency must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the book of reference, the sections, the land plans and the works plans to the Secretary of State for certification that they are true copies of, respectively, the book of reference, sections and plans referred to in this Order; and a document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
