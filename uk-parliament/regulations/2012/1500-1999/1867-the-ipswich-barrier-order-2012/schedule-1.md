# The Ipswich Barrier Order 2012, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — DESCRIPTION OF THE SCHEDULED WORKS[^key-64c806410175766e2dbd6cc302e874de]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 2

[^key-64c806410175766e2dbd6cc302e874de]

- **Work No.1** A barrier across the mouth of the New Cut on the river Orwell, with a moveable gate providing a channel width when open of 20 metres together with piers, a stilling basin, piling, scour protection, flood defence walls linking to other flood defences and other associated structures.
- **Work No.2** A building for control and associated purposes in relation to the barrier.
- **Work No.3** A new underground electricity cable and conduit being a replacement for part of an existing 132 KV cable between TM1686343408 and TM1705243383.
