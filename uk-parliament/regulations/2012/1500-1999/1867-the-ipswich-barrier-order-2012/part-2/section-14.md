# The Ipswich Barrier Order 2012, section 14

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14. Application of docks legislation[^c-572c3c27375a506c1dbf142f0d3acb3f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 32 of the Ipswich Dock Act 1852[^f00019] (obligations in respect of works authorised by the Ipswich Dock Act 1837) has effect subject to the provisions of this Part.
