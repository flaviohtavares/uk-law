# The Ipswich Barrier Order 2012, section 9

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9. River not to be a reservoir[^c-20f3d07d00a0117e5b946d1f57da06f3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regardless of the provisions of subsection (2) of section 1 of the Reservoirs Act 1975[^f00014], no part of the river as is impounded by the scheduled works is, by virtue of the retention of water by means of the scheduled works, to be taken to be a reservoir for the purposes of that Act.
