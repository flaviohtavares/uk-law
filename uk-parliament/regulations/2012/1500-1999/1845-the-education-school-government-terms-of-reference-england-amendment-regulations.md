# The Education (School Government) (Terms of Reference) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 1845

Education, England

The Education (School Government) (Terms of Reference) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

Made

12th July 2012

Laid before Parliament

17th July 2012

Coming into force

1st September 2012

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21(3) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (School Government) (Terms of Reference) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 and come into force on 1st September 2012.

## 2. Amendment to the Education (School Government) (Terms of Reference) (England) Regulations 2000

In the Education (School Government) (Terms of Reference) (England) Regulations 2000[^f00002] omit regulation 8 (school curriculum policy).

Nick Gibb

Minister of State

Department for Education

12th July 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Regulation 2 of these Regulations omits regulation 8 of the Education (School Government) (Terms of Reference) (England) Regulations 2000 (“the 2000 Regulations”), so that head teachers are no longer required to formulate a policy for the secular curriculum of their school for adoption by the governing body.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses or civil society organisations. The impact on the public sector is minimal.
