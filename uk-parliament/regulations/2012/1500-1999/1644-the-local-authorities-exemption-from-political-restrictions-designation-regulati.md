# The Local Authorities (Exemption from Political Restrictions) (Designation) Regulations 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 1644

Local Government, England

The Local Authorities (Exemption from Political Restrictions) (Designation) Regulations 2012

Made

25th June 2012

Laid before Parliament

28th June 2012

Coming into force

26th July 2012

The Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3A(7B) and (7C) and 190 of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Authorities (Exemption from Political Restrictions) (Designation) Regulations 2012 and shall come into force on 26th July 2012.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “the 1989 Act” means the Local Government and Housing Act 1989;
- “head of staff” means the most senior employee of the local authority concerned.

## 2. Application of section 3A of the 1989 Act

Section 3A of the 1989 Act shall apply to the local authorities listed in the first column in the Schedule to these Regulations as if the references to the person designated under section 4 of the 1989 Act as head of paid service, were references to the person listed in the second column of that Schedule.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Section 3A of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 provides for the head of paid service of a local authority in England which is a relevant authority (as defined in section 49(6) of the Local Government Act 2000) to grant exemptions, in certain circumstances, from the political restrictions imposed on certain posts by the 1989 Act. Section 3A(7B) gives the Secretary of State power by regulations to make provision about the application of the section to a local authority that is not required to designate one of its officers as the head of its paid service. These Regulations make that provision.

No impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations as they do not have an impact on business or charities.
