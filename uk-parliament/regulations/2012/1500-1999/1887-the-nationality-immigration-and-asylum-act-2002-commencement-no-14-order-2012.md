# The Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Commencement No. 14) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 1887 (C. 73)

Immigration

The Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Commencement No. 14) Order 2012

Made

17th July 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 162(1) of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002[^f00001].

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Commencement No. 14) Order 2012.

**2.** Section 124 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (authority to carry) shall come into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

James Brokenshire

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

17th July 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 124 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (authority to carry) on the day after the day on which this Order is made.
