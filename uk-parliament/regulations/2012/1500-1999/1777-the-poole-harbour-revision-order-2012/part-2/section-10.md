> Text as at 2026-07-23.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 10. Licensing of moorings[^key-474231fa19b86e62aba3f6d22579c0f2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Commissioners may from time to time grant a licence to any person to place, lay down, maintain, renew and use moorings, buoys and similar apparatus for vessels in the harbour.

(2) A licence—

- (a) may be granted for such a period and on such terms and conditions as the Commissioners think fit; and
- (b) may relate to one mooring, or to several moorings.

(3) The Commissioners may make reasonable charges for the grant of a licence under this article.
