> Text as at 2026-07-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 41. Temporary borrowing powers[^c-c5f642e1f9a22abc986848d6bff409ca]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Commissioners may borrow temporarily, by way of overdraft or otherwise, such sums as they may require for meeting their obligations or discharging their functions.
