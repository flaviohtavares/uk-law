# The Poole Harbour Revision Order 2012, section 47

> Text as at 2026-07-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. Power to lend money[^c-b2d1a5bffe2702427aa901bf2acfe076]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this article, “harbour operations” has the meaning given by section 57 of the 1964 Act.

(2) The Commissioners may lend money for the purposes of the harbour to—

- (a) any subsidiary of the Commissioners; or
- (b) any person carrying on or proposing to carry on harbour operations or any business in the harbour.
