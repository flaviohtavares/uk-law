# The Poole Harbour Revision Order 2012, section 45

> Text as at 2026-07-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. Application of revenue[^key-6fa6f3f1bd70fbd0f0db175154ab54ea]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-0edc6f88da068a6242d1c783d634e53e] All revenues received by the Commissioners shall be applied in the following order—

- (a) in payment of any expenses properly chargeable to revenue incurred by the Commissioners;
- (b) in payment year by year of the interest accruing on any moneys borrowed by the Commissioners; and
- (c) in making provision for the repayment of moneys borrowed by the Commissioners.

(2)[^key-420b1243c9969faa4cd55f8c3206a23d]  The Commissioners may apply any part of the revenues not used for the purposes set out in sub-paragraphs (1)(a) to (c) to any reserve fund established or maintained under article 42 (reserve fund).
