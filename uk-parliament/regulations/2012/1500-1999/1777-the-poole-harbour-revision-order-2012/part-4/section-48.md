# The Poole Harbour Revision Order 2012, section 48

> Text as at 2026-07-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 48. Gratuities[^key-47acc18306a1a090e9f2c72e06fc5ee4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Commissioners may pay such gratuities, pensions or allowances and make such other payments as they may think fit to any past or present employee of the Commissioners or the family or dependants of such an employee.

(2) The Commissioners may enter into and carry into effect, agreements with any insurance company or other body for securing for the benefit of any such employee, family or dependants such gratuities, pensions, allowances or payments as are authorised by this article.
