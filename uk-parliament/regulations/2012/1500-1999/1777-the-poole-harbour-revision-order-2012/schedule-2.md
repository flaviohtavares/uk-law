# The Poole Harbour Revision Order 2012, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2026-07-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — PURPOSES FOR WHICH BYELAWS MAY BE MADE

*Extent: E+W+S*

Article 27

**1.**[^c-54b97aca568b2b72188b837014928e21] Regulating the use, operation and superintendence of the harbour and any works and facilities in the harbour.

**2.**[^c-c6e880728301ceccd887f0b4156084c6] Regulating the admission to, and the movement within, and the departure of vessels from the harbour or the removal of vessels, and for the good order and government of vessels whilst within the harbour.

**3.**[^c-782c738bb96063adefc28d316fa5dbb6] Regulating the shipping and unshipping, landing, carrying, warehousing, stowing, depositing and removing of goods, including marine creatures of any type, within the harbour.

**4.**[^c-be4816c2e39639476f52b6beb6517593] Regulating the embarkation of persons on, or the disembarkation from, vessels in the harbour.

**5.**[^c-683903e916a2223f3a9a2b57250a9cee] Regulating the navigation, berthing, mooring and anchoring of vessels within the harbour and their speed and manner of navigation, the use of engines and the lights and signals to be exhibited or made by, or for the benefit of vessels using, navigating or mooring within the harbour.

**6.**[^c-2cbae762fe95aaf3ca627a58929d8239] Regulating the use of tugs within the harbour.

**7.**[^c-454facd24efa0587258788ef6ffbcfcc] Requiring the use of silencers or other similar apparatus, and the control of noise generally on vessels and otherwise within the harbour.

**8.**[^c-969f6931574ccab5c53c12ab0a93b5f5] Preventing damage or injury to any vessels, goods, vehicles, plant, machinery, property (including aids to navigation), or danger or injury to persons within the harbour.

**9.**[^c-f28d8f61aee150bd9e3bd2a9bf04c7c1] Regulating the conduct of all persons in the harbour not being—

- (a) members of a police force or officers; or
- (b) servants of the Crown; or
- (c) members of a fire brigade whilst in the exercise of their duties as such.

**10.**[^c-e9ba531a6c16b0a937dbf02df0642885] Regulating the placing, maintenance and use of moorings within the harbour.

**11.**[^c-3c8e0cbfcd52b9aa1c5b44dc671c2916] Preventing and removing obstructions or impediments within the harbour.

**12.**[^c-dfd254ebb7d4ce0a7bd81134e939aedb] Prohibiting or regulating the discharge or deposit of ballast, ashes, refuse, rubbish or other material (including any polluting liquid) into the harbour.

**13.**[^c-26587940a9f9374442d73923c122b4a3] Preventing the leaving or disposal of any waste matter except at places, or in a manner, prescribed by the Commissioners.

**14.**[^c-4e5b99c28907e9a7a732110a2450cb3d] Regulating the presence and keeping of vessels within the harbour.

**15.**[^c-3c2d2f024bb7e772037d8ff9b400dd3f] Regulating the careening, breaking, maintenance and repair of vessels and the carrying out of any other works in relation to vessels within the harbour.

**16.**[^c-2ddca1289884658d063e0188ababfa46] Regulating the use of ferries (including the vehicle and passenger chain ferry operated by The Bournemouth-Swanage Motor Road and Ferry Company between Haven Point, Sandbanks and Shell Bay, Studland[^c-6c43d47643bdcddc82e5cb612785c4a8]) within the harbour.

**17.**[^c-dec4df966aedcd735f91697880fb24c1] Regulating the landing or taking off of aircraft (including seaplanes) in the harbour.

**18.**[^c-ee53a7dfc9eef4b6238555177e82f393] Prescribing the lights and signals to be exhibited or made—

- (a) by vessels aground within the harbour;
- (b) by vessels or other devices used for marking obstructions within the harbour; and
- (c) at the entrance to any dock or at any wharf, pier or other work for assisting the navigation of vessels within the harbour.

**19.**[^c-44417d432f17f79dcfcc488456323503] Regulating or prohibiting the fishing for or the taking of marine creatures of any type and by whatever means, from any installation or structure of any kind within the harbour, or from any vessel within the harbour, where such fishing or taking interferes with the operation of, or the safety of navigation in, the harbour.

**20.**[^c-eaab0e715b715cda353923c05272910c] Regulating or prohibiting the digging for bait in the harbour where such digging may endanger any structure or cause obstruction or danger to navigation.

**21.**[^c-c080ad01799eb022e68e7ba96efb9e2c] Regulating in the harbour the use of yachts, sailing boats, rowing boats, pleasure craft and other small craft.

**22.**[^c-c95730bb6ef1a420bf54eba64dcba66b] Providing for the registration of passenger boats and those engaged in navigating passenger boats in the harbour.

**23.**[^c-8ceb36b4ff37845b61dce2c2a01db783] Regulating or prohibiting the use in the harbour of watercraft[^c-70a787247b924ec5567e9f56ff7110b2].

**24.**[^c-0f05968d14ecee846dd3686a483efead] Regulating the holding of regattas and other public events in the harbour.

**25.**[^c-43e439f8629129c005536c342bcda740] Regulating or prohibiting in the harbour diving, surfing, swimming, water skiing and other recreational activities or pursuits of whatever nature but not so as to prevent the use for navigation of the vessels referred to in paragraph 21.

**26.**[^c-4ee68d0d4df6bae5c47e05d59816a7d6] Regulating the launching of vessels and the use of slipways and landing places within the harbour.

**27.**[^c-b62f292ae6906db54a5f24cd88800b22] Prohibiting persons working or employed in or entering the harbour, or any part of the harbour, from smoking in the harbour.

**28.**[^c-89138e8b19e353a15aadbb7878887653] Regulating or preventing the use within the harbour, and within any vessels within the harbour of fires, lights, tobacco, fireworks or any other substance, equipment, tools or appliances which the Commissioners consider involve a risk of fire, explosion or chemical reaction.

**29.**[^c-198f3028244a6e5630489b31b0175261] Prohibiting the use of or regulating the use, movement, speed, placing, loading, fuelling, unloading, driving and parking of vehicles (including railway locomotives and trains) within the harbour.

**30.**[^c-b12f5ab9851df29dd16ffd4a349d6f13] Requiring the reporting of accidents and of other incidents giving rise to the risk of death or injury, or damage to property, occurring within the harbour.

**31.**[^c-1c794967d72b7c181e64aa371453660e] Regulating or prohibiting the carrying on of trading or commercial activities within the harbour.

**32.**[^c-005cbde6b9875e492d9e4849127f2747] Regulating the exercise of the powers vested in the harbourmaster.

**33.**[^c-f62e476624cc3269241a4847140c9f2d] Making the carrying out of specified harbour operations, or the conduct of persons in the harbour, subject to the approval (with or without conditions), control or direction of the harbourmaster, and for authorising the harbourmaster to take such action as may be reasonably required in default of compliance with any such condition, control or direction.

**34.**[^c-3beea5946b634f24a0e5aea3e5c47f25] Regulating the control of animals in the harbour.

**35.**[^c-74c38f1b889c4bf84f29e0897a5861c2] For the conservation of the natural beauty of all or any part of the harbour or of any of the fauna, flora or archaeological or geological or physiographical features in the harbour and all other natural features.

**36.**[^c-080ef7b67b3efbd24d0e9bb5129d52ef] For the purposes specified in section 83 of the 1847 Act.

**37.**[^c-d3a4ff036d3aa26b4ca51d03b73864c0] Prohibiting the entry or regulating the entry, carriage, handling or storage of dangerous goods within the meaning given under regulation 3 of the Dangerous Goods in Harbour Areas Regulations 2016 (meaning of “**dangerous goods**”).
