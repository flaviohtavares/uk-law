# The Poole Harbour Revision Order 2012, section 36

> Text as at 2026-07-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Recovery of charges[^key-b7ef3d13cf0e6d7a64cee80277e8ab40]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In addition to any other remedy given by this Order and by any other enactment as incorporated with this Order, and, in a case where the master of a vessel in respect of which a charge is payable to the Commissioners refuses or neglects to pay all or part of the charge, whether or not the Commissioners’ collector has gone on board the vessel and demanded the charge pursuant to section 44 of the 1847 Act, the Commissioners may recover any charges payable to them as a debt.
