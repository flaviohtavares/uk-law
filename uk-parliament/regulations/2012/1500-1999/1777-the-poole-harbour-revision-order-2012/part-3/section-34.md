# The Poole Harbour Revision Order 2012, section 34

> Text as at 2026-07-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. Refusal to pay charges for landing place, etc.[^key-25eb6668ade2a5c99070a918a4d58a78]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An officer of the Commissioners may prevent a vessel from using a landing place or any other facilities provided by the Commissioners if the master of the vessel refuses to pay—

- (a) the charges for such use; or
- (b) any other charges due to the Commissioners.
