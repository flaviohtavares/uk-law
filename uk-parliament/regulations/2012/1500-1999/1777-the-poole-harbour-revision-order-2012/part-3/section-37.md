# The Poole Harbour Revision Order 2012, section 37

> Text as at 2026-07-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. Harbourmaster may prevent sailing of vessels[^c-4798f3b4b84dfe1795104bf088253a7b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The harbourmaster may prevent the removal or sailing from the harbour of any vessel until evidence has been produced to the harbourmaster of the payment of any charges payable in respect of—

- (a) the vessel;
- (b) passengers on the vessel; or
- (c) goods imported or exported on the vessel.
