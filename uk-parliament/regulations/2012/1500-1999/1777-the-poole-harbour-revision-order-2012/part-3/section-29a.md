# The Poole Harbour Revision Order 2012, section 29A

> Text as at 2026-07-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29A. Charges other than ship, passenger and goods dues[^key-7c6b4e12d05eb15bd39f6a4efce57b7a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Commissioners may demand, take and recover in respect of any dracone, floating dock, crane, rig, drilling rig, floating platform or any other vessel, not being a ship, entering, using, within or leaving the harbour such reasonable charges, including in relation to any goods or passengers carried, as it may determine, and section 30 of the 1964 Act (duty to make available for inspection and keep for sale lists of certain charges) and section 31 of that Act (right of objection) apply, with all necessary modification, to the charges authorised by this article as they apply to ship, passenger and goods dues demanded under section 26 of the 1964 Act.

(2) The Commissioners must not exercise the power in paragraph (1) in relation to the vehicle and passenger chain ferry operated by The Bournemouth-Swanage Motor Road and Ferry Company between Haven Point, Sandbanks and Shell Bay, Studland.
