# The Scarborough and North East Yorkshire Health Care National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 1514

National Health Service, England

The Scarborough and North East Yorkshire Health Care National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2012

Made

10th June 2012

Coming into force

1st July 2012

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1), 272(7) and 273(1) of, and paragraph 28 of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Secretary of State makes this Order on the application of the Scarborough and North East Yorkshire Health Care National Health Service Trust, following the consultation prescribed under paragraph 28(3) of Schedule 4[^f00002] to the Act[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Scarborough and North East Yorkshire Health Care National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2012 and comes into force on 1st July 2012.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

(1) The Scarborough and North East Yorkshire Health Care National Health Service Trust is dissolved.

(2) The Scarborough and North East Yorkshire Health Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00004] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Simon Burns

Minister of State for Health,

Department of Health

10th June 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st July 2012 of the Scarborough and North East Yorkshire Health Care National Health Service Trust established on 1st November 1991 and revokes the Scarborough and North East Yorkshire Health Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991 that established the trust.
