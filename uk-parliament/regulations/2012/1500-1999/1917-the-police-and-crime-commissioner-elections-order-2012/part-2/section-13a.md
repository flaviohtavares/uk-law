## 13A. Combination of polls: Senedd Cymru[^c-802ee710f820488971877a2da99ddbe9][^c-5191b380ccc9fff20a453a4a90884d7c]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the poll at a PCC election is to be taken together with the poll at a Senedd[^c-1cefe35928661020fae49263f9129ba8] election under article 16 of the 2025 Order[^c-64c63bd120a4682e38e4d45ddefc4409], the modifications to the PCC elections rules set out in Part 3, and the forms in Part 4, of Schedule 4 have effect subject to the modifications and exceptions set out in Part 5 of that Schedule.
