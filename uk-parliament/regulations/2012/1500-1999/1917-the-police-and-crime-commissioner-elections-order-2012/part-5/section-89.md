# The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections Order 2012, section 89

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 89. Interference with notices

*Extent: E+W*

Any person who without lawful authority destroys, mutilates, defaces or removes—

- (a) any notice published by the registration officer in connection with the officer's registration duties, or
- (b) any copies of a document which have been made available for inspection in pursuance of those duties,

is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
