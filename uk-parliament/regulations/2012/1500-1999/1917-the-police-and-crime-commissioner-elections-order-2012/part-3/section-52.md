# The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections Order 2012, section 52

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Publication of election addresses of candidates

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Each candidate at a[^c-336c21563bb01d6913493d247e764587] PCC election is entitled to have an election address included on a website which is maintained by or on behalf of the Secretary of State[^c-ae7722ef95837a4520218e6edcb416d8] for the purpose of publishing election addresses of candidates at such an election.

(2) No election address may be included on the website unless it has been prepared and submitted to, and approved by, the police area returning officer in accordance with Schedule 8.

(3) Any expenses incurred by or on behalf of a candidate in respect of the hosting and publication of the candidate's election address are not to be taken, for the purposes of this Part, as an amount of expenses incurred by the candidate for the purposes of the PCC election.

(4) The Secretary of State[^c-bdf4de05bc69535ea5930e3f62d02c92] must take whatever steps the Secretary of State thinks appropriate to promote public awareness of—

- (a) the existence of the website, and
- (b) the availability of printed and other versions of election addresses of candidates.

(5) The Commission may include on the Commission's website information about—

- (a) how to access the website;
- (b) the availability of printed and other versions of election addresses of candidates,

and must include such information in any printed material which the Commission may publish in connection with PCC elections.

(6) Schedule 8 has effect.
