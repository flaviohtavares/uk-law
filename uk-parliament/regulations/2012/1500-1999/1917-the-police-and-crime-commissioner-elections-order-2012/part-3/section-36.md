# The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections Order 2012, section 36

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Power to vary provisions about candidate election expenses

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State[^c-5d3341608a8d3e51df81f051ccbe1eda] may by order made by statutory instrument vary any of the sums to which this article applies—

- (a) where the Secretary of State considers that the variation is expedient in consequence of changes in the value of money, or
- (b) in order to give effect to a recommendation of the Commission.

(2) This article applies to any of the sums for the time being specified in—

- (a) article 31(2);
- (b) article 32(1)(a);
- (c) article 34(4);
- (d) article 35(2).

(3) An order under paragraph (1) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(4) For the purposes of section 1 of the Statutory Instruments Act 1946 [^c21343651] (definition of “Statutory Instrument”), the power to make an order which is conferred by paragraph (1) is to be taken to be conferred by an Act of Parliament.
