# The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections Order 2012, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1917

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Corrupt withdrawal from candidature

*Extent: E+W*

Any person who corruptly induces or procures any other person to withdraw from being a candidate at a PCC election, in consideration of any payment or promise of payment, and any person withdrawing in pursuance of the inducement or procurement, is guilty of an illegal payment.
