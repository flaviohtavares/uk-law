## SCHEDULE 6 — Return and declarations as to candidate election expenses

*Extent: E+W*

Articles 34(7) and 41(1), (2), (5)

**1.** These are the forms referred to in articles 34(7) and 41(1), (2) and (5) [^c21344521].
