# The National Police Records (Recordable Offences) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 1713

Police, England And Wales

The National Police Records (Recordable Offences) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

Made

2nd July 2012

Laid before Parliament

4th July 2012

Coming into force

30th July 2012

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27(4) of the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the National Police Records (Recordable Offences) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 and shall come into force on 30th July 2012.

## 2. Amendment of the National Police Records (Recordable Offences) Regulations 2000

(1) The Schedule to the National Police Records (Recordable Offences) Regulations 2000[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) After paragraph 41 insert—

> **41A.** section 51A of the Sexual Offences Act 2003[^f00003] (offence of soliciting);
>
> **41B.** section 53A of the Sexual Offences Act 2003[^f00004] (offence of paying for sexual services of a prostitute subjected to force etc).

Lynne Featherstone

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

2nd July 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Police National Records (Recordable Offences) Regulations 2000 to make offences contrary to sections 51A and 53A of the Sexual Offences Act 2003 (soliciting and paying for sexual services of a prostitute subjected to force) recordable offences.
