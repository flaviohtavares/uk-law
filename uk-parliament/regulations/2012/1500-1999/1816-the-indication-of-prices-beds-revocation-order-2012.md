# The Indication of Prices (Beds) (Revocation) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 1816

Prices

The Indication of Prices (Beds) (Revocation) Order 2012

Made

11th July 2012

Laid before Parliament

12th July 2012

Coming into force

1st October 2012

In accordance with section 4(3) of the Prices Act 1974[^f00001], the Secretary of State has consulted, in such manner as appeared to him to be appropriate having regard to the subject-matter and urgency of this Order, with such organisations representative of interests substantially affected by this Order as appeared to him, having regard to those matters, to be appropriate.

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4(1)(c) and section 4(4) of that Act[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and coming into force

This Order may be cited as the Indication of Prices (Beds) (Revocation) Order 2012 and comes into force on 1st October 2012.

## 2. Revocation

The Indication of Prices (Beds) Order 1978[^f00003] is revoked.

Norman Lamb

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations, Consumer and Postal Affairs

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

11th July 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Indication of Prices (Beds) Order 1978 ([S.I. 1978/1716](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1978/1716), amended by [S.I. 1979/364](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1979/364)). The Indication of Prices (Beds) Order 1978 prohibited any person who indicated that a bed was or may be for sale by him from indicating a price at which that bed may be sold by another person. Some of its provisions lapsed following repeal, by the Competition Act [1980 (c.21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/21), of the Price Commission Act [1977 (c.33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/33) under which such provisions were made.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen. The Explanatory Memorandum which accompanies this instrument is available alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
