# The Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (Commencement No.7) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 1966 (C. 78)

Prevention And Suppression Of Terrorism

The Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (Commencement No.7) Order 2012

Made

25th July 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 100(5) of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008[^f00001].

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2012.

**2.** Section 27 (meaning of “terrorism offence”) of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 shall come into force on 26th July 2012 only in so far as it extends to England and Wales and Scotland.

James Brokenshire

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

25th July 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 26th July 2012, section 27 of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008.
