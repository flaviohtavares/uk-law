# The British Waterways Board (Transfer of Functions) Order 2012, section 5

> Text as at 2016-04-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Transitional provisions[^c-dd8e163d7f3bb450efa205b49b292751]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 4 (transitional provisions) has effect.

(2) Nothing in article 2 affects the application of section 44[^f00010] of the 1968 Act (account by Minister of receipt and disposal of certain sums) so far as relating to—

- (a) the period commencing on 1st April 2011 and ending on 31st March 2012, and
- (b) the period commencing on 1st April 2012 and ending on the day before the transfer date.
