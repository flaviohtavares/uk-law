# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 296

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 296. Exception for advertisements intended as a reminder

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 291 (form and content of advertisement) and 294 (general requirements) do not apply to an advertisement relating to a medicinal product if the advertisement is intended solely as a reminder of the product and consists solely of—

- (a) in the case of a product other than a homoeopathic medicinal product to which a certificate of registration relates, its name, international non-proprietary name or trademark; and
- (b) in the case of a homoeopathic medicinal product to which a certificate of registration relates, its name, international non-proprietary name, invented name or trademark or the scientific name of the stock or stocks from which it is derived.
