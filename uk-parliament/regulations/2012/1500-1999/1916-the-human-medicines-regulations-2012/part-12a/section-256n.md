# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 256N

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916
>

## 256N. Penalties[^key-935f6544e55359fa0989b19ff0c80cd3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 256M(1), (2) or (4) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum; or
- (b) on conviction on indictment to a fine, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or to both.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 256M(3) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
