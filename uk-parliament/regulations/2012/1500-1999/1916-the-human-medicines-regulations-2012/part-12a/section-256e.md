# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 256E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916

## 256E. Removal of a person’s entry from the list[^c-935f6544e55359fa0989b19ff0c80cd3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The licensing authority[^c-2694adaf84ccabd44691cba8e5c36f87] may remove a person’s entry from the list if—

- (a) regulation 256I(1)(c)[^c-d17f4b9049a798fcc6b53d64a5be817a] applies; or
- (b) a notification to remove the entry is received from the person on the list.
