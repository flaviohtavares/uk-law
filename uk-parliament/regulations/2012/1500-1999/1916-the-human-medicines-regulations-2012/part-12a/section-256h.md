# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 256H

## 256H. Conditions to be met by a person entered on the list[^key-935f6544e55359fa0989b19ff0c80cd3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person entered on the list shall not sell a medicinal product at a distance by information society services unless the following conditions are satisfied.

(2) Condition A is that the person entered on the list must comply with regulation 256B.

(3) Condition B is that without prejudice to the information requirements set out in Directive [2000/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2000/31) of the European Parliament and of the Council on certain legal aspects of information society services, in particular electronic commerce, in the Internal Market, (Directive on electronic commerce), the website used to sell medicinal products at a distance must contain—

- (a) the contact details of the licensing authority[^key-2694adaf84ccabd44691cba8e5c36f87] [^key-ea6bee647d41de547ee4f57b0e6dc47a]...;
- (b) a hyperlink to the website of the licensing authority[^key-a3df7c1ee12f8cd70ef115988eac6c2d].

(4) Condition C is that without prejudice to any implementing Acts adopted by the Commission under Article 85c(3) of the 2001 Directive the website used to sell medicinal products at a distance must contain the common logo which—

- (a) is clearly displayed on every page of the listed person’s website that relates to medicinal products offered for sale at a distance; and
- (b) contains a hyperlink to the entry of that person in the list.
