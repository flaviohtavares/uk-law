## 165. Determination in other cases

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Part prevents the licensing authority from determining that a product is a medicinal product in relation to these Regulations[^key-37d64425c602ee0038a6983d096bf9fe] without following the procedures in this Part when it thinks it appropriate.
