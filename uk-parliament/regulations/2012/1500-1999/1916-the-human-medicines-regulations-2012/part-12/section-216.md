# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 216

## 216. Exceptions to regulation 215

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 215 does not apply if—

- (a) S is a community practitioner nurse prescriber; and
- (b) the prescription only medicine prescribed or administered, or in respect of which S gives directions for administration, is specified in Schedule 13.

(2) Regulation 215(2) does not apply if S is acting in accordance with the directions of another person who is an appropriate practitioner (other than a supplementary prescriber or an approved country health professional[^c-aaf663a09f96298c36ce99e36b2e9782]) in relation to the prescription only medicine in question.
