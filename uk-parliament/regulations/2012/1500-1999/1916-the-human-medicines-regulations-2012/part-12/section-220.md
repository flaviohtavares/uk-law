# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 220

## 220. Sale or supply of medicinal products not subject to general sale

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Unless paragraph (2) applies, a person (“P”) may not sell or supply, or offer for sale or supply, a medicinal product that is not subject to general sale.

(2) This paragraph applies if—

- (a) P is a person lawfully conducting a retail pharmacy business;
- (b) the product is sold, supplied, or offered for sale or supply, at or from premises[^c-ef603b23ffc361c177596c20196b7cc6] that are a registered pharmacy; and
- (c)[^c-32bd4e40b7ceffc62db1b463cc9ed372] P or, if the transaction is carried out on P’s behalf by another person, that other person—
  - (i) is, or acts under the supervision of, a pharmacist, or
  - (ii) acts in accordance with regulation 220A or 220B.

(3) This regulation is subject to Chapter 3.
