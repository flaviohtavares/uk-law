# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 222

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 222. Sale of medicinal products from automatic machines

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person may not sell or offer for sale a medicinal product by means of an automatic machine if the product is not subject to general sale.
