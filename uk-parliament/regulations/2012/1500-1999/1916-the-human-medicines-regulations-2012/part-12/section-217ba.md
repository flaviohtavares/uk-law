# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 217BA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 217BA (Extent: E+W+S). Original pack dispensing: Northern Ireland (Extent: E+W+S)[^c-344ce0ea4fe08bfd07ceed255b95de70][^c-7cb4c2f50a2154d71230f0d1129fa023]

*Extent: NI*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S)) Subject to paragraphs (2) to (4) and regulation 217CA, for the purposes of this Part, the sale or supply of a prescription only medicine is in accordance with a prescription (and with the directions contained in the prescription) where— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) a different quantity is sold or supplied to that ordered on the prescription in order to allow for the sale or supply of the medicine in its manufacturer’s original outer packaging; and *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) the sale or supply is otherwise in accordance with the prescription. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(2 (Extent: E+W+S)) Paragraph (1) does not apply— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) to the sale or supply of a different quantity to that ordered on the prescription in circumstances where the different quantity is more than 10% greater or more than 10% less than the quantity ordered on the prescription; or *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) in circumstances where a pharmacist is carrying out *(Extent: E+W+S)* , supervising or authorising (under regulation 220B) the carrying out of[^c-b92c75e3869c4035a4d7d50cf99fd313] *(Extent: E+W+S)* the sale or supply and the pharmacist considers, in the exercise of their professional skill and judgement, that the sale or supply of a different quantity to that ordered on the prescription may mean that the patient does not, or is not able to, follow the medication regimen as intended by the prescriber. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(3 (Extent: E+W+S)) Paragraph (2) does not apply to— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) a medicine in a form that makes it not practicable to dispense in the exact quantity ordered; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) a medicine in a container that has an integral means of application or from which it is not practicable to dispense an exact quantity; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) a medicine that cannot be dispensed in the quantity ordered without adversely affecting the medicine. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(4 (Extent: E+W+S)) Paragraphs (1) to (3) do not apply in relation to a supply of a prescription only medicine that is subject to paragraphs 2(1) and (1A) of Schedule 2 to the Pharmaceutical Services Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, *(Extent: E+W+S)*

until those Regulations expressly apply paragraphs (1) to (3) to those supplies. *(Extent: E+W+S)*
