## 219. Electronic prescriptions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to a prescription that is not a health prescription for a substance or product for the time being specified in Schedule 1 to the Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2001 or in Schedule 1 to the Misuse of Drugs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002[^key-ddde920292452ec87894de5c7ac200b0].

(2) A prescription only medicine is also sold or supplied in accordance with a prescription given by an appropriate practitioner other than an approved country health professional[^key-e84b5c878cdfdcff208928fbbf5235e8] if—

- (a) conditions A and B in regulation 217 are not met; but
- (b) the conditions in paragraph (4) of this regulation and conditions C to E in regulation 217 are met.

(3)[^key-156e0748bdcba529a6fb67ba91b62445] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The conditions mentioned in paragraph (2)(b)[^key-e17f07837f0c0b255d9f11739768c142] are that the prescription is—

- (a) created in electronic form;
- (b)[^key-b6ceeb55d0c3beebb9c4bd9af64db559] signed with an advanced electronic signature; and
- (c) sent to the person by whom it is dispensed—
  - (i) as an electronic communication (whether or not through one or more intermediaries), and
  - (ii) via the electronic prescription service, if it is for a substance or product for the time being specified in Schedule 2 or 3 to the Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2001 or in Schedule 2 or 3 to the Misuse of Drugs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002.

(5)[^key-1f1bcdeb04a97750d1d6e1b977000199] In this regulation—

- “advanced electronic signature” has the meaning given within Article 3(11) of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council on electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal market;[^key-946df9fb99001d72694bd133e9fe885b]
- “electronic prescription service” means the service of that name which is managed by NHS England, the body corporate established under section 1H of the National Health Service Act 2006.[^key-e4004b031e0ce5f72a9e96fff70afe9a]
