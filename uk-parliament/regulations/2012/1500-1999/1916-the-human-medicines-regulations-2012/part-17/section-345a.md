# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 345A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 345A. Obligation on licensing authority to maintain list of medicinal products to which derogations have applied[^key-5fdd5933688c12a6927e7987630cec40]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The licensing authority must publish a list of medicinal products to which the derogations described in Articles 5a, 8(2a) and (2b), 18a, 20 (second paragraph), 40(1a) and (3a), 48(3) and 104(3) of the 2001 Directive have applied.

(2) The licensing authority must update the list referred to in paragraph (1) at least every six months.
