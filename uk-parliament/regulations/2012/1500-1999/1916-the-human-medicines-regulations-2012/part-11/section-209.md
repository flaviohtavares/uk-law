# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 209

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 209. Penalties

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person guilty of an offence under regulation 207 or 208 is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum; or
- (b) on conviction on indictment to a fine, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or to both.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 207 which relates to a breach of a provision listed in paragraph (3) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum; or
- (b) on conviction on indictment to a fine.

(3) Those provisions are regulations—

- (a) 182(2)(a) and (b), (3) and (5);
- (b) 183(8)(a);
- (c) 184(1)(a) and (b);
- (d) 187(4);
- (e) 188(1)(c) and (e);
- (f) 193(5);
- (g) 198(1) and (3)(a) and (d);
- (h) 199(8); and
- (i) 200(7).
