# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 185

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 185. Recording obligations on the licensing authority

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The licensing authority must record all suspected adverse reactions to medicinal products that—

- (a) occur in the United Kingdom; and
- (b) are reported to it by a holder,[^c-1556050d4cb07e099ee85e105b3bfe5e] a patient or a patient's carer, a health care professional, a coroner or a procurator fiscal.
