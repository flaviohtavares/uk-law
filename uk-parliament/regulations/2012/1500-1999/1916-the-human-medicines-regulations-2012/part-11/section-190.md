# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 190

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 190. Signal detection: holder obligation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-c1eb9433e10ebf37fb11e31b8a95fe2a] The holder must inform—

- (a) the licensing authority, and
- (b) in respect of a UKMA(UK)(Category 2)[^c-20b8fdaa528e901620e2107b3d897d56], a UKMA(NI), a THR(UK), a THR(NI) or an Article 126a authorisation, the EMA,

without delay if it detects any relevant changes in relation to the product.

(2) In this regulation, “**relevant changes**” has the meaning given in regulation 189(5).
