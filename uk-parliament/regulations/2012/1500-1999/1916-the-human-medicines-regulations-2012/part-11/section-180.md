# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 180

## 180. Obligation on licensing authority to audit pharmacovigilance system

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The licensing authority must perform a regular audit of its pharmacovigilance system relating to medicinal products for sale or supply[^key-a9d3baa0dbf6d99211eca5a06d923294] pursuant to a UKMA(GB) or a UKMA(UK)(Category 1)[^key-401f27b188356c32a455754877f41fb2] [^key-b5dcdb74730f87ff103cea0e0103e51e]....

(1A)[^key-2d570bbf9dbf37b09e1218178a382078] The licensing authority must perform a regular audit of its pharmacovigilance system relating to medicinal products for sale or supply in Northern Ireland pursuant to a UKMA(NI) or a UKMA(UK)(Category 2)[^key-47fe48093e906072e743d97e68f7ba45] and report the results of that audit to the European Commission.

(2) The [^key-7f38ab295a95edba0d4a56f4e86976cb]... audit referred to in paragraph (1) must be performed[^key-c221b16668b7ead39513ef2b6bf7c746]—

- (a) on the first occasion no later than 21st September 2013; and
- (b) every two years after the first occasion.

(3)[^key-65097e8081bb59db1c4dd9185682b92a] The results of the audit referred to in paragraph (1A) must be reported to the European Commission—

- (a) on the first occasion no later than 21st September 2021;
- (b) every two years after the first occasion.
