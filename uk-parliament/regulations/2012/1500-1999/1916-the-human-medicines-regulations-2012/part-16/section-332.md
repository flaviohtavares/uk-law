# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 332

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916

## 332. Restrictions on disclosure of information[^c-c4949b5020a1a87af2646f645958ee98]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person (“P”) must not disclose to another person, otherwise than in the performance of P's functions—

- (a) any information relating to a manufacturing process or trade secret obtained by P on premises which P has entered by virtue of regulation 325 or of a warrant under regulation 326; or
- (b) any information obtained by P or given to P in pursuance of these Regulations.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply if—

- (a) P is, or is acting on behalf of, a public authority for the purposes of the Freedom of Information Act 2000 [^c21328891]; and
- (b) the information is not held by the authority on behalf of another person.
