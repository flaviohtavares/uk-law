# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 157

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916

## 157. Requests from EU member States[^c-1518994b48a2f5dbbda151ceae9ee591]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where the licensing authority , in relation to a UKMA(NI),[^c-e56dd2aec3d055f4372f496d7f25127a] is requested by the competent authority of a member State[^c-d9839b5aafcc77551e97ef6af36f295c] to provide in accordance with Article 126a(3)(b) of the 2001 Directive a copy of—

- (a) the assessment report for a medicinal product as mentioned in regulation 64(5) (duties of licensing authority in connection with determination); and
- (b) the marketing authorisation in force for that product.

(2) The licensing authority must supply those documents to the competent authority before the end of the period of thirty days beginning on the day after the request is received.
