# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 173

## 173. Exemption for certain radiopharmaceuticals

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 46 (requirement for authorisation) does not apply where a radiopharmaceutical is prepared—

- (a) at the time when it is intended to be administered;
- (b) in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and by the person by whom it is to be administered;
- (c) from radionuclide generators, radionuclide kits and radionuclide precursors in respect of which a UK marketing authorisation[^key-5b8a6f1e1865c7e28d0fe3c0d2b63f94] [^key-a6871e05b0058999fa79476c0c780be5]... is in force; and
- (d)[^key-139b97692d401534f05355727e2fdbda] for administration—
  - (i) in England and Wales and Scotland in accordance with a licence issued under the Ionising Radiation (Medical Exposure) Regulations 2017;
  - (ii) in Northern Ireland in accordance with a licence issued under the Ionising Radiation (Medical Exposure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018.
