# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 172

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 172. Parallel import licences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibitions in regulation 46 (requirement for authorisation) do not prevent—

- (a) the holder of a parallel import licence from placing the medicinal product to which the licence relates on the market; or
- (b) the sale or supply, or offer for sale or supply, of a medicinal product to which a parallel import licence relates, in accordance with the terms of that licence.

(2)[^key-f512f069d1f84cdd8c3e70287b8046a0] In this regulation “parallel import licence” has the same meaning as in regulation 48(2).
