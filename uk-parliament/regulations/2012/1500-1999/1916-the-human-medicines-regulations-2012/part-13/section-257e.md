## 257E. Regulation-making power as to certain forms of labelling[^c-34931bf2c527113e6d8cffd6c69f916f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Ministers may by regulations require the use of certain forms of labelling of a medicinal product in order to make it possible to ascertain—

- (a) the price of the medicinal product;
- (b) any reimbursement conditions of the National Health Service;
- (c) the legal status for supply to the patient in accordance with regulation 5 (classification), insofar as not already provided for in Schedule 25;
