# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 268

## 268. Offence relating to packaging and package leaflets in Great Britain: holder of authorisation etc[^key-1810219d042a77e5ea5487393bbc524a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to the holder of a UKMA(UK), UKMA(GB)[^key-cbce69606258f372ba2035970ad755ed], certificate of registration or traditional herbal registration for a medicinal product who sells or supplies, offers to sell or supply, or possesses for the purpose of sale or supply , in Northern Ireland[^key-64cece36cc9e092340e74feae3ccc5ec], a medicinal product to which the authorisation, certificate or registration relates.

(2) A person to whom this regulation applies is guilty of an offence if—

- (a) a package or package leaflet relating to the product does not comply with the applicable requirements of this Part [^key-876fe564d229d9467c62ff2cf832532d]... or regulation 50C(4), 50D(8) or 58A(2)(b)[^key-d366f1c0155fe5d2028dd556dc9ffd58]; or
- (b) the product is not accompanied by a package leaflet when one is required by virtue of this Part.
