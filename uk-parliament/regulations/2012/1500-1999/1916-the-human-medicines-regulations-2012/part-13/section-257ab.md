# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 257AB

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916

## 257AB. Transitional arrangements[^c-455e0709565ee64b1438102dc875fd9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The information specified in paragraph 18B of Schedule 24 is not required to appear on the packaging of a medicinal product released for sale or distribution before 1st January 2025, unless the product has been re-packaged or relabelled after that date.
