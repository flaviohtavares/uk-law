## 269. Offences relating to packaging and package leaflets in Great Britain: other persons[^c-4cdd0d3cea75617d997fd4f9a57c77c6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to a person, other than the holder of a UKMA(UK), UKMA(GB)[^c-73e3b335c088b3fd5f455047e1416b0f], certificate of registration or traditional herbal registration for a medicinal product, who, in the course of a business carried on by that person,[^c-ed686e304c6ee11b4cac48caae20e7fd] sells or supplies, or offers to sell or supply the product, or possesses the product for the purpose of sale or supply , in Great Britain[^c-4fc7c73560545ccf45a1f9a785f2615f].

(2) A person to whom this regulation applies is guilty of an offence if the person sells or supplies, or offers to sell or supply, the product, or possesses the product for the purpose of sale or supply, in Great Britain[^c-08a0dfc078afc787b3bea4f90734f156] knowing or having reasonable cause to believe—

- (a) that a package or package leaflet relating to the medicinal product does not comply with the applicable requirements of this Part [^c-735f5c328dd6629cddd5b7254ff5b535]... or regulation 50C(4), 50D(8) or 58A(2)(b)[^c-f5ceaea862183d130aa7bb34395f2aa4]; or
- (b) that the product is not accompanied by a package leaflet when one is required by virtue of this Part.
