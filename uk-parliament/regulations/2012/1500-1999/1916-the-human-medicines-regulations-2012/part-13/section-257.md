# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 257

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 257. Packaging requirements: general

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The information specified in Part 1 of Schedule 24 must appear—

- (a) on the outer packaging of a medicinal product; and
- (b) on the immediate packaging of the product, unless paragraph (2) or (3) applies to the packaging.

(2) This paragraph applies to immediate packaging if the packaging is in the form of a blister pack and is placed in outer packaging which complies with the requirements of Part 1 of Schedule 24.

(3) This paragraph applies to immediate packaging if the packaging is too small to display the information required by Part 1 of Schedule 24.

(4) The information specified in Part 2 of Schedule 24 must appear on immediate packaging to which paragraph (2) applies.

(5) The information specified in Part 3 of Schedule 24 must appear on immediate packaging to which paragraph (3) applies.

(6) Information included on the packaging of a product in accordance with this regulation, regulation 257C[^key-698b834bc78eccac4b1e7095562ab292] [^key-2809a9917daa868fb176d7a93a38706b]..., regulation 261 and Schedule 24 must be easily legible, comprehensible and indelible.

(7) Nothing in this regulation or Schedule 24 applies to a registrable homoeopathic medicinal product.

(8)[^key-04943403c3776f4ba3568ff5ef049ff8] Nothing in this regulation applies to the outer or immediate packaging of an advanced therapy medicinal product [^key-a9c431882352d4599904ffeecd428264]....

(9)[^key-c85f3536957edcc76b2b9e7575a2c9ec] This regulation is subject to regulation 257AA.

(10)[^key-79c82291e07480263b7d669b3f912265] Nothing in this regulation applies to a POC medicinal product that is the subject of a UKMA(UK)(Category 1).
