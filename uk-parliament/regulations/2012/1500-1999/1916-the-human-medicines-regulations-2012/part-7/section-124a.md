# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 124A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916

## 124A. Interpretation of this Part[^key-3f0b75dcf4b4ccfdd222ee046378304f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, “**relevant list**” means—

- (a) the list referred to in Article 16f(1) of the 2001 Directive, as that list may be amended from time to time; or
- (b) if the licensing authority publishes a list under regulation 126A(1), that list.
