## A81. Application of regulations 89 to 94[^c-955e7c3f44449a921831fff55f1960cd][^c-66aefad6163be6d22bc4130e44521407]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 89[^c-8d02d31bc3a65095eded07151abcdf3d] to 94 apply in relation to medicinal products for sale or supply in Northern Ireland (that are not in Northern Ireland by virtue of regulation 167A)[^c-83c339a5462731911bc4742bb63d094e].
