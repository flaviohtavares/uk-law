# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 77

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916

## 77. Record-keeping obligations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The holder of a marketing authorisation or parallel import licence[^c-5142362732af8c1bb05d23f777232dcd] must keep any documents or information that will facilitate the withdrawal or recall from sale or supply of any product to which the authorisation relates.
