# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916

## 25. Duration of licence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A licence granted under this Part remains in force until—

- (a) the licence is revoked by the licensing authority; or
- (b) the licence is surrendered by the holder.
