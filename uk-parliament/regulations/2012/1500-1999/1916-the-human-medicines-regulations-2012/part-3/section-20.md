# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section 20

## 20. Mixing of medicines

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 17(1) (manufacturing of medicinal products) does not apply to the mixing of medicines by—

- (a) a nurse independent prescriber;
- (b) a pharmacist independent prescriber;
- (c) a supplementary prescriber, if the mixing of medicines forms part of the clinical management plan for an individual patient;
- (ca)[^c-330f53d88d1dfa668dc8cd440e3191dc] physiotherapist independent prescriber;
- (cb) podiatrist independent prescriber;
- (cc)[^c-363bbc5db9b022e47e7b43017684e408] a therapeutic radiographer independent prescriber;
- (cd)[^c-349202b3bbfdc311eea358670d831586] a paramedic independent prescriber;
- (d) a person acting in accordance with the written directions of a—
  - (i) doctor,
  - (ii) dentist,
  - (iii) nurse independent prescriber, [^c-551a33b312b9d3db63001f6103aba0a5]...
  - (iv)[^c-fdb5b6c8d26cd8915077c6094b1d9ef0] pharmacist independent prescriber,
  - (v) physiotherapist independent prescriber, [^c-c23ed14a4325ef71c9af22663ecfd74a]...
  - (vi) podiatrist independent prescriber; or
  - (vii)[^c-5beee1715a8c4b092e190512ab1f6dd1] therapeutic radiographer independent prescriber; or
  - (viii)[^c-e186e22cc5841ee85645d08cb68bdd9d] paramedic independent prescriber; or
- (e) a person acting in accordance with the written directions of a supplementary prescriber, if the mixing of medicines forms part of the clinical management plan for an individual patient.

(2) In this regulation “**mixing of medicines**” means the combining together of two or more medicinal products, none of which are MM medicinal products nor POC medicinal products[^c-9fd9f1f4416a9c1fd930ecf55649da45] for the purposes of administering them to meet the needs of an individual patient.
