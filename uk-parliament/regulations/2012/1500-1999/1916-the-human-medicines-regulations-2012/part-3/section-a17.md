# The Human Medicines Regulations 2012, section A17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/1916

## A17. Interpretation[^key-86bf88b35894eebb76eb7f1dfbf9dbd5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “manufacture”, in relation to an active substance, includes any process carried out in the course of making the substance and the various processes of dividing up, packaging, and presentation of the active substance.
