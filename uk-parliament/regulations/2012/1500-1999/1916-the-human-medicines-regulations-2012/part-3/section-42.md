## 42. Conditions for wholesale dealer's licence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations 43 to 45 (not including regulation 43ZA)[^c-800ce34b73aed68a3bd2c37dccd39a4a] (in the case of a wholesale dealer’s licence held in Northern Ireland) or regulations 43 to 45AA[^c-0fe5f26e4123b340e1a527eee0dbf1c8] (including regulation 43ZA)[^c-1637aecc3fb3a7b44910e96271fd9990] (in the case of a wholesale dealer’s licence held in Great Britain) apply to the holder of a wholesale dealer's licence (referred to in those regulations as “the licence holder”) and have effect as if they were provisions of the licence (but the provisions specified in paragraph (2) do not apply to the holder of a wholesale dealer's licence insofar as the licence relates to exempt advanced therapy medicinal products).

(2)[^c-1618d3ee77ba28d6fdf8b5261f6e5cc3] Those provisions are regulations 43(2) and (8) and 44.

(3) The requirements in Part 2 of Schedule 6 apply to the holder of a wholesale dealer's licence insofar as the licence relates to exempt advanced therapy medicinal products, and have effect as if they were provisions of the licence.
