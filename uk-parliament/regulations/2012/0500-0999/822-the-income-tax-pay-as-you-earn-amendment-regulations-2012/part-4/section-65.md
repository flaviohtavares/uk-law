## 65. PAYE income paid after employment ceased[^c-ce44bf7a42e93ebe26a25b3529fad267]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 37 (PAYE income paid after employment ceased)—

- (a) in paragraph (2) for “Subject to paragraph (2A), the” substitute “The”,

- (c) omit paragraph (2B).
