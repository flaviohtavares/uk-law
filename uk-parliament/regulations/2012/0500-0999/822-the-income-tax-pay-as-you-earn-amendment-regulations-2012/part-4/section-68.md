# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) (Amendment) Regulations 2012, section 68

> Text as at 2013-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. How information must or may be delivered by employers[^c-715b6b9d7ca67aba7fda0637880d9c82]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Table 10 in regulation 211 (how information must or may be delivered by employers)—

- (a) in the entries relating to regulations 67(1), 132(1), 157(2)(b) and 184I(2)(b), in column 5 for “yes” substitute “not applicable”, and
- (b) in the entry referring to regulation 184(G)(1) in column 1, for “184(G)(1)” substitute “184G(1)”.
