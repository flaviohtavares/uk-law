> Text as at 2013-04-06.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Commonwealth citizens[^key-ebc8d01ac9a3c253382f28e350bf4274]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 46(1B) omit the words “, or is a Commonwealth citizen (see section 278(2)(a) of ICTA),”.

**67.**[^key-0e870ea6e80b0459046c3d4bc71df46a] In regulation 47(1) omit the words “or being a Commonwealth citizen”.
