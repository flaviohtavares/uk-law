> Text as at 2013-04-06.
>

## 44. Succession to a business: trade disputes[^key-23296bcfe2fda288a666d38e4968cfb6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 104 (succession to a business: trade disputes)—

- (a) in paragraph (4), before “68(3)(b)” insert “67G(3)(b) (payments to and recoveries from HMRC for each tax period by Real Time Information employers) or, as the case may be,”, and
- (b) in paragraph (5), before “68” insert “67G or”.
