# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) (Amendment) Regulations 2012, section 15

> Text as at 2013-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. No Form P45: code treated as issued by HMRC[^key-80922b53bd7e369df5e77209b4681ed6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the heading to regulation 50 (procedure in Form P46 cases: code treated as issued by HMRC) substitute “No Form P45: code treated as issued by HMRC”.

**16.**[^key-f3a9b43764e83157026a16dc187d1ece] In regulation 50—

- (a) for “The” substitute “Code 0T, the”,
- (b) for “47” substitute “46”
- (c) for “49”, in both places, substitute “49E”, and
- (d) omit “Form P46 procedure and”.
