# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) (Amendment) Regulations 2012, section 11

> Text as at 2013-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Procedure if employer receives Form P45[^c-7d63b290ae5e29ead765304c3be7d20a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After regulation 42(6) (procedure if employer receives Form P45) insert—

> (6A) Paragraphs (7) and (8) apply if the employer is either—
>
> - (a) a non-Real Time Information employer, or
> - (b) a Real Time Information employer to whom HMRC has given a notice requiring the employer to send to HMRC Form P45 or Form P46 on the commencement of a new employee’s employment.

.
