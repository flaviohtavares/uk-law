# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) (Amendment) Regulations 2012, section 46

> Text as at 2013-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Electronic payment by large employers: meaning of “large employer”[^key-c94a70c8bc259124639d74f66991e2fe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 198A(2)(b) (large employers), after “employee” insert “or, as the case may be, has not indicated in a return under regulation 67B or 67D that the employee’s employment has ceased”.
