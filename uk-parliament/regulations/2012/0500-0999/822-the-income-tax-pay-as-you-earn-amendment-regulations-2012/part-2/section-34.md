# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) (Amendment) Regulations 2012, section 34

> Text as at 2013-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Annual returns[^key-35f1e84a4e7cc8e22f95a12a37e058a1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before regulation 73 and below the cross heading “Annual returns of relevant payments of tax” insert—

> **72H. Application of regulations 73 to 75**
>
> Regulations 73 to 75 apply to—
>
> - (a) non-Real Time Information employers,
> - (b) Real Time Information employers in relation to tax years in which they were, for the whole of the tax year, non-Real Time Information employers, and
> - (c) Real Time Information employers to whom HMRC has given a notice requiring a return under regulation 73 in respect of a tax year.

.
