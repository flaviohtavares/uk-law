# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) (Amendment) Regulations 2012, section 22

> Text as at 2013-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Application of Chapter 3 of Part 3 of the 2003 Regulations to Real Time Information pension payers[^key-16d74319631d4a62456b4b2ce364d550]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After regulation 54 (scope of Chapter 3 of Part 3) insert—

> **54ZA. Application of this Chapter to Real Time Information pension payers**
>
> (1) Any requirement in this Chapter to complete (howsoever expressed) and send to HMRC Part 3 of Form P45 or Form P46(Pen) applies only to—
>
> - (a) non-Real Time Information pension payers, and
> - (b) Real Time Information pension payers to whom HMRC has given a notice requiring the pension payer to send to HMRC Form P45 or Form P46(Pen) on the commencement of a new pensioner’s pension.
>
> (2) Paragraph (1) is without prejudice to the requirement in regulation 55(3)(b) (PAYE pension income paid by former employer) to complete and give Form P46(Pen) to the pensioner.
