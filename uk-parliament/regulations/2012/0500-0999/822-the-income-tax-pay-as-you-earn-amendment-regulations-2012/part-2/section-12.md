# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) (Amendment) Regulations 2012, section 12

> Text as at 2013-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Procedure where information missing and code not known: employers[^key-8007f958eaa206bb10ea664d9879662f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After regulation 45 (other Forms P45) insert—

> **45A. Application of regulations 46 to 49E: Real Time Information employers and non-Real Time Information employers**
>
> (1) Regulations 46 to 49 (procedure where no Form P45) apply in relation to—
>
> - (a) non-Real Time Information employers, and
> - (b) Real Time Information employers to whom HMRC has given a notice requiring the employer to send to HMRC Form P45 or Form P46 on the commencement of a new employee’s employment.
>
> (2) Regulations 49A to 49E (procedure where employee fails to assist with completion of new employee fields or no Form P45) apply in relation to Real Time Information employers other than those within paragraph (1)(b).
