# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) (Amendment) Regulations 2012, section 43

> Text as at 2013-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Time limits for issue of transfer notices: managed service companies[^key-912b5d8f0c1b5fb0b72791533c1e8d7c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 97D (time limits for issue of transfer notices), after paragraph (3) insert—

> (3A) In a case in which condition A2 in regulation 97B is met, the transfer notice must be served before the end of a period of 12 months beginning with the date on which HMRC serve notice to the employer under regulation 75A(5).

.
