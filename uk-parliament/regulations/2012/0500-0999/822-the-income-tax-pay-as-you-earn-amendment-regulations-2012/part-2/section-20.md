# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) (Amendment) Regulations 2012, section 20

> Text as at 2013-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. No Form P45: subsequent procedure on issue of employee’s code[^c-fea844af60385c396e3cca41c6b2161c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the heading to regulation 53 (Form P46 cases: subsequent procedure on issue of employee’s code) substitute “No Form P45: subsequent procedure on issue of employee’s code”.

**21.**[^c-3199296f50005ad86e7b930ba54b3228] In regulation 53—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “49 (procedure in Form P46 cases)” substitute “49E (procedure where no Form P45)”, and
- (b) in paragraph (2)(b)(ii), for “or 49” substitute “, 49, 49C, 49D or 49E”.
