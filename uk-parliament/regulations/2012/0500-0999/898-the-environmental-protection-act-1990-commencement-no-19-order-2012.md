# The Environmental Protection Act 1990 (Commencement No. 19) Order 2012

> Text as at 2012-03-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

2012 No. 898 (C. 28)

Environmental Protection

The Environmental Protection Act 1990 (Commencement No. 19) Order 2012

Made

20th March 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 164(3) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Environmental Protection Act 1990 (Commencement No. 19) Order 2012.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1st April 2012

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 162(2) of, and Part 2 of Schedule 16 to, the Environmental Protection Act 1990 so far as relating to the repeal of section 1 of the Refuse Disposal (Amenity) Act 1978[^f00002] is 1st April 2012.

Taylor of Holbeach

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

20th March 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 2012 the repeal of section 1 of the Refuse Disposal (Amenity) Act [1978 (c. 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/3) in England and Wales.

No impact assessment has been prepared for this instrument as no effect on business or the voluntary sector is anticipated.
