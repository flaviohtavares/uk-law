# The Postal Services Act 2011 (Transfer of Accrued Pension Rights) Order 2012, section 15

> Text as at 2014-12-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. References to Minister for the Civil Service[^key-92935dd5abe4fc3146b21b3e968dee43]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

References in this Order to the Minister for the Civil Service shall be construed, in relation to times before the coming into force of the Transfer of Functions (Royal Mail Pension Plan) Order 2014, as references to the Secretary of State.
