# The Postal Services Act 2011 (Transfer of Accrued Pension Rights) Order 2012, section 1

> Text as at 2014-12-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-ff04be54069b25be897e60cf775801c8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Postal Services Act 2011 (Transfer of Accrued Pension Rights) Order 2012.

(2) This Article will come into force on 31 March 2012.

(3) Articles 2 to 12 will come into force on a day to be specified by Order made under section 25(4) and (5) of the Act.

(4) Articles 13 and 14 will come into force immediately after articles 2 to 12 have come into force.
