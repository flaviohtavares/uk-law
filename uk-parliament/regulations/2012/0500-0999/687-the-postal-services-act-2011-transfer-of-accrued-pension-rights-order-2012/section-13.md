# The Postal Services Act 2011 (Transfer of Accrued Pension Rights) Order 2012, section 13

> Text as at 2014-12-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Participation of POL in the RMPP and division of the RMPP into different sections[^c-e23a7a81aab2de1aaaa1dfcfafca8741][^c-97d3559dee2867827cac308183950bf4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The trust deed and rules of the RMPP (as amended by article 11) are further amended in accordance with the Statement of Amendments so that—

- (a) the provisions that are in italics and are crossed out in the Statement of Amendments are omitted, and
- (b) the provisions that are in italics and are underlined in the Statement of Amendments are inserted.
