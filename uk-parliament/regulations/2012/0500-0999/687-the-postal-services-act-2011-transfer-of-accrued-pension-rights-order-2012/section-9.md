# The Postal Services Act 2011 (Transfer of Accrued Pension Rights) Order 2012, section 9

> Text as at 2014-12-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Application of occupational pensions legislation to RMSPS[^key-635c0ef54733b97f9161b630a066fe79][^key-cfec166cdbb0f20314c271579bb86e6a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The RMSPS is to be treated as an occupational pension scheme for the purposes of the statutory provisions described in Part 1 of Schedule 4.
