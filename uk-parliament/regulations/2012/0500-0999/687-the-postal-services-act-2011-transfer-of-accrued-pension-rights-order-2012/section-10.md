# The Postal Services Act 2011 (Transfer of Accrued Pension Rights) Order 2012, section 10

> Text as at 2014-12-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**10.**[^key-6674b676c97a524cbb55db61ee315c8a][^key-4a6f0ac57a404ffbaf607baf7e43f0ee] The RMSPS is to be treated as a salary-related contracted out scheme for the purposes of Part III of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00006], as modified by Part II of Schedule 4.
