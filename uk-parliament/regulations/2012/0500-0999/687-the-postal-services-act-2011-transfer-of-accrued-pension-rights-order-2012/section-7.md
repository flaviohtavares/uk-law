# The Postal Services Act 2011 (Transfer of Accrued Pension Rights) Order 2012, section 7

> Text as at 2014-12-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Discharge of Trustee[^c-5589dfbe945d7004b118c70382d0edad][^c-d06000455ad2f37074b09c42acff4eb2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Trustee of the RMPP is discharged from any liability in respect of the following for or in respect of a qualifying member of the RMPP—

- (a) all the qualifying accrued rights; and
- (b) the additional rights or entitlements to or in respect of benefits or future benefits which are removed from the RMPP by the RMPP Amendments.
