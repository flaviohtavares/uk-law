# The Postal Services Act 2011 (Transfer of Accrued Pension Rights) Order 2012, section 5

> Text as at 2014-12-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Transfer of qualifying accrued rights from RMPP to RMSPS[^key-d9aa245e7ba5fdbe68ac242abf19e7d6][^key-2284c830d9a0e50840f966afb02ee4c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following are transferred from the RMPP to the RMSPS—

- (a) all the qualifying accrued rights[^f00005]; and
- (b) the additional rights or entitlements to or in respect of benefits or future benefits which are removed from the RMPP by the RMPP Amendments.
