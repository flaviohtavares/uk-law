# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 779

National Health Service, England

The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2012

Made

8th March 2012

Coming into force

31st March 2012

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 272(7) of, and paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Treasury has consented to the making of this Order as required by paragraph 1(7) of that Schedule.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2012 and comes into force on 31st March 2012.

## 2. Originating capital of NHS trusts

The originating capital of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule is the amount specified in the corresponding entry in column 2 of the Schedule.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital, in accordance with paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 2006, of National Health Service trusts established under that Act between 1st April 2011 and 1st September 2011.
