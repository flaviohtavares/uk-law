# The Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000 (Estimates and Accounts) Order 2012

> Text as at 2015-05-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

2012 No. 717

Government Resources And Accounts

The Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000 (Estimates and Accounts) Order 2012

Made

7th March 2012

Laid before Parliament

8th March 2012

Coming into force

1st April 2012

The Treasury make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4A(3) and (4) of the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000[^f00001].

In accordance with section 4A(8) of that Act the Treasury have consulted, where they think it appropriate, the Scottish Ministers, the Department for Finance and Personnel for Northern Ireland and the Welsh Ministers.

## 1. Title and commencement[^c-0ace76e752f0c618cedd49e6527a7278]

This Order may be cited as the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000 (Estimates and Accounts) Order 2012 and comes into force on 1st April 2012.

## 2. Designations[^c-0452ef78e3cfd6a1522b76c7349efd8b]

A body listed in column 2 of a table in the Schedule is a designated body in relation to the department named in column 1 of that table for the financial year that ends on 31st March 2013.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates specified central government bodies in relation to named government departments for the purpose of those departments’ supply estimates and resource accounts.

The designations have effect for the financial year that ends on 31st March 2013.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument because no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
