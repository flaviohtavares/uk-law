# The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 523

Police, England And Wales

The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

Made

23rd February 2012

Laid before Parliament

29th February 2012

Coming into force

26th March 2012

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 113B(2)(b), 113BA(1) and 113BB(1) of the Police Act 1997[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation, commencement, interpretation and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 and shall come into force on 26th March 2012.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “the 2002 Regulations” mean the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) Regulations 2002[^f00002]; and
- “the 2009 Regulations” mean the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (No. 2) Regulations 2009[^f00003].

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment of regulation 2 of the 2002 Regulations

After the definition of “reprimand” in regulation 2[^f00004] of the 2002 Regulations (interpretation) insert—

> - “taxi driver licence” means a licence granted under—
>   - (i) section 46 of the Town Police Clauses Act 1847[^f00005];
>   - (ii) section 8 of the Metropolitan Public Carriage Act 1869[^f00006];
>   - (iii) section 9 of the Plymouth City Council Act 1975[^f00007];
>   - (iv) section 51 of the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976[^f00008]; or
>   - (v) section 13 of the Private Hire Vehicles (London) Act 1998[^f00009];

.

## 3. Amendment of regulation 5A of the 2002 Regulations

(1) Regulation 5A[^f00010] of the 2002 Regulations (enhanced criminal record certificates: prescribed purposes) is amended as follows.

(2) After sub-paragraph (za) insert—

> - (zb) considering the applicant’s suitability to obtain or hold a taxi driver licence.

## 4. Amendment of regulation 5 of the 2009 Regulations

In regulation 5 of the 2009 Regulations for “or (za)” substitute “, (za) or (zb)”.

## 5. Amendment of regulation 6 of the 2009 Regulations

In regulation 6 of the 2009 Regulations for “or (za)” substitute “, (za) or (zb)”.

Lynne Featherstone

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

23rd February 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) Regulations 2002 and the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (No. 2) Regulations 2009 to enable an application for an enhanced criminal record certificate (including suitability information relating to both children and adults) to be made for the purpose of assessing the applicant’s suitability to obtain or hold a taxi driver licence.
