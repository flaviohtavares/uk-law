# The Authorised Officers (Meat Inspection) (Revocation) (England) Regulations 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 690

Food, England

The Authorised Officers (Meat Inspection) (Revocation) (England) Regulations 2012

Made

4th March 2012

Laid before Parliament

8th March 2012

Coming into force

6th April 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(6) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002].

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003] there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

## 1. Title application and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Authorised Officers (Meat Inspection) (Revocation) (England) Regulations 2012, apply in relation to England only and come into force on 6th April 2012.

## 2. Revocation

The Authorised Officers (Meat Inspection) Regulations 1987[^f00004] are revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Paul Burstow

Minister of State,

Department of Health

4th March 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

**1.** These Regulations revoke the Authorised Officers (Meat Inspection) Regulations 1987 ([S.I. 1987/133](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/133)).

**2.** A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on business or the public or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
