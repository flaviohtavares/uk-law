# The Local Authorities (Conduct of Referendums) (Council Tax Increases) (England) Regulations 2012, section 9

> Text as at 2024-12-04.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Provision of information by relevant billing authority

*Extent: E+W*

A relevant billing authority must provide a precepting authority with such information as the precepting authority may reasonably require in exercising its functions under these Regulations.
