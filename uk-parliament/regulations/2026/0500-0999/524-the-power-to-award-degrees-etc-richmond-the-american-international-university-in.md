# The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Richmond, The American International University in London, Inc.) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 524

Education, England

The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Richmond, The American International University in London, Inc.) Order 2026

Made

14th May 2026

Coming into force

15th May 2026

The Office for Students, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 42(1) and 43(1) of the Higher Education and Research Act 2017[^f00001], makes the following Order.

The Office for Students has requested advice from the relevant body regarding the quality of, and standards applied to, higher education provided by the provider, and has had regard to that advice in accordance with sections 46(1)(a) and (7) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Richmond, The American International University in London, Inc.) Order 2026 and comes into force on 15th May 2026.

## 2. Authorisation to grant awards

Richmond, The American International University in London, Inc. (registered company number FC008955) (also known as Richmond, The American International University in London) is authorised to grant taught awards of the kind mentioned in section 42(2)(a) of the said Act.

**3.** The authorisation in article 2 is for a fixed period beginning on 15th May 2026 and expiring at the end of the day on 15th May 2029.

**4.** Richmond, The American International University in London, Inc. may authorise other institutions to grant such awards as specified in article 2 of this Order on its behalf.

Jean Arnold

Interim Director of Quality and Access

Office for Students

14th May 2026

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order authorises Richmond, The American International University in London, Inc. (UKPRN: 10005470) to grant taught awards for a fixed term beginning on 15th May 2026 and expiring at the end of the day on 15th May 2029.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations. The instrument has no impact on the public sector.
