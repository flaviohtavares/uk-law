# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Insertion of regulation 65ZA

After regulation 65 insert—

> **65ZA. Detained transport asset licences**
>
> The prohibitions in Chapter 4O (Detained transport assets) of Part 5 (Trade) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
>
> **65ZB. Ships licences**
>
> The prohibitions in regulation 57ZA(1) and (2) (prohibition on chartering or operating specified ships) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
