In accordance with [section 133](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/54/section/133) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/54/section/133) of the [Senior Courts Act 1981](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/54/contents), the Lord Chancellor has obtained the concurrence of the Master of the Rolls.

In accordance with [section 92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/39/section/92) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/39/section/92) and [(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/39/section/92) of the [Courts Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/39/contents)[^f00007], the Lord Chancellor has consulted the Lady Chief Justice, the Master of the Rolls, the President of the King’s Bench Division, the President of the Family Division, the Chancellor of the High Court, the Head of Civil Justice, the Deputy Head of Civil Justice and, in relation to civil proceedings, the Civil Justice Council.

In accordance with [section 54](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/section/54) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/section/54) of the [Mental Capacity Act 2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/contents), the Lord Chancellor has consulted the President of the Court of Protection, the Vice-President of the Court of Protection and the Senior Judge of the Court of Protection.

In accordance with [section 42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/15/section/42) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/15/section/42) of the [Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/15/contents)[^f00008], the Lord Chancellor has consulted the Senior President of Tribunals.

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Court and Tribunal Fees (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Change of coming into force) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on 5th July 2026.

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), this Order extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(4) Any amendment made by this Order has the same extent and application as the provision to which it relates.

## 2. Amendment of the Court and Tribunals Fees (Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2026

In Article 1(2) of the Court and Tribunal Fees (Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2026[^f00009] (citation, commencement, extent and application), for “6th July 2026” substitute “13th July 2026”.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Court and Tribunal Fees (Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2026 ([S.I. 2026/642](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/642)) (“**the 2026 Fees Order**”) amends the following instruments relating to court and tribunal fees—

- the Enrolment of Deeds (Fees) Regulations 1994 ([S.I. 1994/601](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/601)),
- the Non-Contentious Probate Fees Order 2004 ([S.I. 2004/3120](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3120)),
- the Court of Protection Fees Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/1745](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1745)),
- the Magistrates’ Courts Fees Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1052](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1052)),
- the Civil Proceedings Fees Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1053](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1053)),
- the Family Proceedings Fees Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1054](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1054)),
- the Upper Tribunal (Lands Chamber) Fees Order 2009 ([S.I. 2009/1114](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/1114)),
- the First-tier Tribunal (Gambling) Fees Order 2010 ([S.I. 2010/42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/42)),
- the Upper Tribunal (Immigration and Asylum Chamber) (Judicial Review) (England and Wales) Fees Order 2011 ([S.I. 2011/2344](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/2344)),
- the First-tier Tribunal (Immigration and Asylum Chamber) Fees Order 2011 ([S.I. 2011/2841](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/2841)), and
- the First-tier Tribunal (Property Chamber) Fees Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/1179](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1179)).

This Order postpones the coming into force of the 2026 Fees Order from 6th July 2026 until 13th July 2026.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no significant impact on business, charities or voluntary bodies is foreseen due to the change of the coming into force date. A full impact assessment covering of the effect of the increase in the application fee for a grant of probate in the 2026 Fees Order is available from the Ministry of Justice, 102 Petty France, London, SW1H 9AJ and is published with an Explanatory Memorandum on https://legislation.gov.uk. A full impact assessment has not been prepared for the remaining measures in the 2026 Fees Order as no significant impact on business, charities or voluntary bodies is foreseen.
