# The Education (Designated Institutions in Further Education) (No. 2) (Revocation) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 575

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Designated Institutions in Further Education) (No. 2) (Revocation) Order 2026

Made

1st June 2026

Laid before Parliament

3rd June 2026

Coming into force

24th June 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 28(1) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Education (Designated Institutions in Further Education) (No. 2) (Revocation) Order 2026 and comes into force on 24th June 2026.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Revocation

The Education (Designated Institutions in Further Education) (No. 2) Order 1993[^f00002] is revoked.

Smith of Malvern

Minister of State

Department for Education

1st June 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Education (Designated Institutions in Further Education) (No. 2) Order 1993 ([S.I. 1993/562](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/562)) (“**the 1993 Order**”) designated Northern College, Barnsley for the purposes of s28(1) Further and Higher Education Act 1992.

Article 2 of this Order revokes the 1993 Order meaning Northern College, Barnsley is no longer designated for these purposes.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
