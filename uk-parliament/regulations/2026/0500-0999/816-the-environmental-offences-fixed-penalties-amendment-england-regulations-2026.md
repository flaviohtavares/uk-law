# The Environmental Offences (Fixed Penalties) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 816

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Environmental Offences (Fixed Penalties) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2026

Made

14th July 2026

Laid before Parliament

15th July 2026

Coming into force

1st September 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [sections 33ZA](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/section/33ZA) [(10A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/section/33ZA) and [97A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/section/97A) of the [Environmental Protection Act 1990](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/contents) (and for section 97A, the Secretary of State is, for England, the appropriate person as defined in section 98(1A)(a) of that Act)[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Environmental Offences (Fixed Penalties) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 1st September 2026.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

(3) These Regulations apply in relation to England.

## 2. Amendment of the [Environmental Offences (Fixed Penalties) (England) Regulations 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1050/contents)

(1) The [Environmental Offences (Fixed Penalties) (England) Regulations 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1050/contents)[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) For [regulation 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1050/regulation/5) substitute—

> **5. Penalty range: litter, distribution of printed matter, graffiti and fly-posting: 1st April 2019 onwards**
>
> (1) The amount of a fixed penalty capable of being specified by a principal litter authority under [section 88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/section/88) [(6A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/section/88) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/section/88) of the [Environmental Protection Act 1990](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/contents) is not less than £65 and not more than £750.
>
> (2) The amount of a fixed penalty capable of being specified by a principal litter authority under [paragraph 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/schedule/3A/paragraph/7) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/schedule/3A/paragraph/7) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/schedule/3A/paragraph/7) of [Schedule 3A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/schedule/3A) to [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/contents) is not less than £65 and not more than £150.
>
> (3) The amount of a fixed penalty capable of being specified by a relevant local authority under [section 43A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/38/section/43A) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/38/section/43A) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/38/section/43A) of the [Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/38/contents) is not less than £65 and not more than £500.

.

## 3. Amendment of the [Environmental Protection Act 1990](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/contents)

(1) The [Environmental Protection Act 1990](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/contents)[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 33ZA](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/section/33ZA) [(9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/section/33ZA) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/section/33ZA), for “£1000” substitute “£5000”.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Environmental Offences (Fixed Penalties) (England) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/1050](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1050)) and the Environmental Protection Act [1990 (c.43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43).

Regulation 2 substitutes regulation 5 of the Environmental Offences (Fixed Penalties) (England) Regulations 2017 to increase, in relation to littering, the upper limit of the amount of a fixed penalty that may be specified by a principal litter authority from £500 to £750.

Regulation 3 amends section 33ZA(9)(a) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 to increase the upper limit of the amount of a fixed penalty that may be specified for a waste deposit offence from £1000 to £5000.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
