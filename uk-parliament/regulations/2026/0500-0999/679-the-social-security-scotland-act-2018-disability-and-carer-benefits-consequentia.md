The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 104, 112(1) and 113(2) and (3) of the Scotland Act 1998[^f00001].

## Part 1 — Citation, commencement and extent

### 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Disability and Carer Benefits) (Consequential Amendments) Order 2026 and comes into force on 16th July 2026.

(2) Part 1 of this Order extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(3) Part 2 of this Order extends to England, Wales and Scotland.

(4) Part 3 of this Order extends to Northern Ireland only.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes amendments in consequence of regulations made under section 28 (carer’s assistance), section 31 (disability assistance) and section 81 (carer’s allowance supplement) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act [2018 (asp 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2018/9).

This Order makes amendments to social security legislation in England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, as the case may be, governing entitlement to certain benefits in consequence of the Carer’s Assistance (Carer Support Payment) (Scotland) Regulations 2023 ([S.S.I. 2023/302](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2023/302)), the Disability Assistance for Older People (Scotland) Regulations 2024 ([S.S.I. 2024/166](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2024/166)), the Disability Assistance (Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 2025 ([S.S.I. 2025/3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2025/3)), the Carer’s Assistance (Young Carer Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2019 ([S.S.I. 2019/324](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2019/324)) and the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021 ([S.S.I. 2021/174](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2021/174)).

Those Regulations introduce arrangements for the payment of various Scottish disability and carer benefits in the form of carer support payment (replacing carer’s allowance), carer’s allowance supplement, young carer’s grant, pension age disability payment (replacing attendance allowance), child disability payments (replacing disability living allowance for children and young people under 16), Scottish adult disability living allowance (replacing disability living allowance) and adult disability payment (replacing personal independence payment).

Where applicable, these Scottish benefits are intended to interact with the reserved benefit system in the same way as the benefits that they replace to ensure equal treatment across Great Britain in the reserved system and in Northern Ireland.

Consequential amendments arising from the introduction of those benefits were previously made to the reserved benefits system and in Northern Ireland via multiple Orders made under section 104 of the Scotland Act 1998 including [S.I. 2018/872](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/872), [2020/989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/989), [2021/786](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/786), [2022/177](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/177), [2023/1218](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/1218), [2024/919](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/919) and [2025/227](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/227). The amendments included in this Order supplement those made in earlier Orders and correct omissions.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
