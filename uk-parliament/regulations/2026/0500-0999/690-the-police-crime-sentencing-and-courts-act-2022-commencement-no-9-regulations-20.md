# The Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022 (Commencement No. 9) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 690 (C. 59)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022 (Commencement No. 9) Regulations 2026

Made

25th June 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 208(1) of the Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022 (Commencement No. 9) Regulations 2026.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 29th June 2026

Section 81 (repeal of the Vagrancy Act 1824 etc) of the Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022 comes into force on 29th June 2026.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force specified provisions of the Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act [2022 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/32) (“**the Act**”). They are the ninth commencement regulations made under the Act.

Regulation 2 brings section 81 of the Act into force on 29th June 2026. Section 81 repeals the Vagrancy Act [1824 (c. 83)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1824/83) which created a range of offences relating to begging and rough sleeping. Section 81 also makes a number of amendments to other legislation consequential on that repeal.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
