# The North Lincolnshire Green Energy Park (Correction) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 533

INFRASTRUCTURE PLANNING

The North Lincolnshire Green Energy Park (Correction) Order 2026

Made

14th May 2026

Coming into force

15th May 2026

The North Lincolnshire Green Energy Park Order 2025 (“**the Order**”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“**the Act**”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period (as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act), the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the correction of errors in the Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed Bassetlaw District Council, East Riding of Yorkshire Council, City of Doncaster Council, North Lincolnshire Council, Lincolnshire County Council, North East Lincolnshire Council, Nottinghamshire County Council and West Lindsey District Council as the relevant local planning authorities[^f00004], that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 119, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the North Lincolnshire Green Energy Park (Correction) Order 2026 and comes into force on 15th May 2026.

## 2. Corrections

The North Lincolnshire Green Energy Park Order 2025 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule (corrections) to this Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

John Wheadon

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning & Innovation

Department for Energy Security and Net Zero 

14th May 2026
