# The Education (Free School Lunches in Maintained Nursery Schools) (Universal Credit) (England) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 706

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Free School Lunches in Maintained Nursery Schools) (Universal Credit) (England) Order 2026

Made

29th June 2026

Laid before Parliament

1st July 2026

Coming into force

17th August 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 512ZB(4B)(a) of the Education Act 1996[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Education (Free School Lunches in Maintained Nursery Schools) (Universal Credit) (England) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on 17th August 2026.

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order “**the 1996 Act**” means the Education Act 1996.

## 3. Persons to be treated as within section 512ZB(4A) of the 1996 Act

A registered pupil at a maintained nursery school[^f00002] in England is to be treated as a person within section 512ZB(4A) of the 1996 Act (provision of free school lunches and milk) if—

- (a) their parent is in receipt of universal credit, and
- (b) they are not within section 512ZB(4) of the 1996 Act[^f00003].

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings registered pupils at maintained nursery schools in England within section 512ZB(4A) (provision of free school lunches and milk) of the Education Act [1996 (c. 56)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56) (“**the 1996 Act**”), for the purpose of receiving free school lunches, if two conditions are met. Those conditions are (a) that their parent is in receipt of universal credit, and (b) they are not already eligible for free school lunches by virtue of being within section 512ZB(4) of the 1996 Act.

A full impact assessment of the effect of extending free school lunch entitlements on the private, voluntary or public sector was prepared for the Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act [2026 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/21), including the impact of extending free school lunch entitlements to pupils in maintained nursery schools. A copy of the impact assessment is available from the Department for Education at Sanctuary Buildings, Great Smith Street, London SW1P 3BT.
