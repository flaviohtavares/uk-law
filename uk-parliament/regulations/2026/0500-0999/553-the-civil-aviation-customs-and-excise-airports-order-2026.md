# The Civil Aviation (Customs and Excise Airports) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 553

CIVIL AVIATION

The Civil Aviation (Customs and Excise Airports) Order 2026

Made

20th May 2026

Coming into force

11th June 2026

The Secretary of State, with the concurrence of the Commissioners for His Majesty’s Revenue and Customs, makes this Order in exercise of the power conferred by article 219 of the Air Navigation Order 2016[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Customs and Excise Airports) Order 2026 and comes into force on 11th June 2026.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Revocation

The Civil Aviation (Customs and Excise Airports) Order 2021[^f00002] is revoked.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Civil Aviation (Customs and Excise Airports) Order 2021 and replaces it with a new Order which designates certain aerodromes to be places for the landing or departure of aircraft for the purposes of the enactments for the time being in force relating to customs and excise. The effect of this Order is that it removes the designation of five aerodromes for these purposes. These aerodromes are Coventry Airport, Doncaster (Sheffield) Airport, Filton Airport, Manston Airport and Plymouth Airport. A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, as no, or no significant impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
