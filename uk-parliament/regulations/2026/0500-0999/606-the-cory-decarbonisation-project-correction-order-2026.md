# The Cory Decarbonisation Project (Correction) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 606

Infrastructure Planning

The Cory Decarbonisation Project (Correction) Order 2026

Made

5th June 2026

Coming into force

8th June 2026

The Cory Decarbonisation Project Order 2025[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008[^f00002] (“**the Act**”), contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period[^f00003] the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00004] for the correction of errors and omissions in the Cory Decarbonisation Project Order 2025.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed London Borough of Bexley as the relevant local planning authority[^f00005], that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Cory Decarbonisation Project (Correction) Order 2026 and comes into force on 8th June 2026.

## 2. Corrections

The Cory Decarbonisation Project Order 2025 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to the Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

John Weadon

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning Delivery

Department of Energy Security and Net Zero

5th June 2026

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors in the Cory Decarbonisation Project Order 2025 ([S.I. 2025/1227](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1227)), a development consent order made under the Planning Act 2008, following a request made under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
