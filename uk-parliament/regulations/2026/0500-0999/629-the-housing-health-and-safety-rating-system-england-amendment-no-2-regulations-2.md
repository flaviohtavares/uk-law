# The Housing Health and Safety Rating System (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2026/571](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/571) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2 and 250(2) of the Housing Act 2004[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Health and Safety Rating System (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 21st June 2026.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment of the Housing Health and Safety Rating System (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

In regulation 10 (transitional provision: inspections) of the Housing Health and Safety Rating System (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2026[^f00002], for “22nd June” substitute “23rd June”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Housing Health and Safety Rating System (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2026 ([S.I. 2026/571](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/571)) (“**the 2026 Regulations**”). The 2026 Regulations made a series of amendments to the Housing Health and Safety Rating System (England) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/3208](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/3208)). Those amendments are subject to a transitional provision in regulation 10 of the 2026 Regulations which clarifies how those amendments apply in relation to inspections which begin either before, or on or after the date on which the amendments come into force. Regulation 10 should have referred to the 23rd June 2026, which is the date on which the 2026 Regulations come into force, rather than referring to the 22nd June. These Regulations correct regulation 10 of the 2026 Regulations by changing the date to 23rd June 2026.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant impact on the private, voluntary of public sector is foreseen.
