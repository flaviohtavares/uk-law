# The Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026 (Establishment of Schools) (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 855

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026 (Establishment of Schools) (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2026

Made

16th July 2026

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2) and (3)

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 73(1), (2) and (5) of the Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026[^f00001].

In accordance with section 73(4) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026 (Establishment of Schools) (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st September 2026, subject to paragraph (3).

(3) Regulation 4 comes into force at the same time as [section 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/26/section/7) of the [Children, Schools and Families Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/26/contents) (power to propose new schools)[^f00002].

(4) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## 7. Amendment of regulation 3 of the Local Government (Structural Changes) (Further Transitional and Supplementary Provision and Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2009

In [regulation 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/276/regulation/3) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/276/regulation/3) of the [Local Government (Structural Changes) (Further Transitional and Supplementary Provision and Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/276/contents) (functions relating to the reorganisation of schools)[^f00008]—

- (a) omit “section 6A (requirement to seek proposals for establishment of new Academies)”[^f00009];
- (b) for “section 10 (publication of proposals with consent of Secretary of State)” substitute “section 10 (proposals other than proposals pursuant to a section 7 notice: publication etc)”;
- (c) omit “section 11 (publication of proposals to establish maintained schools: special cases)”.

## 8. Transitional provision

(1) The amendments made by regulations 5, 6 and 7(b) and (c) do not apply in relation to those cases where, before those provisions come into force, proposals have been published under [section 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/11) of the [Education and Inspections Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/contents) (“**the Act**”)[^f00010] but a final determination of the proposals has not been made.

(2) The amendment made by regulation 7(a) does not apply in relation to cases where, before that provision comes into force, a local authority in England has—

- (a) sought proposals for the establishment of an Academy under [section 6A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/6A) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/6A) of [the Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/contents), and
- (b) specified a date for the proposals to be submitted under [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/6A) of [that section](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/6A),

but a final determination of the proposals has not been made.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make consequential amendments in connection with sections 65 to 69 of the Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act [2026 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/21). Those sections make amendments to the Education and Inspections Act [2006 (c. 40)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40) relating to the establishment of schools.

Regulation 8 contains transitional provisions which set out the circumstances in which the consequential amendments will not have effect.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
