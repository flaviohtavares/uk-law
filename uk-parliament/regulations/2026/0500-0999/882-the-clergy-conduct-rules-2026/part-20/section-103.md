# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 103

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 103. Restriction orders and suspensions: decisions on review or appeal

(1) On an appeal under [section 50](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50/subsection/4) or [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50/subsection/5) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) against a decision to impose a restriction order or suspension, the President of Tribunals or person hearing the appeal may—

- (a) confirm the decision,
- (b) reverse the decision, or
- (c) vary the terms of the restriction order or suspension.

(2) On a review under [section 50](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50) [(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50/subsection/6) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) of a decision not to impose a restriction order or suspension, the President of Tribunals may—

- (a) confirm the decision,
- (b) reverse the decision and, accordingly, impose a restriction order or suspension on the person to whom the decision relates, or
- (c) remit the decision to the relevant officer for reconsideration with such directions (if any) as the President considers appropriate.
