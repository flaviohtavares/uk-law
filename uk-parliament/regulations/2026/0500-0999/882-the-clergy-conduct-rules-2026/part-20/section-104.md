# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 104

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 104. Penalties: decisions on review

On a review of a decision to impose a penalty under [rule 89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/rule/89) or [section 50](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50) [(7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50/subsection/7) or [51](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/51) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/51/subsection/1) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) the President of Tribunals may—

- (a) confirm the penalty,
- (b) vary the penalty,
- (c) reverse the decision to impose the penalty, or
- (d) remit the decision to the person who imposed the penalty (or who would have power to impose the penalty at the time the decision is remitted) for reconsideration with such directions (if any) as the President considers appropriate.
