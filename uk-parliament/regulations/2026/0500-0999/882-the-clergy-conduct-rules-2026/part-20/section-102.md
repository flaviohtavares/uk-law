# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Decisions on review

(1) On a review under [section 50](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50/subsection/1) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), the President of Tribunals may—

- (a) confirm the decision,
- (b) substitute for the decision a decision to allocate the complaint as one of—
  - (i) misconduct (but not serious misconduct), or
  - (ii) serious misconduct, or
- (c) remit the decision to the lead assessor for reconsideration with such directions (if any) as the President considers appropriate.

(2) On a review under [section 50](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50/subsection/2) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), the President of Tribunals may—

- (a) confirm the decision, or
- (b) reverse the decision.

(3) If a decision is reversed under paragraph (2)(b), the President of Tribunals must remit the decision to the lead assessor to reconsider and make a decision under [section 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21/subsection/1) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21/subsection/1), [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21/subsection/1) or [(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21/subsection/1) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents).

(4) On a review under [section 50](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50/subsection/3) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), the President of Tribunals may—

- (a) confirm, reverse or vary a finding of fact or recommendation,
- (b) remit the matter to the case assessor for reconsideration with such directions (if any) as the President considers appropriate, or
- (c) direct the lead assessor to appoint a different case assessor and remit the matter to that person for reconsideration with such directions (if any) as the President considers appropriate.
