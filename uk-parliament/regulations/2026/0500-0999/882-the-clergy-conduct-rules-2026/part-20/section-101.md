# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 101

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 101. Making an appeal or a request for review

(1) This rule applies for the purposes of an appeal against a decision, or a request for a review of a decision, under [rule 89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/rule/89) or [section 50](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/50) or [51](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/51) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents).

(2) The appeal or request for review must be made before the end of 14 days beginning with the day after the decision is made.

(3) The appeal or request for review must be made by notice in writing in the form set out in Form 14.

(4) The notice must—

- (a) state the name, postal address, telephone number and email address of the person making the appeal or request,
- (b) if the person has a legal representative, state the legal representative’s name, postal address, telephone number and email address,
- (c) state the decision to which the appeal or request relates, and
- (d) specify the grounds for making the appeal or request for review.

(5) The grounds on which an appeal or request for review may be made are that the decision to the which the appeal or review relates—

- (a) was based on an error of fact,
- (b) was wrong in law, or
- (c) was unreasonable.

(6) A copy of the decision in respect of which the appeal or request is made must accompany the notice.

(7) The notice must not include, or be accompanied by, any evidence that was not submitted for the purposes of the original decision.
