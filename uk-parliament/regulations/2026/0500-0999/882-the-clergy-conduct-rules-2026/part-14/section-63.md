# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Proceedings cease on death of respondent

Proceedings under [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) and these Rules cease on the death of the respondent.
