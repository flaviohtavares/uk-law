# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Reallocation

(1) Where a referral is made under [section 23](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/23) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/23/subsection/3) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) by the designated person (referral and recommendation for reallocation), the decision of the lead assessor on what to do under [section 23](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/23) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/23/subsection/4) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) must be made before the end of five days beginning with the day after the lead assessor receives the referral.

(2) The lead assessor, having made that decision, must, before the end of three days beginning with the day after that decision was made, inform—

- (a) the parties to the complaint, and
- (b) the responsible bishop or, if the complaint is against an archbishop, the President of Tribunals.
