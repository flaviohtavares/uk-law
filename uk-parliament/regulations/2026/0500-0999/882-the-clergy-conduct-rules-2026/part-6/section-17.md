# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Provision of Code of Practice

(1) The designated person in relation to the complaint must, before the end of seven days beginning with the day after the person was appointed under [section 22](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/22) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/22/subsection/2) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), provide a copy of the Code of Practice to each party to the complaint.

(2) The Code of Practice may be provided by way of an electronic link.
