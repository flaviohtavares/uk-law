# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Withdrawal of admission

(1) The respondent may withdraw an admission made in accordance with rule 18 if permitted to do so by the President of Tribunals.

(2) An application to withdraw an admission must be made in accordance with rule 105.
