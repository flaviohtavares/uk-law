# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Application of this Part

(1) This Part applies where a complaint is allocated under [section 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21/subsection/1) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21/subsection/1) or [24](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/24) [(7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/24/subsection/7) [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/24/subsection/7) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) as a grievance.

(2) But this Part ceases to apply in relation to a complaint if, on a referral under [section 23](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/23) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/23/subsection/3) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), the lead assessor re-allocates the complaint as one of misconduct (whether or not serious misconduct).
