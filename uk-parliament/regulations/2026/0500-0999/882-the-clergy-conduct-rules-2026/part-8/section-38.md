# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Representations and decision

(1) Written representations (if any) made by a party to the complaint under [section 27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/27) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/27/subsection/4) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) must be made before the end of the representation period.

(2) The “**representation period**”, in relation to a report, is the period of 14 days beginning with the day after the report is given to the party under [section 27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/27) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/27/subsection/4) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents).

(3) The decision of the President of Tribunals on what to do under [section 27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/27) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/27/subsection/5) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) must be made before the end of 28 days beginning with the day after the representation period ends.

(4) The President of Tribunals must give a copy of the written record of the decision on what to do under [section 27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/27) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/27/subsection/5) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) to—

- (a) each party to the complaint,
- (b) the responsible bishop in relation to the complaint (unless the complaint is against an archbishop), and
- (c) the Investigation and Tribunals Team.

(5) Where the President of Tribunals makes the determination under [section 27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/27) [(9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/27/subsection/9) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) (referral to lead assessor of complaint of misconduct but not serious misconduct), that determination counts as the President’s decision for the purposes of [section 27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/27) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/27/subsection/5) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents).
