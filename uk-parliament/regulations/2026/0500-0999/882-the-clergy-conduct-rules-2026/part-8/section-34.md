# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Findings on the basis of admissions

If a response under rule 33 includes an admission, the tribunal or court may make a finding of misconduct (either serious misconduct or other misconduct), or a finding of fact, on the basis of the admission (without considering any further evidence).
