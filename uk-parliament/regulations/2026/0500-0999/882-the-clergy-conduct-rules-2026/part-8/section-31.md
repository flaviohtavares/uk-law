# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Application of this Part

This Part applies where a complaint is allocated under [section 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21/subsection/1) [(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21/subsection/1), [23](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/23) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/23/subsection/4) [(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/23/subsection/4) or [24](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/24) [(7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/24/subsection/7) [(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/24/subsection/7) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) as a complaint of serious misconduct.
