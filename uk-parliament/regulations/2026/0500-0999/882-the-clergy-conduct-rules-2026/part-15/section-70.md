# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 70

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 70. Penalties or sanctions imposed without a complaint: copies of decision

(1) This rule applies if a bishop or archbishop, or the President of Tribunals, imposes a penalty or administrative sanction under [section 46](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/46) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) (conviction for offence etc.).

(2) A bishop or archbishop who imposes a penalty or sanction must send a copy of the written record of the decision under [section 46](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/46) [(7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/46/subsection/7) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) to—

- (a) the clerk in Holy Orders on whom the penalty or sanction is imposed,
- (b) the Investigation and Tribunals Team,
- (c) either the provincial registrar (in the case of a decision made by an archbishop) or the diocesan registrar (in the case of a decision made by a bishop), and
- (d) if a diocesan safeguarding officer or the national director of safeguarding was consulted under [section 46](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/46) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/46/subsection/3) [(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/46/subsection/3) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) before the imposition of penalty or sanction, that person.

(3) If the President of Tribunals imposes a penalty or administrative sanction under [section 46](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/46) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents)—

- (a) the decision on the penalty or administrative sanction to impose must be recorded in writing, and
- (b) the President of Tribunals must send a copy of the written record of the decision to—
  - (i) the clerk in Holy Orders on whom the penalty or sanction is imposed,
  - (ii) the other archbishop,
  - (iii) the Investigation and Tribunals Team,
  - (iv) the provincial registrar, and
  - (v) the national director of safeguarding, if that person was consulted under [section 46](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/46) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/46/subsection/3) [(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/46/subsection/3) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) before the imposition of the penalty or sanction.
