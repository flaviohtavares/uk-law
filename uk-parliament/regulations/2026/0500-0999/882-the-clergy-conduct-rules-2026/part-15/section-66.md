# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Penalties: misconduct (but not serious misconduct)

(1) Before imposing a penalty under [section 38](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/38) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/38/subsection/1) or [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/38/subsection/3) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), the responsible bishop or the President of Tribunals must give each of the parties an opportunity to make written representations on—

- (a) whether or not it is appropriate to impose a penalty;
- (b) which penalty or penalties (if any) under [section 37](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/37) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/37/subsection/1) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) it is appropriate to impose.

(2) The representations (if any) must be made before the end of 14 days beginning with the day after the invitation to make those representations is given.
