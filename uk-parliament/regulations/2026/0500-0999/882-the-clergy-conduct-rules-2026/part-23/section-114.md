# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 114

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 114. Application of CCM to Royal Peculiar clergy

Schedule 2 (which makes provision for the application of the CCM and these Rules to clergy holding office in Royal Peculiars) has effect.
