# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Service on litigation friend

(1) This rule applies in relation to a document that is required or authorised to be given to a protected person under [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) or these Rules.

(2) The document—

- (a) must, if it is given, be given to the protected person’s litigation friend, and
- (b) may also be given to the protected person.

(3) If a document is given to the protected person’s litigation friend under paragraph (2)(a), it is treated for the purposes of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) and these Rules as being given to the protected person (whether or not it is also given to the protected person).
