# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Complaint form: additional information

(1) If the complainant is a person who has a proper interest in the complaint by virtue of a nomination under [section 15](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/15) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/15/subsection/2) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/15/subsection/2) [(ii)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/15/subsection/2), [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/15/subsection/4) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/15/subsection/4) or [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/15/subsection/5) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/15/subsection/5) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) (nomination by a PCC or the bishop’s council of a diocese), the complaint form must be accompanied by a copy of the resolution making the nomination.

(2) If the complainant is an archdeacon who has a proper interest in the complaint by virtue of a nomination under [section 15](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/15) [(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/15/subsection/6) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), the complaint form must be accompanied by a copy of the instrument making the nomination.

(3) If the complainant is a person appointed by the President of Tribunals as a litigation friend under [section 17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/17) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), the complaint form must be accompanied by a copy of the order of the President making the appointment.

(4) If the complainant is a person who is subject to a restraint order under [section 32](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/32) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), the complaint form must be accompanied by a copy of the permission given by the President of Tribunals under [section 32](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/32) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/32/subsection/2) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/32/subsection/2) or [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/32/subsection/2) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents).
