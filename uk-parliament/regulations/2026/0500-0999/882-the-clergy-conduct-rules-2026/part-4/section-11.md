# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Complaint about exercise of function conferred by or under [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents)

(1) This rule applies to an application to the President of Tribunals under [section 16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/16) [(10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/16/subsection/10) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) for permission to make a complaint about the exercise of a function conferred by or under [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents).

(2) The application must be made in accordance with rule 105.

(3) A copy of the application must be given to the person who would be the respondent to the complaint if the application for permission under [section 16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/16) [(10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/16/subsection/10) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) were to be granted (“the would-be respondent”).

(4) The President of Tribunals may determine the application without a hearing.

(5) Before determining the application, the President may request representations from the would-be respondent on the application.

(6) If the President exercises the power under paragraph (5), the President must inform the would-be respondent of the right to make written representations on the application; and representations on the application must be made before the end of 14 days beginning with the day after the President makes the request.
