# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Addition of safeguarding professional as party to complaint

(1) On an application under [section 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/28) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/28/subsection/1) [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/28/subsection/1) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), the President of Tribunals may, if satisfied that an issue relating to the safeguarding of a child or vulnerable adult arises, order that the relevant safeguarding professional is to become a party to the complaint.

(2) The application—

- (a) must be made in accordance with rule 105,
- (b) must be accompanied by a copy of every document in the applicant’s possession which relates to the complaint, and
- (c) may be made without notice being given to any person (but the President may direct under rule 4 that written notice of the application must be given to specified persons).

(3) In these Rules, “**relevant safeguarding professional**” means—

- (a) a diocesan safeguarding officer,
- (b) the national director of safeguarding, or
- (c) a member of the national safeguarding team.
