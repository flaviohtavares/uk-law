# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Action after allocation of complaint

(1) The responsible bishop, having received a notification under [section 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21/subsection/4) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), must notify each party to the complaint of the lead assessor’s decision.

(2) The notification required by paragraph (1) (the “decision notice”) must be given—

- (a) in writing, and
- (b) before the end of the five days beginning with the day after the notification under [section 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21/subsection/4) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) is received.

(3) Where [section 22](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/22) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/22/subsection/2) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) applies (allocation as grievance: appointment of designated person), the decision notice must also include the name of the designated person.

(4) Where [section 22](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/22) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/22/subsection/4) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) applies (allocation as misconduct: appointment of case assessor), the decision notice must include the name of the case assessor appointed.

(5) Where [section 22](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/22) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/22/subsection/5) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) applies (allocation as serious misconduct: referral to Investigation and Tribunals Team), the referral to the Investigation and Tribunals Team must be made when the decision notice is given.

(6) The decision must be accompanied by—

- (a) a copy of the complaint form in Form 2, and
- (b) a copy of the lead assessor’s decision in Form 3.
