# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Withdrawal of admission

(1) This rule applies if a response under rule 25 includes an admission.

(2) The respondent may withdraw the admission if permitted to do so by the President of Tribunals.

(3) An application to withdraw the admission must be made in accordance with rule 105.
