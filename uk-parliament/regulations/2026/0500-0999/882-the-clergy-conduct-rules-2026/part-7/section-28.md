# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Report

(1) The case assessor must complete the investigation and issue the report under [section 25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/25) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/25/subsection/2) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) before—

- (a) the end of four months beginning with the day after the case assessor is appointed, or
- (b) the end of such longer period as the President of Tribunals may direct on the application of the case assessor.

(2) An application under paragraph (1)(b) must be made in accordance with rule 105.
