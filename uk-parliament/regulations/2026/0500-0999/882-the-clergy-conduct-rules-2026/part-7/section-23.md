# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Application of this Part

This Part applies where a complaint is allocated under [section 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21/subsection/1) [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/21/subsection/1) or [23](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/23) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/23/subsection/4) [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/23/subsection/4) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) as a complaint of misconduct (but not serious misconduct).
