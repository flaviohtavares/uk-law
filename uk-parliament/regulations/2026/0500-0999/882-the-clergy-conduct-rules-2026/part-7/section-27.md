# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Advice from the Investigation and Tribunals Team

The case assessor must seek advice from the Investigation and Tribunals Team if, during the investigation under [section 24](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/24) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents)—

- (a) a question of law arises, or
- (b) a question arises on the interpretation of a provision of a code of practice issued under [section 5A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2016/1/section/5A) of the [Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2016/1/contents)[^f00003].
