# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Rules may be cited as the Clergy Conduct Rules 2026.

(2) These Rules come into force immediately after the commencement of [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/2) of the [Clergy Conduct Measure 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents)[^f00002].

(3) In these Rules, the Clergy Conduct Measure 2026 is referred to as “**the CCM**”.

(4) A reference in these Rules to giving or sending a document to a person is to be read with rule 111.

(5) An expression used in these Rules and in [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) has the same meaning in these Rules as in [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents).

(6) A reference in these Rules to a numbered Form is to the Form numbered as such in Schedule 1.
