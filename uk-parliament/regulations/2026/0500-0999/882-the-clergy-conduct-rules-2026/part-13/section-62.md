# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 62

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 62. Power to order identity not to be published

(1) The tribunal or court may order that the name and other identifying details of a person who is involved, or referred to, in the proceedings must not be published or otherwise made public.

(2) The tribunal or court may make an order under paragraph (1) only if satisfied that the order—

- (a) is desirable to protect the private life of a person,
- (b) is desirable to protect the interests of a child, or
- (c) is otherwise in the interests of the administration of justice.

(3) An order under paragraph (1) may be of a defined or indefinite duration (and may, accordingly, have effect beyond the conclusion of the proceedings).
