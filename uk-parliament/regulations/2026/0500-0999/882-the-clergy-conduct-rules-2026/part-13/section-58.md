# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Personal attendance of witness

The Chair of the tribunal or court may, at any stage of the proceedings on the complaint, require the personal attendance at the hearing of—

- (a) the author of a witness statement, or
- (b) an expert who has produced a report, or is giving evidence in another form, in accordance with rule 51.
