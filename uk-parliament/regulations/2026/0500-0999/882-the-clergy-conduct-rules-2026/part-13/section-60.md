# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 60

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 60. Power to exclude from hearing

The tribunal or court may exclude a person from the hearing if, in the opinion of the tribunal or court, the person—

- (a) has disrupted the hearing or threatens to disrupt the hearing, or
- (b) has otherwise interfered with the administration of justice.
