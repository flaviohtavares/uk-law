# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 115

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 115. Time limits

(1) The President of Tribunals may extend the duration of a period provided for under these Rules, or revive a period which has expired, unless the extension or revival would be inconsistent with a provision of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents).

(2) Paragraph (1) does not affect the power of any other person to extend or revive a period under these Rules.
