# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 117

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 117. Reporting convictions etc.

Where a clerk in Holy Orders is required under [section 55](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/55) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/55/subsection/2) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) to inform a bishop, archbishop or the President of Tribunals of an occurrence specified in [section 55](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/55) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/55/subsection/1) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), the information must be provided in writing.
