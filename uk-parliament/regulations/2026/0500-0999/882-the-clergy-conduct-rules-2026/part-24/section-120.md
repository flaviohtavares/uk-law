# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 120

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 120. Excluded offences

(1) The following provisions of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) do not apply in relation to excluded offences—

- (a) [section 34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/34) (restriction orders and suspensions: power to impose),
- (b) [section 46](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/46) (conviction for offence etc: power to impose penalty etc), and
- (c) [section 55](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/55) (conviction, arrest, caution etc).

(2) In this rule, “**excluded offence**” means an offence under any of the following provisions—

- (a) section 72 of the Highway Act 1835[^f00007] (riding on footpaths etc.);
- (b) any provision of [Part 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/part/6) of the [Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/contents)[^f00008] (speed limits);
- (c) the following provisions of the [Road Traffic Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/52/contents)[^f00009] —
  - (i) section 14 (seat belts: adults);
  - (ii) section 23 (carrying persons on motorcycles);
  - (iii) section 24 (carrying persons on bicycles);
  - (iv) section 29 (careless and inconsiderate cycling);
  - (v) section 35 (compliance with traffic directions);
  - (vi) section 36 (compliance with traffic signs);
  - (vii) section 41A (breach of requirements as to brakes, steering-gear or tyres);
  - (viii) section 47 (obligatory test certificates);
- (d) a provision specified in [Schedule 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/53/schedule/3) to the [Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/53/contents)[^f00010] (fixed penalty offences).

(3) An offence ceases to be an excluded offence if the person convicted of it is ordered to be disqualified from holding or obtaining a licence to drive a motor vehicle granted under [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/52/part/3) of the [Road Traffic Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/52/contents).
