# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 119

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 119. Vexatious litigants: restraint orders

An application for an order under any of the following provisions of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) must be made in accordance with rule 105—

- (a) [section 32](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/32) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/32/subsection/1) (restraint order);
- (b) [section 33](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/33) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/33/subsection/4) (permission to make or proceed with complaint, application or request);
- (c) [section 33](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/33) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/33/subsection/5) (variation or discharge of a restraint order).
