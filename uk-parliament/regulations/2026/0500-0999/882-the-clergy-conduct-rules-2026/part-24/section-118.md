# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 118

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 118. Reporting divorce or separation order

Where a clerk in Holy Orders is required under [section 56](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/56) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/56/subsection/1) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) to inform a bishop, archbishop or the President of Tribunals that a divorce order is made final or a judicial separation order is made, the information must be provided in writing.
