# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 121

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 121. Irregularities

(1) An irregularity or error of procedure in proceedings under [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) or these Rules does not invalidate any step taken in the proceedings unless—

- (a) the President, the registrar of tribunals or the Chair of the tribunal or court orders that the step is invalid on those grounds, or
- (b) on appeal, the Arches Court of Canterbury or the Chancery Court of York holds that the step is invalid on those grounds.

(2) The President, the registrar of tribunals or the Chair of the tribunal or court may give directions to correct or waive the irregularity.
