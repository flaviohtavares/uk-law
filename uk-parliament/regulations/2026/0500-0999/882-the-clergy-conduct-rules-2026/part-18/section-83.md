# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 83

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 83. Notice of inclusion in the list

(1) The archbishop of the relevant province must, before the end of 14 days beginning with the day after a person is included on the list maintained under [section 58](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/58) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/58/subsection/1) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), give written notice of the inclusion to the person.

(2) A notice under paragraph (1) must—

- (a) state that the person has been included on the list,
- (b) specify the particulars recorded on the list in respect of that person, and
- (c) state that the person has a right of appeal—
  - (i) against the particulars included on the list (under [section 58](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/58) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/58/subsection/3) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents)), and
  - (ii) against the fact of inclusion in the list, but only on the grounds that the person’s name was included in error (under [section 58](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/58) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/58/subsection/4) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents)).
