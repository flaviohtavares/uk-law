# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 84

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 84. Protected convictions

(1) This rule applies where—

- (a) the inclusion of a clerk in Holy orders in the list maintained under [section 58](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/58) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/58/subsection/1) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) is based on a conviction or caution, and
- (b) the conviction or caution becomes protected.

(2) The particulars recorded on the list, in relation to the clerk, may include the fact that the conviction or caution has become protected.

(3) The grounds on which the clerk may appeal under [section 58](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/58) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/58/subsection/3) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) against the particulars recorded in the list include the grounds that the conviction or caution has become protected.

(4) For the purposes of this rule, a conviction or caution is “protected” if it is protected for the purposes of an order made by the Secretary of State under the [Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1974/53/contents)[^f00005].
