# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 82

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 82. Access to the list

(1) The Archbishops of Canterbury and York must make the list maintained under [section 58](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/58) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/58/subsection/1) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) available to—

- (a) the President of Tribunals,
- (b) the Investigation and Tribunals Team,
- (c) diocesan and suffragan bishops,
- (d) persons appointed as lead assessors, and
- (e) provincial and diocesan registrars and their deputies.

(2) The list must not be open to public inspection or made available to a person who is not listed in paragraph (1).
