# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Record of decision on restriction order or suspension

(1) This rule applies if—

- (a) at least one of the conditions in [section 34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/34) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/34/subsection/1) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) is met in relation to a cleric, and
- (b) the relevant officer decides either to impose an order under [section 34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/34) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/34/subsection/2) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) or not to do so.

(2) The relevant officer must—

- (a) record the decision in the form set out in Form 12, and
- (b) retain a copy of that record for such period as the relevant officer considers appropriate.

(3) The record of the decision must include—

- (a) the reasons for the decision, and
- (b) if the relevant officer has consulted a person in accordance with [section 35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/35) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/35/subsection/3) or [36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/36) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/36/subsection/3) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents), a summary of the person’s advice.

(4) If the advice referred to in paragraph (3)(b) is given in writing, a copy of the advice must be included in or attached to the record of the decision.
