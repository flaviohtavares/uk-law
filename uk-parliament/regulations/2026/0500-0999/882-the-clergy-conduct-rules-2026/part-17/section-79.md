# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 79

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 79. Notice of revocation of restriction order or suspension

(1) This rule applies if a relevant officer revokes a restriction order or suspension under [section 34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/34) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/section/34/subsection/3) of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents).

(2) The relevant officer must give notice of the revocation to the cleric in the form set out in Form 11a or 11b.

(3) The revocation takes effect on the date specified in the notice.
