# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 109

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 109. Dismissal of vexatious etc applications

If, on an application for an order under [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) or these Rules, the person to whom the application is made considers that it is vexatious or wholly without merit and dismisses it, the order dismissing the application must record that it was considered to be vexatious or totally without merit.
