# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 108

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 108. Disposal of applications without a hearing

(1) This rule applies where, on an application under [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) or these Rules—

- (a) an order is made without a hearing being held, and
- (b) the parties to the complaint in respect of which the application is made, or other persons affected by the order, have not had an opportunity to make representations in relation to the application.

(2) A party to the complaint, or a person affected by the order, may apply to the tribunal or court or to the President of Tribunals for the order to be set aside, varied or stayed.

(3) An application under paragraph (2), in relation to an order, may only be made—

- (a) before the end of seven days beginning with the day after a copy of the order is given to the applicant under rule 110, or
- (b) within such longer period (if any) as is specified in the order.
