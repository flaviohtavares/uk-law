# The Clergy Conduct Rules 2026, section 106

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 106. Notice of applications

(1) This rule applies where an application is made under a provision of [the CCM](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2026/1/contents) or these Rules, except where it is provided that the application must or may be made without notice to the other parties.

(2) A copy of the application must be given to the parties or the other party to the complaint at the same time as it is given to the person referred to in rule 105(8).
