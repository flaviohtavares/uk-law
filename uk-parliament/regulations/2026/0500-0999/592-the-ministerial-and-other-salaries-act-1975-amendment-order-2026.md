# The Ministerial and other Salaries Act 1975 (Amendment) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 592

PARLIAMENT

The Ministerial and other Salaries Act 1975 (Amendment) Order 2026

Made

3rd June 2026

Coming into force

4th June 2026

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 3rd day of June 2026

Present,

The King's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

In accordance with section 1B of the Ministerial and other Salaries Act 1975[^f00001], a draft of this Order was laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

Accordingly, His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1B of that Act, is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Ministerial and other Salaries Act 1975 (Amendment) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Amendment of the Ministerial and other Salaries Act 1975

(1) The Ministerial and other Salaries Act 1975 (“**the 1975 Act**”) is amended in accordance with articles 3 to 6.

(2) The amendments made by articles 3 to 5 have effect from the beginning of 1st April 2026.

(3) The amendments made by article 6 have effect for the period of 12 months beginning with 1st April 2026 and each successive period of 12 months.

## 3. Ministerial salaries

(1) In section 1(2) (Lord Chancellor’s salary)[^f00002]—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “£68,827” substitute “£75,170”;
- (b) in paragraph (b), for “£101,038” substitute “£110,351”.

(2) In Schedule 1 (Ministerial salaries)[^f00003], for Parts 1 to 4 substitute the provisions in the Schedule to this Order.

## 4. Opposition Leaders’ and Whips’ salaries

## 5. Speakers’ salaries

In section 1 (salaries)—

- (a) in subsection (3) (speaker of the House of Commons)[^f00005], for “£75,766” substitute “£82,750”;
- (b) in subsection (3A) (speaker of the House of Lords)[^f00006], for “£101,038” substitute “£110,351”.

## 6. Alteration of salaries

In section 1A (alteration of salaries), for subsections (1) to (2A) substitute—

> (1) For each period of 12 months beginning with 1st April, the annual amount of any salary payable under section 1 is to be increased by the relevant percentage.
>
> (2) The relevant percentage is the average percentage by which the mid-points of the Senior Civil Service pay bands below that of permanent secretary, having effect from 1st April of the period concerned, have increased compared with the previous 1st April.
>
> (2A) But in relation to the period of 12 months beginning with 1st April 2026, the relevant percentage is—
>
> - (a) the relevant percentage determined in accordance with subsection (2), or
> - (b) if higher, the average percentage by which the mid-points of the Senior Civil Service pay bands (including that of permanent secretary), having effect from 1st April 2026, have increased compared with 1st April 2025.

.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order in Council)

This Order changes the annual amount of salaries payable under section 1 of the Ministerial and other Salaries Act 1975 (“**the 1975 Act**”) to Ministers, Opposition Leaders and Whips and the Commons and Lords Speakers. This Order also updates the formula in section 1A of the 1975 Act that provides for annual alterations of those salaries.

Article 2 provides that the amounts of salaries specified in this Order have effect from the beginning of 1st April 2026. It also provides that the updated formula in section 1A has effect for the period of 12 months beginning with 1st April 2026 and each successive period of 12 months (so that the amounts specified in this Order will be altered for the period beginning with 1st April 2026 and each successive period).

Articles 3 to 5, and the Schedule, set out the new salaries payable under section 1 of the 1975 Act.

Article 6 updates the formula in section 1B of the 1975 Act so that it refers to the mid-points of the Senior Civil Service pay bands below that of permanent secretary, subject to a transitional measure for the period of 12 months beginning with 1st April 2026.
