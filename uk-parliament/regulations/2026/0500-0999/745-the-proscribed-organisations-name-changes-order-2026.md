# The Proscribed Organisations (Name Changes) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 745

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Proscribed Organisations (Name Changes) Order 2026

Made

2nd July 2026

Laid before Parliament

6th July 2026

Coming into force

7th July 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(6)(a) of the Terrorism Act 2000[^f00001].

In accordance with [section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/11/section/3) [(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/11/section/3) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/11/section/3) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/11/contents), the Secretary of State believes that the organisations referred to in article 2 of this Order, being organisations listed in [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/11/schedule/2) to [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/11/contents), are operating wholly or partly under names that are not specified in [that Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/11/schedule/2) (whether as well as or instead of under the specified names).

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Proscribed Organisations (Name Changes) Order 2026 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is laid before Parliament.

(2) This Order extends to the United Kingdom.

## 2. Name changes

(1) The names specified in sub-paragraphs (a), (b) and (c) of paragraph (2), being names not specified in [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/schedule/2) to the [Terrorism Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/contents), are to be treated as other names for organisations listed in [that Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/schedule/2) as set out in those sub-paragraphs.

(2) Those names are—

- (a) The Islamic Thinkers Society which is to be treated as another name for both Al-Ghurabaa and The Saved Sect[^f00002];
- (b) The Resistance Front which is to be treated as another name for Lashkar e Tayyaba[^f00003];
- (c) Babbar Khalsa International which is to be treated as another name for Babbar Khalsa[^f00004].

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

[Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/part/2) of the [Terrorism Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/contents) (c. 11) makes provision about proscribed organisations (including the offences in respect of such organisations set out in sections 11 to 13). An organisation is proscribed if it is listed in [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/schedule/2) to [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/contents) or operates under the same name as an organisation so listed (section 3(1)).

[Section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/3) [(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/3) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/contents) (as inserted by section 22(2) of the Terrorism Act [2006 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/11)) enables the Secretary of State, by order, to provide that a name which is not specified in Schedule 2 to the Act is to be treated as another name for an organisation listed in that Schedule. Article 2 of this Order exercises this power to specify other names for the listed organisations referred to in that article.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary, or public sector is foreseen.
