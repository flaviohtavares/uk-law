# The Iran (Sanctions) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Application of prohibitions and requirements outside the United Kingdom

In regulation 3 (application of prohibitions and requirements outside the United Kingdom)—

- (a) in paragraph (3)(e), for “or a trade licence” substitute “, a trade licence or a ships licence”;
- (b) in paragraph (6)(b), for “or a trade licence” substitute “, a trade licence or a ships licence”.
