# The Iran (Sanctions) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Trade enforcement: application of CEMA

In regulation 84(5) (trade enforcement: application of CEMA), after sub-paragraph (b), insert—

> - (c) regulation 43B(3) (technical assistance relating to ships);
> - (d) regulation 43C(4) (provision of services relating to specified ships);
> - (e) regulation 43D(4) (procurement of services relating to specified ships).
