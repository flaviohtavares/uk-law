# The Iran (Sanctions) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Exceptions from prohibitions relating to ships

After regulation 56A (trade: exceptions in relation to personal effects etc.), insert—

> **56B. Trade: exceptions from prohibitions on services provided or procured relating to specified ships**
>
> (1) The prohibitions specified in paragraph (2) are not contravened by conduct that is necessary to protect or avoid endangering—
>
> - (a) the safety of any ship, or
> - (b) the life of any person.
>
> (2) Paragraph (1) applies to the prohibitions—
>
> - (a) in regulation 43C (provision of services relating to specified ships);
> - (b) in regulation 43D (procurement of services relating to specified ships).
>
> **56C. Ships: exceptions from prohibition on operating specified ships**
>
> (1) A person does not contravene the prohibition in regulation 45A(2) (operating specified ships) by conduct in the United Kingdom or in the territorial sea where the person—
>
> - (a) is not a United Kingdom person, and
> - (b) the person’s presence in the United Kingdom or in the territorial sea arises directly from an exercise of either—
>   - (i) the right of innocent passage, or
>   - (ii) the right of transit passage,
>
>   in accordance with the provisions of the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea[^f00023].
>
> (2) A person does not contravene the prohibition in regulation 45A(2) by conduct necessary to protect or avoid endangering—
>
> - (a) the safety of any ship, or
> - (b) the life of any person.

.
