# The Iran (Sanctions) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Ships licences

After regulation 62 (trade licences), insert—

> **62B. Ships licences**
>
> The prohibitions in regulation 45A(1) and (2) (prohibition on chartering or operating specified ships) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
