# The Iran (Sanctions) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Trade: exception for emergencies in certain cases

In regulation 56 (trade: exception for emergencies in certain cases), for paragraph (2), substitute—

> (2) Paragraph (1) applies to the prohibitions in—
>
> - (a) regulations 28 to 34 (prohibitions relating to restricted goods, etc.);
> - (b) regulations 37 to 43 (prohibitions relating to goods of strategic concern, etc.);
> - (c) regulation 43B (technical assistance relating to ships);
> - (d) regulation 43C (provision of services relating to specified ships);
> - (e) regulation 43D (procurement of services relating to specified ships).
