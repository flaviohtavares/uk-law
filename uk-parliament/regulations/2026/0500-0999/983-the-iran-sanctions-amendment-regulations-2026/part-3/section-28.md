# The Iran (Sanctions) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Penalties for offences

In regulation 79 (penalties for offences)—

- (a) in the opening words of paragraph (1), after “or regulation” insert “45A (prohibition on chartering or operating specified ships),”;
- (b) in the opening words of paragraph (3), after “(trade: licensing offences),” insert “66A (ships: licensing offences),”.
