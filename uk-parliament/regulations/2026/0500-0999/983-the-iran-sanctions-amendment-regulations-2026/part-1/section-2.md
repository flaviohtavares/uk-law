# The Iran (Sanctions) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “**the 2019 Regulations**” mean the Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00003];
- “**the 2023 Regulations**” mean the Iran (Sanctions) Regulations 2023[^f00004].
