# The Iran (Sanctions) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Disclosure of information

In regulation 54 (disclosure of information), in paragraph (2)(d)(ii), for the words from “a prohibition” to the end, substitute “any prohibition in Part 5 (trade) on imports or exports”.
