# The Iran (Sanctions) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Designation of persons

In regulation 5(1) (designation of persons), after sub-paragraph (b), insert—

> - (c) regulation 33D (technical assistance relating to ships);
> - (d) regulation 36A (movement of aircraft);
> - (e) regulation 36G (prohibition on port entry);
> - (f) regulation 36I (movement of ships);
> - (g) regulation 36J (detention of ships);
> - (h) regulation 36K (registration of ships in the United Kingdom);
> - (i) regulation 43B (technical assistance relating to ships).
