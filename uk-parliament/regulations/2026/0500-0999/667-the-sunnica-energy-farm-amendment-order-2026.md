# The Sunnica Energy Farm (Amendment) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 667

Infrastructure Planning

The Sunnica Energy Farm (Amendment) Order 2026

Made

21st May 2026

Coming into force

22nd May 2026

An application has been made, under paragraph 2 of Schedule 6 to the Planning Act 2008[^f00001], to the Secretary of State in accordance with the Infrastructure Planning (Changes to, and Revocation of, Development Consent Orders) Regulations 2011[^f00002] (“**the 2011 Regulations**”) for non-material changes to the Sunnica Energy Farm Order 2024[^f00003].

The Secretary of State, having considered the application, the responses to the publicity and consultation required by Regulations 6 and 7 of the 2011 Regulations, has decided to make the changes on terms that in the opinion of the Secretary of State are not materially different from those proposed in the application.

Accordingly, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 2(1) and (9) of Schedule 6 to the Planning Act 2008, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Sunnica Energy Farm (Amendment) Order 2026 and comes into force on 22nd May 2026.

## 2. Amendment to the Sunnica Energy Farm Order 2024

The Sunnica Energy Farm Order 2024 (“the 2024 Order”) is amended as follows.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Sunnica Energy Farm Order 2024, a development consent order under the Planning Act 2008, following an application made in accordance with the Infrastructure Planning (Changes to, and Revocation of, Development Consent Orders) Regulations 2011 for a non-material amendment under paragraph 2 of Schedule 6 to the Planning Act 2008. This Order amends Schedule 10 of the 2024 Order relating to certified documents.
