# The Peartree Hill Solar Farm Order 2026, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Special category land

(1) On the exercise by the undertaker of the relevant Order powers to acquire rights or impose restrictive covenants over the special category land so much of the special category land as is required for the purposes of the exercise by the undertaker of those rights or enforcement of those restrictive covenants is discharged from all rights, trusts and incidents to which it was previously subject, so far as their continuance would be inconsistent with the exercise of the relevant Order powers.

(2) In this article—

- “**relevant Order powers**” means the rights and powers exercisable over the special category land by the undertaker under article 24 (compulsory acquisition of rights and imposition of restrictive covenants); and
- “**special category land**” means the land in the East Riding of Yorkshire identified in the book of reference and on the special category land plans and forming part of Figham Common and numbered 13-6, 13-8, 14-1 and 14-3 on the special category land plans.
