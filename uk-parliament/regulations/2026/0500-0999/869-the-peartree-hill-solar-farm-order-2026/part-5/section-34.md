# The Peartree Hill Solar Farm Order 2026, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Apparatus and rights of statutory undertakers in closed streets

Where a street is altered or diverted or its use is temporarily prohibited or restricted under article 10 (street works), article 12 (power to alter layout, etc., of streets), article 13 (construction and maintenance of altered streets) or article 14 (temporary closure of streets and public rights of way) any statutory undertaker whose apparatus is under, in, on, along or across the street has the same powers and rights in respect of that apparatus, subject to Schedule 11 (protective provisions), as if this Order had not been made.
