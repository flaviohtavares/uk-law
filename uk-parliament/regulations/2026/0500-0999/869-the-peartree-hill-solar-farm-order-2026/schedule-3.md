# The Peartree Hill Solar Farm Order 2026, SCHEDULE 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 3 — LEGISLATION TO BE DISAPPLIED

Article 8

**1.** The following local enactments and local byelaws do not apply insofar as they are inconsistent with a provision of, or a power conferred by, this Order—

- (a) East Riding Drainage Act 1798[^f00065];
- (b) Beverley and Skidby Drainage Act 1808[^f00066];
- (c) Holderness Drainage Act 1832[^f00067];
- (d) Beverley Commons Act 1836[^f00068];
- (e) Beverley and Barmston Drainage Act 1880[^f00069];
- (f) Kingston-upon-Hull Corporation Act 1901[^f00070];
- (g) Holderness Water Act 1908[^f00071];
- (h) Kingston-upon-Hull Corporation Act 1911[^f00072];
- (i) Kingston-upon-Hull Corporation Act 1922[^f00073];
- (j) Kingston-upon-Hull Corporation Act 1926[^f00074];
- (k) Urban District Council of Hornsea Nuisances Byelaws 1926;
- (l) Administrative County of the East Riding of Yorkshire Good Rule and Government of the County – Uprooting Plants 1930;
- (m) Kingston-upon-Hull Corporation Act 1930[^f00075];
- (n) Haltemprice Urban District Council – Pleasure Grounds and Open Spaces 1951;
- (o) The County of York, East Riding (Removal of Mud Etc. from Wheels of Vehicles) Byelaw 1952;
- (p) The County Council of York, East Riding – The County of York, East Riding (Dropping of Water and Loose Substances from Vehicles on Highways) Byelaw 1961;
- (q) The County Council of York, East Riding – The County of York, East Riding (Protection of Safety Lamps and Barriers) Byelaw 1965;
- (r) Kingston-upon-Hull Corporation Act 1967[^f00076];
- (s) Associated British Ports Act 1987[^f00077];
- (t) Beverley Pasture Masters – Byelaws 1987;
- (u) Beverley Pasture Masters – Byelaws 2004; and
- (v) Beverley and North Holderness Internal Drainage Board Byelaws 2021.
