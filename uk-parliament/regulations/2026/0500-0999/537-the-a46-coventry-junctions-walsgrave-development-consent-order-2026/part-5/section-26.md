## 26. Compulsory acquisition of land[^key-aa455d7316ad9f12ca6603051e6f7ca0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised development, or to facilitate it, or is incidental to it.

(2) This article is subject to article 29(4) (compulsory acquisition of rights and imposition of restrictive covenants) and article 38 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development).
