# The A46 Coventry Junctions (Walsgrave) Development Consent Order 2026, section 27

> Text as at 2026-05-27.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-1b2a124f173973f7f7da6f0593356745]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00068] are incorporated in this Order subject to the modification that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”;
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”; and
- (d) for “compulsory purchase order” substitute “this order”.
