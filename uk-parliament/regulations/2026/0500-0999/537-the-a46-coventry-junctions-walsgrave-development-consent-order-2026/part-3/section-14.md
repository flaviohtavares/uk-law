# The A46 Coventry Junctions (Walsgrave) Development Consent Order 2026, section 14

> Text as at 2026-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14. Access to works[^c-4f95add0a6384c37e34d33c412e8cdfb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker may, for the purposes of the authorised development, form and layout such means of access, or improve existing such means of access, at such locations within the Order limits as the undertaker reasonably requires for the purposes of the authorised development.
