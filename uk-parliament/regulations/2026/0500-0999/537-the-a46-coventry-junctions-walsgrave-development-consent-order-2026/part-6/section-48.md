# The A46 Coventry Junctions (Walsgrave) Development Consent Order 2026, section 48

> Text as at 2026-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 48. Defence to proceedings in respect of statutory nuisance[^key-3b5a802d3afffaaa50168ce7a1c46a0d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where proceedings are brought under section 82(1)[^f00092] (summary proceedings by person aggrieved by statutory nuisance) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 in relation to a nuisance falling within paragraph (d), (g) and (ga) of section 79(1)[^f00093] (statutory nuisances and inspection therefor) of that Act no order is to be made, and no fine may be imposed, under section 82(2)[^f00094] of that Act if—

- (a) the defendant shows that the nuisance—
  - (i) relates to premises used by the undertaker for the purposes of or in connection with the construction or maintenance of the authorised development and that the nuisance is attributable to the carrying out of the authorised development in accordance with a notice served under section 60 (control of noise on construction site), or a consent given under section 61 (prior consent for work on construction site) of the Control of Pollution Act 1974[^f00095]; or
  - (ii) is a consequence of the construction or maintenance of the authorised development and that it cannot reasonably be avoided; or
- (b) the defendant shows that the nuisance is a consequence of the use or operation of the authorised development and that it cannot reasonably be avoided.

(2) Section 61(9) (consent for work on construction site to include statement that it does not of itself constitute a defence to proceedings under section 82 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990) of the Control of Pollution Act 1974 does not apply where the consent relates to the use of premises by the undertaker for the purposes of or in connection with the construction or maintenance of the authorised development.
