# The A46 Coventry Junctions (Walsgrave) Development Consent Order 2026, section 47

> Text as at 2026-05-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. Operational land for purposes of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^key-4dfeef4737977eaf817359782e991467]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Development consent granted by this Order is to be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3) (cases in which land is to be treated as not[^key-3b6a21bce60d87c116d3627d60535c0d] operational land for the purposes of that Act) of the 1990 Act.
