# The Automated Vehicles Act 2024 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 731 (C. 63)

ROADS

The Automated Vehicles Act 2024 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2026

Made

1st July 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 99(1) and (2) of the Automated Vehicles Act 2024[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Automated Vehicles Act 2024 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2026.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 7th January 2027

Part 4 of the Automated Vehicles Act 2024 (marketing restrictions), so far as not already in force, comes into force on 7th January 2027.

Simon Lightwood

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are the third commencement regulations made under the Automated Vehicles Act [2024 (c. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10) (“**the Act**”).

They bring into force Part 4 of the Act, so far as not already in force, on 7th January 2027. Part 4 of the Act makes provision for marketing restrictions in relation to automated vehicles. Section 78 of the Act came into force on 1st January 2026, but only for the purpose of making regulations. These Regulations also commence Schedule 5 to the Act, which makes provision for the enforcement of marketing restrictions.
