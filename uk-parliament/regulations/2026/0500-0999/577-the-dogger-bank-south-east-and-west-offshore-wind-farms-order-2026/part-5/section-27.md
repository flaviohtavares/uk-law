# The Dogger Bank South East and West Offshore Wind Farms Order 2026, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Compulsory acquisition of land: minerals

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00041] are incorporated in this Order, subject to the following modifications—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated;
- (b) for “acquiring authority” substitute “undertaker”;
- (c) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised project”; and
- (d) for “compulsory purchase order” substitute “this Order”.
