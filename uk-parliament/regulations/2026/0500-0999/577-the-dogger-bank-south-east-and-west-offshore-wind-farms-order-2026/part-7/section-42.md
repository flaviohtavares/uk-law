# The Dogger Bank South East and West Offshore Wind Farms Order 2026, section 42

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 42. Certification of plans and documents, etc.

(1) The undertaker must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order , submit to the Secretary of State copies of the documents listed in Schedule 19 (documents to be certified) for certification that they are true copies of the documents referred to in this Order.

(2) A plan or document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
