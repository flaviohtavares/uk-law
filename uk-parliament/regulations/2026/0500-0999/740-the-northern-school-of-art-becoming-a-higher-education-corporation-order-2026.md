# The Northern School of Art (Becoming a Higher Education Corporation) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 740

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Northern School of Art (Becoming a Higher Education Corporation) Order 2026

Made

2nd July 2026

Laid before Parliament

6th July 2026

Coming into force

1st August 2026

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 122ZA of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Northern School of Art (Becoming a Higher Education Corporation) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on 1st August 2026.

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales.

(4) This Order applies to England only.

## 2. Transfer to the higher education sector

On 1st August 2026, the Northern School of Art[^f00002] ceases to be a further education corporation and becomes a higher education corporation.

Smith of Malvern

Minister of State

Department for Education

2nd July 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision for the Northern School of Art further education corporation to become a higher education corporation. On 1st August 2026 that corporation becomes a higher education corporation.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument because no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
