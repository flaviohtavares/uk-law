# The Television Selection Services (Designation) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 531

BROADCASTING

ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS

The Television Selection Services (Designation) Regulations 2026

Made

15th May 2026

Laid before Parliament

19th May 2026

Coming into force

1st July 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 362AF(1)(a) and 402(3) of the Communications Act 2003[^f00001].

In accordance with section 362AF(2) of that Act, the Secretary of State considers that the television selection services[^f00002] designated by these Regulations are used by a significant number of members of the public in the United Kingdom.

In accordance with section 362AF(4) of that Act, the Secretary of State has received a report from OFCOM under section 362AG of that Act[^f00003] relating to the television selection services designated by these Regulations.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Television Selection Services (Designation) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st July 2026.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In these Regulations, a reference to a body corporate includes any body corporate which is a member of the same group of companies as that body corporate.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), two bodies corporate are members of the same group of companies if, and only if—

- (a) one of them is controlled by the other, or
- (b) both of them are controlled by the same person.

(3) In paragraph (2), “**controlled**” has the same meaning as in Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Broadcasting Act 1990[^f00004].

## 3. Designated television selection services

(1) The television selection services known by the name in the first column of the table in the Schedule provided by the person in the second column of that table are regulated television selection services but only so far as the service is provided in connection with internet television equipment[^f00005] first made available to members of the public in the United Kingdom on or after the date in the third column of that table.

(2) A designation of a television selection service as a regulated television selection service under paragraph (1) is not affected by a change to the name by which the service is known.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations designate a number of television selection services as regulated television selection services for the purposes of Part 3A of the Communications Act [2023 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/21) (prominence on television selection services).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary sector or community bodies is foreseen. Instead a de minimis assessment has been prepared as this instrument is likely to entail some costs for businesses, but the net impact is estimated to be below £10 million per year. An Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside the instrument on the UK legislation website, www.legislation.gov.uk.
