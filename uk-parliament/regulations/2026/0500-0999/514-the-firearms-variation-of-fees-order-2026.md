# The Firearms (Variation of Fees) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 514

ARMS AND AMMUNITION

The Firearms (Variation of Fees) Order 2026

Made

12th May 2026

Laid before Parliament

14th May 2026

Coming into force

4th June 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 43 of the Firearms Act 1968[^f00001] and sections 11(4) and 17(9) of the Firearms (Amendment) Act 1988[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Firearms (Variation of Fees) Order 2026 and comes into force on 4th June 2026.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales and Scotland.

## 2. Variation of fees for firearm and shot gun certificates

In section 32(1) of the Firearms Act 1968[^f00003] (fees for certificate and exemption from paying it in certain cases)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “£198” substitute “£204”;
- (b) in paragraph (b), for “£131” substitute “£135”;
- (c) in paragraph (c), for “£47” substitute “£48”;
- (d) in paragraph (d), for “£194” substitute “£200”;
- (e) in paragraph (e), for “£126” substitute “£130”.

## 3. Variation of fees for registration as a firearms dealer

In section 35 of the Firearms Act 1968[^f00004] (fee for registration and renewal thereof)—

- (a) in subsection (1), for “£466” substitute “£480”;
- (b) in subsection (1A), for “£30” substitute “£31”;
- (c) in subsection (3), for “£466” substitute “£480”.

## 4. Variation of fees for co-terminous certificates

In section 11(3) of the Firearms (Amendment) Act 1988[^f00005] (grant of co-terminous certificates)—

- (a) in paragraph (b), for “£71” substitute “£73”;
- (b) in paragraph (d), for “£24” substitute “£25”.

## 5. Variation of fees for visitors’ permits

In section 17(8) of the Firearms (Amendment) Act 1988[^f00006] (visitors’ permits)—

- (a) for “£47” substitute “£48”;
- (b) for “£233” substitute “£240”.

Sarah Jones

Minister of State

Home Office

12th May 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to England and Wales and Scotland, varies certain fees payable under the Firearms Act 1968 and the Firearms (Amendment) Act 1988. The new fee levels are set out below.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant impact on the private sector, voluntary sector or community bodies is foreseen. An economic note is annexed to the Explanatory Memorandum and is available alongside this instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk. Copies may be obtained from the Firearms and Weapons Policy Unit of the Crime Directorate of the Public Safety Group of the Home Office at 2 Marsham Street, London SW1P 4DF.
