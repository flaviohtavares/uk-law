# The Football Governance Act 2025 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 705 (C. 60)

SPORTS GROUNDS AND SPORTING EVENTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Football Governance Act 2025 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2026

Made

29th June 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [section 100](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/21/section/100) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/21/section/100) of the [Football Governance Act 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/21/contents)[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Football Governance Act 2025 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2026.

(2) In these Regulations “**the Act**” means the Football Governance Act 2025.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1st July 2026

(1) The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act is 1st July 2026—

- (a) in Part 5 (duties on clubs and competition organisers etc)—
  - (i) section 46 (duty not to dispose etc of home ground without approval) so far as not already in force;
  - (ii) section 48 (duty not to relocate without approval);
  - (iii) section 49 (duty not to change crest, home shirt colours or name without approval);
  - (iv) section 50 (duty to notify of changes in circumstances relevant to the IFR’s functions);
- (b) in Part 7 (investigatory powers etc)—
  - (i) section 67 (meaning of “**relevant infringement**”) so far as it relates to the provisions being brought into force by paragraph (ii);
  - (ii) the provisions of Schedule 7 (meaning of “**relevant infringement**”) specified in paragraph (2);
  - (iii) section 70 (commitments in lieu of investigations) so far as it relates to paragraph 2(2)(e) of Schedule 7;
- (c) paragraph 4(2) of Schedule 4 (threshold requirements) so far as it relates to section 48(4)(c).

(2) The provisions in Schedule 7 specified for the purposes of paragraph (1)(b)(ii) are—

- (a) paragraph 1 so far as it relates to the provisions being brought into force by sub-paragraphs (b) and (c);
- (b) paragraph 2(2)(e) so far as it relates to the provisions in Part 5 being brought into force by paragraph 2(1)(a);
- (c) paragraph 7(b) so far as it relates to the section 46.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force specified provisions of the Football Governance Act [2025 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/21) (“**the Act**”) on 1st July 2026. They are the fourth set of commencement regulations made under the Act.

Regulation 2(1)(a) brings into force a number of provisions in Part 5 of the Act (duties on clubs and competition organisers etc) which will prevent regulated clubs from making changes to key elements of their heritage without taking steps to obtain the views of their fans.

Regulations 2(1)(b), (c) and 2 bring into force provisions of Part 7 and Schedule 7 so far as they relate to the duties brought into force by regulation 2(1)(a).

Regulation 2(1)(c) will commence the relevant part of Schedule 7 in order for section 48(4)(c) of the Act, which is commenced by regulation (2)(1)(a)(ii), to have legal effect.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as a full impact assessment was published in relation to the provisions in the Football Governance Bill. That impact assessment can be obtained online at https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/football-governance-bill-2024-supporting-documents or by writing to the Department for Culture Media and Sport, 100 Parliament Street, SW1A 2BQ, London, United Kingdom.
