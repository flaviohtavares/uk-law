# The A47 Wansford to Sutton Development Consent (Revocation) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 579

INFRASTRUCTURE PLANNING

The A47 Wansford to Sutton Development Consent (Revocation) Order 2026

Made

2nd June 2026

Coming into force

3rd June 2026

The Secretary of State, in accordance with the Infrastructure Planning (Changes to, and Revocation of, Development Consent Orders) Regulations 2011[^f00001], gave notice of the intention to consider making an Order to revoke the A47 Wansford to Sutton Development Consent Order 2023[^f00002].

The Secretary of State has considered the responses to the publicity carried out in accordance with regulations 55 and 56 of the Infrastructure Planning (Changes to, and Revocation of, Development Consent Orders) Regulations 2011. The Secretary of State is satisfied that there are exceptional circumstances that make it appropriate to exercise the power to make the Order revoking the A47 Wansford to Sutton Development Consent Order 2023 without an application to do so being made.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 3(1) and (7)(b) of Schedule 6 to the Planning Act 2008[^f00003], makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the A47 Wansford to Sutton Development Consent (Revocation) Order 2026 and comes into force on 3rd June 2026.

## 2. Revocation of the A47 Wansford to Sutton Development Consent Order 2023

The A47 Wansford to Sutton Development Consent Order 2023 is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Natasha Kopala

Head of the Transport and Works Act Orders Unit

2nd June 2026

Department for Transport

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the A47 Wansford to Sutton Development Consent Order 2023 (“the DCO”), a development consent order made under the Planning Act 2008. The Secretary of State has made this Order having given notice of her intention to revoke the DCO without an application being made to her pursuant to paragraph 3(1) and (7)(b) of Schedule 6 to the Act.
