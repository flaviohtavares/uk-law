## 13. Amendment to regulation 17

In regulation 17 (unusual occurrences)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), in sub-paragraph (a) omit “an increase in or”;
- (b) in paragraph (2), for “or increase” substitute “of qualifying nuclear material”.
