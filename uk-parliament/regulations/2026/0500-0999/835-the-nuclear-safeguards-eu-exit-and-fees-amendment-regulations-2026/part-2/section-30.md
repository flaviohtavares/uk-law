# The Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit and Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Amendment to regulation 49

In regulation 49 (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “equipment”, for “plants”, in each place it occurs, substitute “facilities”;
- (b) in the definition of “item”, in paragraph (e), omit “, plants”.
