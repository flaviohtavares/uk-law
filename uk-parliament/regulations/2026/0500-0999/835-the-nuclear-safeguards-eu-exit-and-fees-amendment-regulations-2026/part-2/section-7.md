## 7. Amendment to regulation 6

In regulation 6 (accountancy and control of qualifying nuclear material)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), after “must” insert “establish and”;
- (b) in paragraph (3), for “basic technical characteristics” substitute “design”;
- (c) in paragraph (4) for “five” substitute “30”.
