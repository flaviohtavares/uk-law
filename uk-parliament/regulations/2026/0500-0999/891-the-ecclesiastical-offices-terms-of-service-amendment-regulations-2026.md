The Archbishops’ Council makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2009/1/section/2) of the [Ecclesiastical Offices (Terms of Service) Measure 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2009/1/contents)[^f00001] and in accordance with [paragraph 12A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/1998/1/schedule/1/paragraph/12A) of [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/1998/1/schedule/1) to the [National Institutions Measure 1998](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/1998/1/contents)[^f00002] and [section 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2018/7/section/11) [(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2018/7/section/11/subsection/6) and [(7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2018/7/section/11/subsection/7) of the [Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2018/7/contents)[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Ecclesiastical Offices (Terms of Service) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st October 2026.

## 2. No statement of particulars of office etc: increase in time limit for reference to tribunal

In [regulation 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2108/regulation/9) of the [Ecclesiastical Offices (Terms of Service) Regulations 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2108/contents)[^f00004] (no statement of particulars of office etc: increase in time limit for reference to employment tribunal), in [paragraph (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2108/regulation/9), in each of sub-paragraphs (a) and (b), for “three months” substitute “six months”.

The draft of these Regulations was approved by the General Synod on 10th July 2026.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 9 of the Ecclesiastical Offices (Terms of Service) Regulations 2009.

That regulation imposes a time limit for making a reference to the Employment Tribunal where an ecclesiastical office holder has not been provided with a written statement of particulars of office, a statement of changes or an itemised statement of stipend. Where the appointment of the office holder has come to an end, the reference must be made within the three months following the end of the appointment.

These Regulations amend regulation 9(4) so as to replace that three-month period with a six-month period. That ensures consistency with changes made by the Employment Rights Act 2025 (see, in particular, the amendments made by Schedule 12 to that Act).
