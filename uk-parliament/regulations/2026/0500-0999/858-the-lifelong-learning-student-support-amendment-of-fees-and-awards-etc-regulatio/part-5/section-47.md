## 47. Students with full entitlement aged 60 or over[^c-b29c28455b9f3fffb4aa212969da59f6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies to an eligible student described in regulation 43(1)(d).

(2) The base entitlement is equal to—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/858)

where—

- (a) T is the term week rate in respect of a course year of a designated course, namely £152.73, and
- (b) the resulting figure, if it is not a whole number, is rounded up to the nearest £1.
