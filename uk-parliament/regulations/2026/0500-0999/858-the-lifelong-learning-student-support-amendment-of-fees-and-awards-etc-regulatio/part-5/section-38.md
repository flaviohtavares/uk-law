# The Lifelong Learning (Student Support) (Amendment of Fees and Awards etc.) Regulations 2026, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Meaning of “additional weeks entitlement”, “base entitlement”, “loan for living costs entitlement” and “maximum loan entitlement”

In this Part—

- (a) “**additional weeks entitlement**” has the meaning given in regulation 48(2);
- (b) “**base entitlement**” has the meaning given in regulation 43(1);
- (c) “loan for living costs entitlement” is the total amount of loan for living costs for which an eligible student qualifies in respect of a course year of a designated course, calculated in accordance with regulation 54(1);
- (d) “**maximum loan entitlement**” has the meaning given in regulation 49.
