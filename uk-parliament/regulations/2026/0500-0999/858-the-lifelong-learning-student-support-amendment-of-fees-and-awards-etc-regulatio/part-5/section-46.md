# The Lifelong Learning (Student Support) (Amendment of Fees and Awards etc.) Regulations 2026, section 46

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Students with full entitlement under the age of 60 who meet certain conditions[^c-3c3293acb917951a725b2fd29effd110]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) This regulation applies to an eligible student described in regulation 43(1)(c).

(2) The base entitlement is equal to—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/858)

where—

- (a) T is the term week rate for which the student qualifies in respect of a course year of a designated course, namely—
  - (i) for a student in category A in the final year of the course, £341.83;
  - (ii) for a student in category A in a course year other than the final year, £358.56;
  - (iii) for a student in category B in the final year of the course, £481.56;
  - (iv) for a student in category B in a course year other than the final year, £513.83;
  - (v) for a student in category C in the final year of the course, £419.89;
  - (vi) for a student in category C in a course year other than the final year, £460.19;
  - (vii) for a student in category D in the final year of the course, £393.33;
  - (viii) for a student in category D in a course year other than the final year, £411.49, and
- (b) the resulting figure, if it is not a whole number, is rounded up to the nearest £1.

(3) The first £152.73 of the term week rate is a special support loan and the remainder is the loan for maintenance.
