# The Lifelong Learning (Student Support) (Amendment of Fees and Awards etc.) Regulations 2026, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Recognition of initial further education teacher training courses

(1) The list of courses which are, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, initial further education teacher training courses is the list as published from time to time by the Secretary of State under regulation 2B of the 2011 Student Support Regulations.

(2) For the purposes of this regulation, a course may only be considered an initial further education teacher training course by the Secretary of State if successful completion of the course means that a person meets the occupational standard for a learning and skills teacher as determined by the Secretary of State from time to time.
