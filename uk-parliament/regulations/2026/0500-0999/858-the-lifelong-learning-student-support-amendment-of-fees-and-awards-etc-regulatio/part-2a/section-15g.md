# The Lifelong Learning (Student Support) (Amendment of Fees and Awards etc.) Regulations 2026, section 15G

> Text as at 2026-11-19.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15G. Appeals against correction years[^c-e14f03a2477727a4f3b7f6a13df46877]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A franchised provider may appeal the Secretary of State’s decision to apply a correction year, where, on the course start date of any designated course during that implementation year, the franchised provider reasonably believed that it would have a student population below the threshold for the duration of the implementation year.

(2) An appeal under this regulation must be made to the Secretary of State in writing before the end of the period of one month beginning with the day on which the notice of a correction year is issued.
