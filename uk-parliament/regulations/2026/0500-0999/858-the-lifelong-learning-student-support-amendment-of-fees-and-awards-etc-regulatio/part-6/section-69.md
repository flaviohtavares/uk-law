# The Lifelong Learning (Student Support) (Amendment of Fees and Awards etc.) Regulations 2026, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Overseas study - qualifying conditions for the grant for travel

(1) A grant for travel is available to an eligible student in respect of the reasonable expenditure that the Secretary of State determines the eligible student is obliged to incur for the purpose described in paragraph (2).

(2) The purpose is attending, as part of the student’s course—

- (a) an overseas institution whether or not on a period of Turing mobility,
- (b) the Institute, or
- (c) an overseas workplace whether or not on a period of Turing mobility.

(3) The expenditure described in paragraph (1) includes expenditure incurred within and outside the United Kingdom.
