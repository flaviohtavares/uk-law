# The Lifelong Learning (Student Support) (Amendment of Fees and Awards etc.) Regulations 2026, section 72

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 72. Elements of the grants for dependants

The grants for dependants consist of the following elements—

- (a) adult dependants’ grant;
- (b) childcare grant;
- (c) parents’ learning allowance.
