# The Lifelong Learning (Student Support) (Amendment of Fees and Awards etc.) Regulations 2026, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 80. Calculation of parents’ learning allowance

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the amount of parents’ learning allowance is equal to—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/858)

where—

- (a) A is the basic amount referred to in regulation 77(3),
- (b) B, where the dependants’ income for the prior financial year is more than £14,910, is the amount by which the household income exceeds £14,910 divided by 2, and
- (c) B, where the dependants’ income for the prior financial year is £14,910 or less, is nil.

(2) Where the amount of parents’ learning allowance calculated under paragraph (1) is £0.01 or more but less than £50, the amount of parents’ learning allowance payable is £50.

(3) No parents’ learning allowance is payable where the dependants’ income for the prior financial year exceeds £18,957.98.
