## 74. Adjustment for concurrent study[^c-ffc4d942fa4b126b316fc72107b479e2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies where an eligible student is studying two courses where—

- (a) each of those courses has a credit value of at least 120, and
- (b) the periods of study of those courses overlap.

(2) In this regulation—

- (a) “**day**” includes Saturday and Sunday;
- (b) “**course A**” means whichever of the courses begins first;
- (c) “**course B**” means whichever of the courses begins second;
- (d) “**period of overlap**” means the period during which the periods of study of course A and course B overlap.

(3) The eligible student is not entitled to any element of the grants for dependants in respect of any day of course B that falls during the period of overlap.
