## 59. Students who are treated as in attendance on a course[^c-f325fd1b7a0d527a8e5aca59b8e59927]

*Extent: E+W*

A student is treated as being in attendance on a designated course for the purpose of qualifying for a supplementary grant if they are—

- (a) on a period of Turing mobility, or
- (b) a disabled student who is undertaking a designated course in the United Kingdom but is not in attendance because they are unable to attend for a reason which relates to their disability.
