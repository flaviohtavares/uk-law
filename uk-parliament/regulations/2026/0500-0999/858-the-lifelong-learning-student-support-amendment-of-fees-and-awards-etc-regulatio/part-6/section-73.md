# The Lifelong Learning (Student Support) (Amendment of Fees and Awards etc.) Regulations 2026, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Amount of the grants for dependants - general

(1) Subject to regulation 60, grants for dependants are payable in respect of a course year.

(2) An element of the grants for dependants is not payable if the eligible student does not provide requested details of income or other information needed to calculate the amount of that element to which they would otherwise be entitled.
