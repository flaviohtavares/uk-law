# The Lifelong Learning (Student Support) (Amendment of Fees and Awards etc.) Regulations 2026, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Types of supplementary grant available

The following supplementary grants are available to an eligible student in connection with a designated course if the student meets the relevant qualifying conditions in this Part—

- (a) disabled students’ allowance;
- (b) grants for travel;
- (c) grants for dependants.
