## 57. Types of supplementary grant available[^c-ccc26bc112185a7dba7e7e29fe76d252]

*Extent: E+W*

The following supplementary grants are available to an eligible student in connection with a designated course if the student meets the relevant qualifying conditions in this Part—

- (a) disabled students’ allowance;
- (b) grants for travel;
- (c) grants for dependants.
