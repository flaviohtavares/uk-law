# The Lifelong Learning (Student Support) (Amendment of Fees and Awards etc.) Regulations 2026, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33. Availability of fee loans

When assessing an application for support in respect of a course year of a designated course, the Secretary of State must allocate a fee loan to that course year even if a student has insufficient fee loan entitlement balance to pay for the full course of which the course year forms a part.
