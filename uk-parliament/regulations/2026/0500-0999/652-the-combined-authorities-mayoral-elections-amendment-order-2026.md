# The Combined Authorities (Mayoral Elections) (Amendment) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 652

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The Combined Authorities (Mayoral Elections) (Amendment) Order 2026

Made

17th June 2026

Coming into force

18th June 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by [sections 114](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/section/114) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/section/114) and [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/section/114) and [117](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/section/117) [(1A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/section/117) of, and [paragraph 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/schedule/5B/paragraph/12) of [Schedule 5B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/schedule/5B) to, the [Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/contents) (“**the 2009 Act**”)[^f00001] and [section 252](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/section/252) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/section/252) and [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/section/252) of, and [paragraph 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/schedule/2/paragraph/12) of [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/schedule/2) to, the [Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/contents) (“**the 2023 Act**”)[^f00002].

The Secretary of State has consulted the Electoral Commission about this Order in accordance with paragraph 12(4) of Schedule 5B to the 2009 Act and paragraph 12(4) of Schedule 2 to the 2023 Act.

In accordance with section 117(2) of the 2009 Act[^f00003] and section 252(4) of the 2023 Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation, extent and commencement

(1) This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Combined Authorities (Mayoral Elections) (Amendment) Order 2026;
- (b) extends to England and Wales; and
- (c) comes into force the day after the day on which it is made.

(2) This Order does not apply for the purposes of—

- (a) an election for the return of a combined authority mayor in accordance with [Schedule 5B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/schedule/5B) to the [Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/contents), or
- (b) an election for the return of a combined county authority mayor in accordance with [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/schedule/2) to the [Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/contents),

in respect of which the notice of election is published before the date on which this Order comes into force.

## 2. Amendment of the [Combined Authorities (Mayoral Elections) Order 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/67/contents)

The [Combined Authorities (Mayoral Elections) Order 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/67/contents)[^f00004] is amended in accordance with this Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Combined Authorities (Mayoral Elections) Order 2017 ([S.I. 2017/67](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/67), “the 2017 Order”) makes provision for the conduct of elections of combined authority mayors and combined county authority mayors.

Section 63 of, and Schedule 30 to the English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act [2026 (c. 26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/26) changes the voting system at elections for combined authority mayors and combined county authority mayors in England from the simple majority vote system, also known as “first past the post”, to the supplementary vote system. As a consequence of the amendments made by that section to the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act [2009 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20) and the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act [2023 (c. 55)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55) this Order amends the 2017 Order Rules to replace provisions relating to the simple majority vote system for the election of combined authority mayors and combined county authority mayors with provisions which will provide for the supplementary vote system for those elections, largely reversing changes made by the Combined Authorities (Mayoral Elections) (Amendment) Order 2022 ([S.I. 2022/1353](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/1353)).

An impact assessment has been produced for the English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026 including an Addendum on the “supplementary vote” provision. No, or no significant, impact on business, charities or the public or voluntary sector is foreseen. Copies can be obtained at https://bills.parliament.uk/bills/4002/publications or from the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government, 2 Marsham Street, London SW1P 4DF.
