# The Power to Award Degrees etc. (New Model Institute for Technology and Engineering) (Amendment) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 628

Education, England

The Power to Award Degrees etc. (New Model Institute for Technology and Engineering) (Amendment) Order 2026

Made

11th June 2026

Coming into force

1st July 2026

The Office for Students, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 42(1) and 44(1) of the Higher Education and Research Act 2017[^f00001], makes the following Order.

The Office for Students has requested advice from the relevant body regarding the quality of, and standards applied to, higher education provided by the provider and has had regard to that advice in accordance with section 46(1)(b) and 46(7) of that Act.

The Office for Students has complied with the procedure on giving notifications in accordance with section 48 of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Power to Award Degrees etc. (New Model Institute for Technology and Engineering) (Amendment) Order 2026 and shall come into force on 1st July 2026.

## 2. Variation of the Power to Award Degrees etc. (New Model Institute for Technology and Engineering) Order 2023

(1) The Power to Award Degrees etc. (New Model Institute for Technology and Engineering) Order 2023[^f00002] is varied in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) For article 3, substitute—

> **3.** The authorisation in article 2 is for a fixed term beginning on 1st September 2023 and expiring at the end of the day on 30th November 2027.

.

Jean Arnold

Interim Director of Quality and Access

Office for Students

11th June 2026

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the Power to Award Degrees etc. (New Model Institute for Technology and Engineering) Order 2023 to extend New Model Institute for Technology and Engineering’s (UKPRN: 10067406) authorisation to award degrees. The expiry of New Model Institute for Technology and Engineering’s authorisation is changed from 30th November 2026 to 30th November 2027.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations. The instrument has no impact on the public sector.
