## Part 1 — Introduction

### 1. Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Educational Provision for Improving Behaviour) (Application to Academies and Pupil Referral Units and Minor Amendments) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 26th July 2026.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

(4) In these Regulations—

- “**the 2002 Act**” means the [Education Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents);
- “**the 2010 Regulations**” means the [Education (Educational Provision for Improving Behaviour) Regulations 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/contents)[^f00006];
- “**the 2013 Regulations**” means the [Education (Information about Individual Pupils) (England) Regulations 2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2094/contents)[^f00007];
- “**Academy**” means an Academy school[^f00008] or an alternative provision Academy[^f00009];
- “**relevant requirement**” means a requirement imposed by the proprietor of an Academy on a registered pupil to attend any place outside the premises of the Academy for the purpose of receiving educational provision which is intended to improve the behaviour of the pupil.

### 2. Amendments to [regulation 4A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/4A) of [the 2010 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/contents)

In [regulation 4A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/4A/made)[^f00010] of [the 2010 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/contents) (persons who may request a review meeting), in [paragraph (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/4A) [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/4A)—

- (a) for “a statement of special educational needs” substitute “an EHC plan”[^f00011];
- (b) for “statement”, in the second place it occurs, substitute “plan”.

## Part 2 — Educational provision for improving behaviour in Academies

### 3. Application and modification of [section 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) of [the 2002 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) in relation to Academies

[Section 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) of [the 2002 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) (power of governing body in England: educational provision for improving behaviour) applies in relation to Academies as if—

- (a) any reference to a governing body of a maintained school were a reference to a proprietor of an Academy;
- (b) any reference to a maintained school were a reference to an Academy;
- (c) subsection (2) were omitted.

### 4. Application and modification of [the 2010 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/contents) in relation to Academies

(1) [The 2010 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/contents) apply in relation to Academies as if—

- (a) any reference to a requirement imposed under [section 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) of [the 2002 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) included a reference to a relevant requirement imposed before 26th July 2026;
- (b) any reference to a maintained school were a reference to an Academy;
- (c) any reference to a governing body were a reference to a proprietor;
- (d) any reference to a head teacher of a school were a reference to a principal of an Academy;
- (e) in [regulation 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/5/made)[^f00012] (conduct of review meetings), in [paragraph (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/5/made)—
  - (i) in the words before [sub-paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/5/made), for “member of the governing body” there were substituted “person nominated by the proprietor”;
  - (ii) [sub-paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/5/made) were omitted.

(2) In this regulation “**principal**” means the head teacher of an Academy and includes an acting principal.

### 5. Relevant requirements imposed by the proprietor of an Academy before 26th July 2026

Where a pupil remains subject to a relevant requirement that was imposed by the proprietor of an Academy before 26th July 2026, [the 2010 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/contents) apply to that relevant requirement as if—

- (a) in [regulation 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/3/made) (requirements and other matters relating the imposition of a requirement upon a pupil under [section 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) of [the 2002 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents))—
  - (i) in [paragraph (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/3/made), for “determine that they will impose” there were substituted “has imposed”;
  - (ii) for [paragraph (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/3/made) there were substituted—

    > (2) The notice must be given as soon as practicable after 26th July 2026 and not less than six days before the date of the first scheduled review meeting under [regulation 4(1)(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/4/made).

    ;
  - (iii) [paragraph (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/3/made) [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/3/made) were omitted;
- (b) in [regulation 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/4/made) (requirement to keep under review the imposition of a requirement upon a pupil under [section 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) of [the 2002 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents)), for [paragraph (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/4/made) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/4/made) there were substituted—

  > - (a) holding the first review meeting as soon as reasonably practicable after 1st August 2026, and then further review meetings at such intervals as it, having regard to the needs of the pupil, considers appropriate; and

  ;
- (c) in [regulation 4A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/4A/made), in [paragraph (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/regulation/4A/made), after “practicable”, there were inserted “after 1st August 2026”.

## Part 3 — Educational provision for improving behaviour in Pupil Referral Units

### 6. Application and modification of [section 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) of [the 2002 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) in relation to pupil referral units in England

- (a) any reference to a governing body of a maintained school were a reference to a management committee of a pupil referral unit;
- (b) any reference to a maintained school were a reference to a pupil referral unit;

[The 2010 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/contents) apply in relation to pupil referral units in England as if—

- (a) any reference to a maintained school were a reference to a pupil referral unit;
- (b) any reference to a governing body were a reference to a management committee;
- (c) any reference to a head teacher were a reference to a teacher in charge.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations modify [section 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) of the [Education Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) (c. 32) (“**the 2002 Act**”) and the [Education (Educational Provision for Improving Behaviour) Regulations 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/contents) ([S.I. 2010/1156](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156)) (“**the 2010 Regulations**”), so that they apply to Academy schools and alternative provision Academies (“Academies”), and to pupil referral units (in England) in the same way that they apply to maintained schools in England, with specific modifications. These are the first regulations that exercise the power contained within [section 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) of [the 2002 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents).

Regulation 2 replaces a reference to a statement of special educational needs with a reference to an Education, Health and Care plan (“EHC plan”), correcting an error contained in the [Special Educational Needs (Consequential Amendments to Subordinate Legislation) Order 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/2103/contents) ([S.I. 2014/2103](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/2103)) (“**the 2014 Order**”). These Regulations are therefore being issued free of charge to known recipients of [the 2014 Order](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/2103/contents).

Regulation 5 makes specific provision for circumstances where, before these Regulations come into force, a proprietor of an Academy has imposed a requirement on a pupil to attend a place away from the premises of the Academy for the purpose of receiving educational provision to improve the pupils behaviour (“a requirement”), and the pupil remains subject to that requirement immediately after these Regulations come into force. In those circumstances, proprietors who had imposed such a requirement will be obliged to carry out an initial review as soon as reasonably practicable after 1st August 2026, and conduct subsequent reviews at appropriate intervals. They will also be required to provide prescribed notices at least six days prior to any initial review meeting.

Regulations 8 and 9 make amendments to the [Education (Information about Individual Pupils) (England) Regulations 2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2094/contents) ([S.I. 2013/2094](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2094)) (“**the 2013 Regulations**”) and the [Health and Social Care Act 2012 (Continuity of Information: Interpretation) Regulations 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1470/contents) ([S.I. 2015/1470](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1470)) (“**the 2015 Regulations**”) respectively, to reflect the application of [section 29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/29A) of [the 2002 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents) and [the 2010 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1156/contents) to Academies and pupil referral units. Regulation 9 further corrects an error in [paragraph (bb)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1470/schedule/paragraph/bb) of [the Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1470/schedule) to [the 2015 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1470/contents). Consequently these Regulations are being issued free of charge to known recipients of [the 2015 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1470/contents).

Regulation 10 makes saving provision relating to Academies, where a proprietor has imposed a requirement on a pupil that starts before these Regulations come into force, and continues to be in effect after these Regulations come into force. In such circumstances, [paragraph 18A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2094/schedule/1/paragraph/18A) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2094/schedule/1/paragraph/18A) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2094/schedule/1/paragraph/18A) of [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2094/schedule/1) to [the 2013 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2094/contents), as it stood before the amendments made by these Regulations, continues to apply to that Academy in respect of the pupil for the duration of the requirement.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
