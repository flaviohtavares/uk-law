# The Health and Social Care Act 2012 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 852 (C. 73)

HEALTH CARE AND ASSOCIATED PROFESSIONS

The Health and Social Care Act 2012 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2026

Made

15th July 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 306 of the Health and Social Care Act 2012[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Care Act 2012 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2026.

## 2. Commencement of provision

The day appointed for the coming into force[^f00002] of section 208 of the Health and Social Care Act 2012, in so far as it is not already in force, is the 1st October 2026.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care

Stephen Kinnock

Minister of State

Department of Health and Social Care

15th July 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 208 of the Health and Social Care Act [2012 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/7) (“**the Act**”) in so far as it is not already in force. It is the twelfth such Order to be made under the Act.

Section 208 of the Act amends the National Health Service Act 2006 (“**the 2006 Act**”), so as to make provision for the NHS Commissioning Board, now NHS England, to establish lists of Local Pharmaceutical Service performers and those who assist pharmaceutical contractors in the provision of pharmaceutical services.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as this Order itself has no impact on the private sector or civil society organisations. A full impact assessment has been produced in relation to the provisions of the Act, including provisions that are commenced by this Order, and a copy is available at https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/health-and-social-care-bill-2011-combined-impact-assessments.
