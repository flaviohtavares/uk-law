The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 17(2), (3) and (3ZB) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001].

The Secretary of State has consulted representative organisations in accordance with section 134(2) of that Act[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the M27 Motorway (Junctions 4 to 11) (Variable Speed Limits) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

(2) They come into force on 30th June 2026.

(3) They extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment to the M27 Motorway (Junctions 4 to 11) (Variable Speed Limits) Regulations 2021

In the Schedule, in paragraph (4)(e)—

- (a) for the “off-slip road connecting from the westbound carriageway to the A32 and” substitute—

  > - (i) the off-slip road connecting from the westbound carriageway to the A3222;
  > - (ii) the on-slip road connecting from the A3222 to the westbound carriageway;
  > - (iii) the off-slip road connecting from the eastbound carriageway to the A3222; and
- (b) The words “the on-slip road connecting from the A32 to the eastbound carriageway” become sub-paragraph (iv).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend paragraph 4(e) of the Schedule to the M27 Motorway (Junctions 4 to 11) (Variable Speed Limits) Regulations 2021 to introduce variable speed limits on three new slip roads at junction 10 of the M27 motorway.

The new slip roads are—

- (a) the off-slip road connecting from the westbound carriageway to the A3222;
- (b) the on-slip road connecting from the A3222 to the westbound carriageway; and
- (c) the off-slip road connecting from the eastbound carriageway to the A3222.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant impact on private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen. A de minimis assessment has been produced and is published alongside the Regulations at www.legislation.gov.uk. An Explanatory Memorandum is also published alongside the Regulations.
