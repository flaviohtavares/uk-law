# The Care Quality Commission (Additional Functions) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND

Made

7th September 2026

Laid before Parliament

8th September 2026

Coming into force

4th November 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 59(1) and 161(3) of the Health and Social Care Act 2008[^f00001].

In accordance with section 59(2) of that Act[^f00002], the Secretary of State has consulted NHS England before making these Regulations.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Care Quality Commission (Additional Functions) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 4th November 2026.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment of the Care Quality Commission (Additional Functions) Regulations 2011

Omit regulation 4A (provision of training, guidance and support to bodies and individuals) of the Care Quality Commission (Additional Functions) Regulations 2011[^f00003].

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care

Diana Johnson

Minister of State

Department of Health and Social Care

7th September 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Care Quality Commission (Additional Functions) Regulations 2011 (the 2011 Regulations). Regulation 2 omits regulation 4A of the 2011 Regulations, removing the Care Quality Commission’s function of providing training, guidance and support to certain bodies and individuals which was inserted by The Care Quality Commission (Additional Functions) Regulations 2023 [S.I. 2023/1163](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/1163).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or community bodies is foreseen.
