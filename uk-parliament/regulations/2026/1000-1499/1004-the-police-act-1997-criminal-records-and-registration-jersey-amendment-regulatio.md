# The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1004

POLICE

The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made

7th September 2026

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(1)

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 112(1)(b), 113A(1)(b), 113B(1)(b) and 125 of the Police Act 1997[^f00001] as extended to Jersey by the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Jersey) Order 2010[^f00002] and sections 116A(4)(b) and 116A(5)(b) of that Act as extended to Jersey by the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Jersey) (Amendment) Order 2012[^f00003].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) (Amendment) Regulations 2026 and come into force at the end of the period of one week beginning with the day on which they are registered in the Royal Court of Jersey.

(2) These Regulations extend to Jersey.

## 2. Amendments to the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) Regulations 2010

(1) The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) Regulations 2010[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3 (fees for criminal conviction certificates, criminal record certificates and enhanced criminal record certificates)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “£21.50” substitute “£20”;
- (b) in paragraph (b), for “£21.50” substitute “£20”;
- (c) in paragraph (c), for “£49.50” substitute “£41”.

(3) In regulation 3A(1) (fees for requesting up-date information[^f00005] under section 116A of the Police Act 1997)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “£16” substitute “£15”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “£16” substitute “£15”.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the prescribed fees in the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) Regulations 2010 ([S.I. 2010/1087](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1087)).

Regulation 2 amends the fees for criminal conviction certificates (also known as basic certificates), criminal record certificates (also known as standard certificates) and enhanced criminal record certificates and requesting up-date information under section 116A of the Police Act [1997 (c. 50)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/50). The fees are being decreased in England and Wales and these amendments make corresponding decreases to the applicable fees in Jersey.

The amended fees apply to applications for basic certificates, standard certificates, enhanced criminal record certificates and the up-date service in Jersey.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector or community bodies is foreseen.
