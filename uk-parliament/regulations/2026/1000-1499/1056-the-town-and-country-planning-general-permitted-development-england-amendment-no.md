# The Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1056

TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING, ENGLAND

The Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2026

Made

25th September 2026

Laid before Parliament

30th September 2026

Coming into force

21st October 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by [sections 59](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/59), [60](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/60), [61](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/61) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/61) and [333](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/333) [(7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/333) and [(8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/333) of the [Town and Country Planning Act 1990](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/contents)[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on 21st October 2026.

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales.

(4) In this Order, “**the GPDO**” means the [Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) Order 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/596/contents)[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the GPDO

The GPDO is amended in accordance with articles 3 to 9.

## 3. Amendment of Article 2 (interpretation)

In article 2(1)—

- (a) after the definition of “erection”, insert—

  > - “**European site**” has the meaning given by [regulation 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1012/regulation/8) of the [Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1012/contents)[^f00003]

  ;
- (b) in the definition of “National Planning Policy Framework”, for “December 2024” substitute “August 2026”.

## 4. Amendment of Class D of Part 2 of Schedule 2

(1) Class D of Part 2 of Schedule 2 (electrical outlet for recharging vehicles) is amended as follows.

(2) For paragraph D, substitute—

> **D. Permitted development**
>
> The installation, alteration or replacement of an electrical outlet mounted on a wall for recharging electric vehicles—
>
> - (a) within an area lawfully used for off-street parking; or
> - (b) within any other area within the curtilage of a dwellinghouse or block of flats.

.

(3) In paragraph D.1 (development not permitted)—

- (a) the existing text becomes sub-paragraph (1);
- (b) after paragraph (a), insert—

  > - (aa) be installed more than 1.6 metres above ground level;

  ;
- (c) after sub-paragraph (1), insert—

  > (2) Development is not permitted by paragraph (b) of Class D—
  >
  > - (a) if it would result in more than 1 outlet being installed within the curtilage of a dwellinghouse or block of flats; or
  > - (b) within a conservation area.
  >
  > (3) If the electrical outlet would be mounted on a wall of a dwellinghouse or block of flats that immediately adjoins a highway, development is not permitted by Class D if—
  >
  > - (a) the electrical outlet would protrude by more than 0.2 metres from the external wall of the dwellinghouse or block of flats; or
  > - (b) there would be less than 1.5 metres’ width of footway between the outermost projection of the electrical outlet and the edge of the carriageway.

  .

(4) In paragraph D.2 (conditions)—

- (a) the existing text becomes sub-paragraph (1);
- (b) after sub-paragraph (1), insert—

  > (2) Development is permitted by Class D subject to the condition that, where there is a footway between the electrical outlet and the vehicle to be recharged, when the outlet is in use the charging cable must cross the footway by means of an authorised cross-pavement solution.

  .

(5) After paragraph D.2, insert—

> **D3. Interpretation of Class D**
>
> For the purposes of Class D—
>
> - “**authorised**”, in relation to a cross-pavement solution, means installed by, or on behalf of, or with the authorisation of, the relevant highway authority;
> - “**block of flats**” means a building which consists of at least two flats;
> - “**carriageway**” has the meaning given by section 329(1) of the Highways Act 1980;
> - “**cross-pavement solution**” means a pavement-embedded cable channel that allows a cable for the recharging of electric vehicles to safely cross a footway from a domestic premises to a parked vehicle;
> - “**electrical outlet**” means a dedicated charging point for the recharging of electric vehicles not including by means of a standard domestic plug and socket, and including any casing of that electrical outlet;
> - “**footway**” has the meaning given by section 329(1) of the Highways Act 1980.

>
> The installation, alteration or replacement of a cross-pavement solution by, on behalf of, or with the authorisation of, a highway authority.

> **F3. Interpretation of Class F**
>

>
> - “**cross-pavement solution**” means a pavement-embedded cable channel that allows a cable for the recharging of electric vehicles to safely cross a footway from a domestic premises to a parked vehicle;
> - “**footway**” has the meaning given by section 329(1) of the Highways Act 1980.

- (a) is permitted under Class D or Class E of Part 2 of Schedule 2 to the GPDO as it had effect immediately before 21st October 2026, and

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/596](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/596)) (“the GPDO”). The GPDO provides, for the purposes of section 59 of the Town and Country Planning Act [1990 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8), for the grant of planning permission for certain classes of development without the requirement for a planning application to be made under Part 3 of that Act. The classes of permission, known as “permitted development rights” together with their accompanying conditions, limitations and restrictions, are set out in Schedule 2 to the GPDO.

Article 3 introduces a new definition of “European site” into the GPDO and amends the existing definition of “National Planning Policy Framework”.

Articles 4 and 5 amend Classes D and E of Part 2 of Schedule 2 to the GPDO (which deal respectively with electrical outlets and electrical upstands for recharging vehicles) to extend those rights, which previously only applied in areas lawfully used for off-street parking. The amendments introduce new conditions and limitations, including that where there is a footway between the electrical outlet and the vehicle, any charging cable must cross the pavement by means of a cross-pavement solution.

Article 5 also amends Class E.2 to permit the installation of additional units of equipment housing, provided that the cumulative maximum volume of all equipment housing does not exceed 45 cubic metres. Where the equipment housing exceeds 29 cubic metres and is installed on article 2(3) land, the developer will be required to obtain prior approval from the local planning authority.

Class CB of Part 4 of Schedule 2 to the GPDO provides a temporary right to provide buildings on school land where the school has a RAAC-affected building. This right was due to expire at 11.59 p.m. on 24th October 2026. Article 6 extends this right so that it will continue until 11.59 p.m. on 24th October 2028.

Article 7 inserts a new Class F into Part 9 of Schedule 2 to the GPDO to permit, subject to the stated limitations and conditions, the installation, alteration or replacement of a cross-pavement solution by or on behalf of a highway authority, or with its authorisation.

Class B (electricity undertakings) of Part 15 (power related development) of Schedule 2 to the GPDO permits, among other things, the installation of substations by electricity undertakers. Article 8 extends this right so that it applies to substations up to 45 cubic metres in capacity. Where the capacity of the substation exceeds 29 cubic metres, it will be subject to new, additional conditions and limitations, including a requirement to obtain prior approval from the local planning authority where the chamber would be situated on article 2(3) land.

Article 9 amends paragraph B (interpretation) of Part 20 (construction of new dwellinghouses) of Schedule 2 to the GPDO to replace references to the “**Health and Safety Executive**” with references to the “**Building Safety Regulator**”.

Article 10 makes transitional provision in relation to the amendments made by this Order.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as no, or no significant, impact on the private sector, voluntary sector or public sector is foreseen. An explanatory memorandum is published alongside this instrument at www.legislation.gov.uk.
