# The Feed-in Tariffs (Amendment) (British Industrial Competitiveness Scheme Excluded Electricity) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1182

ELECTRICITY

The Feed-in Tariffs (Amendment) (British Industrial Competitiveness Scheme Excluded Electricity) Order 2026

Made

10th November 2026

Laid before Parliament

12th November 2026

Coming into force

3rd December 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 43(3)(a) and 104(2) of the Energy Act 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Feed-in Tariffs (Amendment) (British Industrial Competitiveness Scheme Excluded Electricity) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on the 21st day after the day on which it is laid before Parliament.

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales and Scotland.

## 2. Amendment of the Feed-in Tariffs Order 2012

In the Feed-in Tariffs Order 2012[^f00002], in article 27 (calculation of FIT contributions), in paragraph (6)—

- (a) before the definition of “the capped amount of qualifying renewable electricity” insert—

  > - “**BICS excluded electricity**” has the meaning given in regulation 3(1) of the Renewables Obligation and Electricity Capacity (Amendment) (British Industrial Competitiveness Scheme Excluded Electricity) Regulations 2026[^f00003];

  ;
- (b) in the definition of “relevant amount of electricity”—
  - (i) at the end of paragraph (d), omit “and”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (e), for “year 14 and each subsequent FIT year” substitute “years 14 to 17”;
  - (iii) at the end of paragraph (e), insert “; and”;
  - (iv) after paragraph (e), insert—

    > - (f) for FIT year 18 and each subsequent FIT year, the amount of electricity supplied by A to customers in Great Britain, less the amounts of electricity supplied by A that are, for that FIT year—
    >   - (i) EII excluded electricity, and
    >   - (ii) BICS excluded electricity.

    .

Blair McDougall

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

10th November 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made in exercise of the powers in sections 43(3)(a) and 104(2) of the Energy Act [2008 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/32). It amends the Feed-in Tariffs Order 2012 ([S.I. 2012/2782](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/2782)) to make amendments as to how annual levelisation payments are calculated under article 27 of that Order. In particular, this Order introduces an exemption from that calculation for electricity supplied to customers operating in certain manufacturing sectors, under the British Industrial Competitiveness Scheme.

A full impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of businesses, voluntary bodies and community bodies is available alongside this instrument on https://www.legislation.gov.uk and from the Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade, Old Admiralty Building, Admiralty Place, London, SW1A 2DY.
