# The Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025 (Commencement No. 5 and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1208 (C. 104)

TRANSPORT AND WORKS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025 (Commencement No. 5 and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2026

Made

16th November 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 118(1)(w)(ii) and (y), and (7) of the Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025 (Commencement No. 5 and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2026.

(2) In these Regulations, “**2025 Act**” means the Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025.

## 2. Provisions which come into force on 20th November 2026

The following provisions of the 2025 Act come into force on 20th November 2026—

- (a) section 40(3) (deadline for decisions), and
- (b) section 43 (deemed grant of listed building consent etc).

## 3. Transitional provisions

(1) The amendments made to the Transport and Works Act 1992[^f00002] by section 40(3) apply to a relevant application made before 20th November 2026 only where a relevant notice has not yet been issued in respect of that application.

(2) The amendments made to the Transport and Works Act 1992 by section 43 do not apply to a relevant application made before 20th November 2026.

(3) In this regulation—

- (a) “**relevant application**” means an application for an order under section 1 or 3 of the Transport and Works Act 1992[^f00003];
- (b) “**relevant notice**” has the same meaning as in rule 2 of the Transport and Works (Inquiries Procedure) Rules 2004[^f00004].

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force two provisions in the Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025 (“**the 2025 Act**”). Regulation 2 brings into force sections 40(3) and 43 on 20th November 2026. Both provisions make amendments to the Transport and Works Act 1992. Regulation 3 makes transitional provision. It provides that section 40(3) applies to applications under section 1 or 3 of the Transport and Works Act 1992 made before these Regulations came into force only where a relevant notice has not yet been issued in respect of that application. The amendments made by section 43 do not apply to applications made before these Regulations come into force.

An impact assessment has been produced in relation to the Act and copies can be obtained at https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/bills/cbill/59-01/0196/IA\_annexes.pdf or from the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government at 2 Marsham Street, London, SW1P 4DF, United Kingdom.
