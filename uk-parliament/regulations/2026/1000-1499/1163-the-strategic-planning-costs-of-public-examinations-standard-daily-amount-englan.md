# The Strategic Planning (Costs of Public Examinations) (Standard Daily Amount) (England) Regulations 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [section 303A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/303A) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/303A) of the [Town and Country Planning Act 1990](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/contents)[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Strategic Planning (Costs of Public Examinations) (Standard Daily Amount) (England) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 26th November 2026.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and apply in relation to England only.

## 2. Standard daily amount

(1) In relation to a specified examination carried out by a person appointed by the Secretary of State, the standard daily amount prescribed under [section 303A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/303A) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/303A) of the [Town and Country Planning Act 1990](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/contents) is £993, or an appropriate proportion of that amount in relation to part of a day.

(2) In this regulation, “specified examination” means a public examination under [section 12I](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/5/section/12I) or [12P](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/5/section/12P) [(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/5/section/12P) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/5/section/12P) of the [Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/5/contents)[^f00002].

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which apply in relation to England only, apply where the Secretary of State is authorised to recover costs arising in connection with a public examination under section 12I or 12P(6)(a) of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act [2004 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/5).

Regulation 2 specifies a standard daily amount which may be charged by a person appointed to hold a public examination for each day on which that person is engaged in the holding of, or work connected with, that examination. The amount is £993 per day. Where part of a day is worked, an appropriate proportion may be charged.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
