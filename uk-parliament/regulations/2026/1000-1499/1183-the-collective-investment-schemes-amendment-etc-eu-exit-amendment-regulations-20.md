# The Collective Investment Schemes (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1183

FINANCIAL SERVICES

The Collective Investment Schemes (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made

10th November 2026

Laid before Parliament

12th November 2026

Coming into force

16th December 2026

The Treasury makes these Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by regulation 67(1) of the Collective Investment Schemes (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Collective Investment Schemes (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 16th December 2026.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Amendment of the Collective Investment Scheme (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

(1) The Collective Investment Schemes (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00002], are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 62(3)(d) (temporary recognition for EEA UCITS or sub-fund of EEA UCITS), for “6” substitute “7”.

Christian Wakeford

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make an amendment to the Collective Investment Schemes (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/325](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/325)) (“**CIS Regulations**”), regarding temporary recognition for the purposes of Part 17 of the Financial Services and Markets Act [2000 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8).

Regulation 2 amends regulation 62(3)(d) of the CIS Regulations, to extend the temporary recognition of funds regime, from six years to seven.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
