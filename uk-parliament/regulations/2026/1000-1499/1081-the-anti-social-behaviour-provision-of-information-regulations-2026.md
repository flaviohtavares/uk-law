# The Anti-social Behaviour (Provision of Information) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1081

ANTI-SOCIAL BEHAVIOUR, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Anti-social Behaviour (Provision of Information) Regulations 2026

Made

12th October 2026

Laid before Parliament

14th October 2026

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(1)

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 105A of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014[^f00001].

In accordance with section 105A(6) of that Act, the Secretary of State has consulted such persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Anti-social Behaviour (Provision of Information) Regulations 2026 and come into force on the 21st day after the day on which they are laid.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

**2.** In these Regulations—

- “**the 2014 Act**” means the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014[^f00002];
- “**category**” means the category of anti-social behaviour incident set out in regulation 4 to which the incident reported most closely relates;
- “**specified relevant authority**” means a local authority within the meaning of section 20 of the 2014 Act.

## 3. Duty to provide specified information to the Secretary of State

(1) A specified relevant authority must provide to the Secretary of State the information specified in the Schedule in relation to its local authority area (“**the specified information**”).

(2) The specified information must be provided for each relevant reporting period.

(3) In this regulation, a “**relevant reporting period**” means—

- (a) the period of six months beginning with the day on which these Regulations come into force;
- (b) each successive period of six months.

## 4. Categories of anti-social behaviour

For the purposes of these Regulations, the categories of anti-social behaviour incident are incidents relating to—

- (a) drug-related anti-social behaviour;
- (b) vehicle-related anti-social behaviour;
- (c) environmental anti-social behaviour;
- (d) noise-related anti-social behaviour;
- (e) intimidation, harassment or abuse;
- (f) vandalism, criminal damage or graffiti;
- (g) neighbour-related anti-social behaviour;
- (h) alcohol-related anti-social behaviour;
- (i) dangerous or uncontrolled dogs;
- (j) other anti-social behaviour not otherwise captured in paragraphs (a) to (i) above.

## Schedule — Specified information

*Regulation 3*

### 1. Specified information for the purposes of these Regulations

The number of anti-social behaviour incidents reported to the specified relevant authority, broken down by category.

**2.** In relation to ASB case reviews[^f00003]—

- (a) the number of applications made to the specified relevant authority for an ASB case review;
- (b) the number of ASB case reviews carried out by the specified relevant authority;
- (c) the number of applications for an ASB case review refused by the specified relevant authority, broken down by the reason for refusal;
- (d) the number of ASB case reviews carried out by the specified relevant authority which resulted in a recommendation under section 104(7) of the 2014 Act,

broken down by category.

**3.** In relation to criminal behaviour orders under section 331 of the Sentencing Act 2020[^f00004] relating to anti-social behaviour—

- (a) the number of applications made by the specified relevant authority;
- (b) the number of orders made,

broken down by category.

**4.** In relation to civil orders under the 2014 Act—

- (a) the number of applications made by the specified relevant authority for each type of order;
- (b) the number of orders made of each type,

broken down by category.

**5.** In paragraph 4, “**civil orders under the 2014 Act**” means—

- (a) respect orders under Part A1[^f00005];
- (b) youth injunctions under section 1;
- (c) housing injunctions under section 1A[^f00006].

**6.** In relation to community protection notices—

- (a) the number of warnings issued by the specified relevant authority under section 43(5)(a) of the 2014 Act, broken down by category;
- (b) the number of community protection notices issued by the specified relevant authority under section 43(1), broken down by category;
- (c) for each type of authorised person under section 53 of the 2014 Act—
  - (i) the number of fixed penalty notices issued under section 52 of the 2014 Act in respect of failures to comply with community protection notices;
  - (ii) the number of fixed penalty notices exceeded £250,

  broken down by category.

**7.** In relation to closures—

- (a) the number of closure notices[^f00007] issued by the specified relevant authority;
- (b) the number of closure orders[^f00008] issued by the specified relevant authority,

broken down by category.

**8.** In relation to public space protection orders[^f00009] or expedited orders[^f00010]—

- (a) the number of public space protection orders or expedited orders made by the specified relevant authority, broken down by category;
- (b) in relation to fixed penalty notices issued under section 68 of the 2014 Act in respect of an offence under section 63 or 67 of that Act—
  - (i) the number of notices issued, broken down by category;
  - (ii) the number of notices which were issued by the specified relevant authority, and of those, how many imposed an amount exceeding £250;
  - (iii) the number of notices which were issued by a person authorised by the specified relevant authority under section 68(11) of the 2014 Act, and of those, how many imposed an amount exceeding £250.

### 9. Interpretation

In this Schedule, information “broken down by category” is information provided separately for each category of anti-social behaviour incident to which it relates.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made under section 105A of the Anti-Social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act [2014 (c. 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/12) (“**the 2014 Act**”), which enables the Secretary of State to make regulations placing a duty on specified authorities to provide the Secretary of State with specific information relating to anti-social behaviour. This may include information about reports of anti-social behaviour made to the authority, and their response to anti-social behaviour. Under section 105A(3), a relevant authority can be required to collect or obtain, create or produce, retain or process information in order to comply with this duty. A specified relevant authority for the purposes of these Regulations is a local authority (as defined in section 20 of the 2014 Act).

Regulation 3 sets out a duty to provide the information specified in the Schedule to the Secretary of State. It specifies the intervals at which the information in question must be provided. The first submission must provide the specified information for the period of six months from the day on which the Regulations come into force. Thereafter, the specified information is to be provided every six months, to cover the preceding six months

Regulation 4 sets out the categories of anti-social behaviour incident with regard to which a specified relevant authority is required to provide the information specified in the Schedule.

The Schedule provides the detail of the information to be provided to the Secretary of State. Where information is to be provided broken down by category, this means with reference to the category of anti-social behaviour incident in Regulation 4 to which the information relates.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, though an impact assessment was produced pertaining to the broader package of anti-social behaviour measures introduced by Crime and Policing Act [2026 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/20), of which the enabling power relating to these Regulations forms part.

That assessment is available online: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7ca434ed915d6969f46506/Annex\_A\_Overarching\_IA\_Lords.pdf.
