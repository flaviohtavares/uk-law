# The Withdrawal Agreement (Windsor Framework and Governance) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

NORTHERN IRELAND

CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

Made

11th November 2026

Coming into force

12th November 2026

The Minister for the Cabinet Office makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8C(1) and (2) of, and paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001].

In making these Regulations, the Minister for the Cabinet Office has had special regard to the matters listed in section 46 of the [United Kingdom Internal Market Act 2020](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/27/contents)[^f00002].

In accordance with paragraph 8F(1) of Schedule 7[^f00003] to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Withdrawal Agreement (Windsor Framework and Governance) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

(2) They come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

(3) They extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(2) In sections 6(2), 24(1), 69(10A) and 74(7), for “Protocol on Ireland/Northern Ireland”, in each place those words occur, substitute “Windsor Framework”.

(3) In sections 78A and 78B—

- (a) in subsection (1), for “Protocol on Ireland/Northern Ireland” substitute “Windsor Framework”;
- (b) in subsection (9), for “Protocol” substitute “Windsor Framework”.

(4) In section 78C(1) and 78D(1), for “Protocol on Ireland/Northern Ireland”, in each place those words occur, substitute “Windsor Framework”.

(5) In section 98(1) after the definition of “transferred matter” insert—

> - “**Windsor Framework**” means the part of the withdrawal agreement known as the Windsor Framework by virtue of Joint Declaration No. 1/2023 of 24 March 2023 made between the EU and the United Kingdom in the Joint Committee established by the withdrawal agreement.

(6) In Schedule 6A (Democratic consent process)[^f00006]—

- (a) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) for “Protocol”, in each place it occurs, substitute “Windsor Framework”;
  - (ii) omit the definition of “Protocol”;
- (b) in paragraph 2(1), for “Protocol on Ireland/Northern Ireland” substitute “Windsor Framework”;
- (c) in paragraph 4(4)(b), for “Protocol” substitute “Windsor Framework”;
- (d) in the italic heading before paragraph 21, for “Protocol” substitute “Windsor Framework”;
- (e) in paragraph 21(2), for “Protocol” substitute “Windsor Framework”.

(7) In Schedule 6B (Windsor Framework Democratic Scrutiny)[^f00007]—

- (a) in paragraph 1(6), omit—

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Regulation 2(6) omits section 15C of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 to allow the United Kingdom co-chair to consent to the Joint Committee adopting decisions or recommendations by written procedure for the period in between meetings, provided for in Rule 9(1) of Annex VIII of the Withdrawal Agreement.

Regulation 3(7)(c) amends Schedule 6B to the Northern Ireland Act 1998 to change the number of days the Democratic Scrutiny Committee has to decide to hold an inquiry into a replacement EU act from five working days to ten working days.

Regulation 3(7)(d) clarifies the statutory obligation on Northern Ireland Ministers and departments to comply with reasonable information requests from the Democratic Scrutiny Committee.

Regulation 3(7)(e) and (f) amend Schedule 6B to the Northern Ireland Act 1998 to change the number of days the Democratic Scrutiny Committee has to publish a report of conclusions of an inquiry into a replacement EU act and publish evidence relevant to the inquiry from fifteen working days to twelve working days before the end of the scrutiny period.

The remaining provisions in regulations 2 and 3 update references in the Northern Ireland Act 1998 and European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 to the Windsor Framework as per Joint Declaration No. 1/2023 of 24 March 2023 made between the EU and the United Kingdom in the Joint Committee established by the withdrawal agreement[^f00008].

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument. An explanatory memorandum has been published alongside this instrument at www.legislation.gov.uk.
