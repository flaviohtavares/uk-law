# The Children (Prescribed Orders - Jersey) (England) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1252

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS, ENGLAND

The Children (Prescribed Orders - Jersey) (England) Regulations 2026

Made

25th November 2026

Laid before Parliament

27th November 2026

Coming into force

1st January 2027

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 101(3) of the Children Act 1989[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, Commencement, Extent, Application and Interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Children (Prescribed Orders - Jersey) (England) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st January 2027.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales but apply in relation to England only.

(4) In these Regulations “**the Act**” means the Children Act 1989.

## 2. Transfer of Recovery Orders from Jersey to England

A recovery order made under Article 45 of the Children (Jersey) Law 2002[^f00002] (being an order made by a court in Jersey which appears to the Secretary of State to correspond in its effect to an order which may be made under the Act) shall have effect in all circumstances and for all purposes of the Act in England as if it were a recovery order made under section 50 of the Act.

Josh MacAlister

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make provision for a recovery order made in Jersey to have effect as if it were a recovery order made under section 50 of the Children Act 1989.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on business and civil society organisation. The instrument has a minimal impact on the public sector.
