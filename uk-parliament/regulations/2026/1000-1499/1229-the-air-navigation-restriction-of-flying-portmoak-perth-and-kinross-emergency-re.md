# The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Portmoak, Perth and Kinross) (Emergency) (Revocation) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1229

CIVIL AVIATION

The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Portmoak, Perth and Kinross) (Emergency) (Revocation) Regulations 2026

Made

at 2.40 p.m. on 23rd November 2026

Coming into force with immediate effect

The Secretary of State has decided that it is no longer considered necessary in the public interest to restrict flying in the area specified in the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Portmoak, Perth and Kinross) (Emergency) Regulations 2026[^f00001].

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by article 239 of the Air Navigation Order 2016[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Portmoak, Perth and Kinross) (Emergency) (Revocation) Regulations 2026 and they come into force with immediate effect.

## 2. Revocation of Regulations

The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Portmoak, Perth and Kinross) (Emergency) Regulations 2026 are revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Karen L Michaels

Grade 7

at 2.40 p.m. on 23rd November 2026

Department for Transport

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the statutory instrument which imposed emergency restrictions on flying in the area specified in the Schedule to that instrument, the emergency in question having passed.
