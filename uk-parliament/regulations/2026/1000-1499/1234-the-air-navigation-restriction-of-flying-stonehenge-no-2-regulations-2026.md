# The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Stonehenge) (No. 2) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1234

civil aviation

The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Stonehenge) (No. 2) Regulations 2026

Made

23rd November 2026

Coming into force

21st December 2026

The Secretary of State has decided that it is necessary in the public interest to restrict the flying in the area specified in regulation 3(1) by reason of the intended gathering there of a large number of persons.

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by article 239 of the Air Navigation Order 2016[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Stonehenge) (No. 2) Regulations 2026 and they come into force on 21st December 2026.

## 2. Interpretation

The times referred to in regulation 3(1) are Co-ordinated Universal Time[^f00002].

## 3. Restricted airspace

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), during the period beginning with 2300 hours on 21st December 2026 and ending with 1100 hours on 22nd December 2026 no aircraft is to fly below 2,500 feet above mean sea level within the area bounded by a circle having a radius of 1.5 nautical miles centred at 511044N 0014934W.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to any aircraft—

- (a) flying—
  - (i) in accordance with a permission issued by the Wiltshire Police Drone Team,
  - (ii) in accordance with a clearance issued by the air traffic control unit at Ministry of Defence Boscombe Down, or
  - (iii) within the airspace notified as the Salisbury Plain Danger Areas if it does so with a permission issued by the Senior Air Operations Officer at Salisbury Plain Air Operations, or
- (b) operated by or on behalf of—
  - (i) a Police Air Support Unit, or
  - (ii) an Emergency Medical Service.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations impose restrictions on the flying of aircraft in the vicinity of Stonehenge, Wiltshire during the winter solstice period where there will be a large gathering of persons. The Secretary of State for Transport has decided that flying should be restricted in that area for reasons of public safety and security.

The times referred to in regulation 3(1) are Co-ordinated Universal Time (UTC) which is the same as Greenwich Mean Time (GMT).

In relation to the permission referred in regulation 3(2)(a), the relevant persons may be contacted as notified.

Full details of these Regulations will be promulgated by Briefing Sheet and Notice to Aviation (NOTAM).

The point of contact in the Civil Aviation Authority is Mr Thomas Shainberg, Airspace Regulator, Safety and Airspace Regulation Group, contactable on telephone number 03301 382515.
