# The Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

30th October 2026

Coming into force

31st October 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 46(3), 53(1) and (2)(b) of the Child Maintenance and Other Payments Act 2008[^f00001] (“**the 2008 Act**”).

In accordance with section 53(3) of the 2008 Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement, application and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 31st October 2026.

(2) Regulation 4 applies only in relation to the dependant of a person who dies on or after 31st October 2026 and who, immediately before their death, had diffuse mesothelioma.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

## 2. Amendment of the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations 2008

The Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations 2008[^f00002] are amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4.

## 3. Insertion of regulation 5A

After regulation 5 (amount of lump sum payment) insert—

> **5A. Payment to personal representatives**
>
> (1) Where—
>
> - (a) a claim is made, and
> - (b) the person making the claim dies before payment is made,
>
> payment must be made to the person’s personal representatives, subject to the condition in paragraph (2) having been met.
>
> (2) The condition is that any document that is by law sufficient evidence of the grant of—
>
> - (a) probate of the will of the person,
> - (b) letters of administration of the estate of the person, or
> - (c) confirmation as executor of the person,
>
> has been provided to the Secretary of State.

.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Under the Child Maintenance and Other Payments Act [2008 (c. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/6) (“**the 2008 Act**”), lump sum payments may be made to certain persons with diffuse mesothelioma or their dependants. The Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1963](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1963)) (“**the 2008 Regulations**”) set out the conditions to be met for a lump sum payment to be made, along with the rates of payment.

These Regulations amend the 2008 Regulations to—

- bring the dependant rates of payment in line with the rates for persons with diffuse mesothelioma;
- make it clear to whom payment can be made in the event the person making the claim dies before it is paid.

By virtue of regulation 1(2), the increased rates of pay for a dependant apply only in relation to a case where the sufferer dies on or after 31st October 2026.

A full impact assessment has not been produced as these Regulations concern financial assistance from the Department for Work and Pensions which is out of scope of the Better Regulation Framework.

An Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside these Regulations at www.legislation.gov.uk.
