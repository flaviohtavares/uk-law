# The Electricity (Individual Exemption from the Requirement for a Transmission Licence) (Dogger Bank A) (Amendment) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1222

ELECTRICITY

The Electricity (Individual Exemption from the Requirement for a Transmission Licence) (Dogger Bank A) (Amendment) Order 2026

Made

18th November 2026

Laid before Parliament

23rd November 2026

Coming into force

16th December 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(1) and (6)(b) of the Electricity Act 1989[^f00001].

The Secretary of State has given notice of the proposal to make this Order in accordance with section 5(2) and (3) of that Act and has consulted with the Scottish Ministers[^f00002], and has considered any representations made.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Electricity (Individual Exemption from the Requirement for a Transmission Licence) (Dogger Bank A) (Amendment) Order 2026 and comes into force on 16th December 2026.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales and Scotland.

## 2. Amendment of the Electricity (Individual Exemption from the Requirement for a Transmission Licence) (Dogger Bank A) Order 2025

(1) The Electricity (Individual Exemption from the Requirement for a Transmission Licence) (Dogger Bank A) Order 2025[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2(2)(b) (exemption from section 4(1)(b) of the Electricity Act 1989), for “18th December 2026” substitute “18th April 2027”.

Michael Shanks

Minister of State

Department for Energy Security and Net Zero 

18th November 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Electricity (Individual Exemption from the Requirement of a Transmission Licence) (Dogger Bank A) Order 2025 and The Electricity (Individual Exemption from the Requirement of a Transmission Licence) (Dogger Bank A) (Amendment) Order 2025 granted an exemption to Doggerbank Offshore Wind Farm Project 1 Projco Limited from the requirement to hold an electricity transmission licence in respect of Dogger Bank A Offshore Wind Farm. Those Orders combined provided for exemption until 18th December 2026. This Order extends the duration of the exemption until 18th April 2027.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it is a temporary measure.

No, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been published alongside this instrument and is available at www.legislation.gov.uk.
