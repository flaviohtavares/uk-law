# The Green Hill Solar Farm Order 2026, SCHEDULE 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 3 — LEGISLATION TO BE DISAPPLIED

Article 6

**1.** The following provisions do not apply insofar as they relate to the construction of any numbered work or the carrying out of any operation required for the purpose of, or in connection with, the construction, operation, maintenance or decommissioning of the authorised development—

- (a) London and Birmingham Railway (Northampton and Peterborough Branch) Act 1843[^f00056];
- (b) London and North-western Railway (Newport Pagnell, Olney and Wellingborough Branch) Act 1847[^f00057];
- (c) The Nene Valley Drainage and Navigation Improvement Act 1852[^f00058] and any byelaws made thereunder;
- (d) The Nene Valley Drainage and Navigation Improvement (Amendment) Act 1854[^f00059];
- (e) The Nene Valley Act 1862[^f00060];
- (f) The Newport Pagnell Railway (Extension) Act 1866[^f00061];
- (g) The Gas and Water Orders Confirmation Act 1879[^f00062];
- (h) The Wellingborough and District Tramroads Act 1889[^f00063];
- (i) The Gas Orders Confirmation (No. 2) Act 1890[^f00064];
- (j) The Wellingborough and District Tramroads Act 1890[^f00065];
- (k) The Mid-Northamptonshire Water Board Confirmation (Special Procedure) Act 1949[^f00066];
- (l) The Great Ouse Water Act 1961[^f00067]; and
- (m) The Anglian Water Authority Act 1977[^f00068].
