# The Green Hill Solar Farm Order 2026, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Removal of human remains

Where a licence issued under section 4 of the Protection of Military Remains Act 1986[^f00046] (“**a licence**”) applies to human remains, or where the remains are found in an area identified in the construction environmental management plan as one to be treated as though a licence applies, the undertaker must comply with the terms of the licence.
