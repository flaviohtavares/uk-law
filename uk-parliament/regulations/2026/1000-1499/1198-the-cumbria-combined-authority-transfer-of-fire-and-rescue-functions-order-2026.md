# The Cumbria Combined Authority (Transfer of Fire and Rescue Functions) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1198

FIRE AND RESCUE SERVICES, ENGLAND

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The Cumbria Combined Authority (Transfer of Fire and Rescue Functions) Order 2026

Made

16th November 2026

Laid before Parliament

19th November 2026

Coming into force

10th May 2027

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1A(1), (8) and (9) and 60(2) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00001] and sections 56(1)(a) and 105(2) of the English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026[^f00002].

## Part 1 — Introduction

### 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Cumbria Combined Authority (Transfer of Fire and Rescue Functions) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on 10th May 2027.

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “**the 2004 Act**” means the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004;
- “**the CCFRA**” means the Cumbria Commissioner Fire and Rescue Authority[^f00003];
- “**the CCFRA Area**” means the area of the CCFRA;
- “**the Combined Authority**” means the Cumbria Combined Authority[^f00004];

## Part 2 — Transfer of functions

### 3. Transfer of functions of the CCFRA to the Combined Authority

(1) The functions of the CCFRA that were exercisable in relation to the CCFRA Area immediately before this article came into force are functions of the Combined Authority.

(2) The Combined Authority is the fire and rescue authority for the CCFRA Area for the purposes of the 2004 Act.

(3) The Police, Fire and Crime Commissioner for Cumbria (Fire and Rescue Authority) Order 2022[^f00005] is revoked.

## Part 3 — Transitional arrangements

### 4. Extension of financial year of the CCFRA

(1) The following requirements are modified in the case of the CCFRA for the financial year which began on 1st April 2026.

(2) The requirement in section 3(3) (general requirements for accounts) of the Local Audit and Accountability Act 2014[^f00006] for a relevant authority to prepare a statement of accounts for each financial year[^f00007] ending with 31st March is modified to require, after 9th May 2027, the Combined Authority to prepare a statement of accounts for the period beginning with 1st April 2026 and ending on 9th May 2027 in respect of the CCFRA’s accounts.

(3) For the purposes of regulations 9 (signing and approval of statement of accounts for Category 1 authorities) and 15 (commencement of the period for the exercise of public rights) of the Accounts and Audit Regulations 2015[^f00008], references to the responsible financial officer for a relevant authority are to be read as references to the responsible financial officer for the Combined Authority.

### 5. Continuity

(1) The transfer of the CCFRA’s functions to the Combined Authority by this Order does not affect the validity of anything done before the transfer.

(2) Paragraphs (3) to (5) apply in respect of all functions transferred by this Order.

(3) There may be continued by or in relation to the Combined Authority anything (including legal proceedings) which—

- (a) relates to any of the functions transferred; and
- (b) is in the process of being done by or in relation to the CCFRA immediately before the transfer date.

(4) Anything which—

- (a) was made or done by or in relation to the CCFRA for the purposes of, or otherwise in connection with, any of the functions transferred; and
- (b) is in effect immediately before the transfer date;

has effect as if made or done by or in relation to the Combined Authority.

(5) The Combined Authority is to be substituted for the CCFRA in any instruments, contracts or legal proceedings which—

- (a) relate to any of the functions transferred; and
- (b) are made or commenced before the transfer date.

## Part 4 — Consequential modification

### 6. Consequential modification of the Local Government (Best Value Authorities) (Power to Trade) (England) Order 2009

(1) The Local Government (Best Value Authorities) (Power to Trade) (England) Order 2009[^f00009] is modified as follows.

(2) Article 1(2) (application of order to best value authorities) is to be read as if after sub-paragraph (a) there were inserted—

> - (aa) the Cumbria Combined Authority as a fire and rescue authority;

.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the conferral of the functions of the Cumbria Commissioner Fire and Rescue Authority (“the CCFRA”) on the Cumbria Combined Authority (“**the Combined Authority**”).

A separate statutory instrument is planned to provide for the transfer of the property, rights and liabilities of the CCFRA to the Combined Authority.

Section 1A of the Fire and Rescue Services Act [2004 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/21) provides the Secretary of State with the power to designate a mayoral combined authority as the fire and rescue authority (“the FRA”) for the whole of its area.

Article 3 provides that the Combined Authority is the FRA for its area and transfers the functions of the CCFRA to the Combined Authority. It also revokes the Police, Fire and Crime Commissioner for Cumbria (Fire and Rescue Authority) Order 2022 ([S.I. 2022/1230](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/1230)).

Article 4 extends the financial year of the CCFRA beginning with 1st April 2026 under section 3 of the Local Audit and Accountability Act [2014 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/2).

Article 5 makes transitional arrangements in relation to the transfer of functions.

Article 6 makes a consequential modification to ensure that the requirements of the Local Government (Best Value Authorities) (Power to Trade) (England) Order 2009 ([S.I. 2009/2393](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2393)) that apply to the CCFRA will continue to apply to the Combined Authority following the transfer of functions.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, public or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
