These Regulations are made by—

- (a) the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 6(2) and (4), 7(4), 10(1) and (5), 13(7) and (8), and 15(2) and (3) of the Transport and Works Act 1992[^f00001]; and
- (b) the Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 9 of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00002].

Insofar as these Regulations apply to Wales, they are made with the agreement of the Welsh Ministers[^f00003].

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Transport and Works (Applications, Objections, Listed Buildings, Inquiries and Decisions Procedure) (England and Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 20th November 2026.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

(3) Regulations 1, 2, 3(1), (5)(b) and (6), and 4 apply to England and Wales.

(4) Regulation 3(2) to (5)(a) applies to England only.

(5) The provisions in these Regulations apply in relation to a relevant application as follows—

- (a) in relation to a relevant application made on or after 20th November 2026, all of the regulations apply,
- (b) in relation to a relevant application made before 20th November 2026, and for which a relevant notice has not been issued, only regulation 3 applies, and
- (c) in relation to any other relevant application made before 20th November 2026, none of the regulations apply.

(6) In paragraph (5)—

- (a) “**relevant application**” means an application for an order under section 1 or 3 of the Transport and Works Act 1992, and
- (b) “**relevant notice**” means the written notice as defined in rule 2 (interpretation) of the Transport and Works (Inquiries Procedure) Rules 2004[^f00004].

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend, subject to the transitional provisions in regulation 1, the Transport and Works (Inquiries Procedure) Rules 2004 ([S.I. 2004/2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2018)) (“**the 2004 Rules**”), the Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006 ([S.I. 2006/1466](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/1466)) (“**the 2006 Rules**”) and the Transport and Works Applications (Listed Buildings, Conservation Areas and Ancient Monuments Procedure) Regulations 1992 (“**the 1992 Regulations**”).

Regulation 2 amends the 1992 Regulations. It omits various provisions to facilitate the introduction of the deemed listed building regime into the Transport and Works Act 1992, introduced by section 43 of the Planning and Infrastructure Act [2025 (c. 34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/34). Regulation 3 amends the 2004 Rules. It introduces statutory deadlines for various stages of the inquiry, and reduces the time allowed for the issuance of statements of matters by the Secretary of State under rule 7 of the 2004 Rules. Regulation 4 amends the 2006 Rules. It introduces a three-month statutory deadline for decisions on applications, according to the relevant point at which consideration of the application concludes. It makes provision to facilitate the introduction of deemed listed building consent, conservation area consent, and marine licence by sections 43 and 44 of the Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025. It reduces the time period for various stages in the written representation procedure under rule 24 of the 2006 Rules. It introduces a procedure following the quashing of a decision, in line with the existing arrangements under the 2004 Rules.

A full impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument because the impacts are expected to be below the threshold requiring a full Impact Assessment.

An Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
