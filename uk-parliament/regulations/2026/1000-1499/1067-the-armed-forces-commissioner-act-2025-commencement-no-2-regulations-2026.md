# The Armed Forces Commissioner Act 2025 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1067 (C. 88)

DEFENCE

The Armed Forces Commissioner Act 2025 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2026

Made

1st October 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 7(2) of the [Armed Forces Commissioner Act 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/23/contents)[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Armed Forces Commissioner Act 2025 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2026.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 3rd November 2026

The following provisions of the Armed Forces Commissioner Act 2025 come into force on 3rd November 2026—

- (a) section 3 (procedure for making service complaints);
- (b) Schedule 2 (consequential amendments), so far as it is not already in force.

Calvin Bailey

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Ministry of Defence

1st October 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These are the second commencement Regulations made under the Armed Forces Commissioner Act [2025 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/23) (“**the 2025 Act**”).

Regulation 2 brings into force, on 3rd November 2026, provisions of the 2025 Act that amend references in the service complaints framework from the “specified officer” and “appropriate officer” to the “specified person” and “appropriate person”.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
