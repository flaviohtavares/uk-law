# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1087

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2026

Made

14th October 2026

Coming into force

15th October 2026

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of October 2026

Present,

The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2026,
- (b) comes into force on 15th October 2026, and
- (c) extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Appointment of His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council
