# The Plant Health, Seeds, Seed Potatoes and Plant Propagating Material (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1030

Plant Health, Northern Ireland

Seeds, Northern Ireland

The Plant Health, Seeds, Seed Potatoes and Plant Propagating Material (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2026

Made

15th September 2026

Coming into force

6th October 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8C(1) and (2) of, and paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001].

In accordance with paragraph 8F(1) of Schedule 7 to that Act, a draft of these Regulations was laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## PART 1 — Introductory

### 1. Citation, commencement, interpretation and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Health, Seeds, Seed Potatoes and Plant Propagating Material (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the 21st day after the day on which they are made.

(3) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00002] applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(4) These Regulations extend to Northern Ireland only.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8C(1) and (2) of, and paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16). The purpose of this instrument is to ensure that legislation governing plant health, seeds, seed potatoes and plant propagating material continues to operate effectively by updating outdated references in the legislation in accordance with the Windsor Framework.

Part 2 amends the Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 ([S.R. 1995 No. 415](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1995/415)). Part 3 amends the Marketing of Ornamental Plant Propagating Material Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 ([S.R. 1999 No. 502](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1999/502)). Part 4 amends the Forest Reproductive Material Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 ([S.R. 2002 No. 404](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/404)). Part 5 amends the Seed Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 ([S.R. 2016 No. 190](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2016/190)). Part 6 amends the Marketing of Fruit Plant and Propagating Material Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017 ([S.R. 2017 No. 119](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2017/119)).

Part 7 amends the Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 ([S.R. 2020 No. 293](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2020/293)). Regulation 7(16) inserts Part 2A into Schedule 3 to make non-compliance with the listed EU instruments an offence.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
