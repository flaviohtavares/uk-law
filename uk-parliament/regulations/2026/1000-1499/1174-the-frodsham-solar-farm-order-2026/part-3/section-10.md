# The Frodsham Solar Farm Order 2026, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Power to alter layout, etc. of streets

(1) The undertaker may for the purposes of the authorised development, or in connection with the authorised development, alter the layout of or carry out any works in the streets specified in column 2 of the table in Schedule 4 (permanent alteration of streets) permanently in the manner specified in relation to that street in column 3.

(2) Without prejudice to the specific powers conferred by paragraph (1), but subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), the undertaker may, for the purposes of the authorised development, or in connection with the authorised development, permanently or temporarily alter the layout of any street, including, notwithstanding article 4 (development consent etc. granted by this Order) any street outside of the Order limits and, without limitation on the scope of this paragraph, the undertaker may—

- (a) alter the level or increase the width of any kerb, footway, cycle track or verge; and
- (b) make and maintain passing places.

(3) The powers conferred by paragraph (2) may not be exercised without the consent of the street authority, such consent to be in a form reasonably required by the street authority.

(4) The undertaker must restore any street that has been temporarily altered under paragraph (2) to the reasonable satisfaction of the street authority.

(5) Paragraph (3) does not apply where the undertaker is the street authority for a street in which the works are being carried out.

(6) Paragraph (3) does not apply if the street authority has already provided detailed design approval pursuant to requirement 6(1) (detailed design approval) of Schedule 2 (requirements) in relation to a street in which the undertaker seeks to use the powers given by paragraph (2).
