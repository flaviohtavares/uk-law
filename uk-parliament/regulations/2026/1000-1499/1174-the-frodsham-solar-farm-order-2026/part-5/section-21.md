# The Frodsham Solar Farm Order 2026, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Compulsory acquisition of land

(1) The undertaker may—

- (a) acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised development or to facilitate, or as is incidental to, it; and
- (b) use any land so acquired for the purpose authorised by this Order or for any other purposes in connection with or ancillary to the undertaking.

(2) This article is subject to article 22 (time limit for exercise of authority to acquire land compulsorily), article 23 (compulsory acquisition of rights), article 24 (private rights), article 26 (acquisition of subsoil or airspace only), article 29 (rights under or over streets), article 30 (temporary use of land for constructing the authorised development), article 31 (temporary use of land for maintaining the authorised development), and article 44 (crown rights).
