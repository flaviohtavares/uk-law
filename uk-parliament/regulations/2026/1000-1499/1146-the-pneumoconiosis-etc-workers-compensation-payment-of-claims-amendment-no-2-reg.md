# The Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) (Payment of Claims) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1146

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) (Payment of Claims) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026

Made

30th October 2026

Coming into force

31st October 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1), (2) and (4), and 7(1) and (2) of the Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) Act 1979[^f00001] (“**the 1979 Act**”).

In accordance with section 7(3) of the 1979 Act[^f00002], a draft of this instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement, application and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) (Payment of Claims) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 31st October 2026.

(2) These Regulations apply only in relation to any case in which a person first fulfils the conditions of entitlement to a payment under the Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) Act 1979 on or after 31st October 2026.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Under the Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) Act [1979 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/41) (“**the 1979 Act**”), lump sum payments may be made to certain persons disabled by a disease to which the Act applies, or to dependants of persons who were so disabled before they died. The Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers’ Compensation) (Payment of Claims) Regulations 1988 ([S.I. 1988/668](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/668)) (“**the 1988 Regulations**”) prescribe the amount of payments to be made under the 1979 Act, according to the person’s age and level of disablement.

These Regulations amend the 1988 Regulations to:

- remove reference to defunct assessment boards;
- align the rates of pay for dependants with the rates for sufferers;
- remove the top-up awards;
- introduce a clear relevant date for the purposes of calculating awards;
- clarify to whom payment is to be made where a person makes a claim in life but dies before it can be paid.

By virtue of regulation 1(2), the amendments made by these Regulations apply only in relation to any case in which a person first fulfils the conditions of entitlement to a payment under the Act on or after 31st October 2026.

A full impact assessment has not been produced as these Regulations concern financial assistance from the Department for Work and Pensions which is out of scope of the Better Regulation Framework.

An Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside these Regulations at www.legislation.gov.uk.
