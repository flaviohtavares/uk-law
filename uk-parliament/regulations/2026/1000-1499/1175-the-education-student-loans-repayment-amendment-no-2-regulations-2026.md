The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in relation to England in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 22(1), (2)(g), (3)(a) and (b) and 42(6) of the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998[^f00001].

The Welsh Ministers make these Regulations in relation to Wales in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 22(1), (2)(g), (3)(a) and (b) and 42(6) of that Act[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 6th April 2027.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment of the Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009

(1) The Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009[^f00003] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In regulation 21AA (higher interest threshold for plan 2 loans)[^f00004], in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the “and” after sub-paragraph (a);
- (b) after sub-paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (aa) £52,885 for the repayment threshold years ending on 5th April 2028, 5th April 2029 and 5th April 2030, and

  ;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (b), for “each repayment threshold year ending on or after 5th April 2026” substitute “the repayment threshold years ending on 5th April 2026 and 5th April 2027 and for each repayment threshold year ending on or after 5th April 2031”.

(3) In Schedule 1A (repayment thresholds)[^f00005], in paragraph 2(1) (plan 2 loan)—

- (a) omit the “and” after paragraph (a);
- (b) after paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (aa) £29,385 for the repayment threshold years ending on 5th April 2028, 5th April 2029 and 5th April 2030, and

  ;
- (c) in paragraph (b), for “each repayment threshold year ending on or after 5th April 2026” substitute “the repayment threshold years ending on 5th April 2026 and 5th April 2027 and for each repayment threshold year ending on or after 5th April 2031”.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/470](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/470)) (“**the Repayment Regulations**”) which make provision for the repayment of income-contingent student loans in England and Wales.

The amendments made by these Regulations come into force on 6th April 2027. They provide for the higher interest threshold and the repayment threshold for plan 2 loans to be maintained at £52,885 and £29,385 respectively for the repayment threshold years ending on 5th April 2028, 5th April 2029 and 5th April 2030.

Regulation 2(2)(b) inserts a new sub-paragraph (aa) into regulation 21AA(1) of the Repayment Regulations (higher interest threshold for plan 2 loans), to set the higher interest threshold for plan 2 loans (above which interest is charged at the retail price index plus 3%) for UK based borrowers at £52,885, for the three repayment threshold years ending 5th April 2028, 5th April 2029 and 5th April 2030. Regulation 2(2)(c) amends sub-paragraph (b) of regulation 21AA(1) to provide that the higher interest threshold for plan 2 loans continues to be calculated in accordance with that sub-paragraph for the repayment threshold years ending on 5th April 2026 and 5th April 2027, and for each repayment threshold year ending on or after 5th April 2031.

Regulation 2(3)(b) inserts a new paragraph (aa) into paragraph 2(1) (plan 2 loan) of Schedule 1A to the Repayment Regulations (repayment thresholds), to set the repayment threshold for plan 2 loans at £29,385, for the three repayment threshold years ending on 5th April 2028, 5th April 2029 and 5th April 2030. Regulation 2(3)(c) amends paragraph 2(1)(b) of Schedule 1A to provide that the repayment threshold for plan 2 loans continues to be calculated in accordance with that paragraph for the repayment threshold years ending on 5th April 2026 and 5th April 2027, and for each repayment threshold year ending on or after 5th April 2031.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to these Regulations. As a result, a regulatory impact assessment has been prepared as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with these Regulations. A copy can be obtained from the Skills and Tertiary Education Directorate, Welsh Government, Cathays Park, Cardiff, CF10 3NQ.
