Made

15th September 2026

Coming into force

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 74A(2) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Street Works (Charges for Occupation of the Highway) (No. 2) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on 30th September 2026.

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The requirement to pay charges to an Approved Authority is set out in the Street Works (Charges for Occupation of the Highway) (England) Regulations 2012 (“**the 2012 Regulations**”) ([S.I. 2012/425](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/425)).

This Order provides approval for fourteen local highway authorities to require the payment of charges in accordance with the 2012 Regulations and the Lane Rental Schemes set out in article 2 (“**the Schemes**”) for certain street works carried out in the highway.

The term “Approved Authority” is defined in regulation 2 of the 2012 Regulations. This Order will bring these local highway authorities within the definition of “**Approved Authority**” for the purposes of the 2012 Regulations.

The Schemes, which have been submitted to, and approved by, the Secretary of State, are designed to control the carrying out of works in specified locations at Traffic-Sensitive Times. “Traffic-sensitive times” are defined in the 2012 Regulations. This will be achieved by requiring the payment of a charge, in accordance with the 2012 Regulations, for each day that the street is occupied by the works. These local highway authorities may reduce or waive the daily charge and the Schemes contain guidance as to when a discount will be applied.
