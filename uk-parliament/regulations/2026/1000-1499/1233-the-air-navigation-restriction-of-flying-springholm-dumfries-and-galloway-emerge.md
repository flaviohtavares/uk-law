# The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Springholm, Dumfries and Galloway) (Emergency) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1233

CIVIL AVIATION

The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Springholm, Dumfries and Galloway) (Emergency) Regulations 2026

Made

at 10.38 a.m. on 24th November 2026

Coming into force with immediate effect

The Secretary of State for a reason affecting the public interest deems it necessary to restrict flying in the area specified in the Schedule by reason of an emergency having arisen in that area.

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by article 239 of the Air Navigation Order 2016[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Springholm, Dumfries and Galloway) (Emergency) Regulations 2026 and come into force with immediate effect.

## 2. Restricted airspace

No unmanned aircraft is to fly below 1,700 feet above mean sea level over the area specified in Column 1 of the Schedule, being an area bounded by a circle of the radius specified in Column 2 and centred on the position specified in Column 3.

**3.** These Regulations do not apply to any unmanned aircraft flying in accordance with directions given by Police Scotland.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations impose restrictions of an emergency nature on flying in the area specified in the Schedule. They are intended to be of temporary duration and will be revoked as soon as the emergency in question has passed.
