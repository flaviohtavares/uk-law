## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Scotland Act 1998 (Agency Arrangements) (Specification) (Digital Waste Tracking) Order 2026 and comes into force on 1st January 2027.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Specification of functions

(1) The functions of the Scottish Ministers mentioned in paragraph (2) are specified for the purposes of section 93(1) of the Scotland Act 1998.

(2) Any function that is exercisable by the Scottish Ministers acting as the designated person under, or in accordance with, any regulations made under section 34CA of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00002].

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order in Council)

This Order paves the way for the Scottish Ministers and a Minister of the Crown to enter into agency arrangements, under which a Minister of the Crown can exercise certain functions of the designated person acting under, or in accordance with, any regulations made under section 34CA of the Environmental Protection Act 1990. Any such arrangements would be agreed between Ministers of the Crown and the Scottish Ministers.

This will allow for the running of a new digital system for tracking waste within the United Kingdom. This digital system will replace the current, largely paper-based system used to collect waste data. The service will be implemented in several phases. The designated person must establish, maintain and operate an electronic system for the purpose of tracking controlled waste. The related functions include (but are not limited to) establishing, maintaining and operating an electronic system for the purpose of controlling waste and the collection of fees.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
