# The Service and Administration Charges (England) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1190

LANDLORD AND TENANT, ENGLAND

The Service and Administration Charges (England) Regulations 2026

Made

10th November 2026

Laid before Parliament

13th November 2026

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2)

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 20B and 21C to 21G of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985[^f00001] and paragraph 4A(5) of Schedule 11 to the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Service and Administration Charges (England) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force as follows—

- (a) this regulation, regulations 2 to 4, 8 and 9 and Schedules 1, 5 and 6 come into force on 1st April 2027;
- (b) regulation 10 and Schedule 7 come into force on 1st November 2027;
- (c) regulations 5 to 7 and Schedules 2 to 4 come into force—
  - (i) in relation to a dwelling where the landlord is not a social landlord, on 1st November 2027;
  - (ii) in relation to a dwelling where the landlord is a social landlord, on 1st November 2028.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales but apply only in relation to leases[^f00003] of dwellings[^f00004] in England.

(4) Regulation 5 does not apply to a service charge demand made before the date on which that regulation comes into force in relation to the lease of a dwelling.

(5) Regulations 6 and 7 apply in relation to an accounting period ending on or after the date on which those regulations come into force in relation to the lease of a dwelling.

(6) Regulations 8 and 9 do not apply to a request for information made before 1st April 2027.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “**the 1985 Act**” means the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985;
- “**the 2002 Act**” means the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002;
- “**accounting period**” has the meaning given by section 21E(4) of the 1985 Act;
- “**administration charge schedule**” has the meaning given by paragraph 4A(2) of Schedule 11 to the 2002 Act;
- “**annual report**” means a report required under section 21E(1) of the 1985 Act;
- “**future demand notice**” means a notice for the purposes of section 20B(4) of the 1985 Act;
- “**joint venture landlord**” means a person who—
  - (a) is a landlord of a dwelling, and
  - (b) is a party to an arrangement with a social housing provider or with a wholly-owned subsidiary of a social housing provider, under which the social housing provider or the wholly-owned subsidiary of a social housing provider is responsible for the management of the dwelling or of the building or estate of which the dwelling forms a part;
- “**local housing authority**” means a district council, a county council in England for an area for which there is no district council, a London borough council, the Common Council of the City of London (in its capacity as a local authority) or the Council of the Isles of Scilly;
- “**long lease**” has the meaning given by section 59(3) of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1987[^f00005];
- “**public authority**”, in relation to a request under section 21F of the 1985 Act, means a public authority within the meaning of section 3(1) of the Freedom of Information Act 2000[^f00006] that is not a landlord of the dwelling to which the request relates;
- “**qualifying long-term agreement**” has the meaning given by section 20ZA(2) of the 1985 Act[^f00007];
- “**shared ownership lease**” has the meaning given by section 76(3) of the 2002 Act;
- “**social housing provider**” means—
  - (a) a private registered provider of social housing[^f00008], or
  - (b) a local housing authority;
- “**social landlord**” means—
  - (a) a social housing provider, or
  - (b) a joint venture landlord;
- “**specified information**” means information[^f00009] specified for the purposes of section 21F of the 1985 Act;
- “**statutory access regime**” means a right of access to information conferred by or under an enactment;
- “**wholly-owned subsidiary**” has the meaning given by section 1159 of the Companies Act 2006[^f00010].

## 3. Service of documents

(1) A document that the service charge legislation requires to be provided to a tenant must be provided—

- (a) where the lease makes provision about the method by which documents of that description are to be provided, in accordance with the terms of the lease;
- (b) where the lease makes no such provision—
  - (i) by delivering it personally to the tenant,
  - (ii) by leaving it at the tenant’s last known place of residence,
  - (iii) where the tenant has requested or agreed to receive documents of that description by electronic means in accordance with paragraph (2), and that request or agreement has not been withdrawn, by any such means, or
  - (iv) otherwise, by post to the tenant’s last known place of residence.

(2) A request or agreement under paragraph (1)(b)(iii) must—

- (a) be in writing,
- (b) specify the electronic address or electronic location to which the document is to be sent or at which it is to be made available, and
- (c) be capable of being withdrawn by the tenant by notice in writing.

(3) In this regulation, “service charge legislation” means sections 20B(4)(c), 21C(1)(c), 21D(2)(a), 21E(1) and 21G(3) of the 1985 Act and paragraph 4A(4) of Schedule 11 to the 2002 Act.

## 4. Future demand notices

(1) A future demand notice must—

- (a) be in the form set out in Part 1 of Schedule 1 or in a form substantially to the same effect, and
- (b) contain the information specified in sections 1, 2, 3 and 5 of that form.

(2) The rule in section 20B(7)(c) of the 1985 Act applies where—

- (a) the tenant has been given a future demand notice in respect of relevant costs, and
- (b) a demand for payment of a variable service charge as a contribution towards those costs is served on the tenant more than 18 months after the costs were incurred.

(3) Where, before the expected demand date[^f00011] specified in a future demand notice, the landlord determines that the demand will not be served on or before that date, the landlord may substitute a later expected demand date by giving the tenant a revised future demand notice on or before the original expected demand date.

(4) Where a revised future demand notice is given, the rule in section 20B(7)(c) of the 1985 Act applies as if, for the expected demand date, there were substituted the revised expected demand date specified in that notice.

(5) A revised future demand notice must—

- (a) be in the form set out in Part 2 of Schedule 1 or in a form substantially to the same effect, and
- (b) contain the information specified in sections 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 of that form.

(6) A future demand notice or revised future demand notice may include additional information if the additional information does not omit, alter, obscure, qualify, limit or contradict the information required by paragraph (1)(b) or (5)(b), as applicable.

(7) In this regulation, “**revised future demand notice**” means a notice given under paragraph (3).

## 5. Service charge demands

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), a service charge demand must—

- (a) be in the form set out in Part 1 of Schedule 2 or in a form substantially to the same effect, and
- (b) contain the information specified in that form.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), where a local housing authority demands the payment of a service charge, the demand must—

- (a) be in the form set out in Part 2 of Schedule 2 or in a form substantially to the same effect, and
- (b) contain the information specified in that form.

(3) Where a service charge demand is not the initial service charge demand for an accounting period, the demand is not required to contain information relating to the budget for that accounting period.

(4) Information contained in the section of the relevant form headed “Section 48 notice, where required” is not information specified for the purposes of paragraph (1)(b) or (2)(b).

(5) A service charge demand may include additional information if the additional information does not omit, alter, obscure, qualify, limit or contradict any information or statement required by the relevant form.

(6) In this regulation, “**initial service charge demand**”, in relation to an accounting period, means the first demand for payment of a service charge that falls due in that accounting period.

## 6. Annual reports

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), an annual report must—

- (a) be in the form set out in Part 1 of Schedule 3 or in a form substantially to the same effect, and
- (b) contain the information specified in that form, subject to paragraphs (3) and (4).

(2) Where a landlord charges a fixed service charge and an event fee, an annual report must—

- (a) be in the form set out in Part 2 of Schedule 3 or in a form substantially to the same effect, and
- (b) contain the information specified in that form, subject to paragraphs (3) and (4).

(3) Where a landlord is an intermediate landlord, an annual report must contain the information specified in the relevant form insofar as that information is within the landlord’s possession or control.

(4) Where two or more landlords demand or are entitled to demand a service charge from the tenant in relation to different matters, each landlord must provide an annual report in relation to the matters for which that landlord is responsible, containing the information specified in the relevant form insofar as that information is within that landlord’s possession or control.

(5) A private registered provider of social housing[^f00012] is not subject to the duty in section 21E(1) of the 1985 Act unless the tenant holds the dwelling under a long lease or a shared ownership lease.

(6) In this regulation, “**event fee**” means a fee—

- (a) which is payable on the occurrence of an event specified in a lease in relation to the dwelling, including the grant, assignment or termination of the lease, and
- (b) the amount of which is fixed or can be determined in advance.

## 7. Service charge accounts

(1) Where the tenant does not hold the dwelling under a long lease or a shared ownership lease, the terms in section 21D(2) of the 1985 Act are not to be implied into the lease.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the written statement of account required by the term implied into the lease by section 21D(2)(a) of the 1985 Act must—

- (a) be in the form set out in Part 1 of Schedule 4 or in a form substantially to the same effect, and
- (b) contain the information specified in that form.

(3) Where the landlord is a social landlord—

- (a) the term in section 21D(2)(a) of the 1985 Act is to be implied into the lease as modified by paragraph (4), and
- (b) the term in section 21D(2)(b) of the 1985 Act is not to be implied into the lease.

(4) The modification referred to in paragraph (3)(a) is that section 21D(2)(a) of the 1985 Act applies as if for “a written statement of account” there were substituted “a written expenditure report”.

(5) A written expenditure report provided by a private registered provider of social housing, or by a joint venture landlord whose management arrangement is with a private registered provider of social housing or a wholly-owned subsidiary of a private registered provider of social housing, must—

- (a) be in the form set out in Part 2 of Schedule 4 or in a form substantially to the same effect, and
- (b) contain the information specified in that form.

(6) A written expenditure report provided by a local housing authority, or by a joint venture landlord whose management arrangement is with a local housing authority or a wholly-owned subsidiary of a local housing authority, must—

- (a) be in the form set out in Part 3 of Schedule 4 or in a form substantially to the same effect, and
- (b) contain the information specified in that form.

(7) The standard for the review of financial information specified for the purposes of section 21D(2)(b) of the 1985 Act is—

- (a) where the lease requires the written statement of account to be audited, the International Standard on Auditing (UK) 800 (Revised), Special Considerations—Audits of Financial Statements Prepared in Accordance with Special Purpose Frameworks, dated 25th September 2025[^f00013];
- (b) otherwise, the International Standard on Related Services 4400 (Revised), Agreed-Upon Procedures Engagements, dated April 2020[^f00014].

(8) The statement required by section 21D(2)(b)(ii) of the 1985 Act must be in the form set out in Part 4 of Schedule 4 or in a form substantially to the same effect, and accompany the written report in the manner specified in that form.

(9) A written statement of account or written expenditure report may include additional information if the additional information does not omit, alter, obscure, qualify, limit or contradict any information or statement required by the relevant form.

(10) In this regulation—

- “**written expenditure report**” means a statement of expenditure incurred in respect of service charges payable by the tenant;
- “**written report**” means the report referred to in section 21D(2)(b) of the 1985 Act;
- “**written statement of account**” means the written statement of account referred to in section 21D(2)(a) of the 1985 Act.

## 8. Right to obtain information on request

(1) The information specified for the purposes of section 21F(1) of the 1985 Act is—

- (a) in relation to a tenant who holds the dwelling under a long lease or shared ownership lease, the information specified in Schedule 5;
- (b) in relation to a tenant of a private registered provider of social housing who does not hold the dwelling under a long lease or shared ownership lease, the information specified in Schedule 6.

(2) Information specified in Schedules 5 and 6 is not to be treated as specified information if the person required to request information believes it is possessed by a public authority.

(3) But paragraph (2) does not affect the duty to request that information from any other person.

(4) A request under section 21F(1) of the 1985 Act must be made—

- (a) in writing, and
- (b) no more than six years after the end of the accounting period to which the request relates.

(5) A request under section 21F(4) or (6) of the 1985 Act must—

- (a) be made in writing, and
- (b) be made within 14 days beginning with the day on which the preceding request is made.

(6) A duty to comply with a request under section 21F of the 1985 Act does not apply if—

- (a) the information requested is commercially sensitive and cannot reasonably be redacted,
- (b) the request is vexatious, or
- (c) the request is made to a public authority.

(7) Where a request is made under section 21F(4) or (6) of the 1985 Act, the period specified for the purposes of section 21G(3)(a) of the 1985 Act (“specified period”) is 14 days.

(8) Where a person (“**S**”) requests information under section 21F(6) of the 1985 Act pursuant to a request from another person (“**R**”) and the information is received by S after the end of the specified period, S must provide the information to R within 14 days beginning with the day on which it is received.

(9) Information provided in response to a request under section 21F of the 1985 Act must be provided in writing.

(10) The period specified for the purposes of section 21G(3)(b) of the 1985 Act is three months.

(11) In this regulation, “**preceding request**” means—

- (a) in relation to a request under section 21F(4) of the 1985 Act, the tenant’s request under section 21F(1) of that Act;
- (b) in relation to a request under section 21F(6) of the 1985 Act, the request made under section 21F(4) of that Act.

## 9. Provision of information to tenant

(1) Where a request is made under section 21F(1) of the 1985 Act, the period specified for the purposes of section 21G(3)(a) of the 1985 Act (“specified period”) is—

- (a) where the request relates to information for an accounting period ending within the period of 12 months ending with the day on which the request is made, 30 days beginning with that day;
- (b) where the request relates to information for an accounting period ending more than 12 months but not more than three years before that day, 60 days beginning with that day;
- (c) where the request relates to information for an accounting period ending more than three years but not more than six years before that day, 90 days beginning with that day.

(2) Where a request relates to more than one accounting period, a single specified period applies, determined by reference to the oldest accounting period to which the request relates.

(3) Where the landlord receives specified information after the end of the specified period in response to a request under section 21F(4) or (6) of the 1985 Act, the landlord must provide that information to the tenant within 14 days beginning with the day on which it is received.

## 10. Administration charge schedules

(1) An administration charge schedule must—

- (a) be in the form set out in Schedule 7 or in a form substantially to the same effect, and
- (b) contain the information specified in that form.

(2) Information contained in Part D of the form set out in Schedule 7 is not information specified for the purposes of paragraph (1)(b).

(3) An administration charge schedule may include additional information if it does not omit, alter, obscure, qualify, limit or contradict information required by the form.

## Schedule 5 — Right to obtain information on request: tenants who hold a long lease or shared ownership lease

Regulation 8

### Part 1 — Insurance information

**1.** In this Schedule—

- “**effected**” means, in relation to a policy of insurance, purchased or renewed;
- “**insurance policy**” means a policy of insurance for which a service charge is payable by a tenant;
- “**managing agent**” means a person appointed as an agent of the landlord to discharge any of the landlord’s obligations in relation to the management of—
  - (a) the dwelling, or
  - (b) the estate of which the dwelling forms part;
- “**request**” means a request made by a tenant under section 21F(1) of the 1985 Act;
- “**tenant’s dwelling**” means a dwelling demised to a tenant who makes a request.

**2.** (1) The following is specified information in relation to the current insurance policy—

- (a) the insurance renewal date,
- (b) the date of the most recent insurance reinstatement valuation, and
- (c) details of any claims made against the policy in the three years preceding the date of the request.

(2) The following is not specified information for the purposes of sub-paragraph (1)—

- (a) information which relates only to a building—
  - (i) which is not the tenant’s dwelling, and
  - (ii) of which the tenant’s dwelling does not form part;
- (b) information which the landlord is prohibited from disclosing by or under another enactment.

(3) In this paragraph, “**current insurance policy**” means an insurance policy that is in place at the date of the request.

**3.** (1) The following is specified information in relation to an insurance policy which was effected on or before 30th December 2023—

- (a) a written summary of the insurance,
- (b) the relevant policy, and
- (c) any associated documents.

(2) The following is specified information in relation to an insurance policy which was effected on or after 31st December 2023—

- (a) the relevant policy,
- (b) the name of the insurer,
- (c) the type of insurance,
- (d) a summary of the insured risks,
- (e) a summary of any excluded risks,
- (f) the insured sums including—
  - (i) the total sum insured,
  - (ii) the sum insured in respect of the building if the tenant’s dwelling forms part of a building,
  - (iii) the sum insured in respect of the tenant’s dwelling if one or more of the following apply—
    - (aa) the tenant’s dwelling does not form part of a building,
    - (bb) the sum insured in respect of the tenant’s dwelling is stated in the policy,
- (g) a summary and description of any excess,
- (h) the term and duration,
- (i) the significant features and benefits, and
- (j) the total premium including—
  - (i) the amount of any Insurance Premium Tax payable in respect of the premium,
  - (ii) the amount of any Value Added Tax payable in respect of the premium, and
  - (iii) the premium in respect of—
    - (aa) the building of which the tenant’s dwelling forms part, or
    - (bb) the tenant’s dwelling, if the tenant’s dwelling does not form part of a building.

(3) The following is also specified information in relation to an insurance policy which was effected on or after 31st December 2023 if an insurance broker was appointed in relation to the policy—

- (a) the total commission that any insurer, insurance intermediary, landlord, managing agent or third party received,
- (b) details of any remuneration or financial incentive offered or given to an insurance intermediary, landlord, managing agent or third party,
- (c) the number of alternative quotes obtained from the insurer and any other insurer for insurance policies,
- (d) an explanation of why the insurance policy was consistent with the interests of the customer and the tenant, and
- (e) details of insurance connections.

(4) The following is also specified information in relation to an insurance policy which is effected on or after 1st November 2027—

- (a) the number of alternative quotes obtained for insurance policies,
- (b) the total cost of each alternative quote obtained including tax, and
- (c) details of reasonable steps taken by the landlord to manage any conflict of interest.

(5) In this paragraph—

- “**associated documents**” means accounts, receipts or other documents which provide evidence of payment of any premium due in respect of a relevant policy;
- “**conflict of interest**” means a financial or other interest of the landlord or managing agent which may prejudicially affect the landlord’s compliance with section 20G of the 1985 Act[^f00015];
- “**insurance connections**” means a direct or indirect holding representing 10% or more of the voting rights or capital held by—
  - (a) an insurance intermediary in the insurer, or
  - (b) the insurer or its parent undertaking in an insurance intermediary;
- “**insurance distribution activities**” means, in relation to a contract of insurance, one or more of the following—
  - (a) carrying out transactions as an agent;
  - (b) arranging deals;
  - (c) making arrangements with a view to transactions;
  - (d) assisting in the administration or performance of a contract of insurance;
  - (e) advising;
  - (f) agreeing to carry out any of the activities in paragraphs (a) to (e);
- “**insurance intermediary**” means a person, other than a landlord or managing agent, who carries out insurance distribution activities as their principal professional activity and carries out such activities in relation to the insurance policy;
- “**parent undertaking**” has the meaning given in section 1162 of the Companies Act 2006[^f00016];
- “**relevant policy**” means any policy of insurance under which the tenant’s dwelling, or the building of which the tenant’s dwelling forms part, is insured.

### Part 2 — Service charge and accounts information

**4.** (1) The following is specified information in relation to a service charge—

- (a) statements of account or equivalent accounts information for an accounting period to which the request relates,
- (b) budgets or estimates for an accounting period to which the request relates,
- (c) service charge demands issued to the tenant for an accounting period to which the request relates,
- (d) reconciliation demands or balancing charge information for an accounting period to which the request relates,
- (e) information showing how the tenant’s contribution to the service charge has been calculated,
- (f) information showing the apportionment method used to calculate the tenant’s share of the relevant costs,
- (g) reserve fund or sinking fund statements, where relevant to the service charge payable by the tenant,
- (h) invoices, receipts or other supporting documents evidencing relevant costs, where those documents relate to the service charge payable by the tenant, and
- (i) contracts or agreements under which services, works, insurance or management functions are provided and the costs of which are recovered through the service charge.

(2) The following is not specified information for the purposes of sub-paragraph (1)—

- (a) information which does not relate to the service charge payable by the tenant;
- (b) information which relates only to another dwelling and is not relevant to the calculation, apportionment or reasonableness of the service charge payable by the tenant;
- (c) information which the landlord is prohibited from disclosing by or under another enactment.

### Part 3 — Building and site management information

**5.** (1) The following is specified information in relation to the building or estate of which the tenant’s dwelling forms part—

- (a) fire risk assessments,
- (b) fire risk appraisals of external wall construction and cladding carried out in accordance with PAS 9980:2026[^f00017],
- (c) completed External Wall System 1 forms[^f00018],
- (d) building surveys,
- (e) documents relating to the long-term maintenance and management of the building, including building maintenance plans, asset management plans and planned works programmes,
- (f) documents relating to major works planned, underway or completed in an accounting period to which the request relates,
- (g) consultation notices, estimates, observations and landlord responses relating to major works or qualifying long-term agreements, where relevant to the service charge payable by the tenant,
- (h) information about contracts for maintenance, repair, management, improvement or insurance of the building or estate,
- (i) information about the identity and contact details of any managing agent, resident management company, RTM company, recognised tenants’ association or superior landlord involved in the management of the building or estate,
- (j) documents, reports, notices, certificates or records relating to—
  - (i) fire safety, including a building emergency evacuation plan prepared under the Fire Safety (Residential Evacuation Plans) (England) Regulations 2025[^f00019],
  - (ii) the implementation, at building level, of the residential personal emergency evacuation plan requirements under those Regulations,
  - (iii) mitigating measures arising from that implementation which relate to the building or estate,
  - (iv) building safety, remediation, housing standards or health and safety compliance in respect of the building or estate,
  - (v) any action taken, planned or outstanding in response to a document, report, notice, certificate or record mentioned in sub-paragraphs (i) to (iv),
- (k) records relating to the holding, collection, use or expenditure of service charge funds, including transaction records and records relating to reserve funds or sinking funds, where those records relate to the service charge payable by the tenant,
- (l) previous annual reports and administration charge schedules provided or published under these Regulations, where they relate to the tenant’s dwelling, building or estate,
- (m) information relating to heat networks, energy efficiency, net zero measures, grants, loans or other funding arrangements, where the information relates to works, installations, improvements, services or costs affecting the building or estate and the service charge payable by the tenant, and
- (n) maintenance logs, inspection records, servicing records and ongoing maintenance schedules or work programmes relating to the repair, maintenance or management of the building or estate, where those records relate to services, repairs, maintenance, improvements or costs affecting the service charge payable by the tenant.

(2) The following is not specified information for the purposes of sub-paragraph (1)—

- (a) information which does not relate to the service charge payable by the tenant;
- (b) a survey or report which relates only to another dwelling and does not relate to the building or estate of which the tenant’s dwelling forms part;
- (c) information which the landlord is prohibited from disclosing by or under another enactment;
- (d) a person-centred fire risk assessment, emergency evacuation statement or other information relating to an individual resident which identifies, or could reasonably identify, that resident.

(3) In this paragraph—

- “**resident management company**” means a body corporate which is party to a lease of a building where—
  - (a) the body corporate is limited by guarantee and the members of that body are tenants under leases of dwellings in the building, or
  - (b) the majority of the shares in the body corporate are held by such tenants;
- “**RTM company**” has the meaning given by section 73 of the 2002 Act[^f00020].

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make provision about service charges and administration charges in relation to leases of dwellings in England.

Regulation 3 makes provision about the methods by which documents required under the service charge legislation are to be provided.

Regulation 4 prescribes the forms and required information for future demand notices and revised future demand notices under section 20B of the Landlord and Tenant Act [1985 (c. 70)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/70) (“**the 1985 Act**”). It applies the rule in section 20B(7)(c) and makes provision for a later expected demand date to be substituted under section 20B(8).

Regulation 5 prescribes the form and required information for service charge demands under section 21C of the 1985 Act.

Regulation 6 prescribes the form and required information for annual reports under section 21E of the 1985 Act and makes provision in relation to intermediate landlords, multiple landlords and private registered providers of social housing.

Regulation 7 makes provision about written statements of account and written reports under section 21D of the 1985 Act. It provides for the form and content of written statements of account, modifies or disapplies certain terms implied into leases, specifies the standards applicable to written reports and prescribes the form and manner of the statement required by section 21D(2)(b)(ii). It also prescribes written expenditure report forms for private registered providers of social housing and local housing authorities and provides for the application of those forms to joint venture landlords according to whether the relevant management arrangement is with a private registered provider of social housing or a local housing authority.

Regulation 8 specifies information for the purposes of section 21F of the 1985 Act and makes provision about requests for, the provision of, and access to that information. It also provides that certain duties to request or provide information do not apply in relation to public authorities within the meaning of the Freedom of Information Act [2000 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/36). For this purpose, a public authority which is a landlord in relation to the dwelling to which the request relates is not treated as a public authority.

Regulation 9 specifies the periods within which information must be provided to tenants.

Regulation 10 prescribes the form and required information to be contained in administration charge schedules under paragraph 4A of Schedule 11 to the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act [2002 (c. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/15).

Measures on service of documents, future demand notices and the right to obtain information on request come into force on 1st April 2027. Measures on administration charges come into force on 1st November 2027. Measures on service charge demands, annual reports and service charge accounts come into force on 1st November 2027 in relation to leases of dwellings where the landlord is not a social landlord and on 1st November 2028 where the landlord is a social landlord.

A full impact assessment has been published in relation to the Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act [2024 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/22), which introduces the framework for these provisions, and copies can be obtained at https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/bills/cbill/58-04/0013/LeaseholdandFreeholdReformBillImpactAssessment.pdf or from the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government at 2 Marsham Street, London SW1P 4DF.
