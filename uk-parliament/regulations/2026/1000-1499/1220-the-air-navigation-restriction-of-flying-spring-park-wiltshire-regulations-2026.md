# The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Spring Park, Wiltshire) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 1220

civil aviation

The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Spring Park, Wiltshire) Regulations 2026

Made

18th November 2026

Coming into force

24th November 2026

The Secretary of State has decided that it is necessary in the public interest to restrict the flying of unmanned aircraft in the area specified in regulation 3(1) for reasons of national defence.

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by article 239 of the Air Navigation Order 2016[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Spring Park, Wiltshire) Regulations 2026 and they come into force on 24th November 2026.

## 2. Interpretation

The times referred to in regulation 3(1) are Co-ordinated Universal Time[^f00002].

## 3. Restricted airspace

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), during the period beginning with 0000 hours on 24th November 2026 and ending with 2359 hours on 17th February 2027, no unmanned aircraft is to fly below 1,500 feet above mean sea level within the area bounded by straight lines joining successively the following points—

- (a) 512517.71N 0021333.62W,
- (b) 512517.55N 0021327.14W,
- (c) 512507.95N 0021318.49W,
- (d) 512508.64N 0021309.33W,
- (e) 512514.36N 0021301.45W,
- (f) 512508.15N 0021249.77W,
- (g) 512506.91N 0021245.76W,
- (h) 512506.35N 0021240.65W,
- (i) 512503.46N 0021239.83W,
- (j) 512456.65N 0021254.21W,
- (k) 512457.76N 0021332.31W,
- (l) 512504.04N 0021340.40W,
- (m) 512511.50N 0021337.90W, and
- (n) 512517.71N 0021333.62W.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to any unmanned aircraft—

- (a) operated by or on behalf of—
  - (i) a UK Police Air Support Unit,
  - (ii) a UK Fire and Rescue Service, or
  - (iii) the Ministry of Defence, or
- (b) flying in accordance with a permission issued by the Ark Security Management Team, Spring Park Data Centres.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Secretary of State for Transport has decided that it is necessary in the public interest for reasons of national defence to restrict the flying of unmanned aircraft in the vicinity of Corsham, Wiltshire.

The times referred to in regulation 3(1) are Co-ordinated Universal Time (UTC) which is the same as Greenwich mean Time (GMT).

In relation to the permission referred to in regulation 3(2)(b), the Ark Security Management Team, Spring Park Data Centres may be contacted as notified.

Full details of these Regulations will be promulgated by Notice to Aviation (NOTAM).

The point of contact at the Civil Aviation Authority is the Airspace Regulation (Utilisation) Section, Safety and Airspace Regulation Group, on telephone number 01293 983880.
