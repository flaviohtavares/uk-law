# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 42

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 42. Vehicle movements

(1) Except as provided in paragraphs (4) and (5), on and after such a day as the undertaker may determine, on reaching the point identified in column (1) of Part 2 (straight ahead only) of Schedule 10 (traffic regulation) and identified on the rights of way plans, no person is to cause or permit any vehicle to proceed on any part of the lengths of road described in column (2) of that Part otherwise than straight ahead, except upon the direction of, or with the permission of, a police officer in uniform.

(2) Except as provided in paragraphs (4) and (5), on and after such a day as the undertaker may determine, on reaching the point identified in column (1) of Part 3 (prescribed turns) of Schedule 10 and identified on the rights of way plans, no person is to cause or permit any vehicle to proceed from the length of road described in column (2) of that Part into the road described in column (3) of that Part otherwise than by turning in the direction specified in column (4) of that Part, except upon the direction of, or with the permission of, a police officer in uniform.

(3) Except as provided in paragraphs (4) and (5), on and after such a day as the undertaker may determine, on reaching the point identified in column (1) of Part 4 (no left turn) of Schedule 10 and identified on the rights of way plans, no person is to cause or permit any vehicle to proceed from the length of road described in column (2) of that Part into the road described in column (3) of that Part, except upon the direction of, or with the permission of, a police officer in uniform.

(4) Nothing in paragraphs (1) to (3) applies to render it unlawful to cause or permit a vehicle to proceed on any of the lengths of roads described in those paragraphs, for so long as may be necessary to enable that vehicle to be used—

- (a) in connection with:
  - (i) the removal of any obstruction to traffic; or
  - (ii) the maintenance, improvement, reconstruction or operation of the road in question; or
- (b) for police, ambulance, fire and rescue authority or traffic officer purposes.

(5) Nothing in paragraphs (1) to (3) applies in relation to a vehicle where the traffic authority for the road has given permission by means of a dispensation issued under paragraph (6) in relation to that vehicle which is displayed in the front or nearside of the vehicle.

(6) Upon written application on a form issued by and obtainable from the traffic authority any person duly authorised by the traffic authority may issue a dispensation to exempt that vehicle from the prescribed vehicle movements and restrictions on vehicle movements referred to in paragraphs (1) to (3) on any of the roads described in those paragraphs.

(7) Paragraphs (1) to (6) have effect as if made by order under the 1984 Act, and their application may be varied or revoked by an order made under that Act or any other enactment which provides for the variation or revocation of such orders.
