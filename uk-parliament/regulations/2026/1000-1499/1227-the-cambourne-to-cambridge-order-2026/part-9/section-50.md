# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 50

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 50. Registration of busway services and substitute road services

(1) The undertaker may provide, or secure the provision by other persons of, services for the carriage of passengers by road (“substitute services”) where services on the authorised guided busway have been curtailed, discontinued or temporarily interrupted.

(2) The route, frequency and stopping places of any substitute service need not correspond with the route of the interrupted, curtailed or discontinued service.

(3) Section 6 (registration of local services) of the Transport Act 1985 does not apply to any substitute services.

(4) For the purposes of the application of Part 1 of the Transport Act 1985, but subject to paragraphs (1) to (3), the authorised guided busway is a road within the meaning of section 137(1) and (2) of that Act.
