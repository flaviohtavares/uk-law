# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Power to charge fares

(1) The undertaker may demand, take and recover or waive such charges for carrying passengers or goods on the guided busway system, or for any other services or facilities provided in connection with the guided busway system, as it thinks fit.

(2) The undertaker may enter into and carry into effect agreements with other persons providing public passenger transport services with regard to the issue of tickets and the making of through ticketing arrangements or for the purpose of generally coordinating the provision of those services with the operation of the guided busway system.

(3) In this article, “**public passenger transport service**” has the meaning given by section 63(10)(a)[^f00063] of the Transport Act 1985.
