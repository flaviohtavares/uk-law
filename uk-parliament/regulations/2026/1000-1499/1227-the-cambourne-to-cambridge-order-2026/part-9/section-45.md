# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Trespass on the guided busway system

(1) Any person who—

- (a) trespasses on any part of the guided busway system which is not situated in a street; or
- (b) trespasses on any land of the undertaker in dangerous proximity to the guided busway system or to any electrical or other apparatus used for or in connection with the operation of the guided busway system,

is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

(2) No person is to be convicted of an offence under this article unless it is shown that a notice warning the public not to trespass upon the guided busway system was clearly exhibited and maintained at the stop on the guided busway system nearest the place where the offence is alleged to have been committed.
