# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In this Order—

- “**the 1961 Act**” means the Land Compensation Act 1961[^f00004];
- “**the 1965 Act**” means the Compulsory Purchase Act 1965[^f00005];
- “**the 1980 Act**” means the Highways Act 1980[^f00006];
- “**the 1981 Act**” means the Compulsory Purchase (Vesting Declarations) Act 1981[^f00007];
- “**the 1984 Act**” means the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00008];
- “**the 1990 Act**” means the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00009];
- “**the 1991 Act**” means the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00010];
- “**the 2003 Act**” means the Communications Act 2003[^f00011];
- “**the 2016 Regulations**” means the Environmental Permitting (England and Wales) Regulations 2016[^f00012];
- “**apparatus**” has the same meaning as in Part 3 (street works in England and Wales) of the 1991 Act;
- “**authorised guided busway**” means any guided busway authorised by this Order as part of a scheduled work;
- “**authorised works**” means the scheduled works and any other works authorised by this Order;
- “**book of reference**” means the book of reference certified by the Secretary of State as the book of reference for the purposes of this Order;
- “**bridleway**” has the same meaning as in section 329(1) (further provisions as to interpretation) of the 1980 Act;
- “**building**” includes any structure or erection, or any part of a building, structure or erection;
- “**carriageway**” has the same meaning as in section 329(1) of the 1980 Act;
- “**cycle track**” means a way constituting a highway, being a way over which the public have the following, but no other, rights of way—
  - (a) a right of way on pedal cycles (other than pedal cycles which are motor vehicles within the meaning of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00013]); and
  - (b) a right of way on foot;
- “**deposited plans**” means the plans and drawings certified by the Secretary of State as the deposited plans for the purposes of this Order, being the section drawings, engineering drawings, the rights of way plans, the special category land plan, the Crown land plans and the works and land plans;
- “**electronic transmission**” means a communication transmitted—
  - (a) by means of an electronic communications network; or
  - (b) by other means but while in electronic form;

and in this definition “**electronic communications network**” has the same meaning as in section 32(1)[^f00014] (meaning of electronic communications networks and services) of the 2003 Act;

- “**emergency and maintenance access track**” means a way provided for facilitating access to the authorised guided busway for maintenance purposes and for use in situations of emergency or service perturbation;
- “**engineering drawings**” means the engineering drawings included in the deposited plans;
- “**footway**” and “**footpath**” have the same meaning as in section 329(1) of the 1980 Act;
- “**guided busway**” means a way provided for the use of vehicles which are constructed or adapted to carry more than 8 passengers for hire or reward and use the mode prescribed in article 2(g) of the Transport and Works (Guided Transport Modes) Order 1992[^f00015];
- “**guided busway premises**” means any premises of the undertaker used for, or in connection with, the operation or maintenance of the guided busway system, including any building, parking area and park and ride site;
- “**guided busway system**” means the authorised guided busway and any apparatus or facilities used for its operation and other associated authorised works as a system, or part of a system, of transport for the carriage of passengers and includes any guided transport vehicle;
- “**guided transport vehicle**” means any vehicle lawfully using the guided busway system;
- “**highway**” has the same meaning as in section 328 (meaning of “**highway**”) of the 1980 Act;
- “**highway authority**” has the same meaning as in section 1 (highway authorities: general provision) of the 1980 Act;
- “**limits of deviation**” means the limits of deviation for the scheduled works shown on the works and land plans;
- “**limits of land to be acquired or used**” means the limits of land to be acquired or used shown on the works and land plans;
- “**local planning authority**” means Cambridge City Council for land within the administrative area of Cambridge City Council or South Cambridgeshire District Council for land within the administrative area of South Cambridgeshire District Council;
- “**longitudinal sections**” means the longitudinal sections included in the deposited plans;
- “**maintain**” includes inspect, repair, adjust, alter, remove, reconstruct and replace and “**maintenance**” is to be construed accordingly;
- “**maintainable highway**” has the same meaning as in section 86(1) (highway authorities, highways and related matters) of the 1991 Act;
- “**means of access**” means an access to the highway network from neighbouring land;
- “**National Highways Limited**” means National Highways Limited, company number 09346363, whose registered office is at Bridge House, 1 Walnut Tree Close, Guildford, Surrey, GU1 4LZ;
- “**Order limits**” means the limits of deviation and the limits of land to be acquired or used and shown on the works and land plans;
- “**owner**”, in relation to land, has the same meaning as in section 7[^f00016] of the Acquisition of Land Act 1981;
- “**parking place**” has the same meaning as in section 32 of the 1984 Act;
- “**the rights of way plans**” means the rights of way plans included in the deposited plans;
- “**the scheduled works**” means the works specified in Schedule 1 (scheduled works), or any part of them;
- “**the section drawings**” means the deemed planning proposed site sections and the longitudinal sections included in the deposited plans;
- “**the special category land plan**” means the special category land plan included in the deposited plans;
- “**statutory undertaker**” means—
  - (a) any person who is a statutory undertaker for any of the purposes of the 1990 Act; and
  - (b) any public communications provider within the meaning of section 151(1) (interpretation of Chapter 1) of the 2003 Act;
- “**street**” has the meaning given in section 48[^f00017] (streets, street works and undertakers) of the 1991 Act and includes part of a street;
- “**street authority**”, in relation to a street, has the same meaning as in section 49[^f00018] (the street authority and other relevant authorities) of the 1991 Act;
- “**the traffic authority**” has the same meaning as in section 121A(b)[^f00019] (traffic authorities) of the 1984 Act;
- “**the tribunal**” means the Lands Chamber of the Upper Tribunal;
- “**the undertaker**” means Cambridgeshire County Council;
- “**watercourse**” includes all rivers, streams, ditches, drains, canals, cuts, culverts, dykes, sluices, sewers and passages through which water flows (whether or not the flow is intermittent) except a public sewer or drain; and
- “**the works and land plans**” means the Works and Land Plans included in the deposited plans.

(2) References in this Order to rights over land include references to rights to do, or to place and maintain, anything in, on, or under land or in the air space over its surface and references in this Order to the imposition of restrictive covenants are references to the creation of rights over land which interfere with the interests or rights of another and are for the benefit of land which is acquired under this Order or otherwise comprises guided busway premises.

(3) References in this Order to numbered plots are references to plot numbers on the works and land plans.

(4) Any reference in this Order to a work identified by the number of the work is to be construed as a reference to the work of that number authorised by this Order.

(5) References in this Order to points identified by letters, with or without numbers, or by numbers, are to be construed as references to the points so marked on the deposited plans.

(6) All distances, directions, lengths, points and areas stated in the description of the scheduled works or in any description of powers or lands are approximate and distances between points on a scheduled work are taken to be measured along the scheduled work.
