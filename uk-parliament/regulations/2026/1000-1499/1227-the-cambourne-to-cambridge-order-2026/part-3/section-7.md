# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Power to alter layout, etc., of streets

(1) The undertaker may for the purposes of the authorised works alter the layout of, improve, and carry out other ancillary works in, any street specified in–

- (a) column (1) of Schedule 3 (streets subject to alteration of layout); or
- (b) column (2) of Part 1 (new and improved streets) or column (4) of Part 2 (streets to be stopped up for which a substitute is to be provided) of Schedule 4 (streets and means of access to be stopped up and the provision of new and improved streets and substitutes),

in the manner specified in relation to that street in column (2) of Schedule 3 and column (2) of Part 1 and column (4) of Part 2 of Schedule 4.

(2) Without limitation on the scope of the specific powers conferred by article 5 (power to construct and maintain works) but subject to paragraph (3), the undertaker may, for the purpose of constructing, maintaining or using the guided busway system, alter the layout of any street within the Order limits and the layout of any street having a junction with any such street; and, without limitation on the scope of that power, the undertaker may—

- (a) increase the width of the carriageway of the street by reducing the width of any kerb, footway, cycle track or verge within the street;
- (b) alter the level or increase the width of any such kerb, footway, cycle track or verge;
- (c) replace or alter the surface or surface treatment of the street;
- (d) reduce the width of the carriageway of the street including by forming a reserved area in the street as a stopping place for guided transport vehicles or by carrying out other works for that purpose;
- (e) carry out works for the provision or alteration of parking places, loading bays, bus stop clearways, footways, cycle tracks and bus laybys;
- (f) carry out traffic calming works which are of a description prescribed in the Highways (Traffic Calming) Regulations 1999[^f00038] and which are carried out in compliance with those Regulations;
- (g) carry out works to the carriageway of the street for the purpose of deterring or preventing vehicles other than guided transport vehicles from passing along the guided busway system; and
- (h) make and maintain crossovers or passing places.

(3) Where the street is a maintainable highway, the powers conferred by paragraph (1) must not be exercised without the consent of the street authority, but such consent must not be unreasonably withheld.

(4) If within 28 days of receiving an application for consent under paragraph (3) a street authority fails to notify the undertaker of its decision that street authority is deemed to have granted consent.

(5) When making an application for consent under paragraph (3), the undertaker must notify the street authority of the effect of paragraph (4).
