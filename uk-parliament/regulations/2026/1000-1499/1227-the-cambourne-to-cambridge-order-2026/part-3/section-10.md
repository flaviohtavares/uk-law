# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Rights of way over emergency and maintenance access tracks, etc.

(1) Any public rights of way over the bridleways specified in column (2) of Part 1 (new and improved streets) of Schedule 4 (streets and means of access to be stopped up and the provision of new and improved streets and substitutes) shall be subject to any conflicting use—

- (a) of the emergency and maintenance access tracks for the purposes of the authorised guided busway where the relevant public rights of way follow the emergency and maintenance access track that is provided for the purposes of the guided busway system; or
- (b) such rights as may be granted or reserved by the undertaker to facilitate access to neighbouring land.

(2) The undertaker may, in constructing and maintaining the bridleways to which paragraph (1) applies, deviate laterally from the routes shown on the rights of way plans within the extent of the limits of deviation shown on the works and land plans.

(3) The alignment and width of the bridleways referred to in paragraph (2) must be agreed with the local highway authority prior to the relevant bridleway first opening to the public.
