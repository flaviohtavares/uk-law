# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Apparatus in streets

(1) The undertaker may, for the purposes of or in connection with the construction, maintenance and use of the guided busway system, place and maintain in any street in which the guided busway system is constructed or which has a junction with such a street, any work, equipment or apparatus including, without limitation on the scope of that power, foundations, platforms, road islands, substations and any electrical or other apparatus.

(2) Where—

- (a) the street in question is a maintainable highway; and
- (b) the undertaker is not also the street authority for that street,

the powers conferred by this article must not be exercised within the boundaries of a street outside of the Order limits which has a junction with a street in which the construction of the guided busway system is shown on the works and land plans without the consent of the street authority, but such consent must not be unreasonably withheld.

(3) If within 28 days of receiving an application for consent under paragraph (2) a street authority fails to notify the undertaker of its decision that street authority is deemed to have granted consent.

(4) When making an application for consent under paragraph (2), the undertaker must notify the street authority of the effect of paragraph (3).

(5) In this article, the reference to any work, equipment or apparatus in a street includes a reference to any work, equipment or apparatus under, over, along or upon the street.
