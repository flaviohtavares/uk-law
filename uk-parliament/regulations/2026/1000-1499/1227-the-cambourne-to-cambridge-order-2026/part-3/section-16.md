# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Guided busway crossings

(1) The undertaker may construct the authorised guided busway so as to enable vehicles upon it to cross on the level any highway, street or road crossing the line of the authorised guided busway including those specified in Schedule 5 (highways to be crossed on the level) to this Order.

(2) Any traffic sign placed under the powers conferred by article 40 (traffic signs) on or near a highway or other road to which the public has access is to be treated for the purposes of section 64(4) (general provisions as to traffic signs) of the 1984 Act as having been placed as provided by that Act.

(3) The highway authority and the undertaker may enter into agreements with respect to the construction and maintenance of any guided busway crossing; and such an agreement may contain such terms as to payment or otherwise as the parties consider appropriate.

(4) Without prejudice to the generality of article 6 (power to deviate) the undertaker may in the exercise of the powers of this article alter the level of any highway specified in Schedule 5 to this Order for the purposes of forming a guided busway crossing.

(5) The undertaker may provide, maintain and operate at or near any new guided busway crossing such barriers or other protective equipment as the Secretary of State may in writing approve.

(6) In this article—

- “**barrier**” includes gate;
- “**guided busway crossing**” means the place at which the authorised guided busway crosses a highway or other road on the level under the powers conferred by this article; and
- “**protective equipment**” includes lights, traffic signs (within the meaning of section 64(1) of the 1984 Act), manual, mechanical, automatic, electrical or telephonic equipment or other devices.
