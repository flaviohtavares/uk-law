# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Access and access to works

(1) In addition to the powers contained in paragraph (6) of article 11 (construction of new and stopping up of existing streets, means of access, etc.) the undertaker may, for the purposes of the construction, operation or maintenance of the authorised works—

- (a) form and lay out such means of access, or improve such existing means of access in the locations specified in column (3) of Part 4 (means of access to be closed for which a substitute is to be provided) of Schedule 4 (streets and means of access to be stopped up and the provision of new and improved streets and substitutes) and column (2) of Part 6 (new means of access) of Schedule 4;
- (b) close existing means of access in the location specified in columns (1) and (2) of Part 4 of Schedule 4 and column (2) of Part 5 (means of access to be closed for which no substitute is to be provided) of that Schedule; and
- (c) subject to paragraph (2) form and lay out such other means of access or improve existing means of access at such locations within the Order limits as the undertaker reasonably requires for the purposes of the authorised works.

(2) Where the undertaker is not also the highway authority for the street in question the powers conferred by paragraph (1)(c) must not be exercised without the consent of the highway authority, which consent must not be unreasonably withheld.
