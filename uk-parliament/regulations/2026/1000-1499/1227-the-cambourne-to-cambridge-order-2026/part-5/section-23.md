# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Compulsory acquisition of land

(1) The undertaker may acquire compulsorily—

- (a) so much of the land shown on the works and land plans as lying within the Order limits as land to be acquired compulsorily and described in the book of reference as may be required for the purposes of the authorised works; and
- (b) so much of the land specified in columns (1) and (2) of Schedule 2 (acquisition of certain lands for ancillary works) (being land shown on the works and land plans and described in the book of reference) as may be required for the purpose specified in relation to that land in column (3) of that Schedule,

and may use any land so acquired for those purposes or for any other purposes that are ancillary to the construction, maintenance, use and operation of the guided busway system.

(2) The undertaker may only acquire compulsorily the land comprising plot 14-150 as shown on the works and land plans with the consent of National Highways Limited and at a height that is 5.4 metres above the surface of the M11 special road, but the undertaker may—

- (a) acquire new rights over plot 14-150; and
- (b) exercise the powers contained in article 28 (temporary use of land for construction of works),

below that height for the purposes of installing, maintaining, repairing, removing and replacing a bridge within plot 14-150.

(3) Nothing in paragraph (1) authorises the undertaker to acquire compulsorily any of the plots mentioned in Schedule 6 (land not to be acquired compulsorily), or any rights over that land, but the undertaker may acquire by agreement any part of those plots, or any rights over that land, and use those plots for the purposes of the construction, operation and maintenance of the guided busway system.

(4) This article is subject to article 26 (power to acquire new rights and imposition of restrictive covenants), article 28 (temporary use of land for construction of works), article 34 (power to acquire subsoil or airspace only), article 35 (time limit for exercise of powers of acquisition) and article 60 (Crown rights).
