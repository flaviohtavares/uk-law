# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Certification of plans, etc.

The undertaker must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the book of reference and the deposited plans to the Secretary of State for certification that they are true copies of, respectively, the book of reference and the deposited plans referred to in this Order; and a document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
