# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Power to deviate

(1) In constructing or maintaining any of the scheduled works, the undertaker may—

- (a) deviate laterally from the lines or situations shown on the works and land plans within the limits of deviation; and
- (b) deviate vertically from the levels shown on the section drawings—
  - (i) to any extent not exceeding 1 metre upwards; and
  - (ii) to any extent not exceeding 1 metre downwards.

(2) The undertaker may construct and maintain any emergency and maintenance access track comprised in a scheduled work at a different level to the authorised guided busway which it adjoins.

(3) The undertaker may, in constructing and maintaining any part of the authorised guided busway, provide within the limits of deviation such gaps in or changes to the mode of guidance adopted and such number of ways, cross-overs, lay-bys, bus parking areas and traffic control measures as may be necessary or expedient.

(4) Without limitation on the scope of paragraph (1) in constructing and maintaining the scheduled works the undertaker may, for those works shown on the works and land plans by way of a centreline, extend beyond the points of commencement and termination by up to 3 metres from the points of commencement and termination shown for that scheduled work.

(5) The undertaker may, in constructing and maintaining the new and improved streets described in Part 1 of Schedule 4 (streets and means of access to be stopped up and the provision of new and improved streets and substitutes) and streets to be stopped up for which a substitute is to be provided described in Part 2 of that Schedule deviate laterally from the routes shown on the rights of way plans to the extent of the limits of deviation shown on those plans.
