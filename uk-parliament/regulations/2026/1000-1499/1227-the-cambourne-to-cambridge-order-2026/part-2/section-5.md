# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Power to construct and maintain works

(1) The undertaker may construct and maintain the scheduled works.

(2) Subject to article 6 (power to deviate), the scheduled works may only be constructed in the lines or situations shown on the works and land plans and in accordance with the levels shown on the section drawings.

(3) The undertaker may on the land specified in columns (1) and (2) of Schedule 2 (acquisition of certain lands for ancillary works) carry out and maintain any works specified in relation to that land in column (3) of that Schedule together with all works as may be necessary or expedient in connection with those works.

(4) The undertaker may on the land specified in columns (1) and (2) of Schedule 7 (land in which only new rights, etc. may be acquired) carry out and maintain any works specified in relation to that land in column (3) of that Schedule with all works as may be necessary or expedient in connection with those works.

(5) The undertaker may carry out and maintain such of the following works as may be necessary or expedient for the purposes of, or for purposes ancillary to, the construction of the scheduled works, namely—

- (a) stops, platforms, junctions and stopping places;
- (b) parking and park and ride facilities;
- (c) buildings and other works required for, or in connection with, the control, management or maintenance of the guided busway system or the control or management of any vehicular and pedestrian traffic on or in the guided busway system;
- (d) embankments, cuttings, aprons, abutments, retaining walls, wing walls and culverts;
- (e) works required for the strengthening, improvement, maintenance or reconstruction of any street;
- (f) connections to streets, including ramps, paths, steps and tracks;
- (g) works to install or alter the position of any street furniture or apparatus, including mains, sewers, drains, attenuation tanks, pipes, cables and lights;
- (h) works to install or alter apparatus for utilities or operators of communications networks, including ducts, cables, pipes, drains, inspection chambers, junction pits and accesses;
- (i) works to alter the course of, or otherwise interfere with, watercourses;
- (j) landscaping, attenuation and infiltration ponds, bunds, noise barriers, ecological and other works to mitigate any adverse effects of the construction, maintenance or operation of the other authorised works;
- (k) works for the provision of exchange open space land;
- (l) hoardings and fencing, footways, footpaths and bridleways; and
- (m) facilities and works for the benefit or protection of land or premises affected by the other authorised works.

(6) The undertaker may carry out and maintain such other works (of whatever nature) as may be necessary or expedient for the purposes of, or for purposes ancillary to, the construction of the scheduled works.

(7) The undertaker may remove any works constructed by it under this Order which have been constructed as temporary works or which it no longer requires.

(8) The undertaker may, within the Order limits, carry out and maintain landscaping, ecological mitigation works and other works to mitigate any adverse effects of the construction, maintenance and operation of the authorised works (other than works authorised by this paragraph).
