# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Open space and exchange land

(1) The undertaker may not under the powers of this Order take possession of any part of the open space land until the undertaker has taken possession of the exchange land.

(2) Upon the undertaker taking possession of so much of the open space land as is required for the authorised works that land will be discharged from all rights, trusts and incidents to which it was previously subject.

(3) The undertaker may not under the powers of this Order take possession of any part of the DfT open space land until the undertaker has taken possession of the DfT exchange land.

(4) Upon the undertaker taking possession of so much of the DfT open space land as is required for the authorised works that land will be discharged from all rights, trusts and incidents to which it was previously subject.

(5) The undertaker must lay out, as replacement open space, the exchange land and DfT exchange land of which possession has been taken under paragraphs (1) or (3) before the end of the period of one year beginning with the date the authorised works are first brought into public use.

(6) On the date on which the local planning authority has certified that the exchange land has been laid out and provided to its reasonable satisfaction as open space, the exchange land is to vest in the open space land owner and is to be subject to the same rights, trusts and incidents as attached to the open space land.

(7) On the date on which the local planning authority has certified that the DfT exchange land has been laid out and provided to its reasonable satisfaction as open space, the DfT exchange land is to vest in the DfT open space land owner and is to be subject to the same rights, trusts and incidents as attached to the DfT open space land.

(8) In this article—

- “**DfT exchange land**” means plot 14-144c shown on the works and land plans;
- “**DfT open space land**” means plot 14-154 shown on the works and land plans;
- “**DfT open space land owner**” means the Secretary of State for Transport or its successors in title from time to time;
- “**exchange land**” means plot 14-144b shown on the works and land plans;
- “**the open space land**” means the plot shown on the works and land plans as plot number 14-153; and
- “**the open space land owner**” means Cambridgeshire County Council or its successors in title from time to time.
