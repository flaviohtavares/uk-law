# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, SCHEDULE 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 1 — SCHEDULED WORKS

Articles 2(1), 5 and 6

**In the County of Cambridgeshire, District of South Cambridgeshire, and City of Cambridge**

### In the District of South Cambridgeshire—

**Work No. 1** – A two-way guided busway (414 metres in length), together with an adjoining access track, commencing at Sterling Way, Cambourne, running east through Cambourne, before terminating at the crossing of Broadway, Cambourne. The work includes the improvement of the highway of Broadway.

**Work No. 2** – A two-way guided busway (2,007 metres in length), together with an adjoining emergency and maintenance access track, commencing at a junction with Work No. 1 and the highway of Broadway, Cambourne, before running through the Bourn Airfield in a northern then easterly direction and terminating at a junction with Work No. 3 and St Neots Road, Childerley. The work includes a new access from Bourn Airfield to the highway of Broadway and the improvement of the highway of Broadway.

**Work No. 3** – A two-way guided busway (1,333 metres in length), together with an adjoining emergency and maintenance access track, commencing at a junction with Work No. 2 and St Neots Road, Childerley and continuing in an easterly direction between the A428 and St Neots Road, before terminating at St Neots Road, Dry Drayton, approximately 90 metres west of the St Neots Road and Scotland Road junction. The work includes widening and improvement of St Neots Road to provide a new signalised junction to permit guided buses to turn right from St Neots Road westbound into the guided busway and a new bridleway connection to the southern section of St Neots Road.

**Work No. 4** – Realignment and improvement of St Neots Road in Hardwick and Comberton to provide access to Work No. 6. The realigned section is approximately 253 metres in length, commencing 510 metres west of its junction with Long Road, Comberton, terminating at Work No. 6 and includes a new combined cycle track and footway.

**Work No. 5** - Realignment of St Neots Road in Hardwick and Madingley to provide access to Work No. 6. The realigned section is approximately 430 metres in length, commencing at the junction with Work Nos. 4 and 6 to the West of Long Road, Comberton, terminating approximately 264 metres west of the St Neots Road and Madingley Road junction. The work includes widening of the highways of St Neots Road and Long Road at their junction in Comberton including a new combined cycle track and footway.

**Work No. 6** – A two-way guided busway (359 metres in length), together with an adjoining emergency and maintenance access track, commencing at a junction with Work No. 4 and Work No. 5 running in a south easterly direction before terminating at a junction with Work No. 7 and Long Road, Comberton, and including the widening and improvement of the highway of Long Road, Comberton.

**Work No. 7** – A two-way guided busway (2,293 metres in length), together with an adjoining emergency and maintenance access track, commencing at a junction with Work No. 6 and Long Road Comberton and running in an easterly direction before terminating at a junction with Work No. 8 and Cambridge Road, Coton and including the widening and improvement of the highway of Cambridge Road, Coton.

**Work No. 8** – A two-way guided busway (635 metres in length), together with an adjoining emergency and maintenance access track, and the widening and improvement of the highway of Cambridge Road, Coton, commencing at a junction with Work No. 7 and Cambridge Road Coton, and running in an easterly direction before terminating at a junction with Work No. 9 at the administrative boundary between South Cambridgeshire and Cambridge City Council, to the west of the M11.

### In the City of Cambridge—

**Work No. 9** – A two-way guided busway (430 metres in length), together with an adjoining emergency and maintenance access track, commencing at the administrative boundary between South Cambridgeshire and Cambridge City Council at a junction with Work No. 8 and running east across the M11 motorway before terminating at Ada Lovelace Road, West Cambridge Campus. This work includes a new bridge carrying the guided busway over the M11 motorway, and the diversion of public bridleway 39/30.

**Work No. 10** – A two-way guided busway (1,601 metres in length), commencing at a junction with Charles Babbage Road, in the West Cambridge Campus, and running in a southerly direction through the West Cambridge Campus, then continuing south-east through agricultural land and across Bin Brook, before terminating at Grange Road, Cambridge. An emergency and maintenance access track is provided from Footpath 39/31, south of the West Cambridge Campus, to Grange Road. This work includes a new bridge carrying the guided busway over Bin Brook, west of Grange Road, means of access to adjoining premises and works to improve the highway of Grange Road, Cambridge.
