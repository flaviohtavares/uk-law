# The Cambourne to Cambridge Order 2026, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Restriction on apparatus

For the purposes of section 61 of the 1991 Act (protected streets) any authorised guided busway including any emergency and maintenance access track or other facility provided in connection with it is deemed to have been designated by the street authority as a protected street.
