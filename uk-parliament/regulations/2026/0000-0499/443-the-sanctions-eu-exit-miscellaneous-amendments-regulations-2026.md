# The Sanctions (EU Exit) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2026

This Statutory Instrument corrects errors in [S.I. 2024/644](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/644) and [S.I. 2025/394](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/394), and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of those Statutory Instruments.

The Secretary of State[^f00001], considering that the condition in [section 45](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/45) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/45) of the [Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/contents)[^f00002] is met, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [sections 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/1), [5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/5), [11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/11), [14](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/14), [15](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/15) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/15) and [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/15) [(h)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/15), [16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/16), [17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/17) and [45](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/section/45) of, and [paragraphs 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/schedule/1/paragraph/2) [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/schedule/1/paragraph/2) and [27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/schedule/1/paragraph/27) of [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/schedule/1) to, the [Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/contents).

## Part 1 — Introductory

### 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sanctions (EU Exit) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 13th May 2026.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
