Regulations 4, 5 and 6 of these Regulations are made with the concurrence of the Commissioners for His Majesty’s Revenue and Customs[^f00004].

In accordance with [section 173](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/173) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/173) [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/173) of the [Social Security Administration Act 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/contents), the Social Security Advisory Committee has agreed that the proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security and Statutory Maternity Pay (Evidence of Pregnancy and Compensation of Employers) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

(2) Regulations 1 to 4 and 6(c) come into force on 1st April 2026.

(3) Regulations 5 and 6(a) and (b) come into force on 6th April 2026.

## 2. Extent

(1) Regulations 1 and 2 extend to the whole of the United Kingdom.

(2) Regulations 3, 4 and 5 extend to England, Wales and Scotland.

(3) Regulation 6 extends to Northern Ireland only.

## 3. Amendment of the [Social Security (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1976](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/615/contents)

In paragraph 4 of [Part 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/615/schedule/2) of [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/615/schedule/2) to the [Social Security (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1976](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/615/contents) (rules)[^f00005] omit “shall be completed in ink or other indelible substance and”.

## 4. Amendment of the [Statutory Maternity Pay (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1987](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/235/contents)

In paragraph 4 of [Part 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/235/schedule) of [the Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/235/schedule) to the [Statutory Maternity Pay (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1987](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/235/contents) (rules)[^f00006] omit “shall be completed in ink or other indelible substance and”.

## 5. Amendment of the [Statutory Maternity Pay (Compensation of Employers) and Miscellaneous Amendment Regulations 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1882/contents)

In [regulation 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1882/regulation/3) of the [Statutory Maternity Pay (Compensation of Employers) and Miscellaneous Amendment Regulations 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1882/contents) (determination of the amount of additional payment to which a small employer shall be entitled)[^f00007]—

- (a) for “6th April 2025” substitute “6th April 2026”, and
- (b) for “8.5 per cent.” substitute “9 per cent.”.

## 6. Amendment of the [Statutory Maternity Pay (Compensation of Employers) and Miscellaneous Amendment Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/271/contents)

In [regulation 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/271/contents/made) of the [Statutory Maternity Pay (Compensation of Employers) and Miscellaneous Amendment Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/271/contents) (amount of additional payment to which a small employer shall be entitled)[^f00008]—

- (a) for “6th April 2025” substitute “6th April 2026”, and
- (b) for “8.5 per cent.” substitute “9 per cent.”, and
- (c) at the end of this regulation, insert “, that percentage being the total amount of secondary Class 1 contributions estimated by the Commissioners for His Majesty’s Revenue and Customs as to be paid in respect of statutory maternity pay by all employers in that year, expressed as a percentage of the total amount of statutory maternity pay estimated by them to be paid by all employers in that year.”.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the [Social Security (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1976](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/615/contents) ([S.I. 1976/615](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/615)) (“**the 1976 Evidence Regulations**”) and the [Statutory Maternity Pay (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1987](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/235/contents) ([S.I. 1987/235](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/235)) (“**the 1987 Evidence Regulations**”), to make a minor adjustment to the evidential requirements when applying for maternity allowance or statutory maternity pay respectively.

Amongst other things, [the 1976 Evidence Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/615/contents) set out the evidential requirements for claims for maternity allowance and [the 1987 Evidence Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/235/contents) set out the evidential requirements for claims for statutory maternity pay. In each case, this includes setting out the format of a maternity certificate, how it must be completed and what information it must include. Regulation 3 amends [the 1976 Evidence Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/615/contents) and regulation 4 amends [the 1987 Evidence Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/235/contents) by removing the requirement for a maternity certificate to be completed in ink or other indelible substance to make it possible for non-original copies to be supplied in evidence.

These Regulations also amend the [Statutory Maternity Pay (Compensation of Employers) and Miscellaneous Amendment Regulations 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1882/contents) ([S.I. 1994/1882](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1882)) (“**the GB Compensation Regulations**”) and the [Statutory Maternity Pay (Compensation of Employers) and Miscellaneous Amendment Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/271/contents) ([S.R. 1994 No. 271](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/271)) (“**the NI Compensation Regulations**”).

Amongst other things, [the GB Compensation Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1882/contents) and [the NI Compensation Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/271/contents) enable a small employer (as that term is defined within [those Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/271/contents)) to recover payments of statutory maternity pay in full, as well as an additional amount in respect of such pay. Regulations 5 and 6(a) and (b) of these Regulations increase the additional amount that small employers are entitled to recover from 8.5 per cent. to 9 per cent. of the amount of statutory maternity pay they have paid out. This increase takes effect from 6th April 2026.

Regulation 6(c) corrects an error in [the NI Compensation Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/271/contents) by adding in additional wording to set out what must be taken into account when calculating this additional amount. This additional wording is already included in [regulation 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1882/regulation/3) of [the GB Compensation Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1882/contents)[^f00009] (the equivalent regulations, which apply in Great Britain) but was omitted in error from [the NI Compensation Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/271/contents).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
