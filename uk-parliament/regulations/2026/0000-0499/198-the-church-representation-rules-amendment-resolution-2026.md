# The Church Representation Rules (Amendment) Resolution 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 198

ECCLESIASTICAL LAW, ENGLAND

The Church Representation Rules (Amendment) Resolution 2026

Made

8th February 2026

Laid before Parliament

5th March 2026

Coming into force

6th March 2026

The General Synod, in exercise of the power under [section 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/1969/2/section/7) of the [Synodical Government Measure 1969](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/1969/2/contents)[^f00001], resolves to amend the Church Representation Rules[^f00002] as follows.

In accordance with [section 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/1969/2/section/7) of [that Measure](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/1969/2/contents), the General Synod has passed this Resolution with a majority in each House of not less than two-thirds of those present and voting.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Resolution may be cited as the Church Representation Rules (Amendment) Resolution 2026.

(2) This Resolution comes into force on the day after the day on which it is laid before Parliament.

## 2. Parochial church council: business by correspondence

In section B of Part 9 of the Church Representation Rules (model rules on parochial church councils), in Rule M29 (business by correspondence), for paragraph (2) substitute—

> (2) If, within the period specified by the chair of the PCC beginning with the date on which the proposals are sent, at least one-third of the members of the PCC approve in writing and no member objects in writing, the proposals are to be treated at the end of that period as if they had been approved at a duly convened meeting.

.

Approved by the General Synod on 8th February 2026.

A.S. McGregor

Registrar of the General Synod

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Resolution)

This Resolution amends the Church Representation Rules set out in Schedule 3 to the Synodical Government Measure 1969.

Paragraph 2 amends Rule M29 to provide that, where proposals for approval by a parochial church council are dealt with by correspondence, the proposals are approved only if at least one-third of the members of the PCC approve and no member objects.
