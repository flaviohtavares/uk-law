# The Cosmetic Products Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 (Restriction of Chemical Substances) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (No. 2) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

CONSUMER PROTECTION

The Cosmetic Products Regulation [(EC) No 1223/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1223) (Restriction of Chemical Substances) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (No. 2) Regulations 2026

Made

Laid before Parliament

9th February 2026

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2) and (3)

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 31(1)(f), 31(1)(g)(i) and 32(1) of Regulation [(EC) No 1223/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1223) of the European Parliament and of the Council on cosmetic products[^f00001] (“**the Cosmetic Regulation**”).

In accordance with Article 31(1)(f) of the Cosmetic Regulation, the substances set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations have been classified as CMR[^f00002] substances of category 1B or 2 under Regulation [(EC) No 1272/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1272) of the European Parliament and of the Council on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures[^f00003] (“**the CLP Regulation**”).

In accordance with Article 31(1)(g)(i) of the Cosmetic Regulation, hexyl 2-hydroxybenzoate has been classified as a CMR substance of category 2 under the CLP Regulation and the Secretary of State considers there is sufficient scientific evidence that this substance is safe for use in cosmetic products.

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Cosmetic Products Regulation [(EC) No 1223/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1223) (Restriction of Chemical Substances) (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (No. 2) Regulations 2026.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), these Regulations come into force on 15th August 2026.

(3) Regulation 2(2) comes into force on 23rd March 2027.

(4) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

(5) In these Regulations “**the Cosmetic Regulation**” means Regulation [(EC) No 1223/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1223) of the European Parliament and of the Council on cosmetic products.

## 2. Amendments to the Cosmetic Regulation

(1) The Cosmetic Regulation is amended as follows.

(2) At the end of the table in Annex 2 (list of substances prohibited in cosmetic products), insert the entries set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

(3) At the end of the table in Annex 3 (list of substances which cosmetic products must not contain except subject to the restrictions laid down), insert the entry set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

## 3. Transitional Provisions

(1) Despite the amendment made by regulation 2(2) of these Regulations, a cosmetic product placed on the market before 23rd March 2027 containing any of the substances inserted into Annex 2 to the Cosmetic Regulation by regulation 2(2) may continue to be made available on the market until the end of 22nd September 2027.

(2) Despite the amendment made by regulation 2(3) of these Regulations, a cosmetic product placed on the market before 15th August 2026 containing the substance inserted into Annex 3 to the Cosmetic Regulation by regulation 2(3) may continue to be made available on the market until the end of 14th February 2027.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Regulation [(EC) No 1223/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1223) of the European Parliament and of the Council on cosmetic products (“**the Cosmetic Regulation**”) to prohibit or restrict the use of certain substances in cosmetic products.

Regulation 2(2) and Schedule 1 amend Annex 2 to the Cosmetic Regulation (list of substances prohibited in cosmetic products) to add substances that have been classified as category 1B or 2 carcinogenic, mutagenic or toxic for reproduction (“**CMR**”) substances under Regulation [(EC) No 1272/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1272) of the European Parliament and of the Council on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures, prohibiting these substances from use in cosmetic products. This amendment applies from 23rd March 2027.

Regulation 2(3) and Schedule 2 amend Annex 3 to the Cosmetic Regulation (list of substances which cosmetic products must not contain except subject to the restrictions laid down) to add hexyl 2-hydroxybenzoate (commonly known as “hexyl salicylate”) to restrict its use in cosmetic products, to ensure that different types of cosmetic products contain safe levels of hexyl salicylate in accordance with advice from the Scientific Advisory Group on Chemical Safety of Non-Food and Non-Medicinal Consumer Products. This amendment applies from 15th August 2026.

Regulation 3 sets out the transitional arrangements for cosmetic products containing these substances.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or business sectors is foreseen. An Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside these Regulations on www.legislation.gov.uk.
