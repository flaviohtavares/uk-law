# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Cryptoassets) Regulations 2026, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Amendment of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Professions) (Non-Exempt Activities) Order 2001

(1) The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Professions) (Non-Exempt Activities) Order 2001[^f00049] are amended as follows.

(2) In article 4 (activities to which exemption from the general prohibition does not apply)[^f00050], after paragraph (aa) insert—

> - (ab) article 9M (issuing qualifying stablecoin);
> - (ac) article 9T (dealing in qualifying cryptoassets as principal);
> - (ad) article 9Z6 (qualifying cryptoasset staking);

.
