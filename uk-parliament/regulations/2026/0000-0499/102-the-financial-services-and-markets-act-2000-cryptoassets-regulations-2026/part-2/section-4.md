# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Cryptoassets) Regulations 2026, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. “Issuing qualifying stablecoin” and the “issuer” of that qualifying stablecoin

For the purposes of this Chapter—

- (a) a person is “issuing qualifying stablecoin” where that person, whether an authorised person or not, is carrying on the activity described in article 9M of the Regulated Activities Order (issuing qualifying stablecoin)[^f00012];
- (b) the person described in paragraph (a) is to be considered the “issuer” of that qualifying stablecoin.

## Annotations

[^f00012]: Footnote: Article 9M is inserted by regulation 40(5) of this instrument.
