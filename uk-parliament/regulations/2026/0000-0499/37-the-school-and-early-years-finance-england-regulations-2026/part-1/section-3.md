# The School and Early Years Finance (England) Regulations 2026, section 3

> Text as at 2026-02-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Amendment of the Childcare (Provision of Information About Young Children) (England) Regulations 2009[^key-620e77f9a2d68d3c13001d1395abf4b0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Childcare (Provision of Information About Young Children) (England) Regulations 2009[^f00050] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation)—

- (a) for the definition of “the 2024 Regulations” substitute—
  - ““**the 2026 Regulations**” means the School and Early Years Finance (England) Regulations 2026;”;
- (b) in the definition of “disability access fund”, for “regulation 34(5) of the 2024 Regulations” substitute “regulation 36(5) of the 2026 Regulations”; and
- (c) in the definition of “early years pupil premium” for “regulation 1 of the 2024 Regulations” substitute “regulation 1(4) of the 2026 Regulations”.

(3) In the Schedule, in paragraph 14(2), for “regulation 33 of the 2024 Regulations” substitute “regulation 35 of the 2026 Regulations”.
