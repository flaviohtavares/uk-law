# The School and Early Years Finance (England) Regulations 2026, section 4

> Text as at 2026-02-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Amendment of the Schools Forums (England) Regulations 2012[^c-c9810428d6276a016bc5d39acebb679a]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 8 (meetings and proceedings of schools forum) of the Schools Forums (England) Regulations 2012[^f00051]—

- (a) in each of paragraphs (9A) and (9B), for the words from “regulation 12(1)(d)” to “2025” substitute “regulation 13(1)(d) of the School and Early Years Finance (England) Regulations 2026”; and
- (b) in paragraph (9C), for the words from “regulation 12(1)(e)” to the end substitute “regulation 13(1)(e) of the School and Early Years Finance (England) Regulations 2026”.
