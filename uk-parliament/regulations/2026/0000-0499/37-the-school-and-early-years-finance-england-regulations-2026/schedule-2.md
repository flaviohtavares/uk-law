# The School and Early Years Finance (England) Regulations 2026, Schedule 2

> Text as at 2026-02-11.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## Schedule 2 — CLASSES OR DESCRIPTIONS OF PLANNED EXPENDITURE PRESCRIBED FOR THE PURPOSES OF THE SCHOOLS BUDGET OF A LOCAL AUTHORITY WHICH MAY BE DEDUCTED FROM IT TO DETERMINE THE INDIVIDUAL SCHOOLS BUDGET

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 7 and 9

### Part 1 — Historic commitments

**1.**[^key-9f10f2931ce25f2ab099f97fbf57cb0a] CERA incurred for purposes not falling within any other paragraph of this Schedule or Schedule 1.

**2.**[^key-a84f51d6ec338c68324f9ebd4b6105bf] Expenditure in respect of—

- (a) prudential borrowing;
- (b) termination of employment costs, where the schools forum has approved the charging of these costs to the schools budget for the funding period in which they were incurred and the revenue savings achieved by the termination of employment to which they relate are equal to or greater than the costs incurred;
- (c) services (often known as combined budgets) funded partly from central expenditure and partly from other budgets of the local authority or contributions from other bodies, where the expenditure relates to classes or descriptions of expenditure falling outside those in this Schedule;
- (d) special educational needs transport costs; and
- (e) purposes not falling within any other paragraph of this Schedule, provided that the expenditure does not amount in total to more than 0.1% of the local authority’s schools budget,

which was approved by the schools forum or the Secretary of State before 1st April 2013.

**3.**[^key-4bf540698e80abee59f2f11a23c20d86] Any deductions under paragraph 1 or 2(a) to (e) must not exceed the amount deducted under each of the corresponding paragraphs of Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the 2025 Regulations for the previous funding period.

### Part 2 — Central schools expenditure

**4.**[^key-70c604a4b43c21f11c24f0fae7a65655] Expenditure due to a significant growth in pupil numbers as a result of the local authority’s duty under section 13(1) of the 1996 Act[^f00111] to secure that efficient primary education and secondary education are available to meet the needs of the population of its area, including expenditure resulting from the additional costs associated with establishing a new school.

**5.**[^key-6c0bd6ad055e9367dec1ca1aec9828f3] Expenditure to be incurred due to a decline in pupil numbers in schools maintained by the local authority or in Academy schools where the most recent data provided by a local authority to the Secretary of State in accordance with regulation 3 of, and paragraphs 2 (forecasts of pupil numbers) and 4 (total capacity) of Schedule 1 to, the Information as to Provision of Education (England) Regulations 2016[^f00112] indicates that—

- (a) growth in pupil numbers is expected in the three years starting with the school year beginning in the funding period; and
- (b) additional funding is likely to be necessary to provide for that expected growth in pupil numbers.

**6.**[^key-4c9256cd8fca0e1a7e2075d50b7be3f4] Expenditure to be incurred before the opening of new schools to fund the appointment of staff and to enable the purchase of any goods and services necessary in order to admit pupils.

**7.**[^key-033c0c538dcbc928ebf965973110ed94] Expenditure to make provision for extra classes in order to comply with the School Admissions (Infant Class Sizes) (England) Regulations 2012[^f00113].

**8.**[^key-5b8060840c09d01c36f8e490fb473eec] Expenditure on licences which are negotiated centrally by the Secretary of State for all publicly funded schools.

**9.**[^key-6efe750d5fa4a052af69abcea5f3850a] Expenditure on the operation of the system of admissions of pupils to schools (including expenditure incurred in carrying out consultations under section 88C(2) of the 1998 Act) and in relation to appeals.

**10.**[^key-c639d901c074603033325a939e9e9c3e] Expenditure under section 16 (power to establish, assist and maintain primary or secondary schools) or 18 of the 1996 Act (power to arrange education at non-maintained schools)[^f00114] in making any grant or other payment in respect of fees or expenses (of whatever nature) which are payable in connection with the attendance of pupils at a school which is not maintained by any local authority.

**11.**[^key-79be0c5d62b2d3e0317a6ed0c3ecdc94] Expenditure in relation to boarding fees for pupils in accordance with—

- (a) sections 458[^f00115] or 514[^f00116] of the 1996 Act (in relation to boarding fees for pupils at a maintained school); and
- (b) section 514(2) of the 1996 Act or section 10A[^f00117] of the 2010 Act (in relation to boarding fees for pupils at Academies or alternative provision Academies).

**12.**[^key-aa3cb8cf3dfa3fa75f603c0bf38e7105] Expenditure in connection with the local authority’s functions under section 47A of the 1998 Act (establishment and maintenance of, and consultation with, schools forums).

**13.**[^key-bd823715358bee51823a9f577f9883f3] Expenditure on pay arrears due to staff employed at maintained schools and other staff whose salaries are met from the schools budget, and expenditure on the costs of financing payment of such arrears, where the expenditure is not chargeable to a maintained school’s budget share under the terms of the local authority’s scheme.

### Part 3 — Other central education expenditure

#### 14. Asset management[^key-cac3558f4fd089b300f364025ce0f032]

Expenditure in connection with education functions in relation to the following areas of expenditure—

- (a) management of the local authority’s capital programme including preparation and review of an asset management plan and negotiation and management of private finance transactions; and
- (b) landlord responsibilities of the local authority (including in relation to land leased to Academy schools and alternative provision Academies),

which is not within the description of expenditure at paragraph 78 of this Schedule.

#### 15. Strategic management[^key-8c64b50f0d10c7a5773bbcdf473a090e]

Expenditure on education functions related to—

- (a) functions of the director of children’s services and the personal staff of the director;
- (b) planning for the education service as a whole;
- (c) authorisation and monitoring of expenditure which is not met from the budget shares of schools maintained by the local authority, and all related financial administration;
- (d) the formulation and review of the methods of allocating resources to schools maintained by the local authority, and other bodies;
- (e) internal audit and other tasks necessary for the discharge of the local authority’s chief finance officer’s responsibilities under section 151 of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00118] in so far as the responsibilities do not relate to maintained schools; and
- (f) the preparation and review of plans involving collaboration with other local authority services or with public or voluntary bodies.

#### 16. Other functions[^key-fed410b0abc662a4d24efb3ef49a337b]

Expenditure arising from the local authority’s functions under Chapter 2 of Part 6 of the 1996 Act (school attendance).

**17.**[^key-048380425c90fab80e84e16243bcc122] Expenditure in connection with the local authority’s functions in relation to the standing advisory council on religious education constituted by the local authority under section 390 of the 1996 Act[^f00119] or on the reconsideration and preparation of an agreed syllabus of religious education in accordance with Schedule 31 to the 1996 Act[^f00120].

**18.**[^key-28f7b63fbdc2be19b7b823a0934c86fa] Expenditure in connection with powers and duties performed under Part 2 of the Children and Young Persons Act 1933[^f00121] (enforcement of, and power to make byelaws in relation to, restrictions on the employment of children).

**19.**[^key-84a13ace5a2ff9b703b1485c49dbe49b] Expenditure in relation to consultation and functions preparatory to consultation with or by governing bodies, pupils, or with other interested bodies in connection with education functions, which is not within the description of expenditure at paragraph 68 of this Schedule.

**20.**[^key-fdedbea100b23781022d55c376565169] Expenditure on the local authority’s functions in relation to the exclusion of pupils from schools.

**21.**[^key-e2aa1e1de09d9479626ece947af9e1fb] Expenditure in relation to provision of information to or at the request of the Crown and the provision of other information which the local authority is under a duty to make available, which is not within the description of expenditure at paragraph 70 of this Schedule.

**22.**[^key-e293655c77c1de7a48b1d8bfdb4d1142] Expenditure on education functions related to revenue budget preparation, preparation of information on income and expenditure relating to education for incorporation into the local authority’s annual statement of accounts, and the external audit of grant claims and returns relating to education, that is not within the description of expenditure at paragraph 75 of this Schedule.

**23.**[^key-96a48118e1825cc96310b924c6432ce7] Expenditure in connection with the local authority’s duty under regulation 9A of the School Information (England) Regulations 2008[^f00122].

**24.**[^key-3fb91ea15a969dbf09985dfc79f2dba4] Expenditure in relation to 42.5% of the total employer contribution to the pension costs for—

- (a) members of the teachers’ pension scheme to which the Teachers’ Pensions Regulations 2010[^f00123] apply; and
- (b) members of the Teachers’ Pension Scheme 2015 established under regulation 2 of the Teachers’ Pension Scheme Regulations 2014[^f00124],

who are employed by the local authority but not employed to work in a maintained school under the direct management of the head teacher or governing body.

### Part 4 — Central early years expenditure

- (a) on such provision in a maintained school;
- (b) on relevant early years provision;
- (c) on community early years provision; and

**26.**[^key-f154c911a19ab51c45983c0f10e1203e] Expenditure on determining—

### Part 5 — Children and young people with high needs

**28.**[^key-81c24e8ad8691c5ba576ce0d80b61a3c] Expenditure in respect of pupils with special educational needs in primary and secondary schools, excluding expenditure—

- (b) in respect of pupils in places which the local authority has reserved for children with special educational needs; or
