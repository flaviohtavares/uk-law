# The School and Early Years Finance (England) Regulations 2026, section 6

> Text as at 2026-02-11.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Initial determination of a local authority’s schools budget[^c-ee5fd99cab47a444e2541be8af94f6b3]

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority must, not later than 28th February 2026—

- (a) make an initial determination of its schools budget; and
- (b) give notice of that determination to the governing bodies of the schools which it maintains.
