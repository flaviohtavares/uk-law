# The School and Early Years Finance (England) Regulations 2026, section 18

> Text as at 2026-02-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Low prior attainment factor[^key-a763c4e3e6302c3badd0b70efebe9f9f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In determining budget shares for primary and secondary schools (other than special schools, pupil referral units and nursery schools), excluding any amount to be allocated in respect of nursery classes or community early years provision in those schools, a local authority must ascertain and take into account in its formula the following factors.

(2) An amount in relation to—

- (a) the number of pupils in Reception and Years 1 to 6 who did not reach, or are treated as not having reached, the expected level of development in the early years foundation stage profile, calculated in accordance with paragraphs (3) and (4); and
- (b) the number of pupils in Years 7 to 11 who did not achieve, or are treated as not having achieved, the expected standard at key stage 2, calculated in accordance with paragraphs (5) and (6).

(3) In relation to pupils in Reception and Years 1 to 6, a single per pupil amount based on the total of—

- (a) the number of pupils in Years 1, 2, 3 and 4 who did not reach the expected level of development; and
- (b) where a school has pupils in Reception or in Years 5 or 6, a proxy number representing pupils who have not, or have not yet, been assessed under the early years foundation stage profile[^f00059].

(4) The proxy number referred to in paragraph (3)(b) is calculated—

- (a) in relation to Year 6 pupils, by using the formula A x (B/C), where—
  - (i) A is the number of Year 6 pupils at the school on 2nd October 2025;
  - (ii) B is the total number of pupils in Years 1 to 6 at the school on 3rd October 2024 who did not reach, or are treated as not having reached, the expected level of development, as recorded on the proforma tool; and
  - (iii) C is the total number of pupils in Years 1 to 6 at the school on 3rd October 2024, as recorded on the proforma tool;
- (b) in relation to Year 6 pupils where the data referred to in paragraphs (ii) and (iii) of sub-paragraph (a) is not available (for any reason), by using the formula A x (B/C), where—
  - (i) A is the number of Year 6 pupils at the school on 2nd October 2025;
  - (ii) B is the total number of Year 6 pupils at maintained schools and Academy schools in the local authority’s area on 3rd October 2024 who did not reach the expected level of development; and
  - (iii) C is the total number of Year 6 pupils at maintained schools and Academy schools in the local authority’s area on 3rd October 2024; or
- (c) in relation to Year 6 pupils—
  - (i) where the data referred to in paragraphs (ii) and (iii) of sub-paragraph (a) is not available (for any reason); and
  - (ii) where the local authority considers that applying the formula A x (B/C) to the data referred to in paragraphs (ii) and (iii) of sub-paragraph (b) would lead to a disproportionate or unfair allocation of resources in relation to that school,

  the local authority may vary that formula in its application to that school;
- (d) in relation to Year 5 pupils at a school which has Year 4 pupils, by using the formula A x (B/C), where—
  - (i) A is the number of Year 5 pupils at the school on 2nd October 2025;
  - (ii) B is the number of Year 4 pupils at the school on that date who did not reach the expected level of development; and
  - (iii) C is the total number of Year 4 pupils at the school on that date;
- (e) in relation to Year 5 pupils at a school which has no pupils in Year 4, by using the formula A x (B/C), where—
  - (i) A is the number of Year 5 pupils at the school on 2nd October 2025;
  - (ii) B is the total number of pupils in Years 1 to 6 at the school on 3rd October 2024 who did not reach, or are treated as not having reached, the expected level of development, as recorded on the proforma tool; and
  - (iii) C is the total number of pupils in Years 1 to 6 at the school on 3rd October 2024, as recorded on the proforma tool;
- (f) in relation to Year 5 pupils at a school which has no pupils in Year 4 and where the data referred to in paragraphs (ii) and (iii) of sub-paragraph (e) is not available (for any reason), by using the formula A x (B/C), where—
  - (i) A is the number of Year 5 pupils at the school on 2nd October 2025;
  - (ii) B is the total number of Year 4 pupils at maintained schools and Academy schools in the local authority’s area on that date who did not reach the expected level of development; and
  - (iii) C is the total number of Year 4 pupils at maintained schools and Academy schools in the local authority’s area on that date; or
- (g) in relation to Year 5 pupils—
  - (i) at a school which has no pupils in Year 4;
  - (ii) where the data referred to in paragraphs (ii) and (iii) of sub-paragraph (e) is not available (for any reason); and
  - (iii) where the local authority considers that applying the formula A x (B/C) using the data referred to in paragraphs (ii) and (iii) of sub-paragraph (f) would lead to a disproportionate or unfair allocation of resources in relation to an individual school,

  the local authority may vary that formula in its application to that school;
- (h) in relation to pupils in Reception at a school which has pupils in any of Years 1 to 6, by using the formula A x (B/C), where—
  - (i) A is the number of Reception pupils at the school on 2nd October 2025;
  - (ii) B is the total number of pupils in Years 1 to 6 at the school on that date who did not reach, or are treated as not having reached, the expected level of development; and
  - (iii) C is the total number of pupils in Years 1 to 6 at the school on that date; or
- (i) in relation to pupils in Reception at a school which has no pupils in any of Years 1 to 6, by using the formula A x (B/C), where—
  - (i) A is the number of Reception pupils at the school on 2nd October 2025;
  - (ii) B is the total number of pupils in Years 1 to 6 at maintained schools and Academy schools in the local authority’s area on that date who did not reach, or are treated as not having reached, the expected level of development; and
  - (iii) C is the total number of pupils in Years 1 to 6 at maintained schools and Academy schools in the local authority’s area on that date.

(5) In relation to pupils in Years 7 to 11, a single per pupil amount based on the total of—

- (a) in relation to pupils in each of Years 7, 8, 9 and 10 the number produced by using the formula X x A where—
  - (i) A is the number of pupils in, as the case may be, Year 7, 8, 9 or 10 who did not achieve the expected standard; and
  - (ii) X is—
    - (aa) for Year 10 pupils, 0.54;
    - (bb) for Year 9 pupils, 0.56;
    - (cc) for Year 8 pupils, 0.58; and
    - (dd) for Year 7 pupils, 0.60.
- (b) in relation to pupils in Year 11, a proxy number calculated in accordance with paragraph (6).

(6) The proxy number referred to in paragraph (5)(b) is calculated in relation to pupils in Year 11 using the formula (D x (E/F)) x 0.54, where—

- (a) in relation to Year 11 pupils at a school which has Year 10 pupils—
  - (i) D is the total number of Year 11 pupils at the school on 2nd October 2025;
  - (ii) E is the number of Year 10 pupils at the school on that date who did not achieve the expected standard; and
  - (iii) F is the total number of Year 10 pupils at the school on that date;
- (b) in relation to Year 11 pupils at a school which has no Year 10 pupils—
  - (i) D is the total number of Year 11 pupils at the school on 2nd October 2025;
  - (ii) E is the number of Year 10 pupils at the school on 3rd October 2024 who did not achieve, or are treated as not having achieved, the expected standard, as recorded on the proforma tool; and
  - (iii) F is the total number of Year 10 pupils at the school on 3rd October 2024;
- (c) in relation to Year 11 pupils at a school which has no Year 10 pupils and where the data referred to in paragraphs (ii) and (iii) of sub-paragraph (b) is (for any reason) not available—
  - (i) D is the total number of Year 11 pupils at the school on 2nd October 2025;
  - (ii) E is the total number of Year 10 pupils at maintained schools and Academy schools in the local authority’s area on that date who did not achieve the expected standard; and
  - (iii) F is the total number of Year 10 pupils at maintained schools and Academy schools in the local authority’s area on that date;
- (d) in relation to Year 11 pupils at a school—
  - (i) which has no pupils in Year 10;
  - (ii) where the data referred to in paragraphs (ii) and (iii) of sub-paragraph (b) is not available (for any reason); and
  - (iii) where the local authority considers that applying the formula (D x (E/F)) x 0.54 using the data referred to in paragraphs (ii) and (iii) of sub-paragraph (c) would lead to a disproportionate or unfair allocation of resources in relation to an individual school,

  the local authority may vary that formula in its application to that school.

(7) In this regulation—

- (a) “**the 2019 ARA**” means the document entitled “Key stage 2 Assessment and Reporting Arrangements (ARA)” published by the Secretary of State in October 2018[^f00060] containing provision made under article 11 of the Education (National Curriculum) (Key Stage 2 Assessment Arrangements) (England) Order 2003[^f00061] (“**the 2003 Order**”), made under section 87(3) of the 2002 Act (establishment of the National Curriculum for England);
- (b) “**the 2022 ARA**” means the document entitled “Key stage 2 assessment and reporting arrangements” published by the Secretary of State in October 2021 and updated in November 2021[^f00062] containing provision made under article 11 of the 2003 Order;
- (c) “**the 2023 ARA**” means the document entitled “Key stage 2 assessment and reporting arrangements” published by the Secretary of State in October 2022 and updated in March 2023[^f00063] containing provision made under article 11 of the 2003 Order;
- (d) “**the 2024 ARA**” means the document entitled “Key stage 2 assessment and reporting arrangements” published by the Secretary of State in October 2023[^f00064] containing provision made under article 11 of the 2003 Order;
- (e) “**the 2025 ARA**” means the document entitled “2026 key stage 2 assessment and reporting arrangements” published by the Secretary of State in October 2024 and updated on 29th September 2025[^f00065] containing provision made under article 11 of the 2003 Order;
- (f) “**Academy school**” has the meaning given by section 1A of the 2010 Act, but with the exclusion of special Academies;
- (g) “**the early years foundation stage profile**” means the assessment usually completed in the summer term of the pupil’s Reception year, which is referred to—
  - (i) in relation to references to pupils in Year 1, in section 2 of the document entitled “Early years foundation stage statutory framework for group and school-based providers” published by the Secretary of State on 11th October 2024[^f00066];
  - (ii) in relation to references to pupils in Year 2, in section 2 of the document entitled “Early years foundation stage statutory framework for group and school-based providers” published by the Secretary of State on 8th December 2023[^f00067]; or
  - (iii) in relation to references to pupils in Years 3, 4 and 5, in section 2 of the document entitled “Statutory Framework for the early years foundation stage” published by the Secretary of State on 31st March 2021[^f00068];
- (h) “**the expected level of development**” in relation to pupils in Years 1 to 6, refers to the expected level of development in relation to the early learning goals set out in the early years foundation stage profile;
- (i) a reference to “**the number of pupils who do not achieve the expected standard**” in relation to pupils in Years 7 to 11, refers to the total of—
  - (i) the number of pupils who, in the mathematics or English reading tests at key stage 2, achieved a scaled score of 99 or lower[^f00069] under the 2019 ARA, the 2022 ARA, the 2023 ARA, the 2024 ARA or the 2025 ARA; or
  - (ii) the number of pupils, other than those who fall within paragraph (i), who were given a teacher assessment standard of “working towards the expected standard”, or lower, in English grammar, punctuation and spelling tests under the 2019 ARA, the 2022 ARA, the 2023 ARA, the 2024 ARA or the 2025 ARA;
- (j) “**maintained school**” has the meaning given by section 20(7) of the 1998 Act, but with the exclusion of special schools;
- (k) “**the proforma tool**” means the authority proforma tool provided by the Secretary of State to local authorities to enable them to detail their schools block funding formulae for 2025 to 2026[^f00070].
