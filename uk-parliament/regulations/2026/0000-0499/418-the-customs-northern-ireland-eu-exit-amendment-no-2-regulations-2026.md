# The Customs (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to amend [S.I. 2026/393](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/393) prior to that instrument coming into force and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2026 No. 418

Customs

The Customs (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026

Made

at 9.15 a.m. on 16th April 2026

Laid before the House of Commons

at 1.00 p.m. on 16th April 2026

Coming into force

19th April 2026

The Treasury make these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 30B(3) and (4)(a), 32(7) and (8)(a), (b) and (c), 40(7)(a), (b) and (c) and 40B(2), (3)(b) and (5) of the Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Customs (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 19th April 2026.

## 2. Amendment of the Customs (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

In regulation 1 (citation and commencement) of the Customs (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2026[^f00002] for “20th April” substitute “25th May”.

Gen Kitchen

Christian Wakeford

Two of the Lords Commissioners of His Majesty’s Treasury

At 9.15 a.m. on 16th April 2026

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Customs (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2026 ([S.I. 2026/393](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/393)) to change the coming into force date of those Regulations from 20th April 2026 to 25th May 2026.

A Tax Information and Impact Note (TIIN) has not been produced for this instrument but a TIIN covering the Customs (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2026 will be published on the GOV.UK website at https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/tax-information-and-impact-notes-tiins.
