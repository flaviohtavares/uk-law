The Secretary of State makes these Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36(2A) of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00001].

The Secretary of State has consulted the Electoral Commission in accordance with section 7(1) and (2)(d) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent, saving and interpretation

(1) These Rules may be cited as the Greater London Authority Elections (Amendment) Rules 2026 and come into force on 15th May 2026.

(2) These Rules extend to England and Wales only.

(3) These Rules do not apply to an Authority election for which the notice of election[^f00003] is published before the day on which these Rules come into force.

(4) In paragraph (3), “**Authority election**” has the meaning given in rule 2(1) of the 2007 Rules.

(5) In these Rules, “**the 2007 Rules**” means the Greater London Authority Elections Rules 2007[^f00004].

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Greater London Authority Elections Rules 2007 ([S.I. 2007/3541](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/3541)), which contain the rules governing the conduct of elections of constituency and London members of the London Assembly and the Mayor of London.

Rule 2 of these Rules amends Schedules 4 and 8 to the Greater London Authority Elections Rules 2007, which contain modifications of the standard conduct rules in circumstances where the ballot papers used at the election are to be counted manually (instead of with the use of an electronic counting system). The amendments allow for verification and counting of ballot papers in each Assembly constituency to be undertaken in more than one location within the constituency and at different times.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
