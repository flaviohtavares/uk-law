# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Household Tumble Dryers) Regulations 2026, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2026/318
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Ecodesign requirements[^c-a74aaf10f0e2fc6bbd1becf317ab250f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A household tumble dryer must comply with the following ecodesign requirements when it is placed on the market or put into service[^f00009]—

- (a) the requirements in Schedule 2, or
- (b) in the case of a multi-drum household tumble dryer, the requirements in Schedule 2 read together with Schedule 3.

(2) A manufacturer, importer or authorised representative of a household tumble dryer must comply with Parts 5 and 6 of Schedule 2.
