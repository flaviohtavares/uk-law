# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Household Tumble Dryers) Regulations 2026, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2026/318
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15. Ecodesign requirements - transitional provisions[^c-c6223f09344a0e0d75310551da90dd66][^c-6cb0bc5f478bd52b90e9ad4f96cd020a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-3c1fa3f1cfcdd3305880dbcc654fce61] The requirements of Part 2 are deemed to be met in relation to a household tumble dryer which—

- (a) is placed on the market or put into service in the period—
  - (i) beginning with 10th April 2026, and
  - (ii) ending with 18th January 2027, and
- (b) complies with Regulation (EU) 932/2012 as it had effect immediately before 10th April 2026.

(2) Regulation 6(6) is, in the period beginning with 10th April 2026 and ending with 18th January 2027, to be read as if the reference to Schedule 5 were a reference to Schedule 5 or Schedule 14.

(3) Paragraph 6(b) of Schedule 2 is, in the period beginning with 10th April 2026 and ending with 8th May 2027, to be read as if the reference to the power consumption in off mode not exceeding 0.30 W were a reference to the power consumption in off mode not exceeding 0.50 W.
