- (a) In [Schedule 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/1095/schedule/4), in [paragraph 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/1095/schedule/4/paragraph/3) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/1095/schedule/4/paragraph/3) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/1095/schedule/4/paragraph/3) for the words from “Commission [Regulation (EU) No 932/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0932)” to “household tumble driers” substitute “the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Household Tumble Dryers) Regulations 2026”;

(4)[^c-be63924aa5edb15250f10c487450ae64] In [Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 392/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/392/contents) of 1 March 2012 supplementing [Directive 2010/30/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2010/30/contents) with regard to energy labelling of household tumble driers[^f00020], in Article 3(b), for[^c-c4306e3c8f87f9bfdca62a6afc1f124f] “available” substitute

> available.
>
> Suppliers may make the information from the product fiche available for inspection on a publicly accessible website. Where suppliers make the information available in that manner, they must ensure the product fiche in printed form is made available to dealers on request.
