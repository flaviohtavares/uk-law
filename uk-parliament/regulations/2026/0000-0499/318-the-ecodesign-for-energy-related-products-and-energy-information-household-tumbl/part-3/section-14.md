# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Household Tumble Dryers) Regulations 2026, section 14

## 14. Verification procedure for compliance purposes[^key-922add2f27a22701b8ae7903a3579cd1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The market surveillance authority[^f00017] must apply the following verification procedure when verifying compliance with this Part—

- (a) the procedure set out in Schedule 13, or
- (b) in the case of a multi-drum household tumble dryer, the procedure set out in Schedule 13 read together with Schedule 11.
