The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [sections 365](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/section/365) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/section/365) and [(4)(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/section/365) and [402](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/section/402) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/section/402) of the [Communications Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/contents)[^f00001], including as extended by—

- (a) the [Communications (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Order 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3195/contents)[^f00002];

The Treasury has consented to the making of these Regulations in accordance with [section 365](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/section/365) [(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/section/365) of the [Communications Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/contents).

## 1. Citation, commencement, effect and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Communications (Television Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st April 2026.

(3) These Regulations have effect in respect of TV licences issued on or after 1st April 2026.

(4) These Regulations extend to—

- (a) England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland,
- (b) the Channel Islands, except regulation 4(43) and (44) and Schedule 2, and
- (c) the Isle of Man.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Communications (Television Licensing) Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/692](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/692)) by providing for increases in—

- (a) the amount payable for most categories of TV licence;
- (b) the amount of each instalment for certain categories of TV licence which are payable in instalments.

In summary—

- (a) the amount usually payable for a colour TV licence is increased from £174.50 to £180.00 and that for a black and white TV licence is increased from £58.50 to £60.50 (see regulations 3 and 5);
- (b) the instalments for a premium instalment TV licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £179.50 to £185.00 (see regulation 4(1) and (2));
- (c) the instalments for a budget instalment TV licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £174.50 to £180.00 (see regulation 4(1) and (3) to (9));
- (d) the instalments for an easy entry TV licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £174.50 to £180.00 (see regulation 4(1) and (10) to (42));
- (e) the instalments for a simple payment TV licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £174.50 to £180.00 (see regulation 4(1), (43) and (44)).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
