# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme (Amendment) (Extension to Maritime Activities) Order 2026, SCHEDULE 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 3 — AMENDMENTS TO THE GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS TRADING SCHEME AUCTIONING REGULATIONS 2021

Article 6

### 1. Introductory

The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Auctioning Regulations 2021[^f00014] are amended in accordance with this Schedule.

### 2. Amendments to regulation 9 (Annual volumes of allowances)

(1) Regulation 9 is amended as follows.

(2) In the table in paragraph (10), in column 2 (Base)—

- (a) for “79,059,690” substitute “80,063,992”;
- (b) for “70,127,996” substitute “72,317,337”;
- (c) for “53,498,502” substitute “55,618,096”;
- (d) for “50,918,572” substitute “52,964,678”;
- (e) for “49,320,164” substitute “51,284,366”.

### 3. Amendments to regulation 10 (Adjustments of the auction calendar)

(1) Regulation 10 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1), after sub-paragraph (g), insert—

> - (h) the addition of an activity to article 16(2) of the Trading Scheme Order.

.

### 4. Amendments to regulation 16 (Persons eligible to apply for admission to bid)

(1) Regulation 16 is amended as follows.

(2) For paragraph (1)(a), substitute—

> - (a) an operator having an operator holding account, bidding on its own account, including any parent undertaking, subsidiary undertaking or affiliated undertaking forming part of the same group as the operator;
> - (aa) an aircraft operator having an aircraft operator holding account, bidding on its own account, including any parent undertaking, subsidiary undertaking or affiliated undertaking forming part of the same group as the aircraft operator;
> - (ab) a maritime operator having a maritime operator holding account, bidding on its own account, including any parent undertaking, subsidiary undertaking or affiliated undertaking forming part of the same group as the maritime operator;

.

### 5. Amendments to regulation 25 (Appointment requirements applicable to any auction platform)

(1) Regulation 25 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (8) after “Trading Scheme Order)” insert “or maritime operators (within the meaning of paragraph 3 of Schedule 2A to the Trading Scheme Order)”.

### 6. Amendments to the Schedule (List of elements referred to in regulation 18(5))

(1) The Schedule is amended as follows.

(2) After paragraph 10, insert—

> **10A.** For maritime operators, the emissions monitoring plan issued under paragraph 10 of Schedule 2A to the Trading Scheme Order.

.
