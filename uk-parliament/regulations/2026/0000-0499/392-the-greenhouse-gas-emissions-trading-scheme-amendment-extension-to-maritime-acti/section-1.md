# The Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme (Amendment) (Extension to Maritime Activities) Order 2026, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme (Amendment) (Extension to Maritime Activities) Order 2026 and comes into force on 1st July 2026.

(2) This Order extends to the whole of the United Kingdom.

(3) In this Order references to the “**2020 Order**” are to the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020[^f00002].
