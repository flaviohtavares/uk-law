# The Local Authorities (Changes to Years of Ordinary Elections) (England) (Revocation) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 142

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The Local Authorities (Changes to Years of Ordinary Elections) (England) (Revocation) Order 2026

Made

at 11.24 a.m. on 17th February 2026

Laid before Parliament

at 2.30 p.m. on 17th February 2026

Coming into force

18th February 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 87(1) and (3) and 105(2) and (3) of the Local Government Act 2000[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Changes to Years of Ordinary Elections) (England) (Revocation) Order 2026 and comes into force the day after the day on which it is laid before Parliament.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Revocation

The Local Authorities (Changes to Years of Ordinary Elections) (England) Order 2026[^f00002] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government

Taylor of Stevenage

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government

at 11.24 a.m. on 17th February 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Local Authorities (Changes to Years of Ordinary Elections) (England) Order 2026 ([S.I. 2026/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/96)) made provision for the postponement of local government elections in England in 2026, postponing elections in 30 councils from May 2026 until May 2027.

Article 2 of this Order revokes the Local Authorities (Changes to Years of Ordinary Elections) (England) Order 2026, meaning that local elections to those councils will proceed in May 2026.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
