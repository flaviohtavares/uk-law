# The Merchant Shipping (Monitoring, Reporting and Verification of Carbon Dioxide Emissions) (Revocation) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 245

RETAINED EU LAW REFORM

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Merchant Shipping (Monitoring, Reporting and Verification of Carbon Dioxide Emissions) (Revocation) Regulations 2026

Sifting requirements satisfied

4th February 2026

Made

9th March 2026

Laid before Parliament

12th March 2026

Coming into force

3rd April 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 14(1) and 20(1)(b) of the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (“**the 2023 Act**”)[^f00001].

The Secretary of State is a relevant national authority for the purpose of section 14(1) of the 2023 Act[^f00002].

The requirements of paragraph 6(2) of Schedule 5 to the 2023 Act (relating to the appropriate Parliamentary procedure for these Regulations) have been satisfied.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Monitoring, Reporting and Verification of Carbon Dioxide Emissions) (Revocation) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the 22nd day after the day on which they are laid.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Revocations

The following instruments are revoked—

- (a) [Regulation (EU) 2015/757](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/0757) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2015 on the monitoring, reporting and verification of carbon dioxide emissions from maritime transport, and amending [Directive 2009/16/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0016)[^f00003];
- (b) The Merchant Shipping (Monitoring, Reporting and Verification of Carbon Dioxide Emissions) and the Port State Control (Amendment) Regulations 2017[^f00004];
- (c) The Merchant Shipping (Monitoring, Reporting and Verification of Carbon Dioxide Emissions) (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018[^f00005];
- (d) Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2016/1927](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2016/1927) of 4 November 2016 on templates for monitoring plans, emissions reports and documents of compliance pursuant to [Regulation (EU) 2015/757](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/0757) of the European Parliament and of the Council on monitoring, reporting and verification of carbon dioxide emissions from maritime transport[^f00006];
- (e) Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) 2016/2071](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2016/2071) of 22 September 2016 amending [Regulation (EU) 2015/757](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/0757) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the methods for monitoring carbon dioxide emissions and the rules for monitoring other relevant information[^f00007].

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of the power in section 14(1) of the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act [2023 (c. 28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/28). This is a power to revoke secondary retained EU law without replacing it.

No impact assessment has been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen. Instead, a de minimis assessment has been prepared and has been published, with an Explanatory Memorandum, alongside these Regulations at www.legislation.gov.uk.
