# The Sea Fisheries (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 105

RETAINED EU LAW REFORM

SEA FISHERIES

The Sea Fisheries (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made

5th February 2026

Laid before Parliament

6th February 2026

Coming into force

27th February 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36(1)(b) and (c), (4)(e) and (6)(a) and (c) of the Fisheries Act 2020[^f00001] (“**the 2020 Act**”) and section 15 of the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023[^f00002] (“**the 2023 Act**”).

The Secretary of State has, where necessary, obtained the consent of the Scottish Ministers, the Welsh Ministers and the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs in Northern Ireland (“**DAERA**”) to the making of these Regulations in accordance with section 40 of the 2020 Act[^f00003].

The Secretary of State has consulted the Scottish Ministers, the Welsh Ministers, DAERA and such other persons likely to be affected by the measures contained in these Regulations as the Secretary of State considers appropriate in accordance with section 41(1) of the 2020 Act.

The Secretary of State is a relevant national authority[^f00004] for the purposes of section 15(1) of the 2023 Act.

The Secretary of State is satisfied that there have been developments in scientific understanding and that it is appropriate to make these Regulations to take account of those developments.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sea Fisheries (Amendment) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 27th February 2026.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Council [Regulation (EU) 2020/123](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2020/0123) fixing for 2020 the fishing opportunities for certain fish stocks and groups of fish stocks, applicable in Union waters and, for Union fishing vessels, in certain non-Union waters (Council [Regulation (EU) 2020/123](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2020/0123)), which is secondary assimilated law within the meaning of section 21(1) of the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act [2023 (c. 28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/28), as read with section 12(2) of that Act.

These Regulations make adjustments to the number of European seabass that may be caught within British fishery limits, as specified in Article 10 of Council [Regulation (EU) 2020/123](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2020/0123), for recreational fisheries.

These Regulations also remove picked dogfish from the list of prohibited species contained in Article 16 of Council [Regulation (EU) 2020/123](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2020/0123). It is intended that any fishing restrictions on picked dogfish will be managed through vessel licence conditions.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the public, private or voluntary sector is foreseen. A de minimis assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the cost of business has been prepared and is available from the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, 2 Marsham Street, London, SW1P 4DF.
