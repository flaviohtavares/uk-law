# The Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2026, section 75

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 75. Withdrawal of a supplementary plan

(1) A relevant plan-making authority may withdraw a supplementary plan at any time before it is adopted under section 15EA of the Act.

(2) Where a relevant plan-making authority withdraw a supplementary plan under paragraph (1), the authority must as soon as is reasonably practicable after withdrawing it—

- (a) make a statement of that fact available in accordance with regulation 88,
- (b) send, to each person or body who were invited to make representations under regulation 70 (consultation on the proposed supplementary plan), notification that the supplementary plan has been withdrawn, and
- (c) cease to make all documents relating to the withdrawn supplementary plan (other than the statement mentioned in sub-paragraph (a)) available in accordance with regulation 88.
