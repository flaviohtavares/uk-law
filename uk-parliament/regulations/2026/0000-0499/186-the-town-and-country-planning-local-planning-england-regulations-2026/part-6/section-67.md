# The Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2026, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Interpretation of Part 6

In this Part—

- “**Mayoral general consultation bodies**” means the following—
  - (a) voluntary bodies, some or all of whose activities benefit all or part of Greater London[^f00075];
  - (b) bodies which represent the interests of persons in Greater London who share a protected characteristic under the Equality Act 2010[^f00076];
  - (c) bodies which represent the interests of persons carrying on business in Greater London;
- “**Mayoral specific consultation bodies**” means the following—
  - (a) Active Travel England[^f00077];
  - (b) any person—
    - (i) to whom the electronic communications code applies as a result of a direction given under [section 106](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/section/106) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/section/106) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/section/106) of the [Communications Act 2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21/contents), and
    - (ii) who owns or controls electronic communications apparatus situated in Greater London;
  - (c) Canal & River Trust[^f00078];
  - (d) the Civil Aviation Authority[^f00079];
  - (e) the Coal Authority[^f00080];
  - (f) a combined authority established under [section 103](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/section/103) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/section/103) of the [Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/contents) any part of whose area is in or adjoins Greater London;
  - (g) a combined county authority established under [section 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/section/9) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/section/9) of the [Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/contents) any part of whose area is in or adjoins Greater London;
  - (h) the English Sports Council[^f00081];
  - (i) the Environment Agency[^f00082];
  - (j) the Forestry Commission[^f00083];
  - (k) a highway authority within the meaning of [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/66/section/1) of the [Highways Act 1980](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/66/contents) any part of whose area is in or adjoins Greater London (including the Secretary of State, where the Secretary of State is the highways authority);
  - (l) the Historic Buildings and Monuments Commission for England[^f00084];
  - (m) the Homes and Communities Agency[^f00085];
  - (n) if it exercises functions in Greater London—
    - (i) an integrated care board established under [Chapter A3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/41/part/2/chapter/A3) of [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/41/part/2) of the [National Health Service Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/41/contents),
    - (ii) a person to whom a licence has been granted under [section 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/29/section/6) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/29/section/6) [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/29/section/6) or [(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/29/section/6) of the [Electricity Act 1989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/29/contents),
    - (iii) a person to whom a licence has been granted under [section 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/44/section/7) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/44/section/7) of the [Gas Act 1986](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/44/contents),
    - (iv) a sewerage undertaker appointed under [section 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/56/section/6) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/56/section/6) of the [Water Industry Act 1991](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/56/contents), and
    - (v) a water undertaker appointed under [section 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/56/section/6) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/56/section/6) of the [Water Industry Act 1991](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/56/contents),
  - (o) an Integrated Transport Authority for an integrated transport area within the meaning in section 77(1) of the Local Transport Act 2008[^f00086] which is in or adjoins Greater London;
  - (p) a local policing body any part of whose area is in or adjoins Greater London;
  - (q) the Marine Management Organisation[^f00087];
  - (r) Natural England[^f00088];
  - (s) a neighbourhood forum any part of whose area is in or adjoins Greater London;
  - (t) Network Rail Infrastructure Limited[^f00089];
  - (u) the Office for Nuclear Regulation[^f00090];
  - (v) the Office of Rail and Road[^f00091];
  - (w) a parish council any part of whose area is in or adjoins Greater London;
  - (x) a planning authority any part of whose area adjoins Greater London;
  - (y) Transport for London[^f00092];
  - (z) where the Mayor of London is not the responsible authority for a local nature recovery strategy that relates to all or part of Greater London, the responsible authority for the local nature recovery strategy appointed under [section 105](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/30/section/105) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/30/section/105) of the [Environment Act 2021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/30/contents);
  - (z1) where the persons specified in regulation 70(4)(e) and (f) are not responsible for discharging education functions in their area, the local authority with responsibility for discharging education functions.

[^f00091]: Footnote: The Office of Rail and Road was established by section 15 of the Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003; section 15 was amended by [S.I. 2015/1682](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1682).

[^f00092]: Footnote: Transport for London was established by section 154 of the Greater London Authority Act 1999.
