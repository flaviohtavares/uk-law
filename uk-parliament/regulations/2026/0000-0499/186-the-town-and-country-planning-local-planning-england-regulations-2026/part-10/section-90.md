# The Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2026, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Revocation of Regulations

The Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2012[^f00107] are revoked[^f00108].
