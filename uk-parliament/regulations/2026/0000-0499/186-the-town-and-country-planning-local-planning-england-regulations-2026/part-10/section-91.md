# The Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2026, section 91

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 91. Transitional provisions

The Schedule to these Regulations, which contains transitional provisions in relation to commencing preparation of a local plan or a minerals and waste plan document under the provisions of the Act and these Regulations for the first time, has effect.
