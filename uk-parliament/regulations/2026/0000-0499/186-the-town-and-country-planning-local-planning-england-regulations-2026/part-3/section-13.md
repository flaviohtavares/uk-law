# The Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2026, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Form and content of supplementary plans: general

(1) A supplementary plan must contain—

- (a) the date on which the plan is adopted or approved under Part 2 of the Act, and
- (b) a reasoned justification of the policies contained in the plan.

(2) Where a supplementary plan contains a policy that the relevant plan-making authority[^f00057] consider (in substance) supersedes another policy in the development plan which has effect for the area or a site to which the supplementary plan relates, it must state that fact and identify the superseded policy.

(3) When published on a relevant plan-making authority’s website in accordance with regulation 76(1)(a)(i) and regulation 88(1)(a), a supplementary plan must be in a searchable electronic format.

## Annotations

[^f00057]: Footnote: *See* section 15LH(2) of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 for the definition of *“relevant plan-making authority”* applying to these Regulations.
