# The Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2026, section 89

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 89. Copies of documents

(1) A person may request from the local planning authority, minerals and waste planning authority or Mayor of London (“the relevant body”) a copy of any document made available in accordance with regulation 88.

(2) The relevant body must provide a copy of the document to that person as soon as reasonably practicable after receipt of that person’s request.

(3) The relevant body may make a reasonable charge for a copy of a document—

- (a) provided in accordance with paragraph (2), or
- (b) published as required by or under Part 2 of the Act.
