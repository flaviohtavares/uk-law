# The Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2026, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Direction by the Mayor of London

(1) The time prescribed for the purposes of section 15BA(3)(b) of the Act[^f00055] is the first day after a period of three weeks beginning with the date on which the Mayor of London gives the direction under section 15B(6) or (7) of the Act.

(2) The time prescribed for the purposes of section 15BA(4) of the Act[^f00056] is the period of three weeks beginning with the date on which the Mayor of London gives the direction under section 15B(6) or (7) of the Act.

## Annotations

[^f00055]: Footnote: Including as applied in relation to a minerals and waste plan timetable by section 15BB(4) of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004.
