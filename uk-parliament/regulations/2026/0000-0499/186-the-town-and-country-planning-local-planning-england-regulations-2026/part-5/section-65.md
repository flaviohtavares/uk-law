# The Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2026, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Revocation of a minerals and waste plan document

Where a document which is or forms part of a minerals and waste plan is revoked under section 15G of the Act[^f00073], the minerals and waste planning authority must as soon as is reasonably practicable after the revocation occurs—

- (a) make a statement of that fact available in accordance with regulation 88,
- (b) take such other steps as they consider necessary to draw the revocation of the document which is or forms part of the minerals and waste plan to the attention of persons living or working in their area, and
- (c) cease to make all documents relating to the revoked document (other than the statement mentioned in paragraph (a) and the minerals and waste planning authority’s minerals and waste plan timetable) available in accordance with regulation 88.

## Annotations

[^f00073]: Footnote: Section 15G is applied in relation to a minerals and waste plan by section 15CB(8) of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004; its application in relation to a minerals and waste plan is modified by [S.I. 2026/170](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/170).
