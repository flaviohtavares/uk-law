# The Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 132

Wildlife

The Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (Council Regulation [(EC) No 338/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0338)) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made

12th February 2026

Laid before Parliament

13th February 2026

Coming into force

6th March 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 18(3) and 19(5) of Council Regulation [(EC) No 338/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0338) on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein[^f00001] (“**the Council Regulation**”).

The Secretary of State has consulted the scientific authority[^f00002] in accordance with Article 19(5) of the Council Regulation.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (Council Regulation [(EC) No 338/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0338)) (Amendment) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 6th March 2026.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Amendments to the Annex to Council Regulation [(EC) No 338/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0338)

The Annex to Council Regulation [(EC) No 338/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0338) on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein[^f00003] is amended as follows.

## 3. Amendments to Notes on interpretation of Annexes A, B, C and D

In the section headed “Notes on interpretation of Annexes A, B, C and D”—

- (a) in the annotations following paragraph 12—
  - (i) in annotation #4, in subparagraph (f)—
    - (aa) after “finished products” insert “packaged and ready for retail trade”;
    - (bb) omit “packaged and ready for retail trade and”;
  - (ii) for annotation #10 substitute—

    > - \#10 Designates all parts and derivatives, except finished musical instruments, finished musical instrument accessories and finished musical instrument parts for non-commercial trade only for the purpose of paid and unpaid performance, personal use, display, loan, competition, teaching, appraisal or repair, provided that this does not change the ownership and that such transport is not for sale, transfer or disposal of the specimen outside of the owner’s usual State of residence. Zero quota for wild-harvested specimens (source code W) traded for commercial purposes.

    ;
  - (iii) after annotation #18 insert—

    > - \#19 Designates extracts (including resins, gums and essential oils) and powder, except the following finished products, packaged and ready for retail trade: finished tablets, capsules, pills, perfumes, cosmetics, solutions, emulsions or suspensions (such as infused oils, hydrolates and tinctures), and manufactured incense products (such as incense sticks and incense cones).

    ;
- (b) in the annotations following paragraph 17—
  - (i) for annotation §3 substitute—

    > - §2 Dried and fresh plants, including, where appropriate: leaves, roots/rootstock, stems, seeds/spores, bark and fruits.

    ;
  - (ii) for annotation §4 substitute—

    > - §4 All parts and derivatives, except: (a) seeds and pollen; (b) finished products packaged and ready for retail trade.

    ;
  - (iii) after annotation §4 insert—

    > - §5 All logs, sawn wood, veneer sheets, plywood and transformed wood.

    .

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations implement changes to the Appendices to the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (ratified by the United Kingdom on 2nd August 1976) which were notified to Parties to the Convention under Notification numbers 2025/002, 2025/109 and 2026/005.

Regulations 2 to 7 amend the Annex to the assimilated EU law version of Council Regulation [(EC) No 338/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0338) on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein ([EUR 1997/338](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0338)). That Annex contains two tables, the first setting out Annexes A to C to the Council Regulation, the second setting out Annex D to that Regulation.

No impact assessment has been produced for this instrument as no significant impact on the public, voluntary or private sector is foreseen.
