# The Morgan Offshore Wind Project Generation Assets (Correction) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 441

Infrastructure Planning

The Morgan Offshore Wind Project Generation Assets (Correction) Order 2026

Made

8th April 2026

Coming into force

9th April 2026

The Morgan Offshore Wind Project Generation Assets Order 2025[^f00001] (“the Morgan Offshore Wind Project Generation Assets Order 2025”) which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008[^f00002] (“**the Act**”) contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period[^f00003], the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00004] for the correction of errors and omissions in the Morgan Offshore Wind Project Generation Assets Order 2025.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed each relevant local planning authority[^f00005] for the area in which the land to which the Morgan Offshore Wind Project Generation Assets Order 2025 relates is situated that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to the Act makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Morgan Offshore Wind Project Generation Assets (Correction) Order 2026 and comes into force on 9th April 2026.

## 2. Correction of original order

The Morgan Offshore Wind Project Generation Assets Order 2025 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted, or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

John Wheadon

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning Delivery

Department for Energy Security and Net Zero

8th April 2026

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors in the Morgan Offshore Wind Project Generation Assets Order 2025 ([S.I. 2025/870](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/870)), a development consent order made under the Planning Act 2008, following a request made under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
