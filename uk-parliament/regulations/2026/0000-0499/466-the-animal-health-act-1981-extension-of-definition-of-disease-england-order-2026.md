# The Animal Health Act 1981 (Extension of Definition of Disease) (England) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 466

ANIMALS, ENGLAND

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Animal Health Act 1981 (Extension of Definition of Disease) (England) Order 2026

Made

at 12.24 p.m. on 28th April 2026

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(1)

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 88(2) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Animal Health Act 1981 (Extension of Definition of Disease) (England) Order 2026 and comes into force immediately after it is made.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Extension of the definition of “disease”

For the purposes of the Animal Health Act 1981, as it applies in England, the definition of “disease” in section 88(1) of that Act is extended so as to include swine vesicular disease.

Hayman of Ullock

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

at 12.24 p.m. on 28th April 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends, for the purposes of the Animal Health Act [1981 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/22), the definition of “disease” in section 88(1) of that Act so as to include swine vesicular disease. This Order applies in England only.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
