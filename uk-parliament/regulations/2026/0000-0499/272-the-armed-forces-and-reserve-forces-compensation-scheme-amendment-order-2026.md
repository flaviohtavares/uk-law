# The Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) (Amendment) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

4th March 2026

Laid before Parliament

12th March 2026

Coming into force

6th April 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(2) of the Armed Forces (Pensions and Compensation) Act 2004[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) (Amendment) Order 2026 and comes into force on 6th April 2026.

## 2. Extent

This Order extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 3. Amendment of the Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011

The Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011[^f00002] is amended in accordance with articles 4 to 10.

## 4. Amendment of Article 2

In article 2 (interpretation)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the FTRSPS 2010”;
- (b) omit the definition of “the NRPSPS”;
- (c) insert, in the appropriate places, the following—
  - “**“the FTRS 1997**” means the Full-time Reserve Service Pension Scheme 1997, the rules of which are set out in Schedules 1, 2 and 3 to the Reserve Forces (Full-time Reserve Service Pension Scheme 1997) Regulations 2010[^f00003];”;
  - “**“the NRPS 2011**” means the Non Regular Permanent Staff Pension Scheme, the rules of which are set out in Schedules 1, 3 and 4 to the Reserve Forces Non Regular Permanent Staff (Pension and Attributable Benefits Schemes) Regulations 2011[^f00004];”;
- (d) in the definition of “ill-health pension”—
  - (i) for “FTRSPS 2010” substitute “FTRS 1997”;
  - (ii) for “NRPSPS” substitute “NRPS 2011”.

## 5. Amendment of Article 14

In article 14 (interpretation of Part 3), paragraph 6(b) omit the words from “following” to “paragraph 6(a)”.

## 6. Amendment of Article 24A

In article 24A (award of armed forces independence payment), in paragraph (3), for “£187.45” substitute “£194.60”.

## 7. Amendment of Article 24D

In article 24D (payment of armed forces independence payment in respect of Motability), in paragraph (2), for “£77.05” substitute “£80.00”.

## 8. Amendment of Article 83

In article 83 (additional bereavement grant), in paragraph (5)—

- (a) for “FTRSPS 2010” substitute “FTRS 1997”;
- (b) for “NRRSPS” substitute “NRPS 2011”.

## 9. Amendment of Schedule 2

(3) In paragraph 5, in paragraph (1)(c) of the substituted article 39—

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011 ([S.I. 2011/517](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/517))(“**the Principal Order**”), which provides for benefits to be payable to, or in respect of a person by reason of injury, illness or death caused wholly or partly, by service in the regular or reserve armed forces.

Article 4 updates the definitions for the Full Time Reserve Service Pension Scheme 1997 and the Non Regular Permanent Staff Scheme to align with references in other armed forces legislation.  Articles 8 and 9 update references to these schemes accordingly.

Article 5 amends article 14(6) of the Principal Order to allow all decisions following reconsideration to satisfy Condition B.

Article 6 amends article 24A of the Principal Order to increase the amount of the allowance known as “the armed forces independent payment” which is designed to provide financial support to cover extra costs that a recipient of that benefit may incur as a result of a relevant injury. That amount is increased to £194.60 per week.

Article 7 amends article 24D of the Principal Order to increase the amount that may be paid in settlement of liabilities due under an agreement between a claimant and Motability in respect of the hire or hire purchase of a vehicle. That amount is increased to £80.00 per week.

Article 10 amends Schedule 3 to the Principal Order by specifying additional medical professionals who may diagnose mental disorders for the purposes of the descriptors in Table 3. It inserts a new descriptor into Table 8, to make one temporary award permanent, and also amends Table 10 to increase the relevant tariff amounts.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
