# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions (Amendment) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 101

PENSIONS

The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions (Amendment) Order 2026

Made

3rd February 2026

Laid before Parliament

10th February 2026

Coming into force

6th April 2026

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 3rd day of February 2026

Present,

The King's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty considers it expedient to amend the [Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/606/contents)[^f00001].

ACCORDINGLY, His Majesty, in pursuance of [sections 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/5/section/12) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/5/section/12) and [24](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/5/section/24) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/5/section/24) of the [Social Security (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1977](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/5/contents)[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling Him to do so, is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions (Amendment) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on 6th April 2026.

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## 2. Amendment of the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006

The [Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/606/contents) (“**the principal Order**”) is amended as follows.

## 3. Amendment to Schedule 1 (disablement due to service in the armed forces)

In Schedule 1 to the principal Order—

- (a) for the table in Part 2 substitute the table in Schedule 1 to this Order;
- (b) for tables 1 and 2 in Part 3 substitute tables 1 and 2 in Schedule 2 to this Order; and
- (c) for the table in Part 4 substitute the table in Schedule 3 to this Order.

## 4. Amendment to Schedule 1A (diffuse mesothelioma lump sums)

In Schedule 1A to the principal Order, in paragraph 5(1), for “£140,000” substitute “£185,000”.

## 5. Amendment to Schedule 2 (death due to service in the armed forces)

In Schedule 2 to the principal Order—

- (a) for tables 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 in Part 2 substitute tables 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 in Schedule 4 to this Order; and
- (b) for the table in Part 3 substitute the table in Schedule 5 to this Order.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006 (“**the principal Order**”) ([S.I. 2006/606](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/606)), which makes provision for pensions and other awards in respect of disablement or death due to service before 6th April 2005 in the naval, military and air forces. The principal Order contains the scheme which provides the pensions and other awards.

The Tables in Schedules 1 to 5 to this Order replace tables in Schedules 1 and 2 to the principal Order, in order to increase the rates prescribed for retired pay, pensions, gratuities and allowances in respect of disablement or death due to service in the armed forces.

Article 4 amends paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 1A to the principal Order to increase the amount of the diffuse mesothelioma lump sum.

Some of the instruments referred to in this Order are available online at: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-armed-forces-compensation-scheme-and-service-pensions-order-legislation . Alternatively, copies may be obtained from the Ministry of Defence. Enquiries relating to these instruments may be directed to the Ministry of Defence at the following email address: afcompensation-policy@mod.gov.uk.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
