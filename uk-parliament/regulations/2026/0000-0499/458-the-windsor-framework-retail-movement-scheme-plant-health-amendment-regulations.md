# The Windsor Framework (Retail Movement Scheme: Plant Health) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 458

EXITING THE EUROPEAN UNION

Made

27th April 2026

Laid before Parliament

28th April 2026

Coming into force

15th October 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8C(1) and (2) of, and paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001].

In making these Regulations, the Secretary of State has had special regard to the matters listed in section 46 (and regard to the guidance issued under section 46A) of the United Kingdom Internal Market Act 2020[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Windsor Framework (Retail Movement Scheme: Plant Health) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 15th October 2026.

(3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

## 2. Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2019/2072](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/2072) establishing uniform conditions for the implementation of [Regulation (EU) 2016/2031](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2016/2031) of the European Parliament and the Council, as regards protective measures against pests of plants

(1) Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2019/2072](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/2072) establishing uniform conditions for the implementation of [Regulation (EU) 2016/2031](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2016/2031) of the European Parliament and the Council, as regards protective measures against pests of plants[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In Annex 7, in Part A (plants, plant products and other objects originating in third countries which may only be introduced into Great Britain if special requirements are met)—

- (a) in the table, after entry 30A., insert—

  > |  |  |  |  |
  > | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  > | 30B. | Rhizomes of *Zingiber officinale* Roscoe, other than plants for planting | Any third country, other than EU Member States, Liechtenstein and Switzerland | The rhizomes must be accompanied by an official statement that they:<br>- (a) originate in a country which, in accordance with the measures specified in ISPM4, is known to be free from *Ralstonia pseudosolanacearum* Safni et al.,<br>- (b) originate in an area\* which, in accordance with the measures specified in ISPM4, is known to be free from *Ralstonia pseudosolanacearum* Safni et al., or<br>- (c) originate in a site of production which complies with the following:<br>  - (i) it has been registered and supervised by the national plant protection organisation in the country of origin,<br>  - (ii) official inspections of the site of production and its immediate vicinity have been carried out prior to harvest, and no presence or signs of *Ralstonia pseudosolanacearum* Safni et al. have been found,<br>  - (iii) prior to export, a representative sample of the plants has been tested using appropriate molecular methods and found free from *Ralstonia pseudosolanacearum* Safni et al., and<br>  - (iv) information ensuring traceability to that site of production has been secured during movement of the plants prior to export.<br>\*The name of the area(s) must be included in the phytosanitary certificate under the heading “Place of origin”. |

  ;

  - (i) after “Iraq,” insert “Israel,”;

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations implement the Windsor Framework, in particular by satisfying conditions set out in Article 9 of [Regulation (EU) 2023/1231](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2023/1231) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2023 on specific rules relating to the entry into Northern Ireland from other parts of the United Kingdom of certain consignments of retail goods, plants for planting, seed potatoes, machinery and certain vehicles operated for agricultural or forestry purposes, as well as non-commercial movements of certain pet animals into Northern Ireland (OJ No. L 165, 29.6.2023, p.103), in respect of the movement of certain rest of the world retail goods from Great Britain to Northern Ireland.

Regulation 2 makes amendments to assimilated direct legislation to adjust the entry requirements for certain plants into Great Britain so that they match the requirements for entry of those rest of the world retail goods into Northern Ireland.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
