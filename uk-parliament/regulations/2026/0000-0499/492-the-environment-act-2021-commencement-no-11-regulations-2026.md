ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

The Environment Act 2021 (Commencement No. 11) Regulations 2026

Made

5th May 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 147(3) of the Environment Act 2021[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Environment Act 2021 (Commencement No. 11) Regulations 2026.

(2) In these Regulations, “**the Act**” means the Environment Act 2021.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 7th May 2026

The following provisions of the Act come into force on 7th May 2026—

- (a) section 99 (biodiversity gain in nationally significant infrastructure projects);
- (b) Schedule 15 (biodiversity gain in nationally significant infrastructure projects).

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force provisions of the Environment Act [2021 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/30) (“**the Act**”). These are the eleventh commencement regulations made by the Secretary of State under the Act.

Regulation 2 brings into force on 7th May 2026 specified provisions of the Act concerning biodiversity gain statements in respect of nationally significant infrastructure projects.

Section 99 of, and Schedule 15 to, the Act amend the Planning Act [2008 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/29) (“**the 2008 Act**”), in particular by inserting new Schedule 2A. This Schedule requires the Secretary of State to include a biodiversity gain statement upon the next review of any relevant National Policy Statement (“**NPS**”) concerning development in England and otherwise allows for the Secretary of State to issue a biodiversity gain statement where no NPS is in place. Paragraph 1 of the Schedule confers a regulation-making power on the Secretary of State to exclude descriptions of development from its scope. Sections 104 and 105 of the 2008 Act are amended so as to prevent the Secretary of State from granting an application for development consent to which a biodiversity gain statement applies unless satisfied that the biodiversity gain objective set out in that statement is met.

An impact assessment has not been published for these Regulations as they have no impact on cost to business, the public or voluntary sectors independent of the provisions these Regulations bring into force. A full impact assessment has been published in relation to the Act and copies can be obtained from the website of the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs at www.gov.uk/defra or from the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs at 2 Marsham Steet, London, SW1P 4DF, United Kingdom.
