# The Rampion 2 Offshore Wind Farm (Correction) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 465

INFRASTRUCTURE PLANNING, ENGLAND

The Rampion 2 Offshore Wind Farm (Correction) Order 2026

Made

23rd April 2026

Coming into force

24th April 2026

The Rampion 2 Offshore Wind Farm Order 2025[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“**the Act**”)[^f00002] contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period (as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act), the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the correction of errors in the Rampion 2 Offshore Wind Farm Order 2025.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed Mid-Sussex District Council, Horsham District Council and South Downs National Park Authority as the relevant local planning authorities[^f00004], that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to the Act makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Rampion 2 Offshore Wind Farm (Correction) Order 2026 and comes into force on 24th April 2026.

## 2. Corrections

The Rampion 2 Offshore Wind Farm Order 2025 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

John Wheadon

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning and Innovation

Department for Energy Security and Net Zero

23rd April 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors identified in the Rampion 2 Offshore Wind Farm Order 2025, which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act.
