# The Springwell Solar Farm Order 2026, section 49

> Text as at 2026-09-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. No double recovery[^c-ee9864f05c41b04734e0c944949e3a53]

*Extent: E+W*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any enactment, any contract or any rule of law, or under two or more different provisions of this Order.
