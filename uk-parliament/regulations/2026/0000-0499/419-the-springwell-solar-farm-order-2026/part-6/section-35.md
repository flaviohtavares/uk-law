# The Springwell Solar Farm Order 2026, section 35

> Text as at 2026-09-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Benefit of the Order[^c-c77c3ec29ccadf54ea8b20ac2cf32a25]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to article 36 (consent to transfer the benefit of the Order), the provisions of this Order have effect solely for the benefit of the undertaker save for the grid connection works in relation to which the provisions of this Order have effect for the benefit of the undertaker and NGET.
