# The County Court Rules 1981, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Attendance allowance for witness of fact

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), there may be allowed such sum as the judge or registrar thinks reasonable in respect of the attendance at court of any person (including a party to the proceedings) as a witness of fact or as a witness producing a document.

(2) The sum allowed under paragraph (1) shall not exceed £12 for a police officer or £17 for any other person, unless the registrar on taxing or assessing the costs is satisfied that such sum may be inadequate in the circumstances.
