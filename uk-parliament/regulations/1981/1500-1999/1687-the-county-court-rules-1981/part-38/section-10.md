# The County Court Rules 1981, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Plans in collision actions

In an action arising out of an accident on land due to a collision or apprehended collision, the costs of preparing a plan (other than a sketch plan) of the place where the accident happened shall not be allowed on taxation unless—

- (a) before the trial the court authorised the preparation of the plan, or
- (b) notwithstanding the absence of an authorisation under paragraph (a), the registrar is satisfied that it was reasonable to prepare the plan for use at the trial.
