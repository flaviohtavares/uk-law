# The County Court Rules 1981, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Expenses of inspection

The expenses of any inspection of a place or thing by the judge or a jury under Order 21, rule 6, shall be paid in the first instance by the party on whose application the inspection is made or ordered or, if made or ordered without an application, by the plaintiff and shall be costs in the proceedings unless the judge otherwise orders.
