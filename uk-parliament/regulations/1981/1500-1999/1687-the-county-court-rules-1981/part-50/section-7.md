# The County Court Rules 1981, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Proper officer's obligations to be subject to payment of court fees

Any obligation imposed by these rules on the proper officer of a county court to do any act at the instance of a party to any proceeding in the court shall be subject to the payment of any fee required by a fees order to be paid on the proceeding.
