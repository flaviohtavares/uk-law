# The County Court Rules 1981, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Payment out of small estate

Where a person entitled to a fund in court or a share of such a fund dies intestate and the court is satisfied that no grant of representation of his estate has been made and that the assets of his estate, including the fund or share, do not exceed in value the amount specified in any order for the time being in force under section 6 of the Administration of Estates (Small Payments) Act 1965 the court may order that the fund or share shall be paid, transferred or delivered to the person who, being a widower, widow, child, father, mother, brother or sister of the deceased, would be entitled to a grant of administration of the estate of the deceased.
