# The County Court Rules 1981, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Signing of pleadings settled by counsel

Every pleading or other document settled by counsel for the purpose of proceedings in a county court shall be signed by him and his name shall appear on every copy of the document used in the proceedings.
