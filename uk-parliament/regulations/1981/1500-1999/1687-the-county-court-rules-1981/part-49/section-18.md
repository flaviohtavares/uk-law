# The County Court Rules 1981, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Solicitors Act 1974

Any application under Part III of the Solicitors Act 1974 may be heard and determined by the registrar and may, if the court thinks fit, be dealt with in chambers.
