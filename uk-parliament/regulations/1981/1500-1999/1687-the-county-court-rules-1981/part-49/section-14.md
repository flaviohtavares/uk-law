# The County Court Rules 1981, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Pilotage Act 1913

Order 13, rule 11, shall have effect in relation to an assessor who is to sit with the judge on the hearing of an appeal under section 28 of the Pilotage Act 1913 as if the judge had granted an application by the appellant for the assessor to be summoned.
