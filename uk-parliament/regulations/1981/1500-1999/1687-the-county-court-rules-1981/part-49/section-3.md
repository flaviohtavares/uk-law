# The County Court Rules 1981, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Companies Acts 1948 to 1980

An application to a county court under the Companies Acts 1948 to 1980[^f00002] shall be made by petition if a like application to the High Court would be made by petition.
