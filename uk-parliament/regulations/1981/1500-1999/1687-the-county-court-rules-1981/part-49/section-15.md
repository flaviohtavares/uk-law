# The County Court Rules 1981, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Post Office Act 1969

(1) An application under section 30(5) of the Post Office Act 1969 for leave to bring proceedings in the name of the sender or addressee of a postal packet or his personal representatives shall be made by originating application.

(2) The respondents to the application shall be the Post Office and the person in whose name the applicant seeks to bring proceedings.
