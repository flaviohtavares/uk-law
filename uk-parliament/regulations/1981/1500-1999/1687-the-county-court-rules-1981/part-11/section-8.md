# The County Court Rules 1981, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Counterclaim

A plaintiff against whom a counterclaim is made and any other defendant to a counterclaim may pay money into court in accordance with rule 1 and rules 1 to 7 (except rules 2(2) and 3(4) and (6)) shall apply accordingly with the necessary modifications.
