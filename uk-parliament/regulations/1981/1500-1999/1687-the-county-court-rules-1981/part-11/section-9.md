# The County Court Rules 1981, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Money paid into court under order

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), money paid into court under an order shall not be paid out except in pursuance of an order of the court.

(2) Unless the court otherwise orders, a party who has paid money into court in pursuance of an order made under Order 9, rule 14, or Order 13, rule 1(8)(c), or Order 37, rule 8(1)—

- (a) may by notice to the proper officer and to every other party appropriate the whole or any part of the money and any additional payment, if necessary, to any particular claim made by the other party and specified in the notice, or
- (b) if he pleads a tender, may by his defence appropriate the whole or part of the money as payment into court of the money alleged to be tendered;

and money appropriated in accordance with this rule shall be deemed to be money paid into court in accordance with rule 1 or money paid into court with a plea of tender, as the case may be, and this Order shall apply accordingly.
