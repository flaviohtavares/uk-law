# The County Court Rules 1981, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Notification to foreign court of money paid into home court

Where, after a warrant has been sent to a foreign court for execution but before a final return has been made to the warrant, money is paid into the home court in respect of the sum for which the warrant is issued, the proper officer of the home court shall send notice of the payment to the proper officer of the foreign court.
