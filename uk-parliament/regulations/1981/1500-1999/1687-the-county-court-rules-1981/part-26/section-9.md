# The County Court Rules 1981, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Costs of warrant

(1) The costs of a warrant of execution shall, unless the court otherwise directs, be recoverable under the warrant or, if the warrant is unproductive, under a subsequent warrant of execution against the debtor's goods but shall not be recoverable in any other manner.

(2) Where a request for a warrant of execution is filed by a solicitor in respect of a sum of money exceeding £25, the solicitor shall be entitled to make a charge for filing the request, which may be fixed and allowed without taxation.
