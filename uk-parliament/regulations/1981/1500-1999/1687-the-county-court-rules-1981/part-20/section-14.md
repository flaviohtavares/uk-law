# The County Court Rules 1981, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Interpretation and application

(1) In this Part of this Order “**the Act of 1968**” means the Civil Evidence Act 1968 and any expressions used in this Part of this Order and in Part I of the Act of 1968 have the same meanings in this Part of this Order as they have in the said Part I.

(2) This Part of this Order shall apply in relation to the trial or hearing of an issue arising in an action or matter and to a reference under section 93 of the Act as it applies to the trial or hearing of an action or matter.

(3) Nothing in this Part of this Order shall apply in relation to a reference under section 92 of the Act.
