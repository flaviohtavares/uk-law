# The County Court Rules 1981, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Exercise of jurisdiction

Order 1, rule 8, shall have effect in relation to the jurisdiction of the court under sections 2(2)(a), 2(3), 4(2)(a) and 6(1) of the Act of 1968 as it has effect in relation to any jurisdiction conferred by these rules.
