# The County Court Rules 1981, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Evidence by affidavit

Where the judgment creditor does not reside or carry on business within the district of the court from which the judgment summons issued, evidence by affidavit shall be admissible on his behalf without any such notice as is required by Order 20, rule 7, having been given, unless the judge otherwise directs.
