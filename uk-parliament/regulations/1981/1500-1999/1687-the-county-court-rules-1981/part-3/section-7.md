# The County Court Rules 1981, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Title of proceedings

(1) Every document filed, issued or served in an action or matter shall bear the title of the action or matter and the distinguishing number allotted to it by the court.

(2) The title of an action or matter shall contain a reference to any Act, other than the County Courts Act 1959, by which the court is given power to entertain the proceedings.
