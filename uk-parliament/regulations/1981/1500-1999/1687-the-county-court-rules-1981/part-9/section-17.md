# The County Court Rules 1981, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Interpretation

In the foregoing provisions of this Order, unless the context otherwise requires, “**defence**”, “**admission**” and “**counterclaim**” mean respectively any document which shows that the defendant desires—

- (a) to dispute the whole or any part of the plaintiff's claim,
- (b) to admit the whole or any part of the plaintiff's claim or ask for time for payment of the amount admitted and costs, or
- (c) to set up a counterclaim.
