# The County Court Rules 1981, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Delivery of defence not a waiver

(1) The delivery by a defendant of a defence shall not be treated as a waiver by him of any irregularity in the summons or the service thereof or in any order giving leave to serve the summons out of England and Wales or extending the validity of the summons for the purpose of service.

(2) No application to set aside any proceedings for such an irregularity shall be granted unless made within a reasonable time, nor if the party applying has taken any step in the proceedings, otherwise than by delivering a defence, after knowledge of the irregularity.
