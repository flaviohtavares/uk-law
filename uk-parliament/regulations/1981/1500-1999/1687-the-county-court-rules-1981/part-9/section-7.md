# The County Court Rules 1981, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Default judgment for mortgage money

(1) No judgment shall be entered under rule 6(1)(a) for money secured by a mortgage except with the leave of the court.

(2) An application for the grant of leave under this rule shall be made on notice to the defendant.

(3) The application may be heard and determined by the registrar.

(4) In this rule “**mortgage**” includes a legal and an equitable mortgage and a legal and an equitable charge.
