# The County Court Rules 1981, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Interest after action brought

(1) The sum for which judgment is entered under rule 6(1) may include interest to the date of entering judgment, if such interest is payable by virtue of a statute or contract and is stated in the plaintiff's particulars of claim to be so payable.

(2) A claim shall not be prevented from being treated for the purposes of rule 6(2) as a liquidated demand by reason only that part of it is for interest so payable at an unspecified rate but any such interest shall be computed at the rate which would be allowed in the High Court in like circumstances.
