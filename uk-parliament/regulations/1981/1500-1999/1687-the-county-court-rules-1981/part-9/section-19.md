# The County Court Rules 1981, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Signing of admission etc. and address for service

Every admission, defence or counterclaim delivered pursuant to rule 2 and every answer to which rule 18 relates shall be signed—

- (a) by the defendant if he is acting in person;
- (b) by the defendant's solicitor in his own name or in the name of his firm, if the defendant is acting by solicitor,

and shall state the defendant's address for service.
