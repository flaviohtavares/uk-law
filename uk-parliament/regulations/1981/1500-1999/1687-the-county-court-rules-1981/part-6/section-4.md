# The County Court Rules 1981, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Injunction or declaration relating to land

Where the plaintiff claims under section 51A of the Act an injunction or declaration in respect of, or relating to, any land, or the possession, occupation, use or enjoyment of any land, the particulars shall contain the information which would be required under paragraph (a) and (b) of the last foregoing rule if the action were for recovery of the relevant land.
