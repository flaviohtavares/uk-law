# The County Court Rules 1981, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Transfer of jurisdiction to High Court

Where under section 63(3) of the Act of 1954 the parties to any proceedings agree in writing that the jurisdiction of the court under Part I of the Act of 1927 or Part II of the Act of 1954 with respect to those proceedings shall be transferred to the High Court, either party may file the agreement and thereupon the provisions of Order 16, rule 11, shall apply, with such modifications as may be necessary, as if an order had been made for the transfer of the proceedings to the High Court.
