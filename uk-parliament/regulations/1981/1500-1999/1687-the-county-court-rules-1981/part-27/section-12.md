# The County Court Rules 1981, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Notice of cesser

Where an attachment of earnings order ceases to have effect under section 8(4) of the Act of 1971, the proper officer of the court in which the matter is proceeding shall give notice of the cesser to the person to whom the order was directed.
