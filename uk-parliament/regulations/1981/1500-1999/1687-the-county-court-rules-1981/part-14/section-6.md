# The County Court Rules 1981, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Inspection by court before order

Where, on an application for an order for the production of any document for inspection, privilege from production is claimed or objection to production is made on any other ground, the court may inspect the document for the purpose of deciding whether the claim or objection is valid.
