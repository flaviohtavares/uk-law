# The County Court Rules 1981, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Revocation and variation of orders

Any order made under a power conferred by this Order (including an order made on appeal from the registrar to the judge) may, on sufficient cause being shown, be revoked or varied by a subsequent order or direction of the court made or given at or before the hearing of the action or matter in connection with which the original order was made.
