# The County Court Rules 1981, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Relief in pending matter

The provisions of this Part of this Order relating to an application for interpleader relief by the defendant to an action shall apply with the necessary modifications to an application for such relief by the respondent to a matter.
