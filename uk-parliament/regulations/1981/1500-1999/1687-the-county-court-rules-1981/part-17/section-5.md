# The County Court Rules 1981, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Non-appearance by plaintiff

(1) If the plaintiff does not appear on the pre-trial review, the registrar may, without prejudice to any other power, proceed with the review in his absence or order the action or matter to be struck out.

(2) Order 21, rules 1(2) and (3) and 2(2), shall apply, with the necessary modifications, in relation to the striking out of an action or matter under paragraph (1) as they apply in relation to the striking out of proceedings under Order 21, rule 1(1).
