# The County Court Rules 1981, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Rules as to interlocutory applications to apply

The provisions of these rules relating to interlocutory applications shall have effect as if the pre-trial review were the hearing of an interlocutory application and accordingly the registrar may, on the review, exercise any of the powers exercisable by him on an interlocutory application and may do so of his own motion if no application is made for the exercise of the power.
