# The County Court Rules 1981, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Time for complying with other judgments

Every judgment or order requiring any person to do an act other than the payment of money shall state the time within which the act is to be done.
