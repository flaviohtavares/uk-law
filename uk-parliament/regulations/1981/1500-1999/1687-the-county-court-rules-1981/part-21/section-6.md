# The County Court Rules 1981, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Inspection by judge or jury

(1) The judge by whom any action or matter is heard may inspect any place or thing with respect to which any question arises in the proceedings.

(2) Where an action or matter is tried with a jury and the judge inspects any place or thing under paragraph (1), he may authorise the jury to inspect it also.
