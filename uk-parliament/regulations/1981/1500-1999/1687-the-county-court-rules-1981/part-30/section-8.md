# The County Court Rules 1981, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Directions where dispute as to part payment or notice under rule 5

Where the garnishee—

- (a) pays into court under rule 4(1)(b) a sum which the judgment creditor contends is less than the amount owing by the garnishee to the judgment debtor, or
- (b) makes in a notice given under rule 5 an allegation which the judgment creditor disputes,

the court shall on the return day give directions for the determination of the question at issue.
