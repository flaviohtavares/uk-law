# The County Court Rules 1981, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Order where no payment into court etc

(1) Where the garnishee—

- (a) does not make a payment into court under rule 4 or give notice under rule 5, and
- (b) does not on the return day appear or dispute the debt due or claimed to be due from him to the judgment debtor,

then, if the judgment debtor does not appear or show cause to the contrary, the court may, if it thinks fit, make an order absolute under rule 1 against the garnishee.

(2) An order absolute under rule 1 may be enforced in the same manner as any other order for the payment of money.
