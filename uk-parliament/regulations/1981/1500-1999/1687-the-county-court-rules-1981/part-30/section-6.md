# The County Court Rules 1981, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Order where money paid in by garnishee

If the garnishee makes a payment into court under rule 4, then, subject to rule 8, the court may on the return day, after hearing the judgment creditor and the judgment debtor if they appear, order the payment out of the money to the judgment creditor or make such other order in the proceedings as may be just.
