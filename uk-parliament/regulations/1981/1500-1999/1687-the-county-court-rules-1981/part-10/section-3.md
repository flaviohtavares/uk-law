# The County Court Rules 1981, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Appointment of next friend by court

Where proceedings are commenced or a claim is made by a person under disability without a next friend as required by rule 1, the court may—

- (a) on application appoint as next friend any person who is authorised as mentioned in paragraph (a), or gives such an undertaking as is mentioned in paragraph (b), of rule 2, or
- (b) order the proceedings to be struck out.
