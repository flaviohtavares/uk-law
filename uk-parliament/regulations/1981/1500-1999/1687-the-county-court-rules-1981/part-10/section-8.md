# The County Court Rules 1981, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Application of preceding rules to liquidated sums and matters

(1) Where a minor is sued for a liquidated sum, the court may, on the application of the plaintiff, direct that rule 6 or 7, as the case may be, shall not apply, and if the court directs that rule 6 shall not apply, no appointment of a guardian ad litem under rule 7 shall be necessary.

(2) Rules 5, 6 and 7 and this rule shall have effect in relation to a matter as they have effect in relation to an action, but with the substitution of a reference to an answer for any reference to a defence and with such other modifications as may be appropriate.
