# The County Court Rules 1981, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Prescribed officer

For the purpose of sections 2(3), 5(4) and 14 the prescribed officer in relation to a county court shall be the registrar.
