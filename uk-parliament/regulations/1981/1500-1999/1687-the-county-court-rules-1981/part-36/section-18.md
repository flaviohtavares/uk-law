# The County Court Rules 1981, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Transmission of documents

Any document required to be sent to a court in a reciprocating country under section 5(4) or section 14(1), or rule 15(3) or 17, shall be sent to the Secretary of State for transmission to that court unless the registrar is satisfied that, in accordance with the law of that country, the document may be sent direct to that court.
