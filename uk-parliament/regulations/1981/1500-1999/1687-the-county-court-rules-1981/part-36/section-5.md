# The County Court Rules 1981, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Recording of registration, variation or cancellation by court of registration

On being notified of—

- (a) the registration of a maintenance order in the court to which a certified copy has been sent pursuant to rule 3(2), or
- (b) the variation of a registered maintenance order, or the cancellation of the registration of such an order, by the court in which it is registered,

the registrar shall cause a note of the registration, variation or cancellation to be made in the records of the court relating to the order.
