# The County Court Rules 1981, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Discontinuance of counterclaim

Rules 1 and 2 shall apply, with the necessary modifications, in relation to the discontinuance by a defendant of a counterclaim as they apply in relation to the discontinuance by a plaintiff of an action or matter.
