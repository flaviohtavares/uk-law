# The County Court Rules 1981, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Certiorari or prohibition

A party obtaining from the High Court, on an ex parte application, an order giving leave to make an application for an order of certiorari to remove proceedings from a county court or an order of prohibition to any such court shall forthwith serve a copy of the order on the opposite party and on the proper officer of the county court.
