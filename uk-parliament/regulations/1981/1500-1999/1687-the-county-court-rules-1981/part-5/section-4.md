# The County Court Rules 1981, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Misjoinder or nonjoinder of parties

No action or matter shall be defeated by reason of the misjoinder or nonjoinder of any parties and the court may in any action or matter determine the issues or questions in dispute so far as they affect the rights and interests of the persons who are parties to the action or matter.
