# The County Court Rules 1981, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Appraisement

Where a ship is ordered to be sold or is seized under a warrant of execution, the registrar shall, before selling it, cause the property to be valued by an appraiser and the property shall not be sold for less than the appraised value except by leave of the court.
