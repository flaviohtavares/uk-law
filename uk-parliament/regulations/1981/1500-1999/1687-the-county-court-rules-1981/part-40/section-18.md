# The County Court Rules 1981, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Retention of money where more than one action

Where more actions than one have been commenced against any property and the property has been sold, the proceeds of sale shall, unless the judge otherwise orders, be retained in court to abide the decision of the court in each action.
