# The County Court Rules 1981, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Leave to present bankruptcy petition

An application by a creditor under section 20(3) of the Administration of Justice Act 1965 for leave to present or join in a bankruptcy petition shall be made on notice to the debtor in accordance with Order 13, rule 1, but the court may, if it thinks fit, order that notice be given to any other creditor whose debt is scheduled to the administration order.
