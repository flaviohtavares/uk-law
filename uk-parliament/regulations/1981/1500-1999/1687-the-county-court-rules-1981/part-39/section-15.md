# The County Court Rules 1981, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Reference of question of making a receiving order

Where a court which has no bankruptcy jurisdiction makes an order referring to a court which has such jurisdiction the question whether an administration order should be revoked and a receiving order made under section 11 of the Insolvency Act 1976, the proper officer of the first-mentioned court shall send a copy of the order, together with all the documents in his custody relating to the proceedings, to the proper officer of the court to which the question is referred and shall send notice of the reference to the debtor and to every creditor whose debt is scheduled to the administration order.
