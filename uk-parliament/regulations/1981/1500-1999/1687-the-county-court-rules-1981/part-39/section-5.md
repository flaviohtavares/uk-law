# The County Court Rules 1981, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Appointment of day for consideration

On the filing of a request or list under rule 2 or the receipt of a certificate of judgment forwarded pursuant to section 148(2) of the Act, the proper officer shall appoint a day for consideration of the question whether an administration order should be made and shall give not less than 14 days' notice thereof to the debtor and to each creditor mentioned in the list furnished by the debtor.
