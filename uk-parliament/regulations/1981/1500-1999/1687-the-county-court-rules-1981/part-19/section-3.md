# The County Court Rules 1981, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Reference of other matters in dispute

If it appears to the court at any time after a reference has been made, whether by order or otherwise, that there are any other matters within the jurisdiction of the court in dispute between the parties, the court may order them also to be referred to arbitration.
