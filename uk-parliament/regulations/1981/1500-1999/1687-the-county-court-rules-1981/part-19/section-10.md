# The County Court Rules 1981, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Saving for power of committal

Nothing in this Part of this Order shall authorise the registrar or referee to commit any person to prison or to enforce any order by committal.
