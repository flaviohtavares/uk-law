# The Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 (Commencement) Order 1981

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1981 No. 457 (C.12)

MAGISTRATES' COURTS

The Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 (Commencement) Order 1981

Made

19th March 1981

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 155(7) of the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 (Commencement) Order 1981.

**2.** The Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 shall come into force on 6th July 1981.

W.S.I. Whitelaw

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

19th March 1981

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 into force on 6th July 1981.
