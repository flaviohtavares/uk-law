# The Health Services Act 1980 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1981

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1981 No. 306 (C.8)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The Health Services Act 1980 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1981

Made

2nd March 1981

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 26(2) of the Health Services Act 1980 and of all other powers enabling me in that behalf, I hereby make the following order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Health Services Act 1980 (Commencement No.2) Order 1981.

**2.** The day appointed for the coming into force of section 25(2) of and Schedule 5 to the Health Services Act 1980 shall be 1 April 1981.

Patrick Jenkin

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

2nd March 1981

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation section 25(2) of and Schedule 5 to the Health Services Act 1980 which make amendments to the National Health Service Act 1977 (c. 49) and the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 (c. 29) relating to exemptions from dental and optical charges.

NOTE AS TO EARLIER COMMENCEMENT ORDER

Section 14 of the Act has been brought into force by S.I. 1980/1257 made before the date of this Order.
