# The Magistrates' Courts Rules 1981, section 84

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 84. Notice of bail requirement[^c-20dd88863ae19cde9319b2e2ca69b036]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force.**

(1) This rule applies where the court remands a defendant on bail with requirements under section 128(1)(c) of the Act of 1980 (remand in custody or on bail) and sections 29 and 30 of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 (applications where domestic abuse protection notice has been given; remand under section 29(8) of person arrested for breach of notice).

(2) Unless the court otherwise directs, the designated officer must serve the defendant with a notice of the requirements.

(3) Where the court imposes a requirement with which the defendant must comply before release from custody then forthwith upon compliance the designated officer must send notice to the custodian to release the defendant.
