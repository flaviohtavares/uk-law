# The Magistrates' Courts Rules 1981, section 95A

> Text as at 2024-04-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 95A. Warrant issued out of hours[^key-464d393a107d637f46240e396d02133c][^key-019cf017b41a5dbff8cb90cfea88f3ac]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a warrant is issued by a justice of the peace for any local justice area[^key-12611788eece8181e135dcb15127870d] at a time when the office of the[^key-696fc3c9b97756cb6ac8bf1847bcb991] designated officer[^key-a24dc7829cb6fc48caaa3ddba3089f9c] for that area is closed, the applicant for the warrant shall within 72 hours serve upon the[^key-ac5f474732f49d796c9eb2db326b1b1e] designated officer any information on which the warrant was issued.
