# The Magistrates' Courts Rules 1981, section 75

> Text as at 2024-04-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 75. Abandonment of appeal[^key-903218de03aa7dbaf19997be51483b48][^key-8df652f0a8598e246479bed6fbb2cf2e]

*Extent: E+W*

Where notice to abandon an appeal has been given by the appellant, any recognizance conditioned for the appearance of the appellant at the hearing of the appeal shall have effect as if conditioned for the appearance of the appellant before the court from whose decision the appeal was brought at a time and place to be notified to the appellant by the designated officer[^key-a24dc7829cb6fc48caaa3ddba3089f9c][^key-5e48c447e18d51e8ccf3c300b314b841] for that court.
