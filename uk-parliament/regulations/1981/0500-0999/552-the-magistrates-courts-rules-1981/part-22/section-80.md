# The Magistrates' Courts Rules 1981, section 80

> Text as at 2024-04-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80. Service of documents[^key-727b08440a30040ed4c4debe888de49e][^key-8b2fe1a7798d2509db47b08152b5958d]

*Extent: E+W*

Any document required by rules 76 to 79 to be sent to any person shall, either be delivered to him or be sent by post in a registered letter or by recorded delivery service and, if sent by post to an applicant or respondent, shall be addressed to him at his last known or usual place of abode.
