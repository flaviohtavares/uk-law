# The Magistrates' Courts Rules 1981, section 81A

> Text as at 2024-04-08.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 81A. Duty of justices’ legal adviser[^key-bd352ed901b26527b04f7e76a188a778]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies in relation to a magistrates’ court, including a youth court, which—

- (a) comprises a lay justice or lay justices; and
- (b) agrees to state a case.

(2) A justices’ legal adviser must assist the court by—

- (a) preparing and amending the draft case; and
- (b) completing the case stated.
