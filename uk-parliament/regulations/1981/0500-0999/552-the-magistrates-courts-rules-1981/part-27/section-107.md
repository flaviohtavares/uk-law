> Text as at 2024-04-08.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 107. Application for summons to witness or warrant for his arrest[^c-b9c04dcce5af05bcaad3f17d528497bd][^c-2cf0eea9bc0b0293c59cce0ec2490185]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application for the issue of a summons or warrant under section 97 or 97A[^c-5d7872ff3a6a5cdf8626096256a7f3a1] of the Act of 1980 or paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 to the Act of 1998[^c-3a5487615ee4dc71dc9c2e6f07933a3d] may be made by the applicant in person or by his counsel or solicitor.

(2) An application for the issue of such a summons may be made by delivering or sending the application in writing to the designated officer[^c-a24dc7829cb6fc48caaa3ddba3089f9c][^c-2e44e67817697dbb813095005d2742fa] for the magistrates' court [^c-2ebccf8766e5234fa937866f0787ef44]....
