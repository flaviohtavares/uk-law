# The Magistrates' Courts Rules 1981, section 68

> Text as at 2024-04-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 68. Proof of proceedings[^key-d9a3c22281b766ac605c9726b97e78d4][^key-304254c632c5a4c92308280c65db20a2][^key-93a4dbb82a956bed6dd61c76bf5daf99]

*Extent: E+W*

The register of a magistrates' court, or [^key-769f7e01b8d1c682a6282863adf8ef39]... an extract from the register [^key-f999d1138656d268f9659c9d61016c79]... certified by the[^key-3a4090de1e9f9b7731bdc8faefc95e4a] designated officer[^key-a24dc7829cb6fc48caaa3ddba3089f9c] as a true extract, shall be admissible in any legal proceedings as evidence of the proceedings of the court entered in the register.
