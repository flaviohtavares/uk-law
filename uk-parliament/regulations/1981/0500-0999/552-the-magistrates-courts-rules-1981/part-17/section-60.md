## 60. Enforcement where periodical payments made under more than one order[^key-6f02b4ca8e91fc1888386e917ac30b57]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where periodical payments are required to be made to any person by another person under more than one periodical payments order, proceedings for the recovery of the payments may be brought by way of one complaint. Any such complaint shall indicate the payments due under each order referred to in the complaint.
