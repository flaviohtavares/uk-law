# The Magistrates' Courts Rules 1981, section 49

> Text as at 2024-04-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Duty of clerk to give receipt[^key-42d9b5d8be9640a39fd6cd63d2490f8f][^key-30848df7905e1517b53ff9079d948db5][^key-465a7e67f31dc734f8c0e305fc9a6342]

*Extent: E+W*

The designated officer[^key-a24dc7829cb6fc48caaa3ddba3089f9c][^key-d1595f5251f3c71306b5a4aa858f7fe7] for a magistrates' court shall give or send a receipt to any person who makes a payment to him in pursuance of a conviction or order of a magistrates' court and who asks for a receipt.
