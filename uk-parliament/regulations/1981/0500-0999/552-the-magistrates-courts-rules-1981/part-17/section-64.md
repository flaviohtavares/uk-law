# The Magistrates' Courts Rules 1981, section 64

> Text as at 2024-04-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 64. Direction that money found on defaulter shall not be applied in satisfaction of debt[^key-4c1ea21e781ed0ed7459fd382ffb3723][^key-918ec6bdd121bc6a995f187dcdbe4e47]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the defaulter is committed to, or ordered to be detained in, a prison or other place of detention, any direction given under section 80(2) of the Act of 1980 shall be endorsed on the warrant of commitment.
