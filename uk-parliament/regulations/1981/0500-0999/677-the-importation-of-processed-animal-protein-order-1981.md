## 1. Citation, extent and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

This order, which may be cited as the Importation of Processed Animal Protein Order 1981, shall apply throughout Great Britain, and shall come into operation on 29th April 1982. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

## 2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

In this order, unless the context otherwise requires— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

- “**the Act of 1950 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]**” means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297] the Diseases of Animals Act 1950; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]
- “**the Act of 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]**” means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297] the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1972; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]
- “**animal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]**” means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297] any kind of mammal except man, and any kind of four-footed beast which is not a mammal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]
- “**animal protein *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]**” means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297] any material which contains the whole or any part of any dead animal or bird, or of any fish, reptile, crustacean, or other cold-blooded creature or any product derived from them and includes blood, hatchery waste, eggs, egg shells, hair, horns, hides, hoofs, feathers and manure, any material which contains human effluent and any protein obtained from any of these materials by heat, sedimentation, precipitation, ensiling or other systems of treatment but does not include milk or milk products, shells other than egg shells, fat or dicalcium bone phosphate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]
- “**appropriate Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]**”, in the application of this order to England, means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297] the Minister of Agriculture, Fishers and Food and in the application of this order to Scotland or Wales means the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]
- “**authorised officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]**” means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297] an officer authorised by the appropriate Minister; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]
- “**fat *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]**” means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297] by vegetable or mineral oil and any other oleaginous product obtained by a rendering or a refining process; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

## 3. Saving (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

The provisions contained in this order shall be without prejudice to the licensing provisions contained in the Importation of Animal Products and Poultry Products Order 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297][^f00003] and neither affect, nor are affected by, such provisions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

## 4. Application of section 24 of the Diseases of Animals Act 1950 to a designated organism (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

(1) For the purpose of this order section 24 of the Act of 1950 shall apply in relation to the presence of a designated organism in an animal or bird as if the presence of the organism were a disease to which that Act applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

(2) In this article “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]**designated organism *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]**” means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297] an organism of the genus salmonella being an organism designated by article 3(2) of the Zoonoses Order 1975 for the purpose of section 1 of the Act of 1972. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

## 5. Prohibition on landing of processed animal protein (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

(1) The landing in Great Britain from a place outside Great Britain of any processed animal protein or of any product containing processed animal protein is hereby prohibited except under the authority of a licence in writing issued by the appropriate Minister and in accordance with the conditions of that licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

(2) A licence may be either general or specific and may, in either case, be issued subject to such conditions specified therein as the appropriate Minister may see fit to impose for the purpose of preventing the introduction or spreading of disease into or within Great Britain. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

(3) A general licence shall be issued by the publication of it in such manner and to such extent as appears to the appropriate Minister to be sufficient to bring it to the notice of those persons likely to be affected by it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

(4) The appropriate Minister may vary, revoke or suspend a licence— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

- (a) in the case of a general licence by a notice published, so far as practicable, in the same manner and to the same extent as the licence; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]
- (b) in the case of a specific licence by a notice in writing served on the person to whom the licence was issued. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

## 6. Action in case of contravention of the order (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-029ca74ed6a7f623aae9b1e94847ee3c][^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

(1) Where any processed animal protein or any product containing processed animal protein is landed in Great Britain in contravention of this order or of a licence, an authorised officer may by notice in writing served on the owner or the person in charge of the processed animal protein or of the product containing processed animal protein require him, within such time and in such manner as may be specified in the notice, at the owner's expense and as the person on whom the notice is served may elect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

- (a) to export the processed animal protein or the product containing processed animal protein from Great Britain; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]
- (b) to destroy or otherwise dispose of the processed animal protein or the product containing processed animal protein; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]
- (c) to treat the processed animal protein or the product containing processed animal protein. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

(2) Where the owner or the person in charge of the processed animal protein or of the product containing processed animal protein fails to comply with the requirements of a notice served under paragraph (1) above, an authorised officer may seize or cause to be seized the processed animal protein or the product containing processed animal protein to which the notice relates and carry out or cause to be carried out the requirements of the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

(3) Any reasonable expenses incurred by the person in charge of the processed animal protein or of the product containing processed animal protein in complying with a notice served under paragraph (1) above, and any expenses he has incurred by virtue of article 7(2) below, shall be recoverable on demand by him as a civil debt from the owner of the processed animal protein or of the product containing processed animal protein in respect of which the notice was served.; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

(3) An authorised officer may vary, revoke, or suspend a notice served under this order by a notice in writing served on the person on whom the notice to be varied, revoked or suspended was served. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

(4) For the purpose of exercising his powers to seize any processed animal protein or any product containing processed animal protein under article 6 above an authorised officer may, subject to production of his authority on demand, enter any land, premises, place, vehicle or container taking with him such persons as he considers requisite. An officer entering under this paragraph shall, if required by the owner or the person in charge of the land, premises, place, vehicle or container, state his reasons for entering. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

- (b) the failure by any person to observe any condition of a licence with intent to evade such condition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0d36c12c71aa84c4c71a122076737297]

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order prohibits the landing in Great Britain of any processed animal protein or any product containing processed animal protein from a place outside Great Britain, except under the authority of a licence issued by the appropriate Agriculture Minister and in accordance with the conditions of that licence.

The Order contains powers for requiring any processed animal protein or any product containing processed animal protein which has been landed in contravention of the Order, or in breach of any licence conditions, to be exported or destroyed or treated, without compensation. The Order also contains powers for the seizure of any such processed animal protein or any such product containing processed animal protein in order that this may be effected.

The Order makes the intentional contravention of certain provisions of the Order an indictable offence. A person convicted on indictment is liable to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or to an unlimited fine or both.
