# The Licensing (Alcohol Education and Research) Act 1981 (Commencement) Order 1981

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1981 No. 1324 (C.26)

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Licensing (Alcohol Education and Research) Act 1981 (Commencement) Order 1981

Made

14th September 1981

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 13(3) of the Licensing (Alcohol Education and Research) Act 1981, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Alcohol Education and Research) Act 1981 (Commencement) Order 1981.

**2.** The Licensing (Alcohol Education and Research) Act 1981 shall come into operation on 1st October 1981.

W.S.I. Whitelaw

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

14th September 1981

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 1st October 1981 the whole of the Licensing (Alcohol Education and Research) Act 1981.
