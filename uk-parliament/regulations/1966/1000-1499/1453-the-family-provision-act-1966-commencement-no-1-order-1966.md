# The Family Provision Act 1966 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1966

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1966 No. 1453 (C.16)

FAMILY PROVISION

The Family Provision Act 1966 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1966

Made

18th November 1966

I, Gerald, Baron Gardiner, Lord High Chancellor of Great Britain, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 10(4) of the Family Provision Act 1966, hereby make the following order:—

**1.** The Family Provision Act 1966, except sections 3 and 7 thereof, shall come into force on the 1st January 1967.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the Family Provision Act 1966 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1966.

Gardiner, C

Dated 18th November 1966

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on the 1st January 1967 all the provisions of the Family Provision Act 1966, except section 3 (which came into force on the passing of the Act) and section 7.
