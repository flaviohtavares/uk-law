# The Immigration (Hotel Records) (Amendment) Order 1982

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1982 No. 1025

IMMIGRATION

The Immigration (Hotel Records) (Amendment) Order 1982

Made

22nd July 1982

Laid before Parliament

30th July 1982

Coming into Operation

1st January 1983

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by sections 4(4) and 32(1) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Hotel Records) (Amendment) Order 1982 and shall come into operation on 1st January 1983.

**2.** In Article 2(1) of the Immigration (Hotel Records) Order 1972, in the definition of “alien” for the words “British Nationality Act 1948” there shall be substituted the words “British Nationality Act 1981”.

W. S. I. Whitelaw

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

22nd July 1982

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2(1) of the Immigration (Hotel Records) Order 1972 defines “alien” for the purposes of the Order as having the same meaning as in the British Nationality Act 1948 (c.56). The definition of “alien” in section 32(1) of that Act is repealed by the British Nationality Act 1981 which comes into force on 1st January 1983 (S.I. 1982/933). This Order amends the definition of “alien” in Article 2(1) so as to give it the same meaning there as it has in the Act of 1981 (which defines it in section 50(1)).
