# The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1982

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1982 No. 990 (C.30)

WILDLIFE COUNTRYSIDE

The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1982

Made

16th July 1982

The Secretary of State for the Environment as respects England and the Secretary of State for Wales as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 74(3) of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981, hereby make the following order:

**1.** This order may be cited as the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1982.

**2.** Section 46 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 shall come into force on 19th August 1982.

Michael R.D. Heseltine

Secretary of State for the Environment

13th July 1982

Nicholas Edwards

Secretary of State for Wales

16th July 1982

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order provides for section 46 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 to come into force on 19th August 1982. Section 46 relates to the membership of National Park Authorities.
