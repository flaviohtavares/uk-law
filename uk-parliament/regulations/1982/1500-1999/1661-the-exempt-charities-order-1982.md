# The Exempt Charities Order 1982

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1982 No. 1661

CHARITIES

The Exempt Charities Order 1982

Made

24th November 1982

Coming into Operation

2nd December 1982

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 24th day of November 1982

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of paragraph (c) of Schedule 2 to the Charities Act 1960, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Exempt Charities Order 1982 and shall come into operation on 2nd December 1982.

**2.** The United Medical Schools of Guy's and St. Thomas's Hospitals, an institution connected with the University of London, is hereby declared to be an exempt charity for the purposes of the Charities Act 1960.

N.E. Leigh

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order declares the United Medical Schools of Guy's and St. Thomas's Hospitals to be an “**exempt charity**” within the meaning of the Charities Act 1960.

Exempt charities are not required to be registered with the Charity Commissioners and the Commissioners cannot exercise any of their powers under the Act of 1960 in relation to an exempt charity except at that charity's request.
