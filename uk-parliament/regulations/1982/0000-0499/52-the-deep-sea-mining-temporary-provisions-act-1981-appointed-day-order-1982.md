# The Deep Sea Mining (Temporary Provisions) Act 1981 (Appointed Day) Order 1982

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1982 No. 52 (C.3)

DEEP SEA MINING

The Deep Sea Mining (Temporary Provisions) Act 1981 (Appointed Day) Order 1982

Made

19th January 1982

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 18(2) of the Deep Sea Mining (Temporary Provisions) Act 1981, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Deep Sea Mining (Temporary Provisions) Act 1981 (Appointed Day) Order 1982.

**2.** The day appointed for the purposes of the said Act is 25th January 1982.

John MacGregor

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department of Industry

19th January 1982

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the Deep Sea Mining (Temporary Provisions) Act 1981 into force on 25th January 1982.
