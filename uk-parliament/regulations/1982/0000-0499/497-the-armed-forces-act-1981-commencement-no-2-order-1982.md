# The Armed Forces Act 1981 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1982

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1982 No. 497 (C.16)

DEFENCE

The Armed Forces Act 1981 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1982

Made

27th March 1982

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 29(1) of the Armed Forces Act 1981, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 1981 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1982.

**2.** The provisions of the Armed Forces Act 1981, so far as they are not already in force, shall come into force on 1 May 1982.

Peter Blaker

Minister of State for the Armed Forces

Ministry of Defence

27th March 1982

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 1 May 1982 those provisions of the Armed Forces Act 1981 which are not already in operation.
