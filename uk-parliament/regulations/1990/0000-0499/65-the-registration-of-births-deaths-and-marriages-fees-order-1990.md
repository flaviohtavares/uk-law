# The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 65

REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS, DEATHS, MARRIAGES, ETC ENGLAND AND WALES

The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) Order 1990

Made

17th January 1990

Laid before Parliament

25th January 1990

Coming into force

1st April 1990

The Secretary of State for Health in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(1) and (2) of, and paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 3 to, the Public Expenditure and Receipts Act 1968[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes he following order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

## 2. Fees payable

The fees payable under each of the enactments specified in column 2 of the Schedule to this Order in respect of the matters specified in column 3 shall be the fees specified in relation thereto in column 4 and each of those enactments shall have effect accordingly.

## 3. Revocation of Order

The provisions of this Order shall have effect in place of the provisions of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (No. 2) Order 1988[^f00003] (under which the fees payable were as respectively set out in column 5 of the Schedule to this Order) and that Order is accordingy revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Roger Freeman

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Health,

Department of Health

17th January 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the fees payable for the year beginning 1st April 1990 under the Acts relating to the registration of births, deaths and marriages and associated matters.

The fees increased are those payable under section 5 of the Places of Worship Registration Act 1855, sections 27(6) and (7), 32(5), 41(6), 51(1) and (2), 64(2)(a) and 65(2)(c) of the Marriage Act 1949 and section 30(2)(c), 31(2)(a) and 33(1) of the Births and Deaths Registration Act 1953, the last of those being the fee for a short certificate of birth obtained from the Registrar General.

Otherwise the fees payable from 1st April 1990 are the same as those payable from 1st April 1989.
