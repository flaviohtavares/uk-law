# The Official Secrets Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 199 (C. 8)

OFFICIAL SECRETS

The Official Secrets Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1990

Made

9th February 1990

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 16(6) of the Official Secrets Act 1989 [^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Official Secrets Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1990.

**2.** The Official Secrets Act 1989 shall come into force on 1st March 1990.

David Waddington

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State Home Office

9th February 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st March 1990 the Official Secrets Act 1989.
