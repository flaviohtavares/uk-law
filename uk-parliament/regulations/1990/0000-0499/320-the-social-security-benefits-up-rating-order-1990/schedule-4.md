## SCHEDULE 4 — PART IV OF SCHEDULE 2 TO THE INCOME SUPPORT REGULATIONS AS AMENDED BY THIS ORDER

Article 14(5)

> **PART IV — WEEKLY AMOUNTS OF PREMIUMS SPECIFIED IN PART III**
>
> | *Premium* | *Amount* |
> | --- | --- |
> | **15.** —<br>(1) Lone parent premium. | (1)  £4.10. |
> | (2) [^f00036] Pensioner premium for persons aged under 75–<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 9(a); | (2)<br>- (a) £11.80. |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 9(b). | - (b) £17.95. |
> | (2A) Pensioner premium for persons aged 75 and over–<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 9A(a); | (2A)<br>- (a) £14.40. |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 9A(b). | - (b) £21.60. |
> | (3) Higher Pensioner Premium–<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 10(1)(a) or (b); | (3)<br>- (a) £17.05. |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 10(2)(a) or (b). | - (b) £24.25. |
> | (4) Disability Premium–<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 11(a); | (4)<br>- (a) £15.40. |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 11(b). | - (b) £22.10. |
> | (5) Severe Disability Premium–<br>- (a) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 13(2)(a); | (5)<br>- (a) £28.20. |
> | - (b) where the claimant satisfies the condition in paragraph 13(2)(b)–<br>  - (i) if there is someone in receipt of an invalid care allowance or if he or any partner satisfies that condition only by virtue of paragraph 13(3A)[^f00037]; | - (b)<br>  - (i) £28.20. |
> | - (ii) if no-one is in receipt of such an allowance. | - (ii) £56.40. |
> | (6) Disabled Child Premium– | (6) £15.40 in respect of each child or young person in respect of whom the conditions specified in paragraph 14 are satisfied. |
