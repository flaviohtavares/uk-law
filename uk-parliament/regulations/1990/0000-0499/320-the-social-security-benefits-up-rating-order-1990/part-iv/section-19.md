# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1990, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Revocations

Articles 3 to 11 and 15 to 20 of and Schedules 1 and 3 to 12 to the Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1989[^f00028] are hereby revoked in relation to any particular case as from the date on which the rates or amounts of benefit specified in this Order in relation to that benefit takes effect in relation to that case.
