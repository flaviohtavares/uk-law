# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1990, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Statutory Maternity Pay

The sum specified in regulation 6 of the Statutory Maternity Pay (General) Regulations 1986[^f00023] (lower rate of statutory maternity pay) shall be increased by the substitution for the sum of £36.25 of the sum of £39.25.
