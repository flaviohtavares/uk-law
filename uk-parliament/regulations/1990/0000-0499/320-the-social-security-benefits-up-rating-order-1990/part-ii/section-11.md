# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1990, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Earnings Limits

The sums specified in section 41(2B) of the 1975 Act[^f00024] (earnings limits in respect of child dependency increases) shall be increased from £95, £12 and £95 to £100, £13 and £100 respectively.

## Annotations

[^f00024]: Footnote: Section 41(2B) was inserted by the Health and Social Security Act [1984 (c. 48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/48), section 13, Schedule 5 paragraph 3(c).
