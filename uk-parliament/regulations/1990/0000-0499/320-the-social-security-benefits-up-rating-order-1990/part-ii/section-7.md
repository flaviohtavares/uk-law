# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1990, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Increase in rate of graduated retirement benefit and increments thereof

(1) In the National Insurance Act 1965[^f00019], the sum of 5.71 pence referred to in section 36(1) (graduated retirement benefit) shall be increased by 7.6 per cent.; and from and including 9th April 1990 the reference in that provision to that sum shall accordingly have effect as a reference to 6.14 pence.

(2) It is hereby directed that the sums which are the increases of graduated retirement benefit under Schedule 2 to the Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) (No. 2) Regulations 1978[^f00020] (increases for deferred retirement) shall be increased by 7.6 per cent. of their amount apart from this Order.
