# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1990, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Sums prescribed for the purpose of Family Credit

The sums prescribed in Schedule 4 to the Family Credit Regulations (determination of maximum family credit) shall be increased as from 10th April 1990; and Schedule 4 shall accordingly have effect as set out in Schedule 2 to this Order.
