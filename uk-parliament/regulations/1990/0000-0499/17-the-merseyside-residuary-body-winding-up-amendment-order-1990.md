# The Merseyside Residuary Body (Winding Up) (Amendment) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [SI 1989/2470](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/2470) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1990 No. 17

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Merseyside Residuary Body (Winding Up) (Amendment) Order 1990

Made

8th January 1990

Laid before Parliament

9th January 1990

Coming into force

30th January 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 67(3) of the Local Government Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Merseyside Residuary Body (Winding Up) (Amendment) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 30th January 1990.

**2.** The Merseyside Residuary Body (Winding Up) Order 1989[^f00002] is amended as follows:—

- (a) in article 3(5), for the words “the special fund” there shall be substituted the words “the sums referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2)”;
- (b) in article 7(2), for the words “article 3(1)” there shall be substituted the words “article 3(2)”.

Chris Patten

Secretary of State for the Environment

8th January 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects two errors in the Merseyside Residuary Body (Winding Up) Order 1989 ([S.I. 1989/2470](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/2470)).
