# The A41 London–Birmingham Trunk Road (East of Bourne End to South of Kings Langley) Detrunking Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 284

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The A41 London–Birmingham Trunk Road (East of Bourne End to South of Kings Langley) Detrunking Order 1990

Made

15th February 1990

Coming into force

16th March 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The length of trunk road described in the Schedule to this Order and shown by a broken black line on the deposited plan shall cease to be a trunk road and shall be classified as a classified road as from the date on which the Secretary of State notifies the County Council of Hertfordshire that the new trunk road is open for through traffic.

**2.** In this Order:—

(1) all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway;

(2)

- (i) “classified road” as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is not a principal road for the purposes of enactments or instruments which refer to highways classified as principal roads but is a classified road for the purpose of every enactment and instrument which refers to highways classified by th Secretary of State and which does not specifically refer to their classification as principal roads;

(2)

- (ii) “the deposited plan” means the plan numbered HA10/2E/279 marked “The A41 London–Birmingham Trunk Road (East of Bourne End to South of Kings Langley) Detrunking Order 1990”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY;

(2)

- (iii) “the new trunk road” means the trunk road which the Secretary of State is authorised to construct in pursuance of the A41 London–Birmingham Trunk Road (Kings Langley Bypass) Order 1985[^f00003]and the A41 London–Birmingham Trunk Road (Kings Langley Bypass) Order 1985 Amendment Order and New Trunk Roads Order 1990[^f00004];

(2)

- (iv) “the trunk road” means the London–Birmingham Trunk Road (A41).

**3.** This Order shall come into force on 16th March 1990 and may be cited as the A41 London–Birmingham Trunk Road (East of Bourne End to South of Kings Langley) Detrunking Order 1990.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Peter Emms

Regional Director Eastern Region

Department of Transport

15th February 1990

## SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE TRUNK ROAD

The length of trunk road ceasing to be a trunk road is that length of the trunk road which is situated between a point about 317 m east of its junction with Pouchen End Lane and its junction with the M25 Interchange.
