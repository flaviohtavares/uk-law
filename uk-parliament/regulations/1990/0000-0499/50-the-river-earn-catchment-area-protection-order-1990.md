# The River Earn Catchment Area Protection Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 50 (S.4)

RIVER, SCOTLAND

SALMON AND FRESHWATER FISHERIES

The River Earn Catchment Area Protection Order 1990

Made

15th January 1990

Coming into force

30th January 1990

The Secretary of State, having received relevant proposals, having taken into consideration the need for conservation, having been satisfied as to the availability of fishing for freshwater fish in the area to be prescribed in the following Order and having carried out all necessary consultations, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1 of the Freshwater and Salmon Fisheries (Scotland) Act 1976[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the River Earn Catchment Area Protection Order 1990 and shall come into force on 30th January 1990.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order–

- “freshwater fish” has the same meaning as in section 24(1) of the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Protection) (Scotland) Act 1951[^f00002];
- “the map” means the map marked “Area of the River Earn Catchment Area Protection Order 1990”, signed on behalf of the Secretary of State for Scotland and deposited in the office of the Department of Agriculture and Fisheries for Scotland, Pentland House, Robb’s Loan, Edinburgh.

## 3. Prohibition of fishing without legal right or written permission

Fishing for or taking freshwater fish in the inland waters in the area prescribed in article 4 hereof, without legal right or without written permission from a person having such a right, is hereby prohibited.

## 4. Prescribed area

The prescribed area shall consist of the part of the catchment area of the River Earn which lies within Tayside and Central Regions upstream of the Hilton Viaduct and is delineated by a continuous blue line on the map.

T. J. Kelly

Assistant Secretary, Scottish Office

Department of Agriculture and Fisheries for Scotland,

Pentland House,

Edinburgh

15th January 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prohibits the fishing for or taking of freshwater fish in inland waters in the area prescribed, without legal right or written permission from someone having that right.

The prescribed area is the part of the catchment area of the River Earn which lies within Tayside and Central Regions upstream of the Hilton Viaduct. The area is defined by reference to a detailed large scale map which may be inspected at the office of the Department of Agriculture and Fisheries for Scotland, Pentland House, Robb’s Loan, Edinburgh on any working day between 8.30am and 4.30pm.

The map annexed to and forming part of this note gives a general guide to the boundaries of the prescribed area.
