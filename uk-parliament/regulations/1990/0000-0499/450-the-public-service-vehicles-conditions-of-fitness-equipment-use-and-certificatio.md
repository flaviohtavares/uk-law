# The Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) (Amendment) Regulations 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

Laid before Parliament

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 10(1), 52(1)(a) and 60 of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981 (“the 1981 Act”)[^f00001], and by the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 61(2) of the 1981 Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) (Amendment) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 2nd April 1990.

**2.** The Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) Regulations, 1981[^f00003] are hereby amended so that in the regulations specified in column 1 of the Table below for the amounts specified in column 2 of the Table there shall be substituted the amounts specified in column 3 of the Table.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) Regulations 1981 as follows.

- (a) The fee payable for a certificate of initial fitness is increased from £115 to £123, both on the first application, and on any subsequent application when the test which that application will require includes a test of stability under regulation 6. The fee payable for the certificate on any other subsequent application is increased from £14 to £14.75.
- (b) The fees payable for type approvals are increased from £665, £63. £330 and £1425 to respectively £710, £67, £352 and £1520.
- (c) The fee payable for the issue of a duplicate certificate is increased from £7.75 to £8.25.
