# The Electricity Act 1989 (Scottish Nuclear Limited) Extinguishment of Loans Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 394 (S.43)

ELECTRICITY

The Electricity Act 1989 (Scottish Nuclear Limited) Extinguishment of Loans Order 1990

Made

1st March 1990

Laid before Parliament

9th March 1990

Coming into force

31st March 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 80(1) of the Electricity Act 1989[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Electricity Act 1989 (Scottish Nuclear Limited) Extinguishment of Loans Order 1990 and shall come into force on 31st March 1990 immediately after the vesting of property, rights and liabilities in successor companies effected by virtue of section 67(4) of the Electricity Act 1989.

**2.** Any liability of Scottish Nuclear Limited, being a successor company in Scotland wholly owned by the Crown, in respect of the principal of the relevant loans specified in the Schedule to this Order, being loans made from the National Loans Fund the liability to repay which vests in that company by virtue of section 67(4) of the Electricity Act 1989, is hereby extinguished.

Malcolm Rifkind

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

1st March 1990

This Order extinguishes the liability of Scottish Nuclear Limited in respect of the principal of certain loans made from the National Loans Fund. The total liability extinguished is £1,368,390,398.

These loans were made to the South of Scotland Electricity Board. The liability to repay them is comprised in the property, rights and liabilities of the Board which on 31st March 1990, the appointed transfer date, vest under Section 67(4) of the Electricity Act 1989 in Scottish Nuclear Limited, one of the nominated successor companies, having been allocated to that company by a transfer scheme made under Section 67(1). The liability to repay the loans thus vests in Scottish Nuclear Limited so that the loans are “relevant loans” for the purposes of Section 80 of the Electricity Act 1989 and liability can be extinguished by order under that Section. The extinguishment is effected on 31st March 1990 immediately after vesting in Scottish Nuclear Limited.

The Electricity Act 1989 (Nominated Companies) (Scotland) Order 1989 ([SI 1989/2448](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/2448)) nominated the successor companies. The Electricity Act 1989 (Transfer Date) (Scotland) Order 1990 ([SI 1990/197](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/197)) fixed the transfer date. The South of Scotland Electricity Board Transfer Scheme was made on 31st January 1990 and approved with modification by the Secretary of State on 27th February 1990.
