# The Poultry Laying Flocks (Testing and Registration etc.) (Amendment) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 348

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Poultry Laying Flocks (Testing and Registration etc.) (Amendment) Order 1990

Made

13th February 1990

Coming into force

19th February 1990

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1 and 86(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Poultry Laying Flocks (Testing and Registration etc.) (Amendment) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 19th February 1990.

## 2. Amendment

The Poultry Laying Flocks (Testing and Registration etc.) Order 1989[^f00002] shall be amended as follows —

- (a) article 12 (Public inspection of register) shall be omitted; and
- (b) in paragraph 3 of Schedule 2 (requirements relating to the collection and handling of eggs from laying flocks) for the word “disosed” there shall be substituted the word “disposed”.

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 12th February 1990.

L.S.

John Selwyn Gummer

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Sanderson of Bowden

Minister of State, Scottish Office

13th February 1990

Peter Walker

Secretary of State for Wales

13th February 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Poultry Laying Flocks (Testing and Registration etc.) Order 1989. The only change of substance is the omission from that Order of a requirement for the appropriate Minister to make the Laying Flocks Register available for public inspection (article 2(a)).
