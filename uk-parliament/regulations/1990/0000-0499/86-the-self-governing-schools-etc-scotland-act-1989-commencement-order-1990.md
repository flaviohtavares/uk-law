# The Self-Governing Schools etc. (Scotland) Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 86 (C.1) (S.7)

EDUCATION, SCOTLAND

The Self-Governing Schools etc. (Scotland) Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1990

Made

19th January 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 81(2) of the Self-Governing Schools etc. (Scotland) Act 1989[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Self-Governing Schools etc. (Scotland) Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1990.

**2.** The provisions of the Self-Governing Schools etc. (Scotland) Act 1989 listed in the following table shall come into force on 1st February 1990:–

| *Provision* | *Subject matter* |
| --- | --- |
| Section 67 | Abolition of committee to consider pay and conditions of teaching staff employed in providing further education |
| Section 69(1) and (2) | Testing in primary schools |
| Section 73 | Educational services: extension of power of Secretary of State to make grants |
| Section 74 | Appointment of teachers |
| Section 75 | Dismissal of teachers |
| Section 76 | Remuneration of certain persons employed in providing school education |
| Section 82(2) and Schedule 11 | Repeals |
| Schedule 10, paragraphs 1, 2 and 8(7), (9), (10), (11), (13), (14), (15), (16), (17), (18), (19), (20) and (21) | Minor and consequential amendments |

Ian Lang

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

19th January 1990

(This note does not form part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st February 1990 the following provisions of the Self-Governing Schools etc. (Scotland) Act 1989:- section 67 - Abolition of committee to consider pay and conditions of teaching staff employed in providing further education; section 69(1) and (2) - Testing in primary schools; section 73 - Educational services: extension of power of Secretary of State to make grants; section 74 - Appointment of teachers; section 75 - Dismissal of teachers; section 76 - Remuneration of certain persons employed in providing school education; section 82(2) and Schedule 11 - Repeals; and Schedule 10, paragraphs 1, 2 and 8(7), (9), (10), (11), (13), (14), (15), (16), (17), (18), (19), (20) and (21) - Minor and consequential amendments.

The following provisions came into force on the passing of the Act:- Part I; Part II except section 67; Part III except sections 69(1) and (2), 70, 72 to 76, and 82(2); Schedules 1 to 9; and Schedule 10 except paragraphs 1, 2, 8(7), (9) to (11) and (13) to (21) and 10.
