# The Local Government, Reorganisation (Capital Money) (West Yorkshire) (Amendment) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 496

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government, Reorganisation (Capital Money) (West Yorkshire) (Amendment) Order 1990

Made

7th March 1990

Laid before Parliament

9th March 1990

Coming into force

30th March 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 77 of the Local Government Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Reorganisation (Capital Money) (West Yorkshire) (Amendment) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 30th March 1990.

## 2. Amendment of the capital money order

The Local Government Reorganisation (Capital Money) (Metropolitan Counties) Order 1986[^f00002] shall have effect in respect of the financial year ending 31st March 1990 with the addition—

- (a) at the end of article 4(1) (additions to net capital receipts) of the words “and (c) the sum of £9 million.”; and
- (b) in article 5(1), at the end of the definition of “distributable capital money”, of the words “and (e) the sum of £9 million.”.

Signed by authority of The Secretary of State for the Environment

David Heathcoat-Amory

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

7th March 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Local Government Reorganisation (Capital Money) (Metropolitan Counties) Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/2063](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/2063)) in order to permit the West Yorkshire Residuary Body to retain £9 million of their capital receipts which they would otherwise be required to distribute in the year ending 31st March 1990.
