# The United Kingdom Forces (Jurisdiction of Colonial Courts) (Amendment No. 2) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 240

ANGUILLA

The United Kingdom Forces (Jurisdiction of Colonial Courts) (Amendment No. 2) Order 1990

Made

14th February 1990

Coming into force

14th March 1990

At the Court of Saint James, the 14th day of February 1990

Present,

The Counsellors of State in Council

Whereas Her Majesty in pursuance of the Regency Acts 1937 to 1953 was pleased, by Letters Patent dated the 18th day of January 1990, to delegate to the six Counsellors of State therein named or any two or more of them full power and authority during the period of Her Majesty’s absence from the United Kingdom to summon and hold on Her Majesty’s behalf Her Privy Council and to signify thereat Her Majesty’s approval for anything for which Her Majesty’s approval in Council is required:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth The Queen Mother and His Royal Highness The Prince Charles, Prince of Wales, being authorised thereto by the said Letters Patent, and in pursuance of the powers conferred by section 1(2) of the Anguilla Act 1980 [^f00001], and by and with the advice of Her Majesty’s Privy Council, do on Her Majesty’s behalf order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the United Kingdom Forces (Jurisdiction of Colonial Courts) (Amendment No. 2) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 14th March 1990.

**2.** The United Kingdom Forces (Jurisdiction of Colonial Courts) Order 1965 [^f00002]is amended by inserting in the Schedule in the appropriate alphabetical order the word “Anguilla”.

G I de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds Anguilla to the list of overseas territories to which the United Kingdom Forces (Jurisdiction of Colonial Courts) Order 1965 (S.I. [1965/1203](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1965/1203)) (which provides for withdrawing the jurisdiction of the civil courts of certain overseas territories to try members of Her Majesty’s forces or of a civilian component of those forces for certain offences) extends.
