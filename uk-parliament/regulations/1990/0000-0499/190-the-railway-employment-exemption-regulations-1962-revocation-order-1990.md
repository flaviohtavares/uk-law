# The Railway Employment Exemption Regulations 1962 (Revocation) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 190

EMPLOYMENT

The Railway Employment Exemption Regulations 1962 (Revocation) Order 1990

Made

7th February 1990

Laid before Parliament

19th February 1990

Coming into force

15th March 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 10(3)(a) of the Employment Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Railway Employment Exemption Regulations 1962 (Revocation) Order 1990, and shall come into force on 15th March 1990.

## 2. Revocation

The Railway Employment Exemption Regulations 1962[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Signed by order of the Secretary of State.

Patrick Nicholls

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Employment

7th February 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes on 15th March 1990 the Railway Employment Exemption Regulations 1962. Those Regulations exempt employment by the British Railways Board on railways as engine cleaners, firemen or signal box lads of male persons who have attained the age of sixteen from section 1(3) of the Emploment of Women, Young Persons, and Children Act [1920 (c. 65)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1920/65) (prohibition on employment of young persons at night in industrial undertakings). Section 1(3) of the 1920 Act is repealed by section 10(1) of, and Part I of Schedule 3 to, the Employment Act 1989.
