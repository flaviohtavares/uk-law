# The Coroners' Records (Fees for Copies) (Amendment) Rules 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 140

CORONERS

The Coroners' Records (Fees for Copies) (Amendment) Rules 1990

Made

30th January 1990

Coming into force

1st March 1990

In pursuance of section 24(3) of the Coroners Act 1988[^f00001], I hereby make the following Rules:

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Coroners' Records (Fees for Copies) (Amendment) Rules 1990 and shall come into force on 1st March 1990.

**2.** (1) The Coroners' Records (Fees for Copies) Rules 1982[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of this rule.

(2) In rule 2 —

- (a) in paragraph (i), for “£2.60” there shall be substituted “£3.85”;
- (b) in paragraph (ii), for “£5.50” there shall be substituted “£8.10”;
- (c) in paragraph (iii), for “£5.50” and “30p” there shall be substituted respectively “£8.10” and “45p”, and
- (d) in the proviso, for “Rule 16(2) of the Coroners Rules 1953[^f00003]” there shall be substituted “rule 20(2) of the Coroners Rules 1984[^f00004]”.

(3) In rule 3, for “55p” there shall be substituted “80p”.

David Waddington

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

30th January 1990

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Coroners' Records (Fees for Copies) Rules 1982. They increase the fee payable to a coroner or other person for furnishing a copy of an inquisition or other document in his custody relating to an inquest. The amounts increased by rule 2(2) relate to fees for copies other than photographic copies and the amount increased by rule 2(3) relates to the fee for a photographic copy.
