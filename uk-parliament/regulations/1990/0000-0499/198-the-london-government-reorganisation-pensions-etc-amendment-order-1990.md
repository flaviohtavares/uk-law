# The London Government Reorganisation (Pensions etc.) (Amendment) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 198

LONDON GOVERNMENT

The London Government Reorganisation (Pensions etc.) (Amendment) Order 1990

Made

8th February 1990

Laid before Parliament

15th February 1990

Coming into force

8th March 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 67(3) of the Local Government Act 1985 [^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the London Government Reorganisation (Pensions etc.) (Amendment) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 8th March 1990.

(2) In this Order “the 1989 Order” means the London Government Reorganisation (Pensions etc.) Order 1989 [^f00002].

## 2. Financial administration

Schedule 1 to the 1989 Order (London Pensions Fund Authority) is amended as follows:—

- (a) paragraph 5(b) shall be omitted;
- (b) the following paragraph shall be inserted after paragraph 8—

  > **9.** Section 73 of the 1985 Act shall apply as if the Authority were a new authority.

## 3. Pensions increase

In Part II of Schedule 2 to the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 [^f00003], the paragraph numbered “64C” which was inserted by article 7(6) of the 1989 Order shall be re-numbered “64D”.

Chris Patten

Secretary of State for the Environment

8th February 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the London Government Reorganisation (Pensions etc.) Order 1989 so as to apply to the London Pensions Fund Authority the provisions of section 73 of the Local Government Act 1985 and to disapply the provisions of section 151 of the Local Government Act [1972 (c. 70](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70).). Both sections are concerned with responsibility for financial administration.
