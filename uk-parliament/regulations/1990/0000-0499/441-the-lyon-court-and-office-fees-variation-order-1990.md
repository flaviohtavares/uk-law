# The Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 441 (S.50)

ARMORIAL BEARINGS, ENSIGNS AND FLAGS

The Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1990

Made

28th February 1990

Laid before Parliament

8th March 1990

Coming into force

1st April 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5 of, as read with paragraph 3 of Schedule 3 to, the Public Expenditure and Receipts Act 1968[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

**2.** The fees payable in relation to the matters specified in Schedule B to the Lyon King of Arms Act 1867[^f00002] (and repeated in column 1 of the Schedule to the Order) shall be varied by substituting those specified in column 3 of the Schedule to this Order (the fees formerly payable being shown in column 2).

**3.** The Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1988[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

28th February 1990

This Order increases most of the fees for the matters listed in Schedule B to the Lyon King of Arms Act 1867. The fees were last increased in 1988. These fees are exclusive of stamp duty when stamp duty is exigible.
