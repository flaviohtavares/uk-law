# The Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 136

SEA FISHERIES

The Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) Order 1990

Made

31st January 1990

Laid before Parliament

1st February 1990

Coming into force

2nd February 1990

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretaries of State respectively concerned with sea fishing in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 30(2) and (3) of the Fisheries Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 2nd February 1990.

(2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) Order 1986[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

The principal Order is hereby amended by substituting in article 2 thereof (interpretation) for the definition of the Council Regulation the following definition—

- “the Council Regulation” means Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3094/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/3094) laying down certain technical measures for the conservation of fishery resources[^f00003], as amended by Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 4026/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/4026)[^f00004], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2968/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/2968)[^f00005], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3953/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/3953)[^f00006], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1555/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/1555)[^f00007], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2024/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/2024)[^f00008], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3287/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/3287)[^f00009], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 4193/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/4193)[^f00010], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2220/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1989/2220)[^f00011] and Council Regulation (EEC) No. 4056/ 89[^f00012];

## 3. Revocation

The Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amend-ment) (No. 2) Order 1989[^f00013] is hereby revoked.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/2090](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/2090)) (“the principal Order”), which makes provision for the enforcement of certain of the enforceable Community restrictions and obligations concerning technical measures for the conservation of fishery resources which are contained in Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3094/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/3094) (“the Council Regulation”).

In consequence of an amendment made to the Council Regulation by Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 4056/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1989/4056), this Order further amends the definition of the Council Regulation in article 2 of the principal Order (interpretation) so as to provide for the inclusion of that amendment in that definition (article 2).

The Order revokes the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1989 (S.I. [1989/1576](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1576)) and incorporates its provisions in the new definition (article 3).
