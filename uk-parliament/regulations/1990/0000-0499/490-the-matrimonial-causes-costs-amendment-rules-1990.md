# The Matrimonial Causes (Costs) (Amendment) Rules 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 490 (L.2)

MATRIMONIAL CAUSES

SUPREME COURT OF ENGLAND AND WALES

COUNTY COURTS

The Matrimonial Causes (Costs) (Amendment) Rules 1990

Made

5th March 1990

Laid before Parliament

9th March 1990

Coming into force

1st April 1990

We, the authority having power to make rules of court for the purposes mentioned in section 50 of the Matrimonial Causes Act 1973[^f00001], in exercise of that power and the further power conferred on us by section 64 of the Family Law Act 1986[^f00002], hereby make the following Rules:

**1.** (1) These Rules may be cited as the Matrimonial Causes (Costs) (Amendment) Rules 1990 and shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

(2) In these Rules, unless the context otherwise requires, a rule or Schedule referred to by number means the rule or Schedule so numbered in the Matrimonial Causes (Costs) Rules 1988[^f00003].

(3) These Rules shall apply for the taxation of the costs of work done on or after 1st April 1990.

**2.** In rule 11(3), for the sum “£7.00”, there shall be substituted the sum “£7.50”.
