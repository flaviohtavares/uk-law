# The Education (Inner London Education Authority) (Staff Transfer) Order 1990, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inner London Education Authority) (Staff Transfer) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

**1.** This Order provides for the transfer of certain categories of staff who are in the service of the Inner London Education Authority (“ILEA”) immediately before the abolition of that authority on 1st April 1990. Any member of staff occupying a teaching or non-teaching post in a school or specified institution of further and higher education on 29th September 1989 who is still in post on 31st March 1990, or person who replaces such a member of staff and is in post on 31st March 1990, is designated for transfer to the inner London council which takes over responsibility for the school or institution in question. The Order makes similar provision with regard to the designation of peripatetic and supply teachers, determining the inner London council to which such staff are to be transferred; and with regard to the designation for transfer to specified councils of further specified groups of staff. It also provides for the designation to the London Residuary Body of persons employed in the Greater London Supplies Division of the ILEA Supplies and Transport Services Branch who on 31st March 1990 are seconded to that Body, the staff of Woolverstone Hall School, and of other named persons.
