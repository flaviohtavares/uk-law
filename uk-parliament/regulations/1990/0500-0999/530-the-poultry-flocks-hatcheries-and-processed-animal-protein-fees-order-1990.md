# The Poultry Flocks, Hatcheries and Processed Animal Protein (Fees) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

Coming into force

For the purposes of items 2 to 7 of Part I of the Schedule

1st June 1990

For all other purposes

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 84(1) and 86(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the approval of the Treasury in accordance with the said section 84(1), hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Poultry Flocks, Hatcheries and Processed Animal Protein (Fees) Order 1990 and shall come into force for the purposes of items 2 to 7 of Part I of the Schedule to this Order on 1st June 1990 and for all other purposes on 2nd April 1990.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the appropriate Minister” means, in relation to England, the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, and in relation to Scotland or to Wales, the Secretary of State;
- “the Breeding Flocks Order” means the Poultry Breeding Flocks and Hatcheries (Registration and Testing) Order 1989[^f00002];
- “the Laying Flocks Order” means the Poultry Laying Flocks (Testing and Registration etc.) Order 1989[^f00003];
- “the Minister” means the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

## 3. Fees

There shall be paid—

- (a) to the appropriate Minister in respect of the transactions specified in column (1) of Part I of the Schedule to this Order; or
- (b) to the Minister in respect of the transactions specified in column (1) of Part II of the Schedule to this Order

the fees specified in column (2) of each Part respectively of that Schedule opposite the reference to those transactions, such fees being payable within 14 days of demand made by the appropriate Minister or the Minister (as the case may be) except that the fee specified in respect of a transaction specified in item 5(a) of Part II of that Schedule shall be payable on application for the authorisation referred to in that item.

## 4. Reduced fees

Where any two or more transactions specified in item 5(a) or 7 of Part I of the Schedule to this Order or in item 2 of Part II of that Schedule are carried out on premises situated at the same address on the same occasion, the total fee payable in respect of those transactions shall be reduced by £35.

This Order prescribes the fees to be paid to the appropriate Minister (as defined in the Order) or the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food for the issue of a certificate of registration in respect of laying and breeding flock and hatchery premises. Separate fees are payable for the initial and subsequent inspection of breeding flock or hatchery premises. A reduced fee is payable where the flock and hatchery are situated at the same address (article 3 and the Schedule). Fees are also reduced where two or more specified transactions are carried out on premises situated at the same address on the same occasion (article 4).

Fees are prescribed for the issue of the annual renewal certificate of registration, for the taking or the supervision of the taking of samples by an officer of the Minister, for the examination of those samples and for the inspection and authorisation of laboratories for the purposes of certain Orders relating to poultry and protein processing (article 3 and the Schedule).

The fees prescribed in respect of transactions specified in Part I of the Schedule (except item 1) shall come into force on 1st June 1990 and the remaining fees shall come into force on 2nd April 1990 (article 1).
