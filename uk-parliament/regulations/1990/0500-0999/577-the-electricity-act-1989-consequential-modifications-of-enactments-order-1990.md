# The Electricity Act 1989 (Consequential Modifications of Enactments) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 577

ELECTRICITY

The Electricity Act 1989 (Consequential Modifications of Enactments) Order 1990

Made

12th March 1990

Laid before Parliament

14th March 1990

Coming into force

31st March 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 112(2) of the Electricity Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Electricity Act 1989 (Consequential Modifications of Enactments) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 31st March 1990.

**2.** The enactments mentioned in columns 1 and 2 of the Schedule to this Order are hereby modified to the extent specified in column 3 of that Schedule.

John Wakeham

Secretary of State for Energy

12th March 1990
