# Act of Sederunt (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 799 (S.96)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1990

Made

27th March 1990

Laid before Parliament

10th April 1990

Coming into force

1st May 1990

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Shorthand Writers' Fees) 1990 and shall come into force on 1st May 1990.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of fees

In Chapter II of the Schedule to the Act of Sederunt of 7th May 1935[^f00002], in the paragraphs specified in column 1 of the following table, for the figures specified in column 2, substitute the figures specified in column 3 of that table; and the fees so substituted shall apply to work, in respect of which these fees are chargeable, done on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force:–

## 3. Saving

The fees specified in Chapter IV of the Table of Fees in rule 347 of the rules of the Court of Session[^f00003] which are specified in column 2 of the above table in relation to the paragraphs specified in column 1 of that table shall continue to have effect in respect of work done before this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

J.A.D. Hope

Lord President, I.P.D.

Edinburgh

27th March 1990

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends the fees for shorthand writers in the Sheriff Court by increasing certain fees by about 6 per cent and increases attendance fees to reflect a payment equal to 3 hours (half a day).
