# The Skills Training Agency (Designation) (No. 2) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 929

EMPLOYMENT AND TRAINING

The Skills Training Agency (Designation) (No. 2) Order 1990

Made

18th April 1990

Coming into force

10th May 1990

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 26(2) of the Employment Act 1989 [^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Skills Training Agency (Designation) (No. 2) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 10th May 1990.

## 2. Designation of activities

Each of the activities listed in the Schedule to this Order, being activities for the time being carried on bythe Skills Training Agency, is hereby designated for the purposes of section 26 of the Act.

Signed by order of the Secretary of State Minister of State, Department of Employment

Tim Eggar

18th April 1990

## SCHEDULE — DESIGNATED UNDERTAKINGS

### The activities carried on at each of the following Skillcentres:—

- Cumbria Skillcentre Glasson Industrial Estate Maryport Cumbria
- East Lancs Skillcentre Eagle Street Accrington Lancashire
- Ipswich Skillcentre White House Industrial Estate White House Road Ipswich Suffolk
- Lambeth Skillcentre Units 13 and 14 Ellerslie Square 11 Lyham Road Lambeth London
- St. Helens Skillcentre Bedford Street Parr Industrial Estate St. Helens Merseyside

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates certain activities carried on by the Skills Training Agency for the purposes of section 26 of the Employment Act 1989. These activities are in addition to those designated by the Skills Training Agency (Designation) (No. 1) Order 1990 (S.I. [1990/886](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/886)).
