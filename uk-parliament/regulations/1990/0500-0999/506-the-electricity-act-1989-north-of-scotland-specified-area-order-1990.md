# The Electricity Act 1989 (North of Scotland Specified Area) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 506 (S.64)

ELECTRICITY

The Electricity Act 1989 (North of Scotland Specified Area) Order 1990

Made

7th March 1990

Laid before Parliament

9th March 1990

Coming into force

31st March 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3(2)(a) of the Electricity Act 1989[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Electricity Act 1989 (North of Scotland Specified Area) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 31st March 1990.

(2) In this Order “deposited map” means the maps 1-5 prepared in quadruplicate, docqueted and signed as relative to this Order and marked “The Electricity Act 1989 North of Scotland Specified Area Order 1990 Map” one copy of which is deposited in the Scottish Office, New St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh, one copy of which is deposited at the Office of Electricity Regulation, Glasgow; one copy of which is deposited at the Office of Electricity Regulation, Perth; and the other at the registered office of Scottish Hydro-Electric plc.

**2.** The area of Scotland described in the Schedule to this Order and shown on the deposited map is hereby specified for the purposes of section 3(2)(a) of the Electricity Act 1989.

Malcolm Rifkind

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the area in Scotland in respect of which the Secretary of State and the Director General of Electricity Supply have a duty to exercise their functions under Part I of the Electricity Act 1989 in a manner which is best calculated to secure that prices charged to tariff customers by public electricity suppliers for electricity supplied to premises in that area are in accordance with tariffs which do not distinguish (directly or indirectly) between different parts of that area.

The area is the same, with minor adjustments, as the former district of the North of Scotland Hydro-Electric Board.

The map annexed to and forming part of this Note gives a general guide to the area affected by this Order.

Copies of map 1 and of the detailed maps 2-5 referred to in the Schedule may be inspected at the Scottish Office, New St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh, EH1 3TA; the Office of Electricity Regulation premises at 48 St Vincent Street, Glasgow, G2 5TS and 24 Marshall Place, Perth, PH2 8AG; and at the registered office of Scottish Hydro-Electric plc, currently at 16 Rothesay Terrace, Edinburgh, EH3 7SE.
