# The Marshall Scholarships Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 990

UNIVERSITIES AND COLLEGES

The Marshall Scholarships Order 1990

Made

1st May 1990

Laid before Parliament

9th May 1990

Coming into force

31st May 1990

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 1st day of May 1990

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 1(1) of the Marshall Scholarships Act 1959 [^f00001]or otherwise in Her Majesty vested, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Marshall Scholarships Order 1990 and shall come into force on 31st May 1990.

**2.** Section 1 of the Marshall Aid Commemoration Act 1953 [^f00002]shall have effect with the substitution for the reference to thirty scholarships of a reference to forty scholarships.

G I de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which is made under section 1(1) of the Marshall Scholarships Act 1959, increases from thirty to forty the annual number of scholarships tenable at universities in the United Kingdom by citizens of the United States of America who are graduates of recognised institutions of higher learning in the United States of America. The expenditure incurred in the provision of these scholarships is defrayed by grants out of moneys provided by Parliament.
