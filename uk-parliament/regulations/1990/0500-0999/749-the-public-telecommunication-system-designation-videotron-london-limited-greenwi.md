# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Videotron London Limited) (Greenwich and Lewisham) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 749

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Videotron London Limited) (Greenwich and Lewisham) Order 1990

Made

26th March 1990

Laid before Parliament

29th March 1990

Coming into force

26th April 1990

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to Videotron London Limited on 15th March 1990 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001]rs) (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to the Licence (“the Applicable Cabled Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:–

**9.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Videotron London Limited) (Greenwich and Lewisham) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 26th April 1990.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

Eric Forth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

26th March 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Videotron London Limited on 15th March 1990 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the whole of the London Boroughs of Greenwich and Lewisham. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 29th March 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Videotron London Limited will be a public telecommunications operator in respect of its Greenwich and Lewisham public telecommunication systems when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Atlantic House, Holborn Viaduct, London EC1N 2HQ.
