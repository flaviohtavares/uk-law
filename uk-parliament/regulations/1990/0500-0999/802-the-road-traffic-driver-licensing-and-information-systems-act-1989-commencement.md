# The Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 802 (C.25)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1990

Made

28th March 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him bysection 17(2) of the Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1990.

**2.** The provisions of the Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems)Act 1989 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st June 1990.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Robert Atkins

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

28th March 1990
