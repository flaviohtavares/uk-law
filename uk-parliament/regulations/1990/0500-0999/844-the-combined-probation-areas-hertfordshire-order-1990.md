# The Combined Probation Areas (Hertfordshire) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 844

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Hertfordshire) Order 1990

Made

30th March 1990

Coming into force

1st April 1990

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Hertfordshire) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

**2.** That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Hertfordshire Probation Committee shall be amended by—

- (a) omitting the entries in respect of the petty sessions areas of Hatfield and Welwyn; and
- (b) inserting in alphabetical order in column 1 “Mid Hertfordshire” and corresponding to that in column 3 “3”.

**3.** The justices appointed to be members of the Hertfordshire Probation Committee by the justices for the petty sessional divisions of Hatfield and Welwyn in each case before the abolition of the said petty sessional divisions and their reconstitution to form the new division of Mid Hertfordshire, shall continue to hold office as members of the Committee as if they had been appointed by the justices for the new division of Mid Hertfordshire, until 30th November 1992 and shall then cease to hold office but shall be eligible for re-appointment.

David Waddington

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

30th March 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 to take account of the abolition of the petty sessional divisions of Hatfield and Welwyn and their reconstitution as the new division of Mid Hertfordshire effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Hertfordshire) Order 1990 (S.I. [1990/303](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/303)) which comes into force on 1st April 1990.
