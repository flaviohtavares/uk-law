# The Representation of the People Act 1989 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 519 (C.17)

REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE

The Representation of the People Act 1989 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1990

Made

8th March 1990

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 8(2) of the Representation of the People Act 1989 [^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Representation of the People Act 1989 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1990.

**2.** Sections 1 to 4 of the Representation of the People Act 1989 shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

David Waddington

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State Home Office

8th March 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 1990 those provisions of the Representation of the People Act 1989 which are not already in force.
