# The Fertilisers Regulations 1990, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Revocation

The Fertilisers Regulations 1977[^f00003] and the Fertilisers (Amendment) Regulations 1984[^f00004] are hereby revoked.
