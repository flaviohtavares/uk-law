# The A12 Trunk Road (Leyton Way, Waltham Forest) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 867

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A12 Trunk Road (Leyton Way, Waltham Forest) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1990

Made

19th March 1990

Coming into force

26th March 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 of, and Part IV of Schedule 9 to, the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 [^f00001](“the 1984 Act”), and of all other enabling powers, and after consulting the Secretary of State for Home Deparmarnet pursuant to section 7(6) of the 1984 Act, hereby makes the following order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A12 Trunk Road (Leyton Way, Waltham Forest) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1990and shall come into force on 26th March 1990.

**2.** In this Order—

- “Leyton Way” means the length of the A12 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Waltham Forest known as Leyton Way;
- “northern traffic island” means the island site located in the northern half of the carriageway of Leyton Way at its junction with Whipps Cross Road; and
- “southern traffic island” means the island site located in the southern half of the carriageway of Leyton Way at its junction with Whipps Cross Road.

**3.** The A12 Trunk Road (Leyton Way, Waltham Forest) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1988 [^f00002]is hereby revoked.

**4.** A person causing or permitting a vehicle in Leyton Way to pass to the left of the northern traffic island shall cause that vehicle to turn left into Whipps Cross Road and in no other direction.

**5.** A person causing or permitting a vehicle in Leyton Way to pass between the northern traffic island and the southern traffic island shall cause that vehicle to turn right into Whipps Cross Road and in no other direction.

**6.** A person causing or permitting a vehicle in Whipps Cross Road to turn left into Leyton Way shall cause that vehicle to pass to the left of the southern traffic island.

**7.** No person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding southwestwards in Leyton Way to make a right turn so as to enter Poppleton Road.

**8.** Nothing in this Order shall apply to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a constable in uniform or of a traffic warden.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

S Chatterjee

Director (Transport)

London Regional Office of the Department of Transport

19th March 1990
