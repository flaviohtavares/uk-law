# The Combined Probation Areas (Gloucestershire) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 829

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Gloucestershire) Order 1990

Made

30th March 1990

Coming into force

1st April 1990

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973 [^f00001], and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Gloucestershire) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

**2.** That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 [^f00002]which relates to the Gloucestershire Probation Committee shall be amended by substituting in column 3 —

- (a) “6” for “4” in the entry in respect of the petty sessional division of Gloucester; and
- (b) “5” for “4” in the entry in respect of the petty sessional division of Cheltenham.

David Waddington

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

30th March 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 so as to increase the number of justices appointed to the Gloucestershire Probation Committee. The petty sessional division of Gloucester will appoint 6 justices rather than 4, and Cheltenham will appoint 5 rather than 4.
