# The Vaccine Damage Payments (Specified Disease) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 623

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

PUBLIC HEALTH, SCOTLAND

PUBLIC HEALTH, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Vaccine Damage Payments (Specified Disease) Order 1990

Made

15th March 1990

Laid before Parliament

19th March 1990

Coming into force

9th April 1990

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred bysection 1(2)(i) of the Vaccine Damage Payments Act 1979[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Vaccine Damage Payments (Specified Disease) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 9th April 1990.

## 2. Addition to the diseases to which the Vaccine Damage Payments Act applies

Mumps is specified as a disease to which the Vaccine Damage Payments Act 1979 applies.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Henley

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Social Security

15th March 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds mumps to the diseases to which the Vaccine Damage Payments Act 1979 applies.
