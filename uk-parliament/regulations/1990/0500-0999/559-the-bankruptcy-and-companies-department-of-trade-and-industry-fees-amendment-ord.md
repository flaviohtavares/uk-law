# The Bankruptcy and Companies (Department of Trade and Industry) Fees (Amendment) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 559 (L.8)

INSOLVENCY

COMPANIES

INDIVIDUALS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Bankruptcy and Companies (Department of Trade and Industry) Fees (Amendment) Order 1990

Made

7th March 1990

Coming into force

2nd April 1990

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 133(1) of the Bankruptcy Act 1914[^f00001] and section 663(4) of the Companies Act 1985[^f00002], and with the sanction of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Bankruptcy and Companies (Department of Trade and Industry) Fees (Amendment) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 2nd April 1990.

**2.** Table B in the Schedule to the Bankruptcy Fees Order 1984[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting for the amount “£16.25” shown in relation to Fee No. 11, the amount “£17.83”.

**3.** The Schedule to the Companies (Department of Trade and Industry) Fees Order 1985[^f00004] shall be amended by substituting for the amount “£16.25” shown in relation to Fee No. 14, the amount “£17.83”.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

5th March 1990

Stephen Dorrell

John Taylor

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

7th March 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which amends the Bankruptcy Fees Order 1984 (S.I. [1984/880](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1984/880)) and the Companies (Department of Trade and Industry) Fees Order 1985 (S.I. [1985/1784](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1985/1784)), increases with effect from 2nd April 1990 the fees chargeable under those instruments in respect of the insertion in the London Gazette of certain notices from £16.25 to £17.83.

The said Orders apply to insolvency proceedings where the bankruptcy petition was presented or the winding up commenced before 29th December 1986.
