# The Local Authorities (Councillors) (Declaration of Acceptance of Office) (Scotland) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 956 (S.122)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Local Authorities (Councillors) (Declaration of Acceptance of Office) (Scotland) Order 1990

Made

25th April 1990

Coming into force

3rd May 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33A of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973[^f00001], as read with section 31(7) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

—This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Councillors) (Declaration of Acceptance of Office) (Scotland) Order 1990, and shall come into force on 3rd May 1990.

## 2. Form of declaration of acceptance of office

The declaration of acceptance of office by a person elected to office as a councillor of a local authority shall be in the form prescribed in the Schedule to this Order, or in a form to the like effect.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

25th April 1990

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

DECLARATION OF ACCEPTANCE OF OFFICE

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/956/images/uksi_19900956_en_001)

(This note does not form part of the Order)

This Order prescribes the form of declaration of acceptance of office to be made by a person elected to office as a councillor of a local authority before he can act in that office. The form of declaration requires that person to undertake to be guided by the National Code of Local Government Conduct issued under section 31 of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989.
