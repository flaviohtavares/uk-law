# The Football Spectators Act 1989 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made to correct an omission in [S.I. 1990/690 (C.21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/690) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1990 No. 926 (C.27)

SPORTS GROUNDS AND SPORTING EVENTS

The Football Spectators Act 1989 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1990

Made

20th April 1990

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 27(2) and (3) of the Football Spectators Act 1989[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Football Spectators Act 1989 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1990.

**2.** Schedule 1 to the Football Spectators Act 1989 shall come into force on 24th April 1990.

David Waddington

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

20th April 1990

(This note is not part of the order)

This Order corrects an omission in the Football Spectators Act 1989 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1990 ([S.I. 1990/690](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/690)) and brings Schedule 1 (relevant offences) into force on 24th April 1990.

(This note is not part of the Order)
