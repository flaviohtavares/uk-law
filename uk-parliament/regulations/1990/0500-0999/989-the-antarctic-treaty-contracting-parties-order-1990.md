# The Antarctic Treaty (Contracting Parties) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 989

ANTARCTICA

The Antarctic Treaty (Contracting Parties) Order 1990

Made

1st May 1990

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 1st day of May 1990

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by sections 7(1) and 10(7) of the Antarctic Treaty Act 1967 [^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Antarctic Treaty (Contracting Parties) Order 1990.

**2.** It is hereby certified that the Contracting Parties for the purposes of the Antarctic Treaty Act 1967 areas specified in the Schedule to this Order.

**3.** The Antarctic Treaty (Contracting Parties) Order 1988 [^f00002] is hereby revoked.

G I de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council
