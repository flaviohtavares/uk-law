# The County Court (Amendment) Rules 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 516 (L.5)

COUNTY COURTS

PROCEDURE

The County Court (Amendment) Rules 1990

Made

5th March 1990

Coming into force

1st April 1990

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Amendment) Rules 1990.

(2) In these Rules, unless the context otherwise requires, an Order referred to by number means the Order so numbered in the County Court Rules 1981[^f00001] and Appendix A, B or C means Appendix A, B or C to those Rules.

The community charge

**2.** In relation to domestic property within the meaning of section 66 of the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00002], references in Order 6, rule 3(1)(b) to the net annual value for rating or to the net value for rating shall be construed as references to the value shown on the valuation list in force on 31st March 1990 or to its value by the year, as the case may be.

These Rules amend the County Court Rules 1981 so as —

- (a) to provide for the modification of provisions relating to the rateable value of domestic property as a result of the introduction of the community charge (Rule 2); and
- (b) to revise the provisions relating to costs (Rules 3 to 9).
