# The Thames Water Utilities Limited (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Code of Practice) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 876

WATER, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Thames Water Utilities Limited (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Code of Practice) Order 1990

Made

26th March 1990

Whereas Thames Water Utilities Limited (“the Company”) holds an appointment under Chapter I of Part II of the Water Act 1989[^f00001] as a water undertaker:

And whereas the Company has, pursuant to section 162(6) (a) of that Act, submitted to the Secretary of State for his approval a Code of Practice[^f00002] with respect to the exercise by the Company of powers conferred by or by virtue of paragraph 4 of Schedule 19 to that Act (“the Code of Practice”):

And whereas the Secretary of State considers it appropriate to approve the Code of Practice for the purpose of promoting what appear to him to be desirable practices with regard to the exercise by the Company of those powers:

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 162(7) of the Water Act 1989, and after consulting all such persons as he considers it appropriate to consult, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Thames Water Utilities Limited (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Code of Practice) Order 1990.

## 2. Code of Practice

The Code of Practice prepared by the Company and certified as such by the Secretary of State is hereby approved.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment

Dinah Nichols

An Under Secretary in the

Department of the Environment

26th March 1990
