# The County of Clwyd (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 957

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The County of Clwyd (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1990

Made

26th April 1990

Coming into force in accordance with article 2

Whereas in pursuance of section 58(1) of the Local Government Act 1972 [^f00001], the Local Government Boundary Commission for Wales on 11th December 1989 submitted to me proposals for the future electoral arrangements for the County of Clwyd;

And whereas in giving effect to those proposals I have decided to make certain modifications;

Now, therefore, in exercise of the power conferred upon me by sections 58(2) and 67 of that Act, I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the County of Clwyd (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1990.

**2.** This Order shall come into force on the ordinary day of election of councillors in 1993 (within the meaning of section 37 of the Representation of the People Act 1983 [^f00002]) except that, for the purpose of all proceedings preliminary or relating to an election to be held on or after that day, it shall come into force on 10th October 1992.

**3.** The existing electoral divisions of the County of Clwyd shall be abolished and that County shall be divided into sixty-four electoral divisions, which shall bear the names set out in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order, and each electoral division shall comprise the existing areas specified opposite thereto in column 2 of that Schedule.

Home Office

David Waddington

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

26th April 1990
