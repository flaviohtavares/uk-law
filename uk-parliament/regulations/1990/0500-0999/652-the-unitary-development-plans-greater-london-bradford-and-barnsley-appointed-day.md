# The Unitary Development Plans (Greater London, Bradford and Barnsley) (Appointed Day) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 652

TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Unitary Development Plans (Greater London, Bradford and Barnsley) (Appointed Day) Order 1990

Made

16th March 1990

Laid before Parliament

23rd March 1990

Coming into force

13th April 1990

The Secretary of State for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4(1) of the Local Government Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Unitary Development Plans (Greater London, Bradford and Barnsley) (Appointed Day) Order 1990 and shall come into force on13th April 1990.

**2.** 13th April 1990 is appointed as the day on which Part I of Schedule 1 to the Local Government Act 1985 shall come into force in the areas of the local planning authorities listed in the Schedule hereto.

Chris Patten

Secretary of State for the Environment

16th March 1990

## SCHEDULE

- Hackney London Borough Council
- Hammersmith and Fulham London Borough Council
- Hillingdon London Borough Council
- Lewisham London Borough Council
- City of Bradford
- Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Local Government Act 1985 abolished the Greater London Council and metropolitan county councils. The Act consequently provides for the replacement in their areas of existing structure and local development plans under town and country planning legislation by “unitary development plans”. This Order brings the provisions of the 1985 Act relating to unitary development plans into force in the London Boroughs of Hackney, Hammersmith and Fulham, Hillingdon and Lewisham, and in the City of Bradford and the Metropolitan Borough of Barnsley on 13th April 1990.
