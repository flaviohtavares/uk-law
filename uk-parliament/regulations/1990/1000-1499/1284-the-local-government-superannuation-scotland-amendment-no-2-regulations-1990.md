# The Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Amendment (No.2) Regulations 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in S.I. [1990/422](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/422) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1990 No. 1284 (S.142)

PENSIONS

The Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Amendment (No.2) Regulations 1990

Made

19th June 1990

Laid before Parliament

29th June 1990

Coming into force

20th July 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 7 and 12 of the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with such associations of local authorities as appeared to him to be concerned and such representatives of other persons likely to be affected by the Regulations as appeared to him to be appropriate in accordance with section 7(5) of that Act, and not having considered consultation with any individual local authority desirable, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 20th July 1990.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1987[^f00002].

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1987 (S.I. [1987/1850](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1850)) to correct an error in the provisions for calculation of death gratuity. The error was introduced by the Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1990 (S.I. [1990/422](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/422)) which came into force on 1st April 1990.

The amendment does not affect death gratuities payable in respect of persons who have ceased to serve or died before the amending regulations come into force.
