# The Corn Returns (Scotland) (Variation) Regulations 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1276 (S.140)

AGRICULTURE

CORN CROPS

The Corn Returns (Scotland) (Variation) Regulations 1990

Made

11th June 1990

Coming into force

1st August 1990

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 4, 5 and 14 of the Corn Returns Act 1882[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, extent and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Corn Returns (Scotland) (Variation) Regulations 1990, shall apply to Scotland only and shall come into force on 1st August 1990.

## 2. Variation of principal regulations

The Corn Returns (Scotland) Regulations 1976[^f00003] shall be further varied by substituting for Schedule 2 thereto (which sets out the form of return required by those Regulations) the Schedule to these Regulations.

## 3. Revocation

The Corn Returns (Scotland) (Amendment) Regulations 1981[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

Sanderson of Bowden

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

11th June 1990

## SCHEDULE

Regulation 2

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1276/images/uksi_19901276_en_001)

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Corn Returns (Scotland) Regulations 1976 by substituting a new form of return for the previous form set out in the Corn Returns (Scotland) (Amendment) Regulations 1981. The principal change is the introduction of new footnotes which specify the appropriate use of each row.
