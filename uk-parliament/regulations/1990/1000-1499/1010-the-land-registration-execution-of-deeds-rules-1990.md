# The Land Registration (Execution of Deeds) Rules 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1010

LAND REGISTRATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Land Registration (Execution of Deeds) Rules 1990

Made

23rd April 1990

Laid before Parliament

11th May 1990

Coming into force

31st July 1990

The Lord Chancellor, with the advice and assistance of the Rule Committee appointed in pursuance of section 144 of the Land Registration Act 1925[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that section, hereby makes the following rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These rules may be cited as the Land Registration (Execution of Deeds) Rules 1990 and shall come into force on 31st July 1990.

## 2. New form 19

Form 19 in the Schedule to these rules shall be substituted for Form 19 in the Schedule to the Land Registration Rules 1925[^f00002].

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

Dated 23rd April 1990

## THE SCHEDULE

### FORM 19 — — Transfer of Freehold Land (Whole). h m land registry

(Rule 98)

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1010/images/uksi_19901010_en_001)

(This note does not form part of the Rules)

The rules substitute a new form for Form 19 in the Schedule to the Land Registration Rules 1925 (Transfer of Freehold Land (Whole)). The new form dispenses with the requirement for sealing when execution is by an individual and prescribes different forms of attestation where execution of the transfer is by:

- (i) an individual party signing in person;
- (ii) a person signing by direction on behalf of an individual party;
- (iii) a company registered under the Companies Acts under its common seal; and
- (iv) a company registered under the Companies Acts not under a common seal.
