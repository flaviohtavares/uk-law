# The Inheritance Tax (Delivery of Accounts) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1112

INHERITANCE TAX

The Inheritance Tax (Delivery of Accounts) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 1990

Made

21st May 1990

Laid before the House of Commons

22nd May 1990

Coming into force

1st July 1990

The Commissioners of Inland Revenue, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 256(1) of the Inheritance Tax Act 1984[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

These Regulations may be cited as the Inheritance Tax (Delivery of Accounts) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 1st July 1990.

**2.** These Regulations extend to Northern Ireland only.

## 3. Interpretation

In these Regulations “the Principal Regulations” means the Capital Transfer Tax (Delivery of Accounts) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 1981[^f00002].

## 4. Amendments to Principal Regulations

In Regulation 3 of the Principal Regulations—

- (a) in paragraph (b) for “£100,000” there shall be substituted “£115,000”;
- (b) in paragraph (d) for “1st April 1989” there shall be substituted “1st April 1990”.

T. J. Painter

L. J. H. Beighton

Two of the Commissioners of Inland Revenue

21st May 1990

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Principal Regulations (S.I. [1981/1441](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1981/1441)) as amended by the Inheritance Tax (Delivery of Accounts) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 1989 (S.I. [1989/1080](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1080)) dispensed with the need to deliver an account for the purposes of inheritance tax where (subject to specified exceptions) the value of a deceased’s estate did not exceed £100,000 and where the deceased died on or after 1st April 1989. These Regulations increase that limit to £115,000 in respect of deaths on or after 1st April 1990.
