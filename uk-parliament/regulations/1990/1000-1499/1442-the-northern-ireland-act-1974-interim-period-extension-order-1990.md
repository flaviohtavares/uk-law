# The Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1442

NORTHERN IRELAND

The Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1990

Made

15th July 1990

Coming into force

15th July 1990

Whereas a draft of this Order has been approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1990 and shall come into force forthwith.

**2.** The interim period specified by section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974 shall continue until 16th July 1991. Northern Ireland Office

P.L. Brooke

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State, Northern Ireland Office

15th July 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends until 16th July 1991 the period specified in section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (as extended by the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1989 (S.I. [1989/1096](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1096))) for the operation of the temporary provisions for the government of Northern Ireland contained in Schedule 1 to that Act.
