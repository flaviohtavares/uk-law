# The Rent Book (Forms of Notice) (Amendment) Regulations 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1067

LANDLORD AND TENANT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Rent Book (Forms of Notice) (Amendment) Regulations 1990

Made

11th May 1990

Laid before Parliament

22nd May 1990

Coming into force

12th June 1990

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 5 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Rent Book (Forms of Notice) (Amendment) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 12th June 1990.

**2.** The forms in the Schedule to the Rent Book (Forms of Notice) Regulations 1982[^f00002] are amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph 11 of the forms in Parts I and III, paragraph 13 of the form in Part II and paragraph 8 of the form in Part IV omit “and rates”;
- (b) in paragraph 9 of the form in Part I for the words from “Full” to “11” substitute “Details of these rules are given in the Department of the Environment and Welsh Office booklets” Letting rooms in Your Home “and” Notice That You Must Leave “, nos. 22 and 24”;
- (c) in paragraph 9 of the form in Part II for “” Notice to Quit “, nos. 7 and 11” substitute “” Notice That You Must Leave “, nos. 25 and 22”; and
- (d) in the form in Part III—
  - (i) in paragraph 9 for the words from “booklet” to “1976 .” substitute “Department of the Environment and Welsh Office booklet” Agricultural Lettings “, no. 23 in the series of housing booklets.”; and
  - (ii) in paragraph 10 for “cottage” substitute “property on 31st March 1990. If your property had no rateable value on that date, the provisional rent can be based, broadly, on the property’s letting value”.

Chris Patten

Secretary of State for the Environment

9th May 1990

David Hunt

Secretary of State for Wales

11th May 1990

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the forms of notice (prescribed by the Rent Act (Forms of Notice) Regulations 1982) which must be inserted in rent books or other similar documents under section 5 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 where a person has a right to occupy premises as a residence at a rent payable weekly and the value of board is not a substantial part of the rent. The forms relate to restricted contracts and regulated tenancies under the Rent Act [1977 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/42), statutory tenancies under the Rent (Agriculture) Act [1976 (c. 80)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/80) and assured tenancies and agricultural occupancies under the Housing Act [1988 (c. 50)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/50). The amendments update references to relevant booklets and relate to the abolition of domestic rating.
