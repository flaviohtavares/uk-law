# The Employment Subsidies Act 1978 (Renewal) (Great Britain) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1313

TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT

The Employment Subsidies Act 1978 (Renewal) (Great Britain) Order 1990

Made

27th June 1990

Coming into force

1st July 1990

The Secretary of State, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3(2)(a) of the Employment Subsidies Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order, a draft of which was laid before the House of Commons and approved by resolution of that House in accordance with section 3(3) of the Employment Subsidies Act 1978:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Employment Subsidies Act 1978 (Renewal) (Great Britain) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st July 1990.

## 2. Renewal

The powers of section 1 of the Employment Subsidies Act 1978 shall be renewed for Great Britain until the expiration of the period ending with 31st December 1991.

Signed by order of the Secretary of State.

Patrick Nicholls

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Employment

27th June 1990

We consent.

Michael Fallon

David Lightbown

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

27th June 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order renews for Great Britain until 31st December 1991 the powers of section 1 of the Employment Subsidies Act 1978, which would otherwise not be exercisable after the end of June 1990, the section having been renewed until that date by the Employment Subsidies Act 1978 (Renewal) (Great Britain) Order 1988 (S.I. [1988/2229](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/2229)). Section 1 of the Employment Subsidies Act 1978 authorises the Secretary of State to make payments to employers enabling them to retain in employment persons who might otherwise become unemployed, to take on new employees and generally to maintain or enlarge their labour force.
