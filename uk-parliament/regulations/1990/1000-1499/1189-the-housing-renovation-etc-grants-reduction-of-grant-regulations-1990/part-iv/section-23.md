# The Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations 1990, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Earnings of self-employed earners

“Earnings”, in the case of employment as a self-employed earner, means the gross income of the employment and shall include any allowance paid under section 2 of the Employment and Training Act 1973[^f00043] to the relevant person for the purpose of assisting him in carrying on his business.

## Annotations

[^f00043]: Footnote: [1973 c. 50](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/50); section 2 was substituted by section 25 of the Employment Act [1988 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/19).
