# The Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations 1990, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. The applicable amount

The applicable amount in respect of any one application shall be the aggregate of–

- (a) the total of the weekly applicable amounts of all those persons who are relevant persons in the case of that application, and
- (b) £20;

and the applicable amount of each relevant person shall be determined in accordance with regulation 12.
