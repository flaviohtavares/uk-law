# The Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations 1990, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Reduction in amount of grant

The amount of any grant which may be paid in respect of an application shall, if the financial resources of the applicant or applicants exceed the applicable amount, be reduced from what it would otherwise have been by the amount obtained by multiplying the amount of that excess by–

- (a) where the application is accompanied by an owner-occupation certificate, 51.54,
- (b) where the application is accompanied by a tenant’s certificate, 34.62.
