# The Antarctic Treaty Act 1967 (Isle of Man) (Variation) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1198

ANTARCTICA

The Antarctic Treaty Act 1967 (Isle of Man) (Variation) Order 1990

Made

7th June 1990

Coming into force

7th July 1990

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 7th day of June 1990

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by sections 9 and 10(7) of the Antarctic Treaty Act 1967[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Antarctic Treaty Act 1967 (Isle of Man) (Variation) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 7th July 1990.

**2.** The Antarctic Treaty Act 1967 (Isle of Man) Order 1970[^f00002] is varied by deleting, in paragraphs 3, 4 and 6 of the Schedule, the words “, if approved by resolution of Tynwald,”.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the Antarctic Treaty Act 1967 (Isle of Man) Order 1970 by removing the requirement for certain Orders in Council under the Antarctic Treaty Act 1967 to be approved by Tynwald before they have effect in the Isle of Man.
