## SCHEDULE 2 — LENGTHS OF ROAD WHERE EXEMPTIONS APPLY AS RESPECTS WAITING FOR ANY PURPOSE

Articles 4(2)(a)(b) & (c)

### PART I — 8 am — 7 pm Mondays to Fridays inclusive (all vehicles) — HOLLOWAY ROAD

#### 1. North East Side

Between a point 13 metres south east of a point level with the southern kerbline of Tollington Road and a point 50 metres north west of the north western kerbline of Loraine Road.

**2.** From a point 30 metres south east of the southern kerbline of Loraine Road for a distance of 127 metres.

#### 3. South West Side

From a point 67 metres north west of the north western kerbline of Camden Road south eastwards for a distance of 53 metres.

**4.** From a point 1 metre south east of a point opposite the party wall of Nos 326 and 328 Holloway Road south eastwards for a distance of 155 metres.

#### 7. South West Side
