# The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1990, SCHEDULE 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 1 — THE TRUNK ROAD RED ROUTE

Articles 3 and 4

### PART I — Lengths of road where waiting (if not prohibited by Article 5) is restricted between 8 am — 7 pm Mondays to Fridays inclusive

**1.** Archway Road.

**2.** Archway Roundabout.

**3.** City Road between its junction with Islington High Street, St. John Street and Pentonville Road, and its junction with Wakley Street.

**4.** Goswell Road between its junction with City Road and its junction with Wakley Street.

**5.** Highbury Corner.

**6.** Holloway Road.

**7.** Wakley Street.

### PART II — Lengths of road where waiting (if not prohibited by Article 5) is restricted between 8 am — 7 pm Mondays to Saturdays inclusive

**8.** Islington High Street.

**9.** Upper Street.
