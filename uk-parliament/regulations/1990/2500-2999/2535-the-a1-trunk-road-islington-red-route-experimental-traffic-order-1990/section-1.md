# The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1990, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and expiration

(1) This Order may be cited as the A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1990.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 1st January 1991 and remain in force until 29th June 1992.
