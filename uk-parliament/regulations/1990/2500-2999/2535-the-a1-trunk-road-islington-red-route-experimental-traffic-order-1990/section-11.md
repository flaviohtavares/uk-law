# The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1990, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Suspension of other instruments

There is suspended in their application to the lengths of road specified in the Schedules—

- (a) the Greater London (Waiting Restriction) (Exemptions for Disabled Persons) Order 1975[^f00006], and
- (b) the Islington (Waiting and Loading Restriction) Order 1976[^f00007].
