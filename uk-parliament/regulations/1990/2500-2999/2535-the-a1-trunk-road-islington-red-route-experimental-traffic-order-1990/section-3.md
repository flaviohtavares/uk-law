# The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1990, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Application

This Order shall apply to every part of the lengths of road in the London Borough of Islington specified in Parts I and II of Schedule 1 (such lengths being referred to in this Order as the “trunk road red route”).
