# The Transfer of Functions (Radioactive Substances) (Wales) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 2598

MINISTERS OF THE CROWN

The Transfer of Functions (Radioactive Substances) (Wales) Order 1990

Made

19th December 1990

Laid before Parliament

8th January 1991

Coming into force

29th January 1991

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 19th day of December 1990

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1 of the Ministers of the Crown Act 1975[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Transfer of Functions (Radioactive Substances) (Wales) Order 1990.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 29th January 1991.

## 2. Transfer of functions

The functions of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food under any of the amendments of the Radioactive Substances Act 1960[^f00002] specified in Part V of and Schedule 5 to the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00003], so far as exercisable in relation to Wales, are hereby transferred to the Secretary of State and references to that Minister in any of those amendments shall be construed accordingly.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Transfer of Functions (Wales) (No. 1) Order 1978 (S.I. [1978/272](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1978/272)) transferred functions of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food under the Radioactive Substances Act 1960, as referred to in that Order, so far as those functions were exercisable in relation to Wales, to the Secretary of State. The 1960 Act is amended by Part V of, and Schedule 5 to, the Environmental Protection Act 1990. The present Order transfers the functions of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food under those amendments, so far as exercisable in relation to Wales, to the Secretary of State.
