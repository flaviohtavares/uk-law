# The National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 (Commencement No. 5 and Revocation) (Scotland) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 2510 (C.65) (S.212)

SOCIAL WORK, SCOTLAND

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 (Commencement No. 5 and Revocation) (Scotland) Order 1990

Made

7th December 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 67(2) and (3) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 (Commencement No. 5 and Revocation) (Scotland) Order 1990.

## 2. Appointed day

1st April 1991 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order.

## 3. Revocation

Articles 2(2) and 3 of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 (Commencement No.3 and Transitional Provisions) (Scotland) Order 1990[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Michael B Forsyth

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

7th December 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

In this Order, article 2 brings into force on 1st April 1991 various provisions of Part IV (Community Care: Scotland) and Part V (Miscellaneous and General) of, and Schedules 9 and 10 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 (“the 1990 Act”). The provisions commenced all relate to Scotland and with the exception of one minor repeal all are relevant to community care.

Article 3 of the Order revokes articles 2(2) and 3 of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 (Commencement No.3 and Transitional Provisions) (Scotland) Order 1990. Article 2(2) of that Order was to bring into force on 1st January 1991 provisions of sections 38 and 39 of the 1990 Act relating to the Medical Practices Committee and to general medical practitioners and article 3 of that Order contained a relative transitional provision.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order has been preceded by four earlier Commencement Orders namely–

(1) the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1990 ([S.I. 1990/1329](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1329)) commencing provisions of Parts I and V of, and Schedules 1 to 5 and 8 to 10 to, the Act;

(2) the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 (Commencement No. 2) (Scotland) Order 1990 ([S.I. 1990/1520](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1520)) commencing certain provisions of Parts II and V of, and Schedules 6, 9 and 10 to, the Act relating to Scotland;

(3) the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provisions) (Scotland) Order 1990 ([S.I. 1990/1793](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1793)) commencing certain provisions of Parts II and V of, and Schedules 5, 9 and 10 to, the Act relating to Scotland; and

(4) the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provision) Order 1990 ([S.I. 1990/2218](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/2218)) commencing certain provisions of Parts III and V of, and Schedules 9 and 10 to, the Act which relate mainly to community care in England and Wales.

These Orders, made respectively on 2nd and 18th July 1990, 28th August 1990 and 7th November 1990, brought into force at various dates, both before and after the date of this Order, provisions of Parts I, II, III and V of, and of Schedules 1 to 6 and 8 to 10 to, the Act.

The first three Orders related generally to the National Health Service and the fourth Order related generally to community care.

A list of all provisions of the Act commenced by the previous commencement orders is obtainable free of charge on application to the Scottish Education Department, Room 420, 43 Jeffrey Street, Edinburgh EH1 (ref. ATA/3/10).
