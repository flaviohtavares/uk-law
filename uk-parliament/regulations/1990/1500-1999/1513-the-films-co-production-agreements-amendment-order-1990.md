# The Films Co-Production Agreements (Amendment) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1513

CINEMAS AND FILMS

The Films Co-Production Agreements (Amendment) Order 1990

Made

24th July 1990

Coming into force

14th August 1990

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 24th day of July 1990

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 6(1) of, and paragraph 4(5) of Schedule 1 to, the Films Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to provide, and it is hereby provided, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Films Co-Production Agreements (Amendment) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 14th August 1990.

**2.** The Films Co-Production Agreements Order 1985[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for the Schedule the following—

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Films Co-Production Agreements Order 1985 (“the principal Order”) provides that films made in accordance with the agreements between the Government of the United Kingdom and the Governments of the countries listed in the Schedule, as amended by S.I. [1985/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1985/2001), are to be treated as British films for the purposes of Schedule 1 to the Films Act 1985 (Certification for the purposes of section 72 of the Finance Act [1982 (c. 39)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/39) in case of British films.) The present Order extends the list of agreements scheduled to the principal Order to include an agreement with Australia dated 12th June 1990 (Cmnd. 1143).
