# The Gipsy Encampments (London Borough of Harrow) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1929

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Gipsy Encampments (London Borough of Harrow) Order 1990

Made

19th September 1990

Laid before Parliament

28th September 1990

Coming into force

19th October 1990

The Secretary of State, being satisfied that in all the circumstances it is not expedient to make adequate provision in the area of the London Borough of Harrow for the accommodation of gipsies residing in or resorting to that area, on the application of the council of the London Borough of Harrow, and in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12(1) of the Caravan Sites Act 1968[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Gipsy Encampments (London Borough of Harrow) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 19th October 1990.

**2.** The area of the London Borough of Harrow is designated as an area to which section 10 (prohibition of unauthorised camping in designated areas) of the Caravan Sites Act 1968 applies.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Patrick Nicholls

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

19th September 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the London Borough of Harrow as an area to which section 10 of the Caravan Sites Act 1968 applies.

It is an offence within a designated area for a gipsy to station a caravan for the purpose of residing for any period on any land within the boundaries of a highway, or on any other unoccupied land, or on any occupied land without the consent of the occupier.

Under section 11 of the Act (substituted by section 174 of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980), unlawfully stationed caravans and their occupants may be removed by order of a magistrates' court.
