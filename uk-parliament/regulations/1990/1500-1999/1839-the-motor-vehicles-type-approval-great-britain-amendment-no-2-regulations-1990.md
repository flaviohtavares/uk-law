# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1839

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1990

Made

6th September 1990

Laid before Parliament

10th September 1990

Coming into force

1st October 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 54(1) and 61 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of the Road Traffic Act 1988, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 1st October 1990.

**2.** (1) In the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984[^f00002], the Table in Part II of Schedule 1B shall be amended as follows.

(2) In the entry relating to item 4C in Schedule 1, for paragraph 1 in column (2) and the entries relating to that paragraph in columns (3) and (4) there shall be substituted—

> | **1.** Vehicles propelled by spark ignition engines, constructed or adapted to carry not more than 5 passengers excluding the driver or with a maximum gross weight of not more than 2500 kg, not being an off-road vehicle in either case and which have an engine capacity of: |  |  |  |
> | - (a) less than 1400 cc | see Schedule 1 item 4C | — | — |
> | - (b) not less than 1400 cc and not more than 2000 cc | see Schedule 1 item 4C | 30.9.91 | 30.9.93 |

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984.

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1989 (S.I. [1989/1580](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1580)) provided for the cessation of the requirements specified in item 4C of Schedule 1, in relation to certain vehicles, on specified dates. The effect of the amendment is to revoke the cessation dates relating to some small passenger vehicles with spark ignition engines.

The main effect of the amendment is that most cars with spark ignition engines of less than 1400 cc will continue to be able to meet the type approval requirements relating to exhaust emissions if they comply with Council Directive [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220) (L76, 6.4.70, p.1) (S.E. 1970(I), p.171) as last amended by Council Directive [83/351/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0351) (L197, 20.7.83, p.1) or ECE Regulation 15 as revised on the 20th of October 1981 and corrected on the 9th of June 1982 or ECE Regulation 15 as revised on the 20th of October 1981, corrected on the 9th of June 1982 and amended on the 1st of June 1984.
