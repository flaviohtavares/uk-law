# The Regional and District Health Authorities (Membership and Procedure) Amendment Regulations 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1758

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Regional and District Health Authorities (Membership and Procedure) Amendment Regulations 1990

Made

23rd August 1990

Laid before Parliament

24th August 1990

Coming into force

17th September 1990

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred by section 128(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and paragraph 2(3) of Schedule 1 to the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Regional and District Health Authorities (Membership and Procedure) Amendment Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 17th September 1990.

## 2. University non-officer member for Bloomsbury and Islington Health Authority

In Part I of Schedule 1 to the Regional and District Health Authorities (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 1990[^f00003]for the words “Bloomsbury Health Authority” there shall be substituted the words “Bloomsbury and Islington Health Authority”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Stephen Dorrell

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

Date 23rd August 1990

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Part I of Schedule 1 to the Regional and District Health Authorities (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 1990 which lists District Health Authorities in England which are to have at least one non-officer member who holds a post in a university with a medical or dental school, by substituting for “Bloomsbury Health Authority”, “Bloomsbury and Islington Health Authority.” This is in consequence of the merger of Bloomsbury Health Authority with Islington Health Authority which took place under the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1990 and the National Health Service (Determination of Regions and Districts) Amendment Order 1990.
