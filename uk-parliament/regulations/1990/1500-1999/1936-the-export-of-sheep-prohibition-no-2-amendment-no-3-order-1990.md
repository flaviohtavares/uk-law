# The Export of Sheep (Prohibition) (No. 2) Amendment No. 3 Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1936

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Export of Sheep (Prohibition) (No. 2) Amendment No. 3 Order 1990

Made

22nd September 1990

Coming into force

1st October 1990

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 11 and 86 of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Export of Sheep (Prohibition) (No. 2) Amendment No. 3 Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st October 1990.

## 2. Amendment of the Export of Sheep (Prohibition) (No. 2) Order 1987

The Export of Sheep (Prohibition) (No. 2) Order 1987[^f00002] is hereby amended by substituting for article 2(4)(b) the following sub-paragraph—

> - (b) any sheep which was marked, as a condition of the granting of a consent under section 2(1) of that Act to its removal from a designated area—
>   - (i) with an apricot mark, in the case of a consent given on or before 29th July 1990; or
>   - (ii) with a green mark, in the case of a consent given on or before 1st October 1989; or
>   - (iii) with a blue mark, in the case of a consent given on or before 14th January 1990.

.

## 3. Revocation

The Export of Sheep (Prohibition) (No. 2) Amendment No. 2 Order 1990[^f00003] is revoked.

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 22nd September 1990.

John Selwyn Gummer

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Export of Sheep (Prohibition) (No. 2) Order 1987, as amended, (“the principal Order”) which prohibits, subject to exceptions, the export from Great Britain to a Member State of the European Communities of—

- (a) sheep moved from a place in an area designated for the purposes of Part I of the Food and Environment Protection Act [1985 (c. 48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/48) in accordance with the terms of a consent under section 2(1) of that Act which required that the sheep to which it applies should be marked in a specified manner; and
- (b) sheep which have been moved in contravention of a restriction imposed by an Order made under section 1 of that Act.

The principal Order does not apply to those sheep which have been examined and subsequently marked with an ear-tag by an officer authorised by the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland or Wales or the Department of Agriculture for Northern Ireland or to those sheep moved from a place in a designated area in accordance with the terms of a consent given on or before 30th July 1989, 1st October 1989 or 14th January 1990 which required them to be marked with an apricot, green or blue mark respectively. This Order extends the exception in the principal Order to any sheep moved from a place in a designated area in accordance with the terms of a consent given on or before 29th July 1990, which required it to be marked with an apricot mark (article 2).

The Order revokes and replaces the Export of Sheep (Prohibition) (No. 2) Amendment No. 2 Order 1990 (article 3).
