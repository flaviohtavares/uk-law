# The Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Disciplinary Committee) (Procedure and Evidence) (Amendment) Rules Order of Council 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1959

VETERINARY SURGEONS

The Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Disciplinary Committee) (Procedure and Evidence) (Amendment) Rules Order of Council 1990

Made

28th September 1990

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 28th day of September 1990

By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Whereas in pursuance of paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 2 to the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966[^f00001] the Council of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons has made rules entitled “The Disciplinary Committee (Procedure and Evidence) (Amendment) Rules 1990” :

And whereas by section 25(1) of the said Act such rules shall not have effect unless approved by Order of the Privy Council:

Now, therefore, Their Lordships, having taken the said rules into consideration, are hereby pleased to approve the same as set out in the Schedule to this Order.

This Order may be cited as the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Disciplinary Committee) (Procedure and Evidence) (Amendment) Rules Order of Council 1990.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — THE DISCIPLINARY COMMITTEE (PROCEDURE AND EVIDENCE) (AMENDMENT) RULES 1990

The Council of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons, in exercise of its powers under paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 2 to the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966 hereby makes the following Rules–

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Disciplinary Committee (Procedure and Evidence) (Amendment) Rules 1990.

**2.** Rule 19 of the Disciplinary Committee (Procedure and Evidence) Rules 1967[^f00002] shall be amended by the addition of the following paragraph–

> (4) For any aspect of the conduct of the proceedings for which no specific provision is made in these Rules, the procedure shall be such as the Chairman shall determine.

Pursuant to a resolution passed at a Meeting of the Council of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons on the seventh day of June 1990, the Common Seal of the said Royal College was affixed hereto on the said seventh day of June 1990 in the presence of

H. E. Carter

President

A. Steele-Bodger

Michael Young

Members of Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Rules approved by this Order amend the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners Disciplinary Committee (Procedure and Evidence) Rules 1967 empowering the Chairman of the Disciplinary Committee to determine the procedure of any aspect of the conduct of the proceedings not already covered by specific provision in the Rules.
