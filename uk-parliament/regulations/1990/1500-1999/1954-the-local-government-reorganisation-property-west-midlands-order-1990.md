# The Local Government Reorganisation (Property)(West Midlands) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1954

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Reorganisation (Property)(West Midlands) Order 1990

Made

26th September 1990

Laid before Parliament

4th October 1990

Coming into force

25th October 1990

Whereas the West Midlands Residuary Body, acting pursuant to section 67(2) of the Local Government Act 1985[^f00001] has submitted proposals to the Secretary of State for the transfer of certain property, rights and liabilities to local authorities, and he has decided to give effect to those proposals;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 67(3) of that Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Reorganisation (Property)(West Midlands) Order 1990, and shall come into force on 25th October 1990.

## 2. Transfer of property

All interests of the West Midlands Residuary Body in the land described in Column 1 of the Schedule to this Order shall vest in the local authority specified in Column 2.

## 3. Rights and liabilities

All rights and liabilities of the West Midlands Residuary Body in relation to the land transferred by article 2, and all records relating exclusively to such land, shall vest in the local authority to which it is so transferred.

Chris Patten

Secretary of State for the Environment
