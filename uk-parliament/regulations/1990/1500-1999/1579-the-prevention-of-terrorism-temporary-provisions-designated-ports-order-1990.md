# The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions)(Designated Ports) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1579

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions)(Designated Ports) Order 1990

Made

31st July 1990

Coming into force

3rd September 1990

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 16(3) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) (Designated Ports) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 3rd September 1990.

**2.** In Part I of Schedule 6 to the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989, there shall be omitted, under the heading “Seaports”, the word “Southampton”.

David Waddington

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

31st July 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order removes Southampton from the list of seaports designated, for the purposes of paragraph 8 of Schedule 5 to the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989, in Part I of Schedule 6 to that Act.
