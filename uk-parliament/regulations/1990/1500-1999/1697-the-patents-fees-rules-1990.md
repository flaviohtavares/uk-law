# The Patents (Fees) Rules 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1697

PATENTS

The Patents (Fees) Rules 1990

Made

16th August 1990

Laid before Parliament

20th August 1990

Coming into force

10th September 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 123(1), (2) and (3) of, and paragraph 14 of Schedule 4 to, the Patents Act 1977[^f00001], of the power conferred upon him by the Department of Trade and Industry (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with the Council on Tribunals pursuant to section 10(1) of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1971[^f00003] and with the consent of the Treasury pursuant to subsection (4) of the said section 123, hereby makes the following Rules:—

**1.** (1) These Rules may be cited as the Patents (Fees) Rules 1990 and shall come into force on 10th September 1990.

(2) The Patents (Fees) Rules 1989[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

**2.** These Rules shall be construed as one with the Patent Rules 1982[^f00005] and, to the extent that they continue to apply in relation to existing patents and existing applications, to the Patents Rules 1968[^f00006].

**3.** The fees to be paid in respect of any matters arising under the Patents Act 1949[^f00007] or the Patents Act 1977 shall be those specified in the Schedule to these Rules; and in any case where a form specified in the Schedule as the corresponding form in relation to any matter is required by the Patents Rules 1968 or the Patents Rules 1982 to be used, that form shall be accompanied by the fee specified in respect of that matter or, where payment may be made within a prescribed period of time after the form has been filed, the fee so specified shall be paid within that period.

Douglas Hogg

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules revoke and replace the Patents (Fees) Rules 1989. All fees payable under the Patents Act 1977 with the exception of those on forms 1/77, 20/77, 40/77, 41/77, 44/77, 49/77 and, under the Patents Act 1949 on forms 8, 23, 33, 36, 39, 43, 44, 45, 46, 53, 54, 55, 64, 65 and 68 and in respect of certain forms dealing with international applications are increased with effect from 10th September 1990. It is estimated that the total amount of fees collected per annum at the new levels will be 8 per cent greater than the amount collected per annum at the existing levels.
