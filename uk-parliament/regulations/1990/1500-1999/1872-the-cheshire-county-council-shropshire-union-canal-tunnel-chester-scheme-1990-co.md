# The Cheshire County Council (Shropshire Union Canal Tunnel, Chester) Scheme 1990 Confirmation Instrument 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1872

HIGHWAYS ENGLAND AND WALES

The Cheshire County Council (Shropshire Union Canal Tunnel, Chester) Scheme 1990 Confirmation Instrument 1990

Made

10th September 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Instrument in exercise of powers conferred by section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The Cheshire County Council (Shropshire Union Canal Tunnel, Chester) Scheme 1990 is hereby confirmed without modification.

**2.** The said Scheme as confirmed (hereinafter known as “the Scheme”) is set out in the Schedule hereto and copies of the Scheme with its accompanying plans are deposited at the offices of the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY; The North West Network Management Division, Department of Transport, Sunley Tower, Piccadilly Plaza, Manchester M1 4BE; The Cheshire County Council, County Hall, Chester and The Chester City Council, Town Hall, Chester.

**3.** The Scheme will become operative on the date on which notice that it has been confirmed is first published in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Highways Act 1980.

**4.** This Instrument can be cited as the Cheshire County Council (Shropshire Union Canal Tunnel, Chester) Scheme 1990 Confirmation Instrument 1990.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

D. C. Renshaw

Regional Director North West Region

Department of Transport

10th September 1990
