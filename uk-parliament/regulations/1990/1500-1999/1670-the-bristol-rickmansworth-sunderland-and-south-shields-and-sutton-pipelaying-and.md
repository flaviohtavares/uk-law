# The Bristol, Rickmansworth, Sunderland and South Shields and Sutton (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Codes of Practice) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1670

WATER, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Bristol, Rickmansworth, Sunderland and South Shields and Sutton (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Codes of Practice) Order 1990

Made

3rd August 1990

Whereas Bristol Waterworks Company, Rickmansworth Water Company, Sunderland and South Shields Water Company and The Sutton District Water Plc each hold an appointment under Chapter I of Part II of the Water Act 1989[^f00001] as a water undertaker (“the Companies”):

And whereas each company has, pursuant to section 162(6)(a) of that Act, submitted to the Secretary of State for his approval a Code of Practice[^f00002] with respect to the exercise by the Companies of powers conferred by or by virtue of paragraph 4 of Schedule 19 to that Act (“the Codes of Practice”):

And whereas the Secretary of State considers it appropriate to approve the Codes of Practice for the purpose of promoting what appear to him to be desirable practices with regard to the exercise by the Companies of those powers:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 162(7) of the Water Act 1989, after consulting all such persons as he considers it appropriate to consult, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Bristol, Rickmansworth, Sunderland and South Shields and Sutton (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Codes of Practice) Order 1990.

## 2. Codes of Practice

The Codes of Practice prepared by the Companies and certified as such by the Secretary of State are hereby approved.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment

Dinah Nichols

An Under Secretary in the

Department of the Environment

3rd August 1990
