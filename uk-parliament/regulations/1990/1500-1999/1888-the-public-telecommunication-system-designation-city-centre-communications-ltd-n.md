# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (City Centre Communications Ltd) (North West London) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1888

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation (City Centre Communications Ltd) (North West London) Order 1990

Made

17th September 1990

Laid before Parliament

24th September 1990

Coming into force

22nd October 1990

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to City Centre Communications Ltd on 31st August 1990 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to the Licence (“the Applicable Cabled Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (City Centre Communications Ltd) (North West London) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 22nd October 1990.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

Douglas Hogg

Minister of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

17th September 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to City Centre Communications Ltd on 31st August 1990 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the whole of the London Boroughs of Barnet, Brent, and Hammersmith and Fulham. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 24th September 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, City Centre Communications Ltd will be a public telecommunications operator in respect of its North West London public telecommunications systems when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Atlantic House, Holborn Viaduct, London EC1N 2HQ.
