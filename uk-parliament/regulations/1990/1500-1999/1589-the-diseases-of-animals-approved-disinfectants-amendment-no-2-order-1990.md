# The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants)(Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1589

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants)(Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1990

Made

23rd July 1990

Coming into force

31st July 1990

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1, 7(1)(a), (b) and (c) and (2), 23(f) and (g) and 86(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 31st July 1990.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order the “principal Order” means the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978[^f00002].

## 3. Amendment of principal Order

(1) At the end of Schedule 1 to the principal Order (which lists disinfectants approved by the Minister) there shall be added the entries listed in Schedule 1 to this Order.

(2) The entries listed in Schedule 2 to this Order shall be deleted from Schedule 1 to the principal Order.

(3) For Schedule 2 to the principal Order (which lists disinfectants subject to transitional provisions) there shall be substituted the provisions of Schedule 3 to this Order.

(4) In article 6(2) of the principal Order (which specifies the date until which disinfectants subject to transitional provisions remain approved) for the words “30th June 1990” there shall be substituted the words “31st December 1990”.

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 23rd July 1990.

This Order further amends the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978 by adding to the list of approved disinfectants in that Order those newly approved disinfectants which are mentioned in Schedule 1 to this Order (article 3(1))and by deleting from that list the disinfectant mentioned in Schedule 2 (article 3(2)). The disinfectant deleted may, however, continue to be used as approved disinfectant until 31st December 1990 (article 3(3)and (4)).
