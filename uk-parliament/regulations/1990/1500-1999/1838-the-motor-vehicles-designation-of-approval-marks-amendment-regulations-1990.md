# The Motor Vehicles (Designation of Approval Marks)(Amendment) Regulations 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1838

TRADE DESCRIPTIONS

The Motor Vehicles (Designation of Approval Marks)(Amendment) Regulations 1990

Made

6th September 1990

Laid before Parliament

10th September 1990

Coming into force

1st October 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 80(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with the provisions of section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Designation of Approval Marks) (Amendment) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 1st October 1990.

**2.** The Motor Vehicles (Designation of Approval Marks) Regulations 1979[^f00002] shall be further amended in accordance with the following provisions of these regulations.

**3.** In Schedule 2 after item 54 there shall be added the following item—

> | 70 | 002439 [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1838/images/uksi_19901838_en_001) | Rear marking plates for heavy and long vehicles. | The rear marking plate | 70 15th May 1987 | 1 and 2 |

.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Patrick McLoughlin

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

6th September 1990

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Designation of Approval Marks) Regulations 1979 by prescribing a new item 70 in Schedule 2, being a marking relating to rear marking plates under ECE Regulation 70.

Copies of the ECE Regulation can be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office. The details of the ECE Regulation are as follows:—

- Regulation 70 dated 27th July 1987 and annexed to the Agreement concerning the adoption of uniform conditions of approval for motor vehicle equipment and parts and reciprocal recognition thereof concluded at Geneva on 2nd March 1958 (Cmnd. 2535) as amended (Cmnd. 3562) to which the United Kingdom is a party by an agreement of accession dated 14th January 1963 deposited with the Secretary General of the United Nations on 15th January 1963.
