# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Cambridge Cable Limited) (Cambridge) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1759

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Cambridge Cable Limited) (Cambridge) Order 1990

Made

22nd August 1990

Laid before Parliament

29th August 1990

Coming into force

26th September 1990

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to Cambridge Cable Limited on 13th July 1990 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to the Licence (“the Applicable Cabled Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Cambridge Cable Limited) (Cambridge) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 26th September 1990.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

Tim Sainsbury

Minister for Trade,

Department of Trade and Industry

22nd August 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Cambridge Cable Limited on 13th July 1990 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the whole of the Cambridge district, together with parts of the districts of South Cambridgeshire, East Cambridgeshire, and Huntingdonshire; part of the district of Forest Heath in the County of Suffolk; part of the district of Uttlesford in the County of Essex; and part of the district of North Hertfordshire. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 29th August 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Cambridge Cable Limited will be a public telecommunications operator in respect of its Cambridge public telecommunication systems when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4N 7JJ.
