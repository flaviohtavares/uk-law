# The Redundancy Payments (Merchant Seamen Exclusion)Order 1973 (Revocation) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1583

TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT

The Redundancy Payments (Merchant Seamen Exclusion)Order 1973 (Revocation) Order 1990

Made

26th July 1990

Coming into force

30th September 1990

Whereas a draft of the following Order was laid before Parliament in accordance with section 149(4) of the Employment Protection (Consolidation) Act 1978[^f00001] (“the 1978 Act”) and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 149(1)(a) and section 154(4) of the 1978 Act and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Redundancy Payments (Merchant Seamen Exclusion) Order 1973 (Revocation) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 30th September 1990.

## 2. Revocation

The Redundancy Payments (Merchant Seamen Exclusion) Order 1973[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Signed by order of the Secretary of State

Eric Forth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Employment

26thJuly 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Redundancy Payments (Merchant Seamen Exclusion) Order 1973 (“the 1973 Order”) which excluded certain employments as a merchant seaman from the redundancy payments provisions of the Employment Protection (Consolidation) Act 1978. The effect of the revocation is to apply those provisions to merchant seamen to whom the 1973 Order applied.
