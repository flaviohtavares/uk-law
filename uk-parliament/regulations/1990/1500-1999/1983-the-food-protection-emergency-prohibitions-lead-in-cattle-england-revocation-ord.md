# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Lead in Cattle) (England) (Revocation) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1983

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Lead in Cattle) (England) (Revocation) Order 1990

Made

4th October 1990

Laid before Parliament

5th October 1990

Coming into force

5th October 1990

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(1) and (2) and section 24(1) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Lead in Cattle) (England) (Revocation) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 5th October 1990.

## 2. Revocation

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Lead in Cattle) (England) Order 1990[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 4th October 1990.

John Selwyn Gummer

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Lead in Cattle) (England) Order 1990 which contained emergency prohibitions in order to prevent human consumption of food which may have been rendered unsuitable for that purpose in consequence of animals consuming lead.
