# The Public Telecommunication System Designation(Derbyshire Cablevision Limited) (Derby) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1970

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation(Derbyshire Cablevision Limited) (Derby) Order 1990

Made

28th September 1990

Laid before Parliament

3rd October 1990

Coming into force

31st October 1990

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to Derbyshire Cablevision Limited on 3rd September 1990 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to the Licence (“the Applicable Cabled Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Derbyshire Cablevision Limited) (Derby) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 31st October 1990.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

John Redwood

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

28th September 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Derbyshire Cablevision Limited on 3rd September 1990a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the whole of the City of Derby and small parts of the Borough of Erewash and the District of South Derbyshire. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 3rd October 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Derbyshire Cablevision Limited will be a public telecommunications operator in respect of its Derby public telecommunication systems when the Order comes into force.

A copy of that licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
