# Authorities for London Post-Graduate Teaching Hospitals (Constitution) Amendment Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1855

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

Authorities for London Post-Graduate Teaching Hospitals (Constitution) Amendment Order 1990

Made

12th September 1990

Laid before Parliament

17th September 1990

Coming into force

8th October 1990

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 11 and 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Authorities for London Post-Graduate Teaching Hospitals (Constitution) Amendment Order 1990 and shall come into force on 8th October 1990.

## 2. Amendments to 1990 Order

The Authorities for London Post-Graduate Teaching Hospitals (Constitution) Order 1990[^f00002] is amended:

- (a) by the substitution for the word “to” in the third place where it occurs in article 3 of the word “shall”;
- (b) by the insertion before the words “Eastman Dental Hospital” in articles 5(2), 5(3) and 7(d) of the words “Board of Governors of the”;
- (c) by the substitution for the words “National Heart and Chest Hospital” in column (2) in Schedule 1 and in column (1) in Schedule 2 of the words “Royal Brompton National Heart and Lung Hospitals”;
- (d) by the substitution for the entry “The Brompton Hospital, London SW1” in column (2) in Schedule 2 of the entry “The Royal Brompton and National Heart Hospital, London SW3”.

## 3. Transitional provisions

Nothing in article 2 of this Order shall affect any right or obligation of any body or person, and any document or instrument (whether made before or after the coming into force of this Order) which refers to the Board of Governors of the National Heart and Chest Hospital shall be read as if it referred to the Board of Governors of the Royal Brompton National Heart and Lung Hospitals.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Virginia Bottomley

Minister of State,

Department of Health

12th September 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Authorities for London Post-Graduate Teaching Hospitals (Constitution) Order 1990 by changing the name of the former Board of Governors of the National Heart and Chest Hospital to the Board of Governors of the Royal Brompton National Heart and Lung Hospitals.

The opportunity has been taken to amend the references in the 1990 Constitution Order to the Eastman Dental Hospital. These references should have been to the Board of Governors of the Eastman Dental Hospital.
