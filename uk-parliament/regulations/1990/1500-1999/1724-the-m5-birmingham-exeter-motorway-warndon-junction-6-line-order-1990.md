# The M5 Birmingham-Exeter Motorway (Warndon Junction 6) Line Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1724

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The M5 Birmingham-Exeter Motorway (Warndon Junction 6) Line Order 1990

Made

15th August 1990

Coming into force

31st August 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 41 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The new highway which the Secretary of State proposes to construct along the route described in the Schedule to this Order shall become a trunk road as from the date when this Order comes into force.

**2.** The centre line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the deposited plan.

**3.** The Secretary of State directs as respects any part of a highway which crosses the route of the new trunk road that —

- (a) where the highway is a highway maintainable at the public expense by a local highway authority, the part in question shall be maintained by that authority, and
- (b) where the highway is not a highway so maintainable and is not maintainable under a special enactment or by reason of tenure, enclosure or prescription, the Secretary of State shall be under no duty to maintain the part in question, until,

in either case, a date to be specified in a notice given by the Secretary of State to the highway authority for that highway. The date specified will not be later than the date on which the relevant route is opened for the purposes of through traffic.

**4.** In this Order:—

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| - (i) “the deposited plan” | means the plan numbered HA 10/2 WM 143 marked “The M5 Birmingham Exeter Motor-way (Warndon Junction 6) Line Order 1990” signed by authority of the Secretary of State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY; |
| - (ii) “the new trunk road” | means the highway mentioned in article 1 of this order; |
| - (iii) “the trunk road” | means the Worcester — south of Stafford Trunk Road (A449). |

**5.** This Order shall come into force on 31st August 1990 and may be cited as the M5 Birmingham-Exeter Motorway (Warndon Junction 6) Line Order 1990.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

D. R. Ritchie

Regional Director West Midland Region

Department of Transport

15th August 1990

## SCHEDULE — ROUTE OF THE NEW TRUNK ROAD

The route of the new trunk road is a route at Warndon in the County of Hereford and Worcester and is a roundabout over the M5 Birmingham-Exeter Motorway at Junction 6 to connect the trunk road with Pershore Lane (A4538) and Warndon Lane (U51148).
