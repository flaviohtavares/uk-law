# The Aerodromes (Designation) (Detention and Sale of Aircraft) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1527

CIVIL AVIATION

The Aerodromes (Designation) (Detention and Sale of Aircraft) Order 1990

Made

24th July 1990

Laid before Parliament

31st July 1990

Coming into force

21st August 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 88(10) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Aerodromes (Designation) (Detention and Sale of Aircraft) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 21st August 1990.

**2.** The aerodromes specified in the Schedule hereto are hereby designated for the purposes of section 88 of the Civil Aviation Act 1982.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Patrick McLoughlin

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

24th July 1990

## SCHEDULE

- Exeter
- Humberside
- Liverpool

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 88 of the Civil Aviation Act 1982 empowers the person owning or managing an aerodrome to which the section applies to detain and sell aircraft where default is made in the payment of airport charges. Section 88(10), as amended by the Airports Act 1986, provides that the section applies to any aerodrome owned or managed by any government department or a local authority, other than a district council in Scotland, and to any other aerodrome designated for the purposes of the section by an order made by the Secretary of State. This Order designates the airports listed in the Schedule as aerodromes to which section 88 applies.
