# The Design Right (Proceedings before Comptroller)(Amendment) (No. 2) Rules 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 1699

DESIGNS

The Design Right (Proceedings before Comptroller)(Amendment) (No. 2) Rules 1990

Made

16th August 1990

Laid before Parliament

20th August 1990

Coming into force

10th September 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 250 of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^f00001], with the consent of the Treasury pursuant to subsection (3) of that section as to the fees prescribed under these Rules and after consultation with the Council on Tribunals in accordance with section 10(1) of the Tribunal and Inquiries Act 1971[^f00002], hereby makes the following Rules:—

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Design Right (Proceedings before Comptroller) (Amendment) (No. 2) Rules 1990 and shall come into force on 10th September 1990.

**2.** The Design Right (Proceedings before Comptroller) Rules 1989[^f00003] are amended by substituting for Schedule 2 thereto the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Rules.

Douglas Hogg

Department of Trade and Industry

13thAugust 1990

We consent to the fees prescribed in these Rules.

Michael Fallon

Kenneth Carlisle

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

16th August 1990

SCHEDULE

## SCHEDULE 2 — FEES

Rule 24

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **1.** On reference of dispute (Form 1) under rule 3(1) | £54 |
| **2.** On application (Form 2) under rule 7(1) | £30 |
| **3.** On application (Form 3) under rule 10(1) | £54 |
| **4.** On application (Form 4) under rule 14(1) | £54 |

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules replace Schedule 2 to the Design Right (Proceedings before Comptroller) Rules 1989 to increase (by 8 per cent) from £50 to £54 the fees for the matters referred to in items 1, 3 and 4.
