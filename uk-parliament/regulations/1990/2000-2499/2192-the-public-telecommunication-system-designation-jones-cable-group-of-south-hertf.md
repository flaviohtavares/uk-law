# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Jones Cable Group of South Hertfordshire Limited) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 2192

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Jones Cable Group of South Hertfordshire Limited) Order 1990

Made

2nd November 1990

Laid before Parliament

13th November 1990

Coming into force

11th December 1990

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to Jones Cable Group of South Hertfordshire Limited on 15th October 1990 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984([^f00001]) (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to the Licence (“the Applicable Cabled Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Jones Cable Group of South Hertfordshire Limited) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 11th December 1990.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

Douglas Hogg

Minister of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

2nd November 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Jones Cable Group of South Hertfordshire Limited on 15th October 1990 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the South Hertfordshire area. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 13th November 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Jones Cable Group of South Hertfordshire Limited will be a public telecommunications operator in respect of its South Hertfordshire public telecommunication systems when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
