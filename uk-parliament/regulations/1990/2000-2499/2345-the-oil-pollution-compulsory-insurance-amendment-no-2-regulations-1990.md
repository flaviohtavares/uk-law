# The Oil Pollution (Compulsory Insurance) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 2345

MARINE POLLUTION

The Oil Pollution (Compulsory Insurance) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1990

Made

23rd November 1990

Laid before Parliament

4th December 1990

Coming into force

1st January 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 11(3)(a) of the Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) Act 1971[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Oil Pollution (Compulsory Insurance) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 1st January 1991.

(2) The Oil Pollution (Compulsory Insurance) (Amendment) Regulations 1990[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

**2.** In regulation 5 (fees for certificates) of the Oil Pollution (Compulsory Insurance) Regulations 1981[^f00003] for “£25” there shall be substituted “£33”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Lord Brabazon of Tara

Minister of State,

Department of Transport

23rd November 1990

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Oil Pollution (Compulsory Insurance) Regulations 1981 by increasing the fee to be paid on application for a certificate of compulsory insurance from £25 to £33.
