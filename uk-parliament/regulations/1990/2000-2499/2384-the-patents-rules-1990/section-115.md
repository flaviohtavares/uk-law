# The Patents Rules 1990, section 115

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 115. The Journal

(1) The comptroller shall publish a journal containing particulars of applications for patents and other proceedings under the Act and any other information that he may deem to be generally useful or important.

(2) The journal shall be entitled “The Official Journal (Patents)”.

(3) Unless the comptroller otherwise directs, the Journal shall be published weekly.
