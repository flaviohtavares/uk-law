# The Patents Rules 1990, section 119

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 119. Certified copies

A request under rule 20.9 of those Regulations for a certified copy of an international application as filed with the Patent Office as receiving office and of any corrections thereto shall be made on Patents Form 24/77 and shall be accompanied by the appropriate fee.
