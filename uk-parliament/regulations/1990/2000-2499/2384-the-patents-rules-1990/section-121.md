# The Patents Rules 1990, section 121

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 121. Additional fees for further inventions

Where under rule 68.2 of those Regulations the Patent Office, as International Preliminary Examining Authority, reports to the applicant that the requirement of unity of invention is not complied with and the applicant decides to pay an additional fee in respect of each invention other than the main invention, the additional fee payable to the Patent Office in respect of each invention shall not exceed the appropriate prescribed preliminary examination fee.
