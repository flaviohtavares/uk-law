# The Patents Rules 1990, section 117

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 117. Filing of applications

(1) Where the Patent Office is the competent receiving office under the Patent Co-operation Treaty, an international application shall be filed at it in English in triplicate.

(2) If less than three copies of the international application are so filed, upon preparation by the Patent Office of the number of copies of it required to bring the total to three, the applicant shall on demand pay to the Patent Office the appropriate charge.
