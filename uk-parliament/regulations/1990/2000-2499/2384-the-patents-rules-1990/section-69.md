# The Patents Rules 1990, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Application by Crown under section 51

An application under section 51(1) for an order or entry under section 51(3) shall be made on Patents Form 34/77 and shall be accompanied by a statement of the facts upon which the applicant relies and evidence verifying the statement.
