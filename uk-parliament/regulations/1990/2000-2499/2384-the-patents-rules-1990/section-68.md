# The Patents Rules 1990, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Application under section 48(1) for compulsory licence

An application under section 48(1) shall be made on Patents Form 33/77 and shall be accompanied by a statement in duplicate of the facts upon which the applicant relies and evidence in duplicate verifying the statement.
