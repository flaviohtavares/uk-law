# The Patents Rules 1990, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Procedure after cancellation of entry pursuant to section 47(3)

Where the comptroller cancels an entry in the register pursuant to section 47(3), he shall inform the proprietor of the patent who shall, within such period as the comptroller specifies, file Patents Form 12/77, accompanied by fees to the amount of the balance of all renewal fees which would have been payable if the entry had not been made.
