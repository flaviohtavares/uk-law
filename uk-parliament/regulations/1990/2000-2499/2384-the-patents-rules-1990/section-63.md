# The Patents Rules 1990, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Application by proprietor under section 47(1) for cancellation of entry

An application under section 47(1) shall be made on Patents Form 30/77 and shall be accompanied by Patents Form 12/77 and fees to the amount of the balance of all renewal fees which would have been payable if the entry had not been made.
