# The Patents Rules 1990, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Application under section 46(1) for entry in the register

(1) An application under section 46(1) shall be made on Patents Form 28/77.

(2) Every entry made in the register consequent upon such an application shall be published in the Journal and in such other manner (if any) as the comptroller thinks necessary.
