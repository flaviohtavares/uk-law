# The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 2151

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1990

Made

31st October 1990

Coming into force

1st December 1990

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 31st day of October 1990

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of sections 25(6) and 28(3) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989,[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st December 1990.

**2.** The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Isle of Man) Order 1984[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Isle of Man) Order 1984, which extended that Act to the Isle of Man with exceptions, adaptations and modifications. The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989 which repealed and replaced the 1984 Act is not extended to the Isle of Man, where a corresponding Act of Tynwald applies.
