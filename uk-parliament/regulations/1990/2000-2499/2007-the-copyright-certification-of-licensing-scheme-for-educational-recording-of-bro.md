# The Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts) (Guild Sound and Vision Limited) (Revocation) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 2007

COPYRIGHT

The Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts) (Guild Sound and Vision Limited) (Revocation) Order 1990

Made

8th October 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 143(5) of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts) (Guild Sound and Vision Limited) (Revocation) Order 1990.

**2.** The Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts) (Guild Sound and Vision Limited) Order 1990[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Douglas Hogg

Minister for Industry and Enterprise,

Department of Trade and Industry

8th October 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under section 143(2) of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (“the Act”), the Secretary of State certified with effect from 30th May 1990 a licensing scheme operated by Guild Sound and Vision Limited for the purposes of section 35 of the Act (recordings by educational establishments of broadcasts and cable programmes).

The Secretary of State is required by section 143(5) of the Act to revoke the order certifying the scheme where it ceases to be operated. The Guild Sound and Vision Limited scheme ceased to be operated on 28th September 1990. This Order therefore revokes the earlier Order certifying the scheme.
