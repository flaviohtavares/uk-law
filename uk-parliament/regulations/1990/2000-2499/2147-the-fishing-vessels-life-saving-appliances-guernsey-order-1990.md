# The Fishing Vessels (Life-Saving Appliances) (Guernsey) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 2147

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Fishing Vessels (Life-Saving Appliances) (Guernsey) Order 1990

Made

31st October 1990

Laid before Parliament

8th November 1990

Coming into force

1st December 1990

At the Court at Buckingham Palace , the 31st day of October 1990

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 47(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1979[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Fishing Vessels (Life-Saving Appliances) (Guernsey) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st December 1990.

**2.** In this Order, the expression “the Bailiwick” means the Bailiwick of Guernsey and the territorial waters adjacent thereto.

**3.** The Fishing Vessels (Life-Saving Appliances) Regulations 1988[^f00002] shall extend to the Bailiwick as part of its law, with the modifications specified in the Schedule to this Order.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — MODIFICATIONS IN THE EXTENSION OF THE FISHING VESSELS (LIFE-SAVING APPLIANCES) REGULATIONS 1988 TO THE BAILIWICK OF GUERNSEY

Article 3

**1.** Any reference to a provision made by or under an enactment shall be construed, unless the contrary intention appears, as a reference to that provision as it has effect in the Bailiwick.

**2.** In regulation 1(2) —

- (a) for “15th February 1988” there shall be substituted “the date on which the Fishing Vessels (Life-Saving Appliances) (Guernsey) Order 1990 comes into force”;
- (b) for “On 15th April 1988” there shall be substituted “2 months after that Order comes into force”;
- (c) for “15th April 1988” wherever else occurring there shall be substituted “the relevant date”;
- (d) for “on 15th July 1988” there shall be substituted “5 months after that Order comes into force”;
- (e) for “on 15th October 1988” there shall be substituted “8 months after that Order comes into force”, and
- (f) for “on 15th January” there shall be substituted “11 months after that Order comes into force”.

**3.** (1) In regulation 2(1) —

- (a) after the definition of “person” there shall be inserted the following definition:

  > - “relevant date” means the date which falls 2 months after the Fishing Vessels (Life-Saving Appliances) (Guernsey) Order 1990 comes into force;

  , and
- (b) the definition of “United Kingdom fishing vessel” shall be omitted.

(2) For regulation 2(4) there shall be substituted the following paragraph:

> (4) These Regulations apply to fishing vessels registered in the Bailiwick.

.

**4.** In regulation 3(1) —

- (a) for “until 15th April 1992” there shall be substituted “during the period ending 4 years after the relevant date”,and
- (b) for “15th March 1988” there shall be substituted “the date which falls one month after the Fishing Vessels (Life-Saving Appliances) (Guernsey) Order 1990 comes into force”.

**5.** In regulation 7 —

- (a) for “section 692(1)—(3) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894[^f00003]” there shall be substituted “section67 of the Merchant Shipping (Guernsey) Law 1915”, and
- (b) for “subject to the modification that” there shall be substituted “as if”.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the Fishing Vessels (Life-Saving Appliances) Regulations 1988 to the Bailiwick of Guernsey with the modifications specified in the Schedule to the Order.
