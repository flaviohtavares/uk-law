# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (York Telecommunications Complex Limited) Order 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 2376

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Public Telecommunication System Designation (York Telecommunications Complex Limited) Order 1990

Made

29th November 1990

Laid before Parliament

4th December 1990

Coming into force

1st January 1991

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to York Telecommunications Complex Limited on 29th October 1990 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to the Licence (“the Applicable Cabled Systems”):

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (York Telecommunications Complex Limited) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st January 1991.

**2.** Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

John Redwood

Minister of State

Department of Trade and Industry

29th November 1990

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to York Telecommunications Complex Limited on 29th October 1990 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the whole of the City of York and adjacent parts of the Districts of Selby and Ryedale. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 4th December 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, York Telecommunications Complex Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4N 7JJ.
