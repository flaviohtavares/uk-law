# The Revenue Support Grant (Specified Bodies) Regulations 1990

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1990 No. 2004

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Revenue Support Grant (Specified Bodies) Regulations 1990

Made

4th October 1990

Laid before Parliament

12th October 1990

Coming into force

2nd November 1990

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, after consultation with such representatives of local government as appear to them to be appropriate, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 76(4) and 143(1) of the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:–

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Revenue Support Grant (Specified Bodies) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 2nd November 1990.

## 2. Specified Body

The Local Government Management Board is hereby specified for the purposes of section 76(4) of the Local Government Finance Act 1988.

## 3. Revocation

In relation to any financial year beginning on or after 1st April 1991, the Rate Support Grant (Specified Bodies) Regulations 1988[^f00002] are hereby amended by the deletion, in regulation 2, of the following entries:

> - (a) The Local Government Training Board;

> - (d) The Local Authority Management Services and Computer Committee (LAMSAC);
> - (e) The Local Authorities' Conditions of Service Advisory Board;

.

Chris Patten

Secretary of State for the Environment

2nd October 1990

David Hunt

Secretary of State for Wales

4th October 1990

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Sections 78 and 79 of the Local Government Finance Act 1988 provide for revenue support grant to be payable to any body which provides services for local authorities and which is specified by the Secretary of State in regulations.

These Regulations specify the Local Government Management Board. The Rate Support Grant (Specified Bodies) Regulations 1988 are revoked in so far as those Regulations specify the Local Government Training Board, the Local Authority Management Services and Computer Committee (LAMSAC), and the Local Authorities' Conditions of Service Advisory Board.
