# The Transport (London) Act 1969 (Commencement No.5) Order 1974

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1974 No. 407 (C.10)

TRANSPORT

The Transport (London) Act 1969 (Commencement No.5) Order 1974

Made

6th March 1974

The Secretary of State for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred by section 47 of the Transport (London) Act 1969, and now vested in him[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Transport (London) Act 1969 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1974.

**2.** The provisions of the Transport (London) Act 1969 (hereinafter referred to as “**the Act**”) which are specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order and which relate to the matters respectively specified in relation to those provisions in column 2 of that Schedule, and also the provisions of the Act which are specified in the Appendix to that Schedule, shall come into operation on the 1st April 1974.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

F. J. Ward

An Under Secretary in the Department of the Environment

6th March 1974

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on the 1st April 1974 the provisions of the Transport (London) Act 1969 under which all principal roads in Greater London become metropolitan roads and under which metropolitan roads and proposed metropolitan roads can be divided into different categories for the purposes of the control of development affecting such roads or proposed roads.
