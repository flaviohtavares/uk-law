# The Superannuation (Teaching and Civil Service) Interchange (Revocation) Rules 1974

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1974 No. 777

PENSIONS

The Superannuation (Teaching and Civil Service) Interchange (Revocation) Rules 1974

Made

30th April 1974

Laid before Parliament

10th May 1974

Coming into Operation

1st June 1974

The Secretary of State for Education and Science and the Minister for the Civil Service, in joint exercise of the powers conferred by section 2 of the Superannuation (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1948 as amended by paragraph 25 of schedule 6 to the Superannuation Act 1972 and vested in them[^f00001], hereby make the following rules:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These rules may be cited as the Superannuation (Teaching and Civil Service) Interchange (Revocation) Rules 1974 and shall come into operation on 1st June 1974.

(2) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of these rules as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament; and section 38(2) (effect of repeals) of that Act shall have effect in relation to the rules revoked by these rules as if they were enactments repealed by an Act.

## 2. Revocation of rules

(1) The Superannuation (Teaching and Civil Service) Interchange Rules 1970[^f00002] and the Superannuation (Teaching and Civil Service) Interchange (Amendment) Rules 1972[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not affect the operation of the rules thereby revoked in relation to any person who became, or ceased to be, employed as a teacher in reckonable service or, as the case may be, as a civil servant before 1st June 1972.

Given under the Official Seal of the Secretary of State for Education and Science on 26th April 1974.

Reginald E. Prentice

Secretary of State for Education and Science

Given under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Civil Service on 30th April 1974.

K. H. McNeill

Authorised by the Minister for the Civil Service

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules revoke rules relating to the pensions of persons transferring between teaching and the Civil Service and vice versa. Those rules have now been superseded by the Teachers' Superannuation (Added Years and Inter change) Regulations 1974 (S.I. 1974/260) and the Principal Civil Service Pension Scheme made under section 1 of the Superannuation Act 1972.
