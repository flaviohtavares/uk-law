# The Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1978

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1978 No. 997 (C.25)

MAGISTRATES' COURTS

The Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1978

Made

18th July 1978

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 89(3) of the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1978.

**2.** The provisions of the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978 specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the matters specified in column 2 thereof) shall come into force forthwith.

Merlyn Rees

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

18th July 1978
