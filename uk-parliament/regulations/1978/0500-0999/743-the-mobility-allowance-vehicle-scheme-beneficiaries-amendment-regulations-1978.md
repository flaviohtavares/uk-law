# The Mobility Allowance (Vehicle Scheme Beneficiaries) Amendment Regulations 1978

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1978 No. 743

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Mobility Allowance (Vehicle Scheme Beneficiaries) Amendment Regulations 1978

Made

23rd May 1978

Laid before Parliament

25th May 1978

Coming into Operation

26th May 1978

The Secretary of State for Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 13(1) of the Social Security (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1977 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, without having referred any proposals on the matter to the National Insurance Advisory Committee since it appears to him by reason of urgency that it is inexpedient to do so, hereby makes the following regulations:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These regulations, which may be cited as the Mobility Allowance (Vehicle Scheme Beneficiaries) Amendment Regulations 1978, shall come into operation on 26th May 1978.

(2) The rules for the construction of Acts of Parliament contained in the Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the purposes of the interpretation of these regulations as they apply for the purposes of the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

## 2. Amendment of the Mobility Allowance (Vehicle Scheme Beneficiaries) Regulations 1977

The Mobility Allowance (Vehicle Scheme Beneficiaries) Regulations 1977 shall be amended by substituting in regulation 1(2) (interpretation) for the definition of “vehicle scheme beneficiary” the following:—

> - **\`vehicle scheme beneficiary'** means any person of a class specified in section 13(3)(a), (c) or (d) of the Act or any person of the class specified in section 13(3)(b) of the Act whose application was approved on or after 1st January 1976 and, where an invalid carriage or other vehicle was provided or as the case may be applied for, is a person of any such class in respect of whom the invalid carriage or other vehicle provided or applied for was a vehicle propelled by petrol engine or by electric power supplied for use on the road and to be controlled by the occupant

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Services.

Stanley Orme

Minister for Social Security

Department of Health & Social Security

23rd May 1978

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the provision of the Mobility Allowance (Vehicle Scheme Beneficiaries) Regulations 1977 prescribing categories of persons to whom section 13 of the Social Security (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1977 applies so as to require that where persons have either been provided with, or have applied for, a vehicle, it is a power driven road vehicle controlled by the occupant.
