# The Transport Act 1978 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1978

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1978 No. 1150 (C. 32)

TRANSPORT

The Transport Act 1978 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1978

Made

3rd August 1978

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by section 24(1) of the Transport Act 1978, hereby makes the following order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Transport Act 1978 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1978.

**2.** The provisions of the Transport Act 1978 (hereinafter referred to as ‘**the Act**’) which are specified in column (1) of the Schedule to this Order and which relate to the matters respectively specified in relation to those provisions in column (2) of that Schedule shall come into force on 4th August 1978.

William Rodgers

Secretary of State for Transport

3rd August 1978

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 4th August 1978 the provisions of sections 15, 17, 18 and 21 of the Transport Act 1978 which relate to the National Freight Corporation.
