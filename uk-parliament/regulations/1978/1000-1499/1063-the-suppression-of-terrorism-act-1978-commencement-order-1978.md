# The Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Commencement) Order 1978

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1978 No. 1063 (C. 28)

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Commencement) Order 1978

Made

25th July 1978

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 9(3) of the Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Commencement) Order 1978.

**2.** The Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 shall come into force on 21st August 1978.

Merlyn Rees

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

25th July 1978
