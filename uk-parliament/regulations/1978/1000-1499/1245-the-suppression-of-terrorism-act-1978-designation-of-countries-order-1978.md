# The Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Designation of Countries) Order 1978

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1978 No. 1245

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Designation of Countries) Order 1978

Made

21st August 1978

Laid before Parliament

30th August 1978

Coming into Operation

25th October 1978

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 8 of the Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Designation of Countries) Order 1978 and shall come into operation on 25th October 1978.

**2.** The countries specified in the Schedule hereto are hereby designated as parties to the European Convention on the Suppression of Terrorism signed at Strasbourg on 27th January 1977.

Merlyn Rees

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

21st August 1978

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

- Austria
- Denmark
- Germany, Federal Republic of, and Berlin (West)
- Sweden

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates certain countries for the purposes of the Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 so that they become convention countries within the meaning of the Act. The countries designated are those which are parties to the European Convention on the Suppression of Terrorism.
