# The Rentcharges Regulations 1978

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1978 No. 16

RENTS AND RENTCHARGES

The Rentcharges Regulations 1978

Made

9th January 1978

Laid before Parliament

11th January 1978

Coming into Operation

1st February 1978

The Secretary of State for the Environment (as respects England) and the Secretary of State for Wales (as respects Wales), in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 4(5), 8(2), 9(3), 12 and 15(2) and (3) of the Rentcharges Act 1977 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These regulations may be cited as the Rentcharges Regulations 1978 and shall come into operation on 1st February 1978.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of these regulations as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

(2) In these regulations—

- “**the Act**” means the Rentcharges Act 1977;
- “**the 1927 Act**” means the Landlord and Tenant Act 1927;
- “**Form**” means one of the numbered forms set out in the Schedule to these regulations.

## 3. Applications for apportionment orders

(1) Any application under section 4(1) of the Act for an order apportioning a rentcharge, to which that subsection applies, between the land owned by the applicant and the remaining land affected by the rentcharge shall be made in Form 1.

(2) Any application under section 4(2) of the Act for an order apportioning a rentcharge, to which that subsection applies, between two or more parts of the land owned by the applicant shall be made in Form 2.

## 4. Applications for redemption certificates

(1) Any application under section 8(1) of the Act for a redemption certificate in respect of a rentcharge shall be made in Form 3.

(2) Any application under section 8(1) of the Act, as applied by section 20 of the 1927 Act, for a redemption certificate in respect of an apportioned part of a rent to which the proviso to subsection (1) of the said section 20 applies, shall be made in Form 4.

## 5. Notification of ownership

Any notification required to be given for the purposes of section 9(2) of the Act as to ownership in relation to a rentcharge, or, as the case may be, a rent to which section 20 of the 1927 Act applies, shall be given in Form 5.

## 6. Expenses

(1) Where, in exercise of any of his powers under sections 4(6) and 8(3), the Secretary of State requires the applicant to deliver to him any additional document or to furnish him with any additional information, and the additional document is delivered, or the additional information is furnished, by some person other than the applicant, the reasonable expenses incurred by that other person in delivering the document or furnishing the information shall be borne by the applicant.

(2) Where, in exercise of any of his powers under sections 4(7) and 8(6) the Secretary of State requests the delivery to him of any of the applicant's documents of title which are in the custody of a mortgagee, the reasonable expense of the mortgagee in complying with that request shall be borne by the applicant.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations prescribe for the purposes of the Rentcharges Act 1977 the forms of application for the apportionment of rentcharges and the redemption of rentcharges and other rents. They also prescribe the form of the statement as to ownership required for the purposes of such redemption of rentcharges and other rents.

Provision is further made by these Regulations for the responsibility of the applicant for certain expenses incurred by other persons on his behalf incidentally to his application.
