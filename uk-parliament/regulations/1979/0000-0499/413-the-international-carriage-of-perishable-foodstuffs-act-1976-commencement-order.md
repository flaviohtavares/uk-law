# The International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1979

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1979 No. 413 (C.12)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1979

Made

29th March 1979

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21(2) of the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1976 and of all other enabling powers hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1979.

**2.** The International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1976 shall come into operation on 1st October 1979.

William Rodgers

Secretary of State for Transport

29th March 1979

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 1st October 1979 the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1976.
