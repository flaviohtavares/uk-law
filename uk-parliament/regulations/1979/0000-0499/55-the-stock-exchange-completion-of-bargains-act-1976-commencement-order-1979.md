# The Stock Exchange (Completion of Bargains) Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1979

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1979 No. 55 (C.1)

SECURITIES

The Stock Exchange (Completion of Bargains) Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1979

Made

19th January 1979

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 7(4) of the Stock Exchange (Completion of Bargains) Act 1976 hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Stock Exchange (Completion of Bargains) Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1979.

**2.** The Stock Exchange (Completion of Bargains) Act 1976 shall come into force on 12th February 1979.

Stanley Clinton Davis

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department of Trade

19th January 1979
