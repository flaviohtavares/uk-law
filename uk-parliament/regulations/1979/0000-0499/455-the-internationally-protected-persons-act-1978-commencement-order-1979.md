# The Internationally Protected Persons Act 1978 (Commencement) Order 1979

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1979 No. 455 (C.14)

FUGITIVE CRIMINAL PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Internationally Protected Persons Act 1978 (Commencement) Order 1979

Made

11th April 1979

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 11th day of April 1979

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 5(5) of the Internationally Protected Persons Act 1978 and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** The Internationally Protected Persons Act 1978 shall come into force on 24th May 1979.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the Internationally Protected Persons Act 1978 (Commencement) Order 1979.

N.E. Leigh

Clerk of the Privy Council
