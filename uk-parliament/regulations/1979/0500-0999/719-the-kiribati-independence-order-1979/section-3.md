# The Kiribati Independence Order 1979, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Revocations

(1) The Gilbert and Ellice Islands Order in Council 1915[^f00005], the Gilbert Islands Order 1975, the Gilbert Islands (Amendment) Order 1976, the Gilbert Islands (Amendment) Order 1977 and the Gilbert Islands (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1977 are revoked.

(2) The Emergency Powers Order in Council 1939[^f00006] and any Order in Council amending that Order shall cease to have effect as part of the law of Kiribati on Independence Day.
