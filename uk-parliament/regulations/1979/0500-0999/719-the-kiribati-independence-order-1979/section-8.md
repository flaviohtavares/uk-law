# The Kiribati Independence Order 1979, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Prerogatives and privileges of the Crown

Where under any law in force in Kiribati immediately before Independence Day any prerogatives or privileges are vested in Her Majesty those prerogatives or privileges shall, as from that day, subject to the provisions of the Constitution, vest in the Republic.
