# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 1992, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Withdrawal of EC type-examination certificates

(1) Subject to regulation 69 below and paragraph (2) of this regulation, the United Kingdom notified body which issued an EC type-examination certificate shall withdraw that certificate if it appears that the apparatus to which it relates does not conform with the protection requirements.

(2) A withdrawal of an EC type-examination certificate shall be by notice in writing stating the reasons for the withdrawal.
