# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 1992, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Power of CAA and BABT to charge fees

(1) Without prejudice to the power of the DRA or the RA to charge fees pursuant to regulations made under section 56 of the Finance Act 1973 and subject to paragraph (2) below, the CAA and BABT may charge applicants for EC type-examination certificates such fees as they may determine.

(2) The power in paragraph (1) above includes power to require the payment of fees or a reasonable estimate thereof with the application.

(3) The CAA and BABT shall publish from time to time the scale of fees charged by it, or such information about the basis of calculation thereof as the Secretary of State may direct.
