# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 1992, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Misuse of the CE mark etc.

(1) Any person who affixes the CE mark for the purposes of the EMC Directive, or an inscription liable to be confused therewith, in relation to—

- (a) any relevant apparatus in contravention of regulation 33(5) above; or
- (b) any electrical apparatus other than relevant apparatus in contravention of regulation 33(6) above,

shall be guilty of an offence.

(2) Any person who issues an EC declaration of conformity in relation to—

- (a) any relevant apparatus in contravention of regulation 34(3) above; or
- (b) any electrical apparatus in contravention of regulation 34(4) above,

shall be guilty of an offence.
