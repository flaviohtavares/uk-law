# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 1992, section 83

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 83. Contravention of prohibition notice or suspension notice

Any person who contravenes a prohibition notice or a suspension notice shall be guilty of an offence.
