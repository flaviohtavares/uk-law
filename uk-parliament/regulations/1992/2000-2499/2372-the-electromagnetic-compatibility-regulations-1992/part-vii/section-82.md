# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 1992, section 82

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 82. Supplying or taking into service apparatus in contravention of regulation 28 or 29

Any person who supplies or takes into service relevant apparatus in contravention of regulation 28 or 29 above shall be guilty of an offence.
