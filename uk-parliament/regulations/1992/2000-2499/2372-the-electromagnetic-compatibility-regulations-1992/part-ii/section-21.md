# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 1992, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Active implantable medical devices

(1) These Regulations do not apply to active implantable medical devices.

(2) In this regulation and regulation 22 below, “active implantable medical device ” has the meaning given by Article 1.2(c) of Council Directive [90/385/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0385) on the approximation of laws of the Member States relating to active implantable medical devices[^f00012].
