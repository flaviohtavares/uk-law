# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 1992, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Spark ignition engines of tractors

(1) These Regulations do not apply to the electric ignition systems of a propulsion engine of, or intended for incorporation in, an agricultural or forestry tractor in so far as the electromagnetic disturbance generated thereby is liable to cause radio interference,

(2) In this regulation, “agricultural or forestry tractor ” has the meaning given by Article 1 of Council Directive [75/322/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0322) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to the suppression of radio interference produced by spark-ignition engines fitted to wheeled agricultural or forestry tractors[^f00016], as amended by Article 1 of Council Directive [82/890/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0890) amending the Directives on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to wheeled agricultural or forestry tractors[^f00017].
