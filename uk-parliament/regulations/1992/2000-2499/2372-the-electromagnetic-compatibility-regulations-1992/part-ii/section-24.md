# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 1992, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Spark-ignition engines of vehicles

(1) These Regulations do not apply to the electric ignition systems of a propulsion engine of, or intended for incorporation in, a vehicle in so far as the electromagnetic disturbance generated thereby is liable to cause radio interference.

(2) In this regulation, “vehicle ” has the meaning given by Article 1 of Council Directive [72/245/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0245) on the approximation of laws of the Member States relating to the suppression of radio interference produced by spark-ignition engines fitted to motor vehicles[^f00015].
