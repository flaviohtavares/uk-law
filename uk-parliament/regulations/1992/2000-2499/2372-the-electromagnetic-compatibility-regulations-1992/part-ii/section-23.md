# The Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 1992, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Electrical Energy Meters

(1) These Regulations do not apply to electrical energy meters as regards the immunity thereof.

(2) In this regulation, “electrical energy meter ” means any new direct connected induction meter, with single or multiple tariffs, designed to measure active energy single-phase or polyphase current at 50 H<sub>3</sub> frequency mentioned or referred to in Article 2 of Council Directive [76/891/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0891) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to electrical energy meters[^f00014].
