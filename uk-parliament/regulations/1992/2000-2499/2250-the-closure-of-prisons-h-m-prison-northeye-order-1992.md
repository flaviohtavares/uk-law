# The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Prison Northeye) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2250

PRISONS

The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Prison Northeye) Order 1992

Made

15th September 1992

Laid before Parliament

24th September 1992

Coming into force

15th October 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 37 of the Prison Act 1952[^f00001],I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Closure of Prisons (H.M. Prison Northeye) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 15th October 1992.

**2.** The prison known as Northeye Prison and situate in the county of East Sussex shall close on the coming into force of this Order.

Kenneth Clarke

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

15th September 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the closure of H.M. Prison Northeye on 15th October 1992.
