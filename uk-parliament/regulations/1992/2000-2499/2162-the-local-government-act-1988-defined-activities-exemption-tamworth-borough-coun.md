# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Tamworth Borough Council) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2162

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Tamworth Borough Council) Order 1992

Made

7th September 1992

Laid before Parliament

10th September 1992

Coming into force

1st October 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(9) and 15(5) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Tamworth Borough Council) Order 1992 and shall come into force on1st October 1992.

## 2. Amendment of 1991 Order

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemptions) (England)(No. 3) Order 1991[^f00002] is amended by the substitution, in the Table in article 3, of “1st August 1993” for “1st October 1992.”

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Department of the Environment

7th September 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (Competition), work falling within certain defined activities may be carried out by local authorities only if particular conditions are fulfilled.

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemptions) (England) (No. 3) Order 1991 exempts from the requirements of Part I the management of sports and leisure facilities by Tamworth Borough Council, so long as such work is undertaken at Tamworth Castle Swimming Pool, the Pleasure Grounds, Tamworth, before 1st October 1992. This order substitutes 1st August 1993 for that date.
