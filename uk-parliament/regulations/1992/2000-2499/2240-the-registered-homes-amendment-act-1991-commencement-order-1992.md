# The Registered Homes (Amendment) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2240 (C.77)

SOCIAL SERVICES, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Registered Homes (Amendment) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992

Made

15th September 1992

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the Registered Homes (Amendment) Act 1991[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Registered Homes (Amendment) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992.

## 2. Day appointed

1st April 1993 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the Registered Homes (Amendment) Act 1991.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tim Yeo

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

15th September 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 1993 the Registered Homes (Amendment) Act 1991 (which amends Part I of the Registered Homes Act 1984 so as to require registration in respect of small residential care homes with fewer than four residents and for connected purposes).
