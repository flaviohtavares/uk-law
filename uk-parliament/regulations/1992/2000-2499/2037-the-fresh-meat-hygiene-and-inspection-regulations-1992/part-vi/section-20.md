# The Fresh Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1992, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Records of inspections

Every local authority shall keep in respect of each licensed premises, where appropriate, a record, for the purpose of compliance with the provisions of Council Directive [91/497/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0497), of the results of—

- (a) ante-mortem health inspections;
- (b) post-mortem health inspections; and
- (c) water testing

and the local authority shall supply to the appropriate Minister copies of any such records kept by them as the appropriate Minister may from time to time require; and shall retain any such record for a period of 3 years from the date of the inspection to which it relates.
