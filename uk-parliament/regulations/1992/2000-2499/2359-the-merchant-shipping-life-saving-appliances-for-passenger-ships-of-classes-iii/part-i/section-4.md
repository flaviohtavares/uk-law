# The Merchant Shipping (Life-Saving Appliances for Passenger Ships of Classes III to VI (A)) Regulations 1992, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Application

These Regulations apply to United Kingdom ships when operating as passenger ships of Classes III, IV, V, VI and VI(A).
