# The Merchant Shipping (Life-Saving Appliances for Passenger Ships of Classes III to VI (A)) Regulations 1992, SCHEDULE 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 12 — GENERAL EMERGENCY ALARM SYSTEMS

Regulations 2(3), 5, 6, 7(2), 7(3), 7(4), 8 and 9

**1.** The general emergency alarm system shall be capable of sounding the general emergency alarm signal consisting of seven or more short blasts followed by one long blast on the ship’s whistle or siren

**2.** The system shall be capable of operation from the navigating bridge and, except for the ship’s whistle, also from other strategic points. The system shall be audible throughout all the accommodation and normal crew working spaces.
