# The Merchant Shipping (Life-Saving Appliances for Passenger Ships of Classes III to VI (A)) Regulations 1992, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Stowage of lifejackets

Lifejackets shall be so placed as to be readily accessible and their position shall be clearly indicated.
