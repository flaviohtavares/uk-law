# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Cambrian Mountains) Designation (Amendment No. 2) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2342

AGRICULTURE

The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Cambrian Mountains) Designation (Amendment No. 2) Order 1992

Made

23rd September 1992

Laid before Parliament

1st October 1992

Coming into force

22nd October 1992

Whereas, pursuant to section 18(1) of the Agriculture Act 1986[^f00001] the Secretary of State has by order designated an area in the Cambrian Mountains as an environmentally sensitive area;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(1) and (4) of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury and after consultation with the Countryside Council for Wales[^f00002] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Cambrian Mountains) Designation (Amendment No. 2) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 22nd October 1992.

## 2. Amendment of the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Cambrian Mountains) Designation Order 1986

(1) The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Cambrian Mountains) Designation Order 1986[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2) below.

(2) In paragraph 1(5) of Schedule 1 (requirements to be included in agreement) the words “in any year” shall be inserted immediately after the word “silage”.

David Hunt

Secretary of State for Wales

6th September 1992

We consent,

Gregory Knight

Irvine Patnick

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty\`s Treasury

23rd September 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Cambrian Mountains) Designation Order 1986 (“the principal Order”).

Paragraph 1(5) of Schedule 1 to the principal Order, as amended, provided that one of the requirements to be included in an agreement was that the farmer should exclude livestock from hay meadows for at least seven weeks before the first cut for hay or silage. This amending Order provides that the first cut specified in that requirement means the first cut in any year (article 2).
