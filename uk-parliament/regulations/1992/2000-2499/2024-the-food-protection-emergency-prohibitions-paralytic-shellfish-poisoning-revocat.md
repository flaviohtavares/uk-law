# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (Revocation) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2024 (S.205)

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

PUBLIC HEALTH, SCOTLAND

PUBLIC HEALTH, NORTHERN IRELAND

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (Revocation) Order 1992

Made

21st August 1992

Laid before Parliament

24th August 1992

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 1(1) and (2) and 24(1) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (Revocation) Order 1992 and shall come into force at 1700 hours on 21st August 1992.

## 2. Revocation

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) Order 1992[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

E. Davison

Assistant Secretary, Scottish Office

Pentland House,

Edinburgh

21st August 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) Order 1992 which prohibited—

- (a) fishing for or taking scallops, defined as Queen Scallops (article 1), and fishing for or taking mussels within the area designated in the Schedule to that Order (article 4),
- (b) movement of such scallops and mussels out of that area (article 5),
- (c) landing, using in the preparation or processing for supply of food of, supplying and other specified activities in relation to, such scallops and mussels from the designated area within the United Kingdom (article 6).

The effect of this Order is to remove completely the prohibition in fishing for, taking, moving, landing, using in food production and supplying Queen Scallops and mussels from Scapa Flow, Orkney Islands.
