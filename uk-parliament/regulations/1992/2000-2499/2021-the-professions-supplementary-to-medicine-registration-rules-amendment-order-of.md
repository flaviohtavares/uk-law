# The Professions Supplementary to Medicine (Registration Rules) (Amendment) Order of Council 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2021

PROFESSIONS SUPPLEMENTARY TO MEDICINE

The Professions Supplementary to Medicine (Registration Rules) (Amendment) Order of Council 1992

Made

14th August 1992

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 14th day of August 1992

By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Whereas in pursuance of section 2(3) of the Professions Supplementary to Medicine Act 1960[^f00001] the Council for Professions Supplementary to Medicine has made the Registration (Amendment) Rules 1990 as set out in the Schedule to this Order:

And whereas such rules may not come into force until confirmed by order of the Privy Council:

Now, therefore, Their Lordships having taken the said rules into consideration, are hereby pleased to confirm the same.

This Order may be cited as the Professions Supplementary to Medicine (Registration Rules) (Amendment) Order of Council 1992.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — THE REGISTRATION (AMENDMENT) RULES 1992

The Council for Professions Supplementary to Medicine, in exercise of its powers under section 2 of the Professions Supplementary to Medicine Act 1960, hereby makes the following Rules:

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Registration (Amendment) Rules 1992.

**2.** The Registration Rules 1962 (scheduled to the Professions Supplementary to Medicine (Registration Rules) Order of Council 1962[^f00002]) shall be further amended as follows:

- (i) Rule 15 shall be deleted and the following Rule shall be substituted therefor:

  > **15.** The registration fee shall be paid at the time of the application for registration. With effect from 1st October 1992, the fee shall, subject to Rule 17, be £17, except for an application for registration for a period of less than 6 months, for which the fee shall be £12.

  .
- (ii) Rule 17 shall be deleted and the following Rule shall be substituted therefor:

  > **17.** The registration fee shall cover the inclusion in a Register of particulars inserted under paragraphs (a), (b) and (d) of Rule 4 of these Rules, but shall be increased by £4 for each address entered under paragraph (c) of that Rule.

  .
- (iii) Rules 19(iii) and (iv) shall be deleted and the following shall be substituted therefor:

  > - (iii) Payment of any retention fee shall cover retention in a Register for the relevant period of particulars under paragraphs (a), (b), (d) and (e) of Rule 3 of these Rules but shall be increased by £4 per annum for each address registered under paragraph (c) of that Rule.
  > - (iv) With effect from 1st October 1992 the retention fee in respect of a year or any part of a year shall, subject to paragraph (iii) above, be £17.

  .
- (iv) Rule 20 shall be deleted and the following Rule shall be substituted therefor:

  > - 20. The fee for restoration of a name to the Register shall be £17.

  .

The common seal of the Council for Professions Supplementary to Medicine was hereunto affixed this 22nd day of June 1992 in the presence of:—

S. M. Peretz

Vice-Chairman of the Council

R. Pickis

Registrar

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Rules approved by this Order increase, by approximately 40 per cent, with effect from 1st October 1992 the registration, retention and restoration fees payable to the Boards established under the Professions Supplementary to Medicine Act 1960. The fees were last fixed in 1990.
