# The Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2079

The Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1992

Made

2nd September 1992

Laid before Parliament

9th September 1992

Coming into force

1st October 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 1, 3, 16(10), 34, 35, 50(3) and 126[^f00001] of, and paragraphs 2, 5 and 10(c) of Schedule 1 to, the Building Act 1984[^f00002], and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 1st October 1992.

## 3. Transitional Provisions

These Regulations shall not affect the application of the Building (Prescribed Fees etc.) Regulations 1985 in relation to work in respect of which—

- (a) a building notice was given or full plans were deposited under regulation 11 of the Building Regulations 1985[^f00004] or regulation 11 of the Building Regulations 1991[^f00005]; or
- (b) an initial notice or a public body’s notice was given under section 47(1) (giving and acceptance of initial notice) or 54(1) (giving, acceptance and effect of public body’s notice) of the Building Act 1984,

before 1st October 1992, notwithstanding that on or after that date a notice is or hasbeen given, or plans are or have been deposited, in respect of substantially the same work.

## 4. Revocations

Regulation 2(5)(c) of the Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1986[^f00006], and regulations 3 and 4 of the Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1990[^f00007] are hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment

Sir George Young

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment

2nd September 1992

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Building (Prescribed Fees etc.) Regulations 1985 so as to increase the fees payable to a local authority by—

- (a) approximately 9.4% in respect of the erection of small domestic buildings and connected work (Schedule 1 to the 1985 Regulations), the erection of certain garages, carports (Schedule 2 to those Regulations), and for other types of work not mentioned in either Schedule 1 or 2 (Schedule 3 to those Regulations);
- (b) approximately 50% in respect of the making of small extensions and alterations to domestic buildings (Schedule 2 to those Regulations).
