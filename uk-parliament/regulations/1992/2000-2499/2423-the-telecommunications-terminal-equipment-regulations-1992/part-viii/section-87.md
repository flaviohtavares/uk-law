# The Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 1992, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Duty of enforcement authority to inform the Secretary of State of action taken

The enforcement authority shall, where action has been taken by it to prohibit or restrict the supply or putting into service (whether under these Regulations or otherwise) of any applicable terminal equipment, forthwith inform the Secretary of State of the action taken, and the reasons for it, with a view to this information being passed by him to the Commission.
