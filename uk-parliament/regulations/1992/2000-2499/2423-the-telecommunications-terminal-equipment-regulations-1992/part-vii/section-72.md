# The Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 1992, section 72

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 72. False or misleading information

Any person who, in giving any information which he is required to give under regulation 69(c) or (d) above—

- (a) makes any statement which he knows is false or misleading in a material particular; or
- (b) recklessly makes any statement which is false or misleading in a material particular,

shall be guilty of an offence.
