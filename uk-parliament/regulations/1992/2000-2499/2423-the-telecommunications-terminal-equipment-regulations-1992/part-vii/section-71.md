# The Telecommunications Terminal Equipment Regulations 1992, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. Contravention of prohibition notice or suspension notice

Any person who contravenes a prohibition notice or a suspension notice shall be guilty of an offence.
