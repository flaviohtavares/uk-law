# The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2290

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1992

Made

22nd September 1992

Coming into force

15th October 1992

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1, 7(1)(a), (b), (c), and (2), 23(f) and (g) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 15th October 1992.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order the “principal Order” means the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978[^f00002].

## 3. Amendment

(1) At the end of Schedule 1 to the principal Order (which lists disinfectants approved by the Minister) there shall be added the entries listed in Schedule 1 to this Order.

(2) The entries listed in Schedule 2 to this Order shall be deleted from Schedule 1 to the principal Order.

(3) For the provisions of Schedule 2 to the principal Order (which lists disinfectants subject to transitional provisions) there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in Schedule 3 to this Order.

(4) In article 6(2) of the principal Order (which specifies the date until which disinfectants subject to transitional provisions remain approved) for the words “30th June 1992” there shall be substituted the words “31st December 1992”.

This Order further amends the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978 by adding to the list of approved disinfectants in that Order those newly approved disinfectants which are mentioned in Schedule 1 to this Order (article 3(1)) and by deleting from that list the disinfectants mentioned in Schedule 2 (article 3(2)). The disinfectants deleted may, however, continue to be used as approved disinfectants until 31st December 1992 (article 3(3) and (4)).
