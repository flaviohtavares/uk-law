# The Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2045 (S.206)

REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS, DEATHS, MARRIAGES, ETC.

The Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1992

Made

19th August 1992

Coming into force

1st October 1992

The Registrar General for Scotland, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 21(2)(a) and 54(1)(b) of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the approval of the Secretary of State, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 1st October 1992.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations, “the principal Regulations” means the Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Regulations 1965[^f00002].

## 3. Amendment of the principal Regulations

For the form set out in Schedule 6 to the principal Regulations there shall be substituted the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE — CERTIFICATE OF STILL-BIRTH

Regulation 3

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2045/images/uksi_19922045_en_001)

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Regulations 1965 by substituting a revised form for the certificate of still-birth prescribed by those Regulations. The change made to the form is consequential on the change in the definition of still-birth in the Still-Birth (Definition) Act [1992 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/29).
