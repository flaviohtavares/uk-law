# The Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2978

PENSIONS

The Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order 1992

Made

30th November 1992

Laid before Parliament

7th December 1992

Coming into force

1st January 1993

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 166(1) of the Social Security Act 1975[^f00001]and section 52A(1) of the Social SecurityPensions Act 1975[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order 1992.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 1st January 1993.

## 2. The revaluation percentage for each revaluation period

For the purposes of section 52A of the Social Security Pensions Act 1975, the revaluation percentage for each revaluation period specified in column 1 below is the appropriate percentage specified in column 2.

30th November 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made, as required by section 52A of the Social Security Pensions Act 1975, in the revaluation year beginning on 1st January 1992. The revaluation percentages specified are relevant to the revaluation of benefits under occupational pension schemes, as required by section 52B of, and Schedule 1A to, that Act.
