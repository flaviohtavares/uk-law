# The Water Byelaws (Milngavie Waterworks, Loch Katrine, Loch Arklet, Glen Finglas) Extension Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2934 (S.241)

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Water Byelaws (Milngavie Waterworks, Loch Katrine, Loch Arklet, Glen Finglas) Extension Order 1992

Made

23rd November 1992

Coming into force

25th November 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Water Byelaws (Milngavie Waterworks, Loch Katrine, Loch Arklet, Glen Finglas) Extension Order 1992 and shall come into force on 25th November 1992.

**2.** The period during which the Strathclyde Regional Council, Prevention of Water Pollution (Milngavie Waterworks) Byelaws 1982 and the Strathclyde Regional Council, Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch Katrine, Loch Arklet, Glen Finglas) Byelaws 1985 are to remain in force[^f00002] is hereby extended to 25th May 1993.

T.D. Macdonald

Deputy Chief Engineer, Scottish Office

Scottish Office Environment Department,

27 Perth Street,

Edinburgh

23rd November 1992
