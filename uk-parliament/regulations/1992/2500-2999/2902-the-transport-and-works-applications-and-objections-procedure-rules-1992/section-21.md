# The Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) Rules 1992, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Power to set later time limits

The Secretary of State may in a particular case allow further time for the taking of any step (including a step which he is required or enabled to take himself) which may or must be taken pursuant to these Rules and all reference to a date by which or a period within which any step is required to be taken shall be construed accordingly.
