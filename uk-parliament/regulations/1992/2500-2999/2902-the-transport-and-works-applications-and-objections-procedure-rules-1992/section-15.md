# The Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) Rules 1992, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Orders made by the Secretary of State under section 7

(1) The foregoing rules shall apply to a proposal by the Secretary of State to make an order under the provisions of section 7 with the following modifications.

(2) The following rules shall not apply—

- rule 4;
- sub-paragraphs (c), (d), (e) and ( f ) of rule 5(1);
- sub-paragraphs (b) and (c) of rule 5(2);
- rule 5(9);
- paragraphs (1), (2), (3) and (9) of rule 6;
- paragraphs (1), (2), (3) and (5) of rule 12; and
- rule 13.

(3) In rule 6(4), for the words from “42 days” to “has been provided”, there shall be substituted the words “the date of the application”.

(4) In rule 11(3), for the words from “after the date” to “intends to put to” there shall be substituted the words “less than 14 days before the date of”.

(5) For paragraphs (1) to (3) of rule 12 there shall be substituted—

> (1) The Secretary of State may, where he is satisfied that it is impossible, impracticable or unnecessary for such a provision to be complied with, and subject to the provisions of paragraph (4) below, direct that any provision contained in rules 3, 4, 5 (except paragraph (1)(g) thereof), 7 to 10 (inclusive) and 11(4) shall not apply (or shall apply in part only) to his application.

(6) A notice of a proposal to make an order published by the Secretary of State pursuant to section 7(3)(b) shall contain the address to which all objections, notices and other correspondence relating to the proposal are to be sent and a reference number to be quoted in all communications relating to such proposal.
