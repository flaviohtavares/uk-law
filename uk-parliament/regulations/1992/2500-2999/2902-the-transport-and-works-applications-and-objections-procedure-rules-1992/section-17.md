# The Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) Rules 1992, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Notice of objections and other representations

(1) The Secretary of State shall, as soon as reasonably practicable after an objection has been made, or representations made pursuant to rule 16(3) have been received, serve a copy thereof upon the applicant.

(2) As soon as reasonably practicable after the expiry of the period allowed for the making of objections the Secretary of State shall serve upon the applicant (where he is not the Secretary of State) a list of all objections he has received stating, in each case—

- (a) the name and address of the objector;
- (b) the date or receipt of the objection; and
- (c) the reference number given to the objection.
