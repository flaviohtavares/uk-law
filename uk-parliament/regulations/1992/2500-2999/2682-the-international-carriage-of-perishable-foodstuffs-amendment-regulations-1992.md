# The International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2682

ROAD TRAFFIC

The International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made

28th October 1992

Laid before Parliament

5th November 1992

Coming into force

1st December 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 3(1), 4(1) and 19(1)[^f00001] of the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1976[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations shall come into force on 1st December 1992 and may be cited as the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1992.

**2.** For the Schedule to the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Regulations 1985[^f00003] there shall be substituted the Schedule to these Regulations.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Kenneth Carlisle

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

28th October 1992

## SCHEDULE

(See Regulation 24)

### PART I — (Testing)

**1.** Fee for the testing of a unit of transport equipment £150

**2.** Fee for the use of the facilities of a designated station for each day, or part of a day, in which such facilities are used for the purpose of the testing of a unit of transport equipment £365

**3.** Fee for the certification of a unit of transport equipment which has been tested £50

### PART II — (Type Approval)

**1.** Fee for the testing of a unit of transport equipment as a type vehicle and the inspection of arrangements for ensuring conformity of production £850

**2.** Fee for the use of the facilities of a designated station for each day, or part of a day, in which such facilities are used for the purpose of the testing of a unit of transport equipment as a type vehicle £365

**3.** Fee for the certification of a type vehicle £245

**4.** Fee for the certification of a vehicle which a certifying officer is satisfied conforms with a type vehicle £50

### PART III — (Examination)

**1.** Fee for the examination at the Refrigerated Vehicle Test Centre of a unit of transport equipment £55

**2.** Fee for the examination at a place other than the Refrigerated Vehicle Test Centre of a unit of transport equipment—

- (a) for up to four units presented at the same time for examination, for each unit £160
- (b) for each additional unit £40

**3.** Fee for the certification of a unit of transport equipment which has been examined £50

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations change most of the fees charged under the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Regulations 1985, as amended by the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1987, for testing, type approval and examination of goods vehicles, railway wagons and containers carrying perishable foodstuffs on international journeys.
