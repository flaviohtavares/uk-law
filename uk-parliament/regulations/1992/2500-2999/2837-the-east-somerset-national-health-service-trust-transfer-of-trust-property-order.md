# The East Somerset National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2837

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The East Somerset National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1992

Made

12th November 1992

Laid before Parliament

19th November 1992

Coming into force

4th January 1993

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and facilities formerly managed by Somerset Health Authority resulting from the establishment of East Somerset National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the East Somerset National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 4th January 1993.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means Somerset Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the East Somerset National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 28th September 1992, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 4th January 1993 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

12th November 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the East Somerset National Health Service Trust on 4th January 1993 trust property formerly held by Somerset Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, Richmond House, 79 Whitehall, London SW1A 2 NS.
