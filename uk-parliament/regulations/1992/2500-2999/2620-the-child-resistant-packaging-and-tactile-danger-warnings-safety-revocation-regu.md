# The Child Resistant Packaging and Tactile Danger Warnings (Safety) (Revocation) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2620

CONSUMER PROTECTION

The Child Resistant Packaging and Tactile Danger Warnings (Safety) (Revocation) Regulations 1992

Made

19th October 1992

Laid before Parliament

22nd October 1992

Coming into force

23rd October 1992

Whereas the Secretary of State is a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2 of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to regulation and control of classification, packaging and labelling of dangerous substances and preparations:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 2 of the said Act of 1972 hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Child Resistant Packaging and Tactile Danger Warnings (Safety) (Revocation) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on the day after they are laid.

**2.** The Child Resistant Packaging and Tactile Danger Warnings (Safety) Regulations 1992[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Denton of Wakefield

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

19th October 1992

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Child Resistant Packaging and Tactile Danger Warnings (Safety) Regulations 1992 before those Regulations come into force.
