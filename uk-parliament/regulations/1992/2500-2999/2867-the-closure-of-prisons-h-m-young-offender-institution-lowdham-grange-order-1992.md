# The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Young Offender Institution Lowdham Grange) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2867

PRISONS

The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Young Offender Institution Lowdham Grange) Order 1992

Made

16th November 1992

Laid before Parliament

24th November 1992

Coming into force

1st January 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 37 of the Prison Act 1952[^f00001] I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Closure of Prisons (H.M. Young Offender Institution Lowdham Grange) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st January 1993.

**2.** The prison known as H.M. Young Offender Institution Lowdham Grange and situate in the county of Nottinghamshire shall close on the coming into force of this Order.

Kenneth Clarke

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

16th November 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the closure of H.M. Young Offender Institution Lowdham Grange on 1st January 1993.
