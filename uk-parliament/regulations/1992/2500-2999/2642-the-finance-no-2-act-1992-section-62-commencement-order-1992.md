# The Finance (No. 2) Act 1992, section 62, (Commencement) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2642 (C.82)

INCOME TAX

The Finance (No. 2) Act 1992, section 62, (Commencement) Order 1992

Made

26th October 1992

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 62(4) of the Finance (No. 2) Act 1992[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Finance (No. 2) Act 1992, section 62, (Commencement) Order 1992.

**2.** Section 62 of the Finance (No. 2) Act 1992 shall come into force on 6th April 1993.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Alistair Burt

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Social Security

26th October 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 6th April 1993 section 62 of the Finance (No. 2) Act 1992 which, by amendment of section 347B of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act [1988 (c. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/1) (inserted by section 36(1) of the Finance Act [1988 (c. 39)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/39)) and of sections 36 and 38 of the Finance Act 1988, provides that entitlement to income tax relief is given in respect of certain payments under the Child Support Act [1991 (c. 48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/48) or the Child Support (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 (S.I. [1991/2628 (N.I. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1991/2628)).
