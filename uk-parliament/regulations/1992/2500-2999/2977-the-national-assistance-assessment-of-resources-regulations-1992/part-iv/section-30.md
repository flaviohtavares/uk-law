# The National Assistance (Assessment of Resources) Regulations 1992, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Treatment of liable relative payments

Except where regulation 34(1) applies, a payment made to a resident by a liable relative shall be—

- (a) treated as income; and
- (b) taken into account in accordance with the following provisions of this Part.
