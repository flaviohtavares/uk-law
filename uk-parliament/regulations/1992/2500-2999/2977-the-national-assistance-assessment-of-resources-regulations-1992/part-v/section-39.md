# The National Assistance (Assessment of Resources) Regulations 1992, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Treatment of student loans

Student loans shall be treated in accordance with regulation 66A of the Income Support Regulations[^f00037] (treatment of student loans).

## Annotations

[^f00037]: Footnote: Regulation 66A(2) of the Income Support Regulations was amended by regulation 12 of S.I. [1991/1559](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1559).
