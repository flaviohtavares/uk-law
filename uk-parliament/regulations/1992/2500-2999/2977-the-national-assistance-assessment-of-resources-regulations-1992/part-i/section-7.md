# The National Assistance (Assessment of Resources) Regulations 1992, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Students

The provisions of Parts II and III (income and capital) shall have effect in relation to students subject to the modifications set out in Part V (students).
