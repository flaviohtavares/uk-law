# The National Assistance (Assessment of Resources) Regulations 1992, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Rounding of fractions

Where any calculation under these Regulations of a resident’s ability to pay results in a fraction of a penny, that fraction shall, if it would be to the resident’s advantage, be treated as a penny, otherwise it shall be disregarded.
