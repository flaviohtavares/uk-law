# The National Assistance (Assessment of Resources) Regulations 1992, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Assessing a resident’s ability to pay

For the purposes of section 22(3) of the Act[^f00021] the regulations to which a local authority shall give effect in assessing a resident’s ability to pay are those set out in these Regulations.

## Annotations

[^f00021]: Footnote: Amended by section 31(1) of, and paragraph 3 of Schedule 7 to, the Supplementary Benefits Act 1976, and section 86 of, and paragraph 32 of Schedule 10 to, the Social Security Act 1986. *See also* section 87(3) of the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968.
