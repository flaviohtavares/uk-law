# The National Assistance (Assessment of Resources) Regulations 1992, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Earnings of self-employed earners

A resident’s gross earnings derived from employment as a self-employed earner shall be assessed in accordance with the definition of “earnings” in regulation 37 of the Income Support Regulations[^f00022] (earnings of self-employed earners).
