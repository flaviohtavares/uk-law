# The National Assistance (Assessment of Resources) Regulations 1992, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Calculation of tariff income from capital

A resident’s tariff income from capital shall be calculated in accordance with the method prescribed in regulation 53 of the Income Support Regulations[^f00030] (calculation of tariff income from capital), except that for the purposes of this regulation the references in regulation 53(3) of the Income Support Regulations to regulations 48 and 60 of the Income Support Regulations (income treated as capital and liable relative payments treated as capital) shall be construed as references to regulations 22 and 34 of these Regulations (income treated as capital and liable relative payments treated as capital) respectively.
