# The National Health Service (District Health Authorities) (No. 4) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 2752

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The National Health Service (District Health Authorities) (No. 4) Order 1992

Made

3rd November 1992

Laid before Parliament

10th November 1992

Coming into force

1st December 1992

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 8(1)(b) and (1A)(c) and 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:–

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) (No. 4) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st December 1992.

## 2. Abolition of Authorities

The following District Health Authorities are hereby abolished:–

- (a) Airedale Health Authority;
- (b) Bradford Health Authority;
- (c) Harrogate Health Authority;
- (d) Northallerton Health Authority;
- (e) Scarborough Health Authority;
- (f) York Health Authority.

## 3. Establishment and names of new Authorities

(1) Bradford Health Authority is established a District Health Authority for Bradford District[^f00002].

(2) North Yorkshire Health Authority is established a District Health Authority for North Yorkshire District[^f00002].

## 4. Amendment of the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1990

In consequence of articles 2 and 3 of this Order the entries in columns (1) and (2) of Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1990[^f00003] shall be amended–

- (a) by the omission of the entries relating to Airedale District;
- (b) by the omission of the entries relating to Harrogate District, Northallerton District, Scarborough District and York District and the insertion after the entries relating to Leeds District of the entries–

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | North Yorkshire | North Yorkshire Health Authority |

  .

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

3rd November 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order abolishes on 1st December 1992 the District Health Authorities named in article 2 and establishes the Bradford Health Authority and the North Yorkshire Health Authority. The National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1990 is amended to reflect these changes.

The changes made to District Health Authorities in this Order are reflected in changes to the districts of the abolished Health Authorities made by the National Health Service (Determination of Districts) (No. 4) Order 1992.
