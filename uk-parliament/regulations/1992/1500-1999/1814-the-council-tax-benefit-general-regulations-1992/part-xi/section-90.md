# The Council Tax Benefit (General) Regulations 1992, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2043).** Text as at 1993-10-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1992/1814

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 90. Sums to be deducted in calculating recoverable excess benefit[^key-0b6ccd45bacd0f9469ae381dfe34efbd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In calculating the amount of recoverable excess benefit, the appropriate authority—

- (a) if it determines that a lesser amount was properly allowable in respect of the whole or part of the excess benefit period, shall deduct that amount; and
- (b) may deduct so much of any payment of council tax in respect of the excess benefit period which exceeds the amount, if any, which the claimant was liable to pay for that period under the original erroneous determination.
