# The Council Tax Benefit (General) Regulations 1992, SCHEDULE 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2043).** Text as at 1993-10-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1992/1814

## SCHEDULE 7 — CONSTITUTION OF REVIEW BOARDS

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 70(3)

**1.**[^c-fe09ecfb3de505383d4b59428dff46b3] A Review Board appointed by an authority listed in column (1) of the Table below shall consist of not less than three of the persons specified in relation to that authority in column (2) of that Table.

**Table**

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th><em>(1)</em></th><th><em>(2)</em></th></tr>
<tr><th><em>Authority</em></th><th><em>Composition of Board</em></th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>1. A billing authority other than the Common Council of the City of London.</td><td>1. Councillors of that Authority.</td></tr>
<tr><td>2. The Common Council of the City of London.</td><td>2. Persons who are mayor, aldermen or common councilmen.</td></tr>
<tr><td>3. In Scotland, a levying or district council.</td><td>3. Councillors of that authority or council.</td></tr>
<tr><td>4. In Scotland, a Development Corporation.</td><td>4. Members of a Development Corporation established for the purposes of a new town by Order made, or having effect as made under section 2(1) of the New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968.</td></tr>
<tr><td>5. Scottish Homes.</td><td>5. Members of the Scottish Homes.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

[^f00129]

**2.**[^c-74b0c0cce185d0a6592a019fa3166088] The members of a Review Board shall appoint one of their number to be the Chairman.
