# The Council Tax Benefit (General) Regulations 1992, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1903).** Text as at 1994-12-02. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1992/1814
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 46. Other amounts to be disregarded[^c-ff574babcdebdf93a1df5f4054a11aec]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of ascertaining income other than grant income, covenant income and loans treated as income in accordance with regulation 47[^c-d5d4fcc6bb4f7e727c8924a3378d5a7b], any amounts intended for any expenditure specified in regulation 42(2) (calculation of grant income), necessary as a result of his attendance on the course shall be disregarded but only if, and to the extent that, the necessary expenditure exceeds or is likely to exceed the amount of the sums disregarded under regulation 42(2) or (3)[^c-c7eb3090596d673b5ce97c2cf5da3f58], 43(3) or 44(1)(a) or (c) (calculation of grant and covenant income) on like expenditure.
