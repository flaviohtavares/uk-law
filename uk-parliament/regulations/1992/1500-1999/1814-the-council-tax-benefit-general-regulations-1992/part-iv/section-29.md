## 29. Calculation of capital[^c-641b168f65f060e8b107188cae40ad67]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of Part VII of the Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 as it applies to council tax benefit, the capital of a claimant to be taken into account shall, subject to paragraph (2), be the whole of his capital calculated in accordance with this Part and any income treated as capital under regulation 31 (income treated as capital).

(2) There shall be disregarded from the calculation of a claimant’s capital under paragraph (1), any capital, where applicable, specified in Schedule 5.
