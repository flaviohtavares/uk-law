## 28. Capital limit[^key-7d034a59f9ca7e6307448f6d6e2f4585]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of section 134(1) of the Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 as it applies to council tax benefit (no entitlement to benefit if capital exceeds prescribed amount), the prescribed amount is £16,000.
