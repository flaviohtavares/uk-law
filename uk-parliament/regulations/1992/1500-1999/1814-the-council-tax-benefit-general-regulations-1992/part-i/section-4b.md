# The Council Tax Benefit (General) Regulations 1992, section 4B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1847).** Text as at 1995-07-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1992/1814

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4B. Circumstances in which a person is or is not to be treated as occupying a dwelling as his home[^c-5f61dedeed241677cdd90cbd38c09cfe]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a person is detained in custody pending sentence upon conviction or under a sentence imposed by a court, other than a person who is detained under the Mental Health Act 1983 or the Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1984, he shall not, for any day that he is on temporary release from detention in accordance with Rules made under the provisions of the Prison Act 1952 or the Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989, be treated for the purposes of this regulation as if his detention has ceased.
