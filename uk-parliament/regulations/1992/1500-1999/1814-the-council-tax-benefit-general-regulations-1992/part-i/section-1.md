# The Council Tax Benefit (General) Regulations 1992, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2043).** Text as at 1993-10-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1992/1814

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-f19ff1b73cd74699e9d3e4bc7f963572]

*Extent: E+W+S*

These Regulations may be cited as the Council Tax Benefit (General) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force—

- (a) for the purposes of regulations 1, 2, 61 to 65, 92 and 93 on 17th August 1992;
- (b) for all other purposes on 1st April 1993.
