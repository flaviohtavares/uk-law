# The Seeds (National Lists of Varieties) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1615

SEEDS

The Seeds (National Lists of Varieties) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made

2nd July 1992

Laid before Parliament

15th July 1992

Coming into force

5th August 1992

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland, the Secretary of State for Wales and the Secretary of State for Northern Ireland, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16(1), (1A) and (8) and 38(1) of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001], as extended to Northern Ireland[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf[^f00003], after consultation in accordance with the said section 16(1) with representatives of such interests as appear to them to be concerned, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Seeds (National Lists of Varieties) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 5th August 1992.

## 2. Amendment

In Part I of Schedule 1 to the Seeds (National Lists of Varieties) Regulations 1982[^f00004] there shall be inserted after the words “*Festuca rubra L.*” the words

> - *Festuca pratensis Huds. x*
> - *Lolium multiflorum Lam.*

and after the words “Red fescue, Chewings fescue” the word “Festulolium”.

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 29th June 1992.

John Selwyn Gummer

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Hector Monro

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

29th June 1992

David Hunt

Secretary of State for Wales

30th June 1992

Patrick Mayhew

Secretary of State for Northern Ireland

2nd July 1992

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations which further amend the Seeds (National Lists of Varieties) Regulations 1982 implement Council Directive [70/457/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0457) (OJ L225, 12.10.70, p.1) as extended by Commission Directive [92/19/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0019) (OJ No. L104, 22.4.92, p.61) by adding Festulolium (a cross between Meadow fescue and Italian Ryegrass) to the Regulations.
