# The Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1750 (C.63)

ARMS CONTROL

The Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992

Made

13th July 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 6(2) of the Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992.

**2.** Sections 1 to 5 of the Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 shall come into force on 17th July 1992.

Douglas Hurd

Her Majesty’s Principal Secretary of State for Foreign and Commonwealth Affairs

13th July 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 17th July 1992 Sections 1 to 5 of the Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991.
