# The Child Support (Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases) Regulations 1992, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2022-07-01.

## SCHEDULE 4 — CASES WHERE CHILD SUPPORT MAINTENANCE IS NOT TO BE PAYABLE

Regulation 26(1)(b)(i)

The payments and awards specified for the purposes of regulation 26(1)(b)(i) are—

- (a) the following payments under the Contributions and Benefits Act—
  - (i)[^c-6168a0c75c47612e025bd333b5217720] incapacity benefit under section 30A;
  - (ii) long-term incapacity benefit for widows under section 40;
  - (iii) long-term incapacity benefit for widowers under section 41;
  - (iv) maternity allowance under section 35 or 35B[^c-122577a48ba3d7351ee5945cbfe88380];
  - (v)[^c-86d9851bbd251cc8595c80f4a744d8a6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (vi) attendance allowance under section 64;
  - (vii) severe disablement allowance under section 68;
  - (viii) carer’s allowance[^c-1ce01bb0bafe213ec32fb8dd71ebb376] under section 70;
  - (ix) disability living allowance under section 71;
  - (x) disablement benefit under section 103;
  - (xi)[^c-fe37e2ef2adb065702736a33b0b3952a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (xii) statutory sick pay within the meaning of section 151;
  - (xiii) statutory maternity pay within the meaning of section 164;
- (b) awards in respect of disablement made under (or under provisions analogous to)—
  - (i) the War Pensions (Coastguards) Scheme 1944 [^c1869723];
  - (ii) the War Pensions (Naval Auxiliary Personnel) Scheme 1964 [^c1869724];
  - (iii) the Pensions (Polish Forces) Scheme 1964 [^c1869725];
  - (iv) the War Pensions (Mercantile Marine) Scheme 1964 [^c1869726];
  - (v) the Royal Warrant of 21st December 1964 (service in the Home Guard before 1945) [^c1869727];
  - (vi) the Order by Her Majesty of 22nd December 1964 concerning pensions and other grants in respect of disablement or death due to service in the Home Guard after 27th April 1952 [^c1869728];
  - (vii) the Order by Her Majesty (Ulster Defence Regiment) of 4th January 1971 [^c1869729];
  - (viii) the Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme 1983 [^c1869730];
  - (ix) the Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 1983 [^c1869731]; [^c-31cff7cd7d59a0f953b636a0821b2d56]...
  - (x)[^c-92924b0a029f05f4dc8d7e557b876589] the Armed Forces (Pensions and Compensation) Act 2004; [^c-e8192a80fb93a92e5b7f6d9e6af908a4]...
- (c) the Independent Living (1993) Fund, the Independent Living (Extension) Fund or the Independent Living Fund (2006)[^c-12c1b6db857f75e9090923ec389cd5f1];[^c-fcb31b9458973deada9f32da3bb8ea44] [^c-66014f8b8257ab04dfe43a450f71987d]...
- (d) personal independence payment in accordance with Part 4 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012; and[^c-d6608e775f378bb63f05059c86bbf8c4]
- (e) armed forces independence payment under the Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011.
