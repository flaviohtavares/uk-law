# The Child Support (Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases) Regulations 1992, section 5

> Text as at 2022-07-01.

## 5. The general rule

For the purposes of paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 1 to the Act—

- (a) the value of C, otherwise than in a case where the other parent is the person with care, is nil; and
- (b) the value of P is 0.5.
