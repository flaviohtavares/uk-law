# The Child Support (Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases) Regulations 1992, section 13

> Text as at 2022-07-01.

## 13. The minimum amount

(1) Subject to regulation 26, for the purposes of paragraph 7(1) of Schedule 1to the Act the minimum amount shall be 2 multiplied by[^c-aca4727c63e53155c9ea34a742a19053] 5 per centum of the amount specified in paragraph 1(1)(e) of the relevant Schedule (income support personal allowance for single claimant aged not less than 25).

(2) Where the 5 per centum amount[^c-d6fcf14c94c565e1a5428049f96c936e] calculated under paragraph (1) results in a sum other than a multiple of 5 pence, it shall be treated as the sum which is the next higher multiple of 5 pence.
