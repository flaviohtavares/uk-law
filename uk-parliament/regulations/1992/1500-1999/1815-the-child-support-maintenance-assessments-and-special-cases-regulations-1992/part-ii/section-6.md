# The Child Support (Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases) Regulations 1992, section 6

> Text as at 2022-07-01.

## 6. The additional element

(1)[^key-101c7b0abe4fefc9a5c22de5c4e65021] For the purposes of the formula in paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 1 to the Act, the value of R is—

- (a) where the maintenance assessment in question relates to one qualifying child, 0.15;
- (b) where the maintenance assessment in question relates to two qualifying children, 0.20; and
- (c) where the maintenance assessment ,in question relates to three or more qualifying children, 0.25.

(2) For the purposes of the alternative formula in paragraph 4(3) of Schedule 1 to the Act—

- (a) the value of Z is 1.5[^key-c4d03e8a1141801b4d585fc355c2ad8f];
- (b) the amount for the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of Q is the same as the amount specified in regulation[^key-e3e6a0b4432cf98b7ac196d5f853c887] 3(1)(c)[^key-be88278e8c02710aa69c0b6b72a88747] (income support family premium) in respect of each qualifying child.
