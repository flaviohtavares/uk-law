# The Child Support (Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases) Regulations 1992, section 4

> Text as at 2022-07-01.

## 4. Basic rate of child benefit

For the purposes of paragraph 1(4) of Schedule 1 to the Act “basic rate" means the rate of child benefit which is specified in regulation 2(1)(a)(i) or 2(1)(b) of the Child Benefit Rates Regulations (weekly rate for only, elder or eldest child and for other children)[^c-e353228485ac126becb74586d58e5af9] applicable to the child in question at the effective date.
