# The Plant Breeders' Rights (Amendment) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1939

PLANT BREEDERS' RIGHTS

The Plant Breeders' Rights (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made

3rd August 1992

Laid before Parliament

7th August 1992

Coming into force

28th August 1992

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland, the Secretary of State for Wales and the Secretary of State for Northern Ireland, acting jointly, as respects the United Kingdom, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 9(1) and (5), 36 and 38(1) of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001] as extended to Northern Ireland[^f00002] and the said Minister and the Secretary of State, acting jointly, as respects the Isle of Man, in exercise of the said powers as extended to the Isle of Man[^f00003], and in exercise of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Breeders' Rights (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 28th August 1992.

## 2. Amendment of principal Regulations

Schedule 3 to the Plant Breeders' Rights Regulations 1978[^f00004] (reproductive and other plant material to be delivered to the Controller) shall be amended as follows—

In Part XII (decoratives), after item (26) insert the following item—

> (27) *Galtonia candicans*
>
> - 6 visually healthy bulbs.

.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Plant Breeders' Rights Regulations 1978.

A Scheme has been made to enable plant breeders' rights to be granted in respect of *Galtonia candicans* . Part XII of Schedule 3 to the 1978 Regulations (which Schedule was inserted into the 1978 Regulations by the Plant Breeders' Rights (Amendment) Regulations 1982 and amended by S.I. [1990/2633](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/2633)) has been amended so as to specify the reproductive and other material which must be delivered to the Controller when an application is made for a grant of plant breeders' rights in respect of varieties of *Galtonia candicans* .
