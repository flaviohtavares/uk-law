# The Army, Air Force and Naval Discipline Acts (Continuation) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1712

DEFENCE

The Army, Air Force and Naval Discipline Acts (Continuation) Order 1992

Made

15th July 1992

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of July 1992

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas a draft of this Order has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the Armed Forces Act 1991[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered as follows:—

**1.** The Army Act 1955[^f00002], the Air Force Act 1955[^f00003] and the Naval Discipline Act 1957[^f00004] shall continue in force for a period of twelve months beyond 31st August 1992, that day being the day on which they would otherwise expire.

**2.** This Order may be cited as the Army, Air Force and Naval Discipline Acts (Continuation) Order 1992.

G.I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order enables the provisions of the Army Act 1955, the Air Force Act 1955 and the Naval Discipline Act 1957 to continue in force for a period of twelve months beyond 31st August 1992, which is the latest date provided for in section 1(1) of the Armed Forces Act 1991.
