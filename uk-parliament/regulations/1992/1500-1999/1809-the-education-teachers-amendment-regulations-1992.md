# The Education (Teachers) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1809

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Teachers) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made

21st July 1992

Laid before Parliament

22nd July 1992

Coming into force

1st September 1992

The Secretary of State for Education[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 218(1)(c) and 232(5) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Teachers) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 1st September 1992.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations, “the principal Regulations ” means the Education (Teachers) Regulations 1989[^f00003].

## 3. Amendment

Subject to regulation 4, the principal Regulations shall be amended by the omission of regulation 14, paragraphs 8 and 9(2) of Schedule 1 and Schedule 6.

## 4. Transitional provision

In the case of a person who, on 1st September 1992, has commenced but not completed a period of probation under regulation 14 of and Schedule 6 to the principal Regulations, regulation 14 and Schedule 6 shall continue to have effect until all their provisions have been complied with.

John Patten

The Secretary of State for Education

21st July 1992

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations abolish the requirement for probation under the Education (Teachers) Regulations 1989 with a transitional provision for teachers serving probation on 1st September 1992 when these Regulations come into force. Paragraph 9(1) of Schedule 1 continues to apply to teachers who failed probation under the Schools Regulations 1959 or the Education (Teachers) Regulations 1982.
