# The Social Security Act 1990 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1532 (C.52)

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security Act 1990 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1992

Made

26th June 1992

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 23(2) of the Social Security Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Social Security Act 1990 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1992.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of paragraph 2 of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Act 1990 (occupational and personal pension schemes: employer to make good deficiencies on winding up), and section 14 of that Act in so far as it relates to it, shall be 29th June 1992.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Nicholas Scott

Minister of State,

Department of Social Security

26th June 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into force on 29th June 1992 of paragraph 2 of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Act 1990, which inserts section 58B in the Social Security Pensions Act 1975 to make provision relating to deficiencies in the assets of a scheme on winding up.

(This note is not part of the Order)
