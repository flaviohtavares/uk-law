# The Timeshare Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1941 (C. 68)

CONSUMER PROTECTION

The Timeshare Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992

Made

6th August 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by subsections (6) and (8) of section 12 and section 13(2) of the Timeshare Act 1992[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as The Timeshare Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992.

**2.** The Timeshare Act 1992 shall come into force on 12th October 1992.

Denton of Wakefield

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

6th August 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 12th October 1992 the Timeshare Act 1992.
