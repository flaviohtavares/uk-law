# The Motor Vehicles (Off Road Events) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct errors in [S.I. 1992/1370](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1370) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1992 No. 1523

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Off Road Events) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made

25th June 1992

Laid before Parliament

26th June 1992

Coming into force

1st July 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport, as respects England, the Secretary of State for Wales as respects Wales, and the Secretary of State for Scotland, as respects Scotland, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13A of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Off Road Events) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 1st July 1992.

**2.** In regulation 2 of the Motor Vehicles (Off Road Events) Regulations 1992[^f00002], for the words “Auto-Club Union” there shall be substituted the words “Auto-Cycle Union”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport
