# The Town and Country Planning General (Amendment) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct an error in [S.I. 1992/1492](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1492) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1992 No. 1982

TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Town and Country Planning General (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made

17th August 1992

Laid before Parliament

18th August 1992

Coming into force

19th August 1992

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 316, 333(1) and 336(1) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Town and Country Planning General (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 19th August 1992.

## 2. Effect of planning permission

In regulation 9 of the Town and Country Planning General Regulations 1992[^f00002] (effect of planning permission), for “falling within regulation 3” substitute “of any land by that interested planning authority”.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment

David Maclean

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment

17th August 1992

David Hunt

Secretary of State for Wales

12th August 1992

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Town and Country Planning General Regulations 1992.

The effect of the amendment is that a grant of planning permission for development which falls within regulation 3 of those Regulations (other than development of any land by an interested planning authority or by an interested planning authority jointly with another person specified in the planning application) enures for the benefit of the land and of all persons for the time being interested in it.
