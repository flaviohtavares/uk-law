# The South Devon Health Care National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1129

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The South Devon Health Care National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1992

Made

7th May 1992

Laid before Parliament

14th May 1992

Coming into force

5th June 1992

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by Torbay Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the South Devon Health Care National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the South Devon Health Care National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 5th June 1992.

(2) In this Order:

- “the Authority” means Torbay Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the South Devon Health Care National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust before the date on which this Order is made, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 5th June 1992 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

7th May 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the South Devon Health Care National Health Service Trust on 5th June 1992 trust property formerly held by Torbay Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, Richmond House, 79 Whitehall, London, SW1A 2NS.
