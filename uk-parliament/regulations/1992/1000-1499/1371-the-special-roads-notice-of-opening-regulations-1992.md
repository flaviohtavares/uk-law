# The Special Roads (Notice of Opening) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1371

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Special Roads (Notice of Opening) Regulations 1992

Made

9th June 1992

Laid before Parliament

10th June 1992

Coming into force

1st August 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport as respects England and the Secretary of State for Wales as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 17(5) and 142(1) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and all other enabling powers, and after consulting representative organisations in accordance with section 134(2) of that Act, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Special Roads (Notice of Opening) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 1st August 1992.

## 2. Revocation

The Special Roads (Notice of Opening) Regulations 1962[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

## 3. Notice of opening of a special road

The notice required for the purposes of section 17(5) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 to be published by the traffic authority for a special road, declaring the date on which that road or part of it is open for use as a special road, shall, not less than 7 days before that date, be published in at least one local newspaper circulating in the area in which the special road or, as the case may be, the relevant part of it is situated and in the London Gazette.

John MacGregor

Secretary of State for Transport

3rd June 1992

David Hunt

Secretary of State for Wales

9th June 1992

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the means of publicising the notice of the date on which a new special road, or part of such a road, is first open for use as a special road, and revoke the previous provisions.
