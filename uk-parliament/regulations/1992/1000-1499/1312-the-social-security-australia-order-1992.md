## 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_66c18bdc-4166-44de-c137-c03cec96487b]

This Order may be cited as the Social Security (Australia) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 29th June 1992. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_66c18bdc-4166-44de-c137-c03cec96487b]
