# The Competition and Service (Utilities) Act 1992 Designation Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1360

TELECOMMUNICATIONS

The Competition and Service (Utilities) Act 1992 Designation Order 1992

Made

8th June 1992

Laid before Parliament

10th June 1992

Coming into force

1st July 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 27L and 104 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Competition and Service (Utilities) Act 1992 (Designation) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st July 1992.

**2.** The designated operators for the purposes of sections 27A to 27K of the Telecommunications Act 1984 shall be:

- British Telecommunications plc
- Kingston Communications (Hull) PLC

E. Leigh

Department of Trade and Industry

8th June 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that the designated operators for the purposes of sections 27A to 27K of the Telecommunications Act 1984 (which were inserted by the Competition and Service (Utilities) Act 1992) shall be British Telecommunications plc and Kingston Communications (Hull) PLC.
