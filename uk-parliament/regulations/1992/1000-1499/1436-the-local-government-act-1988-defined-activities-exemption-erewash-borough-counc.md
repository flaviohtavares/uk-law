# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Erewash Borough Council) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1436

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Erewash Borough Council) Order 1992

Made

17th June 1992

Laid before Parliament

25th June 1992

Coming into force

16th July 1992

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(9) and 15(5) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Erewash Borough Council) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 16th July 1992.

**2.** The management of sports and leisure facilities by Erewash Borough Council shall not be treated as a defined activity so long as it is carried out at the West Park Leisure Centre, Long Eaton, before 1st June 1993.

Michael Howard

Secretary of State for the Environment

17th June 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (Competition) work falling within certain defined activities may only be carried out by local authorities if particular conditions are fulfilled.

This Order exempts from the requirements of Part I the management of sports and leisure facilities by Erewash Borough Council so long as it is carried out at the West Park Leisure Centre, Long Eaton, before 1st June 1993.
