# The Court of Session etc. Fees Amendment (No. 2) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of errors in [S.I. 1992/414](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/414) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1992 No. 1230 (S.124)

COURT OF SESSION, SCOTLAND

The Court of Session etc. Fees Amendment (No. 2) Order 1992

Made

20th May 1992

Laid before Parliament

10th June 1992

Coming into force

1st July 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2 of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895([^f00001]), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Court of Session etc. Fees Amendment (No. 2) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st July 1992.

**2.** (1) The Table of Fees in the Schedule to the Court of Session etc. Fees Order 1984([^f00002]) shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2) below.

(2) In Part 1, Head B (fees in the General Department of the Court of Session), items 3 (originating writ in certain consistorial causes) and 6 (certain other writs in consistorial causes), there shall be substituted in column 2 (fee payable) for the amount “ £73” the amount “£40”.

Fraser of Carmyllie

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

13th May 1992

We concur,

Gregory Knight

Irvine Patnick

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

20th May 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects two errors in the Table of Fees which the Court of Session etc. Fees Amendment Order 1992 substituted in the Court of Session etc. Fees Order 1984. Fees in respect of 2 categories of writs in proceedings in the Court of Session were increased in error from £40 to £73. This Order corrects that error.
