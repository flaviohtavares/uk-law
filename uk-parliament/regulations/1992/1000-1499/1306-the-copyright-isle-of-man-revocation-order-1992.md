# The Copyright (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1306

COPYRIGHT

The Copyright (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1992

Made

4th June 1992

Coming into force

1st July 1992

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 4th day of June 1992

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas it appears to Her Majesty that provision with respect to copyright has been made in the law of the Isle of Man otherwise than by extending the provisions of Part I of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^f00001];

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in pursuance of paragraph 36(3) of Schedule 1 to that Act, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Copyright (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st July 1992.

**2.** The Copyright (Isle of Man) Order 1986[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Copyright (Isle of Man) Order 1986, which extended the Copyright Act 1956 to the Isle of Man. It is made consequent on the enactment of legislation in the Isle of Man equivalent to Part I of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988.
