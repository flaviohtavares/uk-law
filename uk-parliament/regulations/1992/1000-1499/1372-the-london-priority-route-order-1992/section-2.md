# The London Priority Route Order 1992, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**2.** The following roads are designated as priority routes for the purposes of Part II of the Road Traffic Act 1991—

- (a) the roads specified in the Schedule to this Order; and
- (b) every slip road (not falling within sub-paragraph (a) above) which links a road mentioned in any paragraph of the Schedule to this Order with a road mentioned in any other paragraph of that Schedule in the vicinity of the place where the two roads join or cross.
