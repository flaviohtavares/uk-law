# The Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct an error in [S.I. 1992 No. 1286](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1286) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1992 No. 1410 (C.46)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order 1992

Made

15th June 1992

Coming into force

30th June 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by section 84 of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 30th June 1992.

**2.** In article 6(5) of the Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1992[^f00002], after the words “where a person is,”there shall be inserted the words “on or”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Kenneth Carlisle

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

15th June 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1992 so as to correct a textual error.
