# The Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1336 (C.43)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992

Made

3rd June 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 8(3) of the Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1992[^f00001] I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as The Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992.

**2.** The Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1992 shall come into force on 1st August 1992.

Kenneth Clarke

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

3rd June 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st August 1992 all the provisions of the Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1992.
