# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1341

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made

4th June 1992

Laid before Parliament

8th June 1992

Coming into force

1st July 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 54(1) and 61 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 1st July 1992.

## 2. Preliminary

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984[^f00002] are further amended in accordance with the following provisions.

## 3. Amendments as to exhaust emissions

(1) In regulation 4—

- (a) in paragraph (3), for “If a vehicle or vehicle part” there is substituted “Save as provided in paragraph (3A) of this regulation, if a vehicle or vehicle part”;
- (b) after paragraph (3) there is inserted the following new paragraph—

  > (3A) The provisions of paragraph (3) of this regulation do not apply in respect of items 4C, 4D, 4E and 4F in Schedule 1.

  ;
- (c) in paragraph (11), for “item 4C, and column (5) of items 4D, 4E and 27” there is substituted “items 4C, 4D, 4E, 4F and 27, and column (6) of items 25 and 26”.

(2) In Schedule 1—

- (a) for item 4D, there is substituted item 4D as specified in Schedule 1 to these regulations;
- (b) in item 4E, in column (6), there is inserted “See regulation 4(11) and Schedule 1B”;
- (c) after item 4E, there is inserted the new items 4F, 4G, 4H and 4J as specified in Schedule 1 to these regulations;
- (d) in item 25, in column (6), there is inserted “See regulation 4(11) and Schedule 1B”;
- (e) in item 26, in column (6), there is inserted “See regulation 4(11) and Schedule 1B”; and
- (f) in item 27, in column (6), there is inserted “See regulation 4(11) and Schedule 1B”.

(3) In Part 1 of Schedule 1B—

- (a) in paragraph 2, after “4E” there is inserted “4F, 25, 26”;
- (b) in paragraph 3(1)(a), for the words “sub-paragraph (2)” there is substituted “sub-paragraphs (2) or (3)”; and
- (c) after paragraph 3(2) there are added the following sub-paragraphs—

  > (3) This sub-paragraph applies in relation to item 4E where a vehicle is fitted with an engine which is described in the Annex to a type-approval certificate granted before the 1st July 1992 in accordance with Council Directive [88/77/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0077)[^f00003].
  >
  > (4) Expressions used in paragraph (3), which are also used in article 3 of Council Directive [91/542/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0542)[^f00004], have the same meaning as in that article.

(4) For Part II of Schedule 1B there is substituted a new Part II as specified in Schedule 2 to these regulations.

## 4. Amendments as to seat belts and their anchorages

In regulation 4, after paragraph (8) there is inserted the following new paragraph—

> (8A) Where, in relation to items 12A(1), 12A(2) and 12A(3) in Schedule 1, a date is specified in column (6) of that Schedule (being a date of cessation of application of type approval requirements), that date shall not apply to any vehicle for which, or for a model of which, there is a type approval certificate or (as the case may be) a Minister’s approval certificate in force at that date.

**5.** In Schedule 1 for item 12A there is substituted items 12A(1), 12A(2) and 12A(3) as specified in Schedule 3 to these regulations.

Signed by authority of The Secretary of State for Transport

Kenneth Carlisle

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

These regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984.

These amendments introduce new type approval requirements as to exhaust emissions and seat belt installations for the vehicles to which the regulations apply, other than vehicles which are expressly exempted. The new type approval requirements for exhaust emissions are contained in the Council and Commission Directives mentioned in the third alternative in item 4D in Schedule 1 to the regulations, the ECE Regulation mentioned in item 4F of that Schedule, the Council and Commission Directives mentioned in item 4G of that Schedule and the Council Directive mentioned in item 4H of that Schedule. The requirements for three-wheeled vehicles are specified in a new item 4J of that Schedule. Also, new provisions are made about the dates of the cessation of the requirements as to fuel, fuel input and particulate emissions respectively specified in items 25, 26 and 27 in Schedule 1 to the 1984 regulations as a result of the addition to that Schedule of new items 4G and 4H.

The new type approval requirements as to seat belt installations are contained in the Council and Commission Directives mentioned in the third alternative in item 12A(1) in Schedule 1 to the regulations, the Council and Commission Directives mentioned in the third alternative in item 12A(2) in that Schedule, the Council Directives mentioned in the third alternative in item 12A(3) in that Schedule and the ECE Regulations mentioned in the sixth and seventh alternatives in that item. Additionally, minor amendments have been made this item to clarify its interpretation.

Copies of the Council and Commission Directives and of the ECE Regulations referred to in the amendments to the regulations can be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office.
