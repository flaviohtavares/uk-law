# The A40 Trunk Road (Western Avenue, Hillingdon) (Prohibition of Use of Gap in Central Reservation) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1252

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A40 Trunk Road (Western Avenue, Hillingdon) (Prohibition of Use of Gap in Central Reservation) Order 1992

Made

26th May 1992

Coming into force

30th May 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(1) and (3) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis pursuant to section 7(6) of that Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A40 Trunk Road (Western Avenue, Hillingdon) (Prohibition of Use of Gap in Central Reservation) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 30th May 1992.

**2.** In this Order—

- “central reservation” means that part of Western Avenue which separates the two carriageways of Western Avenue for the safety and guidance of vehicles using that road; and
- “Western Avenue” means that length of the A40 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Hillingdon known as Western Avenue.

**3.** No person shall cause or permit any vehicle to enter or proceed through the gap in the central reservation which lies opposite the entrance to Northolt Aerodrome.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

J. C. Miles

Director (Network Management) London Regional Office of the

Department of Transport

26th May 1992
