# The Export of Goods (Control) (Serbia and Montenegro Sanctions) (Revocation) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1419

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Export of Goods (Control) (Serbia and Montenegro Sanctions) (Revocation) Order 1992

Made

11th June 1992

Coming into Force

12th June 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Control) (Serbia and Montenegro Sanctions) (Revocation) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 12th June 1992.

**2.** The Export of Goods (Control)(Serbia and Montenegro Sanctions) Order 1992[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

M. Coolican

An Assistant Secretary,

Department of Trade and Industry

11th June 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Export of Goods (Control) (Serbia and Montenegro Sanctions) Order 1992, which prohibited the export of all goods to Serbia and Montenegro without a licence from the Secretary of State.

Prohibition on the export of all goods to Serbia and Montenegro except under the authority of a licence granted by the Secretary of State is now contained in the Serbia and Montenegro (United Nations Sanctions) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/1302](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1302)).
