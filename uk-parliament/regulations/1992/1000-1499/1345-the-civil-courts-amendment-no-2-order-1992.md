# The Civil Courts (Amendment No. 2) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 1345

COUNTY COURTS

The Civil Courts (Amendment No. 2) Order 1992

Made

15th May 1992

Laid before Parliament

9th June 1992

Coming into force

30th June 1992

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2 of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Civil Courts (Amendment No. 2) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 30th June 1992.

**2.** (1) The holding of the Maldon County Court shall be discontinued and Schedule 3 to the Civil Courts Order 1983[^f00002] shall accordingly be amended by deleting the entry relating to that court.

(2) The Chelmsford County Court shall have jurisdiction in proceedings commenced in the Maldon County Court before this Order comes into force.

(3) No process shall be invalid only because the holding of the court described therein by name has been discontinued by this Order.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

Dated 15th May 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Civil Courts Order 1983 so as to close the Maldon County Court in Maldon, Essex. The Chelmsford County Court is given jurisdiction in proceedings commenced in Maldon before its closure.
