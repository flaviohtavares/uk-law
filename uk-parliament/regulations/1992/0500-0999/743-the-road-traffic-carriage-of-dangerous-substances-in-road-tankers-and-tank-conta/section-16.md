# The Road Traffic (Carriage of Dangerous Substances in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1992, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Openings in tanks and valves to be securely closed before commencement of journey

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the driver of a road tanker carrying a dangerous substance or of any other vehicle carrying a dangerous substance in a tank container shall ensure, so far as is practicable, that—

- (a) all openings in the carrying tank of the road tanker or in the tank container concerned; and
- (b) (where any discharge or filling opening in the said carrying tank or tank container is fitted with more than one valve or is fitted with a cap) all such valves and that cap, are securely closed before any journey is commenced.

(2) Nothing in paragraph (1) shall be taken as permitting the proper functioning of any safety device to be compromised.

**16.** Regulation 12—

- (a) imposes duties on respectively the operator of a road tanker carrying a dangerous substance and the operator of any other vehicle carrying a dangerous substance in a tank container with respect to the provision of information about the dangerous substance concerned to the driver of the vehicle; and
- (b) imposes duties on the driver with respect to that information.
