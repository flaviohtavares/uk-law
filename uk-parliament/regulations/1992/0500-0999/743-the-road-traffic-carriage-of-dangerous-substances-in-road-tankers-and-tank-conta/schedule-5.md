### PART II — MODIFICATIONS

**1.** Part I of Schedule 1 to the Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations 1985[^f00027] shall be modified as follows:

- (a) in paragraph 13(1)(a), the words “the carriage of” shall be substituted for the word “conveying” and the words “by road” shall be deleted;
- (b) in paragraph 13(1)(a)(ii), the word “carried” shall be substituted for the word “conveyed”;
- (c) in paragraph 13(1)(b), the word “carried” shall be substituted for the word “conveyed” in the two places where that last-mentioned word appears; and
- (d) in paragraph 13(2), the word “carriage” shall be substituted for the words “conveyance by road” and the words “the Road Traffic (Carriage of Dangerous Substances in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1992” shall be substituted for the words “The Dangerous Substances (Conveyance by Road in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1981”.

**2.** The Dangerous Substances in Harbour Areas Regulations 1987[^f00028] shall be modified as follows:

- (a) the words “the Road Traffic (Carriage of Dangerous Substances in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1992” shall be substituted for the words “the Dangerous Substances (Conveyance by Road in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1981” wherever those last-mentioned words appear in those 1987 Regulations; and
- (b) in the definition of “the IMDG Code” in regulation 2(1), the words “and (4)” shall be inserted between “(3)” and “of”.

**3.** The Pressure Systems and Transportable Gas Containers Regulations 1989[^f00029] shall be modified as follows:

- (a) in paragraph 9 of Part I of Schedule 2, the words “the Road Traffic (Carriage of Dangerous Substances in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1992” shall be substituted for the words the “Dangerous Substances (Conveyance by Road in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1981”;
- (b) in paragraph 2(a) of Part II of Schedule 2, the words “the Road Traffic (Carriage of Dangerous Substances in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1992 applied, or would have applied but for the application of sub-paragraph (d) or (e) of Schedule 2 thereto” shall be substituted for the words “the Dangerous Substances (Conveyance by Road in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1981 applied, or would have applied but for the exceptions specified in regulation 3(1)(c) and (d) thereof”; and
- (c) in paragraph 3(a) of Part II of Schedule 2, the words “the Road Traffic (Carriage of Dangerous Substances in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1992, or would apply but for the application of sub-paragraph (d) or (e) of Schedule 2 thereto” shall be substituted for the words “the Dangerous Substances (Conveyance by Road in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1981 apply or would apply but for the exceptions specified in regulation 3(1)(c) and (d) thereof”.
