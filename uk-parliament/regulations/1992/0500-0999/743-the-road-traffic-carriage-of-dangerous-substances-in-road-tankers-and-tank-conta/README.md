# The Road Traffic (Carriage of Dangerous Substances in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 743

HEALTH AND SAFETY

The Road Traffic (Carriage of Dangerous Substances in Road Tankers and Tank Containers) Regulations 1992

Made

12th March 1992

Laid before Parliament

13th March 1992

Coming into force

for the purposes of all regulations except regulation 7(6) to (9) insofar as those paragraphs apply to the carrying tanks of road tankers and to tank containers which (in either case) are not pressure vessels

1st June 1992

for the purposes of regulation 7(6) to (9) insofar as those paragraphs apply to the carrying tanks of road tankers and to tank containers which (in either case) are not pressure vessels

1st June 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 15(1), (2), (3)(c), (4), (5)(b) and (6)(b), 43(2) to (6) and 82(3)(a) of, and paragraphs 1(1), (2), (3) and (4), 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 14, 15(1) and 16 of Schedule 3 to, the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf and for the purpose of giving effect without modifications to proposals submitted to him by the Health and Safety Commission under section 11(2)(d) of the said Act after the carrying out by the said Commission of consultations in accordance with section 50(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:
