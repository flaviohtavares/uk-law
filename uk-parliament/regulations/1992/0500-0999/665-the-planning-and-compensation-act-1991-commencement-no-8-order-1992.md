# The Planning and Compensation Act 1991 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 665 (C.18)

TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Planning and Compensation Act 1991 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1992

Made

10th March 1992

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 84(2) and (3) of the Planning and Compensation Act 1991[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Planning and Compensation Act 1991 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1992.

## 2. Commencement of provisions concerning advertisements

The following provisions of the Planning and Compensation Act 1991 shall come into force on 6th April 1992—

- section 19, so far as it is not yet in force;
- section 24;
- section 32, so far as it relates to paragraph 38 of Schedule 7 and sub-paragraph (3) of paragraph 53 of that Schedule, so far as that sub-paragraph is not yet in force; and
- section 84(6), so far as it gives effect to the repeal, in Part I of Schedule 19, of paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 1 to the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00002], so far as that repeal is not yet in force.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment

G. S. K. Young

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment

10th March 1992

David Hunt

Secretary of State for Wales

10th March 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 6th April 1992 the following provisions of the Planning and Compensation Act 1991—

- section 19 (receipt and determination of applications) so far as not yet in force;
- section 24 (advertisements);
- section 32 (planning: minor and consequential amendments) so far as that section relates to paragraph 38 of Schedule 7 to the 1991 Act and to paragraph 53(3) of that Schedule to the extent that the latter concerns applications for consent to the display of advertisements; and
- section 84(6) (repeals), so far as it gives effect to the repeal, in Part I of Schedule 19, of paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 1 to the 1990 Act, so far as that repeal is not already in force.

(This note is not part of the Order)
