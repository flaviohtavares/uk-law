# The Aggravated Vehicle-Taking Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 764 (C.23)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Aggravated Vehicle-Taking Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992

Made

12th March 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 4(2) of the Aggravated Vehicle-Taking Act 1992[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Aggravated Vehicle-Taking Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992.

**2.** The Aggravated Vehicle-Taking Act 1992 shall come into force on 1st April 1992.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty"s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

12th March 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 1992 the Aggravated Vehicle-Taking Act 1992.
