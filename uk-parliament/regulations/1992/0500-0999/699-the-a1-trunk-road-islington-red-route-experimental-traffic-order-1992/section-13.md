# The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1992, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Suspensions and Revocations

(1) The following Orders are hereby suspended in so far as they apply to the trunk road red route—

- (a) the Greater London (Waiting Restriction) (Exemptions for Disabled Persons) Order 1975[^f00006], and
- (b) the Islington (Waiting and Loading Restriction) Order 1976[^f00007].

(2) The following Orders are hereby revoked:—

- (a) the A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1990[^f00008];
- (b) the A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Experimental Traffic (Amendment) Order 1991[^f00009];
- (c) the A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Experimental Traffic (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1991[^f00010].
