# The Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 735

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

EDUCATION, SCOTLAND

The Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made

12th March 1992

Laid before Parliament

13th March 1992

Coming into force

6th April 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Education (Student Loans) Act 1990[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Science hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 6th April 1992.

## 2. Amendment of 1991 Regulations

The following amendments shall be made in regulation 11(5) of the Education (Student Loans) Regulations 1991[^f00002] (interpretation of “disability-related benefits”)–

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), at the end the word “and” shall be omitted;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), at the end the word “and” shall be added; and
- (c) after sub-paragraph (b), there shall be added the following paragraph–

  > - (c) a disability living allowance payable under section 37ZA of the Social Security Act 1975 and a disability working allowance payable under section 20 of the Social Security Act 1986[^f00003].

  .

Alan Howarth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Education and Science

12th March 1992

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend with effect from 6th April 1992 regulation 11(5) of the Education (Student Loans) Regulations 1991, which defines the expression “disability-related benefits” for the purposes of the Regulations, by adding a reference to a disability living allowance and a disability working allowance. These allowances were introduced by the Disability Living Allowance and Disability Working Allowance Act [1991 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/21).
