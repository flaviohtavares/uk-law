# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 518

PIPE-LINES

The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) Order 1992

Made

2nd March 1992

Coming into force

24th March 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners)Order 1992 and shall come into force on 24th March 1992.

**2.** For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of the pipe-lines described in Part II of the Schedule hereto.

Colin Moynihan

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Energy

2nd March 1992

## SCHEDULE

### PART I — DESIGNATED OWNERS

- Esso Exploration and Production UK Limited, whose registered office is at Esso House, 21 Victoria Street, London SW1E 5JW.
- Shell U.K. Limited, whose registered office is at Shell-Mex House, Strand, London WC2R 0DX.
