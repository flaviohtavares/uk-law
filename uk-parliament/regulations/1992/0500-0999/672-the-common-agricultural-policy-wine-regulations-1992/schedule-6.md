# The Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) Regulations 1992, SCHEDULE 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 6 — ANALYTICAL TEST FOR QUALITY WINES PSR

Regulation 19

The analytical test shall measure the following factors:–

### 1. Actual Alcoholic Strength

A minimum of 8.5% actual alcoholic strength or 5.5% for wines having a natural alcoholic strength of not less than 10%.

### 2. Total Dry Extract

A minimum of 15 g/l.

### 3. Reducing Sugars

No parameters set: for information only.

### 4. pH

No parameters set: for information only.

### 5. Total Acidity

A minimum of 5 g/l expressed as tartaric acid.

A maximum of 14 g/l expressed as tartaric acid.

### 6. Total Sulphur Dioxide

A maximum as defined in point 7 of Annex 1 to Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 822/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/0822).

### 7. Free Sulphur Dioxide

A minimum of 0 mg/l (at levels below 15 mg/l, it will be necessary to use effective oenological techniques to ensure the stability of the wine).

A maximum of 45 mg/l for dry wines as defined in Article 14(7) of Commission Regulation (EEC) No.3201/90.

A maximum of 60 mg/l for other wines.

### 8. Copper

A maximum of 0.5 mg/l.

### 9. Iron

A maximum of 8 mg/l.

### 10. Sterility

There must be no indication of yeasts or bacteria liable to cause spoilage of the wine.

### 11. Protein Stability

The wine must remain unchanged in appearance after being held at 70° for 15 minutes and subsequently cooled to 20°.
