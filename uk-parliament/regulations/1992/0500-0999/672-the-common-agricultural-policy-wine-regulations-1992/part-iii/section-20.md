# The Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) Regulations 1992, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Additional term of designation

For the purposes of Article 15(1) of Regulation 823/87, a quality wine psr produced in a specified region may be described as a wine of “designated origin”.
