# The Foresterhill Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1991/2899](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/2899) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1992 No. 800 (S.84)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The Foresterhill Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1992

Made

12th March 1992

Coming into force

25th March 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 12A(1), of and paragraphs 1 and 3(1)(a) and (c) of Schedule 7A to the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Foresterhill Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1992 and shall come into force on 25th March 1992.

(2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the Foresterhill Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of definition of “the trust”

In article 1 of the principal Order in the definition of “the trust” for the words “the Foresterhill Hospitals National Health Service Trust” there are substituted the words “The Aberdeen Royal Hospitals National Health Service Trust”.

## 3. Amendment of Article 2 of the principal Order

In article 2 of the principal Order for the words “the Foresterhill Hospitals National Health Service Trust” there are substituted the words “The Aberdeen Royal Hospitals National Health Service Trust”.

## 4. Amendment of Article 4 of the principal Order

For article 4 of the principal Order there shall be substituted the following article:—

> **4. Directors of the trust**
>
> The trust shall have, in addition to the chairman, 5 non-executive directors, one of whom shall be appointed from the University of Aberdeen, and 5 executive directors.

.

Ian Lang

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

12th March 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends articles 1 and 2 of the Foresterhill Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991 to change the name of the Trust to “The Aberdeen Royal Hospitals National Health Service Trust”. It also amends article 4 of that Order to make clear that the number of executive directors of the Trust shall be five.
