# The Patents (Fees) Rules 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 616

PATENTS

The Patents (Fees) Rules 1992

Made

10th March 1992

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1992

Coming into force

11th May 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 123(1), (2) and (3) of, and paragraph 14 of Schedule 4 to, the Patents Act 1977[^f00001], of the power conferred upon him by the Department of Trade and Industry (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with the Council on Tribunals pursuant to section10(1) of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1971[^f00003] and with the consent of the Treasury pursuant to subsection (4) of the said section 123, hereby makes the following Rules:—

**1.** (1) These Rules may be cited as the Patents (Fees) Rules 1992 and shall come into force on 11th May 1992.

(2) The Patents (Fees) Rules 1991[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

**2.** These Rules shall be construed as one with the Patents Rules 1990[^f00005] and, to the extent that they continue to apply in relation to existing patents and existing applications, to the Patents Rules 1968[^f00006].

**3.** The fees to be paid in respect of any matters arising under the Patents Act 1977 or arising under the Patents Act 1949[^f00007] shall be those specified in Parts A and B of the Schedule to these Rules; and in any case where a form specified in Part A or Part B of the Schedule as the corresponding form in relation to any matter is required by the Patents Rules 1990 or, as the case may be, the Patents Rules 1968 to be used, that form shall be accompanied by the fee, if any, specified in respect of that matter or, where payment maybe made within a prescribed period of time after the form has been filed, the fee so specified shall be paid within that period.

Reay

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Industry and Technology

Department of Trade and Industry

6th March 1992

We consent to the making of these Rules.

These Rules revoke and replace the Patents (Fees) Rules 1991 (S.I.[1991/1627](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1627)). Fees in respect of several matters have been increased; the increases take effect from 11th May 1992.

It is estimated that the total amount of fees collected per annum at the new levels will be 10 per cent greater than the amount collected per annum at existing levels.
