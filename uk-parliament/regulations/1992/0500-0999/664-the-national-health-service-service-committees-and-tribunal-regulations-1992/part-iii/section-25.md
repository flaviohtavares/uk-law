# The National Health Service (Service Committees and Tribunal) Regulations 1992, section 25

> Text as at 2023-10-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Withdrawal of representations and suspension of procedures (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9798331cea304f672f5ef8426fcc4ac8][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] The complainant may at any time before the inquiry, with the consent of the Tribunal and on such terms as the Tribunal thinks fit, withdraw his representations by giving notice of withdrawal in writing to the Tribunal. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] If the complainant fails to comply, within the time allowed by the Tribunal for such compliance, with any requirement imposed under regulation 24 or 26, or fails, without having previously offered a reasonable excuse, to appear at an inquiry of which due notice was given under paragraph 1 of Schedule 9, the Tribunal may treat the representations as having been withdrawn. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] Subject to paragraph (3A),[^c-c26dbc6fcd91c98a772a3422a4a9c8da] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] where representations are withdrawn or treated as withdrawn the Tribunal shall immediately so inform the respondent in writing (without prejudice to its power to determine any question as to costs under *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] regulation 31A[^c-6bb4aa2622d6de0d28f5e862a7e0f733] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]). *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(3A (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-08c14811076c7bc217b75943032041d1][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] Where representations are withdrawn or treated as withdrawn after the Tribunal has given a direction under section 49A(2) of the Act, that direction shall cease to have effect; and— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] the Tribunal shall include this information also when informing the respondent under paragraph (3); *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] the Tribunal shall immediately also so inform the Secretary of State in writing that the direction has ceased to have effect; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] the Secretary of State shall so inform anyone to whom he sent a copy of the Tribunal’s decision pursuant to regulation 24A(7). *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] Except where the Tribunal has made a direction under section 49A(2) of the Act,[^c-17d50e7d8b712384c5aea7dac4c20eb1] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] where it appears to the Tribunal that the alleged facts on which any representations are based are being or may be investigated by some other tribunal, body or person, it may direct that no further steps shall be taken under this Part pending the result of the other investigation. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(5 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c2a4bd8bbb15f465af437d3c120e108e][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] The Tribunal may also direct that no further steps shall be taken for the time being under this Part in any other circumstances where it appears to the Tribunal that it would be appropriate so to direct. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
