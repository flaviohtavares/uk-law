## 29 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Applications to the Tribunal (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1b071810cb156f78ec6fdb1547ec4863][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] An application made to the Tribunal shall be sent to the Tribunal. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] The Tribunal shall send to the respondent a copy of the application, together with a copy of any document included with it under *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] regulation 28(2)(d)[^key-ca7f6197dfd8bd0b016c97775981fbf6] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] The Tribunal shall consider the application and— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] if it considers that no good cause has been shown for an inquiry, it may decline to hold an inquiry and to make a direction under section 47 of the Act *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] or (as the case may be) under paragraph 5 of Schedule 1 to the 1997 Act[^key-f677d6b7c4b731897c7184788804c33a] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] and shall give notice in writing to the applicant and the respondent accordingly; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] if it considers that an inquiry should be held, it shall give notice in writing to the applicant and the respondent accordingly. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] The applicant may at any time before the inquiry, with the consent of the Tribunal and on such terms as it thinks fit, withdraw his application by giving notice of withdrawal in writing to the Tribunal; and where an application is withdrawn the Tribunal shall so inform the respondent in writing. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(5 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] Where an inquiry is to be held by way of an oral hearing, the provisions of Schedule 9 shall apply with respect to that hearing. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(6 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] At the conclusion of the inquiry the Tribunal shall, as soon as practicable, issue a decision in writing, signed by *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] the person (being the chairman or a deputy chairman) who presided over the inquiry[^key-6162140cb9cc8c2b805e0c0e56fc01ce] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6], containing— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] its findings of fact; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] its conclusions; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] any direction it decides to give under section 47 of the Act *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] or (as the case may be) under paragraph 5 of Schedule 1 to the 1997 Act[^key-d56fdff30e11c4a2485d4e517b830082] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (d (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] any order it decides to make as to costs. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(7 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] The Tribunal shall send a copy of the Tribunal’s decision to the applicant, the respondent and the Secretary of State; and the Secretary of State shall send a copy of the decision to any other *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] Health Authority or Primary Care Trust[^key-75fa0cf48b62461616882c2d7478c7c1] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] which appears to him to be concerned. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
