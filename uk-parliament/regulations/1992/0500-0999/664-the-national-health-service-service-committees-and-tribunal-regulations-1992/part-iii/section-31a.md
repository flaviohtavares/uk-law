# The National Health Service (Service Committees and Tribunal) Regulations 1992, section 31A

> Text as at 2023-10-20.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31A (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Costs (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f49c35ea34de0f3a229dbd276152052c][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 250(5) of the Local Government Act 1972 (costs) shall apply to Tribunal proceedings as though the reference there to the Minister causing an inquiry to be held under section 250 were to the Tribunal, and as if the reference to the parties at the inquiry were to the parties to Tribunal proceedings. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
