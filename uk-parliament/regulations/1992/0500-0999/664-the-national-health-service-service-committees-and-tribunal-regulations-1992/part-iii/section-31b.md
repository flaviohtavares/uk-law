# The National Health Service (Service Committees and Tribunal) Regulations 1992, section 31B

> Text as at 2023-10-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31B (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Publication of information about applications involving preferential treatment (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-500d93504b4d38b172f434c8ae22642a][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State shall publish in such a manner as he thinks fit information about— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] applications made to Health Authorities *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] or Primary Care Trusts[^key-7750ac2bf3e444148c0e55842c33e513] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] for inclusion in their medical list of the names of medical practitioners in respect of whom Schedule 1 to the 1997 Act applies; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] the right of persons to make representations against such applications. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
