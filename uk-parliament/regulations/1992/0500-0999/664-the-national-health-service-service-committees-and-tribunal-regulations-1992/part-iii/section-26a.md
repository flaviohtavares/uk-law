# The National Health Service (Service Committees and Tribunal) Regulations 1992, section 26A

> Text as at 2023-10-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26A (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Discharge of suspension functions by chairman or deputy chairman (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e11fc4dca5e8b648fb379c8883c40c00][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

*Extent: E+W*

The functions of the Tribunal under section 49A or 49B of the Act may be carried out by the chairman or a deputy chairman of the Tribunal; and where the chairman or a deputy chairman does carry them out, any reference to the Tribunal in this Part or in Schedule 9 shall be construed as a reference to that person. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
