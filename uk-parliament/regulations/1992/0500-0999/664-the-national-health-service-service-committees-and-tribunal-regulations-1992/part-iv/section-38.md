## 38 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Transitional provisions (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f13e5a34726128f2300f395535f68f07][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

*Extent: E+W*

Where, before 1st April 1992— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] a complaint has been made under regulation 4, or a matter has been referred under regulation 6(6), of the National Health Service (Service Committees and Tribunal) Regulations 1974 *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6][^f00020]; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] an application has been made to the Secretary of State under regulation 15(3) of those Regulations; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] a matter has been referred to a Local Representative Committee, or to a joint committee of Local Medical Committees, under regulation 16(1)(a), 17, 18 or 19 of those Regulations; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (d (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] an appeal has been made under regulation 21 of those Regulations; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (e (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] a matter has been referred to an ophthalmic service committee under regulation 23(1) of those Regulations; or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (f (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] representations have been made to the Tribunal under regulation 28, or an application has been made to the Tribunal or the Secretary of State under regulation 43, of those Regulations, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

the provisions of those Regulations shall, notwithstanding regulation 39, continue to apply on and after that date, as respects any investigation, consideration, hearing, referral, report or determination which, by virtue of any provision of those Regulations, falls to be undertaken, held or made in relation to any such application, reference, appeal or representations, or in relation to any appeal under those Regulations from any determination of such application, reference or representations. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
