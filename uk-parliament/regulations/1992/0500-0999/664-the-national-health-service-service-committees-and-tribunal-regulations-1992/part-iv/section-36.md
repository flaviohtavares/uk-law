# The National Health Service (Service Committees and Tribunal) Regulations 1992, section 36

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Fresh appointments (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d21edb1ad3b5524161394686179f4b7d][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] Where a person appointed by the Secretary of State for any purpose of any provision of regulation *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] 10, 11, 12[^c-05e4ab36cf160b17e1c3734df636d8fc] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] or of Schedule 5— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] dies or resigns before the completion of that purpose; or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] is unable or refuses to complete that purpose, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

the Secretary of State may rescind that appointment together with that of any other person so appointed in relation to the same matter, and appoint different persons in accordance with the provision in question. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] Where different persons are appointed pursuant to paragraph (1), any matter referred to them shall be considered afresh. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] For the purposes of this regulation a person has completed the purpose for which he was appointed if he has indicated to any other person also appointed for that purpose the decision to be given or recommendation to be made, even if he has not signed a report embodying the decision or recommendation. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
