# The National Health Service (Service Committees and Tribunal) Regulations 1992, section 34

> Text as at 2023-10-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Power to dispense with requirements as to notices (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-567fef70b354d951019139d339d172b2][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State or the Tribunal may dispense with any requirements of these Regulations respecting notices, applications, documents or otherwise in any case where it appears to the Secretary of State or the Tribunal just and proper to do so. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
