# The National Health Service (Service Committees and Tribunal) Regulations 1992, section 13

> Text as at 2023-10-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Death of practitioner (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8b716a67873796fbb45b5bfa656921e0][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

*Extent: E+W*

Where, at any time after a disciplinary matter has been referred under regulation 5(1) but before the appropriate Health Authority *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] or Primary Care Trust[^key-c3b926ba04c2d1fe7743ca9f5ecd96be] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] makes a determination under regulation 8 in relation to that matter, the practitioner to whom the matter relates dies, no further action shall be taken under these Regulations in relation to that practitioner. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
