## 7 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Investigations by discipline committees (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8b716a67873796fbb45b5bfa656921e0][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] A discipline committee shall investigate any matter which is referred to it. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] Schedule 4 shall have effect with respect to the procedure for investigating disciplinary matters under this Part. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] Where the disciplinary matter concerns an alleged overpayment made to a practitioner pursuant to regulation 4(7) the appropriate Health Authority *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] or Primary Care Trust[^key-d9ebc41967b86be846577efef895d4bd] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] may refer it under regulation 5(1) at any time. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
