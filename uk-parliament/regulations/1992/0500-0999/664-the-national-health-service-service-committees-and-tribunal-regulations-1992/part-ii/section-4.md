(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] Where an appropriate Health Authority *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] or Primary Care Trust[^key-b17667eabd8fb5dfda47d840c7f33186] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] receives information which it considers could amount to an allegation that a practitioner has failed to comply with his terms of service it shall decide either to take no action or to take one or both of the courses of action set out in paragraph (2). *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] The courses of action referred to in paragraph (1) are— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] to refer the matter to another Health Authority *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] or Primary Care Trust *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] for investigation in accordance with regulation 5(1); *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] to refer the information to, as it considers appropriate, the Tribunal, the relevant professional body or the local police authority. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] The appropriate Health Authority *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] or Primary Care Trust *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] shall not proceed under paragraph 2(a) in any case where the allegation and information on which it is based is the subject of a complaint which is being investigated. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

(4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] For the purposes of these Regulations an allegation remains the subject of a complaint which is being investigated until— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] the procedure for investigating the complaint is completed by the delivery of a report of the investigation to a Health Authority *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] or Primary Care Trust *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] by a panel appointed in accordance with directions given under section 17 of the Act; or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] the complaint is withdrawn or abandoned by the person bringing it. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4b55febd390321d4b9fbf710a0be0e3c][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4b55febd390321d4b9fbf710a0be0e3c][^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]

at the relevant time or, where the practitioner referred to in sub-paragraph (a) *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] [^key-00ae89e79ba200bcc228ec4821846def]... was at that time on more than one such list, the Health Authority *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] or Primary Care Trust *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] by arrangement with which the *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] relevant services *(Extent: W)*[^key-1e8c0b98ee10d8e24f8ab72597fa23c7] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6] giving rise to the allegation were provided. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e4e2f3ac-8547-4db6-c01b-d3a5cef933b6]
