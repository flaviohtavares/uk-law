# The National Health Service (General Medical Services) Regulations 1992, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Appointment of medical adviser

An FHSA shall appoint a doctor to assist it in the exercise of its functions pursuant to paragraph 49 of the terms of service for doctors (inquiries about prescriptions and referrals).
