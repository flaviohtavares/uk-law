# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 748 (S.72)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 1992

Made

12th March 1992

Laid before Parliament

13th March 1992

Coming into force

6th April 1992

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 1992 and shall come into force on 6th April 1992.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of solicitors' fees

(1) In the Table of Fees in the Schedule to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) 1989[^f00002], in Chapters I, II, III, IV and VI, for the fees specified in column 2 of the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt substitute the fees specified in column 3 of that Schedule; and such fees in column 3 shall be payable for work done on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

(2) The fees specified in the Table of Fees in the Schedule to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) 1989 which are specified in column 2 of the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt shall continue to have effect in respect of work done before this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

J.A.D. Hope

Lord President, IPD

Edinburgh

12th March 1992
