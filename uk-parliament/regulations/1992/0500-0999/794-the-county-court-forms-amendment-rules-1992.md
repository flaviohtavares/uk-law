# The County Court (Forms)(Amendment) Rules 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 794 (L.6)

COUNTY COURTS

PROCEDURE

The County Court (Forms)(Amendment) Rules 1992

Made

12th March 1992

Coming into force

1st April 1992

**1.** (1) These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Forms) (Amendment) Rules 1992.

(2) In these Rules, a form referred to by number means the form so numbered in the Schedule to the County Court (Forms) Rules 1982[^f00001] and “the main Schedule” means that Schedule.

**2.** Forms N.114 and N.116 in the main Schedule shall be omitted.

**3.** There shall be substituted in the main Schedule, for forms N.38, N.64A and N.65, the forms contained in Schedule 1 to these Rules.

We, the undersigned members of the Rule Committee appointed by the Lord Chancellor under section 75 of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00002], having by virtue of the powers vested in us in that behalf made the foregoing Rules, do hereby certify the same under our hands and submit them to the Lord Chancellor accordingly.

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the County Court (Forms) Rules 1982 so as to revoke forms N.114 and N.116 which relate to custodianship. These Rules also provide new forms of order for oral examination (person other than judgment debtor) (N.38), suspended attachment of earnings order— maintenance (N.64A) and attachment of earnings order (priority maintenance) (N.65).
