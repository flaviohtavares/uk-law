# The Crofter Forestry (Scotland) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 504 (C. 14) (S. 55)

CROFTERS, COTTARS AND SMALL LANDHOLDERS

The Crofter Forestry (Scotland) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992

Made

4th March 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4(2) of the Crofter Forestry (Scotland) Act 1991[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Crofter Forestry (Scotland) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992.

**2.** The Crofter Forestry (Scotland) Act 1991 shall come into force on 1st April 1992.

Strathclyde

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

4th March 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the Crofter Forestry (Scotland) Act 1991 on 1st April 1992.
