# The Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 562

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made

9th March 1992

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1992

Coming into force

6th April 1992

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 102(2) and (4), 137(2) and 138(1)[^f00001] of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 6th April 1992.

## 2. Amendment of Regulations

## 3. Transitional

In respect of any application for a grant made before 6th April 1992 the provisions of the Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) Regulations 1990 shall apply as if these Regulations had not come into force.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the forms in the Schedule to the Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) Regulations 1990. The amendments are consequential upon amendments made by the Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 (S.I. [1992/705](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/705)) to the Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Reduction of Grant) Regulations 1990 (S.I. [1990/1189](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1189)) in relation to disability living allowance and disability working allowance.

The Regulations provide that the amendments do not apply in respect of any application for a grant made before the date on which these Regulations come into force.
