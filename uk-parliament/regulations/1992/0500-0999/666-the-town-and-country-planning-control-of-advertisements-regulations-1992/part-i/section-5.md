## 5 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Requirement for consent (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a57140f2b1d6809bd8df1ddb35e3d19c][^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] No advertisement may be displayed without consent granted by the local planning authority or by the Secretary of State on an application in that behalf (referred to in these Regulations as “express consent”), or granted by regulation 6 (referred to in these Regulations as “deemed consent”), except an advertisement displayed in accordance with paragraph (2) below. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] The display— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] outside any area of special control, of such an advertisement as is mentioned in regulation 3(2); or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] within an area of special control, of such an advertisement as is so mentioned, other than one falling within Class A in Schedule 2, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

is in accordance with this paragraph. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
