# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992, section 19

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Control in areas of special control (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8e1d22c5b8a8ea852df4e7f86d27bb7d][^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Subject to the provisions of this regulation, no advertisements may be displayed in an area of special control unless they fall within— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Classes B to J in Schedule 2; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Classes 1 to 3, 5 to 7 and 9 to 14 in Schedule 3; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] paragraph (2) below. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Advertisements of the following descriptions displayed with express consent come within this paragraph— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] hoardings or similar structures to be used only for the display of notices relating to local events, activities or entertainments; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] any advertisement for the purpose of announcement or direction in relation to buildings or other land in the locality, where reasonably required having regard to the nature and situation of such buildings or other land; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] any advertisement required in the interests of public safety; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (d (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] any advertisement which could be displayed by virtue of paragraph (1)(b) above but for some non-compliance with a condition or limitation imposed by Schedule 3 as respects size, height from the ground, number or illumination or but for a direction under regulation 7; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (e (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] any advertisement within Class 4A, 4B or 8 in Schedule 3. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Express consent may not be given for the display in an area of special control of an illuminated advertisement falling within sub-paragraph (2)(a) or (b) above. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Without prejudice to paragraph (2) above, where an area is designated as an area of special control, advertisements within paragraph (5) below which are being displayed immediately before the relevant order comes into force may continue to be displayed, but only for the period specified in relation thereto in that paragraph. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(5 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] The advertisements and specified periods mentioned in paragraph (4) above are— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] any advertisement within Class 4A or 4B in Schedule 3 for which express consent has not been granted, 5 years from the date on which the order comes into force; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] any advertisement within Class 8 for which express consent has not been granted, 1 year from the date on which the order comes into force or 2 years from the date on which the advertisement was first displayed, whichever period expires later; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] any advertisement for which express consent has been granted, 6 months from the date on which the order comes into force or for the remainder of the period of the express consent, whichever period expires later. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(6 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Nothing in paragraphs (1) to (5) above shall— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] affect a notice served at any time under regulation 8; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] override any condition imposed on a consent, whereby an advertisement is required to be removed; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] restrict the powers of a local planning authority, or of the Secretary of State, in regard to any contravention of these Regulations; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (d (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] render unlawful the display, pursuant to express consent or to Class 14 in Schedule 3, of an advertisement mentioned in paragraph 2(d) or (e) above. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
