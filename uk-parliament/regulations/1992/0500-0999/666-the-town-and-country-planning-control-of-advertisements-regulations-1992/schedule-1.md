# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note) — STANDARD CONDITIONS (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 2(1) *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-9280d2ecdf10decb13bcedc8a274952e][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Any advertisements displayed, and any site used for the display of advertisements, shall be maintained in a clean and tidy condition to the reasonable satisfaction of the local planning authority. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-99ae050015d8575677939b94ecc56223][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Any structure or hoarding erected or used principally for the purpose of displaying advertisements shall be maintained in a safe condition. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-b3ce2c95be212ad495e89b4b9ed1d710][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Where an advertisement is required under these Regulations to be removed, the removal shall be carried out to the reasonable satisfaction of the local planning authority. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-2954615de3fd9faf2fd03b92a37c680a][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] No advertisement is to be displayed without the permission of the owner of the site or any other person with an interest in the site entitled to grant permission. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**5 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-531981ffefdc2b47e9df73e567496d37][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] No advertisement shall be sited or displayed so as to obscure, or hinder the ready interpretation of, any road traffic sign, railway signal or aid to navigation by water or air, or so as otherwise to render hazardous the use of any highway, railway, waterway or aerodrome (civil or military). *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
