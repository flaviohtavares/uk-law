# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992, section 8

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Discontinuance of deemed consent (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-dd7497a853ab3d7e9210f200c02deab2][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] The local planning authority may serve a notice requiring the discontinuance of the display of an advertisement, or of the use of a site for the display of an advertisement, for which deemed consent is granted under regulation 6 if they are satisfied that it is necessary to do so to remedy a substantial injury to the amenity of the locality or a danger to members of the public: but in the case of an advertisement within Class 12 in Schedule 3, they may not do so if the advertisement is also within Class F or Class G in Schedule 2. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] A discontinuance notice— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] shall be served on the advertiser and on the owner and occupier of the site on which the advertisement is displayed; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] may, if the local planning authority think fit, also be served on any other person displaying the advertisement; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] shall specify the advertisement or the site to which it relates; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (d (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] shall specify a period within which the display or the use of the site (as the case may be) is to be discontinued; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (e (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] shall contain a full statement of the reasons why action has been taken under this regulation. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Subject to paragraphs (4) and (5) below, a discontinuance notice shall take effect at the end of the period (being at least 8 weeks after the date on which it is served) specified in the notice. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] If an appeal is made to the Secretary of State under regulation 15, the notice shall be of no effect pending the final determination or withdrawal of the appeal. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(5 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] The local planning authority, by a notice served on the advertiser, may withdraw a discontinuance notice at any time before it takes effect or may, where no appeal to the Secretary of State is pending, from time to time vary a discontinuance notice by extending the period specified for the taking effect of the notice. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(6 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] The local planning authority shall, on serving on the advertiser a notice of withdrawal or variation under paragraph (5) above, send a copy to every other person served with the discontinuance notice. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
