# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992, section 7

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Directions restricting deemed consent (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-885863656a4af73def0aa181b8f82305][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] If the Secretary of State is satisfied, upon a proposal made to him by the local planning authority, that the display of advertisements of any class or description specified in Schedule 3, other than Class 12 or 13, should not be undertaken in any particular area or in any particular case without express consent, he may direct that the consent granted by regulation 6 for that class or description shall not apply in that area or in that case, for a specified period or indefinitely. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Before making any such direction, the Secretary of State shall— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] where the proposal relates to a particular area, publish, or cause to be published, in at least one newspaper circulating in the locality, and on the same or a subsequent date in the London Gazette, a notice that such a proposal has been made, naming a place or places in the locality where a map or maps defining the area concerned may be inspected at all reasonable hours; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] where the proposal relates to a particular case, serve, or cause to be served, on the owner and occupier of the land affected and on any other person who, to his knowledge, proposes to display on such land an advertisement of the class or description concerned, a notice that a proposal has been made, specifying the land and the class or description of advertisement in question. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Any notice under paragraph (2) above shall state that any objection to the making of a direction may be made to the Secretary of State in writing within such period (not being less than 21 days from the date when the notice was given) as is specified in the notice. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] The Secretary of State shall not make a direction under this regulation until after the expiry of the specified period. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(5 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] In determining whether to make a direction, the Secretary of State— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] shall take into account any objections made in accordance with paragraph (3) above; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] may modify the proposal of the local planning authority if— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
  - (i (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] he has notified, in writing, that authority and any person who has made an objection or representation to him of his intention and his reasons for it and has given them a reasonable opportunity to respond; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
  - (ii (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] the intended modification does not extend the area of land specified in the proposal. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(6 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Where the Secretary of State makes a direction, he shall send it to the local planning authority, with a statement of his reasons for making it, and shall send a copy of that statement to any person who has made an objection in accordance with paragraph (3) above. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(7 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Notice of the making of any direction for a particular area shall be published by the local planning authority in at least one newspaper circulating in the locality and, unless the Secretary of State otherwise directs, on the same or a subsequent date in the London Gazette, and such notice shall— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] contain a full statement of the effect of the direction; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] name a place or places in the locality where a copy of the direction and of a map defining the area concerned may be seen at all reasonable hours; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] specify a date when the direction shall come into force, being at least 14 and not more than 28 days after the first publication of the notice. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(8 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Notice of the making of any direction for a particular case shall be served by the local planning authority on the owner and on any occupier of the land to which the direction relates, and on any other person who, to the knowledge of the authority, proposes to display on such land an advertisement of the class or description affected. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(9 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] A direction for an area shall come into force on the date specified in the notice given under paragraph (7) above, and a direction for a particular case shall come into force on the date on which notice is served on the occupier or, if there is no occupier, on the owner of the land affected. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
