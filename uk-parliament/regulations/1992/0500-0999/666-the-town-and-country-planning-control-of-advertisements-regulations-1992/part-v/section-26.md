## 26 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Cancellation or variation of directions (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ac85fccd1f2022c2a7f877a6de2a0027][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
