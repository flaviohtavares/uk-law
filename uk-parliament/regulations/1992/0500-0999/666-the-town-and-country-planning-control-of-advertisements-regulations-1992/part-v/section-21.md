# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992, section 21

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Register of applications (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9021eef032da8a98221f17665b8ccb56][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Every local planning authority shall keep a register containing particulars of— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] any application made to them for express consent for the display of an advertisement, including the name and address of the applicant, the date of the application and the type of advertisement concerned; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] any direction given under these Regulations relating to the application; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] the date and effect of any decision of the local planning authority on the application; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (d (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] the date and effect of any decision of the Secretary of State on an appeal. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] The register shall include an index to enable a person to trace any entry therein. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Any part of the register which relates to land within a particular part of the area of a local planning authority may be kept at a place within or convenient to that part of their area. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Subject to paragraph (3) above, the register shall be kept at the office of the local planning authority. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(5 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Every entry in the register consisting of particulars of an application shall be made within 14 days of the receipt of that application. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(6 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] The register shall be open to public inspection at all reasonable hours. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
