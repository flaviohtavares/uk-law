## 23 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Exercise of powers by the Secretary of State (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7781bee3f6c32989e06130d62eb1ec3d][^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] If it appears to the Secretary of State, after consultation with the local planning authority, that— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] a discontinuance notice should be served under regulation 8; or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] an area of special control order, or an order revoking such an order, should be made under regulation 18, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

he may himself serve such a notice or make such an order. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
