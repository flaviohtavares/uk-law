# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992, section 27

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Contravention of Regulations (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0ab3bd328356ed358125f3678ca348fe][^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

*Extent: E+W*

A person displaying an advertisement in contravention of these Regulations shall be liable on summary conviction of an offence under section 224(3) of *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] the Act *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^f00009] to a fine of an amount not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale and, in the case of a continuing offence, one-tenth of level 3 on the standard scale for each day during which the offence continues after conviction. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
