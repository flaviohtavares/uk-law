> Text as at 2025-07-18.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Directions requiring information (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-aab193bfe372c43c4ec0d2ba325f7371][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State may give a direction to a local planning authority, or to such authorities generally, requiring them to provide him with information required for the purpose of any of his functions under these Regulations. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
