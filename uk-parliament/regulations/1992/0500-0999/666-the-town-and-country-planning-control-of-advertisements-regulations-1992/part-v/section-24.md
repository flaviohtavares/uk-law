# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992, section 24

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Discontinuance notice in respect of authority’s advertisement (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c50de71c1d712e5cb43b62e516ee7fc2][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] If the Secretary of State is satisfied that it is necessary to remedy a substantial injury to the amenity of the locality or a danger to members of the public, he may serve a discontinuance notice under regulation 8 in relation to an advertisement *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] displayed by an interested planning authority[^c-3522dc8c7c7600c05618da8da3f333a3] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Paragraphs (2), (5) and (6) of regulation 8 shall apply to a discontinuance notice to which paragraph (1) above applies as if references to the local planning authority were references to the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Paragraph (3) of regulation 15 shall apply to a discontinuance notice to which paragraph (1) above applies, with such modifications as may be necessary. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
