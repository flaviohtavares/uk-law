# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992, section 11

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Receipt of applications (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bfb01e20ba05dbd2eb648798792b7ab4][^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

*Extent: E+W*

On receipt of an application for express consent, the local planning authority— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] shall send an acknowledgement in writing to the applicant and, in the case of a county planning authority, shall also send a copy of the application and the accompanying plans to the district planning authority within whose area any part of the application site is situated; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] may direct the applicant in writing to provide one of their officers with such evidence as may reasonably be called for to verify any particulars or information given to them. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
