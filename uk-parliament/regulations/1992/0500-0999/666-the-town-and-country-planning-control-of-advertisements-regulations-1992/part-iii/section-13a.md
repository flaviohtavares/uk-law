# The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992, section 13A

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13A (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Applications by an interested planning authority (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4bbf8a4eaa6edfa0ab187aa549fd649a][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] An application for express consent made by an interested planning authority (whether solely or jointly with any other person) to display an advertisement shall be determined by the authority concerned unless the application is referred to the Secretary of State under section 77 of the Act for determination by him. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Any consent granted pursuant to paragraph (1) shall expire— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] at the end of such period as the authority may specify in granting the consent; or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] where no period is so specified, at the end of a period of 5 years; or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] the date on which the interested planning authority ceases (whether solely or jointly) to display the advertisement, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

whichever is the earlier. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
