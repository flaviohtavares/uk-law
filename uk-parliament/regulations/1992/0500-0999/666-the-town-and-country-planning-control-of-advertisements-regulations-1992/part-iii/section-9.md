## 9 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Applications for express consent (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4e791500c354736a6bdaa1d51a062ab3][^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] An application for express consent must be made to the local planning authority. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Subject to paragraph 6, the application must be made electronically or in hard copy on a form published by the Welsh Ministers or a form substantially to the same effect. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] The applicant must— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] include the particulars specified in the form; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] send with the application (whether electronically or otherwise) a plan which— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
  - (i (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] is drawn to an identified scale, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
  - (ii (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] shows the direction of north, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
  - (iii (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] identifies the location of the site by reference to at least two named roads, and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
  - (iv (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] identifies the proposed position of the advertisement. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Unless an application is made electronically or the local planning authority indicates that fewer copies are required, three copies of the completed form and the plan must accompany the application. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(5 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Where the application is one to which directions given by the Welsh Ministers under regulation 10 apply, the applicant must send with the application (whether electronically or otherwise) such particulars, plans or information specified or referred to in those directions as may have been notified to the applicant by the local planning authority. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(6 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] An application made on or after 30 April 2012 and before 31 May 2012, otherwise than by a local planning authority, may be made in writing on a form devised by the local planning authority. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(7 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Where an application is made electronically, the applicant is taken to have agreed— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] to the use by the local planning authority of electronic communication for the purposes of the application; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] that the applicant’s address for that purpose is the address incorporated into, or otherwise logically associated with, the application; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] that deemed agreement under this paragraph subsists until the applicant gives notice in writing— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
  - (i (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] withdrawing any address notified to the authority for that purpose, or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
  - (ii (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] revoking that deemed agreement, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

  and such withdrawal or revocation is final and takes effect on a date specified by the applicant in the notice at least seven days after the date on which the notice is given. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(8 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] An application made electronically must, unless the contrary is proved, be treated as having been delivered at 9.00 a.m. on the next working day after the day on which it is transmitted. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

(9 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] This regulation applies to applications for renewal consent as it applies to applications for consent. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
