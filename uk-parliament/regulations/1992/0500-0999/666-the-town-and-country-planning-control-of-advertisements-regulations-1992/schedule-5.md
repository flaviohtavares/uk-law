### PART I (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note) — PROCEDURE FOR AREA OF SPECIAL CONTROL ORDERS (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-561c0219cd8b9f513a132c7bdf3f5a44][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] A local planning authority who propose— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] to designate an area of special control; or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] to modify an area of special control order, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

shall make an area of special control order designating the area or indicating the modifications by reference to an annexed map. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-eda2d62d2ad1ddef9866a4ac210827d5][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] If an area of special control order contains any descriptive matter relating to the area or the modifications in question, that descriptive matter shall prevail, in the case of any discrepancy with the map, unless the order provides to the contrary. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-a6ac808f89540df60f8cad371cac6b9c][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] As soon as may be after the making of an area of special control order, the authority shall submit it to the Secretary of State for approval, together with— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] two certified copies of the order; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] a full statement of their reasons for making it; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] in the case of an order modifying an existing order, unless the boundaries of the existing area of special control are indicated on the map annexed to the order, a plan showing both these boundaries and the proposed modifications; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (d (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] any additional certified copy of any of the material in subparagraphs (a) to (c) above, which the Secretary of State requires. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-8ca1c06632ba034a55a476ea3e7240ae][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] The authority shall forthwith publish in the London Gazette, and in two successive weeks in at least one newspaper circulating in the locality, a notice in prescribed Form 1. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**5 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-3a0661d75042de3e7553b64533347da7][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] If any objection is made to an order, in the manner and within the time provided for in the prescribed form, the Secretary of State— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] may offer all interested parties an opportunity to make representations to him in writing about any such objection before such date as he may specify; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] may, and at the request of any interested party shall, either provide for a local inquiry to be held or afford to the parties an opportunity of a hearing before a person appointed by him. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**6 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-8e48d29fadd4bd6bca0f9d35cc964f8e][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] After considering any representations or objections duly made and not withdrawn and, where applicable, the report of any person holding an inquiry or hearing, the Secretary of State may, subject to paragraph 7 below, approve the order with or without modifications. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**7 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-45fc52160de3dceef54347b357393e19][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] If the Secretary of State proposes to make a modification for the inclusion of additional land in an order, he shall— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] publish notice of his intention to do so; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] afford an opportunity for the making of objections to, or representations about, the proposed modification; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
- (c (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] if he considers it expedient, provide for a further inquiry or hearing to be held. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**8 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-d57607f5b258f950607b2bba662174ea][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] As soon as may be after the order has been approved, the local planning authority shall publish in the London Gazette, and in two successive weeks in at least one newspaper circulating in the locality, a notice of its approval in prescribed Form 2. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**9 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-baecc0cd085c0db4a4cae71f20190a41][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] An area of special control order shall come into force on the date on which the notice of its approval is published in the London Gazette. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**10 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-1b1d982bbf90ffa7fa377c291be07606][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Where a local planning authority propose to make an order revoking an area of special control order, a map showing the existing area shall be annexed to the order, and the procedure prescribed in paragraphs 2 to 9 of this Schedule in relation to an order modifying an existing order shall be followed, subject to the modification that the prescribed forms of notice under paragraphs 4 and 8 respectively are prescribed Forms 3 and 4. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]

**11 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^c-7f9f47a5fba8f51d0279f23b019f3c41][^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b] Any reference in this Part of this Schedule to a prescribed form is to the form bearing that number in Part II of this Schedule or a form substantially to the like effect. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b8be3ef55c3def2035f5d0777ee2365b]
