# The Design Right (Proceedings before Comptroller) (Amendment) Rules 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 615

DESIGNS

The Design Right (Proceedings before Comptroller) (Amendment) Rules 1992

Made

10th March 1992

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1992

Coming into force

11th May 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 250 of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^f00001], with the consent of the Treasury pursuant to subsection (3) of that section as to the fees prescribed under these Rules and after consultation with the Council on Tribunals in accordance with section 10(1) of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1971[^f00002], hereby makes the following Rules:—

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Design Right (Proceedings before Comptroller) (Amendment) Rules 1992 and shall come into force on 11th May 1992.

**2.** The Design Right (Proceedings before Comptroller) Rules 1989[^f00003] are amended by substituting for Schedule 2 there to the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Rules.

Reay

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

6th March 1992

We consent to the fees prescribed in these Rules.

Irvine Patnick

Nicholas Baker

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

10th March 1992

## SCHEDULE

> **SCHEDULE 2 — FEES**
>
> Rule 24
>
> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | **1.** On reference of dispute (Form 1) under rule 3(1) | £65 |
> | **2.** On application (Form 2) under rule 7(1) | £40 |
> | **3.** On application (Form 3) under rule 10(1) | £65 |
> | **4.** On application (Form 4) under rule 14(1) | £65 |

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules replace Schedule 2 to the Design Right (Proceedings before Comptroller) Rules 1989 to increase, with effect from 11th May 1992, from £60 to £65 the fees for the matters referred to in items 1, 3 and 4, and from £35 to £40 the fee in respect of item 2 in the Schedule.
