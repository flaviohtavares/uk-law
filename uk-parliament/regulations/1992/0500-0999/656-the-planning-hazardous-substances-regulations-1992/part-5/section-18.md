# The Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations 1992, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Appeals against hazardous substances contravention notices

(1) Sections 174, 175(3), (6), 176 and 177[^f00011] of the principal Act shall apply to appeals against hazardous substances contravention notices, subject to the modifications set out in Part 1 of Schedule 4.

(2) The provisions of those sections, as modified under paragraph (1), are set out in Part 5 of Schedule 4.

## Annotations

[^f00011]: Footnote: Section 174 was amended by section 6(1) of, and paragraph 22 of Schedule 7 to, the Planning and Compensation Act [1991 (c. 34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/34). Section 176 was amended by paragraph 23 of that Schedule; and section 177 was amended by section 6(3) of, and paragraph 24 of Schedule 7 to, that Act.
