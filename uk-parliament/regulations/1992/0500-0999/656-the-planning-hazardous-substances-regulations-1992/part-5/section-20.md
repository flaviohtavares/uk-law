# The Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations 1992, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Effect of hazardous substances contravention notices, etc

(1) Sections 178 to 181[^f00012] of the principal Act shall have effect in relation to hazardous substances contravention notices, subject to the modifications set out in Part 2 of Schedule 4.

(2) The provisions of those sections, as modified under paragraph (1), are set out in Part 5 of Schedule 4.

## Annotations

[^f00012]: Footnote: Section 178 was amended by section 7 of, and paragraph 25 of Schedule 7 to, the Planning and Compensation Act [1991 (c. 34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/34); section 179 was substituted by section 8 of that Act; section 180 was substituted by paragraph 26 of Schedule 7 to that Act; and section 181 was amended by paragraph 27 of that Schedule.
