# The Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations 1992, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Validity

(1) Sections 285 and 289[^f00014] of the principal Act shall apply to appeals against hazardous substances contravention notices, subject to the modifications set out in Part 4 of Schedule 4.

(2) Section 25(2) is subject to any order under section 289(4A) of the principal Act, as applied by paragraph (1).

(3) The provisions of sections 285 and 289 of the principal Act, as modified under paragraph (1), are set out in Part 5 of Schedule 4.

## Annotations

[^f00014]: Footnote: Section 285 was amended by paragraph 41 of Schedule 7 to the Planning and Compensation Act 1991; and section 289 was amended by section 6 of that Act.
