# The Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations 1992, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Enforcement register

(1) Section 188[^f00013] of the principal Act (register of enforcement and stop notices) shall have effect in relation to hazardous substances contravention notices, subject to the modifications set out in Part 3 of Schedule 4.

(2) The provisions of that section, as modified under paragraph (1), are set out in Part 5 of Schedule 4.

## Annotations

[^f00013]: Footnote: Section 188 was amended by paragraph 30 of Schedule 7 to the Planning and Compensation Act 1991.
