# The Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 263

SEA FISHERIES

The Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) Order 1992

Made

13th February 1992

Laid before Parliament

14th February 1992

Coming into force

15th February 1992

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretaries of State respectively concerned with sea fishing in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 30(2) and (3) of the Fisheries Act 1981[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 15th February 1992.

## 2. Amendment of the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) Order 1986

The Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) Order 1986[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting in article 2 thereof (interpretation) for the definition of the Council Regulation the following definition—

> - “the Council Regulation” means Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3094/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/3094) laying down certain technical measures for the conservation of fishery resources[^f00003], as amended by Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 4026/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/4026)[^f00004], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2968/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/2968)[^f00005], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3953/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/3953)[^f00006], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1555/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/1555)[^f00007], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2024/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/2024)[^f00008], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3287/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/3287)[^f00009], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 4193/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/4193)[^f00010], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2220/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1989/2220)[^f00011], Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 4056/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1989/4056)[^f00012] and Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3500/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/3500)[^f00013];

## 3. Revocation

The Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) Order 1990[^f00014] is hereby revoked.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) Order 1986 ( “the principal Order”), which makes provision for the enforcement of certain of the enforceable Community restrictions and obligations concerning technical measures for the conservation of fishery resources which are contained in Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3094/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/3094) ( “the Council Regulation”). In consequence of an amendment made to the Council Regulation by Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3500/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/3500), this Order further amends the definition of the Council Regulation in article 2 of the principal Order (interpretation) so as to provide for the inclusion of that amendment in that definition (article 2). The Order revokes the Sea Fishing (Enforcement of Community Conservation Measures) (Amendment) Order 1990 (article 3) and incorporates its provisions in the new definition.
