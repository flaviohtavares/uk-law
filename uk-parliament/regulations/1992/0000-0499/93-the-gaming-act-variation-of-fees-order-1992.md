# The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 93

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 1992

Made

20th January 1992

Laid before Parliament

28th January 1992

Coming into force

1st April 1992

In pursuance of sections 48(5) and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st April 1992.

(2) This Order shall not extend to Scotland.

**2.** The provisions of section 48 of the Gaming Act 1968 specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to fees charged in relation to the matters mentioned in column 2 of that Schedule) shall have effect as if, for the references to the sums specified in those provisions (in respect of which the sums mentioned in column 3 of that Schedule are either the original sums where these have not since been varied or the sums which were last substituted by either of the Orders revoked by this Order), there were substituted references to the sums specified in column 4 of that Schedule.

**3.** The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 1990[^f00002] and the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 1991[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

20th January 1992
