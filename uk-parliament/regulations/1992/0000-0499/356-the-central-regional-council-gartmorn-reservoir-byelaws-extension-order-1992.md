# The Central Regional Council (Gartmorn Reservoir) Byelaws Extension Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 356 (S.30)

WATER SUPPLY, SCOTLAND

The Central Regional Council (Gartmorn Reservoir) Byelaws Extension Order 1992

Made

24th February 1992

Coming into force

29th February 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Central Regional Council (Gartmorn Reservoir) Byelaws Extension Order 1992 and shall come into force on 29th February 1992.

**2.** The period during which the Central Regional Council (Gartmorn Reservoir) Byelaws 1981 are to remain in force[^f00002] is hereby extended to 30th September 1992.

T.D. MacDonald

Deputy Chief Engineer, Scottish Office

Scottish Office Environment Department,

27 Perth Street,

Edinburgh

24th February 1992
