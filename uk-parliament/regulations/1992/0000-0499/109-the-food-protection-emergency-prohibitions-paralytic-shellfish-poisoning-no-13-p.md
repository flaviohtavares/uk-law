# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No. 13 Partial Revocation) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 109 (S.9)

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

PUBLIC HEALTH, SCOTLAND

PUBLIC HEALTH, NORTHERN IRELAND

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No. 13 Partial Revocation) Order 1992

Made

22nd January 1992

Laid before Parliament

23rd January 1992

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(1) and (2) and 24(1) and (3) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No. 13 Partial Revocation) Order 1992 and shall come into force atr 11.00 hours on 22nd January 1992.

## 2. Partial revocation

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No. 13) Order 1991[^f00002] except in so far as it applies to the area described in the Schedule to this order is hereby revoked.

Assistant Secretary, Scottish Office

## SCHEDULE — AREA TO WHICH PROHIBITIONS STILL APPLY

article 2

That area of the sea surrounding the Northern Isles of the Orkney Islands and below the line of the mean high water springs on these Islands bounded as follows: on the north by a straight line extending in an easterly direction from a point at 59° 25'N latitude and 2° 50'W longitude to a point at 59° 25'N latitude and 2° 20'W longitude; then on the east by a straight line extending in a southerly direction from the last mentioned point to a point at 59° 8'N latitude and 2° 20'W longitude; then on the south by a straight line extending in a westerly direction from the last mentioned point to a point at 59° 8'N latitude and 2° 50'W longitude; then on the west by a straight line extending from the last mentioned point to the point at beginning at 59° 25'N latitude and 2° 50'W longitude.

(This not is not part of the Order)

This Order partly revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Pohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No. 13) Order 1991 which imposed a prohibition on the fishing for or taking of (article 4), the movement of (article 5), and the landing of or use in the production of food (article 6) of green crabs and velvet crabs within the area of the sea surrounding all the Orkney Islands as designated in the Schedule to that Order.

This Order removes completely the prohibition on fishing for or taking, landing, or the use in food production of green crabs and velvet crabs from that area described in the Schedule to the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning) (No. 13) Order with the exception of the area described in the Schedule to this Order. Thus the prohibitions continue to apply to green crabs and velvet crabs taken from the area of the sea surrounding Eday, Sanday, North Ronaldsay and the northern part of Stronsay but are removed from green crabs and velvet crabs taken from the area of the sea surrounding the Islands of Papa Westray, Westray, Rousay, Egilsay, Wyre, Gairsay, Shapinsay and the southern part of Stronsay lying to the north of the Mainland; the area of the sea surrounding the Mainland and within the Scapa Flow and the area of sea surrounding the Islands lying to the south of the Mainland such as Hoy, Graemsay, South Walls, Swona, Flotta, South Ronaldsay and Burray.
