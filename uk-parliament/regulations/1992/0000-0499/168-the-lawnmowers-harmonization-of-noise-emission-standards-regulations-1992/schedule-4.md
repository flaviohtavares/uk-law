## SCHEDULE 4 — TABLE OF PERMISSIBLE SOUND POWER LEVELS

Regulation 3(1)

| Cutting width of lawnmower (L) | Permissible sound power level in dB(A)/1 pW |
| --- | --- |
| L ≤ 50cm | 96 |
| 50cm \< L ≤ 120cm | 100 |
