# The West Dorset General Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 389

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLANDAND WALES

The West Dorset General Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1992

Made

26th February 1992

Laid before Parliament

3rd March 1992

Coming into force

1st April 1992

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and facilities formerly managed by West Dorset Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the West Dorset General Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the West Dorset General Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1992 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1992.

(2) In this Order:

- “the Authority” means West Dorset Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the West Dorset General Hospitals National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 21st November 1991, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of Trust Property

On 1st April 1992 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Stephen Dorrell

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

26th February 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the West Dorset General Hospitals National Health Service Trust on 1st April 1992 trust property formerly held by West Dorset Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtainedat the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, Richmond House, 79 Whitehall, London, SW1A 2NS.
