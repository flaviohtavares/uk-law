# The Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 421 (C.11)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1992

Made

2nd March 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 84 of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1992.

## 2. Commencement provisions

The provisions of the Road Traffic Act 1991 specified in the first column of the Schedule to this Order which relate to the matters specified in the second column of that Schedule shall come into force on 1st April 1992 subject to the limitations (if any) specified in the third column of that Schedule.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport.

Christopher Chope

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

2nd March 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 1992 provisions of the Road Traffic Act 1991. The provisions relate to:

- (a) applications for licences to drive hackney carriages and private hire vehicles;
- (b) disabled persons' vehicles; and
- (c) sealing of speed limiters.
