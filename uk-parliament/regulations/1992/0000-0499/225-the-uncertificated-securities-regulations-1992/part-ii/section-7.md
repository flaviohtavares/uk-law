# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Operator’s report

(1) The Operator shall made such reports to the Secretary of State on or in connection with the discharge of its functions under these Regulations as the Secretary of State may require.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (1), the Secretary of State may require that reports deal with specified subjects, or specified periods of time, and may require that they set out details of complaints received by the Operator and of the Operator’s response to complaints.
