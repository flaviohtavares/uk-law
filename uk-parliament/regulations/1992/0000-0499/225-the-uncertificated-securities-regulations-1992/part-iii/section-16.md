# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. The appropriate register

(1) A company shall record the names of holders of units of an uncertificated security of the company on the appropriate register, giving the particulars required by any applicable enactment and the terms of issue of the security (subject to paragraph (2)).

(2) Schedule 2 to these Regulations has effect so as to modify or exclude the provisions of certain provisions of the 1985 Act with respect to the keeping of a register of members and a register of debenture holders.
