# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992, section 83

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 83. Introductory

The provisions of this Part apply:

- (a) where it is proposed to convert an uncertificated security into a certificated security; and
- (b) to require, in certain cases, uncertificated securities to be so converted.
