# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 225

COMPANIES

The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992

Made

11th February 1992

Coming into force in accordance with Regulation I

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 207 of the Companies Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and having consulted the Council on Tribunals under section 10(1) of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1971[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations of which a draft has been laid before Parliament in accordance with section 207(9) of the Companies Act 1989 and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:
