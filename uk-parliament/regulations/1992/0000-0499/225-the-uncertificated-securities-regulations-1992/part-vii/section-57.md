# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Duty of participants acting on behalf of others

It is the duty of any participant making a communication purportedly on behalf of another person by means of a valid communication to do so only with the authority of that person.
