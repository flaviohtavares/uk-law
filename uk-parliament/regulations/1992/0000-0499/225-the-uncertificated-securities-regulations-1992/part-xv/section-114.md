# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992, section 114

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 114. Data protection

Section 190 of the 1986 Act shall apply as if in subsection (a) the reference to a self-regulating organisation included a reference to the Operator, and as if the reference to the functions there mentioned included a reference to functions relating to admission to the system, termination of participation, the imposition of restrictions, and the replacement and supervision of participants.
