# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992, section 110

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 110. Investigations

Sections 105 and 106 of the 1986 Act shall apply with appropriate modifications in relation to participants as it applies in relation to persons carrying on investment business.
