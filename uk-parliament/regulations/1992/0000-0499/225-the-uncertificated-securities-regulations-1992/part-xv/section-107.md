# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992, section 107

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 107. Financial provision by a company to company controllers

It shall be lawful (if it would not otherwise be) for a company or a subsidiary company of it to pay a company controller appointed by it the amount chargeable by the controller as consideration for consenting to the appointment or the continuance of that consent.
