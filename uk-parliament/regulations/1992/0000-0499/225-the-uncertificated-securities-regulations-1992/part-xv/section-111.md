# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992, section 111

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 111. Indemnity rules

Section 53 (other than subsection (2)) of the 1986 Act shall apply with the substitution of a reference to a participant in connection with the performance of his functions under these Regulations for the reference to an authorised person in connection with his investment business.
