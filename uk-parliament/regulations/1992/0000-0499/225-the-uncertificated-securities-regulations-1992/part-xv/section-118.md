# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992, section 118

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 118. Breach of duty not to avoid transactions

The breach of any duty imposed on any person by these Regulations does not, unless otherwise provided, of itself invalidate any transfer of a unit of a security (or the entitlement to it) effected or other act done in contravention of, or as a consequence of the contravention .of, that duty.
