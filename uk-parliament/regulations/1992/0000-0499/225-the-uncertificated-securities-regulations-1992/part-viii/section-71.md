# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. Maintenance of records

Any record required to be maintained by these Regulations may be kept and maintained by the keeper of the record in any form and manner the keeper-thinks fit, provided that it is possible to reproduce a copy of the record in legible form. In particular the record may be updated by its replacement by a new version of the record superseding the existing version.
