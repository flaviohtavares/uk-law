# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Periodic reports

(1) The Complaints Commissioner shall prepare a report on his activities not less often than once a year and submit it to the Secretary of State and the Operator and shall publish it.

(2) In a report under this regulation, the Complaints Commissioner may express views as to any matter relevant to the system which appears to him appropriate in the light of his activities.
