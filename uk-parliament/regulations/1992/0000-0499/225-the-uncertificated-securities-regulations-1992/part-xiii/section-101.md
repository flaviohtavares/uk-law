# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 1992, section 101

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 101. Reports on complaints to be evidence

Section 101(5) of the 1986 Act shall apply to a report by the Complaints Commissioner under regulation 100 as it applies to a report mentioned in that section.
