# The London—Carlisle—Glasgow—Inverness Trunk Road (Cavendish Bridge and Shardlow Diversion) Order 1938 (Revocation) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 181

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The London—Carlisle—Glasgow—Inverness Trunk Road (Cavendish Bridge and Shardlow Diversion) Order 1938 (Revocation) Order 1992

Made

3rd February 1992

Coming into force

14th February 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:—

**1.** The London—Carlisle—Glasgow—Inverness Trunk Road (Cavendish Bridge and Shardlow Diversion) Order 1938[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

**2.** This Order shall come into force on 14th February 1992 and may be cited as the London—Carlisle—Glasgow—Inverness Trunk Road (Cavendish Bridge and Shardlow Diversion) Order 1938 (Revocation) Order 1992.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

D. J. Morrison

Regional Director East Midlands Region

Department of Transport

3rd February 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes an Order made under section 1(3) of the Trunk Roads Act 1936.
