# The Sheriff Court Fees Amendment Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 413 (S. 46)

SHERIFF COURT, SCOTLAND

The Sheriff Court Fees Amendment Order 1992

Made

26th February 1992

Laid before Parliament

10th March 1992

Coming into force

1st April 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2 of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Sheriff Court Fees Amendment Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st April 1992.

**2.** For the Table of Fees in the Schedule to the Sheriff Court Fees Order 1985[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Table of Fees set out in the Schedule to this Order.

**3.** The Sheriff Court Fees Amendment Order 1991[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

25th February 1992

We concur,

Irvine Patnick

Thomas Sackville

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

26th February 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends from 1st April 1992 the Sheriff Court Fees Order 1985 by substi tuting a new Table of Fees in the Schedule to that Order. Most of the fees payable to the sheriff clerk in relation to proceedings in the sheriff court are increased and a new structure for the charging of certain commissary fees is introduced (paragraph 4 of the Schedule).
