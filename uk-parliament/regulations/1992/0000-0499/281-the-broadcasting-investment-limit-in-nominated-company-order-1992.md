# The Broadcasting (Investment Limit in Nominated Company) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 281

BROADCASTING

The Broadcasting (Investment Limit in Nominated Company) Order 1992

Made

18th February 1992

Laid before Parliament

25th February 1992

Coming into force

23rd April 1992

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 137 of the Broadcasting Act 1990[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Broadcasting (Investment Limit in Nominated Company) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 23rd April 1992.

**2.** The target investment limit in relation to the aggregate of the shares for the time being held in National Transcommunications Limited, being the nominated company[^f00002] (which ceased to be wholly owned by the Crown on 24th October 1991), under or by virtue of any enactment, by any Minister of the Crown or any nominee of his, is hereby fixed at nil.

Kenneth Baker

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

18th February 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order sets at nil the target investment limit of the Government shareholding in National Transcommunications Limited, which is the company nominated for the purposes of section 127(1) of the Broadcasting Act 1990.
