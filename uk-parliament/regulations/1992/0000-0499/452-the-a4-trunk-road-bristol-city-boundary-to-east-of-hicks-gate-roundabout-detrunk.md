# The A4 Trunk Road (Bristol City Boundary to East of Hicks Gate Roundabout) (Detrunking) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 452

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The A4 Trunk Road (Bristol City Boundary to East of Hicks Gate Roundabout) (Detrunking) Order 1992

Made

26th February 1992

Coming into force

3rd April 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:—

**1.** The length of the Trunk Road described in the Schedule to this Order and shown by broad striped hatching on the deposited plan shall cease to be a trunk road, and shall be classified as a principal road, as from the date on which this Order comes into force.

**2.** In this Order—

(1) all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway;

(2)

- (i) “the deposited plan” means the plan numbered HA 10/2/SWM72 marked “The A4 Trunk Road (Bristol City Boundary to East of Hicks Gate Roundabout) (Detrunking) Order 1992” signed by authority of the Secretary of State and depo sited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY;
- (ii) “principal road” as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is a principal road for the purposes of enactments and instruments which refer to highways classified as prin cipal roads and is also classified for the purpose of every other enactment and instrument which refers to high ways classified by the Secretary of State:
- (iii) “the Trunk Road” means the A4 Trunk Road.

**3.** This Order shall come into force on 3rd April 1992 and may be cited as the A4 Trunk Road (Bristol City Boundary to East of Hicks Gate Roundabout) (Detrunking) Order 1992.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Elisabeth Hopkins

Regional Director, South West Region,

Department of Transport

26th February 1992

## SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE A TRUNK ROAD

The Trunk Road between the Bristol City Boundary, that is to say, 210 metres west of the centre point of Hicks Gate Roundabout, and a point 300 metres east of the centre point of that roundabout.
