# The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 127 (S.11)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1992

Approved by the house of Commons

Made

16th January 1992

Laid before the House of Commons

16th january 1992

Coming into force

23rd january 1992

Rhe Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Abolition of Domestic Rates Etc. (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with such associations of local authorities as appear to him to be appropriate in accordance with paragraph 2(2) of the said Schedule, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1992 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is approved by a resolutin of the House of Commons.

## 2. Revenue support grants for 1990-91

(1) The local authorities to which revenue support grant is payable in respect of the financial year 1990-91 are specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 to this Order.

(2) The amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 to this Order in respect of the financial year 1990-91 shall be the amount redetermined in relation to that local authority set out in column 2 of that Schedule opposite the name of that local authority.

## 3. Revenue supports grants for 1992-93

(1) The local authorities to which revenue support grant is payable in respect of the financial year 1991-93 are specified in column 1 of Schedule 2 to this Order.

(2) The amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority specified in column 1 of Schedule 2 to this Order in respect of the financial year 1992-93 shall be the amount determined in relation to that local authority set out in column 2 of that Schedule opposite the name of that local authority.

## 4. Revocation

The revenue support Grant (Scotland) (No. 2) Order 1991 (which previously redetermined the amount of revenue support grant payable to each local authority in respect of the financial year 1990-91[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Ian Lang

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

15th January 1992

We consent

This Order determines the amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority specified in Schedule 2 to the Order in respect of the financial year 1992-93.

This Order also redetermines for a second time the amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority specified in Schedule 1 to the Order in respect of the financial year 1990-91.

As a consequence, it revokes the Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) (No. 2) Order 1991 which previously redetermined such amounts.
