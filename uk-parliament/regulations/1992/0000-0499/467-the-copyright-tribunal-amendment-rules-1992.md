# The Copyright Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in S.I. [1991/201](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/201) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1992 No. 467

COPYRIGHT

The Copyright Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 1992

Made

3rd March 1992

Laid before Parliament

6th March 1992

Coming into force

27th March 1992

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 150 of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^f00001], after consultation with the Lord Advocate, hereby makes the following Rules:—

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Copyright Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 1992 and shall come into force on 27th March 1992.

**2.** Rule 50A of the Copyright Tribunal Rules 1989[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Mackay of Clashfern, C.

3rd March 1992

(This note is not part of the Rules)

Rule 50A (notice of intention to exercise right) was inserted in error in the Copyright Tribunal Rules 1989 (S.I. [1989/1129](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1129)) by the Copyright Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 1991 (S.I. [1991/201](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/201)). These Rules revoke it.
