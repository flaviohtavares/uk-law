# The Mental Health (Detention) (Scotland) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 357 (C.10) (S.31)

MENTAL HEALTH

The Mental Health (Detention) (Scotland) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992

Made

20th February 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4(2) of the Mental Health (Detention) (Scotland) Act 1991[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Mental Health (Detention) (Scotland) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992.

**2.** The Mental Health (Detention) (Scotland) Act 1991 shall come into force on 9th March 1992.

Michael B. Forsyth

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

20th February 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Mental Health (Detention) (Scotland) Act 1991 into force on 9th March 1992.
