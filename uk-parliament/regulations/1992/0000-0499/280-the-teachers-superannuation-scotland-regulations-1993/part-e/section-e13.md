# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section E13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E13. Duration of retirement pension

Subject to regulations E14 to E16 and E18, a retirement pension under regulation E6 continues to be payable until the death of the person entitled to it.
