# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section E3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E3. Nature of retiring allowances

Retiring allowances consist of–

- (a) a retirement pension; and
- (b) a retirement lump sum,

calculated in accordance with this Part.
