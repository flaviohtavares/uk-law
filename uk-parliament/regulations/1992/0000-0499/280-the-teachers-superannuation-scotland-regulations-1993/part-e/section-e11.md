# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section E11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E11. Modification for national insurance

Part I of Schedule 9 has effect for modifying the retirement pensions of teachers who were in pensionable employment before 1st April 1980.
