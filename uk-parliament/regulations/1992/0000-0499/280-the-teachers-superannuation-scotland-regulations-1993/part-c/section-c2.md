# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section C2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## C2. Ordinary contributions

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a teacher who is in pensionable employment is to pay contributions of 6 per cent of contributable salary for the time being.

(2) No contributions are to be paid by a teacher in respect of any reckonable service beyond a total of 45 years.
