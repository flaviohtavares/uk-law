# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section H6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## H6. Determination of questions

All questions arising under these Regulations are to be determined by the Secretary of State and a determination by him is final.
