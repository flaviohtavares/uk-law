# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section B3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## B3. Continuing employment

Subject to regulations B5 to B7, a teacher who immediately before the date on which these Regulations come into force was in employment (“the first employment ”) which was reckonable service by virtue of regulation 4(2)(c) or (d) of the 1977 Regulations[^f00007] shall be treated as being in pensionable employment–

- (a) while in the first employment; and
- (b) while in employment which is in continuation of the first employment.

## Annotations
