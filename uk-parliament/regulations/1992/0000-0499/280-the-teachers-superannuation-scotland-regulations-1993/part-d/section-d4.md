# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section D4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## D4. Current added years for which additional contributions have been paid

A teacher who has paid additional contributions for a period in accordance with regulation C8 is entitled to count as reckonable service the period in respect of which they were paid.
