# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1993, section A1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## A1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1992.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 31st March 1992.
