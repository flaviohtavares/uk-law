# The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulations 1992, section 13

> Text as at 2002-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 13 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note). Duty of the applicant after applying for consent (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-15d03e991f62539ba4fb62a1df82ea26][^c-b1cc857da3ec6d872a0dea97a1b3f599][^c-b1cc857da3ec6d872a0dea97a1b3f599]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b1cc857da3ec6d872a0dea97a1b3f599] In section 111 of *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1cc857da3ec6d872a0dea97a1b3f599] the Act *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1cc857da3ec6d872a0dea97a1b3f599] (Consents required by certain persons), after subsection (6) there shall be inserted the following subsection— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1cc857da3ec6d872a0dea97a1b3f599]

> (6A (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b1cc857da3ec6d872a0dea97a1b3f599] Where an applicant for consent for releasing or marketing genetically modified organisms becomes aware, before his application is either granted or rejected, of any new information with regard to any risks there are of damage to the environment being caused as a result of the organisms being released or marketed, he shall notify the Secretary of State of that new information forthwith. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1cc857da3ec6d872a0dea97a1b3f599]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b1cc857da3ec6d872a0dea97a1b3f599] In section 118(1)(e) of *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1cc857da3ec6d872a0dea97a1b3f599] the Act *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1cc857da3ec6d872a0dea97a1b3f599] (Offences), after the words “section 108(5) or (6)” there shall be inserted the words “or section 111(6A)”. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1cc857da3ec6d872a0dea97a1b3f599]
