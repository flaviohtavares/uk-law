# The Gaming (Bingo) Act (Variation of Monetary Limit) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 3076

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming (Bingo) Act (Variation of Monetary Limit) Order 1992

Made

8th December 1992

Laid before Parliament

14th December 1992

Coming into force

11th January 1993

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 2(2)(b) of the Gaming (Bingo) Act 1985[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Bingo) Act (Variation of Monetary Limit) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 11th January 1993.

**2.** The sum of £75,000 is hereby specified as the maximum amount to be paid as a prize in respect of a game of multiple bingo.

Kenneth Clarke

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

8th December 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that the maximum amount to be paid as a prize in respect of a game of multiple bingo shall be £75,000. It thereby replaces the limit of £50,000 which is provided for by section 2(2)(b) of the Gaming (Bingo) Act 1985.
