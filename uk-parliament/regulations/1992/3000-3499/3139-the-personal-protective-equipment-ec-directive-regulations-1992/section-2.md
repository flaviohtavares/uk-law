# The Personal Protective Equipment (EC Directive) Regulations 1992, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “the Directive” means Council Directive [89/686/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0686) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to personal protective equipment[^f00003], a copy of which is printed in the Schedule to these Regulations;

**2.** They provide for the application of the powers to serve prohibition notices, notices to warn, suspension notices and forfeiture orders under the Consumer Protection Act 1987 and for enforcement by weights and measures authorities in Great Britain and district councils in Northern Ireland.
