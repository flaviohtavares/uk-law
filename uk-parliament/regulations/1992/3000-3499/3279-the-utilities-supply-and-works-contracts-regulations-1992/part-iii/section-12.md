# The Utilities Supply and Works Contracts Regulations 1992, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. The Open, Restricted and Negotiated Procedures

For the purposes of seeking offers in relation to a proposed contract a utility shall use the open, the restricted or the negotiated procedure.
