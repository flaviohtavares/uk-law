# The Utilities Supply and Works Contracts Regulations 1992, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Utilities

For the purposes of these Regulations a utility is a person specified in the first column of Schedule 1.
