# The Utilities Supply and Works Contracts Regulations 1992, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Sub-contracting

A utility may require a supplier or contractor to indicate in his tender what part of the contract if any he intends to sub-contract to another person.
