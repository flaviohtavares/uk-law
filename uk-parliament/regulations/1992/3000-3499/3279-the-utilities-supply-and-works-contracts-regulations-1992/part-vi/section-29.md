# The Utilities Supply and Works Contracts Regulations 1992, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Confidentiality of Information

A utility which makes information available to a supplier or contractor pursuant to these Regulations may impose requirements on him for the purpose of protecting the confidentiality of that information.
