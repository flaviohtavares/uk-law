# The Utilities Supply and Works Contracts Regulations 1992, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Contract award notice

(1) A utility which has awarded a supply or a works contract (other than one excluded from the application of these Regulations by regulations 6 to 9 above) shall no later than two months after the award, send to the Official Journal a notice, substantially corresponding to the form set out in Part F of Schedule 4 and including the information therein specified.

(2) The utility may indicate that any of the information included in paragraphs 6 and 9 of the notice is of a sensitive commercial nature, and request that it not be published.
