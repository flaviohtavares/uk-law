# The Combined Probation Areas (West Yorkshire) (Amendment) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 3268

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (West Yorkshire) (Amendment) Order 1992

Made

21st December 1992

Coming into force

1st January 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (West Yorkshire) (Amendment) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st January 1993.

**2.** In article 2(b) of the Combined Probation Areas (West Yorkshire) Order 1992[^f00002], for ““Brighouse””there shall be substituted ““Bradford””.

Kenneth Clarke

One Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

21st December 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends article 2(b) of the Combined Probation Areas (West Yorkshire) Order 1992 to substitute “Bradford” for “Brighouse”.
