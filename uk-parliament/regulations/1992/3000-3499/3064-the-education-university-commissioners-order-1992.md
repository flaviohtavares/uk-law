# The Education (University Commissioners) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 3064

UNIVERSITIES AND COLLEGES

The Education (University Commissioners) Order 1992

Made

7th December 1992

Laid before Parliament

8th December 1992

Coming into force

30th December 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 3(2)(b) of Schedule 11 to the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (University Commissioners) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 30th December 1992.

## 2. Continuation of duties and powers

The University Commissioners' duties and powers shall continue for the period from 31st December 1992 until 1st January 1994.

John Patten

Secretary of State for Education

7th December 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order continues the University Commissioners' powers and duties until 1st January 1994. By virtue of paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 11 to the Education Reform Act 1988, their powers and duties would have ceased on 29th July 1991, but they were extended until 31st December 1992 by the Education (University Commissioners) Order 1991 (S.I. [1991/1427](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1427)).
