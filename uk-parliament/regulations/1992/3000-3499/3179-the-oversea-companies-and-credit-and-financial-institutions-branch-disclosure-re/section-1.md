# The Oversea Companies and Credit and Financial Institutions (Branch Disclosure) Regulations 1992, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation etc.

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Oversea Companies and Credit and Financial Institutions (Branch Disclosure) Regulations 1992.

(2) In these Regulations, “the principal Act” means the Companies Act 1985[^f00003] and “the 1989 Act” means the Companies Act 1989[^f00004].

(3) These Regulations shall come into force on 1st January 1993.

(4) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

## 1. These Regulations implement, through a number of amendments to the Companies Act 1985 (“the Act”), Council Directive 89/666/89 (the “Eleventh Company Law Directive”) on the disclosure requirements in respect of branches opened by certain companies in a Member State (OJ No. L395 30.12.89, pp.36–39) and Council Directive [89/117/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0117) (the “Bank Branches Directive”) on publication of annual accounting documents by credit and financial institutions (OJ No. L44 16.2.89, pp.40–42).
