# The Oversea Companies and Credit and Financial Institutions (Branch Disclosure) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 3179

COMPANIES

BANKS AND BANKING

The Oversea Companies and Credit and Financial Institutions (Branch Disclosure) Regulations 1992

Made

13th December 1992

Coming into force

1st January 1993

Whereas in accordance with paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 2 to the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] a draft of these Regulations has been approved by resolution of each House of Parliament.

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, being a Minister designated[^f00002] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to measures

- (i) relating to the publication of documents, particulars and other items relating to a branch opened by a company governed by the law of another Member State of the European Economic Community or of a State which is not such a Member State, and
- (ii) relating to credit and financial institutions

in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that section and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:
