# The School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 3070 (C.95)

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1992

Made

3rd December 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(5) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1992.

**2.** Section 2(7) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 shall come into force on 4th December 1992.

John Patten

Secretary of State for Education

3rd December 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

The School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1991 (S.I. [1991/1874](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1874)) brought into force sections 1, 4, 5 and 6(1), (2) (4) and (5) of and Schedule 1 to the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991.

The School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provision) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/532](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/532)) brought into force sections 2(1) to (6) and (8), 3 and 6(3) of and Schedule 2 to the 1991 Act.

The School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/988)) brought into force section 2(9) of the 1991 Act.

This Order brings into force on 4th December 1992 the remaining provision of the 1991 Act, section 2(7).
