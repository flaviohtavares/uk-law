# The Durham, Northumberland and Tyne and Wear (County and District Boundaries) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under sections 51(2) and 67(4) of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order which gives effect to proposals submitted to him under sections 47, 48(1) and 51(1) of that Act, more than six weeks before the making of this Order, by the Local Government Boundary Commission for England:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Durham, Northumberland and Tyne and Wear (County and District Boundaries) Order 1992.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 1st December 1993 for any purpose set out in regulation 4(1) of the Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976[^f00002].

(3) For all other purposes, this Order shall come into force on 1st April 1994.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “district” includes a borough, a metropolitan borough and a city;
- “Durham” means the county of Durham, and any reference to Chester-le-Street or to Derwentside is a reference to the Durham district of that name;
- “electoral division” means an electoral division of the county to which an area is transferred by this Order[^f00003];
- “the map” means one or more of the 13 maps prepared by the Department of the Environment, marked “Maps of the Durham, Northumberland and Tyne and Wear (County and District Boundaries) Order 1992”, duly numbered for reference, and Government Area Changes Regulations 1976;
- “Northumberland” means the county of Northumberland, and any reference to Castle Morpeth or to Tynedale is a reference to the Northumberland district of that name;
- “Tyne and Wear” means the metropolitan county of Tyne and Wear, and any reference to Gateshead or to Newcastle Upon Tyne is a reference to the Tyne and Wear district of that name;
- any reference to an area as unparished is a reference to that area not forming part of any parish; and
- except in article 7, “ward” means a ward of the district from or to which an area is transferred[^f00004].

## 7. Consequential change to parish boundaries in Northumberland (Castle Morpeth)

(1) In this article, “ward” means a ward of Castle Morpeth.

(2) In Castle Morpeth, the area marked C on map 1 shall be transferred from the parish of Heddon-on-the-Wall to the parish of Ponteland, to the Ponteland South ward, and to the Ponteland East electoral division.

## 8. Town and country planning

(1) In this article, “provision” means a provision of a development plan (as defined in section 336(1) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00005]).

(2) Any provision applying to an area transferred by this Order shall continue to apply to that area, notwithstanding the transfer, until replaced by a provision which so applies, and which is expressed to supersede the preserved provision.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes mainly minor changes to the boundary of the metropolitan county of Tyne and Wear with the neighbouring counties of Durham and Northumberland. The districts affected are as follows—

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| ## In Tyne and Wear<br>The metropolitan borough of Gateshead<br>The city of Newcastle upon Tyne |  |
| ## In Durham<br>The district of Derwentside<br>The district of Chester-le-Street | ## In Northumberland<br>The borough of Castle Morpeth<br>The district of Tynedale |

The Order also transfers areas within Tyne and Wear between Gateshead and Newcastle upon Tyne, and makes a small change to parish boundaries in Northumberland. Consequential changes are made to parishes, local government electoral areas, petty sessional divisions and coroners' districts.

The map and table forming part of this Note (pages 7, 8 and 9) give a general guide to the location, area and population of the land affected. Prints of the detailed order maps (described in article 2) may be inspected at all reasonable times at the offices of the council for each district affected, and additionally at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment located in 2, Marsham Street, London SW1P 3EB.
