# The Merchant Shipping (Conformation of Legislation) (Virgin Islands) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 3201

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Merchant Shipping (Conformation of Legislation) (Virgin Islands) Order 1992

Made

17th December 1992

Laid before Parliament

6th January 1993

Coming into force

28th January 1993

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 17th day of December 1992

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 735(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894[^f00001], is pleased by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** — This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Conformation of Legislation) (Virgin Islands) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 28th January 1993.

**2.** — The Registrar of Ships (Designation) Act 1992[^f00002] enacted by the Legislature of the Virgin Islands, is hereby confirmed.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

This Order, made under section 735(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894, confirms an Act of 1992 enacted by the Legislature of the Virgin Islands which confers power on the Governor to designate a public officer as the Registrar of Ships and validates the appointment of the Ports Manager as the Registrar of Ships from 9th October 1985 to the appointed day. The Act repeals section 4(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894 as it applies in the Virgin Islands.
