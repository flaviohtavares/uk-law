# The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variation) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 3049

LIBRARIES

The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variation) Order 1992

Made

3rd December 1992

Laid before Parliament

9th December 1992

Coming into force

30th December 1992

Whereas the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982[^f00001] (“the Scheme”) was brought into force on 14 June 1982;

Whereas the Scheme has been varied[^f00002];

And whereas the Secretary of State has, after consultation with the representatives of authors and library authorities and of others who appear likely to be affected, further varied the Scheme;

Now therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(7) of the Public Lending Right Act 1979[^f00003] and now vested in him[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:–

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variation) Order 1992.

**2.** The variation made by the Secretary of State on 3rd December 1992 to Article 46 of the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 whereby paragraph (1)(a) was varied by substituting “1.86p” for “1.81p” shall come into force on 30th December 1992.

Peter Brooke

Secretary of State for National Heritage

3rd December 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 30th December 1992 a variation to the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 so that the sum attributable to each qualifying loan for the purposes of calculating the amount payable in respect of loans of a particular book is increased from 1.81p to 1.86p.
