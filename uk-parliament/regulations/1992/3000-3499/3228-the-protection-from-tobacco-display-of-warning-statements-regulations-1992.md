# The Protection from Tobacco (Display of Warning Statements) Regulations 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 3228

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Protection from Tobacco (Display of Warning Statements) Regulations 1992

Made

17th December 1992

Laid before Parliament

23rd December 1992

Coming into force

20th February 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 4(3) of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling me in that behalf, I hereby make the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Protection from Tobacco (Display of Warning Statements) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 20th February 1993.

## 2. Dimensions of notice exhibited at retail premises and statement

The dimensions of the notice to be exhibited in accordance with section 4(1) of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991 (display of warning statements in retail premises) shall be not less than 297 millimetres by 420 millimetres and the size of the statement to be displayed on the notice shall be such that no character is less than 36 millimetres high.

## 3. Dimensions of notice exhibited on vending machines and statement

The dimensions of the notice to be exhibited in accordance with section 4(2) of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991 (display of warning statements on vending machines) shall be not less than 60 millimetres by 100 millimetres and the size of the statement to be displayed on the notice shall be such that no character is less than 6 millimetres high.

Virginia Bottomley

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

17th December 1992

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe—

- (a) the dimensions of the notice displaying a warning statement which must be exhibited in premises at which tobacco is sold by retail, and on vending machines for the sale of tobacco, and
- (b) the size of the statement which must be displayed on such a notice.
