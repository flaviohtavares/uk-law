# The Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991 (Commencement No.3) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 3227 (C.99)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991 (Commencement No.3) Order 1992

Made

16th December 1992

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 8(2) of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991 (Commencement No.3) Order 1992.

## 2. Appointed days

(1) 17th December 1992 is the day appointed for the coming into force of subsections (3) and (9) of section 4 of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991.

(2) 20th February 1993 is the day appointed for the coming into force of subsections (1), (2), (4), (5), (6), (7) and (8) of section 4 of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991.

Virginia Bottomley

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

16th December 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force

- (i) on 17th December 1992 subsections (3)

  and (9) of section 4 of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991 and;
- (ii) on 20th February 1993 the remaining provisions of section 4 of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991.

This Order concludes the bringing into force of the Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991.
