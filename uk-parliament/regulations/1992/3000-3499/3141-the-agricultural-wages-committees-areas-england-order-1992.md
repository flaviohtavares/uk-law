# The Agricultural Wages Committees (Areas) (England) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 3141

AGRICULTURAL EMPLOYMENT

AGRICULTURAL WAGES

The Agricultural Wages Committees (Areas) (England) Order 1992

Made

10th December 1992

Coming into force

1st January 1993

Whereas there have been passed, in the case of each of the committees for the individual counties and existing combinations of counties referred to in article 2 hereof, resolutions under the provisions of proviso (b) in section 2(1) of the Agricultural Wages Act 1948[^f00001].

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(1) and 18(b) of the said Act and all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following order:—

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Agricultural Wages Committees (Areas) (England) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st January 1993.

## 2. Establishment of new agricultural wages committees

Instead of the agricultural wages committees for each of the counties set out in Part I of the Schedule to this Order, and for each combination of counties set out in Part II of that Schedule, there shall be established an agricultural wages committee for each of the eight combinations set out in Part III of that Schedule.

## 3. Greater London

For the purposes of the Agricultural Wages Act 1948 the administrative area of Greater London shall be deemed to be included in the County of Surrey.

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 10th December 1992

John Selwyn Gummer

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

10th December 1992.
