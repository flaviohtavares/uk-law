# The Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in Welsh) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made to amend [S.I. 1992/1698](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1698) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1992 No. 3140

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in Welsh) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1992

Made

3rd December 1992

Laid before Parliament

16th December 1992

Coming into force

6th January 1993

Whereas the Secretary of State has given notice of the proposal to make an Order to the Curriculum Council for Wales and to all other persons with whom consultation appeared to him desirable, in accordance with section 21(2) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001];

And whereas the Secretary of State in accordance with subsection (3) of the said section 21, duly published a draft of this Order and other documents mentioned in that subsection and sent copies of them to the said Council and to each of the persons consulted by him, and allowed a period of not less than one month for the submission of evidence and representations;

And whereas that period has now expired.

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 4(2)(a) and (b) and (4) of the Education Reform Act 1988, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order in the terms of the said draft:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in Welsh) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 6th January 1993.

**2.** In relation to the Sir Thomas Picton School, to the extent to which the Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in Welsh) Order 1990[^f00002] came into force on 1st August 1992 in pursuance of article 10A and Schedule 3, Part I thereof (Schools in the County of Dyfed) that Order—

- (a) shall cease to be in force, and
- (b) accordingly, shall be of no effect until again brought into force in pursuance of article 10A thereof and Part II of Schedule 3 as amended by the Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in Welsh) (Amendment) Order 1992[^f00003].

David Hunt

Secretary of State for Wales

3rd December 1992

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in Welsh) (Amendment) Order 1992 ([S.I. 1992/1698](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1698)) made minor amendments to Schedule 3 and 4 to the Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in Welsh) Order 1990 ([S.I. 1990/1082](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1082) as subsequently amended). Under the amendment Order Sir Thomas Picton School in the County of Dyfed was moved from the provisions of Part I to Part II of Schedule 3 so as to postpone the coming into force of the provisions of the principal Order in relation to the third key stage for the school by a further two years.

The present Order disapplies provisions in the 1990 Order which brought into force the third key stage for the school on 1st August 1992 and, consequently, the provision shall be brought into force on 1st August 1994.
