# The Export of Goods (Control) Order 1992

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1992 No. 3092

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Export of Goods (Control) Order 1992

Made

8th December 1992

Coming into force

31st December 1992

Whereas under international arrangements, for reasons of public security and to limit the proliferation of weapons, certain goods including nuclear goods and goods capable of both military and civil use (“dual use goods”) have been specified as goods which should be subject to export controls, and whereas technical experts from Member States of the European Communities have prepared a list of certain such nuclear and dual use goods, which list is incorporated in Groups 2 and 3 of Part III of Schedule 1 hereto:

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:
