# The Buckinghamshire Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 785

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Buckinghamshire Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2006

Made

14th March 2006

Coming into force

1st April 2006

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and by paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002].

The Secretary of State for Health considers it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order, having completed the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Buckinghamshire Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st April 2006.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

The Buckinghamshire Mental Health National Health Service Trust established by The Buckinghamshire Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2001[^f00004] is dissolved, and accordingly that Order is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Caroline Flint

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

14th March 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st April 2006 of the Buckinghamshire Mental Health National Health Service Trust established on 19thFebruary 2001.
