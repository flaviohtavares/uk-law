# The Gaming Act 1968 (Variation of Fees) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 541

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Act 1968 (Variation of Fees) Order 2006

Made

1st March 2006

Laid before Parliament

2nd March 2006

Coming into force

1st April 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 48(5) and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming Act 1968 (Variation of Fees) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st April 2006.

(2) This Order extends to Great Britain.

## 2. Amendment of section 48 of the Gaming Act 1968

(1) This article applies to the provisions of section 48 of the Gaming Act 1968 specified in column 1 (which relate to fees charged in relation to those matters mentioned in column 2).

(2) Each of the provisions shall have effect as if for any reference in the provision to a column 3 sum there were substituted a reference to the sum specified in the associated entry in column 4.

(3) In paragraph (2), a “column 3 sum”, in relation to a provision, is a sum—

- (a) specified in column 3, and
- (b) which is referred to in that provision, but as that reference has effect by virtue of the relevant Gaming Act 1968 (Variation of Fees) Order.

(4) In paragraph (3), the reference to the relevant Gaming Act 1968 (Variation of Fees) Order is—

- (a) in the case of section 48(3)(f), to the Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 2004[^f00002]; and
- (b) in the case of the other provisions, to the Gaming Act 1968 (Variation of Fees) Order 2005[^f00003].

(5) In this article a reference to a numbered column is to the column so numbered in the table in the Schedule.

## 3. Revocation

The Gaming Act (Variation of Fees) Order 2004 (for all remaining purposes[^f00004]) and the Gaming Act 1968 (Variation of Fees) Order 2005 are revoked.

Richard Caborn

Minister of State

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

1st March 2006
