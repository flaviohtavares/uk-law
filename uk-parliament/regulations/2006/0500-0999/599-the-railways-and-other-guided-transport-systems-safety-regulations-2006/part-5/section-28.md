# The Railways and Other Guided Transport Systems (Safety) Regulations 2006, section 28

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Offences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A failure to discharge a duty placed on the Office of Rail and Road[^key-f6f6733009452cb8f09f4d96fbf25aec] by these Regulations shall not be an offence under section 33(1)(c) of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974.
