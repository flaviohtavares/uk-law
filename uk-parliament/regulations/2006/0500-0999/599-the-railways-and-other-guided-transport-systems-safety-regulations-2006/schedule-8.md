## SCHEDULE 8 — Format for safety certificates and applications[^key-59c72af0240a50edbd3d10e5343f3412]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 9A

(This Schedule substantially reproduces the provisions of Commission Regulation [(EC) No 653/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/653) on the use of a common European format for safety certificates and application documents in accordance with Article 10 of Directive [2004/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/49) of the European Parliament and of the Council and on the validity of safety certificates delivered under Directive [2001/14/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2001/14), with amendments for the purpose of addressing deficiencies arising out of the UK's withdrawal from the EU.)

### PART 1

**1.** Where Part A of a safety certificate is to be issued, renewed, updated, amended or revoked by the Office of Rail and Road, it must be in the form provided in Part 2.

**2.** Where Part B of a safety certificate is to be issued, renewed, updated, amended or revoked by the Office of Rail and Road, it must be in the form provided in Part 3

**3.** An application for a new, updated, amended or renewed—

- (a) Part A of a safety certificate;
- (b) Part B of a safety certificate; or
- (c) Part A of a safety certificate and Part B of a safety certificate where a combined application is made,

must be made in the form provided in Part 4.

### PART 2 — Part A of a safety certificate
