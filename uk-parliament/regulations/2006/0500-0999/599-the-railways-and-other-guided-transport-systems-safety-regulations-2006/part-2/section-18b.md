## 18B. Common safety targets[^c-694395ca3fb65cfd83a953a000a9d944]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Office of Rail and Road must assess the achievement of common safety targets in accordance with Schedule 11 (common safety targets), which has effect.
