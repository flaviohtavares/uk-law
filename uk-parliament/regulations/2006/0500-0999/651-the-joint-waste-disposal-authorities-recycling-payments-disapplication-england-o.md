# The Joint Waste Disposal Authorities (Recycling Payments) (Disapplication) (England) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 651

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION, ENGLAND

The Joint Waste Disposal Authorities (Recycling Payments) (Disapplication) (England) Order 2006

Made

7th March 2006

Laid before Parliament

10th March 2006

Coming into force

1st April 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 52(1A) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Joint Waste Disposal Authorities (Recycling Payments) (Disapplication) (England) Order 2006.

(2) This Order comes into force on 1st April 2006.

(3) This Order applies in relation to England only.

## 2. Provision to disapply section 52(1) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990

Subsection (1) of section 52 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 (payments for recycling and disposal etc of waste) is disapplied in relation to an authority established under regulation 2(1) of the Waste Regulation and Disposal (Authorities) Order 1985[^f00002] and named in Schedule 1 to that Order.

Ben Bradshaw

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

7th March 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order disapplies the requirement in section 52(1) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 for joint waste disposal authorities established under the Waste Regulation and Disposal (Authorities) Order 1985 to make payments to a waste collection authority for the waste the collection authority retains and recycles in the waste disposal authority’s area.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
