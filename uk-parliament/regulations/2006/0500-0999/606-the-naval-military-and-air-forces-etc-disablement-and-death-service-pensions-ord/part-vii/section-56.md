# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006, section 56

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Abatement of awards in respect of social security benefits[^c2154999]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a pension is awarded to or in respect of a person for any past period for which benefit under an Act referred to in paragraph (3) has also been paid to or in respect of that person (“**the relevant period**”), the amount of pension awarded may be abated by an amount calculated in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) The amount referred to in paragraph (1) is the amount by which the amount of benefit paid during the relevant period exceeds the amount of benefit which would have been payable if the pension had been paid at the same time as the benefit.

(3) The Acts referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) the National Insurance Act 1965[^c1543938];
- (b) any legislation in Northern Ireland corresponding to that Act;
- (c) Parts II to VII of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^c1543939];
- (d) the corresponding provisions of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^c1543940];
- (e) Part I of the Jobseekers Act 1995[^c1543941]
- (f)[^c2154856] Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007.
- (g)[^c2155001] the corresponding provisions of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007.
- (h)[^c2186734] Part 4 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012;[^c2203596]

*Extent: N.I.*

- (ha)[^key-812b50b84272e21b45182e7475d99060] the corresponding provisions of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015;
- (i) Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012; *(Extent: N.I.)*[^key-92d09aca208a382a600a5c93a6568936]

*Extent: N.I.*

- (j)[^key-3e48ba822903ed15294e67cc0c672fc2] the corresponding provisions of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015.
- (k)[^key-c732fdea92d98f6151c8a8a13e53a3fb] Part 2 of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018.
