# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006, section 54

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Chelsea pensioners

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a member of the armed forces is, in the case of members of the Naval or Air Forces by virtue of previous service in the military forces, admitted to Chelsea Hospital as an in-pensioner, his pension shall cease but may be restored in the event of his leaving that Hospital.
