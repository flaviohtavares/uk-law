# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006, section 60

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Failure to draw pension

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a person fails to draw his pension for a continuous period of not less than 12 months the award shall be cancelled and payment of any arrears shall be withheld.

(2) The Secretary of State may, in any particular case, make such further award as may be appropriate, and may pay the arrears in whole or in part.
