# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006, section 11

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Clothing allowance

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A member of the armed forces who is in receipt of retired pay or a pension may be awarded a clothing allowance at the rate specified in paragraph 4 of Part IV of Schedule 1, where either—

- (a) he is in receipt of retired pay or pension in respect of an amputation and regularly wears an artificial limb; or
- (b) the Secretary of State is satisfied that as a result of the disablement which gives rise to an award under this Order there is exceptional wear and tear of the member's clothing.
