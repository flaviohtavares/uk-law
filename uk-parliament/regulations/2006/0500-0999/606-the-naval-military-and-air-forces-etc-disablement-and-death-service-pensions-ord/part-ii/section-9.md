# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006, section 9

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Exceptionally severe disablement allowance

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the disablement of a member of the armed forces is, and in the opinion of the Secretary of State is likely permanently to remain, such that he is, or if he were not in a hospital or other institution would be, in receipt of an allowance for constant attendance at a rate payable under article 8(4) or (5), he shall be awarded an allowance at the appropriate rate specified in paragraph 2 of Part IV of Schedule 1.
