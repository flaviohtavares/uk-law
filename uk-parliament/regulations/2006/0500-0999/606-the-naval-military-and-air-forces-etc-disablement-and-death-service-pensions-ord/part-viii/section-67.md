# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006, section 67

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Invalidity of assignments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any assignment or charge, and any agreement to assign or charge any pension, retired pay, allowance, gratuity or lump sum payment[^key-32edbd21f0d8f90f66c54673a3a51139] which may be or has been awarded under this Order shall be void, and, on the bankruptcy of any person to whom such a payment has been awarded, the payment shall not pass to any trustee or person acting on behalf of the creditors.
