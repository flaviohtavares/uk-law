# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006, section 70

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Transferred powers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing contained in this Order shall prejudice or affect any power transferred to the Secretary of State [^c1543948]under section 1 of the Pensions (Navy, Army, Air Force and Mercantile Marine) Act 1939 [^c1543949].
