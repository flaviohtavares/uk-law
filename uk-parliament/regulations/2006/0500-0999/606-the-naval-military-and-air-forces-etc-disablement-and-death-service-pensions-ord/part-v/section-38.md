# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006, section 38

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Interpretation of Part V

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- (a) “**pension**” includes any retired pay, pension, allowance, other continuing benefit or lump sum payment[^key-44de11bf0a4b5db7209af36011219158] under this Order; and
- (b) “**gratuity**” includes any gratuity under this Order.
