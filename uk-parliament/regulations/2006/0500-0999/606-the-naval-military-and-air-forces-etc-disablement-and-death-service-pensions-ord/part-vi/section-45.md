# The Naval, Military and Air Forces Etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006, section 45

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Interpretation of Part VI

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In articles 48 and 49—

- (a) “**pension**” includes any retired pay, pension, allowance, grant, other continuing benefit or lump sum payment[^c-7602db8ad6ef95cc7f898f263012d0da] payable under this Order or a 1919 to 1921 instrument; and
- (b) “**gratuity**” includes any gratuity payable under any of the instruments referred to in sub-paragraph (a).

(2) In articles 47 and 50—

- (a) “**pension**” includes any retired pay, pension, allowance, other continuing benefit or lump sum payment[^c-3e316947879b9fccda03354881cd3557] payable under this Order; and
- (b) “**gratuity**” means any gratuity payable under this Order.
