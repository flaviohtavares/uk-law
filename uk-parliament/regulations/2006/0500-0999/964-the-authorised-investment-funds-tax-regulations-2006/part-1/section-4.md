> Text as at 2021-12-09.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Definition of “open-ended investment company”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations “**open-ended investment company**” means a company incorporated in the United Kingdom to which section 236 of FISMA 2000 [^c15458861] applies.
