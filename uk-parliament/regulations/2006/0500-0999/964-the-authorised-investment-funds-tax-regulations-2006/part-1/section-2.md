# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 2

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Structure of these Regulations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The structure of these Regulations is as follows—

- this Part contains preliminary provisions and provides for interpretation;
- Part 1A deals with the genuine diversity of ownership condition;[^key-bc808edc3267496041aa22c79103ca99]
- Part 2 deals with the tax treatment of authorised investment funds;
- Part 2A deals with qualified investor schemes[^key-550b0074ccd3c6988b9b1d2148fef2c8]
- Part 2B deals with diversely owned AIFs;[^key-4659e303888337251a5e62473073dfcd]
- Part 3 deals with distributions made by authorised investment funds;
- Part 4 deals with the treatment of participants in authorised investment funds;
- Part 4A deals with Property AIFs;[^key-f4af7836d88501fe30a7a72c2f1ddaa5]
- Part 4B deals with Tax Elected Funds;[^key-08a4b9c711a4c4d38a005dacdbc50828]
- Part 5 deals with compliance;
- Part 6 contains further provisions relating to authorised investment funds;
- Part 6A contains provisions relating to Funds Investing in Non-Reporting Offshore Funds ([^key-abc4b39c110eb51343d83aecc32e233a]FINROFs);
- Part 7 contains consequential amendments and modifications of enactments; and
- Part 8 contains final provisions.
