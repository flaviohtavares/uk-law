# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 3

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Definition of “authorised investment funds”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations “**authorised investment funds**” means—

- (a) open-ended investment companies, and
- (b) authorised unit trust schemes.
