# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Z54

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Z54. Effects of entry[^key-99ec40eae4e63472000e98d5a2a781e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On entry a new distribution period of the authorised investment fund shall begin.
