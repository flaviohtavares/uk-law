# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Z55

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Z55. Duration[^c-99ec40eae4e63472000e98d5a2a781e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Once this Part has begun to apply to an authorised investment fund it shall continue to apply unless and until it ceases to apply in accordance with Chapter 7 of this Part.
