# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Z70

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Z70. Termination by election: authorised investment fund[^c-99ec40eae4e63472000e98d5a2a781e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if a Tax Elected Fund gives a notice under this regulation electing that this Part is to cease to apply to the fund at the end of a specified accounting period.

(2) This Part shall cease to apply to the fund at the end of that accounting period.

(3) A notice under paragraph (1) must—

- (a) be given in writing to the Commissioners,
- (b) be given before the end of the accounting period specified in paragraph (1), and
- (c) give the reasons for the fund leaving the TEF regime.
