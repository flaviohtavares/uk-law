# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Z59

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Z59. Allocation of income[^c-99ec40eae4e63472000e98d5a2a781e8][^c-2d933a33935f7cca9a5f37bd5f6cd159]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-0d9896a8c36fa24e35664feac9a1b525] The total amount available for income allocation in a Tax Elected Fund shall be attributed as follows.

(2) There shall be attributed to TEF distributions (dividends)—

- (a) dividend income,
- (b) property investment income, and
- (c) property business income.

(3) Other income shall be attributed to TEF distributions (non-dividend).
