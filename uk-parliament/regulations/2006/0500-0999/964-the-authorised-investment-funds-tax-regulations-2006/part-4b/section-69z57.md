# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Z57

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Z57. Treatment of property investment income[^c-99ec40eae4e63472000e98d5a2a781e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 7(2) of ICTA (treatment of certain payments and repayments of income tax: set off of tax) shall not apply to payments of property investment income.

(2) Property investment income arising to a Tax Elected Fund shall be treated for the purposes of the Tax Acts as a distribution that is exempt for the purposes of Part 9A of CTA 2009 (company distributions) but shall not be treated as franked investment income.
