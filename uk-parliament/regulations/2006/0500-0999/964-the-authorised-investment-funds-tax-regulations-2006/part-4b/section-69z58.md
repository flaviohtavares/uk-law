# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Z58

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Z58. Treatment of distributions[^c-99ec40eae4e63472000e98d5a2a781e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 931R of CTA 2009 (election that distribution should not be exempt) shall not apply in relation to distributions received by a Tax Elected Fund.
