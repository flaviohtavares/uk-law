# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 85Z5

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85Z5. Scope of this Chapter[^key-e7d651ce46fa813c235c276ae9ffac81]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies if—

- (a) a disposal gives rise to an income gain, and
- (b) that disposal also constitutes the disposal of the units concerned for the purposes of TCGA 1992.

(2) In this Chapter, the disposal specified in paragraph (1)(b) is called the “TCGA disposal”.
