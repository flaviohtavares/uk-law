# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 85S

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85S. Long-term insurance funds of insurance companies[^c-e7d651ce46fa813c235c276ae9ffac81]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No liability to tax arises under regulation 85M in respect of disposals of units of an insurance company’s long-term insurance fund.

(2) In paragraph (1) “insurance company” and “long-term insurance fund” have the same meaning as in section 431(2) of ICTA.
