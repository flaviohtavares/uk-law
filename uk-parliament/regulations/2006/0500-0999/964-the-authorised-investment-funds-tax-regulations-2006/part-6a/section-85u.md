# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 85U

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85U. Application of this Chapter[^key-e7d651ce46fa813c235c276ae9ffac81]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter applies if a participant disposes of an asset and at the time of the disposal—

- (a) the asset consists of units in a FINROF, or
- (b) the asset consists of units in an authorised investment fund that is not a FINROF and the requirements specified in paragraph (3) of regulation 85M are met.
