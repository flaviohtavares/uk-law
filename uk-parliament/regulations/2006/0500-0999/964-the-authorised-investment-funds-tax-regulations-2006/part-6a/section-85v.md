# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 85V

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85V. Disposal of an asset: the basic rule[^key-e7d651ce46fa813c235c276ae9ffac81]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There is a disposal of an asset for the purposes of this Part if there would be a disposal of an asset for the purposes of TCGA 1992.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to the following regulations in this Chapter.
