# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 85Q

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85Q. Exceptions from the charge to tax[^c-e7d651ce46fa813c235c276ae9ffac81]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No liability to tax arises under regulation 85M (the charge to tax: general provisions) if condition A or B is met.

(2) Condition A is that the participant is required to treat units in the FINROF as a loan relationship to which the provisions of Chapter 3 of Part 6 of CTA 2009 apply.

(3) Condition B is that the participant is required to treat units in the FINROF as a derivative contract to which the provisions of Part 7 of CTA 2009 apply.
