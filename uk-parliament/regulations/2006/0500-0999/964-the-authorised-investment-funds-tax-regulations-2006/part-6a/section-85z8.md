# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 85Z8

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85Z8. Application of section 128 of TCGA 1992[^key-e7d651ce46fa813c235c276ae9ffac81]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if there is a disposal to which this Part applies by virtue of—

- (a) regulation 85X (application of section 135 of TCGA 1992), and
- (b) regulation 85Y (application of section 136 of TCGA 1992).

(2) TCGA 1992 has effect as if an amount equal to the income gain to which that disposal gives rise were given (by the person making the exchange) as consideration for the new holding (within the meaning of section 128 of that Act (consideration given or received for new holding on a reorganisation)).
