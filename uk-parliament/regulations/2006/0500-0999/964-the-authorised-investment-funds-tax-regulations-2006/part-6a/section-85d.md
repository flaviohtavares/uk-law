# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 85D

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85D. The investment condition[^c-e7d651ce46fa813c235c276ae9ffac81]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The investment condition is met in relation to an authorised investment fund if the total amount invested in non-reporting funds or FINROFs is more than 50%[^c-80412e11ef00e652af05cc4aeb9f5a2f] of the gross asset value of the authorised investment fund.

(2) This regulation is subject to regulation 85E.
