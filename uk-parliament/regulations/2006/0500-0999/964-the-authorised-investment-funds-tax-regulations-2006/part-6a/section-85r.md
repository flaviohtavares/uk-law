# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 85R

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85R. Trading stock etc.[^key-e7d651ce46fa813c235c276ae9ffac81]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No liability to tax arises under regulation 85M if condition A or B is met.

(2) Condition A is that the units in the fund are held as trading stock.

(3) Condition B is that the disposal of the units is taken into account in computing the profits of a trade.
