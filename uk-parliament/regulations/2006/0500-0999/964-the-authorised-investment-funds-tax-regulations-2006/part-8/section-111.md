# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 111

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 111. Instruments revoked

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following statutory instruments are revoked—

- The Open-ended Investment Companies (Tax) Regulations 1997 [^c15459391];
- The Open-ended Investment Companies (Tax) (Amendment) Regulations 1997 [^c15459401];
- The Open-ended Investment Companies (Tax) (Amendment) Regulations 2002 [^c15459411];
- The Open-ended Investment Companies (Tax) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 [^c15459421].
