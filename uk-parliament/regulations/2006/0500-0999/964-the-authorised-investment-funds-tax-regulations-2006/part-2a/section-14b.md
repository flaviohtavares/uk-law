## 14B. Tax treatment of qualified investor schemes[^c-afbfafb42c05809e4f91e384905f7a73]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The provisions in paragraph (2) shall not apply to a qualified investor scheme in relation to an accounting period of the scheme unless the genuine diversity of ownership condition (see regulation 9A[^c-6106806333f2f4898cb152ad346d5f74]) is met in relation to that accounting period.

(2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) the provisions of Part 2 of these Regulations,
- (aa)[^c-f96bbc09beba2a266776cd26e6bcfb10] the provisions of Part 4 of these Regulations,
- (b) the provisions of Part 4A of these Regulations,
- (c) where the qualified investor scheme is an authorised unit trust scheme, section 468(1A) of ICTA,
- (d) where the qualified investor scheme is an open-ended investment company, section 468A(1) of ICTA,
- (e) in subsection (1) of section 99 of TCGA 1992 (as modified by these Regulations) the words “except that nothing in this section” to the end of that sub-section, and
- (f) section 100 of TCGA 1992.

(3) Where the genuine diversity of ownership condition is not met in relation to an accounting period of the scheme—

- (a)[^c-d4538e5df0adb04682302dad4287bb57] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-bac5c90e827528b61a66e2831b8a01ab] the total amount available for income allocation to participants must only be allocated in accordance with paragraph (1)(b) of regulation 17 (allocation of income) , and[^c-1f2f8cae9853a984980108838e66589b]
- (c) Part 3A of CTA 2010 (companies with small profits) does not apply.

(4) In these Regulations a “qualified investor scheme” means a fund, authorised by the appropriate regulator[^c-52978d66bd499c0ce43f8882e6802436], in which a statement that the fund is a qualified investor scheme is included in the instrument constituting the scheme.
