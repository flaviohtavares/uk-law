## 17. Allocation of income[^c-2d2f6d1f605ab80d4f9e10ce107da66b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-e54d6022fcbb19751461d0f8e7976b1d] The total amount available for income allocation must be allocated in one of the following ways—

- (a) for distribution as yearly interest (see regulations 18 to 21); or
- (b) for distribution as dividends (see regulation 22).

(1A)[^c-b01e3d65d3a9cd36788bff07db5d4643] Paragraph (1) does not apply in relation to an authorised investment fund to which Part 4A or 4B applies.
