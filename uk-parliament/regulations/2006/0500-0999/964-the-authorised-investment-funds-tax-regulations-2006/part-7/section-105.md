> Text as at 2021-12-09.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 105. Modification of section 99 of TCGA 1992

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 99 of TCGA 1992 (application of Act to unit trust schemes) [^c15459341], in subsection (2), the words “ sections 99A and 99AA ” are treated as substituted for “section 99A”.

## Annotations
