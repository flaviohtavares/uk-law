# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 104

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 104. General modification: holder of unit in authorised unit trust

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The modifications specified in this regulation are that references, however expressed, in TCGA 1992 to the holder of a unit within regulation 102(1) (other than references in a definition of a unit holder) have effect as if they included references to the owner of a share in the open-ended investment company concerned.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply—

- (a) to section 99(1) (application of Act to unit trust scheme),
- (b)[^c-0bd602102093f01f66eef31609daa035] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) to section 272(5) (valuation of rights of unit holders), or
- (d) to references in provisions which include reference, whether made expressly or by implication, to shares in, or an owner of shares in, an open-ended investment company.
