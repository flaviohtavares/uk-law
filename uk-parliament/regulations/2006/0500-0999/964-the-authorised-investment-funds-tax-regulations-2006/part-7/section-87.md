# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 87

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Amendments of TMA 1970

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) TMA 1970 [^c15459241] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 98 (penalties in relation to special returns)—

- (a) in subsection (4E) [^c15459251] for “Chapter 3 of Part 12 of the principal Act” substitute “ regulations made under section 17(3) of the Finance (No. 2) Act 2005 (as at 1st April 2006, see the Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006 (S.I. 2006/\[abcd\])) ”.
- (b) in the first column of the Table—
  - (i) omit the entry relating to section 468P(6) of ICTA,
  - (ii) omit the entry relating to regulations under section 468PB(3) of ICTA [^c15459261], and
  - (iii) at the end insert—

    > regulations under section 17(3) of the Finance (No. 2) Act 2005

    .
