## 90. Amendment of FA 1996

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) FA 1996 [^c15459291] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 4(4) of Schedule 10 [^c15459301] (loan relationships: company holdings in unit trusts and offshore funds) for “section 468L(3) of the Taxes Act 1988” substitute “ regulations made under section 17(3) of the Finance (No. 2) Act 2005 (as at 1st April 2006, see regulation 18(3) of the Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006 (S.I. 2006/\[abcd\])) ”.
