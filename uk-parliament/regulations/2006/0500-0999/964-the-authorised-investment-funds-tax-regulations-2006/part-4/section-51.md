# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 51

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Participants chargeable to corporation tax: holdings in qualified investor schemes and long-term asset funds where scheme does not meet the genuine diversity of ownership condition[^key-c0ded38d031784448dcc754738a1c893][^key-a6332a483f9cbf0e5cc2f6d1333aeffa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if—

- (a) a participant has a holding in a qualified investor scheme or a long-term asset fund[^key-195094faa4071988b5a9348f0c51b2f8], and
- (b) the scheme has not met the genuine diversity of ownership condition in regulation 9A[^key-c675f867b14ab5d6180511001bcc2683] in relation to an accounting period.

(2) Section 212 of TCGA 1992 (annual deemed disposal of holdings of unit trusts etc.) does not apply to the participant in relation to that accounting period.

(3) Paragraph 4 of Schedule 10 to FA 1996 (company holdings in unit trusts and offshore funds) shall not apply to the participant in relation to that accounting period.
