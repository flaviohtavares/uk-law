# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 25

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Funds excluded from the ambit of this Part

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part does not apply to an authorised investment fund if the fund—

- (a) is a registered pension scheme within the meaning of Part 4 of the Finance Act 2004 [^c15459001], or
- (b) is treated, under paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 36 to that Act, as having become such a scheme.

## Annotations
