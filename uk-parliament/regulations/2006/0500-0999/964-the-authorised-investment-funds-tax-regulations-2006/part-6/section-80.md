# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 80

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Carrying forward of excess management expenses

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if condition A or B is met.

(2) Condition A is that, in respect of the pre-transfer accounting period of the target trust, the trustees are entitled, under section 75(9) of ICTA [^c15459231] (carry forward of management expenses and sums treated as management expenses), to carry forward an excess amount to the next accounting period of the trust.

(3) Condition B is that—

- (a) the pre-transfer accounting period is the final accounting period of the target trust, and
- (b) the trustees are entitled, under section 75(9) of ICTA, to carry forward an excess amount to what would have been the next accounting period of the trust were the trust to have an accounting period beginning on the transfer date.

(4) With effect from the transfer date, the entitlement is translated into a right in the acquiring company to treat the amount as if it had been carried forward under section 75(9) of ICTA to the first of its accounting periods to end on or after the transfer date.

## Annotations

[^c15459231]: Marginal citation: Section 75 was substituted by section 38(1) of the [Finance Act 2004 (c. 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12).
