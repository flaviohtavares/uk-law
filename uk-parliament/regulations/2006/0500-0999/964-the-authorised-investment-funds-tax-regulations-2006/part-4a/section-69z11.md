# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Z11

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Z11. Appeal against notice under regulation 69Z10[^key-228ceee17a63954421c7bcbab46e5182]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a notice is given to a company under regulation 69Z10, the company may appeal [^key-814d4f9bf67b28835c1e5a72e90f6c3c]....

(2) The notice of appeal must be given to HM Revenue and Customs within a period of 28 days beginning with the day on which the notice under regulation 69Z10 is given.

(3) On an appeal that is notified to the tribunal, the tribunal[^key-d3c57cd5648428ed25b9cf095e68257e] may—

- (a) affirm, vary or cancel the notice, and
- (b) affirm, vary or quash an assessment made under regulation 69Z10(4).
