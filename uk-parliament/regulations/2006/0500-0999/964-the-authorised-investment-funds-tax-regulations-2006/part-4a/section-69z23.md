# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Z23

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Z23. Deduction of tax from PAIF distributions (interest)[^c-228ceee17a63954421c7bcbab46e5182]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On making a PAIF distribution (interest), an open-ended investment company to which this Part applies must deduct a sum representing income tax at the basic rate[^c-36e7455d3d27026c56d57770d057db64] in force for the tax year in which the PAIF distribution (interest) is made.

(2) Accordingly, the sum is one to which section 874 of ITA 2007 applies.

(3)[^c-b385b18eae2d50ff5482c0f8fc842e8e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) This regulation is subject to regulation 69Z24 (distribution payments to be made without deduction of tax).
