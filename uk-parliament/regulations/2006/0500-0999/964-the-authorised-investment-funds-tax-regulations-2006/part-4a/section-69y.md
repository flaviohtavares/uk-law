# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Y

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Y. Chargeability to corporation tax[^c-228ceee17a63954421c7bcbab46e5182]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The net income of F (tax-exempt) (see regulation 69Z1) shall not be charged to corporation tax.

(2) The net income of F (residual) (see regulation 69Z3) shall be charged to corporation tax at the rate applicable for open-ended investment companies (see section 468A(1) of ICTA).
