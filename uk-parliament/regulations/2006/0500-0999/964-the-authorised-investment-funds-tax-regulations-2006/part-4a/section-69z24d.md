# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Z24D

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Z24D. Interpretation[^key-9ace20e83dce64bd3a4dd42645a165be][^key-228ceee17a63954421c7bcbab46e5182]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulations 69Z24A to 69Z24C, “manufactured dividend” and “dividend manufacturer” have the meanings given by Schedule 23A to ICTA .
