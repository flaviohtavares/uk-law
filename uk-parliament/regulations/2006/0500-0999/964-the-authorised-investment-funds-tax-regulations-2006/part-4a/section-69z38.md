# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Z38

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Z38. Appeal against termination notice[^c-228ceee17a63954421c7bcbab46e5182]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An open-ended investment company to which a termination notice is given may appeal [^c-0438a208ce271bf681795fef7eb7012c]....

(2) The notice of appeal must be given to HM Revenue and Customs within a period of 28 days beginning with the day on which the termination notice is given.

(3) On an appeal that is notified to the tribunal, the tribunal[^c-5639152250884cad8faf422b8c755265] shall determine whether it was just and reasonable for HM Revenue and Customs to give the termination notice.

(4) If the tribunal decides[^c-888825749d7cdb33ebdd7105edea387d] that it was, the tribunal must[^c-0bb6f8c832e664097846cfa4974beb70] confirm the notice.

(5) If the tribunal decides that it was not, the tribunal must set aside the notice.
