# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Z24H

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Z24H. Application of section 137 of TCGA[^c-ab1d7c50d09ca8fc955840f12180887d][^c-228ceee17a63954421c7bcbab46e5182]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 137 of TCGA (restriction on application of tax treatment) applies to any transaction specified in regulations 69Z24E or 69Z24F in the circumstances mentioned in that section.
