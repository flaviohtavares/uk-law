# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Z20

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Z20. Property distributions (dividends): liability to tax of participants[^key-228ceee17a63954421c7bcbab46e5182]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A PAIF distribution (dividends) received by a participant in an open-ended investment company to which this Part applies shall be treated as if it were a dividend on shares.

(2) If a PAIF distribution (dividends) is made for a distribution period to a participant chargeable to corporation tax, regulations 48 to 52A shall not apply to the distribution.
