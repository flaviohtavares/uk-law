## 69F. Meaning of “property investment business”[^key-228ceee17a63954421c7bcbab46e5182]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “property investment business” means business consisting of any one or more of—

- (a) property rental business (see regulation 69H);
- (b) owning shares in UK-REITs; and
- (c) owning shares or units in an entity within section 528(4A)(j) of CTA 2010 (overseas equivalent to[^key-68805eeb4048ac35bad02ccd7d4f6254] UK REIT).

(2) In these Regulations “UK-REIT” has the meaning given in section 518(4) of CTA 2010[^key-ee863f5f349b05bf823acca293acf24a].

(5)[^key-83d0c0a7bddaa09ce8e2fc51ed15fc93] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
