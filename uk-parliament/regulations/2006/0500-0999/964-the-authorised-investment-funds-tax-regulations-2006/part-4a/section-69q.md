# The Authorised Investment Funds (Tax) Regulations 2006, section 69Q

> Text as at 2021-12-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69Q. Contents of notice under regulation 69O[^key-228ceee17a63954421c7bcbab46e5182]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if notice is given under regulation 69O.

(2) An existing company notice must specify the accounting period from the beginning of which this Part is to apply to the company (the “specified accounting period”).

(3) An existing company notice must be accompanied by—

- (a) a statement by the manager of the open-ended investment company that the conditions specified regulation 9A and[^key-29c47fe2e8cc0cd005bafa7bc0bbe5e1] in regulations 69E to 69N are reasonably expected to be met in respect of the company throughout the specified accounting period;
- (b) the following documents relating to the company—
  - (i) its instrument of incorporation, and
  - (ii) its prospectus;
- (c) a copy of the application to the appropriate regulator[^key-491c23c52ff6ee42a8b457f833574544] for agreement to changes in the company’s instrument of incorporation and its prospectus; and
- (d) copies of any documents accompanying the application mentioned in sub-paragraph (c) to the extent that those documents do not fall within sub-paragraphs (a) and (b).

(4) A future company notice must specify that this Part will apply to the proposed company from the date of its incorporation and authorisation.

(5) A future company notice must be accompanied by—

- (a) a statement by the applicant that the conditions specified in regulation 9A and[^key-0ebb21e88be5393be74497f6ac52a01b] regulations 69E to 69N are reasonably expected to be met in respect of the proposed company throughout its first accounting period;
- (b) the following documents relating to the proposed company—
  - (i) its proposed instrument of incorporation, and
  - (ii) its [^key-e39a4e349a47b8706ea6ee7b0392c9ce]... prospectus [^key-498787df7e8ee39d0cbd6e030310871e]... ;
- (c) a copy of the application to the appropriate regulator[^key-b6293e389ad455eb93aa9b940bd256b5] for approval of the proposed company as an open-ended investment company; and
- (d) copies of any documents accompanying the application mentioned in sub-paragraph (c) to the extent that those documents do not fall within sub-paragraphs (a) and (b).
