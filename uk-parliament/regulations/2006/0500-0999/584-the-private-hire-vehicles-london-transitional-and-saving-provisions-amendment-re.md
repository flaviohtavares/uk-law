# The Private Hire Vehicles (London) (Transitional and Saving Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 584

METROPOLITAN AND CITY POLICE DISTRICTS

PRIVATE HIRE VEHICLES

The Private Hire Vehicles (London) (Transitional and Saving Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made

6th March 2006

Laid before Parliament

13th March 2006

Coming into force

4th April 2006

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 37(1) and (2) of the Private Hire Vehicles (London) Act 1998[^f00001].

In accordance with section 37(3) of that Act he has consulted Transport for London.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Private Hire Vehicles (London) (Transitional and Saving Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 4th April 2006.

## 2. Amendment to regulation 5 (Issue of temporary permits)

In regulation 5(1)(b) of the Private Hire Vehicles (London) (Transitional and Saving Provisions) Regulations 2003[^f00002] for “1st April 2006” there shall be substituted “1st January 2007”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Karen Buck

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

6th March 2006

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 5 of the Private Hire Vehicles (London) (Transitional and Saving Provisions) Regulations 2003. Regulation 5 enables Transport for London to issue a temporary permit to any person who, before a specified date, duly applies for a London PHV driver’s licence and pays the application fee. These Regulations extend the date from 1st April 2006 to 1st January 2007.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
