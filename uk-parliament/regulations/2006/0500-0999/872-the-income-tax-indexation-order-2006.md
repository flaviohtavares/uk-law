# The Income Tax (Indexation) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 872

INCOME TAX

The Income Tax (Indexation) Order 2006

Made

22nd March 2006

The Treasury make the following Order in compliance with the duty imposed upon them by section 1(6) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Income Tax (Indexation) Order 2006.

## 2. Amounts specified for rate limits for 2006-07

Unless Parliament determines otherwise, for the year 2006-07, the amount treated as specified by virtue of section 1(4) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 shall be—

- (a) in section 1(2)(aa) of that Act (starting rate limit of charge to income tax), £2,150; and
- (b) in section 1(2)(b) of that Act (basic rate limit of charge to income tax) £33,300.

Tom Watson

Dave Watts

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

22nd March 2006

This note is not part of the Order)

Section 1(6) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 (“the Act”) provides that the Treasury shall by order made by statutory instrument before each year of assessment specify the amounts which by virtue of that section shall, unless Parliament otherwise determines, be treated as specified for the purposes of section 1(2)(aa) (starting rate limit of charge to income tax), and section 1(2)(b) (basic rate limit of charge to income tax) of the Act for the year of assessment 2005–06.

These amounts were specified for the year 2005–06 by the Income Tax (Indexation) Order 2005 (S.I. [2005/716](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/716)).

The amounts in section 1(2)(aa) and (b) of the Act are increased by this Order in accordance with the percentage increase in the retail prices index for September 2005 over that for September 2004. The amount in section 1(2)(aa) is rounded up to the nearest £10 in accordance with section 1(4)(a) and the amount in section 1(2)(b) is rounded up to the nearest £100 in accordance with section 1(4)(b). The “retail prices index” is defined in section 833(2) of the Act, as amended by paragraph 22 of Schedule 2 to the Transfer of Functions (Registration and Statistics) Order 1996 ([SI 1996/273](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/273)), as the “general index of retail prices (for all items) published by the Office for National Statistics”. The retail prices index for September 2004 is 188.1 and for September 2005 is 193.1 (based on January 1987 as 100). The retail prices index for September 2005 was published in the November 2005 edition of Economic Trends, number 624, page 92, Table 3.1.

This Order does not impose any costs on business.
