# The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No.12) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 751 (C. 17)

POLICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No.12) Order 2006

Made

14th March 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 336(3) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No.12) Order 2006.

**2.** The following provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 shall come into force in England and Wales on 6th April 2006—

- (a) section 328 (criminal record certificates: amendments of Part 5 of Police Act 1997[^f00002]) in so far as it relates to the entries in Schedule 35 referred to in paragraph (c);
- (b) section 332 (repeals) in so far as it relates to the entries in Schedule 37 referred to in paragraph (d);
- (c) in Schedule 35 (criminal record certificates: amendments of Part 5 of the Police Act 1997)—
  - (i) paragraph 5, to the extent that it is not already in force, and
  - (ii) paragraphs 6, 8, 9 and 12; and
- (d) in Part 11 of Schedule 37 (repeals) the entries relating to—
  - (i) section 125 of the Police Act 1997;
  - (ii) the Private Security Industry Act 2001[^f00003];
  - (iii) the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00004]; and
  - (iv) section 42 of the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002[^f00005].

Andy Burnham

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

14th March 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force in England and Wales on 6th April 2006 the provisions specified in article 2.
