# The Dartford – Thurrock Crossing (Amendment) Regulations 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 585

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The Dartford – Thurrock Crossing (Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made

1st March 2006

Laid before Parliament

15th March 2006

Coming into force

12th April 2006

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 25(1)(b) of the Dartford – Thurrock Crossing Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Dartford – Thurrock Crossing (Amendment) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 12th April 2006.

## 2. Dangerous goods

In regulation 5(1) of the Dartford – Thurrock Crossing Regulations 1998[^f00002], for “the booklet entitled “Dangerous Traffic”, 12th Edition issued in 2003” there shall be substituted “the booklet entitled “Dangerous Traffic” 13th Edition issued in 2005”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Stephen Ladyman

Minister of State

Department for Transport

1st March 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations amend regulation 5 of the Dartford-Thurrock Crossing Regulations 1998 to substitute a reference to the 13<sup>th</sup> edition of the booklet entitled “Dangerous Traffic” issued in 2005.

The booklet may be obtained in person or by post from Le Crossing Company Limited, Crossing Offices, South Orbital Way, Dartfort, Kent, DA1 5PR, the Mersey Tunnels, Merseyside Passenger Transport Authority, George’s Dock Building, George’s Dock Way, Pier Head, Liverpool, L3 1DD or the Tyne Tunnel, Wallsend, Tyne and Wear, NE28 OPD.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on business, charities or voluntary bodies.
