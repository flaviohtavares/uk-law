# The Lord Chancellor (Transfer of Functions and Supplementary Provisions) Order 2006, SCHEDULE 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Supplementary provisions

Article 3

### 1. Criminal Appeal (Northern Ireland) Rules 1968

In the definition of “shorthand writer” in rule 2(1) of the Criminal Appeal (Northern Ireland) Rules 1968[^f00051], for “Lord Chief Justice” substitute “Lord Chancellor under rule 25”.

### 2. Pensions Appeal Tribunals (Posthumous Appeals) Order 1980

Omit Article 11(d) of the Pensions Appeal Tribunals (Posthumous Appeals) Order 1980[^f00052].

### 3. Judicial Pensions (Requisite Benefits) Order 1988

In Schedule 1 to the Judicial Pensions (Requisite Benefits) Order 1988[^f00053], omit “, other than the Lord Chancellor,”.

### 4. Housing (Preservation of Right to Buy) Regulations 1993

In Schedule 2 to the Housing (Preservation of Right to Buy) Regulations 1993[^f00054] (which sets out a modified version of Part V of the Housing Act 1985 as it applies by virtue of those Regulations), omit the text of section 181(4) and (5) of the Housing Act 1985.

### 5. Insolvent Partnerships Order 1994

The Insolvent Partnerships Order 1994[^f00055] is amended as follows.

**6.** (1) The modified text of section 117 of the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00056] as set out in each of—

- (a) paragraph 6 of Schedule 3; and
- (b) paragraph 1 of Schedule 5,

is amended in accordance with this paragraph.

(2) In subsection (5), for “may by order” substitute “may, with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice, by order”.

(3) After subsection (7) insert—

> (8) The Lord Chief Justice may nominate a judicial office holder (as defined in section 109(4) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005) to exercise his functions under this section.

.

**7.** (1) The modified text of section 117 of the Insolvency Act 1986 as set out in each of—

- (a) paragraph 5 of Schedule 4; and
- (b) paragraph 1 of Schedule 6,

is amended in accordance with this paragraph.

(2) In subsection (7), for “may by order” substitute “may, with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice, by order”.

(3) After subsection (9) insert—

> (10) The Lord Chief Justice may nominate a judicial office holder (as defined in section 109(4) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005) to exercise his functions under this section.

.

### 8. Supreme Court (Non-Contentious Probate) Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

In article 2(1)(iii) of the Supreme Court (Non-Contentious Probate) Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00057], for “Lord Chancellor” substitute “Lord Chief Justice”.

### 9. Housing (Right to Acquire) Regulations 1997

In Schedule 2 to the Housing (Right to Acquire) Regulations 1997[^f00058] (which sets out a modified version of Part V of the Housing Act 1985 as it applies by virtue of those Regulations), omit the text of section 181(4) and (5) of the Housing Act 1985.

### 10. Local Probation Boards (Appointments and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2001

In regulation 2(1) of the Local Probation Boards (Appointments and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2001[^f00059], for “Lord Chancellor” substitute “Lord Chief Justice”.

### 11. Courts Act 2003 (Transitional Provisions, Savings and Consequential Provisions) Order 2005

In article 8(3) of the Courts Act 2003 (Transitional Provisions, Savings and Consequential Provisions) Order 2005[^f00060], for “Lord Chancellor” substitute “Lord Chief Justice”.
