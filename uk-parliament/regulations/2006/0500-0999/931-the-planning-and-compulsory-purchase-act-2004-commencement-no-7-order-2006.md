# The Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 931 (C. 26)

TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING, WALES

The Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2006

Made

27th March 2006

The First Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 121(1) and (2) and section 122(3) of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004[^f00001]and after consultation[^f00002] with the National Assembly for Wales, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2006.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 1st April 2006 in relation to Wales

To the extent to which it is not already in force, section 53 of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 shall come into force in relation to Wales on 1st April 2006.

Signed by authority of the First Secretary of State

Kay Andrews

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Office of the Deputy Prime Minister

27th March 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force section 53 of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, in relation to Wales, in so far as it is not already in force. Section 53 amends section 303 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990, which relates to fees and charges.

(This note is not part of the Order)
