# The Asylum Support (Amendment) Regulations 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 733

IMMIGRATION

The Asylum Support (Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made

13th March 2006

Laid before Parliament

17th March 2006

Coming into force

10th April 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 166(3) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3(a) of Schedule 8 to, the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Asylum Support (Amendment) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 10th April 2006.

## 2. Kind and levels of support for essential living needs

For the table at regulation 10(2) of the Asylum Support Regulations 2000[^f00002] substitute:

> <table>
> <thead>
> <tr><th colspan="2"><em><strong>TABLE</strong></em></th></tr>
> </thead>
> <tbody>
> <tr><td>Qualifying couple</td><td>£63.07</td></tr>
> <tr><td>Lone parent aged 18 or over</td><td>£40.22</td></tr>
> <tr><td>Single person aged 25 or over</td><td>£40.22</td></tr>
> <tr><td>Single person aged at least 18 but under 25</td><td>£31.85</td></tr>
> <tr><td>Person aged at least 16 but under 18 (except a member of a qualifying couple)</td><td>£34.60</td></tr>
> <tr><td>Person aged under 16</td><td>£45.58</td></tr>

> </table>

.

## 3. Revocation of the Asylum Support (Amendment) (No 2) Regulations 2005

The Asylum Support (Amendment) (No 2) Regulations 2005[^f00003] are revoked.

Tony McNulty

Minister of State

Home Office

13th March 2006

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 10(2) of the Asylum Support Regulations 2000 by increasing the cash amounts payable, as a general rule, in respect of a person’s essential living needs. These changes take effect from 10th April 2006 and on that date the Asylum Support (Amendment) (No 2) Regulations 2005 which set out the amounts previously payable, are revoked.
