# The Private and Voluntary Health Care (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 539

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND

The Private and Voluntary Health Care (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made

1st March 2006

Laid before Parliament

7th March 2006

Coming into force

1st April 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 31(7) and 118(6) of the Care Standards Act 2000[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Private and Voluntary Health Care (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 1st April 2006.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to England only.

## 2. Amendment of the Private and Voluntary Health Care (England) Regulations 2001

After Part III (conduct of health care establishments and agencies) of the Private and Voluntary Health Care (England) Regulations 2001[^f00002] insert—

> **PART IIIA — FREQUENCY OF INSPECTIONS OF HEALTH CARE ESTABLISHMENTS AND AGENCIES**
>
> **32A. Frequency of inspections**
>
> (1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Commission shall arrange for premises which are used as an establishment, or for the purposes of an agency, to be inspected a minimum of once in every five year period.
>
> (2) Any inspection referred to in paragraph (1) may be unannounced.
>
> (3) In this regulation, “five year period” means a period commencing on 1st April in any year and ending on 31st March in the fifth year following.

.

## 3. Revocations

Regulation 6 (frequency of inspections) of the Commission for Healthcare Audit and Inspection (Fees and Frequency of Inspections) Regulations 2004[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Jane Kennedy

Minister of State,

Department of Health

1st March 2006

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made under the Care Standards Act 2000 (“the Act”), and apply in relation to England only. Regulation 2 inserts Part IIIA (frequency of inspections of health care establishments and agencies) in the Private and Voluntary Health Care (England) Regulations 2001. New regulation 32A amends the minimum frequency of inspections of premises used for the purposes of health care establishments and agencies under section 31(7) of the Act.

Regulation 3 revokes regulation 6 of the Commission for Healthcare Audit and Inspection (Fees and Frequency of Inspections) Regulations 2004.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared for these Regulations and a copy has been placed in the library of each House of Parliament. Copies of the Regulatory Impact Assessment can be obtained from the Department of Health’s website www.doh.gov.uk/regulatoryimpact/index.htm In addition, a copy can be obtained from Room 418, Wellington House, 133-135 Waterloo Road, London SE1 8UG.
