# The Anti-Social Behaviour Act 2003 (Commencement No.4) (Amendment) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 835

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Anti-Social Behaviour Act 2003 (Commencement No.4) (Amendment) Order 2006

Made

20th March 2006

Coming into force

31st March 2006

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 93 and 94(2) of the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003[^f00001], the Secretary of State makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003 (Commencement No.4) (Amendment) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 31<sup>st</sup> March 2006.

**2.** In paragraph (1) of article 4 of the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003 (Commencement No.4) Order 2004[^f00002] for the words “eighteen months” substitute “two years”.

Hazel Blears

Minister of State

Home Office

20th March 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which comes into force on 31<sup>st</sup> March 2006, amends the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003 (Commencement No.4) Order 2004 so as to extend the period of eighteen months for which section 85(5) of that Act has been brought partially into force in relation to juveniles (it is already fully in force in relation to adults), for the purpose of conducting a pilot. The effect is to extend that period for another six months from 1<sup>st</sup> April 2006 and so to make possible the continued conduct of the pilot for that extended period in relation to applications for anti-social behaviour orders in the following county courts:-

Bristol, Central London, Clerkenwell, Dewsbury, Huddersfield, Leicester, Manchester, Oxford, Tameside, Wigan, and Wrexham.
