# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 38

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Veterinary inquiry where avian influenza is suspected or confirmed in vehicles

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An inspector must ensure that a veterinary inquiry is carried out in relation to any vehicle on which he knows or suspects that highly pathogenic avian influenza or highly pathogenic avian influenza virus exists.
