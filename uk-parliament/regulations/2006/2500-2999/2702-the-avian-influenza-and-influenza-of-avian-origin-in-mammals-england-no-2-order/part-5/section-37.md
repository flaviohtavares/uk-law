# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 37

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Veterinary measures at slaughterhouses

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If he suspects that highly pathogenic avian influenza exists at any slaughterhouse or if the Chief Veterinary Officer has confirmed that it exists there, the Secretary of State must—

- (a) serve a notice on the occupier of the slaughterhouse informing him of this and applying the measures set out in paragraphs 3, 4 and 5 of Schedule 1; and
- (b) ensure that a veterinary inquiry is carried out at the slaughterhouse.
