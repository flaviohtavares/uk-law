# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 44

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Reintroduction of poultry and other captive birds

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No person shall reintroduce poultry to a slaughterhouse where highly pathogenic avian influenza was suspected or confirmed until an inspector has confirmed that the cleansing and disinfection referred to in article 43 have been completed and at least 24 hours have elapsed since their completion[^key-9b47614c9e388134a3d8186e338a778d].

(2) No person shall reintroduce poultry or other captive birds to a border control[^key-fc4f67e5c91dde6805dccf686db05f31] post where highly pathogenic avian influenza was suspected or confirmed until an inspector has confirmed that the cleansing and disinfection referred to in article 43 have been completed and at least 24 hours have elapsed since their completion[^key-2f125406d1c2e8a3ae50b1528e739903].

(3) If he considers it necessary to reduce the risk of the spread of avian influenza virus[^key-953086db9474e5f1343e088236204c38], the Secretary of State may, by notice to the occupier of such a border control[^key-08ec618473dceb70bdf8f43dcff66ced] post, prohibit the introduction of animals other than birds onto the premises until the cleansing and disinfection referred to in article 43 have been completed and at least 24 hours have elapsed since their completion[^key-8f41468af6f0197aec6d9c05d7989b5d].
