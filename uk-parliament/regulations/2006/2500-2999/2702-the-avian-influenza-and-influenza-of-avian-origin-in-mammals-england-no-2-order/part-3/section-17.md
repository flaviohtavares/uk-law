# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 17

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Declaration of a temporary control zone

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In a declaration of a temporary control zone, the Secretary of State must impose, in relation to premises in all or such part of the zone as he thinks fit, such measures as he considers necessary to reduce the risk of the spread of avian influenza virus[^key-65e9c1ddd5dc1ab4c41de226fa37097e].
