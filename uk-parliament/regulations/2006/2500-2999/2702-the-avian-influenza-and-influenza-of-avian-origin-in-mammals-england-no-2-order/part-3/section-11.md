# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 11

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Derogations from restrictions applicable at suspect premises

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A notice served under article 10 on the occupier of a laboratory where low pathogenic avian influenza virus is kept must—

- (a) be served by a veterinary inspector; and
- (b) impose such of the measures in Schedule 1 and such measures relating to the operation of the laboratory as the veterinary inspector considers necessary.

(2) In a notice served under article 10 on the occupier of premises other than those referred to in paragraph (1) of this article, or by licence to such an occupier, a veterinary inspector or an inspector acting under his direction may grant derogations from paragraphs 2, 3, 4 and 6 of Schedule 1.

(3) No person shall grant a derogation under paragraph (2) or authorise the granting of such a derogation unless he has considered—

- (a) the risk of the possible spread of avian influenza virus[^c-0fe414f20c66ad2e2976275311c2a237] were the derogation to be granted;
- (b) any precautionary measures which have been or could be taken; and
- (c) the destination of any birds , mammals[^c-14b081066f6afee81f8f4e2ca8a4a35b] or things which would be moved were the derogation to be granted.

(4) In a notice served under article 10 or by licence, a veterinary inspector or an inspector acting under the direction of a veterinary inspector may also grant a derogation from the cleansing and[^c-f98ce2a47ebf1d9741b614a3c6b28612] disinfection requirement in paragraph 9 of Schedule 1 or paragraph 6 of Schedule 9[^c-e143d2256d474baf61e75d2a194e3688] if—

- (a) the premises the subject of the derogation are non-commercial premises;
- (b) the premises contain other captive birds but no poultry;
- (c) he has considered the risk of the possible spread of avian influenza virus[^c-b7dc7e81ecef36ea1f995660b0470267] were the derogation to be granted and any precautionary measures which have been or could be taken.
