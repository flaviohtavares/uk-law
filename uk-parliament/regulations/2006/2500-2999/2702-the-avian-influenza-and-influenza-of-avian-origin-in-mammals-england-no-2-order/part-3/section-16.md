# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 16

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Additional restrictions at suspect premises

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In a notice served under article 13(b), the Secretary of State must state if the measures specified in the notice apply—

- (a) to all poultry and other captive birds on the premises;
- (b) only to certain categories of poultry or other captive birds;
- (c) only to poultry or other captive birds which he suspects are infected;
- (ca)[^c-3f49a49039d5f7b3093393dba1430bd1] to all kept mammals at the premises;
- (cb) to specified kept mammals at the premises.
- (d) to all or only a specified part of the premises.
