# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 13

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Measures to minimise the risk of the spread of avian influenza from suspect premises

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the Secretary of State considers such a measure necessary to minimise the risk of the spread of avian influenza or of avian influenza virus from suspect premises in England, Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland, he must take one or more of the following measures—

- (a) declare a temporary movement restriction zone in such part of England as he considers necessary;
- (b) serve a notice on the owner or occupier of suspect premises in England requiring him to comply with such of the measures in Schedules 1 and 9[^key-6c2a7419f69e4568d220b48041ea6db1] as are specified in the notice;
- (c) declare a temporary control zone in such part of England and of such size as he considers necessary around suspect premises.
