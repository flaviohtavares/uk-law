# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 10

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Notice of restrictions where avian influenza is suspected on premises

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to article 11 and whether or not notification has been made under article 9, an inspector must serve a notice of restrictions on the occupier of any premises where he suspects that avian influenza or influenza of avian origin[^key-48dca6052ce91897faf6c73ee4171674] exists or may have existed within the preceding 56 days.

(2) Subject to article 11, the notice of restrictions must require the occupier of the premises to comply with

- (i)[^key-82a80a1618aebc39a4997650296dec84] in the case of premises where poultry or other captive birds are present, the measures in Schedule; and
- (ii) in the case of premises where mammals are present, the measures in Schedule 1 and Schedule 9;

(3) A veterinary inspector[^key-49d3171e2561ece99000b528b9cd5176] must not withdraw the notice of restrictions unless instructed to do so by the Chief Veterinary Officer or the presence of disease has been negated[^key-5463edca9f10784b3edd18dce3e87754].
