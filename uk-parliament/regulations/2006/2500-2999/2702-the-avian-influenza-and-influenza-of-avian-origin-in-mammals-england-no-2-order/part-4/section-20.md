# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 20

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Killing of birds on premises

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to article 21, the Secretary of State must ensure that poultry and other captive birds to be killed on infected premises under paragraph 5 of Schedule 3 to the Act, are killed there without delay.
