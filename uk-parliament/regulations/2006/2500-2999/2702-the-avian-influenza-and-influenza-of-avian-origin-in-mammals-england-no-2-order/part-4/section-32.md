# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 32

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Restrictions on trade in things from areas where measures have been disapplied

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Unless he is licensed by a veterinary inspector, no person shall supply any of the following for export outside the United Kingdom[^c-998fe03f5a0059f544a2c4f08da7157e] if it has come from premises in respect of which any of the measures in Schedule 4 or 5 have been disapplied under articles 30 or 31—

- (a) poultry;
- (b) other captive birds;
- (c) hatching eggs;
- (d) used litter;
- (e) manure;
- (f) slurry.
