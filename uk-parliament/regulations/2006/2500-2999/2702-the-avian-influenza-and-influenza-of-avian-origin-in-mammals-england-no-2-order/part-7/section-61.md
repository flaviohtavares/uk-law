# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 61

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Measures to control the spread of avian influenza virus in mammals[^key-82f4642b0ef3526cc3abacbe0a12399f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A notice under article 12, 58, 59, 60A or 61A may, in addition to any measures specified in the relevant article, require such additional measures as appear to the Chief Veterinary Officer to be necessary in order to control or monitor the spread of avian influenza virus.

(2) A notice under paragraph (1) must be in writing and may be amended or revoked at any time.

(3) The Chief Veterinary Officer may in addition declare an avian influenza (restrictions on mammals) zone.

(4) Where an avian influenza (restrictions on mammals) zone has been declared, the Chief Veterinary Officer may impose such measures within that zone in addition to those set out in article 12, 58, 59, 60A or 61A as are considered necessary in order to control or monitor the spread of avian influenza virus
