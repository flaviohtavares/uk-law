# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 60

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Movements off the premises

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), no person shall move any mammal tested in the course of an inquiry under article 12[^c-158baa0e01891d4e6761bc6aac4ac629] off the premises until a veterinary inspector has confirmed that the mammal is not infective and licenses the movement.

(2) A veterinary inspector may license the movement of pigs and other mammals in which infection with avian influenza virus[^c-a5aa9d6d2f61d5b03ee91bd39b3e7054] is confirmed to other premises (including slaughterhouses) if he is satisfied (as a result of further tests on the animals) that the movement will not significantly increase the risk of the spread of avian influenza virus[^c-d1dfef7bf3825583455857d7a8ae23d4].
