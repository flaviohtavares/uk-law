## 1. Title, application and commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No 2) Order 2006.

(2) This Order applies in England only and comes into force on 13<sup>th</sup> November 2006.
