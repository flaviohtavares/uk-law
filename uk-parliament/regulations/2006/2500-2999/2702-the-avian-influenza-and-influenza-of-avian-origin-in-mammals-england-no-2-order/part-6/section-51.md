# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 51

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Veterinary surveillance of poultry or other captive birds[^c-bcbb0946665a052257e452b514faa1f7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State must ensure that poultry or other captive birds[^c-c0041c2ffc9a893be39a8456b2d74f94] on infected premises which are special category premises not killed under paragraph 5 of Schedule 3 of the Act are monitored for avian influenza.
