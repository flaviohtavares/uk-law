# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 57A

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57A. Duration of restricted zones[^c-06db32f3148216cf534fd4c74bf01340]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The measures in article 57 must be maintained for at least the minimum period and until the appropriate ministers, on the basis of investigations and laboratory tests carried out in the restricted zone in accordance with the diagnostic manual and a risk assessment, have determined that the risk of the spread of low pathogenic avian influenza is negligible.

(2) In paragraph (1), “**the minimum period**” means—

- (a) in a case where preliminary cleansing and disinfection at the infected premises has been carried out in accordance with one or more of the procedures in article 66, 21 days following completion of that preliminary cleansing and disinfection;
- (b) in any other case, 42 days following the date on which the outbreak is confirmed.
