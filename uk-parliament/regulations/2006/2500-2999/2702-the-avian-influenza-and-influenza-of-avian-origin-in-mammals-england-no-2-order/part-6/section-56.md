# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 56

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Size of zones

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A low pathogenic avian influenza restricted zone must—

- (a) be centred on the outbreak point; and
- (b) subject to article 29(8), have a radius of at least 1 kilometre.

(2) “**Outbreak point**” has the meaning it has in article 29.
