# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (England) (No.2) Order 2006, section 48

> Text as at 2026-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Movement of eggs from premises where low pathogenic avian influenza is confirmed

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the Secretary of State directs that poultry on infected premises are to be slaughtered at a slaughterhouse, he must not license the movement of eggs off the premises prior to the poultry being sent for slaughter unless he is satisfied that the movement would not significantly increase the risk of the spread of low pathogenic avian influenza.

(2) The Secretary of State may only license the movement of such eggs—

- (a) to a designated egg packing centre, in disposable packaging or packaging which has been cleansed and disinfected in accordance with the instructions of a veterinary inspector[^key-f8e3bd18082a0a80230a723546e2f8a1];
- (b) to an egg processing plant; or
- (c) for disposal.
