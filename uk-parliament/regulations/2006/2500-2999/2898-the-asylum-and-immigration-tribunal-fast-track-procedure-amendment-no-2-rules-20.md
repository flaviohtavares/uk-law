# The Asylum and Immigration Tribunal (Fast Track Procedure) (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [SI 2006/2789](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/2789) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 106(1) to (3) and 112(3) of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002[^f00001] and section 40A(3) of the British Nationality Act 1981[^f00002], after consulting the Council on Tribunals in accordance with section 8 of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00003], makes the following Rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal (Fast Track Procedure) (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2006, and shall come into force on 27th November 2006.

## 2. Amendment of the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal (Fast Track Procedure) (Amendment) Rules 2006

In rule 6 of the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal (Fast Track Procedure) (Amendment) Rules 2006[^f00004], for “the Schedule”, substitute “Schedule 1”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal (Fast Track Procedure) (Amendment) Rules 2006 ([S.I 2006/2789](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/2789)) (“the Amendment Rules”), which amend the Asylum and Immigration Tribunal (Fast Track Procedure) Rules 2005 ([S.I. 2005/560](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/560)) (“the Principal Rules”). They are made in consequence of a defect in the Amendment Rules and will be issued free of charge to all known recipients of the Amendment Rules.

Rule 2 amends the Amendment Rules to provide that only Schedule 1 of the Principal Rules is to be omitted. Schedule 2 to the Principal Rules is to remain in force.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business, charities or voluntary bodies.
