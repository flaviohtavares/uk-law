# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2007-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/2910
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Conditions on the use, supply and inspection of absent voter records or lists[^c-aa469c7975d70fb64acc9f4c02bb102c]

*Extent: E+W*

After regulation 61 insert—

> **61A. Conditions on the use, supply and inspection of absent voter records or lists**
>
> The restrictions on the supply, disclosure and use of the full register in regulations 94 and 96 shall apply to information covered by regulations 61(1)(a) and (b), as they apply to the full register, except that the permitted purpose shall mean either—
>
> - (a) research purposes within the meaning of that term in section 33 of the Data Protection Act 1998[^f00077]; or
> - (b) electoral purposes.
