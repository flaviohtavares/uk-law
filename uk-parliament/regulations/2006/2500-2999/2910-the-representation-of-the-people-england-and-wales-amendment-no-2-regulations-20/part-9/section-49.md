# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2007-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/2910
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Proxy postal voters list[^key-7093852b0ca0a4ad07c774e5e54d8db9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the words “list of postal proxies” wherever they occur in the provisions mentioned in paragraph (2) substitute “proxy postal voters list”.

(2) The provisions mentioned in this paragraph are—

- (a) paragraphs (3) and (5)(a) of regulation 72, and
- (b) regulation 73.

(3) In regulation 72(7)(b) for the words “special list” to the end substitute “proxy postal voters list.”

(4) In regulations 72 and 73 for “absent voters list” in each place that it occurs substitute “postal voters list”[^f00071].
