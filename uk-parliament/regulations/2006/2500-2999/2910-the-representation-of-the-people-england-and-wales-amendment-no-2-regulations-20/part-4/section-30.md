# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2007-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/2910
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Replacement of counterfoils by corresponding number lists[^c-68d6f1c2b8186a0969eed530515409ba]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In regulation 72[^f00049] omit paragraph (1) and for “counterfoil attached to the ballot paper” in paragraph (2) substitute “corresponding number list, next to the number and unique identifying mark of the ballot paper issued to that elector”.

(2) In regulation 75, in the heading and in paragraph (1), for the word “counterfoils” substitute “completed corresponding number lists”.
