# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2007-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/2910
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Amendment of regulation 85[^c-7dce0631ca53753648baacacd2525376]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 85[^f00056], for paragraph (1) substitute—

> (1) This regulation applies to any postal voting statement  contained in an envelope that has not been set aside for personal identifier verification in accordance with regulation 84(1A) or (5)[^f00057].
>
> (1A) The returning officer must satisfy himself that the postal voting statement is duly completed.
