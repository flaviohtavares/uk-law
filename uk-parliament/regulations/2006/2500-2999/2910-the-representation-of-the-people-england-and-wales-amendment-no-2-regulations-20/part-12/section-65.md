# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006, section 65

## 65. Replacement of declarations of identity by postal voting statements[^key-6d57583b31ef060d3146233536be785e]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001[^f00086]—

- (a) for each reference to “declaration of identity”, substitute “postal voting statement”; and
- (b) in each of the following places at which there is any reference to “declaration”, substitute “statement”—
  - (i) regulation 79(2),
  - (ii) paragraphs (2), (4) and (7) of regulation 85, and
  - (iii) paragraphs (1) and (4) of regulation 88.
