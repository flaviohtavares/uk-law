# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2007-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/2910

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 63. Persons entitled to be present at proceedings on issue and receipt of postal ballot papers: observers[^c-519a81c69d73f8a3fccd61c5b1719ad1]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 67 and 68 are amended by inserting at the beginning of each regulation the words “Without prejudice to the provisions of section 6A, 6B, 6C, 6D, or 6E of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00085],”.
