# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2007-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/2910
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 67. Transitional provision: regulation 31J[^key-865b378417da3775a358d7bbb877548d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies until paragraph 2(1) and (2) of Schedule 2 to the Children Act 2004[^f00087] comes into force.

(2) Regulation 31J[^f00088] must be read as if for sub-paragraphs (f) to (h) of paragraph (4) there were substituted—

> - (f) any director of social services within the meaning of section 6(1) of the Local Authority Social Services Act 1970[^f00089];

.
