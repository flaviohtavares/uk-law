# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2007-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/2910
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Amendment to registration appeals[^c-6741a17a0fdcf1d142ea532f2e02b34a]

*Extent: E+W*

For regulation 32(1) substitute—

> (1) This regulation makes provision in connection with the right of appeal—
>
> - (a) under section 56(1)(a) of the 1983 Act[^f00027], from the decision of a registration officer regarding an application for registration;
> - (b) under section 56(1)(aa) of the 1983 Act[^f00028], from the decision of a registration officer made in accordance with regulations 31C to 31F[^f00029] that a person was not entitled to be registered or, as the case may be, has ceased to satisfy the conditions for registration set out in section 4 of the 1983 Act;
> - (c) under section 56(1)(ab) of the 1983 Act[^f00030], from a determination of the registration officer under section 9B(2) of the 1983 Act[^f00031], made in accordance with regulation 31H.

.
