## 23. Anonymous registration: amendment relating to edited version of the register[^key-df4a198bd09403094444215be02a8ffa]

*Extent: E+W*

After regulation 93(2)[^f00046] insert—

> (2A) The edited register shall omit all anonymous entries in the register, and any information relating to them.
