# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2007-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/2910
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Revocation of circumstances prescribed under section 10A(5)(b) of the 1983 Act[^key-8083df1cbd2b9d01bf7dca8a442afeba]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Omit regulation 33.

(2) For regulation 34(2)(c) substitute—

> - (c) that person was registered at that address otherwise than in pursuance of—
>   - (i) an application made by virtue of section 7(2) or 7A(2) of the 1983 Act, or
>   - (ii) a declaration of local connection, a service declaration or an overseas elector’s declaration.

.

(3) In regulation 36(2)(b)[^f00032] for “regulation 33(2)(e)” substitute “regulation 31C(2)(d)”.
