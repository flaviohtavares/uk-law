# The Scotland Act 1998 (River Tweed) Order 2006, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Power of Commission to prosecute in England and Wales

Without prejudice to any right of prosecution exercisable by any other competent authority, the Commission shall have the power to prosecute offences under this Order in England and Wales, and to continue to prosecute any such prosecution commenced before the date on which this Order comes into force by or at the instance of the Commissioners appointed under the Tweed Acts.
