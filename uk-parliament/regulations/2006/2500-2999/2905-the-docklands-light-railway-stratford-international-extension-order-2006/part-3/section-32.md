# The Docklands Light Railway (Stratford International Extension) Order 2006, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Open space at Sycamore Close

(1) As from the date on which DLRL enters onto the special category land under section 11(1) of the 1965 Act, the special category land shall thereupon be discharged from all rights, trusts and incidents to which it was previously subject.

(2) In this article “the special category land” means the land shown numbered 334 and 338 on the deposited plans in the London Borough of Newham and forming part of an open space within the meaning of section 19(1) of the 1981 Act, which the Secretary of State has certified for the purposes of that section as not exceeding 250 square yards[^f00026] in area and that the giving in exchange for the special category land of other land is unnecessary.

## Annotations

[^f00026]: Footnote: The metric equivalent is 209 square metres.
