# The Criminal Defence Service Act 2006 (Commencement) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 2491 (C. 83)

legal Services commission, england and wales

The Criminal Defence Service Act 2006 (Commencement) Order 2006

Made

11 September 2006

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5(2) of the Criminal Defence Service Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Defence Service Act 2006 (Commencement) Order 2006.

**2.** The day appointed for the coming into force of those provisions of the Criminal Defence Service Act 2006 which are not already in force is 2nd October 2006.

Vera Baird

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Constitutional Affairs

Date 11 September 2006

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 2nd October 2006 those sections of the Criminal Defence Service Act 2006 (sections 1 to 4) which are not already in force. Section 5 of the Act came into force on Royal Assent.
