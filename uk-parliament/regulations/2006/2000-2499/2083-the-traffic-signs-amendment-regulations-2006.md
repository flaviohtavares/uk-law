# The Traffic Signs (Amendment) Regulations 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 2083

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Traffic Signs (Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made

26th July 2006

Laid before Parliament

31st July 2006

Coming into force

21st August 2006

The Secretary of State for Transport makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 64(1), (2) and (3) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001].

In accordance with section 134(2) of that Act he has consulted with such representative organisations as he thought fit.

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Traffic Signs (Amendment) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 21st August 2006.

## 2. Amendment of the Traffic Signs Regulations 2002

(1) The Traffic Signs Regulations 2002[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 4 after diagram 883 there shall be inserted diagrams 884 and 885 set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

(3) In Schedule 17 in column (2) of item 4, after “883,” there shall be inserted “884, 885,”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

G. Merron

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Transport

26th July 2006

## THE SCHEDULE — DIAGRAMS TO BE ADDED TO SCHEDULE 4 TO THE TRAFFIC SIGNS REGULATIONS 2002

Regulation 2(2)

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/2083/images/uksi_20062083_en_001)

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2002. Regulation 2(2) and the Schedule insert new diagrams, to indicate quiet lanes in England, into Schedule 4 (miscellaneous informatory signs) to those Regulations. Quiet lanes may be designated by local traffic authorities in England in accordance with the Quiet Lanes and Homes Zones (England) Regulations 2006 (S.I. [2006/2082](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/2082)). Regulation 2(3) makes a consequential amendment to Schedule 17 (illumination of signs).

These Regulations were notified in draft to the European Commission in accordance with Directive [98/34/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0034), as amended by Directive [98/48/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0048).

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has been produced and a copy placed in the library of both Houses of Parliament. Copies may be obtained from the Department for Transport, Great Minister House, 76 Marsham Street, London SW1P 4DR. Alternatively copies can be obtained from the Department’s website which is at www.dft.gov.uk.
