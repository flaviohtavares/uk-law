# The Central Leeds Learning Federation (Change to School Session Times) (Revocation) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 2142

education, england

The Central Leeds Learning Federation (Change to School Session Times) (Revocation) Order 2006

Made

2nd August 2006

Laid before Parliament

9th August 2006

Coming into force

1st September 2006

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills makes the following Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 3(1)(b) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Central Leeds Learning Federation (Change to School Session Times) (Revocation) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> September 2006.

## 2. Revocation

The Central Leeds Learning Federation (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006[^f00002] is revoked.

Beverley Hughes

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

2nd August 2006

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under section 3(1)(b) of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 revokes the Central Leeds Learning Federation (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006, which had the effect of exempting the Central Leeds Learning Federation from the provisions of the Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999[^f00003].
