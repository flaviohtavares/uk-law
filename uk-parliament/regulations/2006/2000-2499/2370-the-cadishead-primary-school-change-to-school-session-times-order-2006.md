# The Cadishead Primary School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 2370

education, england

The Cadishead Primary School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006

Made

27th August 2006

Laid before Parliament

8th September 2006

Coming into force

30th September 2006

The governing body of the Cadishead Primary School (“the governing body”) have, in accordance with section 4(2) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001] (“the Act”), consulted the local education authority and such persons as appear to them to be appropriate, including the parents of registered pupils at the school and the staff working at the school;

It is the opinion of the Secretary of State, having regard to the matters set out in section 1(2) of the Act, that the implementation by the governing body of the provisions of this Order may contribute to the raising of educational standards achieved by children in England;

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 2(1) of the Act, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills, on the application of the governing body, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Cadishead Primary School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 30th September 2006.

## 2. Exemption from the School Session Regulations

Regulation 3(a) of the Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999[^f00002] shall not apply to the governing body in relation to the Cadishead Primary School[^f00003] of Allotment Road, Cadishead, Manchester M44 5JD.

## 3. Time period

This Order shall have effect until 31<sup>st</sup> January 2007.

Jim Knight

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

27th August 2006

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under section 2 of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 provides that regulation 3(a) of the Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999 does not apply to the governing body of Cadishead Primary School. This will allow the governing body to make alterations to the school day at the start of the school term in January, rather than at the beginning of the school year. Article 3 specifies that the Order shall have effect until 31<sup>st</sup> January 2007.
