# The Drugs Act 2005 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 2136 (C. 71)

Dangerous drugs

The Drugs Act 2005 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2006

Made

1st August 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 24 (3) of the Drugs Act 2005[^f00001]:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Drugs Act 2005 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2006.

**2.** Section 20 (anti-social behaviour orders: intervention orders) of the Drugs Act 2005 shall come into force on 1st October 2006.

Joan Ryan

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

1st August 2006

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 20 of the Drugs Act 2005 into force on 1st October 2006.
