# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No.9 and Amendment) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 2182 (C. 74)

police, england and wales

The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No.9 and Amendment) Order 2006

Made

8th August 2006

Coming into force

15th August 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 178(8) and (9) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001]:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No.9 and Amendment) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 15th August 2006.

**2.** Section 163(2) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005, insofar as it relates to section 113B (10)(h) of the Police Act 1997, shall come into force on 25th September 2006 in England and Wales only.

**3.** In the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement) (No. 7) Order 2006[^f00002], for the words “(No. 7)” in the title and in article 1 (citation) substitute “(No. 8)”.

Joan Ryan

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

8th August 2006

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 163(2) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 into force on 25th September 2006, to the extent indicated in article 2. The effect is to bring into force, in England and Wales, provisions inserted by that section in the Police Act 1997 which make the British Transport Police a relevant police force for the purpose of obtaining information to be disclosed in an enhanced criminal record certificate under Part V of the 1997 Act.

The Order also re-numbers the [Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement) (No.7) Order 2006 (SI/1871)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/1871) as (No. 8), to take account of the previous [Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No.7) Order 2006 (SSI/381)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2006/381).
