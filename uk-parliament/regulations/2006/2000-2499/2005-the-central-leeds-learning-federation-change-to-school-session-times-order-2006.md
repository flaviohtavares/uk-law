# The Central Leeds Learning Federation (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 2005

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Central Leeds Learning Federation (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006

Made

20th July 2006

Laid before Parliament

28th July 2006

Coming into force

1st September 2006

The governing body of the Central Leeds Learning Federation, which comprises the City of Leeds School and the Primrose High School, (“the governing body”) have, in accordance with section 4(2) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001] (“the Act”), consulted the local education authority and such persons as appear to them to be appropriate, including the parents of registered pupils at the school and the staff working at the school;

It is the opinion of the Secretary of State, having regard to the matters set out in section 1(2) of the Act, that the implementation by the governing body of the provisions of this Order may contribute to the raising of educational standards achieved by children in England;

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 2(1) of the Act, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills, on the application of the governing body, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Central Leeds Learning Federation (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> September 2006.

## 2. Exemption from the School Session Regulations

The Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999[^f00002] shall not apply to the governing body in relation to the City of Leeds School[^f00003] of Bedford Field, Woodhouse Cliff, Leeds, West Yorkshire LS6 2LG and the Primrose High School[^f00004] of Hill Street, Leeds, West Yorkshire LS9 7NL.

## 3. Time period

This Order shall have effect until 31<sup>st</sup> December 2006.

Jim Knight

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

20th July 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under section 2 of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 provides that the Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999 do not apply to the governing body of the Central Leeds Learning Federation, which comprises the City of Leeds School and the Primrose High School. Article 3 specifies that the Order shall have effect until 31<sup>st</sup> December 2006.
