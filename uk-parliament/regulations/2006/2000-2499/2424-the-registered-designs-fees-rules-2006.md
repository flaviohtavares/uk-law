# The Registered Designs (Fees) Rules 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 2424

DESIGNS

The Registered Designs (Fees) Rules 2006

Made

6th September 2006

Laid before Parliament

8th September 2006

Coming into force

1st October 2006

The Secretary of State, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 36 and 40 of the Registered Designs Act 1949[^f00001] makes the following Rules.

He has had regard to the matters specified by article 6 of the Department of Trade and Industry (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Rules may be cited as the Registered Designs (Fees) Rules 2006 and shall come into force on 1st October 2006.

(2) In these Rules, “the Act” means the Registered Designs Act 1949 and “the Schedule” means the Schedule to these Rules.

## 2. Fees for design applications

The fee to be paid for an application for the registration of a design is—

- (a) for the first design included in an application, £60;
- (b) for any design included after the first design, £40.

## 3. Fees to extend the period for which right in the registered design subsists

(1) The fees to be paid to extend the period for which the right in a registered design subsists under section 8(2) of the Act are those specified in Part 2 of the Schedule.

(2) The additional fees prescribed for the purposes of section 8(4) of the Act are those specified in Part 3 of the Schedule.

(3) Where payment of the additional fee is made before the end of the month indicated in the first column of the table in Part 3, the fee to be paid is the amount specified in the second column.

## 4. Other fees payable

The fees to be paid in respect of other matters arising under the Act are those specified in Part 1 of the Schedule.

## 5. Fees to accompany forms

Where a form—

- (a) is required to be used by the Registered Design Rules 2006[^f00003], and
- (b) is specified in Part 1 of that Schedule as the corresponding form in relation to any matter,

that form shall be accompanied by the fee specified in respect of that matter.

## 6. Revocations

The Registered Designs (Fees) Rules 1998[^f00004] and the Registered Designs (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2001[^f00005] are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules prescribed the fees payable in respect of matters arising under the Registered Designs Act [1949 (c. 88)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1949/88). They reflect the changes made by the Regulatory Reform (Registered Designs) Order 2006 ([SI 2006/1974](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/1974)) and the new procedures under the Registered Designs Rules 2006 ([SI 2006/1975](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/1975)).

These Rules also revoke previous Rules relating to fees.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has been placed in the libraries of both Houses of Parliament. Copies of the assessment are available from the Patent Office website (www.patent.gov.uk) or by contacting the Trade Marks and Designs Directorate, The Patent Office, Concept House, Cardiff Road, Newport, NP10 8QQ.
