# The Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005 (Commencement No.3)(England) Order 2006

> Text as at 2006-07-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2006 No. 2006 (C. 69)

environmental protection, england

The Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005 (Commencement No.3)(England) Order 2006

Made

23rd July 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 108(1)(a) of the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005 (Commencement No.3)(England) Order 2006.

(2) In this Order “the 2005 Act” means the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005.

(3) This Order applies in England.

## 2. Commencement of section 84 of the 2005 Act

Section 84 of the 2005 Act, to the extent that it is not already in force[^f00002], shall come into force on 1st October 2006.

Ben Bradshaw

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

23rd July 2006

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force in England section 84 of the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act [2005 (c.16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/16) on 1st October 2006, to the extent that section is not already in force.
