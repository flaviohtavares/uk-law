# The Education (New Secondary School Proposals) (England) Regulations 2006, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Revocation and Savings

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) the Education (Additional Secondary School Proposals) Regulations 2003[^f00016] and the Education (Additional Secondary Schools) (Amendment) Regulations 2003[^f00017] are hereby revoked.

(2) Despite the repeal of section 70 of the 2002 Act [^f00018], the provisions of the Education (Additional Secondary School Proposals) Regulations 2003 and the Education (Additional Secondary Schools) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 shall continue to apply in relation to proposals made under section 70 of the 2002 Act where the notice published pursuant to subsection (1) of that section was published before 1st September 2006.
