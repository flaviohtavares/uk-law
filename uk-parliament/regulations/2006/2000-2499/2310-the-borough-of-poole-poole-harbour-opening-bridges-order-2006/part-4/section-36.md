# The Borough of Poole (Poole Harbour Opening Bridges) Order 2006, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Obstruction of authorised works and misuse of new bridge

Any person who without lawful authority or excuse—

- (a) obstructs another person from constructing any of the authorised works;
- (b) closes or opens the bridges; or
- (c) in any other way interferes with the bridges or their operation,

shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
