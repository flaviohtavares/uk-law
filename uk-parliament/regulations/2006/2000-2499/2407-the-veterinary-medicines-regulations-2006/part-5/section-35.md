# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2006, section 35

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Inspection of pharmacies[^key-cf060070a238ca44d1b8800937626e26]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In relation to a pharmacy, all the powers of an inspector to enforce these Regulations may also be exercised by an officer of the Royal Pharmaceutical Society of Great Britain or the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland appointed for the purpose.
