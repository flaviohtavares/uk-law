# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2006, section 39

> Text as at 2022-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Powers of a court on appeal[^key-0b0a8a6b9e1b1ab1c18cfcb14838bbc8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On an appeal against an improvement notice, the court may either cancel the notice or confirm it, with or without modification.
