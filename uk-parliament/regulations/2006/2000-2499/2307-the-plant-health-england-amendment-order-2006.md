[^key-cb925a8ce8466ae38f442f65f2399e73]EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Plant Health (England) Order 2005 ([SI 2005/2530](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2530)) (“the principal Order”) so as to implement—

Commission Directive [2005/77/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0077) amending Annex V to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread in the Community (OJ No. L296, 12.11.2005, p.17);

Commission Decision [2005/870/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0870) recognising Bulgaria as being free from *Clavibacter michiganensis* (Smith) Davis *et al*. spp *sepedonicus* (Spieckerman and Kotthoff) Davis *et al* (OJ No. L 319, 7.12.2005, p.9);

Commission Directive [2006/35/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0035) amending Annexes I to IV to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community (OJ No. L88, 25.3.2006, p.9);

Commission Decision [2006/473/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0473) recognising certain third countries and areas as being free from *Xanthomonas campestris* (all strains pathogenic to Citrus), *Cercospora angolensis* Carv. et Mendes and *Guignardia citricarpa* Kiely (all strains pathogenic to Citrus) (OJ No. L 187, 8.7.2006, p. 35);

Commission Decision [2006/464/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0464) on provisional emergency measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Community of *Dryocosmus kuriphilus* Yasumatsu (OJ No. L 183, 5.7.2006, p. 29).
