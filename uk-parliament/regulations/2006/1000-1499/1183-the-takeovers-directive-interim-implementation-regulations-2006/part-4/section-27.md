# The Takeovers Directive (Interim Implementation) Regulations 2006, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Summary financial statement

If, in accordance with section 251 of the Companies Act 1985[^f00024] (or as the case may be Article 259 of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00025]), a company sends to an entitled person a summary financial statement instead of a copy of its directors' report the company shall—

- (a) include in the statement the explanatory material required to be included in the directors' report by regulation 26(5); or
- (b) send that material to the entitled person at the same time as it sends the statement.

For the purposes of paragraph (b), section 251(2A) to (2E)[^f00026] (or as the case may be Article 259(2A) to (2E)[^f00027]) applies in relation to the material referred to in that paragraph as it applies in relation to a summary financial statement.
