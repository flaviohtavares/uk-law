# The Takeovers Directive (Interim Implementation) Regulations 2006, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Expressions in the Companies Act 1985

Except as otherwise provided expressions that are defined for the purposes of Part 7 of the Companies Act 1985[^f00028] (or in the case of Northern Ireland, Part 8 of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00029]) have the same meaning in this Part.

## Annotations

[^f00028]: Footnote: Part 7 was inserted by section 2 of the Companies Act [1989 (c. 40)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/40).

[^f00029]: Footnote: Part 8 was amended by Article 3 of [S.I. 1990/593 (N.I.5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1990/593).
