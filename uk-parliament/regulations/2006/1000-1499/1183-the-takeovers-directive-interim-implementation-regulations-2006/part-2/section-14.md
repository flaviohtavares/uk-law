# The Takeovers Directive (Interim Implementation) Regulations 2006, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Recovery of fees or charges

A fee or charge payable by any person by virtue of the rules is a debt due from that person to the Panel, and is recoverable accordingly.
