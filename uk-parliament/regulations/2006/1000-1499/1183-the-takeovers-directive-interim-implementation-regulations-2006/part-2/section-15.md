# The Takeovers Directive (Interim Implementation) Regulations 2006, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Panel as party to proceedings

In the exercise of its functions the Panel is capable (despite being an unincorporated body) of—

- (a) bringing proceedings under this Part in its own name;
- (b) bringing or defending any other proceedings in its own name.
