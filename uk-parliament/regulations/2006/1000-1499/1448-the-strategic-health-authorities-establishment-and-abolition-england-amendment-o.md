# The Strategic Health Authorities (Establishment and Abolition) (England) Amendment Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [SI 2006/1408](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/1408) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2006 No. 1448

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Strategic Health Authorities (Establishment and Abolition) (England) Amendment Order 2006

Made

31st May 2006

Laid before Parliament

7th June 2006

Coming into force

30th June 2006

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 8(4) and section 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001]. She is satisfied that consultation has taken place in accordance with regulations made under section 8(5) of that Act[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Strategic Health Authorities (Establishment and Abolition) (England) Amendment Order 2006 and shall come into force on 30th June 2006.

(2) In this Order, “the principal Order” means the Strategic Health Authorities (Establishment and Abolition) (England) Order 2006[^f00003].

## 2. Amendment of principal Order

In column (2) of Schedule 1 to the principal Order, for the words “North West Somerset” there shall be substituted the words “North Somerset”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Andrew Burnham

Minister of State

Department of Health

31st May 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Strategic Health Authorities (Establishment and Abolition) (England) Order 2006 by correcting the name of the County of North Somerset.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as it has no impact on the costs of business, charities or voluntary bodies.
