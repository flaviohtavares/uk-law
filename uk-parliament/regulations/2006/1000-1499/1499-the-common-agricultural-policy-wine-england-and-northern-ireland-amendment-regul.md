The Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d] [^c15887901] has been designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d] [^c15887911] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d] [^c15887921] and in relation to measures relating to food (including drink) including the primary production of food *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d] [^c15887931].

There has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of the following Regulations as required by Article 9 of Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d] [(EC) No. 178/2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2002/178)[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d] of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d] [^c15887941] laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety, as amended by Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d] [(EC) No. 1642/2003 *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2003/1642)[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d] of the European Parliament and of the Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d] [^c15887951].

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations under the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) (England and Northern Ireland) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 and come into force on 1st July 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

(2) In these Regulations “the 2001 Regulations” mean the Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) (England and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2001 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d] [^c15887961].

## 2. Amendment of the 2001 Regulations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

(1) The 2001 Regulations are amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

(2) In each of the provisions specified in paragraph (3), for the words “Wine Standards Board”, wherever occurring, substitute the word *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d] “ Agency ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d].

(3) The provisions referred to in paragraph (2) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

- (a) the definition of “enforcement authority” in regulation 2(1); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
- (b) regulations 3(1), (3), (4) and (5), 8(3), 9(4) and 18. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

(4) In regulation 2(1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

- (a) insert at the appropriate place the following definition — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

  > - “**the Agency *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]**” means the Food Standards Agency; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

  ; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
- (b) omit the definition of “the Wine Standards Board”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

(5) In regulation 3(6), omit the words “the Wine Standards Board or”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

(6) Substitute regulation 10 with the following regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

> **10. Review of prohibitions etc. on movement of wine-sector products (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]**
>
> (1) This regulation applies where an authorised officer of the Agency— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
>
> - (a) has given a notice under regulation 8(2) (“a regulation 8 notice”); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
> - (b) has refused to give consent to the movement of a controlled wine-sector product under regulation 9 (“a regulation 9 refusal”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
>
> (2) A person to whom a regulation 8 notice has been given, or who has been refused consent under regulation 9, may request a review of the notice or the refusal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
>
> (3) A request made by a person under paragraph (2) shall be made to the Agency within 6 months of the receipt of the regulation 8 notice or regulation 9 refusal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
>
> (4) Where a regulation 8 notice or regulation 9 refusal is sent by post in a properly addressed, pre-paid envelope then, unless the contrary is proved, the notice or refusal, as the case may be, is deemed to have been received on the next working day where it is sent by first class post and on the next but one working day where it is sent by second class post. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
>
> (5) Where a request is made under paragraph (2) within the period specified in paragraph (3) — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
>
> - (a) the Agency shall nominate a person from the list established under paragraph (6) to determine whether the regulation 8 notice or regulation 9 refusal, as the case may be, should stand; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
> - (b) the person nominated shall give the person and the Agency an opportunity to make representations on the matter; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
> - (c) the person nominated shall, within 1 month of being nominated, decide whether the regulation 8 notice or regulation 9 refusal should stand and shall notify the applicant and the Agency of his decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
>
> (6) The Agency shall establish and maintain a list of people who may be nominated for the purposes of conducting a review under this regulation and shall consult those organisations appearing to it to represent the wine industry before including any person on the list. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

.

## 3. Transitional provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

(1) Anything done by or in relation to the Wine Standards Board in the exercise of its functions under the 2001 Regulations before the coming into force of these Regulations shall have effect as if done by or in relation to the Food Standards Agency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

(2) Anything which at the time of the coming into force of these Regulations is in the process of being done by or in relation to the Wine Standards Board in the exercise of its functions under the 2001 Regulations may be continued by or in relation to the Food Standards Agency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

(3) Any reference to the Wine Standards Board in any document relating to anything to which paragraph (1) or (2) applies shall, so far as necessary for giving effect to those provisions, be construed as a reference to the Food Standards Agency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

These Regulations further amend the Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) (England and Northern Ireland) Regulations 2001 (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]**the 2001 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]**”), which provide for the enforcement in England and Northern Ireland of European Community Regulations concerned with the production and marketing of wine and related products. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

These Regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]

- (a) replace references to the Wine Standards Board with references to the Food Standards Agency (regulation 2(1) to (3)) thereby making the Food Standards Agency responsible for the enforcement functions under the 2001 Regulations that were previously the responsibility of the Wine Standards Board; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
- (b) make other consequential amendments arising as a result of the changes made in relation to those enforcement functions (regulation 2(4) and (5)); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
- (c) put in place a different procedure for the review of prohibitions etc. on the movement of wine-sector products (regulation 2(6)); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
- (d) contain transitional provisions (regulation 3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d963b9c15a299cc6c17e61f0953ad82d]
