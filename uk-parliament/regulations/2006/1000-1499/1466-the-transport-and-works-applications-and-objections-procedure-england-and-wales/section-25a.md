## 25A. Determination following hearing[^c-076d3cb345231edcefc118e00c245447]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where the Secretary of State has given an objector an opportunity of appearing before and being heard by a person appointed pursuant to section 11(2).

(2) The Secretary of State must determine the application by the end of the period of three months beginning with the later of—

- (a) the date on which the Secretary of State receives the report of the person appointed; and
- (b) the date on which the Secretary of State receives the last of any additional information requested by the Secretary of State following the hearing.

(3) The Secretary of State must notify the applicant and any objector of the date for determination of the application.
