## 25B. Procedure following quashing of decision[^c-076d3cb345231edcefc118e00c245447]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a determination of an application is quashed in proceedings before any court, the Secretary of State—

- (a) must send to the applicant and those persons who submitted objections a written statement of the matters with respect to which further representations are invited for the purpose of further consideration of the application; and
- (b) must afford to those persons the opportunity of making written representations in respect of those matters.

(2) Any persons making representations under paragraph (1)(b) must submit such representations to the Secretary of State within 3 weeks of the date of the written statement sent under paragraph (1)(a).
