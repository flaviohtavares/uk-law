# The Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006, section 25

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. Submission of written objections and other representations to inquiry or hearing

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the Secretary of State causes a public local inquiry to be held or gives an objector an opportunity of being heard before a person appointed by the Secretary of State, the Secretary of State shall submit every objection and other representation made pursuant to rule 21 either to the inquiry inspector or the person so appointed, as the case may be, as soon as it is reasonably practicable to do so.
