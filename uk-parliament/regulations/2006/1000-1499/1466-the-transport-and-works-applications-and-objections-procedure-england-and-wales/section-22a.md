# The Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006, section 22A

## 22A. No objections or other representations received[^c-3986b336134b269768575a474113d2bf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where—

- (a) no other representation complying with rule 21(1) or (2) is received; and
- (b) either—
  - (i) no objection complying with rule 21(1) or (2) is received; or
  - (ii) it appears to the Secretary of State that each objection received which complies with rule 21(1) or (2) is of a type mentioned in section 10(3)(a) or (b),

this rule applies.

(2) The Secretary of State must determine the application by the end of the period of three months beginning with the later of—

- (a) the expiry date for objections; and
- (b) the date on which the Secretary of State receives the last of any additional information requested following the expiry date for objections.

(3) The Secretary of State must notify the applicant of the date for determination of the application.

(4) Where paragraph (1)(b)(ii) applies, the Secretary of State must also notify each objector—

- (a) that the objection appears to the Secretary of State to be of a type mentioned in section 10(3)(a) or (b); and
- (b) of the date for determination of the application.
