## 4A. Pre-application consultation[^c-e591380a7a4d56964e6b5f590938cd62]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a proposed application includes a request for—

- (a) a deemed marine licence under section 19A(1); or
- (b) a deemed marine licence under section 19A(3),

the applicant must consult the bodies identified in paragraph (2) before submitting pre-application documents pursuant to rule 5.

(2) The bodies referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) in respect of sub-paragraph (a), the Marine Management Organisation; and
- (b) in respect of sub-paragraph (b), Natural Resources Wales.
