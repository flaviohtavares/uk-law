# The Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006, section 5

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Pre-application draft order and explanatory memorandum

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The applicant shall send to the Secretary of State not later than 28 days before making an application—

- (a) a draft of the proposed order; and
- (b) a draft of the explanatory memorandum,

that the applicant intends to submit in pursuance of rule 10(2).

(1A)[^c-3c73217bd1c91796c0c68f3424d353fc] Where the draft of the proposed order referred to in paragraph (1)(a) includes provision deeming a marine licence to have been issued under Part 4 of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009, the applicant must send to the Marine Management Organisation (in relation to England) or Natural Resources Wales (in relation to Wales), a draft of the proposed marine licence not later than 28 days before making an application.

(2) Where an applicant has sent drafts to the Secretary of State in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (1), but subsequently amends any of those drafts, he shall not be required to send any amended drafts to the Secretary of State before making an application unless the applicant is of the opinion that the changes to the draft substantially alter the nature or effect of the proposed order.
