## 22B. Withdrawal of objections and other representations[^c-3986b336134b269768575a474113d2bf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where all objections and other representations complying with rule 21(1) or (2) which have been received are withdrawn before the operative date.

(2) Where paragraph (1) applies, the Secretary of State must determine the application by the end of the period of three months beginning with the later of—

- (a) the date on which the last objection or other representation was withdrawn; and
- (b) the date on which the Secretary of State receives the last of any additional information requested following that withdrawal.

(3) The Secretary of State must notify the applicant of the date for determination of the application.
