# The Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006, section 26

## 26. Power to set later time limits

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-fd0b8ba8210c89357b63ef512b6957f8] The Secretary of State may in a particular case allow further time for the taking of such steps (including a step which he is required or enabled to take himself) which may or must be taken pursuant to these Rules and any reference to a date by which or a period within which any step is required to be taken shall be construed accordingly.

(2)[^c-ec725ef9ee6db5713075d308654d1eac] Where the Secretary of State allows further time for the determination of an application, the Secretary of State must notify the applicant and any objector of the revised date for determination of the application.
