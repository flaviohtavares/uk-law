# The Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006, section 26

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 26. Power to set later time limits

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State may in a particular case allow further time for the taking of such steps (including a step which he is required or enabled to take himself) which may or must be taken pursuant to these Rules and any reference to a date by which or a period within which any step is required to be taken shall be construed accordingly.
