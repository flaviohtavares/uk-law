# The Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006, section 1

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) These Rules may be cited as the Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006.

(2) These Rules shall come into force on 11th September 2006.

## 1.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Construction of a transport system or inland waterway which would involve any compulsory acquisition of land but would not involve any interference with rights of navigation
