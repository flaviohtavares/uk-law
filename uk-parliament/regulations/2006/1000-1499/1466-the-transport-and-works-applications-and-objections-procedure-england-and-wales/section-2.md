# The Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006, section 2

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2. Revocation

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to rule 3, the Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2000 [^c15886481] (“**the 2000 Rules**”) are hereby revoked.

## 2.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Construction of a transport system or inland waterway which would not require the compulsory acquisition of land and not interfere with rights of navigation
