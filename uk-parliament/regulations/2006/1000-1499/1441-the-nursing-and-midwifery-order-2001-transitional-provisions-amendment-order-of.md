# The Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001 (Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order of Council 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 1441

HEALTH CARE AND ASSOCIATED PROFESSIONS

NURSES AND MIDWIVES

The Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001 (Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order of Council 2006

Made

24th May 2006

Coming into force

1st June 2006

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 24th day of May 2006

By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Their Lordships make the following Order in exercise of their powers conferred under article 54(2) of the Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001 (Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order of Council 2006 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> June 2006.

## 2. Amendment of the Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001 (Transitional Provisions) Order of Council 2004

(1) The Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001 (Transitional Provisions) Order of Council 2004[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 4 (modification of the Conduct Rules)—

- (a) before paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (za) in rule 7—
  >   - (i) in paragraph (1), for the words “constituted by, and shall include members of, the Council” were substituted “constituted by the Council”, and
  >   - (ii) in paragraph (5), the words “, of whom at least 1 shall be a Council member” were omitted;

  ;
- (b) after paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (aa) in rule 12—
  >   - (i) in paragraph (1), for the words “constituted by, and shall include members of, the Council” were substituted “constituted by the Council”, and

  ; and
- (c) after paragraph (c) insert—

  > - (ca) in rule 29—
  >   - (i) in paragraph (1), for the words “constituted by, and include members of, the Council” were substituted “constituted by the Council”, and
  >   - (ii) in paragraph (6), the words “, of whom at least 1 shall be a Council member” were omitted;

  .

A K Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001 (Transitional Provisions) Order of Council 2004. That Order made transitional provisions arising out of the implementation of the Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001. The effect of the amendments is that, in relation to outstanding cases relating to the fitness to practise of nurses and midwives, it is no longer a requirement that a member of the Nursing and Midwifery Council should be a member of the Preliminary Proceedings Committee, the Professional Conduct Committee or the Health Committee.
