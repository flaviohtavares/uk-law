# The Identity Cards Act 2006 (Commencement No.1) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 1439(C. 49)

IDENTITY CARDS

The Identity Cards Act 2006 (Commencement No.1) Order 2006

Made

31st May 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 44(3) of the Identity Cards Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Identity Cards Act 2006 (Commencement No.1) Order 2006.

## 2. Commencement

The following provisions of the Identity Cards Act 2006 shall come into force on 7th June 2006—

- (a) section 25 (possession of false identity documents etc.);
- (b) section 26 (identity documents for the purposes of s.25);
- (c) section 30 (amendments relating to offences), except for the purposes of the references to sections 27 and 28 of the Identity Cards Act 2006 in the amendment made by subsection (4)[^f00002];
- (d) section 40 (orders and regulations);
- (e) Schedule 2 (repeals); and
- (f) sections 1(5) to (8) (definition of registrable fact) and 42 (interpretation) so far as necessary for the interpretation of the provisions specified above.

Tony McNulty

Minister of State

Home Office

31st May 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on 7th June 2006, the provisions of the Identity Cards Act 2006 set out in article 2.
