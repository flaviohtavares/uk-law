# The Cross-Border Insolvency Regulations 2006, section 3

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Modification of British insolvency law

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) British insolvency law (as defined in article 2 of the UNCITRAL Model Law as set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations) and Part 3 of the Insolvency Act 1986 [^c15462841] shall apply with such modifications as the context requires for the purpose of giving effect to the provisions of these Regulations.

(2) In the case of any conflict between any provision of British insolvency law or of Part 3 of the Insolvency Act 1986 and the provisions of these Regulations, the latter shall prevail.
