# The Cross-Border Insolvency Regulations 2006, section 8

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 8. Disapplication of section 388 of the Insolvency Act 1986

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing in section 388 of the Insolvency Act 1986 [^c15462851] applies to anything done by a foreign representative—

- (a) under or by virtue of these Regulations;
- (b) in relation to relief granted or cooperation or coordination provided under these Regulations.
