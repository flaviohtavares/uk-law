# The Town and Country Planning (Application of Subordinate Legislation to the Crown) Order 2006, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (England and Wales) Regulations 1999

(1) The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (England and Wales) Regulations 1999[^f00025] apply to the Crown with the following modifications.

(2) In regulation 8 (application referred to the Secretary of State without an environmental statement)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) before “referred” insert “made or”; and
  - (ii) before “referral” insert “making or the”; and
- (b) in paragraph (2) before “referred” insert “made or”.
