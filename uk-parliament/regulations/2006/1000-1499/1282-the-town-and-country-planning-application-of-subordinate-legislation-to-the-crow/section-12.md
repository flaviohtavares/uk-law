# The Town and Country Planning (Application of Subordinate Legislation to the Crown) Order 2006, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Town and Country Planning General Regulations 1992

Regulation 16 of, and Schedule 2 to, the Town and Country Planning General Regulations 1992[^f00013] (notices and counter-notices relating to planning blight) apply to the Crown.
