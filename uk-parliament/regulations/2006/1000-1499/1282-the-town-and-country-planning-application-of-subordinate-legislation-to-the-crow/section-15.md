# The Town and Country Planning (Application of Subordinate Legislation to the Crown) Order 2006, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994

Part 4 of the Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994[^f00016] (adaptation of planning and other controls) applies to the Crown as if, in regulation 54(1) (grant of planning permission), after sub-paragraph (a) there were inserted—

> - (aa) granting planning permission on an application under section 293A[^f00017] of that Act (urgent Crown development);

.
