# The Town and Country Planning (Application of Subordinate Legislation to the Crown) Order 2006, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992

The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992[^f00012] except for regulation 27 (contravention of Regulations) apply to the Crown.
