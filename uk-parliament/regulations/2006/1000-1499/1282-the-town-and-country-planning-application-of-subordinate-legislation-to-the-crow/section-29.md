# The Town and Country Planning (Application of Subordinate Legislation to the Crown) Order 2006, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Landfill (England and Wales) Regulations 2002

The Landfill (England and Wales) Regulations 2002[^f00039] apply to the Crown insofar as they relate to planning permission for landfill.

## Annotations

[^f00039]: Footnote: S.I. [2002/1559](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1559) to which there are amendments not relevant to this instrument. See regulation 5 and paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 2 which impose restrictions on the grant of planning permission for landfill.
