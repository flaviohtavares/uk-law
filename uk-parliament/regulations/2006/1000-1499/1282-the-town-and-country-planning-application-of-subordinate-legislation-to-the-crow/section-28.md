# The Town and Country Planning (Application of Subordinate Legislation to the Crown) Order 2006, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Town and Country Planning (Major Infrastructure Project Inquiries Procedure) (England) Rules 2002

(1) The Town and Country Planning (Major Infrastructure Project Inquiries Procedure) (England) Rules 2002[^f00036] apply to the Crown with the following modifications.

(2) After rule 25 (Mayor of London) insert—

> **25A. Modifications where national security direction given**
>
> The modifications set out in the Schedule shall have effect where a direction is given by the Secretary of State under—
>
> - (a) section 321(3) of the Planning Act[^f00037] (planning inquiries to be held in public subject to certain exceptions); or
> - (b) paragraph 6(6) of Schedule 3 to the Listed Buildings Act[^f00038] (determination of certain appeals by person appointed by the Secretary of State).

.

(3) At the end of the Rules add the Schedule set out in Schedule 3 to this Order.
