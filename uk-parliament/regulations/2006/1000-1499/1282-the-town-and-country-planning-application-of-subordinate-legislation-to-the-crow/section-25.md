## 25. Town and Country Planning Appeals (Determination by Inspectors) (Inquiries Procedure) (England) Rules 2000

The Town and Country Planning Appeals (Determination by Inspectors) (Inquiries Procedure) (England) Rules 2000[^f00033] apply to the Crown.
