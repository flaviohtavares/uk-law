# The Town and Country Planning (Application of Subordinate Legislation to the Crown) Order 2006, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Local Land Charges Rules 1977

The Local Land Charges Rules 1977[^f00005] apply to the Crown insofar as they relate to planning charges.
