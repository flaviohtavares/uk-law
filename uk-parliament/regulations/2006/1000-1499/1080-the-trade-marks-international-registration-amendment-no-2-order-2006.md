# The Trade Marks (International Registration)(Amendment No.2) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct errors in [SI 1996/714](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/714) caused by amendments made to that Statutory Instrument by [SI 2006/763](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/763). This Statutory Instrument is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of [SI 2006/763](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/763).

2006 No. 1080

TRADE MARKS

The Trade Marks (International Registration)(Amendment No.2) Order 2006

Made

7th April 2006

Laid before Parliament

11th April 2006

Coming into force

12th April 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 54 of the Trade Marks Act 1994[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Trade Marks (International Registration) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 12th April 2006.

**2.** In article 10A(1A) and article 13(1B) of the Trade Marks (International Registration) Order 1996[^f00002], for “comptroller” substitute “registrar”.

Barry Gardiner

Minister for Competitiveness

Department of Trade and Industry

7th April 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order correct articles 10A(1A) and 13(1B) of the Trade Marks (International Registration) Order 1996 ([SI 1996/714](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/714)). These provisions were amended incorrectly by the Trade Marks (International Registration) (Amendment) Order ([SI 2006/763](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/763)). This Order substitutes the word “registrar” for the word “comptroller” in those articles.
