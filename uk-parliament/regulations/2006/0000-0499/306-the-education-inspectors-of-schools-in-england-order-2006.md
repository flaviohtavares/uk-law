# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 306

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2006

Made

14th February 2006

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of February 2006

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the power conferred on Her by section 1(2) of the Education Act 2005[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2006.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — Persons appointed

Article 2

- Susan Clipson-Boyles
- Wendy Ripley
- Jane Melbourne
- Maureen Roberts
- Rashida Sharif
- Michael Lynes
- Fergus Currie
- Marie McGuinness
- Christine Davies

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England.
