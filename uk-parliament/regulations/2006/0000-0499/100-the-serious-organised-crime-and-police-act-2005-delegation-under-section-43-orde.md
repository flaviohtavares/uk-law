# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Delegation under section 43) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 100

SERIOUS ORGANISED CRIME AGENCY

The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Delegation under section 43) Order 2006

Made

23rd January 2006

Laid before Parliament

25th January 2006

Coming into force

1st March 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 44(2) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Delegation under section 43) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st March 2006.

**2.** The prescribed level for the purposes of section 44(1) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 is the grade of Deputy Director of SOCA.

Paul Goggins

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

23rd January 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes the grade of Deputy Director of the Serious Organised Crime Agency (“SOCA”) as the prescribed level for the purposes of section 44(1) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005. The effect of section 44 of that Act is that the Director General of SOCA cannot delegate his functions under section 43 (designation of SOCA staff as persons having the powers of constable etc.) to an employee of that Agency who is employed in a grade lower than the grade of Deputy Director of SOCA.
