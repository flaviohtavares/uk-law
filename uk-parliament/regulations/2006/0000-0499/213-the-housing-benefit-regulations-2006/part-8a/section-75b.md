# The Housing Benefit Regulations 2006, section 75B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/213
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 75B. Determinations[^key-a32b4347e5e0f4cdf0adc7d5c4f54d08][^key-3300f6a4af88852333099a324a65cb9a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A relevant authority may make a determination as to whether the benefit cap applies or whether to change the amount of any reduction made in accordance with regulation 75D (reduction of housing benefit) but a relevant authority is not required to do so unless paragraph (2) applies.

(2) This paragraph applies where a relevant authority receives notification from the Secretary of State that—

- (a) the benefit cap may apply;
- (b) there has been a change in the amount of a welfare benefit to which a claimant is entitled; or
- (c) there has been a change to the relevant amount which may affect the amount of housing benefit to which a claimant is entitled.
