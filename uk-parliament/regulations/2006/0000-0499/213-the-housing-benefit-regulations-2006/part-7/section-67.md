## 67. Further disregard of student's income

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where any part of a student's income has already been taken into account for the purposes of assessing his entitlement to a grant , student loan or postgraduate[^c-136bbaf73212acb068171f3a1af782d5] [^c-70365982b4af1a004bfbad24ece06096]... loan, the amount taken into account shall be disregarded in assessing that student's income.
