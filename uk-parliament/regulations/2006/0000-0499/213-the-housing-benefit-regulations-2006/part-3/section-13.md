## 13. Maximum rent

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59] Where an authority has applied to the rent officer for a determination in accordance with regulation 14 (requirement to refer to rent officers) and a rent officer has made a determination or redetermination in exercise of the Housing Act functions, the maximum rent shall be determined in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (17). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(2) In a case where the rent officer has determined a claim-related rent, but is not required to notify the authority of a local reference rent or a single room rent, the maximum rent shall be that claim-related rent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(3) In a case where the rent officer has determined and is required to notify the authority of a local reference rent, the maximum rent shall not exceed twice that local reference rent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(4) Subject to paragraph (5), in the case of a young individual— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

- (a) except where sub-paragraph (b) applies, where the rent officer has determined a single room rent and is required to notify the authority of it, the maximum rent shall not exceed that single room rent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- (b) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
  - (i) the rent officer has determined a single room rent and a claim-related rent and is required to notify the authority of them; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59] the claim-related rent includes payment in respect of meals; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59] the single room rent is greater than the claim-related rent less an amount in respect of meals determined in accordance with paragraph 2 of Part 1 of Schedule 1 (ineligible service charges), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

  the maximum rent shall not exceed the claim-related rent less that amount in respect of meals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(5) Paragraph (4) shall not apply in the case of a claimant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

- (a) to whom paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 to the Consequential Provisions Regulations (saving provision) applies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- (b) to whom paragraph 14 of Schedule 3 (severe disability premium) applies; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- (c) who has a non-dependant residing with him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(6) Subject to the limits specified in paragraphs (3) and (4), in a case where the rent officer has determined both a local reference rent of which he is required to notify the authority and a claim-related rent, and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

- (a) the claim-related rent is higher than the local reference rent, the maximum rent shall be the local reference rent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- (b) the local reference rent is higher than the claim-related rent, the maximum rent shall be the claim-related rent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(7) Subject to the limits specified in paragraphs (3) and (4), in a case where the rent officer has determined a local reference rent of which he is required to notify the authority, but has not determined a claim-related rent and the reckonable rent is more than the local reference rent, the maximum rent shall be the local reference rent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(8) In a case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

- (a) the authority has determined a maximum rent in respect of a dwelling; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- (b) during the award of housing benefit the reckonable rent in respect of that dwelling is reduced to a sum which is less than the reckonable rent at the time that maximum rent was determined, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

- (i) the maximum rent shall not be reduced, where the sum is not less than the maximum rent, during a period ending on the effective date of a decision adopting a determination of a rent officer where that determination was made in exercise of the Housing Act functions pursuant to an application by the authority under regulation 14(1)(c), (d), (e), (f) or (g); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59] the maximum rent shall be reduced to an amount equal to that sum, where that sum is less than the maximum rent during a period ending on the effective date of a decision adopting a determination of a rent officer where that determination was made in exercise of the Housing Act functions pursuant to an application by the authority under regulation 14(1)(c), (d), (e), (f) or (g). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(9) Subject to paragraph (10), in a case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

- (a) a rent officer has made a determination in exercise of the Housing Act functions pursuant to an application by an authority under regulation 14(1)(e); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- (b) subsequent to that determination the reckonable rent for that dwelling is changed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

then in determining a maximum rent in relation to a claim for benefit of a claimant who has a liability to make payments in respect of that dwelling, the authority shall treat the claim-related rent or, as the case may be, reckonable rent to be that determined in or, as the case may be, applicable to, that determination by the rent officer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(10) Paragraph (9) shall not apply in a case where the reckonable rent is reduced to a figure below the figure that would have been the maximum rent if that reckonable rent had not changed; and where this paragraph applies, the maximum rent shall be the reckonable rent, as so reduced. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(11) In a case where the claimant occupies a dwelling which is the same as that occupied by him at the date of death of any person to whom paragraph (16)(b) to (d) applied or, had a claim been made, would have applied, the maximum rent shall be either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

- (a) the maximum rent which applied before the death occurred; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- (b) in a case where there was no maximum rent, the reckonable rent due before the death occurred, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

for a period of 12 months from the date of such a death. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(12) For the purposes of paragraph (11), a claimant shall be treated as occupying the dwelling if paragraph (13) of regulation 7 (circumstances in which a person is or is not to be treated as occupying a dwelling as his home) is satisfied and for that purpose sub-paragraph (b) of that paragraph of that regulation shall be treated as if it were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(13) In a case where a charge for meals is ineligible to be met by housing benefit under regulation 12(3) and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1, there shall be deducted an amount determined in accordance with paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 in respect of meals in the calculation of a person's maximum rent, except where the maximum rent is derived from a rent officer determination under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

- (a) paragraph 3 (exceptional high rents) of Schedule 1 to the Rent Officers Order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59] [^c15407101] and the notice of claim-related rent states pursuant to paragraph 9(1)(c) of that Schedule that an ineligible payment has not been included in it; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- (b) paragraph 5 (single room rents) of that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(14) Subject to paragraph (15), where the relevant authority is satisfied that a person to whom paragraph (16) applies was able to meet the financial commitments for his dwelling when they were entered into, there shall be no maximum rent during the first 13 weeks of the claimant's award of housing benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(15) Paragraph (14) shall not apply where a claimant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59], or the claimant’s partner,[^c-97f603dbc6a183ffdf2758182033c58b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59] was previously entitled to benefit in respect of an award of housing benefit which fell wholly or partly less than 52 weeks before the commencement of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59] the claimant’s[^c-4de14169801e02c4757798b4839e9901] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59] current award of housing benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(16) This paragraph applies to the following persons— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

- (a) the claimant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- (b) any member of his family; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- (c) if the claimant is a member of a polygamous marriage, any partners of his and any child or young person for whom he or a partner is responsible and who is a member of the same household; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- (d) subject to paragraph (17), any relative of the claimant or his partner who occupies the same dwelling as the claimant, whether or not they reside with him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(17) Paragraph (16)(d) shall only apply to a relative who has no separate right of occupation of the dwelling which would enable him to continue to occupy it even if the claimant ceased his occupation of it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(18) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

- “**claim related rent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]**” means the rent notified by the rent officer under paragraph 9(1) of Schedule 1 to the Rent Officers Order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- “**deduction for meals *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]**” means any amount of a person's otherwise eligible rent which is an ineligible service charge by reason of and within the meaning of paragraph 1(a)(i) of Schedule 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- “**local reference rent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]**” means the rent determined by a rent officer under paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 to the Rent Officers Order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- “**reckonable rent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]**” means those payments, which a person is liable to make in respect of the dwelling which he occupies as his home, and which are eligible, or would, but for this regulation, be eligible for housing benefit plus the amount of any deduction for fuel, deduction for meals or water charges, as the case may be, which that person is liable to pay; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]
- “**single room rent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]**” means the rent determined by a rent officer under paragraph 5 of Schedule 1 to the Rent Officers Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2fdd98c383293136721c20e622da6b59]

(1) The maximum rent shall be determined in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (8) where—

- (a) a local authority has applied for a determination in accordance with regulation 14 (requirement to refer to rent officers), a redetermination in accordance with regulation 15 or 16, or a substitute determination or substitute redetermination in accordance with regulation 17 and a rent officer has made a determination, redetermination, substitute determination or substitute redetermination in exercise of the Housing Act functions; or
- (b) an authority is not required to apply to the rent officer for a determination because—
  - (i) regulation 14(2)(a) applies; or
  - (ii) regulation 14(2)(b) applies because paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 2 applies.

(2) In a case where the rent officer has determined a claim-related rent, but is not required to notify the relevant authority of a local reference rent or a single room rent, the maximum rent shall be that claim-related rent.

(3) Subject to the limit specified in paragraph (4), in a case where the rent officer has determined both a local reference rent of which he is required to notify the relevant authority and a claim-related rent, the maximum rent shall be the local reference rent.

(4) In a case to which paragraph 8 of Schedule 3 to the Consequential Provisions Regulations applies, where the rent officer has determined and is required to notify the relevant authority of a local reference rent the maximum rent shall not exceed twice that local reference rent.

(5) Subject to paragraph (6), in the case of a young individual—

- (a) except where sub-paragraph (b) applies, where the rent officer has determined a single room rent and is required to notify the relevant authority of it, the maximum rent shall not exceed that single room rent;
- (b) where—
  - (i) the rent officer has determined a single room rent and a claim-related rent and is required to notify the authority of them;
  - (ii) the claim-related rent includes payment in respect of meals; and
  - (iii) the single room rent is greater than the claim-related rent less an amount in respect of meals determined in accordance with paragraph 2 of Part 1 of Schedule 1 (ineligible service charges),

  the maximum rent shall not exceed the claim-related rent less that amount in respect of meals.

(6) Paragraph (5) shall not apply in the case of a claimant—

- (a) to whom paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 to the Consequential Provisions Regulations (saving provision) applies;
- (b) to whom paragraph 14 of Schedule 3 (severe disability premium) applies; or
- (c) where a non-dependant resides with him.

(7) Where the maximum rent is derived from—

- (a) a claim-related rent and the notification under paragraph 9(1)(c) of Schedule 1 to the Rent Officers Order states that an ineligible amount in respect of meals has been included in that claim-related rent; or
- (b) a local reference rent and the notification under paragraph 9(1)(da) of Schedule 1 to the Rent Officers Order states that an ineligible amount in respect of meals has been included in that local reference rent,

in determining the maximum rent the relevant authority shall deduct an amount determined in accordance with paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 to these Regulations in respect of meals.

(8) This regulation is subject to regulations 13ZA (protection on death and 13 week protection) and 13ZB (change in reckonable rent).

(9) In this regulation—

- “claim-related rent” means the rent notified by the rent officer under paragraph 9(1) of Schedule 1 to the Rent Officers Order;
- “local reference rent” means the rent determined by a rent officer under paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 to the Rent Officers Order.
