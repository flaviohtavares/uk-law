## 18. Application of provisions to substitute determinations or substitute redeterminations

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulations 15, 16 and 17 apply to a substitute determination or substitute redetermination as they apply to the determination or redetermination it replaces. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9fddb627adac6742714febe185227e4d]
