## 15. Applications to the rent officer for redeterminations

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-9fddb627adac6742714febe185227e4d] Subject to paragraph (2) and regulation 16, where a relevant authority has obtained from a rent officer either or both of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9fddb627adac6742714febe185227e4d]

- (a) a determination on a reference made under regulation 14 (requirement to refer to rent officers); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9fddb627adac6742714febe185227e4d]
- (b) a redetermination on a reference made under regulation 16(2)(application for redetermination by rent officer), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9fddb627adac6742714febe185227e4d]

the authority may apply to the rent officer for a redetermination of any determination or redetermination he has made which has effect at the date of the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9fddb627adac6742714febe185227e4d]

(2) No application shall be made for a further redetermination of a redetermination made in response to an application under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9fddb627adac6742714febe185227e4d]

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) and regulation 16 (application for redetermination by rent officer), where a relevant authority has obtained from a rent officer either or both of the following—

- (a) a determination on a reference made under regulation 13D(10) (board and attendance determination) or regulation 14 (requirement to refer to rent officers);
- (b) a redetermination on a reference made under regulation 16(2) (application for redetermination by rent officer),

the authority may apply to the rent officer for a redetermination of any determination or redetermination he has made which has effect at the date of the application.

(2) No application shall be made for a further redetermination of a redetermination made in response to an application under paragraph (1).
