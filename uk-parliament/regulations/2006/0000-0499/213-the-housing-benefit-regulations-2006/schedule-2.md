# The Housing Benefit Regulations 2006, SCHEDULE 2

## SCHEDULE 2 (affected by repeal, see note) — Excluded tenancies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb][^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 14 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

**1.** An excluded tenancy is any tenancy to which any of the following paragraphs applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

**2.** (1) Subject to the following sub-paragraphs, where a rent officer has made a determination, which relates to the tenancy in question or any other tenancy of the same dwelling this paragraph applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

- (a) the tenancy in respect of which that determination was made; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (b) any other tenancy of the same dwelling on terms which are substantially the same, other than the term relating to the amount of rent, as those terms were at the time of that determination or, if earlier, at the end of the tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

(2) For the purposes of any claim, notification, request or application under regulation 14(1) (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]**the later application *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]**”), a tenancy shall not be an excluded tenancy by virtue of sub-paragraph (1) by reference to a rent officer's determination made in consequence of an earlier claim, notification, request or application (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]**the earlier application *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]**”) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

- (a) the earlier and later applications were made in respect of the same claimant or different claimants; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (b) the earlier application was made more than 52 weeks before the later application was made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

(3) Sub-paragraph (1) shall not apply where subsequent to the making of the determination mentioned in that sub-paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

- (a) the number of occupiers of the dwelling has changed and that dwelling is not in a hostel; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (b) there has been a substantial change in the condition of the dwelling (including the making of improvements) or the terms of the tenancy other than a term relating to rent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (c) there has been a rent increase under a term of the tenancy and the term under which that increase was made was either included in the tenancy at the time when the application for that determination was made (or was a term substantially the same as such a term) and that determination was not made under paragraph 1(2), 2(2) or 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the Rent Officers Order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (d) in a case where the rent officer has made a determination under paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 1 to the Rent Officers Order (size and rent determinations), but since the date of the application for that determination— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
  - (i) a child, who is a member of the household occupying the dwelling, has attained the age of 10 years; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] a young person, who is a member of the household occupying that dwelling, has attained the age of 16 years; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] there is a change in the composition of the household occupying the dwelling; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (e) the claimant is a young individual, except in a case where the determination mentioned in sub-paragraph (1) was, or was made in conjunction with, a determination of a single room rent pursuant to paragraph 5 of Schedule 1 to the Rent Officers Order on or after 2nd July 2001. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (f)[^c-3231c564466a8bacbb696ee22832dc56] any person mentioned in paragraph 1A(a) of Schedule 2 to the Rent Officers Order becomes or ceases to be a person who requires overnight care where that affects the size criteria, as set out in that Schedule to that Order, applicable in the claimant’s case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (g)[^c-e18c99d7cccbf90cfb79d929563e2edd] the claimant or the claimant’s partner becomes, or ceases to be, a qualifying parent or carer where that affects the size criteria, as set out in Schedule 2 to the Rent Officers Order, applicable in the claimant’s case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (h)[^c-58f3da48b8d3d07aa8976bd34d221b35] an occupier becomes or ceases to be a child who cannot share a bedroom *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] or a member of a couple who cannot share a bedroom[^c-a398c9a890e4936e1484f3269b3bdf2c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] where that affects the size criteria, as set out in Schedule 2 to the Rent Officers Order, applicable in the claimant’s case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

(4)[^c-6952ccd6648a5855c8ebdce032cb7f10] For the purposes of sub-paragraph (3)(d)(iii) it does not amount to a change in the composition of the household where a son, daughter, step-son or step-daughter of the claimant or the claimant’s partner who is the claimant’s non-dependant ceases to occupy the dwelling as their home because they become a member of the armed forces away on operations, or subsequently resumes occupying the dwelling as their home on ceasing to be a member of the armed forces away on operations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

**3.**[^c-11d4d5d5512b883cd35fe4667a583731] (1) Subject to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] sub-paragraphs (1A) and (2)[^c-e1c8320abbde4a36014ba6929a8af06c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb], this paragraph applies where the landlord is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

- (a) a registered housing association; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (b) a county council, with regard to gypsies’ and travellers’ caravan or mobile home sites and caravans or mobile homes provided on those sites; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (c) a housing authority, with regard to caravan or mobile home sites or houseboat moorings, payments in respect of which are to take the form of a rent allowance in accordance with regulation 91A(3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

(1A)[^c-4a60fcc8743eaf6e13468176c5c5a3c2] In relation to a profit-making registered provider of social housing, sub-paragraph (1)(a) only applies to its social housing (within the meaning of sections 68 to 77 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

(2) Sub-paragraph (1) does not apply where the local authority considers that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

- (a)[^c-ef0ea95bdfff83a8c150ec34251fef84] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the rent payable for that dwelling is unreasonably high. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

(3) Where the circumstances set out in head *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c-8fc7726cbb854943ac4bb7162ddc82ce]... (b) of sub-paragraph (2) exist, the authority must state this in their application for a determination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

(4) In this Schedule “gypsies and travellers” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

- (a) persons with a cultural tradition of nomadism or of living in a caravan; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (b) all other persons of a nomadic habit of life, whatever their race or origin, including— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
  - (i) such persons who, on grounds only of their own or their family’s or dependant’s educational or health needs or old age, have ceased to travel temporarily or permanently; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] members of an organised group of travelling show people or circus people (whether or not travelling together as such). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

**4.** This paragraph applies to a tenancy entered into before— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

- (a) in Scotland, 2nd January 1989; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (b) in any other case, 15th January 1989. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

**5.** This paragraph applies to a regulated tenancy within the meaning of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

- (a) in Scotland, the Rent (Scotland) Act 1984 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c15408371]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (b) in any other case, the Rent Act 1977 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c15408381].

**6.** This paragraph applies to a housing association tenancy which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

- (a) in Scotland, is a tenancy to which Part 6 of the Rent (Scotland) Act 1984 applies; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (b) in any other case, is a housing association tenancy to which Part 6 of the Rent Act 1977 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

**7.** This paragraph applies to a protected occupancy or statutory tenancy within the meaning of the Rent (Agriculture) Act 1976 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c15408391].

**8.** This paragraph applies to a tenancy at a low rent within the meaning of Part 1 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c15408401] or Schedule 10 to the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c15408411].

**9.** This paragraph applies to a tenancy of any dwelling which is a bail hostel or probation hostel approved by the Secretary of State under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] section 13 of the Offender Management Act 2007[^c-976729ed11621bbd5874df45d0ea0f26] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb][^c15408421].

**10.** This paragraph applies to a tenancy of a housing action trust established under Part 3 of the Housing Act 1988 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c15408431].

**11.** (1) Subject to sub-paragraphs (2) and (3) this paragraph applies to a tenancy— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

- (a) in respect of a dwelling comprised in land which has been disposed of under section 32 of the Housing Act 1985 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c15408441] or section 12 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c15408451];
- (b) in respect of a dwelling comprised in land which has been disposed of with the consent required by section 43 of the Housing Act 1985 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c15408461] or section 12 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (c) in respect of which the fee simple estate has been acquired, under the right conferred by Chapter 2 of Part 1 of the Housing Act 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c15408471], otherwise than from a housing action trust within the meaning of Part 3 of the Housing Act 1988, or in respect of which the house has been acquired under the right conferred by Part 3 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1988; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (d) in respect of a dwelling disposed of under the New Towns (Transfer of Housing Stock) Regulations 1990 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c15408481] to a person who is an approved person for the purposes of disposal under those Regulations or in respect of a dwelling disposed of pursuant to powers contained in the New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] [^c15408491] to a housing association. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

(2) This paragraph shall not apply to a tenancy to which sub-paragraph (1) refers if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

- (a) there has been an increase in rent since the disposal or acquisition, as the case may be, occurred; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
- (b) the local authority stated in the application for determination that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
  - (i) the claimant occupies a dwelling larger than is reasonably required by him and any others who occupy that dwelling (including any non-dependant of his and any person paying rent to him); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb] the rent payable for that dwelling is unreasonably high. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]

(3) Where the disposal or acquisition, as the case may be, took place on or after 7th October 2002, sub-paragraph (2)(b) shall apply to a tenancy to which sub-paragraph (1) refers as if head (i) were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de1881acdd2ac6dfaa5a2e7b36484eeb]
