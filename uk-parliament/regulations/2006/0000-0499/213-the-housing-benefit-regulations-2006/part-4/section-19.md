# The Housing Benefit Regulations 2006, section 19

## 19. Persons of prescribed description

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person of a prescribed description for the purposes of section 137(1) of the Act as it applies to housing benefit (definition of family) is a person who falls within the definition of qualifying young person in section 142 of the Act (child and qualifying young person)[^c-6b9f8418bccf3e8c7e48ace6d818c61b], and in these Regulations such a person is referred to as a “**young person**”.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to a person who is—

- (a) on income support, an income-based jobseeker's allowance or an income-related employment and support allowance[^c-8e3c11ead62e9953ebf022c90c3a346e]; [^c-61da8a3d1f73fb8f372860d716df6894]...
- (b)[^c-94f4e878ffa3e51c1adc374dbb9db0fb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) a person to whom section 6 of the Children (Leaving Care) Act 2000 [^c15407131] (exclusion from benefits) applies; or[^c-be12c3fa149b4e60edce35bf039573bb]
- (d) entitled to an award of universal credit

(3) A person of a prescribed description for the purposes of section 137(1) of the Act as it applies to housing benefit (definition of the family) includes a child or young person in respect of whom section 145A of that Act [^c15407141] applies for the purposes of entitlement to child benefit but only for the period prescribed under section 145A(1) of that Act.
