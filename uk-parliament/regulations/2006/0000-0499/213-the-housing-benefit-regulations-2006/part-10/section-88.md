# The Housing Benefit Regulations 2006, section 88

## 88. Duty to notify changes of circumstances

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (6)[^key-4cf377d2f8585da1ed053a615240a871], if at any time between the making of a claim and a decision being made on it, or during the award of housing benefit, there is a change of circumstances which the claimant, or any person by whom or on whose behalf sums payable by way of housing benefit are receivable, might reasonably be expected to know might affect the claimant's right to, the amount of or the receipt of housing benefit, that person shall be under a duty to notify that change of circumstances by giving notice [^key-99a5eb9eaf4259939ccf91e669d8ba43]... to the designated office—

- (a)[^key-0e6ed0e2d8895622264dc26bc261b86f] in writing; or
- (b) by telephone—
  - (i) where the relevant authority has published a telephone number for that purpose or for the purposes of regulation 83 (time and manner in which claims are to be made) unless the authority determines that in any particular case or class of case notification may not be given by telephone; or
  - (ii) in any case or class of case where the relevant authority determines that notice may be given by telephone; or
- (c) by any other means which the relevant authority agrees to accept in any particular case.

(2)[^key-c6fd241620d9c06538da950bc2ed34f8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The duty imposed on a person by paragraph (1) does not extend to changes in—

- (a) the amount of rent payable to a housing authority;
- (b) the age of the claimant or that of any member of his family or of any non-dependants;
- (c) these Regulations;
- (d) in the case of a claimant on income support, an income-based jobseeker's allowance or an income-related employment and support allowance[^key-35256e9aa4c7a682b51c62e08d67d058], any circumstances which affect the amount of income support, an income-based jobseeker's allowance or an income-related employment and support allowance[^key-3b33738230b9725f438e2db772b9cb4a] but not the amount of housing benefit to which he is entitled, other than the cessation of that entitlement to income support, an income-based jobseeker's allowance or an income-related employment and support allowance .

(4) Notwithstanding paragraph (3)(b) or (d) a claimant shall be required by paragraph (1) to notify the designated office of any change in the composition of his family arising from the fact that a person who was a member of his family is now no longer such a person because he ceases to be a child or young person.

(5)[^key-69a9e7b3904fb44d2a9997c0a67f6ac9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^key-810830eab83f7d37c462ecd9cfa5c83b] Where—

- (a) the claimant or the claimant’s partner is in receipt of income support or jobseeker’s allowance;
- (b) the change of circumstance is that the claimant or the claimant’s partner starts employment; and
- (c) as a result of that change of circumstance, either entitlement to that benefit will end or, where the claimant or claimant’s partner is in receipt of a contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance, the amount of that benefit will be reduced,

the claimant may discharge the duty in paragraph (1) by notifying the change of circumstance by telephoning the appropriate DWP office if a telephone number has been provided for that purpose.
