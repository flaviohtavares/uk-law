# The Housing Benefit Regulations 2006, section 72E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/213
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 72E. Calculation of an extended payment where the benefit cap applies[^c-aa3d6cb0c39ae95dd2801f7ccc4f12b0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a claimant’s housing benefit in the benefit week with respect to which the extended payment is to be calculated is reduced in accordance with regulation 75D, the extended payment must be calculated using the amount of housing benefit before any such reduction is made.
