# The Housing Benefit Regulations 2006, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/213
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Benefit Regulations 2006.

(2) These Regulations are to be read, where appropriate, with the Consequential Provisions Regulations and, in a case where regulation 5(2) applies, with the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006 [^c15406451].

(3) Except as provided in Schedule 4 to the Consequential Provisions Regulations, these Regulations shall come into force on 6th March 2006.

(4) The regulations consolidated by these Regulations are revoked, in consequence of the consolidation, by the Consequential Provisions Regulations.
