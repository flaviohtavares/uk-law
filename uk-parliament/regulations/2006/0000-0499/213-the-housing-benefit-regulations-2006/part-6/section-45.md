# The Housing Benefit Regulations 2006, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/213

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. Disregard of capital of child and young person

*Extent: E+W+S*

The capital of a child or young person who is a member of the claimant's family shall not be treated as capital of the claimant.
