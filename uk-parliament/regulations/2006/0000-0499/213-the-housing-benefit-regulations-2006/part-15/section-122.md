## 122. Modifications in respect of former pathfinder authorities[^key-0ac78a3d6f6360f9210dd29870a4b8e0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this regulation and in Schedule 10, “former pathfinder authority” means a relevant authority specified in Part 1 of that Schedule.

(2) The provisions of Part 2 of Schedule 10 apply in relation to the area of a former pathfinder authority.
