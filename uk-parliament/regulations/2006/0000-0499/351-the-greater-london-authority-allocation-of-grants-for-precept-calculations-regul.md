# The Greater London Authority (Allocation of Grants for Precept Calculations) Regulations 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Laid before Parliament

The First Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 88(3)(b) and 89(5)(b) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999[^f00001] makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Greater London Authority (Allocation of Grants for Precept Calculations) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 16th February 2006.

(2) These Regulations have effect in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2006.

## 2. Amounts of P1 and P2

(1) The amounts in column 2 of the Table are specified in respect of the items (other than police grant) listed in column 1, as the amounts the aggregate of which constitutes P1 for the purposes of section 88(2) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999[^f00002].

(2) The amounts in column 3 of the Table are specified in respect of the items listed in column 1, as the amounts the aggregate of which constitutes P2 for the purposes of section 89(4) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999[^f00003].

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Sections 88 and 89 of the Greater London Authority Act 1999 set out how the Greater London Authority is to calculate the amounts of its council tax for the City of London and the remainder of Greater London. Separate calculations are necessary for these two parts of Greater London because the Metropolitan Police Authority is not responsible for the provision of police services in the City of London.

These Regulations which apply for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2006, prescribe the amounts of redistributed non-domestic rates and specified grants, which the First Secretary of State considers relate to the police and non-police expenditure of the Greater London Authority and functional bodies, which the Greater London Authority must take into account when carrying out the calculations of the amounts of council tax for the two parts of Greater London.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, as it has no impact on the costs of businesses, charities or voluntary bodies. Neither does it have any impact on the public sector.
