# The Consistent Financial Reporting (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 437

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Consistent Financial Reporting (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made

22nd February 2006

Laid before Parliament

2nd March 2006

Coming into force

1st April 2006

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 44 of the Education Act 2002 [^f00001]:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Consistent Financial Reporting (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 1st April 2006.

(2) These Regulations apply only in relation to England.

## 2. Amendment to the Consistent Financial Reporting (England) Regulations 2003

For regulation 5 of the Consistent Financial Reporting (England) Regulations 2003[^f00002] substitute:

> **5.** The local education authority shall provide the Secretary of State with any financial statement provided to them in accordance regulation 3 by the third Friday in the July following the financial year in respect of which the statement is made.

Jacqui Smith

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

22nd February 2006

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations substitute a new regulation 5 of the Consistent Financial Reporting (England) Regulations 2003 which prescribes the date by which the local education authority must provide the Secretary of State with their governing bodies' financial statements. This date is changed from the 22 August to the third Friday in July.
