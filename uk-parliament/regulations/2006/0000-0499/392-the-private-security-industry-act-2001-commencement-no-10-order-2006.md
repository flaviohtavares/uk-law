# The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 392 (C. 10)

SECURITY INDUSTRY, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2006

Made

15th February 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 26(2) of the Private Security Industry Act 2001[^f00001].

In accordance with section 24(4)[^f00002] of that Act he has consulted with the Security Industry Authority.

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2006.

**2.** The following provisions of the Private Security Industry Act 2001 shall come into force on 20th March 2006—

- (a) section 14 (register of approved contractors);
- (b) section 15 (arrangement for the grant of approvals) to the extent not already in force;
- (c) section 16 (right to use approved status); and
- (d) section 18 (appeals relating to approvals).

Paul Goggins

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

15th February 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings sections 14, 16 and 18 of the Private Security Industry Act 2001 into force on 20th March 2006 and section 15 of that Act into force on the same date to the extent to which it is not already in force.
