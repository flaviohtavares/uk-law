# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (England) Order 2006, section 60

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 60. Amendment to the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978

*Extent: E+W+S*

In article 2 of the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978[^f00019], for the definition “Foot-and-Mouth Disease Orders”, substitute ““Foot-and-Mouth Disease Orders” means the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (England) Order 2006[^f00020] and the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Control of Vaccination) (England) Regulations 2006[^f00021];“.
