# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (England) Order 2006, section 61

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 61. Amendment to the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Ascertainment of Value) (No. 5) Order 2001

*Extent: E+W+S*

In the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Ascertainment of Value) (No. 5) Order 2001[^f00022]—

- (a) in article 2(1), for “paragraph 3(2)” substitute “paragraphs 2A(10) and 3(2)”; and
- (b) in article 2(6), for “paragraph 3(1)” substitute “paragraph 2A(2) or paragraph 3(1)”.
