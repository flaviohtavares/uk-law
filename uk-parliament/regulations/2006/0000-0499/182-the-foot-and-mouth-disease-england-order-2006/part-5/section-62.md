# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (England) Order 2006, section 62

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 62. Amendment to the Disease Control (England) Order 2003

*Extent: E+W+S*

In the Disease Control (England) Order 2003[^f00023], for article 3 substitute—

> **3. Exception**
>
> This Order shall not apply in relation to a movement to or from a zoo licensed under the Zoo Licensing Act 1981[^f00024].
