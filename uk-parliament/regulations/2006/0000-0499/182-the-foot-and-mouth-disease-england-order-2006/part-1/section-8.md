# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (England) Order 2006, section 8

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 8. Disinfection

*Extent: E+W+S*

Disinfection under this Order shall be carried out with a disinfectant which is—

- (a) approved for use for the purpose of this Order and the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Control of Vaccination) (England) Regulations by the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (England) Order 2007[^c-389663dcae3f135f9592880d25f28dc3];
- (b) used at the concentration specified in that Order; and
- (c) used in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions or recommendations (if any) and in particular, if use is recommended before any date, used before that date.
