# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (England) Order 2006, section 54

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 54. Designated areas and periods for the inspection of vehicles

*Extent: E+W+S*

The following are designated for the purposes of section 65A of the Act (inspection of vehicles)—

- (a) any temporary control zone for the duration of its existence;
- (b) any supplementary movement control zone for the duration of its existence;
- (c) any protection zone for the duration of its existence;
- (d) any surveillance zone for the duration of its existence;
- (e) any restricted zone for the duration of its existence;
- (f) any wild animal infected zone for the duration of its existence.
