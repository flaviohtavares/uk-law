# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (England) Order 2006, section 55A

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 55A. Contingency plans and exercises[^c-ada0fee2a1eeeec58b68ab84b58ce30e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State must carry out alert exercises in real time [^c-58fd52b3a6e7d99986bc9b68220f5608]..., either—

- (a) on two occasions within a five-year period following the date of the last confirmed case, the first of which must occur no later than three years after the approval of the plan; or
- (b) in the case of a major epizootic disease outbreak which was successfully controlled and eradicated, once during the five-year period following the conclusion of that outbreak.
