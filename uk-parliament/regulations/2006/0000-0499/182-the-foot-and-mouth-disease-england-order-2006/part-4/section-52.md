# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (England) Order 2006, section 52

> Text as at 2023-12-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52. Production of records

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any person required to produce a record by a person acting under this Order shall do so without delay.

(2) On production, a person acting under this Order may—

- (a) copy any records, in whatever form they may be held;
- (b) remove any records to enable them to be copied, or where they are kept electronically, require them to be produced in a form which may be taken away.

(3) A person removing records under this article shall give a written receipt for them.
