# The Council Tax Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 43

> Text as at 2015-05-26.

## 43. Council tax benefit taper

The prescribed percentage for the purpose of sub-section (5)(c)(ii) of section 131 of the Act as it applies to council tax benefit, (percentage of excess of income over the applicable amount which is deducted from maximum council tax benefit)(a), shall be 2 6/7 per cent.
