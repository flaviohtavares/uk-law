# The Council Tax Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 71

> Text as at 2015-05-26.

## 71. Methods of recovery

(1) Without prejudice to any other method of recovery a relevant authority may recover any recoverable excess benefit [^c-9bfddb230b63a677d073f887f87979f7]... by any of the methods specified in paragraph (2) and (3) or any combination of those methods.

(2) Excess benefit may be recovered [^c-bda6d30c7caa2685adb504bf9f3583bb]...—

- (a) by payment by or on behalf of the claimant or the person to whom the excess benefit was allowed[^c-0f1616c74bc667184cece2527a6675fd]; or
- (b) by an addition being made by the relevant authority to any amount payable in respect of the council tax concerned.

(3)[^c-42d45251de85c70c8e99236692861f5f] Where recoverable excess benefit cannot be recovered by either of the methods specified in paragraph (2), the relevant authority may request the Secretary of State to recover the outstanding excess—

- (a) from the benefits prescribed in regulation 75(1); or
- (b) where the claimant has one or more partners, from the benefits prescribed in regulation 75(1B)[^c-40eee37d17dfa5191202a053c4242405], provided that the claimant and that partner were a couple both at the time the excess benefit was allowed and when the deduction is made.
