# The Council Tax Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 70

> Text as at 2015-05-26.

## 70. Persons from whom recovery may be sought[^key-54d9c2cedfe545b39526d6d00e76bf48]

Recoverable excess benefit shall be due from the claimant or the person to whom the excess benefit was allowed.
