# The Council Tax Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 69

> Text as at 2015-05-26.

## 69. Authority by which recovery may be made

The relevant authority which allowed the recoverable excess benefit may recover it.
