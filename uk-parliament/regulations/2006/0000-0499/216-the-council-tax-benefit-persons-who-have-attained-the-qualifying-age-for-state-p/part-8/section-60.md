# The Council Tax Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 60

> Text as at 2015-05-26.

## 60. Decisions by a relevant authority

(1) Unless provided otherwise by these Regulations, any matter required to be determined under these Regulations shall be determined in the first instance by the relevant authority.

(2) The relevant authority shall make a decision on each claim within 14 days of the provisions of regulations 53 and 57 (time and manner in which claims are to be made and evidence and information) being satisfied or as soon as reasonably practicable thereafter.
