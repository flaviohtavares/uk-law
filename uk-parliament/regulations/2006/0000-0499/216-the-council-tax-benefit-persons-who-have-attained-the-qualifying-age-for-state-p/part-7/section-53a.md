# The Council Tax Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 53A

> Text as at 2015-05-26.

## 53A. Electronic claims for benefit[^c-1165ac2e86bad4c5625a8954976ce205]

A claim for council tax benefit may be made by means of an electronic communication in accordance with Schedule 8.
