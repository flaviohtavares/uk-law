# The Council Tax Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 39

> Text as at 2015-05-26.

## 39. Capital jointly held

Where a claimant and one or more other persons are beneficially entitled in possession to any capital asset, other than a capital asset disregarded under regulation 37(4), they shall be treated as if each of them were entitled in possession to the whole beneficial interest therein in an equal share and the foregoing provisions of this Section shall apply for the purposes of calculating the amount of capital which the claimant is treated as possessing as if it were actual capital which the claimant does possess.
