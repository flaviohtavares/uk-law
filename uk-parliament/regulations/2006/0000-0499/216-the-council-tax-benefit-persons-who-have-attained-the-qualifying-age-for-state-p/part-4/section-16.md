# The Council Tax Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 16

> Text as at 2015-05-26.

## 16. Claimant in receipt of guarantee credit

In the case of a claimant who is in receipt, or whose partner is in receipt, of a guarantee credit, the whole of his capital and income shall be disregarded.
