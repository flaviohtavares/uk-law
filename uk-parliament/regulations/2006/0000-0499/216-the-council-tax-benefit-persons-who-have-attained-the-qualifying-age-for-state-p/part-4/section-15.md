# The Council Tax Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 15

> Text as at 2015-05-26.

## 15. Calculation of income and capital

The income and capital of—

- (a) the claimant; and
- (b) any partner of the claimant,

shall be calculated in accordance with the rules set out in this Section; and any reference in this Part to the claimant shall apply equally to any partner of the claimant.
