# The Council Tax Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 33

> Text as at 2015-05-26.

## 33. Capital limit

For the purposes of section 134(1) of the Act as it applies to council tax benefit (no entitlement to benefit if capital exceeds a prescribed amount), the prescribed amount is £16,000.
