# The Council Tax Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 9

> Text as at 2015-05-26.

## 9. Persons of prescribed description for the definition of family in section 137(1) of the Act

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person of a prescribed description for the purposes of section 137(1) of the Act (definition of family) as it applies to council tax benefit is a person who falls within the definition of qualifying young person in section 142 of the Act (child and qualifying young person)[^c-c508fa2301874d61093f2e34f3a7f39a], and in these Regulations such a person is referred to as a “**young person**”.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to a person who is—

- (a) on income support, an income-based jobseeker's allowance or an income-related employment and support allowance[^c-d44859c1f9edcc15dd73c09eb3ed3b19] ;or[^c-83bd9a68c113bf9ed7eca16c35c4c077]
- (b)[^c-ed40871f7a06bd7422c00f48f8c7941d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) a person to whom section 6 of the Children (Leaving Care) Act 2000 [^c1541529] (exclusion from benefits) applies.

(3) A person of a prescribed description for the purposes of section 137(1) of the Act as it applies to council tax benefit (definition of the family) includes a child or young person in respect of whom section 145A of the Act [^c1541530] applies for the purposes of entitlement to child benefit but only for the period prescribed under section 145A(1) of the Act.
