# The Immigration Services Commissioner (Designated Professional Body) (Fees) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No.400

IMMIGRATION

The Immigration Services Commissioner (Designated Professional Body) (Fees) Order 2006

Made

20th February 2006

Laid before Parliament

23rd February 2006

Coming into force

16th March 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 86(10) and (12) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Immigration Services Commissioner (Designated Professional Body) (Fees) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 16th March 2006.

## 2. Fees

(1) The fee to be paid to the Immigration Services Commissioner by each designated professional body for the year 1<sup>st</sup> April 2005 to 31<sup>st</sup> March 2006 shall be the sum specified in the Schedule to this Order.

(2) The fee shall be paid on or before 31<sup>st</sup> March 2006.

Tony McNulty

Minister of State

Home Office

20th February 2006

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

| Designated Professional Body | Fee to be paid |
| --- | --- |
| The Law Society | £133,050 |
| The Law Society of Scotland | £5,030 |
| The Law Society of Northern Ireland | £1,220 |
| The Institute of Legal Executives | £4,270 |
| The General Council of the Bar | £6,550 |
| The Faculty of Advocates | £1,220 |
| The General Council of the Bar of Northern Ireland | £1,220 |

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the fee to be paid by the designated professional bodies to the Immigration Services Commissioner (“Commissioner”) for the purpose of meeting the costs incurred by her in discharging her functions under Part V of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999.

The level of the fee varies according to the designated professional body concerned. The different level of fee is designed to reflect the cost of the Commissioner’s regulatory activity referable to each particular body.

The fees specified in the Schedule to this Order reflect the referable costs incurred in the course of the Commissioner’s regulatory activity in the period 1<sup>st</sup> April 2004 to 31<sup>st</sup> March 2005.
