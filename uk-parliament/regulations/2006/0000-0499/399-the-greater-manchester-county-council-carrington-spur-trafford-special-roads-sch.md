# The Greater Manchester County Council (Carrington, Spur, Trafford) (Special Roads) Scheme 1984 Revocation Scheme 2005 Confirmation Instrument 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 399

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The Greater Manchester County Council (Carrington, Spur, Trafford) (Special Roads) Scheme 1984 Revocation Scheme 2005 Confirmation Instrument 2006

Made

16th February 2006

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Instrument in exercise of powers conferred by section 16 and 326 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

**1.** This Instrument may be cited as The Greater Manchester County Council (Carrington, Spur, Trafford) (Special Roads) Scheme 1984 Revocation Scheme 2005 Confirmation Instrument 2006 and shall come into force on the date on which notice that it has been confirmed is first published in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Highways Act 1980.

**2.** The Greater Manchester County Council (Carrington, Spur, Trafford) (Special Roads) Scheme 1984 Revocation Scheme 2005 (“the Scheme”) is hereby confirmed without modifications.

**3.** The Scheme as confirmed is set out in the Schedule hereto and copies of the Scheme are deposited at the offices of the Department for Transport, Floor 13 (IMD), Ashdown House, St Leonards on Sea, Hastings, East Sussex TN37 7GA, and at the offices of the Trafford Borough Council at Trafford Town Hall, Talbot Road, Stretford, Greater Manchester M32 0YU.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport.

J. Blackie

Regional Director Government Office for the North East

16th February 2006

## THE SCHEDULE — TRAFFORD BOROUGH COUNCIL

### THE GREATER MANCHESTER COUNTY COUNCIL (CARRINGTON, SPUR, TRAFFORD) (SPECIAL ROADS) SCHEME 1984 REVOCATION SCHEME 2005

Trafford Borough Council being the highway authority for the special road concerned makes this Scheme in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16 and 326 of the Highways Act 1980 and all other powers enabling it in that behalf.

**1.** The Greater Manchester County Council (Carrington, Spur, Trafford) (Special Roads) Scheme 1984 is hereby revoked.

**2.** This Scheme may be cited as The Greater Manchester County Council (Carrington, Spur, Trafford) (Special Roads) Scheme 1984 Revocation Scheme 2005.

Given under the Common Seal of the Council this 10th day of November 2005.

The COMMON SEAL of TRAFFORD BOROUGH COUNCIL was hereunto affixed in the presence of

W. Partington

Authorised Signatory
