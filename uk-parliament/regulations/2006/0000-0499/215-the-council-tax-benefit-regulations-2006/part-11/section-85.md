# The Council Tax Benefit Regulations 2006, section 85

> Text as at 2018-04-11.

## 85. Persons from whom recovery may be sought[^key-978c90beee0a6f0e1fb06a14a64dd2c7]

(1) Recoverable excess benefit shall be due from the claimant or the person to whom the excess benefit was allowed.
