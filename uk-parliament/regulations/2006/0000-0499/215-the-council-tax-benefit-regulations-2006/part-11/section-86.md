# The Council Tax Benefit Regulations 2006, section 86

> Text as at 2018-04-11.

## 86. Methods of recovery

(1) Without prejudice to any other method of recovery a relevant authority may recover any recoverable excess benefit [^key-0097c086af56bc6d649476dc2bba7e4d]... by any of the methods specified in paragraph (2) and (3) or any combination of those methods.

(2) Excess benefit may be recovered[^key-b6ae22a31d8a5977ba3b49efde702f9c]...—

- (a) by payment by or on behalf of the claimant or the person to whom the excess benefit was allowed[^key-d78bc88ada5aa67432b45503cd4383cd]; or
- (b) by an addition being made by the relevant authority to any amount payable in respect of the council tax concerned.

(3)[^key-c7626232e663d62c59c0909caff0821b] Where recoverable excess benefit cannot be recovered by either of the methods specified in paragraph (2), the relevant authority may request the Secretary of State to recover the outstanding excess—

- (a) from the benefits prescribed in regulation 90(1); or
- (b) where the claimant has one or more partners, from the benefits prescribed in regulation 90(1B)[^key-731d05a184b3a8fa3517537034b3b35e], provided that the claimant and that partner were a couple both at the time the excess benefit was allowed and when the deduction is made.
