# The Council Tax Benefit Regulations 2006, section 92A

> Text as at 2018-04-11.

## 92A. Verifying information[^c-82e10967b04ab3eb7a5e06c279a526c6]

A relevant authority may verify relevant information supplied to, or obtained by, the authority in accordance with regulation 92.
