# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/214

## 5. Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit

(1) Except as provided in paragraph (2), these Regulations apply to a person who has attained the qualifying age for state pension credit.

(2) These Regulations shall not apply in relation to any person if he, or if he has a partner, his partner, is a person on universal credit,[^c-bc74eecfc762fa51741853e608f29811] on income support ,[^c-e578319081230deb74fa9e329147e5e3] on[^c-8f7c1b764dd8408ebf3f783100f0301f] an income-based jobseeker’s allowance or on an income-related employment and support allowance.
