# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 98

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/214

## 98. Requiring information from landlords and agents

Pursuant to section 126A of the Administration Act [^c1541137], where a claim is made to an authority, on which a rent allowance may be awarded, then, in the circumstances prescribed in regulation 99 (circumstances for requiring information), that authority, or any person authorised to exercise any function of the authority relating to housing benefit, may require an appropriate person to supply to that authority or person relevant information, in the manner prescribed in regulation 101 (manner of supply of information).
