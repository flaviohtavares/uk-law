# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/214

## 102. Criminal offence[^key-8ffa5944d71c082ed72303321f591ea2]

Any supplier who fails to supply relevant information to the requirer as, when and how required under regulations 98 to 101 shall be guilty of an offence under section 113 of the Administration Act.
