# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 94

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/214

## 94. Interpretation

In this Section—

- “**the notice**” means the notice prescribed in regulation 99(1)(b) (circumstances for requiring information);
- “**relevant information**” means such information as is prescribed in regulation 100 (relevant information);
- “**the requirer**” means a person within regulation 98 (requiring information from landlords and agents), who requires information pursuant to that regulation;
- “**the supplier**” means an appropriate person who is required, pursuant to regulations 98 and 99, to supply relevant information and any person who is not so required is not, for the purpose of supplying information pursuant to section 126A of the Administration Act [^c1541132] and these Regulations, an appropriate person.
