> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/214

## 64A. Electronic claims for benefit[^c-529228f52616686e6d7980ccb2f9e2ac]

A claim for housing benefit may be made by means of an electronic communication in accordance with Schedule 10.
