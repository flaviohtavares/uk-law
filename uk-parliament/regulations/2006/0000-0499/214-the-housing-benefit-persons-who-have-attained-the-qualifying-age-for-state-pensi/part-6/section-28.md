# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/214

## 28. Calculation of income and capital where state pension credit is not payable

The income and capital of a person to whom neither regulation 26 nor regulation 27 applies shall be calculated or estimated by the relevant authority in accordance with regulations 29 to 49.
