# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/214

## 40. Deduction of tax and contributions of self-employed earners

(1) The amount to be deducted in respect of income tax under regulation 39(1)(b)(i), (2)(b)(i) or (8)(a)(i) (calculation of net profit of self-employed earners) shall be calculated on the basis of the amount of chargeable income and as if that income were assessable to income tax at the [^M_F_f2d1d818-f8a9-4f88-c5ef-017ece38277d]... the basic rate, or in the case of a Scottish taxpayer, the Scottish basic rate,[^c-9af84e19ef5318d957d89a34c4d820c7] of tax applicable to the assessment period less only the personal reliefs to which the claimant is entitled under Chapters 2, 3 and 3A of Part 3 of the Income Tax Act 2007 as are[^c-4cdcbedab572a714c451a0d4445fda5a] appropriate to his circumstances; but, if the assessment period is less than a year, the earnings to which the basic[^M_F_9abe91d2-c1b6-4d0e-d703-171e1b546fe0][^c-fade4be493f286e62df6987af9cd1f20] rate or the Scottish basic rate,[^c-02d0845b8f4993247f5c47051e07a87f] of tax is to be applied and the amount of the personal reliefs deductible under this paragraph shall be calculated on a pro rata basis.

(2) The amount to be deducted in respect of national insurance contributions[^c-aeade343f055d2f6cfe1e1931a3e9120] under regulation 39(1)(b)(i), (2)(b)(ii) or (8)(a)(ii) shall be the total of—

- (a)[^c-c2f152c6c4238ab377d6853ee5d9e9ea] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the amount of Class 4 contributions (if any) which would be payable under section 15 of the Act (Class 4 contributions recoverable under the Income Tax Acts) at the percentage rate applicable to the assessment period on so much of the chargeable income as exceeds the lower limit but does not exceed the upper limit of profits and gains applicable for the tax year applicable to the assessment period; but if the assessment period is less than a year, those limits shall be reduced pro rata.

(3) In this regulation “**chargeable income**” means—

- (a) except where sub-paragraph (b) applies, the earnings derived from the employment less any expenses deducted under paragraph (2)(a) or, as the case may be, (3) of regulation 39;
- (b) in the case of employment as a child minder, one-third of the earnings of that employment.
