# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/214

## 26. Claimant in receipt of guarantee credit

In the case of a claimant who is in receipt, or whose partner is in receipt, of a guarantee credit, the whole of his capital and income shall be disregarded.
