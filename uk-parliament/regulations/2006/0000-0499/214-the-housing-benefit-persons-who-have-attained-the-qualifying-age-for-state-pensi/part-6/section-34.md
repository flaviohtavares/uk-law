# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/214

## 34. Disregard of changes in tax, contributions etc

In calculating the claimant's income the appropriate authority may disregard any legislative change—

- (a) in the basic or other rates of income tax;
- (aa)[^c-f62cd0ecdfa600107d530e9803162f64] in the Scottish basic or other rates of income tax;
- (b) in the amount of any personal tax reliefs under Chapters 2, 3 and 3A of Part 3 of the Income Tax Act 2007[^c-c0de17566eee39dd9ddab0d5b2dc3ec8];
- (c) in the rates of national insurance contributions[^c-dcd36518835a33fa8c49c9d4bee40978] payable under the Act or in the lower earnings limit or upper earnings limit for Class 1 contributions under that Act, or[^c-90e4680e7dd42603beafbd2305778adb] the lower or upper limits applicable to Class 4 contributions under that Act [^c-2e3ffc17d9f1392d03123e80b0cef2c0]...;
- (d) in the amount of tax payable as a result of an increase in the weekly rate of Category A, B, C or D retirement pension or any addition thereto or any graduated pension payable under the Act or a state pension under Part 1 of the Pensions Act 2014[^c-4fd4cfd254c07da805e967bc2cb27f8d]; and
- (e) in the maximum rate of child tax credit or working tax credit,

for a period not exceeding 30 benefit weeks beginning with the benefit week immediately following the date from which the change is effective.
