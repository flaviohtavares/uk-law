# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/214

## 19. Persons of prescribed description

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person of a prescribed description for the purposes of section 137(1) of the Act as it applies to housing benefit (definition of family) is a person who falls within the definition of qualifying young person in section 142 of the Act (child and qualifying young person)[^key-4bf2f559f6da985892c12f876bfb5bfc], and in these Regulations such a person is referred to as a “**young person**”.

(2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to a person who is—

- (a) on income support , an income-based jobseeker’s allowance or an income-related employment and support allowance[^key-bcdd3266a86b399487c0635f24b3592b];[^key-bbb0ef11110d474ec2bedcfac7f1800d]...
- (b)[^key-26f48fba49f01df653bb2eae7ca6245f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) a person to whom section 6 of the Children (Leaving Care) Act 2000 [^c1541028] (exclusion from benefits) applies; or[^key-fd3afc2fcfa84dbf2b65b59f8551cbb6]
- (d) entitled to an award of universal credit.

(3) A person of a prescribed description for the purposes of section 137(1) of the Act as it applies to housing benefit (definition of the family) includes a child or young person in respect of whom section 145A of that Act [^c1541029] applies for the purposes of entitlement to child benefit but only for the period prescribed under section 145A(1) of that Act.
