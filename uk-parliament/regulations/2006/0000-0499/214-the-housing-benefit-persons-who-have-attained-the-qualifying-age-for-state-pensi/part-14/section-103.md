# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 103

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/214

## 103. Modifications in respect of pathfinder authorities

(1)[^c-3c45a8f5a1e5a7f2878b7a96c9317cd3] In this regulation and Schedule 9, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3c45a8f5a1e5a7f2878b7a96c9317cd3] **pathfinder authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3c45a8f5a1e5a7f2878b7a96c9317cd3]** ” means a relevant authority specified in Part 1 of that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3c45a8f5a1e5a7f2878b7a96c9317cd3]

(2) The provisions of Part 2 of Schedule 9 apply in relation to the area of a pathfinder authority on and after the date specified in Part 1 in relation to that authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3c45a8f5a1e5a7f2878b7a96c9317cd3]

(1) In this regulation and in Schedule 9, “former pathfinder authority” means a relevant authority specified in Part 1 of that Schedule.

(2) The provisions of Part 2 of Schedule 9 apply in relation to the area of a former pathfinder authority.
