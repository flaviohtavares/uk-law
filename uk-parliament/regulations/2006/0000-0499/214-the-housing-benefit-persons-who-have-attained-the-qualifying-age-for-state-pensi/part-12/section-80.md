# The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2006/214

## 80. Meaning of overpayment

In this Part, “**overpayment**” means any amount which has been paid by way of housing benefit and to which there was no entitlement under these Regulations (whether on the initial decision or as subsequently revised or superseded or further revised or superseded[^c-c84791c62fdbf33cf8cfa0d54effb18f]) and includes any amount paid on account under regulation 74 (payment on account of a rent allowance) which is in excess of the entitlement to housing benefit as subsequently decided.
