# The School Finance (England) Regulations 2006, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Initial determination of a local education authority’s schools budgets

A local education authority must not later than 31st March 2006—

- (a) make an initial determination of their schools budgets for funding periods 1 and 2, and
- (b) give notice of any such determination to the governing bodies of the schools they maintain.
