# The School Finance (England) Regulations 2006, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Redetermination of allocation of budget shares for funding period 2

Not later than 31st March 2007, a local education authority must redetermine any allocation of schools' budget shares under regulation 10(a) for funding period 2 in accordance with Part 3 of these Regulations.
