# The School Finance (England) Regulations 2006, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Required content of schemes

A scheme prepared by a local education authority under section 48(1) of the 1998 Act[^f00029] must deal with the matters connected with the financing of schools maintained by the authority set out in Schedule 6.

## Annotations

[^f00029]: Footnote: Section 48(1) has been amended by section 40 and paragraphs 2(1) and (2) of Schedule 3 to the 2002 Act.
