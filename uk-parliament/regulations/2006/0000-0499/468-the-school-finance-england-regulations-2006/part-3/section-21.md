# The School Finance (England) Regulations 2006, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Budget share adjustments in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2005: Pupil numbers

(1) This regulation applies to the extent that a school’s budget share for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2005 was determined pursuant to regulation 8(1) or (3) of the 2004 Regulations other than in cases where pupil numbers were weighted according to special educational needs.

(2) To the extent that a school’s budget share for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2005 was—

- (a) determined by reference to an estimate of the number of registered pupils at the school; and
- (b) differences between the estimated number of pupils on the dates determined by the authority in accordance with regulation 9 of the 2004 Regulations, and the actual number of pupils at the school on those dates were not taken into account in a redetermination of the school’s budget share for that financial year,

the local education authority must determine that school’s budget share for funding period 1 so as to take into account those differences.
