# The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies Regulations 2006, SCHEDULE 1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Instruments relevant to Community TSE Regulation[^c-9eb1dd1b34bad5e460be90a16ce6b033]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulation 2

The Community TSE Regulation has been amended by, and must be read with—

- (a) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1248/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1248) amending Annexes III, X and XI to Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards epidemio-surveillance and testing of transmissible spongiform encephalopathies[^f00010];
- (b) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1326/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1326) laying down transitional measures to permit the changeover to Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council for the prevention, control and eradication of certain transmissible spongiform encephalopathies, and amending Annexes VII and XI to that Regulation[^f00011];
- (c) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 270/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0270) amending Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards specified risk material and epidemio-surveillance for transmissible spongiform encephalopathies and amending Regulation [(EC) No 1326/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1326) as regards animal feeding and placing on the market of ovine and caprine animals and products thereof[^f00012];
- (d) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1494/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1494) amending Annexes III, VII and XI to Regulation [(EC) 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and the Council as regards monitoring of bovine spongiform encephalopathy, eradication of transmissible spongiform encephalopathy, removal of specified risk material and rules for the importation of live animals and products of animal origin[^f00013];
- (e) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 260/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0260) amending Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the eradication of transmissible spongiform encephalopathies in ovine and caprine animals and rules for the trade in live ovine and caprine animals and bovine embryos[^f00014];
- (f) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 650/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0650) amending Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the import of live ovine and caprine animals[^f00015];
- (g) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1053/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1053) amending Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and the Council as regards rapid tests[^f00016];
- (h) Act concerning the conditions of accession of the Czech Republic, the republic of Estonia, the Republic of Cyprus, the Republic of Latvia, the Republic of Lithuania, the Republic of Hungary, the Republic of Malta, the Republic of Poland, the Republic of Slovenia and the Slovak Republic and the adjustments to the Treaties on which the European Union is founded[^f00017];
- (i) Regulation [(EC) No 1128/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1128) of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) as regards the extension of the period for transitional measures[^f00018];
- (j) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1139/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1139) amending Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards monitoring programmes and specified risk material[^f00019];
- (k) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1234/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1234) amending Annexes I, IV and XI to Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council and Regulation [(EC) No 1326/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1326) as regards transmissible spongiform encephalopathies and animal feeding[^f00020];
- (l) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1809/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1809) amending Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards rules for importation of live bovine animals and products of bovine, ovine and caprine origin from Costa Rica and New Caledonia[^f00021];
- (m) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1915/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1915) amending Annexes VII, VIII and IX to Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and the Council as regards the trade and import of ovine and caprine animals and the measures following the confirmation of transmissible spongiform encephalopathies in bovine, ovine and caprine animals[^f00022];
- (n) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 2245/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2245) amending Annex III to Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards monitoring of transmissible spongiform encephalopathies on ovine and caprine animals[^f00023];
- (o) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 876/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0876) amending Annex VIII to Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards trade in ovine and caprine animals for breeding[^f00024];
- (p) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1471/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1471) amending Annex XI to Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the import of cervid products from Canada and the United States[^f00025];
- (q) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1492/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1492) amending Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards eradication measures for transmissible spongiform encephalopathies in bovine, ovine and caprine animals, the trade and importation of semen and embryos of ovine and caprine animals and specified risk material[^f00026];
- (r) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1993/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1993) amending Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards Portugal[^f00027];
- (s) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 36/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0036) amending Annexes III and X to Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards epidemio-surveillance for transmissible spongiform encephalopathies in bovine, ovine and caprine animals[^f00028];
- (t) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 214/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0214) amending Annex III to Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards monitoring of transmissible spongiform encephalopathies in caprine animals[^f00029];
- (u) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 260/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0260) amending Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards rapid tests[^f00030];
- (v) Regulation [(EC) No 932/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0932) of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) laying down rules for the prevention, control and eradication of certain transmissible spongiform encephalopathies as regards the extension of the period for transitional measures[^f00031];
- (w) Commission Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0598) prohibiting the placing on the market of products derived from bovine animals born or reared within the United Kingdom before 1 August 1996 for any purpose and exempting such animals from certain control and eradication measures laid down in Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999)[^f00032];
- (x) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1292/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1292) amending Annex IV to Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards animal nutrition[^f00033]; and
- (y) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1974/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1974) amending Annexes X and XI to Regulation [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards national reference laboratories and specified risk material[^f00034].
