# The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies Regulations 2006, section 3

> This provision is not yet in force.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Appointment of competent authority[^c-5657168fa9352596961c781d1a1ce3f5]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

The Secretary of State is the competent authority for the purposes of the Community TSE Regulation except as otherwise specified in these Regulations.
