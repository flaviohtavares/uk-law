# The Traffic Management Act 2004 (Commencement No.3) (England) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 1736 (C. 60)

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The Traffic Management Act 2004 (Commencement No.3) (England) Order 2006

Made

29th June 2006

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 99 of the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Traffic Management Act 2004 (Commencement No.3) (England) Order 2006.

## 2. Commencement of provisions

Section 94 of the Traffic Management Act 2004 shall come into force as respects England on 29th September 2006.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

G. Merron

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Transport

29th June 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 94 of the Traffic Management Act 2004 into force as respects England on 29th September 2006. Section 94 deals with the power to inspect blue badges, these being badges for display on motor vehicles used by disabled persons.
