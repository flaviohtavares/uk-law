# The Judicial Appointments and Discipline (Modification of Offices) (No.2) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 1551

JUDICIAL APPOINTMENTS AND DISCIPLINE

The Judicial Appointments and Discipline (Modification of Offices) (No.2) Order 2006

Made

11th June 2006

Laid before Parliament

16th June 2006

Coming into force

10th July 2006

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on him under section 85(3) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^f00001]—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Judicial Appointments and Discipline (Modification of Offices) (No.2) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 10th July 2006.

## 2. Amendments to Part 3 of Schedule 14 to the Constitutional Reform Act 2005

(1) Part 3 of Schedule 14 to the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 is amended as follows.

(2) At the end insert—

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| President of the Gambling Appeals Tribunal | Paragraph 1(1)(a) of Schedule 8 to the Gambling Act 2005[^f00002] |
| Member of the Gambling Appeals Tribunal | Paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 8 to the Gambling Act 2005 |

Falconer of Thoroton C

Date 11th June 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds offices to which appointments are made by the Lord Chancellor to those listed in Schedule 14 to the Constitutional Reform Act 2005.
