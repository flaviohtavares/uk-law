# The Transport and Works (Model Clauses for Railways and Tramways) Order 2006, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Model clauses

The Secretary of State hereby prescribes—

- (a) the clauses set out in Schedule 1 to this Order as model provisions for incorporation in any draft order which, in accordance with rules made under section 6 of the Act (applications for orders), is required to be submitted with an application for an order under section 1(1)(a) of the Act (orders as to railways); and
- (b) the clauses set out in Schedule 2 to this Order as model provisions for incorporation in any draft order which, in accordance with rules made under section 6 of the Act, is required to be submitted with an application for an order under section 1(1)(b) of the Act (orders as to tramways).
