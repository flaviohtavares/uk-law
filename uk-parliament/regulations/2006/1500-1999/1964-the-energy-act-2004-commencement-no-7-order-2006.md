# The Energy Act 2004 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 1964 (C. 66)

ELECTRICITY

GAS

The Energy Act 2004 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2006

Made

21st July 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 198(2) of the Energy Act 2004[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Energy Act 2004 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2006.

## 2. Commencement

The provisions of the Energy Act 2004 specified in the Schedule shall come into force on 14th August 2006.

Jim Fitzpatrick

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations and Postal Services

Department of Trade and Industry

21st July 2006
