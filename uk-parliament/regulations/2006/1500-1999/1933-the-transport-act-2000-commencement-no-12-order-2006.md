# The Transport Act 2000 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 1933 (C. 63)

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Transport Act 2000 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2006

Made

17th July 2006

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 275(1) of the Transport Act 2000[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Transport Act 2000 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2006.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1 October 2006

Section 248 the Transport Act 2000[^f00002] shall come into force on 1st October 2006.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

G. Merron

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

17th July 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 248 of the Transport Act 2000 which requires operators of railway passenger services, so far as is reasonably practicable, to ensure that any substitute road services allow disabled passengers to travel safely and in reasonable comfort.

(This note is not part of the Order)
