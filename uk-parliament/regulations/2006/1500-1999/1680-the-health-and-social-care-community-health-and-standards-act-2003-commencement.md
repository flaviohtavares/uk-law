# The Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 Commencement (No. 9) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 1680 (C.57)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

SOCIAL CARE, ENGLAND

The Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 Commencement (No. 9) Order 2006

Made

26th June 2006

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 195(1) and 199(1) of the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, interpretation and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 Commencement (No.9) Order 2006.

(2) In this Order—

- “the 2000 Act” means the Care Standards Act 2000[^f00002]; and
- “the Act” means the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003.

(3) This Order applies in relation to England only, apart from article 2(2) which applies in relation to England and Wales.

## 2. Appointed day for provisions relating to fees

(1) 27th June 2006 is the appointed day for the coming into force of section 105(6) of the Act for the purpose of making regulations, and section 105(1) of the Act in so far as it relates to that provision.

(2) 1st August 2006 is the appointed day for the coming into force of—

- (a) section 105(3) to (5) and (7) of the Act; and
- (b) sections 105(1) and (6) of the Act for all remaining purposes.

## 3. Appointed day for provisions relating to complaints

1st September 2006 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act in so far as they are not already in force[^f00003]—

- (a) section 113(1), (3) and (4) (complaints about health care);
- (b) section 114(1), (2), (5) and (6) (complaints about social services);
- (c) section 115 (complaints regulations: supplementary); and
- (d) in Schedule 14, the repeal in Part 2 relating to section 7B of the Local Authority Social Services Act 1970[^f00004] (complaints procedure) and section 196 insofar as it relates to that repeal.

## 4. Transitional provisions pending section 105(2) of the Act coming into force in relation to the CSCI

(1) Pending the coming into force of section 105(2) of the Act in relation to the determination of fees by the CSCI[^f00005], the following transitional provisions shall apply.

(2) Sections 12(2), 15(3), 16(3) and 22(7)(i) of the 2000 Act shall be read as if—

- (i) “or the CSCI” were omitted; and
- (ii) after “the Assembly” there were inserted “or the CSCI”.

(3) Section 15(5) of the 2000 Act shall be read as if after “the Assembly” there were inserted “or the CSCI”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Ivan Lewis

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

26th June 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the ninth Commencement Order under the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 (“the Act”).

Article 2 commences section 105(3) to (7) of the Act which contains amendments to the Care Standards Act 2000 (“the 2000 Act”) consequential on the Commission for Healthcare Audit and Inspection (“the CHAI”) having responsibility for setting their own fees under section 113A of the 2000 Act. It commences section 105(6) of the Act on 27th June 2006 for the purpose of making regulations prescribing the date of payment of annual fees. It commences that section for all purposes, together with sections 105(3) to (5) and (7), of the Act on 1st August 2006 which is the date when the CHAI is to introduce its fees scheme under section 105(2). Article 4 contains transitional provisions in relation to the 2000 Act which will apply until such time as section 105(2) of the Act is commenced in relation to the CSCI.

Article 3 commences on 1st September 2006 sections 113(1), (3) and (4), 114(1), (2) (5) and (6) and 115 (complaints about health care, social services and supplementary provision) of the Act. It commences the repeal of section 7B of the Local Authority Social Services Act 1970 under which the Secretary of State had required, by Order, local authorities to establish complaints procedures.
