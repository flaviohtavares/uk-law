# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval and Approval Marks)(Fees)(Amendment) Regulations 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval and Approval Marks)(Fees)(Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made

19th June 2006

Laid before Parliament

23rd June 2006

Coming into force

9th August 2006

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 61(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001], section 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00002], the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00003] and section 128 of the Finance Act 1990[^f00004].

In relation to the exercise of his powers under the Finance Act 1973 he has the consent of the Treasury.

In accordance with section 195(2) of the Road Traffic Act 1988 he has consulted with representatives of those whom he considers to be likely to be affected by the Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval and Approval Marks)(Fees)(Amendment) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 9th August 2006.

## 2. Preliminary

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval and Approval Marks)(Fees) Regulations 1999[^f00005] shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval and Approval Marks) (Fees) Regulations 1999 (“the 1999 Regulations”) which prescribe the fees payable for the examination of vehicles and vehicle parts, the examination of complete vehicles and the issue of documents in connection with the type approval (both European and National) of vehicles and vehicle parts.

*Regulations 3, 4* and *the Schedule* provide for changes in the amount of fees. The last increase took effect on 1st October 2004.

The increases in fees for examinations of a complete vehicle to which the Great Britain Regulations or the European Regulations apply with a view to the issue of the appropriate documents (Schedule 1) range from 3.03% to 4.08%.

The increases in fees for examinations, with a view to the issue of the appropriate documents, of a complete vehicle to which the Great Britain Regulations for Goods Vehicles apply (Schedule 2) range from 3.46% to 3.53%.

The increases in fees for the issue of documents in respect of vehicle parts (Schedule 3) range from 2.77% to 4.25%.

The increases in fees for the issue of documents in respect of vehicles to which the Great Britain Regulations or the European Regulations apply (Schedule 4) range from 2.77% to 4.25%.

A table showing the amount and size of each individual increase appears in the explanatory memorandum prepared by the Department for Transport for this instrument, a copy of which is published with it on the OPSI website www.opsi.gov.uk/si.

*Regulations 5* and *6* amend Schedule 5 to the 1999 Regulations so as to take account of new or amended Community Instruments or ECE Regulations. (The terms “Community Instruments” and “ECE Regulations” are defined in regulation 3(2) of the 1999 Regulations.)

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business is available from the Vehicle Certification Agency, 1 Eastgate Office Centre, Eastgate Road, Bristol BS5 6XX (telephone number: 01179 524119). A copy has been placed in the library of each House of Parliament.

Copies of the ECE Regulations and Community Instruments referred to in these Regulations may be obtained from the Office of Public Sector Information.
