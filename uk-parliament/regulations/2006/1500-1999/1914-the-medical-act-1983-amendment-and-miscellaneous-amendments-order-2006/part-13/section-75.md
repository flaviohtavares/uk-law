# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 75

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 75. Amendment of the Value Added Tax Act 1994

(1) In Part 2 of Schedule 8 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00074] (zero-rating – the groups), in Group 12 (drugs, medicines, aids for the handicapped)—

- (a) in item 1[^f00075], omit “, the register of medical practitioners with limited registration”; and
- (b) in item 1A[^f00076], omit “or the register of medical practitioners with limited registration”.

(2) In Part 2 of Schedule 9 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994 (exemptions – the groups), in Group 7 (health and welfare)—

- (a) in item 1(a)[^f00077], omit “or the register of medical practitioners with limited registration”; and
- (b) in item 5, omit “or the register of medical practitioners with limited registration”.
