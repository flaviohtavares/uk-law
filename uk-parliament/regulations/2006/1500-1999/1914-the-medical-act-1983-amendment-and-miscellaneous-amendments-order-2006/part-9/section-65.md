# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Second article amending section 29A of the Act

In section 29A[^f00065] (regulations as to licence to practise and revalidation), in subsection (4)(d), after “licence to practise” add “whenever a licensing authority sees fit to do so”.
