# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Amendment of sections 1 and 29A of, and Schedule 1 to, the Act

(1) In the following provisions—

- (a) subsection (3)(c) of section 1[^f00003] (the General Medical Council); and
- (b) the heading of paragraph 19B of Schedule 1[^f00004] (constitution of the General Medical Council),

for “Registration Decisions Panels” substitute “Registration Panels”.

(2) In the following provisions—

- (a) subsection (5)(b) of section 29A[^f00005] (regulations as to licence to practise and revalidation); and
- (b) paragraphs 19B and 23(c) of Schedule 1[^f00006],

for “Registration Decisions Panel” substitute Registration Panel“.
