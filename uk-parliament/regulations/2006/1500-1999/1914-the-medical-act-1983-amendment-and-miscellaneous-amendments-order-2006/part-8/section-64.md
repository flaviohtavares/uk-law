# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Second article amending Schedule 4 to the Act

In paragraph 10(1) of Schedule 4 (proceedings before the Investigation Committee, Interim Orders Panels and Fitness to Practise Panels)—

- (a) for “this Act, a” substitute “this Act or a”; and
- (b) omit “or a direction for erasure given by the General Council under section 39 of this Act”.
