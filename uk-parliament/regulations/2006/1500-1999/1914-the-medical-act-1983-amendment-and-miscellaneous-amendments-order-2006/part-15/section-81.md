# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 81

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 81. Amendment of the Opticians Act 1989

In section 1 of the Opticians Act 1989[^f00086] (constitution and functions of the Council), after subsection (2) insert the following subsection—

> (2A) The main objective of the Council in exercising such of the Council’s functions as affect the health and safety of members of the public is to protect, promote and maintain their health and safety.

.
