# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 72

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 72. Second article amending section 32 of the Act

In section 32[^f00070] (registration fees)—

- (a) in subsection (1)—
  - (i) in paragraph (b), omit “in any year subsequent to the year beginning with the date on which he was first registered”, and
  - (ii) in paragraph (c), omit “or any particular list in it”; and
- (b) omit subsection (8).
