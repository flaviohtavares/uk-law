# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Second article amending section 50 of the Act

In subsection (1)(b) of section 50[^f00073] (default powers of the Privy Council), for “32(1) to (3) or (7) to (9)” substitute “32(1) to (3), (7) or (9)”.
