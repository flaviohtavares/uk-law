# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 92

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 92. Other transitional provisions etc.

(1) The Privy Council may by order make such further transitional, transitory or saving provisions as it considers appropriate.

(2) The power to make an order under paragraph (1) above is exercisable by statutory instrument, and—

- (a) a statutory instrument containing such an order shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament; and
- (b) for the purposes of section 1 of the Statutory Instruments Act 1946[^f00090] (definition of “Statutory Instrument”), this article shall have effect as if contained in an Act of Parliament.

(3) The power vested in the Privy Council to make an order under paragraph (1) above may be exercised by any two or more members of the Council.
