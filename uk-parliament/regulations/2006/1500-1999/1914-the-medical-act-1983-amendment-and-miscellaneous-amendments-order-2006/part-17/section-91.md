# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 91

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 91. Legal assessors rules

Any rules made under paragraph 7 of Schedule 4 to the Act, as in force before the coming into force of article 57(3), which came into force before the coming into force of article 57(3)—

- (a) shall remain in force after the coming into force of article 57(3), notwithstanding the changes to that paragraph made by article 57(3); and
- (b) may be amended or revoked after the coming into force of article 57(3) by rules made under that paragraph, as amended by article 57(3).
