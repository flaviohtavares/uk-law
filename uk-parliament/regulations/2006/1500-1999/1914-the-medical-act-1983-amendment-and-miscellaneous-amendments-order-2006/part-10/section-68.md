# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Amendment of section 29F of the Act

In section 29F[^f00068] (appeals)—

- (a) after subsection (1) insert the following subsection—

  > (1A) If a licensing authority decides under—
  >
  > - (a) section 44C(7) to refuse to grant a licence to practise to a medical practitioner; or
  > - (b) section 44C(8)(a) to withdraw a licence to practise from a medical practitioner,
  >
  > the practitioner may appeal to a Registration Appeals Panel.

  ; and
- (b) in subsection (2), after “subsection (1)” insert “or (1A)”.
