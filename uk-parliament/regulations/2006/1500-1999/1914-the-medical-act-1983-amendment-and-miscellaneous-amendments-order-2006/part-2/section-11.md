# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Amendment of section 34 of the Act

(1) In subsection (1) of section 34[^f00015] (which relates to publication of the register), for the words from “appear in” to the end of the subsection substitute “appear in the register”.

(2) For the heading of section 34 substitute “Publication of the register”.
