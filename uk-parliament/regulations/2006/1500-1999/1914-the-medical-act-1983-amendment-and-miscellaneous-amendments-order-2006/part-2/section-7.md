# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Amendment of section 29B of the Act

In subsection (1)(a) of section 29B[^f00011] (grant, refusal and withdrawal of licence), for “either full registration or limited registration” substitute “full registration”.
