# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. First article amending section 41A of the Act

In subsection (13) of section 41A[^f00018] (interim orders), omit “and to a person registered with limited registration”.

## Annotations

[^f00018]: Footnote: Section 41A was originally inserted by S.I. [2000/1083](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/1083) and was substituted by S.I. [2000/3135](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/3135).
