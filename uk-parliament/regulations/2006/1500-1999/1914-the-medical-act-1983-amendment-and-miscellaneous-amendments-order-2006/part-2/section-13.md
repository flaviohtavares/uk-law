# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Amendment of section 35C of the Act

In subsection (1) of section 35C[^f00017] (functions of the Investigation Committee), for paragraphs (a) to (c) substitute the following paragraphs—

> - (a) a fully registered person; or
> - (b) a person who is provisionally registered,

.
