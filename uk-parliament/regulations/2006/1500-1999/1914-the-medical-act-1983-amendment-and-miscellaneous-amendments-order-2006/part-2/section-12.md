# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. First article amending section 35A of the Act

In subsection (8) of section 35A[^f00016] (General Council’s power to require disclosure of information), for “fully registered person, a provisionally registered person or a person registered with limited registration” substitute “fully registered person or a provisionally registered person”.

## Annotations

[^f00016]: Footnote: Section 35A was originally inserted by S.I. [2000/1083](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/1083) and was substituted by S.I. [2002/3135](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/3135).
