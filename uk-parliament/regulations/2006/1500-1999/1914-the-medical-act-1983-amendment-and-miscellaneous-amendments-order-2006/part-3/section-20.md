# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. First article amending section 3 of the Act

In subsection (1) of section 3[^f00024] (registration by virtue of primary United Kingdom or primary European qualifications), for paragraph (a) substitute the following paragraph—

> - (a) holds one or more primary United Kingdom qualifications and has satisfactorily completed an acceptable programme for provisionally registered doctors; or

.
