# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. First article amending section 50 of the Act

In subsection (1)(b) of section 50[^f00033] (default powers of Privy Council), for “10, 11, 12, 13 or” substitute “10A or”.
