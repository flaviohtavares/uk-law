# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Repeal of sections 10 to 13 of the Act

Omit section 10[^f00026] (experience required for full registration by virtue of primary United Kingdom qualifications), section 11[^f00027] (provisions supplementary to section 10, etc.), section 12[^f00028] (special provisions as to employment in health centres) and section 13[^f00029] (power to appoint visitors of approved hospitals).
