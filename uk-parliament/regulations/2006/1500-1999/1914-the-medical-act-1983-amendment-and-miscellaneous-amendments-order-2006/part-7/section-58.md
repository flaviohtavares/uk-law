# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Amendment of section 35E of the Act

In subsection (3) of section 35E[^f00059] (provisions supplementary to section 35D), for paragraph (b) substitute the following paragraph—

> - (b) sections 31A, 35C, 35CC and 35D above, this section and section 39 below shall continue to apply to him.

.
