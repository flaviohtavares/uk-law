# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Second article amending section 41A of the Act

In subsection (12) of section 41A[^f00060] (interim orders), for “35C to 35E” substitute “31A, 35C to 35E and 39”.

## Annotations

[^f00060]: Footnote: Section 41A was originally inserted by S.I. [2000/1083](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/1083) and was substituted by S.I. [2000/3135](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/3135).
