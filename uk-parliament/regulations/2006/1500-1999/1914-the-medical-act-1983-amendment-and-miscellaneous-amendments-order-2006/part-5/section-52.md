# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 52

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 52. Amendment of section 45 of the Act

In subsection (5) of section 45[^f00053] (disciplinary provisions affecting practitioners who render services while visiting the United Kingdom), for “one or more of his good character, professional competence and health” substitute “his fitness to practise”.

## Annotations
