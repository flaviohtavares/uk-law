# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Second article amending section 3 of the Act

In subsection (1) of section 3[^f00045] (registration by virtue of primary United Kingdom or primary European Qualifications), after “any person” insert “whose fitness to practise is not impaired and”.

## Annotations

[^f00045]: Footnote: Section 3(1) was substituted by S.I. [1996/1591](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1591).
