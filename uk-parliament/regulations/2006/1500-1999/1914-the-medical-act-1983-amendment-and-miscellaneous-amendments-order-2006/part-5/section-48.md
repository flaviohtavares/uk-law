# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 48

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 48. Fourth article amending section 31 of the Act

In subsection (9)(a) of section 31[^f00048] (which relates to the making of regulations with respect to the register), for “good character” substitute “fitness to practise”.
