# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 47

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 47. Second article amending section 19 of the Act

In section 19[^f00047] (full registration of EEA nationals etc. by virtue of overseas primary qualifications etc.), in subsection (1), for paragraph (c) substitute the following paragraph—

> - (c) that his fitness to practise is not impaired,

.
