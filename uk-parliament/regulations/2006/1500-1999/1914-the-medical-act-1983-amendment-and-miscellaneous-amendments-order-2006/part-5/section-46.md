# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 46. Amendment of section 15A of the Act

At the end of subsection (2) of section 15A[^f00046] (provisional registration for EEA nationals), add “if his fitness to practise is not impaired”.
