# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 54

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 54. Third article amending Schedule 3A to the Act

In paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3A[^f00055] (registration appeals), for paragraph (o) substitute the following paragraph—

> - (o) a decision under section 44B of this Act (fitness to practise matters prior to registration) to refuse to register a person or to erase a person’s name from the register;

.
