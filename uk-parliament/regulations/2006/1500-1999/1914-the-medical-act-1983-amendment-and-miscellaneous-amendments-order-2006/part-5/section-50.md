# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 50

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 50. Second article amending section 41 of the Act

In subsection (1) of section 41[^f00050] (restoration of names to the register), after “section 35D above,” insert “or section 44B(4)(b) below,”.
