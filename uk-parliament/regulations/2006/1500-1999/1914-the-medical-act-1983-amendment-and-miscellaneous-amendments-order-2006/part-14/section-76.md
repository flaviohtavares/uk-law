# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 76

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 76. Amendment of the Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations 1989

In the Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations 1989[^f00078]—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(m) of regulation 11 (colour of light shown by lamps and reflectors), for “full, provisional or limited” substitute “full or provisional”; and
- (b) in item 8 in the Table in regulation 27 (restrictions on the use of lamps other than those to which regulation 24 refers), for “full, provisional or limited” substitute “full or provisional”.
