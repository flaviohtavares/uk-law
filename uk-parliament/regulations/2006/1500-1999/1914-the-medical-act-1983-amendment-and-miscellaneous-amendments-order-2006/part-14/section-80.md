# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 80. Revocation of spent provisions

The following provisions are hereby revoked—

- (a) in the European Primary Medical Qualifications Regulations 1996[^f00082]—
  - (i) paragraph (5) of regulation 6 (registration of persons who do not have primary United Kingdom or primary European qualifications); and
  - (ii) paragraphs 2 to 4 of Schedule 2 (minor and consequential amendments);
- (b) in the Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) Order 2002[^f00083]—
  - (i) paragraphs (6) to (8), (9)(b) and (10) of article 6 (registration), and
  - (ii) paragraph (5)(c) of article 9 (further and supplementary);
- (c) paragraph 4 of the Schedule to the Primary Medical Services (Scotland) Act 2004 (Consequential Modifications) Order 2004[^f00084] (modification of public general acts); and
- (d) in the Primary Medical Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Consequential Amendments) Order 2004[^f00085]—
  - (i) article 3 (saving of certain provisions of the Medical Act 1983), and
  - (ii) paragraph 1 of the Schedule (amendment of enactments – the Medical Act 1983).
