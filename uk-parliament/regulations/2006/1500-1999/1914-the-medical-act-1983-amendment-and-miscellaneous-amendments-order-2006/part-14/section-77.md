# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 77

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 77. Amendment of the National Health Service (Performers Lists) Regulations 2004

In paragraph (1) of regulation 23 of the National Health Service (Performers Lists) Regulations 2004[^f00079] (application for inclusion in a medical performers list)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (b), omit “subject to sub-paragraph (e),”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (e), omit the words from “and, if he is” to the end of that sub-paragraph.
