# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. First article amending section 41 of the Act

In subsection (4) of section 41[^f00040] (restoration of names to the register), for “15A or 21” in both places where it occurs, substitute “15A, 21 or 21C”.
