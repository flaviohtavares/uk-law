# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 42

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 42. Fourth article amending Schedule 1 to the Act

In paragraph 11 of Schedule 1[^f00042] (constitution of the General Medical Council), for “21A or 22” substitute “21B or 21C”.
