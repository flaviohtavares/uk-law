# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 43. Second article amending Schedule 3 to the Act

In paragraph 5(2) of Schedule 3[^f00043] (registration: supplementary provisions), for “21A, 22 and 25 or 27” substitute “21B, 21C, 27A or 27B”.
