# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Third article amending section 31 of the Act

In subsection (4) of section 31[^f00039] (which relates to the making of regulations with respect to the register), after “section 21” insert “or 21C”.
