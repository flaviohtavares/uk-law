# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Third article amending section 30 of the Act

In subsection (1) of section 30[^f00038] (which contains general provisions relating to the register)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “21A or 25” substitute “21B or 21C”; and
- (b) in paragraph (c), for “section 27” substitute “section 27A or 27B”.
