# The Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) and Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2006, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Second article amending section 26 of the Act

In section 26[^f00036] (registration of qualifications), in subsection (1), for “21 or 21A” substitute “21, 21B or 21C”.
