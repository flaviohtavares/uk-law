# The Education (Designated Institutions) (No. 2) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 1744

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Designated Institutions) (No. 2) Order 2006

Made

1st July 2006

Laid before Parliament

11th July 2006

Coming into force

1st August 2006

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 129(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Education (Designated Institutions) (No. 2) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st August 2006.

## 2. Designation of institutions

Guildhall School of Music and Drama, being an institution which appears to the Secretary of State to fall within section 129(2) of the Education Reform Act 1988, is designated as an institution eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Higher Education Funding Council for England[^f00002].

Bill Rammell

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

1st July 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, made under section 129 of the Education Reform Act 1988 (the “1988 Act”), designates Guildhall School of Music and Drama (which is an institution conducted by the Corporation of London) as eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Higher Education Funding Council for England. This institution has been so designated because it appears to the Secretary of State to fall within section 129(2) of the 1988 Act.

Section 129 of the 1988 Act enables the Secretary of State to designate as eligible for support from the Higher Education Funding Council for England any institution which appears to him to fall within section 129(2) of the 1988 Act.

An institution falls within section 129(2) of the 1988 Act if its full-time equivalent enrolment number for courses of higher education exceeds 55 per cent of its total full-time equivalent enrolment number.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
