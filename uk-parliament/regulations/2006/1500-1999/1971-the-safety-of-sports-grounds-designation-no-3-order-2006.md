# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (No. 3) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 1971

SPORTS GROUNDS AND SPORTING EVENTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (No. 3) Order 2006

Made

19th July 2006

Laid before Parliament

21st July 2006

Coming into force

11th August 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(1) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00001]. In accordance with section 18(4) of that Act she has consulted with such persons as appear to her requisite.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (No. 3) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 11th August 2006.

## 2. Designation

The sports ground specified in Schedule 1 (being a sports ground in England at which association football matches are played, which is occupied by a club which is a member of the Football League Limited or the Football Association Premier League Limited, and which in the opinion of the Secretary of State has accommodation for more than 5,000 spectators) is designated as a sports ground requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.

Tessa Jowell

Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport

19th July 2006
