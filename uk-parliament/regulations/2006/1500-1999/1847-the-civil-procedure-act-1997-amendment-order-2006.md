# The Civil Procedure Act 1997 (Amendment) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 1847 (L. 7)

SUPREME COURT OF ENGLAND AND WALES

COUNTY COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Civil Procedure Act 1997 (Amendment) Order 2006

Made

10th July 2006

Laid before Parliament

12th July 2006

Coming into force

1st September 2006

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2A(1) of the Civil Procedure Act 1997[^f00001], after consulting in accordance with section 2A(2A) of that Act, and with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice in accordance with section 2A(2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Civil Procedure Act 1997 (Amendment) Order 2006, and shall come into force on 1st September 2006.

## 2. Amendment to the Civil Procedure Act 1997

In section 2 of the Civil Procedure Act 1997 (Rule Committee), in subsection (2)(c), for “one district judge” substitute “either one or two district judges”.

Falconer of Thoroton, C

10th July 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends section 2 of the Civil Procedure Act 1997 so that the Lord Chief Justice may appoint either one or two district judges to the Civil Procedure Rule Committee
