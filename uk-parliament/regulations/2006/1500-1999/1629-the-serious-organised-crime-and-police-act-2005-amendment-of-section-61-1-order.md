# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Amendment of Section 61(1)) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 1629

CRIMINAL LAW: ENGLAND AND WALES

The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Amendment of Section 61(1)) Order 2006

Made

19th June 2006

Coming into force

20th June 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 61(4) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001].

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Amendment of Section 61(1)) Order 2006 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales only.

**2.** At the end of section 61(1) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (offences to which Chapter 1 of Part 2 of that Act applies) there is added—

> - (h) in England and Wales—
>   - (i) any common law offence of bribery;
>   - (ii) any offence under section 1 of the Public Bodies Corrupt Practices Act [1889 (c. 69)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1889/69) (corruption in office);
>   - (iii) the first two offences under section 1 of the Prevention of Corruption Act [1906 (c. 34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1906/34) (bribes obtained by or given to agents).

Vernon Coaker

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

19th June 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 61(1) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 lists the offences to which Chapter 1 of Part 2 of that Act applies. That Chapter confers powers on the Director of Public Prosecutions, the Director of Revenue and Customs Prosecutions and the Lord Advocate in relation to the giving of disclosure notices in connection with the investigation of offences to which that Chapter applies (see section 60(1) of that Act).

This Order amends section 61(1) to add offences to it.
