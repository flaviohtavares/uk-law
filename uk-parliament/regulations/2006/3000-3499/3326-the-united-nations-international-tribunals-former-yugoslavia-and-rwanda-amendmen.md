# The United Nations (International Tribunals) (Former Yugoslavia and Rwanda) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 3326

United nations

The United Nations (International Tribunals) (Former Yugoslavia and Rwanda) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2006

Made

14th December 2006

Laid before Parliament

21st December 2006

Coming into force

22nd January 2007

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of December 2006

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, by virtue and in exercise of the powers vested in Her by section 1 of the United Nations Act 1946[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, as follows:-

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the United Nations (International Tribunals) (Former Yugoslavia and Rwanda) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 22<sup>nd</sup> January 2007.

(2) This Order extends to the United Kingdom.

**2.** Article 3 of the United Nations (International Tribunals) (Former Yugoslavia and Rwanda) (Amendment) Order 2006[^f00002] is amended by deleting the words “12 *bis*, 12 *ter*, 12 *quater,*”.

Meriel McCullagh

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the United Nations (International Tribunals) (Former Yugoslavia and Rwanda) (Amendment) Order 2006 ([S.I. 2006/1923](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/1923)) so as to remedy a technical defect in article 3 of that Order. The technical defect was the deletion of certain articles from the Schedule to the United Nations (International Tribunal) (Rwanda) Order 1996[^f00003] which were not included in the Statute of the International Tribunal for Rwanda.
