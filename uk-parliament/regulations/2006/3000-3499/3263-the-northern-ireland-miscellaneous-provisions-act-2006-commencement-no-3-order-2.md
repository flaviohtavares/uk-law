# The Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 3263 (C. 118)

Northern ireland

The Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2006

Made

7th December 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 31(3) of the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2006.

## 2. Commencement

Section 21 (arms decommissioning: extension of amnesty period) of the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 shall come into force on 10th December 2006.

Paul Goggins

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Northern Ireland Office

7th December 2006

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 21 of the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 on 10th December 2006.
