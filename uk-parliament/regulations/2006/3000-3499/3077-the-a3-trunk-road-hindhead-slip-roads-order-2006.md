# The A3 Trunk Road (Hindhead) Slip Roads Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 3077

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The A3 Trunk Road (Hindhead) Slip Roads Order 2006

Made

2nd November 2006

Coming into force

13th November 2006

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 41 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A3 Trunk Road (Hindhead) Slip Roads Order 2006 and shall come into force on 13th November 2006.

**2.** In this Order—

**3.** The slip roads shall become trunk roads from the date when this Order comes into force.

**4.** The centre lines of the slip roads are indicated by heavy black lines on the plan.

**5.** The Secretary of State directs as respects any part of a highway which crosses the route of a slip road that—

- (a) where the highway is maintainable at the public expense by a local highway authority, the part in question shall be maintained by that authority, and
- (b) where the highway is not so maintainable and is not maintainable under a special enactment or by reason of tenure, enclosure or prescription, the Secretary of State shall be under no duty to maintain the part in question,

until, in either case, a date to be specified in a notice given by the Secretary of State to the highway authority for that highway. The date specified will not be later than the date on which the relevant route is opened for traffic.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Sherwood

## SCHEDULE — ROUTES OF THE SLIP ROADS

The routes of the slip roads are as follows:—

(1) Junction at Bramshott Chase, in the District of East Hampshire in the County of Hampshire and in the Borough of Waverley in the County of Surrey—

- (a) a route to connect from and to a new road which the Secretary of State proposes to construct to Knockhundred Lane at Bramshott Chase (the slip road along this route being given the number 1 on the Site Plan);
- (b) a route to connect from the northbound carriageway of the trunk road (as improved) to a new road which the Secretary of State proposes to construct as a diversion of Hammer Lane (the slip road along this route being given the number 2 on the Site Plan)

(2) Junction at Hazel Grove, in the Borough of Waverley in the County of Surrey—

- (a) two routes to connect from and to the north bound carriageway of the main new road to a new roundabout which the Secretary of State proposes to construct at Hazel Grove (the slip roads along these routes being respectively given the numbers 3 and 4 on the Site Plan);
- (b) two routes to connect to and from the south bound carriageway of the main new road to a new roundabout which the Secretary of State proposes to construct at Hazel Grove (the slip roads along these routes being respectively given the numbers 5 and 6 on the Site Plan).
