# The Luton Dunstable Translink Order 2006, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Substitute road services

The Council may provide or secure the provision by other persons of services for the carriage of passengers by road (“substitute services”) where Translink has been temporarily interrupted, curtailed or discontinued.

(2) The route, frequency and stopping places of any substitute service need not correspond with the route of the interrupted, curtailed or discontinued service.

(3) Section 6 of the 1985 Act shall not apply to any substitute services.
