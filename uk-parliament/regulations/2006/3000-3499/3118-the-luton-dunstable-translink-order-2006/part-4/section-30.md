# The Luton Dunstable Translink Order 2006, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Power to charge fares

The Council may demand, take and recover or waive such charges for carrying passengers or goods on Translink, or for any other services or facilities provided in connection with the operation of Translink, as the Council thinks fit.
