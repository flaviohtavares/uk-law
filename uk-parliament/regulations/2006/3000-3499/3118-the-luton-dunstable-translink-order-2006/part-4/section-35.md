# The Luton Dunstable Translink Order 2006, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Service subsidy agreements

Any service subsidy agreement relating to the operation of guided bus services operating over Translink shall be excluded from section 89(1) of the 1985 Act.
