# The Luton Dunstable Translink Order 2006, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Registration of busway services

For the purposes of the application of Part 1 of the 1985 Act but subject to article 38 (substitute road services), the authorised busway shall be a road within the meaning of section 137(1) of that Act.
