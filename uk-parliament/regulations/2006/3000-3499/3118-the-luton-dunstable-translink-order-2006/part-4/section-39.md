# The Luton Dunstable Translink Order 2006, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Obstruction of construction of Translink

Any person who, without reasonable excuse, obstructs another person from constructing Translink shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
