# The Luton Dunstable Translink Order 2006, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. For the protection of Council and County Council in the event of transfer

If the powers of the Council under this Order to construct the authorised works or any part of those works are transferred to another person by virtue of a transfer agreement under article 42 (power to transfer undertaking), the provisions of Part 6 of Schedule 11 (protective provisions) to this Order shall apply for the protection of the Council and the County Council.
