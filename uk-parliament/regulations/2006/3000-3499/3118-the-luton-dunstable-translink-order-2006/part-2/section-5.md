# The Luton Dunstable Translink Order 2006, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Power to deviate

In constructing or maintaining any of the scheduled works, the Council may—

- (a) deviate laterally from the lines or situations shown on the deposited plans within the limits of deviation for that work shown on those plans, and
- (b) deviate vertically from the levels shown on the deposited sections to any extent not exceeding 3 metres upwards or downwards.
