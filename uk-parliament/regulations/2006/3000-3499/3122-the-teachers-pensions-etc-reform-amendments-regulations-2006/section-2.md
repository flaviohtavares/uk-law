# The Teachers’ Pensions etc. (Reform Amendments) Regulations 2006, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Amendments of regulations

(1) The Teachers’ Superannuation (Additional Voluntary Contributions) Regulations 1994 [^f00003] are amended in accordance with Schedule 1.

(2) The Teachers (Compensation for Redundancy and Premature Retirement) Regulations 1997 [^f00004] are amended in accordance with Schedule 2.

(3) The Teachers’ Pensions Regulations 1997[^f00005] are amended in accordance with Schedule 3, together with Schedule 4.
