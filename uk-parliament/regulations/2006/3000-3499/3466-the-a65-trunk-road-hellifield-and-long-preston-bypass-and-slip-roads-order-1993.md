# The A65 Trunk Road (Hellifield and Long Preston Bypass and Slip Roads) Order 1993 (Revocation) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 3466

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The A65 Trunk Road (Hellifield and Long Preston Bypass and Slip Roads) Order 1993 (Revocation) Order 2006

Made

18th December 2006

Coming into force

12th January 2007

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 41 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A65 Trunk Road (Hellifield and Long Preston Bypass and Slip Roads) Order 1993 (Revocation) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 12th January 2007.

**2.** The A65 Trunk Road (Hellifield and Long Preston Bypass and Slip Roads) Order 1993[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Bagley

A Divisional Director Highways Agency

18th December 2006
