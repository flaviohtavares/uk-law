# The Radioactive Substances (Emergency Exemption) (England and Wales) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 3169

ATOMIC ENERGY AND RADIOACTIVE SUBSTANCES, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Radioactive Substances (Emergency Exemption) (England and Wales) Order 2006

Made

28th November 2006

Laid before Parliament

29th November 2006

Coming into force

30th November 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 15(2) of the Radioactive Substances Act 1993[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement, cessation and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Radioactive Substances (Emergency Exemption) (England and Wales) Order 2006; it comes into force on 30th November 2006 and ceases to have effect on 26th May 2007.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Exemption from authorisation

(1) Radioactive waste relating to the death of Alexander Litvinenko is excluded from the provisions of sections 13 and 14 of the Radioactive Substances Act 1993.

(2) All other provisions of that Act apply to any person who disposes of or accumulates radioactive waste covered by this Order as they apply to persons holding an authorisation under those sections.

Ian Pearson

Minister of State

Department of Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

28th November 2006

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under this Order radioactive waste relating to the death of Alexander Litvinenko is excluded from the provisions of sections 13 and 14 of the Radioactive Substances Act [1993 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/12). Those sections require a person to obtain an authorisation from the Environment Agency concerning the disposal and accumulation of radioactive waste. This Order extends to England and Wales.
