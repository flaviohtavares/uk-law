# The Health Service Commissioner for England (Special Health Authorities) (Revocation) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 3332

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Health Service Commissioner for England (Special Health Authorities) (Revocation) Order 2006

Made

14th December 2006

Laid before Parliament

22nd December 2006

Coming into force

22nd January 2007

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of December 2006

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers in section 2(5)(b) and (6) of the Health Service Commissioners Act 1993[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is ordered, as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Health Service Commissioner for England (Special Health Authorities) (Revocation) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 22nd January 2007.

## 2. Revocations

The following Orders are revoked—

- (a) the Health Service Commissioner for England (Rural Dispensing Committee) Order 1983[^f00002];
- (b) the Health Service Commissioner for England (National Blood Authority) Order 1994[^f00003]; and
- (c) the Health Service Commissioner for England (Special Health Authorities) Order 2004[^f00004].

Meriel McCullagh

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the following Statutory Instruments:

- (i) the Health Service Commissioner for England (Rural Dispensing Committee) Order 1983 ([S.I. 1983/1115](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1983/1115));
- (ii) the Health Service Commissioner for England (National Blood Authority) Order 1994 ([S.I. 1994/2954](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/2954)); and
- (iii) the Health Service Commissioner for England (Special Health Authorities) Order 2004 ([S.I. 2004/1119](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1119));

and removes the UK Transplant, Retained Organs Commission, National Blood Authority and the Rural Dispensing Committee Special Health Authorities (all of which have been abolished), from the Health Service Commissioner’s jurisdiction.
