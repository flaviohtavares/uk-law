# The Police Pensions Regulations 2006, SCHEDULE 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 4 — PROGRESSIVE MEDICAL CONDITIONS

Regulation 51(11)

The progressive medical conditions mentioned in regulation 51(11) are:

- AIDS
- Alzheimer’s disease;
- Cancer;
- Creutzfeld-Jacob disease;
- Huntington’s chorea;
- Motor neurone disease;
- Multiple sclerosis;
- Nieman Pick disease;
- Non-variant Creutzfeld-Jacob disease;
- Parkinson’s disease; and
- Variant Creutzfeld-Jacob disease.
