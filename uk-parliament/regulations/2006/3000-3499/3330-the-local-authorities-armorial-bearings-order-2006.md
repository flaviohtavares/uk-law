# The Local Authorities (Armorial Bearings) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 3330

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The Local Authorities (Armorial Bearings) Order 2006

Made

14th December 2006

Coming into force

15th December 2006

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of December 2006

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 247 of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Armorial Bearings) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 15th December 2006.

**2.** The armorial bearings which, immediately before 1st April 1974, were borne and used by the corporation of the City of Canterbury may be borne and used by Canterbury City Council, provided they have first been exemplified according to the laws of arms and recorded in the College of Arms.

Meriel McCullagh

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order authorises Canterbury City Council to bear and use the armorial bearings formerly borne and used by the corporation of the City of Canterbury.
