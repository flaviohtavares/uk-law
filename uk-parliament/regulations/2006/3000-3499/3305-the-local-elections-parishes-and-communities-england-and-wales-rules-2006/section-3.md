# The Local Elections (Parishes and Communities) (England and Wales) Rules 2006, section 3

> Text as at 2024-12-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Elections Rules

*Extent: E+W*

In the application of the parliamentary elections rules to the election of councillors of the council of a parish [^c-e175e3ce7a6232cbc96941d339b53797]... where the poll at that election is not taken together with the poll at another election under section 36(3), (3AB) or (3AC) of the 1983 Act [^c17726701] or section 15(1) or (2) of the Representation of the People Act 1985 [^c17726711], adaptations, alterations and exceptions shall be made to those rules so that the election shall be conducted in accordance with the Rules set out in Schedule 2 to these Rules.
