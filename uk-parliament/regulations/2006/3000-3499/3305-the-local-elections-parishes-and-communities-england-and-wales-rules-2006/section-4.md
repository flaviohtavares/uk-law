# The Local Elections (Parishes and Communities) (England and Wales) Rules 2006, section 4

> Text as at 2024-12-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Combination of polls

*Extent: E+W*

Where the poll at an election of councillors to the council of a parish [^key-84a83ab6b173b42232eab84c7a4b0fed]... is to be taken together with the poll at a relevant election or referendum, [^c17726721] the Rules set out in Schedule 2 shall apply to the parish [^key-84a83ab6b173b42232eab84c7a4b0fed]... election as shown modified in Schedule 3.
