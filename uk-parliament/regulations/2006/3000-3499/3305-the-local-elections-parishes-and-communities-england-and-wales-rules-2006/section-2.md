# The Local Elections (Parishes and Communities) (England and Wales) Rules 2006, section 2

> Text as at 2024-12-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-e641d7c5b279b3a0c1a641d8a11b7f3c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-635132790911f1dbda63a300ca3d69bc][^c-635132790911f1dbda63a300ca3d69bc] In these Rules,

- “**1983 Act**” means the Representation of the People Act 1983;
- “[^c-b7d0a9e688b9ee5fe83867ac53c2d576]**anonymous elector’s document**” has the same meaning as in regulation 3(1) of the Voter Identification Regulations 2022;
- “**the Combination of Polls Regulations**” means the Representation of the People (Combination of Polls) (England and Wales) Regulations 2004 [^c17726631];
- “[^c-7616ce5446ec8239d62af89517671aa3]**combined authority mayoral election**” means an election of a mayor for a combined authority under Part 6 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009;
- “[^c-72ef7ef5fc536830526bd4d022c3f2f1]**combined county authority mayoral election**” means an election of a mayor for a combined county authority under Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023;
- “**counting observer**” shall have the same meaning as in regulation 2(1) of the Local Authorities (Conduct of Referendums) (England) Regulations 2012[^c-6acf230dfee715dddde0be4bb1e676d7];
- “**counting officer**” shall have the same meaning as in regulation 2(1) of the Local Authorities (Conduct of Referendums) (England) Regulations 2012;
- [^c-27ddc09ca2f6627644fa2ec8c14d9c90]...
- [^c-27ddc09ca2f6627644fa2ec8c14d9c90]...
- “**mayoral election**” means an election conducted under the Local Authorities (Mayoral Elections) (England and Wales) Regulations 2007[^c-95a6ba472059551fb03ae6a64bd27582];
- “**petition organiser**” shall have the same meaning as in regulation 3 of the Local Authorities (Referendums) (Petitions and Directions) (England) Regulations 2000 [^c17726681];
- “[^c-3a4078d5ecd5fb6629d3f687e714c912]**police and crime commissioner election**” means an election of a police and crime commissioner in accordance with Chapter 6 of Part 1 of the 2011 Act;
- “**polling observer**” shall have the same meaning as in regulation 2(1) of the Local Authorities (Conduct of Referendums) (England) Regulations 2012;
- “**principal area**” means, a county in England, a district or a London borough[^c-a4c4e18a3075914b7429642741ec8bc0];
- “**referendum**” means a referendum conducted under the Local Authorities (Conduct of Referendums) (England) Regulations 2012;
- “[^c-698525b0a769ff6eac9c9ac5a14f39a9]**temporary electoral identity document**” has the same meaning as in regulation 3(1) of the Voter Identification Regulations 2022;
- “**voting area**” shall have the same meaning as in regulation 2 of the Local Authorities (Conduct of Referendums) (England) Regulations 2012.

(2) In these Rules, “**relevant election or referendum**” means one or more of the following—

- (a) a Parliamentary election [^c17726691];
- (b)[^c-0e3fc1a7dfa197082ee3d58a3b112ab3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) another local government election;
- (d) a mayoral election; and
- (da)[^c-7f2b7766b22052b7f4bfcbc582c517e1] a combined authority mayoral election;
- (db)[^c-ea62dbd713b9418167171483e4b3c296] a combined county authority mayoral election;
- (e) a referendum,
- (f)[^c-a21de475999b619ba07aae56d2a2dbee] a police and crime commissioner election,

the poll at which is taken together with the poll at the parish [^c-f1d4a86b6856678287aedf67fab837ba]... election.

(3) In the case of a referendum, a reference to—

- (a) a “**candidate**” shall be construed as a reference to a petition organiser;
- (b) an “election agent” or a “**counting agent**” shall be construed as a reference to a counting observer;
- (c) a “**polling agent**” shall be construed as a reference to a polling observer; and
- (d) a “**returning officer**” shall be construed as a reference to a counting officer.

(4) Any reference in these Rules to a district shall be construed as a reference to a county in the case of a county in which there are no district councils.
