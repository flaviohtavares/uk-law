# The Gambling Act 2005 (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2006, section 2

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2. Commencement of provisions of the Gambling Act 2005[^key-c3fedbeb707ff710002627e301f552fe]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The provisions of the 2005 Act specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 shall come into force on 1st January 2007, but where a particular purpose is specified in relation to any such provision in column 2 of that Schedule, the provision concerned shall come into force on that date for that purpose only.

(1A)[^key-621c20f469831c196f3978f1238e6c33] Section 182(4) of the 2005 Act shall come into force on 30th April 2007.

(2) The provisions of the 2005 Act specified in column 1 of Schedule 2 shall come into force on 21st May[^key-9ddd6c1e3cad4f058a7758a6cd043f8a] 2007, but where a particular purpose is specified in relation to any such provision in column 2 of that Schedule, the provision concerned shall come into force on that date for that purpose only.

(3)[^key-5b99cffe52cdf6ee220e7ae3008f2fdd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3A)[^key-38ab5b89c19d45e22438bf7afe20f8f9] The provisions of the 2005 Act specified in column 1 of Schedule 3A shall come into force on 1st August 2007, but where a particular purpose is specified in relation to any such provision in column 2 of that Schedule, the provision shall come into force on that date for that purpose only.

(4)[^key-a59795dd3b6af25f23a1393eb3bf08f9] Save as provided by articles 3 to 5, the 2005 Act apart from the provisions listed in column 1 of Schedule 3B shall come into force on 1st September 2007.

(5) Where a particular purpose is specified in column 2 of Schedule 3B in relation to any provision listed in that Schedule, the reference to that provision in paragraph (4) is only to that provision for the purpose so specified.
