# The Gambling Act 2005 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 3220 (C. 115)

betting, gaming and lotteries

The Gambling Act 2005 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2006

Made

4th December 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 358(1) and (2) of the Gambling Act 2005[^f00001]:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Gambling Act 2005 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2006.

**2.** Paragraph 23 of Schedule 7 to the Gambling Act 2005 shall come into force on the day after this Order is made.

Richard Caborn

Minister of State

Department of Culture, Media and Sport

4th December 2006

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on the day after it is made, paragraph 23 of Schedule 7 to the Gambling Act 2005 (relevant offences: power to amend Part 1). Paragraph 23 contains a power for the Secretary of State to amend by order the provisions of Part 1 of Schedule 7 (the offences).
