# The Network Rail (Thameslink 2000) Order 2006, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Disapplication of enactment relating to Balcombe Bridge

(1) In this article “Balcombe Bridge” means the bridge (numbered by Network Rail 145) in the Parish of Balcombe, District of Mid Sussex, County of West Sussex carrying the London to Brighton Railway over Rocks Lane.

(2) Nothing in section 44 (for protection of East Sussex County Council) of the British Transport Commission Act 1960[^f00017], which relates to Balcombe Bridge, shall apply in relation to Work No. 23 or any other authorised works relating to Balcombe Bridge.
