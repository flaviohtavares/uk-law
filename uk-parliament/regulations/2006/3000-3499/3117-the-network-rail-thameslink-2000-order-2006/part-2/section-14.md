# The Network Rail (Thameslink 2000) Order 2006, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Power to execute street works

(1) Network Rail may, for the purposes of the authorised works, enter upon so much of any of the streets specified in Schedule 3 (streets subject to street works) to this Order as is within the Order limits and may—

- (a) place apparatus in the street;
- (b) maintain apparatus in the street or change its position; and
- (c) execute any works required for or incidental to the authorised works or any works referred to in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) (including, in particular, breaking up or opening the street, or any sewer, drain or tunnel under it, or tunnelling or boring under the street).

(2) This article is subject to paragraph 3 of Schedule 9 (provisions relating to statutory undertakers etc.) to this Order.

(3) In this article “apparatus” has the same meaning as in Part 3 of the Street Works Act.
