# The Network Rail (Thameslink 2000) Order 2006, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Saving for regulated rights etc.

Nothing in this Order shall affect any estate, right or interest granted by Network Rail in respect of a railway facility which is subject to an access contract within the meaning of Part 1 of the Railways Act 1993[^f00028].
