# The Network Rail (Thameslink 2000) Order 2006, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Provision against danger to navigation

(1) In case of injury to or destruction or decay of a tidal work or any part thereof Network Rail shall as soon as reasonably practicable notify Trinity House and shall lay down such buoys, exhibit such lights and take such other steps for preventing danger to navigation as Trinity House shall from time to time direct.

(2) If Network Rail fails to notify Trinity House as required by paragraph (1) or to comply with any requirement of a direction given under the said paragraph it shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
