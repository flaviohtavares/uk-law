# The Local Elections (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Election day

(1) In section 11 of the Act of 1962 (election and term of office of members of district councils)[^f00008], after subsection (1) there shall be inserted:

> (1A) The election day referred to in subsection (1) is the third Wednesday in May.

.

(2) In section 130(1) of the Act of 1962 (interpretation), after the definition of “election court” there shall be inserted:

> - “**election day**” has the meaning given in section 11(1) and (1A);

.

## Annotations

[^f00008]: Footnote: Section 11 was substituted by Article 3(1) of the Electoral law (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 ([S.I. 1972/1264 (N.I.13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1972/1264)).
