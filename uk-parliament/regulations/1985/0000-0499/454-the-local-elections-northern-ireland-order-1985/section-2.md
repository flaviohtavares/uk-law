# The Local Elections (Northern Ireland) Order 1985, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00002] shall apply to Article 1 and the following provisions of this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(2) In this Order “**the Act of 1962**” means the Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962[^f00003].

(3) In this Order expressions have the same meaning as in the Act of 1962.
