# The Control of Pollution Act 1974 (Commencement No. 18) Order 1985

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1985 No. 70 (C.2)

PUBLIC HEALTH, ENGLAND AND WALES

PUBLIC HEALTH, SCOTLAND

The Control of Pollution Act 1974 (Commencement No. 18) Order 1985

Made

23rd January 1985

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under sections 104(1) and 109(2) of the Control of Pollution Act 1974 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:—

**1.** This order may be cited as the Control of Pollution Act 1974 (Commencement No. 18) Order 1985.

**2.** (1) The provisions of the Control of Pollution Act 1974 specified in column 1 of the Schedule hereto (the subject matter of which is described in column 2) shall come into force on 31st January 1985.

(2) Sections 50 (investigation of water pollution problems arising from closure of mines) and 94(2)(a)(ii) (information) of that Act (which are already in force in England and Wales) shall come into force in Scotland on 31st January 1985.

(3) Paragraph (1) above shall have effect subject to any limitations set out in column 1 of the Schedule as to the extent to which any provision is brought into force, and shall not have effect in the Isles of Scilly.

Patrick Jenkin

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

23rd January 1985

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order brings into force on 31st January 1985 in England, Wales and Scotland further provisions of Part II (Pollution of Water) of the Control of Pollution Act 1974 .

The provisions in question make it an offence for any person to cause or knowingly permit poisonous, noxious or polluting matter to enter relevant waters, subject to certain detailed defences, or to cause or knowingly permit the discharge of trade or sewage effluent, or certain other matter, into such waters. Other provisions brought into force by the order are concerned with the duty of authorities to maintain registers of applications for consent for discharges, in accordance with regulations to be made by the Secretary of State; and with operations to remedy or forestall pollution of water.

The order also brings into force certain other provisions making minor and consequential amendments to enactments, and repealing enactments which are replaced by the provisions brought into force by the order; and, in Scotland, sections 50 and 94(2)(a)(ii), which are already in force in England and Wales.
