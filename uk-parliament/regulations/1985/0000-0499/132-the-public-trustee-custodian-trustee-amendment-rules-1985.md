# The Public Trustee (Custodian Trustee) (Amendment) Rules 1985

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1985 No. 132

TRUSTEES

The Public Trustee (Custodian Trustee) (Amendment) Rules 1985

Made

4th February 1985

Laid before Parliament

15th February 1985

Coming into Operation

18th March 1985

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by Section 14(1) of the Public Trustee Act 1906, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Rules:—

**1.** These Rules may be cited as Public Trustee (Custodian Trustee) (Amendment) Rules 1985 and shall come into operation on 18th March 1985.

**2.** In Rule 30(1)(f) of the Public Trustee Rules 1912[^f00001] after the words “British Gas Corporation” there shall be inserted the words “or any subsidiary of the British Gas Corporation”.

Hailsham of St Marylebone, C

Dated 24th January 1985

We concur

Donald Thompson

Tristan Garel-Jones

Two of the Lords Commissioners, of Her Majesty's Treasury

Dated 4th February 1985

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules enable subsidiaries of the British Gas Corporation to act as a Custodian Trustee of schemes and arrangements established by the British Gas Corporation to provide pensions, gratuities and similar benefits to or in respect of persons employed in the Gas industry.
