# The Oil and Pipelines Act 1985 (Appointed Day) Order 1985

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1985 No. 1749

PETROLEUM

The Oil and Pipelines Act 1985 (Appointed Day) Order 1985

Made

15th November 1985

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his power under section 3(1) of the Oil and Pipelines Act 1985, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Oil and Pipelines Act 1985 (Appointed Day) Order 1985.

**2.** The 1st December 1985 is hereby appointed as the transfer date for the purposes of section 3(1) of the Oil and Pipelines Act 1985.

Alick Buchanan-Smith

Minister of State

Department of Energy

15th November 1985

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints 1st December 1985 as the date on which the transfer prescribed by section 3(1) of the Oil and Pipelines Act 1985 shall take place. Section 3, Schedule 2 and Part I of Schedule 4 to the Act will come into force on that date by virtue of the provisions of section 8(3).
