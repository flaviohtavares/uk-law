# The Copyright (Bermuda) (Amendment) Order 1985

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1985 No. 1985

COPYRIGHT

The Copyright (Bermuda) (Amendment) Order 1985

Made

18th December 1985

Laid before Parliament

8th January 1986

Coming into Operation

1st February 1986

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 18th day of December 1985

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, and by virtue of the authority conferred upon Her by sections 31 and 47 of the Copyright Act 1956 and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased to direct, and it is hereby directed, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Copyright (Bermuda) (Amendment) Order 1985 and shall come into operation on 1st February 1986.

**2.** Part I of the First Schedule to the Copyright (Bermuda) Order 1962[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “**the principal Order**”) is amended so that:—

- (a) after the words “and the Films Act 1960(b)” and before the words “except section 8,” are added the words “the Copyright Act 1956 (Amendment) Act 1982” “and the Copyright (Amendment) Act 1983,”; and
- (b) after the words “except section 8,” and before the words “sections 23 to 30,” are added the words “section 21B,”.

**3.** The table in Part II of the First Schedule to the principal Order is amended so as to further modify sections 18 and 21 and to insert a new provision, section 21A, as follows:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Copyright (Bermuda) Order 1962 so as to modify section 18 of the Copyright Act 1956 in its application to Bermuda and to extend section 21 of the Copyright Act 1956 as amended by the Copyright Act 1956 (Amendment) Act 1982 and the Copyright (Amendment) Act 1983 and section 21A of the Copyright Act 1956 as inserted by the Copyright (Amendment) Act 1983, with certain exceptions and modifications set out in Articles 2 and 3 of this Order, to form part of the law of Bermuda.
