# The Repatriation of Prisoners Act 1984 (Commencement) Order 1985

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1985 No. 550 (c. 6)

PRISONERS

REPATRIATION

The Repatriation of Prisoners Act 1984 (Commencement) Order 1985

Made

25th March 1985

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 9(2) of the Repatriation of Prisoners Act 1984, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Repatriation of Prisoners Act 1984 (Commencement) Order 1985.

**2.** The Repatriation of Prisoners Act 1984 shall come into force on 15th April 1985.

Leon Brittan

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

25th March 1985

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 15th April 1985 the Repatriation of Prisoners Act 1984.
