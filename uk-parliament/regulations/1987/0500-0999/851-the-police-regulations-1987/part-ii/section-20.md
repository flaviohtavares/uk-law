# The Police Regulations 1987, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Transfer of personal records

Where a member of a police force transfers to another police force his personal record shall be transferred to the chief officer of police of that other police force.
