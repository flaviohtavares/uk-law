# The Police Regulations 1987, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. Allowance under regulation 59 of the Police Regulations 1971

Where immediately before 1st September 1978 a member of a police force, other than the City of London or metropolitan police force, was entitled to an allowance under regulation 59 of the Police Regulations 1971[^f00025] he shall be entitled to an allowance as follows—

- (a) in the case of an inspector, at the rate of £50 a year;
- (b) in the case of a sergeant or constable, at the rate of £72 a year;

while he remains a member of that force.
