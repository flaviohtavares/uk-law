# The Police Regulations 1987, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Allowance in respect of medical charges

A member of a police force shall be reimbursed any charges incurred in his case under section 77, 78 or 79 of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00024] (which sections relate to charges for certain drugs, medicines and appliances and for dental treatment).
