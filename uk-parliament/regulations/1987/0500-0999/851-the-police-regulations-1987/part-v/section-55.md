# The Police Regulations 1987, section 55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 55. Women’s stocking allowance

A woman member of a police force who is not provided with stockings or tights by the police authority shall be paid in lieu an allowance at such annual rate as shall be determined by the Secretary of State unless she is being paid either a uniform allowance under regulation 54 or a plain clothes allowance under regulation 56(1).
