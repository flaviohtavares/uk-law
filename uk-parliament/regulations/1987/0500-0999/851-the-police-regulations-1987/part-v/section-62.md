# The Police Regulations 1987, section 62

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 62. Typewriter allowance

(1) Where, immediately before the coming into effect of these Regulations a member of a police force was in receipt of a typewriter allowance in respect of the use of a typewriter owned by him which he is authorised by the chief officer of police to use for the purposes of duties normally and from time to time performed by him, he shall, in respect of such use, continue to be paid such an allowance in accordance with Schedule 10, and the police authority shall continue to supply him with typewriter ribbons free of charge.

(2) In this regulation a reference to a typewriter owned by a member of a police force is a reference to a typewriter kept and used by him.
