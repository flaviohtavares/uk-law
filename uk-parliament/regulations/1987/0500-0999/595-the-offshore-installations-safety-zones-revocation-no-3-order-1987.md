# The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (Revocation) (No. 3) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 595

OFFSHORE INSTALLATIONS

The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (Revocation) (No. 3) Order 1987

Made

26th March 1987

Coming into force

28th March 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 21(1) of the Oil and Gas (Enterprise) Act 1982[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (Revocation) (No. 3) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 28th March 1987.

**2.** The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Orders specified in the Schedule hereto are hereby revoked.

Alick Buchanan-Smith

Minister of State,

Department of Energy

26th March 1987

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) Orders specified in the Schedule. The installations specified in the Schedule which were protected by the safety zones established by those Orders have been removed and accordingly those Orders are no longer required.
