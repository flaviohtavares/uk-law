# The Estate Duty (Interest on Unpaid Duty) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 892

TAXES

The Estate Duty (Interest on Unpaid Duty) Order 1987

Made

13th May 1987

Laid before the House of Commons

14th May 1987

Coming into force

6th June 1987

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 30 of the Finance Act 1970[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Estate Duty (Interest on Unpaid Duty) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 6th June 1987.

**2.** The rate of interest payable under the following enactments, namely:

- (a) section 18 of the Finance Act 1896[^f00002];
- (b) section 61(5) of the Finance (1909—10) Act 1910[^f00003]; and
- (c) section 17(3) of the Law of Property Act 1925[^f00004] and section 73(6) of the Land Registration Act 1925[^f00005],shall, as regards interest accruing on or after 6th June 1987 be 6 per cent. per annum.

**3.** Section 8(9) of the Finance Act 1894[^f00006] shall, as regards interest accruing on or after 6th June 1987, have effect with the substitution of the word “six” for the word “eight”.

Peter Lloyd

Mark Lennox-Boyd

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

13th May 1987

(This note is not part of the Order)

Estate Duty is chargeable in relation to deaths before 13th March 1975; it was replaced for deaths on or after that date by capital transfer tax, now known as inheritance tax. Article 2 of this Order provides that interest on unpaid estate duty will run at 6 per cent. (instead of at the previous rate of 8 per cent. as imposed by S.I. [1986/1942](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1942)) under the various provisions which impose a fixed rate of interest. By Article 3 the limit on the discretionary rate of interest which may be charged where payment of estate duty is postponed on grounds of hardship is correspondingly altered by substituting 6 per cent. for 8 per cent. The effect of this Order in conjunction with section 48(1) of the Finance Act [1975 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/7) is that from 6th June 1987 interest will also run at 6 per cent. when overpaid estate duty is repaid.
