# The North of Newcastle-under-Lyme—Tarvin Trunk Road (A51 Diversion North of Hurleston) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 990

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The North of Newcastle-under-Lyme—Tarvin Trunk Road (A51 Diversion North of Hurleston) Order 1987

Made

19th May 1987

Coming into force

17th June 1987

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The new highway which the Secretary of State proposes to construct along the route described in Schedule 1 to this Order shall become a trunk road as from the date when this Order comes into force.

**2.** The centre line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the deposited plan.

**3.** The length of the Trunk Road described in Schedule 2 to this Order and shown by broad striped hatching on the deposited plan shall cease to be a trunk road as from the date on which the Secretary of State notifies the County Council of Cheshire that the new trunk road is open for through traffic.

**4.** In this Order—

(1) all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway;

## SCHEDULE 1 — ROUTE OF NEW TRUNK ROAD

The route of the new trunk road is at Hurleston in the County of Cheshire and is about 0.240 kilometres in length between a point on the trunk road approximately 30 metres north of the entrance to Stoke Manor and a point on the trunk road approximately 24 metres south of the entrance to Yew Tree Farm.

## SCHEDULE 2 — LENGTH OF TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE A TRUNK ROAD

The length of trunk road ceasing to be a trunk road is that length of the Trunk Road which is between the starting and termination points of the route of the new trunk road (described in Schedule 1 hereto).
