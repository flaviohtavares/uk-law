# The Turks and Caicos Islands (Constitution) (Interim Amendment) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 934

CARIBBEAN AND NORTH ATLANTIC TERRITORIES

The Turks and Caicos Islands (Constitution) (Interim Amendment) Order 1987

Made

18th May 1987

Laid before Parliament

17th June 1987

Coming into force

On a day to be appointed under section 1(2)

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 18th day of May 1987

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 5 of the West Indies Act 1962[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

## 1. Citation, construction and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Turks and Caicos Islands (Constitution) (Interim Amendment) Order 1987 and shall be construed as one with the Turks and Caicos Islands (Constitution) Order 1976[^f00002].

(2) This Order shall come into force on such day as the Governor, acting in his discretion, may appoint by proclamation published in the *Gazette.*

## 2. Amendment of Turks and Caicos Islands (Constitution) (Interim Amendment) Order 1986

Paragraph 3(1)(c) of Schedule 2 to the Turks and Caicos Islands (Constitution) (Interim Amendment) Order 1986[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting for the words “up to four persons” the words “up to six persons”, and section 5 of the said Order shall have effect accordingly.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Constitution of the Turks and Caicos Islands, as modified by the Turks and Caicos Islands (Constitution) (Interim Amendment) Order 1986, to increase to a maximum of six the number of persons who may be appointed by the Governor as nominated members of the Executive Council for the Islands.
