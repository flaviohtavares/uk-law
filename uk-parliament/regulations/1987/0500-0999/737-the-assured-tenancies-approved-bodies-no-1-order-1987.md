# The Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (No. 1) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 737

LANDLORD AND TENANT

The Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (No. 1) Order 1987

Made

15th April 1987

Laid before Parliament

30th April 1987

Coming into force

21st May 1987

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 56(4) of the Housing Act 1980[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (No. 1) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 21st May 1987.

**2.** The bodies named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby specified for the purposes of Part II of the Housing Act 1980.

(This note is not part of the Order)

Assured tenancies are tenancies which would otherwise have been protected tenancies or housing association tenancies under the Rent Act [1977 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/42), and which meet certain other conditions. Such tenancies are subject to Part II of the Landlord and Tenant Act [1954 (c. 56)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1954/56), as modified by Schedule 5 to the Housing Act 1980. They can only be granted by bodies approved under section 56 of the 1980 Act.

This Order approves thirteen bodies for the purposes of section 56.
