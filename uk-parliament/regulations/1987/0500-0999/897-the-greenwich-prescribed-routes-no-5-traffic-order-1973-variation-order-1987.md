# The Greenwich (Prescribed Routes) (No.5) Traffic Order 1973 (Variation) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 897

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Greenwich (Prescribed Routes) (No.5) Traffic Order 1973 (Variation) Order 1987

Made

11th May 1987

Coming into force

15th May 1987

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 of and paragraph 27 of Schedule 9 to the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with the Secretary of State for the Home Department, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Greenwich (Prescribed Routes) (No.5) Traffic Order 1973 (Variation) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 15th May 1987.

**2.** The Greenwich (Prescribed Routes) (No.5) Traffic Order 1973[^f00002] shall be varied and have effect as though for the words “No. 669 Rochester Way” in article 7 of that Order there were substituted the words “No. 699 Rochester Way”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

R.M.C. Edridge

An Assistant Chief Engineer in the

Department of Transport

11th May 1987
