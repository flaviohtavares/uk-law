# The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Obstruction

No person shall wilfully obstruct a person authorised under regulation 14 who is acting in the execution of these Regulations.
