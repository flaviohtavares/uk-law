# The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Offences and penalties

Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with any requirement of these Regulations shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable –

- (a) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding £2,000;
- (b) on conviction on indictment to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year, or both.
