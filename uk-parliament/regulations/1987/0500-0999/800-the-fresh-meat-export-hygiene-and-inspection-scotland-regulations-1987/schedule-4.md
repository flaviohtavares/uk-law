# The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) (Scotland) Regulations 1987, SCHEDULE 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 4 — CONDITIONS FOR THE APPROVAL OF TRANSHIPMENT CENTRES

Regulation 4(1)(a)(iv)

The transhipment centre shall have suitable and sufficient facilities for the hygienic unloading, assembly, inspection and loading of meat, including –

- (a) a suitable loading bay; and
- (b) suitable and sufficient refrigerated accommodation readily accessible to the loading bay.
