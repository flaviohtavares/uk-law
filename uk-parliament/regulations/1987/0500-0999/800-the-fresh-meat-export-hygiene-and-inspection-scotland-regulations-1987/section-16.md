# The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Information to be provided by local authorities

Each local authority shall provide the Secretary of State with such information relating to the execution of their duties under these Regulations as he may from time to time require.
