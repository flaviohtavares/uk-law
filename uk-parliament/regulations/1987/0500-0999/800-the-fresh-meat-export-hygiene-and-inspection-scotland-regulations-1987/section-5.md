# The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Premises approved prior to 11th June 1987

Any slaughterhouse, cutting premises, cold store or transhipment centre in respect of which approval granted by the Secretary of State for the purpose of the export of fresh meat is in force immediately prior to 11th June 1987 shall be deemed to be approved in accordance with regulation 4.
