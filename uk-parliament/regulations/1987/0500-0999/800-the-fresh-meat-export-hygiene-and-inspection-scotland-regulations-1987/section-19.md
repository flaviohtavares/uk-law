# The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Application of the Food (Meat Inspection) (Scotland) Regulations 1961 to export slaughterhouses

The provisions of these Regulations are without prejudice to the application in relation to any export slaughterhouse of the Food (Meat Inspection) (Scotland) Regulations 1961, except that the provisions of regulations 4 to 9, 16, 17, 22 and 23 of, and Schedules 1, 2 and 4 to, the 1961 Regulations shall not apply in relation to any such slaughterhouse.
