# The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Enforcement by local authorities

Each local authority shall execute and enforce the provisions of these Regulations in their area with the exception of regulations 4 and 6 which shall be executed and enforced by the Secretary of State.
