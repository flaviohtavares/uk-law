# The Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Gibraltar) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 933

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Gibraltar) Order 1987

Made

18th May 1987

Laid before Parliament

17th June 1987

Coming into force

8th July 1987

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 18th day of May 1987

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty in pursuance of section 735(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Gibraltar) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 8th July 1987.

**2.** The Merchant Shipping (Amendment) Ordinance, 1987[^f00002] is hereby confirmed.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

By this Order the Queen in Council, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 735(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894, confirms a Law enacted by the Legislature of Gibraltar by which, *inter alia*, certain provisions of that Act relating to ships registered in the Colony are repealed.
