# The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 631 (S. 49)

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1987

Made

31st March 1987

Laid before Parliament

10th April 1987

Coming into force

4th May 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 14(2) and (3) and 51 of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with the Gaming Board for Great Britain in accordance with section 51(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1987, and shall come into force on 4th May 1987.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

**2.** In regulation 5(1) of the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Scotland) Regulations 1984[^f00002] (bingo charges)–

- (a) for the sum “£4” in both places where it occurs there shall be substituted the sum “£4.60”, and
- (b) for the sum “£3” in both places where it occurs there shall be substituted the sum “£3.45”.

Ian Lang

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,Scottish Office

New St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

31st March 1987

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the maximum charges which may be made for admission to gaming, on bingo club premises in Scotland.

The maximum charge in respect of a charging period other than the shorter period permitted on a Sunday is increased from £4 to £4.60.

The maximum charge in respect of the shorter charging period permitted on a Sunday is increased from £3 to £3.45.
