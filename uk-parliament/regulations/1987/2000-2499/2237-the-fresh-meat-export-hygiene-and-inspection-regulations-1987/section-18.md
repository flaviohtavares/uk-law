# The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1987, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Application of various sections of the Act

(1) Section 100 (which relates to a contravention due to some person other than the person charged), section 102 (which relates to conditions under which warranty may be pleaded as a defence) and section 103 (which relates to offences in relation to warranties and certificates of analysis) of the Act shall apply for the purposes of these Regulations as if references therein to proceedings taken or brought under that Act included references to proceedings taken or brought for an offence under these Regulations.

(2) Section 116 of the Act (which relates to protection for local government officers acting in good faith) shall have effect for the purposes of these Regulations as if references therein to that Act were references to these Regulations.
