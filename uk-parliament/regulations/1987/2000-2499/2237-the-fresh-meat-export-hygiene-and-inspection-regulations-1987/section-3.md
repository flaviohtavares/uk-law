# The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1987, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Exemption

These Regulations shall not apply to minced meat or to meat which is similarly finely divided or to fresh meat which is exported with the authority of the country of destination and intended exclusively as supplies for international organisations and military forces stationed in its territory but serving under another flag.
