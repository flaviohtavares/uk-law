# The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1987, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Revocations

(1) The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1981[^f00014], the Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) (Amendment) Regulations 1983[^f00015] and the Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) (Amendment) Regulations 1985[^f00016] are hereby revoked.

(2) The reference to the Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1981 shall be omitted from Schedule 2 to the Food (Revision of Penalties) Regulations 1982[^f00017] and Part II of the Schedule to the Food (Revision of Penalties) Regulations 1985[^f00018].
