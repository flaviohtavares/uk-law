# The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1987, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Existing approvals

Any approval to use premises as a slaughterhouse, cutting premises, cold store or transhipment centre granted or deemed to be granted under the Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1981[^f00006] and in force immediately before the commencement of these Regulations shall continue in force and have effect as if it had been granted under and subject to the provisions of these Regulations.
