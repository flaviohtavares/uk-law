# The Fresh Meat Export (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1987, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Penalties and enforcement

(1) If any person contravenes or fails to comply with any provision of these Regulations he shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding £2,000; and
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine or imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or both.

(2) Each local authority shall enforce and execute the provision of these Regulations in their district with the exception of regulations 4 and 6 which shall be enforced and executed by the appropriate Minister.
