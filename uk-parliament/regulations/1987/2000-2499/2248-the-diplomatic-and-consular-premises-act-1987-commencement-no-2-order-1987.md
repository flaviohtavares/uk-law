# The Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 2248 (C.68)

INTERNATIONAL IMMUNITIES AND PRIVILEGES

The Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1987

Made

19th December 1987

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 9 of the Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1987.

**2.** The provisions of the Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st January 1988.

Geoffrey Howe

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Foreign and Commonwealth Office

19th December 1987

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS OF THE DIPLOMATIC AND CONSULAR PREMISES ACT 1987 COMING INTO FORCE ON 1st JANUARY 1988

Article 2

| *Provisions of the Act* | *Subject matter of provisions* |
| --- | --- |
| Section 1 | Acquisition and loss by land of diplomatic or consular status. |
| Section 2 | Vesting of former diplomatic or consular premises in the Secretary of State. |
| Section 3 | Duty to sell premises vested in the Secretary of State. |
| Section 4 | Provisions supplementary to sections 2 and 3. |
| Section 5 | Interpretation. |
| Section 8 | Financial provision. |
| Schedule 1 | Provisions supplementary to sections 2 and 3. |

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st January 1988:

- section 1 of the Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987 (which provides for the acquisition and loss by land of diplomatic or consular status);
- sections 2, 3 and 4 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Act (which provide for the vesting of former diplomatic or consular premises in the Secretary of State and his duty to sell such premises);
- section 5 of the Act (interpretation); and
- section 8 of the Act (financial provision).

(This note is not part of the Order)
