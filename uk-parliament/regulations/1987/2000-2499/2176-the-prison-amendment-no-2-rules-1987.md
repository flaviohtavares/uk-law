# The Prison (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 2176

PRISONS

The Prison (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1987

Made

15th December 1987

Laid before Parliament

30th December 1987

Coming into force

1st March 1988

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 47 of the Prison Act 1952[^f00001], I hereby make the following Rules:

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Prison (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1987 and shall come into force on 1st March 1988.

**2.** The Prison Rules 1964[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

- (a) in rule 21[^f00003] paragraph (1) and the words “convicted” in paragraphs (2) and (3) shall be deleted;
- (b) in rule 22(1) the words “, or under Rule 21(1) of these Rules” shall be deleted;
- (c) in rules 50(g) and 51(4)(g)[^f00004] the words “any of the following:— (i) the right to be supplied with food and drink under Rule 21(1) of these Rules; and (ii)” shall be deleted; and
- (d) in rule 63(1) for the words “Rules 20(1) and” there shall be substituted the word “Rule”.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

15th December 1987

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Prison Rules 1964 to remove the right of an unconvicted prisoner to be supplied with food at his own expense or that of his friends.
