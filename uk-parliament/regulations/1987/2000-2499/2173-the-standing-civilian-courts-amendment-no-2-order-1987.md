# The Standing Civilian Courts (Amendment No. 2) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to amend [S.I. 1987/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2001) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that S.I.

1987 No. 2173

DEFENCE

The Standing Civilian Courts (Amendment No. 2) Order 1987

Made

16th December 1987

Laid before Parliament

17th December 1987

Coming into force

1st January 1988

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 22(4) of and paragraph 12 of Schedule 3 to the Armed Forces Act 1976[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Standing Civilian Courts (Amendment No. 2) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 1st January 1988.

## 2. Amendment

The Standing Civilian Courts (Amendment) Order 1987[^f00002] shall take effect as if for article 3(3) there were substituted:

> (3) For article 49(2)(a)(ii) there shall be substituted the following:—
>
> > - (ii) subject to paragraph (3) below, any previous convictions of his by a civil court, court-martial or a Standing Civilian Court, including a conviction by a Standing Civilian Court in respect of which the award of sentence has been deferred under paragraph 2A of Schedule 5A to the Act of 1955, except, in the case of an accused who has attained 21 years of age, any conviction before he had attained 14 years of age;
>
> .

.

George Younger

Secretary of State for Defence

Dated 16th December 1987

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 3(3) of the Standing Civilian Courts (Amendment) Order 1987 was intended to substitute fresh provision for article 49(2)(a)(ii) of the Standing Civilian Courts Order 1977 ([S.I. 1977/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1977/88)) but misdescribed that provision as article 49(ii) and omitted a comma from the text. This Order therefore corrects those errors by substituting a new article 3(3) in the Amendment Order.
