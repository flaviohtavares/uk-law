# The Airports Byelaws (Designation) (No. 2) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 2246

CIVIL AVIATION

The Airports Byelaws (Designation) (No. 2) Order 1987

Made

30th December 1987

Laid before Parliament

13th January 1988

Coming into force

3rd February 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 63(1) of the Airports Act 1986[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Airports Byelaws (Designation) (No. 2) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 3rd February 1988.

**2.** The airports specified in the Schedule hereto are hereby designated for the purposes of section 63 of the Airports Act 1986.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

David B. Mitchell

Minister of State,

Department of Transport

30th December 1987

## SCHEDULE

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Bembridge | Gloucester/Cheltenham |
| Carlisle | Redhill |
| Dundee | Swansea |

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 63(1) of the Airports Act 1986 provides that where an airport is either designated for the purposes of that section or is managed by the Secretary of State, the airport operator (whether the Secretary of State or some other person) may make byelaws for regulating the use and operation of the airport and the conduct of all persons while within the airport. This Order designates the airports listed in the Schedule for the purposes of section 63.
