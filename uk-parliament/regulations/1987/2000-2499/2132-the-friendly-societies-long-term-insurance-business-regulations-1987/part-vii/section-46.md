# The Friendly Societies (Long Term Insurance Business) Regulations 1987, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 46. Application: Part VII

This Part of these Regulations applies with respect to the determination of the amount of liabilities of societies for the purposes of these Regulations.
