# The Friendly Societies (Long Term Insurance Business) Regulations 1987, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Nature and determination of assets

The determination of the amount of long term liabilities shall take into account the nature and terms of the assets representing the long term fund and the value placed upon them and shall include appropriate provision against the effects of possible future changes in the value of the assets on their adequacy to meet the liabilities.
