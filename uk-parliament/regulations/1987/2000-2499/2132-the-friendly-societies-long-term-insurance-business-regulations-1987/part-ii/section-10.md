# The Friendly Societies (Long Term Insurance Business) Regulations 1987, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Refusal of authorisation

Where the Chief Registrar refuses to issue an authorisation for which an application has been made under regulation 5, 6 or 9 above, he shall inform the applicant society in writing of the reasons for his refusal.
