# The Friendly Societies (Long Term Insurance Business) Regulations 1987, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Exclusion

Nothing in regulation 25 or 26 shall apply to insurance business carried on outside the United Kingdom.
