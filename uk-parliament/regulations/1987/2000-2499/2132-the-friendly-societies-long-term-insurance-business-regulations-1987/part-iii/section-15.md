# The Friendly Societies (Long Term Insurance Business) Regulations 1987, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Long term classes IV and VI

For long term business of class IV or VI the required margin of solvency shall be determined by applying the first calculation.
