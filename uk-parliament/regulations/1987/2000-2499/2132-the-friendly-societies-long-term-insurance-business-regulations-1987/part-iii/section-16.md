# The Friendly Societies (Long Term Insurance Business) Regulations 1987, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Long term class V

For long term business of class V the required margin of solvency shall be equal to 1 per cent of the assets of the relevant tontine.
