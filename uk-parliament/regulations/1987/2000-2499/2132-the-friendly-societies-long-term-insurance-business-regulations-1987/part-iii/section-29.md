# The Friendly Societies (Long Term Insurance Business) Regulations 1987, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Investments of authorised societies

Section 46 (Investment of funds) of the 1974 Act shall, from the date on which a society is authorised under regulation 5 or 6 above, have effect in relation to that society as though the words in subsection (1) from “or any part thereof” to the end of that subsection were deleted.
