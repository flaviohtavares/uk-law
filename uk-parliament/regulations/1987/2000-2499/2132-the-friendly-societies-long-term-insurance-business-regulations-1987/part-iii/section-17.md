# The Friendly Societies (Long Term Insurance Business) Regulations 1987, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Supplementary business

For a contract which contains supplementary business, the required margin of solvency shall be determined by taking the aggregate of the results arrived at–

- (a) in the case of so much of the contract as is within a class of long term business, by applying the appropriate method prescribed for that class in regulation 13, 14, 15 or 16 above, and
- (b) in the case of so much of the contract as is supplementary business, by applying the method of calculation set out in Schedule 3.
