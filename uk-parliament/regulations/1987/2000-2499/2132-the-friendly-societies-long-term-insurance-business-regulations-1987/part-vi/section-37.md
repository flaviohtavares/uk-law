# The Friendly Societies (Long Term Insurance Business) Regulations 1987, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Land

The value of any land of a society (other than land held by the society as security for a debt or to which regulation 42 below applies) shall be not greater than the amount which (after deduction of the reasonable expenses of sale) would be realised if the land were sold at a price equal to the most recent proper valuation of that land which has been provided to the society and any such land of which there is no proper valuation shall be left out of account for the purposes for which this Part of these Regulations applies.
