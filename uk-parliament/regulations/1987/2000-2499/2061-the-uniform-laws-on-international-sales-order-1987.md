# The Uniform Laws on International Sales Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 2061

INTERNATIONAL SALE OF GOODS

The Uniform Laws on International Sales Order 1987

Made

26th November 1987

Laid before Parliament

4th December 1987

Coming into force

1st January 1988

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 26th day of November 1987

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, by virtue of the authority conferred on Her by section 1 (5) of the Uniform Laws on International Sales Act 1967[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Uniform Laws on International Sales Order 1987 and shall come into force on 1st January 1988.

**2.** For the Schedule to the Uniform Laws on International Sales Order 1972[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

| *Contracting State* | *Relevant territories* | *Effective date of ratification* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Kingdom of Belgium | Belgium | 18th August 1972 |
| The Republic of the Gambia | The Gambia | 5th September 1974 |
| The Federal Republic of Germany | Federal Republic of Germany and Berlin (West) | 16th April 1974 |
| The State of Israel | Israel | 18th August 1972 |
| The Kingdom of the Netherlands | The Netherlands | 18th August 1972 |
| The Republic of San Marino | San Marino | 18th August 1972 |
| The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland | The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland | 18th August 1972 |

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order provides a replacement Schedule to the Uniform Laws on International Sales Order 1972. The Schedule specifies the contracting states for the purpose of the Hague Convention of 1964 relating to a Uniform Law on the International Sale of Goods (Cmnd. 5029), and the territories in respect of which they have contracted. The Schedule is updated to include The Gambia and the Federal Republic of Germany, and to remove Italy.
