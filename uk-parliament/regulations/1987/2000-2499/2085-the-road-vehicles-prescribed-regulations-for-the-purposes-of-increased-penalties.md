# The Road Vehicles (Prescribed Regulations for the Purposes of Increased Penalties) Regulations 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 2085

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Vehicles (Prescribed Regulations for the Purposes of Increased Penalties) Regulations 1987

Made

2nd December 1987

Laid before Parliament

11th December 1987

Coming into force

1st January 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 37(3)(a) of the Vehicles (Excise) Act 1971[^f00001], as set out in paragraph 24 of Part I of Schedule 7 to that Act, and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Prescribed Regulations for the Purposes of Increased Penalties) Regulations 1987 and shall come into force on 1st January 1988.

## 2. Prescribing of regulations

The regulations of the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) Regulations 1971[^f00002] specified in column (1) of the Table below, the subject matter of which is briefly described in column (2) of the Table, are hereby prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (a) of section 37(3) of the Vehicles (Excise) Act 1971 (as set out in paragraph 24 of Part I of Schedule 7 to that Act).

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the regulations of the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) Regulations 1971 where a contravention is to attract a maximum penalty of a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale (currently £400). Contraventions of other regulations of the 1971 Regulations not hereby prescribed, and of other sets of regulations made under the Vehicles (Excise) Act 1971, continue to attract a lesser maximum penalty of a fine not exceeding level 2 (£100).
