# The Building Societies (Limit on Non-Retail Funds and Deposits) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 2131

BUILDING SOCIETIES

The Building Societies (Limit on Non-Retail Funds and Deposits) Order 1987

Made

8th December 1987

Laid before Parliament

10th December 1987

Coming into force

1st January 1988

The Building Societies Commission, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 7(15) of the Building Societies Act 1986[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Building Societies (Limit on Non-Retail Funds and Deposits) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 1st January 1988.

## 2. Limit on liabilities in respect of non-retail funds and deposits

The prescribed percentage for the purposes of section 7(3) of the Building Societies Act 1986 (which requires that the liabilities of a building society in respect of its non-retail funds and deposits shall not exceed at any time the prescribed percentage of the society’s total liabilities at that time in respect of shares in or money deposited with it) shall be 40 per cent.

In witness whereof the common seal of the Building Societies Commission is hereunto fixed, and is authenticated by me, a person authorised under paragraph 14 of Schedule 1 to the Building Societies Act 1986, on

D. B. Severn

Secretary to the Commission

7th December 1987.

We consent to this Order.

Tony Durant

David Lightbown

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

8th December 1987

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes 40% in place of 20% as the maximum percentage of a building society’s share and deposit liabilities which may be represented by non-retail funds and deposits. Liabilities in respect of non-retail funds and deposits are described in section 7(4) of the Building Societies Act 1986 as amended by the Building Societies (Non-Retail Funds and Deposits) Order 1987 (S.I. [1987/378](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/378)) and the Building Societies (Banking Institutions) Order 1987 (S.I. [1987/1670](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1670)).
