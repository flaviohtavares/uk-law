# The General Medical Council (Constitution) Amendment Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 457

MEDICAL PROFESSION

The General Medical Council (Constitution) Amendment Order 1987

Made

18th March 1987

Laid before Parliament

26th March 1987

Coming into force

1st May 1987

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 18th day of March 1987

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 1(2) of, and paragraphs 1 and 4 of Schedule 1 to, the Medical Act 1983[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the General Medical Council (Constitution) Amendment Order 1987 and shall come into force on 1st May 1987.

## 2. Amendment of the General Medical Council (Constitution) Order 1979

In paragraph (c) of article 2 of the General Medical Council (Constitution) Order 1979[^f00002] (constitution of the General Medical Council) there shall be substituted for the number “11” the number “13”.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order which is made under the Medical Act 1983 amends Article 2 of the General Medical Council (Constitution) Order 1979 so as to provide for 13 nominated members instead of 11.
