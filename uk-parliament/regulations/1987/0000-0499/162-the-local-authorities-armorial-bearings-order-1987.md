# The Local Authorities (Armorial Bearings) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 162

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Authorities (Armorial Bearings) Order 1987

Made

10th February 1987

Coming into force

1st March 1987

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of February 1987

Present

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, by virtue and in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 247 of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Armorial Bearings) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 1st March 1987.

**2.** Subject to article 4 of this Order, the town council of Longridge may bear and use the armorial bearings which were immediately before 1st April 1974 borne and used by Longridge urban district council.

**3.** Subject to article 4 of this Order, the town council of Yeovil may bear and use the armorial bearings which were immediately before 1st April 1974 borne and used by Yeovil borough council.

**4.** Armorial bearings shall not be borne or used pursuant to article 2 or 3 hereto until they have been exemplified according to the laws of arms and recorded in the College of Arms.

G. I. de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order authorises Longridge town council to bear and use the armorial bearings of the former Longridge urban district council and Yeovil town council to bear and use those of the former borough council of Yeovil.
