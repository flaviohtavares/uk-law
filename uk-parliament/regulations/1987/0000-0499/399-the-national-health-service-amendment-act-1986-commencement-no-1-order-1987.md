# The National Health Service (Amendment) Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 399 (C.11)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The National Health Service (Amendment) Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1987

Made

10th March 1987

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 8(5)(a) of the National Health Service (Amendment) Act 1986[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling me in that behalf, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the National Health Service (Amendment) Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1987.

**2.** Section 3 of the National Health Service (Amendment) Act 1986 shall come into force on 1st April 1987.

Norman Fowler

One of Her Majesty\`s Principal Secretaries of State

10th March 1987

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 1987 section 3 of the National Health Service (Amendment) Act 1986. That section concerns pharmaceutical services provided under Part II of the National Health Service Act [1977 (c. 49)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/49) and under Part II of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act [1978 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/29) and, in particular, substitutes new sections 42 and 27(2) to (5) respectively of those Acts.
