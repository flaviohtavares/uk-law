(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the fees allowable to solicitors and counsel in Scotland for civil legal aid (and for legal aid in certain proceedings relating to children) given under the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986. The fees are the same as were previously prescribed in relation to legal aid for civil proceedings given under the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1967.
