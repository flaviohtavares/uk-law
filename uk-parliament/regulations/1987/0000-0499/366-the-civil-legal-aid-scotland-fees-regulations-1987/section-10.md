# The Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Regulations 1987, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Revocation

Without prejudice to their continuation in effect for the purposes of legal aid under the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1967([^f00004]) by virtue of paragraph 3 of Schedule 4 to the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986, the following Regulations are hereby revoked:–

- The Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees in Civil Proceedings) Regulations 1984([^f00005]);
- The Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees in Civil Proceedings) Amendment Regulations 1985([^f00010]);
- The Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees in Civil Proceedings) Amendment Regulations 1986([^f00011])
