# The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (Amendment) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 4

OFFSHORE INSTALLATIONS

The Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (Amendment) Order 1987

Made

7th January 1987

Coming into Force

9th January 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 21(1) of the Oil and Gas (Enterprise) Act 1982[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (Amendment) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 9th January 1987.

**2.** In column 1 of the Schedule to the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 69) Order 1986[^f00002] for the designation “21/10—FE” there shall be substituted the designation “22/6—FE”.

Alastair Goodlad

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Energy

7th January 1987

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order amends the Offshore Installations (Safety Zones) (No. 69) Order 1986 by changing the designation of the offshore installation to which the Order applies. This is necessary because the designation of the installation has been changed.
