# The Local Land Charges (Amendment) Rules 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 389

LAND CHARGES

The Local Land Charges (Amendment) Rules 1987

Made

9th March 1987

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1987

Coming into force

1st April 1987

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 14 of the Local Land Charges Act 1975 and with the concurrence of the Treasury as to fees, hereby makes the following Rules:

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Local Land Charges (Amendment) Rules 1987 and shall come into force on 1st April 1987.

**2.** For Schedule 3 to the Local Land Charges Rules 1977[^f00002] there shall be substituted the following Schedule:

Hailsham of St. Marylebone, *C.*

Dated 5th March 1987

Dated 9th March 1987

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules increase the fees payable in connection with the registration of local land charges by 6.2%.
