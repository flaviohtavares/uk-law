# The Colleges of Education (Allowances to Governors:Prescribed Bodies) (Scotland) Regulations 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 308 (S. 19)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Colleges of Education (Allowances to Governors:Prescribed Bodies) (Scotland) Regulations 1987

Made

2nd March 1987

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1987

Coming into force

1st April 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 49(1)(e) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973[^f00001] (as read with the definition of “prescribed” in section 235(1) of that Act) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Colleges of Education (Allowances to Governors: Prescribed Bodies) (Scotland) Regulations 1987 and shall come into force on 1st April 1987.

## 2. Prescription of governing bodies of colleges of education

The governing bodies of colleges of education, constituted by the Colleges of Education (Scotland) Regulations 1987[^f00002], are hereby prescribed for the purposes of sections 45 to 47 of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973[^f00003].

John J. MacKay

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,Scottish Office

New St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

2nd March 1987

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the governing bodies of colleges of education in Scotland as bodies to which the provisions of sections 45 to 47 of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 apply, so that members of the governing bodies will be entitled to the relevant allowances under those sections.
