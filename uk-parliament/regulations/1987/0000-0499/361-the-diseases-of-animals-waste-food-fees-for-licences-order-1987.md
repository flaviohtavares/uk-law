# The Diseases of Animals (Waste Food) (Fees for Licences) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 361

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Diseases of Animals (Waste Food) (Fees for Licences) Order 1987

Made

6th March 1987

Laid before the House of Commons

9th March 1987

Coming into force

1st April 1987

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 84(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the approval of the Treasury in accordance with that section, hereby Order as follows:—

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Waste Food) (Fees for Licences) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 1st April 1987.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the appropriate Minister”means, in relation to England, the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, and in relation to Scotland or to Wales, the Secretary of State;;
- “the 1973 Order”means the Diseases of Animals (Waste Food) Order 1973[^f00002].
- “a seasonal operator”means a person who operates plant and equipment for processing waste food on any premises for a period of not more than six months in any twelve months ending with 31st March.

## 3. Fees

There shall be paid to the appropriate Minister in respect of the matters specified in column (1) of the Schedule to this Order the fees specified in column (3) of that Schedule, such fees being payable at the times specified in column (2) of that Schedule.

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 3rd March 1987.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies fees payable in respect of the granting or renewal of licences under Articles 7 and 8 of the Disease of Animals (Waste Food) Order 1973 (as amended), (article 3 and Schedule).

Licences granted under Article 7 of the 1973 Order authorise the operation of plant and equipment used for processing waste food and licences granted under Article 8 of that Order authorise premises to be used for the reception of waste food.
