# The Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 498 (S. 47)

SEEDS

The Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1987

Made

16th March 1987

Laid before Parliament

30th March 1987

Coming into force

20th April 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 16(1), (1A)(e) and (5)(a) of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with representatives of such interests as appear to him to be concerned, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1987 and shall come into force on 20th April 1987.

## 2. Revocation of previous regulations

The Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1985[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

**3.** There shall be paid in advance in respect of matters arising under the Seed Potatoes Regulations 1984[^f00003] referred to in the Schedule hereto or under any regulations amending or superseding them the fees respectively specified in the second column of the said Schedule opposite the references to such matters.

**4.** The said fees shall be payable to the Secretary of State.

**5.** The Secretary of State may exempt any person or class of person or persons generally from compliance in whole or in part with the provisions of these Regulations in relation to payment of fees and any exemptions so made may be made subject to conditions.

John J. MacKay

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,Scottish Office

New St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations which apply to Scotland only and which revoke and supersede the Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1985 prescribe the fees payable in respect of certain matters arising under the Seed Potatoes Regulations 1984.

The fees for crop inspection of all classes have been increased by £1.50 per half-hectare, representing an increase of between 9% and 10%. Other fees remain unchanged.
