# The Motor Vehicles (Competitions and Trials) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 346 (S. 26)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Competitions and Trials) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1987

Made

27th February 1987

Laid before Parliament

12th March 1987

Coming into force

2nd April 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 15(2) of the Road Traffic Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with such representative organisations as he thought fit in accordance with the provisions of section 199(2) of the said Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** (1) These Regulations shall come into force on 2nd April 1987 and may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Competitions and Trials) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1987.

(2) These Regulations shall apply only in Scotland.

**2.** Regulation 7 of the Motor Vehicles (Competitions and Trials) (Scotland) Regulations 1976[^f00002] shall be amended by the substitution therein of“£40” for“£34” and“£23” for“£19”.

Michael Ancram

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,Scottish Office

New St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

27th February 1987

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Competitions and Trials) (Scotland) Regulations 1976 so as to increase the fees payable to the Royal Scottish Automobile Club for the authorisation of events insofar as they are to take place, in whole or in part, in Scotland.

The existing and the increased fees are as follows:-

|  | *Existing* | *Increased* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *(a) Route mileage up to 50 miles* | £34.00 | £40.00 |
| *(b) For each subsequent 50 miles or part thereof* | £19.00 | £23.00 |
| *(c) Cancellation fee* | £10.00 | No change |
