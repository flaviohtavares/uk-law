# The Birmingham—Great Yarmouth Trunk Road (High House and Other Diversions) (Amendment) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 100

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Birmingham—Great Yarmouth Trunk Road (High House and Other Diversions) (Amendment) Order 1987

Made

23rd January 1987

Coming into force

27th February 1987

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The Birmingham—Great Yarmouth Trunk Road (High House and Other Diversions) Order 1949[^f00003] is hereby amended by the deletion from the Schedule of paragraph (3) of Part I and paragraph (3) of Part II.

**2.** This Order shall come into force on 27th February 1987 and may be cited as the Birmingham—Great Yarmouth Trunk Road (High House and Other Diversions) (Amendment) Order 1987.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

G. D. Crane

Regional Director Eastern Region

Department of Transport

23rd January 1987
