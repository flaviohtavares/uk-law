# The Police Pensions Regulations 1987, SCHEDULE G

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE G — Additional and Further Contributions

Regulation G3

**1.** Where, in pursuance of paragraph (2) of Regulation G3 and such an election as is mentioned in paragraph (1) of that Regulation, additional or further pension contributions continue to be payable by a regular policeman, those contributions shall be payable at the rate specified in the second or, as the case may be, in the third column of the following Table opposite to the number of completed years of pensionable service in the first column thereof reckonable by the regular policeman immediately before 1st April 1973 otherwise than by reason of service on or after 1st April 1972 as a member of a police force or of the Royal Ulster Constabulary subject, however, to paragraph 2.

**2.** For the purposes of paragraph 1, no account shall be taken of any service reckonable as pensionable service by virtue of section 10 of the Police Pensions Act 1921[^f00096].
