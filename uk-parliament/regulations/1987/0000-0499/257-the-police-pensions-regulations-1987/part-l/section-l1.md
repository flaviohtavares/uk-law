# The Police Pensions Regulations 1987, section L1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## L1. Authorities responsible for payment of awards

(1) An award which is payable to or in respect of a person by reason of his having served as a regular policeman shall be payable by the police authority of the force in which he last served as such.

(2) An award which is payable to or in respect of a person by reason of his having been injured while serving as a member of a police force other than a regular policeman shall be payable by the police authority of the force in which he was serving when he received the injury.
