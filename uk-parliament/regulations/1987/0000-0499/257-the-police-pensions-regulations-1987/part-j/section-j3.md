# The Police Pensions Regulations 1987, section J3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## J3. Former reversionary member of home police force

Notwithstanding anything in these Regulations, where a person who has been a reversionary member of a home police force becomes or has become a regular policeman in a home police force and an award is payable to or in respect of him by reason of his having received an injury in the execution of his duty as an overseas policeman without his own default, the award shall not be less than it would have been if he had not after being a reversionary member of a home police force become such a regular policeman.
