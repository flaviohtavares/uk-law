# The Police Pensions Regulations 1987, section A2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## A2. Supplementary provisions

These Regulations have effect subject to—

- (a) the transitional provisions set out in Part III of Schedule 1 to the Police Pensions (Supplementary Provisions) Regulations 1987 (“the Supplementary Regulations”), and
- (b) the Police Pensions (War Service) Regulations 1979[^f00005], the Police Pensions (War Service) (Transferees) Regulations 1985[^f00006] and the Police (Injury Benefit) Regulations 1987[^f00007] as amended by Regulations 5, 6 and 7 of the Supplementary Regulations.
