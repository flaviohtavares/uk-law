# The Police Pensions Regulations 1987, section A1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## A1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Police Pensions Regulations 1987 and come into force on the date specified in that behalf in the Police Pensions (Supplementary Provisions) Regulations 1987[^f00004].

## Annotations

[^f00004]: Footnote: [S.I. 1987/256](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/256); Regulation 3 provides that the present Regulations shall come into force on 1st April 1987.
