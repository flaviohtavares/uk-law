# The Police Pensions Regulations 1987, section D2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## D2. Child’s special allowance

(1) This Regulation shall apply to a child of a member of a police force who dies or has died as the result of an injury received without his own default in the execution of his duty.

(2) Subject to Regulations D5 (*limitations*) and E8 (*increase during first 13 weeks*), a child to whom this Regulation applies shall be entitled to a special allowance calculated in accordance with Part II of Schedule D.
