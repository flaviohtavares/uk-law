# The Police Pensions Regulations 1987, section I6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## I6. Servicemen who do not resume service in their former force

Where a serviceman who ceased to serve as a regular policeman in order to undertake a period of relevant service in the armed forces does not or did not resume service in his former force within a month of the end of that period, he shall be treated for the purposes of Regulations A10, A16, B5(2), B6, F3, F4 and F5 as having left his former force at the end of his period of relevant service in the armed forces.
