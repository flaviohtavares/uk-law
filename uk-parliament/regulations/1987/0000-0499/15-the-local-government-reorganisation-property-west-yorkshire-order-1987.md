# The Local Government Reorganisation (Property) (West Yorkshire) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 15

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Reorganisation (Property) (West Yorkshire) Order 1987

Made

12th January 1987

Laid before Parliament

21st January 1987

Coming into force

2nd March 1987

Whereas the West Yorkshire Residuary Body, acting pursuant to section 67(2) of the Local Government Act 1985[^f00001], has submitted proposals to the Secretary of State for the transfer of certain of its properties to local authorities, and he has decided to give effect to those proposals without modification:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferrred on him by section 67(3) of that Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Local Government Reorganisation (Property) (West Yorkshire) Order 1987, and shall come into force on 2nd March 1987.

**2.** There shall vest in each local authority mentioned in the Schedule hereto—

- (a) the relevant land so mentioned; and
- (b) all rights and liabilities acquired or incurred exclusively in connection with that land.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order concerns certain properties formerly owned by the West Yorkshire Metropolitan County Council and used for highways purposes. This order transfers those properties to the councils for the districts in which they are situated.
