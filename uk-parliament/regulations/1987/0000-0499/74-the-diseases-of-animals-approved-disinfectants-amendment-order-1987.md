# The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants)(Amendment) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 74

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants)(Amendment) Order 1987

Made

16th January 1987

Coming into Force

31st January 1987

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1, 7(1)(a), (b) and (c) and (2) and 23(f) and (g) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby order as follows:—

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 31st January 1987.

## 2. Amendment

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:—

- (a) for the provisions of Schedule 1 to that Order (which lists disinfectants approved by the Minister) there shall be substituted the provisions of Schedule 1 to this Order;
- (b) for the provisions of Schedule 2 to that Order (which lists disinfectants subject to transitional provisions) there shall be substituted the provisions of Schedule 2 to this Order; and
- (c) in Article 6(2) of that Order (which relates to disinfectants subject to transitional provisions) for the words “31st December 1986” there shall be substituted the words “30th June 1987”.

## 4. Revocation

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) Order 1986[^f00003] and the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1986[^f00004] are revoked.

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 14th January 1987.

Michael Jopling

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Nicholas Edwards
