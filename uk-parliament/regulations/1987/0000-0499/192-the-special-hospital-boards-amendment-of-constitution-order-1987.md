# The Special Hospital Boards (Amendment of Constitution) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 192

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Special Hospital Boards (Amendment of Constitution) Order 1987

Made

11th February 1987

Laid before Parliament

20th February 1987

Coming into force

13th March 1987

The Secretary of State for Social Services, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 11 of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Special Hospital Boards (Amendment of Constitution) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 13th March 1987.

## 2. Amendment of Orders

(1) Article 4 (constitution) of the Rampton Hospital Board (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1986[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting “9” for “7”.

(2) Article 4 (constitution) of the Broadmoor Hospital Board (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1986[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting “9” for “7”.

(3) Article 4 (constitution) of the Moss Side and Park Lane Hospitals Board (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1986[^f00004] shall be amended by substituting “9” for “7”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Services.

Trumpington

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Health and Social Security

11th February 1987

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Rampton Hospital Board (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1986, the Broadmoor Hospital Board (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1986, and the Moss Side and Park Lane Hospitals Board (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1986 so as to increase maximum membership of the three special health authorities established by those Orders from, in each case, seven members to nine members.
