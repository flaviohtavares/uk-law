# The Public Order Act 1986 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 198 (C.4)

PUBLIC ORDER

The Public Order Act 1986 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1987

Made

13th February 1987

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 41 of the Public Order Act 1986[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Public Order Act 1986 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1987.

(2) In this Order“the 1986 Act” means the Public Order Act 1986.

**2.** The provisions of the 1986 Act specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st April 1987.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State Home Office

13th February 1987
