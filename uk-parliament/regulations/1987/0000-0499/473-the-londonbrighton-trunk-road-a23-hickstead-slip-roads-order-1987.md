# The London—Brighton Trunk Road (A23 Hickstead Slip Roads) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 473

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The London—Brighton Trunk Road (A23 Hickstead Slip Roads) Order 1987

Made

12th February 1987

Coming into force

1st May 1987

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 41 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The new highways which the Secretary of State proposes to construct along the routes described in the Schedule to this Order which connect the main new trunk road with other highways at the places stated in the Schedule (the highways along these routes being in this Order referred to as “the slip roads”), shall become trunk roads as from the date when this Order comes into force.

**2.** The centre lines of the slip roads are indicated by heavy black lines on the deposited plan.

**3.** The Secretary of State directs as respects any part of a highway which crosses the route of any of the slip roads that—

- (a) where the highway is a highway maintainable at the public expense by a local highway authority, the part in question shall be maintained by that authority; and
- (b) where the highway is not a highway so maintainable under a special enactment or by reason of tenure, enclosure or prescription, the Secretary of State shall be under no duty to maintain the part in question,

until, in either case, a date to be specified in a notice given by the Secretary of State to the highway authority for that highway. The date specified will not be later than the date on which the relevant route is opened for the purpose of through traffic.

**4.** In this Order—

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| - (i) “the deposited plan” | means the plan numbered CSE 205/2/26/02, marked “The London—Brighton Trunk Road (A23 Hickstead Slip Roads) Order 1987”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, Marsham Street, London, SW1P 3PY; |
| - (ii) “the main new trunk road” | means the Trunk Road to be constructed by the Secretary of State in pursuance of the London—Brighton Trunk Road (A23 Hickstead) Order 1987[^f00003]; |
| - (iii) “the Trunk Road” | means the London-Brighton Trunk Road (A23). |

**5.** This Order shall come into force on 1st May 1987 and may be cited as the London—Brighton Trunk Road (A23 Hickstead Slip Roads) Order 1987.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

D. Gruffydd Jones

Regional Director, South East Region,

Department of Transport

12th February 1987

## THE SCHEDULE — ROUTES OF THE SLIP ROADS

The routes of the slip roads are the following—

(1) *Junction with Jobs Lane, Hickstead, West Sussex*A route to connect the southbound carriageway of the main new trunk road to Jobs Lane, Hickstead (the slip road along this route being given the reference letter D on the deposited plan).

(2) *Junction of the main new trunk road with the Trunk Road south of Hickstead, West Sussex*A route south of the junction of Jobs Lane, Hickstead and the Trunk Road, to connect two sections of the Trunk Road on the alignment of the Trunk Road (the slip road along this route being given the reference letter E on the deposited plan).
