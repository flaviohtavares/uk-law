# The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987, section 26

> Text as at 2026-07-16.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 26. Income support

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to regulation 21 (direct credit transfer), Schedule 7[^c-69effa80a52c0ca3609a6b4f2a0067c1] shall have effect for determining the [^c-664e0e2aed7e2d81bc4998344f7c7e77]... time at which income support is to be paid[^c-791a3610c6aa11362dd975ebd64f4b83]... and the day when entitlement to income support is to begin.

(2)[^c-3e52b6e24f8b1b0ec8729208d18f45c9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-3e52b6e24f8b1b0ec8729208d18f45c9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Where the entitlement to income support is less than 10 pence or, in the case of a beneficiary to whom section 23A[^c-c602c72090697386a993af1c9b07652e] of the Social Security Act 1986 applies, £5, that amount shall not be payable unless the claimant is also entitled to payment of any other benefit with which income support may be paid[^c-22ef9e4c6cff88a18771c32516e00618] under arrangements made by the Secretary of State.
