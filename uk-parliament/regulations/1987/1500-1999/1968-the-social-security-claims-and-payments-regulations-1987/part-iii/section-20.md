# The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987, section 20

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 20. Time of payment: general provision[^key-2fabd280d3722a6b4d0f04f735c32a84]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to regulations 21 to 26B, benefit shall be paid in accordance with an award as soon as is reasonably practicable after the award has been made.
