## 26B. State pension credit[^c-e8105643ec9d96b55e80706d8b7433e6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Except where paragraph (2) or regulation 26BA[^c-c4baea142a9c59908872e80e4b9a8407] applies, state pension credit shall be payable on Mondays, but subject to regulation 21 where payment is by direct credit transfer[^c-36854c8f35077c2827deb1a37c62d6d6].

(2) State pension credit shall be payable—

- (a)[^c-c5280062eddb7f938f170887be26715c] on the same day as any—
  - (i) state pension under Part 1 of the Pensions Act 2014; or
  - (ii) retirement pension,

  is payable to the claimant; or
- (b) on such other day of the week as the Secretary of State may, in the particular circumstances of the case, determine.

(3)[^c-31c6de135794aedc06c076eaa60f18b1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Subject to regulation 26BA, state pension credit[^c-d5426206dc3d795dace924885a5f1f52] paid otherwise than in accordance with regulation 21[^c-ae986b78d99b97c0e721e3626207e055] shall be paid weekly in advance.

(5) Where the amount of state pension credit payable is less than £1.00 per week, the Secretary of State may direct that it shall be paid at such intervals, not exceeding 13 weeks, as may be specified in the direction.
