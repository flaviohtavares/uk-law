# The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987, section 22

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 22. Long term benefits[^c-6f77e69b479454db2fca0eefa24cdfe7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-4815f70258239544728c11e8cf2303de] Subject to the provisions of this regulation and regulations 22A, 22C and 25(1)[^c-5678baba3128c4478e51ac71143ed49c], long term benefits may be paid at intervals of four weeks in arrears, weekly in advance or, where the beneficiary agrees, at intervals not exceeding 13 weeks in arrears[^c-e0acbcc8cb7fb7f6e0e95d4152ad797c].

(1A) Subject to paragraph (1B), disability[^c-942091aeba0a76d6c0cf89f562bb46a9] living allowance shall be paid at intervals of four weeks.

(1B)[^c-ea7edfff0df589523fb1b9b511f17e95] The Secretary of State may, in any particular case or class of case, arrange that attendance allowance or disability living allowance shall be paid at such other intervals not exceeding four weeks as may be specified.

(2) Where the amount of long-term benefit payable is less than £5.00[^c-376c59e3b987055380ed6504eb26e497] a week the Secretary of State may direct that it shall be paid (whether in advance or in arrears) at such intervals as may be specified not exceeding 12 months.

(3) Schedule 6 specifies the days of the week on which the various long term benefits are payable.
