# The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987, section 28

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Fractional amounts of benefit

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-8cbf9d4a515fa41d2d576a7e4802e533] Subject to paragraph (2),[^key-d2eef7ce2d932f5e61148c3cd3bc1205] where the amount of any benefit payable would, but for this regulation, include a fraction of a penny, that fraction shall be disregarded if it is less than a half penny and shall otherwise be treated as a penny.

(2)[^key-7a8d93f581c9abb15fa8db6b5f1f8e88] Where the amount of any maternity allowance payable would, but for this regulation, include a fraction of a penny, that fraction shall be treated as a penny.
