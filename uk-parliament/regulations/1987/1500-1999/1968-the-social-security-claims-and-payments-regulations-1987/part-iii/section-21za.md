# The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987, section 21ZA

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 21ZA. Payment of arrears of benefit by instalments[^key-1247bbad29ad1244112c3fbf41c83498]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Except where regulation 23 applies, the Secretary of State may pay arrears of benefit in instalments where—

- (a) the Secretary of State considers it is necessary for protecting the interests of the beneficiary; and
- (b) the beneficiary agrees that those arrears may be paid in instalments.
