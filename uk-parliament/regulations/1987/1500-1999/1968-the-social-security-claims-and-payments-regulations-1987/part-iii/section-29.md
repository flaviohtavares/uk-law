# The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987, section 29

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29. Payment to a person under age 18[^c-e7afde91dfaa2575ad8480bac468fcf5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where benefit is paid to a person under the age of 18 (whether on his own behalf or on behalf of another) a direct credit transfer under regulation 21 into that person’s account, or the receipt by him of a payment made by some other means,[^c-e247b708f049387122c35da6c40983d9] shall be a sufficient discharge to the Secretary of State or the Board[^c-ae57ff4b80a253ff3664a4e670e8ec63].
