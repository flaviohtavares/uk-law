# The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987, section 11

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 11. Special provisions where it is certified that a woman is expected to be confined or where she has been confined

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where in a certificate issued or having effect as issued under the Social Security (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1976 [^c2180870] it has been certified that it is to be expected that a woman will be confined, and she makes a claim for maternity allowance in expectation of that confinement any such claim may, unless the Secretary of State otherwise directs, be treated as a claim for incapacity benefit[^c-08aab1163f86cab790071a978a9314ea], severe disablement allowance or an employment and support allowance,[^c-0709b6c8e3559d9ef038040d22a8c2ca] made in respect of any days in the period beginning with either–

- (a) the beginning of the 6th week before the expected week of confinement; or
- (b) the actual date of confinement,

whichever is the earlier, and ending in either case on the 14th day after the actual date of confinement.

(2) Where, in a certificate issued under the Social Security (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1976 it has been certified that a woman has been confined and she claims maternity allowance within three months[^c-30a54fd7dce2676692cbb061efbb7451] of that date, her claim may be treated in the alternative or in addition as a claim for incapacity benefit[^c-e340427ef7f71f86db22e1e88601f2bd], severe disablement allowance or an employment and support allowance,[^c-9cd38694bc7d63d91635535552012ac4] for the period beginning with the date of her confinement and ending 14 days after that date.
