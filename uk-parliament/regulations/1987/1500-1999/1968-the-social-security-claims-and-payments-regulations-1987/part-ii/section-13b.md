# The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987, section 13B

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 13B. Advance claim for and award of disability working allowance[^key-e295359b1f36c979798150c69917a623]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where a person makes a claim for disability working allowance on or after 10th March 1992 and before 7th April 1992 the Secretary of State[^key-bf438f7ef256bdc31da26a83c8b45007] may–

- (a) treat the claim as if it were made for a period beginning on 7th April 1992; and
- (b) award benefit accordingly, subject to the condition that the person satisfies the requirements for entitlement on 7th April 1992.

(2) An award under paragraph (1)(b) shall be reviewed by the Secretary of State if the requirements for entitlement are found not to have been satisfied on7th April 1992.
