# The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987, section 4ZC

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4ZC. Electronic claims for benefit[^c-f16390d9591127ce0503f447676b23eb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any claim for benefit in relation to which this regulation applies, and any certificate, notice, information or evidence given in connection with that claim, may be made or given by means of an electronic communication, in accordance with the provisions set out in Schedule 9ZC.

(2)[^c-c15744e497579a1a821af4024c4dd025] This regulation applies to the following benefits—

- (a) carer’s allowance;
- (b) attendance allowance;
- (ba)[^c-eb279893fd86184883be865fbe9a618d] bereavement support payment;
- (c) disability living allowance;
- (d) graduated retirement benefit;
- (e) a jobseeker’s allowance;
- (f) a retirement pension;
- (g) state pension under Part 1 of the Pensions Act 2014;
- (h) shared additional pension;
- (i) industrial injuries benefit;
- (j) an employment and support allowance.
- (k)[^c-4e3d79df6a4c397db68a8d309ea11193] state pension credit.
- (l)[^c-eb7fefc058c45ea517502c7af61729a4] a social fund funeral payment;
- (m) a social fund payment in respect of maternity expenses;
- (n) maternity allowance.
