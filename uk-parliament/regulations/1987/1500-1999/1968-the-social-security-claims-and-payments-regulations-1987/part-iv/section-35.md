# The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987, section 35

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. Deductions which may be made from benefit and paid to third parties[^c-8790424d11b428f4421460909c7e4f94][^c-60aae49caed60f57ccf88664e166beaa]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) [^c-054760e9278d32a3680f55f3fb588194]... Deductions[^c-e1dcd3679aa2d18ff3b534de29ec56bf] may be made from benefit and direct payments may be made to third parties on behalf of a beneficiary in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 9 and Schedule 9B[^c-3c3a29bf785104a9d976f4a6e94d65e8].

(2) Where a social fund payment for maternity or funeral expenses or expenses for heating which appear to the Secretary of State to have been or to be likely to be incurred in cold weather[^c-5ced70b17eda6d22ed5849ba45d0b171] is made, wholly or in part, in respect of a debt which is, or will be, due to a third person, the payment may be, and in the case of funeral expenses shall be, made to that person and where an instrument of payment is made payable to that person it may be sent to the beneficiary[^c-75be32c73a206873c1c26889c305efde].
