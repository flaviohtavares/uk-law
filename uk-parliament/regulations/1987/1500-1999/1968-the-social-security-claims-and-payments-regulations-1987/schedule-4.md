## SCHEDULE 4 — PRESCRIBED TIMES FOR CLAIMING BENEFIT

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 19(1)

<table>
<tfoot>
<tr><td colspan="2">For the purposes of this Schedule–<br>“<strong>actual date of confinement</strong>” means the date of the birth of the child or, if the woman is confined of twins or a greater number of children, the date of the birth of the last of them; and<br>“<strong>confinement</strong>” means labour resulting in the birth of a living child, or labour after 24 weeks of pregnancy resulting in the birth of a child whether alive or dead.</td></tr>
