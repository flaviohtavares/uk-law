# The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987, section 45

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. Power for the Secretary of State to terminate an arrangement

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State may terminate an arrangement for the payment of disability living allowance by virtue of entitlement to the mobility component at the higher rate[^key-9e7f20dd6d3dde8f7b6a68b68e775c71] on behalf of a beneficiary under regulation 44 on such date as he shall decide–

- (a) if requested to do so by the owner of the vehicle to which the arrangement relates, or
- (b) where it appears to him that the arrangement is causing undue hardship to the beneficiary and that it should be terminated before the end of any of the periods specified in regulation 44(3) or 44(4).
