# The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987, section 14

## 14. Persons of a prescribed description[^c-f652897b5bbb37683cfa49ab833028ae]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person of a prescribed description for the purposes of section 20(11) of the Act as it applies to income support (definition of the family) and section 23(1) and (3)[^c-76995658b399ac4bbfe89f869fd5094a] of the Act (trade disputes) is a person who falls within the definition of qualifying young person in section 142 of the Contributions and Benefits Act (child and qualifying young person)[^c-df5513d865b0f6382a2150db48f86bd9], and in these Regulations such a person is referred to as a “**young person**”.

(2)[^c-bb2b38089218291e742699efe242adce] Paragraph (1) shall not apply to a person who is—

- (a)[^c-f68042c4b8082f1e926d8574a21d4c0e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) entitled to income support or would, but for section 134(2) (provision against dual entitlement of members of family) of the Contributions and Benefits Act, be so entitled; [^c-da92c689a9aa14a2bc84386f9a33b497]...
- (c) a person to whom section 6 of the Children (Leaving Care) Act 2000 (exclusion from benefits) applies [^c-17b56a7ceb9c60900e9431e9fcc81550]...[^c-1a6651081c781ca39f402e301d8633b7]
- (d) entitled to an employment and support allowance or would, but for paragraph 6(1)(d) of Schedule 1 to the Welfare Reform Act (conditions of entitlement to income-related employment and support allowance), be so entitled; or[^c-d02ddfc752fa45fc2113d54054d86beb]
- (e) entitled to universal credit

(3)[^c-95e5158986b4a212f819ff70085c467f] A person of a prescribed description for the purposes of section 137(1) of the Contributions and Benefits Act as it applies to income support (definition of family) includes a child or young person in respect of whom section 145A of that Act applies for the purposes of entitlement to child benefit but only for the period prescribed under section 145A(1) of that Act.
