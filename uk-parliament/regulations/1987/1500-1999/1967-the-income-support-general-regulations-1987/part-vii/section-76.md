# The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1987/1967
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 76. Reduction in certain cases[^key-1383d0e6c4020878699e1d62542181cb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

There shall be deducted from the amount of income support which would, but for this regulation, be payable for a part-week—

- (a) in the case of a claimant to whom regulation 22A (reduction in applicable amount where the claimant is appealing against a decision[^key-47c494e1649a6429be88a71a03fd8020] which embodies a determination[^key-e18ceaa1cccf43e8b3643e6d05e2fda2] that he is not incapable of work) applies, the proportion of the relevant amount specified therein appropriate to the number of days in the part-week;
- (b) where regulation 75(f) (modifications in the calculation of income) applies, one-half of the amount disregarded under regulation 75(f) less the weekly amount of any disregard under Schedule 8 or 9 appropriate to that payment.
