# The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987, section 2A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1987/1967

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2A. Disapplication of section 1(1A) of the Administration Act[^c-03628f12073802fcf064867c760ba490]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 1(1A) of the Administration Act (requirement to state national insurance number) shall not apply—

- (a) to a child or young person in respect of whom income support is claimed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1be53167fb6bddf487c9d971232a6dca]
- (b) to a partner in respect of whom a claim for income support is made or treated as made before 5th October 1998[^c-14f8b3b53c46b5e8e2a6ceba5dddf9d6].
- (c)[^c-03edda57b26f9a666e24a877944db28f] to a person who—
  - (i) is a person in respect of whom a claim for income support is made;
  - (ii) is subject to immigration control within the meaning of section 115(9)(a) of the Immigration and Asylum Act;
  - (iii) is not entitled to any income support for the purposes of section 124 of the Contributions and Benefits Act; and
  - (iv) has not previously been allocated a national insurance number.
