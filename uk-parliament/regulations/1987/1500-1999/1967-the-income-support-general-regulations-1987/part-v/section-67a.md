# The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987, section 67A

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 67A. Further disregard of student’s income[^key-936eb4fd3da0375fc95866c1e4877831]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where any part of a student’s income has already been taken into account for the purposes of assessing his entitlement to a grant , student loan or postgraduate[^key-42e444323c650c64a9f814f1b8bc41bc] [^key-e4f7c649489729eb67007f2b9f9a5eb4]... loan, the amount taken into account shall be disregarded in assessing that student’s income.
