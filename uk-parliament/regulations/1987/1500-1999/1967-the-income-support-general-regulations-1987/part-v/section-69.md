# The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987, section 69

## 69. Disregard of changes occurring during summer vacation[^key-2890018d29720040dc887c1cf3b991dc]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In calculating a student's income there shall be disregarded[^key-8a88ec119d3093776d1bf4d69b8eabe7] any change in the standard maintenance grant occurring in the recognised summer vacation appropriate to the student's course, if that vacation does not form part of his period of study, from the date on which the change occurred up to the end of that vacation.
