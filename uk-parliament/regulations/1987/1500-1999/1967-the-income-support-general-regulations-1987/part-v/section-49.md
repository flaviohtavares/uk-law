# The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987, section 49

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 49. Calculation of capital in the United Kingdom[^key-92c5aeed595d912e0f1da70f51fb1ec5][^key-bddfdf69553edeb5c3d5072df4f59714]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Capital which a claimant possesses in the United Kingdom shall be calculated at its current market or surrender value less—

- (a) where there would be expenses attributable to sale, 10 per cent; and
- (b) the amount of any incumbrance secured on it.
