# The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987, section 68

## 68. Income treated as capital[^key-8687f879e1a2ed7197f74c57f7d26895]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-799cb04955f176e3ff1b1b3458a36568] Any amount by way of a refund of tax deducted from a student's income shall be treated as capital.

(2) An amount paid from access funds as a single lump sum shall be treated as capital.

(3) An amount paid from access funds as a single lump sum which is intended and used for an item other than food, ordinary clothing or footwear [^key-16f41b1d662bb3fefc184fc504565bd8]..., household fuel, rent for which housing benefit is payable or any housing costs[^key-4ea3e4ce0cce383f9616d403c590ecb4] to the extent that they are met under regulation 17(1)(e) or 18(1)(f) (housing costs) [^key-feb12e17cfc7645b8ce92fb6db340222]..., of a single claimant or, as the case may be, of any other member of his family *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_6eafca25-f3a1-4310-9265-f730f9aff82e]his partner, or which is used for an item other than any council tax or water charges for which that claimant or member is liable *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_cde9810d-c974-4bd7-8b68-168335bfb918]partner is liable shall be disregarded as capital but only for a period of 52 weeks from the date of the payment.

(4)[^key-56dbdccaa25a9a52e13a6a630219ceb7] In paragraph (3) “ordinary clothing or footwear” means clothing or footwear for normal daily use, but it does not include—

- (a) school uniforms, or
- (b) clothing or footwear used solely for sporting activities.
