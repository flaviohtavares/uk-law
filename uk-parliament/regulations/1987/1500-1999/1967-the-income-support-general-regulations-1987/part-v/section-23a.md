>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 23A. Income of participants in the self-employment route...[^c-cdf4bfb01f91500b42196f9af47f7b99][^c-03daa001a864ade25f4abfe2fca0b5a1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Chapters II, III, IV, V, VII and VIIA of this Part and regulations 62 to 66A, 68 and 69 shall not apply to any income which is to be calculated in accordance with Chapter IVA of this Part (participants in the self-employment route[^c-c46e3f5c300fc646c7b25ca1750a66c6]...).
