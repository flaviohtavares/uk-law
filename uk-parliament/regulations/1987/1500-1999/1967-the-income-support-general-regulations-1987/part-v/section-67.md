# The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1987/1967

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 67. Disregard of contribution[^c-948901808291359410ce62a9eeac1ea3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the claimant or his partner is a student and, for the purposes of assessing a contribution to the student’s grant[^c-d02c32a60f135d438d5f77f757800597] or student loan[^c-217556e437f757cfcee9b5b741ec23f3], the other partner’s income has been taken into account, an amount equal to that contribution shall be disregarded for the purposes of assessing that other partner’s income.
