## 47. Disregard of capital of child or young person[^key-e6f7f3177dd3cade9a325dd1275832c1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The capital of a child or young person who is a member of the claimant's family shall not be treated as capital of the claimant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b8271a2c98d5596851660bc9e4595d28]
