# The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1987/1967

## 48. Income treated as capital[^c-98f458e545d106afffde5542dbf746ea]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any [^M_F_a74667d3-dcb2-420f-ac64-0c0e29a09b48]... bounty derived from employment to which paragraph 7 of Schedule 8 applies and paid at intervals of at least one year[^c-47d2f3e374bfe29f5d3cc17f53f14067] shall be treated as capital.

(2) Except in the case of an amount to which section 23(5) (a) (ii) of the Act (refund of tax in trade disputes cases) or regulation 41(4) (capital treated as income)[^c-5e732106044bd6860cca66f5916bb19f] applies, any amount by way of a refund of income tax deducted from profits or emoluments chargeable to income tax under Schedule D or E shall be treated as capital.

(3) Any holiday pay which is not earnings under regulation 35(1) (d) (earnings of employed earners) shall be treated as capital.

(4) Except any income derived from capital disregarded under paragraph 1, 2, 4, 6, 12[^c-f23def6a54b67b9c66804985a4f029d7] , 25 to 28, 44 or 45[^c-4fb48832b23ad08bf2d87140f02bb2c9] of Schedule 10, any income derived from capital shall be treated as capital but only from the date it is normally due to be credited to the claimant's account.

(5) Subject to paragraph (6), in the case of employment as an employed earner, any advance of earnings or any loan made by the claimant's employer shall be treated as capital.

(6)[^c-d8ca4194619a711917436a289452a503] Paragraph (5) shall not apply to a person to whom section 23 of the Act (trade disputes) applies or in respect of whom section 20(3) of the Act (conditions of entitlement to income support) has effect as modified by section 23A(b) (effect of return to work).

(7) Any payment under section 30 of the Prison Act 1952 [^c2180779] (payments for discharged prisoners) or allowance under section 17 of the Prisons (Scotland) Act 1952 [^c2180780] (allowances to prisoners on discharge) shall be treated as capital.

(8)[^c-218c0d5193b79ef504722c47d27828d3] Any payment made by a local authority, which represents arrears of payments under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1f52ecfb0165cdeb3f56359ce51bc47]

- (a) paragraph 15 of Schedule 1 to the Children Act 1989 (power of a local authority to make contributions to a person with whom a child lives as a result of a residence order); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1f52ecfb0165cdeb3f56359ce51bc47]
- (b) section 34(6) or as the case may be, section 50 of the Children Act 1975 (payments towards maintenance for children), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1f52ecfb0165cdeb3f56359ce51bc47]

shall be treated as capital. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d1f52ecfb0165cdeb3f56359ce51bc47]

(8A) Any payment made by an authority, as defined in Article 2 of the Children Order which represents arrears of payments under Article 15 of, and paragraph 17 of Schedule 1 to, that Order (contribution by an authority to child’s maintenance), shall be treated as capital. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d425d7275e1dbb0be4f715eb21d421d6]

(9)[^c-0ab6816e66d6eaae8f6b2bdaf8ec8429] Any charitable or voluntary payment which is not made or not due to be made at regular intervals, other than one to which paragraph (10) applies, shall be treated as capital.

(10) This paragraph applies to a payment—

- (a) which is made to a person to whom section 23 of the Act (trade disputes) applies or in respect of whom section 20(3) of the Act (conditions of entitlement to income support) has effect as modified by section 23A(b) of the Act (effect of return to work) or to a member of the family *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9dd9f1967e0d5a081e6ede6b179ca5f7] or to the partner of such a person;
- (ab)[^c-311fb0e69230d4e7ad4c8b870ecf4be7] which is a Grenfell Tower payment;
- (ac)[^c-f28054433fafe0d3f2177a02c66e9f9c] which is a child abuse payment;
- (ad) which is a Windrush payment; [^c-08610ca89ebe5a340b2305452f10b965]...
- (ae)[^c-6021d3c09b8fe048f547cebb37825db5] which is a Post Office compensation payment;
- (af)[^c-3a9f5d43296a42e72ab0763673391d1b] which is an LGBT Financial Recognition Scheme payment;
- (ag)[^c-ee88303b710aa8f41e97f857d2d93cab] which is a miscarriage of justice compensation payment;
- (b) to which regulation 44(2) (modification in respect of children and young persons) applies; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-28c9f92ca7b4ebe944bc398a6b21175d]
- (c) which is made under or by[^c-410c3eb1458a2db6a58d7aa1f1ac9386] the Macfarlane Trust , the Macfarlane (Special Payments) Trust[^c-5bd15edd5dfadbe4526c391ff0502ae3] , the Macfarlane (Special Payments) ([^c-1da32bc99ca73f7742a24d18524d4d95]No. 2) Trust , the Fund[^c-90bcfa6631c6221d8fb313cda491e296] , the Eileen Trust[^c-f9627c97e333c89f6325b992b786de0e] , MFET Limited[^c-10bca104bb49b3a7da5d1a181d01f6e8] , the Skipton Fund, the Caxton Foundation[^c-62e015caa05a5c8d7aed32c3e7d5b68a] , the Scottish Infected Blood Support Scheme[^c-9beb0c6dd7c6caf90675fb92d7272dbf] , an approved blood scheme[^c-ae4127d41d54de327a587dfb698a260a] , the London Emergencies Trust, the We Love Manchester Emergency Fund[^c-5f923b94de4ec257b8dc5f7742157aad] , the National Emergencies Trust[^c-fc02588add2869836cbf35b3f90c97c5] , the Victims of Overseas Terrorism Compensation Scheme[^c-cd3c13998998ba5f64c6a02f9b3ec617] or the Independent Living[^c-c524cf367b9271e9f4acbeb54a5847d5] Fund (2006)[^c-62dc3f79d4c0e72ce7d25861340d07ae].

(11)[^c-92c7e5f07fcc601f345aad2da87b7851] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(12)[^c-85ded5b2b941676bf42fde0f96e6535c] Any arrears of subsistence allowance which are paid to a claimant as a lump sum shall be treated as capital.
