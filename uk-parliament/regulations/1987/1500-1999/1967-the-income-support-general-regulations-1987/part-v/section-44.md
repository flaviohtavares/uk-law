# The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/1987/1967
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 44. Modifications in respect of children and young persons[^c-bba0d5f149b30cebc04c9605f936f0b0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-649b0ca904d07f7336953af47b335cae][^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] Any capital of a child or young person payable by instalments which are outstanding on– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

- (a) the first day in respect of which income support is payable or at the date of the determination of the claim, whichever is the earlier; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]
- (b) in the case of a supersession, the date of that supersession, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

shall be treated as income if the aggregate of the instalments outstanding and the amount of that child’s or young person’s other capital calculated in accordance with Chapter VI of this Part in like manner as for the claimant would exceed £3,000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

(2) In the case of a child or young person who is residing at an educational establishment at which he is receiving relevant education— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

- (a) any payment made to the educational establishment, in respect of that child's or young person's maintenance, by or on behalf of a person who is not a member of the family or by a member of the family out of funds contributed for that purpose by a person who is not a member of the family, shall be treated as income of that child or young person but it shall only be taken into account over periods during which that child or young person is present at that educational establishment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]
- (b) if a payment has been so made, for any period in a benefit week in term-time during which that child or young person returns home, he shall be treated as possessing an amount of income in that week calculated by multiplying the amount of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] personal allowance, any disabled child premium and any enhanced disability premium[^c-0ba72233b6423110a5ed79acb201e9a9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] applicable in respect of that child or young person by the number equal to the number of days in that week in which he was present at his educational establishment and dividing the product by seven; but this sub-paragraph shall not apply where the educational establishment is provided under section 8 of the Education Act 1944 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] [^c2180777] (duty of local authority to secure primary and secondary schools) by a local education authority or where the payment is made under section 49 or 50 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] [^c2180778] (power of education authority to assist persons). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

(3) Where a child or young person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

- (a) is resident at an educational establishment and he is wholly or partly maintained at that establishment by a local education authority under section 8 of the Education Act 1944; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]
- (b) is maintained at an educational establishment under section 49 or 50 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

he shall for each day he is present at that establishment be treated as possessing an amount of income equal to the sum obtained by dividing the amount of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] personal allowance, any disabled child premium and any enhanced disability premium *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] applicable in respect of him by seven. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

(4) Where the income of a child or young person who is a member of the claimant's family calculated in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] Chapters I to V[^c-f9a6baba5f204e937654233f060cbb60] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] of this Part exceeds the amount of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] personal allowance, any disabled child premium and any enhanced disability premium *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] applicable in respect of that child or young person, the excess shall not be treated as income of the claimant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

(5) Where the capital of a child or young person if calculated in accordance with Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] VI *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] of this Part in like manner as for the claimant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be] except as provided in paragraph (1)[^c-b3a00958bbe96a6b6339046585cdb8ac] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be], would exceed £3,000, any income of that child or young person shall not be treated as income of the claimant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

(6) In calculating the net earnings or net profit of a child or young person there shall be disregarded, (in addition to any sum which falls to be disregarded under paragraphs 11 to 13), any sum specified in paragraphs 14 and 15 of Schedule 8 (earnings to be disregarded). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

(7) Any income of a child or young person which is to be disregarded under Schedule 9 (income other than earnings to be disregarded) shall be disregarded in such manner as to produce the result most favourable to the claimant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

(8) Where a child or young person is treated as possessing any income under paragraphs (2) and (3) the foregoing provisions of this Part shall apply for the purposes of calculating that income as if a payment had actually been made and as if it were actual income which he does possess. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]

(9)[^c-407f9518a36e9cd57261216692061c59] For the purposes of this regulation, a child or young person shall not be treated as present at his educational establishment on any day if on that day he spends the night with the claimant or a member of his household. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0ad5b6b34ee9f02d76422a181557be]
