>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 55. Treatment of ... liable relative payments[^c-1c490ecacafdbe05d3fd3efe3271804e][^c-205a0c600f591041b58e6b54ea6edc63]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to regulation 55A[^c-34a8e0eccaa0d02a8fbc3f374cebfeaf] [^c-d23c319d54f6bf71398fd7424c089ea5]... a payment shall—

- (a) to the extent that it is not a payment of income, be treated as income;
- (b) be taken into account in accordance with the following provisions of this Chapter.
