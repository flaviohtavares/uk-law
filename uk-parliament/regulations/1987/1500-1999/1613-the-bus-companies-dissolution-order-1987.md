# The Bus Companies (Dissolution) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 1613

TRANSPORT

The Bus Companies (Dissolution) Order 1987

Made

14th September 1987

Laid before Parliament

23rd September 1987

Coming into force

14th October 1987

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 47(12) and (13) of the Transport Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Bus Companies (Dissolution) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 14th October 1987.

## 2. Dissolution of companies

The Cheltenham District Traction Company, the Gosport and Fareham Omnibus Company and the Mansfield District Traction Company (which are all subsidiaries of the National Bus Company and all incorporated by local Act or by an order under the Light Railways Act 1896[^f00002]) are hereby dissolved.

## 3. Rights and Liabilities

All rights vested in and liabilities binding upon any of the companies named in article 2 of this Order, including (without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing) the obligation to provide services contained in the Gosport and Fareham Omnibus Services Act 1929[^f00003], are hereby extinguished.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

David B. Mitchell

Minister of State,

Department of Transport
