# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 49ZA

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49ZA. Surrendering an award following birth or adoption etc. of child[^c-5986c8596aad8bf1d5a5a32ed59fc05d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An existing award of working families' tax credit shall cease to have effect if the claimant or partner elects, by notice to the Board, to surrender it following the birth of a child, the adoption of a child or young person or the granting of a parental order for a surrogate child.

(2) The award shall terminate with effect from—

- (a) the day on which the notice is given to the Board, if that day is a Monday, or
- (b) the Monday following the day on which the notice is given to the Board, if that day is other than a Monday.
