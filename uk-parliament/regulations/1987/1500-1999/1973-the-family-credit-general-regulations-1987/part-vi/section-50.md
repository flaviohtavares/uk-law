# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 50

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Prevention of duplication of awards of working families' tax credit and income support[^c-67d72c285b96fe7b44f1b406f86b64ff]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where provision is made for the same child or young person in awards for overlapping periods, the first being an award of working families' tax credit and the second an award of working families' tax credit[^c-3b1544a98cc89fdabf9f3fffd501c520], income support , income-based jobseeker’s allowance[^c-325df652d299497bb4cb1e620858d04a] or disabled person’s tax credit[^c-1ebf160167d1732456cf49f87cd00ee1], and at the start of the period of overlap that child or young person is no longer a member of the household of the claimant under the first award, the first award shall terminate with effect from the start of the period of overlap.
