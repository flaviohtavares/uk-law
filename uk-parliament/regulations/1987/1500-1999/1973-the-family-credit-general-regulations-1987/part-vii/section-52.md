# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 52

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Prescribed circumstances for entitlement to working families' tax credit[^M_F_d1399d8c-e881-4847-f88c-165cc0ec5515][^key-eaf9e02f12cbe281da221f81dcb23472]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of section 20(5)(bb) of the Act (prescribed circumstances) where a claimant or a member of his family is entitled to disabled person’s tax credit[^key-51431d4a6794b396a2d95c227dec2dc8], he is entitled to working families' tax credit, if—

- (a) at the date of the claim for working families' tax credit the award of disabled person’s tax credit for him or a member of his family will expire within 42 days; and
- (b) the claimant is or would be otherwise entitled to working families' tax credit by virtue of these Regulations; and
- (c) the claim for working families' tax credit is made in respect of a period which commences immediately after the expiry of the award of disabled person’s tax credit.
