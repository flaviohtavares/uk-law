# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 9

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Circumstances in which a person is to be treated as being no longer a member of the same household

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^M_F_ba5fa7f0-953e-40cb-dd53-22424554c929] Subject to the following provisions of this regulation, where the claimant and any partner of his are living apart from each other they shall be treated as members of the same household unless they do not intend to resume living together.

(2) Where one of the members of a married or unmarried couple is a hospital patient[^M_F_a4e5c100-9354-4873-c354-560ad3e25f63] or detained in custody[^M_F_445e73c3-ea9e-45b6-9a0e-64ed27345808] he shall not be treated, on this account, as ceasing to be a member of the same household as his partner—

- (a) unless he has been a patient[^M_F_4789f678-c51f-4740-dd50-db1728530cf2] in a hospital for 52 weeks or more; or
- (b) unless he is a patient detained in a hospital provided under section 4 of the National Health Service Act 1977 [^c2180971] (special hospitals) or section 90(1) of the Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1984 (provision of hospitals for patients requiring special security); or
- (c) unless he is detained in custody whilst[^M_F_e31de266-bf84-4889-8ecd-15fb1b3b18db] serving a sentence of 52 weeks or more imposed by a court,

but shall be treated as not being a member of the same household as his partner wherever the conditions in sub-paragraphs (a), (b) or (c) are fulfilled.

(3)[^M_F_e312626e-13f6-4ece-9e06-769352ea3d7b] In this regulation “patient” has the same meaning as in regulation 8(3)(a) (membership of the same household).
