# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 13

> Text as at 2018-04-11.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Calculation of income on a weekly basis

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of section 20(5) of the Act (conditions of entitlement to working families' tax credit[^key-b38e24ed2f63a3eb56ebf0728d4b39c8]), the income of a claimant shall be calculated on a weekly basis—

- (a) by ascertaining in accordance with this Chapter and Chapter V of this Part (other income) the amount of his normal weekly income; [^M_F_20ad30dd-6b42-4dc1-8240-1237c08ae943]...
- (b) by adding to that amount the weekly income calculated under regulation 36 (calculation of tariff income from capital); [^key-af75adabf0f56d3a52f9e6396140c9ec]...
- (c)[^key-2fc5429bd35ad377280e732217f4d2e6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^key-0e0d46171841f7abcef04858f42e7247] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^M_F_15e02224-0702-4d49-a530-6dde24ee1749] For the purposes of paragraph (1) “income” includes capital treated as income under regulation 25 (capital treated as income) and income which a claimant is treated as possessing under regulation 26 (notional income).
