# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 11

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Calculation of income and capital of students

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of Chapters II to VI of this Part (income and capital) shall have effect in relation to students and their partners subject to the modifications set out in Chapter VII thereof (students).
