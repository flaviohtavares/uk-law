# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 30

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Disregard of capital of child or young person

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The capital of a child or young person who is a member of the claimant's family shall not be treated as capital of the claimant.
