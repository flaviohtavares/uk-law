# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 14A

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14A. Normal weekly earnings of directors[^M_F_54ebea61-8f90-41f8-b9e0-3afc751aab85]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) and regulation 17 (periods to be disregarded), where a claimant’s income includes earnings from employment as a director, his normal weekly earnings from that employment shall be determined by taking account of his earnings from that employment received in the year immediately preceding the week of claim, whether the amount so received was earned in respect of that period or not.[^M_F_c9f5a42f-4df8-448e-c7bc-bf840e015d24]

(2) Where at the date of claim the claimant has been in employment as a director for less than a year, his normal weekly earnings from that employment shall be determined by taking account of his earnings from that employment received in the period that he has been in that employment[^M_F_3b5ca352-3b0e-4767-cd5f-7fb2c6dccdfb] and by reference to an estimate of the earnings likely to be received in the remainder of the first year of the employment.
