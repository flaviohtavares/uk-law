# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 36

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Calculation of tariff income from capital

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the claimant's capital calculated in accordance with this Chapter exceeds £3,000, it shall be treated as equivalent to a weekly income of £1 for each complete £250 in excess of £3,000 but not exceeding £8,000[^M_F_1fdbc7b4-12c2-427e-f9f1-df1fcdefbf86].

(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1), where any part of the excess is not a complete £250 that part shall be treated as equivalent to a weekly income of £1.

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1), capital includes any income treated as capital under regulation 31 (income treated as capital).
