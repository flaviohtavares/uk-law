# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 12

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Rounding of fractions[^M_F_108f398c-461d-481d-b156-c3ddd4ae56fe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where any calculation under this Part or Part V[^c-14264f35299361cefd6da3c117686227] results in a fraction of a penny that fraction shall, if it would be to the claimant’s advantage, be treated as a penny, otherwise it shall be disregarded.
