> Text as at 2018-04-11.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Capital limit

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of section 22(6) of the Act as it applies to working families' tax credit[^c-67275204a3998aeac7aae78243565b7d] (no entitlement to benefit if capital exceeds prescribed amount), the prescribed amount is £8,000[^M_F_b84dd167-64f1-46b2-dde8-a2af555051a8].
