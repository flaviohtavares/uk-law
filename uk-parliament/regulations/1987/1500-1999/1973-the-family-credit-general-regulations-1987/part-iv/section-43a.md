# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 43A

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43A. Further disregard of student’s income[^key-b0524eab4ee99e8117ab6034394f46b5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where any part of a student’s income has already been taken into account for the purposes of assessing his entitlement to a grant or student loan[^key-2ee16698a563675f2b7480ebee992190], the amount taken into account shall be disregarded in assessing that student’s income.
