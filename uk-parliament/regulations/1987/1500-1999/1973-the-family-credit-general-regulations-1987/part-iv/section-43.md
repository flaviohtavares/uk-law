# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 43

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Disregard of contribution

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the claimant or his partner is a student and , for the purposes of assessing a contribution to the student’s grant[^M_F_fb7161f3-c5d7-4857-a220-3a8989cfb6cd] or student loan[^c-856b17c03a63c3040c28fb4080771629], the other partner’s income has been taken into account, an amount equal to that contribution shall be disregarded for the purposes of assessing that other partner’s income.
