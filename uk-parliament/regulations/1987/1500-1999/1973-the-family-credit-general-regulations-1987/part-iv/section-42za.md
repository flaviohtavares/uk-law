# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 42ZA

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42ZA. Calculation of capital—lump sum access fund payment[^M_F_bfa2b3f4-0153-49f2-ec47-440dcb603f4d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be disregarded from the calculation of a student’s capital under regulation 29(1) any lump sum access fund payment made to the student that is not intended to be used for daily living costs, but only for a period of 52 weeks from the date of receipt of the payment.
