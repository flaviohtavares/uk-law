# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 47

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Applicable amount[^M_F_34218e2e-dff9-4b2f-c1ec-7c9fbe91f719]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The applicable amount for the purposes of section 20(5)(a) of the Act (conditions of entitlement to working families' tax credit[^c-7d4adc669feda2666faacf82d015d072]) shall be £94.50[^c-9af1ffda3f93c1dfd8469838e285f998] per week.

(2)[^M_F_a3b74e89-3b14-472c-ff00-557d72f8d337] For the purposes of section 20(5A) of the Act (date on which applicable amount is to be determined) the prescribed date is the date on which the period under section 20(6) of the Act (period of the award) begins.
