# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 48

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Entitlement to family credit where income exceeds the applicable amount

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The prescribed percentage for the purpose of section 21(3) of the Act (percentage of excess of income over applicable amount which is deducted from maximum working families' tax credit[^key-a4e12c18d81230b8724891a131ea6b77]) shall be 55[^key-4d5bda3ccf24391c10de66c6f39e0350] per cent.
