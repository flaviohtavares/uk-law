# The Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987, section 2A

> Text as at 2018-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2A. Disapplication of section 1(1A) of the Administration Act[^key-a22d283fe1d73c5a81ed0ca4c37c7110]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 1(1A) of the Administration Act (requirement to state national insurance number) shall not apply—

- (a) to a child or young person in respect of whom working families' tax credit[^key-cf320c00de89c9d46fa9c4dd0b8c635f] is claimed;
- (b) to any claim for working families' tax credit made or treated as made before 9th February 1998;
- (c) to a partner in respect of whom a claim for working families' tax credit is made or treated as made before 5th October 1998.
- (d)[^key-b1895c99deae24f79b5aeccae9ce9340] to a partner of a Crown servant posted overseas who has no national insurance number and is not a UK national.
