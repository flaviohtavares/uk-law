# The Insolvency (Amendment) Rules 1987, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In these Rules references to “the principal Rules” are to the Insolvency Rules 1986[^f00002] and a Rule or Schedule or Form referred to by number means the Rule or Schedule or Form so numbered in the principal Rules.

(2) These Rules shall be read and construed as one with the principal Rules.
