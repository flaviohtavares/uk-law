(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules make detailed amendments to the Insolvency Rules 1986, which set out detailed procedures for the conduct of all company and individual insolvency proceedings in England and Wales under the Insolvency Act 1986. These Rules apply to all insolvency proceedings to which the Insolvency Rules 1986 apply on and after 11th January, 1988 whether or not those proceedings were commenced before, on or after that date.

Rule 3(3) also applies Rule 4. 223-CVL of the Insolvency Rules 1986 as amended to those insolvency proceedings specified in paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 11 to the Insolvency Act 1986.
