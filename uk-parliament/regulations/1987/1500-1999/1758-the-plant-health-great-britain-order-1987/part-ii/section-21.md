# The Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1987, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Restriction on the planting of imported potatoes

(1) Without prejudice to article 22, no person shall plant or knowingly cause or permit to be planted in Great Britain any potatoes which have been grown in any place outside Great Britain, Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man, a Member State (other than Denmark or the Federal Republic of Germany), Austria or Switzerland.

(2) Potatoes referred to in paragraph (1) of this article shall include any potatoes which have been derived from such potatoes.

(3) This article shall not apply to any potatoes which have been landed in Great Britain under and in accordance with the conditions of a licence granted under article 42 or which have been derived from potatoes which have been so landed.
