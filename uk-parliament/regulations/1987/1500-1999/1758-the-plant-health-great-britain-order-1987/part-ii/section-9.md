# The Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1987, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Official Statements in respect of imported plants etc.

Any official statement required to be made in accordance with column 3 of Schedule 3 in respect of a plant, soil or growing medium shall, except where the said column 3 requires such a statement to appear on a phytosanitary certificate, be deemed to have been made by the issue of a phytosanitary certificate to accompany that plant, soil or growing medium in accordance with this Order.
