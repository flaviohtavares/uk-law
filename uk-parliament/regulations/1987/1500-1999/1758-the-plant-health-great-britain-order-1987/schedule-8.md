# The Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1987, SCHEDULE 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 8 — PLANT PESTS IN RESPECT OF WHICH PREMISES MAY BE DECLARED INFECTED

Article 15

- *Globodera rostochiensis*(Wollenweber) Behrens,*Globodera pallida*(Stone) Behrens, Potato Cyst Nematodes.
- *Leptinotarsa decemlineata*(Say), Colorado Beetle.
- *Phytophthora fragariae* Hickman, the cause of Red Core Disease of Strawberries, other than in Scotland.
- *Synchytrium endobioticum*(Schilb.) Perc., the cause of Wart Disease of Potatoes.
- *Heterodera schachtii* Schmidt, Beet Cyst Nematode.
