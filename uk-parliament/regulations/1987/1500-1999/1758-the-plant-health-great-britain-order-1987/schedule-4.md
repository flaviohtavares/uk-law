### PART II — PLANTS TO BE ACCOMPANIED BY A PHYTOSANITARY CERTIFICATE ISSUED IN THE COUNTRY OF ORIGIN WHEN RE-EXPORTED TO A MEMBER STATE

Seeds of soya bean (*Glycine max* (L.) Merrill) for planting and exporting to Greece, Spain, France, Italy or Portugal; seeds of cotton (*Gossypium* L.) for planting, for export to Greece or Spain.
