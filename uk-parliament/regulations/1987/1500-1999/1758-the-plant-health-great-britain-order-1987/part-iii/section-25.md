# The Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1987, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Restriction on planting on infected premises

No person shall plant any crop on premises declared infected with Colorado Beetle without the written authority of an inspector.
