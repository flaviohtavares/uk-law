# The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Molesworth Aerodrome) Regulations 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 1885

CIVIL AVIATION

The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Molesworth Aerodrome) Regulations 1987

Made

30th October 1987

Coming into force

14th January 1988

Whereas the Secretary of State for Transport, for reasons affecting the public interest, deems it necessary in the public interest to restrict flying in the vicinity of Molesworth Aerodome:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by article 69 of the Air Navigation Order 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Molesworth Aerodrome) Regulations 1987 and shall come into force on 14th January 1988.

**2.** An aircraft shall not fly below a height of 2,300 feet above mean sea level over any part of the area bounded by a circle of 1.5 nautical miles radius centred at a point N5223.00 W00025.00 unless the commander of the aircraft is flying in accordance with an authorisation given in respect of the aircraft by the notified authority.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Handley M. G. Stevens

An Under Secretary in the

Department of Transport

30th October 1987

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations impose restrictions on flying in the vicinity of Molesworth Aerodrome, from 14th January 1988.
