# The Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section B5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## B5. Persons deemed to be employees of a scheduled body

For the purposes of these Regulations a person of a class described in column 1 of the Table below shall be deemed to be an officer or manual worker, whichever is appropriate, in employment with the body described opposite that class in column 2.

**TABLE**

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th><em>(1)</em></th><th><em>(2)</em></th></tr>
<tr><th>Class</th><th>Deemed employing body</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Registration officer</td><td>The regional council or the islands council who are the local registration authority for the purposes of section 5 of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965 and by whom he was appointed or deemed to have been appointed under section 7 of that Act</td></tr>
<tr><td>Rent officer</td><td>Any local authority with whom the Secretary of State has made arrangements under section 43 of the Rent (Scotland)Act 1984 to provide for the superannuation of the officer</td></tr>
<tr><td>Employees of a passenger transport authority</td><td>The relevant passenger transport executive</td></tr>
<tr><td>Members of passenger transport executives and directors of subsidiaries of passenger transport executives falling within regulation B1(6), (7) or (8)</td><td>The relevant passenger transport executive</td></tr>
<tr><td>Employees of a subsidiary of a passenger transport executive</td><td>The relevant passenger transport executive</td></tr>
<tr><td>Officers and manual workers of Trinity Hospital</td><td>City of Edinburgh District Council</td></tr>
<tr><td>Officers and manual workers of—<br>(a) Heriot Watt College,<br>(b) Heriot Watt University,<br>(c) the Forth Road Bridge Joint Board and<br>(d) Edinburgh College of Art</td><td>Lothian Regional Council</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
