# The Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section G3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## G3. Standard remuneration agreements

A scheduled body may from time to time enter into an agreement with the bodies or persons representative of any class or description of employee of that scheduled body specifying the method which shall for the duration of the agreement determine—

- (a) an amount representing the whole of the remuneration, or
- (b) such part of his remuneration as is specified in the agreement,

of a member of that class or employee of that description.
