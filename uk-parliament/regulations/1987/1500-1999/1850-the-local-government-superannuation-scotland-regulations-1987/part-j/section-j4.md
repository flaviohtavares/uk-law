# The Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section J4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## J4. Amount of transfer value

(1) The amount of any transfer value payable under regulation J2 and of any additional transfer value payable under regulation J3(1) shall be calculated in accordance with the provisions of Parts I to III of Schedule 16.

(2) The amount of any additional transfer value payable under regulation J3(2) shall be calculated in accordance with Part V of Schedule 16.

(3) The transfer value or additional transfer value shall be paid to the scheme managers by the fund authority out of their superannuation fund.

(4) The fund authority shall provide the scheme managers and the person to whom the transfer value or additional transfer value relates with a written notice showing how it was calculated.

(5) A notice provided to a person under paragraph (4) shall include a statement of the kind required by regulation N7(2)(c).
