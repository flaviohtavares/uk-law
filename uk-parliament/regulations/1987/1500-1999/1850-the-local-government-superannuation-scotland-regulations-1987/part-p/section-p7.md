# The Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section P7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## P7. Accounts and audit

As soon as may be after an audit of any of their superannuation funds an administering authority shall send each body whose employees contribute to that fund copies of the revenue account and balance sheet of the fund and of any report by the auditor.
