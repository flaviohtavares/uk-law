# The Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section P17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## P17. Provision to protection of pension

Any increase in a pension which is required by virtue of sections 41A to 41C (protection of pensions) of the Pensions Act shall be paid out of the appropriate superannuation fund.
