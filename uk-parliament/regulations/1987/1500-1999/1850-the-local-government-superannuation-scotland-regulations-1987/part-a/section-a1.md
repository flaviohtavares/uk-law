# The Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section A1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## A1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1987 and shall come into force on 21st December 1987.
