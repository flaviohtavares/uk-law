# The Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1987, section N6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## N6. Decisions by administering authorities as to the amount of benefits

(1) Where a person is entitled to a benefit which is or may become payable out of their superannuation fund, an administering authority are to decide the amount of the benefit.

(2) The amount of a benefit is to be decided as soon as is reasonably practicable after the person becomes entitled to it.

(3) For the purposes of this regulation “benefit” includes a return of contributions and a benefit specified in regulation F6(11) or (15).
