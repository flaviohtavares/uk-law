# The Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) (Amendment) Regulations 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 1199

EVIDENCE

The Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) (Amendment) Regulations 1987

Made

10th July 1987

Laid before Parliament

20th July 1987

Coming into force

17th August 1987

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 22 of the Family Law Reform Act 1969[^f00001], I hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) (Amendment) Regulations 1987 and shall come into force on 17th August 1987.

**2.** For Schedule 2 to the Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) Regulations 1971[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

**3.** The Blood Tests (Evidence of Paternity) (Amendment) Regulations 1986[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Home Office

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

10th July 1987
