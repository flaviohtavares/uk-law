# The Motor Vehicles (Tests) (Amendment) Regulations 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 1144

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Tests) (Amendment) Regulations 1987

Made

2nd July 1987

Laid before Parliament

13th July 1987

Coming into force

3rd August 1987

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 43(2) and (6) of the Road Traffic Act 1972[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 199(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Tests) (Amendment) Regulations 1987 and shall come into force on 3rd August 1987.

**2.** The Motor Vehicles (Tests) Regulations 1981[^f00003] are hereby amended so that in the regulations specified in column 1 of the Table below for the amounts specified in column 2 of that Table there are substituted the amounts specified in column 3 of the Table.

2nd July 1987

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Tests) Regulations 1981 so that with effect from 3rd August 1987:

- (i) the fee payable for the examination of a motor cycle not having a side car attached to it is increased from £7.10 to £7.48;
- (ii) the fee payable for the examination of any other vehicle (not being a vehicle in Class VI) is increased from £11.90 to £12.52; and
- (iii) the fee for the supply of 100 forms of test certificate is increased from £27 to £32.
