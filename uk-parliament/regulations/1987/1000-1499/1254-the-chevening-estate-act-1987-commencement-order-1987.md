# The Chevening Estate Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 1254 (c. 38)

CHEVENING ESTATE

The Chevening Estate Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1987

Made

17th July 1987

The Lord Privy Seal in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 5(2) of the Chevening Estate Act 1987[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Chevening Estate Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1987.

**2.** The Chevening Estate Act 1987 shall come into force on 1st September 1987.

Lord Privy Seal

17th July 1987

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st September 1987 the Chevening Estate Act 1987.
