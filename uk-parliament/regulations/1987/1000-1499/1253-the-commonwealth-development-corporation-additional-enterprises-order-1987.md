# The Commonwealth Development Corporation (Additional Enterprises) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 1253

OVERSEAS DEVELOPMENT AND CO-OPERATION

The Commonwealth Development Corporation (Additional Enterprises) Order 1987

Made

14th July 1987

Laid before Parliament

23rd July 1987

Coming into force

15th August 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 3(3) of the Commonwealth Development Corporation Act 1978[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Commonwealth Development Corporation (Additional Enterprises) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 15th August 1987.

**2.** There shall be added to the classes of enterprises to which the powers of the Corporation relate, enterprises for the provision of financial, management, or consultancy services or the leasing of assets.

Geoffrey Howe

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Dated 14th July 1987

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds enterprises for the provision of financial services, management services, consultancy services or the leasing of assets to the classes of enterprises to which the powers of the Corporation, under the Commonwealth Development Corporation Acts 1978 to 1986, relate.
