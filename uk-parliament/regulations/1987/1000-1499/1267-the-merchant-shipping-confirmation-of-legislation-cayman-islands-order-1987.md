# The Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Cayman Islands) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 1267

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Cayman Islands) Order 1987

Made

21st July 1987

Laid before Parliament

29th July 1987

Coming into force

19th August 1987

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 21st day of July 1987

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 735(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice Of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Cayman Islands) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 19th August 1987.

**2.** The Merchant Shipping (Registry) Law, 1987[^f00002]is hereby confirmed.

G I de Deney

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, made under section 735(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894, confirms a Law enacted by the Legislature of the Cayman Islands which repeals, in relation to the registration of ships in the Cayman Islands, certain provisions of Part I of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894 regarding such registration.
