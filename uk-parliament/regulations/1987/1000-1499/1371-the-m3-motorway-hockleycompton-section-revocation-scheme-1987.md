# The M3 Motorway (Hockley—Compton Section) (Revocation) Scheme 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 1371

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The M3 Motorway (Hockley—Compton Section) (Revocation) Scheme 1987

Made

23rd July 1987

Coming into force

21st August 1987

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Scheme in exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** The M3 Motorway (Hockley—Compton Section) Scheme 1973[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

**2.** This Scheme shall come into force on 21st August 1987 and may be cited as the M3 Motorway (Hockley—Compton Section) (Revocation) Scheme 1987.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

D. Gruffydd Jones

Regional Director South East Region

Department of Transport

23rd July 1987

(This note is not part of the Scheme)

This Scheme revokes the M3 Motorway (Hockley—Compton Section) Scheme 1973, which is a Scheme made under section 11 of the Highways Act [1959 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1959/25), authorising the provision of a special road along a route between Hockley and Compton near Winchester in the County of Hampshire as part of the M3 Motorway. That route is more particularly described in the Schedule to that Scheme and shown by a heavy black line on the deposited plan to that Scheme.
