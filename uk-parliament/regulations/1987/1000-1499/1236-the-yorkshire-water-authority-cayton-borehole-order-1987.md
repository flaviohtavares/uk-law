# The Yorkshire Water Authority (Cayton Borehole) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 1236

WATER, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Yorkshire Water Authority (Cayton Borehole) Order 1987

Made

13th July 1987

Coming into force

14th July 1987

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 23, 32(1) and 59(1)[^f00001] of the Water Act 1945[^f00002] and now vested in him[^f00003], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Yorkshire Water Authority (Cayton Borehole) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 14th July 1987.

(2) This Order shall be included among the enactments which may be cited together as the Yorkshire Water Authority Orders 1974 to 1987.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the Yorkshire Water Authority.
- “the deposited plan” means the plan prepared in duplicate, signed by an Under Secretary in the Department of the Environment and marked “Plan referred to in the Yorkshire Water Authority (Cayton Borehole) Order 1987”, one duplicate of which is deposited and available for inspection at the principal office of the Authority and the other at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment;
- “the Third Schedule” means the Third Schedule to the Water Act 1945;
- “the undertaking” means the water undertaking of the Authority as for the time being authorised by any enactment.

## 3. Application of Third Schedule

For the purposes of this Order, the provisions of the Third Schedule which are specified in column (1) of Schedule 1 to this Order shall, subject to the modifications set out in column (2) of that Schedule, apply to the undertaking and are hereby incorporated in this Order.

## 4. Power to continue, construct and maintain works

The Authority may, when they have acquired the necessary land or sufficient rights therein, construct and maintain on and under the land described in Schedule 2 to this Order a borehole together with (in addition to the works authorised by section 4 of the Third Schedule as applied to the undertaking by this Order) all such pumping stations, wells, boreholes, adits and headings as may be necessary or expedient for augmenting or improving the supply of water obtainable by means of the said borehole.

## 5. Works to form part of undertaking

The works authorised by this Order shall for all purposes form part of the undertaking.
