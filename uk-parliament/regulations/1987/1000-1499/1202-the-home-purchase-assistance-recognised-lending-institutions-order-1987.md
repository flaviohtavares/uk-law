# The Home Purchase Assistance (Recognised Lending Institutions) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 1202

HOUSING, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Home Purchase Assistance (Recognised Lending Institutions) Order 1987

Made

9th July 1987

Coming into force

30th July 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 447(2) of the Housing Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Home Purchase Assistance (Recognised Lending Institutions) Order 1987 and comes into force on 30th July 1987.

**2.** The bodies named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby specified for the purposes of section 447(2) of the Housing Act 1985.

## SCHEDULE — BODIES SPECIFIED FOR THE PURPOSES OF SECTION 447 OF THE HOUSING ACT 1985

**1.** Yorkshire Bank Home Loans Limited.

**2.** Abbey Life Home Loans Limited.

Nicholas Ridley

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

2nd July 1987

We consent,

Peter Lloyd

Michael Neubert

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

9th July 1987

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies two additional bodies for the purposes of section 447 of the Housing Act 1985. Bodies recognised as lending institutions for the purposes of section 447 may receive advances from the Secretary of State to enable them to provide assistance to first-time purchasers of house property (the “Homeloan” Scheme). Certain bodies are listed in section 447(1) and an additional body was specified by the Home Purchase Assistance (Recognised Lending Institutions) Order 1982 (S.I. [1982/976](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1982/976)) which is retained in force by section 2 of the Housing (Consequential Provisions) Act [1985 (c. 71)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/71).
