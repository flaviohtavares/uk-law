# The Gaming (Amendment) Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1987

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1987 No. 1200 (C.33)

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gaming (Amendment) Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1987

Made

10th July 1987

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 2(2) of the Gaming (Amendment) Act 1987[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Amendment) Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1987.

**2.** The Gaming (Amendment) Act 1987 shall come into force on 1st August 1987.

Douglas Hurd

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

10th July 1987

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Gaming (Amendment) Act 1987 into force on 1st August 1987.
