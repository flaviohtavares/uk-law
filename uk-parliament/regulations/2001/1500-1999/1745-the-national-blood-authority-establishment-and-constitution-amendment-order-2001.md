# The National Blood Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1745

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The National Blood Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 2001

Made

7th May 2001

Laid before Parliament

8th May 2001

Coming into force

29th May 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 11(4)(a) and 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement, interpretation and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the National Blood Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 29th May 2001.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Order” means the National Blood Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1993[^f00002].

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Constitution of the Authority

Article 4 of the Order is amended by substituting, in sub-paragraph (1)(b), for the words “five members” the words “six members” and, in sub-paragraph (1)(d), for the words “four members” the words “five members”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hunt

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

7th May 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the National Blood Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1993. The Authority is a Special Health Authority established under section 11 of the National Health Service Act 1977.

In particular, this Order provides for an increase in the number of non-officer members of the Authority from five to six and in the number of officer members from four to five.
