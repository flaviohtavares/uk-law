# The North Sefton & West Lancashire Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1889

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The North Sefton & West Lancashire Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

Made

24th March 2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the North Sefton & West Lancashire Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Order” means the North Sefton & West Lancashire Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999[^f00004];
- “the trust” means the National Health Service trust established by the Order.

## 2. Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

For article 3 of the Order (nature and functions of the trust) there shall be substituted the following article—

> **3. Nature and functions of the trust**
>
> (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1) of the Act.
>
> (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services, namely hospital accommodation and services, and community health services, for the purposes of the health service, at or from Ormskirk & District General Hospital, Wigan Road, Ormskirk, Lancashire L39 2JW
>
> and at or from any associated hospitals, establishments and facilities.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Hunt

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

24th March 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the North Sefton & West Lancashire Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999[^f00005] (“the Establishment Order”) which established the North Sefton & West Lancashire Community National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 substitutes a new article 3 of the Establishment Order (functions of the trust).

This Order is to be read in conjunction with the North Mersey Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001[^f00006], as well as the Mersey Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2001[^f00007].
