# The Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-And-Mouth Disease) (No. 7) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of an omission in the text of [SI 2001/1772](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1772) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the Common Agricultural Policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that section, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Title, commencement and extent

These Regulations may be cited as the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (No. 7) (Amendment) Regulations 2001; they extend to England and come into force at 6 p.m. on 11th May 2001.

## 2. Amendments to the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (No. 7) Regulations 2001

At the end of Regulation 4(3)(b)(ii) of the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (No. 7) Regulations 2001[^f00003], insert “or, in the case of meat from biungulates to which that Directive does not apply, the health mark provided for in Chapter III of Annex I to Council Directive [91/495/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0495) (concerning public health and animal health problems affecting the production and placing on the market of rabbit meat and farmed game meat[^f00004])”.

Hayman

Minister of State Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Dated 3 p.m. on 11th May 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 4(3)(b)(ii) of the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (No. 7) Regulations 2001 which permit the export of fresh meat obtained from cutting plants under certain conditions. The amendment specifies an alternative health mark that must be used in respect of certain biungulates.
