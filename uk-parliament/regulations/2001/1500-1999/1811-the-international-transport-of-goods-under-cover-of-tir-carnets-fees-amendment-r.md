(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the International Transport of Goods under Cover of TIR Carnets (Fees) Regulations 1988 as follows.

The fee for an inspection of a vehicle design-type in connection with the grant of TIR design-type approval is increased by 2.9% from £68 to £70 where the inspection of a design-type which is a variation of a design-type for which a TIR design-type approval has been granted and by 3.1% from £420 to £433 in any other case.

The fee for the issue of a TIR vehicle approval certificate for a vehicle which is of a design-type for which TIR design-type approval has been granted is increased by 5% from £10 to £10.50. The fee for the issue of a duplicate of such certificate is increased by 5% from £10 to £10.50.

The fee for an inspection of a vehicle in connection with the issue of a TIR vehicle approval certificate is increased by 2.2% from £46 to £47 where the inspection is carried out following refusal of such a certificate for the vehicle and by 3% from £66 to £68 in any other case.

Copies of the statutory instruments referred to in these Regulations can be obtained from the Stationery Office.
