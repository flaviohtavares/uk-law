# The Retained Organs Commission (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1813

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Retained Organs Commission (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 2001

Made

9th May 2001

Laid before Parliament

10th May 2001

Coming into force

31st May 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 11(4) and 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Retained Organs Commission (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 31st May 2001.

(2) This Order extends to England only.

## 2. Amendment of the Retained Organs Commission (Establishment and Constitution) Order 2001

In article 4 of the Retained Organs Commission (Establishment and Constitution) Order 2001 (constitution of the Commission)[^f00002], in paragraph (b), for “7” substitute “9”.

Alan Milburn

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State,

Department of Health

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Retained Organs Commission (Establishment and Constitution) Order 2001 by increasing from 7 to 9 the maximum number of members of the Commission who are not officers.
