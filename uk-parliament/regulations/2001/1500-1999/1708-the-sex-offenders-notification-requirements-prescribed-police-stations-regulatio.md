# The Sex Offenders (Notification Requirements) (Prescribed Police Stations) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1708

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Sex Offenders (Notification Requirements) (Prescribed Police Stations) Regulations 2001

Made

2nd May 2001

Laid before Parliament

4th May 2001

Coming into force

1st June 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 2(6C) and 10(6) of the Sex Offenders Act 1997[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Sex Offenders (Notification Requirements) (Prescribed Police Stations) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 1st June 2001.

**2.** In relation to persons subject to the notification requirements of Part I of the Sex Offenders Act 1997, section 2(5)(a) of that Act[^f00002] has effect as if for the reference to any police station in a person’s local police area there is substituted a reference to any police station which is—

- (a) in a person’s local police area, and
- (b) listed in the Schedule to these Regulations.

Charles Clarke

Minister of State

Home Office

2nd May 2001

These Regulations apply to England, Wales and Northern Ireland. The Sex Offenders Act 1997 specifies in Part I that certain sex offenders are subject to requirements to notify certain personal details to the police. Section 2(5) of the Act (as amended by the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000) provides for such notifications to be given in some cases by attending at any police station in a person’s local police area and in other cases by sending a written notice to any such police station. “Local police area” is defined in section 2(7) of the Act. The Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000 also introduced a power for the Secretary of State to provide for section 2(5) of the Sex Offenders Act 1997 to have effect such that instead of the required notification being at, or by writing to, any police station in a person’s local police area, the police station would be one prescribed by regulations. These Regulations have the effect, accordingly, of requiring notifications to be made by attending at, or as the case may be writing to, any police station in a person’s local police area which is also mentioned in the list set out in the Schedule.
