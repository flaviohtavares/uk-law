# The Advisory Centre on WTO Law (Immunities and Privileges) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1868

INTERNATIONAL IMMUNITIES AND PRIVILEGES

The Advisory Centre on WTO Law (Immunities and Privileges) Order 2001

Made

14th May 2001

Coming into force in accordance with Article 1

At the Court at Buckingham Palace the 14th day of May 2001

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas a draft of this Order has been laid before Parliament in accordance with section 10 of the International Organisations Act 1968[^f00001] (“the Act”) and has been approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by virtue and in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 1 of the Act[^f00002] or otherwise in Her Majesty vested, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Advisory Centre on WTO Law (Immunities and Privileges) Order 2001. It shall come into force on the date on which the Agreement establishing the Advisory Centre on WTO Law[^f00003] enters into force.

**2.** The Advisory Centre on WTO Law is an organisation of which the United Kingdom and other sovereign powers are members.

**3.** The Advisory Centre on WTO Law shall have the legal capacities of a body corporate.

A.K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order confers the legal capacities of a body corporate on the Advisory Centre for WTO Law. This legal capacity is conferred in accordance with Article 10(1) of the Agreement establishing the Advisory Centre on WTO Law (Cm. 4721). The Order will enable Her Majesty’s Government to give effect to Article 10(1) of the Agreement, and will come into force on the date on which the Agreement enters into force in respect of the United Kingdom.
