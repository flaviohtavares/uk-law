# The Southampton East Healthcare Primary Care Trust Change of Name and (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1624

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Southampton East Healthcare Primary Care Trust Change of Name and (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

Made

22nd March 2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16A(1), (2) and (3) and 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, following compliance with the consultation requirements contained in regulations made under section 16A(5) of the Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Southampton East Healthcare Primary Care Trust Change of Name and (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

(2) In this Order “the Order” means the Southampton East Healthcare Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2000[^f00003];

- “the trust” means the trust established by the Order.

## 2. Change of name and transitional provisions

(1) The trust shall be called the Southampton City Primary Care Trust instead of the Southampton East Healthcare Primary Care Trust, and accordingly in article 1 of the Order in the definition of “the trust”, and in article 2 of the Order, for the words “Southampton East Healthcare” there are substituted the words “Southampton City”.

(2) The change of name effected by paragraph (1) shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person; or
- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before, on or after the day on which this order comes into force) which refers to the trust by its previous name;

and all instruments and other documents which refer to the trust under its previous name shall be construed as if referring to it under its new name.

## 3. Further Amendment of the Primary Care Trust Establishment Order

In the Schedule to the Order, after “in the City of Southampton – the electoral wards of” there shall be inserted; “Bargate, Bassett”, and after “Harefield” there shall be inserted “Portswood, St. Luke's”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Gisela Stuart

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

22nd March 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the establishment order of the Southampton East Healthcare Primary Care Trust (“the trust”), a Primary Care Trust established by Order under section 16A of the National Health Service Act 1977. It changes the name of the trust from Southampton East Healthcare Primary Care Trust to Southampton City Primary Care Trust. It also expands the area in which the trust is to undertake its functions.
