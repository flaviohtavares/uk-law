# The Noise Emission in the Environment by Equipment for use Outdoors Regulations 2001, section 22

> Text as at 2026-04-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the Provision and Use of Work Equipment Regulations 1998 [^c16921851] in Schedule 1 there shall be added at the end of column one the words “The Noise Emission in the Environment by Equipment for use Outdoors Regulations 2001" and at the end of column two the words “S.I. 2001/1701".

(2) In the Provision and Use of Work Equipment Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 [^c16921861], in Schedule 2 there shall be added at the end of column one the words “The Noise Emission in the Environment by Equipment for use Outdoors Regulations 2001" and at the end of column two the words “S.I. 2001/1701".
