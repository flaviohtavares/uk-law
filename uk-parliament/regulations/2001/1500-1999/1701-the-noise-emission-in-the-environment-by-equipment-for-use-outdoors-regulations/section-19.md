# The Noise Emission in the Environment by Equipment for use Outdoors Regulations 2001, section 19

> Text as at 2026-04-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Penalties

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (1A), *(Extent: N.I.)*[^c-38630db5c86c6bb9c48df5ed68eb2111] A person guilty of an offence under regulation 18(a) shall be liable on summary conviction—

- (a) to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months; or
- (b) to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale,

or to both.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1A)[^c-cbc31ac02bcda13f611d8cd14230bb27] Where a person fails to comply with a requirement in respect of the UK(NI) indication, a person guilty of an offence under regulation 18(a) shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 18(b), (c) *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b92b43b1eeb5374942a442a0d8e71c9b] or paragraphs 9, 10, 11 [^c-668abcf2b00008dc308214635c2785b9]... of Schedule 13 shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
