# The Noise Emission in the Environment by Equipment for use Outdoors Regulations 2001, SCHEDULE 5

> Text as at 2026-04-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 5 — (ANNEX II to the Directive)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 7(2)(d)

### EC (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note) Declaration of conformity[^c-f309bd3ebf0f93cc33b597bb70429d39]

The EC *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f309bd3ebf0f93cc33b597bb70429d39] declaration of conformity must contain the following particulars:

- — Name and address of the manufacturer or his authorised representative established in the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8d78012c-e767-41bc-ac0b-c8195f490fe3] European Union[^c-f7aca2a6aebfc8e9a8bfce761f140186] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8d78012c-e767-41bc-ac0b-c8195f490fe3].
- — Name and address of the person who keeps the technical documentation.
- — Description of the equipment.
- — Conformity assessment procedure followed, and, where appropriate, name and address of the notified *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3d8fd90568bac047b792a032f574dbe5] approved *(Extent: E+W+S)* body involved.
- — Measured sound power level on an equipment representative for this type.
- — Guaranteed sound power level for this equipment.
- — A reference to this Directive *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f76a10f566f7f40cbfd88a69e234759e] these Regulations *(Extent: E+W+S)*.
- — The declaration that the equipment conforms to the requirements of this Directive *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f76a10f566f7f40cbfd88a69e234759e] these Regulations *(Extent: E+W+S)*.
- — Where appropriate, the declaration(s) of conformity and references of the other Community Directives *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faee4d86181999391f1d77d62dd16e69] other enactments *(Extent: E+W+S)* applied.
- — The place and date of the declaration.
- — Particulars of the signatory authorised to sign the legally binding declaration for the manufacturer or his authorised representative established in the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8d78012c-e767-41bc-ac0b-c8195f490fe3] European Union *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_8d78012c-e767-41bc-ac0b-c8195f490fe3].
