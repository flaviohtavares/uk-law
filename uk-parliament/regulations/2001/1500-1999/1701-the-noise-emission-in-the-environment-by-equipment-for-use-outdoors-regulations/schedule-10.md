# The Noise Emission in the Environment by Equipment for use Outdoors Regulations 2001, SCHEDULE 10

> Text as at 2026-04-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 10 — (ANNEX VII to the Directive)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 10(a)

### Unit Verification

*Extent: E+W+S*

**1.**[^M_E_ade491db-245f-4a9c-d576-99763bcdc9d1] This Schedule[^key-79933ef7f8447f610ba09e57ec74011e] describes the procedure whereby the manufacturer, or his authorised representative [^key-5d8613c9b9afad46f3e1292ba8da9cad]..., ensures and declares that the equipment which has been issued with the certificate referred to in point 4 conforms to the requirements of these Regulations[^key-5770c13f2b97a99c04cc97cd9caf5425]. The manufacturer, or his authorised representative [^key-5d8613c9b9afad46f3e1292ba8da9cad]..., must affix the UK[^key-4e81752981a688033de1d6d51b2cbc19] marking supplemented by the information as required in regulation 7(2)(c), regulation 11 and Schedule 7[^key-78ff0b47deeaadc09d4fe5b6ba2515fc] to the equipment and draw up the [^key-cc368255dc07bb0ca11aaf44688664ac]... declaration of conformity referred to in regulation 7(2)(d) and Schedule 5[^key-a6603b9892fb4336d02e10d22ef7b3f9].

*Extent: N.I.*

**1.**[^M_E_e0c62312-d7e0-45aa-e15a-1e8b7dd56110] This Annex describes the procedure whereby the manufacturer, or his authorised representative established in the European Union[^V_NI_5fab665b-ade0-bdfe-2461-7e28673e822e], ensures and declares that the equipment which has been issued with the certificate referred to in point 4 conforms to the requirements of this Directive. The manufacturer, or his authorised representative established in the European Union, must affix the CE marking supplemented by the information as required in Article 11 to the equipment and draw up the EC declaration of conformity referred to in Article 8.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**2.**[^M_E_23363426-3053-460e-c397-fee722e77c6f] The application for a unit verification must be lodged by the manufacturer or his authorised representative [^M_F_8865edc9-30f1-4548-fe3f-67717a5e07ab]... with an approved[^M_F_374a2e51-1d3a-44e5-df6c-142ff8eee775] body chosen by him.

This application must include:

- — the name and address of the manufacturer and, if the application is lodged by the authorised representative, his name and address in addition
- — a written declaration that the same application has not been lodged with any other approved[^M_F_74b2c646-da70-43c9-ed28-8c74a6e42dd5] body,
- — a technical documentation conforming to the requirements set below:
  - — a description of the equipment
  - — trade name
  - — type, series and numbers
  - — the technical data relevant for the identification of the equipment and the assessment of its noise emission, including, if appropriate, schematic drawings and any description and explanation necessary for their understanding
  - — the reference to these Regulations[^M_F_c316bfae-95f3-42f7-fe45-245462c5b24c].

*Extent: N.I.*

**2.**[^M_E_630ef2ca-bcf4-4c8e-acc2-b80716098a13] The application for a unit verification must be lodged by the manufacturer or his authorised representative established in the European Union[^V_NI_3b52351f-d7b5-dd2d-6376-eb53e8f39af1] with a notified body chosen by him.

This application must include:

- — the name and address of the manufacturer and, if the application is lodged by the authorised representative, his name and address in addition
- — a written declaration that the same application has not been lodged with any other notified body,
- — a technical documentation conforming to the requirements set below:
  - — a description of the equipment
  - — trade name
  - — type, series and numbers
  - — the technical data relevant for the identification of the equipment and the assessment of its noise emission, including, if appropriate, schematic drawings and any description and explanation necessary for their understanding
  - — the reference to this Directive.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**3.**[^M_E_a5a828e6-8cf8-4f69-97f2-93e15a79af21] The approved[^key-ff0917747e647d5204571e4dac13329c] body must:

- — examine whether the equipment has been manufactured in conformity with the technical documentation
- — agree with the applicant the location where, in accordance with these Regulations[^key-993204cfbb1aba830f020db2f2441b49], the noise tests will be carried out
- — in accordance with these Regulations, carry out or have carried out the necessary noise tests.

*Extent: N.I.*

**3.**[^M_E_73f55180-3480-4884-c118-920da8439584] The notified body must:

- — examine whether the equipment has been manufactured in conformity with the technical documentation
- — agree with the applicant the location where, in accordance with this Directive, the noise tests will be carried out
- — in accordance with this Directive, carry out or have carried out the necessary noise tests.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**4.**[^M_E_68ddceb1-91ff-4350-9b48-e9b979c8c504] Where the equipment meets the provisions of these Regulations[^key-5c82de9625f6335403522a9ab4cfa773], the approved[^key-2917569c0029d161381a64a01bd341da] body must issue a certificate of conformity to the applicant as described in Schedule 12[^key-cad7016ec54e24888ea4322727cd118e].

If the approved body refuses to issue a certificate of conformity, it must state the detailed grounds for the refusal.

*Extent: N.I.*

**4.**[^M_E_28b42cb3-4f70-4499-aa83-7f78053be8a4] Where the equipment meets the provisions of this Directive, the notified body must issue a certificate of conformity to the applicant as described in Annex X.

If the notified body refuses to issue a certificate of conformity, it must state the detailed grounds for the refusal.

**5.** The manufacturer, or his authorised representative established in the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-999dcea65bf8ed2d9e5f1b8a448b4d30] European Union[^key-f7aca2a6aebfc8e9a8bfce761f140186] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-999dcea65bf8ed2d9e5f1b8a448b4d30], must keep with the technical documentation copies of the certificate of conformity for a period of 10 years from the date on which the equipment is placed on the market.
