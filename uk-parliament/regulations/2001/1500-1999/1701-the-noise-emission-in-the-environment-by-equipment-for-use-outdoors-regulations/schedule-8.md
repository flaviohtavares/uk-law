# The Noise Emission in the Environment by Equipment for use Outdoors Regulations 2001, SCHEDULE 8

> Text as at 2026-04-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 8 — (ANNEX V to the Directive)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 10(b)

### Internal control of production

*Extent: E+W+S*

**1.**[^M_E_601afaf7-ec29-44d6-c409-75258f49b696] This Schedule[^key-5b86adc1ad8f46b169acb284310596d4] describes the procedure whereby the manufacturer, or his authorised representative [^key-23ef60b478dda875752cc0b40fee7856]..., who carries out the obligations laid down in point 2, ensures and declares that the equipment concerned satisfies the requirements of these Regulations[^key-2d36382694bb02013fb1e7bbc04c017d]. The manufacturer, or his authorised representative [^key-24abc5de90cb06881f2f9f4ea6cbf24f]..., must affix the UK[^key-954decf6a8e9bbfae4c640873f6f59ae] marking of conformity and the indication of the guaranteed sound power level as required by regulation 7(2)(c), regulation 11 and Schedule 7[^key-76b62d244853fe842130eb3abece439e] to each piece of equipment and draw up a written [^key-78c49ed30d1fe806f8334e1fd148ee58]... declaration of conformity as required by regulation 7(2)(d) and Schedule 5[^key-ad0a8123045b9e5735bee3150e1c397c].

*Extent: N.I.*

**1.**[^M_E_ba2a088a-2c36-4200-b51e-2280fba69efd] This Annex describes the procedure whereby the manufacturer, or his authorised representative established within the European Union[^V_NI_8194bdbf-95d7-130c-9554-c607f58361e4], who carries out the obligations laid down in point 2, ensures and declares that the equipment concerned satisfies the requirements of this Directive. The manufacturer, or his authorised representative established in the European Union, must affix the CE marking of conformity and the indication of the guaranteed sound power level as required in Article 11 [^V_NI_2122e90b-fbe5-7076-e623-bb2f6b9e69f8] to each piece of equipment and draw up a written EC declaration of conformity as required in Article 8 [^V_NI_0129190d-fc83-4454-b0cf-d9aec20b3ffe].

**2.** The manufacturer, or his authorised representative established in the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-97065590a8da58b164b3ec22839745f7] European Union[^key-f7aca2a6aebfc8e9a8bfce761f140186] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-97065590a8da58b164b3ec22839745f7], must draw up the technical documentation described in point 3 and he must keep it for a period ending at least 10 years after the last product has been manufactured at the disposal of the relevant national authorities for inspection purposes. The manufacturer, or his authorised representative established in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-97065590a8da58b164b3ec22839745f7] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-97065590a8da58b164b3ec22839745f7], may entrust another person to keep the technical documentation. In this case he has to include the name and address of this person in the EC *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-88ee92864f67c39242b5cff8eec088d0] declaration of conformity.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**3.**[^M_E_3b658e43-aa0f-457f-d2b9-0fd952aa97ca] The technical documentation must enable the conformity of the equipment with the requirements of these Regulations[^key-1146dbb14bd122c0a25bf36016c3c95c] to be assessed. It must contain at least the following information:

- — name and address of the manufacturer or his authorised representative [^key-c4abcac580d4ba56d42288ab41c167bd]...
- — a description of the equipment
- — make
- — trade name
- — type, series and numbers
- — the technical data relevant for the identification of the equipment and the assessment of its noise emission, including, if appropriate, schematic drawings and any description and explanation necessary for their understanding.
- — the reference to these Regulations
- — the technical report of noise measurements carried out in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations
- — the technical instruments applied and the results of the evaluation of the uncertainties due to production variation and their relation to the guaranteed sound power level.

*Extent: N.I.*

**3.**[^M_E_c8c2240c-f735-45c7-9dd9-96fe2ab95077] The technical documentation must enable the conformity of the equipment with the requirements of this Directive to be assessed. It must contain at least the following information:

- — name and address of the manufacturer or his authorised representative established in the European Union[^V_NI_b0399ee5-ed2c-3a80-07b8-5e4480c06cbc]
- — a description of the equipment
- — make
- — trade name
- — type, series and numbers
- — the technical data relevant for the identification of the equipment and the assessment of its noise emission, including, if appropriate, schematic drawings and any description and explanation necessary for their understanding.
- — the reference to this Directive
- — the technical report of noise measurements carried out in accordance with the provisions of this Directive
- — the technical instruments applied and the results of the evaluation of the uncertainties due to production variation and their relation to the guaranteed sound power level.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**4.**[^M_E_32aafc33-ef59-4f72-affa-0afdf0320af0] The manufacturer must take all measures necessary in order that the manufacturing process ensure continuing compliance of the manufactured equipment with the technical documentation referred to in points 2 and 3 and with the requirements of these Regulations[^key-d982c9812efaddac526d283fb93790f1].

*Extent: N.I.*

**4.**[^M_E_8172734b-ca93-40a5-8515-304a8a61adb0] The manufacturer must take all measures necessary in order that the manufacturing process ensure continuing compliance of the manufactured equipment with the technical documentation referred to in points 2 and 3 and with the requirements of this Directive.
