# The Noise Emission in the Environment by Equipment for use Outdoors Regulations 2001, SCHEDULE 7

> Text as at 2026-04-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 7 — (ANNEX IV to the Directive)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 2(2) and 11

### Models of the CE (affected by repeal, see note) UK marking of conformity and of the indication of the guaranteed sound power level[^c-6e2e52827f05a888ff0a6f1ce061551d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The CE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6e2e52827f05a888ff0a6f1ce061551d] UK conformity marking must consist of the initials “CE" *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6e2e52827f05a888ff0a6f1ce061551d] "UK" taking the following form *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0350ab43840a8252cb34a308bd02da6] taking the form set out in Annex 2 of Regulation [(EC) No 765/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/765) of the European Parliament and of the Council setting out the requirements for accreditation and market surveillance relating to the marketing of products and repealing Regulation [(EEC) No 339/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1993/339):[^M_F_34fda68f-0eae-40c6-bf62-299de897624b]

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1701/images/uksi_20011701_en_sld_017)

If the CE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6e2e52827f05a888ff0a6f1ce061551d] UK marking is reduced or enlarged according to the size of the equipment the proportions given in the above drawing must be respected. The various components of the CE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6e2e52827f05a888ff0a6f1ce061551d] UK marking must have substantially the same vertical dimensions which may not be less than 5 mm.

The indication of the guaranteed sound power level must consist of the single-number of the guaranteed sound power in dB, the sign L<sub>WA</sub> and a pictogram taking the following form:

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1701/images/uksi_20011701_en_sld_018)

If the indication is reduced or enlarged according to the size of the equipment the proportions given in the above drawings must be respected. However, the vertical dimension of the indication should, if possible, not be less than 40mm.
