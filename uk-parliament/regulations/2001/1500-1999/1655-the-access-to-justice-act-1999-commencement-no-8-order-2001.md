# The Access to Justice Act 1999 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1655 (C.58)

LEGAL PROFESSION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Access to Justice Act 1999 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2001

Made

2nd May 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 108(1) of the Access to Justice Act 1999[^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Access to Justice Act 1999 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2001.

**2.** Section 47 of the Access to Justice Act 1999 shall come into force on 25th May 2001.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

David Lock

Parliamentary Secretary,

Lord Chancellor’s Department

2nd May 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 47 of the Access to Justice Act 1999 into force on 25th May 2001. This section relates to fees for solicitors' practising certificates.

(This note is not part of the Order)
