# The Royal Brompton and Harefield National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 712

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Royal Brompton and Harefield National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2001

Made

6th March 2001

Laid before Parliament

7th Marchh 2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 92(1) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to changes in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals, establishments and facilities managed by the Royal Brompton and Harefield National Health Service Trust and having consulted the trust and the trustees for the Barts and The London National Health Service Trust, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Royal Brompton and Harefield National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Trust” means the Royal Brompton and Harefield National Health Service Trust;
- “the new trustees” means the trustees for the Barts and The London National Health Service Trust[^f00002];
- “the trust property” means the property specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by, agreed upon by and signed on behalf of the old trustees and the new trustees on 27th February 2001.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

(1) On 1st April 2001 the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property[^f00003], shall be transferred from the Trust to the new trustees.

(2) In any trust instrument relating to the trust property, any reference to the name by which the trustees of the trust property are to be known shall be construed as a reference to the new trustees and any reference to the Trust in the name by which the charitable trust governing the trust property is to be known shall be construed as a reference to the Barts and The London National Health Service Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Denham

Minister of State,

Department of Health

6th March 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the trustees for the Barts and The London National Health Service Trust trust property held by the Royal Brompton and Harefield National Health Service Trust and makes related provision. A copy of the schedule of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
