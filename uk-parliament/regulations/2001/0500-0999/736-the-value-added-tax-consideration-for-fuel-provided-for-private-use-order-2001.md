# The Value Added Tax (Consideration for Fuel Provided for Private Use) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 736

VALUE ADDED TAX

The Value Added Tax (Consideration for Fuel Provided for Private Use) Order 2001

Made

7th March 2001

Laid before the House of Commons

7th March 2001

Coming into force

6th April 2001

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 57(4) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Consideration for Fuel Provided for Private Use) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 6th April 2001.

(2) It shall take effect in relation to a taxable person from the beginning of his first prescribed accounting period beginning after 5th April 2001.

(This note is not part of the Order)

VAT is payable if road fuel of a business is used for private motoring. The VAT due must be calculated using flat-rate values related to engine type and size[^f00003].

This Order reduces those flat-rates by an average of 3 per cent for diesel vehicles and 5 per cent for those using other fuels. This reflects the reduction in road fuel costs which has occurred since Table A in section 57 of the Value Added Tax Act [1994 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23) was last amended[^f00004].

The new rates apply to any relevant accounting period starting after 5th April 2001[^f00005].
