# The Transport Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 869 (C. 30)

TRANSPORT

The Transport Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2001

Made

8th March 2001

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 275(1) of the Transport Act 2000[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Transport Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2001.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1st April 2001

The following provisions of the Transport Act 2000 shall come into force on 1st April 2001—

- save for section 3, Part I to the extent it is not already in force, and
- section 274, in so far as it relates to the entries in Part I of Schedule 31.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State, for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Bob Ainsworth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

8th March 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

With the exception of section 3 (restrictions on the provision of air traffic services), this Order brings into force on 1st April 2001 all the remaining provisions of Part I of the Transport Act 2000. These comprise sections—

- 36 (Land) with Schedule 4,
- 37 (Licence holders as statutory undertakers) with Schedule 5, and
- 97 (Amendments) with Schedule 8, to the extent it is not already in force.

Also on 1st April 2001 section 274 (Repeals and revocations) insofar as it relates to Part I of Schedule 31 (Air traffic) is brought into force.

(This note is not part of the Order)
