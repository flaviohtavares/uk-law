# The Social Security (Contributions) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations 2001, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979[^f00003], and, in the following provisions of these Regulations, except where otherwise expressly provided, a reference to a numbered regulation or Schedule is a reference to the regulation of, or Schedule to, the principal Regulations which bears that number.
