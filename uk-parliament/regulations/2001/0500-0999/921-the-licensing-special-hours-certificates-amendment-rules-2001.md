# The Licensing (Special Hours Certificates) (Amendment) Rules 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 921

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Licensing (Special Hours Certificates) (Amendment) Rules 2001

Made

12th March 2001

Coming into force

19th March 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 91 of the Licensing Act 1964[^f00001], hereby makes the following Rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Licensing (Special Hours Certificates) (Amendment) Rules 2001 and shall come into force at the end of one week beginning with the day on which they are made.

## 2. Amendments to the Licensing (Special Hours Certificates) Rules 1982

(1) The Licensing (Special Hours Certificates) Rules 1982[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In rule 5(1)[^f00003] after “section 81A(3)” insert “or section 81AA(1)[^f00004]”.

(3) In rule 6[^f00005] for “or section 81A(3)” insert “, section 81A(3) or section 81AA(1)”.

(4) In rule 7[^f00006] for “or 81” substitute“, 81 or 81AA”.

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Mike O'Brien

Home Office

12th March 2001

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Licensing (Special Hours Certificates) Rules 1982 consequent on the Deregulation (Sunday Licensing) Order 2001 which makes provision for the imposition of a limitation on special hours certificates to exclude Sundays in the case of disturbance or disorder.
