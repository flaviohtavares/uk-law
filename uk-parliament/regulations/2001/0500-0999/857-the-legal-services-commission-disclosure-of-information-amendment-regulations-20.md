# The Legal Services Commission (Disclosure of Information) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 857

LEGAL SERVICES COMMISSION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Legal Services Commission (Disclosure of Information) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

Made

8th March 2001

Laid before Parliament

12th March 2001

Coming into force

2nd April 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 20(2) of the Access to Justice Act 1999[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Services Commission (Disclosure of Information) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 2nd April 2001.

## 2. Amendments to the Legal Services Commission (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 2000[^f00002]

After regulation 5 of the Legal Services Commission (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 2000 the following shall be inserted:

> **6.** The Commission shall not disclose to the prosecuting authority any information which:
>
> - (a) is in connection with the defence of the individual concerned; and
> - (b) may be used for the purposes of the prosecution of that case.

.

Irvine of Lairg , *C.*

Dated 8th March 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Legal Services Commission (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 2000 so as to prohibit the Commission from disclosing to the prosecution any information in connection with the defence which may be used for the prosecution of the case.
