# The Magistrates' Courts (Transfer of Justices' Clerks' Functions) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Rules 2001, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**2.** The following Rules are amended in accordance with the provisions of the Schedule to these Rules:—

- (i) the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) Rules 1922[^f00002] and the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) (Amendment) Rules 2000[^f00003];
- (ii) the Maintenance Orders Act 1950 (Summary Jurisdiction) Rules 1950[^f00004];
- (iii) the Magistrates' Courts (Maintenance Orders Act 1958) Rules 1959[^f00005];
- (iv) the Magistrates' Courts (Backing of Warrants) Rules 1965[^f00006];
- (v) the Magistrates' Courts (Attachment of Earnings) Rules 1971[^f00007];
- (vi) the Magistrates' Courts (Reciprocal Enforcement of Maintenance Orders) Rules 1974[^f00008];
- (vii) the Magistrates' Courts (Reciprocal Enforcement of Maintenance Orders) (Republic of Ireland) Rules 1975[^f00009];
- (viii) the Magistrates' Courts (Recovery Abroad of Maintenance) Rules 1975[^f00010];
- (ix) the Magistrates' Courts (Reciprocal Enforcement of Maintenance Orders) (Hague Convention Countries) Rules 1980[^f00011];
- (x) the Magistrates' Courts (Forms) Rules 1981[^f00012];
- (xi) the Magistrates' Courts (Adoption) Rules 1984[^f00013];
- (xii) the Magistrates' Courts (Discontinuance of Proceedings) Rules 1986[^f00014];
- (xiii) the Magistrates' Courts (Child Abduction and Custody) Rules 1986[^f00015];
- (xiv) the Magistrates' Courts (Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982) Rules 1986[^f00016];
- (xv) the Magistrates' Courts (Family Law Act 1986) Rules 1988[^f00017];
- (xvi) the Magistrates' Courts (Notices of Transfer) Rules 1988[^f00018];
- (xvii) the Magistrates' Courts (Extradition) Rules 1989[^f00019];
- (xviii) the Magistrates' Courts (Social Security Act 1986) (Transfer of Orders to maintain and Enforcement of Maintenance Orders) Rules 1990[^f00020];
- (xix) the Magistrates' Courts (Criminal Justice (International Co-operation)) Rules 1991[^f00021];
- (xx) the Family Proceedings (Children Act 1989) Rules 1991[^f00022];
- (xxi) the Family Proceedings Courts (Constitution) Rules 1991[^f00023];
- (xxii) the Magistrates' Courts (Detention and Forfeiture of Drug Trafficking Cash) Rules 1991[^f00024];
- (xxiii) the Family Proceedings Courts (Matrimonial Proceedings etc.) Rules 1991[^f00025];
- (xxiv) the Magistrates' Courts (Costs Against Legal Representatives in Civil Proceedings) Rules 1991[^f00026];
- (xxv) the Magistrates' Courts (Attendance Centre) Rules 1992[^f00027];
- (xxvi) the Magistrates' Courts (Notice of Transfer) (Children’s Evidence) Rules 1992[^f00028];
- (xxvii) the Magistrates' Courts (Children and Young Persons) Rules 1992[^f00029];
- (xxviii) the Magistrates' Courts (Reciprocal Enforcement of Maintenance Orders) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Rules 1993[^f00030];
- (xxix) the Family Proceedings Courts (Child Support Act 1991) Rules 1993[^f00031];
- (xxx) the Magistrates' Courts (Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996) (Disclosure) Rules 1997[^f00032];
- (xxxi) the Magistrates' Courts (Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996) (Confidentiality) Rules 1997[^f00033];
- (xxxii) the Magistrates' Courts (Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996) (Tainted Acquittals) Rules 1997[^f00034];
- (xxxiii) the Magistrates' Courts (Sex Offender and Anti-social Behaviour Orders) Rules 1998[^f00035];
- (xxxiv) the Magistrates' Courts (Hearsay Evidence in Civil Proceedings) Rules 1999[^f00036].
