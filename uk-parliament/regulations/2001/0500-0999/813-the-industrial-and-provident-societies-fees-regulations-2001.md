# The Industrial and Provident Societies (Fees) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 813

INDUSTRIAL AND PROVIDENT SOCIETIES

The Industrial and Provident Societies (Fees) Regulations 2001

Made

8th March 2001

Laid before Parliament

9th March 2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 70(1) and 71(1) of the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965[^f00001], and by those sections as applied by sections 1(2)(b), 4(1)(c) and 7(2) of the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1967[^f00002], hereby make the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Industrial and Provident Societies (Fees) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “the Act” means the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965;
- “society” means a registered society.

## 3. Fees Payable

The fees set out in the Schedule hereto shall be payable in advance to the Central Office or, in Scotland, to the Assistant Registrar of Friendly Societies for Scotland in respect of the matters specified in the Schedule.

## 4. Consequential Revocations

The Industrial and Provident Societies (Fees) Regulations 2000[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Jim Dowd

Greg Pope

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

8th March 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These regulations increase the fees payable for registration and other transactions under the Industrial and Provident Societies Acts 1965 and 1967. They revoke the Industrial and Provident Societies (Fees) Regulations 2000. Taking all the fees together, some of which remain unchanged, the overall increase is about 3.6%. The increases are needed in order that there is a full recovery of the costs of carrying out the transactions. The fee changes are as follows:—
