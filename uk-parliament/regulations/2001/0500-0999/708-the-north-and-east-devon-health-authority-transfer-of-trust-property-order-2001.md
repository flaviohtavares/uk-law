# The North and East Devon Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 708

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The North and East Devon Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2001

Made

6th March 2001

Laid before Parliament

8th March 2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the proposed changes in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities presently managed by the Exeter and District Community Health Service National Health Service Trust[^f00002], and having consulted that Trust and the North and East Devon Health Authority in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the North and East Devon Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the North and East Devon Health Authority;
- “the trust” means the Exeter and District Community Health Service National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by, agreed upon by and signed on behalf of the Authority and the trust on 31st January 2001.

## 2. Transfers of trust property

On 1st April 2001 the trust property shall be transferred from the trust to the Authority.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Gisela Stuart

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

6th March 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the North and East Devon Health Authority on 1st April 2001 trust property held by the Exeter and District Community Health Service National Health Service Trust. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, Accounts and Trust Allocations Branch, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
