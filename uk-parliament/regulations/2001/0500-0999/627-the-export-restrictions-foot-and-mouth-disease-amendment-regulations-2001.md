# The Export Restrictions (Foot-And-Mouth Disease) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2001-03-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/627
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

2001 No. 627

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Export Restrictions (Foot-And-Mouth Disease) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

Made

1st March 2001

Laid before Parliament

1st March 2001

Coming into force

1st March 2001 at midday

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the Common Agricultural Policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that section, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Title, commencement and extent

These Regulations may be cited as the Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Amendment) Regulations 2001; they extend to the United Kingdom and shall come into force on 1st March 2001 at midday.

## 2. Amendment to the Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) Regulations 2001

The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) Regulations 2001[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting the following provision for regulation 1(2)—

> (2) These Regulations shall apply until midnight on 2nd March 2001.

.

Joyce Quin

Minister of State Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

1st March 2001 at 12.00

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations extend the export restrictions in the Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 until midnight on 2nd March 2001.
