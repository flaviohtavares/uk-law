# The Climate Change Levy (General) Regulations 2001, section 13

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**13.** Regulation 61 is included to allow the levying of distress for the recovery of CCL[^f00020].

## Annotations

[^f00020]: Footnote: CCL is made a “relevant tax” for the purposes of S.I. [1997/1431](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1431). For enforcement by diligence in Scotland, see section 52 of the Finance Act [1997 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/16) as amended by paragraph 7 of Schedule 7 to the Finance Act 2000.
