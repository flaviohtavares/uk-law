# The Climate Change Levy (General) Regulations 2001, section 4

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**4.** Supplies for the production of a range of commodities is exempt from CCL[^f00014]. Regulation 40 provides a non-exhaustive list of those activities that are too remote from the production process to qualify for the exemption.

## Annotations

[^f00014]: Footnote: Paragraphs 13 and 14(1) of Schedule 6 to the Finance Act [2000 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/17).
