# The Climate Change Levy (General) Regulations 2001, section 51F

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51F. CCL treatment dependent on certification[^c-803aa9fa3f649411679cf9cd7eb6114a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Electricity shall not be regarded as QPO electricity for the purposes of regulation 4(1)(a) of the Climate Change Levy (Electricity and Gas) Regulations 2001 (direct supplies of electricity by utility from fully exempt CHP) unless it remains the subject of an unrestricted CHP LEC.
