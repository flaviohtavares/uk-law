# The Climate Change Levy (General) Regulations 2001, section 51N

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51N. Input fuels referable to the production of electricity in combined heat and power stations[^c-c2b456b20870cc6fcc7702c39a2d31a6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 3 has effect for the purpose of determining the extent to which a quantity of a carbon price support rate commodity is referable to the production of electricity in a combined heat and power station.
