# The Climate Change Levy (General) Regulations 2001, section 20

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**20.**[^key-c1da3b9c5ed1b116448f08a7df5d5fc6] For the purposes of paragraph 64(2) of the Act (defence by the Commissioners that repayment by them of an amount claimed would unjustly enrich the claimant) reimbursement arrangements made by a claimant shall be disregarded except where they—

- (a) include the provisions described in regulation 21, and
- (b) are supported by the undertakings described in regulation 25.
