# The Climate Change Levy (General) Regulations 2001, section 12

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Tax credits: general[^key-28cdbf9ada42c758b64cd7f12ec1891f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The provisions of this Part have effect subject to the requirements of Part III and Schedule 1[^key-2e6dd834efabccb5c8fd71b94b4aecf1] (certification scheme for excluded, exempt [^key-92b961540a68ebaaaa39cbb0a48631d8]... [^key-69c2cd53ed1e60671d4c3c2089e14e48]... and reduced-rate supplies).

(2) Accordingly, no tax credit shall arise by virtue of regulation 11 where the circumstances are such that provision is made by Schedule 1[^key-d22563f5f32dd0551973d83766fc1d13] for a tax credit, for the benefit of the recipient, relating to the amount in question.
