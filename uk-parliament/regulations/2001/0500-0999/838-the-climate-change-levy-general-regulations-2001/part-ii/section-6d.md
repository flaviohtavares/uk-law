# The Climate Change Levy (General) Regulations 2001, section 6D

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6D. Cessation of authorisation[^c-a847c1b9d28d10c955d2fdace582a5df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An authorised person shall continue to account for CCL in accordance with the scheme until he ceases to be authorised.

(2) An authorised person ceases to be authorised when—

- (a) at the end of any transitional accounting period[^c-d6acb73b91c3cb67a6763126b554f89f] or current accounting year the amount of CCL on taxable supplies made by him during that period or year has exceeded £2,000;
- (b) his authorisation is terminated in accordance with regulation 6E;
- (c) he—
  - (i) becomes insolvent and ceases to trade, other than for the purpose of disposing of stocks and assets,
  - (ii) ceases business or ceases to be registered,
  - (iii) dies, becomes bankrupt or incapacitated;
- (d) he ceases to operate the scheme of his own volition.
