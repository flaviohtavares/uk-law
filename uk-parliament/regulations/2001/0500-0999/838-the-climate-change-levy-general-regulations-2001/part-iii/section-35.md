# The Climate Change Levy (General) Regulations 2001, section 35

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**35.**[^key-aad03afb14501985ecce182e6b5ea0c9] (1) A taxable supply is to be treated as being [^key-d298579c63219065fa2141d9dce4b50e]... a reduced-rate[^key-274f4efe3b654d070dc8fa9320bb390a] supply only if and to the extent that [^key-8305bc4786666a2a24167477cbad8b86]... the recipient delivers[^key-2927f302bc600184b65d027414aef2a9] to the supplier a certificate that accords with paragraph (2).

(2) Any such certificate shall—

- (a)[^key-ea5fde4eb81be75d8fb09e6379b71c41] represent (as the case may be) that the supply (or a quantified part of it)—
  - (i) meets the requirements of a reduced-rate supply in paragraph 44(1) of the Act (reduced-rate for supplies covered by climate change agreements), [^key-c53cc89dc00af51c986cbfd3c00be258]...
  - (ii)[^key-c53cc89dc00af51c986cbfd3c00be258] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) comply, as necessary, with regulations 37(2), 37(3), 37(4) and 38(2), and
- (c) be supported, if necessary, in accordance with paragraph (3).

(3) Where the certificate represents that a quantified part of the supply meets the requirements for a reduced-rate[^key-a0da24c23568f5cc4cad0d0e0a935033] supply [^key-233ce8f49b050df575afa9768bcb58dd]..., the recipient must support that certificate with an analysis document demonstrating that the part is calculated in a manner consistent with regulation 38 and Schedule 1[^key-23e0c2c058e7b3450c40e8aa0e6585c5].

(4)[^key-6ee901a7dddfb04ba27580ec4783a3e9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-6ee901a7dddfb04ba27580ec4783a3e9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^key-6ee901a7dddfb04ba27580ec4783a3e9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^key-6ee901a7dddfb04ba27580ec4783a3e9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8)[^key-6ee901a7dddfb04ba27580ec4783a3e9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
