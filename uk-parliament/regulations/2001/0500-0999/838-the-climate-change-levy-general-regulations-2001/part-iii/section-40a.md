# The Climate Change Levy (General) Regulations 2001, section 40A

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**40A.**[^c-13b2122d65b58bc98827dadc0c632977] A quantity of a commodity is not to be the subject of a deemed supply under paragraph 24A of the Act (deemed taxable supplies of commodities to be used in producing electricity) so far as it is the subject of a taxable supply as provided for by regulation 40(2) in its application for the purposes of paragraph 14(1) of the Act.
