# The Climate Change Levy (General) Regulations 2001, section 1

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**1.** These Regulations make further provision for climate change levy (CCL) following the Climate Change Levy (Registration and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2001[^f00013]. These Regulations have effect from the introduction of CCL on 1st April 2001.
