# The Climate Change Levy (General) Regulations 2001, section 5

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**5.** Regulations 41, 42 and 45 avoid a double charge to CCL and facilitate the enjoyment of the half-rate[^f00015] and reduced-rate[^f00016] of CCL. In each case a mischief would otherwise arise because of a supply made by or to an electricity supplier whose purchases of taxable commodities are not generally exempt from CCL.
