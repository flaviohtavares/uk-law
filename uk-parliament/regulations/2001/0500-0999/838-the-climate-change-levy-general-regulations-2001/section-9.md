# The Climate Change Levy (General) Regulations 2001, section 9

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**9.** Regulation 53 prescribes limits above which a person is not a small-scale user of electricity or gas. This affects the frequency with which a supplier must issue a climate change levy accounting document to that person[^f00018].

## Annotations

[^f00018]: Footnote: Paragraph 27 of Schedule 6 to the Finance Act 2000.
