# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Rights to or interests in investments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) to (4), any right to or interest in anything which is specified by any other provision of this Part (other than article 88,[^key-4494b41d6265c22f6faf8ff30847219e] 88A, 88B or 88C[^key-ffecb232820966ce0e036c70f16522ca]).

(2) Paragraph (1) does not include interests under the trusts of an occupational pension scheme.

(2A)[^key-1fd883ec597267fc9e9098aef661d12e] Paragraph (2) does not apply where the kind of activity specified in article 53E (advising on conversion or transfer of pension benefits) or article 89BA (operating a pensions dashboard service)[^key-a85949da5d10d6d2ff3bc5d0715cf9e0] is carried on by way of business in relation to the interests under the trusts of an occupational pension scheme.

(3)[^key-4707bf238cc2705bf33c476f016c6bc3] Paragraph (1) does not include any right or interest acquired as a result of entering into a funeral plan contract.

(4) Paragraph (1) does not include anything which is specified by any other provision of this Part.
