## 82A. Greenhouse gas emissions allowances[^key-48ae86512bb57f01d3d19c3fa69a0961]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Greenhouse gas emissions allowances which are auctioned as financial instruments or as two-day spots within the meaning of Article 3.3 of the emission allowance auctioning regulation or regulation 2(1) of the UK auctioning regulations[^key-0fc73554e86d31128c556895a7a74bc9].
