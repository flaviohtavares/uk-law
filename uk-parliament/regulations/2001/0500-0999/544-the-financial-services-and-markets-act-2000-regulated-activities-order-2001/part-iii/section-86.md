## 86. Lloyd’s syndicate capacity and syndicate membership

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The underwriting capacity of a Lloyd’s syndicate.

(2) A person’s membership (or prospective membership) of a Lloyd’s syndicate.
