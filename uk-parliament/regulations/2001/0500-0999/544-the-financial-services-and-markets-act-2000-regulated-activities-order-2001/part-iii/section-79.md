# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Instruments giving entitlements to investments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Warrants and other instruments entitling the holder to subscribe for any investment of the kind specified by article 76, 77 , 77A[^c-d2f3a588a4dd507a236c886266c6dc4a] or 78.

(2) It is immaterial whether the investment to which the entitlement relates is in existence or identifiable.

(3) An investment of the kind specified by this article is not to be regarded as falling within article 83, 84 or 85.
