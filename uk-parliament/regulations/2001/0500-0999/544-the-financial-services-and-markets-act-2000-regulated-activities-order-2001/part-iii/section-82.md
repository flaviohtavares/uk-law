# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Rights under a pension scheme[^key-b7e860d481033078520098801f2ebd0c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Rights under a stakeholder pension scheme.

(2) Rights under a personal pension scheme.

(3)[^key-628353c732426112adb7ee322fc3ed81] Rights or interests under a pension scheme which provides safeguarded benefits (within the meaning given in article 53E(2)).

(4) Paragraph (3) specifies a kind of investment in relation to the kind of activity specified in article 53E (advising on conversion or transfer of pension benefits), and not in relation to any other specified kind of activity.
