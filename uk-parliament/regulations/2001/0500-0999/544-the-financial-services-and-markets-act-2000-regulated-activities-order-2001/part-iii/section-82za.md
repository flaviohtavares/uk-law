# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 82ZA

## 82ZA. Pensions in respect of which information is requested or provided using a pensions dashboard service[^key-ad19389516c21fe649c3d57afd8a0b5b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any pension in respect of which information is requested or provided using a pensions dashboard service as defined in article 89BA(3) (operating a pensions dashboard service).

(2) Paragraph (1) specifies a kind of investment in relation to the kind of activity specified in article 89BA, and not in relation to any other specified kind of activity.
