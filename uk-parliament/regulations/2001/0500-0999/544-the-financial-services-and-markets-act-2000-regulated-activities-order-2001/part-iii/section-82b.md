# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 82B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82B. Emission allowances[^c-52afc23fcd280e17514ffa2fdbdf89a3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-429c881513b7abd8de87a28c03a98bbf] Subject to paragraph (2)—

- (a) emission allowances consisting of any units recognised for compliance (by an EEA State) with the requirements of the emission allowance trading directive; and
- (b) allowances created under article 18 of the trading scheme order and transferable in accordance with that order.

(2) Paragraph (1) only applies to emission allowances in relation to which—

- (a)[^c-8239776c5ae7d7869eda2d268a8043b6] an investment firm or qualifying credit institution is providing or performing investment services and activities on a professional basis,
- (b) a management company which has a Part 4A permission to do so is providing the investment service specified in paragraph 4 or 5 of Part 3 of Schedule 2 or the ancillary service specified in paragraph 1 of Part 3A of that Schedule,
- (c) a market operator is providing the investment service specified in paragraph 8 or 9 of Part 3 of that Schedule, or
- (d) a full-scope UK AIFM which has a Part 4A permission to do so is providing the investment service specified in paragraph 1, 4 or 5 of Part 3 of that Schedule or the ancillary service specified in paragraph 1 of Part 3A of that Schedule.
