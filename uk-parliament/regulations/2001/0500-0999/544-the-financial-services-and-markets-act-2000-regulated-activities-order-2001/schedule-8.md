## SCHEDULE 8 — PERSONS EXCLUDED FROM REGULATED ACTIVITY OF MANAGING AN AIF[^c-4aa2770a0ac653ea032c125648722b47]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 51ZF

### 1. Interpretation of this Schedule

Any expression used in this Schedule which is used in the alternative investment fund managers directive , and not referred to in paragraph 1B,[^c-0cbe4cf0be7dcabfb262bc83f79155ac] has the same meaning as in that directive.

**1A.**[^c-a330dae05ff2e4e67c7956581a18aadf] For the purposes of paragraph 1, in Article 4(1)(o) of the alternative investment fund managers directive (definition of “holding company”) the reference to the Union is to be read as a reference to the United Kingdom.

**1B.** In this Schedule—

“**parent undertaking**” has the meaning given by section 1162 of the Companies Act 2006 [^c23660071] (read with Schedule 7 to that Act);

“**subsidiary**” has the meaning given to “subsidiary undertaking” by that section (read with that Schedule).

### 2. Persons excluded

A small registered UK AIFM, in respect of the AIFs managed by it by virtue of which it is entitled to be registered as a small registered UK AIFM (but not in respect of any other AIFs managed by it).

**3.** An AIFM in so far as it manages one or more AIFs whose only investors are—

- (a) the AIFM,
- (b) the parent undertakings of the AIFM,
- (c) the subsidiaries of the AIFM, or
- (d) other subsidiaries of those parent undertakings,

provided that none of the investors is an AIF.

**4.**[^c-1efac2fc0492891b3f5cbd84c77d4496] The trustees and managers of an occupational pension scheme within the meaning of section 1(1) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^M_F_523ced75-560e-4e60-9726-6af9cdeeb4a9].

**5.** [^c-8ab7ade91f2daa46e7e21b2fdf423ccc]... The European Investment Bank, the European Investment Fund, a bilateral development bank, the World Bank, the International Monetary Fund or any other[^c-de3d955f6837f42fe15c34d494f900a6] supranational institution or similar international organisation, [^c-dfa3394c19d7eca82db40f652fc4a5cb]... in the event that such institution or organisation manages AIFs and in so far as those AIFs act in the public interest.

**6.** A national central bank.

**7.** A national, regional or local government or body or other institution which manages funds supporting social security and pension systems.

**8.** A holding company.

**9.** An employee participation scheme or employee savings scheme.

**10.** A securitisation special purpose entity.

**11.** An AIFM, the registered office of which is not in the United Kingdom[^c-05b922c51b8d5c0d09048fe08197d6c4] , that is registered in the United Kingdom in accordance with[^c-64cbdefd052e4ebb43d662b8846654c9] Part 3 of the Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013[^c-f01229aa97c8af82eef57578ec8c31f4] .
