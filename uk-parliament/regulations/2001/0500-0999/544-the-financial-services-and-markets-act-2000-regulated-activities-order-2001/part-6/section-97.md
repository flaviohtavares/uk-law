# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 97

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Disapplication of section 49(2) of the Act[^c-386d460ab7e8edc226efbef7859ba174]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 49 of the Act (persons connected with an applicant for Part 4 permission), after subsection (2) insert—

> (2A) But subsection (2) does not apply to the extent that the permission relates to—
>
> - (a) an insurance mediation activity (within the meaning given by paragraph 2(5) of Schedule 6); or
> - (b) a regulated activity involving a regulated mortgage contract.

.
