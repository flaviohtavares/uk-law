# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Activities not constituting administration

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following activities do not constitute the administration of assets for the purposes of article 40—

- (a) providing information as to the number of units or the value of any assets safeguarded;
- (b) converting currency;
- (c) receiving documents relating to an investment solely for the purpose of onward transmission to, from or at the direction of the person to whom the investment belongs.
