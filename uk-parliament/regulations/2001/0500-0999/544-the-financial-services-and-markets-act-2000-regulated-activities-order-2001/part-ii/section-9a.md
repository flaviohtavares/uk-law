# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 9A

## 9A. Sums received in exchange for electronic money[^c-5e744c7ca2653c2ff8f8fb938569cfd4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A sum is not a deposit for the purposes of article 5 if it is immediately exchanged for electronic money.
