# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 35A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544

## 35A. Trade repositories[^c-f456f2617050332ab2f3ea256f0eb0bb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-8e996eaf13099684d0e74742aab20614] A trade repository does not carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 25(2) by carrying on its functions of centrally collecting and maintaining records of—

- (a)[^c-dd08ee43fea8a41d6a24d09d632674a0] derivatives under Regulation (EU) 648/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2012 on OTC derivatives, central counterparties and trade repositories; or[^c-525729a164fa08334777d5d2a9996384]
- (b) securities financing transactions under the SFT regulation.

(2)[^c-e6967860d2ab55b4530e471d6cb1b3f6] The reference in paragraph (1)(a) to Regulation (EU) 648/2012 is a reference to that instrument as it forms part of[^c-6aa64a51de4cf2745203704d8f4144b4] assimilated[^c-be23405c021a39cadd3a7fd9e8695424] law.
