## 44. Other exclusions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 40 is also subject to the exclusions in articles 66 (trustees etc.), 67 (profession or non-investment business), 68 (sale of goods and supply of services), 69 (groups and joint enterprises), 71 (employee share schemes)[^key-09ab7a749c7ed9fe6b9499f379d77b04][^key-7aa48b8de156465cb6bceab7394be5e2]...[^key-379c23236ba079b470088730c43c4c3e], 72AA (managers of[^key-a5f3ce6bb0db0d2934f4a2bf5cbffa18] UK UCITS[^key-31c933a7cb62870d31c4e05fefa51fab] and AIFs) , 72C (provisions of information about contracts of insurance on an incidental basis) and 72H (insolvency practitioners)[^key-441ba17852080dee4fd83622be731b56].
