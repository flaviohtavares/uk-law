## 55. Other exclusions etc.[^key-dd42d946708eef76bbd52245deb4c4f5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-f0cf088d1cc91d0f4af02489fbe41656] Article 53 is also subject to the exclusions in articles 66 (trustees etc.), 67, (profession or non-investment business), 68 (sale of goods and supply of services), 69 (groups and joint enterprises), 70 (sale of body corporate) , 72 (overseas persons)[^key-c1c4e4ed45ecf8d0d01c025ed7102f90][^key-ea93d273af376481a3540d0a6ab6f872]... 72B (activities carried on by a provider of relevant goods or services)[^key-6ed20b51e9255ac827e18e36ff825d6a] , 72AA (managers of[^key-97600dca45464bbd7d419dbf5f7a02a9] UK UCITS[^key-8cb4b6c5ebdfdea64da374ecf55ebdda] and AIFs) , 72D (large risks contracts where risk situated[^key-11f42931ecf18747c74a966696f4a509] outside the United Kingdom[^key-61d9db02f2ba1cd4f9124623b190dba9]), 72G (local authorities) and 72H (insolvency practitioners).

(1A)[^key-368055f72bde11eba53c2d640185e897] Article 53 is also subject to article 55A(2).

(2)[^key-e83f2fc6d53e1ffcb930e88b8fa6245a] Articles 53A, 53B[^key-af83ecd94490592ae0878483afe1db06] ,[^key-ec742f191cd3e78d073478e95f0b36b5] 53C, 53D and 53DA[^key-e4c7dc12b76b92bd0f372c3b035e3dac] are also subject to the exclusions in articles 66 (trustees etc.), 67 (profession or non-investment business) [^key-71f54d94ac58fd8ad479a1cc4fb6d5d9]...[^key-29d0b201e836fd28c22f9ace66f6dc1a], 72AA (managers of[^key-e86b9dcba62572dd0f09a24a03b35626] UK UCITS and AIFs) , 72G (local authorities) and 72I (registered consumer buy-to-let mortgage firms).[^key-15818f76090ebb8007b69485137b0936]
