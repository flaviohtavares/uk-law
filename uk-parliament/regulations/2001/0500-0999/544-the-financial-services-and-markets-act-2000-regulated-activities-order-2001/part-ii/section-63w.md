# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 63W

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63W. Unregulated benchmarks[^key-d686ca47d70e55ec032b2f7748406496]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person does not carry on the activity of the kind specified by article 63U where they provide an ESG rating in the course of providing a benchmark to which the UK Benchmarks Regulation does not apply as a result of Article 2(2)(a) to (g) (scope: exclusions) of that Regulation.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), an ESG rating is not provided in the course of providing a benchmark of the kind mentioned in paragraph (1) where the ESG rating is provided as a standalone product or service.
