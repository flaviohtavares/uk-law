## 39E. Debt-counselling[^c-17d5017841c99d8e2e20b44565cb7975]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Giving advice to a borrower about the liquidation of a debt due under a credit agreement is a specified kind of activity.

(2) Giving advice to a hirer about the liquidation of a debt due under a consumer hire agreement is a specified kind of activity.
