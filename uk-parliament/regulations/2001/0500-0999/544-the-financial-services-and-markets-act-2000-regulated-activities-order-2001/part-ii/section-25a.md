# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 25A

## 25A. Arranging regulated mortgage contracts[^key-4c8ca643a6495e4a5a2e21936ebcda7b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Making arrangements—

- (a) for another person to enter into a regulated mortgage contract as borrower; or
- (b) for another person to vary the terms of a regulated mortgage contract falling within paragraph (1A)[^key-09d0835ca3c3703c6b3e5c7af29d6584] entered into by him as borrower [^key-6ac02b72e33ba315df1119c32ed31615]..., in such a way as to vary his obligations under that contract,

is a specified kind of activity.

(1A)[^key-e195b895573f8b1504144e3f80a9b7ce] A regulated mortgage contract falls within this paragraph if—

- (a) the contract was entered into on or after 31st October 2004; or
- (b) the contract—
  - (i) was entered into before 31st October 2004; and
  - (ii) was a regulated credit agreement immediately before 21st March 2016.

(2) Making arrangements with a view to a person who participates in the arrangements entering into a regulated mortgage contract as borrower is also a specified kind of activity.

(2A)[^key-a93a8e7d0fe99d916cbe278daaef8f23] Making arrangements to enter into a regulated mortgage contract with a borrower on behalf of a lender is also a specified kind of activity.

(3) In this article “borrower” and “lender” have the meanings[^key-3d2225e21714cb6d72c48a4607c161ae] given by article 61(3)(a)(i).
