## 36FB. Activities in relation to certain regulated credit agreements[^key-28ba0c8eb64270e8911b4ddf8c52e263][^key-b52642df0c809f1aa295b3f79f2dbaea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There are excluded from article 36A activities carried on in relation to a regulated deferred payment credit agreement.

(4) In this article—

- “**regulated deferred payment credit agreement**” means an agreement—
  - (a) which meets each of the conditions set out in article 60F(2)(a) to (d) (exempt agreements: exemptions relating to number of repayments to be made); and
  - (b) to which paragraph (7A) of that article applies.
