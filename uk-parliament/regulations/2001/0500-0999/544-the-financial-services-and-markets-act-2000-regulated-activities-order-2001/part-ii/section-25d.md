# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 25D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25D. Operating a multilateral trading facility[^c-d9c822a3c269813881cab195c580e1ba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The operation of a multilateral trading facility on which MiFID instruments are traded is a specified kind of activity.

(1A)[^c-978a35d751a82238767110d0754cb8e9] Paragraph (1) does not apply to a kind of activity to which article 25DB applies.

(2) In paragraph (1), “MiFID instrument” means any investment—

- (a) of the kind specified by article 76, 77, 77A,[^c-df566462e763cb75af60c6799f055888] 78, 79, 80, 81, 82B,[^c-3c9f9ef49897e42ab05b3d62ab57b22b] 83, 84 or 85; or
- (b) of the kind specified by article 89 so far as relevant to an investment falling within sub-paragraph (a),

that is a financial instrument.
