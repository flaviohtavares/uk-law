# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Enabling parties to communicate

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-0ff7d230420295d422b2686b4c3b6351] A person does not carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 25(2), 25A(2), 25B(2)[^c-8d7958e54af0c6c871b3e99cd07208ae] , 25C(2) or 25 E(2)[^c-677dd25b57531df3b1d2b9e472205d48] merely by providing means by which one party to a transaction (or potential transaction) is able to communicate with other such parties.

(2)[^c-c7565ac0dd632051ec093c606dabc6fc] A person does not carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 25DB merely by providing a means of communication, where the person is not holding out the means of communication as being provided for the making of qualifying public offers within the meaning of that article.
