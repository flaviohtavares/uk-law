# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 25

## 25. Arranging deals in investments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Making arrangements for another person (whether as principal or agent) to buy, sell, subscribe for or underwrite a particular investment which is—

- (a) a security,
- (b) a relevant investment[^key-1870bccb513e7f1d496ce3a0ac59fa68], [^key-18e60e3d3efbbeb024896e148bd6f60d]...
- (c) an investment of the kind specified by article 86, or article 89 so far as relevant to that article , or[^key-25533b95bbbaa5c76eb703c552f3953f]
- (d) a structured deposit,

is a specified kind of activity.

(2) Making arrangements with a view to a person who participates in the arrangements buying, selling, subscribing for or underwriting investments falling within paragraph (1)(a), (b) , (c) or (d)[^key-8d11894e428209eb5caf374875acee10] (whether as principal or agent) is also a specified kind of activity.

(3)[^key-235cc584d91a1331b5f60af23477d1c2] Paragraphs (1) and (2) do not apply to a kind of activity to which article 25D , 25DA or 25DB, applies.[^key-7725fbc73e78e71d6c23bff973857936]
