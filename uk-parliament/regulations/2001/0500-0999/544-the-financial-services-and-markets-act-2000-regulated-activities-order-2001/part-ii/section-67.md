# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544

## 67. Activities carried on in the course of a profession or non-investment business

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There is excluded from articles 21, 25(1) and (2) , 25A[^c-14d0217a22f2b95f0d6f40630b570add], 25B, 25C,[^c-57f6560d1efd6d84ce15e76cfbd19563] 25E,[^c-b7798497786cd115f2e48776abd2b18b] 39A, 40[^c-c294003c10bba3d0a5a6a19a1efeb617] 53[^c-7ee44783f2fb9aa1b3c6acf00f7901c8] , 53A, 53B,[^c-fc76b8a003c5c871cde0357e93563111] 53C, 53D[^c-642edde8504db419eb965666e40ca148] , 53DA and 55A[^c-b59d9804a715bf681781f9ba40015346] any activity which—

- (a) is carried on in the course of carrying on any profession or business which does not otherwise consist of the carrying on of regulated activities in the United Kingdom[^c-a639e7857cdf9a27d3f75d9d86de6038]; and
- (b) may reasonably be regarded as a necessary part of other services provided in the course of that profession or business.

(2) But the exclusion in paragraph (1) does not apply if the activity in question is remunerated separately from the other services.

(3)[^c-0b594b9b8fe1b2c1cd18877f65ce3a56] This article is subject to article 4(4), (4A) and (4B)[^c-9089b623d9f0f566bc3f44966a3416e9].
