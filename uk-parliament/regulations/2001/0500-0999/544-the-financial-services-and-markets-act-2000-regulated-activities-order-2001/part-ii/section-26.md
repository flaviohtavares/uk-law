# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Arrangements not causing a deal

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There are excluded from articles 25(1), 25A(1), 25B(1)[^key-bb8a261d6308059634cd7493ab2f156e] , 25C(1) and 25E(1)[^key-1d36172946963a7ecabdbb37dcb0712a] arrangements which do not or would not bring about the transaction to which the arrangements relate.
