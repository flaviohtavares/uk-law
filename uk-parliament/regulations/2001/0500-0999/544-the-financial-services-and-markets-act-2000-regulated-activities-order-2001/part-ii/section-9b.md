# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 9B

## 9B. Issuing electronic money[^key-553740a5a5511f8a0755c9ba7dd31279]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Issuing electronic money by—[^key-18ea7417815915bbe07fa6e2a7890ca4]

- (a) a qualifying credit institution[^key-25704e89542d37bc6cf468f062a8f5b0], a credit union or a municipal bank; or
- (b) a person who is deemed to have been granted authorisation under regulation 74 of the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 or who falls within regulation 76(1) of those Regulations,

is a specified kind of activity.
