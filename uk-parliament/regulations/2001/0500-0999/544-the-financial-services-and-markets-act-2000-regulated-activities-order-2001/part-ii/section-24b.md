# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 24B

## 24B. Miscellaneous exclusions[^c-7a9a42135638cfc87269019e64cd6b1b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There is excluded from article 24A any activity carried on by—

- (a) an operator or an aircraft operator having an operator holding account, in either case bidding on its own account, including any parent undertaking, subsidiary undertaking or affiliated undertaking[^c-212c5a0cdff598499fa1b3bb76d7439f] forming part of the same group of undertakings as the operator or the aircraft operator;
- (b) business groupings of persons listed in sub-paragraph (a) bidding on their own account or acting as an agent on behalf of their members; or
- (c) public bodies or state-owned entities of the Member States or of the United Kingdom[^c-818d86eeeac58dd20243b94b31e6823c] that control any of the persons listed in sub-paragraph (a).

(2)[^c-47b0ef8c03ebcded11c75cf049ecda8c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In this article, the expressions “parent undertaking”, “subsidiary undertaking”, "affiliated undertaking"[^c-c67fc0db4202f720e6b3c5d94e135cc0], “business grouping”, “public bodies” and “state-owned entities” have the same meaning as in the UK auctioning regulations[^c-9737819f7b197e86388e75efc95d4238].
