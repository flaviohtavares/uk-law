# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 51ZB

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51ZB. Acting as trustee or depositary of a UK UCITS[^key-dc40b2381edc494a102ae76de171ecc0][^key-c82c4c8e520e00b47d8e7f9bab1ba17a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Acting as—

- (a) the trustee of an authorised unit trust scheme, or
- (b) the depositary of an open-ended investment company or authorised contractual scheme,

where the scheme or company is a UK UCITS[^key-295f2cbda2dc27a2fbf0158634d02720], is a specified kind of activity.

(2) In paragraph (1), “authorised contractual scheme”, “**authorised unit trust scheme**”, “**trustee**” and “**depositary**” have the meanings given by section 237 of the Act (other definitions).
