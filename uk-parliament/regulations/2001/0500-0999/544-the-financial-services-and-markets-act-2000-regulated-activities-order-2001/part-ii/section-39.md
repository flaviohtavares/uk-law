## 39. Other exclusions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 37 is also subject to the exclusions in articles 66 (trustees etc.), 68 (sale of goods and supply of services), 69 (groups and joint enterprises)[^key-ed6a3dea53e29be6836725bd99db000e][^key-ab56d6ee7583455216c20467d839be66]...[^key-b11c7d4f87d1b01304f1047822d99d92], 72AA (managers of[^key-9bc35bbaffb2f3634f040a66e15c42be] UK UCITS[^key-fd1882944c856f784d6a13f5cd0d4fa1] and AIFs) , 72C (provision of information about contracts of insurance on an incidental basis) and 72H (insolvency practitioners)[^key-deaa66eb05981169f039ef436132fd54].
