# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Attorneys

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-f84a66a19cf0eaa7ca2880ed2bf37439] A person does not carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 37 if—

- (a) he is a person appointed to manage the assets in question under a power of attorney; and
- (b) all routine or day-to-day decisions, so far as relating to investments of a kind mentioned in article 37(a), are taken on behalf of that person by—
  - (i) an authorised person with permission to carry on activities of the kind specified by article 37; [^key-d3717d7d5fb99966138ee67705e6f1bb]...
  - (ii) a person who is an exempt person in relation to activities of that kind ; or[^key-ada89d5da4365b5bd1cecd112dee563f]
  - (iii) an overseas person.

(2)[^key-a213dd6ce58df134bba43a97a78308e0] This article is subject to article 4(4).
