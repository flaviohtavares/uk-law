## 58. Arranging deals in contracts of insurance written at Lloyd’s

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The arranging, by the society incorporated by Lloyd’s Act 1871 [^c16224891] by the name of Lloyd’s, of deals in contracts of insurance written at Lloyd’s, is a specified kind of activity.
