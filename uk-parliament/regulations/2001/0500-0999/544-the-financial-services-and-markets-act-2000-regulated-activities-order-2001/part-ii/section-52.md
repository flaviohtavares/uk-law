# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 52

## 52. Establishing etc. a pension scheme[^c-310af5d7c36a8ef84b39e8678feb0aa6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following are specified kinds of activity—

- (a) establishing, operating or winding up a stakeholder pension scheme;
- (b) establishing, operating or winding up a personal pension scheme.
