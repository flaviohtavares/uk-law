## 39C. Other exclusions[^c-f392e4056396d0ebcee1a5fb33bd136d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 39A is also subject to the exclusions in articles 66 (trustees etc.), 67 (profession or non-investment business)[^c-27392d1134b2f301c218464a67715768]..., 72B (activities carried on by a provider of relevant goods or services), 72C (provision of information about contracts of insurance on an incidental basis) , 72AA (managers of[^M_F_57f4e266-ecf2-49c7-863b-9fcef62a72d9] UK UCITS[^c-752fbb1dc2d6c2f61367d98eab5b3f78] and AIFs) , 72D (large risks contracts where risk situated[^c-4670406b4818908d97d97a5ced3772e1] outside the United Kingdom[^c-ddc45ff077e86de2f25889f7d9fea492]), 72G (local authorities) and 72H (insolvency practitioners)).
