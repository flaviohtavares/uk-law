## 63A. Other exclusions[^c-b444742ba1b42a69f47f454ca5f066ba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 61 is also subject to the exclusions in articles 66 (trustees etc.), 72 (overseas persons) ,[^c-4b4c1ee8a957e73f1ba3b70a70d45790][^c-267424fe7135311717aa9fb1dbb200bd]... 72AA (managers of[^c-b082bb534be735120d984ff4fe8fa172] UK UCITS[^c-d84d59feaa074909c1b6df0f383aa2d4] and AIFs) , 72G (local authorities) and 72I (registered consumer buy-to-let mortgage firms).[^c-23693e2ea63499fb05a6cdc4ebaed85c]
