# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 55A

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55A. Providing Targeted Support[^c-65187406fd8bf64c555b320571531604]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Providing targeted support is a specified kind of activity.

(2) Where a person provides targeted support they are not advising an individual for the purposes of article 53 (advising on investments).

(3) For the purposes of paragraphs (1) and (2), a person (“**P**”) provides targeted support when—

- (a) P uses information about an individual to group the individual with other individuals in a group who share—
  - (i) similar characteristics;
  - (ii) similar circumstances; or
  - (iii) similar characteristics and similar circumstances;
- (b) P makes a recommendation which—
  - (i) is provided to the individual;
  - (ii) constitutes a recommendation for the individual to do or not do any of the following—
    - (aa) buy, sell, subscribe for, exchange, redeem, hold or underwrite a particular investment which is a security, structured deposit or a relevant investment; or
    - (bb) exercise any right conferred by such an investment to buy, sell, subscribe for, exchange or redeem such an investment; and
  - (iii) is presented as suitable for the individual on the basis of the individual being in the group; and
- (c) P makes a statement to the individual in accordance with paragraphs (4) and (5).

(4) The statement made by P must specify—

- (a) that the recommendation is not based on a comprehensive consideration by P of the individual’s characteristics or circumstances;
- (b) that the recommendation is not specific to the individual; and
- (c) the characteristics, or circumstances, or both if applicable, of the group which P has used as the basis for the recommendation.

(5) The statement must be made by P at the same time as the recommendation is made to the individual.

(6) In this article,

- “the group” means the group of individuals referred to in paragraph (3)(a).
