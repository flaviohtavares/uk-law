# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 24

## 24. Other exclusions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 21 is also subject to the exclusions in articles 67 (profession or non-investment business), 68 (sale of goods and supply of services), 69 (groups and joint enterprises), 70 (sale of body corporate), 71 (employee share schemes) , 72 (overseas persons)[^c-4e8251b61cef438bbc6b6f5d2f48e143],[^c-df442a6eecb90214d294f9c0adc4407a] [^c-1042c45526a6c0bfe76215f7742c1924]... 72B (activities carried on by a provider of relevant goods or services) , 72AA (managers of[^c-7cdc5886f7be89ce69751a8a1954cac0] UK UCITS[^c-bfc685b6c9bb4491192af74680387e56] and AIFs), 72D (large risks contracts where risk situated[^c-23b2df8a1742863baa9a598e27348a91] outside the United Kingdom[^c-d750811cc2bde9165d40ee4d654db9ad]), 72G (local authorities) and 72H (insolvency practitioners).
