# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Dealing in investments as principal

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-39e937ced0ec2f149478e7b7d54ecd62] Buying, selling, subscribing for or underwriting securities or contractually based investments (other than investments of the kind specified by article 87, or article 89 so far as relevant to that article) as principal is a specified kind of activity.

(2)[^key-c7a5bdb9abd1aac8edb364ad42cfb68a] Paragraph (1) does not apply to a kind of activity to which article 25D or 25DA[^key-6dad6a43f639c473bea74bdd62e0e221] applies.
