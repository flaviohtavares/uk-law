# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544

## 45. Sending dematerialised instructions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Sending, on behalf of another person, dematerialised instructions relating to a security or a contractually based investment[^c-7971608d75d44da26997e404dd93e13c] is a specified kind of activity, where those instructions are sent by means of a relevant system within the meaning of the 2001 Regulations[^c-19064ed3339d09220f4dd03784f951be].

(2) Causing dematerialised instructions relating to a security or a contractually based investment[^c-412043b832522f3829f12241a3e21ef0] to be sent on behalf of another person[^c-e4cbd6e646c6a7cf1e290b5e12fc06a5] by means of such a system is also a specified kind of activity where the person causing them to be sent is a system-participant.

(3) In this Chapter—

- (a)[^c-b7dd5d692038f32bf0eb1be48e107c61] “the 2001 Regulations” means the Uncertificated Securities Regulations 2001;
- (b) “dematerialised instruction", [^c-3f6024acd9aa232ce2b5f83ae427d572]..., “settlement bank" and “system-participant" have the meaning given by regulation 3 of the 2001[^c-3cd5b478443255855b25aee30551a601] Regulations.
