# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 63T

## 63T. Administration of a benchmark by the FCA[^key-4cc94feaae2606bd973245c6a02f914a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The FCA does not carry on the activity of the kind specified by article 63S(1) in relation to a benchmark where the FCA administers the benchmark itself.
