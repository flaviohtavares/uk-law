# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 42A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42A. Depositaries of UK UCITS and AIFs[^key-7c7f1a9c13a2c407dcac1fdd4cb9a79e][^key-5da7de6c0e48c6a3aaba5083261873db][^key-3dfac9d781cc32e2e791b35d02d1d0a7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person does not carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 40 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d371c44805af39974a6513bbde8e5559] article 9N or 40 if the person carries on the activity in relation to—

- (a) a UK UCITS[^key-5efe9417439356e71f35fbff1ae1fd39], and the person has a Part 4A permission to carry on the activity specified in article 51ZB in respect of that UCITS; or
- (b) an AIF, and the person has a Part 4A permission to carry on the activity specified in article 51ZD in respect of that AIF.
