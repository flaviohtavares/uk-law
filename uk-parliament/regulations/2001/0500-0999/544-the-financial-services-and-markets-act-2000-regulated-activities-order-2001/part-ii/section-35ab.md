# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 35AB

## 35AB. Securitisation repositories[^c-9f42c5076cf8674ed75d7a5a0da096cb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A securitisation repository does not carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 25(2) by carrying on its functions of centrally collecting and maintaining records of securitisations under the Securitisation Regulations 2024[^c-ec9b30ceef9559a78c9f464e85741554].
