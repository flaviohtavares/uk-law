# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 9H

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9H. Rules prohibiting the issue of electronic money at a discount[^key-553740a5a5511f8a0755c9ba7dd31279]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The[^key-1f7a7086d04b59011910a07c132647c6] FCA may make rules applying to authorised persons with permission to carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 9B, prohibiting the issue of electronic money having a monetary value greater than the funds received.

(2) Section 138A[^key-764bdb3ccd8824635a0ff221fcd4aea2] of the Act (modification or waiver of rules) applies in relation to rules made under paragraph (1).
