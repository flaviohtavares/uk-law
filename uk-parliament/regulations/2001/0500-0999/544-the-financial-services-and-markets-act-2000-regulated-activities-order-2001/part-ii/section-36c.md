## 36C. Activities for which no fee is paid[^c-b52642df0c809f1aa295b3f79f2dbaea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There are excluded from sub-paragraphs (d), (e) and (f) of article 36A(1) activities carried on by a person for which that person does not receive a fee.

(2) For the purposes of this article, “fee” includes pecuniary consideration or any other form of financial consideration.
