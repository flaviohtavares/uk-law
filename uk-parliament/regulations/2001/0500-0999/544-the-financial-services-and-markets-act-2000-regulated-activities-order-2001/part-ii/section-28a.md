# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 28A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28A. Arranging contracts , plans or agreements to which the arranger is a party[^c-ce33f39122d1a69c154dbef0af119d42][^c-e61c9a81f377b32da1fb01fc5a9497a7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There are excluded from articles 25A(1), 25B(1)[^c-844c1b481aa0dd48d5208e936348d7df] , 25C(1) and 25E(1)[^c-05e892a6f3836b80e7e75bc1ed06493f] any arrangements—

- (a) for a contract[^c-e999ea3f80a9ea380df40a76cafa8e4c] , plan or agreement[^c-102693d2e02c6ec79776f9922cea3516] into which the person making the arrangements enters or is to enter; or
- (b) for a variation of a contract , plan or agreement to which that person is (or is to become) a party.

(2) There are excluded from articles 25A(2), 25B(2)[^c-dc3ac016e009fbb8fae36ef32dddc905] , 25C(2) and 25E(2)[^c-1845055fdd38838056a3a6b805959fa6] any arrangements which a person makes with a view to contracts , plans or agreements[^c-93f23aa9c2b53eba9a5f609ee9f037a2] into which he enters or is to enter.
