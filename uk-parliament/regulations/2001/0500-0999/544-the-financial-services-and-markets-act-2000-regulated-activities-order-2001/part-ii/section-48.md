# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Instructions in connection with takeover offers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There is excluded from article 45 of the act of sending, or causing to be sent, a dematerialised instruction where the person on whose behalf the instruction is sent or caused to be sent is an offeror making a takeover offer.

(2) In this article—

- (a) “offeror" means, in the case of a takeover offer made by two or more persons jointly, the joint offers or any of them;
- (b) “takeover offer" means—
  - (i) an offer to acquire shares (which in this sub-paragraph has the same meaning as in section 974 of the Companies Act 2006[^c-08de2ac540d1f68c79096679b8c63771]) in a body corporate incorporated in the United Kingdom which is a takeover offer within the meaning of Chapter 3 of Part 28[^c-33836874eab565e2ff2dda3ec7cb0d37] of that Act (or would be such an offer if that Part of that Act applied in relation to any body corporate);
  - (ii) an offer to acquire all or substantially all the shares, or all the shares of a particular class, in a body corporate incorporated outside the United Kingdom; or
  - (iii) an offer made to all the holders of shares, or shares of a particular class, in a body corporate to acquire a specified proportion of those shares;

  but in determining whether an offer falls within paragraph (ii) there are to be disregarded any shares which the offeror or any associate of his (within the meaning of section 988 of the Companies Act 2006[^c-af716deac747606b3f993da998236622]) holds or has contracted to acquire; and in determining whether an offer falls within paragraph (iii) the offeror, any such associate and any person whose shares the offeror or any such associate has contracted to acquire is not to be regarded as a holder of shares.
