## 64. Agreeing to carry on specified kinds of activity

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Agreeing to carry on an activity of the kind specified by any other provision of this Part, Part 3A or Part 3B *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-4ce862e4373c38df08c8912ce6d5a4ca] (other than article 5, 9B,[^c-1fbad3d1b9c76443e6156bfa4ff4a78d] 9M, 9S,[^c-5f93e9efe0870fe6e8e4621f8566e4e4] 10, 25D,[^c-5fd0f470aa2cfde94eb1081600a55841] 25DA,[^c-abcbb0efb5a4cac74de05f58a548feb7] 51ZA, 51ZB, 51ZC, 51ZD, 51ZE[^c-c7793df7226f5a388bf0017397f5f9e2][^c-9f6d07408436d2a2b3334e71edded02c], 52 ,[^c-729388029cd6d9a9bcd58ddc7b3948ad] 63N and 63S[^c-daaa184597911fc6e7e93d1c0aabec7e]) is a specified kind of activity.
