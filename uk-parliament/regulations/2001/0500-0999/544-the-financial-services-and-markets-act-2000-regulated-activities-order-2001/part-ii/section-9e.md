# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 9E

## 9E. Revocation of certificate on FCA’s own initiative[^key-553740a5a5511f8a0755c9ba7dd31279][^key-fdc2d3dc3ecbb7cd86bd0a3092308c37]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The[^key-e09fcefdcfe90f05ca1180a8c6d7640b] FCA may revoke a certificate given to a person (“A”) under article 9C if—

- (a) it appears to it that A does not meet the relevant conditions, or has failed to meet the relevant conditions at any time since the certificate was given; or
- (b) the person to whom the certificate was given has contravened any rule or requirement to which he is subject as a result of article 9G.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), A meets the relevant conditions at any time if, at that time, paragraph (4), (5) or (6) of article 9C applies.

(3) Sections 55Z and 55Z3(2)[^key-dd3f2538f0aa427f627527e1c148891e] of the Act apply to the revocation of a certificate under paragraph (1) as they apply to the cancellation of a Part IV permission on the[^key-bec79dbd200f1189463606ccce5c245d] FCA’s own initiative, as if references in those sections to an authorised person were references to a certified person.
