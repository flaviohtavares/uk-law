# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 9Z7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544

## 9Z7. Article 9Z6 exclusion: introducing[^key-8c1e6456b1778ff000e35bb879e9c9a3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There is excluded from article 9Z6 the provision of services solely for the purpose of introducing a person to an authorised person with a Part 4A permission to carry on the regulated activity specified by article 9Z6.
