# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 39J

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39J. Activities carried on in relation to a relevant agreement in relation to land[^key-17d5017841c99d8e2e20b44565cb7975]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There is[^key-b512c067354c7d0cf928d40948c6a8bd] excluded from articles 39D, 39E, 39F and 39G any activity that relates[^key-d0c83db7ae19f37aabcbd941a3d73d1f] to a regulated mortgage contract or a regulated home purchase plan to the extent that the activity constitutes an activity of the kind specified by a provision of Part 2 of this Order other than articles 39D, 39E, 39F and 39G, where entering into that contract as lender constitutes an activity of the kind specified by article 61 or entering into that plan as home purchase provider constitutes an activity of the kind under article 63F[^key-65ca4ac33cbd22fa8829526fa37c2155].
