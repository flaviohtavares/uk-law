# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 39B

## 39B. Claims management on behalf of an insurer etc.[^c-f392e4056396d0ebcee1a5fb33bd136d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person does not carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 39A if he acts in the course of carrying on the activity of—

- (a) expert appraisal;
- (b) loss adjusting on behalf of a relevant insurer; or
- (c) managing claims on behalf of a relevant insurer,

and that activity is carried on in the course of carrying on any profession or business.

(2) In this article—

- (a) “relevant insurer” means—
  - (i) a person who has Part IV permission to carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 10;
  - (ii) a person to whom the general prohibition does not apply by virtue of section 316(1)(a) of the Act (members of the Society of Lloyd's);
  - (iii) [^c-80c0f0f84a28dabaa793de954ad4d939]...;or
  - (iv) a relevant reinsurer;
- (b) “relevant reinsurer” means a person whose main business consists of accepting risks ceded by—
  - (i) a person falling within sub-paragraph (i), (ii) or (iii) of the definition of “relevant insurer”; [^M_F_ad247fde-40eb-4d10-c3c7-6385b999b2af]...
  - (ii)[^M_F_5f23c160-b27d-4bee-a9ba-9f0f6b85ff2d] [^c-ff77ef0cf7f8c7753ee8a1170d8fbcbf]...; or
  - (iii) a person established outside the United Kingdom [^c-973afb09304d86bd642afbba2de32869]... who carries on an activity of the kind specified by article 10 by way of business.
