# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 36

## 36. Other exclusions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-aa2fb022d86838cf11eb9eb872907559] Article 25 is also subject to the exclusions in articles 66 (trustees etc.), 67 (profession or non-investment business), 68 (sale of goods and supply of services), 69 (groups and joint enterprises), 70 (sale of body corporate), 71 (employee share schemes), 72 (overseas persons)[^key-0a4505aeca31d540237080458b156260],[^key-511c0c312891a882765a7ced1862049f][^key-951e472969db88e4d4baaecc4866d556]...72B (activities carried on by a provider of relevant goods or services), 72C (provision of information about contracts of insurance on an incidental basis), 72AA (managers of[^key-5d7f08f4de9945678c7c93be2e021a98] UK UCITS[^key-ed4a7d64073b72a85baebbb8650cd591] and AIFs), 72D (large risks contracts where risk situated[^key-1fba9f6adef954000d647323852beaf8] outside the United Kingdom[^key-606c54cb3b1a39078f58c4466fca6c50]), 72G (local authorities) and 72H (insolvency practitioners).

(2)[^key-7ba33da800466441ff40ba3a8f651de0] Articles 25A, 25B[^key-f5b774854dd23b5fc4ac2f0fbad35396], 25C and 25E[^key-b387aa773de45c2d2acc0adde8e22300] are also subject to the exclusions in articles 66 (trustees etc.), 67 (profession or non-investment business), 72 (overseas persons),[^key-69cc7764efe0d16a209e7036a1db14a8][^key-7e2765859ef3ed7f6932291860d63e69]... 72AA (managers of[^key-9c80469a0ae350f31c7ee3b66fc1ce2d] UK UCITS and AIFs) and 72G (local authorities).

(2A)[^key-ab94c761b9a35b713b2be8eedad4dc48] Article 25A is also subject to the exclusion in article 72I (registered consumer buy-to-let mortgage firms).

(3)[^key-c7e59f731832c955a345a7fee6b53b15] Article 25D is also subject to the exclusions in articles 72 (overseas persons)[^key-911bf4bd2bed25349d4531a976e02061], 72AA (managers of[^key-586c68b9e522c3079eb08419b2b6e62a] UK UCITS and AIFs) and 72H (insolvency practitioners).
