# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 63H

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63H. Administration pursuant to agreement with authorised person[^key-1fcfd710c51963e005973d70ac0f1ddc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is not an authorised person does not carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 63F(2) in relation to a regulated home purchase plan where he administers the plan pursuant to an agreement with an authorised person who has permission to carry on an activity of that kind.
