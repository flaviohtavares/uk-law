# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Introducing

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There are excluded from articles 25(2), 25A(2), 25B(2)[^c-8e25d18189432f14bb00652eb0350cb3] , 25C(2) and 25E(2)[^c-214780bcea9808f8557b38178f9f97d8] arrangements where—

- (a) they are arrangements under which persons (“clients") will be introduced to another person;
- (b) the person to whom introductions are to be made is—
  - (i) an authorised person;
  - (ii) an exempt person acting in the course of a business comprising a regulated activity in relation to which he is exempt; or
  - (iii) a person who is not unlawfully carrying on regulated activities in the United Kingdom and whose ordinary business involves him in engaging in an activity of the kind specified by any of articles 14, 21, 25, 25A,[^c-f776a8d00c4bd6239247ea83aeb16da8] 25B, 25C,[^c-996ad19d81cb4c804bc5bf1aaf24032b] 25E,[^c-63576168d8bffc372c1d8802ae60d5e0] 37 , 39A[^c-1c399d0a35a5a0baf6b314d2b62b1174] 40, 45, 51ZA, 51ZB, 51ZC, 51ZD, 51ZE[^c-c402f9e254226c9bb35d1cbde0e37b06], 52, 53[^c-fe3523cc8f9f09e1505e1e9986833d60], 53A, 53B[^c-e26b1fb8b6699f5cac3a23e8d4279085], 53C and 53D[^c-96fcf6b94558222509749d6738c33598] (or, so far as relevant to any of those articles, article 64), or would do so apart from any exclusion from any of those articles made by this Order; [^c-6293ab06637693e33e37c440189bf1a8]...
- (c) the introduction is made with a view to the provision of independent advice or the independent exercise of discretion in relation to investments generally or in relation to any class of investments to which the arrangements relate ; and[^c-20df05e862a014ff9fd8c7ce94e898c6]
- (d)[^c-26519890242a1af96177882371719c1a] The arrangements—
  - (i) are made with a view to a person entering into a transaction which does not relate to a contract of insurance, or
  - (ii) are of the type specified in article 33B (provision of information – contracts of insurance).
