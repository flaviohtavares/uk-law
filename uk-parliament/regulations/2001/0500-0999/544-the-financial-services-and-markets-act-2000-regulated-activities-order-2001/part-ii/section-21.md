## 21. Dealing in investments as agent

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-b1bb7dac027eb2479bf23403e828fbfb] Buying, selling, subscribing for or underwriting securities, structured deposits[^c-ceb01b80685670eccae8f420f209904e] or relevant investments[^c-5a588cf5a69f7e72effcbfcd682c4930] [^c-3e0341028347af5bd7224980d159e6d5]... as agent is a specified kind of activity.

(2)[^c-b92517e3dd2b1749daa036e4a69ca6e5] Paragraph (1) does not apply to a kind of activity to which article 25D or 25DA[^c-534e41d96f42c77e73db4955e800a3dd] applies.
