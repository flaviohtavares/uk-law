# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 9AZA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544

## 9AZA. Sums in exchange for qualifying stablecoin[^key-429cee7ec41201ea10f5d3f91851a5e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A sum is not a deposit for the purposes of article 5 (accepting deposits) if it is immediately exchanged for qualifying stablecoin.
