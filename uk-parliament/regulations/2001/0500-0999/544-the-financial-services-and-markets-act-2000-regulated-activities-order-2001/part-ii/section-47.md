# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Instructions on behalf of settlement banks

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There is excluded from article 45 the act of sending, or causing to be sent, a dematerialised instruction where the person on whose behalf the instruction is sent or caused to be sent is a settlement bank in its capacity as such.
