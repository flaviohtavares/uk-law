# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 9D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544

## 9D. Applications for certificates[^key-553740a5a5511f8a0755c9ba7dd31279]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of the Act apply to applications to the[^key-d5eaa0a06ade74836fe8f71b93bd48d5] FCA for certificates under 9C (and the determination of such applications) as they apply to applications for Part IV permissions (and the determination of such applications)—

- (a)[^key-297b10e3db270abe559538afd91e1c17] section 55U(1)(b) and (4) to (8);
- (b) section 55V;
- (c) section 55X(2) and (4)(f); and
- (d) section 55Z3(1).
