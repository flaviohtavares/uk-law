# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Administration pursuant to agreement with authorised person[^key-6661d9526e85421527acff4a93e700c4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is not an authorised person does not carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 61(2) in relation to a regulated mortgage contract where he administers the contract pursuant to an agreement with an authorised person who has permission to carry on an activity of that kind.
