# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 54B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54B. Advice given by reason of providing pensions guidance ...[^key-3a6032b4186c31a2f1bac41df47e9252]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person does not carry on an activity of the kind specified in article 53, 53A, 53B, 53C , 53D or 55A[^key-4684eb75eee66386ce097446ca6f7e73] by reason of providing pensions guidance [^key-16299fba5f79043d5da131009cf0873b]....

(2)[^key-7bef8767e84a39a927d3ff14d8084f03] For the purposes of paragraph (1), pensions guidance means—

- (a) information or guidance provided by the Money and Pensions Service in carrying out its pensions guidance function (see section 3 of the Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018);
- (b) information or guidance provided by another person with whom arrangements under section 5(1), (2) or (3) of the Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018 are made to carry out the Money and Pensions Service’s pensions guidance function.
