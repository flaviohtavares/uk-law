# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 63Z

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63Z. Private use[^key-d686ca47d70e55ec032b2f7748406496]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person (“**P**”) does not carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 63U by providing an ESG rating which—

- (a) is provided pursuant to a contract with another person (“**Q**”),
- (b) relates solely to Q, and
- (c) P does not reasonably expect to be made available to another person outside Q’s group.
