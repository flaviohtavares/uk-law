# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 72AA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72AA. Managers of UK UCITS and AIFs[^c-76bfdd3aa1c9aacb34b9c166e3b27d9d][^c-b7845b672dec15416538afac147805be][^c-198b6f5531e35bbdd3426ec8f2a1eefe][^c-f5af79b33d192d2f48921c3c787845f3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies to a person with a Part 4A permission to carry on the activity of the kind specified by article 51ZA or 51ZC.

(2) Activities carried on by the person in connection with or for the purposes of managing a UK UCITS[^c-a13354c569f1bb070821f1ea3f4727dd] or, as the case may be, managing an AIF, are excluded from the activities specified by this Part, other than the activities mentioned in paragraph (1) or the activity of the kind specified by article 55A[^c-aaf0027229bb1bc46fcc83e734d7134a].
