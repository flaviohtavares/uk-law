# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, section 94

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/544
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Members of designated professional bodies[^c-7c6f0811a7b2314b4deb4bcf2a26a35c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A designated professional body must, by notice in writing, inform the[^c-fefe69a876da09d46d097136c27507ba] FCA of—

- (a) the name,
- (b) the address, and
- (c) in the case of a relevant member which is not an individual, the name of the individuals who are responsible for the management of the business carried on by the member, so far as it relates to insurance distribution[^c-f2e65717677bee0b5476f0772d4e283d] activities,

of any relevant member who falls within paragraph (2).

(2) A relevant member of a designated professional body falls within this paragraph if, in accordance with the rules of that body, he carries on, or proposes to carry on any insurance distribution activity but does not have, and does not propose to apply for, Part IV permission on the basis that the general prohibition does not (or will not) apply to the carrying on of that activity by virtue of section 327 of the Act.

(3) A designated professional body must also, by notice in writing, inform the[^c-1a2e1794f1d526b88bfb3ffdc9c54a9a] FCA of any change in relation to the matters specified in sub-paragraphs (a) to (c) of paragraph (1).

(4) A designated professional body must inform the FCA when a relevant member to whom paragraph (2) applies ceases, for whatever reason, to carry on insurance distribution activities.

(5) The FCA may give directions to a designated professional body as to the manner in which the information referred to in paragraphs (1), (3) and (4) must be provided.
