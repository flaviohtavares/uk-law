# Public Order, Northern Ireland The Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Accounts and Audit) Order 1998

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 851

NORTHERN IRELAND

Public Order, Northern Ireland The Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Accounts and Audit) Order 1998

Made

29th December 1998

Laid before Parliament

9th March 2001

Coming into force

29th December 1998

The Secretary of State, in pursuance of section 16(1) and Schedule 1 paragraph 12(6) of the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Accounts and Audit) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 29th December 1998.

**2.** In paragraph 12(5) of Schedule 1 to the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 for the words “31 December” where they appear in both places substitute the words “31 March”.

Northern Ireland Office

Marjorie Mowlam

Minister of State Northern Ireland Office

29th December 1998
