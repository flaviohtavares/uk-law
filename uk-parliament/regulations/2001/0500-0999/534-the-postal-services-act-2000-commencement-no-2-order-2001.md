# The Postal Services Act 2000 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 534 (C. 22)

POSTAL SERVICES

The Postal Services Act 2000 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2001

Made

22nd February 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 130(1) of the Postal Services Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Postal Services Act 2000 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2001.

## 2. Commencement

The provisions of the Postal Services Act 2000 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force insofar as is specified in that Schedule on 26th February 2001.

Alan Johnson,

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Competitiveness,

Department of Trade and Industry

22nd February 2001

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS COMING INTO FORCE ON 26TH FEBRUARY 2001

Article 2

| Provision | Subject matter of provision |
| --- | --- |
| Section 89, except subsection (7) | Schemes as to terms and conditions for provision of a universal postal service |
| Sections 91 and 92 insofar as they relate to section 89(1) to (6) | Limited liability for registered inland packets |
| Section 105(2) | Application of customs and excise enactments to certain postal packets |
| Section 112, except subsections (1) and (8) | Schemes in relation to postal and money orders |
| Section 114, subsections (1) and (2) (insofar as they relate to section 112(2) to (7)) and (3) | Special arrangements with other countries or territories |

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on 26th February 2001, the provisions of the Postal Services Act 2000 specified in the Schedule to the Order, insofar as is specified in the Schedule.
