# The Biocidal Products Regulations 2001, SCHEDULE 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 2 — REGULATIONS RELATING TO BIOCIDAL PRODUCTS

Regulation 3(1)

The Regulations referred to in regulation 3(1)(a) are—

- (a) the Materials and Articles in Contact with Food Regulations 1987[^f00019];
- (b) the Flavourings in Food Regulations 1992[^f00020];
- (c) the Food Additive Labelling Regulations 1992[^f00021];
- (d) the Active Implantable Medical Devices Regulations 1992[^f00022];
- (e) the Egg Products Regulations 1993[^f00023];
- (f) the Medicines (Homoeopathic Medicinal Products For Human Use) Regulations 1994[^f00024];
- (g) the Medical Devices Regulations 1994[^f00025];
- (h) the Marketing Authorisations for Veterinary Medicinal Products Regulations 1994[^f00026];
- (i) the Medicines for Human Use (Marketing Authorisations Etc.) Regulations 1994[^f00027];
- (j) the Plant Protection Products Regulations 1995[^f00028];
- (k) the Dairy Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1995[^f00029];
- (l) the Dairy Products (Hygiene) (Scotland) Regulations 1995[^f00030];
- (m) the Miscellaneous Food Additives Regulations 1995[^f00031];
- (n) the Cosmetic Products (Safety) Regulations 1996[^f00032];
- (o) the Registration of Homoeopathic Veterinary Medicinal Products Regulations 1997[^f00033];
- (p) the Food Safety (Fishery Products and Live Shellfish) (Hygiene) Regulations 1998[^f00034];
- (q) the Medicated Feedingstuffs Regulations 1998[^f00035];
- (r) the Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999[^f00036];
- (s) the Feeding Stuffs Regulations 2000[^f00037];
- (t) the Feeding Stuffs (Scotland) Regulations 2000[^f00038];
- (u) the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2001[^f00039];
