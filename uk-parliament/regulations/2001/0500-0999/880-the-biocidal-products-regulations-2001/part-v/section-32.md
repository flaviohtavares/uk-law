# The Biocidal Products Regulations 2001, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Samples, models and drafts

When required to do so by the Ministers, a person who has submitted an application under regulations 9 to 15 or 17 or a person who places, or has placed, a biocidal product on the market shall provide them with—

- (a) a sample or model of the packaging of, or a sample or draft of the labelling of, the biocidal product in question;
- (b) a sample or draft of any accompanying leaflet integral to the packaging of the biocidal product in question.
