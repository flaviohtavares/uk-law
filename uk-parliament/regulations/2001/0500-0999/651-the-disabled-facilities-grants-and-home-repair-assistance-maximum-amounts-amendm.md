# The Disabled Facilities Grants and Home Repair Assistance (Maximum Amounts) (Amendment) (England) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 651

HOUSING, ENGLAND

The Disabled Facilities Grants and Home Repair Assistance (Maximum Amounts) (Amendment) (England) Order 2001

Made

2nd March 2001

Laid before Parliament

9th March 2001

Coming into force

2nd April 2001

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 76(2) and 146 of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996[^f00001] and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Disabled Facilities Grants and Home Repair Assistance (Maximum Amounts) (Amendment) (England) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 2nd April 2001.

(2) This Order extends to England only.

## 2. Amendment of Order

The Disabled Facilities Grants and Home Repair Assistance (Maximum Amounts) Order 1996[^f00002] is amended in accordance with the following paragraphs.

## 3. Home repair assistance—maximum amount for one application

In Article 4—

- (a) delete “Subject to article 5”; and
- (b) in paragraphs (a) and (b)(ii), for “£2,000” substitute “£5,000”.

## 4. Home Repair Assistance: maximum amount in any three year period

Article 5 is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Nick Raynsford

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

2nd March 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Disabled Facilities Grants and Home Repair Assistance (Maximum Amounts) Order 1996 (“the Order”) which, among other things, prescribes the maximum amount of home repair assistance which a local housing authority may give under Chapter III of Part I of the Housing Grants, Construction And Regeneration Act 1996.

Article 4 of the Order, which prescribes the maximum amount of home repair assistance for one application, is amended to increase that maximum from £2,000 to £5,000.

Article 5 of the Order, which prescribes a maximum amount of home repair assistance payable in respect of the same dwelling, house-boat or mobile home in any period of three years, is revoked.
