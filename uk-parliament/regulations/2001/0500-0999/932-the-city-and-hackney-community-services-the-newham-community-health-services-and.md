# The City and Hackney Community Services, the Newham Community Health Services and the Tower Hamlets Healthcare National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 932

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The City and Hackney Community Services, the Newham Community Health Services and the Tower Hamlets Healthcare National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2001

Made

25th February2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the City and Hackney Community Services, the Newham Community Health Services and the Tower Hamlets Healthcare National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trusts

(1) The City and Hackney Community Services National Health Service Trust established by the City and Hackney Community Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1995[^f00005] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

(2) The Newham Community Health Services National Health Service Trust established by the Newham Community Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1995[^f00006] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

(3) The Tower Hamlets Healthcare National Health Service Trust established by the Tower Hamlets Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1995[^f00007] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Denham

Minister of State,

Department of Health

25th February 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves on 1st April 2001—

- the City and Hackney Community Services National Health Service Trust;
- the Newham Community Health Services National Health Service Trust; and
- the Tower Hamlets Healthcare National Health Service Trust.
