# The Wigan and Leigh Health Services and the Wrightington Hospital National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 719

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Wigan and Leigh Health Services and the Wrightington Hospital National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2001

Made

14th February 2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Wigan and Leigh Health Services and the Wrightington Hospital National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trusts

(1) The Wigan and Leigh Health Services National Health Service Trust established by the Wigan and Leigh Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00005] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

(2) The Wrightington Hospital National Health Service Trust established by the Wrightington Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00006] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hunt

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

14th February 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves on 1st April 2001 the Wigan and Leigh Health Services National Health Service Trust and the Wrightington Hospital National Health Service Trust.

It should be read in conjunction with the Wrightington, Wigan and Leigh National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2001[^f00007] which establishes a new NHS trust.
