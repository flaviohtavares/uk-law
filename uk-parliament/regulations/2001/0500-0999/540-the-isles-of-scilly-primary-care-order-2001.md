# The Isles of Scilly (Primary Care) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 540

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Isles of Scilly (Primary Care) Order 2001

Made

17th February 2001

Coming into force

2nd March 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 41(8) of the National Health Service (Primary Care) Act 1997[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Isles of Scilly (Primary Care) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 2nd March 2001.

## 2. Application of the National Health Service (Primary Care) Act 1997 to the Isles of Scilly

The National Health Service (Primary Care) Act 1997 shall extend to the Isles of Scilly.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Denham

Minister of State,

Department of Health

17th February 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the provisions of the National Health Service (Primary Care) Act 1997 to the Isles of Scilly as from 2nd March 2001.
