# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Export of Vehicles) (Disinfection of Tyres) (Amendment) (No. 5) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2195

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Export of Vehicles) (Disinfection of Tyres) (Amendment) (No. 5) Regulations 2001

Made

15th June 2001

Laid before Parliament

15th June 2001

Coming into force

16th June 2001

The Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by that section, and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Title, extent and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Export of Vehicles) (Disinfection of Tyres) (Amendment) (No. 5) Regulations 2001; they extend to the United Kingdom and shall come into force on 16th June 2001.

## 2. Amendment to the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Export of Vehicles) (Disinfection of Tyres) Regulations 2001

(1) The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Export of Vehicles) (Disinfection of Tyres) Regulations 2001[^f00003] are amended in accordance with this regulation.

(2) In regulation 1(2) of those Regulations, for “19th June 2001” substitute “20th July 2001”.

(3) In regulation 2(1) of those Regulations, for the definition of “the Decision” substitute—

> - “the Decision” means Commission Decision [2001/356/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0356) concerning certain protection measures with regard to foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom and repealing Decision [2001/172/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0172)[^f00004], as last amended by Commission Decision [2001/430/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0430)[^f00005] and Commission Decision [2001/437/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0437)[^f00006];

.

Whitty,

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

15th June 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations implement Commission Decision [2001/430/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0430) amending for the third time Commission Decision [2001/356/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0356) concerning certain protection measures with regard to foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom (OJ No. L153, 8.6.2001, p. 33) in so far as it applies to the disinfection of the tyres of road vehicles transported from the British Islands.

The effect of the Regulations is to extend the time that the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Export of Vehicles) (Disinfection of Tyres) Regulations 2001 (S.I. [2001/887](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/887), as amended) are in force until midnight on 20th July 2001.
