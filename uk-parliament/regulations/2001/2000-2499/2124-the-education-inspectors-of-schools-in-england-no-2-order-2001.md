# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No.2) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2124

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No.2) Order 2001

Made

9th June 2001

Coming into force

11th June 2001

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 9th of June 2001

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 2) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 11th June 2001.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 11th June 2001.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council
