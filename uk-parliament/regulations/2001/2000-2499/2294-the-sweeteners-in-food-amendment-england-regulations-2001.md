# The Sweeteners in Food (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2294

FOOD, ENGLAND

The Sweeteners in Food (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2001

Made

21st June 2001

Laid before Parliament

29th June 2001

Coming into force

27th July 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16(1)(a) and (e), 17(1), 26(1) and (3) and 48(1) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to, the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard, in accordance with section 48(4A) of that Act, to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency, and after consultation in accordance with section 48(4) and (4B) of that Act, makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Title, commencement, extent and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sweeteners in Food (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2001, shall come into force on 27th July 2001 and shall extend to England only.

(2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995

The principal Regulations shall be amended, insofar as they extend to England, in accordance with regulation 3.

**3.** In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation), in the definition of “Directive [95/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0031)” there shall be added at the end the words “and by Directive [2000/51/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0051) insofar as it relates to E965(ii) syrup maltitol”[^f00003].

## 4. Consequential amendments

(1) Insofar as any provision specified in paragraph (2) of regulation 3 (consequential provision) of the Sweeteners in Food (Amendment) Regulations 1999[^f00004] extends to England, paragraph (1) of that regulation shall cease to have effect in relation to such provision.

(2) In the provisions specified in paragraph (3), insofar as they extend to England, references to the principal Regulations shall be construed as references to those Regulations as amended up to and including the amendment effected by these Regulations.

(3) The provisions are—

- (a) the definition of “permitted sweetener” in paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Jam and Similar Products Regulations 1981[^f00005];
- (b) the definition of “additive” in paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Meat Products and Spreadable Fish Products Regulations 1984[^f00006];
- (c) the definition of “sweetener” in Part II of Schedule 1 (categories of food additives) to the Food Additives Labelling Regulations 1992[^f00007];
- (d) the definition of “sweetener” in paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Miscellaneous Food Additives Regulations 1995[^f00008]; and
- (e) in the Food Labelling Regulations 1996[^f00009]—
  - (i) paragraph (1) of regulation 34 (foods containing sweeteners, added sugar and sweeteners, aspartame or polyols); and
  - (ii) in Schedule 8 (misleading descriptions) Part I (general), the condition in column 2 opposite the description “ice cream” in column 1.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Yvette Cooper

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Public Health,

Department of Health

21st June 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which extend to England only, further amend the Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995, which extend to Great Britain, by bringing up to date a reference to Directive [95/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0031) on specific purity criteria so as to cover its amendment by Directive [2000/51/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0051) insofar as that Directive substituted new specifications for maltitol syrup and by bringing up to date references to the 1995 Regulations in other Regulations.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
