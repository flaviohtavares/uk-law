# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2001, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Validation of Licences

The Governor may issue a certificate of validation rendering valid for the purpose of this Order any flight crew licence granted under the law of any country other than the Territory. A certificate of validation may be issued subject to such conditions and for such periods as the Governor thinks fit.
