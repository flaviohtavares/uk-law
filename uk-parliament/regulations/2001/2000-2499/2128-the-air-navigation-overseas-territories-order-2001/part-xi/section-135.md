# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2001, section 135

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 135. Publication of requirements

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), where any provision of this Order provides for the Governor to grant a certificate, licence or other document if he is satisfied as to matters specified in the provision, he shall publish the requirements which must be met by an applicant for such a certificate, licence or other document in order to so satisfy him.

(2) Nothing in this article shall require the Governor to publish requirements in respect of a licence, certificate or other document if he does not exercise his powers to grant the licence, certificate or document in question.
