# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2001, section 134

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 134. Approval for persons to furnish reports

In relation to any of his functions pursuant to any of the provisions of this Order the Governor may, either absolutely or subject to such conditions as he thinks fit, approve a person as qualified to furnish reports to him and may accept such reports.
