# The International Criminal Court Act 2001 (Commencement) (Amendment) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2001 No. 2161 (C.69)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/2161) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2001 No. 2304 (C.77)

INTERNATIONAL CRIMINAL COURT

The International Criminal Court Act 2001 (Commencement) (Amendment) Order 2001

Made

25th June 2001

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 82 of the International Criminal Court Act 2001,[^f00001] I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the International Criminal Court Act 2001 (Commencement) (Amendment) Order 2001.

**2.** Article 3 of the International Criminal Court Act 2001 (Commencement) Order 2001[^f00002] shall be amended by adding at the end the following:

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Schedule 1, paragraph 1 | conferring legal capacity, privileges and immunities on the ICC |

Jack Straw

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State, Foreign and Commonwealth Office

25th June 2001

(This note is not a part of the Order)

This Order amends the International Criminal Court Act 2001 (Commencement) Order 2001 ([S.I. 2001/2161 (C.69)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/2161)) to bring into force forthwith the power in Schedule 1 of the 2001 Act to make an Order in Council to confer legal capacity, privileges and immunities on the International Criminal Court.
