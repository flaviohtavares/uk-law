# The Education (Designated Institutions) (No. 2) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2268

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Designated Institutions) (No. 2) Order 2001

Made

21st June 2001

Laid before Parliament

22nd June 2001

Coming into force

1st August 2001

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 129(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Designated Institutions) (No. 2) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st August 2001.

**2.** The institution which is specified in the Schedule to this Order, being an institution which appears to the Secretary of State to fall within subsection (2) of section 129 of the Education Reform Act 1988, is hereby designated as an institution eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Higher Education Funding Council for England.

Margaret Hodge

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

21st June 2001

## SCHEDULE

Article 2.

The Conservatoire for Dance and Drama

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates as from 1st August 2001 the Conservatoire for Dance and Drama as an institution eligible to receive funds from the Higher Education Funding Council for England.
