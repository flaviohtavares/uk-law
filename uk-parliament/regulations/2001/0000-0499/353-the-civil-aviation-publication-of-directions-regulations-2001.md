# The Civil Aviation (Publication of Directions) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 353

CIVIL AVIATION

The Civil Aviation (Publication of Directions) Regulations 2001

Made

10th February 2001

Laid before Parliament

13th February 2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions in exercise of the powers conferred by section 69(3)(a) of the Transport Act 2000[^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited at the Civil Aviation (Publication of Directions) Regulations 2001, and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

## 2. Publication of Directions

The manner of publication of the directions referred to in section 69(3)(a) of the Transport Act 2000 shall be by publishing them in the Official Record of the Civil Aviation Authority.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Bob Ainsworth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

10th February 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the manner of publication of directions given to the Civil Aviation Authority by the Secretary of State under section 66(1) of the Transport Act 2000 in relation to air navigation in managed areas. They are to be published in the Civil Aviation Authority’s Official Record which can be obtained or inspected at the offices of the Secretary & Legal Adviser, Civil Aviation Authority, CAA House, 45-59 Kingsway, London WC2B 6TE.
