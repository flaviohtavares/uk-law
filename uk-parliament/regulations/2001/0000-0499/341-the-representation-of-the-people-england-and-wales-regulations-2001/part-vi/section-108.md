## 108. Supply of full register etc to certain candidates and restrictions on use[^key-e7e11c4a1689c6ea930c08863d88fdb7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies to a candidate at—

- (a) a Parliamentary [^key-1820c18cfefe35f42e5abc54116b2a53]... election,
- (b) an election to Senedd Cymru[^key-bec6a49e39a3e0f67951d052c0f63078],
- (c) a local government election, and
- (d) an election of a mayor under Part II of the Local Government Act 2000,

where any part of the area in respect of which the candidate stands for election includes the whole or part of a registration area.

(2)[^key-f7bb54329adfdb791905aafa0ef5837f] In this regulation “**candidate**” includes a candidate at an election of a mayor under Part II of the Local Government Act 2000.

(3) In the case of a registered political party which submits a list of candidates at [^key-df9a040983c99ec2f245d622bdbb8190]... an election of the London members of the London Assembly or an election of Members of the Senedd[^key-31d8892f6728fc06aa7aa0f7ee0fbc35], the entitlement otherwise conferred by this regulation on a candidate is conferred on the election agent of that party.

(4) For the purposes of regulation 102(1) above, the relevant part of the documents listed in that provision is so much of them as relate to the area for which the candidate is standing.

(5) No candidate or election agent to whom a copy of the register has been supplied by virtue of this regulation may—

- (a) supply a copy of the full register to any person,
- (b) disclose any information contained in it (that is not contained in the edited register), or
- (c) make use of any such information,

other than for electoral purposes.
