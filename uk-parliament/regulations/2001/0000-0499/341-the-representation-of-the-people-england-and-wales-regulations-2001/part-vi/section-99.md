# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001, section 99

## 99. Supply of free copy of full register etc to Statistics Board and restrictions on use[^c-e7e11c4a1689c6ea930c08863d88fdb7][^M_F_44249a24-ef22-4cbe-d53e-1dfe78054ada]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Each registration officer shall supply, free of charge and on publication, one copy of—

- (a) any revised version of the register published under section 13(1) or (3) of the 1983 Act,
- (b) any notice setting out an alteration to the register published under section 13A(2) ,13AB(2)[^c-302b0a19eff2f48ff3dbee2117222da0] or 13B(3) , (3B) or (3D)[^c-7171d9c5a8519bdc30ef205e07ead17e] of that Act, and
- (c) any list of overseas electors,

to the Statistics Board[^M_F_0ea965b3-2780-4bc8-c94e-05cae95b5d90] (in this regulation referred to as “the Board”).

(2) In paragraph (1) the duty to supply is a duty to supply in data form unless, prior to publication, the Board[^M_F_95eac9a0-274c-48a1-f76e-b075c56d081d] has requested in writing a printed copy instead.

(3) Subject to paragraph (6)[^c-e0d74b8ca8bfa16e1ce3f7f34917ced9] no person employed by the Board[^M_F_ce94e37d-3257-4c73-d7ed-7f32cc09f635] may—

- (a) supply a copy of the full register other than to another such person;
- (b) disclose any information contained in it (that is not contained in the edited register) otherwise than in accordance with paragraph (4) below; or
- (c) make use of any such information other than for statistical purposes.

(4) Subject to paragraph (6)[^c-33021f1c68a195563ee54d3b608112e6] no information which is contained in the full register and not in the edited register may be disclosed otherwise than—

- (a) by allowing a person using the premises of the Board[^M_F_55da2cf1-a132-402a-866d-6f46f2230830] to inspect it under supervision, and
- (b) by publishing information about electors which does not include the name or address of any elector.

(4A)[^c-aaf755affed82149112e89199939e597] Where a copy of the full register is made available by providing the register on a computer screen or otherwise in data form, the Board[^M_F_dba7fbd5-589e-4e28-8c5c-92ecc84a98b3] shall ensure that the manner in, and equipment on, which that copy is provided do not permit any person consulting that copy to—

- (a) search it by electronic means by reference to the name of any person; or
- (b) copy or transmit any part of that copy by electronic means.

(5) A person who inspects the full register , whether a printed copy or in data form,[^c-badf600348e8f9f373925b9390e78150] may not—

- (a) make copies of any part of it, or
- (b) record any particulars included in it,

otherwise than by means of hand-written notes.

(6)[^c-d6ac182a9e8295b1cd73c5ffe92aa933] A person employed by the Board[^M_F_d4708a33-5ecc-48be-b766-79abf047d917] is not prohibited from supplying a copy of, or disclosing information contained in, a version of the full register where—

- (a) more than ten years have expired since that version of the register was first published in accordance with regulation 43; and
- (b) the supply or disclosure is for Article 84A[^c-78444637835e253fced465c5fdbe2c2e][^c-6b84331cf0dc1987fa8a688c428c3baa] GDPR purposes in accordance with the relevant requirement.

(7) No person who obtains a copy of the full register or to whom information contained in it that is not contained in the edited register is disclosed under the circumstances described in paragraph (6) may—

- (a) supply a copy of it,
- (b) disclose any such information, or
- (c) make use of any such information,

otherwise than for Article 84A[^c-8efc767522fe140c5efa240a6db506aa] GDPR purposes in accordance with the relevant requirement.
