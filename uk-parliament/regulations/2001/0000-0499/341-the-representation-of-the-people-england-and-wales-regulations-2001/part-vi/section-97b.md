# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001, section 97B

## 97B. Supply of free copy of full register to the National Library of Scotland[^key-39950fd5d322e552e917290c83aef3b2][^key-e7e11c4a1689c6ea930c08863d88fdb7]

*Extent: E+W*

Each registration officer in England and Wales shall supply, free of charge and on publication—

- (a) one data copy of any revised version of the register published under section 13(1) or (3) of the 1983 Act; and
- (b) one printed copy of any list of overseas electors,

to the National Library of Scotland“.
