## 10. Return and declaration of election expenses[^key-1dce5d15c8e17bb9ac55cd285c3a7de2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purpose of section 75(3) of the 1983 Act, the form of the return of election expenses shall be in Form C and the form of the declaration as to election expenses shall be in Form D.

(2)[^key-51cf6c92a090c7e16934f0c5ee47137f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The price of a copy of any such return, declaration or any accompanying document[^key-f24c80af51a180698d0fd5611b1b79ba] shall be at the rate of 20p for each side of each page.
