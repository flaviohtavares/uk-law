# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (44).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/341
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Forms[^key-005378c3079ad5114d6bd3269ff56255][^key-acadbdc89ea9ad2ec6b2d84d85d05b74][^key-273a0f2a99f34de534e5b4c21749094b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The registration officer shall supply free of charge as many forms for use in connection with—

- (a) applications made under sections 10ZC(1)(a) and 10ZD(1)(a) of the[^key-9d20ab4c0b6605f952aec4ec81e58802] 1983 Act, and
- (b)[^key-284700da1eb9aab29556356c14fc4f9c][^key-410d5dfe3007866a242ded4a23053464][^key-e061851d581bb9c056970f3660b7643d] applications made under Schedule 4 to the 2000 Act and Part IV of these Regulations,

as appear to that officer reasonable in the circumstances to any person who satisfies that officer of his intention to use the forms in connection with an election.

(2) The forms set out in Schedule 3 to these Regulations or forms substantially to the like effect may be used with such variations as the circumstances may require.
