# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001, section 28

## 28. Inspection of applications and objections[^c-ecf32c4f94ddaf3433f2a6d1b8525737]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-a643fffaf0fdcb0b2a998a38278ece9c] An entry on the list of applications for registration kept under regulation 29(2)(a)[^c-49ee65c3b5a62b7eaf7b56b3d1f524aa] and any objection to a person’s registration shall be made available for inspection at the registration officer’s office until the application to which the entry relates[^c-91c72ed9df0e63cf94d2581096517c74] or objection has been determined by the registration officer.

(2)[^c-ab8293d17cb52e6148598fdb0db7df9c] This regulation does not apply to an application for registration which is accompanied by an application for an anonymous entry.

(3)[^c-bef7d623ed1369583cc40b82cbc2e514] In relation to the registration of local government electors in Wales, this regulation does not apply to an application made by a person under the age of 16.
