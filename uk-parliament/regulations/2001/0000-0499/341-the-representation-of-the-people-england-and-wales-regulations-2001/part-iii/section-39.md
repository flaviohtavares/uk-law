## 39. Different letter for each parliamentary polling district[^c-ea99413df59fc93d398e7f85d5f7d37c]

*Extent: E+W*

There shall be a different letter or letters in the register for each parliamentary polling district and such letter or letters shall be deemed to form part of an elector’s number in the register.
