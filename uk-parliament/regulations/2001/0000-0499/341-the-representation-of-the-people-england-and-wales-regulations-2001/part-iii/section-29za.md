## 29ZA. Verification of information provided in an application[^key-361820ec4fc1a755887e2bbeb900e8d4][^key-e07fabf9a90f96a331fd2ca35538c325]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) On receipt of an application under section 10ZC or 10ZD of the 1983 Act made otherwise than through the digital service[^key-9908d907f1184db85ee445c64dcb0270], a registration officer must disclose the name or names, date of birth and national insurance number given under regulation 26 or 26A[^key-1820db27165599f55f00bad1bc27791e] to the Secretary of State for[^key-90773f620ce7db2cc45328c7b580f1e5] Housing, Communities and Local Government[^key-dc75176b2cf4558aac57d65c32dd013d] in such a format and through such a conduit system as the Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government may have notified to the registration officer in writing.

(2) Following receipt of the information from the registration officer or (in the case of an application made through the digital service) from the applicant, the Secretary of State for[^key-778150687944a21c6817e0568722796d] Housing, Communities and Local Government[^key-5683671110515fc3361076bc7b241c44] may disclose the information to the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions[^key-a3ddd90cf9a5ec839301a31a95de926c].

(3) Where information has been disclosed to the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions[^key-bc5585db76ffdf152db09bb1d0eba492] under paragraph (2), the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions may compare it against—

- (a) the name, date of birth and national insurance number of individuals appearing in the following types of databases kept by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions—
  - (i) databases kept for the purposes of functions relating to social security (including such information kept on behalf of the Department for Social Development); and
  - (ii) databases relating to working tax credit, child tax credit and child benefit (being information kept on behalf of Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs); and
- (b) any other information contained in those databases which relates to the information disclosed under paragraph (2).

(4) The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions[^key-7e248eeca11507e35b3b88756bacda08] may disclose the results of the comparison to the Secretary of State for[^key-e1107a66ab0baa08b5f1eeb4f19da4af] Housing, Communities and Local Government[^key-39df76fb076253a9529ff4b363e8891b].

(5) On receipt of such results, the Secretary of State for[^key-8dab864ec8c1c21cebecd6bc0ee7ac21] Housing, Communities and Local Government[^key-dc0ba6625e0873f210076a2fbe58dc98] may disclose them to the registration officer in whose register the applicant has applied to be registered.

(6) Where the Secretary of State for[^key-28c3ca3621ef0f92791e1bcbbee718ea] Housing, Communities and Local Government[^key-6b8b74e59b408567fa7e3c8a52a5cba3] does so, the registration officer must take the results into account in determining the application.

(6ZA)[^key-9d7a89beb9515f08598d5f0bc751ee55] In relation to the registration of parliamentary electors, this regulation does not apply to an application for registration under section 10ZC of the 1983 Act made pursuant to an overseas elector’s declaration.

(6A)[^key-f57e8b0f9de3fd93a7359c6df3071158] In relation to the registration of local government electors in Wales, this regulation does not apply to an application made by a person under the age of 16.

(7) In this regulation—

- (a) “conduit system” has the same meaning as in paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Telecommunications Act 1984;
- (b)[^key-d57623dd930615c8e42b620c75d306bb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
