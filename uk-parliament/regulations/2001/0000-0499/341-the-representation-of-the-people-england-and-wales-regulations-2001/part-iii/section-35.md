# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001, section 35

## 35. Registration officer’s right to inspect certain records[^key-bffa565eec70518a6f48b306ac49537a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A registration officer is authorised to inspect, for the purpose of his registration duties, records kept (in whatever form) by—

- (a) an authority listed in paragraph (2) below, or
- (b) any person providing services to, or authorised to exercise any function of, any such authority.

(2) Those authorities are—

- (a) the council by which he was appointed;
- (aa)[^key-7f4d27d86ca5846a42eef2838f4a7a95] where the council by which he was appointed is a council for a district in a county for which there is a county council, that county council; and
- (b) any superintendent registrar of births, deaths and marriages, registrar of births and deaths or registrar of marriages[^key-286851561bc3e9079bd5888809c729b7].

(3) A registration officer is authorised to make copies of information contained in such records.

(4)[^key-fbec0dbfcbee0549956285ee8a17c109] For the purposes of paragraph (1), a registration officer’s “registration duties” do not include the officer’s functions in relation to a relevant absent voting application.
