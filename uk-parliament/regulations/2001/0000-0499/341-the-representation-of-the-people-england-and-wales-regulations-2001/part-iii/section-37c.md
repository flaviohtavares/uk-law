## 37C (Extent: W). Notice of Registration (Extent: W)[^c-c2a592b32f10355744a9d48a04f101fa]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) A notice given under section 9ZA(3) of the 1983 Act must include— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) a statement that any information collected or provided for the purpose of the duty under section 9ZA of the 1983 Act will be processed in accordance with the data protection legislation; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) where the person is under the age of 16, an explanation of how the person’s information will be held and used; *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) a statement that, if the person is not entitled to be registered, that person must, before the end of the notice period, inform the registration officer of that fact; *(Extent: W)*
- (d (Extent: W)) a request that any other person who receives the notice who is resident at the address to which the notice is addressed inform the registration officer if the person to whom the notice is addressed is not resident at that address; *(Extent: W)*
- (e (Extent: W)) the date of the last day of the notice period; *(Extent: W)*
- (f (Extent: W)) details of how to apply for inclusion in the Parliamentary register of electors for a constituency in Wales, and *(Extent: W)*
- (g (Extent: W)) the registration officer’s contact details. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) Where the person to be registered resides at a property to which regulation 32ZBF(2) applies, the registration officer may give a copy of the notice of registration to the responsible person in respect of the property. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) A registration officer may, during the notice period, send a reminder to the person to whom the notice period applies, or the responsible person to whom a copy of the notice was given, reminding them of any or all of the matters mentioned in section 9ZA(3) of the 1983 Act and paragraph (1) of this regulation, as the registration officer considers appropriate. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) For the purposes of this regulation “ *(Extent: W)***responsible person *(Extent: W)***” has the same meaning as in regulation 32ZBF(8). *(Extent: W)*

(5 (Extent: W)) A notice of registration and any reminders must be made in writing, which must be given by sending the notice to the person’s address. *(Extent: W)*
