## 37B (Extent: W). Processing of information provided or accessed in connection with registration under section 9ZA of the 1983 Act (Extent: W)[^c-c2a592b32f10355744a9d48a04f101fa]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) If a person is required to provide an original document under regulation 37A the registration officer must make a copy of that document and return the original document to the person who provided it. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) Information disclosed under regulation 37A may not be disclosed to any other person, except— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) for the purpose of determining the person’s entitlement to be registered under section 9ZA of the 1983 Act; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) for the purpose of any civil or criminal proceedings. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) A person who discloses information in breach of paragraph 2 is guilty of an offence and liable— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year, or a fine, or to both; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates court, or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or to both. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) In this regulation “ *(Extent: W)***copy *(Extent: W)***” includes an electronic copy. *(Extent: W)*
