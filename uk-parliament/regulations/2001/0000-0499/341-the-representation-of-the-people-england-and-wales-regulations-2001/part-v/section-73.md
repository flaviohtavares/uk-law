## 73. Refusal to issue postal ballot paper[^c-4429e6d1736eb75a423af55e5c27f6cc][^c-c6c32fe3c059c29c228c76b36e77e2ac][^c-ba11f66d678ac0c1d0a30898b646810b][^c-d3c1764becc32f2beef76ea76caa91b4][^c-cdd5f6e87328691d2bf1bfdf8ddb1bea]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a returning officer is satisfied that two or more entries in the postal voters list[^c-88d3ec243dd6846c130382ffc4632936], or the proxy postal voters list[^c-23fb96a157d151601ab26c962f691c47] or in each of those lists relate to the same elector, he shall not issue more than one ballot paper in respect of that elector at any one election.
