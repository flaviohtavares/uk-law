## 71. Time when postal ballot papers are to be issued[^c-d7acaecf1fa508fd197a76421906938e][^c-1696bc60abccd2d474399db3b3988928]

*Extent: E+W*

Postal ballot papers (and postal voting statements) must be issued by the returning officer as soon as it is practicable to do so.
