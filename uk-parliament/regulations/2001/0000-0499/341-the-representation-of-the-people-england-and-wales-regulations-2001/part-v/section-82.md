# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001, section 82

## 82. Receipt of covering envelope[^c-4b65c59d08ff4a5f33592cac4e4e8d17][^c-e8c8919c5d90b4db7fd989be42267e1d][^c-414bc86d7ec94257e5e31c35e3b95923][^c-236940ef853d1215eebc62fcbb188f41][^c-6a79986bc95305e8294e3ce4faa6201b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For local government elections in Wales, the[^c-05bfbd49f7fb661bee3290e387c06696] returning officer shall, immediately on receipt (whether by hand or by post) of a covering envelope (or an envelope which is stated to include a postal vote) before the close of the poll, place it unopened in a postal voters' ballot box.

(1A)[^c-9444d73445c6d2a7f12063561194863d] For elections other than local government elections in Wales, the relevant officer must, immediately on receipt by post of a covering envelope (or an envelope which is stated to include a postal vote) before the close of the poll, place it unopened in a postal voters’ ballot box.

(2) Where an envelope, other than a covering envelope issued by the returning officer—

- (a) has been opened, and
- (b) contains a ballot paper envelope, postal voting statement[^c-cb746b1a3f128a4ef4bf091c331a829e] or ballot paper,

the first-mentioned envelope, together with its contents, shall be placed in a postal voters' ballot box.
