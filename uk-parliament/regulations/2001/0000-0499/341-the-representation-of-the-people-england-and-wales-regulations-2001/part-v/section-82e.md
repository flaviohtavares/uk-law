# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001, section 82E

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 82E. Opening of delivered or collected packets of postal voting documents[^c-c9a6b36f187466900b77627bdd8820eb][^c-dc6129831115d911dc6ceacc6dcbb809][^c-cab7ff9975fcc356ce85ebd609893bf1][^c-a2f4ac15be477f1921881a926276ae86][^c-614ef495b0a1ed63f5b4d7945ca34339]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies to the packets of postal voting documents delivered to or collected by the returning officer in accordance with regulation 79(2A) or (4A) or 82A(1).

(2) Regulations 69(2), 80, 81(6), 83(1) and 84 apply to a packet to which this paragraph applies as if that packet were a postal voters’ ballot box.
