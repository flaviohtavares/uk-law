## 65. Combination of polls[^key-5df95b2321a4485467fc1f95ffe86bfc][^key-357539983d6f61e39bb09771f133b5b3][^key-5398b2ddad8b22d0b4a6362bddc48660][^key-b8a0370d3968a789601f1d466cc4703e][^key-cdb3ac1d5ae2da146f057830c1858614][^key-209d29889017f68b1c8d1c7079d3df93]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-aadb259bbd17c1d7e3d431528287ac29] Where the polls at elections are taken together under—

- (a) subsection (1) or (2) of section 15 of the 1985 Act (combination of polls at parliamentary [^key-0caa349779e665004ced0730ed444fc9]... and local government elections), or
- (b) subsection (3) or (3AB) of section 36 of the 1983 Act (combination of polls at local elections)[^f00043],

the proceedings on the issue and receipt of postal ballot papers in respect of each election may, subject to paragraph (2) and[^key-5da5ac7c392415e25be52a8a01968f90] if the returning officers concerned agree, be taken together.

(2)[^key-3b71ca4b625513e40db8e96918890c9d] Paragraph (1) does not apply where the polls of a UK parliamentary election and a local government election in Wales are taken together.
