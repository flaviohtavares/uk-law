# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (44).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/341
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 67. Persons entitled to be present at proceedings on issue of postal ballot papers[^key-5d5e7f95c90c8933e676ca6fa49c13ee][^key-e5e51cf834081ca3850354b0d777f2f6][^key-57222a4d96cd3e78b4941f5a0a3d9c02][^key-37e3aaf9e30516921211ad0ab402d389][^key-61bbac17107e3140598478df805eb622]

*Extent: E+W*

Without prejudice to the provisions of section 6A, 6B, 6C, 6D, or 6E of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000,[^key-5d38c0359c3be73b1a3d6e727e12440d] no person may be present at the proceedings on the issue of postal ballot papers other than the returning officer and his clerks.
