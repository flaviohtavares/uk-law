# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001, section 63A

## 63A. Corresponding number lists[^c-94be4b6c0bd6766691b50d9fd49fbab9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The form of the corresponding number list to be prepared by a returning officer under rule 19A of the rules in Schedule 1 to the 1983 Act shall be in Form L1.

(2) The form of the corresponding number list to be prepared by a returning officer for the purposes of rules 29(3)(e) and 37(1)(b)[^c-f4c60c1913a459827995628db6ff72c4] and 37(1)(d) of the rules in Schedule 1 to the 1983 Act shall be in Form L2.

(3) The form of the corresponding number list to be prepared by a returning officer under rule 19A of the rules in Schedule 1 to the 1983 Act, when a parliamentary election is combined with another poll under section 15 of the Representation of the People Act 1985 or section 44 or 45[^c-863696bbec26181ccab15ad34f7a80d6] of the Local Government Act 2000 shall be in Form M1.

(4) The form of the corresponding number list to be prepared by a returning officer for the purposes of rules 29(3)(e) and 37(1)(b)[^c-91f9a27d45d7aa792ccbdc9a6566cc27], and 37(1)(d) of the rules in Schedule 1 to the 1983 Act, when a parliamentary election is combined with another poll under section 15 of the Representation of the People Act 1985 or section 44 or 45[^c-9687b7ce71ce4e0c9c44afc1de71bffa] of the Local Government Act 2000 shall be in Form M2.
