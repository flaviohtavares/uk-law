# The Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001, section 62

## 62. Marked register for polling stations[^key-b8fb442644ae39be7a3f0a23ccb3b66f][^key-9bc9003fc17c4018cd2d90c1fc4c87eb][^key-e847de06d6fe43cbab1043c3deddb255][^key-5337dbc7b9d75aa0cf2a4b1793164152][^key-8132a001c81876ba463903bc88d4be87]

*Extent: E+W*

To indicate that an elector or his proxy is entitled to vote by post and is for that reason not entitled to vote in person, the letter “A” shall be placed against the entry[^key-fa49bedc3354f2daa32802ad617d6058] of that elector in any copy of the register, or part of it, provided for a polling station.
