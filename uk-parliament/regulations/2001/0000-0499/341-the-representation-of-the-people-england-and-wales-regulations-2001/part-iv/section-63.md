## 63. Certificate of employment at a parliamentary election[^c-8ef6f9317b5e944ea6eb8b4e5035e569]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The form of certificate in Form F is hereby prescribed for the purposes of rule 32(3) of the elections rules in Schedule 1 to the 1983 Act.

(2) The prescribed officer of police for those purposes is one of or above the rank of inspector.
