## 50. Interpretation of Part IV[^key-022d2e8c62827cb1d48dc84bb1f1f1ee][^key-76a9cd942fe6d5f9e7e9f0c34dcab1aa][^key-49cf05b67acca982b11c83285041d152][^key-99668196b4d7c60bbb5d7181fb8c28c1][^key-2c055e2e0a48465773ed4489fd3bb756]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part of these Regulations—

- “Schedule 4” means Schedule 4 to the 2000 Act;[^key-4888e228f3e9d9157780ab8bda8bb5b0]...
- “absent voter” means an elector who is entitled to vote by proxy or an elector or proxy who is entitled to vote by post;[^key-f661a9b93acd46a56bd856284c0f7318]
- “allotted polling station” has the meaning set out in regulation 53(7) below;
- “[^key-187f110c6d0ac2146d0a09df72d22f8b]**the maximum period**” means the period specified in paragraph 3(1A)(a) or (1B)(a) of Schedule 4.
