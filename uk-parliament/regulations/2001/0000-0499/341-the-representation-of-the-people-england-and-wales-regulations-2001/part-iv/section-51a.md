## 51A. Additional provision concerning the requirement that an application for an absent vote must be signed by the applicant[^key-0057be30b491bfc3839f34bd2fe9d8f4][^key-33dce8499f883be8c1fd5d5a16931b17][^key-1d839215fd27dc77d9c7284d9ced4d06][^key-56e62774d537dd8a33ee2eecd455808e][^key-cde8ceffe9f1b9f9520c80f9b28a3add]

*Extent: E+W*

The registration officer may satisfy himself—

- (a)[^key-7fa8ba1fca543e82b95e13f76f854cc3] that an application under Schedule 4 meets any requirements that it has been signed by the applicant and states his date of birth by referring to any signature and date of birth—
  - (i) previously provided by the applicant to the registration officer or the returning officer; or
  - (ii) previously provided by the applicant to the authority referred to in regulation 35(2)(a), which the registration officer is authorised to inspect for the purposes of his registration duties; and
- (b) as to whether the applicant is unable to provide a signature or a consistent signature due to any disability or inability to read or write.
