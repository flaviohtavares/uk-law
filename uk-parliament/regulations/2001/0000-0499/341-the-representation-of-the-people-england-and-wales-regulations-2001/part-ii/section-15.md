## 15. Contents of service declaration[^c-5af380eff75ad2cb12292589dcdeebce]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In addition to the matters specified in paragraphs (a) to (f) of section 16 of the 1983 Act[^f00010], a service declaration shall state—

- (a) the declarant’s full name and present address,
- (b) the grounds on which the declarant claims a service declaration, and
- (c) such of the particulars specified in paragraph (2), (3) or (4) below as are relevant to the service qualification claimed by the declarant.

(1A)[^c-e92e0ee16cf28d82e493ff234de4d0b1] In relation to the registration of local government electors in Wales, paragraph (1)(c) does not apply in a case where the declarant claims a service qualification under section 14(1A) of the 1983 Act (see instead regulation 15A).

(2) Where the declarant claims a service qualification on the grounds that he is a member of the forces (within the meaning of section 59(1) of the 1983 Act) or the spouse or civil partner[^c-d518433be7aacae34e6440b6c85ba0a7] of such a member, the service declaration shall state—

- (a) the service (whether naval, military or air forces) in which that member serves,
- (b) the rank or rating of that member, and
- (c) the service number of that member;

and where that member serves in the military forces, the service declaration shall in addition state the regiment or corps in which he serves.

(3) Where the declarant claims a service qualification on the grounds that he is a Crown servant to whom regulation 14 above applies or the spouse or civil partner[^c-65268f56800c7b92a71cd011ac59d6ae] of such a servant, the service declaration shall state—

- (a) the name of the Government department in which that servant works, [^c-6c3f175dec97258b58436874595bf3a4]...
- (b) a description of the post of that servant , and[^c-0dbeda3e9722c178648902299c8aad28]
- (c) any staff number, payroll number or other similar identifying number of that servant.

(3A)[^c-887ac3d2bef8ab84ab4721ada4cd868a] In relation to the registration of local government electors in Wales, the reference to “Government department” in paragraph (3) includes any organisation in which a Crown servant works.

(4) Where the declarant claims a service qualification on the grounds that he is a British Council employee or the spouse or civil partner[^c-81573e74008240ec238717a31efe7962] of such an employee, the service declaration shall state—[^c-70889920f6c4b7e7fc43557b411225f7]

- (a) a description of the post of that employee, and
- (b) any staff number, payroll number or other similar identifying number of that employee.
