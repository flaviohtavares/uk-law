## 21. Transmission of overseas elector’s declaration[^key-42cde1c8abe0b4ba6aa10c118a39ec10]

*Extent: E+W*

An overseas elector’s declaration shall be transmitted to the registration officer for that part of a constituency within which is situated the address specified in the declaration in accordance with section 1C(2)(a)(i), (3)(a)(i) or (4) of the 1985 Act by—[^key-5fcd95f783a16ba75a36240aa872b3cc]

- (a) the Secretary of State, where the declaration forms part of an application made through the digital service in accordance with regulation 26(9);
- (b) otherwise, by the declarant.
