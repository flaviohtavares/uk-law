# The Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Funding) Regulations 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 59B(3) and (4) of the Justices of the Peace Act 1997[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Funding) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force for the purposes of regulations 1, 2 and 9 on 31st January 2001 and for all other purposes on 1st April 2001.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In these Regulations—

- (a) “the 1997 Act” means the Justices of the Peace Act 1997;
- (b) “the Authority” means the Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority referred to in section 30A(1) of the 1997 Act[^f00002];
- (c) “the general funding contribution” has the meaning specified in regulation 3;
- (d) “the inner London boroughs' additional funding contribution” has the meaning specified in regulation 4.

(2) References in these Regulations to a year mean a year beginning on 1st April and ending on the following 31st March.

## 3. General funding contribution

(1) References in paragraph (2) to kinds of the Authority’s expenditure do not include the kind of the Authority’s expenditure referred to in regulation 4.

(2) The general funding contribution for any year is an amount equal to 20% of—

- (a) all the Authority’s expenditure of whatever kind for that year; or
- (b) if, in relation to any kind of expenditure for that year, the Lord Chancellor determines a lesser amount under section 59B(3)(b) of the 1997 Act—
  - (i) such amount as may be for the time being determined by the Lord Chancellor for that kind of expenditure and that year; and
  - (ii) all the Authority’s other kinds of expenditure for that year, if any.

## 4. Inner London boroughs' additional funding contribution

The inner London boroughs' additional funding contribution for any year is an amount equal to that part, if any, of the Authority’s expenditure for that year that arises as a result of liability transferred to it for repayment of debt incurred by the Receiver for the Metropolitan Police District in respect of functions in relation to magistrates' courts.

## 5. Apportionment of contributions

The amount that each London local authority shall pay under section 59B(3) of the 1997 Act each year to the Authority shall be—

- (a) in the case of the inner London boroughs, such amount of the general funding contribution for that year as is specified in regulations 6 and 7 and such amount of the inner London boroughs' additional funding contribution for that year as is specified in regulation 8; and
- (b) in the case of all other London local authorities, such amount of the general funding contribution for that year as is specified in regulations 6 and 7.

- A is the figure for that London local authority shown in column 2 of Schedule 1 to these Regulations;
- E is the general funding contribution for that year.

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/47)

where F is 45.3818% of the amount of the inner London boroughs' additional funding contribution for that year and G is the figure shown in column 2 of Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

## 9. Anticipation of payments

(1) A London local authority may anticipate a payment under section 59B of the 1997 Act when making calculations in accordance with section 32 of the Local Government Finance Act 1992[^f00003] (originally or by way of substitute).

(2) The amount of the payment so anticipated shall be equal to the London local authority’s estimate, at the time the calculations (or last calculations) are made, of the amount of the payment which it considers likely will be due.
