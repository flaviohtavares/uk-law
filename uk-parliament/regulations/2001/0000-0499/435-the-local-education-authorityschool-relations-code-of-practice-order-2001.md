# The Local Education Authority—School Relations Code of Practice Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 435

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Local Education Authority—School Relations Code of Practice Order 2001

Made

16th February 2001

Coming into force

16th February 2001

Whereas the Local Education Authority–School Relations Code of Practice was laid in draft before each House of Parliament in accordance with section 127(3) and section 85(3) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001] on 8th January 2001 and neither House has resolved not to approve the draft;

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 85(5) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998, the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Local Education Authority–School Relations Code of Practice Order 2001 and shall come into force on 16th February 2001.

**2.** The Code of Practice on Local Education Authority–School Relations under section 127 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 issued by the Secretary of State in the form of the draft and under DfEE Number 0027/2001 shall come into force on 16th February 2001.

Estelle Morris

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Employment

16th February 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings a new Code of Practice on Local Education Authority–School Relations under section 127 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 into force on 16th February 2001. A copy of the code can be obtained from DfEE Publications, PO Box 5050, Sherwood Park, Annesley, Nottingham NG15 0JD, and may also be found on the Department’s WEB site (http://www.dfee.gov.uk).
