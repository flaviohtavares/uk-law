# The Aerodromes (Designation) (Chargeable Air Services) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 354

CIVIL AVIATION

The Aerodromes (Designation) (Chargeable Air Services) Order 2001

Made

10th February 2001

Laid before Parliament

13th February 2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 77(4) of the Transport Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Aerodromes (Designation) (Chargeable Air Services) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

## 2. Designation

The aerodromes specified in the Schedule hereto are hereby designated for the purposes of section 77(3)(b) of the Transport Act 2000.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Bob Ainsworth

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

10th February 2001

## SCHEDULE — DESIGNATED AERODROMES

Article 2

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| London Heathrow | Aberdeen (Dyce) |
| London Gatwick | Edinburgh |
| London Stansted | Glasgow |

(This note is not part of the Order)

The CAA may, under section 73 of the Transport Act 2000, specify charges in respect of chargeable air services as defined in section 77 of the Act. Air traffic services provided on behalf of the owner or manager of an aerodrome are, by section 77(3)(b) of the Act, excepted from being chargeable air services unless they are provided on behalf of the owner or manager of a designated aerodrome.

This Order designates London Heathrow, London Gatwick, London Stansted, Aberdeen (Dyce), Edinburgh and Glasgow aerodromes for the purposes of this section.
