# The Education (The Arts Institute at Bournemouth Further Education Corporation) (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 337

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (The Arts Institute at Bournemouth Further Education Corporation) (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2001

Made

5th February 2001

Laid before Parliament

9th February 2001

Coming into force

1st August 2001

Whereas it appears to the Secretary of State that the full-time equivalent enrolment number of the institution conducted by The Arts Institute at Bournemouth Further Education Corporation[^f00001] for courses of higher education exceeds 55 per cent of its total full-time equivalent enrolment number;

Now therefore the Secretary of State for Education and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 122A of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00002], hereby makes the following order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (The Arts Institute at Bournemouth Further Education Corporation) (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st August 2001.

**2.** On the date on which this Order comes into force The Arts Institute at Bournemouth Further Education Corporation shall cease to be a further education corporation and shall become a higher education corporation.

Tessa Blackstone

Minister of State

Department for Education and Employment

5th February 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision for the transfer of The Arts Institute at Bournemouth Further Education Corporation to the higher education sector. On 1st August 2001 that corporation becomes a higher education corporation.
