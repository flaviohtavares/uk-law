# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) (Rate of Discount for 2001/02) (England) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 384

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) (Rate of Discount for 2001/02) (England) Regulations 2001

Made

13th February 2001

Laid before Parliament

20th February 2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 49(2) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

These Regulations, which extend to England only[^f00002], may be cited as the Local Authorities (Capital Finance) (Rate of Discount for 2001/02) (England) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

## 2. Rate of discount

The percentage rate of discount prescribed for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2001 for the purposes of defining “r” in section 49(2) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 shall be 7.5 per cent.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Hilary Armstrong

Minister of State

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

13th February 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Part IV of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 makes provision for the capital finance of local authorities. Section 49(2) sets out a formula for determining for the purposes of Part IV the value of the consideration falling to be given by a local authority under a credit arrangement in any financial year after the one in which the arrangement comes into being. The percentage rate of discount prescribed for the financial year is one of the factors referred to in the formula. For the financial year beginning on 1st April 2001 these Regulations prescribe 7.5 per cent. which is 0.4 per cent. less than the rate of discount prescribed for 2000/2001.
