# The Health Act 1999 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 270 (C.16)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Health Act 1999 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2001

Made

30th January 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 67(1) and (2) of the Health Act 1999[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, interpretation and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Health Act 1999 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2001.

(2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Health Act 1999.

(3) This Order extends only to England and Wales.

## 2. Appointed day

9th February 2001 is the day appointed for the coming into force of—

- (a) section 39 of the Act, in so far as it is not already in force;
- (b) section 65(2) of the Act, in so far as it relates to the provisions of Schedule 5 to the Act specified in paragraph (c) of this article; and
- (c) in Schedule 5 to the Act (repeals), the repeals relating to the provisions specified in column (1) of the Schedule to this Order, to the extent specified in column (2) of that Schedule.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hunt

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

30th January 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 9th February 2001 section 39 of the Health Act 1999 in relation to Wales, and, in relation to England, repeals in Schedule 5 to the Act which are consequential upon provisions of the Act brought into force by earlier Commencement Orders.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The following provisions of the Health Act 1999 have been, or are to be, brought into force by commencement order made before the date of this Order. In addition to the provisions listed below, various provisions of Part II of, and Schedules 4 and 5 to, the Act that extend only to Scotland have been brought into force on 1st October 1999 by the [Health Act 1999 (Commencement No. 4) (Scotland) Order 1999, S.S.I. 1999/90 (C. 66)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/1999/90), on 14th October 1999 by the [Health Act 1999 (Commencement No. 6) (Scotland) Order 1999, S.S.I. 1999 No. 115 (C. 73)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/1999/115) and on 1st March 2000 by the [Health Act 1999 (Commencement No. 7) (Scotland) Order 2000, S.S.I. 2000 No. 38 (C. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/38).
