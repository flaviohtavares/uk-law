# The Employment Zones (Amendment) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 261

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Employment Zones (Amendment) Regulations 2001

Made

30th January 2001

Laid before Parliament

5th February 2001

Coming into force

26th February 2001

The Secretary of State for Education and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 60 of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999[^f00001] and after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Employment Zones (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 26th February 2001.

## 2. Amendment of the Employment Zones Regulations 2000

The Schedule to the Employment Zones Regulations 2000[^f00003] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in relation to the employment zone of Brent, after “the London borough of Brent” there shall be added “the London borough of Barnet, the London borough of Camden and the City of Westminster”;
- (b) in relation to the employment zone of Doncaster, after “the metropolitan district of Doncaster” there shall be added “and the metropolitan district of Rotherham”.

Tessa Jowell

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Employment

30th January 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the schedule to the Employment Zones Regulations 2000 (as substituted by the Employment Zones (Amendment) Regulations 2000) by adding, to the Doncaster employment zone, the district of Rotherham and, to the Brent employment zone, the London boroughs of Barnet, Camden and the City of Westminster.
