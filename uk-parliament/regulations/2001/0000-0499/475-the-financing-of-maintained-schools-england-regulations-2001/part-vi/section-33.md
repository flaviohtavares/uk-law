# The Financing of Maintained Schools (England) Regulations 2001, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33. Manner of publication

For the purposes of paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 14 to the 1998 Act (prescribed manner of publication of schemes) a scheme shall be published by the local education authority concerned on its coming into force and in the circumstances prescribed in regulation 34 by—

- (a) furnishing a copy to the governing body and a copy to the head teacher of each school maintained by the authority; and
- (b) making a copy available for reference at all reasonable times and without charge at each school maintained by the authority and at the principal education office of the authority.
