# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating (No. 2) Order 2000, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Statutory Maternity Pay

In regulation 6 of the Statutory Maternity Pay (General) Regulations 1986[^f00035] (lower rate of statutory maternity pay) for the sum of £60.20 there shall be substituted the sum of £62.20.
