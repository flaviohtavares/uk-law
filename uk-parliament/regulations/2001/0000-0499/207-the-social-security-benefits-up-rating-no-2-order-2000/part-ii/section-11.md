# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating (No. 2) Order 2000, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Increase in rate of graduated retirement benefit

(1) In the National Insurance Act 1965[^f00036] the sum of 8.77 pence referred to in section 36(1) (graduated retirement benefit) shall be increased by 3.3 per cent.; and from and including 9th April 2001 the reference in that provision to that sum shall have effect as a reference to 9.06 pence.

(2) The sums which are the increases of graduated retirement benefit under Schedule 2 to the Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) (No. 2) Regulations 1978[^f00037] (increases for deferred retirement) shall be increased by 3.3 per cent. of their amount apart from this Order.

(3) The sums which are the additions under section 37(1) of the National Insurance Act 1965 (additions for widows and widowers) shall be increased by 3.3 per cent. of their amount apart from this Order.
