# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating (No. 2) Order 2000, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “the Computation of Earnings Regulations” means the Social Security Benefit (Computation of Earnings) Regulations 1996[^f00013];
- “the Contributions and Benefits Act” means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00014];
- “the Council Tax Benefit Regulations” means the Council Tax Benefit (General) Regulations 1992[^f00015];
- “the Housing Benefit Regulations” means the Housing Benefit (General) Regulations 1987[^f00016];
- “the Income Support Regulations” means the Income Support (General) Regulations 1987[^f00017];
- “the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations” means the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996[^f00018]; and
- “the Pension Schemes Act” means the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00019].
