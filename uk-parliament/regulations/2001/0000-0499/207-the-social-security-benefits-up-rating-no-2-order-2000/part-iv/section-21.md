# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating (No. 2) Order 2000, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Increase in age-related amounts of contribution-based Jobseeker’s Allowance

In regulation 79(1) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (weekly amounts of contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for the sum of £31.45 there shall be substituted the sum of £31.95;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for the sum of £41.35 there shall be substituted the sum of £42.00; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) for the sum of £52.20 there shall be substituted the sum of £53.05.
