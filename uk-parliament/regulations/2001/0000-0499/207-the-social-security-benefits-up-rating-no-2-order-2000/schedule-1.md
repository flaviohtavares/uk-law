## Annotations

[^f00060]: Footnote: Paragraph 1 was repealed by section 41(5) of, and Schedule 3 to, the Jobseekers Act [1995 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/18). Paragraph 2 was substituted and paragraph 2A was inserted by section 2(2) of the Social Security (Incapacity for Work) Act [1994 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/18); paragraph 3 was repealed by section 11(2) of, and Schedule 2 to, that Act. Paragraph 5 was amended by paragraph 21 of Schedule 4 to the Pensions Act [1995 (c. 26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26).

[^f00061]: Footnote: *See* section 54 of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act [1999 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/30). Bereavement Payment is introduced with effect from 9th April 2001 and replaces Widow’s Payment.

[^f00063]: Footnote: Paragraph 4A was inserted by paragraph 14 of Schedule 8 to the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act [1999 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/30) with effect from 9th April 2001.

[^f00064]: Footnote: Widow’s pension is payable in relation only to deaths occuring before 11th April 1988 (paragraph 14(1) of Schedule 7 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992). The initial rate relates only to the period of 26 weeks following the date of the deceased’s death (paragraph 16(1) of that Schedule). The rate stated is therefore the rate applicable for the 26 weeks following 10th April 1988.
