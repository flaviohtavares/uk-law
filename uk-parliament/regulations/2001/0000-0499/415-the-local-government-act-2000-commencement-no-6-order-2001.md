# The Local Government Act 2000 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 415 (C. 19)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The Local Government Act 2000 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2001

Made

15th February 2001

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 108(5) and (7) of the Local Government Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, interpretation and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 2000 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2001.

(2) In this Order references to sections are references to sections of the Local Government Act 2000.

(3) This Order applies in relation to England only.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 19th February 2001

The following provisions shall come into force on 19th February 2001—

- (a) section 45, in so far as not already in force; and
- (b) sections 99 and 100, with the exception of subsection (4) of section 99 and so much of subsection (3) of section 99 as relates to subsection (4).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Beverley Hughes

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

15th February 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, in relation to England, further provisions of the Local Government Act 2000 (“the Act”).

Article 2 brings into force the remaining provisions in section 45 of the Act (referendums), the regulation making powers in that section having come into force on 7th August 2000.

This article also brings into force all the provisions relating to allowances and pensions for local authority members contained in sections 99 and 100 of the Act, with the exception of the abolition of the attendance allowance which is currently payable under section 18 of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989.

(This note is not part of the Order)
