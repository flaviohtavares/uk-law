# The Company and Business Names (Chamber of Commerce, Etc.) Act 1999 (Commencement) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 258 (C.15)

COMPANIES

BUSINESS NAMES

The Company and Business Names (Chamber of Commerce, Etc.) Act 1999 (Commencement) Order 2001

Made

1st February 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 5(2) of the Company and Business Names (Chamber of Commerce, Etc.) Act 1999[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Company and Business Names (Chamber of Commerce, Etc.) Act 1999 (Commencement) Order 2001.

## 2. Commencement

The Company and Business Names (Chamber of Commerce, Etc.) Act 1999 shall come into force on 10th May 2001.

Kim Howells

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Consumers and Corporate Affairs,

Department of Trade and Industry

1st February 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Company and Business Names (Chamber of Commerce, Etc.) Act 1999 into force on 10th May 2001.
