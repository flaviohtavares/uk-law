# The United Lincolnshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 154

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The United Lincolnshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

Made

23rd January 2001

Coming into force

5th February 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the United Lincolnshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 5th February 2001.

(2) In this Order, “the Order” means the United Lincolnshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2000[^f00004].

## 2. Additional non-executive director

In article 4 of the Order (directors of the trust), for “5 non-executive” there shall be substituted “7 non-executive”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Yvette Cooper

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Public Health,

Department of Health

23rd January 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the constitution of the United Lincolnshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust, an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by increasing the number of non-executive directors from 5 to 7.
