## 10. Return and declaration of election expenses[^c-a15d332c3e146d0d8b19ae2d003c4ba3]

*Extent: S*

(1) For the purpose of section 75(3) of the 1983 Act, the form of the return of election expenses shall be in Form C and the form of the declaration as to election expenses shall be in Form D.

(2)[^c-7d75132e93b4086e686f4608779e0c1a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The fee for a copy of any such return or declaration or any accompanying document[^c-f0080dff0c2e0b7e1b45b180a3cfa438] shall be at the rate of 20p for each side of each page.
