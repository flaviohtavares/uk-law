## SCHEDULE 1 — ENABLING POWERS[^key-7e80303304d41e25c4a7f6e6c95f5ab8]

*Extent: S*

Preamble

[^key-7e80303304d41e25c4a7f6e6c95f5ab8]These Regulations are made in exercise of the following powers:

- (a) sections 10A(7) and (9)[^f00044], 13A(1) and (6), 53[^f00045] and 201(1) and (3)[^f00046] of, rule 24[^f00047] of Schedule 1 and Schedule 2[^f00048] to, the Representation of the People Act 1983 (“the 1983 Act”);
- (b) sections 3(5), (6) and (7)[^f00049] of the Representation of the People Act 1985 (“the 1985 Act”);
- (c) having regard to the definition of “prescribed” in section 202(1) of the 1983 Act, sections 4(4)[^f00050], 9(2)[^f00051], 10A(1), (3), (5) and (7), 13(3), 13A(2), 13AB(2),[^key-37b33d8b7e24b769f13ac4ab581c4db4] 13B(3), 14(1), 16, 56(1) and (5) and 57, 75(3) and 89(1) of, and rules 24, 28(3), 29(3A)[^f00052], 32(3) and 45(1B)[^f00053] in Schedule 1 to, that Act;
- (d) having regard to the definition of “prescribed” in section 202(1) of the 1983 Act and section 27(2) of the 1985 Act, section 2(3) of the 1985 Act[^f00054];
- (e) having regard to the definition of “prescribed” in section 202(1) of the 1983 Act and paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 4 to the Representation of the People Act 2000, the following provisions in that Schedule, namely, paragraphs 3(1)(b) and (2)(c), 4(1)(b), (2)(c) and (4)(a), 6(7) and (8) and 7(5)(c) and (7);
- (f) having regard to the designation[^f00055] of the Secretary of State for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00056] in relation to measures relating to the rights of citizens o f the Union to vote at European Parliamentary elections and local government elections in Scotland, that section 2(2); and
- (g) paragraph 2(4)(b) of Schedule 1 to the European Parliamentary Elections Act 1978[^f00057].
