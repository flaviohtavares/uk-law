## 22. Notification about registration as overseas elector[^c-56d8b3ea681a51b2b4b4efcae3aeca93]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the registration officer is satisfied that the declarant qualifies as an overseas elector under the provisions of section 1A[^c-13786bdcb83138fbced3738cadf216a8] of the 1985 Act, he shall so notify the declarant.

(2) Where the registration officer rejects an application for registration in pursuance of an overseas elector’s declaration because–

- (a) in his opinion the declarant does not qualify as an overseas elector under section 1A[^c-03b590d6ff8dcdfcea729708b3273fc8] of the 1985 Act, or
- (b) the declaration does not satisfy the requirements of section 1C of the 1985 Act or regulation 18[^c-6ec3b3623126b6c703add78460a457c7], [^c-bbe995bce1507575916f1d001779f894]...
- (c)[^c-bbe995bce1507575916f1d001779f894] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

the registration officer must notify the declarant in writing and set out the officer’s reasons for rejecting the application for registration.[^c-d4fdde919f9321df18724cf7e7784b88]
