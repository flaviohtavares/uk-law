# Representation of the People (Scotland) Regulations 2001, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/497
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 67. Persons entitled to be present at proceedings on issue of postal ballot papers[^c-00385e4cb5b9a3cfbe5096f4e4d9c1cc][^c-225af750e57a83da4ee854fc6b832108]

*Extent: S*

Without prejudice to the provisions of section 6A, 6B, 6C, 6D or 6E of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000,[^c-8ff69993e81fbb0ab55dd861864a90a7] No person may be present at the proceedings on the issue of postal ballot papers other than the returning officer and his staff.
