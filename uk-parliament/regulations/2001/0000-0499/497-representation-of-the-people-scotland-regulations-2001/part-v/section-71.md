## 71. Time when postal ballot papers are to be issued[^key-9b7c91a2e39b7376b0e2c5d3c47e3e05][^key-de8f3529f4e33a1e3f215263ee7ad9b4]

*Extent: S*

Postal ballot papers (and postal voting statements) must be issued by the returning officer as soon as it is practicable to do so.
