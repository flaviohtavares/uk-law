## 89. Sealing of receptacles[^key-55c213cd4a70f000492399ee145863ac][^key-32f25d69dfc78246739e4946bafcae0f]

*Extent: S*

(1) As soon as practicable after the completion of the procedure under regulation 88(3) and (4) above, the returning officer shall make up into separate packets the contents of–

- (a) the receptacle of rejected votes,
- (b)[^key-47632fb18fa3c1ca95a6cf5f9b5be9e9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) the receptacle of rejected ballot paper envelopes, [^key-b49db936b58d431d566ff3840d798627]...
- (d) the lists of spoilt, lost or cancelled[^key-37dc0bc20a1b710d99a4d1710efb10ab] postal ballot papers,
- (e)[^key-92287ea622811b9a2bc1bc1df26bc788] the receptacle for rejected votes (verification procedure), and
- (f) the receptacle for postal voting statements (verification procedure),
- (g)[^key-7700d50afcaeedc9e43aa764fefbd695] the packets of rejected postal voting documents and accompanying return of postal voting documents forms made up in accordance with regulations 79C(2) and 82C(2),

and shall seal up such packets.

(2) Any document in those packets marked “provisionally rejected” shall be deemed to be marked “rejected”.
