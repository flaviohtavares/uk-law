## 82. Receipt of covering envelope[^c-b1377bc68206c0be1184666157afb583][^c-632064a4f9221ff6de34dbce995f56b8]

*Extent: S*

(1) The returning officer shall, immediately on receipt by post[^c-446a40376c0775745987dc6d0983ce18] of a covering envelope (or an envelope which is stated to include a postal vote) before the close of the poll, place it unopened in a postal voters' ballot box.

(2) Where an envelope, other than a covering envelope issued by the returning officer–

- (a) has been opened, and
- (b) contains a ballot paper envelope, postal voting statement[^c-2b209e325b4b4e3f16d6e35aa92cfb23] or ballot paper,

the first mentioned envelope, together with its contents, shall be placed in a postal voters' ballot box.
