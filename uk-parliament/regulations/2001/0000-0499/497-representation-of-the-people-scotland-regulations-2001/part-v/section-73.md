## 73. Refusal to issue postal ballot paper[^key-34f368e5f66681a1a5886696c77a400a][^key-c0859435c2a568a027f7d12fd6320340]

*Extent: S*

Where a returning officer is satisfied that two or more entries in the postal voters list[^key-1db7644f049ee8911ab127a3ed23502c], or the proxy postal voters list[^key-09d1021467d6f7c9e948cf883ffe2911] or in each of those lists relate tothe same elector, he shall not issue more than one ballot paper in respect of that elector at any one election.
