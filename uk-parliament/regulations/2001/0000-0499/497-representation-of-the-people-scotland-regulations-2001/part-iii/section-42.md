## 42. Marking of names[^key-fcfea2c936b190770936f82c6d6d9f29]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where no mark appears against a person’s entry[^key-1f3db8ae1fb7a548bac096290855139b] in the register of electors, this indicates that he is registered in the registers of parliamentary and local government electors.

(2)[^key-adba3bfc958e3f314004e111e07d56eb] To indicate that a qualifying foreign national or a person to whom section 3(1A) of the 1983 Act applies is registered only in the register of local government electors, the letter “M” shall be placed against their entry.

(4)[^key-bde346e5116d0b06ca62e4d6b76b3e01] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) To indicate that any other person is registered only in the register of local government electors, the letter “L” shall be placed against his entry.

(6) To indicate that an overseas elector is registered only in the register of parliamentary electors, the letter “F” shall be placed against his entry.
