## 39. Different letter, number or combination of letter and number for each parliamentary polling district[^key-7b09100180e09211fcbd3b32c79a2dbc]

*Extent: S*

There shall be a different letter or letters, number or numbers, or combination of letter and number or letters and numbers in the register for each parliamentary polling district and such letter or letters, number or numbers, or combination shall form part of an elector’s number in the register.
