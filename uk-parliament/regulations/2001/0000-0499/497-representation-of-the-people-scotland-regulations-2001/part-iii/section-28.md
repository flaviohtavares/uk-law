## 28. Inspection of applications and objections[^key-54bfcf8d5ad5f5a7d6b69646fc1051df]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-b365adc4cbe233c42a33864bbe50e2be] An entry on the list of applications for registration kept under regulation 29(2)(a)[^key-468d114be647601c9a1562e9a62b7a8f] and any objection to a person’s registration shall be made available for inspection at the registration officer’s office until the application to which the entry relates[^key-379add7353ad79d5b4268a3d4d0c8964] or objection has been determined by the registration officer.

(2)[^key-479c81323ae26ac3e7fd775a85a1ee1a] This regulation does not apply to an application for registration which is accompanied by an application for an anonymous entry.

(3)[^key-3f480d495c7503a7dd6c6ccb80cb981e] In relation to the registration of local government electors, this regulation does not apply to an application made by a person under the age of 16.
