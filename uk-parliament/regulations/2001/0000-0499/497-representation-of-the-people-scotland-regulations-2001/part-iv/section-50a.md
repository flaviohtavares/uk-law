## 50A. Disapplication of Part IV[^key-866ab468e009c3279be97d0578dea0e8]

*Extent: S*

This Part does not apply where a postal or proxy vote is being or has been sought in respect solely of local government elections in Scotland or of a particular local government election in Scotland.
