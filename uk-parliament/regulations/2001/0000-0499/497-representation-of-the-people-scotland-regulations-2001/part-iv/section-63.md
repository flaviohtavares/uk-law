## 63. Certificate of employment at a parliamentary election[^c-1fe041e62cbce5421b95475971c58d0f]

*Extent: S*

(1) The form of certificate in Form F is hereby prescribed for the purposes of rule 32(3) of the elections rules in Schedule 1 to the 1983 Act.

(2) The prescribed officer of police for those purposes is an officer of or above the rank of inspector.
