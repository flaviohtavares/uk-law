## 50. Interpretation of Part IV[^c-6a7b822554d16f3cb8c77d0d2cab466f]

*Extent: S*

In this Part of these Regulations–

- “Schedule 4” means Schedule 4 to the 2000 Act; and
- “absent voter” means an elector who is entitled to vote by proxy or an elector or proxy who is entitled to vote by post;[^c-b48b467a333fce8f165befa57524c4e0]
- “allotted polling station” has the meaning set out in regulation 53(7) below.
- “[^c-a973ca709fd43bee09dd13209697f6de]**the maximum period**” means the period specified in paragraph 3(1A)(a) or (1B)(a) of Schedule 4.
