# Representation of the People (Scotland) Regulations 2001, section 61A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/497
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 61A. Conditions on the use, supply and inspection of absent voter records or lists[^key-eb8d2e7297106ce986dba23869818c94][^key-3721942cb8cac3118bf87a578d2c1aea]

*Extent: S*

The restrictions on the supply, disclosure and use of the full register in regulations 94 and 95 shall apply to information covered by regulations 61(1)(a) and (b), as they apply to the full register, except that the permitted purpose shall mean either–

- (a)[^key-e5378c23a42a4f4f13ef0d09d6e7e9fc] Article 84A[^key-1b7cd2f40c610fe3833c920ffd713423] GDPR purposes; or
- (b) electoral purposes.
