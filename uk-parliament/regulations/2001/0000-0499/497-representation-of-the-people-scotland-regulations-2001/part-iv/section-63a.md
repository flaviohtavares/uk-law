# Representation of the People (Scotland) Regulations 2001, section 63A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/497

> Extent: Scotland.

## 63A. Corresponding number lists[^key-d287a359caee441dcbe5774919b0243f]

*Extent: S*

(1) The form of the corresponding number list to be prepared by a returning officer under rule 19A of the rules in Schedule 1 to the 1983 Act shall be in Form L1.

(2) The form of the corresponding number list to be prepared by a returning officer for the purposes of rules 29(3)(e) and 37(1)(b)[^key-c56a0abc3921d14313abffc76a1416df] of the rules in Schedule 1 to the 1983 Act shall be in Form L2.

(3) The form of the corresponding number list to be prepared by a returning officer under rule 19A of the rules in Schedule 1 to the 1983 Act, when a parliamentary election is combined with another poll under section 15 of the Representation of the People Act 1985 shall be in Form M1.

(4) The form of the corresponding number list to be prepared by a returning officer for the purposes of rules 29(3)(e) and 37(1)(b)[^key-11871aea49934a2451f9598509a6502a] of the rules in Schedule 1 to the 1983 Act, when a parliamentary election is combined with another poll under section 15 of the Representation of the People Act 1985 shall be in Form M2.
