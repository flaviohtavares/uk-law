# The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Nuclear Installations) (Revocation) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2904

CIVIL AVIATION

The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Nuclear Installations) (Revocation) Regulations 2001

Made

10th August 2001

Coming into force

forthwith

The Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 85 of the Air Navigation Order 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Nuclear Installations) (Revocation) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force forthwith.

**2.** The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Nuclear Installations) Regulations 1998[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions

Ian McBrayne

Head of Civil Aviation Division,

Department of Transport, Local Government and the Regions

10th August 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Nuclear Installations) Regulations 1998. The 1998 Regulations have been replaced by the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Nuclear Installations) Regulations 2001 (S.I. [2001/1607](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1607)).
