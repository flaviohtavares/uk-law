# The Children (Leaving Care) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2878 (C.96)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS, ENGLAND

The Children (Leaving Care) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2001

Made

7th August 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 8(2) of the Children (Leaving Care) Act 2000[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Children (Leaving Care) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2001.

(2) This Order extends to England only.

## 2. Appointed Day

1st October 2001 is the day appointed for the coming into force of sections 1 to 5, and 7(1) to (4) of the Children (Leaving Care) Act 2000.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Jacqui Smith

Minister of State,

Department of Health

7th August 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the Children (Leaving Care) Act 2000 (“the Act”) apart from section 6 (exclusion from benefits) in relation to England, on 1st October 2001. The provisions brought into force amend the Children Act [1989 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41) as respects the arrangements to be made for the care and support of children who are, or have been looked after by local authorities.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the first commencement order to be made in England. Sections 1 to 5 and 7(1) to (4) have been brought into force in Wales by the [Children (Leaving Care) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2001 (SI 2001 No. 2191) (W. 153) (C. 71)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2001/2191).

## Annotations

[^f00001]: Footnote: [2000 c. 35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/35). The power is exercisable, as respects section 6, by the Secretary of State alone, and as respects the remaining provisions, in relation to England by the Secretary of State and in relation to Wales, the National Assembly for Wales.
