# The North Somerset Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2858

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The North Somerset Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2001

Made

3rd August 2001

Laid before Parliament

9th August 2001

Coming into force

1st September 2001

Whereas an application for the purpose of extending and varying an education action zone has been made to the Secretary of State with the consent of the governing bodies of every school participating in the zone.

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 10(2), and 138(7) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order shall be cited as the North Somerset Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st September 2001.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “North Somerset Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the North Somerset Education Action Zone Order 1998[^f00002].

## 3. Extension of the North Somerset Education Action Zone

The North Somerset Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 1st September 2001.

## 4. Variation of the North Somerset Education Action Zone Order 1999

There shall be inserted after article 9 of the North Somerset Education Action Zone Order 1998 the following article:

> **9A. Removal from office by Forum**
>
> A member of the Forum may be removed from office by the Forum in the following circumstances—
>
> - (a) in the case of a member who was a governor of a zone school at the time he was appointed to the Forum pursuant to either article 4(a) or article 5, if that person ceases to be a governor at that zone school, or a governor of the relevant category as the case may be;
> - (b) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to either article 4(a) or 5, if the school whose governing body appointed him, or to which he was connected, is discontinued;
> - (c) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 4(c), if the body in question is dissolved or otherwise ceases to exist; and
> - (d) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 5, if that person no longer appears to the Forum to represent the interests of either business, parents of compulsory school age children, church organisations, residents or tenants' associations, pupils at zone schools, parents of children with special educational needs, teachers, or governing bodies of schools maintained by North Somerset Council, in the area served by zone schools, as the case may be.

**5.** (1) The North Somerset Education Action Zone Order 1998[^f00002] is varied as follows:

(2) In Schedule 1 of that Order the words, “Nord Anglia Plc”, “Learning Partnership West”, “TLO Ltd”, “Bromcom Computers plc”, “Tesco Stores Ltd”, “Rathbone CI”, “YMCA”, “Springboard Opportunity Group”, “North Somerset Early Years Partnership”, and “University of Bath” are deleted.

(3) The following words are added to Schedule 1 after “Bath Spa University College”; “Connexions West of England”, “Alvis Brothers”, “Weston and Somerset Mercury”, “Rathbone Training”, “North Somerset Childcare Partnership”, “Weston-super-Mare Civic Society”, “North Somerset Play Forum”, “Priory Community School”, “Reading is Fundamental”, “Weston and Worle Tenants Group”, “Activ Sports West Partnership”, “Whitecross Village Community Association”, “Community Groups Forum”, “The Bristol Port Company”, “Asda” and “Promethean Ltd”.

(4) In Schedule 2 of that Order the words “Inclusion Support Service South” are substituted for the words “Links Centre”, and the words “Corpus Christi Catholic School” are substituted for “Corpus Christi RC Primary School”.

(5) In Schedule 2 the words “Oldmixon Primary School” are substituted for “Climixon Infant School”.

Stephen Timms

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

3rd August 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the North Somerset Education Action Zone for a further period of two years. It also varies the partners in the zone and gives the Forum an additional power to remove its members in certain circumstances.
