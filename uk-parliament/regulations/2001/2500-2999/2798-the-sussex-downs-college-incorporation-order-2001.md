# The Sussex Downs College (Incorporation) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2798

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Sussex Downs College (Incorporation) Order 2001

Made

31st July 2001

Laid before Parliament

3rd August 2001

Coming into force

1st September 2001

Whereas the Secretary of State for Education and Skills has received a proposal from the Learning and Skills Council for England, made in accordance with section 51 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001] (“the Act”), for the establishment of a body corporate under section 16(1)(a) of the Act;

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 16(1) and 17 of the Act and for the purpose of giving effect to that proposal, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Sussex Downs College (Incorporation) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st September 2001 (“the commencement date”).

**2.** There shall be established as from the commencement date a body corporate called “Sussex Downs College”, for the purpose of establishing and conducting as from the operative date a new educational institution of that name.

**3.** The operative date in relation to the further education corporation established by this Order shall be 1st November 2001, and the corporation shall conduct Sussex Downs College from that date.

Margaret Hodge

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

31st July 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes a further education corporation called Sussex Downs College under Section 16(1) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 for the purpose of conducting the educational institution called by the same name. It appoints 1st November 2001 as the operative date for the purposes of Part 1 of that Act.
