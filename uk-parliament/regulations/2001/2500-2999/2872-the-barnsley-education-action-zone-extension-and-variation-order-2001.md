# The Barnsley Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2872

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Barnsley Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001

Made

3rd August 2001

Laid before Parliament

9th August 2001

Coming into force

1st September 2001

Whereas an application for the purpose of extending and varying an education action zone has been made to the Secretary of State with the consenting bodies of every school participating in the zone.

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 10(2), 10(3) and 138(7) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencment

This Order shall be cited as the The Barnsley Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st September 2001.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “Barnsley Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Barnsley Education Action Zone Order 1998[^f00002].

## 3. Extension of the Education Action Zone

The Barnsley Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 1st September 2001.

## 4. Variation of the Barnsley Education Action Zone Order 1998

There shall be inserted after article 9 of the Barnsley Education Action Zone Order 1998 the following article—

> **9A. Removal from office by Forum.**
>
> A member of the Forum may be removed from the office by the Forum in the following circumstances—
>
> - (a) in the case of a member who was a governor of a zone school at the time he was appointed to the Forum pursuant to either article 4(a) or article 5(1), if that person ceases to be a governor at that zone school, or a governor of the relevant category as the case may be;
> - (b) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to either article 4(a) or 5(1), if the school whose governing body appointed him, or to which he was connected, is discontinued;
> - (c) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 4(c), if the body in question is dissolved or otherwise ceases to exist; and
> - (d) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 5(1), if that person no longer appears to the Forum to represent the interests of either businesses, staff of the zone schools, parents of compulsory school age children, or community organisations, in the area served by the zone schools, as the case may be.

**5.** (1) The Barnsley Education Action Zone Order 1998[^f00003] is varied as follows:

(2) In Schedule 1 the following words are deleted: “Barnsley and Doncaster TEC”, “Bull Information Systems Limited”, “Barnsley and District NHS Trust”, “British Telecommunications plc”; “Link Training”, “Bretton College”, “Sheffield University”, “Sheffield Hallam University”, “Voluntary Action Barnsley”, “South Yorkshire Probation”, “Barnsley Crime Prevention Partnership”, “The Princes Trust”, “Community Partnerships”, “Workers Educational Association”, “Barnsley Football Club”.

(3) In Schedule 1 the words “Barnsley Health Action Zone” and “Barnsley Learning Skills Council” are added after the words “Construction Industry Training Board”.

(4) In Schedule 2 after the words “Cudworth Pontefract Road Primary School”, is inserted “Edmonds Primary School”, and after “Grange Gate Junior School” is inserted, “Greenacre School”.

(5) In Schedule 2 the words “Priory School” are deleted and substituted with, “Priory School and Sports College”.

(6) In Schedule 2 the words, “Birkwood Primary School”, “Crevesford School”, “Worsborough Lobwood Primary School”, “Pontefract Road Junior and Infant School”, and “Worsborough Dale Nursery School” are deleted.

Stephen Timms

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

3rd August 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the Barnsley Education Zone for a further period of two years. The Order also varies the partners in the zone and the schools which comprise it, and gives the Forum an additional power to remove its members in certain circumstances.
