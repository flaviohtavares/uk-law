# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Mutual Societies) Order 2001, section 3

> Text as at 2014-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Interpretation[^c-df75b148a7b154960128ab2332751d2a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42] In this Order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]

- “the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42] 2000 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]” means the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
- “assistant registrar” means an assistant registrar of friendly societies for the central registration area, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
- “the assistant registrar for Scotland” means the assistant registrar of friendly societies for Scotland, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
- “the Board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]” means the Building Societies Investor Protection Board, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
- “building society” has the same meaning as in the Building Societies Act 1986 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42][^f00002],
- “the central office” means the central office of the registry of friendly societies, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
- “the central registration area” means the area defined by section 4(1)(a) of the Friendly Societies Act 1974 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42][^f00003] (as it had effect immediately before its repeal by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42] 2000 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]),
- “the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42] Chief Registrar *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]” means the Chief Registrar of friendly societies, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
- “commencement” means the beginning of the day on which section 19 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42] 2000 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42] comes into force, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
- “enactment” includes an enactment contained in subordinate legislation within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42][^f00004], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
- “the last period” means the period beginning with 1st April 2001 and ending at commencement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]

(2) In this Order, “transferred function” means any function transferred by article 4 and, in relation to any transferred function, “transferor” means the person from whom the function is transferred and “transferee” means the person to whom it is transferred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]

(3) In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires, any reference to an article by number alone is a reference to the article so numbered in this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
