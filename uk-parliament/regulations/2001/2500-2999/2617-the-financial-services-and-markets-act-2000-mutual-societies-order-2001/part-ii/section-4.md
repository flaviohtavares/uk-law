# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Mutual Societies) Order 2001, section 4

> Text as at 2014-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Transfer of functions[^key-0ee92b3c2a44b47edc858288238982b8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42] The functions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]

- (a) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42] Chief Registrar *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42], the assistant registrar for Scotland, the assistant registrars and the central office listed in Part I of Schedule 1 to this Order, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
- (b) of the Friendly Societies Commission listed in Part II of Schedule 1 to this Order, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
- (c) of the Building Societies Commission listed in Part III of Schedule 1 to this Order, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]

are transferred to the Treasury. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]

(2) All other functions which, immediately before commencement were functions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]

- (a) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42] Chief Registrar *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42], the assistant registrar for Scotland, the assistant registrars or the central office, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
- (b) of the Friendly Societies Commission, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
- (c) of the Building Societies Commission, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]

are transferred to the Authority, subject to any repeal or amendment made by any provision of this Order or by any other provision of or made under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42] 2000 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42].

(3) Schedule 2 makes provision about the application of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42] 2000 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42] in relation to functions transferred (or to be transferred) to the Authority by paragraph (2) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]

(4) For the purposes of the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 1981 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42][^f00005], paragraph (2) above is to be regarded as giving rise to the transfer of an undertaking by virtue of each of sub-paragraphs (a), (b) and (c) of that paragraph, whether or not it would otherwise be so regarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8888c2f632e760099049c37a634f2e42]
