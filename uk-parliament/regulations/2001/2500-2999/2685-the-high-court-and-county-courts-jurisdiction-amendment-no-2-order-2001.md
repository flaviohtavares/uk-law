# The High Court and County Courts Jurisdiction (Amendment No. 2) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2685 (L. 28)

COUNTY COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

SUPREME COURT OF ENGLAND AND WALES

The High Court and County Courts Jurisdiction (Amendment No. 2) Order 2001

Made

24th July 2001

Laid before Parliament

24th July 2001

Coming into force

15th October 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 1 and 120 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^f00001], having consulted as required by section 1(9), makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the High Court and County Courts Jurisdiction (Amendment No. 2) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 15th October 2001.

## 2. Amendment to the High Court and County Courts Jurisdiction Order 1991

After article 8A of the High Court and County Courts Jurisdiction Order 1991[^f00002], there shall be inserted the following:

> **8B. Enforcement of possession orders against trespassers**
>
> (1) A judgment or order of a county court for possession of land made in a possession claim against trespassers may be enforced in the High Court or a county court.
>
> (2) In this article “a possession claim against trespassers” has the same meaning as in Part 55 of the Civil Procedure Rules 1998[^f00003].

Irvine of Lairg, *C.*

Dated 24th July 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the High Court and County Courts Jurisdiction Order 1991 so as to provide that a judgment or order of a county court for possession of land made in a possession claim against trespassers under Part 55 of the Civil Procedure Rules 1998 may be enforced in the High Court or a county court.
