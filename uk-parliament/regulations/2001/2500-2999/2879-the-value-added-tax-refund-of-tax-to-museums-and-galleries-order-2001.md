# The Value Added Tax (Refund of Tax to Museums and Galleries) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2879

VALUE ADDED TAX

The Value Added Tax (Refund of Tax to Museums and Galleries) Order 2001

Made

9th August 2001

Laid before the House of Commons

9th August 2001

Coming into force

1st September 2001

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 33A(9) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Refund of Tax to Museums and Galleries) Order 2001 and comes into force on 1st September 2001.

**2.** Section 33A of the Value Added Tax Act 1994 applies to the bodies listed in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order.

**3.** The museums and galleries listed in column 2 of the Schedule are, for the purposes of section 33A of the Value Added Tax Act 1994, relevant museums and galleries in relation to the bodies listed opposite them in the Schedule.

**4.** Where column 3 of the Schedule indicates a particular date, in the case of the museum or gallery to be found opposite that date, section 33A of the Value Added Tax Act 1994 shall apply as if, in subsection (1)(c) of that section, that date were substituted for 1st April 2001.

John Heppell

Graham Stringer

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

9th August 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which comes into force on 1st September 2001, names (in column 1 of the Schedule) the bodies entitled to claim refunds of VAT under section 33A of the Value Added Tax Act [1994 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23) (“the Act”). Column 2 of the Schedule specifies the museums and galleries to which such claims shall relate.

Section 33A(1)(c) of the Act provides that section 33A shall apply where a supply, acquisition or importation is made to a body on or after 1st April 2001, unless Column 3 specifies a later date in relation to a particular museum or gallery.
