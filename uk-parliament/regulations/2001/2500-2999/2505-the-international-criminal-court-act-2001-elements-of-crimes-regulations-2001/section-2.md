## 2 (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note). The Elements of Crimes (Extent: E+W+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f65485593d7d2f54b50fbfc32d392b79][^key-0e87e16c225dc3ac02c6fbda9d2ab953][^key-0e87e16c225dc3ac02c6fbda9d2ab953]
