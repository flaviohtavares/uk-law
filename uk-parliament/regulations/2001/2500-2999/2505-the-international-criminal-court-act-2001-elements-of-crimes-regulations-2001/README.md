(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, made under the International Criminal Court Act 2001, set out the Elements of Crimes contained in the Report of the Preparatory Commission for the International Criminal Court adopted on 30th June 2000. In accordance with section 50(2) of the Act these shall be taken into account by a court considering offences under domestic law in Part 5 of the Act.
