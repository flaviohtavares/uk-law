# The Education (Student Support) (Dance and Drama) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2893

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Student Support) (Dance and Drama) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

Made

4th August 2001

Laid before Parliament

10th August 2001

Coming into force

1st September 2001

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 22(1) and (2), 42(6) and 43(1) of the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Support) (Dance and Drama) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 1st September 2001.

**2.** The Education (Student Support) (Dance and Drama) Regulations 1999[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

**3.** In regulation 4(d)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (iii) for “College of the Royal Academy of Dancing” substitute “Royal Academy of Dance”[^f00003]; and
- (b) delete “(vii) London Contemporary Dance School;”.

Margaret Hodge

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

4th August 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which come into force on 1st September 2001, amend the list of institutions providing designated courses as set out in regulation 4 of the Education (Student Support) (Dance and Drama) Regulations 1999.
