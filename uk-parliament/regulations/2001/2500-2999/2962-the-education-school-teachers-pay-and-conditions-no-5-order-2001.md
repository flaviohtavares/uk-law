# The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 5) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2962

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 5) Order 2001

Made

23rd August 2001

Laid before Parliament

29th August 2001

Coming into force

1st September 2001

Whereas the review body appointed under section 1(1) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991[^f00001] (“the Act”) have made a report following the reference to them of certain matters;

And whereas the Secretary of State has consulted, in accordance with section 2(1) of the Act[^f00002], such associations of local education authorities, such bodies representing the interests of governors of foundation, voluntary, and foundation special schools and such bodies representing school teachers as appeared to her to be concerned;

Now, therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1), (3) and (4) and 5(4) of the Act[^f00003] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and revocation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 5) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st September 2001.

(2) The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 3) Order 2000[^f00004], the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 4) Order 2000[^f00005], the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 2001[^f00006], the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 2) Order 2001[^f00007], the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 3) Order 2001[^f00008] and the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 4) Order 2001[^f00009] are hereby revoked.

## 2. Application

This Order applies to school teachers (as defined in section 5(1) and (2) of the Act[^f00010] in England and Wales.

## 3. School Teachers' Pay and Conditions

It is hereby directed that the provisions set out in the document published by Her Majesty’s Stationery Office and entitled “School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2001”[^f00011] shall have effect from 1st September 2001.

Stephen Timms

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

23rd August 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order which is made under section 2(1) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (“the Act”) refers to a Document entitled “School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2001” (“the Document”) published by Her Majesty’s Stationery Office, which is obtainable from Government book shops and through booksellers.

It revokes the Orders giving effect to and amending the previous School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document as well as an Order which incorrectly referred to a School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2001 which had not in fact been published at the time the Order was made.

The Order directs that the provisions set out in the Document shall have effect from 1st September 2001.

This Order applies to all school teachers (as defined in section 5(1) and (2) of the Act) in England and Wales. By virtue of section 2(6)(a) of the Act, the remuneration of school teachers to whom the Order applies is to be determined and paid to them by local education authorities in accordance with the pay scales and other provisions of the Document. By virtue of section 2(6)(b) of the Act, the provisions set out in the Document relating to the statutory conditions of employment have effect as terms of the contracts of employment of school teachers to whom the Order applies.
