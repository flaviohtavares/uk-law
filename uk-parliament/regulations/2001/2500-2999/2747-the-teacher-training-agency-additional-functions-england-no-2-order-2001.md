# The Teacher Training Agency (Additional Functions) (England) (No. 2) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2747

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Teacher Training Agency (Additional Functions) (England) (No. 2) Order 2001

Made

25th July 2001

Laid before Parliament

31st July 2001

Coming into force

2nd September 2001

Being of the opinion that the Teacher Training Agency[^f00001] may, having regard to their general objectives, appropriately discharge the additional functions hereby conferred and having carried out such consultation as appears to her to be appropriate pursuant to section 16(3) of the Education Act 1994[^f00002], the Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16(1) and 23(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Teacher Training Agency (Additional Functions) (England) (No. 2) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 2nd September 2001.

(2) This Order shall only apply in relation to England.

## 2. Conferring of additional function

The additional function of administering on behalf of the Secretary of State any scheme established by her for the payment of grants under the Education (Teacher Training Bursaries) (England) Regulations 2001[^f00003] is hereby conferred on the Teacher Training Agency.

Stephen Timms

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

27th July 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

This Order confers on the Teacher Training Agency the additional function of administering the scheme of paying grants to institutions providing initial teacher training courses for the purpose of providing training bursaries to students undertaking such courses. The scheme was established under the Education (Teacher Training Bursaries) (England) Regulations 2001 (S.I. [2001/2622](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/2622)).
