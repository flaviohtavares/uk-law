# The Employment Zones (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2521

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Employment Zones (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2001

Made

12th July 2001

Laid before Parliament

19th July 2001

Coming into force

13th August 2001

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 60 of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Employment Zones (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 13th August 2001.

## 2. Amendment of the Employment Zones Regulations 2000

The Schedule to the Employment Zones Regulations 2000[^f00003] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in relation to the employment zone of Brighton and Hove, after “the non-metropolitan district of Hove” there shall be added “and the district of Adur”.
- (b) in relation to the employment zone of Plymouth, after “The non-metropolitan district of Plymouth” there shall be added “and the districts of Caradon, South Hams and West Devon”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Nick Brown

Minister of State,

Department for Work and Pensions

12th July 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Schedule to the Employment Zones Regulations 2000 by adding, to the Brighton and Hove employment zone, the district of Adur, and, to the Plymouth employment zone, the districts of Caradon, South Hams and West Devon.

These Regulations do not impose a charge on business.
