# The New Addington Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2868

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The New Addington Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001

Made

3rd August 2001

Laid before Parliament

9th August 2001

Coming into force

1st September 2001

Whereas an application for the purpose of extending and varying an education action zone has been made to the Secretary of State with the consent of the governing bodies of every school participating in the zone.

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 10(2) and 138(7) of the Schools Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order shall be cited as the New Addington Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st September 2001.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “New Addington Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the New Addington Education Action Zone Order 1998[^f00002].

## 3. Extension of the New Addington Education Action Zone

The New Addington Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 1st September 2001.

## 4. Amendment of the New Addington Education Action Zone Order 1998

There shall be inserted after article 9 of the New Addington Education Action Zone Order 1998 the following article—

> **9A. Removal from office by Forum**
>
> A member of the Forum may be removed from office by the Forum in the following circumstances—
>
> - (a) in the case of a member who was a governor of a zone school at the time he was appointed to the Forum pursuant to either article 4(a) or article 5, if that person ceases to be a governor at that zone school, or a governor of the relevant category as the case may be;
> - (b) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to either article 4(a) or 5, if the school whose governing body appointed him, or to which he was connected, is discontinued;
> - (c) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 4(c), if the body in question is dissolved or otherwise ceases to exist; and
> - (d) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 5(2), if that person no longer appears to the Forum to represent the interests of either business, parents of pupils at zone schools, local statutory services, voluntary organisations, or the local community, as the case may be.

## 5. Variation of the New Addington Education Action Zone Order 1998

(1) The New Addington Education Action Zone Order 1998[^f00002] is varied as follows:

(2) The words “one person” in article 4(c) are deleted and substituted with, “either one or two persons”;

(3) After article 4(c) the following words are inserted, “4(d) the chair of the EAZ strategy group.”;

(4) Articles 5(c) and 5(d) are deleted and the following words are substituted—

> **5.**
>
> - (c) either three or four additional members from persons appearing to them to represent the interests of local statutory services, voluntary organisations, or the local community.

.
