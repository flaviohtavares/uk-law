# The Value Added Tax (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 3828

VALUE ADDED TAX

The Value Added Tax (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2001

Made

27th November 2001

Laid before the House of Commons

3rd December 2001

Coming into force

1st January 2002

The Commissioners of Customs and Excise, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 3(4) of, and paragraph 17 of Schedule 1 to, the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Amendment) (No.3) Regulations 2001 and come into force on 1st January 2002.

**2.** The Value Added Tax Regulations 1995[^f00002] are amended as follows.

**3.** In Schedule 1, for forms numbered 1 and 2 substitute respectively forms numbered 1 and 2 in the Schedule to these Regulations.

New King’s Beam House 22 Upper Ground London SE1 9PJ

Martin Brown

Commissioner of Customs and Excise

27th November 2001
