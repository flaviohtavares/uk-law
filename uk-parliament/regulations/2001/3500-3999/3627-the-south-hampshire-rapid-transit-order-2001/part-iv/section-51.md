# The South Hampshire Rapid Transit Order 2001, section 51

> Text as at 2003-09-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 51. Concessionary travel[^c-bb8a4cd270dcdd19e98df694bcfc4a3e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Sections 93 to 102 and 112 of the Transport Act 1985 (which make provision for travel concession schemes) shall apply to transit services, notwithstanding that such services may not be eligible services as defined in section 94(4) of that Act.
