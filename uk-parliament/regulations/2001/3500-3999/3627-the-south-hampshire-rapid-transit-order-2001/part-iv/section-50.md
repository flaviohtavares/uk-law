# The South Hampshire Rapid Transit Order 2001, section 50

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 50. Disapplication of duties respecting provision of transport services

Section 63(7) of the Transport Act 1985[^f00035] shall not apply to the undertaker in respect of transit services procured by it under this Order, and any service subsidy agreement relating to the operation of a transit service shall be excluded from section 89(1) of that Act.
