# The South Hampshire Rapid Transit Order 2001, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Saving for highway authorities

Nothing in this Order shall affect any power of a highway authority to widen, alter, divert or improve any highway along which a street tramway is laid.
