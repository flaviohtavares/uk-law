# The South Hampshire Rapid Transit Order 2001, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Planning permission: supplementary matters

Planning permission which is deemed by virtue of a direction under section 90(2A) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00027] to be granted in relation to works authorised by this Order shall be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)(a) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990.
