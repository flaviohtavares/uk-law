# The South Hampshire Rapid Transit Order 2001, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2001-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3627
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. No apparatus in tunnel without consent[^c-ff5f5a2c5f4a1af7e81b113816c99c6a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Notwithstanding anything contained in any other enactment, no person shall enter upon, break up or interfere with the tunnel or any part thereof for the purpose of placing or doing anything in or in relation to any sewer, drain, main, pipe, wire or other apparatus or executing any work except with the written consent of the undertaker and in accordance with such terms and conditions as the undertaker may determine.
