## 15. Construction of bridges and tunnels[^c-08f4121884a7c9e84ca31fddd596c28e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any bridge or tunnel to be constructed under this Order for carrying a highway over or under a tramroad shall be constructed in accordance with plans and specifications approved by the highway authority, but such approval shall not be unreasonably withheld.
