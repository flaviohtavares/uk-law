# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 110

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Interpretation[^c-ca892cec1db596a9529bff5c02b01290]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2(1) of the Income Tax (Life Assurance Premium Relief) Regulations 1978 (interpretation)[^f00106] in the definition of “industrial branch business” after “means” insert “any such business carried on before 1st December 2001 as was”.
