# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 151

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 151. Miscellaneous amendments[^key-13fff8cf94ca262d3f8027fe07a8e975]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For “section 34A regulations” substitute “equalisation reserves rules” in the following places—

- (a) regulation 3(3)(a);
- (b) regulation 4(1)(a);
- (c) regulation 5(2) (twice);
- (d) regulation 6(1), (2) and (4);
- (e) regulation 7(1)(a), (3) and (6);
- (f) regulation 8(1)(a), (2) and (4);
- (g) regulation 8A(3)[^f00143];
- (h) the heading to regulation 9 and paragraph (3) of that regulation;
- (i) regulation 10(1), (2)(a), (3) and (5);
- (j) regulation 12.

(2) This Article has effect in relation to periods of account ending on or after 1st December 2001.
