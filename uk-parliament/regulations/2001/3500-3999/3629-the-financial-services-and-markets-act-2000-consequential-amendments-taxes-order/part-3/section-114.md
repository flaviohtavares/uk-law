# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 114

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Notice of charge and payment[^key-8b6408d321ea5109d9cf4d474104d8a0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 4A(1)(a)(ii) (notice of charge and payment)[^f00109] for “a recognised self-regulating organisation, a recognised professional body” substitute “the Financial Services Authority”.
