# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 117

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117. Interpretation[^key-7d1c73b94bc711f59cf9251cd6094b27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2 of the Income Tax (Definition of Unit Trust Scheme) Regulations 1988 (interpretation) for the definition of “participant” substitute—

> - “participant”, in relation to a unit trust scheme, shall be construed in accordance with section 235 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000;

.
