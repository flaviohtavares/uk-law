# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 142

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 142. Miscellaneous amendments[^c-47ab38c2a7fc8be0e30a02719ae21109]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the following regulations of the Insurance Companies (Overseas Life Assurance Business) (Compliance) Regulations 1995 for “long term” substitute “long-term”—

- (a) regulation 2(1)[^f00134] (twice);
- (b) regulation 5(1A)(a)(i) and (ii)[^f00135];
- (c) regulation 7(1A)(a)(i) and (ii)[^f00136];
- (d) regulation 7A(2)(a)(i) and (ii)[^f00137];
- (e) regulation 8(1A)(a)(i) and (ii)[^f00138];
- (f) regulation 13(1A)(a)(i) and (ii)[^f00139];
- (g) regulation 14(1A)(a)(i) and (ii)[^f00140];
- (h) regulation 14A(2)(a)(i) and (ii)[^f00141].
