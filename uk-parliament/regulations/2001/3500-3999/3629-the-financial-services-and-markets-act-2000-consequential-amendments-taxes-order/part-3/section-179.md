# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 179

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 179. Tax credits on distributions to insurance companies[^c-8328538187294cc95cb167fce871de25]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 4(1) of the Individual Savings Account (Insurance Companies) Regulations 1998 (tax credits on distributions to insurance companies) for “long term business” substitute “long-term insurance”.
