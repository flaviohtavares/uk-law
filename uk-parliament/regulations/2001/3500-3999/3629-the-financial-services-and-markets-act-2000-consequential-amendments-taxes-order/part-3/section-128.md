# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 128

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128. Gross payments[^key-35e18c350470e43f1185f962a50e8192]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 4(l)(r) of the Income Tax (Building Societies) (Dividends and Interest) Regulations 1990 (gross payments)[^f00122] for “75(8) of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “237(1) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
