## 139. Cessation of individual member’s underwriting business—terminal loss relief[^c-97cd48c63aef1cbf4fb2bae3fbf69c11]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 14(2) of the Lloyd’s Underwriters (Tax) Regulations 1995 (cessation of individual member’s underwriting business—terminal loss relief) for “premiums” substitute “premium”.
