# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 141

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 141. Reporting of chargeable events[^key-f6c3f3a81c8281014bed647f4ee43078]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 13(2)(b) of the Retirement Benefits Schemes (Information Powers) Regulations 1995 (reporting of chargeable events) for “section 96D of the Insurance Companies Act 1982” substitute “sections 417(1) and 423 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 respectively”.
