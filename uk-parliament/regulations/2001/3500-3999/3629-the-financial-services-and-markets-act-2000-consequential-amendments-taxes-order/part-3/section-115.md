# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 115

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. The Taxes Management Act 1970: provisions to apply[^key-cf340d66521a2679f41209add6faa54c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Amend the Schedule (the Taxes Management Act 1970: provisions to apply)[^f00110] as follows.

(2) In the Table in Part I in the entry relating to section 25(4)[^f00111] in the second column for the words from “paragraphs” to “1995” substitute “paragraph 36(2) of Part III of the Schedule to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Exemption) Order 2001”.

(3) In Part II in section 25(4) (as modified) for the words from “paragraphs” to “1995” substitute “paragraph 36(2) of Part III of the Schedule to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Exemption) Order 2001”.
