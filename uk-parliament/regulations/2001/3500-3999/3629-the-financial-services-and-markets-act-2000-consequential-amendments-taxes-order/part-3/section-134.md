# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 134

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 134. A, B and C factors: introductory[^key-06f6884409b31c7030551bb63a8ea3cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 1 to the Debts of Overseas Governments (Determination of Relevant Percentage) Regulations 1990 in paragraph 1(1) (A, B and C factors: introductory) in the definition of “regional development bank” for the words from “for the time” to “1987” substitute “listed in Part I of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Exemption) Order 2001”.
