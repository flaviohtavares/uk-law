## 185. Definition of personal portfolio bond (applying to all policies or contracts whenever issued or made)[^key-30c18ae70645594c7124124e97e66d82]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 4(4)(c) (definition of personal portfolio bond (applying to all policies or contracts whenever issued or made)) for “75(8) of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “236 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
