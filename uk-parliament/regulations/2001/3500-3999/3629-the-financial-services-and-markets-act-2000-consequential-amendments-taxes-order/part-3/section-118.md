# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 118

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Interpretation[^c-024a62f0a06ed86c21d377c1864d8f91]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2 of the Stamp Duty and Stamp Duty Reserve Tax (Definition of Unit Trust Scheme) Regulations 1988 (interpretation) for the definition of “participant” substitute—

> - “participant”, in relation to a unit trust scheme, shall be construed in accordance with section 235 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000;

.
