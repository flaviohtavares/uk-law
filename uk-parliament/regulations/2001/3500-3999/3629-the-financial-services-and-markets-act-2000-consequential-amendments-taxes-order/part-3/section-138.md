# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 138

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. Specified assets[^c-da55ee7547ad13fc0dcfa3fd4c77e836]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 7(9) and (13) of the Exchange Gains and Losses (Insurance Companies) Regulations 1994 (specified assets)[^f00133] for “long term business” substitute “long-term insurance”.
