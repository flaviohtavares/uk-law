# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 188

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 188. Annuity contracts[^key-83c1b8291f77ada29a2991b615bc85b3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 7 of the Personal Pension Schemes (Information Powers) Regulations 2000 (annuity contracts) for “section 96D of the Insurance Companies Act 1982” substitute “sections 417(1) and 423 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 respectively”.
