# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 130

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 130. Interpretation[^c-54a9595b064af2a33928712fea0f5993]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)[^f00123] for the definition of a “society or institution” substitute—

> - a “society, person or institution” means a building society or a person falling within section 840A(1)(b) of the Taxes Act or a relevant European institution;

.
