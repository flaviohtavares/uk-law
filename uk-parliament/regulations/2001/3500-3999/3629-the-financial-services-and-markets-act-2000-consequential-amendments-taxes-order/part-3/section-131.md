# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 131

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Notification by society or institution of intention to operate accounts[^key-e83b7b36e6629c7824ed22cfd9804220]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 5(3) (notification by society or institution of intention to operate accounts)[^f00124] for “is authorised under the Banking Act 1987” substitute “falls within section 840A(1)(b) of the Taxes Act”.
