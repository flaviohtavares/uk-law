# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 174

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 174. Qualifying investments for an insurance component[^c-a0d74ec157e18bea036abe413d860588]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 9(3) (qualifying investments for an insurance component) for sub-paragraph (c) substitute—

> - (c) the policy evidences or secures a contract of insurance which—
>   - (i) falls within paragraph I or III of Part II of Schedule 1 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001, or
>   - (ii) would fall within either of those paragraphs if the insurer was a company with permission under Part 4 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 to effect or carry out contracts of insurance;

.
