## 137. Settlement and replacement of debts[^key-27d6f2c7a78dca8f6dddac23fa57e604]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2(4) of the Exchange Gains and Losses (Alternative Method of Calculation of Gain or Loss) Regulations 1994 (reduction in exchange gains and losses) for “long term” substitute “long-term” (twice).
