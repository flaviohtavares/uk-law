# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 143

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 143. Interpretation[^key-0078553c3a53941d98c078db132712a0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2 of the Capital Gains Tax (Pension Funds Pooling Schemes) Regulations 1996 (interpretation) in the definition of “unit trust scheme” for “75(8) of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “237(1) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
