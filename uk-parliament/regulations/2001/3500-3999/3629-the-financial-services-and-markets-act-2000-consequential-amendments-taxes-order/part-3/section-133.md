# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 133

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133. Miscellaneous amendments[^c-d49f5325a68191075ea3978ce6e6e209]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the following regulations after “societies” insert “, persons”—

- (a) regulation 3(b), (c) and (cd)[^f00126];
- (b) regulation 12(3)(a) (twice) and (d).

(2) In the following places after “society” insert “, person”—

- (a) regulation 3(cc) and (d);
- (b) regulation 4 (three times);
- (c) the heading to regulation 5 and paragraphs (1) and (3) of that regulation;
- (d) regulation 6(1), (3)(b) and (c) and (5)[^f00127];
- (e) regulation 7(1)(a) (twice) and (3)(b)(iii);
- (f) regulation 7A(1) (twice), (2), (3)(b)(i) and (ii) and (4)[^f00128];
- (g) regulation 7B(1), (2)(a) and (b) (twice);
- (h) regulation 8(1) (twice), (2), (3)(a) and (4);
- (i) regulation 9(1);
- (j) the heading to regulation 10 and that regulation (twice);
- (k) the heading to regulation 11 and that regulation;
- (l) the heading to regulation 12 and paragraphs (1) (three times), (2) and (3) (four times) of that regulation[^f00129];
- (m) the heading to regulation 13 and paragraphs (1) (three times) and (3) (six times) of that regulation[^f00130];
- (n) the heading to regulation 14 and paragraphs (1) and (2) (twice) of that regulation[^f00131];
- (o) regulation 15;
- (p) regulation 16[^f00132].
