# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 176

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 176. Account manager ceasing to qualify[^key-71281002cf8ed6a004dbe79631977e7c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 20(1)(d) (account manager ceasing to qualify)—

- (a) for “an institution authorised under the Banking Act 1987” substitute “a person falling within section 840A(1)(b) of the Taxes Act”;
- (b) in paragraph (i) for “be authorised under the Banking Act 1987” substitute “fall within section 840A(1)(b) of the Taxes Act”.
