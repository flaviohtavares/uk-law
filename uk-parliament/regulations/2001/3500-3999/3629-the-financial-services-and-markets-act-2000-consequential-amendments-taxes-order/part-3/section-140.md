# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 140

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 140. Prescribed percentage rates of return[^key-873b5cdba97a17ee1dd88c59bc57e6cb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 7(11) of the Insurance Companies (Taxation of Reinsurance Business) Regulations 1995 (prescribed percentage rates of return for the purposes of regulations 4 and 5)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “long term” substitute “long-term”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b)(i) and (ii) for “long term business” substitute “long-term insurance”.
