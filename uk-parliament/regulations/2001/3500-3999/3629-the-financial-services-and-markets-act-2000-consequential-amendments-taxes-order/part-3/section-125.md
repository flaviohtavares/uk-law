# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 125

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 125. Returns of information by plan manager[^c-87d78161272f986603b8f71dfd1f478c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 24A(3) (returns of information by plan manager)[^f00119]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for paragraph (iv) substitute—

  > - (iv) units in, or shares of, securities schemes and warrant schemes,

  ;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (a) for paragraph (vi) substitute—

  > - (vi) units in, or shares of, fund of funds schemes, and

  .
