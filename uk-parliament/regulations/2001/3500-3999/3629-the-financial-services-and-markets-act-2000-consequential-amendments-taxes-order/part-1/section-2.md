# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 2

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-10ffa02844582574980c54528bf2bbbb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Order—

- “chargeable securities” has the meaning given by section 99(3) to (12) of the Finance Act 1986[^f00002];
- “instrument” has the meaning given by section 122(1) of the Stamp Act 1891[^f00003];
- “period of account” has the meaning given by section 834(1) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988[^f00004].
