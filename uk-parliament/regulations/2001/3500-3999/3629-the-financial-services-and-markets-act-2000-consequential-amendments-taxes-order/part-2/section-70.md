## 70. Interpretation[^c-3aff09cb83accd2647581e87f7e183ca]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 288(1) (interpretation) in the definition of “collective investment scheme” for “same meaning as in the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “meaning given by section 235 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
