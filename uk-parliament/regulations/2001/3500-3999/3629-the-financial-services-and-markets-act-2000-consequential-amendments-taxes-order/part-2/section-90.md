# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 90

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Open-ended investment companies[^key-ca04140533fc551c7325d7aa734fcb6c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 152(6) (power of the Treasury to make regulations in relation to tax treatment of open-ended investment companies) for the definition of “open-ended investment company” substitute—

> - “open-ended investment company” shall have the meaning given by section 236 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000;

.
