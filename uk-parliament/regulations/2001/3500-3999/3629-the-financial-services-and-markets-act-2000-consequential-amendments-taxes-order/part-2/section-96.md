# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 96

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Miscellaneous amendments[^key-9352877cf30d640b84de420c4d7bab1d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the following provisions for “long term” substitute “long-term”—

- (a) paragraph 12(3) of Schedule 9;
- (b) the following paragraphs of Schedule 11[^f00103]—
  - (i) paragraph 3(b);
  - (ii) paragraph 3A(1)(b), (2), (3)(a) and (4);
  - (iii) paragraph 5(1)(a);
  - (iv) paragraph 6;
- (c) paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 15.

(2) In paragraph 3A(1)(a) of Schedule 11 for “long term business” substitute “long-term insurance”.
