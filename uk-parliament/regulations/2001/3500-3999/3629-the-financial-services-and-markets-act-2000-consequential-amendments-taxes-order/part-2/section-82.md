# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 82

> Text as at 2016-09-15.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Miscellaneous amendments[^key-16c1dfbce315721adf8daeb4bb09f265]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the following places for “premiums” substitute “premium”—

- (a)[^key-d6740e556f33ca65e55058a1435a3786] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) section 172(1)(b)[^f00094];
- (c) the sidenote to section 174 and subsections (1)(a), (2) and (3) of that section[^f00095];
- (d) section 182(1)(ca)[^f00096];
- (e) section 184(1) (in the definition of “ancillary trust fund”) and (2)(b)[^f00097];
- (f) paragraph 1(b) of Schedule 19;
- (g) the following paragraphs of Schedule 20—
  - (i) paragraph 4(1) and (2);
  - (ii) paragraph 5(1).
