# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 65

> Text as at 2016-09-15.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Options and forfeited deposits[^key-202ab51672bc3f4c0979c3ba7e97e985]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 144(8)(c) (options and forfeited deposits)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (i) for the words from “authorised” to “that Act” substitute “authorised person within the meaning given by section 143(8)”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (iii) omit “or institution” (twice).
