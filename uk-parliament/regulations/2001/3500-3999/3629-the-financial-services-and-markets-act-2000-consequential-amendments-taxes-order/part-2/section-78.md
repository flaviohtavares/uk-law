# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 78

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Insurance companies[^c-4a6770ab1f80e1f7e37d8f20a0e929b0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 168(7) (insurance companies)[^f00091] for the words from “any insurance” to the end substitute “the business of effecting or carrying out contracts of insurance and, for the purposes of this subsection, “contract of insurance” has the meaning given by Article 3(1) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001.”.
