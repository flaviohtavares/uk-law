> Text as at 2016-09-15.

## 109. Repeals and revocations in consequence of the amendments made by this Part[^c-fb5a5a546149b39e2fc7ef6e98631e13]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The enactments mentioned in the Schedule to this Order (which include provisions that are spent or are of no practical utility) are repealed or revoked to the extent specified.
