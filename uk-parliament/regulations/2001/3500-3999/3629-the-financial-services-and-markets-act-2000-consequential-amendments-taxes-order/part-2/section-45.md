> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Material interests in offshore funds[^key-822e4fa1461167d177636097894f1d6b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 759(1A) (meaning of collective investment scheme)[^f00049] for “same meaning as in the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “meaning given by section 235 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
