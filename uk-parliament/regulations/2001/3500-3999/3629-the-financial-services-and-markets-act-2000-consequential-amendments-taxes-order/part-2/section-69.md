# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 69

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Application of Act to recognised investment exchanges[^c-97d310ac70d022fc77dff920de63c432]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 285 (power to provide for provisions referring to The Stock Exchange to have effect in relation to recognised investment exchanges) for “of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “given by section 285(1)(a) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
