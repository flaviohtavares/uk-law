# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 86

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Provisions relating to the Railways Act 1993[^c-b5956b8f4a25977b588b7c81b418c4f1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In paragraph 19(7) of Schedule 24 (provisions relating to the Railways Act 1993[^f00099]: leased assets) in the definition of “securities” for “142 of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “74(5) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.

(2) This Article has effect in relation to disposals of securities made on or after 1st December 2001.
