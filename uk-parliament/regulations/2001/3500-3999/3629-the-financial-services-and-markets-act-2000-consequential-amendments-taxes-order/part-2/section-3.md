# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 3

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Returns and other information: stock jobbers' transactions[^key-734498ee9a40c2b0185eb18024c9c89b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 21(7) of the Taxes Management Act 1970[^f00005] in the definition of “broker” for “of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “given by section 285(1)(a) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
