# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 52

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Miscellaneous amendments[^key-d54d0f58de1509358141a89cc6bd6872]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the following provisions for “long term business fund” substitute “long-term insurance fund”—

- (a) section 333B(1), (2)(b), (3) and (9) (in the definition of “long term business fund”);
- (b) section 431(2) (in the definition of “long term business fund”);
- (c) section 432A(1)(a) and (9B) (in paragraph (b) in the definition of “investment reserve”)[^f00057];
- (d) section 432AA(2), (3) and (5)[^f00058];
- (e) section 438(1)[^f00059];
- (f) section 438B(1)[^f00060];
- (g) section 440(4)(e)[^f00061];
- (h) section 440A(2)(d)[^f00062];
- (i) section 440B(3)(a)[^f00063];
- (j) section 755A(1)(c), (5), (7) and (12) (in the definition of “long term business fund”)[^f00064];
- (k) the following paragraphs of Schedule 19AA[^f00065]—
  - (i) paragraph 2(1)(b), (2) (twice) and (3) (four times);
  - (ii) paragraph 3(1)(a), (2)(a) and (3)(a);
  - (iii) paragraph 4(1), (2) and (5) (in paragraph (b) in the definition of “investment reserve”);
- (l)[^key-c0eeb66eb62b6d174e75a1cdf9a67c58] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) In the following provisions for “long term” substitute “long-term”—

- (a) section 431(2) (in the definition of “long term business fund”);
- (b) section 432ZA(2)(a) and (b), (3) and (7) (twice)[^f00067];
- (c) section 432A(2)(f), (8)(a) (twice) and (b) and (9B) (in the first place where it occurs in the definition of “investment reserve”);
- (d) section 432AA(4)(d);
- (e) section 432C(1)[^f00068];
- (f) section 432D(1)[^f00069];
- (g) section 440B(4) (in section 440A(2)(a) treated as substituted);
- (h) section 444A(5)[^f00070];
- (i) section 463(1), (2) and (3)[^f00071];
- (j) section 466(2) (in the definition of “long term business”)[^f00072];
- (k) section 804B(4)(b) and (5)(b)[^f00073];
- (l) section 804C(13)(b)[^f00074];
- (m) the following paragraphs of Schedule 19AA—
  - (i) paragraph 2(3)(d) (in the second place where it occurs);
  - (ii) paragraph 4(2)(a) and (b), (4)(b) and (5) (in the first place where it occurs in the definition of “investment reserve”);
- (n) paragraph 5(5)(a) and (b) of Schedule 19AB[^f00075];
- (o)[^key-c53512598722a41acba6b0861790fec0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
