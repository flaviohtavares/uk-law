# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 15

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Insurance business: application of sections 43A to 43E of the Taxes Act[^c-b06ef52141101412f19854e7e0b315d7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 43F(1) (insurance business)[^f00014] for “Part II of the Insurance Companies Act 1982[^f00015]” substitute “Chapter 9 of the Prudential Sourcebook (Insurers)”.

(2) This Article has effect in relation to periods of account ending on or after 1st December 2001.
