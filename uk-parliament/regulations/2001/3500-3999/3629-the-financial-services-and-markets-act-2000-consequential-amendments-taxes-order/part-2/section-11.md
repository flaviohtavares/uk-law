# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 11

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Inheritance tax: power to amend definitions of “market maker”[^c-105ebb2e739b9f0ecfcf66364bd3ba1d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In sections 106(4)(a) and 107(4)(a) (power to amend sections 105(7) and 234(4) of the Inheritance Tax Act 1984) for “of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “given by section 285(1)(a) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
