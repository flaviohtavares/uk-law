# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 67

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Spreading of gains and losses accruing as a result of deemed disposal under section 212[^key-e2aebe3c00b6b21012872c8d4fe9ef01]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 213(5) (spreading of gains and losses accruing as a result of deemed disposal under section 212)[^f00087] for the words from “there” to “1982” substitute “an insurance business transfer scheme has effect to transfer business which consists of the effecting or carrying out of contracts of long-term insurance from one person (“the transferor”) to another (“the transferee”)”.

(2) This Article has effect in relation to any transfer under a scheme falling within section 105 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, including an excluded scheme falling within Case 2, 3 or 4 of subsection (3) of that section.
