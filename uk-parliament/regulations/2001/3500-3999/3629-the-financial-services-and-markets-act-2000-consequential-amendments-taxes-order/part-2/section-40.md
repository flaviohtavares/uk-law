# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 40

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Gifts of shares and securities to charities etc.[^c-2a98ab865293f5131b22ec86f812253c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 587B(9) (gifts of shares and securities to charities etc.)[^f00044] in the definition of “offshore fund” for “of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “given by section 235 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
