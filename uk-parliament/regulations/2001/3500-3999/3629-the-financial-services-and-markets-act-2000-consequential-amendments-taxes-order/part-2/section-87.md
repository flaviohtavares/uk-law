# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments) (Taxes) Order 2001, section 87

> Text as at 2016-09-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Miscellaneous amendments[^key-214314b5203e770a6e99d49b7aefcf2a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the following places for “premiums” substitute “premium”—

- (a) section 219(2), (4) [^key-7bddfae486dbddb575458336f725f9f4]...;
- (b) section 220(1) and (2)(b);
- (c) the sidenote to section 222 and subsections (1)(a), (2) and (3) of that section;
- (d) section 226(2) and (3);
- (e) section 229(ca)[^f00101];
- (f) section 230(1) (in the definition of “ancillary trust fund”) and (2)(a)(i).
