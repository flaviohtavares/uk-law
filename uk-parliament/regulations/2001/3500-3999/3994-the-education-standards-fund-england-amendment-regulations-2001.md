# The Education Standards Fund (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 3994

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education Standards Fund (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

Made

12th December 2001

Laid before Parliament

14th December 2001

Coming into force

7th January 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 484, 489 and 569(4) of the Education Act 1996[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Education Standards Fund (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 7th January 2002.

**2.** The Education Standards Fund (England) Regulations 2001[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

**3.** In regulation 5 for “5(a) and 6(a), (c) and (d)” there shall be substituted “ 5(a) and (c) and 6(a), (c) and (d)”.

**4.** In Schedule 1 the following sub-paragraph shall be inserted after sub-paragraph 5(b)—

> - (c) Support for research projects aimed at investigating, developing and facilitating routes for teaching assistants to qualify as teachers.

.

Stephen Timms

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

12th December 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education Standards Fund (England) Regulations 2001. They add a new purpose for or in connection with which grant is payable. The new purpose concerns research projects aimed at investigating, developing and facilitating the ways in which teaching assistants may progress to qualification as school teachers.
