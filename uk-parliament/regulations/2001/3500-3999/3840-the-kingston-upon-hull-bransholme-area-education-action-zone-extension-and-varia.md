# The Kingston upon Hull (Bransholme Area) Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 3840

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Kingston upon Hull (Bransholme Area) Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001

Made

28th November 2001

Laid before Parliament

3rd December 2001

Coming into force

1st January 2002

Whereas an application for the purpose of extending and varying an Education Action Zone has been made to the Secretary of State with the consent of the governing bodies of every school participating in the zone. Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 10(2), 10(3) and 138(7) of the Schools Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This order shall be cited as the Kingston upon Hull (Bransholme Area) Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st January 2002.

## 2. Interpretation

In this order—

- “Kingston upon Hull (Bransholme Area) Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Kingston upon Hull (Bransholme Area) Education Action Zone Order 1998[^f00002].

## 3. Extension of the Kingston upon Hull (Bransholme Area) Education Action Zone Order 1998

The Kingston upon Hull (Bransholme Area) Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 1st January 2002.

## 4. Variation of the Kingston upon Hull (Bransholme Area) Education Action Zone Order 1998

There shall be inserted after article 9 of the Kingston upon Hull (Bransholme Area) Education Action Zone Order 1998 the following article—

> **9A. Removal from office by Forum**
>
> A member of the Forum may be removed from office by the Forum in the following circumstances—
>
> - (a) in the case of a member who was a governor of a zone school at the time he was appointed to the Forum pursuant to either article 4(a) or article 5, if that person ceases to be a governor at that zone school, or a governor of the relevant category as the case may be;
> - (b) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to either article 4(a) or 5, if the school whose governing body appointed him, or to which he was connected, is discontinued;
> - (c) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 4(c), if the body in question is dissolved or otherwise ceases to exist;
> - (d) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 5(b), if that person no longer appears to the Forum to be an appropriate person to be a member.

28th November 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order extends the Kingston upon Hull (Bransholme Area) Education Action Zone for a further period of two years. The order varies the partners in the Zone, amends the names of two of the schools which comprise the zone and also gives the Forum an additional power to remove members in certain circumstances.
