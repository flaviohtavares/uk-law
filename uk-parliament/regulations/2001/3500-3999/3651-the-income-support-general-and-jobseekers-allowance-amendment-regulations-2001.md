# The Income Support (General) and Jobseeker’s Allowance Amendment Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 3651

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Income Support (General) and Jobseeker’s Allowance Amendment Regulations 2001

Made

8th November 2001

Laid before Parliament

16th November 2001

Coming into force

10th December 2001

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 123(1)(a), 135(1), 137(1) and 175(1) and (3) to (5) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001] and sections 4(5), 35(1) and 36(1), (2) and (4) of the Jobseekers Act 1995[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00003], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Income Support (General) and Jobseeker’s Allowance Amendment Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 10th December 2001.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Income Support (General) Regulations 1987 (S.I. [1987/1967](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1967)) and the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996 (S.I. [1996/207](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/207)) so as to amend the formula for the calculation of the weekly amount of a person’s housing costs to take account of the abolition of mortgage interest payable under deduction of tax.

These Regulations do not impose a charge on business.
