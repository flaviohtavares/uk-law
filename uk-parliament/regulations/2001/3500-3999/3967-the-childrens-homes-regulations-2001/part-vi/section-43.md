# The Children’s Homes Regulations 2001, section 43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 43. Revocation

The following Regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Children’s Homes Regulations 1991[^f00023];
- (b) regulation 2 of the Children (Homes, Arrangements for Placement, Review and Representations) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 1993[^f00024];
- (c) the Children’s Homes Amendment Regulations 1994[^f00025]; and
- (d) regulation 4 of the Children (Protection from Offenders) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 1997[^f00026].
