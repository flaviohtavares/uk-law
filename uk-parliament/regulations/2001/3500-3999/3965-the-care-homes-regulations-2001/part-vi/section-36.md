# The Care Homes Regulations 2001, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Offences

Regulation 43 shall have effect as if for paragraph (1) there were substituted the following paragraph—

> (1) A contravention or failure to comply with any of the following provisions shall be an offence—
>
> - (a) regulations 4, 5, 11, 12(1) to (4), 13(1) to (4) and (6) to (8), 14, 15, 16(1), (2)(a) to (j) and (l) to (n) and (3), 17 to 26 and 37 to 40, to the extent that those regulations have effect subject to Part VI of these Regulations;
> - (b) regulations 31 and 34; and
> - (c) the provisions referred to in paragraph (2) of regulation 32, to the extent that they apply to the registered person by virtue of that paragraph.
