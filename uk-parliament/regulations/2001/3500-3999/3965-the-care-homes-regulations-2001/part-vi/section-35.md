# The Care Homes Regulations 2001, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Review of quality of care

Regulation 24 shall have effect as if—

- (a) the system referred to in paragraph (1) of regulation 24 included monitoring at appropriate intervals the matters set out in Schedule 7;
- (b) in paragraph (2) of regulation 24, after the words “any review conducted by him” there were added the words “, or any matters monitored”;
- (c) in paragraph (3) of regulation 24, for the words “and their representatives” there were substituted the words “, their representatives, the parents of the children accommodated at the care home and, in relation to those children, the placing authorities”.
