# The Care Homes Regulations 2001, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Interpretation

In regulation 2, paragraph (1) shall have effect as if—

- (a) at the end of the definition of “service user” there were added the words “, or any child who is accommodated in the care home”;
- (b) the following definitions were added at the appropriate places—

  > - “placement plan” has the meaning given to it in regulation 12 (child’s placement plan) of the Children’s Homes Regulations 2001[^f00010];
  > - “placing authority” has the meaning given to it in regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of the Children’s Homes Regulations 2001;

  .
