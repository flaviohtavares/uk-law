# The London Underground (East London Line Extension) (No. 2) Order 2001, section 32

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32. Control of construction sites: appeals[^key-0ed1b5e6a9180cc1a405c8a90b1ea729]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Sections 60 (control of noise on construction sites) and 61 (prior consent for work on construction sites) of the Control of Pollution Act 1974 shall have effect, in relation to works carried out in exercise of the powers conferred by this Order, as if in subsection (7) of each section (appeal against failure to give consent or the giving of qualified consent) for the words “a magistrates' court” there were substituted the words “the Secretary of State”.
