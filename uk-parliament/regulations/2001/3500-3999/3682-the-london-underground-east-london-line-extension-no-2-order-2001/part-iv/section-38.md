# The London Underground (East London Line Extension) (No. 2) Order 2001, section 38

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 38. Maintenance of approved works etc.[^key-21ceb25f895142c84638140258aebca7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where pursuant to regulations[^f00027] made under section 41 of the 1992 Act (approval of works, plant and equipment) approval has been obtained from the Health and Safety Executive with respect to any works, plant or equipment (including vehicles) forming part of the railways authorised by this Order, such works, plant and equipment shall not be used in a state or condition other than that in which they were at the time that the approval was given unless any change thereto does not materially impair the safe operation of the railways so authorised.

(2) If without reasonable cause the provisions of paragraph (1) above are contravened, the Company shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

(3) No proceedings shall be instituted in England and Wales in respect of an offence under this article except by or with the consent of the Health and Safety Executive or the Director of Public Prosecutions.
