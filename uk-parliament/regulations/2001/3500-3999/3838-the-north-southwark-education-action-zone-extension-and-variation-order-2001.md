# The North Southwark Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 3838

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The North Southwark Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001

Made

28th November 2001

Laid before Parliament

3rd December 2001

Coming into force

1st January 2002

Whereas an application for the purpose of extending and varying an Education Action Zone has been made to the Secretary of State with the consent of the governing bodies of every school participating in the zone. Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 10(2), 10(3) and 138(7) of the Schools Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This order shall be cited as the North Southwark Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st January 2002.

## 2. Interpretation

In this order—

- “North Southwark Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the North Southwark Education Action Zone Order 1998[^f00002].

## 3. Extension of the North Southwark Education Action Zone Order 1998

The North Southwark Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 1st January 2002.

## 4. Variation of the North Southwark Education Action Zone Order 1998

There shall be inserted after article 9 of the North Southwark Education Action Zone Order 1998 the following article—

> **9A. Removal from office by Forum**
>
> A member of the Forum may be removed from office by the Forum in the following circumstances—
>
> - (a) in the case of a member who was a governor of a zone school at the time he was appointed to the Forum pursuant to either article 4(a) or article 5, if that person ceases to be a governor at that zone school, or a governor of the relevant category as the case may be;
> - (b) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to either article 4(a) or 5, if the school whose governing body appointed him, or to which he was connected, is discontinued;
> - (c) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 4(c), if the body in question is dissolved or otherwise ceases to exist;
> - (d) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 5(a), (b), (c), (d), (e), (f), (g), (h), (j) or (k), if that person no longer appears to represent the interests of either head teachers of the zone schools; staff at zone schools; parents of children of compulsory school age at zone schools; the voluntary, community and charitable sectors in the area served by the zone schools; the police and youth justice system; the careers service in the area served by the zone schools; the Social Services Department of the Council of the London Borough of Southwark; or arts organisations or education and business partnership in the area served by the zone schools, as the case may be;
> - (e) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 5(d), if that person no longer is a member of the Council of the London Borough of Southwark; and
> - (f) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 5(l), if that person no longer appears to the Forum to be able to further the objects of the zone.

**5.** (1) The North Southwark Education Action Zone Order 1998[^f00003] is varied as follows:

(2) In schedule 1 the words “Optimum Health Services National Health Service Trust” and “Capstan Limited” are deleted, and the words “WS Atkins Education”, “Community Health South London NHS Trust”, “The Children’s Society”, and “Shakespeare’s Globe” are inserted.

Stephen Timms

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

28th November 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order extends to the North Southwark Education Action Zone for a further period of two years. The order varies the partners in the zone and also gives the Forum an additional power to remove members in certain circumstances.
