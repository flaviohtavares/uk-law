# The Family Health Services Appeal Authority (Change of Name) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 3562

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Family Health Services Appeal Authority (Change of Name) Order 2001

Made

1st November 2001

Laid before Parliament

2nd November 2001

Coming into force

24th November 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 11(4) and 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Family Health Services Appeal Authority (Change of Name) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 24th November 2001.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Establishment Order” means the Family Health Services Appeal Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Order[^f00002];
- “the Regulations” means the Family Health Services Appeal Authority Regulations[^f00003];
- “the Health Service Commissioner Order” means the Health Service Commissioner (Family Health Services Appeal Authority) Order[^f00004];
- “the Special Health Authority” means the Special Health Authority established by article 2 of the Establishment Order.

## 2. Change of name of the Special Health Authority

The name by which the Special Health Authority is known is changed to the Family Health Services Appeal Authority (Special Health Authority).

## 3. Consequential and transitional provisions

(1) In the Establishment Order, the Regulations or the Health Service Commissioner Order for the name “Family Health Services Appeal Authority” wherever it is used the name “Family Health Services Appeal Authority (Special Health Authority)” shall be substituted.

(2) The change of name under this Order shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person, or
- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before, on or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to the Special Health Authority by its previous name and all instruments and other documents which refer to it under its previous name shall be construed as referring to it under its new name.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Hutton

Minister of State,

Department of Health

1st November 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order changes the name of the Family Health Services Appeal Authority to the Family Health Services Appeal Authority (Special Health Authority) and makes consequential amendments to the Family Health Services Appeal Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1995, the Family Health Services Appeal Authority Regulations 1995 and to the Health Service Commissioner (Family Health Services Appeal Authority) Order 1995. The Order also makes transitional provisions. This change of name is occasioned by the creation of a new body to be known as the Family Health Services Appeal Authority by the Health and Social Care Act [2001 (c. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/15) to discharge specified functions relating to practitioners providing services under Part II of the National Health Services Act [1977 (c. 49)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/49).
