# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Transitional Provisions) (Partly Completed Procedures) Order 2001, section 70

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Power to order restitution[^key-02a5afcdf04adcdc3d02fbd6b5fc0d13]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies where—

- (a) the Authority proposes to require a person (“P”) to make payments under section 384(5), pursuant to the power conferred on it by article 3 of the Civil Remedies Order;
- (b) the reasons for which the Authority proposes to take action relate to or arise from the same failure, misconduct or other contravention which was the subject of the incomplete disciplinary proceedings to which P was subject immediately before commencement;
- (c) the relevant SRO in relation to those incomplete disciplinary proceedings would have had power to order P to make payments corresponding to the power of the Authority under section 384(5) at the conclusion of those proceedings;
- (d) the expedited decision conditions are satisfied.

(2) In a case where this article applies, the Authority may give a decision notice under section 386(1) without having given a warning notice.
