# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Transitional Provisions) (Partly Completed Procedures) Order 2001, section 88

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Power of the Authority to make arrangements for the interim tribunal[^c-ff213ab3aef8a02c033c832218710b51]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Authority may make such arrangements as appear to it to be necessary or expedient for the purpose of establishing and maintaining the interim tribunal and in particular may—

- (a) enter into contracts for the provision of accommodation and any other facilities required by the interim tribunal;
- (b) pay the persons appointed to serve on the interim tribunal (including the President) such remuneration and allowances as it determines, having regard to the remuneration and allowances that were payable to persons falling within article 87(2)(a) before commencement.
