# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Transitional Provisions) (Partly Completed Procedures) Order 2001, section 73

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Public statement about registered person or individual[^key-ff6d3d5981bde8c30ca9ad5fd03b8c2d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies where—

- (a) the Authority proposes to publish a statement about a person (“RI”) under section 66(3)(b), pursuant to the power conferred on it by article 9 of the Civil Remedies Order;
- (b) the reasons for which the Authority proposes to take action relate to or arise from the same failure, misconduct or other contravention which was the subject of the incomplete disciplinary proceedings to which RI was subject immediately before commencement;
- (c) the relevant SRO in relation to those incomplete disciplinary proceedings would have had power to make a statement about RI corresponding to the power of the Authority under section 66(3)(b) at the conclusion of those proceedings;
- (d) the expedited decision conditions are satisfied.

(2) In a case where this article applies, the Authority may give a decision notice under section 67(4) without having given a warning notice.
