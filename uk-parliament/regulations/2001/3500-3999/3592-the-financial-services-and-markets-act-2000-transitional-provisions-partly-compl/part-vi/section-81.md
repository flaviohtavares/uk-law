# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Transitional Provisions) (Partly Completed Procedures) Order 2001, section 81

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Definition of “intervention measure”[^c-f48ab075eae167a2c953212e1ba013a9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter, an “intervention measure” means—

- (a) an Intervention within the meaning of Rule 2.1 and 2.2 of Chapter VIII of the IMRO rules;
- (b) an intervention notice given under Rule 9.3 of the PIA rules;
- (c) an intervention order imposed by Rule 7–12 of the SFA rules.
