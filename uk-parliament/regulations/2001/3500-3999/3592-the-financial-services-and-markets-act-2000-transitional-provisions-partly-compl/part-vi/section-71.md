# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Transitional Provisions) (Partly Completed Procedures) Order 2001, section 71

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Cancellation of Part IV permission[^key-30dc754f9249d12e09f0b65c1c35af20]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies where—

- (a) the Authority proposes to cancel a person’s Part IV permission under section 54;
- (b) the reasons for which the Authority proposes to take action relate to or arise from the same failure, misconduct or other contravention which was the subject of the incomplete disciplinary proceedings to which P was subject immediately before commencement;
- (c) the relevant SRO in relation to those incomplete disciplinary proceedings would have had power to expel P from membership at the conclusion of those proceedings;
- (d) the expedited decision conditions are satisfied.

(2) In a case where this article applies, the Authority may give a decision notice under section 54(2) without having given a warning notice.
