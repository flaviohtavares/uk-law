# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Transitional Provisions) (Partly Completed Procedures) Order 2001, section 32

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Proceedings under section 52A of the Friendly Societies Act[^c-3dd89e59d4366d8f671b9ee06c420d5a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), any proceedings pending before the court for an injunction under section 52A[^f00017] of the Friendly Societies Act lapse on commencement.

(2) Any court before which proceedings under section 52A of the Friendly Societies Act are pending at commencement may by order make provision for such incidental, consequential and supplementary matters (including as to costs) as are appropriate having regard to paragraph (1).
