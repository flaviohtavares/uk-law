# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Transitional Provisions) (Partly Completed Procedures) Order 2001, section 25

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Interpretation[^c-fe91ffe9289d87b7476cd36b0ab776fd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “the Mutual Societies Order” means the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Mutual Societies) Order 2001[^f00015];
- “Schedule 3” means Schedule 3 to the Building Societies Act;
- “Schedule 13” means Schedule 13 to the Friendly Societies Act[^f00016].
