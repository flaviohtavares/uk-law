# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Transitional Provisions) (Partly Completed Procedures) Order 2001, section 102

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Interpretation[^key-94cdf4c39719ca3afcad869a46ae16b8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- (a) references to section 97 are references to section 97 of the Financial Services Act (as it has effect by virtue of the 2BCD Regulations and ISD Regulations);
- (b) “the Financial Services Tribunal” means the Financial Services Tribunal established under Chapter IX of Part I of the Financial Services Act.
