# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Transitional Provisions) (Partly Completed Procedures) Order 2001, section 129

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Decisions taken by the Authority giving effect to building society tribunal determinations[^c-03560bd48af60ca55c5931569f264c96]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 122 applies (with appropriate modifications) where the Authority gives effect to a direction given by a building society tribunal as it applies in respect of directions given by a friendly society tribunal.
