# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Transitional Provisions) (Partly Completed Procedures) Order 2001, section 118

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Interpretation[^key-f8e68f9c879296a928df51fc6b7e7a21]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- (a) references to “the original Friendly Societies Act” are references to that Act as it had effect immediately before commencement;
- (b) references to “the amended Friendly Societies Act” are references to that Act as it has effect immediately after commencement by virtue of Schedule 18, the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Mutual Societies) Order 2001 or any other order made under the Act.
