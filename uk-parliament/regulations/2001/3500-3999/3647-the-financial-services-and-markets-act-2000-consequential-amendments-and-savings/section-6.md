# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Savings) (Industrial Assurance) Order 2001, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Section 16 of the Interpretation Act 1978

Nothing in this Order prejudices the operation of section 16 of the Interpretation Act 1978[^f00006] in the case of any right acquired, or liability or obligation incurred, before commencement under any provision of the Industrial Assurance Acts or the Northern Ireland Order.
