# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Limitations on exemptions for small and medium-sized companies in relation to accounts etc.[^c-a058b92dbaec0d780753df00aea17c7b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 247A of the 1985 Act (limitations on exemptions for small and medium-sized companies in relation to accounts etc.)[^f00018] is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1), for paragraph (a) substitute—

> - (a) the company is, or was at any time within the financial year to which the accounts relate—
>   - (i) a public company,
>   - (ii) a person who has permission under Part 4 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 to carry on one or more regulated activities, or
>   - (iii) a person who carries on insurance market activity;

.

(3) In subsection (2), for paragraphs (b) to (d) substitute—

> - (b) a person who has permission under Part 4 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 to carry on a regulated activity, or
> - (c) a person who carries on an insurance market activity.

.
