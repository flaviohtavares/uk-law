# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Limitations on exemptions for small and medium-sized groups[^key-c665e0908598f4be58b102736d033185]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph (2) of Article 256 of the 1986 Order (exemption for small and medium-sized groups)[^f00057], for sub-paragraphs (b) to (d) substitute—

> - (b) a person who has permission under Part 4 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 to carry on a regulated activity,
> - (c) a person who carries on insurance market activity.

.
