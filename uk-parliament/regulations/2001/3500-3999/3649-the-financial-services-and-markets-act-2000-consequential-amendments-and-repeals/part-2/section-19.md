# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Exceptions to rules relating to substantial property transactions involving directors[^c-ea32c2162231c1bb3244b430ad80bc5a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 321(4) of the 1985 Act (exceptions to rules in section 320: transactions through independent broker on recognised investment exchange)[^f00023], for “Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
