# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Purchase by company of its own shares: definition of “off-market” and “market” purchase[^key-705131b3ce24964b1485fb86f35650df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 163 of the 1985 Act (meaning of “off-market” and “market” purchase)[^f00013] is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (2)(a) for “Part IV of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “Part 6 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.

(3) For subsection (4) substitute—

> (4) “Recognised investment exchange” means a recognised investment exchange other than an overseas investment exchange.
>
> (5) Expressions used in the definition contained in subsection (4) have the same meaning as in Part 18 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.
