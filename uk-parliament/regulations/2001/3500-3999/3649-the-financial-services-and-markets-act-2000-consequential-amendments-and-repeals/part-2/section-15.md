# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Summary financial statements by listed public companies[^c-d3c51d3e5e9c2349b1106ebeb90407e5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 251(1) of the 1985 Act (summary financial statements by listed public companies)[^f00020], for the definition of “listed”, and the word “and” preceding it, substitute—

> - “listed” means included in the official list by the competent authority for the purposes of Part 6 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (official listing); and
> - “the official list” has the meaning given in section 103(1) of that Act.

.
