## 78. Exercise of powers in relation to Northern Ireland[^key-a5af413dcba18dcfedc0ec8f74b13610]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 88 of the Companies Act 1989 (exercise of powers in relation to Northern Ireland), in subsection (3), for paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) section 449 of the Companies Act 1985 and sections 86 and 87 above (restrictions on disclosure of information);

.
