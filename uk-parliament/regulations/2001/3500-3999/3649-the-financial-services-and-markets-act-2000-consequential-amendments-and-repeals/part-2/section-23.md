# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Punishment for destroying etc documents of an authorised insurance company[^key-025e0a88938e74527964bd41741811a0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 450 of the 1985 Act (punishment for destroying, mutilating etc company documents) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)[^f00029] the words “, or of an insurance company to which Part II of the Insurance Companies Act 1985 applies,” are repealed.

(3) After that subsection insert—

> (1A) Subsection (1) applies to an officer of an authorised insurance company which is not a body corporate as it applies to an officer of a company.

.
