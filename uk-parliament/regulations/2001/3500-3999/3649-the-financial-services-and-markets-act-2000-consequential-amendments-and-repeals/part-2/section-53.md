# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Limitations on exemptions for dormant companies[^c-f1cf0134c041ee80d43f2eca5f3e151c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph (3) of Article 257AA of the 1986 Order (dormant companies)[^f00058], for sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) substitute—

> - (a) a person who has permission under Part 4 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 to carry on one or more regulated activities;
> - (b) a person who carries on insurance market activity.

.
