# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Membership of holding company[^key-a98acfcadb00c1b6396a515385c7ffd8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 33 of the 1986 Order (membership of holding company)[^f00048] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3)(a) the words “(within the meaning of the Financial Services Act 1986)” are repealed.

(3) After paragraph (3) insert—

> (3A) In paragraph (3)(a) “recognised investment exchange” and “overseas investment exchange” have the same meaning as in Part 18 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

.
