# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Purchase by company of its own shares: definition of “off-market” and “market” purchase[^c-21644ae81156b27facdeec783c4cb37d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 173 of the 1986 Order (meaning of “off-market” and “market” purchase) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (2)(a)[^f00049] for “Part IV of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “Part 6 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.

(3) For paragraph (4)[^f00050] substitute—

> (4) “Recognised investment exchange” means a recognised investment exchange other than an overseas investment exchange.
>
> (5) Expressions used in the definition contained in paragraph (4) have the same meaning as in Part 18 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.
