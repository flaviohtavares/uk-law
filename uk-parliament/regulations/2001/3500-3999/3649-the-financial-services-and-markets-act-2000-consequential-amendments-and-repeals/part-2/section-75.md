# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 75

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Repeal of spent or unnecessary provisions[^key-56de63975db4cfa54700e5cbed28e6ba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of the Companies Act 1989 are repealed—

- (a) sections 72 to 77;
- (b) section 79;
- (c) section 81;
- (d) section 119(3);
- (e) section 156;
- (f) section 168;
- (g) section 169(1) and (4);
- (h) section 171;
- (i) sections 192 to 197;
- (j) section 200(1);
- (k) sections 201 to 206;
- (l) in section 213, subsections (5)(b) and (6);
- (m) section 214;
- (n) in Schedule 10, paragraphs 36 and 37;
- (o) in Schedule 20, paragraph 26;
- (p) Schedule 21;
- (q) Schedule 22; and
- (r) Schedule 23.
