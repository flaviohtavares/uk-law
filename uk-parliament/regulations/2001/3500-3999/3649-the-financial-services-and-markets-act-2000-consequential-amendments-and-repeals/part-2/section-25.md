# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Production of documents subject to customer confidentiality[^c-cb6ebdc8b3724575290ca5ce7bea3eb0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 452 of the 1985 Act (privileged information) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (3)[^f00031], for the words from “or the customer is a person on whom a requirement has been imposed under that section,” to the end of that subsection, substitute

> or the customer is—
>
> - (a) a person on whom a requirement has been imposed under that section, or
> - (b) a person on whom a requirement to produce information or documents has been imposed by the Secretary of State, or by a person appointed by the Secretary of State to conduct an investigation, under section 171 or 173 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

.
