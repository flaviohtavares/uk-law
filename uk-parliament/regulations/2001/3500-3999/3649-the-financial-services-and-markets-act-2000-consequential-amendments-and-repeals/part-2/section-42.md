# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. References to the Companies Orders in certain provisions to include references to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^c-30ecb23eed3ce3f74ee6deaa348f8f99]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 2B of the 1986 Order (construction of certain references to the Companies Orders)[^f00046] for “Parts IV and V of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “Part 6 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
