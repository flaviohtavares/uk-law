# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 59

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Exceptions to rules relating to substantial property transactions involving directors[^c-37d25215d4d873d42afe32e5ebdd0b9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 329(4)[^f00063] of the 1986 Order (exceptions to rules in Article 328: transactions through independent broker on recognised investment exchange), for “Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.”.
