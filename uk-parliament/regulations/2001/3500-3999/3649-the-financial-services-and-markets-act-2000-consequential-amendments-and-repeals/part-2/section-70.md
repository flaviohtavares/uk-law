# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Form and content of company accounts: small companies[^c-38b3531cb1e319511615fcc6af6bca39]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For paragraph 54 of Schedule 8[^f00073] to the 1986 Order (form and content of company accounts for small companies: listed investments) substitute—

> **54.** (1) “Listed investment” means an investment as respects which there has been granted a listing on—
>
> - (a) a recognised investment exchange other than an overseas investment exchange; or
> - (b) a stock exchange of repute outside Northern Ireland.
>
> (2) “Recognised investment exchange” and “overseas investment exchange” have the meaning given in Part 18 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.
