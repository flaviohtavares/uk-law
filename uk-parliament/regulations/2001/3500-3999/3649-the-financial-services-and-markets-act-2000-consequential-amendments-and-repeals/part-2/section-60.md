# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 60

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Duty of company to notify recognised investment exchange of certain matters concerning directors notified to the company[^key-6f6fbe8f1443ada14cd7e68a9b36a4a5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 337 of the 1986 Order (duty of company to notify recognised investment exchange of matters notified to it under Articles 332 or 336), after paragraph (3) insert—

> (4) In paragraph (1) “recognised investment exchange” has the same meaning as in Part 18 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

.
