## 90. Transfer of securities[^key-09d17d2e5b78f4a2c9a3b261d07a8d9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 207(1) of the Companies Act 1989 (transfer of securities), in paragraph (a), for “Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
