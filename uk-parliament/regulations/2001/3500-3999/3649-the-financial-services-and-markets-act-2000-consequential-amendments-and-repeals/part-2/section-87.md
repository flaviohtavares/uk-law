# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 87

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Indemnity for certain acts[^key-a34a61a879e92c7a0cc1d5a534b3d83a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 184(5) of the Companies Act 1989 (indemnity for certain acts), for the words from “exercising” to “procedures” substitute “to whom the exercise of any function of a recognised investment exchange or recognised clearing house is delegated under its default rules”.
