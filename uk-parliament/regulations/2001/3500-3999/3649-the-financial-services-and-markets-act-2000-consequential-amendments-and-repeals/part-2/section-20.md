# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Duty of company to notify recognised investment exchange of certain matters concerning directors notified to the company[^c-a153473292ac91e14b4034b4b9badc9d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 329 of the 1985 Act (duty of company to notify recognised investment exchange of matters notified to it under sections 324 or 328)[^f00024] is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1) the words “within the meaning of the Financial Services Act 1986” are repealed.

(3) After subsection (3) insert—

> (4) In subsection (1) “recognised investment exchange” and “overseas investment exchange” have the same meaning as in Part 18 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

.
