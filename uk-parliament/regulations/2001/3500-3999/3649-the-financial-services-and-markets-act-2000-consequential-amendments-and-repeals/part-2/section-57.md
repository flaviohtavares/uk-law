# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Other distributions by investment companies[^key-402b238269da89a887ad687d59f4bdc7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 273 of the 1986 Order (other distributions by investment companies) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (4)(a) the words “within the meaning of the Financial Services Act 1986” are repealed.

(3) After paragraph (4)[^f00062] insert—

> (4A) In paragraph (4)(a) “recognised investment exchange” and “overseas investment exchange” have the same meaning as in Part 18 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

.
