> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Application of Part 5 in relation to overseas investment exchanges and clearing houses[^key-ade8ad5e8f97293feb07cd4f08fc9d09]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 92(1) of the Companies (No. 2) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990 (power to make provision in relation to certain overseas investment exchanges and clearing houses)—

- (a) after “Department” insert “and the Treasury”; and
- (b) for “Secretary of State” substitute “the Treasury”.
