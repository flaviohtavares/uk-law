# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Other distributions by investment companies[^c-a0e9937ce7f40329c502cb4fc8b50af1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 265 of the 1985 Act (other distributions by investment companies)[^f00022] is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (4)(a) the words “within the meaning of the Financial Services Act 1986” are repealed.

(3) After subsection (4) insert—

> (4A) In subsection (4)(a) “recognised investment exchange” and “overseas investment exchange” have the same meaning as in Part 18 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

.
