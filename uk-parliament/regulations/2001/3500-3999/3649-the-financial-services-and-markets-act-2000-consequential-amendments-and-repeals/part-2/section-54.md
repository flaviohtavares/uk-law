# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 54

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Cases where exemptions not available[^key-429a73747a9edebafdeb61d6861193bb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (1) of Article 257B of the 1986 Order (cases where exemptions not available)[^f00059] is amended as follows.

(2) For sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) it was a person who had permission under Part 4 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 to carry on a regulated activity,

.

(3) After sub-paragraph (b) insert—

> - (bb) it carried on an insurance market activity,

.

(4) For sub-paragraph (d) substitute—

> - (d) it was an appointed representative, within the meaning of section 39 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000,

.
