# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 74

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Repeal of spent provisions[^key-1b2bb5fceb50032c7d7f85341c91e579]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of the Companies Consolidation (Consequential Provisions)(Northern Ireland) Order 1986 are repealed—

- (a) Article 22; and
- (b) in Part 2 of Schedule 1, the entries relating to-
  - (i) the Policyholders Protection Act 1975;
  - (ii) the Insurance Brokers (Registration) Act 1977; and
  - (iii) the Insurance Companies Act 1982.
