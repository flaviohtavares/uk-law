# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Power of Treasury and Secretary of State to make further provision for purposes of Part 7[^c-fb903eb8aea0cb546ad86cb2f7dbed5e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After subsection (3) of section 185 of the Companies Act 1989 (power of Treasury and Secretary of State to make further provision for purposes of Part 7) insert—

> (4) References in this section to the provisions of this Part include any provision made under section 301 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

.
