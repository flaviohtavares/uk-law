# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 92

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Repeal of spent or unnecessary provisions[^key-91d65f38833c681b2f4f6633ad2dee48]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of the Companies (No. 2) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990 are repealed—

- (a) Articles 24 to 26;
- (b) Article 54(3);
- (c) Article 91; and
- (d) Article 93.
