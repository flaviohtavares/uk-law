# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 99

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Indemnity for certain acts[^c-96da5d572b475518d640620a1cedab11]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 105(5) of the Companies (No. 2) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990 (indemnity for certain acts), for the words from “exercising” to “procedures” substitute “to whom the exercise of any function of a recognised investment exchange or recognised clearing house is delegated under its default rules”.
