# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Production of documents subject to customer confidentiality[^key-144cd0224372da0914a2348e6537c7cb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 445 of the 1986 Order (privileged information) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3)[^f00069], for the words from “or the customer is a person on whom a requirement has been imposed under that Article,” to the end of that paragraph, substitute

> or the customer is—
>
> - (a) a person on whom a requirement has been imposed under that Article, or
> - (b) an authorised insurance company on whom a requirement to produce information or documents has been imposed by or on behalf of the Secretary of State under Part XI of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

.
