# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 148

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 148. Disclosure of information concerning public service pension schemes[^key-a99b4946a21852df20a339d5cc471a9a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 172 of the Pensions Act 1995 (information in connection with public service pensions), in subsection (1)(a), after “section 62 of the Financial Services Act 1986” insert “or under section 150 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
