# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 157

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 157. Disclosure of information concerning public service pension schemes[^key-ce712fecf4a7233e1e3784bed5fa6429]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 164 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (information in connection with public service pensions), in paragraph (1)(a), after “section 62 of the Financial Services Act 1986” insert “or section 150 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
