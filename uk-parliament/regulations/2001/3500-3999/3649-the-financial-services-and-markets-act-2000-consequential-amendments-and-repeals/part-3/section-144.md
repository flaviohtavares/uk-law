## 144. Salary related occupational pension schemes: discharge of liabilities by insurance etc[^c-794fe05484129a63aee3b3203053e4bf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 74 of the Pensions Act 1995 (discharge of liabilities by insurance etc.), in subsection (3)(c), for “insurance companies” substitute “insurers”.
