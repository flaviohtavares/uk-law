## 107. Authorised providers in relation to superannuation schemes for teachers[^c-70752d581ee76c8e936fe59ed2057d4f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 9(6) of the Superannuation Act 1972 (superannuation schemes as respects teachers) for the definition of “authorised provider”[^f00100] substitute—

> - “authorised provider” has the meaning given in section 1;

.
