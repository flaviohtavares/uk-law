# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 109

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 109. Information in connection with police officers' pensions[^key-e0a1b27a368a5d7a7dd891c42e0b4367]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 8A[^f00102] of the Police Pensions Act 1976 (information in connection with police pensions), in subsection (2)(a), after “section 62 of the Financial Services Act 1986” insert “or section 150 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
