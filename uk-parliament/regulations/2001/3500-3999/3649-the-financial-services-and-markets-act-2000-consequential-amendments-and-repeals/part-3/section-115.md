# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 115

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. Repeal of spent amendments[^key-eb06c6850468f40456e7a93422fc4f60]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of Schedule 6 to the Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993 (which are amendments to other legislation that are spent as a result of the coming into force of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000) are repealed—

- (a) paragraph 41;
- (b) paragraph 64;
- (c) paragraph 65; and
- (d) paragraph 68.
