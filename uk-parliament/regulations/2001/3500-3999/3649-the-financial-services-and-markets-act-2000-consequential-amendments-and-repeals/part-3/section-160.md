# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 160

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 160. Pension credits: qualifying arrangements[^key-499363c1c0003ff325db0c50363dec95]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph 6 of Schedule 5 to the Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 (pension credits: qualifying arrangements) is amended as follows.

(2) In sub-paragraph (2)—

- (a) for “insurance company” substitute “insurer”; and
- (b) in paragraph (a), the word “ordinary” is repealed.

(3) For sub-paragraph (3) substitute—

> (3) “Insurer” and “long-term insurance business” have the meaning given in section 175A of the Pension Schemes Act.

.
