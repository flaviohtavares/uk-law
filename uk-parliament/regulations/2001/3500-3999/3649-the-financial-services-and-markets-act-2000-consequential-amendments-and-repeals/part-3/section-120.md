## 120. Entitlement to guaranteed minimum pensions: treatment of entitlements[^c-e7e098a3b2d45bb06a73097c2739b768]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 47(3)(d) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 (Further provisions concerning entitlement to guaranteed minimum pensions for the purposes of section 46), for “Policyholders Protection Act 1975” substitute “Financial Services Compensation Scheme”.
