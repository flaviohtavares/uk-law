# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 156

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 156. Disclosure of information to Compensation Board[^c-44ae811f89c8d65ab9f1bec63f91ea30]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 112(7) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (bodies which may disclose information to the Compensation Board)—

- (a) sub-paragraphs (f) and (i) are repealed;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (k), insert—

  > - (ka) the Financial Services Authority;
  > - (kb) a body corporate established in accordance with section 212(1) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Services Compensation Scheme: the scheme manager);

  .
