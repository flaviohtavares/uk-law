# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 116

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Discharge of liability where guaranteed minimum pension secured by insurance policies or annuity contracts[^key-f95b2ca815107f6a7d100fcdd5814714]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 19 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 (discharge of liability where guaranteed minimum pension secured by insurance policies or annuity contracts) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (4)(a)—

- (a) for “insurance company” substitute “insurer”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (i), the word “ordinary” is repealed.

(3) Subsection (7) is repealed.
