# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 131

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Discharge of protected rights on winding up: insurance policies[^key-4379e04e694377e93576770ed556f752]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 28A of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 (discharge of protected rights on winding up: insurance policies), in subsection (4)—

- (a) for “insurance company” substitute “insurer”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (i), the word “ordinary” and the words “(within the meaning of the Insurance Companies Act 1982)” are repealed.
