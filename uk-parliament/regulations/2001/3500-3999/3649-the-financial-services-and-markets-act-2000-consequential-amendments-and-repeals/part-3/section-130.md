## 130. Ways of giving effect to the protected rights: the pension and annuity requirements[^c-aa07fe360c8e7b8e8755d68884d82be0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 25 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 (the pension and annuity requirements) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (3)(b) for “insurance company” substitute “insurer”.

(3) In subsection (4), for “insurance company”, in both places, substitute “insurer”.
