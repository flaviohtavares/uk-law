## 113. Retirement date for holders of judicial offices etc: repeal of references to old tribunals[^key-16956793f9cb4f3d27856b30e32ddb3b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 26(8) of the Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993 (retirement date for members of certain tribunals), paragraphs (e), (f) and (j) are repealed.
