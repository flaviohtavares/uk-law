# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 264

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 264. Functions to be exercised by the Authority[^key-399cc0f8db2cb276ebe0df6d35e919cf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Lloyd’s Act 1871 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 35 (salvage operations as to wreck of Lutine) for “the Treasury”[^f00178] substitute “the Financial Services Authority”.

(3) In section 39 (agreements for incorporation of other Societies, &c) for “the Treasury”[^f00179] substitute “the Financial Services Authority”.
