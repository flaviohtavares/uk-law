## 333. Application of section 19 to employers' associations[^c-7bd6c544c34783b5ebd7ab79e842d6a9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 129(3) of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 (application of section 19 of that Act to employers' associations), the words “industrial assurance or” are repealed.
