# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 320

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 320. Law applicable to a contract of insurance[^c-dd7e52e9aca59b9ba119f34b33096c69]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 2 of the Contracts (Applicable Law) Act 1990 (conventions to have force of law), for subsection (1A)[^f00236] substitute—

> (1A) The internal law for the purposes of Article 1(3) of the Rome Convention is the provisions of the regulations for the time being in force under section 424(3) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

.
