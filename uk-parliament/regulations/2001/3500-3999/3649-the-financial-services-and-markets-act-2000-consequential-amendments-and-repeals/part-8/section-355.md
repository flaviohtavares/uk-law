# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 355

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 355. Transfer of engagements of registered social landlord[^c-8e9028942a07010581b91f3a9511dda7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 48(3) of the Housing Act 1996 (copy of transfer of engagements of social landlord which is an industrial and provident society under an order under section 46), for “appropriate registrar and registered by him” substitute “Financial Services Authority and registered by it”.
