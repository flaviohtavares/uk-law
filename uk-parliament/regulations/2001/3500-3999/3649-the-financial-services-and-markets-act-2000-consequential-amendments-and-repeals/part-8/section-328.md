# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 328

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 328. Restriction on disclosure of information[^c-78baf31fefd3af0445143816a4756fd7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For paragraph (e) of section 206(3) of the Water Industry Act 1991 (restrictions on disclosure of information) substitute—

> - (e) for the purpose of enabling or assisting the Secretary of State, the Treasury or the Financial Services Authority to exercise any powers conferred by or under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 or by the enactments relating to companies or insolvency;
> - (ea) for the purpose of enabling or assisting any inspector appointed under enactments relating to companies to carry out his functions;

.
