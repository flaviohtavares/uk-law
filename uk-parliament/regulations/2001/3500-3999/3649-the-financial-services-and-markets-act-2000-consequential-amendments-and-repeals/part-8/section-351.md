## 351. Registration of social landlords[^c-6605387fa974bf6379ab6ad4e324b461]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 3(3) of the Housing Act 1996 (recording of registration as a social landlord), in sub-paragraph (b), for “appropriate registrar” substitute “Financial Services Authority”.
