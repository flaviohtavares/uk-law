## 352. Removal of social landlord from register[^key-a2e74092e9ed2e4b86448b34c55af1d3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 4(6) of the Housing Act 1996 (notification of removal of society from register of social landlords), in sub-paragraph (b), for “appropriate registrar” substitute “Financial Services Authority”.
