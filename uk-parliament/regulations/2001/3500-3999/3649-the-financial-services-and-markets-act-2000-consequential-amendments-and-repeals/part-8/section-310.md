# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 310

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 310. Repeal of unnecessary provisions[^key-ecdb3dd4d36f4559032bd466f048e8de]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 22 of the Consumer Protection Act 1987 (application of provisions relating to misleading price indications to services and facilities) is amended as follows.

(2) Subsection (3) is repealed.

(3) In subsection (5), the definitions of “appointed representative”, “authorised person” and “investment business” are repealed.
