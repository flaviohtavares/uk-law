# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 342

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 342. Repeal of spent provisions[^key-370416eeeffc41455cd66e4a897d05d3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 1993 (which are spent) are repealed—

- (a) in Schedule 5, paragraphs 7 to 13; and
- (b) in Schedule 6, the entries relating to the Financial Services Act 1986 and the Banking Act 1987.
