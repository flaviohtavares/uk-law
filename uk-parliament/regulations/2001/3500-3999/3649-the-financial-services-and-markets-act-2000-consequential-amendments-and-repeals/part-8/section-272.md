# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 272

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 272. Registered securities to which section 1 of the Act applies[^key-e75498ea41a126bed3ae578bd4cb1c8c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1(4) of the Stock Transfer Act (Northern Ireland) 1963 (registered securities which may be transferred by simplified form) in paragraph (e)[^f00192], for “the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “Part 17 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
