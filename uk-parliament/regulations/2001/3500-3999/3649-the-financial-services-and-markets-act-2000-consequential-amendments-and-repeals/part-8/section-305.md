# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 305

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 305. Petition for winding-up by the Secretary of State[^key-203119cda8096a7f69c22a7d19dab86d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In subsection (1) of section 124A of the Insolvency Act 1986 (petition for winding-up on the grounds of public interest), for paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) any report made by inspectors under—
>   - (i) section 167, 168, 169 or 284 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, or
>   - (ii) where the company is an open-ended investment company (within the meaning of that Act), regulations made as a result of section 262(2)(k) of that Act;
> - (bb) any information or documents obtained under section 165, 171, 172, 173 or 175 of that Act,

.
