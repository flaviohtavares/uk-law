# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 336

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 336. Removal of unnecessary right of appeal[^c-953744f0c2182ac42f1e78b6c827d11d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 11(1)[^f00248] of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992 (right of appeal from certain tribunals on a point of law) the word “, 18” is repealed.
