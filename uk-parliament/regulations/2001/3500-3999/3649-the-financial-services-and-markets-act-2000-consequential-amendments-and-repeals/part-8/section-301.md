# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 301

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 301. Agreements to indemnify certain lenders in England and Wales: consultation requirements[^key-6b34b49428bf92b0e6b48e9f9bea1905]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 84(5)(a)[^f00229] of the Housing Associations Act 1985 (requirement to consult before approving form of agreements with building societies), for “Building Societies Commission” substitute “Financial Services Authority”.
