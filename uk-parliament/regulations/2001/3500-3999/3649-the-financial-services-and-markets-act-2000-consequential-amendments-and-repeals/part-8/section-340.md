# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 340

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 340. Penalties under implementing regulations[^key-78bd8ac9892c8f96aa4ec9e30ad85bef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 70(2)[^f00251] of the Criminal Justice Act 1993 (penalties under implementing regulations), for “under sections 39, 40 and 41 of the Banking Act 1987” substitute “punishable on summary conviction under section 177 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
