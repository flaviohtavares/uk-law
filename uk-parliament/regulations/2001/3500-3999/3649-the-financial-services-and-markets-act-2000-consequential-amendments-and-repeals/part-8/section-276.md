# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 276

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 276. Meaning of “securities”[^key-7154021ae5a43b0cd3a00a30e3efd184]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1(2)[^f00197] of the Local Authorities' Mutual Investment Trust Act 1968 (extension of Company’s power to invest: meaning of securities), the words “within the meaning of the Financial Services Act 1986” are repealed.
