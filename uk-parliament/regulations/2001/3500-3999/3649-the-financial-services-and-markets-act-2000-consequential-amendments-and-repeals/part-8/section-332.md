# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 332

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 332. Application of section 99 of Friendly Societies Act 1992 to trade unions[^key-37026ee3e27a18c82baae541f4c2e59b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 19(1)[^f00245] of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 (application of section 99 of Friendly Societies Act 1992 to trade unions) for “an industrial assurance company” substitute “a friendly society”.
