## 354. Notice of agreed proposals where registered social landlord insolvent[^c-4f99c708e318d09c96a17ca52d12b193]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 45(4) of the Housing Act 1996 (notification of agreed proposals where registered social landlord is insolvent), in sub-paragraph (d), for “appropriate registrar” substitute “Financial Services Authority”.
