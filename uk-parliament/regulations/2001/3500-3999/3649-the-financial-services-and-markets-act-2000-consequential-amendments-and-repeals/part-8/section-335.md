## 335. Consultation on tribunal rules[^key-cb65164f59fdf553bdffc2cdd1f83472]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 8 of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992 (consultation on tribunal rules) is amended as follows.

(2) Subsection (2) is repealed.

(3) For paragraphs (a) and (b)[^f00247] of subsection (3) substitute “with respect to any tribunal specified in Part 2 of Schedule 1.”.
