# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 356

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 356. Removal of definition of “appropriate registrar”[^key-793442e7a9b77f1fd9e9b16c57432878]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 57(1) of the Housing Act 1996 (definitions for the purposes of Part 1 of that Act) the entry relating to the “appropriate registrar” is repealed.

(2) In the Table set out in section 64 of that Act (index of defined expressions) the entry relating to the “appropriate registrar” is repealed.
