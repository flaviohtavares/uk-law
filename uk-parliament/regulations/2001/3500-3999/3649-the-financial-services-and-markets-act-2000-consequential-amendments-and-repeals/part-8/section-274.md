# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 274

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 274. Bodies no longer subject to investigation[^key-008b8c295f0091a2440b8b7cd9d1d5fa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 2[^f00194] to the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967, the entries in relation to the following are repealed—

- the Building Societies Commission;
- the Friendly Societies Commission; and
- the Registry of Friendly Societies (and Note 2, which relates to that entry is also repealed).
