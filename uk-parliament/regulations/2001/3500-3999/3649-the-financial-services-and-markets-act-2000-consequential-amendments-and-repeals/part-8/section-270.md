# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 270

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 270. Registered securities to which section 1 of the Act applies[^c-636f0d9f347d2e5dadd162cbf114ac25]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1(4) of the Stock Transfer Act 1963 (registered securities which may be transferred by simplified form) in paragraph (e)[^f00190], for “the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “Part 17 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
