# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 334

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 334. Concurrence required for removal of members of certain tribunals[^c-1bbb5ca77aab2a54decb121b2631d44d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 7(2)[^f00246] of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992 (tribunals in relation to which requirement of concurrence to removal of members does not apply) the words “,4” and “, 21A” are repealed.
