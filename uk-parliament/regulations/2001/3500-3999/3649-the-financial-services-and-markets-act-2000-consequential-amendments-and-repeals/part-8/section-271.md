# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 271

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 271. Meaning of “securities”: units of a collective investment scheme[^c-df36facc98286eb30420f64424c890f5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 4(1)[^f00191] of the Stock Transfer Act 1963 (interpretation), in the definition of “securities”, for “Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
