# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 287

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 287. Repeal of spent disqualifications[^key-e29420cffd5a92d583712f91c8e4a613]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 1[^f00209] to the House of Commons Disqualification Act 1975 (disqualifying offices) is amended as follows.

(2) In Part 2, the following entries are repealed—

- “The Building Societies Commission.”; and
- “The Friendly Societies Commission.”.

(3) In Part 3, the following entries are repealed—

- “Chairman of a designated agency within the meaning of the Financial Services Act 1986 if he is in receipt of remuneration.”;
- “Chairman of a transferee body within the meaning of Schedule 11 to the Financial Services Act 1986 if he is in receipt of remuneration.”;
- “Registrar or Assistant Registrar of Friendly Societies.”.
