# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 282

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 282. Insurance by local authorities against accidents to members[^c-a429b0b95472060a8a9c4fb10149c33b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 140 of the Local Government Act 1972 (insurance by local authorities against accidents to members) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)[^f00203] for the words from “insurance” to “1982” substitute “accident insurance”.

(3) After subsection (3), insert—

> (3A) References to accident insurance must be read with—
>
> - (a) section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000;
> - (b) any relevant order under that section; and
> - (c) Schedule 2 to that Act.

.
