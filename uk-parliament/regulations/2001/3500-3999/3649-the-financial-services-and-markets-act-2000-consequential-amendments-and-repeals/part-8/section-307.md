# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 307

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 307. Power to apply Parts 1 to 7 to former authorised institutions[^key-163bb2754e6008e89da8a20fb65ea0bb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 422(1)[^f00233] of the Insolvency Act 1986 (power to apply first group of Parts to banks etc), for “authorised institutions and former authorised institutions within the meaning of the Banking Act 1987” substitute “any person who continues to have a liability in respect of a deposit which was held by him in accordance with the Banking Act 1979 or the Banking Act 1987”.
