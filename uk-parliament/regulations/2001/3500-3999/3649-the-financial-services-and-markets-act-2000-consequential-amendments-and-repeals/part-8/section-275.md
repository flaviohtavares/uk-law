# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 275

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 275. Functions of administrative staff of certain tribunals subject to investigation by Commissioner[^c-b23bd534177a59ba29e8f52b0236863b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 4[^f00195] to the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967 (relevant tribunals for the purposes of section 5(7)), before the entry relating to the Financial Services Tribunal[^f00196] insert—

> - Financial Services and Markets Tribunal constituted under section 132 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.
