## 353. Appeal against decision in relation to register of social landlords[^c-e0fa3c0ce785f93e6e27d9ea7f8c3db9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 6(3) of the Housing Act 1996 (notification of appeal against decision concerning register of social landlords), in sub-paragraph (b), for “appropriate registrar” substitute “Financial Services Authority”.
