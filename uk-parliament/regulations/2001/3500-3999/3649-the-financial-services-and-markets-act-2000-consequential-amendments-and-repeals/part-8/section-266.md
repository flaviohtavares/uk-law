# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 266

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 266. Meaning of “legal proceedings”[^c-60a4c74010ef7df616fc907026258691]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 10 of the Bankers' Books Evidence Act 1879 (interpretation), in the definition of “legal proceedings”, for paragraph (c)[^f00181] substitute—

> - (c) an investigation, consideration or determination of a complaint by a member of the panel of ombudsmen for the purposes of the ombudsman scheme within the meaning of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

.
