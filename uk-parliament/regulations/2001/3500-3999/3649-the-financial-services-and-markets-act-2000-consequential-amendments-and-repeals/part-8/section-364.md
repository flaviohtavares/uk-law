# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 364

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 364. Repeal of unnecessary provisions[^key-be6380a0f84c4aeae60c56ea0e922497]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00259] are repealed—

- (a) in section 56(5), paragraphs (c), (d) and (e);
- (b) in section 57(1), paragraphs (e), (h) and (j);
- (c) in section 66(5), paragraphs (a), (c) and (e);
- (d) in section 68(2), paragraphs (b), (d) and (e);
- (e) in Schedule 1, paragraphs 32, 40 and 43; and
- (f) in Schedule 2, in paragraph 16(2), paragraphs (a), (b), (c) and (d).
