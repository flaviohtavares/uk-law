## 349. Determination of status of particular bodies[^key-1b7d0e9c17155e742ca47e09a6554877]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 64(3) of the Police Act 1996 (power of chief registrar of friendly societies to determine status of bodies for the purposes of that section) is repealed.
