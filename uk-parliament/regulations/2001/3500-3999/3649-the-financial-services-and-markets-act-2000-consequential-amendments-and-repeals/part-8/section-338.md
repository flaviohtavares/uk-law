# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 338

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 338. Removal of references to old tribunals[^c-8ea549c21c7d38fd0a3c6d3e1e458572]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 1 to the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992 (tribunals subject to the Council’s supervision) the entries relating to the following matters are repealed—

- Banking;
- Building Societies; and
- Friendly Societies[^f00249].
