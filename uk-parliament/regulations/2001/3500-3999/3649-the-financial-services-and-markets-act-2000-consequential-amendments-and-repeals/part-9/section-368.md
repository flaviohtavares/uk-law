# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 368

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 368. Interpretation of the 1974 Regulations[^key-c1bc60d9c6ae72c5ad25e9b4593f8980]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph 3 of regulation 1[^f00262] of the Local Authority (Stocks and Bonds) Regulations 1974 (Title, commencement and interpretation), in the definition of “negotiable bonds”, for “Part IV of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “Part 6 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
