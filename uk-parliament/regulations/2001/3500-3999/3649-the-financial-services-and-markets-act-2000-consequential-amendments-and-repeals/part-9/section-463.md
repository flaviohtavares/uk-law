# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 463

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 463. General representatives[^c-8d1433d69264244709939b1543d7b8d5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Schedule to the Insurance Premium Tax Regulations 1994, in Form 1 (Application for Registration)[^f00311], in question 3(a) for “who meets the requirements of section 10 of the Insurance Companies Act 1982” substitute “as defined in section 57(16A) of the Finance Act 1994”.
