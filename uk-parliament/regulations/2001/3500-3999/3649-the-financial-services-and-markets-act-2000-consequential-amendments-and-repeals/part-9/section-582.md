# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 582

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 582. Revocation of references to former tribunals[^key-eed64b1d64d7952fff79a0166b56cc3b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 1 to the Scotland Act 1998 (Functions Exercisable in or as Regards Scotland) Order 1999—

- (a) paragraphs 8 and 23 (which relate to the tribunal established under section 47 of the Building Societies Act 1986[^f00364]), and
- (b) paragraph 9 (which relates to the Financial Services Tribunal),

are revoked.
