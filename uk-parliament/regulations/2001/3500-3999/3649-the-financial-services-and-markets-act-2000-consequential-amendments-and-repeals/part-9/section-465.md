# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 465

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 465. Meaning of “policy holder”[^key-1437bdf152cee6f54bce77858865bb8a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 2(1) of the Friendly Societies (Accounts and Related Provisions) Order 1994 (interpretation), in the definition of “policy holder” for “the Insurance Companies Act 1982” substitute “any order made under section 424(2) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 and for the time being in force”.
