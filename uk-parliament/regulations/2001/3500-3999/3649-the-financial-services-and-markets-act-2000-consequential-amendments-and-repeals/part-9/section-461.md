# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 461

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 461. Revocation of references to person no longer performing regulatory functions[^key-2f4092f6cac577cf71cec32b51c758f1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 4(1)[^f00310] of the Drug Trafficking Offences Act 1986 (Crown Servants and Regulators etc) Regulations 1994 (designation of persons performing regulatory etc. functions)—

- (a) sub-paragraphs (b), (c), (d), (f), (g), (i), (j), (k) and (l) are revoked; and
- (b) for sub-paragraph (e) substitute—

  > - (e) a designated professional body within the meaning of section 326(2) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000;

  .
