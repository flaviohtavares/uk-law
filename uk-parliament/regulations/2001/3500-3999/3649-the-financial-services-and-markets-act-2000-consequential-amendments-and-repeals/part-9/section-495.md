# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 495

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 495. Information to be supplied on request[^c-c1f1538dd23e8bf484f5e278dbcb4db7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 46 of the Credit Institutions (Protection of Depositors) Regulations 1995—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “, an EEA institution and a non-EEA institution”, substitute “and an EEA institution”;
- (b) in paragraph (4)(a)(i), for “relevant UK scheme”, substitute “Financial Services Compensation Scheme”;
- (c) in paragraphs (4)(b)(ii) and (10)(b), for “a UK scheme”, substitute “the Financial Services Compensation Scheme”;
- (d) paragraphs (4)(c) and (d) are revoked;
- (e) in paragraph (8), for “relevant authority”, substitute “Authority”;
- (f) paragraph (9) is revoked;
- (g) in paragraph (10)(b), for “Chapter I or Part II of these Regulations”, substitute “section 214(5) of the 2000 Act and the Electing Participants Regulations”;
- (h) paragraphs (10)(c) and (d) are revoked.
