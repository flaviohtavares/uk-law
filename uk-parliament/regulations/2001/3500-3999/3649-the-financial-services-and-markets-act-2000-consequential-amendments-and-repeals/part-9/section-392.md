# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 392

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 392. Definition of services and facilities: investment business[^key-0d961ef32458ffba28e1b9412c314940]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Introduction to the Schedule to the Consumer Protection (Code of Practice for Traders on Price Indications) Approval Order 1988, in the definition of “Services and Facilities” the words “those provided by a person who is an authorised person or appointed representative under the Financial Services Act 1986 in the course of investment business,” are revoked.
