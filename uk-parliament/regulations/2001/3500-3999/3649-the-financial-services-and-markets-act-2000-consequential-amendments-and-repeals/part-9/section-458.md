## 458. Assets of Haiti: definition of “investments”[^c-a84df8f0f89b381a3c236ec97950d53c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph (4) of article 9 of the Haiti (United Nations Sanctions) (Channel Islands) Order 1994 (assets of Haiti)[^f00308], for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) “investments” means any investment of a kind specified by any relevant order made under section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 which is not a security.
