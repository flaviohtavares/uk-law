# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 570

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 570. Interpretation of the 1998 Order[^c-6e1654cb279476fe4dc14206bf9fe285]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph (2) of article 1 of the Bank of England (Information Powers) Order 1998 (citation, commencement and interpretation), in the definition of “securities”—

- (a) the word “relevant” is revoked; and
- (b) for “the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “Part 17 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
