# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 496

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 496. Information in explanatory literature[^c-900fa05efb65aa0cbf9d923e8966a11e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 47 of the Credit Institutions (Protection of Depositors) Regulations 1995—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “, an EEA institution and a non-EEA institution”, substitute “and an EEA institution”;
- (b) in paragraph (2)(a)(i), for “relevant UK scheme”, substitute “Financial Services Compensation Scheme”;
- (c) in paragraph (2)(b)(ii), for “a UK scheme”, substitute “the Financial Services Compensation Scheme”;
- (d) paragraphs (2)(c) and (2)(d) are revoked;
- (e) in paragraph (6)—
  - (i) in the definition of “relevant deposit”, for “, an EEA institution or non-EEA institution”, substitute “or an EEA institution”;
  - (ii) the definition of “the relevant UK scheme” is revoked.
