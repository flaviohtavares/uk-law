## 422. Assets of Serbia and Montenegro: definition of “investments”[^c-b388a163e298a4ef5cf3039ab4f55dc8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph 4 of article 10 of the Serbia and Montenegro (United Nations Sanctions) (Dependent Territories) Order 1992 (assets of Serbia and Montenegro), for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) “investment” means any investment of a kind specified by any relevant order made under section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 which is not a security.

.
