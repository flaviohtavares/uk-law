# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 455

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 455. Revocation of unnecessary provision[^c-a1a0f885d1aeec5deea66e19df53116d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 2[^f00305] of the Financial Services (Disclosure of Information) (Designated Authorities) (No. 8) Order 1994 (designation of authorities and specification of functions), the words “section 180(3) of the Financial Services Act 1986 and” are revoked.
