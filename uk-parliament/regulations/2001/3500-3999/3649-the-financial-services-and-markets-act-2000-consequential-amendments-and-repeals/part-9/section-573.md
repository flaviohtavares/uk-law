## 573. Assets of UNITA or persons connected with UNITA: definition of “investments”[^c-b51449f95d3ac0544a06408b0ffbe026]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph (4) of article 7 of the Angola (United Nations Sanctions) (Isle of Man) Order 1998 (assets of UNITA or persons connected with UNITA)(a), for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) “investment” means any investment of a kind specified by any relevant order made under section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 which is not a security;

.
