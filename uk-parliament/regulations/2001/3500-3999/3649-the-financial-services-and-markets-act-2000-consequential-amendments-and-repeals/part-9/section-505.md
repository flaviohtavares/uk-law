# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 505

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 505. Specified kinds of investments[^key-fe537c4d68a29581cb49651f4d485904]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In paragraphs (4) and (5) of regulation 8 of the Public Offers of Securities Regulations 1995 (form and content of prospectus)—

- (a) for “securities falling within paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Act” substitute “shares”, and
- (b) for “securities”, in both places, substitute “shares”.

(2) In paragraph (4) of regulation 9 to those Regulations (general duty of disclosure in prospectus) for “a certificate or other instrument falling within paragraph 5 of Schedule 1 to the Act” substitute “a certificate or other instrument representing securities”.
