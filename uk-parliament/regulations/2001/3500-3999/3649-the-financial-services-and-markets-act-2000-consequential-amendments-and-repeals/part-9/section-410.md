# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 410

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 410. Enforcement of the 1990 Regulations[^key-218dc80a3058194c917111126e7e7f02]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 11 of the Insurance Companies (Legal Expenses Insurance) Regulations 1990 (enforcement) for “as a failure by it to satisfy an obligation to which it is subject by virtue of the Insurance Companies Act 1982” substitute “as if it were a contravention of a requirement imposed on it by or under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, and Part XIV of that Act shall apply in the event of any such breach”.
