# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 529

## 529. Notification by fund managers of any conflict of interest[^key-826d6c20307e35069baa1b2070f3b240]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 5 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Scheme Administration) Regulations 1996 (Manner and terms of appointment and removal of professional advisers), for paragraph (2)(b)(i)[^f00340] substitute—

> - (i) in the case of a fund manager, or a person carrying out functions referred to in regulation 2(c), who is subject to rules made under section 138 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (General rule making power) which impose requirements in relation to conflicts of interest, confirm in writing that he will notify the trustees or managers of any conflicts of interest to which he is or may be subject in relation to the scheme, in accordance with those requirements, and—

.
