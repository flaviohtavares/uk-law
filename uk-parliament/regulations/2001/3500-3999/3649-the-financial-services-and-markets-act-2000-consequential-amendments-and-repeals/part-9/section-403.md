# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 403

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 403. Powers to apply Parts 2 to 7 to former authorised institutions[^c-1e41ad233c50c8bc1305f854e4f9b8ec]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 366 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 (power to apply Parts 2 to 7 to banks etc.)[^f00291], for “authorised and former authorised institutions within the meaning of the Banking Act 1987” substitute “any company which continues to have a liability in respect of a deposit which was held by it in accordance with the Banking Act 1979 or the Banking Act 1987.”.
