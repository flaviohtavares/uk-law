# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 378

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 378. The Scheme Manager of the Financial Services Compensation Scheme[^c-8ad72a299d125561cde89cbeca53a06e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Rule 4.1 of those Rules (Voluntary winding up; winding up by court), in paragraph (1)(b), for “Deposit Protection Board” substitute “the scheme manager”.

(2) In Rule 4.72 (Additional provisions as regards certain meetings)—

- (a) in paragraph (2)[^f00273] for “to the Deposit Protection Board” substitute “to the scheme manager established under section 212(1) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”, and
- (b) in paragraphs (3), (4), (5), (6) and (7) for “the Board” substitute “the scheme manager”.

(3) In Schedule 1 (Deposit Protection Board’s Voting Rights) for “Deposit Protection Board” or “Board” wherever occurring, substitute “scheme manager”.
