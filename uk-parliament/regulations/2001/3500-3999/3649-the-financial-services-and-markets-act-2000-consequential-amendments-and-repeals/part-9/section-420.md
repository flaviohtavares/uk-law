# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 420

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 420. Actionable contravention giving rise to loss[^c-14ab1ac35c6c0bf2f5547371a05b5fb3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 2 to the Firemen’s Pension Scheme Order 1992, in sub-paragraph (1)(b) of paragraph F6A[^f00298] (previous service reckonable following actionable loss) after “section 62 of the Financial Services Act 1986” insert “or section 150 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
