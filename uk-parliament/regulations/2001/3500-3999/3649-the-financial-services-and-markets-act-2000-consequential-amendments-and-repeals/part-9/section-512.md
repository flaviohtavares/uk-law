# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 512

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 512. Revocation of spent provision[^c-82f15bde37027bbfdf2165dbfce2d5d8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of the Public Offers of Securities Regulations 1995 are revoked—

- (a) regulation 17 and Schedule 2 (amendments to the Financial Services Act 1986 and minor consequential amendments and repeals);
- (b) regulation 24 and Schedule 5 (amendments to regulations made under the Banking Act 1987);
- (c) Schedule 3 (offers of securities to the public in the United Kingdom).
