# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 560

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 560. Persons to whom information may be provided[^c-cf264d49cbc02a55691c4d775bc1eef4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph (2) of regulation 3 of the Police Pensions (Provision of Information) Regulations 1997 (persons to whom information may be supplied)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “section 44 of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “that Act”;
- (c) sub-paragraph (c) is revoked;
- (d) for sub-paragraphs (d) to (f) substitute—

  > - (d) a designated professional body within the meaning of Part XX of that Act;
  > - (e) the scheme manager within the meaning of that Act;
  > - (f) the Financial Services Authority;

  .
