# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 435

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 435. Winding up of an authorised deposit taker or former authorised institution[^key-ddc01c6e78edce449bb45a6727b22291]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In paragraph (1)(b) of article 10 of the Iraq (United Nations) (Sequestration of Assets) Order 1993 (Scope of Third Party Rights) for “or is the subject of a winding-up order under section 92 of the Banking Act” substitute “or is, or has been, an authorised deposit taker or is a former authorised institution, and is the subject of a winding-up order under section 367 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^f00300]”.

(2) In paragraph (3)(iv) of article 20 of that Order (restitution), for “or a winding-up order made against it under section 92 of the Banking Act” substitute “or is an authorised deposit taker or former authorised institution and has a winding-up order made against it under section 367 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.

## Annotations

[^key-ddc01c6e78edce449bb45a6727b22291]: Commencement: Art. 435 in force at 1.12.2001, see [art. 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/3649/article/1)

[^f00300]: Footnote: Article 12 of the [Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Transitional Provisions and Savings) (Civil Remedies, Discipline, Criminal Offences etc.) (No. 2) Order 2001 (S.I. 2001/ 3083)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/3083) provides that a former authorised institution may be treated as if it were a person who has been an authorised person for the purposes of s.367(1)(a) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.
