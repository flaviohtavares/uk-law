# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 536

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 536. Provision of information[^key-2974e4ea749379dedb2057c7a572f585]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Teachers' Superannuation (Provision of Information and Administrative Expenses etc) Regulations 1996 are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 1 (citation, commencement and interpretation), the definition of “the 1986 Act” is revoked;
- (b) in paragraph (2) of regulation 3 (provision of information)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a), for “the 1986 Act” substitute “the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (b), for “section 44 of the 1986 Act” substitute “that Act”;
  - (iii) sub-paragraph (c) is revoked;
  - (iv) for sub-paragraphs (d) to (f) substitute—

    > - (d) a designated professional body within the meaning of Part XX of that Act;
    > - (e) the scheme manager within the meaning of that Act;
    > - (f) the Financial Services Authority;

    .
