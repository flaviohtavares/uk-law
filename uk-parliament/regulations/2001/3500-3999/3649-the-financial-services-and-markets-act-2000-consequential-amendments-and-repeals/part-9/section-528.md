# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 528

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 528. Relevant occupational pension schemes[^key-2e3bceb07f8f8ea0939a91d87f6af675]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph (3) of regulation 3 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Scheme Administration) Regulations 1996 (Exemptions from the professional advisers requirements)—

- (a) the words “(within the meaning of the Financial Services Act 1986)” are revoked; and
- (b) for sub-paragraph (a) substitute—

  > - (a) relevant schemes of a kind mentioned in paragraph (4) of regulation 4 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Carrying on Regulated Activities by Way of Business) Order 2001[^f00339] (Managing investments: occupational pension schemes);

  .
