# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 563

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 563. Interest on late payment of certain benefits[^c-0744a005d0fcb6df021147f826fd6f96]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In regulation E34 of the Teachers' Pensions Regulations 1997[^f00358], for paragraph (5)(b) substitute—

> - (b) “the reference banks” means the four largest persons for the time being who—
>   - (i) have permission under Part 4 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 to accept deposits;
>   - (ii) are incorporated in the United Kingdom and carrying on there a regulated activity of accepting deposits;
>   - (iii) quote a base rate applicable to sterling deposits.

.

(2) After paragraph (5) of that regulation insert—

> (6) Paragraph (5)(b) must be read with—
>
> - (a) section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000;
> - (b) any relevant order under that section; and
> - (c) Schedule 2 to that Act.

.
