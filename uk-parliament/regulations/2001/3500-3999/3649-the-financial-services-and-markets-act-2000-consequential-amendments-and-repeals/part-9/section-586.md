# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 586

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 586. Restrictions on persons taking part in proceedings of the governing body or their committees[^key-e5a739a58abd280e7b1ad81c030e63ba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph 2 of Schedule 7 to the Education (School Government) (Wales) Regulations 1999 (pecuniary interests), for sub-paragraph (8) substitute—

> (8) In this paragraph, “securities” means any investments which are treated as securities for the purposes of any relevant order made under section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

.
