## 374. Requirements with respect to policies of insurance[^key-1821702d058e076fa2b091c3bffa5685]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 101 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 (requirements with respect to policies of insurance) is amended as follows.

(2) For paragraph (1) substitute—

> (1) In this Part “authorised insurer” has the same meaning as in Article 12.

.

(3) In paragraph (2) the words “referred to in paragraph (1)” are repealed.
