# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 517

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 517. Information to be provided in the case of a common deposit fund[^c-1594beac3b05bc40c830bfb889a0d93c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 2 to the Charities (Accounts and Reports) Regulations 1995 (Form and content of statement of accounts: Common investment funds and common deposit funds) is amended as follows.

(2) In Part III (Balance sheet), for sub-paragraph (c)(i) and (ii) of paragraph 3, substitute—

> - (i) deposits at the Bank of England;
> - (ii) deposits with a person who has permission under Part 4 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 to accept deposits;

.

(3) In Part VI (Interpretation) after paragraph 3 insert—

> **4.** In Part III of this Schedule, paragraph (3)(c)(ii) must be read with—
>
> - (a) section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000;
> - (b) any relevant order under that section; and
> - (c) Schedule 2 to that Act.

.
