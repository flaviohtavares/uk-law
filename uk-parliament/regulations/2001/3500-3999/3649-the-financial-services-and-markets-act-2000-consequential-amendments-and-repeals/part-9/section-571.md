## 571. Assets of UNITA or persons connected with UNITA: definition of “investments”[^key-f2409318759a49ef54e022fe711b2249]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph (4) of article 7 of the Angola (United Nations Sanctions) Order 1998 (assets of UNITA or persons connected with UNITA)[^f00359], for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) “investment” means any investment of a kind specified by any relevant order made under section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 which is not a security;

.
