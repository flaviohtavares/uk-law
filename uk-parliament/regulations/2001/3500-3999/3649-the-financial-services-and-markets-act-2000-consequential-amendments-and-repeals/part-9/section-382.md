# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 382

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 382. Mis-sold pensions[^key-b3cd601e369ffef145989886deb5c73e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation F11[^f00277] of the Police Pensions Regulations 1987 (mis-sold pensions) in paragraph (1)(b) after “section 62 of the Financial Services Act 1986” insert “or section 150 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
