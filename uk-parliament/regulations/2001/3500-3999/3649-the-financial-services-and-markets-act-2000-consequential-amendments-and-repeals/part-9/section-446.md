## 446. Interpretation of article 12: assets of Libya[^c-7ee3940f97869183b9d50d38508af778]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 12 of the Libya (United Nations Sanctions) (Channel Islands) Order 1993 (assets of Libya) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (5), for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) “investment” means any investment of a kind specified by any relevant order made under section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 which is not a security.

.
