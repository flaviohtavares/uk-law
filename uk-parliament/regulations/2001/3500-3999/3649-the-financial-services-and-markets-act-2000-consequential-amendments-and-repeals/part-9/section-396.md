# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 396

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 396. Exemption where the creditor is an authorised deposit taker[^c-97e51275702da5bd96224258cbc1729f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph (2) of article 2 of the Consumer Credit (Exempt Agreements) Order 1989 (exemption of certain consumer credit agreements secured on land) for “or a building society authorised under the Building Societies Act 1986, or an authorised institution under the Banking Act 1987 or a wholly owned subsidiary of such an institution,” substitute “or a deposit taker (within the meaning given by section 16(10) of the Act),”.
