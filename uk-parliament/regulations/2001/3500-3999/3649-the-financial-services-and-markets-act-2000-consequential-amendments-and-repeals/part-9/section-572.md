## 572. Assets of UNITA or persons connected with UNITA: definition of “investments”[^key-c310234b65c485db06d0dc9251572f73]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph (4) of article 8 of the Angola (United Nations Sanctions) (Channel Islands) Order 1998 (assets of UNITA or persons connected with UNITA)(a), for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) “investment” means any investment of a kind specified by any relevant order made under section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 which is not a security;

.
