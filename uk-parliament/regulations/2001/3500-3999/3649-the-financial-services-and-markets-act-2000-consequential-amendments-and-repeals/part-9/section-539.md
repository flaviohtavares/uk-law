# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 539

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 539. Exclusions from jurisdiction[^key-457149908a37b99560d91d5a44cff4d1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Personal and Occupational Pension Schemes (Pensions Ombudsman) Regulations 1996, for paragraph (1) of regulation 4 (exclusions from jurisdiction) substitute—

> (1) The Pensions Ombudsman shall not investigate or determine any complaint or dispute which can be dealt with under the compulsory jurisdiction of the ombudsman scheme provided for by Part 16 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, other than a complaint or dispute relating to the management of a personal pension scheme.
