# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 462

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 462. Interpretation of the 1994 Regulations[^key-770fcaeffa16668b167636a55fb39f55]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 20 of the Insurance Premium Tax Regulations 1994 (interpretation), in paragraph (2)(a)—

- (a) for “by section 17(1) of the Insurance Companies Act 1982” substitute “in accordance with rules made under Part 10 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”; and
- (b) for “in regulation 3 of the Insurance Companies (Accounts and Statements) Regulations 1983” substitute “in those rules”.
