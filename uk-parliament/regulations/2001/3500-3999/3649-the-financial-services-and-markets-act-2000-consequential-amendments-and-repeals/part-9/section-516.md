# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 516

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 516. Meaning of “collective investment scheme”[^c-dddd27ffc26dd0087a9845f1cd866573]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Part IV of Schedule 1 to the Charities (Accounts and Reports) Regulations 1995 (Form and content of statements of accounts—Notes to accounts), in paragraph 1(l)(ii)(C) for “section 75 of the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “section 235 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
