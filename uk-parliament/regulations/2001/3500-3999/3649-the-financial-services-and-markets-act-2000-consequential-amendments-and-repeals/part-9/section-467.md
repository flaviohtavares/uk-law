# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 467

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 467. Winding up of an insolvent partnership which is an authorised person[^key-5389b1d348fa5f1579f35c4a51c12b27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 19 of the Insolvent Partnerships Order 1994 (supplemental and transitional provisions), for paragraph (4) substitute—

> (4) Nothing in this Order is to be taken as preventing a petition being presented against an insolvent partnership under section 367 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, or any other enactment.
