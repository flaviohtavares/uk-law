## 459. Assets of Haiti: definition of “investments”[^key-0cd4147d193e5f13aa0f894a60796024]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph (4) of article 9 of the Haiti (United Nations Sanctions) (Isle of Man) Order 1994 (assets of Haiti)[^f00309], for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) “investments” means any investment of a kind specified by any relevant order made under section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 which is not a security.
