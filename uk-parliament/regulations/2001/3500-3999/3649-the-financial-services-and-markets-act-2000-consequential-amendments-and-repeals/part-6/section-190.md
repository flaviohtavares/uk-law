# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 190

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 190. Requirements for rules of registered societies[^key-999499f3a3cca41077e82bb00e2a7ede]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 2 to the Friendly Societies Act 1974 (matters to be provided for by the rules of societies registered under that Act) is amended as follows.

(2) In sub-paragraph (2) of paragraph 11, the words “within the meaning of the Insurance Companies Act 1974” are repealed.

(3) After that sub-paragraph insert—

> (2A) In sub-paragraph (2) “long-term business” has the meaning given by section 117(2) of the Friendly Societies Act 1992.

.
