# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 210

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 210. Revocation of redundant provisions[^key-cbf3c096094180897b275c18d1e4d8d9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following enactments are revoked—

- (a) the Friendly Societies (Fees) Order 1971 ([S.I. 1971/1900](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1971/1900));
- (b) the Friendly Societies Regulations 1975 ([S.I. 1975/205](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1975/205));
- (c) the Friendly Societies Act 1992 (Consequential Provisions) (No 2) Regulations 1993 ([S.I. 1993/1187](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/1187));
- (d) the Friendly Societies Appeal Tribunal Regulations 1993 ([S.I. 1993/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/2002));
- (e) the Friendly Societies (Qualifications of Actuaries No 2) Regulations 1993 ([S.I. 1993/2518](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/2518));
- (f) the Friendly Societies (Auditors) Order 1994 ([S.I. 1994/132](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/132))[^f00153];
- (g) the Friendly Societies (Insurance Business) Regulations 1994 ([S.I. 1994/1981](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1981));
- (h) The Friendly Societies (Authorisation) Regulations 1994 ([S.I. 1994/1982](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1982));
- (i) the Friendly Societies (Activities of a Subsidiary) Order 1995 ([S.I. 1995/3062](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/3062));
- (j) The Friendly Societies (Insurance Business) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 ([S.I. 1996/3008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3008));
- (j) the Friendly Societies (Activities of a Subsidiary) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/3009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3009));
- (k) the Friendly Societies (Insurance Business) (Amendment) Regulations 1997 ([S.I. 1997/966](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/966));
- (l) the Friendly Societies (Activities of a Subsidiary) Order 1998 ([S.I. 1998/2328](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2328));
- (m) the Friendly Societies (Activities of a Subsidiary) (No 2) Order 1998 ([S.I. 1998/2696](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2696));
- (n) the Friendly Societies (Insurance Business) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 ([S.I. 1998/3034](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/3034));
- (o) the Friendly Societies (Insurance Business) (Amendment) Regulations 2000 ([S.I. 2000/1700](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/1700));
- (p) the Friendly Societies (General Charge and Fees) Regulations 2001 ([S.I. 2001/816](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/816)).
