## 180. Advertising dissolution of society[^key-f20a1567aa42bc50729746c866d98750]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 58(6) of the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965[^f00126] (instrument of dissolution) the words “at the expense of the society” are repealed.
