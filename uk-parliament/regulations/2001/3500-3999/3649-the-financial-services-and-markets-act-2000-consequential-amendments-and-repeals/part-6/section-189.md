# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 189

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 189. Advertising dissolution of society[^c-160ca32a70f4b1915ab0567be614e31e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 94(7)[^f00133] of the Friendly Societies Act 1974 (instrument of dissolution), the words “at the expense of the society or branch” are repealed.
