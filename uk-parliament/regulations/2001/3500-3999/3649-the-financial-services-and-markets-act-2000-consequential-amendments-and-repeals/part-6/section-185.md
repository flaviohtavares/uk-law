# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 185

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 185. Interpretation[^c-897f3fa4e9b1a873ce348b83025ff3b8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 21 of the Friendly and Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1968 (interpretation), in the definition of “annual return”[^f00129] for the words “appropriate registrar” substitute “Authority”.
