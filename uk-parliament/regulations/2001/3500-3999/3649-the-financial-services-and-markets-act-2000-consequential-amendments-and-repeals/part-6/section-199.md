# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 199

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 199. Repeal of unnecessary provisions etc.[^key-f2aeafed73eee2272df2c7a4d9c5ec6e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 119 of the Building Societies Act 1986 is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1) after the definition of “ordinary resolution” insert—

> - “own funds” means own funds as defined in Section 1 of Chapter 2 of Title V of the Banking Consolidation Directive;

.

(3) In subsection (1) the definition of “currency” is repealed[^f00142].

(4) Subsections (2A) and (2C) are repealed[^f00143].
