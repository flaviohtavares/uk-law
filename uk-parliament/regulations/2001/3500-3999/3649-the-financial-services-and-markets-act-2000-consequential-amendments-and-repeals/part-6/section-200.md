# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 200

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 200. Application of companies insolvency legislation to building societies[^key-47e69c946f3935b38150dd1e012f94b1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Schedule 15 to the Building Societies Act 1986, in paragraph 3(2), before paragraph (a) insert—

> - (aa) every reference to a company registered in Scotland shall have effect as a reference to a building society whose principal office is situated in Scotland;

.

(2) In Schedule 15A[^f00144] to the Building Societies Act 1986, in paragraph 2(2) before paragraph (a) insert—

> - (aa) every reference to a company registered in Scotland shall have effect as a reference to a building society whose principal office is situated in Scotland;

.

(3) Paragraphs 209(b) and 210(b) of Schedule 3 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Mutual Societies) Order 2001 are revoked.
