## 181. Power to make regulations[^c-879c4eabe39ad5cf366ac7cb347af982]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 71 of the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965 is repealed.

(2) In sections 2(3) and 10(3), for the words from “in the prescribed form” to the end substitute “bearing the Authority’s seal”.
