# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 215

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 215. Revocation of redundant provisions[^c-cec601ebf56618b4bf95e5fe072fde90]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following enactments are revoked—

- (a) the Industrial and Provident Societies (Forms and Procedure) Regulations 1996 ([S.I. 1996/3121](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3121));
- (b) the Industrial and Provident Societies (Fees) Regulations 2001 ([S.I. 2001/813](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/813));
- (c) the Industrial and Provident Societies (Credit Unions) Regulations 1979 ([S.I. 1979/937](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1979/937));
- (d) the Industrial and Provident Societies (Credit Unions) (Fees) Regulations 2001 ([S.I. 2001/814](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/814)).
