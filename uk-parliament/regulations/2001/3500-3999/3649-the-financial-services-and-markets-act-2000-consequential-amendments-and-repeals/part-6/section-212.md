# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 212

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 212. Revocation of redundant provisions[^key-cb304fad41962ce78a1d8f52c273b9f9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following enactments are revoked—

- (a) the Building Societies Appeal Tribunal Regulations 1987 ([S.I. 1987/891](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/891))[^f00154];
- (b) the Building Societies (Designation of Prescribed Regulatory Authorities) Order 1988 ([S.I. 1988/630](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/630));
- (c) the Building Societies Appeal Tribunal (Amendment) Regulations 1993 ([S.I. 1993/983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/983));
- (d) The Building Societies (Auditors) Order 1994 ([S.I. 1994/525](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/525))[^f00155];
- (e) The Building Societies (Designation of Prescribed Regulatory Authorities) Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/2302](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2302));
- (f) The Building Societies (General Charge and Fees) Regulations 2001 ([S.I. 2001/815](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/815)).
