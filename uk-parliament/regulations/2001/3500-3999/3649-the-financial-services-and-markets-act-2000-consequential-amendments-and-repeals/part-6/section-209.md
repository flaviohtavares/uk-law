# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 209

## 209. Transfer of engagements: interpretation[^key-bd926df7bd33d19cab4e094f49de1c8b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph 18[^f00152] of Schedule 15 to the Friendly Societies Act 1992 for the words “the Insurance Companies Act 1982” substitute “Schedule 3 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
