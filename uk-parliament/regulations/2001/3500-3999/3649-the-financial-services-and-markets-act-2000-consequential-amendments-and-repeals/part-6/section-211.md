# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 211

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 211. Revocation of references to dissolved bodies, repealed legislation etc.[^c-4f0eb045eaf81cbc356d5cb1ab0f6feb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the Friendly Societies Act 1992 (Commencement No 3 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1993 ([S.I. 1993/16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/16))—

- (a) in article 1(2) (interpretation), the definitions of “assistant registrar”, “Chief Registrar”, “Commission” and “registrar” are revoked; and
- (b) articles 5, 7 and 9 (transitional provisions) are revoked.

(2) In the Friendly Societies Act 1992 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) Regulations 1993 ([S.I. 1993/932](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/932))—

- (a) in regulation 2 (interpretation), the definitions of “the 1987 Regulations”, “incorporated friendly society” and “registrar” are revoked; and
- (b) regulations 3 to 5 (transitional provisions) are revoked.

(3) In the Friendly Societies Act 1992 (Commencement No 6 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1993 ([S.I. 1993/2213](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/2213))—

- (a) in article 1(2), the definition of “Industrial Assurance Commissioner” is revoked; and
- (b) article 6 is revoked.

(4) Article 7 of the Friendly Societies Act 1992 (Commencement No 7 and Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 1993 ([S.I.1993/3226](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/3226)) is revoked.

(5) In regulation 9 (associated bodies) of, and paragraph 12 of Schedule 3 to, the Friendly Societies (Accounts and Related Provisions) Regulations 1994 ([S.I. 1994/1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1983)), for “Commission” substitute “Authority”.

(6) In the Friendly Societies Act 1992 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions) Regulations 1995 ([S.I. 1995/710](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/710)), regulations 3 and 5 are revoked.
