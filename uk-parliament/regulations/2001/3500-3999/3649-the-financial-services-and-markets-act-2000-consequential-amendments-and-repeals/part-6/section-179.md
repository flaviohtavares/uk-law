# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 179

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 179. Cancellation of registration of society[^c-30ed4a339a0234c9537fd14863266031]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 16(1) of the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965[^f00124] (cancellation of registration of society), the words “by writing under his hand or seal or, in Scotland” are repealed.

(2) In the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Mutual Societies) Order 2001[^f00125], paragraph 218(a) of Schedule 3 is revoked.
