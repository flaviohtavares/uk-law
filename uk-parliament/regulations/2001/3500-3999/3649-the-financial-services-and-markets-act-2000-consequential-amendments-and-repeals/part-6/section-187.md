# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 187

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 187. References to Chief Registrar etc.[^c-d6408d0ddb492f67cbee69e9d3bbb60b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In sections 82(5)[^f00131], 84(3) and 84A(6)[^f00132] of the Friendly Societies Act 1974, for the words from “Chief Registrar or,” to the end substitute “Authority”.

(2) In Schedule 3 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Mutual Societies) Order 2001, paragraphs 27, 29(b) and 30 are revoked.
