# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 213

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 213. Revocation or amendment of references to dissolved bodies, repealed legislation etc.[^c-c144cc1ee704e99a42a0657f669de41d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the Building Societies Act 1986 (Rules and Miscellaneous Transitional Provisions) Order 1986 ([S.I. 1986/2168](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/2168)), articles 3(4) and (5), 4, 10 and 11 are revoked.

(2) In the Building Societies Act 1986 (Powers and Miscellaneous Transitional Provisions) Order 1986 ([S.I. 1986/2169](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/2169)), the definition of “the registrar” in article 2 and articles 4, 8 and 11 are revoked.

(3) In regulation 2 of, and paragraph 28 of Part I of Schedule 1 to, and Schedule 3 to, the Building Societies (Transfer of Business) Regulations 1998 ([S.I. 1998/212](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/212)), for “Building Societies Commission” in each place those words appear, substitute “Authority”.

(4) In regulation 6 of, and Schedule 4 to, the Building Societies (Accounts and Related Provisions) Regulations 1998 ([S.I. 1998/504](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/504))[^f00156], for “Commission”, substitute “Authority”.

(5) In regulation 3 of the Building Societies (Business Names) Regulations 1998 ([S.I. 1998/3186](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/3186)) and in the cross-heading before that regulation, for “Commission” substitute “Authority”.

(6) In regulation 2 of, and the Schedule to, the Building Societies (Merger Notification Statement) Regulations 1999 ([S.I. 1999/1215](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1215)), for “Building Societies Commission” in each place those words appear, substitute “Authority”.
