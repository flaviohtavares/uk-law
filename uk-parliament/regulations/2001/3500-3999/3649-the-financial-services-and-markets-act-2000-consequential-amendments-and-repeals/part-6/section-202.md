# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 202

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 202. Law applicable to contracts of insurance[^c-98fbf0f1614f7768bf3f4e663c732217]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of the Friendly Societies Act 1992 are repealed (having been re-enacted in the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Law Applicable to Contracts of Insurance) Regulations 2001[^f00145])—

- (a) section 101 (law applicable to contracts of insurance with friendly societies)[^f00146];
- (b) Schedule 20[^f00147] (law applicable to certain contracts of insurance).
