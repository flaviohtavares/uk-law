# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. The principal repeals and revocations[^c-910794fee141fdef28b459ad1ed670e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following enactments are repealed—

- (a) the Policyholders Protection Act 1975[^f00002];
- (b) the Insurance Companies Act 1982[^f00003];
- (c) the Financial Services Act 1986[^f00004];
- (d) the Banking Act 1987[^f00005];
- (e) the Insurance Companies (Reserves) Act 1995[^f00006]; and
- (f) the Policyholders Protection Act 1997[^f00007].

(2) The following instruments are revoked—

- (a) the Banking Co-ordination (Second Council Directive) Regulations 1992[^f00008];
- (b) the Insurance Companies (Third Insurance Directives) Regulations 1994[^f00009]; and
- (c) the Investment Services Regulations 1995[^f00010].
