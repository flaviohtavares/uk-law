# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 169

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 169. The register maintained under the Consumer Credit Act 1974[^c-4f4e346fd5746cc40198310bed633e45]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 35 of the Consumer Credit Act 1974 (the register), after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) The Director shall also cause to be kept in the register any copy of any notice or other document relating to a consumer credit EEA firm which is given to the Director by the Financial Services Authority for inclusion in the register.

.
