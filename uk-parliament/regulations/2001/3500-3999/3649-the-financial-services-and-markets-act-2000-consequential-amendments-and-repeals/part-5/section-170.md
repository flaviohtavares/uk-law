# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 170

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 170. Enforcement of agreements made by unlicensed trader[^key-217b3f713928c10651776e48d459b6d8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 40 of the Consumer Credit Act 1974 (enforcement of agreements made by unlicensed trader), after subsection (5) insert—

> (6) This section does not apply to a regulated agreement, other than a non-commercial agreement, made by a consumer credit EEA firm unless at the time it was made that firm was precluded from entering into it as a result of—
>
> - (a) a consumer credit prohibition imposed under section 203 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000; or
> - (b) a restriction imposed on the firm under section 204 of that Act.

.
