## 172. Conduct of business regulations[^key-c8d362a79ba229b07dbdfe81abb682cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 54 of the Consumer Credit Act 1974 (conduct of business regulations), for “a licensee” substitute “a person to whom the regulations apply”.
