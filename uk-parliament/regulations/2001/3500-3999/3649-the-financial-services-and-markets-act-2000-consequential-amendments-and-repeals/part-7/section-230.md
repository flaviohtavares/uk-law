# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 230

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 230. Exclusion from financial promotion restriction[^key-8b4a7d373ff46d409fd060cd0e5a8300]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For section 31(2) of the Enterprise and New Towns (Scotland) Act 1990 (application and disapplication of certain provisions) substitute—

> (2) Section 21 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (restrictions on financial promotion) shall not apply to any invitation or inducement (within the meaning of that section) which Scottish Enterprise communicates in the discharge of its functions.
