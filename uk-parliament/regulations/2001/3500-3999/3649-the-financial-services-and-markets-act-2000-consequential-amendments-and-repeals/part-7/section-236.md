## 236. Revocation of instruments made under the Banking Act 1987[^c-a2e76c597cdcaccea3505c0bdc2d85d8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following instruments are revoked—

- (a) The Banking Appeal Tribunal (Scottish Appeals) Regulations 1987 ([S.I. 1987/1336 (S.95)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1336));
- (b) The Banking Appeal Tribunal (Scottish Appeals) Amendment Regulations 1993 ([S.I. 1993/1061 (S.150)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/1061)).
