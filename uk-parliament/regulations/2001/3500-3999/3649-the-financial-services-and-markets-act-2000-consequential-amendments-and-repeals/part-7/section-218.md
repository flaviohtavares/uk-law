> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 218. Meaning of “securities”[^key-1cbd0f52c26e5c003238708da76ee3ba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For subsection (1) of section 42[^f00158] of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 (interpretation of sections 39 and 41) substitute—

> (1) In sections 39 and 41 of this Act “securities” has the meaning given by section 92.
