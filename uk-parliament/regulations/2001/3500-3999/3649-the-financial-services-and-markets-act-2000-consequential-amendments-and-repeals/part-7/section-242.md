# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 242

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 242. Meaning of “listed investment”[^c-00ad310c56c0873edb082d66fa333fa0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph 15 of Schedule 2 to the Registered Housing Associations (Accounting Requirements) (Scotland) Order 1993 (investments)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1)(b) for the words “the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (3) for the words “the Financial Services Act 1986” substitute “the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
