# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 245

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 245. Prescribed “relevant institutions”[^c-e025233ba78a689f404ae903357ef5cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2A of the Charities (Dormant Accounts) (Scotland) Regulations 1995[^f00170] for the words “(being institutions mentioned in Schedule 2 to the Banking Act 1987)” substitute “that is to say a company which was, immediately before the repeal of the Banking Act 1987, exempted from the prohibition in section 3 of that Act by virtue of section 4(1) of, and paragraph 4 of Schedule 2 to, that Act”.
