# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 237

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 237. Actionable loss arising from pension mis-selling[^key-0d51a3fdf8c7bba6331c359ddcbbc1a7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation B4B(4)(b) of the Local Government Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 1987(rejoining pensionable employment)[^f00165]—

- (a) after the words “section 62 of the Financial Services Act 1986” insert “or section 150 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”; and
- (b) the words “made under the Act” are revoked.
