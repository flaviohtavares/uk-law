# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 219

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 219. Contracts deemed to be insurance policies[^key-b815dffa63c5af670a5549bbe499cca8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 86(3) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 (insurance by local authorities against accidents to members) for the words “the Insurance Companies Act 1982”[^f00159] substitute “the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000”.
