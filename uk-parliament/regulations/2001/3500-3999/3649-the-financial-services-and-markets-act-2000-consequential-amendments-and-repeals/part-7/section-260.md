# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Consequential Amendments and Repeals) Order 2001, section 260

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2018-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3649
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 260. Listed investments shown on balance sheet[^c-40fa3eb1db333561b8818232793e2654]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph 28 of the Schedule to the Registered Housing Associations (Accounting Requirements) (Scotland) Order 1999, for the words from “granted a listing” to the end, substitute—

> granted a listing on—
>
> - (a) a recognised investment exchange other than an overseas investment exchange within the meaning of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000; or
> - (b) any stock exchange of repute outside Great Britain.

.
