# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Order 2001, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2001/3929
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Order may be cited as the Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Order 2001 and shall come into force—

- (a) as to articles 1 and 2, paragraphs 1(a), 1(b)(ii) and 17 of Schedule 2 and, so far as it relates to those paragraphs, article 4, on 25th January 2002; and
- (b) as to the remainder of this Order, on 1st March 2002.
