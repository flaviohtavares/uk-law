# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 4) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 3934

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 4) Order 2001

Made

11th December 2001

Coming into force

12th December 2001

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of December 2001

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 4) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 12th December 2001.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 12th December 2001.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council
