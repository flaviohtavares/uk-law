# Education (City Academies) (Subject Areas) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 3631

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

Education (City Academies) (Subject Areas) Order 2001

Made

8th November 2001

Laid before Parliament

9th November 2001

Coming into force

30th November 2001

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 482(2A)(d) of the Education Act 1996[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Education (City Academies) (Subject Areas) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 30th November 2001.

(2) This Order shall only apply to England.

## 2. Subject Areas

A city academy may have an emphasis on business, enterprise and information technology in its curriculum.

Stephen Timms

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

8th November 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

This Order enables a city academy to specialise in the area of business, enterprise and information technology by having an emphasis on business, enterprise and information technology in its curriculum. The power to establish and maintain city academies does not extend to Wales.
