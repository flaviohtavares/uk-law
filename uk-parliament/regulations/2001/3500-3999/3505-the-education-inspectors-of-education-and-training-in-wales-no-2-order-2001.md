# The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) (No. 2) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 3505

EDUCATION, WALES

The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) (No. 2) Order 2001

Made

31st October 2001

Coming into force

28th November 2001

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 31st day of October 2001

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) (No. 2) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 28th November 2001.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales or Arolgwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru with effect from 28th November 2001.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council
