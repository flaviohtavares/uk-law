# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 2001, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2024-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 3 — Procedure for giving directions[^key-b0b488950a1b1292c8e54666012d35c9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 5(4), 7(3) and 8(4)

**1.**[^key-6c9f1d545a71d883a90c41576059d467][^key-90acd305b4596a769dc9f4593d1a7264] Before—

- (a)[^key-ca2ad35895f1b28f48e50de88e92905b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-a8c1878a0673e08d97a44525f1c5b647] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) giving a direction under regulation 8,

the Bank of England must give written notice of its intention to do so to the Operator.

**2.**[^key-29f4a4876b99624671e85b3487d50e1f] A notice under paragraph 1 shall—

- (a)[^key-5a69f91fbd8bc1a2ba4550624ff1358d] state why the Bank of England intends to [^key-29cca91b32ee5094eb2830345e59972e]... give the direction; and
- (b) draw attention to the right to make representations conferred by paragraph 3.

**3.**[^key-8700732d9498c1c9157740623306676d][^key-32be401a15aa18a7023d47dcaaf20ea9] Before the end of the period for making representations, the Operator may make representations to the Bank of England.

**4.**[^key-4c452d76217f816b13897f07235776e6][^key-6c67816223b1c4655db47c845e4c241d] The period for making representations is such period as is specified in the notice (which may, in any particular case, be extended by the Bank of England).

**5.**[^key-190b9b12375b5cf5a9092d93fff44bae] In deciding whether to [^key-48d4766cc1e38431d6ce75fb599e7d3e]... give the direction [^key-ca4ad9c09d02eb3a4ce51102bd31d1ab]... the Bank of England must[^key-0678f76c23c9996c2055527a18924aa0] have regard to any representations made in accordance with paragraph 3.

**6.**[^key-d21ba491f970607664a2346f1609b3b0][^key-da88d31526b31bb42fec07ba21b33e13] (1) When the Bank of England has decided whether to—

- (a)[^key-2d3111ab39b091ce2a9571fe1d3fee2d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-61ca81a8b8863642eb7e879ea0ee382e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) give the direction;

it must give the Operator written notice of its decision and the reasons for its decision[^key-fe6ce88831a807ffd5bfbcc6143613e5].

**7.**[^key-d9b8e1b5aa6c13e2e4aee02ea10d7da2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**8.**[^key-fbdb0e3a95f62605d8b6f8f4fb68f6c7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**9.**[^key-9f0dbe1e87660b7b3e8483b8bbbd01aa] If the Bank of England decides to give the direction, the Operator may refer the matter to the Upper Tribunal.

**10.** Part 9 of the 2000 Act (hearings and appeals) applies to a reference to the Upper Tribunal under paragraph 9 as it applies to a reference to the Upper Tribunal under the 2000 Act, with the following modifications—

- (a) a reference is a “**disciplinary reference**” for the purposes of section 133 of the 2000 Act if it is in respect of a decision to give a direction under this Schedule;
- (b) section 133(1)(a) and (c) of the 2000 Act does not apply.
