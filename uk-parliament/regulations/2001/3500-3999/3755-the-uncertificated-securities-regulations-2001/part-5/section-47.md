# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 2001, section 47

> Text as at 2024-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Liability of officers for contraventions[^key-1cfd958118240f4a1e2bf91b3a53e361]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In regulation 16(7), 20(7), 21(5), 22(5), 28(9), 32(9) or (10), 33(11) or 42(6) or (12) an officer of a participating issuer shall be in default in complying with, or in contravention of, the provision mentioned in that regulation if, and only if, he knowingly and wilfully authorised or permitted the default or contravention.

(2) In regulation 20(7), 21(4), 22(4), 27(9), 32(9), 33(11) or 42(12) an officer of an Operator shall be in default in complying with, or in contravention of, the provision mentioned in that regulation if, and only if, he knowingly and wilfully authorised or permitted the default or contravention.
