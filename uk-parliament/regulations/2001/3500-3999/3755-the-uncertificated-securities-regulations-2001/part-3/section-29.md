# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 2001, section 29

> Text as at 2024-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Registration to be in accordance with regulations 27 and 28[^key-400f07012ba471e8cb8030abd738d8f9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any purported registration of a transfer of title to an uncertificated unit of a security other than in accordance with regulation 27 or 28 shall be of no effect.
