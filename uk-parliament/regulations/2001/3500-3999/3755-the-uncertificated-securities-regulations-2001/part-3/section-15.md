# The Uncertificated Securities Regulations 2001, section 15

> Text as at 2024-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15.[^key-477bed73aedf03be1ea159b47203f7c5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This regulation applies to a class of shares if a company’s articles of association are in all respects consistent with—

- (a) the holding of shares of that class in uncertificated form;
- (b) the transfer of title to shares of that class by means of a relevant system; and
- (c) these Regulations.
