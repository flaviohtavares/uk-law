# The Greater London Authority Act 1999 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 3603 (C.116)

LONDON GOVERNMENT

PRIVATE HIRE VEHICLES

The Greater London Authority Act 1999 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2001

Made

5th November 2001

The Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 425(2) of the Greater London Authority Act[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Greater London Authority Act 1999 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2001.

## 2. Commencement

Section 254 of, and Schedule 21 to, the Greater London Authority Act 1999 shall, so far as not already in force, come into force on 7th November 2001.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

David Jamieson

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department for Transport, Local Government and the Regions

5th November 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 7th November 2001 section 254 of, and Schedule 21 to, the Greater London Authority Act 1999 in so far as those provisions are not already in force. Section 254 contains provisions in relation to the Private Hire Vehicles (London) Act 1998 and Schedule 21 amends the 1998 Act.

(This note is not part of the Order)
