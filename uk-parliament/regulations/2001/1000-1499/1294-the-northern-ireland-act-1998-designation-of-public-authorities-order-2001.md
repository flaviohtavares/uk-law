# The Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Designation of Public Authorities) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1294

NORTHERN IRELAND

The Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Designation of Public Authorities) Order 2001

Made

4th April 2001

Coming into force

5th April 2001

Whereas a draft of this Order has been approved by each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 75(3)(a) and (d) of the Northern Ireland Act 1998[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Designation of Public Authorities) Order 2001 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

**2.** The departments, corporations and bodies listed in Schedule 1 to this Order (being departments, corporations and bodies listed in Schedule 2 to the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967[^f00002]) are hereby designated for the purposes of section 75 of the Northern Ireland Act 1998.

**3.** The persons listed in Schedule 2 to this Order are hereby designated for the purposes of section 75 of the Northern Ireland Act 1998.

John Reid

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

4th April 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates bodies for the purposes of section 75 of the Northern Ireland Act 1998.

The effect is to make the bodies listed in the Schedules to the Order “public authorities” for the purposes of that section. Such a public authority is subject to the statutory duties, set out in section 75(1) and (2), to have regard in carrying out its functions relating to Northern Ireland—

- to the need to promote equality of opportunity; and
- to the desirability of promoting good relations between people of different religious belief, political opinion or racial group.
