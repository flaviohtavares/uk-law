# The Teacher Training Agency (Additional Functions) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1392

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Teacher Training Agency (Additional Functions) Order 2001

Made

4th April 2001

Laid before Parliament

5th April 2001

Coming into force

26th April 2001

Being of the opinion that the Teacher Training Agency may, having regard to their general objectives, appropriately discharge the additional function hereby conferred and having carried out such consultation as appears to him to be appropriate pursuant to section 16(3) of the Education Act 1994[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 16(1) and 23(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Teacher Training Agency (Additional Functions) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 26th April 2001.

**2.** In addition to their other functions the Teacher Training Agency may approve persons for the purpose of carrying out the assessment of candidates seeking exemption from induction requirements in accordance with paragraph 17 of Schedule 1 to the Education (Induction Arrangements for School Teachers) (England) Regulations 1999[^f00002].

Estelle Morris

Minister of State

Department for Education and Employment

4th April 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order confers on the Teacher Training Agency, established under section 1 of the Education Act 1994, the additional function of assessing experienced overseas teachers seeking exemption from induction requirements in accordance with paragraph 17 of Schedule 1 to the Education (Induction Arrangements for School Teachers) (England) Regulations 1999.
