# The Non-Domestic Rating (Alteration of Lists and Appeals) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1271

RATING AND VALUATION, ENGLAND

The Non-Domestic Rating (Alteration of Lists and Appeals) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2001

Made

29th March 2001

Laid before Parliament

30th March 2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 55(2) and (6) and 143(1) of the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

These regulations, which extend to England only, may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rating (Alteration of Lists and Appeals) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

## 2. Amendment of regulations

The Non-Domestic Rating (Alteration of Lists and Appeals) Regulations 1993[^f00002] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 13A, after paragraph (11) insert the following—

  > (11A) Notwithstanding the foregoing provisions of this regulation, the proposal shall be treated as served in March 2001 (and the alteration shall have effect accordingly) where—
  >
  > - (a) the alteration is made in pursuance of a proposal served after 31st March 2001 and before 1st July 2001;
  > - (b) the circumstances relied upon as the grounds of the proposal arose before 1st April 2001; and
  > - (c) the relevant hereditament is in a local government area listed in Schedule 3.

  ;

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Non-Domestic Rating (Alteration of Lists and Appeals) Regulations 1993 to extend, in the cases specified, the time from which an alteration to the list will have effect. The cases are in relation to local rating lists compiled in 2000 where—

- (a) the alteration is made pursuant to a proposal served after 31st March and before 1st July 2001;
- (b) the grounds of the proposal arose before 1st April 2001; and
- (c) the relevant hereditament is in an area listed in Schedule 3.

In these cases, the proposal is treated as served in March 2001.
