# The Employment Appeal Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 2001 (Amendment) Rules 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1476

EMPLOYMENT TRIBUNALS

The Employment Appeal Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 2001 (Amendment) Rules 2001

Made

11th April 2001

Laid before Parliament

12th April 2001

Coming into force

16th April 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 30(1), (2)(a), (b), (c) and (d) and (2A) and 41(4) of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with the Lord President of the Court of Session, hereby makes the following Rules:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Employment Appeal Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 2001 (Amendment) Rules 2001 and shall come into force on 16th April 2001.

## 2. Amendment of the Employment Appeal Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 2001

Rule 1(1) of the Employment Appeal Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 2001[^f00002] shall be amended by the substitution of the words “16th July 2001” for the words “18th April 2001”.

Irvine of Lairg, *C.*

11th April 2001

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Employment Appeal Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 2001 (S.I. [2001/1128](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1128)) by changing the coming into force date of those Rules from 18th April 2001 to 16th July 2001.
