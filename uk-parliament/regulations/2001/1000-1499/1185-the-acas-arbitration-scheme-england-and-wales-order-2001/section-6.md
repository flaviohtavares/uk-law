# The ACAS Arbitration Scheme (England and Wales) Order 2001, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Awards of compensation

An award of a basic amount shall be treated as a basic award of compensation for unfair dismissal for the purposes of section 184(1)(d)[^f00005] of the 1996 Act (which specifies such an award as a debt which the Secretary of State must satisfy if the employer has become insolvent).

## Annotations

[^f00005]: Footnote: Section 184(1)(d) was amended by the Employment Rights (Dispute Resolution) Act [1998 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/8), section 12(4).
