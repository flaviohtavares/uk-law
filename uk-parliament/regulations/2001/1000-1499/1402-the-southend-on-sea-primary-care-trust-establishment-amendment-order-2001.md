# The Southend on Sea Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1402

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Southend on Sea Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

Made

24th March 2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16A(1), (2) and (3) and 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, following compliance with the consultation requirements contained in regulations made under section 16A(5) of the Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Southend on Sea Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

## 2. Amendment of Primary Care Trust Establishment Order

In the Southend on Sea Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2000[^f00003] article 8 shall be deleted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Hunt

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

24th March 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the establishment order of the Southend on Sea Primary Care Trust (“the trust”), a Primary Care Trust established by Order under section 16A of the National Health Service Act 1977 by removing the restriction contained in article 8 of the order so that the trust may provide services directly to patients.
