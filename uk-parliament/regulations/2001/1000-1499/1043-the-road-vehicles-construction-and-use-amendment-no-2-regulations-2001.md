# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1043

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2001

Made

14th March 2001

Laid before Parliament

20th March 2001

Coming into force

1st October 2001

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 41 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 1st October 2001.

## 2. Preliminary

The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986[^f00002] shall be further amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

## 3. Amendment of regulation 46 (Seat belt anchorage points)

For regulation 46 there shall be substituted the regulation set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

(1) Regulation 47 shall be amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (2)—

## 6. Amendment of regulation 48A (Minibuses and coaches to be fitted with additional seat belts when used in certain circumstances)

## SCHEDULE — REGULATION TO BE SUBSTITUTED FOR REGULATION 46

Regulation 3

> **46. Seat belt anchorage points**
>
> (1) This regulation applies to a motor vehicle which is not an excepted vehicle and is—
>
> - (a) a bus first used on or after 1st April 1982;
> - (b) a wheeled motor car first used on or after 1st January 1965;
> - (c) a three-wheeled motor cycle which has an unladen weight exceeding 255 kg and which was first used on or after 1st September 1970; or
> - (d) a heavy motor car first used on or after 1st October 1988.
>
> (2) Each of the following is an excepted vehicle—
>
> - (a) a goods vehicle (other than a dual-purpose vehicle)—
>   - (i) first used before 1st April 1967;
>   - (ii) first used on or after 1st April 1980 and before 1st October 1988 and having a maximum gross weight exceeding 3500 kg; or
>   - (iii) first used before 1st April 1980 or, if the vehicle is of a model manufactured before 1st October 1979, first used before 1st April 1982 and, in either case, having an unladen weight exceeding 1525 kg;
> - (b) an agricultural motor vehicle;
> - (c) a motor tractor;
> - (d) a works truck;
> - (e) an electrically propelled goods vehicle first used before 1st October 1988;
> - (f) a pedestrian-controlled vehicle;
> - (g) a vehicle which has been used on roads outside Great Britain, whilst it is being driven from the place at which it arrived in Great Britain to a place of residence of the owner or driver of the vehicle, or from any such place to a place where, by previous arrangement, it will be provided with such anchorage points as are required by this regulation and with such seat belts as are required by regulation 47;
> - (h) a vehicle having a maximum speed not exceeding 16 mph;
> - (i) a motor cycle equipped with a driver’s seat of a type requiring the driver to sit astride it, and which is constructed or assembled by a person not ordinarily engaged in the trade or business of manufacturing vehicles of that description;
> - (j) a locomotive.
>

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986 in relation to seat belts and their anchorage points in the light of Directives [96/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0036), [96/38/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0038) and [2000/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0003).

*Article 3* and *the Schedule* substitute a new *regulation 46* which specifies what anchorage points are to be fitted to which vehicles and the technical requirements for anchorage points. *Regulation 4* amends *regulation 47* which makes corresponding provision for seat belts and *regulation 5* makes a consequential amendment to *regulation 48* (maintenance of seat belts and anchorages). *Regulation 6* amends *regulation 48A* so as to enable rearward-facing seats meeting specified requirements to count towards the minimum number of forward-facing seats with seat belts that must be provided on minibuses and coaches used to carry groups of children on organised trips. *Regulation 7* corrects a reference to a Community Directive in item 59 in Table I in Schedule 2. *Regulation 8* inserts some references to ECE Regulations 14.03, 14.04 and 14.05 in Table II in Schedule 2.

These Regulations have been notified to the European Commission pursuant to Directive [98/34/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0034) of 22nd June 1998 laying down a procedure for the provision of information in the field of technical standards and regulations (O.J. 1998 No. L204, p.37).

A regulatory impact assessment has been prepared and copies are available from DETR, zone 2/06 Great Minster House, 76 Marsham Street, London SW1P 4DR (Website:http://www.detr.gov.uk/). Copies have also been placed in the Libraries of each House of Parliament.

Copies of the Regulations and Directives which are relevant to these Regulations can be obtained from the Stationery Office.
