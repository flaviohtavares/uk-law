## 1. Title, commencement and extent

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) Regulations 2001; they extend to England and Wales and come into force on 15th April 2001.

## 3. Amendment of the Plant Protection Products (Fees) Regulations 1995

In so far as they extend to England and Wales, Regulation 2 of the Plant Protection Products (Fees) Regulations 1995 [^c15539941] shall be amended by substituting for the definition of “principal Regulations" the following definition—

> “principal Regulations" means the Plant Protection Products Regulations 1995 as amended by—
>
> - (a) the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) Regulations 1997 [^c15539951];
> - (b) the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1997 [^c15539961];
> - (c) the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) Regulations 2001 [^c15539971];

## 4. Revocation

The Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1999 [^c15539981] are revoked so far as they extend to England and Wales.

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

These Regulations, which extend to England and Wales, further amend the Plant Protection Products Regulations 1995 (S.I. 1995/887) which implement in Great Britain Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414) concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market (OJ No. L230, 19.8.91, p.1 as read with OJ No. L170, 25.6.92, p.40).

In so far as they extend to England and Wales the Regulations amend the definition of “the Directive" in S.I. 1995/887 so as to implement—

**•.** Commission Directive [2000/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0010) (OJ No. L57, 2.3.2000, p.28);

**•.** Commission Directive [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0049) (OJ No. L197, 3.8.2000, p.32) (as from 1st July 2001);

**•.** Commission Directive [2000/50/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0050) (OJ No. L198, 4.8.2000, p.39),which add fluroxypyr, metsulfuron-methyl and prohexadione-calcium respectively to Annex I to the Directive. The Regulations make a consequential amendment to the Plant Protection Products (Fees) Regulations 1995 (S.I. 1995/888).

In so far as they extend to England and Wales, the Regulations revoke the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1999 (S.I. 1999/3430, which made amendments to S.I. 1995/887 and 1995/888) which are consolidated in these Regulations.
