# The Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Scotland) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1460 (S. 8)

EMPLOYMENT TRIBUNALS, SCOTLAND

The Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Scotland) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

Made

11th April 2001

Laid before Parliament

12th April 2001

Coming into force

16th April 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 24(2) of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974[^f00001], sections 1(1), 4(6) and (6A), 7(1), (3) and (5), 9(1), (2) and (4), 10(2), (5), (6) and (7), 10A(1), 11(1), 12(2), 13, 19 and 41(4) of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996[^f00002] and paragraph 37 of Schedule 6 to the Scotland Act 1998[^f00003], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with the Council on Tribunals, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Scotland) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 16th April 2001.

## 2. Amendment of the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Scotland) Regulations 2001

Regulation 1(2) of the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Scotland) Regulations 2001[^f00004] shall be amended by the substitution of the words “16th July 2001” for the words “18th April 2001”.

Stephen Byers,

Secretary of State for Trade and Industry

11th April 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Scotland) Regulations 2001 (S.I. [2001/1170](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1170) S. 7) by changing the coming into force date of those Regulations from 18th April 2001 to 16th July 2001.
