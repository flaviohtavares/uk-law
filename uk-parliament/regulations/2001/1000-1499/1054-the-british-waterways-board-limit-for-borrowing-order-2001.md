# The British Waterways Board (Limit for Borrowing) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1054

TRANSPORT

The British Waterways Board (Limit for Borrowing) Order 2001

Made

19th March 2001

Coming into force

20th March 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 19(3)(iv) of the Transport Act 1962[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order, a draft of which has, in accordance with section 19(3A) of that Act, been approved by resolution of the House of Commons:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the British Waterways Board (Limit for Borrowing) Order 2001 and shall come into force on the day following the day on which it is made.

## 2. Specified limit of borrowing

£35 million is hereby specified for the purposes of section 19(3)(iv) of the Transport Act 1962.

## 3. Revocation

The British Waterways Board (Limit for Borrowing) Order 1983[^f00002] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Bob Ainsworth

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions

19th March 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the borrowing limit of the British Waterways Board from £30 million to £35 million, and revokes an earlier order fixing that limit.
