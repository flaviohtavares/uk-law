## SCHEDULE 7 — Corresponding Northern Ireland Enactments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 156(4)

**1.** In this Schedule—

- “the 1998 Order” means the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 [^c17677851];
- “the 2000 Act” means the Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act 2000;
- “the Transfer Order” means the Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 [^c17677861];
- “the Welfare Reform Act” means the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999; and
- “the Welfare Reform Order” means the Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 [^c17677871].
