# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 116

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Modification of section 162(5) of the Administration Act

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 162 of the Administration Act (destination of contributions), subsection (5) [^c17675721] (which specifies the amount of the national health service allocation to be deducted from each class of contribution prior to their payment into the National Insurance Fund) shall be modified, in the case of contributions paid at the rate reduced in accordance with regulation 119(1), as if, instead of the percentage figure specified in paragraph (b) of that subsection, there were specified the percentage figure “0.6”.
