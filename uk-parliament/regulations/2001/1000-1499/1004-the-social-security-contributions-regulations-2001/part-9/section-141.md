# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 141

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 141. Treatment of serving members of the forces as present in Great Britain

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of regulation 145(1)(a) a serving member of the forces shall, in respect of his employment as such, be treated as present in Great Britain [^c17675941].

## Annotations

[^c17675941]: Textual amendment: *See* however regulation 6 of [S.I. 1975/493](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1975/493) as to the treatment of contributions paid by members of the forces for the purposes of entitlement to benefit in Northern Ireland.
