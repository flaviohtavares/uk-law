# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 114C

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114C (Extent: E+W+S). UKCS oil field licensee certificate (Extent: E+W+S)[^key-d21af438f6bba22709ab181823a2fde6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S)) Where a UKCS continental shelf workers certificate holder has notified *(Extent: E+W+S)* HMRC *(Extent: E+W+S)* that the employer intends to supply continental shelf workers to an oil field licensee an officer of Revenue and Customs must issue a UKCS oil field licensee certificate to the oil field licensee. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(2 (Extent: E+W+S)) The UKCS oil field licensee certificate must include— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) the name of the oil field *(Extent: E+W+S)* licensee; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) the registered office of that oil field licensee; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) the oil field licence number; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (d (Extent: E+W+S)) the name of the UKCS continental shelf workers certificate holder; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (e (Extent: E+W+S)) the date on which it is issued; and *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (f (Extent: E+W+S)) a description of the continental shelf workers to whom it *(Extent: E+W+S)* applies *(Extent: E+W+S)*. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(3 (Extent: E+W+S)) Where a UKCS oil field licensee certificate is in force the holder of that certificate is not liable to pay any contributions in respect of any continental shelf worker of a description set out in the certificate. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(4 (Extent: E+W+S)) If a UKCS continental shelf workers certificate is cancelled by an officer of Revenue and Customs that officer must also, by notice in writing, cancel the UKCS oil field licensee certificate. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(5 (Extent: E+W+S)) A notice under paragraph (4) must— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) be sent on the same day as the notice cancelling the UKCS continental shelf workers certificate; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) specify the date of cancellation of the UKCS oil field licensee certificate; and *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) notify the oil field licensee *(Extent: E+W+S)* that it is liable *(Extent: E+W+S)* to meet its obligations as a secondary contributor. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(6 (Extent: E+W+S)) The date of cancellation of the UKCS oil field licensee certificate must be the same date as that specified in the UKCS continental shelf workers certificate cancellation notice. *(Extent: E+W+S)*
