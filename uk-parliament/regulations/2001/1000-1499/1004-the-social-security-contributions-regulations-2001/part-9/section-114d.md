# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 114D

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114D (Extent: E+W+S). Interpretation of regulations (Extent: E+W+S) 114 (Extent: E+W+S) to 114C (Extent: E+W+S)[^key-d21af438f6bba22709ab181823a2fde6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulations 114 to 114C— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- “associated company” means any company within the meaning of section 449 of the Corporation Tax Act 2010; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- “an equivalent *(Extent: E+W+S)* PAYE *(Extent: E+W+S)* provision” means any provision in the *(Extent: E+W+S)* PAYE *(Extent: E+W+S)* Regulations which has an equivalent effect to the provisions in regulations 114A to 114C; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- “employer’s *(Extent: E+W+S)* PAYE *(Extent: E+W+S)* reference” has the meaning given in regulation 2(1) of the *(Extent: E+W+S)* PAYE *(Extent: E+W+S)* Regulations; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- “offshore installation” means— *(Extent: E+W+S)*
  - (a) a structure which is, is to be, or has been, put to a relevant use while in water; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
  - (b) but a structure is not an offshore installation if— *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (i) it has permanently ceased to be put to a relevant use, *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (ii) it is not, and is not to be, put to any other relevant use, and *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (iii) since permanently ceasing to be put to a relevant use, it has been put to a use which is not a relevant use; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
  - (c) a use is a relevant use if it is— *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (i) for the purposes of exploiting mineral resources, *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (ii) for the purposes of exploration with a view to exploiting mineral resources, *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (iii) for the storage of gas in or under the shore or the bed of any waters, *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (iv) for the recovery of gas so stored, *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (v) for the conveyance of things by means of a pipe, *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (vi) mainly for the provision of accommodation for individuals who work on or from a structure which is, is to be, or has been put to any of the above uses while in the water, *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (vii) for the purposes of decommissioning any structure which has been used for or in connection with any of the relevant uses above; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
  - (d) a structure is put to use while in water if it is put to use while— *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (i) standing in any waters, *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (ii) stationed (by whatever means) in any waters, or *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (iii) standing on the foreshore or other land intermittently covered with water; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
  - (e) a “structure” includes a ship or other vessel except where it is used wholly or mainly— *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (i) for the transport of supplies; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (ii) as a safety vessel; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (iii) for a combination of (i) and (ii); or *(Extent: E+W+S)*
    - (iv) for the laying of *(Extent: E+W+S)* cables; and *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- “oil field licensee” means the holder of a licence under Part 1 of the Petroleum Act 1998 in respect of the area in which the duties of the continental shelf worker’s employment are performed; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- “UKCS continental shelf workers certificate” means a certificate issued under regulation 114A; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- “UKCS oil field licensee certificate” means a certificate issued under regulation 114C(1). *(Extent: E+W+S)*
