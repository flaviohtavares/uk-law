> Text as at 2026-07-20.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114B (Extent: E+W+S). UKCS continental shelf workers certificate holder: obligations and responsibilities (Extent: E+W+S)[^c-d21af438f6bba22709ab181823a2fde6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A UKCS continental shelf workers certificate holder must— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) make such deductions, returns and repayments as are required of a secondary contributor; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) keep written records of— *(Extent: E+W+S)*
  - (i (Extent: E+W+S)) the name, date of birth, and national insurance number of the continental shelf workers supplied; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
  - (ii (Extent: E+W+S)) the name, registered office and oil field licence number of the oil field licensee to whom each of the workers were supplied; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
  - (iii (Extent: E+W+S)) the offshore installation to which each of the workers were supplied; and *(Extent: E+W+S)*
  - (iv (Extent: E+W+S)) the dates between which the workers worked on the offshore installation; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) keep the records required by sub-paragraph (b) for a period of 6 years from the end of the tax year to which they *(Extent: E+W+S)* relate; *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (d (Extent: E+W+S)) where an officer of Revenue and Customs requires them in writing to do so, provide copies of the records required by sub-paragraph (b) to *(Extent: E+W+S)* HMRC *(Extent: E+W+S)* within 30 days of the date of the *(Extent: E+W+S)* request; and *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (e (Extent: E+W+S)) before supplying the oil field licensee with continental shelf workers for the first time, inform *(Extent: E+W+S)* HMRC *(Extent: E+W+S)* in writing of the details of the oil field licensee including name, business address, and oil field licence number of the oil field licensee. *(Extent: E+W+S)*
