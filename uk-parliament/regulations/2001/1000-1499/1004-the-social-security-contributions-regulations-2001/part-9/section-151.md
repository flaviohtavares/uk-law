## 151. Option to pay Class 2 contributions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding section 11(1) of the Act and regulation 150, a volunteer development worker who by virtue of that regulation is a self-employer earner—

- (a)[^key-65329d6eda30653fdc23b32d2a568a87] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) shall be entitled to pay a Class 2[^key-975f3a7ba4ba360b1170c0f8c037b8d8] contribution if he so wishes at the rate prescribed in regulation 152(b).
