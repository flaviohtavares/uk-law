# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 67

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Collection and recovery of earnings-related contributions, and Class 1B contributions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the provisions of regulations 68 and 70, earnings-related contributions and Class 1B contributions shall be paid, accounted for and recovered in like manner as income tax deducted from the PAYE[^c-4c10c8fcf45f5041d475311d0437cf1e][^c-01db00bcb1169df23257464b6c9e08e4] income by virtue of regulations under section 684 of ITEPA 2003 (PAYE Regulations).

(1A)[^c-f68d2cc8fa247332601014c821f34cab] PAYE income has the meaning given in section 683 of ITEPA 2003.

(2) [^c-975ca13ed1dd29d474e9a9504e50da47]...The provisions contained in Schedule 4, (which contains provisions derived from the[^c-514898ce2cf2d6e49779608e5a6593e2] PAYE Regulations with extensions and modifications) shall apply to and for the purposes of earnings-related contributions and Class 1B contributions.

(3)[^c-408c11af78a80b44fd2c238d244a996c] Schedules 4A (real time returns) and 4B (additional information about payments) apply to and for the purposes of earnings-related contributions.
