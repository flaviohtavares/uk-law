# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 88

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Notification of change of address

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person to whom section 11(5B) of the Act (class 2 contributions treated as paid) applies[^c-04c87f43fbfa01b23d346423c3ad2f96]; or paying Class 2 contributions [^c-984795f16c9018b3f1fc55eede7bd97a]... or Class 3 contributions, shall immediately notify the Board of any change of his address in writing or by such means of electronic communications as may be approved.
