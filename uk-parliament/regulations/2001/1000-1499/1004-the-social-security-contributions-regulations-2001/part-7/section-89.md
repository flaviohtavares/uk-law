# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 89

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Method of, and time for, payment of Class 2 and Class 3 contributions etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where Class 2 or Class 3 contributions are payable by a person other than in accordance with the Taxes Management Act 1970 (as modified by section 11A of the Act) or in accordance with,[^c-3b693fe32a7eea11037c9c5988a3905d] arrangements approved under regulation 90 or in accordance with regulation 90ZA or 148C[^c-5d1ee45182488bb902526d0b43774d36], such contributions shall be paid in accordance with paragraph[^c-5d854e13e59611003cf191320bb00226]... (2A),[^c-0dff2087d80435aef1056fdcc614e616] (3) or (4), as the case may be.

(1A)[^c-e903c1eb4ff4b76665c4cd1183dc475d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-4736691a3cb4bde32ec49926c9e5de6c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^c-fbcc15a5c015b98949b9aff815f6b96b] Where—

- (a) a person who is entitled, although not liable, to pay a Class 2 contribution in any year has notified HMRC of his entitlement in accordance with the provisions of regulation 87,[^c-ff88e3764b6e2700f1ef0ca7ddcbe1ff] 87A or 87AA[^c-41cf37d3dc2c5192301c0c29eb6a2b43]; and
- (b) HMRC has, no later than the notification date, issued him with written notice of the amount he may pay in respect of his entitlement in that period;

that person may, if the person so wishes, pay to HMRC a sum not exceeding that amount.

(3) Where—

- (a) a person [^c-a2d8867c436f36231556832c2ce87f07]... who is entitled to pay a Class 3 contribution, in any year, has notified HMRC[^c-d7b65d6951ac1e25e5d1230a8b192968] of his entitlement in accordance with the provisions of regulation 87, 87A or 87AA[^c-13a015cdda32898a39d6a2fc0ec462da]; and
- (b) HMRC, within 14 days after the end of a contribution quarter which commences in that year, have issued him with written notice of the amount he may pay in respect of his entitlement in that quarter;

that person may, if he so wishes, pay to the Board a sum not exceeding that amount.

(4) Where—

- (a) paragraph (5) [^c-a096d7b2502921614c28dfa11bf81c26]... applies to a person; and
- (b) HMRC[^c-8721df832deaa1dbecd3d23d01f79d5e] have then, in respect of that [^c-1f287afeb46b38afb67c679a0a107a34]... entitlement to pay Class 2 or Class 3 contributions, issued or re-issued him, as the case may be, with written notice of [^c-1f287afeb46b38afb67c679a0a107a34]... the amount of his entitlement;

that person [^c-87b051c8fe0889427aa23c41e66587b2]... may pay a sum not exceeding the amount of his entitlement, to HMRC.

(5)[^c-2844432a6eaebeab4f5918eeeabfb87a] This paragraph applies to a person who—

- (a) has notified HMRC in accordance with the provisions of regulation 87, 87A or 87AA[^c-ce36a3076b786b8296e67cf936d14f81] that—
  - (i)[^c-08f18d25e30ace7ce27778d0db61d83d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii) he is entitled although not liable to pay a Class 2 contribution in a tax year[^c-50b850f7b80ca657ce421ebcd52b23b2], or is entitled to pay a Class 3 contribution in a contribution quarter; and
- (b) has—
  - (i) not, by the notification date, had written notice issued to him in respect of that week or weeks of the kind referred to in paragraph (2A);
  - (ii) not had written notice issued to him in respect of that week or weeks of a kind mentioned in paragraph (3) and more than 14 days have elapsed since the end of the contribution quarter in question; or
  - (iii) notified HMRC in accordance with regulation 87, 87A or 87AA[^c-0272d12453554159ac751ed6f9c51cd8] that he has [^c-b4459bcfd1dba18c326fcf7529fc91cd]... ceased to be entitled to pay Class 2 or Class 3 contributions[^c-0026b89b3884d3390a1ffc7ffa783005]....

(6)[^c-03e8d76afb662f288c3fccd311612d23] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) In this regulation—

- (a)[^c-480dcc5732c04d1d01a52ccfd140d5e3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-480dcc5732c04d1d01a52ccfd140d5e3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-97874c372d76708ba9f1ec1fadc339b1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) “contribution quarter” means one of the four periods of not less than 13 contribution weeks commencing on the first, fourteenth, twenty-seventh or fortieth contribution week, as the case may be, in any year;
- (e) “notification date” means 31st October following the end of the tax year.[^c-29e3f3ee55d13825f5340ddfb067a62d]
