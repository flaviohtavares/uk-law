# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 90G

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90G. Use of unauthorised method of electronic communications[^c-1ab31b2bc581b84dd904b2af96d1cb94]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to information which is required to be delivered to the Board or to an official computer system under a provision of these Regulations.

(2) The use of a method of electronic communications for the purpose of delivering such information is conclusively presumed not to have resulted in the delivery of that information, unless that method of electronic communications is for the time being approved for delivery of that kind under that provision.
