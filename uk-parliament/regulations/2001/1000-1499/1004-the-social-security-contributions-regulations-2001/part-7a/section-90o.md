# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 90O

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90O. Standards of accuracy and completeness[^key-1ab31b2bc581b84dd904b2af96d1cb94]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any paragraph 22 return[^key-7189978a685a4a536900a31dd57ae2ab] delivered by a method of electronic communications must meet the standards of accuracy or completeness set by specific or general directions given by the Board.

(2) Any paragraph 22 return which fails to meet those standards must be treated as not having been delivered.
