# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 90A

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90A. Whether information has been delivered electronically[^c-1ab31b2bc581b84dd904b2af96d1cb94]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of these Regulations, information is taken to have been delivered to an official computer system by an approved method of electronic communications only if it is accepted by that official computer system.

(2) References in these Regulations to information and to the delivery of information must be construed in accordance with section 135(8) of the Finance Act 2002 (mandatory e-filing).
