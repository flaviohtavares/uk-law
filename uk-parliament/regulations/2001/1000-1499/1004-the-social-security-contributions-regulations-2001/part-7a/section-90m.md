# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 90M

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90M. Paragraph 22 return and specified payments[^c-1ab31b2bc581b84dd904b2af96d1cb94][^c-7cbac529080998939c7f7c8185836d9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “paragraph 22 return” means the return and accompanying information required by paragraph 22 of Schedule 4 (return by employer at the end of the year);[^c-826269c5a0f05eda9da90fedc32fcd52]
- “specified payments” means payments of earnings-related contributions under paragraph 10 (payments made monthly by employer) or paragraph 11 (payments made quarterly by employer) of Schedule 4.[^c-8d322ec68d5c197d857f0360ba22e745]
