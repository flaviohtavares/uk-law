# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 90C

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90C. Proof of identity of person sending or receiving electronic delivery[^c-1ab31b2bc581b84dd904b2af96d1cb94]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The identity of—

- (a) the person sending any information delivered by an approved method of electronic communications to the Board,
- (b) the person receiving any information delivered by an approved method of electronic communications by the Board,

is presumed, unless the contrary is proved, to be the person recorded as such on an official computer system.
