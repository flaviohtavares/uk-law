# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, SCHEDULE4B

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4B — Additional information about payments[^c-8b5d82ccbe8541eab136a65461ff020a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 67(3)

**1.** The amounts specified in this Schedule are as follows and terms used in this Schedule which are defined for the purposes of Schedule 4 bear the same meaning as in that Schedule.

### 2. Deductions in respect of statutory payments

In respect of statutory maternity pay paid during the year to date to all employees the total of the amounts determined under regulation 3 (determination of the amount of additional payment to which a small employer shall be entitled) of the Statutory Maternity Pay (Compensation of Employers) and Miscellaneous Amendments Regulations 1994 and deducted by virtue of regulation 4 (right of employer to prescribed amount) of those Regulations.

**3.** In respect of [^c-926fe0aca9850624aa024dbee77b1f52]... statutory paternity pay paid during the year to date to all employees, the total of the amounts determined under regulation 5 (deductions from payments to HMRC) of the Statutory Paternity Pay and Statutory Adoption Pay (Administration) Regulations 2002.

**4.**[^c-18d784dd266e705c9841b225ac289abc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**4A.**[^c-6fbc611fed50cca2e5a81d067c3bf6ae] In respect of statutory shared parental pay paid during the year to all employees, the total amounts determined under regulation 5 (deductions from payments to the Commissioners) of the Statutory Shared Parental Pay (Administration) Regulations 2014.

**5.** In respect of statutory adoption pay paid during the year to date to all employees, the total of the amounts determined under regulation 5 of the Statutory Paternity Pay and Statutory Adoption Pay (Administration) Regulations 2002.

**5A (Extent: E+W+S).**[^M_F_d16fa979-899b-4de6-fc9f-e365c56724a8] In respect of statutory parental bereavement pay paid during the year to date to all employees, the total amounts determined under regulation 5 of the Statutory Parental Bereavement Pay (Administration) Regulations 2020. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

*Extent: E+W+S*

**5B.**[^c-30a0a48ee275c2b653d7f890aaa2296d] In respect of statutory neonatal care pay paid during the year to date to all employees, the total amounts determined under regulation 5 of the Statutory Neonatal Care Pay (Administration) Regulations 2025.

**6.**[^c-d435929721c749a405d2e2b8c27079e4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### 7. Regional secondary contributions holiday for new businesses

The total of the appropriate amounts within the meaning given by section 7 of the National Insurance Contributions Act 2011 (regional secondary contributions holiday for new businesses) deducted by or refunded to the employer under section 4 of that Act in the year to date.
