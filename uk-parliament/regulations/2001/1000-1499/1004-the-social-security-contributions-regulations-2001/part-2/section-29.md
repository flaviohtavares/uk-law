# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 29

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Liability for Class 1 contributions of persons over pensionable age

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If—

- (a) earnings are paid to or for the benefit of an earner after he attains pensionable age; and
- (b) those earnings would normally fall to be paid before the date on which he reaches pensionable age,

section 6(3) of the Act (liability for Class 1 contributions) [^c17675271] shall not operate to except him from liability for primary Class 1 contributions in respect of those earnings.

## Annotations

[^c17675271]: Textual amendment: Section 6 was substituted by paragraph 2 of Part I of Schedule 9 to the Welfare Reform Act.
