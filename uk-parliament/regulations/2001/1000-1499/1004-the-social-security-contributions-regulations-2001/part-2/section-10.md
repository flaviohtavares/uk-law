# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 10

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Earnings limits and thresholds

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of sections 5(1)[^key-0b2664c8dcae8cde5ea335be59c14ae7], 9A and 9B[^key-b5db89b65a1d64f8842d159704bb1d9e] of the Act (earnings limits and thresholds to be specified for each tax year in respect of Class 1 contributions), for the tax year which begins on 6th April 2026[^key-f9e4fac8cd0ec1bbe21f69058cecc77b]—

- (a) the lower earnings limit (for primary Class 1 contributions) shall be £129[^key-b618212dfb800c0464fced6a8fc40761];
- (b) the upper earnings limit (for primary Class 1 contributions) shall be £967[^key-bedccabaf18699d446a04686a1354eeb];
- (c) the primary threshold (for primary Class 1 contributions) shall be £242[^key-243cb8de5a474d604fbe046ecad4e366]; [^key-dfd2afb026aa269c89b59b19799555a1]...
- (d) the secondary threshold (for secondary Class 1 contributions) shall be £96[^key-724305c3a56a7b11723555d42ec3d3df];[^key-de4f4cb431dc07f6f573c7a35bc5e879] [^key-f51e2dac360a111a55ac40d6e8031356]...
- (e) the upper secondary threshold for secondary Class 1 contributions in relation to the Under 21 group (for the upper limit of the age-related secondary percentage) shall be £967[^key-60502ad0dc8e1da81423203db07e5dde]; and[^key-4f0524c87994b76444b3c426855ce5d2]
- (f) the upper secondary threshold for secondary Class 1 contributions in relation to relevant apprentices (for the upper limit of zero-rate secondary Class 1 contributions) shall be £967[^key-1d9dcc96fe943c5b8531591627c18b6a]
