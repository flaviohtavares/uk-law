# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 16

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Aggregation of earnings paid after pensionable age

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding the provisions of regulation 15, a payment of earnings to which regulation 28 applies shall not be aggregated with any other earnings.
