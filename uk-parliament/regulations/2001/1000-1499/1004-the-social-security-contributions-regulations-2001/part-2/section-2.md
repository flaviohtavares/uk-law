# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 2

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Earnings periods

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except where regulation 8 applies, the amount, if any, of earnings-related contributions payable or, where section 6A of the Act [^c17675151] applies, treated as having been paid, in respect of earnings paid to or for the benefit of an earner in respect of an employed earner’s employment shall, subject to regulations 7 and 12 to 19, be assessed on the amount of such earnings paid, or treated as paid, in the earnings period specified in regulation 3, 4, 5, 6, or 9.

## Annotations

[^c17675151]: Textual amendment: Section 6A was inserted by paragraph 3 of Part I of Schedule 9 to the Welfare Reform Act.
