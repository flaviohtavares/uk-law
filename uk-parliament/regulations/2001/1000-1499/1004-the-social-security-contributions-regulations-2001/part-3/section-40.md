## 40. Prescribed general earnings in respect of which Class 1A contributions not payable[^c-707fc1b6b5631ec70ad1d12a22d6d65a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Class 1A contributions shall not be payable in respect of the general earnings[^c-03e9bdf75c5afdc2dfe7d5a2bad8a33b] prescribed by paragraphs (2) to (7).

(2) The general earnings[^c-abd2dce0b597b787a75a640fe2b19c47] prescribed by this paragraph are those[^c-bf7051a1e777d5b893888aa0d100d2a5] which are excluded from the calculation of a person’s earnings in respect of any employed earner’s employment by virtue of the following provisions of Schedule 3—

- (za)[^c-76fc49182765cb518057b035bca76026] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (a) in Part VI, paragraphs 2(b), 3 to 5, 7, 10 and 11;[^c-474fcfda00126d9c60a72a6ef69b0d1d]
- (ab)[^c-c3f8f07c37380eebeb1c04b413b5c58b] in Part 7, paragraph 12;
- (b) in Part VIII, paragraphs 4 to 5[^c-5d6db086d47df743b9be79cb5f0257a2] and 13;
- (c) in Part IX, paragraphs 3 to 7A[^c-01c2447af5f94853ba32e2be76a0d309]; and
- (d) in Part X, paragraphs 5, 9, 11 to 13 and 15[^c-3c8a04b73ae204eb6ea2b40959445602].

(3) The general earnings prescribed by this paragraph are those which are payments which are not excluded from the calculation of a person’s earnings in respect of any employed earner’s employment by virtue of paragraph 1 of Part II of Schedule 3 (payments in kind), but which are so excluded by virtue of paragraph 3 of Part VIII of Schedule 3 (qualifying travelling expenses) or paragraph 9 of that Part (specific and distinct expenses).
