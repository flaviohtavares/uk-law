# The Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001, section 98

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 98. Revocation of certificates of exception and deferment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where under regulation 97(4)(a) the Board revoke a certificate of exception or deferment—

- (a) they shall be responsible for calculating the contributions due under section 15 of the Act for the year specified in paragraph 97(4)(b) (being the current or a past year) and for the collection of those contributions;
- (b) the applicant shall—
  - (i) furnish, or cause to be furnished, to the Board all such information or evidence as they may require for the purpose of calculating those contributions, and
  - (ii) within such period as the Board may direct, pay to them the contributions so calculated.
