> Text as at 2026-07-20.

## SCHEDULE 1 — PROVISIONS CONFERRING POWERS EXERCISED IN MAKING THESE REGULATIONS

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Preamble

In this Schedule—

- “the 1998 Act” means the Social Security Act 1998 [^c17677881];
- “the 1988 Order” means the Social Security (Northern Ireland Order 1998 [^c17677891];
- “the 2000 Act” means the Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act 2000 [^c17677901];
- “the Transfer Act” means the Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) Act 1999 [^c17677911]
- “the Transfer Order” means the Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 [^c17677921]; and
- “the Welfare Reform Act” means the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 [^c17677931].
