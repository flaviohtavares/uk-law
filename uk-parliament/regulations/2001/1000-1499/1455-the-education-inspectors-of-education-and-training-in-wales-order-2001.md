# The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1455

EDUCATION, WALES

The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2001

Made

11th April 2001

Laid before Parliament

25th April 2001

Coming into force

15th May 2001

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 11th day of April 2001

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 15th May 2001.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales with effect from 15th May 2001.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

- Julia Helen Cantle Longville
- Adela Baird
- Robert James Hancock
- Linda Howells

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales.
