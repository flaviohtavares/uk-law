# The Postal Services Act 2000 (Consequential Modifications No. 1) Order 2001, section 5

> Text as at 2011-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Supplementary provision[^key-ddbec6a60b555b2e2f1f3c95672387bd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In sections 111 to 114 and 117 of, and Part I of Schedule 8 to, the Postal Services Act 2000, “the Post Office company” has the meaning given by section 62(8) of that Act.
