# The Thurrock and Basildon College (Incorporation) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 1497

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Thurrock and Basildon College (Incorporation) Order 2001

Made

17th April 2001

Laid before Parliament

23rd April 2001

Coming into force

1st June 2001

Whereas the Secretary of State for Education and Employment has received a proposal from the Further Education Funding Council for England, made in accordance with section 51 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001] (“the Act”), for the establishment of a body corporate under section 16(1)(a) of the Act;

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16(1) and 17 of the Act and for the purpose of giving effect to that proposal, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Thurrock and Basildon College (Incorporation) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st June 2001 (“the commencement date”).

**2.** There shall be established as from the commencement date a body corporate called “Thurrock and Basildon College”, for the purpose of establishing and conducting as from the operative date a new educational institution of that name (to be formed by the merger of Thurrock College and Basildon College).

**3.** The operative date in relation to the further education corporation established by this Order shall be 1st August 2001, and the corporation shall conduct Thurrock and Basildon College from that date.

Tessa Blackstone

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Employment

17th April 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes a further education corporation called Thurrock and Basildon College under section 16(1) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 for the purpose of conducting the educational institution called by the same name. It appoints 1st August 2001 as the operative date for the purposes of Part 1 of that Act.
