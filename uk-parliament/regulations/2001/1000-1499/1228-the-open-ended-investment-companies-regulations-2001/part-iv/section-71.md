# The Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations 2001, section 71

> Text as at 2022-06-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Register of open-ended investment companies

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Authority must maintain a register of open-ended investment companies.

(2) The Authority may keep the register in any form it thinks fit provided that it is possible to inspect the information contained on it and to obtain a copy of that information (or any part of it) for inspection.
