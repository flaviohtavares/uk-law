# The Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations 2001, section 73

> Text as at 2022-06-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Delivery of documents to the Authority

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any document which is required by these Regulations to be delivered to the Authority to be recorded on the register maintained pursuant to regulation 71 must be delivered in such form as the Authority may from time to time specify.
