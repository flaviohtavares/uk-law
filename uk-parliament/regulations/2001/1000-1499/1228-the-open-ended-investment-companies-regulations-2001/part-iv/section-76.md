# The Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations 2001, section 76

> Text as at 2022-06-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Provision by the Authority of documents in electronic form[^key-967e0cc0f0f5d41f5fd9bfd4aef81ac8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any requirement of these Regulations as to the supply by the Authority of a document may, if the Authority thinks fit, be satisfied by the communication by the Authority of the information in electronic form[^key-3ceba8ca6aec18c9631320b9a3643523].
