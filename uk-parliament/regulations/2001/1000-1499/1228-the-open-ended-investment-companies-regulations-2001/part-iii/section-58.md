# The Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations 2001, section 58

> Text as at 2022-06-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Execution of deeds or other documents by attorney[^key-dc4015f470e4cfa855a897946a512e16]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Under the law of England and Wales or Northern Ireland[^key-a306ce0138853b8dc9634b04db1d898b] an open-ended investment company may, by instrument executed as a deed[^key-a350031c791649604ad6de3fe093d917], empower any person, either generally or in respect of any specified matters, as its attorney, to execute deeds on its behalf [^key-5a1e34820d64b14c35252993f923996e]....

(2) A deed executed by such an attorney on behalf of the company has effect as if executed by the company.[^key-b9811bda09a32aa365c2121d94e55e19]
