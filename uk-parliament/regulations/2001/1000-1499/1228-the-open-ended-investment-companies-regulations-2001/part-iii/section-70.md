# The Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations 2001, section 70

> Text as at 2022-06-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Mergers and divisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 6 to these Regulations makes provision with respect to mergers and divisions involving open-ended investment companies other than mergers[^key-ab0aa3f0b0e57a8ae78ac6ffff035700] to which Part 4 of the Undertakings for Collective Investment in Transferable Securities Regulations 2011 applies[^key-07bc64a599560509119ed30a420ca9cf].
