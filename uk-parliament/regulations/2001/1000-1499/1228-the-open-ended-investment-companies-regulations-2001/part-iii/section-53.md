# The Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations 2001, section 53

> Text as at 2022-06-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Power incidental to carrying on business

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An open-ended investment company has power to do all such things as are incidental or conducive to the carrying on of its business.
