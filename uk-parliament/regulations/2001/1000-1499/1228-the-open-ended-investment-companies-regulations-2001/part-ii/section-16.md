# The Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations 2001, section 16

> Text as at 2022-06-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Representations against refusal of authorisation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the Authority proposes to refuse an application made under regulation 12, it must give the applicant a warning notice.

(2) If the Authority decides to refuse the application—

- (a) it must give the applicant a decision notice; and
- (b) the applicant may refer the matter to the Upper Tribunal[^key-a17d708c0e24c37d42d797d77e0dc366].
