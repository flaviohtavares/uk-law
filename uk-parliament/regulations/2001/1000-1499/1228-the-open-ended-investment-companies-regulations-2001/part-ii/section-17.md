# The Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations 2001, section 17

> Text as at 2022-06-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Certificates

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If an open-ended investment company which complies with the conditions necessary to enable it to be a UCITS[^key-08fd7ca763d05cb85252e468a4770f0a] so requests, the Authority may issue a certificate to the effect that the company complies with those conditions.

(2) Such a certificate may be issued on the making of an authorisation order in respect of the company or at any subsequent time.
