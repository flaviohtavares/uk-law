# The Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations 2001, section 11B

> Text as at 2022-06-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11B. Cross sub-fund investment[^c-db4d75bc47e2dcda7c7ebda09a25b553]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding section 658 of the Companies Act 2006 and any rule of law which prohibits or restricts a company from acquiring its own shares, an umbrella company may, for the account of any of its sub-funds, and in accordance with FCA[^c-22e07a67ed7afbb1a72aaed221fbf07f] rules, acquire by subscription or transfer for consideration, shares of any class or classes, however described, representing other sub-funds of the same umbrella company.
