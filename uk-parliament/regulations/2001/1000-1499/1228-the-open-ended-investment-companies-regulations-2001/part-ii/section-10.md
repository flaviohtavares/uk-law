## 10. The Authority’s procedures

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 395 of the Act (the[^c-5832ecda0763739cf141816a16cd3d89] FCA’s and the PRA’s procedures) applies to the procedure relating to the Authority’s functions in relation to supervisory notices, warning notices and decision notices given under any provision of these Regulations.
