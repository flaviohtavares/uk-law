# The Criminal Defence Service (Funding) (Amendment) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2001/855](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/855) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2001 No. 1143

LEGAL SERVICES COMMISSION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Criminal Defence Service (Funding) (Amendment) Order 2001

Made

23rd March 2001

Laid before Parliament

26th March 2001

Coming into force

2nd April 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 13(3) and 14(3) of the Access to Justice Act 1999[^f00001], having had regard to the matters specified in section 25(3) and having consulted the General Council of the Bar and the Law Society, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Defence Service (Funding) (Amendment) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 2nd April 2001.

## 2. Amendment to the Criminal Defence Service (Funding) Order 2001[^f00002]

In article 2 of the Criminal Defence Service (Funding) Order 2001, in the definition of “the Contract”, the following shall be inserted after “2001”;

- “, as amended on 12th March 2001”;

Irvine of Lairg, *C.*

Dated 23rd March 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made in order to correct the omission, in the definition of “the Contract” in the Criminal Defence Service (Funding) Order, of reference to the amendments made to that contract on 12th March 2001 to provide for different rates of remuneration for police station work.
