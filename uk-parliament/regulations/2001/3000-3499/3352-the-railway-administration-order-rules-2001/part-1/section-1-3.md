# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 1.3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1.3. Extent

The Rules apply in relation to protected railway companies which the courts in England and Wales have jurisdiction to wind up.
