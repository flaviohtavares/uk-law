# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 10.6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10.6. “Petition”

References to “petition” means petition for a railway administration order to be made in relation to a protected railway company.
