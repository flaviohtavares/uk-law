# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 10.10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10.10. Expressions used generally

(1) “File in court” means deliver to the court for filing.

(2) “The Gazette” means The London Gazette.

(3) “Business day” means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday, Christmas Day, Good Friday or a day which is a bank holiday in any part of Great Britain under or by virtue of the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971[^f00011].
