# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 10.4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10.4. Notice, etc. to solicitors

Where in railway administration proceedings a notice or other document is required or authorised to be given to a person, it may, if he has indicated that his solicitor is authorised to accept service on his behalf, be given instead to the solicitor.
