# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 10.7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10.7. “Venue”

References to the “venue” for any proceedings or attendance before the court, or for a meeting, are to the time, date and place for the proceedings, attendance or meeting.
