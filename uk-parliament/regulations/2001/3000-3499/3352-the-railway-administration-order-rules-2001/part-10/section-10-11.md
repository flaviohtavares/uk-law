# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 10.11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10.11. Application

The Rules apply to railway administration proceedings commenced on or after the date on which the Rules come into force. Nothing contained in the Insolvency Rules 1986[^f00012] shall apply to such proceedings commenced on or after that date.
