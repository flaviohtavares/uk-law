# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 10.5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10.5. Notice to joint special railway administrators

Where two or more persons are acting jointly as the special railway administrator in any proceedings, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to them all.
