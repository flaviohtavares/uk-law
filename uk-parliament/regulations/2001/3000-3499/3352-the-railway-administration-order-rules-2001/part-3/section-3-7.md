# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 3.7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.7. Notice to members of proposals to creditors

For the purposes of section 23(2)(b) of the 1986 Act, the notice shall be published once in the Gazette and once in the newspaper in which the making of the railway administration order was advertised.
