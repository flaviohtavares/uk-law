# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 5.8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5.8. Order filling vacancy

Where the court makes an order filling a vacancy in the office of special railway administrator, the same provisions apply in respect of giving notice of, and advertising, the order as in the case of the railway administration order.
