# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 3352

INSOLVENCY, ENGLAND AND WALES

COMPANIES, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001

Made

6th October 2001

Laid before Parliament

8th October 2001

Coming into force

7th October 2001

Whereas the only provision made by the following Rules is provision applying rules made under section 411 of the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00001], with modifications, for the purposes of provision made by sections 59 to 65 of, and Schedules 6 and 7 to, the Railways Act 1993[^f00002];

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of his powers under section 411 of the Insolvency Act 1986 and section 59(5) of the Railways Act 1993, with the concurrence of the Secretary of State, hereby makes the following Rules:

These Rules set out the procedure for the conduct of railway administration proceedings for protected railway companies under the Insolvency Act 1986 and the Railways Act 1993. They apply the relevant rules contained in the Insolvency Rules 1986 with modifications.
