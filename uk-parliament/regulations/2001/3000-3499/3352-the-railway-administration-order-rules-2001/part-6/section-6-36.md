# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 6.36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.36. Special railway administrator’s solicitor

Where in railway administration proceedings the attendance of the special railway administrator’s solicitor is required, whether in court or in chambers, the special railway administrator himself need not attend, unless directed by the court.
