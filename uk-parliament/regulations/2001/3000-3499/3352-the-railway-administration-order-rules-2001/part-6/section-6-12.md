# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 6.12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.12. Enforcement of court orders

In any railway administration proceedings, orders of the court may be enforced in the same manner as a judgment to the same effect.
