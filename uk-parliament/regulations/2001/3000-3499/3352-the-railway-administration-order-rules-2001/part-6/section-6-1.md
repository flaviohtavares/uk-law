# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 6.1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.1. Preliminary

This Chapter applies to any application made to the court in railway administration proceedings, except a petition for a railway administration order.
