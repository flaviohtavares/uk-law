# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 6.11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.11. Appointment and remuneration of shorthand writers

(1) In the High Court the judge may in writing nominate one or more persons to be official shorthand writers to the court.

*\[Form 21\]*

(2) The court may, at any time in the course of railway administration proceedings, appoint a shorthand writer to take down the evidence of a person examined in the course of those proceedings.

*\[Forms 21 and 22\]*

(3) The remuneration of a shorthand writer appointed in railway administration proceedings shall be paid by the party at whose instance the appointment was made, or out of the assets of the protected railway company, or otherwise, as the court may direct.

(4) Any question arising as to the rates of remuneration payable under this Rule shall be determined by the court in its discretion.
