# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 6.16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.16. Court records

The court shall keep records of all railway administration proceedings, and shall cause to be entered in the records the taking of any step in the proceedings, and such decisions of the court in relation thereto, as the court thinks fit.
