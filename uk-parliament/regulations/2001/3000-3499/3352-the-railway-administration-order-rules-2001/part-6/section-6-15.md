# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 6.15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.15. Title of proceedings

Every railway administration proceeding shall, with any necessary additions, be intituled “IN THE MATTER OF …(naming the protected railway company to which the proceedings relate) AND IN THE MATTER OF THE INSOLVENCY ACT 1986 AND THE RAILWAYS ACT 1993”.
