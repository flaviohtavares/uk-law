# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 6.21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.21. Application of the Civil Procedure Rules

Subject to provision to inconsistent effect made as follows in this Chapter CPR Part 43 (scope of costs rules and definitions), Part 44 (general rules about costs), Part 45 (fixed costs), Part 47 (procedure for detailed assessment of costs and default provisions) and Part 48 (costs—special cases) shall apply to railway administration proceedings with any necessary modifications.
