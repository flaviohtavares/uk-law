# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 6.38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.38. Restriction on concurrent proceedings and remedies

Where in railway administration proceedings the court makes an order staying any action, execution or other legal process against the property of a protected railway company, service of the order may be effected by sending a sealed copy of the order to whatever is the address for service of the claimant or other party having the carriage of the proceedings to be stayed.
