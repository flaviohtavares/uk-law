# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 6.31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.31. Affidavit in support of application

(1) Except where made by the official receiver, an application under Rule 6.30(3) shall be supported by an affidavit of a registered medical practitioner as to the mental or physical condition of the incapacitated person.

(2) In the excepted case, a report made by the official receiver is sufficient.
