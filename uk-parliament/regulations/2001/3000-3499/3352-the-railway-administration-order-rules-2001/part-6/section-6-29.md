# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 6.29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.29. Introductory

(1) The Rules in this Chapter apply where in railway administration proceedings it appears to the court that a person affected by the proceedings is one who is incapable of managing and administering his property and affairs either—

- (a) by reason of mental disorder within the meaning of the Mental Health Act 1983[^f00009], or
- (b) due to physical affliction or disability.

(2) The person concerned is referred to as “the incapacitated person”.
