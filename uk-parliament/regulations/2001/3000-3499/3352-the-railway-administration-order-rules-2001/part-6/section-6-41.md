# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 6.41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6.41. Payment into court

The CPR relating to payment into and out of court of money lodged in court as security for costs apply to money lodged in court under the Rules.
