# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 9.6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9.6. Forms for use in railway administration proceedings

(1) The forms contained in the Schedule to the Rules shall be used in, and in connection with, railway administration proceedings.

(2) The forms shall be used with such variations, if any, as the circumstances may require.
