# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 9.15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9.15. Charge for copy documents

Where in railway administration proceedings the special railway administrator is requested by a creditor or member to supply copies of any documents, he is entitled to require the payment of the appropriate fee in respect of the supply of the documents.
