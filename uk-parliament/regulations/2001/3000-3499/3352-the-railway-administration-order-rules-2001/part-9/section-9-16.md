# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 9.16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9.16. Non-receipt of notice of meeting

Where in accordance with the 1986 Act or the Rules a meeting of creditors is called or summoned by notice, the meeting is presumed to have been duly summoned and held, notwithstanding that not all those to whom the notice is to be given have received it.
