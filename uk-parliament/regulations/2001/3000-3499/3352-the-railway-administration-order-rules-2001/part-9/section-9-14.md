# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 9.14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9.14. Right to copy documents

Where under the 1986 Act or the Rules a person has a right to inspect documents, the right includes that of taking copies of those documents, on payment—

- (a) in the case of documents on the court’s file of proceedings, of the fee chargeable under any order made under section 130 of the Supreme Court Act 1981[^f00010], and
- (b) otherwise, of the appropriate fee.
