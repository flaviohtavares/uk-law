# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 9.20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9.20. Punishment of offences

Section 431 (summary proceedings) of the 1986 Act, as it applies to England and Wales, has effect in relation to offences under the Rules as to offences under that Act.
