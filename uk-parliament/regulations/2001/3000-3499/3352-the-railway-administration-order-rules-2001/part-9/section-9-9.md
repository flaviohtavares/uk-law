# The Railway Administration Order Rules 2001, section 9.9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9.9. Service by post

(1) Section 149 of the 1993 Act applies as regards the service of documents in railway administration proceedings.

(2) Where a document is served by post, the document is treated as served, where first class post is used on the second business day after the date of posting, and where second class post is used on the fourth business day after the date of posting, unless the contrary is shown.

(3) The date of posting is presumed, unless the contrary is shown, to be the date shown in the post-mark on the envelope in which the document is contained.
