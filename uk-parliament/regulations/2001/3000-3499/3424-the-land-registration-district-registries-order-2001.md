# The Land Registration (District Registries) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 3424

LAND REGISTRATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Land Registration (District Registries) Order 2001

Made

16th October 2001

Coming into force

2nd January 2002

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 132(1) of the Land Registration Act 1925[^f00001], and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order, which supersedes the Land Registration (District Registries) Order 2000[^f00002], may be cited as the Land Registration (District Registries) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 2nd January 2002.

(2) In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires:

- “district land registry” means a district registry of the Land Registry within the meaning of section 132 of the Land Registration Act 1925;
- “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order.

## 2. District Land Registries

The district of each district land registry shall comprise on and after 2nd January 2002 the administrative areas specified opposite its name in column 2 of the Schedule.
