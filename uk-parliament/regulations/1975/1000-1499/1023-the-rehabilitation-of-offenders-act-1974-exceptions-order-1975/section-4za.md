**4ZA (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^key-55e87983ade9f85a24d20cf89a7e6545][^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] Neither paragraph (b) of section 4(3) of, nor paragraph 3(5) of Schedule 2 to, the Act applies in relation to— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] any office, employment or occupation specified in paragraph *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] 1,[^key-f995d3c7e70d5ccdfde03db2ecddc013] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] 6, 16, 17, 18, 18A, 31, 32, 35 or 36 of Part II of that Schedule or paragraph 1 or 8 of Part III of that Schedule or any other work specified in paragraph 35 or 36 of Part II of that Schedule; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] any action taken for the purpose of safeguarding national security. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718]
