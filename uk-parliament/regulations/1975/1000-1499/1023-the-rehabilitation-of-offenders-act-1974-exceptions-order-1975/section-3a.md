**3A (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^key-e67b309fab7f72f6eb4bca1388278b69][^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] (1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] Subject to paragraph (1A), neither[^key-344b0d222bafebc21415125d8b83422d] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] section 4(2) of, nor paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 2 to, the Act applies to a question to which paragraph (2) or (3) applies. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718]

(1A (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e29ae7baaf8035529f4828456d18a56][^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] Paragraph (1) does not apply in relation to a protected caution or a protected conviction. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] This paragraph applies to any question asked by or on behalf of any person (“A”), in the course of the duties of A’s office or employment, in order to assess the suitability of the person to whom the question relates (“B”) for any work which is a controlled activity relating to children within the meaning of section 21 of the 2006 Act *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] as it had effect immediately before the coming into force of section 68 of the Protection of Freedoms Act 2012[^key-c263f143d27ec64f8dc34f7f2016e12b] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718], where the person questioned is told at the time the question is asked, that by virtue of this Order, spent convictions are to be disclosed but only if that person knows that B— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] is a person barred from regulated activity relating to children within the meaning of section 3(2) of the 2006 Act; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] is included in the list kept under section 1 of the Protection of Children Act 1999; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] is subject to a direction made under section 142 of the Education Act 2002. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] This paragraph applies to any question asked by or on behalf of any person (“A”), in the course of the duties of A’s office or employment, in order to assess the suitability of the person to whom the question relates (“B”) for any work which is a controlled activity relating to vulnerable adults within the meaning of section 22 of the 2006 Act *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] as it had effect immediately before the coming into force of section 68 of the Protection of Freedoms Act 2012[^key-e66eebfe8b70775e78e11f1b5eb7092a] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718], where the person questioned is told at the time the question is asked, that by virtue of this Order, spent convictions are to be disclosed but only if that person knows that B— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] is a person barred from regulated activity relating to vulnerable adults within the meaning of section 3(3) of the 2006 Act; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718] is included in the list kept under section 81 of the Care Standards Act 2000. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f78d75a614c86a2b6fc0bb6b9d60d718]
