# The Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1975 No. 1375 (c.42)

SPORTS GROUNDS

The Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975

Made

14th August 1975

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 19(6) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975.

**2.** The Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975 shall come into force on 1st September 1975.

Roy Jenkins

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

Whitehall

14th August 1975

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975 on 1st September 1975.
