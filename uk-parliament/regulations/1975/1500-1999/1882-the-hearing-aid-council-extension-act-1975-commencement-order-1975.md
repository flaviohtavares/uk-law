# The Hearing Aid Council (Extension) Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1975 No. 1882 (C.53)

HEARING AID COUNCIL

The Hearing Aid Council (Extension) Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975

Made

19th November 1975

The Secretary of State in exercise of the power conferred by section 2(2) of the Hearing Aid Council (Extension) Act 1975 hereby orders as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Hearing Aid Council (Extension) Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975.

**2.** The Hearing Aid Council (Extension) Act 1975 shall come into operation on 29th December 1975.

Alan Williams

Minister of State

Department of Prices and Consumer Protection

19th November 1975
