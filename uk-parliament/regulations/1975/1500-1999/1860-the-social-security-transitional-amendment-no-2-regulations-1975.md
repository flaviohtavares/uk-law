# The Social Security (Transitional) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 1975

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1975 No. 1860

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Social Security (Transitional) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 1975

Made

14th November 1975

Laid before Parliament

17th November 1975

Coming into Operation

19th November 1975

The Secretary of State for Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 2 of, and paragraphs 3, 5 and 9 of Schedule 3 to, the Social Security (Consequential Provisions) Act 1975 and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following regulations:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Transitional) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 1975 and shall come into operation on 19th November 1975.

## 2. Cancellation of revocation

So as to provide for regulation 6 of the Social Security (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 1975[^f00001] (transitional provisions relating to certain persons who are or have been outside Great Britain) to continue in force after the coming into operation of the Social Security (Transitional) Amendment Regulations 1975[^f00002], regulation 4 of the last mentioned regulations, which makes provision for the revocation of the said regulation 6, is hereby revoked.

Barbara Castle

Secretary of State for Social Services

14th November 1975
