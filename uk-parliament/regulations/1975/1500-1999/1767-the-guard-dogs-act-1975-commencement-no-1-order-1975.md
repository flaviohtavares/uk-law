# The Guard Dogs Act 1975 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1975

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1975 No. 1767 (C.50)

DOGS

The Guard Dogs Act 1975 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1975

Made

29th October 1975

In pursuance of section 8(2) of the Guard Dogs Act 1975, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Guard Dogs Act 1975 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1975.

**2.** (1) The Guard Dogs Act 1975, except the provisions specified in paragraph (2) below, shall come into force on 1st February 1976.

(2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) above are—

- (a) section 2 (restriction on keeping guard dogs without a licence);
- (b) section 3 (guard dog kennel licences);
- (c) section 4 (appeals);
- (d) section 5 (offences, penalties and civil liability) so far as its provisions relate to sections 2, 3, 4 and 6; and
- (e) section 6 (regulations).

Roy Jenkins

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

Whitehall

29th October 1975

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st February 1976 the provisions of the Guard Dogs Act 1975, except those provisions which relate to the establishment of a licensing scheme for guard dog kennels.
