# The Industry Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1975 No. 1881 (C.52)

INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT MANUFACTURING UNDERTAKINGS

The Industry Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975

Made

18th November 1975

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 39(6) of the Industry Act 1975 (hereinafter referred to as “**the Act**”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Industry Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975.

**2.** The provisions of the Act shall come into force on 20th November 1975.

Eric Varley

Secretary of State for Industry

18th November 1975

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on the date specified in it the provisions of the Industry Act 1975.
