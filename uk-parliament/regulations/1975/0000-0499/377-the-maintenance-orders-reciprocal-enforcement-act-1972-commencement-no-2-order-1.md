# The Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1972 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1975

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1975 No. 377 (c.11)

MAINTENANCE OF DEPENDANTS

The Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1972 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1975

Made

13th March 1975

In pursuance of section 49(2) of the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1972, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1972 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1975.

**2.** Part II of the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1972 shall come into operation on 12th April 1975.

Roy Jenkins

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

Whitehall

13th March 1975

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 12th April 1975 Part II of the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1972, which provides for the reciprocal enforcement of claims for the recovery of Maintenance.
