# The Fishing Vessels (Safety Provisions) Rules 1975, section 127

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 127. Duration of certificates

Except as otherwise provided in these Rules, a United Kingdom fishing vessel certificate shall be in force for 48 months from the date of its issue or such shorter period as may be specified in the certificate.
