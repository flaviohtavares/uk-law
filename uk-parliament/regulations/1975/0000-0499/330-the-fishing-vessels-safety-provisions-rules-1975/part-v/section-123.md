# The Fishing Vessels (Safety Provisions) Rules 1975, section 123

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 123. Surveys and periodical inspections

Every vessel of 12 metres in length and over to which these Rules apply shall be surveyed and periodically inspected in accordance with the requirements of this Part of these Rules.
