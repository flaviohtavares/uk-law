## Annotations

[^f00011]: Footnote: The outer limits of the smooth water areas specified in the second column of this Schedule shall be taken to be the corresponding inner limits of the partially smooth water areas specified in the third column of this Schedule.

[^f00012]: Footnote: Unless otherwise indicated these limits apply at all times of the year. In this Schedule “**summer**” means the months of April to October, inclusive, and “**winter**” means the months of November to March, inclusive.
