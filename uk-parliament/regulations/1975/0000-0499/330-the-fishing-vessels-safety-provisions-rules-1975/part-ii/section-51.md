# The Fishing Vessels (Safety Provisions) Rules 1975, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Refrigerating plants

(1) In every vessel of 12 metres in length and over to which these Rules apply refrigerating plants shall be of a design and construction adequate for the service for which they are intended and shall be so installed and protected as to reduce to a minimum any danger to persons on board.

(2) In every such vessel ammonia and methylchloride shall not be used as refrigerants.
