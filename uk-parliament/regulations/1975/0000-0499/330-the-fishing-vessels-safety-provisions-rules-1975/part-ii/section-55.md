# The Fishing Vessels (Safety Provisions) Rules 1975, section 55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 55. Structural fire protection—general

Every vessel of 12 metres in length and over to which these Rules apply shall be so constructed and equipped that there is no substantial fire risk to the vessel or to persons on board the vessel.
