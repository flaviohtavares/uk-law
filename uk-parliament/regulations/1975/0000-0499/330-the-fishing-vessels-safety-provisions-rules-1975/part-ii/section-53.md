# The Fishing Vessels (Safety Provisions) Rules 1975, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Spare gear

In every vessel of 12 metres in length and over to which these Rules apply adequate spare gear shall be provided for main and auxiliary machinery and electrical equipment and installations of the vessel having regard to the intended service of the vessel.
