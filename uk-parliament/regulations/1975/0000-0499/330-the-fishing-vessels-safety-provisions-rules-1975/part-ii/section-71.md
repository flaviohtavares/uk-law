# The Fishing Vessels (Safety Provisions) Rules 1975, section 71

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 71. Flags and signalling equipment

Every vessel of 12 metres in length and over to which these Rules apply shall be provided with:—

- (a) a proper complement of flags and pennants for communication by the international code of signals when proceeding on a distant water voyage;
- (b) an efficient signalling lamp capable of being used both by day and by night and which shall be provided with a battery of sufficient capacity to operate the lamp continuously for not less than two hours.
