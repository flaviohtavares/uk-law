# The Fishing Vessels (Safety Provisions) Rules 1975, section 100

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 100. Stowage of pyrotechnic distress signals

In every vessel to which these Rules apply all pyrotechnic distress signals shall be packed in a watertight container and shall be clearly and indelibly labelled to indicate their purpose.
