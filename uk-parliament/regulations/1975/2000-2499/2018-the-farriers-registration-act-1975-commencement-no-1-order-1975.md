# The Farriers (Registration) Act 1975 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1975

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1975 No. 2018 (C.56)

ANIMALS

The Farriers (Registration) Act 1975 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1975

Made

2nd December 1975

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 19(2) of the Farriers (Registration) Act 1975, I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Farriers (Registration) Act 1975 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1975.

**2.** The Farriers (Registration) Act 1975, except section 16, shall come into force on 1st January 1976.

Roy Jenkins

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

Home Office

Whitehall

2nd December 1975

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st January 1976 the provisions of the Farriers (Registration) Act 1975 other than section 16 (offences by unregistered persons).
