# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 1374

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2023

Made

13th December 2023

Coming into force

14th December 2023

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 13th day of December 2023

Present,

The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2023 and comes into force on 14th December 2023.

## 2. Appointment of His Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The person named in the Schedule to this Order is appointed as His Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Ceri King

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council
