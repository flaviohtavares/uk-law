# The Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 1088

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2023

Made

11th October 2023

Coming into force

1st January 2024

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of October 2023

Present,

The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 113(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2023 and comes into force on 1st January 2024.

## 2. Appointment to the Office of His Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

Sir Martyn Ellis Oliver is appointed to the office of His Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills for a period of 5 years from 1st January 2024.

## 3. Revocation

The Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2021[^f00002] is revoked.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints Sir Martyn Ellis Oliver to be His Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills for a period of 5 years from 1st January 2024.
