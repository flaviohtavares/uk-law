# The Awel y Môr Offshore Wind Farm Order 2023, section 13

> Text as at 2024-07-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Access to works[^key-b050dcfa08b9dd4fda217c61bde97371]

*Extent: E+W*

The undertaker may, for the purposes of the authorised development, form and lay out means of access, or improve existing means of access for the purposes of the authorised development within the Order limits from the streets listed in Schedule 5 (access to works) and shown on the street works and access plan; and as part of Work No. 41.
