# The National College for Advanced Transport and Infrastructure (Designated Institution in Further Education and Revocations) (Revocation) Order 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 1057

Education, England

The National College for Advanced Transport and Infrastructure (Designated Institution in Further Education and Revocations) (Revocation) Order 2023

Made

29th September 2023

Laid before Parliament

2nd October 2023

Coming into force

23rd October 2023

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 28(1) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement, and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the National College for Advanced Transport and Infrastructure (Designated Institution in Further Education and Revocations) (Revocation) Order 2023 and comes into force on 23rd October 2023.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Revocation

The National College for Advanced Transport and Infrastructure (Designated Institution in Further Education and Revocations) Order 2021[^f00002] is revoked.

Robert Halfon

Minister of State

Department for Education

29th September 2023

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the National College for Advanced Transport and Infrastructure (Designated Institution in Further Education and Revocations) Order 2021 ([S.I. 2021/446](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/446)) (“**the 2021 Order**”) with the effect that the National College for Advanced Transport and Infrastructure is no longer a designated institution for the purposes of section 28 of the Further and Higher Education Act [1992 (c. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/13). This Order extends to England and Wales and, in line with the 2021 Order, it applies in relation to England only.

The 2021 Order revoked the National College for High Speed Rail (Incorporation) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/1457](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1457)) and the National College for High Speed Rail (Government) Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/1458](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1458)). Those revocations will be unaffected by this Order, by virtue of sections 15 and 23 of the Interpretation Act [1978 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/30).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations. The instrument has no impact on the public sector.
