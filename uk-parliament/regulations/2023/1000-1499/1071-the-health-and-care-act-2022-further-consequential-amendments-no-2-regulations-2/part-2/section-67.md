# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 67

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Amendment of the Functions of the National Health Service Commissioning Board and the NHS Business Services Authority (Awdurdod Gwasanaethau Busnes y GIG) (Primary Dental Services) (England) Regulations 2013[^c-6b5b7bbff4c450a7f4fd1e6afc99582f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Functions of the National Health Service Commissioning Board and the NHS Business Services Authority (Awdurdod Gwasanaethau Busnes y GIG) (Primary Dental Services) (England) Regulations 2013[^f00056] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1, in paragraph (2)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
