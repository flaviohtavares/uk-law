> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Amendment of the National Health Service (Performers Lists) (England) Regulations 2013[^key-d55fd8d1c104525656a4c063a35ec3f5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Performers Lists) (England) Regulations 2013[^f00050] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
