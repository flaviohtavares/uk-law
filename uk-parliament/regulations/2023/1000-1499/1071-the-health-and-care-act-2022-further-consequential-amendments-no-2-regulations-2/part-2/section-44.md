## 44. Amendment of the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2010[^c-74aedbd7b7f2542c9fcfbdc35820b4c4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2010[^f00035] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1) in the definition of “equivalent body” in sub-paragraph (a), for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
