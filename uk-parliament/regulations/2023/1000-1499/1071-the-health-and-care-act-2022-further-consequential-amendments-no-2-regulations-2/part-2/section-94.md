# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 94

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Amendment of the National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Scotland) Regulations 2018[^key-bc05a82f566a427ce442fce0016ec263]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Scotland) Regulations 2018[^f00076] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 6—

- (a) in paragraph 19(1)(b) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”;
- (b) in paragraph 88(2) in the definition of “NHS body” for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
