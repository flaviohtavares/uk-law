# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 34

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Amendment of the Local Authority Social Services and National Health Service Complaints (England) Regulations 2009[^c-2358d182352f271f3945a03b53921edb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Local Authority Social Services and National Health Service Complaints (England) Regulations 2009[^f00028] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 7—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(b) for “Board” substitute “NHS England”;
- (b) in paragraph (1A)(b) for “Board”, in each place it occurs, substitute “NHS England”;
- (c) in paragraph (3)(a) for “Board” substitute “NHS England”;
- (d) in paragraph (3)(b) for “Board” substitute “NHS England”;
- (e) in paragraph (4) for “Board”, in the second place it occurs, substitute “NHS England”;
- (f) in paragraph (5)(a) for “Board” substitute “NHS England”.
