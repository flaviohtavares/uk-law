# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 69

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Amendment of the National Health Service (Direct Payments) Regulations 2013[^c-ad8dd6a820162e3d8011083ef6f87dc1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Direct Payments) Regulations 2013[^f00058] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
