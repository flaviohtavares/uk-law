> Text as at 2025-02-24.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Amendment of the Healthcare (European Economic Area and Switzerland Arrangements) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^c-eaeec92299b6439107c8562b9017d4b1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Healthcare (European Economic Area and Switzerland Arrangements) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00082] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1, in paragraph (3) in the definition of “relevant health board”, in paragraph (a) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England, the body corporate”.
