## 37. Amendment of the Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 2009 (English language text)[^c-27600fcfd063bac30db45f393331367f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The English language text of the Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 2009[^f00030] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1, in paragraph (2) in the definition of “health service body” in sub-paragraph (e), for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
