# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 57

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Amendment of the NHS Bodies and Local Authorities (Partnership Arrangements, Care Trusts, Public Health and Local Healthwatch) Regulations 2012[^key-c0483184aa8312701db34babcab4ff25]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The NHS Bodies and Local Authorities (Partnership Arrangements, Care Trusts, Public Health and Local Healthwatch) Regulations 2012[^f00046] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
