# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 84

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Amendment of the National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015[^c-1a56db580cab6dabb891f1c2683bd510]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015 [^f00069] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the Act;

  .
