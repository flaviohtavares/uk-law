# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 101

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Amendment of the Regulated Adoption Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019 (Welsh language text)[^c-bd17eb33249aa3d92dd2b183576d1fc4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Welsh language text of the Regulated Adoption Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019 (Rheoliadau Gwasanaethau Mabwysiadu Rheoleiddiedig (Darparwyr Gwasanaethau ac Unigolion Cyfrifol) (Cymru) 2019) is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “ystyr “GIG Lloegr” *(“NHS England”)* yw’r corff corfforaethol a sefydlwyd o dan adran 1H o Ddeddf y Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol 2006;

  .

(3) In regulation 31—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(a) for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”;
- (b) in paragraph (3)(a) for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”;
- (c) in paragraph (7)(a) for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol”, in each place it occurs, substitute “GIG Lloegr”.

(4) In Schedule 3—

- (a) in the title of Part 2 for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”;
- (b) in the title of Part 3 for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.
