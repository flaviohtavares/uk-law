## 74. Amendment of the Emergency Ambulance Services Committee (Wales) Regulations 2014 (English language text)[^key-cae334edf7e24d07f38eb541bb981ef3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The English language text of the Emergency Ambulance Services Committee (Wales) Regulations 2014[^f00061] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in the definition of “health service body” for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
