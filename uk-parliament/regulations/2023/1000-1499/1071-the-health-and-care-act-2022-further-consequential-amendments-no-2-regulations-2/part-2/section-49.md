## 49. Amendment of the National Health Service (Concerns, Complaints and Redress Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2011 (Welsh language text)[^key-418353fd5a38118ac0fc9e5b92363061]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Welsh language text of the National Health Service (Concerns, Complaints and Redress Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2011 (Rheoliadau’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol (Trefniadau Pryderon, Cwynion ac Iawn) (Cymru) 2011) is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 34, in paragraph (1)(cb) for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.
