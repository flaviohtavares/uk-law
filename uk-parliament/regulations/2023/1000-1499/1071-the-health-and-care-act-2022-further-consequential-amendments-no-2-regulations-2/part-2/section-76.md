# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 76

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Amendment of the Care and Support (Provision of Health Services) Regulations 2014[^c-d894bbeee13b64fcdae907e878b14d9a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Care and Support (Provision of Health Services) Regulations 2014[^f00062] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1, in paragraph (2)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the National Health Service Act 2006;

  .
