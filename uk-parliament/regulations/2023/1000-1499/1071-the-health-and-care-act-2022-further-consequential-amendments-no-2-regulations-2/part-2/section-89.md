## 89. Amendment of the Registration of Private Dentistry (Wales) Regulations 2017 (Welsh language text)[^c-d67ca319a1af9484c08c3badcacf886f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Welsh language text of the Registration of Private Dentistry (Wales) Regulations 2017 (Rheoliadau Cofrestru Deintyddiaeth Breifat (Cymru) 2017) is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3, in paragraph (1) in the definition of “rhestr perfformwyr deintyddol” for “Fwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.
