## 105. Amendment of the Digital Health and Care Wales (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 2020 (English language text)[^c-76a79bbc518c1fc02755aa40b05071df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The English language text of the Digital Health and Care Wales (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 2020[^f00084] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in the definition of “health service body” for paragraph (g) substitute—

> - (g) NHS England, the body corporate established under section 1H of the National Health Service Act 2006,

.
