# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 31

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Amendment of the Mental Health (Hospital, Guardianship, Community Treatment and Consent to Treatment) (Wales) Regulations 2008 (English language text)[^key-55f83f5a47ff8b86d4dd99999df043e0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The English language text of the Mental Health (Hospital, Guardianship, Community Treatment and Consent to Treatment) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^f00026] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 23, in paragraph (6)(b) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.

(3) In regulation 25, in paragraph (5) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.

(4) In regulation 26, in paragraph (6)(b) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
