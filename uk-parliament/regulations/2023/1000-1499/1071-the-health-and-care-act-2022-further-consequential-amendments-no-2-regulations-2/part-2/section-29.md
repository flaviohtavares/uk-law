# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 29

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Amendment of the General Ophthalmic Services Contracts Regulations 2008[^key-420938dd70d307d18d04a902b8c3309b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The General Ophthalmic Services Contracts Regulations 2008[^f00024] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the Act;

  .

(3) In Schedule 1, in the title of paragraph 14 for “Board” substitute “NHS England”.
