## 33. Amendment of the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2009[^c-f5d1cd3d4cd25090ed3184e04eddc546]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2009[^f00027] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1), in the definition of “equivalent body” in sub-paragraph (a), for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
