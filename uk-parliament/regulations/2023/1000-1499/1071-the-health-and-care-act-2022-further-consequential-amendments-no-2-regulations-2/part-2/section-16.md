## 16. Amendment of the Special Guardianship (Wales) Regulations 2005 (Welsh language text)[^c-f835251a92f582273012b64585a82079]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Welsh language text of the Special Guardianship (Wales) Regulations 2005 (Rheoliadau Gwarcheidiaeth Arbennig (Cymru) 2005) is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3, in paragraph (3)(b) for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.

(3) In regulation 6, in paragraph (1)(c) for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.

(4) In regulation 11, in paragraph (3) for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.
