# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 42

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Amendment of the National Health Service (Functions of the First-tier Tribunal relating to Primary Medical, Dental and Ophthalmic Services) Regulations 2010[^key-5da05daeb1739fa7b4989d8af76f73fc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Functions of the First-tier Tribunal relating to Primary Medical, Dental and Ophthalmic Services) Regulations 2010[^f00033] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the Act;

  .
