## 83. Amendment of the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015 (Welsh language text)[^key-ce5cc57bd6e8b0caa70535129668ba19]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Welsh language text of the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015 (Rheoliadau Cynllunio Gofal, Lleoli ac Adolygu Achosion (Cymru) 2015) is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1), in the definition of “darparwr gofal iechyd” for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.

(3) In regulation 14, in paragraph (2)(f) for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.
