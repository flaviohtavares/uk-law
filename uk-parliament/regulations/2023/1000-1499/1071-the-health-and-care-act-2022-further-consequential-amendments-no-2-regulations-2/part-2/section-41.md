## 41. Amendment of the Welsh Health Specialised Services Committee (Wales) Regulations 2009 (Welsh language text)[^key-517e919263b0d98be62a5ec6ee47d0b0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Welsh language text of the Welsh Health Specialised Services Committee (Wales) Regulations 2009 (Rheoliadau Pwyllgor Gwasanaethau Iechyd Arbenigol Cymru (Cymru) 2009) is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in the definition of “corff gwasanaeth iechyd” for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.
