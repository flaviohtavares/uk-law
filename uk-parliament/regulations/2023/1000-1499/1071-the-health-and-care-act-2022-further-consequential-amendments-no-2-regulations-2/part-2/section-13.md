# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 13

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Amendment of the Adoption Support Services (Local Authorities) (Wales) Regulations 2005 (English language text)[^key-86cffa17b88f10ac23ff12ee408f4f30]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The English language text of the Adoption Support Services (Local Authorities) (Wales) Regulations 2005[^f00012] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 5, in paragraph (1)(db) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.

(3) In regulation 8, in paragraph (4)(a) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.

(4) In regulation 10, in paragraph (3)(b)(i) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.

(5) In regulation 13, in paragraph (2)—

- (a) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”;
- (b) for “National Health Service Commissioning Board”, in the second place it occurs, substitute “NHS England”.
