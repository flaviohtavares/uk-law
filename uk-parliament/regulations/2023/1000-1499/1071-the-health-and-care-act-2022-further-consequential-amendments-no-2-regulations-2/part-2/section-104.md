## 104. Amendment of the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) (Wales) Regulations 2020 (Welsh language text)[^key-e82a2e08cd3a31ae94d76eba2e1baee7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Welsh language text of the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) (Wales) Regulations 2020 (Rheoliadau’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol (Gwasanaethau Fferyllol) (Cymru) 2020) is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1), in the definition of “corff cyfatebol” for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.

(3) In regulation 42, in paragraph (2)(h) for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.

(4) In Schedule 5, in paragraph 28(3)(c)(iv) for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.

(5) In Schedule 6, in paragraph 17(3)(c)(iv) for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.
