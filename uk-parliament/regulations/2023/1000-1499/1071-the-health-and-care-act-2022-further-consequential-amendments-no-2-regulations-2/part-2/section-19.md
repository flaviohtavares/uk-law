> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Amendment of the National Health Service (Dental Charges) Regulations 2005[^c-4aa0efd1fac400f1a148eb8839d48741]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Dental Charges) Regulations 2005[^f00016] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
