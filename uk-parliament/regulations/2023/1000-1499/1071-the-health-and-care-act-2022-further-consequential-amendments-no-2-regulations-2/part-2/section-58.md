# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 58

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Amendment of the Local Authority (Public Health, Health and Wellbeing Boards and Health Scrutiny) Regulations 2013[^c-59584e6278943b5acfb31299f88473d9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Local Authority (Public Health, Health and Wellbeing Boards and Health Scrutiny) Regulations 2013[^f00047] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 20, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
