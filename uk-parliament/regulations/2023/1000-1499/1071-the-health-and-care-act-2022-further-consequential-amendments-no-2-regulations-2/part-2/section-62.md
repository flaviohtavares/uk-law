# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 62

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Amendment of the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013[^key-3509ca5f060a1177135852ad0592a7f9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013[^f00051] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “NHSCB”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
