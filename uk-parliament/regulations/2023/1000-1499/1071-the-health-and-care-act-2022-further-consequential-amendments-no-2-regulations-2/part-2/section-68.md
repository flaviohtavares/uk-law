# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 68

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Amendment of the National Health Service (Procurement, Patient Choice and Competition) (No.2) Regulations 2013[^c-d4e1306957e4328ba21e6f2fd571db33]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Procurement, Patient Choice and Competition) (No.2) Regulations 2013[^f00057] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1, in paragraph (2)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
