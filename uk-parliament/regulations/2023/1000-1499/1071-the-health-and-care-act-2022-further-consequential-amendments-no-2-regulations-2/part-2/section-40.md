## 40. Amendment of the Welsh Health Specialised Services Committee (Wales) Regulations 2009 (English language text)[^key-98dbe363241b28758ca3e12fc0436b3b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The English language text of the Welsh Health Specialised Services Committee (Wales) Regulations 2009[^f00032] is amended as follows.
