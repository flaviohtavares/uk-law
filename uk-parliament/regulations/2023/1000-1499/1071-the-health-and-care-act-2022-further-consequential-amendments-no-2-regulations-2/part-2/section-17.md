>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Amendment of the National Health Service (General Dental Services Contracts) Regulations 2005[^c-959cd6df22fc24effaf8a55f126d23ae]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (General Dental Services Contracts) Regulations 2005[^f00014] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
