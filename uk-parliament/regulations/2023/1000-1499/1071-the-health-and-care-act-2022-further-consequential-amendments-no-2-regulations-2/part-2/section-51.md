# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 51

> Text as at 2025-02-24.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Amendment of the Council Tax Reduction (State Pension Credit) (Scotland) Regulations 2012[^key-b7fb8c6601fde90fd893d934d66b03f0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Council Tax Reduction (State Pension Credit) (Scotland) Regulations 2012[^f00041] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 35, in paragraph (2)(d)(v) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board established by section 1H of the National Health Service Act 2006” substitute “NHS England, the body corporate established under section 1H of the National Health Service Act 2006”.
