# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 8

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Amendment of the Health and Personal Social Services (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004[^c-e32c4037580a129a74d69764d83b2e64]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Health and Personal Social Services (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00008] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in the definition of “medical card” for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.

(3) In Schedule 5—

- (a) in paragraph 22(1)(b) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”;
- (b) in paragraph 89(2) in the definition of “NHS body” for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
