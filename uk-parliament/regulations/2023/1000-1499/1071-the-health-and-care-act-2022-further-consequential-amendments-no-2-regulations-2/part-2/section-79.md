# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 79

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Amendment of the Social Security (Information-sharing) (NHS Payments and Remission of Charges etc) (England) Regulations 2015[^key-ea1de8ee6b9007392df21e154021ff5c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Social Security (Information-sharing) (NHS Payments and Remission of Charges etc) (England) Regulations 2015[^f00065] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2—

- (a) omit the definition of “the NHSCB”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
