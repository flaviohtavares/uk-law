# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 63

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Amendment of the National Health Service (Primary Dental Services) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2013[^key-a1d8c176faad30068b94b72ca63194d4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Primary Dental Services) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2013[^f00052] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1—

- (a) in paragraph 1 for “Board” substitute “NHS England”;
- (b) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) omit the definition of “the Board”;
  - (ii) at the appropriate place insert—

    > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

    ;
- (c) in paragraph 12(2)(a) for “the “Board”” substitute ““NHS England””.

(3) In Schedule 2—

- (a) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) omit the definition of “the Board”;
  - (ii) at the appropriate place insert—

    > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

    ;
- (b) in paragraph 12(2)(a) for “the “Board”” substitute ““NHS England””.
