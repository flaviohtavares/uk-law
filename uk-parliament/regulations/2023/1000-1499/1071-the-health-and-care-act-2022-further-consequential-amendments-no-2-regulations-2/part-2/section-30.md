> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Amendment of the Primary Ophthalmic Services Regulations 2008[^key-660372e24961738bd1eb4bb260e0f17e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Primary Ophthalmic Services Regulations 2008[^f00025] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the Act;

  .
