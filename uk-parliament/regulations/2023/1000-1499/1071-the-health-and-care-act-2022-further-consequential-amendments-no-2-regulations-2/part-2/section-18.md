>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Amendment of the National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) Regulations 2005[^key-28de5031c3e3213fc32eb970b84740ef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) Regulations 2005[^f00015] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
