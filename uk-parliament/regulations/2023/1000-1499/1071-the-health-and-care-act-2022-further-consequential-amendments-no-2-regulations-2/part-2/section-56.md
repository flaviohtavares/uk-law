# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 56

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Amendment of the National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012[^c-12c29023119d5a7bea04cd0b79664976]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service Commissioning Board and Clinical Commissioning Groups (Responsibilities and Standing Rules) Regulations 2012[^f00045] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
