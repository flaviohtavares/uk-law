## 90. Amendment of the Trade Union (Facility Time Publication Requirements) Regulations 2017[^c-43415da8ad3e12c937b2a6f9456b23fc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Trade Union (Facility Time Publication Requirements) Regulations 2017[^f00073] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1, for paragraph 32 substitute—

> **32.** NHS England, the body corporate established under section 1H of the National Health Service Act 2006.
