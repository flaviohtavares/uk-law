> Text as at 2025-02-24.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Amendment of the Special Guardianship (Wales) Regulations 2005 (English language text)[^key-8421c1b55155461903c9be2f7749156a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The English language text of the Special Guardianship (Wales) Regulations 2005[^f00013] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3, in paragraph (3)(b) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.

(3) In regulation 6, in paragraph (1)(c) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.

(4) In regulation 11, in paragraph (3) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
