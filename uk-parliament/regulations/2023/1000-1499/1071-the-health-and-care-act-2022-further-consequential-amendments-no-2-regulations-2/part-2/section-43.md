# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 43

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Amendment of the Infrastructure Planning (Compulsory Acquisition) Regulations 2010[^key-cb8e14ce629f183f3a0133029fbc10bf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Infrastructure Planning (Compulsory Acquisition) Regulations 2010[^f00034] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “National Health Service Commissioning Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the National Health Service Act 2006;

  .
