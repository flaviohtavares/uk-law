## 85. Amendment of the National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015[^key-fa1664a2cfeb31cb8dc3f67568001fa6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015[^f00070] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the Act;

  .
