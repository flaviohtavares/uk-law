## 88. Amendment of the Registration of Private Dentistry (Wales) Regulations 2017 (English language text)[^c-ceb5663f344638fabc0c3dca454a6023]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The English language text of the Registration of Private Dentistry (Wales) Regulations 2017[^f00072] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3, in paragraph (1) in the definition of “dental performers list” for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
