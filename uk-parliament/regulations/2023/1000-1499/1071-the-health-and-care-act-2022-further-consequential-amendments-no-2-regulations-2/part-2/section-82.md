# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 82

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Amendment of the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015 (English language text)[^key-e423f3fd5446a4bc78bce021a9c1e170]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The English language text of the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015[^f00068] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1) in the definition of “health care provider” for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.

(3) In regulation 14, in paragraph (2)(f) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
