# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 95

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Amendment of the National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2018[^key-a0db0d514a613dc21cf2e06ac01c20a3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2018[^f00077] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1, in paragraph 55(2) in the definition of “NHS body” for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.

(3) In Schedule 2, in paragraph 13(1)(b) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
