# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 54

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Amendment of the NHS Commissioning Board Authority (Abolition and Transfer of Staff, Property and Liabilities) and the Health and Social Care Act 2012 (Consequential Amendments) Order 2012[^key-7fa65104203a1b7f41af1da8035c88d3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The NHS Commissioning Board Authority (Abolition and Transfer of Staff, Property and Liabilities) and the Health and Social Care Act 2012 (Consequential Amendments) Order 2012[^f00043] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the Act;

  .
