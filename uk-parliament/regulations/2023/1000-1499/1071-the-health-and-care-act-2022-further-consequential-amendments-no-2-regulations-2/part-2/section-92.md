## 92. Amendment of the Health Education and Improvement Wales Regulations 2017 (English language text)[^c-b42089b23957aeaf6951c010b4aed2b2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The English language text of the Health Education and Improvement Wales Regulations 2017[^f00075] is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in the definition of “health service body” in sub-paragraph (e) for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
