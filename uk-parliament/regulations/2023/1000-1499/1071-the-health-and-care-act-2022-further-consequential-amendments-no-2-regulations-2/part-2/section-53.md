## 53. Amendment of the Velindre National Health Service Trust Shared Services Committee (Wales) Regulations 2012 (Welsh language text)[^c-dbf6f23a1e9d9bf77036f9e815773e5d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Welsh language text of the Velindre National Health Service Trust Shared Services Committee (Wales) Regulations 2012 (Rheoliadau Pwyllgor Cydwasanaethau Ymddiriedolaeth Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol Felindre (Cymru) 2012) is amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in the definition of “corff gwasanaeth iechyd” for “Bwrdd Comisiynu’r Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol” substitute “GIG Lloegr”.
