# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 60

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Amendment of the National Health Service and Public Health (Functions and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2013[^c-67311355ba2efde923260dddad6170de]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service and Public Health (Functions and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2013[^f00049] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1, in paragraph (2)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the end insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act.

  .
