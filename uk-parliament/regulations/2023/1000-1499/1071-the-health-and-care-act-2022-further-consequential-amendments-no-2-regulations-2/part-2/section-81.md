# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 81

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Amendment of the National Health Service (Charges for Drugs and Appliances) Regulations 2015[^key-7c7986d40d7a95565e14235b6dfbc470]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Charges for Drugs and Appliances) Regulations 2015[^f00067] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
