# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Further Consequential Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2023, section 64

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Amendment of the National Health Service (Primary Ophthalmic Services) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2013[^key-7588247c9981494ead9337b721185289]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (Primary Ophthalmic Services) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2013[^f00053] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1, paragraph (2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (d), omit the definition of “the Board”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (e), at the end of the definition of “the GOS Regulations” omit “and”;
- (c) after sub-paragraph (e) insert—

  > - (ea) “**NHS England**” means the body corporate established under section 1H of the 2006 Act;

  .
