## 20. Amendment of the National Health Service (General Ophthalmic Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2006[^key-281a143b80142d33c5b4ecd76ac31a2e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The National Health Service (General Ophthalmic Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2006[^f00017] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1), in paragraph (a) of the definition of “equivalent body”, for “the National Health Service Commissioning Board” substitute “NHS England”.
