# The Government of Wales Act 2006 (Schedule 9A – Devolved Welsh Authorities) (Amendment) Order 2023

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of October 2023

Present,

The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 157A of the Government of Wales Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Government of Wales Act 2006 (Schedule 9A – Devolved Welsh Authorities) (Amendment) Order 2023.

(2) This Order comes into force on 7 November 2023.

(3) In this Order, “**the 2006 Act**” means the Government of Wales Act 2006.

## 2. Amendment of Schedule 9A to the 2006 Act

In Schedule 9A to the 2006 Act (devolved Welsh authorities)—

- (a) omit—
  - “The Special Educational Needs Tribunal for Wales or Tribiwnlys Anghenion Addysgol Arbennig Cymru.”;
- (b) after the entry for a county council, county borough council or community council in Wales insert—

  > - The Education Tribunal for Wales or Tribiwnlys Addysg Cymru.

  [^f00002].

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Schedule 9A (Devolved Welsh Authorities) to the Government of Wales Act [2006 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/32) (“**the 2006 Act**”) and comes into force on 7 November 2023.

Article 2 amends Schedule 9A to the 2006 Act in consequence of the Additional Learning Needs and Education Tribunal (Wales) Act [2018 (anaw 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2018/2) (“**the Act**”). The Act reforms the law on education and training for children and young people with additional learning needs. Section 91 of the Act continues the Special Educational Needs Tribunal for Wales and renames it the Education Tribunal for Wales.

Schedule 9A to the 2006 Act specifies the list of devolved Welsh authorities. This Order varies an entry in that list by removing “The Special Educational Needs Tribunal for Wales or Tribiwnlys Anghenion Addysgol Arbennig Cymru” and replacing it with “The Education Tribunal for Wales or Tribiwnlys Addysg Cymru” in consequence of the change of name made by section 91 of the Act.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
