# The Iran (Sanctions) Regulations 2023, section 22

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Immigration[^key-5f4328ab25663ef82af48d3b3b7231b1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of this regulation is an excluded person for the purposes of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00007].
