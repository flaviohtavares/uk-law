> Text as at 2026-09-29.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Export of restricted goods[^key-bbfbebd3bc643bf8ac9e42d913d8d9ef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of restricted goods to, or for use in, Iran is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 8 (Exceptions and licences).
