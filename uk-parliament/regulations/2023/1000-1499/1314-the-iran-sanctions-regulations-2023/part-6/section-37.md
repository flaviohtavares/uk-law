# The Iran (Sanctions) Regulations 2023, section 37

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Export of goods of strategic concern[^c-3b0ee76dfee87fe88340793923cce191][^c-c65c83512f0ca96d94d19583d6adcef6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of goods of strategic concern to, or for use in, Iran is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 8 (Exceptions and licences).
