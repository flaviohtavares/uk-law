# The Iran (Sanctions) Regulations 2023, section 24

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Definition of “internal repression goods” and “internal repression technology”[^key-aa049da76fc5030905c71151487c0514]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of regulation 23—

- “**internal repression goods**” means—
  - (a) any thing specified in Schedule 2, other than—
    - (i) any thing which is internal repression technology, or
    - (ii) any thing for the time being specified in—
      - (aa) Schedule 2 to the Export Control Order 2008, or
      - (bb) Annex Ⅰ of the Dual-Use Regulation, and
  - (b) any tangible storage medium on which internal repression technology is recorded or from which it can be derived;
- “**internal repression technology**” means any thing which is described in Schedule 2 as software or technology, within the meaning of that Schedule.
