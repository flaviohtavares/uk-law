> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Definition of “restricted goods” and “restricted technology”[^key-b26c85e5fe7e82619cfd43c768210a61]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**restricted goods**” means—
  - (a) internal repression goods, and
  - (b) interception and monitoring goods;
- “**restricted technology**” means—
  - (a) internal repression technology, and
  - (b) interception and monitoring technology.
