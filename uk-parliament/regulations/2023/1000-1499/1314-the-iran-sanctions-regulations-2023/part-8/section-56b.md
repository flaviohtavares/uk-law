## 56B. Trade: exceptions from prohibitions on services provided or procured relating to specified ships[^c-4776a3dc8618cd2e805efdf44d3f4c10]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibitions specified in paragraph (2) are not contravened by conduct that is necessary to protect or avoid endangering—

- (a) the safety of any ship, or
- (b) the life of any person.

(2) Paragraph (1) applies to the prohibitions—

- (a) in regulation 43C (provision of services relating to specified ships);
- (b) in regulation 43D (procurement of services relating to specified ships).
