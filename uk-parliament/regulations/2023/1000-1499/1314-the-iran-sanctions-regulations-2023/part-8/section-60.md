> Text as at 2026-09-29.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Treasury licences[^key-eddc38fb12de7eeefa6c9e75e0c0a359]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibitions in regulations 15 to 19 (asset-freeze etc.) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Treasury under this paragraph.

(2) The Treasury may issue a licence which authorises acts by a particular person only where the Treasury consider that it is appropriate to issue the licence for a purpose set out in Schedule 5.
