# The Iran (Sanctions) Regulations 2023, section 63

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Licences: general provisions[^c-78d84d98be32e8a7349dfb08703e1307]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences, director disqualification licences , trade licences and ships licences[^c-70c0bd8f5ae57c58f753421212639a37].

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may—

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^c-de365dc9ccdb81b505ca011e9753c8e3]**P**”), issues[^c-c7c760e1a2621cf797408f7f86aa2703] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^c-395d7da82cad044030ac6248b293dc11] must give written notice to P[^c-21dfd17ca67ad3cf4acc4c555295a36d] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
