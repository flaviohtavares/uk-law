## 62. Trade licences[^c-c9cad536649d21ce9ac6b5f6d889f9d7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The prohibitions in Chapters 2, 3 and 4 of Part 6 (Trade) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
