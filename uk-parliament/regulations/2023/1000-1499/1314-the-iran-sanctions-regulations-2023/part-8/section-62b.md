> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62B. Ships licences[^c-12945535dae14d22e65673d8862cf371]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The prohibitions in regulation 45A(1) and (2) (prohibition on chartering or operating specified ships) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
