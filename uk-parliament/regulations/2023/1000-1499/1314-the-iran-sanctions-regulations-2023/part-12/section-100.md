# The Iran (Sanctions) Regulations 2023, section 100

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100. Transitional provisions: prior obligations[^c-ee1a7e50dc120817632203dad895b92d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to a person (“**P**”) who, immediately before the relevant date, was designated by the Secretary of State under regulation 5 (power to designate persons) of the 2019 Regulations for the purposes of—

- (a) regulations 11 to 15 (asset-freeze etc.) of those Regulations, or
- (b) regulation 17 (immigration) of those Regulations.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), any reference in a provision mentioned in paragraph (4) to the date on which P became a designated person is a reference to the date on which P was designated by the Secretary of State under regulation 5 of the 2019 Regulations.

(3) Where P was named in Annex I of the EU Iran (Human Rights) Regulation immediately before IP completion day[^f00054], any reference in a provision mentioned in paragraph (4) to the date on which P became a designated person is a reference to the date on which P was named in that Annex.

(4) The provisions referred to in paragraphs (2) and (3) are—

- (a) regulation 55(5) (finance: exceptions from prohibitions), and
- (b) paragraph[^c-0d00326fcea1cd8091a02a8e4b9688a0] 10(a) of Schedule 5 (Treasury licences: purposes).

(5) In this regulation—

- “**the EU Iran (Human Rights) Regulation**” means Council [Regulation (EU) No359/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0359) of 12 April 2011 concerning restrictive measures directed against certain persons, entities and bodies in view of the situation in Iran[^f00055], as it has effect in EU law.
