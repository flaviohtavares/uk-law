> Text as at 2026-09-29.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94A. Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008[^c-b0c14b4d5bf5f77c2a6705c6d24957dc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008 (embargoed destinations) is not to be taken to prohibit anything prohibited by Part 6 (Trade).
