## 14. Meaning of “designated person” in Part 3[^c-af94ccbff0766969c30dbd4aeb25de25]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part a “**designated person**” means a person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of regulations 15 to 19.
