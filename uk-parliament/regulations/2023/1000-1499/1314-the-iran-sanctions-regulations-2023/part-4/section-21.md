## 21. Director disqualification sanctions[^c-a60e1b57253b7cce5c443b83c5a01d48]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is designated under regulation 5 (power to designate persons) for the purposes of this regulation is subject to director disqualification sanctions for the purposes of—

- (a) section 11A of the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986[^f00005], and
- (b) Article 15A of the Company Directors Disqualification (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^f00006].
