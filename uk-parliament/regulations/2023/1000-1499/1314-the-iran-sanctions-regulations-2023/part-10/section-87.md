# The Iran (Sanctions) Regulations 2023, section 87

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Monetary penalties[^c-2cb7a52afb2992785f1cf824c78c102a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each provision in Part 6 (Trade) which contains a prohibition imposed for a purpose mentioned in section 3(1) or (2) of the Act is to be regarded as not being financial sanctions legislation for the purposes of Part 8 of the Policing and Crime Act 2017[^f00042].
