# The Iran (Sanctions) Regulations 2023, section 86

> Text as at 2026-09-29.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Application of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^c-1d28f703e1af1916da8ec3b3416df0c9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (investigatory powers)[^f00041] applies to any offence under Part 3 (Finance) or regulation 64 (finance: licensing offences).
