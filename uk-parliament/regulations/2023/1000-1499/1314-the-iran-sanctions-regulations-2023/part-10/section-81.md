# The Iran (Sanctions) Regulations 2023, section 81

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Jurisdiction to try offences[^key-27b7060ad732db64587e41596811ab4d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where an offence under regulation 11(6) or 13(6) (confidentiality), Part 3 (Finance), regulation 64 (finance: licensing offences) , regulation 66A (ships: licensing offences)[^key-09c718b542e1d008b70b9aaeafdf7613] or regulation 68(6) or 72 (information offences in connection with Part 3) is committed in the United Kingdom—

- (a) proceedings for the offence may be taken at any place in the United Kingdom, and
- (b) the offence may for all incidental purposes be treated as having been committed at any such place.

(2) Where an offence under these Regulations is committed outside the United Kingdom—

- (a) proceedings for the offence may be taken at any place in the United Kingdom, and
- (b) the offence may for all incidental purposes be treated as having been committed at any such place.

(3) In the application of paragraph (2) to Scotland, any such proceedings against a person may be taken—

- (a) in any sheriff court district in which the person is apprehended or is in custody, or
- (b) in such sheriff court district as the Lord Advocate may determine.

(4) In paragraph (3) “**sheriff court district**” is to be read in accordance with the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995 (see section 307(1) of that Act)[^f00030].
