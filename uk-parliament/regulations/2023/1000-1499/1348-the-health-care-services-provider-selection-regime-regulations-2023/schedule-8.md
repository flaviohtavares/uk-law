# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 8

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 8 — Content of notice inviting offers

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 11(4)

**1.**[^c-aaa38c631498b0ef64dbb264529359d2] The contract or framework agreement title and reference.

**2.**[^c-c692452edc8aa3a796e3ce7d2b093fc7] A description of the relevant health care services to which the contract or framework agreement will relate, including the most relevant CPV code.

**3.**[^c-1957c386c9c01a851d8dbb0d99ddfa46] The intended or estimated dates—

- (a) between which the services must be provided and the duration of the contract including potential extensions beyond the initial term; or
- (b) of the term of the framework agreement.

**4.**[^c-a84607ec28ac05f5acaae0434f6c6ff9] The approximate lifetime value of the contract or framework agreement.

**5.**[^c-b4a86a984e94b937e561e8effd1abd59] The contract or framework award criteria.

**6.**[^c-e486d393e02aa5f27ff83b282ec8560b] Where the notice relates to a proposed framework agreement, the relevant authorities which will be able to use the framework agreement.

**7.**[^c-d23c2775cc71712f3a5faac74bd52a3b] A statement as to how offers must be made, which must be by electronic means.

**8.**[^c-df27059e6c0a9f3dca11051bb257a7a8] A statement as to how offers will be assessed, including whether the assessment will be in stages.
