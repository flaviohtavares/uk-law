# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 3 — Content of notice of intention to award to the existing provider under Direct Award Process C

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 9(4)

**1.**[^key-e0780a899d6ab29a882c5a751ab0686d] A statement that the relevant authority is intending to award a contract to an existing provider following Direct Award Process C.

**2.**[^key-cf08d19cff3f2f794525195abaa8df82] The contract title and reference.

**3.**[^key-a18058df41a3d9a0ef19d6e411d29559] The name and address of the registered office or principal place of business of the provider to whom an award is to be made.

**4.**[^key-a414500bf65137f939f671ae531a56a8] A description of the relevant health care services to which the contract relates, including the most relevant CPV code.

**5.**[^key-ae5e94cc6e84cd576330f883cddbc67d] The approximate lifetime value of the contract.

**6.**[^key-4ff9642abf1fae860ace2319a142ec2f] Details of the award decision-makers.

**7.**[^key-07160f50baa11a975da054cdb34f3762] A statement explaining the award decision-makers’ reasons for selecting the chosen provider, with reference to the key criteria.

**8.**[^key-31a453b6666805c07cce13b998289ac7] Any declared conflicts or potential conflicts of interest.

**9.**[^key-8a1805a8d759f592218ce01c5940d61f] Information as to how any conflicts or potential conflicts of interest were managed.
