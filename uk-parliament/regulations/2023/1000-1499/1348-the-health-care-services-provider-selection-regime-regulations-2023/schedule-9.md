# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 9

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 9 — Content of communication to unsuccessful provider

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 11(8)(b)

**1.**[^key-366c1284ed5f11a7dada9caa1bc46097] The contract or framework agreement title and reference.

**2.**[^key-bc1c933773740225178e91dae1ce890a] The contract or framework award criteria.

**3.**[^key-4fa5df4be6c1ba400781d2cb33fd1973] The reasons why the successful provider was successful.

**4.**[^key-c5675f52b8a2d9a23e5a5b0fcc6f7b89] The reasons why the unsuccessful provider was unsuccessful.

**5.**[^key-18db33346e992a1a1170c3c6599a0788] The dates of the beginning and end of the period in which written representations may be made.
