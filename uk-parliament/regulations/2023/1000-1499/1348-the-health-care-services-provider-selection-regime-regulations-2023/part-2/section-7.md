# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, section 7

> Text as at 2026-03-24.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Direct Award Process A[^c-d0c9f01e5934c08ed4528fda0d0e8ea1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the relevant authority follows Direct Award Process A, the process is that the relevant authority—

- (a) awards any contract without a competition, and
- (b) submits for publication on the central digital platform[^c-c53e3e18eb222f2d517263a9df82b741] a notice of the award.

(2) The notice referred to in paragraph (1)(b) must—

- (a) include the information set out in Schedule 2, and
- (b) be submitted for publication within 30 days of the contract being awarded.
