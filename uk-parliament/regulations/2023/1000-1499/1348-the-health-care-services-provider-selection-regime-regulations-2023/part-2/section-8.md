# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, section 8

> Text as at 2026-03-24.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8. Direct Award Process B[^key-38feb9482762e6221ccaf2c625b94c87]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the relevant authority follows Direct Award Process B, the process is that the relevant authority—

- (a) awards any contract without a competition, and
- (b) submits for publication on the central digital platform[^key-96a6a41d7ba01f37e70bcf55536ab975] a notice of the award.

(2) The notice referred to in paragraph (1)(b) must—

- (a) include the information set out in Schedule 2, and
- (b) be submitted for publication within 30 days of the contract being awarded.
