# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 4 — Content of notice following award under Direct Award Process C

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 9(11)(a)

**1.**[^key-28be4d85b735811654d11ce541f86b7d] A statement that an award has been made following Direct Award Process C.

**2.**[^key-12e097e002f2ac64dc1caf96bc6bb7f3] The contract title and reference.

**3.**[^key-c61ca4bb8a5fddb78858f1c3d52d0ac3] The name and address of the registered office or principal place of business of the provider to whom the contract has been awarded.

**4.**[^key-b8a9c712b7227d505dc6b2d1e83ce7f7] A description of the relevant health care services to which the contract relates, including the most relevant CPV code.

**5.**[^key-1b752b125c6d5b8cd161a801a633c6f6] The lifetime value of the contract or, where it is not known, the amounts payable to the provider under the contract.

**6.**[^key-c92525da9b4e6e513df0c530042c197c] The dates between which the contract provides for the services to be provided.

**7.**[^key-d9e8f600f6eefd431383608f729ca293] Details of the award decision-makers.

**8.**[^key-f9e07f07b74ddf807fe687faf4fac540] Any declared conflicts or potential conflicts of interest.

**9.**[^key-2dfac16fd75afcbe0c9c102dff8d1a97] Information as to how any conflicts or potential conflicts of interest were managed.
