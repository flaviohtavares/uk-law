# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 10

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 10 — Content of notice of intention to make an award to the chosen provider, or conclude a framework agreement with the chosen provider, under the Competitive Process

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 11(10)

**1.**[^key-4714b0a6c555c0d9b0977af87d23664b] A statement that the relevant authority is intending to award a contract to a provider or conclude a framework agreement under the Competitive Process.

**2.**[^key-de74ec85ea3affa6843761001ebcc3b4] The contract or framework agreement title and reference.

**3.**[^key-45bf79bbb6340b94546bca6f22ca16bd] The name and address of the registered office or principal place of business of the provider to whom a contract is to be awarded or with whom a framework agreement is to be concluded.

**4.**[^key-67bd9740c1c58ce67b5de2366a7a5dc6] A description of the relevant health care services to which the contract or framework agreement relates, including the most relevant CPV code.

**5.**[^key-eae92e53357b0885ab318a9fc58a89d8] Where the notice relates to the conclusion of a framework agreement, the duration of the agreement and the relevant authorities which will be able to use the framework agreement.

**6.**[^key-e76ddedd4698d583f7738d88422667de] The approximate lifetime value of the contract or framework agreement.

**7.**[^key-f6f659747e2c1768a1db79eb42760fd7] Details of the decision-makers.

**8.**[^key-a9524b6c26f7694bd8ca6281075454ed] A statement explaining the decision-makers’ reasons for selecting the chosen provider, with reference to the key criteria.

**9.**[^key-da99f37f2c9f302ab17ee65e76296147] Any declared conflicts or potential conflicts of interest.

**10.**[^key-b4938d697669f49bfb40b78d57b0af5d] Information as to how any conflicts or potential conflicts of interest were managed.

**11.**[^key-8e5e5566980373a0bc8e0c785e51e0e7] Where appropriate, an indication whether a framework agreement was used.
