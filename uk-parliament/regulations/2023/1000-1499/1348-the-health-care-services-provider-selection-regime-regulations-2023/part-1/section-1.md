# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, section 1

> Text as at 2026-03-24.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and territorial application[^c-733a693182086e8ad356519616bfc6bd]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 1st January 2024.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and apply in relation to England only.
