# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 7

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 7 — Content of notice following award under the Most Suitable Provider Process

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 10(15)(a)

**1.**[^c-d3958246e8986a4e1c65bb4d0b2546c2] A statement that an award has been made following the Most Suitable Provider Process.

**2.**[^c-8c40d570047fb101f4eece92eaeed58d] The contract title and reference.

**3.**[^c-c82277e26ebe4c0aead4a904f9b1ce20] The name and address of the registered office or principal place of business of the provider to whom the contract has been awarded.

**4.**[^c-b127162e8027453caaecc95a0c1b8daf] A description of the relevant health care services to which the contract relates, including the most relevant CPV code.

**5.**[^c-097d261b3b175b52ff667fb36ffffac4] The lifetime value of the contract or, where it is not known, the amounts payable to the provider under the contract.

**6.**[^c-b621373cf30785449f7b21b15813ff85] The dates between which the contract provides for the services to be provided.

**7.**[^c-3a7b2fda8b2315f358694b859987e211] Details of the award decision-makers.

**8.**[^c-b23cbf8530729dfe5747f7d33c95dc38] Any declared conflicts or potential conflicts of interest.

**9.**[^c-75fe06b36521d72a0451500d1433a776] Information as to how any conflicts or potential conflicts of interest were managed.
