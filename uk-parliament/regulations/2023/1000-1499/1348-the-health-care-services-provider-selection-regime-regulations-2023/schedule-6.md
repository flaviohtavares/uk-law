# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 6

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 6 — Content of notice of intention to award to the chosen provider under the Most Suitable Provider Process

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 10(8)

**1.**[^key-cec89d51c33441e9bb8c2f0846de59ae] A statement that the relevant authority is intending to award a contract to a provider following the Most Suitable Provider Process.

**2.**[^key-6828e2dd6a0c8c77846cb1363d8bab7b] The contract title and reference.

**3.**[^key-fcea8019f5b5f5f9d49c9474525570c2] The name and address of the registered office or principal place of business of the provider to whom an award is to be made.

**4.**[^key-6d82835d0f4f6d6709f3deed7a4b64bc] A description of the relevant health care services to which the contract relates, including the most relevant CPV code.

**5.**[^key-e6e6cd61187a4eb11d0a68fb7ca56eed] The approximate lifetime value of the contract.

**6.**[^key-c93ac672abee42e8a4cd226ac3167915] Details of the award decision-makers.

**7.**[^key-ec51dfeb1582e5d672239d567c092562] A statement explaining the award decision-makers’ reasons for selecting the chosen provider, with reference to the key criteria.

**8.**[^key-dd2b0823ccad142bd8fdc0e11f79ce62] Any declared conflicts or potential conflicts of interest.

**9.**[^key-d343ff6be7144fccd765c521f02d1f82] Information as to how any conflicts or potential conflicts of interest were managed.
