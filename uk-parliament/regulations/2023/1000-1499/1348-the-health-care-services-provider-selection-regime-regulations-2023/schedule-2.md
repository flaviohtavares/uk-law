# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Content of notice of award which followed Direct Award Process A or Direct Award Process B or award based on a framework agreement without a competition

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 7(2)(a), 8(2)(a) and 18(6)(a)

**1.**[^c-3a8de986c41648911e3e58900ea89ca2] A statement that an award has been made, following Direct Award Process A or Direct Award Process B, as the case may be, or a statement that the award is an award based on a framework agreement and made without a competition.

**2.**[^c-4670773599b8b8c15bf15fa1652e838a] The contract title and reference.

**3.**[^c-9a1be345ce50b8eeb61fc5f94061fa1c] The name and address of the registered office or principal place of business of the provider to whom the contract has been awarded.

**4.**[^c-5f895f0a26d796de538c57729da9c91b] A description of the relevant health care services to which the contract relates, including the most relevant CPV code.

**5.**[^c-8448c48c0847795dacb0fffe5ca1f6d9] The lifetime value of the contract or, where it is not known, the amounts payable to the provider under the contract.

**6.**[^c-39ea7185ab66ba4804f08d04ef4693b2] The dates between which the contract provides for the services to be provided.

**7.**[^c-881dfbe81e9d6bd802f66aeb7311f233] Details of the award decision-makers.

**8.**[^c-278e5c39943364f16d9fce30ae50947a] Any declared conflicts or potential conflicts of interest.

**9.**[^c-924ee07edece00c13ddd587084c9b2de] Information as to how any conflicts or potential conflicts of interest were managed.
