# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 12

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 12 — Notice of contract or framework agreement modification

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 13(5)(b)

**1.**[^c-9a8d1f75d281299a79c758f5a5b986e3] The contract or framework agreement title and reference.

**2.**[^c-c59cea93395f5dca08b6993cec70f7c3] A description of the relevant health care services to which the contract or framework agreement relates, including the most relevant CPV code.

**3.**[^c-7279afa004cc13f73c341d722d63ca51] The effective date of the modification.

**4.**[^c-84201365b164acd7d10dd1ce5333a489] A brief description of the modification.

**5.**[^c-cc5504821802df0b8a37044cfa668f90] Any change in the lifetime value of the contract or framework agreement.

**6.**[^c-8e3522809226e4e68e7db58a7be02b68] Any change in the term length of the contract or framework agreement.

**7.**[^c-14cea12270811c8bcfa7a40b57d4d9ed] Details of the decision-makers.

**8.**[^c-61e51938a548af23d9af36b531a57dda] Any declared conflicts or potential conflicts of interest.

**9.**[^c-ebf58bcd2de02df5e2b6e7590a1226af] Information as to how any conflicts or potential conflicts of interest were managed.
