# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 15

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 15 — Invitation to providers party to the framework agreement to submit an offer

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 18(8)

**1.**[^key-bfa0b2c59dd4b1d2e1173272efc71f31] A description of the relevant health care services to which the contract relates, including the most relevant CPV code.

**2.**[^key-153a94a4534d586c099688aeead1ee66] The contract award criteria.

**3.**[^key-956c989bb57b46ffdfc5dea7debe7fca] The intended or estimated dates between which the services must be provided and the duration of the contract including potential extensions beyond the initial term.

**4.**[^key-f3088b78b219efaf0c6c514c36810992] The approximate lifetime value of the contract.
