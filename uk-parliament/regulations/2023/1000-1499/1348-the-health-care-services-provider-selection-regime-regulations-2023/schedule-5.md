# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 5

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 5 — Content of notice of intention to follow the Most Suitable Provider Process

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 10(3)

**1.**[^key-ea2c473ba06889c91852700e6e10e6ef] A statement that the relevant authority is intending to follow the Most Suitable Provider Process to award a contract.

**2.**[^key-a413f82947cb79a23571722f62667d3d] The contract title and reference.

**3.**[^key-9e9c18a780cc2297cbb9883ddebaabab] A description of the relevant health care services to which the contract relates, including the most relevant CPV code.

**4.**[^key-98b28334c40db78d78e797636044bbb4] Details of the award decision-makers.
