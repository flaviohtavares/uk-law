# The Health Care Services (Provider Selection Regime) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 13

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 13 — Notice of urgent award

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 14(5)(b)

**1.**[^c-cfdc0a1342687b3f83388094b2a0ba52] A statement that in the view of the relevant authority the award was urgent.

**2.**[^c-43b26b1b57034d1e2bb530ddfbc40520] The contract title and reference.

**3.**[^c-cecd71d044a7c8d9ebf3159c5ecdf656] The name and address of the registered office or principal place of business of the provider to whom the contract has been awarded.

**4.**[^c-a2143998699750d695df4cc59cc5b8cc] A description of the relevant health care services to which the contract relates, including the most relevant CPV code.

**5.**[^c-c39e9056baa24504599bec1a12fe9ab0] The lifetime value of the contract or, where it is not known, the amounts payable to the provider under the contract.

**6.**[^c-40850ad2d1c24d3b85264644a61780aa] The dates between which the contract provides for the services to be provided.

**7.**[^c-929f873e5ee663d0df9beb645b88e9e9] The reasons for the dates referred to in paragraph 6, if that period is greater than 12 months.

**8.**[^c-a5b9a81253b7094ae3a8fa1128303a4b] Details of the decision-makers.

**9.**[^c-e842d785dbe85eaf197f796810659716] Any declared conflicts or potential conflicts of interest.

**10.**[^c-09bdf5c93e18f2631e729e109d62c023] Information as to how any conflicts or potential conflicts of interest were managed.

**11.**[^c-15eb14f4d4093ea31ec94befa489a24b] The reasons why regulation 14 applied.
