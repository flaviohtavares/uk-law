# The Longfield Solar Farm (Correction) Order 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 1241

Infrastructure Planning

The Longfield Solar Farm (Correction) Order 2023

Made

20th November 2023

Coming into force

21st November 2023

The Longfield Solar Farm Order 2023 (“**the Order**”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“**the Act**”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period (as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act), the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the correction of errors in the Order under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed Braintree District Council and Chelmsford City Council as the relevant local planning authorities[^f00004], that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Longfield Solar Farm (Correction) Order 2023.

(2) This Order comes into force on 21st November 2023.

## 2. Corrections

The Longfield Solar Farm Order 2023 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero

John Wheadon

Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning Delivery

Department for Energy Security and Net Zero

20th November 2023

(This note is not part of the Order)
