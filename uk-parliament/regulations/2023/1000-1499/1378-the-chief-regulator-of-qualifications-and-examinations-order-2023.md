# The Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations Order 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 1378

Education, England

The Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations Order 2023

Made

13th December 2023

Coming into force

14th December 2023

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 13th day of December 2023

Present,

The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty by and with the advice of His Privy Council, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 2(1)(za) of Schedule 9 to the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations Order 2023 and comes into force on 14th December 2023.

## 2. Extent and Application

(1) This Order extends to England and Wales.

(2) This Order applies in relation to England only.

## 3. Appointment of chief executive of the Office of Qualifications and Examinations

Sir Ian Bauckham is appointed as the chief executive of the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation and is to be known as the Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations, from 1st January 2024.

Ceri King

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints Sir Ian Bauckham to the role of chief executive of the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation, which is established by section 127 of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009. In accordance with paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 9 to that Act, the person appointed to that position is to be known as the Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations.
