> Text as at 2026-01-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Updating information provided with an application under Part 3 of Schedule 4 (low-volume participants)[^c-e35ce3977c12ca3ee4e0f54908926953]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A low-volume CRTS participant and a low-volume VRTS participant must notify the administrator without delay if there is a change in the information provided with the application made under Part 3 of Schedule 4 which affects the participant’s eligibility to be a low-volume CRTS participant or a low-volume VRTS participant, as the case may be.
