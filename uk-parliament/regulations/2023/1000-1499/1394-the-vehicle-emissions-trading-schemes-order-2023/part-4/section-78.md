# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 78

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Error notification[^c-4f5de238bfdd281773c10266b6a446c2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On or before 31st August of each year following a scheme year, a participant in the Trading Schemes or an SPV manufacturer may notify the administrator of any errors identified in the information notified by the administrator under article 77(2) or (4).

(2) A notification given under paragraph (1)—

- (a) must identify the error; and
- (b) may be accompanied by evidence which demonstrates that the administrator has made an error.

(3) The administrator must take into account a notification given under this article in the preparation of the final information under article 79.
