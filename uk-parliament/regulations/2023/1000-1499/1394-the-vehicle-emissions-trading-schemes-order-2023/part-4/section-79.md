# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 79

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Preparation and notification of final information[^key-63a94fd2b6e9e5e85d26f511217b2309]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On or before 31st October of each year following a scheme year, the administrator must prepare the final information mentioned in article 77(1) and (3) and notify it in writing to each participant in the Trading Schemes and each SPV manufacturer.
