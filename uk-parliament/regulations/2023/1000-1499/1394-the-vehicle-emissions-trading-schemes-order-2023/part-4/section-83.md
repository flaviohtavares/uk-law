# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 83

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Updating information provided with an application under Schedule 5 (pool participants)[^c-eb08eb8700ae10a28291ffa8f85e3077]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Each manufacturer which is a member of a pool participant in the Trading Schemes must notify the administrator without delay if there is a change to the identity of the manufacturer notified to the administrator as the contact point in accordance with paragraph 5(c)(i) of Schedule 5.

(2) A notification under paragraph (1) must be accompanied by the evidence mentioned in paragraph 7(b) of Schedule 5 in relation to the new manufacturer which will act as the contact point.
