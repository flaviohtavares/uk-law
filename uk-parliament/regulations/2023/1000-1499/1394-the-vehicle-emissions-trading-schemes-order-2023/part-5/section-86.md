# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 86

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Power to require production of documents or provision of information[^c-264af6b1293339a719edd0b2c7123296]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The administrator or an authorised person may, by a written notice given to P, require P—

- (a) to provide the administrator or the authorised person with information or documents; and
- (b) to provide such information or documents in the form specified in the notice and within such period, or at such time, as is specified in the notice.
