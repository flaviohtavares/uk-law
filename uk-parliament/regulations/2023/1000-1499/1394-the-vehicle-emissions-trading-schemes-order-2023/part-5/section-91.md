> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Legal professional privilege[^c-070748054954096037bb6b6978f69ec4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Part requires any person to produce a document which that person would be entitled to withhold the production of on the grounds of legal professional privilege.
