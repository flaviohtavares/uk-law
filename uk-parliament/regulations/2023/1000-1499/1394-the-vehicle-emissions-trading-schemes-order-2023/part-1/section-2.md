# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 2

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Extent[^c-e950988ee69e8ba3483bb1b7d3843df6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order extends to England and Wales and Scotland, subject to paragraph (2) and paragraph (3)[^c-d04269dbdc07aa8cf1e5a65409c10aa3].

(2) This Part and Part 8 extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(3)[^c-715d09583133a179dbb0658f2b725327] On and after 1st January 2025, Parts 2 to 7 and 9 extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
