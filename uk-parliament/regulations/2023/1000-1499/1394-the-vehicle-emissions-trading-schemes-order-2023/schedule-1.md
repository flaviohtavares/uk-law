# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, Schedule 1

## Schedule 1 — Baseline for the CCTS and VCTS

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 3(1)

### Part 1 — Baseline for the CCTS

#### 1. Interpretation and general[^c-fe60884796579c5e83a5478504a162c0]

In this Part—

- (a) the period beginning with 1st January 2021 and ending with 31st December 2021 is referred to as “**2021**”;
- (b) the period beginning with 1st January 2022 and ending with 31st December 2022 is referred to as “**2022**”;
- (c) the period beginning with 1st January 2023 and ending with 31st December 2023 is referred to as “**2023**”;
- (d) “adjusted specific emissions of CO<sub>2</sub>”, in relation to a car, means the specific emissions of CO<sub>2</sub> of the car, reduced where applicable by the number of grams of CO<sub>2</sub> per kilometre specified in the certificate of conformity of that car as being due to any eco-innovation;
- (e) “**exempt manufacturer**” means a manufacturer to which Article 4, points (b) and (c) of Article 7(4), Article 8 and points (a) and (c) of Article 9(1) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/631](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0631) did not apply by virtue of Article 2(4) of that Regulation;
- (f) “**excess emissions premium**” means a requirement to pay a specified amount where a manufacturer’s average specific emissions exceed its specific emissions target under Article 8 of [Regulation (EU) 2019/631](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0631);
- (g) “**non-ZE car**” means a car which has specific emissions of CO<sub>2</sub> greater than zero grams per kilometre;
- (h) “**pool**” means a group of manufacturers which formed a pool for the purposes of meeting their obligations under Article 4 of [Regulation (EU) 2019/631](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0631);
- (i) “**pool manager**” means a manufacturer nominated as the manager of a pool in accordance with Article 6(2)(b) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/631](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0631);
- (j) “**specific emissions target**” has the same meaning as it had in [Regulation (EU) 2019/631](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0631), as it stood immediately before the coming into force of this Order;
- (k) “**2021 individual NZE average emissions**”, in relation to a manufacturer, means the average of the adjusted specific emissions of CO<sub>2</sub> of the non-ZE cars of which that manufacturer is the manufacturer and which were registered during 2021;
- (l) “**2021 individual specific emissions target**” means the specific emissions target for a manufacturer for 2021;
- (m) “**2021 pool specific emissions target**” means the specific emissions target to which the manufacturers in a pool were collectively subject, for the purposes of meeting their obligations under Article 4 of [Regulation (EU) 2019/631](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0631) for 2021, in accordance with Article 6 of that Regulation;
- (n) “**2021 individual performance**”, in relation to a manufacturer, means the average of the specific emissions of CO<sub>2</sub>, as specified in the information published by the Secretary of State pursuant to Article 9(1)(b) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/631](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0631)[^f00028], of the non-ZE cars of which the manufacturer was the manufacturer and which were registered during 2021;
- (o) “**2021 pool performance**”, in relation to a pool, means the average of the specific emissions of CO<sub>2</sub>, as specified in the information published by the Secretary of State pursuant to Article 9(1)(b) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/631](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0631), of the non-ZE cars of which a member of the pool was the manufacturer and which were registered during 2021;
- (p) “**2021 individual excess**”, in relation to a manufacturer, is calculated by applying the following formula—

  [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/1394)

  where—

  - “A” is the manufacturer’s 2021 individual performance; and
  - “B” is the manufacturer’s 2021 individual specific emissions target;
- (q) “**2021 pool excess**”, in relation to a pool, is calculated by applying the following formula—

  [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/1394)

  where—

  - “C” is the pool’s 2021 pool performance; and
  - “D” is the pool’s 2021 pool specific emissions target

**2.**[^c-7ac2ff6a93691cc1e99ec6c893948625] Paragraphs 5 to 19 apply for the purposes of calculating the baseline for a CCTS participant consisting of a manufacturer which is not a member of a pool CCTS participant.

**3.**[^c-9d771595af166c3911c372f32ec9d717] Paragraph 20 applies for the purposes of calculating the baseline for a CCTS participant which is a pool CCTS participant.

**4.**[^c-55ba636d68d3a97ff06943409e368772] A baseline which is calculated in accordance with this Part, if not a whole number, is calculated to three decimal places only.

#### 5. Existing manufacturer which met 2021 target[^c-ed90f699ec8626866e9ac9bdbfc65e90]

Paragraph 6 applies for the purpose of calculating the baseline for a CCTS participant—

- (a) which had a 2021 individual specific emissions target; and
- (b) in relation to which no excess emissions premium was imposed for 2021 on—
  - (i) the manufacturer; or
  - (ii) the pool manager for a pool of which the manufacturer was a member for 2021.

**6.**[^c-cca0be374279586c7d59bd348d0995c5] Where this paragraph applies, the CCTS participant’s baseline is the higher of—

- (a) the manufacturer’s 2021 individual NZE average emissions; or
- (b) the manufacturer’s 2021 individual specific emissions target.

#### 7. Exempt manufacturer for 2021[^c-31f7360a4f7af6ff9bad2f8c4b52b88f]

The baseline for a CCTS participant which was an exempt manufacturer for 2021 is the manufacturer’s 2021 individual NZE average emissions.

#### 8. Existing manufacturer which did not meet 2021 target[^c-05a60b8b4133567b30f687dfcc82a697]

Paragraph 9 applies for the purpose of calculating the baseline for a CCTS participant—

- (a) which had a 2021 individual specific emissions target;
- (b) which was not a member of a pool for 2021; and
- (c) on which an excess emissions premium was imposed for 2021.

**9.**[^c-54b0524772d2ee050cfbadb29d844546] Where this paragraph applies, the CCTS participant’s baseline is the number resulting from the application of the following formula—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/1394)

where—

- “E” is the manufacturer’s 2021 individual NZE average emissions; and
- “F” is the manufacturer’s 2021 individual excess.

#### 10. Existing manufacturer in non-compliant pool met individual 2021 target[^c-bb5616316d78a9751eb19a17e533ab0b]

Paragraph 11 applies for the purpose of calculating the baseline for a CCTS participant which—

- (a) had a 2021 individual specific emissions target;
- (b) was a member of a pool for 2021 and—
  - (i) an excess emissions premium was imposed on the pool manager for that pool for 2021; but
  - (ii) the manufacturer met its individual 2021 specific emissions target.

**11.**[^c-3c44123e7b90cd8b0f9484a7f1be8c43] Where this paragraph applies, the CCTS participant’s baseline is the higher of—

- (a) the manufacturer’s 2021 individual NZE average emissions; or
- (b) the manufacturer’s 2021 individual specific emissions target.

#### 12. Existing manufacturer in non-compliant pool did not meet individual 2021 target[^c-2a4ee62a67e1d3a2f48da2d865ab82b1]

Paragraph 13 applies for the purpose of calculating the baseline for a CCTS participant where—

- (a) the manufacturer had a 2021 individual specific emissions target;
- (b) the manufacturer was a member of a pool for 2021;
- (c) an excess emissions premium was imposed on the pool manager for that pool for 2021; and
- (d) the manufacturer did not meet its 2021 individual specific emissions target.

**13.**[^c-171a0b4a861248fbbdaa2cc71c467999] Where this paragraph applies, the CCTS participant’s baseline is the number resulting from the application of the following formula—

#### 14. New manufacturer in 2022[^c-c06ebe3abf414cd4f0f70a41ba1cd067]

Paragraph 15 applies for the purpose of calculating the baseline for a CCTS participant which—

- (a) did not have a 2021 individual specific emissions target;
- (b) was not an exempt manufacturer for 2021; and
- (c) is the manufacturer of a non-ZE car which was registered during 2022.

**15.**[^c-b07e56bde8a9969772ba3ba90910a9f3] Where this paragraph applies, the CCTS participant’s baseline is the average of the adjusted specific emissions of CO<sub>2</sub> of the non-ZE cars of which that CCTS participant is the manufacturer and which were registered during 2022.

#### 16. New manufacturer in 2023[^c-198f156a6dcae8a8bece2843d844cae0]

Paragraph 17 applies for the purpose of calculating the baseline for a CCTS participant which—

- (a) did not have a 2021 individual specific emissions target;
- (b) was not an exempt manufacturer for 2021;
- (c) is not the manufacturer of a non-ZE car which was registered during 2022; and
- (d) is the manufacturer of a non-ZE car which was registered during 2023.

**17.**[^c-ce94b2a3e8efc6592e2732e3ee4cbbcc] Where this paragraph applies, the CCTS participant’s baseline is the average of the adjusted specific emissions of CO<sub>2</sub> of the non-ZE cars of which that CCTS participant is the manufacturer and which were registered during 2023.

#### 18. New manufacturer from 2024[^c-d8a9d3414b93c16475f2c01760171104]

Paragraph 19 applies for the purpose of calculating the baseline for a CCTS participant which—

- (a) did not have a 2021 individual specific emissions target;
- (b) was not an exempt manufacturer for 2021;
- (c) is not the manufacturer of a non-ZE car which was registered during 2022 or 2023; and
- (d) becomes a CCTS participant, or a member of a pool CCTS participant, on or after the date on which this Order comes into force.

**19.**[^c-cecf566299346faacbca03f6213a5be0] Where this paragraph applies, the CCTS participant’s baseline is the average of the adjusted specific emissions of CO<sub>2</sub> of all the non-ZE cars registered during the calendar year preceding the scheme year in which the participant first becomes a CCTS participant, or a member of a pool CCTS participant.

#### 20. Pool CCTS participant[^c-c9dc666cf599fc28769c4c5fdfcf6014]

(1) The baseline for a CCTS participant which is a pool CCTS participant is calculated as follows.

- *Step 1*

  Calculate the baseline for each manufacturer which is a member of the pool CCTS participant in accordance with whichever of paragraphs 5 to 19 is applicable to each manufacturer.
- *Step 2*

  Multiply each baseline calculated in accordance with step 1—

  - (a) by the number of non-ZE cars of which the manufacturer was the manufacturer and which were registered in 2021; or
  - (b) where the manufacturer was not the manufacturer of a non-ZE car which was registered in 2021, by the number of non-ZE cars of which the manufacturer is the manufacturer and which were registered during the calendar year in which the manufacturer first became a CCTS participant or a member of a pool CCTS participant.
- *Step 3*

  Add together the numbers resulting from step 2.
- *Step 4*

  Calculate the total number of non-ZE cars of which the manufacturers which are members of the pool CCTS participant are the manufacturer and which were registered—

  - (a) during 2021; or
  - (b) where the manufacturer was not the manufacturer of a non-ZE car which was registered in 2021, during the calendar year in which the manufacturer first became a CCTS participant or a member of a pool CCTS participant.
- *Step 5*

  Divide the number resulting from step 3 by the number resulting from step 4.

(2) The pool CCTS participant’s baseline is the number resulting from step 5.
