# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 100

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100. Failure to provide information under article 81 (information required for the purposes of administering the Trading Schemes)[^key-4887382fd50d3754ff8f3ec6e7859857]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The administrator may impose the penalties in paragraph (2) where—

- (a) the administrator has required a participant in the Trading Schemes or an SPV manufacturer to provide information in a notice in accordance with article 81; and
- (b) the participant or manufacturer has failed to comply with the notice by the time specified in it.

(2) The penalties are—

- (a) the financial penalty of £7,500; and
- (b) publication.
