> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103. Refusal to allow access to premises[^c-36cb9cc97fd59841b389a595a944d086]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The administrator may impose the penalties in paragraph (2) where a person in control of any premises refuses the administrator or an authorised person access to those premises, or intentionally obstructs the administrator or an authorised person from accessing to those premises, contrary to article 88.

(2) The penalties are—

- (a) the financial penalty of £75,000;
- (b) a block; and
- (c) publication.
