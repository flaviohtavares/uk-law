# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 18

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. CRTS credits: general[^key-b58802d7737cc351a20ff42f24c1acd6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A CRTS participant may acquire a CRTS credit in accordance with articles 19, 20 , 23 to 23B, 57A and 58A[^key-3e8e866473ed6007af510de45226620b].

(2) Where it[^key-d9ff45e478597c736456a6ffbbc39cc7] acquires a CRTS credit for a scheme year, a CRTS participant may—

- (a) surrender that CRTS credit for that scheme year in accordance with article 27; or
- (b) trade the credit in accordance with article 25 during the trading window following that scheme year , unless the credit was acquired by the CRTS participant in accordance with article 23, 23A, 23B, 57A or 58A[^key-b8d3c0243e447be4632be6a7d6ca7ba1].

(3) An SPV car manufacturer may acquire a CRTS credit in accordance with article 21 or 22.

(4) An SPV car manufacturer may trade a credit acquired for a scheme year in accordance with article 26 or 26A[^key-d07a24b3e96937aa4998fb313117e631] during the trading window following that scheme year.

(5) Where a CRTS participant or an SPV car manufacturer acquires a CRTS credit in accordance with articles 19 to 23B[^key-7b9c02be1c4caa6f18e417dddb372a24], the administrator must update the registry accordingly (see also the requirement for the administrator to update the registry in articles 57A(7) and 58A(8))[^key-23984f80ffbddf9e0344a31b4114181d].
