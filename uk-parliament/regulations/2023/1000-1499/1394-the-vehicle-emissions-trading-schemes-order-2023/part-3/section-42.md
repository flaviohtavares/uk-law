# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 42

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Activity to which the VRTS applies and unit of measurement of activity[^c-b3c5512b723351ea02f8f91a55d8d635]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The activity to which the VRTS applies is the registration of an NZE van during the trading period.

(2) The unit of measurement of that activity is the registration of one NZE van.
