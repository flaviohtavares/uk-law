# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 10

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Activity to which the CRTS applies and unit of measurement of activity[^key-e63124378069c48b61c1ead0f9895897]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The activity to which the CRTS applies is the registration of an NZE car during the trading period.

(2) The unit of measurement of that activity is the registration of one NZE car.
