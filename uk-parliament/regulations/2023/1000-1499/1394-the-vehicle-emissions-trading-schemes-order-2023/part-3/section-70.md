# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 70

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Accounting for activity in the VCTS[^key-880c540d85d55986e46de61031861659]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For each scheme year, and no later than 31st December of the year following a scheme year, a VCTS participant must account for each unit of its activity during the scheme year, measured in accordance with article 68, by surrendering a VCTS allowance.

(2) The administrator must update the registry to record the surrender of VCTS allowances.
