# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 33

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. CCTS allowances[^key-239411b8d64d65cd45026c98dc155b90]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A CCTS allowance is an allowance for an NZE car which is registered during a scheme year to emit one gram of CO<sub>2</sub> per kilometre.
