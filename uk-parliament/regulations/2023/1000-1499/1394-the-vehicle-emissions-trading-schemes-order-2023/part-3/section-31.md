# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 31

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Activity to which the CCTS applies and unit of measurement of activity[^c-47a2aae99ab19d3b85f61e662306a4ea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The activity to which the CCTS applies is the registration of an NZE car during the trading period.

(2) The unit of measurement of that activity is each gram of CO<sub>2</sub> per kilometre emitted by such a car.
