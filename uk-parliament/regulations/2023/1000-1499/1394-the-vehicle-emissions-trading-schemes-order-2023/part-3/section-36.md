# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 36

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Measuring activity in the CCTS[^key-3d2aa505381a1b2e146b4b0eaa5e01ff]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The number of units of activity that must be accounted for by a CCTS participant for a scheme year is the sum of the number of grams of CO<sub>2</sub> per kilometre emitted by each NZE car of which the participant is the manufacturer and which is registered during the scheme year.

(2)[^key-1db2c0461102c03105562f31e45fe15d] For the purposes of this article, the number of grams of CO<sub>2</sub> per kilometre emitted by an NZE car is the specific emissions of CO<sub>2</sub> of the car, with that number having been—

- (a) if applicable, reduced in accordance with paragraph (3); and
- (b) if applicable, corrected in accordance with Article 13(3) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/631](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/0631) (lack of correspondence of CO<sub>2</sub> emission and fuel consumption values).

(3) An NZE car’s specific emissions of CO<sub>2</sub> are reduced by the number of grams of CO<sub>2</sub> per kilometre specified in the car’s certificate of conformity as CO<sub>2</sub> savings achieved by any eco-innovation, unless—

- (a) the Secretary of State has given a notice to the administrator in accordance with Article 12(2)(b) of [Regulation (EU)725/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0725)[^f00015] which relates to that eco-innovation; and
- (b) the notice was given during the scheme year which immediately preceded the scheme year for which a CCTS participant’s number of units of activity in the CCTS is measured.

(4) A CCTS participant’s number of units of activity during a scheme year, if not a whole number, is calculated to three decimal places only.
