# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 65

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. VCTS allowances[^c-f423864700c7dc49beb1062fc5eb1052]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A VCTS allowance is an allowance for an NZE van which is registered during a scheme year to emit one gram of CO<sub>2</sub> per kilometre.
