# The Vehicle Emissions Trading Schemes Order 2023, section 109

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 109. Interpretation of Part 8[^c-57b480607d1f5a67aabc75d7ef3cfc22]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**[Regulation (EU) 1014/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1014)**” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1014/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1014) of 10 November 2010 on monitoring and reporting of data on the registration of new passenger cars pursuant to Regulation [(EC) No 443/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0443) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00021];
- “**[Regulation (EU) 63/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0063)**” means [Commission Regulation (EU) No 63/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0063) of 26 January 2011 laying down detailed provisions for the application for a derogation from the specific CO<sub>2</sub> emission targets pursuant to Article 11 of Regulation [(EC) No 443/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0443) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00022];
- “**[Regulation (EU) 293/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0293)**” means Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU)293/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0293) of 3 April 2012 on monitoring and reporting of data on the registration of new light commercial vehicles pursuant to [Regulation (EU) No510/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0510) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00023];
- “**[Regulation (EU) 114/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/0114)**” means Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) No114/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/0114) of 6 November 2012 supplementing [Regulation (EU) No510/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2011/0510) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to rules for the application for a derogation from the specific CO<sub>2</sub> emissions targets for new light commercial vehicles[^f00024];
- “**[Regulation (EU) 2017/1152](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/1152)**” means Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2017/1152](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/1152) of 2 June 2017 setting out a methodology for determining the correlation parameters necessary for reflecting the change in the regulatory test procedure with regard to light commercial vehicles and amending Implementing [Regulation (EU) No 293/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0293)[^f00025];
- “**[Regulation (EU) 2017/1153](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/1153)**” means Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2017/1153](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/1153) of 2 June 2017 setting out a methodology for determining the correlation parameters necessary for reflecting the change in the regulatory test procedure and amending [Regulation (EU) No 1014/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1014)[^f00026].
