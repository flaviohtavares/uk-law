# The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 1084

Children And Young Persons

The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 2023

Made

11th October 2023

Coming into force

1st November 2023

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of October 2023

Present,

The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Him by section 2(2) of the Child Abduction and Custody Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 2023 and comes into force on 1st November 2023.

## 2. Amendment of the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986

In the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986[^f00002], for Schedule 1 substitute Schedule 1 as set out in the Schedule to this Order.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986 ([S.I. 1986/1159](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1159)) (“**the 1986 Order**”).

This Order substitutes a new Schedule 1 for the existing Schedule 1 to the 1986 Order, adding Jamaica to the list of Contracting States to the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction, done at The Hague on 25th October 1980 (Cm.33). The date of the coming into force of the Convention as between the United Kingdom and Jamaica is 1st November 2023.

An Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument because there is no, or no significant, impact on business in the United Kingdom.
