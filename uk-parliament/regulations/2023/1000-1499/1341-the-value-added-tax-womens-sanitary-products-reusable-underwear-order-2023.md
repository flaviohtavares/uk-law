# The Value Added Tax (Women’s Sanitary Products: Reusable Underwear) Order 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 1341

VALUE ADDED TAX

The Value Added Tax (Women’s Sanitary Products: Reusable Underwear) Order 2023

Made

6th December 2023

Laid before the House of Commons

8th December 2023

Coming into force

1st January 2024

The Treasury make this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 30(4) and 96(9) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Women’s Sanitary Products: Reusable Underwear) Order 2023 and comes into force on 1st January 2024.

## 2. Amendment to Schedule 8 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994

In Schedule 8 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994 (zero-rating), in Group 19 (women’s sanitary products)[^f00002], in Note (1), at the end insert—

> (d) reusable underwear that is designed, and marketed, as being primarily for use for absorbing, or otherwise collecting, lochia or menstrual flow.

.

Scott Mann

Stuart Anderson

Two of the Lords Commissioners of His Majesty's Treasury

6th December 2023

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Value Added Tax Act [1994 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23) (“**VATA**”) to apply a zero-rate of value added tax to the supply of reusable period underwear.

Article 2 amends Group 19 of Schedule 8 to VATA to add reusable period underwear to the list of descriptions included within the definition of “women’s sanitary products” that are zero-rated supplies for the purposes of value added tax.

A Tax Information and Impact Note covering this instrument will be published on the government website at https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/tax-information-and-impact-notes-tiins.
