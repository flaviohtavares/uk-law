> Text as at 2025-01-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Enquiries relating to professional qualifications and other conditions of practice[^c-51005be2553ece60f18ebedf24375fbc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A regulator must deal promptly with enquiries from specified state professionals about—

- (a) the professional qualifications required to practise the regulated profession; and
- (b) the conditions that apply to the practice of the regulated profession.
