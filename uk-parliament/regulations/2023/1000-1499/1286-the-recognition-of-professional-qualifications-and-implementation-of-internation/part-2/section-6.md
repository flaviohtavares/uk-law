# The Recognition of Professional Qualifications and Implementation of International Recognition Agreements (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 6

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Application of Part 2[^c-cd5f6ec0f25d24d9e97d2bf16f1b92fa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), this Part applies to a regulator that receives an application.

(2) This Part does not apply to medical regulators[^f00008] except in relation to an application—

- (a) for registration in the General Practitioner Register kept under section 34C of the Medical Act 1983[^f00009] or in the Specialist Register kept under section 34D of that Act; [^c-7fd3433b32ccfeb54136158ac60470da]...
- (b) to use a title prescribed under section 26(3) of the Dentists Act 1984[^f00010] ; or[^c-5d969d29cfc1571aa8c10fd613788167]
- (c) for registration in the Anaesthesia Associates and Physician Associates Register kept under article 5(1) of the Anaesthesia Associates and Physician Associates Order 2024.
