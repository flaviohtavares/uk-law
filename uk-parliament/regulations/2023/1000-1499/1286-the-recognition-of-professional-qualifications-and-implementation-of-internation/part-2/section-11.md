# The Recognition of Professional Qualifications and Implementation of International Recognition Agreements (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 11

> Text as at 2025-01-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Knowledge of language[^c-06a16206ef3f7760eeac3d2e932e55dd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A regulator may require that an applicant demonstrates they possess the language skills necessary for practice of a regulated profession.

(2) If a regulator requires an applicant to undertake a language test, the test must be proportionate to the activity to be pursued.
