# The Recognition of Professional Qualifications and Implementation of International Recognition Agreements (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 12

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Appeals[^c-d35b123fce3368ad928bbd771a7ec8d2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A regulator must provide a right of appeal for an applicant against its—

- (a) decision to refuse to recognise an applicant’s professional qualifications; and
- (b) failure to notify the applicant of its decision in respect of an application within the time period referred to in regulation 10(1)(c).
