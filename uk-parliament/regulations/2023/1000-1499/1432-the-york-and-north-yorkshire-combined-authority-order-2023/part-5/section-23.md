# The York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority Order 2023, section 23

> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Amendment of the Sub-national Transport Body (Transport for the North) Regulations 2018[^key-3385f99ee734d2f8f0a496798a50750f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Sub-national Transport Body (Transport for the North) Regulations 2018[^f00079] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) in the definition of “constituent authorities”—

- (a) omit “The Council of the City of York” and “North Yorkshire Council”; and
- (b) after “Westmorland and Furness Council[^f00080]”, insert “The York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority”.
