# The York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority Order 2023, section 35

> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Secondments[^key-88f1000eadc17a60fd45d5ae01090eaf]

*Extent: E+W*

In the case of a person who immediately before 7th May 2024 is seconded to the police and crime commissioner for North Yorkshire, the secondment is to have effect, beginning on that date as a secondment to the Combined Authority.
