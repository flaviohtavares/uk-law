> Text as at 2026-07-22.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Transfer of police and crime commissioner functions[^key-5744d444e99869a39be2c4c57c80312b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the Mayor is to be treated, in relation to the Mayor’s PCC functions[^f00095], as a police and crime commissioner for the purposes of all police and crime commissioner enactments, whenever passed or made[^f00096].

(2) In their application to the Mayor, the police and crime commissioner enactments set out in Schedule 5 apply with the modifications set out in that Schedule.

(3) Paragraph (1) does not apply to the enactments set out in Schedule 6.
