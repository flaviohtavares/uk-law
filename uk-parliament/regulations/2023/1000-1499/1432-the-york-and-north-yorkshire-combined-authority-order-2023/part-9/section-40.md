> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Modification to the Local Government Act 1972[^key-9caf17eec4b09e7d0145d02b62424584]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 86 of the Local Government Act 1972 (declaration by local authority of vacancy in office in certain cases)[^f00102] applies in relation to the Mayor with the following modification.

(2) After section 86(1)(c), insert—

> or
>
> - (d) ceases to be the Mayor by virtue of section 63 (vacancy where acting commissioner acts for 6 months) of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011,

.
