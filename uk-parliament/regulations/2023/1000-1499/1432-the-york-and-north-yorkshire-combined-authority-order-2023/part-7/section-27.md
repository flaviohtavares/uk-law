# The York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority Order 2023, section 27

> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Functions exercisable only by the Mayor[^key-b47a0e5d3f37d9f4e7828afa79b63679]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The functions of the Combined Authority specified in paragraph (2) are general functions exercisable only by the Mayor.

(2) The functions referred to in paragraph (1) are the functions of the Combined Authority corresponding to the functions in the following enactments—

- (a)[^key-fec2125e068c676fd514216f3cf051ac] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) section[^key-09302ed8f4ad133a73236d297a493657] 154(1) (grants to bus service operators) of the 2000 Act;
- (c)[^key-77466770f6828c47efe563d1990b7160] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^key-51f948d65b5f7169e3174a50e2044604] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) in relation to the functions conferred by article 30 of this Order, the BRS Act; and
- (f)[^key-4d707a5ef7b84d6edb965131d31b7f93] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-3ff16aff7ea285d96b92e0129d53d9c7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) For the purposes of the exercise of any mayoral function[^key-9cf2c479b233fbc6f042b5623491e4b0] the Mayor may do anything that the Combined Authority may do under section 113A of the 2009 Act (general power of EPB or combined authority)[^f00088].

(5) The Mayor must not make arrangements under section 107D(3)(b) of the 2009 Act (functions of mayors: general) in relation to the functions specified in paragraph (2)(e), in relation to a political adviser appointed under article 5.
