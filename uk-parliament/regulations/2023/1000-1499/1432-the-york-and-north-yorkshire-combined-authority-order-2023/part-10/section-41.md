# The York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority Order 2023, section 41

> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Transfer of functions of the North Yorkshire Police, Fire and Crime Commissioner Fire and Rescue Authority[^c-672732d331d7b5fa59f626d9c34ea999][^c-6908e923ae5bd3dc360a63d327cdfb0d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-672732d331d7b5fa59f626d9c34ea999] The functions of the FRA that are exercisable in relation to the Area are to be functions of the Combined Authority.

(2)[^c-6908e923ae5bd3dc360a63d327cdfb0d] The Combined Authority is the fire and rescue authority for the Area for the purposes of the 2004 Act.

(3)[^c-6908e923ae5bd3dc360a63d327cdfb0d] The FRA is abolished.
