# The York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority Order 2023, section 51

> Text as at 2026-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Secondments[^c-cad850fabe3045c1372689b963c60eaa]

*Extent: E+W*

In the case of a person who, immediately before 7th May 2024 is seconded to the FRA, the secondment is to have effect, beginning on 7th May, as a secondment to the Combined Authority.
