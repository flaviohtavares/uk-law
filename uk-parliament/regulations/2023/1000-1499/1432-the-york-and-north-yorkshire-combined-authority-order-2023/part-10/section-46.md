## 46. Police, Fire and Crime Panel: oversight functions in relation to fire and rescue functions[^c-31b52553d074e45b400b99ae759fcd40]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Police, Fire and Crime Panel has the oversight functions set out in articles 47 to 49, and by virtue of Schedule 7, in relation to—

- (a) any fire and rescue functions of the Mayor that are exercisable by the deputy mayor for policing and crime as a result of arrangements described in article 43(2)(a); and
- (b) fire and rescue functions exercisable by the Mayor.

(2) The modifications of enactments set out in Schedule 7 have effect for the purpose of the application of those enactments to the Combined Authority as a fire and rescue authority.
