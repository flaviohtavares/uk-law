# The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Removal of Prisoners for Deportation) Order 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 1368

Prisons, England And Wales

The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Removal of Prisoners for Deportation) Order 2023

Made

12th December 2023

Coming into force

16th January 2024

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 260(6)(b) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00001], makes the following Order.

In accordance with section 330(5)(a) of that Act, a draft of this Order was laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Removal of Prisoners for Deportation) Order 2023.

(2) This Order comes into force on 16th January 2024.

(3) This Order extends to England and Wales only.

## 2. Amendment to section 260 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003

In section 260(2)(b)[^f00002] of the Criminal Justice Act 2003, for “one year” substitute “545 days”.

Edward Argar

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

12th December 2023

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends section 260 of the Criminal Justice Act [2003 (c. 44)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/44) (“**the 2003 Act**”).

Sections 260 and 261 of the 2003 Act allow for the removal of determinate sentence foreign national prisoners from prison for the purposes of deportation from the UK. As currently drafted, section 260(2) provides that the Secretary of State may remove from prison an eligible fixed-term prisoner up to one year before their automatic release point once they have served at least half of the custodial part of their sentence (the minimum pre-removal custodial period).

This Order increases the maximum removal period in section 260(2)(b) by substituting a reference to 545 days (18 months), in place of the previous reference to one year. This means that the pre-removal custodial period is now the later date of a) half of the requisite custodial period or b) 545 days (eighteen months) before the end of the requisite custodial period.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
