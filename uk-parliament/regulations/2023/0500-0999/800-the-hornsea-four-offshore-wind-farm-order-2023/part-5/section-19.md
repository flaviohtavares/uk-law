> Text as at 2025-04-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 19. Compulsory acquisition of land: minerals[^c-eab6c3b08824df0951a787f4392412f8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 are incorporated in this Order, subject to the following modifications—

- (a) for “acquiring authority” substitute “undertaker”; and
- (b) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised project”.
