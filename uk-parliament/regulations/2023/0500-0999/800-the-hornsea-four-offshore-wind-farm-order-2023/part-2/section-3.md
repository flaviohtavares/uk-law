# The Hornsea Four Offshore Wind Farm Order 2023, section 3

> Text as at 2025-04-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Development consent etc. granted by this Order[^c-15da534cc1e138b3ec890c063d9b313d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order and to the requirements, the undertaker is granted—

- (a) development consent for the authorised development; and
- (b) consent for the ancillary works,

to be carried out within the Order limits.

(2) Work Nos. 1 to 5 (save for those elements of Work No. 5(a) located landward of MHWS in order to connect to Work No. 6) must be constructed within the Order limits seaward of MHWS and Work Nos. 6 to 10 (save for those elements of Work Nos. 9(a) and 9(d) located seaward of MHWS for foreshore access) must be constructed within the Order limits landward of MHWS.
