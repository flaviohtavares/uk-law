# The Hornsea Four Offshore Wind Farm Order 2023, section 48

> Text as at 2025-04-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 48. Modification of Section 106 agreements relating to land[^c-4181fd51f66236abac828169e6c4f994]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker will not be bound by any obligation which would fall on any owner or occupier of land which is bound by any of the section 106 agreements.
