# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 122

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 122. Costs and expenses of witnesses

(1) An officer of the relevant licensee nuclear company to which the relevant licensee nuclear company administration proceedings relate is not to receive an allowance as a witness in an examination or other proceedings before the court except as directed by the court.

(2) A person making any application in relevant licensee nuclear company administration proceedings is not to receive an allowance as a witness for attending the hearing of the application, but the costs officer may allow that person’s expenses of travelling and subsistence.
