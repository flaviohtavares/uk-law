# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 136

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 136. Further information and disclosure

(1) A party to relevant licensee nuclear company administration proceedings may apply to the court for an order—

- (a) that in accordance with CPR Part 18[^f00052] (further information) another party—
  - (i) clarify a matter that is in dispute in the proceedings, or
  - (ii) give additional information in relation to such a matter, or
- (b) for disclosure from any person in accordance with CPR Part 31[^f00053] (disclosure and inspection of documents), save where rules 18 or 24 apply.

(2) An application under this rule may be made without notice to any other party.
