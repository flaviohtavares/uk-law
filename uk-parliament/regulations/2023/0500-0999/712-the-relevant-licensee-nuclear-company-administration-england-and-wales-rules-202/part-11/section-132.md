# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 132

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 132. Rights of audience

Rights of audience in relevant licensee nuclear company administration proceedings are the same as in insolvency proceedings.
