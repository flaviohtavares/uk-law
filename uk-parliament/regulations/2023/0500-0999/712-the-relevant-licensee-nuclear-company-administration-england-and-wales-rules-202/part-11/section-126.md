# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 126

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 126. Witness statement in support of application

An application under rule 125(3) must be supported by a witness statement made by a registered medical practitioner as to the mental or physical condition of the incapacitated person.
