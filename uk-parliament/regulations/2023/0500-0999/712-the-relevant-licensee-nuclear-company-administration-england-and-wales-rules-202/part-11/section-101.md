# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 101

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 101. Preliminary

This Chapter applies to any application made to the court in relevant licensee nuclear company administration proceedings, except an application for an RLNC administration order.
