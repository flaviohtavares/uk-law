# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 116

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 116. Application and interpretation

(1) This Chapter applies to costs of and in connection with relevant licensee nuclear company administration proceedings.

(2) In this Chapter, “**costs**” includes charges and expenses.

(3) CPR Parts 44 and 47[^f00046] (which relate to costs) apply to such costs.
