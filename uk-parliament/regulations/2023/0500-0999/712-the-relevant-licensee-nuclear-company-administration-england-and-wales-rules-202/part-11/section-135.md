# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 135

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 135. Payment into court

CPR Part 37[^f00051] (miscellaneous provisions about payment into court) applies to money lodged in court under these Rules.
