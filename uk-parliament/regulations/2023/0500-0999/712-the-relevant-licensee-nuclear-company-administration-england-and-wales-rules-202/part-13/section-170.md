# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 170

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 170. Documents relating to the office of nuclear administrators

A document relating to the office of the nuclear administrator must also specify—

- (a) the name of the nuclear administrator;
- (b) the date of the event of which notice is delivered or of the notice (as applicable);
- (c) where the document relates to an appointment, the court making the appointment;
- (d) where the document relates to the termination of an appointment, the reason for that termination;
- (e) the postal address of the nuclear administrator.
