# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 148

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 148. Delivery of documents to joint nuclear administrators

Delivery of a document to one of joint nuclear administrators is to be treated as delivery to them all.
