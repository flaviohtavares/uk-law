# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 160

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 160. Service on joint nuclear administrators

Service of a document on one of joint nuclear administrators is to be treated as service on all of them.
