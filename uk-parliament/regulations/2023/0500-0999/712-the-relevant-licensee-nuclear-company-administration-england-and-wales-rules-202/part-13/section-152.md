# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 152

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 152. Electronic delivery by nuclear administrators

(1) Where a nuclear administrator delivers a document by electronic means, the document must contain, or be accompanied by, a statement that the recipient may request a hard copy of the document and a telephone number, email address and postal address that may be used to make that request.

(2) A nuclear administrator who receives such a request must deliver a hard copy of the document to the recipient free of charge within five business days of receipt of the request.
