# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 151

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 151. Electronic delivery of notices to enforcement officers, etc.

Where anything in the 1986 Act or these Rules provides for the delivery of a notice to an enforcement officer or enforcement agent, it may be delivered by electronic means to a person who has been authorised to receive such a notice on behalf of a specified enforcement officer or specified enforcement agent or on behalf of enforcement officers or enforcement agents generally.
