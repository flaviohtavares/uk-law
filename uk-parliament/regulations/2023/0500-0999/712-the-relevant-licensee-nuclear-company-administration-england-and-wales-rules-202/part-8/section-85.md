# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Remuneration of new nuclear administrator

If a new nuclear administrator is appointed in place of another, any court order in effect under rule 84 immediately before the former nuclear administrator ceased to hold office continues to apply in respect of the remuneration of the new nuclear administrator until a further court order is made in accordance with those provisions.
