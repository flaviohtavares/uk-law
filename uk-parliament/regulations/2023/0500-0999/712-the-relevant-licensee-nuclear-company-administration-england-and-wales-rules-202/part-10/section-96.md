# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 96

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 96. Notice of vacation of office when nuclear administrator ceases to be qualified to act

A nuclear administrator who has ceased to be qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the relevant licensee nuclear company and gives notice in accordance with paragraph 89 of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act[^f00043] must also deliver notice to—

- (a) the Secretary of State,
- (b) the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority, and
- (c) the registrar of companies.

## Annotations

[^f00043]: Footnote: Paragraph 89 was amended by the Enterprise Act 2002, Schedule 16, paragraph 1.
