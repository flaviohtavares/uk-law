# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 182

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 182. False claim of status as creditor, etc.

Where these Rules provide for creditors, members or contributories of a relevant licensee nuclear company a right to inspect any documents, whether on the court file or in the hands of a nuclear administrator or other person, it is an offence for a person, with the intention of gaining sight of documents which the person has not under these Rules any right to inspect, falsely to claim a status which would entitle the person to inspect them.
