# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 80. Disqualification from dividend

If a creditor contravenes any provision of the 1986 Act or these Rules relating to the valuation of securities, the court may, on the application of the nuclear administrator, order that the creditor be wholly or partly disqualified from participation in any dividend.
