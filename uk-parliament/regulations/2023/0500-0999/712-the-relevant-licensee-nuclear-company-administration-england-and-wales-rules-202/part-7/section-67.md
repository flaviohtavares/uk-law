# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Debts of insolvent relevant licensee nuclear company to rank equally

Debts other than preferential debts rank equally between themselves in the relevant licensee nuclear company administration and, after the preferential debts, must be paid in full unless the assets are insufficient for meeting them, in which case they abate in equal proportions between themselves.
