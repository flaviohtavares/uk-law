# The Relevant Licensee Nuclear Company Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2023, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Distribution to creditors generally

(1) This Chapter applies where the nuclear administrator makes, or proposes to make, a distribution to any class of creditors other than secured creditors.

(2) Where the distribution is to a particular class of creditors, a reference in this Chapter to creditors is, in so far as the context requires, a reference to that class of creditors only.
