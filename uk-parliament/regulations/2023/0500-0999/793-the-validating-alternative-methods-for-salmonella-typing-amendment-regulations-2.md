# The Validating Alternative Methods for Salmonella Typing (Amendment) Regulations 2023

The Validating Alternative Methods for Salmonella Typing (Amendment) Regulations 2023

Made

12th July 2023

Laid before Parliament

13th July 2023

Coming into force

4th August 2023

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 4(1) of Regulation [(EC) 2160/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2160) on the control of salmonella and other specified food-borne zoonotic agents[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

In accordance with the requirements of Article 2A(2) of that Regulation, the Secretary of State has obtained the consent of the Scottish Ministers and the Welsh Ministers.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Validating Alternative Methods for Salmonella Typing (Amendment) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 4th August 2023.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, and Scotland.

## 2. Amendment to Commission Regulation (EU) No 1190/2012 concerning a Union target for the reduction of Salmonella Enteritidis and Salmonella Typhimurium in flocks of turkeys

(1) [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1190/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/1190) concerning a Union target for the reduction of *Salmonella* Enteritidis and *Salmonella* Typhimurium in flocks of turkeys[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In the Annex, in point 3.4 (alternative methods), for the words from “ISO 16140-2” to the end substitute “EN ISO 16140-2 (for alternative detection methods) or EN ISO 16140-6 (for validation of the confirmation or typing procedure of the typing method)”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of powers conferred by Article 4(1) of Regulation [(EC) 2160/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2160) on the control of salmonella and other specified food-borne zoonotic agents. They extend to England and Wales, and Scotland.

These Regulations amend the regulations concerning salmonella in poultry and poultry products to include a reference to a standard adopted by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) for validating alternative typing methods for salmonella.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
