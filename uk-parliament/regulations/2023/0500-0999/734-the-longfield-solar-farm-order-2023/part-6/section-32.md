# The Longfield Solar Farm Order 2023, section 32

> Text as at 2026-02-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 32. Benefit of the Order[^c-c23bb9aedc34f18ccf66439c6d312830]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to article 33 (consent to transfer the benefit of the Order), the provisions of this Order have effect solely for the benefit of the undertaker save for the Bulls Lodge substation works in relation to which the provisions of this Order have effect for the benefit of the undertaker and NGET and Work No. 6(k) in relation to which the provisions of this Order have effect for the benefit of the undertaker and UK Power Networks Limited.
