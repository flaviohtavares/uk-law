# The Service Police (Complaints etc.) Regulations 2023, section 87

> Text as at 2023-11-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Interpretation of this Part[^key-822d53a4df623647d5b75167d490d01d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**super-complaint**” means a complaint made to the Chief Inspector under regulation 88;
- “**trade union**” has the meaning given in section 1 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992[^f00016].
