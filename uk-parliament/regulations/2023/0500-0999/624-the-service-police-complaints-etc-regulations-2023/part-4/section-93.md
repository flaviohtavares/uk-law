# The Service Police (Complaints etc.) Regulations 2023, section 93

> Text as at 2023-11-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Duty to acknowledge receipt[^key-b903abe0406d7a66d955f64a86d05f57]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Chief Inspector must acknowledge receipt of a super-complaint before the end of the period of 5 working days beginning with the day on which the super-complaint is received or, if that day is not a working day, beginning with the first working day after that day.
