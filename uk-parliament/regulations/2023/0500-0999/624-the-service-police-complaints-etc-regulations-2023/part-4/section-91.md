# The Service Police (Complaints etc.) Regulations 2023, section 91

> Text as at 2023-11-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Guidance about super-complaints[^key-af14ee195298d4abc681d284d763c65d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Chief Inspector may give guidance in relation to the making of super-complaints, to bodies designated by the Secretary of State in accordance with this Part.

(2) The Chief Inspector may revise any guidance given under paragraph (1).
