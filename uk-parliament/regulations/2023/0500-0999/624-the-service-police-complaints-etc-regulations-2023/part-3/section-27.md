# The Service Police (Complaints etc.) Regulations 2023, section 27

> Text as at 2023-11-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Duty to record DSI matters[^key-6fdc0e5bd10a027e3bfea940feb4b051]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a DSI matter comes to the attention of the appropriate authority in relation to that matter, the authority must record that matter.
