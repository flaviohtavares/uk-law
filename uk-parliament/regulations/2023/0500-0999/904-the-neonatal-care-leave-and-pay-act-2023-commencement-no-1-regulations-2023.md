# The Neonatal Care (Leave and Pay) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 904 (C. 49)

TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT

The Neonatal Care (Leave and Pay) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2023

Made

9th August 2023

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(3) of the Neonatal Care (Leave and Pay) Act 2023[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Neonatal Care (Leave and Pay) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2023.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 21st August 2023

The following provisions of the Neonatal Care (Leave and Pay) Act 2023 come into force on 21st August 2023—

- (a) section 1, but only for the purpose of bringing paragraph 49 of the Schedule into force; and
- (b) paragraph 49 of the Schedule, but only for the purpose of preparatory work for the making of payments of statutory neonatal care pay.

Kevin Hollinrake

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State (Minister for Enterprise, Markets and Small Business)

Department for Business and Trade

9th August 2023

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force paragraph 49 of the Schedule to the Neonatal Care (Leave and Pay) Act [2023 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/20) for the purpose of enabling HMRC to begin preparatory work for the making of payments of statutory neonatal care pay. The Regulations also bring into force section 1 of the 2023 Act for the purpose of bringing into force paragraph 49 of the Schedule.
