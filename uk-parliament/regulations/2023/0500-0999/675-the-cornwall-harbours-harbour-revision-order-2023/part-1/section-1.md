# The Cornwall Harbours Harbour Revision Order 2023, section 1

> Text as at 2023-07-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^c-0765d23fefd559741f9c6104fd12cae8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Cornwall Harbours Harbour Revision Order 2023 and shall come into force on 14th July 2023.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales.
