## 68. Revocation / Repeal[^c-69eaf9084cf7bedf7a0e4b418dadcf60]

*Extent: E+W*

On the date this Order comes into force the enactments mentioned in the first and second columns of Schedule 4 (revocation / repeal) shall be revoked or repealed (as appropriate) to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
