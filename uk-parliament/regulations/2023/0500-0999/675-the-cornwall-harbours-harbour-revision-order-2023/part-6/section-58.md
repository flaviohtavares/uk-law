# The Cornwall Harbours Harbour Revision Order 2023, section 58

> Text as at 2023-07-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Power to delegate functions[^key-4989c6b8d0434da2afe9d708b941669b]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to paragraph 9B of Schedule 2 to the Act of 1964 (functions which cannot be delegated under a power conferred by a harbour revision order), the Council may delegate the performance of any of its functions to be carried out by any such company as is referred to in article 57(1)(b).
