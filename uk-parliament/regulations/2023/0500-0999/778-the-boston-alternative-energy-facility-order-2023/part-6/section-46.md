# The Boston Alternative Energy Facility Order 2023, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 46. Deemed marine licence

The marine licence set out in Schedule 9 (deemed marine licence) is deemed to have been issued under Part 4 of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 for the licensed activities set out in Part 2, and subject to the licence conditions set out in Parts 3 and 4, of that licence.
