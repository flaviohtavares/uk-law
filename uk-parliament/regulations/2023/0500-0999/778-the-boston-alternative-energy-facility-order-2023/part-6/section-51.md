# The Boston Alternative Energy Facility Order 2023, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. No double recovery

Compensation will not be payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any enactment, any contract or any rule of law.
