# The Boston Alternative Energy Facility Order 2023, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Compulsory acquisition of land

(1) The undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised development or to facilitate it, or as is incidental to it.

(2) This article is subject to article 27 (time limit for exercise of authority to acquire land compulsorily) and paragraph (9) of article 33 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development).
