# The Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act 2020 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 759

Family Law, England And Wales

The Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act 2020 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2023

Made

4th July 2023

Laid before Parliament

6th July 2023

Coming into force

1st August 2023

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the power conferred by section 6(2) of the Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act 2020[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These regulations may be cited as the Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act 2020 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 1st August 2023.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment to the Registration of Marriage Regulations 2015

In Schedule 3 (evidence) to the Registration of Marriage Regulations 2015[^f00002], in paragraph 5(1)(a)—

- (a) after “decree absolute” insert “or final order”;
- (b) after “marriage” insert “or nullity of marriage order”.

Bellamy

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

4th July 2023

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make amendments to the Registration of Marriage Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/207](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/207)) which are consequential on the amendments made to the Matrimonial Causes Act [1973 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/18) by the Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act [2020 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/11) updating the terminology in relation to divorce and nullity applications.

A full impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary, or public sector is foreseen.
