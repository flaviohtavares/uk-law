# The Aviation Safety (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Amendment of Article 5 of Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014

(1) Article 5 (certifying staff) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “M.A.801(d)” substitute “M.A.801(c)”;
- (b) omit “CAO.A.035(d) and”;
- (c) after “CAO.A.040(b)” insert “and CAO.A.040(c)”.

(3) After paragraph 6 insert—

> **7.** Limited certifying staff authorisations issued to flight engineer licence holders pursuant to point 145.A.30(j)(3) or (4) of Annex II (Part-145) before 1 July 2024 continue to be valid until they expire or until they are revoked by the maintenance organisation.
