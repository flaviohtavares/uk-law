# The Aviation Safety (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Amendment of Article 4 of Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014

In Article 4 (approvals for organisations involved in the continuing airworthiness of aircraft), after paragraph 6 insert—

> A maintenance organisation that holds a valid approval certificate issued in accordance with Annex II (Part-145) must correct any findings of non-compliance related to the implementation of the Safety Management System requirements before 1 July 2026.
>
> Where, on or after 1 July 2026, the organisation has not closed such findings, the approval certificate must be revoked, limited or suspended in whole or in part.
