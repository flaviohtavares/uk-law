# The Aviation Safety (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Amendment of Section A of Annex III to Commission Regulation (EU) 1321/2014

In Section A (technical requirements), in Subpart A (aircraft maintenance licence)—

- (a) for point 66.A.20(b)(1) substitute—

  > **1.** in compliance with the applicable requirements of Annex I (Part-M), Annex II (Part-145), Annex Vb (Part-ML) and Annex Vd (Part-CAO); and

  ;
- (b) in point 66.A.25(a), for “categories B2L and” substitute “category”.
