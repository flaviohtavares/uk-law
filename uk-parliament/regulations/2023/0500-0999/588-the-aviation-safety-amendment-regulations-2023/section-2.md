# The Aviation Safety (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012 (initial airworthiness)

[Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/0748) of 3rd August 2012 laying down implementing rules for the airworthiness and environmental certification of aircraft and related products, parts and appliances, as well as for the certification of design and production organisations[^f00002], is amended in accordance with regulations 3 to 11.
