# The Aviation Safety (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014 (continuing airworthiness)

[Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/1321) on the continuing airworthiness of aircraft and aeronautical products, parts and appliances, and on the approval of organisations and personnel involved in these tasks is amended in accordance with regulations 13 to 31.
