# The Criminal Procedure (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2023, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Amendment to the preamble to the Criminal Procedure Rules

In the preamble to the Criminal Procedure Rules 2020, in sub-paragraph (b), in the first column, headed “Rule”, immediately after the entry for Part 23 insert “24.9” and in the second column, headed “Power”, in the corresponding position insert “Section 16K(1) of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980[^f00033]”.

## Annotations

[^f00033]: Footnote: [1980 c. 43](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/43); section 16K is inserted by section 3 of the Judicial Review and Courts Act [2022 (c. 35)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/35) with effect from a date to be appointed.
