# The Criminal Procedure (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2023, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

These Rules may be cited as the Criminal Procedure (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2023 and come into force on 2nd October 2023.

**2.** In these Rules a reference to a Part or rule by number alone means the Part or rule so numbered in the Criminal Procedure Rules 2020[^f00003].
