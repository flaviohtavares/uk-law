# The Little Crow Solar Park (Correction) Order 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 311

Infrastructure Planning

The Little Crow Solar Park (Correction) Order 2023

Made

9th March 2023

Coming into force

10th March 2023

The Little Crow Solar Park Order 2022[^f00001] (“**the Order**”), which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008[^f00002] (“**the Act**”) contains correctable errors within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, and before the end of the relevant period (as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act), the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00003] for the corrections of errors and omissions in the Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed the relevant local planning authority for the area in which the land to which the Order relates is situated that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 119 of, and paragraph 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Little Crow Solar Park (Correction) Order 2023 and comes into force on 10th March 2023.

## 2. Corrections

The Little Crow Solar Park Order 2022 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy, Security and Net Zero

David Wagstaff

Deputy Director Energy Infrastructure Planning

Department for Energy, Security and Net Zero

9th March 2023

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors in the Little Crow Solar Park Order 2022, which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a request made under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
