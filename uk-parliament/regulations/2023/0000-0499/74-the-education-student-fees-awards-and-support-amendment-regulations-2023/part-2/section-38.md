# The Education (Student Fees, Awards and Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 38

> Text as at 2023-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Designation of courses for higher technical qualifications[^c-6d28643dadf1830cd19f7c5ea68eb570]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)[^f00031]—

- (a) after the definition of “allied health profession subject” insert—

  > - “**approved HTQ**” means an approved technical education qualification (within the meaning of Chapter A1 of Part 1 of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Learning and Children Act 2009[^f00032] (see section A12(1) of that Act)) which is—
  >   - (a) included in the list of qualifications maintained by the Institute for Apprenticeships and Technical Education[^f00033] in accordance with section A2HA of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Learning and Children Act 2009 (list of technical education qualifications); and
  >   - (b) at level 4 or 5—
  >     - (i) on the Framework for Higher Education Qualifications of UK-Degree Awarding Bodies[^f00034], as amended from time to time; or
  >     - (ii) as described in the Ofqual Handbook: General Conditions of Recognition[^f00035], as amended from time to time;

  ;
- (b) for the definition of “preliminary course” substitute—

  > - “**preliminary course**” means—
  >   - (a) a course mentioned in paragraph 2 to 4 of Schedule 2, or overseas equivalent, that is taken before a full-time degree course (other than a first degree course for the initial training of teachers);
  >   - (b) a course mentioned in paragraph 12 of Schedule 2 that is taken before a full-time degree course; or
  >   - (c) a foundation degree course, or overseas equivalent, taken before a full-time honours degree course;

  .

**39.**[^c-a23ea47a7683b44ff744db1864d73641] (1) In regulation 22 (standard entitlement of current system students on certain degree courses etc)[^f00036]—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(a), for the words from “mentioned in paragraph” to “intensive course” substitute “specified in paragraph (2A)”;
- (b) after paragraph (2) insert—

  > (2A) The courses mentioned in paragraph (2)(a) are—
  >
  > - (a) an intensive course;
  > - (b) a course mentioned in paragraph 2, 3 or 4 of Schedule 2 or overseas equivalent;
  > - (c) a course mentioned in paragraph 12 of Schedule 2;
  > - (d) a foundation degree course or overseas equivalent.
  >
  > (2B) For the purposes of paragraph (2)(a) it does not matter whether a course specified in paragraph (2A)(b), (c) or (d) was completed on a full-time, part-time or full-time distance learning basis.

  .

**40.**[^c-5c1f08ecc68f40bf6602fbeb78df3952] In regulation 139(2A) (designated part-time courses)[^f00037], after sub-paragraph (c) insert—

> - (ca) a course which leads to an approved HTQ;

.

**41.**[^c-968142900d67e882f2c6ee8ad8c2c491] In Schedule 2 (designated courses), after paragraph 11 insert—

> **12.** A course for an approved HTQ.

.
