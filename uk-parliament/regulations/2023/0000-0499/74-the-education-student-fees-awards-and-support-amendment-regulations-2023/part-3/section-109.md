# The Education (Student Fees, Awards and Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 109

> Text as at 2023-06-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 109. Exceptions: training courses for further education teachers[^key-801f3c6538fd10957d12388aa24c402a]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 5(2A)(a) (qualifying persons: exceptions)[^f00086], for the words from “a course” to “Postgraduate Certificate in Education” substitute “a course falling within regulation 2(1ZA)(e), (f), (g), (h) or (i) of the Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011”.
