# The Education (Student Fees, Awards and Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 55

> Text as at 2023-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 55. Insertion of definition of “the course start date”[^c-957f9bf6231018c83eb4a39122c25dd5]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 2 (interpretation), after the definition of “award” insert—

> - “**the course start date**” means the day on which the first term of the first academic year of a course actually begins;

.

**56.**[^c-8c4faaba7b5ebba705af3ea7ccc8d754] (1) Schedule 1 (eligible students)[^f00049] is amended as follows.—

(2) In paragraph 2A(1) (persons who are settled in the United Kingdom)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “the day on which the first term of the first academic year of the course actually begins” substitute “the course start date”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “the day on which the first term of the first academic year of the course actually begins” substitute “the course start date”.

(3) In paragraph 8(1)(c) (persons who are settled in the United Kingdom and have exercised a right of residence elsewhere), for “the day on which the first term of the first academic year of the course actually begins” substitute “the course start date”.

(4) In paragraph 8A(1)(d) (persons who are settled in the United Kingdom and have exercised a right of residence elsewhere), for “the day on which the first term of the first academic year of the course actually begins” substitute “the course start date”.
