# The Education (Student Fees, Awards and Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 133

> Text as at 2023-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 133. Amendment of regulation 9 of the Education (Student Support) (European University Institute) Regulations 2010[^c-d4cf969b1354b952323ac385685d8c0f]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 9 (eligible students) of the Education (Student Support) (European University Institute) Regulations 2010 (as amended by regulation 70 of these Regulations)—

- (a) in paragraph (11C)(a), for “eligible person” substitute “eligible student”;
- (b) in paragraph (11D)(a), for “eligible person” substitute “eligible student”.
