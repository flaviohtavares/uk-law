# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 70D

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70D. Repayment of any excess approximated pension payments[^c-d108ef33542de5adb120a824558ba2a9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where the approximated pension payments amount in relation to a person is greater than the post-amendment day benefits increase amount.

(2) The administrators may recover the difference between the two amounts mentioned in paragraph (1) from the person, including by way of deduction from any benefits to which P is entitled under the principal scheme, including in such instalments from payments of retirement pension as the administrators may determine.
