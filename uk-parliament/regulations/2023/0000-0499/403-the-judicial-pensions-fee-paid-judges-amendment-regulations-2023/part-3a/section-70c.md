# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 70C

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70C. Additional payment where no or insufficient approximated pension payments made[^key-d108ef33542de5adb120a824558ba2a9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where the approximated pension payments amount in relation to a person is less than the post-amendment day benefits increase amount.

(2) The administrators must pay to the person the amount of the difference between the two amounts mentioned in paragraph (1).
