# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 69

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Additional payment where no or insufficient interim amendments payments made[^key-fb31be08717288061fcdb87eda8b1846]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where the interim amendments payments amount in relation to a person is less than the pre-amendment day benefits increase amount.

(2) The administrators must pay to the person the amount of the difference between the two amounts mentioned in paragraph (1).
