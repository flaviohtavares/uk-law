# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) (Amendment) Regulations 2023, section 1

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-0dd000606d5774ead678c6f8c2201871]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) (Amendment) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 1st April 2023.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
