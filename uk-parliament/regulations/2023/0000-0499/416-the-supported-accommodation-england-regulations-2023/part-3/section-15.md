# The Supported Accommodation (England) Regulations 2023, section 15

> Text as at 2023-10-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Compliance with regulations[^c-9be08d7bd35ff81929ed0e398f63c87e]

*Extent: E+W*

Where there is more than one registered person in respect of the supported accommodation undertaking, anything which is required under these Regulations to be done by the registered person is not, if done by one of the registered persons, required to be done by any of the other registered persons.
