# The Supported Accommodation (England) Regulations 2023, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2023-10-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Information and documents to be included in each child’s case records

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 24

### 1. Personal details in relation to the child[^key-0d3766411d1e7d89548a33231cc4d5c3]

The child’s name and any name by which the child has previously been known, other than a name used by the child prior to adoption.

**2.**[^key-5508f8f8664f06d833b651b8d5112890] The child’s date of birth and sex.

**3.**[^key-ff1ef6fe7677334885356dd73e406e1b] The child’s religion, if any.

**4.**[^key-696b7592c019deb8271ae2d65f57c8bf] The child’s ethnicity, and the child’s cultural and linguistic background.

**5.**[^key-6270066533026b7cb8bc071d2eac0f77] The child’s address immediately before being admitted to the supported accommodation.

**6.**[^key-19c412f8486d2e65d7396f09bbd14d43] The address, and the type of accommodation, to which the child is to go when the child is discharged from the supported accommodation.

**7.**[^key-5a7a1df754c1bcee97d81215bc5de271] The dates on which any money or valuables are deposited by or on behalf of the child for safekeeping, the amount of money or a description of the valuables, and the dates on which any money is withdrawn, and any valuables are returned.

**8.**[^key-8ae35732916f41f62ea144af9e196197] The statutory provision under which the child is accommodated in the supported accommodation.

### 9. Contact details of certain persons in relation to the child[^key-092d117098bd635bbbf6bada728af1d2]

The name of an individual who may be contacted at, and the address and telephone number of, the accommodating authority.

**10.**[^key-21617e10e1e3cb584cb9597bcbe9da56] The name, address, telephone number and the religion, if any, of the child’s parents.

**11.**[^key-9ab2fb06de5fd057835a698985dd29c9] The name, address and telephone number of any social worker assigned to the child by the accommodating authority.

**12.**[^key-061b513f6a2d72ff7fe86df537ae1b07] If the child attends a school or college—

- (a) the name, address and telephone number of the school or college, and
- (b) if the school has a member of staff who has been designated by its governing body under section 20(1) of the Children and Young Persons Act 2008[^f00050], the name and telephone number of the designated member of staff.

**13.**[^key-f0f943171410c29bd3d65d4701561200] The name, address and telephone number of any employer of the child.

### 14. Information relating to the care, protection or safety of the child[^key-b508579b2993ee51dbca7bb6342a8b2e]

The date and circumstances of all incidents where a child goes missing from the premises used as supported accommodation, including any information relating to the child’s whereabouts during the period of absence.

**15.**[^key-3bac36f6beb1096dbbdf30780d7d13b7] The date and circumstances of any measure of restraint used in relation to the child.

**16.**[^key-df8d512877cbf306d2fab953ec1d85c5] Arrangements for, and any restrictions on, contact between the child, the child’s parents, and any other person.

### 17. Plans or reports relating to the child[^key-1f4f8e957b4c7815fa27958bf7576e87]

A copy of any statement of special educational needs in relation to the child.

**18.**[^key-48260106ba4af3e5772f4ee69f69d73f] Every school or college report received in respect of the child while the child is accommodated by the supported accommodation undertaking.

**19.**[^key-7c23c571f9d4a9f3e5cad24566f69819] A copy of any relevant plan for the child.

**20.**[^key-0e377a171a34fd0c5df9e750d6cadd31] The date and result of any review of any relevant plan for the child.

### 21. Health matters in relation to the child[^key-87048b029986b034aa33e9aa6e1a98c7]

The name of the general medical practitioner with whom the child is a registered patient, the address of the premises at which the child’s primary medical services are usually provided and the name and address of the child’s registered dental practitioner.

**22.**[^key-7e659978e2d30bfdc356a04be8d8ee7b] Details of any accident involving the child or serious illness the child has had while accommodated by the supported accommodation undertaking.

**23.**[^key-7c643849550da056ddd4ba4d2c47ed77] Details of any immunisation, allergy, or medical examination of the child and of any medical or dental need or treatment of the child.

**24.**[^key-287de607a389f6ace1c6b0f890166177] Details of any health examination or developmental test conducted with respect to the child at, or in connection with, the child’s school or college.

**25.**[^key-0dd43af7555284dc52542270793bfe9c] Details of any medicines kept for the child at the premises used as supported accommodation, including details of—

- (a) any medicines which the child is permitted to self-administer;
- (b) the administration of any medicine to the child;
- (c) the disposal of any medicine.

**26.**[^key-1fe2dcd8836f8fe24cd461011fbf7ed2] Any special dietary or health needs of the child.
