# The Supported Accommodation (England) Regulations 2023, section 19

> Text as at 2023-10-28.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Temporary staff[^c-b5ff2ef718da5cd6b45e0d05195a4923]

*Extent: E+W*

The registered person must ensure that the employment of any person on a temporary basis does not prevent children from receiving from the supported accommodation undertaking such continuity of support as is reasonable to meet their needs.
