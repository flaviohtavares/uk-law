## 21. Missing child policy[^c-fef3d20f7bd596fc8b67e1d3373c1018]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The registered person must prepare and implement a policy setting out—

- (a) the steps taken, and to be taken, to prevent children accommodated by the supported accommodation undertaking from going missing, and
- (b) the procedures to be followed, and the roles and responsibilities of persons working for the supported accommodation undertaking, in relation to a child who is, or has previously been, missing.

(2) Before implementing, or making an amendment to, the missing child policy, the registered person must—

- (a) consult, and take into account the views of, each relevant person, and
- (b) have regard to any relevant local authority or police protocols on missing children.
