# The Supported Accommodation (England) Regulations 2023, section 23

> Text as at 2023-10-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Contingency plan policy[^c-24bf503b97b978c1050a9b3541226128]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The registered person must prepare and implement a policy setting out—

- (a) what would happen to the children accommodated by the supported accommodation undertaking, and
- (b) what measures would be taken to ensure the appropriate transfer of records,

in the event that the undertaking ceases, whether permanently or temporarily, to provide supported accommodation, as a result of closure, conditions or restrictions imposed on the registration of the undertaking, or because of a suspension or cancellation of registration.

(2) The registered person must provide the contingency plan policy to a local authority considering accommodating a child with the supported accommodation undertaking.
