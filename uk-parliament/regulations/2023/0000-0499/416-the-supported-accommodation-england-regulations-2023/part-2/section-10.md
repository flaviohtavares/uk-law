# The Supported Accommodation (England) Regulations 2023, section 10

> Text as at 2023-10-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Workforce plan[^key-cde70144ace873cf4bbe9cac0b9169d0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The registered person must compile, in relation to the supported accommodation undertaking, a plan (“the workforce plan”) which covers the following matters in respect of the recruitment and employment of all staff—

- (a) the management and staffing structure;
- (b) the experience and qualifications of managers and other staff, and any further training required for those managers and staff;
- (c) the processes and timescales for staff to complete induction, probation and core training, including in safeguarding and health and safety;
- (d) the process for managing and addressing poor performance;
- (e) the process and timescales for supervision and monitoring of staff.

(2) The registered person must—

- (a) keep the workforce plan under review and, where appropriate, revise it, and
- (b) make a copy of the workforce plan available on request to the CIECSS.
