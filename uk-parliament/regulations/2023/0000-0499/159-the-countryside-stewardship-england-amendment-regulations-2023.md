# The Countryside Stewardship (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 159

Agriculture, England

Countryside, England

The Countryside Stewardship (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

Made

at 10.00 a.m. on 16th February 2023

Laid before Parliament

at 3.00 p.m. on 16th February 2023

Coming into force

13th March 2023

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 98 of the Environment Act 1995[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Countryside Stewardship (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 13th March 2023.

(2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales but apply to England only.

## 2. Amendment to the Countryside Stewardship (England) Regulations 2020

In the Countryside Stewardship (England) Regulations 2020[^f00002], for Part 2 of the Schedule (management activities) substitute the new Part 2 set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Countryside Stewardship (England) Regulations 2020 (“**the 2020 Regulations**”) ([S.I. 2020/41](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/41)) and are made pursuant to section 98 of the Environment Act [1995 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/25).

Regulation 2 amends, with effect from 13th March 2023, the maximum payment rates per agreement year for management activities carried out as part of a countryside stewardship agreement under regulation 3 of the 2020 Regulations.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
