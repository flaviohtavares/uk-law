# The Secretaries of State for Energy Security and Net Zero, for Science, Innovation and Technology, for Business and Trade, and for Culture, Media and Sport and the Transfer of Functions (National Security and Investment Act 2021 etc) Order 2023, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Functions under the National Security and Investment Act 2021 and the Enterprise Act 2002 to be exercisable concurrently

(1) The functions of the Secretary of State under the National Security and Investment Act 2021[^f00024] are to be exercisable concurrently with the Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster.

(2) The functions of the Secretary of State under sections 85 to 95 of, and Schedules 7 and 8 to, the Enterprise Act 2002[^f00025] are to be exercisable concurrently with the Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster.
