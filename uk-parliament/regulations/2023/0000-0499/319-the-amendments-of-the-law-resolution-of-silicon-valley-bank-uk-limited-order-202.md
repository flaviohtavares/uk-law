# The Amendments of the Law (Resolution of Silicon Valley Bank UK Limited) Order 2023

> Text as at 2023-06-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

2023 No. 319

Banks And Banking

The Amendments of the Law (Resolution of Silicon Valley Bank UK Limited) Order 2023

Approved by both Houses of Parliament

Made

at 7.36 a.m. on 13th March 2023

Coming into force

at 8.00 a.m. on 13th March 2023

Laid before Parliamentat 3.30 p.m. on 13th March 2023

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 75 of the Banking Act 2009[^f00001].

This Order is made for the purpose of enabling the powers under Part 1 of that Act to be used effectively in connection with the sale of Silicon Valley Bank UK Limited to a private sector purchaser (HSBC UK Bank plc).

In accordance with section 75(1) of that Act, the Treasury have had regard to the special resolution objectives.

In accordance with section 75(8) of that Act, the Treasury think it necessary to make this Order without a draft of it having been approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

Accordingly, the Treasury make the following Order.

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation[^c-77f70b8aeb3c014baac5a293a07971b6]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Amendments of the Law (Resolution of Silicon Valley Bank UK Limited) Order 2023.

(2) This Order comes into force on 13th March 2023 at 8.00 a.m.

(3) This Order extends to the United Kingdom.

(4) In this Order—

- “**FSMA**” means the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^f00002];
- “[^c-c808a5e99c9e24849abfa0dcf98ec66a]**group**” has the meaning given by section 421 of FSMA;
- [^c-f15aa01a216e8536976746368e7d12c4]...
- “**HSBC UK Bank plc**” means the company incorporated on 23rd December 2015 with[^c-9ec2c4e08df0c23a25a4582eeda24d01] the company registered number 09928412;
- “**Silicon Valley Bank UK Limited**” means the company incorporated on 3rd April 2020 with[^c-7c508646a3ddce4c6b11a3927ab7f834] the company registered number 12546585;
- “[^c-cc458341d77dddc98851392ff2632d3e]**undertaking**” has the meaning given by section 1161 of the Companies Act 2006.

## 2. Modification of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Excluded Activities and Prohibitions) Order 2014[^c-2e674209a89cffc5a9306b32fc65d7e0]

(1) Article 14(4) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Excluded Activities and Prohibitions) Order 2014[^f00004] (prohibitions: financial institution exposures) is to have effect with the modification set out in paragraph (2) in relation to exposures incurred by HSBC UK Bank plc where Silicon Valley Bank UK Limited is the relevant financial institution.

(2) Paragraph (b)(i) is to be read as if the words “conducted on arm’s length terms” were omitted.

## 3. Modification of the FCA’s and PRA’s rule-making powers[^c-6eac7cc7e69fd0a6e299e33db445d533]

(1) Sections 137A(1), 137G(1) and 192XA(1) of FSMA[^f00005] (the FCA’s general rules, the PRA’s general rules and PRA rules applying to holding companies) have effect as if modified by inserting at the end of each provision[^c-fbf03b2a333acbc21db14deab7f8ac36]—

> or for the purposes of, to facilitate, or in consequence of, the Silicon Valley Bank UK Limited Mandatory Reduction and Share Transfer Instrument 2023.

.

(2) Section 138A of FSMA[^f00006] (modification or waiver of rules) is modified so it also[^c-ee644d624ca08e5dbfcd801aca72ab35] applies in relation to HSBC UK Bank plc and any undertaking in its group[^c-98d988d6b04a47b4d3de25e13aa04754]—

- (a) in the absence of an application by HSBC UK Bank plc or the relevant undertaking in its group[^c-5b114b53001ce8c18f5a3027eb4f178b];
- (b) without any requirement for consent by HSBC UK Bank plc or the relevant undertaking in its group[^c-211fc63d8c3cfcd3e70954482374791d];
- (c) without the requirement that the regulator be satisfied about the matters referred to in subsection (4); and
- (d) subject instead to the condition that it appears to the regulator that modification or waiver (as the case may be) is necessary for the purposes of, to facilitate, or in consequence of, the Silicon Valley Bank UK Limited Mandatory Reduction and Share Transfer Instrument 2023.

## 4. Modification of the FCA’s and PRA’s duties to consult on rule changes[^c-852f99a3f7937b72fd93881f92af0a21]

(1) Sections 138I(1)(b) and (2) to (5), 138J(1)(b) and (2) to (5), 138K, 144D and 144E(6) of FSMA[^f00007] (consultation by the FCA and PRA) do not apply in relation to rules made by the FCA or the PRA (as the case may be) if the rules are for the purposes of, to facilitate, or in consequence of, the Silicon Valley Bank UK Limited Mandatory Reduction and Share Transfer Instrument 2023.

(2) Section 139A(5) of FSMA[^f00008] (power of the FCA to give guidance) has effect as if modified by inserting after “(consultation)”—

> as modified by article 4(1) of the Amendments of the Law (Resolution of Silicon Valley Bank UK Limited) Order 2023

.

Amanda Solloway

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order is made for the purpose of enabling Part 1 of the Banking Act 2009 to be used effectively in connection with the sale of Silicon Valley Bank UK Limited to a private sector purchaser.

Article 2 modifies the application of Article 14(4) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Excluded Activities and Prohibitions) Order 2014 ([S.I. 2014/2080](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/2080)) so that the requirement for transactions to take place at arms-length is removed.

Article 3 modifies certain rule-making powers in the Financial Services and Markets Act [2000 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8) so that they are exercisable by the Prudential Regulation Authority and the Financial Conduct Authority in connection with the Silicon Valley Bank UK Limited Mandatory Reduction and Share Transfer Instrument 2023 (“the transfer instrument”).

Article 4 modifies certain duties of the FCA and the PRA to consult on rule changes, where the rules relate to the transfer instrument.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
