# The Environmental Impact Assessment (Miscellaneous Amendments Relating to Harbours, Highways and Transport) Regulations 2017, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Environmental Impact Assessment (Miscellaneous Amendments Relating to Harbours, Highways and Transport) Regulations 2017 and come into force on 5th December 2017.

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) to (6), these Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(3) The amendments made by Schedules 1, 2 and 4 have the same extent as the provisions which they amend.

(4) The amendments made by Schedule 3 extend to England and Wales only.

(5) In Schedule 5—

- (a) Part 1 extends to Northern Ireland only;
- (b) Part 2 extends to Scotland only.

(6) Paragraphs 1 and 4 to 12 of Schedule 6 extend to England and Wales only.
