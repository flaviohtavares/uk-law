# The Environmental Impact Assessment (Miscellaneous Amendments Relating to Harbours, Highways and Transport) Regulations 2017, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “the 1964 Act” means the Harbours Act 1964[^f00005];
- “the 1980 Act” means the Highways Act 1980[^f00006];
- “the 1992 Act” means the Transport and Works Act 1992[^f00007];
- “the 2006 Rules” means the Transport and Works (Applications and Objections Procedure) (England and Wales) Rules 2006[^f00008];
- “the commencement date” means the day on which these Regulations come into force.
