> Text as at 2026-07-16.

## 25. Application of this Part

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part applies to enforcement—

- (a) in Scotland, and
- (b) in respect of offshore installations in the Scottish offshore area (see paragraphs 1 and 4 of Schedule 2).
