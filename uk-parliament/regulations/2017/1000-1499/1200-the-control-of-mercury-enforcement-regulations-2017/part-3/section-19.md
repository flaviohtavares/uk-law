# The Control of Mercury (Enforcement) Regulations 2017, section 19

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Application of this Part and interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies to enforcement by DAERA in Northern Ireland.

(2) In this Part, “**appeals commission**” means the planning appeals commission which continues to be established in accordance with section 203 of the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 [^c23086411].
