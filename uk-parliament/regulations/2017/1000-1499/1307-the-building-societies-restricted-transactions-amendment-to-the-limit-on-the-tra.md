# The Building Societies (Restricted Transactions) (Amendment to the Limit on the Trade in Currencies) Order 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 1307

Building Societies

The Building Societies (Restricted Transactions) (Amendment to the Limit on the Trade in Currencies) Order 2017

Made

19th December 2017

Laid before Parliament

20th December 2017

Coming into force

6th April 2018

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 9A(12)(a) of the Building Societies Act 1986[^f00001], makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Building Societies (Restricted Transactions) (Amendment to the Limit on the Trade in Currencies) Order 2017.

(2) This Order comes into force on 6th April 2018.

## 2. Amendment of section 9A of the Building Societies Act 1986

In subsection (3)(a) of section 9A of the Building Societies Act 1986 for “£100,000” substitute “£3 million”.

Mark Spencer

Andrew Griffiths

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

19th December 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 9A(3)(a) of the Building Societies Act [1986 (c.53)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/53) prohibits a building society, or a subsidiary undertaking of a building society, from trading in currencies where the amount or value of the consideration given by the society or undertaking exceeds £100,000. This Order increases that amount or value to £3 million.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no significant impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
