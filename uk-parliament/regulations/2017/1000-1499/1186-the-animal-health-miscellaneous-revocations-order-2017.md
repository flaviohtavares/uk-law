# The Animal Health (Miscellaneous Revocations) Order 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 1186

Animals, England

The Animal Health (Miscellaneous Revocations) Order 2017

Made

27th November 2017

Coming into force

1st January 2018

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 8(1) and 88(2) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Animal Health (Miscellaneous Revocations) Order 2017 and comes into force on 1st January 2018.

(2) This Order applies to England only.

## 2. Revocations

The following Orders are revoked—

- (a) the Diseases of Animals (Extension of Definitions) Order 1971[^f00003];
- (b) the Disease Control (England) (Amendment) Order 2007[^f00004].

George Eustice

Minister of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

27th November 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes two instruments relating to animal health which are spent or otherwise superseded.

The Diseases of Animals (Extension of Definitions) Order 1971 ([S.I. 1971/531](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1971/531)) which is revoked by article 2(a), applied to Great Britain and is revoked in relation to England.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
