# The Immigration Act 2016 (Commencement No. 6) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 1210 (C. 113)

Immigration

The Immigration Act 2016 (Commencement No. 6) Regulations 2017

Made

4th December 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 94(1) and (2) of the Immigration Act 2016[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration Act 2016 (Commencement No. 6) Regulations 2017.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 1st January 2018

Section 73 of the Immigration Act 2016 (extension to Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland) comes into force on 1st January 2018.

Brandon Lewis

Minister of State

Home Office

4th December 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of these Regulations)

Regulation 2 of these Regulations brings into force section 73 of the Immigration Act 2016 which enables the Secretary of State to make regulations to extend any of the provisions of sections 69 to 72 of that Act to Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland or to make provision which has a similar effect and applies to Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
