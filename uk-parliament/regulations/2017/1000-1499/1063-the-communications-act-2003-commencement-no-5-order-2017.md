# The Communications Act 2003 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 1063 (C. 100)

Electronic Communications

The Communications Act 2003 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2017

Made

1st November 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 411(2) of the Communications Act 2003[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Communications Act 2003 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2017.

## 2. Commencement

Section 401 of the Communications Act 2003 shall come into force on 3rd November 2017.

Karen Bradley

Secretary of State

Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport

1st November 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for section 401 of the Communications Act [2003 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21) to come into force on 3rd November 2017. Section 401, which was amended by section 100 of the Digital Economy Act [2017 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/30), enables Ofcom to publish a statement of principles, and in accordance with that statement, it can retain receipts from certain functions under the Wireless Telegraphy Act [2006 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/36), rather than pay them into the Consolidated Fund.
