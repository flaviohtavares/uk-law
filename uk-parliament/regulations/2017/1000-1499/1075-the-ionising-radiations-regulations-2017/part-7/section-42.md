# The Ionising Radiations Regulations 2017, section 42

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Modifications and revocation

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Schedule 9, which contains modifications to primary legislation and instruments, has effect.

(2) The Ionising Radiations Regulations 1999 [^c23074621] are revoked.
