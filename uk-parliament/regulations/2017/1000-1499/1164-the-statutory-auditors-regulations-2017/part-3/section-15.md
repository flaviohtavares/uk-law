# The Statutory Auditors Regulations 2017, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Amendments to the Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors Regulations 2016

The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors Regulations 2016[^f00024] are amended as follows.

**16.** In regulation 2 (interpretation), in the definition of “audit reporting requirements”—

- (a) in paragraph (b), after “495, ” insert “496, ”;
- (b) in paragraph (d), after “498A” insert “and 503 to 506”;
- (c) in paragraph (f), after “495” insert “, 496”.
