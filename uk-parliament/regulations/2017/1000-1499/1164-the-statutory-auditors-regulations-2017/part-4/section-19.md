# The Statutory Auditors Regulations 2017, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Revocations, repeals and saving

(1) The enactments listed in the table in Schedule 4 (revocations and repeals) are revoked or repealed to the extent specified.

(2) Other than in relation to entries 1 to 4 in the table[^f00026], the revocations and repeals have effect in relation to financial years of qualifying banks beginning on or after 30th November 2018.

(3) In this regulation—

- “financial year” has the meaning given by regulation 2(1) of the Bank Accounts Directive (Miscellaneous Banks) Regulations 2008[^f00027];
- “qualifying bank” has the meaning given by regulations 2(1) and 3 of those Regulations.

## Annotations
