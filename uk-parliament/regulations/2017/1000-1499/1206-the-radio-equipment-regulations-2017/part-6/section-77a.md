# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 77A

## 77A. Transitional provision for common charger radio equipment[^c-9c55f9e636d9f412a7bfc2646348e4a5]

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations prevents the making available on the market of common charger radio equipment which—

- (a) is placed on the market prior to 28th December 2024 or, in relation to laptops, 28th April 2026; and
- (b) is in conformity with the requirements of these Regulations in force in relation to such equipment before such date.
