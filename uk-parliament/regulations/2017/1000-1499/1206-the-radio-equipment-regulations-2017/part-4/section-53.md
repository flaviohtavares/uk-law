# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 53. Register of approved bodies[^M_E_f69d621b-9939-4ae7-d7a3-708c6e7ca342][^c-d6e40bc42648058f214378e3512799f9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State must—

- (a) assign an approved body identification number to each approved body; and
- (b) compile and maintain a register of—
  - (i) approved bodies;
  - (ii) their approved body identification numbers;
  - (iii) the activities for which they have been approved; and
  - (iv) any restrictions on those activities.

(2) The register referred to in paragraph (1) must be made publicly available.

## 53. Operational obligations of notified bodies[^M_E_f132a941-2cb5-4075-9c13-5fa9f1b0c7f1]

*Extent: N.I.*

When a notified body carries out a relevant conformity assessment procedure, Schedule 9 (operational obligations of notified bodies) has effect.
