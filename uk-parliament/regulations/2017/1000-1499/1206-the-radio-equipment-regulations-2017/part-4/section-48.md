# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Presumption of conformity of approved bodies[^M_E_a0d19329-edd7-43e4-cec9-ccee7c72093b][^c-d6e40bc42648058f214378e3512799f9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where a conformity assessment body demonstrates its conformity with the criteria laid down in a designated standard (or part of such standard), the Secretary of State is to presume that the conformity assessment body meets the approved body requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.

## 48. Presumption of conformity of notified bodies[^M_E_81b6f60a-c8f7-44fe-86e9-06ec250923ce]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a conformity assessment body demonstrates its conformity with the criteria laid down in a harmonised standard (or part of such a standard), the reference of which has been published in the Official Journal, the Secretary of State must presume that the conformity assessment body meets the notified body requirements covered by that standard (or part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.
