## Schedule 1C (Extent: N.I.) — Pictograms indicating whether a charging device is or is not included with the common charger radio equipment (Extent: N.I.)[^key-ca1d6e17cb8661608d9b30b7d6a06e6a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 39A(1)(b) *(Extent: N.I.)*

**1 (Extent: N.I.).** The pictogram to be used where a charging device is included with the common charger radio equipment is: *(Extent: N.I.)*

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1206/images/uksi_20171206_en_epp_002.gif)

**2 (Extent: N.I.).** The pictogram to be used where a charging device is not included with the common charger radio equipment is: *(Extent: N.I.)*

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1206/images/uksi_20171206_en_epp_003.gif)

**3 (Extent: N.I.).** The pictogram may vary in appearance (for example as to its colour, solid or hollow, line thickness) and, subject to paragraph 4, it may be reduced or enlarged, but in the event of any variation in appearance the pictogram used must remain visible and legible. *(Extent: N.I.)*

**4 (Extent: N.I.).** If the pictogram is reduced or enlarged, the proportions set out in the relevant drawing at paragraph 1 or 2 must be used and the dimension marked “a” in the relevant drawing must be greater than or equal to 7mm, irrespective of any variation in appearance permitted by paragraph 3. *(Extent: N.I.)*

**5 (Extent: N.I.).** The pictogram must be printed onto the packaging or affixed to the packaging as a sticker. *(Extent: N.I.)*

**6 (Extent: N.I.).** Where the common charger radio equipment is made available by distance selling, the pictogram must be positioned close to the price indication. *(Extent: N.I.)*
