# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Presumption of conformity[^M_E_fe365e25-c65f-4e7c-8c67-766ae9485ddf]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Radio equipment which is in conformity with a designated[^c-77a45292b49b62ee1bdf3fdac9319673] standard (or part of such a standard), [^c-fc4e6f2ea808e7850dbb20f11b5323a4]... must be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.

## 40. Presumption of conformity[^M_E_e56f992f-e7d7-4554-a9e4-db3c5353e1a0]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Radio equipment which is in conformity with a harmonised standard (or part of such a standard), the reference to which has been published in the Official Journal, must be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.
