# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Simplified ... declaration of conformity[^M_E_630bce6c-2939-4316-9b5b-051dd1e4fdd2][^c-ebfa183c8cc231c144d8e4d9cffe830e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where only a simplified [^c-d4086d21fc5b6cdae08d282d746651d1]... declaration of conformity is provided pursuant to regulation 13(3), it must contain the elements specified and have the model structure set out in Schedule 7 (simplified [^c-d4086d21fc5b6cdae08d282d746651d1]... declaration of conformity).

(2) The full text of the [^c-d4086d21fc5b6cdae08d282d746651d1]... declaration of conformity must be made available at the internet address referred to in the simplified [^c-d4086d21fc5b6cdae08d282d746651d1]... declaration of conformity.

## 43. Simplified EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_d6645b43-123e-4546-8963-79979bd509c8]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where only a simplified EU declaration of conformity is provided pursuant to regulation 13(3), it must contain the elements specified and have the model structure set out in Schedule 7 (simplified EU declaration of conformity).

(2) The full text of the EU declaration of conformity must be made available at the internet address referred to in the simplified EU declaration of conformity.
