# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 63. Enforcement action in respect of formal non-compliance[^M_E_ec162b27-fe22-4ba8-82e5-b2f9b381cd9f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where an enforcing authority makes one of the following findings relating to radio equipment, it must require a relevant economic operator to put an end to the non-compliance within such reasonable period as the authority specifies—

- (a) the UK[^c-bf484dcdac1395bad06b5cbae94a5c4d] marking—
  - (i) has not been affixed, or
  - (ii) has been affixed otherwise than in accordance with regulations 39 (prohibition on improper use of UK[^c-7cc3d507dff3cab90df0223ca9f55a69] marking) and 44 (UK marking),
- (b) where the conformity assessment procedure set out in Schedule 4 (full quality assurance) is applied, the identification number of the approved[^c-9a02927f558dd7fe93645290e06fabc4] body—
  - (i) has not been affixed, or
  - (ii) has been affixed otherwise than in accordance with regulation 44 (UK[^c-142a4e99881c61692647680cb843d1c0] marking),
- (c) the [^c-34f34161bdf79b7aab2d1aba4d737a79]... declaration of conformity—
  - (i) has not been drawn up, or
  - (ii) has been drawn up otherwise than in accordance with regulations 10 ([^c-34f34161bdf79b7aab2d1aba4d737a79]... declaration of conformity and UK[^c-37f2edfe763f5e64a4ad319f3631b500] marking) and 42 ([^c-34f34161bdf79b7aab2d1aba4d737a79]... declaration of conformity),
- (d) the technical documentation is either not available or not complete,
- (e) the following information that is required to be included is absent, false or incomplete—
  - (i) the information specified in regulation 12(1) or (2) (identification of the radio equipment and manufacturer), or
  - (ii) the information specified in regulation 23(1) (information identifying importer),
- (f) the information specified in regulation 13(1), (2) or (3) (instructions and information to be included with the radio equipment) is absent,
- (g) the requirements set out in regulation 38 (identification of economic operators) on the identification of economic operators have not been met.

(2) Until the specified period has elapsed, the enforcing authority must not commence proceedings under these Regulations, or take any other enforcement action under these Regulations, against the relevant economic operator in respect of the non-compliance referred to in paragraph (1).

(3) Where the non-compliance referred to in paragraph (1) persists beyond the specified period, the enforcing authority must take appropriate measures to—

- (a) restrict or prohibit corresponding radio equipment being made available on the market,
- (b) ensure that the radio equipment is withdrawn, or
- (c) ensure that the radio equipment is recalled.

(4) This regulation does not apply where radio equipment presents a risk.

## 63. Enforcement action in respect of formal non-compliance[^M_E_022d29bb-fb6a-46a6-f1df-bee9482fe5b8]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where an enforcing authority makes one of the following findings relating to radio equipment, it must require a relevant economic operator to put an end to the non-compliance within such reasonable period as the authority specifies—

- (a) the CE marking—
  - (i) has not been affixed, or
  - (ii) has been affixed otherwise than in accordance with regulations 39 (prohibition on improper use of CE marking) and 44 (CE marking),
- (aa)[^c-2747d2e8899fa0d274b9b27bcfa98de4] where a notified body established in the United Kingdom has been involved in a conformity assessment procedure pursuant to regulation 41—
  - (i) the UK(NI) indication has not been affixed; or
  - (ii) the UK(NI) indication has been affixed other than in accordance with regulation 44A;
- (b) where the conformity assessment procedure set out in Schedule 4 (full quality assurance) is applied, the identification number of the notified body—
  - (i) has not been affixed, or
  - (ii) has been affixed otherwise than in accordance with regulation 44 (CE marking),
- (c) the EU declaration of conformity—
  - (i) has not been drawn up, or
  - (ii) has been drawn up otherwise than in accordance with regulations 10 (EU declaration of conformity and CE marking) and 42 (EU declaration of conformity),
- (d) the technical documentation is either not available or not complete,
- (e) the following information that is required to be included is absent, false or incomplete—
  - (i) the information specified in regulation 12(1) or (2) (identification of the radio equipment and manufacturer), or
  - (ii) the information specified in regulation 23(1) (information identifying importer),
- (f) the information specified in regulation 13(1), (2) , (2A)(a)[^c-224d9812bb5387061b45bf6f67d222b1] or (3) (instructions and information to be included with the radio equipment) or regulation 14(1) to (2A) (information to be included where there are restrictions on putting into service or requirements for authorisation of use)[^c-632d93b52f004f51b208098a490168c2] is absent,
- (fa)[^c-05c4a694c2a6d324a84d7e2dc40f7746] the label required by regulations 13(2A)(b), 24(3)(a) or 31(1A)(a) has not been displayed or supplied, or is not otherwise in accordance with regulation 13(2B) or (2C),
- (g) the requirements set out in regulation 38 (identification of economic operators) on the identification of economic operators have not been met,
- (h)[^c-4dbaa9586bddbe0d487d767d46cf9d63] the requirement set out in regulation 39A(1)(a) that an economic operator must also offer the common charger radio equipment without any charging device, has not been met,
- (i) the pictogram required by regulation 39A(1)(b) is absent, or does not comply with the requirements of regulation 39A(1)(b).

(2) Until the specified period has elapsed, the enforcing authority must not commence proceedings under these Regulations, or take any other enforcement action under these Regulations, against the relevant economic operator in respect of the non-compliance referred to in paragraph (1).

(3) Where the non-compliance referred to in paragraph (1) persists beyond the specified period, the enforcing authority must take appropriate measures to—

- (a) restrict or prohibit corresponding radio equipment being made available on the market,
- (b) ensure that the radio equipment is withdrawn, or
- (c) ensure that the radio equipment is recalled.

(4) This regulation does not apply where radio equipment presents a risk.
