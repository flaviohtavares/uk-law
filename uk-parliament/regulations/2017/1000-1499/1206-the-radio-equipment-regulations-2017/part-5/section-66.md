# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 66

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Penalties[^M_E_c02622dd-b02f-4397-af02-9925183a665b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person guilty of an offence under regulation 65 is liable on summary conviction—

- (a) in England and Wales, to a fine or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or to both,
- (b) in Scotland and Northern Ireland, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or to both.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under regulations 11, 16, 28, 29 and 35[^c-28c95db2a1ec16c4bc04c2c22549abfb] is liable on summary conviction—

- (a) in England and Wales, to a fine,
- (b) in Scotland and Northern Ireland, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.

## 66. Penalties[^M_E_1b900a0d-1613-43cd-a47e-f0b089ff5850]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3)[^c-f3ff97af9e24e4f6e2061c0f773312e9], a person guilty of an offence under regulation 65 is liable on summary conviction—

- (a) in England and Wales, to a fine or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or to both,
- (b) in Scotland and Northern Ireland, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or to both.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under regulations 11, 16, 28, 29 and 35[^V_NI_b8185fdd-fc34-e265-11ee-3d096dfc8c8c] is liable on summary conviction—

- (a) in England and Wales, to a fine,
- (b) in Scotland and Northern Ireland, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.

(3)[^c-4cd6b2fd911a5f661a90c1b519dc1be9] A person guilty of an offence under regulation 65(2) insofar as the requirement relates to a UK(NI) indication is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
