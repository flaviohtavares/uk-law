# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 62. Enforcement action in respect of radio equipment which is in conformity, but presents a risk[^M_E_e037de94-7d2c-4571-fb44-5d7198b2dc03]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where, having carried out an evaluation under regulation 59 (evaluation of radio equipment presenting a risk), an enforcing authority finds that although the radio equipment is in conformity with Part 2, it presents a risk, the enforcing authority must require a relevant economic operator to take appropriate measures to—

- (a) ensure that the radio equipment, when placed on the market, no longer presents a risk,
- (b) withdraw the radio equipment within a prescribed period, or
- (c) recall the radio equipment within a prescribed period.

(2) Where an enforcing authority is not the Secretary of State and it takes measures under paragraph (1), it must notify the Secretary of State immediately.

(3)[^c-b796cbdc7e57e3ccbc8ddf481d64a9ba] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The notification referred to in paragraph (2)[^c-3fbb58f8641458cbd747660939bdc14a] must include details about the radio equipment and, in particular—

- (a) the data necessary for the identification of the radio equipment concerned,
- (b) the origin and the supply chain of the radio equipment,
- (c) the nature of the risk involved, and
- (d) the nature and duration of the measures taken by the enforcing authority.

(5) In this regulation, “**prescribed period**” means a period which is—

- (a) prescribed by the enforcing authority, and
- (b) reasonable and commensurate with the nature of the risk presented by the radio equipment.

## 62. Enforcement action in respect of radio equipment which is in conformity, but presents a risk[^M_E_9db71e13-c852-48aa-8bdc-1665cb31d3bf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where, having carried out an evaluation under regulation 59 (evaluation of radio equipment presenting a risk), an enforcing authority finds that although the radio equipment is in conformity with Part 2, it presents a risk, the enforcing authority must require a relevant economic operator to take appropriate measures to—

- (a) ensure that the radio equipment, when placed on the market, no longer presents a risk,
- (b) withdraw the radio equipment from Northern Ireland[^c-044180e85066201101886655968dce3b] within a prescribed period, or
- (c) recall the radio equipment from Northern Ireland[^c-beeb752a6f025be6d19115a0f4b5c0dc] within a prescribed period.

(2) Where an enforcing authority is not the Secretary of State and it takes measures under paragraph (1), it must notify the Secretary of State immediately.

(3) Where the Secretary of State receives notification under paragraph (2) or takes measures under paragraph (1), the Secretary of State must notify the European Commission and the other relevant states[^c-9c542f56dcd11750dca11c22e7b43791] immediately.

(4) The notifications referred to in paragraphs (2) and (3) must include details about the radio equipment and, in particular—

- (a) the data necessary for the identification of the radio equipment concerned,
- (b) the origin and the supply chain of the radio equipment,
- (c) the nature of the risk involved, and
- (d) the nature and duration of the measures taken by the enforcing authority.

(5) In this regulation, “**prescribed period**” means a period which is—

- (a) prescribed by the enforcing authority, and
- (b) reasonable and commensurate with the nature of the risk presented by the radio equipment.
