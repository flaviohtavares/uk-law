> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Prohibition on placing on the market radio equipment which is not in conformity

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An importer must not place radio equipment on the market unless it is in conformity with the essential requirements.
