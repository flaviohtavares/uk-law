# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Duty to act with due care

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

When making radio equipment available on the market, a distributor must act with due care to ensure that it is in conformity with Part 2.
