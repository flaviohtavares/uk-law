# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Duty to take action in respect of radio equipment placed on the market which is considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_c5ae3e89-2ab7-481c-f3e8-467b03acdbfd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An importer who considers, or has reason to believe, that radio equipment which they have placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring the radio equipment into conformity,
- (b) withdraw the radio equipment, or
- (c) recall the radio equipment.

(2) Where the radio equipment presents a risk, the importer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority [^c-1a50ec79a69d3e09c27a3f0b1ba7ccec]... of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the radio equipment is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2, and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.

## 27. Duty to take action in respect of radio equipment placed on the market which is considered not to be in conformity[^M_E_d7c7a148-52fc-4b3d-f15e-598ac74d3317]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An importer who considers, or has reason to believe, that radio equipment which they have placed on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring the radio equipment into conformity,
- (b) withdraw the radio equipment, or
- (c) recall the radio equipment.

(2) Where the radio equipment presents a risk, the importer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority, and the competent national authorities of any other relevant state[^c-480477dc4b14febbf0730947b4d6183b] in which the importer made the radio equipment available on the market, of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the radio equipment is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2, and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
