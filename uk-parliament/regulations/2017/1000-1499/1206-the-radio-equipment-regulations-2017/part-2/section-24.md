## 24. Instructions and safety information[^M_E_7b40ecd7-e30f-4e98-99ee-4987c0443804]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) When placing radio equipment on the market, an importer must ensure that it is accompanied by instructions and safety information that are clear, legible and in easily understandable English[^c-46038879df8735599c510bac5a04db33].

## 24. Instructions and safety information[^M_E_82532438-35ac-4f81-98d1-c6d79e2c3199]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) When placing radio equipment on the market, an importer must ensure that it is accompanied by instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by consumers and other end-users in the relevant state[^c-b4b190e984b9c5c1a9b4d468b3a40617] in which the radio equipment is to be made available to such consumers and other end-users.

(2) Where the radio equipment is being made available to consumers and other end-users in Northern Ireland[^c-1c6c8124f6f5b32076bb112399c4a654], the language which can be easily understood by consumers and other end-users is English.

(3)[^c-ce0025ea6018f134c1b5a3d87b11f818] When making common charger radio equipment available to consumers or other end-users, an importer must ensure the radio equipment displays or is supplied with a label which is in accordance with regulation 13(2B) and (2C).
