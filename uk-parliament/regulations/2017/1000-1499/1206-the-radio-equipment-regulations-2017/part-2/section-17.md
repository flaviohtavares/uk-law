# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Compliance procedures for series production[^M_E_05d749ba-d529-4e1e-a68a-d5a7b5b76cbd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A manufacturer must ensure, before placing radio equipment on the market, that procedures are in place to ensure that series production remains in conformity with Part 2.

(2) In doing so, the manufacturer must take adequate account of—

- (a) any change in radio equipment design or characteristics, and
- (b) any change in a designated[^c-928e39f0840a238a6ba21b068c84f933] standard or in another technical specification by reference to which the [^c-b94d9400434eca5672e6d87259a2606a]... declaration of conformity was drawn up.

## 17. Compliance procedures for series production[^M_E_1db67a61-c66a-4785-f595-8f535e3f3c14]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A manufacturer must ensure, before placing radio equipment on the market, that procedures are in place to ensure that series production remains in conformity with Part 2.

(2) In doing so, the manufacturer must take adequate account of—

- (a) any change in radio equipment design or characteristics, and
- (b) any change in a harmonised standard or in another technical specification by reference to which the EU declaration of conformity was drawn up.
