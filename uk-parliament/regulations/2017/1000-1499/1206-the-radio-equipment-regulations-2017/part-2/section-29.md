# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 29. Provision of information and cooperation[^M_E_e0f8b081-6721-4b7c-962f-ce780d581835]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Further to a reasoned request from an enforcing authority [^c-25ae91d9d487114ee6c3d2fdb470346b]..., an importer, within such period as the authority may specify, must provide the authority with all the information and documentation necessary to demonstrate that the radio equipment is in conformity with Part 2.

(2) A request referred to in paragraph (1)—

- (a) may only be made during the period of 10 years beginning on the day that the importer places the radio equipment on the market, and
- (b) must be accompanied by the reasons for making the request.

(3) The information referred to in paragraph (1)—

- (a) may be provided in electronic form, and
- (b) must be in a language which can be easily understood by the enforcing authority[^c-8b5f70bd0c25021ad4ef02a16b8b436f].

(4) An importer must, at the request of the enforcing authority or the competent national authority, cooperate with the authority on any action taken to—

- (a) evaluate radio equipment in accordance with regulation 59 (evaluation of radio equipment presenting a risk),
- (b) eliminate the risks posed by radio equipment which the importer has placed on the market.

## 29. Provision of information and cooperation[^M_E_72e17f6d-c052-44d4-f17b-380cd4b94c0f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Further to a reasoned request from an enforcing authority or a competent national authority of another relevant state[^c-32b4dee3157eb64cf98e3b4f214e232c], an importer, within such period as the authority may specify, must provide the authority with all the information and documentation necessary to demonstrate that the radio equipment is in conformity with Part 2.

(2) A request referred to in paragraph (1)—

- (a) may only be made during the period of 10 years beginning on the day that the importer places the radio equipment on the market, and
- (b) must be accompanied by the reasons for making the request.

(3) The information referred to in paragraph (1)—

- (a) may be provided in electronic form, and
- (b) must be in a language which can be easily understood by the authority concerned.

(4) An importer must, at the request of the enforcing authority or the competent national authority, cooperate with the authority on any action taken to—

- (a) evaluate radio equipment in accordance with regulation 59 (evaluation of radio equipment presenting a risk),
- (b) eliminate the risks posed by radio equipment which the importer has placed on the market.
