# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Technical documentation and conformity assessment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before placing radio equipment on the market, a manufacturer must—

- (a) draw up the relevant technical documentation in accordance with regulation 45 (technical documentation), and
- (b) ensure the relevant conformity assessment procedure is carried out.
