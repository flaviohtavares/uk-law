# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Construction must allow operation without infringement of requirements[^M_E_c9184588-306d-4f1a-f338-e0d837781868][^key-30ecce9972602e8a57fbc68a413b6b91]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Before placing radio equipment on the market, a manufacturer must ensure it has been constructed so that the radio equipment can be operated [^key-14c5a6ec2e467ec7f4f32619d1665ec0]... without causing an infringement of the applicable requirements on the use of the radio spectrum [^key-14c5a6ec2e467ec7f4f32619d1665ec0]....

## 8. Construction must allow operation in at least one relevant State[^M_E_0fe9c1bd-b117-4706-f0fa-b5819f9fcecb][^key-97e0acd3ae5ad05f1d514335b9f02533]

*Extent: N.I.*

Before placing radio equipment on the market, a manufacturer must ensure it has been constructed so that the radio equipment can be operated in at least one relevant state[^key-c7861ef387b995b1930f3a63a2eab9e8] without causing an infringement of the applicable requirements on the use of the radio spectrum in the relevant relevant state or relevant states[^key-fed3600b7c9d9c1d7a109923c69c95cd].
