## 37 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note). Translation of declaration of conformity[^c-4030fec9b3f68d4cf0c5f8bdb3e91ee7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4030fec9b3f68d4cf0c5f8bdb3e91ee7] Before placing radio equipment on the market or making radio equipment available on the market, an economic operator must ensure that the EU declaration of conformity and, where appropriate, the simplified EU declaration of conformity is prepared in, or translated into, the language required by the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4030fec9b3f68d4cf0c5f8bdb3e91ee7] relevant state[^c-2456f790a12732f4b5fbd1be5592935a] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4030fec9b3f68d4cf0c5f8bdb3e91ee7] in which it is to be placed on the market or made available on the market. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4030fec9b3f68d4cf0c5f8bdb3e91ee7]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4030fec9b3f68d4cf0c5f8bdb3e91ee7] Where the radio equipment is to be placed on the market or made available on the market in *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4030fec9b3f68d4cf0c5f8bdb3e91ee7] Northern Ireland[^c-e48b680e87d60eb6fea3851dfcc99a5b] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4030fec9b3f68d4cf0c5f8bdb3e91ee7], the language required is English. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4030fec9b3f68d4cf0c5f8bdb3e91ee7]
