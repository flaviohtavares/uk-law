# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Storage and transport

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a distributor has responsibility for radio equipment, the distributor must ensure that the conditions under which it is stored or transported do not jeopardise the radio equipment's conformity with the essential requirements.
