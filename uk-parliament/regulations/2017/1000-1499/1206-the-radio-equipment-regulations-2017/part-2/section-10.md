# The Radio Equipment Regulations 2017, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-01-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1206
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 10. Declaration of conformity and UK marking[^M_E_8de7e497-1da6-48ac-d53e-93202c5ffb6a][^c-37afc87ebf6f42814955822b1de27ba7][^c-b6f4efe53b9a4e8f93e09e635a91d487]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the compliance of radio equipment with the essential requirements has been demonstrated by a relevant conformity assessment procedure, the manufacturer must, before placing the radio equipment on the market—

- (a) draw up a[^c-b761f15f5d5044c656e6439cd9fcc737] declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 42 (declaration of conformity)[^c-bc44e52d8d45748e15be631ccc6427eb], and
- (b) affix the UK[^c-380ec44301c439009c464b1d23a34780] marking in accordance with regulation 44 (UK marking)[^c-1a62ef9eac75520bda3b1a2440c7b0cb].

(2) The manufacturer must keep the [^c-3955d58b6f120c549f3c3543baef007d]... declaration of conformity up to date.

(3)[^c-bc96c3ee539288f0c7d46a3fc7e98875] Where radio equipment is subject to more than one enactment requiring the drawing up of a declaration of conformity, the manufacturer must draw up a single declaration of conformity which identifies each enactment by its title.

## 10. EU declaration of conformity and CE marking[^M_E_ea32ba8f-237f-442d-c265-0aea365b9cae]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the compliance of radio equipment with the essential requirements has been demonstrated by a relevant conformity assessment procedure, the manufacturer must, before placing the radio equipment on the market—

- (a) draw up an EU declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 42 (EU declaration of conformity), and
- (b) affix the CE marking in accordance with regulation 44 (CE marking).

(2) The manufacturer must keep the EU declaration of conformity up to date.

(3) Where radio equipment is subject to more than one NI Protocol obligation[^c-86315f650cba68172646273176b30fa6] requiring an EU declaration of conformity to be drawn up, the manufacturer must draw up a single EU declaration of conformity which—

- (a) meets the requirements of all of the EU instruments concerned,
- (b) identifies the EU instruments, and
- (c) includes references to the publication of those EU instruments in the Official Journal.
