# The Boston Barrier Order 2017, section 29

> Text as at 2023-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Survey of tidal works[^c-5091d97175fbe9beb1d62e97e35ed5cd]

*Extent: E+W*

If the Secretary of State or the Harbour Authority deems it expedient, the Secretary of State or the Harbour Authority may at any time order a survey and examination of a tidal work or of the site upon which it is proposed to construct the tidal work, and any expenditure reasonably incurred by the Secretary of State or the Harbour Authority in relation to any such survey and examination is recoverable from the Agency.
