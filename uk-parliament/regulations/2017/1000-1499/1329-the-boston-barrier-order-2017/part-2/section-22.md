# The Boston Barrier Order 2017, section 22

> Text as at 2023-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Planning permission[^c-19eb4b2ff9e1136a2ab15133625f8915]

*Extent: E+W*

Any planning permission which is deemed by a direction given under section 90(2A) (development with government authorisation)[^f00041] of the 1990 Act to be granted in relation to the authorised works is to be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)(a) (cases in which land is to be treated as operational land) of that Act.
