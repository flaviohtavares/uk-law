# The Boston Barrier Order 2017, section 8

> Text as at 2023-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8. River not to be a reservoir[^key-086cf3a1ea1e01e17a798335f1f5e5c0]

*Extent: E+W*

Regardless of the provisions of section A1 of the Reservoirs Act 1975[^f00033], no part of the river as is impounded by the scheduled works is, by virtue of the retention of water by means of the scheduled works, to be taken to be a reservoir for the purposes of that Act.
