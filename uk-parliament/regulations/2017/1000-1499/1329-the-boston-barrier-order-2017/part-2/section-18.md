## 18. Diversion of flow of water[^key-3c8e221303345efdc88b0fe4df5f75d9]

*Extent: E+W*

The Agency may by means of the authorised works divert, intercept, stop up or otherwise interfere with the waters of, or the flow of water in, the river.
