# The Boston Barrier Order 2017, section 9

> Text as at 2023-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Power to execute street works[^key-df0806160f1327a6571d8ff685ae3af2]

*Extent: E+W*

The Agency may, for the purposes of and to the extent necessary for the construction of the authorised works, enter upon so much of any existing path or street as is within the Order limits and any street abutting the Order limits or which has a junction with such a street and may break up or open the path or street, or any sewer, drain, or tunnel under it, or tunnel or bore under the path or street.
