## 55. Saving for Trinity House[^c-e79fd246060aa269a5f9f00aad659e6a]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in this Order prejudices or derogates from any of the powers, rights or privileges, or the jurisdiction or authority, of Trinity House.
