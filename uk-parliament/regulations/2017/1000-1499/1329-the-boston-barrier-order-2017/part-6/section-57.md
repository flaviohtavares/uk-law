# The Boston Barrier Order 2017, section 57

> Text as at 2023-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 57. Minerals[^c-f294f5d98a3b06cebc17ceb9949803bb]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in this Order affects the rights of any person entitled to any mine or minerals of any description whatsoever under a street or other land in, on, or adjacent to which the authorised works are constructed to work the mine or get the minerals but this does not affect any liability (whether civil or criminal) of the person so entitled in respect of any damage to the authorised works resulting from the exercise of any such rights.
