# The Boston Barrier Order 2017, section 67

> Text as at 2023-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 67. Arbitration[^key-223621cbe1f3ef310659da0b887795bc]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless otherwise provided in this Order, any difference under any provision of this Order is to be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the President of the Institution of Civil Engineers.
