# The Boston Barrier Order 2017, section 68

> Text as at 2023-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 68. No double recovery[^key-05d16286d9d430449b89f83d1936712f]

*Extent: E+W*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law, or under two or more different provisions of this Order.
