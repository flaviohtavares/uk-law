# The Boston Barrier Order 2017, section 45

> Text as at 2023-07-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Misuse of scheduled works, etc.[^c-2ce0b4e3887e275963a1d2f0ec8d173b]

*Extent: E+W*

Any person who without lawful authority or reasonable excuse—

- (a) closes or opens the barrier and/or the flood gates; or
- (b) in any other way interferes with the scheduled works or their operation,

is guilty of an offence and for every such offence is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
