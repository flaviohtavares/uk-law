# The Conservation of Offshore Marine Habitats and Species Regulations 2017, section 47

> Text as at 2026-05-21.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Prohibition of certain methods of capturing or killing wild animals

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to the capturing or killing of a wild animal—

- (a) of any of the species listed in Schedule 3; or
- (b) of a European protected species, where the capture or killing is otherwise permitted in accordance with these Regulations.

(2) Subject to regulation 55, a person is guilty of an offence if that person uses for the purpose of capturing or killing any such wild animal—

- (a)[^c-8f3a27f5c3e2ac7bab20782f1aede55f] any means listed in paragraph 1 or 2 of Schedule 3A;
- (b) any form of capturing or killing from any mode of transport listed in paragraph 3 of Schedule 3A;
- (c) any form of capturing or killing from a ship whilst it is being propelled by a motor; or
- (d) any form of capturing or killing from an aircraft.
