# The Conservation of Offshore Marine Habitats and Species Regulations 2017, section 23

> Text as at 2026-05-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Duty in relation to management schemes

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a management scheme has been established, any competent authority which established that scheme must take reasonable steps to exercise its functions in accordance with that scheme.
