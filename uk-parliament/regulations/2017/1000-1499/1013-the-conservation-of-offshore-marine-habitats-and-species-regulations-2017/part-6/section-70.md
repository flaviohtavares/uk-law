# The Conservation of Offshore Marine Habitats and Species Regulations 2017, section 70

> Text as at 2026-05-21.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Jurisdiction of courts

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Proceedings for prosecuting an offence under these Regulations may be taken, and the offence may for all incidental purposes be treated as having been committed, in any part of the United Kingdom.
