# The Conservation of Offshore Marine Habitats and Species Regulations 2017, section 67

> Text as at 2026-05-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Power to use reasonable force

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A wildlife officer, or a person assisting an officer by virtue of regulation 66, may use reasonable force, if necessary, in the exercise of functions under these Regulations.
