# The Conservation of Offshore Marine Habitats and Species Regulations 2017, section 73

> Text as at 2026-05-21.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Territorial Waters Jurisdiction Act 1878

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 3 of the Territorial Waters Jurisdiction Act 1878 [^c23068331] (consents to prosecutions of offences committed on the open sea by persons who are not British citizens) does not apply to any proceedings for an offence under these Regulations.
