# The Conservation of Offshore Marine Habitats and Species Regulations 2017, section 68

> Text as at 2026-05-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Liability of wildlife officers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A wildlife officer, or a person assisting an officer by virtue of regulation 66, is not to be personally liable in any civil or criminal proceedings for anything done in the purported exercise of functions under these Regulations if the court is satisfied that the act was done in good faith and there were reasonable grounds for doing it.
