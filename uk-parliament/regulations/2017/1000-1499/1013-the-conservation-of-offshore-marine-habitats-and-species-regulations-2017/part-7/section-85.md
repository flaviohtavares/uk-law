> Text as at 2026-05-21.

## 85. Saving

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding the revocation of the Offshore Marine Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 2007, regulation 75 (amendments to the Offshore Petroleum Activities (Conservation of Habitats) Regulations 2001 [^c23068411]) continues to have effect.
