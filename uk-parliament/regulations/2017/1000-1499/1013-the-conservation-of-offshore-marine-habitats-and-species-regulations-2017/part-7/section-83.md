# The Conservation of Offshore Marine Habitats and Species Regulations 2017, section 83

> Text as at 2026-05-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Revocations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following are revoked—

- (a) the Offshore Marine Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 2007 [^c23068371];
- (b) the Offshore Marine Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 [^c23068381];
- (c) the Offshore Marine Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 [^c23068391];
- (d) the Offshore Marine Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 [^c23068401].
