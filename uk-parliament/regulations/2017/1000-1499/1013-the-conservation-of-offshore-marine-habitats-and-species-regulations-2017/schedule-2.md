## SCHEDULE 2 — EXCLUDED POPULATIONS OF CERTAIN SPECIES

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 46(5)(a)

| *Common Name* | *Scientific Name* | *Excluded countries and areas* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beaver, Eurasian | Castor fiber | Estonia, Finland, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, and Sweden |
| Hamster, European (or Black bellied) | Cricetus cricetus | Hungary |
| Wolf, Grey | Canis lupus | Bulgaria, Estonia, Greece north of the 39th parallel, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Slovakia, Spain north of the River Duero, and the reindeer management area in Finland as defined in paragraph 2 of Finnish Act No. 848/90 of 14th September 1990 on reindeer management [^c23068431] |
| Lynx, Eurasian | Lynx lynx | Estonia |
| Viper, Seoane's | Vipera seoanni | Spain |

NOTE. Common names are given in the first column of this Schedule by way of guidance only; in the event of any dispute or proceedings, the common name must not be taken into account.
