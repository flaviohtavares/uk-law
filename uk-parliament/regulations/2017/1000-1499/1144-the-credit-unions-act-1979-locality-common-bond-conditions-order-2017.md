# The Credit Unions Act 1979 (Locality Common Bond Conditions) Order 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 1144

Credit Unions

The Credit Unions Act 1979 (Locality Common Bond Conditions) Order 2017

Made

23rd November 2017

Laid before Parliament

27th November 2017

Coming into force

6th April 2018

The Treasury in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1B(3)(a) and (5) of the Credit Unions Act 1979[^f00001] make the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Credit Unions Act 1979 (Locality Common Bond Conditions) Order 2017 and comes into force on 6th April 2018.

## 2. Amendment to the Credit Unions Act 1979

For the purpose of section 1B(3)(a) (further requirements where common bond relates to locality) the specified figure for the maximum number of potential members of the society is three million.

Heather Wheeler

Mark Spencer

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

23rd November 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases from two to three million the maximum number of potential members of a credit union under section 1(B)(3)(a) of the Credit Unions Act [1979 (c.34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/34) where the common bond for qualification for membership relates to locality.
