# The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017, section 85

## 85. Development consent: review

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The review provisions apply to any order granting development consent under the Planning Act 2008 unless—

- (a) the development to which it related has been completed before the site becomes a European site , a Ramsar site[^c-2720637881fb1075815d242d293ade02] or a European offshore marine site;
- (b) it included a requirement as to the time within which the development to which it related was to be begun and that time has expired without the development having been begun; or
- (c) the development consent was granted for a limited period and that period has expired.

(2) In any such review of an order granting development consent, the competent authority must—

- (a) consider whether any adverse effects could be overcome by imposing requirements under paragraph 5(4)(c), (d) or (e) of Schedule 6 to the Planning Act 2008 (changes to, and revocation of, orders granting development consent); and
- (b) if it considers that those effects could be so overcome, impose those requirements by making such an order under paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 6 to that Act [^c23344181] as may be required.
