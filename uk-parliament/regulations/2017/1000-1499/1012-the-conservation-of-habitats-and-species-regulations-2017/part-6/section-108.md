# The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017, section 108

## 108. Co-ordination for land use plan prepared by more than one authority

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following provisions apply where two or more local planning authorities prepare a relevant joint plan[^c-0e61ed35fe5a5cc4efe6bcd66deda5c4] [^c23344541].

(2) Nothing in paragraph (1) or (1A)[^c-f1143d1dc54c95f4eb3d41f3273b2cc5] of regulation 105 requires a local planning authority to assess any implications of a relevant joint plan[^c-f4a78a739f24cce81f3cf747fe7c4150] which would be more appropriately assessed under that provision by another local planning authority.

(3) The appropriate authority may issue guidance to local planning authorities for the purposes of regulation 105(1) or (1A)[^c-dce1ec09170ea296ec64c373bdb7f5f2] as to the circumstances in which a local planning authority may or should adopt the reasoning or conclusions of another local planning authority as to whether a relevant joint plan[^c-c8b1876a693d0d059265a143975345eb]—

- (a) is likely to have a significant effect on a European site , a Ramsar site[^c-3f9e05c2c1855829e14f2ac082298473] or a European offshore marine site; or
- (b) will adversely affect the integrity of a European site , a Ramsar site[^c-9af28037f4d0c02b4e4a57ee6f032256] or a European offshore marine site.

(4) The local planning authorities concerned must have regard to any such guidance.

(5) In determining whether a relevant joint plan[^c-aa02afa1e54ceb238f281279b87cc72e] should be adopted under regulation 107, a local planning authority must seek and have regard to the views of the other local planning authorities concerned.

(6)[^c-c3949eaf6f66f2c9bedb2513a73969f5] In this regulation “**relevant joint plan**” means—

- (a) a joint spatial development strategy, joint local plan or joint supplementary plan (within the meaning of Part 2 of the 2004 Planning Act),
- (b) a document which is or forms part of a joint minerals and waste plan under sections 15I and 15IA of that Act (as applied by section 15CB(8) of that Act), or
- (c) a joint local development plan under section 72 of that Act.
