# The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1012

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Interpretation of Part 6

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- “**the assessment provisions**” means regulations 63 and 64;
- “[^c-a3391dd9335dac291734d0c09709f5d3]**relevant offshore wind plan or project**” means a relevant offshore wind activity in relation to which the Secretary of State is the appropriate authority for the purposes of making regulations under section 293(1)(b) of the Energy Act 2023;
- “**the review provisions**” means regulations 65 and 66.

(2) In this Part, any reference to—

- (a) the giving or granting of any consent, permission or other authorisation (except in the heading to any regulation or in any reference to any such heading), or
- (b) directing that planning permission is deemed to be granted,

is to be taken, in relation to any consent, permission or authorisation which is capable of being varied or modified, to include a reference to its variation or modification.
