## 86. Interpretation of Chapter 2

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Chapter, except regulations 84 and 85, is to be construed as one with the TCPA 1990.

(2) In regulations 84 and 85, the terms “development” and “development consent” have the meanings given by the Planning Act 2008 [^c23344191].
