## 132. Interpretation of Part 7

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

“**premises**” includes land (including buildings), movable structures, vehicles, vessels, aircraft and other means of transport; a “**species offence**” means an offence under—

- (a) regulation 43 (protection of certain wild animals: offences);
- (b) regulation 45 (prohibition of certain methods of capturing or killing wild animals);
- (c) regulation 47 (protection of certain wild plants: offences); or
- (d) regulation 60 (offence of breaching licence condition).
