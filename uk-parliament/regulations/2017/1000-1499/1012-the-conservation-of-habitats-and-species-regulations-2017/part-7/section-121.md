# The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017, section 121

## 121. Restrictions on taking samples from live specimens

*Extent: E+W*

(1) No sample may be taken by virtue of regulation 119 or 120 from a live animal except by a veterinary surgeon.

(2) No sample may be taken by virtue of those regulations from a live animal or plant unless the person taking it is satisfied on reasonable grounds that taking it will not cause lasting harm to the specimen.
