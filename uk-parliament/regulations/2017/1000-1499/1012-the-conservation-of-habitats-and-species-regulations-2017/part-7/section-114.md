## 114. Wildlife inspectors

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part, “**wildlife inspector**” means a person authorised in writing under this regulation by the appropriate authority (but see regulation 141(6)).

(2) An authorisation under paragraph (1) is subject to any conditions or limitations specified in it.
