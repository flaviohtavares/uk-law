# The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1012
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Relevant licensing body

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of regulations 55, 56 and 57, “**relevant licensing body**” has the meaning given in this regulation.

(2) Subject to paragraph (2A),[^key-7267efd7cdf62c6ca9f7d2ccbf17c733] in the case of a licence granted under regulation 55(1) for a purpose specified in any of paragraph (2)(a) to (d) of that regulation, “**relevant licensing body**”, in relation to England, means—

- (a) so far as the licence relates to the restricted English inshore region, the Marine Management Organisation; and
- (b) otherwise, Natural England.

(2A)[^key-2b11bf37af67a3091b72dc6545922d3b] In the case of a licence granted to Natural England under regulation 55(1) for a purpose specified in any of paragraph (2)(a) to (d) of that regulation, to facilitate the carrying out of any conservation measures, “**relevant licensing body**” means the Secretary of State.

(3) Subject to paragraph (4A),[^key-ec57a87d208b657383013b0671854fce] in the case of a licence granted in relation to England under any of the provisions specified in paragraph (4), “**relevant licensing body**” means the appropriate authority.

(4) The provisions referred to in paragraph (3) are—

- (a) regulation 55(1), where the licence is granted for a purpose specified in any of paragraph (2)(e) to (g) of that regulation;
- (b) regulation 55(4); or
- (c) regulation 56.

(4A)[^key-62bedb6d9a613e1c0e33a26bc529fe97] In the case of a licence that, by virtue of paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 to the Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025, is treated as having been granted to a developer under regulation 55(1) for any of the purposes specified in regulation 55(2)(e) to (g), “**relevant licensing body**” means—

- (a) so far as the licence relates to the restricted English inshore region, the Marine Management Organisation; and
- (b) otherwise, Natural England.

(5) In the case of a licence granted in relation to Wales, “**relevant licensing body**” means the Natural Resources Body for Wales.

(6) In this regulation[^key-cbe6dfebd989c70e1ace9f0cc1b0f02b], “**restricted English inshore region**” means so much of the English inshore region as lies to seaward of mean low water mark;

(7)[^key-16ef3f308e423eec0a3b40bb2a143c00] In paragraph (2A), “**conservation measure**” has the same meaning as in Part 3 of the Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025 (development and nature recovery).
