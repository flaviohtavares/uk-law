## 8. Disapplication of Part 12 of FSMA

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Part 12 of FSMA (control over authorised persons) [^c23090431] does not apply in relation to a person who decides to acquire or increase control, or reduce or cease control, over a transformer vehicle carrying on the activity specified in article 13A of the Regulated Activities Order.
