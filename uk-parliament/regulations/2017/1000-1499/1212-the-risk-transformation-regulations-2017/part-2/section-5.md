# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Amendment of the Regulated Activities Order

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Regulated Activities Order is amended as follows.

(2) After article 12A (information society services and managers of UCITS and AIFs) [^c23090381] insert—

> **12B. Transformer vehicles: insurance risk transformation**
>
> A transformer vehicle [^c23090391] does not carry on an activity of a kind specified by article 10 by assuming a risk from an undertaking, provided the assumption of the risk is a specified kind of activity falling within article 13A (transformer vehicles: insurance risk transformation).

[^c23090401].

(3) After article 13 (application of sections 327 and 332 of FSMA) insert—

> **CHAPTER 3A — Insurance risk transformation**
>
> **13A. Transformer vehicles: insurance risk transformation**
>
> It is a specified kind of activity for a transformer vehicle to assume a risk from an undertaking where—
>
> - (a) the undertaking assumes a risk under a contract of insurance (“the underlying risk”); and
> - (b) the assumption of risk by the transformer vehicle has the legal or economic effect of transferring some or all of the underlying risk to the transformer vehicle.

.

(4) After article 19 (risk-management) [^c23090411] insert—

> **19A. Transformer vehicles: insurance risk transformation**
>
> A transformer vehicle does not carry on an activity of a kind specified by article 14 by assuming a risk from an undertaking, provided the assumption of the risk is a specified kind of activity falling within article 13A (transformer vehicles: insurance risk transformation).

.
