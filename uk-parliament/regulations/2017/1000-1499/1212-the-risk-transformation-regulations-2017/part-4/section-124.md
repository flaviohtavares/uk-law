# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 124

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212

## 124. Meaning of “distribution”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulations 125 and 126, “**distribution**” means every description of a distribution of an asset to a shareholder, except for the redemption or acquisition of shares held by that shareholder in accordance with regulation 106 or 107.
