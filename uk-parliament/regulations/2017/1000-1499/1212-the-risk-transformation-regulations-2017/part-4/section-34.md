# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Delivery of documents to the FCA

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where these Regulations require a person to deliver a document to the FCA to be recorded on the register, the person—

- (a) may deliver the document by electronic means; and
- (b) subject to paragraph (a), must deliver the document in such form as the FCA may from time to time require.
