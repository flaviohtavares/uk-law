# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Company seal: England and Wales, and Northern Ireland

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A protected cell company may have a common seal.

(2) A protected cell company which has a common seal must have its name engraved in legible characters on the seal.

(3) This regulation does not form part of the law of Scotland.
