# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 161

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 161. Validity of allotment etc not affected

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Chapter affects the validity of any allotment or sale of securities or of any agreement to allot or sell securities.
