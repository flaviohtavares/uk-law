# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 146

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 146. Inspection of records

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The records referred to in regulation 145 must be kept available for inspection at the protected cell company's registered office or an alternative inspection location notified to the FCA in accordance with regulation 67.

(2) A person holding voting shares issued on behalf of the core of the protected cell company may—

- (a) inspect the records without charge; and
- (b) require a copy of any of the records on payment of a fee (which may not exceed the administrative cost to the protected cell company of providing the copy).
