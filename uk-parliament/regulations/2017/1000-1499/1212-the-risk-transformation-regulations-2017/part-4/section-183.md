# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 183

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 183. Restoration: when an application may be made

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An application to restore a cell or a protected cell company must be made within a period of six years beginning with the date when the cell or protected cell company (as the case may be) was dissolved.
