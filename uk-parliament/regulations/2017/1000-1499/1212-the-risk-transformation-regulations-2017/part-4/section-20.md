# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212

## 20. Instrument of incorporation: model articles

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A protected cell company's instrument of incorporation may apply (with or without modification) all or any part of the model articles set out in the Companies (Model Articles) Regulations 2008 [^c23090571], except to the extent that it would contravene a requirement of this Part.
