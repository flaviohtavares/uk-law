# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Meaning of “records and accounts”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter, “records and accounts” of a protected cell company means the records and accounts of the protected cell company kept by the protected cell company in accordance with regulation 46.
