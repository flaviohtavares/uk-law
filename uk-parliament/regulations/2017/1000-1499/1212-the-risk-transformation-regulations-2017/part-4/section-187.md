## 187. Jurisdiction and procedure in respect of offences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 403 of FSMA (jurisdiction and procedure in respect of offences) [^c23090861] applies to an offence under these Regulations as it applies to an offence under FSMA.
