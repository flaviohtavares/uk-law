# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 134

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 134. Application

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of this Chapter apply—

- (a) to the persons holding voting shares issued on behalf of the core of the protected cell company; and
- (b) with any necessary modifications, to the persons holding a class of such shares.
