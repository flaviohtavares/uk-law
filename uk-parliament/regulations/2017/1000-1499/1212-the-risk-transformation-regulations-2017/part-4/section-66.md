# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 66

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Official seal for share certificates

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a protected cell company has a common seal.

(2) The protected cell company may have, for use for sealing shares issued by the protected cell company and for sealing documents creating or evidencing such shares, an official seal with a facsimile of its official seal with the addition on its face of the word “securities”.

(3) Such an official seal has the same effect as the company's common seal when affixed to a document.
