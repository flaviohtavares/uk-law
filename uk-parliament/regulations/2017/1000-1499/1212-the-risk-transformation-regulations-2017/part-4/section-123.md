# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 123

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Joint shareholdings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On the death of any one of the joint holders of a share, the survivors are to be the only persons recognised by the protected cell company as having any title to or any interest in those shares.
