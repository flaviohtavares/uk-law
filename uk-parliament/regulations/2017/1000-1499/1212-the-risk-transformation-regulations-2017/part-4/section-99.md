# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Contravention of regulation 91(4) or 93(3)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A provision, whether contained in the instrument of incorporation, a contract or otherwise, is void to the extent that it is inconsistent with regulation 91(4) or 93(3).

(2) An application of assets, or agreement to apply assets, in contravention of regulation 93(3) is void.
