# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 164

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 164. Relationship with regulation 46

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any accounts or records which a protected cell company is required to keep in accordance with the Companies Act regime, as applied by regulation 163, are in addition to the accounts and records that the protected cell company must keep in accordance with regulation 46 (records and accounts of assets, liabilities and obligations).
