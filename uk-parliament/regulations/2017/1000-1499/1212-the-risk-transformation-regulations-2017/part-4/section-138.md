# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 138

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. Calling a shareholders' meeting

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The directors of a protected cell company may call a general meeting.

(2) The persons holding voting shares issued on behalf of the core of a protected cell company may require the directors to call a general meeting.

(3) The directors are required to call a general meeting once the protected cell company has received requests to do so from persons representing at least 5% of the voting shares issued on behalf of the core of the protected cell company.

(4) A request—

- (a) must state the general nature of the business to be dealt with at the meeting; and
- (b) may include the text of a resolution that may properly be moved and is intended to be moved at the meeting.

(5) A request must be authenticated by the person or persons making it.
