# The Risk Transformation Regulations 2017, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/1212
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Offence of carrying on other activities

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A protected cell company that contravenes the prohibition in regulation 57(2) is guilty of an offence and liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or—
  - (i) in England and Wales, a fine, or both;
  - (ii) in Scotland or Northern Ireland, a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or a fine, or both.

(2) In proceedings for an offence under paragraph (1), it is a defence for the accused to show that it took all reasonable precautions and exercised all due diligence to avoid committing the offence.
