# The M20 Junction 10a Development Consent Order 2017, section 22

> Text as at 2019-09-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 22. Compulsory acquisition of land - incorporation of the mineral code[^c-dc4b5dc7a98df959868f2c51dc922985]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00042] are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
