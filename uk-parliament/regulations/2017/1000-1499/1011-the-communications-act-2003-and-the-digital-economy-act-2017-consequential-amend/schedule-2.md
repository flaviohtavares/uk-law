# The Communications Act 2003 and the Digital Economy Act 2017 (Consequential Amendments to Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2017, SCHEDULE 2

## SCHEDULE 2 — AMENDMENT OF ROAD TRAFFIC LEGISLATION

Regulation 2(2)

### PART 1 — PRELIMINARY

#### 1. References to telecommunications apparatus

In the instruments set out in the first column of the Tables in Parts 2 and 3 below, in the provisions set out in the third column, for—

- (a) “telecommunications apparatus as defined in paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 2 of the Telecommunications Act 1984”;
- (b) “telecommunications apparatus as defined in Schedule 2 of the Telecommunications Act 1984”; or
- (c) “telecommunications apparatus as defined in Schedule 2 to the Telecommunications Act 1984”,

as the case may be, substitute “electronic communications apparatus within the meaning given in paragraph 5 of the electronic communications code set out in Schedule 3A to the Communications Act 2003”.

#### 2. A556 (Knutsford to Bowdon Improvement) Development Consent Order 2014

(1) The A556 (Knutsford to Bowdon Improvement) Development Consent Order 2014[^f00035] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 14 (clearways), in paragraph (3)(a)(iii), for “telecommunications apparatus as defined in paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 2 to the Telecommunications Act 1984” substitute “electronic communications apparatus as defined in paragraph 5 of the electronic communications code set out in Schedule 3A to the Communications Act 2003”.

(3) In Schedule 8 (protective provisions), in Part 2 (for the protection of operators of electronic communications code networks)—

- (a) in paragraph 15—
  - (i) omit the definition of “conduit system”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (a) of the definition of “electronic communications code network”, for “conduit system” substitute “infrastructure system”;
  - (iii) in the appropriate place insert—

    > - “infrastructure system” has the same meaning as in the electronic communications code and references to providing an infrastructure system are to be construed in accordance with paragraph 7 of that code;

    ;
- (b) in paragraph 16, for “paragraph 23 of Schedule 2 to the Telecommunications Act 1984” substitute “Part 10 of Schedule 3A to the Communications Act 2003”.
