# The Licensing Act 2003 (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 411

Licences And Licensing

The Licensing Act 2003 (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2017

Made

14th March 2017

Laid before Parliament

16th March 2017

Coming into force

6th April 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25A(1)[^f00001], 37(3)(a), 47(2), 54, 71(5), 91, 100(4) and (5)[^f00002], 104A(2)(a)[^f00003], 106A(4)(a)[^f00004], 107(7), 125(4), 133, 169A(3), 178(1)(b), 193[^f00005] and 197(2) of the Licensing Act 2003[^f00006].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Licensing Act 2003 (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2017 and come into force on 6th April 2017.

## 2. Amendments to the Licensing Act 2003 (Personal licences) Regulations 2005

(1) The Licensing Act 2003 (Personal licences) Regulations 2005[^f00007] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 5(a)—

- (a) after “unique to the licence”, insert “and”;
- (b) omit “and the date of the expiry of the licence”.

(3) In regulation 6[^f00008]—

- (a) in the heading, omit “or renewal”;
- (b) in paragraph (1), omit “or renewal”;
- (c) omit paragraph (2).

(4) In regulation 7(c)[^f00009], substitute—

> - (c) a declaration by the applicant, in the form set out in Schedule 3, that the applicant—
>   - (i) has not been convicted of a relevant offence or a foreign offence,
>   - (ii) has not been required to pay an immigration penalty, or
>   - (iii) has been convicted of such an offence or required to pay such a penalty, accompanied by details of the nature and date of the conviction or penalty and any sentence in respect of such conviction.

.

(5) Omit regulation 8.

(6) For the form in Schedule 1[^f00010], substitute the form in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

(7) Omit Schedule 2[^f00011].

(8) For the form in Schedule 3[^f00012], substitute the form in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

(9) Omit Schedule 4.

## 3. Amendment to the Licensing Act 2003 (Fees) Regulations 2005

(1) The Licensing Act 2003 (Fees) Regulations 2005[^f00013] are amended as follows.

(2) In the table in Schedule 6, in the thirteenth row of Column 1, omit “or renewal”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Licensing Act 2003 (Personal licences) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/41](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/41)) and the Licensing Act 2003 (Fees) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/79](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/79)) so as to remove references to the renewal of personal licences and the accompanying fee. Section 68 of the Deregulation Act [2015 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/20) removes the need for the holders of personal licences to apply to renew them.

These Regulations also amend the Licensing Act 2003 (Premises Licences and Club Premises Certificates) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/42)), the Licensing Act 2003 (Permitted Temporary Activities)(Notices) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/2918](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2918)) and the Licensing Act 2003 (Persistent Selling of Alcohol to Children)(Prescribed Form of Closure Notice) Regulations 2012 ([S.I. 2012/963](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/963)) so as to replace certain forms in those Regulations in order to reflect the following changes in legislation. Section 85 of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act [2012 (c. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/10) removes the limit on the amount of a fine that a magistrates’ court can impose. The Immigration Act [2016 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/19) introduces measures to regulate the granting of personal licences to those convicted of certain immigration offences or who have been required to pay a civil immigration penalty, and to prevent those without an entitlement to live and work in the UK from holding a personal or premises licence. These changes are made by way of amendment to the Licensing Act [2003 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/17). The Legislative Reform (Entertainment Licensing) Order 2014 ([S.I. 2014/3253](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/3253)) also amends the Licensing Act 2003 so that in certain circumstances the provision of regulated entertainment no longer needs to be authorised under the 2003 Act.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no or no significant impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
