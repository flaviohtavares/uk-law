# The Combined Authorities (Mayoral Elections) Order 2017, section 7

> Text as at 2026-06-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Questioning of a CA/CCA mayoral election[^c-dced8a9d1f12bbd39024269fbd2aacf4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of paragraph 12(6) of Schedule 5B to the 2009 Act and paragraph 12(7) of Schedule 2 to the 2023 Act[^c-cfdf42c318e77259f01a680b0e5b3159], Part 3 (legal proceedings) of the 1983 Act shall have effect in relation to the questioning of a CA/CCA mayoral election[^c-95342ffb1151083cd33f7d9271339a82] as it has effect in relation to the questioning of an election under the Local Government Act 1972 [^c22991011].
