# The Combined Authorities (Mayoral Elections) Order 2017, section 2

> Text as at 2026-06-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Order—

- “**the 1983 Act**” means the Representation of the People Act 1983 [^c22990851];
- “**the 2009 Act**” means the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009;
- “[^key-cd9ba9244b405cf384f4c39d2f6162d8]**the 2023 Act**” means the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023;
- “**the Elections Regulations**” means the Representation of the People (England and Wales) Regulations 2001 [^c22990861];
- “the [^key-61c9653023c2a92360c821018c942d4f]... Mayoral Elections Rules” means the rules set out in Schedule 1 or Schedule 3 to this Order as the case may be;
- “**the Referendums Regulations**” means the Local Authorities (Conduct of Referendums) (England) Regulations 2012 [^c22990871] or the Local Authorities (Conduct of Referendums) (Council Tax Increases) (England) Regulations 2012 [^c22990881];
- “**CA/CCA returning officer**” means the combined authority returning officer or, as the case may be, the combined county authority returning officer;
- “**candidate**” means a candidate to be a combined authority mayor or, as the case may be, a combined county authority mayor[^key-b4931013ce4bce3c82ae359acb19978d];
- “**combined authority mayor**”, in relation to the area of a combined authority, means the mayor for the area of the authority by virtue of an order under section 107A(1) [^c22990891] of the 2009 Act;
- “[^key-edda9b2311a8f8f70288ffe43023cc48]**combined authority mayoral election**” means an election for the return of a combined authority mayor;
- “**combined authority returning officer**” means—
  - (a) the person appointed under article 6; or
  - (b) any person appointed under subsection section 35(4) of the 1983 Act by a person of a description mentioned in paragraph (a);
- “**combined county authority mayor**”, in relation to the area of a combined county authority, means the mayor for the area of the authority by virtue of regulations made under section 27(1) of the 2023 Act;
- “**combined county authority mayoral election**” means an election for the return of a combined county authority mayor;
- “**combined county authority returning officer**” means—
  - (a) the person appointed under article 6A, or
  - (b) any person appointed under section 35(4) of the 1983 Act by a person of a description mentioned in paragraph (a);
- “[^key-bed3becb733bf6dca3c9134c0374fdc2]**constituent council**” means—
  - (a) in relation to the area of a combined authority—
    - (i) a county council the whole or any part of whose area is within the area of the combined authority, or
    - (ii) a district council whose area is within the area of the combined authority;
  - (b) in relation to the area of a combined county authority—
    - (i) a county council for an area within the combined county authority’s area, or
    - (ii) a unitary district council for an area within the combined county authority’s area;
- “election” or “**CA/CCA mayoral election**” means an election for the return of a combined authority mayor or, as the case may be, a combined county authority mayor;
- “**electoral area**” means the area for the combined authority or combined county authority[^key-e160d79ea1abacc5d7992917c3d8415a] for which the election is held;
- [^key-105a250821b2a228fe10d7ff3af902b2]...
- “**local government election**” has the same meaning as in section 203(1) [^c22990921] of the 1983 Act;
- “**local government mayoral election**” means an election for the return of an elected mayor in accordance with Part 1A of the Local Government Act 2000 [^c22990931];
- “**police and crime commissioner election**” means an election of a police and crime commissioner in accordance with Chapter 6 of Part 1 of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011 [^c22990941];
- “**proper officer**” has the meaning given by section 270(3) of the Local Government Act 1972 [^c22990951];
- “**referendum**” means a referendum conducted under the Referendums Regulations;
- “**register of local government electors**” means the register maintained under section 9 of the Representation of the People Act 1983 [^c22990961];
- “**relevant election or referendum**” means one or more of the following—
  - (a) a Parliamentary election,
  - (b) [^key-105a250821b2a228fe10d7ff3af902b2]...
  - (c) a local government election,
  - (d) a local government mayoral election,
  - (e) [^key-bb350d4ba2cf05c494a4459910ebf491]...
  - (f) a referendum,
  - (g) a police and crime commissioner election,

  the poll at which is taken together with the poll at the [^key-2ea4919d149d771a640e0e150cdce1bf]... mayoral election;
- “**returning officer**”, in relation to a CA/CCA[^key-09814c17e79925daca74d53f4300217b] mayoral election, means—
  - (a) the person appointed in accordance with article 5, or
  - (b) any person appointed under section 35(4) [^c22990971] of the 1983 Act by a person of a description mentioned in paragraph (a).
