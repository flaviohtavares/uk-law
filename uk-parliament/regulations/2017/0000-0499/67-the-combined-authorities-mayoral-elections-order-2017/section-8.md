# The Combined Authorities (Mayoral Elections) Order 2017, section 8

> Text as at 2026-06-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Free delivery of election addresses

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Each candidate at a CA/CCA[^c-42df9b7060b02a2a85957cde780e927a] mayoral election shall be entitled (subject to and in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 5) to have an election address prepared on behalf of the candidate included in a booklet of election addresses—

- (a) prepared by the CA/CCA returning officer, and
- (b) delivered by that officer to each person entitled to vote at that election.

(2) Candidates' election addresses shall be delivered at the expense of the combined authority or, as the case may be, combined county authority[^c-f8c908602c339cdc76b7c87b22ce5eaf].

(3) Schedule 5 (which makes provisions supplementing paragraph (1) above) shall have effect.
