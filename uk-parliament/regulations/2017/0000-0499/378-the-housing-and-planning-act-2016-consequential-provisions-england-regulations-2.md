# The Housing and Planning Act 2016 (Consequential Provisions) (England) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 378

Housing, England

The Housing and Planning Act 2016 (Consequential Provisions) (England) Regulations 2017

Made

13th March 2017

Laid before Parliament

15th March 2017

Coming into force

6th April 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 213 of the Housing and Planning Act 2016[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Housing and Planning Act 2016 (Consequential Provisions) (England) Regulations 2017 and come into force on 6th April 2017.

(2) These Regulations apply to the disposal of property in England only.

## 2. Amendment of the Land Registration Rules 2003: Rule 183A

In rule 183A of the Land Registration Rules 2003[^f00002], omit paragraph (1A).

## 3. Amendment of the Land Registration Rules 2003: Schedule 4

(1) Schedule 4 of the Land Registration Rules 2003[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In form W—

- (a) omit (a);
- (b) in (b) for “any other” substitute “a”; and
- (c) before “to that disposition” insert “where consent”; and
- (d) for “under” substitute “is required by”.

(3) In form X—

- (a) omit (a);
- (b) in (b) for “any other” substitute “a”;
- (c) before “to that disposition” insert “where consent”; and
- (d) for “under” substitute “is required by”.

(4) In form Y omit—

- (a) “*or private registered provider of social housing*”; and
- (b) “(as modified, in the case of a private registered provider of social housing, by section 179 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008)”[^f00004].

(5) In form KK—

- (a) after “lease”, insert “of property in Wales”;
- (b) omit “*\[choose whichever bulleted clause is appropriate\]*”; and
- (c) omit “the Regulator of Social Housing”.

Gavin Barwell

Minister of State

13th March 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Land Registration Rules 2003 ([S.I. 2003/1417](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/1417)) as a result of the repeal of the consent regime made by Schedule 4 to the Housing and Planning Act [2016 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/22).

Regulation 2 revokes paragraph (1A) of rule 183A, which requires a private registered provider of social housing who applies for registration as the proprietor or a registered estate or registered charge to include a statement to that effect, and regulation 3 makes amendments to the relevant obligatory forms of restriction set out in Schedule 4 to the Land Registration Rules 2003.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
