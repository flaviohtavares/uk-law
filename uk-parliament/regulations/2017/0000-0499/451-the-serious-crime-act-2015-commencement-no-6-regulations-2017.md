# The Serious Crime Act 2015 (Commencement No. 6) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 451 (C. 39)

Criminal Law, England And Wales

The Serious Crime Act 2015 (Commencement No. 6) Regulations 2017

Made

20th March 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 88(1) of the Serious Crime Act 2015[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Serious Crime Act 2015 (Commencement No. 6) Regulations 2017.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 3rd April 2017

Section 67 (sexual communication with a child) of the Serious Crime Act 2015 comes into force on 3rd April 2017.

Elizabeth Truss

Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

20th March 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are the sixth commencement regulations made by the Secretary of State under section 88(1) of the Serious Crime Act [2015 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9). Regulation 2 commences section 67 of that Act, which inserts a new section 15A into the Sexual Offences Act [2003 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42) containing the offence of sexual communication with a child.
