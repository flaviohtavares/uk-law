# The Deregulation Act 2015 (Birmingham City Council Act 1985) (Repeal) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

13th March 2017

Laid before Parliament

17th March 2017

Coming into force

10th April 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 75(6) of the Deregulation Act 2015[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Deregulation Act 2015 (Birmingham City Council Act 1985) (Repeal) Regulations 2017 and come into force on 10th April 2017.

## 2. Repeal of the Birmingham City Council Act 1985

The Birmingham City Council Act 1985[^f00002] is repealed.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Andrew Jones

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

13th March 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations repeal the Birmingham City Council Act 1985 and are made under provision in the Deregulation Act 2015 (“the 2015 Act”). That provision in the 2015 Act empowers the Secretary of State to repeal, by statutory instrument, any local Act passed before the 2015 Act which made provision for authorising races or trials of speed between motor vehicles on highways in England and Wales. The Birmingham City Council Act 1985 is one such Act.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no significant impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
