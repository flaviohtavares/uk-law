# The St Edmundsbury (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2017/118](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/118) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2017 No. 163

Local Government, England

The St Edmundsbury (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2017

Made

16th February 2017

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

The Local Government Boundary Commission for England, in exercise of the power conferred by section 92(3) of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007[^f00001] makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the St Edmundsbury (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2017.

(2) This article comes into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

## 2. Amendment of the St Edmundsbury (Electoral Changes) Order 2017

(1) The St Edmundsbury (Electoral Changes) Order 2017[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 3(2) (Related alterations of electoral division boundaries), replace “Cambridgeshire (Electoral Changes) Order 2016” with “County of Suffolk (Electoral Changes) Order 2004”.

(3) In footnote (b) on page 2, replace “[S.I. 2016/1222](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/1222)” with “[S.I. 2004/3252](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3252)”.

Sealed with the seal of the Local Government Boundary Commission for England

Jolyon Jackson

Chief Executive

Local Government Boundary Commission for England

16th February 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends article 3 on page 1 and footnote (b) on page 2 of the St Edmundsbury (Electoral Changes) Order 2017 to give full effect to the proposals made by St Edmundsbury Borough Council to the Local Government Boundary Commission for England for related alternations to county electoral division boundaries. The alterations are related to parish boundary changes made by the St Edmundsbury (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order 2016.

It should also be noted that, in the explanatory note to the St Edmundsbury (Electoral Changes) Order 2017, reference was made to “electoral divisions in the county of Cambridgeshire”. This should have read “electoral divisions in the County of Suffolk”.
