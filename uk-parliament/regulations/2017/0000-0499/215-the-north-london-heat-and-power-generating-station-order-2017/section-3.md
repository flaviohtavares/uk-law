## 3. Development consent etc. granted by Order[^key-5836136c6f36c097fde23d56dc1c9af7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to the provisions of this Order, the undertaker is granted development consent for the authorised development described in Schedule 1 (authorised development) to be constructed, operated and maintained within the Order limits.
