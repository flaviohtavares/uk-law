# The North London Heat and Power Generating Station Order 2017, section 35

> Text as at 2018-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. No double recovery[^c-fa42137220164066090e5983bff29147]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law.
