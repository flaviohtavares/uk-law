# The North London Heat and Power Generating Station Order 2017, section 34

> Text as at 2018-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. Arbitration[^c-cf93d2e33dcb0563849e366200aad449]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any difference or dispute under any provision of this Order, unless otherwise provided for in this Order or unless otherwise agreed between the parties, is to be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the Secretary of State.
