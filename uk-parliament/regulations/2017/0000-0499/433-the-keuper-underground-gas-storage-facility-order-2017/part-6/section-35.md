# The Keuper Underground Gas Storage Facility Order 2017, section 35

> Text as at 2024-05-16.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Certification of plans etc.[^key-a85ae1a4553469368bdb3d297e70d7b2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker must, as soon as reasonably practicable after the making of the Order, submit to the Secretary of State copies of–

- (a) the book of reference (document ref: 4.3);
- (b) the book of reference and land plans clarifications and errata (document ref:10.14);
- (c) the Order limits drawing nos: 13-03-01/HOL/24/100-107/B1;
- (d) the land plans drawing nos: 13-03-01/HOL/24/610-617/B1;
- (e) the works plans drawing nos: 13-03-01/HOL/24/500-503/B1, 13-03-01/HOL/24/504/B2, 13-03-01/HOL/24/505-506/B1[^key-51fef29538ee85e36936972ee4bd4998], 13-03-01/HOL/24/509/B1, 13-03-01/HOL/24/510/B3[^key-7992d13dd44f7e499d6793a18bd216cb], 13-03-01/HOL/24/511/B1, 13-03-01/HOL/24/512/B2 and 13-03-01/HOL/24/513-514/B1;
- (f) the street works and access plan drawing no.:13-03-01/HOL/24/413/B1;
- (g) the environmental statement (document refs: 6.1-6.3);
- (h) the environmental statement clarifications and errata (document ref: 10.13);
- (i) the elevation drawing nos::
  - (i) 13-03-01/HOL/24/236/B1;
  - (ii) 13-03-01/HOL/24/270/B5[^key-02311deeac14301403560ca9df7e42ab], 13-03-01/HOL/24/271/B2, 13-03-01/HOL/24/272/B1, 13-03-01/HOL/24//273/B2 and 13-03-01/HOL/24/274/B2[^key-a3917b6ec920b9bc4a4e31fe40e18bda]; and
  - (iii) 13-03-01/HOL/24/278/B1;
- (j) the seismic survey report (document ref: 9.1) Revision A[^key-068e15b5b664a45851f618ae2386c962];
- (k) the sub-surface safety assessment report (document ref: 9.2) Revision B[^key-8c228d006b06051877a2d4df9bab91f6];
- (l) the preliminary study of gas design capacity (document ref: 9.3) Revision B[^key-5a2e582dbdf2ca2f405c650ce453c0b4];
- (m) the landscaping plans drawing nos: 13-03-01/HOL/24/240-262/B1, 13-03-01/HOL/24/263-264/B2[^key-cb5c97d9a09f144607d75f76f0ec5cd0] and 13-03-01/HOL/24/266/B2 and 13-03-01/HOL/24/267-268/B1[^key-b942fa863fcc7912168effc16ee2f949];
- (n) the statutory undertakers’ apparatus plan drawing no: 13-03-01/HOL/24/346/B1;
- (o) the routing plan drawing no: 13-03-01/HOL/24/405/B1; and
- (p) the draft CEMP and annexes 1 - 4 (document ref 6.5 rev4, August 2016),

for certification that they are true copies of the documents referred to in this Order.

(2) A plan or document so certified under paragraph (1) is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
