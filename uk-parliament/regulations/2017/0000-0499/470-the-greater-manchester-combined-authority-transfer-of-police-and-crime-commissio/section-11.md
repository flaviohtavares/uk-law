# The Greater Manchester Combined Authority (Transfer of Police and Crime Commissioner Functions to the Mayor) Order 2017, section 11

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Modifications to the Local Government Act 1972[^c-5404a64a9ab7e46b64dbfb0a7275213c]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 86 of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00018] applies in relation to the mayor with the following modification.

(1) After section 86(1)(c), insert—

> - (d) ceases to be the mayor by virtue of section 63 of the 2011 Act;

.
