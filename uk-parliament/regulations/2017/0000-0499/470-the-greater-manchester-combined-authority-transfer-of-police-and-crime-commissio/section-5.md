# The Greater Manchester Combined Authority (Transfer of Police and Crime Commissioner Functions to the Mayor) Order 2017, section 5

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Secondments[^key-14791eeb1a73d01c8c8f4596e30086cd]

*Extent: E+W*

In the case of a person who, immediately before 8th May 2017 is seconded to the police and crime commissioner, the secondment is to have effect, after that time, as a secondment to the combined authority.
