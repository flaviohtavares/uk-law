# The Brechfa Forest Wind Farm Connection (Correction) Order 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 343

Infrastructure Planning

The Brechfa Forest Wind Farm Connection (Correction) Order 2017

Made

9th March 2017

Coming into force

10th March 2017

The Brechfa Forest Wind Farm Connection Order 2016 (the “Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received, before the end of the relevant period referred to in paragraph 1(6)(a) of that Schedule, a written request from Western Power Distribution (South Wales) Plc to correct errors in the Order. In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of that Schedule, the Secretary of State informed Carmarthenshire County Council that the request had been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Brechfa Forest Wind Farm Connection (Correction) Order 2017 and comes into force on 10th March 2017.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects errors in the Brechfa Forest Wind Farm Connection Order 2016, which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a written request from Western Power Distribution (South Wales) Plc under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
