# The Cattle Identification (Amendment) Regulations 2017

The Secretary of State is a minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in respect of matters relating to measures in the veterinary and phytosanitary fields for the protection of public health.

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Cattle Identification (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st March 2017.

## 2. Amendment of the Cattle Identification Regulations 2007

The Cattle Identification Regulations 2007[^f00003] are amended in accordance with the following provisions.

## 3. Amendment of regulation 2

In regulation 2, in the definition of “cattle passport”, after “England, Wales or Scotland under Article 6(1)” insert “or 6a”.

## 4. Amendment of regulation 10

In regulation 10—

- (a) in paragraph (2)—
  - (i) the words from “only includes” to the end become sub-paragraph (a);
  - (ii) at the end insert—

    > ; and
    >
    > - (b) includes a power to enter premises by reasonable force if need be

    ; and
- (b) in paragraph (3) for “all checks and examinations” substitute “any actions”.

## 5. Amendment of regulation 11

In regulation 11, for “the second paragraph of Article 22(1)” substitute “Article 22(2)”.

## 6. Amendment of regulation 12

In regulation 12[^f00004], after “No 494/98” insert “and Article 22(2)(d) of Regulation [(EC) No 1760/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1760)”.

  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (4), for “requirement in Article 4(1)” substitute “requirements in Articles 4 and 4a”;

## 8. Amendment of Schedule 2

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make amendments to the Cattle Identification Regulations 2007 ([S.I. 2007/529](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/529)).

These amendments are consequential to the amendments made by Regulation (EU) No 653/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council to Regulation [(EC) No 1760/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1760) as regards electronic identification of bovine animals and labelling of beef.

The amendments to Regulation 10 clarify the powers of inspectors, including extending the power to enter premises to include the use of reasonable force if necessary.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
