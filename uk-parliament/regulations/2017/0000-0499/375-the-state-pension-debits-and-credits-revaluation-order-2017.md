# The State Pension Debits and Credits (Revaluation) Order 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 375

Social Security

The State Pension Debits and Credits (Revaluation) Order 2017

Made

13th March 2017

Laid before Parliament

15th March 2017

Coming into force

10th April 2017

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions has reviewed the general level of prices in Great Britain in accordance with section 148AD(1) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00001].

It appears to the Secretary of State that relevant debits or credits[^f00002] have not, during the review period, maintained their value in relation to the general level of prices.

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 148AD(2) and (3) and 189(4) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the State Pension Debits and Credits (Revaluation) Order 2017 and comes into force on 10th April 2017.

## 2. The increase in the amount of relevant debits or credits

For the purposes of paragraph 3 of each of Schedules 8 and 10 to the Pensions Act 2014[^f00004] (pension sharing: appropriate weekly rate and reduction), the amount of relevant debits or credits for the tax year specified in the first column of the table set out in the Schedule to this Order is to be increased by the percentage of their amount specified by the corresponding entry in the second column.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Richard Harrington

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

| --- | --- |
| 2016-2017 | 1.0 |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made following a review under section 148AD(1) of the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5).

Article 2 of this Order revalues credits and debits under section 49A(2) of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act [1999 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/30) by increasing them by the specified percentage increase that corresponds to the tax year in which the person became entitled to the credit or subject to the debit. The specified percentage increase is also used as the “appropriate revaluation percentage” to determine the appropriate weekly rate of a person’s state pension under section 13 of the Pensions Act [2014 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/19) in accordance with paragraph 3 of each of Schedules 8 and 10 to that Act.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as it has no impact on business or civil society organisations.
