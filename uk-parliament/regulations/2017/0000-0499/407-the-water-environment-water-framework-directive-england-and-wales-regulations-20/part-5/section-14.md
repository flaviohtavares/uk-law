# The Water Environment (Water Framework Directive) (England and Wales) Regulations 2017, section 14

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Environmental objectives: application of regulations 15 to 19

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 15 to 19 must be applied in a way that—

- (a) does not permanently exclude or compromise the achievement of the environmental objectives set in relation to any other water body within the same river basin district;
- (b) is not inconsistent with any other[^c-86fcd5c92911b4803c6a84b167aa464c] assimilated[^c-1675269fa51525e84029cb084405d65a] law;
- (c) guarantees at least the same level of protection for bodies of water as the EU instruments repealed by Article 22 of the WFD.
