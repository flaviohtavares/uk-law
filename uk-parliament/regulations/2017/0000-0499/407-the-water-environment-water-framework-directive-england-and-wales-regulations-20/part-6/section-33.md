# The Water Environment (Water Framework Directive) (England and Wales) Regulations 2017, section 33

> Text as at 2025-02-27.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. River basin management plans: duties on public bodies

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State, the Welsh Ministers, the Agency, NRW and each public body must, in exercising their functions so far as affecting a river basin district, have regard to—

- (a) the river basin management plan for that district as approved under regulation 31, and
- (b) any supplementary plan prepared under regulation 32.
