# The Water Environment (Water Framework Directive) (England and Wales) Regulations 2017, section 28

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Review of river basin management plans

*Extent: E+W*

The appropriate agency must, by such date as the appropriate authority directs—

- (a) review and update each river basin management plan, and
- (b) submit each updated plan to the appropriate authority.
