# The Infrastructure Act 2015 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 315 (C. 30)

Infrastructure Planning

The Infrastructure Act 2015 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2017

Made

8th March 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 57(5)(a) of the Infrastructure Act 2015[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Infrastructure Act 2015 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2017.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 5th April 2017

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 27 (Two-person Panels) of the Infrastructure Act 2015 is 5th April 2017.

Gavin Barwell

Minister of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

8th March 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force section 27 of the Infrastructure Act [2015 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/7).
