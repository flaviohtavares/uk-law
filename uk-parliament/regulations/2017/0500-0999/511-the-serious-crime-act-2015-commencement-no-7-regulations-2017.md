# The Serious Crime Act 2015 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 511 (C. 47)

Criminal Law, England And Wales

The Serious Crime Act 2015 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2017

Made

30th March 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 88(1) of the Serious Crime Act 2015[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Serious Crime Act 2015 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2017.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 3rd April 2017

The following provisions of the Serious Crime Act 2015 come into force on 3rd April 2017—

- (a) section 85(1) (minor and consequential amendments), for the purposes of the provisions listed in sub-paragraph (b) of this regulation; and
- (b) in Schedule 4 (minor and consequential amendments)—
  - (i) paragraph 63;
  - (ii) paragraph 66(2);
  - (iii) paragraph 68(2); and
  - (iv) paragraph 70(2).

Phillip Lee

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

30th March 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are the seventh commencement regulations made by the Secretary of State under section 88(1) of the Serious Crime Act [2015 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9) (“the 2015 Act”). Regulation 2 brings into force on 3 April 2017 the consequential provisions in Schedule 4 to the 2015 Act relevant to the commencement of section 67 of that Act by [SI 2017/451](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/451).
