# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/701
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Right to refer matters to the Tribunal

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the appropriate regulator requires an investment firm, credit institution, or recognised investment exchange to remove a person from the management board under regulation 38—

- (a) the firm, credit institution, or exchange may refer the matter to the Tribunal; and
- (b) the person to whom the requirement relates may refer the matter to the Tribunal.
