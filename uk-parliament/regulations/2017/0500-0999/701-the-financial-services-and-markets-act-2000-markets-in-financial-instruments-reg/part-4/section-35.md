# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/701
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Synchronisation of business clocks

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A member of, or participant in, a trading venue (“M”) must comply with the requirement set out in paragraph (2) if—

- (a) M is established in[^c-624d0ce7d1dd9d12108205a2dbb45378] the United Kingdom;
- (b)[^c-fe9e9df06a224a61b6f2fa33dc53bfc1] M falls within paragraph (1A) of regulation 30; and
- (c) M does not have a Part 4A permission to carry on a regulated activity which is any of the investment services and activities[^c-4fcd9ccd97b1e95580f72a054c67e829].

(2) M must synchronise the business clock M uses to record the date and time of any reportable event with the business clock the trading venue uses for that purpose to the level of accuracy specified in Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/574 of 7 June 2016 supplementing Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to regulatory technical standards for the level of accuracy of business clocks.[^c-592fd43f37d96784119b81ff063eae3d]
