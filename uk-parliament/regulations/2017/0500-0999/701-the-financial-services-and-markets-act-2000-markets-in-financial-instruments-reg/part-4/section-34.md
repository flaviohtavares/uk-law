# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/701
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Acting as a general clearing member

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A member of, or participant in, a regulated market or multilateral trading facility that acts as a general clearing member for other persons (“M”) must comply with the requirements set out in paragraph (2) if—

- (a) M is established in[^key-bdcf0eceb444d46e5f94c7d5c60dd32b] the United Kingdom;
- (b)[^key-2c6653cc705e19ff2af1f7b5a198346d] M falls within paragraph (1A) of regulation 30; and
- (c) M does not have a Part 4A permission to carry on a regulated activity which is any of the investment services and activities[^key-6332737b85be93a21c79d949675d3026].

(2) M must have in place effective systems and controls to ensure—

- (a) M's clearing services are only provided to persons who —
  - (i) are suitable recipients of those services; and
  - (ii) meet clear criteria applied by those systems and controls regarding which persons are suitable to receive clearing services; and
- (b) requirements are imposed on the persons to whom clearing services are being provided to reduce risks to M and to the market.

(3) M must have a binding written agreement with any person to whom they are providing clearing services detailing the rights and obligations of both parties arising from the provision of the service.

(4) In this regulation “**clearing services**” means the services provided by M in the course of acting as a general clearing member for other persons.
