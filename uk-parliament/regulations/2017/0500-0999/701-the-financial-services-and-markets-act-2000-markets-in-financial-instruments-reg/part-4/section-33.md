## 33. Provision of information to the FCA concerning direct electronic access

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA may require a member of, or participant in, a regulated market or multilateral trading facility subject to the requirements set out in regulation 32 (“M”) to provide on a regular or ad hoc basis—

- (a) a description of the systems and controls[^c-cb9e5bc211fb9bda4433d39f07a553af] mentioned in regulation 32(4)[^c-78b98e6fb6e07a0bab6ee55f15834a1c];
- (b) evidence that those systems and controls[^c-1dcace56882fdfcdc47f6145505ddf88] have been applied; and
- (c) the information stored in accordance with regulation 32(9) .
