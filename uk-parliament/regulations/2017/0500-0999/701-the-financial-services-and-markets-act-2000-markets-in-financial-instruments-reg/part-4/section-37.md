# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/701

## 37. Interpretation of Part 4[^c-8344de2da7772ff6396fc373af970a5f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part, a person is established in the United Kingdom if the person has its registered office, or (if it has no registered office), its head office, in the United Kingdom.
