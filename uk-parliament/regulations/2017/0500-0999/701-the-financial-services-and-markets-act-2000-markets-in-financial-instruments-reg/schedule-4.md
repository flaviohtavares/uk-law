# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017, SCHEDULE 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/701
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4 — Amendments to other primary legislation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 50(3)

### 1. Building Societies Act 1986

In section 81B(1) of the Building Societies Act 1986 [^c23019721] (interpretation of Part 8), in the definition of “regulated market”, for the words from “Article” to “2004” substitute “ Article 4.1.21 of Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”.

### 2. Finance Act 1991

In section 116(4)(aa) of the Finance Act 1991 [^c23019731] (investment exchanges and clearing houses: stamp duty), for the words from “Directive” to “2004” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”.

### 3. Friendly Societies Act 1992

(1) The Friendly Societies Act 1992 [^c23019741] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 69A(4) [^c23019751] (duty to prepare individual accounts), for the words from “Directive” to “2004” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”.

(3) In section 69E(5) [^c23019761] (duty to prepare group accounts), for the words from “Directive” to “2004” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”.

### 4. Data Protection Act 1998

In paragraph 6(3) of Schedule 7 (miscellaneous exemptions) to the Data Protection Act 1998 [^c23019771], in the definition of “instrument”, for the words from “Directive” to “2004” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”.

### 5. Competition Act 1998

In paragraph 3(5) of Schedule 3 (general exclusions) to the Competition Act 1998 [^c23019781] in paragraph (a) of the definition of “EEA regulated market”, for the words from “Article” to “2004” substitute “ Article 56 of Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”.

### 6. Terrorism Act 2000

(1) Schedule 3A to the Terrorism Act 2000 [^c23019791] (regulated sector and supervisory authorities) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1(5)(a) [^c23019801] for “point 14” substitute “ point 21 ”.

(3) In paragraph 3(1) [^c23019811], in the definition of “the Markets in Financial Instruments Directive”, for the words from “[2004/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0036)” to “2004” substitute “ 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”.

### 7. Proceeds of Crime Act 2002

(1) Schedule 9 to the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 [^c23019821] (regulated sector and supervisory authorities) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1(5)(a) [^c23019831] for “point 14” substitute “ point 21 ”.

(3) In paragraph 3(1) [^c23019841], in the definition of “the Markets in Financial Instruments Directive”, for the words from “[2004/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0039)” to “2004” substitute “ 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”.

### 8. Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005

In section 381E(3) of the Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005 [^c23019851] (exception for returns from certain shares) for the words from “Directive” to “4.1(14))” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on markets in financial instruments (see Article 4.1.21) ”.

### 9. Companies Act 2006

(1) The Companies Act 2006 [^c23019861] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 474(1) [^c23019871] (minor definitions for Part 15), in the definition of “MiFID investment firm”—

- (a) for the words from “Directive” to “2004” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”;
- (b)[^key-4a7818de617d21397079f738ec22deb3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^key-9ba9df929671badba52a59819979d230] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In section 494A [^c23019881] (interpretation), in the definition of “transferable securities”, for “Directive [2004/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/39)” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU ”.

(4) In section 519A(2) [^c23019891] (meaning of “public interest company”, “non-public interest company” and “exempt reasons”), for “Directive [2004/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/39)” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU ”.

(5) In section 539 [^c23019901] (minor definitions), in the definition of “MiFID investment firm”—

- (a) for the words from “Directive” to “2004” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”;
- (b) at the end of paragraph (a) insert “ and ”; and
- (c) for paragraphs (b) and (c) substitute—

  > - (b) a company which is an exempt investment firm as defined by regulation 8 (Meaning of “exempt investment firm” in Chapter 1) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017 (S.I. 2017/XXXX);

  .

(6) In section 853E(6) [^c23019911] (duty to notify trading status of shares) for the definition of “relevant market” substitute—

> “**relevant market**” means—
>
> - (a) a recognised investment exchange, as defined in section 285(1)(a) (exemption for recognised exemption exchanges and clearance houses) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (“**the Act**”); and
> - (b) any other market which is a regulated market,
>
> but not an overseas investment exchange, as defined by section 313 (interpretation of Part 18) of the Act..

.

(7) In section 1173 (minor definitions: general)—

- (a) in subsection (1) [^c23019921]—
  - (i) in the definition of “regulated market” for the words from “Directive” to “4.1(14))” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on markets in financial instruments (see Article 4.1.21)) ”;
  - (ii) in the definition of “transferable securities”, for “Directive [2004/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/39)” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU ”; and
- (b) in subsection (2), in the definition of “regulated market”, for the words from “Council” to “field” substitute “ Directive [2004/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/39) of the European Parliament and of the Council on markets in financial instruments ”.

(8) In section 1241(3) (meaning of “**registered third country auditor**” and “**UK-traded non-EEA company**”)—

- (a) in the definition of “regulated market”, for “Article 4.1(14) of Directive [2004/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/39)” substitute “ Article 4.1.21 of Directive 2014/65/EU ”; and
- (b) in the definition of “transferable securities”, for “Article 4.1(18)” substitute “ Article 4.1.44 ”.

(9) In paragraph 20A of Schedule 10 (recognised supervisory bodies) [^c23019931] in the definition of “transferable securities” for “Directive [2004/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/39)” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU ”.

### 10. Income Tax Act 2007

In section 274(4) of the Income Tax Act 2007 [^c23019941] (requirements for the giving of approval)—

- (a) for “Directive [2004/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0039)” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU ”; and
- (b) for “Article 4.1(14)” substitute “ Article 4.1.21 ”.

### 11. Counter-Terrorism Act 2008

In paragraph 7 of Schedule 7 (terrorist financing and money laundering) to the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 [^c23019951], in the definition of “the markets in financial instruments directive”, for the words from “Directive” to “2004” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”.

### 12. Corporation Tax Act 2010

In section 1158(4) (meaning of “investment trust”) of the Corporation Tax Act 2010 [^c23019961] —

- (a) for “Directive [2004/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0039)” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU ”; and
- (b) for “Article 4.1(14)” substitute “ Article 4.1.21 ”.

### 13. Finance Act 2010

In paragraph 45(15) of Schedule 1 (bank payroll tax) to the Finance Act 2010 [^c23019971], for the words from “Directive” to “2004” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”.

### 14. Finance Act 2011

In paragraph 13(4) of Schedule 19 to the Finance Act 2011 [^c23019981] (the bank levy) in the definition of “dealing on own account”, for the words from “Directive” to “2004” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”.

### 15. Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013

In section 11(3)(b) of the Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013 [^c23019991] (review of proprietary trading: interpretation), for the words from “Directive” to “2004” substitute “ Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 ”.
