# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/701
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. FCA power to require information

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA may, in such manner as it may direct, require a person to provide information on, or concerning—

- (a) a position the person holds in a relevant commodity derivative or an applicable OTC commodity derivative[^c-464e0c1d60320f82b947e1c87efb6ecc] [^c-3d7da228d4f56479328fa490fa0de20d]...; or
- (b) trades a person has undertaken, or intends to undertake, in a relevant commodity derivative or an applicable OTC commodity derivative [^c-3d7da228d4f56479328fa490fa0de20d]....

(2) The FCA may, in such manner as it may direct, require the operator of a trading venue to provide information on, or concerning, trades a person has undertaken, or intends to undertake in a relevant commodity derivative or an applicable OTC commodity derivative[^c-8a2689ae6fe33edfcfdfff635b9f08a9] [^c-64caf5258cc8f06f3a8fee520818a5eb]....

(3)[^c-3caaef80a12f9124aae3edb5aa52d684] For the purposes of this regulation a commodity derivative is a “relevant” commodity derivative if—

- (a) requirements imposed by rules under regulation 15A(1)[^c-612b012a739be24736ee48f7f5f32ff7] apply to the commodity derivative (or to commodity derivatives of that class), or
- (b) it is a commodity derivative (or falls within a class of commodity derivatives) to which the FCA is considering making such requirements apply.
