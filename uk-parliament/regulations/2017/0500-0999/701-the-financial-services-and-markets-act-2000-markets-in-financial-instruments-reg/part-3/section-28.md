# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/701
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. FCA power to intervene

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the FCA considers it necessary for the purpose of advancing one or more of its operational objectives referred to in section 1B(3) of the Act[^key-07fbe6aafc8e6dd6512833334f7f412b] the FCA may—

- (a) limit the ability of a person to enter into a contract for a commodity derivative or an applicable OTC commodity derivative[^key-ef3b5e1a2c4d72cacaa100d16becb362];
- (b) restrict the size of a position a person may hold; or
- (c) require a person to reduce the size of a position held.

(2) The FCA may exercise the power under paragraph (1) notwithstanding that the limitation, restriction, or reduction would be more restrictive than a position limit established in accordance with rules made under regulation 15A or[^key-b498f00ce5a85b5c957e8c91ff6e88fa] by the FCA under regulation 16 [^key-b9ca23f45c940f9f49076dc2878782ae]...

(3) Paragraph (1) applies regardless as to whether the person is in the United Kingdom or not where the position relates to a commodity derivative traded on a trading venue established in the United Kingdom [^key-0c792ff07fc8d97f98fef0c72eac0e0e]....

(4) If the FCA imposes a limitation, restriction, or requirement under paragraph (1) it must issue a notice to the person.

(5) A person on whom a limitation, restriction or reduction has been imposed under paragraph (1) may refer that matter to the Tribunal.
