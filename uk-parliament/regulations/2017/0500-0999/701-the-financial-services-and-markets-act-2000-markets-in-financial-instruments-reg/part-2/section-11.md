# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/701
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Third country firms registered with the FCA: provision of services[^key-9e57b561c6e0d2886e2979eaf39bf8f3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-e5d2a6e2ef13cec05097ef41bd5e42da] A third country firm registered with the FCA[^key-4ec81db35d6af2b58073557a2313ce69] is not to be regarded as carrying on a regulated activity if it carries on the activity in the course of exercising rights under Article 46.1 (general provisions) of the markets in financial instruments regulation.

(2)[^key-7fed54888dd3bf494cf3a776f03210f4] For the purposes of this regulation, third country firms which are included on the register with ESMA immediately before IP completion day under Article 46.2 of the markets in financial instruments regulation are to be treated after exit day as being registered with the FCA.
