# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/701
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 29th June 2017 —

- (a) to enable the following to be done under the Act, as amended and applied by these Regulations—
  - (i) rules to be made under section 137R [^c23017341] (financial promotion rules) of the Act;
  - (ii) rules to be made under sections 213 [^c23017351] (the compensation scheme) or 214 [^c23017361] (general) of the Act;
  - (iii) rules to be made under paragraph 23 of Schedule 1ZA [^c23017371] (the Financial Conduct Authority) to the Act, including rules that may be made as a result of amendments by these Regulations to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Qualifying EU Provisions) Order 2013 [^c23017381];
  - (iv) fees to be charged under any rules that are made by virtue of paragraph (iii); and
  - (v)[^c-68190692c061f12a92b9e1d78224652c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) to enable rules to be made under regulation 11 (FCA rules) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Recognition Requirements for Investment Exchanges and Clearing Houses) Regulations 2001 [^c23017401].

(3) These Regulations come into force on 3rd July 2017—

- (a) to enable—
  - (i) applications to be made under regulation 4;
  - (ii) directions to be given under regulation 16(1) to establish position limits applying on or after 3rd January 2018;
  - (iii) applications to be made, directions to be given, and requirements to be imposed, under regulation 17;
  - (iv) requirements to be imposed and directions to be given under regulation 27;
  - (v) requirements to be imposed under regulations 31 and 33; and
  - (vi) the FCA and the PRA to prepare and issue a statement of policy under paragraph 14 of Schedule 1; and
- (b) to enable notifications to be given, reports or applications to be made, directions to be given, and requirements to be imposed under regulation 47 for the purposes of—
  - (i) any UK legislation which was relied on by the United Kingdom before IP completion day to implement[^c-8f5912bed4ace31e909df70283602f09] the markets in financial instruments directive, including any assimilated[^c-a3a9323f20d7cd2aa82098302e181bea][^c-6930662e72a847db5ba8458cd2d7571a] law made under the directive, as it has effect on or after 3rd January 2018; and
  - (ii) the markets in financial instruments regulation, including any assimilated[^c-50e961383409f5e70c4430311e0afe6a] law made under that regulation, as it applies under Article 55 [^c23017411] of that regulation; and
- (c) for the purposes of—
  - (i) paragraphs 6, 7, 16, 19, 20, 22, 23 and 28 of Schedule 1; and
  - (ii) paragraphs 25 and 26 of that Schedule in so far as they relate to a matter referred to in sub-paragraph (a) or (b) or in paragraph (i) of this sub-paragraph.

(4) These Regulations come into force on 31st July 2017 —

- (a) to enable the following to be done under the Act, as amended and applied by these Regulations—
  - (i) notices to be given or sent under section 312A(1)(b) [^c23017421] (exercise of passport rights by EEA market operator) or 312C [^c23017431] (exercise of passport rights by recognised investment exchange) of the Act;
  - (ii) entries to be added, removed, or altered in the record the FCA maintains under section 347(1) [^c23017441] of the Act;
  - (iii) notices relating to any relevant regulated activity to be given or received under paragraph 13(1) [^c23017451], (1B) [^c23017461], or (1C) [^c23017471] of Schedule 3 to the Act ;
  - (iv) the PRA to give consent under paragraph 13(1D) [^c23017481] of that Schedule;
  - (v) preparations to be made under paragraph 13(1E) [^c23017491] or (1F) [^c23017501] of that Schedule;
  - (vi) notices relating to any relevant regulated activity to be given or received under paragraph 14(1)(ba) [^c23017511], (1B) [^c23017521], or (1C) [^c23017531] of that Schedule ;
  - (vii) preparations to be made under paragraph 14(1D) [^c23017541] or 14(1E) [^c23017551] of that Schedule; and
  - (viii) notices relating to any relevant regulated activity to be given or received under paragraph 19(2) [^c23017561], (4) [^c23017571], (7B) [^c23017581], (8) [^c23017591], (11) [^c23017601], or (12) [^c23017611], or paragraph 20(1) [^c23017621], (3) [^c23017631], or (4) [^c23017641] of that Schedule; and
- (b) to enable notices to be given under the following provisions of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (EEA Passport Rights) Regulations 2001 [^c23017651] for the purposes of Schedule 3 to the Act, as amended and applied by these Regulations—
  - (i) regulation 4 [^c23017661] (management companies, credit institutions and financial institutions: changes);
  - (ii) regulation 4A [^c23017671] (investment firms: changes to branch details);
  - (iii) regulation 5A [^c23017681] (investment firms: changes to services);
  - (iv) regulation 11 [^c23017691] (UK management companies, credit institutions and financial institutions);
  - (v) regulation 11A [^c23017701] (UK investment firms: changes to branch details); and
  - (vi) regulation 12A [^c23017711] (UK investment firms: changes to services).

(5) In paragraph (4) “**relevant regulated activity**” means a regulated activity which relates to any specified activity or specified investment in Part 2 or 3 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 [^c23017721] that was amended or inserted by the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) (Amendment) Order 2017 [^c23017731].

(6) These Regulations come into force for all other purposes on 3rd January 2018.
