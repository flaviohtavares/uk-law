# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017, section 2A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/701

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2A. References to EU Regulations etc[^key-c6ff2fc68593074bd258fda5858c9d6b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any reference in these Regulations to any EU Regulation, EU decision or EU tertiary legislation (within the meaning of section 20 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018) is, unless the contrary intention appears, to be treated as a reference to that EU regulation, EU decision or EU tertiary legislation as it forms part of[^M_F_1774686d-a781-4120-bc34-a2bdfefbf6e6] assimilated[^key-8593986976f33620300897c46a4e2537] law.
