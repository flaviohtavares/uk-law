# The Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017, section 24

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Training

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A relevant person must—

- (a) take appropriate measures to ensure that its relevant employees , and any agents it uses for the purposes of its business whose work is of a kind mentioned in paragraph (2),[^key-49c97463ecdcb8ed306378479ba7ccbf] are—
  - (i) made aware of the law relating to money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing[^key-fd5a5edf028be203ebd49ed597d9a8cf], and to the requirements of data protection, which are relevant to the implementation of these Regulations; and
  - (ii) regularly given training in how to recognise and deal with transactions and other activities or situations which may be related to money laundering, terrorist financing or proliferation financing[^key-0854882a8478dba0987b8208e834aca7];
- (b) maintain a record in writing of the measures taken under sub-paragraph (a), and in particular, of the training given to its relevant employees and to any agents it uses for the purposes of its business whose work is of a kind mentioned in paragraph (2)[^key-dd6d74547ee45e4ff6c6043a6635d7b4].

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), a relevant employee is an employee whose work is—

- (a) relevant to the relevant person's compliance with any requirement in these Regulations, or
- (b) otherwise capable of contributing to the—
  - (i) identification or mitigation of the risk of money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing[^key-f86cb526aff30b0b50acfec3ff3a2e24] to which the relevant person's business is subject; or
  - (ii) prevention or detection of money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing[^key-bb6565dfbdb754a4136113c2f6f92704] in relation to the relevant person's business.

(3) In determining what measures are appropriate under paragraph (1), a relevant person—

- (a) must take account of—
  - (i) the nature of its business;
  - (ii) its size;
  - (iii) the nature and extent of the risks of money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing[^key-46a432ffe8c08013a5df15926a03ca6b] to which its business is subject; and
- (b) may take into account any guidance which has been—
  - (i) issued by the FCA; or
  - (ii) issued by any other supervisory authority or appropriate body and approved by the Treasury.
