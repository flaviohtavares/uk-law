# The Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017, section 74A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/692
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74A. Reporting requirements: cryptoasset businesses[^key-1763ea41155a1057576257682ad35384][^key-57627b588a33b3b62eda0f5351a956a2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Each cryptoasset exchange provider and custodian wallet provider (“cryptoasset business”) must provide to the FCA such information as the FCA may direct—

- (a) about compliance by the cryptoasset business with requirements imposed in or under Parts 2 to 6 and 7A[^key-746ecf5a98236abe5a2eaff15c76da1a] of these Regulations;
- (b) which is required by the FCA for the purpose of calculating charges under regulation 102 (costs of supervision); or
- (c) which is otherwise reasonably required by the FCA in connection with the exercise by the FCA of any of its supervisory functions.

(2) The information referred to in paragraph (1) must be provided at such times and in such form, and verified in such manner, as the FCA may direct.
