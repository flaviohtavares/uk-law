# The Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017, section 64A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/692
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64A. Application of this Part[^key-925bf4af81ae826d96e80339ef17e366]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies in respect of a cryptoasset transfer which is not excluded by paragraph (2) or (3).

(2) This Part does not apply in respect of a transfer of funds within the meaning of Article 3.9 of the funds transfer regulation.

(3) This Part does not apply to a cryptoasset transfer where both the originator and the beneficiary is a cryptoasset business acting on its own behalf.
