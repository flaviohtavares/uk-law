# The Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017, section 64H

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/692
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64H. Provision of information[^c-925bf4af81ae826d96e80339ef17e366]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A cryptoasset business must respond fully and without delay to a request in writing from a law enforcement authority (within the meaning of regulation 44(10)) for any information held in connection with this Part which that authority reasonably requires in connection with the authority’s functions.
