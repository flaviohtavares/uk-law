# The Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017, section 101

## 101. Recovery of charges and penalties through the court

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any charge or penalty imposed on a relevant person or on a payment service provider by the FCA or the Commissioners under these Regulations is a debt due from that person to the FCA or the Commissioners respectively, and is recoverable accordingly.
