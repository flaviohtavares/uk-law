## 106. General restrictions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

These Regulations do not authorise or require—

- (a)[^c-60e9178b81496a864034660664da6f92] a disclosure in contravention of the data protection legislation; or
- (b) a disclosure which is prohibited by any of Parts 1 to 3 or 5 to 7 of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016 [^c23049021].
