# The Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017, section 110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/692
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Revocation and saving provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The old money laundering regulations and the old transfer of funds regulations are revoked.

(2) The old money laundering regulations and the old transfer of funds regulations shall continue to have effect where the conduct constituting a contravention of one of those Regulations, or an offence under one of those Regulations began before the date on which these Regulations come into force.

(3) Where the old money laundering regulations or the old transfer of funds regulations continue to have effect, a penalty or an offence under the relevant Part of these Regulations is not to have effect in such circumstances.

(4) Where the conduct is found to have been committed over a period of two or more days, or at some point during a period of two or more days, it is to be taken for the purposes of paragraph (2) to have been begun on the earliest of those days.

(5) The “**old money laundering regulations**” means—

- (a) the Money Laundering Regulations 2007 [^c23049041];
- (b) the Money Laundering (Amendment) Regulations 2007 [^c23049051];
- (c) the Money Laundering (Amendment) Regulations 2011 [^c23049061];
- (d) the Money Laundering (Amendment) Regulations 2012 [^c23049071]; and
- (e) the Money Laundering (Amendment) Regulations 2015 [^c23049081].

(6) The “**old transfer of funds regulations**” means the Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2007 [^c23049091].
