# The Immigration Act 2016 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 929 (C. 79)

Financial Services And Markets

Immigration

The Immigration Act 2016 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2017

Made

18th September 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 94(1) and (2) of the Immigration Act 2016[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration Act 2016 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2017.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 30th October 2017

The following provisions of the Immigration Act 2016 come into force, for all remaining purposes, on 30th October 2017—

- (a) section 45 (bank accounts); and
- (b) Schedule 7 (bank accounts).

Brandon Lewis

Minister of State

Home Office

18th September 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of these Regulations)

Regulation 2 of these Regulations brings into force section 45 of, and Schedule 7 to, the Immigration Act [2016 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/19), for all remaining purposes, on 30th October 2017.
