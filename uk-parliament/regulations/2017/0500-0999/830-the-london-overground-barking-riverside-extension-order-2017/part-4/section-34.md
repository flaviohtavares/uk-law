# The London Overground (Barking Riverside Extension) Order 2017, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Power to operate and use the authorised works

TfL may operate and use the authorised works as a system, or part of a system, of transport for the carriage of passengers and for any connected ancillary purposes.
