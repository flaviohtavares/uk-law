## 6. Benefit of this Order[^c-5aba34366ec0d7779e1ff5360a6157f9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to article 7 (consent to transfer benefit of this Order), the provisions of this Order have effect solely for the benefit of the undertaker.
