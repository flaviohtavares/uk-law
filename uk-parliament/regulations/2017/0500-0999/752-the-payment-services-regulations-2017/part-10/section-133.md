# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 133

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/752
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133. Complaints

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Payment Systems Regulator must maintain arrangements designed to enable persons to submit complaints to it that a qualifying[^c-6c056b6d361164da741ba815c7814389] requirement has been breached.

(2) Where it considers it appropriate, the Payment Systems Regulator must include in any reply to a complaint under paragraph (1) details of the ombudsman scheme established under Part 16 of the 2000 Act (the ombudsman scheme).
