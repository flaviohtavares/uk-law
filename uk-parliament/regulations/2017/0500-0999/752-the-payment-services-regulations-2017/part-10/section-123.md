# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 123

## 123. Interpretation of Part 10

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**the 2013 Act**” means the Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013 [^c23031151];
- “**compliance failure**” means a failure by a regulated person to comply with—
  - (a) a qualifying[^key-6c138a65e5f07871899aaf2e5521192a] requirement; or
  - (b) a direction given under regulation 125(1)[^key-8e317d4531daac6b59ff81dbd7c2360c];
- [^key-66d19b464b8bf1d7dac5882315a9843a]...
- “**general direction**” has the meaning given in regulation 125(5);
- “**general guidance**” has the meaning given in regulation 134(2);
- “[^key-c9e7a898347ccada400a9c44de169dbe]**qualifying requirement**” means an obligation, prohibition or restriction imposed by—
  - (a) regulation 61 (information on ATM withdrawal charges),
  - (b) Part 8 (access to payment systems and bank accounts), with the exception of the obligation imposed on the FCA by regulation 105(5) (access to bank accounts), or
  - (c) a direction given by the Payment Systems Regulator under regulation 125(2A);
- “**regulated person**” means a person on whom a qualifying[^key-01674283ce655758cc6e1e5a086fe15a] requirement is imposed.
