# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 147

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/752

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 147. Duty to co-operate and exchange of information

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA, the Commissioners and the Payment Systems Regulator may[^c-ad3da848a73ac4f760c958f24be8a8a7] take such steps as they consider appropriate to co-operate with each other and—

- (a)[^c-3e03fb1c50f102dcdf1864ca431294cc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-68087a816729eb3be441bd830f48c7e1] the Bank of England; and
- (c) any other public authority exercising functions in the United Kingdom in relation to payment service providers,

for the purposes of the exercise by those bodies of their functions under these Regulations and other relevant legislation[^c-eb850c7dbbf38cf3970162489b60aeee].

(2) Subject to the requirements of the Data Protection Act 1998 [^c23031261], section 348 of the 2000 Act (restrictions on disclosure of confidential information by FCA etc.) [^c23031271] (as applied with modifications by paragraph 8 of Schedule 6 to these Regulations), regulation 105 of the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017 [^c23031281] (disclosure by the Commissioners) and any other applicable restrictions on the disclosure of information, the FCA, the Commissioners and the Payment Systems Regulator may provide information to each other and—

- (a) the bodies mentioned in paragraph (1)(c)[^c-8102edb656de2f2202b226ad730734c2];
- (b)[^c-e33283532c5c40ff3b63b6b2e2d88b87] the Bank of England when acting in its capacity as a monetary and oversight authority;
- (c) where relevant, other public authorities responsible for the oversight of payment and settlement systems;

for the purposes of the exercise by those bodies of their functions under these Regulations and other relevant legislation[^c-2d914af0b6e69825378ee9f0a4f73947].

(3) Part 9 of the Enterprise Act 2002 [^c23031291] (information) does not prohibit disclosure of information under paragraph (2) but a person to whom that Part applies must have regard to the considerations mentioned in section 244 of that Act (specified information: considerations relevant to disclosure) before making any such disclosure.

(4)[^c-a54ba43fe4a88342a72434607531b731] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
