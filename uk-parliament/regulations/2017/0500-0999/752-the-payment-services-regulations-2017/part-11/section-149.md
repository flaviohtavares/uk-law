## 149. Saving of Payment Services Regulations 2009

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding the revocation of the Payment Services Regulations 2009 by regulation 157 (revocations), those Regulations continue to apply in relation to services provided before 13th January 2018.
