# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/752
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Payment accounts and sums received for the execution of payment transactions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any payment account held by an authorised payment institution or a small payment institution must be used only in relation to payment transactions.
