# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 96

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Force majeure

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person is not liable for any contravention of a requirement imposed on it by or under this Part where the contravention is due to abnormal and unforeseeable circumstances beyond the person's control, the consequences of which would have been unavoidable despite all efforts to the contrary.

(2) A payment service provider is not liable for any contravention of a requirement imposed on it by or under this Part where the contravention is due to the obligations of the payment service provider under other provisions of [^c-d1767e998561b703840c55878edc4484]... national law.
