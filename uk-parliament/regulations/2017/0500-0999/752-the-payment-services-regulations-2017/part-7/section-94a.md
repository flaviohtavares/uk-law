# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 94A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/752
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94A. Liability of payment service provider for charges and interest as a consequence of delay under regulation 86(2B)[^c-b483f8484747b5939dc16e333effe700]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A payment service provider is liable to its payment service user for—

- (a) any charges for which the payment service user is responsible; and
- (b) any interest which the payment service user must pay,

as a consequence of a delay to the execution of a payment order in reliance on regulation 86(2B), irrespective of whether the payment order is ultimately executed.
