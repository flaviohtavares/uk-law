# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/752
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Cash placed on a payment account

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a payment service user places cash on its payment account with a payment service provider in the same currency as that payment account, the payment service provider must—

- (a) if the user is a consumer, micro-enterprise or charity, ensure that the amount is made available and value dated immediately after the receipt of the funds;
- (b) in any other case, ensure that the amount is made available and value dated no later than the end of the next business day after the receipt of the funds.
