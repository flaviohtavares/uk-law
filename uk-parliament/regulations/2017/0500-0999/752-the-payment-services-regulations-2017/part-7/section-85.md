# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 85

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/752
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Application of regulations 86 to 88

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations 86 to 88 , except paragraphs (2A) to (2D) of regulation 86,[^key-8d1d8972871948b13734368be53429e5] apply to any payment transaction—

- (a)[^key-c1bc48aad37a9337dbaabad55d9e4e7e] executed wholly within the qualifying area in euro under a payment scheme which operates across the qualifying area
- (b) executed wholly within the United Kingdom in sterling; or
- (c) executed wholly under a payment scheme which operates across the qualifying area and[^key-4a0a6ddc0421cdf445867fa3c4c76f87] involving only one currency conversion between the euro and sterling, provided that—
  - (i) the currency conversion is carried out in the United Kingdom; and
  - (ii) in the case of cross-border payment transactions, the cross-border transfer takes place in euro.

(1A)[^key-0f9d0bf13899304e853e9ed91a50887d] Paragraphs (2A) to (2D) of regulation 86 (payment transactions to a payment account) apply to any payment transaction which is—

- (a) authorised in accordance with regulation 67 (consent and withdrawal of consent);
- (b) executed wholly within the United Kingdom in sterling; and
- (c) not initiated by or through a payee.

(2) In respect of any other payment transaction, the payment service user may agree with the payment service provider that regulations 86 to 88 (except regulation 86(3)) do not apply.

(3)[^key-55dab6201d08caca6fcf6512a01f8e1e] In paragraph (1), “**the qualifying area**” means the area of the United Kingdom and the EEA States.
