# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/752
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Value date and availability of funds

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The credit value date for the payee's payment account must be no later than the business day on which the amount of the payment transaction is credited to the account of the payee's payment service provider.

(2) Paragraph (3) applies where—

- (a) the transaction does not involve a currency conversion by the payee’s payment service provider[^c-74e06f318f10d796e7b2969d3e6438e7];
- (b) the transaction involves a currency conversion by the payee’s payment service provider[^c-9cd6fdf9c96cde455e9ec937dc86b477] between the euro and pounds sterling [^c-99800a18e863d65be067e9d207170dc5]...; or
- (c) the transaction involves only one payment service provider.

(3) The payee's payment service provider must ensure that the amount of the payment transaction is at the payee's disposal immediately after that amount has been credited to that payment service provider's account.

(4) The debit value date for the payer's payment account must be no earlier than the time at which the amount of the payment transaction is debited to that payment account.
