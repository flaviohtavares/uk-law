# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/752
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Information requirements for account information service providers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A registered account information service provider [^c-93eb78b75cc77edb38904e843d272925]... must provide to the payment service user—

- (a) such information specified in Schedule 4 (prior general information for framework contracts) as is relevant to the service provided;
- (b) all charges payable by the payment service user to the account information service provider and, where applicable, a breakdown of those charges.
