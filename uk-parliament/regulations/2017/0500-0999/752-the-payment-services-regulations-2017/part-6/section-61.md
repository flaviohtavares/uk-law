# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/752
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Information on ATM withdrawal charges

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A provider of cash withdrawal services falling within paragraph 2(o) of Schedule 1 must ensure that a customer using such services is provided with information on withdrawal charges falling within regulations 43 (information required prior to the conclusion of a single payment service contract), 45 (information required after receipt of the payment order), 46 (information for the payee after execution) and 57 (currency and currency conversion), before the withdrawal and on receipt of the cash.
