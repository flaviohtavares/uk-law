# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 121

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/752

## 121. FCA's exemption from liability in damages[^c-17cafcf62e4395d6a748c67e97abce93]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The functions of the FCA under these Regulations are to be treated for the purposes of paragraph 25 (exemption from liability in damages) of Part 4 of Schedule 1ZA to the 2000 Act [^c23031141] as functions conferred on the FCA under that Act.
