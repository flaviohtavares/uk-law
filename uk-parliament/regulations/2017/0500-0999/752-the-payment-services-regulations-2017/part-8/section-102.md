# The Payment Services Regulations 2017, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/752
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Application of regulation 103

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 103 does not apply to the following kinds of payment systems—

- (a) a designated system;
- (b) a payment system consisting solely of payment service providers belonging to the same group.
