# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 99

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Death

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a JAVC member dies, any lump sum payable to that JAVC member under the JAVC scheme must be paid to the person (if any) nominated by that JAVC member for the purposes of this paragraph by notice in writing to the administrators.

(2) If no such nomination is made, the administrators must pay the amount payable to the personal representatives of the JAVC member.
