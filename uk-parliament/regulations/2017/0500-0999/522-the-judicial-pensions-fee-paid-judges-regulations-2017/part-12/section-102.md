# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 102

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Administrative expenses

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any administrative expenses incurred by an authorised provider by virtue of acting as such under this Part may be defrayed, to the extent agreed by the administrators, out of the realisable value of any investments made by the authorised provider with the JAVC member's scheme contributions under regulation 94 and any transfer payment accepted under regulation 96.
