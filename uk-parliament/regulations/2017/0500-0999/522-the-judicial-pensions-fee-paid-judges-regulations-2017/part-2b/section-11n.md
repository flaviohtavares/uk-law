# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 11N

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11N. Lump sum: entitlement and amount[^key-dcd5561111f11f4a3b5252377ca63e9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a member (“**P**”) retires on or after the amendment day and becomes entitled to the payment of a pension under this Part.

(2) At the time P becomes entitled to the payment of a pension, P becomes entitled to a lump sum of twice the annual rate of pension payable to P under this Part.
