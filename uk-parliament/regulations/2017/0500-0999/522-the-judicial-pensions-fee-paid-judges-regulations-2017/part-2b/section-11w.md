# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 11W

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11W. Payment and application of an eligible child’s pension[^key-dcd5561111f11f4a3b5252377ca63e9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A pension to which a person is entitled under regulation 11U is payable at such intervals, not exceeding 3 months, as the Treasury may determine.

(2) A person to whom a pension or part of a pension under regulation 11U is paid must apply the pension for the benefit of—

- (a) all eligible children of the deceased member; or
- (b) such eligible children of the deceased member as the Treasury may direct.
