# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 55A

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55A. Calculation of initial pre-commencement dependants’ contributions amount[^key-738db59ed99d6329f931f943617a1c08]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The initial pre-commencement dependants’ contributions amount in relation to each eligible office held by P is determined under paragraph (2) , (2A)[^key-767ed12816657d12661d10fedf699040] or (3) as applicable.

(2) For an office specified in Table 1 or Table 2 of Schedule 1 where the office was first held before 31st March 1995[^key-82d39b4e85630ab07330f932ee9c82ab]—

- *Step 1*

  Determine the total of fees paid to P in respect of qualifying fee-paid days falling within the period before 7th April 2000.
- *Step 2*

  Multiply the total determined under Step 1 by the percentage specified in column 3 of the first row of Table 1 of the Contributions Tables.

(2A)[^key-cf163a12f04672903e92d09a1853a257] For an office specified in Table 1 or Table 2 of Schedule 1 where the office was first held on or after 31st March 1995, paragraph (2) applies with the exception that the reference to column 3 should be read as column 4.

(3) For offices specified in Table 3 or Table 4 of Schedule 1—

- *Step 1*

  Determine the total of any fees paid to P in respect of qualifying fee-paid days falling within the period before 7th April 2000.
- *Step 2*

  Multiply the total determined under Step 1 by the percentage specified in column 4 of the first row of Table 1 of the Contributions Tables.
- *Step 3*

  Determine the total of any fees paid to P in respect of any service credit days.
- *Step 4*

  Multiply the total determined under Step 3 by the appropriate percentage specified in Table 8.
- *Step 5*

  Add together the amounts determined under Steps 2 and 4.
