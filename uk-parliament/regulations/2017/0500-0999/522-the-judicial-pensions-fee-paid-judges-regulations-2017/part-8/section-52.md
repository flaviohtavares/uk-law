# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 52

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Interpretation[^key-dfae7c7aeda379fd5cb11ce519520821]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**fee period**” means a period specified in column 1 of Table 1 of the Contributions Tables[^key-0ba967b951c533bc4f87acb974867047];
- “[^key-9eea1c60e1791d4abac6b84959b055ce]**the initial pre-commencement dependants’ contributions amount**” means the amount payable in accordance with this Part for the period before 7th April 2000;
- “**the pre-commencement contributions amount**” has the meaning given by regulation 53(1);
- “**P**” means a member who is not an opted-out member;
- “[^key-f4a92eba8a8f071a102ece9d39a91e53]**the Contributions Tables**” means Tables 1 to 10 in Schedule 3.
