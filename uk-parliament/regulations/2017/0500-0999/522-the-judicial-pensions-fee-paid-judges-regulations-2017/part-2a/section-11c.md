# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 11C

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11C. Elections available to members[^key-dcd5561111f11f4a3b5252377ca63e9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the conditions in regulation 11B are met, P, or if P has died, P’s personal representative, may nevertheless elect for benefits in relation to the relevant office which would otherwise be calculated under the pre-1995 provisions to be calculated under the post-1995 provisions, by sending a notice to the appropriate Minister.

(2) A notice under this regulation—

- (a) must be in the form determined by the appropriate Minister;
- (b) must be received by the appropriate Minister within the period of 12 months beginning with the day on which P retires or dies, or [^key-eb4b18aa266c34fa0eb4721ae098806f]... within the period of 48[^key-897cd499ede0e254da8cef4f36126b66] months beginning with the amendment day , whichever is the later[^key-d073f9c7f32e75c1d533118bad32ff55];
- (c) has effect the day it is received; and
- (d) is irrevocable.
