# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 84

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Application to accept payment into scheme

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) P may ask the administrators to accept a payment into the principal scheme (“a transfer payment”) representing the cash equivalent of P's accrued rights in any registered pension scheme.

(2) The administrators may refuse to accept the payment or any part of it.

(3) Unless paragraph (4) applies to P[^key-6cc672a1f337124df5bf60483b1cf50b] a request under paragraph (1) must be made—

- (a) in writing;
- (b) before P has reached normal pension age; and
- (c) not less than one year before P becomes entitled to a pension under these Regulations on retirement from P's eligible fee-paid judicial office.

(4)[^key-f3b93a4ea6ab666381af5b73293f8375] If P meets the requirement in regulation 83(1), P may make a request in writing within the period of one year beginning with the amendment day and must nominate a date on which the transfer is to take effect, between 6th February 2013 and the amendment day, which must be a day on which P would have met the requirements set out in paragraph (3)(b) and (c).
