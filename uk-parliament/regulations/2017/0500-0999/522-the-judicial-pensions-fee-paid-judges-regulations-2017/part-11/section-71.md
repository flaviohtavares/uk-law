# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 71

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Disapplication of other provisions about transfers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 4ZA of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 (transfer rights: general) [^c23060591] and Chapter 1 of Part 4ZA of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 (transfer rights: general) [^c23060601] do not apply in relation to the relevant schemes.

## Annotations

[^c23060591]: Textual amendment: [Part 4ZA](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/522/part/4ZA) was inserted by paragraph 8 of Schedule 4 to the [Pension Schemes Act 2015 (c.8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/8).

[^c23060601]: Textual amendment: [Chapter 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/522/chapter/4) of Part 4 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 was renumbered as Chapter 1 of Part 4 ZA of that Act by paragraph 50 of Schedule 4 to the Pension Schemes Act 2015.
