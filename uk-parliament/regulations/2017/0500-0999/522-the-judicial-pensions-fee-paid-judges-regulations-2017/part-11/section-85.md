# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 85

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Cancellation of request

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) P may, by notice in writing given to the administrators, cancel a request made under regulation 84 at any time before it has been accepted.

(2) If P cancels a request, P may make another.
