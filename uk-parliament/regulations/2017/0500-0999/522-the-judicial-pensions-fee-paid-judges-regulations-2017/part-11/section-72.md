# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 72

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Transfers out

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the conditions set out in paragraph (2) are met, regulations 73 to 82 apply to P—

- (a) to or in respect of whom benefits are payable under one or more of the relevant schemes; and
- (b) the last day which counts towards P's reckonable service (“last day of reckonable service”) or the last day on which P held an eligible fee-paid judicial office is on or after the commencement day.

(2) The conditions are that—

- (a) P has not retired;
- (b) on P's last day of reckonable service—
  - (i) P has accrued rights to benefits under the principal scheme; or
  - (ii) P would have such rights if P's qualifying judicial service had also ended on that date, and
- (c) either—
  - (i) P's age on 6th February 2013 was more than one year under normal pension age, and P exercises the option within twelve months of the amendment day (in which case the restriction in regulation 79 does not apply to P)[^c-b3ff996861c942249f58f9962cfdc343]; or
  - (ii) P's age on the date P exercises the option is at least one year under normal pension age.
