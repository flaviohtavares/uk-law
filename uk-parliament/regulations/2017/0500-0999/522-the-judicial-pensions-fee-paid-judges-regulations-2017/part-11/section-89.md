# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 89

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Refusal to accept

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the additional pension available to be transferred into the scheme is less than twice the aggregate accrued annual rate of the guaranteed minimum pension (if any) of P at the relevant date, the transfer payment must be rejected by the administrators.
