# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 69

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Repayments under this Part to be paid with compound interest

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the appropriate Minister makes a payment to a member under this Part, it must be paid together with compound interest at the rate announced annually in relation to the Principal Civil Service Pension Scheme.
