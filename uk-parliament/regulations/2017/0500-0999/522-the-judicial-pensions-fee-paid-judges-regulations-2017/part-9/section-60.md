# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 60

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Liability to pay contributions on fees in respect of qualifying fee-paid days

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

P must pay a contribution to the appropriate Minister on any fee paid to P in respect of a qualifying fee-paid day where the day is, or is after, the commencement day.
