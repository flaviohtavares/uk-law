## 62. Amount of contribution

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The amount of the contribution payable on a fee under regulation 60 is the sum of—

- (a) the personal benefits contribution, and
- (b) the dependants' benefits contribution.

(2) The amount of the personal benefits contribution is the amount of the fee [^key-80982da7d7e41bb9e75d0af0810b4573]... multiplied by the percentage specified in column 2 of Tables 1 to 7 in Schedule 3[^key-88bc27a9152de09936849f0df19fb92c] for the relevant fee year, in the row relating to P's annualised fees for that year.

(3) The amount of the dependants' benefits contribution is the amount of the fee [^key-078b6c5fde6d0f307debb2926d41f3dd]... multiplied by the percentage specified in the relevant column in Tables 1 to 7 in Schedule 3[^key-26974f34618801eade0d447d3d68da1c] and for this purpose the relevant column is determined under paragraph (3ZA) or (3ZB)[^key-a780aa21d2a0c9dabd51372dd6a1f396].

(3ZA)[^key-e78f41a08a89bf5fb2306b1bde0eb689] If the fee was for an office specified in Table 1 or Table 2 of Schedule 1 and that office was first held before 31st March 1995[^key-72c6b514816f06a8e08c5dd69ccd139a], the relevant column is column 3 ; if the fee was for an office specified in Table 1 or Table 2 of Schedule 1 and that office was first held on or after 31st March 1995, the relevant column is column 4[^key-3ed1548100af69ab799907528b5d9a73].

(3ZB) If the fee was for an office specified in Table 3 or 4 of Schedule 1, the relevant column is column 4.
