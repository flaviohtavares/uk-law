# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 14

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Retirement between the ages of 60 and 65: entitlement to reduced pension

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where—

- (a) P[^key-813d70b1faf322e960ccd57a3c690e40] retires (whether before, on or after the commencement day); and
- (b) on the day on which P retires, P—
  - (i) has reached the age of 60 but not the age of 65, and
  - (ii) has at least 5 years' qualifying judicial service.

(2) On the pension start day, P becomes entitled to the payment for life of a pension in respect of any relevant offices[^key-6f76306c8907ed58f587dfa3b637607a].

(3) In this regulation “**the pension start day**” means—

- (a) the day after that on which P retires; or
- (b) if later, the commencement day.
