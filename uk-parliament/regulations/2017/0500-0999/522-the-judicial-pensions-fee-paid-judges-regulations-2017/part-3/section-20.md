## 20. Annual rate of ill-health ordinary pension

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 13 applies in relation to the annual rate of a pension to which a member becomes entitled under regulation 19 as it applies in relation to the annual rate of a pension to which a member becomes entitled under regulation 12.
