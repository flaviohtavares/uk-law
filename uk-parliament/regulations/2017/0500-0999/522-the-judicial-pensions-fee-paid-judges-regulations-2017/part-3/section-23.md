# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 23

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Ill-health certification condition

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The ill-health certification condition is met in relation to P[^key-2551f9ee6a03eab8941abbebf2e9179a] if the appropriate Minister—

- (a) has received a medical certificate—
  - (i) stating that P has suffered a permanent breakdown in health that makes P incapable of discharging the duties of the judicial office or offices which P held immediately before retirement; or
  - (ii) (where P retired due to ill-health before the commencement day or, as applicable, before the amendment day[^key-1d2406bdcecfa7e6833aa9cc75247089]), stating that at the date on which P had retired, P had suffered a permanent breakdown in health that made P incapable of discharging the duties of the judicial office or offices which P held immediately before retirement;
- (b) is satisfied as to the matters contained in that certificate; and
- (c) has, where the appropriate Minister is the Lord Chancellor, consulted—
  - (i) the Lord Chief Justice of England and Wales (if P held a judicial office in England and Wales);
  - (ii) the Lord Chief Justice of Northern Ireland (if P held a judicial office in Northern Ireland).

(2) For the purposes of this regulation P's breakdown in health is “permanent” if it is likely to continue until—

- (a) the day on which P reaches the age of 65; or
- (b) if later, the day on which, if P had not retired, P would have completed 5 years' qualifying judicial service.

(3) The Lord Chief Justice of England and Wales may nominate a judicial office holder (as defined in section 109(4) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 [^c23060391]) to exercise any of his or her functions under this regulation.

(4) The Lord Chief Justice of Northern Ireland may nominate—

- (a) the holder of one of the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 [^c23060401], or
- (b) a Lord Justice of Appeal (as defined in section 88 of that Act [^c23060411]),

to exercise any of his or her functions under this regulation.
