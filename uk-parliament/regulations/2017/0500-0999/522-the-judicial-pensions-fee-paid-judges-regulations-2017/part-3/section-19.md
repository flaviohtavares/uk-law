# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 19

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Retirement aged 65 or over due to ill-health: entitlement to ill-health ordinary pension

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where—

- (a) P has accrued reckonable service in relation to one or more relevant offices and[^c-40175e35c24a36fc2dd23e2da76c4f98] retires (whether before, on or after the commencement day);
- (b) on the day on which P retires, P—
  - (i) has reached the age of 65; and
  - (ii) has less than 5 years' qualifying judicial service; and
- (c) the ill-health certification condition is met in relation to P.

(2) On the pension start day, P becomes entitled to the payment for life of a pension in respect of the relevant offices[^c-cdfea173e85a277e89e79934745e3490].

(3) In this regulation “**the pension start day**” means—

- (a) the day after that on which P retires, or
- (b) if later, the commencement day.
