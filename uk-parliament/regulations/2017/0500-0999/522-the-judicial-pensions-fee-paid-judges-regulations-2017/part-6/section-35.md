# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 35

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Interpretation and application of Part[^c-42c77cf3ed84d3d666cdabb3945e5414]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-822984db1f45b259b6b77fa63b7e3485] In this Part[^c-d20a19769bdd6cd0f1c5480eb03e9022]—

- (a) “**surviving adult**”, in relation to a deceased member (“P”), means P's surviving spouse or surviving civil partner;
- (b) “**surviving spouse**”, in relation to P, means a person who—
  - (i) was married to P on the date of P's death, and
  - (ii) if P retired before death, entered into the marriage before P retired;
- (c) “**surviving civil partner**”, in relation to P, means a person who—
  - (i) was in a civil partnership with P on the date of P's death, and
  - (ii) if P retired before death, entered into the civil partnership before P retired;
- (d) “**surviving adult's pension**” means a pension payable to a surviving adult under this Part.

(2)[^c-fe717d9d3858750a793f9aa4a788353f] This Part applies in respect of P where P is a person to whom Part 3 would have applied if P had retired on the day P died.
