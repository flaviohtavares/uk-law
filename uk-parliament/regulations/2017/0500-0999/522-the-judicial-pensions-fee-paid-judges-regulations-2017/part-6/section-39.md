# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 39

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Payment of a surviving adult's pension

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A surviving adult's pension is payable at such intervals, not exceeding 3 months, as the Treasury may determine.
