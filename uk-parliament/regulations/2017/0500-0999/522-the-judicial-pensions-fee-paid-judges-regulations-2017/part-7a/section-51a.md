# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 51A

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51A. Application of Part[^c-1a570871de08c474840e7718f4583fc2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part applies where on 1st April 2023, a member (“**P**”)—

- (a) has or had a liability to pay contributions under Part 8 or 9; and
- (b) has made an excess contributions payment.
