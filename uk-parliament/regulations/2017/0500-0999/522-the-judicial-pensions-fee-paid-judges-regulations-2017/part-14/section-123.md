# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 123

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Purchase of added surviving adult's pension by unretired JASAPS members[^c-971df86ab0f62d77acd744612359db68]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A notice given under regulation 117(2) by an unretired[^c-ec4a73cc3edc4d41d98d0b20a4dac91d] JASAPS member (“M”) must specify—

- (a) M's assumed contribution commencement date;
- (b) M's assumed retirement age;
- (c) the number of qualifying fee-paid days in any office in relation to which benefits are to be calculated under the post-1995 provisions[^c-18975221fd6ffc067a2a78034727fbe8] which M proposes to work each year to the period ending with the date on which M reaches M's assumed retirement age; and
- (d) the number of units of added surviving adult's pension which M intends to purchase.

(2) The maximum number of units which an unretired[^c-1743160e8622de5d65885379a103d214] member may purchase in the JASAPS is the amount which the administrators determine would produce a pension equal to a sum calculated in accordance with the formula—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/522)

where—

- A is—
  - (i) where M held a single judicial office on the assumed contribution date, the salary which would have been M's appropriate annual salary if M had retired on that date;
  - (ii) where M held more than one judicial office on the assumed contribution date, the salary which would have the highest appropriate salary of these offices if M had retired on that date; and
- Y is M's reckonable service in any office in relation to which benefits are to be calculated under the post-1995 provisions[^c-08c07462ed54d47b68060ac6323b5535] between the date on which M first held eligible fee-paid judicial office and the date on which M reaches M’s assumed retirement age as if the principal scheme had not closed to further accruals[^c-d910370f5a5507c7b894415dcfa439c0] (assuming that M works the number of qualifying fee-paid days notified under paragraph (1)(c)) expressed in years and fractions of a year to four decimal places;
- Z is—
  - (i) if M is purchasing added years, the number of added years which M would have purchased by the date on which M reaches M's assumed retirement age, or
  - (ii) if M is not purchasing added years, nil.

(3) The date on which M is to start payment of periodic contributions (“the periodic contributions start date”) must—

- (a) be within 12 months of the date on which M gives the notice, and
- (b) be agreed between M and the administrators.
