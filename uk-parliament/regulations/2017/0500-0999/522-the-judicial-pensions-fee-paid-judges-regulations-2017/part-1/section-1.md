# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 1

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

These Regulations may be cited as the Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017 and come into force on—

- (a) 1st April 2017, or
- (b) if later, the day after the day on which they are made.
