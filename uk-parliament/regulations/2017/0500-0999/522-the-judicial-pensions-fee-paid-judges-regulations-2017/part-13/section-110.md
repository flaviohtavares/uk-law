# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 110

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Purchase of added years by unretired FPJAYS members[^c-fba4d10b387881fe55401157ace994f7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A notice given by an unretired[^c-5ec753a1a9ff0899b51a4f44ad5bfc96] FPJAYS member (“M”) under regulation 104(2) must specify—

- (a) M's assumed contribution commencement date;
- (b) M's assumed retirement age;
- (c) the number of qualifying fee-paid days which M proposes to work each year in any office in relation to which benefits are to be calculated under the post-1995 provisions[^c-0ca43ffa0c10e606c7f94e2c5bd8e285] to the period ending with the date on which M reaches M's assumed retirement age; and
- (d) the number of added years M intends to purchase.

(2) The date on which M is to start payment of periodic contributions (“the periodic contributions start date”) must—

- (a) be within 12 months of the date on which M gives the notice, and
- (b) be agreed between M and the administrators.

(3) The maximum number of added years that M may purchase under the FPJAYS is to be determined in accordance with the formula

- (a) A is the aggregate length of reckonable service in eligible fee-paid judicial offices that M would have at M's assumed retirement date and for this purpose regulation 103(1A) and (1B) does not apply[^c-1cc37fc3f242f6b71e03f939b27602d9], assuming that M works the number of qualifying fee-paid days notified under paragraph (1)(c);
- (b) B is the aggregate length of service in qualifying judicial office (within the meaning of Part 1 of the Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993) that M would have at M's assumed retirement date; and
- (c) C is the equivalent value of any retained benefits to which M was entitled at the assumed contribution commencement date.

(4) Where the maximum number of added years would (apart from this paragraph) be negative, it is instead nil.
