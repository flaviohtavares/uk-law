# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 114

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Limit on children's pension benefits

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any benefits that are paid under the FPJAYS in the form of a children's pension under regulation 42 cease to be payable on the date that the recipient attains the age of 23.
