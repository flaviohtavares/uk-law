# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 115

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. Reduction of benefits

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where the administrators of the FPJAYS pay the whole or any part of the lifetime allowance charge arising on a benefit crystallisation event relating to the FPJAYS in relation to a FPJAYS member.

(2) The pensions or other benefits payable under this Part to or in respect of the FPJAYS member may (notwithstanding any other provision of these Regulations) be reduced by such amount as, in accordance with normal actuarial practice, reflects the amount of the payments made.
