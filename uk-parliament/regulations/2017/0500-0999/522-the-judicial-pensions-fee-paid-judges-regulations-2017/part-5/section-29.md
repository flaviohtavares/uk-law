# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 29

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Application of Part and interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies where—

- (a) a person (“**the transferee**”) becomes entitled to a pension credit under section 29(1)(b) of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 [^c23060421] (“**the 1999 Act**”) or under Article 26(1)(b) of the Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 [^c23060431] (“**the 1999 Order**”); and
- (b) that pension credit is derived from the rights of another person (“P”) under the principal scheme.

(2) In this Part “the transferee”, “**the 1999 Act**”, “**the 1999 Order**” and “**P**” have the meanings given by paragraph (1).
