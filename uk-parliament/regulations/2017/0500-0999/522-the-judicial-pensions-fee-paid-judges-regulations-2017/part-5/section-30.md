# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 30

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Pension credit derived from the principal scheme: entitlement to pension

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On the pension start day the transferee becomes entitled to the payment for life of a pension.

(2) In paragraph (1) “**the pension start day**” means—

- (a) the day on which the transferee reaches the age of 65, or
- (b) if later, the day after the last day of the implementation period as determined in accordance with section 34 of the 1999 Act [^c23060441] or Article 31 of the 1999 Order [^c23060451].
