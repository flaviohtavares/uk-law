# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 34

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Exclusions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The appropriate Minister has no power to accept for the benefit of a member of the principal scheme—

- (a) a payment under paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 5 to the 1999 Act or paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 5 to the 1999 Order,
- (b) a payment under section 95 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 [^c23060461] or section 91 of the Pensions Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 [^c23060471], or
- (c) any other payment,

to the extent that that payment directly or indirectly represents a pension credit.

[^c23060471]: Textual amendment: [1993 c. 49](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49). Section 91 was amended by section 71 of, and paragraph 55 of Schedule 4 to, the Pensions Scheme Act 2015.
