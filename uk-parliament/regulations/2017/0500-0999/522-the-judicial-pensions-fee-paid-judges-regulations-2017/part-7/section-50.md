# The Judicial Pensions (Fee-Paid Judges) Regulations 2017, section 50

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Additional payment where no or insufficient interim payments made

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where the interim payments amount in relation to a person is less than the pre-commencement benefits amount in relation to that person.

(2) The administrators must pay to the person the amount of the difference between the two amounts mentioned in paragraph (1).
