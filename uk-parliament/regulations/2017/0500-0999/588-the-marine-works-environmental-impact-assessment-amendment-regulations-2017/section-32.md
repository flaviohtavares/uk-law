# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations 2017, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Amendment of Schedule 4

(1) Schedule 4 (request for a scoping opinion) is amended as follows.

(2) For paragraph 1(b) substitute—

> - (b) a brief description of the specific characteristics of the regulated activity and the project, including their nature, purpose, location and technical capacity;

.

(3) After paragraph 1(b), insert—

> - (ba) an explanation of the likely significant effects of the regulated activity and the project on the environment;

.

(4) In the opening words of paragraph 5, for “consider” substitute “take into account”.

(5) At the end of paragraph 5(a), insert “, including its location and technical capacity”.

(6) For paragraph 5(d) substitute—

> - (d) any information provided by the applicant about the project and the regulated activity.

.

(7) In paragraph 8(1)(b)[^f00026], for “, the 2009 Act or the 2010 Act” substitute “or the 2009 Act”.
