# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations 2017, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Amendment of regulation 7

Regulation 7 (determination: Annex I projects) is amended as follows—

- (a) in the heading, for “Annex I” substitute “Schedule A1”;
- (b) for “9 and 10” substitute “9 to 10”;
- (c) for “an Annex I” substitute “a Schedule A1”.
