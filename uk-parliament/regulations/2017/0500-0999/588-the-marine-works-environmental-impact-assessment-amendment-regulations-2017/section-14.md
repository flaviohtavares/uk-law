# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations 2017, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. New regulation 15A

After regulation 15, insert—

> **15A. Co-ordination**
>
> Where, in respect of a regulated activity, there is a requirement to carry out—
>
> - (a) an environmental impact assessment; and
> - (b) an assessment under the Habitats Directive or the Wild Birds Directive,
>
> the appropriate authority must, where appropriate, ensure that the assessments are co-ordinated.
