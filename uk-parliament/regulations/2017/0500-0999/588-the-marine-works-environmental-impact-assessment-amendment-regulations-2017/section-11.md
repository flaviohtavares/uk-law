# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations 2017, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Amendment of regulation 10A

In regulation 10A(2) and (5)[^f00020] (further provisions in relation to Article 2(4) of the EIA Directive)—

- (a) for “an Annex I” substitute “a Schedule A1”;
- (b) for “an Annex II” substitute “a Schedule A2”.
