# The Marine Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations 2017, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Amendment of regulation 8

Regulation 8 (determination: Annex II projects) is amended as follows—

- (a) in the heading, for “Annex II” substitute “Schedule A2”;
- (b) in paragraph (1), for “9 and 10” substitute “9 to 10”;
- (c) in paragraphs (1) and (2), for “an Annex II” substitute “a Schedule A2”.
