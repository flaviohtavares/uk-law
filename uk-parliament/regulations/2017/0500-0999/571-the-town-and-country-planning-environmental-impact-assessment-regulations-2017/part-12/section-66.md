# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017, section 66

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 66. Application to the High Court

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of Part 12 of the Act (validity), the reference in section 288 [^c23016931] of the Act (Proceedings for questioning the validity of other orders, decisions and directions) to action of the Secretary of State which is not within the powers of the Act shall be taken to extend to a grant of planning permission or subsequent consent by the Secretary of State in contravention of regulation 3 or 36.

## Annotations

[^c23016931]: Marginal citation: Section 288 was amended by section 91 of, and paragraphs 1 and 4 of Schedule 16 to, the [Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2).
