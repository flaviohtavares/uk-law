# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017, section 67

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Hazardous waste and material change of use

*Extent: E+W*

A change in the use of land or buildings to a use for a purpose mentioned in paragraph 9 of Schedule 1 involves a material change in the use of that land or those buildings for the purposes of section 55(1) of the Act (meaning of “**development**” and “**new development**”) [^c23016941].
