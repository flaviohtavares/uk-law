# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017, section 76

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 76. Revocation and transitional provisions

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) to (4), the 2011 Regulations are revoked.

(2) Notwithstanding the revocation in paragraph (1), the 2011 Regulations continue to apply where before the commencement of these Regulations—

- (a) an applicant, appellant or qualifying body, as the case may be, has submitted an environmental statement or requested a scoping opinion; or
- (b) in respect of local development orders, the local planning authority has in connection with that order prepared an environmental statement or a scoping opinion or requested a scoping direction.

(3) Notwithstanding the revocation in paragraph (1), Parts 1 and 2 of the 2011 Regulations continue to apply to—

- (a) requests for a screening opinion or direction;
- (b) screening opinions adopted by the relevant planning authority; and
- (c) screening directions made by the Secretary of State;

where, before the coming into force of these Regulations, such requests were made or the relevant planning authority or the Secretary of State, as the case may be, initiated the making or adoption of such screening opinions or screening directions.

(4) In this regulation—

- “**the 2011 Regulations**” means the Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2011 [^c23017031]; and
- “environmental statement”, “scoping direction”, “scoping opinion”, “screening direction and “screening opinion” have the meanings given by regulation 2 of the 2011 Regulations.
