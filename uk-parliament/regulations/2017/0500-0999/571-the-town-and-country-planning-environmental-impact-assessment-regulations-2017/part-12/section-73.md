# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017, section 73

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Amendment of the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) Order 2015

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Article 3 of the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) Order 2015 [^c23017001] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

(2) In paragraph (10)—

- (a) for “the Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2011” substitute “ the Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017 ”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) after each reference to “EIA development” insert “ within the meaning of those Regulations ”.

(3) In paragraph (11) after “EIA development” in each place where it occurs in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) insert “ within the meaning of those Regulations ”.

(4) In paragraphs (10) and (11)—

- (a) for “regulation 5” substitute “ regulation 6 ”;
- (b) for “regulation 4(7) or 6(4)” substitute “ regulation 5(3) ”; and
- (c) for “regulation 4(4)” substitute “ regulation 63(1)(a) ”.
