# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2017, section 70

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. Application to the Crown

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations shall apply to the Crown with the following modifications.

(2) In regulation 13 (application referred to the Secretary of State without an environmental statement)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) before “referred” insert “ made or ”; and
  - (ii) before “referral” insert “ making or the ”; and
- (b) in paragraph (3), for “application referred” substitute “ application made or referred ”.
