# The National Grid (Richborough Connection Project) Development Consent Order 2017, section 19

> Text as at 2018-05-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Compulsory acquisition of right: incorporation of the mineral code[^key-8b60a0a2a0f75336ed13452e7adaf5c8]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00020] (minerals) are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” there is substituted “the undertaker”.
