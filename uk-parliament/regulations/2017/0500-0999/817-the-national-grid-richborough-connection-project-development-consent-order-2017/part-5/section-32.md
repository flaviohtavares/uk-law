## 32. Statutory undertakers[^c-d51ea1393d78e1c0c9bb01de595c3726]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to the provisions of Schedule 14 (Protective Provisions), the undertaker may—

- (a) acquire compulsorily interests, rights or impose restrictions over, the land belonging to statutory undertakers shown on the land plans within the Order limits and described in the book of reference; and
- (b) extinguish the rights of, or remove or reposition the apparatus belonging to statutory undertakers over or within the Order land.
