# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 690

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2017

Made

14th June 2017

Coming into force

15th June 2017

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of June 2017

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2017 and comes into force on 15th June 2017.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council
