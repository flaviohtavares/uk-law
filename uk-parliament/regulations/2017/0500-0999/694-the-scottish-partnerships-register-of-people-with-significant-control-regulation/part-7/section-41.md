# The Scottish Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2017, section 41

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Restriction on ... disclosure of URA information by the registrar[^key-68c3467a584966e8d58caa4554ec0275]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The registrar must omit URA information from the material on the register that is available for inspection where—

- (a) it is contained in a document delivered to the registrar in which such information is required to be stated, and
- (b) in the case of a document having more than one part, it is contained in a part of the document in which such information is required to be stated.

(2) The registrar is not obliged to check other documents or (as the case may be) other parts of the document to ensure the absence of URA information.

(3) The registrar must not disclose[^key-f7954db118c08d1ae46816f5c7b7bfeb] URA information except—

- (a)[^key-68f2bb91e3cdedf02a75c0a727d4f2d0] in accordance with section 1110F (disclosure by the registrar) of the Companies Act 2006, or
- (b) in accordance with regulation 43 (disclosure under court order).
