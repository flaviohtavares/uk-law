# The Scottish Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2017, section 78

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Consequential amendment to the Companies Act 2006

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 790C(7) of the Companies Act 2006, after paragraph (a) insert—

> - (aa) it is an eligible Scottish partnership within the meaning of regulation 3(2) of the Scottish Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2017.
