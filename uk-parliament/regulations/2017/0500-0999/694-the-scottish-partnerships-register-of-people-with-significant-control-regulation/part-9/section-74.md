# The Scottish Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2017, section 74

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 1132 (production and inspection of documents where offence suspected) of the Companies Act 2006 applies, with the following modifications—

- (a) for each occurrence of “a company” and “the company” substitute “ an eligible Scottish partnership ” or “the eligible Scottish partnership”, as the case may be; and
- (b) for “secretary of the company, or such other officer of it” substitute “ such officer of the eligible Scottish partnership ”.
