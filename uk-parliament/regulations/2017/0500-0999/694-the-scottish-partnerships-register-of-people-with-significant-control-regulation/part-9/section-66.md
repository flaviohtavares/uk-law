# The Scottish Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2017, section 66

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 1090 (form and manner in which copies to be provided) of the Companies Act 2006 applies in relation to information delivered to the registrar by an eligible Scottish partnership under these Regulations, modified so that it reads as follows—

> **1090. Form and manner in which copies to be provided**
>
> The registrar may determine the form and manner in which copies are to be provided under section 1086.
