# The Scottish Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2017, section 77

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Interpretation of this Part

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The provisions of this Part have effect together with any other provisions of the Companies Act 2006 having effect for the purposes of those provisions.

(2) Subject to paragraph (1), a term used in a provision of the Companies Act 2006 as applied by this Part which has a meaning given elsewhere in these Regulations has that meaning in this Part.
