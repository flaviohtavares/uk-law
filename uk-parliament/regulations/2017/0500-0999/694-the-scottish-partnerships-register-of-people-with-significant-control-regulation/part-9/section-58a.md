# The Scottish Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2017, section 58A

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**58A.**[^c-98915dc7e574996a8929b3d5b503fb64] Section 1080(2) of the Companies Act 2006 applies to eligible Scottish partnerships, modified so that it reads as follows—

> (2) The records relating to eligible Scottish partnerships are referred to collectively as “**the register**”.

.
