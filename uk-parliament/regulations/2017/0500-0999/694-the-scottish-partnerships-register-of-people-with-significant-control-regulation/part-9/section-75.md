# The Scottish Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2017, section 75

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Application of Part 37 of the Companies Act 2006

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 1156 (meaning of “the court”) of the Companies Act 2006 applies, modified so that for every occurrence of “the Companies Acts” substitute “ the Scottish Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2017 ”.
