# The Scottish Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2017, section 5

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Duty on Scottish qualifying partnerships to register

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A Scottish qualifying partnership must deliver to the registrar the information set out in regulation 6, referred to in these Regulations as the “**registration information**”, together with the information required by Part 5 (duty to deliver information to the registrar) within the period of 14 days beginning with—

- (a) 24th July 2017, if it is a qualifying partnership on that day;
- (b) any day after 24th July 2017 on which it becomes a qualifying partnership, either as a result of—
  - (i) its formation,
  - (ii) a change in its partners, or
  - (iii) a change in the partners in its partners.
