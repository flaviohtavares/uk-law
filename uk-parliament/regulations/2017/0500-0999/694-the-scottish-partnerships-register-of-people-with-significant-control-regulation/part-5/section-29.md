# The Scottish Partnerships (Register of People with Significant Control) Regulations 2017, section 29

> Text as at 2025-11-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Additional matters where a notice given under regulation 10 or 11 is complied with after the time specified in the notice

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where—

- (a) a statement has been delivered to the registrar under regulation 27 or 28, and
- (b) the addressee of the notice to which the statement relates has complied with the notice after the time specified in the notice.

(2)[^c-8da8d6c2dde8fe1a35c834f4fa0c1ff6] The eligible Scottish partnership must within the period of 14 days beginning with the day it becomes aware that the notice has been complied with deliver to the registrar a statement—

- (a) that the notice has been complied with after the time specified in the notice, and
- (b) providing the date on which the notice was complied with.
