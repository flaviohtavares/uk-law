# The East Anglia THREE Offshore Wind Farm Order 2017, section 3

> Text as at 2022-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Development consent etc. granted by the Order[^c-945ac06f0a0c33bc16b44906f7e6e23a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Order and to the requirements the undertaker is granted—

- (a) development consent for the authorised development; and
- (b) consent for the ancillary works,

to be carried out within the Order limits.

(2) Subject to the requirements, Work Nos. 1 to 5A must be constructed within the Order limits seaward of MHWS and Work Nos. 5B to 69 must be constructed within the Order limits landward of MLWS.
