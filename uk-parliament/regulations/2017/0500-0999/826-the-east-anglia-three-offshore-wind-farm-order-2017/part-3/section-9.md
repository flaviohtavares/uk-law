# The East Anglia THREE Offshore Wind Farm Order 2017, section 9

> Text as at 2022-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9. Temporary stopping up of public rights of way[^key-6ce7df3647717aeef9590e7ee459e352][^key-bfd4376aad39f55830998b7b845460e2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker may, in connection with the carrying out of the authorised project, temporarily stop up each of the public rights of way specified in columns (2) of Schedule 3 (public rights of way to be temporarily stopped up) to the extent specified in column (3), by reference to the letters shown on the temporary stopping up of public rights of way plan.
