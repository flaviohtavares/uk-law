# The East Anglia THREE Offshore Wind Farm Order 2017, section 28

> Text as at 2022-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Deemed marine licences under the 2009 Act[^c-cfcb5dff063730f74233f311de181b2e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The marine licences set out in Schedules 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 15 are deemed to have been granted under Part 4 of the 2009 Act (marine licensing) for the licensed marine activities set out in Part 1, and subject to the conditions set out in Part 2, of each licence.
