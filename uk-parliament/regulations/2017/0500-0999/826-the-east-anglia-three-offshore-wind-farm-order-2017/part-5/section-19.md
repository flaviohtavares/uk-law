# The East Anglia THREE Offshore Wind Farm Order 2017, section 19

> Text as at 2022-11-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 19. Application of the Compulsory Purchase (Vesting Declarations) Act 1981[^c-d3e135565dae6a534b9a7edd3a408562]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The 1981 Act applies as if this Order were a compulsory purchase order.

(2) The 1981 Act, as so applied, has effect with the following modifications.

(3) Section 5 (earliest date for execution of declaration) is omitted.

(4) Section 5A (time limit for general vesting declaration) is omitted[^f00027].

(5) References to the 1965 Act in the 1981 Act must be construed as references to that Act as applied by section 125 of the 2008 Act to the compulsory acquisition of land under this Order.
