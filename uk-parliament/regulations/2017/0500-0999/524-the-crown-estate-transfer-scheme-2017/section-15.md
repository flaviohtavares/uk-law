# The Crown Estate Transfer Scheme 2017, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Publicity

No later than one month after the transfer date, the transferee must publish notice of the transfer in the Edinburgh Gazette and the London Gazette in such form as the transferee considers appropriate.
