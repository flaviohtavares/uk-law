# The Crown Estate Transfer Scheme 2017, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Scottish assets

Schedule 1 (Scottish assets) specifies the property, rights and interests that as at the transfer date appear to the Treasury to be the Scottish assets.
