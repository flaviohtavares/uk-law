# The Crown Estate Transfer Scheme 2017, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Designated rights and designated liabilities

The rights and liabilities specified in Schedule 2 (designated rights and designated liabilities) transfer to the transferee on the transfer date.
