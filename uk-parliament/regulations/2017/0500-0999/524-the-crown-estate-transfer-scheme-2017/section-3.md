# The Crown Estate Transfer Scheme 2017, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Transfer date and transferee

This Scheme transfers the existing Scottish functions to Crown Estate Scotland (Interim Management) on the day on which it comes into force.
