# The Crown Estate Transfer Scheme 2017, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Scheme may be cited as the Crown Estate Transfer Scheme 2017.

(2) This Scheme comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(3) Subject to sub-paragraph (4), this Scheme extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(4) An amendment made by Schedule 5 has the same extent as the enactment to which it relates.
