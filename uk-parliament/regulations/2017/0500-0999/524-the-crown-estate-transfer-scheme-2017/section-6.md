# The Crown Estate Transfer Scheme 2017, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Protection of employment

Schedule 3 (protection of employment) makes provision to ensure that the employment of any person in Crown employment is not adversely affected by the transfer.
