# The Crown Estate Transfer Scheme 2017, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Protection of UK-wide interests

Schedule 4 (protection of UK-wide interests) makes provision in relation to defence and national security, telecommunications, the exploitation of oil and gas and electricity infrastructure.
