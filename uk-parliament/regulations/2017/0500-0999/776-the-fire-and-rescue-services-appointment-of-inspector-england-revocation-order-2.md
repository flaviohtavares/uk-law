# The Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) (England) (Revocation) Order 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 776

Fire And Rescue Services, England

The Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) (England) (Revocation) Order 2017

Made

19th July 2017

Coming into force

26th July 2017

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 19th day of July 2017

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 28(1) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) (England) (Revocation) Order 2017 and comes into force on 26th July 2017.

## 2. Revocation of appointment

The Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) (England) Order 2013[^f00002] is revoked.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the appointment of Peter Holland as an inspector under section 28(1) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
