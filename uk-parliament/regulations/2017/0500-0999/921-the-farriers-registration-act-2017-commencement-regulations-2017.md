# The Farriers (Registration) Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 921 (C. 77)

Farriers Registration Council

The Farriers (Registration) Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2017

Made

14th September 2017

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 3(2) of the Farriers (Registration) Act 2017[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Farriers (Registration) Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2017.

## 2. Commencement

The day appointed for the coming into force of sections 1 and 2 of, and the Schedule to, the Farriers (Registration) Act 2017 is 15th September 2017.

Gardiner of Kimble

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

14th September 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force in Great Britain on 15th September 2017 sections 1 and 2 of, and the Schedule to, the Farriers (Registration) Act 2017. Section 3 of the Act came into force in Great Britain on 27th April 2017. The Act is now fully in force.
