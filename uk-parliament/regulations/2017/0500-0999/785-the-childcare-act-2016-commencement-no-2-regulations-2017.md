# The Childcare Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 785 (C. 63)

Children And Young Persons, England

The Childcare Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2017

Made

19th July 2017

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(2) of the Childcare Act 2016[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Childcare Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2017.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1st September 2017

Subsections (1) and (6) of section 1 of the Childcare Act 2016 (*duty to secure 30 hours free childcare available for working parents*) come into force on 1st September 2017.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Education

Robert Goodwill

Minister of State

Department for Education

19th July 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made under the Childcare Act [2016 (c.5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/5) (“The Act”). These Regulations bring into force, on 1st September 2017, those provisions of the Act which are not already in force. The provisions are sub-sections (1) and (6) of section 1 of the Act which relate to the Secretary of State’s duty to secure 30 hours of free childcare for working parents.

Certain provisions came into force on the passing of the Act (see section 7(1)) and all remaining provisions (other than those commenced by these Regulations) came into force on 3rd November 2016 pursuant to the Childcare Act 2016 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/1055](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/1055)).
