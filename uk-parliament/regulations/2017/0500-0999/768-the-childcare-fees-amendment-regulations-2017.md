# The Childcare (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

The Childcare (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

Made

18th July 2017

Laid before Parliament

20th July 2017

Coming into force

1st September 2017

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 89(1) and 104(2) of the Childcare Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Childcare (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2017 and come into force on 1st September 2017.

## 2. Amendment to Childcare (Fees) Regulations 2008

In paragraph (7) of regulation 10 (annual fee for other early years providers registered in the early years register) of the Childcare (Fees) Regulations 2008[^f00002], for “2017” substitute “2019”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made under the Childcare Act [2006 (c.21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/21) (“the 2006 Act”). These Regulations amend regulation 10(7) of the Childcare (Fees) Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1804](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1804)). The effect of the amendment is to extend an existing transitional provision, which applies to childcare providers (other than childminders) who transferred automatically from the childcare register under Part 10A of the Children Act [1989 (c.41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41) to the early years register under the 2006 Act on 1st September 2008. The transitional provision means that the threshold between the annual fee rates set out in regulation 10(2) and (3) is based on a period of 4 hours (rather than 3 hours). This amendment has the effect of extending the transitional provision until 31st August 2019, enabling early years providers who were registered prior to 2008 to benefit from a fees rate of £50 where they are operating for a period of less than 4 hours per day.
