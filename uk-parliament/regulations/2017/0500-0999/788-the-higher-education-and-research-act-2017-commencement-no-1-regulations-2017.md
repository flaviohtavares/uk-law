# The Higher Education and Research Act 2017 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 788 (C. 64)

Education, England

The Higher Education and Research Act 2017 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2017

Made

20th July 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 124(5) and (6) of the Higher Education and Research Act 2017[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Higher Education and Research Act 2017 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2017.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1st January 2018

The following provisions of the Higher Education and Research Act 2017 come into force on 1st January 2018—

- (a) section 1 (the Office for Students);
- (b) Schedule 1 (the Office for Students).

Joseph Johnson

Minister of State

Department for Education

20th July 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are the first commencement regulations made under the Higher Education and Research Act 2017 (“the Act”). They bring section 1 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Act into force on 1st January 2018. Section 1 provides for the establishment of the Office for Students (“OfS”). Schedule 1 makes further provision about the OfS, including its membership, staffing and procedure, its accounts, audit and reporting responsibilities and its supplementary powers. Schedule 1 also amends the Public Records Act [1958 (c. 51)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1958/51), the Parliamentary Commissioner Act [1967 (c. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1967/13), the House of Commons Disqualification Act [1975 (c. 24)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/24), the Northern Ireland Assembly Disqualification Act [1975 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/25), the Freedom of Information Act [2000 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/36) and the Equality Act [2010 (c. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15). The amendments add the OfS to the list of bodies subject to provisions of those Acts.
