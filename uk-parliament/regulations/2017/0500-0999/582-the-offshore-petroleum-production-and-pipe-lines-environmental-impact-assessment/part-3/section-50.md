# The Offshore Petroleum Production and Pipe-lines (Environmental Impact Assessment and other Miscellaneous Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2017, section 50

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Amendment of regulation 5 (pre-application opinion by the Secretary of State as to content of environmental statement)[^c-52882762451b401e1c317d48ae36d843]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In regulation 5—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “the information to be provided”, substitute “the scope and level of detail to be included by the applicant”; and
- (b) in paragraph (2)(a)—
  - (i) after “into account”, insert “on the information provided”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (a)(i), after “works”, insert “including location and technical capacity”, and
  - (iii) omit paragraph (iv).
