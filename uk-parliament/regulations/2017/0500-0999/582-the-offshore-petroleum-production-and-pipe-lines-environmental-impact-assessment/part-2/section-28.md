# The Offshore Petroleum Production and Pipe-lines (Environmental Impact Assessment and other Miscellaneous Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2017, section 28

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Amendment of regulation 3 (environmental statements)[^key-fda20139e6ec3926ca8f47d107ab4da2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 3—

- (a) in paragraph (1), after “(5) and regulations”, insert “3A (exempt pipe-line works),”;
- (b) after paragraph (5), insert—

  > (5A) Where in relation to EIA development there is, in addition to a requirement for an environmental statement to be prepared in accordance with these Regulations, also a requirement to carry out a Habitats Regulations Assessment, the Secretary of State shall where appropriate ensure that the preparation of that assessment and the environmental statement are coordinated.

  ; and
- (c) after paragraph (6), insert—

  > (7) In this regulation, a “Habitats Regulations Assessment” means an assessment under regulation 61 of the Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2010 in respect of the proposed pipe-line works.

  .
