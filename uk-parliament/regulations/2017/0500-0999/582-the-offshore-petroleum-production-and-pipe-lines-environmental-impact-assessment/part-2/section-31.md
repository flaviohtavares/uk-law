# The Offshore Petroleum Production and Pipe-lines (Environmental Impact Assessment and other Miscellaneous Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2017, section 31

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Amendment of regulation 7 (pre-application requests to the Secretary of State for an opinion as to content of environmental statement)[^key-c97c3c282f4a3b02e56e435da99d3dec]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 7—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “the information to be provided” substitute “the scope and level of detail to be included by the gas transporter”; and
- (b) in paragraph (2)(a)—
  - (i) after “into account”, insert “on the information provided”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (i), after “works”, insert “including its location and technical capacity”, and
  - (iii) omit paragraph (iv).
