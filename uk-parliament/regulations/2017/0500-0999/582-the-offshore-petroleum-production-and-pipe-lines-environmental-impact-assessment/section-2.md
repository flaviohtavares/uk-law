# The Offshore Petroleum Production and Pipe-lines (Environmental Impact Assessment and other Miscellaneous Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2017, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-890fa83b1713fc2ab2aa41ef71ef2219]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “the 1999 Offshore Regulations” means the Offshore Petroleum Production and Pipe-lines (Assessment of Environmental Effects) Regulations 1999[^f00006];
- “the 1999 Pipe-line Regulations” means the Public Gas Transporter Pipe-line Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 1999[^f00007]; and
- “the 2000 Regulations” means the Pipe-line Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2000[^f00008].
