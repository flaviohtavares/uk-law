## 49. Conformity assessments carried out under Module B (EU-type examination)[^c-d5efb2df7d18fb1d23295f2b4824452b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0] Where an economic operator has a conformity assessment carried out on a product under Module B (EU-type examination) of Annex *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0] II *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0] to *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0] Decision 768/2008 *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0]/ *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0]EC *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0] in relation to a product, the conformity assessment must be carried out in the manner set out in Schedule 10. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0] A production type may cover several versions of the assessed product, if— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0] the differences between the versions of the product do not affect the level of safety and the other requirements concerning the performance of the product; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0] the different versions of the product are referred to in the corresponding EU-type examination certificate, if necessary by means of amendments to the original certificate. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0] For the purposes of this regulation— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0]

- “production type” means an examination of the adequacy of the technical design of the watercraft through examination of the technical documentation specified in paragraph 3 of Schedule 10 and a specimen watercraft that is representative of the completed watercraft that is to be or has been produced by the manufacturer. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef3155ee32a7a0fd6db77f6b54f40da0]
