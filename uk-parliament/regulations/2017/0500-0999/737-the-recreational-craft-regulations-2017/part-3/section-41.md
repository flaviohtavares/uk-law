# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Presumption of conformity[^key-12670231f3c374f20e69238889c21b77][^M_E_f1dd6d15-62a6-4bf3-a6ef-5b13143876f4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A product which is in conformity with a designated[^key-404ab8ee4a2a54d3d4c70d0562f6286f] standard (or part of such a standard) [^key-32a5144087d97c1871437e7998830b75]... is to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.

## 41. Presumption of conformity[^V_NI_ea422919-6b3d-1435-bedf-e5046ad6b1ec][^M_E_0292a6f5-8b18-4038-a824-47caddc439d9]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A product which is in conformity with a harmonised standard (or part of such a standard) the reference to which has been published in the Official Journal of the European Union is to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.
