# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 54

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. UK marking[^key-31db2365f2e1dfe26d98aa1b9a416235][^M_E_3ed1d1ea-3bb9-45a6-cafd-334baf4c4d88][^key-d0e8257c8a1f2c588f9f6b053506ba4d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-793afd7ebf12c4be2883884456939242] The UK marking must be affixed visibly, legibly and indelibly—

- (a) to the product; or
- (b) where paragraph (1A) applies, to—
  - (i) a label affixed to the product; or
  - (ii) a document accompanying the product.

(1A)[^key-a2519228f2acce5b59411c148baee2a8] For a period of seven years[^key-6e701baa69690ff2eb7f02d73fad8e10] beginning with IP completion day, the UK marking may be affixed to—

- (a) a label affixed to the product; or
- (b) a document accompanying the product.

(2) Where paragraph (1A) does not apply and[^key-f7a45c2617a88ddeecb80edc4fd384c1] it is not possible or warranted, on account of the nature of a component to affix the UK[^key-9b4dfb51865bffd611b18271c83cf8f0] marking in accordance with paragraph (1)(a)[^key-b224390d9ed4e4778de80880f8f93f44], the UK marking must be affixed to—

- (a) the packaging; and
- (b) the accompanying documents.

(3) Except where paragraph (3A) applies[^key-763445596175c80848217cd20fa24fb4] where the product referred to in paragraph (1)—

- (a) is a watercraft, the UK[^key-50a35ff11de356db0b520e9f534bff4d] marking must be affixed to the watercraft builder’s plate and mounted separately from the watercraft identification number; or
- (b) is a propulsion engine, the UK marking must be affixed on the engine.

(3A)[^key-b80c04fb1608787891e0e182aa6417da] For a period of 24 months beginning with IP completion day, the UK marking may be affixed to—

- (a) a label affixed to the watercraft or propulsion engine; or
- (b) a document accompanying the watercraft or propulsion engine.

(4) The UK[^key-b74349ecab2227c8673c02c454014512] marking must be followed by the identification number of the approved[^key-3366e213daa0acc01d8d21157c82f0e0] body where—

- (a) that body is involved in the production control phase of the conformity assessment under Modules A (internal production control), A1 (internal production control plus supervised product testing) or C (conformity to type based on internal production control) set out in Schedule 15[^key-f509f1e928b4bf10e8fbc9a5f576f1a7]; or
- (b) where a post construction assessment set out in Schedule 5 of these Regulations is being used.

(5) The identification number of the approved[^key-b9c8d704a222484a2e938ad61316787b] body must be affixed—

- (a) by the approved body itself; or
- (b) under the instructions of the approved body by the manufacturer.

## 54. CE marking[^V_NI_74d0dd3b-ac75-34aa-fc30-f0c70d710d0d][^M_E_4e5aa607-60e5-4546-db4e-5b8448494521]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The CE marking must be affixed visibly, legibly and indelibly to the product.

(2) Where it is not possible or warranted, on account of the nature of a component to affix the CE marking in accordance with paragraph (1), the CE marking must be affixed to—

- (a) the packaging; and
- (b) the accompanying documents.

(3) Where the product referred to in paragraph (1)—

- (a) is a watercraft, the CE marking must be affixed to the watercraft builder’s plate and mounted separately from the watercraft identification number; or
- (b) is a propulsion engine, the CE marking must be affixed on the engine.

(4) The CE marking must be followed by the identification number of the notified body where—

- (a) that body is involved in the production control phase of the conformity assessment under Modules A (internal production control), A1 (internal production control plus supervised product testing) or C (conformity to type based on internal production control) set out in Annex II of Decision 768/2008; or
- (b) where a post construction assessment set out in Schedule 5 of these Regulations is being used.

(5) The identification number of the notified body must be affixed—

- (a) by the notified body itself; or
- (b) under the instructions of the notified body by the manufacturer.
