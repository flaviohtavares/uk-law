# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Conformity assessments carried out under Module F (conformity to type based on product verification)[^key-290bc9da0dbbd88f41fe9f0fa13315bd][^M_E_8e0c0eee-92e3-4cfb-87bc-c67ed4169c46]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where an economic operator has a conformity assessment carried out on a product under Module F (conformity to type based on product verification) as set out in Schedule 15[^key-fe41a2afbff8d24513f8cf191469b9ef], the procedure set out in Schedule 7 applies.

## 51. Conformity assessments carried out under Module F (conformity to type based on product verification)[^V_NI_e40b0603-d625-6ce8-227c-ff59b066928f][^M_E_a19425fa-af80-4390-f2c9-9769d5c03817]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where an economic operator has a conformity assessment carried out on a product under Module F (conformity to type based on product verification) of Annex II to Decision 768/2008/EC, the procedure set out in Schedule 7 applies.
