# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Applicable conformity assessment procedures[^key-2944f5152b237dc7a234b5b2b91b2045][^M_E_017b7d98-4518-4255-ab29-e484194e5993]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Before placing a product on the market the manufacturer must apply the conformity assessment procedures set out in the modules set out in Schedule 15[^key-d90922e2e0fd728f21505e2683bb7948] and referred to in regulations 44 (design and construction) to 47 (noise emissions: personal watercraft).

## 42. Applicable conformity assessment procedures[^V_NI_a92f4ac0-1173-d711-6907-b3f1b4a9461a][^M_E_fb344192-d534-4083-c15b-edec207475d2]

*Extent: N.I.*

Before placing a product on the market the manufacturer must apply the conformity assessment procedures set out in the modules set out in Annex II of Decision 768/2008 and referred to in regulations 44 (design and construction) to 47 (noise emissions: personal watercraft).
