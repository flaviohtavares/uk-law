# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, SCHEDULE 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4 — (Annex IV of the Directive) — EU (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note) DECLARATION OF CONFORMITY No xxxxx[^c-528a75a7991dd73dd3020f8936ac83c6][^c-c6afe599da922fdd21f35a78d6136e01]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 10

- No xxxxx (Product: product, batch, type, or serial number):
- Name and address of the manufacturer or his authorised representative \[The authorised representative must also give the business name and address of the manufacturer\] or the private importer.
- This declaration of conformity is issued under the sole responsibility of the manufacturer or the private importer or the person referred to in Article 19(3) or (4) of Directive 2013/53/EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e7ca89cbecc63db22ca1976f6c4896c0]regulation 43(2) or (3) of the Recreational Craft Regulations 2017 (S.I. 2017/737, “the Regulations”) *(Extent: E+W+S)*.
- Object of the declaration (identification of product allowing traceability. It may include a photograph, where appropriate):
- The object of the declaration described in point 4 is in conformity with the relevant Union harmonisation legislation *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-93c9b11dcaaf4ad311548bf4ab3a7b4d] statutory requirements *(Extent: E+W+S)*:
- References to the relevant harmonised *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd807771908e8609a77c5e4264ba108] designated *(Extent: E+W+S)* standards used or references to the other technical specifications in relation to which conformity is declared:
- Where applicable, the notified *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-96fc55de9d74fd395d53f1569b14caac] approved *(Extent: E+W+S)* body … (name, number) performed … (description of intervention) and issued the certificate:
- Identification of the person empowered to sign on behalf of the manufacturer or his authorised representative
- Additional information:

  The EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccee9ffae49760b2fcc99f090fcb34ad] declaration of conformity shall include a statement of the propulsion engine manufacturer and that of the person adapting an engine in accordance with points (b) and (c) of Article 6(4) *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-efc5207faeb5a572b9aa23e76d805498] regulation 7(1)(c)(iii) *(Extent: E+W+S)* that:

  - ((a)) when installed in a watercraft, in accordance with the installation instructions accompanying the engine, the engine will meet:
    - ((i)) the exhaust emission requirements of this Directive *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-425f60d0dc96a7b92367a26f63b913ad] the Regulations *(Extent: E+W+S)*;
    - ((ii)) the limits of Directive 97/68/EC as regards engines type-approved in accordance with Directive 97/68/EC which are in compliance with stage III A, stage III B or stage IV emission limits for CI engines used in other applications than propulsion of inland waterway vessels, locomotives and railcars, as provided for in point 4.1.2 of Annex I to that Directive; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4ac9200d31a893d67d4c0814c22a27a0] or
    - ((iii)) the limits of Regulation (EC) No 595/2009 as regards engines type-approved in accordance with that Regulation.

  The engine must not be put into service until the watercraft into which it is to be installed has been declared in conformity, if so required, with the relevant provision of this Directive *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-425f60d0dc96a7b92367a26f63b913ad] the Regulations *(Extent: E+W+S)*.

  If the engine has been placed on the market during the additional transitional period provided for in Article 55(2) *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1a03a0d1296dd7da25523f418b60def1] regulation 89(2) *(Extent: E+W+S)*, the EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccee9ffae49760b2fcc99f090fcb34ad] declaration of conformity shall contain an indication thereof.

  Signed for and on behalf of:

  (place and date of issue)

  (name, function) (signature)
