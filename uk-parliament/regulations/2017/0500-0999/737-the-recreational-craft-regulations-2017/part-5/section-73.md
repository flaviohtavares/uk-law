# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 73

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Offences[^key-3873f1fea224c77eaa30649ae5ef6105]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It is an offence for a person to contravene or fail to comply with any requirement of—

- (a) regulation 6;
- (b) regulations 8 to 16;
- (c) regulation 17(3);
- (d)[^key-bce6ab63947e30bdb5a71e0fc9658bb9] regulations 18 to 25;
- (e) regulation 26(3);
- (f) regulations 27 to 31;
- (g) regulation 32(3);
- (h) regulation 34;
- (i) regulation 36 to 38; or
- (j) regulation 40.

(2) It is an offence for any person to contravene or fail to comply with any requirement of a withdrawal or recall notice served on that person by an enforcing authority under these Regulations.
