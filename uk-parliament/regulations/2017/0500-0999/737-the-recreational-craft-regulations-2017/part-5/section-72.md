## 72. Restrictive measures[^key-e8107b1d2fe4934f7ea0dac290e38a2a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

When enforcing these Regulations, an enforcing authority must comply with the requirements of Article 21 of RAMS (as amended from time to time) in relation to any measure to—

- (a) prohibit or restrict a product from being made available on the market;
- (b) withdraw a product from the market; or
- (c) recall a product.
