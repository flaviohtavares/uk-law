## 65. Designation of market surveillance authorities[^key-ad67aa063f23b04e516dd1e7965321b5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The market surveillance authority is—

- (a) within its area in Great Britain, the weights and measures authority; and
- (b) in Northern Ireland, every district council.
