## 74. Penalties[^c-e6c9c478b3f193d5d98568b4e94e4bed]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to regulation 74A, *(Extent: N.I.)*[^c-16b3cc7953911bbde1fc00ef985d8d0f] any person who is guilty of an offence under regulation 73 (offences) is liable on summary conviction—

- (a) in England and Wales—
  - (i) to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months;
  - (ii) to a fine; or
  - (iii) to both.
- (b) in Scotland and Northern Ireland—
  - (i) to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months; or
  - (ii) to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale; or
  - (iii) to both.
