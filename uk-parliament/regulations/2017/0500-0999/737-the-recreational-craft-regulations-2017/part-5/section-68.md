# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 68

## 68. Evaluation of a product presenting a risk[^key-e271d0b5e165d5acbf1f61ce4b15d823]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the market surveillance authority has sufficient reason to believe that a product presents a risk, that authority must carry out an evaluation of that product in order to determine whether the product satisfies the requirements of Part 2 of these Regulations in respect of that product.

(2) Where an enforcing authority other than a market surveillance authority has sufficient reason to believe that a product presents a risk, that authority may carry out an evaluation of that product in order to determine whether the product satisfied the requirements of Part 2 of these Regulations in respect of that product.
