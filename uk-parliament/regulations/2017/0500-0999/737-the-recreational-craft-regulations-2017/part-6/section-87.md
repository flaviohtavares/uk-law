# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Amendment to the Co-ordination of Regulatory Enforcement (Regulatory Functions in Scotland and Northern Ireland) Order 2009[^key-61d7a2bf50efb6b8dd1091a8ff154d45]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Co-ordination of Regulatory Enforcement (Regulatory Functions in Scotland and Northern Ireland) Order[^f00019] is amended as follows—

- (a) in Part 4 of Schedule 1 omit the entry “The Recreational Craft Regulations 2004” ; and at the end insert “The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017”; and
- (b) in Part 2 of Schedule 2 omit the entry “The Recreational Craft Regulations 2004” and at the end insert “The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017”.
