# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 85

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Amendment to the Enterprise Act 2002 (Part 9 Restrictions on Disclosure of Information) (Specification) Order 2004[^c-79005ace164baa1e1d6416db1186321a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Enterprise Act 2002 (Part 9 Restrictions on Disclosure of Information) (Specification) Order 2004[^f00017] Schedule 1 (specified subordinate legislation) is amended as follows—

- (a) “The Recreational Craft Regulations 1996 insofar as not revoked by the Recreational Craft Regulations 2004” substitute “The Recreational Craft Regulations 2004 insofar as not revoked by the Recreational Craft Regulations 2017”; and
- (b) omit the entry “The Recreational Craft Regulations 2004” and at the appropriate place insert “The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017”.
