# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, SCHEDULE 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737

## SCHEDULE 8 — (Annex VIII of the Directive) — SUPPLEMENTARY PROCEDURE TO BE APPLIED UNDER CONFORMITY TO TYPE BASED ON INTERNAL PRODUCTION CONTROL (MODULE C)[^key-12fdee73fce2c03d12bf79f9ef32f0b1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 52

In the cases referred to in Article 24(5) *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1916a330f9613e82578c4a40216815ed] regulation 52 *(Extent: E+W+S)* when the quality level appears unsatisfactory, the following procedure shall apply:

An engine is taken from the series and subjected to the test described in Part B of Annex I *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dbc166e6c8af7af2ec6447c25128127c] Schedule 1 *(Extent: E+W+S)*. Test engines shall have been run in, partially or completely, in accordance with the manufacturer’s specifications. If the specific exhaust emissions of the engine taken from the series exceed the limit values in accordance with Part B of Annex I *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dbc166e6c8af7af2ec6447c25128127c] Schedule 1 *(Extent: E+W+S)*, the manufacturer may ask for measurements to be done on a sample of engines taken from the series and including the engine originally taken. To ensure the conformity of the sample of engines with the requirements of this Directive *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8ad9e89810961dcf0b6a8dcc8968efc7] these Regulations *(Extent: E+W+S)*, the statistical method described in Annex VII *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-576c93e488bb02e18d22de677b7f7625] Schedule 7 *(Extent: E+W+S)* shall be applied.
