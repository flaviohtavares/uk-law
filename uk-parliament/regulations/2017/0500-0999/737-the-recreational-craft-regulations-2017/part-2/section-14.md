# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Duty to provide information[^key-d345702688b577ce511303b9783fc4a6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before placing a product on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that the product is labelled with—

- (a) the manufacturer’s name;
- (b) their registered trade name or registered trade mark; and
- (c) an address, which is the single point at which they can be contacted.

(2)[^key-06b6c5a89712010eeb2735628efacbae] Where it is not possible to provide the information referred to in paragraph (1) on the product, the manufacturer must ensure that the information is provided—

- (a) on the packaging; or
- (b) in a document accompanying the product.
