## 22. Instructions and safety information[^c-b25bbcf81981791703c930468eca1d44][^M_E_d071fc92-a887-4810-99cd-cdc72d858b14]

(1) When placing a product on the market, an importer must ensure that it is accompanied by instructions and safety information in English[^c-1f75789e0e357877cdf2e94d9f1b326c].

## 22. Instructions and safety information[^V_NI_704d4378-a3fc-d363-e96a-eaed630d4a76][^M_E_e1ecb5e6-4b5c-4164-d88b-b2ea8becec0e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) When placing a product on the market, an importer must ensure that it is accompanied by instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by consumers and other end-users in the relevant state[^c-360dc36f9764abb43e57e4060c9e7285] in which the product is to be made available.

(2) When the product is being made available to consumers and other end-users in Northern Ireland[^c-f319f3a6b011f957b1cd7ca4bd090534], the language which can be easily understood by consumers and other end-users is English.
