## 15. Instructions and safety information[^c-f8cfce8ef8c05b6eb0a8d35c3a96c0c3][^M_E_6e7060b1-3dbb-4ec1-bcfa-3e1ad7f57628]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) When placing a product on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that it is accompanied by instructions and safety information in English[^c-97051f70ae119598cce1d4a11048679a].

(2)[^c-b11e7aeaa6903382e28d71cf881def18] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## 15. Instructions and safety information[^V_NI_bf436e82-cfdf-3e30-b459-40a53798ec4a][^M_E_b0550470-d4ef-4b83-875d-62f1ccf575da]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) When placing a product on the market, a manufacturer must ensure that it is accompanied by instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by consumers and other end-users in the relevant state[^c-9ec00ab9ea0821775b669a5adef707dc] in which the product is to be made available.

(2) When the product is being made available to consumers and other end-users in Northern Ireland[^c-1d17ad9f99e6e7ec1ada27997de4883d], that language which can be easily understood is English.
