# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Duty of manufacturers to retain technical documentation and EU (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note) declaration of conformity[^c-f31c1ad142980d0e543d0b6428cb06a2][^c-c50424a22e5aab41cfd0a65346659f4b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A manufacturer must keep the technical documentation and the EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-667c0f0c32cd3ad56bde6eeda4d05660] declaration of conformity drawn up in respect of a product for a period of 10 years beginning with the day on which the product is placed on the market.
