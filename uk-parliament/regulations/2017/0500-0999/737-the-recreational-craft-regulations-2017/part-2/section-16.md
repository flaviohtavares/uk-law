# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 16

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Duty to take action in respect of product placed on the market which is considered not to be in conformity[^c-9df8ed70169337db40cbdbfa0f52c3d8][^M_E_98b34add-8378-4080-c165-692d9ad9e9b8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A manufacturer who considers or has reason to believe that a product that it has placed on the market is not in conformity Part 2, must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring the product into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the product from the market; or
- (c) recall it.

(2) Where the product presents a risk, the manufacturer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority [^c-6941c5036727234d97fd91577819412d]... of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the reason why the product is not considered to be in conformity; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.

## 16. Duty to take action in respect of product placed on the market which is considered not to be in conformity[^V_NI_36134950-89fb-934a-325d-c5370b08ce13][^M_E_229aeadb-7051-4fef-eeed-0726f2e0428a]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A manufacturer who considers or has reason to believe that a product that it has placed on the market is not in conformity Part 2, must immediately take the corrective measures necessary to—

- (a) bring the product into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the product from the market; or
- (c) recall it.

(2) Where the product presents a risk, the manufacturer must immediately inform the market surveillance authority and the competent national authorities in any relevant state[^c-a3f6ee70a2e6cec474bbc2fe6ca6f387] in which the manufacturer has made the product available on the market of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the reason why the product is not considered to be in conformity; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
