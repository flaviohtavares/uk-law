# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 12

## 12. Compliance procedures for series production[^key-5f48eaf14f91a793d32621176149b94c][^M_E_fd85718d-f6de-4535-e9c6-72ec4ca14a69]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The manufacturer of a product which is manufactured by series production must ensure that, before placing the product on the market, procedures are in place to ensure that any product so manufactured will be in conformity with the manufacturer’s obligations under Part 2.

(2) In doing so, the manufacturer must take adequate account of—

- (a) any changes in the design or characteristics of a product; and
- (b) any changes in the designated[^key-f3f3e5fc12afe21d9639c093eb769ad5] standard or in another technical specification by reference to which the [^key-61b0d9a94840bdda5bbe917e40addd57]... declaration of conformity of the product was drawn up.

## 12. Compliance procedures for series production[^V_NI_b1a81226-a833-88f7-b977-cfd20fb1c983][^M_E_f97a3fe6-ef2a-4b84-dcdd-ff5151292e4e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The manufacturer of a product which is manufactured by series production must ensure that, before placing the product on the market, procedures are in place to ensure that any product so manufactured will be in conformity with the manufacturer’s obligations under Part 2.

(2) In doing so, the manufacturer must take adequate account of—

- (a) any changes in the design or characteristics of a product; and
- (b) any changes in the harmonised standard or in another technical specification by reference to which the EU declaration of conformity of the product was drawn up.
