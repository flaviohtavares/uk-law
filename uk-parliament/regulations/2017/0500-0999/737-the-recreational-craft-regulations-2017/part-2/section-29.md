# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 29

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Duty not to make a product available on the market where a distributor suspects that it is not in conformity[^key-26ddc8b7aba47719f2d023e3673aa7fc][^M_E_23b87e22-849a-4353-c7ba-163fada3f75d][^key-294ac8e665517ff658dc20a472e49334]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where a distributor considers or has reason to believe that a product is not in conformity with the essential requirements, the distributor must not make the product available[^key-f4faa33e75a667b17ceebfabc828e942] on the market.

(2) Where a product presents a risk, the distributor must inform the manufacturer or importer, and the market surveillance authority [^key-5fe90baaf5abd72887f2977c67e2e57d]... of that risk.

## 29. Duty not to make a product available on the market where a distributor suspects that it is not in conformity[^V_NI_9bbd8b75-6e17-a944-dc15-08033bc55863][^M_E_db9fc3fc-cd1a-433e-ea28-8dd8768b4d48][^V_NI_040c6a0c-222c-305a-1eec-e49d142a48c5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a distributor considers or has reason to believe that a product is not in conformity with the essential requirements, the distributor must not make the product available[^V_NI_dd1d2e80-d1ae-c00e-276f-7c40a1cb99f7] on the market.

(2) Where a product presents a risk, the distributor must inform the manufacturer or importer, and the market surveillance authority and the competent national authorities of other relevant states[^key-f00a6c55b47d1afccbedd22b654b1ed9] in which the distributor has made the product available on the market of that risk.
