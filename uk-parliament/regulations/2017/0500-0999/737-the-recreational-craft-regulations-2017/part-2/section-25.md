# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Duty of importers to retain technical documentation and EU (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note) declaration of conformity[^c-4462443ff7d421c9e567b184c1cd8158][^c-33f514e8fce267b04217681a26f55c55]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An importer must, for the period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the product is placed on the market—

- (a) keep a copy of the EU *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8c656a7a788de9f677abed6eb9f40006] declaration of conformity at the disposal of the market surveillance authorities; and
- (b) ensure that the technical documentation relating to that product can be made available to the market surveillance authorities upon request.
