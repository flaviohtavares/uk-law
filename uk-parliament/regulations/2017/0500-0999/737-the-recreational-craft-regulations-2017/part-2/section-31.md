# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Duty to take action in respect of watercraft placed on the market which is considered not to be in conformity[^key-747d83a53f0c172c47ed7a6a7dfbe983][^M_E_1cefc848-04bf-4093-d902-32cbeea7f8da]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A distributor who considers or has reason to believe that a product that the distributor has made available on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must ensure that corrective measures necessary are taken to—

- (a) bring the product into conformity;
- (b) to withdraw the product from the market; or
- (c) recall it.

(2) Where the product presents a risk, the distributor must immediately inform the market surveillance authority [^key-73926caefffc0496df6b5552033b3908]... of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the reason or reasons why the product is not considered to be in conformity; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.

## 31. Duty to take action in respect of watercraft placed on the market which is considered not to be in conformity[^V_NI_1f87ef2e-7baa-4a37-d4ad-98e5f1521199][^M_E_eabe6c02-d4df-4300-bcb0-3ca6e97ff2f5]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A distributor who considers or has reason to believe that a product that the distributor has made available on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must ensure that corrective measures necessary are taken to—

- (a) bring the product into conformity;
- (b) to withdraw the product from the market; or
- (c) recall it.

(2) Where the product presents a risk, the distributor must immediately inform the market surveillance authority and the competent national authorities of any other relevant state[^key-c4f2fdae23d0406571d9e92bd23d196b] in which the distributor has made the product available on the market of the risk, giving details of—

- (a) the reason or reasons why the product is not considered to be in conformity; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
