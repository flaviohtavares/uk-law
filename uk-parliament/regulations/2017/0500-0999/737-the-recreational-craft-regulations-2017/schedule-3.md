# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, SCHEDULE 3

## SCHEDULE 3 — (Annex III of the Directive) — DECLARATION BY THE MANUFACTURER OR THE IMPORTER OF THE PARTLY COMPLETED WATERCRAFT (ARTICLE 6(2))[^key-8abcc1a4605e41001de9abdfa4afcd0c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 7

The declaration by the manufacturer or the importer established in the Union referred to in Article 6(2) *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80fb801c5ddc2e2e863c20368bfb46ff] established in the United Kingdom referred to in regulation 7(1)(b) *(Extent: E+W+S)* shall contain the following:

- ((a)) the name and address of the manufacturer;
- ((b)) the name and address of the representative of the manufacturer established in the Union *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9a89110630a73d15d588327152942fe2] established in the United Kingdom *(Extent: E+W+S)* or, if appropriate, of the person responsible for the placing on the market;
- ((c)) a description of the partly completed watercraft;
- ((d)) a statement that the partly completed watercraft complies with the essential requirements that apply at this stage of construction; this shall include references to the relevant harmonised *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9b8237882d52412fd123dab46a44a644] designated *(Extent: E+W+S)* standards used, or references to the specifications in relation to which compliance is declared at this stage of construction; furthermore, it is intended to be completed by other legal or natural persons in full compliance with this Directive *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d25ad919c1b9ccac91f66d29c1c45ec8] these Regulations *(Extent: E+W+S)*.
