# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, SCHEDULE 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737

## SCHEDULE 6 — (Annex VI of the Directive) — SUPPLEMENTARY REQUIREMENTS WHEN INTERNAL PRODUCTION CONTROL PLUS SUPERVISED PRODUCTION TESTS SET OUT IN MODULE A1 IS USED (ARTICLE 24(2))[^c-6f0fb17c2316a1ae3ea7c0b67be116e1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 50

**Design and construction**

On one or several watercrafts representing the production of the manufacturer one or more of the following tests, equivalent calculation or control shall be carried out by the manufacturer or on his behalf:

- ((a)) test of stability in accordance with point 3.2 of Part A of Annex I *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-25651ab4eb823ab0d31f39b035f59b42] Schedule 1 *(Extent: E+W+S)*;
- ((b)) test of buoyancy characteristics in accordance with point 3.3 of Part A of Annex I *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-25651ab4eb823ab0d31f39b035f59b42] Schedule 1 *(Extent: E+W+S)*.

**Noise emissions**

For recreational craft fitted with inboard or stern drive engines without integral exhaust and for personal watercraft, on one or several watercraft representing the production of the watercraft manufacturer, the sound emission tests defined in Part C of Annex I *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-25651ab4eb823ab0d31f39b035f59b42] Schedule 1 *(Extent: E+W+S)* shall be carried out by the watercraft manufacturer, or on his behalf, under the responsibility of a notified *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-817bc1fe46067ad2808aa2529e4e167d] an approved *(Extent: E+W+S)* body chosen by the manufacturer.

For outboard engines and stern drive engines with integral exhaust, on one or several engines of each engine family representing the production of the engine manufacturer, the sound emission tests defined in Part C of Annex I *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-25651ab4eb823ab0d31f39b035f59b42] Schedule 1 *(Extent: E+W+S)* shall be carried out by the engine manufacturer, or on his behalf, under the responsibility of a notified *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-817bc1fe46067ad2808aa2529e4e167d] an approved *(Extent: E+W+S)* body chosen by the manufacturer.

Where more than one engine of an engine family is tested, the statistical method described in Annex VII *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c670ca29162a3af68fb8016b916e1aab] Schedule 7 *(Extent: E+W+S)* shall be applied to ensure conformity of the sample.
