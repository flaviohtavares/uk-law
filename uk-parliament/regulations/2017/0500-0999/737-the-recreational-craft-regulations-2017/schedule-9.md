# The Recreational Craft Regulations 2017, SCHEDULE 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2017/737

## SCHEDULE 9 — (Annex IX of the Directive) — TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION[^c-53afea93504d46c2b9ab9106a4a205d1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 9

The technical documentation referred to in Article 7(2) and Article 25 *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2a747546dcebe85a78d2c9b268c2347c] shall, as far as it is relevant for the assessment, contain the following:

- ((a)) A general description of the type;
- ((b)) Conceptual design and manufacturing drawings and schemes of components, sub-assemblies, circuits, and other relevant data;
- ((c)) Descriptions and explanations necessary for the understanding of said drawings and schemes and the operation of the product;
- ((d)) A list of the standards referred to in Article 14 *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6bf2538e3d5c24fecef77f926265ca3] regulation 41 *(Extent: E+W+S)*, applied in full or in part, and descriptions of the solutions adopted to fulfil the essential requirements when the standards referred to in Article 14 *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6bf2538e3d5c24fecef77f926265ca3] regulation 41 *(Extent: E+W+S)* have not been applied;
- ((e)) Results of design calculations made, examinations carried out and other relevant data;
- ((f)) Test reports, or calculations namely on stability in accordance with point 3.2 of Part A of Annex I *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6c6ec3196e054f64bd010999af9ab235] Schedule 1 *(Extent: E+W+S)* and on buoyancy in accordance with point 3.3 of Part A of Annex I *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6c6ec3196e054f64bd010999af9ab235] Schedule 1 *(Extent: E+W+S)*;
- ((g)) Exhaust emissions test reports demonstrating compliance with Section 2 of Part B of Annex I *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6c6ec3196e054f64bd010999af9ab235] Schedule 1 *(Extent: E+W+S)*;
- ((h)) Sound emissions test reports demonstrating compliance with Section 1 of Part C of Annex I *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6c6ec3196e054f64bd010999af9ab235] Schedule 1 *(Extent: E+W+S)*.
