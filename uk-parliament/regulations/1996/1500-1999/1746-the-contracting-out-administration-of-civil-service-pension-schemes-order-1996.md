# The Contracting Out (Administration of Civil Service Pension Schemes) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1746

CONTRACTING OUT

The Contracting Out (Administration of Civil Service Pension Schemes) Order 1996

Made

4th July 1996

Coming into force

5th July 1996

Whereas a draft was laid before Parliament and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament;

Now, therefore, the Minister for the Civil Service, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 69(2), (3) and (4) and 79 of the Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994[^f00001], and having consulted the office-holders whose functions may be affected by it[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order—

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Contracting Out (Administration of Civil Service Pension Schemes) Order 1996 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the 1972 Act” means the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00003]; and
- “the function” means the function conferred by section 1(1)[^f00004] or under section 1(2)[^f00005] of the 1972 Act of administering schemes made under section 1 of that Act and from time to time in force.

## 3. Contracting out of function

The function may be exercised by (or by employees of) such person as may be authorised in that behalf by the Minister or office-holder whose function it is.

D L Willetts

Parliamentary Secretary Office of Public Service for the Minister for the Civil Service

4th July 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 3 of this Order enables the Minister for the Civil Service and other Ministers and office-holders who have functions in relation to the administration of Civil Service Pension Schemes under the Superannuation Act 1972 to authorise private contractors to carry out those functions on their behalf to such extent as is specified in the authorisation.
