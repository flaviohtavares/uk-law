# The Transport and Works Act 1992 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1609 (C.33)

TRANSPORT

The Transport and Works Act 1992 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1996

Made

17th June 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 70(1) and (2) of the Transport and Works Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order: —

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Transport and Works Act 1992 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1996.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Transport and Works Act 1992.

## 2. Provisions coming into force

The provisions of the Act specified in the first column of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the matters specified in the second column of that Schedule) shall come into force on 8th July 1996.

## 3. Savings

The repeal of section 124 of the Transport Act 1968[^f00002] (except as it applies in Scotland) shall not affect the validity of an Order made under that section and in force immediately before 8th July 1996.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Watts

Minister of State,

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 8th July 1996 the provisions of the Transport and Works Act 1992 specified in the Schedule to the Order.

These include provisions as to the placement of signs and barriers at private crossings of railways and tramways, and which authorise the making by the Secretary of State of regulations prescribing signs and barriers which he authorises for use at such crossings. They also authorise the Secretary of State to give to a railway or tramway operator directions for the placing of crossing signs or barriers.

The Order also brings into force, subject to a saving, provision for section 124 of the Transport Act 1968 (which gives power for obligations to be imposed by the Secretary of State in respect of level crossings with roads other than public carriage roads) to cease to have effect in its application in England and Wales.

In addition, the Order brings into force provisions which exclude tramcars from certain legislation that relates to hackney carriages (Metropolitan Public Carriage Act 1869), to private hire-cars (London Cab Act 1968, section 4), and to private hire vehicles (Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976, Part II), and also from legislation that bestows on local authorities powers in relation to tramway carriages similar to those they possess in relation to hackney carriages (Tramways Act 1870, section 48).
