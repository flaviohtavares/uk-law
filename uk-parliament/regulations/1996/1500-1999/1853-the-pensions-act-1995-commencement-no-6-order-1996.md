# The Pensions Act 1995 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been amended by [S.I. 1996/2150](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2150) which is printed herein as pages 5 & 6 and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of [S.I. 1996/1853](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1853).

1996 No. 1853 (C.38)

PENSIONS

The Pensions Act 1995 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1996

Made

15th July 1996

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 180(1) of the Pensions Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Act 1995 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1996.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 116(1) of the Pensions Act 1995, is the day following the day on which this Order is made.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Oliver Heald

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Social Security

15th July 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into force of section 116(1) of the Pensions Act 1995 (penalties for breach of regulations).
