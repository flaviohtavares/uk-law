# The Construction (Health, Safety and Welfare) Regulations 1996, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Training

Any person who carries out any activity involving construction work where training, technical knowledge or experience is necessary to reduce the risks of injury to any person shall possess such training, knowledge or experience, or be under such degree of supervision by a person having such training, knowledge or experience, as may be appropriate having regard to the nature of the activity.
