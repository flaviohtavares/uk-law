# The Construction (Health, Safety and Welfare) Regulations 1996, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Explosives

An explosive charge shall be used or fired only if suitable and sufficient steps have been taken to ensure that no person is exposed to risk of injury from the explosion or from projected or flying material caused thereby.

**11.** A copy of the cost benefit assessment prepared in respect of these Regulations can be obtained from the Health and Safety Executive, Construction Policy Section, Safety Policy Directorate, Rose Court, 2 Southwark Bridge, London SE1 9HS. A copy has been placed in the Library of each House of Parliament.
