# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Waltham Forest London Borough Council) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1578

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Waltham Forest London Borough Council) Order 1996

Made

17th June 1996

Laid before Parliament

26th June 1996

Coming into force

17th July 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(9) and 15(5) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Waltham Forest London Borough Council) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 17th July 1996.

## 2. Exemption of repair and maintenance of vehicles

The repair and maintenance of vehicles[^f00002] by the council of the London borough of Waltham Forest shall not be treated as a defined activity so long as it is carried out during the period beginning with 17th July 1996 and ending with 31st March 1997.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Paul Beresford

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department of the Environment

17th June 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (competition), work falling within certain defined activities may be carried out by local authorities only if specified conditions are fulfilled.

This Order exempts from the requirements of Part I the repair and maintenance of vehicles by the Waltham Forest London Borough Council so long as the work is carried out during the period beginning with 1st July 1996 and ending with 31st March 1997.
