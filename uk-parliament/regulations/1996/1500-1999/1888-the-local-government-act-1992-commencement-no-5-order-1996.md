# The Local Government Act 1992 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1888 (C.40)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Local Government Act 1992 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1996

Made

18th July 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 30(3) of the Local Government Act 1992[^f00001], and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1992 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1996.

## 2. Provisions of the Local Government Act 1992 coming into force on 8th August 1996

The repeals in the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00002] in Part I of Schedule 4 to the Local Government Act 1992 shall come into force on 8th August 1996.

Paul Beresford

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

18th July 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 8th August 1996 the repeals in the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 in Part I of Schedule 4 to the Local Government Act 1992 (ie, the repeals of sections 13(6), 16(2) and (3) and the words “(1) to (3)” in section 19A(1)(f) of the 1980 Act).

(This note is not part of the Order)
