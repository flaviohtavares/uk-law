# The Winchester School of Art Higher Education Corporation (Dissolution) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1761

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Winchester School of Art Higher Education Corporation (Dissolution) Order 1996

Made

8th July 1996

Laid before Parliament

9th July 1996

Coming into force

1st August 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 128(1), (2) and (6) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Employment, after consultation with Winchester School of Art higher education corporation[^f00002] and the Higher Education Funding Council for England in accordance with subsection (4) of the said section 128 and with the consent of the University of Southampton given in accordance with subsection (2) of that section, hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Winchester School of Art Higher Education Corporation (Dissolution) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st August 1996.

**2.** In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Education Reform Act 1988; and
- “the corporation” means Winchester School of Art higher education corporation.

**3.** (1) The corporation is dissolved.

(2) The property, rights and liabilities of the corporation, other than rights and liabilities under any contract of employment, are transferred to and vest in the University of Southampton.

**4.** Section 127 of the Act shall apply in respect of any person employed immediately before 1st August 1996 by the corporation to work solely at the institution conducted by the corporation as if the reference in that section—

- (a) to the transfer date were to 1st August 1996;
- (b) to the transferor authority were to the corporation; and
- (c) to the corporation were to the University of Southampton.

Eric Forth

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Employment

8th July 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves Winchester School of Art higher education corporation and transfers the property, rights and liabilities of that corporation to the University of Southampton (article 3). Contracts of employment of staff of the corporation are transferred separately (article 4) by applying Section 127 of the Education Reform Act 1988 (which is concerned with the transfer of staff to higher education corporations) with appropriate modifications.
