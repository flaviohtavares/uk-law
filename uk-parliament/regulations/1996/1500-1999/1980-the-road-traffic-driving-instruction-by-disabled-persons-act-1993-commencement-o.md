# The Road Traffic (Driving Instruction by Disabled Persons) Act 1993 (Commencement) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1980 (c. 43)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic (Driving Instruction by Disabled Persons) Act 1993 (Commencement) Order 1996

Made

23rd July 1996

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(2) of the Road Traffic (Driving Instruction by Disabled Persons) Act 1993[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Driving Instruction by Disabled Persons) Act 1993 (Commencement) Order 1996.

**2.** The Road Traffic (Driving Instruction by Disabled Persons) Act 1993 shall come into force on 9th September 1996.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Steven Norris

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

23rd July 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Road Traffic (Driving Instruction by Disabled Persons) Act 1993 into force on 9th September 1996.
