# The Medicinal Products: Prescription by Nurses etc. Act 1992 (Commencement No.2) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1505 (C.28) (S.133)

MEDICINES

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The Medicinal Products: Prescription by Nurses etc. Act 1992 (Commencement No.2) Order 1996

Made

5th June 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 6(2) of the Medicinal Products: Prescription by Nurses etc. Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Medicinal Products: Prescription by Nurses etc. Act 1992 (Commencement No.2) Order 1996.

## 2. Appointed Day

1st July 1996 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 3 of the Medicinal Products: Prescription by Nurses etc. Act 1992.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

5th June 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st July 1996 section 3 of the Medicinal Products: Prescription by Nurses etc. Act 1992 (extension of pharmaceutical services in Scotland). Section 3 is the last provision from that Act to be brought into force, sections 4 to 6 having come into force on Royal Assent and sections 1 and 2 having been commenced on 3rd October 1994 by S.I. [1994/2408](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/2408).
