# The Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (Application to Customs and Excise) (Amendment) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1860

CUSTOMS AND EXCISE

The Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (Application to Customs and Excise) (Amendment) Order 1996

Made

18th July 1996

Laid before Parliament

18th July 1996

Coming into force

1st September 1996

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 114(2) of the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (Application to Customs and Excise) (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st September 1996.

**2.** The Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (Application to Customs and Excise) Order 1985[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

- (a) In Schedule 2, Part 1, Column 2 for the word “grade” there shall be substituted the word “title”.
- (b) For Schedule 2, Part 2 there shall be substituted the following:—

18th July 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

In consequence of the abolition of certain civil service grades and the introduction of job titles and a pay-band system for officers of HM Customs and Excise, this Order amends the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (Application to Customs and Excise) Order 1985 and the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (Application to Customs and Excise) Order 1987 by applying the equivalent job titles and pay-banding, as appropriate, of officers of HM Customs and Excise to equivalent ranks of constables and police officers.
