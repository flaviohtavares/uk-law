# The Kent and Canterbury Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1701

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Kent and Canterbury Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

Made

2nd July 1996

Laid before Parliament

9th July 1996

Coming into force

31st July 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities previously managed by the former Canterbury and Thanet Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Kent and Canterbury Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted the former East Kent Health Authority[^f00003] and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Kent and Canterbury Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996, and shall come into force on 31st July 1996.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the East Kent Health Authority established on 1st April 1996[^f00004];
- “the old Authority” means the East Kent Health Authority established on 1st April 1994[^f00005] and abolished on 1st April 1996[^f00006];
- “the Trust” means the Kent and Canterbury Hospitals National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the old Authority and the Trust on 27th November 1995, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the old Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 31st July 1996 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

2nd July 1996

(This note does not form part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Kent and Canterbury Hospitals National Health Service Trust on 31st July 1996 trust property held by the East Kent Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, NHS Trust Unit, Room 1N33, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
