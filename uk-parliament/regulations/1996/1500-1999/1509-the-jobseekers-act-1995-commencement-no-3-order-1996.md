# The Jobseekers Act 1995 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1509 (C.29)

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Jobseekers Act 1995 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1996

Made

10th June 1996

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 41(2) and (3) of the Jobseekers Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Jobseekers Act 1995 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1996.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of Schedule 2 to the Jobseekers Act 1995 specified in the Schedule to this Order, and section 41(4) of that Act in so far as it relates to those provisions, is 11th June 1996.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Roger Evans

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Social Security

10th June 1996

This, the third Order commencing provisions of the Jobseekers Act [1995 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/18), provides for the coming into force on 11th June 1996 of certain provisions in Schedule 2 which make consequential amendments to other legislation, including the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5).
