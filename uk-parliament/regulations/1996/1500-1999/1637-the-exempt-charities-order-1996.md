# The Exempt Charities Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1637

CHARITIES

The Exempt Charities Order 1996

Made

26th June 1996

Coming into force

1st August 1996

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 26th day of June 1996

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of paragraph (c) of Schedule 2 to the Charities Act 1993[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Exempt Charities Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st August 1996.

**2.** The London Business School is hereby declared to be an exempt charity for the purposes of the Charities Act 1993.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order declares the London Business School to be an exempt charity within the meaning of the Charities Act 1993 with effect from 1st August 1996. An exempt charity is not required to be registered with the Charity Commissioners and the Commissioners cannot exercise any powers under the Act in relation to an exempt charity except at the charity’s request.
