# The Finance Act 1994, sections 244 and 245, (Commencement) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2316 (C. 59)

TAXES

The Finance Act 1994, sections 244 and 245, (Commencement) Order 1996

Made

9th September 1996

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 245(8) of the Finance Act 1994[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 1994, sections 244 and 245, (Commencement) Order 1996.

**2.** Sections 244 and 245 of the Finance Act 1994 shall come into force on 4th November 1996.

Bowen Wells

Richard Ottaway

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

9th September 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings sections 244 and 245 of the Finance Act 1994 (which relate to the production of documents on the transfer of land in Northern Ireland) into force on 4th November 1996.
