# The Sexual Offences (Conspiracy and Incitement) Act 1996 (Commencement) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2262 (C.57)

CRIMINAL LAW, ENGLAND AND WALES

CRIMINAL LAW, SCOTLAND

CRIMINAL LAW, NORTHERN IRELAND

The Sexual Offences (Conspiracy and Incitement) Act 1996 (Commencement) Order 1996

Made

2nd September 1996

In exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 7(2) of the Sexual Offences (Conspiracy and Incitement) Act 1996[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Sexual Offences (Conspiracy and Incitement) Act 1996 (Commencement) Order 1996.

**2.** The Sexual Offences (Conspiracy and Incitement) Act 1996 shall come into force on 1st October 1996.

Ann Widdecombe

Minister of State

Home Office

2nd September 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st October 1996 the Sexual Offences (Conspiracy and Incitement) Act 1996.
