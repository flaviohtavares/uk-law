# The Security Service Act 1996 (Commencement) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2454 (C. 64)

SECURITY SERVICE

The Security Service Act 1996 (Commencement) Order 1996

Made

23rd September 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 4(2) of the Security Service Act 1996[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Security Service Act 1996 (Commencement) Order 1996.

**2.** The Security Service Act 1996 shall come into force on 14th October 1996.

Home Office

Michael Howard

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

23rd September 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 14th October 1996 all the provisions of the Security Service Act 1996.
