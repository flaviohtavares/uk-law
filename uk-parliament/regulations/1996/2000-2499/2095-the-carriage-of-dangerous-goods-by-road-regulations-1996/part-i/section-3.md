# The Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Regulations 1996, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Application of these regulations

Subject to the provisions of Schedule 2, these Regulations shall apply to and in relation to the carriage of dangerous goods.
