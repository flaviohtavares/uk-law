# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemptions) (Bedford Borough Council and Suffolk Coastal District Council) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2068

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemptions) (Bedford Borough Council and Suffolk Coastal District Council) Order 1996

Made

6th August 1996

Laid before Parliament

13th August 1996

Coming into force

3rd September 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(9) and 15(5) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemptions) (Bedford Borough Council and Suffolk Coastal District Council) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 3rd September 1996.

## 2. Exemptions

The management of sports and leisure facilities by the Bedford Borough Council shall not be treated as a defined activity so long as it is undertaken at the Oasis Beach Pool, Cardington Road, Bedford before 1st April 1998.

**3.** The management of sports and leisure facilities by the Suffolk Coastal District Council shall not be treated as a defined activity so long as it is undertaken at The Sports Centre, High Road East, Felixstowe, Suffolk before 1st January 1999.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Paul Beresford

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

6th August 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (competition), work falling within certain defined activities may be carried out by local authorities only if particular conditions are fulfilled.

This Order exempts from the requirements of Part I the management of sports and leisure facilities by the Bedford Borough Council so long as it is undertaken at the site named in the Order before 1st April 1998 and by Suffolk Coastal District Council so long as it is undertaken at the site named in the Order before 1st January 1999.
