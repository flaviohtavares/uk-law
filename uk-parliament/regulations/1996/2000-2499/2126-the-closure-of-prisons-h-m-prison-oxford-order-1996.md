# The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Prison Oxford) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2126

PRISONS

The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Prison Oxford) Order 1996

Made

14th August 1996

Laid before Parliament

16th August 1996

Coming into force

7th September 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 37 of the Prison Act 1952[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Closure of Prisons (H.M. Prison Oxford) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 7th September 1996.

**2.** The prison known as H.M. Prison Oxford and situate in the county of Oxfordshire shall close on the coming into force of this Order.

Timothy Kirkhope

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

14th August 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the closure of H.M. Prison Oxford on 7th September 1996.
