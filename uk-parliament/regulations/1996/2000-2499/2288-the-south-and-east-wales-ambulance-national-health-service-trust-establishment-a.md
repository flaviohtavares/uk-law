# The South and East Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2288

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The South and East Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 1996

Made

22nd August 1996

Coming into force

5th September 1996

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the South and East Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 5th September 1996.

## 2. Amendment of NHS Trust Establishment Order

In the South and East Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00003], for article 4 there shall be substituted—

> **4.** The trust shall have, in addition to the chairman, four non-executive directors and four executive directors.

.

William Hague

Secretary of State for Wales

22nd August 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the South and East Wales Ambulance National Health Service (Establishment) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/2740](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2740)) by providing that the number of executive and non-executive directors of the NHS trust established by that Order shall, in each case, be four.
