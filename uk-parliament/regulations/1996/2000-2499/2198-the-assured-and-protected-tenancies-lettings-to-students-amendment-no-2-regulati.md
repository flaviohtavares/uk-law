# The Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2198

LANDLORD AND TENANT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1996

Made

22nd August 1996

Laid before Parliament

30th August 1996

Coming into force

20th September 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1988[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 20th September 1996.

**2.** Schedule 2 to the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1988[^f00002] shall be amended by the addition at the end of “Derbyshire Student Residences Limited”.

Eric Forth

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Employment

22nd August 1996

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Schedule 2 to the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1988 to specify Derbyshire Student Residences Limited for the purposes of paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1988. The effect of specification is that a tenancy granted by the body to a student at an educational institution specified in the 1988 Regulations is not an assured tenancy for the purposes of the 1988 Act.
