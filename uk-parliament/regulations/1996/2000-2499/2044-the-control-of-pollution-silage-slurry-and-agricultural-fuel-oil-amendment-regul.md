# The Control of Pollution (Silage, Slurry and Agricultural Fuel Oil) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2044

WATER, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Control of Pollution (Silage, Slurry and Agricultural Fuel Oil) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

Made

3rd August 1996

Laid before Parliament

5th August 1996

Coming into force

31st August 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 92 and 219(2)(e) and (f) of the Water Resources Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Control of Pollution (Silage, Slurry and Agricultural Fuel Oil) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 31st August 1996.

## 2. Removal of time limit for exemption for field silage methods in use in the 3 years immediately before 1st March 1991

Regulation 7(2)(b) of the Control of Pollution (Silage, Slurry and Agricultural Fuel Oil) Regulations 1991[^f00002] (time limit for the exemption for field silage methods in use in the 3 years immediately before 1st March 1991) is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Robert Jones

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment

3rd August 1996

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke regulation 7(2)(b) of the Control of Pollution (Silage, Slurry and Agricultural Fuel Oil) Regulations 1991. Regulation 7 allowed field silage methods which were in use in the 3 years immediately before 1st March 1991 to continue to be used in certain circumstances until 1st September 1996. These Regulations remove the time limit of 1st September 1996.
