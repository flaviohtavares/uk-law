# The Carriage of Explosives by Road Regulations 1996, section 33

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 33. Miscellaneous amendments

(1) The 1983 Regulations shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 9.

(2) In regulation 5(10) of the Coal and Other Safety-Lamp Mines (Explosives) Regulations 1993[^f00026] for the words “Road Traffic (Carriage of Explosives) Regulations 1989” there shall be substituted the words “Carriage of Explosives by Road Regulations 1996”.
