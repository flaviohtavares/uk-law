# The Carriage of Explosives by Road Regulations 1996, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Enforcement

Notwithstanding regulation 3 of the Health and Safety (Enforcing Authority) Regulations 1989[^f00025], the enforcing authority for these Regulations shall be the Health and Safety Executive.
