# The Carriage of Explosives by Road Regulations 1996, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Transitional provision

Until 1st January 1997 it shall be sufficient compliance with the requirements of these Regulations if the requirements of the 1989 Regulations are complied with as if they had been in force.
