# The Carriage of Explosives by Road Regulations 1996, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Carriage in bulk

No person shall cause or permit the carriage in bulk of explosives which are explosive substances.
