# The Carriage of Explosives by Road Regulations 1996, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Part III: Information

Regulation 15 and Schedule 5 make provision with regard to the displaying of information on vehicles and containers. Regulation 16 requires the consignor to provide the operator with the information set out in Part I of Schedule 6. Regulation 17 requires the operator to ensure that the driver or attendant has in their possession the Transport Documentation, which is set out in Part II of Schedule 6. Regulation 18 requires the driver and attendant to keep the Transport Documentation readily available throughout the carriage.
