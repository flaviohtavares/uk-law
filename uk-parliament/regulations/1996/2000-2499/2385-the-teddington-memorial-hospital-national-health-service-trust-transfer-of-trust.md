# The Teddington Memorial Hospital National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2385

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Teddington Memorial Hospital National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

Made

11th September 1996

Laid before Parliament

13th September 1996

Coming into force

4th October 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities previously managed by the former Hounslow and Spelthorne Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Teddington Memorial Hospital National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted the former Ealing Hammersmith and Hounslow Health Authority[^f00003] and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Teddington Memorial Hospital National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996, and shall come into force on 4th October 1996.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the Ealing, Hammersmith & Hounslow Health Authority established on 1st April 1996[^f00004];
- “the old Authority” means the Ealing Hammersmith and Hounslow Health Authority established on 1st April 1993[^f00005] and abolished on 1st April 1996[^f00006];
- “the Trust” means the Teddington Memorial Hospital National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the old Authority and the Trust on 20th March 1996, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the old Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 4th October 1996 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

11th September 1996

(This note does not form part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Teddington Memorial Hospital National Health Service Trust on 4th October 1996 trust property held by the Ealing Hammersmith and Hounslow Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, NHS Trust Unit, Room 1W29, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
