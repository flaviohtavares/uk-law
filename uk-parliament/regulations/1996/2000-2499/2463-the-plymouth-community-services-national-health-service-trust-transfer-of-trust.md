# The Plymouth Community Services National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2463

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Plymouth Community Services National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

Made

23rd September 1996

Laid before Parliament

30th September 1996

Coming into force

21st October 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of St. Mary’s Hospital, Isles of Scilly, formerly managed by the Cornwall Healthcare National Health Service Trust and now managed by the Plymouth Community Services National Health Service Trust, having consulted those Trusts in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Plymouth Community Services National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 21st October 1996.

(2) In this Order “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Cornwall Healthcare National Health Service Trust and the Plymouth Community Services National Health Service Trust on 15th July 1996, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both those Trusts.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 21st October 1996 the trust property shall be transferred from the Cornwall Healthcare National Health Service Trust to the Plymouth Community Services National Health Service Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

23rd September 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Plymouth Community Services National Health Service Trust on 21st October 1996 trust property formerly held by the Cornwall Healthcare National Health Service Trust. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, Room IN33, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
