# The Solihull Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2360

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Solihull Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

Made

10th September 1996

Laid before Parliament

17th September 1996

Coming into force

8th October 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of establishments and facilities previously managed by the former East Birmingham Health Authority and now managed by the Solihull Health Authority, having consulted the former North Birmingham and the former Solihull Health Authorities[^f00002] in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Solihull Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 8th October 1996.

(2) In this Order—

- “the first Authority” means the Birmingham Health Authority;
- “the second Authority” means the Solihull Health Authority;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the former North Birmingham and the former Solihull Health Authorities on 8th November 1995, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both of those Authorities.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 8th October 1996 the trust property shall be transferred from the first Authority to the second Authority.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

10th September 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Solihull Health Authority on 8th October 1996 trust property held by the Birmingham Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, NHS Trust Unit, Room 1N33F, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
