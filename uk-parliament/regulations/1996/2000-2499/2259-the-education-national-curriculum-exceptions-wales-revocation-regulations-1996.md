# The Education (National Curriculum) (Exceptions) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2259

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The Education (National Curriculum) (Exceptions) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 1996

Made

5th September 1996

Laid before Parliament

9th September 1996

Coming into force

1st October 1996

Having made such arrangements for consultation on his proposals pursuant to section 242(1) of the Education Act 1993[^f00001] as he considered appropriate, the Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 17 and 232(5) and (6) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (National Curriculum) (Exceptions) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 1st October 1996.

## 2. Revocation of Regulations

The Education (National Curriculum) (Exceptions) (Wales) Regulations 1994[^f00003] are revoked.

Jonathan Evans

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

5th September 1996

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Education (National Curriculum) (Exceptions) (Wales) Regulations 1994.
