# The Defence Evaluation and Research Agency Trading Fund (Amendment) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of S.I. [1995/650](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/650).

1996 No. 1447

GOVERNMENT TRADING FUNDS

The Defence Evaluation and Research Agency Trading Fund (Amendment) Order 1996

Made

4th June 1996

Laid before the House of Commons

5th June 1996

Coming into force

1st August 1996

The Secretary of State for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 1(1) and 6(1) of the Government Trading Funds Act 1973[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the concurrence of the Treasury, makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Defence Evaluation and Research Agency Trading Fund (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st August 1996.

**2.** In Article 3 of the Defence Evaluation and Research Agency Trading Fund Order 1995[^f00002] there shall be deleted the words:

> - the Atomic Weapons Establishment, Foulness;

.

Michael Portillo

Secretary of State for Defence

2nd June 1996

We concur

Derek Conway

Bowen Wells

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

4th June 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order removes the Atomic Weapons Establishment, Foulness from the list of bodies given trading fund status by the Defence Evaluation and Research Agency Trading Fund Order 1995.
