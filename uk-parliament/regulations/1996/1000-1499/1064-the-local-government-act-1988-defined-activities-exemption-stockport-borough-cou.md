# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Stockport Borough Council) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1064

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Stockport Borough Council) Order 1996

Made

4th April 1996

Laid before Parliament

17th April 1996

Coming into force

8th May 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(9) and 15(5) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Stockport Borough Council) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 8th May 1996.

## 2. Exemption of catering work

Catering for purposes of schools by the Stockport Borough Council shall not be treated as a defined activity so long as it is carried out before 1st September 1997 in a school mentioned in the Schedule.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Paul Beresford

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

4th April 1996

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

- All Saints Church of England Infant and Junior School
- Arden Primary School
- Broadstone Hall Infant and Junior School
- Brookhead Junior School
- Cheadle Primary School
- Cherry Tree Infant and Junior School
- Demmings Infant School
- Etchells Primary School
- Maycroft Primary School
- North Cheshire Jewish Primary School
- Priestnall School
- Warren Wood Primary School
- Woodley Infant and Junior School

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (competition), work falling within certain defined activities may be carried out by local authorities only if certain conditions are fulfilled.

This Order exempts from the requirements of Part I catering for purposes of schools by the Stockport Borough Council so long as it is carried out in certain schools before 1st September 1997.
