# The Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (Commencement No.2) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1336 (C.25)

DISABLED PERSONS

The Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (Commencement No.2) Order 1996

Made

16th May 1996

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 70(3) of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (Commencement No.2) Order 1996.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 specified in article 3 below is 17th May 1996.

## 3. Provisions commenced

The provisions referred to in article 2 above are—

- (a) section 1 (Meaning of “disability” and “disabled person”);
- (b) section 2 (Past disabilities);
- (c) section 3 (Guidance);
- (d) section 16(3) in so far as it relates to the definitions of “sub-lease” and “sub-tenancy”, and (5) in so far as it relates to paragraph 4 of Schedule 4 (Sub-leases etc);
- (e) section 28 (Provision of advice and assistance);
- (f) section 59 (Statutory authority and national security);
- (g) section 62(1), (2) and (7) (Restriction of publicity: industrial tribunals);
- (h) section 63(1), (2), (4), (5) and (6) (Restriction of publicity: Employment Appeal Tribunal);
- (i) section 67 (Regulations and orders);
- (j) section 68(1) (Interpretation);
- (k) section 69 (Financial provisions);
- (l) section 70(7) (House of Commons disqualification);
- (m) Schedule 1 (with the exception of paragraph 7) (Provisions Supplementing Section 1);
- (n) Schedule 2 (Past Disabilities);
- (o) Schedule 4, paragraph 4 (Sub-leases etc.).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

MacKay of Ardbrecknish

Minister of State,

Department of Social Security

16th May 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 17th May 1996 certain provisions of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 relating to the definition of disability; past disabilities; guidance; definition of lease, sub-lease and sub-tenancy; advice and assistance; statutory authority and national security; restriction of publicity in industrial tribunals and the Employment Appeal Tribunal; regulations and orders; interpretation; financial provisions; House of Commons disqualification; provisions supplementing the definition of disability and past disabilities.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (Commencement No.1) Order 1995[^f00002] brought into force on 1st January 1996 sections 50, 51 and 52 of, and Schedule 5 to, the Disability Discrimination Act 1995.
