# The A4 Trunk Road (Great West Road, Hounslow) (Prohibition Of Use Of Gap In Central Reserve) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1113

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A4 Trunk Road (Great West Road, Hounslow) (Prohibition Of Use Of Gap In Central Reserve) Order 1996

Made

16th April 1996

Coming into force

22nd April 1996

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(1) and (3) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis pursuant to section 7(6) of that Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A4 Trunk Road (Great West Road, Hounslow) (Prohibition Of Use Of Gap In Central Reserve) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 22nd April 1996.

**2.** In this Order—

- “central reserve” means that part of Great West Road which separates its two carriageways for the safety and guidance of vehicles using that road; and
- “Great West Road” means that length of the A4 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Hounslow known as Great West Road.

**3.** No person shall cause of permit any vehicle to enter or proceed through the gap in the central reserve which lies opposite Lionel Road, London W3.

**4.** The London Borough of Hounslow (Prescribed Routes) (No. 5) Traffic Order 1988[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

**5.** Article 5 of the Trunk Roads (Various Roads, Hounslow) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1973[^f00004] shall be omitted.

**6.** The Trunk Road (Great West Road, Hounslow) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1977[^f00005] is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of The Secretary of State for Transport

A. R. Mellors

Assistant Director Highways Agency London Network Management Division

16th April 1996
