# The Education (Grant-Maintained and Grant-Maintained Special Schools) (Finance) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1334

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The Education (Grant-Maintained and Grant-Maintained Special Schools) (Finance) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

Made

13th May 1996

Laid before Parliament

20th May 1996

Coming into force

11th June 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 81(2), 82(2), 83(2), 88, 89, 90, 94 and 301(6) of the Education Act 1993[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Wales hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Grant-maintained and Grant-maintained Special Schools) (Finance) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 11th June 1996.

## 2. Amendment of Regulations

Schedule 5 to the Education (Grant-maintained and Grant-maintained Special Schools) (Finance) (Wales) Regulations 1996[^f00002] shall be amended by the addition at the end of Schedule 5, after paragraph 2(e) of that Schedule, of the following sub-paragraph:

> - (f) expenditure incurred or to be incurred by the governing body of primary schools in connection with—
>   - (i) the procurement of professional advice for the purposes of the school; and
>   - (ii) staff absences.

.

William Hague

Secretary of State for Wales

13th May 1996

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Schedule 5 to the Education (Grant-maintained and Grant-maintained Special Schools) (Finance) (Wales) Regulations 1996 (which apply only to grant-maintained and grant-maintained special schools situated in Wales) by adding an additional sub-paragraph which provides that special purpose grants may be paid to meet expenditure incurred or to be incurred by the governing bodies of primary schools in connection with staff absences and the procurement of professional advice.
