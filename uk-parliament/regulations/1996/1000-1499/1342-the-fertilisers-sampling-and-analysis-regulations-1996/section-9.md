## 9. Revocations

The Fertilisers (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1991[^f00005], the Fertilisers (Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) Regulations 1991[^f00006] and the Fertilisers (Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) Regulations 1994[^f00007] are hereby revoked.
