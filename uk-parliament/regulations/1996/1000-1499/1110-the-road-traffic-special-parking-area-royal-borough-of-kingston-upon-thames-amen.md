# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames) (Amendment) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1110

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames) (Amendment) Order 1996

Made

16th April 1996

Laid before Parliament

25th April 1996

Coming into force

20th May 1996

Whereas the council of the royal borough of Kingston upon Thames has applied to the Secretary of State for Transport for an order to made under section 76 of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and the Secretary of State has consulted the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis in accordance with section 76(2) of that Act:

NOW, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 76(1) and section 77(6) of Road Traffic Act 1991 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames) (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 20th May 1996.

## 2. Amendment of the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames) Order 1994

(1) The Schedule to the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames) Order 1994[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) After paragraph 10 there shall be inserted the following paragraph—

> **10A.** Highfield Road between its junction with Tolworth Rise North service road and a point 21m north west of the south eastern boundary of 153 Tolworth Rise North.

(3) After paragraph 25 there shall be inserted the following paragraph—

> **25A.** Tolworth Rise North service road between a point opposite the common boundary of 143 and 145 Tolworth Rise North and a point 5m north east of the common boundary of 157 and 159 Tolworth Rise North.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Steven Norris

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

16th April 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Schedule to the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames) Order 1994, which lists the roads excluded from the special parking area designated by that Order.
