# The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Jersey) (Revocation) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1140

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Jersey) (Revocation) Order 1996

Made

24th April 1996

Coming into force

1st July 1996

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 24th day of April 1996

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of sections 25(6) and 28(3) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Jersey) (Revocation) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st July 1996.

**2.** The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Jersey) Order 1984[^f00002]is hereby revoked.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1984 (Jersey) Order 1984, which extended that Act to Jersey with exceptions, adaptions and modifications. A Jersey law corresponding to the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989, which has not been extended to Jersey, is intended to come into force on the same day as this Order.
