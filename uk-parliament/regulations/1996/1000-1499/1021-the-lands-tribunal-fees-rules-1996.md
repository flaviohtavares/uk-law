# The Lands Tribunal (Fees) Rules 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1021

LANDS TRIBUNAL

The Lands Tribunal (Fees) Rules 1996

Made

1st April 1996

Coming into force

1st May 1996

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3 of the Lands Tribunal Act 1949[^f00001], after consultation with the Council on Tribunals in accordance with section 8(1) of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00002], and with the approval of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Rules—

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Lands Tribunal (Fees) Rules 1996 and shall come into force on 1st May 1996.

**2.** (1) The “1996 Rules” means the Lands Tribunal Rules 1996[^f00003] and any reference to a rule by number alone shall be construed as a reference to the rule so numbered in the 1996 Rules.

(2) Any reference to a hearing in the Schedule to these Rules shall include a reference to the procedure under rule 27 *(Determination of proceedings without a hearing)* and the fee shown as payable for a hearing in the said Schedule shall be payable where the matter is determined in accordance with rule 27.

**3.** The fees to be taken in respect of proceedings before the Lands Tribunal shall be those specified in the Schedule to these rules.

**4.** The hearing fee shall, unless the Tribunal otherwise directs, be payable by the party by whom the proceedings were instituted (without prejudice to his right to recover the amount of the fee from any other party by virtue of any order as to costs) on receipt of notification from the registrar.

**5.** The proceedings referred to in paragraph 1(1), 6(2), 6(3), and 6(5) of the Schedule do not include an appeal against a determination by the Commissioners of Inland Revenue under the Finance (1909–1910) Act 1910[^f00004] or under the Finance Act 1975[^f00005] or a reference under section 47(1) or section 47A of the Taxes Management Act 1970[^f00006].

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

Dated 27th March 1996

We approve

Derek Conway

Bowen Wells

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

Dated 1st April 1996

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules provide for a new scale of fees to be taken in proceedings before the Lands Tribunal, following the reovcation of the Lands Tribunal Rules 1975 ([S.I. 1975/299](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1975/299)) by the Lands Tribunal Rules 1996, which contain the rules of procedure of the Tribunal. The 1975 Rules incorported both rules of procedure and fees.

The fees were amended by the Lands Tribunal (Amendment) Rules 1977 ([S.I. 1977/1820](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1977/1820)), 1984 ([S.I. 1984/793](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1984/793)), 1986 ([S.I. 1986/1322](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1322)) and 1990 ([S.I. 1990/1382](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1382)). These Rules are revoked by the 1996 Rules.
