# The Radioactive Material (Road Transport) (Great Britain) Regulations 1996, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. General obligation

The consignor and the carrier of radioactive material in a vehicle, and the driver of the vehicle, shall exercise reasonable care to ensure that the material shall not, in the course of transport, cause any injury to health, or any damage to property or to the environment.
