# The Radioactive Material (Road Transport) (Great Britain) Regulations 1996, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Storage in transit

The carrier of any consignment shall comply with the requirements of Schedule 34 as to storage of the consignment in transit.
