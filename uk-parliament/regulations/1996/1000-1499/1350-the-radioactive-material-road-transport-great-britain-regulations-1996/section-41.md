# The Radioactive Material (Road Transport) (Great Britain) Regulations 1996, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Revocation of existing Regulations

The Radioactive Substances (Carriage by Road) (Great Britain) Regulations 1974[^f00008] and the Radioactive Substances (Carriage by Road) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1985[^f00009] are hereby revoked.
