# The Radioactive Material (Road Transport) (Great Britain) Regulations 1996, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Package inspection prior to shipment

(1) No person shall send or make the first shipment of any package unless the package meets the requirements of Schedule 29.

(2) No person shall send or make any shipment of any package unless the package meets the requirements of Schedule 30.
