# The Radioactive Material (Road Transport) (Great Britain) Regulations 1996, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Undeliverable consignments

The carrier of any consignment which proves to be undeliverable shall place it in a safe location, inform the Secretary of State as soon as possible and ask for his approval of further action.
