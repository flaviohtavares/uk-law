# The Regional Flood Defence Committee (Welsh Region) (Amendment) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a default in [S.I. 1996/538](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/538) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1996 No. 1007

LAND DRAINAGE

The Regional Flood Defence Committee (Welsh Region) (Amendment) Order 1996

Made

1st April 1996

Coming into force

1st April 1996

Whereas:—

(1) In accordance with The Welsh National Water Development Authority (Regional Land Drainage Committee) Order 1973, the total number of members of the Regional Flood Defence Committee (Welsh Region) (hereinafter referred to as “the Committee”) consists of nineteen members.

(2) The National Rivers Authority (hereinafter referred to as “the Authority”) in accordance with section 11(2) of the Water Resources Act 1991[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the 1991 Act”) have submitted to the Secretary of State (hereinafter referred to as “the Minister”) a provisional determination (hereinafter called “the determination”) varying the total number of members of the Committee from nineteen to twenty one.

(3) The Minister has confirmed the determination by virtue of the Regional Flood Defence Committee (Welsh Region) Order 1996[^f00002].

Now therefore the Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 11(4) and (5) of the 1991 Act and of every other power enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order shall be cited as the Regional Flood Defence Committee (Welsh Region) (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

## 2. Amendment of the Regional Flood Defence Committee (Welsh Region) Order 1996

The Regional Flood Defence Committee (Welsh Region) Order 1996[^f00003] is amended by the insertion after article 2 of:—

> **3. Revocation**
>
> The Welsh National Water Development Authority (Regional Land Drainage Committee) Order 1973[^f00004] is hereby revoked.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Gwilym Jones

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

1st April 1996

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

This Order revokes the Welsh National Water Development Authority (Regional Land Drainage Committee) Order 1973 ([S.I. 1973/1924](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1973/1924)), consequent upon the provisions of the Regional Flood Defence Committee (Welsh Region) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/538](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/538)).
