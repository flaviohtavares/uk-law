# The New Town (East Kilbride) Dissolution Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1066 (S.115)

NEW TOWNS

The New Town (East Kilbride) Dissolution Order 1996

Made

29th March 1996

Coming into force

4th April 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 36G(1) of the New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with East Kilbride Development Corporation, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the New Town (East Kilbride) Dissolution Order 1996 and shall come into force on 4th April 1996.

(2) In this Order, “East Kilbride Development Corporation” means the East Kilbride Development Corporation established by the New Town (East Kilbride) (Development Corporation) Order 1947[^f00002].

**2.** The Secretary of State hereby appoints 5th April 1996 as the date for the dissolution of East Kilbride Development Corporation.

George Kynoch

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

29th March 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 5th April 1996 as the date for the dissolution of the East Kilbride Development Corporation.
