# The Housing and Planning Act 1986 (Commencement No.19) (Scotland) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 1276 (C.22) (S.125)

TOWN AND COUNTRY PLANNING, SCOTLAND

The Housing and Planning Act 1986 (Commencement No.19) (Scotland) Order 1996

Made

7th May 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 57(2) of the Housing and Planning Act 1986[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Housing and Planning Act 1986 (Commencement No.19) (Scotland) Order 1996.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1st June 1996

The following provisions of the Housing and Planning Act 1986 shall come into force on 1st June 1996:—

- (a) section 53(1) (minor and consequential amendments) in so far as it relates to paragraphs 57 and 58 of Schedule 11; and
- (b) section 53(2) (repeals) in so far as it relates to the repeals in Part IV of Schedule 12 specified in the Schedule to this Order.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st June 1996 provisions of the Housing and Planning Act 1986 which amend the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972. They amend sections 53(2) and (4) (listed buildings) and 267(9) (local inquiries) of the 1972 Act. In consequence all the amendments to the 1972 Act made by the 1986 Act will be in force, apart from the inadvertent repeal of section 273(9) of the 1972 Act which has not been commenced.

The Order also brings into force a number of other minor repeals.

This is the final commencement order for Scotland relating to the 1986 Act.
