# Occupational Pension Schemes (Contracting-out) Regulations 1996, section 15

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15. Further information and change of circumstances.

*Extent: E+W+S*

An employer who makes an election with a view to the issue of a contracting-out certificate[^c-301b54089b719fbf9d55beb405f1f4fa]..., shall furnish to the Secretary of State such reports, accounts and other documents and information relating to the scheme as the Secretary of State requires, and, in particular, shall notify the Secretary of State of any such change of circumstances affecting the scheme as he may have required the employer to notify as soon as practicable after its occurrence.
