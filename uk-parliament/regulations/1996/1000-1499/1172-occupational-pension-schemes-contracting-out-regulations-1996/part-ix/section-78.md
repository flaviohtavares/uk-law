# Occupational Pension Schemes (Contracting-out) Regulations 1996, section 78

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 78. Revocations.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to the savings provided for in regulations 15 and 16 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Schemes that were Contracted-out) (No.2) Regulations 2015[^c-4c6b73de86453fc6fdb8ab946e660228], the regulations listed in column 2 of Schedule 2 to these Regulations (some of which are spent) are revoked to the extent specified in column 3 of that Schedule.
