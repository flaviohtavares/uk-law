# Occupational Pension Schemes (Contracting-out) Regulations 1996, section 29

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29. Schemes which cannot be certified under section 9(2B) of the 1993 Act.

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of section 9(2B)(d) of the 1993 Act (prescribed class or description of schemes which may not be salary-related contracted-out schemes) the prescribed schemes are—

- (a)[^key-ec3f4918f2977ba86b1ed134d11a7487] a scheme which is not registered under section 153 of the Finance Act 2004, unless it is, or was formerly, a relevant statutory scheme.
- (b) a money purchase scheme.
