# Occupational Pension Schemes (Contracting-out) Regulations 1996, section 24

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 24. Circumstances when actuary other than actuary appointed under section 47 of the 1995 Act may certify broad equivalence.

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of section 12A(6) of the 1993 Act (scheme actuary appointed under section 47 of the 1995 Act to certify whether pensions are broadly equivalent to or better than those in a reference scheme except in prescribed circumstances) the prescribed circumstances are where a scheme is not required to appoint an actuary for the scheme under section 47 of the 1995 Act.
