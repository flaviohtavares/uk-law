# Occupational Pension Schemes (Contracting-out) Regulations 1996, section 65

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 65. Modification of section 16 of the 1993 Act.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In such a case as is specified in regulation 66— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aa5ed86bc4cc5ba4689f01c5250a7eac]

- (a) section 16(1) of the 1993 Act shall be modified so as to have effect as if there were added at the end thereof the words “or, in respect of the earner’s earnings factor or the weekly equivalent mentioned in section 14(2) for any relevant year in a period of linked qualifying service, shall be taken to be that factor or weekly equivalent as increased in accordance with the provisions of the scheme under regulations made under section 16(3)", and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aa5ed86bc4cc5ba4689f01c5250a7eac]
- (b) where earnings factors of an earner whose accrued rights to guaranteed minimum pensions, including such rights derived from linked qualifying service, have been transferred under section 20(1) of the 1993 Act or regulations made thereunder to another contracted-out scheme, section 16(2) and (3) of that Act shall be modified so as to have effect as if it permitted that scheme to provide for those earnings factors to be determined for the purpose of section 14(2) of that Act without reference to any order coming into force under section 148 of the Administration Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aa5ed86bc4cc5ba4689f01c5250a7eac]
  - (i) after the relevant year in which his service in the contracted-out employment, by reference to the scheme from which those rights were transferred, was terminated where such earnings factors have previously fallen to be determined by reference to orders under section 148 of the Administration Act, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aa5ed86bc4cc5ba4689f01c5250a7eac]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aa5ed86bc4cc5ba4689f01c5250a7eac] in and after the relevant year in which that service was terminated in any other case, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aa5ed86bc4cc5ba4689f01c5250a7eac]

  and as if section 16(3) of the 1993 Act had effect accordingly, save, in a case to which sub-paragraph (ii) applies, for treating the reference to the amount of the increase in section 16(3) as a reference to the amount by which the earnings factors relevant to the weekly equivalent would be increased. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aa5ed86bc4cc5ba4689f01c5250a7eac]
