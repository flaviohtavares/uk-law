# Occupational Pension Schemes (Contracting-out) Regulations 1996, section 62

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 62. Fixed rate revaluation of guaranteed minimum pensions for early leavers.[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This regulation applies to a case where a scheme provides, under section 16(2) of the 1993 Act, for the earnings factors of an earner whose service in contracted-out employment by reference to the scheme is terminated before he attains pensionable age to be determined by reference to the last order that comes into force under section 148 of the Administration Act before the end of the tax year in which his service is terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), in a case to which this regulation applies, the prescribed percentage for the purpose of section 16(3) of the 1993 Act (revaluation of earnings factors) is, in relation to each period of service in respect of which accrued rights to guaranteed minimum pension have been acquired under, or transferred to, the scheme— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31]

- (a) where the period of service terminated before 6th April 1988, 8.5 per cent. compound; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31]
- (b) where that period of service terminated on or after 6th April 1988 but before 6th April 1993, 7.5 per cent. compound; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31]
- (c) where that period of service terminated on or after 6th April 1993 but before 6th April 1997, 7 per cent. compound; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31]
- (d) where that period of service terminates on or after 6th April 1997 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31] but before 6th April 2002, 6.25 per cent. compound;[^key-af33aa6bf75d81b498aee1182c1605fb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31]
- (e)[^key-09550e1c175bc1cd4c0492464ff5e6ce] where that period of service terminates on or after *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31] 6th April 2002 but before 6th April 2007, 4.5 per cent.[^key-db7253b0dd58cd03b24277fb1166cfbe] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31] compound. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31]
- (f)[^key-75bd380c3bfeb6e5abe435cd02b5de53] where that period of service terminates on or after *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31] 6th April 2007 but before 6th April 2012, 4 per cent.[^key-db86eb1879388c942e8e6b7ab3fff4c6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31] compound. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31]
- (g)[^key-8e2efb9d01e0ab6fa92c20b72f7b164a] where that period of service terminates on or after 6th April 2012, 4.75 per cent. compound. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31]

(3) Paragraph (2) only applies so as to permit the same provision to be made for all members of the scheme, so however that separate provision may be made for members as regards their rights to guaranteed minimum pensions under the scheme arising in respect of a transfer or transfer payment made in accordance with section 20(1) (transfer of accrued rights) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31] [^key-2a8c49d58e2f97a4f8e706b9171b0980] ... of the 1993 Act or regulations made under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31] that provision[^key-58c68f2aac9cf46d9ccc2007d88eb729] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31] or for members whose guaranteed minimum pensions are, or are to be, appropriately secured within the meaning of section 19(3) of the 1993 Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4bc186aa03da09a136add5ad114c1c31]
