# Occupational Pension Schemes (Contracting-out) Regulations 1996, section 66

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 66. Circumstances in which the modifications to the 1993 Act set out in regulation 65 apply.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The case referred to in regulation 65 is where, following the termination of an earner’s service in contracted-out employment by reference to a scheme, the following conditions are satisfied, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9c57df86b170c3e1f557a549a755b011]

- (a) a transfer under section 20(1) of the 1993 Act, or regulations made thereunder, of the earner’s accrued rights to guaranteed minimum pensions under that scheme (in this regulation called the “transferring scheme") to another contracted-out scheme (in this regulation called the “receiving scheme") is made, or if already begun is completed, on or after 5th April 1983; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9c57df86b170c3e1f557a549a755b011]
- (b) the earner commences or has commenced employment which is contracted-out by reference to the receiving scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9c57df86b170c3e1f557a549a755b011]
- (c) by virtue of section 14(1) of that Act as modified by regulations made under section 20 of the 1993 Act, the calculation, under the provisions of the receiving scheme, of the earner’s guaranteed minimum pension for the purposes of section 14(2) falls to include earnings factors, or the weekly equivalent derived therefrom, arising out of contracted-out employment in any period of linked qualifying service which was contracted-out employment by reference to the transferring scheme; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9c57df86b170c3e1f557a549a755b011]
- (d) either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9c57df86b170c3e1f557a549a755b011]
  - (i) the receiving scheme provides for the earnings factors or weekly equivalent derived therefrom to be increased at the rate by which they, or it, fell to be increased under the provisions of the transferring scheme or would have fallen to be increased under the provisions of that scheme relating to an earner whose service in contracted-out employment by reference to the scheme is terminated before he attains pensionable age, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9c57df86b170c3e1f557a549a755b011]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9c57df86b170c3e1f557a549a755b011] if the provisions of the transferring scheme provided for those earnings factors to be increased by reference to orders under section 148 of the Administration Act, the receiving scheme provides for those earnings factors or the weekly equivalent derived therefrom to be increased in accordance with the provisions of section 16(2) and (3) of the 1993 Act, or regulations made under section 16(3) of that Act, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9c57df86b170c3e1f557a549a755b011]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9c57df86b170c3e1f557a549a755b011] if the transferring scheme provided for those earnings factors or the weekly equivalent derived therefrom to be increased in accordance with the provisions of either section 16(2) and (3) of the 1993 Act or of regulations made under section 16(3) of that Act, the receiving scheme includes provision for those earnings factors to be increased by reference to orders under section 148 of the Administration Act from the date of termination of the earner’s service in the period of contracted-out employment from which those earnings factors arose, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9c57df86b170c3e1f557a549a755b011]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9c57df86b170c3e1f557a549a755b011] if a transfer of the earner’s accrued rights to the receiving scheme is not one to which sub-paragraph (d)(iii) applies and those rights were previously being increased at a rate calculated by reference to orders under section 148 of the Administration Act, the receiving scheme includes provision for those earnings factors or the weekly equivalent derived therefrom to be increased at a rate calculated otherwise than by reference to orders under section 148 of the Administration Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9c57df86b170c3e1f557a549a755b011]
