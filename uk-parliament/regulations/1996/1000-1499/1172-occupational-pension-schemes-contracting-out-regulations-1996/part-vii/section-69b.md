## 69B. Conversion of guaranteed minimum pensions into other benefits: survivors’ benefits[^c-28d1b07f1597536b5f30954ac0cc3a09]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] For the purposes of Condition 4 of section 24B of the 1993 Act, the circumstances in which, and periods during which, the converted scheme must provide survivors’ benefits are prescribed in this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]

(2) The circumstances are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]

- (a) an earner’s pre-conversion benefits include a guaranteed minimum pension, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
- (b) the scheme before conversion provided that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
  - (i) i)if such an earner dies leaving a widow, widower or surviving civil partner (whether before or after attaining pensionable age), the widow, widower or surviving civil partner will be entitled to a guaranteed minimum pension, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] in a case where section 17(6) of the 1993 Act applies, such a widower’s, widow’s *(Extent: S)*[^c-f97846e2b1417502b11137f1a663b67c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] or surviving civil partner’s guaranteed minimum pension will be payable in the circumstances set out in regulation 57(a) to (c). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]

(3) The periods are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]

- (a) for widows, widowers and surviving civil partners, any period mentioned in section 17(4A)(a) and (b) of the 1993 Act (minimum pensions for widows and widowers), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
- (b) for widows, widowers and surviving civil partners whose entitlement by virtue of the earner’s contributions to a widowed parent’s allowance or bereavement allowance has come to an end at a time after the widow, widower or surviving civil partner attained the age of 45, so much of the period beginning with the time when that entitlement came to an end as neither— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
  - (i) comprises a period during which the widow, widower or surviving civil partner and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] another person are living together as a married couple, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e5ba0d812da22276cfd150804a81b7f3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]another person are living together as a married couple, *(Extent: S)* *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e5ba0d812da22276cfd150804a81b7f3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] nor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] falls after the time of any— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] marriage, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] formation of a civil partnership, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]

    by the widow, widower or surviving civil partner which takes place after the earner’s death, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
- (c) for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] widows whose spouse was a man[^c-a37ea05250724b2ccd3e5435d2c016dd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4], any period for which a widowed mother’s allowance or widow’s pension is payable to the widow by virtue of the earner’s contributions, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
- (d) for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] widowers, widows whose spouse was a woman[^c-ebae63bf592103ec1d2fc9981596356a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] and surviving civil partners, any period which is, by virtue of regulation 58, a prescribed period for the purposes of section 17(6) of the 1993 Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-45627ffad0bb2034fc6737f5f421a9c7][^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] The following provisions do not apply where the earner died before 5th December 2005— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] paragraph (3)(b)(i) so far as it relates to the living together of two people of the same sex; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] paragraph (3)(b)(ii)(aa) so far as it relates to a marriage involving two people of the same sex; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4] paragraph (3)(b)(ii)(bb). *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1a1952dfa4c8691723af1aa81537d4]
