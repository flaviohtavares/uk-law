# The Electricity (Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976) (Exemptions) Order 1996, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Electricity (Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976) (Exemptions) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 10th June 1996.

(2) In this Order—

- “the 1976 Act” means the Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976[^f00003] and “agreement”, “information provision” and “restriction” have the same meaning as in that Act;
- “the 1989 Act” means the Electricity Act 1989; and
- “the Department” means the Department of Trade and Industry.
