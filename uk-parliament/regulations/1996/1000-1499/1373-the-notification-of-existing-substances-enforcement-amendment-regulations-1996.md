# The Notification of Existing Substances (Enforcement) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 1994/1806](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1806) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001], being the Minister designated[^f00002] for the purpose of that subsection in relation to the notification and control of substances, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

## 1. Citation, commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Notification of Existing Substances (Enforcement) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 20th June 1996.

## 2. Amendment of the Notification of Existing Substances (Enforcement) Regulations 1994

The following amendments are made to the Notification of Existing Substances (Enforcement) Regulations 1994[^f00003]:—

- (a) in regulation 4(3) (g), for the words “(in the absence of persons other than a person nominated to be present and any person whom the inspector may allow to be present)” there shall be substituted the words “(in the absence of anyone, other than someone nominated by that person to be present and anyone whom the inspector may allow to be present)”;
- (b) in regulation 5(3), the words “which is not to take immediate effect” shall be omitted; and;
- (c) in regulation 7(3), for the words “the statutory maximum” there shall be substituted “level 5 on the standard scale”.
