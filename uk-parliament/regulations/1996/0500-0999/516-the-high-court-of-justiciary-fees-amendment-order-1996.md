# The High Court of Justiciary Fees Amendment Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 516 (S.50)

HIGH COURT OF JUSTICIARY, SCOTLAND

The High Court of Justiciary Fees Amendment Order 1996

Made

28th February 1996

Laid before Parliament

11th March 1996

Coming into force

1st April 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2 of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the High Court of Justiciary Fees Amendment Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

**2.** For the Table of Fees in the Schedule to the High Court of Justiciary Fees Order 1984[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Table of Fees set out in the Schedule to this Order.

**3.** Article 2(b) of the High Court of Justiciary Fees Amendment Order 1994[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

21st February 1996

We concur,

Simon Burns

Liam Fox

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

28th February 1996
