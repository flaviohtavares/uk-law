# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1996, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Sum specified for child benefit

In regulation 2 of the Child Benefit and Social Security (Fixing and Adjustment of Rates) Regulations 1976[^f00026] (weekly rates of child benefit)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (1), for the sum of £10.40 there shall be substituted the sum of £10.80;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) of paragraph (1), for the sum of £8.45 there shall be substituted the sum of £8.80; and
- (c) in paragraph (2), the sum specified is £6.30.
