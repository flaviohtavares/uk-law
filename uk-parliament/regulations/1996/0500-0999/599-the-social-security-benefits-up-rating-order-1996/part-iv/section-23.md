# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 1996, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Transitional Provisions

Notwithstanding the increases of the sums payable in respect of family credit and disability working allowance made by articles 16 and 17 of this Order, where a period of entitlement to family credit or disability working allowance is running at 9th April 1996, the rate at which it is payable shall be the weekly rate in force for the period prior to 9th April 1996 until the end of the period of entitlement.
