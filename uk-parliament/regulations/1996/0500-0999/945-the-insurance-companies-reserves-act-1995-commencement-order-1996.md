# The Insurance Companies (Reserves) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 945 (C.17)

INSURANCE

The Insurance Companies (Reserves) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1996

Made

24th March 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 4(2) of the Insurance Companies (Reserves) Act 1995[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Insurance Companies (Reserves) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1996.

**2.** Sections 1 and 3 of the Insurance Companies (Reserves) Act 1995 shall come into force on 30th April 1996.

Anthony Nelson

Minister of State,

Department of Trade and Industry

24th March 1996
