# The Insurance Companies (Accounts and Statements) Regulations 1996, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Insurance Companies (Accounts and Statements) Regulations 1996.

(2) Regulation 34 below shall come into force on 30th April 1996 and all other regulations shall come into force on 23rd December 1996.
