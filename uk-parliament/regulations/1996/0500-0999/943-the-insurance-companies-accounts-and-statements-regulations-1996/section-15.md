# The Insurance Companies (Accounts and Statements) Regulations 1996, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Currencies other than sterling

Every company which, in respect of a financial year, prepares a Form under regulation 11 or 13 above containing figures in a currency other than sterling shall prepare Form 36 in accordance with the requirements of Schedule 2 below.
