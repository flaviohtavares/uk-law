# The Insurance Companies (Accounts and Statements) Regulations 1996, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Additional information on general business (direct and facultative reconciliation business)

(1) Every company which carries on general business shall, with respect to the financial year in question and in accordance with the requirements of Schedule 2 below, prepare—

- (a) Form 33 for business reported on Forms 21, 22 and 23 but not reported on Form 31 or 32; and
- (b) Form 35 for business reported on Forms 24 and 25 but not reported on Form 34.

(2) No separate Form 33 or 35 need be prepared where, in the cases referred to in regulation 13(3)(a) or (b) above, no separate Forms 31, 32 or 34 need be prepared.
