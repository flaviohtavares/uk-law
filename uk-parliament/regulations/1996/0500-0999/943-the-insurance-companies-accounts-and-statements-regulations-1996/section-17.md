# The Insurance Companies (Accounts and Statements) Regulations 1996, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Additional information on long term business

Every company which carries on long term business shall, in respect of the financial year in question and in accordance with the requirements of Schedule 3 below, prepare—

- (a) Forms 41 to 45 in respect of each revenue account prepared separately under regulation 8(b)(i) above; and
- (b) summary Forms 41 to 44 in respect of each summary Form 40 prepared under regulation 8(b)(ii) above.
