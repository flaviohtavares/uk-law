# The Insurance Companies (Accounts and Statements) Regulations 1996, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Content and form of accounts

Every account, balance sheet, note, statement, report and certificate required to be prepared by a company pursuant to section 17(1), (2) and (3) of the Act (annual accounts and balance sheets) shall be prepared in the manner hereinafter specified and shall fairly state the information provided on the basis required by these Regulations.
