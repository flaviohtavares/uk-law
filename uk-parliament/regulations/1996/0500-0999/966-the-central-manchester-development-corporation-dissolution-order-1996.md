# The Central Manchester Development Corporation (Dissolution) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 966

URBAN DEVELOPMENT

The Central Manchester Development Corporation (Dissolution) Order 1996

Made

28th March 1996

Coming into force

1st April 1996

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 166 of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having consulted the councils of the City of Manchester and the Metropolitan Borough of Trafford, hereby makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Central Manchester Development Corporation (Dissolution) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

## 2. Dissolution of the corporation

The Central Manchester Development Corporation shall be dissolved on 1st July 1996.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment

David Curry

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment

28th March 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

By virtue of section 166(3) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980, on the coming into force of this Order the Central Manchester Development Corporation will cease to act except for the purpose of preparing its final accounts and report and winding up its affairs. The corporation will be dissolved on 1st July 1996.
