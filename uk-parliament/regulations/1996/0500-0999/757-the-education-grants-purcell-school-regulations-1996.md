# The Education (Grants) (Purcell School) Regulations 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 757

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Grants) (Purcell School) Regulations 1996

Made

12th March 1996

Laid before Parliament

12th March 1996

Coming into force

27th March 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 100(1)(b) and (3) of the Education Act 1944[^f00001] and vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Grants) (Purcell School) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 27th March 1996.

## 2. Payment of grant

The Secretary of State may before 31st March 1996 pay a grant of such amount as she may determine to the governors of the Purcell School, Harrow (“the School”) in respect of expenditure to be incurred by the governors on or in connection with the purchase of premises to accommodate the School at a different site, or in connection with the transfer of the School to those premises.

## 3. Conditions of payment

The payment of grant under these Regulations shall be dependent on the fulfilment of such conditions as may be specified by the Secretary of State.

## 4. Requirements to be observed

Where grant is paid under these Regulations the governors of the School shall comply with such requirements (including requirements as to the repayment of grant or the payment to the Secretary of State of other sums representing the value of the premises purchased with the aid of grant or interest on sums due to her) as may be specified by the Secretary of State.

Cheryl Gillan

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Education and Employment

12th March 1996

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations authorise the Secretary of State to pay before 31st March 1996 a grant to the governors of the Purcell School, Harrow, to help meet the expenditure to be incurred by them in acquiring and transferring to new premises for the School. Payment is subject to such conditions and requirements as the Secretary of State may specify.
