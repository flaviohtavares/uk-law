# The Coroners' Districts (Designation of Relevant Councils) (Wales) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 662

CORONERS

The Coroners' Districts (Designation of Relevant Councils) (Wales) Order 1996

Made

7th March 1996

Coming into force

1st April 1996

The Secretary of State in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 1(1)(bb) of the Coroners Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Coroners' Districts (Designation of Relevant Councils) (Wales) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

**2.** For each coroner’s district listed in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order, being a coroner’s district created by the Coroners' Districts (Wales) Order 1996[^f00002] lying partly in each of two or more Welsh counties or county boroughs, the council specified opposite thereto in column 2 of that Schedule is hereby designated as the relevant council for the purposes of the Coroners Act 1988.

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

7th March 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates relevant councils for the coroners' districts in Wales listed in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order. Such designation is necessary where a coroner’s district in Wales lies partly in each of two or more Welsh counties or county boroughs. The relevant council is responsible for the appointment of coroners and other functions under the Coroners Act 1988.
