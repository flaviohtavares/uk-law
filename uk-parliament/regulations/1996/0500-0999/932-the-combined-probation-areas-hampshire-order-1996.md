# The Combined Probation Areas (Hampshire) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 932

PROBATION

The Combined Probation Areas (Hampshire) Order 1996

Made

24th March 1996

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2 of the Probation Service Act 1993[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that section, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Hampshire) Order 1996.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 1st April 1996 save that, for the purpose of making appointments to the probation committee for the Hampshire Probation Area to take effect on that date, this Order shall come into force forthwith.

**2.** That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Hampshire Probation Area[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting in column 3—

- (a) “1” for “2” in the entry relating to the petty sessions area of the Isle of Wight;
- (b) “1” for “3” in the entries relating to the petty sessions areas of New Forest, North East Hampshire, North West Hampshire, South East Hampshire and Southampton; and
- (c) “1” for “4” in the entry relating to the petty sessions area of South Hampshire.

Home Office

Blatch

Minister of State

24th March 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/1713](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1713)) so as to reduce from twenty-one to seven the number of justices appointed to the Hampshire Probation Committee.
