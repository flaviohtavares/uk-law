# The Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) Regulations 1996, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Interpretation of Part III

(1) In this Part—

- “Regulation 823/87” means Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 823/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/0823), as amended, laying down special provisions relating to quality wines produced in specified regions[^f00026];
- “quality wines psr” has the meaning assigned to it by Article 1(2) of Regulation 823/87;
- “specified regions” means, unless the context otherwise requires, English Vineyards and Welsh Vineyards as defined in regulation 15.

(2) Other expressions used in this Part have, in so far as the context admits, the same meanings as in Regulation 823/87.
