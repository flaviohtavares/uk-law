# The Offshore Installations and Wells (Design and Construction, etc.) Regulations 1996, section 15

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15. Design with a view to suspension and abandonment

*Extent: E+W+S*

The well-operator shall ensure that a well is so designed and constructed that, so far as is reasonably practicable—

- (a) it can be suspended or abandoned in a safe manner; and
- (b) after its suspension or abandonment there can be no unplanned escape of fluids from it or from the reservoir to which it led.
