# The Offshore Installations and Wells (Design and Construction, etc.) Regulations 1996, section 23

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 23. Certificates of exemption

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) and to any of the provisions imposed by the European Union[^key-75c03eebb7c54b375726a11453a496f0] (as they had effect immediately before IP completion day)[^key-92f052b3be7baaecf5e41f0123353eea] in respect of the encouragement of improvements in the safety and health of workers at work or under Directive 2013/30/[^key-7815ca5ea7d5c0f66f69dc033d74b17e]EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 June 2013 on safety of offshore oil and gas operations and amending Directive [2004/35/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/35), the Executive may, by a certificate in writing, exempt any person, installation, well or class of persons, installations or wells from any requirement or prohibition imposed by these Regulations and any such exemption may be granted subject to conditions and with or without limit of time and may be revoked by a certificate in writing at any time.

(2) The Executive shall not grant any such exemption unless, having regard to the circumstances of the case and, in particular, to—

- (a) the conditions, if any, which it proposes to attach to the exemption; and
- (b) any other requirements imposed by or under any enactments which apply to the case,

it is satisfied that the health and safety of persons who are likely to be affected by the exemption will not be prejudiced in consequence of it.
