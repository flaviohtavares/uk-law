# The Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 625 (C.12)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1996

Made

6th March 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 172(2) of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1996.

**2.** Section 22 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (management of secure accommodation) shall come into force on 8th March 1996.

Stephen Dorrell

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Department of Health

6th March 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 8th March 1996 section 22 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (management of secure accommodation).
