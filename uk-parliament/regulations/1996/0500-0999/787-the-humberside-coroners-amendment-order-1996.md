# The Humberside (Coroners) (Amendment) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 787

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

CORONERS

The Humberside (Coroners) (Amendment) Order 1996

Made

14th March 1996

Laid before Parliament

15th March 1996

Coming into force

1st April 1996

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 17 and 26 of the Local Government Act 1992[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Humberside (Coroners) (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

**2.** In articles 3(2) and 4(b) of the Humberside (Coroners) Order 1996[^f00002], for the words “North Lincolnshire” there shall be substituted the words “North East Lincolnshire”.

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

14th March 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Humberside (Coroners) Order 1996 to provide that when that Order comes into force on 1st April 1996 the North East Lincolnshire District Council shall become responsible for the appointment of coroners and other functions in connection with coroners in the North Lincolnshire and Grimsby Coroner’s District.
