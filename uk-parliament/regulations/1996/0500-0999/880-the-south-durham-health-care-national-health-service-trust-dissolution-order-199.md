# The South Durham Health Care National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 880

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The South Durham Health Care National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1996

Made

18th March 1996

Coming into force

1st April 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the South Durham Health Care National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

The South Durham Health Care National Health Service Trust established by the South Durham Health Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00005] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

18th March 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st April 1996 of the South Durham Health Care National Health Service Trust.
