# The Local Government (Direct Labour Organisations) (Accounts) (Scotland) Regulations 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 784 (S.90)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SCOTLAND

The Local Government (Direct Labour Organisations) (Accounts) (Scotland) Regulations 1996

Made

4th March 1996

Laid before Parliament

14th March 1996

Coming into force

1st April 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 10(4) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Direct Labour Organisations) (Accounts) (Scotland) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

## 2. Amendment of section 10(2) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980

Section 10(2) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00002] as it applies to Scotland is amended as follows—

- (a) by substituting for paragraph (a) the following paragraphs—

  > - (a) general highway works on a trunk road;
  > - (aa) general highway works on a road other than a trunk road;

  ; and
- (b) by inserting at the end the words “, and in this subsection “trunk road” has the meaning given in section 151(1) of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00003]”.

George Kynoch

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

4th March 1996

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Local authorities who undertake construction or maintenance work are required by section 10(1) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 to keep separate accounts in respect of each description of work in section 10(2) of the Act. These Regulations further amend the descriptions of construction or maintenance work in section 10(2) so as to distinguish in Scotland between trunk roads and roads other than trunk roads. The previous amendment to section 10(2) for Scotland was made by the Local Government (Direct Labour Organisations) (Accounts) (Scotland) Regulations 1982 (S.I. [1982/319](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1982/319)) which provides a separate description for general water and sewerage work.
