# The Essex Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2602

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE,ENGLAND AND WALES

The Essex Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1996

Made

14th October 1996

Coming into force

28th October 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Essex Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1996 and shall come into force on 28th October 1996.

## 2. Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

In Article 4 of the Essex Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00003] for “5 non-executive” there shall be substituted “4 non-executive” and for “5 executive” there shall be substituted “4 executive”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

John Horam

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Health

14th October 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the constitution of an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by reducing the number of non-executive directors from 5 to 4 and the number of executive directors from 5 to 4.
