# The Bodmin and Wenford Light Railway Order 1996, SCHEDULE 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 1 — THE BOARD’S RAILWAY

Article 2(1)

A portion (10,060 metres in length) of the former Bodmin and Wenford Railway in the district of North Cornwall in the County of Cornwall, authorised by the Bodmin and Wadebridge Railway Act 1832[^f00007] as amended by—

(1) the Bodmin and Wadebridge Railway Act 1835[^f00008];

(2) the Bodmin and Wadebridge Railway Act 1865[^f00009];

(3) the North Cornwall Railway Act 1882[^f00010];

(4) the South-western Railway Act 1886[^f00011]; and

(5) the South-western Railway Act 1891[^f00012],

commencing in the parish of Lanivet at Boscarne Junction on the railway authorised to be worked as a light railway by the Bodmin Railway Centre Light Railway Order 1989[^f00013], passing through the town of Bodmin and the parishes of Helland and Blisland, and terminating in the parish of St. Breward at a point on the north side of the unclassified road from Blisland to St. Tudy at the boundary of the premises of ECC International Limited.
