# The Bodmin and Wenford Light Railway Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2867

TRANSPORT

The Bodmin and Wenford Light Railway Order 1996

Made

1st November 1996

Coming into force

2nd November 1996

The Secretary of State for Transport, on the application of the North Cornwall District Council and Bodmin and Wenford Rail Freight Limited for an Order under the Light Railways Act 1896[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”), being satisfied in accordance with section 7 of the Act that its requirements in relation to the publication and service of notices, and consideration of objections, have been satisfied, and having considered the Order as required by section 9 of the Act, and in exercise of powers conferred by sections 3, 7 and 10 to 12 of the Act and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—
