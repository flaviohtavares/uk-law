# The Bodmin and Wenford Light Railway Order 1996, SCHEDULE 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 4 — PARTICULARS OF SIGNS TO BE PROVIDED AT THE LEVEL CROSSINGS OF FOOTPATHS

Article 9(2)

Signs shall be provided on both sides of the railway bearing the words “Stop, look, listen, beware of trains” and shall face towards persons approaching the level crossing.
