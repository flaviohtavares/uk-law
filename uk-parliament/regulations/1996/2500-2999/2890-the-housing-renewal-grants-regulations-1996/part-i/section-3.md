# The Housing Renewal Grants Regulations 1996, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Persons from abroad

(1) No grant shall be payable under Chapter I of Part I of the Act if the applicant, or any of the applicants, is a person from abroad within the meaning of regulation 7A (persons from abroad) of the Housing Benefit (General) Regulations 1987[^f00027], as those regulations have effect from time to time.

(2) In this regulation, “applicant” means a person who applies, or who joins in an application, for a grant under Chapter I of Part I of the Act.
