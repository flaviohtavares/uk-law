# The Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2926

PENSIONS

The Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order 1996

Made

21st November 1996

Laid before Parliament

28th November 1996

Coming into force

1st January 1997

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3 to the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order 1996.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 1st January 1997.

## 2. The revaluation percentage for each revaluation period

For the purposes of paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3 to the Pension Schemes Act 1993, the revaluation percentage for each revaluation period specified in column 1 below is the appropriate percentage specified in column 2.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made, as required by paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3 to the Pension Schemes Act 1993, in the revaluation year beginning on 1st January 1996. The revaluation percentages specified are relevant to the revaluation of benefits under occupational pension schemes, as required by section 84 of, and Schedule 3 to, that Act.

This Order does not impose any costs on business.
