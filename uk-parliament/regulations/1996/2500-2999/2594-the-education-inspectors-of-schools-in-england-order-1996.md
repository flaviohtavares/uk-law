# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2594

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 1996

Made

15th October 1996

Laid before Parliament

25th October 1996

Coming into force

18th November 1996

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of October 1996

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the Education (Schools) Act 1992[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 18th November 1996.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 19th November 1996.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

- Dorian Bradley
- Janet Brennan
- Christine Mary Brown
- Peter Daw
- Anthony Peter Matthews
- Michael Raleigh
- Maurice John Smith
- Barbara Wintersgill

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England.
