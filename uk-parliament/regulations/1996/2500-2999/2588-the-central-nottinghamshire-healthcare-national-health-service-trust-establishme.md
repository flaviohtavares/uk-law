# The Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2588

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1996

Made

12th October 1996

Coming into force

12th November 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having completed consultation with the Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1996 and shall come into force on 12th November 1996.

## 2. Amendment of Establishment Order

In Article 3(2) of the Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00004] (functions of the trust)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “Newark General Hospital, London Road, Newark-on-Trent, Nottinghamshire NG24 1TG” there shall be substituted “Newark Hospital, Bowbridge Road, Newark, Nottinghamshire NG24 4DK”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “Pine House, Ransom Hospital, Rainworth, Nr Mansfield, Nottinghamshire NG21 0ER” there shall be substituted “Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare Trust Headquarters, Southwell Road, Mansfield, Nottinghamshire NG18 4HH”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Health

12th October 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992 to change the address at which hospital accommodation and services and community health services are to be provided.
