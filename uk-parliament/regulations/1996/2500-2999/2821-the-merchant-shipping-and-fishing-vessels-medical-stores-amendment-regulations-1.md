# The Merchant Shipping and Fishing Vessels (Medical Stores) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2821

MERCHANT SHIPPING

SAFETY

The Merchant Shipping and Fishing Vessels (Medical Stores) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

Made

5th November 1996

Laid before Parliament

14th November 1996

Coming into force

5th December 1996

Whereas the Secretary of State is a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to the safety of ships and the health and safety of person on them;

And whereas, in so far as the following Regulations are made in exercise of powers conferred by section 85 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00003], the Secretary of State has in pursuance of subsection (4) of section 86 of the said Act consulted the persons referred to in that subsection;

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by:

- (a) the said section 2(2);
- (b) sections 85(1)(a) and (3) and (5) to (7) and 86(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995;
- (c) section 103(3) of the Medicines Act 1968[^f00004];

and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping and Fishing Vessels (Medical Stores) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 5th December 1996.

## 2. Amendment of 1995 Regulations

In the Table in regulation 4(1) of the Merchant Shipping and Fishing Vessels (Medical Stores) Regulations 1995[^f00005] for the words “no more than 30 nautical miles from” in Column 1, entry 3 there shall be substituted the words “very close to”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Goschen

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

5th November 1996

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Merchant Shipping and Fishing Vessels (Medical Stores) Regulations 1995. The amendment adopts the wording of Council Directive [92/29/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0029) of 31st March 1992 (O.J. No. L113, 30.4.92, p. 19), which the 1995 Regulations implement, in defining the area of operation for vessels carrying category C stores as “very close to shore”.
