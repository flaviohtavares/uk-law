# The Broadcasting (Percentage of Television Multiplex Revenue) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2759

BROADCASTING

The Broadcasting (Percentage of Television Multiplex Revenue) Order 1996

Made

30th October 1996

Laid before Parliament

30th October 1996

Coming into force

20th November 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 13(2) of the Broadcasting Act 1996[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Broadcasting (Percentage of Television Multiplex Revenue) Order 1996.

**2.** No percentage of multiplex revenue shall be specified under paragraph (f) of section 7(1) of the Broadcasting Act 1996 in relation to any notice published by the Commission in pursuance of that section while this Order is in force.

**3.** This Order shall come into force on 20th November 1996 and shall cease to have effect on 30th September 2002.

Virginia Bottomley

Secretary of State for National Heritage

30th October 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that no percentage of multiplex revenue will be payable by the holder of a multiplex licence granted by the Independent Television Commission pursuant to any notice published by them during the period for which this Order remains in force.
