# The Parliamentary Commissioner (No. 2) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2601

PARLIAMENTARY COMMISSIONER

The Parliamentary Commissioner (No. 2) Order 1996

Made

15th October 1996

Laid before Parliament

25th October 1996

Coming into force

18th November 1996

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of October 1996

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(2) of the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows: —

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Parliamentary Commissioner (No. 2) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 18th November 1996.

**2.** Schedule 2 to the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967[^f00002] shall be amended by the insertion in the appropriate place in alphabetical order of the entries “English Sports Council” and “United Kingdom Sports Council”.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order adds the English Sports Council and the United Kingdom Sports Council to the list of departments and authorities subject to investigation by the Parliamentary Commissioner for Administration.
