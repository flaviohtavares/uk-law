# The Beef (Marketing Payment) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 1996/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2005) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

1996 No. 2561

AGRICULTURE

The Beef (Marketing Payment) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

Made

8th October 1996

Laid before Parliament

11th October 1996

Coming into force

1st November 1996

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, being Ministers designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred on them by that section and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:—

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Beef (Marketing Payment) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 1st November 1996.

## 2. Amendment

Regulation 9 of the Beef (Marketing Payment) Regulations 1996[^f00003], shall be replaced by—

> **9.** Except where the appropriate Minister recovers from any applicant a payment or part of a payment paid to that applicant as a result of an error of that Minister, he may charge interest on any payment recovered under regulation 8 at the rate of one percentage point above the sterling three months London Interbank Offered Rate on a day to day basis for the period from the date of payment to the date of reimbursement by the applicant.

.

Douglas Hogg

Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

7th October 1996

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Jonathan Evans

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Welsh Office

8th October 1996

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct an error in regulation 9 of the Beef (Marketing Payment) Regulations 1996 so that the period for which the Minister may charge interest on a payment recovered pursuant to regulation 8 is specified.
