# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1996, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Mobile cranes as exempted goods vehicles

Paragraph (1)(c) of regulation 48 shall apply until 1st January 1998 as if the words “except a mobile crane” were omitted.
