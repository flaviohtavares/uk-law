# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1996, section 75

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 75. Service personnel

The traffic commissioner for the South Eastern Traffic Area is hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 183(6) of the 1988 Act (discharge of Part IV functions in relation to HM Forces).
