# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1996, section 72

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 72. Saving in respect of entitlement to drive mobile project vehicles

In relation to a person who was at a time before 1st January 1997 the holder of a licence authorising the driving of vehicles of a class included in category B (except a licence authorising only the driving of vehicles in sub-category B1 or B1 (invalid carriages)), regulation 6(7) shall apply as if paragraphs (b) and (c) and the words “on behalf of a non-commercial body” were omitted.
