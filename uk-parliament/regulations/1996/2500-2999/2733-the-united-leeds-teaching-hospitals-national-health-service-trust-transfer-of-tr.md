# The United Leeds Teaching Hospitals National Health Service trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2733

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The United Leeds Teaching Hospitals National Health Service trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

Made

28th October 1996

Laid before Parliament

4th November 1996

Coming into force

25th November 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities previously managed by the former Leeds Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the United Leeds Teaching Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted the Leeds Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the United Leeds Teaching Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 25th November 1996.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the Leeds Health Authority, established on 1st April 1996[^f00003];
- “the old Authority” means the former Leeds Health Authority, established on 1st April 1991[^f00004] and abolished on 1st April 1996[^f00005];
- “the Trust” means the United Leeds Teaching Hospitals National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the old Authority and the Trust on 19th December 1995, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the old Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

On 25th November 1996 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health,

John Horam

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

28th October 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the United Leeds Teaching Hospitals National Health Service Trust on 25th November 1996 trust property previously held by the former Leeds Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, IN33F, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
