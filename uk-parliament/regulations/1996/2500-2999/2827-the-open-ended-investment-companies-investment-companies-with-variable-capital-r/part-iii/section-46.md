# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996, section 46

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 46. Share transfers[^c-79b839cd4194584f960c5c5fc9a9fd21]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

Schedule 5 to these Regulations (which makes provision for the transfer of registered and bearer shares in an investment company with variable capital) shall have effect.
