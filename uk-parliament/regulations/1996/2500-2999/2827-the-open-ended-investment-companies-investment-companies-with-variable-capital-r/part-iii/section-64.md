# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Mergers and divisions

Schedule 7 to these Regulations (which makes provision with respect to mergers and divisions involving investment companies with variable capital) shall have effect.
