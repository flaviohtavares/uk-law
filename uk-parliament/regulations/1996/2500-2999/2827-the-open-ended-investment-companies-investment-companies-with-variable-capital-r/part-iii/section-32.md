# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Capacity of company

(1) The validity of an act done by an investment company with variable capital shall not be called into question on the ground of lack of capacity by reason of anything in these Regulations, SIB regulations or the company’s instrument of incorporation.

(2) Nothing in paragraph (1) above shall affect the duty of the directors to observe any limitations on their powers.
