# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996, section 34

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. No duty to enquire as to capacity etc[^c-5727973b26e5561564bcc1fea6febdcb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

Subject to regulation 33(4)(b)(ii) above, a party to a transaction with an investment company with variable capital is not bound to enquire—

- (a) as to whether the transaction is permitted by these Regulations, SIB regulations or the company’s instrument of incorporation; or
- (b) as to any limitation on the powers referred to in paragraph (1)(a) or (b) of regulation 33 above.
