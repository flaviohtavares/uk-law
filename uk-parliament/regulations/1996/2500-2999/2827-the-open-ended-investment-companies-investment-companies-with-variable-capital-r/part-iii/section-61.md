# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996, section 61

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 61. Reports: accounts[^c-56e9416977fe50289f5509e544508a12]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The annual report of an investment company with variable capital shall, in respect of the annual accounting period to which it relates, contain accounts of the company.

(2) The company’s auditors shall make a report to the company’s shareholders in respect of the accounts of the company contained in its annual report.

(3) A copy of the auditors' report shall form part of the company’s annual report.
