# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996, section 49

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 49. Particulars to appear in correspondence etc[^c-5ba62bc90536a5b04f86b519b89dcb4c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Every investment company with variable capital shall have the following particulars mentioned in legible characters in all letters of the company and in all other documents issued by the company in the course of business, that is to say—

- (a) the company’s place of registration;
- (b) the number with which it is registered;
- (c) the address of its head office; and
- (d) the fact that it is an investment company with variable capital.

(2) Where, in accordance with section 705 of the 1985 Act[^f00020] (companies' registered numbers) (as that section has effect by virtue of Schedule 1 to these Regulations), the registrar of companies makes any change of existing registered numbers in respect of any investment company with variable capital then, for a period of three years beginning with the date on which the notification of the change is sent to the company by the registrar, the requirement of paragraph (1)(b) above is satisfied by the use of either the old number or the new.
