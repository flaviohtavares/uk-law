# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996, section 48

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 48. Name to appear in correspondence etc[^c-d158c426812c5cd822f687504bedd52e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Every investment company with variable capital shall have its name mentioned in legible characters in all letters of the company and in all other documents issued by the company in the course of business.

(2) If an officer of a company or a person on the company’s behalf signs or authorises to be signed on behalf of the company any cheque or order for money or goods in which the company’s name is not mentioned as required by paragraph (1) above he is personally liable to the holder of the cheque or order for money or goods for the amount of it (unless it is duly paid by the company).
