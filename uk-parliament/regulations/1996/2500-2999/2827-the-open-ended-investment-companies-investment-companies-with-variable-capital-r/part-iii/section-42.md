# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996, section 42

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 42. Bearer shares

An investment company with variable capital may, if its instrument of incorporation so provides, issue shares (“bearer shares”) evidenced by a share certificate, or by any other documentary evidence of title for which provision is made in the instrument of incorporation, which indicates—

- (a) that the holder of the document is entitled to the shares specified in it; and
- (b) that no entry will be made on the register of shareholders identifying the holder of those shares.
