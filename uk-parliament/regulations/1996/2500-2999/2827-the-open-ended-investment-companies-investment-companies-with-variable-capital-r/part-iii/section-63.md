# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Auditors

Schedule 6 to these Regulations (which makes provision with respect to the auditors of investment companies with variable capital) shall have effect.
