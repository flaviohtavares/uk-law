# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996, section 43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 43. Register of shareholders

Schedule 4 to these Regulations (which makes provision with respect to the register of shareholders of an investment company with variable capital) shall have effect.
