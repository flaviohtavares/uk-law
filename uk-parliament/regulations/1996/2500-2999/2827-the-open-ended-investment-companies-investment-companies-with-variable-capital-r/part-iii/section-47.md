# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996, section 47

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. Power incidental to carrying on business[^c-e60a3104f4545dc889bcda69474fd33b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

An investment company with variable capital shall have power to do all such things as are incidental or conducive to the carrying on of its business.
