# The Open-Ended Investment Companies (Investment Companies with Variable Capital) Regulations 1996, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Investigations: functions of Secretary of State and SIB

In regulations 22 to 24 below, “relevant authority” means the Secretary of State or SIB and, in relation to any investigation carried out by an inspector, means the person who appointed the inspector in question.
