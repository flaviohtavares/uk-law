# The Trusts of Land and Appointment of Trustees Act 1996 (Commencement) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2974 (C. 91)

TRUSTS OF LAND

The Trusts of Land and Appointment of Trustees Act 1996 (Commencement) Order 1996

Made

25th November 1996

Coming into force

1st January 1997

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27(2) of the Trusts of Land and Appointment of Trustees Act 1996[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Trusts of Land and Appointment of Trustees Act 1996 (Commencement) Order 1996.

**2.** The Trusts of Land and Appointments of Trustees Act 1996 shall come into force on 1st January 1997.

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.*

Dated 25th November 1996.

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides that the Trusts of Land and Appointment of Trustees Act 1996 shall come into force on 1st January 1997.
