# The Licensing (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 1996 Commencement Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2670 (C. 78) (S.209)

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Licensing (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 1996 Commencement Order 1996

Made

14th October 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3(2) of the Licensing (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 1996[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 1996 Commencement Order 1996.

## 2. Appointed date

The date appointed as the date on which the Licensing (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 1996 shall come into force is 21st October 1996.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Minister of State,

Scottish Office

14th October 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 21st October 1996 the Licensing (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 1996.
