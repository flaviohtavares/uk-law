# The Intelligence Services Act 1994 (Dependent Territories) (Amendment) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2876

INTELLIGENCE SERVICES

The Intelligence Services Act 1994 (Dependent Territories) (Amendment) Order 1996

Made

19th November 1996

Coming into force

10th December 1996

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 19th day of November 1996

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12(4) of the Intelligence Services Act 1994[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Intelligence Services Act 1994 (Dependent Territories) (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 10th December 1996.

**2.** The Intelligence Services Act 1994 (Dependent Territories) Order 1995[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

- “Bermuda” is added to the list in the Schedule to the Order (colonies to which certain provisions of the Act extend).

N. H Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends section 5(1) (warrants) and section 11(1) (interpretation) of the Intelligence Services Act 1994 to Bermuda.
