# The Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 (Commencement No. 3) (Scotland) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2797 (C.82) (S.215)

ENERGY CONSERVATION

The Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 (Commencement No. 3) (Scotland) Order 1996

Made

31st October 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 9(2) and (3) of the Home Energy Conservation Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 (Commencement No. 3) (Scotland) Order 1996.

## 2. Commencement

The Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 shall come into force in Scotland on 1st December 1996.

Raymond S Robertson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Scottish Office

31st October 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 (“the Act”) into force in Scotland on 1st December 1996. The Act is amended by the Energy Conservation Act [1996 (c. 38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/38) which is being brought into force in Scotland on the same date by virtue of S.I. [1996/2796](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2796).

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Act has been brought into force in Northern Ireland by [1995 S.R. No. 455](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1995/455) (on 1st January 1996 for sections 3(1) and 4(1) and (2), and on 1st April 1996 for the remainder of the Act) and in England by S.I. [1995/3340](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/3340) (on 15th January 1996 for sections 3(1) and 4(1) and (2), and on 1st April 1996 for the remainder of the Act). The Act remains to be brought into force in Wales.
