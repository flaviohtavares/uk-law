# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Cleveland Police Authority) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 2965

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Cleveland Police Authority) Order 1996

Made

26th November 1996

Laid before Parliament

3rd December 1996

Coming into force

24th December 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(9) and 15(5) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Cleveland Police Authority) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 24th December 1996.

**2.** In this Order “the Authority” means the Cleveland Police Authority.

## 3. Exemption of cleaning of police buildings

The cleaning of police buildings by the Authority shall not be treated as a defined activity so long as it is carried out within their area before 30th June 1997.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Paul Beresford

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of the Environment

26th November 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (competition), work falling within certain defined activities may be carried out by police authorities only if particular conditions are fulfilled.

This Order exempts from the requirements of Part I the cleaning of police buildings by the Cleveland Police Authority so long as it is undertaken within their area before 30th June 1997.
