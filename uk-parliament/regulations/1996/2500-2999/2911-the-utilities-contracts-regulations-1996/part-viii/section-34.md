# The Utilities Contracts Regulations 1996, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Revocation

These Regulations revoke the Utilities Supply and Works Contracts Regulations 1992[^f00026] and the Utilities Supply and Works Contracts (Amendment) Regulations 1993[^f00027].
