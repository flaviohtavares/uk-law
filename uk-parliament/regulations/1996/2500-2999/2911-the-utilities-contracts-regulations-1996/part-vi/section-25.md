# The Utilities Contracts Regulations 1996, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Sub-contracting

A utility may require a provider to indicate in his tender what part of the contract if any he intends to sub-contract to another person.
