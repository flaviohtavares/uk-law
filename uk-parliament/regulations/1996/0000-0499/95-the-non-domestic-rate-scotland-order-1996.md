# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 95 (S.5)

RATING AND VALUATION

The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 1996

Made

15th January 1996

Laid before Parliament

26th January 1996

Coming into force

1st April 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 7B(1) and 37(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

## 2. Non-domestic rate for 1996-97

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Secretary of State prescribes a rate of 44.9 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 1996-97.

George Kynoch

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office
