# The Extradition (Designated Commonwealth Countries) Order 1991 (Amendment) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 279

EXTRADITION

The Extradition (Designated Commonwealth Countries) Order 1991 (Amendment) Order 1996

Made

14th February 1996

Laid before Parliament

26th February 1996

Coming into force

18th March 1996

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of February 1996

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 5(1) of the Extradition Act 1989[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Extradition (Designated Commonwealth Countries) Order 1991 (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 18th March 1996.

**2.** The Extradition (Designated Commonwealth Countries) Order 1991[^f00002] shall be amended by inserting the words “South Africa” under the words “Solomon Islands” in Schedule 1 to that Order which lists countries designated for the purposes of section 1(2) of the Extradition Act 1989.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates South Africa as a Commonwealth country for the time being mentioned in Schedule 3 to the British Nationality Act [1981 (c. 61)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/61) for the purposes of the Extradition Act 1989.
