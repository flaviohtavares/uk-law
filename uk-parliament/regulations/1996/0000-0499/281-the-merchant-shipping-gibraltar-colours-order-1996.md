# The Merchant Shipping (Gibraltar Colours) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 281

MERCHANT SHIPPING

The Merchant Shipping (Gibraltar Colours) Order 1996

Made

14th February 1996

Laid before Parliament

26th February 1996

Coming into force

19th March 1996

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of February 1996

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 2(3)(b) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Gibraltar colours) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 19th March 1996.

**2.** (1) The adoption for the purpose of section 2(3)(b) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995 as proper colours for ships registered in Gibraltar of the red ensign defaced with the arms of Gibraltar as specified in paragraph (2) below is hereby authorised.

(2) The positioning and proportions of the defacement shall be in accordance with the illustration in the Schedule hereto.

N. H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — The Red Ensign Flag of Gibraltar

Article 2

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/281/images/uksi_19960281_en_001)

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order authorises ships registered in Gibraltar to fly as proper colours a flag consisting of the red ensign defaced with the arms of Gibraltar.
