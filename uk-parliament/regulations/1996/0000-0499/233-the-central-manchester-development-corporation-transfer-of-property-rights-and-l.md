# The Central Manchester Development Corporation (Transfer of Property, Rights and Liabilities) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 233

URBAN DEVELOPMENT

The Central Manchester Development Corporation (Transfer of Property, Rights and Liabilities) Order 1996

Made

7th February 1996

Laid before Parliament

14th February 1996

Coming into force

26th March 1996

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 165A of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having consulted the councils of the City of Manchester and the Metropolitan Borough of Trafford, hereby makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Central Manchester Development Corporation (Transfer of Property, Rights and Liabilities) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 26th March 1996.

## 2. Transfer of property, rights and liabilities

There are hereby transferred to the Secretary of State any property, rights and liabilities vested for the time being in the Central Manchester Development Corporation which have not been transferred under—

- (a) an agreement made under section 165 of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00002]; or
- (b) article 4 of the Central Manchester Development Corporation (Planning Functions) Order 1996[^f00003].

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment

David Curry

Minister of State,

Department of the Environment

7th February 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Secretary of State any residual property, rights and liabilities of the Central Manchester Development Corporation. It is proposed that the Central Manchester Development Corporation will be dissolved on 1st July 1996.
