# The Companies Act 1985 (Miscellaneous Accounting Amendments) Regulations 1996, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Amendment of section 237

In section 237 of the 1985 Act (duties of auditors)[^f00006] after subsection (4) insert—

> (4A) If the directors of the company have taken advantage of the exemption conferred by section 248 (exemption for small and medium-sized groups from the need to prepare group accounts) and in the auditors' opinion they were not entitled so to do, the auditors shall state that fact in their report.

## Annotations

[^f00006]: Footnote: Section 237 was substituted by section 9 of the Companies Act 1989.
