# The Companies Act 1985 (Miscellaneous Accounting Amendments) Regulations 1996, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Amendment of section 246

(1) Section 246 of the 1985 Act (exemptions for small and medium-sized companies)[^f00007] is amended as follows.

(2) In subsections (1A) and (1B)—

- (a) for “immediately above the signature” substitute “above the signature”, and
- (b) for “that advantage has been taken” substitute “to the effect that advantage has been taken”.

## Annotations

[^f00007]: Footnote: Section 246 was substituted by section 13(1) of the Companies Act 1989 and amended by regulation 4 of S.I. [1992/2452](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2452).
