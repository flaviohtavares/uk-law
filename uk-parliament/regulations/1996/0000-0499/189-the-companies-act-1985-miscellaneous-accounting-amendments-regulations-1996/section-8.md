# The Companies Act 1985 (Miscellaneous Accounting Amendments) Regulations 1996, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Amendment of section 247

In section 247(6) of the 1985 Act (calculation of number of employees to determine whether company small or medium-sized)[^f00008] for “weekly” substitute “monthly”.

## Annotations

[^f00008]: Footnote: Section 247 was substituted by section 13(1) of the Companies Act 1989 and amended by regulation 5 of S.I. [1992/2452](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2452).
