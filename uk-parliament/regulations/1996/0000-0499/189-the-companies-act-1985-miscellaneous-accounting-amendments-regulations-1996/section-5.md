# The Companies Act 1985 (Miscellaneous Accounting Amendments) Regulations 1996, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Amendment of section 234

(1) Section 234 of the 1985 Act (duty to prepare directors' report)[^f00005] is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (l)(b), the words from “and the” to the end are hereby repealed.

(3) In subsection (4)—

- (a) omit the words from “Part IV” to “employees, and”, and
- (b) at the end insert—

  > - Part VI relates to the company’s policy on the payment of creditors.

## Annotations

[^f00005]: Footnote: Section 234 was substituted by section 8(1) of the Companies Act 1989.
