# The Companies Act 1985 (Miscellaneous Accounting Amendments) Regulations 1996, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Amendment of section 228

Section 228(2)(d)(ii) of the 1985 Act (statement in accounts of company claiming exemption from preparation of group accounts of whether GB parent undertaking registered in England and Wales or in Scotland)[^f00004] is hereby repealed.
