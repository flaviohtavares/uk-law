# The Companies Act 1985 (Miscellaneous Accounting Amendments) Regulations 1996, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Amendment of section 248

Subsections (3) and (4) of section 248 of the 1985 Act (auditors' report on entitlement to claim exemption from preparation of group accounts)[^f00009] are hereby repealed.

## Annotations

[^f00009]: Footnote: Section 248 was substituted by section 13(3) of the Companies Act 1989.
