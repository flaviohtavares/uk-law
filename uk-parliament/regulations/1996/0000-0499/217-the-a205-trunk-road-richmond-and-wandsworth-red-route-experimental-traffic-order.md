# The A205 Trunk Road (Richmond and Wandsworth) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1995 (Amendment No.1) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 217

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A205 Trunk Road (Richmond and Wandsworth) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1995 (Amendment No.1) Order 1996

Made

3rd February 1996

Coming into force

13th February 1996

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 9 and 10 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as the A205 Trunk Road (Richmond and Wandsworth) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1995 (Amendment No. 1) Order 1996, and shall come into force on 13th February 1996.

## 2. Amendment

The A205 Trunk Road (Richmond and Wandsworth) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1995[^f00003] shall have effect as follows:

- (a) item 14 in Schedule 2C is deleted; and
- (b) for items 24 and 25 of Schedule 4 there are substituted the following items—

**24.** Upper Richmond Road West the south side, from the eastern kerb-line of Penrhyn Crescent to a point 23 metres east of the party wall of 385/387 and 398 Upper Richmond Road West.

**25.** Upper Richmond Road West the south side, from a point 3 metres west of the party wall of 367 and 369 Upper Richmond Road West to the party wall of 327 and 329 Upper Richmond Road West.

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State for Transport.

Derek Turner

Traffic Director for London

3rd February 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends certain provisions of the A205 Trunk Road (Richmond and Wandsworth) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1995 as follows—

- (a) revokes the designation of the disabled person’s parking bay in Upper Richmond Road West, south side outside nos. 367/369 and imposes no stopping at any time prohibitions in its place;
- (b) imposes no stopping at any time prohibitions in a further length of Upper Richmond Road West, south side, between nos. 381/389 and Penrhyn Crescent.
