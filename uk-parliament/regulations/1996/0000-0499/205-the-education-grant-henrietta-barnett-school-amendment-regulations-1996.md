# The Education (Grant) (Henrietta Barnett School) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 205

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Grant) (Henrietta Barnett School) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

Made

3rd February 1996

Laid before Parliament

9th February 1996

Coming into force

1st March 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 100(1)(b) of the Education Act 1944[^f00001] and vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Grant) (Henrietta Barnett School) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 1st March 1996.

(2) Any reference in these Regulations to the principal Regulations is a reference to the Education (Grant) (Henrietta Barnett School) Regulations 1994[^f00003].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Regulations

Regulation 2(1) of the principal Regulations (payment of grant) shall be amended by the insertion of the words “or other” after the word “legal” in sub-paragraph (a) and by the substitution for the words “written notice dated 18th March 1993 terminating the Henrietta Barnett school’s right to use and occupy” of the words “the Henrietta Barnett school’s use and occupation of”.

Robin Squire

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department for Education and Employment

3rd February 1996

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Education (Grant) (Henrietta Barnett School) Regulations 1994 (“the principal Regulations”) authorise the Secretary of State to pay grant to the governing body of the Henrietta Barnett school in respect of legal advice, assistance or representation (and compensation) incurred, or to be incurred, by them in connection with a written notice dated 18th March 1993 terminating the school’s right to use and occupy all or any part of the premises of the Hampstead Garden Suburb Institute. These Regulations amend the principal Regulations by extending the power more generally to pay grant in respect of advice, assistance or representation in connection with that school’s use and occupation of those premises.
