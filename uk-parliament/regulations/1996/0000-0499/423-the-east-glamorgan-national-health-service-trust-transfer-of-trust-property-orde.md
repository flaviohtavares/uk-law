# The East Glamorgan National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 423

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The East Glamorgan National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

Made

16th February 1996

Laid before Parliament

1st March 1996

Coming into force

1st April 1996

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Mid Glamorgan Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the East Glamorgan National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the East Glamorgan National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the Mid Glamorgan Health Authority;
- “the Trust” means the East Glamorgan National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 11th December 1995, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

## 2. Transfer of Trust Property

On 1st April 1996 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

William Hague

Secretary of State for Wales

16th February 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the East Glamorgan National Health Service Trust on 1st April 1996 trust property formerly held by the Mid Glamorgan Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Welsh Office, HFM3, New Crown Building, Cathays Park, Cardiff CF1 2NQ.
