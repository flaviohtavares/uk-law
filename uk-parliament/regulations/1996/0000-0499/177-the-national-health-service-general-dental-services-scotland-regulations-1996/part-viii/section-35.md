# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Publication of particulars

Copies of the dental list, the local directory of dentists, these Regulations including the terms of service, the lists of listed drugs and the Statement of Dental Remuneration shall be available for inspection at the offices of the Health Board and such other places in its area as appear to the Health Board to be convenient for informing all persons interested and shall be kept revised and up to date.
