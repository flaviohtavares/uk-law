# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Drugs

The fees payable by a Health Board to a dentist in respect of listed drugs supplied by him under paragraph 22(1) of Schedule 1 to these regulations for use before a supply can otherwise be obtained under paragraph 23 of that Schedule shall be calculated in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Tariff.
