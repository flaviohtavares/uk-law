# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 11th March 1996.
