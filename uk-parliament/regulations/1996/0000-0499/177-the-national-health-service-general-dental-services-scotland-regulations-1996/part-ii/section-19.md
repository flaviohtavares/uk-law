# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Health Board patient information leaflets

A Health Board shall compile and make available to any person who may reasonably require one, a document about the provision of general dental services by salaried dentists in its area (a “Health Board patient information leaflet”) which shall include the information specified in Schedule 6.
