# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Information about assistants

A Health Board shall send to the Board any information it receives under paragraph 35(5) of Schedule 1 (employment of assistants) within 7 days of the date of its receipt of the information.
