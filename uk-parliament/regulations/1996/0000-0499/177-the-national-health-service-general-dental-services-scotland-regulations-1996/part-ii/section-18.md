# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Arrangements for emergency cover

It shall be the responsibility of a Health Board to make any arrangements that may be necessary to enable a salaried dentist whose name is included in its dental list to comply with his obligation under paragraph 6 of Schedule 1.
