# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 82

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 82. Income-based jobseeker’s allowance[^key-c1eff3dd38e3b0bc58378a0f627fcf1f]

(1) Regulations 83 to 85[^key-4d12b3d2cf19aa1b2a0261633eec83bf] and 87 apply in the case of an income-based jobseeker’s allowance but not a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance.

(2) Regulations 86A to 86C[^key-5ada612d5e46b7dff9b35474d486c0d6] only apply in the case of a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance.
