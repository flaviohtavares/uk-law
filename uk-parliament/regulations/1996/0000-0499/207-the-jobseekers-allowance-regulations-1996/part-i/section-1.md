# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 1

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 1. Citation, commencement, interpretation and application[^c-c99b83d30de92c326ec193fea1298801][^c-ad4018ab80bfd47b64840318f675b52a][^c-318b89989f303cbbcb974a148eee1d26]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 7th October 1996.

(2A)[^c-2f0b8bc1760b09024b64bde8957ab5ac] These Regulations do not apply to a particular case on any day on which section 33(1)(a) of the 2012 Act (abolition of income-based jobseeker’s allowance) is in force and applies in relation to that case.
