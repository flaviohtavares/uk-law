# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 119

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 119. Disregard of payments treated as not relevant income

Where the Secretary of State treats any payment as not being relevant income for the purposes of section 74A of the Administration Act [^c1774312] (payment of benefit where maintenance payments collected by Secretary of State), that payment shall be disregarded in calculating a claimant’s income.

## Annotations

[^c1774312]: Textual amendment: [1992 c.5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5); section 74A was inserted by the [Child Support Act 1995 (c.34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/34).
