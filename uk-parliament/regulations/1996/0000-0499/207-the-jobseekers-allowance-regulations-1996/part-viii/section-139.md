# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 139

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 139. Disregard of changes occurring during summer vacation

In calculating a student’s income the Secretary of State[^key-386ebc04e7bbefe63b06c95c978a6c83] shall disregard any change in the standard maintenance grant occurring in the recognised summer vacation appropriate to the student’s course, if that vacation does not form part of his period of study, from the date on which the change occurred up to the end of that vacation.
