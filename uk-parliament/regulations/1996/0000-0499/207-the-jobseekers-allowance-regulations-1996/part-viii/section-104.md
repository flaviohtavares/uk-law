# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 104

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 104. Capital treated as income

(1) Any capital payable by instalments which are outstanding on the first day in respect of which an income-based jobseeker’s allowance is payable, or, in the case of a suppression[^c-a0e46b7419d3742788aad665a307e410], the date of that suppression, shall, if the aggregate of the instalments outstanding and the amount of the claimant’s capital otherwise calculated in accordance with Chapter VI of this Part exceeds [^c-96d53b6291678085b7754cfa048ee276]... £16,000[^c-010028364f2dbea70093ccaea7f56829] be treated as income.

(2) Any payment received under an annuity shall be treated as income.

(3) In the case of a person who is, or would be, prevented from being entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance by section 14 (trade disputes), any payment under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1b3dd4e5f96680a26bc69a6467e5f492] section 17, 23B, 23C or 24A of the Children Act 1989[^c-70f4e163fc7fabe57e63cfb19254ea3a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1b3dd4e5f96680a26bc69a6467e5f492][^c1774297] or, as the case may be, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1b3dd4e5f96680a26bc69a6467e5f492] section 12 of the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968 or sections 29 or 30 of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995[^c-21e2a526fd95c499e543f7f9b7ab56fc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1b3dd4e5f96680a26bc69a6467e5f492] [^c1774298] (local authorities’ duty to promote welfare of children and powers to grant financial assistance to persons in, or formerly in, their care) shall be treated as income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1b3dd4e5f96680a26bc69a6467e5f492]

(4) Any earnings to the extent that they are not a payment of income shall be treated as income.

(5)[^c-6507b51de9f7d269c34381e4973b410e] Any Career Development Loan paid pursuant to section 2 of the Employment and Training Act 1973 shall be treated as income.

(6)[^c-27b598c7802b1286595d381e2801cfd2] Where an agreement or court order provides that payments shall be made to the claimant in consequence of any personal injury to the claimant and that such payments are to be made, wholly or partly, by way of periodical payments, any such periodical payments received by the claimant (but not a payment which is treated as capital by virtue of this Part), shall be treated as income.
