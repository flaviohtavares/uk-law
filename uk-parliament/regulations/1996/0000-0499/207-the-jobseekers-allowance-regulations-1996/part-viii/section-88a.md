# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 88A

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 88A. Income of participants in the self-employment route ...[^key-70ed6fc7e66fec6fcdcdafff857de17e][^key-5ab8e955142280fbedccdcd7dc0d6bd1]

Chapters II, III, IV, V, VII and VIII of this Part and regulations 131 to 136, 138 and 139 shall not apply to any income which is to be calculated in accordance with Chapter IVA of this Part (participants in the self-employment route[^key-c18e362f8ba1305fa98b54a16c29ab09]...).
