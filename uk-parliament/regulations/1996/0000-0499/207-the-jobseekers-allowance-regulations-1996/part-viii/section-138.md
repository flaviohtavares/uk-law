# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 138

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 138. Income treated as capital

(1)[^c-fafaef5e46c286698641d8b36e2deab4] Any amount by way of a refund of tax deducted from a student’s income shall be treated as capital.

(2)[^c-96124fc964992e704b8b37853ac2e9d4] An amount paid from access funds as a single lump sum shall be treated as capital.

(3) An amount paid from access funds as a single lump sum which is intended and used for an item other than food, ordinary clothing or footwear[^c-b116c85adb645ec2e02ed918093cc724]..., household fuel, rent for which housing benefit is payable or any housing costs[^c-02aed17a20a9385b81d0fd2adc717f6d] to the extent that they are met under regulation 83(f) or 84(1)(g) (housing costs)[^c-9620e1e959b8fa182201e04bdd37d0b6]..., of a single claimant or, as the case may be, of any other member of his family *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b65eecae1218168d1194a1fe4579f3a2] his partner, or which is used for an item other than any council tax or water charges for which that claimant or member is liable *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-16f14402aa4d160f5a3cb0a11049917a] partner is liable shall be disregarded as capital but only for a period of 52 weeks from the date of the payment.

(4)[^c-e88ef02aa376871eff4fda948c6f3409] In paragraph (3) “ordinary clothing or footwear” means clothing or footwear for normal daily use, but it does not include—

- (a) school uniforms, or
- (b) clothing or footwear used solely for sporting activities.
