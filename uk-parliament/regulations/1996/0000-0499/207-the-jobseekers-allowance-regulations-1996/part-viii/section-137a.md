# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 137A

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 137A. Further disregard of student’s income[^c-56ecad6cc79d6aee4f9acec0be0ee28f]

Where any part of a student’s income has already been taken into account for the purposes of assessing his entitlement to a grant , student loan or postgraduate[^c-46f89ca67bd9e264efc3747b092415ca] [^c-71b91afacebcd21aca0ccb64b103434e]... loan, the amount taken into account shall be disregarded in assessing that student’s income.
