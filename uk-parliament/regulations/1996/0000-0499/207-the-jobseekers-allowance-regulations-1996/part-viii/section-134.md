## 134. Relationship with amounts to be disregarded under Schedule 7

No part of a student’s covenant income or grant income shall be disregarded under paragraph 15 of Schedule 7 (charitable and voluntary payments)[^c-5a741e309a6d10ee27d9024d3caeeba3]....
