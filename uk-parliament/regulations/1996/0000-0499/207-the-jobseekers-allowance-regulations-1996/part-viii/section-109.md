# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 109

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 109. Disregard of capital of child or young person

The capital of a child or young person who is a member of the claimant’s family shall not be treated as capital of the claimant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bb94238ce54340569bb6e10b79fd9e90]
