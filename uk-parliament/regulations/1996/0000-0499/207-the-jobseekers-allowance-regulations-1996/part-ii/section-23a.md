# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 23A

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 23A. Attendance by members of a joint-claim couple[^c-5f8a634a4aed5e9df74792a9275c050a]

Each member of a joint-claim couple shall participate in an interview in such manner, time and place[^c-1ab0aa1aa60e8c3f30a5de23de319648] as the employment officer may specify by a notification which is given or sent to that member and which may be in writing, by telephone or by electronic means.
