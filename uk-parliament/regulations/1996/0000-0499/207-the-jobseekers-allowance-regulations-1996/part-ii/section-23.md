# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 23

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 23. Attendance[^c-3b13759afbe6b01320933eb7d09e74a2]

A claimant shall participate in an interview in such manner, time and place[^c-a023d7ff76529f3147b5433298167d92] as an employment officer may specify by a notification which is given or sent to the claimant and which may be in writing, by telephone or by electronic means.
