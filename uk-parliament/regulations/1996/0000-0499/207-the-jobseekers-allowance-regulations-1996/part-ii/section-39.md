# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 39

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 39. Variation of Agreement: matters to be taken into account

In giving a direction under section 10(6)(b) or (d) the Secretary of State[^c-8a251055745ff5e2c186e4a5a556d30d] shall take into account the preference of the claimant if he considers that both the claimant’s proposals and those of the employment officer satisfy the requirements of section 10(5).
