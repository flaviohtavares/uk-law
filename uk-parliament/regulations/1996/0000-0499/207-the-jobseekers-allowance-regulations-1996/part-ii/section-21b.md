# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 21B

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 21B. Further circumstances in which a person is to be treated as actively seeking employment: lone parents with children aged 5 not in full-time education[^c-4f9dcc56bbf359ea976679e46f02605b]

A lone parent who is treated for any period as being available for employment under regulation 17B shall be treated as actively seeking employment for the same period
