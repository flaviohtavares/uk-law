# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 157

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 157. Special provisions in respect of share fishermen

The Act and the foregoing provisions of these Regulations shall have effect in relation to share fishermen subject to the provisions of this Chapter.
