# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 162

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 162. Remunerative work

In determining the number of hours in which a person is engaged in remunerative work for the purposes of establishing entitlement to a contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance, no account shall be taken of any hours in which a person is engaged in work as a share fisherman.
