# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 167

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 167. Modification of contribution conditions for volunteer development workers

Section 2 (the contribution-based conditions) shall apply with the modifications that after the words “Class 1 contributions" in each place where they appear there shall be inserted the words “ or Class 2 contributions under Case G of Part 9 of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 2001[^key-96a962340a067f47816ec089cf5f2185]”.
