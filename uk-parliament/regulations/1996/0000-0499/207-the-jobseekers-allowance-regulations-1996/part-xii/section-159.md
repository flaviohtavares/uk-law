# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 159

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 159. Modifications of sections 19 and 20

For the purposes of sections 19 and 20 (circumstances in which a jobseeker’s allowance is not payable) employment as a share fisherman shall be treated as employment as an employed earner.
