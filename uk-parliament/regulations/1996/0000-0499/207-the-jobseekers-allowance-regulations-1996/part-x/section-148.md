## 148. Applicable amount in urgent cases (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46][^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

(1) For the purposes of calculating any entitlement to an income-based jobseeker’s allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] but not a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance[^key-c4e6083fb3ab211708b0e31bfd1740bf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] under this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

- (a) except in a case to which sub-paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] [^key-8a4835a7dcd4aab725d629be405944dd]... (c) or (d) applies, a claimant’s weekly applicable amount shall be the aggregate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (i)[^key-7d37f2ced94bbc18e3e7eba8ac167bd8] 90 per cent. of the amount applicable (reduced where appropriate in accordance with regulation 145 (applicable amount in hardship cases)) in respect of himself or, if he is a member of a couple or of a polygamous marriage, of the amount applicable in respect of both of them under paragraph 1(1), (2) or (3) of Schedule 1 or, as the case may be, the amount applicable in respect of them under regulation 84 (polygamous marriages); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-439798e83ba9a2c8a28aeb08a5f4cd26][^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] the amount applicable under paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 in respect of any child or young person who is a member of his family except a child or young person whose capital, if calculated in accordance with Part VIII in like manner as for the claimant, except as provided in regulation 106(1) (modifications in respect of children and young persons), would exceed £3,000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac8bf5cd63e61226ecf2c8f719a66060] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] the amount, if applicable, specified in Part *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] II or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8641fe947a5702089b6eb1a4df3bd5a2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] III *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] of Schedule 1 (premiums); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] any amounts applicable under regulation 83(f) or, as the case may be, 84(1)(g) (housing costs); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (v)[^key-11ecf9f21878fdee2c69325e95931843] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (vi (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] the amount of any protected sum which may be applicable to him in accordance with regulation 87(2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
- (b)[^key-0fd1f5b21b7f3a4af6d302c6f1614641] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^key-240d7bfc8fb7c6921536122cdfc2eab7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^key-c60ac57c6a05b3fb7f05bc848b92c1af] except where sub-paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] [^key-8a4835a7dcd4aab725d629be405944dd]...(c) applies, in the case of a person to whom any paragraph, other than *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] paragraph 13A[^key-abb662d176e520921c9c86bfe562cb2d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] in column (1) of Schedule 5 (special cases) applies, the amount shall be 90 per cent. of the amount applicable in column 2 of that Schedule in respect of the claimant and partner (if any), plus, if applicable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (i)[^key-3d6b5d02886b05a84c68bdcf34666797] any amount in respect of a child or young person who is a member of the family except a child or young person whose capital, if calculated in accordance with Part VIII in like manner as for the claimant, except as provided in regulation 106(1) (modifications in respect of children and young persons), would exceed £3,000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3698e2d4465556812afc13c2182cef6a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] any premium under Part *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] II or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-80b947c7edfef63cfb92ba74254f2aef] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] III *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] of Schedule 1; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] any amounts applicable under regulation 83(f) or, as the case may be, 84(1)(g); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] the amount of the protected sum which may be applicable to him in accordance with regulation 87(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

(1A)[^key-d337e9407e2418e180a46a416a336be3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-92eb850990776b790a29545050c17f7d] Where the calculation of a claimant’s applicable amount under this regulation results in a fraction of a penny that fraction shall be treated as a penny. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
