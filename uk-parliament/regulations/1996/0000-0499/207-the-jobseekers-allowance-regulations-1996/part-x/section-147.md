# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 147

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 147. Urgent cases (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46][^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

(1) In a case to which this regulation applies, a claimant’s weekly applicable amount and his income and capital shall be calculated for the purposes of an income-based jobseeker’s allowance in accordance with the following provisions of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

(2) This regulation applies in accordance with the following provisions to– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

- (a)[^key-3152141fd24587285676f8fd0cfc64cf] a claimant to whom paragraph (2A) applies (persons not excluded from income-based jobseeker’s allowance under section 115 of the Immigration and Asylum Act); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
- (b) a claimant to whom paragraph (6) (certain persons whose income is not readily available to them) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

(2A)[^key-b620916519334cea39eedb431f286cd6] This paragraph applies to a person not excluded from entitlement to income-based jobseeker’s allowance under section 115 of the Immigration and Asylum Act by virtue of regulation 2 of the Social Security (Immigration and Asylum) Consequential Amendments Regulations 2000 except for a person to whom paragraphs 3 and 4 of Part I to the Schedule to those Regulations applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

(3)[^key-ac73e8fa1f9a25d5330e589892139af1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-ac73e8fa1f9a25d5330e589892139af1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-ac73e8fa1f9a25d5330e589892139af1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) This paragraph shall only apply to a person who is treated as possessing income by virtue of regulation 105(6) and (7) (notional income) where the income he is treated as possessing is not readily available to him; and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

- (a) the amount of jobseeker’s allowance payable to him otherwise than under this Part is less than the amount of a jobseeker’s allowance payable to him under this Part; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
- (b) the Secretary of State[^key-971cc52be66115c164fae4f32ec82d19] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] is satisfied that, unless the provisions of this Part are applied to the claimant, the claimant or his family will suffer hardship. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
