# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 149

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 149. Assessment of income and capital in urgent cases (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46][^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

(1) The claimant’s income shall be calculated in accordance with Part VIII subject to the following modifications— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

- (a)[^c-378359be76cb09bc0b8d9f3ac7fd6ec2] any income other than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (i) a payment of income or income in kind made under the Macfarlane Trust, the Macfarlane (Special Payments) Trust, the Macfarlane (Special Payments) (No.2) Trust, the Fund, the Eileen Trust or the Independent Living *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] Fund (2006)[^c-6f7596285aed27d5e2b2d725bf167abe] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] income to which paragraph 5, 7 (but only to the extent that a concessionary payment would be due under that paragraph for any non-payment of income support *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] or of employment and support allowance under regulation 164 of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations[^c-4850ffd3a63c1b68366dd998ed3b071b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] under regulation 70 of these Regulations or of jobseeker’s allowance under regulation 147 of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996 (urgent cases)), 31, 39(2), (3) or (4), 40, 42, 52 or 57 of Schedule 9 (disregard of income other than earnings) applies, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

  possessed or treated as possessed by him, shall be taken into account in full notwithstanding any provision in that Part disregarding the whole or any part of that income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
- (b) any income to which regulation 116 (calculation of tariff income from capital) applies shall be disregarded; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
- (c) income treated as capital by virtue of regulation 110(1),(2),(3) and (9) (income treated as capital) shall be taken into account as income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
- (d) in a case to which paragraph (2)(b) of regulation 147 (urgent cases) applies, any income to which regulation 105(6) and (7) (notional income) applies shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

(2) The claimant’s capital calculated in accordance with Part VIII, but including any capital referred to in paragraphs 3 and, to the extent that such assets as are referred to in paragraph 11 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] consist[^c-b68d61e44ec9634ecb3cc6a88bdcd7b7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] of liquid assets, 11 and, except to the extent that the arrears referred to in paragraph 12 consist of arrears of housing benefit payable under Part VII of the Benefits Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] [^c1774327][^c-6de088ec42ce07dfc34bfc232aec9d0f]... or any arrears of benefit due under regulation 147 of these Regulations or regulation 70 of the Income Support Regulations (urgent cases)[^c-912783843432d0ab8ef4ee806b2bc905] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46], 12, 14(b), 24 and 32 of Schedule 8 (capital to be disregarded) shall be taken into account in full and the amount of a jobseeker’s allowance which would, but for this paragraph be payable under this regulation, shall be payable only to the extent that it exceeds the amount of that capital. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
