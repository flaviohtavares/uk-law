## 148A. Applicable amount in urgent cases: joint-claim couples (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ebe110f8effbc80ff26433ba2ec2cbb1][^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46][^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

(1) For the purpose of calculating any entitlement to a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance under this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

- (a) except in a case to which sub-paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] [^c-050c1d77e6aea51d92effd1b1f239fb3]...(c) or (d) applies, a joint-claim couple’s weekly applicable amount shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46], subject to paragraph (1A),[^c-03fd45b0e1841afdf35e371b92118241] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] be the aggregate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (i)[^c-7a2fcc1408dc590250cc4a893503ef09] 90 per cent. of the amount applicable (reduced where appropriate in accordance with regulation 146G (applicable amount in hardship cases for joint-claim couples)) in respect of the couple under paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 1 or, as the case may be, the amount applicable in respect of them under regulation 86B (joint-claim couples: polygamous marriages); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] the amount, if applicable, specified in Part IVA of Schedule 1 (premiums); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] any amounts applicable under regulation 86A(d) or, as the case may be, 86B(e) (housing costs); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a13e9d4aa058f530ded1a32eb36e3ac2][^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-c1dfe8cb3d4be3f02af4ad48621db47a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-1ba87ee3411197ebedb0e5a47ef5217d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-78e57216c96731a38f0ac975080cb7d2] except where sub-paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] [^c-050c1d77e6aea51d92effd1b1f239fb3]...(c) applies, in the case of a member of a joint-claim couple to whom any paragraph of Schedule 5A (applicable amounts of joint-claim couples in special cases) applies, the amount shall be 90 per cent. of the amount applicable in column (2) of that Schedule in respect of the joint-claim couple plus, if applicable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (i) any premium under Part IVA of Schedule 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46] any amounts applicable under regulation 86A(d) or, as the case may be, 86B(e) (housing costs). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]

(2) Where the calculation of a joint-claim couple’s applicable amount under this regulation results in a fraction of a penny that fraction shall be treated as a penny. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-828c5e2291dc1f3523c026207c354a46]
