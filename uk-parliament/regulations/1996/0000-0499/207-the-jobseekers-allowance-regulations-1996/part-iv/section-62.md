# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 62

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 62. Registration

(1) Except in the circumstances set out in paragraphs (2) and (3) a young person to whom section 3(1)(f)(ii) or (iii) or section 3A(1)(e)(i) or (ii)[^c-5097d069f6491080ec867e3188d7bb06] applies other than one falling within regulation 61(1)(a) or (f), must register with such person as the Secretary of State may specify[^c-3f0da5bd8d1ce379a20ee68c29f2f9b2] for both employment and training.

(2)[^c-2b4b8e4b8e636bf7f0e23bfa982bdf7d] A young person must register with Jobcentre Plus for both employment and training where the Secretary of State has specified a person other than Jobcentre Plus for the purposes of paragraph (1) and—

- (a) the young person is unable to register with that person because of an emergency affecting that person such as a strike or fire, or
- (b) the young person would suffer hardship because of the extra time it would take to register with that person.
