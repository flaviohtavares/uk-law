# The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996, section 59

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 59. Young persons in the child benefit extension period

(1) For the period specified in paragraph (2), this regulation applies to a young person who falls within paragraph (2) of regulation 57.

(2) The period in the case of any person falling within paragraph (1) is the child benefit extension period, except where regulation 61(1)(d) or (e) applies.
