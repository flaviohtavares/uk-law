# The West Glasgow Hospitals University National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 324 (S.24)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, SCOTLAND

The West Glasgow Hospitals University National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1996

Made

12th February 1996

Coming into force

14th February 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 12A(1) of and paragraphs 1 and 3(1)(b) of Schedule 7A to the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the West Glasgow Hospitals University National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1996 and shall come into force on 14th February 1996.

(2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the West Glasgow Hospitals University National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of Article 3 of the principal Order

For article 3 of the principal Order there shall be substituted the following:—

> **3.** (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 12A(1) of the Act.
>
> (2) The trust’s functions (which include functions which the Secretary of State considers appropriate in relation to the provision of services by the trust for one or more health boards) shall be—
>
> - (a) to own and manage under section 12A(1)(a) of the Act the hospital establishments or facilities known as the Western Infirmary, Drumchapel Hospital, Gartnavel General Hospital, Belvidere Oncology Unit, Glasgow Homeopathic Hospital and Glasgow Eye Infirmary, all in Glasgow and establishments associated with those hospitals, including management of the facilities provided at and from those establishments, and also including the management of any teaching and research facilities associated with these establishments and the support services related thereto; and
> - (b) to provide and manage under section 12A(1)(b) of the Act a new hospital and associated facilities to replace Glasgow Homeopathic Hospital.

.

James Douglas-Hamilton

Minister of State, Scottish Office

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

12th February 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order substitutes a new article 3 in the West Glasgow Hospitals University National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993 to empower the trust to provide a new hospital to replace Glasgow Homeopathic Hospital.
