# The Motor Vehicle Tyres (Safety) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 3227

CONSUMER PROTECTION

The Motor Vehicle Tyres (Safety) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

Made

20th December 1996

Laid before Parliament

23rd December 1996

Coming into force

31st December 1996

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 11 of the Consumer Protection Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation in accordance with section 11(5) of that Act with such organisations as appear to him to be representative of interests substantially affected by the following Regulations, and the Health and Safety Commission in relation to the application of the following Regulations to Great Britain, hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Motor Vehicle Tyres (Safety) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 31st December 1996.

**2.** (1) Regulation 11 of the Motor Vehicle Tyres (Safety) Regulations 1994[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In paragraphs (1) and (2) for “1st January 1997” in both places where those words occur, there shall be substituted “1st July 1997”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Bowis

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Transport

20th December 1996

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles Tyres (Safety) Regulations 1994 by extending, from 1st January 1997 to 1st July 1997, the transitional exemption date laid down in regulation 11(1) and (2) for the supply of VR and ZR designated tyres and bias-belted and diagonal-ply tyres.
