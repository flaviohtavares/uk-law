# The Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986 (Fees) (No. 1) Order 1996

> Text as at 2013-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

1996 No. 3090

FEES AND CHARGES

The Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986 (Fees) (No. 1) Order 1996

Made

9th December 1996

Coming into force

10th December 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 102(4) of the Finance (No. 2) Act 1987[^f00001], a draft of this Order having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the House of Commons, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.**[^key-80108e86e92d78c0e61a16987377e028] This Order may be cited as the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986 (Fees) (No. 1) Order 1996 and shall come into force on the day after it is made.

**2.**[^key-dcbad4b1cfcbacdf70c3c9b2b62de2dd] (1) In relation to the functions mentioned in paragraph (2) below, the costs of which fall to be taken into account on the exercise of the power to fix the fees to which paragraph (3) below applies, the following matter, namely, any deficit incurred before the exercise of the relevant power, is hereby specified for the purposes of section 102(4) of the Finance (No. 2) Act 1987 as one which may be taken into account in determining those costs.

(2) The said functions are those of the Secretary of State, the inspectors and the Committee for the Protection of Animals Used for Scientific Purposes[^key-6fc1996d63b0c2975ffb45782d5a19d4] under the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986[^f00002].

(3) This paragraph applies to fees payable under section 8 of that Act.

Tom Sackville

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

9th December 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that past deficits may be taken into account in the determination of fees fixed by the Secretary of State under section 8 of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986.
