(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations regulate adoption agencies and make provision inter alia for how adoption societies shall be approved by the Secretary of State (regulation 3), their appointment of adoption panels (regulation 7), the functions of the adoption panels (regulation 11) and the duties of adoption agencies in the adoption process.
