## 1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Citation and commencement (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0871a3674ca8b162975ce4b2d8999adf][^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512][^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]

This Order may be cited as the Homelessness (Suitability of Accommodation) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 20th January 1997. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]

## 2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Matters to be taken into account (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c086498e151ae4a41d5297468677026c][^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512][^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]

In determining whether it would be, or would have been, reasonable for a person to continue to occupy accommodation and in determining whether accommodation is suitable for a person there shall be taken into account whether or not the accommodation is affordable for that person and, in particular, the following matters— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] the financial resources available to that person, including, but not limited to,— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (i (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] salary, fees and other remuneration; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (ii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] social security benefits; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (iii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] payments due under a court order for the making of periodical payments to a spouse or a former spouse, or to, or for the benefit of, a child; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (iv (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] paymrnts of child support maintenance due under the Child Support Act 1991 *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512][^f00002]
- (v (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] contributions to the costs in respect of the accommodation which are or were made or which might reasonably be expected to be, or have been, made by other members of his household; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (vi (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] pensions *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (vii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] financial assistance towards the costs in respect of the accommodation, including loans, provided by a local authority, voluntary organisation or other body; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (viii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] benefits derived from a policy of insurance; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (ix (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] savings and other capital sums; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] the costs in respect of the accommodation, including, but not limited to,— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (i (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] payments of, or by way of, rent; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (ii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] payments in respect of a licence or permission to occupy the accommodation; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (iii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] mortgage costs; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (iv (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] payments of, or by way of, service charges; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]

  - (viii (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] payments by way of deposit or security in respect of the accommodation; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
  - (ix (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] payments required by an accommodation agency; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512] payments which that person is required to make under a court order for the making of periodical payments to a spouse or a former spouse, or to, or for the benefit of, a child and payments of child support maintenance required to be made under the Child Support Act 1991; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]

## 3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Circumstances in which accommodation is not to be regarded as suitable (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7f770f7825ffa0ddcb4ab0c9d55ebce5][^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512][^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]

For the purposes of section 197(1) of the Housing Act 1996 (duty where other suitable accommodation available), accommodation shall not be regarded as suitable unless the local housing authority are satisfied that it will be available for occupation by the applicant for at least two years beginning with the date on which he secures it. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-487b0fa7d52f9aae4b0ae71c388a2512]

This Order specifies matters for the purposes of sections 177(3)(b) and 210(2)(b) of the Housing Act 1996 (homelessness). It specifies matters to be taken into account in determining whether it would be, or would have been, reasonable for a person to continue to occupy accommodation and matters to be taken into account in determining whether accommodation is suitable for a person.
