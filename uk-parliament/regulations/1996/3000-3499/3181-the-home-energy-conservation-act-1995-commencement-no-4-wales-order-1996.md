# The Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 (Commencement No. 4) (Wales) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 3181 (C.100)

ENERGY CONSERVATION

WALES

The Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 (Commencement No. 4) (Wales) Order 1996

Made

17th December 1996

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 9(2) and (3) of the Home Energy Conservation Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 (Commencement No. 4) (Wales) Order 1996.

## 2. Commencement

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Home Energy Conservation Act 1995 shall come into force on 1st April 1997.

(2) Sections 3(1) and 4(1) and (2) of that Act shall come into force on 10th January 1997.

## 3. Application

This Order shall apply in relation only to energy conservation authorities whose areas are in Wales.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Jonathan Evans

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office

17th December 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force in Wales the following provisions of the Home Energy Conservation Act 1995—

- on 10th January 1997, sections 3(1) and 4(1) and (2), and
- on 1st April 1997, the remainder of the Act.

As regards Wales, there remain no further provisions of the Act to be brought into force.

The following provisions of the Act are brought into force by commencement order made before the date of this Order.
