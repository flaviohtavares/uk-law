# The Passenger Transport Executives (Capital Finance) (Amendment) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 3058

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Passenger Transport Executives (Capital Finance) (Amendment) Order 1996

Made

4th December 1996

Laid before Parliament

10th December 1996

Coming into force

31st December 1996

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 39(5), (6) and (7) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Passenger Transport Executives (Capital Finance) (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 31st December 1996.

**2.** Paragraph (2) of article 1 of the Passenger Transport Executives (Capital Finance) Order 1990[^f00002]shall be amended so that—

- (a) for the definition of “relevant sum” there shall be substituted the following—

  > - “relevant sum” means a sum, other than a special sum, paid by any person to an Executive in pursuance of a Deed of Assumption;

  ; and
- (b) for the words “31st July 1995” in the definition of “special sum”, there shall be substituted the words “31st March 1997”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Watts

Minister of State,

Department of Transport

4th December 1996

(This note is not part of this Order)

This Order amends the definitions of “relevant sum” and “special sum” in article 1 of the [Passenger Transport Executives (Capital Finance) Order 1990 (S. I. 1990/720)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/720).
