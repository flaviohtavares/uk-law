# The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 3172

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

WALES

The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1996

Made

19th December 1996

Laid before Parliament

7th January 1997

Coming into force

1st March 1997

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 19th day of December 1996

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(1), (4) and (5) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st March 1997.

**2.** Roy Lewis James, Esquire, is hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales for a period commencing on 1st March 1997 and expiring on 31st May 1997.

**3.** The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1994[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

N.H. Nicholls

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order reappoints Roy Lewis James to be Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales for the period from 1st March 1997 to 31st May 1997. He was appointed under the Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1994 for a period of two years and two months commencing on 31st December 1994.
