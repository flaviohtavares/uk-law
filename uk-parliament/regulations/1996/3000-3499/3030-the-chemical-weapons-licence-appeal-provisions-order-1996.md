# The Chemical Weapons (Licence Appeal Provisions) Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 3030

CHEMICAL WEAPONS

The Chemical Weapons (Licence Appeal Provisions) Order 1996

Made

2nd December 1996

Laid before Parliament

4th December 1996

Coming into force

1st January 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 20(4) of the Chemical Weapons Act 1996[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Chemical Weapons (Licence Appeal Provisions) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st January 1997.

## 2. Rules concerning the conduct of appeals

The model rules concerning the conduct of appeals set out in Chapter 1 of the Schedule to the Deregulation (Model Appeal Provisions) Order 1996[^f00002] shall apply for the purposes of section 20(4) of the Chemical Weapons Act 1996 with the modifications set out in the Schedule hereto.

Anthony Nelson,

Minister for Trade,

Department of Trade and Industry

2nd December 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adopts the model rules concerning the conduct of appeals set out in Chapter I of the Schedule to the Deregulation (Model Appeal Provisions) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/1678](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1678)) for the purposes of section 20(4) of the Chemical Weapons Act [1996 (c. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/6) (“the Act”) subject to the modifications specified in the Schedule to the Order.

By virtue of sections 19 and 20 of the Act no person may produce, use or have in his possession for a permitted purpose a Schedule 1 toxic chemical or precursor as defined in section 19(2) of the Act except under the authority of and in accordance with the terms of a licence granted by the Secretary of State. The Order sets out the rules which must be followed if a person wishes to appeal against a refusal by the Secretary of State to grant, renew or vary a licence or against a variation or revocation by him of a licence.
