# The A406 London North Circular Trunk Road Popes Lane (B4491) to Western Avenue (A40) Improvement Orders 1988 Revocation Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 3002

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The A406 London North Circular Trunk Road Popes Lane (B4491) to Western Avenue (A40) Improvement Orders 1988 Revocation Order 1996

Made

27th November 1996

Coming into force

19th December 1996

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 41 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as The A406 London North Circular Trunk Road Popes Lane (B4491) to Western Avenue (A40) Improvement Orders 1988 Revocation Order 1996.

**2.** The following Orders are hereby revoked—

- (a) The A406 London North Circular Trunk Road (Popes Lane (B4491) to Western Avenue (A40) Improvement, Trunk Road) Order 1988[^f00003], and
- (b) The A406 London North Circular Trunk Road (Popes Lane (B4491) to Western Avenue (A40) Improvement, Detrunking) Order 1988[^f00004].

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

D W WARD

Director Road Programme Directorate Southern Operations

27th November 1996
