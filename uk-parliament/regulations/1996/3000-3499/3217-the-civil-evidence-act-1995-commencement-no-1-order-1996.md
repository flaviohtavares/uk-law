# The Civil Evidence Act 1995 (Commencement No. 1)Order 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 3217 (C. 101)

EVIDENCE

The Civil Evidence Act 1995 (Commencement No. 1)Order 1996

Made

19th December 1996

Coming into force

31st January 1997

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 16(2) of the Civil Evidence Act 1995[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Civil Evidence Act 1995 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1996.

**2.** Except for sections 10 and 16(5), the Civil Evidence Act 1995 shall come into force on 31st January 1997.

Mackay of Clashfern,C.

Dated 19th December 1996

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that, except for sections 10 and 16(5), the Civil Evidence Act 1995 shall come into force on 31st January 1997.
