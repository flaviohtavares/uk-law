# The Non-Domestic Rating Contributions (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

RATING AND VALUATION

Made

6th December 1996

Laid before Parliament

10th December 1996

Coming into force

31st December 1996

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 140(4), 143(1) and (2) of, and paragraphs 4 and 6 of Schedule 8 to, the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rating Contributions (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 31st December 1996.

## 2. Amendments having effect as from 1997/98

(1) The Non-Domestic Rating Contributions (Wales) Regulations 1992[^f00002] shall have effect in relation to financial years beginning on or after 1st April 1997 with the following amendments.

(2) In paragraph 2(12) of Schedule 2, for “0.992” there shall be substituted “0.962”.

(3) In paragraph 8(1) of Schedule 2, for “1.5 per cent.” there shall be substituted “1.4 per cent.”.

(4) For Schedule 4 there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations apply to Wales.

Under Part II of Schedule 8 to the Local Government Finance Act 1988 billing authorities are required to pay amounts (called non-domestic rating contributions) to the Secretary of State. Provisional amounts are paid during the year, final calculations and payments being made after the year ends. They are calculated in accordance with the Non-Domestic Rating Contributions (Wales) Regulations 1992 (“the 1992 Regulations”).

These Regulations make various technical amendments to the 1992 Regulations for financial years beginning with 1997/98.
