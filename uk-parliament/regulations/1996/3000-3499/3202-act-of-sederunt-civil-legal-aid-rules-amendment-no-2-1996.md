# Act of Sederunt (Civil Legal Aid Rules) (Amendment No. 2) 1996

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1996 No. 3202 (S.242)

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE, SCOTLAND

Act of Sederunt (Civil Legal Aid Rules) (Amendment No. 2) 1996

Made

10th December 1996

Coming into force

1st January 1997

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 38 of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986([^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Civil Legal Aid Rules) (Amendment No. 2) 1996 and shall come into force on 1st January 1997.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of Act of Sederunt (Civil Legal Aid Rules) 1987

The Act of Sederunt (Civil Legal Aid Rules) 1987[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for the definition of “the Regulations” in rule 1(2) (interpretation) the following–

> - “the Regulations” means the Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) Regulations 1996.

[^f00003]

Rodger of Earlsferry

Lord President, I.P.D.

Edinburgh

10th December 1996

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends the Act of Sederunt (Civil Legal Aid Rules) 1987 by replacing a definition referring to the Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) Regulations 1987 with a definition referring to the Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) Regulations 1996 (which consolidated with amendments and revoked the earlier regulations together with certain other regulations).
