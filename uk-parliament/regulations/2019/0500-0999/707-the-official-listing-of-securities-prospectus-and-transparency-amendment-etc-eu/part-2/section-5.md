## 5. Applications for listing[^c-458bae72247b669c2bae134421a49123]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 75 (applications for listing), in subsection (6), for “officially listed in another EEA State” substitute “ listed in a country or territory outside the United Kingdom ”.
