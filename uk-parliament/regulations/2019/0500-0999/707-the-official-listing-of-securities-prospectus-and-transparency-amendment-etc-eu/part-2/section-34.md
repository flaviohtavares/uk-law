# The Official Listing of Securities, Prospectus and Transparency (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/707
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Meaning of “offer of transferable securities to the public” etc.[^key-99ed0b66e9e6c0013ba64081dfa1b889]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 102B (meaning of “offer of transferable securities to the public” etc.) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (5)(a), after “a regulated market” insert “ , as defined in Article 2(1)(13) of the markets in financial instruments regulation [^c23674891] ”.

(3) For subsection (6) substitute—

> (6) “**Multilateral trading facility**” has the same meaning as in the markets in financial instruments regulation (see Article 2(1)(14) of that Regulation) [^c23674901].

.
