# The Official Listing of Securities, Prospectus and Transparency (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/707
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Appointment by FCA of persons to carry out investigations[^c-6b2bda4bbdad11dcc460027f06860b74]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 97 (appointment by FCA of persons to carry out investigations), in subsection (1)—

- (a)[^c-7d280b3dd1b124cf0ffc4f683d644bf2] in paragraph (a), for sub-paragraph (ii) (including the “or” at the end) substitute—

  > - (ii) any other provision of qualifying transparency legislation, or

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (b), for sub-paragraph (ii) (including the “or” at the end) substitute—

  > - (ii) any other provision of qualifying transparency legislation, or

  ;
- (c) in paragraph (ba) [^c23674841], for the words from “by that person of” to the end substitute “ by that person of qualifying transparency legislation; ”.
