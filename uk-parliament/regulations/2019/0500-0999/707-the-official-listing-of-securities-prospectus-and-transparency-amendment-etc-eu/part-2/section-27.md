# The Official Listing of Securities, Prospectus and Transparency (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/707
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Corporate governance rules[^c-e38d45c108f48a862ef0e8ebf7075b57]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 89O (corporate governance rules) [^c23674791] is amended as follows.

(2) For subsection (1) substitute—

> (1) The FCA may make rules (“**corporate governance rules**”) relating to the corporate governance of issuers who have requested or approved admission of their securities to trading on a regulated market.

.

(3) Omit subsections (3) and (4).
