## 22. Notification of voting rights held by issuer[^c-9ba333c145819a755ae391081fe30a25]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 89D (notification of voting rights held by issuer) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1), for the words from “the issuer” to the end substitute “ the issuer in accordance with the following provisions ”.

(3) In subsection (2), omit “under subsection (1)(b)”.
