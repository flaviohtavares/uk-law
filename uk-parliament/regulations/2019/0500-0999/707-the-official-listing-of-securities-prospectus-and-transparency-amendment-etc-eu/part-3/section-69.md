# The Official Listing of Securities, Prospectus and Transparency (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/707
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. European Parliament and Council Regulation (EU) 2017/1129[^key-5350878972b9730feec530f98786f937]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Regulation (EU) 2017/1129 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2017 on the prospectus to be published when securities are offered to the public or admitted to trading on a regulated market, and repealing Directive [2003/71/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2003/71) , omit Article 3(2).[^key-6679bcaaf7f1e72dcadf652965c20c71]
