## 2. Interpretation[^c-e2ca0f7ba84df9fb2c19945dc623705e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “**FSMA 2000**” means the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 [^c23674681];
- “**the FCA**” means the Financial Conduct Authority.
