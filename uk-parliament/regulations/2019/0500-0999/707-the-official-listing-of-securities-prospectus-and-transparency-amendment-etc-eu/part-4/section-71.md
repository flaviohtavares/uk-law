## 71. Transfer of directive functions to the Treasury[^key-c2045fb4554067d4bf80045cb17c8bf3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Treasury may make regulations for the purposes specified in Part 2 of Schedule 2.

(2) The power to make regulations conferred on the Treasury by this regulation is exercisable by statutory instrument.

(3) The regulations may—

- (a) contain incidental, supplemental, consequential and transitional provision, and
- (b) make different provision for different purposes.

(4) A statutory instrument containing regulations under this regulation is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
