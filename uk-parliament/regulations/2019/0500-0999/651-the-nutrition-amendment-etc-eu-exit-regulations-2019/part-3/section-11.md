# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 11

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Amendment of the Kava-kava in Food (England) Regulations 2002[^c-0283c0d7b4dd755d4250d1f132e070c8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Kava-kava in Food (England) Regulations 2002 [^c23668621] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation) [^c23668631]—

- (a) omit the definitions of “EEA Agreement”, “EEA State” and “free circulation in member States”;
- (b) after the definition of port health authority, insert—

  > - “**third country**” means a country other than the United Kingdom.

  .

(3) In regulation 3 (prohibition on sale etc of food consisting of or containing Kava-kava) [^c23668641] for paragraph (2), substitute—

> (2) The prohibition imposed by paragraph (1) shall not apply where the food consisting of or containing Kava-kava is imported from a third country if the food is being, or is to be, exported to a third country.

.
