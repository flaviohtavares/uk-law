# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 15

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Amendment of the Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Addition of Substances for Specific Nutritional Purposes) (England) Regulations 2009[^key-b8f9adfea00526c0b8da78283fcb6103]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Addition of Substances for Specific Nutritional Purposes) (England) Regulations 2009 [^c23668741] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1 (specified provisions) [^c23668751], in the “subject matter” column of the table—

- (a) in the entry relating to Article 2(1), for “covered by Directive 2009/39 of the European Parliament and the Council on foodstuffs intended for particular nutritional uses” substitute “(foodstuffs which, owing to their special composition or manufacturing process, are clearly distinguishable from foodstuffs for normal consumption, which are suitable for their claimed nutritional purposes and which are marketed in such a way as to indicate such suitability);
- (b) in the entry relating to Article 3(2), for “competent authority referred to in Article 11 of Directive 2009/39” substitute “ Secretary of State, Welsh Ministers or Food Standards Scotland[^key-6478226de675360a1ba5f55906a8dae3] ”;
- (c) in the entries relating to Article 4(2) and (3), for “EU legislation” substitute “ retained EU law ”.
