# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 10

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Amendment of the Medical Food (England) Regulations 2000[^c-c74465ac3fa60d868e90838a24500340]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Medical Food (England) Regulations 2000 [^c23668561] are amended as follows.

(2) Regulation 2 (interpretation) [^c23668571] is renumbered as paragraph (1) of that regulation.

(3) After paragraph (1) as so renumbered, insert—

> (2) In these Regulations, any reference to compliance with a provision of the Directive is to be read as a reference to complying with that provision as would be required if the provision formed part of domestic law.
>
> (3) For the purposes of these Regulations, Articles 4 to 5 of, and the Annex to, the Directive are to be read subject to the modifications set out in Schedule 1.

.

(4) In regulation 5A [^c23668581] (application of the improvement notice provisions of the Act), for “Schedule”, in each place where it appears, substitute “ Schedule 2 ”.

(5) The Schedule is renumbered as Schedule 2.

(6) Before Schedule 2 as so renumbered, insert—

> **SCHEDULE 1 — Modifications to the Directive**
>
> Regulation 2(3)
>
> **1.** The Directive is modified as follows.
>
> **2.** Article 4(2) is to be read as if for “Article 3 of Directive [79/112/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1979/112),” there were substituted “ Article 9 of Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 ”.
>
> **3.** Article 5 is to be read as if—
>
> - (a) in paragraph 1—
>   - (i) for “where a product is manufactured in a third country” there were substituted “ where a product is manufactured outside of the United Kingdom ”;
>   - (ii) for the first reference to “Member States” there were substituted “ territories within the United Kingdom ”;
>   - (iii) the words “Member States may, if they can demonstrate that notification is not necessary in order to monitor those products efficiently in their territory, not impose that obligation” were omitted.
> - (b) in paragraph 2, for “are those referred to in Article 9(4) of Directive [89/398/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1989/398)” there were substituted–
>
>   > are–
>   >
>   > - (a) in respect of England, the Secretary of State,
>   > - (b) in respect of Scotland, Food Standards Scotland [^c23668591],
>   > - (c) in respect of Wales, the Welsh Ministers,
>   > - (d) in respect of Northern Ireland, the Food Standards Agency [^c23668601].
>
>   .
>
> **4.** In the Annex, paragraph 4 is to be read as if for “Directive [91/321/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1991/321) and its subsequent modifications” there were substituted “ Directive [2006/141/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2006/141) [^c23668611] ”.

.
