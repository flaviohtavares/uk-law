# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 14

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Amendment of the Infant Formula and Follow-on Formula (England) Regulations 2007[^c-d27678c806e8cd16be97f5c135a23ee1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Infant Formula and Follow-on Formula (England) Regulations 2007 [^c23668711] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation) [^c23668721], after paragraph (6) insert—

> (7) In these Regulations, any reference to compliance with a provision of the Directive is to be read as a reference to complying with that provision as would be required if the provision formed part of domestic law.

.

(3) In regulation 12 (listed substances and their purity criteria (infant formula and follow-on formula)) [^c23668731], in paragraph (3)(a) for “EU legislation” substitute “ retained EU law ”.
