# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 79

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1389[^c-f79c6ce2154f3d80477327c46a1ce62f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1389 of 17 August 2016 authorising a health claim made on foods and referring to children's development and health is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) in paragraph 1, for “Union” substitute “ Great Britain[^c-c3d6bed9439978d7b799ac10c001d9f9] ”;
- (b) omit paragraph 2.

(3) Omit Article 2.
