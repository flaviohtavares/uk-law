# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 34

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) No 1161/2010[^c-02a15ba933a4de9149ec9f89d7f90688]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) No 1161/2010 of 9 December 2010 refusing to authorise a health claim made on foods, other than those referring to the reduction of disease risk and to children's development and health is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) omit “Union”;
- (b) for “as provided for in Article 13(3) of Regulation [(EC) No 1924/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1924)” substitute “ in the Annex to Regulation (EU) 432/2012 ”;
- (c) omit the second sentence.

(3) Omit Article 2.
