# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 82

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1412[^key-b6db66f1906babb64cf4c7767746c2a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1412 of 24 August 2016 refusing to authorise a health claim made on foods and referring to the reduction of disease risk is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1, for the words from “shall not” to the end of that Article, substitute “ may not be made on foods on the Great Britain[^key-20507acc6f7512b2579d4911b0749892] market ”.

(3) Omit Article 2.
