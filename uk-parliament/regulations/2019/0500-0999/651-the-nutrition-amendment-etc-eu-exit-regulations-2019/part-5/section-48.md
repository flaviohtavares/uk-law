# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 48

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) No 1048/2012[^key-bb7c876b7379ee361408c56890b88173]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) No 1048/2012 of 8 November 2012 on the authorisation of a health claim made on foods and referring to the reduction of disease risk is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) in paragraph 1, for “European Union” substitute “ Great Britain[^key-fe07b6d83f4437dc3ba9efb05db12522] ”;
- (b) omit paragraph 2.

(3) Omit Article 2.
