# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 74

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/372[^c-e9daaa44679a602b52a8a65641d9d51e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/372 of 15 March 2016 refusing to authorise a health claim made on foods and referring to the reduction of disease risk is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1, for the words from “shall not” to the end of that Article, substitute “ may not be made on foods on the Great Britain[^c-2d4b74aa764bf912ed2392607f301f1e] market ”.

(3) Omit Article 2.
