# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 35

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) No 1162/2010[^key-5e2db72872be9d965e06d262706aed2a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) No 1162/2010 of 9 December 2010 refusing to authorise certain health claims made on foods and referring to the reduction of disease risk and to children's development and health is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1–

- (a) in the first sentence, for the words from “shall not” to the end of that sentence, substitute “ may not be made on foods on the Great Britain[^key-ae81e93b5a772d34cffa5efc5fa1d8c9] market ”;
- (b) omit the second sentence.

(3) Omit Article 2.
