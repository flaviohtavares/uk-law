# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 38

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) No 665/2011[^key-0b63cd545b3e03442343c62e5ea67f08]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) No 665/2011 of 11 July 2011 on the authorisation and refusal of authorisation of certain health claims made on foods and referring to the reduction of disease risk is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) in paragraph 1, for “European Union” substitute “ Great Britain[^key-bf3c264399cbbc2ee499aa950adc0f36] ”;
- (b) omit paragraph 2.

(3) In Article 2, for the words from “shall not” to the end of that Article, substitute “ may not be made on foods on the Great Britain market ”

(4) Omit Article 3.
