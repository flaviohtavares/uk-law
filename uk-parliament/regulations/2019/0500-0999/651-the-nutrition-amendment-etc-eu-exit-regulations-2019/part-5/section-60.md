# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 60

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) No 1228/2014[^key-31a5f819eaca1db4a4e38b1ad840eaf2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) No 1228/2014 of 17 November 2014 authorising and refusing to authorise certain health claims made on foods and referring to the reduction of disease risk is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) in paragraph 1, for “Union” substitute “ Great Britain[^key-598d27a981d3fe4b1c99f2b709124d71] ”;
- (b) omit paragraph 2.

(3) In Article 2, for the words from “shall not” to the end of that Article, substitute “ may not be made on foods on the Great Britain market ”.

(4) Omit Article 3.
