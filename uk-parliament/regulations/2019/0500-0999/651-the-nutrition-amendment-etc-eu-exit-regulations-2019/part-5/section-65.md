# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 65

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) 2015/402[^key-4981cd9b6b5ac68be598a7c053c3ebfb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) 2015/402 of 11 March 2015 refusing to authorise certain health claims made on foods, other than those referring to the reduction of disease risk and to children's development and health is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) omit “Union”;
- (b) for “as provided for in Article 13(3) of Regulation [(EC) No 1924/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1924)” substitute “ in the Annex to Commission Regulation (EU) 432/2012 ”;

(3) Omit Article 2.
