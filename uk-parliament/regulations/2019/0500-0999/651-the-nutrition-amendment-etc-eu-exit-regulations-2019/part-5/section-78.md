# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 78

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1381[^key-61d4e8ceeaac641d8984c88c3cb76510]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1381 of 16 August 2016 refusing to authorise a health claim made on foods and referring to children's development and health is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1, for the words from “shall not” to the end of that Article, substitute “ may not be made on foods on the Great Britain[^key-c81ab5007ef6236e3dbe8be9ab9375e4] market ”.

(3) Omit Article 2.
