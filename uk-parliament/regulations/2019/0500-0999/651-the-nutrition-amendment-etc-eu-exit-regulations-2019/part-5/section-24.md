# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 24

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Amendment of Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1024/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1024)[^c-c58a11b9c555a3f19dbd9cf1cce1e842]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1024/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1024) of 29 October 2009 on the authorisation and refusal of authorisation of certain health claims made on food and referring to the reduction of disease risk and to children's development and health is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) for “Community” substitute “ Great Britain[^c-7429da595bb6b23358751560e8255daf] ”;
- (b) omit the second sentence.

(3) For Article 2, substitute—

> **2.**
>
> The health claims set out in Annex II to this Regulation may not be made on foods on the Great Britain market.

.

(4) Omit Article 3.
