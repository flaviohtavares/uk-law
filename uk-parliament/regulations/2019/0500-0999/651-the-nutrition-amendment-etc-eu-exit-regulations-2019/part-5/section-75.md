# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 75

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Amendment of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/854[^key-c5f12c0f14385275f3c994bdb8e19d4f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/854 of 30 May 2016 authorising certain health claims made on foods, other than those referring to the reduction of disease risk and to children's development and health and amending Regulation (EU) No 432/2012 is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) omit “Union”;
- (b) for “as provided for in Article 13(3) of Regulation [(EC) No 1924/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1924)” substitute “ in the Annex to Regulation (EU) 432/2012 ”;

(3) Omit Article 3.
