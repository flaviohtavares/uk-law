> Text as at 2026-08-12.

## 47. Amendment of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 489/2012[^key-3fb83cca72d2a5f0134c569443d13d66]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 489/2012 of 8 June 2012 establishing implementing rules for the application of Article 16 of Regulation [(EC) No 1925/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1925) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the addition of vitamins and minerals and of certain other substances to foods is revoked.
