# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 57

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) No 1135/2014[^key-f5e033a679f23a169efead2afd96e9a2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) No 1135/2014 of 24 October 2014 on the authorisation of a health claim made on foods and referring to the reduction of disease risk is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) in paragraph 1, for “Union” substitute “ Great Britain[^key-7ab7bc63e52d4a7617b15dfb70f2ee22] ”;
- (b) omit paragraph 2.

(3) Omit Article 2.
