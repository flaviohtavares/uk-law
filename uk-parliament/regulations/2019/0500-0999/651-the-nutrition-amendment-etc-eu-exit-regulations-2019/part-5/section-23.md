# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 23

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Amendment of Commission Regulation [(EC) No 984/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/984)[^key-d14cffae7c91d9c69be532d6078e5d7e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 984/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/984) of 21 October 2009 refusing to authorise certain health claims made on food, other than those referring to the reduction of disease risk and to children's development and health is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1, for “Community” substitute “ Great Britain[^key-badfe763557539f1807d1729cf246479] ”.

(3) Omit Articles 2 and 3.
