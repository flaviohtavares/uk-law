# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 54

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) No 40/2014[^c-f9c1d8ee50fe601ab773dbf9fde40eea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) No 40/2014 of 17 January 2014 authorising a health claim made on foods, other than those referring to the reduction of disease risk and to children's development and health and amending Regulation (EU) No 432/2012 is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) omit “Union”;
- (b) for “as provided for in Article 13(3) of Regulation [(EC) No 1924/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1924)” substitute “ in the Annex to Regulation (EU) 432/2012 ”.

(3) Omit Article 3.
