# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 32

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) No 957/2010[^key-2a669301e97c7a402179780021204478]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) No 957/2010 of 22 October 2010 on the authorisation and refusal of authorisation of certain health claims made on food and referring to the reduction of disease risk and to children's development and health is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) in the first sentence, for “European Union” substitute “ Great Britain[^key-6d4864be2cf285294f7d47f161d07192] ”;
- (b) omit the second sentence.

(3) In Article 2—

- (a) in the first sentence, for the words from “shall not” to the end of that sentence, substitute “ may not be made on foods on the Great Britain market ”;
- (b) omit the second sentence.

(4) Omit Article 3.
