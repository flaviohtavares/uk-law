# The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 59

> Text as at 2026-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Amendment of Commission Regulation (EU) No 1226/2014[^c-2d2649fc9df383d12934936f4db7b82e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) No 1226/2014 of 17 November 2014 on the authorisation of a health claim made on foods and referring to the reduction of disease risk is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) in paragraph 1, for “Union” substitute “ Great Britain[^c-fd7f6940dd142ed90b2548381169ac71] ”;
- (b) omit paragraph 2.

(3) Omit Article 2.
