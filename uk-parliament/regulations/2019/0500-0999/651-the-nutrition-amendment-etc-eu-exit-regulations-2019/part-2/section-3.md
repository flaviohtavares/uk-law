## 3. Purity criteria for substances listed in Schedule 2[^key-0524ed15d3e7dd71516d28722808d685]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations may be made to set purity criteria for any vitamin and mineral substance listed in Schedule 2.
