# The Syria (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^key-7b0bf00736f8eb34c6425e150e888f4f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons by name in accordance with regulation 5A (conditions for the designation of persons by name)[^key-ec909c72f419fa4d8b630b80ebb61ddc] for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 11 to 15 (asset-freeze etc);
- (aa)[^key-813591827649d10c1fb28d6d6fca0d80] regulation 23A (director disqualification sanctions);
- (b) regulation 24 (immigration).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purposes of different provisions mentioned in[^key-6e13dddce78fd0037ceb781e0f829bd3] paragraph (1).
