# The Syria (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 24

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Immigration[^key-d1ae955867a147598b62f6cd13f1e892]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of this regulation is an excluded person for the purposes of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971 [^c23698051].
