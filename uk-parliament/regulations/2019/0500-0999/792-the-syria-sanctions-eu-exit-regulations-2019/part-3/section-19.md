# The Syria (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 19

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Sale or purchase of bonds[^c-5a703f9d4ff54bb4f87f9605e738a294]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person (“P”) must not directly or indirectly—

- (a) sell to a person falling within paragraph (4), or
- (b) purchase from such a person,

Assad regime bonds[^c-13e43451486d0c1373917db55d5e598b] or bonds guaranteed by the Assad regime[^c-eaaa7ffc943cabe69edd52f9ef1e6cae] and issued between 19th January 2012 and 8th December 2024[^c-ec3a27531c86cf3035b7bf22c1a83bfb] if P knows, or has reasonable cause to suspect, that the sale or purchase of such bonds is directly or indirectly to or from such a person.

(2) A person (“P”) must not provide brokering services to a person falling within paragraph (4) in respect of Assad regime bonds[^c-275d4971f19cbe7ac3e77536adbbb80c] or bonds guaranteed by the Assad regime[^c-ce83eb3df8631d5d923f139e7e780233] and issued between 19th January 2012 and 8th December 2024[^c-43c17eea24887a65a7473c9ef88f9ce7], if P knows, or has reasonable cause to suspect, that the person falls within paragraph (4).

(3) A person (“P”) must not provide—

- (a) brokering services,
- (b) advertising services, or
- (c) any other service,

if P knows, or has reasonable cause to suspect, that the provision of the service assists a person falling within paragraph (4) to sell or purchase Assad regime bonds[^c-45a696be42a73f6fd42c09fdc85c88ff] or bonds guaranteed by the Assad regime[^c-b8ca70901d61762ebaf6e4304dace2d4].

(4) The following persons fall within this paragraph—

- (a)[^c-4642eb3434e18cc3ff9c00bf20468dc0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-dfc01b22b6410035535144d195133d1d] a Syrian credit or financial institution
- (c) a person acting on behalf of or at the direction of a person falling within sub-paragraph [^c-31e40dc3faa02d674ebc628f1fe0e189]... (b);
- (d) a person, other than an individual, which is owned or controlled directly or indirectly (within the meaning of regulation 7) by a person falling within sub-paragraph [^c-d74c8ecd38941b371c6a90db4efd30af]... (b) or (c).

(5) Paragraphs (1) to (3) are subject to regulation 60 (exception for acts done for purposes of national security or prevention of serious crime).

(6) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) to (3) commits an offence.

(7) For the purposes of this regulation, “Assad regime bonds”[^c-317770814e48b628f38a207d89622067] means bonds issued by the Assad regime[^c-9c84d142fdf146f61b2974d85da90b67].

(8)[^c-310f17614df18364a2debb0076d1ce4c] For the purposes of this regulation, “**Syrian credit or financial institution**” means—

- (a) a credit or financial institution domiciled in Syria; or
- (b) a branch or subsidiary, wherever located, of a credit or financial institution domiciled in Syria.
