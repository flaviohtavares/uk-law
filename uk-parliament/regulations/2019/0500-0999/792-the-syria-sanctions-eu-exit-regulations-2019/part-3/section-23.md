# The Syria (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 23

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Interpretation of Part 3[^key-a47344ea1fc8917fbee4e6f2778ee622]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- “**branch**” means—
  - (a) [^key-aa1f7b6d3234a4be8a8187b9778cf503]...
  - (b) in relation to a credit or financial institution domiciled in Syria, a place of business which forms a legally dependent part of that institution and which carries out all or some of the transactions inherent in the business of that institution;
- “**credit or financial institution domiciled in Syria**” means a person, other than an individual, who—
  - (a) in the case of an undertaking domiciled in Syria, by way of business—
    - (i) operates a currency exchange office,
    - (ii) transmits money (or any representation of monetary value) by any means, or
    - (iii) cashes cheques that are made payable to customers, and
  - (b) in the case of any other person domiciled in Syria, would satisfy the threshold conditions for permission under Part 4A of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 [^c23698001] if it had its registered office (or if it does not have one, its head office) in the United Kingdom;
- “**subsidiary**” has the meaning given by section 1159 of the Companies Act 2006 [^c23698011];
- [^key-f0b5716a071464bba23f32b4c70ba7da]...

(2) In paragraph (1), the definitions of—

- (a) “credit or financial institution domiciled in Syria”, [^key-8c399585f94119c38d2c15205e3733ed]...
- (b)[^key-8c399585f94119c38d2c15205e3733ed] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

is to be read[^key-ead3ac1d27db2f8832f3a63b85a9bef4] with section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 [^c23698021], any relevant order under that section [^c23698031] and Schedule 2 to that Act [^c23698041].
