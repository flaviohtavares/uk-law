# The Syria (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 79

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Penalties for offences[^key-da8756f99f30047cac6a550f34b95f75]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who commits an offence under any provision of Part 3 (Finance) [^key-bccc4bda387063088a1c23ee1a2a76a6]... or regulation 65 (finance: licensing offences), is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-5981108ef275fed3fbe813ea3c8cff88] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 7 years or a fine (or both).

(2) A person who commits an offence under any provision of Part 5 (Trade) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-7269b7ea104382c7eea72934b8e37e60] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 10 years or a fine (or both).

(3) A person who commits an offence under regulation 9(6) (confidentiality), [^key-367e90db9c9dff33dc24844ddb98a710]... 65A (director disqualification: licensing offences),[^key-681a23ba751d78cb78fa0e4cb84adec9] 66 (trade: licensing offences), [^key-a0d25fd71de929f72927bdc49b0d7ad1]... 75(6) or 76(5) (information offences in connection with trade licences) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-01f6286db5ad081de80af5975e30653b] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or a fine (or both).

(4) A person who commits an offence under regulation [^key-2b74eb41646debf35db205c57191ddef]... 69(6) (finance: reporting obligations) or 73 (information offences in connection with Part 3) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (or both).

(4A)[^key-5744726305d26b45d96dd1c0ebefba86] A person who commits an offence under regulation 57(6) (exceptions relating to petroleum products: notification requirement) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to a fine;
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale;
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.

(5) In relation to an offence committed before 2nd May 2022[^key-4e342c9000662cdee8bd0cedad6cfdae] the reference in each of paragraphs (1)(a), (2)(a) and (3)(a) to the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-cd1d49f01ed8c3f99a8d4a5b434f18c3] is to be read as a reference to 6 months.
