# The Syria (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 48

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Provision of interception and monitoring services[^c-7623170d0cf15c475804da0e7774586f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly provide interception and monitoring services to, or for the benefit of, the Government of Syria[^c-c44ea2b91c1d9b440ec7397ac55b7bbf].

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes the prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with that offence (“P”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the services were provided to, or for the benefit of, the Government of Syria[^c-c50ee5832fa695e4df53fb6165c6dd0b].
