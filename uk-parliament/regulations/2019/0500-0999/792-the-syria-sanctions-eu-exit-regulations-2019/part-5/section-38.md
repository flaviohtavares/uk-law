# The Syria (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 38

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Transport of military goods ...[^key-c2bc2118a52dcc1cdb981cb1d7b4b6e8][^key-63505f60a99a36fa310e0af4303de693]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly supply or deliver goods to which this paragraph applies from a place in Syria to a third country where those goods—

- (a) originate in Syria, or
- (b) are consigned from Syria.

(2)[^key-8112a8dafd637beb274c555d2b818002] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Paragraph (1) applies to—

- (a)[^key-801cc2cc6b344ebfbc4c7c73956c1657] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) military goods.

(4) Paragraph (1) is[^key-4d532a4591365004abf1aac438d178bb] subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).

(5) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) [^key-b0b25b5a4dd18e419b3ddc802f942c5c]... commits an offence, but—

- (a) it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening paragraph (1)(a) [^key-b0b25b5a4dd18e419b3ddc802f942c5c]... to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the goods originated in Syria;
- (a) it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening paragraph (1)(b) to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the goods were consigned from Syria.

(6) In this regulation—

- [^key-e838f58d645bd9572e28dba4d105c15d]...
- “third country” means a country that is not the United Kingdom, the Isle of Man or Syria
