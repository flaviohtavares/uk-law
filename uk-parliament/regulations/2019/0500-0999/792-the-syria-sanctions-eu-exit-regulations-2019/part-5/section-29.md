# The Syria (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 29

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Export prohibition[^key-fffb2be6089a23255d8834482d787e24]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of goods to which this paragraph applies to, or for use in, Syria is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).
