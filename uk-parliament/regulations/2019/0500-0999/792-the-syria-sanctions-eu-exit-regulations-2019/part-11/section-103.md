# The Syria (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 103

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103. Transitional Provisions: prior obligations[^c-50f4c63e0ce520d8a6a4173be4609378]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where—

- (a) a person was named in Annex ⅠⅠ or ⅠⅠa of the EU Syria Regulation immediately before IP completion day[^c-b38614cc44eae27be546e754c96532bf], and
- (b) the person is a designated person immediately before IP completion day,

any reference in a provision mentioned in paragraph (2) to the date on which a person became a designated person is a reference to the original listing date.

(2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) regulation 55(5) (asset-freeze etc: exceptions from prohibitions), and
- (b) in Schedule 6 (Treasury licences: purposes)—
  - (i)[^c-a3db8ade209a55d24206cb18b414daac] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii) paragraph 15(a);
  - (iii) paragraph 16(3);
  - (iv) paragraph 16(5)(b).

(3) In this regulation—

- “**designated person**” has the same meaning as it has in Chapter 1 of Part 3 (Finance);
- “**original listing date**” means the earlier of—
  - (a) the date on which the person was named in Annex ⅠⅠ or Annex ⅠⅠa of the EU Syria Regulation, and
  - (b) if the person was also named in Annex ⅠⅠ of Council Regulation (EU) No 442/2011 of 9 May 2011, concerning restrictive measures in view of the situation in Syria [^c23698541], the date on which the person was named in that Annex.
