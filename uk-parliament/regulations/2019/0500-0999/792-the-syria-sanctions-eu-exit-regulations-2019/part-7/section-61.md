> Text as at 2026-05-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Treasury licences[^c-2941e90dccc2ec76f9c1e1507df47f31]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibitions in regulations 11 to 15 (asset-freeze etc) [^c-9dde702fcf78aa1bec943df6332035ae]... do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Treasury under this paragraph.

(2) The Treasury may issue a licence which authorises acts by a particular person only where—

- (a) in relation to acts which would otherwise be prohibited by regulations 11 to 15, the Treasury consider that it is appropriate to issue the licence for a purpose set out in Part 2 of Schedule 6, [^c-ff836504ab1f0576e064e495ce5ffb41]...
