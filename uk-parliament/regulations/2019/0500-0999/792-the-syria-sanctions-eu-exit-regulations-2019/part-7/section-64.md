# The Syria (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 64

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Licences: general provisions[^key-4b5d52e50a50e326db2e9fd610c1ac2c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to [^key-ac6e50ef64e03ec8efaf1804bf54e25c]... Treasury licences , director disqualification licences[^key-acd834239a87d17468630bbaec62b6d1] and trade licences.

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may—

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^key-851011a506cf4743828ac2012469a223]**P**”), issues[^key-ad1f8756a521c4fb59cd0455d48dce2e] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^key-c236772721f72f6bb8113f16a4bc6f50] must give written notice to P[^key-4ec154d886f441920d8a5595cc2fb327] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
