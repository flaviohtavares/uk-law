## 23. Council Regulation [(EEC) No 1536/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/1536) laying down common marketing standards for preserved tuna and bonito[^key-101326dc4dd278359a0fd600a324dcfd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Regulation [(EEC) No 1536/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/1536) laying down common marketing standards for preserved tuna and bonito is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1 for “Community” substitute “ United Kingdom ”.

(3) In Article 5(3) omit “in the Member State in which the products are marketed”.

(4) In Article 6 omit “Community”.

(5) Omit Article 9.
