# The Common Fisheries Policy (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 21

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Commission Regulation [(EEC) No 3703/85](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1985/3703) laying down detailed rules for applying the common marketing standards for certain fresh or chilled fish[^c-8b2229431bd9a9d698d0bdf0d70e96a2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation [(EEC) No 3703/85](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1985/3703) laying down detailed rules for applying the common marketing standards for certain fresh or chilled fish is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1 for “Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”.

(3) In Article 2 for “7(1) and 8(3) of Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ 5(1) and 8(2) of Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”.

(4) In Article 3—

- (a) for “7(1) and 8(3) of Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ 5(1) and 8(2) of Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”; and
- (b) for “the Member State” substitute “ a fisheries administration ”.

(5) In Article 4—

- (a) for “Member States shall” substitute “ A fisheries administration must ”; and
- (b) for “Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”.

(6) In Article 5—

- (a) for “8(4) of Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ 8(3) of Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”;
- (b) for “national provisions or commercial rules applied in Member States” substitute “ requirements in any other enactment ”;
- (c) omit “, as provided for in Article 8(5) of Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103),”; and
- (d) for “national provisions on matter of commercial law” substitute “ requirements in any other enactment ”.

(7) In Article 6—

- (a) for “7 and 8 of Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ 5 and 8 of Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”; and
- (b) for “the inspection provided for in Article 4 of Regulation [(EEC) No 3796/81](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1981/3796)” substitute “ inspection ”.

(8) In Article 7—

- (a) in paragraph 1 for “Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”; and
- (b) in paragraph 2 omit “in the Member States”.

(9) In Article 8—

- (a) in paragraph 1 for “Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”
- (b) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) in point (a) for “Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”,
  - (ii) in point (b) for “6, 7 and 8 of Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ 4, 5 and 8 of Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”, and
  - (iii) in point (c) for “11 of Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ 12 of Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”; and
- (c) in paragraph 3 omit “as laid down in Article 6(6) of Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)”.

(10) In Article 9—

- (a) for “Member States shall” substitute “ A fisheries administration must ”; and
- (b) for “Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”.

(11) In Article 10, in the second indent of paragraph 3 for “the office responsible for weights and measures in the Member State concerned” substitute “ a local weights and measures authority ”.

(12) In Article 11—

- (a) for “Member States shall” substitute “ a fisheries administration must ”;
- (b) in the first indent—
  - (i) before “vessels” insert “ United Kingdom fishing ”, and
  - (ii) for “Regulation [(EEC) No 103/76](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1976/0103)” substitute “ Council Regulation [(EC) No 2406/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2406) ”; and
- (c) in the second indent, for “the vessels” substitute “ in relation to United Kingdom fishing vessels ”.

(13) Omit Article 12.

(14) In Annex 1, in the table note for “Article 2 of Regulation [(EEC) No 3796/81](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1981/3796)” substitute “ Article 33 of Regulation (EU) No 1379/2013 ”.
