# The Common Fisheries Policy (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 34

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Commission Decision [95/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0084) concerning the implementation of the Annex to Council Regulation [(EEC) No 2930/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/2930) defining the characteristics of fishing vessels[^key-bf973226e879342e70cd42df27a6f1d4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Decision [95/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0084) concerning the implementation of the Annex to Council Regulation [(EEC) No 2930/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/2930) defining the characteristics of fishing vessels is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) for “a<sub>1</sub>, a<sub>2</sub> and a<sub>3</sub> set out in Annex I to Regulation [(EEC) No 2930/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/2930)” substitute “ a<sub>1</sub> and a<sub>2</sub> set out in Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2017/1130 of the European Parliament and of the Council defining characteristics for fishing vessels ”; and
- (b) omit “the tables in”.

(3) Omit Articles 2 to 5.

(4) In Annex 1—

- (a) in the heading for “a<sub>1</sub>, a<sub>2</sub> AND a<sub>3</sub>” substitute “ a<sub>1</sub> AND a<sub>2</sub> ”.
- (b) in the heading to paragraph 1 omit “and existing vessels of overall length less than 15 metres for which London Convention parameters are available”;
- (c) in paragraph 1 for “Regulation [(EEC) No 2930/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/2930)” substitute “ Regulation (EU) 2017/1130 ”;
- (d) in the heading to paragraph 2 for “Existing vessels of overall length less than 15 metres” substitute “ Vessels of overall length less than 15 metres existing on 1 January 1995 ”;
- (e) in paragraph 2 omit the words from “For Spain, Greece and Italy” to the end;
- (f) omit paragraph 3; and
- (g) in paragraph 4—
  - (i) for “administration of the Member State concerned” substitute “ Secretary of State ”; and
  - (ii) omit “Where the tonnage is so determined” to the end.

(5) Omit Annexes 2 and 3.
