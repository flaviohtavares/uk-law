# The Common Fisheries Policy (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 35

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Commission Regulation [(EEC) No 954/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/0954) on sampling of catches for the purpose of determining the percentage of target species and protected species when fishing with small-meshed nets[^key-52e1eeb1446ecb03d5e2608d855f44e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation [(EEC) No 954/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/0954) on sampling of catches for the purpose of determining the percentage of target species and protected species when fishing with small-meshed nets is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 3—

- (a) for “the representative of the competent authorities of the Member State” substitute “ a representative of a fisheries administration ”; and
- (b) for “Article 3 of Council Regulation [(EEC) No 2057/82](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1982/2057) and Commission Regulation [(EEC) No 2807/83](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1983/2807)” substitute “ Council Regulation [(EC) No 1224/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1224) ”.

(3) In Article 5 omit paragraphs 5 and 6.

(4) Omit Articles 8 and 9.
