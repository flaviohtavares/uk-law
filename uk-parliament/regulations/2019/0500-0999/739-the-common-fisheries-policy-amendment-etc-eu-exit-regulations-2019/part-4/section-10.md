# The Common Fisheries Policy (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 10

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Commission Regulation (EU) No 468/2010 establishing the EU list of vessels engaged in illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing[^c-59ae0397d8b3f982a460c876c22b1e77]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) No 468/2010 establishing the EU list of vessels engaged in illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1 for “EU” substitute “ United Kingdom ”.

(3) In Article 2—

- (a) for “EU” substitute “ United Kingdom ”; and
- (b) omit the final two subparagraphs.

(4) In the Annex—

- (a) in the heading for “EU” substitute “ United Kingdom ”;
- (b) for the heading to PART A substitute— “ Vessels listed by the United Kingdom ”; and
- (c) for the heading to PART B substitute— “ Vessels listed by Regional Fisheries Management Organisations ”.
