# The Common Fisheries Policy (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 11

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Council Implementing Decision 2014/170/EU establishing a list of non-cooperating third countries in fighting IUU fishing pursuant to Regulation [(EC) No 1005/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1005) establishing a Community system to prevent, deter and eliminate illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing[^key-d69dac8a0297366ab7e8ed592add663b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Implementing Decision 2014/170/EU establishing a list of non-cooperating third countries in fighting IUU fishing pursuant to Regulation [(EC) No 1005/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1005) establishing a Community system to prevent, deter and eliminate illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1—

- (a) for “Union” substitute “ United Kingdom ”; and
- (b) omit “third”.

(3) Omit Article 2.

(4) In the Annex, in the heading omit “third”.
