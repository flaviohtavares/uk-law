## 12. *Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1010/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1010) laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation [(EC) No 1005/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1005) establishing a Community system to prevent, deter and eliminate illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading to Title 1 for “MEMBER STATES” substitute “ UNITED KINGDOM ”.

(3) In Article 3 omit paragraphs 3 and 4.

(4) In Article 4—

- (a) for “State” in each place it occurs, substitute “ state ”;
- (b) in point (b) for the words from “Chapter IV” to the end, substitute “ Article 9 of Council Regulation [(EC) No 1224/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1224); ”;
- (c) in point (c) for “port in the port Member State” substitute “ a port in the United Kingdom ”;
- (d) in point (d) for “the port Member State” substitute “ a fisheries administration ”; and
- (e) omit the final subparagraph.
