# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 123

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Revocation of Council Regulation (EU) No 331/2014[^key-799e404db679e79dd2e0f76435a18f09]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Regulation (EU) No 331/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 March 2014 establishing an exchange, assistance and training programme for the protection of the euro against counterfeiting (the ‘Pericles 2020’ programme) and repealing Council Decisions [2001/923/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2001/923), [2001/924/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2001/924), [2006/75/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2006/75), [2006/76/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2006/76), [2006/849/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2006/849) and [2006/850/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2006/850) is revoked.
