# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 59

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Amendment of the Firearms Act 1968[^key-d8374603fad12f867b34e509f8db2e03]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Firearms Act 1968 [^c23691331] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 5A [^c23691341] (exemptions from requirement of authority under s.5), omit subsection (3).

(3) In section 22 (acquisition and possession of firearms by minors), omit subsection (1A) [^c23691351].

(4) In section 27 (special provisions about firearm certificates), omit subsection (1A) [^c23691361].

(5) In section 28 (special provisions about shot gun certificates), omit subsection (1C) [^c23691371].

(6) Omit section 32A (documents for European purposes) [^c23691381] and the italic cross-heading before that section.

(7) Omit section 32B (renewal of European firearms pass) [^c23691391].

(8) Omit section 32C (variation, endorsement etc. of European documents) [^c23691401].

(9) In section 42A (information as to transactions under visitors' permits) [^c23691411]—

- (a) in subsection (1)(b)—
  - (i) omit “or (d)”;
  - (ii) omit “or purchases or acquisitions by collectors etc”;
  - (iii) for “the member States” substitute “ Great Britain ”;
- (b) in subsection (2)(b), for “the member State” substitute “ Great Britain ”.

(10) In section 48 (production of certificates) [^c23691421] —

- (a) omit subsection (1A);
- (b) in subsection (2) omit “or document”;
- (c) omit subsection (4).

(11) In section 57 (interpretation)—

- (a) in subsection (4) [^c23691431], omit the following definitions—
  - (i) “another member State” and “other member States”;
  - (ii) “Article 7 authority”;
  - (iii) “European firearms pass”;
- (b) omit subsection (4A) [^c23691441].

(12) In Part 1 of Schedule 6 (prosecution and punishment of offences) [^c23691451], in the table, omit the entries relating to sections 32B(5), 32C(6) and 48(4) of that Act.
