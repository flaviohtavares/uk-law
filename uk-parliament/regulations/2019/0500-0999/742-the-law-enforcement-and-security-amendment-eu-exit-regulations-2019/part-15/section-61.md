# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 61

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Amendment of the Firearms (Amendment) Act 1988[^c-67b2d20c730acbbd3d201b9f7a2f3bfd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Firearms (Amendment) Act 1988 [^c23691461] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 8A [^c23691471] (controls on defectively deactivated weapons), in each of subsections (2) and (3), for “the EU” in each place those words occur substitute “ the United Kingdom ”.

(3) In section 17 [^c23691481] (visitors' permits)—

- (a) in subsection (1A) [^c23691491]—
  - (i) in paragraph (b), omit “to a place outside the member States without first being taken to another member State”;
  - (ii) at the end of paragraph (b) insert “ or ”;
  - (iii) omit paragraph (d) and the “or” immediately preceding that paragraph;
- (b) omit subsection (3A) [^c23691501].

(4) In section 18 [^c23691511] (firearms acquired for export)—

- (a) omit subsection (1A) [^c23691521];
- (b) in subsection (4) [^c23691531] omit “and, in a case where the transaction is one for the purposes of which a document such as is mentioned in subsection (1A)(a) above is required to be produced, particulars of the agreement contained in that document”;
- (c) omit subsection (6) [^c23691541].

(5) Omit section 18A [^c23691551] (purchase or acquisition of firearms in other member States).

(6) In section 18B [^c23691561] (permitted electronic means), in subsection (1)—

- (a) in the opening words omit “or 18A”;
- (b) in paragraph (a), for “the section concerned” substitute “ that section ”.
