# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 41

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Revocation of additional legislation[^key-dd99ea50703f580ea2465f0bccdf6fcc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following Orders are revoked—

- (a) The European Police Office (Legal Capacities) Order 1996 [^c23691161];
- (b) The European Communities (Immunities and Privileges of the European Police Office) Order 1997 [^c23691171];
- (c) The European Communities (Immunities and Privileges of the European Police Office) (Amendment) Order 2004 [^c23691181].
