# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 3

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. General interpretation[^c-940a1e4c444222013abb6f05ea929db5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “**the 1990 Schengen Convention**” means the Convention implementing the Schengen Agreement of 14 June 1985 between the Governments of the States of the Benelux Economic Union, the Federal Republic of Germany and the French Republic on the gradual abolition of checks at their common borders [^c23690911];
- “**the CJDP Regulations**” means the Criminal Justice and Data Protection (Protocol No 36) Regulations 2014 [^c23690921];
- “**commencement day**” means the date and time on which these Regulations come into force;
- “**the Withdrawal Act**” means the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018.
