# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 12

> Text as at 2026-05-07.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Amendment of the Controlled Drugs (Drug Precursors) (Community External Trade) Regulations 2008[^c-1222fb5f694970ff7f2e07c1957faeb3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Controlled Drugs (Drug Precursors) (Community External Trade) Regulations 2008 [^c23691041] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation), omit the definition of “customs territory of the Community” and the word “and” immediately before it.

(3) In regulation 3(2) (competent authorities)—

- (a) after “17” omit “(except references to competent authorities of a third country)”;
- (b) for “26(5) and 32” substitute “ and 26(5) ”;
- (c) omit paragraph (6).

(4) In regulation 6(2) [^c23691051] (requirements, offences and penalties: exports), omit “either” and “or other competent authorities at the point of exit from the customs territory of the European Union”.

(5) In paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) of regulation 7 (requirements, offences and penalties: imports) [^c23691061], for “customs territory of the European Union” substitute “ United Kingdom ”.
