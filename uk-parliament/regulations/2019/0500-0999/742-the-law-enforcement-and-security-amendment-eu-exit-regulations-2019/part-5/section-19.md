## 19. Revocation of Regulation (EU) 2017/2101[^key-b416aaa8320927ff6b86d7cd7c583f9a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation (EU) 2017/2101 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 November 2017 amending Regulation [(EC) No 1920/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1920) as regards information exchange on, and an early warning system and risk assessment procedure for, new psychoactive substances is revoked.
