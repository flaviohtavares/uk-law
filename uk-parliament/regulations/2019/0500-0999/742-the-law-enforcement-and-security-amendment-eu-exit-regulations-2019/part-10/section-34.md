# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 34

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Revocation of Regulation (EU) 2018/1726[^c-9ab7bd4222f8d7a14ddca83a0fc0925c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation (EU) 2018/1726 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 November 2018 on the European Union Agency for the Operational Management of Large-Scale IT Systems in the Area of Freedom, Security and Justice (eu-LISA), and amending Regulation [(EC) No 1987/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1987) and Council Decision 2007/533/JHA and repealing Regulation (EU) No 1077/2011 is revoked.
