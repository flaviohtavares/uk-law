# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 116

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Revocation of Commission Decision (EU) 2016/809[^key-8b355d654f644ffa771f046cc49e1866]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Decision (EU) 2016/809 of 20 May 2016 on the notification by the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland of its wish to participate in certain acts of the Union in the field of police cooperation adopted before the entry into force of the Treaty of Lisbon and which are not part of the Schengen acquis is revoked.
