# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 114

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Interpretation[^c-d5c2f4704be786ccfdf0585fe961bb2e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, “**the Prüm Decision**” means Council Decision 2008/615/JHA of 23 June 2008 on the stepping up of cross-border cooperation, particularly in combating terrorism and cross-border crime.
