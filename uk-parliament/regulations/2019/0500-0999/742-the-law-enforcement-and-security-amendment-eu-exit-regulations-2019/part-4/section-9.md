# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 9

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Consequential amendment of the Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000[^key-c86697d91fe9ec99351f112ec9a4868f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 76A of the Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000 [^c23691021] (foreign surveillance operations)—

- (a) in each of subsections (3) and (10)—
  - (i) omit paragraph (a) and the “or” at the end of that paragraph;
  - (ii) in paragraph (b), omit “other”;
- (b) in subsection (11), omit the definition of “the Schengen Convention”.
