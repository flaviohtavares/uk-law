# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 72

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Saving provision – investigation teams operating in the UK on or after commencement day[^key-ad74320e1a4ae7aab934466601200bf3][^key-bfdb170a2f2096c44482c7bf707c19de]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 67 to 69 and 71 do not apply in a case to which any of the following provisions apply (and see Articles 62(2) and 63(1)(a) of the withdrawal agreement, Articles 50(2) and 52(a) of the EEA EFTA separation agreement and sections 7A to 7C of the Withdrawal Act)—

- (a) regulation 10 (transitional provision – surveillance which is not completed before commencement day);
- (b) article 62(2) or 63(1)(a) of the withdrawal agreement; or
- (c) article 50(2) or 52(a) of the EEA EFTA separation agreement.
