## 49. Amendment of the Control of Explosives Precursors etc. Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014[^key-5cf2dfb814a448a7a4844aef3bfdce61]

(1) The Control of Explosives Precursors etc. Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 [^c23691211] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) omit the definition of “EEA State”;
