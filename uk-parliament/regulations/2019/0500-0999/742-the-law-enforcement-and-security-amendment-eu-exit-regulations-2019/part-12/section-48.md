# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 48

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Amendment of the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001[^key-1579379bc56ce856faf9e040f7dacfe2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 18(4)(b) of the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001 [^c23691201] (restriction on disclosure of information for overseas purposes), for “an EU obligation” substitute “ a retained EU obligation ”.
