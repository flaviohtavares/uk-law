# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 137

## 137. Revocation of the Atlas Council Decision[^key-0a83340244d3b113960e4a3be9cd9e59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to regulations 138 (transitional provisions – assistance provided to member States on or after commencement day) and 139 (transitional provisions – assistance provided to the United Kingdom after commencement day), the Atlas Council Decision is revoked.
