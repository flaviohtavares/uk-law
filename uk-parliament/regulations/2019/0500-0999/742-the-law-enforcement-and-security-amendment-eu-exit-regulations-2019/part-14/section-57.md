# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 57

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Transitional provision[^key-20b9a3bed65360f4a165a341ec2d9918]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-742365c474ca1526c2eda23edbb2ffc3] Regulations 53(2), 55, and 56 do not apply in a case where, before commencement day—

- (a) a person has been arrested under a Part 1 warrant (within the meaning of the 2003 Act);
- (b) a person has been arrested under section 5 of the 2003 Act (provisional arrest) [^c23691311], or
- (c) a person has been extradited to or from the UK.

(2)[^key-4d63b95a89ce852fd6a29c888c15e7aa] See Article 62(1)(b) of the withdrawal agreement, Article 51 and Title IV of Part 3 of the EEA EFTA separation agreement and sections 7A to 7C of the Withdrawal Act.
