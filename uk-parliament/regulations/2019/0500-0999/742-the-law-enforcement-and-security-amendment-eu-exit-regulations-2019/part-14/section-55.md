> Text as at 2026-05-07.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Amendment of the Extradition Act 2003 (Designation of Part 1 Territories) Order 2003[^c-95353c52baa4e4a169eb39cdc5dc7462]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Extradition Act 2003 (Designation of Part 1 Territories) Order 2003 [^c23691291] is amended as follows.

(2) For article 2 (designated territories) substitute—

> Gibraltar is designated for the purposes of Part 1 of the Extradition Act 2003.

.
