## 90. Amendment of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (Designation of Participating Countries) (Scotland) Order 2009[^key-50766bde904e54bec42f05f1a9b49b54]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(2) For article 2 substitute—

> **2.**
>
> Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Italy,[^key-95abe075ee1572c4cbc99084f9be6eb7] Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain and Sweden are designated as participating countries under section 51(2)(b) of the 2003 Act [^key-92a0925ac8c2f3042a2f52ebeec116d8]....
