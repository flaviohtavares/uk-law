# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 78

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Amendment of the Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995[^key-a7f3a737603701506041adc60a96cfda]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 27 of the Criminal Law (Consolidation) Scotland Act 1995 (Lord Advocate's direction) [^c23691781], for subsection (2) substitute—

> (2) The Lord Advocate may also give a direction under this section by virtue of section 15(4) of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 or on a request made by the Attorney-General of the Isle of Man, Jersey or Guernsey acting under legislation corresponding to this Part of this Act.

.
