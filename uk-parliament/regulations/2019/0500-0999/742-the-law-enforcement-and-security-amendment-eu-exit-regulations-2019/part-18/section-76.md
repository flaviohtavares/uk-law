# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 76

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Amendment of the Criminal Justice Act 1988[^key-f3be51df37148926e8cbbfa617233e04]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 13 to the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (evidence before service courts) [^c23691721], in paragraph 6 (letters of request etc.), in sub-paragraph (1), omit “, and no order shall be made or validated under Part 2 of the Criminal Justice (European Investigation Order) Regulations 2017,”.
