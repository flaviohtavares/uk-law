# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 77

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Amendment of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^key-190b2543f823a17af45e6d38540c150f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995 [^c23691731] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 210(1) (consideration of time spent in custody) [^c23691741], in paragraph (c), omit “or regulation 20 or 54 of the Criminal Justice (European Investigation Order) Regulations 2017”.

(3) In section 267A (citation of witnesses for precognition) [^c23691751], omit subsection (1A).

(4) In section 272 (evidence by letter of request or on commission) [^c23691761], omit subsection (14).

(5) In section 273 (television link evidence from abroad) [^c23691771], omit subsection (5).
