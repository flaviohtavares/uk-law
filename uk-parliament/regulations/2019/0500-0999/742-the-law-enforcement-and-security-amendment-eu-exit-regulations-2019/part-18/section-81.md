## 81. Amendment of the Criminal Justice (Evidence) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004[^c-02d9f92c1fb098c277f06e9a757b981e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 21 of the Criminal Justice (Evidence) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (hearsay evidence: business and other documents) [^c23691831], in paragraph (4)(b)(iii) omit “an order under Part 2 of the Criminal Justice (European Investigation Order) Regulations 2017”.
