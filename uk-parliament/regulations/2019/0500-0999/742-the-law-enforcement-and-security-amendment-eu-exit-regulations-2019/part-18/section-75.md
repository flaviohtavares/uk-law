# The Law Enforcement and Security (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 75

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Amendment of the Criminal Justice Act 1987[^key-293a42c364b654e822a9cbd8a6f5e3cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Criminal Justice Act 1987 [^c23691691] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 2 (Director's investigation powers) [^c23691701]—

- (a) in subsection (1A), for paragraph (b) substitute—

  > - (b) the Secretary of State acting under section 15(2) of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003, in response to a request received from a person mentioned in section 13(2) of that Act (an “overseas authority”).

  ;
- (b) in subsection (18), omit the definition of “overseas authority”.

(3) In section 3(6) (disclosure of information) [^c23691711], in paragraph (n), for “the Treaty on European Union or any other” substitute “ a ”.
