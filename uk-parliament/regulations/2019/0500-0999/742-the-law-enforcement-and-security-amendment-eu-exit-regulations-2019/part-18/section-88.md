## 88. Amendment of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (Designation of Participating Countries) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) Order 2009[^c-e4cfcbdc8b8a71495b843ce49e2e923f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (Designation of Participating Countries) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) Order 2009 [^c23691881] is amended as follows.

(2) For article 3 substitute—

> **3.**
>
> Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Italy,[^c-ed43a88e0dff665918eade262650b4cb] Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain and Sweden are designated as participating countries under section 51(2)(b) of the 2003 Act [^c-f53d8fc031050a042208b670e5623193]....
