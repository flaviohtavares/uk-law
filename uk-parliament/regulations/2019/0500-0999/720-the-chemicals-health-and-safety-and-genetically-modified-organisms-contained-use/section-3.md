# The Chemicals (Health and Safety) and Genetically Modified Organisms (Contained Use) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 3

> Text as at 2025-02-27.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Revocation of Commission Regulation (EU) No 440/2010[^key-076eb2eef26beda9485941ad4a3ecaa9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Regulation (EU) No 440/2010 of 21 May 2010 on the fees payable to the European Chemicals Agency pursuant to Regulation [(EC) No 1272/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/1272) of the European Parliament and of the Council on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures is revoked.
