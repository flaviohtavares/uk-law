# The A19/A184 Testo’s Junction Alteration (Correction) Order 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 941

Infrastructure Planning

The A19/A184 Testo’s Junction Alteration (Correction) Order 2019

Made

14th May 2019

Coming into force

15th May 2019

The A19/A184 Testo’s Junction Alteration Development Consent Order 2018 (“the Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of the Act[^f00003].

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, before the end of the relevant period, as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00004] for the correction of errors and omissions in the Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed South Tyneside Council, as the local planning authority for the area in which the land to which the Order relates is situated, that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraph 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the A19/A184 Testo’s Junction Alteration (Correction) Order 2019 and comes into force on 15th May 2019.

## 2. Corrections

The A/19/A184 Testo’s Junction Alteration Development Consent Order 2018 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Natasha Kopala

Head of Transport and Works Act Orders Unit

Department for Transport

14th May 2019

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects correctable errors in the A19/A184 Testo’s Junction Alteration Development Consent Order 2018 ([S.I. 2018 No. 994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/994)), a development consent order made under the Planning Act [2008 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/29), following a request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
