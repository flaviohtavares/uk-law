## 7. Amendment of regulation 5 (classification of medicinal products)[^key-ee37b274e61b3192a226ccad3f8e4521]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 5 is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-1d8157fc860828ff734c640918a2fc1d] In paragraph (1)(b), before “a product that” insert “in the case of a medicinal product for sale or supply in Northern Ireland,”.

(3) In paragraph (2)—

- (a)[^key-102a3d8a36fe6692f4e3880e004456a8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-451623ec82b33da7d0aea7954191df27] in sub-paragraph (d), before “an Article 126a” insert “in the case of a medicinal product for sale or supply in Northern Ireland,”.

(4) In paragraph (3)—

- (a)[^key-026c6898518962253a7bf316bcaf3aae] in sub-paragraph (b), before “a medicinal product” insert “in the case of a medicinal product for sale or supply in Northern Ireland, ”;
- (b)[^key-e16e2dff0a046d6fb88ae5c02400bb70] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) In paragraph (4), in sub-paragraph (b), before “an Article” insert “in the case of a medicinal product for sale or supply in Northern Ireland,”.[^key-a52864a768cc28833754a657681942da]

(6) In paragraph (5)—

- (a)[^key-301c6da6c774bdae73ce0b06d8113842] in sub-paragraph (b), before “a product that” insert “in the case of a medicinal product for sale or supply in Northern Ireland,”; and
- (b)[^key-5c44e8830599a59faf93554655abe31f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
