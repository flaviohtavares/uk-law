# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 134

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 134. Amendment of regulation 164 (effect of determination)[^key-0d32cf5151dc177e760edf466270b337]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 164(2)(a) and (b)—

- (a) insert “ UK ” before “marketing authorisation”; and
- (b)[^key-2fbc916808352e144ffe60f421f031b4] for “Article 126a authorisation” insert “, only in relation to a product for sale or supply in Northern Ireland, an Article 126a authorisation or an EU marketing authorisation,”.
