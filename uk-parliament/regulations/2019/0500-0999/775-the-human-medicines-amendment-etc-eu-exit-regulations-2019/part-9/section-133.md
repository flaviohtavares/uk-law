# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 133

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133. Amendment of regulation 159 (provisional determination)[^key-a180d183e397ff59de6de5eb2b78d443]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 159(1)—

- (a) insert “ UK ” before “marketing authorisation”; and
- (b)[^key-721b83a6ddd6128ae2fde0bcf2985bfa] for “Article 126a authorisation” insert “, only in relation to a product for sale or supply in Northern Ireland, an Article 126a authorisation or an EU marketing authorisation,”.
