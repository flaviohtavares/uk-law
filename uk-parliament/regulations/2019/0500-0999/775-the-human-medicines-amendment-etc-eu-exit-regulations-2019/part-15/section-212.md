# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 212

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 212. Amendment of regulation 280 (general principles)[^key-a4953e2dfc370dc8b3558ffd511a817b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 280[^key-04c8ebbb7b5f2f4ca5d8541c5aad7092] —

- (a) in paragraph (1)[^key-f8d275f5d421a284970f38f2b5709a22] for “marketing authorisation,” substitute “ UK marketing authorisation, EU marketing authorisation,[^key-019065cb863b77b6da671f9d03ccbe81] ”; and
- (b)[^key-d44f0c1dd6f566106cac1ff0f184e274] after paragraph (1) insert—

  > (1A) Where an advertisement mentioned in paragraph (1) relates to a product in relation to which there is a separate authorisation or registration in force in Great Britain and in Northern Ireland, it may not be published in the whole United Kingdom unless it complies with the particulars listed in the summary of the product characteristics in each of those authorisations or registrations (as the case may be).

  .
