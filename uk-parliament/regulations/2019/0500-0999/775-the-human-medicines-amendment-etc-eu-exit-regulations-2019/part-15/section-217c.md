# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 217C

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 217C. Amendment of regulation 307 (corrective statement)[^M_I_f845981a-78f1-48f6-c5b4-2d3c25e60530][^c-3023b79077f82805eda3be30f08a40cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 307—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a), after “subject of the notice” insert—

  > in—
  >
  > - (i) Great Britain;
  > - (ii) Northern Ireland; or
  > - (iii) both Great Britain and Northern Ireland

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(b), after “that advertisement” insert—

  > in—
  >
  > - (i) Great Britain;
  > - (ii) Northern Ireland; or
  > - (iii) both Great Britain and Northern Ireland

  ;
- (c) in paragraph (2)(a), for “, either in full or in part; and” substitute—

  > in respect of—
  >
  > - (i) Great Britain;
  > - (ii) Northern Ireland; or
  > - (iii) both Great Britain and Northern Ireland,
  >
  > either in full or in part; and

  .
