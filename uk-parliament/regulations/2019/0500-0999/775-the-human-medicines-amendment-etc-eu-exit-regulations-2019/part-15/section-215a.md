# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 215A

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 215A. Amendment of regulation 298 (free samples for persons qualified to prescribe or supply medicinal products)[^M_I_ae25c194-20a7-4f86-edec-7eef90617086][^c-3745dc8198c206c5b397cc1bf75cac83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 298, for paragraph (5)(a) substitute—

> - (a) is no larger than the smallest presentation of the product that is available for sale—
>   - (i) in the case of a medicinal product for sale or supply in Great Britain, in Great Britain, or
>   - (ii) in the case of a medicinal product for sale or supply in Northern Ireland, in Northern Ireland;

.
