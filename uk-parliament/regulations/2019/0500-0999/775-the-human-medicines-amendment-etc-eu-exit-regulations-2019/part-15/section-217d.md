# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 217D

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 217D. Amendment of regulation 311 (application for injunction)[^M_I_dd0437c5-f36d-471d-ce86-8630a2c756a8][^c-3023b79077f82805eda3be30f08a40cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 311—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a), for “Chapter 2; and” substitute—

  > Chapter 2 in respect of—
  >
  > - (i) Great Britain;
  > - (ii) Northern Ireland; or
  > - (iii) both Great Britain and Northern Ireland; and

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (3), after “ the advertisement” insert—

  > in—
  >
  > - (i) Great Britain;
  > - (ii) Northern Ireland; or
  > - (iii) both Great Britain and Northern Ireland,
  >
  > as the case may be.

  .
