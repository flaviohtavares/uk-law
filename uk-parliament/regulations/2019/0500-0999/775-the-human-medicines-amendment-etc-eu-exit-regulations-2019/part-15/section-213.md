# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 213

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 213. Amendment of regulation 281 (duties of authorisation holders and registration holders)[^key-c784f959f429256c1a90811a2a62c697]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 281(1)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), insert “ UK ” before “marketing authorisation”;
- (b)[^key-878641762ab5e4f53e2ed857deca22d5] omit “or” at the end of sub-paragraph (c); and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (d), after “for a medicinal product” insert—

  > ; or
  >
  > - (e) an EU marketing authorisation for a medicinal product.

  .
