# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 198

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 198. Amendment of regulation 257 (packaging requirements: general)[^c-6e41b1bcb918541d06e150800839ab77]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 257 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (6), after “this regulation,” insert “ regulation 257C where the product is for sale or supply in Great Britain only[^c-4bddbf38b3cdf3dea70fafce18bf5681],”.

(3) After paragraph (7) insert—

> (8) Nothing in this regulation applies to the outer or immediate packaging of an advanced therapy medicinal product for sale or supply in Great Britain only[^c-3bddfca4b4ea5ca50b31b7a22ce00da1].

.
