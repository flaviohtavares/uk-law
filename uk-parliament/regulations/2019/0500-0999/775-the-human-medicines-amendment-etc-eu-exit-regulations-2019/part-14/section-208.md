# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 208

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 208. Amendment of regulation 269 (offences relating to packaging and package leaflets: other persons)[^c-1309fb8f97631b6cc5c6bab50d3476fe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 269 [^c23885411] is amended as follows.

(1A)[^c-d965e8ca15d81ca1b9ffc7102ed5e7fc] In the heading to the regulation, after “packaging and package leaflets” insert “in Great Britain”.

(2)[^c-7000dadd0ae3abe8f845b0e03d7170e2] In paragraph (1)—

- (a) for “marketing authorisation, Article 126a authorisation” substitute “UKMA(UK), UKMA(GB)”;
- (b) after “the purpose of sale or supply” insert “, in Great Britain”.

(2A)[^c-87c9032ea9dcfdbdefc9f9434f4a886e] In paragraph (2), after “for the purpose of sale or supply,” insert “in Great Britain”.

(3) In paragraph (2)(a)—

- (a) for “Article 28 or 32 of the Paediatric Regulation” substitute “ regulation 50C(4), 50D(8) or 58A(2)(b) ”; and
- (b) omit “, Article 9 of Commission Regulation 2016/161”.
