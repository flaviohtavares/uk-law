# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 203

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 203. Amendment of regulation 260 (package leaflets)[^c-d05c15079a6d18cd99cb36725d18b76f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 260 is amended as follows.

(2) After paragraph (1) insert—

> (1A) If the medicinal product is an advanced therapy medicinal product for sale or supply in Great Britain only[^c-8f29cb36b49510c5e421c9fdb5d7b951] (other than an exempt advanced therapy medicinal product), the package leaflet must contain the information specified in Part 3 of Schedule 27 in the order specified in that Part.

.

(3) In paragraph (2), after “Part 2 of that Schedule)” insert “ , or where the product is an advanced therapy medicinal product for sale or supply in Great Britain only[^c-1db584ccb61704bb968592bc56a0d8d2], the information specified in Part 3 of that Schedule, ”.

(4) In paragraph (3), for “marketing authorisation[^c-5527cda7c6b878fff7e91c32dcec5bc3]...” substitute “ UK marketing authorisation , EU marketing authorisation,[^c-7b7ed4a1f4096caecba589cdeeb03399] ”.
