# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 199

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 199. Amendment of regulation 257A (packaging requirements: medicinal products required to bear safety features)[^M_I_3742a371-26e7-414d-b4b3-618586d47475][^c-14b81dfca04a9df61879b4766b4d66d2]
