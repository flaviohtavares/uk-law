# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 132A

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 132A. Amendment of regulation 157 (requests from other member States)[^M_I_3f72faf5-51f6-4268-e492-34095d32e530][^key-ef43389d5afda7ebcc00721e5d882b11]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 157(1)—

- (a) in the heading for “other member States” substitute “EU member States”; and
- (b) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) after “where the licensing authority” insert “, in relation to a UKMA(NI),”; and
  - (ii) for “another member State” substitute “a member State”.
