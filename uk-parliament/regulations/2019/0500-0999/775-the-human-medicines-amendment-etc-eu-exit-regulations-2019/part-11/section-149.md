# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 149

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 149. Amendment of regulation 189 (signal detection: licensing authority obligations)[^key-6acbe857d804fdfcb660061c1180dec1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 189 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “in the Eudravigilance database” substitute “ that it collects by virtue of operating its pharmacovigilance system under this Part ”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (d), for “regulations 59 to 61” substitute “ regulations 59, 60 and 61 ”.

(3)[^key-974553c55ca8607a6f35bd58414f3a91] In paragraphs (2) and (3), for “The licensing” insert “In relation to medicinal products subject to a UKMA(UK), a UKMA(NI), a THR(UK), a THR(NI) or an Article 126a authorisation, the licensing”.
