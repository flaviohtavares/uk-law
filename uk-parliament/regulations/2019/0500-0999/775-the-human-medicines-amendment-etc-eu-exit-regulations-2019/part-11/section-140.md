# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 140

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 140. Amendment of regulation 180 (obligation on licensing authority to audit pharmacovigilance system)[^c-1ce96ccfab007efcda1f96a56048a07c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 180 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1),

- (a)[^c-2ceeacfcd9f6e7ec1bec2c307b921c87] after “its pharmacovigilance system” insert “relating to medicinal products for sale or supply in Great Britain” and
- (b)[^c-539c0f9204fe62a1b22e0e634e4ea6bf] omit “and report the results of that audit to the European Commission”.

(2A)[^c-a48b953ba4e8a44c5a3c6d0bf6f69c9f] After paragraph (1) insert—

> (1A) The licensing authority must perform a regular audit of its pharmacovigilance system relating to medicinal products for sale or supply in Northern Ireland and report the results of that audit to the European Commission.

.

(3) In paragraph (2)—

- (a) omit “results of the”; and
- (b) for “reported to the European Commission” substitute “ performed ”.

(4)[^c-b79709737d9d7f880c745720c3b1892c] After paragraph (2) insert—

> (3) The results of the audit referred to in paragraph (1A) must be reported to the European Commission—
>
> - (a) on the first occasion no later than 21st September 2021;
> - (b) every two years after the first occasion.

.
