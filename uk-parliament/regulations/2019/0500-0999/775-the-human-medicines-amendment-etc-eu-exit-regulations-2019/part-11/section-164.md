## 164. Amendment of regulation 203 (obligations on licensing authority in relation to national medicines web-portal)[^key-46598b9e98ba1bd8741f904c09218959]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 203 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1), omit from “linked” to the end.

(3)[^key-a07a54fc1080f0ab28fdf98481f42ef4] In paragraph (2), after sub-paragraph (d) insert—

> - (da) the list published by the licensing authority under, or which applies by virtue of, regulation 202A;
