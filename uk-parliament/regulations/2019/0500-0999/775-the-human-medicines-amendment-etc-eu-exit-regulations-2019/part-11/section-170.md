> Text as at 2021-08-03.

## 170. Amendment of regulation 206 (infringement notices)[^c-29da56644af32e3c6c494f9ab776dede]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 206 [^c23885131] is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-eb1bdb64aca140c42d47ec978b1746a0] In paragraph (3), after “paragraph (1)” insert “in relation to a product authorised for sale or supply under a UKMA(NI), UKMA(UK), THR(NI) or THR(UK)”.

(3) In paragraph (4) after sub-paragraph (a) insert—

> - (aa) Schedule 12A;
