# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 152

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 152. Amendment of regulation 192 (obligation to submit periodic safety reports: derogation from general requirements)[^key-5f9f73aa7c1c81b13058c538ae3103ab]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 192 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)(a), insert “ UK ” before “marketing authorisation”.

(3) In paragraph (3), after “EMA” insert “and the licensing authority or, in the case of a holder of a UKMA(GB), to the licensing authority only,[^key-0ba4ed2887925fec2364f39d18c30424]

(4)[^key-f57c3d7d85a0e796ec92f8d5e2af1e40] In paragraph (9), after “paragraph (3)(a)” insert “from the holder of a UKMA(UK), UKMA(NI), THR(UK), THR(NI) or Article 126a authorisation”.
