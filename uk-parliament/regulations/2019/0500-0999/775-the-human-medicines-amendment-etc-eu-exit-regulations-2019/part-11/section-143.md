# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 143

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 143. Amendment of regulation 184 (obligation on holder to audit pharmacovigilance system)[^c-75110494a69e49d4d0e55e921b270c5f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 184, after paragraph (2) insert—

> (3) The holder of a UKMA(GB) or THR(GB)[^c-3083ce3d0cbabb8119e12d770e231a1b] must also comply with the requirements of paragraph 13 of Schedule 12A in relation to auditing the pharmacovigilance system.

.
