# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 157

> Text as at 2021-08-03.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 157. Amendment of regulation 197 (EU urgent action procedure)[^c-28f4a2352aea84cd427da9561a7cb550][^c-598095756f9a6c85e87c75c3c3094425]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In[^c-f67be99956d67cdb30da178256467b6f] regulation 197 , in paragraph (1), after “class of medicinal products” insert “authorised for sale or supply under a UKMA(NI), UKMA(UK), THR(NI), THR(UK) or Article 126a authorisation”[^c-e862658e4f17d8116630a2e1371ec87c] .
