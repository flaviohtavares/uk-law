# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 145

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 145. Amendment of regulation 186 (reporting obligations on the licensing authority)[^M_I_1f492c40-c993-4779-8d22-5fb7cdf22014][^c-0979d65ae6e5c722941a2d35a37128f5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 186—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for sub-paragraphs (d) and (e) substitute—

  > - (d) submit reports of serious suspected adverse reactions in Northern Ireland that it has recorded under regulation 185 in relation to—
  >   - (i) a UKMA(NI),
  >   - (ii) a UKMA(UK),
  >   - (iii) a THR(NI),
  >   - (iv) a THR(UK), or
  >   - (v) an Article 126a authorisation,
  >
  >   to the EMA before the end of the period of 15 days beginning on the day following the day on which the report was received; and
  > - (e) submit reports of non-serious suspected adverse reactions in Northern Ireland that it has recorded under regulation 185 in relation to—
  >   - (i) a UKMA(NI),
  >   - (ii) a UKMA(UK),
  >   - (iii) a THR(NI),
  >   - (iv) a THR(UK), or
  >   - (v) an Article 126a authorisation,
  >
  >   to the EMA before the end of the period of 90 days beginning on the day following the day on which the report was received.

  ;
- (b) omit paragraph (4).
