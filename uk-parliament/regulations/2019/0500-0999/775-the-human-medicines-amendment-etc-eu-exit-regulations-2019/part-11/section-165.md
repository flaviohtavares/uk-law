> Text as at 2021-08-03.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 165. Amendment of regulation 204 (obligation on licensing authority in relation to public announcements)[^key-2c48677b0e1690b5ae5d0b20352dd915][^key-1402e809034344d41886e1e016e8303f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In[^key-4b39c0f60a4a3220a79bac0b9e1e96df] regulation 204 , in paragraph (1), after “pharmacovigilance concerns” insert “which relate to products authorised under a UKMA(NI) or UKMA(UK)[^key-0711d76602a82d9a67a93f07caa2f8ef].
