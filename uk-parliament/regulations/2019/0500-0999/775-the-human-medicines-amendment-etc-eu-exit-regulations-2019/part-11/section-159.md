## 159. Amendment of regulation 199 (submission of draft study protocols for required studies)[^key-916890d620b8379e7f89cd82dc5d3332]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 199 is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-5342028d08ec4cdb8e12b545c7b4f47a] In paragraph (2) for “to the body specified in paragraph (3)” to the end substitute—

> to—
>
> - (a) the body specified in paragraph (3) and the licensing authority (where not otherwise required by paragraph (3)), where the authorisation is a UKMA(NI) or UKMA(UK);
> - (b) the licensing authority, where the authorisation is a UKMA(GB),
>
> before the study is commenced.

.

(3)[^key-316262528b03f4fc3e7f304ba432d6cf] In paragraph (4)—

- (a) after “protocol is submitted” insert “only”;
- (b) after “paragraphs (2) and (3)(a)” insert “(and is not submitted to the Pharmacovigilance Risk Assessment Committee)”.
