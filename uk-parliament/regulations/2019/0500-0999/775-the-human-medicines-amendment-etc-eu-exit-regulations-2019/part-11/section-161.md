> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 161. Amendment of regulation 201 (submission and evaluation of final study reports for required studies)[^c-347274092d394bcef58108f07c7e1130]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 201 is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-d2b603d24849be4dbfbe717869232b43] In paragraph (2) for “to the body specified in paragraph (3)” to the end substitute—

> to—
>
> - (a) the body specified in paragraph (3) and the licensing authority (where not otherwise required by paragraph (3)), where the authorisation for the product is a UKMA(NI) or UKMA(UK);
> - (b) the licensing authority, where the authorisation for the product is a UKMA(GB),
>
> a final study report and an abstract of the study results.

.

(3)[^c-9e90f39770f0015c6d20aa548080c835] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) In paragraph (4), omit “for reports falling under paragraph (3)(a)” and “for reports falling under paragraph (3)(b)[^c-0013669516661e56c107efcddd9fca5d]
