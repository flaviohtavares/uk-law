# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 154

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154. Amendment of regulation 194 (responding to a single assessment of PSUR under Article 107e of the 2001 Directive)[^key-24a9aa74abb0af0fb613055dabad657d][^key-6961f13cf76e90c24c0f11c2ec0c73ba][^key-beebb08275bddbd608222ef3bbe70958]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 194(1) after “medicinal product” insert “authorised under a UKMA(NI), UKMA(UK), THR(NI), THR(UK) or Article 126a authorisation”.
