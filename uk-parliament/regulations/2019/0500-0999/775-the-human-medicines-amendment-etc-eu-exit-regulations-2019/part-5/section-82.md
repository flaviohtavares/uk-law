# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 82

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Amendment of regulation 73 (obligation to notify placing on the market etc)[^c-7aee830fe99fd36513dd1005bf4306d0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 73 [^c23884941] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (5A)(c), for “third country” substitute “ country other than the United Kingdom ”.

(3)[^c-0205cdfbf62bd734a709e70ed76930ea] In paragraph (5C), for “UK marketing authorisation” insert “UKMA(NI) or UKMA(UK)”.
