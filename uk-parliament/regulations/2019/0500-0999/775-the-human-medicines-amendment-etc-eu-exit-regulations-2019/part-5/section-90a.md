# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 90A

> Text as at 2021-08-03.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90A. Amendment of regulation 89 (offences in connection with withdrawal of product from market)[^M_I_b14fd4c4-3877-4e65-db19-81cf7981c01f][^c-a3d25bbd06dff3bfd47ed38b67897e3f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*
