# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 81

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Amendment of regulation 72 (sale etc of suspended medicinal product)[^M_I_06066963-dd5c-406c-c833-08add28d3113][^c-4151a3315c2f0ce8d55b7d4e57a0e344]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 72(1), for “regulation 69 or 70(2) or Article 20(4) of Regulation [(EC) No 726/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/0726)” substitute—

> —
>
> - (a) in the case of a medicinal product authorised for sale or supply by a UKMA(GB), regulation 69;
> - (b) in the case of a medicinal product authorised for sale or supply by a UKMA(NI) or UKMA(UK), regulation 69 or Article 20(4) of Regulation [(EC) No 726/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/0726).

.
