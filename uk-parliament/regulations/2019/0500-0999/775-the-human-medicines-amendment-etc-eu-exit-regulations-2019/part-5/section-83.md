# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 83

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Amendment of regulation 75 (obligation to provide information relating to safety etc)[^key-dcddaac9bb6fb29df3e6ba27cd1ba6c9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 75(5) [^c23884951]—

- (a) for sub-paragraph (a) substitute—

  > - (a) in a country other than the United Kingdom;

  and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), insert “ UK ” before “marketing authorisation”.
