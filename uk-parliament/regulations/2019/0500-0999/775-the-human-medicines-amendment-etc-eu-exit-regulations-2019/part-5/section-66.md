# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 66

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Amendment of regulation 60 (conditions of UK marketing authorisation: exceptional circumstances)[^M_I_d1857864-fa74-4bba-f9ff-aebdc40608ad][^c-10293bac94ddef373f27ff9a4e9e43c9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 60—

- (a) after “UK marketing authorisation” in each place it occurs (including the heading to the regulation) insert “or parallel import licence”;
- (b) after “the authorisation” in each place it occurs insert “or licence”;
- (c) in paragraph (3), after “an authorisation” insert “or licence”;
- (d) for paragraph (9) substitute—

  > (9) The licensing authority must notify the EMA of any UKMA(NI) or UKMA(UK) that it has granted subject to a condition included in accordance with this regulation.

  ;
- (e) in paragraph (10), after “a marketing authorisation” insert “or licence”.
