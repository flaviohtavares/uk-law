# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 84

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Amendment of regulation 76 (obligation in relation to product information)[^M_I_55a3b3b3-fa37-405f-f099-d3b17258571b][^key-7854ff9a104acbaf2744bcbc65eb845f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For regulation 76(2), substitute—

> (2) In this regulation “current scientific knowledge” includes the conclusions of the assessment and recommendations made public by means of—
>
> - (a) in the case of a medicinal product authorised for sale or supply by a UKMA(NI) or a UKMA(UK)—
>   - (i) the European medicines web-portal established in accordance with Article 26 of Regulation [(EC) No 726/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/0726), and
>   - (ii) the UK web-portal established in accordance with regulation 203(1);
> - (b) in the case of a medicinal product authorised for sale or supply by a UKMA(GB), the UK web-portal established in accordance with regulation 203(1).
