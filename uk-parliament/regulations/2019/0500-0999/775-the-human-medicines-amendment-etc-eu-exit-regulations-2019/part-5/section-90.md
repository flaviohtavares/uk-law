# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 90

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Application of regulations 81 to 94 (offences relating to EU marketing authorisations)[^M_I_76bc4d53-834b-4105-df8b-e06892402d77][^c-a3d25bbd06dff3bfd47ed38b67897e3f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before regulation 81 (obligation to update information supplied in connection with EU application), insert—

> **A81. Application of regulations 81 to 94**
>
> Regulations 81 to 94 apply in relation to medicinal products for sale or supply in Northern Ireland.

.
