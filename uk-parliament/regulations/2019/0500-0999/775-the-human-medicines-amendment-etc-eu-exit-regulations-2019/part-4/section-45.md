# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 45

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Amendment of regulation 46 (requirement for authorisation)[^c-d14007fa5e01e5448641ede7fe74d494]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 46 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), before “marketing authorisation”, insert “ UK ”;
- (b)[^c-1a323d6e6edc99dc9025a28efcf7be69] after sub-paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (aa) an EU marketing authorisation;

  .

(3) In paragraph (3), before “European Economic Area” insert “ United Kingdom or the ”.

(4) In paragraph (6)—

- (a)[^c-8ef45c266363220944b657b453833253] after “in force for the product” insert “in the country in which the product is intended to be sold or supplied, or offered for sale or supply”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (a), before “marketing authorisation”, insert “UK”; and
- (c) after sub-paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (aa) an EU marketing authorisation;

  .

(5)[^c-99b2f59a709d9a531ee1bc7f507a6eb2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) In paragraph (9), before “European Economic Area” insert “ United Kingdom or the ”.

(7) In paragraph (11)(a), before “European Economic Area” insert “ United Kingdom or the ”.
