# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 190

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 190. Amendment of regulation 232 (exemption for supply etc under a PGD by dental practices and clinics: England and Wales)[^M_I_db4c99ac-96a5-46a4-8fbe-20ab285185cb][^key-c9d248ed5d0c336f79151af9ca3ed3b6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For regulation 232(8) substitute—

> (8) Condition F is that when the product is supplied, a UKMA(GB), UKMA(UK), certificate of registration, THR(GB) or THR(UK) is in force in relation to it.
