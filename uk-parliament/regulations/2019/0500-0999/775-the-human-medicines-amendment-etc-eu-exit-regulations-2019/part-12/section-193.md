# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 193

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 193. Amendment of Schedule 17 (exemptions for sale, supply or administration by certain persons)[^key-0a034c8ad88647719710f7448d774164]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 17 [^c23885281] is amended as follows.

(2) In the table in Part 1, in column 1 in entry 10, for “marketing authorisations” substitute “UK marketing authorisations, EU marketing authorisations[^key-aaa65077ded5c8870425b8e1f732d379].

(3) In the table in Part 4, in columns 1 and 2 in entry 9, for “marketing authorisation” substitute “UK marketing authorisation, EU marketing authorisation[^key-5d56de3b83e02879e7b5f275ce655b61].
