# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 34

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Amendment of regulation 43A (requirement for wholesale dealers to decommission the unique identifier)[^M_I_31893983-9eb8-4eac-c574-739652e5634e][^c-e438817247ccdbe84a7479b8ab2a820b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 43A—

- (a) in paragraph (2) for “in the United Kingdom” substitute “in Northern Ireland”; and
- (b) in paragraph (3)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (g) omit “a police force in England, Wales or Scotland or”; and
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (l) for “care” substitute “nursing”.
