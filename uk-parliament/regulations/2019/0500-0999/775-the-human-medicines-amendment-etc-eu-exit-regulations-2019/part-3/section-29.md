# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 29

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Amendment of regulation 38 (imports)[^c-da76f6434c32268253c61002d079689e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 38 [^c23884551] is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-0de10adc01de037707eb57f32641577b] In the heading, after “states other than EEA states” insert “/ countries other than approved countries for import”.

(3)[^c-018c045b89a662b74ca1d0a6038d0601] In paragraph (2) for “from a state other than an EEA State” substitute—

> from—
>
> - (a) in the case of an import into Great Britain, a country other than an approved country for import, or
> - (b) in the case of an import into Northern Ireland, a country other than an EEA State

.

(4)[^c-92048ef9b6ee93d5c77c9c7cb9e4c0e1] In paragraph (3)(b) for “a state other than an EEA State” substitute “, in the case of an import into Great Britain, a country other than an approved country for import and in the case of an import into Northern Ireland, a country other than an EEA State”.
