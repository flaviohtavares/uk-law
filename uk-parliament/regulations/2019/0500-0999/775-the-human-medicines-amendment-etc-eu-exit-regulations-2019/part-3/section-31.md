# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 31

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Amendment of regulation 42 (conditions for wholesale dealer's licence)[^M_I_013f0b6d-fadf-41a7-f84b-6fda83e66816]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 42 [^c23884571] is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-b643e8240e7776eaaf31f5499ad3e0f5] In paragraph (1), after “45” insert “(in the case of a wholesale dealer’s licence held in Northern Ireland) or regulations 43 to 45AA (in the case of a wholesale dealer’s licence held in Great Britain)”.

(3)[^key-76b01e3f066ea3e1ca9796612488284b] In paragraph (4)—

- (a) for “The requirements” substitute “Where a wholesale dealer’s licence relates to wholesale dealings in Northern Ireland, the requirements”; and
- (b) for “provisions of a wholesale dealer’s” substitute “provisions of that”.
