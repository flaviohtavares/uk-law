# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 224

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 224. Amendment of regulation 341 (decisions under the Human Medicines Regulations 2012)[^M_I_97fc1d16-d95e-4135-e08a-25bdf5546e45][^c-0857e006066cb7e0327d883dae3b2d38]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 341(4)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), insert “UK” before “marketing authorisation”;
- (b) after paragraph (a), insert—

  > - (aa) a decision to grant or revoke an EU marketing authorisation;

  .
