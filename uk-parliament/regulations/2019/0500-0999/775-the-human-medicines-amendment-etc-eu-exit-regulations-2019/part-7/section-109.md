# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 109

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 109. Insertion of regulation 124A (interpretation)[^c-d16a653c8cf9ffe1f1a9563e6bc0872f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before regulation 125 (traditional herbal medicinal products), insert—

> **124A. Interpretation of this Part**
>
> In this Part, “**relevant list**” means—
>
> - (a) the list referred to in Article 16f(1) of the 2001 Directive, as that list may be amended from time to time; or
> - (b) if the licensing authority publishes a list under regulation 126A(1), that list.

.
