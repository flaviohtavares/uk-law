# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 120

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Amendment of regulation 136 (revocation by licensing authority: further provisions)[^c-8040a842b41de2bb212171fde65be0c7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulation 136 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)(a), for “the list referred to in” to the end substitute—[^c-fa6ca91e338197a08254b253e3ed9050]

> - (i) the list referred to in Article 16f(1) of the 2001 Directive, in the case of a THR(NI) or THR(UK);
> - (ii) the list established under regulation 126A where the application is for a THR(GB); and

(3) Omit paragraph (3).
