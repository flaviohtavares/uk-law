# The Human Medicines (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 98

> Text as at 2021-08-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 98. Amendment of regulation 102 (regulation-making power to amend regulation 102(4) to (6))[^key-6c9ed0fed5981bd6ad637803855bf8e0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 102 (application of Part 6), at the end insert—

> (7)[^key-ea7652c633321f3899a6de40b8f34f80] The Secretary of State may make regulations in respect of Great Britain to amend paragraphs (4) to (6).
>
> (8) The Secretary of State may only exercise the power in paragraph (7) if the Secretary of State considers that it is necessary to do so because of new scientific evidence.

.
