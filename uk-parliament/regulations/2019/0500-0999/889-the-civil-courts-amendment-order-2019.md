# The Civil Courts (Amendment) Order 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 889

Senior Courts Of England And Wales

County Court, England And Wales

The Civil Courts (Amendment) Order 2019

Made

23rd April 2019

Laid before Parliament

29th April 2019

Coming into force

30th April 2019

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 99(1) of the Senior Courts Act 1981[^f00001], having consulted the Lord Chief Justice in accordance with that section, makes the following Order.

## 1. Commencement and citation

This Order may be cited as the Civil Courts (Amendment) Order 2019 and shall come into force on 30th April 2019.

## 2. Amendments to the 2014 Order

(1) The Civil Courts Order 2014[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In the Schedule—

- (a) omit the following places specified in the first column—
  - (i) Chichester; and
  - (ii) Colchester;
- (b) omit the County Court hearing centres named in the second column corresponding to those places listed in sub-paragraph (a); and
- (c) in the second column, omit the references to the following County Court hearing centres—
  - (i) Chippenham and Trowbridge; and
  - (ii) Banbury.

Lucy Frazer

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Justice

Ministry of Justice

23rd April 2019

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Civil Courts Order 2014 ([S.I. 2014/819](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/819)), which specifies the locations and names of district registries of the High Court in England and Wales.

The amendments—

- (a) remove references to places served by district registries which have either closed (Chichester) or will close on the date that this Order comes into force (Colchester); and
- (b) remove references to County Court hearing centres which have now also closed.
