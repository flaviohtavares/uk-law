# The Medical Devices (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2022-05-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-3ecdeaf16fcf3cd671036eef0c93ca4e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “**the Act**” means the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018;
- “**the 2002 Regulations**” means the Medical Devices Regulations 2002 [^c23697091].
