# The Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 41

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41.[^key-6b0d79d584becf6fad747f31f9781776]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 4, for point (b), substitute—

> - (b) the plastic input must originate from plastic materials and articles that have been manufactured in accordance with legislation on plastic food contact materials and articles, in particular, Council Directive [78/142/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1978/142) of 30 January 1978 on the approximation of laws of the Member States relating to materials and articles which contain vinyl chloride monomer and are intended to come into contact with foodstuffs and Directive [2002/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/72) relating to plastic materials and articles intended to come into contact with foodstuffs;

.
