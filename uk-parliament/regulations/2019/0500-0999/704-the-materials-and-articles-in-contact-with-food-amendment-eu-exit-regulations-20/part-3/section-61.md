# The Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 61

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61.[^c-e2e27266e45a41642ff972a22324ad34]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 9—

- (a) in paragraph 1, for “the relevant Community and national provisions”, substitute “ retained EU law and any other enactment ”;
- (b) in paragraph 2, omit “Community”;
- (c) in paragraph 3—
  - (i) for “a specific Community or national measure on”, substitute, “retained EU law or any other enactment relating to”;
  - (ii) for “Community provisions”, substitute “ retained EU law ”.
