# The Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 14

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14.[^c-8f3344fcf7c441c839450a2dfea04da1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 4—

- (a) in paragraph 1, for “the Community provisions applicable to food, such as the provisions of Directive [89/107/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1989/107) on food additives and related implementing measures, or, if no Community provisions exist, with the national provisions applicable to food”, substitute “ retained EU law and any other enactment applicable to food ”;
- (b) in paragraph 2, for “the relevant Community provisions applicable to food shall be authorised and used in accordance with the relevant Community provisions applicable to food, and shall comply with the provisions of this Regulation and its implementing measures”, substitute “ retained EU law and any other enactment applicable to food ”.
