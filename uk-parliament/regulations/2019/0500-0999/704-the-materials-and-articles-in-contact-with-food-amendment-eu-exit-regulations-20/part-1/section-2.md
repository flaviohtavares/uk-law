# The Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-ef32436c42cc4fa9192546ffd12ec553]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “**2012 Regulations**” means the Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (England) Regulations 2012 [^c23678661];
- “**Regulation 1935/2004**” means Regulation [(EC) No. 1935/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/1935) of the European Parliament and of the Council on materials and articles intended to come into contact with food and repealing Directives [80/590/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1980/590) and [89/109/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1989/109);
- “**Regulation 1895/2005**” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1895/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/1895) on the restriction of use of certain epoxy derivatives in materials and articles intended to come into contact with food;
- “**Regulation 2023/2006**” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2023/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/2023) on good manufacturing practice for materials and articles intended to come into contact with food;
- “**Regulation 282/2008**” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 282/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/282) on recycled plastic materials and articles intended to come into contact with foods and amending Regulation [(EC) No 2023/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/2023);
- “**Regulation 450/2009**” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 450/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/450) on active and intelligent materials and articles intended to come into contact with food;
- “**Regulation 10/2011**” means Commission Regulation (EU) No. 10/2011 on plastic materials and articles intended to come into contact with food;
- “**Regulation 2018/213**” means Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/213 on the use of bisphenol A in varnishes and coatings intended to come into contact with food and amending Regulation (EU) No 10/2011 as regards the use of that substance in plastic food contact materials.
