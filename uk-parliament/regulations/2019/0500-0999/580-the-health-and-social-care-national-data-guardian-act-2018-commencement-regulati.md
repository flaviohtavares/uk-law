# The Health and Social Care (National Data Guardian) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 580 (C. 14)

National Health Service, England

The Health and Social Care (National Data Guardian) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2019

Made

14th March 2019

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 5(1) of the Health and Social Care (National Data Guardian) Act 2018[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Social Care (National Data Guardian) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2019.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1st April 2019

The Health and Social Care (National Data Guardian) Act 2018 comes into force on 1st April 2019.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care.

Blackwood

Parliamentary-Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health and Social Care

14th March 2019

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force, on 1st April 2019, all of the provisions of the Health and Social Care (National Data Guardian) Act 2018.
