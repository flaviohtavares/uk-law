# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 116

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116.[^key-77877fec2514e35365fc375505d11c46]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 8L (interpretation) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)—

- (a) in the definition of “person from a third country”, for “third country”, in both places, substitute “ country outside the United Kingdom ”;
- (b) in the definition of “review period”, omit “or (7)”;
- (c) omit the definition of “third country”.

(3) In paragraph (3), for “European Economic Area state” substitute “ country outside the United Kingdom ”.

(4) In paragraph (4), for “European Economic Area state” substitute “ country outside the United Kingdom ”.
