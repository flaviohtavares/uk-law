# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 134

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 134.[^key-38b5ee014316dab4b7450aef279f5c44]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 8A (duty on the Authority to have regard to the need for consultation and co-operation with other authorities) is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-194820120d596a696f033f0023ea9a19] At the end of the heading insert “: electricity”.

(3) In paragraph (1)(b), omit “the Gas Directive,” and “the Gas Regulation”.

(4) In paragraph (2), for “Third Package” substitute “Electricity Directive”.

(5) In paragraph (3), in the definition of “region”—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), omit “in relation to electricity,”;
- (b) omit sub-paragraph (b) and the “and” preceding it.
