# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 121

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 121.[^key-b5be0517ab34b4508f98efe31812ee96]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 27 (general functions) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3B)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (b), for “and (r) to” substitute “ , (r) and ”;
- (b) omit sub-paragraph (d).

(3) After paragraph (3B) add—

> (3C) For the purposes of paragraph (3B)(c), the reference to Article 41(3)(a) is to be read as if the reference in that provision to Article 41 were a reference to Article 41 as implemented in Northern Ireland immediately before IP completion day[^key-e530380828e1715ded4de58c9da291be].
