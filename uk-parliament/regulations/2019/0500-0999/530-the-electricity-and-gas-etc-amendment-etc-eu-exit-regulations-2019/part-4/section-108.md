# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 108

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108.[^c-738482311497c6e44145775813e65aa1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 8D (report where applicant connected with a country outside the European Economic Area) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “European Economic Area” substitute “ United Kingdom ”.

(3) In paragraph (1), for “third country”, in both places, substitute “ country outside the United Kingdom ”.

(4) In paragraph (2), omit “and the European Economic Area”.

(5) In paragraph (3)(b), omit “third”.
