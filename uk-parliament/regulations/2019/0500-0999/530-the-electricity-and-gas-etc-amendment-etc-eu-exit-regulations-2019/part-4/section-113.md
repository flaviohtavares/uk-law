# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 113

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 113.[^c-0f1119e6bc573d8b30009e4431b8695d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 8I (monitoring and review of certification) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3)—

- (a) for “3rd March 2013” substitute “ IP completion day[^c-2a5132d3d600a59e7042b9264fc646c2] ”;
- (b) for “third country” substitute “ country outside the United Kingdom ”;
- (c) omit “and the European Commission”.

(3) In paragraph (4), omit “final”.

(4) Omit paragraphs (6) and (7).

(5) In paragraph (8), omit “or (6)”.

(6) In paragraph (12A), for “paragraphs (5) or (7)” substitute “ paragraph (5) ”.
