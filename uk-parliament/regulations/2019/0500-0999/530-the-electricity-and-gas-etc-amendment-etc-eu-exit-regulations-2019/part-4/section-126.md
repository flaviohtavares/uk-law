# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 126

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126.[^key-d297a097d7d898c411220abddeed97ea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 45 (directions restricting the use of certain information), in paragraph (1A), for the words from “information” to the end substitute “ commercially sensitive information obtained in the course of carrying out its business, except in accordance with a legal duty to disclose information. ”.
