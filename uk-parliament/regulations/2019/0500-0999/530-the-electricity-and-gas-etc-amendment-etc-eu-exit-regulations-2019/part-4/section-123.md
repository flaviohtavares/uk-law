# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 123

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123.[^key-d27546f0eb1ddd7208a680c5320ceb43]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 38A (requirements for major pipelines etc.), in paragraph (5)—

- (a) for “the criteria” substitute “ objective and non-discriminatory criteria ”;
- (b) omit “for the purposes of and in accordance with Article 4(2) of the Directive”.
