# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 143

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 143.[^c-14c4727e6484c589fcf1e6168d2b483c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 56 (power to amend Part VII), in paragraph (1)—

- (a)[^c-0239f5038cbe062821512de7cc9746e6] at the end of sub-paragraph (b)(i) omit “or”;
- (ab) after sub-paragraph (b)(i) insert—

  > - (ia) any retained EU obligation; or

  ;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b)(ii), for “member State” substitute “ country ”.
