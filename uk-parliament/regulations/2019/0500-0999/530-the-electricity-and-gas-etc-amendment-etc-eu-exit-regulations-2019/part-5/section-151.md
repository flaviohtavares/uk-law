# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 151

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 151. The Gas Regulation[^c-be5a82403bd580ab6e641af60da95baf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Gas Regulation is amended as set out in Schedule 2.

(2) In this regulation and in Schedule 2, “**the Gas Regulation**” means Regulation [(EC) 715/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/715) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 on conditions for access to the natural gas transmission networks and repealing Regulation [(EC) No 1775/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/1775) [^c23650601].
