# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 152

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 152. Revocations and savings[^c-ddfa0e7f0a6b25c9ac384f66ec8cdbbd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The EU Regulations and Decisions specified in Schedule 3 are revoked.

(2) The revocation of the TEN-E Regulation by paragraph (1) and Schedule 3 is subject to the savings provisions in Schedule 4.

(3) In this regulation and in Schedules 3 and 4, “**the TEN-E Regulation**” means Regulation (EU) No 347/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 April 2013 on guidelines for trans-European energy infrastructure and repealing Decision No [1364/2006/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/1364/2006) and amending Regulations [(EC) No 713/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/713), [(EC) No 714/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/714) and [(EC) No 715/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/715).
