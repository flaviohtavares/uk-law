## SCHEDULE 3 — EU Regulations and Decisions: Revocations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 152(1)

**1.**[^c-6f5fc19d8eb116741171c992ab802dc7] Council Regulation [(EEC) No 625/83](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1983/625) of 15 March 1983 establishing specific measures of Community interest relating to energy strategy.

**2.**[^c-5499fe5c75a7a27dfb0e2b20683ee85b] Council Regulation [(EEC) No 1890/84](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1984/1890) of 26 June 1984 introducing special measures of Community interest relating to energy strategy.

**3.**[^c-f04556b7f0a3e0a6eb42afd334a30313] Regulation [(EC) No 663/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/663) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 establishing a programme to aid economic recovery by granting Community financial assistance to projects in the field of energy.

**4.**[^c-e8161ce6f9effe49b92bbfd8c32e0493] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**5.**[^c-87cf7220ea7b09ccadec328f5ac9f4b8] Regulation (EU) No 1233/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 December 2010 amending Regulation [(EC) 663/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/663) establishing a programme to aid economic financial recovery by granting Community financial assistance to projects in the field of energy.

**6.**[^c-87b75c3cd3096b6b87eb4ce630a3a9ed] Commission Decision of 11 August 2011 establishing the composition and the operational provisions of the Gas Coordination Group and repealing Commission Decision [2006/791/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2006/791).

**7.**[^c-cd326a7db1ec767f5c399a1bd6ebea8a] Commission Decision of 15 November 2012 setting up the Electricity Coordination Group.

**8.**[^c-11e84fc5389a03511faad0d97b6f3fca] The TEN-E Regulation.

**9.**[^c-2069267c4e7489262ad734a431047ae1] Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1391/2013 of 14 October 2013 amending Regulation (EU) 347/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council on guidelines for trans-European energy infrastructure as regards the Union list of projects of common interest.

**10.**[^c-30857f47f92316b51c0c5f63992936c8] Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/89 of 18 November 2015 amending Regulation (EU) No 347/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the Union list of projects of common interest.

**11.**[^c-9e1443058ebd08384834269b2b9cf954] Commission Decision of 9 March 2016 setting up a Commission expert group on electricity interconnection targets.

**12.**[^c-ff5806e2efaff60ee4d052f63a463f31] Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/540 of 23 November 2017 amending Regulation (EU) No 347/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the Union list of projects of common interest.
