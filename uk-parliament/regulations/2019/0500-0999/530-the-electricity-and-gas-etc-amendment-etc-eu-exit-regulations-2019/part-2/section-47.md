# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 47

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47.[^c-0301e4737d9aacf370a7ae52abf8e1f0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 10E (grounds for certification) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) in paragraph (a)—
  - (i) omit “preliminary”;
  - (ii) for “an applicant should be certified” substitute “ [^c-06f809cae3fc7c973330aa835695419c]... to certify an applicant ”;
- (b) omit paragraph (b).

(3) In subsection (2), omit “decide that the applicant should be certified, or”.

(4) In subsection (8)—

- (a) omit paragraph (a);
- (b) in paragraph (b)—
  - (i) omit “must not decide that the applicant should be certified, and”;
  - (ii) omit “or any other EEA state”;
- (c) omit paragraph (c).

(5) After subsection (8) insert—

> (9) For the purposes of subsection (4), Article 9(9) of the Electricity Directive is to be treated as if the comparison with the provisions of Chapter 5 of the Electricity Directive were a comparison with the provisions of Chapter 5 as they applied in relation to a member State immediately before IP completion day[^c-a556d949ad8cd797213666920f63c94a].
>
> (10) For the purposes of subsection (5), the references to Article 13(1) and (2) of the Electricity Directive are to be treated as references to those provisions with the following modifications—
>
> - (a) Article 13(1) is to be read as if “Such designation shall be subject to approval by the Commission” were omitted;
> - (b) Article 13(2)(b) is to be read as if the reference to Article 12 of the Electricity Directive were a reference to Article 12 with the omission of point (h);
> - (c) Article 13(2)(e) is to be read as if—
>   - (i) for “Regulation [(EC) No 714/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/714)” there were substituted “ the Electricity Regulation ”, and
>   - (ii) the words from “including” to the end were omitted.

.
