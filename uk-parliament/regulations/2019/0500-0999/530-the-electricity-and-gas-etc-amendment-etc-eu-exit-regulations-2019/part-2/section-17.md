# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 17

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17.[^c-f3ea1db9e202c3bce145c5a5bc89031d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 8J (designation for the purposes of EU gas legislation) is amended as set out in paragraphs (2) to (5).

(2) In the heading, for “EU gas legislation” substitute “ the Gas Regulation ”.

(3) In subsection (2)—

- (a) omit “fourth,”;
- (b) for the words from “Article” to “operators)” substitute “ the Gas Regulation ”.

(4) In subsection (3), for “that Article” substitute “ the Gas Regulation ”.

(5) In subsection (4)—

- (a) after paragraph (a) insert “ and ”;
- (b) omit paragraph (c) and the “and” preceding it.

(6) A person who is designated as a gas transmission system operator under section 8J of the Gas Act 1986 for the purposes of Article 10(2) of the Gas Directive immediately before IP completion day[^c-a9f7af9d44c8e5aaf8d079d216549d67] is to be treated, on and after IP completion day, as a person who is designated as a gas transmission system operator under section 8J for the purposes of the Gas Regulation, if and for so long as the person designated or (as the case may be) the person who nominated the person designated continues on and afterIP completion day to hold a licence under section 7 or 7A of the Gas Act 1986 and to be certified under section 8F of that Act.

(7) In paragraph (6)—

- “**the Gas Directive**” means Directive [2009/73/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/73) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 concerning common rules for the internal market in natural gas and repealing Directive [2003/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2003/55) [^c23650371] , as amended by Directive (EU) 2019/692 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 April 2019[^c-a82f4f4f0a4545a4919f9881a1ac9eb8];
- “**the Gas Regulation**” means Regulation [(EC) No 715/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/715) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 on conditions for access to the natural gas transmission networks and repealing Regulation [(EC) No 1775/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/1775).
