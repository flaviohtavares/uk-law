# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 29

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29.[^key-77a09c2712c9ec549a1c7f78d0d06c58]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 19DZA (duty to allow others to use LNG import or export facilities: exemptions in relation to take-or-pay commitments) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (5)(b)(ii), for the words from “, within” to “that paragraph;” substitute “ which applies to the owner; ”.

(3) After subsection (5) insert—

> (5A) In subsection (5)(b)(ii) “**public service obligation**” has the same meaning as in section 19D.

.

(4) Omit subsection (7).

(5) Omit subsection (9).
