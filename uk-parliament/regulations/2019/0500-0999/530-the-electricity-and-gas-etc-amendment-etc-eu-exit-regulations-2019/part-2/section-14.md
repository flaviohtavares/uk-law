# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 14

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14.[^key-716870e3e72bf6903d3ea84105dcdadb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Notwithstanding the amendments of sections 8D and 8E of the Gas Act 1986 made by regulations 12 and 13, if an application under section 8D is made before IP completion day[^key-bef4680d13e52d4180f27761f245994f] and the applicant is a person from an EEA state or a person controlled by a person from an EEA state—

- (a) the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority is not required to notify the Secretary of State of the application under section 8D(3), and
- (b) the Secretary of State is not required to prepare a report under section 8E in relation to the application.
