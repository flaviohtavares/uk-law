> Text as at 2025-02-27.

## 77. Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007[^c-637d94ed0e5a8ee233d0832bd59a6553]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007 [^c23650441], in section 19A (guidance for energy consumers), in subsection (2), after “Gas Directive” insert “ which is in force immediately before IP completion day[^c-85f1f63640209eeefd42506da4541127]”.
