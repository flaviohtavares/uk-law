# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 15

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15.[^key-fffe9b95caa53722acd6b0a1356fca04]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 8F (certification) is amended as set out in paragraphs (2) to (5).

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) omit “preliminary”;
- (b) for “it should” substitute “ to ”.

(3) In subsection (4)—

- (a) omit “preliminary”;
- (b) after paragraph (a) insert “ and ”;
- (c) omit paragraph (c) and the “and” before it.

(4) Omit subsections (5) to (8).

(5) After subsection (8) insert—

> (9) The Authority must publish its decision to certify or to refuse to certify the applicant, together with the reasons for it, in such manner as it considers appropriate.

.

(6) Where, immediately before IP completion day[^key-693c3ee4b3f601b2dac32818ed55efd9], the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority has made a preliminary decision under section 8F of the Gas Act 1986 on an application for certification, but not a final decision under Article 3 of Regulation [(EC) No 715/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/715) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 on conditions for access to the natural gas transmission networks and repealing Regulation [(EC) No 1775/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/1775), the Authority's preliminary decision is to be treated on and after IP completion day as if it were a final decision under Article 3 made immediately before IP completion day.

(7) If a preliminary decision of the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority is treated as a final decision to certify, or to refuse to certify, an applicant because of paragraph (6), the Authority must publish, in such manner as it considers appropriate, a document setting out that decision and the reasons for it.
