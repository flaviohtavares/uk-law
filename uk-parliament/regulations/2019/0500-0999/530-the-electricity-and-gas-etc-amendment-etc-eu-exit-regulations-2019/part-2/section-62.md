# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 62

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62.[^key-589bcd4d17226a263b2a5f2c6b9a5b73]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 6A (provisions imposing obligations enforceable as relevant requirements) is amended as set out in paragraphs (1A) to (4A)[^key-2edcc67624e5885d69dea9bd75bb82f6].

(1A)[^key-1600295bb2580fb657e3b6ca48e755e6] In paragraph 1 (relevant provisions in relation to all licence holders)—

- (a) omit paragraph (ca);
- (b) omit paragraph (f).

(2)[^key-0b3ff5c56f2e46bcb760b900010ff9af] In paragraph 4 (persons required to be certified as to independence)—

- (a) in paragraph (ca), omit—
  - (i) “the CACM Regulation,”;
  - (ii) “or the FCA Regulation”;
- (b) in paragraph (d)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (iv), for “16(1) to (4)” substitute “16(1), (2), (4)”;
  - (ii) omit sub-paragraph (v);
  - (iii) omit sub-paragraphs (viii) to (xi);
  - (iv) omit sub-paragraph (xiii);
- (c) omit paragraph (g).

(3) In paragraph 9 (electricity undertakings which are relevant producers or suppliers)—

- (a) omit paragraph (e);
- (b) omit paragraph (f).

(4) Omit paragraph 9B (nominated electricity market operators).

(4A)[^key-58f15593005cb92b95315dbcf264ac9e] In paragraph 10 (interpretation), omit the definition of “the FCA Regulation”.

(5) For the purposes of sections 27A to 28 of the Electricity Act 1989, a reference in Schedule 6A to the Electricity Act 1989 to a provision of assimilated direct[^key-b606d46a6bacce1ce879f37b7e0a5ead] legislation is to be treated on and after IP completion day[^key-b2a4f77f9c62bc35934658c5d80f0472] as including a reference to that provision as it had effect in EU law at any time before IP completion day.
