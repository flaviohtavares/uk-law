# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 12

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12.[^key-6f2952a051dd3690136b8eb9776ae82a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 8D (application for certification), in subsection (3)—

- (a) omit “on or after 3 March 2013”;
- (b) for “third country”, in both places, substitute “ country outside the United Kingdom ”;
- (c) in paragraph (a), omit “and the European Commission”;
- (d) in paragraph (b)—
  - (i) omit “to the Secretary of State”;
  - (ii) omit “or any other EEA state”.
