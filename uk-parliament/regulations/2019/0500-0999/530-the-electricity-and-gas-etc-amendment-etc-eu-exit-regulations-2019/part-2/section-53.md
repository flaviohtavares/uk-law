# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 53

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53.[^key-24f4667d7692978e8ed641def2e729a3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 10O (interpretation of sections 10A to 10O), in subsection (1)—

- (a) in the definition of “certified”, for the words from “taken” to “certified) by” substitute “ certified by ”;
- (b) in the definition of “person from a third country”, for “third country”, in both places, substitute “ country outside the United Kingdom ”;
- (c) omit the definition of “third country”.
