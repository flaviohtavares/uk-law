# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 44

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44.[^c-bd8a99425b5f6b0cb90cb5e766ec49a0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) — Section 10C (report where applicant connected with a country outside the European Economic Area) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “European Economic Area” substitute “ United Kingdom ”.

(3) In subsection (1), for “third country”, in both places, substitute “ country outside the United Kingdom ”.

(4) In subsection (2), omit “or any other EEA state”.

(5) In subsection (5)(b), omit “third”.
