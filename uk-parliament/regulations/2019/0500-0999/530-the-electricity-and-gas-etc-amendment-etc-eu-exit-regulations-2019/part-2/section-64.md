# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 64

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64.[^c-b482ada2c96f6d17e2d857dc187d8209]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 3A (designation of Gas and Electricity Markets Authority as regulatory authority for Great Britain) is amended as follows.

(2) For the heading substitute “ Exercise of designated regulatory functions etc ”.

(3) Omit subsection (1).

(4) In subsection (2)(a), for “functions as designated regulatory authority for Great Britain” substitute “ designated regulatory functions ”.

(5) Omit subsection (3).
