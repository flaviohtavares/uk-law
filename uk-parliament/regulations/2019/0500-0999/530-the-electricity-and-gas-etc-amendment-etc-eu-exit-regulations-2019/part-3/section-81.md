# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 81

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. The Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets) Regulations 2011[^key-4d91dcd4a5aa1681e860d75d334c4c8c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets) Regulations 2011 [^c23650491], regulation 51 (review of regulations) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraph (2).

(3) In paragraph (6), omit—

- (a) the definition of “2010 Amending Decision”;
- (b) the definition of “the 2012 Amending Decision”;
- (c) the definition of “the 2013 Amending Regulation”;
- (ca)[^key-fbf238509cc04bf5a298123bf8814c36] the definition of “ACER Regulation”;
- (d) the definition of “CACM Regulation”;
- (da)[^key-4ff961354ef5f1d9ffbeeca7754745b3] the definition of “Electricity Regulation”;
- (e) the definition of “ITC Regulation”;
- (f) the definition of “Third Package”.
