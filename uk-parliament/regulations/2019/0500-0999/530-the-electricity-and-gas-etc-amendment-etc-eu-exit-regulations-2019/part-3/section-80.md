# The Electricity and Gas etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 80

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. The Gas (Applications for Licences and Extensions and Restrictions of Licences) Regulations 2010[^c-59fddd1ef2b9361c0453878936e547f1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Gas (Applications for Licences and Extensions and Restrictions of Licences) Regulations 2010 [^c23650481], in regulation 3 (interpretation), in paragraph (1), in the definition of “point of single contact”, for the words from “an” to the end substitute “ the electronic assistance facility referred to in regulation 38 of the Provision of Services Regulations 2009; ”.
