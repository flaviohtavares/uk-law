# The REACH etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 8

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 8 — Revocation of retained direct EU legislation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 10

**1.**[^key-c69c899f928ddb4138f5a7475bdfb527] The following retained direct EU legislation is revoked—

- (a) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 506/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/506) imposing testing and information requirements on the importers or manufacturers of certain priority substances in accordance with Council Regulation [(EEC) No 793/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1993/793) on the evaluation and control of the risks of existing substances;
- (b) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1238/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/1238) on laying down rules on the qualifications of the members of the Board of Appeal of the European Chemicals Agency;
- (c) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 465/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/465) imposing, pursuant to Council Regulation [(EEC) No 793/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1993/793), testing and information requirements on importers and manufacturers of certain substances that may be persistent, bioaccumulating and toxic and are listed in the European Inventory of Existing Commercial Chemical Substances;
- (d) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 466/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/466) imposing testing and information requirements on the importers and manufacturers of certain priority substances in accordance with Council Regulation [(EEC) No 793/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1993/793) on the evaluation and control of the risks of existing substances;
- (e) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 771/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/771) laying down the rules of organisation and procedure of the Board of Appeal of the European Chemicals Agency;
- (f) Commission Decision 2010/226/EU on the re-examination of the restriction concerning short-chain chlorinated paraffins (SCCPs) listed in Annex XVII to Regulation [(EC) No 1907/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1907) of the European Parliament and of the Council.
