## 116. Revocation of Implementing Regulations[^c-dff2191be673fad050816dba58d1b286]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following Regulations are revoked—

- (a) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/832 of 16 May 2017 conferring protection under Article 99 of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 on the name Bürgstadter Berg (PDO) [^c23706011];
