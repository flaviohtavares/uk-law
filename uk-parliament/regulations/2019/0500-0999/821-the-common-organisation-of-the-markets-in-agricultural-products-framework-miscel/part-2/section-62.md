# The Common Organisation of the Markets in Agricultural Products Framework (Miscellaneous Amendments, etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 62

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62.[^key-b92256c77cadeeb4d6d2d15074c2eab8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 199(3), for point (a) substitute—

> - (a) the products have been exported from the United Kingdom in accordance with the applicable export provisions, as construed in accordance with section 35(2) of the Taxation (Cross-border) Trade Act 2018 [^c23705931];

.
