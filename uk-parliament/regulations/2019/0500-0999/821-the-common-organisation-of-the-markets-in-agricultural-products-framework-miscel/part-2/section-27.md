# The Common Organisation of the Markets in Agricultural Products Framework (Miscellaneous Amendments, etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 27

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27.[^c-b3ee539a334446d47229547f4a2db03c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 80—

- (a)[^c-e9edbe404c199dc3d1fe04e49e2f8a84] in paragraph 1, for “the Union”, in both places it occurs, substitute “Great Britain”;
- (b) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) in the first subparagraph—
    - (aa)[^c-871909bf7d0416b1974ef6bfab752169] for “the Union”, in the first place it occurs, substitute “Great Britain”;
    - (bb) omit point (a);
    - (cc) in point (b), omit “national”;
  - (ii)[^c-531359edad85dc3f75a11294ee08b414] in the second subparagraph, for “Member States” substitute “and provided the relevant authorities in Wales and Scotland agree, the Secretary of State”;
- (c) in paragraph 3, for “Commission” substitute “ Secretary of State ”.
