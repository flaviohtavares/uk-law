# The Common Organisation of the Markets in Agricultural Products Framework (Miscellaneous Amendments, etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 80

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80.[^key-cf5e12fd25bd71c9a4849339cef2679b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Annex 4—

- (a) in the heading, omit “Union”;
- (b) in Section A—
  - (i) in the heading, omit “Union”;
  - (ii) in part 3 (Classification), in paragraph 2, in the second subparagraph, for “Member States are authorised to” substitute “ The appropriate[^key-38418d6ff147b7acca692af0b17f84f8] authority may ”;
  - (iii) in part 5 (Classification and identification)—
    - (aa) in the first paragraph, omit “Union”;
    - (bb) in the second paragraph, for “Member States” substitute “ the appropriate[^key-0985eb43e5cc153940f5e99f5d95f154] authority ”;
- (c) in Section B—
  - (i) in the heading, omit “Union”;
  - (ii) in part 4 (Lean-meat content), in paragraph 1, for “Commission” substitute “ appropriate[^key-4fe64284bf8a49f73e0e506b952cbc16] authority ”;
  - (iii) in part 5 (Identification of carcasses)—
    - (aa) for “by the Commission” substitute “ in retained EU law ”;
    - (bb) omit “Union”;
- (d) in Section C—
  - (i) in the heading, omit “Union”;
  - (ii)[^key-16d300012dcc8b8389a5dab899f423bc] in part 4 (Presentation), in the second paragraph—
    - (aa) for “Member States are authorised to” substitute “ The appropriate authority may ”;
    - (bb) for “refeence” substitute “ reference ”;
  - (iii) in part 5 (Identification of carcasses), omit “Union”.
