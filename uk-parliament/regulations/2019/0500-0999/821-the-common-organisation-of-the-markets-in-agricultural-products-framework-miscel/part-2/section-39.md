# The Common Organisation of the Markets in Agricultural Products Framework (Miscellaneous Amendments, etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 39

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39.[^c-ee0dbe83a9912c6d91e5b91ef3337647]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 102—

- (a) in paragraph 1, in point (a), for “Commission” substitute “ Secretary of State ”;
- (b) in paragraph 2—
  - (i)[^c-e3eb62a3df688ec64c0117353ff0d433] for “the territory of the Union” substitute “Great Britain”;
  - (ii) for the words from “Directive” to the end substitute “ the Trade Marks Act 1994 [^c23705861] ”.
