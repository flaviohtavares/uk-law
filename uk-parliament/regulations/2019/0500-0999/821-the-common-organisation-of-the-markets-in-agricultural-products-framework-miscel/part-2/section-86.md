## 86. Amendment of Regulation (EU) No 1370/2013[^c-33eb8eff9e8a6784031ef726b54e6dc5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Regulation (EU) No 1370/2013 determining measures on fixing certain aids and refunds related to the common organisation of the markets in agricultural products is amended in accordance with regulations 87 to 93.
