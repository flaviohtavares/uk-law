# The Common Organisation of the Markets in Agricultural Products Framework (Miscellaneous Amendments, etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 41

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41.[^key-eec4b8dbebd9880205bcc298c1a7e902]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 104—

- (a) for “Commission” substitute “ Secretary of State ”;
- (b) for “third” substitute “ other ”;
- (c)[^key-39b4110d3d5cef4259fd0da9be54e105] for “the Union” in the first place it occurs, substitute “Great Britain”;
- (d) for “the Union” in the second place it occurs, substitute “the United Kingdom”.
