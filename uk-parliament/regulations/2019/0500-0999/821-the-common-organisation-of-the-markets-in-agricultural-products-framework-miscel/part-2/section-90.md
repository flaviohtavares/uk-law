## 90.[^key-04d0949e6e9799badc9fff1640344993]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

- (a) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) omit “Union”;
  - (ii) for the words from “Member” to the end, substitute “ the aid granted by the relevant authority under Article 23 of that Regulation ”;
- (b) in paragraph 2, in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) omit “Union”;
  - (ii) for the words from “Member” to the end, substitute “ the aid granted by the relevant authority under Article 23 ”;
- (c) in paragraph 3, omit “Union”;
- (d) omit paragraphs 4 to 6.
