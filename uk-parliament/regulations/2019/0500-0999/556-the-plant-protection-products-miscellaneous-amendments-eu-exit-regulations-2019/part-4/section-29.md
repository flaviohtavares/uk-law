# The Plant Protection Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 29

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Revocation of retained EU legislation and saving[^c-9030d690941d8cdb9dbb07bdb40cd749]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The retained EU legislation in Schedule 2 is revoked.

(2) Despite paragraph (1), a grace period contained within an EU instrument listed in Schedule 2 which expires after IP completion day[^c-2c122d26ed6d70be6db90e26d14521dc] continues to have effect, and is treated as if it had been set by each competent authority in relation to its constituent territory in accordance with Article 21(6)(b) of Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107).
