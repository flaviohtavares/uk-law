# The Plant Protection Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 18

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Commission Regulation (EU) No 546/2011 implementing Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards uniform principles for evaluation and authorisation of plant protection products[^c-716cbb0939693906e8e44af19ba76967]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) No 546/2011 implementing Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards uniform principles for evaluation and authorisation of plant protection products is amended as follows.

(2) Before Article 1, insert—

> **Article A1 — Interpretation**
>
> **1.**
>
> Paragraph 2 applies where Commission Regulation (EU) No 283/2013 setting out the data requirements for active substances applies in relation to an application by virtue of—
>
> - (a) Article 1 of that Regulation, or
> - (b) Article A1(2) of Commission Regulation (EU) No 544/2011 implementing Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the data requirements for active substances.
>
> **2.**
>
> Where this paragraph applies, a reference in this Regulation to Commission Regulation (EU) No 544/2011, or a specified part of Commission Regulation (EU) No 544/2011, is to be read in relation to that application as a reference to Commission Regulation (EU) No 283/2013, or the equivalent part of Commission Regulation (EU) No 283/2013 (as the case may be).
>
> **3.**
>
> Paragraph 4 applies where Commission Regulation (EU) No 284/2013 setting out the data requirements for plant protection products applies in relation to an application by virtue of—
>
> - (a) Article 1 of that Regulation, or
> - (b) Article A1(2) of Commission Regulation (EU) No 545/2011 implementing Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the data requirements for plant protection products.
>
> **4.**
>
> Where this paragraph applies, a reference in this Regulation to Commission Regulation (EU) No 545/2011, or a specified part of Commission Regulation (EU) No 545/2011, is to be read in relation to the application as a reference to Commission Regulation (EU) No 284/2013, or the equivalent part of Commission Regulation (EU) No 284/2013 (as the case may be).
