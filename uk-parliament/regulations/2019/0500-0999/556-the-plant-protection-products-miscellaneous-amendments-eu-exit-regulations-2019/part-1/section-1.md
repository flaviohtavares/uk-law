# The Plant Protection Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 1

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation[^key-6b6086ca503c4def0f44054700632775][^key-2cf7eddd22f5efc527be5e2b5fbaeecb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Protection Products (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, and come into force on exit day.

(1A)[^key-0b658defcbf40d9fb771f2006e9aeb10] Regulation 28 and Schedule 1 extend to Great Britain.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “**Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107)**” means Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market;
- “competent authority” and “constituent territory” have the meanings given in Article 3A of Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107) (as inserted by regulation 3(5)).
