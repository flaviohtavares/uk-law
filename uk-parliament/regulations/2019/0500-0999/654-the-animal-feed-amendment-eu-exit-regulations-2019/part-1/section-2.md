# The Animal Feed (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-531d67738097dfeb6de1bd276dba33fc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “**Regulation 1831/2003**” means Regulation [(EC) No. 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2003/1831) of the European Parliament and of the Council on additives for use in animal nutrition;
- “**Regulation 183/2005**” means Regulation [(EC) No. 183/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/183) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down requirements for feed hygiene;
- “**Regulation 378/2005**” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 378/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/378) on detailed rules for the implementation of Regulation [(EC) No. 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2003/1831) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the duties and tasks of the Community Reference Laboratory concerning applications for authorisations of feed additives;
- “**Regulation 429/2008**” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 429/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/429) on detailed rules for the implementation of Regulation [(EC) No. 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2003/1831) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the preparation and the presentation of applications and the assessment and the authorisation of feed additives;
- “**Regulation 152/2009**” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 152/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/152) laying down the methods of sampling and analysis for the official control of feed;
- “**Regulation 767/2009**” means Regulation [(EC) No. 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/767) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the placing on the market and use of feed, amending European Parliament and Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2003/1831) and repealing Council Directive [79/373/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1979/373), Commission Directive [80/511/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1980/511), Council Directives [82/471/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1982/471), [83/228/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1983/228), [93/74/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1993/74), [93/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1993/113) and [96/25/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1996/25) and Commission Decision [2004/217/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/2004/217);
- “**Regulation 892/2010**” means Commission Regulation (EU) No. 892/2010 on the status of certain products with regard to feed additives within the scope of Regulation [(EC) No. 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2003/1831) of the European Parliament and of the Council;
- “**Regulation 619/2011**” means Commission Regulation (EU) No. 619/2011 laying down the methods of sampling and analysis for the official control of feed as regards presence of genetically modified material for which an authorisation procedure is pending or the authorisation of which has expired;
- “**Regulation 68/2013**” means Commission Regulation (EU) No. 68/2013 on the Catalogue of feed materials;
- “**Regulation 2015/786**” means Commission Regulation (EU) 2015/786 defining acceptability criteria for detoxification processes applied to products intended for animal feed as provided for in Directive [2002/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/32) of the European Parliament and of the Council;
- “**Regulation 2017/625**” means Regulation (EU) 2017/625 of the European Parliament and of the Council on official controls and other official activities performed to ensure the application of food and feed law, rules on animal health and welfare, plant health and plant protection products, amending Regulations [(EC) No 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2001/999), [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396), [(EC) No 1069/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1069), [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107), (EU) No 1151/2012, (EU) No 652/2014, (EU) 2016/429 and (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament and of the Council, Council Regulations [(EC) No 1/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/1) and [(EC) No 1099/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1099) and Council Directives [98/58/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1998/58), [1999/74/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1999/74), [2007/43/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2007/43), [2008/119/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/119) and [2008/120/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/120), and repealing Regulations [(EC) No 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/854) and [(EC) No 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/882) of the European Parliament and of the Council, Council Directives [89/608/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1989/608), [89/662/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1989/662), [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1990/425), [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1991/496), [96/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1996/23), [96/93/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1996/93) and [97/78/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1997/78) and Council Decision [92/438/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eud/1992/438) (Official Controls Regulation).
- ”Regulation 2020/354” means Commission Regulation (EU) 2020/354 establishing a list of intended uses of feed intended for particular nutritional purposes and repealing Directive[^key-1d115086b061b31568dd2c624f3af53d] [2008/38/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/38).
