# The Animal Feed (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 45

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45.[^c-c9301f8f37c6287c50a1b525bf6a88eb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For Article 17, substitute—

> **Exemption from on-site visits**
>
> **1.**
>
> Competent authorities are exempted from the obligation to carry out on site visits, as provided for in Article 13, of feed businesses which act solely as traders, without holding the products on their premises.
>
> **2.**
>
> Such feed businesses must submit to the competent authority a declaration, in a form decided upon by the competent authority, to the effect that the feeds placed on the market by them comply with the conditions of this Regulation.

.
