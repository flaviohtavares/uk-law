# The Animal Feed (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 99

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99.[^c-212c27cff36ef9961b9c3d490ee32ba6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For Article 10, substitute—

> **List of intended uses of feed intended for particular nutritional purposes**
>
> The appropriate authority may prescribe how the list of intended uses set out in Directive [2008/38/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/38) may be updated, to include—
>
> - (a) how an intended use is to be assessed;
> - (b) how the conditions of use should be determined;
> - (c) the procedure by which—
>   - (i) an application for adding a new use may be made;
>   - (ii) an amendment to a use or one or more of its conditions may be authorised; and
>   - (iii) a use may be removed.

.
