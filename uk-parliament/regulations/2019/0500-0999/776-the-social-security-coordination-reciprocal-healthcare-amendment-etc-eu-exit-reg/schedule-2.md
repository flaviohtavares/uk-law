## SCHEDULE 2 — Regulation [(EC) No 987/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/987)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 13

**1.**[^key-b47234150f751316e0570857342553be] Regulation [(EC) No 987/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/987) is amended as follows.

**2.**[^key-33af8920c5785609ff52148829e3ad93] Omit Article 22.

**3.**[^key-226dd935f0493a9f59cb07c4debd6e9e] In Article 23—

- (a) for “Member State”, in both places it occurs, substitute “ State ”;
- (b) omit “17, 19(1), 20,”.

**4.**[^key-25bc015733850cb863db5ef2618e78f8] Omit Articles 24 to 26.

**5.**[^key-9f9a0c8c4ecac2e1d21ee0bc174efe00] Omit Article 29.

**6.**[^key-f61813c6006309188b42278c31c4333b] Omit Article 31.

**7.**[^key-6315ba37dd40ce58ab9b94b16cc8a3cb] (1) Article 32 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1, for “benefits in kind or in cash” substitute “ cash benefits ”.

(3) Omit paragraph 2.

(4) Omit paragraph 3.

**8.**[^key-92daa2345cab8381ad3eef41089dc1d9] (1) Article 33 is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, omit “in kind and”.

(3) In paragraph 1, for “Articles 24 to 27” substitute “ Article 27 ”.

(4) Omit paragraph 2.

**9.**[^key-36cdca986223d9f83959d766c5c15a2b] Omit Article 35.

**10.**[^key-b0a55b4e09222d31d921e5367de4c9e8] Omit Article 41.

**11.**[^key-02c9e7408b1477d059fc18474b2161d8] In Title 4, omit Chapter 1 (reimbursement of the cost of benefits in application of Article 35 and Article 41 of Regulation [(EC) No 883/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/883)).

**12.**[^key-633aae80ebe55b9b78748215170abcf7] (1) Annex 1 [^c23695541] is amended as follows.

(2) Under the heading BELGIUM-UNITED KINGDOM, omit point (b).

(3) Under the heading DENMARK-UNITED KINGDOM, omit “benefits in kind and”.

(4) Under the heading IRELAND-UNITED KINGDOM, omit the words from “Articles 36(3)” to “1408/71) and”.

(5) Omit the heading SPAIN-UNITED KINGDOM and the text under it.

(6) Under the heading FRANCE-UNITED KINGDOM, omit point (b).

(7) Omit the heading ITALY-UNITED KINGDOM and the text under it.

(8) Omit the heading HUNGARY-UNITED KINGDOM and the text under it.

(9) Omit the heading MALTA-UNITED KINGDOM and the text under it.

(10) Omit the heading PORTUGAL-UNITED KINGDOM and the text under it.

(11) Under the heading FINLAND-UNITED KINGDOM, omit the words from “Articles 36(3)” to “in kind) and”.

(12) Under the heading SWEDEN-UNITED KINGDOM, omit the words from “Article 36(3)” to “in kind) and”.

**13.**[^key-1eba60899a4fcebfd417a6e3efc8020f] Omit Annex 3.
