# The Social Security Coordination (Reciprocal Healthcare) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 3

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. The National Health Service Act 2006[^key-76fe7e6fd6df12566b59d18e8870eb93]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the National Health Service Act 2006—

- (a) in section 183 (payment of travelling expenses) [^c23695391], omit paragraph (a)(iii) and the “or” preceding it;
- (b) in section 275 (interpretation), omit the definition of “Regulation [(EC) No. 883/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/883)”[^c23695401] so far as it relates to section 183(a)(iii).
