# The Social Security Coordination (Reciprocal Healthcare) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 4

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. The National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^key-d3fe4ed973f6fc8df5eaee80cfa99ef5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006—

- (a) in section 131 (payment of travelling expenses) [^c23695411], omit paragraph (a)(iii) and the “or” preceding it;
- (b) in section 206 (interpretation), omit the definition of “Regulation [(EC) No. 883/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/883)”[^c23695421] so far as it relates to section 131(a)(iii).
