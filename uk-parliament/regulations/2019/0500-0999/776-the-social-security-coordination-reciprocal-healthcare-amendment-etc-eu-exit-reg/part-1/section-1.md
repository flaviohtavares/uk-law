# The Social Security Coordination (Reciprocal Healthcare) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 1

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-c42b5add4987d7c1833cd4de10207cdc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security Coordination (Reciprocal Healthcare) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force on exit day [^c23695381].

(2) An amendment, repeal or revocation made by these Regulations has the same extent and application as the provision being amended, repealed or revoked.
