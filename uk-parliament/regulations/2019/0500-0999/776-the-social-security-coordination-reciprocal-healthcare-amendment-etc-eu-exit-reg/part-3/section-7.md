## 7. The National Health Service and Public Health (Functions and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2013[^c-a2600f3d11b7008e2d1435dd81d2025f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the National Health Service and Public Health (Functions and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2013 [^c23695451], omit regulations 2 to 8 (exercise of EU functions), so far as not omitted by the National Health Service (Cross-Border Healthcare and Miscellaneous Amendments etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c23695461].
