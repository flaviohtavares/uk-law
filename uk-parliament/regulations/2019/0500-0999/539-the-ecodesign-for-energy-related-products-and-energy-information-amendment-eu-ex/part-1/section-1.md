# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-72636e7318c0e0c97722fefdd77ac320][^key-92f08cfe23ace58eed12977ef9df4e02]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019.

(2) This regulation and Part 2 come into force on the 21st day after the day on which these Regulations are made.

(3) The remaining provisions of these Regulations come into force on IP completion day[^key-a7047683dfb59d9c8e710448f7c12d68].

(4)[^key-027f9572e35eedee8da79b06ca4ad73f] Regulations 3 and 5 and Schedules 1 and 3 extend to England and Wales and Scotland only.

(5) The remainder of these Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
