## 22.[^c-2a8ce44102da9c6afb6a330e7f239e8c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

- (a) in paragraph 1 for “Commission” substitute “ Authority ”;
- (b) in paragraph 2(b) after “Authority” insert “ , or provided to the European Food Safety Authority, if provided before IP completion day[^c-92da8759f0136cf8541f047c5a7b3324], ”;
- (c) in paragraph 4—
  - (i) for “Commission” substitute “ Authority ”;
  - (ii) omit “and the Member States”.
