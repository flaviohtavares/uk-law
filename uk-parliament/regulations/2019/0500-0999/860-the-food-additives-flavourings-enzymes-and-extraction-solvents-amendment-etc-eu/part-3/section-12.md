# The Food Additives, Flavourings, Enzymes and Extraction Solvents (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 12

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12.[^c-1d1c87f5a630dc51bddba505e37699aa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 3 at the end insert—

> **5.**
>
> “**Authority**” means—
>
> - (a) as regards England and Wales[^c-4fb924c94f74d2094cd73ebda1311641] , the Food Standards Agency;
> - (b) as regards Scotland, Food Standards Scotland[^c-345e0d0c9e05b6bc5dd17e9c9656d550]
>
> **6.**
>
> “prescribe”, means prescribe by regulations;
>
> **7.**
>
> “**appropriate authority**” means—
>
> - (a) in relation to England, the Secretary of State;
> - (b) in relation to Wales, the Welsh Ministers;
> - (c) in relation to Scotland, the Scottish Ministers;
> - (d)[^c-305e509e4b9329dc081295629864ac94] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> **8.**
>
> “**Regulation 1321/2013**” means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 1321/2013 establishing the Union list of authorised smoke flavouring primary products for use as such in or on foods and/or for the production of derived smoke flavourings;
>
> **9.[^c-651ef8d95e88279c6a9117c14041a7fc]**
>
