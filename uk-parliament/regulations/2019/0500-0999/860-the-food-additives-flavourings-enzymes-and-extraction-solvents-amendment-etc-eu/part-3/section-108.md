# The Food Additives, Flavourings, Enzymes and Extraction Solvents (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 108

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108.[^key-837a1deb5bfec141465737aaeb702986]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 31 in the first paragraph—

- (a) for “Community” substitute “ domestic ”;
- (b) for “Annexes to Directives [94/35/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1994/35), [94/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1994/36) and [95/2/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1995/2)” substitute “ existing lists ”;
- (c) for “shall be adopted by the Commission in accordance with the regulatory procedure with scrutiny referred to in Article 28(4)” substitute “ are to be prescribed by the appropriate authority ”.
