# The Food Additives, Flavourings, Enzymes and Extraction Solvents (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 151

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 151.[^key-b23e02692afa9ed6f8e41aa11ac040c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Annex in the Model Letter accompanying an application for Food Additives—

- (a) for “European Commission Directorate General Directorate Unit” substitute “ appropriate authority ”;
- (b) for “in the European Union” substitute “ in the United Kingdom ”;
- (c) for “EU list” substitute “ domestic list ”.
