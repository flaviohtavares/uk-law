# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Revocations[^key-44ffad2877c1978e852d09025a7a3c83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 426

[^key-44ffad2877c1978e852d09025a7a3c83]The following Regulations are revoked:

- (a) [Commission Regulation (EC) No 104/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/0104) of 22 January 2004 laying down rules on the organisation and composition of the Board of Appeal of the European Aviation Safety Agency;
- (b) [Commission Regulation (EC) No 473/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/0473) of 22 March 2006 laying down implementing rules for the Community list of air carriers which are subject to an operating ban within the Community referred to in Chapter II of Regulation [(EC) No 2111/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2111) of the European Parliament and of the Council;
- (c) [Commission Regulation (EC) No 474/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/0474) of 22 March 2006 establishing the Community list of air carriers which are subject to an operating ban within the Community referred to in Chapter II of Regulation [(EC) No 2111/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2111) of the European Parliament and of the Council;
- (d) [Commission Regulation (EC) No 768/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/0768) of 19 May 2006 implementing [Directive 2004/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/0036) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the collection and exchange of information on the safety of aircraft using Community airports and the management of the information system;
- (e) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 646/2012 of 16 July 2012 laying down detailed rules on fines and periodic penalty payments pursuant to Regulation [(EC) No 2016/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/2016) of the European Parliament and of the Council;
- (f) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 628/2013 of 28 June 2013 on working methods of the European Aviation Safety Agency for conducting standardisation inspections and for monitoring the application of the rules of Regulation [(EC) 216/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/216) of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing [Commission Regulation (EC) No 736/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/0736);
- (g) [Commission Regulation (EU) No 319/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/0319) of 27 March 2014 on the fees and charges levied by the European Aviation Safety Agency, and repealing Regulation [(EC) No 593/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/0593).
