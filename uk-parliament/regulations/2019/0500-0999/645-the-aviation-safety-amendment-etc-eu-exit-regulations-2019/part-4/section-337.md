# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 337

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 337. Amendment of Annex 8 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^c-876406f124d78a9af735ec3e94c5d9ca]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 8 (Part-DTO) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit point DTO.GEN.105.

(3) In points DTO.GEN.110 to DTO.GEN.150, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point DTO.GEN.155—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In points DTO.GEN.230 and DTO.GEN.270, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
