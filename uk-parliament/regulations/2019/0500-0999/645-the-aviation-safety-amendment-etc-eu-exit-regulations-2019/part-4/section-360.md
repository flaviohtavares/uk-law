# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 360

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 360. Amendment of Subpart AOC of Annex 3 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 965/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0965)[^key-503ec825fdf8267fd3636528fe29751f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart AOC is amended as follows.

(2) In point ORO.AOC.100, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point ORO.AOC.110—

- (a) in points (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “subject to an operating ban” substitute “included on the United Kingdom safety list”;
- (c) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in point (d)—
  - (i) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (1), for “EU” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iii) in point (3), omit “EU”;
- (e) in points (e) and (f), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point ORO.AOC.115—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “EU safety requirements” substitute “United Kingdom safety requirements”;
  - (ii) in point (2)—
    - (aa) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) for “such authority” substitute “the CAA”;
- (b) in point (c), for “subject to an operating ban” substitute “included on the United Kingdom safety list”.

(5) In point ORO.AOC.120—

- (a) in point (a), in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (ii) in in point (1), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) in point (2)(iii) —
    - (aa) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) for “that authority” substitute “the CAA”.
