# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 293

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 293. Amendment of Article 2 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-4d35da003bbb3073e7781297bc48b398]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 2 (definitions) is amended as follows.

(2) In point (4)—

- (a) for “recognised,” substitute “recognised by the CAA,”;
- (b) after “in accordance with” omit “the”;
- (c) omit the words from “, by a Member State” to the end.

(3) In point (5), for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”.

(4) In point (9)—

- (a) for “recognised,” substitute “recognised by the CAA”;
- (b) omit the words from “, by a Member State” to the end.

(5) In point (10), for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”.

(6) In point (13)—

- (a) after “in accordance with” omit “the”;
- (b) for the words from “a Member State” to “relevant JAR” substitute “the CAA”.

(7) In points (14), (15) and (18), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.
