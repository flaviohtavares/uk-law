# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 272

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 272. Amendment of Subpart A of Section B of Annex 3 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-6e1ffb1ccf2081d66fb999f6b0806f47]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section B, Subpart A (general) is amended as follows.

(2) In point 66.B.1, for the words from “competent authorities” to the end substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 66.B.10—

- (a) in the heading, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (a)—
  - (i) omit the first paragraph;
  - (ii) in the second paragraph, for “This competent authority” substitute “The CAA”;
- (c) in points (b) and (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point 66.B.20(a) and (b)(6), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) Omit point 66.B.25.

(6) In point 66.B.30, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
