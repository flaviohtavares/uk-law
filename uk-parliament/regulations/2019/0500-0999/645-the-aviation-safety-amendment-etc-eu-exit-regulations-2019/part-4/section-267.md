# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 267

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 267. Amendment of the Appendices to Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-607966636619b679116d9eb758487af4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Appendices to Annex 2 are amended as follows.

(2) In Appendix 1 for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”.

(3) In Appendix 3—

- (a) on the first page of the form—
  - (i) for “\[Member State (\*)\] A Member of the European Union (\*\*)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “\[Member State Code (\*)\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iii) for “\[Competent authority of the Member State (\*)\]” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
  - (iv) for “For the competent authority: \[Competent authority of the Member State (\*)\]” substitute “For the CAA”;
  - (v) for “EASA Form 3-145 Issue 4”[^c-8704a06aabd9856c565f49157202d940] substitute “CAA Form 3-145 Issue 1”;
  - (vi) omit the notes at “(\*)” and “(\*\*)”;
- (b) on the second page of the form—
  - (i) for “\[Member State Code (\*)\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “For the competent authority: \[Competent authority of the Member State (\*)\]” substitute “For the CAA”;
  - (iii) for “EASA Form 3-145 Issue 4”[^c-8926874e4febd61e6c3441afeecd1177] substitute “CAA Form 3-145 Issue 1”;
  - (iv) omit the note at “(\*)”.
