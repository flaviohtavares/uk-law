# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 318

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 318. Amendment of Annex 4 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-06e4aa091400f27c2fe32a58b6bd701d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 4 (Part-MED) is amended as follows.

(2) In Subpart A—

- (a) omit point MED.A.001;
- (b) in point MED.A.010—
  - (i) omit the seventh indent (definition of ‘licensing authority’);
  - (ii) in the tenth indent (definition of ‘accredited medical conclusion’) for “licensing authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) in the appropriate place, insert the following definitions—

    > - ‘AeMC’ means aeromedical centres;

    ;

    > - ‘AME’ means aero-medical examiners;

    ;

    > - ‘GMP’ means general medical practitioners;

    ;

    > - ‘OHMP’ means occupational health medical practitioners;

    ;
- (c) in point MED.A.025—
  - (i) in point (a)—
    - (aa) in point (3), for the words from “licensing authority” to “competent authority,” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) in point (4), for “licensing authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (b)—
    - (aa) in point (1), for “licensing authority” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) in point (3), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
    - (cc) in point (4), for “licensing authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) in point (c)—
    - (aa) for “licensing authority” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iv) in point (d), for “national legislation” substitute “any other enactment”;
  - (v) in point (e), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (vi) omit point (f);
- (d) in point MED.A.035, in point (a) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (e) in point MED.A.040—
  - (i) in points (b)(3) and (c)(2), omit the words from “if so permitted” to the end of the sentence;
  - (ii) in points (e) and (f), for “licensing authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (f) in point MED.A.046—
  - (i) in point (a), for “licensing authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (b), for “the licensing authority” and “that authority” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (iii) in point (c), for “licensing authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (g) in point MED.A.050(a), in both places the words occur, for “licensing authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In Subpart B, in points MED.B.001, MED.B.010 to MED.B.040, MED.B.050 to MED.B.080, and MED.B.090, in each place the words occur, for “licensing authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In Subpart D—

- (a) omit point MED.D.001(f);
- (b) in points MED.D.005, MED.D.010, and MED.D.015(d), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point MED.D.020(a), for the words from “competent authority” to “place of business” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in points MED.D.025 and MED.D.030, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (e) in point MED.D.035—
  - (i) in point (a), omit “exercise their activity in a Member State where GMPs”;
  - (ii) in point (b), for “the national law of the Member State of their competent authority” substitute “a relevant enactment”;
  - (iii) in point (c), for “national law of the Member State of their competent authority” substitute “a relevant enactment”;
  - (iv) in point (d), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (f) in point MED.D.040—
  - (i) at the start, omit the words from “In Member States” to “to OHMPs,”;
  - (ii) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
