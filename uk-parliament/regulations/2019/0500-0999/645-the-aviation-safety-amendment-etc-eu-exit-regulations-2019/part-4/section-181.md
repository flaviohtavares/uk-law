> Text as at 2025-02-27.

## 181. [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748) (initial airworthiness)[^c-1395a6c3849894de2b323e6168518a8b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748) of 3 August 2012 laying down implementing rules for the airworthiness and environmental certification of aircraft and related products, parts and appliances, as well as for the certification of design and production organisations, is amended in accordance with regulations 182 to 235.
