# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 323

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 323. Amendment of Subpart CC of Annex 6 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-eadd52383b5e1c3b55a7545f05e2f1cf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart CC is amended as follows.

(2) In point ARA.CC.100—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) for point (1) and the words to “State” immediately following it, substitute “by the CAA; and/or”;
  - (ii) in point (2), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point ARA.CC.105, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point ARA.CC.200—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) for the words from the start to “attestations, the competent authority” substitute “The CAA”;
  - (ii) in the second and third places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
