# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 396

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 396. Amendment of Subpart C of Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^c-9479d94fe7ebcda6be31a737fa98eef0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart C (oversight and enforcement) of Annex 2 is amended as follows.

(2) In point ATCO.AR.C.001—

- (a) in points (a) and (b), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit points (d) and (e).

(3) In point ATCO.AR.C.005, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point ATCO.AR.C.010—

- (a) in point (a), in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) for the words from the start to “issuing of the licence” substitute “The CAA”;
  - (ii) at the start of points (1) and (2) omit “it”;
- (c) omit point (c).
