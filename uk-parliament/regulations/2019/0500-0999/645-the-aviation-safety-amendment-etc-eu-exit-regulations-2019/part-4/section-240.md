> Text as at 2025-02-27.

## 240. Amendment of Article 5 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^key-d89ca0d9156569bfd6e89571eb5849d5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 5 (certifying staff) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 2, for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”.

(3) In paragraph 6, for the words from “the national laws” to the end substitute “other relevant enactments continue to apply”.
