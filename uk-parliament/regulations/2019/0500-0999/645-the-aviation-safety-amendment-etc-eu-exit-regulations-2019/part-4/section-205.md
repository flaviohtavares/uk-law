## 205. Amendment of Subpart M of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-f7775de7210f96cdf9e153673863f336]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart M (repairs) is amended as follows.

(2) In point 21.A.431, in point (e), in both places it occurs, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”.

(3) In point 21.A.431B, in point (a)(2), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point 21.A.432B, in each place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point 21.A.433—

- (a) in point (a)(1)—
  - (i) [^c-2db3bb449f19dadf09ad782b83a77225]... for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
  - (ii) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii)[^c-0bd5816a2e7d866f07910cfb4854c955] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-edbd0a0903be464644b8c6739f045f4b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) In point 21.A.435, [^c-4a29674f17319f82418d6bb5d0208896]... for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(7)[^c-e09a52a57b493bec28134efb4c66b546] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) In point 21.A.443, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(9) In point 21.A.445—

- (a) in point (a), in points (1) and (2), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in both places it occurs, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”.

(10) In point 21.A.447, in point (a), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(11) In point 21.A.449, in points (a) and (b), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(12) In point 21.A.451—

- (a) in the heading, for “EPA” substitute “UKPA”;
- (b) in point (a)(1)(ii), for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
- (c) in points (a)(2) and (b)(2), for “EPA” substitute “UKPA”.
