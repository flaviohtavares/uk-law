# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 284

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 284. Amendment of Annex 5a to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-b7f39130590c0735af6448941fc88708]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 5a (Part-T) is amended as follows.

(2) In the introductory part of Annex 5a—

- (a) in the list of contents—
  - (i) omit the entry for point T.1;
  - (ii) in the heading for Section B, for “competent authorities” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (iii) at the entry for point T.B.102, for “competent authority” substitute “The CAA”;
- (b) omit the entry for point T.1.

(3) In Section A (technical requirements)—

- (a) in Subpart B (continuing airworthiness), in point T.A.201(1)(a) and (f)(ii), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in Subpart E (maintenance organisation), in point (3), for “competent authority of the operator” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in Subpart G (additional requirements for continuing airworthiness management organisations approved pursuant to Annex 5c (Part-CAMO)[^c-f187d106004aecaaa88449686d6f4cb5]—
  - (i) in point T.A.711 for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point T.A.715, in point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii)[^c-7a277d232a7b8cf17c2c2d81101c6952] in point T.A. 716, in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;

(4) In Section B (additional procedures for competent authorities)—

- (a) in the heading, omit “for competent authorities”;
- (b) in Subpart A (general)—
  - (i) in point T.B.101, for “competent authorities in charge of” substitute “CAA for the purposes of”;
  - (ii) in point T.B.102—
    - (aa) in the heading, for “Competent authority” substitute “The CAA”;
    - (bb) in point (1), for the words from “A Member State” to “This competent authority” substitute “The CAA”;
    - (cc) in point (4), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) in point T.B.104(3), omit “another Member State, the Agency or”;
- (c) in Subpart B (accountability)—
  - (i) in point T.B.201—
    - (aa) in point (1), for “competent authority as specified in T1” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) in points (2) and (3), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point T.B.202(3) and (4), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in Subpart G (additional requirements for continuing airworthiness management organisations approved pursuant to Annex 5c (Part-CAMO)[^c-6f6c29cb1752a83605642cbdbf2b1039], in points T.B.702 , T.B.704[^c-f5b1bbcbf310f5ea0d2186ca4f3c8116] and T.B.705, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
