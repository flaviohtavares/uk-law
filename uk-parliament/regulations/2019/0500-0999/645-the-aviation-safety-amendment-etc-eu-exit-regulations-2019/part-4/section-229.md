# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 229

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 229. Amendment of Appendix 5 to Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-9b245bf89fd86eaa2f093763cd162baf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Appendix 5 is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “EASA Form 24” substitute “CAA Form 24”.

(3) For “Competent Authority logo” substitute “CAA logo”.

(4) In the form—

- (a) for “\[Member State of registry\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) for “\[competent authority of the Member State\]” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
- (c) in box 6, for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) at the end, for “EASA Form 24 Issue 2” substitute “CAA Form 24 Issue 1”.
