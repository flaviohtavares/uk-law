# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 195

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 195. Amendment of Subpart A of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-8e163cd20ffef4abae146b336f123648]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A (technical requirements), Subpart A (general provisions) is amended as follows.

(2) In point 21.A.3A—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “European” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) in both places it occurs, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) in the first place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (1)—
    - (aa) in the first place it occurs, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
    - (bb) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
    - (cc) in the second place it occurs, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
  - (iii) in point (2), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c)—
  - (i) in point (1)—
    - (aa) for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
    - (bb) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (2)—
    - (aa) in the first place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
    - (cc) in the second place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 21.A.3B—

- (a) in point (a), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), in both places it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c)—
  - (i) in the first place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
  - (iii) in points (1) and (2) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point 21.A.4, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”.
