# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 261

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 261. Amendment of Subpart I of Section B of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-fb0dce08e450852ebe4fea977a3db859]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section B, Subpart I (airworthiness review certificate) is amended as follows.

(2) In point M.B.901, in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point M.B.902—

- (a) in the heading, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (a)—
  - (i) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “EASA Form 15a” substitute “CAA Form 15a”;
  - (iii) in the second place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in points (b), (c) and (d), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point M.B.903, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
