# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 214

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 214. Amendment of Subpart F of Section B of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-9ddc4df3853c88aa20e7b54b7fd7f9d7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section B, Subpart F (production without production organisation approval) is amended as follows.

(2) In points 21.B.120 and 21.B.125, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 21.B.130, in point (a)—

- (a) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “EASA” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point 21.B.135—

- (a) in the first place it occurs, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (a), in both places it occurs, for “EASA” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (b)—
  - (i) for “competent authority of the Member State” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in both places it occurs, for “EASA” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) in point (5), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In points 21.B.140 and 21.B.145, in each place it occurs, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(6) In point 21.B.150—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (d)—
  - (i) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in both places it occurs, for “EASA” substitute “CAA”.
