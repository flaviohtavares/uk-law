## 237. Amendment of Article 1 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^key-247135170c2b9ea17ca2c5aa579fae46]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 1 (subject matter and scope), is amended as follows.

(2) In point (a)—

- (a) in point (i)—
  - (i) for “a Member State” substitute “the United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “an EU” substitute “a UK”;
- (b) in point (ii)—
  - (i) for “an EU” substitute “a UK”;
  - (ii) for “a Member State” substitute “the United Kingdom”.

(3) In point (b), for “a Member State” substitute “the United Kingdom”.
