# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 330

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 330. Amendment of Appendix 2 to Annex 6 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-6ac208381d365b78543d51ac0bf40a09]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Appendix 2 is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, omit “EASA”.

(3) In the first paragraph, omit “in a Member State”.

(4) In the form—

- (a) at point 3, for “State of issue” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) at point 8, for “Competent authority” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority (CAA)”;
- (c) at point 9, for “Issuing body” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) for “EASA Form 142” substitute “CAA Form 142”.

(5) After the word “Instructions:”—

- (a) in point (a), for “EASA Form 142” substitute “CAA Form 142”;
- (b) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (d)—
  - (i) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “competent authority of the Member State” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) omit point (e).

(6) In Item 2—

- (a) for “the UN country code of the Member State” substitute “GBR”;
- (b) for “competent authority (e.g. BE-08-xxxx)” substitute “CAA”.

(7) In Item 3, for “The Member State where the attestation is issued” substitute “United Kingdom”.

(8) In Item 8—

- (a) for “competent authority of the Member State where the attestation is issued” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in the second place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(9) In Item 9—

- (a) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “the term ‘competent authority’ and” substitute “its”;
- (c) in the third place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(10) In item 10, for “issuing body” substitute “CAA”.

(11) In Item 12 for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
