# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 265

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 265. Amendment of Section A of Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-77874fe369ac126bf68deb3f29886583]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A (technical requirements) is amended as follows.

(2) In point 145.A.15—

- (a) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “such authority” substitute “the CAA”.

(3) In point 145.A.30—

- (a) in points (b)(2) and (e), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (f), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (j)—
  - (i) in points (1) and (2), for “Community territory” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) in point (5), in the last paragraph, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d)[^c-d592ce09816d340e8f4010eccffc0088] in point (k)(6), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point 145.A.35(h), for “competent authorities, the Agency and the Member State” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point 145.A.40(a)(i), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(6) In point 145.A.42(a)(i), for “an EASA Form 1” substitute “a CAA Form 1”.

(7) In point 145.A.45(b)(4), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(8) In point 145.A.50(d)—

- (a) for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”;
- (b) for “an EASA Form 1” substitute “a CAA Form 1”.

(9) In points 145.A.55, 145.A.60, 145.A.65, 145.A.70, 145.A.85, 145.A.90 and 145.A.95, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
