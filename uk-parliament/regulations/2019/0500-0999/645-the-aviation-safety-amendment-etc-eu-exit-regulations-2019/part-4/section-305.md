# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 305

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 305. Amendment of Article 11b of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-38a436ad16723f88425aa504948e64a0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 11b (oversight capabilities) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraphs 1 to 3.

(3) In paragraph 4, for “Member States shall ensure that competent authority” substitute “The CAA must ensure that its”.

(4) In paragraph 5, for the words from the start to “and/or oversight tasks”, substitute “The CAA”.

(5) In paragraph 6, for the words from “legal provisions of” to the end, substitute “the Air Navigation Order 2016”.
