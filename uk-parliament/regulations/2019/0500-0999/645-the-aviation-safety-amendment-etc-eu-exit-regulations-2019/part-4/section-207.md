# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 207

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 207. Amendment of Subpart P of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-8395a024080d0f6c0417004d58a00876]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart P (permit to fly) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit point 21.A.705.

(3) In point 21.A.707, in point (a)—

- (a) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “that authority” substitute “the CAA”.

(4) In point 21.A.709—

- (a) in point (a)(1), in both places it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (a)(2), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In 21.A.710—

- (a) in point (a)(1), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c)—
  - (i) for “Agency, the competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “Agency or the competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(6) In point 21.A.711—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “EASA” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in points (b), (c) and (d), for “EASA” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in points (f) and (g), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(7) In point 21.A.715, for the words from “one or more” to the end substitute “English”.

(8) In point 21.A.719, in point (b), omit the words from “provided the aircraft remains” to the end.

(9) In points 21.A.721 and 21.A.723(c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(10) In point 21.A.729—

- (a) in point (a), for “Agency and competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “Agency or the competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
