# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 354

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 354. Amendment of Subpart GEN of Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 965/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0965)[^c-864b96a8d5b748e1ca794f6da489a54d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart GEN is amended as follows.

(2) In point ARO.GEN.115, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point ARO.GEN.120—

- (a) in point (a), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (c), in both places where the words occur,[^c-9613c919873f227a7af6efa667994f6b] for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (d)—
  - (i) in both places it occurs, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) at the end of point (1), omit “and”;
  - (iii) omit points (2) and (3);
- (d) in point (e), for the words from “it shall” to the end substitute “the CAA must make them available to all organisations and persons under its oversight”.

(4) Omit point ARO.GEN.125.

(5) In point ARO.GEN.135—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit point (b);
- (c) in point (c)—
  - (i) omit “and (b)”;
  - (ii) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in point (d), omit the words from “The competent authority” to the end.

(6) In point ARO.GEN.200—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) in the first place it occurs, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (1), for “that competent authority” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (iii) in points (4) and (5), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) omit points (c) and (d).

(7) In point ARO.GEN.205—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “Member States” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (ii) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(8) In point ARO.GEN.210—

- (a) in points (a) and (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit point (c).

(9) In point ARO.GEN.220—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) omit point (7);
  - (iii) in point (9)—
    - (aa) omit “and notification to the Agency”;
    - (bb) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(10) In point ARO.GEN.300—

- (a) in points (a) and (b), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit points (d) and (e);
- (c) in point (f), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(11) In point ARO.GEN.305, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(12) In point ARO.GEN.310, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(13) In point ARO.GEN.330, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(14) In point ARO.GEN.345, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(15) In point ARO.GEN.350—

- (a) in point (a), for the words from “competent authority” to “ARO.GEN.300(a)” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in points (b), (c) and (d), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) omit point (e).

(16) In point ARO.GEN.355—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) for the words from “competent authority responsible” to “ARO.GEN.300(a)” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in the second place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) for “to (d)” substitute “to (c)”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority that identified the non-compliance” substitute “CAA”.

(17) In point ARO.GEN.360, for “competent authority that identified the non-compliance” substitute “CAA”.
