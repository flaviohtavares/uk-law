# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 294

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 294. Amendment of Article 4 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^c-7351dbf251eefb12e093cfaed081d288]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 4 (existing national pilots’ licences) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraphs 1 to 6.

(3)[^c-8c1d96d1ed44856fa047e1e8448e2279] In paragraph 7—

- (a) at the start, for “A Member State” substitute “ The CAA ”;
- (b) in point (a), for “Member State” substitute “ CAA ”;
- (c) in point (b)—
  - (i) in point (i), for “national territory of the authorising Member State” substitute “ United Kingdom ”;
  - (ii) in point (ii), for “authorising Member State” substitute “ United Kingdom ”;
- (d) in point (c), for “Member State” substitute “ CAA ”;
- (e) omit point (d);
- (f) in point (e), for “Member States” substitute “ CAA ”.

(4) In paragraph 8—

- (a) for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
- (b) in points (a) and (b), for “Member State” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c), for “Member State’s national territory” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (d) in point (d), for “Member State” substitute “CAA”;
- (e) omit point (e);
- (f) in point (f), for “Member State” substitute “CAA”;
- (g) omit point (g).

(5) Omit paragraph 9.
