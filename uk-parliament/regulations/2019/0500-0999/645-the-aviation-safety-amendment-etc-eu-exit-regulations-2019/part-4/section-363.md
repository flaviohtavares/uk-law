> Text as at 2025-02-27.

## 363. Amendment of Subpart MLR, Subpart FC, Subpart CC and Subpart TC of Annex 3 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 965/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0965)[^c-19ffa2e02905f9acf75484031bf5043f]
