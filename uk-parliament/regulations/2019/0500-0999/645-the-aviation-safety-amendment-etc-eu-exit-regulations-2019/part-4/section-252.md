# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 252

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 252. Amendment of Subpart G of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^key-46f323d0683e801bd15b61dcf1027a00]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart G (continuing airworthiness management organisation) is amended as follows.

(2) In points M.A.702 and M.A.703, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point M.A.704—

- (a) in point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (c), for the words from “competent authority” to the end substitute “CAA”.

(4) In points M.A.706, M.A.707(b) and M.A.708(b)(2), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point M.A.710—

- (a) in point (a)(10), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (e), in both places the words occur, for “EASA Form” substitute “CAA Form”;
- (c) in point (f), for “Member State of Registry of that aircraft” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in point (h), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(6) In point M.A.711—

- (a) in point (a)(4), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) omit the words “registered in one of the Member States”;
  - (ii) in point (2), for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”.

(7) In points M.A.712(f), M.A.713, M.A.715 and M.A.716, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
