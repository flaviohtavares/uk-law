# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 253

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 253. Amendment of Subpart H of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-57b8b8f8231a2e90cb1f54c49034487d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart H (certificate of release to service - CRS) is amended as follows.

(2) In point point M.A.801(c)(3)[^c-dbb56ca18947d0863a878cc562645578], for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point M.A.802(b), for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”.

(4) In point M.A.803(a)(1), for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”.
