# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 398

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 398. Amendment of Subpart E of Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^c-4b868669aa0ad4172536126a914de3f3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart E (training organisations and course approvals) of Annex 2 is amended as follows.

(2) In point ATCO.AR.E.001—

- (a) in points (a) and (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (c)—
  - (i) for “without prior competent authority’s approval” substitute “without the CAA’s prior approval”;
  - (ii) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point ATCO.AR.E.005—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit point (b).

(4) In point ATCO.AR.E.010—

- (a) in point (a), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) for “competent authority’s” substitute “CAA’s”;
  - (ii) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c), in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point ATCO.AR.E.015, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
