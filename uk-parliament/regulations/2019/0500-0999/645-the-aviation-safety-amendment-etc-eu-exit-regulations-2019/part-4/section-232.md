# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 232

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 232. Amendment of Appendix 8 to Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-7584a9e31e4a05ff8b792d87f7ae278b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Appendix 8 is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “EASA Form 52” substitute “CAA Form 52”.

(3) In the form—

- (a) in Block 2, for “\[Member State\] A Member State of the European Union” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) at the end, for “EASA Form 52 Issue 2” substitute “CAA Form 52 Issue 1”;
- (c) omit footnotes (1) and (2).

(4) In the “Instructions for the use of Aircraft Statement of Conformity EASA Form 52”—

- (a) in the heading, for “EASA Form 52” substitute “CAA Form 52”;
- (b) in paragraph 1.2—
  - (i) for “(EASA Form 52)” substitute “(CAA Form 52)”;
  - (ii) for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in paragraph 2.1, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in paragraph 2.3—
  - (i) at the end of the first sentence insert “in English”;
  - (ii) omit the second sentence;
- (e) in paragraph 3.2, for “to the competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “to the CAA”;
- (f) in paragraph 3.3, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (g) omit the instructions for Block 2;
- (h) in the instructions for Block 7, for “competent authority of the Member State” substitute “CAA”;
- (i) in the instructions for Block 21, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
