# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 199

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 199. Amendment of Subpart F of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-ef408157e8a97200cec56e4eac386b6a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart F (production without production organisation approval) is amended as follows.

(2) In points 21.A.124, 21.A.125A, 21.A.125B, 21.A.125C and 21.A.126, in each place it occurs, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 21.A.129—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (f)(2)—
  - (i) for “Agency and the competent authority of the Member State” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “established by the Agency” substitute “established by the CAA”;
  - (iii) omit the words from “or accepted by” to the end;
- (c) In point 21.A.130—
  - (i) in point (a), in both places it occurs, for “EASA” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in points (c) and (d), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
