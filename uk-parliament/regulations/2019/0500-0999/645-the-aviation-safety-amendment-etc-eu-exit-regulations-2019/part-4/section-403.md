# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 403

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 403. Amendment of Subpart A of Annex 4 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^key-4640471c12eea458b3ee0cb8a323dc14]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart A (general requirements) of Annex 4 (Part-ATCO.MED) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit point ATCO.MED.A.001.

(3) In point ATCO.MED.A.010(f), for the words from “competent authority” to “this Part” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point ATCO.MED.A.025—

- (a) in point (c), for “national legislation” substitute “any other enactment”;
- (b) in point (d), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point ATCO.MED.A.035(a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
