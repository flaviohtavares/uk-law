# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 316

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 316. Amendment of Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-138b7f18fa6708175c3aacc00427df7c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 2 (conditions for the conversion of existing national licences and ratings for aeroplanes and helicopters) is amended as follows.

(2) In section A (aeroplanes)—

- (a) in paragraph 1 (pilot licences)—
  - (i) in the first paragraph, for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (d), in the table, in both places the words occur, for “Member State of licence issue” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) in paragraph 2 (instructor certificates), for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
- (c) in paragraph 3 (SFI certificate), in both places the words occur (including in the table), for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
- (d) in paragraph 4 (STI certificate)—
  - (i) in the first paragraph—
    - (aa) for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
    - (bb) omit “of that State”;
  - (ii) in the table, for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”.

(3) In section B (helicopters)—

- (a) in paragraph 1 (pilot licences)—
  - (i) in the first paragraph, for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (d), in the table, in both places the words occur, for “Member State of licence issue” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) in paragraph 2 (instructor certificates), for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
- (c) in paragraph 3 (SFI certificate), in both places the words occur (including in the table), for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
- (d) in paragraph 4 (STI certificate)—
  - (i) in the first paragraph—
    - (aa) for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
    - (bb) omit “of that State”;
  - (ii) in the table, for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”.
