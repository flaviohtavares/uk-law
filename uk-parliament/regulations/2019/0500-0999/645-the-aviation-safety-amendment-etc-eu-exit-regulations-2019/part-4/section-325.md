# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 325

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 325. Amendment of Subpart FSTD of Annex 6 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-a79d396da4b5c189d0f3c0b1f900d328]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart FSTD is amended as follows.

(2) In point ARA.FSTD.100—

- (a) in points (a) and (b), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (c)—
  - (i) in point (1), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) omit point (2).

(3) In points ARA.FSTD.110 to ARA.FSTD.140, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
