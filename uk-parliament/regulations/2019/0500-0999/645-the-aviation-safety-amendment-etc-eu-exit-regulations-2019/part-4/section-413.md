# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 413

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 413. Amendment of Article 7 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 139/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/0139)[^key-b2d67be2a3b092c24028121d7a6a6faf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 7 (deviations from certification specifications) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Competent Authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In paragraphs 2 and 3, in each place the words occur, for “Competent Authority” substitute “CAA”.
