# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 395

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 395. Amendment of Subpart B of Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^c-b0986b1ec05e3c2e5917e80265514d23]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart B (management) of Annex 2 is amended as follows.

(2) In point ATCO.AR.B.001—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) in the first place it occurs, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (1), for “that competent authority” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (iii) in points (4) and (5), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) omit points (c) and (d).

(3) In point ATCO.AR.B.005, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point ATCO.AR.B.010—

- (a) in points (a) and (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit point (c).

(5) In point ATCO.AR.B.015—

- (a) in point (a), for “Competent authorities” substitute “The CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) omit point (7);
  - (iii) in point (12)—
    - (aa) omit “and notification to the Agency”;
    - (bb) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
