# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 370

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 370. Amendment of Annex 8 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 965/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0965)[^key-15d77248cbea56af847550fa1a346f51]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 8 (Part-SPO) is amended as follows.

(2) In point SPO.GEN.005(c)—

- (a) in point (1), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (2), for “a Member State” substitute “the United Kingdom”.

(3) In Subpart A (general requirements)—

- (a) omit point SPO.GEN.100;
- (b) in point SPO.GEN.101, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in points SPO.GEN.107, SPO.GEN.140, SPO.GEN.145 and SPO.GEN.150, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In Subpart B (operational procedures), in points SPO.OP.110 and SPO.OP.195, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In Subpart D (instruments, data and equipment), in points SPO.IDE.A.105 and SPO.IDE.H.105, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(6) In Subpart E (specific requirements), in point SPO.SPEC.HESLO.110, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
