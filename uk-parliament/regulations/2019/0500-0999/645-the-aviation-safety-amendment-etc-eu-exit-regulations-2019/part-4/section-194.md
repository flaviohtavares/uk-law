# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 194

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 194. Amendment of introductory section to Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-d4721f2d89d5cbc8c5e2809aa42cb32b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The list of contents is amended as follows—

- (a) omit the entry for point 21.1;
- (b) in Section A—
  - (i) at the entries for points 21.A.109 and 21.A.118A, for “EPA” substitute “UKPA”;
  - (ii) at the entries for points 21.A.179 and 21.A.209, omit “within Member States”;
  - (iii) at the entry for point 21.A.451, for “EPA” substitute “UKPA”;
  - (iv) at the entry for Subpart O, for “European” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (v) at the entries for points 21.A.604, 21.A.606, 21.A.607 and 21.A.609, in each place it occurs, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
  - (vi) omit the entry for point 21.A.705;
  - (vii) at the entry for point 21.A.807, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
- (c) in Section B—
  - (i) in the heading, for “competent authorities” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (ii) at the entries for points 21.B.20 and 21.B.25, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) at the entry for Subpart O, for “European” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (d) in the list of Appendices, in each place the words occur, for “EASA” substitute “CAA”.

(2) Omit the entry for point 21.1.
