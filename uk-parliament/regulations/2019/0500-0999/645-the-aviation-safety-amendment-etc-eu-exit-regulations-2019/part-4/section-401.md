# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 401

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 401. Amendment of Subpart B of Annex 3 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^c-085c6a3ae696352cf3eed4e01556da69]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart B (requirements for air traffic controller training organisations) of Annex 3 is amended as follows.

(2) In point ATCO.OR.B.001, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point ATCO.OR.B.005—

- (a) in point (a), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in points (b) and (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point ATCO.OR.B 015—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “their competent authority” substitute “the CAA”;
- (c) in points (c) to (f), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In points ATCO.OR.B.020, ATCO.OR.B.025, ATCO.OR.B.030, ATCO.OR.B.035 and ATCO.OR.B.040, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
