# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 215

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 215. Amendment of Subpart G of Section B of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-0e6c9a21342cc9d955260552e7900cc7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section B, Subpart G (production organisation approval) is amended as follows.

(2) In points 21.B.220 and 21.B.225, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 21.B.230—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “EASA” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “EASA Form 55” substitute “CAA Form 55”.

(4) In points 21.B.235, 21.B.240, 21.B.245 and 21.B.260, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
