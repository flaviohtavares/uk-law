# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 331

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 331. Amendment of Appendix 3 to Annex 6 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-ab6f9a8a87ad65a047f08ba13bb0f25b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Appendix 3, the form is amended as follows.

(2) On the first page—

- (a) for “European Union (\*)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) in each place the words occur, for “Competent Authority” and “\[Competent Authority\]” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
- (c) omit the note at “(\*)”;
- (d) for “EASA Form 143 Issue 2”[^key-1b00467eedf6ca2d4dc026b14b6f8b10] substitute “CAA Form 143 Issue 1”[^key-ca0dce256bcccab30065308f9d3ba618].

(3) On the second page—

- (a) for “\[Competent Authority\]” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit “For the Member State / EASA”;
- (c) for “EASA Form 143 Issue 2”[^key-84f74b0c8e3135d3bf2d080b5ab9df3f] substitute “CAA Form 143 Issue 1”[^key-845cc4da9530731850495c331efcf57e].
