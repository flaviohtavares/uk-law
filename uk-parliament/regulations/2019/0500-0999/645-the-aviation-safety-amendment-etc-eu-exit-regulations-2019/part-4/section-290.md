# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 290

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 290. Amendment of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/64](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1964/2015)0[^key-6ee51a97c697d1e3fb84c65e0d0df263]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 1 (Part-26) is amended as follows.

(2) In the list of contents, omit the entry for point 26.10 (competent authority).

(3) In Subpart A (general provisions)—

- (a) omit point 26.10;
- (b) in point 26.20, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point 26.30, in each place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.
