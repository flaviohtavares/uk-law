# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 210

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 210. Amendment of Subpart A of Section B of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-1eb78eff16941f857dec1f6cef84ed6d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section B, Subpart A (general provisions) is amended as follows.

(2) In point 21.B.5—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority [^c-ca4dbe740c2d0a8d420881064bf5c8ce]...” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) omit “Member States”.

(3) Omit point 21.B.20.

(4) In point 21.B.25—

- (a) in the heading, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit point (a);
- (c) in point (b)(2)—
  - (i) for “competent authority of the Member State” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) omit the words from “, including” to the end.

(5) In point 21.B.30—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority of the Member State” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit point (b).

(6) In point 21.B.35—

- (a) omit point (a);
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority of the Member State” substitute “CAA”.

(7) In point 21.B.40—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority of the Member State” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit point (b).

(8) Omit point 21.B.45.

(9) In point 21.B.55—

- (a) for “competent authority of the Member State” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for the words from “the respective” to the end substitute “any relevant enactment”.

(10) Omit point 21.B.60.
