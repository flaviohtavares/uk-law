# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 228

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 228. Amendment of Appendix 4 to Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-d49ac9336683bfa37290f8aa4058d706]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Appendix 4 is amended as follows—

- (a) in the first box, for the words from “Member State of the Competent Authority” to “issued by EASA” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) for “in all Member States” substitute “in the United Kingdom”;
- (c) for “non-Member” substitute “other”;
- (d) at the end, for “EASA Form 20b” substitute “CAA Form 20b”.
