# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 266

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 266. Amendment of Section B of Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^key-167f7eeabb58511e4bd8b64b584a8f8b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section B (procedure for competent authorities) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “competent authorities” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 145.B.01, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point 145.B.10—

- (a) in the heading, for “Competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (1), for the words from the start to “This competent authority” substitute “The CAA”;
- (c) in point (4), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) Omit point 145.B.15.

(6) In point 145.B.20, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(7) In point 145.B.25—

- (a) in points (1) and (2), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (3), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(8) In points 145.B.30, 145.B.35, 145.B.40, 145.B.45, 145.B.50, 145.B.55 and 145.B.60, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
