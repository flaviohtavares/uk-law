# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 204

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 204. Amendment of Subpart K of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-0e21f1e47ef6cec5fcd0e22af398aa73]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart K (parts and appliances) is amended as follows.

(2) In point 21.A.303, in point (b), for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”.

(3) In point 21.A.305—

- (a) omit “Union”;
- (b) in the first place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
- (d) in the second place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point 21.A.307, in point (a), for “EASA” substitute “CAA”.
