# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 200

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 200. Amendment of Subpart G of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-5c2bb1724b720715c94b15094c188437]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart G (production organisation approval) is amended as follows.

(2) In points 21.A.134, 21.A.135, 21.A.143, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 21.A.145—

- (a) in point (b)(1), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (c)(1), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In points 21.A.147, 21.A.153, 21.A.157, 21.A.158 and 21.A.159, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point 21.A.163—

- (a) in points (b), (c) and (d), for “EASA” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (e), for “its competent authority” substitute “the CAA”.

(6) In point 21.A.165—

- (a) in point (c)—
  - (i) in point (1), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in points (2) and (4), for “EASA” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (f)(2)—
  - (i) for “Agency and the competent authority of the Member State” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “established by the Agency” substitute “established by the CAA”;
  - (iii) omit the words from “or accepted by” to the end;
- (c) in point (h), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
