# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 321

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 321. Amendment of Subpart GEN of Annex 6 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^c-c036c71bd2f33267a369e21a20ad4127]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart GEN is amended as follows.

(2) In point ARA.GEN.115, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point ARA.GEN.120—

- (a) in point (a), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (d)—
  - (i) in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) at the end of point (1) omit “; and”;
  - (iii) omit points (2) and (3);
- (d) in point (e)—
  - (i) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) at the end of point (1), omit “; and”;
  - (iii) omit point (2);
  - (iv) omit the paragraph commencing “The competent authority shall provide”.

(4) Omit point ARA.GEN.125.

(5) In point ARA.GEN.135—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit point (b);
- (c) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in point (d), omit the words from “The competent authority” to the end.

(6) In point ARA.GEN.200—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (1), for “that competent authority” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (iii) in points (4) and (5), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) omit points (c) and (d).

(7) In point ARA.GEN.205—

- (a) in point (a), for “Member States” substitute “the CAA”;
- (b) in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(8) In point ARA.GEN.210—

- (a) in points (a) and (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit point (c).

(9) In point ARA.GEN.220—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) in the first place it occurs, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) omit point (7);
  - (iii) in point (8)—
    - (aa) omit “and notification to the Agency”;
    - (bb) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(10) In point ARA.GEN.300—

- (a) in points (a) and (b), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit points (d) and (e);
- (c) in point (f), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(11) In point ARA.GEN.305(a)—

- (a) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “ARO.RAMP” substitute “Subpart RAMP of Annex II to the Regulation on Air Operations”.

(12) In points ARA.GEN.305(b) to ARA.GEN.330, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(13) In point ARA.GEN.350—

- (a) in points (a) to (da), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit point (e).

(14) In point ARA.GEN.355—

- (a) in points (a) to (c), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit point (d);
- (c) in point (e), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
