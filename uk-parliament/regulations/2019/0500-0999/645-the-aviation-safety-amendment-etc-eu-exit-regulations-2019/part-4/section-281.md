# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 281

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 281. Amendment of Section A of Annex 4 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^key-f72a66e4bc063040496ed9f51ed4e192]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A (technical requirements) is amended as follows.

(2) In Subpart A (general), in point 147.A.15(a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In Subpart B (organisational requirements)—

- (a) in points 147.A.100, 147.A.105, and 147.A.130 to 147.A.140, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point 147.A.145(a)(v)(2), for the words from “either” to “competent authority” substitute “the CAA”;
- (c) from point 147.A.150 to 147.A.155, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in point 147.A.160—
  - (i) in point (a)(2), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (c)—
    - (aa) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) for “this authority” substitute “the CAA”.
