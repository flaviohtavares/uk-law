# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 276

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 276. Amendment of Subpart E of Section B of Annex 3 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-61cf0abb24c21e70ab8c91bf3485121f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section B, Subpart E (examination credits) is amended as follows.

(2) In point 66.B.400, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 66.B.405—

- (a) omit point (b);
- (b) in point (d), in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point 66.B.410, in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
