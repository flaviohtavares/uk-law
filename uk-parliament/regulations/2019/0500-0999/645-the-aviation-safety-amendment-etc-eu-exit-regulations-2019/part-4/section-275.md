# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 275

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 275. Amendment of Subpart D of Section B of Annex 3 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^key-137d68089cb3c5731f40896e1a9b377c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Section B, Subpart D (conversion of certifying staff qualifications), point 66.B.300 is amended as follows—

- (a) omit point (a);
- (b) in points (b), (c) and (d), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
