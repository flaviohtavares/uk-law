# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 382

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 382. Amendment of Article 4 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^c-c41a77113c7107f0c5bb1819c253cc17]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 4 (definitions)—

- (a) in points (2), (5) and (11), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (29), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
