# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 314

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 314. Amendment of Subpart K of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-c85b9bb504cae9d59ffbf3543aa59c20]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart K (examiners) is amended as follows.

(2) In point FCL.1000—

- (a) in point (b)(1)—
  - (i) for “Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (c)—
  - (i) in the introductory phrase, for “Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) in point (1)—
    - (aa) for “Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”;
    - (bb) for “competent authority of the Member State” substitute “CAA”;
    - (cc) in point (iii), for the words from “competent authority” to “European” substitute “CAA an adequate level of knowledge of United Kingdom”;
  - (iii) in point (2)(i), for “Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”.

(3) In point FCL.1015—

- (a) in point (a), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), omit points (4) and (5);
- (c) omit point (c).

(4) In point FCL.1020—

- (a) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “competent authority responsible for the examiner’s certificate” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point FCL.1025(b)—

- (a) in point (2), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (3)—
  - (i) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “competent authority responsible for the examiner’s certificate” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (4), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(6) In point FCL.1030—

- (a) in point (b)—
  - (i) in point (2), for “competent authority responsible for the applicant’s licence” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (3)—
    - (aa) for the words from “competent authority responsible for” to “the competent authority that issued the examiner certificate” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) omit points (iv) and (v);
- (b) in point (d), for the words from the start to “applicant’s licence”, substitute “Upon request by the CAA”.
