# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 377

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 377. Amendment of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 452/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/0452)[^key-54c538bd3d46c5428f7466404b66571f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 1 (Part-TCO) is amended as follows.

(2) In section 1—

- (a) in point TCO.100, for “territory subject to the provisions of the Treaty” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) in points TCO.105 and TCO.110, in each place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point TCO.115—
  - (i) in point (a), for “Agency or the Member State in whose territory one of its aircraft has landed” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (b), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In section 2—

- (a) in point TCO.200—
  - (i) in point (a)—
    - (aa) in point (2), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) in point (4), for “Union” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) in point (b), for “territory subject to the provisions of the Treaty”, substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iii) in point (c), for “Union” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iv) in points (d) and (e), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point TCO.205, for “territory to which the Treaty applies” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (c) in point TCO.215, for “Agency or the competent authority of the Member State where the aircraft has landed” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In section 3—

- (a) in point TCO.300, in each place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point TCO.305(a)—
  - (i) in point (1), in both places it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (2), for the words from “pursuant to” to “of the Council” substitute “under any relevant enactment”;
- (c) in point TCO.315, in each place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in point TCO.320—
  - (i) in point (a)—
    - (aa) in point (3), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) in point (4), for the words from “pursuant to” to the end substitute “under any relevant enactment”;
    - (cc) in point (6), for “territory subject to the provisions of the Treaty” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) in point (b), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (e) in point TCO.325, in each place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.
