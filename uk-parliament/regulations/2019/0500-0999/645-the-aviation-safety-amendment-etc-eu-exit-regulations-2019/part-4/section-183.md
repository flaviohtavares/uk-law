# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 183

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 183. Amendment of Article 2 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-e9523ad3c939d48e8812bc051928cef9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 2 (products, parts and appliances certification), in paragraph 2—

- (a) in the first place the words occur, for “a Member State” substitute “the United Kingdom”;
- (b) in the second place the words occur, for “a Member State” substitute “the Secretary of State”.
