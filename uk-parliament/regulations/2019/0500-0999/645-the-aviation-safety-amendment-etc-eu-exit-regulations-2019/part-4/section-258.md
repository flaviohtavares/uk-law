# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 258

> Text as at 2025-02-27.

## 258. Amendment of Subpart C of Section B of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^key-c206e547d70c5473fbd1c95238659cb5]
