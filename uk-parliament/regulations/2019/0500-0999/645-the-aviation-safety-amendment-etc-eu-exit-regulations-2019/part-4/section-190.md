# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 190

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 190. Amendment of Article 9 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-46db276f5345114526e040e14f77bdaf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 9 (production organisations) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 2—

- (a) for “non-Member State” substitute “foreign state”;
- (b) in point (b) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In paragraph 3, after “Member State” insert “or the United Kingdom”.
