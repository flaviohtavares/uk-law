# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 225

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 225. Amendment of Appendix 1 to Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-3eab0d9c9646f2a582eb5c8e54aa6e5b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Appendix 1 is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”.

(3) In “EASA Form 1”—

- (a) in box 1, for “Approving Competent Authority/Country” substitute “CAA/UK”;
- (b) in box 2, for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”;
- (c) at the end, for “EASA Form 1-21 Issue 2” substitute “CAA Form 1-21 Issue 1”.

(4) In the “Instructions for the use of EASA Form 1”—

- (a) in the heading, for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”;
- (b) in the paragraph which begins “These instructions relate”, in both places the words occur, for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”;
- (c) in paragraph 2.6, omit “, and if appropriate, in one or more other languages”;
- (d) in paragraph 5—
  - (i) in the instructions for Block 1, omit the paragraph which begins “State the name”;
  - (ii) in the instructions for Block 2, in both places the words occur, for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”;
  - (iii) in the instructions for Block 4, for “EASA Form 55” substitute “CAA Form 55”;
  - (iv) in the instructions for Block 12, in both places the words occur, for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”;
  - (v) in the instructions for Block 13c, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
