# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 397

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 397. Amendment of Subpart D of Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^c-a17aa0e1cbdcbfa8984f5411db501008]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart D (issue etc. of licences, ratings, endorsements and authorisations) of Annex 2 is amended as follows.

(2) In point ATCO.AR.D.001—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “, issue and exchange” substitute “and issue”;
  - (iii) in point (2), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in points (b), (c), (d) and (e), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point ATCO.AR.D.005, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
