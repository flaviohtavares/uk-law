# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 386

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 386. Amendment of Article 11 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^c-0f7977482550f674a6a706da64bd4dd4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 11(2)—

- (a) in the first paragraph, for “Member States” substitute “the CAA”;
- (b) in the second paragraph—
  - (i) for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (ii) omit the words from “it shall notify” to “In that case,”.
