# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 332

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 332. Amendment of Appendix 4 to Annex 6 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^c-dfa4a816b866bd54a5ec852ee2a47e02]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Appendix 4 is amended as follows.

(2) In the introduction—

- (a) for “EASA Form 145” substitute “CAA Form 145”;
- (b) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) for “competent authority of issue” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In the form—

- (a) on the first page—
  - (i) for “European Union (\*)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) in the first place the words occur, for “Competent Authority” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
  - (iii) for “\[competent authority\]” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
  - (iv) omit the note at “(\*)”;
  - (v) for “EASA Form 145” substitute “CAA Form 145”;
- (b) on the second page—
  - (i) for “\[competent authority\]” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “For the Member State/EASA” substitute “For the CAA”;
  - (iii) for “EASA Form 145” substitute “CAA Form 145”.
