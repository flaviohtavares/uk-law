# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 389

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 389. Amendment of Subpart A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^c-a97512fd781c162e93f1f5f6ec600963]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart A (general requirements) of Annex 1 (Part-ATCO) is amended as follows.

(2) In point ATCO.A.005—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority which issued that licence”, substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) Omit point ATCO.A.010.

(4) In point ATCO.A.015(d), for “competent authority as defined in that procedure” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point ATCO.A.020—

- (a) in points (a) and (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (c), for “competent authority which is issuing the air traffic controller licence” substitute “CAA”.
