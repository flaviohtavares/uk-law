# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 381

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 381. Amendment of Article 3 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^key-fc5ac64f34983a0c38d50624680f7918]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 3 (provision of air traffic control services)—

- (a) in paragraph 2, for “Member States” substitute “the Secretary of State”;
- (b) in paragraph 3—
  - (i) for “Member States” substitute “The Secretary of State”;
  - (ii) omit “their”.
