# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 235

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 235. Amendment of Appendix 11 to Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-ac7817674459415a00626e52a6b9f9a5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Appendix 11 (EASA Form 65) is amended as follows—

- (a) in the heading, for “EASA Form 65” substitute “CAA Form 65”;
- (b) for “\[Member State\] (\*)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (c) omit “A Member of the European Union (\*\*)”;
- (d) for “\[Member State code (\*\*)\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (e) for “competent authority: \[competent authority identification (\*)2\]” substitute “CAA”;
- (f) for “EASA Form 65, Issue 2” substitute “CAA Form 65, Issue 1”;
- (g) omit the notes at “(\*)” and “(\*\*)”.
