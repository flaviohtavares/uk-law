# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 248

> Text as at 2025-02-27.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 248. Amendment of Subpart C of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^key-5b953dea6c360c78e28c3d0eb8b69d67]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart C (continuing airworthiness) is amended as follows.

(2) In point M.A.301, in point (f)[^key-20ce5564b867a944922fd8a689cc1670]—

- (a) in point (3)[^key-81c63aa89ba88b10d3650d26d9bc97d0], for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (4)[^key-fead6511c17c29e1959000fd9b6a75dd], for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point M.A.302—

- (a) in point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (c)—
  - (i) in the second paragraph[^key-a40b44ee741c1787a0328ecebc8ae1f8], for the words from “competent authority” to the end substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) omit the third paragraph[^key-6cccbb320f444fb98e1890e37af99007];
- (c) in point (d)—
  - (i) in point (1)[^key-0f534b3e8d9fe9da21d3fba68288262f], for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in “point (2)(i)[^key-1b7da8c6c3b2b7cf294c012a9cd2c080], for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
- (d)[^key-cdb66046ebff67b14ffa6dc215fad5d9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^key-8bdcc99ec637df04acb6fd2e526b1c82] in point (h), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;

(4) In points M.A.303 and M.A.304, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point M.A.305—

- (a)[^key-d5e7775161996211a839d42391a617e2] in points (c) and (e), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (e), for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”;
- (c) in point (f), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(6)[^key-79ac772e457bf97040641b420b85d80e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
