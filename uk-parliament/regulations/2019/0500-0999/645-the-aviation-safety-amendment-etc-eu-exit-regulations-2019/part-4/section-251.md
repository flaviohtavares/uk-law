# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 251

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 251. Amendment of Subpart F of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^key-017b028e5a50c662990ba218447cb85d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart F (maintenance organisation) is amended as follows.

(2) In points M.A.602, M.A.603 , M.A.604 and M.A.606, in each place the words occur[^key-b2c3df5b2a42d5b384940ed9f09929cc], for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point M.A.607—

- (a)[^key-4dcee0fc24feab7ef75701b813a0f591] in point (a)(1), for “the law of the Member State” substitute “any relevant enactment;
- (b) in point (b), in the last paragraph, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point M.A.613, in both places the words occur, for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”.

(5) In points M.A.614, M.A.615, M.A.617, M.A.618 and M.A.619, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
