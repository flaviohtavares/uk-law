# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 234

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 234. Amendment of Appendix 10 to Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-a1533b90cfa750978f758ea3cf08e1b8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Appendix 10 (EASA Form 55) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “EASA Form 55” substitute “CAA Form 55”.

(3) On the first page of the form—

- (a) for “\[Member State\] (\*)” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit “A Member of the European Union (\*\*)”;
- (c) for “\[Member State code (\*)\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (d) for “\[Competent Authority of the Member State\]” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
- (e) for “competent authority: \[competent authority identification (\*)\]” substitute “CAA”;
- (f) for “EASA Form 55a issue 2” substitute “CAA Form 55a issue 1”;
- (g) omit the notes at “(\*)” and “(\*\*)”.

(4) On the second page of the form—

- (a) for “\[Member State\] (\*)” and “A Member State of the European Union (\*\*)” substitute “the United Kingdom”;
- (b) for “\[Member State code (\*)\]”, in both places it occurs, substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (c) for “\[competent authority identification (\*)\]” substitute “the CAA”;
- (d) for “EASA Form 55b Issue 2” substitute “CAA Form 55b Issue 1”;
- (e) omit the notes at “(\*)” and “(\*\*)”.
