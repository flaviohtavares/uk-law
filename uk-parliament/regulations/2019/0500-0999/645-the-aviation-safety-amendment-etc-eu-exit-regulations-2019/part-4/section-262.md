# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 262

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 262. Amendment of the Appendices to Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-b88eede0e25ef19d0ddce01de6758c4e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Appendices to Annex 1 are amended as follows.

(2) In Appendix 1—

- (a) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in paragraph 4—
  - (i) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “competent authorities of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in paragraph 5.1, in points (5) to (10), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in paragraph 5.2—
  - (i) in point (7), for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (8), for “competent authority of the member State of registry” substitute “the CAA”.

(3) In Appendix 2—

- (a) in the first three places the words occur (including the heading), for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”;
- (b) in paragraph 2.2, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in paragraph 5—
  - (i) in the instructions for Block 1—
    - (aa) for “Approving Competent Authority/Country” substitute “CAA/UK”;
    - (bb) omit the paragraph commencing “State the name”;
  - (ii) in the instructions for Block 2, in both places the words occur, for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”;
  - (iii) in the instructions for Block 4, for “EASA form 3” substitute “CAA Form 3”;
  - (iv) in the instructions for Block 11—
    - (aa) in row (i), for “Authority” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) in the footnote, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (v) in the instructions for Block 12, in the first and last paragraphs, for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”;
  - (vi) in the instructions for Blocks 14b and 14c, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in the form—
  - (i) in block 1, for “Approving Competent Authority / Country” substitute “CAA/UK”;
  - (ii) in block 2, for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”;
  - (iii) at the end, for “EASA Form 1-MF/CAO/145 Issue 3”[^c-91ff3cf5da35126463ed4c72bb96a68e] substitute “CAA Form 1-MF/CAO/145 Issue 1”[^c-b8bb54498da0dc74c57fae1faf79b992].

(4) In Appendix 3—

- (a) in the heading, for “EASA Form 15” substitute “CAA Form 15”;
- (b) in the first form—
  - (i) for “\[Member State\] A Member of the European Union (\*)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “\[Member State Code\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iii) for “EASA Form 15b Issue 6”[^c-08b5cbf465cd91a94c83caa073658a83] substitute “CAA Form 15b Issue 1”;
  - (iv) omit the note at “(\*)”;
- (c) in the second form—
  - (i) for “\[Member State\] A Member of the European Union (\*)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “\[competent authority of the Member State\]” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
  - (iii) for “EASA Form 15a Issue 5”[^c-fa710a24b65d0902c11545ec9686bc8a] substitute “CAA Form 15a Issue 1”;
  - (iv) omit the note at “(\*)”;
- (d) in the third form—
  - (i) for “\[Member State\] A Member of the European Union (\*)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “\[Member State Code\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iii) for “EASA Form 15c Issue 1” substitute “CAA Form 15c Issue 1”;
  - (iv) omit the note at “(\*)”.

(5) In Appendix 4—

- (a) in points 3 to 6, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point 9, for “competent authorities” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point 12, in the note at the end, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(6) In Appendix 5—

- (a) on the first page of the form—
  - (i) for “\[Member State (\*)\] A Member of the European Union (\*\*)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “\[Member State Code(\*)\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iii) for “\[competent authority of the Member State (\*)\]” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
  - (iv) for “For the competent authority: \[Competent authority of the Member State (\*)\]” substitute “For the CAA”;
  - (v) for “EASA Form 3-MF Issue 5”[^c-7c75682b7f664271a0404e5538892c73] substitute “CAA Form 3-MF Issue 1”;
  - (vi) omit the notes at “(\*)” and “(\*\*)”;
- (b) on the second page of the form—
  - (i) for “\[Member State Code (\*)\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “For the competent authority: \[Competent authority of the Member State (\*)\]” substitute “For the CAA”;
  - (iii) for “EASA Form 3-MF Issue 5”[^c-bac8051def55daedc9294df327da5db5] substitute “CAA Form 3-MF Issue 1”;
  - (iv) omit the note at “(\*)”.

(7) In Appendix 6—

- (a) on the first page of the form—
  - (i) for “\[Member State (\*)\] A Member of the European Union (\*\*)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “\[Member State Code (\*)\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iii) for “\[Competent authority of the Member State (\*)\]” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
  - (iv) in condition 7, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (v) for “For the Competent Authority: \[Competent authority of the Member State (\*)\]” substitute “For the CAA”;
  - (vi) for “EASA Form 14-MG Issue 5”[^c-41b6ab25eb2524095d50b33dbc060dbb] substitute “CAA Form 14 issue 1”;
- (b) on the second page of the form—
  - (i) for “\[Member State Code (\*)\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “For the competent authority: \[Competent authority of the Member State (\*)\]” substitute “For the CAA”;
  - (iii) for “EASA Form 14-MG Issue 5”[^c-a15443a121df9d31936684c9643bd19b] substitute “CAA Form 14 issue 1”;
  - (iv) omit the notes at “(\*)” and “(\*\*)”.
