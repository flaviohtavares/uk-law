# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 224

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 224. Amendment of the Appendices to Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-86c50f15037ed59a7d7fa85ad0a18713]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The list of contents for the Appendices is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “EASA forms” substitute “CAA forms”.

(3) In the paragraph commencing “The EASA”—

- (a) for “EASA (‘European Aviation Safety Agency’)” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “Member States” substitute “The CAA”;
- (c) for “EASA Forms” substitute “CAA Forms”.

(4) In the list of Appendices, in each place the words occur, for “EASA Form” substitute “CAA Form”.
