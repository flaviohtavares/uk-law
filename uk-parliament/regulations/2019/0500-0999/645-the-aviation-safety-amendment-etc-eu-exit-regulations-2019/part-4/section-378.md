# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 378

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 378. Amendment of Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 452/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/0452)[^c-ef1f3389e0542ffee311b3c932a4a2e5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 2 (Part-ART) is amended as follows.

(2) In section 1—

- (a) in point ART.100, for “Member States and the Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point ART.105, in both places it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) omit point ART.110;
- (d) in point ART.115, in each place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In section 2—

- (a) in point ART.200—
  - (i) in point (a), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (d)—
    - (aa) in the first sentence, for the words from the start to “Member States,” substitute “The CAA must”;
    - (bb) in the second sentence, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) in point (e), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point ART.205—
  - (i) in point (a), for the words from “pursuant to” to “Agency” substitute “under any relevant enactment, the CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (b)—
    - (aa) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) for “Commission” substitute “Secretary of State”;
  - (iii) in point (c)—
    - (aa) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) for “Agency’s” substitute “CAA’s”;
  - (iv) omit point (e);
- (c) in point ART.210—
  - (i) in point (a)—
    - (aa) in the first place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) in point (2), for “EU” substitute “United Kingdom”;
    - (cc) in point (3)(ii), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
    - (dd) in point (5), for the words from “pursuant to” to the end substitute “under any relevant enactment”;
  - (ii) in point (c), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in points ART.215, ART.220, ART.225 and ART.230, in each place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (e) in point ART.235—
  - (i) in the first place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (a)(3), for the words from “a measure” to the end, substitute “an operating ban under any relevant enactment”;
  - (iii) in points (b) to (d), for “Agency” substitute “CAA;
  - (iv) in point (e)—
    - (aa) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) in point (2), for the words from “pursuant to” to the end substitute “under any relevant enactment”;
  - (v) in point (f), for “Regulation [(EC) No 2111/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2111), the Agency” substitute “any relevant enactment, the CAA”.
