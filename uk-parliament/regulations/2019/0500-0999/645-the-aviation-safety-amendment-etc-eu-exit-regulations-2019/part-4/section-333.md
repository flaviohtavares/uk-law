# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 333

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 333. Amendment of Appendix 5 to Annex 6 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-40bfc997cd9fb12a475a9f9078ba7652]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Appendix 5, in the form—

- (a) for “European Union (1)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
- (c) for “\[competent authority\]” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
- (d) omit the footnote;
- (e) for “EASA Form 146” substitute “CAA Form 146”.
