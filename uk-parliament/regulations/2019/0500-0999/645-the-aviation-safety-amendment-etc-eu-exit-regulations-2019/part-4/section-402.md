# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 402

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 402. Amendment of Subpart C of Annex 3 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^key-0e16131f6c7fc28c277617f2cf5b906f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart C (management of air traffic controller training organisations) of Annex 3 is amended as follows.

(2) In point ATCO.OR.C.005(b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point ATCO.OR.C.020(c), for “the applicable national data protection law” substitute “data protection legislation within the meaning of section 3(9) of the Data Protection Act [2018 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12)”.
