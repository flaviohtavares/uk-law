## 236. [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321) (continuing airworthiness)[^c-f08939312fcaa5af2bdd4a361f2b6944]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321) on the continuing airworthiness of aircraft and aeronautical products, parts and appliances, and on the approval of organisations and personnel involved in these tasks is amended in accordance with regulations 237 to 284.
