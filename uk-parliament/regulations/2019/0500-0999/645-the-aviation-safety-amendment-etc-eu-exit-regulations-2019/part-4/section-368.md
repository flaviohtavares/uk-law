# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 368

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 368. Amendment of Annex 6 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 965/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0965)[^c-812c0e622957c1b4688be01a21ca97b9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 6 (Part-NCC) is amended as follows.

(2) In Subpart A (general requirements)—

- (a) omit point NCC.GEN.100;
- (b) in points NCC.GEN.106(f), NCC.GEN.145(e), and NCO.GEN.150(e), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In Subpart B (operational procedures), in point NCC.OP.110(a)(2), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In Subpart D (instruments, data and equipment), in points NCC.IDE.A.105(b) and NCC.IDE.H.105(b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
