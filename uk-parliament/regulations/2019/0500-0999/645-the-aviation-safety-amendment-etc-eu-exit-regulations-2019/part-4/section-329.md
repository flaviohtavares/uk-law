# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 329

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 329. Amendment of Appendix 1 to Annex 6 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-3c7f761673a2ee562a14f45ba0f1b0ab]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Appendix 1 to Annex 6 is amended as follows.

(2) In the first paragraph, for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”.

(3) In point (a)—

- (a) in point (1)—
  - (i) in point (III), for “the UN country code of the State of licence issue” substitute “GBR”;
  - (ii) in point (IV), omit “, even if the script of the national language(s) is other than Latin”;
  - (iii) in points (VIII) and (XI), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (2)—
  - (i) omit point (XIII);
  - (ii) in point (XIV), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point (c)—

- (a) omit “the national language(s) and in”;
- (b) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(6) In the form—

- (a) on the first page—
  - (i) for “competent authority name and logo” substitute “CAA logo”;
  - (ii) in the first place the words occur, for “determined by the competent authority” substitute “determined by the CAA”;
  - (iii) for “European Union” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iv) in the second and third places the words occur, for “determined by the competent authority” substitute “determined by the CAA”;
  - (v) for “EASA Form 141 Issue 2” substitute “CAA Form 141 Issue 1”;
  - (vi) omit “‘European Union’ to be deleted for non-EU Member States”;
- (b) on the second page—
  - (i) for “State of issue” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) in both places the words occur, for “issuing competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) for “the UN country code of the State of licence issue” substitute “GBR”;
- (c) in the paragraphs which follow the words “Additional pages – Requirements:”, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
