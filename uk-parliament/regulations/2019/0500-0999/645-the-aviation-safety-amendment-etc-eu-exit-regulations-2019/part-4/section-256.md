# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 256

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 256. Amendment of Subpart A of Section B of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^key-5f3bc82f46088b0850b56637e668c975]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section B, Subpart A (general) is amended as follows.

(2) In point M.B.101, for the words from “competent authorities” to the end substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point M.B.102—

- (a) for the heading, substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (a)—
  - (i) for the words from the start to “This competent authority” substitute “The CAA”;
  - (ii) after “organisational structure” insert “for the application and enforcement of Section A of this Part”;
- (c) in point (d), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point M.B.104—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authorities” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) in point (4), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii)[^key-8b4e724f5a83b97dc7aaa2b2f96cea72] omit point (7)
  - (iii) in point (9), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (d)—
  - (i) in point (4), for “competent authority”[^key-c513501b40e9881f7037f41a3cb11b95] substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (7), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) omit point (f).

(5) Omit point M.B.105.
