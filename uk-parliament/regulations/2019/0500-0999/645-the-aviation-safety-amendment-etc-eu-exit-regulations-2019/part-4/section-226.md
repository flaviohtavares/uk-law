# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 226

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 226. Amendment of Appendix 2 to Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-5599c4c8ba2a67f133af7f80fecbc887]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Appendix 2 is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “EASA Form 15a” substitute “CAA Form 15a”.

(3) In the form—

- (a) for “\[Member State\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) omit “A Member State of the European Union (\*)”;
- (c) for “\[competent authority of the Member State\]” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
- (d) at the end—
  - (i) for “EASA Form 15a Issue 4” substitute “CAA Form 15a Issue 1”;
  - (ii) omit “(\*) Delete for non-EU Member States”.
