# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 380

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 380. Amendment of Article 2 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^key-221ce02fd297e6c8ef77a0a841e51808]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 2 (compliance with requirements and procedures) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraphs 1 and 2, for “competent authority referred to in Article 6” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In paragraph 4—

- (a) for “territory to which the Treaty applies” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) for “territory subject to the provisions of the Treaty” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (c) in point (b), for “competent authority referred to in Article 6” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In paragraph 5—

- (a) in the first place the words occur, for “territory of the Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority referred to in Article 6” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in the second place the words occur, for “territory of the Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”.
