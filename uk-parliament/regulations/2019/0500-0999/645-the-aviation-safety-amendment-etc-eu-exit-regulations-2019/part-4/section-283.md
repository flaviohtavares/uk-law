# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 283

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 283. Amendment of the Appendices to Annex 4 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-198d3cc31f33157981df35360af6e513]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Appendices to Annex 4 are amended as follows.

(2) In Appendix 2—

- (a) in the title, for “EASA Form 11” substitute “CAA Form 11”;
- (b) on the first page of the form—
  - (i) for “\[Member State (\*)\] A Member of the European Union (\*\*)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “\[Member State code (\*)\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iii) in the first place the words occur, for “\[Competent authority of the Member State (\*)\]” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
  - (iv) for “For the competent authority: \[Competent authority of the Member State (\*)\]” substitute “For the Civil Aviation Authority”;
  - (v) for “EASA Form 11 Issue 6”[^c-9db15a7ec3aaf243d0ecc834d6a7c0b9] substitute “CAA Form 11 Issue 1”;
  - (vi) omit the notes at “(\*)” and “(\*\*)”;
- (c) on the second page of the form—
  - (i) for “\[Member State code (\*)\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “For the competent authority: \[Competent authority of the Member State (\*)\]” substitute “For the Civil Aviation Authority”;
  - (iii) for “EASA Form 11 Issue 6”[^c-46822efc99dee628c7cf655b864cfee3] substitute “CAA Form 11 Issue 1”;
  - (iv) omit the note at “(\*)”.

(3) In Appendix 3—

- (a)[^c-6dcdd68a20e22a20fb6ff043a1d967f8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) in the first form—
  - (i) in both places the words occur, for “\[Member State code (\*)\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “EASA Form 148 Issue 3”[^c-fe587767c5f41b1e1bd2af1cdcf84181] substitute “CAA Form 148 Issue 1”;
  - (iii) omit the note at “(\*)”;
- (c) in the second form—
  - (i) in both places the words occur, for “\[Member State code (\*)\]” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “EASA Form 149 Issue 4”[^c-c9a10f994b801ccfaa6cf380d0184f0c] substitute “CAA Form 149 Issue 1”;
  - (iii) omit the note at “(\*)”.
