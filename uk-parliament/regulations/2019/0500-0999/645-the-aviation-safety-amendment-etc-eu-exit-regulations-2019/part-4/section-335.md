# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 335

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 335. Amendment of Appendix 8 to Annex 6 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^c-6ef617c648ea29f124ca1c4efd5a8caf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Appendix 8, the form entitled “Training programme approval” is amended as follows.

(2) For “European Union (\*)” substitute “United Kingdom”.

(3) In the first place the words occur, for “Competent authority” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”.

(4) For “Issuing authority” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”.

(5) For “above-mentioned competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(6) For “\[competent authority\]” substitute “CAA”.

(7) Omit the note at “(\*)”.

(8) For “EASA Form XXX Issue 2”[^c-9f566fc3b6712332538380b33b4a6011] substitute “CAA Form XXX Issue 1”.
