# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 254

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 254. Amendment of Subpart I of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-c8c9f286f7c32ab596f7f3562f24307f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart I (airworthiness review certificate) is amended as follows.

(2) In point M.A.901—

- (a) in point (a), for “EASA Form 15a” substitute “CAA Form 15a”;
- (b) in points (d) and (f) to (i)[^c-9a1902e6947a48b777f608837ee781ea], in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c)[^c-b40fab37d67846dfb6000376c6002807] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In point M.A.902—

- (a) for point (a)(3) (and the “or” after it), substitute “the aircraft is not on the United Kingdom aircraft register; or”;
- (b) in point (b)(2), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) Omit point M.A.903.

(5) In point M.A.904—

- (a) in the heading, for “EU” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “a Member State” substitute “the United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) in point (1), for “competent authority of the Member State of registry”[^c-b14feb858498c83a3d85272d3dcd63e9] substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in points (b), (c) and (d), for “competent authority of the Member State of registry”[^c-a12cfef4d35fd74c358bce6a7814e354] substitute “CAA”;
- (d)[^c-7f95f74cf46c8001b4b4be51c0ba2fb6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) In point M.A.905, in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
