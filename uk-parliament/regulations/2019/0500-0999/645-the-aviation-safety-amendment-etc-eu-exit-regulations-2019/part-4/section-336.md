# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 336

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 336. Amendment of Annex 7 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^c-7f992a33d3b6e11a8d8b8c37e19599f1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 7 (Part-ORA) is amended as follows.

(2) In Subpart GEN—

- (a) omit point ORA.GEN.105;
- (b) in point ORA.GEN.115, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point ORA.GEN.120—
  - (i) in point (a), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (b), in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in points ORA.GEN.130 and ORA.GEN.135, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (e) in point ORA.GEN.140—
  - (i) for “authorised by:” substitute “authorised by the CAA.”;
  - (ii) omit points (a) and (b);
- (f) in point ORA.GEN.150, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (g) in point ORA.GEN.155—
  - (i) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) omit the “and” after point (a);
  - (iii) omit point (b);
- (h) in points ORA.GEN.160, ORA.GEN.200 and ORA.GEN.205, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In Subpart ATO—

- (a) in points ORA.ATO.105 and ORA.ATO.135, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point ORA.ATO.150, in both places the words occur, for “one of the Member States” substitute “the United Kingdom”.

(4) In Subpart FSTD—

- (a) in points ORA.FSTD.100, ORA.FSTD.110, ORA.FSTD.120 and ORA.FSTD.200, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point ORA.FSTD.205(a), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point ORA.FSTD.210, in point (a)—
  - (i) in point (1), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (3), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in points ORA.FSTD.225 to ORA.FSTD.235, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
