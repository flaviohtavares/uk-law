# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 222

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 222. Amendment of Subpart P of Section B of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-9593d7798f36978c0a0f81bacb4af399]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section B, Subpart P (permit to fly) is amended as follows.

(2) In point 21.B.520, in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 21.B.525—

- (a) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “EASA” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In points 21.B.530 and 21.B.545, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
