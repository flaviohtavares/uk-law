# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 359

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 359. Amendment of Subpart GEN of Annex 3 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 965/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0965)[^c-8d183cff818af4f24f9ee3725bb50594]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart GEN is amended as follows.

(2) Omit point ORO.GEN.105.

(3) In point ORO.GEN.110(j), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point ORO.GEN.115, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point ORO.GEN.120—

- (a) in point (a), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(6) In point ORO.GEN.130, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(7) In point ORO.GEN.135, in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(8) In point ORO.GEN.140(a)—

- (a) for “one of the following authorities” substitute “the CAA”;
- (b) omit points (1) and (2).

(9) In point ORO.GEN.150(c)—

- (a) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “that authority” substitute “the CAA”.

(10) In point ORO.GEN.155—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) at the end, omit “; and”;
- (b) omit point (b).

(11) In point ORO.GEN.160, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(12) In point ORO.GEN.205(b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
