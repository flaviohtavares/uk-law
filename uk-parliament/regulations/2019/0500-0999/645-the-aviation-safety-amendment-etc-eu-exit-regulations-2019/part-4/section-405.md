# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 405

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 405. Amendment of Subpart C of Annex 4 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^c-595b163c6f9192df1244ae39451c2e93]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart C (aeromedical examiners) of Annex 4 is amended as follows.

(2) Omit point ATCO.MED.C.001(c).

(3) In points ATCO.MED.C.005 and ATCO.MED.C.010, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point ATCO.MED.C.015(a), for the words from “competent authority” to “place of business” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In points ATCO.MED.C.020 and ATCO.MED.C.025, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
