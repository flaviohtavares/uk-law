## 292. Amendment of Article 1 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^c-73d3ab28bd7163e40740745a873b513b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*
