# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 182

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 182. Amendment of Article 1 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-34e0117fc9fd33c1b8b8494096b4db18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 1 (scope and definitions), in paragraph 2—

- (a) in point (g)—
  - (i) for “‘ETSO’” substitute “‘UKTSO’”;
  - (ii) in both places the words occur, for “European Technical Standard Order” substitute “United Kingdom Technical Standard Order”;
  - (iii) for “European Aviation Safety Agency (the ‘Agency’)” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority (the ‘CAA’)”;
- (b) after point (g) insert—

  > - (gg) ‘Agency’ means the European Union Aviation Safety Agency;

  ;
- (c) in point (h)—
  - (i) for “‘EPA’” substitute “‘UKPA’”;
  - (ii) in both places it occurs, for “European” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iii) for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”.
