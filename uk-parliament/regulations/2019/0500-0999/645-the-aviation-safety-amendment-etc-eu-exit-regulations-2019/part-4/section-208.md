# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 208

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 208. Amendment of Subpart Q of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-739c5558d38b6b40bdef54206454c032]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart Q (identification of products, parts and appliances) is amended as follows.

(2) In points 21.A.801 and 21.A.803, in each place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 21.A.804—

- (a) in point (a)(3)—
  - (i) for “EPA” substitute “UKPA”;
  - (ii) for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
- (b) in point (b), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point 21.A.807—

- (a) in the heading and in point (a), in each place it occurs, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
- (b) in point (b), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.
