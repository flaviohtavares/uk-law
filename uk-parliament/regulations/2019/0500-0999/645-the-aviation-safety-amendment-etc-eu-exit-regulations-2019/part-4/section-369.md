# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 369

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 369. Amendment of Annex 7 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 965/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0965)[^key-642a5b308401d93c506f6e8be9acb52d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 7 (Part-NCO) is amended as follows.

(2) In Subpart A (general requirements)—

- (a) omit point NCO.GEN.100;
- (b) in point NCO.GEN.101, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in points NCO.GEN.103, NCO.GEN.105, NCO.GEN.135 and NCO.GEN.140, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in point NCO.GEN.145—
  - (i) in point (a)—
    - (aa) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) at the end omit “; and”;
  - (ii) omit point (b);
- (e) in point NCO.GEN.155(b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In Subpart B (operational procedures), in point NCO.OP.110(a)(2), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In Subpart E (specific requirements), in point NCO.SPEC.HESLO.110, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
