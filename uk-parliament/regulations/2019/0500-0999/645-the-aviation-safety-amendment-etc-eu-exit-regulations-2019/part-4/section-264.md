# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 264

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 264. Amendment of the introductory part of Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-4b5878fe6a1f52863eb9d4cfd7cf6d8f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The introductory part of Annex 2 is amended as follows.

(2) In the list of contents—

- (a) in the heading for Section B, for “Competent authorities” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) at the entry for point 145.B.10, for “Competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) omit the entry for point 145.B.15;
- (d) at the entry for Appendix 1, for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”.

(3) Omit the entry for point 145.1.
