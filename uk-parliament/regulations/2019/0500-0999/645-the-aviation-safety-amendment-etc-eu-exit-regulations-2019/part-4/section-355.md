# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 355

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 355. Amendment of Subpart OPS of Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 965/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0965)[^key-b92e160f041abd128e530ab9e7952341]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart OPS is amended as follows.

(2) In point ARO.OPS.100, in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point ARO.OPS.105—

- (a) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in the second place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “aviation authority”.

(4) In point ARO.OPS.110—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) omit point (4);
- (b) in points (b)(2) and (c)(2), for “subject to an operating ban” substitute “included on the United Kingdom safety list”;
- (c) in point (d)—
  - (i) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in the second place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “aviation authority of the state”;
- (d) in point (e), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point ARO.OPS.150—

- (a) in points (a) and (b), for “competent authority of the operator” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (c)—
  - (i) for “competent authority of the operator” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in the second place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in points (d) and (e), for “competent authority of the operator” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) omit point (f).

(6) In point ARO.OPS.155(a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(7) In point ARO.OPS.200, in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(8) In point ARO.OPS.205, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(9) In point ARO.OPS.210, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(10) In point ARO.OPS.215—

- (a) in point (a), for “Member State” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(11) In point ARO.OPS.230, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(12) In point ARO.OPS.235—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (d) in point (d), for “competent authority and the Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(13) In point ARO.OPS.240—

- (a) in both places the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “competent authorities” substitute “aviation authorities”.

(14) In point ARO.OPS.300—

- (a) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “territory of the Member State” substitute “United Kingdom”.
