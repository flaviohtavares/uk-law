> Text as at 2025-02-27.

## 311. Amendment of Subpart H of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-40387c57728e70f8de90420e42ddb358]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart H (class type ratings) is amended as follows.

(2) In point FCL.700(b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point FCL.720.A(c)[^key-932e182e75173775f9c4cbddcf63307a], for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”.
