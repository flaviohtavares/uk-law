# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 411

> Text as at 2025-02-27.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 411. Amendment of Article 5 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 139/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/0139)[^key-7dc6c5f6b8e7de1703e796716074e81c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 5 (exemptions) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraph 1.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) for “Member State” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for the words from “they shall inform” to the end substitute “the CAA must revoke the exemption”.

(4) Omit paragraphs 3 and 4.
