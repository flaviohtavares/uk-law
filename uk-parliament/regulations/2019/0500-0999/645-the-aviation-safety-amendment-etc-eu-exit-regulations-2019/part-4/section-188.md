# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 188

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 188. Amendment of Article 7a of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-3b3da4eba6b9217a9f3cdedcd4256f47]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 7a (operational suitability data) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1, in both places the words occur, after “an EU operator” insert “or a UK operator”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) after “an EU operator” insert “or a UK operator”;
- (b) in the second place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.
