# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 282

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 282. Amendment of Section B of Annex 4 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-ee29eecec012a0e86abf2585d4a32755]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section B (procedures for competent authorities) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading for Section B, for “competent authorities” substitute “the CAA”.

(3) In Subpart A (general)—

- (a) in point 147.B.05, for the words from “competent authorities” to the end substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point 147.B.10—
  - (i) in the heading, for “Competent Authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (a)—
    - (aa) for the words from the start to “This competent authority” substitute “The CAA”;
    - (bb) after “organisational structure” insert “for the application and enforcement of Section B of this Part”;
  - (iii) in points (b) and (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in points 147.B.20 and 147.B.25, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In Subpart B (issue of an approval)—

- (a) in point 147.B.110—
  - (i) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (d), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point 147.B.130, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In Subpart C (revocation, suspension and limitation of the maintenance training organisation approval), in point 147.B.200, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
