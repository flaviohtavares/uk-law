# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 216

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 216. Amendment of Subpart H of Section B of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-919f8cdd592c066200b95cf47886a9e9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section B, Subpart H (certificates of airworthiness and restricted certificates of airworthiness) is amended as follows.

(2) In point 21.B.320, in both places the words occur, for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 21.B.325—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “EASA” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b)—
  - (i) for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “EASA” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c)—
  - (i) for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “EASA” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point 21.B.326, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point 21.B.327—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (1)(ii)—
    - (aa) in the first place the words occur, for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
    - (cc) in the second place the words occur, for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) in point (2)(ii), in both places the words occur, for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(6) In points 21.B.330 and 21.B.345, in each place the words occur, for “competent authority of the Member State of registry” substitute “CAA”.
