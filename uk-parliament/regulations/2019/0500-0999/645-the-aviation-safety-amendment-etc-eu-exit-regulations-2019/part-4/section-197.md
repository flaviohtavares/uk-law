# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 197

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 197. Amendment of Subpart D of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-12b6a65b8d00fe4bec1111298b751866]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart D (changes to type-certificates and restricted type-certificates) is amended as follows.

(2) In points 21.A.90B, 21.A.93, 21.A.95, 21.A.97, 21.A.101 [^key-d606cea153611a496e494465d8203e8e]... and 21.A.105, in each place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 21.A.108—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “EU operators” substitute “United Kingdom operators”;
  - (ii) for “an EU operator” substitute “a United Kingdom operator”;
- (b) in point (b), for “EU operators” substitute “United Kingdom operators”;
- (c) for point (c)(1) (and the “and” after it), substitute “the CAA; and”.

(4) In point 21.A.109—

- (a) in the heading, for “EPA” substitute “UKPA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “EPA (European Part Approval)” substitute “UKPA (United Kingdom Part Approval)”.
