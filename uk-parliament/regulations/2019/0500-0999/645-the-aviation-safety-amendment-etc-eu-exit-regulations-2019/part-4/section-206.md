# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 206

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 206. Amendment of Subpart O of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-dc68a40e77a54af6de9bb3485090fb77]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart O (European Technical Standard Order Authorisations) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “European” substitute “United Kingdom”.

(3) In points 21.A.601 and 21.A.602A, in each place it occurs, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”.

(4) In point 21.A.602B—

- (a) for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
- (b) in point (b)(1), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point 21.A.603, in point (a)—

- (a) for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
- (b) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(6) In point 21.A.604, in each place it occurs (including the heading), for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”.

(7)[^key-37910eea9c7c82c8f3f184ef23566ec2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8)[^key-37910eea9c7c82c8f3f184ef23566ec2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9) In points 21.A.607 (including the heading) and 21.A.608, in each place it occurs, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”.

(10) In point 21.A.609—

- (a) in each place it occurs (including the heading), for “ETSO” substitute “ UKTSO[^key-45182c30172154d96e4aaaf891cfee95] ”;
- (b) in point (d), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(11) In point 21.A.610—

- (a) in point (a), for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
- (b) in point (b), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(12) In point 21.A.611—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
  - (ii) in both places it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in points (b) and (c), in each place it occurs, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”.

(13) In point 21.A.613, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(14) In point 21.A.615—

- (a) in the first two places it occurs (including the heading), for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
- (c) in the third place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(15) In point 21.A.619—

- (a) in both places it occurs, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”;
- (b) in both places it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(16) In point 21.A.621, for “ETSO” substitute “UKTSO”.
