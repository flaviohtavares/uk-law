# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 280

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 280. Amendment of introductory part of Annex 4 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-573d32528b8f96c0afe98825bf09cfc0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The introductory part of Annex 4 is amended as follows.

(2) In the list of contents—

- (a) in the heading for Section B, for “competent authorities” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) at the entry for point 147.B.10, for “Competent authority” substitute “The CAA”;
- (c) at the entry for Appendix 2, for “EASA Form 11” substitute “CAA Form 11”;
- (d) at the entry for Appendix 3, for “EASA Forms” substitute “CAA Forms”.

(3) Omit the entry for point 147.1.
