# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 299

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 299. Amendment of Article 10 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-3ee6bf41ea6dcb49b085e0acc375978b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 10 (credit for pilot licences obtained during military service) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, after “obtained during” insert “United Kingdom”.

(3) In paragraph 1—

- (a) after “holders of” insert “United Kingdom”;
- (b) for “Member State where they served” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In paragraph 2, for “Member State in consultation with the Agency” substitute “CAA”.
