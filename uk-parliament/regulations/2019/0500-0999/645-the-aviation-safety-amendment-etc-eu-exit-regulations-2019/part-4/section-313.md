## 313. Amendment of Subpart J of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-e857749f89fd7db7149d2d4e26b00c02]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart J (instructors) is amended as follows.

(2) In point FCL.900—

- (a)[^key-23d97de6358590a393d577659bbae34c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) in point (c)—
  - (i) in the introductory phrase, for “Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) in point (1)—
    - (aa) for “Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”;
    - (bb) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
    - (cc) in point (iii), for the words from “competent authority” to “European” substitute “CAA an adequate level of knowledge of United Kingdom”;
  - (iii) in point (2)(i), for “Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”.

(3) In point FCL.945, for “competent authority responsible for the applicant’s licence” substitute “CAA”.
