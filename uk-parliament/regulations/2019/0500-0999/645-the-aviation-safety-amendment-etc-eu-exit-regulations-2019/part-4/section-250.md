# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 250

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 250. Amendment of Subpart E of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-d398313436009456e203ca7b078d79f0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart E (components) is amended as follows.

(2) In point M.A.501(a)(1), for “an EASA Form 1” substitute “a CAA Form 1”.

(3) In point M.A.502—

- (a) in point (b)—
  - (i) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “an EASA Form 1” substitute “a CAA Form 1”;
- (b) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in points (d) and (e), for “an EASA Form 1” substitute “a CAA Form 1”.
