# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 296

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 296. Amendment of Articles 5 to 7 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-7d5915230a31903f29bb1c3ee04f32d1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Omit the following—

- (a) Article 5 (existing national pilots’ medical certificates and aero-medical examiners certificates);
- (b) Article 6 (conversion of flight test qualifications);
- (c) Article 7 (existing national flight engineers’ licences).
