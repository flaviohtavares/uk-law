# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 334

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 334. Amendment of Appendix 7 to Annex 6 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1178)[^key-6702db34926e3eaf02044cf215e0208a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Appendix 7 is amended as follows.

(2) In the form—

- (a) for “European Union (\*)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) in the first place the words occur, for “Competent Authority” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
- (c) in both places it occurs, for “\[competent authority\]” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
- (d) omit the note at “(\*)”;
- (e) for “EASA Form 148” substitute “CAA Form 148”.

(3) In the attachment to the aero-medical examiner certificate, for “\[Competent Authority\]” substitute “CAA”.
