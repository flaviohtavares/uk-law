# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 400

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 400. Amendment of the Appendices to Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^c-ee39047539d4122dc7db52ae6e483307]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Appendices to Annex 2 are amended as follows.

(2) In Appendix 1—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) in point (1)—
    - (aa) in point (III) for “State of licence issue” substitute “United Kingdom”;
    - (bb) in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (2), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (c), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (d), omit the words from “and, if required” to the end;
- (d) in EASA Form 152—
  - (i) for “Competent authority’s name and logo” substitute “Civil Aviation Authority”;
  - (ii) in the first place the words occur, for “determined by the competent authority” substitute “determined by the CAA”;
  - (iii) for “European Union” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iv) in the second and third places the words occur, for “determined by the competent authority” substitute “determined by the CAA”;
  - (v) for “EASA Form 152” substitute “CAA Form 152”;
  - (vi) omit “‘European Union’ to be deleted for non-EU Member States.”;
  - (vii) in footnote (1), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (viii) in points (V), (VIII) and (IX), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ix) in point (XI), for “issuing competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In Appendix 2, in EASA Form 153—

- (a) on the first page—
  - (i) for “European Union (1)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) for “EASA Form 153” substitute “CAA Form 153”;
  - (iv) omit footnote (1);
- (b) on the second page—
  - (i) next to “Signed”, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “Member State/EASA” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) for “EASA Form 153” substitute “CAA Form 153”;
  - (iv) in footnotes (2), (4), (5) and (6), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In Appendix 3, in EASA Form 148—

- (a) On the first page—
  - (i) for “European Union (2)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
  - (iii) in footnote (1) for “EASA Form 148” substitute “CAA Form 148”;
  - (iv) omit footnote (2);
- (b) on the second page, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In Appendix 4, in EASA Form 146—

- (a) for “European Union (2)” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in footnote (1), for “EASA Form 146” substitute “CAA Form 146”;
- (d) omit footnote (2).
