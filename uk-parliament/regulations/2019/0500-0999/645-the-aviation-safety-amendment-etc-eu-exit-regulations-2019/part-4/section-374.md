# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 374

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 374. Amendment of Article 3 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 452/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/0452)[^c-0553ad2000fcb05a1befbbab9a7faacf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 3 (authorisations)—

- (a) for “territory subject to the provisions of the Treaty” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.
