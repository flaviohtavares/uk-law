# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 246

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 246. Amendment of the introductory part of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^c-7203844154e525401484bc9393577820]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The list of contents is amended as follows—

- (a) in Section A—
  - (i) omit the entry for point M.A.903;
  - (ii) at the entry for point M.A.904, for “EU” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) in Section B—
  - (i) in the heading, for “competent authorities” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (ii) at the entry for point M.B.102, for “Competent authority” substitute “The CAA”;
  - (iii) omit the entry for point M.B.105;
  - (iv) at the entry for point M.B.902, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in the list of Appendices—
  - (i) at the entry for Appendix 2, for “EASA Form 1” substitute “CAA Form 1”;
  - (ii) at the entry for Appendix 3, for “EASA Form 15” substitute “CAA Form 15”.

(2) Omit point M.1.
