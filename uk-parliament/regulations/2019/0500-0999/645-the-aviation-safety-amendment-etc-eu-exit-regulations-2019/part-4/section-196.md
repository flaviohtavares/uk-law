# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 196

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 196. Amendment of Subpart B of Section A of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^c-117c4056c185c77e78afbc1439f74d8f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section A, Subpart B (type-certificates and restricted type-certificates) is amended as follows.

(2) In points 21.A.14, 21.A.15, [^c-4f0759337a7eba66eced221bbe77daa5]... 21.A.19, 21.A.20, 21.A.21, [^c-48b946b264294d581c96dd988e7d5ece]... 21.A.33, 21.A.35, 21.A.41, 21.A.51, 21.A.55, 21.A.57 and 21.A.61, in each place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point 21.A.62—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “EU operators” substitute “United Kingdom operators”;
  - (ii) for “an EU operator” substitute “a United Kingdom operator”;
- (b) in point (b), for “EU operators” substitute “United Kingdom operators”;
- (c) for point (c)(1) (and the “and” after it), substitute “the CAA; and”.
