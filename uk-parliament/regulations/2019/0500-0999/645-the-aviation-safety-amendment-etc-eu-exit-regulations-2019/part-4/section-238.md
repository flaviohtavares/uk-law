# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 238

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 238. Amendment of Article 2 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^key-e39a6c7304c6c1615b5ec5a596cb0dad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 2 (definitions) is amended as follows.

(2) In point (i), for “Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”.

(3) In point (p)—

- (a) in point (ii), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (iii), for “a Member State” substitute “the United Kingdom”.
