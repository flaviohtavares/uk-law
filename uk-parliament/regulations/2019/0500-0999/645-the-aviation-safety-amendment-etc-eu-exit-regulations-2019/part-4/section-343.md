# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 343

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 343. Amendment of Article 5 of [Commission Regulation (EU) No 965/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0965)[^key-6de82d7210ccd93c8f3475ac78ff42a9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 5 (air operations), in paragraph 5—

- (a) for “a Member State” substitute “the United Kingdom”;
- (b) for “Union” substitute “United Kingdom”.
