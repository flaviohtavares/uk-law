> Text as at 2025-02-27.

## 274. Amendment of Subpart C of Section B of Annex 3 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/1321)[^key-12e7796c8899267ace00567996b32924]
