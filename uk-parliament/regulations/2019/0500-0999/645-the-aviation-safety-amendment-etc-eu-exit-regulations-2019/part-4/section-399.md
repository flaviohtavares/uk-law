# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 399

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 399. Amendment of Subpart F of Annex 2 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^key-dcb8087bac67c54565036231390b719e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart F (aero-medical certification) of Annex 1 is amended as follows.

(2) In point ATCO.AR.F.001, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point ATCO.AR.F.005—

- (a) in point (a)(3), for “state in which the ATCO licence has been issued or applied for” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point (c)—
  - (i) omit “the national language(s) and in”;
  - (ii) for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In points ATCO.AR.F.010, ATCO.AR.F.015 and ATCO.AR.F.020, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.
