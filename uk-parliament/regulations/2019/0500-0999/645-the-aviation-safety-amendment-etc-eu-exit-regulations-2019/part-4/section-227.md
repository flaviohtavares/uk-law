# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 227

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 227. Amendment of Appendix 3 to Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 748/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/0748)[^key-5fe49f36d33e8093c1f0b204a913380c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Appendix 3 is amended as follows—

- (a) for “Competent authority logo” substitute “CAA logo”;
- (b) for “in all Member States” substitute “in the United Kingdom”;
- (c) for “non-Member” substitute “other”;
- (d) for “competent authority representative” substitute “CAA representative”;
- (e) at the end, for “EASA Form 20a” substitute “CAA Form 20a”.
