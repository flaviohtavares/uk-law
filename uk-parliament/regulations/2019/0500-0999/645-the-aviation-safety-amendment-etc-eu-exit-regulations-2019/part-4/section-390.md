# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 390

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 390. Amendment of Subpart B of Annex 1 to [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1934/2015)0[^c-f6ce79bd41e1df458c9337f5e7240953]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subpart B (licences, ratings and endorsements) of Annex 1 is amended as follows.

(2) In point ATCO.B.020—

- (a) omit point (c);
- (b) in point (d), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In point ATCO.B.025—

- (a) in point (a), in each place the words occur, for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authorities” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In point ATCO.B.030—

- (a) in point (a), omit the words from “and, if applicable” to “Publications”;
- (b) in point (d), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) In point ATCO.B.040—

- (a) in point (a), for “competent authority” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b), for “competent authorities” substitute “CAA”.
