## SCHEDULE 4 — Consequential amendment of Regulation [(EC) No 80/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/0080)[^key-dcab93f300ace5eef08daf89b8355978]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 428

### Amendment of Article 5 of Regulation [(EC) No 80/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/0080)[^key-dcab93f300ace5eef08daf89b8355978]

In Article 5(3) of Regulation [(EC) No 80/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/0080) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 January 2009 on a Code of Conduct for computerised reservation systems and repealing [Council Regulation (EEC) No 2299/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1989/2299)—

- (a) in the first place the words occur, for “subject to an operating ban” substitute “included on the United Kingdom safety list”;
- (b) in the first place it occurs, omit “Community”;
- (c) in the second place it occurs, for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom”.
