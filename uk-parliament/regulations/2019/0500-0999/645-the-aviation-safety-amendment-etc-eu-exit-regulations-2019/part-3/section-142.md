# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 142

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 142. Amendment of Article 69 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-c224f855306c9fff79b0efc6f290ae8e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 69 (qualified entities) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) for the words from the start to “tasks” substitute “The CAA may allocate its tasks”;
  - (ii) for the words from “the delegated acts” to “the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”;
- (b) in the second subparagraph, for the words from the start to “entities” substitute “The CAA”;
- (c) omit the third subparagraph.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) for the words from the start to “applicable” substitute “The CAA”;
- (b) omit “that they granted”.

(4) In paragraph 3—

- (a) for the words from the start to “grant it” substitute “The CAA may grant a qualified entity”;
- (b) for the words “Agency or the national competent authority” substitute “CAA”.

(5) Omit paragraphs 4 and 5.
