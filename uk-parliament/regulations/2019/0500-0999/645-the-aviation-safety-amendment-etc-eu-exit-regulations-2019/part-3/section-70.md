# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 70

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Amendment of Article 15 of Regulation (EU) No 376/2014[^key-1a106ab08772d30a03f3ba1cd2fc1156]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 15 (confidentiality and appropriate use of information) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) at the start, for “Member States” substitute “The CAA”;
  - (ii) omit “their”;
  - (iii) omit “and the Agency”;
  - (iv) for “Articles 4, 5 and 10” substitute “Articles 4 and 5”;
- (b) in the second subparagraph—
  - (i) at the start, for “Each Member State,” substitute “The CAA and”;
  - (ii) for “a Member State, or the Agency” substitute “the United Kingdom”;
  - (iii) for the words from “national legal acts” to the end substitute “data protection legislation within the meaning of section 3(9) of the Data Protection Act [2018 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12)”.

(3) In paragraph 2, in the second paragraph, for “Member States, the Agency” substitute “The CAA”.

(4) Omit paragraph 3.

(5) In paragraph 4—

- (a) for the words from the start to “Article 6(3) and their” substitute “The CAA and”;
- (b) after “administration of justice” insert “must”.
