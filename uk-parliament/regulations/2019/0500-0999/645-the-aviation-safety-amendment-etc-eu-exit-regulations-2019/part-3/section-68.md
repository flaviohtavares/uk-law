# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 68

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Amendment of Article 13 of Regulation (EU) No 376/2014[^c-74fe504951c28d8577665e34a4792c19]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 13 (occurrence analysis and follow-up at national level) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, omit “at national level”.

(3) In paragraphs 1, 2 and 3, in each place it occurs, for “a Member State” substitute “the United Kingdom”.

(4) In paragraph 4—

- (a) in the first sentence—
  - (i) for the words from “a Member State” to “paragraph 5” substitute “the United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “the competent authority of that Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
- (b) in the last paragraph, for “A competent authority of a Member State” substitute “The CAA”.

(5) Omit paragraph 5.

(6) In paragraph 6—

- (a) for “Each Member State and the Agency” substitute “The CAA”;
- (b) for “Articles 4(6), 5(2) and 5(3) substitute “Articles 4(6) and 5(2)”.

(7) In paragraph 7, for “a Member State or the Agency” substitute “the CAA”.

(8) In paragraph 8—

- (a) for “each Member State and the Agency” substitute “the CAA”;
- (b) for “a Member State and the Agency” substitute “the CAA”.

(9) Omit paragraph 9.

(10) In paragraph 10, for “Member States” substitute “The Secretary of State and the CAA”.

(11) In paragraph 11, for “each Member State” substitute “the CAA”.

(12) In paragraph 12, for “Member States” substitute “The CAA”.
