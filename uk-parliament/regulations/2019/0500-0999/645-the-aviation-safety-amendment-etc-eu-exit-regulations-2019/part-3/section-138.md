# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 138

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. Amendment of Article 61 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-c48fe5504d6f6a7ad7f5ba31e1896a07]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 61 (delegated powers) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for the words from “Commission” to “Article 128,” substitute “Secretary of State may make regulations”;
- (b) in point (a), for “territory to which the Treaties apply” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (c) in point (d), omit “and to the implementing acts adopted on the basis thereof”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) for “adopting the rules referred to in” substitute “making regulations under”;
- (b) for “Commission” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (c) in point (c)—
  - (i) for “Commission” substitute “Secretary of State”;
  - (ii) in point (ii)—
    - (aa) for “the delegated acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”;
    - (bb) for “the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”.
