# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 143

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 143. Amendment of Article 70 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-ba88304776b6b043dfb48a703368398f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 70 (safeguard provisions) is amended as follows

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) for the words from “delegated acts” to “thereof” substitute “regulations made under it”;
  - (ii) for “a Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (iii) in point (a), for “that Member State” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (iv) in point (b)—
    - (aa) for “Member State” substitute “CAA”;
    - (bb) for the words from “the delegated acts” to the end substitute “regulations made under it”;
- (b) omit the second subparagraph.

(3) Omit paragraphs 2, 3 and 4.
