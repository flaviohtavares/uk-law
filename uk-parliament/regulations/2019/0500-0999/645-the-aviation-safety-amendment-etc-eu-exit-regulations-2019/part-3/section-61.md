# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 61

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Amendment of Article 2 of Regulation (EU) No 376/2014[^key-57879bf6e65c3c80f92ec578c08f56f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 2 (definitions) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraphs 13 and 14.

(3) In paragraph 15, for “a Member State” substitute “the United Kingdom”.

(4) Omit paragraphs 16 and 17.

(5) After paragraph 18 insert—

> (19) ‘the CAA’ means the Civil Aviation Authority.

.
