# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 9

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Amendment of Article 3 of [Council Regulation (EEC) No 3922/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1991/3922)[^c-f6f3982ef144f0915dfb38ebb032ac65]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 3, paragraph 1, is amended as follows—

- (a) for “Without prejudice to Article 11, the common” substitute “The”;
- (b) for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (c) for “commercial transportation by aeroplane” substitute “air taxi, emergency medical service and single pilot commercial air transport operations”.
