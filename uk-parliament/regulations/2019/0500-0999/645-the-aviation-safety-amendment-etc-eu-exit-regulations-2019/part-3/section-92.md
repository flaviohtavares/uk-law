# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 92

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Amendment of Article 12 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-7d45a35d432f6f31b934cfd1e233f05b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 12 (design of parts)—

- (a) in the first paragraph, for “delegated acts” substitute “regulations”;
- (b) in the second paragraph, for “the delegated acts” substitute “regulations”;
- (c) in the third paragraph—
  - (i) for “the delegated act” substitute “regulations”;
  - (ii) for “the delegated acts” substitute “regulations”.
