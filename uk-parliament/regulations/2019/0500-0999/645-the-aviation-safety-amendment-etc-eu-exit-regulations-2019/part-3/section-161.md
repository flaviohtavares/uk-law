# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 161

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 161. Amendment of Article 92 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-4503ad51dcf8569a6cbac26a4ae126d7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 92 (aviation training) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) in the first two places it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “in its official publication” substitute “on its website”;
- (c) in the third place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit “and national competent authorities’”.
