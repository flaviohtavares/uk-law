## 166. Amendment of Articles 117 to 119 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-ee1fe25f0f2cf54dc7b15f15b7f97612]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

- (a) Article 117 (annual and multi-annual programming);

- (c) Article 119 (transparency and communication).
