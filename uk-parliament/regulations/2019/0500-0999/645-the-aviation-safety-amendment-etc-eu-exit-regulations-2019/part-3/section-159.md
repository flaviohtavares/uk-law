# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 159

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 159. Amendment of Article 90 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-712fa64f2d90e74836ee85da077aa0c8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 90 (international cooperation) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “Commission in its” substitute “Secretary of State in the”;
- (c) in both places it occurs for “European” substitute “United Kingdom”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) at the start for “The Agency” substitute “The CAA”;
- (b) for “Agency, in prior consultation with the Commission,” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) for “Union and its Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”.

(4) In paragraph 3—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) for “The Agency shall assist Member States” substitute “The CAA must assist the Secretary of State”;
  - (ii) in the first place it occurs, for “their” substitute “the United Kingdom’s”;
  - (iii) in the second and third places it occurs, for “their” substitute “its”;
- (b) omit the second subparagraph.

(5) Omit paragraphs 4 and 5.

(6) In paragraph 6—

- (a) for “paragraphs 1 to 5” substitute “paragraphs 1 to 3”;
- (b) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) for “Agency’s” substitute “CAA’s”.
