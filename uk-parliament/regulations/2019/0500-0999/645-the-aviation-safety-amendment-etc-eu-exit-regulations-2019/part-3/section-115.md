# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 115

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. Amendment of Article 38 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-41465e4f89bcb2650e11e149c9898c5f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 38 (protection of aerodrome surroundings) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Member States” substitute “The Secretary of State and the CAA”;
- (b) for “their territory” substitute “the United Kingdom”.

(3) In paragraph 2, for the words from “the competent authorities” to the end, substitute “the CAA”.

(4) In paragraph 3—

- (a) for “Commission shall” substitute “Secretary of State may”;
- (b) for “adopt implementing acts” substitute “make regulations”;
- (c) omit the last sentence.
