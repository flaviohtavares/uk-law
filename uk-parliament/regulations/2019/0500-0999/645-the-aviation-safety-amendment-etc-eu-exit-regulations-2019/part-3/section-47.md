# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 47

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Amendment of Article 13 of Regulation (EU) No 996/2010[^key-c92dd93c7c5f81eea30fd7246ffef49e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 13 (preservation of evidence), in paragraph 1, for the words from the beginning to “occurred” substitute “Where the accident or serious incident occurred in the United Kingdom, the safety investigation authority”.
