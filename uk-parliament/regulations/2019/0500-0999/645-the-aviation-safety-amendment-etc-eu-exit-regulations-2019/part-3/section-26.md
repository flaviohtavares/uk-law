# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 26

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Amendment of Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No 2111/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2111)[^key-46cad5e978a0b64eb27ff046ebe3b154]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 9 (publication) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom safety”;
- (b) for “in the Official Journal of the European Union” substitute “by the CAA on the CAA website”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) for “The Commission and the Member States” substitute “The Secretary of State and the CAA”;
- (b) for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom safety”.

(4) In paragraph 3—

- (a) for “national civil aviation authorities, the European Aviation Safety Agency” substitute “the CAA”;
- (b) for “territory of the Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (c) for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom safety”.
