# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 135

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 135. Amendment of heading for Section 8 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-cfb2df67abd9d78b69b97201f93ba048]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the heading for Section 8 (aircraft used by a third-country operator into, within or out of the Union), for “Union” substitute “United Kingdom”.
