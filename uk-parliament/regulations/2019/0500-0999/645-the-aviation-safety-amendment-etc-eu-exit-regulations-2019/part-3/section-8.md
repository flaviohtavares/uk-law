# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 8

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Amendment of Article 2 of [Council Regulation (EEC) No 3922/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1991/3922)[^c-7b96bd467fbdaca091a83e3454db7e38]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 2 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (a)—

- (a) in both places it occurs, for “a Member State” substitute “the United Kingdom”;
- (b) for “that Member State” substitute “the United Kingdom”;
- (c) in the first place it occurs, for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (d) for “Community legislation” substitute “Regulation [(EC) No 1008/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/1008) of the Parliament and of the Council on common rules for the operation of air services”.

(3) Omit paragraph (g).

(4) For paragraph (i) substitute—

> - (i) ‘the CAA’ means the Civil Aviation Authority.

.
