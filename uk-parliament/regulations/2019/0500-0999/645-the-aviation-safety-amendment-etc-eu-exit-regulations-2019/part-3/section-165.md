# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 165

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 165. Amendment of Article 116 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-429b196ab4fe45873d09c7a999072055]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 116 (procedures for taking decisions) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Management Board” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (b) omit the words from “, subject to the requirements” to the end;
- (c) in point (c), for “this Regulation” substitute “the Civil Aviation Authority Regulations 1991”.

(3) In paragraph 2, for “Management Board” substitute “CAA”.
