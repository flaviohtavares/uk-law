# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 154

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154. Amendment of Article 83 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-a2cc9dc91e9c9f549d00cd606506d93e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 83 (investigations by the Agency) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit “national competent authorities or”.

(4) In paragraph 2—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) in the first two places the words occur, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (b), for “the delegated and implementing acts adopted on the basis thereof” substitute “regulations made under it”;
  - (iii) omit point (c);
- (b) in the second subparagraph—
  - (i) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “the delegated and implementing acts adopted on the basis thereof” substitute “regulations made under it”;
  - (iii) after “in compliance with” (in the first place it occurs) omit “the”;
  - (iv) omit “of the Member State”;
- (c) omit the last subparagraph.

(5) In paragraph 3, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(6) Omit paragraph 4.
