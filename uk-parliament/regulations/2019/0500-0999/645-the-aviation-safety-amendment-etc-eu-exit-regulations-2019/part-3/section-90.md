# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 90

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Amendment of Article 8 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-c8a49a3cc34e7aab89b105e4c70fac10]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 8 (state plan for aviation safety) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for the words from “each Member State” to “stakeholders” substitute “the Secretary of State, in consultation with the CAA and other relevant stakeholders”;
- (b) after the word “affecting”, for “its” substitute “the”.

(3) Omit paragraph 2.
