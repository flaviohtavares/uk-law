# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 109

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 109. Amendment of Article 31 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-a90747e9beaf7e6655eb50391860a4c4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 31 (implementing acts as regards air operations) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “Implementing acts” substitute “Regulations”.

(3) In paragraph 1—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) omit “the uniform implementation of and”;
  - (ii) for “Commission” substitute “Secretary of State”;
  - (iii) for “adopt implementing acts” substitute “make regulations”;
  - (iv) in point (e), for “territory to which the Treaties apply” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) omit the second subparagraph.

(4) In paragraph 2, for the words from the start to “Commission”, substitute “When making regulations, the Secretary of State”.
