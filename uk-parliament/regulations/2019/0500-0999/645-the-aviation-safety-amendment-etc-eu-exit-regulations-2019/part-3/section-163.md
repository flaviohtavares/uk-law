# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 163

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 163. Amendment of Articles 94 to 114 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-607942e5b75270fd5e8963c2c4886b43]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Omit the following—

- (a) Article 94 (legal status, seat and local offices);
- (b) Article 95 (staff);
- (c) Article 96 (privileges and immunities);
- (d) Article 97 (liability);
- (e) Article 98 (functions of the management board);
- (f) Article 99 (composition of the management board);
- (g) Article 100 (chairperson of the management board);
- (h) Article 101 (meetings of the management board);
- (i) Article 102 (voting rules of the management board);
- (j) Article 103 (executive director);
- (k) Article 104 (responsibilities of the executive director);
- (l) Article 105 (powers of the Board of Appeal);
- (m) Article 106 (members of the Board of Appeal);
- (n) Article 107 (exclusion and objection);
- (o) Article 108 (decisions subject to appeal);
- (p) Article 109 (persons entitled to appeal);
- (q) Article 110 (time limit and form);
- (r) Article 111 (interlocutory revision);
- (s) Article 112 (examination of appeals);
- (t) Article 113 (decisions on appeal);
- (u) Article 114 (actions before the Court of Justice).
