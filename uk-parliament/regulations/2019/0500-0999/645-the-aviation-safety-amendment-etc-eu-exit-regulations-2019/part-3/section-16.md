> Text as at 2025-02-27.

## 16. Amendment of the title of Regulation [(EC) No 2111/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2111)[^c-47e07cadb8369f97d768538c188cf364]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The title is amended as follows—

- (a) in the first place it occurs, omit “Community”;
- (b) in the second place it occurs, for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom”.
