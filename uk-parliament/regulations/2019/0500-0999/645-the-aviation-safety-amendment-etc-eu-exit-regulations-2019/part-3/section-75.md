# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 75

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Amendment of Article 20 of Regulation (EU) No 376/2014[^c-3f59630b6886b1119936cab139cd80a6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 20 (access to documents and protection of personal data)—

- (a) omit paragraph 1;
- (b) in paragraph 2, for the words from “national legal acts” to the end substitute “data protection legislation within the meaning of section 3(9) of the Data Protection Act [2018 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12)”.
