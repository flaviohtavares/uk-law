# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 95

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Amendment of Article 15 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-fc9c9d917f5d507e9f463c6cb8eb1283]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 15 (organisations) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1, in both places the words occur, for “the delegated acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) in the second subparagraph, for “the adoption of the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”;
- (b) in the last subparagraph—
  - (i) for “the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”;
  - (ii) omit “adopted”.

(4) In paragraphs 3 and 4, for “the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”.

(5) In paragraph 5—

- (a) for “the adoption of the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”;
- (b) for “implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”;
- (c) in the last place the words occur, for “implementing acts” substitute “regulations”.
