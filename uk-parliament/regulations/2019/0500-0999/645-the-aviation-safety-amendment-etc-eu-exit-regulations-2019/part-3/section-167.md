# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 167

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 167. Amendment of Articles 120 to 126 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-a31cf1267ad793693af6e3d8015d9d86]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Omit the following—

- (a) Article 120 (budget);
- (b) Article 121 (implementation and control of the budget);
- (c) Article 122 (combating fraud);
- (d) Article 123 (security rules on the protection of classified and sensitive non-classified information);
- (e) Article 124 (evaluation);
- (f) Article 125 (financial rules);
- (g) Article 126 (fees and charges).
