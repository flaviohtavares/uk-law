# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 55

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Amendment of Article 22 of Regulation (EU) No 996/2010[^c-50b9e72276c8c251e6da895a31bf934c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 22 (access to documents and protection of personal data)—

- (a) omit paragraph 1;
- (b) in paragraph 2, for the words from “[Directive 95/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1995/0046)” to the end substitute “data protection legislation within the meaning of section 3(9) of the Data Protection Act [2018 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12)”.
