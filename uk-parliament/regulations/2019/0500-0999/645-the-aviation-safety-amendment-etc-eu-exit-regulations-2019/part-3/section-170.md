# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 170

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 170. Amendment of Article 132 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-4b34ec291d83f2b08eef69e7436c3741]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 132 (processing of personal data) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Member States” substitute “the Secretary of State and the CAA”;
- (b) for the words from “the national laws” to the end substitute “data protection legislation within the meaning of section 3(9) of the Data Protection Act [2018 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12)”.

(3) Omit paragraph 2.
