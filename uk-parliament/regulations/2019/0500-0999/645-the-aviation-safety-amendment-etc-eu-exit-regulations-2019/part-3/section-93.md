# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 93

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Amendment of Article 13 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-1ed9ebed09a3e486cddb641b35ffb36e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 13 (design of non-installed equipment)—

- (a) in the first and second paragraphs, for “the delegated acts” substitute “regulations”;
- (b) in the third paragraph—
  - (i) for “the delegated act” substitute “regulations”;
  - (ii) for “the delegated acts” substitute “regulations”.
