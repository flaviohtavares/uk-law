# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 179

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 179. Amendment of Annex VIII to Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-137cf9c5e7ebf98a7dfca023f58ce88f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex VIII (essential requirements for ATM/ANS and air traffic controllers) is amended as follows.

(2) In point 2.7 for “Union” substitute “national”.

(3) In point 2.8—

- (a) for “Member States” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (b) for “as implemented under Regulation [(EC) No 551/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/0551)” substitute “as given effect under Eurocontrol airspace management documents”;
- (c) omit “in the context of the common transport policy”.

(4) In point 5.1(g), omit “Union”.
