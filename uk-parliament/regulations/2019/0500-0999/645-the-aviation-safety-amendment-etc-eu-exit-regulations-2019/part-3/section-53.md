# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 53

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Amendment of Article 20 of Regulation (EU) No 996/2010[^key-162b309242bc24bd835e567fbb61ba4c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 20 (information on persons and dangerous goods on board) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Union” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) for the words from “territories” to “apply” substitute “United Kingdom”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) omit “in charge”;
- (b) for “each Member State” substitute “the Secretary of State”.

(4) In paragraph 4, omit “the legal acts of the Union and”.
