# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 180

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 180. Amendment of Annex IX to Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-4840ebed15ba8ca55c08d31c8b3dedeb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex IX (essential requirements for unmanned aircraft) is amended as follows.

(2) In point 1.1 omit “Union and national”.

(3) In point 2.2(a), for the words from “the delegated act” to “Article 57” substitute “regulations made under Articles 57 and 58”.

(4) In point 2.4.5, for “Union law” substitute “the law of the United Kingdom”.

(5) In point 4—

- (a) in point 4.1—
  - (i) for “Member States” substitute “the United Kingdom”;
  - (ii) for “the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”;
- (b) in points 4.2 and 4.3, for “ the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”.
