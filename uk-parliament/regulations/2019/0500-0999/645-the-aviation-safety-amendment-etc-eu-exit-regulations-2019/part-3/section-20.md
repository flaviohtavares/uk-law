# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 20

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Amendment of Article 3 of Regulation [(EC) No 2111/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2111)[^c-c1b2774c1ae8c1528fc2b3c04192f4b5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 3 (establishment of the Community list) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom Safety”.

(3) In paragraph 1—

- (a) in the first sentence—
  - (i) in the first place it occurs, for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom on safety grounds”;
  - (ii) for “Community list” substitute “United Kingdom safety list”;
  - (iii) after “be established” insert “by the Secretary of State”;
- (b) omit the second sentence.

(4) In paragraph 2—

- (a) in the first place it occurs, omit “common”;
- (b) for “imposing an operating ban on an air carrier” substitute “including an air carrier on the United Kingdom safety list”;
- (c) in the second place the words occur, for “common criteria” substitute “criteria in the Annex”;
- (d) for “ The Commission is empowered to adopt delegated acts in accordance with Article 14a amending the Annex in order to modify the common criteria[^c-487b556442ae304250683fd2e3bab789]” substitute “The Secretary of State may modify the Annex by regulations”;
- (e)[^c-b56fee9ebea059a4cba4ccf72d251f28] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Omit paragraphs 3 and 4.
