# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 104

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 104. Amendment of Article 25 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-24509e367217b86415bed8c7eb06ab91]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 25 (flight simulation training devices) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1, for “the adoption of implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”.

(3) In paragraphs 2 to 5, in each place the words occur, for “the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”.
