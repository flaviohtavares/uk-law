## 17. Amendment of Article 1 of Regulation [(EC) No 2111/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2111)[^key-803c3069c2bc94e472d867b0378f5fb7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 1 (subject matter) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1, in point (a)—

- (a) for “Community list, based on common criteria” substitute “list, based on the criteria in the Annex”;
- (b) in the second place it occurs, for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom”.

(3) Omit paragraphs 2 and 3.
