# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 147

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 147. Amendment of Article 76 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-72e5400d7c9feb22d385fc057518efdf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 76 (Agency measures) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “Agency measures” substitute “CAA measures”.

(3) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for the words from the start to “assist the Commission” substitute “The CAA must, upon request, assist the Secretary of State”;
- (b) for “delegated and implementing acts to be adopted on the basis of this Regulation” substitute “regulations to be made under it”;
- (c) for “the Agency submits to the Commission” substitute “the CAA submits to the Secretary of State”.

(4) Omit paragraph 2.

(5) In paragraph 3—

- (a) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “delegated and implementing acts adopted on the basis of” substitute “regulations made under”;
- (c) for “the delegated and implementing acts adopted on the basis thereof” substitute “regulations made under it”.

(6) In paragraph 4—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) for “The Agency” substitute “The CAA”;
  - (ii) for the words from “paragraph 6” to the end substitute “Articles 77 and 79 to 83”;
- (b) omit “the second, third, fourth and fifth subparagraphs”.

(7) Omit paragraphs 5, 6 and 7.
