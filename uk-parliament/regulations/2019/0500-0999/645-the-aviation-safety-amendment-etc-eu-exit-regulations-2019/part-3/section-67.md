# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 67

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Amendment of Articles 8 to 12 of Regulation (EU) No 376/2014[^key-6f79f150945ec1e77dc5e7586e5b15d0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Omit the following—

- (a) Article 8 (European Central Repository);
- (b) Article 9 (exchange of Information);
- (c) Article 10 (dissemination of information stored in the European Central Repository);
- (d) Article 11 (processing of requests and decisions);
- (e) Article 12 (record of requests and exchange of information).
