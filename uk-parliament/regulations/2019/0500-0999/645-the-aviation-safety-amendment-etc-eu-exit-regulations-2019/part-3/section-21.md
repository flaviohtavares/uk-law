# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 21

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Amendment of Article 4 of Regulation [(EC) No 2111/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2111)[^c-35c1cdbf0a1a9b65d25173cd720d2e53]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 4 (updating of the Community list) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom safety”.

(3) In paragraph 1—

- (a) at the start, for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom safety”;
- (b) for point (a) substitute—

  > - (a) to include an air carrier which is subject to an operating ban in the United Kingdom imposed on the basis of the criteria in the Annex;

  ;
- (c) in point (b)—
  - (i) in the first two places it occurs, for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom safety”;
  - (ii) for “common criteria” substitute “criteria in the Annex”;
  - (iii) in the third place it occurs, for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom safety”;
- (d) for point (c) substitute—

  > - (c) to modify the entry for an air carrier in the United Kingdom safety list to reflect a decision under article 250(1B) of the Air Navigation Order 2016 to amend a decision under article 250(1A) of that Order.

(4) In paragraph 2—

- (a) for the words from the start to “Member State,” substitute “The Secretary of State, having consulted the CAA,”;
- (b) in the first place it occurs, for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom safety”;
- (c) for the words from “in accordance with” to “common criteria” substitute “on the basis of the criteria in the Annex”;
- (d) in the second place it occurs, for “Commission” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (e) in the second place it occurs, for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom safety”.

(5) Omit paragraph 3.
