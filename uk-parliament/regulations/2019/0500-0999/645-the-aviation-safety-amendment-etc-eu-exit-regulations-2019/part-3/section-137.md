> Text as at 2025-02-27.

## 137. Amendment of Article 60 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-6264afb2fa6b21374e738e1746841cee]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 60 (compliance) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 2, in both places the words occur, for “the delegated acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”.

(3) In paragraph 3, for “territory to which the Treaties apply” substitute “United Kingdom”.
