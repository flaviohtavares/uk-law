# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 45

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Amendment of Article 11 of Regulation (EU) No 996/2010[^c-eec89c3ec6fe229d6d63adac5b9afb3c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 11 (status of safety investigators), in paragraph 2—

- (a) for “the legal acts of the Union or national” substitute “any relevant”;
- (b) in point (g) omit “EASA”.
