> Text as at 2025-02-27.

## 145. Amendment of Articles 72 to 74 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-69506bfbd9c274c7aaf4580e042d5853]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Omit the following—

- (a) Article 72 (information gathering, exchange and analysis);
- (b) Article 73 (protection of the source of information);
- (c) Article 74 (repository of information).
