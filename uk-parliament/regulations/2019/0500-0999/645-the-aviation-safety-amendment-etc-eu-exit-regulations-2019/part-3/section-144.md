# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 144

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 144. Amendment of Article 71 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-e8b5b3eb0dadff17e579a21c97e2c33d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 71 (flexibility provisions) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) for “Member States” substitute “The CAA”;
  - (ii) for “the delegated or implementing acts adopted on the basis of” substitute “regulations made under”;
  - (iii) in point (c), for “Member State” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit the second subparagraph.

(3) Omit paragraphs 2 and 3.
