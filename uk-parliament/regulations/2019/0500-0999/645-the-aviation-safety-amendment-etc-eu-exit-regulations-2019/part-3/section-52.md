## 52. Amendment of Article 18 of Regulation (EU) No 996/2010[^c-d2631d6d1091f0ae34fa9dd4eb6fccc4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 18 (follow-up to safety recommendations and safety recommendations database) is amended as follows.
