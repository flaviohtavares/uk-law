# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 153

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 153. Amendment of Article 82 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-61a5e24f7493c714bf27a98a45704f79]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 82 (third-country aircraft operators and international safety oversight) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit the words from “unless a Member State” to the end.

(3) In paragraph 2, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(4) In paragraph 3—

- (a) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in both places the word occurs, for “Commission” substitute “Secretary of State”.
