> Text as at 2025-02-27.

## 155. Amendment of Articles 84 to 86 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-69124fb04e7c3a0ea30f4e21aae36c20]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Omit the following—

- (a) Article 84 (fines and periodic penalty payments);
- (b) Article 85 (monitoring of Member States);
- (c) Article 86 (research and innovation).
