# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 46

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Amendment of Article 12 of Regulation (EU) No 996/2010[^c-56ca9eedffcebdb07c8e712f76ec4dd2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 12 (coordination of investigations), in paragraph 3—

- (a) in the first paragraph, for “Member States shall ensure that safety investigation authorities” substitute “The Secretary of State must ensure that the safety investigation authority”;
- (b) omit the last paragraph.
