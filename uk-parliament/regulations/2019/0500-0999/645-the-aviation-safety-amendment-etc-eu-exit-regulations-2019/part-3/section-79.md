# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 79

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Amendment of Annex 1 to Regulation (EU) No 376/2014[^key-b0ac4a48b69929ab104f6379757c8d5c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 1 (list of requirements applicable to the mandatory and voluntary occurrence reporting schemes) is amended as follows.

(2) In the paragraph commencing “Note:”, for the words from “competent authorities” to “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In paragraph 1—

- (a) in the first sentence, for “, Member States and the Agency” substitute “and the CAA”;
- (b) in point (6)—
  - (i) omit the first indent;
  - (ii) in the second indent, after “Narrative” insert “(in English)”.

(4) In paragraph 2, in each place the words occur, for “, Member States and the Agency” substitute “and the CAA”.
