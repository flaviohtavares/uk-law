# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 86

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Amendment of Article 4 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-5b95e96810bc2943a1f3abbcbe906534]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 4 (principles for measures under this Regulation) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “the Commission, the Agency and the Member States” substitute “the Secretary of State and the CAA”;
- (b) omit point (f);
- (c) in point (h), for “Union and of the Member States” substitute “United Kingdom”.

(3) In paragraph 2, for “the Commission, the Agency and the Member States,” substitute “the Secretary of State and the CAA”.
