# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 164

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 164. Amendment of Article 115 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-c2ca2bfd66746261956a19673f238b60]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 115 (procedures for the development of opinions, certification specifications and other detailed specifications, acceptable means of compliance and guidance material) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Management Board” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (a)—
  - (i) omit “the civil and”;
  - (ii) omit “of the Member States”;
- (c) in point (b) omit “the relevant European”;
- (d) in point (c), in both places it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) the first time it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) the first time it occurs, for “the Member States” substitute “relevant stakeholders”;
- (c) omit the words from “To that effect” to the end.

(4) In paragraph 3—

- (a) at the start for “Agency” substitute CAA”;
- (b) for the words from “in the official” to the end substitute “on the CAA’s website”.
