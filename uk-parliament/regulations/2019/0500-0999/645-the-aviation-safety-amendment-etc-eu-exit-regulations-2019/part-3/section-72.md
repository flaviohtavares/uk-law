# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 72

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Amendment of Article 17 of Regulation (EU) No 376/2014[^c-59ad49c8f1f46d6996c96c5b1f74fb69]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 17 (updating of the annexes) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “annexes” substitute “annex”.

(3) In the first paragraph—

- (a) for the words from the start to “Article 18” substitute “The Secretary of State may make regulations”;
- (b) omit point (b);
- (c) in point (c)—
  - (i) for “any of the Annexes” substitute “Annex 1”;
  - (ii)[^c-159318679822202c4d8523e9ce4f5f0b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii) for “Union” substitute “Secretary of State”.

(4) In the last paragraph—

- (a) for the words from “Agency” to “Article 14(2)” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) for “Commission” substitute “Secretary of State”.
