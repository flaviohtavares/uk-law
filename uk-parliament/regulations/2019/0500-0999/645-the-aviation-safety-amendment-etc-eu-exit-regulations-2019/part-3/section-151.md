# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 151

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 151. Amendment of Article 80 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-20ce0f6a465ad739f7bfcdf549f01be5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 80 (ATM/ANS) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) in the first place it occurs, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) in point (a)—
  - (i) for “Member States are” substitute “the United Kingdom is”;
  - (ii) for “above the territory to which the Treaties apply” substitute “of the United Kingdom”;
- (c) omit points (b) and (c);
- (d) in point (d)—
  - (i) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “points (a) and (b)” substitute “point (a)”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) for the words from “including” to “Agency” substitute “the CAA”;
- (b) for “the delegated acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”.
