# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 11

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Amendment of Article 8 of [Council Regulation (EEC) No 3922/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1991/3922)[^key-e8d8b274494fa3383e532dfbbe22f6b2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 8 is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraph 1.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) in the first subparagraph, for “A Member State” substitute “The CAA”;
- (b) omit the subparagraphs following the first subparagraph.

(4) Omit paragraph 3.

(5) In paragraph 4—

- (a) for the words from the start to “Member State” substitute “The Secretary of State”;
- (b) omit the words from “until Community rules” to the end.
