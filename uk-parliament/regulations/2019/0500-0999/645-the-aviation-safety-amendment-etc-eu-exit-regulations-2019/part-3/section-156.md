# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 156

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 156. Amendment of Article 87 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^key-b12caededd52382cdae0d80da39ef208]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 87 (environmental protection) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1, for “Agency” substitute “CAA”.

(3) Omit paragraphs 2 and 3.

(4) In paragraph 4—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “Union” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) in the second subparagraph—
  - (i) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “Union” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (c) in the third subparagraph—
  - (i) for “Agency” substitute “CAA”;
  - (ii) for “Member States” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (d) in the fourth subparagraph, for “Union” substitute “United Kingdom”.
