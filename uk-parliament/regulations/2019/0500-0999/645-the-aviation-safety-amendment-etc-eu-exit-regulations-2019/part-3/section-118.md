# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 118

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Amendment of Article 41 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-97cbcf0e70b910c186efb27f1ebf8d05]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 41 (ATM/ANS providers) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraphs 2, 3 and 4, for “the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”.

(3) In paragraph 5—

- (a) for “the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”;
- (b) for “Member States” substitute “the CAA”;
- (c) omit the last sentence.

(4) In paragraph 6—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) in the first place it occurs, for “Member States” substitute “the CAA”;
  - (ii) in point (a), for “Member States are” substitute “the United Kingdom is”;
  - (iii) in point (b), for “Member State granting the exemption” substitute “United Kingdom”;
  - (iv) in point (d), for “Member State concerned” substitute “CAA”;
- (b) omit the second, third and fourth subparagraphs.
