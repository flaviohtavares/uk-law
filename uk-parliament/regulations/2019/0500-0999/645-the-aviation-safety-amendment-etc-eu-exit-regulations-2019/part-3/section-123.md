# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 123

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Amendment of Article 46 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-70d22d923ef95c4e05e254f9efd16ce8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 46 (design of airspace structures) is amended as follows—

- (a) for “Member States” substitute “The CAA”;
- (b) for “the detailed rules adopted by the Commission pursuant to” substitute “regulations made by the Secretary of State under”.
