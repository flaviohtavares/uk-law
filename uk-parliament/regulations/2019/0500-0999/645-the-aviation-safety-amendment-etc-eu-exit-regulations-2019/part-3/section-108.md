# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 108

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108. Amendment of Article 30 of Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-9f2d6841f29e2e70f1368480acf825c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 30 (aircraft operators) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “territory to which the Treaties apply” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) for “the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”;
- (c) in point (a), for “implementing acts” substitute “regulations”.

(3) In paragraphs 2 to 5, for “the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”.

(4) In paragraph 6—

- (a) in the first place the words occur, for “Member States” substitute “The Secretary of State”;
- (b) for “territory to which the Treaties apply” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (c) for the words from “that territory” to “carry” substitute “the United Kingdom but in a territory for which the United Kingdom carries”.

(5) In paragraph 7—

- (a) for “the implementing acts referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”;
- (b) for “the delegated act referred to in” substitute “regulations made under”.
