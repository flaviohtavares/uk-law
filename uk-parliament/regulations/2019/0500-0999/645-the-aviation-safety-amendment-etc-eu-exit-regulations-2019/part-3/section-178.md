# The Aviation Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 178

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 178. Amendment of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139[^c-74dfbd1e6c8b0d937868e9aa5eeb1f18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Annex VII (essential requirements for aerodromes)—

- (a) in point 2.2.2, omit “Union”;
- (b) in point 3.3, for “Member States” substitute “CAA”;
- (c) in point 4.2.2, omit “Union”.
