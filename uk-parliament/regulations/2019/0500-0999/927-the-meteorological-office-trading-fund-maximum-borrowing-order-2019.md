# The Meteorological Office Trading Fund (Maximum Borrowing) Order 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 927

Government Trading Funds

The Meteorological Office Trading Fund (Maximum Borrowing) Order 2019

Made

12th May 2019

Laid before Parliament

15th May 2019

Coming into force

5th June 2019

The Secretary of State, with the concurrence of the Treasury, makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 2C of the Government Trading Funds Act 1973[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Meteorological Office Trading Fund (Maximum Borrowing) Order 2019 and come into force on 5th June 2019.

## 2. Amendment of maximum borrowing limit

In article 5 of the Meteorological Office Trading Fund Order 1996[^f00002] for “£200,000,000”, substitute “£300,000,000”.

Chris Skidmore

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

12th May 2019

We concur

Mike Freer

Paul Maynard

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

9th May 2019

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Meteorological Office Trading Fund was established by the Meteorological Office Trading Fund Order 1996 on 1st April 1996.

This Order increases the maximum that the Meteorological Office Trading Fund may borrow from £200,000,000 to £300,000,000.

An Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument because it has no impact on business, charities or voluntary bodies.
