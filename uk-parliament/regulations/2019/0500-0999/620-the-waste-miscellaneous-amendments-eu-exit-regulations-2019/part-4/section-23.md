# The Waste (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 23

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Revocations[^c-69b10a4dab64d29638ae001d93b340fd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The retained direct EU legislation listed in the Schedule is revoked.

(2) In the EEA Agreement, in Annex 20, in Chapter 5—

- (a) omit the adaptation in point 32aa;
- (b) omit points 32da, 32ea, 32ed, 32fb, 32fc and 32fec;
- (c) omit the adaptations in points 32ffa to 32ffc.
