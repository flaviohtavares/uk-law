## 15. Council Decision [2003/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2003/33) establishing criteria and procedures for the acceptance of waste at landfills[^c-1004988e6b123b6d8338ebd4a71172a7]

(1) Council Decision [2003/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2003/33) establishing criteria and procedures for the acceptance of waste at landfills is amended as follows.
