# The Waste (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Environmental Protection Act 1990[^key-340912b0f3d7814c15b12cddad68a9e1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Environmental Protection Act 1990 [^c23655901] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 33(13) [^c23655911], as it extends to England and Wales, for the words from “Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/98) [^c23655921]” to the end substitute “ the Waste Framework Directive ”.

(3) In section 34(2A) [^c23655931], as it extends to Scotland, for “Waste Directive” substitute “ Waste Framework Directive ”.

(4) In section 57(8) [^c23655941], as it extends to England and Wales, in the definition of “waste”, for “Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/98) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste” substitute “ the Waste Framework Directive ”.

(5) In section 62A(1)(b) and (2)(b) [^c23655951], for “Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/98), as last amended by Council Regulation (EU) 2017/997 [^c23655961]” substitute “ the Waste Framework Directive ”.

(6) In section 75 [^c23655971]—

- (a) after subsection (1), insert—

  > (1A) “**Waste Framework Directive**” means Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/98) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste, as last amended by Council Regulation (EU) 2017/997.

  ;
- (b) in subsection (2)—
  - (i) as it extends to England and Wales, for “Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/98) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste” substitute “ the Waste Framework Directive ”;
  - (ii) as it extends to Scotland, in paragraph (a), for “Waste Directive” substitute “ Waste Framework Directive ”;
- (c) omit subsection (12) as it extends to Scotland.
