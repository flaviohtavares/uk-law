# The Waste (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 3

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Waste and Emissions Trading Act 2003[^c-f8a53b6ea22227799f158a63a673ffe2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 37 of the Waste and Emissions Trading Act 2003 [^c23655981] is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (2), as it extends to England and Wales and Scotland, at the end insert “ , as last amended by Council Regulation (EU) 2017/997 ”.

(3) For subsection (2), as it extends to Northern Ireland, substitute—

> (2) For the purposes of this section, “**the Waste Directive**” means Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/98) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste, as last amended by Council Regulation (EU) 2017/997.

.
