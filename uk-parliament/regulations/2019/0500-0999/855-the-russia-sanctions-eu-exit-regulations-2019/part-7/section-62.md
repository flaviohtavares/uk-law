# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 62

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Exception relating to trade restrictions in relation to non-government controlled Ukrainian territory[^c-007bf371eaf53101747d3ecd18216ed5][^c-28596fe1c9bd6d91652211f2e023f19e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A prohibition in Chapter 2 of Part 5 in so far as it is applied to non-government controlled Ukrainian territory by regulation 53A or Chapter 2A of Part 5 (goods and technology relating to non-government controlled Ukrainian territory),[^c-cfc638fdd85a0e879df5ccdddced71fc] regulation 47 (imports from non-government controlled Ukrainian territory) or regulation 52(3)(a) (financial services and funds in relation to imports from non-government controlled Ukrainian territory)[^c-ee703314ed2c39c5d125a4be32c5f516] is not contravened by any action in relation to goods which—

- (a) originate in non-government controlled Ukrainian territory[^c-ddff3730a32ad20c2feed76c522e987e], and
- (b) are the subject of a certificate of origin.

(2) In this regulation a “**certificate of origin**” means a document issued by the Government of Ukraine or a person acting on behalf of the Government of Ukraine confirming that the goods originate in Ukraine.
