# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 65

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Trade licences[^key-f34a0745bb8c5320f23e2ee676f9c1a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The prohibitions in Chapters 2 to 6 and Chapter 6B[^key-4cd13c012d8419d2386901add588efd6] of Part 5 (Trade) (other than the prohibitions in[^key-5d73105e6b9fe1e9fa37f6a86e5c3cb8] Chapter 4IA and Chapter 4O of that Part[^key-717b19aa88e25beb5a90c41b499e94d9]) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
