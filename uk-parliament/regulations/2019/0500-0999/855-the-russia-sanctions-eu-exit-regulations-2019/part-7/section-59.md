# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 59

## 59. Exceptions relating to loans and credit arrangements[^key-929d0d4e682e218db35807e229b18c73]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibitions in regulation 17 (loans and credit arrangements) are not contravened by the grant of—

- (a)[^key-7a4f78fb86d285cc3f765b295ee3dc56] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a relevant loan that has a specific and documented objective of making emergency funds available to meet applicable solvency or liquidity criteria for a relevant subsidiary;
- (c)[^key-4b9cfbfc668e85f3a594297507af9ffd] a relevant loan consisting of a drawdown or disbursement made under an arrangement entered into before—
  - (i) in the case of a category 1 loan, 15th September 2014;
  - (ii) in the case of a category 2 loan, a category 3 loan or a category 4 loan, 1st March 2022;
  - (iii)[^key-58d99dec55ea5fa019e9616d2a611c9a] in the case of a category 5 loan, 29th October 2022[^key-81381886bc22261da667e9c161f363e4];
  - (iv)[^key-9cf7c1b23199a02c06bb6dd279467480] in the case of a category 6 loan, 16th December 2022;

  where the conditions in paragraph (2) are met.

(2) The conditions referred to in paragraph (1)(c) are that—

- (a) all the terms and conditions of such drawdowns or disbursements—
  - (i)[^key-b3b52b54e74890c78d7d9cd24b0a6139] were agreed before—
    - (aa) in the case of a category 1 loan, 15th September 2014;
    - (bb) in the case of a category 2 loan, a category 3 loan or a category 4 loan, 1st March 2022;
    - (cc)[^key-25d8b1c8e1f56fe06b7f658d527933c1] in the case of a category 5 loan, 29th October 2022[^key-87a530107f5ae51a45c23a27e2558e65];
    - (dd)[^key-d198f31dc98befb98a170b1fa1651198] in the case of a category 6 loan, 16th December 2022;
  - (ii) have not been modified on or after that date; and
- (b) a contractual maturity date has been fixed for the repayment in full of all funds made available and for the cancellation of all the rights and obligations under the arrangement.

(3) In this regulation—

- [^key-e7c1629cce8efc992815de10406d7782]...
- “[^key-43c8ac677934c9ffa9ecf6e2eeb04c76]**category 1 loan**” has the meaning given to it in regulation 17;
- “**category 2 loan**” has the meaning given to it in regulation 17;
- “**category 3 loan**” has the meaning given to it in regulation 17;
- “**category 4 loan**” has the meaning given to it in regulation 17;
- “[^key-4245eb291c67e312cfe227df4620e706]**category 5 loan**” has the meaning given to it in regulation 17;
- “[^key-9cbc18fc61beb113404037ba5ad743d8]**category 6 loan**” has the meaning given to it in regulation 17;
- [^key-4df7862659c9cb3b55f64754c14e6197]...
- [^key-194fe497144a7f9abfbddbb8bd787a40]...
- “**relevant loan**” has the meaning given to it in regulation 17;
- “**relevant subsidiary**” means a person, other than an individual, which is—
  - (a) incorporated or constituted under the law of any part of the United Kingdom, and
  - (b) owned (within the meaning of regulation 16(7)) by a person, other than an individual, that is connected with Russia.[^key-00e28ce05fdd7268f3db0fa01846163a]
