> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64A. Director disqualification licences[^c-8f6263fc012d76cda009fb7940078c63]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may issue a licence in relation to any person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of regulation 19B (director disqualification sanctions) providing that the prohibitions in—

- (a) section 11A(1) of the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986, and
- (b) Article 15A(1) of the Company Directors Disqualification (Northern Ireland) Order 2002,

do not apply to anything done under the authority of that licence.
