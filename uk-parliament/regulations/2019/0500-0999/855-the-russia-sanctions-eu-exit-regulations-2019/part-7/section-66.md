# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 66

> Text as at 2026-05-20.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Licences: general provisions[^key-20c73903fdf84a0a6ded12828b96a8cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences ,[^key-27b4281f0c9f6a98a607c4b484d74f7c] director disqualification licences,[^key-25c5a1147bbfed7a1679bab84cf75adc] trade licences , detained transport asset licences, ships licences and aircraft licences.[^key-093e02cd89318eac5634779540acc614]

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may —

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^key-1b8d0e7281b7d641475eb5f89fdedcbd]**P**”), issues[^key-ffc93defcb357ab79f0c4f7aafdacb34] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^key-9dd4910c8ad582359e2b428fb4993abb] must give written notice to P[^key-8559e64e6f87248dd9729fae734fd89f] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
