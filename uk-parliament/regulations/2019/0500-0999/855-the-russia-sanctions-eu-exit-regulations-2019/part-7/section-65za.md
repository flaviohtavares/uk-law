# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 65ZA

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65ZA. Detained transport asset licences[^c-b050c1b41d03b4d01290ca8211223ce7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The prohibitions in Chapter 4O (Detained transport assets) of Part 5 (Trade) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
