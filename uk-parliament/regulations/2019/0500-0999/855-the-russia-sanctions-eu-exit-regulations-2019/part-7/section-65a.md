# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 65A

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65A. Aircraft licences[^c-8e41d5c8c845f0fd6f3db3bc5f87d40e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The prohibition in regulation 57J(1) (prohibition on overflight or landing) does not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
