# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 60I

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60I. Trade: exceptions in relation to Energy-related Goods and energy-related technology[^c-500a0b1bdb26b7dbd14dd01a1ed8ab05][^c-d48925954ecfad047a2f05f7ccba83e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibitions in regulations 44 (financial services relating to energy-related goods and energy-related technology[^c-d93541bef1c35da09c783837b1524fcc]) and 45 (brokering services: non-UK activity relating to energy-related goods and energy-related technology) are not contravened in relation to the provision of insurance or reinsurance to a relevant person with regard to that person’s activities outside the energy sector in Russia.

(2) The prohibitions in Chapter 4 (energy-related goods , energy-related technology[^c-ccb659518527b8743a7dc43fc78f0641] and related activities) are not contravened by a relevant activity that is necessary for the purposes of a UK petroleum project.

(3) In this regulation—

- “**energy related goods**” has the meaning given in regulation 21(1) (interpretation of Part 5);
- “[^c-afdbd8028539f80e7c9e5feac851e303]**energy-related technology**” has the meaning given in regulation 21(1) (interpretation of Part 5);
- “**relevant activity**” means any activity which would, in the absence of this regulation, contravene the prohibitions specified in paragraphs (1) and (2);
- “**relevant person**” means a person who is not a person connected with Russia;
- “**UK petroleum project**” means an oil or gas exploration or production project that is wholly or partially located within—
  - (a) the United Kingdom;
  - (b) waters adjacent to the United Kingdom up to the seaward limits of the territorial sea, or the seabed and subsoil below them;
  - (c) areas from time to time designated under section 1(7) of the Continental Shelf Act 1964 (designation of areas of continental shelf), and any waters within the limits of such areas.
