# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 94

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Interpretation of Part 10[^c-87fcb401d5ce7cf45dbba02c02a281bd][^c-9a762809efe51dd830cbd3443433defe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), any expression used in this Part and in section 19 or 20 of the Act has the same meaning in this Part as it has in section 19 or (as the case may be) section 20 of the Act.

(2) For the purpose of interpreting any reference to “prohibited goods” or “relevant goods” in this Part, any reference in section 19 or 20 of the Act to a “relevant prohibition or requirement” is to be read as a reference to any prohibition specified in regulation 89(2)(a) to (e)[^c-cf09200befd3bb4caa5d4ecc9fef26a8].
