## SCHEDULE 3BA — Metals[^c-d4f4063ce54340066f2aa84dcae087da]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 46IF(1)

### PART 1

#### 1. Interpretation

Paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 applies for the purposes of interpreting Part 2.

### PART 2

#### 2. Specified products for the purposes of this Schedule

| 74 | Copper and articles thereof |
| 75 | Nickel and articles thereof |
