# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 4

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Purposes[^c-34a7d3c2d8178d189e5e774852f56fde]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The regulations contained in this instrument that are made under section 1 of the Act are for the purposes of —[^c-88182fb654a049bf4e22f4846fba020c]

- (a)[^c-4f95b6f041ee1a56b29db5794794ddbc] encouraging Russia to cease actions destabilising Ukraine or undermining or threatening the territorial integrity, sovereignty or independence of Ukraine.
- (b)[^c-f597c6525d6b37eb852c0235db27f293] promoting the payment of compensation by Russia for damage, loss or injury suffered by Ukraine on or after 24th February 2022 as a result of Russia’s invasion of Ukraine.
