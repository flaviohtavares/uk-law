# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-eddecbfeb7c337872f2cfc2901056378]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “**the Act**” means the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018;
- “[^key-d6f0e5276b5d39210312589e05edc447]**aircraft licence**” means a licence under regulation 65A;
- “**arrangement**” includes any agreement, understanding, scheme, transaction or series of transactions, whether or not legally enforceable (but see paragraph 12 of Schedule 1 for the meaning of that term in that Schedule);
- “**CEMA**” means the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 [^c23948991];
- “**the Commissioners**” means the Commissioners for Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs;
- “**conduct**” includes acts and omissions;
- “**Crimea**” means the Autonomous Republic of Crimea and the city of Sevastopol;
- “[^key-7d22d371365c7cf1dece94baf69ff9cf]**detained transport asset licence**” means a licence under regulation 65ZA;
- “[^key-78a3a98b1a059637b1dffa58721e6fd2]**director disqualification licence**” means a licence under regulation 64A;
- “**document**” includes information recorded in any form and, in relation to information recorded otherwise than in legible form, references to its production include producing a copy of the information in legible form;
- “**the EU Russia Regulations**” means the following, as they have effect in EU law—
  - (a) Council Regulation (EU) No 269/2014 of 17 March 2014 (concerning restrictive measures in respect of actions undermining or threatening the territorial integrity, sovereignty and independence of Ukraine) [^c23949001],
  - (b) Council Regulation (EU) No 692/2014 of 23 June 2014 (concerning restrictive measures in response to the illegal annexation of Crimea and Sevastopol) [^c23949011], and
  - (c) Council Regulation (EU) No 833/2014 of 31 July 2014 (concerning restrictive measures in view of Russia's actions destabilising the situation in Ukraine) [^c23949021];
- [^key-7c4b6abc06f1d81b8bfa0001ee4cf609]...
- “[^key-99bf20835389fe592144707e56be4dd2]**non-government controlled areas of the Donetsk, Kherson Luhansk and Zaporizhzhia oblasts**” means the parts of the Donetsk, Kherson, Luhansk and Zaporizhzhia oblasts which are for the time being controlled by an authority other than the Government of Ukraine;
- “[^key-3660c9aaf2fce5a046ada382a03363f8]**non-government controlled Ukrainian territory**” means Crimea and non-government controlled areas of the Donetsk, Kherson, Luhansk and Zaporizhzhia oblasts;
- “[^key-1208369c708bdc0fd0c5fcc0e579aa01]**OFCOM**” means the Office of Communications;
- “**ships licence**” means a licence under regulation 65ZB;
- “**trade licence**” means a licence under regulation 65;
- “**Treasury licence**” means a licence under regulation 64(1); the “**Ukraine Financial Sanctions Regulations**” means—
  - (a) The Ukraine (European Union Financial Sanctions) (No.2) Regulations 2014 [^c23949031], and
  - (b) The Ukraine (European Union Financial Sanctions) (No.3) Regulations 2014 [^c23949041];
- “**United Kingdom person**” has the same meaning as in section 21 of the Act;
- “**working day**” means any day other than—
  - (a) Saturday or Sunday,
  - (b) Christmas Day or Good Friday, or
  - (c) a day which is a bank holiday under the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 in any part of the United Kingdom.
