## 46Z16W. Import of relevant processed synthetic diamonds[^c-5fd6ede33123510b50481d6608301376][^c-876278a10227d7e24a62920f4473f2c1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The import of relevant processed synthetic diamonds is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).
