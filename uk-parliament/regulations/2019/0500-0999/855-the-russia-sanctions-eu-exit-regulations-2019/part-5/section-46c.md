# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 46C

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46C. Interpretation[^c-8764836de1030d7d45b3e9ee594c19db][^c-ba5e8b433b46f408a162a8ceb4524539]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**iron and steel products**” means any thing specified in Schedule 3B;
- “**third country**” means a country that is not the United Kingdom, the Isle of Man or Russia.
