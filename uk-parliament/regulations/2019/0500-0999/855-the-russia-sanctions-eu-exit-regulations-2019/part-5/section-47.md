# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 47

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Imports from non-government controlled Ukrainian territory[^key-61c23a02df1cdc96340364d9971683bd][^key-792dc5473eaa7a8c19b308b34c689348]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The import of goods which originate in non-government controlled Ukrainian territory is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).
