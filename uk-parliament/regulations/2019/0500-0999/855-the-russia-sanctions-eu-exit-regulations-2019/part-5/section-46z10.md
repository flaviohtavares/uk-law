# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 46Z10

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46Z10. Meaning of “relevant day”[^key-876278a10227d7e24a62920f4473f2c1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2022-07-21).**

In this Chapter, “**relevant day**” means the day on which this Chapter comes into force.
