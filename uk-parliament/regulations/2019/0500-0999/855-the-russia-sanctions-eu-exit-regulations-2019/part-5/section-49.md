# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 49

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Supply and delivery of infrastructure-related goods[^c-3fa8c025210d4ff4c2bd34960b434ccc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly supply or deliver infrastructure-related goods from a third country to a place in non-government controlled Ukrainian territory[^c-a9c3f0f30ac3e2da339bba68495bf04c].

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes the prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with that offence to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the goods were destined (or ultimately destined) for non-government controlled Ukrainian territory.

(4) In this regulation, “**third country**” means a country that is not the United Kingdom, the Isle of Man or non-government controlled Ukrainian territory.
