## 46Z4. Import of oil and oil products[^key-876278a10227d7e24a62920f4473f2c1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The import of oil and oil products, on or after the relevant day, which are consigned from Russia is prohibited.

(2) The import of oil and oil products, on or after the relevant day, which originate in Russia is prohibited.

(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) are subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).
