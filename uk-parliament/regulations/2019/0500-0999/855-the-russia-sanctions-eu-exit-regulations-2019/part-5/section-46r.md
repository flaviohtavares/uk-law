# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 46R

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46R. Interpretation[^key-7788dbe4e19a81e510e80c310a1dcf8e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**Schedule 3D[^key-d05b8e5189cf7b8ce7bd24791d0a2d1a] revenue generating goods**” means any thing specified in Schedule 3D;
- “**third country**” means a country that is not the United Kingdom, the Isle of Man or Russia.
