# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 43

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Technical assistance relating to energy-related goods and energy-related technology[^c-c92953c0fad4f00843d2b06c10077f6d][^c-34bc59ac7722928becabbed40b292ffb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly provide technical assistance relating to energy-related goods or energy-related technology[^c-ca2f183ab5ff56fc126c87dfa1a32e9d]—

- (a) to a person connected with Russia; or
- (b) for use in Russia.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 7 (Exception and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but—

- (a) it is a defence for a person charged with an offence of contravening paragraph (1)(a) (“**P**”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the person was connected with Russia;
- (b) it is a defence for a person charged with an offence of contravening paragraph (1)(b) to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the goods or technology[^c-f20ac81e4eb8b633b0dffbe7afb6c42f] were for use in Russia.
