# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 22

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Export of restricted goods[^key-043b74db0ed8ef14c4188e0f2290bc11][^key-e05faf242109170c5cd57783936e39d6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of restricted goods to, or for use in, Russia is prohibited.

(1A)[^key-2631d1d43c295c0cb76012e787329c04] The export of maritime goods for the placing on board of a Russian-flagged vessel is prohibited.

(2) Paragraphs (1) and (1A) are[^key-2780976df1e3aa1b526728abad29e6e0] subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).
