## 46M. Interpretation[^key-7788dbe4e19a81e510e80c310a1dcf8e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter, “**jet fuel and fuel additives**” means the goods listed under that heading in Part 8 of Schedule 2A.
