# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 46S

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46S. Import of Schedule 3D revenue generating goods[^key-7788dbe4e19a81e510e80c310a1dcf8e][^key-867a3131e9312718379de7bb0375c576]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The import of Schedule 3D revenue generating goods which are consigned from Russia is prohibited.

(2) The import of Schedule 3D revenue generating goods which originate in Russia is prohibited.

(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) are subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).
