> Text as at 2026-05-20.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46Z16K. Import of diamonds and diamond jewellery[^key-248c158d51396b14f41ec648cf9cbdae][^key-876278a10227d7e24a62920f4473f2c1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The import of diamonds and diamond jewellery which are consigned from Russia is prohibited.

(2) The import of diamonds and diamond jewellery which originate in Russia is prohibited.

(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) are subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).
