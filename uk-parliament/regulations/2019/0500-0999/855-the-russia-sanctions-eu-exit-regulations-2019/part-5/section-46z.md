# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 46Z

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46Z. Technical assistance relating to G7 dependency and further goods and G7 dependency and further technology[^key-876278a10227d7e24a62920f4473f2c1][^key-885328520e7da22b4fcb2ba80742261a][^key-26d046fb347e96b2887024e62a449b4f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly provide technical assistance relating to G7 dependency and further goods[^key-7f73afe742d81fb22d841296579f6186] or G7 dependency and further technology[^key-58305a98ebce2070a32727c163df43a5] —

- (a) to a person connected with Russia; or
- (b) for use in Russia.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but—

- (a) it is a defence for a person charged with an offence of contravening paragraph (1)(a) (“**P**”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the person was connected with Russia;
- (b) it is a defence for a person charged with an offence of contravening paragraph (1)(b) to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the goods or technology[^key-0d74a30e9bf518c295e4161a7d0216f6] were for use in Russia.
