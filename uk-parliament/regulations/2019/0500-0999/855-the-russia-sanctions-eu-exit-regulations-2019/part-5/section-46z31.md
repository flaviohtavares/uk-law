# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 46Z31

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46Z31. Technical assistance relating to Russia’s vulnerable goods and Russia’s vulnerable technology[^key-b679795f4dfac9be9858fb514b5a3313][^key-f4ffba409e49fc71f7790cb9d9364f24]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly provide technical assistance relating to Russia’s vulnerable goods or Russia’s vulnerable technology[^key-31b9b6823eaa559d4f360427b66a9ba2]—

- (a) to a person connected with Russia; or
- (b) for use in Russia.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but—

- (a) it is a defence for a person charged with an offence of contravening paragraph (1)(a) (“**P**”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the person was connected with Russia;
- (b) it is a defence for a person charged with an offence of contravening paragraph (1)(b) to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the goods or technology[^key-36f8c1a3eb3d0234c2f48e87f3f13ebb] were for use in Russia.
