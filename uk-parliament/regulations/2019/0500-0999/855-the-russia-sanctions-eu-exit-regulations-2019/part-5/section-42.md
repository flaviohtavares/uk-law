# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 42

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Making energy-related goods and energy-related technology available[^key-6a43b296916c96e4191e8e34695ac3bc][^key-3df6e0847730523b91b4bc5257656fa3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly make energy-related goods or energy-related technology[^key-ce3b548cc1aba81e3507f07b8922fc25] available for use in Russia.

(1A)[^key-0d44615315c2689461fb7693c464282a] A person must not directly or indirectly make energy-related goods or energy-related technology[^key-b568560d5109d550f5c0ecfc4f11333b] available to a person connected with Russia.

(2) Paragraphs (1) and (1A) are[^key-1f43550e69a8ea2d7e17df1b678eb93e] subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with that offence to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the goods or technology[^key-1b708e5f184414e4671a9942cbab7f60] were for use in Russia.

(4)[^key-3db2b6c59f0ca239b0d186c9a12d5878] A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1A) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with that offence (“**P**”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the person was connected with Russia.
