# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 46IF

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46IF. Interpretation[^c-cfa6031e6b5670717c2014baf5643812]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**metals**” means any thing specified in Schedule 3BA;
- “**third country**” means a country that is not the United Kingdom, the Isle of Man or Russia.
