# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 46X

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46X. Brokering services relating to Schedule 3D revenue generating goods[^c-7788dbe4e19a81e510e80c310a1dcf8e][^c-9a5ba4fb15eab37da53ac8c5c4a9712f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly provide brokering services in relation to any arrangements described in regulation 46W(1)(a) or (b)[^c-5b4bf21a39565a2d2e150c43e3d73bee].

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with that offence to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the brokering services were provided in relation to an arrangement mentioned in that paragraph.
