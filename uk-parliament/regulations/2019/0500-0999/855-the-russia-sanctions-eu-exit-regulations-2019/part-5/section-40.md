# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 40

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Export of energy-related goods[^key-38960a7d23bd3362775dccca49a84adc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-70e244f3cfaf7b4b4de52d5ca1da4a96] The export of energy-related goods to, or for use in, Russia is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 7 (Exceptions and licences).
