# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 39

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Interpretation of this Chapter[^c-e334d7070c4cdd1cd887f322e191668d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Chapter “**Russia**” includes Russia's exclusive economic zone and continental shelf (which terms are to be interpreted in accordance with the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea) [^c23949071].
