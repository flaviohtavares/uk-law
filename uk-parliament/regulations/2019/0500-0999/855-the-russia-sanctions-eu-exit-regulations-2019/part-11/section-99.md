# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 99

> Text as at 2026-05-20.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Other revocations and amendments[^c-e296691a54d034d6c77aedbe3c35536f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Ukraine Financial Sanctions Regulations are revoked.

(2) The Export Control (Russia, Crimea and Sevastopol Sanctions) Order 2014 [^c23949501] is revoked.
