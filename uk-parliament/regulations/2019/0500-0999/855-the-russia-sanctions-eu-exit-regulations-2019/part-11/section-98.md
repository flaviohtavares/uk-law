# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 98

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 98. Revocations of relevant retained EU law[^c-9f6f9abacf4db3c81e5cb58b1ba3dbea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following are revoked—

- (a) Council Regulation (EU) No 269/2014 of 17 March 2014 (concerning restrictive measures in respect of actions undermining or threatening the territorial integrity, sovereignty and independence of Ukraine) [^c23949471];
- (b) Council Regulation (EU) No 692/2014 of 23 June 2014 (concerning restrictive measures in response to the illegal annexation of Crimea and Sevastopol) [^c23949481];
- (c) Council Regulation (EU) No 833/2014 of 31 July 2014 (concerning restrictive measures in view of Russia's actions destabilising the situation in Ukraine) [^c23949491].
