# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 87

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Application of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^key-cbe6f86f2e36a42f7f5af77930813551]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (investigatory powers) [^c23949371] applies to any offence under Part 3 (Finance) , regulations 46Z9B to 46Z9D (maritime transportation of certain oil and oil products)[^key-c63a911aaf2891bfca711c2e6eb6b196] , regulation 54D (legal advisory services)[^key-ed6370fb864667d6c5dcd0753ddc2729] or regulation 67 (finance: licensing offences).
