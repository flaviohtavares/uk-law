# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 80

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Penalties for offences[^key-852d2d0b81921d94a250bbe772e2ddfa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who commits an offence under any provision of Part 3 (Finance), regulation 67 (finance: licensing offences), 57 (Crimean ports direction, Donetsk ports direction or Kherson and Zaporizhzhia ports direction)[^key-7abe06e152f4ab6d886c4b49cd1e732c][^key-a7d297fca03d206d4a85f9a3040f62a6], 57ZA (prohibition on chartering or operating specified ships),[^key-77b8017b4b6f2e98ef4505ba8f4acce1] 57A (prohibition on port entry), 57B(4) (directions prohibiting port entry), 57C(3) (movement of ships) or 57N(1) to (4) (aircraft offences) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-ff403ecf005896111d8b34b81f021c44] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 7 years or a fine (or both).

(2) A person who commits an offence under any provision of Part 5 (Trade) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-3a9694525227f734fc32454d7bee5e79] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 10 years or a fine (or both).

(3) A person who commits an offence under regulation 9(6) or 9B(6)[^key-ad945880ca2f3b8da25dd76918e66fb2] (confidentiality), 67A (director disqualification: licensing offences),[^key-012e369cd26a7a6fd4d0f137ed6790f2] 68 (trade: licensing offences), 68ZA (detained transport asset: licensing offences), 68ZB (ships: licensing offences),[^key-02ab8b83753d506aec6c5a974b1bc279] 68A (aircraft: licensing offences),[^key-be1109173bac6433d4ecbad57f86b5f0] 76(6) (general trade licences: records) or 77(5) (general trade licences: inspection of records) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-b0cc704e2f23366a959a1ca740cb92bf] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or a fine (or both).

(4) A person who commits an offence under regulation 57B(6) (directions prohibiting port entry: confidentiality), 57C(5) (movement of ships: confidentiality), 57N(5) (aircraft: confidentiality),[^key-1051f110046deb99ccae457b7017029a] 70(6) , 70A(6)[^key-dd9d14e707736e32f41593e1abcff744] or 74 (information offences in connection with Part 3) , or 77B (internet services: information offences)[^key-57f0227fbd54b2c9cb5d7086e124554e] is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (or both).

(5) In relation to an offence committed before 2nd May 2022[^M_F_a7e0c32c-52e5-4b79-fff3-30a61c56cc68] the reference in each of paragraphs (1)(a), (2)(a) and (3)(a) to the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-04d168b66d51fd85ac9fe3f0811f7be8] is to be read as a reference to 6 months.
