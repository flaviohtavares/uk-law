# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 88

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Monetary penalties[^key-6a4131daa056dd7624d30c0c110b872d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each provision in Part 5 (Trade) which contains a prohibition imposed for a purpose mentioned in section 3(1) or (2) of the Act and regulation 70A (designated persons: reporting obligations) are[^key-aa4266f35e6ddc4a674169b45d18f36e] to be regarded as not being financial sanctions legislation for the purposes of Part 8 of the Policing and Crime Act 2017 [^c23949381].
