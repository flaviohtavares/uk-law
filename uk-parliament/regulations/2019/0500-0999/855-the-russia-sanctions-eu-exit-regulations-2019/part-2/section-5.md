# The Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2026-05-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^key-a9b11f64201f89f6239e7b2713f2828e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons by name in accordance with regulation 5A (conditions for the designation of persons by name)[^key-33a9d780142df76a61e4c3cf50b754ac] for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 11 to 15 (asset-freeze etc.);
- (aa)[^key-4ac36089bee3e9462aad21fd9b35bb07] regulation 17A (corresponding banking relationships and processing payments)[^key-aeb5e37b5d4aa75981f52d9d9ef0dcbc];
- (ab)[^key-b5e6c5f79ffd870a17a19729f2c6e81a] regulation 18C (trust services);
- (ac)[^key-72414f54ece1a85d6e87c295f85f4f5c] regulation 19B (director disqualification sanctions);
- (b) regulation 20 (immigration);[^key-dfa05a317fe6e604ad683f297570537c]
- (bza)[^key-fd8d17d0837f3059b19e9782e106fc33] regulation 46A (technical assistance relating to aircraft and ships);
- (bzaa)[^key-7244e614d6c2912267153ce7da2410bd] regulation 46Z39 (detained transport assets);
- (bzb)[^key-bda7f0ccabc47df1b1e966ad01e15053] regulation 54A (internet services);
- (ba)[^key-9001babc57a786db7e209ad51ea60091] regulations 57A and 57C to 57E (ships: prohibition on port entry etc.);[^key-c54e403a9e48a06c7905de15011c8262]
- (bb)[^key-6ffb151fe17e983077abdc8c5ea42bce] regulations 57J and 57M (aircraft).

(1A)[^key-868c4001722fe74253190a2cebf74e49] The Secretary of State may provide that persons of a description specified by the Secretary of State are designated persons for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 11 to 15 (asset-freeze etc.);
- (b) regulation 17A (corresponding banking relationships and processing payments)[^key-3c79c1e3544f28370abbc79d8ae2b197];
- (ba)[^key-4b5cb25fecb23164bce38967dd3a8409] regulation 18C (trust services);
- (c) regulation 20 (immigration);
- (d) regulation 46A (technical assistance relating to aircraft and ships);
- (dza)[^key-0f800467760063002cd32873b5307926] regulation 46Z39 (detained transport assets);
- (da)[^key-06b42e3b3ff4faafad1fbfa1111dc88c] regulation 54A (internet services);
- (e) regulations 57A and 57C to 57E (ships: prohibition on port entry etc.);
- (f) regulations 57J and 57M (aircraft).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purposes of different provisions mentioned in paragraph (1) or (1A)[^key-5c53028c8a563292c6327cd269651816].

(3)[^key-d534b81fb7aaeb2517978cf8a1a5b167] For the purposes of these Regulations, persons “designated under regulation 5” for the purpose of a particular regulation means—

- (a) persons who are designated by name under paragraph (1) for the purposes of that particular regulation, and
- (b) where the Secretary of State makes provision under paragraph (1A) that persons of a specified description are designated persons for the purposes of that particular regulation, persons of that description.
