## 10. Meaning of “designated person”[^key-1f5608a7d3b95eacc8bb935e76d0ec36]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter a “**designated person**” means a person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of regulations 11 to 15.
