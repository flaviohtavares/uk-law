# The International Accounting Standards and European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

## 2. Interpretation[^c-163ee22f392edbc26dfbb3f07df9abdf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations, “**financial year**” is to be construed in relation to any amendment to an enactment made by these Regulations, as having the same meaning as in that enactment.
