# The International Accounting Standards and European Public Limited-Liability Company (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 20

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Revocations and transitional provision[^key-ebbef1ff04ebf6518a9acb2d84a5095b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 2 has effect and the instruments listed in that Schedule are revoked, subject to paragraph (2).

(2) Any provisions in the instruments listed in Schedule 2 concerning the application of international accounting standards adopted in accordance with Regulation [(EC) No. 1606/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2002/1606) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the application of international accounting standards [^c23673361] before IP completion day[^key-39c3232f9f4d68745be021caf2533908], apply to the international accounting standards adopted by virtue of regulation 4[^key-d3a097f68131c3ad7c4929431d32393f] as they applied to the international accounting standards adopted by the Commission.
