# The Electricity Network Codes and Guidelines (System Operation and Connection) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation , commencement and extent[^c-1966bb27ff593967582b36030bdc9d51][^c-5e524688306b7bb6a8e5fef8b487c750]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Electricity Network Codes and Guidelines (System Operation and Connection) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019.

(2) These Regulations come into force on exit day.

(3)[^c-6894bd1ce94f0c06cbd589a0d846e5b3] These Regulations do not extend to Northern Ireland.
