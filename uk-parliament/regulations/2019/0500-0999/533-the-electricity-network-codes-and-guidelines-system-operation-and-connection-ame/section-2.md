# The Electricity Network Codes and Guidelines (System Operation and Connection) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-54f7ba71acb9860e149c4c5ff341529d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In these Regulations—

- “**the Electricity Transmission System Operation Regulation**” means Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1485 establishing a guideline on electricity transmission system operation;
- “**the Emergency and Restoration Regulation**” means Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/2196 establishing a network code on electricity emergency and restoration.
