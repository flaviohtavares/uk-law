# The Designs and International Trade Marks (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 10

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Other amendments to legislation[^key-c48f61843b790ac7d5e4fe9364d3d948]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 8, which contains other amendments to legislation, has effect.

(2) Subject to regulation 10A,[^key-50e999adf4af16447c8056d28e38aa53] any application or proceeding under the 1994 Act which was made or commenced before the coming into force of these Regulations shall be dealt with under the 1994 Act as it had effect before paragraph (1) comes into force.
