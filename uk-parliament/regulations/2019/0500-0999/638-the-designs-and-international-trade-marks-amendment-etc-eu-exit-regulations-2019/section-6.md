# The Designs and International Trade Marks (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 6

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. International trade marks[^key-5efea41bf20a7eae3819c9a528c4cf00]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 4 contains amendments to the 1994 Act to make provision for certain international trade marks protected in the European Union to be treated as registered trade marks under the 1994 Act from IP completion day[^key-2eee181fc8bd782b93780217b1dd71f9] and about certain applications for the protection of an international trade mark in the European Union and transformation applications made before IP completion day.
