# The Designs and International Trade Marks (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 3

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Amendments to the Design Regulation and the 2005 Regulations relating to the creation and application of the supplementary unregistered design etc.[^key-8ab12df6d641cb7a02274c236b5f0412]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Design Regulation is amended by Part 1 of Schedule 1 to create the supplementary unregistered design.

(2) The 2005 Regulations are amended by Part 2 of Schedule 1 to apply them to the supplementary unregistered design.
