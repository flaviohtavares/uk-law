# The Zimbabwe (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 49

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Penalties for offences[^c-b78ad2abe3b6a784e442978f7d50ef81][^c-36b9dfd823b99f992c93608608dfd4af]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who commits an offence under any provision of Part 3 (finance) or regulation 36 (finance: licensing offences), is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-be67d3b1fd41bfb8f5376f4901660ca7] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 7 years or a fine (or both).

(2) A person who commits an offence under any provision of Part 5 (trade) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-90d222c2f5b0ba4eb8091043c159de44] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 10 years or a fine (or both).

(3) A person who commits an offence under regulation 9(6) (confidentiality), 36A (director disqualification: licensing offences),[^c-3cc515cc2472b08121cc62d5b9f82282] 37, 45(6) or 46(5) (offences in connection with trade licences) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-fc2d1dc1698c5034760371b48a89be7e] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or a fine (or both).

(4) A person who commits an offence under regulation 39(6) or 43 (information offences in connection with Part 3) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (or both).

(5) In relation to an offence committed before 2nd May 2022[^c-a1b38d44d95640038e740e6934e2e6ec] the reference in each of paragraphs (1)(a), (2)(a) and (3)(a) to the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-7364ccc978357c38e6de410bfc85f98e] is to be read as a reference to 6 months.
