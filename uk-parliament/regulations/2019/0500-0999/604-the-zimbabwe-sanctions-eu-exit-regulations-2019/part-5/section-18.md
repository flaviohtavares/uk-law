# The Zimbabwe (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 18

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Definition of “restricted goods” and “restricted technology”[^c-f79398b0b277e5aa1ee5978f0b2a0db3][^c-33059575561b1bd35b57f2457100a8dc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**restricted goods**” means—
  - (a) internal repression goods, and
  - (b) military goods;
- “**restricted technology**” means—
  - (a) internal repression technology, and
  - (b) military technology.
