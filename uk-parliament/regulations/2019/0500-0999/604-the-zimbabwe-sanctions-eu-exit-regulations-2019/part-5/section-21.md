# The Zimbabwe (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 21

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Export of restricted goods[^key-95fb6e5e71bc7fb747c0ceeaa98c547c][^key-1c9f18848af3ea37ab6568962a44ebd4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of restricted goods to, or for use in, Zimbabwe is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (exceptions and licences).
