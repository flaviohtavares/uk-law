# The Zimbabwe (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 68

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Other revocations and amendments[^key-891ccf56e4fab74cbbb69485519a9d42][^key-ca9879f622023b3047a469f2a1d21f52]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Zimbabwe (Financial Sanctions) Regulations 2009 [^c23947901] are revoked.

(2) The Zimbabwe (Sale, Supply, Export, Technical Assistance, Financing and Financial Assistance and Shipment of Equipment)(Penalties and Licences) Regulations 2004 [^c23947911] are revoked.
