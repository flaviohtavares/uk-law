# The Zimbabwe (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 65

> Text as at 2026-05-13.

## 65. Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008[^c-48d22c5d7e018427f9dc2cc597f9db88][^c-4dcbe67c89a452b799fcc5f3a3afedbd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008 (embargoed destinations) is not to be taken to prohibit anything prohibited by Part 5 (trade).
