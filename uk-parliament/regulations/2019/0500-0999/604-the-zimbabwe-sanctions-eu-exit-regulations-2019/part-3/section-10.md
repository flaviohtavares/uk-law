## 10. Meaning of “designated person” in Part 3[^c-ba7d65e2a448fbbe103cec3879c3b9b7][^c-ff4c198b99d11f31dceb41b628a3b038]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part a “**designated person**” means a person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of regulations 11 to 15.
