# The Zimbabwe (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^key-ea7080a6459ef372b60e39b20d53be94][^key-1a4b8986f403991e3b53f1e9d622fbc2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons by name in accordance with regulation 5A (conditions for the designation of persons by name)[^key-8cdb468dce32aa652350db8604967bec] for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 11 to 15 (finance); [^key-13197cdfc901ad34fe7928cac3603eb0]...
- (aa)[^key-c3f3482214da0a9cf1bf9522140f5685] regulation 16A (director disqualification sanctions);
- (b) regulation 17 (immigration).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purposes of different provisions mentioned in[^key-f0a57d52a901d5183f22ea0d2fe87dd1] paragraph (1).
