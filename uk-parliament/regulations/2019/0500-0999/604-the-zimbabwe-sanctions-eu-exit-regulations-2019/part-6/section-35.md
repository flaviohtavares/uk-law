## 35. Licences: general provisions[^key-212eb1e4b123199d65e840732bc8014b][^key-fd80d0a6b5f795efb2e4aeadbcf0f449]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences , director disqualification licences[^key-dbc33c72582fa2418666f05667131701] and trade licences.

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may —

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^key-f78adfba67204fe0276f3590c380fa15]**P**”), issues[^key-31ebdcf98ab158acd4569982ddf706f6] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^key-09729adb4b9f2a5f6c0ee7a649fff6b3] must give written notice to P[^key-f11c0e33e83a45dd1912c6921a71235d] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
