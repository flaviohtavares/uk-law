## 2. Extent[^key-9494529ff341a0dc85a42d5cfaebfe53]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations extend as follows.

(2) This Part and Part 3 extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(3) Parts 2 and 4 extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

(4) Part 5 extends to Northern Ireland.
