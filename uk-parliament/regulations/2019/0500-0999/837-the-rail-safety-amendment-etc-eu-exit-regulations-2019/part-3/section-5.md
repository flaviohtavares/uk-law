# The Rail Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Revocation of Commission Regulation [(EC) No 653/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/653)[^c-e41ef06b66e926a19b08e8f32516f48e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Regulation [(EC) No 653/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/653) on the use of a common European format for safety certificates and application documents in accordance with Article 10 of Directive [2004/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/49) of the European Parliament and of the Council and on the validity of safety certificates delivered under Directive [2001/14/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2001/14) is revoked, save in respect of the tunnel system.
