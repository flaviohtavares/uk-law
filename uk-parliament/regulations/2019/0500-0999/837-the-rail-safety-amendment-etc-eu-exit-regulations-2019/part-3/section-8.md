# The Rail Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 8

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

## 8. Revocation of Commission Decision 2012/226/EU[^key-5e611a2790a74e191c8e30f48aa2c37c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Decision 2012/226/EU on the second set of common safety targets (notified under document C(2012) 2084) as regards the rail system is revoked.
