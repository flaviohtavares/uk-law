> Text as at 2020-12-31.

## 7. Revocation of Commission Regulation (EU) No 445/2011[^c-d7454490f31a7db400983debf4802fd8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Regulation (EU) No 445/2011 on a system of certification of entities in charge of maintenance for freight wagons and amending Regulation [(EC) No 653/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/653) is revoked.
