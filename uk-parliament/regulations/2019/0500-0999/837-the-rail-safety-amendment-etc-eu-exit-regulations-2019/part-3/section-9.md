# The Rail Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 9

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Revocation of Commission Implementing Decision 2013/753/EU[^key-88349aa7ea720a5f5581156dd191e4dd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Implementing Decision 2013/753/EU amending Decision 2012/226/EU on the second set of common safety targets for the rail system (notified under document C(2013) 8780) is revoked.
