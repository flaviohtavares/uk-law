# The Rail Safety (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 11

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Amendment to Annex XIII to the Agreement on the European Economic Area[^key-f6d12d75f135ec9d8f99dc0f1735653a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Points 42ea, 42eb and 42ed of Annex XIII to the Agreement on the European Economic Area signed at Oporto on 2nd May 1992, so far as that Annex forms part of domestic law by virtue of section 3(2)(b) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018, are omitted.
