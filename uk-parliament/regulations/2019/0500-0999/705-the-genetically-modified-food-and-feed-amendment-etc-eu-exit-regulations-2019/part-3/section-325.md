# The Genetically Modified Food and Feed (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 325

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 325. Amendment of Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2017/1209[^key-999b6dbcdac169cbfd36e5278dd0f836]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2017/1209 authorising the placing on the market of products containing, consisting of, or produced from genetically modified maize Bt11 × 59122 × MIR604 × 1507 × GA21, and genetically modified maize combining two, three or four of the events Bt11, 59122, MIR604, 1507 and GA21 pursuant to Regulation [(EC) No. 1829/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2003/1829) of the European parliament and of the Council on genetically modified food and feed is amended as follows.
