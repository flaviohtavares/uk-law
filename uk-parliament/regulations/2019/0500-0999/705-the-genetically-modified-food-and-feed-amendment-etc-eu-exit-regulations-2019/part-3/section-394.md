# The Genetically Modified Food and Feed (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 394

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 394. Amendment of Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/2046[^M_I_65f32a0b-fe18-48f6-a0da-a80300f596e1][^key-e3c18dc0526fab5ab5105330a3ca9305]
