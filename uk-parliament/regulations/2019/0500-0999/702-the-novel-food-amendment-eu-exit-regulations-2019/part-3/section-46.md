# The Novel Food (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 46

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46.[^c-2825f3db53d0d28a45d630105594ac0b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 9—

- (a) in paragraph 1, for “Commission, the Member States and the Authority”, substitute “ appropriate authority and the Food Safety Authority ”;
- (b) for paragraph 2, substitute—

  > **2.**
  >
  > The duly reasoned safety objections submitted by the Food Safety Authority to the appropriate authority in accordance with Article 15(2) of Regulation (EU) 2015/2283 must include the following information—
  >
  > - (a) the name and description of the traditional food from a third country;
  > - (b) a scientific statement indicating why the traditional food from a third country may pose a safety risk to human health.

  .
