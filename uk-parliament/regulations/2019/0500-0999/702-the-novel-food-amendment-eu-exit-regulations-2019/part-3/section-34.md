# The Novel Food (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 34

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34.[^key-710dfa178611d02f9fc907d05f61c4f9][^key-f94410af57bc87d6e72a007f8c7bcf8f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 28—

- (a) in each place in which it occurs, for “Commission shall” substitute “ appropriate authority must ”;
- (b) in paragraph 1, for “Authority” substitute “ Food Safety Authority ”;
- (c) in paragraph 4, for “the Commission has received the opinion of the Authority” substitute “ the opinion has been received ”.
