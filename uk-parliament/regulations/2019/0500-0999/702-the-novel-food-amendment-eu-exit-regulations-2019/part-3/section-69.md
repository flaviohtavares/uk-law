# The Novel Food (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 69

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69.[^key-4c9b719d40ce807369b0dfb085fc88f6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 5—

- (a) in each place in which it occurs, for “recipient Member State”, substitute “ Food Safety Authority ”;
- (b) in paragraph 4, for “other Member States and the Commission”, substitute “ appropriate authority ”.
