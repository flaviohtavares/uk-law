# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 78

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78.[^key-7748134dd0ce6102e8e908a5224eec74]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 21(2)—

- (a) in the first subparagraph, for the words from “Commission shall” to “Article 37(2)” substitute “ appropriate authority may, by regulations ”;
- (b) in the fourth subparagraph, for the words “Commission may” to the end of the paragraph substitute “ appropriate authority may, by regulations, withdraw such products or substances. ”.
