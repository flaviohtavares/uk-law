# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 73

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73.[^key-a6070f46dc9699bf2cc72f58982b4271]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 16—

- (a) in paragraph 1, in the first subparagraph, for the words from “Commission shall” to “Article 37(2)” substitute “ appropriate authority may, by regulations ”;
- (b) in paragraph 3—
  - (i) in point (a), for the words from “Commission may” to “Article 37(2)” substitute “ appropriate authority may, by regulations ”;
  - (ii) omit point (b);
  - (iii) in point (c), for the last sentence substitute “ The appropriate authority may, by regulations, withdraw such products or substances. ”.
