# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 19

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19.[^c-8c6fad8fadfaeddd6aaffa3da0c56c97]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 78—

- (a) in paragraph 3—
  - (i) for the words from “Commission” to “Article 227” substitute “ appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland[^c-5a658e2287808707811d718a1fd613a6] may make regulations ”;
  - (ii) for “delegated acts”, in the second place it occurs, substitute “ regulations ”;
- (b) in paragraph 4—
  - (i) omit “and Member States”;
  - (ii) for the words from “Commission” to “Article 227” substitute “ appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland may make regulations ”;
- (c) in paragraph 5, for the words from “Commission” to “Article 227” substitute “ appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland may make regulations ”.
