# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 39

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39.[^key-060457a62373ddecb5bc035ac435b512]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 1, in the second subparagraph [^c23706621], after point (b) insert—

> - (c) ‘appropriate authority’ means:
>   - (i) the relevant authority [^key-c1d49a953f2158d8fcfeb2dfe603d3df]..., or
>   - (ii) if consent is given by the relevant authority [^key-c1d49a953f2158d8fcfeb2dfe603d3df]..., the Secretary of State.

.
