# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 29

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29.[^key-806e70e04865f7189a415453c367359d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 186—

- (a) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) for the words from “Commission” to “Article 227” substitute “ Secretary of State may make regulations ”;
  - (ii) for “third” substitute “ other ”;
- (b) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) for “a third”, in both places it occurs, substitute “ another ”;
  - (ii) for the words from “concluded” to “States” substitute “ to which the United Kingdom is a party, the Secretary of State may make regulations laying down the circumstances in which the competent authority is required ”.
