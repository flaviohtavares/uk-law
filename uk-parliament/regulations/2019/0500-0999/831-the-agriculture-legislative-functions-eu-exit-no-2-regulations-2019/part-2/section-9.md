# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 9

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9.[^key-4cc99909e5181820a5cb2b48d8728f49]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 21—

- (a) for the words from “The Commission” to “classification” substitute “ By way of derogation from point C.III of Annex IV, the appropriate authority may make regulations providing for the following criteria for classification of lambs of less than 13kg carcass weight ”;
- (b) omit the second subparagraph.
