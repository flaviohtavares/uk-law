# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 26

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26.[^key-f73f196b69bbe3449f8b07713aef565e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 91—

- (a) for “Commission may adopt implementing acts” substitute “ appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland[^key-2713414fed135074088ffd3a22e26970] may make regulations ”;
- (b) in point (a), omit the words from “on the basis” to the end;
- (c) omit the second subparagraph.
