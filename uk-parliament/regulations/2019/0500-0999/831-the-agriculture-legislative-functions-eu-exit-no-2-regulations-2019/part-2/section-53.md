## 53.[^key-97c9170e432f882464b9927ba5e04d12]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

- (a) omit the first sentence;
- (b) for “Member States” substitute “ relevant authority ”;
- (c) for “Union” substitute “ retained EU ”;
- (d) for “Commission shall adopt implementing acts” substitute “ appropriate authority may make regulations ”;
- (e) omit the last sentence;
