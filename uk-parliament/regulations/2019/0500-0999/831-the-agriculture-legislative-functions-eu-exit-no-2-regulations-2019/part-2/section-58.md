# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 58

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58.[^key-f0bcd6cf728b01e3fbfb28b106122926]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 12—

- (a) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) for the words from the beginning to “shall”, in the second place it occurs, substitute “ Where the appropriate authority decides to buy-in products under Article 13(2)[^key-e90f72e9d982cbba032f15e7c84fe739] of Regulation (EU) No. 1308/2013 or Article 3(2) of Regulation (EU) No 1370/2013[^key-3b4be243315ee3a4c2221e6c916f075f], it must publish a tendering procedure. The tendering procedure must ”;
  - (ii) after point (b) insert—

    > - (c) in the case of a restricted tendering procedure under Article 3(3) of Regulation (EU) No. 1370/2013, the region covered.

    ;
- (b) for paragraph 2 substitute—

  > **2.**
  >
  > The appropriate authority may open a tendering procedure for buying-in of beef by category or region on the basis of the two most recent weekly market prices recorded. The appropriate authority may close the tendering procedure on the basis of the most recent weekly market prices recorded.

  ;
- (c) omit paragraph 3;
- (d) in paragraph 5—
  - (i) in point (a)—
    - (aa) for “a Member State” substitute “ the United Kingdom ”;
    - (bb) for “Member State”, in the second place it occurs, substitute “ United Kingdom ”;
  - (ii) in point (b), for “Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1249/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/1249)” substitute “ Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/1182 and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/1184. ”;
  - (iii) in point (c)—
    - (aa) for “a Member State” substitute “ the United Kingdom ”;
    - (bb) for “that Member State” substitute “ the United Kingdom ”.
