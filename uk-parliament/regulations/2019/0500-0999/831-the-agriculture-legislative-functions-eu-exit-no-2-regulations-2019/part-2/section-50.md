## 50.[^key-88957758b876abb58c9a328ef5923bba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 8—

- (a) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) for “Commission shall adopt implementing acts, laying down” substitute “ relevant authority may publish ”;
  - (ii) omit “Union's”;
  - (iii) omit the last sentence;
- (b) for paragraph 2 substitute—

  > **2.**
  >
  > Following publication of the work programme, the appropriate authority may issue a call for proposals for simple programmes to implement the work programme.
