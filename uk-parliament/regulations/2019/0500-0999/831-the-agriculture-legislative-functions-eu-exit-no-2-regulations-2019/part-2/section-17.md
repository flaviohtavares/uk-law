# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 17

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17.[^key-735850a81e0d2a7eb87fb5fed48d50c2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 75—

- (a) in paragraph 2, for the words from “Commission” to “Article 227” substitute “ appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland[^key-408cfa1aa0d3f2ab7e50ace5bb05e7cc] may make regulations ”;
- (b) in paragraph 6—
  - (i) for the words from “Commission” to “Article 227” substitute “ appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland may make regulations ”;
  - (ii) for “delegated acts”, in the second place it occurs, substitute “ regulations ”;
  - (iii) omit the words from “and shall” to the end.
