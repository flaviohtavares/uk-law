# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 13

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13.[^c-686dc58168d3ec557a9acdd0efc02f1a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 37—

- (a) for the words from “Commission” to “Article 227” substitute “ appropriate authority may make regulations ”;
- (b) in point (a)(ii), for “the additional requirements to be determined by Member States” substitute “ any additional requirements ”;
- (c) in point (c), omit “Union” in both places it occurs;
- (d) in point (d)—
  - (i) in point (i), for “Member States” substitute “ the relevant authority ”;
  - (ii) in point (ii), for “(a), (b), (c) and (i)” substitute “ (b) and (c) ”;
  - (iii) omit points (iii) to (vii), (x) and (xiii);
- (e) in point (e)—
  - (i) in the words before point (i), for “national” substitute “ additional ”;
  - (ii) omit point (iii).
