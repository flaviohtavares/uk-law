# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 56

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56.[^key-551163237c9f2293552d0b8a96cba7c5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 25—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) for “Commission shall adopt implementing acts” substitute “ appropriate authority may make regulations ”
  - (ii) omit “common” in the third place it occurs;
  - (iii) omit the last sentence;
- (b) in the second subparagraph, for “All interested parties shall provide the Commission” substitute “ Proposing organisations must provide the relevant authority ”
