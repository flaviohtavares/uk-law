## 40.[^key-335d9f5bf19f4d3cfa93f2e08ca611fd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

- (a) for “Commission” substitute “ appropriate authority ”;
- (b) for the second sentence substitute “ When necessary, in the light of developments in production and markets, the appropriate authority may make regulations amending the reference thresholds. ”.
