# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 22

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22.[^c-5659f4daa461aee737212814085f88f4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For Article 83(4) substitute—

> **4.**
>
> In order to ensure the correct and transparent application of this Article:
>
> - (a) the appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland[^c-2e4a19df49dd234ecceecb31be268167] may make regulations laying down the conditions for the application of paragraph 1 of this Article;
> - (b) the Secretary of State may make regulations laying down the conditions for the application of paragraphs 2 and 3 of this Article, as well as the conditions for the holding, circulation and use of the products obtained from the experimental practices referred to in paragraph 3 of this Article.

.
