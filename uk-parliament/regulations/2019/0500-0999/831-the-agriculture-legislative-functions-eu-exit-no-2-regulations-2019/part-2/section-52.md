# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 52

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52.[^key-da196e2efbf07c5474e74355af3791b5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 13—

- (a) in paragraph 1, for the words from “Commission” to “Article 22,” substitute “ appropriate authority may make regulations ”;
- (b) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) for “Commission shall adopt implementing acts” substitute “ appropriate authority may make regulations ”;
  - (ii) omit the last sentence.
