# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 6

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6.[^c-5f95cccb3e7977f65a913200025e0fe3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 18—

- (a) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) for the words from “Commission” to “Article 227” substitute “ appropriate authority may, where necessary, make regulations ”;
  - (ii) omit “Union”;
  - (iii) after “prices” insert “ in the United Kingdom ”;
- (b) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) for “Commission may adopt implementing acts” substitute “ appropriate authority may make regulations ”;
  - (ii) omit the second subparagraph;
- (c)[^c-ff8229cd49c126d79beba5dc7d5881be] omit paragraph 3.
