# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 20

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20.[^c-90987206749a574b52e7a0d014097fbf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 79—

- (a) in paragraph 1, for the words from “Commission” to “Article 227” substitute “ appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland[^c-af2978aca837895f18fb274db3066c19] may make regulations ”;
- (b) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) for “adopting the acts referred to in” substitute “ making regulations under ”;
  - (ii) for “Commission” substitute “ appropriate authority in England, Wales or Scotland ”.
