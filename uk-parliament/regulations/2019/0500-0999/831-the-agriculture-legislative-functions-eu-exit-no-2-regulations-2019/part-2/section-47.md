# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019, section 47

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47.[^key-70b7abde0f6635483633d247eb3ddb50]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For Article 4(3) substitute—

> **3.**
>
> The appropriate authority may make regulations laying down detailed rules concerning the visibility of commercial brands during demonstrations or tastings and on information and promotional material, as referred to in paragraph 1, as well as the uniform conditions under which a single brand may be displayed.
