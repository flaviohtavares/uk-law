# The Specific Food Hygiene (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 8

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8.[^c-6b0fb144de35d1572335c931080a0676]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 6—

- (a)[^c-3c0b9f61d354317c67b6fd5c8ba7f6a1] in the heading, for “the Community” substitute “ Great Britain ”;
- (b) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) in point (a), in both places in which it occurs, omit “third”;
  - (ii) in point (b) (ii), omit “or in approved Community establishments”;
  - (iii) in point (c) (iii), omit “Community”.
