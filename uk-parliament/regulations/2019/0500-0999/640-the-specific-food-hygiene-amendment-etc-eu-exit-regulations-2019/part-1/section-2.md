# The Specific Food Hygiene (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-720ecf1f27540fe5266c8a7ad38ca8f5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “**Regulation [(EC) 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853)**” means Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down specific hygiene rules for food of animal origin;
- “**Regulation [(EC) 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/854)**” means Regulation [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/854) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down specific rules for the organisation of official controls on products of animal origin intended for human consumption;
- “**Regulation (EU) 101/2013**” means Commission Regulation (EU) No. 101/2013 concerning the use of lactic acid to reduce microbiological surface contamination on bovine carcases;
- “**Regulation (EU) 2015/1474**” means Commission Regulation (EU) 2015/1474 concerning the use of recycled hot water to remove microbiological surface contamination from carcases;
- “**Regulation (EU) 636/2014**” means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 636/2014 on a model certificate for the trade of unskinned large wild game.
- “[^key-b184db6d61d938aa8078d597e92098b5] **Regulation 2074/2005** ” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2074/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2074) laying down implementing measures for certain products under Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853) of the European Parliament and of the Council and for the organisation of official controls under Regulation [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/854) of the European Parliament and of the Council, derogating from Regulation [(EC) No. 852/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/852) of the European Parliament and of the Council and amending Regulations [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853) and [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/854);

  “**Regulation 2015/1375**” means Commission Implementing Regulation laying down specific rules on official controls for Trichinella in meat;

  “**Regulation 2016/759**” means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/759 drawing up lists of third countries, parts of third countries and territories from which Member States are to authorise the introduction into the Union of certain products of animal origin intended for human consumption, laying down specific requirements, amending Regulation [(EC) No. 2074/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2074) and repealing Decision [2003/812/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2003/812);

  “**Regulation 2017/185**” means Regulation (EU) 2017/185 laying down transitional measures for the application of certain provisions of Regulations [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853) and [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/854) of the European Parliament and of the Council.
