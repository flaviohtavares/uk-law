# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^key-8edb8f2d089fa2a2cddf3c8e3ed98176][^key-64f2eda180939b6da04cc6f77e98ccf1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons [^c23946971] by name for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 11 to 15 (Finance); [^key-57c4e0e4c6f2d60a69221cfd4995f04d]...
- (aa)[^key-b3ac2b1937977cd4dab49bc2e55c160f] regulation 16A (Director disqualification sanctions);
- (b) regulation 17 (Immigration) ; and[^key-0e1ab1f0e825f9791e6acb4a8a42e442]
- (c)[^key-2b1690e706062a164d34f85df4a23670] regulation 27N (technical assistance in relation to aircraft and ships);
- (ca)[^key-0538179fc65e9ca72bdf7d16174a5188] regulation 27P (internet services);
- (d) regulations 29A and 29CA (aircraft);
- (e) regulations 29F, 29H, 29I and 29J (ships: prohibition on port entry etc.).

(1A)[^key-a208545c88dc725a02fde5e6767f322e] The Secretary of State may provide that persons of a description specified by the Secretary of State are designated persons for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 11 to 15 (finance);
- (b) regulation 17 (immigration);
- (c) regulation 27N (technical assistance relating to aircraft and ships);
- (ca)[^key-c66cdcdb56a44da0a724f22e84298ad9] regulation 27P (internet services);
- (d) regulations 29A and 29CA (aircraft);
- (e) regulations 29F, 29H, 29I and 29J (ships: prohibition on port entry etc.).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purposes of different provisions mentioned in[^key-e691be7ab04533debd2341e18909dbc2] paragraph (1) or (1A)[^key-518ed7649ce9ffc28088a32ed97f6100].

(3)[^key-86e0b34ba34e0f877e4c9de58d4f532d] For the purposes of these Regulations, persons “designated under regulation 5” for the purpose of a particular regulation means—

- (a) persons who are designated by name under paragraph (1) for the purposes of that particular regulation, and
- (b) where the Secretary of State makes provision under paragraph (1A) that persons of a specified description are designated persons for the purposes of that particular regulation, persons of that description.
