> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Licences: general provisions[^c-53d2085a6dbdd144cc854ce24037f252][^c-8791d7594579dcad6756feccad10adc0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences, director disqualification licences,[^c-b5737c4f4f25bf4f2cbbd1ce1698c291] trade licences and aircraft licences[^c-4ab1a5199562423f9fec9fa5cab03a86].

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may —

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^c-786ce0ad23111eefc503bbc301901abe]**P**”), issues[^c-d949de26d5ab2ed4a85c051130078033] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^c-4d94f2c6d8b86b4d7036c275df6b9e07] must give written notice to P[^c-0556319ef36cd74de95ed9d008d89d47] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
