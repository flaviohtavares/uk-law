# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 32

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Treasury licences[^key-516639095a909b9d6646d6b0c067866a][^key-e95b16989bccae01b6984aa0e4a7920b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibitions in regulations 11 to 15 (asset-freeze etc) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Treasury under this paragraph.

(1ZA)[^key-f8f43101a5f771432d82700f078dfef1] The prohibitions in 15A (securities and money market instruments) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Treasury under this paragraph.

(1A)[^key-a71d232d971ff0121be95fdcae8fca35] The prohibitions in regulation 15B (loans and credit) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Treasury under this paragraph.

(1B) The prohibition in regulation 15C (insurance and reinsurance services) does not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Treasury under this paragraph.

(1C)[^key-3799e123a72743a760f664d0bd8a3824] The prohibition in regulation 15CA (provision of financial services relating to foreign exchange reserve and asset management) does not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Treasury under this paragraph.

(1D) Without prejudice to the generality of the powers conferred by paragraphs (1) to (1C), a licence issued by the Treasury under those paragraphs may, in particular, authorise acts which would otherwise be prohibited by any of regulations 11 to 15CA for a particular period beginning with—

- (a) the coming into force of the prohibition, or
- (b) where the prohibition relates to a person designated for the purposes of regulations 11 to 15, the date on which the person was designated.

(2) The Treasury may issue a licence under paragraph (1)[^key-a948cee52df61f58e4f9c1557b462ab1] which authorises acts by a particular person only where the Treasury consider that it is appropriate to issue the licence for a purpose set out in Part 1 of[^key-3dbe5e714c7a4629517663bb28694f65] Schedule 3.

(3)[^key-863d69882c2721acf3fc80b5d3360a23] The Treasury may issue a licence under paragraph (1A) which authorises acts by a particular person only where the Treasury consider that it is appropriate to issue the licence for a purpose set out in Part 2 of Schedule 3.

(4) The Treasury may issue a licence under paragraph (1B) which authorises acts by a particular person only where the Treasury consider that it is appropriate to issue the licence for a purpose set out in Part 3 of Schedule 3.

(5)[^key-5ff19c331658f0ae43cd8c9ac3468ced] The Treasury may issue a licence under paragraph (1C) which authorises acts by a particular person only where the Treasury consider that it is appropriate to issue the licence for a purpose set out in Part 4 of Schedule 3.
