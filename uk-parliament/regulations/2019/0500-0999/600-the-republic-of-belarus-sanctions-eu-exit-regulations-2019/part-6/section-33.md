> Text as at 2026-05-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Trade licences[^key-8917412b7d0a569b45d6203f2fb1f635][^key-7b169b00a9f8788c589eced0074419f0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The prohibitions in Chapters 2 to 2C[^key-489973065c7122525e0df2ed30f6afcb] of Part 5 (Trade) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
