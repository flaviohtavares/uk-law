# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 48

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Penalties for offences[^key-501f4e3e4561033b16660692068d2607][^key-993871ecf52da7f09812eb35fa065ea9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who commits an offence under any provision of Part 3 (Finance) ,[^key-777f7ef3d1159766958e053cd62d6f50] regulation 29D(A1) to (3) (aircraft offences), 29F (prohibition on port entry), 29G(4) (directions prohibiting port entry), 29H(3) (movement of ships) or[^key-b60c853a5e4dca20138535adbe8416d4] or regulation 35 (finance: licensing offences), is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-3cf4a83a56eb7b8b3388bada60115249] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 7 years or a fine (or both).

(2) A person who commits an offence under any provision of Part 5 (Trade) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-a351ada08b6cc7976e92a4e1b762421b] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 10 years or a fine (or both).

(3) A person who commits an offence under regulation 9(6) (confidentiality), 35A (director disqualification: licensing offences),[^key-de2f0590ba31b3a5963d480c8ad61052] 36 (trade: licensing offences) and 36A (aircraft: licensing offences)[^key-9832d3325046594a09385e05d121612d], 44(6) or 45(5) (offences in connection with trade licences) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-ac204d9f1ffb849e2a24020a61d4c049] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or a fine (or both).

(4) A person who commits an offence under regulation 29D(4) (confidentiality),[^key-9d6966dcb92f0cfae449744f7eeba1bd] 29G(6) (directions prohibiting port entry: confidentiality), 29H(5) (movement of ships: confidentiality),[^key-9ef12ea18efba2a3bc13dd0be657aadb] 38(6) ,[^key-c172b84f455ec027cf879dbbc6ef7f3e] 38A(6) (designated persons: reporting obligations),[^key-68fcc028d5ecfd2f2219f75a80aa5ea1] 42 (information offences in connection with Part 3) , or 45B (internet services: information offences)[^key-06b1be7e14e85bce14d9a854e062c52e] is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (or both).

(5) In relation to an offence committed before 2nd May 2022[^key-52c1c0871fc9d85ee3787d17ad783e32] the reference in each of paragraphs (1)(a), (2)(a) and (3)(a) to the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-5fc11ec892700644fc5deed66ee86101] is to be read as a reference to 6 months.
