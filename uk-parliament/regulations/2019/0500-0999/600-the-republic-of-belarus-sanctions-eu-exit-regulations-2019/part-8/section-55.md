# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 55

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Application of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^c-ca6b2c93eaf26d8843565a7c72936400][^c-6ba931c2136bc66ed5f7f4684c0f287d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (investigatory powers) [^c23947291] applies to any offence under Part 3 (Finance) or regulation 35 (finance: licensing offences).
