# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 56

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Monetary penalties[^key-9d974dcc3d9aff3c6a8d7045d2ebdaab]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each provision in Part 5 (Trade) which contains a prohibition imposed for a purpose mentioned in section 3(1) or (2) of the Act , or regulation 38A (designated persons: reporting obligations)[^key-562ca6d7b531738afcb71f68090b6493] is to be regarded as not being financial sanctions legislation for the purposes of Part 8 of the Policing and Crime Act 2017.
