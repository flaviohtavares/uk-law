# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 3

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Application of prohibitions and requirements outside the United Kingdom[^c-fe298f0d445fb2a52966ec59d58a07e4][^c-ef4e113784a4754a3b0bf3ccd569ac50]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A United Kingdom person may contravene a relevant prohibition by conduct wholly or partly outside the United Kingdom.

(2) Any person may contravene a relevant prohibition by conduct in the territorial sea.

(3) In this regulation[^c-d4faeb7b7e3a971bfe39ecad7cacc19a] a “**relevant prohibition**” means any prohibition imposed—

- (a)[^c-66eef70c79d206f1d8cb2945e9a08c99] regulation 9(2) (confidential information),
- (b) by Part 3 (Finance),
- (c) by Part 5 (Trade), [^c-34e7b19ed56cd5960271e71aa7fac55d]...
- (ca)[^c-a56dc9655d9c0972b4e481d226ac6073] by regulation 29C(6) (disclosure of confidential information), [^c-e9c86026c9daa8ab4a5a9b6d8d977563]...
- (cb)[^c-7aa0b85ae26400c6e3d9acf2a6ffeba9] by Part 5B (Ships), or
- (d) by a condition of a Treasury licence or a trade licence.

(4) A United Kingdom person may comply, or fail to comply, with a relevant requirement by conduct wholly or partly outside the United Kingdom.

(5) Any person may comply, or fail to comply, with a relevant requirement [^c-b53ca4880dddb4b6a4ce1bef70cd032d]... by conduct in the territorial sea.

(6) In this regulation a “**relevant requirement**” means any requirement imposed—

- (a) by or under Part 7 (Information and records), or by reason of a request made under a power conferred by that Part,[^c-3b1b6875ee3c5ccdfd108379ede37865]
- (b) by a condition of a Treasury licence, a trade licence or an aircraft licence[^c-be9f24ab5778e02258f96e3d3bda69c4],
- (c)[^c-8e61eaef243491736b9354be07784681] by a direction under regulation 29A(2) (direction by air traffic control to operator or pilot of Belarusian aircraft), or
- (d) by a direction under regulation 29H (movement of ships).

(7) Nothing in this regulation is to be taken to prevent a relevant prohibition or a relevant requirement from applying to conduct (by any person) in the United Kingdom.
