## 10. Meaning of “designated person” in Part 3[^key-6959ec71a795169f290f5c171a4ddd0f][^key-fd5f12d72d60c8ad3c569b8919dd1754][^key-69323e20aac84b8219e22c1b52707c06]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part a “**designated person**” means a person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of regulations 11 to 15.
