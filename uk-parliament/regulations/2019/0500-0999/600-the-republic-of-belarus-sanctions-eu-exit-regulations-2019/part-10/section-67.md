# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 67

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Other revocations and amendments[^c-5bac8f459b2bb9e939682fcbe93d9cff][^c-b2ad46569a3a3d4a9c0cf209e4db845e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Belarus (Asset-Freezing) Regulations 2013 [^c23947391] are revoked.

(2) The Export Control (Belarus) and (Syria Amendment) Order 2011 [^c23947401] is revoked.
