## 64. Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008[^key-371f83ec8ccd54adc213438aa7126d54][^key-38c5df19dd4774d77a83ac9e7d850824]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008 (embargoed destinations) is not to be taken to prohibit anything prohibited by Part 5 (Trade).
