# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 17

> Text as at 2026-05-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Immigration[^key-f59035d2617d5318ce006c585d72222c][^key-479bceaca00ae52e2bffcf9dc256f8fc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of this regulation is an excluded person for the purposes of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971 [^c23946981].
