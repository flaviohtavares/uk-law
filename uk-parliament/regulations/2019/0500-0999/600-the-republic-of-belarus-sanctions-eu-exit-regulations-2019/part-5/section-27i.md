# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 27I

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27I. Import of goods originating in, or consigned from, Belarus[^key-35bee44ed08174597adfa5d6b4c2a55e][^key-4a336aacae9f11e2a8b42c338bba279b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The import of goods to which this paragraph applies which originate in Belarus is prohibited.

(2) The import of goods to which this paragraph applies which are consigned from Belarus is prohibited.

(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) apply to—

- (za)[^key-057bf1fa1ff7723f6ef0968afe1d7125] aluminium;
- (a)[^key-776c6af95e1317531254b5c3f128e877] arms and related materiel;
- (b) cement;
- (c) gold and gold jewellery which has been exported from Belarus on or after the relevant day;
- (d) iron and steel products;
- (e) mineral products;
- (f) potash;
- (g) relevant processed gold;
- (h) rubber;
- (i) wood.

(4) Paragraphs (1) and (2) are subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).

(5)[^key-e57bbd9f937d0bf6ab9bcd3ba9d3783a] In this regulation , regulation 27J and regulation 27K[^key-7105b8c8bb5534e61785ad33d9f8a557] “**arms and related materiel**” means—

- (a) military goods, and
- (b) any thing which falls within chapter 93 of the Goods Classification Table, other than military goods.

(6) For the purposes of the definition of “arms and related materiel”, whether a thing “falls within chapter 93 of the Goods Classification Table” is to be interpreted in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2B.

(7)[^key-e1f43f2d7ca3e9c3fd235d4af1b12fe0] In this Chapter, “**relevant day**” means the day on which this paragraph comes into force.
