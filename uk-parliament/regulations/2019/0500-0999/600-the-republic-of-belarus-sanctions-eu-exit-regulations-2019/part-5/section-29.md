# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 29

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Defences[^c-805fed95cc5de667a03ba39e61cdb4a7][^c-c52a7ce2360a178e9317f78c471c870d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where a person relies on a defence under Chapter 2[^c-75b0f426c36a74272e2e724f8aaebb7c] [^c-903dd63985b429028c751f456188b016]..., 2B or 2C of this Part.

(2) If evidence is adduced which is sufficient to raise an issue with respect to the defence, the court must assume that the defence is satisfied unless the prosecution proves beyond reasonable doubt that it is not.
