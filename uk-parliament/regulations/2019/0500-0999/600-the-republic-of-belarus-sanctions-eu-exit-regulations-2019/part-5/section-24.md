# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 24

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Transfer of technology to a place in, or person connected with, Belarus[^c-c427773bb818520b2a1c1afdc0f237fa][^c-c4b056eebc3fc2281afd47e1b960148d][^c-b2443fee33ed16783ca3b27a324c1da0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not—

- (a) transfer technology to which this paragraph applies[^c-0c297b5671e9b41348d9eb50fb7922f7] to a place in Belarus;
- (b) transfer technology to which this paragraph applies[^c-e4a0e8998529599cfbb2d0f9369bac75] to a person connected with Belarus.[^c-83449e4cc8c26c78251e0b0538957fc5]

(1A)[^c-13c89dc635a643c7f0d31a28559db266] Paragraph (1) applies to—

- (a)[^c-14ff72d90c71f5cabc9ce35be172e0a9] chemical and biological weapons-related technology;
- (b) critical-industry technology;
- (c) dual-use technology;
- (d) interception and monitoring technology;
- (e) internal repression technology;
- (f) machinery-related technology;
- (g) military technology;
- (h) oil refining technology;
- (i) quantum computing and advanced materials technology.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but—

- (a) it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening paragraph (1)(a) to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the transfer was to a place in Belarus;
- (b) it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening paragraph (1)(b) (“P”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the person was connected with Belarus.
