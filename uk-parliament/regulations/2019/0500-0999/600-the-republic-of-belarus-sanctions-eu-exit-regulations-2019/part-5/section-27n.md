# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 27N

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27N. Technical assistance relating to aircraft and ships[^key-35bee44ed08174597adfa5d6b4c2a55e][^key-1f55ef0fdc464a824ad11024866dc257]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly provide technical assistance to, or for the benefit of, any designated person, where the assistance relates to an aircraft or a ship.[^key-a5f944fe9ac11420f8de3b73234c5ed6]

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to any technical assistance which is prohibited under regulation 25 (technical assistance relating to certain goods and technology).[^key-5e90bf117ff5c7bed96f2c444b7a11b0]

(3) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).

(4) A person who contravenes the prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with that offence (“P”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the technical assistance was provided to or for the benefit of a designated person[^key-9bf03da3d79d7a06ceff35916105b5aa].

(5)[^key-831bb14f646ae59572603bb32460bcfc] In this regulation—

- “**aircraft**” includes unmanned aircraft and aircraft capable of spaceflight activities;
- “**designated person**” means a person designated under regulation 5 (power to designate persons) for the purposes of this regulation;
- “**ship**” includes every description of vessel (including a hovercraft) used in navigation;
- “**spaceflight activities**” has the meaning given in section 1(6) of the Space Industry Act 2018.
