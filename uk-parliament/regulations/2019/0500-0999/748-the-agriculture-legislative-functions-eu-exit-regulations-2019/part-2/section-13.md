# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 13

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13.[^c-bf1f8be539d507743dde665eadf1a0cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 63—

- (a) in paragraph 4, for the words from “Commission” to “Article 115” substitute “ appropriate authority may make regulations ”;
- (b) in paragraph 5—
  - (i) for “The Commission shall adopt implementing acts” substitute “ The appropriate authority may make regulations ”;
  - (ia)[^c-9fc23c5dde2040e207d62f02c2f016e6] in point (b), omit “in respect of unduly allocated payment entitlements and”.
  - (ii) omit the second subparagraph.
