# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 9

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9.[^c-703e6795645a739e758cc992daa32d6f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 46, for paragraphs 5 and 6 substitute—

> **5.**
>
> The appropriate authority may make regulations laying down specific conditions applying to the information to be booked in the accounts kept by paying agencies.
>
> **6.**
>
> The appropriate authority may make regulations to make provision for the financing of intervention measures in the form of public storage, and other expenditure financed by agricultural support.

.
