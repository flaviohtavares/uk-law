# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 55

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55.[^key-5cccb53adfa219b0f1a514c727b59999]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 16(5)—

- (a) for “Union law” substitute “ statutory requirements ”;
- (b) for “Union instruments” substitute “ EU-derived domestic legislation or retained direct EU legislation ”;
- (c) for the words from “Commission” to “Article 83” substitute “ appropriate authority may make regulations ”;
- (d) omit “Union” in the third place it occurs.
