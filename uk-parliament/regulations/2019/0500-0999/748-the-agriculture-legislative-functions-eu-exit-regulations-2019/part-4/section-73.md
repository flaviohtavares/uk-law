# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 73

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Amendment of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013[^c-aaaebad26117469c2f98a94561dddc0e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and laying down general provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund as amended by the European Structural and Investment Funds Common Provisions (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 [^c23689591] is amended in accordance with regulations 74 to 86.
