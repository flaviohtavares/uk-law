# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 67

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67.[^c-6996c28f6787d5eebe91515c0332a822]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 47(6)—

- (a) for “secure the financial interests of the Union” substitute “ proper management of public money ”;
- (b) for the words from “Commission” to “Article 83” substitute “ appropriate authority may make regulations ”.
