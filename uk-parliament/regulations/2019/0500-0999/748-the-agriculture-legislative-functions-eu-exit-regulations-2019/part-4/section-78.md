# The Agriculture (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 78

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78.[^key-9f151bfbc62c183f5340b7c2868629aa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 38—

- (a) in paragraph 3, in the second subparagraph—
  - (i) for “Commission shall adopt implementing acts” substitute “ appropriate authority may make regulations in relation to support for rural development ”;
  - (ii) omit the second sentence;
- (b) in paragraph 4, omit the final subparagraph;
- (c) in paragraph 10—
  - (i) for “The Commission shall adopt implementing acts” substitute “ The appropriate authority may make regulations in relation to support for rural development ”;
  - (ii) omit the second sentence.
