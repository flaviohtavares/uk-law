# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Amendments to Article 21

In Article 21—

- (a) for “The competent authorities” substitute “A competent authority”;
- (b) for “they have” substitute “it has”;
- (c) omit “or Community”.
