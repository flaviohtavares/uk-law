# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 111

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 111. Amendment to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/1245

(1) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/1245 setting out a preliminary correlation table between codes of the Combined Nomenclature provided for in [Council Regulation (EEC) No 2658/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/2658) and entries of waste listed in Annexes III, IV and V to Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013) of the European Parliament and of the Council on shipments of waste is amended as follows.

(2) Omit Article 2 and the words “This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.”.
