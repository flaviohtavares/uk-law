# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Amendments to Article 6

(1) Article 6 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 4, in the second subparagraph—

- (a) for “into the Community” substitute “from a country other than an EU country”;
- (b) omit “in the Community”.

(3) In paragraph 7 omit “within the Community”.

(4) Omit paragraph 9.
