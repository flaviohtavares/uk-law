# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 95

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 95. Substitution of Article 55

For Article 55 substitute—

> **Article 55 — Designation of frontier crossing points**
>
> The Secretary of State may designate places at which waste entering or leaving the United Kingdom is controlled. If the Secretary of State decides to designate such places, a shipment of waste must not be allowed to use any other places within the United Kingdom for the purposes of entering or leaving the United Kingdom.
