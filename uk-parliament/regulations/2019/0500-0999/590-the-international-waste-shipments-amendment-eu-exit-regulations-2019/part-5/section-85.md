# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Amendments to Article 45

(1) Article 45 is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, after “imports from a” insert “non-EU,”.

(3) In the words before point (a), for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom”.

(4) In point (a), after “from a” insert “non-EU”.

(5) In point (b), after “through any” insert “non-EU”.
