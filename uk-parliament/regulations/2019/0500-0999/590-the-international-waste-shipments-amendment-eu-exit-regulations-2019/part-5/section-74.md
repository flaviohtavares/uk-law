# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Amendments to Article 36

(1) Article 36 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1, in the words before point (a)—

- (a) for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) for “countries” substitute “non-EU countries”.

(3) In paragraph 3—

- (a) for the words from “Member States” to “provisions” substitute “A competent authority may, in an exceptional case, adopt a procedure”;
- (b) for “91/689/EEC” substitute “2008/98/EC[^f00040]”;
- (c) omit the words from “of 3<sup> </sup>May 2000” to the end.

(4) In paragraph 4—

- (a) for “91/689/EEC”, in the first place it occurs, substitute “2008/98/EC”;
- (b) for the words “Article 1(4), second indent, of [Directive 91/689/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0689)” substitute “Articles 3(2) and 7 of [Directive 2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098)”.

(5) After paragraph 5 insert—

> **6.** For the purposes of this Article, Liechtenstein shall be deemed to be a country to which the OECD Decision applies.
