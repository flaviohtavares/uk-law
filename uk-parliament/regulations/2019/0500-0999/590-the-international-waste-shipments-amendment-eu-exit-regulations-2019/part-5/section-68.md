# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Amendments to Article 32

(1) Article 32 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Community” substitute “United Kingdom”;
- (b) for “third” substitute “non-EU”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) for the words from “Community” to “Member State” substitute “United Kingdom,”;
- (b) for “third” substitute “non-EU”.
