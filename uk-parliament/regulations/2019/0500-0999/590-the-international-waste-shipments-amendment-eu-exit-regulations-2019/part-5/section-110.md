# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 110

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 110. Amendments to Regulation [(EC) No 1418/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1418)

(1) [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1418/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1418) concerning the export for recovery of certain waste listed in Annex III or IIIA to Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013) of the European Parliament and of the Council to certain countries to which the OECD Decision on the control of transboundary movements of wastes does not apply is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1, after “Export” insert “from the United Kingdom”.

(3) In Article 1a, omit “by the Commission”.

(4) Omit Article 3 and the words “This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.”.
