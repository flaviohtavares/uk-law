# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 103

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 103. Amendments to endnotes to provisions of Annex 1C

(1) The endnotes to provisions of Annex 1C are amended as follows.

(2) In the endnote for “consignee” in paragraph 6 of Annex 1C, for “Outside the European Community” substitute “In non-EU countries”.

(3) In the endnote for “notifier” in paragraph 13 of Annex 1C, for “Outside the European Community” substitute “In non-EU countries”.

(4) In the endnote for “broker” in paragraph 15 of Annex 1C, for “some third” substitute “non-EU”.

(5) In the endnote for “producer of the waste” in paragraph 20 of Annex 1C, for “Outside the European Community” substitute “In non-EU countries”.

(6) In the endnote for paragraph 22 of Annex 1C, for “European Community”, in each place it occurs, substitute “United Kingdom”.

(7) In the second endnote for paragraph 26 of Annex 1C, for “Outside the European Community” substitute “In non-EU countries”.
