# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 92

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 92. Amendments to Article 51

For Article 51 substitute—

> **Article 51 — Reports**
>
> **1.** The Secretary of State must, before the end of each calendar year—
>
> - (a) prepare the report for the previous calendar year referred to in Article 13(3) of the Basel Convention; and
> - (b) transmit the report to the Secretariat of the Basel Convention in accordance with that Article.
>
> **2.** The Secretary of State must, before the end of each calendar year—
>
> - (a) prepare a further report for the previous calendar year based on the questionnaire and table in Annex 9; and
> - (b) publish the further report in a manner which the Secretary of State considers appropriate.
