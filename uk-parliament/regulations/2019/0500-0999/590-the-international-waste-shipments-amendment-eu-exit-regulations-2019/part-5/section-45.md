# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Amendments to EU Waste Shipments Regulation

Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013) of the European Parliament and of the Council on shipments of waste is amended in accordance with regulations 46 to 109.
