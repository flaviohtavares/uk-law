# The International Waste Shipments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Amendment to regulation 16

In regulation 16 as it forms part of the law of each of Northern Ireland and Scotland, for “Paragraph 4(1)(b) of Schedule 4 to the Waste Management Licensing Regulations 1994” substitute “Paragraph 6(1)(c) of Schedule 4 to the Waste Management Licensing (Scotland) Regulations 2011[^f00007]”.
