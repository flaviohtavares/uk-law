# The Pesticides (Maximum Residue Levels) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-af46cfb71797ee96b1cce9a7b2f3619b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pesticides (Maximum Residue Levels) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force on exit day.

(2) In these Regulations, “**Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396)**” means Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) of the European Parliament and of the Council on maximum residue levels of pesticides in or on food and feed of plant and animal origin.
