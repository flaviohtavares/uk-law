# The Pesticides (Maximum Residue Levels) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 1

## SCHEDULE 1 — Transitional provisions and savings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 15

### PART 1 — Interpretation

#### 1. Interpretation[^c-42a03dc5e9e40b34a1e1424885cc037c]

In this Schedule—

- “competent authority” and “constituent territory” have the meanings given in Article 3A of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) (as inserted by regulation 3(5));
- “**MRLs register**” has the meaning given by Article 3(2)(zb) of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) (as inserted by regulation 3(4)(c)(i)).

### PART 2 — Existing MRLs etc. under Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396)

#### 2. Existing MRL product list[^c-597901e998258089ffa831bdf5dec42e]

The list of products in Parts A and B of Annex 1 to Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) as it had effect immediately before IP completion day[^c-72681a4fc669676c0bed8e5a9eda4c20] is taken to be the list of products established by each competent authority in relation to its constituent territory in accordance with Article 4(1) of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396).

#### 3. Existing MRLs under Annexes 2 and 3 to Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396)[^c-028332a680831d4f03b8dd8134fc7cb6]

(1) In accordance with this paragraph, an MRL which immediately before IP completion day[^c-e7f786b2ccbe348a9776610a42265c2e] is set out in an entry in a table in Annex 2 or 3 to Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) as it has effect immediately before IP completion day is taken to have been set by each competent authority in relation to its constituent territory—

- (a) for an MRL set out in an entry in the table in Annex 2, under Articles 14(1B)(a) and 15(2)(b) of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396);
- (b) for an MRL set out in an entry in the table in Part A of Annex 3, under Articles 14(1B)(a) and 15(2)(a)(i) of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396);
- (c) for an MRL set out in an entry in the table in Part B of Annex 3, under Articles 14(1B)(a), 15(2)(a)(ii) and 16 of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396).

(2) An MRL to which sub-paragraph (1) applies is taken to apply from the date which it applied from in accordance with a regulation made under Article 14(1) of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) as it had effect immediately before IP completion day, except in accordance with sub-paragraph (3).

(3) For the purposes of Article 15(3) of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396), an MRL to which sub-paragraph (1)(b) applies is taken to apply from IP completion day.

(4) In sub-paragraph (1), “Annex 2” and “**Annex 3**” means Annex 2 and 3 respectively to Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) as it has effect immediately before IP completion day and read in accordance with paragraph 4.

(5) Sub-paragraph (6) applies where an MRL to which sub-paragraph (1) applies is immediately before IP completion day subject to a requirement for the European Commission—

- (a) to take specified information into account when reviewing that MRL if the information is submitted by a specified date, or the lack of that information if it is not submitted by that date; or
- (b) to take into account the commercial availability of a specified reference standard when reviewing that MRL by a specified date, or the unavailability of it if it is not commercially available by that date.

(6) Where this sub-paragraph applies, on and after IP completion day the MRL is taken to be subject to the requirement as modified as follows—

- (a) a reference in the requirement to the Commission is to be read as a reference to the competent authority;
- (b) if the specified date occurred before IP completion day and on or before that specified date—
  - (i) the specified information was submitted to the European Commission, the specified date is to be read as the date eight months after the day on which IP completion day falls[^c-b0108be550ccde2a2b6b7f8812e311f3];
  - (ii) the specified information was not submitted to the European Commission, the requirement is to be read as a requirement on the competent authority to take the lack of that information into account when reviewing the MRL.

(7) For the purposes of sub-paragraphs (5) and (6), a date, a reference standard or information is “specified” if it is specified in the requirement.

### PART 3 — Ongoing applications for MRLs

#### 10. Ongoing applications under Article 7 of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396)[^c-9cef4b95c329e870021e2145518031b0]

(1) This paragraph applies to an application where—

- (a) before IP completion day[^c-62161a787fca0b2996fcc8e59fb8ce2a], that application was submitted under Article 7 of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) as it had effect immediately before IP completion day to the United Kingdom for evaluation, and
- (b) immediately before IP completion day, a regulation or decision adopted under Article 14(1) of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) as it had effect immediately before IP completion day in relation to that application has not entered into force.

(2) An application to which this paragraph applies is taken as being made under Article 7 of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) on the date on which it was made, to each competent authority in relation to its constituent territory.

(3) Anything done before IP completion day in relation to an application to which this paragraph applies—

- (a) by the United Kingdom as the evaluating member State;
- (b) by the European Food Safety Authority under Article 10 or 11 of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) as it had effect immediately before IP completion day;

is taken to have been done by the competent authority.

### PART 4 — Ongoing MRL assessments

#### 11. Ongoing EFSA assessments under Article 12 of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396)[^c-5d71522351cee81b303225519abe8096]

(1) This paragraph applies where—

- (a) before IP completion day[^c-3ab8e3946525bb791ef48af1301aa8af], the European Food Safety Authority is required to provide a reasoned opinion in respect of an active substance, in accordance with Article 12(1) of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) as it had effect immediately before IP completion day, and
- (b) immediately before IP completion day, either—
  - (i) such an opinion has not been provided in accordance with Article 12(1) of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) as it had effect immediately before IP completion day, or
  - (ii) such an opinion has been provided, but a regulation or decision made as a result of that reasoned opinion under Article 14(1) of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) as it had effect immediately before IP completion day has not come into force.

(2) A competent authority may produce a reasoned opinion within a period of 36 months beginning with the day after the day on which IP completion day falls[^c-c04f87ed0d4c3c8b41d4ccbe3a3e5ba7] in respect of that active substance in relation to its constituent territory, except where sub-paragraph (3) applies.

(3) Where at the end of the 36 month period described in sub-paragraph (2) there are outstanding renewals of authorisations under Article 43 of Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107) relating to that active substance in relation to its constituent territory, a competent authority may instead produce a reasoned opinion before the end of the period of 6 months beginning with the date on which the last of those outstanding renewals is concluded.

(4) Articles 12(3) to (6) and 14 of Regulation [(EC) No 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/396) apply to a reasoned opinion under sub-paragraph (2) as they apply to a reasoned opinion under Article 12(1) of that Regulation.

(5) In providing a reasoned opinion under sub-paragraph (2), the competent authority may also consider relevant information provided by an interested person, including (but not limited to)—

- (a) the GAP;
- (b) evidence of an authorisation;
- (c) relevant assessments undertaken in other countries;
- (d) data required by regulations made under Article 8(4) of Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107), including, where appropriate, toxicological data and data on routine analytical methods for use in control laboratories, as well and plant and animal metabolism data.

(6) In sub-paragraph (5), “**interested person**” includes manufacturers, growers, importers and producers of products listed in a list in Part 1 of the MRLs register in relation to the competent authority's constituent territory.

(7) In this Article, “**Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107)**” means Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market.
