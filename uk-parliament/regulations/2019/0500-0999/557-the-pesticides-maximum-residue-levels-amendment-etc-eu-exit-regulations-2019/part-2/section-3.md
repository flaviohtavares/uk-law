# The Pesticides (Maximum Residue Levels) (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 3

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Chapter 1[^c-a04693ddd80061f10644adf66ffe462c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Chapter 1 is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1, omit “and harmonised Community provisions”.

(3) In Article 2—

- (a) in paragraphs 1, 2 and 3, for “covered by Annex I” substitute “ listed in Part 1 of the MRLs register in relation to a constituent territory ”;
- (b) in paragraph 3—
  - (i) omit “to third countries”;
  - (ii) omit “third” in the second place it occurs;
- (c) omit paragraph 4.

(4) In Article 3—

- (a) for the heading substitute “ **Definitions: general** ”;
- (b) in paragraph 1, for “Article 2, points 1 and 4 of Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1991/414) [^c23667921]” substitute “ Article 2(1) and (2) of Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107) ”;
- (c) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) before point (a) insert—

    > - (za) ‘Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107)’ means Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market;
    > - (zb) ‘MRLs register’ means the register maintained in accordance with Article 46A;
    > - (zc)[^c-a2991994cc1552012015fd3677810f64] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

    ;
  - (ii) in point (a), in the second sentence—
    - (aa) for the words from “, in conformity with” to “in a given climate zone” substitute “ of the principles of integrated pest management referred to in Article 14 of and Annex 3 to Directive [2009/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/128) of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a framework for Community action to achieve the sustainable use of pesticides [^c23667931] ”;
    - (bb)[^c-7a9135677b6df5009816a06699b00779] at the end, insert—

      > , and for these purposes Directive [2009/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/128) is to be read as if—
      >
      > - (i) Article 3(10)(b) were omitted;
      > - (ii) in Article 14—
      >   - — obligations on Member States were obligations on the competent authorities;
      >   - — paragraph 3 were omitted.

      ;
  - (iii) in point (c)—
    - (aa) omit “as defined in Article 2, point 1 of Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1991/414)”;
    - (bb) for “covered by Annex I to this Regulation” substitute “ listed in Part 1 of the MRLs register in relation to a constituent territory ”;
  - (iv) in point (g)—
    - (aa) in the first indent, for “the Community” substitute “ a constituent territory ”;
    - (bb) in the second indent, omit “Community”.

(5) After Article 3 insert—

> **Article 3A — Definitions: “competent authority” and “constituent territory”**
>
> **1.**
>
> In this Regulation, a reference to a competent authority or a constituent territory is to be interpreted in accordance with the provisions of this Article.
>
> **2.**
>
> The Secretary of State is the competent authority for the constituent territory of England.
>
> **3.**
>
> The Welsh Ministers are the competent authority for the constituent territory of Wales.
>
> **4.**
>
> The Scottish Ministers are the competent authority for the constituent territory of Scotland.
>
> **5.[^c-cc2b0195022a2b06c4ff348986c361d3]**
>
> . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

.

(6) In Article 4—

- (a) in the heading, omit “harmonised”;
- (b) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) in the first sentence, for the words from “harmonised MRLs” to the end substitute “ MRLs apply in relation to a constituent territory must be established and maintained by the competent authority in a list in Part 1 of the MRLs register ”;
  - (ii) omit the second sentence;
  - (iii) in the third sentence—
    - (aa) for “Annex I” substitute “ A list in Part 1 of the MRLs register in relation to a constituent territory ”;
    - (bb) omit “harmonised”;
- (c) for paragraph 2 substitute—

  > **2.**
  >
  > A competent authority may add, amend or remove an entry from the list in Part 1 of the MRLs register accordingly as the competent authority considers appropriate.
  >
  > **3.**
  >
  > The Secretary of State may exercise a function under this Article instead of the competent authority—
  >
  > - (a) in relation to Wales, with the consent of the Welsh Ministers;
  > - (b) in relation to Scotland, with the consent of the Scottish Ministers;
  > - (c)[^c-55fcf035bab37733dbd76ad54754e95d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

  .

(7) For Article 5 substitute—

> **Article 5 — Active substances for which no MRLs are required**
>
> **1.**
>
> A competent authority must establish and maintain a list in Part 4 of the MRLs register of active substances evaluated under Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107) for which MRLs are not required in relation to its constituent territory, taking into account the uses of those active substances and the matters referred to in Article 14(2)(a), (c) and (d).
>
> **2.**
>
> A competent authority must update the MRLs register accordingly as soon as reasonably practicable—
>
> - (a) after first establishing a list in accordance with paragraph 1, and
> - (b) after adding to or removing an entry from that list.
>
> **3.**
>
> The Secretary of State may exercise a function under this Article instead of the competent authority—
>
> - (a) in relation to Wales, with the consent of the Welsh Ministers;
> - (b) in relation to Scotland, with the consent of the Scottish Ministers;
> - (c)[^c-131526f3a82bbdc4dcc013657745a711] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

.
