# The Ozone-Depleting Substances and Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 35A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35A. The Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases Regulations 2015[^c-748e360285feb94142d8859c85d5e3b7]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases Regulations 2015, as they apply in Northern Ireland, are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1(2), after “1998” insert “or they deal with controls on gases, products and equipment traded between Great Britain and Northern Ireland”.

(3) In regulation 6, for “the United Kingdom” substitute “Northern Ireland”.

(4) In regulation 7(1), for “the United Kingdom” substitute “Northern Ireland”.

(5) In regulation 28(1), for “the United Kingdom” substitute “Northern Ireland”.
