## 44. Article 11 (restrictions on the placing on the market)[^c-6b03f6ccf33b76dd09703bc0ed6a75ed]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 11 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 3—

- (a) in the first subparagraph, omit the words from “Following” to “Member State and” and begin the sentence with “Taking”;
- (b) for “the Commission may, exceptionally, by means of implementing acts,” substitute “ the appropriate authority may, exceptionally, by regulations ”;
- (c) omit the second subparagraph.

(3) Omit paragraph 6.
