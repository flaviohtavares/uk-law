# The Ozone-Depleting Substances and Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 49

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Article 17 (registry)[^c-45e118ee778ce99f43ebde607498aa1f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 17 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) for “1 January 2015” substitute “IP completion day[^c-1e1cc14df0d4aadbe67a2b87be14f892]”;
  - (ii) for “Commission” substitute “appropriate authority”;
- (b) in the third subparagraph—
  - (i) for the first reference to “Commission” substitute “appropriate regulator”;
  - (ii) for the second reference to “Commission” substitute “appropriate authority”.

(3) In paragraph 2, in the first sentence—

- (a) for “Commission” substitute “appropriate authority”;
- (b) for “means of implementing acts” substitute “regulations”;
- (c) omit the second sentence.

(4) In paragraph 3—

- (a) for “Commission” substitute “appropriate regulator”;
- (b) omit “via the registry”.

(5) Omit paragraph 4.
