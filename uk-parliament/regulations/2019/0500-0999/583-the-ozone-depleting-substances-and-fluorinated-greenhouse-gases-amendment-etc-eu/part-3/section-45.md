# The Ozone-Depleting Substances and Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 45

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Article 12 (labelling and product and equipment information)[^key-c9f3dc2cde949caecd5927985310e4e4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 12 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 4, omit the second subparagraph.

(3) In paragraph 14—

- (a) in the first sentence—
  - (i) for “Commission may, by means of implementing acts” substitute “ appropriate authority may, by regulations ”;
  - (ii) omit the words from “and may repeal” to “Regulation [(EC) No 842/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/842)”;
- (b) omit the second sentence.

(4) In paragraph 15, for the words from “Commission shall” to “amending” substitute “ appropriate authority may, by regulations, amend ”.

(5) After paragraph 15 insert—

> **6.**
>
> Labels, markings on packages and accompanying documents shall appear in English and may also appear in other languages.
