# The Ozone-Depleting Substances and Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 47

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Article 15 (reduction of the quantity of hydrofluorocarbons placed on the market)[^c-36299333307bd22c5e0a265d0bd37640]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 15 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Commission” substitute “appropriate regulator”;
- (b) for “the Union” substitute “any part of Great Britain[^c-8c1d4b9916104d62ad3cfb6df4f845de]”.

(3) In paragraph 2, in the second subparagraph, in points (a) and (c), for “the Union”, in each place it occurs, substitute “any part of Great Britain[^c-0105816238cea69b37c15389dd79d46f]”.

(4) In paragraph 4—

- (a) in the first subparagraph, in the words before point (a)—
  - (i) omit the words from “Following” to “Member State and” and begin the sentence with “Taking”;
  - (ii) for “the Commission may, exceptionally, by means of implementing acts” substitute “the appropriate authority may, exceptionally, by regulations”;
- (b) omit the second subparagraph.
