# The Ozone-Depleting Substances and Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 41

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Article 8 (recovery)[^key-07785d4866f9069d0438c9046102f224]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 8, in paragraph 3—

- (a) in the second subparagraph, for the words from “Directive” to “Council” substitute “the Motor Vehicles (Refilling of Air Conditioning Systems by Service Providers) Regulations 2009 [^c23643371];
- (b) in the third subparagraph, for “Directive [2006/40/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2006/40)” substitute “ the Motor Vehicles (Refilling of Air Conditioning Systems by Service Providers) Regulations 2009 ”.
