# The Ozone-Depleting Substances and Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 61

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1497/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/1497)[^c-3059446897ef4f690056f53a23249447]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1497/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/1497) establishing, pursuant to Regulation [(EC) No 842/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/842) of the European Parliament and of the Council, standard leakage checking requirements for stationary fire protection systems containing certain fluorinated greenhouse gases, after Article 8 omit the words “This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States”.
