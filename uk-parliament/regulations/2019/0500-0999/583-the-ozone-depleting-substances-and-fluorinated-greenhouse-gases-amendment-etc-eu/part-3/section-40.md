# The Ozone-Depleting Substances and Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 40

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Article 6 (record keeping)[^key-d3095dffab2b18d9f87d30f013eb5590]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 6 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 2—

- (a) in the first subparagraph, for “competent authorities of the Member States” substitute “ appropriate regulator ”;
- (b) in the second subparagraph—
  - (i) for the words from “competent” to “Commission” substitute “ appropriate regulator ”;
  - (ii) for “Directive [2003/4/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2003/4) of the European Parliament and of the Council or Regulation [(EC) 1367/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1367) of the European Parliament and of the Council” substitute “ the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 [^c23643351] or the Environmental Information (Scotland) Regulations 2004 [^c23643361] ”.

(3) In paragraph 3, in the third subparagraph—

- (a) for the words from “competent” to “Commission” substitute “ appropriate regulator ”;
- (b) for “Directive [2003/4/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2003/4) or Regulation [(EC) 1367/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1367)” substitute “ the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 or the Environmental Information (Scotland) Regulations 2004 ”.

(4) In paragraph 4—

- (a) in the first sentence, for “Commission may, by means of an implementing act” substitute “ appropriate authority may, by regulations ”;
- (b) omit the second sentence.
