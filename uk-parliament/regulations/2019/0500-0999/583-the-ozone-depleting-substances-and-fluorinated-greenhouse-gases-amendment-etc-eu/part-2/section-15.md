# The Ozone-Depleting Substances and Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 15

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Article 16 (release for free circulation in the Community of imported controlled substances)[^key-e8d51ec403ed089fd9c7deaf9fcbef38]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 16 is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “the Community” substitute “any part of Great Britain[^key-c69ee285916152f57dd68a1c4a1e3396]”.

(3) In paragraph 1, in the first subparagraph—

- (a) for “the Community” substitute “any part of Great Britain[^key-867e11dcb3421049f09358f7229ec66f]”;
- (b) for “Commission” substitute “appropriate regulator”;
- (c) for “1 January 2010 to 31 December 2010” substitute “the day after that on which IP completion day[^key-25801cb3697f723c4ae1adabeafa876c] falls to 31 December 2021[^key-a049302a8a1108f9bd927117bddae792]”;
- (d) omit the words from “in accordance” to the end of the sentence.

(4) In paragraph 2, for “Commission”, in each place it occurs, substitute “appropriate regulator”.
