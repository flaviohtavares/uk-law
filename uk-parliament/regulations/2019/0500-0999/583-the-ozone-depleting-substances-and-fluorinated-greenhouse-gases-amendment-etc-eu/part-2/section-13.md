# The Ozone-Depleting Substances and Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 13

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Article 14 (transfer of rights and industrial rationalisation)[^key-050fe3ee6e0c686066b10c1ace6fed2a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 14 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “the Community” substitute “any part of Great Britain[^key-abcec063ad2eccb17870e083ec0d2aad]”;
- (b) for “Commission” substitute “appropriate regulator”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) in the first sentence—
  - (i) for the words from “competent authority” to “situated” substitute “appropriate authority”;
  - (ii)[^key-430e6452b36132fd2ac40e08e428c6fc] for “the Member State concerned” substitute “Great Britain”;
  - (iii) omit “of that Member State”;
  - (iv) omit “its”;
- (b) omit the final sentence.

(4) Omit paragraph 3.

(5) In paragraph 4—

- (a) for “Commission may” substitute “Secretary of State may, by regulations,”;
- (b) omit the words from “both” to “situated and”;
- (c)[^key-f9c27628f71b59a2783e0f92f0704f27] for “Community” substitute “Great Britain”.
