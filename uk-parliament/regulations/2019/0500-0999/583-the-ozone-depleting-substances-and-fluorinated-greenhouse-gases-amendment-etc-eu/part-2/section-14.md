# The Ozone-Depleting Substances and Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 14

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Article 15 (imports of controlled substances or of products and equipment containing or relying on controlled substances)[^key-38ed8dfaf8c3252914af68026c6e2602]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 15 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 2(g), for “competent authority of the Member State concerned” substitute “appropriate regulator”.

(3) In paragraph 3—

- (a)[^key-4ce36391fab52aacd78cbe807c45e698] for “the customs territory of the Community”, in each place it occurs, substitute “Great Britain”;
- (b) for “as referred to in Regulation [(EC) No 450/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/450)” substitute “under the Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018 [^c23643301]”;
- (c) for “the Community” substitute “any part of Great Britain[^key-b31ee58c0c6c76ffff25aef0af0b441a]”;
- (d) for “Commission” substitute “Environment Agency”.
