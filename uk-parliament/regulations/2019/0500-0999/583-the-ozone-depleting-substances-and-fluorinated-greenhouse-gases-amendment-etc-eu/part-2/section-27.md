# The Ozone-Depleting Substances and Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 27

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Article 27 (reporting by undertakings)[^key-3d6995351000107859b2ba3ae3958286]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 27 is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-cd73d8285dc67f1ff91697157f7abb78] In paragraph 1, for the words from “Each year” to “concerned,” substitute “By 31 March 2022, and by 31 March each year thereafter, each undertaking must communicate to the appropriate regulator”.

(3) In paragraphs 2(b), (c) and (h), for “the Community” substitute “any part of Great Britain[^key-082c329036e9fba66004b6a51671d9cb]”.

(4) In paragraphs 3(a) and (e), for “the Community” substitute “any part of Great Britain[^key-f2d20f504b0348c8bfada10f7377d414]”.

(5) In paragraph 4(c), for “the Community” substitute “any part of Great Britain[^key-3064c74f3439eb9f7d00fd5b214c8d51]”.

(6) In paragraph 7—

- (a)[^key-d36fa8c06c999f06750bd20c45ee2037] for the words from “Each year” to “concerned,” substitute “By 31 March 2022, and by 31 March each year thereafter, each producer or importer which holds a licence under Article 10(6) must, for each substance for which an authorisation has been received, report to the appropriate regulator”;
- (b) for “the Community” substitute “any part of Great Britain[^key-7857c2456c5285ccffbda59ac678184f]”.

(7) In paragraph 8, for “Commission” substitute “appropriate regulator”.

(8) For paragraph 9 substitute—

> **9.**
>
> The Secretary of State may determine the format of the reports referred to in paragraphs 1 to 7 and publish the determination.

(9) In paragraph 10—

- (a) in the first subparagraph, for “Commission may” substitute “appropriate authority may, by regulations,”;
- (b) omit the second subparagraph.
