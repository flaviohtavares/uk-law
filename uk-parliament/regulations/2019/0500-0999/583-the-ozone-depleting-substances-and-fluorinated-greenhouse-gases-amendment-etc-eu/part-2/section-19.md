# The Ozone-Depleting Substances and Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 19

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Article 20 (trade with a State not party to the Protocol and a territory not covered by the Protocol)[^c-6b6d378df0707c9c61697107c7f704b1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 20 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 2, in the first sentence—

- (a) for “Commission may adopt rules applicable” substitute “appropriate authority may make regulations in relation”;
- (b) for “the Community” substitute “any part of Great Britain[^c-77437f5668ec2608f8974e0f7b5dc6d0]”;
- (c) omit the third sentence.

(3) In paragraph 3—

- (a) in the first sentence, for “Commission” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (b) omit the second sentence.

(4) In paragraph 4—

- (a) in the second subparagraph, for “Commission” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (b) omit the third subparagraph.
