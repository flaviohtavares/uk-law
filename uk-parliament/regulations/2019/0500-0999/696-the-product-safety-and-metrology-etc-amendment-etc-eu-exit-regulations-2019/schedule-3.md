# The Product Safety and Metrology etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 3 — Amendment of the Consumer Protection Act 1987

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 6

### 1. Introduction[^c-72ff99369729a9432b1c1d9182935484]

The Consumer Protection Act 1987 is amended in accordance with paragraphs 2 to 6.

### 2. Amendment of section 1[^c-7e27803bdad62f85f599bed8de98f59b]

In section 1(1)—

(1) for “shall have effect” substitute “ was enacted ”;

(2) for “is” substitute “ was ”.

### 3. Amendment of section 2[^c-49fa01e4c7fdcc93d8db867d4a333b65]

In section 2(2)(c) for the words from “a member State” to “member States” substitute “ the United Kingdom ”.

### 4. Amendment of section 4[^c-008583ecd7c2ed90498b0a13e27fd52f]

In section 4(1)(a) before “EU” insert “ retained ”.

### 5. Omission of section 8[^c-2d80da6835cdb68a4bcb8ef988ada8e5]

Omit section 8.

### 6. Saving for Orders in Council made under section 8[^c-be4cc7e836d2bc33b8c412272de3b8dd]

An Order in Council made under section 8 continues to have effect despite the repeal of that section by paragraph 5.
