## 31. Amendment of the Recreational Craft Regulations 2017 and related amendment[^key-1ec88be67227611e88c513edc662be0b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 28 contains amendment of the Recreational Craft Regulations 2017 [^c24326281] and of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/1 of 3 January 2017 on procedures for watercraft identification under Directive 2013/53/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on recreational and personal watercraft.
