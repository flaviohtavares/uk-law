# The Product Safety and Metrology etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 14

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 14 — Amendment of the Accreditation Regulations 2009

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 17

### 1. General[^c-cdd2cf0813e31c633153cac98ce42f8b]

The Accreditation Regulations 2009 are amended in accordance with paragraphs 2 to 4.

**2.**[^c-d539a6770b68f5a9c65de786c7250cec] In regulation 2—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) for the definition of “the EC Regulation” substitute—

    > “**RAMS**” means Regulation [(EC) No 765/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/765) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9th July 2008 setting out the requirements for accreditation and market surveillance relating to the marketing of products and repealing Regulation [(EEC) No 339/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1993/339);
- (b) in paragraph (2) for “the EC Regulation” in both places in which it occurs substitute “ RAMS ”.

**3.**[^c-aa9b4bc0e19335ba7446f03cc3b18948] In regulation 3—

- (a) for “the EC Regulation” substitute “ RAMS ”;
- (b) for “national accreditation body” substitute “ UK national accreditation body ”.

**4.**[^c-c8a4871ab3f190a4d5846020fe62b869] In regulations 4(1), 5 and 6 and paragraph (1) of the Schedule for “the EC Regulation” substitute “ RAMS ”.
