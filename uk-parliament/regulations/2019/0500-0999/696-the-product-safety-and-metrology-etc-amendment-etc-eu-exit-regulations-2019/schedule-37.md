# The Product Safety and Metrology etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 37

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 37 — Revocation of retained direct EU and EEA legislation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 40

### 1. General[^c-d12965358b07bcc087cb3496a5183403]

The following are revoked—

- (a) Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2679/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1998/2679) of 7 December 1998 on the functioning of the internal market in relation to the free movement of goods among the Member States;
- (b)[^c-5de0ebd0a6b3ab34ca566309dafb78b4] Regulation (EU) 2019/515 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 March 2019 on the mutual recognition of goods lawfully marketed in another Member State and repealing Regulation [(EC) No 764/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/764);
- (c) Decision [(EC) No. 768/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/768) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 July 2008 on a common framework for the marketing of products and repealing Council Decision [93/465/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/1993/465);
- (d) Regulation (EU) No. 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 on European standardisation, amending Council Directives [89/686/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1989/686) and [93/15/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1993/15) and Directives [94/9/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1994/9), [94/25/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1994/25), [95/16/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1995/16), [97/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1997/23), [98/34/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1998/34), [2004/22/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/22), [2007/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2007/23), [2009/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/23) and [2009/105/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/105) of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Decision [87/95/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/1987/95) and Decision No [1673/2006/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/1673/2006) of the European Parliament and of the Council;
- (e) the following provisions of Part 2 of Annex II to the EEA Agreement—
  - (i) paragraphs 2, 3d, and 3f of Chapter XIX; and
  - (ii) paragraph 1 of Chapter XX.
