# The Product Safety and Metrology etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Amendment of the Hallmarking Act 1973

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 4

### 1. General[^key-4e18c3c64be4f7a1d344bf23d97b4f9c]

The Hallmarking Act 1973 is amended as follows.

**2.**[^key-62d59e0300324ec83922b411bea50e90] In section 2—

- (a) in subsection (1)(d), after “United Kingdom” insert “ before exit day ”;
- (b) for subsection (2A), substitute—

  > (2A) In this section “**EEA state**” has the meaning which it had under Schedule 1 to the Interpretation Act 1978 before exit day.

**3.**[^key-4e6bd4ea03afd9d0ce918692e5fdb27c] In section 22(1), in paragraph (c) of the definition of “sponsor's mark”, after “EEA state” insert “ other than the United Kingdom before exit day ”.
