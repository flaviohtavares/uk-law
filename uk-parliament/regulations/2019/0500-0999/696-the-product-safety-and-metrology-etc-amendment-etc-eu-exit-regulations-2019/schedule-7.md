# The Product Safety and Metrology etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 7

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 7 — Amendment of the Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment Regulations 1998

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 10

### 1. General[^key-191626c2031ef75ce0e4f3f0c28905db]

(1) The Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment Regulations 1998 are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) for “EC declaration of conformity” substitute “ declaration of conformity ”[^c24326651] ;
- (b) for paragraph (b) of that definition, substitute—

  > - (b) the requirements for a declaration of conformity in Article 15 of Regulation (EU) 2016/425 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2016 on personal protective equipment and repealing Council Directive [89/686/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1989/686); or

  ;

(3) In regulation 9(1)(b)(thorough examination and inspection), for “an EC declaration of conformity” substitute “ a declaration of conformity ”.

(4) In regulation 11(1) (keeping of information), for “an EC declaration of conformity” substitute “ a declaration of conformity ”.
