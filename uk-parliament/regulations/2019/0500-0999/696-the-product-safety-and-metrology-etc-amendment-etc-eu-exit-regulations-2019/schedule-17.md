# The Product Safety and Metrology etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 17

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 17 — Amendment of the Weights and Measures (Revocations) Regulations 2015

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 20

### 1. General[^key-bd22d0b2312ac8131c84562acbbe1e40]

(1) The Schedule to the Weights and Measures (Revocations) Regulations 2015 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 4—

- (a) the existing text becomes sub-paragraph (1);
- (b) after that sub-paragraph insert—

  > (2) But those Regulations, to the extent saved by sub-paragraph (1), cease to have effect on IP completion day[^key-5f41b412372b719e9d2da4c04eccf64c] so far as they provide for the carrying out of EEC initial verification in Great Britain[^key-03732bfa78dc18f08404ffd783b143ea], except pursuant to an application made before that day.

(3) In paragraph 5—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (3) at the beginning insert “ Subject to sub-paragraph (5), ”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (4) at the beginning insert “ Subject to sub-paragraph (5), ”;
- (c) after sub-paragraph (4) insert—

  > (5) The 1988 Regulations (to the extent saved by sub-paragraphs (3) and (4)) cease to have effect on IP completion day[^key-bcfc7d66113b51cf188f81fbcf62706d] so far as those Regulations provide for the carrying out of EEC initial verification in Great Britain[^key-6ae59052f78df23e5e92c66597da17a0], except pursuant to an application made before that day.

  .
