# The Product Safety and Metrology etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 11

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 11 — Amendment of the Weights and Measures (Packaged Goods) Regulations 2006

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulation 14

### 1. Introduction[^c-257d81734727d86cdac81aab41a471e4]

The Weights and Measures (Packaged Goods) Regulations 2006 [^c24326681] are amended in accordance with paragraphs 2 to 5.

### 2. Amendment to regulation 2[^c-85eee2cc7eb8daf677cd73aa9aef54ea]

In regulation 2 (interpretation) omit the definition of “Member State”.

### 3. Amendment to regulation 3[^c-9fc66cb7e9ca822657dbbc532c6446b2]

In regulation 3 (scope of application)—

- (a) omit paragraph (5);
- (b) after paragraph (6) insert—

  > (7) The obligations on the importer set out in regulation 5(1)(b) and regulation 6(1)(c) to ensure the package or outer container is marked with specified contact information do not apply where—
  >
  > - (a) the importer has imported the package or outer container from an EEA state within the period of 24 months[^c-bc1ad60d42653f0c4a0d3dfc4faeb7b9] beginning with IP completion day[^c-c78329ba91988a40b406e27e9e4401ca]; and
  > - (b) it is marked with the contact information of the person in that EEA state who packed or imported the package or who arranged for the packer to make up or the importer to import the package.

  .

### 4. Amendment to regulation 11[^c-3faaee15c4475e5b019232e39c3e2ca2]

Omit regulation 11 (notices to local weights and measures authorities).

### 5. Amendment to regulation 15[^c-e3f09ed8e37a2d94b05ace4a55fe636a]

In regulation 15 (offences relating to E-marks) omit paragraph (2).
