# The Food and Drink, Veterinary Medicines and Residues (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation , commencement and extent[^key-bf5ba86f6744e5854718c80547d04ff0][^key-b8d08ac005e68b9cc93b77ea019d7fd8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Food and Drink, Veterinary Medicines and Residues (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the later of exit day or the day after the day on which they are made.

(3)[^key-30df48ce5f389edd9cea0961ff1dc237] Regulation 17 and Schedule 8 extend to Great Britain.
