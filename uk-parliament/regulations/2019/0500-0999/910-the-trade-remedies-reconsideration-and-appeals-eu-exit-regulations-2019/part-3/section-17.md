# The Trade Remedies (Reconsideration and Appeals) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 17

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Appeals to the Upper Tribunal in relation to a determination or decision made by the Secretary of State[^c-5c54939196e661473a3060e32c059c53][^c-422cb9bce362ed5399a57a1aca9e70f7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An interested party may appeal to the Upper Tribunal for a review of a determination or decision[^c-6c7d1f27407c6c208664a7e1e7106380] made by the Secretary of State listed in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

(2) In Schedule 2 to these Regulations and for the purpose of paragraph (1)—

- (a) Part 1 contains a list of determinations and decisions[^c-eba7bc9790c998e6e220ccf2d7dc86ee] made under Schedule 4 or 5;
- (aa)[^c-2d77209aa615cef7d92ae0faa5f72ef4] Part 1A contains a list of decisions made under Schedule 5 as applied by Schedule 5A (bilateral safeguarding remedies);
- (b) Part 2 contains a list of determinations and decisions made under the Dumping and Subsidisation Regulations;
- (c) Part 3 contains a list of determinations and decisions made under the Safeguards Regulations;
- (d) Part 4 contains a determination and a decision[^M_F_75f4e988-7157-4a14-ba79-7ec3533ebf8b] made under these Regulations.
- (e)[^c-f3fd22164c83be0adfb509ebee0cd4d7] Part 5 contains a list of decisions made under the Bilateral Safeguards Regulations.
