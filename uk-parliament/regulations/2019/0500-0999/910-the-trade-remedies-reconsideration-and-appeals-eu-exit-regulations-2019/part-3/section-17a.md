# The Trade Remedies (Reconsideration and Appeals) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 17A

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17A. Appeals to the Upper Tribunal in relation to a decision by the Secretary of State under the Trade Remedies (Review and Reconsideration of Transitioned Trade Remedies) Regulations 2022[^key-6a9fe698e4dcc44aaac50a9cd9fe6c43]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An interested party may appeal to the Upper Tribunal for a review of a decision made by the Secretary of State under regulation 12 or 14 of the Trade Remedies (Review and Reconsideration of Transitioned Trade Remedies) Regulations 2022.
