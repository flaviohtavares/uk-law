# The Trade Remedies (Reconsideration and Appeals) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 28

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Modifications to Part 3[^key-8025c0c8b23939854c2cf96fbc651ab7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The heading to regulation 17 (appeals to the Upper Tribunal in relation to a determination made by the Secretary of State) and that regulation have effect as if, for “determination” or “determinations”, in each place where this occurs, there were substituted “ decision ” or “decisions” as appropriate.
