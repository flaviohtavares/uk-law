# The Trade Remedies (Reconsideration and Appeals) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 3

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Deemed Service[^key-f85ac54c03d461ff1c4c23998a844df5][^key-f3cbe5f842931b9eb31f2d4b61ed0892]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any document submitted to the TRA is deemed to have been submitted to the TRA on the earlier of—

- (a) the first working day after the day on which the document is received by the TRA; or
- (b) the day on which the TRA issues an acknowledgement of receipt.
