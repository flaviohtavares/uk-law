# The Trade Remedies (Reconsideration and Appeals) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 12

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Initiation of reconsideration[^key-2d283ee87b8c1e05fd04868ceacefa50]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the TRA accepts an application for reconsideration of an original decision that was published in a notice, the TRA must publish a notice of initiation of the reconsideration setting out—

- (a) a description of the goods to which the reconsideration relates; and
- (b) the original decision to be reconsidered.

(2) Where the TRA accepts an application for reconsideration of an original decision that was not published in a notice, the TRA must notify the applicant of the initiation of the reconsideration.
