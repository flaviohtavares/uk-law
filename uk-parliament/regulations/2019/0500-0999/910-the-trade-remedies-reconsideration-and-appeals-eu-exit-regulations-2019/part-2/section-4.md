# The Trade Remedies (Reconsideration and Appeals) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 4

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Public file[^c-d67ee974a4c9b76f92b2178bc7e23720]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-d9e6027c4086c9440b769849a1ef931f] The TRA must, in respect of a reconsideration[^c-2aac6fd6014ec4540d396c2692cc8708], establish and maintain a file which is open to the public (a “public file”) containing information, other than confidential information, which the TRA considers material to the reconsideration.

(2)[^c-93bfc603eef36ca867249c73b14ee790] Paragraph (1) does not apply to a reconsideration of a determination made under paragraph 9(4) of Schedule 4 to the Act to reject an application for the initiation of a dumping investigation or a subsidisation investigation.
