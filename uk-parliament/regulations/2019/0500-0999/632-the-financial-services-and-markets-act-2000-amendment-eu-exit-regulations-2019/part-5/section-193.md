# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 193

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 193. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Relevant Authorised Persons) Order 2015[^c-1c2c11b192a6b35fca2a14b0939861e6][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 2(2) and (3)(b) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Relevant Authorised Persons) Order 2015 [^c23661011] (relevant authorised persons) omit, in both places it occurs, “or resulting from Schedule 3 or 4 to the Act”.
