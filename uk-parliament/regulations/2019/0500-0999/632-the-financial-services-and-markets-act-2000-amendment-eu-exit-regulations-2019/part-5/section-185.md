# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 185

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 185. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Controllers) (Exemption) Order 2009[^key-b08d604d2dd0077e78fc41b3cfa50353][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Controllers) (Exemption) Order 2009 [^c23660621], in article 2 (interpretation), in the definition of “relevant UK authorised person”[^c23660631], for paragraphs (a) to (c) substitute—

> - (a) a credit institution which has permission under Part 4A of the Act to carry on the regulated activity of accepting deposits;
> - (b) an investment firm (as defined in section 424A of the Act);
> - (c) a management company (as defined in section 237(2) of the Act);

.
