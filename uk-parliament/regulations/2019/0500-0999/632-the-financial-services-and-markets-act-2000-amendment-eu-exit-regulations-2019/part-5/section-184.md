# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 184

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 184. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Control of Business Done at Lloyd's) Order 2001[^c-c705abacccc9dd211ec69f298c2a9b0e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Control of Business Done at Lloyd's) Order 2001 [^c23660611] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 3(a) for “114A” substitute “ 113 ”.

(3) In article 4(a) for “an EEA State” substitute “ the United Kingdom or Gibraltar[^c-9b4956af8bdd7a9b8f8bc41580a224bf] ”.

(4) In article 5—

- (a) omit paragraph (2);
- (b) in paragraph (2A) for “Paragraphs 2, 3, 4 and 5 of Part 1 of Schedule 12” substitute “ Paragraph 2 of Part 1 of Schedule 12 ”.
