# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 189

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 189. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (PRA-regulated Activities) Order 2013[^c-13d22088f37a7b220cd9cfa62c580e11][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 3 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (PRA-regulated Activities) Order 2013 [^c23660851] (dealing in investments as principal: designation by the PRA) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (2) [^c23660861]—

- (a) at the end of sub-paragraph (a) insert “ or ”;
- (b) omit sub-paragraph (c) and the preceding “or”.

(3) In paragraph (3) [^c23660871]—

- (a) for sub-paragraph (a) [^c23660881] substitute—

  > - (a) is an investment firm that is required to have initial capital of 730,000 euro or would be required to do so if it had a Part 4A permission;

  ;
- (b) for sub-paragraph (c) substitute—

  > - (c) is established in a country other than the United Kingdom but would meet the requirement specified in sub-paragraph (a) if P were established in the United Kingdom and had obtained the necessary authorisation in the United Kingdom for its business.

  .
