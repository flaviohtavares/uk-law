# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 181

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 181. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Variation of Threshold Conditions) Order 2001[^c-aada98082121878325f94f84ad1c765e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Variation of Threshold Conditions) Order 2001 [^c23660571] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2A (non-EEA insurers: specified regulator), in the heading, for “Non-EEA” substitute “ Non-UK ”.

(3) In article 3 [^c23660581] (non-EEA insurers)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “non-EEA” substitute “ non-UK ”;
- (b) omit paragraph (2);
- (c) in the heading, for “Non-EEA” substitute “ Non-UK ”.
