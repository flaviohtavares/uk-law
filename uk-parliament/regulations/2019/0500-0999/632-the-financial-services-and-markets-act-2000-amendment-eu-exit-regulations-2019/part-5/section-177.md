# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 177

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 177. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Exemption) Order 2001[^c-383ce45560affd74958e630b1c4d6d9b][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Exemption) Order 2001 [^c23660401] is amended as follows.

(2) In Part 1 of the Schedule [^c23660411] (persons exempt in respect of any regulated activity other than insurance business) omit paragraphs 2 to 6.

(3) In Part 4 of the Schedule (persons exempt in respect of particular regulated activities), in paragraph 48 (social housing)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1A)(b)(i), for “the mortgages directive does not apply by virtue of Article 3(2) of that directive” substitute “ section 423A(3) of the Act applies ”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (4), in the definition of “bridging loan”, for “Article 4(23) of the mortgages directive” substitute “ article 60G(9) of the Regulated Activities Order ”.
