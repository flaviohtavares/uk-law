# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 178

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 178. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Appointed Representatives) Regulations 2001[^key-1ed711942811d45ffb7b98b5cd20c1c2][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Appointed Representatives) Regulations 2001 [^c23660421] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1 (citation, commencement and interpretation), in paragraph (2) [^c23660431] omit the definitions of “EEA credit institution” and “EEA investment firm”.

(3) In regulation 2 (descriptions of business for which appointed representatives are exempt)—

- (a)[^key-ea817c3f45e95a92ae53e390790d3e07] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) in paragraph (1A) [^c23660451], for “a person who has a Part 4A permission for the purposes of the capital requirements directive or the markets in financial instruments directive, an EEA investment firm, or an EEA credit institution,” substitute “ a CRR firm, or a relevant investment firm or credit institution, ”;
- (c) for paragraph (1B) [^c23660461] substitute—

  > (1B) In paragraph (1A)—
  >
  > - “**clients**” has the meaning given in Article 2.1.7 of the markets in financial instruments regulation;
  > - “**CRR firm**” has the meaning given in Article 4.1.2A of the capital requirements regulation, except that it does not include an investment firm;
  > - “**financial instruments**” has the meaning given in Article 2.1.9 of the markets in financial instruments regulation;
  > - “**relevant investment firm or credit institution**” means a person falling within Article 1.2(a)(i) of the markets in financial instruments regulation.

  .

(4) In regulation 3 (requirements applying to contracts between authorised persons and appointed representatives)—

- (a) in paragraph (1) [^c23660471], for the words before “must” substitute “ It is a prescribed requirement for the purposes of section 39(1)(a)(ii) of the Act that a contract between a principal and a representative ”;
- (b) omit paragraph (1A) [^c23660481];
- (c) in paragraph (6) [^c23660491]—
  - (i) in the words before sub-paragraph (a) omit “, except where paragraph (1A) applies,”;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (a), for “Article 4.1.29 (definitions) of the markets in financial directive” substitute “ Part 3 of Schedule 2 to the Regulated Activities Order [^c23660501] ”;
  - (iii) in sub-paragraph (b), for “Article 4.1.43 of that directive” substitute “ Article 2.1.23 of the markets in financial instruments regulation ”.
