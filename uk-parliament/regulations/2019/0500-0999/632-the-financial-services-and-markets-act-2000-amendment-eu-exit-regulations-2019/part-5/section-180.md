# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 180

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 180. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Service of Notices) Regulations 2001[^c-32ad10318b24da0c2a54b8ba75990a17][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Service of Notices) Regulations 2001 [^c23660541] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1(2) [^c23660551] (interpretation) omit the definition of “host state regulator”.

(3) In regulation 6 [^c23660561] (deemed service), in paragraph (1), in the table for “in any EEA State (other than the United Kingdom)” substitute “ outside the United Kingdom ”.

(4) Omit regulation 11 (day of service on a host state regulator).
