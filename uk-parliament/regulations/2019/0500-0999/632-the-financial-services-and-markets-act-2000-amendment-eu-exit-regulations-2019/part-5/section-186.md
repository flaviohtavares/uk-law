# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 186

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 186. The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Prescribed Financial Institutions) Order 2013[^key-c5025178cb10b613b2a3780edece44d2][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Prescribed Financial Institutions) Order 2013 [^c23660641] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 1(2) [^c23660651] (interpretation)—

- (a) for the definition of “insurance holding company” substitute—

  > “**insurance holding company**” has the same meaning as in regulation 2(1) of the Solvency 2 Regulations 2015 [^c23660661];

  ;
- (b) for the definition of “mixed financial holding company” substitute—

  > “**mixed financial holding company**” has the meaning given in regulation 1(2) of the Financial Conglomerates and Other Financial Groups Regulations 2004 [^c23660671];

  ; and
- (c) omit the following definitions—
  - (i) “credit institution”;
  - (ii) “insurance undertaking”;
  - (iii) “reinsurance undertaking”;
  - (iv) “third-country insurance undertaking”;
  - (v) “third-country reinsurance undertaking”.

(3) In article 2 [^c23660681] (prescribed financial institutions)—

- (a) in paragraph (4) [^c23660691] omit “(within the meaning given in Article 32.4 of the recovery and resolution directive)”; and
- (b) after paragraph (4) insert—

  > (4A) For the purposes of paragraph (4) a company is failing or likely to fail where, if the company were a bank (within the meaning given in section 2 of the Banking Act 2009 [^c23660701]), it would be failing or likely to fail by virtue of meeting any of the circumstances in section 7(5C)(b) to (e) of that Act.

  .
