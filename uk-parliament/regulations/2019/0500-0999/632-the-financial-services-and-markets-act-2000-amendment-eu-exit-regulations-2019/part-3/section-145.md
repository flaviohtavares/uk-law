# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 145

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 145. Article 61 (regulated mortgage contracts)[^key-025bf294e4f257286b78a4f87544e3e1][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 61 (regulated mortgage contracts) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3)(a)(ii) [^c23659581] omit “in the EEA”.

(3) In paragraph (4), after sub-paragraph (a) insert—

> (aa) “land”—
>
> - (i) in relation to a contract entered into before IP completion day[^key-b5452f4aa9820e7cfdada9a971d8bcff], means land in the United Kingdom or within the territory of an EEA State;
> - (ii) in relation to a contract entered into on or after IP completion day, means land in the United Kingdom.

.
