# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 150

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 150. Article 72E (Business Angel-led Enterprise Capital Funds)[^key-8ebbf11f646ea01f5fafb3acfef8d107][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In article 72E [^c23659841] (Business Angel-led Enterprise Capital Funds), in paragraph (8)—

- (a) for “in accordance with the law of an EEA State” substitute “ under the law of any part of the United Kingdom ”;
- (b) for “within the territory of an EEA State” substitute “ in the United Kingdom ”.
