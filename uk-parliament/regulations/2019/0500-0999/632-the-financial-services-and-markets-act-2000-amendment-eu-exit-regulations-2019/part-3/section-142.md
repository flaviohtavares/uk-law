# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 142

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 142. Article 60HA (exempt agreements: exemptions not permitted under the mortgages directive)[^c-118a713b344670390f12365df6eb7a04][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 60HA [^c23659511] (exempt agreements: exemptions not permitted under the mortgages directive) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “exemptions not permitted under the mortgages directive” substitute “ provision qualifying articles 60E, 60F and 60H ”.

(3) In paragraph (1) [^c23659521], for the words after “article 60E(2) or (5)” substitute “ or 60F(4) if it is a mortgage agreement to which paragraph (2) does not apply ”.

(4) In paragraph (2)—

- (a) after “This paragraph applies” insert “ to an agreement ”;
- (b) omit paragraph (a);
- (c) in paragraph (b), for “of Article 4(23) of the mortgages directive” substitute “ given by article 60G(9) ”.
