# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 137

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 137. Article 54 (advice given in newspapers etc)[^c-aeabda6545c4d66495e7baf50a55abbc][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In article 54 (advice given in newspapers etc), in paragraph (1)(b)(va) [^c23659421], after “land” and after “building” insert “ in the United Kingdom ”.

(2) The amendment made by paragraph (1) does not apply in relation to any advice published or given before IP completion day[^c-042b3b860b643b7f55e0a8e2140f8d9b].
