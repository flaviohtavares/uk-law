# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 134

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 134. Article 51ZD (acting as trustee or depositary of an AIF)[^c-d8b075ead863db28c0935b2628cb48b7][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 51ZD [^c23659391] (acting as trustee or depositary of an AIF) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (2) omit sub-paragraph (b) and the preceding “or”.

(3) In paragraph (3), for sub-paragraph (a) substitute—

> - (a) a person appointed as a depositary by the AIFM in compliance with a requirement imposed by rule 3.11.10, 3.11.12 or 3.11.33 of the Investment Funds sourcebook; or

.
