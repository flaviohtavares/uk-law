# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 129

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Article 35 (international securities self-regulating organisations)[^key-4bc0ed4b9b7e043b4a39b978ced287f1][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In article 35 (international securities self-regulating organisations) paragraph (3) is amended as follows.

(2) In sub-paragraph (ba) [^c23659311] omit “an EEA central counterparty (as defined in section 285(1)(c) of the Act) or”.

(3) In sub-paragraph (bb) [^c23659321] omit “an EEA CSD (as defined in section 285(1)(f) of the Act) or”.
