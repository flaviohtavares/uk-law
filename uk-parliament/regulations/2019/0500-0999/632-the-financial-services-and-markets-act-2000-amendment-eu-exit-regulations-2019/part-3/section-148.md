# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 148

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 148. Article 72AA (managers of UCITS and AIFs)[^c-32bf67dc06dc7fadadd74419861273bf][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Article 72AA [^c23659661] (managers of UCITS and AIFs)—

- (a) in the heading, for “UCITS” substitute “ UK UCITS ”;
- (b) in paragraph (2), for “a UCITS” substitute “ a UK UCITS ”.

(2) In consequence of the amendments made by paragraph (1), for “UCITS” substitute “ UK UCITS ” in the following provisions—

- (a) article 9AA [^c23659671];
- (b) article 12A [^c23659681];
- (c) article 20 [^c23659691];
- (d) article 24;
- (e) article 36(1), (2) and (3);
- (f) article 39;
- (g) article 39C [^c23659701];
- (h) article 44;
- (i) article 50;
- (j) article 51A [^c23659711];
- (k) article 52A [^c23659721];
- (l) article 55(1) and (2);
- (m) article 58A [^c23659731];
- (n) article 60A [^c23659741];
- (o) article 63A [^c23659751];
- (p) article 63E [^c23659761];
- (q) article 63I [^c23659771];
- (r) article 63M [^c23659781].
