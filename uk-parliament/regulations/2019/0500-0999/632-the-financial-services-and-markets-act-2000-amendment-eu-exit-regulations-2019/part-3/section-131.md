>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Article 42A (depositaries of UCITS and AIFs)[^key-7296475f401eb40d543e361084c59f6b][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 42A [^c23659351] (depositaries of UCITS and AIFs) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “UCITS” substitute “ UK UCITS ”.

(3) In paragraph (a), for “a UCITS” substitute “ a UK UCITS ”.
