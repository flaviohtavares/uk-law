# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 143

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 143. Article 60JA (payment institutions)[^key-8a69040a88a0a6cdfd52c988e7490dca][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Omit article 60JA [^c23659531] (payment institutions).

(2) In relation to a person who has transitional authorisation by virtue of paragraph 14 of Schedule 3 to the Electronic Money, Payment Services and Payment Systems (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018 [^c23659541] (“**the 2018 Regulations**”), the amendment made by paragraph (1) does not apply during the transition period (within the meaning given by paragraph 22 of that Schedule).

(2A)[^key-a51ee928582132a1b5ee4007958b3425] In relation to a person who has a continued authorisation for a limited purpose in accordance with paragraph 26 of Schedule 3 to the 2018 Regulations or is exempt from the prohibition in regulation 138(1) of Payment Services Regulations 2017 by virtue of paragraph 36 of Schedule 3 to the 2018 Regulations, the amendment made by paragraph (1) does not apply during the extension period (within the meaning given by paragraph 31 of that Schedule) or the period of exemption (within the meaning given by paragraph 36 of that Schedule).

(3) Despite the repeal of Schedule 5 to the Payment Services Regulations 2017 [^c23659551] by the 2018 Regulations, that Schedule has effect, with any necessary modifications, in relation to a case where the amendment made by paragraph (1) does not apply because of paragraph (2) or (2A)[^key-7ba2c5f24000f2cbcf7c3c53a73ae487].
