# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 174

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 174. Article 59 (annual accounts and directors' report)[^key-3eb338584e9605c2d48c95f24dbdc9bb][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^key-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 59 (annual accounts and directors' report) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)(b)—

- (a) at the end of sub-paragraph (ai) [^c23660171] insert “ or ”;
- (b) at the end of sub-paragraph (i) [^c23660181] omit “or”;
- (c) omit sub-paragraph (iii) [^c23660191].

(3) In paragraph (7) omit sub-paragraph (e).

(4) The amendments made by this regulation do not apply in relation to any report for a financial year beginning before IP completion day[^key-b09ecc02a06bda9553b8407c4f05c1ca].
