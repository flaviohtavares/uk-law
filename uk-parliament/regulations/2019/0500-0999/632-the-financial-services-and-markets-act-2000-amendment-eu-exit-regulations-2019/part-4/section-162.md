# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 162

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 162. Introduction and transitional provision[^c-6d3ae0c9e4eb685c2f8d42b8a36b5eb3][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e][^c-660b7df424255946745807230422752e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005 [^c23660111] is amended as set out in this Part.

(2) Nothing in this Part causes a communication to constitute a breach of the prohibition in subsection (1) of section 21 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 [^c23660121] (restrictions on financial promotion) if—

- (a) a contract entered into before IP completion day[^c-f6745f07d59a077aaae8f414ad472997] required the communication to be made, and
- (b) the communication would not have constituted a breach of the prohibition had it been made before IP completion day[^c-fa4beca76df72cab84a2eb272e951f60].
