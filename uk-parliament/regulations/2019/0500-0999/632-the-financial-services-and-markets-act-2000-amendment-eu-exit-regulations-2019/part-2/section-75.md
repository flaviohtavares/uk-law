# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 75

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Section 391A (publication: special requirements relating to capital requirements directive)[^key-b8bcd81537c71a1ff28e1ed7c27276d3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 391A [^c23658231] (publication: special requirements relating to capital requirements directive) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “the capital requirements directive” substitute “ capital requirements ”.

(3) In subsection (1) for “to which Article 68(1) of the capital requirements directive applies.” substitute —

> for breach of—
>
> - (a) a provision made in or under this Act for the purpose of implementing the capital requirements directive, or
> - (b) a provision of the capital requirements regulation.

.

(4) In subsection (6) [^c23658241]—

- (a) at the end of paragraph (a) insert “ and ”; and
- (b) omit paragraph (c), and the “and” preceding it.
