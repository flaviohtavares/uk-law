## 52. Section 189 (assessment: procedure)[^c-14b962352d0ad36d3f100ea1b933438c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 189 [^c23657871] (assessment: procedure), in subsections (1A), (1ZB)(a) and (1ZB)(b), for “credit institution” substitute “ qualifying credit institution ”.
