# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 91

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Section 425A (consumers: regulated activities etc carried on by authorised persons)[^c-04243754ca0c7edab023210748aa23dd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 425A [^c23658641] (consumers: regulated activities etc carried on by authorised persons) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (3)(b), for “credit institutions” substitute “ qualifying credit institutions ”.

(3) In subsection (7) [^c23658651]—

- (a) omit the definition of “credit institution”[^c23658661];
- (b) in the definition of “relevant ancillary service”, for “Section B of Annex I to the markets in financial instruments directive” substitute “ Part 3A of Schedule 2 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 (S.I. 2001/544) ”.
