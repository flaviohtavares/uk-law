## 109. Paragraph 8 (certificates as to financial resources)[^c-ba1816721cdb68a813093fa7eb7c973d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph 8 (certificates as to financial resources) in sub-paragraph (2) [^c23658831]—

- (a) in sub-paragraphs (a) and (aa) omit “or with permission under Schedule 4”;
- (b) omit paragraph (b);
- (c) in paragraph (c) for “(a), (aa) or (b)” substitute “ (a) or (aa) ”.
