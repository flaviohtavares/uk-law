# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 74

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Section 391 (publication)[^c-1f31749451521d40f947dc6024c806ac]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 391 [^c23658151] (publication) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit subsections (7A) [^c23658161] and (7B) [^c23658171].

(3) In subsection (8A) [^c23658181], for “requirement imposed by the CSD regulation or any directly applicable regulation made under the CSD regulation” substitute “ CSD requirement ”.

(4) After subsection (8A) insert—

> (8AA) A “CSD requirement” is a requirement imposed by—
>
> - (a) the CSD regulation,
> - (b) any EU regulation, originally made under the CSD regulation, which is retained direct EU legislation, or
> - (c) any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made under the CSD regulation on or after IP completion day[^c-2f8798a1a68f73149dd2db1e9cc87251].

.

(5) In subsection (8B) [^c23658191], for “requirement imposed by the market abuse regulation or a directly applicable EU regulation made under the market abuse regulation” substitute “ market abuse requirement ”.

(6) After subsection (8B) insert—

> (8BA) A “market abuse requirement” is a requirement imposed by—
>
> - (a) the market abuse regulation,
> - (b) any EU regulation, originally made under the market abuse regulation, which is retained direct EU legislation, or
> - (c) any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made under the market abuse regulation on or after IP completion day[^c-4e52b2393b25f0d9d9f631c52d77fbb1].

.

(7) In subsection (8D) [^c23658201]—

- (a) omit the “or” at the end of paragraph (a);
- (b) in paragraph (b)—
  - (i) for “directly applicable regulation” substitute “ EU regulation, originally ”, and
  - (ii) at the end insert “ which is retained direct EU legislation, ”;
- (c) after paragraph (b) insert—

  > or
  >
  > - (c) any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made under the PRIIPs regulation on or after IP completion day[^c-7a0e726fc1e5ed9ad1bb4ba39cd41b20],

  .

(8) In subsection (8E) [^c23658211], for the words from “imposed by” to “this section” substitute—

> imposed by—
>
> - (a) the EU Benchmarks Regulation 2016,
> - (b) any EU regulation, originally made under the EU Benchmarks Regulation 2016, which is retained direct EU legislation, or
> - (c) any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made under the EU Benchmarks Regulation on or after IP completion day[^c-b9212b3196af46d145f1b993dc204e16],
>
> this section

.

(9) Omit subsection (8F) [^c23658221].
