# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Section 137Q (price stabilising rules)[^key-50232c7b2b5903e54c60632308e3df21]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 137Q (price stabilising rules) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (3A)(b) [^c23657631] for “EEA” substitute “ United Kingdom ”.

(3) For subsection (5) [^c23657641] substitute—

> (5) In this section references to Article 5 of the market abuse regulation include—
>
> - (a) any technical standards originally adopted or made under that Article which are retained direct EU legislation, and
> - (b) any technical standards made under that Article by the FCA.
