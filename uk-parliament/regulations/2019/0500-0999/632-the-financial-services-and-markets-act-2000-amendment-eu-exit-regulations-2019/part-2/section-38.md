# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Section 137R (financial promotion rules)[^key-a7ff11a16911aa1f5fbaad0a3e0a9048]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 137R (financial promotion rules) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (5)(b) [^c23657651], for the words from “requirements” to “(as defined in section 71I(5)),”[^key-2d69a52fe5860051b49dd6d5faae037a] substitute “ listed requirements ”.

(3) In subsection (5)(c) [^c23657661], for “requirements mentioned in paragraph (b)” substitute “ listed requirements ”.

(4) After subsection (5) insert—

> (5A) In subsection (5) “**the listed requirements**” means—
>
> - (a) requirements under the law of any part of the United Kingdom that appear to the FCA to correspond to requirements of—
>   - (i) Articles 24 (general principles and information to clients) and 25 (assessment of suitability and appropriateness and reporting to clients) of the markets in financial instruments directive,
>   - (ii) Commission Delegated Directive (EU) 2017/593 of 7 April 2016, so far as adopted under those Articles,
>   - (iii) Article 77 of the UCITS directive,
>   - (iv) Articles 10 and 11 of the mortgages directive, [^key-e86454c8cdd41f536692172f940f5501]...
>   - (v) Article 17 of the insurance distribution directive, or[^key-162c3a7426277ad0a68e9918d4cb6461]
>   - (vi)[^key-c7d12f54549e139d97441366248ad4bd] Article 44a of the recovery and resolution directive (as defined in paragraph (c)), and
> - (b) requirements of any retained direct EU legislation originally made under Article 24(13) or 25(8) of the markets in financial instruments directive.
> - (c)[^key-1f806865878f0affeb940dc1fb146f7f] In paragraph (a)(vi), “recovery and resolution directive” means [Directive 2014/59/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2014/0059) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 establishing a framework for the recovery and resolution of credit institutions and investment firms, as it had effect immediately before IP completion day.
