# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 92

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Section 425C (“qualifying EU provision”)[^key-41a32b9970e2494608ebd31d7984e957]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For section 425C [^c23658671] (“qualifying EU provision”) substitute—

> **425C. “Qualifying provision”**
>
> In this Act “**qualifying provision**” means a provision of any of the following—
>
> - (a) retained direct EU legislation;
> - (b) technical standards made in accordance with Chapter 2A of Part 9A;
> - (c) subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made by virtue of regulations made under section 8 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018.

.
