# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 68

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Section 343 (information given by auditor or actuary to a regulator: persons with close links)[^c-154c398f33e96c23934394bf41683ede]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 343 [^c23658041] (information given by auditor or actuary to a regulator: persons with close links)—

- (a) omit subsection (9).
- (b) in subsection (10) [^c23658051], for “ “credit institution” and “investment firm” have” substitute “ “investment firm” has”.
