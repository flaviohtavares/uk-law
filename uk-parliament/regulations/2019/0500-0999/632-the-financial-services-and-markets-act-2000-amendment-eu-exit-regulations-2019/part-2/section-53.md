# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Section 190 (requests for further information)[^key-9fe2c767ea80b4515b9fbf7bd57d1348]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 190 (requests for further information) [^c23657881], in subsection (4)—

- (a) in paragraph (a) [^c23657891], for “European Union” substitute “ United Kingdom or Gibraltar[^key-41c8bd922a537cafcd4d4aa13937f35b] ”;
- (b) for paragraph (b) [^c23657901], substitute—

  > - (b) is not subject to supervision under the laws of the United Kingdom (or any part of the United Kingdom) or of Gibraltar[^key-4a6df05681ff4b6b19e063402fb54996] relied on immediately before IP completion day[^key-357d0d11a6d0d982c1110cd567c0f9c7] to implement—
  >   - (i) the UCITS directive;
  >   - (ii) the Solvency 2 Directive;
  >   - (iii) the markets in financial instruments directive; or
  >   - (iv) the capital requirements directive,
  >
  >   including rules made by the appropriate regulator under this Act, in force on IP completion day , and, as amended from to time, in all other cases.

  .
