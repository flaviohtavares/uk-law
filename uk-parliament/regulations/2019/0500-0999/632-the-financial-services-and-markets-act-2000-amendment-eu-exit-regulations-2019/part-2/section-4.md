# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Section 1H (further interpretative provisions for sections 1B to 1G)[^c-cda82e5e7f9663a73982f81f4c9a5aaa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 1H [^c23657181] (further interpretative provisions) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (2)(d), for “credit institutions” substitute “ qualifying credit institutions ”.

(3) In subsection (8)—

- (a) omit the definition of “credit institution”[^c23657191];
- (b) in the definition of “relevant ancillary service”, for “Section B of Annex I to the markets in financial instruments directive” substitute “ Part 3A of Schedule 2 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 ”[^c23657201] .
