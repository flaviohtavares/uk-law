# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Section 186 (assessment criteria)[^key-923e0ba309db2cff94d344f1179338e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 186 [^c23657841] (assessment criteria), in paragraph (f)(i), for the words from “within the” to “terrorist financing” substitute “ as defined in regulation 3(1) of the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017 ”[^c23657851] .
