# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 86

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Section 418 (carrying on regulated activities in the United Kingdom)[^key-23988ae91839604a88ad007eee1652fb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 418 [^c23658491] (carrying on regulated activities in the United Kingdom) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1) [^c23658501], for the words before “described” substitute “ In the cases ”.

(3) Omit subsections (2) and (3) [^c23658511].

(4) In subsection (5AA) [^c23658521]—

- (a) in paragraph (b)(i) and (ii), for “an EEA State” substitute “ the United Kingdom ”;
- (b) in paragraph (d), for “the EEA” substitute “ the United Kingdom ”.

(5) In subsection (6), for the words before “it is irrelevant” substitute “ For the purposes of the preceding subsections ”.

(6) Omit subsection (7) [^c23658531].

(7) For subsection (8) [^c23658541] substitute—

> (8) For the purposes of this section, an AIF is “marketed” when—
>
> - (a) the person managing the AIF (“the AIFM”) makes a direct or indirect offering or placement of units or shares of the AIF to or with an investor domiciled or with a registered office in the United Kingdom, or
> - (b) another person makes such an offering or placement at the initiative of, or on behalf of, the AIFM.
