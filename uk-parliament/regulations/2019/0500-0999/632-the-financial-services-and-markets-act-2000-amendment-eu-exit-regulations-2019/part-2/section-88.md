# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Section 422A (disregarded holdings)[^key-025322ba7e12233ea2624bab72adce4b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^c23658561]In section 422A (disregarded holdings)—

- (a)[^key-c5961de4a22de618ec8432b1a696248a] in subsection (4)—
  - (i) in paragraph (a) [^c23658571], for the words from “article 4.1.7” to “directive” substitute “ article 2.1.6 of the markets in financial instruments regulation ”;
  - (ii) for paragraph (b), substitute—

    > - (b) has a Part 4A permission to carry on one or more investment services and activities;

    ;
- (b)[^key-c5961de4a22de618ec8432b1a696248a] in subsection (5), for “credit institution” substitute “ qualifying credit institution ”;
- (c)[^key-c5961de4a22de618ec8432b1a696248a] in subsection (6)—
  - (i) in the opening words and in paragraph (b), for “credit institution” substitute “ qualifying credit institution ”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (a)(ii), omit the words from “in accordance with” to the end;
- (d)[^key-c5961de4a22de618ec8432b1a696248a] in subsection (7) [^c23658581], for “Article 2.1(b) of the UCITS Directive” substitute “ section 237(2) ”;
- (e)[^key-025322ba7e12233ea2624bab72adce4b] in subsection (9A) [^c23658591], for the words from “Commission” to “financial instruments” substitute “ the market abuse regulation and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No. 1052/2016 of 8 March 2016 supplementing Regulation (EU) No. 596/2014 of the European Parliament and the Council with regard to the regulatory technical standards for conditions applicable to buy-back programmes and stabilisation measures ”;
- (f)[^key-c5961de4a22de618ec8432b1a696248a][^key-0c8a35c3edbed524cb8d7d04c3c6da99] for subsection (10), substitute—

  > (10) For the purposes of this section “**qualifying credit institution**” includes a credit institution which is authorised under the law of Gibraltar relied on immediately before IP completion day to implement the capital requirements directive.
