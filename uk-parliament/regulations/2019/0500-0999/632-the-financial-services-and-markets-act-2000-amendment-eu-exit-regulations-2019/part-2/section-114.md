# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 114

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Paragraph 10 (insurance business transfers effected outside the United Kingdom)[^key-350c7cea3b8eb04fdb845f34f8e32a18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph 10 (insurance business transfers effected outside the United Kingdom)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1) for “any of the conditions in sub-paragraphs (2), (3) or (4)” substitute “ the condition in sub-paragraph (4) ”;
- (b) omit sub-paragraphs (2) and (3) [^c23658881];
- (c) in sub-paragraph (4) [^c23658891] for “a UK authorised person” to the end substitute “ a UK authorised person as defined in section 105(8) ”.
