# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Section 71D (sections 71B and 71C: conditions)[^key-4ef776ac156ea5e7c887ee8de4347374]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 71D (sections 71B and 71C: conditions) in subsection (2) for “any measure of a kind described in Article 27(1) of the recovery and resolution directive” substitute “ any measure defined as a “relevant measure” by article 107 of the Bank Recovery and Resolution (No. 2) Order 2014 (S.I. 2014/3348) ”.
