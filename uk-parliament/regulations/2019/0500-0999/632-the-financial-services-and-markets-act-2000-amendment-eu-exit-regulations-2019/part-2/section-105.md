## 105. Paragraph 1 (insurance business transfer schemes)[^key-416b8ed89bd33ea6018a2114f1724208]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For paragraph 1 [^c23658741] (insurance business transfer schemes) substitute—

> **1.**
>
> For the purposes of section 111(2) the appropriate certificate, in relation to an insurance business transfer scheme, is a certificate under paragraph 2.
