# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Section 139A (power of the FCA to give guidance)[^key-08e691a89c506aa62a9a32796df594b7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 139A (power of the FCA to give guidance), in subsection (4) [^c23657701] for “or a directly applicable regulation made under the market abuse regulation” substitute “ , any retained EU direct legislation originally made under the market abuse regulation or any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made on or after IP completion day[^key-89b37ddaffc20ce866f8c7943c08940c] under the market abuse regulation ”.
