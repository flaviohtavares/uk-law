# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Section 39A (certain tied agents operating outside United Kingdom)[^key-fcac528e245110553ab557f215cc392a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 39A [^c23657371] (certain tied agents operating outside United Kingdom) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)(a), for the words after “who is” substitute “ established in the United Kingdom; and ”.

(3) In subsection (4), for the words after “Condition B is that” substitute “ the FCA is satisfied that no such business is, or is likely to be, carried on by the agent in the United Kingdom. ”.

(4) In subsection (6)(c) [^c23657381] omit sub-paragraph (ii).

(5) In subsection (6)(d) [^c23657391] omit “or register”.

(6) For subsection (8) [^c23657401] substitute—

> (8) Section 39(7) applies for the purposes of this section.

.

(7) In subsection (9) omit the definition of “competent authority”[^c23657411].

(8) The amendments made by paragraphs (2), (3) and (7) do not apply during the three-year transitional period in relation to contracts entered into before IP completion day[^key-38723bbdfd8ffb1836fcd9e5a0c052a3].

(9) In relation to such contracts, section 39A(6)(d) has effect during the three-year transitional period as if “or on the register of tied agents of an EEA State maintained pursuant to Article 29 of Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 on markets in financial instruments” were inserted after “entered on that record”.

(10) In paragraphs (8) and (9) “**the three-year transitional period**” means the period that—

- (a) begins with IP completion day[^key-66f8a2991b485e5216891ff9c9dc3998], and
- (b) ends at the end of the period of three years starting with the first day after IP completion day.
