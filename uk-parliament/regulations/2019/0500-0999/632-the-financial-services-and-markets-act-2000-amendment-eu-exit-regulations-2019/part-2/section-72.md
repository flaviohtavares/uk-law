## 72. Section 384 (power of FCA or PRA to require restitution)[^c-251d4c40e01b6d1d806929165f69d8f1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 384 [^c23658121] (power of FCA or PRA to require restitution) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (7)(a) [^c23658131], for “qualifying EU provision” substitute “ qualifying provision ”.

(3) In subsection (10) [^c23658141] for “qualifying EU provision” in both places substitute “ qualifying provision ”.
