# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Section 168 (appointment of persons to carry out investigations in particular cases)[^c-b57656448e013351efced5a9fa21d0de]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 168 (appointment of investigators: particular cases), in subsection (4)—

- (a) in paragraph (jc)(ii) [^c23657751] for “a directly applicable EU regulation made” substitute “ any retained EU direct legislation originally made under the market abuse regulation or any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made on or after IP completion day[^c-6e72567fe4737b179c75078e351c6f33]”;
- (b) in paragraph (k) [^c23657761] for “qualifying EU provision” substitute “ qualifying provision ”.
