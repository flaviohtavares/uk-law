# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Section 420 (parent and subsidiary undertaking)[^key-ecb9a5b1c7d020d92655af4e2052b821]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 420 (parent and subsidiary undertaking), in subsection (2)(b)—

- (a)[^key-bc337555974b717139608cf79b1ffcbd] omit “other than the United Kingdom”;
- (b)[^key-ecb9a5b1c7d020d92655af4e2052b821] for “Seventh Company Law Directive” substitute “ Directive 2013/34/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on the annual financial statements, consolidated financial statements and related reports of certain types of undertakings, amending Directive [2006/43/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2006/43) of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives [78/660/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1978/660) and [83/349/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1983/349) ”[^c23658551] .
