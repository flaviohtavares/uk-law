# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Section 391E (publication: special provisions relating to the insurance distribution directive)[^key-65fe10c534a8a1cb6b951be2b483ff46]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 391E [^c23658301] (publication: special provisions relating to the insurance distribution directive) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “the insurance distribution directive” substitute “ insurance distribution ”.

(3) In subsection (1), for “to which Article 32 of the insurance distribution directive applies” substitute “ for breach of a provision made in or under this Act for the purpose of implementing the insurance distribution directive ”.

(4) Omit subsection (5).
