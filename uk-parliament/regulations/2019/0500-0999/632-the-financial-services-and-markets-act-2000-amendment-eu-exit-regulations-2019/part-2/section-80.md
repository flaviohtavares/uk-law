# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Section 398 (misleading FCA or PRA: residual cases)[^c-ebc8bd8d9beeae57426e5a2813d713f3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 398 [^c23658311] (misleading FCA or PRA: residual cases) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1A) [^c23658321]—

- (a) for paragraph (ea) substitute—

  > - (ea) any EU regulation, originally made under the markets in financial instruments directive, which is retained direct EU legislation;

  ;
- (b) for paragraph (eb) substitute—

  > - (eb) any of the following—
  >   - (i) the markets in financial instruments regulation,
  >   - (ii) any EU regulation, originally made under the markets in financial instruments regulation, which is retained direct EU legislation, and
  >   - (iii) any subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978) made under the markets in financial instruments regulation on or after IP completion day[^c-a5d0133f679a13104ccc54da1f74a195];

  .
