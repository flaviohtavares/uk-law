# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 77

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Section 391C (publication: special provisions relating to the UCITS directive)[^c-c54edb5e3269da06ad882540b390d014]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 391C [^c23658261] (publication: special provisions relating to the UCITS directive) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “the UCITS directive” substitute “ UCITS ”.

(3) In subsection (1) for “to which Article 99 of the UCITS directive applies” substitute “ for breach of a provision made in or under this Act for the purpose of implementing the UCITS directive ”.

(4) In subsection (7) [^c23658271], omit paragraph (b), and the “and” preceding it.
