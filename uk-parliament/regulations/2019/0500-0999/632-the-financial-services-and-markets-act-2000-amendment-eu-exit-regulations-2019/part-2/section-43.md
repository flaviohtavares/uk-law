# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Section 165A (PRA's power to require information: financial stability)[^key-476c7bd61df0821234f216625eb3db9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 165A [^c23657711] (PRA's power to require information: financial stability) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (8), in the definition of “management”, for “Annex II to the UCITS Directive” substitute “ Schedule 6 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 [^c23657721] ”.

(3) In subsection (9)(b) [^c23657731], for the words from “meaning given” to the end substitute “ same meaning as in the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 (see article 3(1) of that Order). ”.
