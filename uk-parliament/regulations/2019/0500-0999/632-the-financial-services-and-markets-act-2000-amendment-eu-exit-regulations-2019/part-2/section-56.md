# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Section 191G (interpretation of Part 12)[^key-cbf773191fa02d144342a76857b487fc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 191G [^c23657931] (interpretation), in subsection (1)—

- (a) omit the definition of “credit institution”[^c23657941];
- (b) in the definition of “UK authorised person”, in paragraph (b), after “Schedule 5” insert “ , or a person treated as having a Part 4A permission to carry on a regulated activity by virtue of regulation 71 of the Collective Investment Schemes (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ”[^c23657951] .
- (c)[^key-076dce081f087050fef6ac5729bf1367] in the appropriate place, insert—

  > “**qualifying credit institution**” includes a credit institution which is authorised under the law of Gibraltar relied on immediately before IP completion day to implement the capital requirements directive.
