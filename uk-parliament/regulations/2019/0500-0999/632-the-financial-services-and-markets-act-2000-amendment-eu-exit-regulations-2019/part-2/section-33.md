# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Further amendments to Part 7 of the Act[^key-96f67c07fa1bafd3a25b47b804ccde72]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 111 (sanction of the court for business transfer schemes) in subsection (2)(a) [^c23657571] for “certificates have” substitute “ certificate has ”.

(2) In section 112 (effect of order sanctioning business transfer scheme) omit subsection (9) [^c23657581].

(3) Omit sections 114 (rights of certain policyholders), 114A [^c23657591] (notice of transfer of reinsurance contracts) and 116 (effect of insurance business transfers authorised in other EEA States).
