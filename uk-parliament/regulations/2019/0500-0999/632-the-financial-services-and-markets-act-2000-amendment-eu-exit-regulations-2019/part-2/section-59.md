# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Section 192JB (rules requiring parent undertakings to facilitate resolution)[^c-d26ec97734379ebf2e963c7ac0a6fbb1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 192JB [^c23657971] (rules requiring parent undertakings to facilitate resolution), in subsection (4), for paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) “**group financial support agreement**” means an agreement for the provision of financial support, by a member of the group of the parent undertaking, to an institution in the group which, at any time after the agreement is concluded, comes to need financial support;

.
