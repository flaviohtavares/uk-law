# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 111

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 111. Paragraph 9B (ring-fencing transfer schemes)[^c-2f636ef1389d9d2e804ae6ec22fd5505]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For paragraph 9B [^c23658851] (ring-fencing transfer schemes) substitute—

> **9B.**
>
> For the purposes of section 111(2) the appropriate certificates, in relation to a ring-fencing transfer scheme, are—
>
> - (a) a certificate given by the PRA certifying its approval of the application, and
> - (b) a certificate under paragraph 9C.

.
