# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Section 391B (publication: special provisions relating to the transparency obligations directive)[^key-15f8552c395e309b8309cc366b54ca1e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 391B [^c23658251] (publication: special provisions relating to the transparency obligations directive) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, for “the transparency obligations directive” substitute “ transparency obligations ”.

(3) In subsection (1) for “to which Article 29(1) of the transparency obligations directive applies” substitute “ for breach of a provision made in or under this Act for the purpose of implementing the transparency obligations directive ”.
