# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 205

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/632
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 205. Annual reports and complaints[^c-d43c4cd5a9f7d15c0d4828649aade92d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Power to give a transitional direction is treated as a “**relevant sub-delegated power**” for the purposes of paragraph 32 of Schedule 7 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018.

(2) Functions under this Part are to be treated as not being “**relevant functions**” for the purposes of section 84 of the Financial Services Act 2012 (arrangements for the investigation of complaints relating to exercise of relevant functions of regulators).
