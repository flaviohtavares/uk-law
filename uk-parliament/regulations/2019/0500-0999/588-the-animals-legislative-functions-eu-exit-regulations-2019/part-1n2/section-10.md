# The Animals (Legislative Functions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 10

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Commission Regulation (EU) No 142/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down health rules as regards animal by-products and derived products not intended for human consumption and implementing Council Directive [97/78/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1997/78) as regards certain samples and items exempt from veterinary checks at the border under that Directive[^key-55d966e47e2b85fcd655da6c9b06dec2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation (EU) No 142/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down health rules as regards animal by-products and derived products not intended for human consumption and implementing Council Directive [97/78/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1997/78) as regards certain samples and items exempt from veterinary checks at the border under that Directive is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 25(2), in point (e)(ii), for “Chapter 21 of Annex XV” substitute “ the model made available or published for the time being by the appropriate authority ”.

(3) In Article 31, for “set out in Annex XV hereto” substitute “ made available or published for the time being by the appropriate authority ”.

(4) In Article 32(7), for “set out in Section 10 of Chapter III of Annex XVI hereto” substitute “ made available or published for the time being by the appropriate authority ”.

(5) In Annex 8—

- (a) in Chapter 3—

  - (iii) in paragraph 6—

    - (bb) after point (h), omit the model commercial document;
- (b) in Chapter 4, in Section 1, in paragraph 2, for “laid down in Chapter III” substitute “ made available or published for the time being by the appropriate authority ”.

(6) In Annex 11, in Chapter 1, in Section 1—
