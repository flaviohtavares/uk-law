# The Conservation of Habitats and Species (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Amendment of regulation 45 (protection of wild animals listed in Annex IV(a) to the Habitats Directive)

(1) Regulation 45 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraphs (11)(c) and (12), for “relevant EU instrument”, substitute “relevant retained EU law”; and

(3) In sub-paragraph 12(b), at the beginning add “transposes an instrument”.
