# The Conservation of Habitats and Species (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Amendment of regulation 54 (introduction of new species)

In regulation 54(8), at the end, insert “unless the United Kingdom is entitled under international law to exercise those powers without the consent of the flag state”.
