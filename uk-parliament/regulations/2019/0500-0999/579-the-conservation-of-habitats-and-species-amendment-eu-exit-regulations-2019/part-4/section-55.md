# The Conservation of Habitats and Species (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 55. Amendment of regulation 40 (protection of wild birds, their eggs and nests)

In regulation 40—

- (a) in paragraphs (5)(c) and (6), for “relevant EU instrument”, substitute “relevant retained EU law”; and
- (b) in paragraph 6(b), for “is adopted” substitute “transposes an instrument adopted”.
