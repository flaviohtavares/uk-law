# The Conservation of Habitats and Species (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 62

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 62. Amendment of regulation 49 (offences relating to wild plants listed in Annex IV(b) to the Habitats Directive)

In regulation 49(6), before sub-paragraph (a), insert—

> - (aa) it was taken from the wild in the United Kingdom without contravention of the law and before 10th June 1994;
