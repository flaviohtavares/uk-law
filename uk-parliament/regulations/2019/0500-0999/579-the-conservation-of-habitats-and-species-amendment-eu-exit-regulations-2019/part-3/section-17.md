## 17. Amendment of regulation 44 (protection of certain wild animals: defences)

In regulation 44(7), before sub-paragraph (a), insert—

> - (aa) it was taken from the wild in the United Kingdom without contravention of the law and before 10<sup>th</sup> June 1994;
