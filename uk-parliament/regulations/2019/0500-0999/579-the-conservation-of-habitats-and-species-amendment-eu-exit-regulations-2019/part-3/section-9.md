# The Conservation of Habitats and Species (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Amendment of regulation 10 (duties in relation to wild bird habitat)

In regulation 10(8), for “area to which the new Wild Birds Directive applies” substitute “seaward limits of the offshore marine area (as defined in regulation 4(2)”.
