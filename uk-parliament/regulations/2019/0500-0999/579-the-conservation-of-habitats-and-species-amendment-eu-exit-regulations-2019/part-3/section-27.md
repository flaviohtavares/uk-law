# The Conservation of Habitats and Species (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Amendment of regulation 116 (powers of entry: wildlife inspectors)

In regulation 116(9), in the definition of “third country ship”—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “(other than Gibraltar) which is not a member State” substitute “other than the United Kingdom”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “a member State”, substitute “the United Kingdom”.
