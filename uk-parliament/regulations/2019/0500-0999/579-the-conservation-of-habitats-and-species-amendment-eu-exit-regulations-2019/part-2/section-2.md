# The Conservation of Habitats and Species (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Amendment of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981: England and Wales

In section 27 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981[^f00002], as it extends to England and Wales, in the definition of “wild bird”, after “visitor to”, insert “the United Kingdom or”.

## Annotations

[^f00002]: Footnote: [1981 c.69](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/69). the definition of “wild bird” in Section 27 was amended in relation to England and Wales by [S.I. 2004/1487](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1487).
