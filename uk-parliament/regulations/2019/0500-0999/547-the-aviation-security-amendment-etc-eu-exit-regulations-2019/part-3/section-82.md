# The Aviation Security (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 82

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82.[^c-69ca305229998e1aed7eca42cecbd3e5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For point 6.8.1.1 substitute—

> **6.8.1.1.**
>
> Any air carrier carrying cargo or mail from an airport in another country for transfer, transit or unloading at any airport falling within the scope of Regulation [(EC) No 300/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/300) shall be designated as an ‘Air Cargo or Mail Carrier operating into the United Kingdom from Another Country Airport’ (UK-ACC3) by the appropriate authority.
>
> This requirement does not apply in respect of a country or territory designated by the appropriate authority.
