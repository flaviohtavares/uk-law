# The Aviation Security (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 71

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71.[^key-6d6fbae9286c1a79c1f5fd7b4f725bd3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In point 6.3.2.6—

- (a) in point (c), for “except for consignments listed in points 6.2.1(d) and (e) of Commission Implementing Decision C(2015) 8005” substitute “ except for small consignments or consolidations of such consignments designated by the appropriate authority ”;
- (b) in point (e), omit point (ii) and the “or” after it.
