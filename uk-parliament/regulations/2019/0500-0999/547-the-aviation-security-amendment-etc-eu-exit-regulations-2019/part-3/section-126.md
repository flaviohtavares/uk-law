# The Aviation Security (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 126

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

## 126. Revocation of Commission Regulation (EU) No 72/2010[^key-c710d1be82fdc31569775d6b512e1ae6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Regulation (EU) No 72/2010 of 26 January 2010 laying down procedures for conducting Commission inspections in the field of aviation security is revoked.
