> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>

## 83.[^c-e0299ee57de5bcfd0a107eaa64e85804]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In point 6.8.1.2—

- (a) for “ACC3”, in both places it occurs, substitute “ UK-ACC3 ”;
- (b) in point (b), for “an EU aviation security validation report” substitute “ an assessment by the appropriate authority of relevant evidence (which for the purpose of this Regulation may include a validation report) ”.
