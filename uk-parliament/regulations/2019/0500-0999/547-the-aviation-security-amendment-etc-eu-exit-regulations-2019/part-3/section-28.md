# The Aviation Security (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 28

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28.[^key-0e3fbc09e69916dd36b83665029bc8e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In point 5.1 for paragraph 2 substitute—

> **2.**
>
> Hold baggage may be exempted from screening if—
>
> - (a) it arrives from a domestic airport; or
> - (b) it arrives from any other airport designated by the appropriate authority.
