# The Aviation Security (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 127

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Revocation of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/103[^c-3f600fb8a124dcab0eab05cd2d72fdb7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/103 of 23 January 2019 amending Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1998 as regards clarification, harmonisation and simplification as well as strengthening of certain specific aviation security measures is revoked.
