# The Aviation Security (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. The Aviation Security Act 1982[^key-53880e3b6fb5b56a28e172b3bdd1362e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Aviation Security Act 1982 [^c23637161] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 20—

- (a) omit subsections (3A), (4A) and (6); and
- (b) in subsection (4) for “subsections (3) and (3A)” substitute “ subsection (3) ”.

(3) In section 21E omit subsection (4).
