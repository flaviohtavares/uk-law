# The Common Fisheries Policy and Aquaculture (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 35

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Council Regulation [(EEC) No 2136/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1989/2136) laying down common marketing standards for preserved sardines and trade descriptions for preserved sardines and sardine-type products[^key-e7123c4f792e52e1a15c6a37124ce93c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Regulation [(EEC) No 2136/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1989/2136) laying down common marketing standards for preserved sardines and trade descriptions for preserved sardines and sardine-type products is amended as follows.

(2) For Article 8 substitute—

> **Article 8**
>
> A fisheries administration may make regulations in relation to the application of this Regulation, in particular the sampling plan for assessing conformity of manufacturing batches with the requirements of this Regulation.
>
> Article 43 of Regulation (EU) No 1379/2013 applies in relation to regulations made under this Article in the same way that it applies to regulations made under that Regulation.
