# The Common Fisheries Policy and Aquaculture (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 36

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Council Regulation [(EEC) No 1536/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1992/1536) laying down common marketing standards for preserved tuna and bonito[^key-f6b9d4e8584ff581a543d92bb1316650]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Regulation [(EEC) No 1536/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1992/1536) laying down common marketing standards for preserved tuna and bonito is amended as follows.

(2) For Article 8 substitute—

> **Article 8**
>
> **1.**
>
> A fisheries administration may make regulations in relation to the application of this Regulation.
>
> **2.**
>
> Article 43 of Regulation (EU) No 1379/2013 applies in relation to regulations made under this Article in the same way that it applies to regulations made under that Regulation.
