# The Common Fisheries Policy and Aquaculture (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 42

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Council Regulation [(EC) No 708/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/708) concerning use of alien and locally absent species in aquaculture[^key-8881759632f9ce93a9219c20c46377a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Regulation [(EC) No 708/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/708) concerning use of alien and locally absent species in aquaculture is amended as follows.

(1A)[^key-6c6c3146002bd175f89397df55deaf45] In Article 23, for the second and third paragraphs substitute—

> To facilitate the sharing of information contained in its register, the appropriate authority must use the information system set out in Commission Regulation 535/2008.
>
> The appropriate authority may, by regulations, amend provisions of Commission Regulation 535/2008 relating to the establishment and development of an information system.

.

(2) In Article 24—

- (a) for paragraph 1 substitute—

  > **1.**
  >
  > The appropriate authority may, by regulations, amend Annex 1 or 3 in order to adapt them to technical and scientific progress.
  >
  > **1A.**
  >
  > The Secretary of State may, by regulations—
  >
  > - (a) amend Annex 2 in order to adapt it to technical and scientific progress;
  > - (b) amend Annex 4 so as to add a species.
  > - (c)[^key-888aa56983db489d77cb00ae643329d2] amend Article 2 of Commission Regulation [(EC) 535/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/535) to adapt the specifications for the conditions necessary for adding species to Annex 4, as provided for in paragraph 3.

  ;
- (b) omit paragraph 2;
- (c) in paragraph 3—
  - (i) for the words from “In order” to “have” substitute “ Regulations under paragraph 1A(b) may only be made where the Secretary of State is satisfied that the aquatic organism has ”, and
  - (ii) for “Union” substitute “ United Kingdom ”;
- (d) after paragraph 3 insert—

  > **3A.**
  >
  > In paragraph 3, “long time (with reference to its life cycle)” and “adverse effect” have the meanings given in Article 2 of Commission Regulation [(EC) No 535/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/535).

  ; and
- (e) omit paragraphs 4 to 6.

(3) Omit Articles 24a to 24c.

(4) Before Article 25 insert—

> **Article 24d — Regulations: devolved legislative function**
>
> **1.**
>
> This Article applies to regulations made under Article Articles 23 and 24(1)[^key-f3bee258e08e4608567c9b91d60ca195].
>
> **2.**
>
> Regulations made by the Secretary of State or the Welsh Ministers are to be made by statutory instrument.
>
> **3.**
>
> For regulations made by the Scottish Ministers, see section 27 of the Interpretation and Legislative Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 [^c24328361].
>
> **4.[^key-95ed3e06c75e9ccc3618747745e9f7dc]**
>
> . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> **5.**
>
> A statutory instrument containing regulations made by the Secretary of State is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
>
> **6.**
>
> A statutory instrument containing regulations made by the Welsh Ministers is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of the National Assembly for Wales.
>
> **7.**
>
> Regulations made by the Scottish Ministers are subject to the negative procedure (see section 28 of the Interpretation and Legislative Reform (Scotland) Act 2010).
>
> **8.[^key-95ed3e06c75e9ccc3618747745e9f7dc]**
>
> . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> **9.**
>
> Such regulations may—
>
> - (a) contain consequential, incidental, supplementary, transitional or saving provision (including provision amending, repealing or revoking enactments (which has the meaning given by section 20(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 [^c24328391]));
> - (b) make different provision for different purposes.
>
> **Article 24e — Regulations: consent required**
>
> **1.**
>
> This Article applies to regulations made under Article 24(1A).
>
> **2.**
>
> The Secretary of State may only make regulations in relation to the whole of Great Britain[^key-23d47442aad8e34b016a902578d82055].
>
> **3.**
>
> The Secretary of State may not make regulations without the consent of—
>
> - (a) in relation to Wales, the Welsh Ministers;
> - (b) in relation to Scotland, the Scottish Ministers;
> - (c)[^key-a885928ae6600ae426219de2303949ba] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> **4.**
>
> Where either[^key-32d52b7596469460cf2284084eecaf4d] of the parties mentioned in paragraph 3(a) or (b)[^key-4f83a27ecda370694e3775d41b6e6955] requests that the Secretary of State make regulations to which this Article applies, the Secretary of State must have regard to that request.
>
> **5.**
>
> Regulations made by the Secretary of State are to be made by statutory instrument.
>
> **6.**
>
> A statutory instrument containing regulations is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
>
> **7.**
>
> Such regulations may—
>
> - (a) contain consequential, incidental, supplementary, transitional or saving provision (including provision amending, repealing or revoking enactments (which has the meaning given by section 20(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018));
> - (b) make different provision for different purposes.

.
