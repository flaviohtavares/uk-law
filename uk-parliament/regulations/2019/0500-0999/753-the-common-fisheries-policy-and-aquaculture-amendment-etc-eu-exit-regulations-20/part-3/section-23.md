# The Common Fisheries Policy and Aquaculture (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 23

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Commission Regulation [(EC) No 2056/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2001/2056) establishing additional technical measures for the recovery of the stocks of cod in the North Sea and to the West of Scotland[^key-6fd4d8d451a583f8f98ed8b9f7029c0a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 2056/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2001/2056) establishing additional technical measures for the recovery of the stocks of cod in the North Sea and to the West of Scotland is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1(1) for “Community” substitute “ United Kingdom ”.

(3) In Article 4—

- (a) in paragraph 4 omit the words from “However, during 2002” to the end;
- (b) in paragraph 5 for “Article 20(1) of Council Regulation [(EEC) No 2847/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1993/2847) of 12 October 1993 establishing a control system applicable to the common fisheries policy” substitute “ Article 47 of Council Regulation [(EC) No 1224/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1224) ”.

(4) In Article 8(1) omit the second sentence.

(5) In Article 9—

- (a) for “Notwithstanding” substitute “ Subject to obtaining the relevant licence or authorisation, and notwithstanding ”; and
- (b) for “Community” substitute “ United Kingdom ”.

(6) Omit Article 10.
