# The Common Fisheries Policy and Aquaculture (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 17

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Commission Regulation [(EC) No 494/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2002/494) establishing additional technical measures for the recovery of the stock of hake in ICES sub-areas III, IV, V, VI and VII and ICES divisions VIII a, b, d, e[^key-5aeb3ea6354ab95fa04e27bf808ba5a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 494/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2002/494) establishing additional technical measures for the recovery of the stock of hake in ICES sub-areas III, IV, V, VI and VII and ICES divisions VIII a, b, d, e is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1 for “fishing vessels” substitute “ United Kingdom fishing vessels ”.

(3) In Article 5(2)—

- (a) for “Article 20(1) of Regulation [(EEC) No 2847/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1993/2847) of 1 October 1993 establishing a control system applicable to the common fisheries policy” substitute “ Article 47 of Council Regulation [(EC) No 1224/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1224) ”; and
- (b) for “Article 20(1) of Regulation [(EEC) No 2847/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1993/2847)” in both places it occurs, substitute “ Article 47 of Council Regulation [(EC) No 1224/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1224) ”.

(4) Omit Article 7.
