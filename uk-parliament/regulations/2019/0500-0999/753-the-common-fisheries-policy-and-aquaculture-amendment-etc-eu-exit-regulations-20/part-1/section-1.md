# The Common Fisheries Policy and Aquaculture (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 1

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-e748da66b85fe5578f9d878f035e7207]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

These Regulations may be cited as the Common Fisheries Policy and Aquaculture (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force on the later of exit day or the day after the day on which they are made.
