# The Common Fisheries Policy and Aquaculture (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE — REVOCATIONS

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 2

[^c-605851387302449e0d18eeb37d2b98b2]The following legislation is revoked—

- (a) Council Regulation (EU) 2018/120 fixing for 2018 the fishing opportunities for certain fish stocks and groups of fish stocks, applicable in Union waters and, for Union fishing vessels, in certain non-Union waters;
- (b) Commission Implementing Decision of 19 March 2014 establishing a specific control and inspection programme for fisheries exploiting stocks of bluefin tuna in the Eastern Atlantic and the Mediterranean, swordfish in the Mediterranean and for fisheries exploiting stocks of sardine and anchovy in the Northern Adriatic Sea;
- (c) Council Regulation [(EC) No 1967/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1967) concerning management measures for the sustainable exploitation of fishery resources in the Mediterranean Sea;
- (d)[^c-785cbd7da660567da06d95f8aa643361] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1922/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1999/1922) laying down detailed rules for the application of Council Regulation [(EC) No 850/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1998/850) as regards conditions under which vessels exceeding eight metres length overall shall be permitted to use beam trawls within certain waters of the Community.
