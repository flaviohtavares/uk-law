# The Equality Act (Age Exceptions for Pension Schemes) (Amendment) Order 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

Laid before Parliament

24th April 2019

Coming into force

15th May 2019

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 61(8) and 207(4) of the Equality Act 2010[^f00001].

In accordance with section 61(9) of the Equality Act 2010, the Secretary of State has consulted such persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Equality Act (Age Exceptions for Pension Schemes) (Amendment) Order 2019 and comes into force on 15th May 2019.

## 2. Amendments to the Equality Act (Age Exceptions for Pension Schemes) Order 2010

(1) The Equality Act (Age Exceptions for Pension Schemes) Order 2010[^f00002] is amended in accordance with this article.

(2) In Schedule 1 (occupational pension schemes: excepted rules, practices, actions and decisions) in paragraph 14(1)(a)[^f00003] for “65” substitute “that member’s state pension age”[^f00004].

(3) In Schedule 1 in paragraph 14(2) in the definition of “relevant state retirement pension rate” for the words “paragraph 2(5) of Schedule 28 to the Finance Act” substitute “regulation 4(3)(b) (pensions bridging: pensionable age on or after 6th April 2016) of the Registered Pension Schemes (Bridging Pensions) and Appointed Day Regulations 2016[^f00005]”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Equality Act (Age Exceptions for Pension Schemes) Order 2010 ([S.I. 2010/2133](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2133)) (“the 2010 Order”).

Article 2(2) amends an exception in the 2010 Order relating to an occupational pension scheme’s age related equality obligations under the Equality Act [2010 (c.15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15). The effect of the amendment is to update the age related exception so that occupational pension schemes can continue to reduce a pensioner member’s pension benefits by an amount not exceeding the relevant state retirement pension rate with effect from any time between age 60 and the date they reach their state pension age.

Article 2(3) amends the definition of “relevant state retirement pension rate”. The term was defined in accordance with the meaning given in paragraph 2(5) of Schedule 28 to the Finance Act [2004 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12). Paragraph 2(5) was repealed by section 20(1) of the Finance Act [2016 (c.24)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/24). The term was re-defined in the Registered Pension Schemes (Bridging Pensions) and Appointed Day Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/1005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/1005)) (“the 2016 Regulations”). Article 2(3) updates the definition in the 2010 Order so it refers to the relevant part of the definition in the 2016 Regulations.

An impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is published with the Explanatory Memorandum alongside this instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
