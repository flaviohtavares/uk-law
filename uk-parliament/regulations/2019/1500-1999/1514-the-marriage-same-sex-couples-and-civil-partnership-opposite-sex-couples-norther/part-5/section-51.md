# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 51

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Consequential amendments[^key-109b4b2c40935dc375d50e453f842b16]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 20(9) of the Marriage (Same Sex Couples) Act 2013 (amendments made by Part 2 of Schedule 5 do not extend to Northern Ireland), after “Northern Ireland” insert “(but see regulation 42(1) of the Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019)”.

**52.**[^key-738f75b2718259120b5fe4376bfd4dbc][^key-109b4b2c40935dc375d50e453f842b16] In article 3(2) of the Marriage and Civil Partnership (Scotland) Act 2014 (Consequential Provisions and Modifications) Order 2014[^f00068] (provisions of the Order extending to England and Wales, and Scotland, only), after “Scotland only” insert “(but see regulation 43(1) of the Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019)”.
