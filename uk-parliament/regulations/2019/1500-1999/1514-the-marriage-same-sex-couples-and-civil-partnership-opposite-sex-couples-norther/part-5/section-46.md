# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 46

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Foreign gender change and marriage[^c-6329371b88ec04c3cd07ef0afac348e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 21 (foreign gender change and marriage) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit subsection (1A)[^f00064] (subsections (2) to (5) apply to Northern Ireland only).

(3) Omit subsections (2) to (5)[^f00065] (non-recognition of foreign marriage following foreign gender change).
