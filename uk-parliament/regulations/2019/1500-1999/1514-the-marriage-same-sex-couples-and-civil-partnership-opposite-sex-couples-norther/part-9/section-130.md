## 130. Housing on Farms Act (Northern Ireland) 1972[^c-73105e2b8684d59b67b03dcc64a658e9]

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 16 of the Housing on Farms Act (Northern Ireland) 1972[^f00214], in the definition of “family”, after “or marriage” insert “or civil partnership”.
