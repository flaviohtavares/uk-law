# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 143

>

## 143. Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^c-8ce2f287a0f56ac6bdbfb1dc7d0ce6f0]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00230] in each of the following provisions, for “husband” substitute “spouse”—

- (a) Article 85ZE(7)(a);
- (b) Article 85ZG(7)(a)[^f00231].
