# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 139

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 139. Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^c-10d62a8a615f94487b97895a48d267a2]

*Extent: N.I.*

In paragraph 3(c)(ii) of Schedule 1 to the Judgments Enforcement (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00226] (amounts deductible for purposes of a superannuation scheme are deductible in calculating “attachable earnings”), for “widows,” substitute “surviving spouses, surviving civil partners,”.
