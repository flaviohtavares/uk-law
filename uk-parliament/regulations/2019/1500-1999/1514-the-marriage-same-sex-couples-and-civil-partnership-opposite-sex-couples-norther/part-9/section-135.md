# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 135

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 135. Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^c-36d1cf102b5f5f46158a9334e0af5b11]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00219] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 29A[^f00220]—

- (a) in paragraph (9), for the words from “if—” to the end substitute “if the two persons are married to, or civil partners of, each other, or are living together as if spouses of each other.”;
- (b) omit paragraph (10).

(3) In Article 31AA(6)(a)[^f00221], after “marriage” insert “or civil partnership”.
