# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 134

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 134. Fatal Accidents (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^c-955f5a3e634995649e619c4921eadfc0]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 5(3) of the Fatal Accidents (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00218] (disregard of certain post-death benefits accruing to widows)—

- (a) for “widow”, in both places it occurs, substitute “surviving spouse”,
- (b) for “her husband”, substitute “that person’s spouse”, and
- (c) for “her”, in the second place it occurs, substitute “the surviving spouse’s”.
