## 127. King George VI Memorial Youth Council Act (Northern Ireland) 1957[^c-ad7bcf5ff3fc8f223b5e47f3465216af]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The First Schedule to the King George VI Memorial Youth Council Act (Northern Ireland) 1957[^f00210] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 23 (superannuation), for “wives, widows”, in both places it occurs, substitute “spouses, surviving spouses, civil partners, surviving civil partners”.
