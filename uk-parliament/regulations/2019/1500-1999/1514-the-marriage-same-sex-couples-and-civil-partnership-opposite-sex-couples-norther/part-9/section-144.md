# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 144

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 144. Employment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^key-45195b02149ff8acdc95fbe39a38d83a]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 11 of the Employment (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^f00232] (power to require information) in paragraph (2)(b), for “spouse or” substitute “spouse, civil partner or other”.
