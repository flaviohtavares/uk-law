# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 132

> Text as at 2020-12-07.

## 132. Solicitors (Northern Ireland) Order 1976[^c-8868d6dd6283ab8e7a9462aed4c19737]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 28(2)(b) of the Solicitors (Northern Ireland) Order 1976[^f00216], for “widow” substitute “surviving spouse or civil partner”.
