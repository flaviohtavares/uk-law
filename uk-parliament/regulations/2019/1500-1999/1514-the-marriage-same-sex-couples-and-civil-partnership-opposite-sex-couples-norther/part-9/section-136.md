# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 136

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 136. Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^key-1969389fdb3362c51737e02102541cdd]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Schedule 4 to the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^f00222], in each of the following provisions, for “widow” substitute “surviving spouse or civil partner”—

- (a) in Case 14, paragraph (a);
- (b) in Case 15, paragraphs (a) and (d);
- (c) in Case 16, paragraph (1)(a) and (b)(i) and (ii).
