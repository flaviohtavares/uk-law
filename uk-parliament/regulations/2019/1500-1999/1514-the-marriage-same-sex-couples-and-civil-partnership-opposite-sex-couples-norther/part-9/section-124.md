# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 124

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 124. Criminal Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 1945[^c-e0b382c9ecc2cdf838211791cd4e942c]

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 37 of the Criminal Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 1945[^f00206] (replacement of presumption of marital coercion with a statutory defence thereof)—

- (a) for “wife”, in the second place it occurs, substitute “spouse or civil partner”;
- (b) for “husband”, in the third place it occurs, substitute “other spouse or civil partner”.
