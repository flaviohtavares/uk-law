## 131. Land Acquisition and Compensation (Northern Ireland) Order 1973[^c-dafc74490b241993b12b78ca44b5c6ef]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 47(6)(a)(ii) of the Land Acquisition and Compensation (Northern Ireland) Order 1973[^f00215], after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”.
