# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 138

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. Domestic Proceedings (Northern Ireland) Order 1980[^key-f6f914341ba46484143ee502ad4c0a6f]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 2(2) of the Domestic Proceedings (Northern Ireland) Order 1980[^f00224], in the definition of “child”, for the words from “includes” to the end substitute “is to be construed in accordance with Article 155(1) of the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00225]”.
