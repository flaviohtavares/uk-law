# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 129

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Civil Evidence Act (Northern Ireland) 1971[^c-19d87a70cd7acc62206ff1c064d3b048]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Civil Evidence Act (Northern Ireland) 1971[^f00212] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 10(3) and (4)[^f00213], for “husband or wife” substitute “spouse or civil partner”.

(3) In section 14(2), in the words after the definition of “legal proceedings”—

- (a) for “husband or wife” substitute “spouse or civil partner”;
- (b) after “married to” insert “, or the civil partner of,”.
