## 128. Land Registration Act (Northern Ireland) 1970[^c-2675efd6da823eb03d3211aef6995a27]

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 82(3) of the Land Registration Act (Northern Ireland) 1970[^f00211], for “husband or wife” substitute “spouse or civil partner”.
