# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 172

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 172. Pre-13th January 2020 will is not revoked by recognition of existing overseas relationship[^c-8f8443f067254174506510dc4bc465a4]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where a person—

- (a) is treated as a civil partner by virtue of a relevant pre-commencement relationship, and
- (b) has, before 13th January 2020, made a will,

Article 13A of the Wills and Administration Proceedings (Northern Ireland) Order 1994[^f00288] is not to apply to that will.
