# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 174

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 174. Disapplication and modification of earlier transitional provision[^c-b74578f802086802ba7a2fa74d7f4a2d]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Articles 3 and 4 of the Civil Partnership (Treatment of Overseas Relationships) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00289] do not apply in relation to a relevant pre-commencement relationship.

(2) Article 5 of that Order does not apply in relation to a relationship within regulation 173(1).
