## 107. Pension Protection Fund (General and Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^c-94c0ed5ee4270571786605be3dfb2ac7]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the Pension Protection Fund (General and Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00170], in the definition of “relevant partner” in regulation 1(2)—

- (a) for “— (a)” substitute “but”, and
- (b) for the words from “husband and wife” to the end substitute “spouses of each other;”.
