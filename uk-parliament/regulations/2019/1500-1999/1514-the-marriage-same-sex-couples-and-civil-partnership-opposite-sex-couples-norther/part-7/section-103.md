# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 103

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103. Pension Protection Fund (Compensation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^c-b6dcad8c8c508ce8904a786c1cd0153f]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 1(2) of the Pension Protection Fund (Compensation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00166], in the definition of “relevant partner”—

- (a) for “— (a)” substitute “but”, and
- (b) for the words from “are husband and wife” to the end substitute “were spouses of each other;”.
