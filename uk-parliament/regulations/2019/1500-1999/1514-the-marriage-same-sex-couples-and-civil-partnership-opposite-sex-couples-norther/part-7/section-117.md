# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 117

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117. Discretionary Support Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^c-3c05104fc930709aa463e4198bddcec4]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the Discretionary Support Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00182], in regulation 3(1), in the definition of “couple”—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “a man and a woman who are married to” substitute “, two people who are married to, or civil partners of,”,
- (b) for paragraph (b) substitute—

  > - (b) two people who are not married to, or civil partners of, each other but are living together as if spouses of each other;

  ,
- (c) omit paragraphs (c) and (d), but not the “or” at the end, and
- (d) omit the words after paragraph (e).
