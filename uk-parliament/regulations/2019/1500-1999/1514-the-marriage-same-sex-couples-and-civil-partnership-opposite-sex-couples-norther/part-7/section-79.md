# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 79

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 79. Social Security (Widow’s Benefit, Retirement Pensions and Other Benefits) (Transitional) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1979[^c-d0e051b87e02f6a342f4fc6c11e08d19]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the Social Security (Widow’s Benefit, Retirement Pensions and Other Benefits) (Transitional) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1979[^f00139], in regulation 7(10) (person whose former spouse was a pre-1975 contributor)—

- (a) after “former spouse”, in each place it occurs, insert “or former civil partner”,
- (b) after “said spouse” insert “or civil partner”,
- (c) after “former spouse’s” insert “or former civil partner’s”, and
- (d) after “whichever is the earlier” insert “(or, in the case of a civil partnership, the year 1975/76)”.
