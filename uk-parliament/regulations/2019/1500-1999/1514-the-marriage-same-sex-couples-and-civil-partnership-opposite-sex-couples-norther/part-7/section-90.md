# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 90

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 90. Contracting-out (Transfer and Transfer Payment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^c-e113793fbacb7fafd4f854e4e5a0f6d2]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the Contracting-out (Transfer and Transfer Payment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00151], in paragraph 6 of Schedule 2, after “widower’s” insert “or surviving civil partner’s”.
