# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 99

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 99. State Pension Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^c-e76f7e843d0a6e9bf18080f59911e6f4]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 1(2) of the State Pension Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00162], for the definition of “couple” substitute—

> - “couple” means—
>   - (a) two people who are married to, or civil partners of, each other and are members of the same household, or
>   - (b) two people who are not married to, or civil partners of, each other but are living together as if spouses of each other;

.
