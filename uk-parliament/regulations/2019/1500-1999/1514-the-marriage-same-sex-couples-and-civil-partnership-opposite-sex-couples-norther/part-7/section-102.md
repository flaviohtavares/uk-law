# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 102

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Financial Assistance Scheme Regulations 2005[^key-c947f3c5d6ed53460f9365b9631384f4]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 2(1) of the Financial Assistance Scheme Regulations 2005[^f00165], for the definition of “partner” substitute—

> - “partner” means a person who was not married to, or in a civil partnership with, the qualifying member but who was living with that member as if that person and the qualifying member were a married couple or civil partners;

.
