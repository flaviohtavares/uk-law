# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 78

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1978[^c-a0c6e1d8a6431f44e787765cefd1f129]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 3 in Schedule 3 to the Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1978[^f00137] (which sets out regulations 2 and 3 of the Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1978[^f00138] as continued in force with modifications), after “former spouse” insert “or former civil partner”.
