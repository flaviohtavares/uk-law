# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 101

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Travelling Expenses and Remission of Charges Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004[^c-ec85dcd0d69f1de8301a16278812488b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Travelling Expenses and Remission of Charges Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00164] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2—

- (a) in the definition of “couple”, for “means a married couple or unmarried couple as specified under” substitute “has the meaning given by”, and
- (b) in the definition of “partner” omit “married or an unmarried”.

(3) In Schedule 1, in Table A, in the paragraph 8A treated as inserted in Schedule 8 to the 1987 Regulations, omit “married or unmarried”.
