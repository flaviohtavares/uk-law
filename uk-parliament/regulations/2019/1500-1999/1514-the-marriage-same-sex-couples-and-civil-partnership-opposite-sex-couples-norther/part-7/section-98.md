# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 98

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 98. Tax Credit (Appeals) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002[^c-3a61530404c4106fed260b0f75a40424]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 1(3) of the Tax Credits (Appeals) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00161], for the definition of “couple” substitute—

> - “couple” means—
>   - (a) two people who are married to, or civil partners of, each other and are members of the same household, or
>   - (b) two people who are not married to, or civil partners of, each other but are living together as if spouses of each other;

.
