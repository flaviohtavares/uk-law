# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 109

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 109. Rate Relief regulations of 2007[^c-558735af21b8b921a8cec2511bbce31e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In the Rate Relief (Qualifying Age) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00173], omit regulation 52(4) and (5) (competence and compellability of spouses as witnesses).

(2) In the Rate Relief (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00174], omit regulation 53(4) and (5) (competence and compellability of spouses as witnesses).

(3) The amendments made by paragraphs (1) and (2) apply only in relation to offences committed on or after 13th January 2020.
