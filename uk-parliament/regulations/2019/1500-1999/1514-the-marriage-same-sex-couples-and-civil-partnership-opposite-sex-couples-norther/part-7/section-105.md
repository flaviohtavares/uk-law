## 105. Naval, Military and Air Forces etc (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006[^c-1f83cc8900acb06fd362b9e3e9eee89f]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Schedule 6 to the Naval, Military and Air Forces etc (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006[^f00168], in item 26, in the third column, after “who has not” insert “married or”.
