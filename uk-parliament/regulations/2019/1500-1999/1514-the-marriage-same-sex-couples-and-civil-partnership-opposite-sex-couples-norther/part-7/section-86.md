## 86. Social Security (Additional Pension) (Contributions Paid in Error) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^c-9619e69087181da78134528bfc4bea71]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulations 2(3) and 3(2) of the Social Security (Additional Pension) (Contributions Paid in Error) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00147], after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”.
