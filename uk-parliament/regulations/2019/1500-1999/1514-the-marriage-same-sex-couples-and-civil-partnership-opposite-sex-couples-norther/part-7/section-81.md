# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 81

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 81. Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^c-50e9f3bbf0e989aa755f6ecb3a3b530c]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 2 of the Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00141], for the definition of “couple” substitute—

> - “couple” means—
>   - (a) two people who are married to, or civil partners of, each other and are members of the same household, or
>   - (b) two people who are not married to, or civil partners of, each other but are living together as if spouses of each other;

.
