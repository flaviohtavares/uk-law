# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 92

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Occupational Pension Schemes (Scheme Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^c-3c45e930f3c85be21009579656e97daa]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the Occupational Pension Schemes (Scheme Administration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00153], in regulation 7(d), for “husband or wife” substitute “spouse or civil partner”.
