# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 80

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 80. Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^c-09b56902c8b6dc22c0cb7cd50adf0c7b]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 2(1) of the Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00140], for the definition of “couple” substitute—

> - “couple” means—
>   - (a) two people who are married to, or civil partners of, each other and are members of the same household, or
>   - (b) two people who are not married to, or civil partners of, each other but are living together as if spouses of each other;

.
