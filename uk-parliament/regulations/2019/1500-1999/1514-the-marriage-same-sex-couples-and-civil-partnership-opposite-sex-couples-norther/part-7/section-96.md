# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 96

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 96. Child Support (Maintenance Calculations and Special Cases) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^c-abef92e00dd617fa1713734d2e6ee56f]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 1(2) of the Child Support (Maintenance Calculations and Special Cases) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00159], for the definition of “couple” substitute—

> - “couple” means—
>   - (a) two people who are married to, or civil partners of, each other and are members of the same household, or
>   - (b) two people who are not married to, or civil partners of, each other but are living together as if spouses of each other;

.
