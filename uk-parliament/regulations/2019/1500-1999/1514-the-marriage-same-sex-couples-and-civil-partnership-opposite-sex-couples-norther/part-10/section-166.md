# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 166

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 166. Assembly Members (Independent Financial Review and Standards) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^c-6afe6162d733f8b9e225846dd3fc0b0a]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the Assembly Members (Independent Financial Review and Standards) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00275], in each of the following provisions, in the definition of “cohabitant”, for the words from “of—” to the end substitute “of two people who are living together as if spouses of each other”—

- (a) paragraph 3 of Schedule 1;
- (b) paragraph 3 of Schedule 3.
