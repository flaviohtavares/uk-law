# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 164

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 164. Regional Business Services Organisation (Membership and Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^c-7f4cd09b28f1caf6cd7579b80c77e044]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulation 9 of the Regional Business Services Organisation (Membership and Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00273] (disclosure of pecuniary interest and exclusion of Chair and members of the RBSO in proceedings on account of pecuniary interest) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (4), in the words after sub-paragraph (b)—

- (a) after “persons” insert “or civil partners”;
- (b) after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”.
