# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 151

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 151. Court fees orders of 1996[^c-f548d711b715cb643f4252d248471dd0]

*Extent: N.I.*

In each of the following provisions, for “married couple or an unmarried couple (as defined respectively in section 3(5) and (6)” substitute “couple (as defined by section 3(5A)[^f00252]”—

- (a) Article 9(2)(d)(i) of the Court of Judicature Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00253];
- (b) Article 9(2)(d)(i) of the Judgment Enforcement Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00254];
- (c) Article 10(2)(d)(i) of the Magistrates’ Courts Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00255];
- (d) Article 8(2)(d)(i) of the County Court Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00256];
- (e) Article 9(2)(d)(i) of the Court of Judicature (Non-Contentious Probate) Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00257];
- (f) Article 8(2)(d)(i) of the Family Proceedings Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00258].
