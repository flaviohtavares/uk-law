## 159. Regulation and Improvement Authority (Registration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^c-58e96630f74ee76ccc9f37d23f4ccf7e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulation 2 of the Regulation and Improvement Authority (Registration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00268] (interpretation) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1), in the definition of “relative”—

- (a) in paragraph (a), after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”;
- (b) in paragraph (b), for “his or his spouse” substitute “the person or the person’s spouse or civil partner”;
