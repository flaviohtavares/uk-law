# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 150

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 150. Motor Vehicles (International Circulation) Order (Northern Ireland) 1990[^key-f311942c94b04292a407ec95e8f863b0]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the Motor Vehicles (International Circulation) Order (Northern Ireland) 1990[^f00251] in each of the following provisions, for “wife or husband” substitute “spouse or civil partner”—

- (a) Article 3(7), in paragraph (a) of the definition of “dependant”;
- (b) Article 4(4)(a).
