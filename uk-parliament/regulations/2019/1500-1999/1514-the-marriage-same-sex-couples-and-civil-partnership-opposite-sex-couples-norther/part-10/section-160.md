# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 160

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 160. Nursing Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^c-f6e7d694163cfc71aa5418a716790fb6]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 2 of the Nursing Homes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00269] (interpretation), in paragraph (1), in the definition of relative—

- (a) in each of paragraphs (a), (b) and (c), after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”;
- (b) for the words from “references” to the end substitute “references to “spouse or civil partner”, in relation to any person, include a former spouse or former civil partner and a person who is living with the person as if they were spouses of each other;”.
