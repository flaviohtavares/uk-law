# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 149

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 149. Extension to Northern Ireland of amendments made in consequence of the introduction of same-sex marriage in England and Wales[^c-86e453706d5ad000db69ff82466e04a3]

*Extent: N.I.*

The amendments made by the following provisions extend also to Northern Ireland—

- (a) in Schedule 1 to the Marriage (Same Sex Couples) Act 2013 (Consequential Provisions) Order 2014[^f00237] (consequential amendments to subordinate legislation)—
  - (i) paragraph 5 (which amends the Merchant Shipping (Maintenance of Seamen’s Dependants) Regulations 1972)[^f00238]);
  - (ii) paragraph 7 (which amends the Merchant Shipping (Returns of Birth and Deaths) Regulations 1979[^f00239]);
  - (iii) paragraph 31 (which amends the Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations 2001[^f00240]);
  - (iv) paragraph 48(1), (3), (4) and (5) (which amends the Attributable Benefits Scheme as applied to the navy and marines);
  - (v) paragraph 50(1), (3), (4) and (5) (which amends the Attributable Benefits Scheme as applied to the army);
  - (vi) paragraph 52(1), (3), (4) and (5) (which amends the Attributable Benefits Scheme as applied to the air force);
- (b) in the Marriage (Same Sex Couples) Act 2013 (Consequential and Contrary Provisions and Scotland) Order 2014[^f00241]—
  - (i) in Schedule 1—
    - (aa) paragraph 2 (which amends the Pensions Commutation Act 1871[^f00242]);
    - (bb) paragraph 3 (which amends the Local Government (Emergency Provisions) Act 1916[^f00243]);
    - (cc) paragraph 17 (which amends the Transport Act 1978[^f00244]);
  - (ii) in Schedule 3, paragraph 1 (which amends the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971[^f00245]);
- (c) in Schedule 1 to the Marriage (Same Sex Couples) Act 2013 and Marriage and Civil Partnership (Scotland) Act 2014 (Consequential Provisions) Order 2014[^f00246], paragraph 2 (which amends the Service Departments Registers Order 1959[^f00247]);
- (d) in the Schedule to the Marriage (Same Sex Couples) Act 2013 (Consequential and Contrary Provisions and Scotland) and Marriage and Civil Partnership (Scotland) Act 2014 (Consequential Provisions) Order 2014[^f00248]—
  - (i) paragraph 4 (which amends the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Special Provisions) Act 1957[^f00249]);
  - (ii) paragraph 10 (which amends the Forgery and Counterfeiting Act 1981[^f00250]).
