# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 153

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 153. Northern Ireland Social Care Council (Appointments and Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^c-b114fca4ee658692c38fdb92a4d16415]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 10 of the Northern Ireland Social Care Council (Appointments and Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00260] (disclosure of pecuniary interest etc), in paragraph (4), in the words after paragraph (b), for “married persons living together the interest of one spouse” substitute “two people who are spouses, or civil partners, of each other and who are living together, the interest of one of them”.
