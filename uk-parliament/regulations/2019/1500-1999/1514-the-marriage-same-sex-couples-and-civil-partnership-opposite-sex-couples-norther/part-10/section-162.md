## 162. Probation Board for Northern Ireland Victim Information Scheme 2005[^c-85840df790403c904a50fbf99d4489a7]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 3(4)(a) of the Probation Board for Northern Ireland Victim Information Scheme 2005[^f00271], after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”.
