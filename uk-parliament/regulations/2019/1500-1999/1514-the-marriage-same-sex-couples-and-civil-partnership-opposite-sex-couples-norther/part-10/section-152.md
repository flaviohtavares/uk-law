## 152. Individual Learning Accounts Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000[^c-c64c650e22c18923331ff43a551eff1e]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 4 of the Individual Learning Accounts Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00259] (meaning of “eligible person”) in each of paragraphs (3)(b), (c) and (d), after “spouse,” insert “civil partner,”.
