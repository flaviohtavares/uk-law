# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 158

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 158. Foyle Area (Control of Drift and Draft Net Fishing) Regulations 2004[^key-840078fad97c6a7ddcbe4737b5c8fae7]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Foyle Area (Control of Drift and Draft Net Fishing) Regulations 2004[^f00266] are amended as follows.

(2) In each of the following provisions, after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”—

- (a) regulation 2, in the definition of “single parent”;
- (b) regulation 4(3)(b);
- (c) regulation 4(5);
- (d) regulation 4(6) (in both places “spouse” occurs);
- (e) regulation 4(9)(b);
- (f) regulation 4(11)(b).

(3) In regulation 5(2), for “or spouse” substitute “, spouse or civil partner”.

(4) In regulation 6A[^f00267], after “spouse” insert “, civil partner”.
