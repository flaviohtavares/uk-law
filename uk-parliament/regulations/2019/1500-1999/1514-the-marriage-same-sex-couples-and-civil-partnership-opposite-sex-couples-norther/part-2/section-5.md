# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 5. Parties being of the same sex no longer to be a ground on which a marriage is void[^c-3e12b7db6e1c4c5427b6384e857da47b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In Article 13(1) of the Matrimonial Causes (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^f00003] (grounds on which a marriage is void), omit sub-paragraph (e) (parties not respectively male and female).

(2) In section 4(1) of the Northern Ireland (Executive Formation and Exercise of Functions) Act 2018[^f00004], omit paragraph (b) (guidance connected to Article 13(1)(e) of the 1978 Order) and the preceding “and”.
