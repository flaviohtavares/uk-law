# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 67

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^c-d7edb0eff89e1ff3388966a7032c5ae3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00117], section 110(5) and (6) (competence and compellability of spouses as witnesses) are omitted.

(2) In consequence of paragraph (1), in Schedule 5 to the Healthy Start Scheme and Day Care Food Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00118], in the entry for section 110 of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) 1992—

- (a) in column 1, for “, (3)(a) and (5)” substitute “and (3)(a)”, and
- (b) in column 2, omit paragraph (d).

(3) The amendments made by paragraphs (1) and (2) apply only in relation to offences committed on or after 13th January 2020.
