## 59. Superannuation (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 1969[^key-953908576fed09a4401acd0d938eb736]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Superannuation (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland) 1969[^f00085] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 1(5) (meaning of “derivative benefit” in section 1(3)), after “widow’s” insert “, widower’s, surviving civil partner’s”.
