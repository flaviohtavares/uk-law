# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 65

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 65. Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^c-dc1c82d64adcbee86305a81143892343]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 171ZL(4B) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00095] (statutory adoption pay: meaning of “member of a couple”)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), after “Wales” insert “or Northern Ireland”, and
- (b) omit paragraph (b).
