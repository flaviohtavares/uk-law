# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 58

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Superannuation (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^key-61579ee580c4bb34e8af72491cd0d929]

*Extent: N.I.*

In paragraph 9(3) of Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00084] (power in relation to children adopted after end of marriage or relevant service), after “marriage”, in each place it occurs, substitute “or civil partnership”.
