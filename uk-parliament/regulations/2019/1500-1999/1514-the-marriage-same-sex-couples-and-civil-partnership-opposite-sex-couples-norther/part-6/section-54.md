# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 54

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Judicial Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 1951[^c-bddbea7b70e8f9c7783819aa85a85540]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Judicial Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 1951[^f00070] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 7[^f00071] (widows’, widowers’ and surviving civil partners’ pensions), after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) A surviving civil partner’s pension may not be granted in respect of a person’s service if—
>
> - (a) the person retired from relevant service before 13th January 2020, and
> - (b) power to grant the pension relies on the Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019.

.
