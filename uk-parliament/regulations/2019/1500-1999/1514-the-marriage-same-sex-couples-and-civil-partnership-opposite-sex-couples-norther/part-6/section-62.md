# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 62

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1979[^c-5d49068fd41470dce7fe8e71f7e7a312]

*Extent: N.I.*

For Article 5(2A) and (2B) of the Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1979[^f00088] substitute—

> (2A) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c), two persons are in a qualifying relationship if they are living together as if spouses of each other.

.
