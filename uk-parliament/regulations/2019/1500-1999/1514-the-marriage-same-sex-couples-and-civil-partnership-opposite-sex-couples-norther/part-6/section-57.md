# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 57

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 57. Lands Tribunal and Compensation Act (Northern Ireland) 1964[^c-0a6b911fba0e97f37d72674ec31f0fac]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Lands Tribunal and Compensation Act (Northern Ireland) 1964[^f00082] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 2A[^f00083] (election to opt out of relevant pension scheme)—

- (a) in subsection (2), in the definition of “judicial pension scheme”—
  - (i) after “widow’s” insert “, widower’s, surviving civil partner’s”, and
  - (ii) after “widow” insert “, or widower or surviving civil partner,”, and
- (b) in subsection (9)(b), after “widow’s” insert “, or widower’s or surviving civil partner’s,”.
