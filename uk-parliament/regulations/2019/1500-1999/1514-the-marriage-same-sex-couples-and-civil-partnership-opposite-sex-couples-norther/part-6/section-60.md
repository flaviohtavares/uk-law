# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 60

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 60. Social Security (Northern Ireland) Act 1975[^c-02fc3de4632572a43068969359640313]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Schedule 10 to the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Act 1975[^f00086] (superannuation allowances for Social Security Commissioners)—

- (a) in paragraph 7(8)(b) (interpretation), for “widow’s” substitute “surviving spouse’s, surviving civil partner’s”, and
- (b) in paragraph 7B(3)(b) (persons who may appeal), for “widow or widower,” substitute “surviving spouse or surviving civil partner,”.
