## 61. Social Security Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1975[^key-f604b08a741d13054228d22790f89679]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In the Social Security Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1975[^f00087], Article 69 is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-50dc9de465b4b788ffe4dc59ba9aefaa] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-50dc9de465b4b788ffe4dc59ba9aefaa] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) In paragraph (5ZC)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (b), after “this paragraph” insert “, but this is subject to sub-paragraph (c)”, and
- (b) after sub-paragraph (b) insert—

  > - (c) applies to a widow’s or widower’s or surviving civil partner’s pension, where the pension is one to which entitlement relies on the Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, only in respect of amounts payable on or after 13th January 2020;

  .
