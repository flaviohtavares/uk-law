# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 70

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^c-32d7602edd90b317603b4482794e5464]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 68 of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00129] (child benefit for lone parents)—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(b), for “as his spouse or civil partner” substitute “as if spouses of each other”, and
- (b) omit paragraph (3).
