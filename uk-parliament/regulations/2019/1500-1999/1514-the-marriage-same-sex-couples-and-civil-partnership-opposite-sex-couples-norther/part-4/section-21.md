# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 21

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 21. Fatherhood[^c-8461ad944486e9b7043731b390fdba36]

*Extent: N.I.*

In section 2(1)(a) of the Family Law Act (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00022] (presumption, where man is married to mother of a child at the birth or at any time during the pregnancy, that man is father of the child), after “was married to” insert “, or was the civil partner of,”.
