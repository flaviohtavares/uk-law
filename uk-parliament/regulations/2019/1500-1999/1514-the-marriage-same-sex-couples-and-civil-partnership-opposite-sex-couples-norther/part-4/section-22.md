# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 22

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Registration of births[^key-77471ab4d641b8988b2f3c692bf50281]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Births and Deaths Registration (Northern Ireland) Order 1976[^f00023] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 2(2A)[^f00024] (interpretation of references to marital status of parents at time of child’s birth), after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.

(3) In Article 14[^f00025] (registration of father where parents not married)—

- (a) in the heading, after “not married” insert “or civil partners”, and
- (b) in paragraph (1), after “not married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.

(4) In Article 18(1)(b)[^f00026] (re-registration of birth in certain cases)—

- (a) in the words before paragraph (i), after “not married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”, and
- (b) in paragraph (i), after “were so married” insert “or were civil partners of each other”.

(5) In Article 19(7) (re-registration of births of legitimated persons: interpretation), after “marriage” insert “or civil partnership”.

(6) In Article 37(7)(a)(i) and (ii) and (b)(i)[^f00027] (parents as qualified applicants for registration of change of child’s name), after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.
