# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 33

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Fostering[^c-f24c16c64c43dd1cde20345edca6b22f]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Schedule 1 to the Foster Placement (Children) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00049] (information as to prospective foster parent and other family and household members), in paragraph 1—

- (a) after “marital” insert “or civil partnership”;
- (b) after “marriage” insert “or civil partnership”.
