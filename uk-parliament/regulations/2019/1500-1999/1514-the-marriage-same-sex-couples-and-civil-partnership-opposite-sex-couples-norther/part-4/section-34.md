# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 34

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Child abduction[^key-925b2000d250969589881eb3234aca4c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Child Abduction (Northern Ireland) Order 1985[^f00050] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 2(2)(d)[^f00051] (interpretation of references to children and parents), after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.

(3) In Article 3(2)(b)[^f00052] (“connected person” includes person believed to be father of a child born to unmarried parents), after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.

(4) In Article 4—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(a) and (b)[^f00053] (meaning of “parent” etc), after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”;
- (b) in paragraph (3)(a) (defences), after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.
