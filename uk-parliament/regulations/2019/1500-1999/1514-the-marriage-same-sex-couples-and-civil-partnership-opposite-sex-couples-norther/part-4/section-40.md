# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 40

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Representations procedure[^key-a33a0850799f8e795150a4b0bbf25aaf]

*Extent: N.I.*

In regulation 10 of the Representations Procedure (Children) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00058] (application to voluntary organisations and registered children’s homes), in paragraph (2)(c), after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”.
