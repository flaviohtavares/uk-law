# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 37

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Declarations as to marital status: jurisdiction of the courts[^c-8bd621beafff30b22ab6869709ed67d5]

*Extent: N.I.*

In Article 31 of the Matrimonial and Family Proceedings (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^f00057] (declarations as to marital status)—

- (a) in paragraph (2) (when court has jurisdiction), after “if (and only if)” insert “paragraph (2A) applies or”, and
- (b) after paragraph (2) insert—

  > (2A) This paragraph applies where—
  >
  > - (a) the two people concerned are of the same sex and married each other under the law of Northern Ireland, and
  > - (b) it appears to the court to be in the interests of justice to assume jurisdiction in the case.

  .
