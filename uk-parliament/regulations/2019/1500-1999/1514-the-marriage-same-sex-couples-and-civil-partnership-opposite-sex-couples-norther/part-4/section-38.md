# The Marriage (Same-sex Couples) and Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2019, section 38

> Text as at 2020-12-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Property rights where parents not married to, or civil partners of, each other[^key-df5985fcf3a332577fe7e35e46b5d4b2]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Article 157 of the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (property rights) is amended as follows.

(2) After “not married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.

**39.**[^key-7076b7338814de26fcdba30233a95b86][^key-df5985fcf3a332577fe7e35e46b5d4b2] (1) Schedule 6 to the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (property rights where parents not married to each other) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, after “not married to” insert “or civil partners of”.

(3) In paragraph 1 (succession on intestacy), in sub-paragraph (2), after “not married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.

(4) In paragraph 4 (entitlement to grant of probate etc), in sub-paragraph (1)(a), after “not married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.
