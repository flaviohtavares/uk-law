# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (England) Regulations 2019, section 14

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Introduction[^key-e449a26f1f12a22334a8085b6ba421dc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part applies if a plant health inspector suspects that a controlled plant pest or prohibited material is present or likely to be present, or becomes aware that a controlled plant pest or prohibited material is present, on any premises in England.

(2) In this Part—

- “**premises**” includes any place, including any land, building, vehicle, vessel, aircraft, hovercraft, freight container, railway wagon, trailer or movable building or structure;
- “**prohibited material**” means—
  - (a) a plant, plant product or other object which is carrying, or is infested by or infected with, a controlled plant pest or may be carrying, or be infested by or infected with, a controlled plant pest;
  - (b) a plant, plant product or other object the entry of which into Great Britain[^key-7722d92e88c6a2299e2a576036e44e21] is prohibited under a[^key-56bfd09c37bf676e7998fc49e213bc82] plant health rule [^key-2f80a01136b48e2599ca59f8f321c719]...;
  - (c) a plant, plant product or other object the movement of which [^key-2c06966f943780fa2d81fdf76132b9bb]... into, within or from England, is prohibited under a[^key-cdb15773bfb6a755f41081ba99143d4e] plant health rule [^key-376bd5609892f2826a8fd56b93c8e8ec]....
