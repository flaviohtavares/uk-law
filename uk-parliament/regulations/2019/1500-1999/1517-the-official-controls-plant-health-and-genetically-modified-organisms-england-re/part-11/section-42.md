# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (England) Regulations 2019, section 42

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 42. Improper use of ... plant passports , Northern Ireland plant health labels or certificates[^key-f975f2105bbf9a9dd9f6f2e2ca8732c6][^key-394c55abe8cb71a98f08d5abc66c8bbd][^key-8d09e48d4b4edcbaa33458c014f69edb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person commits an offence if the person—

- (a) dishonestly issues a UK plant passport[^key-a2722886fc9d91538c24ec50bbc552b7] , a Northern Ireland plant health label[^key-be6aadb74b42d7e063edb4bbcbe81161] or a certificate;
- (b) dishonestly alters a [^key-072f432b3aa71cecbb01db7f8163a629]... plant passport , a Northern Ireland plant health label or a certificate; or
- (c) dishonestly re-uses a [^key-db015eb616e121a61a34ca77c3529bdc]... plant passport , a Northern Ireland plant health label or a certificate.

(2) In paragraph (1), “**certificate**” means a phytosanitary certificate for export, a phytosanitary certificate for re-export or a pre-export certificate.
