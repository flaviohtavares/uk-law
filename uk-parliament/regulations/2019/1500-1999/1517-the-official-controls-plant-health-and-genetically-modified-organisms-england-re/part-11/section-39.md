# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (England) Regulations 2019, section 39

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. Failure to comply with requirements of notices etc.[^c-5504f08b63894ef7c55b100c52d277b6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-d7d4765cc4dbf983f8d843b14a77824b] A person commits an offence if the person fails to comply with—

- (a) a provision or condition of a notice which has been served on the person under these Regulations or has effect under or by virtue of these Regulations;
- (b) a provision or condition of an authorisation or permit which has been granted to the person under these Regulations or has effect under or by virtue of these Regulations; or
- (c) a provision or condition of a direction given under these Regulations.

(2)[^c-c62b39860717652d4b8d611db3fe4275] Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply in relation to any notice served under Schedule 4A.
