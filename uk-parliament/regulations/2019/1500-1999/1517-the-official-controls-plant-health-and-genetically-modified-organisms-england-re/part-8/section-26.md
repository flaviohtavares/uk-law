# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (England) Regulations 2019, section 26

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Notification requirements in relation to other plants and plant products[^c-44245820e6c6d9d92f72b38489ab6015]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A professional operator who is bringing [^c-6ff5eab3af976a2d031ab97b9ec2497c]... into England any solid fuel wood from a third country to which Article 47(1) of the Official Controls Regulation does not apply[^c-96c3aeac085c0f783bbbd9cd54f0a084] must, before or no later than four days after the date of their arrival in England, provide written notification to a[^c-256c00d3c069e02f99428042388e7011] plant health inspector of the matters referred to in paragraph (2)—

- (a)[^c-921f4ca666fbec5df462a01273ff1be9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-921f4ca666fbec5df462a01273ff1be9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-921f4ca666fbec5df462a01273ff1be9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The matters are—

- (a) the expected date of the arrival of the consignment or, if the consignment has arrived in England, the date on which it first arrived in England;
- (b) the intended destination of the consignment, or if the consignment has arrived at its intended destination in England, its current location;
- (c) the genus, species and quantity of the [^c-059455ca88ebb8d24fa7439811c85502]... wood in the consignment;
- (d) the country from which the [^c-adbcb306473a3efb522aba3d848577cd]... wood has been, or is[^c-2e69ad84c6860ba35663cb0d1ca543db] to be, consigned;
- (e)[^c-2f7e2e1e719a0d2528c0f5f63d91d9c9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^c-a23125cb813985f3a2fb94c332679eeb] the address of the consignor; and
- (g) details of any phytosanitary treatments applied to the wood.

(3) In this regulation—

- [^c-2bd6d73f12cc49517280a2bf5e07caea]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**solid fuel wood**” means fuel wood in the form of logs, billets, twigs, faggots or other similar forms.
