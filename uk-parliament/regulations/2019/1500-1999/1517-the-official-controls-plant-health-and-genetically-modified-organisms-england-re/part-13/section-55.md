## 55. Amendment to secondary legislation on official controls in relation to genetically modified organisms[^c-dad01683c0058787447608184e676e24]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 2(1) of the Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food, Plant Health Fees etc.) Regulations 2019 [^c23956351], in the definition of “relevant legislation”, for “sub-paragraphs (a), (c) to (e), (f)” substitute “ sub-paragraphs (a) to (f) ”.
