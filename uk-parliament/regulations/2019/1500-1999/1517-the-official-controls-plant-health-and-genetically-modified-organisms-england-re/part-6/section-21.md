# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (England) Regulations 2019, section 21

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. Authorisations for other purposes[^key-dab0ab2ecb12a6e4e98b010a80e1f024]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-2da31e05b6f877e247d7f860f4489d5b] The appropriate authority may grant an authorisation to permit—

- (a) the carrying out of any activity specified in a plant health derogation;
- (b) the introduction into England, the movement within England, or the holding or multiplication in England, of a potential quarantine plant pest for official testing, scientific or educational purposes, trials, varietal selection or breeding; or
- (c) the carrying out of any other activity which requires the approval of the appropriate authority under , or by virtue of,[^key-9f16affb24f01b2334c712d7a9901131] the [^key-3f6797301ccf107a6314267c6b19852b]... Plant Health Regulation, the Official Controls Regulation , any regulations made under the Plant Health Regulation or the Official Controls Regulation,[^key-4702bb38d71b71326bb2dbb895e48c38] or these Regulations.

(2) An application for any such authorisation must be made to the appropriate authority in the manner and form required by the appropriate authority.

(3)[^key-6b1cacecb47e631d65e7c6881bb80f93] In paragraph (1)—

- “plant health derogation” means—
  - (a) a derogation from provisions of the [^key-5b20bcb9f8408e8fd0aaecf163f0d094]... Plant Health Regulation which is set out in assimilated[^key-a3d33baf519b32e5206c891b1c24fa76][^key-b208312709e3147b62d37fda10364d5a] law or regulations made under the [^key-5b20bcb9f8408e8fd0aaecf163f0d094]... Plant Health Regulation or the Official Controls Regulation; [^key-13fee3a433f2eb7a20db91b22e528159]...
  - (b) [^key-13fee3a433f2eb7a20db91b22e528159]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “potential quarantine plant pest” means a plant pest which is not a GB quarantine pest or a provisional GB quarantine pest[^key-76e9e7af22e2dc19cc8e29c520c76d20], but which, in the opinion of the appropriate authority, fulfils the criteria set out in Subsection 1 of Section 3 of Annex 1 to that Regulation or may fulfil the criteria in Subsection 2 of that Section.
