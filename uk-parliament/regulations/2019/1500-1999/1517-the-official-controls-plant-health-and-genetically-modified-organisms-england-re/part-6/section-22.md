# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (England) Regulations 2019, section 22

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Authorisations granted by an appropriate authority[^key-474fb25c1cc7a7eb5e5d3e73385c63d9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-1ea742b03b1b56c177286d91e68c6010] An authorisation granted by an appropriate authority for the purposes of the [^key-f6ebe55d4b1a27e662a21143eeef3c2d]... Plant Health Regulation or[^key-87178fd56637861791cc963ae189542c] the Official Controls Regulation or for the purposes of, or under,[^key-aff22ff76820a6be93ac4b5974794e8f] these Regulations must be in writing and may be granted—

- (a) subject to conditions;
- (b) for an indefinite period or a specified period.

(2) An authorisation granted by an appropriate authority may permit the appropriate authority to modify, suspend or revoke the authorisation at any time by notice in writing.
