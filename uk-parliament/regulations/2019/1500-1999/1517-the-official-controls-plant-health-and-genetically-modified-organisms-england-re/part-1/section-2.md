# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (England) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Interpretation: general[^key-a19c8eafe007be693b06e7fefb233baf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In these Regulations—

- “**the 2005 Order**” means the Plant Health (Forestry) Order 2005 [^c23956001];
- “**the 2015 Order**” means the Plant Health (England) Order 2015 [^c23956011];
- “**appropriate authority**” means the Secretary of State or the Forestry Commissioners, and is to be construed in accordance with regulation 6;
- “**commencement date**” means the date on which these Regulations come into force;
- “**controlled consignment**” means a consignment containing any plant, plant product or other object—
  - (a) which may not be brought into Great Britain[^key-cd29787e67c87d1c1d76c3f91568fcd8] without a phytosanitary certificate for export or a phytosanitary certificate for re-export pursuant to—
    - (i) Article 72 or 74 of the [^key-b433d022f92df1d8f11f8015abafbbfd]... Plant Health Regulation;
    - (ii) [^key-c7eb1e64510b1e4eef11bdfd7d47229a]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
    - (iii) any other [^key-b68d9e9370cc00004166d5c4fd7b6e94]... plant health rule, other than Article 73 of the [^key-b68d9e9370cc00004166d5c4fd7b6e94]... Plant Health Regulation; or
  - (b) which was exported from Great Britain to a third country and is returning to Great Britain following the refusal by that third country to allow its entry into the country;
- “**controlled plant pest**” means—
  - (a) a plant pest of a description specified in Annex 2, 2A,[^key-7aedc6c1aa7aebdded147deed7972b86] 3 or 4 to the Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation;
  - (b) [^key-78498e7cc523b567757cb3e955a8ef8b]
  - (c) a plant pest subject to any other [^key-b654d12a2de35ef0cf8545f4dcd7bd57]... plant health rule, including a potential quarantine plant pest within the meaning given in regulation 21(3)[^key-7b2f1c71a06f9ccba93bc2631babab02];
- “**Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2000/29)**” means Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2000/29) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community [^c23956021];
- [^key-a300b3873507893a37760f6e1267abe9]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**EU Plant Health Regulation**” means Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament and of the Council on protective measures against pests of plants [^c23956031] as it has effect in EU law[^key-e2936386893a19a6bfc3a9dafa9ed28c];
- [^key-6acbab6cdebd39ce22f14603c1e4c32a]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**ISPM 15**” means International Standard for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 of March 2002 on Guidelines for regulating wood packaging material in international trade, prepared by the Secretariat of the International Plant Protection Convention established by the Food and Agriculture Organisation of the United Nations [^c23956041];
- “[^key-4643c1d793484788144a35dbd19f569c]**Northern Ireland plant health label**” has the meaning given in regulation 2 of the Windsor Framework (Plant Health) Regulations 2023;
- “**Official Controls Regulation**” means Regulation (EU) 2017/625 of the European Parliament and of the Council on official controls and other official activities performed to ensure the application of food and feed law, rules on animal health and welfare, plant health and plant protection products, insofar as it applies to [^key-cb9179cd48f79ad41c8d80ce37f76131]... plant health rules [^c23956051];
- “**Phytosanitary Conditions Regulation**” means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/2072 establishing uniform conditions for the implementation of Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament and the Council, as regards protective measures against pests of plants [^c23956061];
- “[^key-9aac0cf6dd6562c781ba1c44a38bb1d2]**place of destination**” in relation to any specified goods, means the first place where the goods are delivered for unloading in Great Britain, and for this purpose “**specified goods**” means plants, plant products and other objects specified in the Schedule to the Official Controls and Phytosanitary Conditions (Amendment) Regulations 2021;
- “**plant health inspector**” means an official plant health officer appointed by the Secretary of State or the Forestry Commissioners;
- “[^key-3b9c7a9551362bf941dc65fe73b3c0a1]**Plant Health Regulation**” means Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament and of the Council on protective measures against pests of plants;
- “**plant health rule**” means a rule of a kind mentioned in Article 1(2)(g) of the Official Controls Regulation;
- “plant passport” means a passport which is a UK plant passport or other permitted plant passport;[^key-dc973d9fb8946e10fb830d6fb069ccb4]
- “**plant pest**” means a pest within the meaning given in Article 1(1) and (2) of the [^key-8623e24a1bb4a266a12fd98fd64d9b70]... Plant Health Regulation;
- “**regulated item**” means—
  - (a) any plant, plant product or other object to which a[^key-a7149941e09ed55a888b8c0a21223e6c] plant health rule applies, other than any plant, plant product or other object which is part of a controlled consignment; or
  - (b) a controlled plant pest.

(2) Unless the context otherwise requires, words and expressions which are not defined in these Regulations and appear in the [^key-048ebfed75d4ed2c7631457a10e249a4]... Plant Health Regulation or the Official Controls Regulation have the same meaning in these Regulations as they have in [^key-048ebfed75d4ed2c7631457a10e249a4]... Plant Health Regulation or the Official Controls Regulation (as the case may be).
