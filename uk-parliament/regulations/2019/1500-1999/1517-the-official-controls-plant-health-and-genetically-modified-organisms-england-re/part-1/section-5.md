# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (England) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Article 82 of the ... Plant Health Regulation: meaning of “close proximity”[^c-12bd5763a04d93b1ba644658d785c519][^c-53ee39edc833754f086a868ed9c8144e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of Article 82 of the [^c-94d0f5900ca069fed1703a8276c86a1f]... Plant Health Regulation, the premises of a registered operator are to be regarded as being in “close proximity” to other premises of that operator if any point on the boundary of its operative area, or of any of its operative areas, is within ten miles of any point on the boundary of the operative area, or of any of the operative areas, of the other premises.

(2) In paragraph (1), “**operative area**”, in relation to the premises of a registered operator, means—

- (a) in the case of premises which are used entirely by the registered operator to carry out one or more of the activities mentioned in Article 65(1) of the [^c-406439cfad2988b52905ac575fd7e5d1]... Plant Health Regulation, the area comprising those premises;
- (b) in the case of any other premises used by the registered operator to carry out one or more of the activities mentioned in Article 65(1) of the [^c-406439cfad2988b52905ac575fd7e5d1]... Plant Health Regulation, an area within the premises which is used by the registered operator to carry out any of those activities.
