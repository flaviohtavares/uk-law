# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (England) Regulations 2019, section 35

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Disclosure of information to other competent authorities[^key-74831af1d58056e1721124d8a9fa5c6a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State and the Forestry Commissioners may disclose information—

- (a) to each other for the purposes of the [^key-dc2ee8f82057e168893521b25a937a85]... Plant Health Regulation, the Windsor Framework (Plant Health) Regulations 2023,[^key-5f528285f3b75a1ffc152d96b00d05e9] the Official Controls Regulation or these Regulations; or
- (b) to any other competent authority in United Kingdom or to a CD authority[^key-70b40f6dd441fb90925ef3b752567a5c] for the purposes of the [^key-949de9b0c922b73db9a1199369d36ec1]... Plant Health Regulation , the Windsor Framework (Plant Health) Regulations 2023[^key-15b5480aee002aad12878ad9aa55d355] or the Official Controls Regulation.

(2) Nothing in paragraph (1) affects any other power or requirement of the Secretary of State or the Forestry Commissioners to disclose information.
