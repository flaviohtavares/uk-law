# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 21

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21.[^c-09981e818bac08d5193793f6d9b9f60d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1253DD (agreement of EEA competent authority) [^c23586451]—

- (a) in the heading, for “EEA competent authority” substitute “ third country competent authority ”;
- (b) in subsection (1)(b)(i) [^c23586461], for “another EEA State” substitute “ an equivalent third country or transitional third country ”;
- (c) in subsections (2) and (3), for “EEA competent authority” substitute “ third country competent authority ”.
