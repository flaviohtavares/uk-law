# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 6

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6.[^c-b45976c1b92923aa1d66efde5ae145bf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 494A (interpretation) [^c23586111]—

- (a) for the definition of “audit committee” substitute—

  > “**audit committee**” means a body which performs—
  >
  > - (a) the functions referred to in—
  >   - (i) rule 7.1.3 of the Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules sourcebook made by the Financial Conduct Authority [^c23586121] (audit committees and their functions) under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 [^c23586131], or
  >   - (ii) rule 2.4 of the Audit Committee Part of the Rulebook made by the Prudential Regulation Authority [^c23586141] (audit committee) under that Act,
  >
  >   as they have effect on IP completion day[^c-a701cb8212215c835c1cd2d6fd1d08d3], or
  > - (b) equivalent functions.

  ;
- (b) omit the definition of “Audit Directive”;
- (c) in the definition of “public interest entity”[^c23586151]—
  - (i) in paragraph (a), for “regulated market” substitute “ UK regulated market ”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (b), for “other than one listed in Article 2 of Directive 2013/36/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on access to the activity of credit institutions and investment firms” substitute “ which is a CRR firm within the meaning of Article 4(1)(2A) of that Regulation ”;
  - (iii) for paragraph (c), substitute—

    > - (c) a person who would be an insurance undertaking as defined in Article 2(1) of Council Directive [91/674/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1991/674) of 19 December 1991 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the annual accounts and consolidated accounts of insurance undertakings [^c23586161] as that Article had effect immediately before IP completion day[^c-89c85006be176250dfdf6667dc17199f], were the United Kingdom a member State;

    ;
- (d) omit the definitions of “regulated market” and “transferable securities”[^c23586171].
