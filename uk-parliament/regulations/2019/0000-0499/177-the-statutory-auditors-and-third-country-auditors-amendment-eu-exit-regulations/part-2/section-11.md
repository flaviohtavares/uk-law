# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 11

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11.[^c-b759ae6e212c5d8096d253f6291e9d13]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1210 (meaning of “statutory auditor” etc)—

- (a) in subsection (1)(c)(ii) [^c23586251], for “regulated market” substitute “ UK regulated market ”;
- (b) in subsection (3)—
  - (i) omit the definition of “bank”[^c23586261];
  - (ii) for the definition of “insurer” substitute—

    > “**insurer**” means a person who would be an insurance undertaking, as defined in Article 2(1) of Council Directive [91/674/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1991/674) of 19 December 1991 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the annual accounts and consolidated accounts of insurance undertakings as that Article had effect immediately before IP completion day[^c-fb63df9f729c25769aa54b05be20efbc], were the United Kingdom a member State;

    ;
  - (iii) omit the definition of “regulated market”[^c23586271].
