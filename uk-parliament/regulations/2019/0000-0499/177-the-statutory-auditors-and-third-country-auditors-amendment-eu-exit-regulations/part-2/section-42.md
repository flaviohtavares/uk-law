# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 42

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42.[^c-68bf9a857c888476af141c92aa153851]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 15D(4) (permitted disclosure of information obtained under compulsory powers) [^c23586811]—

- (a) in paragraph (aa)(i), for “UK-traded non-EEA companies” substitute “ UK-traded third country companies ”;
- (b) in paragraph (h), for “EU obligation” substitute “ retained EU obligation ”.
