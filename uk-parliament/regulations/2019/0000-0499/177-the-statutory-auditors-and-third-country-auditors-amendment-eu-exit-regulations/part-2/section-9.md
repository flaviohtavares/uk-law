# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 9

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9.[^key-54121c19a77813d317611a64cab6f883]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 539 (minor definitions), in the definition of “MiFID investment firm”[^c23586221]—

- (a) for “Article 4.1.1 of Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 on markets in financial instruments,” substitute “ Article 2(1A) of Regulation (EU) No. 600/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 on markets in financial instruments and amending Regulation (EU) No. 648/2012 [^c23586231] ”;
- (b) in paragraph (a), for “to which that Directive does not apply by virtue of Article 2 of that Directive” substitute “ which is exempted from the definition of “investment firm” by Schedule 3 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 (S.I. 2001/544) [^c23586241] ”.
