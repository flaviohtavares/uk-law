# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 15

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15.[^c-1d137a6f667f0884bcc37315c62f46d4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1241 (meaning of “**registered third country auditor**” and “**UK-traded non-EEA company**”)—

- (a) in the heading [^c23586301], for “UK-traded non-EEA company” substitute “ UK-traded third country company ”;
- (b) in subsection (2) [^c23586311]—
  - (i) for “UK-traded non-EEA company” substitute “ UK-traded third country company ”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (b), for “regulated market” substitute “ UK regulated market ” and omit “situated or operating in the United Kingdom”;
- (c) omit subsection (3) [^c23586321].
