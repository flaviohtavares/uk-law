# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 34

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34.[^key-81cd3cb4a2d6cb3499727601540decce]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 11 (recognised professional qualifications), in paragraph 9 (practical training)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (4)(b), omit “other than the United Kingdom”;
- (b) after sub-paragraph (4), insert—

  > (5) For the purpose of sub-paragraph (4), Gibraltar is to be treated as if it were an EEA State.

  .
