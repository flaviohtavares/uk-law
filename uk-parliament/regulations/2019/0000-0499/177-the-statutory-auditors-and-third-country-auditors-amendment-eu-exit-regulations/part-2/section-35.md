# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 35

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35.[^key-dcf8cb000a6dfa68ae486b57e91c8302]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 11A (specified persons, descriptions, disclosures etc for the purposes of section 1224A) [^c23586711]—

- (a) in Part 2 (specified descriptions of disclosures), in paragraph 78 [^c23586721], for “EU obligation” substitute “ retained EU obligation ”; and
- (b) in Part 3 (overseas regulatory bodies), in paragraph 79—
  - (i) for “EEA competent authority” substitute “ approved third country competent authority ”;
  - (ii) for “EEA competent authorities” substitute “ approved third country competent authorities ”.
