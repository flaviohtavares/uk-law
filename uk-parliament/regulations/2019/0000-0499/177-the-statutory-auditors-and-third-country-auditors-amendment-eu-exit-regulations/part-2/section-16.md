# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 16

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16.[^c-e13ab6ff8471c7075dcbbfcd8756c1ff]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 1242 (duties of registered third country auditors)—

- (a) in subsection (1) [^c23586331], for “UK-traded non-EEA company” substitute “ UK-traded third country company ”;
- (b) in subsection (2)(a) [^c23586341], for “UK-traded non-EEA companies” substitute “ UK-traded third country companies ”.
- (c)[^c-ac399e12258a32749a2a15442ef80b70] in subsection (4)(b)—
  - (i) for “UK-traded non-EEA company” substitute “ UK-traded third country company ”;
  - (ii) for “UK-traded non-EEA companies” substitute “ UK-traded third country companies ”;
- (d) in subsection (4)(c)—
  - (i) for “UK-traded non-EEA company” substitute “ UK-traded third country company ”;
  - (ii) for “UK-traded non-EEA companies” substitute “ UK-traded third country companies ”.
