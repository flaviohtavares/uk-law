# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 36

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36.[^key-284346618737a68549d60dc23f6622a6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 12 (arrangements in which registered third country auditors are required to participate)—

- (a) in the italic cross-heading above paragraph 1 [^c23586731], for “UK-traded non-EEA companies” substitute “ UK-traded third country companies ”;
- (b) in paragraph 1 (arrangements for independent monitoring of audits of UK-traded non-EEA companies), in sub-paragraph (1)(a) [^c23586741], for “UK-traded non-EEA companies” substitute “ UK-traded third country companies ”;
- (c) in paragraph 2(1)(a) (arrangements for independent investigations for disciplinary purposes) [^c23586751], for “UK-traded non-EEA companies” substitute “ UK-traded third country companies ”.
