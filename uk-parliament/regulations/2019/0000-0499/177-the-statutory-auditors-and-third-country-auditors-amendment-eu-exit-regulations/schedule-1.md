# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2026-05-13.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Approval of Equivalent Third Countries and Transitional Third Countries

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 108

### 1. Equivalent third countries[^key-017fafe589f73c982495c795aec7c0e3]

(1)[^key-4bf4c6e3bdc4df7fca34280401790634] The EEA States and Gibraltar are approved as equivalent third countries for financial years beginning on or after IP completion day and are listed in column 1 of Table 1 of this Schedule.

(1A)[^key-760506b79f8e65045cf67fd15f3cb06d] The countries determined by the European Commission immediately before IP completion day as having an equivalent system of audit inspections, investigations and sanctions to those in the European Union by virtue of an instrument adopted under Article 46(2) of [Directive 2006/43/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0043) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17th May 2006 on statutory audits of annual accounts and consolidated accounts, amending Council Directives [78/660/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0660) and [83/349/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0349) and repealing Council [Directive 84/253/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1984/0253), are approved as equivalent third countries and are listed in column 1 of Table 1 of this Schedule.

(2)[^key-4996147cae2d83f7b1882da5606757c2] Approval is granted for an indefinite period, unless a date is recorded in column 2 of the Table against a country’s name, in which case the approval ceases to apply for financial years beginning after that date.
