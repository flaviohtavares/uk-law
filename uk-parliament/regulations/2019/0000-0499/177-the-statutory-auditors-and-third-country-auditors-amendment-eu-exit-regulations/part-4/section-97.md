# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 97

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97.[^key-d09d2f67619591887e7ab9d10b052d1c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 23 (powers of competent authorities), for paragraph 1 substitute—

> (1) Without prejudice to Article 26, in carrying out the competent authority's tasks, neither the competent authority nor any other regulatory authority or body of a Member State may interfere with the content of audit reports.

.
