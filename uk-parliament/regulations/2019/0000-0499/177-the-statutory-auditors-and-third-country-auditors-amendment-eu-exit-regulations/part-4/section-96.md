# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 96

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96.[^c-678f965ac999e15fa832193f1df7f047]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 21 (conditions of independence)—

- (a) in the first subparagraph—
  - (i) for “competent authorities” substitute “ competent authority ”;
  - (ii) omit “and audit firms”;
- (b) in the second subparagraph, for “competent authorities” substitute “ competent authority ”;
- (c)[^c-7cd698548e934302111d3bf30cffd85f] omit the third subparagraph.
- (d) in the fourth subparagraph—
  - (i) for “those authorities” substitute “ the competent authority ”;
  - (ii) omit “and audit firms”.
