# The Statutory Auditors and Third Country Auditors (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2.[^key-77eb672e59c26b7c9fd9fc12aff9de91]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

These Regulations come into force on exit day [^c23586081], except for—

(a)[^key-3eb4a9a17dbed7b0b2a3da1649b382ce] regulation 14, which comes into force 21 days after the day on which the Statutory Auditors, Third Country Auditors and International Accounting Standards (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 are made; and

(b) Part 5, which comes into force on 22 February 2019.
