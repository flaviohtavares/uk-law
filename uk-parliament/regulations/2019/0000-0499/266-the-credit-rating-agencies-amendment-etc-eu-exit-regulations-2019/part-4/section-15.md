# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 15

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Right to refer a decision to the Upper Tribunal[^key-3dbae8626eaa09460c202c3b62c6b36b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the FCA decides to—

- (a) impose a penalty on a person[^key-58e7baf036c793f5aba55e98432f64cc] under regulation 7, or
- (b) publish a statement in respect of a person under regulation 10,

the person may refer the matter to the Upper Tribunal.
