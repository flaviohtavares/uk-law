# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 6

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Guidance: procedure, etc.[^key-a7b5d03dda4555cacb78fc2371b584b2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA may—

- (a) publish its guidance;
- (b) offer copies of its published guidance for sale at a reasonable price;
- (c) if it issues guidance in response to a request made by a credit rating agency, make a reasonable charge for that guidance.

(2) Section 139B of the Act (notification of FCA guidance to the Treasury) [^c23565571] applies to guidance under regulation 5 which is—

- (a) given to credit rating agencies generally,
- (b) intended to have continuing effect, and
- (c) given in writing or other legible form,

as it applies to general guidance (within the meaning of that section).
