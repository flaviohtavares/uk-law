# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 4

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Rules: procedure, etc.[^key-57a9089cfb5b73fe07acef25c7640dbc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following provisions of the Act apply in respect of rules made under regulation 3 as they apply in respect of rules made by the FCA under that Act.

(2) The provisions are—

- (a) section 138A (modification or waiver of rules) [^c23565511];
- (b) section 138B (publication of directions under section 138A) [^c23565521];
- (c) section 138F (notification of rules) [^c23565531];
- (d) section 138G (rule-making instruments) [^c23565541];
- (e) section 138H (verification of rules) [^c23565551];
- (f) section 138I (consultation by the FCA) [^c23565561] as if—
  - (i) in subsection (6), after paragraph (e) there were inserted—

    > - (f) regulation 3 of the Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment, etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019.

    ;
  - (ii) in subsection (10), after paragraph (b) there were inserted—

    > , or
    >
    > - (c) to rules made by the FCA under regulation 3 of the Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment, etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019.

    ;
  - (iii) after that subsection there were inserted—

    > (10A) Subsection (2)(d) does not apply to rules made by the FCA under regulation 3 of the Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment, etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019.
