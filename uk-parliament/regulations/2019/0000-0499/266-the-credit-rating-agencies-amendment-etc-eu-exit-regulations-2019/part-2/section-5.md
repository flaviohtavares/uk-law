# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Guidance[^c-40b02e3d560797fa9ef1398105031a3c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The FCA may give guidance consisting of such information and advice as it considers appropriate in respect of—

- (a) the operation of—
  - (i) these Regulations,
  - (ii) the CRA Regulation, or
  - (iii) rules made under regulation 3;
- (b) any matters relating to the functions of the FCA under these Regulations or the CRA Regulation;
- (c) any other matters about which it appears to the FCA to be desirable to give information or advice in respect of these Regulations or the CRA Regulation.
