# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 1

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-e075aa6df758aabe6777e588b2e224b4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019.

(2) This Part and Part 8 of these Regulations come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

(3) Parts 2 to 7, 9 and 10 of these Regulations come into force on exit day.
