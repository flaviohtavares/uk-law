# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 49

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Interpretation of Part 8[^c-b3a9294224f9889122ba897702040cc4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- “**advance application**” means an application submitted during the period—
  - (a) beginning with the day on which this Part comes into force, and
  - (b) ending immediately before IP completion day[^c-4a3d28bad72d841c5b9f84c9170c9026];
- “**working day**” means a day other than—
  - (a) a Saturday or a Sunday,
  - (b) Christmas Day or Good Friday, or
  - (c) a day which is a bank holiday under the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 [^c23565771] in any part of the United Kingdom.

(2) In this Part, reference to a group of undertakings means a group consisting of—

- (a) a parent undertaking and its subsidiaries, within the meaning of Articles 1 and 2 of Directive [83/349/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1983/349) of 13 June 1983 [^c23565781], and
- (b) undertakings linked to each other by a relationship mentioned in Article 12.1(a) or (b) of that Directive, whose occupation includes the issuing of credit ratings.
