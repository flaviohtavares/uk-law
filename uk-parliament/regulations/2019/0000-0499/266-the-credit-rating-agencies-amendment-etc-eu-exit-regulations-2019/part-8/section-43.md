> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Acknowledgement of receipt[^key-eac5fcf7f905dcfc50f484a843187ca7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The FCA must, before the end of a period of 10 working days beginning with the day on which it receives a notification under regulation 42, send the person making the notification an acknowledgment of receipt.
