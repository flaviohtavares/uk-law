## 29. Application of regulation 28[^key-bbb0d51989541a64deb8231de2d1612c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 28 applies to a person—

- (a) who satisfies the conditions in regulation 30;
- (b) for the period determined in accordance with regulation 31.
