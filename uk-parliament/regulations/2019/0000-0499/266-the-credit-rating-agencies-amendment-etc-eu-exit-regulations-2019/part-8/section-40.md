# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 40

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Application of regulation 39[^c-f0b1c9e887966ea444941e39a8697f50]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 39 applies to a credit rating agency who—

- (a) satisfies the condition in regulation 41, and
- (b) notifies the FCA in accordance with regulation 42.
