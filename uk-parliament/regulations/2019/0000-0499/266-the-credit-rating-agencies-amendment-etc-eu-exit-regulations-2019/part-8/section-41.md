# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 41

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Condition to be satisfied for regulation 39 to apply[^key-839329b7d5f9e0d82c0667f2bb9bdb9f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The condition is that the credit rating agency is, immediately before IP completion day[^key-5216ab870cee05e2dc55bd18c4166244], certified in accordance with Article 5.2 of the CRA Regulation.
