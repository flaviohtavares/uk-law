> Text as at 2024-01-01.

## 46. Offences by bodies corporate[^key-d51061dfd4544d7da15991c0e1f3cade]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 400 of the Act (offences by bodies corporate etc.) [^c23565761] applies in respect of an offence under section 398 of that Act (as applied by regulation 45), as it applies in respect of an offence under that Act.
