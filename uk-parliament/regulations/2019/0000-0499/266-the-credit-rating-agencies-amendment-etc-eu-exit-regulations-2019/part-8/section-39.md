# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 39

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Certification under the CRA Regulation[^c-03a62aa0eba821f632c54b3b51795e07]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Notwithstanding Article 5 of the CRA Regulation, a person to whom this regulation applies is, on and after IP completion day[^c-ab0e8d9b7bfe4e08e24f12bc945ac15b], a certified credit rating agency for the purposes of the CRA Regulation.

(2) Reference in an enactment to a person certified under the CRA Regulation, however expressed, includes a person certified by virtue of this regulation.
