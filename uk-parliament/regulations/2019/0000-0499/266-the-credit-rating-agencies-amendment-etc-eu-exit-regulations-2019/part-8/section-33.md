# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 33

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Registration under the CRA Regulation[^c-ddd1ee7ac061ba57150e9ec022b28311]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Notwithstanding Articles 14 to 18A of the CRA Regulation, a person to whom this regulation applies is, on and after IP completion day[^c-78530f7f67e10ad0c1c6b40ea6c43109], registered as a credit rating agency under Chapter 1 of Title 3 of the CRA Regulation.

(2) Reference in any enactment to a person registered under that Chapter, however expressed, includes a person registered by virtue of this regulation.
