# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 37

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Acknowledgement of receipt[^key-6bf15ea2a9095f949bd554b05050503e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The FCA must, before the end of a period of 10 working days beginning with the day on which it receives a notification under regulation 36, send the person making the notification an acknowledgment of receipt.
