## 34. Application of regulation 33[^c-7b72f1892d3c7de16aa4f5fe81175b8a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 33 applies to a person who—

- (a) satisfies the conditions in regulation 35, and
- (b) notifies the FCA in accordance with regulation 36.
