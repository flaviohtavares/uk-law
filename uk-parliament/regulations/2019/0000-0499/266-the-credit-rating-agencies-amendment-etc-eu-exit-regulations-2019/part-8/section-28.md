# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 28

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Temporary deemed registration under the CRA Regulation[^c-c82ab7f375ab716f0f6b53be871637c2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person to whom this regulation applies is to be treated as if the person is registered as a credit rating agency under Chapter 1 of Title 3 of the CRA Regulation.

(2) Reference in an enactment to a person registered as a credit rating agency under that Chapter, however expressed, is to be read, unless the contrary intention appears, as including a person treated as being so registered by virtue of this regulation.
