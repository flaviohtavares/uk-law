# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 11

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Warning notice[^key-06a9a7ba9ce76470b2468a6c050b7b03]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the FCA proposes to—

- (a) impose a penalty on a person[^key-2685421e0ebf96cc4bd478f235304040] under regulation 7, or
- (b) publish a statement in respect of a person under regulation 10,

it must give the person a warning notice.

(2) A warning notice about a proposal to impose a penalty must state the amount of the penalty.

(3) A warning notice about a proposal to publish a statement must set out the terms of the statement.
