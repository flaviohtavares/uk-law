## 10. Public censure[^key-2f6a871b9328788e1f824f55130f7ebb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the FCA considers that a person[^key-c34d4d9f48ed2a993966c5b3198ed1ad] has contravened a requirement imposed by or under these Regulations, or by or under the CRA Regulation, the FCA may publish a statement to that effect.
