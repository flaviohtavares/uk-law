# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 12

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Decision notice[^key-b3f028960124b8b43480a9af6270d678]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If, having considered any representations made in response to a warning notice, [^c23565581] the FCA decides to—

- (a) impose a penalty under regulation 7 (whether or not of the amount proposed), or
- (b) publish a statement under regulation 10 (whether or not in the terms proposed),

it must without delay give the person[^key-578f154bc09b45b137ae8469d78870d6] concerned a decision notice.

(2) In the case of a penalty, the decision notice must state the amount of the penalty.

(3) In the case of a statement, the decision notice must set out the terms of the statement.
