# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 20

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Meaning of “consumer”[^key-0fc9f461b5f7e4a4b0bc8fa46bb3740b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of Part 26 of the Act, as applied by regulation 19, section 425A of the Act [^c23565721] (consumers: regulated activities etc. carried on by authorised persons) applies as if, in subsection (3), after paragraph (a) there were inserted—

> - (ab) a credit rating agency, registered under Chapter 1 of Title 3 of Regulation [(EC) No 1060/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1060) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 September 2009 on credit rating agencies, carrying out a credit rating activity (within the meaning of Article 3.1 of that Regulation);

.
