# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 22

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Offences by bodies corporate[^c-5343e470dae8cbb99b10246879a38e10]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 400 of the Act [^c23565741] (offences by bodies corporate etc.) applies in respect of an offence under—

- (a) section 177 of that Act (as applied by regulation 18), and
- (b) section 398 of that Act (as applied by regulation 21),

as it applies in respect of an offence under that Act.
