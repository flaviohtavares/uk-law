# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 67

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67.[^key-ae7c90fee8994816db840ed64d3f45f0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 10—

- (a) in paragraph 2a—
  - (i) for “Directive [2003/6/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2003/6)”, substitute “ Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 ”;
  - (ii) for “Article 6(3) of that Directive”, substitute “ Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 ”;
- (b) in paragraph 6, for “ESMA or any competent authority”, in each place it occurs, substitute “ the FCA ”.
