## 69.[^key-9c76fd4e7a0fff3d8ec63af5c208e42e]

- (a) in the heading, for “European rating platform”, substitute “ Public rating database ”;
- (b) in paragraph 1, for “ESMA”, substitute “ the FCA ”;
- (c) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) for “ESMA”, substitute “ The FCA ”;
  - (ii) omit “(European rating platform)”;
  - (iii) omit the second paragraph.
