# The Credit Rating Agencies (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 101

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Revocation of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No. 272/2012[^key-d43402082ca2064b9744ac9e9dde6e3c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 272/2012 of 7 February 2012 supplementing Regulation [(EC) No 1060/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1060) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to fees charged by the European Securities and Markets Authority to credit rating agencies is revoked.
