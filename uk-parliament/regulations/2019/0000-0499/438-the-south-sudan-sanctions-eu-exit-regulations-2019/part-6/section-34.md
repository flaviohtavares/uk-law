# The South Sudan (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 34

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Trade licences[^key-5b9b60ba1276ebba8fd4c38a7427f07d][^key-102e41f01f4232a7be3312b45914f4fd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The prohibitions in Chapters 2 and 3 of Part 5 (Trade) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
