# The South Sudan (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 35

> Text as at 2026-05-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Licences: general provisions[^c-fb523fcb0c29b1f563a8176ec85081c6][^c-2fb0ef2e109873e410187e5168d2e6f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences , director disqualification licences[^c-43bc33f87b544e6cef89c41c88fbfb15] and trade licences.

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may —

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^c-b1c4dd02c031ee11c65382609b753da1]**P**”), issues[^c-263ec9f66e2cd6ddc1825527c6a56615] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^c-d5094e4c7c954e19ad2ca5fe68c573b9] must give written notice to P[^c-e94ab7b1bace15555dbb7acd8ba38fcd] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
