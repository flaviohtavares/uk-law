# The South Sudan (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 56

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Application of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^c-e0b24d571336c0e4cdc0629cba2c1551][^c-bf9fa4e5fefda82353e5f5ed7b894370]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (investigatory powers) [^c23619991] applies to any offence under Part 3 (Finance) or regulation 36 (finance: licensing offences).
