# The South Sudan (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 65

> Text as at 2026-05-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008[^c-071d41983aa2a21798780b9792fa65bc][^c-75bc5ebfa424c3892d2ac311de3cfe3d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008 (embargoed destinations) is not to be taken to prohibit anything prohibited by Part 5 (Trade).
