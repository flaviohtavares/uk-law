# The South Sudan (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 21

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Export of military goods[^c-549346d1c4e4f5fc4d28a17bd7514893][^c-054bdce26b67f65e790aca9f64d7bdd2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of military goods to, or for use in, South Sudan is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).
