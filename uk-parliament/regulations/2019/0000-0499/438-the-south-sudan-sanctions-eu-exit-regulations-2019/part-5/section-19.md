# The South Sudan (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 19

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Definition of “military goods” and “military technology”[^key-fa7538b115507071ab3de69ab8065e06][^key-5c524caefc12720de1354d765f45e3d0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**military goods**” means—
  - (a) any thing for the time being specified in Schedule 2 to the Export Control Order 2008 [^c23619691], other than any thing which is military technology, and
  - (b) any tangible storage medium on which military technology is recorded or from which it can be derived;
- “**military technology**” means any thing for the time being specified in Schedule 2 to the Export Control Order 2008 which is described as software or technology.
