> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Meaning of “designated person” in Part 3[^key-bcbb1401eb354cec3104a1b4ca9f814d][^key-17db0ebe45b4a3640a188ba69d8af0d2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part a “**designated person**” means—

- (a) a person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of regulations 12 to 16 (asset-freeze etc), or
- (b) a person who is a designated person for the purposes of those regulations by reason of regulation 10.
