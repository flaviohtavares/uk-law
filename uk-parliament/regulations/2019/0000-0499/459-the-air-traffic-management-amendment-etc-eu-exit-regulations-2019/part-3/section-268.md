# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 268

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 268. Article 10 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1206/2011[^c-9f4086332dfdcd80e8613768e158b03d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 10 (additional requirements for Member States)—

- (a) in the heading for “Member States” substitute “ the CAA ”,
- (b) for “Member States” substitute “ The CAA ”, and
- (c) omit “compliance with this Regulation including”.
