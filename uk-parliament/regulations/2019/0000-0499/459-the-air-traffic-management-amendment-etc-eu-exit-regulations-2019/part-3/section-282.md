# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 282

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 282. Article 8 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1207/2011[^c-ab4536ea9c480ca3a64851b846ae0081]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 8 (State aircraft) is amended as follows.

(2) In each of paragraphs 1 and 2 for “Member States” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”.

(3) Omit paragraphs 3 and 4.

(4) In paragraph 5 for “the State aircraft identified in paragraph 3” substitute “ : ” then insert—

> - (a) State aircraft that cannot be equipped with secondary surveillance radar transponders that comply with the requirements set out in Part A of Annex 2, and
> - (b) transport-type State aircraft with a maximum certified take-off mass exceeding 5 700 kg or having a maximum cruising true airspeed capability greater than 250 knots, that cannot be equipped with secondary surveillance radar transponders that comply with the requirements set out in Part B and Part C of Annex 2,

.

(5) In paragraph 6 for “Member States” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”.

(6) In paragraph 7 for “Member State that has designated them” substitute “ CAA ”.

(7)[^c-b1256c6ed17616f9261e34346bfc5545] In paragraph 8—

- (a) for “Member States” substitute “The Secretary of State”, and
- (b) for “Single European Sky airspace” substitute “airspace provided for in Article 1(3) of Regulation [(EC) No 551/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/0551) of the European Parliament and of the Council”.
