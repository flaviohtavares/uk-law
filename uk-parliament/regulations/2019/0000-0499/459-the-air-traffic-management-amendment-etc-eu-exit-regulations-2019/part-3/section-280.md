# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 280

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 280. Article 6 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1207/2011[^c-b5669adc4d3d34ef4b1c30dbdfdeb101]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 6 (spectrum protection) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Member States” substitute “ the CAA ”, and
- (b) for “a Member State” substitute “ the United Kingdom ”.

(3) In paragraph 3—

- (a) for “Member States” substitute “ the CAA ”, and
- (b) for “a Member State” substitute “ the United Kingdom ”.

(4) Omit paragraph 4.
