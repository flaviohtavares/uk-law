> Text as at 2020-12-31.

## 294. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 923/2012: introduction[^key-de6fdc5ee2b5db92913739190647b22a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 923/2012 of 26 September 2012 laying down the common rules of the air and operational provisions regarding services and procedures in air navigation and amending Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1035/2011 and Regulations [(EC) No 1265/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/1265), [(EC) No 1794/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1794), [(EC) No 730/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/730), [(EC) No 1033/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/1033) and (EU) No 255/2010 is amended in accordance with regulations 295 to 308.
