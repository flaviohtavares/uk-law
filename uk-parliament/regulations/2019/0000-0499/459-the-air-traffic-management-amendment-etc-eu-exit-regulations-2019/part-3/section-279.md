# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 279

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 279. Article 5 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1207/2011[^c-d08cfe91a43c2940e6687dd6e354f6ae][^c-0eac92f51f6cc26ed9392a19960a8120]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 5 (interoperability requirements), in paragraph 5, for “Single European Sky airspace”, in both places it occurs, substitute “airspace provided for in Article 1(3) of Regulation [(EC) No 551/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/0551) of the European Parliament and of the Council”.
