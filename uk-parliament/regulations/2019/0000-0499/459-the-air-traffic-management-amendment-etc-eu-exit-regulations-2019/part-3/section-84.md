# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 84

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Article 1 of Regulation [(EC) No 2150/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2150)[^key-8ed75cd379b1170a17e8a4db9a7bc395]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 1 (subject matter) is amended as follows.

(2) In the first unnumbered paragraph—

- (a) for “Single European Sky” substitute “ UK ATMS and in relation to the EATMN ”, and
- (b) omit “within the limits of the common transport policy”.

(3) In the second unnumbered paragraph for “Member States” substitute “ the United Kingdom ”.
