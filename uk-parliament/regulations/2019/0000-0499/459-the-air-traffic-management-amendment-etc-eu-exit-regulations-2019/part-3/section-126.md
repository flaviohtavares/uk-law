# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 126

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126. Article 2 of Commission Regulation [(EC) No 29/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/29)[^c-94c4c223bcd86b38063c4c91579db392]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 2 (definitions), in point 13—

- (a) omit “within the European Air Traffic Management Network”, and
- (b) for “covered by this Regulation” substitute “ under the responsibility of the States participating in that service ”.
