# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 176

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 176. Article 5 of Regulation (EU) No 255/2010[^c-d4507eebc9b0b078ffcf85b1c8abf06a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 5 (obligations of Member States concerning the central unit for ATFM) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading omit “of Member States”.

(3) In the words before point (a) for “Member States shall” substitute “ The Secretary of State must endeavour to ”.

(4) In each of points (a) and (d) for “EATMN” substitute “ UK ATMS ”.
