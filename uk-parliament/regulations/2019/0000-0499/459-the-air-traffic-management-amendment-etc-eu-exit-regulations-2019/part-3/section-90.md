## 90. Article 7 of Regulation [(EC) No 2150/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2150)[^key-e5e7122f661cc5328c3bfbb82e94bfc2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

- (a) for “Member States” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”,
- (b) omit “they introduce”, and
- (c) at the end insert “ are introduced ”.
