# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 166

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 166. Annex 2 to Commission Regulation (EU) No 73/2010[^key-3c4dcc3b4cdfb454c9b6b5a0a41f14c9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 2 (aeronautical data exchange format requirements referred to in Article 5) is amended as follows.

(2) In Part A (IAIP, aerodrome mapping and electronic obstacle data), in point 2 for “national supervisory authority” substitute “ CAA ”.

(3) In Part B (electronic terrain data), in point 2 for “national supervisory authority” substitute “ CAA ”.
