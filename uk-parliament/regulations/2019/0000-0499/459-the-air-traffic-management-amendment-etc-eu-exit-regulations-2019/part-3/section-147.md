# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 147

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 147. Article 10 of Commission Regulation [(EC) No 262/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/262)[^c-f11bb4dfa72d68cb9d7f404de432872f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 10 (conformity assessment)—

- (a) for “an EC” substitute “ a ”,
- (b) after “, or” insert “ if the manufacturer is not established in the United Kingdom ”, and
- (c) for “Community” substitute “ United Kingdom ”.
