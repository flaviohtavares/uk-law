# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 131

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Article 11 of Commission Regulation [(EC) No 29/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/29)[^c-3f2472987c84fdc054993a481a067499]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 11 (conformity or suitability for use of constituents) is amended as follows.

(2) The first unnumbered paragraph becomes paragraph 1.

(3) In that paragraph—

- (a) for “an EC” substitute “ a ”,
- (b) after “, or” insert “ if the manufacturer is not established in the United Kingdom ”, and
- (c) for “Community” substitute “ United Kingdom ”.

(4) The second unnumbered paragraph becomes paragraph 2.
