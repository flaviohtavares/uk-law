# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 319

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 319. Article 12 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1079/2012[^c-7d7f3abe76eba70b4e01fc7547432021]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 12 (verification of systems) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1 for “their national supervisory authority” substitute “ the CAA ”.

(3) In paragraph 2 for “a notified” substitute “ an appointed ”.

(4) In paragraph 3 for “an EC” substitute “ a ”.
