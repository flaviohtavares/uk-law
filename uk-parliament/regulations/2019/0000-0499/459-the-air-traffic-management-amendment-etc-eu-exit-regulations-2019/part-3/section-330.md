# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 330

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 330. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 409/2013[^c-21857ad05b16c04e83ff3d53ce16663e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 409/2013 of 3 May 2013 on the definition of common projects, the establishment of governance and the identification of incentives supporting the implementation of the European Air Traffic Management Master Plan is revoked.
