# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 88

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Article 5 of Regulation [(EC) No 2150/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/2150)[^key-a04532eeb35ba4d2e26b1ebdde895c71]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 5 (pre-tactical airspace management (level 2)) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1 for “Member States” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”.

(3) In the unnumbered paragraph after paragraph 1 for “In those Member States where” substitute “ Where ”.

(4) In paragraph 2—

- (a) for “Two or more Member States” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”,
- (b) after “establish” insert “ or participate in ”, and
- (c) at the end insert “ with one or more Member States ”.

(5) In paragraph 3 for “Member States” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”.
