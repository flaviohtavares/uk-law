# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 127

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Article 3 of Commission Regulation [(EC) No 29/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/29)[^key-2254a015f2a6b6b4e80b29c02d207b96]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 3 (data link services), in paragraph 4—

- (a) for “Member States which decide” substitute “ If the Secretary of State decides ”, and
- (b) after “requirements,” insert “ the Secretary of State ”.
