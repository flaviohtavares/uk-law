# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 179

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 179. Article 10 of Regulation (EU) No 255/2010[^key-237ef355bf027344d80418ebeeaf7151]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 10 (obligations concerning critical events) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Member States shall” substitute “ The Secretary of State must endeavour to ”, and
- (b) for “EATMN” substitute “ UK ATMS ”.

(3) In paragraph 2, in point (c) omit “Member States'”.
