# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 278A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 278A. Article 2 of Commission implementing Regulation (EU) No 1207/2011[^M_I_a7a6ef2e-4dc8-48c5-d35f-bf4b8b298119][^key-d189066d5b75a9b3b814a295d4dd5e1e]
