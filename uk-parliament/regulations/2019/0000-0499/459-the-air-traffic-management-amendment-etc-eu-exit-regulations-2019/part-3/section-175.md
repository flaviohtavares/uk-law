# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 175

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 175. Article 4 of Regulation (EU) No 255/2010[^c-9b595f36979b9b51523ce972c3e5dd0c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 4 (general obligations of Member States) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading for “Member States” substitute “ the Secretary of State ”.

(3) In paragraph 1 for “Member States” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”.

(4) In paragraph 2 omit “Member States”.

(5) In paragraph 4—

- (a) omit the first sentence, and
- (b) for “this common reference document” insert “ the common reference document created by Eurocontrol containing the policies, procedures and description for route and traffic orientation. ”.

(6) In paragraph 5 for “Those procedures shall be” substitute “ The Secretary of State must endeavour to ensure that those procedures are ”.
