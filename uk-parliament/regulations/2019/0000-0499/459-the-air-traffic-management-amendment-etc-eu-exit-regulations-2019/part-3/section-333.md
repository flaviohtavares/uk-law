# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 333

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 333. Article 1 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 716/2014[^key-8384a13ed27244df3d82752d820f37fc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 1 (subject matter and scope) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1 for “sets up” substitute “ provides for the application of requirements derived from ”.

(3) In paragraph 2 for the words from “European” to “552/2004” substitute “ United Kingdom Air Traffic Management System (UK ATMS) ”.
