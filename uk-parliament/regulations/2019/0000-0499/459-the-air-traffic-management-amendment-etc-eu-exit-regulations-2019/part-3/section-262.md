# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 262

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 262. Article 3 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1206/2011[^key-b48edaf3937fad7ee119559caea782f7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 3 (definitions) is amended as follows.

(2) In point 10—

- (a) after “SSR codes to” insert “ the United Kingdom, ”, and
- (b) after “agreed by” insert “ the United Kingdom and ”.

(3) in point 12—

- (a) omit “within the European Air Traffic Management Network”, and
- (b) for “covered by this Regulation” substitute “ under the responsibility of the States participating in that service ”.
