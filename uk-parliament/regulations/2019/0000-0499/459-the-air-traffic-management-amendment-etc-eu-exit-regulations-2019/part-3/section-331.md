# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 331

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 331. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 657/2013[^c-d5abb73763321e942c2806261e18972f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 657/2013 of 10 July 2013 amending Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1079/2012 laying down requirements for voice channels spacing for the single European sky is revoked.
