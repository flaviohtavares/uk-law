# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 114

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Commission Regulation [(EC) No 633/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/633): introduction[^c-297618a8074fad33d11f5f2a0c39415e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Regulation [(EC) No 633/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/633) of 7 June 2007 laying down requirements for the application of a flight message transfer protocol used for the purpose of notification, coordination and transfer of flights between air traffic control units is amended in accordance with regulations 115 to 123.
