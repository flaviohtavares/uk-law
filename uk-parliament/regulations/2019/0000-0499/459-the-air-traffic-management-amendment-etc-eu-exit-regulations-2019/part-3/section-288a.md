# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 288A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 288A. Article 14a of Commission implementing Regulation (EU) No 1207/2011[^M_I_4437cca5-9f36-4279-cdb0-51ead694e684][^c-8fc6310ae74d20cacd1179192ac37e4b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 14a (flight plans)—

- (a) omit “communicated as per Article 8(3)”, and
- (b) for “Single European Sky airspace” substitute “airspace provided for in Article 1(3) of Regulation [(EC) No 551/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/0551) of the European Parliament and of the Council”.
