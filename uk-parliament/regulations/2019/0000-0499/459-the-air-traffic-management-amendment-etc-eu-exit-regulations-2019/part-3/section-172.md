# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 172

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 172. Article 1 of Regulation (EU) No 255/2010[^c-df556cc8ad1cdd9a8b6311a746a39d18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 1 (subject matter and scope) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “European” substitute “ United Kingdom ”,
- (b) for “network” substitute “ system ”, and
- (c) for “EATMN” substitute “ UK ATMS ”.

(3) In paragraph 3 omit point (f).
