> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 313. Article 5 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1079/2012[^c-744422afef5db2c789a34c4872658c3f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 5 (obligations of operators) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 2—

- (a) for “From 1 January 2014 an” substitute “ An ”, and
- (b) after “class A, B or C of the” insert “ under the responsibility of the United Kingdom or the ”.

(3) In paragraph 4 omit “from 1 January 2018”.
