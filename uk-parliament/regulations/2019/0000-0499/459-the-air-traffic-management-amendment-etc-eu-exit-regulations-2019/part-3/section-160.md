# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 160

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 160. Article 11 of Commission Regulation (EU) No 73/2010[^c-9dbddba51588a651d41fa518d05c4e6b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 11 (conformity or suitability for use of constituents)—

- (a) omit “EC” in the first place it occurs,
- (b) after “, or” insert “ if the manufacturer is not established in the United Kingdom ”, and
- (c) for “Union” substitute “ United Kingdom ”.
