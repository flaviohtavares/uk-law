# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 167

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 167. Annex 7 to Commission Regulation (EU) No 73/2010[^c-00dfa7bd100d6a99b53a2abe620c9173]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Annex 7 (quality, safety and security management requirements referred to in Article 10) is amended as follows.

(2) In Part A (quality management system), in point 2 for “national supervisory authority” substitute “ CAA ”.

(3) In Part C (security management objectives), in point 2 for “national supervisory authority” substitute “ CAA ”.
