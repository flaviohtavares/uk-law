# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 128

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128. Article 7 of Commission Regulation [(EC) No 29/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/29)[^key-6a8e5c265a0d378472bd0d4f554c9c88]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 7 (general obligations of Member States for data link communications) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading for “Member States” substitute “ the CAA ”.

(3) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Member States which have designated ATS providers in the airspace referred to in Article 1(3)” substitute “ The CAA ”, and
- (b) for “their” substitute “ its ”.

(4) In each of paragraphs 2 and 3 for “Member States” substitute “ The CAA ”.
