## 372. Commission Decision of 7.7.2011[^c-287342a41b8e4c3564c94d4c1e36fd24]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Decision of 7.7.2011 on the nomination of the Network Manager for the air traffic management (ATM) network functions of the single European sky is revoked.
