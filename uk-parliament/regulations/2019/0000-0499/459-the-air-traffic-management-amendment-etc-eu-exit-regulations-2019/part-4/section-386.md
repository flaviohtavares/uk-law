# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 386

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 386. Council Decision (EU) 2016/1764[^c-b3b93b3b53fdca496513f0c6fa8f1687]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Decision (EU) 2016/1764 of 29 September 2016 on the position to be taken by the European Union within the EU-ICAO Joint Committee on the Decision on the adoption of an annex on Air Traffic Management to the Memorandum of Cooperation between the European Union and the International Civil Aviation Organization providing a framework for enhanced cooperation is revoked.
