# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 382

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 382. Council Decision (EU) 2015/2394[^c-6a43db633dd38cd2843b085103bf3f74]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Decision (EU) 2015/2394 of 8 December 2015 on the position to be taken by the Member States on behalf of the European Union, concerning the decisions to be adopted by the Permanent Commission of Eurocontrol, with regard to the roles and tasks of Eurocontrol and on centralised services is revoked.
