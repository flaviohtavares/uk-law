# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 379

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 379. Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2015/348[^c-f03ff817dba767ec8a86640c23dcf216]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2015/348 of 2 March 2015 concerning the consistency of certain targets included in the national or functional airspace block plans submitted pursuant to Regulation [(EC) No 549/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/549) of the European Parliament and of the Council with the Union-wide performance targets for the second reference period is revoked.
