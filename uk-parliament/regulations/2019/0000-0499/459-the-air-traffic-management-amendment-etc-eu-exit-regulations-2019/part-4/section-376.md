# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 376

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

## 376. Commission Implementing Decision 2014/132/EU[^key-4ff918adb1e5bc5b3bc630911af0a992]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Implementing Decision of 11 March 2014 setting the Union-wide performance targets for the air traffic management network and alert thresholds for the second reference period 2015-19 is revoked.
