# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 38

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Article 17 of Regulation [(EC) No 550/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/550)[^c-d9068fccd67118d1423e2d3e7641dd27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For Article 17 (revision of annexes) substitute—

> **Article 17 — Revision of Annexes**
>
> **1.**
>
> The Secretary of State may by regulations made by statutory instrument amend Annex 1 or 2.
>
> **2.**
>
> Regulations under paragraph 1—
>
> - (a) may make different provision for different purposes;
> - (b) may make transitional, transitory or saving provision;
> - (c) may make consequential, incidental or supplementary provision.
>
> **3.**
>
> A statutory instrument containing regulations under paragraph 1 is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
