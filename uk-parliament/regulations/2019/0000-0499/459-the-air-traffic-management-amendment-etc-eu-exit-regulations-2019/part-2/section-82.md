# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 82

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Council Regulation (EU) No 721/2014[^key-92dafdb1a3a9e8a41742ad20738c3ff1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Regulation (EU) No 721/2014 of 16 June 2014 amending Regulation [(EC) No 219/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/219) on the establishment of a Joint Undertaking to develop the new generation European air traffic management system (SESAR) as regards the extension of the Joint Undertaking until 2024 is revoked.
