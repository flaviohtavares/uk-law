# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 79

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Article 4 of Regulation [(EC) No 1070/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1070)[^c-1ff627f16c8dca9a7c32242710fd9932]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 4 omit—

- (a) paragraph 2 (which substitutes a new Article 9 for Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No 552/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/552)),
- (b) paragraph 3 (which inserts a new paragraph 2a of Article 10 of that Regulation), and
- (c) paragraph 4 (which amends Annex 2 to that Regulation).
