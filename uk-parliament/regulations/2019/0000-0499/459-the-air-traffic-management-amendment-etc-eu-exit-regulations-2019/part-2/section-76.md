# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 76

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Article 1 of Regulation [(EC) No 1070/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1070)[^c-c739a6ca23a7d6099df40cfbf01ffb64]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 1 omit—

- (a) paragraph 4 (which substitutes a new Article 5(4) for Article 5(4) of Regulation [(EC) No 549/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/549)),
- (b) paragraph 5 (which substitutes new Articles 6 to 11 for Articles 6 to 11 of that Regulation), and
- (c) paragraph 7 (which inserts Article 13a of that Regulation).
