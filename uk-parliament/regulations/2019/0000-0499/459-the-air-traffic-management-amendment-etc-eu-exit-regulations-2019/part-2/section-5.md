# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Article 3 of Regulation [(EC) No 549/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/549)[^c-50979235d5acf0386385c406fc413213]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 3 (fields for action by the Community) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading omit “by the Community”.

(3) In paragraph 1—

- (a) in the words before point (a) omit “for the creation of the single European sky”,
- (b) in the words after point (c), after “adopted” insert “ before exit day ”,
- (c) in those words for “regulations” substitute “ Regulations ”, and
- (d) at the end of those words insert “ as well as with the regulations made on or after exit day by the Secretary of State under any of the Regulations referred to in this Article. ”.
