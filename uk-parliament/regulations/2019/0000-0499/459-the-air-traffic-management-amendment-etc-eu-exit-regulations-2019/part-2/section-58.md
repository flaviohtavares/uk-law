# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 58

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Article 5 of Regulation [(EC) No 552/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/552)[^key-9af18f72c40d2914367f96b7e8a4bc47]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 5 (declaration of conformity or suitability for use of constituents) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading for “EC declaration” substitute “ Declaration ”.

(3) In paragraph 1 for “an EC” substitute “ a ”.

(4)[^key-35913fd56ef352574fd687e4bcd74f5d] In paragraph 2 omit “established in the Community” and “EC”.

(5) In paragraph 3 omit “EC”.

(6) After paragraph 3 insert—

> **3A.**
>
> Paragraph 3B applies in relation to an EC declaration of conformity or suitability for use made before exit day in accordance with Article 5 of Regulation [(EC) No 552/2204](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2204/552) [^c24460671] which continues to be in force or effective on or after exit day by virtue of Part 3 of Schedule 8 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018.
>
> **3B.**
>
> The declaration is, for the purposes of this Regulation and the other measures referred to in Article 3 of the framework Regulation, to be treated as a declaration made in accordance with this Article by a manufacturer [^key-aa4862f49883fc401fd68a4aee590cfc]....

.

(7) In paragraph 4 for “notified bodies referred to in Article 8” substitute “ bodies appointed to carry out those tasks ”.
