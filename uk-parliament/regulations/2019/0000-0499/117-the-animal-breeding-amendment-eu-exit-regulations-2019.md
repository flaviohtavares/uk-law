The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of, and paragraph 21(b) of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 [^c23536241].

The requirements of paragraph 3(2) of Schedule 7 to that Act (relating to the appropriate Parliamentary procedure for these Regulations) have been satisfied.

## PART 1 — Introductory

### 1. Citation and commencement[^c-a8671c37ea478d876d480af9eba3720b]

These Regulations may be cited as the Animal Breeding (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force on exit day.
