> Text as at 2026-09-29.

## 72. Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008[^key-41c2f152baea519c991e450146ebf092][^key-506805a656fae3e9831a10c3f9bb8439]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008 (embargoed destinations) is not to be taken to prohibit anything prohibited by Part 5 (Trade).
