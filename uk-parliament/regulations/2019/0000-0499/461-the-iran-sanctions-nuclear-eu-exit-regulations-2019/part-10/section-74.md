# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 74

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Revocations[^key-cbe2e677bee3a24739b8b4268c639067][^key-8284667dd29a68b52649bf1c4501c3a3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Regulation (EU) No 267/2012 of 23 March 2012 concerning restrictive measures against Iran and repealing Regulation (EU) No 961/2010 is revoked.

(2) The Iran (European Union Financial Sanctions) Regulations 2016 [^c23622301] are revoked.

(3) The Export Control (Iran Sanctions) Order 2016 [^c23622311] is revoked.

(4) The Iran (Financial Sanctions) Order 2007 [^c23622321] is revoked.
