# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 78

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Transitional Provisions: prior obligations[^c-28deebcd4ced49beab3968db729a7be3][^c-70557067fea58fa304d6f2c69f1dcf67]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-c8c116330da04604b5fe6ab24f837197] Where immediately before 31 January 2020 at 11.00pm—

- (a) a person was named in Annex IX of the EU Iran Regulation, and
- (b) the person was a designated person,

any reference in a provision mentioned in paragraph (3) to a time when the person was not a designated person is to be read on the basis that the person became a designated person on the date on which the person was named in that Annex.

(2)[^c-a9bb939074d5caf82abb23077c4eb8be] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The provision referred to in paragraph (1) is—[^c-c31cf9af0a2cc14b58dc646b222c6f3c]

- (a) regulation 37(5) (finance: exceptions from prohibitions), [^c-34b6a8890d7c9db413216b2dc12545b1]...
- (ab)[^c-393acdbb98f49244eed000550ba7b00a] regulation 37ZZB (finance: exceptions relating to payment of Treasury debt), and
- (b)[^c-03d0064d05e71de3dcf709b1b6b25e43] paragraphs 7A(b)(i), 7B and 10C of Schedule 2 (Treasury licences: purposes).

(4) In paragraph (1), the reference to the date on which a person was named in Annex IX of the EU Iran Regulation—

- (a) if the person had previously been named in Annex V of the 2007 EU Iran Regulation, is to be read as the date on which the person was named in that Annex;
- (b) if the person had previously been named in Annex VIII of the 2010 EU Iran Regulation but not in Annex V of the 2007 EU Iran Regulation, is to be read as the date on which the person was named in Annex VIII of the 2010 EU Iran Regulation.

(5) In this regulation—

- “**the 2007 EU Iran Regulation**” means Council Regulation [(EC) No 423/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0423) of 19 April 2007 concerning restrictive measures against Iran [^c23622371];
- “**the 2010 EU Iran Regulation**” means Council Regulation (EU) No 961/2010 of 25 October 2010 on restrictive measures against Iran and repealing Regulation [(EC) No 423/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0423) [^c23622381];
- “**designated person**” has the same meaning as it has in Part 3 (Finance);
