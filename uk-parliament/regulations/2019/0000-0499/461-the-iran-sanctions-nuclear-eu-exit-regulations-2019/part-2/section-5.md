# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^key-3346a642554c1e7f8f8c1750fde78454][^key-f67ada2f02b86b81b79827294f3431f3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons, by name, in accordance with regulation 5A (conditions for the designation of persons by name)[^key-a0329d20f0afb0e36c2c6b6cc933abec] for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 12 to 17 (asset-freeze etc);
- (aa)[^key-01a7ab4db9233862806f16a239e14070] regulation 18A (director disqualification sanctions);
- (b) regulation 19 (immigration);
- (c)[^key-9b0f4699303fbdd25853ad74dc725411] regulation 33D (technical assistance relating to ships);
- (d) regulation 36A (movement of aircraft);
- (e) regulation 36G (prohibition on port entry);
- (f) regulation 36I (movement of ships);
- (g) regulation 36J (detention of ships);
- (h) regulation 36K (registration of ships in the United Kingdom);
- (i) regulation 43B (technical assistance relating to ships).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purposes of different provisions mentioned in paragraph (1).
