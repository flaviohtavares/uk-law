# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 11

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Meaning of “designated person” in Part 3[^c-b9817ee3b0820bd0e6d0da67302ea7b1][^c-eacd23f12d98b1f09ef54303e0b73ae1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part a “**designated person**” means—

- (a) a person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of regulations 12 to 17, or
- (b) a person who is a designated person for the purposes of those regulations by reason of regulation 10.
