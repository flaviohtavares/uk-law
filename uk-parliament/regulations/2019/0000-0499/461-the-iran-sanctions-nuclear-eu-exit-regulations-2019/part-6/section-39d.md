# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 39D

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39D. Ships: exceptions from prohibitions on port entry[^key-d20cca4efc8a8e16ee7b680685cbc2c4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibition in regulation 36G(1) (prohibition on port entry) is not contravened by providing a ship with access to a port if—

- (a) a port entry direction has been given in relation to the ship under regulation 36I(1) (movement of ships), or
- (b) the access is needed by the ship in a case of emergency.

(2) The prohibition in regulation 36G(2) is not contravened by the entry into port of a ship if—

- (a) a port entry direction has been given in relation to the ship under regulation 36I(1), or
- (b) the entry is needed by the ship in a case of emergency.
