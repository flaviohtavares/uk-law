# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 42

> Text as at 2026-09-29.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Licences: general provisions[^c-94f217bb6143478b7a1b3a08b6d01e32][^c-90bed094b333af688947017f45ff041c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences , director disqualification licences[^c-944ac88b3a1e933e49db7a27d650ca3b] , trade licences, aircraft licences and ships licences[^c-c582d0fc19ea6ddd126f21bfcdd5e356].

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may —

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^c-655dc346ec683dce12bc3b8fa8ad545d]**P**”), issues[^c-eed80eee5a5d3b1aa63367737b8acef9] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^c-a42c8f9ac3b366c1ab00d65a6d37d153] must give written notice to P[^c-302cfa3abad713cc41bef7f68b9801e6] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
