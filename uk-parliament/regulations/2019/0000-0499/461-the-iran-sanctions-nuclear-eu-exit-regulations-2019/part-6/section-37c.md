# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 37C

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37C. Trade: exceptions in relation to energy-related goods and energy-related technology[^c-4aa1f039f85342d0414ce1418ac6ad8e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibitions in regulations 27M (financial services and funds relating to energy-related goods and energy-related technology) and 27N (brokering services: non-UK activity relating to energy-related goods and energy-related technology) are not contravened in relation to the provision of insurance or reinsurance to a relevant person where the provision of such insurance or reinsurance is not connected with the energy sector in Iran.

(2) The prohibitions in Chapter 1B of Part 5 (exports and related activities: energy-related goods and energy-related technology) and Chapter 1C of Part 5 (sectoral software goods and sectoral software and technology) are not contravened by a relevant activity that is necessary for the purposes of a UK petroleum project.

(3) In this regulation—

- “**energy-related goods**” has the meaning given in regulation 34(1) (interpretation of Part 5);
- “**energy-related technology**” has the meaning given in regulation 34(1);
- “**relevant activity**” means any activity which would, in the absence of this regulation, contravene the prohibitions in Chapters 1B and 1C of Part 5;
- “**relevant person**” means a person who is not a person connected with Iran;
- “**UK petroleum project**” means an oil or gas exploration or production project that is wholly or partially located within—
  - (a) the United Kingdom;
  - (b) waters adjacent to the United Kingdom up to the seaward limits of the territorial sea, or the seabed and subsoil below them;
  - (c) areas from time to time designated under section 1(7) of the Continental Shelf Act 1964 (designation of areas of continental shelf), and any waters within the limits of such areas.
