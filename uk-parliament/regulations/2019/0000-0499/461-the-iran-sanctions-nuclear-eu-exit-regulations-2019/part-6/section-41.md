# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 41

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Trade licences[^key-59030afb40e38e267378c569b4d33934][^key-c38b33055b6e968d9547a187999bc5c5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The prohibitions in Chapters 1 to 3 of Part 5 (Trade) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
