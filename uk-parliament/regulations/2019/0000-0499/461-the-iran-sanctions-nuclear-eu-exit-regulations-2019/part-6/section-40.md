# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 40

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Treasury licences[^c-3cb03229635969f595bd46d0891d94c4][^c-01761a4a65a7d606ea1e52d6f2b3d473]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibitions in regulations 12 to 17 (asset-freeze etc) and regulations 17A to 17D (investments, accounts and corresponding banking relationships, insurance and reinsurance and sale or purchase of bonds)[^c-d0d40b4d9ffaa5115bd018806e707f01] do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Treasury under this paragraph.

(2) The Treasury may issue a licence which authorises acts by a particular person in relation to a non-UN designated person only where the Treasury consider that it is appropriate to issue the licence for a purpose set out in Part 1 or 2 of Schedule 2[^c-305f003028f35ce4e680132c3b01c93f].

(3) The Treasury may issue a licence which authorises acts in relation to a UN designated person only where the Treasury consider that it is appropriate to issue the licence for a purpose set out in Part 1 or 1A[^c-f8565d1ad88d0190188ad2af57e74b11] of Schedule 2.

(4) In paragraph (3) “**UN designated person**” means a person who is a designated person for the purposes of regulations 12 to 17 by reason of regulation 10 (designation of persons named by or under UN Security Council Resolutions).

(5)[^c-d45a2c7d2a48fe234e2e2846414b9bf1] The reference in paragraph (2) to a non-UN designated person is to be read as including a person who is owned or controlled directly or indirectly (within the meaning of regulation 7) by the non-UN designated person.

(6) The reference in paragraph (3) to a UN designated person is to be read as including a person who is owned or controlled directly or indirectly (within the meaning of regulation 7) by the UN designated person.
