# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-363f9c74a8fe071a3ed28dfd8b7f169f][^key-1218ac0187e37c24aabb018db8e50c40]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-1b9f2b064284ea652f10d4fc4590ebfe] In these Regulations—

- “**the Act**” means the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018;
- “[^key-ade142dd1db70b6d26ffd7455e59135b]**aircraft licence**” means a licence issued by the Secretary of State under regulation 41A (aircraft licences);
- “**arrangement**” includes any agreement, understanding, scheme, transaction or series of transactions, whether or not legally enforceable (but see Schedule 1 for the meaning of that term in that Schedule);
- “**CEMA**” means the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 [^c23621821];
- “**the Committee**” means the Committee of the Security Council established under paragraph 18 of resolution 1737;
- “**the Commissioners**” means the Commissioners for Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs;
- “**conduct**” includes acts and omissions;
- “[^key-d1d1cef535d84fdd527ca4e789ad2f71]**director disqualification licence**” means a licence under regulation 40A;
- “**document**” includes information recorded in any form and, in relation to information recorded otherwise than in legible form, references to its production include producing a copy of the information in legible form;
- “[^key-8f246e943e14e039b64dcc391a4e343d]**the Dual-Use Regulation**” means Council Regulation [(EC) No 428/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0428) of 5 May 2009 setting up a Community regime for the control of exports, transfer, brokering and transit of dual-use items;
- “**the EU Iran Regulation**” means Council Regulation (EU) No 267/2012 of 23 March 2012 concerning restrictive measures against Iran and repealing Regulation (EU) No 961/2010 [^c23621831] as it has effect in EU law;
- “[^key-7fbe59c71d81097bf717c1c0d931039e]**the Government of Iran**” includes its public bodies, corporations or agencies, its armed forces or any person acting on its behalf or at its direction;
- “**the Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action**” means the document attached as Annex A to resolution 2231;
- “**non-UN designated person**” means a person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of regulations 12 to 17;
- “**resolution 1737**” means resolution 1737 (2006)[^key-faa2751e71f6560a7bf46269aa5474fa] adopted by the Security Council on 23 December 2006;
- “[^key-3c4d1e51442c5e0a26401c5ef560458d]**resolution 1747**” means resolution 1747 (2007) adopted by the Security Council on 24 March 2007;
- “**resolution 1803**” means resolution 1803 (2008) adopted by the Security Council on 3 March 2008;
- “**resolution 1929**” means resolution 1929 (2010) adopted by the Security Council on 9 June 2010;
- “**resolution 2231**” means resolution 2231 (2015)[^key-85696ad0b429d9e557be0507225d9e24] adopted by the Security Council on 20 July 2015;
- “[^key-44cdeeae725f7eb4f56539e67546a34e]**ships licence**” means a licence issued by the Secretary of State under regulation 41B (ships licences);
- “**trade licence**” means a licence under regulation 41;
- “[^key-887e9efcab99b7c6c3fb9c15b183b224]**United Kingdom person**” has the meaning given in section 21 of the Act;
- “**Treasury licence**” means a licence under regulation 40(1).

(2)[^key-7f57d85b4f6f2dae6358b3487a49647b] In these Regulations, a person is to be regarded as connected with Iran if the person is—

- (a) an individual who is, or an association or combination of individuals who are, ordinarily resident in Iran,
- (b) an individual who is, or an association or combination of individuals who are, located in Iran,
- (c) a person, other than an individual, which is incorporated or constituted under the law of Iran,
- (d) a person, other than an individual, which is domiciled in Iran, or
- (e) a person, other than an individual, which is owned or controlled directly or indirectly (within the meaning of regulation 7) by a person within any of sub-paragraphs (a) to (d).
