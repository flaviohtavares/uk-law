# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 3

## 3. Application of prohibitions and requirements outside the United Kingdom[^key-1f1ac563019121418736a325d7aeb2ac][^key-5ce098cf90660ace47c0d9ff8bae708a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A United Kingdom person may contravene a relevant prohibition by conduct wholly or partly outside the United Kingdom.

(2) Any person may contravene a relevant prohibition by conduct in the territorial sea.

(3) In this regulation[^key-4368c40be744f38aa35656ec3e44a8d2] a “**relevant prohibition**” means any prohibition imposed—

- (a) by regulation 9(2) (confidential information),
- (b) by Part 3 (Finance),
- (c) by Part 5 (Trade), [^key-4a3ba4afc2492e0a8c0f016b46d8bf7b]...
- (ca)[^key-92ef3c828d83e9e54c7baa533edbc477] by or under Part 5B (Ships), or
- (d) by a condition of a Treasury licence , a trade licence or a ships licence[^key-cbb1bf820ed21a5d53742cd50bd6d2c7].

(4) A United Kingdom person may comply, or fail to comply, with a relevant requirement by conduct wholly or partly outside the United Kingdom.

(5) Any person may comply, or fail to comply, with a relevant requirement by conduct in the territorial sea.

(6) In this regulation a “**relevant requirement**” means any requirement imposed—

- (a) imposed by or under Part 7 (Information and records), or by reason of a request made under a power conferred by that Part, or
- (b) by a condition of a Treasury licence , a trade licence or a ships licence[^key-ed1a0fe0be0e6bfea164d02d90e3ae2f].

(7) Nothing in this regulation is to be taken to prevent a relevant prohibition or a relevant requirement from applying to conduct (by any person) in the United Kingdom
