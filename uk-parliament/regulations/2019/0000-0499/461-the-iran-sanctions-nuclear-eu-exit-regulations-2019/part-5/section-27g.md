# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 27G

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27G. Interpretation[^key-29bf5c8659ab2e2f015214b7c9ad1095]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Chapter “**Iran**” includes Iran's exclusive economic zone and continental shelf (which terms are to be interpreted in accordance with the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea).
