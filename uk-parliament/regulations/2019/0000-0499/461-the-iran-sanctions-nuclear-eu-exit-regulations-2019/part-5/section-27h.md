# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 27H

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27H. Export of energy-related goods[^c-29bf5c8659ab2e2f015214b7c9ad1095]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of energy-related goods to, or for use in, Iran is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).
