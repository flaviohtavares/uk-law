# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 33D

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33D. Technical assistance relating to ships[^c-db7f3e7e8e25298b2313fe4e7c4a4013]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly provide technical assistance relating to a ship to, or for the benefit of, a designated person.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with an offence of contravening paragraph (1) (“**P**”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the technical assistance was provided to, or for the benefit of, a designated person.

(4) In this regulation, “**designated person**” means a person designated under regulation 5 (power to designate persons) for the purposes of this regulation.
