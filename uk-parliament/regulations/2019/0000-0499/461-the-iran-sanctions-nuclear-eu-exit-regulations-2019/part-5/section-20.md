# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 20

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Meaning of “restricted goods” and “restricted technology” in this Chapter[^c-ca252f0b3315cb42540f3bba6234f4d1][^c-305e2e1eddb9ffc83d5d74610bd85f73]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**restricted goods**” means—
  - (za) maritime goods,[^c-9b53e451d3b9ee14f2ec7d3f4205ed13]
  - (a) military goods,
  - (b) missile-list goods,
  - (c) nuclear-list goods,
  - (d) graphite and relevant metals,
  - (e) other restricted goods;
- “**restricted technology**” means—
  - (za) maritime technology,[^c-dbd74cda3b17c6285f80a96636e66e9b]
  - (a) military technology,
  - (b) missile-list technology,
  - (c) nuclear-list technology,
  - (d) other restricted technology.
  - (e) [^c-d46bc2164a0a7b3bebf1a5c91945d3b0]...
