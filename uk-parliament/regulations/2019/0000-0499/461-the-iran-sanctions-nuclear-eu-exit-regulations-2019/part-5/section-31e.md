# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 31E

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31E. Acquisition of gold, precious metals and diamonds[^key-4ad1f2c796308dd4c656dc2768c0e8b2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly acquire gold, precious metals or diamonds which—

- (a) originate in Iran, or
- (b) are located in Iran.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but—

- (a) it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening the prohibition in paragraph (1)(a) (“**P**”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the gold, precious metals or diamonds originated in Iran,
- (b) it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening the prohibition in paragraph (1)(b) (“**Q**”) to show that Q did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the gold, precious metals or diamonds were located in Iran.
