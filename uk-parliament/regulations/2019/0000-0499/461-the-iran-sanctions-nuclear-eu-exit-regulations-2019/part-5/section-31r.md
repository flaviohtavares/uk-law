> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31R. Supply and delivery of petrochemicals to a third country[^c-4ad1f2c796308dd4c656dc2768c0e8b2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly supply or deliver petrochemicals from a place in Iran to a third country.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes the prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening paragraph (1) (“**P**”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the supply or delivery was from a place in Iran, whether directly or indirectly.
