# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 31C

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31C. Brokering services relating to relevant goods and technology[^key-c4d34ec79d3132b6349a11b6904a481b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly provide brokering services in relation to any arrangement described in regulation 31B(1).

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening paragraph (1) (“**P**”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the brokering services were being provided in relation to an arrangement mentioned in regulation 31B(1).
