> Text as at 2026-09-29.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Export of restricted goods to, or for use in, Iran[^c-6721bd123e16c9cfd5f3524f137e59a6][^c-dfe7aab40f67cca005d2cea75db72b43]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of restricted goods to, or for use in, Iran is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).
