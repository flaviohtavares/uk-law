# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 27Q

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27Q. Supply and delivery of sectoral software goods[^key-29bf5c8659ab2e2f015214b7c9ad1095]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly supply or deliver sectoral software goods from a third country to a place in Iran.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening paragraph (1) (“**P**”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the goods were destined (or ultimately destined) for Iran.
