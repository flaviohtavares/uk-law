# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 19

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Immigration[^key-bd343cbdf977e7f838552c010ee9a5b2][^key-fe06a645fe2005f1572cc1fdb3335d06]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of this regulation is an excluded person for the purposes of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971 [^c23621881].
