# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 56

> Text as at 2026-09-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Penalties for offences[^c-470d5d183b46d48d5ccdde8fa2e00445][^c-910ac279549be8fa86fc0364fe65854f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who commits an offence under any provision of Part 3 (Finance) , regulation 36D(1), (2) or (3) (aircraft offences), 36F (prohibition on chartering or operating a specified ship), 36G(4) (prohibition on port entry), 36H(4) (directions prohibiting port entry) or 36I(3) (movement of ships: directions),[^c-c6888a49ecccd8e7a587dd10e5fac740] or regulation 43 (finance: licensing offences), is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-428869651dba9de97415c455f1cdfafc] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 7 years or a fine (or both).

(2) A person who commits an offence under any provision of Part 5 (Trade) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-916d1925dde130b4e5790a1b38b99f65] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 10 years or a fine (or both).

(2A)[^c-48a1142610b8f4b750729d798f6b14eb] A person who commits an offence under regulation 36D(4) (disclosure of information), 36H(6) (directions prohibiting port entry: confidentiality) or 36I(5) (movement of ships: confidentiality) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (or both).

(3) A person who commits an offence under regulation 9(6) (confidentiality), 43A (director disqualification: licensing offences),[^c-bd571693a4741909aec1372bd49a0cd8] 44 (Trade: licensing offences), 44A (aircraft: licensing offences), 44B (ships: licensing offences),[^c-92fc4cbcdac7ce2fc58dae0f2176d286] 52(6) or 53(5) (offences in connection with trade licences) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-2b812418508b05feae9faf24959be8c1] or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
- (d) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or a fine (or both).

(4) A person who commits an offence under regulation 46(6) or 50 (information offences in connection with Part 3) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (or both);
- (c) on summary conviction in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (or both).

(5) In relation to an offence committed before 2nd May 2022[^c-3e5c4c86b10f8558d56d22fe043e00d7] the reference in each of paragraphs (1)(a), (2)(a) and (3)(a) to the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-4c781d0292625c902f2770504e703c7a] is to be read as a reference to 6 months.
