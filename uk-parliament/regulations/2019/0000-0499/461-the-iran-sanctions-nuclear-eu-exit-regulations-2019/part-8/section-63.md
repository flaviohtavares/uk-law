# The Iran (Sanctions) (Nuclear) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 63

> Text as at 2026-09-29.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Application of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^c-24a7897153887465b3884d1cc20beb92][^c-2d9fe2950fa12a3063fabf910edb9406]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (investigatory powers) [^c23622201] applies to any offence under Part 3 (Finance) or regulation 43 (finance: licensing offences).
