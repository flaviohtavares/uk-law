# The Construction Products (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-8e201a473d09e711bda88a569ae2a068]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “**the 2011 Regulation**” means Regulation (EU) No 305/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2011 laying down harmonised conditions for the marketing of construction products and repealing Council Directive [89/106/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0106);
- “**the 2013 Regulations**” means the Construction Products Regulations 2013 [^c23684231].
