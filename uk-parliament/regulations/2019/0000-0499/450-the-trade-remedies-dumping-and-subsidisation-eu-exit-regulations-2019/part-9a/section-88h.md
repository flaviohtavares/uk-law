# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 88H

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88H. Determination to revoke[^c-a124b99d559275a9b8b9a0ffa482a27d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if the TRA has determined that the application to goods of an anti-dumping amount or a countervailing amount should be revoked.

(2) The TRA must recommend to the Secretary of State that the application to goods of the anti-dumping amount or the countervailing amount should be revoked.
