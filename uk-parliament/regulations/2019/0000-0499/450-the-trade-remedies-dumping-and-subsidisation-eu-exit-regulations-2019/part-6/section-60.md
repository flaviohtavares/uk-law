# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 60

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Facilitation visit[^c-ab6f454504109c97bfd1907624e40382]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), the TRA may make such arrangements in connection with a facilitation visit in the United Kingdom as it considers appropriate.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), in exceptional circumstances, the TRA may make such arrangements in connection with a facilitation visit in any foreign country or territory as it considers appropriate, provided that the requirements set out in regulation 58(4) to (7) are met.

(3) The TRA must not conduct a facilitation visit after the statement of essential facts is published.

(4) In the application of regulation 58 (verification visit) to paragraph (2), reference in that regulation to a verification visit is be taken to include a facilitation visit.
