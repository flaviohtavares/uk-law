# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 61A

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61A. Alternative options for final affirmative determination[^key-7fd824ea99ae30f61959b8b640f529eb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The TRA must consider giving two or more options as part of its recommendation to the Secretary of State under paragraph 17(3) or (4) of Schedule 4 to the Act in the following circumstances—

- (a) where the TRA considers that applying an anti-dumping amount or a countervailing amount in accordance with its proposed recommendation would not meet the economic interest test;
- (b) where the TRA otherwise considers that it is appropriate.
