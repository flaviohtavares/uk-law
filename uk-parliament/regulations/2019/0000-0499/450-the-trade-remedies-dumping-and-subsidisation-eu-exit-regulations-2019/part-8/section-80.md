# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 80

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Monitoring compliance with an undertaking[^key-36845a5fe5bc09d6845c51f7622d2648]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The TRA must monitor compliance with the terms and conditions of an undertaking (see regulation 78).

(2) Part 6 applies to this Part to the extent that the TRA considers relevant.
