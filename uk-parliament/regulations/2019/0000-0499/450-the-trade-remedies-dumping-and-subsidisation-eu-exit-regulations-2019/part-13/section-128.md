## 128.[^key-3bfc3526eac0e6f64a2fb556218e2d34]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 83 (breach of an undertaking) has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a), “or the Secretary of State” were omitted; and
- (b) in paragraphs (1)(b), for “its” there were substituted “the Secretary of State's”.
