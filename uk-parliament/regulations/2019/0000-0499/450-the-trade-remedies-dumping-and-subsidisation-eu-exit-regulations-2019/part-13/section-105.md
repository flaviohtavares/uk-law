> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 105.[^c-72692e30ffde5ba6bf07bfe16a6b0ab7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 41 (revision of scope of an investigation) has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “its determination”, there were substituted “ the Secretary of State's decision ”; and
- (b) at the end of paragraph (5)(a), there were inserted “(or been otherwise available to the Secretary of State, where no application was made)”.
