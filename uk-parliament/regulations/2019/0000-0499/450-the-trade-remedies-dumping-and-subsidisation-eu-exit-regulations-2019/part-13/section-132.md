# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 132

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 132.[^key-06357cc23e936c684e16cf5d58d66a1d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 91 (relevant period in a dumping investigation) has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “recommend”, there were substituted “ determine, as part of making a preliminary decision, ”; and
- (b) in paragraph (3), for “recommendation”, there were substituted “ determination ”.
