# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 96

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Determination to transition EU trade remedies measure[^c-dc68a8dc60108bd892ea7da55f232855]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by publishing a notice determine to transition an EU trade remedies measure.

(2) A notice under paragraph (1) may be published at any time before replacement of EU trade duty.
