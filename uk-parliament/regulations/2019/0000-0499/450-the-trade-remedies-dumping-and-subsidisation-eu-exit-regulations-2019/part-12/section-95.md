# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 95

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Initiating an investigation before replacement of EU trade duty[^key-627292997480e8ca53f301dbda9be9b4][^key-dc68a8dc60108bd892ea7da55f232855]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if the TRA is requested to initiate an investigation in an application made by or on behalf of an applicant UK industry before replacement of EU trade duty.

(2) The TRA may initiate an investigation only to the extent the application relates to goods which are the subject of—

- (a) an anti-dumping investigation initiated by the European Commission in accordance with article 5 of the EU anti-dumping regulation; or
- (b) a countervailing duty investigation initiated by the European Commission in accordance with article 10 of the EU countervailing regulation.
