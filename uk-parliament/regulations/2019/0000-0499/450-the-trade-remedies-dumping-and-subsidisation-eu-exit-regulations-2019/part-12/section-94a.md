# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 94A

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94A. Treatment of trade remedies measure transitioned under this Part[^key-dc68a8dc60108bd892ea7da55f232855]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies for the purposes of the other Parts of these Regulations.

(2) An anti-dumping amount or a countervailing amount applied by public notice under regulation 96A(1) or 101C(2)(a)[^key-8ebb4670398e43b87ab166734a685c12] is to be treated as if it were an anti-dumping amount or a countervailing amount[^key-f2ce1418ffc3d09e2707702b17d0112c]—

- (a) applied under section 13(2) of the Act;
- (b) giving effect to a recommendation made by the TRA under paragraph 17(3) or (4) of Schedule 4 to the Act;
- (c) following an investigation conducted in accordance with Schedule 4 to the Act and the other Parts of these Regulations.

(3) A reference to a public notice under section 13 includes a reference to a public notice under regulation 96A(1) or 101C(2)(a)[^key-8b766c842315d28805c0835139482691].
