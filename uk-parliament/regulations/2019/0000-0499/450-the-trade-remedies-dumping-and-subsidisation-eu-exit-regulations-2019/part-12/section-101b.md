# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 101B

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101B. Acceptance of recommendation before replacement of EU trade duty[^key-dc68a8dc60108bd892ea7da55f232855]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if the Secretary of State accepts under regulation 101(1) a recommendation[^key-da622a1c8a628ec46203fec87b783abd] before replacement of EU trade duty.

(2) The Secretary of State must publish a notice containing—

- (a) in relation to a recommendation of variation—
  - (i) the information set out in regulation 101A(2)(a)(i) to (iii)[^key-b5bfecbfc96cf14c3541c7a5d92887a4];
  - (ii) a statement that the EU trade remedies measure will be transitioned on replacement of EU trade duty;
- (b) in relation to a recommendation of revocation—
  - (i) the information set out in regulation 101A(2)(a)(i) to (iii)[^key-f9a6783330a4334cf526f44b85d5df74];
  - (ii) a statement that the EU trade remedies measure will not be transitioned on replacement of EU trade duty.
