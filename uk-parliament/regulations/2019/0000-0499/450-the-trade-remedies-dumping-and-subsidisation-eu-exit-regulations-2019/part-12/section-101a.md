# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 101A

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101A. Rejection of recommendation[^c-dc68a8dc60108bd892ea7da55f232855]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if the Secretary of State rejects under regulation 101(1) a recommendation[^c-f9295aad61b5d9159f46c59b19811590] and does not make a decision under regulation 101D[^c-0c87f96a3007d8e79487bb000adddde0].

(2) The Secretary of State must—

- (a) publish a notice containing—
  - (i) a description of the goods to which the notice relates;
  - (ii) a summary of the review;
  - (iii) the recommendation made under regulation 100(1);
  - (iv) the reasons for rejecting the recommendation;
- (b) notify interested parties;
- (c) lay a statement before the House of Commons setting out the reasons for rejecting the recommendation.
