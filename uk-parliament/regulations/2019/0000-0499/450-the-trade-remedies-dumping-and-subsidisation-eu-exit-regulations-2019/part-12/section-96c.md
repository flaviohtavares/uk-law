# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 96C

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96C. Transition of varied EU trade remedies measure[^key-dc68a8dc60108bd892ea7da55f232855]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if the Secretary of State has accepted under regulation 101(1) a recommendation of variation[^key-bb1142e355cfdfecf92c5cd8eb441ad5] in relation to the relevant EU trade remedies measure.

(2) The information specified under regulation 96A(2)(c) must be consistent with that recommendation.
