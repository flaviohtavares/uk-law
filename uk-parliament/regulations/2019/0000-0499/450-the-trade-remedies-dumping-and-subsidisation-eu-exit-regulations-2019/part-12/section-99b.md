# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 99B

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99B. Determination of export price[^c-bf01f55b60c8a167108f8ce668b12f11][^c-dc68a8dc60108bd892ea7da55f232855]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if the TRA constructs an export price for the purposes of a transition review in accordance with regulation 15.

(2) The TRA may deduct any anti-dumping amount paid if it is not reflected in resale prices and subsequent selling prices in the United Kingdom.
