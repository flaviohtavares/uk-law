# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 97

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Transition review of EU trade remedies measure[^c-dc68a8dc60108bd892ea7da55f232855]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The TRA must review every EU trade remedies measure specified in a determination notice.

(2) The TRA may conduct a transition review—

- (a) before replacement of EU trade duty, in which case the review is of an EU trade remedies measure; or
- (b) after replacement of EU trade duty, in which case the review is of a UK trade remedies measure.
