# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 94C

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94C. Scope review of trade remedies measure transitioned under this Part[^key-dc68a8dc60108bd892ea7da55f232855]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies for the purposes of a scope review in relation to an anti-dumping amount or a countervailing amount applied by public notice under regulation 96A(1) or 101C(2)(a)[^key-33175210a3dbe55e7ef6ab764447254b].

(2) Regulation 74 (scope review) has effect with the omission of paragraph (4)(a).
