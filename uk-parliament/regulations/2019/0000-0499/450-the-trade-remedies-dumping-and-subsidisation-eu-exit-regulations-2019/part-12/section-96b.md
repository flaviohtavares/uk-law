# The Trade Remedies (Dumping and Subsidisation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 96B

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96B. Transition of expired EU trade remedies measure[^key-dc68a8dc60108bd892ea7da55f232855]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if an EU trade remedies measure specified in a determination notice remains in force pending the outcome of an EU expiry review.

(2) The period specified under regulation 96A(2)(c)(iii) must end 30 days after replacement of EU trade duty unless regulation 96C applies.

(3) In this regulation, “EU expiry review” means a review conducted by the European Commission in accordance with—

- (a) Article 11(2) of the EU anti-dumping regulation; or
- (b) Article 18 of the EU countervailing regulation.
