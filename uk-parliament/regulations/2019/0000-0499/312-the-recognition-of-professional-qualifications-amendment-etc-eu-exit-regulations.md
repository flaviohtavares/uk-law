# The Recognition of Professional Qualifications (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

> Text as at 2021-06-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

2019 No. 312

Exiting The European Union

Professional Qualifications

Education, England

The Recognition of Professional Qualifications (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

Made

19th February 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State is designated [^c23763971] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 [^c23763981] (“**the 1972 Act**”) in relation to recognition of higher education diplomas, formal qualifications, or experience in the occupation, required for the pursuit of professions or occupations.

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the 1972 Act, section 132(1) and (2) of the Education Act 2002 [^c23763991] and section 8(1) of, and paragraph 21(b) of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 [^c23764001] (“**the 2018 Act**”).

In accordance with paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 7 to the 2018 Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-a665e33530aa56879ab000ae3cbfa03d]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Recognition of Professional Qualifications (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019.

(2) This regulation, regulation 2 (so far as it relates to Part 1 of Schedule 1) and Part 1 of Schedule 1 come into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made.

(3) The remainder of these Regulations comes into force on exit day.

(4) In these Regulations—

- “**the 2007 Regulations**” means the European Communities (Recognition of Professional Qualifications) Regulations 2007 [^c23764011];
- “**the principal Regulations**” means the European Union (Recognition of Professional Qualifications) Regulations 2015 [^c23764021].

## 2. Amendment etc. of the principal Regulations and the 2007 Regulations[^key-50e5d44efcb3a9dd71e048fa98d5497c][^key-ef64ae6dc03e0338267aefdc27a5d2ff]

Schedule 1 contains—

- (a) amendments of the principal Regulations;
- (b) saving and transitional provisions relating to the principal Regulations and the 2007 Regulations.

## 3. Amendment etc. of secondary legislation relating to school teachers[^key-24bbb34ce7e2c3e474886a1b947a7950]

Schedule 2 contains amendments of, and saving and transitional provision relating to, legislation relating to school teachers.

## 4. Revocation of retained direct EU legislation[^key-7ebbb00a48446242734ca766577bbe5f]

The following instruments are revoked—

- (a) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/983 of 24 June 2015 on the procedure for issuance of the European Professional Card and the application of the alert mechanism pursuant to Directive [2005/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2005/36) of the European Parliament and of the Council;
- (b) Commission Delegated Decision (EU) 2016/790 of 13 January 2016 amending Annex V to Directive [2005/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2005/36) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the evidence of formal qualifications and the titles of training courses;
- (c) Commission Regulation (EU) No 623/2012 of 11 July 2012 amending Annex II to Directive [2005/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2005/36) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the recognition of professional qualifications;
- (d) Commission Regulation (EU) No 213/2011 of 3 March 2011 amending Annexes II and V to Directive [2005/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2005/36) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the recognition of professional qualifications;
- (e) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 279/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/279) of 6 April 2009 amending Annex II to Directive [2005/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2005/36) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the recognition of professional qualifications;
- (f) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 755/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/755) of 31 July 2008 amending Annex II to Directive [2005/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2005/36) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the recognition of professional qualifications;
- (g) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1430/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/1430) of 5 December 2007 amending Annexes II and III to Directive [2005/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2005/36) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the recognition of professional qualifications.

### PART 3 — Transitional and saving provision[^key-bc225fccdbdecb504262eac11345a303]

#### 40. Saving of decisions etc. taken before IP completion day[^M_I_b12b671a-d919-43a6-ba68-e25a284b2383]

The amendments and revocations made by these Regulations do not affect the validity of any action or decision taken or right accrued under the principal Regulations or the 2007 Regulations before IP completion day, except as provided in this Part.

#### 41. Language testing – transitional and saving provision[^M_I_f696b9eb-63b5-4cfe-82bc-cfc957cf2133]

(1) Where a competent authority has recognised a professional qualification—

- (a) before IP completion day, under the principal Regulations; or
