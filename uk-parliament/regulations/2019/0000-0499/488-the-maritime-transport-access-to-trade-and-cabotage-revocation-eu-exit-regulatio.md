# The Maritime Transport Access to Trade and Cabotage (Revocation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 488

Exiting The European Union

Merchant Shipping

The Maritime Transport Access to Trade and Cabotage (Revocation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

Made

6th March 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001].

In accordance with paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 7 to that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Maritime Transport Access to Trade and Cabotage (Revocation) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force on exit day.

## 2. Revocation of [Council Regulation (EEC) No 2919/85](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1985/2919)

[Council Regulation (EEC) No 2919/85](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1985/2919) laying down the conditions for access to the arrangements under the Revised Convention for the navigation of the Rhine relating to vessels belonging to the Rhine Navigation is revoked.

## 3. Revocation of [Council Regulation (EEC) No 4055/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/4055)

[Council Regulation (EEC) No 4055/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/4055) applying the principle of freedom to provide services to maritime transport between member States and between member States and third countries is revoked.

## 4. Revocation of [Council Regulation (EEC) No 4057/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/4057)

[Council Regulation (EEC) No 4057/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/4057) on unfair pricing practices in maritime transport is revoked.

## 5. Revocation of [Council Regulation (EEC) No 4058/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/4058)

[Council Regulation (EEC) No 4058/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/4058) concerning coordinated action to safeguard free access to cargoes in ocean trades is revoked.

## 6. Revocation of [Council Regulation (EEC) No 3921/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/3921)

[Council Regulation (EEC) No 3921/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/3921) laying down the conditions under which non-resident carriers may transport goods or passengers by inland waterway within a member State is revoked.

## 7. Revocation of [Council Regulation (EEC) No 3577/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/3577)

[Council Regulation (EEC) No 3577/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/3577) applying the principle of freedom to provide services to maritime transport within member States (maritime cabotage) is revoked.

## 8. Revocation of Council Regulation [(EC) No 1356/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/1356)

Council Regulation [(EC) No 1356/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/1356) on common rules applicable to the transport of goods or passengers by inland waterway between member States with a view to establishing freedom to provide such transport services is revoked.

## 9. Revocation of Council Regulation [(EC) No 789/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0789)

[Council Regulation (EEC) No 789/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0789) on the transfer of cargo and passenger ships between registers within the Community and repealing [Council Regulation (EEC) No 613/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/0613) is revoked.

## 10. Revocation of Council [Decision 83/573/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1983/0573)

Council [Decision 83/573/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1983/0573) concerning counter-measures in the field of international merchant shipping is revoked.

## 11. Revocation of Decision [167/2006/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/19167/2006)

Decision No [167/2006/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/19167/2006) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the activities of certain third countries in the field of cargo shipping is revoked.

## 12. Amendment of Annex XIII to the EEA agreement

Paragraphs 43, 43a, 43b, 53, 53a, 56q, 57, 58 and 59 of Annex XIII to the EEA agreement are omitted.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address failures of retained EU law to operate effectively and other deficiencies arising (in particular under paragraphs 8(2)(a) and 8(2)(c)) from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union.

These Regulations revoke a number of pieces of retained EU legislation in the field of maritime transport market access and regulation.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.

An Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
