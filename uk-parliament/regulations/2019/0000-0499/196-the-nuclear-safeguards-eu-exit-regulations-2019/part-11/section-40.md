# The Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 40

> Text as at 2026-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Publication of information by the ONR[^key-ec24cfaacb1e536018a548a4f0fd3ac1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The ONR must publish on the ONR website, and update where appropriate, information relating to—

- (a) withdrawals of qualifying nuclear material from safeguards during the preceding calendar year; and
- (b) inventories of civil plutonium and uranium in the United Kingdom at the end of each calendar year.
