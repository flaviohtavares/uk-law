# The Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 24

> Text as at 2026-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Communication of change of date[^key-7553b452894531864d83fd717383d42f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An operator must inform the ONR, without delay, of any change in the dates for packing before transfer, transport or unpacking of qualifying nuclear material, which have been given in the notifications provided for under regulations 21 or 22, and must provide an indication of the revised dates if known, unless the change gives rise to a special report under Regulation 16.
