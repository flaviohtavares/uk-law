# The Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 21

> Text as at 2026-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Exports[^key-e86e1fec74077a25a1da2f426bc1b29b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An operator must give advance notification to the ONR if any qualifying nuclear material is exported outside the United Kingdom—

- (a) where the consignment exceeds one effective kilogram; or
- (b) where a qualifying nuclear facility transfers a total quantity of materials to the same State that could exceed one effective kilogram in any consecutive period of twelve months, even though no single consignment exceeds one effective kilogram.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), an operator must give the notification under paragraph (1) after the conclusion of the contractual arrangements leading to the transfer, using the form set out in Part 5 of Schedule 1, and must ensure that the notification reaches the ONR at least 13[^key-ba4530f5ed2bd69e27ad972bf78174d6] days before the day on which the material is to be packed for transfer.

(3) If so required for reasons of physical protection, special arrangements concerning the form and transmission of the notification may be agreed upon between an operator and the ONR.

(4) This regulation does not apply to ores nor to waste.
