# The Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 16

> Text as at 2026-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Special report[^c-1d1f0a2d2c6a8bce04accdca967801ed]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An operator must send to the ONR a special report whenever the circumstances referred to in regulation 17 or 23 arise.

(2) The ONR may—

- (a) request further details or explanations in connection with a special report; and
- (b) specify, in the particular safeguard provisions for a qualifying nuclear facility, additional requirements concerning the type of information to be supplied in a special report.

(3) If the ONR requests further detail or explanation in connection with a special report, the operator must send it to the ONR without delay.
