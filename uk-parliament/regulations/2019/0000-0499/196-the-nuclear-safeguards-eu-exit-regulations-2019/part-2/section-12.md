# The Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 12

> Text as at 2026-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Accounting reports[^c-f2868a93cc6cc5f1cf5e1677a46001ad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An operator must provide the ONR with accounting reports in accordance with regulations 14[^c-4077f937a88319e454116e8f0765ef43] to 20 in respect of each material balance area.

(2) An operator must ensure that the accounting reports contain up to date information and must correct the information at a later date if necessary.

(3) On a written request by the ONR, an operator must supply further details, explanations, amplifications or clarifications of any information set out in the relevant accounting report within the period of 15 days beginning with the day on which the operator receives the request from the ONR.
