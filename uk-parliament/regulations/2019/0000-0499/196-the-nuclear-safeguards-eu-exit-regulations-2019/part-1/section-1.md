# The Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 1

> Text as at 2026-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-9a8fe37b368165a0f571c2efefe23afe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit) Regulations 2019.

(2) Subject to paragraph 3, these Regulations come into force on exit day.

(3) Regulations 7 to 9 come into force on 1st January 2021.
