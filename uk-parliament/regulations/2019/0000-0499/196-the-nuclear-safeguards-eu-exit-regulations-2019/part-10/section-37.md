# The Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 37

> Text as at 2026-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Access to safeguards equipment[^key-5fc49a2bb385110b1b8d8eacedb8b9ee]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An operator must permit the ONR to have reasonable access to the safeguards equipment in a qualifying nuclear facility.
