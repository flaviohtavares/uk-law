## 51. General consequential and supplementary amendments Part 1 of Schedule 3[^c-d747558285057bdf42fde13ac54901df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 3, Part 1 contains consequential and supplementary amendments of the Nuclear Safeguards and Electricity (Finance) Act 1978 [^c23563311], the Nuclear Safeguards Act 2000 and the Nuclear Safeguards (Notification) Regulations 2004 [^c23563321].
