# The Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 47

> Text as at 2026-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Notification of change[^c-2ec6d9266c5d19b5dee9fd2bf360ec6a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An operator or other person, who has sent a notification to the Secretary of State under regulation 45 or 46(3), must inform the Secretary of State of any material change in the information provided within the period of 15 days beginning with the day on which any change in the information occurs.
