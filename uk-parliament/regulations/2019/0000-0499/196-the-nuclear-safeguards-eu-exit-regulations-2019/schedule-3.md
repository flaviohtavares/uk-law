# The Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2026-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 3 — Consequential and supplementary amendments for Nuclear Safeguards Act 2000 and related legislation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 51 and 52

### PART 1

#### 1. Nuclear Safeguards and Electricity (Finance) Act 1978[^c-1f521b630e99a99ea9c9bdf19e28393e]

The Nuclear Safeguards and Electricity (Finance) Act 1978 is amended as follows.

**2.**[^c-0ede13cced4f4941e0a3c32b8b2b4d15] In section 1—

- (a) in sub-section (1), for “made on 6th September at Vienna between the United Kingdom, the European Economic Energy Community and the International Atomic Energy Agency” substitute “ made on 7th June 2018 at Vienna between the United Kingdom and the International Atomic Energy Agency ”;
- (b) in sub-section (2), omit the words from the beginning to “1977, and”;
- (c) in paragraph (a) of subsection (2), omit the words “(of which the Protocol attached thereto forms an integral part)”.

**3.**[^c-70b77fd3bb40e44f6e128560568c0434] In section 2(1)—

- (a) in paragraph (a) for “articles 71 to 84” substitute “ articles 69 to 82 ”;
- (b) in paragraph (b) for “article 50” substitute “ article 48 ”;
- (c) in the words after paragraph (b) for “article 85 of the Safeguards Agreement” substitute “ article 83 of the Safeguards Agreement ”.

**4.**[^c-65953faf92b2113f5afe1e9c1efdfcd3] In section 2(2)—

- (a) in paragraph (a)—
  - (i) for “articles 5, 9(c) and 87” substitute “ articles 4, 9(c) and 85 ”;
  - (ii) omit “and the provisions of the Protocol which forms part of the Safeguards Agreement”;
- (b) in paragraph (b) for “where article 83 applies” substitute “ where article 81 applies ”.

**5.**[^c-c4ddbf32e2fcaa56cc36ff335bdc96ac] In section 2(7) for “article 92(2)” substitute “ article 90 ”.

**6.**[^c-64e80b1b55bd45551caad71c9ab35f33] In section 3(1)(b) for “article 76(d)” substitute “ article 74(d) ”.

#### 7. Nuclear Safeguards Act 2000[^c-2c6bc1e99802b37c4564a691d5bdc3bf]

The Nuclear Safeguards Act 2000 is amended as follows.

**8.**[^c-8c6683d5c3f4a263be3bb66ab45da881] In section 1(1)—

- (a) in the definition of “Additional Protocol” for “on 22nd September 1998 (Cm.4282)” substitute “ on 7th June 2018 ”;
- (b) in the definition of “Additional Protocol information” omit “, or the third or fourth paragraph of Annex III to,”;
- (c) in the definition of “Agency inspector” for “Article 85 of the Safeguards Agreement” substitute “ Article 83 of the Safeguards Agreement ”;
- (d) in the definition of “Safeguards Agreement” for “on 6th September 1976 between the United Kingdom, the European Atomic Energy Community and the Agency” substitute “ on 7th June 2018 between the United Kingdom and the Agency ”.

**9.**[^c-ee5f4365426293097c33cd3ce56694a7] In section 1(3) for “22nd September 1998” substitute “ 7th June 2018 ”.

#### 10. The Nuclear Safeguards (Notification) Regulations 2004[^c-3086119c82825f80de4aec85d6b8c59f]

The Nuclear Safeguards (Notification) Regulations 2004 are amended as follows.

**11.**[^c-fa678e4c210c6bcbdf3cbf05e988762e] In regulation 2, omit the definition of “the commencement date”.

**12.**[^c-6afefed3f01c5ee98dbd0821a76364ac] Omit regulation 3.

**13.**[^c-82db108fbdc9307723edd534d01b7504] In the Schedule—

- (a) in the heading for Part I for “ACTIVITIES REFERRED TO IN REGULATIONS 3(1) AND 4(1)” substitute “ ACTIVITIES REFERRED TO IN REGULATION 4(1) ”; and
- (b) in paragraph 1 for “The activities referred to in regulations 3(1) and 4(1) are” substitute “ The activities referred to in regulation 4(1) are ”.

### PART 2 — General consequential and supplementary amendments

#### 14. Revocation of retained EU law[^c-132d8763be4f72b6f61b428f7d580df6]

(1) The following are revoked—

(2) Commission Regulation (EURATOM) 302/2005 [^c23563331]; and

(3) any Decision made by the Commission under—

- (a) Article 6 of Regulation (EURATOM) 302/2005; or
- (b) Articles 7 and 8 of Regulation (EURATOM) 3227/76 [^c23563341]

which was directed to a person in the United Kingdom and in force on commencement day.
