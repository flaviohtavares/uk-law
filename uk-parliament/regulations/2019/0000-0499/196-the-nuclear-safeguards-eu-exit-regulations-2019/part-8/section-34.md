# The Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 34

> Text as at 2026-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Qualifying nuclear facilities which are used partly for civil activities[^key-7e66eaf625e1f3d4d605cbea49c0bfe4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to anything done for defence purposes within the meaning of section 70 of the Energy Act 2013.

(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1), these Regulations apply to qualifying nuclear material which is used in civil activities when that material is in a qualifying nuclear facility which is partly used for civil activities.
