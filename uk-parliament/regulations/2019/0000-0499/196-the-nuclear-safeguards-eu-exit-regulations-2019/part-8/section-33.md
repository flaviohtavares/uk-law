# The Nuclear Safeguards (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 33

> Text as at 2026-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Withdrawal from civil activities[^c-0d1c61e5673d0d7da49d885b151f9107]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An operator must not withdraw qualifying nuclear material from civil activities except with the previous written consent of the ONR.

(2) An operator must provide the ONR with advance notification of any proposed withdrawal of qualifying nuclear material from civil activities using the form set out in Part 12 of Schedule 1, such notice to be received by the ONR at least 14 days before the day on which the qualifying nuclear material is to be withdrawn.
