# The Over the Counter Derivatives, Central Counterparties and Trade Repositories (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provision) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/335
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Professional secrecy[^key-928fd5007f14b3fd17898ec6c680145e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 83 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1 omit—

- (a) “designated in accordance with Article 22 and the authorities referred to in Article 81(3), for ESMA”;
- (b) “or ESMA”.

(3) In paragraph 3, omit “ESMA,” in both places where it appears.

(4) In paragraph 4 omit—

- (a) “ESMA,”;
- (b) “or the relevant central banks”;
- (c) “competent authority or other”.

(5) In paragraph 5, for the words from “national law” to the end of the paragraph substitute “ any provision of the law applying in any part of the United Kingdom ”.
