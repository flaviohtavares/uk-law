## 25. Authorisation of a CCP[^c-1d9283d671b7aa52716b48303dd513a6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 14 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “legal person established in the Union” substitute “ body corporate or unincorporated association established in the United Kingdom ”;
- (b) for “the competent authority of the Member State where it is established” substitute “ its competent authority ”.

(3) Omit paragraphs 2 and 5.
