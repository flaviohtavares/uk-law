# The Over the Counter Derivatives, Central Counterparties and Trade Repositories (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provision) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/335
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Intragroup transactions[^c-42d19746935058b376141058c64aa5ad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 3 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1—

- (a) for “Union” substitute “ United Kingdom ”;
- (b) at the end insert “ before IP completion day[^c-fe5e5196695ff40b9328fe6d2be76137] or[^c-b967a341fe2a490641dc9025fe20e46e] the Treasury have made regulations under that Article in respect of that third country after IP completion day”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) in point (a)—
  - (i) in point (i)—
    - (aa) for “Union” substitute “ United Kingdom ”;
    - (bb) at the end insert “ before IP completion day[^c-58bf11025338d46e621ae0e7929f19ac] or[^c-63b829e005756b1a2f2453e1234824c4] the Treasury have made regulations under that Article in respect of that third country after IP completion day”;
  - (ii) in point (iv) after “control procedures;” insert “ or ”;
- (b) omit points (b) and (c);
- (c) in point (d)—
  - (i) for “Union” substitute “ United Kingdom ”;
  - (ii) at the end insert “ before IP completion day[^c-9c44eb297cf8f4af58648d0ee11a5621] or[^c-a834bf1f19d0375fcd96b1bb0cf8e66a] the Treasury have made regulations under that Article in respect of that third country after IP completion day ”.

(4) In paragraph 3, for point (b) substitute—

> - (b) covered by the same supervision on a consolidated basis in accordance with the Capital Requirements Regulation, or in relation to a group the parent undertaking of which has its head office in a third country, the same supervision on a consolidated basis by a third-country competent authority verified as equivalent to that governed by the principles laid down in accordance with the Capital Requirements Regulation.

.
