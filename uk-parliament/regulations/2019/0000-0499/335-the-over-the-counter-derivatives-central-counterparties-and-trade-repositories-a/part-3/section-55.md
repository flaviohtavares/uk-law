# The Over the Counter Derivatives, Central Counterparties and Trade Repositories (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provision) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/335
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Approval of interoperability arrangements[^c-ebc370f88c7d1340c81f9c0169dfb6f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 54 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1, for “competent authorities of the CCPs involved” substitute “ CCP's competent authority ”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) for “competent authorities” substitute “ competent authority ”;
- (b) omit the words from “or authorised” to “three years”.

(4) For paragraph 3 substitute—

> **3.**
>
> Where the competent authority considers that the requirements laid down in paragraph 2 are not met, it shall provide explanations in writing regarding its risk considerations to the CCPs involved.

.

(5) Omit paragraph 4.
