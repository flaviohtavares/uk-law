## 52. Reporting of information[^key-4bcff63e8a413e61d854129f8808bf8e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 50c, in paragraph 3—

- (a) for the words from the beginning to “specify” substitute “ The Bank of England, having consulted the PRA and the FCA, may make technical standards specifying ”;
- (b) omit from “EBA shall submit” to the end of the paragraph.
