# The Over the Counter Derivatives, Central Counterparties and Trade Repositories (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provision) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/335
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Public censure[^c-6b328e89c3e119a3fe005df92927c08f][^c-e14e223ae42233d4a3601a3b32d86fd7][^c-e14e223ae42233d4a3601a3b32d86fd7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the FCA considers that a trade repository has contravened a requirement imposed by or under this Part, the EMIR regulation or the TRATP Regulations, the FCA may publish a statement to that effect.
