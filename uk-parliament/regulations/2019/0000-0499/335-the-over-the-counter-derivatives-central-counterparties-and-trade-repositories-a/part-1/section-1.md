# The Over the Counter Derivatives, Central Counterparties and Trade Repositories (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provision) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2019/335
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-7d36533923d8082c01a257c6d480642d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Over the Counter Derivatives, Central Counterparties and Trade Repositories (Amendment, etc., and Transitional Provision) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force on exit day.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “**FSMA**” means the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 [^c23594661];
- “**EMIR regulation**” means Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2012 on OTC derivatives, central counterparties and trade repositories [^c23594671] as[^key-273962cfdf65465845edc392ccbcd282] it forms part of[^key-dda7ab872b7e7625f711a2c43b46c7e0] assimilated[^key-9917eb7b0ecd32727ed7e6c0b5a19396] law;
- “**FCA**” means the Financial Conduct Authority [^c23594681].
