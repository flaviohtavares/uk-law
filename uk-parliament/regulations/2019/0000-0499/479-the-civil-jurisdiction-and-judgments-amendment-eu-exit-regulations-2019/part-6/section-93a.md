# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 93A

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93A. Saving relating to jurisdiction, recognition and enforcement: application of Articles 67 and 69 of the withdrawal agreement[^M_I_f6ed0a8f-6f3b-49a6-c259-92437589d6d3][^c-406b27f917192e62bb15ecd39c9de47a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations affects the application of paragraphs 1(a) and 2(a) of Article 67 and paragraph 2 of Article 69 of the withdrawal agreement and legislation amended or revoked by these Regulations continues to have effect for the purposes of those paragraphs as if the amendments or revocations had not been made.
