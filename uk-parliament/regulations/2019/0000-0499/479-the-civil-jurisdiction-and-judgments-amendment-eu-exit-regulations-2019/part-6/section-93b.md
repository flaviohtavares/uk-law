# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 93B

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93B. Saving for requests received before IP completion day through the European Judicial Network: application of Article 68 of the withdrawal agreement[^M_I_b436e413-eb12-440b-8b6b-a534649baaee][^c-406b27f917192e62bb15ecd39c9de47a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations affects the application of paragraph (c) of Article 68 of the withdrawal agreement and Council Decision of 28 May 2001 establishing a European Judicial Network in civil and commercial matters ([2001/470/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2001/470)) continues to have effect for the purposes of that paragraph.
