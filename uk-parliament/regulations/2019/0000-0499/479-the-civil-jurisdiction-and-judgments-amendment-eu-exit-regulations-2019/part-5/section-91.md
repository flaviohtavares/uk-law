# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 91

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2015/281[^c-f757d6d735d49d37ce4a5f3e367957c0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 542/2014 of 26 November 2014 replacing Annexes I and II of Regulation (EU) No 1215/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council on jurisdiction and the recognition and enforcement of judgments in civil and commercial matters is revoked.
