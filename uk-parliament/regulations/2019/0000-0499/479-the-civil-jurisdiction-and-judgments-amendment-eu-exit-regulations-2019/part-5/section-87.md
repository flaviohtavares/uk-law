> Text as at 2024-03-01.

## 87. Council Decision of 15 October 2007 ([2007/712/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2007/712))[^c-c5a0e5367b477e188d5706a894be647b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Decision of 15 October 2007 on the signing, on behalf of the Community, of the Convention on jurisdiction and the recognition and enforcement of judgments in civil and commercial matters ([2007/712/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2007/712)) is revoked.
