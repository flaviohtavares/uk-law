# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 90

> Text as at 2024-03-01.

## 90. Regulation (EU) No 542/2014[^key-08cb973ef66b55da849c8b0255fb7e69]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation (EU) No 542/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 amending Regulation (EU) No 1215/2012 as regards the rules to be applied with respect to the Unified Patent Court and the Benelux Court of Justice is revoked.
