# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 66

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Social Security Administration Act 1992[^c-074be2720fe81f37095e4fee21d69635]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Social Security Administration Act 1992 [^c23624501] in section 108 (reduction of expenditure on income support: certain maintenance orders to be enforceable by the Secretary of State)—

- (a) after subsection (4)(a)(i), insert “ or ”;
- (b) omit subsection (4)(a)(iii) and the “or” preceding it.
