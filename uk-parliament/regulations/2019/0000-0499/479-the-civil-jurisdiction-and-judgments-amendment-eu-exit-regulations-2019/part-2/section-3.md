> Text as at 2024-03-01.

## 3. Attachment of Earnings Act 1971[^c-356c4a8c1074afbc0f6ab05e22f1423a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Attachment of Earnings Act 1971 [^c23624451], in Schedule 1 (maintenance orders to which this Act applies)—

- (a) omit paragraph 13 (orders under Part 1 of the Civil Jurisdiction and Justice Act 1982);
- (b) omit paragraph 14 (orders under Council Regulation [(EC) No 44/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2001/44)).
