## 67. Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^key-8bcc45accf8a16865f44f44cde50fbf5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 [^c23624511] in section 103 (reduction of expenditure on income support: certain maintenance orders to be enforceable by the Department)—

- (a) after subsection (4)(a)(i), insert “ or ”;
- (b) omit subsection (4)(a)(iii) and the “or” preceding it.
