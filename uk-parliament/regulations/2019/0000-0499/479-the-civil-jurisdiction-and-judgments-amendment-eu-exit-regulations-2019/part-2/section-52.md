# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 52

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52.[^c-9109f478919e428e8066083e994c6938]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 50 (interpretation: general) is amended as follows.

(2) In the definition of “the Accession Convention” “the 1982 Accession Convention”, “the 1989 Accession Convention” and “the 1996 Accession Convention”, for “, “the 1989 Accession Convention” and “the 1996 Accession Convention”” substitute “and “the 1989 Accession Convention”.

(3) At the appropriate place insert—

> “**the expert reports relating to the 1968 Convention**” means—
>
> - (a) the reports by Mr. P. Jenard on the 1968 Convention and the 1971 Protocol;
> - (b) the report by Professor Peter Schlosser on the Accession Convention;
> - (c) the report by Professor Demetrios I. Evrigenis and Professor K. D. Kerameus on the 1982 Accession Convention; and
> - (d) the report by Mr. Martinho de Almeida Cruz, Mr. Manuel Desantes Real and Mr. P. Jenard on the 1989 Accession Convention;

.

(4) Omit the definitions of—

- (a) “Article”;
- (b) “Brussels Contracting State”;
- (c) “the Brussels Conventions”;
- (d) “the Lugano Convention”;
- (e) “Regulation State”;
- (f) “State bound by the Lugano Convention”.

(5) In the definition of “tribunal”, omit paragraph (b).
