# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 25

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25.[^c-5a4758e0892cc4baae29f60808f16daf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 15 (interpretation of Part 1 and consequential amendments) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) in the definition of “judgment”, omit the words from “Article 25” to “Lugano Convention or”;
- (b) omit the definition of “maintenance order”;
- (c) omit the definition of “payer”.

(3) In subsection (2)—

- (a) omit “4, 4A,”;
- (b) omit “, 5 or 5A”.

(4) In subsection (3), omit the words from “the 1968” to “Lugano Convention or”.
