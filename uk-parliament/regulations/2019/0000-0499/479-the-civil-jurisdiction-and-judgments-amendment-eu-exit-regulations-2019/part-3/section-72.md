# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 72

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982 (Provisional and Protective Measures) (Scotland) Order 1997[^key-1e2edb56e748530a94ddd9a3001b239e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982 (Provisional and Protective Measures) (Scotland) Order 1997 [^c23624561] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2—

- (a) in paragraph (a), omit the words from “a Brussels” to “Convention or”;
- (b) in paragraph (b), for the words from “the scope” to the end substitute “ scope of the 2005 Hague Convention as determined by Articles 1 and 2 of that Convention. ”.

(3) In article 3, omit the words from “a Brussels” to “Convention or”.
