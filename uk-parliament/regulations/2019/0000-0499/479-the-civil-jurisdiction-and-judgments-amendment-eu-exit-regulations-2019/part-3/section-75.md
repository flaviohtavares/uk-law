# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 75

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Order 2001[^c-00223e68b9d6fa79f181ff76eb20f652]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Order 2001 [^c23624591] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2 (interpretation), omit the definitions of—

- (a) “the 2005 Agreement”;
- (b) “the Regulation”;
- (c) “the Regulation State”.

(3) Omit article 3 (the Regulation).

(4) Omit article 3A (the 2005 Agreement).

(5) Omit Schedule 1 (the Regulation).
