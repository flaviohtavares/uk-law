# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 77

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. The Armed Forces (Service of Process in Maintenance Proceedings) Regulations 2009[^c-de0fbd41eecd5fbae1408e3884602acc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Armed Forces (Service of Process in Maintenance Proceedings) Regulations 2009 [^c23624611], in paragraph (1), in the definition of “maintenance order”, omit “or Part 1 of the Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982 or Council Regulation [(EEC) No 44/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2001/44)”.
