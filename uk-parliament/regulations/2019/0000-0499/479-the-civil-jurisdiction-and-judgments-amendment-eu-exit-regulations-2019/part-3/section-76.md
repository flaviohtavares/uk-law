# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 76

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. The Employment Tribunals (Enforcement of Orders in Other Jurisdictions) (Scotland) Regulations 2002[^key-7ec808c6a26e696499aa3165935f3b55]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Employment Tribunals (Enforcement of Orders in Other Jurisdictions) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 [^c23624601] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation), omit the definitions of—

- (a) “Contracting State”;
- (b) “Regulation State”.

(3) Omit regulation 3 (recognition or enforcement of tribunal decisions in another Contracting State).

(4) Omit regulation 4 (recognition or enforcement of tribunal decisions in another Regulation State).
