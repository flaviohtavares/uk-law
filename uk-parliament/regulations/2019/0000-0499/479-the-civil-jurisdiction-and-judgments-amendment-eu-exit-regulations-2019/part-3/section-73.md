# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 73

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982 (Gibraltar) Order 1997[^key-209427dfeea6d3eb4904552568f7d91b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982 (Gibraltar) Order 1997 [^c23624571] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2(a), after “paragraph (b)” insert “as they had effect immediately before IP completion day[^key-a966fc7d61b56fd8b28de61645cff7e6]”.

(3) Renumber article 4 as paragraph (1) of that article, and—

- (a) in the paragraph (1)(b) so formed, for “the reports mentioned in section 3(3)” substitute “the expert reports relating to the 1968 Convention referred to in section 50”; and
- (b) after the paragraph (1) so formed, insert—

  > (2) The requirement in paragraph (1)(a) applies only in relation to principles laid down, or decisions made, by the European Court before IP completion day[^key-d33548d5a1830d5d5baba1c28f24daac].

  .
