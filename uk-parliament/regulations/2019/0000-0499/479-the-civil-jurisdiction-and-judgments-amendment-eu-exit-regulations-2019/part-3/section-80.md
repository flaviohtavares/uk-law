## 80. The International Recovery of Maintenance (Hague Convention 2007 etc.) Regulations 2012[^c-27ef3df9dee767b8c74181034f3979e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 2 to the International Recovery of Maintenance (Hague Convention 2007 etc.) Regulations 2012 [^c23624641]—

- (a) in paragraph 1 (application), omit sub-paragraphs (a) and (b); and
- (b) in paragraph 2 (interpretation), omit the definitions of “the Council Regulation” and “the Lugano Convention”.
