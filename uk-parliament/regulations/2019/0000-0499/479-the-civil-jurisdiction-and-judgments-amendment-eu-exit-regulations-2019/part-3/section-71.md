# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 71

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982 (Interim Relief) Order 1997[^c-11239bcba312bf46ccefd9c43d2efd7b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982 (Interim Relief) Order 1997 [^c23624551], in article 2—

- (a) in paragraph (a)—
  - (i) omit the words from “a Brussels” to “Lugano Convention,”;
  - (ii) omit the words after “Hague Convention State”;
- (b) in paragraph (b), for “the scope of the Regulation as determined by Article 1 of the Regulation” substitute “ scope of the 2005 Hague Convention as determined by Articles 1 and 2 of that Convention ”.
