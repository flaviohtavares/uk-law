# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 78

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. The Armed Forces (Forfeitures and Deductions) Regulations 2009[^c-a29d707a39f8c804c896ebca54a93360]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Armed Forces (Forfeitures and Deductions) Regulations 2009 [^c23624621] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation), omit the definition of “the 2000 Council Regulation”.

(3) In regulation 8 (deduction from pay – external maintenance order), in paragraph (10)(a), omit sub-paragraphs (iii) and (iv).

(4) In regulation 9 (meaning of “maintenance order”), in paragraph (2), omit sub-paragraphs (a) and (b).
