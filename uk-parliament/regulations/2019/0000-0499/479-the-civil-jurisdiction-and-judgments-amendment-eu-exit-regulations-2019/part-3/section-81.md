# The Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 81

> Text as at 2024-03-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. The International Recovery of Maintenance (Hague Convention 2007 etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012[^key-e4650fe2869d06fdbeeebc535291953a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 2 to the International Recovery of Maintenance (Hague Convention 2007 etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 [^c23624651]—

- (a) in paragraph 1 (application), omit sub-paragraphs (a), (b), (c) and (d); and
- (b) in paragraph 2 (interpretation), omit the definitions of “the 1968 Brussels Convention”, “the 1988 Lugano Convention”, “the Council Regulation” and “the Lugano Convention”.
