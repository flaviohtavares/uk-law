# The Drivers' Hours and Tachographs (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 28

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Community Drivers' Hours and Recording Equipment Regulations 2007[^c-993b46567a3183c2a02d896b48d41d9d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In regulation 3 of the Community Drivers' Hours and Recording Equipment Regulations 2007 [^c23620881], after “Regulation” insert “ and Article 2.2(k) of the European Agreement concerning the Work of Crews of Vehicles Engaged in International Road Transport (as applied by Article 2(3) of that Regulation) ”.
