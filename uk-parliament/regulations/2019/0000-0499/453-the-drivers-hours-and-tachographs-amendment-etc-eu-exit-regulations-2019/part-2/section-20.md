# The Drivers' Hours and Tachographs (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 20

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988[^key-9f7380dfed1bdf8671debad56db30ffb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In Schedule 3 to the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988 [^c23620751], after the entry relating to section 97(1) of the Transport Act 1968 insert—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Section 97ZA(1) and (2) of that Act | Using vehicle in contravention of requirements relating to installation, use or repair or recording equipment in accordance with the AETR. |
