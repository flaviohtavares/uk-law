## 27. Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Tachograph Card Fees) Regulations 2005[^c-b3354381823f7c940b1995b7aa539b13]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In regulation 3(2)(a)(i) of the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) (Tachograph Card Fees) Regulations 2005 [^c23620861], for “Commission Regulation [(EEC) No 3820/85](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1985/3820)” substitute “ Regulation [(EC) No 561/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/561) of the European Parliament and of the Council [^c23620871] ”.
