# The Drivers' Hours and Tachographs (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 47

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Road Traffic (Foreign Vehicles) Act 1972[^c-9f950ad5faf79c5d85a276a0df29a341]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 1(1)(a) of the Road Traffic (Foreign Vehicles) Act 1972 [^c23621071], for “an EU instrument” substitute “ a provision of retained direct EU legislation ”.
