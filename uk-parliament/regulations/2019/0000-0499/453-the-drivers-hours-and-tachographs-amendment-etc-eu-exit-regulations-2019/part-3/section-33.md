# The Drivers' Hours and Tachographs (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 33

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33.[^key-dad469223a5f7b5b3e2e840311ff78d2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 96 (as amended by regulation 3 of these Regulations)—

- (a) in subsection (11A) [^c23620931], for “, another member State, or a contracting third country” substitute “ or another contracting country ”;
- (b) in subsection (13) [^c23620941] omit “section 95(1) of this Act or”.
