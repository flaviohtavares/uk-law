# The Drivers' Hours and Tachographs (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 73

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73.[^key-e4bcfa945268e4c49bcd70202ea7937f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 1(1) for the words from “Regulation (EC)” to the end substitute—

> - (a) Regulation [(EC) No 561/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2006/561);
> - (b) regulations 36A to 36C and 70A of, and Schedule 3B to, the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986 [^c23621211];
> - (c) the Road Transport (Working Time) Regulations 2005;
> - (d) regulations 42, 43 and 83 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 [^c23621221];
> - (e) the Road Transport (Working Time) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005.

.
