# The Drivers' Hours and Tachographs (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 84

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84.[^c-f649bf95475d84f10bc30d2305ecbac7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 24 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1, for “Member States” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) in the first sentence—
  - (i) for “Member States” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”;
  - (ii) after “manufacturers” insert “ approved, controlled and certified under paragraph 1 ”;
- (b) in the second sentence, for “they” substitute “ the Secretary of State ”.

(4) In paragraph 3, in point (a), for “Member States” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”.

(5) In paragraph 4, for “Member States and their competent authorities” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”.

(6) Omit paragraph 5.

(7) In paragraph 6, for “The competent authorities in Member States” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”.
