# The Drivers' Hours and Tachographs (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 82

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82.[^c-c3758f01937d102e512e49f43e10264f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Article 22—

- (a) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) for “the competent authorities of the Member States” substitute “ by the Secretary of State ”;
  - (ii) at the end insert “ (but see Article 25A) ”;
- (b) in paragraph 2 omit “referred to in Article 15”;
- (c) in paragraph 3, omit the second and third sentences;
- (d) in paragraph 5, in the second indent, for the last sentence substitute “ The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision about the form of the written statement ”.
