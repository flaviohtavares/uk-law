# The Drivers' Hours and Tachographs (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 44

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44.[^c-06dfef6e349068aa26b428555619bc8d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 89 is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) in the definition of “the applicable Community rules”[^c23621031], for the words from “means” to the end substitute “ has the meaning given by section 103(1) of the Transport Act 1968 ”;
- (b) in the definition of “contracting third country”[^c23621041]—
  - (i) omit “third”;
  - (ii) omit “other than a member State”;
  - (iii) omit paragraph (b) (and the “or” before it).
