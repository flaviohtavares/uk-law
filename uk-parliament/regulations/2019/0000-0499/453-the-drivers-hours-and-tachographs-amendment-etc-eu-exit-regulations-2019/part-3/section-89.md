# The Drivers' Hours and Tachographs (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 89

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89.[^c-34bc36a2bd00f2cec6994ce9d18e3f18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 29 is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1, for “Issuing authorities” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”.

(3) In paragraph 2—

- (a) in the first sentence, for the words from “competent authority” to the end substitute “ Secretary of State ”;
- (b) in the second sentence, for the words from “competent authorities” to the end substitute “ Secretary of State ”.

(4) In paragraph 3, for the words from “competent authorities”, in the first place it occurs, to the end substitute “ Secretary of State ”.

(5) In paragraph 4—

- (a) in the first sentence, for the words from “competent authorities” to the end substitute “ Secretary of State ”;
- (b) in the second sentence—
  - (i) for “Those authorities” substitute “ The Secretary of State ”;
  - (ii) omit “their”.
