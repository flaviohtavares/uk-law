# The Drivers' Hours and Tachographs (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 53

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53.[^key-7762cd18ca024ee9818b354599a2db30]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Regulation 4 [^c23621111] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)—

- (a) omit “Pursuant to Article 3(2) of the EU Tachographs Regulation,”;
- (b) for “that Regulation” substitute “ the EU Tachographs Regulation ”.

(3) In paragraph (2)—

- (a) omit “Pursuant to Article 3(3) of the EU Tachographs Regulation,”;
- (b) for “that Regulation” substitute “ the EU Tachographs Regulation ”.
