# The Drivers' Hours and Tachographs (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 113

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

## 113. Annex XIII to the EEA agreement[^c-99701b16064f551a816ffcad72bd4ed7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Annex XIII to the EEA agreement, omit the following (which refer to Regulations which are revoked by these Regulations)—

- (a) point 21ba;
- (b) point 26b.
