# The Ecumenical Relations Measure 2018 (Commencement) Order 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 99

Ecclesiastical Law, England

The Ecumenical Relations Measure 2018 (Commencement) Order 2019

Made

23rd January 2019

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4 of the Ecumenical Relations Measure 2018[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Ecumenical Relations Measure 2018 (Commencement) Order 2019.

## 2. Commencement on 1st February 2018

Sections 1 to 3 of the Ecumenical Relations Measure 2018 come into force on 1st February 2019.

\+Justin Cantuar:

Archbishop of Canterbury

\+Setamu Ebor:

Archbishop of York

23rd January 2019

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the substantive provisions of the Ecumenical Relations Measure 2018.

Article 2 provides for sections 1 to 3 to come into force on 1st February 2019. Section 2 came into force on Royal Assent, in accordance with that section.
