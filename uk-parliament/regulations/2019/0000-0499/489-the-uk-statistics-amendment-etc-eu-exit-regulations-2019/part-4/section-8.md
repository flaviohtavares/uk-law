# The UK Statistics (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 8

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Amendment and revocation of retained direct EU legislation[^c-8f870d4c87e42b8904163da664bcb12b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The retained EU regulations and EU decisions set out in Schedule 1 are revoked.

(2) The points in Annex XXI to the EEA agreement, as it forms part of domestic law, set out in Schedule 2 are omitted.
