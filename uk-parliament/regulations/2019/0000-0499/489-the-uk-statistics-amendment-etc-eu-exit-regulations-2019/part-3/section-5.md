# The UK Statistics (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2025-02-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Amendment of the Electricity Supplier Obligations (Amendment & Excluded Electricity) Regulations 2015[^key-70bdc53d2da98293e0e311fa262d637c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 2(1) of the Electricity Supplier Obligations (Amendment & Excluded Electricity) Regulations 2015 (interpretation)[^f00007], in the definition of “NACE Rev 2”, after “Regulation [(EC) No 1893/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1893) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006 (relating to the statistical classification of economic activities)(3)” insert “as that Regulation had effect in EU law immediately before exit day”.
