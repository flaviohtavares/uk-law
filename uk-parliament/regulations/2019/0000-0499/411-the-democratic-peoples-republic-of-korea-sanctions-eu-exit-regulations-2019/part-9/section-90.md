# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 90

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Trade licences[^key-23eee3c1ecab3ee75dadb185789bec28][^key-72bd0f45ea2cf9b7d493fe1bc9964aeb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The prohibitions in Chapters 1, 2 and 3 of Part 6 (Trade) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Secretary of State under this regulation.
