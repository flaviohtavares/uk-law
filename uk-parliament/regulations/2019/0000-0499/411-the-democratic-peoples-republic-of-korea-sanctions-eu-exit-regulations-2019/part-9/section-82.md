# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 82

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Land: exceptions from prohibitions[^c-a69674f4c3809ea10d8975ef09cc995a][^c-66ec18a594ce22c24814f3f8ca984e53]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibition in regulation 57A[^c-19cea4e09b99c398e7b3b2de4ce58dd7](1)(a) (leasing or making available of land) is not contravened by the leasing or otherwise making available of land for the operation of a diplomatic mission or consular post.

(2) The prohibition in regulation 57A[^c-a83ff58131b065e7b982b66b3f3320fc](1)(c) (activities linked to the use of land) does not apply to the provision of goods or services which—

- (a) are necessary for the operation of a diplomatic mission or consular post, and
- (b) cannot be used to generate income or profit directly or indirectly for the Government of the DPRK.
