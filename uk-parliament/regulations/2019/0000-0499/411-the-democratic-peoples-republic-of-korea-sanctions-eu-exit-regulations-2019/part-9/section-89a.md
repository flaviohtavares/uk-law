# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 89A

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89A. Director disqualification licences[^key-267c00208c221f05436e00b63522168c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may issue a licence in relation to any person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of regulation 33A (director disqualification sanctions) providing that the prohibitions in—

- (a) section 11A(1) of the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986, and
- (b) Article 15A(1) of the Company Directors Disqualification (Northern Ireland) Order 2002,

do not apply to anything done under the authority of that licence.
