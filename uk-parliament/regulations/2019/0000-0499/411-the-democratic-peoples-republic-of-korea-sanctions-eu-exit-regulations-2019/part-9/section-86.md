# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 86

## 86. Ships: exceptions from prohibitions on port entry[^key-855784ed0165c06f11e1c68285f89805][^key-dea29fe7adc693d04852fdb8f5f3e306]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibition in regulation 71(1) is not contravened by providing a ship with access to a port if—

- (a) a port entry direction has been given in relation to the ship under regulation 73 (movement of ships), or
- (b) the access is needed by the ship in a case of emergency.

(2) The prohibition in regulation 71(2) is not contravened by the entry into port of a ship if—

- (a) a port entry direction has been given in relation to the ship under regulation 73, or
- (b) the entry is needed by the ship in a case of emergency.
