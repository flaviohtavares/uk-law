# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 34

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Immigration[^c-5c57fd57ca2a03a393cdb071dbe2a3bb][^c-7653c65b996922a3a3e7b82ad227ae7b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of this regulation is an excluded person for the purposes of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971 [^c23636481].
