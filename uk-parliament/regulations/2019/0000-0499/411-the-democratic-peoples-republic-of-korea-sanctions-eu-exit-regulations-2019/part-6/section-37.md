# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 37

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Supply and delivery of restricted goods[^key-12b70f8386a20f768614ae6290469adf][^key-48d0d0fd7c544d61953f3cd7336a463d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not—

- (a) directly or indirectly supply or deliver restricted goods from a third country to a place in the DPRK;
- (b) directly or indirectly supply or deliver restricted goods from a place in the DPRK to a third country.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 9 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but—

- (a) it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening paragraph (1)(a) to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the goods were destined (or ultimately destined) for the DPRK;
- (b) it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening paragraph (1)(b) to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the supply or delivery was from a place in the DPRK, whether directly or indirectly.

(4) In this regulation “**third country**” means a country that is not the United Kingdom, the Isle of Man or the DPRK.
