# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 45

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Exports of certain goods[^c-f440ce6c9b79edfed2f56541b3cdaca7][^c-474b4c579c0e6906b8b1b1e6a8e6c146]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of the following goods to, or for use in, the DPRK is prohibited—

- (a) aviation fuel;
- (b) condensates and natural gas liquids;
- (c) crude oil;
- (d) helicopters and vessels;
- (e) industrial machinery, vehicles, iron, steel and other metals;
- (f) luxury goods;
- (g) refined petroleum products.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 9 (Exceptions and licences).
