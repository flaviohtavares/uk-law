# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 46

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Supply and delivery of certain goods to the DPRK[^key-f4d4d7f6c533148545e8273b56312c32][^key-5987f519fc4c4950c379797c4dd91878]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly supply or deliver goods to which this paragraph applies from a third country to a place in the DPRK.

(2) Paragraph (1) applies to—

- (a) aviation fuel;
- (b) condensates and natural gas liquids;
- (c) crude oil;
- (d) helicopters and vessels;
- (e) industrial machinery, vehicles, iron, steel and other metals;
- (f) luxury goods;
- (g) refined petroleum products.

(3) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 9 (Exceptions and licences).

(4) A person who contravenes the prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with that offence to show that the person did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the goods were destined (or ultimately destined) for the DPRK.

(5) In this regulation “**third country**” means a country that is not the United Kingdom, the Isle of Man or the DPRK.
