# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 35

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Exports of restricted goods[^key-3a9ed238c908be279d38298df80574b9][^key-18af41bf1367bc7bc1dbc701d04263b7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of restricted goods to, or for use in, the DPRK is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 9 (Exceptions and licences).
