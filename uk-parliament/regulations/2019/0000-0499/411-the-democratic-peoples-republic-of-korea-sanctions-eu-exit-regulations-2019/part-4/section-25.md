# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 25

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Bank accounts for diplomatic missions etc.[^c-6e1a3bf73efaf767c0acb6f1217a58a8][^c-64246d4128ca4f0183aa55ba69fa4835]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A UK credit or financial institution must not open a bank account for—

- (a) a diplomatic mission or consular post of the DPRK, or
- (b) a national of the DPRK who is a member of a diplomatic mission or consular post of the DPRK.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 9 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes the prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence.
