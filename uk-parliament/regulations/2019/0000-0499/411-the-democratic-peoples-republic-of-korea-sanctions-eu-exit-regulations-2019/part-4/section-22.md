# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 22

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Acquisition or extension of ownership interest by a DPRK credit or financial institution[^c-5b9ade225c06ddbb89d5d429ad233ed2][^c-4ac2582d5ee6a4c144d28f7218a8f65f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person falling within regulation 18(2) must not directly or indirectly acquire or extend a participation, or acquire any ownership interest, in a UK credit or financial institution.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 9 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes the prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence.
