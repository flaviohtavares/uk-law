# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 21

> Text as at 2026-05-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. DPRK credit or financial institutions: branches, subsidiaries, and representative offices[^key-489c44e915001cde70984aaedd37c9af][^key-d24b8baef6b3f23e3bd147923c7146a0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person (“P”) must not operate or facilitate the operation of—

- (a) a branch,
- (b) a subsidiary, or
- (c) a representative office,

of a person falling within regulation 18(2) if P knows, or has reasonable cause to suspect, that the branch, subsidiary or representative office is a branch, subsidiary or representative office of such a person.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 9 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence.
