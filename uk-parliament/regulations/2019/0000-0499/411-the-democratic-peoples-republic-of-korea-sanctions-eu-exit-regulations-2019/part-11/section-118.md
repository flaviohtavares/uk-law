# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 118

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Monetary penalties[^c-189bbcc2ce9aa7c4c5812b70754f1c32][^c-356cd96fa8b590d502954193fef4200a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each prohibition in Part 6 (Trade) which contains a prohibition imposed for a purpose mentioned in section 3(1) or (2) of the Act is to be regarded as not being financial sanctions legislation for the purposes of Part 8 of the Policing and Crime Act 2017 [^c23636831] (monetary penalties).
