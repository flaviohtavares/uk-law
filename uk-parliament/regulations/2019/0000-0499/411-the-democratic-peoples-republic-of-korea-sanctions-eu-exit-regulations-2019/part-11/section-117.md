# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 117

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117. Application of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^key-bc0c5b11800140783baf0bf7242562af][^key-7fdb6bba0807ce3ca124b4ba428b16f8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (investigatory powers) [^c23636821] applies to an offence under—

- (a) Part 4 (Finance),
- (b) regulation 70 (dealing with UN-designated ships subject to asset-freeze), or
- (c) regulation 95 (finance: licensing offences).
