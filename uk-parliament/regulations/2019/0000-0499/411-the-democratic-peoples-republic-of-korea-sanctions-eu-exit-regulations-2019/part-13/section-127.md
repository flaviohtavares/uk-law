## 127. Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008[^key-0ac187b99dc957eda4e577ce6a21d82e][^key-7f9ca11cd57c405b503f7fd6df267d6c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008 (embargoed destinations) is not to be taken to prohibit anything prohibited by Part 6 (Trade).
