# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 130

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 130. Revocations[^key-840adb1b606814e3f37248f3216c7a0c][^key-a14aa5d25e2bb44364e71ca8926ba71f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council Regulation (EU) No 2017/1509 of 30 August 2017 concerning restrictive measures against the Democratic People's Republic of Korea and repealing Regulation [(EC) No 329/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0329) is revoked.

(2) The North Korea (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2009 [^c23636951] is revoked.

(3) The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (European Union Financial Sanctions) Regulations 2017 [^c23636961] are revoked.

(4) The Export Control (North Korea Sanctions) Order 2018 [^c23636971] is revoked.
