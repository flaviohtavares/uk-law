# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 76

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Registration etc. of ships in the DPRK[^c-621063e98aa6d74e2cf527118005441f][^c-5953f9759dbd29c6a7ef84831eadce19]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not register a ship in the DPRK.

(2) A person must not obtain authorisation for a ship to fly the flag of the DPRK.

(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) are subject to Part 9 (Exceptions and licences).

(4) A person who contravenes the prohibition in paragraph (1) or (2) commits an offence.
