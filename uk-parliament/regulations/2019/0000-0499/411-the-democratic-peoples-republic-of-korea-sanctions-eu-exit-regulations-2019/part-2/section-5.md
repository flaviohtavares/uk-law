# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^key-edb25fa7109043a05aa15c8bb3d445cb][^key-cce7d42f1274daf106ee8244ce50c29b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons, by name, in accordance with regulation 5A (conditions for the designation of persons by name)[^key-61f8a5d65640a1b57c53a640a6b8d412] for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 13 to 17 (asset-freeze etc.) and regulations 23 (closure of representative offices) and 24 (business arrangements with designated persons);
- (aa)[^key-d697a0bf7989d1d946168eef64c54412] regulation 33A (Director disqualification sanctions);
- (b) regulation 34 (immigration);
- (c) regulations 58 (leasing or chartering of ships and aircraft) and 59 (crew services for ships and aircraft);
- (d) regulations 71 (ships: prohibition on port entry) and 73 (movement of ships).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purposes of different provisions mentioned in paragraph (1).
