# The Democratic People's Republic of Korea (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 67

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Offences[^key-736ab8baa918880ce2e36add893d71f3][^key-898b6d66bd9aca5828146798679d5b20]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a prohibition in regulation 65(1) is contravened by the flight or landing of a DPRK aircraft, the operator and pilot in command of the aircraft commit an offence.

(2) It is an offence for an airport operator to fail, without reasonable excuse, to comply with a direction given by the Secretary of State under regulation 65(7), (9) or (10).

(3) It is an offence for a person to whom a direction is given under regulation 65(4) (direction by air traffic control to operator or pilot of DPRK aircraft) to fail to comply with the direction.

(4) It is an offence for a person to whom a direction is given under regulation 65(6) (direction by airport operator to operator or pilot of DPRK aircraft) to fail to comply with the direction.

(5) A person who contravenes the prohibition in regulation 66(6) (disclosure of direction) commits an offence.
