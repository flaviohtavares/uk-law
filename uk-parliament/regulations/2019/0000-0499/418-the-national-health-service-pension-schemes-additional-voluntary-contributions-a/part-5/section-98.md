# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 98

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 98. Amendment of Schedule 2

In Schedule 2 (pension sharing on divorce or nullity of marriage), in paragraph 11 (payment of lump sum on death), in sub-paragraph (2)[^f00072] for each reference to “nominated partner” ) substitute “surviving scheme partner”.

## Annotations

[^f00072]: Footnote: The expression “nominated partner” was inserted by regulation 2(1) and (16)(d) of [S.I. 2008/655](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/655).
