# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 97

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 97. Amendment of regulation 15

In regulation 15 (payments by the Secretary of State), in paragraph (3), in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b)[^f00071] for each reference to “nominated partner” substitute “surviving scheme partner”.

## Annotations

[^f00071]: Footnote: The expression “nominated partner” was inserted into sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) by regulation 2(1) and (12)(a) of [S.I. 2008/655](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/655). A further relevant amendment to sub-paragraph (b) was made by regulations 53 and 62(1) and(3)(b) of [S.I. 2015/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/96).
