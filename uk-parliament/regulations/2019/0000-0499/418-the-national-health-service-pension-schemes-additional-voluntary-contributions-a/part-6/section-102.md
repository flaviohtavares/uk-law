# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Amendment of regulation 7

In regulation 7 (surviving partner allowances), for paragraph (3)[^f00076]substitute—

> (3) Except if paragraph (3A) applies, a surviving partner is not entitled to receive an allowance—
>
> - (a) if the marriage took place or the civil partnership was formed after the later of the date on which—
>   - (i) the deceased last ceased to be employed as person to whom these Regulations apply; or
>   - (ii) the earning ability of the deceased was permanently reduced as a result of the injury or disease; or
> - (b) if the Secretary of State is not satisfied that the deceased’s partner was the deceased’s scheme partner for a continuous period of at least two years ending with the later of the dates referred to in (a).

.
