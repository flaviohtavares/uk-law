# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 105

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 105. Amendment of regulation 11

In regulation 11 (lump sum payment on death), in paragraph (1), in sub-paragraph (b), for “nominated partner”[^f00079] substitute “surviving scheme partner”.

## Annotations

[^f00079]: Footnote: The expression “nominated partner” was inserted by regulation 3(1) and (9)(b) of [S.I. 2008/655](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/655).
