# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 104

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 104. Amendment of regulation 9

In regulation 9 (dependent relative’s allowance), in paragraph (1), in sub-paragraph (a), for “nominated partner’s”[^f00078] substitute “surviving scheme partner’s”.

## Annotations

[^f00078]: Footnote: The expression “nominated partner’s” was inserted by regulation 3(1) and (8)(a) of [S.I. 2008/655](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/655).
