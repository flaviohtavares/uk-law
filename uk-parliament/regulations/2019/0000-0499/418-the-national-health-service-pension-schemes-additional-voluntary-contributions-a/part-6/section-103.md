# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 103

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 103. Amendment of regulation 8

In regulation 8 (child’s allowance), in paragraph (2), in sub-paragraph (c), for “nominated partner”[^f00077] substitute “surviving scheme partner”.

## Annotations

[^f00077]: Footnote: The expression “nominated partner” was inserted by regulation 3(1) and (7)(a) of [S.I. 2008/655](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/655).
