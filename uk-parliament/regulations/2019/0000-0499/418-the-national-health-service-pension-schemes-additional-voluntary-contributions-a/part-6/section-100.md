# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 100

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 100. Amendment of regulation 2

(1) Regulation 2 (interpretation) is amended as follows.

(2) At the appropriate place in the alphabetical order insert—

> - “scheme partner” has the meaning given in regulation 2D;

;

> - “surviving scheme partner” has the meaning given in regulation 2E;

.

(3) In the definition of “surviving partner”[^f00074] for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

## Annotations

[^f00074]: Footnote: The definition of “surviving partner” was inserted by regulation 3(1) and (2)(b) of [S.I. 2008/655](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/655).
