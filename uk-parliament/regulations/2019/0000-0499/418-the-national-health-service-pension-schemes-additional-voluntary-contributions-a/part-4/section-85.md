# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Amendment of regulation 110

(1) Regulation 110 (guaranteed minimum pension etc) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (8), in sub-paragraph (a) for “rights under section 9(2B) of the 1993 Act” substitute “the person’s section 9(2B) rights”.

(3) In paragraph (9), in sub-paragraph (b) for “his rights under section 9(2B) of the 1993 Act” substitute “the person’s section 9(2B) rights”.
