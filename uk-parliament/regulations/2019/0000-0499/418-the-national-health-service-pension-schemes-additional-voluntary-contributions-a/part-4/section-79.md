# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 79

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 79. Amendment of regulation 31

In regulation 31 (members’ contributions: practitioners and non-GP providers)—

- (a) in paragraph (3), for “of the scheme years 2015/16 to 2018/19” substitute “scheme year from 2015/16”;
- (b) in paragraph (9), for the heading to the table substitute “Table Scheme Years from 2015/16”.
