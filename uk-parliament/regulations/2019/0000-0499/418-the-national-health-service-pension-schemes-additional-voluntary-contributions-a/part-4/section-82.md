# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 82

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 82. Amendment of regulation 38

In regulation 38 (members’ contributions: supplementary: medical practitioners and non-GP providers), in paragraph (3), in sub-paragraph (a) for “NDPS is the number of days of group D service from the date M’s service commenced in the scheme year to the end of the scheme year” substitute “NDPS is the number of days of M’s group D service in the scheme year”.
