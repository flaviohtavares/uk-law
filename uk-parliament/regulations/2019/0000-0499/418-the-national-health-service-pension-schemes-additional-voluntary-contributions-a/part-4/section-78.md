# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Amendment of regulation 30

In regulation 30 (members’ contributions: employees), in paragraph (3), for—

- (a) “of the scheme years 2015/16 to 2018/19” substitute “scheme year from 2015/16”;
- (b) the heading to the table substitute “Table Scheme Years from 2015/16”.
