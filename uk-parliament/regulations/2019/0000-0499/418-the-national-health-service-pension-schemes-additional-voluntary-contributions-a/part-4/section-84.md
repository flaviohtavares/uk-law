# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 84

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 84. Amendment of regulation 41

In regulation 41 (amount of refund), in paragraph (2), in sub-paragraph (a)—

- (a) for “as is permitted pursuant to section 61” substitute “under section 55”;
- (b) after “Act”, insert “or article 3 of the 2016 Order”.
