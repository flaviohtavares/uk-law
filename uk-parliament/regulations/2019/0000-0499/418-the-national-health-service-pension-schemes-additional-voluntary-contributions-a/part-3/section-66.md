# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Amendment of regulation 3.D.18

(1) Regulation 3.D.18 (guaranteed minimum pensions etc) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (7)[^f00061], in sub-paragraph (a) for “rights under section 9(2B) of the 1993 Act” substitute “the person’s section 9(2B) rights”.

(3) In paragraph (8), in sub-paragraph (b) for “his rights under section 9(2B) of the 1993 Act” substitute “the person’s section 9(2B) rights”.

## Annotations

[^f00061]: Footnote: Paragraph (7) was amended by regulation 103 and paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 of [S.I. 2009/2446](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2446).
