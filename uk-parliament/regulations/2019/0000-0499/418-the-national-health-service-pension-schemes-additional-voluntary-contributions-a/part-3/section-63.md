# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Amendment of regulation 3.C.2

In regulation 3.C.2 (members’ contribution rate)—

- (a) in paragraph (16)[^f00058] for “NDPS is the number of days of dentist performer service from the date the dentist performer service commenced in the scheme year to the end of the scheme year” substitute “NDPS is the number of days of dentist performer service in the scheme year”;
- (b) in paragraph (22)[^f00059]—
  - (i) for sub-paragraph (b) substitute “(b) in respect of each scheme year from 2015-2016, table 2.”;
  - (ii) for the heading to table 2 substitute “Scheme Years from 2015-16”.
