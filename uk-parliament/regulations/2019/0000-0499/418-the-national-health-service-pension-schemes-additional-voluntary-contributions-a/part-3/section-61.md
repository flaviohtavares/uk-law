# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Amendment of regulation 3.A.7

In regulation 3.A.7 (meaning of “pensionable earnings”), in paragraph (2), in sub-paragraph (e)[^f00055], after “any sums paid” insert “by an employing authority or a local authority”.

## Annotations

[^f00055]: Footnote: Sub-paragraph (e) was inserted by regulations 22 and 53(1) and (5) of [S.I. 2013/413](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/413).
