# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 46. Amendment of regulation 2.C.4

In regulation 2.C.4 (contribution rate and determination of pensionable earnings for non-GP providers), in paragraph (20), for—

- (a) “in respect of the 2015-2016 to 2018-2019 scheme years, table 2” substitute “in respect of each scheme year from 2015-2016, table 2”;
- (b) the heading to table 2, substitute “Scheme Years from 2015-2016”.
