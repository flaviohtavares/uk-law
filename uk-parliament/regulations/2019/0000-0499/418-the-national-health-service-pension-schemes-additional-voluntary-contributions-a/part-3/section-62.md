# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 62

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 62. Amendment of regulation 3.A.13

(1) Regulation 3.A.13 (meaning of pensionable earnings in relation to other practitioners) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1), in sub-paragraph (d)[^f00056], after “any sums paid” insert “by an employing authority or a local authority”.

(3) In paragraph (4)[^f00057], after “2006 Act” insert “pursuant to a contract with an employing authority or a local authority”.

## Annotations

[^f00056]: Footnote: Sub-paragraph (d) was inserted by regulations 22 and 57(1) and (3) of [S.I. 2013/413](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/413).

[^f00057]: Footnote: Paragraph (4) was amended by regulations 22 and 57(1) and (5) of [S.I. 2013/413](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/413) and regulations 20 and 43(1) and (3) of [S.I. 2016/245](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/245).
