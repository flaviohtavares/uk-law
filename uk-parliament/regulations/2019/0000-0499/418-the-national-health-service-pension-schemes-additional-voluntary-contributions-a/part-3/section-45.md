# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Amendment of regulation 2.C.2

In regulation 2.C.2 (contribution rate for members other than non-GP providers), in paragraph (2)[^f00047], for—

- (a) “of the scheme years 2015-16 to 2018-2019 inclusive” substitute “scheme year from 2015-16”;
- (b) the heading to the table substitute “Scheme Years from 2015-16”.
