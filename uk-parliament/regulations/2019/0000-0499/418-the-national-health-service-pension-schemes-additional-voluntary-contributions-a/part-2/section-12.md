# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Amendment of regulation G6

In regulation G6 (member marries after leaving pensionable employment), in paragraph (4)—

- (a) for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”;
- (b) for “nominated partner’s pension” substitute “scheme partner’s pension”;
- (c) for “nominated partner pension” substitute “surviving scheme partner’s pension”.
