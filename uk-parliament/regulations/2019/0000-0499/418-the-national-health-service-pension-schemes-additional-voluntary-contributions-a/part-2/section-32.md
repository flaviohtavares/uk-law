# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Amendment of regulation Q1

(1) Regulation Q1 (right to buy additional service) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraph (5A).

(3) In paragraph (5B)[^f00030]—

- (a) for “nominated partner’s pension” substitute “scheme partner’s pension”;
- (b) for “nominated partner” substitute “surviving scheme partner”.

## Annotations

[^f00030]: Footnote: Paragraph (5B) was inserted by regulation 45(1) and (5) of [S.I. 2008/654](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/654).
