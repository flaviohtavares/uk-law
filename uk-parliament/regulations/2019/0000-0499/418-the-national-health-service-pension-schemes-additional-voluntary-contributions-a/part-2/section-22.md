# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Amendment of regulation H1

In regulation H1 (dependent child), in paragraph (1)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (c) for “nominated partner” substitute “surviving scheme partner”;
- (b) for sub-paragraph (e) substitute—

  > - (e) a child of a member’s surviving scheme partner if the Secretary of State is satisfied that the surviving scheme partner was the member’s scheme partner before the date on which the member leaves pensionable employment;

  .
