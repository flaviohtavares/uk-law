# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Amendment of regulation H7

(1) Regulation H7 (dependent child not being maintained by surviving parent or spouse or civil partner or nominated partner of a parent) is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading[^f00027] for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

(3) In paragraph (1)[^f00028] for each reference to “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

(4) In paragraph (2)[^f00029] for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.
