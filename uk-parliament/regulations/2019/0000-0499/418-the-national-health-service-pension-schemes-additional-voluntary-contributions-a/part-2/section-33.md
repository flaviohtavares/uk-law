## 33. Amendment of regulation Q5

(1) Regulation Q5 (paying for unreduced retirement lump sum by single payment) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraph (2A).

(3) In paragraph (2B)—

- (a) for each reference to “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”;
- (b) for “nominated partner’s pension” substitute “scheme partner’s pension”.
