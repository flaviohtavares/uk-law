# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Amendment of regulation H3

(1) Regulation H3 (member dies in pensionable employment)[^f00017] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (4A) for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

(3) In paragraph (4B)—

- (a) for “nominated partner’s pension” substitute “surviving scheme partner’s pension”;
- (b) for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

(4) In paragraph (4C)—

- (a) for “nominated partner’s pension” substitute “surviving scheme partner’s pension”;
- (b) for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

(5) In paragraph (4D) for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

(6) In paragraph (4E) for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

## Annotations

[^f00017]: Footnote: Paragraphs (4A) to (4E) were inserted by regulation 31(1) and (4) of [S.I. 2008/654](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/654).
