# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Amendment of regulation K1

In regulation K1 (contracting-out conditions to be overriding), in paragraph (3), for sub-paragraph (a) substitute—

> - (a) “contracting-out conditions” means—
>   - (i) the conditions specified in section 12E of the 1993 Act; and
>   - (ii) the conditions or requirements that apply in respect of a member’s section 9(2B) rights;

.
