# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Amendment of regulation G5

In regulation G5 (member dies within 12 months after leaving pensionable employment without pension or preserved pension), in paragraph (2), for “section 55(2) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993” substitute “section 55 of the 1993 Act or article 3 of the 2016 Order”.
