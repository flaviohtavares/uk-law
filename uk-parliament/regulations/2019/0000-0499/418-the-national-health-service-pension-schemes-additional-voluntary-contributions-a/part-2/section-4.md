# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Amendment of regulation D1

In regulation D1 (contributions by members), in paragraph (1A)[^f00012], for—

- (a) “of the scheme years 2015-2016 to 2018-2019 inclusive”, substitute “scheme year from 2015-2016”;
- (b) the heading to the table, substitute “Scheme Years from 2015-16”.

## Annotations

[^f00012]: Footnote: Paragraph (1A) was inserted by regulation 7(1) and (2) of [S.I. 2008/654](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/654) and amended by regulations 2 and 5(1) and (2) of [S.I. 2015/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/96).
