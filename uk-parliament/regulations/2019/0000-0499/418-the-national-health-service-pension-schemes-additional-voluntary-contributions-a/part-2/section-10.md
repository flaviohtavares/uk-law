# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Amendment of regulation F5

In regulation F5 (payment of lump sum), for paragraph (12)[^f00015] substitute—

> (12) In this regulation “surviving partner” means—
>
> - (a) a surviving scheme partner; or
> - (b) one of the following, who survives the member—
>   - (i) a widow;
>   - (ii) a widower;
>   - (iii) a civil partner.

.
