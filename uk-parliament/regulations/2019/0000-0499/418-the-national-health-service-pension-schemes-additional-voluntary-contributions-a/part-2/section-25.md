# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Amendment of regulation H5

(1) Regulation H5 (member dies with preserved pension) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (4)[^f00024] for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

(3) In paragraph (5)[^f00025] for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

(4) In paragraph (6)[^f00026]—

- (a) for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”;
- (b) for “surviving nominated partner’s pension” substitute “surviving scheme partner’s pension”.
