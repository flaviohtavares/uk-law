# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Amendment of regulation L2

In regulation L2 (refund of contributions), in paragraph (3), for “section 55(2) of the 1993 Act” substitute “section 55 of the 1993 Act or article 3 of the 2016 Order”.
