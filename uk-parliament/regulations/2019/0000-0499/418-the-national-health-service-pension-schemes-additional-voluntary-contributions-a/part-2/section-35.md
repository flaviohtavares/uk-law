# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Amendment of regulation S4

(1) Regulation S4 (benefits on death in pensionable employment after pension under regulation E2 becomes payable) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (4) for each reference to “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

(3) In paragraph (6)(b)[^f00031] for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

(4) In paragraph (10)[^f00032] for “nominated partner’s pension” substitute “scheme partner’s pension”.

(5) In paragraph (11)[^f00033] for “nominated partner’s pension” substitute “scheme partner’s pension”.

(6) In paragraph (12)(b)[^f00034] for “nominated partner pension” substitute “surviving scheme partner’s pension”.
