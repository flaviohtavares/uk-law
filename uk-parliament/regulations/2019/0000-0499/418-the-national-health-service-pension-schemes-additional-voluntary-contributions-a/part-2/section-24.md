# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Amendment of regulation H4

(1) Regulation H4 (member dies after pension becomes payable) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (3) for “nominated partner”[^f00018] substitute “scheme partner”.

(3) In paragraph (4)[^f00019] for “nominated partner” substitute “scheme partner”.

(4) In paragraph (5)—

- (a) for “nominated partner”[^f00020] substitute “scheme partner”;
- (b) for “surviving nominated partner’s pension”[^f00021] substitute “surviving scheme partner’s pension”;

(5) In paragraph (6)[^f00022]—

- (a) for “surviving nominated partner’s pension” substitute “surviving scheme partner’s pension”;
- (b) for “surviving nominated partner” substitute “surviving scheme partner”.

(6) In paragraph (7)[^f00023]—

- (a) for “surviving nominated partner’s pension” substitute “surviving scheme partner’s pension”;
- (b) for “surviving nominated partner” substitute “surviving scheme partner”.
