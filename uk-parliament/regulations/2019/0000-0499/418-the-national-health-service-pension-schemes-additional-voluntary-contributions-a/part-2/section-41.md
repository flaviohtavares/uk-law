# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Amendment of Schedule 2B

In Schedule 2B (independent providers)[^f00041], for paragraph 41 substitute—

> **41.** If an Independent Provider fails to pay an employer contribution surcharge, that provider is liable to pay the supplementary charge and interest referred to in regulation T9 and interest is to continue to accrue on the late paid surcharge for so long as it remains unpaid and further supplementary charges may be levied in respect of it.

.
