# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Amendment of regulation D3

In regulation D3 (further contributions by employing authorities in respect of excessive pay increases), after paragraph (19A) insert—

> (19B) An increase in a member’s pensionable pay pursuant to the “Framework agreement on the reform of Agenda for Change” adopted on 27 June 2018[^f00014] is to be ignored for the purposes of this regulation.

.

## Annotations

[^f00014]: Footnote: The Framework agreement covers the years from 1 April 2018 to 31 March 2021 and sets out, in Annex A, the new pay structure for those years. The Framework agreement can be found at www.nhsemployers.org/your-workforce/2018-contract-refresh/framework-agreement. Hard copies can be obtained from the NHS Pensions team, Department of Health and Social Care, Quarry House, Leeds, LS2 7UE.
