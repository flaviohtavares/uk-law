# The National Health Service Pension Schemes, Additional Voluntary Contributions and Injury Benefits (Amendment) Regulations 2019, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Amendment of regulation K7

(1) Regulation K7 (state scheme premiums) is amended as follows.

(2) For paragraph (1) substitute—

> (1) The Secretary of State may discharge the liability to provide any guaranteed minimum pension by paying a state scheme premium under section 55 of the 1993 Act or article 3 of the 2016 Order.

.

(3) In paragraph (2), for “section 55(2) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993” substitute “section 55 of the 1993 Act or article 3 of the 2016 Order”.
