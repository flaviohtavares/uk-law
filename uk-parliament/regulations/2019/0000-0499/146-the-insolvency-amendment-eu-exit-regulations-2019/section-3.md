## 3. Extent and application[^key-e6e19183e5e1b80f903e0a0ef7914744]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any provision of these Regulations amending or applying an enactment has the same extent as the enactment amended or applied, except that—

- (a)[^key-fbc0742bef7dcdd7cc05a21a9dcff5aa] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the amendments made to the Insolvency Act 1986[^key-f55e71b54fbb0214fd4ab3e03a2e8736] by Part 7 of the Schedule apply to Scotland only.
