# The Finance Act 2004 (Standard Lifetime Allowance) Regulations 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 29

Income Tax

The Finance Act 2004 (Standard Lifetime Allowance) Regulations 2019

Made

10th January 2019

The Treasury, in exercise of the power conferred by section 218(2D) of the Finance Act 2004[^f00001], make the following Regulations.

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Finance Act 2004 (Standard Lifetime Allowance) Regulations 2019.

## 2. Standard Lifetime Allowance for the tax year 2019-20

The standard lifetime allowance for the tax year 2019-20 is £1,055,000.

Jeremy Quin

Rebecca Harris

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

10th January 2019

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the regulation)

These Regulations set out the standard lifetime allowance for the tax year 2019-20, as is required by section 218(2D) of the Finance Act 2004.

A Tax Information and Impact Note has not been prepared for this instrument as it gives effect to previously announced policy and relates to a predetermined indexation formula.
