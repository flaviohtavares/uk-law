# The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 57

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Monetary penalties[^c-fb057be175bb75803a6037f29427e88e][^c-1357018f3f070cc653d2e7666ce3c773]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions are to be regarded as not being financial sanctions legislation for the purposes of Part 8 of the Policing and Crime Act 2017 [^c23619391]—

- (a) regulation 23(1) (making military goods and military technology available);
- (b) regulation 24(1) (transfer of military technology);
- (c) regulation 26(1) and (2) (financial services and funds relating to military goods and military technology);
- (d) regulation 27(1)(f) and (g) (brokering services relating to financial services and funds relating to military goods and military technology),
- (e) regulation 28(1) (enabling or facilitating the conduct of armed hostilities).
