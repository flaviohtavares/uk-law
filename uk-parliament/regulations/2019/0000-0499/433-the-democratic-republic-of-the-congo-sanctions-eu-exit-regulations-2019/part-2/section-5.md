# The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^key-cddd77cc2a66a919bcef46d2f055942b][^key-ee79b00686f509e4d65e282b2e033ca7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons by name in accordance with regulation 5A (conditions for the designation of persons by name)[^key-843733e1aa55f87d62dfb742dd02f7ef] for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 12 to 16 (asset freeze etc.); [^key-c4017a639d206f22abe90d4a2f7f1e77]...
- (aa)[^key-a6527220102fb35a23b02cdf4c6c4b78] regulation 17A (director disqualification sanctions).
- (b) regulation 18 (Immigration).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purposes of different provisions mentioned in[^key-00ccbbc028350171cfe2145150856550] paragraph (1).
