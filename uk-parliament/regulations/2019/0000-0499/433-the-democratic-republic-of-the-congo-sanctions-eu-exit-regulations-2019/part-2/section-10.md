# The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 10

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Designation of persons named by or under UN Security Council Resolutions[^key-552d46bcb488215778ca5d483a8fd605][^key-406529a8d2999a484db29888987b1525]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Each person for the time being named for the purposes of paragraph 11 of resolution 1807[^key-c4ce59cce23fdc477a1fb91e16519ab7] by the Security Council or the Committee is a designated person for the purposes of regulations 12 to 16 (asset-freeze etc.) (whose purposes include compliance with the UN obligation mentioned in regulation 4(3)(b)) [^c23619061].

(2) Nothing in this regulation affects the power under regulation 5 to designate persons (in addition to those designated by this regulation) for the purposes of regulations 12 to 16.
