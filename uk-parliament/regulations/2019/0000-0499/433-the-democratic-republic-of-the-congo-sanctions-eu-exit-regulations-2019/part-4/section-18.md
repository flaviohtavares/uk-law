# The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 18

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Immigration[^c-ea6d13a7fb5da0dcf820eea1cd0b1128][^c-b18d6ca250fb39da74773a748cbdb2a9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of this regulation is an excluded person for the purposes of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971 [^c23619071].
