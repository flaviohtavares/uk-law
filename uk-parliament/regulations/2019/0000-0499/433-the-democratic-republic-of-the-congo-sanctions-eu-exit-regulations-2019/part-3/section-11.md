# The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 11

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Meaning of “designated person” in Part 3[^key-5a5724fd9d6ff727cad868e08740cfa2][^key-cf8df9ac829cb178d944da9d3a26439c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part a “**designated person**” means —

- (a) a person who is designated under regulation 5 for the purposes of regulations 12 to 16 (asset freeze etc.), or
- (b) a person who is a designated person for the purposes of those regulations by reason of regulation 10.
