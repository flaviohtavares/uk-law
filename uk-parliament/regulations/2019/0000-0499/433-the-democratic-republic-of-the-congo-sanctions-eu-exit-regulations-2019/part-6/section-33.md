# The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 33

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Treasury licences[^key-45af8169fd735f03511f884ec36642e5][^key-54b560715f427e93b9a9be90b3c4101e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prohibitions in regulations 12 to 16 (asset-freeze etc.) do not apply to anything done under the authority of a licence issued by the Treasury under this paragraph.

(2) The Treasury may issue a licence which authorises acts by a particular person in relation to a non-UN designated person only where the Treasury consider that it is appropriate to issue the licence for a purpose set out in Part 2 or 3 of Schedule 2[^key-ad25b482f80caeab16723bc9d00f4de1].

(3) The Treasury may issue a licence which authorises acts in relation to a UN designated person only where the Treasury consider that it is appropriate to issue the licence for a purpose set out in Part 2 or 2A[^key-f0946aa0c067020e479758b57f0c61d1] of Schedule 2.

(4) In this regulation “**UN designated person**” means—

- (a) a person who is a designated person for the purposes of regulations 12 to 16 by reason of regulation 10 (designation of persons named by or under UN Security Council resolutions), or
- (b) a person who is designated under regulation 5 (power to designate persons) for the purposes of regulations 12 to 16 and whose designation is (in the opinion of the Secretary of State) required by a provision mentioned in regulation 4(3).

(5)[^key-374d2a2c1028fa49707a5a193a7fd086] The reference in paragraph (2) to a non-UN designated person is to be read as including a person who is owned or controlled directly or indirectly (within the meaning of regulation 7) by the non-UN designated person.

(6) The reference in paragraph (3) to a UN designated person is to be read as including a person who is owned or controlled directly or indirectly (within the meaning of regulation 7) by the UN designated person.
