# The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 35

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Licences: general provisions[^key-034508304144ee34f360c53d9d1c500e][^key-cf950ae443d0eed212d530712ec7edc2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences , director disqualification licences[^key-6f22c28de4d8aaa5623bc661c78be088] and trade licences.

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may —

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^key-12a29fb577253165b2800febf9dfa07e]**P**”), issues[^key-1498ad00656a8d0336c949ffcd233114] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^key-4578c77fb454eb4c0ff82c9257051718] must give written notice to P[^key-4984659c206f0692518b66823593db20] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
