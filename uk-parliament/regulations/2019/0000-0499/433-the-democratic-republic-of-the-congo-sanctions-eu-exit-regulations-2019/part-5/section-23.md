# The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 23

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Making military goods and military technology available[^key-d4458b8b5facd456833da5053772059e][^key-f5bdfddfd00711c404c4e1d2c038dd2b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly make military goods or military technology available to a non-governmental person operating in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes a prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of contravening paragraph (1) (“P”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the non-governmental person was operating in the Democratic Republic of the Congo;
