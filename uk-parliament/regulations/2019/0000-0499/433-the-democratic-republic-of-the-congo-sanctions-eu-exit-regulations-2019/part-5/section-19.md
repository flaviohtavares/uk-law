# The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 19

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Definition of “military goods” and “military technology”[^key-e99a401168a794864c0b1ef2548c2ae0][^key-1d4561957dae74d59bfec7b98729db7c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**military goods**” means—
  - (a) any thing for the time being specified in Schedule 2 to the Export Control Order 2008 [^c23619081], other than any thing which is military technology, and
  - (b) any tangible storage medium on which military technology is recorded or from which it can be derived;
- “**military technology**” means any thing for the time being specified in Schedule 2 to the Export Control Order 2008 which is described as software or technology.
