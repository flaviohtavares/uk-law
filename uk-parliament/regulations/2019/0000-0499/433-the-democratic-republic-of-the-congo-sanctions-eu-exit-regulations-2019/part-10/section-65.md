# The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 65

> Text as at 2026-05-13.

## 65. Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008[^key-11d7d3054ed3ac756e1b630937e0a164][^key-429c7583ca0025fcac3153646f561e73]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 20 of the Export Control Order 2008 (embargoed destinations) is not to be taken to prohibit anything prohibited by Part 5 (Trade).
