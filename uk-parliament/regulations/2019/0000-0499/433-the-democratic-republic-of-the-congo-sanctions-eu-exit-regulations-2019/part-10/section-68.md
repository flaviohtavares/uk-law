# The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 68

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Other revocations and amendments[^c-91a2231968d8e35cda6c6313a17c1155][^c-6aca4e42fd8cd714a578d41282430783]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Asset-Freezing) Regulations 2012 [^c23619481] are revoked.

(2) Articles 4 to 8 of the Export Control (Democratic Republic of Congo Sanctions and Miscellaneous Amendments and Revocations) Order 2015 [^c23619491] are omitted.

(3) In the Schedule to the United Nations and European Union Financial Sanctions (Linking) Regulations 2017 [^c23619501], omit the following row from the table—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | United Nations Security Council Resolution 1807 (2008) | Council Regulation [(EC) 1183/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1183) of 18th July 2005, imposing certain specific restrictive measures directed against persons acting in violation of the arms embargo with regard to the Democratic Republic of the Congo. |
