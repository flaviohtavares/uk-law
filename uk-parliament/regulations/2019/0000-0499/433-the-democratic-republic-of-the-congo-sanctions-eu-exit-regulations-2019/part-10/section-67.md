# The Democratic Republic of the Congo (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 67

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Revocation of the EU DRC Regulation[^c-ebe76fe988e02397f073962c8d649c76][^c-7bbbad6b2c543ed044c89f649e1e379c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Regulation [(EC) No 1183/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1183) of 18 July 2005, imposing certain specific restrictive measures directed against persons acting in violation of the arms embargo with regard to the Democratic Republic of the Congo, is revoked.
