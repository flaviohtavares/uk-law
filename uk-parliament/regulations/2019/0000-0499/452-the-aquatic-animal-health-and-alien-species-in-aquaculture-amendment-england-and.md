The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of, and paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 [^c23667871].

The requirements of paragraph 3(2) of Schedule 7 to that Act (relating to the appropriate Parliamentary procedure for these Regulations) have been satisfied.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-628860fc4f22116608f1e2b86aa6231a]

These Regulations may be cited as the Aquatic Animal Health and Alien Species in Aquaculture (Amendment) (England and Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force on exit day.

.

(4)[^key-2e4959e41651589841cb536f2a428671] For regulation 25 substitute—

Regulation 2(12)

[^key-2a80f1ffd6d7dcc4a7b0421b9a232419] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers in the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 (c. 16) to address deficiencies in domestic legislation (in particular under section 8(2)(a) to (d) of that Act) arising from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union.

These Regulations make amendments to subordinate legislation in relation to aquatic animal health and alien and locally absent species in aquaculture in England and Wales.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
