# The Competition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 55

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55.[^c-58304c946dad8638014bf6bcadf35255]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 130 [^c23883171], in the table—

- (a) omit the entry for “EU law”;
- (b) omit the entry for “EC Merger Regulation”;
- (c) in the entry for “Public interest consideration”, in the second column, for “Sections 42(3) and 67(9)” substitute “ Section 42(3) ”.

  > | Transferred EU merger commitments | Section 95A(6) |
