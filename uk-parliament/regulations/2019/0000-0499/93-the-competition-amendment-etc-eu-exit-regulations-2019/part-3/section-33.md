# The Competition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 33

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33.[^c-2ee1916c359f33e97745f2928c2576af]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 16(6) [^c23882971], for the definition of “infringement issue” substitute—

> “**infringement issue**” means any question relating to whether or not an infringement of the Chapter I prohibition or the Chapter II prohibition has been or is being committed;

.
