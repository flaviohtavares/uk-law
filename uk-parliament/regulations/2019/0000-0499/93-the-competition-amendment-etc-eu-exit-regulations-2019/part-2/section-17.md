# The Competition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 17

> Text as at 2024-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17.[^c-7a12e85dbd12332ecf404d664d8f17da]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 49C(11) [^c23882801], for the definition of “infringement decision” (but not the final “and”) substitute—

> “**infringement decision**” means a decision of the CMA that the Chapter I prohibition or the Chapter II prohibition has been infringed,

.
