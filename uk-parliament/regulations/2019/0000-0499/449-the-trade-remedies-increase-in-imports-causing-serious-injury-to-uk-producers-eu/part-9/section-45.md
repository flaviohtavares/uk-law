# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 45

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Interpretation for Part 9[^key-1ea384a0314c62a057d29c66239788fa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**additional rate of import duty**” means the rate of import duty applicable to goods outside of the amount of a quota;
- “EU Regulation” means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/159 of 31 January 2019 imposing definitive safeguard measures against imports of certain steel products[^key-b8c88ae27c09fefe992e70f47210cfae];
- “replacement day” means the day and time at[^key-5a05fac03abe27c86c352566612d5dba] which paragraph 1 of Schedule 7 to the Act comes into force in so far as it relates to any EU safeguard duty[^key-70500acb5fb41a8958e80826e565a117]
- “**specified**” means specified by the Secretary of State in the determination referred to in regulation 46(2).
