# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 42H

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42H. Determination to revoke[^key-00f86e4c54f9730392b505827865b475]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if the TRA determines the application to goods of a definitive safeguarding remedy should be revoked.

(2) The TRA must recommend to the Secretary of State that the application to goods of the definitive safeguarding remedy is revoked.
