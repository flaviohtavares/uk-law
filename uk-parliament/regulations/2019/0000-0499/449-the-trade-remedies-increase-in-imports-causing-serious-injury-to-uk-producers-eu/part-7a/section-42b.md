# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 42B

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42B. Suspension of a definitive safeguarding remedy[^key-00f86e4c54f9730392b505827865b475]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by public notice suspend the application to goods of a definitive safeguarding remedy for the period it is subject to an international dispute investigation.

(2) The suspension begins the day after the day on which the public notice is published.

(3)[^key-6242f2b4a9976badacc448bf318c3ba1] The suspension ends the day after the day on which—

- (a) the TRA publishes notice under—
  - (i) regulation 42F(2); or
  - (ii)[^key-606609cd3a269ec79808f78325dd3074] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the Secretary of State publishes notice under—
  - (i) paragraph 22(4)(a) of Schedule 5 to the Act; or
  - (ii) regulation 42I(4)(a).
