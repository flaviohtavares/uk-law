# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 28A

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28A. Alternative options for final affirmative determination[^key-1ae440d6e311c59d4118ef69a99b4233]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The TRA must consider giving two or more options as part of its recommendation to the Secretary of State under paragraph 16(3) of Schedule 5 to the Act in the following circumstances—

- (a) where the TRA considers that applying a definitive safeguarding amount or making relevant goods subject to a tariff rate quota in accordance with its proposed recommendation would not meet the economic interest test;
- (b) where the TRA otherwise considers that it is appropriate.
