# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 22

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Market share requirement[^c-a06e5f940c4de02f69b2757b55f13a1b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purpose of paragraph 7 of Schedule 5 to the Act, the market share requirement is met where the TRA is satisfied that UK producers' “share” of the market is—

- (a) at least 1 per cent.; or
- (b) such other higher share as the TRA considers appropriate taking into account the goods and the particular market for those goods.
