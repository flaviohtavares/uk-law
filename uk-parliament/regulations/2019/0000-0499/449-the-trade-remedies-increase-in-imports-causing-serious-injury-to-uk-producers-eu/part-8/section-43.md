# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 43

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Developing country exception[^key-711af89f10fbbf83349033650da35dc1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the TRA must not recommend the application of a provisional safeguarding remedy or definitive safeguarding remedy to goods concerned which are imports from a developing country member of the WTO accounting for 3 per cent. or less of the total imports of those goods into the United Kingdom (a “low volume exporter”).

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply in respect of any developing country member of the WTO if the imports from all low volume exporters collectively account for more than 9 per cent. of the total imports of the goods concerned into the United Kingdom.
