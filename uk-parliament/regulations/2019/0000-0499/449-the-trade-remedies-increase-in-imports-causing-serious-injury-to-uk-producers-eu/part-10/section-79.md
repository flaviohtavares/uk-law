# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 79

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79.[^key-029c9fc38a5f72612e53e5ce4cdff2a0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Paragraph 4 (notice of the Secretary of State accepting or rejecting a recommendation by the TRA regarding a provisional safeguarding remedy) of the Schedule has effect as if—

- (a) in the heading before that paragraph, for “accepting or rejecting a recommendation by the TRA”, there were substituted “ deciding to give effect, or not to give effect, to a preliminary decision ”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (d), for “recommendation”, there were substituted “ preliminary decision ”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (e), for “accepts the TRA's recommendation”, there were substituted “ decides to give effect to the preliminary decision ”; and
- (d) in sub-paragraph (f)—
  - (i) for “rejects the TRA's recommendation”, there were substituted “ decides not to give effect to the preliminary decision ”; and
  - (ii) for “rejection”, there were substituted “ decision ”.
