# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 80

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80.[^key-ec07f893d546394df0d962da1fc808c1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Paragraph 5 (notice of a final affirmative determination with no recommendation regarding a definitive safeguarding remedy) of the Schedule has effect as if—

- (a) in the heading before that paragraph, for “recommendation”, there were substituted “ preliminary decision ”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (e), for “recommendation”, there were substituted “ preliminary decision ”.
