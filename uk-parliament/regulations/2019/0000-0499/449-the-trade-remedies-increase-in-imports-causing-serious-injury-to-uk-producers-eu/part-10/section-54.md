# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 54

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Further modifications to Part 1[^c-a5444897cbdba0592c20d6058ebb1f50]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 2 (interpretation) has effect as if—

- (a) in the definition of “application”, for “paragraph 7(1)(a) of Schedule 5 to the Act”, there were substituted “ paragraph 7(1)(a)(i) of Schedule 5 to the Act ”; and
- (b) in the definition of “pace of liberalisation”, for “paragraphs 17(4)(b) or 18(5)(b)”, there were substituted “ paragraphs 17(4)(b) or 18(4)(b) ”.
