# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 53

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Unless otherwise specified in this Part, these Regulations have effect as if—

- (a) for “TRA”, in each place where this occurs (excluding regulation 1), there were substituted “ [^key-23a6f8be47e35d63173128762fc1eba7]... Secretary of State ”;
- (b) for “TRA's”, in each place where this occurs, there were substituted, “Secretary of State's”;
- (c) for “it”, where this is a reference to the TRA, in each place where it occurs, there were substituted “ the Secretary of State ” or “Secretary of State” (whichever is appropriate)[^key-957b49d76100dd4dc68c35b0d642b061] ; and
- (d) for “its”, in the following provisions, there were substituted “ the ”
  - (i) regulation 13(4)(a);
  - (ii) regulation 19(2);
  - (iii) regulation 26;
  - (iv) regulation 29(1)(a)(ii);
  - (v) regulation 30(2)(a);
  - (vi) regulation 34(2)(a);
  - (vii) regulation 35(5)(a);
  - (viii)[^key-27e765d23942dcc3288995bea94fe5ee] regulation 35A(3)(a);
  - (ix) regulation 35B(5)(a);
  - (x) regulation 42C(1);
  - (xi) paragraph 7(c) of the Schedule.
