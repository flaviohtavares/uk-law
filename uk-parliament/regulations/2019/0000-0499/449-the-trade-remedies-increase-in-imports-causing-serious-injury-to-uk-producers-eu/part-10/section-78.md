# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 78

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78.[^c-775b5326c5b5250b0cf2c979ffae6542]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Paragraph 3 (notice of a provisional affirmative determination with no recommendation regarding a provisional safeguarding remedy) of the Schedule has effect as if—

- (a) in the heading before that paragraph, for “recommendation”, there were substituted “ preliminary decision ”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (e), for “recommendation”, there were substituted “ preliminary decision ”.
