## 66. Further modifications to Part 6[^key-ed4ae739019ea6c69fec38b0a6cfb371]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 34 (mid-term review) has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(b), “the Secretary of State and” were omitted; and
- (b) in paragraph (4), for “determine”, there were substituted “ make a preliminary decision ”.
