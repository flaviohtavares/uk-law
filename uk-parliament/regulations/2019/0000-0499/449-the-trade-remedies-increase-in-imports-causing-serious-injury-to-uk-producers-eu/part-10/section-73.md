# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 73

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Further modifications to Part 9[^key-0b5a58dc7481ed2ad3c19dccc7382ec5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 48 (treatment of tariff rate quotas under this Part etc) has effect as if, in paragraph (1)—

- (a) for “the Secretary of State's acceptance of a recommendation made by the TRA”, there were substituted “ a decision by the Secretary of State to give effect to a preliminary decision made ”; and
- (b) for “acceptance”, there were substituted “ decision ”.
