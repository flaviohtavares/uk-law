# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 55

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Further modifications to Part 4[^c-a3c7d426b68f6b0991211a0300955e21]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 10 (determination and assessment of an adequate amount to prevent or remove serious injury and remedies) has effect as if—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “paragraphs 12(4), 13(5), 17(4)(a) and 18(5)(a) of Schedule 5 to the Act”, there were substituted “ paragraphs 12(4), 13(4), 17(4)(a) and 18(4)(a) of Schedule 5 to the Act ”; and
- (b) in paragraphs (4) and (5)(c), for “recommend the imposition of”, there were substituted “ make a preliminary decision to impose ”.
