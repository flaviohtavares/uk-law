# The Trade Remedies (Increase in Imports Causing Serious Injury to UK Producers) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 62

> Text as at 2024-05-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62.[^c-9aab2d4ccef6ea5c83dc5b1dd746a575]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 24 (registration of interest and the issuing of questionnaires) has effect as if, for paragraph (2)(b), there were substituted—

> - (b) UK producers, importers and overseas exporters (or associations thereof) which—
>   - (i) the applicant UK producers have identified in their application; or
>   - (ii) where no application has been made, the Secretary of State has identified as being relevant to the investigation;

.
