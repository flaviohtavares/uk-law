# The Venezuela (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 37

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Licences: general provisions[^c-520e1c58e38b7aaafd2c9e5f8fcc789b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to Treasury licences , director disqualification licences[^c-c9b653498efb28538cf07c77b5077a75] and trade licences.

(2) A licence must specify the acts authorised by it.

(3) A licence may be general or may authorise acts by a particular person or persons of a particular description.

(4) A licence may —

- (a) contain conditions;
- (b) be of indefinite duration or a defined duration.

(5) A person who issues a licence may vary, revoke or suspend it at any time.

(6) A person who , on the application of a person (“[^c-6e4c960f8be67be61d8fa09359dfb5c1]**P**”), issues[^c-ec06918abf828a9397b01c4093730870] a licence which authorises acts by a particular person , or varies, revokes or suspends that licence,[^c-d204ef72539818586d150dcb6fd8afd3] must give written notice to P[^c-77ebf80b2a21b4b984afc1437fd15d48] of the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.

(7) A person who issues, varies, revokes or suspends a general licence or a licence which authorises acts by persons of a particular description must take such steps as that person considers appropriate to publicise the issue, variation, revocation or suspension of the licence.
