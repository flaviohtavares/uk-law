# The Venezuela (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 22

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Export of restricted goods[^c-04a2002a12ba7ee900755be9b6371635]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The export of restricted goods to, or for use in, Venezuela is prohibited.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).
