# The Venezuela (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 18

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Definition of “restricted goods” and “restricted technology”[^c-6687bc2cc3277c11252692e23bf301e7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**restricted goods**” means—
  - (a) military goods,
  - (b) internal repression goods, and
  - (c) interception and monitoring goods;
- “**restricted technology**” means—
  - (a) military technology,
  - (b) internal repression technology, and
  - (c) interception and monitoring technology.
