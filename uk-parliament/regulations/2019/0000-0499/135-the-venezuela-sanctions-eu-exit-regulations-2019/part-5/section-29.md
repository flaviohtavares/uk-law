# The Venezuela (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 29

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Provision of interception and monitoring services[^key-b0235040e6edba18a08c4a59154902eb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not directly or indirectly provide interception and monitoring services to, or for the benefit of, the Government of Venezuela[^key-9004e9573edd59c07942c0cf267aa7f0].

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to Part 6 (Exceptions and licences).

(3) A person who contravenes the prohibition in paragraph (1) commits an offence, but it is a defence for a person charged with that offence (“P”) to show that P did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect that the services were provided to, or for the benefit of, the Government of Venezuela[^key-bcc901772f7f5507ad7aac500a69ebf1].
