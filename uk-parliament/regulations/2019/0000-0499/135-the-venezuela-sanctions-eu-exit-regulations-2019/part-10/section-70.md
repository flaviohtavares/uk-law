# The Venezuela (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 70

> Text as at 2026-05-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Other revocations[^c-c8edcaf5b52f9da59542608662375e4e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Venezuela (European Union Financial Sanctions) Regulations 2017 [^c23543291] are revoked.

(2) The Export Control (Venezuela Sanctions) Order 2018 [^c23543301] is revoked.
