## 69. Revocation of the Venezuela Council Regulation[^c-46f200f43d847e64e357555e8b3693cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Council Regulation (EU) No 2017/2063, concerning restrictive measures in view of the situation in Venezuela is revoked.
