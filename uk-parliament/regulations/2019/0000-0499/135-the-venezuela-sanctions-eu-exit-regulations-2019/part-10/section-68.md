# The Venezuela (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 68

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Trade: overlapping offences[^key-2fc25fbd51c62b3645f29ae48579081d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person is not to be taken to commit an offence under the Export Control Order 2008 if the person would, in the absence of this regulation, commit an offence under both—

- (a) article 34 , 37 or 38[^key-30a9698c8bf4eff1aac6160c0d09e0ca] of that Order, and
- (b) any provision of Part 5 (Trade) or regulation 39 (trade: licensing offences), 47(6) or 48(5) (offences in connection with record-keeping).
