# The Venezuela (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Power to designate persons[^key-aa076b00f35f7e8ce2ecb65bf2976995]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may designate persons by name in accordance with regulation 5A (conditions for the designation of persons by name)[^key-d2facc68e5791e10d8bceb99a9cd6c7b] for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) regulations 11 to 15 (Finance); [^key-6f02b4ab854d91e28377a93d7f6dc276]...
- (aa)[^key-f5fba9b5b46aadf4d67b9964d0a6df17] regulation 16A (Director disqualification sanctions);
- (b) regulation 17 (Immigration).

(2) The Secretary of State may designate different persons for the purposes of different provisions mentioned in[^key-21cd2b062ce6d15a2b049de4d234ce6c] paragraph (1).
