# The Venezuela (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, section 59

> Text as at 2026-05-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Monetary penalties[^c-b14af71c4c65a8773a45da65ea277b4c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions are to be regarded as not being financial sanctions legislation for the purposes of Part 8 of the Policing and Crime Act 2017 [^c23543201]—

- (a) regulation 24(1)(a) (making restricted goods and restricted technology available);
- (b) regulation 25(1)(b) (transfer of restricted technology);
- (c) regulation 27(1) and (2) (financial services and funds relating to restricted goods and restricted technology);
- (d) regulation 28(1)(f)(i) and (g) (brokering services relating to financial services and funds relating to restricted goods and restricted technology) ;[^c-43b029a8ba810d51f21b126c073cce3d]
- (e)[^c-939e38561583704224e0ee45f1f9388b] regulation 30(1) (military activities or otherwise enabling or facilitating the conduct of armed hostilities).
