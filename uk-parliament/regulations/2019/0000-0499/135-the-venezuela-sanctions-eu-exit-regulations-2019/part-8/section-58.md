> Text as at 2026-05-13.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Application of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^key-b117250a67749f5cf8f5a5c88e4149ac]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (investigatory powers) [^c23543191] applies to any offence under Part 3 (Finance) or regulation 38[^key-542fb5347cf7f2964d83f01a9fdc5dfc] (finance: licensing offences).
