# The Data Protection, Privacy and Electronic Communications (Amendments etc) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 4

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4 — Amendments consequential on provisions of the 2018 Act

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 7

### 1. Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001[^key-6e25fcf523be79be4b72897b0eeabc18]

(1) Part 1 of Schedule 4 to the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001 (extension of existing disclosure powers) [^c23720841] is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraph 42.

(3) After paragraph 53F insert—

> **53G.** Section 132(1) of the Data Protection Act 2018.

### 2. Investigatory Powers Act 2016[^key-2236f7dae3795894d820f2b65024e5f6]

In section 202(4) of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016 (restriction on use of class BDP warrants) [^c23720851], in the definition of “sensitive personal data”, for “section 2(a) to (f) of the Data Protection Act 1998” substitute “ section 86(7)(a) to (e) of the Data Protection Act 2018 ”.

### 3. Data Protection Act 2018[^key-319c14776e391815a938e028111e305c]

In Schedule 19 to the Data Protection Act 2018 (minor and consequential amendments) [^c23720861], omit paragraphs 76 and 201.
