# The Control of Trade in Endangered Species (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2019

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

2019 No. 1354

Wildlife

The Control of Trade in Endangered Species (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2019

Made

at 9.59 a.m. on 16th October 2019

Laid before Parliament

at 4.00 p.m. on 16th October 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(1)

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 [^c23898931].

The Secretary of State is designated for the purposes of that section in relation to—

- (a) the environment [^c23898941], and
- (b) wild animals [^c23898951].

## PART 1 — Introductory

### 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^c-929a9ed4439e75a45527a8c1fd0ad345]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Control of Trade in Endangered Species (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2019 and come into force 21 days after the day on which they are laid.

(2) Regulation 2 extends[^c-f7ea637e28dbce91ac85f65dc4c96c58] to Scotland only.

(3) Regulation 6 extends to England and Wales only.

## PART 2 — Amendment of primary legislation

### 2. The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981[^c-f6d798d1c1967be47c66e4fe14db2581]

*Extent: S*

In section 7(3)(c) [^c23898961] of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 [^c23898971]—

- (a) for “Control of Trade in Endangered Species (Enforcement) Regulations 1997 [^c23898981] (S.I. 1997/1372)” substitute “ Control of Trade in Endangered Species Regulations 2018 [^c23898991] (S.I. 2018/703) ”;
- (b) for “under Regulation 9 of those Regulations” substitute “ under paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 to those Regulations which consists of the conduct specified in the table in that paragraph as the subject-matter of Regulation 8(6) of those Regulations ”.

### 3. The Serious Crime Act 2007[^c-189b35e8729248f38370d0ee74c91b79]

In Schedule 1 to the Serious Crime Act 2007 [^c23899001], for each of paragraphs 13(4), 16M(4) and 29(4) substitute—

> (4) An offence under paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 1 to the Control of Trade in Endangered Species Regulations 2018 (S.I. 2018/703).
>
> (5) An offence under paragraph 2 of that Schedule which consists of the conduct specified in the table in that paragraph as the subject matter of Article 16(1)(c) or (d) of Council Regulation [(EC) No 338/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0338) on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein [^c23899011].

.

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

These Regulations update out of date references in primary and secondary legislation to the Control of Trade in Endangered Species (Enforcement) Regulations 1997 (S.I. 1997/1372) by replacing those references with references to the Control of Trade in Endangered Species Regulations 2018 (S.I. 2018/703).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
