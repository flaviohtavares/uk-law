# The Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) Regulations 2019, section 34

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Regulations about registration of civil partnership following a change of gender by either party[^key-c73ef5d839d6938dba416a1f919452a1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 3, in paragraph 11A(3)[^f00055], in the definition of “qualifying civil partnership”, for “each” substitute “one, or each,”.
