# The Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) Regulations 2019, section 29

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Grant of full gender recognition certificate after death of civil partner while section 4A application pending[^c-15922db0733233cea82d54d0f0352886]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 4B[^f00050] is amended as follows.

(2) In the heading, after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”.

(3) In subsection (1)—

- (a) in the words before paragraph (a)—
  - (i) omit “or (3)”;
  - (ii) after “spouse” insert “or civil partner”;
- (b) in paragraph (a), after “has died” insert “or under section 5A(2) in a case where a civil partner has died”.

(4) In subsection (3)—

- (a) in the definition of “new application”, after “section 5(2)” insert “or (as the case may be) section 5A(2)”;
- (b) in the definition of “required evidence”, after “section 5(4)” insert “or (as the case may be) section 5A(4)”.
