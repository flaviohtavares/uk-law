## SCHEDULE 1 — Specified overseas relationships: opposite-sex relationships[^key-83bbebaef0e0564e8a0ea48885d92f0a]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 5(6)

[^key-83bbebaef0e0564e8a0ea48885d92f0a]

> **PART 2 — Opposite-sex relationships**
