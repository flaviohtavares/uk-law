# The Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) Regulations 2019, section 16

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Parental status of civil partners in cases of assisted reproduction[^c-1c4eebbcfa096e98638dc30d04406769]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 2008 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 35[^f00029]—

- (a) in the heading, after “married to” insert “, or civil partner of,”;
- (b) in subsection (1)—
  - (i) in paragraph (a), after “marriage with a man” insert “or a civil partnership with a man”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (b) and in the words after that paragraph, after “marriage” insert “or civil partnership”.

(3) In section 38(2)—

- (a) after “common law” insert “or section A1(2) of the Legitimacy Act 1976[^f00030]”;
- (b) after “marriage” insert “or civil partnership”.

(4) In section 40[^f00031]—

- (a) in the heading, for “husband etc” substitute “male spouse, civil partner or intended parent”;
- (b) in subsection (1)—
  - (i) in paragraph (b), after “marriage with a man” insert “or a civil partnership with a man”;
  - (ii) in paragraphs (c), (d) and (e), after “marriage” insert “or civil partnership”.

(5) In section 42(1)[^f00032], after “a civil partnership” insert “with another woman”.

(6) In section 45(2)—

- (a) after “common law” insert “or section A1(2) of the Legitimacy Act 1976”;
- (b) after “marriage” insert “or civil partnership”.

(7) In section 46[^f00033]—

- (a) in the heading, for “civil partner or wife or intended female parent” substitute “female spouse, civil partner or intended parent”;
- (b) in subsection (1)(b), for “or marriage with another woman” substitute “with a woman or a marriage with a woman”.

(8) In section 48(6)(b), after “party to a” insert “marriage or”.
