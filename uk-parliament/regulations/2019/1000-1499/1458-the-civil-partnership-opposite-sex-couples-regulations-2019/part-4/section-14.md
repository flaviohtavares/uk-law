# The Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) Regulations 2019, section 14

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**14.**[^c-fa3990176b67a8308802a42ddfc341a6] (1) In section 50 of the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 2008[^f00025], omit subsection (2).

(2) The repeal of that subsection does not affect the status of any person born before the date on which these Regulations come into force who was treated as legitimate by virtue of that subsection (but is not so treated by virtue of section 1 of the Legitimacy Act 1976 as amended by these Regulations).
