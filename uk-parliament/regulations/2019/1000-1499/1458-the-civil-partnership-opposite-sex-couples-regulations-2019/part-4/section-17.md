# The Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) Regulations 2019, section 17

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Registration of birth of children of civil partners[^c-ac4eb806e9ccb564f43ea8311f0870ac]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Births and Deaths Registration Act 1953[^f00034] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 10[^f00035]—

- (a) in the heading, omit “where parents not married”, in the first place it occurs;
- (b) in subsection (1), in the words before paragraph (a), after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”;
- (c) in subsection (2), in the words before paragraph (a), after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”;
- (d) in subsection (3), after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.

(3) In section 10A[^f00036], in subsection (1), in the words before paragraph (a), after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.
