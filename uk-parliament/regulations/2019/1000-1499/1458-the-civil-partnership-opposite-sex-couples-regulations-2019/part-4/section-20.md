# The Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) Regulations 2019, section 20

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**20.**[^c-8d07a1cbfce6ca1e6a472e787398a689] In section 5 of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages Act (Special Provisions) Act 1957[^f00042]—

- (a) in subsection (1), in the words after paragraph (b) (but before the proviso), after “subsequent marriage” insert “or civil partnership”;
- (b) in subsection (2), after “marriage”, in both places it occurs, insert “or formation of the civil partnership”;
- (c) omit subsection (3)(b).
