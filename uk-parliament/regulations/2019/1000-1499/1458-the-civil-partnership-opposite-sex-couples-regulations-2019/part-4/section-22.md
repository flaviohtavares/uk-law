# The Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) Regulations 2019, section 22

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Status of male civil partners as fathers for purposes of nationality law[^key-9b73bbde4edb062d82ce1a5216bcb1ad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 50(9A)(a) of the British Nationality Act 1981[^f00044], after “husband” insert “or male civil partner”.
