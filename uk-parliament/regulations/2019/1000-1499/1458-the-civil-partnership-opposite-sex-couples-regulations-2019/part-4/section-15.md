# The Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) Regulations 2019, section 15

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Parental responsibility of civil partners[^c-fc764091318010482635f9369c51d891]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Children Act 1989[^f00026] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 2[^f00027], in subsections (1), (2) and (3), after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.

(3) In section 4, in subsection (1), in the words before paragraph (a)[^f00028], after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.

(4) In section 105, in subsection (2), after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.
