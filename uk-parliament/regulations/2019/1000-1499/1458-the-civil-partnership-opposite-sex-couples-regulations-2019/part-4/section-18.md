# The Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) Regulations 2019, section 18

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**18.**[^c-c3991bd6191e38d6d761610dc17edaa5] (1) The Registration of Births and Deaths Regulations 1987[^f00037]are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 9, in paragraph (7)[^f00038]—

- (a) omit sub-paragraph (a) (including the final “or”);
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), after “with, the” insert “father or”.

(3) In the heading of Part 4, after “married to” insert “, or civil partners of,”.

(4) In regulation 19, in paragraph (b)(ii)[^f00039]—

- (a) omit “her marriage to the father, or”;
- (b) after “with, the” insert “father or”.
