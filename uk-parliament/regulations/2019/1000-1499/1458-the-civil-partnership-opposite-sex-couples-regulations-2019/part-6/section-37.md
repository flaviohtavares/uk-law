# The Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) Regulations 2019, section 37

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Limitation of right to convert civil partnership into marriage to same-sex couples[^key-90b665de3d6dee8d0e393697e0ccbbda]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In section 9 of the Marriage (Same Sex Couples) Act 2013[^f00059], after subsection (2) insert—

> (2A) Subsections (1) and (2) apply only where both parties to the civil partnership are of the same sex.

.

(2) In regulation 2 of the Marriage of Same Sex Couples (Conversion of Civil Partnership) Regulations 2014[^f00060], in the definition of “civil partnership”, for the words from “is either” to the end substitute—

> —
>
> - (a) is between two people who are of the same sex, and
> - (b) is either an England and Wales civil partnership, or a civil partnership within section 9(3) of the 2013 Act;

.
