# The Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) Regulations 2019, section 39

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. Recognition of overseas dissolution etc. granted outside country of domicile[^c-006d2d00dc6fae27e48292e2c5d4bcad]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 237 of the Civil Partnership Act 2004[^f00066], in subsection (2)(b)(ii), for “legal relationships between two people of the same sex” substitute “relationships of the sort that the civil partnership in question is (whether generally, between two persons of the same sex, or between two persons of the opposite sex)”.
