# The Civil Partnership (Opposite-sex Couples) Regulations 2019, section 11

> Text as at 2024-09-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Religious employment of persons in same-sex or opposite-sex civil partnerships[^key-10af118f8433862729f3b2eb5a445006]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In Schedule 9 to the Equality Act 2010, in paragraph 2(4)—

- (a) in paragraph (ca)[^f00018], after “married to” insert “, or the civil partner of,”;
- (b) after paragraph (ca) insert—

  > - (cb) a requirement not to be the civil partner of a person of the opposite sex;

  .
