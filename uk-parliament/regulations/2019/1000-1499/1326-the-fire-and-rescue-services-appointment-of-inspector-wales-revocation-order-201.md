# The Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) (Wales) (Revocation) Order 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 1326

Fire And Rescue Services, Wales

The Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) (Wales) (Revocation) Order 2019

Made

8th October 2019

Coming into force

9th October 2019

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 8th day of October 2019.

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 28(1) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) (Wales) (Revocation) Order 2019 and comes into force on 9th October 2019.

## 2. Revocation of appointment

The Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) (Wales) Order 2018[^f00002] is revoked.

Ceri King

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the appointment of Andrew John Fry as an inspector under section 28(1) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 in its application to Wales.
