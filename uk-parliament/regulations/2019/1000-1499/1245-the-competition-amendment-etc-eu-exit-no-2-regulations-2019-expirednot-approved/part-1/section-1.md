# The Competition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_31998cca-85bf-46cf-c9a9-298c923a0684][^M_F_31998cca-85bf-46cf-c9a9-298c923a0684]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Competition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019 and come into force immediately before exit day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31998cca-85bf-46cf-c9a9-298c923a0684]

(2) An amendment, repeal or revocation made by these Regulations has the same extent as the provision to which it relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31998cca-85bf-46cf-c9a9-298c923a0684]

(3) In these Regulations “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31998cca-85bf-46cf-c9a9-298c923a0684]**the 2019 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31998cca-85bf-46cf-c9a9-298c923a0684]**” means the Competition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_31998cca-85bf-46cf-c9a9-298c923a0684] [^c23845301].

## Annotations

[^M_F_31998cca-85bf-46cf-c9a9-298c923a0684]: Editorial: This statutory instrument was made under a procedure whereby it ceases to have effect at the end of the period of 28 days beginning with the day on which it was made unless, during that period, the instrument is approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament. It was not approved by Parliament within that period and so has ceased to have effect.
