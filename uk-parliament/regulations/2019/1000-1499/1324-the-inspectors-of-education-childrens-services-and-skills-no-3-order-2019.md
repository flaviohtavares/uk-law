# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 1324

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2019

Made

8th October 2019

Coming into force

10th October 2019

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 8th day of October 2019

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2019 and comes into force on 10th October 2019.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Ceri King

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council
