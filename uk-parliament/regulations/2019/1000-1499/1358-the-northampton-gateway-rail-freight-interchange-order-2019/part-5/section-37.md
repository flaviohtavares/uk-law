# The Northampton Gateway Rail Freight Interchange Order 2019, section 37

> Text as at 2025-10-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. No double recovery[^key-e48f9a2d058c1e328a54c46553e3d1fa]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Compensation is not payable both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law to the extent the compensation relates to the same detriment.
