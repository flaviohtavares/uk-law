# The Northampton Gateway Rail Freight Interchange Order 2019, section 28

> Text as at 2025-10-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-3b923e5d4df18db956fc2ea57f14e987]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00044] are incorporated in this Order subject to the following modifications—

- (a) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”;
- (b) for the “undertaking” substitute “authorised development”; and
- (c) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated.
