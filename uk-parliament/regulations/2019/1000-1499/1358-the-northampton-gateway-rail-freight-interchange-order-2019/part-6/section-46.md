# The Northampton Gateway Rail Freight Interchange Order 2019, section 46

> Text as at 2025-10-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 46. Certification of plans and documents[^c-61ce5c10ee5bd6e78639097d016faae1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit to the Secretary of State copies of the plans and documents identified in Schedule 16 (certification of plans and documents) for certification that they are true copies of those plans and documents

(2) A plan or document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
