# The Children and Social Work Act 2017 (Consequential Amendments) (Social Workers) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Consequential amendments of Orders of Council made under the Health Professions Order 2001

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 2(a)

### 1. Health Professions (Parts of and Entries in the Register) Order of Council 2003[^c-6b2d44e71b105edcb241d92fc635d443]

In the Health Professions (Parts of and Entries in the Register) Order of Council 2003[^f00005]—

- (a) in article 1(2) (citation, commencement and interpretation), for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”,
- (b) in the table in Schedule 1 (parts of the register)—
  - (i) in column 1 omit “Part 16–Social Workers”,
  - (ii) in column 2 omit “Social Worker”.

### 2. Health Professions Council (Registration and Fees) Rules Order of Council 2003[^c-9c15a481f4f85615d69b57be67a2461c]

In the Schedule to the Health Professions Council (Registration and Fees) Rules Order of Council 2003[^f00006]—

- (a) in rule 2 (interpretation), in the definition of “the Order”, for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”,
- (b) in rule 2A (application to visiting health or social work professionals from relevant European States) and in the heading to that rule, omit “or social work” in both places,
- (c) in rule 11C (information required on indemnity arrangements), omit paragraph (4),
- (d) in the table in Schedule 5 (registration period)—
  - (i) in column 1 omit “Social Workers”,
  - (ii) in column 2, in the entry relating to social workers, omit “30th November in an even-numbered year”.

### 3. Health Professions Council (Screeners) Rules Order of Council 2003[^c-c6dba2eb3b64a917dcbbdeee1ee3264d]

In rule 2 (interpretation) in the Schedule to the Health Professions Council (Screeners) Rules Order of Council 2003[^f00007], in the definition of “the Order”, for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”.

### 4. Health Professions Council (Investigating Committee) Procedure Rules Order of Council 2003[^c-87530a522af51e710e27d2358b94e170]

In rule 2 (interpretation) in the Schedule to the Health Professions Council (Investigating Committee) Procedure Rules Order of Council 2003[^f00008], in the definition of “the Order”, for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”.

### 5. Health Professions Council (Conduct and Competence Committee) (Procedure) Rules Order of Council 2003[^c-2f4832ead3e644d8488cca670d13a453]

In rule 2 (interpretation) in the Schedule to the Health Professions Council (Conduct and Competence Committee) (Procedure) Rules Order of Council 2003[^f00009], in the definition of “the Order”, for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”.

### 6. Health Professions Council (Health Committee) (Procedure) Rules Order of Council 2003[^c-8e9377b81297daf2ec6069677f43a209]

In rule 2 (interpretation) in the Schedule to the Health Professions Council (Health Committee) (Procedure) Rules Order of Council 2003[^f00010], in the definition of “the Order”, for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”.

### 7. Health Professions Council (Functions of Assessors) Rules Order of Council 2003[^c-f5bd59feac00baa6e4b5634294d7b45f]

In rule 2 (interpretation) in the Schedule to the Health Professions Council (Functions of Assessors) Rules Order of Council 2003[^f00011], in the definition of “the Order”, for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”.

### 8. Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001 (Legal Assessors) Order of Council 2003[^c-00db333caf74da27e1fffd57e68de241]

In the Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001 (Legal Assessors) Order of Council 2003[^f00012]—

- (a) in the title, for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”,
- (b) in article 1 (citation, commencement and interpretation)—
  - (i) in paragraph (1), for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”,
  - (ii) in paragraph (2), in the definition of “the Order”, for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”.

### 9. Health Professions Council (Registration Appeals) Rules Order of Council 2003[^c-3d03f48c46d3265af12d43fc615de2f3]

In rule 2 (interpretation) in the Schedule to the Health Professions Council (Registration Appeals) Rules Order of Council 2003[^f00013], in the definition of “Order”, for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”.

### 10. Health and Care Professions Council (Constitution) Order 2009[^c-423d707b2eee311f5feb57d47fb56244]

In article 1(2) (interpretation) of the Health and Care Professions Council (Constitution) Order 2009[^f00014], in the definition of “the Order”, for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”.

### 11. Health Professions Council (Practice Committees and Miscellaneous Amendments) Rules Order of Council 2009[^c-8f1e7033246a68d0c5dff28ac53aa8a5]

In rule 2 (interpretation) in the Schedule to the Health Professions Council (Practice Committees and Miscellaneous Amendments) Rules Order of Council 2009[^f00015]—

- (a) in the definition of “lay member”, in paragraph (a)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (i), after “a registrant” insert “(other than a person who was registered in that part of the register which related to the social work profession in England)”,
  - (ii) omit “or” after sub-paragraph (iii),
  - (iii) omit sub-paragraph (iv),
- (b) in the definition of “the Order”, for “Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001” substitute “Health Professions Order 2001”.
