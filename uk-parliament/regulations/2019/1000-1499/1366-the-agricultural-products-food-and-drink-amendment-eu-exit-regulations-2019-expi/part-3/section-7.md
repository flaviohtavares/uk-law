# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), section 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Regulation (EU) 2019/787 of the European Parliament and of the Council (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936][^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Regulation (EU) 2019/787 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the definition, description, presentation and labelling of spirit drinks, the use of the names of spirit drinks in the presentation and labelling of other foodstuffs, the protection of geographical indications for spirit drinks, the use of ethyl alcohol and distillates of agricultural origin in alcoholic beverages is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(2) In Article 3— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

- (a) after point (7) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > (7a) ‘an Article 30(4) approval notice’ means a notice published under Article 30(4) relating to a decision of the Secretary of State to approve an application for a geographical indication; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
  >
  > (7b) ‘enters into force’, in relation to a reference to an international agreement, includes, where the provisional application of that agreement is agreed between the parties before it enters into force, the provisional application of the agreement and ‘entry into force’ is to be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
  >
  > (7c) ‘EUIA’ means an international agreement made between the European Union and a third country that provides for the protection of a geographical indication of the third country in the European Union; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  ;
- (b) in point (12) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] [^c23913531], after “and” insert “, except in the definition of ‘EUIA’ in point (7c) and in Annex 1A,”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
- (c) after point (12) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > (12a) ‘the TMA’ means the Trade Marks Act 1994; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
  >
  > (12b) ‘the Types Table’ means the table in Part 1 of Annex 1A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  .

(3) After Article 3 insert the new Article 4 in Part 1 of Schedule 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(4) In Article 36(2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

- (a) omit the words from “if” to “concerned,”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
- (b) for “Union” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] “ United Kingdom ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936];
- (c) for “Commission” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] “ Secretary of State ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936];
- (d) for the words from “under” to the end substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] “ in, or under, the TMA ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936].

(5) After Article 36 insert the new Articles 36a and 36b in Part 2 of Schedule 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(6) After Annex 1 insert the new Annex 1A in Part 3 of Schedule 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

## Annotations

[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]: Editorial: This statutory instrument was made under a procedure whereby it ceases to have effect at the end of the period of 28 days beginning with the day on which it was made unless, during that period, the instrument was approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament. It was not approved by Parliament within that period and so has ceased to have effect.
