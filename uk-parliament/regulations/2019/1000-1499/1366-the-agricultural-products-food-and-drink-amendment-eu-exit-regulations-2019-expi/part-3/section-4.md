# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), section 4

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936][^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(2) In Article 93, after paragraph 1 insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

> **1a.**
>
> For the purpose of Article 102a and Annex 8A: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
>
> - (a) ‘an Article 99 approval notice’ means a notice published under Article 99(3) relating to a decision of the Secretary of State to approve an application to register a designation of origin or geographical indication; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
> - (b) ‘enters into force’, in relation to a reference to an international agreement, includes, where the provisional application of that agreement is agreed between the parties before it enters into force, the provisional application of the agreement and ‘entry into force’ is to be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
> - (c) ‘EUIA’ means an international agreement made between the European Union and a third country that provides for the protection of a designation of origin or geographical indication of the third country in the European Union; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
> - (d) ‘EU Regulation 1308/2013’ means Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] [^c23913511] as it had effect before exit day; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
> - (e) ‘the relevant period’ means the period beginning on exit day and expiring at the end of the day that falls nine months after the day on which exit day falls; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
> - (f) ‘the Types Table’ means the table in Part 1 of Annex 8A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
> - (g) ‘the United Kingdom's PDOs and PGIs Register’ means the register established and maintained by the Secretary of State under Article 104. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
>
> **1b.**
>
> In the following provisions ‘third country’ means any country except that it does not include any part of the British Islands: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
>
> - (a) the definition of ‘EUIA’ in paragraph 1a(c); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
> - (b) Annex 8A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

.

(3) After Article 93 insert the new Article 93a in Part 1 of Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(4) In Article 95, after paragraph 1 insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

> **1a.**
>
> An application to protect a designation of origin or a geographical indication for a wine produced in the United Kingdom must be submitted to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

.

(5) In Article 99, after paragraph 2 insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

> **3.**
>
> Where the Secretary of State decides to approve an application under point (b) of the first paragraph, the Secretary of State must: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
>
> - (a) inform the applicant and any interested parties of the decision, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
> - (b) publish the decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

.

(6) After Article 102 insert the new Articles 102a and 102b in Part 2 of Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(7) After Annex 8 insert the new Annex 8A in Part 3 of Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
