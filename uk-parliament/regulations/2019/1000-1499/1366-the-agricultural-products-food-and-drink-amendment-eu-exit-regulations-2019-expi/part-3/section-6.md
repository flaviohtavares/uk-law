# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), section 6

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/33 (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936][^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/33 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards applications for protection of designations of origin, geographical indications and traditional terms in the wine sector, the objection procedure, restrictions of use, amendments to product specifications, cancellation of protection, and labelling and presentation is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(2) In Article 1a— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

- (a) after the definition relating to the Article 25 Register insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > - ‘an Article 115(2) approval notice’ means a notice published under the second subparagraph of Article 115(2) of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 relating to a decision of the Secretary of State to approve an application to protect a traditional term; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  ;
- (b) after the definition of “country” insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > ‘enters into force’, in relation to a reference to an international agreement, includes, where the provisional application of that agreement is agreed between the parties before it enters into force, the provisional application of the agreement and ‘entry into force’ is to be construed accordingly;‘EUIA’ means an international agreement made between the European Union and a third country that provides for the protection of a traditional term used in the third country in the European Union; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  ;
- (c) in the definition of “third country”, after “and” insert “, except in the definition of ‘EUIA’ in this Article and in Annex 7A,”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
- (d) after the definition of “relevant legislation” insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > ‘the relevant period’ means the period beginning on exit day and expiring at the end of the day that falls nine months after the day on which exit day falls; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
  >
  > ‘the TMA’ means the Trade Marks Act 1994; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
  >
  > ‘the Types Table’ means the table in Part A of Annex 7A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  ;

(3) After Article 1a insert the new Article 1b in Part 1 of Schedule 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(4) In Article 32(3), in the first subparagraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

- (a) omit “, where national legislation so provides,”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
- (b) for “Union” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] “ United Kingdom ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936];
- (c) for the words from “under” to the end substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] “ in, or under, the TMA ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936].

(5) After Article 32 insert the new Articles 32a and 32b in Part 2 of Schedule 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(6) After Annex 7 insert the new Annex 7A in Part 3 of Schedule 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

## Annotations
