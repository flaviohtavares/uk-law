# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), section 5

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Regulation (EU) No 251/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936][^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Regulation (EU) No 251/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the definition, description, presentation, labelling and the protection of geographical indications of aromatised wine products is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(2) In Article 2(1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

- (a) after point (3) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > (3a) ‘an Article 16 approval notice’ means a notice published under the second paragraph of Article 16 relating to a decision of the Secretary of State to grant an application to register a geographical indication; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  ;
- (b) after point (6) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > (6a) ‘enters into force’, in relation to a reference to an international agreement, includes, where the provisional application of that agreement is agreed between the parties before it enters into force, the provisional application of the agreement and ‘entry into force’ is to be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
  >
  > (6b) ‘EUIA’ means an international agreement made between the European Union and a third country that provides for the protection of a geographical indication of the third country in the European Union; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
  >
  > (6c) ‘EU Regulation 251/2014’ means Regulation (EU) No 251/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the definition, description, presentation, labelling and the protection of geographical indications of aromatised wine products *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] [^c23913521] as it had effect before exit day; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  ;
- (c) in point (8), for “means” substitute “and ‘the United Kingdom's GIs Register’ mean”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
- (d) after point (8) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > (8a) ‘the relevant period’ means the period beginning on exit day and expiring at the end of the day that falls nine months after the day on which exit day falls; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  ;
- (e) in point (9), after “and” insert “, except in the definition of ‘EUIA’ in point (6b) and in Annex 2A,”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
- (f) after point (9) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > (10) ‘the TMA’ means the Trade Marks Act 1994; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
  >
  > (11) ‘the Types Table’ means the table in Part A of Annex 2A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  .

(3) In Chapter 3 insert as the first Article in that Chapter the new Article 8a in Part 1 of Schedule 3. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(4) After Article 19 insert the new Articles 19a and 19b in Part 2 of Schedule 3. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(5) After Annex 2 insert the new Annex 2A in Part 3 of Schedule 3. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

## Annotations
