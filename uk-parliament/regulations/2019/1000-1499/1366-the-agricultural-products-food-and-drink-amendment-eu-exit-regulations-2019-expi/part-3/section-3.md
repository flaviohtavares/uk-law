# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), section 3

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936][^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council on quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(2) In Article 3— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

- (a) after point (13) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > (13a) ‘an Article 52(4) approval notice’ means a notice published under Article 52(4) relating to a decision of the Secretary of State to approve an application to register a designation of origin or geographical indication; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  ;
- (b) after point (17) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > (17a) ‘enters into force’, in relation to a reference to an international agreement, includes, where the provisional application of that agreement is agreed between the parties before it enters into force, the provisional application of the agreement and ‘entry into force’ is to be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  ;
- (c) after point (20) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > (20a) ‘EUIA’ means an international agreement made between the European Union and a third country that provides for the protection of a designation of origin or geographical indication of the third country in the European Union; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  ;
- (d) after point (21) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > (21a) ‘the relevant period’ means the period beginning on exit day and expiring at the end of the day that falls nine months after the day on which exit day falls; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  ;
- (e) in point (22), after “and” insert “, except in the definition of ‘EUIA’ in this Article and in Annex 1A,”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
- (f) after point (22) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  > (23) ‘the TMA’ means the Trade Marks Act 1994 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] [^c23913501];
  >
  > (24) ‘the Types Table’ means the table in Part 1 of Annex 1A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
  >
  > (25) ‘the United Kingdom's PDOs and PGIs Register’ means the register established and maintained by the Secretary of State under Article 11(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

  .

(3) After Article 3 insert the new Article 3a in Part 1 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(4) In Article 14(2), in the first sentence— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

- (a) omit the words from “if” to “concerned”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
- (b) for “Union” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] “ United Kingdom ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936];
- (c) for “Commission” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] “ Secretary of State ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936];
- (d) for the words from “under Council” to “2008/95/EC” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] “ in, or under, the TMA ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936].

(5) After Article 14 insert the new Articles 14a and 14b in Part 2 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

(6) After Annex 1 insert the new Annex 1A in Part 3 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

## Annotations
