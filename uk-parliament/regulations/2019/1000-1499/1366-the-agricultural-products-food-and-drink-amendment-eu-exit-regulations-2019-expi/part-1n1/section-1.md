# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), section 1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936][^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

These Regulations may be cited as the Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force on exit day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

## Annotations

[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]: Editorial: This statutory instrument was made under a procedure whereby it ceases to have effect at the end of the period of 28 days beginning with the day on which it was made unless, during that period, the instrument was approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament. It was not approved by Parliament within that period and so has ceased to have effect.
