# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), section 2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. The Trade Marks (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936][^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In the Trade Marks (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] [^c23913441], in Schedule 5, after paragraph 7(1) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]

> (1A) Paragraph 1 does not apply to an application to register a trade mark that was pending immediately before exit day referred to in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936]
>
> - (a) Article 14a of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council on quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] [^c23913451];
> - (b) Article 102a of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] [^c23913461];
> - (c) Article 19a of Regulation (EU) No 251/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the definition, description, presentation, labelling and the protection of geographical indications of aromatised wine products *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] [^c23913471];
> - (d) Article 32a of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/33 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards applications for protection of designations of origin, geographical indications and traditional terms in the wine sector, the objection procedure, restrictions of use, amendments to product specifications, cancellation of protection, and labelling and presentation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] [^c23913481];
> - (e) Article 36a of Regulation (EU) 2019/787 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the definition, description, presentation and labelling of spirit drinks, the use of the names of spirit drinks in the presentation and labelling of other foodstuffs, the protection of geographical indications for spirit drinks, the use of ethyl alcohol and distillates of agricultural origin in alcoholic beverages *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_5201fda8-a76a-487d-a184-d2bb13a42936] [^c23913491].
