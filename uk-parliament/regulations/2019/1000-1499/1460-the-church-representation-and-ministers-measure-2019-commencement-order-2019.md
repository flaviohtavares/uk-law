# The Church Representation and Ministers Measure 2019 (Commencement) Order 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 1460

Ecclesiastical Law, England

The Church Representation and Ministers Measure 2019 (Commencement) Order 2019

Made

6th November 2019

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Church Representation and Ministers Measure 2019[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Church Representation and Ministers Measure 2019 (Commencement) Order 2019.

(2) In this Order “the Measure” means the Church Representation and Ministers Measure 2019.

## 2. Commencement on 1st January 2020

The Measure so far as not already in force comes into force on 1st January 2020.

\+Justin Cantuar:

Archbishop of Canterbury

\+Sentamu Ebor:

Archbishop of York

6th November 2019

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the substantive provisions of the Church Representation and Ministers Measure 2019, including Schedule 1 which substitutes a new Schedule 3 (Church Representation Rules) in the Synodical Government Measure 1969.
