# The Drax Power (Generating Stations) Order 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2020-08-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Operation of authorised development[^c-e2200d57b2c7ff5881658aadbb6ae1ee]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker is authorised to use and operate the generating stations comprised in the authorised development.

(2) This article does not relieve the undertaker of any other requirement to obtain any permit or licence or any obligation under any legislation that may be required to authorise the operation of a generating station.
