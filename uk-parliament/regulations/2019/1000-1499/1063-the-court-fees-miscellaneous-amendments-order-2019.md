# The Court Fees (Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 1063 (L. 7)

Senior Courts Of England And Wales

County Court, England And Wales

Family Proceedings, England And Wales

Magistrates’ Courts, England And Wales

Mental Capacity, England And Wales

The Court Fees (Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2019

Made

27th June 2019

Laid before Parliament

1st July 2019

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Lord Chancellor, with the consent and sanction of the Treasury, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 92(1) and (2) of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001] (“the 2003 Act”) and sections 54(1) and (2) of the Mental Capacity Act 2005[^f00002] (“the 2005 Act”).

The Lord Chancellor has had regard to the matters referred to in section 92(3) of the 2003 Act.

In accordance with section 92(5) and (6) of the 2003 Act and section 54(3) of the 2005 Act, the Lord Chancellor has consulted the Lord Chief Justice, the Master of the Rolls, the President of the Queen’s Bench Division, the President of the Family Division, the Chancellor of the High Court, the Head of Civil Justice, the Deputy Head of Civil Justice, the Civil Justice Council, the President of the Court of Protection, the Vice-President of the Court of Protection and the Senior Judge of the Court of Protection.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Court Fees (Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2019 and comes into force on the twenty-first day after the day on which it is laid.

## 2. Amendments to the Non-Contentious Probate Fees Order 2004

(1) The Non-Contentious Probate Fees Order 2004[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In the table in Schedule 1 (fees to be taken), for the entries in columns 1 and 2 from “8 Copy documents” to the end of the entry for 8(d), substitute—

> | 8 Copy documents |  |

> | (c) where a search of the index is required, in addition to fee 8(a) or (b) as appropriate, for each period of 4 years searched after the first 4 years. | £4 |

## 3. Amendments to the Court of Protection Fees Order 2007

(1) The table in Schedule 1 (fees to be taken) to the Court of Protection Fees Order 2007[^f00004] is amended as follows.

(4) In the entry for hearing fees (article 6), for the amount in column 2 substitute “£485.00”.

## 5. Amendments to the Family Proceedings Fees Order 2008

(1) The Family Proceedings Fees Order 2008[^f00008] is amended as follows.

(2) After article 3A, insert—

> **3B.** (1) In proceedings under the Guardianship (Missing Persons) Act 2017—
>
> - (a) fee 5.1 (application in existing proceedings without notice or by consent); and
> - (b) fee 5.3 (application in existing proceedings on notice);

(3) In the entry for fee 2.1 (application to state case to High Court), for the amount in column 2 substitute “£135”.

(4) In the entry for fee 2.3 (licensing appeal), for the amount in column 2 substitute “£60”.

(5) In the entry for fee 2.4 (appeal where no other fee specified), for the amount in column 2 substitute “£60”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Non-Contentious Probate Fees Order 2004 ([S.I. 2004/3120](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3120)), the Court of Protection Fees Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/1745](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1745)), the Civil Proceedings Fees Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1053](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1053)), the Family Proceedings Fees Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1054](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1054)) and the Magistrates’ Courts Fees Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1052](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1052)).

Articles 2, 3, 4 and 6 of this Order amend Schedule 1 to each of the Non-Contentious Probate Fees Order 2004, the Court of Protection Fees Order 2007, the Civil Proceedings Fees Order 2008 and the Magistrates’ Courts Fees Order 2008 to decrease certain fees payable in non-contentious probate matters and proceedings in the Court of Protection and civil proceedings (including civil proceedings in the Magistrates’ Courts).

Articles 4 and 5 of this Order amend the Civil Proceedings Fees Order 2008 and the Family Proceedings Fees Order 2008 to provide the Public Guardian with an exemption from paying application fees in proceedings under the Guardianship (Missing Persons) Act [2017 (c. 27)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/27).

A full impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business, the voluntary sector and the public sector is available from the Ministry of Justice, 102 Petty France, London, SW1H 9AJ and is published with an Explanatory Memorandum alongside the instrument on https://legislation.gov.uk.
