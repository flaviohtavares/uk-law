# The Abergelli Power Gas Fired Generating Station Order 2019, section 19

> Text as at 2020-01-10.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 19. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^key-79488d1e146763efdfbe3c673f595589]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 are incorporated into this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
