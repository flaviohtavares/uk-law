# The Cross-border Trade (Public Notices) (EU Exit) (Revocation) Regulations 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Exiting The European Union

Customs And Excise

Value Added Tax

The Cross-border Trade (Public Notices) (EU Exit) (Revocation) Regulations 2019

Made

23rd October 2019

Laid before House of Commons

24th October 2019

Coming into force

25th October 2019

The Treasury make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 51(1) and (3) of the Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Cross-border Trade (Public Notices) (EU Exit) (Revocation) Regulations 2019 and come into force on 25th October 2019.

## 2. Revocation

The Cross-border Trade (Public Notices) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00002] are revoked.

Colin Clark

Rebecca Harris

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

23rd October 2019

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Cross-border Trade (Public Notices) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/1307](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/1307)) to remove the temporary power for the Treasury to make in a public notice, on the recommendation of the Commissioners for HMRC, such provision in relation to value added tax, any duty of customs or any excise duty as they consider appropriate in the public interest in consequence of, or otherwise in connection with, the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
