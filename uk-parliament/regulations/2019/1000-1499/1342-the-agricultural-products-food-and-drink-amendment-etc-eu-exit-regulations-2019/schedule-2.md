> **1.** Any power to make regulations under this Regulation is exercisable by: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

> - (a) the Secretary of State in relation to England; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (b) the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs in relation to Northern Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (c) the Scottish Ministers in relation to Scotland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (d) the Welsh Ministers in relation to Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **2.** But the power to make regulations may be exercised by the Secretary of State for the whole or part of the United Kingdom if consent is given by: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> - (a) the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs for regulations applying in relation to Northern Ireland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (b) the Scottish Ministers for regulations applying in relation to Scotland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (c) the Welsh Ministers for regulations applying in relation to Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **3.** Such regulations may: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> - (a) contain supplementary, incidental, consequential, transitional or saving provision (including provision amending, repealing or revoking enactments); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (b) make different provision for different purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

>
> - (a) an enactment contained in any Order in Council, order, rules, regulations or other instrument made under an Act, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

> - (c) an enactment contained in an instrument made under a Measure or Act of the National Assembly for Wales, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

>
> **2.** Except as specified in paragraph 3, a statutory instrument containing regulations made by the Secretary of State under this Regulation is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

> **3.** A statutory instrument containing regulations made by the Secretary of State under Article 26 that amend Annex 2 may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **Article 25b (affected by repeal, see note) — Regulations: Northern Ireland (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**

> **2.** Except as specified in paragraph 3, regulations made by the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs are subject to negative resolution within the meaning of section 41(6) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [^c23897881] as if they were a statutory instrument within the meaning of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
