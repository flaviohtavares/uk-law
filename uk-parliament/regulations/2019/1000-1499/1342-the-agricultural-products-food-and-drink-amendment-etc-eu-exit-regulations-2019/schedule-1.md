# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), SCHEDULE 1

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 (affected by repeal, see note) — The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015: new regulation 4A (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulation 3 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

> **4A. Transitional provision: withdrawal from the EEA and the EU (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**
>
> (1) The following waters are accredited, that is to say treated for the purposes of these Regulations as if they were natural mineral waters recognised by the Agency under regulation 4(2)(d)(i)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> - (a) established EU recognised natural mineral waters; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (b) established Icelandic recognised natural mineral waters; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (c) established Norwegian recognised natural mineral waters. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> (2) The accreditation in paragraph (1) continues to have effect in relation to a natural mineral water to which subparagraph (a), (b) or (c) of that paragraph applies unless a notification is given under paragraph (3), (4) or (5) respectively, in which case the accreditation ceases on the relevant accreditation cessation date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> (3) In the case of an established EU recognised natural mineral water, if the Department of Health is of the opinion that there is at least one established recognised UK natural mineral water that is not treated by the responsible authority in at least one member State as a recognised mineral water for the purposes of Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [2009/54/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/54)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^c23897861], the Department of Health may notify the European Commission that the accreditation provided for in paragraph (1)(a) in relation to established EU recognised natural mineral waters is to cease on a date specified in that notification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> (4) In the case of an established Icelandic recognised natural mineral water, if the Department of Health is of the opinion that there is at least one established recognised UK natural mineral water that is not treated as a recognised mineral water in Iceland for the purposes of Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [2009/54/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/54)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c], the Department of Health may notify the Icelandic Food and Veterinary Authority that the accreditation provided for in paragraph (1)(b) in relation to established Icelandic recognised natural mineral waters is to cease on a date specified in that notification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> (5) In the case of an established Norwegian recognised natural mineral water, if the Department of Health is of the opinion that there is at least one established recognised UK natural mineral water that is not treated in Norway as a recognised mineral water for the purposes of Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [2009/54/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/54)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c], the Department of Health may notify the Norwegian Food Safety Authority that the accreditation provided for in paragraph (1)(c) in relation to established Norwegian recognised natural mineral waters is to cease on a date specified in that notification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> (6) No notification may be given under paragraph (3), (4) or (5) before the end of the period of six months beginning on the day on which exit day falls. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> (7) The accreditation cessation date specified in a notification given under paragraph (3), (4) or (5) must be a date that is at least six months after the date on which the notification is given, beginning with the day after the day on which that notification is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> (8) The Department of Health must publish a copy of any notification given under paragraph (3), (4) or (5) in such manner as appears appropriate to the Department of Health in order to bring its effect to the notice of those that the Department of Health considers likely to be, or representative of those likely to be, affected in Northern Ireland as soon as is reasonably practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> (9) The Department of Health must from time to time publish, in such manner as appears appropriate to the Department of Health, a list of the names of the established EU, Icelandic and Norwegian recognised natural mineral waters that are treated as accredited natural mineral waters under paragraph (1) (“the paragraph 9 list”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> (10) Where a notification is given under paragraph (3), (4) or (5), the Department of Health must update the paragraph 9 list as soon as reasonably practicable after the accreditation cessation date specified in the notification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> (11) The paragraph 9 list is to be treated as conclusive evidence that the waters are accredited natural mineral waters for the purposes of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> (12) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> - “**accreditation cessation date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**” means the cessation date as notified by the Department of Health under paragraph (3), (4) or (5); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - “**Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [2009/54/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/54)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**” means Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [2009/54/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/54)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] as incorporated into the EEA Agreement, and as it had effect, immediately before exit day; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - “**established EU recognised natural mineral water *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>   - (a) a natural mineral water extracted from the ground in any member State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>     - (i) that immediately before exit day had the status of a recognised natural mineral water for the purposes of Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [2009/54/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/54)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>     - (ii) for which that recognition remains in force in the member State in which it was extracted from the ground; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>   - (b) a natural mineral water extracted from the ground in a third country— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

>   - (a) that immediately before exit day had the status of a recognised natural mineral water for the purposes of Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [2009/54/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/54)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
