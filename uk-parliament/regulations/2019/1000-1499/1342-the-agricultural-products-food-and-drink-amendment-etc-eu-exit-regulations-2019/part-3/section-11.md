# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), section 11

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/274 (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/274 laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the scheme of authorisations for vine plantings, certification, the inward and outward register, compulsory declarations and notifications, and of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the relevant checks *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [^c23897761] is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

(2) After Article 1 insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

> **Article 1a (affected by repeal, see note) — The competent authority (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**
>
> The competent authority, in relation to a function to be exercised in the United Kingdom, or any part of it, under this Regulation means the person designated to carry out that function by regulation 4 of the Wine Regulations 2011 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [^c23897771].

.

(3) In Article 12— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

- (a) in paragraph 2, in the first subparagraph omit “and Chapter VI of this Regulation”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
- (b) in paragraph 3 omit “of the Member States”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

(4) In Article 13(2)(b) omit the words from “presented” to the end. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

(5) In Article 15(3), in the first subparagraph omit the second sentence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

(6) Omit Article 19. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

(7) In Article 20(4)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

- (a) in the first subparagraph, for the words from “Member” to “days” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] “ on a case by case basis, the Food Standards Agency, in relation to England, Northern Ireland and Wales, and Food Standards Scotland, in relation to Scotland, may, on the application of an operator, authorise longer deadlines not exceeding 30 days where the Food Standards Agency or Food Standards Scotland (as the case may be) considers it appropriate to do so ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c];
- (b) omit the second subparagraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
