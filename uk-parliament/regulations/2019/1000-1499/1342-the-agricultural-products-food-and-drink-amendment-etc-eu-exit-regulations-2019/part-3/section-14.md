# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), section 14

## 14. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/34 (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

**Not yet in force.**

(2) In Article 7(2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

- (a) for “Member State” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] “ applicant ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c];
- (b) omit the words from “or the” to “question,”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
- (c) for “Commission” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] “ Secretary of State ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c].

(4) In Article 14— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

- (a) in the heading omit “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**”;
- (b) omit “Union”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
- (c) for the words from “reproduced” to the end substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] “ the symbol for a protected designation of origin or protected geographical indication, as relevant, established pursuant to Article 12(2) of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council on quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [^c23897811], as read with Article 12(7) of that Regulation ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c].

(5) In Article 15— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

- (a) omit paragraph 6; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
- (b) insert as paragraph 7— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

  > **7.**
  >
  > In this Article, in relation to the United Kingdom, ‘the responsible competent authorities’ means the competent authority specified in regulation 4(1) of the Wine Regulations 2011, as read with paragraph (3) of that regulation in relation to Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

  .

(6) Omit Article 16. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

(7) In Article 25, after paragraph 2 insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

> **3.**
>
> A traditional term to which paragraph 4 applies may be entered on the United Kingdom's Traditional Terms Register where the condition in paragraph 5 is satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **4.**
>
> This paragraph applies to a traditional term used in a third country that must be protected in the United Kingdom pursuant to an international agreement to which the United Kingdom and the third country are contracting parties (‘the UK-third country agreement’). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **5.**
>
> The condition is that the traditional term is a traditional term that was protected in the European Union immediately before exit day pursuant to an international agreement to which the European Union and the third country were contracting parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **6.**
>
> The same protection is to be afforded to a traditional term entered in the United Kingdom's Traditional Terms Register pursuant to paragraph 3 as a traditional term included on the register following the approval of an application submitted in compliance with this Regulation and Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/33. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **7.**
>
> In relation to the protection of a traditional term entered on the United Kingdom's Traditional Terms Register pursuant to paragraph 3, the following conditions apply to the use of the term: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> - (a) insofar as relevant, the conditions laid down in the law of the third country relating to the use of the traditional term, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (b) any conditions laid down in the UK-third country agreement relating to the use of the traditional term. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **8.**
>
> Unless the Secretary of State, when making an entry, specifies a date in the United Kingdom's Traditional Terms Register as the date the entry is to take effect, the entry is to be treated as taking effect: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> - (a) in a case where the entry is on the register as established by the Secretary of State on exit day, on exit day; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (b) in any other case, immediately the entry is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **9.**
>
> In this Article ‘the United Kingdom's Traditional Terms Register’ means the register referred to in paragraph 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
