# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), section 9

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 668/2014 (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 668/2014 laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council on quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [^c23897741] omit Article 13(1), (2) and (5) and Annex 10. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

## Annotations

[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]: Editorial: This statutory instrument was made under a procedure whereby it ceases to have effect at the end of the period of 28 days beginning with the day on which it was made unless, during that period, the instrument was approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament. It was not approved by Parliament within that period and so has ceased to have effect.

[^c23897741]: Marginal citation: It is prospectively amended on exit day by [S.I. 2019/865](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/865).
