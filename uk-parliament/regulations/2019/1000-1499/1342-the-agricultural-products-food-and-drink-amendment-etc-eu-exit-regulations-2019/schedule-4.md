### PART 1 (affected by repeal, see note) — New Chapter 4 (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

> **CHAPTER 4 (affected by repeal, see note) — GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATIONS: APPEALS (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**
>
> **Article 43 (affected by repeal, see note) — Appeals: general (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**
>
> **1.** An appeal may be made to the FTT against a decision of the Secretary of State specified in column 1 of the table in Annex 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **2.** Such an appeal may be made: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> - (a) in all cases, by a person specified in the corresponding entry in column 2 of the table in Annex 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (b) in the case of a decision affecting an application submitted by the authorities of a third country, by the authorities of that third country. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **3.** In determining such an appeal the FTT: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> - (a) must consider the decision appealed against afresh; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (b) may take into account evidence that was not available to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **4.** The FTT may: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> - (a) dismiss the appeal, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (b) if it allows the appeal, exercise any power specified in the corresponding entry in column 3 of the table in Annex 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **5.** The Secretary of State may consider a decision specified in column 1 of the table in Annex 2 afresh if evidence becomes available to the Secretary of State after making the original decision that was not available to the Secretary of State at the time of the original decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **6.** Paragraph 5 applies even though an appeal has been made in respect of the original decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **7.** Where the Secretary of State decides to consider an original decision afresh in a case where an appeal has been made to the FTT in respect of that decision: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> - (a) the Secretary of State must notify the FTT of the Secretary of State's decision to consider the original decision afresh and publish in such manner as appears appropriate to the Secretary of State from time to time a notice informing the person who made the appeal against the decision and the public of that decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (b) the appeal to the FTT is suspended until such time as the Secretary of State has made a fresh decision in relation to the matter; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (c) the Secretary of State must, following the making of the fresh decision, notify the FTT of that decision and publish in such manner as appears appropriate to the Secretary of State from time to time a notice informing the person who made the appeal against the decision and the public of that decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **8.** If the Secretary of State makes the same decision again, the appeal to the FTT restarts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **9.** If the Secretary of State makes a different decision, the appeal to the FTT ceases unless the FTT directs otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **Article 43a (affected by repeal, see note) — Appeals: applications to register geographical indications (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**
>
> **1.** Where an appeal is made to the FTT relating to a decision to register a geographical indication and the Secretary of State has made an entry in the United Kingdom's GIs Register registering the geographical indication, the entry in the register is to be maintained but is in suspense, and must be marked to indicate that it is in suspense. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **2.** The suspension provided for in paragraph 1 continues until the FTT has determined the appeal and: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> - (a) the marking relating to the suspension of the entry is removed from the register by the Secretary of State in a case where the FTT dismisses the appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (b) the entry relating to the geographical indication is removed from the register by the Secretary of State in a case where the FTT determines that the decision must be quashed and directs the Secretary of State to remove the entry relating to the geographical indication from the register; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (c) the marking relating to the suspension of the entry is removed from the register by the Secretary of State in a case where the FTT remits the matter to the Secretary of State for reconsideration and fresh decision and the Secretary of State, following such reconsideration, decides to approve the application and maintain the entry on the register relating to the geographical indication; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (d) the entry relating to the geographical indication is removed from the register by the Secretary of State in a case where the FTT remits the matter to the Secretary of State for reconsideration and fresh decision and the Secretary of State, following such reconsideration, decides to reject the application relating to the geographical indication. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **3.** Where the FTT remits a matter to the Secretary of State for reconsideration and fresh decision: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> - (a) the Secretary of State, after making a fresh decision, must publish in such manner as appears appropriate to the Secretary of State from time to time a notice informing the applicant and the public of that fresh decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (b) the provisions of this Chapter and Annex 2 apply to the fresh decision made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **4.** After making a change to the United Kingdom's GIs Register of the type specified in points (a) to (d) of paragraph 2, the Secretary of State must publish in such manner as appears appropriate to the Secretary of State from time to time a notice informing the applicant and the public of the action taken. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **5.** Where an appeal is made to the FTT relating to a decision to register a geographical indication and the Secretary of State has not made an entry in the United Kingdom's GIs Register, the Secretary of State must not make an entry in the register until the FTT has determined the appeal and any necessary consequent action or decision has been taken by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **Article 43b (affected by repeal, see note) — Appeals: applications to amend product specifications (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**
>
> **1.** Paragraph 2 applies where an appeal is made to the FTT relating to a decision of the Secretary of State to approve an application to amend a product specification for a geographical indication and the Secretary of State has not replaced the copy of the product specification attached to the entry for the geographical indication in the United Kingdom's GIs Register with the approved amended product specification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **2.** Until the FTT has determined the appeal and any necessary consequent action or decision has been taken by the Secretary of State, the copy of the product specification attached to the entry in the United Kingdom's GIs Register for the geographical indication applies without amendment but the entry in the register must be marked to indicate that an appeal relating to the amendment of the product specification is pending. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **3.** Paragraph 4 applies where an appeal is made to the FTT relating to a decision of the Secretary of State to approve an application to amend the product specification for a geographical indication and the Secretary of State has attached a copy of the amended product specification to the entry for the geographical indication in the United Kingdom's GIs Register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **4.** Until the FTT has determined the appeal and any necessary consequent action or decision has been taken by the Secretary of State, the copy of the product specification in the United Kingdom's GIs Register is to be replaced with a copy of the previous version of the product specification but the entry in the register must be marked to indicate that an appeal relating to the amendment of the product specification is pending. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **5.** Where an appeal is made to the FTT relating to a decision of the Secretary of State to reject an application to amend a product specification for a geographical indication, the existing provisions of the product specification attached to the entry for the geographical indication in the United Kingdom's GIs Register apply until the FTT has determined the appeal and any necessary consequent action or decision has been taken by the Secretary of State, but the entry for the geographical indication must be marked to indicate that an appeal relating to an application to amend the product specification is pending. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **Article 43c (affected by repeal, see note) — Appeals: applications to cancel geographical indications (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**
>
> **1.** Where an appeal is made to the FTT relating to a decision of the Secretary of State to cancel the registration of a geographical indication and the Secretary of State has not removed the entry from the United Kingdom's GIs Register, the entry in the register is to be maintained but must be marked to indicate that an appeal relating to the cancellation of the entry is pending until the FTT has determined the appeal and any necessary consequent action or decision has been taken by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **2.** Where an appeal has been made to the FTT relating to a decision of the Secretary of State to cancel the protection of a geographical indication and the Secretary of State has removed the entry in the United Kingdom's GIs Register, the entry in the register is to be restored but must be marked to indicate that an appeal relating to the cancellation of the entry is pending until the FTT has determined the appeal and any necessary consequent action or decision has been taken by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
>
> **3.** Where an appeal has been made to the FTT relating to a decision of the Secretary of State not to cancel the registration of a geographical indication, the entry in the United Kingdom's GIs Register relating to the geographical indication must be marked to indicate that an appeal relating to the cancellation of the entry is pending until the FTT has determined the appeal and any necessary consequent action or decision has been taken by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

## Annotations

[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]: Editorial: This statutory instrument was made under a procedure whereby it ceases to have effect at the end of the period of 28 days beginning with the day on which it was made unless, during that period, the instrument was approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament. It was not approved by Parliament within that period and so has ceased to have effect.
