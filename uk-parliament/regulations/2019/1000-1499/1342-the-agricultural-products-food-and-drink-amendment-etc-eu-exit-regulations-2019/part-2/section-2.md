# The Agricultural Products, Food and Drink (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (expired—not approved), section 2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. The Tribunal Procedure (First-tier Tribunal) (General Regulatory Chamber) Rules 2009 (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c][^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Tribunal Procedure (First-tier Tribunal) (General Regulatory Chamber) Rules 2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [^c23897541] are amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

(2) In rule 1(3), after the definition of “respondent” insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

> “**spirit drink decision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**” means a decision of the Secretary of State specified in column 1 of the table in Annex 2 to Regulation (EU) 2019/787 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the definition, description, presentation and labelling of spirit drinks, the use of the names of spirit drinks in the presentation and labelling of other foodstuffs, the protection of geographical indications for spirit drinks, the use of ethyl alcohol and distillates of agricultural origin in alcoholic beverages *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [^c23897551];
>
> “**traditional term wine decision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]**” means a decision of the Secretary of State specified in column 1 of the table in Annex 8 to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/33 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards applications for protection of designations of origin, geographical indications and traditional terms in the wine sector, the objection procedure, restrictions of use, amendments to product specifications, cancellation of protection, and labelling and presentation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c] [^c23897561];

.

(3) In rule 22(6), after sub-paragraph (j) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

> - (k) in an appeal against a spirit drink decision, within 28 days of the date on which the decision was published; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]
> - (l) in an appeal against a traditional term wine decision, within 28 days of the date on which the decision was published. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae02f2c0-c7f6-4c16-8c31-d069c004917c]

.
