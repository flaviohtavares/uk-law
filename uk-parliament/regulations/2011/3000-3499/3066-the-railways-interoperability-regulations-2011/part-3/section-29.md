# The Railways (Interoperability) Regulations 2011, section 29

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Notification to the European Commission of incorrect declaration[^key-980e2a15b60cbb211fdeaaf760285187]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where it appears to the Safety Authority that an interoperability constituent in relation to which an EC declaration of conformity or suitability for use has been drawn up fails to meet the essential requirements relating to it, it may[^key-356b2716b43d37b6beddfac842c694b1] give notice of that fact in writing to the European Commission and EU Member States[^key-0dd3c11a95ff236c3c4cc94d4e2432f4].

(2) That notice may[^key-e3607609159b024bb148a67b4200f4e4] specify—

- (a) whether the failure to comply was due to the inadequacy of a TSI; and
- (b) if it was not—
  - (i) the steps taken to prohibit or restrict the use of that interoperability constituent or withdraw or recall the interoperability constituent;
  - (ii) the reasons for taking those steps; and
  - (iii) any measures taken against a person who drew up the declaration.

(3)[^key-7d8ee8c264eed98b3e024411222c453c] This regulation does not apply in relation to the Channel Tunnel system.
