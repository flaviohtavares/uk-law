# The Railways (Interoperability) Regulations 2011, section 36

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. National vehicle register

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to a vehicle for which there is an authorisation under these Regulations for it to be placed in service.

(2) If the Safety Authority issues an authorisation, the person who applied for the authorisation must apply to the registration entity for it to assign a European vehicle number before the vehicle is placed in service.

(3) The registration entity must assign a unique alphanumeric identification code (a “European vehicle number”) to each vehicle for which an application is made and maintain a register of vehicles (“National Vehicle Register”).

(4)[^c-9fa6b88db8e3b12312e65c9fe08d4767] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Any person who places in service a vehicle must ensure it is marked with the European vehicle number assigned to it.

(6) The person who applied for the authorisation must, no later than 14 days after the date of authorisation, provide particulars to the registration entity to enable the registration entity to enter the information on the National Vehicle Register that is necessary to conform to the common specifications referred to in paragraph (7), and such further information as the registration entity may reasonably require.

(7) In respect of particulars provided under paragraph (6) the registration entity must ensure that the National Vehicle Register conforms to the common specifications as set out in the Annex to Commission Decision [2007/756/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0756) [^c21139511] as amended from time to time.

(8) Where there is a material change to any of the particulars provided under paragraph (6) or to any of the particulars provided under regulation 33(7) or (10) of the Railways (Interoperability) Regulations 2006 [^c21139521], the owner of the vehicle must ensure that the registration entity is given the particulars of the change and the registration entity must alter the National Vehicle Register accordingly.

(9)[^c-ffdf33713e78bfaf5d06dc1d59193e01] Where a vehicle is also registered in an EU Member State the registration entity may notify the entity responsible for the national vehicle register in that EU Member State of any relevant changes to the National Vehicle Register.

(10) The registration entity must make the National Vehicle Register available for inspection—

- (a)[^c-821ccc1d46b351aeab8919b5261fef5e] by the Safety Authority or the Rail Accident Investigation Branch;
- (b) in response to a reasonable request by—
  - (i)[^c-a370cb485c4d61c9dd24ebdd00efe968] the Office of Rail and Road, DFI, the Intergovernmental Commission, or any EU regulatory body designated in accordance with relevant EU law,
  - (ii)[^c-47e127ce1e8a032dec13945de5cd9167] the European Union Agency for Railways
  - (iii) railway undertakings;
  - (iv) infrastructure managers;[^c-1e672ced3e1226aa9eb60aed45d7efe5]...
  - (v) owners of project subsystems ; or[^c-cba577b42ccba62ad2f6b42eca63060c]
  - (vi) the national body or bodies responsible for railway safety in the country where the project subsystem is used.

(11) In Great Britain the Secretary of State and in Northern Ireland the DFI[^c-771156b83436a877e6148e1053b84c59] must designate a person, who must be independent of any railway undertaking, to be the registration entity from time to time; and different persons may be designated to maintain the register in different parts of the United Kingdom.
