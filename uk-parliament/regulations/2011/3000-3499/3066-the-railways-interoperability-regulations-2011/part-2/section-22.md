# The Railways (Interoperability) Regulations 2011, section 22

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Fees payable to the Competent Authority

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Competent Authority may charge such reasonable fee in connection with, or incidental to, carrying out its functions under regulations 13 and 14, as it may determine.
