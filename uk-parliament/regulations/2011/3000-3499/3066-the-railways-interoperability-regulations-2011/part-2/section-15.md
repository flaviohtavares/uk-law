# The Railways (Interoperability) Regulations 2011, section 15

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Essential requirements for project subsystems

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of these Regulations, the essential requirements for a project subsystem are deemed to be met if the project subsystem conforms with —

- (a) all applicable NTSNs[^key-39bb15458ea2144afb16e9d041c54d5c];
- (b) where paragraph (2) applies, the requirements of all applicable NTRs[^key-d38f6ee384ca8a66e41c830925ea3239], subject to any dispensation granted under regulation 46(1); and
- (c) where such a dispensation applies, any conditions of that dispensation.

(1A)[^key-60ae59c179009710f24faf63113ab3bb] For the purposes of paragraph (1)(a), a project subsystem is deemed to conform with an applicable NTSN, save for any UK specific case set out in that NTSN, if—

- (a) the person applying for an authorisation provides evidence that the project subsystem has been assessed under EU law as conforming with a TSI in force at the time when the application for authorisation is made; and
- (b) the applicable NTSN specifies that it substantially reproduces the provisions of that TSI, in accordance with regulation 3B(7).

(2) This paragraph applies to a project subsystem where—

- (a) there are no applicable NTSNs[^key-0f68542a71cfeb78956cdfb70b6dc886];
- (b) a relevant NTSN[^key-0df17016cdfe30be651f993772f17b13] does not govern all elements of the project subsystem;
- (c)[^key-bf8e3a3780225d0b251d620cf6bd9dd8] an exemption from conformity with the whole or part of a relevant NTSN has been granted in accordance with regulations 14 and 14A in relation to that subsystem; or
- (d) the Competent Authority has determined under regulation 13 that the whole or part of an NTSN[^key-4d6987b903a646c4f7561e5e96c8bf71] does not apply to that subsystem.
