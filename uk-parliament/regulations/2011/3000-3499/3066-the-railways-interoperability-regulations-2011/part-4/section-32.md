# The Railways (Interoperability) Regulations 2011, section 32

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Approved bodies and designated bodies: certificates etc.[^c-8795eae95f7777264fd27339f5a71d60]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where an approved body[^c-c91208d718f4e027a373d9f5b38e7994] or designated body proposes to decline to draw up a UK[^c-8134b66682eba99fea59a19ec15a43f2] certificate of verification or an ISV in relation to a project subsystem, or proposes to decline to confirm that a UK declaration[^c-cabad303a8932c2f5dcfc0542b358995] of conformity or suitability for use can be drawn up in respect of an interoperability constituent, it must—

- (a) give notice in writing to the relevant person of the reasons why it proposes to do so;
- (b) give the relevant person the opportunity to make representations in writing within a period of 28 days beginning with the day on which such notice is given; and
- (c) consider any representations made within that period by the relevant person before making its decision.

(2) An approved body or designated body must not draw up a UK[^c-af0b891e3b93f82d2d6e6a4971ea40a7] certificate of verification unless it is drawn up in accordance with the applicable part of Schedule 4[^c-58b33c1b2be0c577a98e98c2c391af18].

(3) An approved body must not confirm that a UK declaration[^c-8eeef19c288c86ae22fa7067918e7e87] of conformity or suitability for use can be drawn up in respect of an interoperability constituent unless satisfied that that constituent conforms to such of the NTSNs[^c-ceccde86fd0ca0a2713dc51f7aa5d69d] as are required by regulation 25.

(3A)[^c-575ced7b6324963472229cbfcea9f7f0] A designated body must not confirm that a UK declaration of conformity or suitability for use can be drawn up in respect of an interoperability constituent unless satisfied that the constituent conforms to such of the UK specific cases as are required by regulation 25.

(4)[^c-0e6e24106a81379f38a94fa3d234bfc9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) The “**relevant person**” means the person who engaged the approved body[^c-47f52a8d091cae00e28ce31e5dcf91ba] or designated body for the relevant matter.
