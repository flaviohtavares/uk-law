# The Railways (Interoperability) Regulations 2011, section 34

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Fees of the Secretary of State

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may charge such reasonable fee in connection with, or incidental to, carrying out the Secretary of State's functions under regulation 31 as the Secretary of State may determine.
