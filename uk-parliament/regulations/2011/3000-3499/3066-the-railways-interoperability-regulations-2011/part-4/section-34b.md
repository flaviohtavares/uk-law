# The Railways (Interoperability) Regulations 2011, section 34B

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34B. UK national accreditation body[^key-015e83e9fff8831acd44f6d390e78b40]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may authorise the UK national accreditation body to carry out the following activities on behalf of the Secretary of State—

- (a) assessing whether a body meets the approved body or designated body requirements;
- (b) exercising functions in accordance with regulation 31;
- (c) compiling and maintaining the register of approved bodies in accordance with regulation 34A.

(2) In this regulation—

- “RAMS” means Regulation [(EC) No 765/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/0765) of the European Parliament and of the Council setting out the requirements for accreditation and market surveillance relating to the marketing of products and repealing Regulation [(EEC) No 339/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1993/0339);
- “UK national accreditation body” means the body appointed by the Secretary of State in accordance with Article 4 of RAMS.
