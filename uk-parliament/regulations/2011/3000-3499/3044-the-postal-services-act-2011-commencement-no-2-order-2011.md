# The Postal Services Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 3044 (C. 115)

Postal Services

The Postal Services Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2011

Made

19th December 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 93(3) of the Postal Services Act 2011[^f00001].

In accordance with section 6(4) of that Act Post Office Limited was designated for the purposes of section 6(1) of that Act by The Post Office Limited (Designation) Order 2011[^f00002]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Postal Services Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2011.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 20th December 2011

Section 1 of the Postal Services Act 2011 shall come into force on 20th December 2011.

Edward Davey

Minister for Employment, Consumer and Postal Affairs

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

19th December 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force section 1 of the Postal Services Act 2011 on 20th December 2011.
