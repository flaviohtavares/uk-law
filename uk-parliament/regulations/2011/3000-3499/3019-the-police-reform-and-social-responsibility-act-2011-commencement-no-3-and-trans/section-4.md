# The Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2011, section 4

> Text as at 2012-11-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Transitional provisions in relation to the accounts of the Metropolitan Police Authority[^c-f1ce3b447eb54748fa6fc20f21e992c6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 7 and 8 of the Accounts and Audit (England) Regulations 2011[^f00003] shall not apply to the Metropolitan Police Authority in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2011.
