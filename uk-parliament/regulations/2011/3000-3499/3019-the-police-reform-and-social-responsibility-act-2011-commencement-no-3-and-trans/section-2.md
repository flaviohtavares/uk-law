# The Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2011, section 2

> Text as at 2012-11-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 15th December 2011[^c-654f6f2724d5cc538735056426a91563]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the 2011 Act is 15th December 2011—

- (a) section 11(1) and (2);
- (b) section 17(4) to (7), to the extent that those provisions allow the Secretary of State to make a financial code of practice, to revise the code and to lay the code or revision of the code before Parliament;
- (c) section 82(1) and (12).
