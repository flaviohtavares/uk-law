# The Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2011, section 1

> Text as at 2012-11-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and interpretation[^key-65d6322b9466596aa41d0787921625ab]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2011.

(2) In this Order—

- (a) “the 1996 Act” means the Police Act 1996[^f00002];
- (b) “the 2011 Act” means the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011.
