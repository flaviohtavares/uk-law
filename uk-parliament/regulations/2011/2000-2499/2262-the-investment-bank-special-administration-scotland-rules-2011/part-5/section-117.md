# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 117

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 117. Costs of making a claim

Unless the court orders otherwise, every claimant under rule 115 or 116 bears the cost of making a claim, including expenses incurred in providing documents or evidence or responding to requests for further information.
