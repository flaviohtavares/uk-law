# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 62

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 62. Suspension

Once only in the course of a meeting, the chair may, without an adjournment, declare it suspended for any period up to 1 hour.
