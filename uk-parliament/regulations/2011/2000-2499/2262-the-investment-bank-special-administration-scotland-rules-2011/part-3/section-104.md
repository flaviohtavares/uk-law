# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 104

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 104. Retention of proxies

(1) Proxies used for voting at any meeting shall be retained by the chair of the meeting.

(2) The chair shall deliver the proxies forthwith after the meeting to the administrator (where the administrator was not the chair).

(3) The administrator shall retain all proxies in the sederunt book.
