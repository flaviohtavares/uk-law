# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 98

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 98. Formal defects

The acts of the creditors’ committee established for a special administration are valid despite any defect in the appointment, election or qualifications of any member of the committee or any committee member’s representative or in the formalities of its establishment.
