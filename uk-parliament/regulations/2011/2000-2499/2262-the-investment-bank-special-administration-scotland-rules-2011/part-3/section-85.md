# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Quorum

A meeting of the creditors’ committee is duly constituted if due notice of it has been given to all the members, and at least 2 members are present or represented.
