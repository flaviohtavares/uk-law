# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 52

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 52. Content of notice for meetings

(1) Notice of a meeting of the creditors, clients or a meeting of creditors and clients, must contain the following information—

- (a) a statement that the proceedings are being held in the court and the court reference number;
- (b) the full name, registered address, registered number and any other trading names of the investment bank;
- (c) the full name and business address of the administrator;
- (d) details of the venue, the date and time of the meeting;
- (e) whether the meeting is—
  - (i) an initial creditors and clients’ meeting under paragraph 51,
  - (ii) to consider revisions to the administrator’s proposals under paragraph 54(2),
  - (iii) a further creditors’, or creditors and clients’, or clients’ meeting under paragraph 56, or
  - (iv) a meeting under paragraph 62,

unless the court orders that it be given by advertisement only in accordance with rule 51.

(2) Where the court orders an extension to the period set out in paragraph 51(2)(b), the administrator shall notify each person who was sent notice in accordance with paragraph 49(4) (or in a special administration (bank administration), paragraph 9 to Schedule 2 to the Regulations).
