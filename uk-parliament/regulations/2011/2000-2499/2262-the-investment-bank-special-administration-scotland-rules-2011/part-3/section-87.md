# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Resignation

A member of the creditors’ committee may resign by notice in writing delivered to the administrator.
