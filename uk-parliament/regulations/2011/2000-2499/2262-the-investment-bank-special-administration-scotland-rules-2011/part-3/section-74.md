# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Administrator voting

(1) Where a resolution is proposed which affects a person in respect of that person’s remuneration or conduct as the administrator, the vote of that person, or of their firm or of any partner or employee of the administrator shall not be reckoned in the majority required for passing the resolution.

(2) Paragraph (1) applies with respect to a vote given by a person (whether personally or on their behalf by a proxy-holder) either as creditor or client, or contributory or as proxy-holder for a creditor, client or contributory.
