# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 66

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 66. Calculation of voting rights (creditors)

Section 50 of the 1985 Act[^f00016] (entitlement to vote and draw dividend) (as applied by rule 127) applies with regard to a creditor’s entitlement to vote in the special administration.

## Annotations

[^f00016]: Footnote: Section 50 has been amended by the Bankruptcy and Diligence etc. (Scotland) Act [2007 (asp 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2007/3), section 226(2), schedule 6 Part 1, and [S.I. 2003/2109](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2109).
