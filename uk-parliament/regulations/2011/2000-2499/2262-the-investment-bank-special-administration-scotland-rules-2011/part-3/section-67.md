# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Calculation of voting rights: special cases (creditors)

(1) An owner of goods under a hire-purchase or chattel leasing agreement, or a seller of goods under a conditional sale agreement, is entitled to vote in respect of the amount of the debt due and payable by the investment bank on the date on which it entered special administration.

(2) In calculating the amount of any debt for the purpose of paragraph (1), no account is to be taken of any amount attributable to the exercise of any right under the relevant agreement so far as the right has become exercisable solely by virtue of—

- (a) the making of a special administration application, or
- (b) the investment bank entering special administration.
