# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Requisite majorities at members or contributories’ meetings

(1) At a meeting of members or contributories of the investment bank, voting rights are as at a general meeting of the investment bank, subject to any provision of the articles affecting entitlement to vote, either generally or at a time when the investment bank is in liquidation.

(2) References in this rule to a person’s share include any other interests which that person may have as a member of the investment bank.
