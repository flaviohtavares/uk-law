# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 72

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 72. Requisite majorities

At a meeting of creditors, clients, or of creditors and clients, a resolution is passed when a majority (in value) of those present and voting, in person or by proxy, have voted in favour of it.
