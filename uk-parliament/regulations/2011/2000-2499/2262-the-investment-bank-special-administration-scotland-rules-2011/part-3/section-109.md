# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 109

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 109. Application in a special administration (bank administration)

If an application referred to in rule 108(1) is made before the Bank of England has given an Objective A Achievement Notice—

- (a) the administrator must notify the Bank of England of the time and place of the hearing;
- (b) the Bank of England may appear or be represented at the hearing;

and if an order is made, the administrator must send a copy to the Bank of England as soon as reasonably practicable.
