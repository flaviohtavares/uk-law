# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 143

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 143. Application to replace (special administration (bank insolvency))

(1) This rule applies in a special administration (bank insolvency) before the Objective A committee has passed a full payment resolution.

(2) Where there is a vacancy in the office of administrator the Bank of England must appoint a replacement administrator as soon as reasonably practicable.

(3) The rules for the appointment of an administrator in Chapter 2 of Part 2 shall apply to the appointment of a replacement administrator.
