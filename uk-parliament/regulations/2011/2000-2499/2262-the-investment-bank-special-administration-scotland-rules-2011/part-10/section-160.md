# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 160

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 160. Second excepted case

(1) Where a person (“**P**”) to whom section 216 of the 1986 Act applies as having been a director or shadow director of the investment bank applies for leave of the court under that section not later than 7 days from the date on which the investment bank went into special administration, P may, during the period specified in paragraph (2), act in any of the ways mentioned in section 216(3), notwithstanding that P has not the leave of the court under that section.

(2) The period referred to in paragraph (1) begins with the day on which the investment bank goes into special administration and ends either on the day falling 6 weeks after that date or on the day on which the court disposes of the application for leave under section 216, whichever of those days occurs first.
