# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 158

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 158. Application for leave under section 216(3) of the 1986 Act

When considering an application for leave under section 216(3) of the 1986 Act, the court may call on the administrator or any former administrator of the investment bank for a report of the circumstances in which that investment bank became insolvent, and the extent (if any) of the applicant’s apparent responsibility for its doing so.
