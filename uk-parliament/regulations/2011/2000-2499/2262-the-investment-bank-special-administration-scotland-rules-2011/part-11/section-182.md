# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 182

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 182. Notifications relating to court orders

In addition to the information required by rule 179, a notification relating to a court order must also specify—

- (a) the nature of the court order; and
- (b) the date of the order.
