# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 185

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 185. Notifications made to other persons at the same time

(1) Where under the Regulations or these Rules a notice or other document is to be sent to another person at the same time that it is to be sent to the registrar of companies, that requirement may be satisfied by sending to that other person a copy of the notification sent to the registrar.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply—

- (a) where additional information is prescribed for the notification to the other person; or
- (b) where the notification to the registrar of companies is incomplete.
