# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 178

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 178. Application of Chapter 3

This Chapter applies where under the Regulations or these Rules information is to be sent or delivered to the registrar of companies.
