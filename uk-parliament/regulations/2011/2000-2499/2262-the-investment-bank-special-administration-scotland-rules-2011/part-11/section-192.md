# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 192

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 192. Service on joint administrators

Where there are joint administrators in a special administration, service on one of them is to be treated as service on all of them.
