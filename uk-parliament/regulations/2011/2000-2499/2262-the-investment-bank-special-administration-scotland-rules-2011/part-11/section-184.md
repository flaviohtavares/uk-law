# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 184

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 184. Notifications of more than one nature

A notification which includes a notification of more than one nature must satisfy the requirements applying in respect of each of those notifications.
