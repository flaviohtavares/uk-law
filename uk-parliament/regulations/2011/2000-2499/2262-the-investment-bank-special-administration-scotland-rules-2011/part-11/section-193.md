# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 193

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 193. Proceedings commenced in the wrong court

Where a special administration is commenced in a court other than the Court of Session, that court may order the transfer of the proceedings to the Court of Session.
