# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 177

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 177. Omission of unobtainable information

Information required by rules 174 and 175 to be included in a notice may be omitted if it is not reasonably practicable to obtain it.
