# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 149

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 149. Final progress report

“**Final progress report**” means a progress report which includes a summary account of—

- (a) the administrator’s original proposals (including whether the FSA has given a direction under regulation 16 and whether that direction has been withdrawn);
- (b) any major changes to, or deviations from, those proposals in the course of the special administration;
- (c) the steps taken during the special administration, including in a special administration (bank insolvency) or a special administration (bank administration), the steps taken to achieve Objective A; and
- (d) the outcome.
