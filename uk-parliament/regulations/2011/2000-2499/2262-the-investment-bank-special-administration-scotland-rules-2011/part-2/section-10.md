# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Expenses

If the court makes a special administration order, the expenses of the applicant, and of any other party whose expenses are allowed by the court, shall be regarded as expenses of the special administration.
