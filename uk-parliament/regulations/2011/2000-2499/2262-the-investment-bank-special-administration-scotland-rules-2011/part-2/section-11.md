# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Notice of special administration order

(1) If the court makes a special administration order, the applicant shall immediately after the order is made, send a certified copy of the order to—

- (a) the administrator; and
- (b) the FSA (if not the applicant).

(2) If the court makes an order under regulation 7(1)(d), or any other order under regulation 7(1)(f), it may direct (or give directions as) to whom, and how, notice of that order is to be given.
