# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Remuneration of the administrator

As soon as practicable after the making of a special administration (bank administration) order, the Bank of England shall fix the terms on which, in accordance with rule 135, the administrator is to be remunerated in respect of—

- (a) work done by the administrator in pursuit of Objective A;
- (b) work done by the administrator in pursuit of Objectives 2 and 3 of the special administration objectives.
