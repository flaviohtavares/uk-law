# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Failure to find or maintain caution

(1) If the person (“**P**”) appointed under rule 17 fails to find or to maintain their caution, the court may remove P and make such order as it thinks fit as to expenses.

(2) If an order is made under this rule removing P, or discharging the order appointing P, the court shall give directions as to whether any, and if so what, steps should be taken for the appointment of another person in P’s place.
