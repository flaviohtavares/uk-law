# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Notice of order

If the court makes a special administration (bank administration) order, the Bank of England shall immediately after the order is made, send a certified copy of the order to—

- (a) the administrator;
- (b) the FSA; and
- (c) the FSCS.
