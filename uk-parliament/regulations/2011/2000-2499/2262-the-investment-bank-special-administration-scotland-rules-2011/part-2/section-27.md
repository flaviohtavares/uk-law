# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Expenses

If the court makes a special administration (bank administration) order, the following are payable as an expense of the special administration—

- (a) the Bank of England’s expenses of making the application; and
- (b) any other expenses allowed by the court.
