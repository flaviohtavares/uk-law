# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Appointment of person under section 135 of the 1986 Act

An application to the court for the appointment of a person under section 135 of the 1986 Act (as applied by Table 2 in section 145(6) of the 2009 Act[^f00012] and by paragraph 6 of Schedule 2 to the Regulations) may be made by the Bank of England.

## Annotations

[^f00012]: Footnote: Section 145 was amended by section 21 of the Financial Services Act [2010 (c. 28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/28).
