# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Authentication of the administrator’s appointment

A copy certified by the clerk of court of the order of court appointing the administrator under rule 14 shall be sufficient evidence for all purposes and in any proceedings that that person has been appointed to exercise the powers and perform the duties of administrator in the special administration (bank insolvency) of that investment bank.
