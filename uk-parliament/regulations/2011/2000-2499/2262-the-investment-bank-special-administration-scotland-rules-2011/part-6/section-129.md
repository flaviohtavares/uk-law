# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 129

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 129. Administrator to allow inspection of proofs

The administrator shall, so long as submitted claims are in the administrator’s hands, allow them to be inspected, at all reasonable times on any business day, by any of the following persons—

- (a) any creditor who has submitted a claim (unless that claim has been wholly rejected for purposes of dividend or otherwise);
- (b) any contributory of the company; and
- (c) any person acting on behalf of either of the above.
