# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 124

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 124. Application of Part 6

(1) This Part applies in any case where the administrator proposes to make a distribution to creditors or any class of them.

(2) Where the distribution is to a particular class of creditors, references in this Part (except rule 132(4)(b)) to creditors shall, so far as the context requires, be references to that class of creditors only.
