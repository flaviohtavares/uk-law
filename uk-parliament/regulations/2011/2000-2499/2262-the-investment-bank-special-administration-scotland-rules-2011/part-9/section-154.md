# The Investment Bank Special Administration (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 154

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 154. Applications under section 176A(5) of the 1986 Act to disapply section 176A

An application under section 176A(5) of the 1986 Act[^f00027] (share of assets for unsecured creditors) shall include averments—

- (a) that the application arises in respect of a special administration;
- (b) as to the financial position of the investment bank; and
- (c) as to the basis of the applicant’s view that the cost of making a distribution to unsecured creditors would be disproportionate to the benefits.
