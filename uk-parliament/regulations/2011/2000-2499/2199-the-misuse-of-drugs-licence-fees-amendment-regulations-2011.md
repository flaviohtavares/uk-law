# The Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [SI 2010/2497](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2497) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2011 No. 2199

Dangerous Drugs

The Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

Made

5th September 2011

Laid before Parliament

8th September 2011

Coming into force

3rd October 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by sections 30, 31 and 37(1)[^f00001] of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971[^f00002].

In accordance with section 31(3) of that Act the Secretary of State has consulted with the Advisory Council on the Misuse of Drugs.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 and shall come into force on 3<sup>rd</sup> October 2011.

## 2. Amendment of the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) Regulations 2010

Regulation 1(3) of the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) Regulations 2010[^f00003] is revoked, and for “Citation, commencement, interpretation and extent” in the heading to that regulation there is substituted “Citation, commencement and interpretation”.

Baroness Browning

Minister of State

Home Office

5th September 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) Regulations 2010 (the “2010 Regulations”) were made on 12<sup>th</sup> October 2010 and came into force on 15<sup>th</sup> November 2010. Regulation 1(3) of the 2010 Regulations - which provided that the 2010 Regulations extended to England, Wales and Scotland, other than the provisions cited in regulation 1(3) which extended to the United Kingdom – was included in the 2010 Regulations in error. That error is corrected in these Regulations which revoke regulation 1(3) of the 2010 Regulations and substitute a new heading to regulation 1 of the 2010 Regulations.
