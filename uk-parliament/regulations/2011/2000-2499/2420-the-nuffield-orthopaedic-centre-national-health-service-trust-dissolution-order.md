# The Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 2420

National Health Service, England

The Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2011

Made

4th October 2011

Coming into force

1st November 2011

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1), 272(7) and 273(1) of, and paragraph 28(1) of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order on the application of the Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust following the completion of the consultation prescribed under section 25(3) of, and paragraph 28(3) of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 1st November 2011.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

(1) The Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust is dissolved.

(2) The Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990[^f00003] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Paul Burstow

Minister of State,

Department of Health

4th October 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1<sup>st</sup> November 2011 of the Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust established on 21st December 1990 and revokes the Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990 that established the trust.
