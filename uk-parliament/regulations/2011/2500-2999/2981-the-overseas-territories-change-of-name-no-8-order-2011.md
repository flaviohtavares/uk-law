# The Overseas Territories (Change of Name) (No. 8) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 2981

Overseas Territories

The Overseas Territories (Change of Name) (No. 8) Order 2011

Made

14th December 2011

Coming into force

14th January 2012

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of December 2011

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 315(2) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00001] and section 150 of the Senior Courts Act 1981[^f00002], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is ordered, as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Overseas Territories (Change of Name) (No. 8) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 14th January 2012.

## 2. Amendment of certain instruments

(1) Article 4 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1979 (Saint Helena) Order 1980[^f00003] is amended by substituting for the words “St Helena and its Dependencies” the words “St Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha”.

(2) The Admiralty Jurisdiction (St Helena and its Dependencies) Order 1969[^f00004] is amended by –

- (a) substituting for the words “St Helena and its Dependencies”, wherever they appear, the words “St Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha”; and
- (b) substituting for the words “St Helena or its Dependencies”, wherever they appear, the words “St Helena, Ascension or Tristan da Cunha”;

and accordingly the Order may be cited as the Admiralty Jurisdiction (St Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha) Order 1969.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends certain statutory instruments relating to shipping and admiralty jurisdiction to reflect the current name of the British overseas territory of St Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha.
