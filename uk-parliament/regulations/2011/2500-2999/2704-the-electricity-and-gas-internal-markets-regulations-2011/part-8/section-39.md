# The Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets) Regulations 2011, section 39

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Enforcement provisions of the Electricity Act 1989

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Part 1 of the Electricity Act 1989 (electricity supply) [^c21104171] is amended as follows.

(2) For the italic heading preceding section 25 [^c21104181] substitute—

|  |
| --- |
|  |
| “*Enforcement of obligations of regulated persons*”. |

(3) In each of the following provisions, for “licence holder” wherever it occurs substitute “ regulated person ”

- (a) section 25 (orders for securing compliance);
- (b) section 26 (procedural requirements) [^c21104191];
- (c) section 27 (validity and effect of orders) [^c21104201];
- (d) section 27A (penalties) [^c21104211];
- (e) section 27C (time limits on the imposition of financial penalties) [^c21104221];
- (f) section 27E (appeals) [^c21104231];
- (g) section 27F (recovery of penalties) [^c21104241];
- (h) section 28(1) (power to require information etc) [^c21104251].

(4) In section 25(8)—

- (a) after the definition of “provisional order” insert—

  > “**regulated person**” means a person who is one or more of the following—
  >
  > - (a) a licence holder;
  > - (b) a distribution exemption holder;
  > - (c) a supply exemption holder;
  > - (d) an electricity undertaking which is a relevant producer or supplier;

  ;
- (b) in the definition of “relevant condition”, for “his licence” substitute “ any licence held by that person ”;
- (c) in the definition of “relevant requirement”, for the words from “him” to the end of the definition substitute “ that person under a provision specified in Schedule 6A as a relevant provision in respect of that person ”.

(5) After Schedule 6 insert the Schedule set out in Schedule 4.
