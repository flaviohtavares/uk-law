# The Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets) Regulations 2011, section 38

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Saving provision in relation to enforcement action under section 22 of the Gas Act 1986

*Extent: E+W+S*

The amendments in regulation 37(1) do not affect any proceedings under section 22 of the Gas Act 1986 commenced before the day on which these Regulations come into force.
