## 40. Amendment of Electricity and Gas (Determination of Turnover for Penalties) Order 2002

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Electricity and Gas (Determination of Turnover for Penalties) Order 2002 [^c21104261] is amended as follows.

(2) In each of the following, for “licence holder” wherever it occurs substitute “ regulated person ”

- (a) the definitions of “applicable turnover” and “business year” in article 2;
- (b) article 3(1), (3) and (4).
