# The Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets) Regulations 2011, section 19

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Independence of electricity distributors

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 6(2) of the Electricity Act 1989 (licences authorising supply, etc) [^c21103831], for “a supply licence” substitute—

> - (a) a generation licence; or
> - (b) a supply licence.

.
