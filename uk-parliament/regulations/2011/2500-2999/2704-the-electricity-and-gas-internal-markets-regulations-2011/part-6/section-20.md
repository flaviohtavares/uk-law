# The Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets) Regulations 2011, section 20

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Duties of gas distribution exemption holders and supply exemption holders

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Gas Act 1986 is amended as follows.

(2) After section 6A [^c21103841] insert—

> **6B. Duties of distribution exemption holders**
>
> Schedule 2AA (which relates to the duties of distribution exemption holders) has effect.
>
> **6C. Duties of supply exemption holders**
>
> Schedule 2AB (which relates to the duties of supply exemption holders) has effect.

.

(3) After Schedule 2A [^c21103851] insert the Schedules set out in Schedule 1.
