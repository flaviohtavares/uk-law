# The Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets) Regulations 2011, section 25

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Definitions in the Utilities Act 2000

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 106(1) of the Utilities Act 2000 (interpretation), in the appropriate places insert—

> “**the Agency**” means the Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators established under the Agency Regulation;

;

> “**the Agency Regulation**” means Regulation 2009/713/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 establishing an Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators [^c21103891];

;

> “**designated regulatory authority**” means an authority designated in accordance with Article 35 of the Electricity Directive or Article 39 of the Gas Directive;

;

> “**designated regulatory authority for Great Britain**” means the authority designated by virtue of section 3A;

;

> “**the Electricity Directive**” means Directive [2009/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0072) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 concerning common rules for the internal market in electricity and repealing Directive [2003/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0054) [^c21103901];

; and

> “**the Gas Directive**” means Directive [2009/73/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0073) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 concerning common rules for the internal market in natural gas and repealing Directive [2003/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0055) [^c21103911];

.
