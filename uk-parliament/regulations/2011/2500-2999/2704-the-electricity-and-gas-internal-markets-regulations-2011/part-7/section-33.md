# The Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets) Regulations 2011, section 33

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Binding decisions of the Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators and of the European Commission in relation to electricity

*Extent: E+W+S*

After section 3D of the Electricity Act 1989 [^c21104021] insert—

> **3E. Binding decisions of the Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators and of the European Commission**
>
> The Authority must carry out its functions under this Part in the manner that it considers is best calculated to implement, or to ensure compliance with, any binding decision of the Agency or the European Commission made under the Electricity Directive, the Electricity Regulation or the Agency Regulation in relation to electricity.

.
