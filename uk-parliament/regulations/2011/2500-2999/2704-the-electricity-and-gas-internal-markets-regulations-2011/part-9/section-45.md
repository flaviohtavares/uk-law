# The Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets) Regulations 2011, section 45

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. Minor and consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In section 10(4) of the Electricity Act 1989 (duty to copy notice and comply with direction) [^c21104561] omit “, 11”.

(2) In paragraph 19A(9) of Schedule 7 to the Competition Act 1998 (manner in which general functions are to be carried out) [^c21104571], in the definition of “special reference group”—

- (a) in paragraph (d) omit “24 or”; and
- (b) in paragraph (e) omit “12 or”.

(3) In section 104(1) of the Utilities Act 2000 (specialist members of the Competition Commission) [^c21104581]—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “section 24, 26A or” substitute “ any of sections 23B to 23G [^c21104591] or section ”; and
- (b) in paragraph (b), for “section 12, 14A or” substitute “ any of sections 11C to 11H or section ”.
