# The Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets) Regulations 2011, section 49

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 49. Modifications of standard conditions of gas licences

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Schedule 7 to these Regulations (which modifies the standard conditions of transporter, interconnector and supply licences granted under Part 1 of the Gas Act 1986 [^c21104731]) has effect.

(2) In section 81(2) of the Utilities Act 2000 (standard conditions of gas transporter, supply and shipping licences) [^c21104741], after “made” insert “ under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, ”.

(3) In section 150(5) of the Energy Act 2004 (standard conditions of gas interconnector licences) [^c21104751], after “made” insert “ under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, ”.
