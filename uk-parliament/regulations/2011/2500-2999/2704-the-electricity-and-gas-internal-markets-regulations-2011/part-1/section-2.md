# The Electricity and Gas (Internal Markets) Regulations 2011, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S*

In these Regulations, “**standard condition**” in relation to—

- (a) a licence granted under section 7, 7ZA or 7A(1) of the Gas Act 1986 [^c21103471], means a condition which by virtue of section 81(2) of the Utilities Act 2000 [^c21103481] or section 137(3) or 150(5) of the Energy Act 2004 [^c21103491] is a standard condition for the purposes of that licence;
- (b) a licence granted under section 6(1)(b) to (e) of the Electricity Act 1989 [^c21103501], means a condition which by virtue of section 33(1) of the Utilities Act 2000 [^c21103511] or section 146(5) of the Energy Act 2004 is a standard condition for the purposes of that licence.
