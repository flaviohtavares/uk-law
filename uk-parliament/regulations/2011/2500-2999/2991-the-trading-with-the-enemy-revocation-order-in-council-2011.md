# The Trading with the Enemy (Revocation) Order in Council 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 2991

Trading With The Enemy

The Trading with the Enemy (Revocation) Order in Council 2011

Made

14th December 2011

Coming into force

15th December 2011

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of December 2011

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 14 and 15(5) of the Trading with the Enemy Act 1939[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Trading with the Enemy (Revocation) Order in Council 2011 and comes into force on 15th December 2011.

## 2. Revocation

The Orders specified in the Schedule are revoked.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Orders in Council listed in the Schedule.

Those Orders extended the application of certain provisions of the Trading with the Enemy Act 1939 to the named territories and to the property vested in the China Custodian.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
