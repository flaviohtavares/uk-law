# The Central Office of Information Trading Fund (Revocation) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 2884

Government Trading Funds

The Central Office of Information Trading Fund (Revocation) Order 2011

Made

29th November 2011

Laid before the House of Commons

7th December 2011

Coming into force

30th December 2011

The Minister for the Cabinet Office, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 6(1) of the Government Trading Funds Act 1973[^f00001], with the concurrence of the Treasury, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Central Office of Information Trading Fund (Revocation) Order 2011 and comes into force on 30th December 2011.

## 2. Revocation

The Central Office of Information Trading Fund Order 1991[^f00002] and the Central Office of Information Trading Fund (Variation) Order 1992[^f00003] are revoked.

Francis Maude

Minister for the Cabinet Office

We concur

Michael Fabricant

Jeremy Wright

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

29th November 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Central Office of Information Trading Fund Order 1991 and the Central Office of Information Trading Fund (Variation) Order 1992. As a result, the COI Trading Fund established by that Order ceases to exist.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
