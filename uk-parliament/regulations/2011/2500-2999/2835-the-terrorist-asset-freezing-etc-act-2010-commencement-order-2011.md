# The Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 2835 (C. 99)

Prevention And Suppression Of Terrorism

The Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2011

Made

23rd November 2011

The Treasury, in exercise of the power conferred by section 55(2) of the Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010[^f00001], make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2011.

**2.** The following provisions of the Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010 shall come into force on 31st March 2012—

- (a) section 51 (Northern Ireland credit unions);
- (b) section 52 (consequential amendments and repeals) so far as relating to section 51;
- (c) paragraph 11 of Part 2 of Schedule 1 (consequential amendments); and
- (d) Part 2 of Schedule 2 (repeals and revocations).

Michael Fabricant

Angela Watkinson

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

23rd November 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 31st March 2012 those provisions of the Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act [2010 (c.38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/38) which are not already in force to effect the transfer to the Financial Services Authority of the supervisory responsibilities of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment in Northern Ireland for Northern Ireland credit unions contained in Schedule 7 to the Counter-Terrorism Act [2008 (c.28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/28) (terrorist financing and money laundering).
