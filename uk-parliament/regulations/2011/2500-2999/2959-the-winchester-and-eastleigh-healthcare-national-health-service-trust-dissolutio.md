# The Winchester and Eastleigh Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 2959

National Health Service, England

The Winchester and Eastleigh Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2011

Made

8th December 2011

Coming into force

9th January 2012

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1) and 273(1) of, and paragraph 28(1) of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order on the application of the Winchester and Eastleigh Healthcare National Health Service Trust following the completion of the consultation prescribed under section 25(3) of, and paragraph 28(3) of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Winchester and Eastleigh Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 9th January 2012.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

(1) The Winchester and Eastleigh Healthcare National Health Service Trust is dissolved.

(2) The Winchester and Eastleigh Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00003] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Simon Burns

Minister of State,

Department of Health

8th December 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 9th January 2012 of the Winchester and Eastleigh Healthcare National Health Service Trust established on 1st November 1993 and revokes the Winchester and Eastleigh Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993 that established the trust.
