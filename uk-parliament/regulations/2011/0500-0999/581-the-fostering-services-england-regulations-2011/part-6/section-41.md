# The Fostering Services (England) Regulations 2011, section 41

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Compliance with Regulations

*Extent: E+W*

Where there is more than one registered person in respect of a fostering agency, anything which is required under these Regulations to be done by the registered person, need only be done by one of the registered persons.
