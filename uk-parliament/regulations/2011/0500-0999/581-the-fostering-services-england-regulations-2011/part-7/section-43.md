# The Fostering Services (England) Regulations 2011, section 43

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Amendment of Regulations

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Care Planning Regulations are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1) for the definition of “the 2002 Regulations” substitute “ “**the Fostering Services Regulations**” means the Fostering Services (England) Regulations 2011; ”.

(3) In the following regulations, for “the 2002 Regulations” wherever those words appear substitute “ the Fostering Services Regulations ”

- (i) regulation 2(1) in the definition of “fostering service provider”,
- (ii) regulation 6(3)(d),
- (iii) regulation 21(1),
- (iv) regulation 23(1),
- (v) regulation 24(1)(b) and (2)(c), and
- (vi) regulation 25(4)(b) and (6).

(4) In regulation 22(2)(c) for “28(5)(b) of the 2002 Regulations” substitute “ 27(5)(b) of the Fostering Services Regulations ”.

(5) For paragraph 3(4) of Schedule 2 substitute “ Any circumstances in which F must obtain the prior approval of either the responsible authority or P before making decisions in relation to C or C's care. ”.

(6) In paragraph 3(6) of Schedule 2 for “28(5) of the 2002 Regulations” substitute “ 27(5)(b) of the Fostering Services Regulations ”.

(7) In paragraph 5 of Schedule 7 for “agreement” substitute “ plan ”.
