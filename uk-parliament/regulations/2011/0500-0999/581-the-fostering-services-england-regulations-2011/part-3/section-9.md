# The Fostering Services (England) Regulations 2011, section 9

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Notification of offences

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2020-09-25).**

Where the registered person or the responsible individual is convicted of any criminal offence, whether in England and Wales or elsewhere, that person must without delay give notice in writing to the Chief Inspector of—

- (a) the date and place of the conviction,
- (b) the offence of which they were convicted, and
- (c) the penalty imposed on them in respect of the offence.
