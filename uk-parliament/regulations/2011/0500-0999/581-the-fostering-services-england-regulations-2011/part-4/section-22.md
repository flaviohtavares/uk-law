> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Records with respect to fostering services

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The fostering service provider must maintain and keep up to date the records specified in Schedule 2.

(2) The records referred to in paragraph (1) must be retained for at least 15 years from the date of the last entry.
