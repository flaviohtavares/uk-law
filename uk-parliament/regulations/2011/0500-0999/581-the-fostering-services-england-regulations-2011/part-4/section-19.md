# The Fostering Services (England) Regulations 2011, section 19

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Staffing of fostering service

*Extent: E+W*

The fostering service provider must ensure that there is a sufficient number of suitably qualified, competent and experienced persons working for the purposes of the fostering service, having regard to—

- (a) the size of the fostering service, its statement of purpose, and the numbers and needs of the children placed by it, and
- (b) the need to safeguard and promote the health and welfare of children placed with foster parents.
