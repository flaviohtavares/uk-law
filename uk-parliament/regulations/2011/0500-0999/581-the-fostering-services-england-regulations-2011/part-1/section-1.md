# The Fostering Services (England) Regulations 2011, section 1

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Fostering Services (England) Regulations 2011 and come into force on 1st April 2011.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to England only.
