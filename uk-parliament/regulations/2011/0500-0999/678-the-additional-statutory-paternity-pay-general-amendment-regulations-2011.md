# The Additional Statutory Paternity Pay (General) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2010/1056](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1056) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2011 No. 678

Terms And Conditions Of Employment

The Additional Statutory Paternity Pay (General) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

Made

7th March 2011

Laid before Parliament

9th March 2011

Coming into force

1st April 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 171ZEE(2) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001] and after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that the proposals in respect of these Regulations shall not be referred to it[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Additional Statutory Paternity Pay (General) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 and shall come into force on 1st April 2011.

## 2. Amendment to the Additional Statutory Paternity Pay (General) Regulations 2010

In regulation 14 of the Additional Statutory Paternity Pay (General) Regulations 2010[^f00003] (additional statutory paternity pay period (adoption)), in paragraph (1), for “before” substitute “after”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, issued free of charge, amend the Additional Statutory Paternity Pay (General) Regulations 2010 (“the Principal Regulations”) to correct a drafting error in regulation 14 of those Regulations. The error concerns the date of the beginning of the additional statutory paternity pay period (adoption).

An impact assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations since they have the same effect on the costs to business and the voluntary sector as the Principal Regulations. A copy of the impact assessment for the Principal Regulations is available from the BIS website (www.bis.gov.uk) and is published with the Explanatory Memorandum alongside the principal Regulations on www.legislation.gov.uk.
