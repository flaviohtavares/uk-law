# The Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011, section 44

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Manner of making a claim

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A claim for benefit must be—

- (a) in writing;
- (b) on a form approved for that purpose by the Secretary of State;
- (c) signed by or on behalf of the claimant; and
- (d) given or sent to the Service Personnel and Veterans Agency within the time specified in article 47, 48 or 49 whichever is applicable.
