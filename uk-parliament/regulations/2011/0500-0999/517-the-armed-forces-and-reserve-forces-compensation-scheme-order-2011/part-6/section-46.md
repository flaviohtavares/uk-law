# The Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011, section 46

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Date of claim

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph[^c2153170](3), the date on which a claim is made is the date on which it is received by the Service Personnel and Veterans Agency.

(2) [^c2153172]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Where article 45(1) applies, the date of claim is to be treated as—

- (a) the day after the day on which the member is discharged on medical grounds; or
- (b) the day after the date of death of the member.
