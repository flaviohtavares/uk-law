# The Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011, section 69A

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69A. Invalidity of assignments and bankruptcy[^key-d6e06a55d4f04ca56a98ce9d7061790c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any assignment or charge, and any agreement to assign or charge, any pension, allowance, benefit or lump sum which may be or has been awarded to a person under this Order is void.

(2) On the bankruptcy of a person referred to in paragraph (1) no part of an award referred to in that paragraph shall pass to any trustee or person acting on behalf of the creditors.
