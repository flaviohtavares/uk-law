# The Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011, section 89

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Time limit for application for reconsideration

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the period for making an application for a reconsideration under article 53(3) in relation to a decision made before 9th May 2011 is 1 year starting from the date the decision was given or sent to the claimant.

(2) The period of 1 year does not apply to an application for a reconsideration if the period of 3 months for making an application under article 45(1) of the AFCS 2005 expired before 9th May 2011.
