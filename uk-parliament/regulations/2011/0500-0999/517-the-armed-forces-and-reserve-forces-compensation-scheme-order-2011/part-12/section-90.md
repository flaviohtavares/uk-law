# The Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011, section 90

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Fast payment and medical expenses

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Article 27 (fast payment) applies only where an injury is sustained on or after 9th May 2011.

(2) Where a former member whose service ended before 9th May 2011 applies for medical expenses under article 28, that article is to be construed in accordance with paragraph (3).

(3) The condition in article 28(2)(b) is satisfied if the person becomes ordinarily resident outside the United Kingdom within 1 year starting with 9th May 2011, and paragraph (5) of that article is to be construed accordingly.
