> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 65. Time of payment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Guaranteed income payment, survivor's guaranteed income payment and child's payment are paid monthly in arrears unless, in any particular case, the Secretary of State arranges otherwise.
