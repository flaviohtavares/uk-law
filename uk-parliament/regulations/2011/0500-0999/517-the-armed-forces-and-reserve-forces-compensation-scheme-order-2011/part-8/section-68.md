# The Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011, section 68

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Suspension in other cases

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies where—

- (a) an appeal has been brought against a decision of an appropriate tribunal, the Upper Tribunal, a Commissioner or a court in relation to a case (“**the primary case**”); and
- (b) if such an appeal were to be allowed a question would arise in relation to another case (“**the secondary case**”) whether the award of benefit in that case ought to be reviewed.

(2) Where paragraph (1) applies the Secretary of State may direct that payment of the benefit under the award in the secondary case be suspended, in whole or in part until such an appeal has been determined.
