# The Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011, section 65A

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65A. Time of payment for armed forces independence payment[^c2179264]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Armed forces independence payment is paid every 4 weeks in arrears, unless in any particular case the Secretary of State arranges otherwise.
