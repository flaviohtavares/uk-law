# The Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011, section 42

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Cessation of guaranteed income payment and survivor's guaranteed income payment on admission to the Royal Hospital, Chelsea

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any guaranteed income payment , armed forces independence payment[^c2179260] or survivor's guaranteed income payment payable to a former member will cease to be paid on admission to the Royal Hospital, Chelsea, as an in-pensioner but may be restored, if the former member subsequently leaves the hospital.
