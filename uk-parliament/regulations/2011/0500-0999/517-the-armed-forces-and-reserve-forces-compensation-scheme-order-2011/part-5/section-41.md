# The Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011, section 41

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Negligence or misconduct

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may withhold up to 40% of benefit (except for armed forces independence payment)[^c2179258] where the negligence or misconduct of a member or former member contributed to that person's injury or death.
