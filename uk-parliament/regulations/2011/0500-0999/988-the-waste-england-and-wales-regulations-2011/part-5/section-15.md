# The Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/988

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Guidance

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The appropriate authority may give guidance on the discharge of the duties in regulations 12 to 14.

(2) An establishment or undertaking discharging any of the duties in regulations 12 to 14 must, in doing so, have regard to any such guidance.
