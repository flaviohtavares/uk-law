# The Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/988
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**the 1989 Act**” means the Control of Pollution (Amendment) Act 1989 [^c2081387];
- “**compliance notice**” has the meaning given in regulation 38(1);
- “**regulation authority**” has the meaning given in section 9 of the 1989 Act [^c2081388];
- “**restoration notice**” has the meaning given in regulation 40(1);
- “**specified person**” has the meaning given in regulation 24(5);
- “**stop notice**” has the meaning given in regulation 39(1).
