# The Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/988
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Offence and penalties

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person is guilty of an offence who—

- (a) if they are not a specified person, fails to comply with regulation 25; or
- (b) fails to comply with a compliance notice, stop notice or restoration notice.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under—

- (a) paragraph (1)(a); or
- (b) paragraph (1)(b) in relation to contravention of regulation 25,

is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.

(3) A person guilty of an offence under paragraph (1)(b) in relation to contravention of regulation 12(1), 13(2)[^c-20a077e91751276956d7db8c4b6be6ab] or 14(1) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine.
