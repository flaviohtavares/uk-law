## 21. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, “**offshore licensing functions**” means—

- (a) functions under Part 2 of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985 [^c2081363];
- (b) functions under Part 4 of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 [^c2081364],

other than functions which must be exercised by statutory instrument.
