# The Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/988
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Inspections

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where an appropriate authority has offshore licensing functions in relation to establishments or undertakings carrying on disposal or recovery of waste.

(2) The appropriate authority must ensure that appropriate periodic inspections of those establishments or undertakings are made [^c2081365].

## Annotations

[^c2081365]: Marginal citation: Section 11 of the [Food and Environment Protection Act 1985 (c. 48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/48) and sections 246 to 248 of the [Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/23) provide for powers relevant to inspection.
