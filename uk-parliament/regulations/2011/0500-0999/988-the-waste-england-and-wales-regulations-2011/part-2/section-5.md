# The Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/988
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Purposes etc. of waste prevention programmes

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The appropriate authority must ensure that a waste prevention programme—

- (a) is compatible with the objectives in paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 1;
- (aa)[^c-6981b6e821d20e9939ff3db124b3b64a] includes one or more programmes of food waste prevention measures;
- (b) has as its purpose a contribution towards breaking the link between economic growth and the environmental impacts associated with the generation of waste;
- (c) is expressed in writing and—
  - (i) sets out the objectives of the programme and a description of existing waste prevention measures and their contribution to waste prevention[^c-ff6d05edef4b197a040d0407b46b1df3]; [^c-e0646dfa5d5da12be2986b44dbfd3ad8]...
  - (ii) if it is integrated into a waste management plan or other programme, clearly identifies the programme's waste prevention measures;
  - (iii)[^c-d9ebd8a6343ef8f51c6e909a003cc5d6] sets out at least the waste prevention measures listed in Schedule 1, Part 5; and
  - (iv) where relevant, describes the contribution of instruments listed in Schedule 1, Part 6 to waste prevention.
