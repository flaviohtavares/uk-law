# The Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/988
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Inspections

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a planning authority has planning functions in relation to establishments or undertakings carrying on disposal or recovery of waste.

(2) The planning authority must ensure that appropriate periodic inspections of those establishments or undertakings are made [^c2081360].

## Annotations

[^c2081360]: Marginal citation: Sections 196A-196C of the [Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8) provide for powers relevant to inspection. Those sections were inserted by the [Planning and Compensation Act 1991 (c. 34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/34).
