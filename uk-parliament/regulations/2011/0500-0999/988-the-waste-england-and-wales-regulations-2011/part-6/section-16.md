# The Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/988
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. General interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- “**the 1990 Act**” means the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 [^c2081345];
- “**the 2004 Act**” means the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 [^c2081346];
- “**the 2008 Act**” means the Planning Act 2008 [^c2081347];
- “[^c-e05b3c77dfda237c30591b356a0a895b]**the 2024 Act**” means the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024;
- “**infrastructure consent**” means the consent required by section 19 of the 2024 Act;
- “**local planning authority**” has the same meaning as in 1990 Act;
- “**mineral planning authority**” has the same meaning as in section 1 of the 1990 Act [^c2081348];
- “**planning permission**” has the meaning given in section 336 of the 1990 Act [^c2081349].

(2) In this Part, “**the planning Acts**” means—

- (a) the 1990 Act;
- (b) the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990 [^c2081350];
- (c) the Planning (Hazardous Substances) Act 1990 [^c2081351];
- (d) the Planning (Consequential Provisions) Act 1990 [^c2081352];
- (e) the 2004 Act; [^c-60986677368a3ef6d02b62690a233bcc]...
- (f) the 2008 Act ;[^c-d29dde42087c87845e7bf5d7b71638e6] [^c-67c160e9de202f4d1382228b672db930]...
- (g) the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 ; and[^c-92794ff8aa12080d0e29aed7505d4783]
- (h) the 2024 Act.

(3) In this Part, “**planning authority**” means—

- (a) a local planning authority;
- (ba)[^c-5a110c2c48b925a8f8b26345fa5ac71c] a local planning authority or minerals and waste planning authority for the purposes of Part 2 of the 2004 Act;
- (c) a person appointed under paragraph 1 of Schedule 6 to the 1990 Act [^c2081353];
- (d) a government department in respect of its functions under the planning Acts; or
- (e) an appropriate authority in respect of its functions under the planning Acts.
