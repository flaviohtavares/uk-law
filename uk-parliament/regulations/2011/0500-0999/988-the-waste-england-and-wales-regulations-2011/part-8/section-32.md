# The Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/988
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Revocation of registration

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A registration may be revoked if—

- (a)[^key-22c8530222a3f7446a8607de590ad4ec] the registered person or another relevant person has been convicted of a relevant offence within the meaning of regulation 29;
- (b) in the opinion of the appropriate body[^key-a889482f29cfee2daf8c78c441fb4a86], it is undesirable for the registered carrier, broker or dealer to continue to be authorised to act as a carrier or broker of, or a dealer in, controlled waste (as the case may be).

(2) If a registration is revoked the appropriate body[^key-96067d9ad906b9e64fb4fba4c3099250] must notify the registered person and give written reasons for the revocation.

(3) A revocation comes into force—

- (a) where no appeal is made, 28 days after the date the notification is received; or
- (b) where an appeal is made, when it is withdrawn or refused.
