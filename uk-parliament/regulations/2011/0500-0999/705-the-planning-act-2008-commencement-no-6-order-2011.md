# The Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 705 (C. 29)

Infrastructure Planning

The Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2011

Made

8th March 2011

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 241(8) of the Planning Act 2008[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No.6) Order 2011.

(2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Planning Act 2008.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 6th April 2011 in relation to England and Wales

Sections 14(1)(o) and 29 of the Act shall come into force, in relation to England and Wales, on 6th April 2011.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Bob Neill

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

8th March 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force, on 6th April, 2011, the following provisions of the Act, in relation to England and Wales—

section 14(1)(o) (nationally significant infrastructure projects:general) in relation to waste water treatment plants; and

sections 29 (waste water treatment plants).
