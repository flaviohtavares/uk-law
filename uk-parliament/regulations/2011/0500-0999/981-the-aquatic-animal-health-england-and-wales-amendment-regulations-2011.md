The Secretary of State and the Welsh Ministers are each designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy.

The Secretary of State in relation to England and the Welsh Ministers in relation to Wales make these Regulations under the powers conferred by that section.

## 1. Title and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Aquatic Animal Health (England and Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 and come into force on 1st May 2011.

## 2. Amendment to the Aquatic Animal Health (England and Wales) Regulations 2009

(1) The Aquatic Animal Health (England and Wales) Regulations 2009[^f00003] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3 (interpretation)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), omit the definition of “Decision [2004/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0453)”;
- (b) in paragraph (3), omit the words “Decision [2004/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0453),”.

(3) In paragraphs (1) and (2) of regulation 17 (certification requirements), omit the words “Article 5 of Decision [2004/453/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0453) or”.

(4) In regulation 25 (contents and publication of an initial designation), after paragraph (3), insert—

> (3A) An initial designation may be amended, suspended or withdrawn by further notice.

.

(5) In regulation 29 (contents and publication of a confirmed designation), after paragraph (3), insert—

> (3A) A confirmed designation may be amended, suspended or withdrawn by further notice.

.

(6) At the end of the table in Schedule 1 (list of diseases)—

- (a) in the left hand column (disease) add “Ostreid herpesvirus 1 µvar (OsHV-1 µvar)”; and
- (b) in the corresponding entry in the right hand column (susceptible species) add “Pacific oyster (*Crassostrea gigas*)”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of these Regulations)

The Aquatic Animal Health (England and Wales) Regulations 2009 (the 2009 Regulations) implement Council Directive [2006/88/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0088) (OJ No L 238, 24.11.2006, p14) on animal health requirements for aquaculture animals and products thereof and on the prevention and control of certain diseases in aquatic animals.

These Regulations amend the 2009 Regulations to remove references to repealed legislation, make provision relating to the amendment, suspension and withdrawal of initial and confirmed designations and add Ostreid herpesvirus 1 µvar to the list of notifiable diseases,

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on business, the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
