# The Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme (Amendment) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 811

Pensions

The Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme (Amendment) Order 2011

Made

15th March 2011

Laid before Parliament

17th March 2011

Coming into force

11th April 2011

The Secretary of State, with the approval of the Treasury, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 2 of the Personal Injuries (Emergency Provisions) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002]:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme (Amendment) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 11th April 2011.

(2) In this Order “the principal Scheme” means the Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme 1983[^f00003].

## 2. Amendments to the principal Scheme

(1) For Schedule 3 to the principal Scheme substitute the Schedule set out in Schedule 1 to this Order.

(2) For Schedule 4 to the principal Scheme substitute the Schedule set out in Schedule 2 to this Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme 1983 (“the principal Scheme”) which makes provision for the payment of pensions and allowances to or in respect of civilians who were killed or injured during the 1939-1945 World War.

Article 2(1) substitutes a new Schedule 3 to the principal Scheme. The new Schedule 3 (which varies the rates of pensions and allowances payable in respect of disablement and of earnings or income thresholds) is contained in Schedule 1 to this Order.

Article 2(2) substitutes a new Schedule 4 to the principal Scheme. The new Schedule 4 (which varies the rates of pensions and allowances in respect of death) is contained in Schedule 2 to this Order.
