# The Sports Grounds and Sporting Events (Designation) (Amendment) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 1186

Sports Grounds And Sporting Events, England And Wales

The Sports Grounds and Sporting Events (Designation) (Amendment) Order 2011

Made

28th April 2011

Laid before Parliament

4th May 2011

Coming into force

26th May 2011

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 9(3) of the Sporting Events (Control of Alcohol etc) Act 1985[^f00001] makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Sports Grounds and Sporting Events (Designation) (Amendment) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 26th May 2011.

(2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

## 2. Amendment to the Sporting Grounds and Sporting Events (Designation) Order 2005

(1) The Sports Grounds and Sporting Events (Designation) Order 2005[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In Part 1 of Schedule 2 after “Welsh Premier League,” insert “or whose home ground is for the time being situated outside England and Wales,”.

James Brokenshire

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

28th April 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which comes into force on 26th May 2011, amends the Sports Grounds and Sporting Events (Designation) Order 2005 for the purposes of designated sporting events to which the Sporting Events (Control of Alcohol etc) Act 1985 applies.

This Order includes in the list of designated sporting events, any football match in which one or both clubs have their home ground for the time being outside England and Wales.
