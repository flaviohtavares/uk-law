# The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 1122 (C. 46)

Criminal Law, Northern Ireland

Criminal Procedure, Northern Ireland

The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2011

Made

12th April 2011

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 182(5) of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^f00001].

In accordance with section 182(7) of that Act[^f00002], the Secretary of State has consulted the Department of Justice.

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2011.

## 2. Commencement

Sections 75(2)(c), 77(1)(c) and 81(3) of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (anonymity in investigations conducted by the Serious Organised Crime Agency)[^f00003] shall come into force in Northern Ireland on 2nd May 2011.

Owen Paterson

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

12th April 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 75(2)(c), 77(1)(c) and 81(3) of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 in Northern Ireland on 2nd May 2011. These provisions relate to anonymity of persons in certain investigations conducted by the Serious Organised Crime Agency.
