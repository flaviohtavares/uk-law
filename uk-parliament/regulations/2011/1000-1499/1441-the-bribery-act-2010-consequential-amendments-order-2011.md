Made

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 17(4) of the Bribery Act 2010[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-6fbc8ffd35b4e4e50e47e055d31fef4b]

This Order may be cited as the Bribery Act 2010 (Consequential Amendments) Order 2011 and comes into force on 1st July 2011.

## 2. Consequential amendments[^c-a53268f0637f914b83249f238860b359]

The amendments in the Schedule, which are consequential on the entry into force of the Bribery Act 2010, have effect[^f00002].

Kenneth Clarke

Secretary of State for Justice

Ministry of Justice

7th June 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes amendments to subordinate legislation in consequence of the Bribery Act 2010 (“the Act”): it comes into force on 1st July 2011. The amendments reflect the abolition of the common law offences of bribery and embracery by section 17(1)(a) of the Act and the repeal, by Schedule 2 to the Act, of the Public Bodies Corrupt Practices Act [1889 (c. 69)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1889/69), the Prevention of Corruption Act [1906 (c. 34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1906/34), and the Prevention of Corruption Act [1916 (c. 64)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1916/64).

Paragraph 1 of the Schedule to this Order (“**the Schedule**”) amends Schedule 1 to the Extradition Act 2003 (Parties to International Conventions) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/46](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/46)) by substituting references to offences abolished or repealed by the Act with offences under section 1 and 6 of the Act.

Paragraph 2 of the Schedule amends regulation 23(1) of the Public Contracts Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/5)) by inserting references to offences under section 1 and 6 of the Act.

Paragraph 3 of the Schedule amends regulation 26(1) of the Utilities Contracts Regulations ([S.I. 2006/6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/6)) by inserting references to offences under section 1 and 6 of the Act.

Paragraph 4 of the Schedule amends the National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/236](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/236)) by repealing sub-paragraph (b) of article 117(2) of that Order.

Paragraph 5 of the Schedule amends part 6 of Schedule 1 to the Criminal Defence Service (Funding) Order ([S.I. 2007/1174](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1174)) by inserting references to offences under section 1, 2 and 6 of the Act.

Paragraph 6 of the Schedule amends Schedule 8 to the Electricity (Competitive Tenders for Offshore Transmission Licences) Regulations ([S.I. 2010/1903](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1903)) by substituting the references to offences repealed by the Act with references to offences under section 1, 2 and 6 of the Act.

Section 17(4) of the Bribery Act 2010 confers a power on the relevant national authority to make such supplementary, incidental or consequential provision for the purposes of the Act or in consequence of the Act; section 17(10) defines “relevant national authority”. The Secretary of State is the relevant national authority in relation to the provision made in this Order. The Order amends only secondary legislation, and is subject, therefore, by section 17(7) of the Act, to the negative procedure.
