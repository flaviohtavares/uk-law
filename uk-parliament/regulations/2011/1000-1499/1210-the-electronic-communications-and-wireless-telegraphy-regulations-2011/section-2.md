# The Electronic Communications and Wireless Telegraphy Regulations 2011, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Implementation of Directives [2009/136/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0136) and [2009/140/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0140)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 1 (which contains provision amending the Communications Act 2003 [^c20853171] and other enactments for the purpose of implementing Directive [2009/136/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0136) of the European Parliament and of the Council [^c20853181] and Directive [2009/140/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0140) of the European Parliament and of the Council [^c20853191]) has effect.

(2) Schedule 2 (which contains provision amending the Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006 [^c20853201] for the purpose of implementing Directive [2009/140/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0140)) has effect.
