> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 258. Witness statement in support of application[^key-941d2d2cf4ca8ebe7553bef99f0c2b58]

*Extent: E+W*

An application under rule 257(3) must be supported by a witness statement made by a registered medical practitioner as to the mental or physical condition of the incapacitated person.
