## 235. Service of court documents within the jurisdiction[^key-786497635d503027f01aba0c93d65d47]

*Extent: E+W*

Except where different provision is made in the regulations or these rules, CPR Part 6 applies in relation to the service of court documents with such modifications as the court may direct.
