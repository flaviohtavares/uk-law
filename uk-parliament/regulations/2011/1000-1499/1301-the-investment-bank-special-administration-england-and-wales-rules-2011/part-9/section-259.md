> Text as at 2018-02-26.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 259. Service of notices following appointment[^key-d7de1e2fa218c247922bb39087305f00]

*Extent: E+W*

Any notice served on, or sent to, a person appointed under rule 257 has the same effect as if it had been served on, or given to, the incapacitated person.
