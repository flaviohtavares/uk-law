# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 228

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 228. Office copies of documents[^c-795233f6b1cdac184e8e6885de729f9e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court must provide an office copy of any document from the court file of the special administration to any person who has under the Rules the right to inspect the court file where that person has requested such a copy.

(2) A person’s rights under this rule may be exercised on that person’s behalf by that person’s solicitor.

(3) An office copy provided by the court under this rule must be in such form as the registrar thinks appropriate, and must bear the court’s seal.
