# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 245

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 245. Adjournment of the hearing of an application[^key-9ff5ba8704101efc8cdaf361821e2017]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court may adjourn the hearing of an application on such terms as it thinks just.

(2) The court may give directions as to the manner in which any evidence is to be adduced at a resumed hearing and in particular as to—

- (a) the taking of evidence wholly or partly by witness statement or orally;
- (b) the cross-examination of the maker of a witness statement; or
- (c) any report to be made by the administrator.
