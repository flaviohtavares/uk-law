# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 237

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 237. Service of orders staying proceedings[^c-32b958dc8ce570fd56663725ab692ff5]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the court makes an order staying any action, execution or other legal process against the property of the investment bank, service within the jurisdiction of the order may be effected by serving a sealed copy of the order on the address for service of the claimant or other party having the carriage of the proceedings to be stayed.
