# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 261

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 261. Application of Chapter 10[^c-4b4d96a968147ef5c0a30174c77571ed]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Chapter applies in relation to costs in connection with the special administration.

(2) In this Chapter, a reference to costs includes charges and expenses.
