# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 238

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 238. Service on joint office-holders[^key-a340c510559603482d0c33a4140a1d7f]

*Extent: E+W*

Where there are joint administrators, service of court documents on one of them is to be treated as service on all of them.
