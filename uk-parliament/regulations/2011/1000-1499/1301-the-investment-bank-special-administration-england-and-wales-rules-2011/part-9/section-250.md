# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 250

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 250. Application of following rules[^key-6845df03ea1101c801c418be7cb0ca39]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This chapter applies to applications to the court for an order under section 236 of the 1986 Act[^f00019] (inquiry into company dealings).

(2) In this Chapter, “**the respondent**” means the person in respect of whom an order is applied for.
