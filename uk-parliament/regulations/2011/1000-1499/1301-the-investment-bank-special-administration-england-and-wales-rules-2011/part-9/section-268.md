# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 268

> Text as at 2018-02-26.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 268. Costs and expenses of witnesses[^key-10a9a92e0d23318845d9e60e041420af]

*Extent: E+W*

Except as directed by the court, no allowance as a witness in any examination or other proceedings before the court is to be made to an officer of the investment bank to which the proceedings relate.
