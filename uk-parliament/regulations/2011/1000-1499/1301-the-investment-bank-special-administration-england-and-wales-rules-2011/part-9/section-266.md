# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 266

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 266. Award of costs against the administrator[^c-2d5c948c738325d0e0190e4f122cde36]

*Extent: E+W*

Without prejudice to any provision of the Regulations or Rules by virtue of which the administrator is not in any event to be liable for costs and expenses, where the administrator is made a party to any proceedings on the application of another party to the proceedings, the administrator is not to be personally liable for the costs unless the court otherwise directs.
