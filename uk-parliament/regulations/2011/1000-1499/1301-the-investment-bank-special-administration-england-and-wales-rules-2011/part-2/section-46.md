# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 46

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Costs[^key-694e913a830a9261756350fa3cccb9a4]

*Extent: E+W*

If the court makes a special administration (bank administration) order, the following are payable as an expense of the bank administration—

- (a) the Bank of England’s costs of making the application; and
- (b) any other costs allowed by the court.
