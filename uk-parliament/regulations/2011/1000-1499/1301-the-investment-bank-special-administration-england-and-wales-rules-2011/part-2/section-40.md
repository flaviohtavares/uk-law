# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 40

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Filing[^key-11487bba35fcb22e16060dc211470665]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The application, and its accompanying documents, must be filed with the court, together with enough copies of the application and accompanying documents for service under rule 41.

(2) Each filed copy—

- (a) shall have the seal of the court applied to it;
- (b) shall be endorsed with the date and time of filing;
- (c) shall be endorsed with the venue for the hearing of the application (fixed by the court under rule 43); and
- (d) shall be issued to the Bank of England.
