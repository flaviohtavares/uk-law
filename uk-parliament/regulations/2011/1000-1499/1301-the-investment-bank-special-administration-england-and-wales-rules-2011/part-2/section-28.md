# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 28

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Authentication of administrator’s appointment[^c-35dd5bed49cc87e7d74e4c2ccd8069f5]

*Extent: E+W*

A sealed copy of the court’s order may in any proceedings be adduced as proof that the person appointed is duly authorised to exercise the powers and perform the duties of the administrator in the special administration (bank insolvency).
