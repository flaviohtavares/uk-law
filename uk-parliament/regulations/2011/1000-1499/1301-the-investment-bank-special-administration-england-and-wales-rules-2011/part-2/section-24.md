# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 24

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Leave for the applicant to withdraw[^key-3e4809f37dd5abe8d9f9ba55e75b4ba9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The applicant may withdraw the application for a special administration (bank insolvency) order at any time before the hearing with the permission of the court.

(2) An application for permission under paragraph (1) may be made without notice.

(3) The court may grant permission on such terms as the court thinks fit.
