# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 11

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Proof of service[^key-42478816db416811bef15d0e3ecc0d31]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Service of the application shall be verified by a witness statement specifying the date and time on which, and the manner in which, service was effected.

(2) The witness statement, with a sealed copy of the application exhibited to it, shall be filed with the court—

- (a) as soon as is reasonably practicable; and
- (b) in any event, before the hearing of the application.
