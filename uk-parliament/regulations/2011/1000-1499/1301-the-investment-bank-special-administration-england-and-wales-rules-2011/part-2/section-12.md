# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 12

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Further notification[^c-7ba8130f5e4e3c10ba954d74186bdba8]

*Extent: E+W*

As soon as reasonably practicable after filing the application, the applicant must notify—

- (a) any enforcement officer or other officer whom the applicant knows to be charged with effecting an execution or other legal process against the investment bank or its property;
- (b) any person whom the applicant knows to have distrained against the investment bank or its property; [^c-364ae7a5afa98ab89b532dcfb2e2f03e]...
- (c)[^c-46418dbcb61682d2322daefa64808bb6] the FCA (if not the applicant); and
- (d) if the application relates to a PRA-authorised person, the PRA (if not the applicant).
