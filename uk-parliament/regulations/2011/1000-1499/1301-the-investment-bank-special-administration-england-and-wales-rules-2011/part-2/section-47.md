# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 47

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 47. Notice of order[^c-d6b8e88e29fe3173403469e32d977076]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the court makes a special administration (bank administration) order, it shall send 4 sealed copies to the Bank of England.

(2) The Bank of England shall as soon as is reasonably practicable send—

- (a) a sealed copy to the administrator;
- (b)[^c-6d89e1fddc2b4685ed435e6059d583e1] a sealed copy to the FCA;
- (ba) if the investment bank concerned is a PRA-authorised person, a sealed copy to the PRA;
- (c) a sealed copy to the FSCS.
