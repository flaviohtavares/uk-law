# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 156

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 156. Admission and rejection of proofs for dividend[^key-afe7dfeacc53c9614195a1951832263b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A proof may be admitted for dividend either for the whole amount claimed by the creditor, or for part of that amount.

(2) If the administrator rejects a proof in whole or in part, the administrator shall prepare a written statement of reasons for doing so, and send it as soon as reasonably practicable to the creditor.
