# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 149

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 149. Debts of investment bank to rank equally[^key-8596fca2f1f9cff6bd493c06c0cda523]

*Extent: E+W*

Debts, other than preferential debts, rank equally between themselves in the special administration and, after the preferential debts, shall be paid in full unless the assets are insufficient for meeting them, in which case they abate in equal proportions between themselves.
