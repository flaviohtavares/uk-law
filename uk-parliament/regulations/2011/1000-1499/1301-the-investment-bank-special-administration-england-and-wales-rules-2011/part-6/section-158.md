> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 158. Withdrawal or variation of proof[^key-3b5e1d451c28fc19266af2acee37e5e1]

*Extent: E+W*

A creditor’s proof may at any time, by agreement with the administrator, be withdrawn or varied as to the amount claimed.
