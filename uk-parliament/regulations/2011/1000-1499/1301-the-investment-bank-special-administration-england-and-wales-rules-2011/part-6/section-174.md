# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 174

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 174. Realisation of security by creditor[^key-ac4a7047e88c15644b8772b2bb9135c4]

*Extent: E+W*

If a creditor who has valued their security subsequently realises it (whether or not at the instance of the administrator)—

- (a) the net amount realised shall be substituted for the value previously put by the creditor on the security; and
- (b) that amount shall be treated in all respects as an amended valuation made by the creditor.
