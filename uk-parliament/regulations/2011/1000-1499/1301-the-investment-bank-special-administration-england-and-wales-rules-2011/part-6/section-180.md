# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 180

> Text as at 2018-02-26.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 180. Payments of dividend and related matters[^key-fbf9d97775fa62324d6d65605a292241]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The dividend may be distributed simultaneously with the notice declaring it.

(2) Payment of dividend may be made by post, or arrangements may be made with any creditor for it to be paid in another way, or held for collection.

(3) Where a dividend is paid on a bill of exchange or other negotiable instrument, the amount of the dividend shall be endorsed on the instrument, or on a certified copy of it, if required to be produced by the holder for that purpose.
