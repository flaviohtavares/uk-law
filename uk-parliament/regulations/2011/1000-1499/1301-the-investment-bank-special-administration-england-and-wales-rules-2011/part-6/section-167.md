# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 167

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 167. Payments of a periodical nature[^key-94884a47dfa37d555f3a3ae6c4bd7a77]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the case of rent and other payments of a periodical nature, the creditor may prove for any amounts due and unpaid up to the date when the investment bank entered special administration.

(2) Where at that date any payment was accruing due, the creditor may prove for so much as would have fallen due at that date, if accruing from day to day.
