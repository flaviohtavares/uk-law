# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 153

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 153. Costs of proving[^c-c7c7deacc40ec4a67c77a86af26951e4]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless the court otherwise orders—

- (a) every creditor bears the cost of proving their own debt, including costs incurred in providing documents or evidence under rule 152; and
- (b) costs incurred by the administrator in estimating the quantum of a debt under rule 160 are payable out of the assets as an expense of the administration.
