# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 163

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 163. Discounts[^c-bf98f095a427676fd2dc95d4879f147b]

*Extent: E+W*

There shall in every case be deducted from the claim all trade and other discounts which would have been available to the investment bank but for it going into special administration, except any discount for immediate, early or cash settlement.
