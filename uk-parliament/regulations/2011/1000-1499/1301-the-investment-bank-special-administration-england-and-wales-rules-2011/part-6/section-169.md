# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 169

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 169. Debt payable at a future time[^c-42380eb5eb948a53027ac9c42a9be549]

*Extent: E+W*

A creditor may prove for a debt of which payment was not yet due on the date when the investment bank entered special administration, subject to rule 186.
