# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 171

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 171. Surrender for non-disclosure[^c-67e66a061f6d9489b4763dcb1a15f684]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a secured creditor omits to disclose their security in their proof of debt, the creditor shall surrender their security for the general benefit of creditors, unless the court, on application by that creditor, relieves them from the effect of this rule on the ground that the omission was inadvertent or the result of honest mistake.

(2) If the court grants that relief, it may require or allow the creditor’s proof of debt to be amended, on such terms as may be just.
