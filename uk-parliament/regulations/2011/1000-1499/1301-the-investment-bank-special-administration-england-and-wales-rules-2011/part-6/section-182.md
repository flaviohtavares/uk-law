# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 182

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 182. Proof altered after payment of dividend[^key-40a95005f7d7caf59bb4d4828399650e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If after payment of dividend the amount claimed by a creditor in their proof is increased, the creditor is not entitled to disturb the distribution of the dividend; but is entitled to be paid, out of any money for the time being available for the payment of any further dividend, any dividend or dividends which that creditor has failed to receive.

(2) Any dividend or dividends payable under paragraph (1) shall be paid before the money there referred to is applied to the payment of any such further dividend.

(3) If, after a creditor’s proof has been admitted, the proof is withdrawn or expunged, or the amount is reduced, the creditor is liable to repay to the administrator any amount overpaid by way of dividend.
