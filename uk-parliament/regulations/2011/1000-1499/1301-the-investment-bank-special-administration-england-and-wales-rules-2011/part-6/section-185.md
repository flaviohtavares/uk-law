# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 185

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 185. Assignment of right to dividend[^c-920e9c3091a6b85d05ee8a95da9c3059]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a person, entitled to a dividend, gives notice to the administrator that they wish the dividend to be paid to another person, or that they have assigned that entitlement to another person, the administrator shall pay the dividend to that other accordingly.

(2) A notice given under this rule must specify the name and address of the person to whom payment is to be made.
