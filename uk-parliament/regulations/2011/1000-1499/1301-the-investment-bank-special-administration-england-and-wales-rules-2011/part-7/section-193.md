# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 193

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 193. Interest in property to be declared on request[^key-21f10120fdd8d0ca1f19649d9dab3104]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If, in the case of property which the administrator has the right to disclaim, it appears to the administrator that there is some person ‘P’ who claims, or may claim, to have an interest in the property, the administrator may give notice to P calling on that person to declare within 14 days whether P claims any such interest and, if so, the nature and extent of it.

(2) Failing compliance with the notice, the administrator is entitled to assume that P has no such interest in the property as will prevent or impede its disclaimer.
