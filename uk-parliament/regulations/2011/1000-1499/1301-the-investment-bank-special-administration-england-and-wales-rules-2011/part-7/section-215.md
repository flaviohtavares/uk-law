> Text as at 2018-02-26.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 215. Notification and advertisement of appointment of replacement administrator[^key-bf04d20f9d972ca75895d091313322c9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a replacement administrator is appointed, the same provisions apply in respect of giving notice of, and advertising, the replacement appointment as in the case of the appointment, subject to rule 218.

(2) All statements, consents etc as are required shall also be required in the case of the appointment of a replacement.

(3) All notices shall clearly identify that the appointment is of a replacement administrator.
