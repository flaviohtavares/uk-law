# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 190

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 190. Additional notices[^c-f3979260cb8fba1283cd4acb68e14fb4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The administrator disclaiming property may at any time send or give copies of the notice of the disclaimer to any persons who in the administrator’s opinion ought, in the public interest or otherwise, to be informed of the disclaimer.

(2) Paragraph (1) is without prejudice to the administrator’s obligations under sections 178 to 180 of the 1986 Act and rules 188 and 189.
