# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 194

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 194. Disclaimer presumed valid and effective[^key-993cc63633530f4a35e841e8d3018682]

*Extent: E+W*

Any disclaimer of property by the administrator is presumed valid and effective, unless it is proved that the administrator has been in breach of their duty with respect to the giving of notice of disclaimer, or otherwise, under sections 178 to 180 of the 1986 Act or under this Chapter of the Rules.
