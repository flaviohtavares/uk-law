# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 83

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 83. Suspension[^c-95f7196f4a5b766c8cca3f200b123098]

*Extent: E+W*

Once only in the course of a meeting, the chair may, without an adjournment, declare it suspended for any period up to 1 hour.
