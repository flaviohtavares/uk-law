# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 107

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 107. The chair at meetings[^key-56790eaf0ca56b6a7ec27781e6887790]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The chair at any meeting of the creditors’ committee must be the administrator, or a person appointed by the administrator in writing to act.

(2) A person so appointed must be either—

- (a) one who is qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the investment bank; or
- (b) an employee of the administrator or the administrator’s firm who is experienced in insolvency matters.
