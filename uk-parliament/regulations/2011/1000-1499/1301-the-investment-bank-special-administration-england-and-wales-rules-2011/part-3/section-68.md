# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 68

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 68. Meetings generally[^c-ea00011936428b171336e2087e744e9b]

*Extent: E+W*

This chapter, except where different provision is made in the Regulations or these Rules, applies to meetings summoned by the administrator under—

- (a) paragraph 51 (initial meeting);
- (b) paragraph 54(2) (meeting to consider revision to the administrator’s proposals);
- (c) paragraph 62 (general power to summon meetings),

or following a request or a direction from the court under paragraph 56 (further creditors’ meetings).
