# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 114

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 114. Procedure at meetings[^c-c2f27ed60b38b4d0b65ac6cfcfa2171f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) At any meeting of the creditors’ committee, each member of it (whether present or represented) has one vote, and a resolution is passed when a majority of the members present or represented have voted in favour of it.

(2) Every resolution passed must be recorded in writing and authenticated by the chair, either separately or as part of the minutes of the meeting, and the record must be kept with the records of the proceedings.
