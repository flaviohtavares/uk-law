# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 112

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 112. Removal[^c-340d5a9bf8a5e2164111c0b64b855651]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A member of the committee may be removed by resolution at a meeting of creditors and clients, at least 14 days’ notice having been given of the intention to move that resolution.

(2) The resolution in paragraph (1) will be voted on only by the relevant class of voter in respect of the member to be removed.
