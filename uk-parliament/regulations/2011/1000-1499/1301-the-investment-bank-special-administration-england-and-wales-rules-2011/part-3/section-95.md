# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 95

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 95. Requisite majorities at contributories’ meetings[^key-df6de9bdc5782778f90490c2e3ab6fac]

*Extent: E+W*

At a meeting of contributories, voting rights are as at a general meeting of the investment bank, subject to any provision of the articles affecting entitlement to vote, either generally or at a time when the investment bank is in liquidation.
