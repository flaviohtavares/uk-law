# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 113

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 113. Vacancies[^key-b6c9ce8f0f33001c99d8c5c442a18b9d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following applies if there is a vacancy in the membership of the creditors’ committee.

(2) The vacancy need not be filled if the administrator and a majority of the remaining members of the committee so agree, provided that—

- (a) the total number of members does not fall below 3; and
- (b) the administrator thinks that the make-up of the committee will continue to reflect all parties with an interest in the achievement of the special administration objectives.

(3) The administrator may appoint a person (being qualified under these Rules to be a member of the committee) from the same class of voters as the previous member to fill the vacancy, if—

- (a) a majority of the other members of the committee agree to the appointment; and
- (b) the person concerned consents to act.
