# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 132

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 132. Application in a special administration (bank administration)[^key-31475b208a81393fe109da9acd52e583]

*Extent: E+W*

If an application referred to in rule 131(1) is made before the Bank of England has given an Objective A Achievement Notice—

- (a) the administrator must notify the Bank of England of the time and place of the hearing;
- (b) the Bank of England may appear at the hearing;
- (c) if an order is made, the administrator must send a copy to the Bank of England as soon as is reasonably practicable.
