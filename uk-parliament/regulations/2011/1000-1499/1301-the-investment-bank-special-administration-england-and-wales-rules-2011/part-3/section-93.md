## 93. Voting at meetings of creditors and clients[^c-d769a7247838c6f12805317102e93afd]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies to meetings of creditors and clients.

(2) If the administrator thinks it appropriate, the creditors and clients may vote on the same resolution at the meeting, however the creditors and the clients shall vote separately on the resolution.

(3) In a special administration (bank insolvency) the FSCS shall be entitled to vote as a creditor under this rule and rule 86 has effect with respect to its voting rights.
