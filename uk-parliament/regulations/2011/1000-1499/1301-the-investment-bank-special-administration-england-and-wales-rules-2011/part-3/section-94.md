# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 94

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 94. Requisite majorities[^key-49137a5a40cb8bf9db994025c844660a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), at a meeting of creditors or clients, or of creditors and clients, a resolution is passed when a majority (in value) of those present and voting, in person or by proxy, have voted in favour of it.

(2) Any resolution is invalid if those voting against it include more than half in value of the creditors, or, as the case may be, clients, to whom notice of the meeting was sent and who are not, to the best of the chair’s belief, persons connected with the investment bank.
