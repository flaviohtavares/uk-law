# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 62

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Notice to officers[^c-c05977fbf6df93a1e96efaa41a81b959]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where rule 61 applies, notice to attend the meeting must be given to every present or former officer of the investment bank whose presence the administrator thinks is required at the same time that notice is sent to creditors and clients.

(2) That notice must contain—

- (a) a statement that the proceedings are being held in the High Court and the court reference number;
- (b) the full name, registered address, registered number and any other trading names of the investment bank;
- (c) the full name and business address of the administrator; and
- (d) details of the venue of the meeting.

(3) Every person who receives a notice under paragraph (1) must attend.
