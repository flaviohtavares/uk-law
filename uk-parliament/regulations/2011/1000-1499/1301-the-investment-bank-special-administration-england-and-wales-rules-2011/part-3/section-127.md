# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 127

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 127. Retention of proxies[^c-9d651b955dcd329f958bbcc144286a91]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject as follows, proxies used for voting at any meeting shall be retained by the chair of the meeting.

(2) The chair shall deliver the proxies, as soon as reasonably practicable after the meeting, to the administrator (where the administrator is someone other than the chair).
