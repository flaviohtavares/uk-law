# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 71

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71. Notice of meeting by individual notice: content and accompanying documents[^key-17aa846abb1db31128dd5a7a43c6e37e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies except where the court orders under rule 72 that notice of a meeting be given by advertisement only.

(2) Notice summoning a meeting must specify the purpose of and venue for the meeting and state that claims or proofs and (if applicable) proxies must be lodged at a specified place not later than 12.00 hours on the business day before the date fixed for the meeting in order that creditors or clients may be entitled to vote at the meeting.

(3) Forms of proxy complying with rule 125 must be sent out with every notice summoning a meeting.
