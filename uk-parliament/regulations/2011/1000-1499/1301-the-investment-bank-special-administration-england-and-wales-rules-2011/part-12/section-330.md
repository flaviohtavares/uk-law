# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 330

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 330. Venue[^key-62a47cbe7e4f9858e2a46c5ca618e7a8]

*Extent: E+W*

References to the “venue” for any proceeding or attendance before the court, or for a meeting, are to the time, date and place for the proceeding, attendance or meeting or to the time and date for a meeting which is held in accordance with section 246A without any place being specified for it.
