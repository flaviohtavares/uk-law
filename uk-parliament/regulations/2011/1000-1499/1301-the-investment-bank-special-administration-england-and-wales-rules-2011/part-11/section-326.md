# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 326

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 326. Proceedings commenced in the wrong court[^c-276ebfe528e1173148f0ce8cbf2c1100]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a special administration is commenced in a court other than the High Court, that court may order the transfer of the proceedings to the High Court.
