# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 319

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 319. Notifications made to other persons at the same time[^c-1d6247c10e5dc6380389f1a1f52ad766]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where under the Regulations or the Rules a notice or other document is to be sent to another person at the same time that it is to be sent to the registrar of companies, that requirement may be satisfied by sending to that other person a copy of the notification sent to the registrar.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply—

- (a) where additional information is prescribed for the notification to the other person; or
- (b) where the notification to the registrar of companies is incomplete.
