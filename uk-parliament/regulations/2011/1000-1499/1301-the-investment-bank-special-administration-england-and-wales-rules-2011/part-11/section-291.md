# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 291

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 291. Application[^key-61cfbb8b8d8b80187eafac3ef0b3544c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), this Chapter applies where a notice or other document is required to be given, delivered or sent under the Regulations or the Rules.

(2) This Chapter does not apply to the submission of documents to the registrar of companies.

(3) Rules 295 to 298 do not apply to the filing of any notice or other document with the court.
