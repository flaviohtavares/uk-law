# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 300

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 300. Notice etc to joint administrators[^c-f8b34b6bb9dc2336d3eaa9c9b4d43ada]

*Extent: E+W*

Where there are joint office-holders in a special administration, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to all of them.
