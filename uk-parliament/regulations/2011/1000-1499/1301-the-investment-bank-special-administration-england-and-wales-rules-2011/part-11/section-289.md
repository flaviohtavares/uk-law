# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 289

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 289. Postal delivery of documents[^c-5ba4bd0af699d7687608fe779d152224]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless in any particular case some other form of delivery is required by the Regulations or the Rules or an order of the court, a notice or other document may be sent by post in accordance with the rules for postal service in CPR Part 6 and sending by such means has effect as specified in those rules.
