# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 290

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 290. Notice etc to solicitors[^key-f2e11b014f8fc5b60557dcef6023809d]

*Extent: E+W*

Where under the Regulations or the Rules a notice or other document is required or authorised to be given, delivered or sent to a person, it may be given, delivered or sent instead to a solicitor authorised to accept delivery on that person’s behalf.
