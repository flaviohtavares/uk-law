# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 302

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 302. Notice to enforcement officers[^key-83d29a6c95fc2ea22506cbcc27103f8b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies in relation to any provision of the Regulations or the Rules which makes provision for the giving of notice to an enforcement officer.

(2) Any such notice as is mentioned in paragraph (1) may be given by electronic means to any person who has been authorised to receive such notice on behalf of a specified enforcement officer or on behalf of enforcement officers generally.
