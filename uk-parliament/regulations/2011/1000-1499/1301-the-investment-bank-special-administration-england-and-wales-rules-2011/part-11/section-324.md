# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 324

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 324. Time limits[^c-2993c2acf4fbda99ff038a5d40fcc0d1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The provisions of CPR rule 2.8[^f00025] (time) apply, as regards computation of time, to anything required or authorised to be done by the Rules.

(2) The provisions of CPR rule 3.1(2)(a) (the court’s general powers of management) apply so as to enable the court to extend or shorten the time for compliance with anything required or authorised to be done by the Rules.
