> Text as at 2018-02-26.

## 316. Notifications relating to court orders[^c-43932304b95b1ed0fdc41d37f5f64947]

*Extent: E+W*

In addition to the information required by rule 313, a notification relating to a court order must also specify—

- (a) the nature of the court order; and
- (b) the date of the order.
