# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 285

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 285. False claim of status as creditor, etc[^key-d1491ba3b142d786057d32414324504e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies where the Rules provide a right for—

- (a) creditors;
- (b) clients;
- (c) members of the investment bank; or
- (d) contributories,

to inspect any documents, whether on the court’s file or in the hands of the administrator or other person.

(2) It is an offence for a person (‘P’), with the intention of obtaining a sight of documents which P has not under the Rules any right to inspect, falsely to claim a status which would entitle P to inspect them.

(3) A person guilty of an offence under this rule is liable to imprisonment or a fine, or both.
