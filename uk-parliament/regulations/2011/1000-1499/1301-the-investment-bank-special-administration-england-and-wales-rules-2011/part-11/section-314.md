# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 314

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 314. Notification relating to the administrator[^c-f7cf2a2047988440b53e8919bcd3e42b]

*Extent: E+W*

In addition to the information required by rule 313, a notification relating to the office of the administrator must also specify—

- (a) the name and business address of the administrator;
- (b) the date of the event notified;
- (c) where the notification relates to an appointment, the person, body or court making the appointment; and
- (d) where the notification relates to the termination of an appointment, the reason for that termination (for example, resignation).
