# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 321

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 321. Right to copy documents[^c-3e09549c7dff7e6bc2eef0ac4475ac65]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the Regulations or the Rules confer a right for any person to inspect documents, the right includes that of taking copies of those documents, on payment—

- (a) in the case of documents on the court’s file of proceedings, of the fee chargeable under any order made under section 92 of the Courts Act 2003[^f00024]; and
- (b) in any other case, of the appropriate fee.
