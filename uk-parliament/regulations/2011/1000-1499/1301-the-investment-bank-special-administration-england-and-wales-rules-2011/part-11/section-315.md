# The Investment Bank Special Administration (England and Wales) Rules 2011, section 315

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 315. Notifications relating to documents[^key-069dfa2c027dbe4b74b7765063883e03]

*Extent: E+W*

In addition to the information required by rule 313, a notification relating to a document (for example, a statement of affairs) must also specify—

- (a) the nature of the document; and
- (b) the date of the document; or
- (c) where the document relates to a period of time (for example a report) the period of time to which the document relates.
