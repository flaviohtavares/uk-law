# The Inshore Fisheries and Conservation (Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2011

> Text as at 2011-03-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2010/2188](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2188) and [S.I. 2010/2213](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2213) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of those Statutory Instruments.

2011 No. 177

Fisheries

The Inshore Fisheries and Conservation (Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2011

Made

27th January 2011

Laid before Parliament

1st February 2011

Coming into force

7th March 2011

The Secretary of State, after consulting the bodies specified in section 152(2) of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^f00001], makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 152(1) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Inshore Fisheries and Conservation (Miscellaneous Amendments) Order 2011.

(2) This Order comes into force on 7th March 2011.

## 2. Amendment to the Cornwall Inshore Fisheries and Conservation Order 2010

(1) Article 16 of the Cornwall Inshore Fisheries and Conservation Order 2010[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraph (1).

## 3. Amendment to the Isles of Scilly Inshore Fisheries and Conservation Order 2010

(1) Article 16 of the Isles of Scilly Inshore Fisheries and Conservation Order 2010[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) Omit paragraph (1).

Richard Benyon

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

27th January 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects a defect in the Cornwall Inshore Fisheries and Conservation Order 2010 and in the Isles of Scilly Inshore Fisheries and Conservation Order 2010.

No impact assessment has been prepared in relation to this order as it has no impact on the cost of business.
