# The Electronic Money Regulations 2011, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/99
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Reporting requirements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An electronic money issuer must give the Authority such information in respect of its issuance of electronic money and provision of payment services and its compliance with requirements imposed by or under Parts 2 to 5 of these Regulations as the Authority may direct.

(1A)[^key-d9d96676371cd0936fa7650ef109e7d0] An electronic money institution must give the Authority such information in respect of its compliance with requirements imposed by or under section 312R of the 2000 Act as the Authority may direct.

(2) Information required under this regulation must be given at such times and in such form, and verified in such manner, as the Authority may direct.

(3)[^key-d19b78efe7b385b22f2b17942e0fae46] A direction under this regulation must specify the purpose for which the information is required, as appropriate, and the time within which the information is to be given.
