# The Electronic Money Regulations 2011, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/99

## 61. Authority's exemption from liability in damages[^key-904149e0f9db63595de1a29592836a45]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The functions of the Authority under these Regulations are to be treated for the purposes of paragraph 25 (exemption from liability in damages) of Part 4 of Schedule 1ZA to the[^key-ac29e612089544c09c884b9335874c49] 2000 Act as functions conferred on the Authority under that Act.
