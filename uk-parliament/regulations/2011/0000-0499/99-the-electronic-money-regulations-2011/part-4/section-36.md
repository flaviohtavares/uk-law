# The Electronic Money Regulations 2011, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/99
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Reliance

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where an electronic money institution relies on a third party for the performance of operational functions it must take all reasonable steps to ensure that these Regulations and the Payment Services Regulations 2017[^c-86a51a3054016d987ab67852fa7f0f73] are complied with.

(2) Without prejudice to paragraph (1), an electronic money institution is responsible, to the same extent as if it had expressly permitted it, for anything done or omitted by any of its employees or by a distributor, agent, branch or any other entity issuing, distributing or redeeming electronic money, or providing payment services, on its behalf or to which activities are outsourced.
