# The Electronic Money Regulations 2011, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/99
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Prohibition of interest

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An electronic money issuer must not award—

- (a) interest in respect of the holding of electronic money; or
- (b) any other benefit related to the length of time during which an electronic money holder holds electronic money.
