# The Electronic Money Regulations 2011, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/99
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Application for authorisation if requirements cease to be met[^key-c179a8ea6c468b18f23bb49a579192c8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a small electronic money institution ceases to comply with a condition in regulation 13(3), (4), (8) or (9)[^key-5f645488a5a8e681dfa1cf175b1ba1b5], the institution concerned must, within 30 days of becoming aware of the change in circumstances, apply to become an authorised electronic money institution under regulation 5 if it intends to continue issuing electronic money in the United Kingdom.
