## 24A. Insolvency Regulations[^c-90f2f4873c16319bace629d4f9e4cc81]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 93(4) and 233 to 236 of the Banking Act 2009 apply to authorised electronic money institutions and small electronic money institutions with the modifications set out in Schedule 2ZA.
