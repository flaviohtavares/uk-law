# The Electronic Money Regulations 2011, section 73

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/99
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Prohibition on contracting-out

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A term contained in an agreement between an electronic money issuer and an electronic money holder or a payment service user is void if, and to the extent that, it is inconsistent with a provision for the protection of an electronic money holder or a payment service user contained in these Regulations or the Payment Services Regulations 2017[^c-36a2efba3131f0fe72bf3d9ae1528fce].
