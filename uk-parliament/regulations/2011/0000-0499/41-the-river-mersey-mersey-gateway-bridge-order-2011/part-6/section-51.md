# The River Mersey (Mersey Gateway Bridge) Order 2011, section 51

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 51. Saving for Trinity House and protection of navigation[^key-3d0700822c4e24f1e351782a12d2feb5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Nothing in this Order shall prejudice or derogate from any of the powers, rights or privileges, or the jurisdiction or authority of Trinity House.

(2) Schedule 8 (protection of navigation) shall have effect.
