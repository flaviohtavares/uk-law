# The River Mersey (Mersey Gateway Bridge) Order 2011, section 59

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 59. Certification of plans, etc.[^c-b2d458529354489e4e27a00dc72a70fd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker shall, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the book of reference, the sections, the works plans, the land plans, the rights of way plans, the open space and exchange land plans to the Secretary of State for certification that they are, respectively, true copies of the book of reference, sections, works plans, land plans, rights of way plans, the open space and exchange land plans referred to in this Order; and a document so certified shall be admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
