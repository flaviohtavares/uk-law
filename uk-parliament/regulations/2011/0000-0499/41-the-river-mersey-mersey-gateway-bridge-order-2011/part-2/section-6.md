# The River Mersey (Mersey Gateway Bridge) Order 2011, section 6

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. Restriction on works in the River Mersey and saltmarshes[^key-46ad2aa203c2e369215881445bb90f6b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regardless of the provisions of article 5 (power to deviate), no permanent work or part of such work shall be constructed in the River Mersey except in those areas shaded grey on the works plans.
