# The Seed Marketing Regulations 2011, section 2

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 2. Meaning of “marketing”

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

(1) In these Regulations “marketing” means the sale, holding with a view to sale, offer for sale or any disposal, supply or transfer aimed in each case at commercial exploitation of seed to third parties, whether or not for consideration.

(2) But marketing does not include trade that is not aimed at commercial exploitation, such as—

- (a) the supply of seed to official testing and inspection bodies; or
- (b) the supply of seed to a person who provides processing services but who does not acquire title to the seed.
