# The Seed Marketing Regulations 2011, section 31

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 31. Confidentiality

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

If the breeder so requests, the description of genealogical components of seed must not be disclosed by the Secretary of State.
