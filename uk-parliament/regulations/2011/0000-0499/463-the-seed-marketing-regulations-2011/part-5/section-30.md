# The Seed Marketing Regulations 2011, section 30

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 30. Marketing seed under a specific derogation

*Extent: E+W+S+NI*

The Secretary of State may temporarily permit the marketing of seed not satisfying the requirements of minimum germination under conditions determined in accordance with[^key-77bbd096b7fd2422066b7d4e2214e62f] [Commission Regulation (EC) No 217/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/0217) laying down rules for the application of Council Directives [66/401/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1966/0401), [66/402/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1966/0402), [2002/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0054), [2002/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0055) and [2002/57/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0057) as regards the authorisation of Member States to permit temporarily the marketing of seed not satisfying the requirements in respect of the minimum germination[^f00014].
