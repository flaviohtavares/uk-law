# The School Governance (Contracts) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 1500

Education, England

The School Governance (Contracts) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 2011

Made

15th June 2011

Laid before Parliament

20th June 2011

Coming into force

20th July 2011

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 4(a) of Schedule 1 to the Education Act 2002[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the School Governance (Contracts) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 2011 and come into force on 20th July 2011.

## 2. Revocation

The School Governance (Contracts) (England) Regulations 2005[^f00002] are revoked.

Nick Gibb

Minister of State

Department for Education

15th June 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the School Governance (Contracts) (England) Regulations 2005 which require the governing bodies of maintained schools to have regard to the Code of Practice on Workforce Matters in Public Sector Service Contracts (commonly referred to as the “Two-Tier Code”) when entering into a contract which involves a transfer of staff . This follows a decision by the Government to withdraw the Code.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
