# The North Somerset Levels Internal Drainage Board Order 2011

> Text as at 2011-04-14.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2011 No. 1662

Land Drainage, England

The North Somerset Levels Internal Drainage Board Order 2011

Made

14th April 2011

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

Under section 3(1)(a) of the Land Drainage Act 1991[^f00001], the Environment Agency has prepared a Scheme making provision for the amalgamation of the North Somerset (2005) Internal Drainage District and the West Mendip Internal Drainage District.

In accordance with section 3(1) of that Act, the Environment Agency has submitted the Scheme to the Secretary of State for confirmation.

The Secretary of State has published a notice of intent to make the following Order confirming the Scheme in accordance with paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3 to that Act. The Secretary of State has sent this notice to the relevant local authorities and other bodies specified in paragraph 2(2) of that Schedule.

No objection has been made to the draft Order.

Accordingly, the Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(5) and (7) of that Act and now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the North Somerset Levels Internal Drainage Board Order 2011 and, if confirmed by the Secretary of State in accordance with paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 3 to the Land Drainage Act 1991, comes into force in accordance with that paragraph.

## 2. Confirmation of the Scheme

(1) The Scheme submitted by the Environment Agency is confirmed.

(2) The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## 3. Secretary of State’s expenses

The expenses of the Secretary of State in connection with the making and confirmation of this Order must be borne by the Environment Agency.

Grant Horsburgh

A Civil Servant, for and on behalf of the

Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

## SCHEDULE — SCHEME SUBMITTED BY THE ENVIRONMENT AGENCY

Article 2(2)

**1.** This Scheme comes into force on the day after the day on which the Order confirming this Scheme is confirmed.

**2.** In this Scheme—

- “the abolished boards” means the North Somerset (2005) Internal Drainage Board and the West Mendip Internal Drainage Board;
- “the commencement date” means the date on which this Scheme comes into force;
- “the new Board” means the internal drainage board constituted by virtue of paragraph 3(3) of this Scheme;
- “the North Somerset (2005) Internal Drainage Board” means the internal drainage board constituted by the scheme confirmed by the North Somerset Internal Drainage Board Order 2005[^f00003] and “the North Somerset (2005) Internal Drainage District” means the internal drainage district constituted by that scheme as confirmed;
- “property” means, in relation to any of the abolished boards, any property which is vested in that abolished board immediately before the commencement date and includes books of account, other books, deeds, maps, papers and other documents, in whatever medium held, and computer and other electronic records;
- “rights and obligations” means, in relation to any of the abolished boards, all rights, powers, duties, obligations and liabilities which are vested in or which fall to be discharged by that abolished board immediately before the commencement date; and
- “the West Mendip Internal Drainage Board” means the internal drainage board constituted by the scheme confirmed by the Somerset Rivers Catchment Board (Gordano Valley, North Somerset, West Mendip, Upper Axe, Lower Axe and Upper Brue Internal Drainage Districts) Order 1933[^f00004] and “the West Mendip Internal Drainage District” means the internal drainage district constituted by that scheme, as confirmed.

**3.** (1) The North Somerset (2005) Internal Drainage Board and the West Mendip Internal Drainage Board are abolished.

(2) The North Somerset (2005) Internal Drainage District and the West Mendip Internal Drainage District are amalgamated into, and are constituted as, one internal drainage district to be known as “the North Somerset Levels Internal Drainage District”.

(3) A new internal drainage board to be known as “the North Somerset Levels Internal Drainage Board” is constituted for the North Somerset Levels Internal Drainage District.

**4.** The new Board is to consist of 14 elected members elected in accordance with the provisions made by or under Schedule 1 to the Land Drainage Act 1991.

**5.** As from the commencement date, all property and rights and obligations of the abolished boards are transferred to and vested in, or fall to be discharged by, the new Board.

**6.** All arrears of rates levied by the abolished boards before the commencement date in respect of any period ending before the commencement date may be recovered by the new Board, in the same manner as if they had been rates levied by the new Board.

**7.** This Scheme operates as conclusive evidence of any thing transferred under this Scheme without the necessity of further assignments, conveyance or deed of transfer.

**8.** The accounts of each of the abolished boards must be made up to the day before the commencement date by the new Board and audited as if this Scheme had not come into force.

Dated 24 August 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order confirms a Scheme submitted by the Environment Agency for the amalgamation of the North Somerset (2005) Internal Drainage District and the West Mendip Internal Drainage District into one new internal drainage district (“the North Somerset Levels Internal Drainage District”). The North Somerset (2005) Internal Drainage Board and the West Mendip Internal Drainage Board are abolished and a new internal drainage board (“the North Somerset Levels Internal Drainage Board”) is constituted for the new internal drainage district.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
