# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 80B

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80B. 2016 cohort students with full entitlement who satisfy the conditions in regulation 71(1)(h)[^c-43430d0a02d11d10d1203287dcce2eaa]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies to a 2016 cohort student who satisfies all the conditions in regulation 71(1)(h).

(2) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part and paragraphs (6) and (7)[^c-192c843af234b92655c09678b2913d92], the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year other than the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986)

where—

- X is—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £10,757[^c-ea97acea3aba474cd0c4ddeaf41b5ea9];
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £15,415[^c-f4e35e39b2f0dcf734f8281c34535b1a];
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £13,806[^c-26e68e0aafad82deb23739600c35c288];
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £12,345[^c-0c30ccc4928ac8dd6f03afb8c9ee94aa];
- Y is—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £1 for every complete £4.088 by which the student’s household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £42,875;
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £1 for every complete £4.37 by which the student’s household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £42,875;
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £1 for every complete £4.265 by which the student’s household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £42,875;
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £1 for every complete £4.179 by which the student’s household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £42,875;
- Z is—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £1 for every complete £6.54 by which the student’s household income exceeds £42,875;
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £1 for every complete £6.36 by which the student’s household income exceeds £42,875;
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £1 for every complete £6.41 by which the student’s household income exceeds £42,875;
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £1 for every complete £6.47 by which the student’s household income exceeds £42,875.

(3) The loan for living costs for an academic year other than the final year of a course that is not an intensive course, where regulation 71(1)(h) applies, is made up of a loan for maintenance and a special support loan, whereby the maximum loan for maintenance is—

- (a) for a student in category A, £6,175[^c-354fbf728ebe5677eff12d9efd16687a];
- (b) for a student in category B,£10,833[^c-5b97bb1a0d1edaa7c53cb9dfdc8f40b1];
- (c) for a student in category C, £9,224[^c-d4afe3c32f2024c40fda55cb82a122f2];
- (d) for a student in category D, £7,763[^c-7c45b49235188c40a1eda97906ec0b35];

and any remainder is a special support loan.

(4) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part and paragraphs (6) and (7)[^c-993f7a41015c776d6656531b45c1ddcb], the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986)

where—

- X is—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £10,255[^c-55f990fbef1534adb941ad041e52bc35];
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £14,447[^c-1d63708053fe2d1d463d92c5860f28b5];
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £12,597[^c-5fd70f355513dc24319d35651318d6fe];
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £11,800[^c-fddb81f0c7d46096a26c85c1eab738a0];
- Y is—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £1 for every complete £4.054 by which the student’s household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £42,875;
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £1 for every complete £4.295 by which the student’s household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £42,875;
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £1 for every complete £4.174 by which the student’s household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £42,875;
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £1 for every complete £4.137 by which the student’s household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £42,875;
- Z is—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £1 for every complete £6.54 by which the student’s household income exceeds £42,875;
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £1 for every complete £6.36 by which the student’s household income exceeds £42,875;
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £1 for every complete £6.41 by which the student’s household income exceeds £42,875;
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £1 for every complete £6.47 by which the student’s household income exceeds £42,875.

(5) The loan for living costs for an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course, where regulation 71(1)(h) applies, is made up of a loan for maintenance and a special support loan, whereby the maximum loan for maintenance is—

- (a) for a student in category A, £5,673[^c-931204948f1d7c2873335038f5a9b66c];
- (b) for a student in category B, £9,865[^c-5468d79a011875bd909c38f6e369df12];
- (c) for a student in category C, £8,015[^c-08661b4ef04e1b2cc77fdb3441d8f9ac];
- (d) for a student in category D, £7,218[^c-6bf33b4072801ded80391f06113dc5db];

and any remainder is a special support loan.

(6) Where the maximum amount of loan for living costs is calculated in accordance with paragraph (2) or (4) and is less than the minimum level for the academic year specified in regulation 105, the minimum level for the academic year is payable.

(7)[^c-471bf68df75b7f020c7ad3479e4f8ae9] Where this regulation applies to an eligible care leaver, the formula in each of paragraphs (2) and (4) is to be read as “X”.
