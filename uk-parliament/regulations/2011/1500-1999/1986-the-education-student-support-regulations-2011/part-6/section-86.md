# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 86

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 86. Students who are treated as in attendance on a course

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A student to whom this regulation applies is treated as being in attendance on the designated course for the purpose of qualifying for the loan for living costs or the long courses loan.

(2) This regulation applies to—

- (a) a[^c-deb37d1cbba4b6d9fb105bf675afbced] compressed degree student[^c-f4b238f530a812da1ccdfcecbd105cf1];
- (b) a student on a period of study or period of work placement in an Erasmus year;
- (c) a disabled student who—
  - (i) is not a compressed degree student[^c-2b128317c884a251b6b2c222becb391d]; and
  - (ii) is undertaking a designated course [^c-bb5dc41dbdbfe5d70e627c2c55739ffb]... in the United Kingdom but is not in attendance because the student is unable to attend for a reason which relates to the student's disability.
