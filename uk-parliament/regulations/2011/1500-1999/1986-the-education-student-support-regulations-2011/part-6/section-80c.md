# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 80C

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80C. 2016 cohort students with full entitlement who satisfy the conditions in regulation 71(1)(i)[^key-43430d0a02d11d10d1203287dcce2eaa]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies to a 2016 cohort student who satisfies both the conditions in regulation 71(1)(i).

(2) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part and paragraph (4)[^key-41a116ba7dd3070cbda43303d52dfc14], the loan for living costs is a special support loan and the maximum amount for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies is equal to—

[formula: X-Y](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986)

where—

- X is £4,582[^key-43cc740feb41adc8f1ae47339c028771];
- Y is £1 for every complete £4.16 by which the student’s household income exceeds £25,000.

(3) Where the amount of loan for living costs calculated under paragraph (2) is less than £50, the amount of loan for living costs payable is nil.

(4)[^key-f06516352c3561efd5066c400ada84f0] Where this regulation applies to an eligible care leaver, the formula in paragraph (2) is to be read as “X”.
