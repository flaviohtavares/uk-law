# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 88

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 88. Deductions from loans for living costs

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A deduction from the amount of loan for living costs calculated under this Part in respect of a current system student with full entitlement who is not a [^key-5479ada9dcb4932746648f4d50820700]... 2009 , 2012 or 2016[^key-42d194bca93cbd1b7c45723e9157235b] cohort student [^key-d45b1844d4c0f12120cb2ac177c83cdf]... may be made in accordance with Part 8.

(2) There is no deduction under Part 8 from the amount of loan for living costs calculated under this Part in respect of a [^key-a14f3c3f3f7c561e89ec6c310f92cb03]... 2009 , 2012 or 2016[^key-547a49d09c5b9aa3232ad147c10d271c] cohort student or a student with reduced entitlement.
