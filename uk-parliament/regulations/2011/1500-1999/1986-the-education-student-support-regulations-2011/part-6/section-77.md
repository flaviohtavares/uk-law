# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 77

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 77. Current system students with full entitlement who are not ... 2009, 2012 or 2016 cohort students[^c-f9f5a569973c9e7343a8c5537cbe7bac][^c-32ad8a6e43b0d5fb27756fc786aec7b0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies to a current system student with full entitlement (other than a [^c-e01513556e133021716e1eb1b9149bbb]... 2009, 2012 or 2016 cohort student).

(2) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year other than the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986)

where—

- X is—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £5,046[^c-f0f05243b1e976a2eb1d1cb74e4c44a0];
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £9,131[^c-9c1b5b7c54173c5bf831fec3594446e5];
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £7,768[^c-a34aa45f74869ac302f9b4bf4cddeb11];
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £6,520[^c-3697ec15ac4ed26037ba772ab0b5e1e7];
- Y is the maintenance grant amount.

(3) Subject to Chapter 4 of this Part, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986)

where—

- X is—
  - (i) for a student in category A, £4,558[^c-be4c0b34ebbd1e8ffefaf43ebb9a9c38];
  - (ii) for a student in category B, £8,312[^c-f0a68fa16f9f13f19f3891537d7b3876];
  - (iii) for a student in category C, £6,754[^c-056644e8c546bcdbc38294f09b6017d8];
  - (iv) for a student in category D, £6,031[^c-1236fdeb2bbc317508005dc88f8d891c];
- Y is the maintenance grant amount.
