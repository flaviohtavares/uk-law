# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 81

> Text as at 2026-09-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 81. Long courses loan

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A [^c-d5bc4e13aa9c3c88b7f2d4e490c5356e]... 2009 , 2012 or 2016[^c-da0326af59d2c4927df079610d1f6dc8] cohort student qualifies for a long courses loan in connection with the student's attendance on a designated course if the student—

- (a) qualifies for a loan for living costs under this Part;
- (b) is required to attend the course for a period exceeding 30 weeks and 3 days in an academic year; [^c-a0c6e5710d279a2c9b0464ee59b57949]...
- (c) is not a student with reduced entitlement; and[^c-232c99eb0f7258c6e16d57fe6e765e13]
- (d)[^c-8d9c201982e57d2ab4ea193637d17fc1] is not a 2016 cohort student who meets the conditions set out in regulation 71(1)(i).

(2) The maximum amount of the long courses loan is[formula: X + Y](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986) where—

- *X* is calculated in accordance with paragraph (3),
- *Y* is calculated in accordance with paragraph (4);

(3) Where a [^c-0d344e985e6ffba40913753e2e366d19]... 2009 , 2012 or 2016[^c-d116c79bdb4964ee3d5e36de68ae0099] cohort student is required to attend a course for a period exceeding 30 weeks and 3 days in an academic year, the number of weeks or part weeks of attendance in the academic year beyond 30 weeks and 3 days is multiplied by the relevant amount in paragraph (5);

(4) Where a [^c-bf7337105282fcac226a6a9b7523304b]... 2009 , 2012 or 2016[^c-3ed3722699220a88aa47c53ccc7edf53] cohort student attends a course for a period of not less than 45 weeks in any continuous period of 52 weeks, the total number of weeks in the 52-week period during which the student does not attend is multiplied by the relevant amount in paragraph (5);

(5) The amount is—

- (a) for a student in category A, £77[^c-415af3cb4d39d49313a31891f0e98cab];
- (b) for a student in category B, £149[^c-bff7d1304b66c5af3c14782005819bd9];
- (c) for a student in category C, £161[^c-b1c79fc93567f0a2101cc4a49c9487f5];
- (d) for a student in category D, £116[^c-2c1bdced6ec22ca344d72f71850b052e].

(6) Subject to paragraph 6A, a deduction[^c-fd5506bc3961f20aa2b5882205002712] from the amount of long courses loan calculated in accordance with this regulation may be made in accordance with Part 8.

(6A)[^c-fb49660dc27b59e3ef5fe8b5b0b1e9f3] Paragraph (6) does not apply in relation to a long courses loan for an eligible care leaver.

(7) For the purposes of calculating the maximum amount of the long courses loan, a student’s attendance on a pre-registration course or a postgraduate pre-registration course includes any time the student is required to spend, as part of that course, undertaking clinical training at a hospital or other premises.[^c-c176366153c68c7212109697097cba9e]
