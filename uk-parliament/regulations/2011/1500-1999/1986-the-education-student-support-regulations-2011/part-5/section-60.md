> Text as at 2026-09-01.

## 60. Amount of the maintenance grant – current system students who are not ... 2009, 2012 or 2016 cohort students[^c-f20f490d7b712d1dd27d56ee37c6ac79][^c-4f398b9ecec85a0d917ad82669b72b98]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-bd18b7ee18356ac4a96bdf5dc6c1803c] The maximum amount of maintenance grant available to a current system student who is not a [^c-1656d028c9af88dc2ce459974b2ee247]... 2009, 2012 or 2016 cohort student in respect of an academic year is £3,998[^c-af868be345ed7ea635d83b0ba5ceb97d].

(2)[^c-3a8f661c878d704d87bb29ff80a3f14b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-3a8f661c878d704d87bb29ff80a3f14b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-6848eee3fd538042c6fb66a619a7764f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) [^c-3648076c4cb3a702b8cfb1d92228f9c0]A current system student other than a ... 2009, 2012 or 2016 cohort student[^c-dabcfdf70f3f7d99093ebd27783ec2ed] who qualifies for a maintenance grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £18,360 or less, the student receives £3,998[^c-c2a2e526c69dd0d819165915b3aec518];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £18,360 but does not exceed £39,590[^c-a1e38eee9d03bc429f3212981866cbd0], the student receives an amount equal to[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986) where—

  *M* is £3,998

  *A* is £1 for every complete £4.34 by which the household income exceeds £18,360 but does not exceed £28,121;

  *B* is £1 for every complete £6.75 by which the household income exceeds £28,121 but does not exceed £39,590;
- (c) where the household income exceeds £39,590[^c-2d341cfc6c59e20eaf1de232e68341c7], no maintenance grant is payable.
