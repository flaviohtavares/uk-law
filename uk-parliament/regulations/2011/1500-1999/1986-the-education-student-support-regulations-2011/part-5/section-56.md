## 56. Qualifying conditions for the maintenance grant

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A current system student who is not a 2016 cohort student[^c-d3c5d7813a8412a8185418bcf6b759ec] qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a maintenance grant in connection with the student's attendance on a designated course (other than a distance learning course).

(2) A current system student does not qualify for a maintenance grant if the student qualifies for a special support grant.

(3) If a current system student does not qualify for a fee loan in respect of an academic year of the designated course, the student cannot qualify for a maintenance grant for that year unless the reason that the student does not qualify for a fee loan is that—

- (a) the year is an Erasmus year in accordance with regulation 19(2)[^c-26f38a2b1902f848e899b3e2848dff13].[^c-f15f6642d489d372b3ae3ade6963f70f]
- (b)[^c-5671a5de585e9dcee62017670413a74f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) A current system student does not qualify for a maintenance grant if the current course is the graduate entry accelerated programme.

(5)[^c-80254b6094dafffa730eb412108bae78] Subject to paragraph (6), a maintenance grant is payable to a current system student[^c-869823dbf2dfd7a8c0f6fa1cccd01733] in respect of the four quarters of the academic year.

(6)[^c-eac37a9f156ed2a6371dba2acfa4a5a8] Where any of the following events occur in the course of an academic year, a student may qualify for a maintenance grant in respect of such quarters of the academic year as begin after the relevant event occurs—

- (a) a course designation event which results in the student’s course becoming a designated course; or
- (b) an event falling within regulation 2A(2)(c), (d), (e) or (f), or 2A(3)(a), (b) or (c), which results in the student becoming an eligible student.
