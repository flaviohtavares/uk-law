## 59. Amount of the maintenance grant – 2012 cohort students

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The maximum amount of maintenance grant available to a 2012 cohort student in respect of an academic year is £4,473[^key-cec2938ce6652a58906891ee24e83b01].

(2) A 2012 cohort student who qualifies for a maintenance grant in respect of an academic year receives, subject to sub-paragraph (c), an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £25,000 or less, the student receives £4,473[^key-4092228bc8f224a3943548aacf1e087a];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £42,737[^key-162d79b19711387f82e2200a03a48999] the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986) where—

  *M* is £4,473[^key-7c0ccf0430dc7e68b52d924088291e92]

  *A* is £1 for every complete £4.01 by which the household income exceeds £25,000; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £42,737[^key-3b74aae14e4f155f855fa882f85b549f], no maintenance grant is payable.
