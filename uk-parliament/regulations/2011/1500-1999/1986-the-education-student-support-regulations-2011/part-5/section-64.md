## 64. Amount of the special support grant – 2012 cohort students

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The maximum amount of special support grant[^key-f94f0558f8b96a95d98f167477d1d023] available to a 2012 cohort student in respect of an academic year is £4,473[^key-7aa1313e565ecf822ee01fdb76410f84].

(2) A 2012 cohort student who qualifies for a special support grant in respect of an academic year receives, subject to sub-paragraph (c), an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £25,000 or less, the student receives£4,473[^key-e4dea0dd83be23b48548cf215a279dc3];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £42,737[^key-f5b8f96328f970f40be7fe43769d8cb3] the student receives an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986) where—

  *M* is £4,473[^key-96df9ba04703ac9ae0d0d713cf2e3624]

  *A* is £1 for every complete £4.01 by which the household income exceeds £25,000; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £42,737[^key-97ee9c978ab3f6bf8dde8b6349ec5c59], no special support grant is payable.
