# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 65

> Text as at 2026-09-01.

## 65. Amount of the special support grant – current system students who are not ... 2009, 2012 or 2016 cohort students[^key-1880029f303a814909717cfea7278d38][^key-852354451561591768e88865050ff056]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-32ba33257689879bb8fca3df6e7966a6] The maximum amount of special support grant available to a current system student who is not a [^key-339d06a372e663bf1041b8049953fba2]... 2009, 2012 or 2016 cohort student in respect of an academic year is £3,998[^key-69f4c927387e64d6edd41c6ae701b4d9].

(2)[^key-10731e7493163deb2185a6c3cfaa91df] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-10731e7493163deb2185a6c3cfaa91df] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-cd87ed0f0ecd22f2544fab4954ff2295] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) [^key-f95908043446037f7c27addb2727ceab]A current system student other than a ... 2009, 2012 or 2016 cohort student[^key-dfe26c41b2254c4070e0d28862b6fcf5] who qualifies for a special support grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £18,360 or less, the student receives £3,998[^M_F_587b26b3-e211-486c-d632-bb6bb230a2a0];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £18,360 but does not exceed £39,590[^key-81399b3c05861bd46c76486268a760f7], the student receives an amount equal to[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986) where—

  *M* is £3,998

  *A* is £1 for every complete £4.34 by which the household income exceeds £18,360 but does not exceed £28,121;

  *B* is £1 for every complete £6.75 by which the household income exceeds £28,121 but does not exceed £39,590;
- (c) where the household income exceeds £39,590[^key-ebf09266bb40ed017767959333015f1e], no special support grant is payable.
