## 63. Amount of the special support grant – 2009 cohort students

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-fcf3f276d8b55f8698613557e4345d79] The maximum amount of special support grant available to a 2009 cohort student in respect of an academic year is £3,998[^key-ff7fa54dfeceb073aaa43593051ba2ba]

(2)[^key-bae4b1261e52a98f8fe36b99d48ffb8a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-bae4b1261e52a98f8fe36b99d48ffb8a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-1975d9af8190f58a8b668528eb3e9853] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) [^key-5b6c44c92201d184a626c724a2a47789]A 2009 cohort student ... who qualifies for a special support grant in respect of an academic year receives, subject to sub-paragraph (c), an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £25,000 or less, the student receives £3,998[^key-933584020e87f76935055a87ceaa7630];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £50,726[^key-bf85e1cc148f3310ac44c5292adafa38], the student receives an amount equal to[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986) where—

  *M* is £3,998

  *A* is £1 for every complete £3.89 by which the household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £34,294

  *B* is £1 for every complete £10.54 by which the household income exceeds £34,294 but does not exceed £50,726; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £50,726[^key-83107c57e8bc7da1de03edff9db2c8eb], no special support grant is payable.
