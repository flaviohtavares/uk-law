## 58. Amount of the maintenance grant – 2009 cohort students

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-c8421baf845489ad15f0043a8b375f67] The maximum amount of maintenance grant available to a 2009 cohort student in respect of an academic year is £3,998[^M_F_f96bd539-e492-4bc3-8bf9-1c5d6a488934].

(2)[^c-99ae0f2849bf0965a6c0f3d4f5d18b86] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-99ae0f2849bf0965a6c0f3d4f5d18b86] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-87184671eef31721fa0b607e8fb663c2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) [^c-08f18ffe579630efbb3d12faa2594dc4]A 2009 cohort student ... who qualifies for a maintenance grant in respect of an academic year receives, subject to sub-paragraph (c), an amount as follows in respect of that year—

- (a) where the household income is £25,000 or less, the student receives £3,998[^c-8b24d707814ea873ebe6bbc226642b3c];
- (b) where the household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £50,726[^c-b2bfd757b316a171ee7f9cd28793b2be], the student receives an amount equal to[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986) where—

  *M* is £3,998

  *A* is £1 for every complete £3.89 by which the household income exceeds £25,000 but does not exceed £34,294

  *B* is £1 for every complete £10.54 by which the household income exceeds £34,294 but does not exceed £50,726; and
- (c) where the household income exceeds £50,726[^c-61eb136b5767b38383ea67f1b9208fb8], no maintenance grant is payable.
