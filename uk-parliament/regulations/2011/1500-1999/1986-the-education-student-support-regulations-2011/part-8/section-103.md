# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 103

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 103. Order of application – ... 2009 , 2012 or 2016 cohort students[^c-c6602eff0c018e9c4bb256a9e1669bb0][^c-28d5372d6fe01d699154a4106fefbd0c]

*Extent: E+W*

The contribution or the remainder of the contribution, as the case may be, is to be applied as follows—

- (a) first, to reduce the amount of adult dependants' grant calculated in accordance with regulation 47;
- (b) second, to reduce the childcare grant calculated in accordance with regulation 47;
- (c) third, to reduce the parents' learning allowance calculated in accordance with regulation 47 (except the first £50 of the allowance);
- (d) fourth, to reduce the long courses loan for which the eligible student, other than an eligible care leaver, qualifies under[^c-889d968a715a3ec15d1b337a9f080a42] Chapter 3 of Part 6;
- (e) fifth, to reduce the grant for travel calculated in accordance with Chapter 5 of Part 5.
