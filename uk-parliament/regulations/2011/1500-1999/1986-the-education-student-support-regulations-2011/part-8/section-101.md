# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 101

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 101. Application of contribution – current system students who are not ... 2009, 2012 or 2016 cohort students[^c-29ea0d6475b606dc9ed04a6c73d03ee3][^c-214cd177cc6497d45d1dde01518b8480]

*Extent: E+W*

In the case of a current system student who is not a [^c-f5db28f7fdbb4ae1778fbbd90e862dd5]... 2009, 2012 or 2016 cohort student, the Secretary of State must apply the contribution in accordance with regulation 104.
