## 105. Minimum level of loan for living costs

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (7), for a student other than a 2009 , 2012 or 2016[^key-5fc12f15229ff867b4596f035280842e] cohort student, the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulations 72(5) [^key-e5494212126d34190dd8d964dce9d80c]... and 104(d) is—

- (a) £3,782[^key-17d1d813a0dedc66e8181fb839edeec9], in the case of a student in category A;
- (b) £6,847[^key-233d0dc28c158b00c17dccc5174024ac], in the case of a student in category B;
- (c) £5,827[^key-5ba940001eb0d3d8a87e456fac8c936e], in the case of a student in category C;
- (d) £4,890[^key-6e6ffdda801f7177aab3273114b5e324], in the case of a student in category D.

(2) Subject to paragraph (7), for a student other than a 2009 , 2012 or 2016[^key-d0cc6b38a2a4d42bb8ada8338e90264b] cohort student, where the academic year in question is the final year of a course other than an intensive course, the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulations 72(5) [^key-99c37ebe07247bad8976423620920d18]... and 104(d) is—

- (a) £3,421[^key-09178112cf58cfad5f314a6de1b9ff0c], in the case of a student in category A;
- (b) £6,235[^key-279f150f90474f37cf23af42cb9c03eb], in the case of a student in category B;
- (c) £5,066[^key-fda8e7dc3600e6fa5cc5559c007e9314], in the case of a student in category C;
- (d) £4,525[^key-aaa40cf3b9e7d8ce24af2f2e32202da7], in the case of a student in category D.

(3) Subject to paragraphs (4) and (7), for a 2009 cohort student the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulation 74(5)[^key-3133126ddac9cc8e2693efb8a6210e8e] is—

- (a) £3,798[^key-4f9bb641bd39577c28a5fdbd14c9ad02], in the case of a student in category A;
- (b) £6,855[^key-fad6da5c49994c56b98c668661302365], in the case of a student in category B;
- (c) £5,831[^key-eec7a1c05a9e26534810062751c95eb6], in the case of a student in category C;
- (d) £4,898[^key-ccbf95223297232719dfefba984dc530], in the case of a student in category D.

(4) Subject to paragraph (7), for a 2009 cohort student where the academic year in question is the final year of a course other than an intensive course, the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulation 74(5)[^key-fa314d4d87732c6b6c2222ceb4e023ce] is—

- (a) £3,447[^key-6aa67a7322fc0ac2a2dd2f0b2302f593], in the case of a student in category A;
- (b) £6,241[^key-ec0ea4145d03b0c19351a123a1aea4ee], in the case of a student in category B;

- (d) £4,534[^key-083626ec46ae776ecd6c079a6583c8d7], in the case of a student in category D.

(5) Subject to paragraphs (6) and (7), for a 2012 cohort student, the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulations 76 and 104(d) is—

- (a) £4,013[^key-c594a8ea57df794f0b64a0161a6a70e5], in the case of a student in category A;
- (b) £7,041[^key-fd38459ae3bdd52dc69e9b01ce64f96d], in the case of a student in category B;

- (d) £5,050[^key-87dd287212c56ad17b40e1c67beb71c2], in the case of a student in category D.

(6) Subject to paragraph (7), for a 2012 cohort student, where the academic year in question is the final year of a course other than an intensive course, the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulations 76 and 104(d) is—

- (a) £3,687[^key-6f5f63928ecb0c94ab5fb72a1742b833], in the case of a student in category A;
- (b) £6,413[^key-6cf29771b1174f0a021c9a41106805e5], in the case of a student in category B;
- (c) £5,212[^key-943a687c05ffdcd12dad3aa506ce66ba], in the case of a student in category C;
- (d) £4,694[^key-43490d54a991b1f1689628e6b0179737], in the case of a student in category D.

(6A)[^key-1406f4dc99c993a9f30764645dbc416c] Subject to paragraphs (6B) and (7), for a 2016 cohort student who satisfies all the conditions in regulation 71(1)(g), the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulation 80A is—

- (a) £4,013[^key-5a8e865fc6f316fd93cb1087a1319e4f], in the case of a student in category A;
- (b) £7,039[^key-d2df12b7d048a22dc34f558784476949], in the case of a student in category B;
- (c) £5,996[^key-c5844aaeb727277534e3ac2f998bdb69], in the case of a student in category C;
- (d) £5,048[^key-1c93528c4570b389cc74e40deb1ef68e], in the case of a student in category D.

(6B) Subject to paragraph (7), for a 2016 cohort student who satisfies all the conditions in regulation 71(1)(g), where the academic year in question is the final year of a course other than an intensive course, the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulation 80A is—

- (a) £3,687[^key-f9601ce7e36267af31e4c5d7d23007ec], in the case of a student in category A;
- (b) £6,413[^key-a1df2bb49874d0b137bf3daac2b661ad], in the case of a student in category B;
- (c) £5,210[^key-47caea43abff2ea3df51386cbb4956f2], in the case of a student in category C;
- (d) £4,693[^key-d36ba77c8e3ff49b14c61481c112f19c], in the case of a student in category D.

(6C) Subject to paragraphs (6D) and (7), for a 2016 cohort student who satisfies all the conditions in regulation 71(1)(h), the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulation 80B is—

- (a) £4,013[^key-79627b35e17c363e04c44292527a7c64], in the case of a student in category A;
- (b) £7,039[^key-bd1a470480c7383c57efd8bff5600f69], in the case of a student in category B;
- (c) £5,996[^key-c5b00223362ad02ab20e0c574e3e43a9], in the case of a student in category C;
- (d) £5,048[^key-987a15e7ecb78674f4395d5014bbb2b8], in the case of a student in category D.

(6D) Subject to paragraph (7), for a 2016 cohort student who satisfies all the conditions in regulation 71(1)(h), where the academic year in question is the final year of a course other than an intensive course, the “minimum level for the academic year” in regulation 80B is—

- (a) £3,687[^key-561b9e38003ac513481c4f9ed11e99ce], in the case of a student in category A;
- (b) £6,413[^key-e86499d96008fada1d452cd6d4673192], in the case of a student in category B;
- (c) £5,210[^key-7b0e4e5310769bc76ef37573ba500561], in the case of a student in category C;
- (d) £4,693[^key-0eca248c68e2bc2c64a5db6b606d97ad], in the case of a student in category D.

(7) Where different categories apply for different quarters of the academic year, the minimum levels in paragraphs (1) to (6D)[^key-d18e192a99b401533c5ea807ba6073e4] are the aggregate of the amounts determined under paragraph (8) for each of the quarters in respect of which a loan is payable.

(8) The amount determined for each quarter is one third of the amount in paragraph (1), (2), (3), (4), (5) , (6), (6A), (6B), (6C) or (6D)[^key-ea8804f7eb7d3552de150d122461ef60] which corresponds to the rate applicable for the quarter.

(9) The rate applicable for a quarter is determined in accordance with regulation 83.

(10) Categories A to D have the meaning given in regulation 90.
