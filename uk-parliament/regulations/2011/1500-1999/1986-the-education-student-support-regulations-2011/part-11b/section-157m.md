# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 157M

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 157M. Students who are treated as in attendance on a course[^c-38517a92bbf535ed07f05c172588bf2e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A student to whom this regulation applies is treated as being in attendance on the designated part-time course for the purpose of qualifying for the loan for living costs.

(2) This regulation applies to—

- (a) a student on a period of study or period of work placement in an Erasmus year;
- (b) a disabled student who is undertaking a designated part-time course in the United Kingdom but is not in attendance because the student is unable to attend for a reason which relates to the student’s disability.
