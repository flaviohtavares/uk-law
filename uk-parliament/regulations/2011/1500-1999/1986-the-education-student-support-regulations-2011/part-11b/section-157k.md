# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 157K

> Text as at 2026-09-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 157K. Quarters in respect of which the loan for living costs is payable to eligible part-time students[^c-38517a92bbf535ed07f05c172588bf2e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to regulation 138A, the loan for living costs is payable in respect of three quarters of the academic year.

(2) The loan for living costs is not payable in respect of the quarter in which, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, the longest of any vacation occurs.
