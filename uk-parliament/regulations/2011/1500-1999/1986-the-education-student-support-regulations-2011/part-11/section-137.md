# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 137

> Text as at 2026-09-01.

## 137. Eligible part-time students

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies for support in connection with the student's undertaking a designated part-time course subject to and in accordance with this Part and Parts 11A and 11B[^key-5fd852c013cf9e84dc5251551c6e7417].

(2)[^key-157c5c3a890f08be4a7b7543b39f428b] Subject to the following provisions of this regulation, a person is an eligible part-time student in connection with a designated part-time course if in assessing that person’s application for support the Secretary of State determines that the person falls within one of the categories set out—

- (a) in paragraphs 2, 2A, 3, 4, 4A, 4B, 4C, 4D, 4E, 5, 6A, 7A, 8A, 9A, 9B, 9BA[^key-c14e9e46972a2ec5d22071d08fe88be4] 9BB,[^key-d6700da5b058ac2b4d0da18ed5b13715] 9C, 9D, 10ZA, 11A, 12A , 13[^key-a6830a3e6f9629df7a5ec484da4d884e] , 14, 15 and 16[^key-a0385a97d8d2c246ba9cfb34c24e501a] in Part 2 of Schedule 1; or
- (b) in paragraphs 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 10A, 11 and 12 in Part 2 of Schedule 1, where paragraph (2A) applies.

(2A) This paragraph applies where—

- (a) in connection with a designated part-time course beginning before 1st August 2021, the Secretary of State—
  - (i) in assessing an application for support by a person (“A”) determined that A fell within one of the categories set out in paragraphs 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 10A, 11 and 12 in Part 2 of Schedule 1 in relation to an academic year of the course beginning before 1st August 2021; or
  - (ii) would have so determined had A made an application for support in accordance with this Part in relation to an academic year of the course beginning before that date; and
- (b) A applies for support in connection with that course or a designated part-time course to which A’s status as an eligible part-time student is transferred from that course in accordance with this Part.

(2B) In connection with a designated part-time course beginning on or after 1st January 2028, paragraph (2) has effect as if it did not mention paragraphs 8A , 9B, 9BA and 9D[^key-ac0619a9ce776f8b6bf3d794e601669f].

(3) A person (“A”) is not an eligible part-time student if—

- (za)[^key-818535de5ececd140e1236e840cea397] A is studying on a course as part of an apprenticeship;
- (a)[^key-568f691081f9fb1aec732980f160788e] A is, in connection with the part-time course—
  - (i) eligible to apply for a healthcare bursary whether or not the amount of such bursary is calculated by reference to income;
  - (ii) eligible to apply for any allowance under the Nursing and Midwifery Student Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2007; or
  - (iii) eligible for a healthcare tuition payment;
- (b) A is in breach of any obligation to repay any loan;
- (c) A has reached the age of 18 and has not ratified any agreement for a loan A made when A was under the age of 18;
- (d) A has, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, shown by A's conduct that A is unfitted to receive support; or
- (e)[^key-bcc66e86a59a39d3a32a0da35a59c86b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3A)[^key-773eecd4b600bff151c68f4ada563449] A person (“A”) is not an eligible part-time student in connection with a pre-registration course which leads to a qualification for a profession in respect of which A is already registered in the relevant part or parts of the register maintained by the Health and Care Professions Council, the Nursing and Midwifery Council or the General Dental Council.

(3B)[^key-80f7b99113b9acd05434bf682e0927e2] A person (“**A**”) is not an eligible part-time student if A is enrolled on a course which is designated under—

- (a) regulation 4 of the Education (Postgraduate Master’s Degree Loans) Regulations 2016,
- (b) regulation 4 of the Education (Postgraduate Doctoral Degree Loans and the Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) (Amendment) (No. 2) etc.) Regulations 2018, or
- (c) regulation 10 or 11 of the Lifelong Learning (Student Support) (Amendment of Fees and Awards etc.) Regulations 2026,

and is receiving support under any of those sets of Regulations for that course.

(4)[^key-3bd33227bd620b476843e1ba32b7d650] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) For the purposes of paragraphs (3)(b) and (3)(c), “**loan**” means a loan made under any provision of the student loans legislation.

(6) In a case where the agreement for a loan is subject to the law of Scotland, paragraph (3)(c) only applies if the agreement was made—

- (a) before 25th September 1991; and
- (b) with the concurrence of the borrower's curator or at a time when the borrower had no curator.

(10) Where—

- (a) the Secretary of State determined that, by virtue of being a refugee[^key-9873ee0a03cdf9f184e2e84f5501926d] or the spouse, civil partner, child or step-child of a refugee[^key-5d6af488c40979128118a19172ec3626], a person (“A”) was an eligible part-time student in connection with an application for support for an earlier year of the current part-time course or an application for support in connection with a designated course [^key-728cb4f7e7d0c83e3dedd8b2fc72fb78]... or other designated part-time course from which A's status as an eligible part-time student or eligible student[^key-84c81732a7f84d15e208978989b72c9c] has been transferred to the current part-time course; and
- (b)[^key-e918bb084f29492772697bc339576993] as at the day before the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support begins, the refugee status of A or of A’s spouse, civil partner, parent or step-parent, as the case may be, has expired and—[^key-d394cfc0a2e4c8e91b54f2aa6739c712]
  - (i) no further leave to remain has been granted and paragraph SUI 13.1 of the immigration rules does not apply in respect of an application for such leave[^key-99a455d52f4d6ef5d6e93601ee0605fb];
  - (ii) no appeal is pending (within the meaning of section 104 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002); and
  - (iii) the person (A or A’s spouse, civil partner, parent or step-parent) whose refugee status has expired has not become a British or Irish citizen,

A's status as an eligible part-time student terminates immediately before the first day of the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support.

  - (i) an application for support for an earlier year of the current part-time course, or
  - (ii) an application for support in connection with a designated part-time course or other designated course from which A’s status as an eligible part-time student or eligible student has been transferred to the current part-time course, and
- (b)[^key-5158d847e9faedfad0ff6c8c4fc00889] as at the day before the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support begins, the period for which the person granted stateless leave is allowed to stay in the United Kingdom has expired and—

(13) An eligible part-time student may not, at any one time, qualify for support for—

- (a) more than one designated part-time course;
- (b) a designated part-time course and a designated course;
