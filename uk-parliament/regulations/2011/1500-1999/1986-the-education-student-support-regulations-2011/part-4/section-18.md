# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 18

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Students to be treated as in attendance on a course

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A student to whom this regulation applies is treated as if the student were in attendance on the designated course for the purpose of qualifying for a fee loan[^key-94bbc3e92830a7b44d482dfb11cd52cb].

(2) This regulation applies to—

- (a) a[^key-39018dc8d95b0cce8176f074c134c10f] compressed degree student[^key-00fc8023f1b5a344d6a7e0fe8f6af60e]; or
- (b) a disabled student who—
  - (i) is not a compressed degree student[^key-f3976bc03ae5a81c3d32428b3a70b5f5]; and
  - (ii) is undertaking a designated course [^key-5a11441c0a86a741e67f30ab31b5810a]... in the United Kingdom but is not in attendance because the student is unable to attend for a reason which relates to the student's disability.
