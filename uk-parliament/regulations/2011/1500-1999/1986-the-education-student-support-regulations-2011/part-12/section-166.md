# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 166

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 166. Amount of grant

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-743296c89efc58b570462b7740c5e4ef] Subject to paragraph (2), the grant under this Part is the amount that the Secretary of State considers appropriate in accordance with the student’s circumstances.

(1A)[^c-26fca664873b0b5da02e2017df35b592] Subject to paragraph (2), the amount of the grant under this Part for additional expenditure on a computer is an amount equal to the expenditure the student is obliged to incur less £200.

(2)[^c-53e7b12192a45c2e48b2bde591c037f4] The amount of the grant in respect of an academic year—

- (a) for expenditure other than travel expenditure, must not exceed £27,783[^c-0da97751faffe039ec559051488f45fd];
- (b) for travel expenditure, must not exceed an amount equal to that expenditure.

(3)[^c-4e9b7d759d0200224dfa343546a1d038] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-497f3dd1556df72f7e88879dfeaefd7a] Subject to paragraph (5), the grant under this Part is payable in respect of the four quarters of the academic year [^c-7630c8269eb4e9fe74922375cd40b11b]...

(5) Where an event falling within regulation 160(1)(a) or (c)[^c-b8ee3bc7279bed35117efa4be08f1a97] occurs in the course of an academic year, a student may qualify for a grant under this Part in respect of—[^c-06059065ab1ec6b25ef32961622e10e2]

- (a) the quarter in which the relevant event occurs, and
- (b) if relevant, such quarters as begin after the relevant event occurs.

(6)[^c-f5aed84d65f203317dd9f07a2d62ddee] Where an event falling within regulation 160(1)(b) occurs, a student may qualify for a grant under this Part in respect of the four quarters of the first academic year.
