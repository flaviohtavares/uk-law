# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 110

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 110. General

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2)—

- (a) fee loans in connection with designated courses[^key-af380998a9ab431aa8216915e6d18f8b] are payable in accordance with regulation 113;
- (b)[^key-da26faa9594a03cb871f4eff5a427bae] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^key-1250b6e003f8ccb86e78c995f6b6730a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) loans for living costs in connection with designated courses[^key-bf6d62dd30c1b2af397a9294fd979bea] and long courses loans are payable in accordance with regulation 116;

(2) Regulations 111 and 112 apply in respect of the payment of [^key-690bb12a8d293aa53319170dbcf1dad3]...loans other than loans for living costs in connection with designated part-time courses[^key-82b0c1a9efc2afc1fd19daa9f211ee4d].
