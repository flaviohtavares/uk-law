# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 2A

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2A. Meaning of “in-year qualifying event”, “protected category event”, “settled status event”, “course designation event” etc. and related matters[^c-46d16eb4c76bfb0b4a3db785a9a83d0c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation defines “in-year qualifying event”, “protected category event” “settled status event”, “course designation event”, “part-time course designation event” and “postgraduate course designation event” and makes related provision for the purposes of these Regulations.

(2) “**In-year qualifying event**”, in relation to a student, means—

- (a) a protected category event which occurs after the course start date;
- (b) a settled status event;
- (ba)[^c-50dd0c98919f3cc29c25cb7447547c20] the student becomes a person described in paragraph 2 of Schedule 1;
- (c) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 3(1)(a) of Schedule 1;
- (d) where regulation 4(2A), 137(2A) or 159(3A) applies, the student becomes a person described in paragraph 12(a) of Schedule 1;
- (e) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6A(1)(a) of Schedule 1, or where regulation 4(2A), 137(2A) or 159(3A) applies, in paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 1;
- (f) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 11A(a) of Schedule 1 or, where regulation 4(2A), 137(2A) or 159(3A) applies, in paragraph 11(1)(a) of Schedule 1; or
- (g) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 9B(1)(a)(ii) of Schedule 1.

(3) “**Protected category event**”, in relation to a student, means—

- (a) the student or the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent is recognised as a refugee;
- (b) the student or the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent becomes a person granted stateless leave;
- (c) the student or the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent becomes a person granted humanitarian protection;
- (d) the student becomes a person granted section 67 leave;
- (e) the student or the student’s parent[^c-c4a32a90b00e4ae87d0ad202bd97b414] becomes a person granted indefinite leave to enter or remain[^c-c886041166c7c9f2fc59eff06d5d9a27] as a victim of domestic violence or domestic abuse;
- (f) the student becomes a person granted Calais leave;
- (g) the student or the student’s parent[^c-d4706fb1c798a92f7999d51b529f1854] becomes a person granted indefinite leave to enter or remain[^c-d10d85afbe5a209dfcf7e42d0a3caec7] as a bereaved partner;
- (h) the student or the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent[^c-d55a56cdd49d2e34cfe6f92ada5668a1] becomes a person granted leave under one of the Afghan Schemes; or
- (i) the student or the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent[^c-985f7f27362e0e654d34dcaf02eda314] becomes a person granted leave under one of the Ukraine Schemes.

(4) “**Settled status event**”, in relation to a student means—[^c-609f471fe2a4b261d9a74dbf60465daf]

- (a) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 2A or 9BB of Schedule 1; or
- (b) the student becomes a family member described in paragraph 9A(1)(a), 9C(1)(a) or 9D(1)(a) or (2)(a) of Schedule 1.

(5) “**Course designation event**”, in relation to a course, means—

- (a) the course is designated under regulation 5(10);
- (b) where the course begins before 1st September 2028,[^c-2dbd9bbe50f2c4331dcfcbac587b1261] the English higher education provider which is providing the course, or on whose behalf the course is being provided, becomes a registered provider;
- (c) the course becomes a Northern Irish designated full-time course, a Scottish designated full-time course or a Welsh designated full-time course ;[^c-40f3bacb51d2112786959ef4f628c27c] [^c-e904dae5c6efdb1562430a31a90a6487]...
- (d) the course becomes a recognised initial further education teacher training course ;[^c-b9ce81bb76e232b8564e254eaeed703d]
- (e) where the course begins on or after 1st September 2028—
  - (i) the English higher education provider, on whose behalf the course is being provided, becomes a registered provider, and
  - (ii) the course is provided by a qualifying franchised provider (or multiple qualifying franchised providers)

(6) “**Part-time course designation event**”, in relation to a course, means—

- (a) the course is designated under regulation 139(7);
- (b) where the course begins before 1st September 2028,[^c-2deed06054ff5c90c7933baab81f6d87] the English higher education provider which is providing the course, or on whose behalf the course is being provided, becomes a registered provider;
- (c) the course becomes a Northern Irish designated part-time course, a Scottish designated part-time course or a Welsh designated part-time course ;[^c-fd815e68d6d428fe1e514df23a85d222] [^c-8e88800b540c359355866ea5a939b13e]...
- (d) the course becomes a recognised initial further education teacher training course ; or[^c-aad6bd11139e5a9e43b955a0679ef665]
- (e) where the course begins on or after 1st September 2028—
  - (i) the English higher education provider, on whose behalf the course is being provided, becomes a registered provider, and
  - (ii) the course is provided by a qualifying franchised provider (or multiple qualifying franchised providers)

(7) “**Postgraduate course designation event**”, in relation to a course, means—

- (a) the course is designated under regulation 161(4)[^c-00ec0e07e33ce3422d48146d35af6c40];
- (b) where the course begins before 1st September 2028,[^c-96726296c58cd789087e8f4e153423da] the English higher education provider which is providing the course, or on whose behalf the course is being provided, becomes a registered provider; [^c-c487006e63e08e5f53c6d4f61b5b1c9d]...
- (c) the course becomes a Northern Irish designated postgraduate[^c-ff0f7f993cc76701645941a56abd4585] course, a Scottish designated postgraduate course or a Welsh designated postgraduate course ; or[^c-bbf780fb29583927a6aab7cb1444a352]
- (d) where the course begins on or after 1st September 2028—
  - (i) the English higher education provider, on whose behalf the course is being provided, becomes a registered provider, and
  - (ii) the course is provided by a qualifying franchised provider (or multiple qualifying franchised providers).

(8) Where a protected category event occurs in relation to a student after the course start date, paragraph (9) applies for the purposes of determining whether that event results in the student becoming an eligible student, eligible part-time student or eligible postgraduate student.

(9) The student is to be treated, for the purposes of determining whether the student satisfies any requirement in Schedule 1 to be ordinarily resident in England on the course start date, as if the student was, on the course start date, lawfully residing in the place where the student was residing on that date.
