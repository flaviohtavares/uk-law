# The Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011, section 3

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Revocation, savings and transitional provisions

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the following regulations are revoked on 1st September 2012—

- (a) the 2009 Regulations;
- (b) the Education (Student Support) Regulations 2009 (Amendment) Regulations 2010 [^c21072271];
- (c) regulation 5 and regulations 10 to 12 of the Education (Student Fees, Awards and Support) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 [^c21072281];
- (d) the Education (Student Support) (Dance and Drama) Regulations 1999 [^c21072291]; and
- (e) the Education (Student Support) (Dance and Drama) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 [^c21072301].

(2) Regulation 113 and 114 of the 2009 Regulations are revoked on 1st September 2011.

(3) The 2009 Regulations continue to apply to the provision of support to students in relation to an academic year which begins on or after 1st September 2010 but before 1st September 2012.
