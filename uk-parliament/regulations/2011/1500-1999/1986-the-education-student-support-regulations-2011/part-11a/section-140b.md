## 140B. Availability of support to prisoners[^key-9753772f84013983d28350ff3d1e0e57]

*Extent: E+W*

An eligible part-time student who is a prisoner qualifies for support under this Part only—

- (a) if the student is an eligible prisoner; or
- (b) in respect of an academic year during which the student enters prison or is released from prison.
