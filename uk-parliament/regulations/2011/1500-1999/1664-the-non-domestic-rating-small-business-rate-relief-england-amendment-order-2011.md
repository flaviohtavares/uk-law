# The Non-Domestic Rating (Small Business Rate Relief) (England) (Amendment) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 1664

Rating And Valuation, England

The Non-Domestic Rating (Small Business Rate Relief) (England) (Amendment) Order 2011

Made

7th July 2011

Laid before Parliament

12th July 2011

Coming into force

3rd August 2011

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 43(4B)(a), 44(9)(a), 143(1) and (2) of the Local Government Finance Act 1988 [^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, application and commencement

This Order, which applies to England only, may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rating (Small Business Rate Relief) (England) (Amendment) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 3rd August 2011.

## 2. Amendment of the Non-Domestic Rating (Small Business Rate Relief) (England) Order 2004

(1) The Non-Domestic Rating (Small Business Rate Relief) (England) Order 2004 [^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) For the heading for article 7, substitute “Amount of E between 1st October 2010 and 30th September 2012”.

(3) In article 7(1), for “2011”, substitute “2012”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Bob Neill

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

7th July 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision for a continued temporary increase in the level of Small Business Rate Relief until 30th September 2012.

Article 2 amends the Non-Domestic Rating (Small Business Rate Relief) (England) Order 2004 in order to increase the level of relief for a further year beyond the current temporary increase, which would have ended on 30th September 2011.
