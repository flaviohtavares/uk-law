# The Payments to the Churches Conservation Trust Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 1729

Ecclesiastical Law, England

The Payments to the Churches Conservation Trust Order 2011

Made (Sealed by the Church Commissioners)

2nd June 2011

Approved by the General Synod

9th July 2011

Laid before Parliament

21st July 2011

Coming into force

1st April 2012

We, the Church Commissioners for England, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 53 of the Pastoral Measure 1983[^f00001], as amended by the Pastoral (Amendment) Measure 1994[^f00002] and the Dioceses, Pastoral and Mission Measure 2007[^f00003], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Interpretation

In this Order “the Trust” means the Churches Conservation Trust, “the Commissioners” means the Church Commissioners, “the 1983 Measure” means the Pastoral Measure 1983 and “the funding period” means the period specified in paragraph 2.

## 2. Funding period for Part III of 1983 Measure

The funding period for the purposes of Part III of the 1983 Measure shall be the period beginning with 1st April 2012 and ending with 31st March 2015.

## 3. Payments to the Trust

In respect of the funding period the total amount (comprising grants made under section 44(10) of the 1983 Measure and moneys allocated under section 52(1) of that Measure) to be paid by the Commissioners to the Trust shall be £4,065,000.

## 4. Condition for payments to the Trust

A payment on account of the sum specified in paragraph 3 shall be made to the Trust only when the Commissioners are satisfied that the payment is a portion of a specific sum of money needed by the Trust and that the balance of that sum will be paid to the Trust out of moneys provided by Parliament.

## 5. Revocation

The Payments to the Churches Conservation Trust Order 2008[^f00004] is hereby revoked.

## 6. Citation and coming into force

This Order may be cited as The Payments to the Churches Conservation Trust Order 2011 and shall come into force on 1st April 2012.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides, in respect of the period from 1st April 2012 to 31st March 2015, that the total amount to be paid to the Churches Conservation Trust shall be £4,065,000, comprising grants made by the Church Commissioners and moneys allocated by them out of the net proceeds of sale or exchange of churches closed for regular public worship or of land occupied by them or net premiums for the grant of leases of such churches or land. The Order also provides that the total amount can only be paid if it is a proportion of a sum needed by the Trust and Parliament has approved the payment of the balance of that sum.
