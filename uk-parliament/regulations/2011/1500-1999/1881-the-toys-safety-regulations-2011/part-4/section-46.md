# The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 46. Provision of information by approved bodies to other approved bodies[^M_E_a230ae76-edec-45a6-f40a-188ffc20901a][^c-7473cb6c0424f03fbe7166b0059628e8][^c-04cb8347cfa3f9d1b8c06f36ebd3aad6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An approved[^c-aba5f1317b25b0bd3f25f1a8bb88971f] body must provide other approved[^c-c07e3291f4c414e8fb48572d259d585b] bodies which carry out similar conformity assessment activities covering the same toys with relevant information on issues relating to negative and, on request, positive conformity assessment results.

## 46. Provision of information by UK notified bodies to other notified bodies

*Extent: N.I.*

A UK notified body must provide other notified bodies which carry out similar conformity assessment activities covering the same toys with relevant information on issues relating to negative and, on request, positive conformity assessment results.
