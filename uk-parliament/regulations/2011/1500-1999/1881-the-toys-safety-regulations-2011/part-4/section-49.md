## 49. Subcontracting by a UK notified body

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41] A UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41] notified body may subcontract a specific task or activity connected with conformity assessment or have recourse to a subsidiary to carry out a task or activity if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41]

- (a) the body is satisfied that the subcontractor or subsidiary meets the requirements laid down in paragraphs 2 to 11 of Article 26 of the Directive (requirements relating to notified bodies); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41]
- (b) the economic operator for whom the task or activity is to be carried out has consented to the task or activity being performed by that person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41]

(2) A UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41] notified body which subcontracts a specific task or activity connected with conformity assessment or has recourse to a subsidiary to carry out a task or activity— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41]

- (a) must inform the Secretary of State that the body is satisfied that the subcontractor or subsidiary meets the requirements laid down in paragraphs 2 to 11 of Article 26 of the Directive; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41]
- (b) remains responsible for the proper performance of the task or activity (irrespective of where the subcontractor or subsidiary is established). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41]

(3) The Secretary of State may request a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41] notified body to provide to the Secretary of State, within a specified period, any relevant documents concerning the assessment of the qualifications of the subcontractor or subsidiary and the tasks or activities carried out by the subcontractor or subsidiary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41]

(4) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41] notified body must comply with the request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a5bad28de05244474d36ee75d142cc41]
