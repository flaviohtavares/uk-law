# The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011, section 40F

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/1881

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 40F. Operational matters in relation to approved bodies[^c-f863ec81be828ea5ab97b3ccedf81f21]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the terms of its appointment and to regulation 44, an approved body must carry out the conformity assessment activities and modules—

- (a) in respect of which the body's approval was given under regulation 40B; or
- (b) in respect of which body's notification as a notified body was made.

(2) Where an approved body carries out a conformity assessment procedure, it must do so in accordance with Schedule 6.
