# The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Toys to which these Regulations apply[^M_E_c7342d4c-6945-4e05-c0f0-8869d01f077d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) These Regulations apply to toys placed on the market on or after 19th August 2011.

(2) Toys are products designed or intended (whether or not exclusively) for use in play by children under 14 years old.

(3) These Regulations do not apply to—

- (a) playground equipment intended for public use;
- (b) automatic playing machines intended for public use, whether coin operated or not;
- (c) toy vehicles equipped with combustion engines;
- (d) toy steam engines;
- (e) slings and catapults;
- (f) products listed in Schedule 1[^c-d6a66921dc0a695a4ff57faebee3fd56], as amended from time to time.

## 4. Toys to which these Regulations apply

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) These Regulations apply to toys placed on the market on or after 19th August 2011.

(2) Toys are products designed or intended (whether or not exclusively) for use in play by children under 14 years old.

(3) These Regulations do not apply to—

- (a) playground equipment intended for public use;
- (b) automatic playing machines intended for public use, whether coin operated or not;
- (c) toy vehicles equipped with combustion engines;
- (d) toy steam engines;
- (e) slings and catapults;
- (f) products listed in Annex I to the Directive, as amended from time to time.
