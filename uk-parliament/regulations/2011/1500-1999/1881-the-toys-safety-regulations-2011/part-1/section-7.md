# The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 7. Presumption of conformity[^M_E_772833c6-01f8-4163-bfb2-20e7d2b28c6d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A toy which conforms with designated[^c-fb0b922c4a83a951e04dbe93a56b4769] standards shall be presumed to comply with the essential safety requirements to the extent that those requirements are covered by those standards.

(2) The presumption set out in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.

## 7. Presumption of conformity

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A toy which conforms with harmonised standards shall be presumed to comply with the essential safety requirements to the extent that those requirements are covered by those standards.

(2) The presumption set out in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.
