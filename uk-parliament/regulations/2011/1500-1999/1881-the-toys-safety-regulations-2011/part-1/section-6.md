# The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/1881

## 6. Particular safety requirements for toys placed on the market before 20th July 2013[^c-2d0ebf64847bcf31a8048b1194abbea4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a toy is placed on the market before 20th July 2013, the particular safety requirements in respect of chemical properties are those in paragraph 3 of Part II of Annex *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d0ebf64847bcf31a8048b1194abbea4] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d0ebf64847bcf31a8048b1194abbea4] to Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d0ebf64847bcf31a8048b1194abbea4] [88/378/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0378)[^c-2d0ebf64847bcf31a8048b1194abbea4] on the approximation of the laws of the Member States concerning the safety of toys *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d0ebf64847bcf31a8048b1194abbea4] [^c20987601], and not those in Part III of Annex *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d0ebf64847bcf31a8048b1194abbea4] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d0ebf64847bcf31a8048b1194abbea4] to the Directive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d0ebf64847bcf31a8048b1194abbea4]
