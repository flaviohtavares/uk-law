# The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011, section 3B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/1881

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3B. Annexes to EU legislation as Schedules[^c-631fc29f962cdf59a8eb6c1b09c1092b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Schedules 1, 2, 4 and 5 reproduce provisions of the Annexes I, II, IV and V (respectively) to the Directive with amendments to correct deficiencies in assimilated[^c-a237ae1c5de9fbbeaa58a4a7787c8dcb] law.

(2) A reference to a provision of Schedules 1, 2, 4, 5 is a reference to the equivalent provision of the relevant Annex to the Directive as set out in the relevant Schedule.

(3) Schedule 6 reproduces provisions of Annex II to Decision No [768/2008/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/19768/2008) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 July 2008 on a common framework for the marketing of products, and repealing Council Decision [93/465/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/1993/465) (“Decision No [768/2008/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/19768/2008)”) which are relevant to these Regulations, as it has effect immediately before IP completion day, with amendments to correct deficiencies in assimilated[^c-c69a04b17286c730a9e08e3fa2602b91] law.

(4) A reference to a provision of Schedule 6 is a reference to the equivalent provision of Annex II of Decision No [768/2008/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/19768/2008) as set out in that Schedule.
