# The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 8. Exception for trade fairs or exhibitions[^M_E_84c21e7e-1959-43db-c0e8-8f2a23249ca4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A toy which does not bear the UK[^c-0f97362647792a1df4c55e953a3f4a96] marking, or in relation to which any other requirement of these Regulations is not complied with, may be shown or used at a trade fair or exhibition.

(2) Such a toy must be accompanied by a sign which indicates clearly that—

- (a) the toy does not comply with these Regulations[^c-98da255e0c4e51fe58816407c3954fb1]; and
- (b) the toy will not be made available in the United Kingdom[^c-8718d142fb3b369a59cc975e8381004b] before being brought into conformity with these Regulations.

## 8. Exception for trade fairs or exhibitions

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A toy which does not bear the CE marking, or in relation to which any other requirement of these Regulations is not complied with, may be shown or used at a trade fair or exhibition.

(2) Such a toy must be accompanied by a sign which indicates clearly that—

- (a) the toy does not comply with the Directive; and
- (b) the toy will not be made available in the relevant market[^c-a4c3598a7019f49948d1a9ec373f2b85] before being brought into conformity with the Directive.
