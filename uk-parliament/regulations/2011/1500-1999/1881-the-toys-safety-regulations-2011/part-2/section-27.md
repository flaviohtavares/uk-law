# The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Information identifying importer[^M_E_e35bfa8d-0ea0-49c9-ecea-3eb9ab4d5ba7][^key-7fd37ba846cb66f4cb949b035d947a93]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An importer must ensure that the following information is marked on the toy—

- (a) the importer's name, registered trade name or registered trade mark; and
- (b) the address at which the importer can be contacted.

(2)[^key-99349ef1cb63fd8c50bf884154436090] Paragraph (1) does not apply where—

- (a) either—
  - (i) the size or nature of the toy precludes the information from being marked on the toy;
  - (ii) the importer would have to open the toy's packaging in order to mark the information on the toy; or
  - (iii) the importer imported the toy from an EEA state or Switzerland and places it on the market within the period of seven years[^key-202dbf0ff8905474c7d8b5274c5c28c0] beginning with IP completion day; and
- (b) the importer ensures that the information referred to in paragraph (1) is set out on the toy's packaging or in a document accompanying the toy.

## 27. Information identifying importer

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An importer must ensure that the following information is marked on the toy—

- (a) the importer's name, registered trade name or registered trade mark; and
- (b) the address at which the importer can be contacted.

(2) The information may instead be marked on the toy's packaging or on a document accompanying the toy where—

- (a) the size or nature of the toy precludes the information from being marked on the toy; or
- (b) the importer would have to open the toy's packaging in order to mark the information on the toy.
