# The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Declaration of conformity and UK marking[^M_E_5da83ac0-98fa-420f-ddfa-71ba5be568fe][^M_F_5178b8b5-a2ad-48ff-b0b5-9aeaf678c146][^M_F_428ee496-679e-4e55-d31b-0ac66bb5ff80]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where it has been demonstrated by performance of the applicable conformity assessment procedure that a toy will comply with the essential safety requirements during its foreseeable and normal period of use, the manufacturer must—

- (a) draw up a[^c-22225a684a485bc41a1f52b5aabea32c] declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 16(1) to (4); and
- (b) affix a UK[^c-ad5de077dd1f10fc92a6004120892c1a] marking in relation to the toy in accordance with regulation 18.

## 15. EC declaration of conformity and CE marking

*Extent: N.I.*

Where it has been demonstrated by performance of the applicable conformity assessment procedure that a toy will comply with the essential safety requirements during its foreseeable and normal period of use, the manufacturer must—

- (a) draw up an EC declaration of conformity in accordance with regulation 16(1) to (4); and
- (b) affix a CE marking in relation to the toy in accordance with regulation 18.
