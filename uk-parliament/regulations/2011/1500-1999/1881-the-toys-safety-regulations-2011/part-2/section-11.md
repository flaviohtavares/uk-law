# The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Design and manufacture of toys in accordance with essential safety requirements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The manufacturer must ensure that the toy has been designed and manufactured to comply with the essential safety requirements during its foreseeable and normal period of use.
