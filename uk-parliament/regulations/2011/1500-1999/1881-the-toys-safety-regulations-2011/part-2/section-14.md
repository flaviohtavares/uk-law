# The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 14. Application for Type examination[^M_E_bf11b90a-fe83-41e5-af50-e6db1453dc44][^c-ae47259a28200aa8f3ab28ad2eaf4455]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An application for Type examination to be performed in relation to a toy must—

- (a) be made to an approved[^c-a49351181324cd4f07cd14bf5894acd9] body;
- (b) be made in accordance with Module B;
- (c) include a description of the toy;
- (d) indicate the address at which the toy has been or will be manufactured; and
- (e) [^c-098fa0ce4d984a1ad3d7e63b676d15e0]... be accompanied by such fee as may be required by the body in accordance with regulation 50 (charging of fees by approved[^c-3852f5588cc55412210b346b29acdc11] body).

## 14. Application for EC-type examination

*Extent: N.I.*

An application for EC-type examination to be performed in relation to a toy must—

- (a) be made to a notified body;
- (b) be made in accordance with Module B;
- (c) include a description of the toy;
- (d) indicate the address at which the toy has been or will be manufactured; and
- (e) if the application is made to a UK notified body, be accompanied by such fee as may be required by the body in accordance with regulation 50 (charging of fees by UK notified body).
