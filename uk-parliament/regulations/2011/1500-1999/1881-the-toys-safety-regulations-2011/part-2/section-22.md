# The Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-02-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2011/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 22. Submission of Type examination certificate for review[^M_E_53cc5d6e-51e0-4c45-8675-21bb8f732fdb][^c-d961528382429487c815949ab9bed64f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A Type[^c-83c8077f13500917732a847111c16a9c] examination certificate issued in relation to a toy must be submitted by the manufacturer to an approved[^c-aaf00326e44339edcf1e4f5e169c2727] body for review if—

- (a) any change is made to—
  - (i) the manufacturing process for the toy;
  - (ii) any raw material used in the toy; or
  - (iii) any component of the toy;
- (b) 5 years have elapsed since the certificate was issued without it having being reviewed by an approved body;
- (c) 5 years have elapsed since the certificate was last reviewed by an approved body without it having being reviewed again by an approved body; or
- (d) the manufacturer is of the view that a review of the certificate is necessary for any other reason.

## 22. Submission of EC-type examination certificate for review

*Extent: N.I.*

An EC-type examination certificate issued in relation to a toy must be submitted by the manufacturer to a notified body for review if—

- (a) any change is made to—
  - (i) the manufacturing process for the toy;
  - (ii) any raw material used in the toy; or
  - (iii) any component of the toy;
- (b) 5 years have elapsed since the certificate was issued without it having being reviewed by a notified body;
- (c) 5 years have elapsed since the certificate was last reviewed by a notified body without it having being reviewed again by a notified body; or
- (d) the manufacturer is of the view that a review of the certificate is necessary for any other reason.
