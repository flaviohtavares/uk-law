# The Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 1690

Education, England

Education, Northern Ireland

The Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations Order 2011

Made

13th July 2011

Coming into force

14th July 2011

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 13th day of July 2011

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 2(1)(a) of Schedule 9 to the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations Order 2011 and comes into force on 14th July 2011.

## 2. Appointment of chair of the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation

Amanda Spielman is appointed to chair the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation.

Judith Simpson

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints Amanda Spielman to chair the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation, which is established under section 127 of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009. In accordance with paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 9 to that Act, the person appointed to that position is to be known as the Chief Regulator of Qualifications and Examinations.
