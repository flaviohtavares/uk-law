# The Agency Workers (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 2010/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/93) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] in relation to employment rights and duties[^f00002].

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Agency Workers (Amendment) Regulations 2011 and shall come into force on 1st September 2011.

## 2. Amendments to the Agency Workers Regulations 2010

(1) The Agency Workers Regulations 2010[^f00003] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3 (the meaning of agency worker), for paragraph (1)(b)(ii) substitute—

> - (ii) any other contract with the agency to perform work or services personally.

.

(3) In regulation 10 (permanent contracts providing for pay between assignments), in paragraph (1)(c) after “during any period under the contract” insert “after the end of the first assignment under that contract”.

(4) In regulation 14 (liability of temporary work agency and hirer)—

- (a) in paragraph (1) at the beginning insert “Subject to paragraph (3),”;
- (b) in paragraph (2) omit the words “Subject to paragraph (3),”;
- (c) in paragraph (3), for sub-paragraph (a) substitute—

  > - (a) obtained, or has taken reasonable steps to obtain, relevant information from the hirer—
  >   - (i) about the basic working and employment conditions in force in the hirer;
  >   - (ii) if needed to assess compliance with regulation 5, about the relevant terms and conditions under which an employee of the hirer is working where—
  >     - (aa) that employee is considered to be a comparable employee in relation to that agency worker for the purposes of regulation 5(4), and
  >     - (bb) those terms and conditions are ordinarily included in the contract of such a comparable employee;
  >
  >     and
  >   - (iii) which explains the basis on which it is considered that the employee referred to in sub-paragraph (ii)(aa) is a comparable employee;

  ; and
- (d) omit paragraph (4).

(5) In Schedule 2 (consequential amendments), in paragraph 16—

- (a) for “paragraph (o)” substitute “paragraph (p)”;
- (b) for “after paragraph (p)” substitute “after paragraph (q)”; and
- (c) in the words inserted as paragraph (q), for “(q)” substitute “(r)”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, issued free of charge, amend the Agency Workers Regulations 2010 (“the Principal Regulations”) to correct drafting errors in regulations 3, 10 and 14 and Schedule 2. The errors in the regulations concern the meaning of agency worker and the provisions concerning permanent contracts providing for pay between assignments and the liability of the temporary work agency and hirer. The error in the Schedule is a numbering error.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations since they have the same effect on the costs to business and the voluntary sector as the Principal Regulations. A copy of the impact assessment for the Principal Regulations is available from the BIS website (www.bis.gov.uk) and is published with the Explanatory Memorandum alongside the Principal Regulations on www.legislation.gov.uk.
