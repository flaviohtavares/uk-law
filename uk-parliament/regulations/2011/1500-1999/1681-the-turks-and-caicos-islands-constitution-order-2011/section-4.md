# The Turks and Caicos Islands Constitution Order 2011, section 4

> Text as at 2024-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Establishment of Constitution[^key-923b4ff8cf32ff30d642424d56731d18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 2 shall have effect as the Constitution of the Turks and Caicos Islands from the appointed day; but the Governor, acting in his or her discretion and with the prior approval of a Secretary of State, may by proclamation published in the *Gazette* provide that any provisions of the Constitution shall not come into force until such later day or days as he or she in like manner may appoint, and in that case such provisions shall come into force on the day or days so appointed.
