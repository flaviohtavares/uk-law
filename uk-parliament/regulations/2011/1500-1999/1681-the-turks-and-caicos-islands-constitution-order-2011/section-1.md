# The Turks and Caicos Islands Constitution Order 2011, section 1

> Text as at 2024-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-d4ebad6d1940a407363aa285161bf063]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Turks and Caicos Islands Constitution Order 2011.

(2) This Order shall come into force on such day as the Governor, acting in his or her discretion, may appoint by proclamation published in the *Gazette.*
