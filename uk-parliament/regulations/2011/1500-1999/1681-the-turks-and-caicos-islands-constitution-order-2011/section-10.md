> Text as at 2024-11-06.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Director of Public Prosecutions[^key-05de0d7d99bb98ef0eddf679c1b9bc9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Until a person is appointed to the office of Director of Public Prosecutions in accordance with the Constitution, the Attorney General shall perform the functions of that office.
