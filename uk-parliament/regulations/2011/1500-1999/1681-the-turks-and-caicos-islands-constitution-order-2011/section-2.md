# The Turks and Caicos Islands Constitution Order 2011, section 2

> Text as at 2024-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-0650c622c9a484a7724955c5a4328777]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Order—

- “the appointed day” means the day appointed by the Governor under section 1(2);
- “the Constitution” means the Constitution set out in Schedule 2;
- “the existing Constitution” means the Constitution set out in Schedule 2 to the Turks and Caicos Islands Constitution Order 2006[^f00002] as amended and supplemented by the Turks and Caicos Islands Constitution (Interim Amendment) Order 2009[^f00003] and the Turks and Caicos Islands (Interim Amendment) (Amendment) Order 2010[^f00004].

(2) Section 131 of the Constitution shall apply for the purposes of interpreting sections 1 to 14 of this Order and otherwise in relation to those sections as it applies for the purposes of interpreting and in relation to the Constitution.
