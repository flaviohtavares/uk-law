# The Gas and Electricity (Registers) (Revocation) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 1420

Gas

Electricity

The Gas and Electricity (Registers) (Revocation) Order 2013

Made

6th June 2013

Laid before Parliament

12th June 2013

Coming into force

12th July 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36(4) and (5) of the Gas Act 1986[^f00001] and section 49(5) and (6) of the Electricity Act 1989[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Gas and Electricity (Registers) (Revocation) Order 2013 and comes into force on 12th July 2013.

## 2. Revocation of the Gas (Register) Order 1988

The Gas (Register) Order 1988[^f00003] is revoked.

## 3. Revocation of the Electricity (Register) Order 1990

The Electricity (Register) Order 1990[^f00004] is revoked.

Verma

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Energy and Climate Change

6th June 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Gas (Register) Order 1988 ([S.I. 1988/159](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/159)) and the Electricity (Register) Order 1990 ([S.I. 1990/194](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/194)).

The Gas (Register) Order 1988 prescribed the hours during which the register maintained by the Director General of Gas Supply (later the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority) under section 36 of the Gas Act [1986 (c.44)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/44) was to be kept open for public inspection and the fees payable to the Director for the supply of copies or extracts from that register.

The Electricity (Register) Order 1990 prescribed the hours during which the register maintained by the Director General of Electricity Supply (later the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority) under section 49 of the Electricity Act [1989 (c.29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/29) was to be kept open for public inspection and the fees payable to the Director for the supply of copies or extracts from that register.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen. The gas and electricity registers are now available online free of charge.
