# The Chemical Weapons (Licence Appeal Provisions) (Revocation) (No. 2) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 1129

Chemical Weapons

The Chemical Weapons (Licence Appeal Provisions) (Revocation) (No. 2) Order 2013

Made

14th May 2013

Laid before Parliament

20th May 2013

Coming into force

12th June 2013

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 20(4) of the Chemical Weapons Act 1996[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Chemical Weapons (Licence Appeal Provisions) (Revocation) (No. 2) Order 2013 and comes into force on 12th June 2013.

## 2. Revocation

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Chemical Weapons (Licence Appeal Provisions) Order 1996[^f00002] is revoked.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to a decision of the Secretary of State made before the coming into force of this Order—

- (a) to refuse to grant, renew or vary a licence; or
- (b) to vary or revoke a licence.

**3.** The Chemical Weapons (Licence Appeal Provisions) (Revocation) Order 2013[^f00003] is revoked.

Verma

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Energy and Climate Change

14th May 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes, with a saving, the Chemical Weapons (Licence Appeal Provisions) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/3030](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3030)). That Order made provision under section 20(4) of the Chemical Weapons Act [1996 (c. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/6) with respect to appeals against the refusal to grant, renew or vary a licence or against a variation or revocation of a licence.

This Order also revokes the Chemical Weapons (Licence Appeal Provisions) (Revocation) Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/1045](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1045)). That Order made provision repealing the Chemical Weapons (Licence Appeal Provisions) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/3030](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3030)) and was made and registered but was not printed.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
