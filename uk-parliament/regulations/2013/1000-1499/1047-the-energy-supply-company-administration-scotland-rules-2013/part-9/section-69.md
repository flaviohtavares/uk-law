# The Energy Supply Company Administration (Scotland) Rules 2013, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Application under section 176A(5) of the 1986 Act to disapply section 176A of the 1986 Act

An application under section 176A(5) of the 1986 Act (share of assets for unsecured creditors) shall include averments as to—

- (a) the fact that the application arises in the course of an energy supply company administration;
- (b) the financial position of the energy supply company;
- (c) the basis of the energy administrator’s view that the cost of making a distribution to unsecured creditors would be disproportionate to the benefits; and
- (d) whether any other insolvency practitioner is acting in relation to the energy supply company and, if so, the address of that insolvency practitioner.
