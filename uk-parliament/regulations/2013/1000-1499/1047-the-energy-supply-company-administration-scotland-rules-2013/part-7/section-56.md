# The Energy Supply Company Administration (Scotland) Rules 2013, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Notification by energy administrator of court order

(1) Where the court makes an order to end the energy supply company administration, the energy supply company administrator shall notify the registrar of companies in Form ESCA16(S), attaching a copy of the court order and a copy of the final progress report.

(2) Where the court makes an order to end the energy supply company administration and the applicant was not the energy administrator then that applicant shall give a copy of the order to the energy administrator.
