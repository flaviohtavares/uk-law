# The Energy Supply Company Administration (Scotland) Rules 2013, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Notice of intention to resign

The energy administrator must give to the persons specified below at least 5 business days’ notice in Form ESCA20(S) of the energy administrator’s intention to resign, or to apply for the court’s leave to do so—

- (a) the Secretary of State;
- (b) GEMA;
- (c) if there is a continuing energy administrator of the energy supply company, to the continuing energy administrator; and
- (d) if there is no such energy administrator, to the energy supply company and its creditors.
