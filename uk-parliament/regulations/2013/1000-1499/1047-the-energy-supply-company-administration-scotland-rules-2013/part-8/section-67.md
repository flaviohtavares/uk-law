# The Energy Supply Company Administration (Scotland) Rules 2013, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Joint or concurrent appointments

(1) Where a person is appointed in accordance with paragraph 103 of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act to act as energy administrator jointly or concurrently with the persons then acting, the same provisions shall apply, subject to this Rule and to such other modification as may be necessary, in respect of the making of this appointment as in the case of the original appointment of an administrator.

(2) The additional energy administrator shall send notice of the appointment in Form ESCA23(S) to the registrar of companies.
