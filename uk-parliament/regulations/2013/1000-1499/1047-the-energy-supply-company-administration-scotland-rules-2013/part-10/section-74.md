# The Energy Supply Company Administration (Scotland) Rules 2013, section 74

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 74. Retention of proxies

(1) Proxies used for voting at any meeting shall be retained by the chair of the meeting.

(2) The chair shall deliver the proxies forthwith after the meeting to the energy administrator (where that is someone other than the chair).

(3) The energy administrator shall retain all proxies in the sederunt book.
