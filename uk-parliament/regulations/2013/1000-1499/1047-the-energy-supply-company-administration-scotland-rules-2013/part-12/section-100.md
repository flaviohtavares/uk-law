# The Energy Supply Company Administration (Scotland) Rules 2013, section 100

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 100. Omission of unobtainable information

Information required under Rules 97 and 98 to be included in a notice may be omitted if it is not reasonably practicable to obtain it.
