# The Energy Supply Company Administration (Scotland) Rules 2013, section 91

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 91. Confidentiality of documents

(1) Where the energy administrator considers, in the case of a document forming part of the records of those proceedings—

- (a) that it should be treated as confidential; or
- (b) that it is of such nature that its disclosure would be calculated to be injurious to the interest of the creditors or the members,

the energy administrator may decline to allow it to be inspected by a person who would otherwise be entitled to inspect it.

(2) Where under this Rule the energy administrator refuses inspection of a document, the person who made that request may apply to the court for an order to overrule the refusal and the court may either overrule it altogether, or sustain it, either unconditionally or subject to such conditions, if any, as it thinks fit to impose.

(3) Nothing in this Rule entitles the energy administrator to decline to allow inspection of any claim or proxy.
