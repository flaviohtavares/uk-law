# The Energy Supply Company Administration (Scotland) Rules 2013, section 93

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 93. Punishment of offences

Section 431 (summary proceedings) of the 1986 Act, as it applies to Scotland, has effect in relation to offences under these Rules as to offences under the 1986 Act.
