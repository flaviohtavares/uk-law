# The Energy Supply Company Administration (Scotland) Rules 2013, section 94

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 94. Forms for use in energy administration proceedings

(1) The forms contained in the Schedule to these Rules shall be used in, and in connection with, energy supply company administration proceedings.

(2) The forms shall be used with such variations, if any, as the circumstances may require.
