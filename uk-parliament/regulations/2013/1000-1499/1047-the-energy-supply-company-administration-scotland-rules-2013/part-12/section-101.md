# The Energy Supply Company Administration (Scotland) Rules 2013, section 101

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 101. Fees, expenses, etc.

(1) All fees, costs, charges and other expenses incurred in the course of the energy administration are to be regarded as expenses of the energy supply company administration.

(2) The expenses associated with the prescribed part shall be paid out of the prescribed part.
