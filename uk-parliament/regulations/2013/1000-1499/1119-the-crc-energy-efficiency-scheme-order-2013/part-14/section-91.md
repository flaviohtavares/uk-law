# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 91

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Standard of proof (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where an appeal is made to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the Scottish Ministers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the Planning Appeals Commission; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) an independent person appointed under paragraph (3) of article 89. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Where this paragraph applies, the standard of proof to be applied by the appeal body in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a breach of a provision of this Order; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) in respect of any determination by the administrator under this Order, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

is proof on the balance on probabilities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
