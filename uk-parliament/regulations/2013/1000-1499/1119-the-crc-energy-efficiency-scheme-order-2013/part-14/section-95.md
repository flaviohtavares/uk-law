# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 95

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. National security (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No provision of this Order requires the Crown to provide information to the administrator or to any other person where to do so would, in the opinion of the person who holds or controls the information, be contrary to the interests of national security. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
