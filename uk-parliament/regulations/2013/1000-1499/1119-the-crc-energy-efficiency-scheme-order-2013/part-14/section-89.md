# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 89

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Appeal body (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the case of an appeal against a determination, notice or penalty made or given by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the Secretary of State, the appeal body is the First-tier Tribunal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the Environment Agency, the appeal body is the First-tier Tribunal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) the Natural Resources Body for Wales, the appeal body is the First-tier Tribunal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) the Scottish Environment Protection Agency, the appeal body is the Scottish Ministers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (e) the chief inspector, the appeal body is the Planning Appeals Commission. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Paragraph (3) applies where the appellant is or includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the Natural Resources Body for Wales; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the Scottish Environment Protection Agency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Where this paragraph applies, the appeal body is an independent person which the following appoint in writing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers, where the appellant is or includes the Natural Resources Body for Wales; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the Scottish Ministers, where the appellant is or includes the Scottish Environment Protection Agency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) Where the appellant is or includes the chief inspector, the appeal body is the Planning Appeals Commission. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(5) For the purposes of this article, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**independent person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” means a person who has no individual interest in the matter subject to the appeal and is independent of the parties to the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
