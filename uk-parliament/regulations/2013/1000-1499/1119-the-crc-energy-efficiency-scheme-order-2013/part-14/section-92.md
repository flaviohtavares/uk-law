# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 92

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Determination of an appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The appeal body may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) in respect of a determination, enforcement notice or penalty— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) cancel or affirm it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] if it affirms it, do so in its original form or with such modification as it sees fit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) instruct the administrator to do or not to do any thing which is within the power of the administrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
