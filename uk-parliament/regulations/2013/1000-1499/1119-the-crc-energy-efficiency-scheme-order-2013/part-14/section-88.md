# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 88

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Grounds of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The grounds on which a determination, notice or penalty may be appealed are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) that it was based on an error of fact; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) that it was wrong in law; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) that it was unreasonable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
