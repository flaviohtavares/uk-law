# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 87

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Appeals: general (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following appeals may be made to the appeal body under this Order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) an appeal by a public body or undertaking notified of a determination referred to in article 57(1), against that determination; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) an appeal by a person served with an enforcement notice, against that notice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) an appeal by a public body or undertaking given notice that they are liable to a civil penalty, against the imposition of that penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
