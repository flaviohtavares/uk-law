# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, SCHEDULE 3

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 3 (affected by repeal, see note) — Undertakings and participant equivalents (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

### Undertakings and participant equivalents (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

#### 1. Undertakings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

In this Order, subject to paragraph 4— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) in relation to an undertaking, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**group *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” means those undertakings which are group undertakings in respect of each other; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) “**group undertaking *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” has the meaning given by section 1161(5) of the Companies Act 2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] [^c21789111] but where “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**undertaking *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” has the meaning given in this Order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) “highest parent undertaking” is the undertaking in the group which is not a subsidiary of any other undertaking in the group; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) “**member *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” in relation to group undertakings means an undertaking which is part of a group; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (e) “parent undertaking”, “subsidiary undertaking” and related expressions have the same meanings as in Part 38 of the Companies Act 2006 but where “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**undertaking *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” has the meaning given in this Order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (f) “**undertaking *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) an undertaking as defined in section 1161(1) of the Companies Act 2006; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] as if that definition included an unincorporated association that has a charitable purpose, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

  but an undertaking does not include a public body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

#### 2. Participant equivalents (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(1) An undertaking is a “participant equivalent” when so provided under this paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) for the purposes of Part 2; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) for the whole phase. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Subject to sub-paragraph (3), for the purposes of Part 2 where an undertaking (“B”)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) is a member of a group that is a participant (“G”); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) would, if B was not a member of G, have been required to register as a participant under Part 2, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

B is a participant equivalent as a member of G. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) B would be a participant equivalent as a member of G under sub-paragraph (2); but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) G is subject to article 27(2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

the provisions of section 1 of Part 3 of Schedule 5 vary sub-paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

#### 3. Participant equivalents: movement between groups during a phase (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(1) Subject to paragraph 4, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) B was a participant equivalent as a member of a group under paragraph 2; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) during a phase B becomes a member of another participant group of undertakings ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] “ G2 ”),

B is a participant equivalent as a member of G2 for the remainder of that phase. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Paragraph 12 of section 2 of Part 3 of Schedule 5 makes provision in relation to the change described in sub-paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Subject to paragraph 4, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) B is a participant equivalent as a member of a group under paragraph 2; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) during a phase B becomes a member of a group of undertakings which is not a participant ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] “ G3 ”),

paragraph 10 of section 2 of Part 3 of Schedule 5 makes provision in relation to that change. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) Subject to paragraph 4, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) B is a participant equivalent as a member of G under paragraph 2; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) during a phase B together with B's subsidiary undertakings cease to be a member of G, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

paragraph 9 of Part 3 of Schedule 5 makes provision in relation to that change. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

#### 4. Undertakings or groups of undertakings as participants (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

Where during a phase an undertaking or group of undertakings— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) is a member of a group; but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) is a participant separate from the group, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

for such time as it is a participant separate from the group, it must be treated as if it was not a member of the group for the phase. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
