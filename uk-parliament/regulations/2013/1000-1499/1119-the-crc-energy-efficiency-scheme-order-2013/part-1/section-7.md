# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 7

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Penalties, offences, appeals, revocations, continuing effect and amendments (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A participant which fails to comply with this Order may be liable under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) Part 12 to a civil penalty; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) Part 13 to a criminal penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Part 14 provides for appeals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Part 15 provides for revocations, continuing effect and amendments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
