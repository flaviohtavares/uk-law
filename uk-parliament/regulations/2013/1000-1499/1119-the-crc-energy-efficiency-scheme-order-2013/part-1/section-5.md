# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 5

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Registration and requirements of participants and others (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Part 2 provides for registration as a participant for a phase of the scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) In respect of a phase a participant must comply with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) Part 3 to provide annual reports on CRC supplies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) Part 4 to surrender allowances equal to the participant's CRC emissions; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) Part 5 to keep and audit records relating to the requirements of Part 2 to 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) The following have effect in respect of Parts 2 to 5— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) Schedule 2 (public bodies); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) Schedule 3 (undertakings and participant equivalents); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) Schedule 4 (information on registration); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) Schedule 5 (changes to participants). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) Part 6 provides for persons to provide information and assistance to participants and the administrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
