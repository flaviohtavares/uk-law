# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 10

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Co-operation and provision of information (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The bodies constituting the administrator must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) co-operate with each other; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) provide each other with such of the information provided to or obtained by them under any of Parts 2 to 4, 6 to 8, 10 or 11 of this Order as they may require to enable them to carry out their duties as an administrator under this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The administrator must provide to a national authority such of the information described in paragraph (1)(b) as that authority may lawfully require in relation to compliance with and enforcement of this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
