# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 8

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Groups: liability to comply with this Order (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where an applicant or a participant is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a group of undertakings; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) an independent college group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Each member of a group described in paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) is jointly and severally liable to comply with requirements placed on the group under Parts 2 to 12; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) may be liable to a criminal penalty under Part 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) For a group of public bodies (except an independent college group)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the body listed in article 55(4) which is a member of that group is liable to comply with Part 2 and not any other member of the group; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the body in whose name the compliance account is set up is liable to comply with requirements placed on the group under Parts 3 to 12 and not any other member of the group; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) subject to article 86, any member of that group may be liable to a criminal penalty under Part 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
