# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 2

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. The trading scheme: phases and application (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order establishes a trading scheme in relation to scheme activities for a trading period of six phases which comprise— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) five consecutive phases, each of five years, where the initial phase commences on 1st April 2014; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) a final phase of four years, commencing on 1st April 2039. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) This Order does not apply to an organisation which enjoys an exemption or relief from taxes under Schedule 1 to the International Organisations Act 1968 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] [^c21788541].
