# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 6

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Powers and duties of the administrator (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The administrator has the powers and duties set out under the following Parts of this Order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) Part 7 to administer the scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) Part 8 to publish information relating to a participant's performance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) Part 9 to impose charges; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) Part 10 to monitor compliance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (e) Part 11 to enforce failures to comply with this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
