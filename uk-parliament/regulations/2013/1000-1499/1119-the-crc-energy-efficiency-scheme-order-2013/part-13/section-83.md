# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 83

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Penalties (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person guilty of an offence under article 82(1) or (2) is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) in England and Wales or Northern Ireland— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) on summary conviction to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] a fine not exceeding £50,000 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1500424fdbe96c16a5e6618aec81d4c8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] a fine *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 3 months, or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] on conviction on indictment, to a fine or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 2 years, or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

  and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) in Scotland— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding £50,000 or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 12 months, or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] on conviction on indictment, to a fine or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 2 years, or both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) A person guilty of an offence under article 82(4) or (5) is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) in England and Wales or Northern Ireland— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 3 months, or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] on conviction on indictment, to a fine or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 2 years, or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

  and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) in Scotland— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 12 months, or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] on conviction on indictment, to a fine or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 2 years, or both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
