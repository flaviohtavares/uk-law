# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 85

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Scottish partnerships (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where an offence under this Part is committed by a Scottish partnership and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) it is committed with the consent or connivance of a partner; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) it is attributable to any neglect on the partner's part, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

the partner as well as the partnership is guilty of the offence and is liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) In paragraph (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**partner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” includes a person purporting to act as a partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
