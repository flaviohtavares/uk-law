# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 86

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. The Crown (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order applies to the Crown but no contravention of it by the Crown makes the Crown criminally liable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1), this Order applies to persons in the public service of the Crown as it applies to other persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
