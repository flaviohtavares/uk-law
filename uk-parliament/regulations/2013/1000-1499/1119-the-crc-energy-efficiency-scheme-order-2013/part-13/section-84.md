# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 84

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Bodies corporate (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where an offence under this Part is committed by a body corporate and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) it is committed with the consent or connivance of an officer; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) it is attributable to any neglect on the officer's part, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

the officer as well as the body corporate is guilty of the offence and is liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) An “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**”, in relation to a body corporate, means a director, manager, secretary or other similar officer of the body, or a person purporting to act in any such capacity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) If the affairs of a body corporate are managed by its members, paragraph (1) applies in relation to the acts or defaults of a member in connection with that member's functions of management as if the member were a director of the body corporate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
