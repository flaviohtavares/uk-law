# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 61

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Chargeable activities (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In article 60, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**chargeable activity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” means any of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) registration of a participant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) maintaining a participant for each year of a phase where it is a participant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) establishing an account, other than a compliance account; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) maintaining such account for each year of a phase where it is required; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (e) making a determination under article 31(3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The administrator— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) must require the charge for registration as a participant to be paid before it makes the registration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) in respect of any other charge, may require it to be paid before it carries out the relevant chargeable activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) is not required to reimburse a charge paid where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) the chargeable activity is not completed; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] an applicant or participant liable to pay it does not remain within the scheme for all the period in respect of which the charge is payable or has been calculated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
