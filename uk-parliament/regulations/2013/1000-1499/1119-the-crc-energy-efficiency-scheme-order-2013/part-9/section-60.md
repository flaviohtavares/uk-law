# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 60

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Charges (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The administrator may charge an applicant or participant for the chargeable activities in article 61. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Payment of a charge is not received by the administrator until the administrator has cleared funds for the full amount due and a charge if unpaid may be recovered by the administrator as a civil debt. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) A charge must be calculated by reference to the costs of administering the scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) The administrator may apply different charges for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the same chargeable activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) different classes of applicant or participant in respect of the same chargeable activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
