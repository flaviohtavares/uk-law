# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 64

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Collection and remittance of charges (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An administrator (other than the Environment Agency) must pay to the Secretary of State any charge received by it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) An administrator—— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) may collect a charge on behalf of another administrator; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) must remit charges received to the Secretary of State or, where the Secretary of State directs, as directed to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) the Scottish Ministers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] the Welsh Ministers; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] the Department of the Environment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
