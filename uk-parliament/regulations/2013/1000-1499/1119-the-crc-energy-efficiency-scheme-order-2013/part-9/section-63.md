# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 63

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Revised charges (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The administrator may draw up a revised charging document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the administrator must not apply a revised charging document unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) in such manner as it considers appropriate for bringing a proposed document to the attention of those likely to be affected by it, the administrator— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) sets out its proposals; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] specifies the period within which representations or objections must be made to it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

  and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) it receives approval to the revised charging document from the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) The Secretary of State must consider any representations or objections made by any person to the proposed revised charging document before the Secretary of State decides whether or not to approve it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) The administrator must not take the steps required under paragraph (2)(a) unless it first consults the following on its proposals— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the Scottish Ministers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) the Welsh Ministers; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) the Northern Ireland departments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(5) A revised charging document must be published and made available before it is to take effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
