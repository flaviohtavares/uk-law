# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 62

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Amount of charges (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The amount of a charge payable under article 60(1) is that set out in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) version 1 of the document named “CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Charges” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c21788871] made available by the administrator on or before this Order is made; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) any replacement or revision of that document (“revised charging document”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Article 60(3) must be complied with in respect of the amount of a charge under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
