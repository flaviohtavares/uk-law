#### 7. Franchise agreements (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the premises described in sub-paragraph (1); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) other premises used by the franchisee in relation to carrying on the franchise business. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

#### 8. Franchise agreements not existing (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

#### 9. Supplies to franchisees regarded as supplies to franchisors (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
