# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 66

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Inspections (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following paragraphs, the administrator may inspect any premises and any thing in or on those premises in order to monitor compliance with Parts 2 to 9 of this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Reasonable prior notice must be given before exercising the power of inspection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) An administrator may authorise such persons (“authorised persons”) who appear suitable to exercise the administrator's powers of inspection under this article. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) A person in control of the premises to which the administrator or authorised person reasonably requires access must allow the administrator or authorised person to have access to those premises. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(5) A person acting on behalf of the administrator may, when inspecting premises— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) require the production of any record; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) take measurements, photographs, recordings or copies of any thing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) require any person at the premises to provide facilities and assistance to the extent that is within that person's control. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(6) The power of inspection does not apply to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a prohibited place for the purposes of the Official Secrets Act 1911 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] [^c21788891]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) any other premises to which the Crown restricts access on the grounds of national security, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

except to the extent agreed by the person in control of such place or premises. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
