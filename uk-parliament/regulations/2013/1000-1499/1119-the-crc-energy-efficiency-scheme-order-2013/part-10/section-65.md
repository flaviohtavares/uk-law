# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 65

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Compliance notices (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The administrator may request a person to provide it with such information as it believes it requires in relation to monitoring compliance with Parts 2 to 9 of this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The administrator must request the information referred to in paragraph (1) by a written notice (“a compliance notice”) served on the person to whom it is addressed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) A compliance notice may be in such form as the administrator sees fit but must state the date by which compliance with the notice is required. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) A compliance notice may be varied or revoked in writing by the administrator at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(5) Where a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) fails to comply with a compliance notice; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) in the opinion of the administrator, supplies incomplete or inaccurate information, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

the administrator may instead determine *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] [^c21788881] the information requested. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
