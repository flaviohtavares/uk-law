# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 28

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Determinations by the administrator (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The administrator may determine *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] [^c21788841] whether or not— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) an undertaking is a member of a group; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) article 27(2) applies to a group or undertaking. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
