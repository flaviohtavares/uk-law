# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 13

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Registration and certificates (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the administrator is satisfied that an application has been duly made, it must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) register the applicant as a participant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) issue a certificate of registration to the participant, the certificate to be in such form as the administrator thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) An applicant registered under paragraph (1) is a participant for the relevant phase, unless the administrator cancels that registration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Until the end of 31st March 2025,[^c-b4583bd12f0df1db4ae78b878e471c73] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] the administrator must maintain an up to date list of participants. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
