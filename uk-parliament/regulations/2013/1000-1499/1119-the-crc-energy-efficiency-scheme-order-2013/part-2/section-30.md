# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 30

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Operators: separate participation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) an undertaking (“O”) is an operator; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) O is required to register as a participant, whether on its own or as a member of a group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) O may apply for registration as a separate participant in respect of any relevant trust in relation to which O carries on a regulated activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) O is registered as a participant in respect of a relevant trust as described in sub- paragraph (2); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) O would otherwise be part of a group which is a participant for the relevant phase, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

O is not to be regarded as part of that group for the phase in respect of the scheme activities of the relevant trust. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) In this article, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**relevant trust *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” means a trust where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the assets of the trust include premises to which a supply of electricity or gas is made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) no beneficiary of the trust is entitled to half or more of the assets of the trust; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) the trust carries on scheme activities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
