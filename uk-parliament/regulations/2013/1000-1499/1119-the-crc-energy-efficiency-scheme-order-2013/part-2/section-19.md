# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 19

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Universities and colleges: England (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This chapter applies to governing bodies of a college of a university and a university— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) described in Part 4 of Schedule 1 to the 2000 Act; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) where the university is wholly or mainly situated in England. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) For the purposes of this chapter, whether a college is a college of a university is determined on the qualification day of the qualification year, whatever applied earlier in that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
