# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 27

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Changes to undertakings and groups (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a requirement to register applies to a group or undertaking under article 24 or 25; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) a change described in section 1 of Part 3 of Schedule 5 applies to that group or undertaking after the qualification day but before the group or undertaking makes an application for registration in accordance with article 12 (“the post-qualification period”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Where this paragraph applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a group or undertaking to which article 24 or 25 would otherwise apply must instead register as a participant as provided by section 1 of Part 3 of Schedule 5; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) other undertakings affected by such change must comply with that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
