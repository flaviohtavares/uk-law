# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 14

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Government departments and the devolved administrations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following public bodies must apply for registration as a participant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a government department; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the Scottish Ministers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) the Welsh Assembly Government; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) a Northern Ireland Department; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (e) a public body in respect of which a local authority decision is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Where a public body listed in paragraph (1) is a member of a group, paragraph (1) applies to that group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Paragraph (1) is not satisfied in respect of a body described in sub-paragraphs (a) to (d) where part only of that body is registered as a participant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
