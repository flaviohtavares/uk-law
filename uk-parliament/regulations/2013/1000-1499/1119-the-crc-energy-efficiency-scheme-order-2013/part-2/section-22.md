# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 22

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Applications (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where this article applies, the following which exist must apply for registration as separate participants in respect of a phase— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the university and colleges; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) an independent college group; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) an independent college which is not a member of one of the groups listed in sub-paragraph (a) or (b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The administrator must be notified with the application— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) by the university and colleges— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) whether or not an independent college is a member of the group; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] if not, the identity of the independent college; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) by an independent college or an independent college group, the identity of the university. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
