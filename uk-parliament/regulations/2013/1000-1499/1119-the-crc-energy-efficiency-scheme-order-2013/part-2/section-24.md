# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 24

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Undertakings: applications by groups (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies to a group of undertakings but is subject to article 27. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Subject to paragraph (4), a group must apply for registration as a participant in respect of a phase where during the qualification year for that phase, it meets the qualification criteria. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Paragraph (2) applies notwithstanding the fact that an insolvency procedure is applied to a group member during the qualification year or post-qualification period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) Paragraph (2) does not apply where the whole of that group has permanently ceased carrying on a scheme activity in the United Kingdom *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] on or before the last date for making an application for registration provided by article 12[^key-19801971d4620a6b02ee4a0c9d4545c9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6].

(5) An insolvency procedure is applied to an undertaking for the purposes of this article in the circumstances described by paragraph 120(7) or 120(9) of Schedule 6 to the Finance Act 2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] [^c21788831].
