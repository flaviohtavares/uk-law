# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 25

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Undertakings: applications other than by groups (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) and article 27, an undertaking must apply for registration as a participant in respect of a phase where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) it is not a member of a group; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) during the qualification year for that phase, it meets the qualification criteria. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Paragraph (1) applies notwithstanding the fact that an insolvency procedure is applied to an undertaking during the qualification year or post-qualification period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Paragraph (1) does not apply where an undertaking has permanently ceased carrying on a scheme activity in the United Kingdom *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] on or before the last date for making an application for registration provided by article 12[^c-0e406eb5fd50bb7e0c9791e5e49d900b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6].

(4) An insolvency procedure is applied to an undertaking for the purposes of this article in the circumstances described by paragraph 120(7) or 120(9) of Schedule 6 to the Finance Act 2000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
