# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 26

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Undertakings: disaggregation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) an undertaking or a group of undertakings (“B”) is a member of a group (“A”); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) at the time A applies for registration in accordance with article 12, B is not or does not include the highest parent undertaking registered in the United Kingdom that is a member of A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Paragraph (3) applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) A applies for registration in accordance with article 11; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] [^key-8d9080bf61bb6ff0139bd9e171663526]...
- (b) B applies for registration as a participant separate from A by the last working day of April in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] any year of a phase; and[^key-5228db1b386660da691db34ae2b15f19] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c)[^key-63969c152a3029770180fc91c8a476e9] A notifies the administrator that A agrees that B may apply for registration as a separate participant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) When this paragraph applies and the applications by A and B are duly made the administrator must register A and B as separate participants. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
