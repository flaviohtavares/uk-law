# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 11

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Applications, information and charges (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A requirement to apply for registration as a participant means that an application for registration must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) be made to the administrator and, unless otherwise agreed by the administrator, be made using the Registry; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) the information described in Schedule 4; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] the charge for registration as a participant under article 60. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) When requested by the administrator, the applicant must provide such suitable and up to date evidence of identity as the administrator may require in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the intended account holder of the compliance account; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the individuals who will access the compliance account. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) The administrator may require other information from applicants or any particular applicant in order to effect a registration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) The requirements to apply for registration as a participant under this Part apply in respect of each phase. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
