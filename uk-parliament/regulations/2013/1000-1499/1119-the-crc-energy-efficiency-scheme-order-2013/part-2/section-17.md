# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 17

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Public bodies: applications other than by groups (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies to a public body which is not a member of a group and is not a body to which article 14(1) or chapter 3 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Where this paragraph applies, the public body must apply for registration as a participant in respect of a phase where, during the qualification year for that phase or any part of that year, it meets the qualification criteria. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
