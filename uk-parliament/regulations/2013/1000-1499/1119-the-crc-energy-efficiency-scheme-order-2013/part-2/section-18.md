# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 18

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Determinations by the administrator (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the administrator may determine *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] [^c21788821] whether or not a public body is a member of a group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to a public body or group to which any of the following apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) chapter 3; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) paragraph 7, 8 or 9 of section 2 of Schedule 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) a government decision or local authority decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
