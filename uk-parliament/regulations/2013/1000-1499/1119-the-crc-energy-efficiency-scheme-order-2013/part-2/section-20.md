# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 20

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Qualifying electricity (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The governing bodies of colleges of a university and the university (“the university and colleges”) are a group for the purposes of paragraph (2) whether or not those bodies have a legal identity separate from each other. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Articles 21 and 22 apply where the university and colleges meet the qualification criteria. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Where the university and colleges do not meet the qualification criteria, none of them are required to apply for registration as a participant in respect of a phase. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
