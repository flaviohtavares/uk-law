# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 15

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Other public bodies (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies to a public body except a public body to which article 14 or chapter 3 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Except where a government decision provides to the contrary, for the purposes of articles 16 and 17, whether— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a group exists or not; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) whether a public body is or is not a member of a group, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

are matters determined on the qualification day of the qualification year, whatever applied earlier in that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
