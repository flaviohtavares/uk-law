# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, SCHEDULE 6

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 6 (affected by repeal, see note) — The Registry (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 50 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

### 1. Setting up accounts (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(1) The administrator must ensure the Registry allows the following accounts to be held— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a compliance account for a participant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) as agreed by the administrator— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) additional accounts for a participant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] accounts for third parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The administrator may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) limit the number of accounts in respect of a participant or third party; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) set up other accounts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) The administrator must set up one compliance account for a participant where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) it has registered an applicant as a participant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) it has completed to its satisfaction the identity checks required under article 52(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) The public body or undertaking in whose name an account is held is the account holder. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

### 2. Account holders and information (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

The administrator must ensure that the Registry provides the following information— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) to an account holder— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) the number of allowances it holds; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] a summary of any transfer, surrender or cancellation of allowances relating to that account holder made during the previous five years; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) to a participant, its CRC emissions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) from CRC supplies in its most recent annual report, where so provided; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] from CRC supplies determined under article 33(2); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] applied under article 74(4)(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) to a participant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) the number of allowances in its compliance account which are available to comply with Part 4; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] matters notified to the participant by the administrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

### 3. Recording of transfers between accounts (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

The administrator must ensure that the Registry records the transfer of allowances between accounts made by account holders and to make that record— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) for a transfer made on a working day, if possible, that working day or otherwise the following working day; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) for a transfer made on a non-working day, if possible the following working day or otherwise the next following working day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

### 4. Updating of accounts (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

The administrator must ensure that the cancellation and surrender of allowances is recorded in the relevant accounts and that record is made as soon as practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

### 5. Non-compliance accounts and third party accounts (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(1) A participant may request the administrator to provide accounts for it in the Registry in addition to a compliance account, on terms agreed by the administrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) A person who is not a participant (“a third party”) may request the administrator to provide accounts for it in the Registry on terms agreed by the administrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Such terms must require that the participant or third party complies with any administrative rules drawn up by the administrator under article 50(4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
