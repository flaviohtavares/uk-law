# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 34

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Changes affecting participants (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where changes affecting a participant take place in an annual reporting year as described in Part 1 or 2, or section 2 of Part 3, of Schedule 5— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the participant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) in respect of section 2 of Part 3 of Schedule 5, undertakings which are not participants, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

must comply with such of those provisions as are applicable to them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
