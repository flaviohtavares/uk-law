# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 31

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Provision of annual reports (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A participant must provide to the administrator a report which complies with article 32 on its CRC supplies during an annual reporting year (“an annual report”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) A participant must provide the annual report— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) unless otherwise agreed by the administrator, using the Registry; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) by no later than the last working day of July after the end of the annual reporting year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Where by 40 days after the due date a participant has failed to provide the annual report, the administrator may determine *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] [^c21788851] the report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
