# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 72

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Discretion in waiving, imposition and modification of civil penalties (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the administrator considers appropriate, the administrator may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) waive a civil penalty; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) allow additional time to pay; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) impose a lower financial penalty or substitute a lower financial penalty where one has already been imposed; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) modify the application of a publication or blocking penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Where at any time before a financial penalty is due to be paid the administrator ceases to be satisfied that the person is liable for that penalty, the administrator may serve a further notice on that person to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) withdraw the penalty notice; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) modify the penalty notice by substituting a lower financial penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
