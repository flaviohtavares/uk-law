# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 74

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Failures in respect of annual reports (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The penalties in the following paragraphs apply where a participant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) fails to provide an annual report contrary to article 31(1); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) provides late an annual report contrary to article 31(2)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The penalties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a financial penalty of £5000 and publication; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) where the report is provided— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) no more than 40 days after the due date, a financial penalty of £500 for each day the report is late after the due date; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] more than 40 days after the due date or not at all, a financial penalty of £40,000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Paragraphs (4) and (5) apply where the annual report is provided *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] after the last working day of October after the end of the annual reporting year[^key-d79f4739aad0655cfc50b8bd203efa2f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] or not at all. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) Where this paragraph applies, the following additional penalties apply to the participant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the CRC emissions of the participant for the year to which the annual report relates are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) double the CRC emissions reported in the annual report of the previous year; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] where no such report exists, double the CRC emissions which the administrator calculates the participant made in the year for which the annual report is not provided; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the participant must immediately acquire allowances and surrender them in accordance with Part 4 equal to the CRC emissions which apply under sub-paragraph (a) (or such additional allowances having regard to any allowances surrendered on time for the annual reporting year); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) a financial penalty of £40 per tCO *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]<sub>2</sub> of so much of the CRC emissions which apply under sub-paragraph (a) but— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) deducting the emissions represented by those allowances (if any) which are surrendered by the participant on time for the year to which the annual report relates; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] before the doubling is applied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) blocking. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(5) Where this paragraph applies and a participant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) fails to comply with paragraph (4)(b) by the 31st March after the annual report was due; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) continues in the scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

the allowances required to be surrendered under paragraph (4)(b) are added to the quantity of allowances required to be surrendered in the next year that compliance with Part 4 is required. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(6)[^key-b9cba6b0650c9f44004772c2c5401251] After the end of 28th February 2022, the administrator must not impose a penalty requiring a participant to acquire and surrender additional allowances under paragraph (4)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
