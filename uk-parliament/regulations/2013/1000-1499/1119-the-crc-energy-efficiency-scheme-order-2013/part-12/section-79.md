# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 79

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Failures to maintain records (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The penalties in paragraph (2) apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the administrator has given notice under article 68 in respect of a failure to comply with article 40 or 41; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the participant has failed to comply by the time stated in that notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The penalties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a financial penalty at the rate of £40 per tCO *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]<sub>2</sub> of so much of the CRC emissions of the participant in the annual reporting year immediately preceding the year in which the non-compliance is discovered; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) publication. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Where a participant fails to keep records as provided by article 40 and 41, the penalties are a financial penalty of £5000 and publication. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
