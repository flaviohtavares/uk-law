# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 76

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Inaccurate annual reports (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The penalties in paragraph (3) apply where a participant provides an inaccurate annual report contrary to article 31. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) In paragraph (1), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**inaccurate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” means where any of the supplies or emissions reported differ by more than 5% from the supplies or emissions which should have been reported, ignoring any estimation adjustment under paragraph 31 of section 6 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) The penalties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a financial penalty of £40 per tCO *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]<sub>2</sub> of so much of those supplies or emissions which were inaccurately reported; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) publication. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
