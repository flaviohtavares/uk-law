# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 81

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Blocking and publication (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The administrator may impose the penalty of blocking until— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the failure is remedied; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) any financial penalty imposed in respect of the same failure is paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(1A)[^key-bef486f5872dbf4a3f45df3a33e3de28] The penalty of blocking must not be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) imposed; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) if already imposed, continued, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

after the end of 31st March 2022. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Publication— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) must not take place until the time to appeal against the penalty under Part 14 has expired and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) no appeal against the penalty has been made; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] where an appeal against the penalty has been made and the participant is unsuccessful in that appeal, until after the disposal of that appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) lasts for one year but the administrator may impose the penalty for a longer period, if it believes the seriousness of the failure justifies such longer period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2A)[^key-6bb9142f44474b3562e9157785c53015] The penalty of publication must not— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) be imposed; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) if already imposed, be continued, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

after the end of 31st March 2022. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- “**blocking *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” means to prevent or restrict the operation of an account of a participant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- “**publication *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” means to publish on a part of the Registry which is accessible to the public— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (a) the name of the participant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (b) details of the failure in respect of which a civil penalty has been imposed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (c) the penalty amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
