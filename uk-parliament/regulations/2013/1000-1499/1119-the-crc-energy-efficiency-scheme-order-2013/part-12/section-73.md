# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 73

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Failures in respect of registration (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The penalties in paragraph (2) apply where a public body or undertaking— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) fails to apply for registration as a participant contrary to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) article 11; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] Schedule 5, where an application for registration is required under that Schedule; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

  or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) applies late for registration as a participant contrary to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) article 12; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] paragraph 2(1) of Part 1, paragraph 2(1) of Part 2, or paragraph 1 or 7 of Part 3, of Schedule 5, where an application for registration is required under those Parts of that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The penalties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the financial penalties of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) £5000; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] £500 for each day starting on the day after an application under article 11 must be made until the application for registration is made, subject to a maximum of 80 days; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

  and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) publication. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) The penalties in paragraph (4) apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a public body or undertaking fails to report details of each settled half hourly meter under paragraph 6 of Schedule 4 where an application for registration is required under Part 2 or Schedule 5; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) an undertaking fails to provide the information required under paragraph 4(2)(d) of Part 3 of Schedule 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) The penalties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the financial penalty of £500 for each meter not reported; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) publication. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
