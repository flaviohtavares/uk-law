# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 77

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Failures to surrender allowances contrary to Part 4 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The penalties in paragraphs (2) and (3) apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a participant fails to surrender sufficient allowances contrary to Part 4; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) that failure is apparent to the administrator at the time compliance is required, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

but do not apply where a penalty is imposed under article 74(4) or (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The penalties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the participant must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) immediately acquire such additional allowances as are equal to the amount which should have been surrendered (“the shortfall allowances”); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] surrender the shortfall allowances in accordance with Part 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) a financial penalty of £40 per tCO *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]<sub>2</sub> of so much of the emissions represented by the shortfall allowances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) publication; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) blocking. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Where a participant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) fails to comply with paragraph (2)(a) by the 31st March after the surrender should have been made; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) continues in the scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

the shortfall allowances are added to the quantity of allowances required to be surrendered in the next year that compliance with Part 4 is required. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4)[^key-5437a8c5c2374385dbc509e29155e379] After the end of 28th February 2022, the administrator must not impose a penalty requiring a participant to acquire and surrender additional allowances under paragraph (2)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
