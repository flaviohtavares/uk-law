# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 70

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Civil penalties (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the administrator is satisfied that a person is liable to a civil penalty under this Part, the administrator may serve a notice on that person (“penalty notice”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) A penalty notice must specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the article of and, where applicable, the provision of the Schedule to this Order that is breached; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) to whom the penalty must be paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) A penalty notice in respect of a financial penalty must specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) where no daily penalty applies or the total amount of the daily penalty can be determined at the date of service of the notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) the total amount due; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] where applicable, how it has been calculated; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] the date by which it must be paid; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) where a daily penalty rate applies and the total amount of the daily penalty cannot be determined at the date of service of the notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) the amount of the initial penalty; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] details of the applicable daily rate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) Where a notice has been served under paragraph (3)(b) and the total amount of the daily penalty can be determined after the date of service of the notice, the administrator must serve a further notice on the person liable to the penalty which complies with paragraph (3)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(5) The administrator must remit a financial penalty received to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
