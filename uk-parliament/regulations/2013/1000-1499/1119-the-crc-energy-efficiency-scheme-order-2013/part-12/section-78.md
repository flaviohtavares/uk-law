# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 78

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Later discovered failures to surrender allowances contrary to Part 4 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The penalties in paragraph (2) apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) by reference to its annual report, a participant complied with Part 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the administrator finds, within five years of the date on which compliance with Part 4 is required in respect of that report, that the participant reported fewer CRC supplies in that report than it should have done; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) in consequence, the participant surrendered fewer allowances than it should have done to comply with Part 4 (“the shortfall allowances”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The penalties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) where the participant is a participant at the time paragraph (1)(b) applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) the shortfall allowances are added to the quantity of allowances required to be surrendered in the next annual reporting year that the shortfall is found; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] publication; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

  or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) where the participant is not a participant at the time paragraph (1)(b) applies, a financial penalty which represents the value of the shortfall allowances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) The value under paragraph (2)(b) means the value of allowances in the sale of allowances by the Environment Agency immediately before the shortfall was found. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
