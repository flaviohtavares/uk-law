# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 68

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Notices to provide information: compliance with articles 40 and 41 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The administrator may, by a written notice served on a participant, require that participant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) to furnish information in relation to a failure or suspected failure to comply with article 40 or 41; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) to do so in the form specified in the notice and within such period following service of the notice or at such time as is specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) A notice to provide information under paragraph (1) may be withdrawn at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
