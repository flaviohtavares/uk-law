# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 69

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Enforcement notices (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The administrator may serve an enforcement notice on any person who fails to comply with a provision of this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) An enforcement notice must be in writing and specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the provision of this Order in respect of which there has been a failure; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the matters constituting the failure; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) the steps that must be taken to remedy the failure; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) the period within which those steps must be taken. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) An enforcement notice may be varied or withdrawn at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) If a person fails to comply with an enforcement notice, the administrator— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) may do what that person was required to do; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) may recover from an applicant or participant served with a notice the costs of doing so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
