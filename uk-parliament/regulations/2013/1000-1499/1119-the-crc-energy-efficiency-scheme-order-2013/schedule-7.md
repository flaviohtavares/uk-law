# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, SCHEDULE 7

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 7 (affected by repeal, see note) — Appeals procedure (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 93 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

### SECTION 1 (affected by repeal, see note) — Procedure for appeals against determinations, notices or penalties made or given by the Scottish Environment Protection Agency (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**1.** This section applies to appeals against determinations, notices or penalties made or given by the Scottish Environment Protection Agency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**2.** A person who wishes to appeal to the Scottish Ministers (“the appeal body”) under article 87 must give to the appeal body written notice of the appeal together with a statement of the grounds of appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**3.** The appeal body must as soon as is reasonably practicable send to the administrator a copy of that notice and statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**4.** An appellant may withdraw an appeal by notifying the appeal body and the appeal body must as soon as is reasonably practicable notify the administrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**5.** Notice of appeal in accordance with paragraph 2 is to be given before the expiry of the period of 40 calendar days after the date of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the determination referred to in article 57(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) service of an enforcement notice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) imposition of a civil penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

### SECTION 2 (affected by repeal, see note) — Procedure for appeals against determinations, notices or penalties made or given by the chief inspector or the Department of the Environment (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**6.** This section applies to appeals against determinations, notices or penalties made or given by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the chief inspector; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the Department of the Environment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**7.** A person who wishes to appeal to the Planning Appeals Commission (“the appeal body”) under article 87 must give to the appeal body written notice of the appeal together with a statement of the grounds of appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**8.** The appeal body must as soon as is reasonably practicable send to the administrator a copy of that notice and that statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**9.** An appellant may withdraw an appeal by notifying the appeal body and the appeal body must as soon as is reasonably practicable notify the administrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**10.** Notice of appeal in accordance with paragraph 7 is to be given before the expiry of the period of 47 calendar days beginning with the date of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the determination referred to in article 57(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) service of an enforcement notice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) imposition of the civil penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**11.** The appeal body must determine the appeal and paragraphs (1), (3), (4) and (5) of Article 111 of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] [^c21789141] apply in relation to the determination of the appeal as they apply in relation to the determination of an appeal under that Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**12.** The Planning Appeals Commission must determine the process for determining appeals taking into account any requests of either party to the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**13.** An appeal under this section must be accompanied by a fee and Article 127(2)(b) of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 has effect as if the reference to an appeal under that Order included a reference to an appeal under this Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

### SECTION 3 (affected by repeal, see note) — Procedure for appeals against determinations, notices or penalties made or given by the Welsh Ministers or the Scottish Ministers (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**14.** This section applies where the appellant is or includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the Natural Resources Body for Wales; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the Scottish Environment Protection Agency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**15.** Where the appellant wishes to appeal to an independent person appointed by the Welsh Ministers or the Scottish Ministers under article 89(3) (“the appeal body”) the appellant must give written notice of the appeal together with a statement of the grounds of appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**16.** (1) Where the appellant is or includes the Natural Resources Body for Wales, the notice of appeal in accordance with paragraph 15 is to be given before the dates described in sub-paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The dates referred to in sub-paragraph (1) are 28 calendar days after the date of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the determination referred to in article 57(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) service of an enforcement notice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) imposition of the civil penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**17.** (1) Where the appellant is or includes the Scottish Environment Protection Agency, the notice of appeal in accordance with paragraph 15 is to be given before the dates described in sub-paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The dates referred to in sub-paragraph (1) are 40 calendar days after the date of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the determination referred to in article 57(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) service of an enforcement notice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) imposition of the civil penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**18.** The appeal body may decide an appeal received late. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**19.** An appellant may withdraw an appeal by notifying the appeal body, and as soon as is reasonably practicable the appeal body must notify the administrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**20.** The appeal body may publicise the appeal where it considers it appropriate to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**21.** The Welsh Ministers or the Scottish Ministers as appropriate must appoint an independent person to hear an appeal on behalf of that body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**22.** The appeal body may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) adopt such procedures as it sees fit to determine an appeal, taking into account any requests of the parties to the appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) request an independent person to make a determination in relation to any subject matter of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**23.** On request by the administrator, the appeal body may award the administrator its reasonable costs of an appeal where the appeal body has given the appellant written notice that in its opinion— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the appeal is frivolous or vexatious or otherwise has no reasonable prospects of success; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the appeal is conducted in an unreasonable or vexatious manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**24.** The costs under paragraph 23— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) are those agreed by the parties to the appeal or in default of agreement, as found by the appeal body; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) if unpaid, are recoverable as a civil debt by the administrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
