# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 54

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Cancellation of registrations of participants (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), the administrator must cancel the registration of a participant where the administrator is satisfied that a participant has permanently ceased to carry on a scheme activity in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Where a participant (“A”) leaves a group (“B”) and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) A does not register as a separate participant from B in a subsequent phase; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) B no longer requests that A is a separate participant in a subsequent phase, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

the administrator must cancel the registration of A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) The administrator must give a participant notice that it intends to cancel its registration and unless the participant agrees otherwise, the registration must not be cancelled earlier than 3 months after the date of the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) Cancellation of the registration of a participant must be made by removing the participant from the list of participants held by the administrator and notice that the cancellation has been made must be given in writing to the former participant as soon as possible. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(5) Where the registration of a participant is cancelled *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] before the end of 31st March 2022[^key-133b72cce2bb8096ed88982657df6478] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6], the compliance account must be closed and any allowances held in the account immediately prior to its closure must be cancelled by the administrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(6) The administrator is not required to cancel a registration until such time as the administrator is satisfied that the participant has complied with any outstanding requirement under this Order applicable to that participant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(7) Where cancellation of a registration is required under section 2 of Part 3 of Schedule 5, the administrator must comply with paragraphs (4) and (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(8)[^key-30d70ca636103bdc22a6cba73b5c96ab] Nothing in this Order requires the administrator to cancel the registration of a participant after the end of 31st March 2025. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
