# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 55C

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55C. Records and information on or after 1st April 2022 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-039b28f6cae33d896e6d9b8764bcb99b][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) During the period beginning with 1st April 2022 and ending with 31st March 2025 (‘the relevant period’), the administrator must maintain a record of the information held in the Registry at the end of 31st March 2022. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The record referred to in paragraph (1) may be stored electronically. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) The record referred to in paragraph (1) must include a record of the information referred to in paragraph 2 of Schedule 6 as at the end of 31st March 2022. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) If— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a participant; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) an account holder, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

makes a request in writing during the relevant period to the administrator to provide the information referred to in paragraph (5), the administrator must provide that information as soon as reasonably practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(5) The information referred to in this paragraph is the information that would have been provided by the Registry on 31st March 2022 in accordance with paragraph 2 of Schedule 6 to that participant or account holder. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(6) The request and information may be made and provided electronically. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
