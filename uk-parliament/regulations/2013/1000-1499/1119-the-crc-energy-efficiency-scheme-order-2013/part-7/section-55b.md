# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 55B

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55B. Communications on or after 1st April 2022 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-039b28f6cae33d896e6d9b8764bcb99b][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Communications occurring on or after 1st April 2022 between the administrator and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a participant; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) a third party, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

must take place in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Communications referred to in paragraph (1) may take place by post or by electronic means. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
