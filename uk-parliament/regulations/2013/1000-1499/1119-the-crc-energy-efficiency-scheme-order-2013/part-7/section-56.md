# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 56

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Notification (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A participant must notify any change in its proper address *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] occurring before 1st April 2025[^key-fff853765a59bc665fb3d19c1af8d7c4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] to the administrator within 10 days of the change. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
