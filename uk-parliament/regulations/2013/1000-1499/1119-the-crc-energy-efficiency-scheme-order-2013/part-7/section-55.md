# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 55

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Account holders (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The account holder in respect of the compliance account for a group of undertakings is, as the applicant or participant chooses— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the highest parent undertaking of the group; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) a member of the group, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

with its principal place of activity in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Where no undertaking exists as provided under paragraph (1), the highest parent undertaking of the group must appoint a representative with a principal place of activity in the United Kingdom as the account holder. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Where a participant is a group of undertakings and it intends to change the account holder— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the participant must notify the administrator of the intended new account holder; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) that account holder must be an undertaking which complies with paragraph (1) or, as appropriate, paragraph (2); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) the administrator must approve the change. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) Where a group of public bodies includes the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a government department; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the Scottish Ministers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) the Welsh Assembly Government; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) a Northern Ireland Department; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (e) a local authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (f) a university, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

subject to paragraph (5), the account holder in respect of the compliance account is that body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(5) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a body listed in paragraph (4) is part of more than one group; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) a public body other than one listed in paragraph (4) is a participant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

the account holder in respect of the compliance account is such body as the administrator agrees. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
