# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 51

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Security of the Registry (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The administrator— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) must take reasonable steps to ensure that the operation of the Registry is secure from misuse, including use by those not entitled to use it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) may suspend the operation of the Registry or any account where it believes security of the Registry may be at risk by not doing so; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) must ensure that information which relates to an account holder or a participant (other than information to which article 58 or 59 applies) is not accessible by another account holder or participant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
