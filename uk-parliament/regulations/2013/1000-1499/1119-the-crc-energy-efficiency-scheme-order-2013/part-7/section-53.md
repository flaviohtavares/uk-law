# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 53

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Preventing or suspending use of the Registry (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The administrator may suspend or restrict a participant's use of the Registry if that participant or any individual acting on its behalf— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) is in breach of this Order or any administrative rules concerning the operation of the Registry; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) in the belief of the administrator, is using or intends to use the Registry for or in connection with a criminal offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The administrator must give notice to the participant of such suspension or restriction except in relation to the registration of an applicant or where paragraph (1)(b) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
