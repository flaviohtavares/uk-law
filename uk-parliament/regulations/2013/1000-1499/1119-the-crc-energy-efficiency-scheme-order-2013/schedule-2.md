- (b) a local authority decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) A government decision in relation to participation during a phase or any part of a phase must be notified in writing as soon as possible to the administrator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) A notification required under sub-paragraph (1) or (2) must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) state the period for which the decision has effect, which may commence before the date of the notification but must not commence in a phase which has completed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) identify the public bodies affected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) A government decision or local authority decision may be varied or revoked in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
