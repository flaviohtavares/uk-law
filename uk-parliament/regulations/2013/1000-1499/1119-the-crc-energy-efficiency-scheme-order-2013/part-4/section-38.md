# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 38

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Allowances and trading (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Until the end of 31st March 2025,[^key-f8fe48115c914c96334fcb766fede8ac] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] the administrator must maintain a record in respect of an allowance which shows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the year in which the allowance issued; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the allocation in which the allowance was issued; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) the date of issue of the allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) to whom the allowance was issued; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (e) the account in which the allowances are held from time to time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (f) transfers of the allowances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (g) when the allowance is cancelled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Before 1st April 2022,[^key-bcf9521a762cdbb3eda298e1ac629239] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] trading in allowances is permitted by participants and third parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
