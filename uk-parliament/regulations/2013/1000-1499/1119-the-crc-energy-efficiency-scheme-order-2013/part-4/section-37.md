# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 37

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Cancellation of allowances and surplus surrendered allowances (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The administrator must in respect of a participant cancel such quantity of allowances in the cancellation account which is equal to the participant's CRC emissions for the relevant year of the phase— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) except where sub-paragraph (b) or (c) applies, as stated in the annual report; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) further to a determination under article 31(3); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) as provided under article 74(4)(a) or (5), 77(2)(a) or (3) or 78(2)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Where a participant surrenders to the cancellation account more allowances (“surplus allowances”) than required under article 36(1), the surplus allowances— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) must remain in the cancellation account; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) subject to article 35— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) are to be treated as surrendered in respect of the subsequent year (“year 2”) in which the participant is required to comply with article 35 where that year is in the same phase; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] must be cancelled by the administrator in accordance with paragraph (1) before any other allowances which are surrendered. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Where the surplus allowances exceed the quantity required to be surrendered in year 2, subject to article 35, paragraph (2)(b) applies to the years after year 2 until no surplus allowances remain. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a participant surrenders to the cancellation account surplus allowances; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the account holder makes a request *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] before the end of 31st March 2025[^key-51e79fee5e3a08a36afcfada60363c60] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] to the Secretary of State for the repayment of the balance, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

the Secretary of State may repay the balance to the account holder. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4A)[^key-1196f3abde862dd72deaa445578e5cd9] Where the account holder makes a request to the Secretary of State for repayment during the period beginning with 1st April 2022 and ending with 31st March 2025 with respect to any allowances held in the compliance account at the end of 31st March 2022, the Secretary of State may make a repayment to the account holder. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(5) Any repayment made by the Secretary of State under paragraph (4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] or paragraph (4A)[^key-6215571084d80467013a36f82bd16ad8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] may be subject to a deduction of any banking charges incurred during that transaction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
