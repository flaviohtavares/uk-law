# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 35

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Validity of allowances (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), an allowance is valid for the purposes of compliance with article 36— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) for the year in respect of which it was issued; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) for any subsequent year, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

but an allowance issued in a phase is not valid in respect of CRC emissions made in a subsequent phase. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a participant is required to acquire and surrender additional allowances under article 74(4)(b) or 77(2)(a) in respect of an annual reporting year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the participant holds an allowance which is valid for the following year (“year 2”), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

the participant may not surrender the allowance which is valid for year 2 in order to comply with those articles. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) A participant must acquire the additional allowances from a special allocation or from a third party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) An allowance is not valid for any purpose other than a purpose for which it is valid under paragraphs (1) and (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(5) In paragraph (3), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**special allocation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” means the issue of allowances conducted by the Environment Agency under regulation 10 of the 2012 Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
