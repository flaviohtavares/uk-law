# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 42

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Audit of records (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A participant must, on at least an annual basis, carry out audits of the records required to be maintained under this Part and do so to ensure its compliance with those requirements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The satisfactory completion of such an audit must be evidenced in writing (“an audit certificate”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) The audit certificate may be in such form as the participant sees fit but must be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) signed by a person who exercises management control in respect of the activities of the participant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) kept with its records. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
