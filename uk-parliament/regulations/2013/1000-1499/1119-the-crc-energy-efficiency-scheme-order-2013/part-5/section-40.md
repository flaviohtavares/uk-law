# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 40

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Records: general (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A participant must maintain records in respect of the information— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) used to compile its annual report; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) relevant to any of the changes described in Schedule 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
