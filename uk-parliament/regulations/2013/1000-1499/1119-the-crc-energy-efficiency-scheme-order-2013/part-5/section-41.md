# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 41

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Records: public disclosure (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a participant has informed the administrator that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) it discloses publicly each year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) its emissions reduction targets; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] its performance against them; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) a person with management control has responsibility in respect of those matters; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) it operates an employee engagement programme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

the participant must maintain records of the disclosure, the person or the programme, as applicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) In paragraph (1)(c), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**employee engagement programme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” means a programme organised or supported by the participant which enables employees of that participant to make regular contributions to the ways in which the participant may reduce the emissions made or caused by the participant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
