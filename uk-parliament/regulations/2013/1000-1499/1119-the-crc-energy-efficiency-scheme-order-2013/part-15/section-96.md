# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 96

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Revocations, continuing effect and amendments (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the 2010 Order and the 2011 Order are revoked. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The 2010 Order and the 2011 Order continue to have effect in relation to the first phase under the 2010 Order, subject to the amendments contained in Schedule 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Schedule 9 (amendments to the CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2010) has effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
