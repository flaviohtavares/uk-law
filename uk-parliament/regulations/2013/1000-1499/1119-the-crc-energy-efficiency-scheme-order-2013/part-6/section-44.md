# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 44

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Information on electricity and gas supplied from authorised suppliers (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A participant may request in writing the information under paragraph (2) from those authorised suppliers of electricity or gas that hold a licence to make such a supply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The information under this paragraph is the amount of electricity or gas supplied to the participant by an authorised supplier in—— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the annual reporting year in which the request is made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the alternative period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) The information under paragraph (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) may be calculated on the basis of the amount of electricity or gas supply billed to the participant during the annual reporting year or the alternative period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) may cover a different 12 month period from the annual reporting year, which commences no more than 31 calendar days before the beginning of the annual reporting year or no more than 31 calendar days after the beginning of the annual reporting year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) Where a supplier described in paragraph (1) receives such a request, that supplier must reply in writing within 6 weeks of the end of the annual reporting year or the alternative period to which the information relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(5) In this article “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**alternative period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]**” means a period of 12 months which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) commences no earlier than 31 calendar days before an annual reporting year begins; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) finishes no later than 31 calendar days after an annual reporting year ends. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
