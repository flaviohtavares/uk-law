# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, section 59

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Further publication (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where an appeal is made against— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a determination under article 31(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the imposition of a penalty described in article 74(4)(a)(ii). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) Where this paragraph applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the administrator may publish a list of those participants which have in respect of the annual reporting year made any such appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) subject to paragraph (3), where any such appeal results in the information published under article 58 being changed, the administrator must as soon as possible publish the amended information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(3) Publication under paragraph (2)(b) must not take place until the completion of all such appeals made by all participants. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(4) The administrator may publish amended information at any time where it discovers any error or omission in the published information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
