# The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013, SCHEDULE 4

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 4 (affected by repeal, see note) — Information on registration (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6][^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

### 1. General (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

Information in this section is required under Part 2 and Schedule 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

### 2. Contact information (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

Subject to paragraph 4, as applicable to the applicant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) name, postal address, email address and telephone number; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the principal place of activity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) any company registration number and registered office; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) any trading or other name by which the applicant is commonly known. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**3.** Where the applicant is a group of undertakings— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) the information in paragraph 2 in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (i) the registering member of the group; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] each participant equivalent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] the highest parent undertaking located in the United Kingdom; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6] where applicable, the highest parent undertaking located outside the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

  and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) where an undertaking other than the highest parent undertaking is to be the account holder of the compliance account, the information in paragraph 2 in respect of that undertaking. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**4.** Where the applicant is a group of public bodies, the information in paragraph 2 is required only in respect of the following in that group— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

- (a) a government department; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (b) the Scottish Ministers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (c) the Welsh Assembly Government; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (d) a Northern Ireland Department; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (e) a local authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (f) a university; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
- (g) for a group which does not include a public body described in sub-paragraphs (a) to (f), the body in the group intended to be the account holder in respect of the compliance account. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

**5.** The name, postal address, email address and telephone number of at least three individuals who will act as contacts for the applicant, one of whom must exercise management control in respect of the public body or undertaking which is to be the account holder of the compliance account. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

### 6. Total supplies of qualifying electricity (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(1) A list of all settled half hourly meters which measured the supply of qualifying electricity to the applicant in the qualification year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]

(2) The total amount of qualifying electricity in the qualification year and the amount of qualifying electricity for each individual participant equivalent that is a member of the group. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8168caadfdb8153f9ac40f265229b9e6]
