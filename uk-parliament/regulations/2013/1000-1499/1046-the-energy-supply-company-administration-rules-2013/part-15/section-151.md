# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 151

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 151. Application

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), this Chapter applies where a notice or other document is required to be given, delivered or sent under the 1986 Act or these Rules by any person, including an energy administrator.

(2) This Chapter does not apply to the service of—

- (a) any petition or application to the court;
- (b) any evidence in support of that petition or application; or
- (c) any order of the court.

(3) This Chapter does not apply to the delivery of documents to the registrar of companies.
