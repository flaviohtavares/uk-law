# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 155

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 155. Notice etc to solicitors

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where under the 1986 Act or these Rules a notice or other document is required or authorised to be given, delivered or sent to a person, it may be given, delivered or sent instead to a solicitor authorised to accept delivery on that person's behalf.
