# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 171

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 171. Application of CPR Part 6 to service of court documents outside the jurisdiction

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

CPR Part 6 applies to the service of court documents outside the jurisdiction with such modifications as the court may direct.
