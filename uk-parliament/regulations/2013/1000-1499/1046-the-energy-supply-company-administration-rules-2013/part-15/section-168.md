# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 168

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 168. Application of CPR Part 6 to service of court documents within the jurisdiction

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except where different provision is made in these Rules, CPR Part 6 applies in relation to the service of court documents within the jurisdiction with such modifications as the court may direct.
