# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 153

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 153. Postal delivery of documents

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Unless in any particular case some other form of delivery is required by the 1986 Act, these Rules or an order of the court, a notice or other document may be sent by post in accordance with the rules for postal service in CPR Part 6 and sending by such means has effect as specified in those rules.
