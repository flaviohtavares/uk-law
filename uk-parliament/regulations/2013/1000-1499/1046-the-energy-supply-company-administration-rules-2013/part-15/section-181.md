# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 181

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 181. Omission of unobtainable information

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Information required under this Chapter to be included in a notice to be gazetted may be omitted if it is not reasonably practicable to obtain it.
