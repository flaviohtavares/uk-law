# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 158

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 158. Notice to joint energy administrators

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where two or more persons are acting jointly as the energy administrator in energy supply company administration proceedings, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to them all.
