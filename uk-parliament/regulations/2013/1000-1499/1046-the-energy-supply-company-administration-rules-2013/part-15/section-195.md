# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 195

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 195. Right to copy documents

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the 1986 Act or these Rules confer a right for any person to inspect documents, the right includes that of taking copies of those documents, on payment—

- (a) in the case of documents on the court's file of proceedings, of the fee chargeable under any order made under section 92 of the Courts Act 2003 [^c21778911]; and
- (b) in any other case, of the appropriate fee.
