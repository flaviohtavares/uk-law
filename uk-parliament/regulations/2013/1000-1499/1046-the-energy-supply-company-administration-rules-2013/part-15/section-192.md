# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 192

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 192. Notifications of more than one nature

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A notification which includes a notification of more than one nature must satisfy the requirements applying in respect of each of those notifications.
