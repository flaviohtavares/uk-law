# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 189

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 189. Notifications relating to court orders

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In addition to the information required by Rule 186, a notification relating to a court order must also specify—

- (a) the nature of the court order; and
- (b) the date of the order.
