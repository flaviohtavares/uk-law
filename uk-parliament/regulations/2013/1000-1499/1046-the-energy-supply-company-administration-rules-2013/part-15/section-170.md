# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 170

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 170. Service on joint energy administrators

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where there are joint energy administrators in energy supply company administration proceedings, service on one of them is to be treated as service on all of them.
