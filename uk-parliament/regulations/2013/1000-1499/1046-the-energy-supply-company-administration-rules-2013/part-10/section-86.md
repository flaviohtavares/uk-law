# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 86

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Notice of resignation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The notice of resignation must be in Form ESCA14.

(2) The notice must be filed with the court, and a copy delivered to the registrar of companies.

(3) A copy of the notice of resignation must be sent by the energy administrator not more than 5 business days after it has been filed with the court to all those to whom notice of intention to resign was sent.
