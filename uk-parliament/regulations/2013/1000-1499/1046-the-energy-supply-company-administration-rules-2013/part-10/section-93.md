# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 93

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Notification to registrar of companies

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The replacement or additional energy administrator must deliver notice of the appointment to the registrar of companies.
