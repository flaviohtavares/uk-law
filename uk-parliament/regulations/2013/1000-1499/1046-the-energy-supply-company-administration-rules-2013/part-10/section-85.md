# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 85

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Notice of intention to resign

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The energy administrator must in all cases give at least 5 business days' notice in Form ESCA13 of the energy administrator's intention to resign, or to apply for the court's permission to do so, to the following persons—

- (a) the Secretary of State;
- (b) GEMA;
- (c) if there is a continuing energy administrator of the energy supply company, to that continuing energy administrator; and
- (d) if there is no such energy administrator, to the energy supply company and its creditors.
