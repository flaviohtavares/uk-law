# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 203

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 203. “Energy supply company administration proceedings”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

“**Energy supply company administration proceedings**” means any proceedings under sections 154 to 171 of, and Schedules 20 and 21 to, the 2004 Act or these Rules.
