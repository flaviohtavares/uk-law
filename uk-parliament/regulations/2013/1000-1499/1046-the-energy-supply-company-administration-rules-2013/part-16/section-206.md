# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 206

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 206. “Venue”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

References to the “venue” for any proceeding or attendance before the court, or for a meeting, are to the time, date and place for the proceeding, attendance or meeting or to the time and date for a meeting which is held in accordance with section 246A of the 1986 Act without any place being specified for it.
