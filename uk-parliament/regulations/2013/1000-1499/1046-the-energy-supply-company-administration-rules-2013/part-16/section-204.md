> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 204. “The appropriate fee”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

“**The appropriate fee**” means 15 pence per A4 or A5 page and 30 pence per A3 page.
