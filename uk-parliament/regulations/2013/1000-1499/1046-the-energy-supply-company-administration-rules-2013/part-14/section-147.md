# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 147

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 147. Costs, expenses, etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) All fees, costs, charges and other expenses incurred in the course of the energy supply company administration proceedings are to be regarded as expenses of the energy supply company administration.

(2) The costs associated with the prescribed part shall be paid out of the prescribed part.
