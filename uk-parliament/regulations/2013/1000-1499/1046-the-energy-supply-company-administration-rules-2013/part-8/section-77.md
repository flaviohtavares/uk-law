# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 77

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Remuneration of new energy administrator

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a new energy administrator is appointed in place of another, any court order in effect under Rule 76 immediately before the former energy administrator ceased to hold office continues to apply in respect of the remuneration of the new energy administrator until a further court order is made in accordance with those provisions.
