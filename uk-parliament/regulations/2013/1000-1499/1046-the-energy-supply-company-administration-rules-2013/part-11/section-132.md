# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 132

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 132. Office copies of documents

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any person who has under these Rules the right to inspect the court file of energy supply company administration proceedings may require the court to provide the person with an office copy of any document on the file.

(2) A person's rights under this Rule may be exercised on the person's behalf by the person's solicitor.

(3) An office copy provided by the court under this Rule must be in such form as the registrar thinks appropriate, and must bear the court's seal.
