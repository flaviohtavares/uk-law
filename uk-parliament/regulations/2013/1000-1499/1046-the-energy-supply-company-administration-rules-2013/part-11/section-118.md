# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 118

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Costs and expenses of witnesses

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Except as directed by the court, no allowance as a witness in any examination or other proceedings before the court shall be made to an officer of the energy supply company to which the energy supply company administration proceedings relate.

(2) A person making any application in energy supply company administration proceedings shall not be regarded as a witness on the hearing of the application, but the costs officer may allow the person's expenses of travelling and subsistence.
