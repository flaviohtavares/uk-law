# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 126

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126. Principal court rules and practice to apply

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The CPR and the practice and procedure of the High Court (including any practice directions) apply to energy supply company administration proceedings with any necessary modifications, except so far as inconsistent with these Rules.

(2) All energy supply company administration proceedings shall be allocated to the multi-track for which CPR Part 29 (the multi-track) makes provision; accordingly those provisions of the CPR which provide for allocation questionnaires and track allocation will not apply.
