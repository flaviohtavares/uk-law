# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 115

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. Costs paid otherwise than out of the assets of the energy supply company

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the amount of costs is decided by detailed assessment under an order of the court directing that the costs are to be paid otherwise than out of the assets of the energy supply company, the costs officer must note on the final costs certificate by whom, or the manner in which, the costs are to be paid.
