> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Rights of audience

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Rights of audience in energy supply company administration proceedings are the same as in insolvency proceedings.
