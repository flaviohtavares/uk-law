# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 122

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 122. Witness statement in support of application

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An application under Rule 121(3) must be supported by a witness statement made by a registered medical practitioner as to the mental or physical condition of the incapacitated person.
