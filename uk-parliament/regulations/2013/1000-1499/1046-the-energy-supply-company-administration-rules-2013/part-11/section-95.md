# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 95

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Preliminary

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter applies to any application made to the court in energy supply company administration proceedings, except an application for an esc administration order.
