# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 112

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Application of Chapter 8

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies in relation to costs in connection with energy supply company administration proceedings.

(2) In this Chapter a reference to costs includes charges and expenses.
