# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 99

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Application under section 176A(5) of the 1986 Act

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An application under section 176A(5) of the 1986 Act may be made without the application being served upon or notice being given to any other party.
