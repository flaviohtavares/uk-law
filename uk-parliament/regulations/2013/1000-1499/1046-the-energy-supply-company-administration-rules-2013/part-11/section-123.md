# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 123

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Service of notices following appointment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any notice served on, or sent to, a person appointed under Rule 121 has the same effect as if it had been served on, or sent to, the incapacitated person.
