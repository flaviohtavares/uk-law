# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 106

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 106. Nomination and appointment of shorthand writers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the High Court the judge or registrar and, in a county court, a district judge may in writing nominate one or more persons to be official shorthand writers to the court.

(2) The court may, at any time in the course of energy supply company administration proceedings, appoint a shorthand writer to take down the evidence of a person examined under section 236 of the 1986 Act.
