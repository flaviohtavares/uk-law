# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 120

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Introductory

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Rules in this Chapter apply where in energy supply company administration proceedings it appears to the court that a person affected by the proceedings is one who lacks capacity within the meaning of the Mental Capacity Act 2005 [^c21778881] to manage and administer the person's property and affairs either—

- (a) by reason of lacking capacity within the meaning of the Mental Capacity Act 2005; or
- (b) due to physical affliction or disability.

(2) The person concerned is referred to as “**the incapacitated person**”.
