# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 136

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 136. Retention of proxies

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject as follows, proxies used for voting at any meeting must be retained by the chair of the meeting.

(2) The chair must deliver the proxies, as soon as reasonably practicable after the meeting, to the energy administrator (where the chair is not the energy administrator).
