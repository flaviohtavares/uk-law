# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 83

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Provision of information to the Secretary of State

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the energy supply company administration ends pursuant to paragraph 79, 83 or 84 of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act, the energy administrator must, at the same time as sending the final progress report under Rule 83(1), provide the Secretary of State with the following information—

- (a) a breakdown of the relevant debts (within the meaning of section 98(4) of the 2011 Act) of the energy supply company, which remain outstanding; and
- (b) details of any shortfall (within the meaning of section 98(3)(a) of the 2011 Act) in the property of the energy supply company available for meeting those relevant debts.
