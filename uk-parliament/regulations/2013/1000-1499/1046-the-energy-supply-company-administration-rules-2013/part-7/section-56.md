# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 56

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Payments of a periodical nature

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the case of rent and other payments of a periodical nature, the creditor may prove for any amounts due and unpaid up to the date when the energy supply company entered energy supply company administration.

(2) Where at that date any payment was accruing due, the creditor may prove for so much as would have fallen due at that date, if accruing from day to day.
