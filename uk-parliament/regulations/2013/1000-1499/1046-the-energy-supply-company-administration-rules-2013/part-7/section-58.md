# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 58

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Debt payable at future time

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A creditor may prove for a debt of which payment was not yet due on the date when the energy supply company entered energy supply company administration, subject to Rule 75 (adjustment of dividend where payment made before time).
