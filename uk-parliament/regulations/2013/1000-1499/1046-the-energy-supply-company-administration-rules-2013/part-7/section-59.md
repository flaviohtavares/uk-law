# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 59

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Value of security

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A secured creditor may, with the agreement of the energy administrator or the permission of the court, at any time alter the value which the secured creditor's proof of debt puts upon the secured creditor's security.

(2) However, if a secured creditor has voted in respect of the unsecured balance of the secured creditor's debt the secured creditor may re-value the secured creditor's security only with permission of the court.
