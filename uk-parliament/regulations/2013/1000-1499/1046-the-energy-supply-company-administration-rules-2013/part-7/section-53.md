# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 53

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Discounts

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall in every case be deducted from the claim all trade and other discounts which would have been available to the energy supply company but for its energy supply company administration except any discount for immediate, early or cash settlement.
