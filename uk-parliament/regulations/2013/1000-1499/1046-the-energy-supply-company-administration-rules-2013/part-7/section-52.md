# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 52

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Secured creditors

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a secured creditor realises the secured creditor's security, the secured creditor may prove for the balance of the secured creditor's debt, after deducting the amount realised.

(2) If a secured creditor voluntarily surrenders a security for the general benefit of creditors, the secured creditor may prove for the secured creditor's whole debt, as if it were unsecured.
