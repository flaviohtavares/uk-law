# The Energy Supply Company Administration Rules 2013, section 55

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Debt in foreign currency

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purpose of proving a debt incurred or payable in a currency other than sterling, the amount of the debt shall be converted into sterling at such rate as is agreed between the relevant creditor and the energy administrator or, where no agreement can be reached, as the court determines.
