# The Burma/Myanmar (Financial Restrictions) (Revocation) Regulations 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 1096

Criminal Law

The Burma/Myanmar (Financial Restrictions) (Revocation) Regulations 2013

Made

7th May 2013

Laid before Parliament

at 1.00 p.m. on 8th May 2013

Coming into force

at 3.00 p.m. on 8th May 2013

The Treasury are designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to restrictive measures against persons or bodies listed by an international organisation.

The Treasury make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Burma/Myanmar (Financial Restrictions) (Revocation) Regulations 2013 and come into force at 3.00 p.m. on 8th May 2013.

## 2. Revocation

The following instruments are revoked—

- (a) the Burma/Myanmar (Financial Restrictions) Regulations 2009[^f00003], and
- (b) the Burma/Myanmar (Financial Restrictions) (Suspension) Regulations 2012[^f00004].

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Council Regulation [(EC) No. 194/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0194) (“**the first Council Regulation**”) of 25 February 2008 renewing and strengthening the restrictive measures in respect of Burma/Myanmar and repealing Regulation [(EC) No. 817/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/0817) (O.J. L 66, 10.3.2008, p.1) contains various sanctions measures imposed in respect of Burma/Myanmar. Those measures include financial sanctions which require the funds and economic resources of certain persons, entities and bodies to be frozen and which impose restrictions on the financing of certain enterprises. Parts 3 and 5 of the Burma/Myanmar (Financial Restrictions) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/1495](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/1495), “**the 2009 Regulations**”) provide for criminal penalties for breach of the financial sanctions provisions of the first Council Regulation.

Various sanctions measures contained in the first Council Regulation, including the financial sanctions measures, were suspended until 30th April 2013 by Council Regulation (EU) No. 409/2012 of 14 May 2012 (O.J. No. L 126, 15.5.2012, p.1) with effect from 16th May 2012. The Burma/Myanmar (Financial Restrictions) (Suspension) Regulations 2012 ([S.I. 2012/1302](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1302), “**the 2012 Regulations**”) accordingly suspended Parts 3 and 5 of the 2009 Regulations until 30th April 2013.

Council Regulation (EU) No. 401/2013 of 2 May 2013 revokes all of the financial sanctions measures contained in the first Council Regulation. These Regulations accordingly revoke the 2009 Regulations and the 2012 Regulations.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the costs of business or the voluntary sector is foreseen. Further information is available from Financial Sanctions, HM Treasury, 1 Horse Guards Road, London SW1A 2HQ and on the HM Treasury website ( www.hm-treasury.gov.uk).
