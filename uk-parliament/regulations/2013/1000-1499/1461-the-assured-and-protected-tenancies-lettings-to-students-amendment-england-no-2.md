# The Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) (England) (No. 2) Regulations 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 1461

Landlord And Tenant, England

The Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) (England) (No. 2) Regulations 2013

Made

11th June 2013

Laid before Parliament

13th June 2013

Coming into force

8th July 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1988[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) (England) (No. 2) Regulations 2013 and come into force on 8th July 2013.

## 2. Amendment of the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1998

In Schedule 2 to the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1998[^f00002], insert in the appropriate places―

- (a) “UPP (Broadgate Park) Limited”;
- (b) “UPP (Clifton) Limited”; and
- (c) “UPP (Nottingham) Limited”.

David Willetts

Minister of State for Universities and Science

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

11th June 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Schedule 2 to the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1998 so that UPP (Broadgate Park) Limited, UPP (Clifton) Limited and UPP (Nottingham) Limited are specified for the purpose of paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1988. The effect of the specifications is that a tenancy granted by these companies to a student at an educational institution specified in the 1988 Regulations is not an assured tenancy for the purposes of the 1988 Act.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations as they will have no impact on the costs of business, charities or voluntary sectors. The Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the Regulations on www.legislation.gov.uk.
