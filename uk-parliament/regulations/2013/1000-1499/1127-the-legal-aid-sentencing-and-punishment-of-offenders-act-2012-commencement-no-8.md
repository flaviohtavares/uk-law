# The Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 1127 (C. 50)

Criminal Law

The Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2013

Made

11th May 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 151(1) of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2013.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 14th May 2013

Section 148 of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 comes into force on 14th May 2013.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

Damian Green

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

11th May 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 148 of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act [2012 (c. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/10) (“the 2012 Act”). This section amends section 76 of the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act [2008 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/4) (“the 2008 Act”), which provides a gloss on the common law of self-defence and the defences provided by section 3(1) of the Criminal Law Act [1967 (c. 58)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1967/58) and section 3(1) of the Criminal Law Act (Northern Ireland) [1967 (c. 18 (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/apni/1967/18)). Section 148 of the 2012 Act amends section 76 of the 2008 Act so that the law relating to self-defence and related defences is set out clearly in one place.
