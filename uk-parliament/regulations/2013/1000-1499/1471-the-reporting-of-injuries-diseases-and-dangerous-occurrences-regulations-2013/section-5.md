# The Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations 2013, section 5

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Non-fatal injuries to non-workers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where any person not at work, as a result of a work-related accident, suffers—

- (a) an injury, and that person is taken from the site of the accident to a hospital for treatment in respect of that injury; or
- (b) a specified injury on hospital premises,

the responsible person must follow the reporting procedure, subject to regulations 14 and 15.
