# The Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations 2013, section 7

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Dangerous occurrences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where there is a dangerous occurrence, the responsible person must follow the reporting procedure, subject to regulations 14 and 15.
