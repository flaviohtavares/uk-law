# The Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations 2013, section 20

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Review

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State must from time to time—

- (a) carry out a review of these Regulations;
- (b) set out the conclusions of that review in a report; and
- (c) publish the report.

(2) In carrying out the review the Secretary of State must, so far as is reasonable, have regard to how—

- (a) Council Directive [89/391/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0391) on the introduction of measures to encourage improvements in the safety and health of workers at work [^c21824521] (which is implemented by means of regulations 4 and 6);
- (b) Council Directive [92/91/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0091) concerning the minimum requirements for improving the safety and health protection of workers in the mineral-extracting industries through drilling [^c21824531] (which is implemented by means of regulations 4, 6 and 7);
- (c) Council Directive [92/104/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0104) on the minimum requirements for improving the safety and health protection of workers in surface and underground mineral-extracting industries [^c21824541] (which is implemented by means of regulations 4, 6 and 7);
- (d) Directive [2000/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0054) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the protection of workers from risks related to exposure to biological agents at work [^c21824551] (which is implemented by means of regulations 7 and 9);
- (e) Directive [2004/37/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0037) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the protection of workers from the risks related to exposure to carcinogens or mutagens at work [^c21824561] (which is implemented by means of regulation 9);
- (f) Directive [2004/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0049) of the European Parliament and of the Council on safety on the Community's railways and amending Council Directive [95/18/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0018) on the licensing of railway undertakings and Directive [2001/14/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0014) on the allocation of railway infrastructure capacity and the levying of charges for the use of railway infrastructure and safety certification [^c21824571] (which is implemented by means of regulations 4, 6 and 7);
- (g) Regulation [(EC) No 1338/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1338) of the European Parliament and of the Council on Community statistics on public health and health and safety at work [^c21824581] (which is implemented by means of regulations 4, 6, 8 and 12); and
- (h) Council Directive 2010/32/EU implementing the Framework Agreement on prevention from sharp injuries in the hospital and healthcare sector concluded by HOSPEEM and EPSU [^c21824591] (which is implemented by means of regulation 7),

are implemented in other member States.

(3) The report must in particular—

- (a) set out the objectives intended to be achieved by the regulatory system established by these Regulations;
- (b) assess the extent to which those objectives are achieved; and
- (c) assess whether those objectives remain appropriate and, if so, the extent to which they could be achieved with a system that imposes less regulation.

(4) The first report under this regulation must be published before the end of the period of five years beginning with the day on which these Regulations come into force.

(5) Reports under this regulation are afterwards to be published at intervals not exceeding five years.
