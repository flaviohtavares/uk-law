# The Residential Holiday Schemes for Disabled Children (England) Regulations 2013, section 1

> Text as at 2016-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application[^key-767a41d3121c10293956bfa51e9d6254]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Residential Holiday Schemes for Disabled Children (England) Regulations 2013 and come into force on 1st July 2013.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to holiday schemes for disabled children carried on in England.
