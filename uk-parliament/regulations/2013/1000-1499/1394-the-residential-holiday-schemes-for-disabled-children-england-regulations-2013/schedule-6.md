# The Residential Holiday Schemes for Disabled Children (England) Regulations 2013, SCHEDULE 6

> Text as at 2016-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 6 — Matters to be monitored by the registered person

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 30

**1.**[^c-d45f3703da6c5dc16918803cf34934aa] The deposit and issue of money and other valuables handed in for safekeeping.

**2.**[^c-fa3b80b5b8e2be2f8fa59b7f4a35b95f] Accidents involving, and injuries to, persons in premises or sites used by the scheme to accommodate children.

**3.**[^c-ef8008565163866bae47253e8ac83cd3] Illnesses of children accommodated by the scheme.

**4.**[^c-6f2e4514f3685ed7d454901830eed8e0] Complaints in relation to children accommodated by the scheme and the outcomes of those complaints.

**5.**[^c-57abf0f8710083072f66c5c641c9e0fc] Allegations or suspicions of abuse in respect of children accommodated by the scheme and the outcome of any related investigation.

**6.**[^c-9ceaf787d1780ba1fd5d6ef7f442d749] Visitors to premises or sites used by the scheme to accommodate children, including visitors to children accommodated.

**7.**[^c-7541171492ef87a4f90ef3b501aaa0fc] Notifications of the events in Schedule 5.

**8.**[^c-c1b4c852d8722b32c6f246be096e7994] The use of measures of control, discipline and restraint in respect of children accommodated by the scheme.

**9.**[^c-05abed21c14559556f0f8d46d1c85932] Risk assessments for health and safety purposes and subsequent action taken.

**10.**[^c-3904a4100ab9adba08bf51673102a2af] First aid, medical treatment or medicines administered to a child accommodated by the scheme.

**11.**[^c-e1144308a20581e40308e7bc6b638984] Fire drills and tests of alarms and of fire equipment.
