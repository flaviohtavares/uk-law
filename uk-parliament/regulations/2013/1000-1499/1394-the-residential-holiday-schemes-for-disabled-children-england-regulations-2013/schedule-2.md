# The Residential Holiday Schemes for Disabled Children (England) Regulations 2013, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2016-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Information required in respect of persons seeking to carry on, manage or be employed to work at a scheme

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 5, 7, 22

**1.**[^c-6c9501e37094e4ac963c56db02d76633] Proof of identity, including a recent photograph.

**2.**[^c-bb2ce76429b5634fe8657f4e4dd91b9a] Either—

- (a) where the certificate is required for a purpose related to registration under Part 2 of the Act or the position falls within regulation 5A of the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) Regulations 2002[^f00017], an enhanced criminal record certificate issued under section 113B of the Police Act 1997[^f00018] which includes suitability information relating to children (within the meaning of section 113BA(2)[^f00019] of that Act) and, where applicable, suitability information relating to vulnerable adults (within the meaning of section 113BB(2)[^f00020] of that Act); or
- (b) in any other case, a criminal record certificate issued under section 113A[^f00021] of the Police Act 1997.

**3.**[^c-edca50e71e5cb9d3646e8785ab0a763a] Two written references, including a reference from the person’s most recent employer, if any.

**4.**[^c-1a606f993293b527dde1d4c78ff4841d] Documentary evidence of any relevant qualifications.
