# The Residential Holiday Schemes for Disabled Children (England) Regulations 2013, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2016-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Matters to be included in the statement of purpose

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 3

**1.**[^c-4fd52f3aedcc2ac80ca1eaefb3389ce5] A statement of the overall aims of the scheme, and the objectives to be attained with regard to children provided with accommodation by the scheme.

**2.**[^c-780d852df420ebc8ab12d00da5d28a20] A statement of the facilities and services to be provided for children accommodated by the scheme.

**3.**[^c-40b3100eb26138b64b6061e4af755802] The name and business address of the registered provider and, if different, the registered manager.

**4.**[^c-0427de879bd01ab3f84a52025fefbedd] The relevant experience and qualifications of the registered provider and, if different, the registered manager.

**5.**[^c-ea424382c261f50a46255326c468fee2] The experience, number and relevant qualifications of employees and, if the employees are all of one sex, a description of the means by which the scheme is to promote appropriate role models of both sexes.

**6.**[^c-263ff965b478579c73f02394bb0ea33e] The arrangements for the development, supervision and training of employees.

**7.**[^c-fe4df1c9e9956e07e80e7ce21d43b737] The age range, numbers, range of needs and sex of children for whom it is intended that the scheme is to provide accommodation.

**8.**[^c-7d6d482abd8ccd945bf8aad212278609] A description of the scheme’s underlying ethos and philosophy and, where this is based on a theoretical or therapeutic model, a description of that model.

**9.**[^c-d5d6cb2f1c2a192c209361375942fc29] The arrangements made to protect and promote the health and welfare of children accommodated by the scheme.

**10.**[^c-3548e12ec38cf9be682873b8712c271d] The arrangements made for promoting appropriate behaviour, and for the control, discipline and restraint of children accommodated by the scheme.

**11.**[^c-6503735da4a2b7b4e7d12ee7ea285c5d] The arrangements made for child protection and to counter bullying.

**12.**[^c-0cd870ff0188aff18de797648987e955] The arrangements made for dealing with an incident where a child goes missing from premises or sites used by the scheme to accommodate children.

**13.**[^c-52ce9c27e8a1d0ac34ea368c7c2a2d8c] A description of any electronic or mechanical means of surveillance of children which may be used in premises or sites used by the scheme to accommodate children.

**14.**[^c-01bd9159a993ec6fcdf0ac04544ab197] The fire precautions and associated emergency procedures in place in accommodation for children provided by the scheme.

**15.**[^c-be8e4c4a992b56fbdfd64d8f4d0f3def] The arrangements for contact between children accommodated by the scheme and their parents.

**16.**[^c-a530868478b109aa07b8acd30aa2cf51] The arrangements for dealing with complaints.

**17.**[^c-19c5deb79627a83c843fc3cb34fbab3a] The type of accommodation, including the sleeping accommodation, provided, and, where applicable, how children are to be grouped, and in what circumstances they are to share bedrooms.

**18.**[^c-75147533de7f203fe8f5339157d99929] The address, telephone number and such other contact details as the registered person considers necessary for each site at which the registered provider intends to provide accommodation.
