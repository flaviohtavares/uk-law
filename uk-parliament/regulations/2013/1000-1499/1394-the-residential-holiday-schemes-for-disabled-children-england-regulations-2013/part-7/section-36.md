# The Residential Holiday Schemes for Disabled Children (England) Regulations 2013, section 36

> Text as at 2016-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Amendment of the Children’s Homes Regulations 2001[^key-caca820ec4cb5568826fbf18de50c396]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 3(2) of the Children’s Homes Regulations 2001[^f00014] for “The exceptions in paragraphs (1)(b), (c), and (d) do not apply” substitute “The exception in paragraph 1(c) does not apply”.
