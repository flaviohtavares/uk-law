# The Residential Holiday Schemes for Disabled Children (England) Regulations 2013, section 35

> Text as at 2016-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Application of Part 2 of the Act to persons carrying on or managing a scheme[^c-b7389e343061a054c464ddabcb6079bf]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to regulation 39, the provisions of Part 2 of the Act, insofar as they apply to England and are not already applied by the Care Standards Act 2000 (Extension of the Application of Part 2 to Holiday Schemes for Disabled Children) (England) Regulations 2013, apply to registered persons in accordance with the modifications set out in Schedule 7.
