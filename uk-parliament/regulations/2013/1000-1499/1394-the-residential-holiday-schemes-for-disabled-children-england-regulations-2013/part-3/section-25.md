# The Residential Holiday Schemes for Disabled Children (England) Regulations 2013, section 25

> Text as at 2016-04-06.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Other records[^key-63728f40a857faa4e96dddf90b912176]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The registered person must maintain records of the matters specified in Schedule 4 and ensure that they are kept up to date.

(2) The records referred to in paragraph (1) must be retained for a period of at least fifteen years from the date of the last entry.
