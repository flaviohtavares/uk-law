# The Residential Holiday Schemes for Disabled Children (England) Regulations 2013, section 19

> Text as at 2016-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Use of surveillance[^key-ef64f14a57bb115833ecedd9792a05c1]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to any requirement for electronic monitoring imposed by a court, the registered person must ensure that no electronic or mechanical monitoring device is used for the surveillance of a child accommodated by the scheme, except—

- (a) for the purpose of promoting and safeguarding that child’s welfare; and
- (b) where the following conditions are met—
  - (i) that child’s placing authority consents to the use of the device in question;
  - (ii) so far as practicable in the light of that child’s age and understanding, that child is informed in advance of the intention to use the device; and
  - (iii) the use of the device is no more restrictive than necessary, having regard to that child’s need for privacy.
