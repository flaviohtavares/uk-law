# The Residential Holiday Schemes for Disabled Children (England) Regulations 2013, section 24

> Text as at 2016-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Children’s holiday scheme case records[^key-82863940d9f5cc925fc7c56e8a3b5863]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The registered person must maintain, in respect of each child who is accommodated by the scheme, a record in writing which—

- (a) includes the documents, information and records specified in Schedule 3 in relation to that child;
- (b) is kept up to date; and
- (c) is signed and dated by the author of each entry.

(2) The record in paragraph (1) must be kept securely for a period of at least fifteen years from the date when the child was last accommodated by the scheme.
