# The Residential Holiday Schemes for Disabled Children (England) Regulations 2013, section 21

> Text as at 2016-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. Staffing of holiday schemes for disabled children[^key-41f4993c3c4ad67b6b8112a5db60f582]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The registered person must ensure that, at all times, having regard to—

- (a) the size of the scheme, its statement of purpose, and the number and needs of the children accommodated; and
- (b) the need to safeguard and promote the health and welfare of those children,

there is a sufficient number of suitably competent, experienced and trained employees.

(2) Where the scheme provides care and accommodation at more than one site, the registered person must be able to—

- (a) provide support on a daily basis at any site; and
- (b) attend each site in person.
