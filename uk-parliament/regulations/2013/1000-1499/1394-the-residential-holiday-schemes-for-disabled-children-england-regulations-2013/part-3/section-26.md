## 26. Notifiable events[^key-df3ce34a4231ab2dba2785df0aca1a9b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If, in relation to the scheme, an event in column 1 of the table in Schedule 5 takes place, the registered person must without delay notify the persons indicated in column 2 of that table in respect of that event.

(2) Any notification made in accordance with this regulation which is given orally must be confirmed in writing.
