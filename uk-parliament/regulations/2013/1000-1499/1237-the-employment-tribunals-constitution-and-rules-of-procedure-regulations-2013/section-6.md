# The Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013, section 6

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. Regional Employment Judges and the Vice President

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Lord Chancellor may appoint Regional Employment Judges.

(2) The President (England and Wales) or the Regional Employment Judge for an area may nominate an Employment Judge to discharge the functions of the Regional Employment Judge for that area.

(3) The Lord President may appoint a Vice President.

(4) The President (Scotland) or the Vice President may nominate an Employment Judge to discharge the functions of the Vice President.

(5) Appointments and nominations under this regulation shall be from the salaried[^M_F_2dad64cb-349e-424e-c17d-f410b1db118e] Employment Judges on the panel referred to in regulation 8(2)(a).
