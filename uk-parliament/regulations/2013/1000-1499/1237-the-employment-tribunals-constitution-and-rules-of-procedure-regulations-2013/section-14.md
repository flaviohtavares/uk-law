# The Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013, section 14

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14. Register and proof of judgments

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Lord Chancellor shall maintain a register containing a copy of all judgments and written reasons issued by a Tribunal which are required to be entered in the register under the Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024[^c-d21f7b62a294a574ca61051f26af559f].

(2)[^c-a24b3018db38d0d4f50d5decf1f80b7f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) A document purporting to be certified by a member of staff of a Tribunal to be a true copy of an entry of a judgment in the register shall, unless the contrary is proved, be sufficient evidence of the document and its contents.
