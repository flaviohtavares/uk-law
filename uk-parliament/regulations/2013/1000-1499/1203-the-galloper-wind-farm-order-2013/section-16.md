> Text as at 2017-11-30.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 16. Compulsory acquisition of land[^key-7693367b4c0ad200cb9d1dbd4acf18d8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised project or to facilitate, or is incidental, to it.

(2) This article is subject to article 18 (compulsory acquisition of rights) and article 23 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised project).
