  - (ii) the environmental statement submitted with the application for this Order and the environmental information contained in the clarifications, corrections and omissions document accepted by the Examining authority as a late submission, together with the table of environmental mitigation measures in relation to protected species dated 16 July 2012 and the two tables of offshore and onshore environmental mitigation measures dated 16 July 2012 submitted to the Examining authority as part of the examination of the application for this Order;
  - (iii) the design and access statement as submitted in the application for this Order; and
  - (iv) the landscape strategy as submitted in the application for this Order,

for certification that they are true copies of the documents referred to in this Order.

(2) A plan or document so certified shall be admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
