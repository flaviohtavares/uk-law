# The Localism Act 2011 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 797 (C. 38)

Town And Country Planning, England

Housing, England And Wales

The Localism Act 2011 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2013

Made

*5th April 2013*

The Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 240(2) and (7) of the Localism Act 2011[^f00001], makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Localism Act 2011 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2013.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Localism Act 2011; and
- “the commencement date” means the day after the day on which this Order is made.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on the commencement date in relation to England and Wales

Sections 116 and 121 of, and Schedules 9 to 12 to, the Act (so far as they are not already in force) come into force for all remaining purposes in relation to England and Wales on the commencement date.

## 3. Provisions coming into force on 1st October 2013 in relation to England and Wales

Section 167 of, and Schedule 15 to, the Act shall come into force on 1st October 2013.

## 4. Revocation

Article 5 of the Localism Act 2011 (Commencement No. 7 and Transitional, Saving and Transitory Provisions) Order 2012[^f00002] is revoked with effect from the commencement date.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Nick Boles

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

5th April 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force, in relation to England and Wales, sections 116 and 121 and Schedules 9 to 12 of the Localism Act [2011 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/20) (“the Act” for all remaining purposes on the day after this Order is made.

Article 3 brings section 167 and Schedule 15 of the Act (abolition of the Housing Revenue Account Subsidy system in England) into force on 1st October 2013.

Article 4 makes a consequential revocation of a previous transitional, saving and transitory provision relating to neighbourhood planning in a previous commencement order.
