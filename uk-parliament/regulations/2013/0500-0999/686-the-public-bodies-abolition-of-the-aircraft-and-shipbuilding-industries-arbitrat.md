# The Public Bodies (Abolition of the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries Arbitration Tribunal) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 686

Public Bodies

The Public Bodies (Abolition of the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries Arbitration Tribunal) Order 2013

Made

21st March 2013

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2) and (3)

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1), 6(1) and (5) and 35(2) of the Public Bodies Act 2011[^f00001] (“the Act”).

In accordance with section 8 of the Act, the Secretary of State considers that this Order—

- (a) serves the purpose of improving the exercise of public functions, having had regard to the factors set out in section 8(1); and
- (b) does not remove any necessary protection or prevent any person from continuing to exercise any right or freedom which that person might reasonably expect to continue to exercise.

The consent of the Northern Ireland Assembly has, so far as required, been obtained in accordance with section 9(3) of the Act.

The Secretary of State has consulted in accordance with section 10 of the Act.

A draft of this Order, and an explanatory document containing the information required by section 11(2) of the Act, have been laid before Parliament in accordance with section 11(1) after the end of the period of twelve weeks mentioned in section 11(3). In accordance with section 11(4) of the Act, the draft of this Order has been approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament after the expiry of the 40-day period referred to in that provision.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Public Bodies (Abolition of the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries Arbitration Tribunal) Order 2013.

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), this Order comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(3) Paragraph 10 of Schedule 1 comes into force on the day after that on which the other provisions of this Order come into force.

## 2. Abolition of the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries Arbitration Tribunal

The Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries Arbitration Tribunal is abolished.

## 3. Amendments consequential etc

Schedules 1 and 2 (which make consequential etc provision) have effect.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order abolishes the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries Arbitration Tribunal established under section 42 of the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries Act [1977 (c.3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/3).

Article 3 makes consequential repeals, revocations and amendments of Acts and other instruments specified in Schedules 1 and 2. Article 3 also removes the reference to the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries Arbitration Tribunal in Schedule 1 to the Public Bodies Act [2011 (c.24)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/24).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen. The explanatory document is published with this Order on www.legislation.gov.uk.
