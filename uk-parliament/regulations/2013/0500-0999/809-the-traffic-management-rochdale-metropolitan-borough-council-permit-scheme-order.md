# The Traffic Management (Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council) Permit Scheme Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 809

Highways, England

The Traffic Management (Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council) Permit Scheme Order 2013

Made

28th March 2013

Coming into force

29th April 2013

The Secretary of State for Transport having approved the Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme under section 34(2) of the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001] makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 34(4) and (5) and 39(2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Traffic Management (Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council) Permit Scheme Order 2013 and comes into force on 29th April 2013.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme ” means the permit scheme set out in the Schedule to this Order in terms commonly known as the “Greater Manchester Permit Scheme” which was prepared and submitted to the Secretary of State by Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council and has been approved by the Secretary of State; and
- “specified streets” has the meaning given by regulation 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007[^f00002].

## 3. Commencement of Permit Scheme

The Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme comes into effect on 29th April 2013.

## 4. Application of Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007

Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007 shall apply to the specified streets within Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Norman Baker

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order gives effect to the Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme.

The Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme is designed to control the carrying out of works in specified streets in Rochdale by imposing an obligation to obtain a permit from Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council before undertaking the works.

*Article 3* gives effect to the permit scheme, which will come into effect on 29th April 2013. The permit scheme referred to in this article is set out in the Schedule to the Order.

*Article 4* applies Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007 (which makes provision for the disapplication and modification of enactments in relation to specified works in specified streets) to the specified works and specified streets within the Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme.

A copy of the Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme is deposited and can be inspected at the offices of Rochdale Metropolitan Borough Council, Number 1 Riverside, Smith Street, Rochdale, OL16 1XU and is available to inspect on the Council’s website http://www.rochdale.gov.uk.
