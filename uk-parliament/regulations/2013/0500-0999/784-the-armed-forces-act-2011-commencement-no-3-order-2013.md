# The Armed Forces Act 2011 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 784 (C. 37)

Defence

The Armed Forces Act 2011 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2013

Made

2nd April 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 32(3) of the Armed Forces Act 2011[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 2011 (Commencement No.3) Order 2013.

**2.** In this Order “the Act” means the Armed Forces Act 2011.

## 3. Commencement

Section 25 of the Act shall come into force on 6th April 2013.

Mark Francois

Minister of State

Ministry of Defence

2nd April 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 25 of the Armed Forces Act [2011 (c.18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/18) on 6<sup>th</sup> April 2013.
