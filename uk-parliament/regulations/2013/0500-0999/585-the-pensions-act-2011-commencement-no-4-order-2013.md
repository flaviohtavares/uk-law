# The Pensions Act 2011 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 585 (C. 21)

Pensions

The Pensions Act 2011 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2013

Made

12th March 2013

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 38(4) of the Pensions Act 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Act 2011 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2013.

## 2. Commencement of provisions

The following provisions of the Pensions Act 2011 come into force on 13th March 2013—

- (a) section 22 (Pension Protection Fund) in so far as it is not already in force; and
- (b) the following paragraphs of Schedule 4 (Pension Protection Fund)—
  - (i) paragraph 1 (introductory provision for amendments to the Pensions Act 2004[^f00002]) in so far as it is not already in force;
  - (ii) paragraphs 21 to 28 (postponement of compensation); and
  - (iii) paragraphs 29 to 36 (pension compensation sharing: postponement of compensation).

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Steve Webb

Minister of State

Department for Work and Pensions

12th March 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings certain provisions of the Pensions Act [2011 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/19) into force on 13th March 2013, in so far as they are not already in force.

Article 2 commences the following provisions of the Pensions Act 2011—

- section 22, which relates to the Pension Protection Fund;
- paragraph 1 of Schedule 4, which introduces provisions for amendments to the Pensions Act 2004;
- paragraphs 21 to 28 of Schedule 4, which relate to postponement of compensation; and
- paragraphs 29 to 36 of Schedule 4, which relate to pension compensation sharing and postponement of compensation.
