# The Damages for Bereavement (Variation of Sum) (England and Wales) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 510

Damages, England And Wales

The Damages for Bereavement (Variation of Sum) (England and Wales) Order 2013

Made

5th March 2013

Laid before Parliament

11th March 2013

Coming into force

1st April 2013

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1A(5) of the Fatal Accidents Act 1976[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Damages for Bereavement (Variation of Sum) (England and Wales) Order 2013 and comes into force on 1 April 2013.

(2) This Order applies only to causes of action which accrue on or after 1 April 2013.

## 2. Variation of sum

In section 1A(3) of the Fatal Accidents Act 1976, for “£11,800” substitute “£12,980”.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Helen Grant

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

5th March 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which applies only to causes of action accruing on or after 1<sup>st</sup> April 2013, amends section 1A(3) of the Fatal Accidents Act 1976 by increasing the sum which may be awarded as damages for bereavement in England and Wales from £11,800 to £12,980.
