# The Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2013

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2013 No. 531

National Health Service, England

The Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2013

Made

7th March 2013

Coming into force

22nd March 2013

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1) and 272(7) of, and paragraphs 5 and 7 of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order following the completion of the consultation prescribed under section 25(3) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-defe51c11cfd1f2143ddf6aaf8b6fc0d]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2013 and comes into force on 22nd March 2013.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the National Health Service Act 2006;
- “community health services” means any services—
  - (a) whose provision must be arranged by an integrated care board[^c-e9d203b1c909c2e0d4516c9ea2c9e4d8] under section 3(1)(g) or (i)[^c-b331e189de9b9ec2936254b50a226a18] of the Act[^f00003];
  - (b) which are of a kind mentioned in section 3(1)(g) or (i)[^c-01d85f596263a288c403633db22553d6] of the Act, but—
    - (i) whose provision must be arranged by NHS England[^c-b16a306d7b36639928496b815d2e2c3b] under regulations made under section 3B of the Act[^f00004]; or
    - (ii) which may be provided by the Secretary of State or a local authority under section 2B of the Act[^f00005];
  - (c) which a local authority must provide under paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Act[^f00006];
  - (d) which a local authority may provide under paragraph 2 or 7A of Schedule 1 to the Act[^f00007]; or
  - (e) which the Secretary of State must provide under paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Act;
- “establishment date” means 22nd March 2013;
- [^c-3826ebe2e4f14e043e843237447f5f6f]...
- “the trust” means the National Health Service trust established by article 2.

## 2. Establishment and name of the trust[^c-cd09c97215ec2d3dc103149ab6000ba2]

There is established a National Health Service trust which is called the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust.

## 3. Nature and functions of the trust[^c-0bf6c98492f01038918a3368f9554f9c]

The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services, namely hospital accommodation and services and community health services, for the purposes of the health service.

## 4. Directors of the trust[^c-ea869032f3299aa37be5e0c7071f639c]

(1) The trust shall have, in addition to the chair, 5 executive directors[^c-9950d00052757b31d32f616d8e76d84f] and 6 non-executive directors[^c-8512c22638dd4695c9574e6ce6644513].

(2) If the Secretary of State suspends the chair, or a non-executive director of the trust, the number of non-executive directors of the trust shall increase during the period of suspension by the number of persons so suspended.

## 5. Accounting date of the trust[^c-6f73f45a251c41af09a3d6a59d7915af][^c-21314a8b5e95fe154b46bd9fd7500d93]

The accounting date of the trust shall be 31st March.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes on 22nd March 2013 a National Health Service trust called the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (“the trust”).

The Order sets out the functions of the trust (article 3) and the number of executive and non-executive directors of the trust (article 4). It also sets out the operational date (the date on which the trust assumes all its functions), the accounting date of the trust (article 5), the limited functions that the trust may carry out between its establishment and operational dates (article 6) and makes provision for assistance to be given by the South West Strategic Health Authority (article 7).
