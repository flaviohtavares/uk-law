# The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 705 (C. 32)

Prevention And Suppression Of Terrorism

The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2013

Made

25th March 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 182(5) of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2013.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 22nd April 2013

Section 117(4), (5), (6), (7) and (8) of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (detention of persons under section 41 of the Terrorism Act 2000[^f00002]) shall come into force on 22nd April 2013.

James Brokenshire

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Home Office

25th March 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 117(4), (5), (6), (7) and (8) of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 on 22nd April 2013. These provisions amend section 51 of the Police Reform Act [2002 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/30) so that local policing bodies must ensure that particular arrangements in relation to the visiting of terrorist detainees by independent custody visitors are in place.
