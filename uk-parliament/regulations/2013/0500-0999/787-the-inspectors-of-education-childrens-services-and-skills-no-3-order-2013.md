# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 787

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2013

Made

9th April 2013

Coming into force

10th April 2013

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 9th day of April 2013

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2013 and comes into force on 10th April 2013.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Christopher Berry

Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council
