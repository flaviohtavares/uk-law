# The Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 (Consequential Provisions and Modifications) Order 2013, SCHEDULE 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2024-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/602
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — EXTENSION OF MODIFICATIONS OF ENACTMENTS MADE BY 2012 ACT

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 25

### Pensions (Increase) Act 1971

**1.**[^c-0fdb240163b5b4ef9a8dc26b974b735e] In paragraph 44 of Schedule 2 to the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971[^f00031] (official pensions), for “a relevant authority (as defined in section 6 of the Fire (Scotland) Act [2005 (asp 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/5))” substitute “the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service”.

### Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974

**2.**[^c-84cedc76d31efc21ccb5d3cc47e0a7b4] In section 51A of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (application of Part to police)[^f00032]—

- (a) in subsection (2E), for paragraph (a) substitute—

  > - (a) section 24 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act [2012 (asp 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2012/8);

  ; and
- (b) in subsection (3)(b), for “each chief officer of police in Scotland” substitute “the chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland”.

### Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984

**3.**[^c-6d470744b4398a53acd569abf0bf8655] In section 87(1) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 (exemption of fire brigade, ambulance and police vehicles from speed limits)[^f00033], for “a relevant authority (as defined in section 6 of the Fire (Scotland) Act [2005 (asp 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/5))” substitute “the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service”.

### Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994

**4.**[^c-b9533828f8dc60ef2ea416bbc45ddee8] (1) The Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994[^f00034] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 102(5) (arrangements for the provision of prisoner escorts)[^f00035], for “prescribed under section 9(1A)(b) of the Police (Scotland) Act [1967 (c.77)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1967/77)” substitute “under section 28 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act [2012 (asp 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2012/8)”.

(3) In section 163 (local authority powers to provide closed-circuit television)[^f00036]—

- (a) in subsection (3), after ‘area’ insert “or, in Scotland, the local commander designated for the local authority’s area”; and
- (b) in subsection (4)—
  - (i) omit the definition of “chief officer of police”; and
  - (ii) after the definition of “local authority” insert—

    > - “local commander” has the meaning given by section 44 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act [2012 (asp 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2012/8);

    .

### Police Act 1996

**5.**[^c-fbc48af2f12bd3659439700d8c1602f2] (1) The Police Act 1996[^f00037] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 59(2) (police federations)[^f00038], for “section 26(2A) of the Police (Scotland) Act 1967” substitute “under section 48 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 in so far as relating to the matters described in section 52 of that Act,”.

(3) In section 60(2) (regulations for police federations)[^f00039]—

- (a) in paragraph (c), for “police authorities” substitute “the Scottish Police Authority”;
- (b) in paragraph (d), for “police authorities” substitute “the Scottish Police Authority”; and
- (c) in paragraph (e), for “26 of the Police (Scotland) Act 1967” substitute “48 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012”.

(4) Omit section 61(6). *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8d8cb5c5453ede8282b19c54758c601]

(5) In section 62(1A)(a) (functions of the Board with respect to regulations) *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8d8cb5c5453ede8282b19c54758c601][^f00040], for “26 or 27 of the Police (Scotland) Act 1967” substitute “48 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012”. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8d8cb5c5453ede8282b19c54758c601]

(6) In section 62(3) (functions of the Board with respect to regulations) *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8d8cb5c5453ede8282b19c54758c601][^f00041], omit “, section 27 of the Police (Scotland) Act 1967”. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8d8cb5c5453ede8282b19c54758c601]

(7) In section 63 (Police Advisory Boards for England and Wales and for Scotland)—

- (a) in subsection (1)—
  - (i) omit “and a Police Advisory Board for Scotland”; and
  - (ii) omit “in those countries respectively”; and
- (b) in subsection (2)[^f00042], for “each of the Police Advisory Boards” substitute “the Police Advisory Board for England and Wales”.

(8) Omit section 64(4E) and (4F)[^f00043].

(9) In section 99(1) (jurisdiction of metropolitan police officers)[^f00044], for “a police force maintained under the Police (Scotland) Act 1967” substitute “the Police Service of Scotland”.

### Police Act 1997

**6.**[^c-7e6b9df80fbb05e68fa1f15474be02e0] (1) The Police Act 1997[^f00045] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 93 (authorisations to deal with property etc.)—

- (a) in subsection (3)[^f00046]—
  - (i) after paragraph (za)[^f00047] insert—

    > - (zb) if the authorising officer is within subsection (5)(d), by a constable of the Police Service of Scotland;

    ;
  - (ii) in paragraph (a)[^f00048], for “(5)(d) to” substitute “(5)(e),”;
  - (iii) after paragraph (e)[^f00049] insert—

    > - (ea) if the authorising officer is within subsection (5)(ia), by a staff officer of the Police Investigations and Review Commissioner.

    ;
- (b) after subsection (3A)[^f00050] insert—

  > (3ZA) An authorisation under this section may be given by the authorising officer within subsection (5)(ia) only where it relates to the taking of action in pursuance of paragraph (b)(i) of section 33A of the Police, Public Order and Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2006.

  ;
- (c) in subsection (5)[^f00051]—
  - (i) for paragraph (d) substitute—

    > - (d) the chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland, or any deputy chief constable or assistant chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland who is designated for the purposes of this paragraph by the chief constable;

    ;
  - (ii) after paragraph (i) insert—

    > - (ia) the Police Investigations and Review Commissioner;

    ; and
- (d) in subsection (6)(b)[^f00052], for the words from “of” to “maintained” substitute “or (ia) of subsection (5), means Scotland”.

(3) In section 94 (authorisations given in absence of authorising officer)—

- (a) in subsection (1)(b[^f00053]), for “, (c) or (d)” substitute “or (c)”;
- (b) in subsection (2)[^f00054], after paragraph (g) insert—

  > - (ga) where the authorising officer is within paragraph (ia) of that subsection, by a staff officer of the Police Investigations and Review Commissioner who is designated by the Commissioner for the purposes of this section.

  .

(4) In section 95 (authorisations: form and duration etc.)[^f00055], in subsection (6), for “or (e),” substitute “, (d), (e)”.

(5) In section 105(3) (appeals)[^f00056], for “, (c) or (d)” substitute “or (c)”.

(6)[^M_F_c15c4e69-564d-44af-85e1-c38e000baab2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) In section 126 (interpretation of Part 5)—

- (a) in the definition of “chief officer”[^f00059], for sub-paragraph (ii) substitute—

  > - (ii) the chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland, and

  ;
- (b) in the definition of “police authority”[^f00060], for sub-paragraph (i) substitute—

  > - (i) the Scottish Police Authority, and

  .

### Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004

**7.**[^c-430e8bd32879d31eb23c87f1c282c23e] (1) The Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00061] is amended as follows.

(2) For subsection (10) of section 34 (pensions etc.)[^f00062] substitute—

> (10) In this section “Scottish fire authority”—
>
> - (a) means the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service, and
> - (b) except in subsections (2)(e) and (h), includes a relevant authority as defined in section 6 of the Fire (Scotland) Act [2005 (asp 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/5) (despite the repeal of that section by the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act [2012 (asp 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2012/8)).

.

(3) In subsection (6) of section 35[^f00063] (definitions for purpose of information in connection with pensions etc.), after the definition of “prescribed” add—

> - “Scottish fire authority”—
>   - (a) means the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service, and
>   - (b) in subsection (5) includes a relevant authority as defined in section 6 of the Fire (Scotland) Act [2005 (asp 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/5) (despite the repeal of that section by the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act [2012 (asp 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2012/8)).

.

### Gambling Act 2005

**8.**[^c-7b70b19fc13dd5c01535ca13f332c05b] In section 157 of the Gambling Act 2005 (responsible authorities in relation to premises)[^f00064], for paragraph (f) substitute—

> - (f) the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service,

.

### Corporate Manslaughter and Corporate Homicide Act 2007

**9.**[^c-cca10bad1eec013bd50f234b6e8f9e57] In section 6(2) of the Corporate Manslaughter and Corporate Homicide Act 2007 (duty of care for certain organisations in emergencies)[^f00065], for paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service;

.
