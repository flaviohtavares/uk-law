# The Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 (Consequential Provisions and Modifications) Order 2013, section 7

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Liability for unlawful conduct of member of Ministry of Defence Police[^c-15ba2c1cdd3ba69cd1a6d4450fea6aef]

*Extent: S*

 Section 24 of the 2012 Act applies in relation to a member of the Ministry of Defence Police providing assistance to the Police Service of Scotland in pursuance of section 2A of the 1987 Act[^f00022] as it applies to a constable engaged on service with the Police Service of Scotland.
