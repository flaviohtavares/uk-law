# The Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 (Consequential Provisions and Modifications) Order 2013, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2024-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/602
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Offences[^c-cdd3c1a4d95b2a0c7f4d5d12ef245c7d]

*Extent: S*

(1) Sections 1, 3 and 4 of the Emergency Workers (Scotland) Act 2005 (assaulting or impeding providers of emergency services, persons assisting providers and supplementary provisions)[^f00030] apply in relation to the following persons as they apply in relation to a person acting in a capacity mentioned in section 1(3)(b) of that Act—

- (a) a person acting in the capacity of an employee of a 2004 Act authority providing assistance under arrangements made by virtue of article 19(1) or 20(1);
- (b) a person acting in the capacity of a fire and rescue officer providing assistance under arrangements made by virtue of article 19(2) or 20(2).

(2) A person guilty of an offence by virtue of paragraph (1) is liable, on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 12 months or to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, or to both.
