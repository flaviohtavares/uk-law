# The Social Security Pensions (Flat Rate Accrual Amount) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 529

Social Security

The Social Security Pensions (Flat Rate Accrual Amount) Order 2013

Made

6th March 2013

Laid before Parliament

11th March 2013

Coming into force

6th April 2013

In accordance with section 148AA(1) (revaluation of flat rate accrual amount) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00001], the Secretary of State has reviewed the general level of earnings obtaining in Great Britain.

It appears to the Secretary of State that the general level of earnings has increased during the review period.

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 148AA(3) to (6) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Social Security Pensions (Flat Rate Accrual Amount) Order 2013 and shall come into force on 6th April 2013.

## 2. Flat Rate Accrual Amount

For the purpose of paragraph 13(2) of Schedule 4B to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00002], the flat rate accrual amount for the tax year beginning 6th April 2013 and subsequent tax years shall be £91.00.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Steve Webb

Minister of State

Department for Work and Pensions

6th March 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made following a review by the Secretary of State under section 148AA(1) of the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5) of the general level of earnings in Great Britain with a view to determining whether, and if so by how much, the general level of earnings has increased during the period from 1st October 2011 to 30th September 2012. As a result of that review, it appears to the Secretary of State that the general level of earnings during that period has increased by 1.8%. This Order therefore directs, in accordance with section 148AA(4) of that Act, that the flat rate accrual amount for the tax year beginning 6th April 2013, and subsequent tax years, shall be £91.00.

The flat rate accrual amount is a flat rate cash amount of additional pension which applies to accruals in the first earnings band under Schedule 4B to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4). This method of calculation of additional pension applies from the flat rate introduction year, which by virtue of the Social Security Pensions (Flat Rate Introduction Year) Order 2011 ([S.I. 2011/2953](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/2953)) is the tax year beginning 6th April 2012.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the private sector or civil society organisations.
