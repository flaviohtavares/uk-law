# The Hinkley Point C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2013, section 78

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 78. Use of temporary jetty[^key-d88a6794995bc157b94899c96811d441]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The undertaker shall only use the temporary jetty for the purposes of, or in connection with, the construction of nuclear energy related facilities at Hinkley Point C, Somerset.
