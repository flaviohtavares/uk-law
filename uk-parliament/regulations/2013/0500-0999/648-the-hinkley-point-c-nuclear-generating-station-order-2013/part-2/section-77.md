# The Hinkley Point C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2013, section 77

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 77. Charges[^key-7a2ef9c07e81cfae5641158559705fa7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker may levy charges for any services performed by it in the exercise and performance of its statutory powers and duties at the harbour.

(2) The undertaker may confer total or partial exemption from, allow rebates to or make compositions with any person with respect to, charges and may vary or extinguish any such exemption, rebate or composition.

(3) In this article “charges” means any charges other than ship, passenger and goods dues.
