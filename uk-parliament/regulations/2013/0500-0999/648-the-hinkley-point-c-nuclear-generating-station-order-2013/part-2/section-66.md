# The Hinkley Point C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2013, section 66

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 66. Provision against danger to navigation[^key-58dd159ab864922052768b9523f0529f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In case of damage to, or destruction or decay of, a tidal work or any part of it, the undertaker shall as soon as reasonably practicable notify Trinity House and Bridgwater Harbour Authority and shall lay down such buoys, exhibit such lights, and take such other steps for preventing danger to navigation, as Trinity House may from time to time direct.
