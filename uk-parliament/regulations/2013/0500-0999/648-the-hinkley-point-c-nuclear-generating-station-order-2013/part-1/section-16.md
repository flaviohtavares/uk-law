# The Hinkley Point C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2013, section 16

> Text as at 2023-05-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 16. Status of footpaths created or improved[^key-5b85b5488c95055f42f259921bc270e6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

With effect from the date on which the highway authority is satisfied that the footpaths specified in columns (1) and (2) of Schedule 7 (status of footpaths created or improved) have been created or improved to the standard specified in a footpath implementation plan[^key-41178098c83bae292a1e79787ebd9f52], the footpaths in question shall be deemed to have the status specified in column (3) of that Schedule.
