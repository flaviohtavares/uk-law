# The Offender Management Act 2007 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 1963 (C. 82)

Probation, England And Wales

The Offender Management Act 2007 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2013

Made

6th August 2013

Coming into force

6th January 2014

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 41(4) of the Offender Management Act 2007[^f00001].

A draft of this Order was laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament in accordance with section 41(4) of that Act.

## 1. Citation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Offender Management Act 2007 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2013.

(2) This Order comes into force on 6<sup>th</sup> January 2014.

## 2. Provisions coming into force in England and Wales on 6th January 2014

The following provisions of the Offender Management Act 2007 come into force on 6<sup>th</sup> January 2014—

(1) section 28 (application of polygraph conditions); and

(2) section 29 (effect of polygraph conditions).

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

Jeremy Wright

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

6th August 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the sixth commencement order made under the Offender Management Act [2007 (c.21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/21). It brings provisions concerning the application and effect of polygraph conditions (contained in sections 28 and 29 of the Offender Management Act 2007) into force. These provisions enable the Secretary of State to include polygraph conditions for certain offenders released on licence.
