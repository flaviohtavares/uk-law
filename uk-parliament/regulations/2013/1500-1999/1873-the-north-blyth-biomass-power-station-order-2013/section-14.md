# The North Blyth Biomass Power Station Order 2013, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the minerals code

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00021] (minerals) are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
