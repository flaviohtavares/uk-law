# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 6) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 1717

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 6) Order 2013

Made

10th July 2013

Coming into force

11th July 2013

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 10th day of July 2013

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 6) Order 2013 and comes into force on 11th July 2013.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills with effect from 1st September 2013.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

- Pamela Louise Fearnley
- Merryl Hedda Ford
- Morag Pamela Kophamel

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
