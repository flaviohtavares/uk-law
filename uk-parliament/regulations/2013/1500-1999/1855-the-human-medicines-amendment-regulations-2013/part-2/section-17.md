# The Human Medicines (Amendment) Regulations 2013, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Amendment of regulation 68

In regulation 68 (revocation, variation and suspension of UK marketing authorisation), after paragraph (11) insert—

> (11A) Condition K is that the manufacture of the product to which the authorisation relates is not carried out in compliance with the particulars provided under paragraphs 5 and 9 of Schedule 8.
