# The Human Medicines (Amendment) Regulations 2013, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Amendment of regulation 110

In regulation 110 (revocation, variation and suspension of certificate of registration), after paragraph (8) insert—

> (8A) Condition H is that the manufacture and control of the product to which the certificate relates is not in compliance with the particulars provided under regulation 103(8)(c) and (d).
