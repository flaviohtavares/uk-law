# The Human Medicines (Amendment) Regulations 2013, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Insertion of regulation A17

In Part 3, before the heading “Manufacturing and wholesale dealing” there is inserted—

> Manufacture and distribution of medicinal products and active substances
>
> **CHAPTER 1 — Interpretation**
>
> **A17. Interpretation**
>
> In this Part “**manufacture**”, in relation to an active substance, includes any process carried out in the course of making the substance and the various processes of dividing up, packaging, and presentation of the active substance.
>
> CHAPTER 2
