# The Human Medicines (Amendment) Regulations 2013, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Amendment of regulation 206

(1) Regulation 206 (infringement notices) is amended as follows.

(2) For paragraph (1) substitute—

> (1) If an enforcement authority has objective grounds for considering that any person (“**P**”) has contravened any relevant provision, it may serve upon P a notice in writing (referred to in this Part as an “**infringement notice**”)—
>
> - (a) informing P of the authority’s grounds for considering that P has contravened one or more relevant provision;
> - (b) specifying the relevant provision;
> - (c) specifying the measures which P must take in order to ensure that the contravention does not continue or, as the case may be, does not recur;
> - (d) requiring P to take those measures, within such period as may be specified in the notice;
> - (e) specifying the further action (if any) that the enforcement authority may take.

(3) After paragraph (3) insert—

> (4) In this regulation “**relevant provision**” means a provision of—
>
> - (a) this Part;
> - (b) Chapter 3 of Title II of Regulation [(EC) No 726/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0726); or
> - (c) the Implementing Regulation.
