# The Human Medicines (Amendment) Regulations 2013, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Amendment of regulation 84

In regulation 84 (EU marketing authorisations: failure to provide information as to safety etc), in paragraph (2) for “16(4)” substitute “16(3a)[^f00016]”.

## Annotations

[^f00016]: Footnote: Article 16(4) of Regulation [(EC) No 726/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0726) was re-numbered as Article 16(3a) by virtue of the Corrigendum (OJ L 348, 31.12.2010, p1) to Regulation (EU) No 1235/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 December 2010 amending, as regards pharmacovigilance of medicines for human use, Regulation [(EC) No 726/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0726) laying down Community procedures for the authorisation and supervision of medicinal products for human and veterinary use and establishing a European Medical Agency, and Regulation [(EC) No 1394/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1394) on advanced therapy medicinal products.
