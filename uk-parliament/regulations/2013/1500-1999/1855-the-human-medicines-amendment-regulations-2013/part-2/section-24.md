# The Human Medicines (Amendment) Regulations 2013, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Amendment of regulation 210

In regulation 210 (offences relating to pharmacovigilance obligations under Regulation [(EC) No 726/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0726)), in paragraphs (3)(a) and (6)(a) for “16(4)” substitute “16(3a)”.
