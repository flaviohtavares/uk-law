# The Human Medicines (Amendment) Regulations 2013, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Amendment of regulation 330

In regulation 330 (analysis of samples: other cases), for paragraphs (1) and (2) substitute—

> (1) This regulation applies where a person other than an inspector or a person authorised by an enforcement authority has purchased an active substance or a medicinal product.
>
> (2) The person may submit a sample of the active substance or medicinal product for analysis to the public analyst for the area in which the active substance or medicinal product was purchased or, if for the time being there is no public analyst for the area, to the public analyst for another area.
