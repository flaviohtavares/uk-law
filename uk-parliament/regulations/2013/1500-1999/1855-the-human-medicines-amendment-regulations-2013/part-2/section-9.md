## 9. Amendment of regulation 34

In regulation 34 (offences: breach of regulations and false information and defence concerning starting materials)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “, 18(1) or 32” substitute “or 18(1)”;
- (b) in paragraphs (4) and (5), for “37(2)(b)” substitute “37(3)” .
