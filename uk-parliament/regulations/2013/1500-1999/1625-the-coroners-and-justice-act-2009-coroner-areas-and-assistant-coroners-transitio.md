# The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Coroner Areas and Assistant Coroners) Transitional Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 1625

Coroners, England And Wales

The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Coroner Areas and Assistant Coroners) Transitional Order 2013

Made

2nd July 2013

Laid before Parliament

4th July 2013

Coming into force

25th July 2013

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 2, paragraph 2(1)(a) of Schedule 3 and paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 22 to the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^f00001] and after consulting in accordance with paragraph 1(4) of Schedule 2 and paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 3 to that Act, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Coroner Areas and Assistant Coroners) Transitional Order 2013.

**2.** This Order comes into force on the 25th July 2013.

## 3. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “coroner’s district” means a coroner’s district constituted under the Coroners Act 1988[^f00002], as it exists immediately before the day on which this Order comes into force;
- “coroner area” means a coroner area specified by this Order.

## 4. Coroners’ districts specified as coroner areas

Each coroner’s district listed in the table in the Schedule to this Order is specified as a coroner area with the same name (but ending with “coroner area” instead of “coroner’ district”).

## 5. Appointment of an assistant coroner for each coroner area

At least one assistant coroner must be appointed for each coroner area specified by this Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This note specifies new coroner areas under the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 and sets out the minimum number of assistant coroners that must be appointed to each coroner area. As required by the 2009 Act, the coroner areas created by this order correspond with the coroners’ districts under the Coroners Act 1988 and have the same names.
