# The Leeds Railway Station (Southern Entrance) Order 2013, section 10

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Access to works[^c-26cf62b131230d754b795e31fb3b125c]

*Extent: E+W*

The promoter may, for the purposes of the authorised works—

- (a) form and lay out means of access, or improve existing means of access, in the locations specified in columns (1) and (2) of Schedule 7 (access to works) at or about the points marked “A” on the deposited plans; and
- (b) form and lay out such other means of access or improve existing means of access, at such locations within the Order limits as the promoter reasonably requires for the purposes of the authorised works, as may be approved by the highway authority, but such approval must not be unreasonably withheld.
