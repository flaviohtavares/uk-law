# The Leeds Railway Station (Southern Entrance) Order 2013, section 5

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Power to deviate[^key-716aec07571d7fa56acce151ad1dd88b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In constructing or maintaining any of the scheduled works, the promoter may subject to paragraph (2)—

- (a) deviate laterally from the lines or situations shown on the deposited plans to the extent of the limits of deviation for that work; and
- (b) deviate vertically from the levels shown on the deposited sections—
  - (i) to any extent not exceeding 2 metres upwards; or
  - (ii) to any extent not exceeding 2 metres downwards.

(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1)(b), the promoter may deviate vertically from the levels shown on the deposited sections to any extent downwards in respect of the vertical piles supporting Work No. 1.
