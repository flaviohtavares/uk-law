# The Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 1948

Terms And Conditions Of Employment

The Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2013

Made

2nd August 2013

Laid before Parliament

6th August 2013

Coming into force

30th August 2013

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 7 and 41(4) of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2013 and come into force on 30th August 2013.

## 2. Amendment to the Biocidal Products and Chemicals (Appointment of Authorities and Enforcement) Regulations 2013

In regulation 27(2) of the Biocidal Products and Chemicals (Appointment of Authorities and Enforcement) Regulations 2013[^f00002] for “Schedule 4 to the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004”[^f00003] insert “Schedule 1 to the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013”[^f00004].

## 3. Amendment to the Health and Safety (Fees) Regulations 2012

(1) The Health and Safety (Fees) Regulations 2012[^f00005] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulations 17(2)(c) and 24(6)(c) for “regulation 16(1) and (3)(b) of, and Schedules 1 and 4 to, the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004” insert “Schedule 1 to the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013”.

## 4. Amendments to the Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2010

(1) Schedule 4 to the Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2010[^f00006] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 5(6)(b) for “Rules 34 to 36” insert “rules 70 to 73”.

(3) In paragraph 5(7) for “Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004, as modified by the provisions of Schedule 4 to those Regulations” insert “Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013”.

## 5. Amendment to the Discipline of Judges (Designation) Order 2009

In regulation 3(2) of the Discipline of Judges (Designation) Order 2009[^f00007] for “regulation 8(3)(b) or (c) of the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004” insert “regulation 8(2)(b) or (c) of the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013”.

## 6. Amendment to the Judicial Appointments Order 2008

In Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Judicial Appointments Order[^f00008] 2008 for “regulation 8(3)(a) of the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004” insert “regulation 8(2)(a) of the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013”.

## 7. Amendments to the REACH Enforcement Regulations 2008

(1) Part 2 of Schedule 8 to the REACH Enforcement Regulations 2008[^f00009] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 2 for “Schedule 4 to the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004” insert “Schedule 1 to the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013”.

(3) In paragraph 3 for “Schedule 4” insert “Schedule 1”.

Jo Swinson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations and Consumer Affairs

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

2nd August 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make amendments to a number of statutory instruments consequential on the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013. The Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2013 revoked and replaced the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004.
