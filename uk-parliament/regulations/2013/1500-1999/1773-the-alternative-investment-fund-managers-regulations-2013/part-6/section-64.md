# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 64

## 64. Procedure on suspension (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A suspension under regulation 63 takes effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) immediately, if the notice given under paragraph (3) states that that is the case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) on such date as may be specified in the notice; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) if no date is specified in the notice, when the matter to which it relates is no longer open to review. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) A suspension may be expressed to take effect immediately (or on a specified date) only if the FCA, having regard to the ground on which it is suspending the AIFM's entitlement to market, considers that it is necessary for the suspension to take effect immediately (or on that date). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) If the FCA proposes to suspend an AIFM's entitlement to market an AIF, or suspends such entitlement with immediate effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) it must give separate written notice to the AIFM and (if applicable) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] third country AIF custodian[^c-63e87cff0227e8bca0d1eec88e24ae32] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of that AIF; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the AIFM or the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] third country AIF custodian *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] may refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) A notice under paragraph (3)(a) must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) give details of the suspension; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) inform the person to whom it is given of when the suspension takes effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) state the FCA's reasons for giving the suspension and for its determination as to when the suspension takes effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) inform the person to whom it is given that it may make representations to the FCA within such period as may be specified in it (whether or not it has referred the matter to the Tribunal); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (e) inform the person to whom it is given of its right to refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) The FCA may extend the period allowed under the notice for making representations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(6) Paragraph (7) applies if, having considered any representations made by a person to whom the notice was given, the FCA decides— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) to make the suspension in the way proposed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) to make the suspension in a way other than that proposed, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) if it has been made, not to revoke the suspension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(7) If this paragraph applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the FCA must give separate written notice to the AIFM and (if applicable) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] third country AIF custodian *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the AIFM or the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] third country AIF custodian *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] may refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(8) If, having considered any representations made by a person to whom the notice was given, the FCA decides— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) not to make the suspension in the way proposed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) to revoke a suspension, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

it must give separate written notice to the AIFM and (if applicable) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] third country AIF custodian *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b].

(9) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c), section 391(8) of the Act (publication) applies as if a notice under paragraph (3)(a), (7)(a) or (8) were a supervisory notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
