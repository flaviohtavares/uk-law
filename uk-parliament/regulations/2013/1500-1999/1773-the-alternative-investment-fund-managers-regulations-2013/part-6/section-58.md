# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Marketing of AIFs managed by small third country AIFMs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A small third country AIFM must give written notification to the FCA before marketing an AIF managed by it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] , except where the AIF is recognised under section 271A of the Act[^c-5b4d09a028e74b192fcb0241451e471b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b].

(2) The notification must include a statement confirming that the following conditions are met— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the AIFM is the person responsible for complying with the implementing provisions relating to the marketing of the AIF; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the AIFM is a small third country AIFM. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) The AIFM must provide the FCA with such information as the FCA directs on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the main instruments in which the AIFM trades, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the principal exposures and most important concentrations of the AIFs that it manages, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

in order to enable the FCA to monitor systemic risk effectively. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) The FCA may not give a direction under paragraph (3) that requires an AIFM to provide information— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) if an investor's acquisition of units or shares of the AIF results from marketing that is permitted because of the notification, after the date on which the final such investor disposes of such units or shares; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) if there is no acquisition of units or shares of the AIF resulting from such marketing, after the date on which the AIFM ceases marketing the AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
