# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 62

## 62. Revocation of entitlement to market (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA may revoke an AIFM's entitlement to market an AIF following a notification under regulation 57, 58 or 59 if it appears to the FCA that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the AIFM, or the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] third country AIF custodian[^c-9ef52eb5f5259850cbe08be0d4a4dd44] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of that AIF, has contravened an implementing provision which applies to it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the AIFM, or the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] third country AIF custodian *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of that AIF, has in purported compliance with an implementing provision, knowingly or recklessly given the FCA information which is false or misleading in a material particular; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) one or more of the conditions confirmed in the notification as being met is no longer satisfied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) the AIF is wound up; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (e) none of sub-paragraphs (a) to (d) applies, but it is undesirable in the interests of investors or potential investors that the AIF should continue to be marketed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) If the FCA proposes to revoke an AIFM's entitlement to market an AIF on a ground mentioned in paragraph (1)(a), (b), (c) or (e), it must give a warning notice to the AIFM and, in the case of an entitlement to market following a notification under regulation 57, to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] third country AIF custodian *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of that AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) If the FCA decides to revoke an AIFM's entitlement to market an AIF under paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) it must give a decision notice to the AIFM and, in the case of an entitlement to market following a notification under regulation 57, to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] third country AIF custodian *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of that AIF; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the AIFM or the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] third country AIF custodian *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] may refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
