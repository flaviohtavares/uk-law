# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773

## 55. Change to information provided with application (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If there is a material change to the information mentioned in regulation 54(2)(b), the AIFM must give written notice of the change to the FCA— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) in the case of a change planned by the AIFM (“planned change”), at least one month before implementing the change; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) in other cases, as soon as reasonably practicable and, at the latest, immediately after an unplanned change has occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) If a planned change would mean the AIFM no longer complied with an implementing provision applicable to it, the FCA must inform the AIFM without undue delay that it must not implement the change. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) If— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) a planned change is implemented, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) an unplanned change takes place, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

as a result of which the AIFM no longer complies with an implementing provision that applies to it, the FCA must take steps to ensure that the AIFM complies with that implementing provision or ceases to market the AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) The FCA may use its powers under section 55J and 55L of the Act (variation or cancellation on initiative of regulator and imposition of requirement by the FCA) or the power applied by regulation 56 in taking the steps mentioned in paragraph (3), but this paragraph does not limit the powers of the FCA. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
