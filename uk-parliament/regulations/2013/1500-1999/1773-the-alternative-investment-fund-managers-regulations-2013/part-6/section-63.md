## 63. Suspension of entitlement to market (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If one of the grounds in paragraph (2) is met, the FCA may suspend any entitlement of an AIFM to market an AIF arising out of a notification under regulation 57, 58 or 59 for a specified period, until the occurrence of a specified event or until specified conditions are complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) The grounds are that it appears to the FCA that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the AIFM, or the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] third country AIF custodian[^key-dc33dcb6a8a91a0fefc294e5a9abbecd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of that AIF, has contravened, or is likely to contravene, an implementing provision that applies to it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the AIFM, or the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] third country AIF custodian *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of that AIF, has in purported compliance with an implementing provision, knowingly or recklessly given the FCA information which is false or misleading in a material particular; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) one or more of the conditions confirmed in the notification as being met is no longer satisfied; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) none of paragraphs (a) to (c) applies, but it is undesirable in the interests of investors or potential investors that the AIF should continue to be marketed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**specified *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means specified by the FCA in a notice given under regulation 64. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
