## 49. Marketing by full-scope (affected by repeal, see note) Gibraltar (affected by repeal, see note) AIFMs of certain AIFs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-e711cda366d63f7a3a4ad22e5bf5e0f4][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-fc3f3347d3403b05b05b50c22ed1e552] A full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] Gibraltar[^key-711e3585f0cba589552b563dbd6b36f5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM may not market in the United Kingdom an AIF that does not fall within regulation 57(1) unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) in the case of marketing to a professional investor, the FCA has received a regulator's notice in relation to the marketing of the AIF in accordance with Schedule 3 to the Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^key-d2ed65baa2f4532aadf81556ac104f51]...; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) in the case of marketing to a retail investor— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) the FCA has received a regulator's notice in relation to the marketing of the AIF in accordance with Schedule 3 to the Act; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the FCA has approved the marketing in accordance with regulation 54 and has not suspended or revoked that approval. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2)[^key-1fd93cc652956d7a869dc4279dedd5d5] References in this regulation to Schedule 3 to the Act are to that Schedule as it applies in relation to Gibraltar AIFMs and Gibraltar AIFs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
