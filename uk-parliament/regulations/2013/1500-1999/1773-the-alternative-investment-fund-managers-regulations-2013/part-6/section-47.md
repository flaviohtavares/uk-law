## 47. Marketing at the initiative of the investor (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations 49 to 51 do not apply to an offering or placement of units or shares of an AIF to an investor made at the initiative of that investor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
