# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773

## 45. References in this Part to an AIFM or an investment firm marketing an AIF (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) an AIFM markets an AIF when the AIFM makes a direct or indirect offering or placement of units or shares of an AIF managed by it to or with an investor domiciled or with a registered office in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the United Kingdom or Gibraltar[^key-5153265168ba3f0d88e1269ee60bd01f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], or when another person makes such an offering or placement at the initiative of, or on behalf of, the AIFM; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) an investment firm markets an AIF when it makes a direct or indirect offering or placement of units or shares of the AIF to or with an investor domiciled or with a registered office in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the United Kingdom or Gibraltar *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] at the initiative of, or on behalf of, the AIFM of that AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
