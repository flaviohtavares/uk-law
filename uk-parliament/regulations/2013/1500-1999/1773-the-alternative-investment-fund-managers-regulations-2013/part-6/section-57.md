# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 57

## 57. Marketing of third country AIFs managed by full-scope UK AIFMs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-5e6505db99a71cea6ab28ca8d17a6454][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An AIF falls within this paragraph if it is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) a third country AIF, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) a UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIF or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] a Gibraltar AIF[^M_F_b5465350-af75-489d-e125-b68e23548f62] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] that is a feeder AIF, the master AIF of which is either managed by a third country AIFM or is a third country AIF, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

managed by an AIFM falling within paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(1A)[^key-958d887391a31e2c6ceadae4d02bd00d] An AIF does not fall within paragraph (1) if it is recognised under section 271A of the Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) An AIFM falls within this paragraph if it is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] Gibraltar AIFM[^key-ade2a7307f3071e6ebb0c8102c04fed4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b].

(3) An AIFM falling within paragraph (2) must give written notification to the FCA before marketing an AIF managed by it that falls within paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) The notification must include a statement confirming that the following conditions are met— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) subject to paragraph (5), the AIFM complies with the requirements of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, these Regulations, any EU tertiary legislation made under the directive which forms part of[^key-05835ef53482e68dc7e84b8aade480db] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] assimilated[^key-4a773f6d2c4ac5b144487e33645e6b02] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] law and rules made by the FCA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b];
- (b) appropriate cooperation arrangements for the purpose of systemic risk oversight and in line with international standards are in place between the FCA (in the case of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the Financial Services Commission of Gibraltar (in the case of a Gibraltar AIFM)[^key-d4d59c45f21d98a8236b6f0743922892] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], and the supervisory authorities of the relevant third country, in order to ensure an efficient exchange of information that enables the FCA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] or the Financial Services Commission of Gibraltar[^key-6b9899c2f146d52f79daff48af992242] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] to carry out its duties in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] these Regulations or the Financial Services (Alternative Investment Fund Managers) Regulations 2013 of Gibraltar[^key-b661f054285668ab800a21b8ca16b9ef] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b];
- (c) the relevant third country is not listed as a Non-Cooperative Country and Territory by the Financial Action Task Force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) The AIFM need not comply with the requirements of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] section 3.11 of the Investment Funds sourcebook, and regulations 29 to 32 of these Regulations[^key-9dbc5397ee3717334460a0b691306366] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], provided that the AIFM— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) ensures that one or more entities, other than the AIFM, are appointed to carry out the duties mentioned in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] rules 3.11.4(2), 3.11.20, 3.11.21, 3.11.23 and 3.11.25 of the Investment Funds sourcebook (a “third country AIF custodian”)[^key-72a913faeee66402d151b6d00e71264d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) informs the FCA about the identity of such entities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(6) In this regulation, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**the relevant third country *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) in the case of a third country AIF, the country where the AIF is established; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) in a case falling within paragraph (1)(b), the country where the master AIF is established. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
