# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 54

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773

## 54. FCA approval for marketing (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM seeking to market an AIF managed by the AIFM that does not fall within regulation 57(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] Gibraltar[^key-387e19a2505c9c397c285460ca1a031b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM, seeking to market to retail investors an AIF managed by the AIFM— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) that does not fall within regulation 57(1); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] where the FCA has not received a regulator's notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] referred to in paragraph 14(1)(b) of Schedule 3 to the Act as that paragraph applies in relation to Gibraltar[^key-18e8343768ff3e0ef7f04fdb99f26d40] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-eba92ddeb3f5a09a128efb9b1084f6be]...

(2) The AIFM must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) apply to the FCA for approval in such manner as the FCA may direct; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) provide such information as the FCA may reasonably require for the purpose of determining the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) The FCA must determine an application by a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM, in so far as that application is for approval to market to professional investors, within 20 working days of receipt of a completed application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) The FCA must determine an application by a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] Gibraltar[^key-043d37ee901b76e08615db57bfaca664] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM, or an application by a full scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM in so far as that application is for approval to market to retail investors— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) if the application relates to an AIF in respect of which the AIFM has made an application for authorisation under section 242 or 261C of the Act or under regulation 12 of the Open-Ended Investment Companies Regulations 2001 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862151], or for recognition under section 272 of the Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) before the end of the period for determining that application for authorisation or recognition; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] if later, within 20 working days of receipt of the completed application under this regulation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) otherwise, within 20 working days of receipt of the completed application under this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) If the FCA approves the application, it must inform— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the AIFM concerned; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b)[^key-a3a67c5b282c59dc667202f024ee8af8] where the AIF concerned is a Gibraltar AIF, the Financial Services Commission of Gibraltar. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(6) If the FCA proposes to refuse the application, it must give written notice to the AIFM concerned, stating the reasons for the proposed refusal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(7) If the FCA decides to refuse the application— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) it must give written notice to the AIFM concerned, informing the AIFM of its right to refer the matter to the Tribunal; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the AIFM may refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(8) The FCA may refuse an application if it appears to the FCA that the AIFM does not or is unlikely to comply with an implementing provision that applies to it, or would apply to it if the application were approved. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
