# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 56

## 56. Suspension or revocation of approval granted to a full-scope (affected by repeal, see note) Gibraltar AIFM (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-2b631edf1d8c5d680551d0835b29873c][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the FCA has approved marketing by a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] Gibraltar AIFM *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] under regulation 54, regulations 62 to 64 apply in relation to that approval as they apply in relation to an entitlement to market an AIF following a notification under regulation 57, 58 or 59, but with the following modifications— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) references to a notification under regulation 57, 58 or 59 are to be read as references to an application under regulation 54; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) references to an entitlement to market are to be read as references to an approval to market; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) references to an Article 36 custodian are to be omitted; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) regulations 62(1)(c) and 63(2)(c) are to be omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
