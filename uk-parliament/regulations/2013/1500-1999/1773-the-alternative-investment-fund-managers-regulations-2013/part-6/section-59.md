# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Marketing (affected by repeal, see note) of AIFs managed by other third country AIFMs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-4a7ef2ff98510fffd5edbe359f3c8db4][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A third country AIFM that is not a small AIFM must give written notification to the FCA before marketing an AIF managed by it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] , except where the AIF is recognised under section 271A of the Act[^key-dfb2cc22d6b5a9a1f8bff291f5b50b8a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] .

(2) Subject to paragraph (4A),[^key-46dfb7da132c2659f80a5868a28bcd16] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the notification must include a statement confirming that the following conditions are met— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the AIFM is the person responsible for complying with the implementing provisions relating to the marketing of the AIF; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the AIFM complies with the requirements of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] sections 3.2, 3.3 and 3.4 of the Investment Funds sourcebook[^key-6b2d09e57b6e8b9a032bb40aa0502a55] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] in so far as such provisions are relevant to the AIFM and the AIF to be marketed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) if applicable, the AIFM complies with Part 5 in relation to the AIF to be marketed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) appropriate cooperation arrangements for the purpose of systemic risk oversight and in line with international standards are in place between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) the FCA and, if applicable, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the Financial Services Commission of Gibraltar[^key-52e4c7b50097ba882e632254a5ea5e9e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the supervisory authorities of the country where the third country AIFM is established and, if applicable, of the third country where the AIF is established, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

  in order to ensure an efficient exchange of information that enables the FCA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] to carry out its duties in accordance with the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, these Regulations, any EU tertiary legislation made under the directive which forms part of[^key-57fa733d79b58eb87d83fbb34bbf72d7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] assimilated[^key-8c3583ad9ba0aa8558d32e34bcac46b9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] law and rules made by the FCA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b];
- (e) the country where the third country AIFM and, if applicable, the third country AIF is established is not listed as a Non-Cooperative Country and Territory by the Financial Action Task Force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4A),[^key-8dc0ee1ccbcade1d2439a0888395bf53] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] during the period specified in paragraph (4), an AIFM that has given a notification under paragraph (1) in respect of an AIF must comply with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the implementing provisions applicable to full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFMs which relate to the provisions of Articles 22 to 24 of the directive in so far as such provisions are relevant to the AIFM and the AIF; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) Part 5 in respect of that AIF (if applicable). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) Subject to paragraph (4A),[^key-5cb7fb421f0e0949ace35c5a1ca19134] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the period specified in this paragraph starts on the date on which the AIFM gives the notification under paragraph (1) and ends— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) if an investor's acquisition of units or shares of the AIF results from marketing that is permitted because of the notification, on the date on which the final such investor disposes of such units or shares; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) if there is no acquisition of units or shares of the AIF resulting from such marketing, on the date on which the AIFM ceases marketing the AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4A)[^key-a7f18cdf6662f26db19b887ad22c1475] Paragraphs (2)(a) to (c), (3) and (4) do not apply to any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIF[^key-fba6c0582806db88ca643823960412d0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] which has been declared to be a recognised scheme under section 272 of the Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
