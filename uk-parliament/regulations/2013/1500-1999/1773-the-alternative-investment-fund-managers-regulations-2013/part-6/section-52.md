# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 52

## 52. Contravention by unauthorised person (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**unlawful marketing *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means the marketing of an AIF— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) by an AIFM or an investment firm that is not an authorised person in contravention of regulation 49, 50 or 51; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) by an AIFM that is not an authorised person in contravention of a provision of the EuSEF Regulation or the EuVECA Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c)[^key-80e137ddc412ff2a21ef11d8f42abb6c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), section 25 of the Act (contravention of section 21) applies to unlawful marketing as it applies to the contravention of section 21(1) of the Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) When applied by paragraph (2), the reference in section 25(1)(a) to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months is to be read as a reference to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) Section 168 of the Act (appointment of persons to carry out investigations in particular cases) applies as if the reference at subsection (2)(c) to a contravention of section 21 of the Act included reference to unlawful marketing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) Section 30 of the Act (enforceability of agreements resulting from unlawful communications) applies in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) controlled agreements entered into in consequence of unlawful marketing, as it applies in relation to controlled agreements entered into in consequence of an unlawful communication; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the exercise of rights conferred by a controlled investment in consequence of unlawful marketing, as it applies in relation to the exercise of such rights in consequence of an unlawful communication. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
