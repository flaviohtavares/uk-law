## 53. Contravention by authorised person (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**unlawful marketing *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means the marketing of an AIF— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) by an authorised person that is an AIFM or an investment firm in contravention of regulation 49, 50 or 51; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) by an authorised person that is an AIFM in contravention of a provision of the EuSEF Regulation or the EuVECA Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c)[^c-1a9e574ff53b61dba4d111ecae253cbe] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Unlawful marketing is actionable at the suit of a private person who suffers loss as a result of such marketing, subject to the defences and other incidents applying to actions for breach of statutory duty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) Section 168 of the Act (appointment of persons to carry out investigations in particular cases) applies as if the reference at subsection (2)(c) to a contravention of section 238 of the Act included reference to unlawful marketing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
