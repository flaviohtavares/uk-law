# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 69A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69A. Transfer of the directive functions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-67de8f8e285e1fb10c34b91e04706a5f][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Treasury may make regulations by statutory instrument for the purposes specified in Part 1 of Schedule A1 to these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) A statutory instrument which contains regulations under paragraph (1) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) The FCA may make rules for purposes specified in Part 2 of Schedule A1 to these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) Rules made under paragraph (3) are to be treated for the purposes of the Act as if they were made under section 137A of the Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) The FCA may make technical standards for the purposes specified in Part 3 of Schedule A1 to these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
