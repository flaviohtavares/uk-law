# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Determination of applications

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA must determine an application for entry on the register of small registered UK AIFMs before the end of the period of three months beginning with the date on which it receives the completed application.

(1A)[^c-5012ec741114459d644b8d61662320c9] Paragraph (1) does not apply in relation to an application for registration as a SEF Manager[^c-2a2e80c9417c896afd802d5e6e80ff3a] or a RVECA Manager[^c-92485b2988c9b132c793124a27774329] (in respect of which see Article 15(4) of the EuSEF Regulation or Article 14(4) of the EuVECA Regulation).

(2) The FCA may determine an incomplete application if it considers it appropriate to do so; and it must in any event determine such an application within six months beginning with the date on which it first receives the application.

(3) If the FCA decides to enter an AIFM on the register, it must give written notice of its determination to the applicant.
