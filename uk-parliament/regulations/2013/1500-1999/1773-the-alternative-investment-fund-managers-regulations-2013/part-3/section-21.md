## 21. Disclosure obligations of small registered (affected by repeal, see note) UK (affected by repeal, see note) AIFMs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9b4f0dde6756033a7c7b3753f7fcd987][^key-91c8bda53601fd18e6b00493b0569efc][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM must provide the FCA with such information as the FCA may direct under paragraph (2) or (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) The FCA may direct that a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM must provide information on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the main instruments in which the AIFM trades, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the principal exposures and most important concentrations of the AIFs that it manages, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

in order to enable the FCA to monitor systemic risk effectively. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) The FCA may direct that a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM must provide information in relation to its compliance with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the implementing provisions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the EuSEF Regulation, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) the EuVECA Regulation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

in order to enable the FCA to discharge its supervisory obligations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) Information provided under paragraph (1) must be given at such times and in such manner, and verified in such manner, as the FCA may direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
