## 15. Small registered AIFMs ceasing to meet the requirements for registration (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM ceases to meet any of the conditions in regulation 10(2)(a)(i), (iii) or (iv) or (b)(i) or (c), (3)(a)(i), (b), (c) or (d)(i), or (4)(a) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c-8851a5139fa6e5516f0fce77ed33c015]..., it must inform the FCA immediately. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) If proceedings begin against an individual responsible for the operation or management of a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM registered pursuant to the conditions in regulation 10(2) for a disqualification order or disqualification undertaking under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986, the AIFM must inform the FCA immediately. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) Paragraph (4) applies if a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM registered pursuant to the conditions in regulation 10(2) or (3)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) does not comply with the requirement at Article 4.2 of the Commission Delegated Regulation (exceeding the threshold: situation not temporary) to apply for a Part 4A permission to carry on the regulated activity of managing an AIF; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) does not appoint a person with such permission to act as AIFM of the AIF within the period mentioned in Article 4.2 of that Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) The AIFM's registration, to the extent that it was granted pursuant to the conditions in regulation 10(2) or (3), is treated as revoked upon the expiry of the period mentioned in Article 4.2 of the Commission Delegated Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
