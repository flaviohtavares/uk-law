## 20. Procedure on suspension (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The suspension of the registration of a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM takes effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) immediately, if the notice given under paragraph (3) states that that is the case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) on such date as may be specified in the notice; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) if no date is specified in the notice, when the matter to which it relates is no longer open to review. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) A suspension may be expressed to take effect immediately or on a specified date only if the FCA, having regard to the ground on which it is exercising its power under regulation 19, considers that it is necessary for the suspension to take effect immediately or on that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) If the FCA proposes to suspend a registration, or suspends a registration with immediate effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) it must give written notice to the AIFM; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the AIFM may refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) A notice given under paragraph (3)(a) must state— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the details of the suspension; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) when the suspension takes effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) the FCA's reasons for imposing the suspension and for its determination as to when the suspension takes effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) that the AIFM to whom it is given may make representations to the FCA within such period as may be specified in it (whether or not it has referred the matter to the Tribunal); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (e) that the AIFM has the right to refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) The FCA may extend the period allowed under the notice for making representations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(6) Having considered any representations made by the AIFM to whom the notice was given— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the FCA must give written notice to the AIFM as to whether it decides— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) to make the suspension in the way proposed (or, if the suspension has already been imposed, not to revoke the suspension), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] to make the suspension in a way other than that proposed (or, if the suspension has already been imposed, to amend the suspension), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] not to make the suspension (or, if the suspension has already been imposed, to revoke the suspension); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) unless the FCA decides not to make, or to revoke, the suspension, the AIFM may refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(7) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c), section 391(8) of the Act (publication) applies as if a notice under paragraph (3)(a) or (6)(a) were a supervisory notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
