## 17. Grounds for revocation of registration (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5507c00ecc2e38be14d6d0f82ca31c99][^c-bdd2601b134106c770b0de9673adeb43][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the FCA may revoke the registration of a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM, including its registration under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] Article 15[^c-0e37e0ceae98f900b0ce5ab4c368bab8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of the EuSEF Regulation or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] Article 14[^c-95c5966a5f746008dd455fc70c4ed5cc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of the EuVECA Regulation, if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the AIFM does not meet a condition in regulation 10(2), (3) or, as the case may be, (4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b](a) or (b)[^c-afcb5fa14b15b32a69bef84439d42f4c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b];
- (b) the AIFM has contravened an implementing provision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-1bbadedfa1026b383c7494f197e1ac62]... that applies to it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) the AIFM applies for or consents to the revocation of its registration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) the AIFM is wound up; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (e) a fee due in respect of the registration has not been paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) If an AIFM applies to the FCA for permission in accordance with regulation 16(2)(b), the FCA may not revoke the registration of the AIFM on the ground that the AIFM is not a small AIFM until the FCA has determined the AIFM's application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
