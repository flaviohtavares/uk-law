## 19. Grounds for suspension of registration (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d06f7a98b6df3810b0046dee8f40b67a][^c-1f13741ad048f3a974922a0a437ba3fe][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If one of the grounds in paragraph (2) is met, the FCA may suspend the registration of a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM for a specified period, until the occurrence of a specified event, or until specified conditions are complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) The grounds are that it appears to the FCA that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the AIFM does not meet a condition in regulation 10(2), (3) or, as the case may be, (4); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the AIFM has contravened, or is likely to contravene, an implementing provision, or a requirement imposed by the EuSEF Regulation or the EuVECA Regulation, that applies to it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**specified *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means specified by the FCA in a notice given under regulation 20. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
