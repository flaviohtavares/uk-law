## 23. Application of FCA rules to small authorised (affected by repeal, see note) UK (affected by repeal, see note) AIFMs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Notwithstanding the powers of the FCA under the Act, the FCA may not make a rule of a kind specified for the purposes of this regulation to the extent that such rule applies to a small authorised *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) A rule of a kind specified for the purposes of this regulation that was made by the FCA before 22nd July 2013 does not apply to a small authorised *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), a rule is of a kind specified for the purposes of this regulation if it— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) is made by the FCA for the purposes of implementing the directive; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) has the same effect as a provision in the Commission Delegated Regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) A rule is not of a kind specified for the purposes of this regulation to the extent that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) it is an implementing provision relating to Article 3 of the directive; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) it applied to the AIFM in respect of the management of an AIF immediately before 22nd July 2013, or would so have applied if the AIFM had managed the AIF at that time; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) it has the same effect as a rule which falls within sub-paragraph (b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) This regulation ceases to have effect on 22nd July 2015. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
