## 18. Procedure on revocation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the FCA proposes to revoke the registration of a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM on the grounds mentioned in regulation 17(1)(a) or (b) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] , or under Article 22 of the EuSEF Regulation or Article 21 of the EuVECA Regulation[^c-4eb6472233231fe941fb92ff9120f067] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], the FCA must give the AIFM a warning notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) If the FCA decides to revoke the registration of a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM on *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] such grounds or under such an Article[^c-6e1762c921e7d2dd09a6b0874bf0d061] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]—

- (a) the FCA must give the AIFM a decision notice, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the AIFM may refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
