## 67. Use of information by FCA (affected by repeal, see note) ...[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-ffc8183391951b51497717d622af8f76]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA must use the information it gathers under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] rules 3.4.2 to 3.4.6C of the Investment Funds sourcebook and Articles 110 and 111 of the Commission Delegated Regulation[^c-3876a2752246eb63353e7da3c686ae78] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] in respect of AIFMs it supervises for the purposes of identifying the extent to which the use of leverage contributes to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the build up of systemic risk in the financial system; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the risks of disorderly markets; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) the risks to the long-term growth of the economy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
