## 68. Limits on leverage (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) assess the risks that the use of leverage by full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFMs with respect to the AIFs managed by them could entail; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) use the measures in paragraph (2), if they are necessary in order to ensure the stability and integrity of the financial system, to limit the extent to which the use of leverage by a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM with respect to the AIFs managed by it contributes to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) the build up of systemic risk in the financial system; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the risks of disorderly markets. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) The measures are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) imposing limits on the level of leverage that such an AIFM may employ; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) imposing other restrictions on the management of such an AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
