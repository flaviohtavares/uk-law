# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 81

## 81. Amendments to secondary legislation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 2 (which contains amendments to secondary legislation) has effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) Paragraph (3) has effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) for the purpose of the FCA and PRA making rules, giving directions and imposing requirements, and for the purpose of the FCA giving guidance, immediately after Schedule 2 has effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) for all other purposes, on the date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] on which regulation 3 of and Schedule 1 to the Alternative Investment Fund Managers (Amendment) Regulations 2013 come into force[^key-383c5aa129f3d586b40b47bd7dfbbc34] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b].

(3) In paragraph 11 of Schedule 8 to the Regulated Activities Order (persons excluded from regulated activity of managing an AIF) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862351], at the end insert *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] “ , that is registered in the United Kingdom in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] Part 3 of these Regulations[^key-9cd9b2148acccb4136f304c548607c1c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] ”.
