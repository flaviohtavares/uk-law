# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 43

## 43. Asset stripping (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) When an AIF acquires control of a non‑listed company or an issuer, for a period of 24 months following the acquisition of control, the AIFM managing the AIF— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) must not facilitate, support or instruct any distribution, capital reduction, share redemption or acquisition by the company or issuer of its own shares; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) in so far as the AIFM is authorised to vote on behalf of the AIF at the meetings of the governing body of the company or issuer, must not vote in favour of a distribution, capital reduction, share redemption or acquisition by the company or issuer of its own shares; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) in any event must use its best efforts to prevent distributions, capital reductions, share redemptions or the acquisition by the company or issuer of its own shares. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) In paragraph (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**distribution *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means a distribution to shareholders, including a payment of dividends and of interest relating to shares— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) made when on the closing date of the last financial year the net assets as set out in the company or issuer's annual accounts are, or following such a distribution would become, lower than the amount of the subscribed capital plus those reserves which may be not distributed under the law or the statutes, on the understanding that where the uncalled part of the subscribed capital is not included in the assets shown in the balance sheet, this amount must be deducted from the amount of subscribed capital; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the amount of which would exceed the amount of the profits at the end of the last financial year plus any profits brought forward and sums drawn from reserves available for this purpose, less any losses brought forward and sums placed to reserve in accordance with the law or the statutes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) In paragraph (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**capital reduction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” does not include a reduction in subscribed capital, the purpose of which is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) to offset losses incurred; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) to include sums of money in a non-distributable reserve, provided that, following that operation, the amount of such reserve is not more than 10% of the reduced subscribed capital. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) In paragraph (1) “share redemption” or “acquisition by the company or issuer of its own shares”— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) refers to a redemption or acquisition of shares, including shares previously acquired by the company or issuer and held by it, and shares acquired by a person acting in that person's own name but on the company or issuer's behalf, that would have the effect of reducing the net assets below the amount mentioned in paragraph (2)(a); but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) does not include an acquisition of shares in the circumstances described in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] section 659(2)(b) or (c) of the Companies Act 2006, or an acquisition permitted by Chapter 4 of Part 18 of that Act[^key-0ee74a06d3aef6b5f64440c1bfb3ac2a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] .

(5) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- “[^key-4ecd8146ee427b25eef07acf6b44ddf3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**law *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means the law of the United Kingdom, or of any part of the United Kingdom *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- [^key-9a7a50054b983ca72e1fd74080e4adac]...
- “**statutes *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means the instruments of incorporation of the company or issuer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
