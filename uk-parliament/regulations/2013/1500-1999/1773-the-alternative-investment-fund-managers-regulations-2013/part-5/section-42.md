# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773

## 42. Annual report of AIFs exercising control of non-listed companies (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) When an AIF acquires control of a non‑listed company, the AIFM managing the AIF must include in the AIF's annual report, prepared in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] rules 3.3.2 to 3.3.8 of the Investment Funds sourcebook[^key-82d6eade486d67111821a48c99c890d4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], the information in paragraph (3) relating to the non-listed company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2)[^key-04781e06aa8afc48279c6ef9bd1160f5] The duty in paragraph (1) does not arise if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the non-listed company has its registered office in the United Kingdom and is required to draw up an annual report under the law applicable in the United Kingdom; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the AIFM ensures that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) the annual report of the non-listed company contains the information in paragraph (3); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the report is made available by the board of directors of the company to the employees' representatives or, where there are none, to the employees themselves within the period in which the annual report must be drawn up. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) The information is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) a fair review of the development of the company's business representing the situation at the end of the period covered by the annual report; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) any important events that have occurred since the end of the financial year of the AIF; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) the company's likely future development; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) in relation to the company's acquisition or disposal of its own shares— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) the reasons for acquisitions made during the financial year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the number and nominal value or, in the absence of a nominal value, the accountable par of the shares acquired and disposed of during the financial year and the proportion of the subscribed capital which they represent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] in the case of acquisition or disposal for a value, the consideration for the shares; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the number and nominal value or, in the absence of a nominal value, the accountable par of all the shares acquired and held by the company and the proportion of the subscribed capital which they represent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) If the information in paragraph (3) is included in the company's annual report, the AIFM must make the information available to the investors of the AIF in so far as already available within six months following the end of the financial year of the AIF, and in any event, no later than the date on which the annual report of the company must be drawn up *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^key-e5562f840bd41bcc1134c931ab15c8b8]...[^c21862131].

(5) If the information in paragraph (3) is included in the AIF's annual report, the AIFM must request and use its best efforts to ensure that the board of directors of the non‑listed company makes that information available to employees' representatives of the company or, where there are none, to the employees themselves, no later than six months following the end of the financial year of the AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
