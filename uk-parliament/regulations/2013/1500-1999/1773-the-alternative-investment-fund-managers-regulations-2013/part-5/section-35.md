# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 35

## 35. Ways of acquiring control or shares (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies where control is acquired of a non-listed company or issuer in one of the following ways— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) one AIF acquires control individually; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) two or more AIFs, managed by the same AIFM, acquire control jointly on the basis of an agreement aimed at acquiring such control; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) two or more AIFs, managed by two or more AIFMs, acquire control jointly on the basis of an agreement aimed at acquiring such control. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) This Part also applies where an AIF acquires, holds or disposes of shares of a non-listed company (but control is not acquired). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) This Part does not apply where the non-listed company or issuer is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) a small or medium-sized enterprise within the meaning of Article 2(1) of the Annex to Commission Recommendation 2003/361/ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of 6 May 2003 concerning the definition of micro, small and medium-sized enterprises *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862091]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) a special purpose vehicle with the purpose of purchasing, holding or administering real estate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
