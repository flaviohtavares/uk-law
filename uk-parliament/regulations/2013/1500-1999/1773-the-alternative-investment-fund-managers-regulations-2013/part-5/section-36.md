# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 36

## 36. Meaning of “control” and calculation of voting rights (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Part, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**control *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) for a non-listed company, holding more than 50% of the voting rights of the company; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b)[^c-46a1329663859f17ad9e3cfe502ab153] for an issuer, holding the percentage of voting rights that confers control in the United Kingdom, calculated in accordance with rules made by the Panel on Takeovers and Mergers under the Companies Act 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) When calculating the percentage of voting rights for the purposes of this Part, if paragraph (1)(b) does not apply, in addition to the voting rights held directly by the AIF, the voting rights of the following are included— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) an undertaking controlled by the AIF; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) a person acting in their own name but on behalf of the AIF or on behalf of an undertaking controlled by the AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) For the purposes of this Part, if paragraph (1)(b) does not apply, voting rights are calculated on the basis of all the shares to which voting rights are attached even if the exercise of those rights is suspended. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
