# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. FCA powers in relation to this Part (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM that contravenes any provision of this Part is to be treated as having contravened rules made under section 137A of the Act (FCA's general rule-making power). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) Information to be provided to the FCA under regulation 38(1) or (2) or 40(4), or made available to the FCA under regulation 39(1), must be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) provided or made available in such manner as the FCA may direct; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) accompanied by such further information as the FCA may direct in order to verify compliance with requirements under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) The FCA may give guidance consisting of such information and advice as it considers appropriate with respect to this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) Before giving guidance with respect to this Part, the FCA must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) publish— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) a draft of the proposed guidance in the way appearing to the FCA to be best calculated to bring it to the attention of the public; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] a notice that representations about the proposals may be made to the FCA within a specified time; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) have regard to any representations made in accordance with that notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) Paragraph (4) does not apply if the FCA considers that the delay caused by those procedures would be prejudicial to the interests of a non-listed company or issuer, or the shareholders or employees of such a company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
