# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 37

## 37. Confidential information (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) employees of a non-listed company or issuer, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) representatives of the employees, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) experts who assist the employees or representatives, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

who receive information in accordance with this Part, or corresponding provisions adopted in an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] EEA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^key-309a432dd4fb28ea102cea9c7a87037a]... implementing Articles 26 to 30 of the directive (obligations for AIFMs managing AIFs which acquire control of non-listed companies and issuers). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) If the information mentioned in paragraph (1) is provided in confidence— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) regulation 25 of the Information and Consultation of Employees Regulations 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862111] (breach of statutory duty), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) regulation 25 of the Information and Consultation of Employees Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862121] (breach of statutory duty), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

applies to the information as it applies to information or documents given in confidence by an employer pursuant to the employer's obligations under those Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
