# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 39

## 39. Disclosure in case of acquisition of control (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) When an AIF acquires control of a non‑listed company or an issuer, the AIFM managing the AIF must make available the information in paragraph (2) to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the company or issuer; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the shareholders of the company or issuer of which the identities and addresses are available to the AIFM or can be made available by the company or issuer or through a register to which the AIFM has or can obtain access; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) the FCA. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) The information is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the identity of the AIFM which either individually or in agreement with other AIFMs manages the AIF or AIFs that have acquired control; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the policy for preventing and managing conflicts of interest, in particular between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) the AIFMs or the AIFs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the company or issuer; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) the specific safeguards to ensure that any agreement between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) the AIFMs or the AIFs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the company or issuer, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

  is concluded at arm's length; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) the policy for external and internal communication relating to the company or issuer, in particular as regards employees of the company or issuer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) The AIFM must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) in its notification to the company or issuer, request the board of directors of the company to give the employees' representatives or, where there are none, the employees themselves, without undue delay the information in paragraph (2); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) use its best efforts to ensure that the board of directors complies with its request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
