# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 23H

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23H. Procedure in relation to the refusal of an application for MMF authorisation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-3b527e917c2ee83def9be4a1b51a0f6d][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the FCA proposes to refuse an application made in respect of an AIF for authorisation as an MMF under Article 5 of the MMF Regulation, it must give the applicant a warning notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) If the FCA decides to refuse an application— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) it must give the applicant a decision notice; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the applicant may refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
