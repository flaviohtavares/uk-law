# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 23G

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773

## 23G. Manner of application for MMF authorisation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3b527e917c2ee83def9be4a1b51a0f6d][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An application for an AIF to be authorised as an MMF under Article 5 of the MMF Regulation must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) be made in such a manner as the FCA may direct; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) contain or be accompanied by such information as the FCA may reasonably require for the purpose of determining the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) At any time after receiving an application and before determining it, the FCA may require the applicant to provide it with such further information as it considers necessary to enable it to determine the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) Different directions may be given, and different requirements imposed, in relation to different applications or categories of application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) The FCA may require an applicant to provide information which it is required to give under paragraph (1)(b) or paragraph (2) in such form, or to verify it in such manner, as the FCA may specify. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
