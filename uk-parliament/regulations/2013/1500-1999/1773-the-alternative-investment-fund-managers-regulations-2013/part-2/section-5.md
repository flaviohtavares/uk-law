# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773

## 5. Applications for Part 4A permission (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) applies to a regulator for permission under Part 4A of the Act to carry on the regulated activity of managing an AIF; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) upon being given such permission would not be a small authorised *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) Where the applicant has a Part 4A permission pursuant to which it may carry on the regulated activity of managing a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UCITS *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], the regulator may not require the applicant to provide information or documents which the applicant provided when applying for that Part 4A permission if such information or documents remain up to date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) The regulator must not give the Part 4A permission unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the applicant would be an AIFM and would be the only AIFM of each AIF it managed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the regulator is satisfied that the applicant will comply with the implementing provisions applicable to a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) the applicant has sufficient initial capital and own funds in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] rule 11.2.1 and section 11.3 of the Interim Prudential sourcebook for Investment Businesses[^c-4dec092029682c1b95ad8eb54bfcd058] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) the shareholders or members of the AIFM that have qualifying holdings are suitable taking into account the need to ensure the sound and prudent management of the AIFM. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) The regulator must determine the application before the end of the period of three months beginning with the date on which it received the complete application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) The regulator may extend the period mentioned in paragraph (4) for an additional period of three months where it considers it necessary due to the specific circumstances of the case, and where it has notified the applicant accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(6) Where the application is made to the PRA and cannot be determined by the PRA without the consent of the FCA, the FCA's decision must also be made within the period required by sub-paragraph (4), as extended under sub-paragraph (5) where applicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(7) If the regulator does not determine the application before the end of the period of six months beginning with the date on which it received the complete application, the applicant may refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(8) An application is complete for the purposes of paragraph (4) or (7) if it contains— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) information on the persons effectively conducting the business of the applicant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) information on the identities of the applicant's shareholders or members, whether direct or indirect, that have qualifying holdings and on the amount of those holdings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c)[^c-ff3562694ce0bf6f5364008ed8b8b7f7] a programme of activity setting out the organisational structure of the applicant, including information on how the applicant intends to comply with its obligations under the following provisions, so far as they apply to an AIFM— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] rules made by the FCA; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] Commission Delegated Regulation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) information on the remuneration policies and practices of the applicant that have been or will be adopted pursuant to implementation provisions relating to Article 13 of the directive; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (e) information about the investment strategies, including the types of underlying funds if the AIF is a fund of funds, and the applicant's policy as regards the use of leverage, and the risk profiles and other characteristics of the AIFs the applicant manages or intends to manage, including information about the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c-9cb7073cf25943ca3bfce173c96ab8e3]... third countries in which AIFs are established or are expected to be established; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (f) information on where the master AIF is established if an AIF that the applicant manages or intends to manage is a feeder AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(9) Paragraph (10) applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) any of the information required under implementing provisions relating to Articles 7.2(e) and 7.3(c) to 7.3(e) of the directive (information to be provided in application for authorisation) was missing from the application; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the Part 4A permission is given less than one month after the applicant supplied the missing information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(10) The Part 4A permission is to be treated as given one month after the applicant submitted the missing information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
