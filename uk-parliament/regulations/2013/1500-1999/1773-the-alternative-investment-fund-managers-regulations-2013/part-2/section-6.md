## 6. Supplementary provisions about AIFMs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM that is an external AIFM is unable to ensure compliance by an AIF it manages, or by another entity on the AIF's behalf, with an implementing provision for which the AIF is responsible, the AIFM must immediately inform— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the FCA, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c-71bbf15d121eeeadb56f4d10ce410ff3]...
- (b)[^c-e481fe032ba0525f332537e5f09790fa] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

about the non-compliance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) The FCA must require the AIFM to take steps to remedy the situation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) If the non-compliance persists despite the steps mentioned in paragraph (2) being taken, the FCA must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) require the AIFM to cease acting as manager of that AIF *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] ; and[^c-91c6ee5816130741bc691afa4e2abfd2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) require the AIFM to stop marketing the AIF; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c-c23c25821e5fc1fe7065e03f5d67ba3d]...
- (c)[^c-ed7cc8ea4e656187269b5791432b7714] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The FCA may use its powers under section 55J and 55L of the Act (variation or cancellation on initiative of regulator and imposition of requirement by the FCA) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862041] to impose the requirements mentioned in paragraphs (2) and (3), but this paragraph does not limit the powers of the FCA. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
