# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773

## 3. Meaning of “AIF” (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) “**AIF *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means a collective investment undertaking, including investment compartments of such an undertaking, which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) raises capital from a number of investors, with a view to investing it in accordance with a defined investment policy for the benefit of these investors; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b)[^c-47e94f0cc953c1ea614b1554108d5418] is not a UK UCITS; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) An AIF may be open-ended or closed-ended, and constituted in any legal form, including under a contract, by means of a trust or under statute. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) None of the following entities is an AIF— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) an institution for occupational retirement provision which falls within the scope of Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [2003/41/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2003/41)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 3 June 2003 on the activities and supervision of institutions for occupational retirement provision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862021];
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d1f99a2a267a0acdb1ef41bdb252a8d2][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] an occupational pension scheme, within the meaning of section 1(1) (categories of pension schemes) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) a holding company; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) an employee participation scheme or employee savings scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) a securitisation special purpose entity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
