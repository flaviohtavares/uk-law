# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Meaning of “AIFM”, “managing an AIF”, “external AIFM” and “internal AIFM” (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) “**AIFM *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means a legal person, the regular business of which is managing one or more AIFs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) Managing an AIF means performing at least risk management or portfolio management for the AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) The AIFM of an AIF may be either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) another person appointed by or on behalf of the AIF and which through that appointment is responsible for managing the AIF (“external AIFM”); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) where the legal form of the AIF permits internal management and where the AIF's governing body chooses not to appoint an external AIFM, the AIF itself (“internal AIFM”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) None of the following entities is an AIFM— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) an institution for occupational retirement provision which falls within the scope of Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [2003/41/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2003/41)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 3 June 2003 on the activities and supervision of institutions for occupational retirement provision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862031], including, where applicable, the authorised entities responsible for managing such institutions and acting on their behalf referred to in Article 2.1 of that directive, or the investment managers appointed pursuant to Article 19.1 of that directive, in so far as they do not manage AIFs; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0aec93003a0e4e03db3a8ca99984b1b3][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] an occupational pension scheme, within the meaning of section 1(1) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993, including its trustees and managers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the European Central Bank, the European Investment Bank, the European Investment Fund, a bilateral development bank, the World Bank, the International Monetary Fund, any other supranational institution or similar international organisation, or a European Development Finance Institution, in the event that such institution or organisation manages AIFs and in so far as those AIFs act in the public interest; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) a national central bank; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) a national, regional or local government or body or other institution which manages funds supporting social security and pension systems; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (e) a holding company; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (f) an employee participation scheme or employee savings scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (g) a securitisation special purpose entity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
