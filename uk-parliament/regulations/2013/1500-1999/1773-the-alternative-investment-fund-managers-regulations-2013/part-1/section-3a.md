# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 3A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773

## 3A. Arrangements not constituting an AIF: qualifying stablecoin arrangements (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-993cc68284cf87d076b93d391c92816f][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Arrangements do not constitute an AIF where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) they are arrangements under which money or an asset is held for the stabilisation of a qualifying stablecoin, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) both the conditions in paragraph (2)(a) and (b) are met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) The conditions referred to in paragraph (1)(b) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) that the issuer of the qualifying stablecoin does not pay, or arrange for another to pay, interest or yield arising from money or an asset held for the stabilisation of the qualifying stablecoin, or the benefits accruing from such to a holder; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) that under normal conditions, the qualifying stablecoin offers a holder a right to redeem the stablecoin with the issuer at the same value, in the currency the stablecoin referenced at issue (excluding any fees that may be payable on redemption). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) For the purposes of this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) “**qualifying stablecoin *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” has the meaning given by article 88G of the Regulated Activities Order (qualifying stablecoin); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) money or an asset is held for the stabilisation of a qualifying stablecoin if it is held for the purpose mentioned in article 88G(2)(b) of the Regulated Activities Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
