# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 78C

## 78C. Period during which regulation 78A(1) is to apply (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1aeb64d700e16d69c7c3934318daad59][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The period referred to in regulation 78A(1) (“the relevant period”) ends— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) after three years beginning with the day on which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day[^c-b1b05752e1daeda72def73732126fcee] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] occurs; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) if paragraph (4) applies, the day determined in accordance with paragraph (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) Paragraph (4) applies where a person has given written notification under regulation 57 (subject to paragraph (3)), 58 or 59— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) during such period, ending no later than the end of the period of two years beginning with the day on which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] occurs (“the two year period”), as the FCA may direct, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) if the FCA does not direct such a period, before the end of the two year period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) If an AIF was marketed in the United Kingdom by an EEA AIFM before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] under regulation 57, the AIFM must give notice to the FCA for the purposes of paragraph (2) under regulation 58 or 59. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) Where this paragraph applies, the relevant period ends with the day after the day on which notice is given by the person concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) If the FCA exercises the power to direct a period during which notice may be given, the FCA must direct the same period in relation to all persons to whom the power relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(6) Subject to paragraph (5), the power to give a direction under this regulation includes the power— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) to give different directions to different persons or categories of person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) to vary or revoke a previous direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
