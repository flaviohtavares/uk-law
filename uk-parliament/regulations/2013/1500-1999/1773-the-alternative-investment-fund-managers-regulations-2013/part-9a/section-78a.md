## 78A. Temporary marketing permissions: AIFs etc (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1aeb64d700e16d69c7c3934318daad59][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If this regulation applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) an AIF, a EuVECA or a EuSEF (a “relevant fund”) may be marketed in the United Kingdom on the same terms and subject to the same conditions as the relevant fund was, or could have been, marketed in the United Kingdom before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day[^key-4338c3058a1efe3a4534ff158d12b472] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] until the end of the period determined in accordance with regulation 78C; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) an AIFM which is authorised (in accordance with Article 6.1 of the alternative investment fund managers directive) by its home state regulator (“an EEA AIFM”) may continue to market a UK AIF in the United Kingdom on the same terms and subject to the same conditions as it was able to do before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] until the end of the period determined in accordance with regulation 78C. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) This regulation applies in relation to AIFs where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) an EEA AIFM satisfied the conditions entitling it to market an EEA AIF in the United Kingdom before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] under regulation 49; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the entitlement to market that AIF has not been suspended or revoked before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b];
- (c) the AIFM of the AIF has, before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], notified the FCA that it wishes the relevant fund to have temporary permission to be marketed in the United Kingdom after *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] under paragraph (1); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) the AIFM is an authorised person, or is authorised or registered as an AIFM in a Member State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) This regulation applies in relation to EuVECAs where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the EuVECA is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) registered under Article 14a of the EuVECA Regulation (as it applies in the European Union) in a Member State, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] managed by a small registered UK AIFM or a EuVECA Manager; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the FCA has, before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], received a notification under Article 16 of the EuVECA Regulation that the United Kingdom has been added to the list of Member States in which the manager intends to market the EuVECA; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) the AIFM or EuVECA Manager of the EuVECA— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) has not been subject to any sanctions under Article 18 of the EuVECA Regulation, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] has notified the FCA that it wishes the relevant fund to have temporary permission to be marketed in the United Kingdom after *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) This regulation applies in relation to EuSEFs where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the EuSEF is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) registered under Article 15a of the EuSEF Regulation (as it applies in the European Union), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] managed by a small registered UK AIFM or a EuSEF Manager; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the FCA has, before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], received a notification under Article 17 of the EuSEF Regulation that the United Kingdom has been added to the list of Member States in which the manager intends to market the EuSEF; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) the AIFM or EuSEF Manager marketing the relevant fund— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) has not been subject to any sanctions under Article 19 of the EuSEF Regulation, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] has notified the FCA that it wishes the relevant fund to have temporary permission to be marketed in the United Kingdom after *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) This regulation applies in relation to EEA AIFMs marketing UK AIFs where the EEA AIFM— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) satisfied the conditions entitling it to market the UK AIF in the United Kingdom before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] under regulation 49; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) is authorised or registered as an AIFM in a Member State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) has notified the FCA that it wishes to have temporary permission to market the UK AIF in the United Kingdom after *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(6) For the purposes of paragraphs (2)(c), (3)(c)(ii), (4)(c)(ii) and (5)(c), the notification must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) be made in such manner, and during such period, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) contain, or be accompanied by, such information, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

as the FCA may direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(7) The power to give a direction under this regulation includes the power— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) to give different directions to different persons or categories of person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) to vary or revoke a previous direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
