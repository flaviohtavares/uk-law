# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 78B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773

## 78B. Deemed authorisation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1aeb64d700e16d69c7c3934318daad59][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) During the period referred to in regulation 78A(1), an EEA AIFM (as defined by regulation 78A(1)(b)) is to be treated as if it is an authorised person for the purposes of the Act if it satisfies the conditions in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) An EEA AIFM satisfies the conditions in this paragraph if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) immediately before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day[^key-6e971178b21a5968974a1cb409c3bcce] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the EEA AIFM— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (i) was marketing an EEA AIF or a UK AIF in the United Kingdom in reliance on its rights under Article 32 of the directive; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] was authorised to carry on a regulated activity in the United Kingdom by virtue of section 31(1)(b) or (c) (but not section 31(1)(a)) of the Act; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) after *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] IP completion day *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], it is permitted to market that EEA AIF under regulation 78A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
