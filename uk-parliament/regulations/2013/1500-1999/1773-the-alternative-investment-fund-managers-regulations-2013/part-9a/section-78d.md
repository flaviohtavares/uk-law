# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 78D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78D. FCA powers (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1aeb64d700e16d69c7c3934318daad59][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The FCA’s powers under regulations 62, 63 and 64 are exercisable in relation to an AIF, EuVECA or EuSEF marketed under regulation 78A as they are in relation to an entitlement to market an AIF following a notification under regulation 57, 58 or 59, but with the modification that references in those regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) to a notification under regulation 57, 58 or 59 are to be read as references to notification under regulation 78A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) to an AIF are to be read as including references to a EuVECA and EuSEF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
