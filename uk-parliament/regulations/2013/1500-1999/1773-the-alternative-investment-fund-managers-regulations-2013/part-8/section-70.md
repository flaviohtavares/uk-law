## 70. Application of procedural provisions of the Act (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Part 9 of the Act applies in the case of a matter referred to the Tribunal under these Regulations as it applies in the case of a matter referred to the Tribunal under the Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) Part 26 of the Act applies to warning notices and decision notices given under these Regulations as it applies to such notices given under the Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
