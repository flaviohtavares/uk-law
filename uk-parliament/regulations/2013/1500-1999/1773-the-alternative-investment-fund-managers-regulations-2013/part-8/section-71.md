# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 71

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773

## 71. Application of provisions of the Act to unauthorised AIFMs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following provisions of the Act apply in respect of an unauthorised AIFM as they apply in respect of an authorised person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) section 165 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862161] (regulators' power to require information: authorised persons *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]);
- (b) section 166 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862171] (reports by skilled persons); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) section 167 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862181] (appointment of persons to carry out general investigations); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) section 176(3) (entry of premises under warrant); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (e) section 205 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862191] (public censure); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (f) section 206 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862201] (financial penalties). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) The following provisions of the Act apply in relation to the exercise of the powers under the provisions applied by paragraph (1)(a) to (c) as they apply in relation to the exercise of those powers in respect of authorised persons— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) section 169 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862211] (investigations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] in support of overseas regulator); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) section 170 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862221] (investigations: general); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) section 171 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862231] (powers of persons appointed under section 167); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) section 174 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862241] (admissibility of statements made to investigators); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (e) section 175 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862251] (information and documents: supplemental provisions); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (f) section 176 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862261] (entry of premises under warrant); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (g) section 176A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862271] (retention of documents taken under section 176); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (h) section 177 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862281] (offences). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) Sections 207 to 211 of, and paragraph 20 of Schedule 1ZA to, the Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862291] apply in relation to the exercise of the FCA's powers under section 205 or 206 of the Act as applied by paragraph (1)(e) and (f) as they apply in relation to their exercise in respect of authorised persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) Unauthorised AIFMs are to be treated as regulated persons for the purposes of paragraph 21 of Schedule 1ZA to the Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) In this regulation “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**unauthorised AIFM *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means a person who is not an authorised person but who is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] Gibraltar AIFM[^key-d9d6dd395f3282c09f8b0839817f1e50] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b];
- (c) a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] Gibraltar AIFM[^key-1ce79a199cb78f8a938eecf8cb100618] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] that is entitled to market an AIF following a notification under regulation 57; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (d) an AIFM that is entitled to market an AIF following a notification under regulation 58; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (e) an AIFM to which the requirement at regulation 59(3) applies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^key-f619da5134fbdeef84d72bfe76560d6a]...
- (f) a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] Gibraltar AIFM[^key-11a5e60afe286258815e42d538a10cf4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] that is exercising a right to market an AIF arising out of the EuSEF Regulation or the EuVECA Regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] ; or[^key-838b9d89996e567c7f393023b47e0a9f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (g) an AIFM to which regulation 75 (closed-ended AIFs whose subscription period has closed) applies *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
