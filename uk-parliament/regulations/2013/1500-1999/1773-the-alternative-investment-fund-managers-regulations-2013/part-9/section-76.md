# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 76

## 76. Permission to act as trustee or depositary of an AIF (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- “**the new activity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means the activity specified in article 51ZD of the Regulated Activities Order (acting as trustee or depositary of an AIF) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862311];
- “**the old activity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means the activity of acting as trustee of an authorised unit trust scheme, acting as the depositary of an authorised contractual scheme or acting as the depositary of an open-ended investment company, as specified in article 51(1)(b), (bb) or (c) of the Regulated Activities Order as it was in force immediately before 22nd July 2013 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862321];
- “the relevant date” in respect of a person means the earlier of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (a) the date on which the FCA or PRA notifies the person that their application for Part 4A permission to carry on the new activity has been determined; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
  - (b) 22nd July 2014. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) Section 20(1) and (1A) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862331] of the Act (authorised persons acting without permission) does not apply to an authorised person who carries on the new activity before the relevant date without a Part 4A permission to carry on that activity if the person meets condition A and condition B. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) Condition A is that the person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) carries on the activity for an AIF that is not an authorised AIF; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) has a Part 4A permission to carry on the old activity (see paragraph (5)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) Condition B is that the person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) meets the requirements for appointment as a depositary imposed by implementing provisions relating to Article 21.3 of the directive; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) has its registered office or has established a branch in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) If a person has a Part 4A permission to carry on the old activity immediately before 22nd July 2013 and that person continues to carry on that activity on or after 22nd July 2013, the amendments to other enactments made by these Regulations which relate to removal from the Regulated Activities Order of the specification of the old activity do not apply in respect of that person until the relevant date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(6) Article 42A of the Regulated Activities Order (depositaries of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UCITS *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] and AIFs) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862341] applies to a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) is carrying on the new activity without a Part 4A permission to carry on that activity but who, pursuant to paragraph (2), is not to be taken to have contravened a requirement, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) does not have a Part 4A permission to carry on the regulated activity specified in article 40 of the Regulated Activities Order (safeguarding and administering investments), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

as it does to a person who has a Part 4A permission to carry on the new activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(7) A person who carries on the new activity and who, pursuant to paragraph (2), need not have a Part 4A permission to carry on that activity, must comply with all other implementing provisions in respect of that activity that would apply if the person had such a Part 4A permission. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
