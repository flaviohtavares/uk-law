# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 74

## 74. Closed-ended AIFs that make no additional investments (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to an AIFM in so far as immediately before 22nd July 2013 it manages an AIF that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) is closed-ended; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) makes no additional investments after 22nd July 2013. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) Notwithstanding regulation 72(3), the AIFM need not[^key-b6356bd5ffb3edb0d2d29a2d56704367] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] apply for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) permission under Part 4A of the Act to manage that AIF; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) registration as a small registered *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM in respect of that AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) The AIFM need not comply with the implementing provisions in respect of that AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4)[^key-fd266cff9144b16f144c00e3f9da3898] If the AIFM is given a Part 4A permission to carry on the regulated activity of managing an AIF, article 72AA of the Regulated Activities Order (managers of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UCITS *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] and AIFs) does not apply in respect of the AIFM's management of the AIF referred to in paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) If, at the same time that the AIFM is given a Part 4A permission to manage an AIF, the AIFM's Part 4A permission is also varied by the removal from the regulated activities to which that permission relates of regulated activities that the AIFM carries on in connection with or for the purposes of managing the AIF referred to in paragraph (1), the AIFM is to be treated as if the AIFM's Part 4A permission had not been varied by such removal at such time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(6) If, immediately before 22nd July 2013, the AIFM had a Part 4A permission to carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 51 of the Regulated Activities Order (establishing, operating or winding up a collective investment scheme) as that article applied at that date , from the relevant date (as defined in regulation 72(7)) that permission is to be treated as if it were a Part 4A permission to carry on an activity of the kind specified by article 51ZE of the Regulated Activities Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
