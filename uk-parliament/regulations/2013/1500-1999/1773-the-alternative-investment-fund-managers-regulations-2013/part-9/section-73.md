# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 73

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. AIFs subject to prospectus directive (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to an AIF, the securities of which are subject to an offer to the public under a prospectus that has been drawn up and published in accordance with the prospectus directive before 22nd July 2013, for the duration of the validity of that prospectus. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) Part 6 does not apply in respect of an AIF falling within paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) In this regulation, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**prospectus directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]**” means Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [2003/71/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2003/71)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 November 2003 on the prospectus to be published when securities are offered to the public or admitted to trading *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] [^c21862301].
