# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773

## 25. Disqualification of external valuer (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If it appears to the FCA that an external valuer has failed to comply with an implementing provision applicable to it, it may disqualify the valuer from acting as an external valuer, or from so acting for any particular class of AIFs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) If the FCA proposes to disqualify a valuer under this section, it must give the valuer a warning notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) If the FCA decides to disqualify a valuer— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) it must give the valuer a decision notice; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the valuer may refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) The FCA may remove any disqualification imposed under this regulation if satisfied the disqualified person will in future comply with the implementing provisions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
