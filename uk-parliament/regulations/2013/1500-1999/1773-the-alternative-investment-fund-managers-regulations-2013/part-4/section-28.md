# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 28

## 28. Liability following delegation (affected by repeal, see note) ...[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-b04eacf5031d31ed00a58a412885a264]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Irrespective of any contractual arrangements that provide otherwise, any liability of a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM to an AIF it manages, or to an investor of such an AIF, is not affected by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the delegation of functions by the AIFM to a third party (“a delegate”); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) any sub-delegation of such functions by the delegate to another person (“a sub-delegate”); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (c) any further sub-delegation of such functions by a sub-delegate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) A delegate or sub-delegate which has delegated or sub-delegated such functions must review on an ongoing basis the services provided by the person to whom functions have been delegated or sub-delegated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
