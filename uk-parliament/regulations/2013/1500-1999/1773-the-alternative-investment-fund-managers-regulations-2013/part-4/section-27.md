# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 27

## 27. Revocation of approval for delegation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA may revoke its approval granted under regulation 26. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) If the FCA proposes to revoke its approval it must give the applicant a warning notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) If the FCA decides to revoke its approval— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) it must give the applicant a decision notice; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the applicant may refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
