# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773

## 29. Depositary liability: general provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation and regulations 30 to 32 apply in relation to the depositary of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIF managed by a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] a Gibraltar AIFM[^c-d987ca8e1a9d51f9f421484d7734b68d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b].

(2) Any obligation or liability of a depositary under regulation 30 or 31 to the investors of the AIF may be invoked directly or indirectly through the AIFM, depending on the nature of the legal relationship between the depositary, the AIFM and the investors. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) For the purposes of regulations 30 to 32, the provision of services as specified by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the Financial Markets and Insolvency (Settlement Finality) Regulations 1999 by a designated system (within the meaning of regulation 2(1) of those Regulations)[^c-123bbe00388d25e0ddb245f70066db6e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], or the provision of similar services by a third-country securities settlement system, is not to be considered a delegation of custody functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
