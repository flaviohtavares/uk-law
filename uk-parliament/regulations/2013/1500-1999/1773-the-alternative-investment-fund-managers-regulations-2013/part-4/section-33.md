## 33. Management of third country AIFs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM may manage a third county AIF that is not marketed in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the United Kingdom or Gibraltar[^key-97b5feece6a5ba748114b928d3e7d3bc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] only if appropriate cooperation arrangements are in place between the FCA and the supervisory authorities of the third country where the AIF is established in order to ensure an efficient exchange of information that enables the FCA to carry out its duties in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, these Regulations, and any EU tertiary legislation made under the directive which forms part of[^key-832818160841fecf9d4dd25bf3dd0e93] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] assimilated[^key-2ea41b8b62167cf57265774269a24728] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] law *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b].
