# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Valuation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An external valuer must carry out the valuation function described in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] section 3.9 of the Investment Funds sourcebook[^key-0cb7abfa4b2f8f1ae06a5f44600cc0c5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] impartially, and with all due skill, care and diligence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) An external valuer may not delegate such valuation function to a third party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) If the FCA considers the appointment of an external valuer does not comply with the implementing provisions, the FCA may require that another external valuer be appointed instead. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) Any liability of a full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM to an AIF managed by it, or to an investor of such an AIF, arising out of the AIFM's responsibility for the proper valuation of AIF assets, the calculation of the net asset value of the AIF and the publication of that net asset value, is not affected by the appointment by the AIFM of an external valuer in respect of that AIF. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) Irrespective of any contractual arrangements that provide otherwise, an external valuer is liable to the AIFM of an AIF in respect of which the external valuer is appointed for any losses suffered by the AIFM as a result of the external valuer's negligence or intentional failure to perform its tasks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
