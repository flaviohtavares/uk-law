# The Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-02-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1773
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Depositary liability for other losses (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If an AIF, or investors of an AIF, have suffered losses other than the loss by the depositary or a third party to which custody has been delegated or sub-delegated of financial instruments held in custody in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] rule 3.11.21 of the Investment Funds sourcebook[^c-47f1896dc778cad2f92cb9194d2e061a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b], the depositary is liable to the AIF, or investors of the AIF, if the losses are a result of the depositary's negligent or intentional failure to comply with an implementing provision that applies to it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) Irrespective of any contractual arrangements that provide otherwise, any liability of the depositary to the AIF, or to investors of the AIF, under paragraph (1) is not affected by any delegation by the depositary of the functions referred to in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] rules 3.11.21 and 3.11.23 of the Investment Funds sourcebook[^c-4d92e0602b3460b59cbc44a1739552ba] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b].
