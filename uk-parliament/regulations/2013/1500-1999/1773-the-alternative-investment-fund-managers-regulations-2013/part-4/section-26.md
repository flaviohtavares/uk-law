## 26. Approval for delegation of functions by full-scope (affected by repeal, see note) UK (affected by repeal, see note) AIFMs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b][^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A full-scope *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] UK *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b] AIFM may not delegate its functions of portfolio management or risk management for an AIF to an undertaking, and that undertaking may not sub-delegate such function to another undertaking, unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) the undertaking to whom the functions are to be delegated or sub-delegated falls within paragraph (2); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the FCA has approved such delegation or sub-delegation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(2) An undertaking falls within this paragraph if it is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) authorised or registered for the purpose of asset management; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) subject to supervision in relation to its asset management function. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(3) An application for approval under paragraph (1)(b)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) must be made in such manner as the FCA may direct; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) must contain or be accompanied by such information as the FCA may reasonably require for the purposes of determining the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(4) At any time after receiving an application and before determining it, the FCA may require the applicant to provide it with such further information as it considers necessary to enable it to determine the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(5) An application under paragraph (3) must be determined by the FCA within one month of receiving the completed application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(6) The FCA may determine an incomplete application if it considers it appropriate to do so; and it must in any event determine such an application within two months of receiving the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(7) If the FCA proposes to refuse an application under paragraph (3) it must give the applicant a written notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

(8) If the FCA decides to refuse an application under paragraph (3)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]

- (a) it must give the applicant a written notice explaining its reasons for the decision; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
- (b) the applicant may refer the matter to the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-399b199dab6c889dc1487936a3b4dd9b]
