# The Added Tribunals (Employment Tribunals and Employment Appeal Tribunal) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 1892

Tribunals And Inquiries

The Added Tribunals (Employment Tribunals and Employment Appeal Tribunal) Order 2013

Made

28th July 2013

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 42(3) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^f00001].

The Lord Chancellor has consulted the Senior President of Tribunals and the Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council in accordance with section 42(5) before making this Order.

In accordance with section 49(5), a draft of this Order was laid before and has been approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Added Tribunals (Employment Tribunals and Employment Appeal Tribunal) Order 2013.

## 2. Employment tribunals and the Employment Appeal Tribunal to be “added tribunals”

Employment tribunals and the Employment Appeal Tribunal are “added tribunals” for the purpose of section 42 of the 2007 Act.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Helen Grant

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

28th July 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for employment tribunals and the Employment Appeal Tribunal to be “added tribunals” for the purpose of section 42 of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007.

Adding these tribunals enables the Lord Chancellor to make an order under that provision to impose fees for anything they deal with.
