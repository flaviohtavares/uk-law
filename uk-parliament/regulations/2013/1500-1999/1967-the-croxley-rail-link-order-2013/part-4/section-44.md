# The Croxley Rail Link Order 2013, section 44

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 44. Certification of plans etc.[^c-7d420c4e4740a9b6e23436c1bf42b2c9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Council must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the book of reference, the deposited sections and the deposited plans to the Secretary of State for certification that they are, respectively, true copies of the book of reference, deposited sections and deposited plans referred to in this Order; and a document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
