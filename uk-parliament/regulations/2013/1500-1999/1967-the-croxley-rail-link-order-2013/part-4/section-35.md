# The Croxley Rail Link Order 2013, section 35

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. Power to operate and use railway[^c-990b50bb12846e8c7f845e0c78579d91]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Council may operate and use the authorised works as a system, or part of a system, of transport for the carriage of passengers and goods.

(2) Nothing in this Order, or in any enactment incorporated with or applied by this Order, affects the operation of Part 1 of the Railways Act 1993.
