# The Croxley Rail Link Order 2013, section 43

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 43. Disposal of land to LUL[^c-4d90378f02ad8fc5ff6637aacaac83fd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 123(2) of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00025] (which requires the consent of the Secretary of State for certain disposals of land by a principal council) does not apply to the disposal of land to LUL authorised by this Order.
