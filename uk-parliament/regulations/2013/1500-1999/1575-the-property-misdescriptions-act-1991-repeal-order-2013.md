# The Property Misdescriptions Act 1991 (Repeal) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 1575

Consumer Protection

The Property Misdescriptions Act 1991 (Repeal) Order 2013

Made

26th June 2013

Laid before Parliament

28th June 2013

Coming into force

1st October 2013

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to consumer protection and in relation to measures relating to the control of advertising.

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Property Misdescriptions Act 1991 (Repeal) Order 2013 and comes into force on 1 October 2013.

## 2. Repeal of the Property Misdescriptions Act 1991

The Property Misdescriptions Act 1991[^f00003] is repealed.

## 3. Consequential amendments

The amendments in the Schedule have effect.

Jo Swinson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations and Consumer Affairs

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

26th June 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order repeals the Property Misdescriptions Act 1991 and makes consequential amendments to other enactments.

Since the introduction of the 1991 Act, [Directive 2005/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0029) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning unfair business-to-consumer commercial practices (OJ No. L 149, 11.6.2005, p 22) and [Directive 2006/114/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0114) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning misleading and comparative advertising (OJ No. L 376, 27.12.2006, p 21) have been implemented in the United Kingdom. They provide the same protection for consumers as the 1991 Act but in a broader and more modern framework. The 1991 Act has therefore become unnecessary.

Article 2 repeals the Property Misdescriptions Act 1991.

Article 3 and the Schedule make consequential amendments to other enactments.

An impact assessment has been prepared in relation to this Order and will be placed in the Library of each House of Parliament. Copies may also be obtained via www.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment\_data/file/34614/12-1154-estate-agents-market-regulatory-reform-impact.pdf.
