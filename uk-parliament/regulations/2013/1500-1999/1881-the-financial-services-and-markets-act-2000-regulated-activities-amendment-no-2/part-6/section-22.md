# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2013, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Saving provision related to article 21

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Articles 1, 3 and 4 of the Consumer Credit Licensing (Representations) Order 1976 [^c23373951] continue to apply in so far as section 34 or 34ZA [^c23373961] of the 1974 Act continue to have effect by virtue of Part 8; and for that purpose each reference to the Director is to be treated as a reference to the FCA.
