# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2013, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Information and investigation powers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 165 of the Act (regulators' power to require information) [^c23374391] has effect as if each reference to “authorised person” (except in subsection (7)) included a reference to a person who at any time held a standard licence under the 1974 Act.

(2) Section 168 of the Act (appointment of persons to carry out investigations in particular cases) [^c23374401] applies as if—

- (a) subsection (1) included a reference to an offence under the 1974 Act;
- (b) subsection (4) included a reference to circumstances suggesting that a person may have, before 1st April 2014, failed to comply with a requirement imposed on that person under section 33A, 33B or 36A of the 1974 Act.

(3) Part 27 of the Act (offences) [^c23374411] applies as if references to an offence included references to an offence under Part 11 of the Act as modified by this Order.
