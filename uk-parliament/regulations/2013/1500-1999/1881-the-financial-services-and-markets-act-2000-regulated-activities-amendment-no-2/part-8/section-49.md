## 49. Offences committed under the 1974 Act before 1st April 2014

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 402(1) of the Act (power of FCA to institute proceedings for certain offences) [^c23374381] applies as if it included a reference to offences committed under the 1974 Act before 1st April 2014.
