# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2013, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1881

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Interpretation of Part 8

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “appeal period”, “debtor”, “hirer”, “licence”, “**licensee**”, “**regulated agreement**” and “**standard licence**” have the meanings given by the 1974 Act (disregarding any repeal of the 1974 Act by this Order);
- “**appropriate regulator**” means—
  - (a) in relation to a person who is, or is applying to become, a PRA-authorised person, the PRA;
  - (b) in any other case, the FCA.
