# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2013, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Failure to comply with information requirement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies if, before 1st April 2014, a relevant person (“A”) had failed to do something A was required to do by virtue of section 36B or 36C of the 1974 Act (power of OFT to require information or to require access to premises) [^c23374271].

(2) Section 36E of the 1974 Act (failure to comply with information requirement) continues to apply in connection with A's failure as if the reference to the OFT were a reference to the FCA.

## Annotations

[^c23374271]: Marginal citation: Section 36B was inserted by the Consumer Credit Act 2006, section 46. Section 36C was inserted by section 47 of that Act.
