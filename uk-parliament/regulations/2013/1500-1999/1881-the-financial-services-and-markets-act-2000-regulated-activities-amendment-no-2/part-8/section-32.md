# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2013, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Applications for a standard licence where determination has been made but appeal period has not ended

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraphs (2) to (4) apply if, before 1st April 2014—

- (a) the OFT had given a notice to a person (“A”) of its determination to refuse to issue a standard licence to A in accordance with A's application, and
- (b) the appeal period in relation to that decision had not ended.

(2) The notice is to be treated as—

- (a) if A does not have a Part 4A permission, a decision notice given under section 55X(4) [^c23374111] of the Act by the FCA to A of the decision to refuse A's application for Part 4A permission;
- (b) in any other case, a decision notice given under section 55X(4) of the Act of the decision of the appropriate regulator to refuse A's application to vary A's Part 4A permission.

(3) But if A had, before 1st April 2014, submitted a notice of appeal to the First-tier Tribunal under section 41 of the 1974 Act (appeals to First-tier Tribunal under Part 3) [^c23374121] —

- (a) section 55Z3 of the Act (right to refer matters to the Tribunal) [^c23374131] does not apply, and
- (b) for the purposes of section 55V(4) [^c23374141] of the Act, the appropriate regulator is not to be taken as having determined the application.

(4) The notice has effect subject to any necessary modifications.
