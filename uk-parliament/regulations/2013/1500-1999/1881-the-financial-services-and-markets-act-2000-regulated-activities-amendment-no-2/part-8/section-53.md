# The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2013, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-07-11. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/1881
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Waivers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any of the following given or made by the OFT which are in effect immediately before 1st April 2014 are to have effect as if they had been given or made by the FCA—

- (a) a direction given under section 60(3) of the 1974 Act (form and content of agreements) [^c23374431];
- (b) a determination made under regulations made under section 64(4) of the 1974 Act (duty to give notice of cancellation rights) [^c23374441];
- (c) a direction given under section 101(8) or (8A) of the 1974 Act (right to terminate hire agreement) [^c23374451];
- (d) a direction given under section 160(1) of the 1974 Act (alternative procedure for business consumers) [^c23374461].
