## 20. Amendments of the 1974 Act

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The 1974 Act is amended as follows.

(2) Part 1 (sections 1 to 7) (Office of Fair Trading) [^c23372981] is omitted.

(3) In section 8 (consumer credit agreements) [^c23372991], for subsection (3) substitute—

> (3) A consumer credit agreement is a regulated agreement within the meaning of this Act if it is a regulated credit agreement for the purposes of Chapter 14A of Part 2 of the Regulated Activities Order.

.

(4) In section 15 (consumer hire agreements) [^c23373001] for subsection (2) substitute—

> (2) A consumer hire agreement is a regulated agreement with the meaning of this Act if it is a regulated consumer hire agreement for the purposes of Chapter 14B of Part 2 of the Regulated Activities Order.

.

> **20. Total charge for credit**
>
> In this Act, “**the total charge for credit**” has the meaning given by the Regulated Activities Order for the purposes of Chapter 14A of Part 2 of that Order.

.
