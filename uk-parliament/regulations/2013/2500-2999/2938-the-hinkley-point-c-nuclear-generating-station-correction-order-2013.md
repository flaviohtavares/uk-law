# The Hinkley Point C (Nuclear Generating Station) (Correction) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 2938

Infrastructure Planning

The Hinkley Point C (Nuclear Generating Station) (Correction) Order 2013

Made

19th November 2013

Coming into force

20th November 2013

The Hinkley Point C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2013 (“the Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00002], contained correctable errors within the meaning of the Act.

Before the end of the relevant period, as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act, the Secretary of State received written requests for the correction of errors in the Order.

The Secretary of State has informed West Somerset District Council, Sedgemoor District Council and Somerset County Council, the local planning authorities for the area in which the land to which the Order relates is situated, that the requests have been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Hinkley Point C (Nuclear Generating Station) (Correction) Order 2013 and comes into force on 20th November 2013.

## 2. Corrections

(1) Tables 1, 2, 3 and 4 of the Schedule make corrections in accordance with paragraph (2) to the articles of, and Schedules 1, 2 and 14 to, the Hinkley Point C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2013 (“the corrected Order”) respectively.

(2) In relation to each of the provisions of the corrected Order specified in the first column of each Table, the erroneous text or omission identified in the second column of that Table is corrected in accordance with the instructions in the third column.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy and Climate Change

Giles Scott

Head of National Infrastructure Consents

Department of Energy and Climate Change

19th November 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order corrects errors identified in the Hinkley Point C (Nuclear Generating Station) Order 2013, a development consent order under the Planning Act 2008, following requests under paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
