# The Prevention of Social Housing Fraud Act 2013 (Commencement) (England) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 2622 (C. 105)

Housing, England

The Prevention of Social Housing Fraud Act 2013 (Commencement) (England) Order 2013

Made

14th October 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12(3) of the Prevention of Social Housing Fraud Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Prevention of Social Housing Fraud Act 2013 (Commencement) (England) Order 2013.

## 2. Application and commencement

The Prevention of Social Housing Fraud Act 2013 (apart from section 12) comes into force in England only on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Local Government

Kris Hopkins

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

14th October 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, in England, the Prevention of Social Housing Fraud Act 2013. The Act (apart from section 12, which came into force on Royal Assent) comes into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

A full impact assessment was prepared for the Act and is available from http://www.gov.uk/government/publications/prevention-of-social-housing-fraud-act-2013. Copies are also available from the House of Commons Library.
