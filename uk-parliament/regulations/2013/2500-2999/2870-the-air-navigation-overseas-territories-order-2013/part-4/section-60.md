# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 60

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Glider pilot-minimum age[^c-2871221868124f676a92081142a46e83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person under the age of 16 years must not act as pilot-in-command of a glider.
