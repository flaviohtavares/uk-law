# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 59

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Instruction in flying[^key-c6e64fad80116652bc0403dc27559ec3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies to instruction in flying given to any person flying or about to fly an aircraft for the purpose of becoming qualified for—

- (a) the grant of a pilot’s licence;
- (b) the inclusion or variation of any rating in a licence.

(2) The holder of a licence must not give any instruction in flying to which this article applies unless—

- (a) the licence granted or rendered valid under this Order entitles the holder to act as pilot-in-command of the aircraft for the purpose and in the circumstances under which the instruction is to be given; and
- (b) the licence includes an instructor’s rating entitling the holder to give the instruction.
