# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 126

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126. Aerial work[^key-cadeac1e19c04d33fdb10adf63cb08db]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Aerial work means any purpose (other than commercial air transport) for which an aircraft is flown if valuable consideration is given or promised in respect of the flight or the purpose of the flight.

(2) If the only such valuable consideration consists of remuneration for the services of the pilot the flight is deemed to be a private flight for the purposes of Part 3.
