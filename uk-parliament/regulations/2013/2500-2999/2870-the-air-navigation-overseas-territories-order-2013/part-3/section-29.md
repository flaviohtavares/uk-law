# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 29

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Permit to fly or special flight permit ceasing to be in force[^c-305fdbbe96ebbe9616d65f327206bdc0][^c-3f1b5b9116ab991e69adb2afe41eec5f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A permit to fly or special flight permit issued for an aircraft ceases to be in force—

- (a) if any conditions of the permit are not complied with;
- (b) until the completion of any inspection, modification or maintenance of the aircraft or any of its equipment, required for ascertaining whether the aircraft remains airworthy and—
  - (i) made mandatory by the Governor or, in the case of any aircraft registered other than in the Territory, by the competent authority of the State of registry or of the State of the operator of the aircraft, as the case may be[^c-5030d838ff2705cbe85bacd1f1790b86]; or
  - (ii) required as a condition of the permit to fly or special flight permit;
- (c) if the aircraft, engines or propellers, or such of its equipment as is necessary for the airworthiness of the aircraft, are modified or repaired; unless the repair or modification has been approved by the Governor or by a person or organisation approved by the Governor for the purpose or, in the case of any aircraft registered other than in the Territory, by the competent authority of the State of registry or of the State of the operator of the aircraft, as the case may be[^c-4bfe2efa57122ddd95e5b820bf564192].
