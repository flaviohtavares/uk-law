# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 166

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 166. Journey Log[^c-45dc9734ca704d795b70df68efbbe8f1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An operator of an aircraft used for the purposes of international air navigation must record and retain the information specified in instructions given by the Governor under article 74(1) in a journey log, in such form as is specified in those instructions.

(2) The Governor may permit an operator not to keep a journey log if the relevant information is available in other documents that are required to be kept under the provisions of this Order.

(3) An operator must ensure that all entries in a journey log are made concurrently and be permanent in nature.
