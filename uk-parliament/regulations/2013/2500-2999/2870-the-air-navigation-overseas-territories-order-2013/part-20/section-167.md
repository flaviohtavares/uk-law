# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 167

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 167. Documents to be carried[^key-63e90bdc3dca6f5f8c984543cd9b9160]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An aircraft must not fly unless it carries the documents that it is required to carry under the law of the country in which it is registered.

(2) An aircraft registered in the Territory must, when in flight, carry the documents specified in instructions given by the Governor or identified in the specified requirements.
