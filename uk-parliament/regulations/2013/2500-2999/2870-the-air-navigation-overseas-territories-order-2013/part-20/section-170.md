# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 170

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 170. Power to inspect and copy documents and records[^c-50f279f1840e0f483fd4610d1f1cfcb1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An authorised person has the power to inspect and copy any certificate, licence, log book, document or record that under this Order is required to be produced to that person.
