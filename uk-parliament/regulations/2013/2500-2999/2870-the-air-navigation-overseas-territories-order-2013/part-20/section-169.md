# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 169

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 169. Production of aeronautical telecommunication service documents and records[^c-f9851c80463f3b6877041feb00504243]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The holder of an approval under article 153 must within a reasonable time after being requested to do so by an authorised person, cause to be produced to that person any documents and records relating to any aeronautical telecommunication service used or intended to be used in connection with the provision of a service to an aircraft.
