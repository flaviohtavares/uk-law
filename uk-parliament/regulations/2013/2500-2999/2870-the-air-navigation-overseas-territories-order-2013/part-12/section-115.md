# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 115

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. Inspections by the operator for damage, leakage or contamination[^key-165a2b47b693dbb90efac44501b40510]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The operator of an aircraft in which dangerous goods are to be carried must ensure that packages and freight containers which contain such goods are inspected for evidence of damage or leakage before being loaded on an aircraft, in accordance with the specified requirements.
