# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 114

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Method of loading by the operator[^key-bfcbab8353bbdc8556368e0f9b127242]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The operator of an aircraft in which dangerous goods are to be carried must ensure that such goods are not carried in any compartment occupied by passengers or on the flight deck, except in circumstances permitted by the specified requirements.
