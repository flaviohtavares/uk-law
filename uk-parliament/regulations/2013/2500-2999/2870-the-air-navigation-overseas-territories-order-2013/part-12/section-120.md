# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 120

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Provision of information in respect of cargo[^c-337a383fc510b6aaef77b24defaddc1e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The operator of an aircraft in which cargo is to be carried and any agent of the operator must ensure that notices giving information about the carriage of dangerous goods are displayed in accordance with the specified requirements.
