# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 122

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 122. Preservation of documents and records[^key-0f7e5ad67b0c4df990f45d86ac65e776]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The operator of an aircraft on which dangerous goods are carried must preserve in a legible form for not less than three months any dangerous goods transport document or other document prescribed in the specified requirements.
