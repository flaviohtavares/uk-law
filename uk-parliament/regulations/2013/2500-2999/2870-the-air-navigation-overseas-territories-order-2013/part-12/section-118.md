# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 118

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Provision of training by operators and shippers[^key-ac6d01891c3255b2229843e7700a9aef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The operator of an aircraft, its handling agent, a shipper of dangerous goods by air and any agent of the shipper, must establish and undertake training programmes for those of their employees and staff who are involved in the carriage of goods or passengers by air.

(2) In the case of an operator resident in the Territory[^key-14b29fcd5e592266eebfab7cd6e27c70] or its agents, the training programme must be approved by, or acceptable, to the Governor.

(3) In the case of a shipper or its agent, the training programme must meet the specified requirements.
