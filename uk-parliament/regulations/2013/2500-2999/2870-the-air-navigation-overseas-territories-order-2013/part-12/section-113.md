# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 113

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 113. Acceptance of dangerous goods by the operator[^key-b08c97bc3c32cfd04ae69044fbd37d51]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The operator of an aircraft in which dangerous goods are to be carried must ensure that—

- (a) no package or freight container containing dangerous goods is accepted for carriage until it has been inspected to determine that it complies with the specified requirements;
- (b) the goods are not forbidden for carriage by air in any circumstances; and
- (c) any package or freight container is accompanied by a dangerous goods transport document.
