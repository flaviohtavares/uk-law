# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 192

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 192. Approval for persons to furnish reports[^key-083ee39e60e24b48b6bff57ef47bec98]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Governor may approve a person as qualified to provide reports, and may accept such reports, in relation to any of the Governor’s functions under any of the provisions of this Order.
