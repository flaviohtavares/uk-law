# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 142

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 142. Use of radio call signs at aerodromes[^key-9683ae5c8a15bd593173a5f648d3c2c0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The person in charge of an aerodrome provided with means of two-way radio communication must not cause or permit any call sign to be used for a purpose other than a purpose for which that call sign has been notified.
