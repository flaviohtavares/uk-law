# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 161

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 161. Customs and Excise airports[^c-0b9f59904615056a6b7ee15d56ddc63f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Governor may by order designate any aerodrome to be a place for the landing or departure of aircraft for the purpose of the enactments for the time being in force relating to customs and excise.

(2) The Governor may by order revoke any designation so made.
