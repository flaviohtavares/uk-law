# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 1

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and Commencement[^c-f950b09bbbd6e8053d68b0e4d1911895]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Order may be cited as the Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013 and comes into force on 1st January 2014.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**1.** This Order sets out the provisions applicable to the Overseas Territories listed in Schedule 6 to enable the United Kingdom to comply with the Convention on International Civil Aviation (known as the Chicago Convention) and, in particular, the Annexes to that Convention. This Order replaces the Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/3468](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/3468)), the [Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) (Amendment) Order 2008 (S,I. 2008/3125)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/3125) and the Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) (Amendment) Order 2011 ([S.I. 2011/237](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/237).
