# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 77

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Wearing of survival suits by crew[^key-4b6517c17c0945f1ae22b7b1bd4b6ec0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Each member of the crew of an aircraft registered in the Territory must wear a survival suit if the specified requirements demand that such a suit is to be worn.
