# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 88

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Marking of break-in areas[^key-e4a9436341398ef09c45ca741f75a41e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies to all aircraft registered in the Territory.

(2) An operator must ensure that, if areas of the fuselage suitable for break-in by rescue crews in emergency are marked on aircraft to which this article applies, such areas are marked upon the exterior surface of the fuselage with markings to show the areas (in this article referred to as “break-in areas”) which can, for purposes of rescue in an emergency, be most readily and effectively broken into by persons outside the aircraft.

(3) The break-in areas must be marked in accordance with the specified requirements.
