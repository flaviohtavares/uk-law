# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 97

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Training programme[^key-9be47af03725368b90a6790fe11b0cb1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The operator of every aircraft registered in the Territory and flying for the purpose of commercial air transport must—

- (a) establish a ground and flight training programme, approved by the Governor, to ensure that flight and cabin crew members are adequately trained and competent to perform their duties; and
- (b) ensure that the details of the approved training programme are incorporated into the operations manual required by article 96.

(2) This article does not apply to aircraft flying, or intended by the operator of the aircraft to fly, solely under and in accordance with the terms of a police air operator’s certificate.

(3) The operator must make such amendments or additions to the training programme as the Governor may require for the purpose of ensuring the safety of the aircraft, or of persons or property carried in it, or for the safety of air navigation.
