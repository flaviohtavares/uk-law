# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 66

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Keeping and production of records of exposure to cosmic radiation[^c-d9f3f357f4b46ad2d1a910180490ab0c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A relevant undertaking must keep a record for the period and in the manner specified by the Governor of the exposure to cosmic radiation of air crew assessed under article 65 and the names of the air crew concerned.

(2) A relevant undertaking must, within a reasonable period after being requested to do so by an authorised person, produce to that person the record required to be kept under paragraph (1).

(3) A relevant undertaking must, within a reasonable period after being requested to do so by a person, in respect of whom a record is required to be kept under paragraph (1), supply a copy of that record to that person.
