# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 179

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 179. Smoking in aircraft[^key-663245ad85b868f18e3a3f355d0441f4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person must not smoke in any compartment of an aircraft registered in the Territory at a time when smoking is prohibited in that compartment by a notice to that effect exhibited by or on behalf of the pilot-in-command of the aircraft.
