# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 176

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 176. Endangering safety of aircraft[^c-e45d5c89c523fdd71d31cf7304b9246c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person must not recklessly or negligently act in a manner likely to endanger an aircraft, or any person in the aircraft.
