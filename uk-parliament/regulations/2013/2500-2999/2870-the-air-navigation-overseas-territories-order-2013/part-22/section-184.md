# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 184

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 184. Stowaways[^key-5d685a5befdc1196bae6688ab3859af0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person must not secrete himself or herself for the purpose of being carried in an aircraft without the consent of either the operator or the pilot-in-command or of any other person entitled to give consent to being carried in the aircraft.
