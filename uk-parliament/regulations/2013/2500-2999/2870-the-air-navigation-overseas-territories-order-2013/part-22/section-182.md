# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) Order 2013, section 182

> Text as at 2024-04-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 182. Obstruction of persons[^key-36d72c169f5f52c0ad0b5e05a78ea600]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person must not intentionally obstruct or impede any person acting in the exercise of powers or the performance of duties under this Order.
