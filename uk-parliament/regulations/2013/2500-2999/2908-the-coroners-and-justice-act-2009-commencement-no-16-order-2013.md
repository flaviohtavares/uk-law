# The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 16) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 2908 (C. 118)

Tribunals, England And Wales

The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 16) Order 2013

Made

14th November 2013

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in the exercise of the power conferred by section 182(4) of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 16) Order 2013.

## 2. Commencement of provisions of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009

Section 148 (security in tribunal buildings) of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 is commenced on 18th November 2013.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Shailesh Vara

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

14th November 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order commences section 148 of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009, which contains a power for the Lord Chancellor to make an order allowing for the designation of tribunal security officers and to apply the provisions in Part 4 of the Courts Act [2003 (c.39)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/39) relating to court security to tribunals.
