# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013, section 1

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-9a2506fc8c810538416bfec5d216cb22][^key-568d12d128052ca6057739015ca9dc0b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013 and shall come into force on 11th November 2013.

(2)[^key-0ffa3b6ab61d04372881863c3bed5ceb] Part 1 extends to England and Wales and Northern Ireland only.

(3) Part 2 extends to Scotland only.
