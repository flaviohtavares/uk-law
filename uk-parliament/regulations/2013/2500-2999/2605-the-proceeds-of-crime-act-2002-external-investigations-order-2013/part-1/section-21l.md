# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013, section 21L

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 21L (Extent: E+W). Receivers in connection with interim freezing orders (Extent: E+W)[^M_F_6f5a60ae-0a79-45ea-f23d-57ee03aae285]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W)) This article applies where the High Court makes an interim freezing order on an application by an enforcement authority. *(Extent: E+W)*

(2 (Extent: E+W)) The court may, on an application by the enforcement authority, by order appoint a receiver in respect of any property to which the interim freezing order applies. *(Extent: E+W)*

(3 (Extent: E+W)) An application under paragraph (2) may be made at the same time as the application for the interim freezing order or at any time afterwards. *(Extent: E+W)*

(4 (Extent: E+W)) The application may be made without notice if the circumstances of the case are such that notice of the application would give rise to a risk of any external order that might subsequently be obtained being frustrated. *(Extent: E+W)*

(5 (Extent: E+W)) In its application the enforcement authority must nominate a suitably qualified person for appointment as a receiver. *(Extent: E+W)*

(6 (Extent: E+W)) The person nominated may be a member of staff of the enforcement authority. *(Extent: E+W)*
