# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013, section 4

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Action on receipt of request in relation to an external investigation[^c-c14c33ae46c9f130c4c58ead4e7468b8]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may refer to the Director General of NCA , a relevant Director,[^c-8bdc78c548b72c8833249b3cfecabffe] [^c-e5a6ef27ac65734b149b926fe1756c25]... the FCA, the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs or an enforcement authority a request for assistance in relation to an external investigation.

(2) The Director General of NCA , a relevant Director,[^c-5511b2b5e33252e874ba3d3078d61851] [^c-bbfa4bc1758a944187ecbc13fd7630f7]... the FCA, the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs or an enforcement authority may on receipt of the request for assistance act under this Part.
