# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013, section 21E

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 21E. Statements[^c-edc9bff5ab79f1844fe87f371d901184]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) A statement made by a person in response to a requirement imposed by an unexplained wealth order may not be used in evidence against that person in criminal proceedings.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply—

- (a) in the case of proceedings under Part 2 or 4[^c-93d4c20420c3c2ec96b0c28335ac56a7] of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (confiscation: England and Wales or Northern Ireland[^c-ac4d5184c7f8baa7305fba8e9b99f027]),
- (b) in the case of proceedings under Part 2 or 4[^c-6d73d4cf47f6fef9eff0c326a6556e87] of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005 (giving effect in England and Wales or Northern Ireland[^c-97318d9ce91467abf70b6d649ec1a571] to external requests in connection with criminal investigations or proceedings and to external orders arising from such proceedings),
- (c) on a prosecution for an offence under section 5 of the Perjury Act 1911 or Article 10 of the Perjury (Northern Ireland) Order 1979[^c-78828d125df89b79ebad79b4565b6d39] (false statements), or
- (d) on a prosecution for some other offence where, in giving evidence, the person makes a statement inconsistent with the statement mentioned in paragraph (1).

(3) A statement may not be used by virtue of paragraph (2)(d) against a person unless—

- (a) evidence relating to it is adduced, or
- (b) a question relating to it is asked,

by the person or on the person’s behalf in proceedings arising out of the prosecution.
