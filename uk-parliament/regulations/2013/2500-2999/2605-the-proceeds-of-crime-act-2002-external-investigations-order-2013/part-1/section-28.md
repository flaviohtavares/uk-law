# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013, section 28

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Supplementary[^c-3296c043253b00d5ccc51e96d8ca0de7]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) An application for a customer information order may be made ex parte to a High Court judge in chambers.

(2) An application to discharge or vary a customer information order may be made to the High Court by—

- (a) the person who applied for the order,
- (b) any person affected by the order.

(3) The High Court—

- (a) may discharge the order,
- (b) may vary the order.

(4) If an officer of a description mentioned in paragraph (a), (b) or (c) of the meaning of appropriate officer (see article 2 (interpretation))[^c-14635c31479a564c90f154be6d93697e] applies for a customer information order, an application to discharge or vary the order may be made by a different officer of the same description[^c-89d9a346cbcfc74392a96342fc46cec8].

(5) References to a person who applied for a customer information order must be construed accordingly.

(6) An NCA officer may not make an application for a customer information order or an application to vary such an order unless they are a senior appropriate officer or they are authorised to do so by a senior appropriate officer.

(7) Paragraphs (2) to (5) do not apply to orders made in England and Wales.
