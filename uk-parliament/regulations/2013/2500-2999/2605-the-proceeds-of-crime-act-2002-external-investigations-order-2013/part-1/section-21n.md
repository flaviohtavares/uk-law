# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013, section 21N

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21N (Extent: E+W). Supervision of article 21L receiver and variations (Extent: E+W)[^M_F_6f5a60ae-0a79-45ea-f23d-57ee03aae285]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W)) Any of the following persons may at any time apply to the High Court for directions as to the exercise of the functions of a receiver appointed under article 21L (receivers in connection with interim freezing orders)— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) the receiver; *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) a party to the proceedings for the appointment of the receiver or the interim freezing order concerned; *(Extent: E+W)*
- (c (Extent: E+W)) a person affected by an action taken by the receiver; *(Extent: E+W)*
- (d (Extent: E+W)) a person who may be affected by an action proposed to be taken by the receiver. *(Extent: E+W)*

(2 (Extent: E+W)) Before it gives directions under paragraph (1) the court must give an opportunity to be heard to— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) the receiver; *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) the parties to the proceedings for the appointment of the receiver and for the interim freezing order concerned; *(Extent: E+W)*
- (c (Extent: E+W)) a person who may be interested in the application under paragraph (1). *(Extent: E+W)*

(3 (Extent: E+W)) The court may at any time vary or discharge— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) the appointment of a receiver under article 21L, *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) an order under article 21M (powers of receivers appointed under article 21L), or *(Extent: E+W)*
- (c (Extent: E+W)) directions under this article. *(Extent: E+W)*

(4 (Extent: E+W)) Before exercising a power under paragraph (3) the court must give an opportunity to be heard to— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) the receiver; *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) the parties to the proceedings for the appointment of the receiver, for the order under article 21M or (as the case may be) for the directions under this article; *(Extent: E+W)*
- (c (Extent: E+W)) the parties to the proceedings for the interim freezing order concerned; *(Extent: E+W)*
- (d (Extent: E+W)) any person who may be affected by the court’s decision. *(Extent: E+W)*
