# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013, section 21J

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21J (Extent: E+W). Exclusions (Extent: E+W)[^M_F_6f5a60ae-0a79-45ea-f23d-57ee03aae285]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W)) The power to vary an interim freezing order includes (amongst other things) power to make exclusions as follows— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) power to exclude property from the order, and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) power, otherwise than by excluding property from the order, to make exclusions from the prohibition on dealing with the property to which the order applies. *(Extent: E+W)*

(2 (Extent: E+W)) Exclusions from the prohibition on dealing with the property to which the order applies (other than exclusions of property from the order) may also be made when the order is made. *(Extent: E+W)*

(3 (Extent: E+W)) An exclusion may (amongst other things) make provision for the purpose of enabling any person— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) to meet the person’s reasonable living expenses, or *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) to carry on any trade, business, profession or occupation. *(Extent: E+W)*

(4 (Extent: E+W)) An exclusion may be subject to conditions. *(Extent: E+W)*

(5 (Extent: E+W)) Where the court exercises the power to make an exclusion for the purposes of enabling a person to meet legal expenses that the person has incurred, or may incur, in respect of proceedings under this Order, it must ensure that the exclusion— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) is limited to reasonable legal expenses that the person has reasonably incurred or reasonably incurs, *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) specifies the total amount that may be released for legal expenses in pursuance of the exclusion, and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (c (Extent: E+W)) is made subject to the same conditions as would be the required conditions (see article 198 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Requests and Orders) Order 2005 (legal expenses excluded from freezing: required conditions)) if the order had been made under article 147 of that Order (application for property freezing order). *(Extent: E+W)*

(6 (Extent: E+W)) The court in deciding whether to make an exclusion for the purposes of enabling a person to meet legal expenses in respect of proceedings under this Order— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) must have regard to the desirability of the person being represented in any proceedings under this Part in which the person is a participant, and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) must disregard the possibility that legal representation of the person in any such proceedings might, were an exclusion not made *(Extent: E+W)* —[^key-6b669adab8ea2ebad233e8baa4950863] *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (i (Extent: E+W)) be made available under arrangements made for the purposes of Part 1 of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012, or *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (ii (Extent: E+W)) be funded by the Legal Services Agency Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)*

(7 (Extent: E+W)) If excluded property is not specified in the order it must be described in the order in general terms. *(Extent: E+W)*
