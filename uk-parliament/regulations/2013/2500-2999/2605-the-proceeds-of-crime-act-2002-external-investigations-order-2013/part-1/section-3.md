# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013, section 3

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Scope of Part 1[^c-8eadbea1b461eb3552934a10f99a30da]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) This Part has effect for the purpose of enabling the Director General of NCA , a relevant Director or (in relation to an unexplained wealth order or an interim freezing order[^c-fff7d07347008557ea057318bb5e4bbf] [^c-834562394c003d80c32e13eb89767c56]...) an enforcement authority to assist an external investigation by obtaining orders and warrants from the High Court.

(2) The powers conferred by this Part are exercisable in relation to a person or property subject to an external investigation[^c-1793cab34494870515fc306cf2fa059e] only if the Director General of NCA , the relevant Director or (in relation to an unexplained wealth order or an interim freezing order[^c-b1c1ca401ee53097ffb357f52b587a2b] [^c-eba1d7143eb97f23f39ca37c2d566c44]...) the enforcement authority believes paragraph (3) applies in relation to—

- (a) the country or territory of the overseas authority carrying out the external investigation, and
- (b) England and Wales or (if the powers are to be exercised in Northern Ireland) Northern Ireland.

(3) This paragraph applies if—

- (a) a criminal investigation in connection with the person or[^c-b4a658b9ae72e18b7aeafffe8986465d] property has not begun,
- (b) a criminal investigation in connection with the person or[^c-ba688719a57fb0e1205d3c59650a498b] property has begun but it is unlikely criminal proceedings will be brought, or
- (c) criminal proceedings brought in connection with the person or[^c-52a43e47f1e0df5167c81d522877b155] property have been concluded without an order having been made in relation to the person or property.

(4) Criminal proceedings have been concluded when—

- (a) the person against whom the criminal proceedings are brought is acquitted,
- (b) the person against whom the criminal proceedings are brought is pardoned,
- (c) the person against whom the criminal proceedings are brought is convicted and sentenced,
- (d) the person against whom the criminal proceedings are brought is convicted and the conviction is quashed,
- (e) the criminal proceedings are discontinued, or
- (f) any jury is discharged without a finding otherwise than in circumstances where the criminal proceedings are continued without a jury.
