# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (External Investigations) Order 2013, section 67

> Text as at 2025-04-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 67. Supplementary[^c-3a465544d457d813b53f68dc2498375b]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application for an account monitoring order may be made ex parte to a sheriff in chambers.

(2) An application to discharge or vary an account monitoring order may be made to the sheriff by—

- (a) the Scottish Ministers,
- (b) any person affected by the order.

(3) The sheriff may—

- (a) discharge the order,
- (b) vary the order.
