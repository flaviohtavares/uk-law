# The Tobacco Products (Amendment) Regulations 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Excise

The Tobacco Products (Amendment) Regulations 2013

Made

22nd October 2013

Laid before Parliament

23rd October 2013

Coming into force

1st January 2014

The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs[^f00001] make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8B(2) of the Tobacco Products Duty Act 1979[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Tobacco Products (Amendment) Regulations 2013 and come into force on 1st January 2014.

## 2. Amendment of the Tobacco Products Regulations 2001

The Tobacco Products Regulations 2001[^f00003] are amended as follows.

**3.** In regulation 23 (exceptions to the requirement to carry a fiscal mark) after regulation 23(2A)[^f00004], insert—

> (2B) Specified tobacco products that do not contain any tobacco are not required to carry a fiscal mark.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations come into force on 1st January 2014 and amend regulation 23 of the Tobacco Products Regulations 2001 ([S.I. 2001/1712](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1712)) which prescribes exceptions to the requirement to carry a fiscal mark for the purposes of the Tobacco Products Duty Act [1979 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/7).

The exemption of herbal smoking products from excise duty was removed (except for products exclusively used for medical purposes) by section 182 of the Finance Act [2013 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/29). Regulation 3 creates an exception to the need to carry a fiscal mark for specified tobacco products that do not contain any tobacco.

A Tax Information and Impact Note was published on 11th December 2012 alongside the draft Finance Bill clauses for 2013 and is available on the HMRC website at http://www.hmrc.gov.uk/thelibrary/tiins.htm. It remains an accurate summary of the impacts that apply to this instrument.
