# The Registered Designs (Isle of Man) Order 2013, section 4

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Revocation[^c-0ceb9fb5c78b77b78e6b51e8da7ac0fd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Registered Designs (Isle of Man) Order 2001[^f00003] is revoked.

(2) Nothing in paragraph (1) affects the further modifications of the 1949 Act having effect under Schedule 2 to that Order in relation to registrations under that Act made, and applications for registration made but not determined, before the coming into force of that Order.
