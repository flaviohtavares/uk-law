# The Registered Designs (Isle of Man) Order 2013, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-8ed43873a2caecc4d0829859be6b30b8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Order—

- (a) “the 1949 Act” means the Registered Designs Act 1949;
- (b) “the 1988 Act” means the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988.
