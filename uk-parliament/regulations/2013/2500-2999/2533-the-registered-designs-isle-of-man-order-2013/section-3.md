# The Registered Designs (Isle of Man) Order 2013, section 3

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Modification of the 1949 and 1988 Acts in their application to the Isle of Man[^c-d818f433d862868e207b0d359b167850]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The 1949 Act shall have effect in the Isle of Man subject to the modifications specified in Schedule 1 to this Order.

(2) Part IV (registered designs) of, and Schedules 3 and 4 to, the 1988 Act shall extend to the Isle of Man subject to the exceptions and modifications specified in Schedule 2 to this Order.
