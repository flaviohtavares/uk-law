# The Registered Designs (Isle of Man) Order 2013, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Modifications of provisions of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 as it has effect in the Isle of Man

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 3(2)

**1.**[^key-98f204d3abdfc3ef35efbf97dfa783a2] Any reference to an Act of Parliament (including the 1949 Act and the 1988 Act) or to a provision of such an Act shall be construed as a reference to that Act or provision as it has effect in the Isle of Man.

**2.**[^key-823dd3a37181dfc615cc8e033b88935d] Omit sections 265 and 268.

**3.**[^key-0cee8ca70ffb8fc22b5b0f082926495e] In Schedule 3, omit paragraph 1, 2, 3(4), 4, 6, 9 and 31(2) and (5).

**4.**[^key-259d728f5dc024f86347e554c377b5e5] Schedule 4 shall have effect as if the text of the 1949 Act contained therein were the text of that Act as modified by Schedule 1 to this Order.
