# The Localism Act 2011 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 2931 (C. 119)

Town And Country Planning, England

The Localism Act 2011 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2013

Made

19th November 2013

The Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 240(2) of the Localism Act 2011[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Localism Act (Commencement No. 3) Order 2013.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 17th December 2013

Section 122 of the Localism Act 2011, so far as not already in force, comes into force on 17th December 2013.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Kris Hopkins

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

19th November 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force, so far as not already in force, section 122 of the Localism Act (consultation before applying for planning permission) on 17th December 2013.
