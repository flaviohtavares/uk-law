# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 7) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 2596

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 7) Order 2013

Made

9th October 2013

Coming into force

10th October 2013

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 9th day of October 2013

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 7) Order 2013 and comes into force on 10th October 2013.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — Persons appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 10th October 2013

Article 2

- Kevin Joseph Flanagan
- John Gregg
- Adam Mark Higgins
- Lisa Jane Moore
- Christopher David Smith
- Christine Lynn Williams

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
