# The Trade Marks (Isle of Man) Order 2013, section 3

> Text as at 2025-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Revocation[^c-0ba41014ec19444cc84f3c7ee4dc1d4b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following instruments are revoked―

- (a) the Trade Marks Act 1994 (Isle of Man) Order 1996[^f00002];
- (b) the Trade Marks Act 1994 (Isle of Man) (Amendment) Order 2002[^f00003];
- (c) the Trade Marks Act 1994 (Isle of Man) (Amendment) Order 2004[^f00004].
