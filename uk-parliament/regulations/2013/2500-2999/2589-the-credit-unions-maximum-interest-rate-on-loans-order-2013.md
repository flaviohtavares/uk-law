# The Credit Unions (Maximum Interest Rate on Loans) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 2589

Credit Unions

The Credit Unions (Maximum Interest Rate on Loans) Order 2013

Made

8th October 2013

Laid before Parliament

9th October 2013

Coming into force

1st April 2014

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 11(5) and (7) of the Credit Unions Act 1979[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Credit Unions (Maximum Interest Rate on Loans) Order 2013 and comes into force on 1st April 2014.

## 2. Maximum interest rate

The rate specified for the purposes of section 11(5) of the Credit Unions Act 1979 is three per cent per month.

## 3. Revocation

This Order revokes the Credit Unions (Maximum Interest Rate on Loans) Order 2006[^f00002].

Desmond Swayne

Stephen Crabb

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

9th October 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the limit on the interest which a credit union may charge on loans made by it under the Credit Unions Act [1979 (c.34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/34) from 2% to 3% per month.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the costs of business or the voluntary sector is foreseen.
