# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 2013, section 10

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 10. Former stakeholder pension schemes

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The information mentioned in paragraph (2) must be given in accordance with this regulation where a scheme is removed from the register of stakeholder pension schemes kept under section 2 of the 1999 Act [^c2193088] (registration of stakeholder pension schemes).

(2) The information is a statement that the scheme—

- (a) has been removed from the register,
- (b) is no longer a stakeholder pension scheme, and
- (c) is required to begin winding up.

(3) The information must be given to members except excluded persons.

(4) The information must be given no more than two weeks after[^c-47375ac19a3415d03e24aeb2b921ef2c] the trustees or managers of the scheme are notified that the scheme has been removed from the register.
