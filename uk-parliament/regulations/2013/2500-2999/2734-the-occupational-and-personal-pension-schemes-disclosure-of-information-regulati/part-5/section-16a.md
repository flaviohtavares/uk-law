# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 2013, section 16A

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 16A. Statements of benefits: cash balance benefits[^c-1d86681c84c4ef3ee4381e0a9ec3e5f7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The information mentioned in paragraph (2) (in addition to the information required under regulation 16 (statements of benefits: non money purchase benefits)) must be given to any member in accordance with this regulation where—

- (a) the member has rights to cash balance benefits;
- (b) the member requests that information; and
- (c) information has not been given to that member under this regulation in the 12 months before that request.

(2) The information is that listed in paragraphs 6A, 9 to 14, 16A and 17 of Schedule 6 (statements of benefits: money purchase benefits).

(3) The information must be given as soon as practicable but no more than two months after the date the request is made.
