# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 2013, section 1

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement, expiry, revocations and amendments

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 2013 and come into force on 6th April 2014.

(2) On 6th April 2015 the following provisions cease to have effect—

- (a) paragraphs 2 and 3 of Schedule 6, and
- (b) paragraph 11(1)(b) and (2) of Schedule 8.

(3) The following regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Personal Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 1987 [^c2193075], and
- (b) the Occupational Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 1996 [^c2193076].

(4) The amendments made by Schedule 9 have effect.
