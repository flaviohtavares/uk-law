# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 2013, section 13

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 13. Other information to be given on request

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The information listed in Part 3 of Schedule 3 must be given to a relevant person in accordance with this regulation where the relevant person makes a request for the information.

(2) The information must be given within two months of the date the request is made.

(3) The information must be given in accordance with regulation 29.
