> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Abolition of the Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council[^key-84524951b1128d45dfa01a2001fb8ba6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council is abolished.

(2) The Schedule (which makes consequential provision etc) has effect.
