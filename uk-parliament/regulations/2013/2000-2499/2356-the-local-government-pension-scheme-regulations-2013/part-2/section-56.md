# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 2013, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/2356
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Accounts and audit

*Extent: E+W*

(1) After any of its pension funds has been audited, an administering authority must immediately send copies of the following to each body whose employees are active members—

- (a) a summary of the revenue account and balance sheet of the fund; and
- (b) any report by the auditor.

(2) The pension input period for the purposes of section 238 of the Finance Act 2004 [^c21900371] is the year ending on 31st March 2015 and each year ending on 31st March after that year.
