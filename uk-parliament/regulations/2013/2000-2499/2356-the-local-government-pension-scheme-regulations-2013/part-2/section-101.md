# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 2013, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/2356
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 101. Effect of acceptance of transfer value

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a transfer value has been accepted under regulation 100 (inward transfer of pension rights), the administering authority must credit the active member's pension account with the appropriate amount of earned pension.

(2)[^key-de6fa1da5208c81cca5af934a84cd283] The appropriate amount of earned pension for the purposes of paragraph (1) is calculated—

- (a) in the case of a Club Transfer, in accordance with the Club Memorandum; and
- (b) in any other case in accordance with actuarial guidance issued by the Secretary of State.
