## 117. Elected Members[^key-8701481b966d53af56dd7684a7d1c5ab]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), these Regulations apply to elected members in respect of their elected membership with the modifications set out in Schedule 4.

(2) The Secretary of State may, by written direction, modify these Regulations as they apply to elected members for the purposes set out in [section 25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/25/section/25)[(8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/25/section/25) of the [Public Service Pensions Act 2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/25/contents).
