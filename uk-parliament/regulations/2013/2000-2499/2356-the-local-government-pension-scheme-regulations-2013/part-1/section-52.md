# The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 2013, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/2356

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Pension debits

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Administering authorities shall have regard to actuarial guidance issued by the Secretary of State as to reduction of benefits payable under these Regulations in consequence of a pension debit.

(2) An administering authority shall make such adjustments to a member's pension accounts as are required to give effect to a pension debit.
