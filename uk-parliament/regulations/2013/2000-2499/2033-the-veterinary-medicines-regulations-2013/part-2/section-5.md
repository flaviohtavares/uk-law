# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2013, section 5

> Text as at 2024-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 5. Manufacture of veterinary medicinal products

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The holder of a marketing authorisation must ensure that every stage in the manufacture of the veterinary medicinal product is carried out by the manufacturer specified in the marketing authorisation[^f00012].

(2) Schedule 2 (the manufacture of veterinary medicinal products) has effect.

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d78c82a844e705fdd26945bcfbfcef1d] “Manufacture” includes any part of the manufacture of a veterinary medicinal product until the finished product is ready for sale in its final form as specified in the marketing authorisation but does not include the manufacture of an ingredient or breaking open the package of a veterinary medicinal product *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d78c82a844e705fdd26945bcfbfcef1d][^f00013]. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d78c82a844e705fdd26945bcfbfcef1d]
