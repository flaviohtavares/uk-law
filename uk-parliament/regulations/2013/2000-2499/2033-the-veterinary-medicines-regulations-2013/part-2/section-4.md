# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2013, section 4

> Text as at 2024-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 4. Placing a veterinary medicinal product on the market[^M_E_12dd41d7-649f-494c-e077-35223270abc3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-2f2e825a26c4d5db9601a0952f2926f7] No person may place a veterinary medicinal product on the market unless the Secretary of State has—

- (a) as regards a product to which Schedule 1B applies, issued a QNIG certificate in respect of that product;
- (b) otherwise, granted a marketing authorisation in respect of that product.

(2) No person may certify data in relation to an application for a marketing authorisation or in relation to an existing marketing authorisation if they know that those data are false, or do not believe that they are accurate.

(3) Schedule 1 (marketing authorisations) has effect.

(4)[^c-3e524acc6e532eda508e532ee51585bf] Schedule 1A (converted EU marketing authorisations) has effect.

(5)[^c-ac74261c25807f9b6a727040eec6d10e] Schedule 1B (Northern Ireland qualifying good marketing authorisations) has effect.

## 4. Placing a veterinary medicinal product on the market[^M_E_69f6ce19-437d-4951-fdfd-4991d7ebc0d6]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) No person may place a veterinary medicinal product on the market unless that product has been granted a marketing authorisation by the Secretary of State or the Agency.

(2) No person may certify data in relation to an application for a marketing authorisation or in relation to an existing marketing authorisation if they know that those data are false, or do not believe that they are accurate.

(3) Schedule 1 (marketing authorisations) has effect.
