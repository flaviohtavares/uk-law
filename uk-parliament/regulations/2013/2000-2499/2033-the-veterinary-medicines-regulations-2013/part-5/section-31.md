# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2013, section 31

> Text as at 2024-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 31. Exports[^M_E_359f4316-96ba-4cc9-d329-de4fbedf464e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) No person may export a veterinary medicinal product for use in another country[^c-513a8a74a4c19a6fcc78db7ab2986617] unless the veterinary medicinal product may be lawfully supplied or administered in that country.

(2) If a veterinary medicinal product has been manufactured in accordance with a marketing authorisation, or if a product without a marketing authorisation has been manufactured under a manufacturing authorisation, and the product is intended for export [^M_F_cfa011ea-4dbd-4a5a-a14c-179923ab74cc]..., the Secretary of State must, at the request of the exporter or the competent authorities of the country to which it is being exported, provide a certificate that (as the case may be)—[^c-e8782317a0792f352697bfbf93d565a6]

- (a) the manufacturer holds a manufacturing authorisation;
- (b) the manufacturer holds a certificate of good manufacturing practice; or
- (c) the product has been marketed under a marketing authorisation.

(3) When issuing the certificate the Secretary of State must take account of any relevant administrative arrangements in relation to the form and content of such certificates which are in existence between the United Kingdom and the country to which the product is to be exported[^c-fa631959d363f4c47a893186ef7236e9].

(4) If the veterinary medicinal product is authorised in the United Kingdom the Secretary of State must ensure that the exporter or the competent authorities of the importing[^c-f0ab22a3fc2abcc5e62d4de4268f6220] country has access to the summary of product characteristics.

## 31. Exports[^M_E_7962c1aa-2eeb-4aaf-9d7e-9465dd1f9cd6]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) No person may export a veterinary medicinal product for use in another country[^c-f98adbf212176bda61b7baee099b1982] unless the veterinary medicinal product may be lawfully supplied or administered in that country.

(2) If a veterinary medicinal product has been manufactured in accordance with a marketing authorisation, or if a product without a marketing authorisation has been manufactured under a manufacturing authorisation, and the product is intended for export outside the European Union, the Secretary of State must, at the request of the exporter or the competent authorities of the country to which it is being exported, provide a certificate to that effect.

(3) When issuing the certificate the Secretary of State must take account of the model certificates issued by the World Health Organization[^f00014].

(4) If the veterinary medicinal product is authorised in the United Kingdom the Secretary of State must ensure that the exporter or the competent authorities of the third country has access to the summary of product characteristics.
