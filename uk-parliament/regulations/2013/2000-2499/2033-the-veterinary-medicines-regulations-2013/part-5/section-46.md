# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2013, section 46

> Text as at 2024-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 46. Review[^M_E_b6f8864a-5406-4dad-91f6-799663ff0ef2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Before the end of each review period, the Secretary of State must—

- (a) carry out a review of these Regulations other than the fees provisions;
- (b) set out the conclusions of the review in a report; and
- (c) publish the report.

(2)[^c-e28e818ee767caf9151877db87bb21f6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-6baca5b007795fb9fe88514c6f13f4bd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The report must in particular—

- (a) set out the objectives intended to be achieved by the regulatory system established by these Regulations, other than the fees provisions;
- (b) assess the extent to which those objectives are achieved; and
- (c) assess whether those objectives remain appropriate and, if so, the extent to which they could be achieved with a system that imposes less regulation.

(5) In this regulation—

- (a) “review period” means the period of five years ending on 31st December 2028[^c-5ec174858e747748aa23bc04dfa142cc], and, subject to paragraph (6), each successive period of five years thereafter; and
- (b) “the fees provisions” means regulation 16 and Schedule 7.

(6) If a report under this regulation is published before the last day of the review period to which it relates, the following review period is to begin with the day on which that report is published.

## 46. Review[^M_E_f3ab0e61-0c49-47e4-ab74-ed9e2dffab53]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Before the end of each review period, the Secretary of State must—

- (a) carry out a review of these Regulations other than the fees provisions;
- (b) set out the conclusions of the review in a report; and
- (c) publish the report.

(2) In carrying out the review the Secretary of State must, so far as is reasonable, have regard to how the EU instruments, or provisions of EU instruments, to which this regulation applies are implemented in [^c-68c0f0afe4d238f4430eeb354cbf9743]... member States.

(3) The EU instruments, and provisions of EU instruments, to which this regulation applies are—

- (a) Council [Directive 90/167/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1990/0167) laying down conditions governing the preparation, placing on the market and use of medicated feedingstuffs in the Community, so far it is not superseded by Regulation [(EC) No 183/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/0183)[^f00023];
- (b) Commission [Directive 91/412/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1991/0412) laying down the principles and guidelines of good manufacturing practice for veterinary medicinal products[^f00024];
- (c) [Directive 2001/82/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2001/0082) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the Community code relating to veterinary medicinal products[^f00025];
- (d) Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety, in so far as it applies to veterinary medicinal products used in feedingstuffs;
- (e) Regulation [(EC) No 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2003/1831) of the European Parliament and of the Council on additives for use in animal nutrition, in so far as it applies to veterinary medicinal products used in feedingstuffs;
- (f)[^c-99a51bbd73b88d54d9ab68c1918d65a8] Regulation (EU) 2017/625;
- (g) Regulation [(EC) No 183/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2005/0183) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down requirements for feed hygiene, in so far as it applies to veterinary medicinal products used in feedingstuffs;
- (h) [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1234/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/1234)[^f00026];
- (i) Regulation [(EC) No 470/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/0470) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00027];
- (j) Article 8 of Regulation [(EC) No 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/0767) of the European Parliament and of the Council, and Articles 15 and 17 of that Regulation as they refer to the labelling requirements for feedingstuffs containing specified feed additives[^f00028]; and
- (k) Commission Regulation (EU) No 37/2010[^f00029].

(4) The report must in particular—

- (a) set out the objectives intended to be achieved by the regulatory system established by these Regulations, other than the fees provisions;
- (b) assess the extent to which those objectives are achieved; and
- (c) assess whether those objectives remain appropriate and, if so, the extent to which they could be achieved with a system that imposes less regulation.

(5) In this regulation—

- (a) “review period” means the period of five years beginning with the day on which these Regulations come into force, and, subject to paragraph (6), each successive period of five years thereafter; and
- (b) “the fees provisions” means regulation 16 and Schedule 7.

(6) If a report under this regulation is published before the last day of the review period to which it relates, the following review period is to begin with the day on which that report is published.
