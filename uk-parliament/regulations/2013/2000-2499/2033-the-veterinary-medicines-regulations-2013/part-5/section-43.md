# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2013, section 43

> Text as at 2024-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, NI.

## 43. Offence

*Extent: E+W+S*

It is an offence[^f00017] to fail to comply with—

- (a) regulation 4(1) or (2);
- (b) regulation 5(1);
- (c) regulation 7(2), (3), (4) or (5);
- (d) regulation 8;
- (e) regulation 9(1);
- (f) regulation 10(1), (1A)[^c-d4e123a1e7f127069e9f9ad12fb31576] or (2);
- (fa)[^c-505dcd78f31fda769efb9f98d48bf324] regulation 10A(1), (3) or (5);
- (g) regulation 11(1) or (4) or (6)[^c-d8b7a84149b26ec7b9cae145abea44de];
- (h) regulation 13;
- (i) regulation 17;
- (j) regulation 18
- (k) regulation 19
- (l) regulation 20
- (m) regulation 21
- (n) regulation 22
- (o) regulation 23
- (p) regulation 24;
- (pa)[^c-795801b9c1296d9e1aa03a17878c9817] regulation 24A(1)
- (q) regulation 25(1);
- (r) regulation 26(1), (2) or (6);
- (s) regulation 27(1);
- (t) regulation 31(1);
- (u) regulation 37;
- (v) an improvement notice issued under regulation 38; or
- (w) regulation 41(3).

## 43. Offence

*Extent: N.I.*

It is an offence[^f00017] to fail to comply with—

- (a) regulation 4(1) or (2);
- (b) regulation 5(1);
- (c) regulation 7(2), (3), (4) or (5);
- (d) regulation 8;
- (e) regulation 9(1);
- (f) regulation 10(1) or (2);
- (g) regulation 11(1);
- (h) regulation 13;
- (i) regulation 17;
- (j) regulation 18
- (k) regulation 19
- (l) regulation 20
- (m) regulation 21
- (n) regulation 22
- (o) regulation 23
- (p) regulation 24;
- (q) regulation 25(1);
- (r) regulation 26(1), (2) or (6);
- (s) regulation 27(1);
- (t) regulation 31(1);
- (u) regulation 37;
- (v) an improvement notice issued under regulation 38; or
- (w) regulation 41(3).
