# The Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2013, section 3

> Text as at 2024-05-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Products to which these Regulations do not apply

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to a veterinary medicinal product based on radio-active isotopes.

(2) They do not apply in relation to a product intended for administration in the course of a procedure licensed under the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986[^f00011], except that, if the animals are to be put into the human food chain, the only products that may be administered to the animals are—

- (a) authorised veterinary medicinal products administered in accordance with their marketing authorisation; or
- (b) products administered in accordance with an animal test certificate granted under paragraph 9 of Schedule 4.
