# The Disabled Persons’ Parking Badges Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 2202 (C. 91)

Road Traffic

The Disabled Persons’ Parking Badges Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2013

Made

3rd September 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7 of the Disabled Persons’ Parking Badges Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Disabled Persons’ Parking Badges Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2013.

## 2. Appointed day

The appointed day for the coming into force of the Disabled Persons’ Parking Badges Act 2013 is 8th October 2013.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Norman Baker

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

3rd September 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order commences the Disabled Persons’ Parking Badges Act 2013 on 8th October 2013.
