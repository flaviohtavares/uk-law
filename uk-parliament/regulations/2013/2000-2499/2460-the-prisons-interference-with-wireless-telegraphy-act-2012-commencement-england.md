# The Prisons (Interference with Wireless Telegraphy) Act 2012 (Commencement) (England and Wales) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 2460 (C. 98)

Prisons, England And Wales

The Prisons (Interference with Wireless Telegraphy) Act 2012 (Commencement) (England and Wales) Order 2013

Made

24th September 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 5(3) of the Prisons (Interference with Wireless Telegraphy) Act 2012[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and extent

This Order may be cited as the Prisons (Interference with Wireless Telegraphy) Act 2012 (Commencement) (England and Wales) Order 2013.

**2.** This Order extends to England and Wales only.

## 3. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of sections 1 to 4 of the Prisons (Interference with Wireless Telegraphy) Act 2012 is 21st October 2013.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Jeremy Wright

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

24th September 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings sections 1 to 4 of the Prisons (Interference with Wireless Telegraphy) Act [2012 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/20) into force in respect of England and Wales on 21st October 2013.
