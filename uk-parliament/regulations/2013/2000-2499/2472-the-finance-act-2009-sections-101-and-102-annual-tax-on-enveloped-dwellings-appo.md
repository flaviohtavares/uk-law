# The Finance Act 2009, Sections 101 and 102 (Annual Tax on Enveloped Dwellings) (Appointed Day) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 2472 (C. 100)

Annual Tax On Enveloped Dwellings

The Finance Act 2009, Sections 101 and 102 (Annual Tax on Enveloped Dwellings) (Appointed Day) Order 2013

Made

26th September 2013

The Treasury make this Order exercising the powers in sections 104(3) and 104(4) of the Finance Act 2009[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2009, Sections 101 and 102 (Annual Tax on Enveloped Dwellings) (Appointed Day) Order 2013.

## 2. Appointed day

1 October 2013 is appointed as the day on which sections 101 and 102 of the Finance Act 2009[^f00002] come into force for the purposes of annual tax on enveloped dwellings[^f00003] and penalties assessed in relation to that tax.

Stephen Crabb

Anne Milton

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

26th September 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order concerns annual tax on enveloped dwellings and related penalties. For these purposes, it brings into force on 1 October 2013 the late payment interest rules for sums due to HM Revenue and Customs, and the repayment interest rules for sums to be paid by them[^f00004]. The first payments of the tax are due on 31 October 2013[^f00005].

A Tax Information and Impact Note has not been prepared for this instrument as it gives effect to previously announced policy and is an appointed day order.
