# The Financial Services Act 2012 (Consequential Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Order 2013, section 4

> Text as at 2026-07-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Amendment of subordinate legislation (references to Part IV of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000)[^key-5ef6b70598944b9f7ac82e25df37ca39]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In subordinate legislation for references to Part IV of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^f00003] (however expressed) substitute references to Part 4A of that Act.
