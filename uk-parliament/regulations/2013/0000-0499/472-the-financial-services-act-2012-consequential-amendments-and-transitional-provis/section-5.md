# The Financial Services Act 2012 (Consequential Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Order 2013, section 5

> Text as at 2026-07-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Amendments of subordinate legislation (references to Part X of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000)[^c-21c3467a73486483c0b6998da7f362b5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In subordinate legislation for references to Part X of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (however expressed) substitute references to Part 9A of that Act.
