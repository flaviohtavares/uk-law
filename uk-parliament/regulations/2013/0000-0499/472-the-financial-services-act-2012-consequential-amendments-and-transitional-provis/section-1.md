# The Financial Services Act 2012 (Consequential Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Order 2013, section 1

> Text as at 2026-07-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-1583243eb9389278ad2164998a3c0a62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Financial Services Act 2012 (Consequential Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Order 2013 and comes into force on 1st April 2013.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Financial Services Act 2012;
- “subordinate legislation” has the meaning given by the Interpretation Act 1978[^f00002].
