# The National Treatment Agency (Abolition) and the Health and Social Care Act 2012 (Consequential, Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2013, section 2

> Text as at 2020-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**the Agency**” means the National Treatment Agency established by article 2 of the National Treatment Agency (Establishment and Constitution) Order 2001 [^c2155696]; and
- “**the transfer date**” means 1st April 2013.
