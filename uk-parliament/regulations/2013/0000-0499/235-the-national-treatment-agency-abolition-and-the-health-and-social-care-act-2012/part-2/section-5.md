# The National Treatment Agency (Abolition) and the Health and Social Care Act 2012 (Consequential, Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2013, section 5

> Text as at 2020-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Transfer of property and liabilities

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any property [^c2155697] held by the Agency immediately before the transfer date is on the transfer date transferred to the Secretary of State.

(2) Any criminal liabilities of the Agency are on the transfer date transferred to the National Health Service Litigation Authority [^c2155698].

(3) All other liabilities of the Agency are on the transfer date transferred to the Secretary of State.

## Annotations

[^c2155697]: Marginal citation: “**Property**” includes rights; *see* [section 275(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/235/section/275/1) of the National Health Service Act 2006.

[^c2155698]: Marginal citation: The National Health Service Litigation Authority is a Special Health Authority established by [S.I. 1995/2800](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2800), to which there are amendments not relevant to this Order.
