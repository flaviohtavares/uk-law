# The National Treatment Agency (Abolition) and the Health and Social Care Act 2012 (Consequential, Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2013, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2020-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — The National Treatment Agency: amendments and revocations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 9

### Instruments amended

#### 1. The National Health Service Trusts (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 1990

In the National Health Service Trusts (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 1990 [^c2155706], in regulation 11 (disqualification of appointment for chairman and non-executive directors), omit paragraph (5A).

#### 2. The Health Service Commissioner for England (Special Health Authorities) Order 2005

In the Schedule to the Health Service Commissioner for England (Special Health Authorities) Order 2005 (Special Health Authorities designated as subject to investigation) [^c2155707], omit the entry for the National Treatment Agency.

### Instruments revoked

#### 3. The National Treatment Agency (Establishment and Constitution) Order 2001

The National Treatment Agency (Establishment and Constitution) Order 2001 [^c2155708] is revoked.

#### 4. The National Treatment Agency Regulations 2001

The National Treatment Agency Regulations 2001 [^c2155709] are revoked.

#### 5. The National Treatment Agency (Amendment) Regulations 2001

The National Treatment Agency (Amendment) Regulations 2001 [^c2155710] are revoked

#### 6. The National Treatment Agency (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 2003

The National Treatment Agency (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 2003 [^c2155711] is revoked.
