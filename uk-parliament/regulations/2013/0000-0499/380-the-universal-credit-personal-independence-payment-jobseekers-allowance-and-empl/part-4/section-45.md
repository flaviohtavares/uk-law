# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations 2013, section 45

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. Time of payment: general provision

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to the other provisions of this Part, benefit is to be paid in accordance with an award as soon as is reasonably practicable after the award has been made.
