# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations 2013, SCHEDULE 1

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — POWERS EXERCISED IN MAKING THESE REGULATIONS

*Extent: E+W+S*

Preamble

**1.** The following provisions of the Administration Act—

- (a) section 1(1), (1C) [^c2167243];
- (b) section 5(1)(a), (b), (c), (d), (g), (i), (j), (k), (l), (m), (p), (q), (1A), (2A), (2B), (2C), (3B) [^c2167244];
- (c) section 7A(2)(b) [^c2167245];
- (d) section 15A(2) [^c2167246];
- (e) section 111A(1A)(d), (1B)(d), (1D)(c), (1E)(c) [^c2167247];
- (f) section 112(1A)(d), (1B)(d), (1C)(c), (1D)(c) [^c2167248];
- (g) section 189(1) and (5) to (6) [^c2167249];
- (h) section 191 [^c2167250].

**2.** Paragraph 7A of Schedule 2 to the Abolition of Domestic Rates etc. (Scotland) Act 1987 [^c2167251].

**3.** Paragraph 6 of Schedule 4 to the Local Government Finance Act 1988 [^c2167252].

**4.** Section 24(2)(b), (c) and (d) and section 30 of the Criminal Justice Act 1991 [^c2167253].

**5.** Section 43(2) of the 1991 Act [^c2167254].

**6.** Paragraphs 1 and 6(2)(b) of Schedule 4 and paragraph 6 of Schedule 8 to, the Local Government Finance Act 1992 [^c2167255].

**7.** Sections 32 and 92 of, and paragraph 3(1)(a), (b), (2)(a), (b) and (c) of Schedule 1 to the 2012 Act [^c2167256].
