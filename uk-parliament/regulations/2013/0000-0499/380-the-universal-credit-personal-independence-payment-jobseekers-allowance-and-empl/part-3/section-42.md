# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations 2013, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-09-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/380
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Notification for purposes of sections 111A and 112 of the Administration Act

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulations 43 to 44 below prescribe the person to whom, and manner in which, a change of circumstances must be notified for the purposes of sections 111A(1A) to (1G) [^c2167220] and 112(1A) to (1F) [^c2167221] of the Administration Act (offences relating to failure to notify a change of circumstances).

## Annotations

[^c2167220]: Marginal citation: Section 111A was inserted by section 13 of the [Social Security Administration (Fraud) Act 1997 (c.47)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/47) and subsections (1A) to (1G) were inserted by section 16(1)(b) and (2) of the [Social Security Fraud Act 2001 (c.11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/11).

[^c2167221]: Marginal citation: Subsections (1A) to (1F) of section 112 were inserted by section 16(3) of the Social Security Fraud Act 2001.
