# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations 2013, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-09-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/380
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Duration of awards

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A claim for universal credit is to be treated as made for an indefinite period and any award of universal credit on that claim is to be made for an indefinite period.

(2) The provisions of Schedule 4 are to have effect in relation to claims for a jobseeker's allowance made during periods connected with public holidays.
