# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations 2013, section 27

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27. Time within which a claim for personal independence payment is to be made

*Extent: E+W+S*

A claim for personal independence payment must be made on the first day of the period in respect of which the claim is made.
