# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations 2013, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-09-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/380
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 30. Amendment of claim

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person who has made a claim for benefit may amend it at any time before a determination has been made on the claim by notice in writing received at an appropriate office, by telephone call to a telephone number specified by the Secretary of State or in such other manner as the Secretary of State may decide or accept.

(2) Any claim amended in accordance with paragraph (1) may be treated as if it had been so amended in the first instance.
