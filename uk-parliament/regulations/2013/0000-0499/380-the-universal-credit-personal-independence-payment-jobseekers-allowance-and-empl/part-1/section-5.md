# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations 2013, section 5

## 5. Disapplication of section 1(1A) of the Administration Act

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 1(1A) [^c2167208] of the Administration Act (requirements in respect of a national insurance number) is not to apply to a child or a qualifying young person in respect of whom universal credit is claimed.

## Annotations

[^c2167208]: Marginal citation: Section 1(1A) of the Administration Act was inserted by section 19 of the [Social Security Administration (Fraud) Act 1997 (c.47)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/47).
