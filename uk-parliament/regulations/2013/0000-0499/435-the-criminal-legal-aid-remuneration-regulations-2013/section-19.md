# The Criminal Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013, section 19

> Text as at 2026-07-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Amount of interim payments in cases awaiting determination of fees

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where entitlement to an interim payment arises under regulation 18, the amount payable is 40% of the total claim for fees, less any sum already paid.

(2) Regulations 28 to 30 do not apply to an interim payment under this regulation.
