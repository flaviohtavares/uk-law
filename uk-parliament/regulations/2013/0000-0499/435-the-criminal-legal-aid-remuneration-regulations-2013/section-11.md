# The Criminal Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013, section 11

> Text as at 2026-07-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Proceedings for contempt

*Extent: E+W*

Where representation is provided in proceedings referred to in section 14(g) of the Act (proceedings for contempt in the face of a court), the Lord Chancellor may only pay remuneration for services in accordance with Schedules 1, 2 and 3.
