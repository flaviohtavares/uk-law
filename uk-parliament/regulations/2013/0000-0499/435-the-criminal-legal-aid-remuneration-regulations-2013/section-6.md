> Text as at 2026-07-28.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Proceedings in the Court of Appeal

*Extent: E+W*

Claims for fees by representatives in proceedings in the Court of Appeal must be made and determined in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 3 to these Regulations.
