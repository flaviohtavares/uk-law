# The Universal Credit Regulations 2013, section 112

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/376

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 112. Application of ESA or JSA sanctions to universal credit

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 11 has effect in relation to persons who are, or have been, entitled to an employment and support allowance or a jobseeker's allowance and who are, or become, entitled to universal credit.
