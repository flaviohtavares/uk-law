# The Universal Credit Regulations 2013, section 108

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/376
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 108. Suspension of a reduction where fraud penalty applies

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A reduction in the amount of an award under section 26 or 27 of the Act is to be suspended for any period during which the provisions of section 6B, 7 or 9 of the Social Security Fraud Act 2001 [^c2165971] apply to the award.

(2) The reduction ceases to have effect on the day on which that period begins and begins again on the day after that period ends.

## Annotations

[^c2165971]: Marginal citation: [2001 c.11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/11). Section 6B was inserted by section 24(1) of the Welfare Reform Act 2009.
