## 27. Award to include LCWRA element[^key-20865576447d944befd8d4c74851503d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-bf61d5ebd7907c4753b6ff815470921a] An award of universal credit is to include an amount in respect of the fact that a claimant has limited capability for work and work-related activity (“the LCWRA element”).

(2) The amount of that element is[^key-93134fadc9d48457bf2890d3eb6c31a0] given in the table in regulation 36 by reference to whether the claimant is—[^key-518bb3373ef6299e621640453c29f4d4]

- (a) a pre-2026 claimant,
- (b) a severe conditions criteria claimant,
- (c) terminally ill, or
- (d) any other claimant.

(3)[^c2190870][^c2190872][^c2190873][^key-61d87546e8ada704a8bc05b5a26cbd82][^key-d94aa2d3a11f91a6e6939f2ca7ec1a22][^key-91f88f5a233bada10358462caf1fadf0][^key-6c43f6aca946f4135c0f1a365e122bb3][^key-44ff4bdafbb8444f6e06bf114c7cee17][^key-d9ce3d243628c2c331ef1429e531fa7a][^key-0a4a75f151f29e19dd6d845e3521f34d][^M_C_b91707c5-9db6-4894-a80f-123b87970721][^M_C_0a9fd43e-7d05-4c8a-a8aa-ad120ce10cac][^M_C_64807adb-4938-4210-a851-9c410bdf24f7][^key-4abbec1becfc369dd2f8e52fb51d6ad0] Whether a claimant has limited capability for [^key-ba9ae6d24236a90d6acc408d61892fb5]... work and work-related activity or is a severe conditions criteria claimant[^key-bfb9592b67e9da4a3f9891ee45e50cdf] is determined in accordance with Part 5.

(4)[^key-b19f0b9670451bad64ef7cceded50300] In the case of joint claimants, where each of them has limited capability for work and work-related activity,

- (a)[^key-85aa19a6fe9273b414e0e0483636f364] the award is only to include one LCWRA element , and[^key-9ec526b8502eaf22658bb3a53059868e]
- (b) where the LCWRA element for each of them is a different amount, the LCWRA element to be included is the higher amount.
