## 79. Circumstances where the benefit cap applies[^key-f6847fd62f45bf08dc562162f1046d4a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Unless regulation 82 or 83 applies, the benefit cap applies where the welfare benefits to which a single person or couple is entitled during the reference period exceed the relevant amount determined under regulation 80A (relevant amount)[^key-5abb37132478452865d8f9afc4eb4a11].

(2) The reference period for the purposes of the benefit cap is the assessment period for an award of universal credit.
