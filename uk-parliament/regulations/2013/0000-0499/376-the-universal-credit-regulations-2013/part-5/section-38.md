# The Universal Credit Regulations 2013, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/376

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 38. Introduction[^c-573f69329725f81695aeb010db67b39b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The question whether a claimant has limited capability for work, or for work and work-related activity, or is a severe conditions criteria claimant,[^c-5052d33f296ca47df51483518c1434e5] is to be determined for the purposes of the Act and these Regulations in accordance with this Part.
