# The Universal Credit Regulations 2013, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/376
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 44. Medical examinations

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where it falls to be determined whether a claimant has limited capability for work or for work and work-related activity or is a severe conditions criteria claimant[^key-d7be289377621d67146b22c518a37ab4], the claimant may be called by or on behalf of a health care professional approved by the Secretary of State to attend a medical examination.

(2) Where a claimant who is called by or on behalf of such a health care professional to attend a medical examination in person, by telephone or by video[^key-2cc7c36304f2d4c5ff029fd4abbf63df] fails without a good reason to attend or submit to the examination, the claimant is to be treated as not having limited capability for work or for work and work-related activity or as not being a severe conditions criteria claimant (as the case may be)[^key-228a0d18816fde67252e54d17208d6f4].

(3) But paragraph (2) does not apply unless—

- (a) notice of the date, time and place of the examination was given to the claimant at least 7 days in advance; or
- (b) notice was given less than 7 days in advance and the claimant agreed to accept it.
