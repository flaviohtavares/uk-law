# The Universal Credit Regulations 2013, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/376
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 48. Capital disregarded

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any capital specified in Schedule 10 is to be disregarded from the calculation of a person's capital (see also regulations 75 to 77).

(2) Where a period of 6 months is specified in that Schedule, that period may be extended by the Secretary of State where it is reasonable to do so in the circumstances of the case.
