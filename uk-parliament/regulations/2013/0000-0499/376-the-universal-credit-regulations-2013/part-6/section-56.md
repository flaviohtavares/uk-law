# The Universal Credit Regulations 2013, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/376
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 56. Employee involved in trade dispute

*Extent: E+W+S*

A person who has had employed earnings and has withdrawn their labour in furtherance of a trade dispute is, unless their contract of service has been terminated, to be assumed to have employed earnings at the same level as they would have had were it not for the trade dispute.
