# The Universal Credit Regulations 2013, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/376

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. Jointly held capital

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a person and one or more other persons have a beneficial interest in a capital asset, those persons are to be treated, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, as if they were each entitled to an equal share of the whole of that beneficial interest.
