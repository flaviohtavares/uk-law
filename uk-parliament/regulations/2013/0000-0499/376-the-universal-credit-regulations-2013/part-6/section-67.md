> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 67. Meaning of “retirement pension income”

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), in regulation 66(1)(a) “**retirement pension income**” has the same meaning as in section 16 of the State Pension Credit Act 2002 [^c2165951] as extended by regulation 16 of the State Pension Credit Regulations 2002 [^c2165952].

(2) Retirement pension income includes any increase in a Category A or Category B retirement pension mentioned in section 16(1)(a) of the State Pension Credit Act 2002 which is payable under Part 4 of the Contributions and Benefits Act in respect of a person's partner.
