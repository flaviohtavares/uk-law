# The Universal Credit Regulations 2013, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/376
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 69. Calculation of student income - student loans and postgraduate ... loans[^c-c075e6bf88fae4035ff2e22b24e4561d][^c-bf0cca5e6fbdcfe6c2ff27f6d08f85a1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where, in accordance with regulation 68(2), a person's student income is to be based on the amount of a student loan for a year, the amount to be taken into account is the maximum student loan (including any increases for additional weeks) that the person would be able to acquire in respect of that year by taking reasonable steps to do so.

(1A)[^c-9dfbead425f80a8d20a414977dad0524] Where, in accordance with regulation 68(2), a person’s student income is to be based on the amount of a postgraduate [^c-cd33d590efec987e03b2096b525b1d34]... loan for a year, the amount to be taken into account is 30 per cent. of the maximum postgraduate [^c-cd33d590efec987e03b2096b525b1d34]... loan that the person would be able to acquire by taking reasonable steps to do so.

(2) For the purposes of calculating the maximum student loan in paragraph (1) or the maximum postgraduate[^c-682b0cac5a1877d8fa95579623b2f4c1] [^c-f8bec36d3e059504bf4de92f2bed6c08]... loan in paragraph (1A) it is to be assumed no reduction has been made on account of—

- (a) the person's means or the means of their partner, parent or any other person; or
- (b) any grant made to the person.
