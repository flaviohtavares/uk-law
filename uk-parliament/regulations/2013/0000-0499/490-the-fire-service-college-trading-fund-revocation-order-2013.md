# The Fire Service College Trading Fund (Revocation) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 490

Government Trading Funds

The Fire Service College Trading Fund (Revocation) Order 2013

Made

4th March 2013

Laid before the House of Commons

8th March 2013

Coming into force

1st April 2013

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 6(1) of the Government Trading Funds Act 1973[^f00001], with the concurrence of the Treasury, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Fire Service College Trading Fund (Revocation) Order 2013 and comes into force on 1st April 2013.

## 2. Revocation

The Fire Service College Trading Fund Order 1992[^f00002] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Brandon Lewis

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

4th March 2013

We concur

Desmond Swayne

David Evennett

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

4th March 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Fire Service College Trading Fund Order 1992 (“the 1992 Order”). As a result, the trading fund established by the 1992 Order ceases to exist.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
