# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2013, section 62

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 62. Prescribed amounts[^key-88585b34432cea362620b5f0d0b4e946]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to regulation 63 (special cases) the amount prescribed for the purposes of the calculation of the amount of a claimant's employment and support allowance under section 2(1)(a) of the Act is -

- (a) where the claimant satisfies the conditions set out in section 2(2) [^key-4342057021b0e1814271abf3e1e6d6a4]... of the Act or the claimant is a member of the work-related activity group and satisfies the conditions set out in Part 1 to Schedule 1 to the Act[^key-98149f8780df275baf35fb82a1efeb58], £95.55[^key-af05eb5213e82a3299f865b16b2d3bdf]; or
- (b) where the claimant does not satisfy the conditions set out in section 2(2) [^key-e29cbb72ea8f2b0eff65785c4f217767]... of the Act or the claimant is a member of the work-related activity group and satisfies the conditions set out in Part 1 to Schedule 1 to the Act[^key-dd807edebae1f8193f3a38ad2fc31c69]—
  - (i) where the claimant is aged not less than 25, £95.55[^key-211b70d1407cc79c03d243c880a5abd5]; or
  - (ii) where the claimant is aged less than 25, £75.65[^key-9743b2ed80c4c5b1a32e615694eaabfc].

(2)[^key-9f88b23d0fce0a3f9bc13a25a5db57fa] Subject to regulation 63 the amount of the support component is £50.35[^key-6fee54b0ea93e69e2919d1fdd8edca7e].
