# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2013, section 64

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 64. Permanent health insurance

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of sections 2(1)(c) and 3(3) of the Act (deductions from an employment and support allowance) “pension payment” is to include a permanent health insurance payment.
