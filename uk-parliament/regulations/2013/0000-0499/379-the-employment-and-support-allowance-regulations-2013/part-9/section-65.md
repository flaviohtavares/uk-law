# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2013, section 65

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 65. Financial Assistance Scheme

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purposes of sections 2(1)(c) and 3(3) of the Act (deductions from an employment and support allowance) “pension payment” is to include a Financial Assistance Scheme payment.

(2) In this regulation “**Financial Assistance Scheme payment**” means a payment made under the Financial Assistance Scheme Regulations 2005 [^c21661451].
