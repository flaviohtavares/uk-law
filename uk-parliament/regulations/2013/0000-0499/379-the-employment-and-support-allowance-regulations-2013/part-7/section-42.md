# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2013, section 42

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Application of regulations where there is dual entitlement

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This regulation applies where a person is entitled to universal credit and an employment and support allowance.

(2) The work-related requirements under sections 11B to 11I of the Act [^c21661271] and regulations 46 to 49 of these Regulations do not apply to such a person.

(3) Reductions relating to the award of an employment and support allowance under section 11J of the Act [^c21661281] and regulations 50 to 60 of these Regulations do not apply to such a person.

## Annotations

[^c21661271]: Marginal citation: Sections 11B to 11I were inserted by section 57 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012.

[^c21661281]: Marginal citation: Section 11J was inserted by section 57 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012.
