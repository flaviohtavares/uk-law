# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2013, section 60

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 60. Lower daily reduction rate

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The daily reduction rate is 40% of the rate applicable under regulation 59(1) if, at the end of the benefit week, the claimant falls within—

- (a) section 11E of the Act;
- (b) section 11D(2)(c) of the Act; or
- (c) regulation 47(1)(b), (c), (f) or (g).

(2) The daily reduction rate is nil if, at the end of the benefit week, the claimant falls within section 11D(2)(a) of the Act.
