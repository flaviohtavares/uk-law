# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2013, section 59

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 59. Daily reduction rate

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The daily reduction rate for the purposes of regulation 58 is, unless regulation 60 applies, the amount prescribed for the claimant under regulation 62(1) or, where applicable, regulation 63(2), multiplied by 52 and divided by 365.

(2) The amount of the daily reduction rate is to be rounded down to the nearest 10 pence.
