# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2013, section 92

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 92. Absence of member of family of member of Her Majesty's forces

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A claimant is to continue to be entitled to an employment and support allowance during any period of temporary absence from Great Britain if the claimant is a member of the family of a member of Her Majesty's forces and temporarily absent from Great Britain by reason only of the fact that the claimant is living with that member.

(2) In this regulation “**member of the family of a member of Her Majesty's forces**” means the spouse, civil partner, son, daughter, step-son, step-daughter, father, father-in-law, step-father, mother, mother-in-law or step-mother of such a member.
