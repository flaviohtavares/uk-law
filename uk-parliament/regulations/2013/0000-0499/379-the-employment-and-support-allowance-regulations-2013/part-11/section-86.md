# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2013, section 86

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 86. Linking period

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any period of limited capability for work which is separated from another such period by not more than 12 weeks is to be treated as a continuation of the earlier period.
