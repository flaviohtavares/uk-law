# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2013, section 91

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 91. Absence in order to receive NHS treatment

*Extent: E+W+S*

A claimant is to continue to be entitled to an employment and support allowance during any period of temporary absence from Great Britain if—

- (a) while absent from Great Britain, the claimant continues to satisfy the other conditions of entitlement to that employment and support allowance; and
- (b) that period of temporary absence is for the purpose of the claimant receiving treatment at a hospital or other institution outside Great Britain where the treatment is being provided—
  - (i) under section 6(2) of the Health Service Act (Performance of functions outside England) [^c21661661] or section 6(2) of the Health Service (Wales) Act (Performance of functions outside Wales);
  - (ii) pursuant to arrangements made under section 12(1) of the Health Service Act (Secretary of State's arrangements with other bodies) [^c21661671], section 10(1) of the Health Service (Wales) Act (Welsh Ministers' arrangements with other bodies), paragraph 18 of Schedule 4 to the Health Service Act (joint exercise of functions) [^c21661681] or paragraph 18 of Schedule 3 to the Health Service (Wales) Act (joint exercise of functions) [^c21661691]; or
  - (iii) under any equivalent provision in Scotland or pursuant to arrangements made under such provision.
