# The Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2013, section 95

> Text as at 2026-04-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 95. Treating a claimant as not having limited capability for work

*Extent: E+W+S*

The claimant is to be treated as not having limited capability for work if the claimant is disqualified for receiving an employment and support allowance during a period of imprisonment or detention in legal custody if that disqualification is for more than six weeks.
