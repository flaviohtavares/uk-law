# The Child Poverty Act 2010 (Extension of Publication Deadline) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 411

Children And Young Persons

The Child Poverty Act 2010 (Extension of Publication Deadline) Order 2013

Made

22nd February 2013

Laid before Parliament

1st March 2013

Coming into force

25th March 2013

Following a request by the Social Mobility and Child Poverty Commission, the Minister of State for Schools makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8B(5) and section 28(1) of the Child Poverty Act 2010[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Child Poverty Act 2010 (Extension of Publication Deadline) Order 2013.

(2) It comes into force on 25th March 2013.

## 2. Extension of publication deadline

The publication deadline, before which the Commission would otherwise be required by section 8B(1) of the Child Poverty Act 2010[^f00002] to publish its first annual report, is extended from 8th May 2013 to 26th September 2013.

David Laws

Minister of State

Department for Education

22nd February 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 8B(1) of the Child Poverty Act [2010 (c.9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/9) requires the Social Mobility and Child Poverty Commission (“the Commission”) to publish its first annual report before 8th May 2013. This Order, made under section 8B(5) of that Act following a request by the Commission, extends the publication deadline so that the report may be published at any time before 26th September 2013.

A full impact assessment has not been published for this instrument as it has no impact on the private sector or civil society organisations.

## Annotations

[^f00001]: Footnote: [2010 c.9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/9). Section 8 is substituted, and sections 8A to 8C inserted, by paragraph 2 of Schedule 13 to the Welfare Reform Act [2012 (c.5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/5) (“the 2012 Act”).

[^f00002]: Footnote: Section 8B(1) requires the Commission to publish a report before each anniversary of the coming into force of section 8B.
