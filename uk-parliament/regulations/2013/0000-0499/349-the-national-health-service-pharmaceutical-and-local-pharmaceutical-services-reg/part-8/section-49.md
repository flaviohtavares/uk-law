# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/349

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Necessary services for temporary patients

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A dispensing doctor who provides pharmaceutical services to patients on a patient list may provide necessary pharmaceutical services to a person who has been accepted by the dispensing doctor as a temporary patient.
