# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/349

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Routine applications for inclusion in or amendments to a pharmaceutical list

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations, a “routine application” is any application, other than an excepted application, by a person—

- (a) for inclusion in a pharmaceutical list who is not already included in it; or
- (b) who is included in a pharmaceutical list and who is seeking—
  - (i) to open, within the area of the relevant HWB, additional premises at or[^key-5e390b55ec16e4382b03803462c440fa] from which to provide the same or different pharmaceutical services,
  - (ii) to relocate to different premises, and at or from[^key-3e4d5de4661c00943127ec8ca15501c8] those premises to provide the same or different pharmaceutical services, or
  - (iii) to provide, at or[^key-abd23e809ddd08d471543b5ff0ee1dfe] from the person's listed chemist premises, services that are in addition to those already listed in relation to that person.
