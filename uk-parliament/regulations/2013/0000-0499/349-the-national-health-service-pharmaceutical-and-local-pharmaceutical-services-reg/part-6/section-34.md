## 34. Deferral of consideration of applications for inclusion in a pharmaceutical list on fitness grounds

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^key-3ad3bf8250f8eff96e15f0ca275d0729] In this regulation, “A” means, where an application for inclusion in a pharmaceutical list is made by a person who is—

- (a) an individual, the individual making the application;
- (b) a partnership, any partner in the partnership making the application; or
- (c) a body corporate—
  - (i) except for the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), (c)(ii), (e), (g), (i) and (k), the body corporate making the application, and
  - (ii) any director or superintendent of the body corporate making the application.

(1) An application for inclusion in a pharmaceutical list by a person [^key-9970bdc906aa51a6bc484fc11d07877b]...who is not already included in it may be deferred if NHS England[^key-fedf3951d93bcf656ad03378f887d7d5] is satisfied that—

- (a) there are, in respect of A [^key-179d1533b4e1c660d33b0c4c55724d2f]...—
  - (i) criminal proceedings in the United Kingdom, or
  - (ii) proceedings elsewhere relating to conduct, which, if it had occurred in the United Kingdom, would constitute a criminal offence,

  which, if they result in a conviction, or the equivalent of a conviction, would be likely to lead to the person’s removal from the pharmaceutical list, if the person[^key-d0b68e442dfef015a5fcda620aa89a7d] were to be included in it;
- (b) in respect of a body corporate of which A is, or has in the preceding 6 months been, or was at the time of the originating events, a director or superintendent, there are—
  - (i) criminal proceedings in the United Kingdom, or
  - (ii) proceedings elsewhere relating to conduct, which, if it had occurred in the United Kingdom, would constitute a criminal offence,

  which, if they resulted in a conviction, or the equivalent of a conviction, would be likely to lead to the person’s removal from the pharmaceutical list, if the person[^key-6c9c1e575b9b5efc76e70da23b0685a1] were to be included in it;
- (c) there is an investigation anywhere in the world—
  - (i) by A's [^key-eb026b2c27bf4163db5eb446df0e96c4]... licensing or regulatory body, or
  - (ii) relating to A [^key-44a20d776820c5bda11cd020dbc92330]... in A's professional capacity (including one by NHS England or another primary care organisation),

  which, if the outcome were adverse, would be likely to lead to the removal of the person from the pharmaceutical list if the person[^key-0840ff74159ab1c0a84be797b1525088] were to be included in it;
- (d) A [^key-39981fe34a6c1fe30ee68a38d12cc822]...is suspended from a relevant list;
- (e) a body corporate of which A [^key-7889b50ed7e4e210192a9e9ae9b85671]... was, at the time of the originating events, a director or superintendent, is suspended from a relevant list;
- (f) the First-tier Tribunal is considering an appeal by A [^key-2d8bda4eb322db30fa052c137d935f7a]... against a decision of NHS England or another primary care organisation—
  - (i) to refuse an application for inclusion in a relevant list, or
  - (ii) to include A conditionally in, or to remove or contingently remove A from, a relevant list,

  and if that appeal were to be unsuccessful, NHS England would be likely to remove the person from the pharmaceutical list if the person[^key-5f7b508ad67069c7ae6f533933c91766] were to be included in it;
- (g) the First-tier Tribunal is considering an appeal by a body corporate of which A [^key-fdc085acc04eb36c93bc62c623758529]... was, at the time of the originating events, or has in the preceding 6 months been, a director or superintendent, against a decision of NHS England or another primary care organisation—
  - (i) to refuse an application by that body corporate for inclusion in its list,
  - (ii) to include A conditionally in, or to remove or contingently remove A from, a relevant list,

  and if that appeal were to be unsuccessful NHS England would be likely to remove the person from the pharmaceutical list if the person[^key-88b8793d48ebe74e0aa42236f3d3518a] were to be included in it;
- (h) A [^key-1c5b3adcdabdd7fadaf6192d04b9fa06]... is being investigated by the NHS CFA[^key-fe2e441bd94ebd4e5a124a473d0c58e4] in relation to any fraud, where the result, if adverse, would be likely to lead to the removal of the person from the pharmaceutical list if the person[^key-272940e2841c4cc9bdadb77e297bbe49] were to be included in it;
- (i) a body corporate, of which A [^key-945b618b3d4724e858d6b68e15fd8130]...was, at the time of the originating events, a director or superintendent, is being investigated by the NHS CFA[^key-154c78defc89b15bbb301b2afcbcf70a] in relation to any fraud, where the result if adverse would be likely to lead to the removal of the person from the pharmaceutical list if the person[^key-e26f32b4a42f065af85a0b8e3d9a8db4] were to be included in it;
- (j) the First-tier Tribunal is considering an application from NHS England or a Local Health Board for a national disqualification of A [^key-2c454e3dd3662dcb10640000259d19c0]...;
- (k) the First-tier Tribunal is considering an application from NHS England or a Local Health Board for a national disqualification of a body corporate of which A [^key-abd2db797d488990ed5559e002c25d5e]... was, at the time of the originating events, a director or superintendent; or
- (l) NHS England or another primary care organisation, for a reason relating to unsuitability, fraud or efficiency of service provision—
  - (i) is considering removal (other than voluntary removal) or contingent removal of the applicant from a relevant list, or
  - (ii) has taken a decision to remove (other than voluntary removal) or contingently remove the applicant[^key-cbee29f53ca339cc514d94bef3b258d8] from a relevant list but that decision has yet to take effect.

(2) NHS England may only defer a decision under paragraph (1) until—

- (a) the outcome of the cause for the deferral is known; or
- (b) the reason for the deferral no longer exists.
