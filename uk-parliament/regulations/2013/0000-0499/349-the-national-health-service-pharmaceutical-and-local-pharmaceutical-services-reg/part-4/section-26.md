# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/349
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Change of ownership applications

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 129(2A) of the 2006 Act [^c21646801] (regulations as to pharmaceutical services) does not apply to an application from a person who is not included in a pharmaceutical list for inclusion in the list, or from a person included in a pharmaceutical list for inclusion in that list also in respect of other premises than those already listed in relation to that person, if—

- (a) the applicant (X) is undertaking to provide pharmaceutical services at or from[^c-8b4121e8c507d3a4002d325ad7df4e27] premises—
  - (i) that are already listed chemist premises, and
  - (ii) at or from[^c-a0815c08e4de21eda635f7e8cf4dc810] which another person (Y) is providing pharmaceutical services;
- (b) X is proposing to carry on at or from[^c-b6b2601270c8c27d13fa4284b421601b] the listed chemist premises, in place of Y, the business in the course of which Y is providing pharmaceutical services at or from[^c-9c7f088611b992db36d85fced7f8f7f9] those premises;
- (c) X is undertaking to provide the same pharmaceutical services as those that Y is providing; and
- (d) the provision of pharmaceutical services at or from[^c-42dd26857b9976837d59991f51d1c8f0] the premises will not be interrupted (except for such period as NHS England[^c-fedf3951d93bcf656ad03378f887d7d5] may for good cause allow).

(2) Section 129(2A) of the 2006 Act does not apply to an application from a person who is not included in a pharmaceutical list for the area of a HWB (HWB1) for inclusion in that list, or from a person included in a pharmaceutical list for inclusion in that list also in respect of other premises than those already listed in relation to that person, if—

- (a) the applicant (X) is undertaking to provide the pharmaceutical services that another person (Y)—
  - (i) is providing at or from[^c-9580f53fae2d161a7ed9fedfc9879e89] listed chemist premises (“Y's premises”), whether in the area of HWB1 or a neighbouring HWB, or
  - (ii) has provided at or from[^c-cbceb766b7cf53d63259e4a306d590ea] Y's premises but Y is no longer able to provide pharmaceutical services at or from[^c-80176cc44ccc293ea1ef85f1815ac6b2] those premises for reasons that NHS England accepts are good cause;
- (b) X is proposing to carry on, in place of Y, the business in the course of which Y is providing, or has provided, pharmaceutical services at or from[^c-f5f2a4035e201b429f00feeb3d69488a] Y's premises;
- (c) X is undertaking to provide the same pharmaceutical services as Y is providing or has provided at or from[^c-797c3c43d4cbb3ebe726d18ff43f4dde] Y's premises, but at or from[^c-ea045211ada2e24237946690b2ee0647] different premises (“X's premises”);
- (d) had Y[^c-df3f2eed9eb2558189fd0a5a839f8c90] applied to move to X's premises, that application would have been granted under regulation 24; and
- (e) in a case where[^c-dac1cdaf6e1566d6afb41bae98817945] pharmaceutical services—
  - (i) are being provided at or from[^c-a0b3874ed18aba52c4c61f2ca6394cf5] Y's premises, the provision of pharmaceutical services will not be interrupted (except for such period as NHS England may for good cause allow) by the move of the business from Y's premises to X's premises, or
  - (ii) are not being provided at or from[^c-ed419fc09cd325a1ef96f6c44c4cc96f] Y's premises, the provision of pharmaceutical services will commence at or from[^c-2e103beed24a53a74ea0e24743d397aa] X's premises within the period that NHS England considers is an acceptable period for the interruption of the provision of pharmaceutical services by the business that X is taking over.

(3)[^c-74be3d6664acedf331abe6639931fd72] An application pursuant to paragraph (1) must be refused if it relates to distance selling premises, unless the application, if made pursuant to regulation 25(1), would not be refused pursuant to regulation 25(2).

(4) An application pursuant to paragraph (2) must be refused if the existing pharmacy premises from which the applicant is seeking to relocate are distance selling premises, unless the premises to which the applicant is seeking to relocate are also distance selling premises (and this is in addition to the requirement that arises by virtue of paragraph (2)(d) that the application, if made pursuant to regulation 25(1), would not be refused pursuant to regulation 25(2)).
