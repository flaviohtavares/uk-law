# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/349
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Applications from NHS chemists in respect of providing directed services

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 129(2A) of the 2006 Act [^c21646751] (regulations as to pharmaceutical services) does not apply to an application by a person already included in a pharmaceutical list for inclusion in that list also in respect of services that are directed services that are not already listed in relation to that person.

## Annotations

[^c21646751]: Marginal citation: Section 129(2A) was inserted by the [Health Act 2009 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/21), [section 26(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/21/section/26/3), and has been amended by the [Health and Social Care Act 2012 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/7), [section 207(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/7/section/207/4), and Schedule 4, paragraph 66(5).
