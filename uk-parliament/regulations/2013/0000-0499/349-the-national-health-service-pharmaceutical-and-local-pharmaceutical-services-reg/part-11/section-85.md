# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013, section 85

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/349
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. General power to revoke suspensions in appropriate circumstances

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If an NHS chemist is suspended from a pharmaceutical list, in addition to NHS England’s[^key-fedf3951d93bcf656ad03378f887d7d5] powers to terminate suspensions under section 157(3)(b) of the 2006 Act [^c21647061] (review of decisions) on a review, NHS England may terminate the suspension at any time, in appropriate circumstances.

(2) If NHS England terminates a suspension under paragraph (1), it must notify the NHS chemist that it has done so.
