## 112. Sharing of information about LPS chemists and their employees with the General Pharmaceutical Council

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where NHS England[^key-fedf3951d93bcf656ad03378f887d7d5] has concerns about the fitness of an LPS chemist, or a pharmacist or pharmacy technician employed by an LPS chemist, to provide local pharmaceutical services, it must where appropriate pass on those concerns and any relevant supporting evidence to the General Pharmaceutical Council.
