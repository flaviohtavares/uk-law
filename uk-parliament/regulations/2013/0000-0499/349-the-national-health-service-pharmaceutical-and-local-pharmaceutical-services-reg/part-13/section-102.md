# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/349
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Terms of service for LPS schemes: general

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Each LPS scheme must specify—

- (a) the LP services to be provided under the scheme, which must include the dispensing of drugs; and
- (b) the address of the premises at or from which those services are to be provided (“the scheme premises”).

(2) If the provider of services under an LPS scheme is a health service body, the scheme must state that it is an NHS contract.

(3) Each LPS scheme must contain the terms, or terms which make provision that has the same effect as the terms, set out in Schedule 7.
