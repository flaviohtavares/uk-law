# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013, section 67A

## 67A. Condition relating to entering administration[^key-8478e64c438ebe8d57aebca2f771fb2a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An NHS chemist must, if the NHS chemist enters administration, notify NHS England[^key-fedf3951d93bcf656ad03378f887d7d5] of that fact.

(2) For these purposes, “enters administration” has the meaning given in paragraph 1(2)(b) of Schedule B1 to the Insolvency Act 1986 (administration).
