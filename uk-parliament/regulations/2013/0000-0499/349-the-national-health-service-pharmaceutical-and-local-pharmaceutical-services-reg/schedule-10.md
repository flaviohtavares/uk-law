### 2. Revocation of the National Health Service (Local Pharmaceutical Services and Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2002

The National Health Service (Local Pharmaceutical Services and Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2002 [^c21648151] are revoked.

### 3. Revocation of the National Health Service (Local Pharmaceutical Services and Pharmaceutical Services) (No. 2) Regulations 2002

The National Health Service (Local Pharmaceutical Services and Pharmaceutical Services) (No. 2) Regulations 2002 [^c21648161] are revoked.

### 4. Revocation of the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) Amendment Regulations 2005

The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) Amendment Regulations 2005 [^c21648171] are revoked.

### 5. Revocation of the National Health Service (Local Pharmaceutical Services etc.) Regulations 2006 and amendments to those Regulations

(1) The National Health Service (Local Pharmaceutical Services etc.) Regulations [^c21648181] are revoked.

(2) The following provisions are revoked—

- (a) regulation 4 of the National Health Service (Miscellaneous Amendments Relating to Independent Prescribing) Regulations 2006 [^c21648191];
- (b) regulation 8 of the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) (Remuneration for Persons Providing Pharmaceutical Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 [^c21648201];
- (c) Part 2 of the National Health Service (Miscellaneous Amendments Relating to Community Pharmaceutical Services and Optometrist Prescribing) Regulations 2009 [^c21648211];
- (d) paragraphs 134 to 136 of Schedule 3 to the Transfer of Tribunal Functions Order 2010 [^c21648221]; and
- (e) paragraph 53 of Schedule 4 to the Pharmacy Order 2010 [^c21648231].

### 6. Revocation of the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 [^c21648241] are revoked.

### 7. Amendments to the Local Authority Social Services and National Health Service Complaints (England) Regulations 2009

In the Local Authority Social Services and National Health Service Complaints (England) Regulations 2009 [^c21648251]—

- (a) in the definition of “relevant complaints procedure” in regulation 2 (interpretation), for paragraphs (i) to (iii) of sub-paragraph (a) substitute the following paragraphs—

  > - (i) paragraph 34 of Schedule 4 to the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013;
  > - (ia) paragraph 24 of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013;
  > - (ii) paragraph 8 of Schedule 6 to the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013; or
  > - (iii) paragraph 25 of Schedule 7 to the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013; or

  ; and
- (b) paragraph 2 of the Schedule is revoked.

### 8. Revocation of the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Amendment Regulations 2009

The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Amendment Regulations 2009 [^c21648261] are revoked.

### 9. Revocation of the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services and Local Pharmaceutical Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services and Local Pharmaceutical Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 [^c21648271] are revoked.

### 10. Revocation of the National Health Service (Local Pharmaceutical Services) Amendment Regulations 2012

The National Health Service (Local Pharmaceutical Services) Amendment Regulations 2012 [^c21648281] are revoked.

### 11. Revocation of the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2012 and of amendments to those Regulations

(1) The following Regulations are revoked—

- (a) the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2012 [^c21648291]; and
- (b) National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2012 (Amendment) Regulations 2012 [^c21648301].

### 12. Amendment of the Local Authorities (Partnership Arrangements, Care Trusts, Public Health and Local Healthwatch Arrangements etc.) Regulations 2012

In regulation 12(3)(d) of the Local Authorities (Partnership Arrangements, Care Trusts, Public Health and Local Healthwatch Arrangements etc.) Regulations 2012 [^c21648311] (National Health Service payments by local authorities to specified NHS bodies in respect of prescribed functions) for “National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2012” substitute “ National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013 ”.
