# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical and Local Pharmaceutical Services) Regulations 2013, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2013/349
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Refusal: same or adjacent premises

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-09d47781ad1acad9a5b2fe90fc6762a5] A routine or excepted application , other than a consolidation application[^key-ac3fb963928906f697f7fd3aeed83414] or a change of ownership application pursuant to regulation 26(1)[^key-2241ec99837fc0402926c305e316be57], must be refused where paragraph (2) applies.

(2) This paragraph applies where—

- (a) a person on the pharmaceutical list (which may or may not be the applicant) is providing or has undertaken to provide pharmaceutical services (“the existing services”) at or[^key-1232d14b8ff5ac516d31876f0c0dc32e] from—
  - (i) the premises to which the application relates, or
  - (ii) adjacent premises; and
- (b) NHS England[^key-fedf3951d93bcf656ad03378f887d7d5] is satisfied that it is reasonable to treat the services that the applicant proposes to provide as part of the same service as the existing services (and so the premises to which the application relates and the existing listed chemist premises should be treated as the same site).
