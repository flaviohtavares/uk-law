# The West Earlham Infant School and West Earlham Junior School (School Day and School Year Regulations) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 473

Education, England

The West Earlham Infant School and West Earlham Junior School (School Day and School Year Regulations) Order 2013

Made

4th March 2013

Laid before Parliament

11th March 2013

Coming into force

8th April 2013

The governing bodies of the West Earlham Infant School[^f00001] and the West Earlham Junior School[^f00002] (“the Schools”), qualifying bodies for the purposes of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Education Act 2002[^f00003] (“the Act”) have, in accordance with section 4(2) of the Act, consulted the local authority and such persons as appear to them to be appropriate, including the parents of the registered pupils at the Schools and the staff working at the Schools.

It is the opinion of the Secretary of State pursuant to section 1(1)(a) of the Act, having regard to the matters set out in section 1(2), that the implementation by the Schools’ governing bodies of the provisions of this Order may contribute to the raising of educational standards in England.

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(1) of the Act, the Secretary of State, on the application of the Schools’ governing bodies, makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the West Earlham Infant School and West Earlham Junior School (School Day and School Year Regulations) Order 2013 and comes into force on 8th April 2013.

## 2. Relaxation of the Education (School Day and School Year) (England) Regulations 1999

In respect of the application of regulation 3(2) of the Education (School Day and School Year) (England) Regulations 1999[^f00004] to the Schools, in relation to the 2012-2013 school year[^f00005], for “380” substitute “378”.

## 3. Time period

This Order has effect up to and including 31st August 2013.

John Nash

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Education

4th March 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under section 2 of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 provides for the number of school sessions required at West Earlham Infant School and West Earlham Junior School to be reduced from 380 to 378 in the 2012-2013 school year.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on businesses or civil society organisations is foreseen.
