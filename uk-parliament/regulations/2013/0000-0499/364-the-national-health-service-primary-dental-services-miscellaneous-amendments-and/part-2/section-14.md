# The National Health Service (Primary Dental Services) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2013, section 14

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Amendment of regulation 21[^c-3ca65e3fbe6e41e1e9fbbe356460aa5e]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 21 (finance)—

- (a) at the beginning of paragraphs (1) and (3), for “Subject to regulation 24A,” substitute “Subject to regulation 24B,”;
- (b) in paragraphs (1) and (2), in each place, for “the Primary Care Trust” substitute “the Board”; and
- (c) in paragraph (3), for “a Primary Care Trust” substitute “the Board”.
