# The National Health Service (Primary Dental Services) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2013, section 13

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Amendment of regulation 19[^key-7e0bc7c427d18361ee5113eae69d2aff]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 19 (under provision of units of dental activity or units of orthodontic activity), in paragraphs (1), (2)(b)(ii), (3) and (4), for “the Primary Care Trust” substitute “the Board”.
