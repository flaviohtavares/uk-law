# The National Health Service (Primary Dental Services) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2013, section 1

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-248dc988151e469263f23ef6d6d9a9d4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (Primary Dental Services) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2013 and come into force on 1st April 2013.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “the GDS Contracts Regulations” means the National Health Service (General Dental Services Contracts) Regulations 2005[^f00002]; and
- “the PDS Agreements Regulations” means the National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) Regulations 2005[^f00003].
