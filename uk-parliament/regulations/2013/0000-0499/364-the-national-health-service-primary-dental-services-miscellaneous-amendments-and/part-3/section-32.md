# The National Health Service (Primary Dental Services) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2013, section 32

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Amendment of regulation 15[^key-53f7e0277e83c85698d83e7d58bff0f1]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 15 (under-provision of units of dental activity or units of orthodontic activity), in paragraphs (1), (2)(b)(ii), (3), and (4), for “the Relevant Body” substitute “the Board”.
