# The National Health Service (Primary Dental Services) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2013, section 33

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Amendment of regulation 17[^key-954fa8255e670dd264cd7992494550ac]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 17 (finance)—

- (a) at the beginning of paragraphs (1) and (3), for “Subject to regulation 20A,” substitute “Subject to regulation 20B,”;
- (b) in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b), and (2)(a) and (c), for “the Relevant Body” substitute “the Board”; and
- (c) in paragraph (3) for “a Relevant Body”, substitute “the Board”.
