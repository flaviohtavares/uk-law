# The National Health Service (Primary Dental Services) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2013, section 49

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Amendment of regulation 8[^key-c9866414a5d6487f4efef2135f250c26]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 8 (making and recovery of charges)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “a Primary Care Trust” substitute “the Board”; and
- (b) in paragraph (2), for “or (5)”, substitute “, (5) or 13A(2)”;
- (c) in paragraph (3), for “regulation 4”, substitute “regulation 4 or 13A(2)”; and
- (d) in paragraphs (1)(c) and (2)(b) and (d)(i), for “the relevant Primary Care Trust” substitute “the Board”.
