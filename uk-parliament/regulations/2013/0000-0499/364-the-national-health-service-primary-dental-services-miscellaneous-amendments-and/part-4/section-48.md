# The National Health Service (Primary Dental Services) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2013, section 48

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 48. Amendment of regulation 7[^c-4ddd6319ec5eafc0daac8a8b90b6f98d]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 7 (conditions for exemption under the Act)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a), for “the relevant Primary Care Trust” substitute “the Board”; and
- (b) in paragraphs (1)(b), and (4), for “a Primary Care Trust” substitute “the Board”.
