# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 2013, section 31

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Tribunal decisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may supersede a decision of the First-tier Tribunal or Upper Tribunal which—

- (a) was made in ignorance of, or was based upon a mistake as to, some material fact; or
- (b) in a case where section 26(5) (appeals involving issues that arise in other cases) of the 1998 Act applies, was made in accordance with section 26(4)(b)of that Act [^c2167604].
