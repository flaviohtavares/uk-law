# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 2013, section 28

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Loss of benefit cases

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A decision that a benefit is payable to a claimant may be superseded where that benefit ceases to be payable or falls to be reduced by virtue of section 6B, 7 or 9 of the Fraud Act (loss of benefit provisions) [^c2167603].
