# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 2013, section 22A

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22A. Extension[^c-50513b7f3cf411abfe752ec93cda3646]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may extend the length of a fixed term award of personal independence payment, where the Secretary of State considers it necessary to do so to safeguard the efficient administration of personal independence payment.
