# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 2013, section 25

> Text as at 2026-07-16.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Decisions against which no appeal lies

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A decision specified in Schedule 2 (decisions against which no appeal lies) to the 1998 Act or prescribed in regulation 50(2) (decisions which may or may not be appealed) may be superseded.
