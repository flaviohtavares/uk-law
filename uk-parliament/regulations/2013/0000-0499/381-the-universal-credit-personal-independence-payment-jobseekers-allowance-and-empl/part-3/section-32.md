# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 2013, section 32

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Decisions which may not be superseded

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A decision which may be revised under section 9 of the 1998 Act may not be superseded under Chapter 1 of this Part unless—

- (a) circumstances arise in which the Secretary of State may revise the decision under Part 2; and
- (b) further circumstances arise in relation to that decision which—
  - (i) are not set out in that Part; but
  - (ii) are set out in Chapter 1 of this Part or are ones where a superseding decision may be made in accordance with regulation 33(3).
