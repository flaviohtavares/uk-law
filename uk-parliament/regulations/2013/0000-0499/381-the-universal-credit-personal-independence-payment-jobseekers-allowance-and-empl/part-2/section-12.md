# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 2013, section 12

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Award of another benefit

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where—

- (a) the Secretary of State makes a decision to award a benefit to a claimant (“**the original award**”); and
- (b) an award of another relevant benefit or[^key-014dddf94bc642fbbd69331bc2ad6b91] Scottish disability or carer benefit[^key-ba35becf5262aa5e2247d670a72884af] or of an increase in the rate of another relevant benefit or Scottish disability or carer benefit is made to the claimant or, in the case of universal credit, to a member of their family, for a period which includes the date on which the original award took effect,

the Secretary of State may revise the original award.
