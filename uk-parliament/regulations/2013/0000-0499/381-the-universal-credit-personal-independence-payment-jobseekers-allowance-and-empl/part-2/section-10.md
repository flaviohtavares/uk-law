# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 2013, section 10

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Decisions against which no appeal lies

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A decision may be revised where the decision is one which is—

- (a) specified in Schedule 2 (decisions against which no appeal lies) to the 1998 Act; or
- (b) prescribed by regulation 50(2) (decisions which may or may not be appealed).
