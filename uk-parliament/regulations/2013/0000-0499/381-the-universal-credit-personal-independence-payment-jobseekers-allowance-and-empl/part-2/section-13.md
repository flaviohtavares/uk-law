# The Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker's Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations 2013, section 13

> Text as at 2026-07-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Advance awards etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A decision pursuant to regulation 32, 33 or 34 of the Claims and Payments Regulations 2013 to make an advance award of benefit may be revised if the conditions for entitlement are found not to have been satisfied at the start of the period for which the claim is treated as having been made.
