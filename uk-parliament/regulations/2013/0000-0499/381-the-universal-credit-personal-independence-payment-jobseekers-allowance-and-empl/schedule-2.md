## SCHEDULE 2 — DECISIONS AGAINST WHICH AN APPEAL LIES

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 50(1)

**1.** A decision as to whether a person is entitled to a benefit for which no claim is required by virtue of regulation 6, 7 or 9(6) and (7) of the Claims and Payments Regulations 2013.
