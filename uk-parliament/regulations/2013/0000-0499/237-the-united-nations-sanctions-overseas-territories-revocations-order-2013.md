# The United Nations Sanctions (Overseas Territories) (Revocations) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 237

Overseas Territories

The United Nations Sanctions (Overseas Territories) (Revocations) Order 2013

Made

12th February 2013

Laid before Parliament

19th February 2013

Coming into force

12th March 2013

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of February 2013

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 1 of the United Nations Act 1946[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the United Nations Sanctions (Overseas Territories) (Revocations) Order 2013 and comes into force on 12th March 2013.

(2) This Order extends to the territories listed in Schedule 1.

## 2. Revocations

The Orders listed in Schedule 2 are revoked.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE 1 — Territories to which this Order extends

Article 1(2)

- Anguilla
- Bermuda
- British Antarctic Territory
- British Indian Ocean Territory
- Cayman Islands
- Falkland Islands
- Gibraltar
- Montserrat
- Pitcairn, Henderson, Ducie and Oeno Islands
- St Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha
- South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands
- The Sovereign Base Areas of Akrotiri and Dhekelia in the Island of Cyprus
- Turks and Caicos Islands
- Virgin Islands

## SCHEDULE 2 — Orders Revoked by Article 2

Article 2

- The Liberia (United Nations Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2001[^f00002]
- The Liberia (United Nations Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) (No. 2) Order 2001[^f00003]
- The Liberia (United Nations Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) (Amendment) Order 2003[^f00004]
- The Sierra Leone (United Nations Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2000 [^f00005]

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Liberia (United Nations Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2001, the Liberia (United Nations Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) (No. 2) Order 2001, the Liberia, (United Nations Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) (Amendment) Order 2003, and the Sierra Leone (United Nations Sanctions) (Overseas Territories) Order 2000.

These Orders cease to have effect on the termination of the United Nations sanctions measures which they implemented. This Order formally revokes the Orders.
