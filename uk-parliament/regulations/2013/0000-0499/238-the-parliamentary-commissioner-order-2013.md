# The Parliamentary Commissioner Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 238

Parliamentary Commissioner

The Parliamentary Commissioner Order 2013

Made

12th February 2013

Laid before Parliament

19th February 2013

Coming into force

1st April 2013

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of February 2013

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 4(2) and 5(4) of the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to make the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Parliamentary Commissioner Order 2013 and comes into force on 1st April 2013.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967.

## 3. Amendment to Schedule 3 to the Act

In Schedule 3 to the Act[^f00003] (matters not subject to investigation) insert after paragraph 10(1)—

> (1A) Sub-paragraph (1)(a) shall not apply to any action (not otherwise excluded from investigation by this Schedule) which is taken by the Secretary of State in connection with the provision of any allowance, grant, supplement or benefit under—
>
> - (a) the Naval, Military and Air Forces etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006 ([S.I.2006/606](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/606)); or

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967 (“the Act”). Article 2 of this Order amends Schedule 2 to the Act (departments etc. subject to investigation). It adds the Regulatory Policy Committee to the list of departments and authorities which are subject to investigation by the Parliamentary Commissioner for Administration; it omits from that list those bodies referred to in article 2(b); and it amends those entries in the list referred to in article 2(c) and (d). Article 3 amends Schedule 3 to the Act (matters not subject to investigation) to exclude certain actions of the Secretary of State in connection with the occupational benefit schemes under the Naval, Military and Air Forces etc. (Disablement and Death) Service Pensions Order 2006 and the Armed Forces and Reserve Forces (Compensation Scheme) Order 2011.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
