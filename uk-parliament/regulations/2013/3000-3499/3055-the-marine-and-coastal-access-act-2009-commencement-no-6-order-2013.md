# The Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 3055 (C. 127)

Marine Management

The Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2013

Made

3rd December 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following order, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 324(3) and (4) of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^f00001].

**1.** This order may be cited as the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2013.

**2.** The appointed day for the coming into force of section 41 of, and Part 1 of Schedule 4 to, the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 is 31<sup>st</sup> March 2014.

Baroness Warsi

Senior Minister of State

Foreign and Commonwealth Office

3rd December 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This instrument brings into force on 31 March 2014 the provisions of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 concerning the declaration of an exclusive economic zone and the consequential amendments to other legislation.
