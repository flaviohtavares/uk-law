# The Defamation Act 2013 (Commencement) (England and Wales) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 3027 (C. 125)

Defamation, England And Wales

The Defamation Act 2013 (Commencement) (England and Wales) Order 2013

Made

2nd December 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 17(4) of the Defamation Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Defamation Act 2013 (Commencement) (England and Wales) Order 2013.

## 2. Appointed day

The Defamation Act 2013 comes into force on 1st January 2014 so far as it is not already in force.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

McNally

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

2nd December 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, in England and Wales, those provisions of the Defamation Act [2013 (c. 26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/26) that did not come into force on Royal Assent.
