# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 30

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Prioritisation of the reuse of whole appliances[^key-c479e3e25710a89fe62cf2a637373272]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In respect of any WEEE for which it is responsible under these Regulations, an operator of a scheme must[^key-ef30cf36d568ce78dae54914320311b6] ensure that systems are set up to prioritise the reuse of whole appliances.
