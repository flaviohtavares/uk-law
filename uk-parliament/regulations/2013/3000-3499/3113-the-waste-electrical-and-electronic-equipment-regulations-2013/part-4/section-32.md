# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 32

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Recovery[^c-bd81582233fe5d3cd8b0ce24ddbcce86]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In respect of any WEEE for which it is responsible under these Regulations, an operator of a scheme must[^c-7d8551b54a44ca7348fb03372ad15254] ensure—

- (a) that systems are set up to provide for the recovery of such WEEE; and
- (b) that such WEEE is—
  - (i) recovered or recycled by a reprocessor, or
  - (ii) exported by an approved exporter for recovery or recycling outside the United Kingdom.

(2) Paragraph 1(b) will not apply to WEEE reused as a whole appliance.
