> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 12 — Criteria for approval as a designated collection facility

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 70

**1.**[^c-8663c4f33b36cf952b4983d724398a52] The facility is likely to subsist for at least one compliance period.

**2.**[^c-c6b9a4f525647561efaf029bd40999ce] That—

- (a) the operator of the collection facility complies with the code of practice; and
- (b) the collection facility has the capacity and arrangements in place to collect WEEE in compliance with the code of practice.

**3.**[^c-01aaa6d48bfccff5eb28c221554ce467] The operator of the collection facility is willing to work with one or more operator of a scheme to secure the collection and removal of WEEE from a private household that is deposited at the facility during each compliance period.

**4.**[^c-aaf7477c1518f5d4d8dc197a2d2f7181] The operator of the collection facility holds a relevant authorisation.
