# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 21

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Declaration of EEE producer registration number[^key-baedc347aa758d8b4bfe33c88f7eef7b][^key-4cb0a147088d6756f02b4ffcf6c70dc8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A producer, or where one has been appointed, an authorised representative, must declare his EEE producer registration number to any distributor to whom he intends to sell, sells, or otherwise supplies EEE.
