# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 22

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Marking EEE with the crossed out wheeled bin symbol[^c-433e690cc0462230c94c6c253f569294]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the application of regulation 24A,[^c-50701ced2d102c1baa8b2fe3bb6b3574] a producer or authorised representative must[^c-9668c0320373341a56540c45c3d54f9c] mark EEE that he places[^c-3b2a8cab424b38aea7c32570b0e525be] on the market with the symbol shown in Schedule 6 (“the crossed out wheeled bin symbol”).

(2) Except where paragraph (3) applies, the crossed out wheeled bin symbol must be affixed in a visible, legible and indelible form to each item of equipment.

(3) In exceptional cases, where this is necessary because of the size or function of the product, the crossed out wheeled bin symbol must be printed on—

- (a) the packaging;
- (b) the instructions for use; and
- (c) the accompanying warranty.
