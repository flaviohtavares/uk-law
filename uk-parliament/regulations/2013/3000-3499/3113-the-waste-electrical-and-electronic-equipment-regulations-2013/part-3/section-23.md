# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 23

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Marking EEE with a date mark[^key-5a8331ccef866bf32c6b0ed7ed32955b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the application of regulation 24A,[^key-dff7a66666d7cc59605dede5f1ae7a9e] a producer or authorised representative must[^key-2e858172881380159b8665cf1af55a28] mark EEE that he places[^key-af220ef96f0c09cfbe639c99b60f34ed] on the market in such a manner that the equipment can be easily identified as having been placed on the market after 13th August 2005 (“the date mark”).

(2) The date mark must be affixed in a visible, legible and indelible form to each item of equipment.
