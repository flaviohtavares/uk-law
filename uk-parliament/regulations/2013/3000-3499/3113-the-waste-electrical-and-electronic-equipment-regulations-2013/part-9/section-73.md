# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 73

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Evidence notes[^c-9cd41e42d9bc40dba0a525e0e9f57bcd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It will be the duty of the Secretary of State to approve the format of evidence notes issued by an operator of an AATF or an approved exporter.
