# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 81

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Approval of authorised treatment facilities and exporters[^c-7ab20e57d363f7f1313139268df8b80c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The appropriate authority must[^c-bbb369cd9c075d7fc22cc47a418baf2c] maintain and publish a list of all AATFs, operators of AATFs and approved exporters.

(2) The appropriate authority must[^c-0609f2ceac3a13bfeac8b173de6a8857] publish the format in which —[^c-1a253d296b6f6ed2264cffed1e6b41ea]

- (a) the information referred to in Part 1 of Schedule 11 must be submitted to it in an application for approval made under regulation 61 or in an application for an extension of a grant of approval made under regulation 62 , and[^c-33814b3b6cab07e0ad7279ad632004d4]
- (b) the reports referred to in regulation 66 must be submitted to it in accordance with that regulation.
