# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 51

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Prohibition on showing the costs of financing the collection, treatment and environmentally sound disposal of WEEE from private households[^key-6ca0b77429b4e47895d55cea9fea2857]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-6f8101384f6953ed388e7c824477acbb] A producer[^key-d2c4c75b42a341ae0c709d2aacfde5d1] , non-UK supplier[^key-8df06b281ba2e90407dbbf47cc93d647] or distributor must not show a person, who purchases EEE otherwise than in the course of a business, at the time of sale of new EEE the costs of financing the collection, treatment and environmentally sound disposal of WEEE from private households.

(2)[^key-9a2743e84afbfda5fd5d93ed875240e9] For the purposes of any obligation placed on a non-UK supplier under paragraph (1), reference to the producer in sub-paragraph (f)(v) of the definition of “appropriate authority” in regulation 2 is to be read as though it referred to the non-UK supplier.
