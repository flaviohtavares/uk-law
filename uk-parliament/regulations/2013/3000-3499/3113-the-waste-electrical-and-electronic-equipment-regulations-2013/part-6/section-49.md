# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 49

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. WEEE from private households which presents a health and safety risk[^c-6362bf7a0264b008f33d2ca18f2a323b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations will prevent any person from refusing to handle WEEE from private households that presents a health and safety risk to any individual because of contamination.
