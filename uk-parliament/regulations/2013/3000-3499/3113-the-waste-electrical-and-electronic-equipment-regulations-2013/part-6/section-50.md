# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 50

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Take back: WEEE from private households[^key-ea92989d2d050a5668cd2198cb1cf53b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations will prevent an operator of a scheme from establishing and operating a system to take back WEEE from private households provided that system is consistent with the Directive.
