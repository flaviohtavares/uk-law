# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 54

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Shipments of used EEE[^key-a0e28c99482771f163acba656adce4e7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An approved exporter who exports used EEE must[^key-b5d47517a7727e238d09700dcf97de7a] comply with the requirements of Schedule 9.

(2) The records referred to in Schedule 9 must be kept for a period of at least four years commencing on the date on which any such record is made and must be made available to the appropriate authority on demand.
