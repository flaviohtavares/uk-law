# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 10

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Assimilated law etc[^key-37cc2abd8353a9819a211e01567e6a04][^key-95e2f1f6bd65956bb3d7e0534d8b2dce][^key-62bdb7d7ff1692b3b3af648bddb1e7a1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations will affect the application of assimilated[^key-f43ac21cda510b1b08edb841a2bd9f7c][^key-c0306761372e4a00063c982e60150893] law and, in relation to Northern Ireland, EU legislation which has effect by virtue of the Protocol on Ireland/Northern Ireland in the EU withdrawal agreement, imposing requirements on—

- (a) safety and health;
- (b) chemicals, in particular Regulation [(EC) No 1907/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1907) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18th December 2006 concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH), establishing a European Chemicals Agency;
- (c) waste management; or
- (d) product design.
