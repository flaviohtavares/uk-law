# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 85

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Determination of appeals[^c-de54087a361c5660e1d974c5f4699262]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where, on an appeal made under regulation 83, the appeal body determines that the decision of the appropriate authority will be altered it will be the duty of that appropriate authority to give effect to the determination.
