# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 91

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Penalties[^c-214237bcf60d3e9ffe1a121baeb57813][^c-d6227d8b29a9bc91b9625f0eb0b59c0b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who is guilty of an offence under regulation 90(1), (3), (5), (7) or (8) will be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine.

(2) A person who is guilty of an offence under regulation 82(6), 89(10) or 90 (2), (2A),[^c-21e5de343019a791df200fd9fe8445c0] (4), (6), (9), (10), (11), (12), (13) or (15) will be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
