# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 96

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Revocations and Savings[^c-80078a447c620f4efaaec85cc8d3c707]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following instruments are revoked by these Regulations—

- (a) The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment (Amendment) Regulations ([SI 2007/3454](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/3454));
- (b) The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment (Amendment) Regulations 2009 ([SI 2009/2957](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2957));
- (c) The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2009 ([SI 2009/3216](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/3216));
- (d) The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment (Amendment) Regulations 2010 ([SI 2010/1155](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1155));

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4) the Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2006 ([SI 2006/3289](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/3289)) are revoked.

(3) Regulation 4 of the Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2006 ([SI 2006/3289](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/3289)) will continue to have effect.

(4) Regulation 66 and Schedule 11 of the Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2006 will continue to have effect in respect of appeals commenced on or before 31st December 2013.

(5) The provisions referred to in paragraphs (3) and (4) will continue to apply as they did immediately before their revocation for all of the purposes in relation to the collection, treatment and recycling of WEEE.
