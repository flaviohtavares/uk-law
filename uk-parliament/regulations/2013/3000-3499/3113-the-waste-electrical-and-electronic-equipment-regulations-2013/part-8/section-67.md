# The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2013, section 67

> Text as at 2025-08-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Record keeping[^c-69777f7dc88d40f91d1696d2f74aea25]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An AATF or an approved exporter must[^c-9870a55ae6f700a63de90efb4305f0f0] maintain records that enable completion of the reports referred to in regulation 66(1) and 66(2)[^c-4f5b4e5313cef8dd6c468476a6f6886b] for each quarter period in a relevant approval period.

(2) The records referred to in paragraph (1) must be kept for a period of at least four years commencing on the date on which any such record is made and must be made available to the appropriate authority on demand.
