# The School Organisation (Establishment and Discontinuance of Schools) Regulations 2013, section 17

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Time limits for local authority referrals to adjudicator prior to determination[^key-5920204ee5f4e8825bb5ca652571b6a5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In cases where the local authority are required to refer a proposal to the adjudicator[^key-c0a99a31002533321a3dda22dd13908e] under paragraph 10 of Schedule 2 to the Act they must make the referral[^key-655e741fec52c34eaf55926afa3f5bba] within two weeks of the end of the representation period.

(2) In cases where the local authority are required to refer proposals to the adjudicator under paragraph 13[^f00032] of Schedule 2 (duty to refer where determination delayed) to the Act, they must do so within one week of the end of the two month period referred to in regulation 14[^key-1e2b5e034f0eaea3de53a80bc548c4cc].
