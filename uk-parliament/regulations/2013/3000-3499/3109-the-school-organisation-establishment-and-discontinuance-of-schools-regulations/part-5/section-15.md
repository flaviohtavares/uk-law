# The School Organisation (Establishment and Discontinuance of Schools) Regulations 2013, section 15

> Text as at 2026-09-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Period in which proposals are to be determined under paragraph 19 of Schedule 2 to the Act[^key-6aed6d310e5d1e3368c707f2c4dc8b00]

*Extent: E+W*

The local authority’s determination under paragraph 19(1)[^f00020] of Schedule 2 to the Act must be made within two months of the end of the representation period.
