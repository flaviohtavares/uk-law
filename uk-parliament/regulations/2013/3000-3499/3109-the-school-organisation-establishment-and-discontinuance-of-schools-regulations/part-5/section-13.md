# The School Organisation (Establishment and Discontinuance of Schools) Regulations 2013, section 13

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Objections and comments[^key-ff23ed556b05c61f8cb68d6ef25adec3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the local authority are to consider the proposals under paragraph 8 of Schedule 2 to the Act, [^key-a6dd65cfa563289ffbdebd8d39e0b0a5]... the adjudicator is to consider proposals under paragraph 10 of Schedule 2 or the Secretary of State is to consider the proposals under paragraphs 10 or 12 of Schedule 2[^key-54aada28b5e850f54188a1574faf1f8e], any person may send objections or comments in relation to proposals published in accordance with sections 7, 10, [^key-0b70b91db71796e6fa7a4a445d368ea8]... or 15 to the local authority within four weeks of—

- (a) the date of publication of the proposals; [^key-e91dda792c2753a608e4efaf34364340]...
- (b)[^key-e91dda792c2753a608e4efaf34364340] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-cf4a02f0a0c0a1818a146cfaa746148a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-b144d11b4d890ee376b0865e6720f5f9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-3936a5235b79473aea58815c881f36f9] Within two weeks of the end of the representation period, the local authority must forward any objections or comments which they have received under paragraph (1)—

- (a) in cases where proposals have been referred to the adjudicator in accordance with paragraph 10 of Schedule 2 to the Act, to the adjudicator;
- (b) in cases where the local authority are to consider the proposals under paragraph 8 of Schedule 2 to the Act or proposals have been referred to the Secretary of State in accordance with paragraphs 10 or 12 of Schedule 2 to the Act, to the Secretary of State.

(5) Within two weeks of the end of the representation period, the local authority must forward any objections or comments of their own in relation to the proposals—

- (a) in cases where proposals have been referred to the adjudicator in accordance with paragraph 10 of Schedule 2 to the Act, to the adjudicator;
- (b) in cases where proposals have been referred to the Secretary of State in accordance with paragraphs 10 or 12 of Schedule 2 to the Act, to the Secretary of State.
