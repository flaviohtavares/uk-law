# The School Organisation (Establishment and Discontinuance of Schools) Regulations 2013, section 14

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Period in which proposals are to be determined under paragraph ... 8(4) of Schedule 2 to the Act[^key-08fe822a6e59d37a096fdfdb43e71369][^key-fb4e4efbee8cc55149d0bdfbe7a11a6d]

*Extent: E+W*

In cases where paragraph 13(1)(a) and (b) of Schedule 2 to the Act applies, the[^key-3a6135e3ecbfca5a0d68e1a09349d2ad] local authority must determine whether to give approval under paragraph 8(4)[^key-d26ff8ed5870a72dd71e2b14bbb77655] of Schedule 2 to the Act within two months of the end of the representation period.
