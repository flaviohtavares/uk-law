# The School Organisation (Establishment and Discontinuance of Schools) Regulations 2013, section 24

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Schools established outside the area of the relevant local authority[^key-259b48ee8544e762eda70e40a825d705]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Part 1 of Schedule 4 has effect for specifying the modifications to which section 7 and Schedule 2 to the Act are to be subject where proposals published under section 7 relate to a school which is proposed to be situated in an area other than that of the local authority which published the notice.

(2) Part 2 of Schedule 4 has effect for specifying the modifications to which section 10 [^key-20ac6c989cc35e1df1b8af5db0c9e204]... and Schedule 2 to the Act are to be subject where proposals published under section 10 [^key-563ad3fe4503e66ab25d3daad2b36587]... relate to a school which it is proposed be established in an area other than that of the local authority which it is proposed should maintain the school.
