# The School Organisation (Establishment and Discontinuance of Schools) Regulations 2013, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2026-09-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Information to be included in section 10 ... proposals to establish a new school[^c-daf481205b218fdf8eba7e29f58cdee7]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 8[^c-38bbd9d92930436c4115ec98e77d6e14]

### 1. Contact details[^c-3408b97feb8439bc328a72d094589bd1]

The name and contact address of the local authority or the proposers (as the case may be).

### 2. Implementation[^c-1b4edf63fc465c5221b4042e468a72f4]

The date on which it is proposed that the school be opened or, where it is proposed that the opening be implemented in stages, the dates of and information about each stage.

**3.**[^c-ff695c66af0eb343d604e56da2b1213e] A[^M_F_fb6e8be9-26a6-4a15-dfc9-022e1998b581] statement as to whether the proposals are to be implemented by the local authority or by the proposers, and if the proposals are to be implemented by both,

- (a) a statement as to the extent that they are to be implemented by each body, and
- (b) a statement as to the extent to which the capital costs of implementation are to be met by each body.

### 4. Reason for the new school[^c-c9db09ea4fe7bbd3067de49588dc48ee]

A statement explaining the reason why the new school is considered necessary and whether it is to replace an existing school or schools.

### 5. Category[^c-baf29bd4b31e2ded8d7ddd6333206dc7]

The category of school that it is proposed be established (a foundation or foundation special school and, if so, whether it is to have a foundation, a voluntary aided or voluntary controlled[^M_F_82ac4431-aee6-401b-a132-bf70332f833c] school, a community or community special school, a pupil referral unit[^M_F_3655971f-3d89-4372-d99f-d8ea4787c1e8] or a local authority maintained nursery school) [^M_F_a124f1ad-4cd5-4ea1-a8b6-08db9a13abe4]....

### 6. Ethos and religious character[^c-b8a4ca942bfdeca5d250cefbb5900b10]

A short statement setting out the proposed ethos of the school , any religious character which the school is to have and any philosophy[^M_F_92b84b5e-aed3-474d-a4e3-a2c7f2707aa2] the school will adhere to.

**7.**[^M_F_f4c9774c-332a-4149-f2d3-2a206857ccd4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**8.**[^c-abde511f846b8bfbd92124fce0312d1f] Where the statement mentioned in paragraph 6 sets out[^M_F_2b4b1444-d8ca-4b66-c416-7992b4f9a121]—

- (a) [^M_F_6fcc8ca6-d026-4077-b12b-b4dca53c7571]... a religious character, evidence of the demand in the area for education in accordance with the tenets of the religion; or
- (b) [^M_F_a2c66bf1-a5fe-4573-a849-3fedf8d70718]... a particular philosophy, evidence of the demand for education in accordance with that philosophy that is not already met in other maintained schools or Academies in the area.

### 9. Pupil numbers and admissions[^c-4dca16f12147351212c229d8b8567570]

The numbers (distinguishing between compulsory and non-compulsory school age pupils), age range, sex, and special educational needs of pupils (distinguishing between boarding and day pupils) for whom provision is to be made at the school and whether the school is to provide sixth form education[^M_F_fe2c069d-e96d-4bff-e6c2-ee4edff32522].

### 10. Admission arrangements[^c-de687e0e18ab9ded6a4a14bd8f097aa5]

The[^M_F_9d4fecca-6b0f-4a21-aa50-577d3682840b] proposed admission arrangements and over-subscription criteria which, subject to any contrary arrangements being determined pursuant to regulations made under section 72 of SSFA 1998, the school will have[^M_F_cd5051cb-9010-4897-cf43-d82086956bd6] including, where the school is proposed to be a foundation or voluntary school which is to have a religious character—

- (a) the extent to which priority for places is proposed to be given to children of the school’s religion or religious denomination; and
- (b) the extent, if any, to which priority is to be given to children of other religions or religious denominations or to children having no religion or religious denomination.

### 11. Early years provision[^c-8b11d5f5e8b11930b9a15f80ff0b8c71]

Where the proposals are to include provision for pupils below compulsory school age[^M_F_fd466ee1-cf59-4764-e6b7-79a728b39664]—

- (a) details of how the early years provision will be organised, including the number of full-time and part-time pupils, the number of places, the number and length of sessions in each week, and the services for disabled children that will be offered;
- (b)[^M_F_938c902f-6a01-4b87-9bf5-711872b1f6ef] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^M_F_938c902f-6a01-4b87-9bf5-711872b1f6ef] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^M_F_938c902f-6a01-4b87-9bf5-711872b1f6ef] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^M_F_938c902f-6a01-4b87-9bf5-711872b1f6ef] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### 12. Sixth form provision[^M_F_32c50275-27ef-41e7-e463-4cb012205252]

### 13. Special educational needs provision[^c-d82ce6a5a4a3d5f907351f1f6f35c8fe]

Whether the school will have provision that is recognised by the local authority as reserved for children with special educational needs and, if so, the nature of such provision.

**14.**[^c-b9d14993061da3c19d72eead47b93124] Details of how the school will be inclusive of pupils with special educational needs.[^M_F_15d30866-c8ab-411a-9655-ef4b1c7f0172]

**15.**[^c-2ce7e680911d24ca473932ed410b2fcd] Where the school will replace existing educational provision for children with special educational needs—

- (a) a statement on how the proposer believes the proposal is likely to lead to improvements in the standard, quality and range of educational provision for these children;
- (b)[^M_F_c2c51dbb-fd52-4e08-9f83-fcb4c7129e07] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### 16. Single sex school[^c-52232fcbd8e9f7f0931abf67ffc8f533]

Where the school is to admit pupils of a single sex—

- (a) evidence of local demand for single sex education and how this will be met if the proposals are approved; and
- (b) a statement giving details of the likely effect the new school will have on the balance of provision of single sex education in the area.

### 17. Curriculum[^c-087455ef2b87040966836348f5370699]

Confirmation that the school will meet the general requirements in relation to the curriculum contained in section 78 of EA 2002[^f00037] and an outline of any provision that will be in addition to the basic curriculum required by section 80 of EA 2002[^f00038], in particular any 14-19 vocational education.

### 18. Relevant experience of proposers[^c-3d9125dc78ba68f14b7e84af9ce54f51]

Evidence of any relevant experience in education held by the proposers including details of any involvement in the improvement of standards in education.

### 19. Effects on standards and contributions to school improvement[^c-5d75bc6f2211b4fcaad9e208ebdcde4e]

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### 20. Location and costs[^c-4d9f07e4c53b8fd5250389b8f82cf187]

A statement about—

- (a) the area or particular community or communities which the new school is expected to serve;
- (b) the location of the site or sites including, where appropriate, the postal address or addresses;
- (c) the current ownership and tenure (freehold or leasehold) on which the site will be held, and if the site is to be held on a lease, details of the proposed lease;
- (d) whether the site is currently used for the purposes of another school and if so why the site will no longer be required by the other school;
- (e) the estimated capital costs of providing the site and how those costs will be met (including the extent to which the costs are to be met by the proposers and the local authority) and how the proposers intend to fund their share of the costs of implementing the proposals (if any);
- (f) whether planning permission is needed under the Town and Country Planning Act 1990, and when it is anticipated that it will be obtained;
- (g) confirmation from the Secretary of State or local authority (as the case may be) that funds will be made available (including costs to cover any necessary site purchase).

### 21. Travel[^c-589cd4e7cc7aaca6c91bf4064ef5fdde]

The proposed arrangements for travel of pupils to the school.

### 22. Federation[^c-3e8c11231c8d7b8a2a1d3512b8b00008]

Details of any proposals for the school to be established as a federated school.

### 23. Voluntary aided schools[^c-4e8542447328ab1a1e23128cfc1dbdc6]

Where the school is to be a voluntary aided school—

- (a) details of the trusts on which the site is to be held; and
- (b) confirmation that the governing body will be able and willing to carry out their obligations under Schedule 3 to SSFA 1998.[^f00039]

### 24. Foundation schools[^c-877b68f615616bf6827def51d2c3c5cb]

Where the school is to be a foundation or foundation special school, confirmation as to—

- (a) whether it will have a foundation and if so, the name or proposed name of the foundation;
- (b) the rationale for the foundation and the particular ethos that it will bring to the school;
- (c) the details of membership of the foundation, including the names of the members;
- (d) the proposed constitution of the governing body; and
- (e) details of the foundation’s charitable objects.

### 25. Independent schools entering the maintained sector[^c-40cff14e623d3c7aa6cff21de70f3193]

Where a school is an independent school entering the maintained sector—

- (a)[^c-ae980f5268ffa7dfc039766bc3f81beb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a statement as to whether the premises will meet the requirements of the School Premises (England) Regulations 2012[^f00040] and, if not,
  - (i) details of how the premises are deficient; and
  - (ii) details of how it is intended to remedy the deficiency.
