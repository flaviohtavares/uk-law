# The School Organisation (Establishment and Discontinuance of Schools) Regulations 2013, section 2

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-886a29a8546307d2fc3ab9f9a01d43ea]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In these Regulations—

- “the Act” means the Education and Inspections Act 2006;
- “EA 1996” means the Education Act 1996[^f00002];
- “EA 2002” means the Education Act 2002[^f00003];
- “[^c-8857b7149782e663ae452aa5d403fe44]**alternative provision school**” means a pupil referral unit or an alternative provision Academy;
- [^c-bb1bc57048383c58cb2cf130ed172e05]...
- [^c-3fe63fbec68eb2a808a8429415113554]...
- “discontinuance proposals” means proposals published under section 15 by a local authority or by a governing body to discontinue a school;
- [^c-323dd7b44b67c98bd7dbadd4ac3cf7e5]...
- “[^c-c4570bca2b21d6066cd70026663abcc4]**mainstream school**” means a community school, foundation school, voluntary school, or an Academy school which meets the requirements of [section 1A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/1A)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/1A) of the [Academies Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/contents);
- “**notice**” means a notice under [section 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40/section/7) of the Act;
- “representation period” means the period in which objections or comments must be sent as set out in regulation 13(1);
- “revocation proposals” means proposals published under paragraph 21(4) of Schedule 2 to the Act;
- “sixth form education” means full-time secondary education suitable to the requirements of pupils over compulsory school age[^f00006];
- [^c-b4fa3b0445ef5190745e883663f26ce6]...
- “special school” means a community special or a foundation special school , or an Academy school which meets the requirements of[^c-697dd0e47542cfe779c6b823498613f2] [section 1A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/1A)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/section/1A) of the [Academies Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/32/contents); and
- “SSFA 1998” means the School Standards and Framework Act 1998.

(2) In these Regulations, any reference to sections 7, 10, 11, 12 and 15 is a reference to that section of the Act[^f00008].
