# The School Organisation (Establishment and Discontinuance of Schools) Regulations 2013, section 4

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Prescribed interval after which a ... notice may specify a date for the submission of proposals[^c-70336c6e8165d62f0a0c4ec089874640][^c-8737f3e8f9058a6a206ef6ec6a4d4446]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of section 7(3)(c), the prescribed interval is an interval of six[^c-cf16d27746ee84b514667991b7ce80a6] weeks from the date of publication of the [^M_F_8ba4c267-ae4a-4b5a-ab27-17d356aa158a]... notice.
