# The School Organisation (Establishment and Discontinuance of Schools) Regulations 2013, section 6

> Text as at 2026-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Proposals made pursuant to a ... notice[^key-d890a8d7523b929c504fadcb5c868c13][^key-10457bea59a20bfb586702801a8d5293]

*Extent: E+W*

Proposals made pursuant to a [^key-1141f89d43bd7baad957d6ffa67e93d7]... notice must [^key-43bba540bad345af33f9151390ca6f01]...—

- (a)[^key-d0c586897b266e8e6e350f5681b8a9b4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) confirm that the proposed school will meet each of the requirements set out in the specification which correspond to the matters in Part 2 of Schedule A1 or explain[^key-354aa49b4bce4de85c7edefab985962f] any differences between the information in the proposal and those requirements[^key-128cfab98f58cc4c7ba85864f370b752]; and
- (c) include[^key-7dd8aa1b747420b4fde5c747239cca9e] a statement describing the type and character (including any religious character) of the school and summarising the principal elements of the admission arrangements which the school will have, subject to any contrary admission arrangements which may be determined pursuant to regulations made under section 72 of the SSFA 1998[^key-606c04e0e8c42333d4f3f3761cd554d9];
- (d)[^key-df1b6f87c0fc171b292eefcb177fe289] specify which of the following categories of school it is proposed be established—
  - (i) community school;
  - (ii) community special school;
  - (iii) foundation school;
  - (iv) foundation special school;
  - (v) voluntary aided school;
  - (vi) voluntary controlled school;
  - (vii) Academy school which meets the requirements of section 1A(1) of the Academies Act 2010;
  - (viii) Academy school which meets the requirements of section 1A(2) of the Academies Act 2010;
  - (ix) alternative provision Academy; or
  - (x) pupil referral unit.
