# The Merchant Shipping (Prevention of Pollution) (Limits) (Revocation) Regulations 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 3042

Marine Pollution

The Merchant Shipping (Prevention of Pollution) (Limits) (Revocation) Regulations 2013

Made

3rd December 2013

Laid before Parliament

18th December 2013

Coming into force

31st March 2014

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Article 2 of the Merchant Shipping (Prevention of Pollution)(Law of the Sea Convention) Order 1996[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, makes the following Regulations.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Prevention of Pollution) (Limits) (Revocation) Regulations 2013 and come into force on 31st March 2014.

**2.** The Merchant Shipping (Prevention of Pollution) (Limits) Regulations 1996[^f00002] and the Merchant Shipping (Prevention of Pollution) (Limits) Regulations 1997[^f00003] are revoked.

Baroness Warsi

Senior Minister of State

Foreign and Commonwealth Office

3rd December 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations revoke the Merchant Shipping (Prevention of Pollution)(Limits) Regulations 1996 and the Merchant Shipping (Prevention of Pollution)(Limits) Regulations 1997, which are superseded by the declaration of an Exclusive Economic Zone under section 41 of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009.
