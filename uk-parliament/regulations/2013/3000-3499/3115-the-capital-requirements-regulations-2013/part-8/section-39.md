## 39. Meaning of “permission” and “protected item” in this Part

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-d5195c4dc92fddbf0015aae83e6cb0a6] In this Part—

- “**permission**” means a decision made by the PRA[^key-e9677f320edf4e8362b915e8c33d60d7] in relation to an institution under a power conferred on the PRA[^key-8074491464cf12dbba09f38b0654a1c7] by—
  - (a) the capital requirements regulation; [^key-37619606c8a10fa2549a235b15732cf2]...
  - (aa) section 144G of FSMA and CRR rules (as defined in Part 9D of FSMA);[^key-5d469905bc389030b00e30316dbe77b8]
  - (ab) section 192XC of FSMA and section 192XA rules (as defined in Part 12B of FSMA);
  - (b) any of the Specified EU Regulations under the Capital Requirements Regulation, as amended from time to time; or[^key-64d74ca10b0ae15e3f0d93e3b5114aa7]
  - (c) a technical standard made by the PRA[^key-95284fc48ddc38876b24a8c26e7dc4fd] [^key-964e939c3a653279101e5b29b18fcbd2]... under the functions conferred on them by the Capital Requirements Regulation as amended by Part 4, Chapter 3 of the Capital Requirements (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018.
- “**protected item**” has the same meaning as in section 413 of FSMA.
