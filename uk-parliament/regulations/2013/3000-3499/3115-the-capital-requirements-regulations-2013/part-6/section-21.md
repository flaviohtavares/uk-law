## 21. Assessment of equivalence of consolidated supervision by supervisory authorities in third countries[^key-a382e52e9684920f924093638e79f4f9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this regulation, a “relevant institution” is an institution which satisfies the following conditions—

- (a) the institution is authorised by the PRA [^key-25a3de461d796c60929ca0478011b0ec]...
- (b) the parent undertaking of the institution is an institution, financial holding company or mixed financial holding company whose head office is not located in the United Kingdom[^key-fa8b86585da80057ca9ba891986fcfe7];
- (c) the institution is not subject to supervision on a consolidated basis by the[^key-299d35f4776d310627fa91f981b4ce0d] [^key-3e10e4047b3df13213c3f32da17dfa9d]... PRA;
- (d) the PRA [^key-9e6bf10b24d08c50b3e0254b045aeb97]... would (but for paragraph (4)) be responsible for supervision of the institution on a consolidated basis; and
- (e) either—
  - (i) the parent undertaking of the institution, or any regulated entity established in the United Kingdom[^key-1bf185c12a62886905e54475a636dbc6] which is a member of the same group as the institution, has requested the assessment referred to in paragraph (2) be carried out; or
  - (ii) the PRA [^key-f3e774bd7689fa868d148e6eee025291]... has decided on its own initiative to carry out that assessment.

(2)[^key-ac969a4b7b191ef3d8a0e9cf9653213a] The PRA must assess whether a relevant institution is subject to supervision on a consolidated basis by a supervisory authority of a country outside the United Kingdom which is equivalent to the standard of supervision on a consolidated basis applied by the PRA in accordance with [Directive 2013/36/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0036) UK law, the capital requirements regulation and CRR rules.

(3)[^key-67a3a2a6d47ae1445c2d8a25d7992a6a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Where the PRA[^key-ae5f6448d008d8dde31cc7cf246a9e75] concludes that a relevant institution is not subject to equivalent supervision in accordance with paragraph (2), the PRA may apply—

- (a) the requirements of Directive 2013/36/EU UK law[^key-33c92383fd0bea80d2d231be934a29bc], the capital requirements regulation and CRR rules[^key-3052599902c788d162e2aaa6b6d626af] to the institution, amended as necessary; or
- (b) other appropriate supervisory techniques, which must be designed to achieve the objectives of supervision on a consolidated basis [^key-648445bb54e394eb44902ae43aaf3714]...

(5) The other appropriate supervisory techniques referred to in paragraph (4) may include a requirement on the institution to establish a financial holding company or mixed financial holding company with its head office in the United Kingdom[^key-c93168797292a96c6e628e1e156708dc], so that supervision on a consolidated basis may be applied in relation to the consolidated situation of that holding company.
