# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 140

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 140. Retention of proxies[^key-aa5a70055f86525526c594a064ad76a0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject as follows, proxies used for voting at any meeting shall be retained by the chair of the meeting.

(2) The chair shall deliver the proxies, as soon as reasonably practicable after the meeting, to the postal administrator (where that is someone other than the chair).
