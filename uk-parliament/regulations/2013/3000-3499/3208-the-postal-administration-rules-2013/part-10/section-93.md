# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 93

> Text as at 2018-02-26.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 93. Notification to Registrar of Companies[^c-4c9946bd1ec77db680e061ea93a57f26]

*Extent: E+W*

The replacement or additional postal administrator shall send notice of the appointment to the registrar of companies.
