# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 88

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 88. Notice of vacation of office when postal administrator ceases to be qualified to act[^key-6944c0bfe219491534110251d04862c6]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the postal administrator who has ceased to be qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the company gives notice in accordance with paragraph 89, the postal administrator shall also give notice to the registrar of companies.
