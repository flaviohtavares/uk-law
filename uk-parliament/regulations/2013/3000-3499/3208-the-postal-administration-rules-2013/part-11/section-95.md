# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 95

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 95. Preliminary[^c-65c367ec7953e14a43a4ab795ad49988]

*Extent: E+W*

This Chapter applies to any application made to the court in postal administration proceedings under the Rules, except an application for a postal administration order.
