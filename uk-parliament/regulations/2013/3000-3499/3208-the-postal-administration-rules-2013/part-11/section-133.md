# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 133

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 133. Service of orders staying proceedings[^c-16e4e73f98d9efa2db8e433d5f53d514]

*Extent: E+W*

Where in postal administration proceedings the court makes an order staying any action, execution or other legal process against the property of the company, service of the order may be effected by sending a sealed copy of the order to whatever is the address for service of the claimant or other party having the carriage of the proceedings to be stayed.
