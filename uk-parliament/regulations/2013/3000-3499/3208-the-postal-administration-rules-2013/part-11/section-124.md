# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 124

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 124. Introductory[^c-bef88f2fb4cd76b8184ebeff43a63411]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Rules in this Chapter apply where in postal administration proceedings it appears to the court that a person affected by the proceedings is one who lacks capacity within the meaning of the Mental Capacity Act 2005[^f00014] to manage and administer their property and affairs either—

- (a) by reason of lacking capacity within the meaning of the Mental Capacity Act 2005, or
- (b) due to physical affliction or disability.

(2) The person concerned is referred to as “**the incapacitated person**”.
