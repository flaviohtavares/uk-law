# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 131

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 131. Right of audience[^key-35faea55feb6f76190f79c7c946e84ce]

*Extent: E+W*

Rights of audience in postal administration proceedings are the same as in insolvency proceedings.
