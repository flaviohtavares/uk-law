# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 99

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 99. Notice of application under section 176A(5) of the 1986 Act[^key-9a62210caf05b75ff80e5f8f42ac952e]

*Extent: E+W*

An application under section 176A(5) of the 1986 Act may be made without the application being served upon or notice being given to any other party.
