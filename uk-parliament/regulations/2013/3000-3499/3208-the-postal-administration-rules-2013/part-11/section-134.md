# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 134

> Text as at 2018-02-26.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 134. Payment into court[^c-f9dbff1490048c618000999642ae9e65]

*Extent: E+W*

CPR Part 37 (miscellaneous provisions about payment into court) applies to money lodged in court under the Rules.
