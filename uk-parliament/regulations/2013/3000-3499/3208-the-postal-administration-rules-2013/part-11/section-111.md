# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 111

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 111. Remuneration[^c-08c48217b22d87ac0c40fadb5a02a506]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The remuneration of a shorthand writer appointed in postal administration proceedings shall be paid by the party at whose instance the appointment was made, or out of the assets of the company, or otherwise, as the court may direct.

(2) Any question arising as to the rates of remuneration payable under this Rule shall be determined by the court in its discretion.
