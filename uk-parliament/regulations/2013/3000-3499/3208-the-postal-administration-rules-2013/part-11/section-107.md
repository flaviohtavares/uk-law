# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 107

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 107. Proceedings commenced in wrong court[^key-43619b053874754b1f4dc6575e563496]

*Extent: E+W*

Where postal administration proceedings are commenced in a court which is, in relation to those proceedings, the wrong court, that court may—

- (a) order the transfer of the proceedings to the court in which they ought to have been commenced;
- (b) order that the proceedings be continued in the court in which they have been commenced; or
- (c) order the proceedings to be struck out.
