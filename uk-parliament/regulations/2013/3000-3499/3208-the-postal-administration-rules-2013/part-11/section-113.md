# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 113

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 113. Orders enforcing compliance with the Rules[^key-ef8b4867260a414e5ecd711f18a9863e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court may, on application by the postal administrator, make such orders as it thinks necessary for the enforcement of obligations falling on any person in accordance with—

- (a) paragraph 47 (duty to submit statement of affairs in postal administration), or
- (b) section 235 of the 1986 Act[^f00013] (duty of various persons to co-operate with postal administrator).

(2) An order of the court under this Rule may provide that all costs of and incidental to the application for it shall be borne by the person against whom the order is made.
