> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 126. Witness statement in support of application[^c-43c8f102eca6c793a372b28d6751f7e7]

*Extent: E+W*

An application under Rule 125(3) must be supported by a witness statement made by a registered medical practitioner as to the mental or physical condition of the incapacitated person.
