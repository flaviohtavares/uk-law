# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 127

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 127. Service of notices following appointment[^key-aec96cb1d689527179a13271379423bf]

*Extent: E+W*

Any notice served on, or sent to, a person appointed under Rule 125 has the same effect as if it had been served on, or given to, the incapacitated person.
