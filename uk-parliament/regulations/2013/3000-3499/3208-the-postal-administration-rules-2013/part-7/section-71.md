# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 71

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71. Notice of no dividend, or no further dividend[^c-06fb5e0f5e26f10d33ab502b0ce1f038]

*Extent: E+W*

If the postal administrator gives notice to creditors that the postal administrator is unable to declare any dividend or (as the case may be) any further dividend, the notice shall contain a statement to the effect either—

- (a) that no funds have been realised; or
- (b) that the funds realised have already been distributed or used or allocated for defraying the expenses of the postal administration.
