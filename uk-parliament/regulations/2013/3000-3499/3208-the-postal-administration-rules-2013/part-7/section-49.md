# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 49

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Withdrawal or variation of proof[^c-6dc79e0889e1b60192ea2fcc10a329a1]

*Extent: E+W*

A creditor’s proof may at any time, by agreement between themselves and the postal administrator, be withdrawn or varied as to the amount claimed.
