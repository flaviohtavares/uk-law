# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 54

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 54. Discounts[^c-5ea7faea7096b581017fda3c622f3fec]

*Extent: E+W*

There shall in every case be deducted from the claim all trade and other discounts which would have been available to the company but for its postal administration except any discount for immediate, early or cash settlement.
