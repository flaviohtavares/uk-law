# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 208

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 208. “Venue”[^c-053ae0d4473828faa14567830df96732]

*Extent: E+W*

References to the “venue” for any proceeding or attendance before the court, or for a meeting, are to the time, date and place for the proceeding, attendance or meeting.
