# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 211

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 211. Application of the 1986 Act[^c-6e85fb7091b98a1dd6725fb3b2ae12f4]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of the Rules, any reference in the 1986 Act to “leave” of the court is to be construed as meaning “permission” of the court.
