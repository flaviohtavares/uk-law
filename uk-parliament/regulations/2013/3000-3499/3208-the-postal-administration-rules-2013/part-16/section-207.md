# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 207

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 207. “Petitioner”[^c-65faa1ece1a87666db5a8f46d4a19308]

*Extent: E+W*

In winding-up, references to “the petitioner” include any person who has been substituted as such.
