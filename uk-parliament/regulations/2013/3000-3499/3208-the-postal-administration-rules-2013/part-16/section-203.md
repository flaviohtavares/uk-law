# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 203

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 203. “The appropriate fee”[^c-a947008f59107b1e22cf5697fadcc924]

*Extent: E+W*

“**The appropriate fee**” means 15 pence per A4 or A5 page, and 30 pence per A3 page.
