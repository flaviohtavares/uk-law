# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 197

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 197. Charges for copy documents[^key-f4528ffbbeb2962dda8a3e1a05f9953b]

*Extent: E+W*

Except where prohibited by the Rules, a postal administrator is entitled to require the payment of the appropriate fee for the supply of documents requested by a creditor or member.
