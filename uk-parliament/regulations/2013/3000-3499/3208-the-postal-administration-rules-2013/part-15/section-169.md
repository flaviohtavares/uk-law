# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 169

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 169. Notice etc to joint postal administrators[^c-13b625a185fad3416a14663deffb2128]

*Extent: E+W*

Where there are joint postal administrators, delivery of a document to one of them is to be treated as delivery to all of them.
