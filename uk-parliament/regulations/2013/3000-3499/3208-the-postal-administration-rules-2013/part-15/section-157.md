# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 157

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 157. Postal delivery of documents[^c-1d54d8aeab003d906464cb4b8d18a649]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless in any particular case some other form of delivery is required by the 1986 Act, the Rules or an order of the court, a notice or other document may be sent by post in accordance with the rules for postal service in CPR Part 6 and sending by such means has effect as specified in those rules.
