# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 186

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 186. Application of this Chapter[^c-c7e294a9ad5a63e5e780dc4bb8a116f2]

*Extent: E+W*

This Chapter applies where under the 1986 Act or the Rules information is to be sent or delivered to the registrar of companies.
