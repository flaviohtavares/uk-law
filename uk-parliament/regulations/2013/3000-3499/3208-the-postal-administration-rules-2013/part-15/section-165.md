# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 165

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 165. Electronic delivery by postal administrator[^c-4548013796e8dc1485480c8861a3e964]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a postal administrator gives, sends or delivers a notice or other document to any person by electronic means, the notice or document must contain or be accompanied by a statement that the recipient may request a hard copy of the notice or document and specifying a telephone number, e-mail address and postal address which may be used to request a hard copy.

(2) Where a hard copy of the notice or other document is requested, it must be sent within 5 business days of receipt of the request by the postal administrator.

(3) A postal administrator must not require a person making a request under paragraph (2) of this Rule to pay a fee for the supply of the document.
