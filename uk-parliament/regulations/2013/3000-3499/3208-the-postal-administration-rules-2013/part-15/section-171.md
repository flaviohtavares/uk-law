# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 171

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 171. Service on joint postal administrators[^c-90dc9ae48d632753a137164947558d28]

*Extent: E+W*

Where there are joint postal administrators, service on one of them is to be treated as service on all of them.
