# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 200

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 200. Postal administrator’s security[^key-e99f6bb1bf73edff2716b2a18b6933e4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Wherever under the Rules any person has to appoint a person to the office of postal administrator that person must, before making the appointment, be satisfied that the person appointed or to be appointed has security for the proper performance of the office of postal administrator.

(2) In any postal administration proceedings the cost of the postal administrator’s security shall be defrayed as an expense of the postal administration.
