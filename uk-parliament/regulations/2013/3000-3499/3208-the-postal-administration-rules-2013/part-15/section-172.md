# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 172

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 172. Application of CPR Part 6 to service of court documents outside the jurisdiction[^c-e78c31e8b252f53cf398466ed1fbfa4f]

*Extent: E+W*

CPR Part 6 applies to the service of court documents outside the jurisdiction with such modifications as the court may direct.
