# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 182

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 182. Omission of unobtainable information[^key-f412caf5727b105104c6fb1ebdcddd61]

*Extent: E+W*

Information required under this Chapter to be included in a notice to be gazetted may be omitted if it is not reasonably practicable to obtain it.
