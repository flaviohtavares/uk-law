# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 158

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 158. Non-receipt of notice of meeting[^key-b843e126a83e40734f06658ed45f14f0]

*Extent: E+W*

Where in accordance with the 1986 Act or the Rules, a meeting of creditors or other persons is summoned by notice, the meeting is presumed to have been duly summoned and held, notwithstanding that not all those to whom the notice is to be given have received it.
