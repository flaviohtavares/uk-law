# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 193

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 193. Notifications of more than one nature[^c-c8866fe7fd29034366a1af970d534d7c]

*Extent: E+W*

A notification which includes a notification of more than one nature must satisfy the requirements applying in respect of each of those notifications.
