# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 192

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 192. Notifications relating to other events[^key-071c4ce3024efe6263188f61fd31b648]

*Extent: E+W*

In addition to the information required by Rule 187, a notification relating to any other event (for example the coming in to force of a moratorium) must specify—

- (a) the nature of the event including the section of the 1986 Act or Rule under which it took place; and
- (b) the date the event occurred.
