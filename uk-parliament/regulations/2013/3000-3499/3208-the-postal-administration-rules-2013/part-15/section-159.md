# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 159

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 159. Notice etc to solicitors[^key-d8d64ea6a237400c8bd28b3ffef8d38b]

*Extent: E+W*

Where under the 1986 Act or the Rules a notice or other document is required or authorised to be given, delivered or sent to a person, it may be given, delivered or sent instead to a solicitor authorised to accept delivery on that person’s behalf.
