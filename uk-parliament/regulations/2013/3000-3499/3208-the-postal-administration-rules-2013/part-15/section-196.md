# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 196

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 196. Right to copy documents[^key-64353dbc73e1a14dca792418d54c46fb]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the 1986 Act or the Rules confer a right for any person to inspect documents, the right includes that of taking copies of those documents, on payment—

- (a) in the case of documents on the court’s file of proceedings, of the fee chargeable under any order made under section 92 of the Courts Act 2003[^f00018], and
- (b) in any other case, of the appropriate fee.
