# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 190

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 190. Notifications relating to court orders[^c-2c16784bcac17a2203fb7222d749db0a]

*Extent: E+W*

In addition to the information required by Rule 187, a notification relating to a court order must also specify—

- (a) the nature of the court order; and
- (b) the date of the order.
