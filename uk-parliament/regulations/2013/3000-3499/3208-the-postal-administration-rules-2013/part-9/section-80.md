# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 80

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80. Notification by postal administrator of court order[^key-2597dafdb9fbd8345df1d6d7a305dcaa]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the court makes an order to end the postal administration, the postal administrator must send to the registrar of companies a copy of the court order and a copy of the postal administrator’s final progress report.

(2) As soon as reasonably practicable, the postal administrator must send a copy of the order and the final progress report to all other persons who received notice of the postal administrator’s appointment.
