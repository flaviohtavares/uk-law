# The Postal Administration Rules 2013, section 78

> Text as at 2018-02-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 78. Final progress reports[^key-219bef0f41a6f633e854afdba1bf74fc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part reference to a progress report is to a report in the form specified in Rule 34.

(2) The final progress report means a progress report which includes a summary of—

- (a) the postal administrator’s proposals;
- (b) any major amendments to, or deviations from, those proposals;
- (c) the steps taken during the postal administration; and
- (d) the outcome.
