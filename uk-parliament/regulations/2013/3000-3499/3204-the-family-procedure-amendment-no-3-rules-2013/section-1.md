# The Family Procedure (Amendment No.3) Rules 2013, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, interpretation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Family Procedure (Amendment No.3) Rules 2013 and come into force on the date on which section 17(3) of the Crime and Courts Act 2013[^f00006] is brought fully into force.
