# The Ashton Vale to Temple Meads and Bristol City Centre Rapid Transit Order 2013, section 3

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Application of enactments relating to railways[^c-6bb430f16d4b15a2fd67d5af7075312a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The provisions of the Railway Regulation Acts 1840 to 1893[^f00011] do not apply in relation to the harbour railway system.

(2) The provisions of the Highway (Railway Crossings) Act 1839[^f00012] do not apply in relation to harbour railway system.
