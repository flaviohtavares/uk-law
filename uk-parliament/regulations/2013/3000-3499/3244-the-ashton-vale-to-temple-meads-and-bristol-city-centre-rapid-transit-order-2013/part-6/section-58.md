> Text as at 2017-01-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 58. Saving for highway authorities[^key-9cdf5f325615c903394eefe198b355af]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing in this Order affects any power of a highway authority to widen, alter, divert or improve any highway along which the authorised transport systems are constructed.
