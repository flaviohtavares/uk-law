# The Ashton Vale to Temple Meads and Bristol City Centre Rapid Transit Order 2013, section 57

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 57. Minerals[^key-50823186335144d9a636de4cf060e812]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing in this Order affects the right of any person entitled to any mine or minerals of any description whatsoever under a street along which the transport systems are laid to work the mine or get the minerals; but this does not affect any liability (whether civil or criminal) of the person so entitled in respect of damage to the transport systems resulting from the exercise of any such right.
