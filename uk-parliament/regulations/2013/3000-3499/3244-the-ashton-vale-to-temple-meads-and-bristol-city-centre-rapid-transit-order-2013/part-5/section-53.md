# The Ashton Vale to Temple Meads and Bristol City Centre Rapid Transit Order 2013, section 53

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 53. Notice of penalty fare provisions[^key-b413c5ffc83ee51bdb53cf7e7b3fd3ac]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) It is the duty of the promoter to secure that a warning notice meeting the requirements of paragraph (2) is posted—

- (a) at every rapid transit stop, in such a position as to be readily visible to prospective passengers; and
- (b) in every rapid transit vehicle, in such a position as to be readily visible to passengers travelling on that rapid transit vehicle.

(2) A warning notice posted under paragraph (1) must (however expressed) indicate the circumstances (as provided in article 50 (penalty fares)) in which passengers may be liable to pay a penalty fare and state the amount of the penalty fare.
