# The Ashton Vale to Temple Meads and Bristol City Centre Rapid Transit Order 2013, section 22

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 22. Obstruction of construction of authorised works[^c-75041c025decec1e3b7d0dbf36413a87]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any person who, without reasonable excuse—

- (a) obstructs another person acting under the authority of the promoter in setting out the lines of the scheduled works, or in constructing any of the authorised works; or
- (b) interferes with, moves or removes any apparatus belonging to any person acting under the authority of the promoter,

is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
