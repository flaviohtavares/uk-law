# The Ashton Vale to Temple Meads and Bristol City Centre Rapid Transit Order 2013, section 37

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. Power to charge fares[^key-9eb6a6910c3017b41a174d9753532fb4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The promoter may demand, take and recover or waive such charges for carrying passengers or goods on the transport systems, or for any other services or facilities provided in connection with the transport systems, as it thinks fit.

(2) The promoter may enter into and carry into effect agreements with other persons providing public passenger transport services with regard to the issue of tickets and the making of through ticketing arrangements or for the purpose of generally co-ordinating the provision of those services with the operation of the transport systems.

(3) In this article, “public passenger transport service” has the meaning given by section 63(10)(a) of the Transport Act 1985[^f00021].
