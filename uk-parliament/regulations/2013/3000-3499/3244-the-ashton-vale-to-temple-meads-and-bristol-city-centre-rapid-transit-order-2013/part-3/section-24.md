# The Ashton Vale to Temple Meads and Bristol City Centre Rapid Transit Order 2013, section 24

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 24. Application of Part 1 of the 1965 Act[^key-673eb11de6f2b7602e1ec3060ceaca64]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Part 1 of the 1965 Act, in so far as not modified by or inconsistent with the provisions of this Order, applies to the acquisition of land under this Order—

- (a) as it applies to a compulsory purchase to which the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00019] applies; and
- (b) as if this Order were a compulsory purchase order under that Act.

(2) Part 1 of the 1965 Act, as applied, has effect as if section 4 (which provides a time limit for compulsory purchase of land) and paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 3 (which makes provision as to the giving of bonds) were omitted.
