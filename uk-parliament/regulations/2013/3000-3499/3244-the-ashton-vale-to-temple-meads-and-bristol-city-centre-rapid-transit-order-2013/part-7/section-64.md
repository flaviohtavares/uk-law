# The Ashton Vale to Temple Meads and Bristol City Centre Rapid Transit Order 2013, section 64

> Text as at 2017-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 64. Certification of plans, etc.[^c-630587cf46f66634c023ea1b740d3f96]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The promoter must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the book of reference and the Order plans to the Secretary of State for certification that they are true copies of, respectively, the book of reference and the Order plans referred to in this Order; and a document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
