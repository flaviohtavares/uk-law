# The Transfer of Functions Concerning School Lunches etc. (England) (Amendment) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 3111

Education, England

The Transfer of Functions Concerning School Lunches etc. (England) (Amendment) Order 2013

Made

8th December 2013

Laid before Parliament

10th December 2013

Coming into force

1st January 2014

The Secretary of State for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 512A of the Education Act 1996[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Transfer of Functions Concerning School Lunches etc. (England) (Amendment) Order 2013 and comes into force on 1st January 2014.

## 2. Amendment

(1) The Education (Transfer of Functions Concerning School Lunches etc.) (England) (No. 2) Order 1999[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2(1) delete the words after “each maintained school in England” to the end of the sentence.

David Laws

Minister of State

Department for Education

8th December 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Education (Transfer of Functions Concerning School Lunches etc.) (England) (No. 2) Order 1999 so as to provide that the duty to provide school lunches in accordance with section 512(3) and (4) of the Education Act 1996, and the duty to provide school lunches free of charge in accordance with section 512ZB(1) of that Act, is transferred from local authorities to the governing body of each maintained school in England, irrespective of whether the school’s budget share includes a specific amount in respect of meals and other refreshment.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses, civil society organisations or on the public sector.
