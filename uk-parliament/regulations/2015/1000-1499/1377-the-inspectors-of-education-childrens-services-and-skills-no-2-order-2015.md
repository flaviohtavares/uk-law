# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1377

Education, England

Children And Young Persons, England

The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2015

Made

10th June 2015

Coming into force

11th June 2015

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of June 2015

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2015 and comes into force on 11th June 2015.

## 2. Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council
