# The Council of the City of Wakefield (Wakefield Eastern Relief Road) Bridge Scheme 2015 Confirmation Instrument 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1474

Highways, England

The Council of the City of Wakefield (Wakefield Eastern Relief Road) Bridge Scheme 2015 Confirmation Instrument 2015

Made

11th June 2015

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Secretary of State for Transport, makes this Instrument in exercise of the powers conferred by section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002]:

**1.** This Instrument may be cited as The Council of the City of Wakefield (Wakefield Eastern Relief Road) Bridge Scheme 2015 Confirmation Instrument 2015 and shall come into force on the date on which notice that it has been confirmed is first published in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Highways Act 1980.

**2.** The Council of the City of Wakefield (Wakefield Eastern Relief Road) Bridge Scheme 2015 (“the Scheme”) is hereby confirmed with modifications.

**3.** The Scheme as confirmed is set out in the Schedule hereto (including the plan) and copies of the Scheme with its accompanying plan are deposited at the offices of the Department for Transport, Floor 13 (IMD), Ashdown House, St Leonards on Sea, Hastings, East Sussex, TN37 7GA and at the offices of the Council of the City of Wakefield, Wakefield One, Burton Street, Wakefield, WF1 2EB.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

C J Miller

A Senior Civil Servant in the Department for Transport

11th June 2015

## SCHEDULE

Regulation 3

### THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF WAKEFIELD (WAKEFIELD EASTERN RELIEF ROAD) BRIDGE SCHEME 2015

The Council of the City of Wakefield (hereinafter referred to as “the Council”) make this Scheme in exercise of their powers under section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf—

**1.** The Council are authorised to construct over the navigable waters of the River Calder the Bridge specified in the Schedule to this Scheme as part of the highway which they are proposing to construct between the A638 Doncaster Road to the south and the A642 Aberford Road to the north as part of the Wakefield Eastern Relief Road scheme, in the Metropolitan District of Wakefield.

**2.** This Scheme may be cited as The Council of the City of Wakefield (Wakefield Eastern Relief Road) Bridge Scheme 2015.

Given under the Common seal of the Council

on the 19<sup>th</sup> day of March 2015.

THE COMMON SEAL of the Council was hereunto affixed in the presence of:

Authorised Signatory

## SCHEDULE TO THE SCHEME

Regulation 3

### Plans and Specifications of the Council of the City of Wakefield (Wakefield Eastern Relief Road) Bridge Scheme 2015

The location and general design of the Bridge are shown on drawing 1058431-SPEC-STR-001-B.

### Points of Commencement and Termination

Old Power Station Site Approach - From a point at the south bank of the River Calder 308 metres east of the railway bridge and 75 metres west of the outfall to Oakenshaw Beck.

Keyland (Yorkshire Water) Approach - From a point at the north bank of the River Calder 307 metres east of the railway bridge and 135 metres south west of the overhead power lines.

### Span

The bridge has a clear span of 58 metres.

### Headway

The headway shall not be less than 2.5 metres above the towpath and not be less than 5.0 metres above the river water level. This headway being determined by a water level of 19.320 above Ordnance Datum.

### Waterway

The entire navigable width of the waterway is maintained.

### Overall Dimensions

The overall Bridge width will be 13.5 metres. The Bridge will carry a 7.3 metre wide single carriageway. A 2 metre footway will be provided to the western side. On the eastern side there will be a 3 metre wide shared cycle way/footway.

Details can be seen on the Scheme Drawing No 1058431-SPEC-STR-001-B and related Drawings; 1058431/RCB/STR/100 and 1058431/RCB/STR/101.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Instrument)

This instrument confirms a scheme to construct over the navigable waters of the River Calder the Bridge as part of the highway which they are proposing to construct between the A638 Doncaster Road to the south and the A642 Aberford Road to the north as part of the Wakefield Eastern Relief Road scheme, in the Metropolitan District of Wakefield.

The Secretary of State has modified this scheme as follows:

(1) In the first line of the opening paragraph ‘make this Order’ has been amended to ‘make this Scheme’.

(2) In the first paragraph of the SCHEDULE TO THE SCHEME ‘1058431-SPEC-STR-001-A’ has been amended to ‘1058431-SPEC-STR-001-B’.

(3) In the paragraph titled OVERALL DIMENSIONS ‘A 2 metre footway will be provided to the eastern side. On the western side there will be a 3 metre wide shared cycle way/footway’ has been amended to ‘A 2 metre footway will be provided to the western side. On the eastern side there will be a 3 metre wide shared cycle way/footway’.

(4) In the Schedule to the Scheme the last sentence ‘Details can be seen on Drg No1058431-SPEC-STR-001-A; 1058431/RCB/STR/100 and 1058431/RCB/STR/101’ has been amended to ‘Details can be seen on Scheme Drawing No 1058431-SPEC-STR-001-B and related Drawings; 1058431/RCB/STR/100 and 1058431/RCB/STR/101.

(5) The title of the Plan ‘THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF WAKEFIELD (WAKEFIELD EASTERN RELIEF ROAD) SCHEME 2015’ has been amended to ‘THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF WAKEFIELD (WAKEFIELD EASTERN RELIEF ROAD) BRIDGE SCHEME 2015’.
