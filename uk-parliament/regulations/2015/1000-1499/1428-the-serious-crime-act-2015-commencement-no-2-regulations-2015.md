# The Serious Crime Act 2015 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1428 (C. 80)

Family Law, England And Wales

Family Law, Northern Ireland

The Serious Crime Act 2015 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2015

Made

25th June 2015

The Secretary of State, having consulted the Department of Justice in Northern Ireland in accordance with section 88(7)(e) of the Serious Crime Act 2015[^f00001], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 88(1) of that Act.

## 1. Citation

These Regulations may be cited as the Serious Crime Act 2015 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2015.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 17th July 2015

The following provisions of the Serious Crime Act 2015 come into force on 17th July 2015—

- (a) section 73 (female genital mutilation protection orders);
- (b) section 85(1) (minor and consequential amendments), for the purposes of the provisions listed in sub-paragraph (c) of this regulation; and
- (c) in Schedule 4 (minor and consequential amendments)—
  - (i) paragraph 5;
  - (ii) paragraph 13;
  - (iii) paragraph 17;
  - (iv) paragraph 87(2); and
  - (v) paragraph 88.

Mike Penning

Minister of State

Ministry of Justice

25th June 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are the second commencement regulations made under the Serious Crime Act [2015 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9) (“the 2015 Act”).

Regulation 2 brings into force on 17th July 2015 section 73 of the 2015 Act, which inserts new section 5A of and Schedule 2 to the Female Genital Mutilation Act [2003 (c. 31)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/31) to make provision for female genital mutilation protection orders in England and Wales and in Northern Ireland. Regulation 2 also brings into force on the same day consequential amendments in Schedule 4 to the 2015 Act that relate to these provisions.
