# The Norfolk County Council (Norwich Northern Distributor Road (A1067 to A47(T))) Order 2015, section 21

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 21. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-145f2a2530dff6a028bc5dfd80111ca4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00024] are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
