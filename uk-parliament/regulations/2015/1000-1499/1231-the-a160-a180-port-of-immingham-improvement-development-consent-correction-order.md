# The A160/A180 (Port of Immingham Improvement) Development Consent (Correction) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1231

Infrastructure Planning

The A160/A180 (Port of Immingham Improvement) Development Consent (Correction) Order 2015

Made

21st April 2015

Coming into force

22nd April 2015

The A160/A180 (Port of Immingham Improvement) Development Consent Order 2015 (“the original Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”), contains correctable errors as defined in paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 4 to the Act[^f00002].

Before the end of the relevant period, as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request for the correction of errors in the original Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed North East Lincolnshire Council and North Lincolnshire Council, the local planning authorities for the area in which the land to which the original Order relates is situated, that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order—

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the A160/A180 (Port of Immingham Improvement) Development Consent (Correction) Order 2015 and comes into force on 22nd April 2015.

**2.** In this Order, “the original Order” means the A160/A180 (Port of Immingham Improvement) Development Consent Order 2015.

## 3. Corrections to the original Order

(1) The original Order is corrected as follows.

(2) For the erroneous text in article 38 (certification of plans, etc.) of the original Order, as identified in columns (1) and (2) of the Schedule (correction of article 38) to this Order, substitute the text in column (3).

(3) In Schedule 2 (requirements) to the original Order—

- (a) in paragraph 3(1), after “CEMP” insert “for that part”;
- (b) in paragraph 10(1), after “surveys” insert “for that part”;
- (c) in paragraph 11(1), after “method statement” insert “for that part”;
- (d) in paragraph 12(1)—
  - (i) after “No” insert “part of the”; and
  - (ii) after “construction” insert “of that part”; and
- (e) in paragraph 14—
  - (i) for “The authorised development must not” substitute “No part of the authorised development is to”; and
  - (ii) for “as part of the scheme” substitute “for that part”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Martin Woods

Head of the Transport and Works Act Orders Unit

Department for Transport

21st April 2015

The Order corrects errors in the A160/A180 (Port of Immingham Improvement) Development Consent Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/129](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/129)), a development consent order under the Planning Act [2008 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/29), following a request under paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
