# The Deregulation Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I.2015/994 (C.69)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/994) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2015 No. 1405 (C. 78)

Deregulation

The Deregulation Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2015

Made

22nd June 2015

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 115(9) of the Deregulation Act 2015[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Deregulation Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2015.

## 2. Amendment of Order

(1) The Deregulation Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2015[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 13 (transitional provision) after “Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2,” insert “section 9 and Schedule 3”.

(3) In the Schedule, after Part 3 insert —

> **Part 3A**
>
> **Transitional provision relating to the commencement of section 9 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Act.**
>
> The amendments made by section 9 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Act do not affect sections 151 and 152 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00003] as they apply to any policy or security that has been cancelled —
>
> - (a) by mutual consent; or
> - (b) by virtue of any provision contained in it,
>
> before 30th June 2015.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Andrew Jones

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

22nd June 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Deregulation Act 2015 (Commencement No.1 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/994) (C.69)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/994) which brings the majority of the provisions of the Act into force.

This Order inserts an additional transitional provision so that the amendments made by section 9 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Act in respect of sections 151 and 152 of the Road Traffic Act [1988 (c.52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/52) only apply to policies or securities that are cancelled on or after 30th June 2015. This distinction affects the circumstances in which insurers can rely on section 152 of the Road Traffic Act 1988, in relation to their duty to satisfy judgments against persons insured or secured against third party risks under section 151 of the Road Traffic Act 1988.
