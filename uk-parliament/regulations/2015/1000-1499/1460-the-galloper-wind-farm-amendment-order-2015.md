# The Galloper Wind Farm (Amendment) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1460

Infrastructure Planning

The Galloper Wind Farm (Amendment) Order 2015

Made

2nd July 2015

Coming into force

3rd July 2015

An application has been made to the Secretary of State under paragraph 2 of Schedule 6 to the Planning Act 2008[^f00001] (“the 2008 Act”), and in accordance with Part 1 of the Infrastructure Planning (Changes to, and Revocation of, Development Consent Orders) Regulations 2011[^f00002] (“the 2011 Regulations”), for a change which is not material to the Galloper Wind Farm Order 2013[^f00003].

The Secretary of State, having considered the application and the responses to the consultation under regulation 7 of the 2011 Regulations, has determined to make an Order giving effect to the proposed change comprised in the application which in the opinion of the Secretary of State does not make any material change to the Galloper Wind Farm Order 2013.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 153 of, and paragraph 2 of Schedule 6 to, the Act, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Galloper Wind Farm (Amendment) Order 2015 and comes into force on 3rd July 2015.

## 2. Amendment to the Galloper Wind Farm Order 2013

(1) The Galloper Wind Farm Order 2013 is amended as follows.

(2) In Part 3 of Schedule 1, in paragraph 8(1), for “7 metres” substitute “7.5 metres”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy and Climate Change

Giles Scott

Head of National Infrastructure Consents and Coal Liabilities

Department of Energy and Climate Change

2nd July 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Galloper Wind Farm Order 2013, a development consent order under the Planning Act 2008, following an application under paragraph 2 of Schedule 6 to that Act for a non-material change to increase the diameter of the monopile foundations from 7.0 metres to 7.5 metres in order to allow the foundations to meet the requirements needed to satisfy the turbine design limits. The change to the Order takes effect from the date specified in the Order.
