# The Swansea Bay Tidal Generating Station Order 2015, section 45

> Text as at 2024-11-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023[^key-4fcf23b24d30c03d90210d832d1f1c04][^key-a0d03246a15f745b5b707fd6001d88f2]

*Extent: E+W*

This Order has effect as a consent under the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023[^key-85a9f949b43af524132514724ae474dc] in respect of the authorised development in respect of the pillboxes shown on planning drawings nos. 2.4.42 and 2.4.43 and tank traps located on the existing Swansea Port sea wall irrespective of the date upon which any such features are included in a Schedule under that Act.
