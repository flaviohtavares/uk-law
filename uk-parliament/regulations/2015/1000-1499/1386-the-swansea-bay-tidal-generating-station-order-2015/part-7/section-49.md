# The Swansea Bay Tidal Generating Station Order 2015, section 49

> Text as at 2024-11-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Certification of plans, etc.[^key-6722057b485c5118d77c4af828fea83e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker must, as soon as practicable after the date on which this Order is made, submit to the Secretary of State copies of the documents listed in Schedule 7 (documents to be certified) for certification that they are true copies of the plans or documents referred to in this Order.

(2) A plan or document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
