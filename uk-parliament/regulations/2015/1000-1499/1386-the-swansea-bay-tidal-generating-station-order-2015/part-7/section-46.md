# The Swansea Bay Tidal Generating Station Order 2015, section 46

> Text as at 2024-11-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Licences relating to water, etc.[^key-ab06fe0895624e7ca81d7bdbafaa71ef]

*Extent: E+W*

The requirement under section 25 of the Water Resources Act 1991[^f00028] to obtain a licence before constructing, altering, repairing or removing any impoundment works does not apply to the authorised development.
