# The Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 2015/627](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/627) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2015 No. 1359

Town And Country Planning, England

The Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

Made

8th June 2015

Laid before Parliament

9th June 2015

Coming into force

10th June 2015

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5 of the Planning (Hazardous Substances) Act 1990[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

These Regulations may be cited as the Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Amendment) Regulations 2015, apply to England only, and come into force on 10th June 2015.

## 2. Amendment of the Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations 2015

(1) Schedule 1 to the Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations 2015[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In Part 2, in column 2 of entry 18, after “Liquefied petroleum gas: 25” add the symbol “\*”.

(3) In Part 4—

- (a) in paragraph 5, in the definition of Q<sub>LX</sub>,<sub> </sub>before “Natural Gas (including liquefied natural gas)” insert “Liquefied petroleum gas and”;
- (b) in paragraph 17(a), for “notes 14 and 15” in both places it appears, substitute “notes 15 and 16”; and
- (c) in paragraph 17(b), for “note 13(a) and 14(a)”, substitute “note 14(a) and 15”.

Signed by the authority of Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

James Wharton

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

8th June 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This statutory instrument corrects errors in Schedule 1 to The Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations 2015. The impact will be as set out in the impact assessment for those Regulations and this instrument by itself has no additional impact. The impact assessment for The Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations 2015 is available from the Department for Communities and Local Government, 2 Marsham Street, London SW1P 4DF, and is published with the Explanatory Memorandum alongside those Regulations on www.legislation.gov.uk.
