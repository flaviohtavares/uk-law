# The Prosecution of Offences Act 1985 (Criminal Courts Charge) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1970

Criminal Law, England And Wales

The Prosecution of Offences Act 1985 (Criminal Courts Charge) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

Made

2nd December 2015

Laid before Parliament

3rd December 2015

Coming into force

24th December 2015

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 21C(1) and 29 of the Prosecution of Offences Act 1985[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Prosecution of Offences Act 1985 (Criminal Courts Charge) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 24th December 2015.

## 2. Amendments to the Prosecution of Offences Act 1985 (Criminal Courts Charge) Regulations 2015

Omit regulation 3 (amount of the criminal courts charge) of, and the Schedule to, the Prosecution of Offences Act 1985 (Criminal Courts Charge) Regulations 2015[^f00002].

Michael Gove

Lord Chancellor

Ministry of Justice

2nd December 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Prosecution of Offences Act 1985 (Criminal Courts Charge) Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/796](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/796)) to omit regulation 3 and the Schedule, which specified the amount of the criminal courts charge payable in different classes of case.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
