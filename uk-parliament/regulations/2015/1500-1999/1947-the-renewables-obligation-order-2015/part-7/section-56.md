# The Renewables Obligation Order 2015, section 56

> Text as at 2026-03-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Generating stations using peat

*Extent: E+W*

ROCs are not to be issued in respect of any electricity generated by a generating station during a month in which it generates electricity wholly or partly from peat.
