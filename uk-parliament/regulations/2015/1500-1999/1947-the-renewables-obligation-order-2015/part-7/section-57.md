# The Renewables Obligation Order 2015, section 57

> Text as at 2026-03-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 57. Generating stations using landfill gas

*Extent: E+W*

ROCs are not to be issued in respect of any electricity generated by a generating station from landfill gas unless the electricity is generated—

- (a) by a generating station to which article 34 applies,
- (b) using pre-2013 capacity,
- (c) in the way described in Schedule 5 as “closed landfill gas”, or
- (d) using the heat from a turbine or engine.
