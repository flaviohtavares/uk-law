# The Renewables Obligation Order 2015, section 59

> Text as at 2026-03-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 59. Generating stations using Solid Recovered Fuel

*Extent: E+W*

ROCs are not to be issued in respect of any electricity generated by a generating station from Solid Recovered Fuel unless—

- (a) the Solid Recovered Fuel constitutes biomass, or
- (b) the generating station is a qualifying CHP station.
