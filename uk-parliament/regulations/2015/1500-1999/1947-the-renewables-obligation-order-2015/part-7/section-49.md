# The Renewables Obligation Order 2015, section 49

> Text as at 2026-03-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Electricity in respect of which a CFD or investment contract applies

*Extent: E+W*

ROCs are not to be issued in respect of any electricity in respect of which a CFD or investment contract applies.
