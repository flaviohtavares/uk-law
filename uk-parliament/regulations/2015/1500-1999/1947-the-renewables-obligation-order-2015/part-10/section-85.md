# The Renewables Obligation Order 2015, section 85

> Text as at 2026-03-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 85. Determination of matters by the Authority

*Extent: E+W*

Where this Order provides for any matter to be determined by the Authority—

- (a) the Authority may require the operator of the generating station to demonstrate to the Authority's satisfaction how that matter is to be determined, and
- (b) the Authority is entitled to have regard to any material (whether or not produced to it by the operator of the generating station) if, in its opinion, that material indicates how the matter is to be determined.
