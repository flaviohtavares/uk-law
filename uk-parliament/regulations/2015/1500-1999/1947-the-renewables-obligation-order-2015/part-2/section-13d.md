## 13D. Determining the number of UK ROCs to be produced by a designated electricity supplier in order to discharge its renewables obligation: obligation periods beginning on or after 1st April 2027[^c-ec088d02ddfcb2fa0a6d26a041f7de12]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This article applies in respect of an obligation period beginning on or after 1st April 2027.

(2) Before the start of an obligation period to which this article applies, the Secretary of State must estimate, in megawatt hours—

- (a) the total amount of EII excluded electricity, and
- (b) the total amount of BICS excluded electricity,

likely to be supplied to customers in Great Britain during that period by—

- (i) designated electricity suppliers, and
- (ii) electricity suppliers on whom the renewables obligation has been imposed by an order made under sections 32 to 32M of the Act by the Scottish Ministers.

- (b) D is the estimate made by the Secretary of State under paragraph (2)(a) of the total amount of EII excluded electricity likely to be supplied to customers in Great Britain during the obligation period;
- (c) E is equal to—

  [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1947)
- (d) F is the estimate made by the Secretary of State under paragraph (2)(b) of the total amount of BICS excluded electricity likely to be supplied to customers in Great Britain during the obligation period.

(6) The Secretary of State must publish, by the 1st October preceding the obligation period, the number of UK ROCs that a designated electricity supplier is required to produce in respect of each megawatt hour of relevant electricity that it supplies during that period in order to discharge its renewables obligation for that period.
