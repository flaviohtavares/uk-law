# The Renewables Obligation Order 2015, section 7

> Text as at 2026-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. The renewables obligation

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The renewables obligation is imposed on each electricity supplier supplying electricity in England and Wales (a “designated electricity supplier”).

(2) The renewables obligation is that, subject to articles 67 and 68, each designated electricity supplier must, by the specified day, produce to the Authority, in respect of each megawatt hour of relevant electricity that it supplies[^c-d302a83f445750c38c80cf330b23f148] during an obligation period, the number of UK ROCs determined in accordance with articles 13 to[^c-53075bceb13c0559199bd642e5bf2eff] 13E[^c-e1031e992214f3e9c1ec5ff1c4a1def0].

(3) To enable the number referred to in paragraph (2) to be determined, the Secretary of State must first determine, for the obligation period in question, calculations A and B and the total number of UK ROCs required to be produced by designated electricity suppliers in accordance with articles 8 to 12.

(4) Where the number of UK ROCs that a designated electricity supplier is required to produce by virtue of paragraph (2) is not a whole number, it is to be rounded to the nearest whole number (one-half being rounded upwards).

(5)[^c-d8daf779a0551e6b3e022125552fcf5e] In this Part, “relevant electricity” means—

- (a) in respect of—
  - (i) any obligation period beginning on or after 1st April 2019 and ending on or before 31st March 2027[^c-637279bc25ea54ec89f84e1fe80c70ad]; or
  - (ii) any month of an obligation period in respect of which a revised obligation level applies in accordance with article 13B(7)(a)(ii), (b)(ii), (8)(a) or (b)(ii),

  any electricity supplied to customers in England and Wales other than EII excluded electricity; [^c-11f08d74e57a12681277ad3a6826dd64]...
- (aa)[^c-c91f7c33f5273fe86432b4271d307d97] in respect of—
  - (i) any obligation period beginning on or after 1st April 2027; or
  - (ii) any month of an obligation period in respect of which a revised obligation level applies in accordance with article 13E,

  any electricity supplied to customers in England and Wales other than EII excluded electricity and BICS excluded electricity; and
- (b) in respect of any other period, any electricity supplied to customers in England and Wales.
