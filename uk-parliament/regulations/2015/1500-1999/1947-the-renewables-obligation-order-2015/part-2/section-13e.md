# The Renewables Obligation Order 2015, section 13E

> Text as at 2026-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13E. Revision of the obligation level published on or before 1st October 2026 in respect of the 2027/28 obligation period[^c-ec088d02ddfcb2fa0a6d26a041f7de12]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may revise the obligation level published on or before 1st October 2026 in respect of the 2027/28 obligation period.

(2) The power conferred by paragraph (1) may only be exercised once and may not be exercised after 31st March 2027.

(3) Before exercising the power conferred by paragraph (1), the Secretary of State must estimate, in megawatt hours, both—

- (a) the total amount of EII excluded electricity, and
- (b) the total amount of BICS excluded electricity,

likely to be supplied to customers in Great Britain during the 2027/28 obligation period by—

- (i) designated electricity suppliers, and
- (ii) electricity suppliers on whom the renewables obligation has been imposed by an order made under sections 32 to 32M of the Act by the Scottish Ministers.

(4) A revision of the obligation level for the 2027/28 obligation period is to be calculated as follows—

- (a) where the total obligation for the 2027/28 obligation period is calculation A, revision of that obligation level is to be calculated in accordance with article 13D(3);
- (b) where the total obligation for the 2027/28 obligation period is calculation B, revision of that obligation level is to be calculated in accordance with article 13D(4).

(5) Where, in exercise of the power conferred by paragraph (1), the Secretary of State revises the obligation level in respect of the 2027/28 obligation period, the Secretary of State must publish the revised obligation level within the period of seven days beginning with the day on which that power is exercised.

(6) In exercising the power conferred by paragraph (1), the Secretary of State may provide that the obligation level published on or before 1st October 2026 applies for part of the 2027/28 obligation period and the revised obligation level as calculated in accordance with paragraph (4) applies for the remainder of the 2027/28 obligation period.

(7) In this article—

- “**2027/28 obligation period**” means the obligation period beginning on 1st April 2027;
- “**obligation level**” means the number of UK ROCs that a designated electricity supplier is required to produce in respect of each megawatt hour of relevant electricity that it supplies to customers in England and Wales during an obligation period in order to discharge its renewables obligation for that period as determined under article 13D(3) or (4).
