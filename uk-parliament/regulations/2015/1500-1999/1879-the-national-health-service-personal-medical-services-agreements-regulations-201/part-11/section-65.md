# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 65

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65. Confidentiality of personal data: nominated person

*Extent: E+W*

The contractor must nominate a person with responsibility for practices and procedures relating to the confidentiality of personal data held by it.
