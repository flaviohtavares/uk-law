# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 71

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71. Practice leaflet

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A contractor which provides essential services must compile a document (a “practice leaflet”) which must include the information specified in Part 6 of Schedule 2.

(2) The contractor must review its practice leaflet at least once in every period of 12 months and make any amendments necessary to maintain its accuracy.

(3) The contractor must make available a copy of the leaflet, and any subsequent updates, to its patients and prospective patients.
