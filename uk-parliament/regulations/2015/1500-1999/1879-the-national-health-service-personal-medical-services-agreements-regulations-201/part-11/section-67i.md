# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 67I

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 67I. Collection of data relating to use of the online consultation tool and video consultations[^key-116a46fdce677eda1c01c5d30a5e5bc7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The contractor must make available to NHS England such information as is specified by NHS England that is available to the contractor in connection with use of the online consultation tool under regulation 64ZD and video consultations under regulation 64ZF.

(2) The contractor must make information available to NHS England under paragraph (1) within such reasonable time frame as may be specified by NHS England.
