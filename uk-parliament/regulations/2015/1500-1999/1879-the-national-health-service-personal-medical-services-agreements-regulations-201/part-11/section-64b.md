# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 64B

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 64B. Patient access: other availability of directly bookable appointments[^c-c33257c8acb8840f94f4b77627efc671]

*Extent: E+W*

A contractor must ensure that all of its directly bookable appointments are made available for booking by telephone or in person.
