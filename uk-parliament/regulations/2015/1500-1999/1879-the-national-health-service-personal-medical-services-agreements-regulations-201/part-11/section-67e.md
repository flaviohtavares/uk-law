# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 67E

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 67E. NHS England Workforce Collection[^key-67ccf55ee844f4c1c9c21a716bdcce0f][^key-5df31569bcb410a3531dd930e9c64e29][^key-289f02903579890afa2e88be2b7162be]

*Extent: E*

(1) A contractor must record and submit any data required by NHS England[^key-3a20a1f888478c6d3e111deeee7dd59e] for the purposes of the NHS Workforce Collection[^key-ec617724230cca2c57658051cfc4652a] (known as the “Workforce Minimum Data Set”) in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2)[^key-e21c30f7d772cb28efc1ec7c864c5eb2] The data referred to in paragraph (1) must be—

- (a) appropriately coded, reviewed and updated by the contractor in line with agreed standards set out in guidance published by NHS England;
- (b) submitted to NHS England—
  - (i) using the data entry module on the National Workforce Reporting Service, which is a facility provided by NHS England to the contractor for this purpose; and
  - (ii) at such intervals during the financial year as are notified to the contractor by NHS England.
