# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 61

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 61. Summary Care Record

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A contractor which provides essential services must, in any case where there is a change to the information included in a patient's medical record, enable the automated upload of summary information to the Summary Care Record, when the change occurs[^c-71e87547b460f87f279c7a2c62817c42], using approved systems provided to it by NHS England[^c-9520329cffeb7671913a444ac58ebf84].

(2) In this regulation—

- “**Summary Care Record**” means the system approved by NHS England for the automated uploading, storing and displaying of patient data relating to medications, allergies, adverse reactions and, where agreed with the contractor and subject to the patient's consent, any other data (other than any information recorded in accordance with regulation 60A)[^c-28d53b49346585f5026a911e706fcf02] or any information about ethnicity provided under regulation 64C[^c-f827766e0bddf8892458d79650086f21] taken from the patient's electronic record; and
- “**summary information**” means items of patient data that comprise the Summary Care Record.
