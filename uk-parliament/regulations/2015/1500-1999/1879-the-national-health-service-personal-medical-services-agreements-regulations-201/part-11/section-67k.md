# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 67K

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 67K. NHS staff surveys[^key-478e0dde76d216fa2120b48ae5b9ff99]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The contractor must make available to NHS England such information as specified by NHS England in connection with staff surveys that relate to the contractor’s staff.

(2) The contractor must make information available to NHS England under paragraph (1) within such reasonable time frame as may be specified by NHS England.
