## 64ZG. Meaning of “appropriate software” for the purposes of regulations 64ZD, 64ZE and 64ZF[^c-921894a871b2468308ebbfa5dcba65bc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of regulations 64ZD, 64ZE and 64ZF software which is used to provide a method of communication or facility (as the case may be) is appropriate if the software meets—

- (a) the requirements in the GPIT Operating Model relevant to that software, or
- (b) requirements which are equivalent in their effect to the relevant requirements in the GPIT Operating Model.
