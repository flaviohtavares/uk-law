# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 7

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Right of appeal

*Extent: E+W*

A person who has been given a notice by NHS England[^key-9520329cffeb7671913a444ac58ebf84] under regulation 6(1) may appeal to the First-tier Tribunal [^c22689971] against the decision of NHS England that the conditions in regulation 5 are not met.

[^c22689971]: Marginal citation: An appeal may be made to the First-tier Tribunal (Primary Health Lists) against a decision by the National health Service Commissioning Board to refuse to enter a person in a list, to remove a person from a list or regarding the conditions relating to that person's entry in a list. The First-tier Tribunal was established in 2008 by Part 1 of the [Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (c.15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/15). The Health, Education and Social Chamber is responsible for hearing appeals concerning matters relating to the Health Service in England and Wales.
