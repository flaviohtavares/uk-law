# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 4

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Conditions: general

*Extent: E+W*

(1) NHS England[^c-9520329cffeb7671913a444ac58ebf84] may only enter into an agreement if the conditions specified in regulation 5 are met.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to the provisions of any scheme made by the Secretary of State under section 300 (transfer schemes) (before its repeal), section 302 (transfer schemes in respect of previously transferred property)[^c-f8892ed4cfa157716c05b000de96357b] and section 303 (power to make consequential provision) of the Health and Social Care Act 2012 [^c22689791].
