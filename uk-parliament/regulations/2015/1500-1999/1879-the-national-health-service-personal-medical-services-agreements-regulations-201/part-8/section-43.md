# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 43

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Doctors with provisional registration

*Extent: E+W*

A contractor may not, by reason only of having employed or engaged a person who is—

- (a) provisionally registered under section 15, 15A or 21 of the Medical Act 1983 [^c22690141]; and
- (b) acting in the course of that person's employment in a resident medical capacity in a post-registration programme,

reduce the total number of hours in which other staff assist in the performance of medical services under the agreement.
