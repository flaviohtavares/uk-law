# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 48

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 48. Prescribing: general

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The contractor must ensure that—

- (a) any prescription form or repeatable prescription issued or created by a prescriber;
- (b) any home oxygen order form issued by a health care professional; and
- (c) any listed prescription items[^c-3056dfe05e092b7593ab4f36515e7f78] voucher issued by a prescriber or any other person acting under the agreement,

complies as appropriate with the requirements in regulations 49, 50 and 52 to 55.

(2)[^c-5e5ec8d31611c7467ff19459353c72a6] In regulations 49, 50 and 52 to 56, a reference to “drugs” includes contraceptive substances and a reference to “appliances” includes contraceptive appliances.
