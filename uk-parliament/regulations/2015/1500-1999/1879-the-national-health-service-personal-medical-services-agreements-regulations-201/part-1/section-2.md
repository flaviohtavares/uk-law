# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 2

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Application

*Extent: E+W*

These Regulations apply to an agreement—

- (a) to which the National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2004 [^c22689231] applied immediately before the date on which these Regulations come into force; or
- (b) which is entered into between a contractor and NHS England[^key-9520329cffeb7671913a444ac58ebf84] on or after that date.
