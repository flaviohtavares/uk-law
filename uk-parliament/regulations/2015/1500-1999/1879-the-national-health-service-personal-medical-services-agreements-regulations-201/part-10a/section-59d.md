# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 59D

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 59D. Vaccines and immunisations: catch-up campaigns[^key-f51426e6fdab03ade6c6903a2e4987a4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The contractor must participate in a manner reasonably required by NHS England[^key-9520329cffeb7671913a444ac58ebf84] in one vaccines and immunisations catch-up campaign in each financial year.

(2) In this regulation “vaccines and immunisations catch-up campaign” means a campaign which is aimed at maximising the uptake of a particular vaccine or immunisation by patients who are eligible for it but have not received that vaccine or immunisation for any reason (other than a decision to refuse the vaccine or immunisation).
