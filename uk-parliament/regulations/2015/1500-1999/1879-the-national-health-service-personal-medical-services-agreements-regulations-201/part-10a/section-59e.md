# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 59E

> Text as at 2026-06-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 59E. Vaccines and immunisations: additional staff training[^key-f51426e6fdab03ade6c6903a2e4987a4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The contractor must ensure that all staff involved in the administration of vaccines and immunisations are trained in the recognition and initial treatment of anaphylaxis.

(2) This regulation does not affect the contractor’s obligations under Part 8.
