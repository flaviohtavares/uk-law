# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 59G

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 59G. Vaccines and immunisations: exception for private arrangements[^key-f51426e6fdab03ade6c6903a2e4987a4]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in this Part applies in relation to the offer or administration of any vaccine or immunisation to a patient under a private arrangement.
