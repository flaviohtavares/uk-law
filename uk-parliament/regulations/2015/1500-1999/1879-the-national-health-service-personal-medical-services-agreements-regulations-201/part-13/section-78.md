# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 78

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 78. Interpretation of this Part

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part, “**any dispute arising out of or in connection with the agreement**” includes any dispute arising out of or in connection with the termination of the agreement.

(2) A term of the agreement which makes provision in respect of the requirements of this Part is to survive even where the agreement has terminated.
