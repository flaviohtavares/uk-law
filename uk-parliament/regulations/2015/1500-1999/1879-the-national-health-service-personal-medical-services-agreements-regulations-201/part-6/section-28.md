# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 28

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Opt outs: interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**out of hours opt out notice**” means a notice given under regulation 30(1) to opt out permanently of the provision of out of hours services;
- “OOH day” is the day specified by the contractor in the out of hours opt out notice which the contractor gives to NHS England[^key-9520329cffeb7671913a444ac58ebf84] for the commencement of the out of hours opt out;
- “B day” is the day six months after the date on which the out of hours opt out notice was given; and
- “C day” is the day nine months after the date on which the out of hours opt out notice was given.
