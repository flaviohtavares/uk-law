# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 31

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Informing patients of opt outs

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Before any out of hours opt out takes effect, NHS England[^key-9520329cffeb7671913a444ac58ebf84] and the contractor must discuss how to inform the contractor's patients of the proposed opt out.

(2) The contractor must, if requested by NHS England, inform its registered patients of an opt out and the arrangements made for them to receive the out of hours services by—

- (a) placing a notice in the contractor's waiting rooms; or
- (b) including the information in the contractor's practice leaflet.
