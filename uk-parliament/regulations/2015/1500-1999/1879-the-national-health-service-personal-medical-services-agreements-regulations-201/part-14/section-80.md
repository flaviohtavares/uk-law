# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 80

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80. Friends and Family Test

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A contractor which provides essential services must give all patients who use the contractor's practice the opportunity to provide feedback about the service received from the contractor's practice through the Friends and Family Test [^c22690431].

(2) The contractor must—

- (a) report the results of completed Friends and Family Tests to NHS England[^c-9520329cffeb7671913a444ac58ebf84]; and
- (b) publish the results of such completed Tests [^c22690441].

(3) In this regulation, “**Friends and Family Test**” means the arrangements that a contractor which provides essential services is required by NHS England to implement to enable its patients to provide anonymous feedback about the patient experience at the contractor's practice.
