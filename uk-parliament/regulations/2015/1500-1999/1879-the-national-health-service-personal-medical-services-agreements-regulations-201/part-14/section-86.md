# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 86

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 86. Compliance with legislation and guidance

*Extent: E+W*

The contractor must—

- (a) comply with all relevant legislation; and
- (b) have regard to all relevant guidance issued by NHS England[^c-9520329cffeb7671913a444ac58ebf84], the Secretary of State or local authorities in respect of the exercise of their functions under the Act.
