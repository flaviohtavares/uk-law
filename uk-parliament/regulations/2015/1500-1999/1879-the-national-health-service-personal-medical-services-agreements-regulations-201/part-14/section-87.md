# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 87

> Text as at 2026-06-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 87. Third party rights

*Extent: E+W*

The agreement does not create any right enforceable by any person who is not a party to it.
