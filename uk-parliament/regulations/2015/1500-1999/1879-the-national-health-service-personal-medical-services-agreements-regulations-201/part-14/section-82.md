# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 82

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 82. Co-operation with the Secretary of State and NHS England[^c-817b516753f12f840c2c561e96f04d77]

*Extent: E+W*

The contractor must co-operate with—

- (a) the Secretary of State in the discharge of the Secretary of State's duty under section 1F of the Act [^c22690461] (duty as to education and training); and
- (b) NHS England where NHS England is discharging the Secretary of State's duty under section 1F of the Act by virtue of its functions under section 97(1) of the Care Act 2014 [^c22690481] (planning education and training for health workers etc.).
