# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 23

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Sub-contracting

*Extent: E+W*

An agreement must contain terms which prevent a contractor from sub-contracting any of its obligations to provide clinical services under the agreement except in the circumstances provided for in Part 5 of Schedule 2.
