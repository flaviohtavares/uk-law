# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 27

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Other required terms

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), an agreement must also contain provisions which are equivalent in their effect to the provisions set out in Parts 6 to 14 of, and Schedules 1 and 2 to, these Regulations, unless the agreement is of a type or nature to which a particular provision does not apply.

(2) The requirement in paragraph (1) does not apply to the provisions specified in—

- (a) regulation 76(5) to (14);
- (b) regulation 77; and
- (c) paragraph 40(5) to (9) and 41(5) to (17) of Schedule 2,

which are to have effect in relation to the matters set out in those provisions.
