# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 12

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Health service contract

*Extent: E+W*

If, by virtue of regulation 9 or 10 the contractor is to be regarded as a health service body, the agreement must state that it is an NHS contract.
