# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services Agreements) Regulations 2015, section 24

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Variation of agreements

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a variation of, or amendment to, the agreement may only be made in the circumstances provided for in Part 8 of Schedule 2.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not prevent a variation of, or amendment to, an agreement in the circumstances provided for in—

- (a) regulation 25;
- (b) Part 6; and
- (c)[^c-3bdf82aeeb77f60e5acf5be70244cb94] paragraphs 43(8), 43A(9) and 52 of Schedule 2.
