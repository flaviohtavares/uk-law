# The Progress Power (Gas Fired Power Station) Order 2015, section 18

> Text as at 2020-07-28.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Compulsory acquisition of land[^c-557013ab4b33d711ff76ca2d498d16ab]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The undertaker may acquire compulsorily so much of the Order land as is required for the authorised development or to facilitate it, or as is incidental to it.

(2) This article is subject to article 21 (compulsory acquisition of rights etc.), article 24 (acquisition of subsoil only) and article 27 (temporary use of land for carrying out the authorised development).
