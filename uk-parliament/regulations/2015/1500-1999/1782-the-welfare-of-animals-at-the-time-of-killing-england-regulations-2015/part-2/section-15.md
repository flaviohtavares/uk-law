# The Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (England) Regulations 2015, section 15

> Text as at 2025-02-27.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Licences

*Extent: E+W*

The competent authority must grant and register a licence if—

- (a) the applicant meets the conditions in regulation 16; and
- (b) the competent authority is satisfied the applicant is a fit and proper person to hold a licence.
