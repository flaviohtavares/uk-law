# The Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (England) Regulations 2015, section 47

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 47. Revocations

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following are revoked—

- (a) the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995[^f00017];
- (b) the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) (Amendment) Regulations 1999[^f00018]; and
- (c) regulation 10(3) of, and Part 3 of Schedule 8 to, the Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000[^f00019];
- (d) the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2000[^f00020];
- (e) the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2001[^f00021];
- (f) the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2003[^f00022];
- (g) the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2006[^f00023];
- (h) the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2007[^f00024]; and
- (i) the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2012[^f00025].
