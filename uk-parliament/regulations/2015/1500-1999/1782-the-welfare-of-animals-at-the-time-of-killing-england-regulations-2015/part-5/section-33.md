# The Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (England) Regulations 2015, section 33

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Penalties

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 30 or 31 is liable on summary conviction to a fine, except where specified in paragraph (2).

(2) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 30(1)(g) in relation to a contravention of Article 3 is liable on summary conviction to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months.
