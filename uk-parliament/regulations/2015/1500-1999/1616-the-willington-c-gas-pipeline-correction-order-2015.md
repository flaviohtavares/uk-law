# The Willington C Gas Pipeline (Correction) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1616

Infrastructure Planning

The Willington C Gas Pipeline (Correction) Order 2015

Made

18th August 2015

Coming into force

19th August 2015

The Willington C Gas Pipeline Order 2014 (the “Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (the “Act”)[^f00002], contained correctable errors within the meaning of the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received, before the end of the relevant period referred to in paragraph 1(6)(a) of that Schedule, a written request from RWE Generation UK plc to correct errors and omissions in the Order. In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of that Schedule, the Secretary of State informed Derbyshire County Council, East Staffordshire Borough Council, South Derbyshire District Council and Staffordshire County Council that the request had been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers in paragraph 1(4) and (8) of that Schedule, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Willington C Gas Pipeline (Correction) Order 2015 and comes into force on 19th August 2015.

## 2. Corrections

The Willington C Gas Pipeline Order 2014 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made; and
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy and Climate Change

Giles Scott

Head of National Infrastructure Consents and Coal Liabilities Unit

Department of Energy and Climate Change

18th August 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order corrects errors and omissions in the Willington C Gas Pipeline Order 2014, which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a request from RWE Generation UK plc under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
