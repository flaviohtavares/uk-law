# The Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010 (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1763

Prevention And Suppression Of Terrorism

The Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010 (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 2015

Made

8th October 2015

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 8th day of October 2015

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the power conferred on Her by section 54(1) of the Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010 (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 2015 and shall come into force on the day after it is made.

## 2. Revocation of the Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010 (Isle of Man) Order 2011

The Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010 (Isle of Man) Order 2011[^f00002] is revoked.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act 2010 (Isle of Man) Order 2011 ([S.I. 2011/749](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/749)), which extended to the Isle of Man, subject to modifications, Part 1 of, and Part 1 of Schedule 2 to, the Terrorist Asset-Freezing etc. Act [2010 (c.38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/38). The 2011 Order has been superseded by the Terrorism and Other Crime (Financial Restrictions) Act 2014 (an Act of Tynwald).
