# The Competition Appeal Tribunal Rules 2015, section 113

> Text as at 2025-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 113. Funding Arrangements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to section 47C(8) of the 1998 Act [^c22671831] and rule 93(4), the rules on funding arrangements made under Part 2 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 [^c22671841] apply to proceedings before the Tribunal.
