# The Competition Appeal Tribunal Rules 2015, section 115

> Text as at 2025-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. General power of the Tribunal

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the provisions of these Rules, the Tribunal may regulate its own procedure.

(2) A power of the Tribunal under these Rules to make an order or direction includes a power to vary or revoke the order or direction.

(3) The President may issue practice directions in relation to the procedures provided for by these Rules.
