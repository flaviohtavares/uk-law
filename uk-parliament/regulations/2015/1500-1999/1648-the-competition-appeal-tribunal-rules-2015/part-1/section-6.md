> Text as at 2025-12-12.

## 6. Tribunal address for service

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The address for the filing or service of documents at or on the Tribunal (referred to in these Rules as “**the Tribunal address for service**”) is: The Registrar of the Competition Appeal Tribunal, Victoria House, Bloomsbury Place, London WC1A 2EB or such other address as may be notified on the Tribunal website from time to time.
