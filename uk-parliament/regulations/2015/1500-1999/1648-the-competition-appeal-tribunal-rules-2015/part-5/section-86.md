# The Competition Appeal Tribunal Rules 2015, section 86

> Text as at 2025-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Individual settlement by the class representative in opt-in proceedings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the class representative in opt-in collective proceedings is a member of the class and settles in whole or part its personal claim included within the collective proceedings, it shall promptly give notice of that fact—

- (a) to all represented persons; and
- (b) to the Tribunal.
