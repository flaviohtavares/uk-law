# The Competition Appeal Tribunal Rules 2015, section 118

> Text as at 2025-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Revocation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following Rules are revoked—

- (a) the Competition Appeal Tribunal Rules 2003 [^c22671861]
- (b) the Competition Appeal Tribunal (Amendment and Communications Act Appeals) Rules 2004 [^c22671871].
