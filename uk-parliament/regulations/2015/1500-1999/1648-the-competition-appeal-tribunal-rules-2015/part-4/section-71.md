# The Competition Appeal Tribunal Rules 2015, section 71

> Text as at 2025-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Transfer of claims from the Tribunal

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Tribunal may, at any stage of the proceedings, on the request of a party or of its own initiative, and after considering any observations of the parties, direct that all or part of a claim made in proceedings brought under section 47A of the 1998 Act (proceedings before the Tribunal: claims for damages etc.) [^c22671741] be transferred to—

- (a) the High Court or the county court in England and Wales or Northern Ireland; or
- (b) the Court of Session or the Sheriff Court in Scotland.

## Annotations

[^c22671741]: Marginal citation: [1998 c. 41](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/41); [section 47A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1648/section/47A) was substituted by paragraph 4 of Schedule 8 to the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
