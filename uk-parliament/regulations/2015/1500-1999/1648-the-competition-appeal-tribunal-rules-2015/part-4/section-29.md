# The Competition Appeal Tribunal Rules 2015, section 29

> Text as at 2025-12-12.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Application of Rules to proceedings in Scotland

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In respect of proceedings in Scotland, references in this Part to “claimant” and “defendant” are to be read respectively as references to “pursuer” and “defender”.
