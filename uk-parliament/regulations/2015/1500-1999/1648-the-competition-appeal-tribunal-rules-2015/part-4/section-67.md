# The Competition Appeal Tribunal Rules 2015, section 67

> Text as at 2025-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Injunctions generally

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This rule and rules 68 to 70 do not apply to proceedings in Scotland.

(2) The Tribunal may by order (whether interim or final) grant an injunction in all cases in which it appears to the Tribunal to be just and convenient to do so.

(3) Any such order may be made either unconditionally or on such terms and conditions as the Tribunal thinks just.
