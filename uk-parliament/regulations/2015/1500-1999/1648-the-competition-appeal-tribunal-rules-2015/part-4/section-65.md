# The Competition Appeal Tribunal Rules 2015, section 65

> Text as at 2025-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Restriction on use of a privileged document inspection of which has been inadvertently allowed

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a party inadvertently discloses a privileged document, the party who has seen the document may use it or its contents only with the permission of the Tribunal.
