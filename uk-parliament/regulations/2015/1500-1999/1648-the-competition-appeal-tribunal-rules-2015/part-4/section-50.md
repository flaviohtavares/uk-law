# The Competition Appeal Tribunal Rules 2015, section 50

> Text as at 2025-12-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Intervention

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Rule 16 (except paragraphs (2) and (8) to (10)) applies to claims falling within this Part.

(2) The CMA may submit written observations to the Tribunal on issues relating to the application of [^key-c72758d72775a63e206251fedb7f4d76]... Chapter I or II of Part 1 of the 1998 Act and, with the permission of the Tribunal, submit oral observations to the Tribunal.
