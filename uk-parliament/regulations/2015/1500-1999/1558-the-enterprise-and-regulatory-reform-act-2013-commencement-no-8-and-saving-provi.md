# The Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 8 and Saving Provisions) (Revocation) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1558

Copyright

The Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 8 and Saving Provisions) (Revocation) Order 2015

Made

20th July 2015

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 100 and 103(3) and (4) of the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 8 and Saving Provisions) (Revocation) Order 2015.

## 2. Revocation

The Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 8 and Saving Provisions) Order 2015[^f00002] is revoked.

Baroness Neville-Rolfe

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

20th July 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (Commencement No. 8 and Saving Provisions) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/641](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/641)). This latter Order would otherwise have brought section 74 of the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (exploitation of design derived from artistic work) into force from 6th April 2020, subject to saving provisions.
