# The Consumer Rights Act 2015 (Commencement No. 3, Transitional Provisions, Savings and Consequential Amendments) Order 2015, section 2

> Text as at 2016-03-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-dac6d3a9808ae1358fd2fd694788bad9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Order—

- “the 2004 Regulation” means Regulation [(EC) No 261/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0261) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00002] establishing common rules on compensation and assistance to passengers in the event of denied boarding and of cancellation or long delay of flights, and repealing Regulation [(EEC) No 295/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/0295)[^f00003];
- “the 2010 Regulation” means Regulation (EU) No 1177/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00004] concerning the rights of passengers when travelling by sea and inland waterway and amending Regulation [(EC) No 2006/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/2006)[^f00005];
- “the Act” means the Consumer Rights Act 2015;
- “the Carriage by Air Conventions” are—
  - (a) the Warsaw Convention;
  - (b) the Warsaw Convention, as amended at the Hague, 1955;
  - (c) the Warsaw Convention, as amended by Additional Protocol No.1 of Montreal, 1975;
  - (d) the Warsaw Convention, as amended at the Hague, 1955, and by Additional Protocol No.2 of Montreal, 1975;
  - (e) the Warsaw Convention, as amended at the Hague, 1955, and by Protocol No. 4 of Montreal, 1975;
  - (f) the Convention supplementary to the Warsaw Convention, for the Unification of Certain Rules Relating to International Carriage by Air Performed by a Person other than the Contracting Carrier, signed at Guadalajara, 18th September 1961; and
  - (g) the Convention for the Unification of Certain Rules for International Carriage by Air, signed at Montreal 28th May 1999;
- “consumer transport service” means—
  - (a) a rail passenger service;
  - (b) carriage by air to which the 2004 Regulation or the Carriage by Air Conventions or enactments giving effect to the provisions of those Conventions apply; and
  - (c) sea and inland waterway transport to which the 2010 Regulation applies;
- “rail passenger service” means any rail passenger service supplied by any undertaking other than those specified in paragraphs (a) and (b) of article 2(2) of Directive 2012/34/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21st November 2012 establishing a single European railway area (recast)[^f00006];
- “the Warsaw Convention” means the Convention for the Unification of Certain Rules Relating to the International Carriage by Air, signed at Warsaw 12th October 1929.
