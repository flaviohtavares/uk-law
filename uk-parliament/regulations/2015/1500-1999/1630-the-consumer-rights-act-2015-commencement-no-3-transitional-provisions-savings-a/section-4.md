# The Consumer Rights Act 2015 (Commencement No. 3, Transitional Provisions, Savings and Consequential Amendments) Order 2015, section 4

> Text as at 2016-03-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Provisions coming into force on 6th April 2016[^key-9deac372e8b550201084693e035f3e64]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purpose of a contract to supply a consumer transport service the following provisions of the Act come into force on 1st October 2016[^key-4aa504fc5bac37045bbbb7f9eb0508a5], subject to the transitional provisions and savings in article 6—

- (a) section 48(1) to (4) (contracts covered by this Chapter);
- (b) sections 49 to 59; and
- (c) paragraphs 2 to 6, 10 to 17 and 21 to 27 of Schedule 4.
