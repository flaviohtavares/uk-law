# The Hirwaun Generating Station Order 2015, section 5

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Operation of authorised development[^c-0d5af088c68cf588ea0ede6fecc665f8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker is hereby authorised to operate the generating station comprised in the authorised development.

(2) This article does not relieve the undertaker of any requirement to obtain any permit or licence or any obligation under any legislation that may be required to authorise the operation of a generating station.
