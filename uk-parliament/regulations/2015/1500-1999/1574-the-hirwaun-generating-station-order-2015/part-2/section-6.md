> Text as at 2017-12-28.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 6. Benefit of the Order[^c-cb9862cb9f6cce92b616a2db16524a10]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to article 7 (consent to transfer benefit of the Order), the provisions of this Order will have effect solely for the benefit of Hirwaun Power Limited.
