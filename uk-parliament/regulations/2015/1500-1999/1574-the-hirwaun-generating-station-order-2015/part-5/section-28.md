# The Hirwaun Generating Station Order 2015, section 28

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Statutory undertakers[^key-a7cb03a3bf88a71fba3790c6adaad3b9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Subject to the provisions of Schedule 7 (protective provisions), the undertaker may—

- (a) acquire compulsorily the land belonging to statutory undertakers shown on the land plans within the limits of the land to be acquired and described in the book of reference;
- (b) extinguish or suspend the rights of, remove or reposition the apparatus belonging to statutory undertakers shown on the land plans and described in the book of reference; and
- (c) acquire compulsorily the new rights over land belonging to statutory undertakers shown on the land plans and described in the book of reference.
