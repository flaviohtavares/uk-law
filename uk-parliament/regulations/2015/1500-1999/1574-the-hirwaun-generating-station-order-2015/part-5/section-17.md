# The Hirwaun Generating Station Order 2015, section 17

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 17. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^key-10b07112e58c981788a7989a8fdececb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981(minerals) are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) paragraph 8(3) is not incorporated; and
- (b) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”.
