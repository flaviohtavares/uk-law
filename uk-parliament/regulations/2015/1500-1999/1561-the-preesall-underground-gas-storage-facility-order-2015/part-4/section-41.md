# The Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility Order 2015, section 41

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Arbitration[^key-10320764b2a5bcfba382a8265a8c8a15]

*Extent: E+W*

Any difference or dispute under any provision of this Order (other than a difference or dispute which falls to be determined by the tribunal) shall, unless otherwise provided for in this Order and unless otherwise agreed between the parties, be referred to and settled by a single arbitrator to be agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, to be appointed on the application of either party (after giving notice in writing to the other) by the President of the Institution of Civil Engineers.
