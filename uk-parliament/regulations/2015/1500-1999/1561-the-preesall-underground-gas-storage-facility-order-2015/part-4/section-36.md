# The Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility Order 2015, section 36

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Operational land for purposes of the 1990 Act[^key-faf8cd365a0329bdb140d6c23e28db28]

*Extent: E+W*

Development consent granted by this Order shall be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)(a) of the 1990 Act (cases in which land is to be treated as not being operational land for the purposes of that Act).
