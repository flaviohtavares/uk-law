# The Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility Order 2015, section 27

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. No double recovery[^c-8a35e1d1cbbe6502d8d0e91e00e779a9]

*Extent: E+W*

Compensation shall not be payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law, or under two or more different provisions under this Order.
