# The Social Security (Crediting and Treatment of Contributions, and National Insurance Numbers) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1828

Social Security

The Social Security (Crediting and Treatment of Contributions, and National Insurance Numbers) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

Made

26th October 2015

Laid before Parliament

2nd November 2015

Coming into force

30th November 2015

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 182C(1) and 189(1) and (4) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00001].

In accordance with section 173(1)(b) of that Act, the Secretary of State has obtained the agreement of the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals in respect of these Regulations need not be referred to it.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Crediting and Treatment of Contributions, and National Insurance Numbers) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 30th November 2015.

## 2. Amendment to the Social Security (Crediting and Treatment of Contributions, and National Insurance Numbers) Regulations 2001

In regulation 9(2A) (application for allocation of national insurance number) of the Social Security (Crediting and Treatment of Contributions, and National Insurance Numbers) Regulations 2001[^f00002], after “regulation 13” insert “or 13A”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Social Security (Crediting and Treatment of Contributions, and National Insurance Numbers) Regulations 2001 ([S.I. 2001/769](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/769)) to disapply the requirement to apply for a National Insurance number in the case of a person in respect of whom the Secretary of State or the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs are notified that a biometric immigration document is to be issued under regulation 13A of the Immigration (Biometric Registration) Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/3048](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/3048) as amended by [S.I. 2015/433](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/433)) (“the 2008 Regulations”). Regulation 13A covers cases involving an application for entry clearance. There is already a similar disapplication for cases where a biometric immigration document is issued under regulation 13 of the 2008 Regulations.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on business or civil society organisations is foreseen and the impact on the public sector is minimal.
