## 71ZG. Meaning of “appropriate software” for the purposes of regulations 71ZD, 71ZE and 71ZF[^c-0f98c28c37a34ee7a14bc18911e7bc35]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of regulations 71ZD, 71ZE and 71ZF the software used for the purposes of providing a facility or method of communication (as the case may be) is appropriate if the software meets—

- (a) the requirements in the GPIT Operating Model relevant to that software, or
- (b) requirements which are equivalent in their effect to the relevant requirements in the GPIT Operating Model.
