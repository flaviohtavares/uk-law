# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 71B

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71B. Patient access: other availability of directly bookable appointments[^c-f13d3119af9b5b6b3d1485ee6e935c32]

*Extent: E+W*

A contractor must ensure that all of its directly bookable appointments are made available for booking by telephone or in person.
