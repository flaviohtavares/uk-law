# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 74E

> Text as at 2026-06-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 74E. NHS England Workforce Collections[^key-36f6994f6677e51a30d71eaf37926e68][^key-635a2fd50ee560b5e1bc8b118f0a86ce][^key-dc491744748016e3887cad7444ab4e83]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A contractor must record and submit any data required by NHS England[^key-36648e99bdcf0e3e341795c4620f9707] for the purposes of the NHS Digital Workforce Collection[^key-6e4003ae1e47ca45fdbb460492178ff4] (known as the “Workforce Minimum Data Set”) in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2)[^key-181423f50e456522be369b42d58afe48] The data referred to in paragraph (1) must be—

- (a) appropriately coded, reviewed and updated by the contractor in line with agreed standards set out in guidance published by NHS England;
- (b) submitted to NHS England—
  - (i) using the data entry module on the National Workforce Reporting Service, which is a facility provided by NHS England to the contractor for this purpose; and
  - (ii) at such intervals during the financial year as are notified to the contractor by NHS England.
