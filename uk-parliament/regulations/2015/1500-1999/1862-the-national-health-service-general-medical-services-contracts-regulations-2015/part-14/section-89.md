# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 89

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 89. Co-operation with NHS England[^key-27a783e1510d45f884edbc32ee2f5c83]

*Extent: E+W*

The contractor must co-operate with NHS England in the discharge of any of NHS England’s obligations, or the obligations of NHS England’s accountable officers, under the Controlled Drugs (Supervision and Management of Use) Regulations 2013 [^c22715141].
