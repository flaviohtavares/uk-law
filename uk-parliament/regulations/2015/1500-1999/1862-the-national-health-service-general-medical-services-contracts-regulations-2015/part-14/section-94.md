# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 94

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 94. Compliance with legislation and guidance

*Extent: E+W*

The contractor must—

- (a) comply with all relevant legislation; and
- (b) have regard to all relevant guidance issued by NHS England[^c-27a783e1510d45f884edbc32ee2f5c83], the Secretary of State or local authorities in respect of the exercise of their functions under the Act.
