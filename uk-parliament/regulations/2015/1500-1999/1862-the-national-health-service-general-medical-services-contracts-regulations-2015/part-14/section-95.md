# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 95

> Text as at 2026-06-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 95. Third party rights

*Extent: E+W*

The contract does not create any right enforceable by any person who is not a party to it.
