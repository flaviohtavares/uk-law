# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 88

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 88. Friends and Family Test

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A contractor must give all patients who use the contractor's practice the opportunity to provide feedback about the service received from the practice through the Friends and Family Test [^c22715121].

(2) The contractor must—

- (a) report the results of completed Friends and Family Tests to NHS England[^key-27a783e1510d45f884edbc32ee2f5c83]; and
- (b) publish the results of such completed Tests [^c22715131].

(3) In this regulation, “**Friends and Family Test**” means the arrangements that a contractor is required by NHS England to implement to enable its patients to provide anonymous feedback about the patient experience at the contractor's practice.
