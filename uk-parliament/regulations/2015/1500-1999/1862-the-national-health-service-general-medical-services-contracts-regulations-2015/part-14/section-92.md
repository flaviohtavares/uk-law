# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 92

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 92. Public liability insurance

*Extent: E+W*

The contractor must at all times hold adequate public liability insurance in relation to liabilities to third parties arising under or in connection with the contract which are not covered by the indemnity arrangement referred to in regulation 91.
