# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 85

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 85. Interpretation of this Part

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part, “**any dispute arising out of or in connection with the contract**” includes any dispute arising out of or in connection with the termination of the contract.

(2) A term of the contract which makes provision in respect of the requirements of this Part is to survive even where the contract has terminated.
