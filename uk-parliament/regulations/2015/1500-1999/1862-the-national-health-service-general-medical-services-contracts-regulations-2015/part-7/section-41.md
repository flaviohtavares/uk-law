# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 41

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Qualifications of performers: health care professionals

*Extent: E+W*

A health care professional (other than one to whom regulation 40 applies) may not perform clinical services under the contract unless—

- (a) that person is registered with the professional body relevant to that person's profession; and
- (b) that registration is not subject to a period of suspension.
