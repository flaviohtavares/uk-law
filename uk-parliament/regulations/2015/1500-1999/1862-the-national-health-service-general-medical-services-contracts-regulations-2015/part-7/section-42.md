# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 42

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 42. Conditional registration or inclusion in primary care list

*Extent: E+W*

Where the registration of a health care professional or, in the case of a medical practitioner, the inclusion of that practitioner's name in a primary care list, is subject to conditions, the contractor must ensure compliance with those conditions in so far as they are relevant to the contract.
