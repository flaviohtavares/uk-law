# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 49

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Terms and conditions

*Extent: E+W*

The contractor may only offer employment to a general medical practitioner on terms which are no less favourable than those contained in the document entitled “Model terms and conditions of service for a salaried general practitioner employed by a GMS practice” published by the British Medical Association and the NHS Confederation as item 1.2 of the supplementary documents to the GMS contract 2003 [^c22714761].
