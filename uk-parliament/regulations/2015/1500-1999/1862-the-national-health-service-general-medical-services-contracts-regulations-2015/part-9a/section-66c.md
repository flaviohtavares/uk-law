# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 66C

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 66C. Vaccines and immunisations: catch-up campaigns[^c-ba316bfeaeeb96b221e8667175f51004]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The contractor must participate in a manner reasonably required by NHS England[^c-27a783e1510d45f884edbc32ee2f5c83] in one vaccine and immunisations catch-up campaign in each financial year.

(2) In this regulation “vaccine and immunisations catch-up campaign” means a campaign which is aimed at maximising the uptake of a particular vaccine or immunisation by patients who are eligible for it but have not received that vaccine or immunisation for any reason (other than a decision to refuse the vaccine or immunisation).
