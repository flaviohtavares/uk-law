# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 66F

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 66F. Vaccines and immunisations: exception for private arrangements[^key-ba316bfeaeeb96b221e8667175f51004]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in this Part applies in relation to the offer or administration of any vaccine or immunisation to a patient under a private arrangement.
