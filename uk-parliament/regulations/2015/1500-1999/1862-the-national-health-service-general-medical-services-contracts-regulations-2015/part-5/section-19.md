# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 19

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Minor surgery[^key-c0d4c04cad62a3e0299074635fde8d7c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to Part 6, a contract may provide for the provision by a contractor of minor surgery.

(2) A contract which includes minor surgery must contain a term which requires the contractor to provide such facilities as are necessary to enable the contractor to properly perform that service.
