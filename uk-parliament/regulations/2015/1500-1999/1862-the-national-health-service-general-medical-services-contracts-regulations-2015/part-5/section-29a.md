# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 29A

> Text as at 2026-06-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29A. Variation of contracts: integrated care provider contracts[^key-739be0436f4c94ca748593f16af312a3]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 3A has effect in relation to the variation of a contract in circumstances where the contractor wishes to perform or provide primary medical services under an integrated care provider contract as described in paragraph 3 of that Schedule.
