# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 28

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Sub-contracting

*Extent: E+W*

A contract must contain terms which prevent a contractor from sub-contracting any of its obligations to provide clinical services under the contract except in the circumstances provided for by Part 5 of Schedule 3.
