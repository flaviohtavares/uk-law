# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 14

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Health service contract

*Extent: E+W*

If, by virtue of regulation 10 or 11, a contractor is to be regarded as a health service body, the contract must state that it is an NHS contract.
