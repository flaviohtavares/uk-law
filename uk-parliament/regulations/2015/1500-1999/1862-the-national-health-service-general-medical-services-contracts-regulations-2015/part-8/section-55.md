# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 55

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 55. Prescribing: general

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The contractor must ensure that—

- (a) any prescription form or repeatable prescription issued or created by a prescriber;
- (b) any home oxygen order form issued by a health care professional; and
- (c) any listed prescription items[^key-a90eb4230acdd1b7caab6aef92b515ee] voucher issued by a prescriber or any other person acting under the contract,

complies as appropriate with the requirements in regulations 56, 57 and 59 to 63.

(2)[^key-414b133497e6a2fff082b12633bfb9b4] In regulations 56, 57 and 59 to 63, a reference to “drugs” includes contraceptive substances and a reference to “appliances” includes contraceptive appliances.
