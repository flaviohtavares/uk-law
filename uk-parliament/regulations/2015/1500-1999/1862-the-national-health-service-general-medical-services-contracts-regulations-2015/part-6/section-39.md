# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) Regulations 2015, section 39

> Text as at 2026-06-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. Informing patients of opt outs

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Before any opt out takes effect, NHS England[^key-27a783e1510d45f884edbc32ee2f5c83] and the contractor must discuss how to inform the contractor's patients of the proposed opt out.

(2) The contractor must, if requested by NHS England, inform its registered patients of an opt out and of the arrangements made for those patients to receive minor surgery[^key-f6da7cf2d0965cba142a12a758b38342] or out of hours services by—

- (a) placing a notice in the contractor's practice waiting rooms; or
- (b) including the information in the contractor's practice leaflet.

(3) In this regulation, “**opt out**” means an out of hours opt out, a permanent opt out or a temporary opt out.
