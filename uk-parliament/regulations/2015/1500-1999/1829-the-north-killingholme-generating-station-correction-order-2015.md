# The North Killingholme (Generating Station) (Correction) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1829

Infrastructure Planning

The North Killingholme (Generating Station) (Correction) Order 2015

Made

26th October 2015

Coming into force

27th October 2015

The North Killingholme (Generating Station) Order 2014 (“the Order”)[^f00001], which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00002], contains correctable errors within the meaning of the Act[^f00003].

Before the end of the relevant period, as defined in paragraph 1(6)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received a written request from the applicant[^f00004] for the correction of errors and omissions in the Order.

In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State has informed North Lincolnshire Council and North East Lincolnshire Council that the request has been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraphs 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the North Killingholme (Generating Station) (Correction) Order 2015 and comes into force on 27th October 2015.

## 2. Corrections

The Order is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and
- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy and Climate Change

Giles Scott

Head of National Infrastructure Consents Unit

Department of Energy and Climate Change

26th October 2015

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

| Provision | How the correction is to be made | Text to be substituted, inserted or omitted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Part 1 (Authorised development) of Schedule 1 | After “A nationally significant infrastructure project comprising a generating station” insert | “with a nominal gross electrical output of up to 470 MWe” |
| Part 1 (Authorised development) of Schedule 1 | After “Work No. 1 — a combined cycle plant”, omit | “with a nominal gross electrical output of up to 470 MWe” |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order corrects errors and omissions in the North Killingholme (Generating Station) Order 2015, a development consent order made under the Planning Act 2008, following a written request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
