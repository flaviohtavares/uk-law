# The Dogger Bank Teesside A and B Offshore Wind Farm Order 2015, section 4

> Text as at 2024-11-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 4. Maintenance of authorised project[^c-47214bfbfd032ed5f5ca294d1a43b84a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker may at any time maintain, and maintain from time to time, the authorised project except to the extent that this Order or any agreement made under this Order provides otherwise.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not relieve the undertaker of any requirement to obtain a licence under Part 4 of the 2009 Act (marine licensing).
