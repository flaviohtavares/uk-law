# The Dogger Bank Teesside A and B Offshore Wind Farm Order 2015, section 5

> Text as at 2024-11-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Operation of generating station[^c-b8e46d4f240fd9b9ca118d92694ef1fe]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker is authorised to operate the generating stations[^f00018] comprised in the authorised development.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not relieve the undertaker of any requirement to obtain a permit or licence under any other legislation that may be required from time to time to authorise the operation of a generating station.
