# The Dogger Bank Teesside A and B Offshore Wind Farm Order 2015, section 39

> Text as at 2024-11-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 39. Deemed licences under Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^key-192deb5224b0725e7878642985d999aa]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The following marine licences set out in Schedules 8 to 11 are deemed to have been issued under Part 4 of the 2009 Act (marine licensing) for the licensed activities specified in Part 1 of each licence and subject to the conditions specified in Part 2 of each licence—

- (a) Marine Licence 1 (set out in Schedule 8);
- (b) Marine Licence 2 (set out in Schedule 9);
- (c) Marine Licence 3 (set out in Schedule 10);
- (d) Marine Licence 4 (set out in Schedule 11).
