# The Dogger Bank Teesside A and B Offshore Wind Farm Order 2015, section 37

> Text as at 2024-11-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. Operational land for purposes of Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^key-69183178966f569903ca99ee082a8960]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Development consent granted by this Order must be treated as specific planning permission for the purposes of section 264(3)(a) of the 1990 Act (cases in which land is to be treated as operational land for the purposes of that Act).
