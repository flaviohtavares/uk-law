# The Dogger Bank Teesside A and B Offshore Wind Farm Order 2015, section 31

> Text as at 2024-11-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. Statutory undertakers[^c-b08c6d0144cfe07800fc45cd95140d4a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In accordance with this Order and subject to Parts 1 to 4 of Schedule 12 (protective provisions), the undertaker may—

- (a) acquire compulsorily the land belonging to statutory undertakers shown on the land plans within the Order limits and described in the book of reference;
- (b) extinguish rights of, and remove or reposition apparatus belonging to, statutory undertakers within the Order limits; and
- (c) acquire compulsorily the new rights over land belonging to statutory undertakers within the Order limits referred to in Schedule 5 and described in the book of reference.

(2) In this article, “statutory undertaker” means—

- (a) a person falling within section 127(8) of the 2008 Act; and
- (b) a public communications provider (as defined in section 151(1) of the Communications Act 2003[^f00037]).
