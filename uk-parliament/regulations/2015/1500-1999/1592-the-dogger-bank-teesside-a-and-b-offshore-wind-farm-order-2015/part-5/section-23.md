## 23. Compulsory acquisition of land: minerals[^key-da04a119dc674374d5cb050c94131480]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Part 2 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981(minerals) is incorporated in this Order, subject to the modifications that for “acquiring authority” there is substituted “undertaker”.
