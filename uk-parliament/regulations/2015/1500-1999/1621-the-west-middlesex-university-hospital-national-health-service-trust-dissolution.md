# The West Middlesex University Hospital National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1621

National Health Service, England

The West Middlesex University Hospital National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2015

Made

19th August 2015

Coming into force

1st September 2015

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25(1), 272(7) and 273(1) of, and paragraph 28 of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order on the application of the West Middlesex University Hospital National Health Service Trust, following the consultation prescribed under paragraph 28(3) of Schedule 4 to that Act[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the West Middlesex University Hospital National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2015 and comes into force on 1st September 2015.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS trust

(1) The West Middlesex University Hospital National Health Service Trust is dissolved.

(2) The West Middlesex University Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00003] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Ben Gummer

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department of Health

19th August 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st September 2015 of the West Middlesex University Hospital National Health Service Trust established on 4th November 1992 and revokes the West Middlesex University Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992 that established the trust.
