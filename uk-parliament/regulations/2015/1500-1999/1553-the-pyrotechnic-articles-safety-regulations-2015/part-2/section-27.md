# The Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015, section 27

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Duty to take action in respect of pyrotechnic articles made available on the market which are not in conformity[^M_E_df93ed0c-e25a-40f2-8ab3-fa6e6e4f4713]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A distributor who considers, or has reason to believe, that a pyrotechnic article which the distributor has made available on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must make sure that the necessary corrective measures are taken to—

- (a) bring that pyrotechnic article into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the pyrotechnic article; or
- (c) recall the pyrotechnic article.

(2) Where the pyrotechnic article presents a risk, the distributor must immediately inform the market surveillance authority, [^key-ed53ae33eba17cffb4c4dfdbb98a4988]... of that risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the pyrotechnic article is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.

## 27. Duty to take action in respect of pyrotechnic articles made available on the market which are not in conformity[^M_E_2502eb53-fedb-44bc-d6d7-9e90f75d2077]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A distributor who considers, or has reason to believe, that a pyrotechnic article which the distributor has made available on the market is not in conformity with Part 2 must make sure that the necessary corrective measures are taken to—

- (a) bring that pyrotechnic article into conformity;
- (b) withdraw the pyrotechnic article; or
- (c) recall the pyrotechnic article.

(2) Where the pyrotechnic article presents a risk, the distributor must immediately inform the market surveillance authority, and the competent national authorities of the other relevant states[^key-81d27c3a26f996f6ae902da196aed2d3] in which the distributor has made the pyrotechnic article available on the market, of that risk, giving details of—

- (a) the respect in which the pyrotechnic article is considered not to be in conformity with Part 2; and
- (b) any corrective measures taken.
