# The Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015, section 14

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Prohibition on placing on the market pyrotechnic articles which are not in conformity

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An importer must not place a pyrotechnic article on the market unless it is in conformity with the essential safety requirements.
