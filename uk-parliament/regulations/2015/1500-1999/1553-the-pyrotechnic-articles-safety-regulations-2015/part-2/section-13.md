# The Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015, section 13

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 13. Compliance procedures for series production[^M_E_e9eecfab-d8ad-4581-97f7-0fae380bb6a6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A manufacturer of pyrotechnic articles which are manufactured by series production must ensure that, before placing such a pyrotechnic article on the market, procedures are in place to ensure that any pyrotechnic article so manufactured will be in conformity with Part 2.

(2) In doing so, the manufacturer must take adequate account of—

- (a) any change in pyrotechnic article design or characteristics; and
- (b) any change in a designated[^c-302a6964432f2fc4b1a2bf2594df478d] standard or in another technical specification by reference to which the [^c-dba20d90a792a62b67b2e6174399bf5a]... declaration of conformity was drawn up.

## 13. Compliance procedures for series production[^M_E_c37ec58b-2bed-4384-b7f4-163dfa262a0c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A manufacturer of pyrotechnic articles which are manufactured by series production must ensure that, before placing such a pyrotechnic article on the market, procedures are in place to ensure that any pyrotechnic article so manufactured will be in conformity with Part 2.

(2) In doing so, the manufacturer must take adequate account of—

- (a) any change in pyrotechnic article design or characteristics; and
- (b) any change in a harmonised standard or in another technical specification by reference to which the EU declaration of conformity was drawn up.
