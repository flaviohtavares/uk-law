# The Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015, section 19

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 19. Retention of technical documentation and ... declaration of conformity[^M_E_f11aa0c3-7fe2-44b1-9084-9f7369d84464][^c-b0944c540e5019de6777b25cb02b5bcf]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An importer must, for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the pyrotechnic article is placed on the market—

- (a) keep a copy of the [^c-6be83e87e195dedb95cb1e1c9b57f70e]... declaration of conformity at the disposal of enforcing authorities; and
- (b) ensure that the technical documentation can be made available to enforcing authorities, upon request.

## 19. Retention of technical documentation and EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_62b5b3ce-7314-46ba-9e96-e20b633ab394]

*Extent: N.I.*

An importer must, for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the pyrotechnic article is placed on the market—

- (a) keep a copy of the EU declaration of conformity at the disposal of enforcing authorities; and
- (b) ensure that the technical documentation can be made available to enforcing authorities, upon request.
