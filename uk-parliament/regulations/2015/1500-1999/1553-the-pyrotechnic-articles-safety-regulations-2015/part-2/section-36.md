# The Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015, section 36

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note). Translation of declaration of conformity (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-709b7041164462db7bdc426e744715c1][^key-709b7041164462db7bdc426e744715c1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-709b7041164462db7bdc426e744715c1] Before making a pyrotechnic article available on the market, an economic operator must ensure that the EU declaration of conformity is prepared in, or translated into, the language required by the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-709b7041164462db7bdc426e744715c1] relevant state[^key-9321665cc5be00f486b94a5ac1ad57a9] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-709b7041164462db7bdc426e744715c1] in which it is to be made available on the market. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-709b7041164462db7bdc426e744715c1]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-709b7041164462db7bdc426e744715c1] Where the pyrotechnic articles is to be made available on the market in *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-709b7041164462db7bdc426e744715c1] Northern Ireland[^key-0329af6e82d070db488e7b3210626e88] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-709b7041164462db7bdc426e744715c1], the language required is English. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-709b7041164462db7bdc426e744715c1]
