# The Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015, section 10

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 10. Retention of technical documentation and ... declaration of conformity[^M_E_55ce515e-a166-451c-dbe6-3ff48a613d2c][^M_F_de3138d6-9407-44b2-82fa-10d4c383e65a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A manufacturer must keep the technical documentation and the [^M_F_cae3610d-dd12-4264-dff6-e83b50b9b55d]... declaration of conformity drawn up in respect of a pyrotechnic article for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the pyrotechnic article is placed on the market.

## 10. Retention of technical documentation and EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_9fd939fd-8c49-406e-bf83-e15d44d8a2c6]

*Extent: N.I.*

A manufacturer must keep the technical documentation and the EU declaration of conformity drawn up in respect of a pyrotechnic article for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which the pyrotechnic article is placed on the market.
