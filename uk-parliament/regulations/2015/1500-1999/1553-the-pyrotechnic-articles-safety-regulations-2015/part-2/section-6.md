# The Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015, section 6

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Categorisation[^M_E_bbd72a55-82a9-48cc-bb4c-089f3395a525]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Before placing a pyrotechnic article on the market, a manufacturer must—

- (a) categorise it using the categories set out in Schedule 1 (categories of pyrotechnic article), according to its—
  - (i) type of use; or
  - (ii) purpose and level of hazard, including its noise level; and
- (b) ensure that an approved body[^key-4f3685e949dc09e95b3f5261a74f0224] has confirmed that categorisation as part of a relevant conformity assessment procedure.

## 6. Categorisation[^M_E_a51be9f6-9b57-41ca-bf54-a7b8a38745c7]

*Extent: N.I.*

Before placing a pyrotechnic article on the market, a manufacturer must—

- (a) categorise it using the categories set out in Schedule 1 (categories of pyrotechnic article), according to its—
  - (i) type of use; or
  - (ii) purpose and level of hazard, including its noise level; and
- (b) ensure that a notified body has confirmed that categorisation as part of a relevant conformity assessment procedure.
