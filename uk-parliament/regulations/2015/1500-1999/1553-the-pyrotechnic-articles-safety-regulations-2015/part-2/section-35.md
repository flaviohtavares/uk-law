# The Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015, section 35

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 35. Supply of UK safety data sheet[^M_E_31485cc9-f679-43a6-f9e6-907fd7324dd7][^c-19182876a1889615793f699c09c962c0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

When making a pyrotechnic article for a vehicle available on the market to a professional user, an economic operator must supply to the professional user the UK[^c-8b2cb37b0d071b540faa585695fc6528] safety data sheet referred to in regulation 12(3) (labelling of pyrotechnic articles for vehicles)—

- (a)[^c-032bc604d7e1e7b5b84a7ebac286c2a4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) on paper or in electronic form, provided that the format chosen is accessible to the professional user.

## 35. Supply of safety data sheet[^M_E_6e1f5b4b-0e48-401e-a140-ae9286bfe41f]

*Extent: N.I.*

When making a pyrotechnic article for a vehicle available on the market to a professional user, an economic operator must supply to the professional user the safety data sheet referred to in regulation 12(3) (labelling of pyrotechnic articles for vehicles)—

- (a) in the language requested by that professional user; and
- (b) on paper or in electronic form, provided that the format chosen is accessible to the professional user.
