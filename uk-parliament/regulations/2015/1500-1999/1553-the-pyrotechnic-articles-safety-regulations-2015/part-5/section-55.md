# The Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015, section 55

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Exercise of enforcement powers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

When enforcing these Regulations, the enforcing authority must exercise its powers in a manner which is consistent with—

- (a) regulation 56 (evaluation of pyrotechnic articles presenting a risk);
- (b) regulation 57 (enforcement action in respect of pyrotechnic articles which are not in conformity and which present a risk);
- (c) regulation 58 (EU safeguard procedure); *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c75dca6adeed7818e2ac60000f910f64]
- (d) regulation 59 (enforcement action in respect of pyrotechnic articles which are in conformity, but present a risk);
- (e) regulation 60 (enforcement action in respect of formal non-compliance); and
- (f) regulation 61 (restrictive measures).
