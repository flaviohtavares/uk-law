# The Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015, section 61

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Restrictive measures

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

When enforcing these Regulations, an enforcing authority must comply with the requirements of Article 21 of RAMS (as amended from time to time) *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-62fb8f86b270381b8eaa315159ad7623] in relation to any measure to—

- (a) prohibit or restrict a pyrotechnic article being made available on the market;
- (b) withdraw a pyrotechnic article; or
- (c) recall a pyrotechnic article.
