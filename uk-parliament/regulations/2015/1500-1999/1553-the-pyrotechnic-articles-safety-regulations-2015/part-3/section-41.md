# The Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015, section 41

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. ... Declaration of conformity[^M_E_493fa44d-db7f-49c3-94bb-5bda363b3540][^key-66afd8984ec14cfaf425b6f28dc20a7d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The [^key-53ee01ce940a1be0cd3d171b3afa0bf7]... declaration of conformity for a pyrotechnic article must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential safety requirements has been demonstrated in respect of the pyrotechnic article;
- (b) contain the elements specified in Schedule 2A[^key-af47e7b6ddc0df0151e79465ffa35f99] for the relevant conformity assessment procedure followed in respect of the pyrotechnic article; and
- (c) have the model structure set out in Schedule 3A[^key-1c7a21368a057289847a3b38353b07a4].

## 41. EU declaration of conformity[^M_E_d812922d-d5b4-478e-a1f7-4ad351654635]

*Extent: N.I.*

The EU declaration of conformity for a pyrotechnic article must—

- (a) state that the fulfilment of the essential safety requirements has been demonstrated in respect of the pyrotechnic article;
- (b) contain the elements specified in Annex II to the Directive (as amended from time to time) for the relevant conformity assessment procedure followed in respect of the pyrotechnic article; and
- (c) have the model structure set out in Annex III to the Directive (as amended from time to time).
