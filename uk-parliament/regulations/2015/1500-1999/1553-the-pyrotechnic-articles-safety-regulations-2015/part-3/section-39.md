# The Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015, section 39

> Text as at 2025-02-27.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland; Northern Ireland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 39. Presumption of conformity[^M_E_3d3d53ab-12f6-4c70-d119-79b5729944a1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A pyrotechnic article which is in conformity with a designated[^c-3ec392c0a66f7dba931bf81aca5bbc9d] standard (or part of such a standard) [^c-fb07afb8aeaacb4712749fb90c9d44b0]... is to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential safety requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.

## 39. Presumption of conformity[^M_E_105410a1-4af9-4dec-a391-5ea1610b3cdf]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A pyrotechnic article which is in conformity with a harmonised standard (or part of such a standard) the reference to which has been published in the Official Journal of the European Union is to be presumed to be in conformity with the essential safety requirements covered by that standard (or that part of that standard).

(2) The presumption in paragraph (1) is rebuttable.
