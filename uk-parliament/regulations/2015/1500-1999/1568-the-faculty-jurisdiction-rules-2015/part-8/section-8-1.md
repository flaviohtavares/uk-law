# The Faculty Jurisdiction Rules 2015, section 8.1

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England.

## 8.1. Removal of article to place of safety[^c-80e1666cfc8379ed72ab5bdfdd5adcc5]

*Extent: E*

(1) Where the archdeacon is of the opinion that an article should be removed to a place of safety immediately, an order made by the archdeacon under section 53[^c-fb106fdaf23c18f662c1f5f89809ba3a] of the Measure must be in Form 13.

(2) In any other case where an archdeacon is considering making an order under section 53[^c-61ae2344704b2c763150d25be639f0fc] of the Measure—

- (a) the notice required by section 53(3)(a)[^c-acb9e81acf40cd5b33d4d3da1b034ced] must be in Form 14; and
- (b) if the archdeacon makes an order, it must be in Form 15.
