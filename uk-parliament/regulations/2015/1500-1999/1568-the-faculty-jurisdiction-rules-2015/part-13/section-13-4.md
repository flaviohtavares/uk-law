## 13.4. Judge’s witness[^c-69445fd08c9ec2ec5eb3a82aa0932f63]

*Extent: E*

The court may direct the attendance of any of the following to give evidence at a hearing if it appears that the person may be able to give relevant evidence and is willing to give it—

- (a) a member or officer of the Church Buildings Council;
- (b) a member or officer of the Diocesan Advisory Committee;
- (c) any other person.
