## 13.1. Evidence of non-parties generally[^key-f91da79286182890c41ef239cde518a0]

*Extent: E*

(1) A person who is not a party to proceedings may apply to give evidence at a hearing.

(2) An application under paragraph (1) must be accompanied by a witness statement containing the evidence that is intended to be given by the person concerned.
