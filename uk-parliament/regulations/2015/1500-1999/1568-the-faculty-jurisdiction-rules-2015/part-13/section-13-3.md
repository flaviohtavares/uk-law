## 13.3. Evidence of Historic England and national amenity societies[^key-f7f7aeb53572d78711b98b7d0fd0c19c]

*Extent: E*

(1) Historic England or a national amenity society may apply to give evidence whether or not it has been given special notice under rule 9.3.

(2) An application under this rule must be—

- (a) submitted to the registry not less than 21 days before the hearing; and
- (b) accompanied by a witness statement containing the evidence to be relied on.
