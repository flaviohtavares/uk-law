## 13.2. Evidence of the Church Buildings Council[^key-db9cbaaa8bd4a3e14889929c3f0d3381]

*Extent: E*

(1) The Church Buildings Council may apply to give evidence whether or not it has been consulted under rule 9.6 or 9.7.

(2) An application under this rule must be—

- (a) submitted to the registry not less than 21 days before the hearing; and
- (b) accompanied by a witness statement containing the evidence to be relied on.
