## 27.3. Amendment of appeal notice[^c-3284350695bc7e88527b66371b24b50c]

*Extent: E*

(1) An appeal notice may not be amended without the permission of the appeal court.

(2) If the appeal court gives permission for an appeal notice to be amended, permission may be given on such terms as the court thinks just (which may include terms relating to costs).
