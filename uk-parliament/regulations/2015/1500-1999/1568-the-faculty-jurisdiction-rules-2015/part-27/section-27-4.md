## 27.4. Withdrawal of appeal[^c-f9bdc1a037072c6704af47f3ea0bff81]

*Extent: E*

(1) An appeal may not be withdrawn without the permission of the appeal court.

(2) Permission to withdraw an appeal may be given on such terms as the appeal court thinks just (which may include terms relating to costs).
