## 22.4. Review of findings of the Court of Ecclesiastical Causes Reserved[^c-a301bf25ea935c1b69ccd8a39f925b88]

*Extent: E*

(1) A finding of the Court of Ecclesiastical Causes Reserved may be reviewed by a Commission of Review.

(2) Permission is not needed to file a petition seeking a review under paragraph (1).

(3) Part 26 makes provision for the procedure to be followed in seeking a review of a finding of the Court of Ecclesiastical Causes Reserved by a Commission of Review.
