## 15.1. Scope and effect of interim faculties[^c-9797dc6c4f2a1c59cac7bb9e23871ed1]

*Extent: E*

(1) An interim faculty may be granted by the chancellor for any matter in respect of which a faculty might be granted following the final determination of a petition.

(2) Subject to the terms on which it is granted, an interim faculty constitutes authority to carry out the works or proposals in respect of which it is granted in the same way as a faculty that is granted following the final determination of a petition.
