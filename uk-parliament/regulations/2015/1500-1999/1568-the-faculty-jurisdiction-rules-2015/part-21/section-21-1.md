> Text as at 2024-01-01.

## 21.1. Application of Parts 21 to 27 to appeals[^c-880638d1462e006fe0e666b84e7be08d]

*Extent: E*

Parts 21 to 27 (in addition to the other Parts referred to in rule 2.1(2)) apply for the purpose of appeals from judgments, orders or decrees of consistory courts in faculty proceedings and in proceedings for an injunction or a restoration order.
