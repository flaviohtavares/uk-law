> Text as at 2024-01-01.

> Extent: England.

## 14.2. Directions[^c-ea70e77e9b3faec6c550796434eb5255]

*Extent: E*

(1) Where an order is made under rule 14.1 the chancellor may give directions for the purpose of determining the proceedings on consideration of written representations.

(2) If a party fails to comply with a direction under paragraph (1) the chancellor may proceed to dispose of the proceedings without further reference to that party.
