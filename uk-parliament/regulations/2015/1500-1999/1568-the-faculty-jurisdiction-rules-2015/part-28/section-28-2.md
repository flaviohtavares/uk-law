## 28.2. Revocation[^key-db0aa06265ca143be2ad0e68b9e05a85]

*Extent: E*

The Faculty Jurisdiction (Appeals) Rules 1998[^f00012] and the Faculty Jurisdiction Rules 2013[^f00013] are revoked (but subject to rule 28.3).
