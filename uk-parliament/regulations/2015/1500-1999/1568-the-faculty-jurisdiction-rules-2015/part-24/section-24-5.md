## 24.5. Transfer of appeals[^c-c2a3f56d0c243b072d9020579ca493e9]

*Extent: E*

(1) If the provincial court considers that an appeal to any extent relates to matter involving doctrine, ritual or ceremonial, it must transfer the appeal to the Court of Ecclesiastical Causes Reserved.

(2) Where the provincial court makes an order under paragraph (1), the registrar of the Court of Ecclesiastical Causes Reserved may give directions for the purpose of transferring the appeal (which may include directions dispensing with or modifying any of the provisions of Part 25).
