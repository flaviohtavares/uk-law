## 25.2. Service of appeal documents[^key-1d0a0f917ed3b650fc66f9aae6e1c81d]

*Extent: E*

(1) Within 7 days of the date on which the notice of appeal is filed with the registrar of the Court the appellant must serve a copy of the notice of appeal on—

- (a) every other party; and
- (b) the diocesan registrar.

(2) The registrar of the Court must send a copy of the notice of appeal to—

- (a) any person or body who was given special notice of the petition and who sent representations under rule 9.5(1)(a) or who gave evidence under rule 13.3 (Evidence of Historic England and national amenity societies); and
- (b) the Church Buildings Council.
