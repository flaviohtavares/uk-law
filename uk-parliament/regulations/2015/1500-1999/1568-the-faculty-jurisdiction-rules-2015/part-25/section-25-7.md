## 25.7. Registrars[^key-4e4047d6194a993fd21dd12782b85a21]

*Extent: E*

(1) The registrar of the province of Canterbury and the registrar of the province of York are joint registrars of the Court.

(2) The duties of the registrar of the Court in relation to an appeal are to be carried out by the registrar of the province comprising the diocese from whose consistory court the appeal is brought unless the joint registrars determine otherwise in a particular case.
