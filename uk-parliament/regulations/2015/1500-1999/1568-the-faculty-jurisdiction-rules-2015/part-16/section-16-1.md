# The Faculty Jurisdiction Rules 2015, section 16.1

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England.

## 16.1. Applicant[^c-ad62695212a722c3ee3ac6a8fdeb00f3]

*Extent: E*

An application for an injunction or restoration order may be made by—

- (a) the archdeacon;
- (b) any other person appearing to the court to have a sufficient interest in the matter.
