## 19.3. Appeals against registrar’s assessment[^c-b33ca9a123e432572dee33ef25da591e]

*Extent: E*

(1) Any appeal against the registrar’s assessment of costs relating to proceedings in a consistory court is to be made to the chancellor in such manner as the chancellor may direct.

(2) On an appeal the chancellor may confirm or vary the registrar’s assessment.
