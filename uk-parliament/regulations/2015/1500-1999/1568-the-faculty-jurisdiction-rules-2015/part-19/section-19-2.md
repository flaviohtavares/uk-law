## 19.2. Assessment of costs[^key-d8ff15dae1806bc2b2325691043e9e10]

*Extent: E*

Unless the amount of any costs is specified in the order made by the court, costs are to be assessed by the registrar in such manner as the registrar thinks just, subject to any directions given by the court.
