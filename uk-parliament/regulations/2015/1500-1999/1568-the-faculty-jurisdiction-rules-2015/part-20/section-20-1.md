## 20.1. Inspection of church etc.[^c-49fec3fbd7688fd1ae8aba6b79b4f7a0]

*Extent: E*

The court may at any stage in proceedings inspect any church, other building, article or thing which is the subject of the proceedings or concerning which any question arises in the proceedings.
