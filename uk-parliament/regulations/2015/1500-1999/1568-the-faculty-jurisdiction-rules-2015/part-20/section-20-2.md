> Text as at 2024-01-01.

> Extent: England.

## 20.2. Non-compliance[^c-cad07f1882df6e963ac6fba40d9f4fe6]

*Extent: E*

(1) A failure to comply with any provision of these Rules does not render any proceedings void unless the court directs otherwise.

(2) Where there has been a failure to comply with any provision of these Rules, the court may set aside (either wholly or in part) or vary any faculty, judgment, order or decree on such terms as appear to the court to be just.
