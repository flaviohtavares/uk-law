# The Faculty Jurisdiction Rules 2015, section 9.1

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England.

## 9.1. Special notice[^key-9a245aba0dc37b971646a3ddb8323f29]

*Extent: E*

If the chancellor directs [^key-876221733a26cbe7778c6dd181194467]... any person or body to be given special notice of faculty proceedings the registrar must serve on that person a copy of the public notice and any other documents directed by the chancellor.
