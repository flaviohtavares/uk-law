# The Faculty Jurisdiction Rules 2015, section 9.7

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England.

## 9.7. Discretionary consultation with the Church Buildings Council[^c-e6993085154cd5a319bfeba57e9879fc]

*Extent: E*

In any case where rule 9.6 does not apply (or does not apply to a particular proposal) but the chancellor thinks that the advice of the Church Buildings Council would be of assistance, the chancellor may seek its advice.
