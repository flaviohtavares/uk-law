# The London North West Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 650

National Health Service, England

The London North West Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2015

Made

6th March 2015

Coming into force

31st March 2015

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 27 and 272(7) of, and paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

The Treasury has consented to the making of this Order as required by paragraph 1(7) of that Schedule.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the London North West Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2015 and comes into force on 31st March 2015.

## 2. Originating capital of NHS trust

The originating capital of the London North West Healthcare National Health Service Trust[^f00002] is £294,802,000.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Earl Howe

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Department of Health

23rd February 2015

We consent,

Gavin Barwell

David Evennett

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

6th March 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital, in accordance with paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 2006, of the London North West Healthcare National Health Service Trust.
