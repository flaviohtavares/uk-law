# The Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 874 (C. 54)

Immigration

The Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2015

Made

23rd March 2015

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 73(1) and 75(3) of the Immigration Act 2014[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2015.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 6th April 2015

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Immigration Act 2014 is 6th April 2015—

- (a) section 39 (related provision: charges for health services);
- (b) section 73(6) (transitional and consequential provision) so far as is necessary for the purpose of the provision listed in sub-paragraph (c); and
- (c) Part 9 of Schedule 9 (provision relating to persons unable to acquire nationality because natural father not married to mother).

James Brokenshire

Minister of State

Home Office

23rd March 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the fifth commencement order made under the Immigration Act 2014.

Article 2 brings into force section 39 of, and Part 9 of Schedule 9 to, that Act on 6th April 2015.
