# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 53

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Commander’s duty concerning the loading of aircraft[^c-c20866d5d82f0cad4c63f746ac1faffc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies to an aircraft registered in the Isle of Man that is being loaded for a flight.

(2) The commander must not cause or permit it to be loaded except in accordance with this Part.

(3) In this Part, loading includes the suspension of a load from the aircraft.
