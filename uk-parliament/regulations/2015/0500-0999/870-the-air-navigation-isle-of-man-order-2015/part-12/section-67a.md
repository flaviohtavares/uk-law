# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 67A

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67A. Use of oxygen[^c-c501bf0145de6abd19977189d134adcf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This article applies to an aircraft—

- (a) registered in the Isle of Man;
- (b) that is pressurised; and
- (c) that is intended to operate above an altitude where the atmospheric pressure is less than 376 hPa.

(2) The operator of the aircraft must ensure that there is available at the flight duty station of the aircraft for each flight crew member a quick-donning type of oxygen mask that will readily supply oxygen upon demand.
