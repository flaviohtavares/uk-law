# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 99M

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99M. Requirement for a minimum equipment list[^key-1ff103de54cdaaef966c909c1ed2be6a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The operator of an aircraft of a type that has a master minimum equipment list established must—

- (a) prepare and ensure that a minimum equipment list for the aircraft is approved by the Department; and
- (b) include the details of the minimum equipment list procedures in the company operations manual.
