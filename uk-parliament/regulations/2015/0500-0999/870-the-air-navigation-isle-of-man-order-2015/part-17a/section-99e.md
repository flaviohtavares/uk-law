# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 99E

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99E. Fatigue risk management programme[^key-1ff103de54cdaaef966c909c1ed2be6a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The operator of an aircraft must establish and implement a fatigue risk management programme that ensures that personnel involved in the operation and maintenance of aircraft do not carry out their duties when fatigued.

(2) The operator must include the programme in the company operations manual in respect of the aircraft.
