# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 99C

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99C. Checklists to be used[^c-1ff103de54cdaaef966c909c1ed2be6a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The operator of an aircraft must ensure that checklists are used by the flight crew of the aircraft prior to, during and after all phases of its operations, and in emergencies, all as detailed in the aircraft’s operations manual.

(2) The operator must ensure that the design and utilisation of checklists takes into account human factors.

(3) The commander of the aircraft must ensure that the checklists are fully complied with.
