# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 161

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 161. Application of Order to certain aircraft registered elsewhere[^key-3c55e0d13a9eccb81252059b8e8b29b4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Department may direct that such of the provisions of this Order and of any regulations having effect under this Order as may be specified in the direction have effect as if reference in those provisions to aircraft registered in the Isle of Man included references to the aircraft specified in the direction.

(2) A direction under paragraph (1) may only specify an aircraft registered elsewhere than in the Isle of Man but for the time being under the management of a person who, or of persons each of whom, is qualified to hold a legal or beneficial interest by way of ownership in an aircraft registered in the Isle of Man.
