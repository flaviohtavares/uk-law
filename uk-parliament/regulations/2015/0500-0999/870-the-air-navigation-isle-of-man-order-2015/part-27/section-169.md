# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 169

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 169. Meaning of operator[^key-7df4af227baa62c4738fde60567beb5f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

References in this Order to the operator of an aircraft are, for the purposes of the application of any provision of this Order in relation to a particular aircraft, references to the person who at the relevant time has the management of the aircraft.
