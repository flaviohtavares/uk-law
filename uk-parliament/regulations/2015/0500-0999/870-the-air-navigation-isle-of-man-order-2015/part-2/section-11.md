# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 11

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Prohibition of use for commercial air transport or aerial work[^key-2084ed1f3734b6fed5d850307d2ac67b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except in the case of a transfer of functions under Article 83 of the Chicago Convention, an aircraft registered in the Isle of Man prior to the date of the coming into operation of the Civil Aviation (Aircraft Registration and Marking) Order 2022 (SD 2022/0073)[^key-eed677bc53e360e7bc2481b799edc71c] must not fly on a flight for the purpose of commercial air transport or aerial work.
