# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 73

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Endangering safety of an aircraft[^c-398511642abe2558d3ebd0fc032fcd19]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person must not recklessly or negligently act in a manner likely to endanger an aircraft, or a person in an aircraft.
