# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 74

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Endangering safety of any person or property[^key-e3dfd426545fdda913d85c9618b4160c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person must not recklessly or negligently cause or permit an aircraft to endanger a person or any property.
