# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 79

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Stowaways[^c-2be148541dfa355fbd65d0c8d631f715]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person must not secrete himself or herself for the purpose of being carried in an aircraft without the consent of the operator or the commander or of any other person entitled to give consent to the person being carried in the aircraft.
