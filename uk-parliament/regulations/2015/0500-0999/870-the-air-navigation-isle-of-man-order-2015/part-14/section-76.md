# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 76

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Smoking in aircraft[^key-d473c4bb0eddd0f4703784868b3df213]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Notices indicating when smoking is prohibited must be exhibited in an aircraft registered in the Isle of Man so as to be visible from each passenger seat.

(2) A person must not smoke in any compartment of an aircraft registered in the Isle of Man at a time when smoking is prohibited in that compartment by a notice to that effect exhibited by or on behalf of the commander of the aircraft.
