# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 36

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Required flight crew of aircraft[^key-9f3308bbf9f180ba57b3a1aacb43bbc5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An aircraft must not fly unless it carries a flight crew of the number and description required by the law of the country in which it is registered.
