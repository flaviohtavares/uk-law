# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 86

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Use of flight recording systems[^c-92b8856fc4e54a3f88fbbfd4f696661e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On a flight on which a flight data recorder, a cockpit voice recorder or a combined cockpit voice recorder/flight data recorder is required by paragraph 4(3), (4), or (5) of the Table in Schedule 3 to be carried in an aeroplane, the recorder must always be in use from the beginning of the take-off run to the end of the landing run.

(2) On a flight on which a cockpit voice recorder, a flight data recorder or a combined cockpit voice recorder/flight data recorder is required by paragraph 4(12) of the Table in Schedule 3 to be carried in a helicopter, the recorder must always be in use from the time the rotors first turn for the purpose of taking off until the rotors are next stopped.
