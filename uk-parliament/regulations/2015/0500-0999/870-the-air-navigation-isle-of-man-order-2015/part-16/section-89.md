# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 89

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Power to inspect and copy documents and records[^c-5ddaaf877229020e8f5d357592e2e48f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An authorised person may inspect and copy any certificate, licence, log book, document or record that the person has the power to require to be produced under this Order.
