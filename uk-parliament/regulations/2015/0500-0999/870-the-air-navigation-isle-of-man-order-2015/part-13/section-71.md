# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 71

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Exceptions concerning carriage of weapons and munitions of war[^key-300282848b6c6aa938bd75629bf1edde]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Part applies to any sporting weapon or munition of war taken or carried on board an aircraft registered in a country other than the Isle of Man if the sporting weapon or munition of war may under the law of the country in which the aircraft is registered be lawfully taken or carried on board for the purpose of ensuring the safety of the aircraft or of persons on board.
