# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 69

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Carriage of weapons and of munitions of war – requirement for permission and for commander to be informed[^c-47016618be4bf007fef17b091a31e357]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to article 71, an aircraft must not carry any munition of war unless—

- (a) the munition of war is carried with the permission of the Department; and
- (b) the commander of the aircraft is informed in writing by the operator before the flight commences of the type, mass or quantity and location of any munition of war on board or suspended beneath the aircraft and any conditions of the permission of the Department.

(2) Subject to article 71, it is unlawful for an aircraft to carry any sporting weapon or munition of war in any compartment or apparatus to which passengers have access.
