# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 172

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 172. Application of Part[^c-844ed7a7271d9924e49d19c84737c86f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part applies to a commercial air transport flight by an aircraft registered in the Isle of Man prior to the date of the coming into operation of the Civil Aviation (Aircraft Registration and Marking) Order 2022 (SD 2022/0073)[^c-eb38ff5945e8904127b0c3472c9a2559].
