# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 149

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 149. Notifying competent authority of the detention of a third-country aircraft[^c-0635e426fa83dcb250da72e87723e111]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If an authorised person detains a third-country aircraft the person must immediately inform the competent authority of the State of the operator of the detention and, where necessary, of the State in which the aircraft is registered.
