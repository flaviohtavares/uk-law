# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 156

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 156. Exemption from Order[^c-0bc7dac96c5429ee3040d30bf28b4908]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Department may exempt from any of the provisions of this Order (other than articles 64, 85, 139, 140, [^c-879c477d8fe2de5a8a995b93652b9788]... 145 and 157) or any regulations referred to in article 155, an aircraft or person or class of aircraft or person, subject to such conditions as it thinks fit.
