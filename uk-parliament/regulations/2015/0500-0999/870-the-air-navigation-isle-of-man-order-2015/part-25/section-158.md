# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 158

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 158. Approval of persons to supply reports[^c-2186d8799ea72731b5f16943dd534941]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In relation to any of its functions under any of the provisions of this Order the Department may approve a person as qualified to supply reports to it and may accept such reports.
