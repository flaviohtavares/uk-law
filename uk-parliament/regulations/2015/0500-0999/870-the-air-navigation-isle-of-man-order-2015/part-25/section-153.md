> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 153. Obstruction of persons[^c-940014f977d2a58a6d8aaf4aae24b1c4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person must not intentionally obstruct or impede a person who is exercising a power or performing a duty under this Order.
