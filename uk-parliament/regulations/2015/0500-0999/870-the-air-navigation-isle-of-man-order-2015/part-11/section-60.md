# The Air Navigation (Isle of Man) Order 2015, section 60

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Operation of airborne collision avoidance system[^c-0a7150b0724258a5371ffb7e1fc9ca0b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On a flight on which an airborne collision avoidance system is required to be carried in an aeroplane by article 34 and Schedule 4, the system must be operated in accordance with procedures that are suitable having regard to the purposes of the equipment.
