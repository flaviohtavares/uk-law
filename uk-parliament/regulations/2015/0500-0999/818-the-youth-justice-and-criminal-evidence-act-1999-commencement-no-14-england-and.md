# The Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999 (Commencement No. 14) (England and Wales) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 818 (C. 50)

Criminal Law, England And Wales

The Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999 (Commencement No. 14) (England and Wales) Order 2015

Made

19th March 2015

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 68(3) of the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999 (Commencement No. 14) (England and Wales) Order 2015.

(2) In this Order “the 1999 Act” means the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 13th April 2015

The following provisions of the 1999 Act come into force in England and Wales on 13th April 2015—

- (a) section 45 (power to restrict reporting of criminal proceedings involving persons under 18), insofar as it applies to England and Wales;
- (b) section 48(a) (amendments relating to other reporting restrictions);
- (c) section 49[^f00002] (offences under Chapter IV), in so far as it has effect for the purposes of sections 45 and 45A[^f00003] of the 1999 Act;
- (d) section 50[^f00004] (defences), in so far as it has effect for the purposes of sections 45 and 45A of the 1999 Act;
- (e) section 51 (offences committed by bodies corporate), in so far as it as effect for the purposes of sections 45 and 45A of the 1999 Act;
- (f) section 52 (decisions as to the public interest) in so far as it has effect for the purposes of sections 45 and 45A of the 1999 Act;
- (g) in Schedule 2[^f00005] (reporting restrictions: miscellaneous amendments)—
  - (i) paragraph 1; and
  - (ii) paragraph 3, except for paragraph 3(9)[^f00006]; and
- (h) in Schedule 7 (transitional provisions and savings), paragraphs 1 and 6[^f00007], in so far as they have effect for the purposes of section 45 of the 1999 Act.

Shailesh Vara

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings provisions of the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act [1999 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23) (“the 1999 Act”) about reporting restrictions relating to under 18 year olds in criminal proceedings in England and Wales into force on 13th April 2015.

Article 2(a) brings into force section 45 of the 1999 Act which permits courts in certain criminal proceedings to restrict the publication of identifying information about any person under the age of 18.

Article 2(c) to (f) bring into force sections 49 to 52 of the 1999 Act, which make provision about the making of, and breaches of, reporting restrictions, insofar as those provisions apply to orders under section 45 of the 1999 Act, and under section 45A of that Act (lifetime reporting restrictions in criminal proceedings for witnesses and victims under 18) which is inserted into the 1999 Act by section 78 of the Criminal Justice and Courts Act [2015 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2) and also comes into force on 13th April 2015.

Article 2(b) and (g) bring into force paragraphs 1 and 3 of Schedule 2 of the 1999 Act which amends section 49 the Children and Young Persons Act [1933 (c. 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1933/12) (provision about reporting restrictions arrangements in certain youth and magistrates’ court proceedings).

Article 2(h) commences paragraphs 1 and 6 of Schedule 7, which make transitional provision, in so far as they apply to section 45 of the 1999 Act, so that the provisions of that section are not applicable in relation to proceedings instituted before the commencement of the section.
