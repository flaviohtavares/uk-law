# The Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations 2015, section 34

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Application to the Crown

*Extent: E+W*

These Regulations apply to the Crown as if, in Schedule 2 to these Regulations, after paragraph 2 there were inserted—

> **2A.**
>
> Hazardous substances consent is not required for the presence of a hazardous substance in, on, over or under land at military establishments, installations or storage facilities.
