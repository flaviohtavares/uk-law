> Text as at 2025-05-01.

## 15. Appeals against hazardous substances contravention notices

*Extent: E+W*

Sections 174, 175(3) and (6), 176 and 177 of the principal Act apply to appeals against hazardous substances contravention notices, subject to the modifications set out in Part 1 of Schedule 4.
