> Text as at 2025-05-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Effect of hazardous substances contravention notices, etc

*Extent: E+W*

Sections 178 to 181 of the principal Act have effect in relation to hazardous substances contravention notices, subject to the modifications set out in Part 2 of Schedule 4.
