# The Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations 2015, section 19

> Text as at 2025-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Validity

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Sections 285 and 289 of the principal Act apply to appeals against hazardous substances contravention notices, subject to the modifications set out in Part 4 of Schedule 4.

(2) Section 25(2) is subject to any order under section 289(4A) of the principal Act, as applied by paragraph (1).
