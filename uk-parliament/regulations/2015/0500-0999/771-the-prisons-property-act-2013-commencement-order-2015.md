# The Prisons (Property) Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 771 (C. 42)

Prisons, England And Wales

The Prisons (Property) Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015

Made

5th March 2015

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 2(2) of the Prisons (Property) Act 2013[^f00001]:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Prisons (Property) Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015.

## 2. Commencement

Section 1 (power to destroy or otherwise dispose of property) of the Prisons (Property) Act 2013 comes into force on 26th March 2015.

Andrew Selous

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Ministry of Justice

5th March 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 1 of the Prisons (Property) Act [2013 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/11) (“the Act”) into force on 26th March 2015. Section 1 of the Act inserts into the Prison Act [1952 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1952/52) a new section 42A which introduces a power for prison governors and directors to destroy or otherwise dispose of unauthorised property confiscated or found in specific circumstances.
