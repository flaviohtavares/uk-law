# The Children's Homes (England) Regulations 2015, section 15

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Duty to have regard to statements of standards published under section 23 of the Care Standards Act 2000

*Extent: E+W*

If the Secretary of State publishes a statement of standards in relation to children's homes under section 23 of the Care Standards Act 2000, the registered person must have regard to the statement [^c22409111].

## Annotations

[^c22409111]: Marginal citation: Section 23 was amended by: paragraphs 103 and 110 of Schedule 3 to the ACA 2002; paragraphs 16 and 21 of Schedule 9 to the [Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 (c.43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/43); section 28 of the CYPA 2008 and section 104 of the C&FA 2014.
