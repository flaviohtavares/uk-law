# The Children's Homes (England) Regulations 2015, section 55

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 55.

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 4(1)(b) of the Refuges (Children's Homes and Foster Placements) Regulations 1991 [^c22409261], for “Parts III to V of the Children's Homes Regulations 2001” substitute “ Parts 2 and 4 to 6 of the Children's Homes (England) Regulations 2015 ”.
