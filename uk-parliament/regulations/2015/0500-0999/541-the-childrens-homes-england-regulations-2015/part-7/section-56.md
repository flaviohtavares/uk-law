> Text as at 2022-07-01.

## 56. Revocations

*Extent: E+W*

The following regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Children's Homes Regulations 2001 [^c22409271];
- (b) the Children's Homes (Amendment) Regulations 2011 [^c22409281].
