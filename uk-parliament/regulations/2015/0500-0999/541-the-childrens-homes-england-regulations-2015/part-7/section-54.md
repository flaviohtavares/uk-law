# The Children's Homes (England) Regulations 2015, section 54

> Text as at 2022-07-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 54.

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 1 to the Licensing and Management of Houses in Multiple Occupation and Other Houses (Miscellaneous Provisions) (England) Regulations 2006 [^c22409251], for “Children's Homes Regulations 2001” substitute “ Children's Homes (England) Regulations 2015 ”.
