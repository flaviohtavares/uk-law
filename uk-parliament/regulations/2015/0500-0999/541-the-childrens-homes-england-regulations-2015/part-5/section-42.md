# The Children's Homes (England) Regulations 2015, section 42

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 42. Notification of offences

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a person listed in paragraph (2) is convicted of a criminal offence, whether in England and Wales or elsewhere, that person must without delay give notice in writing to HMCI of—

- (a) the date and place of the conviction;
- (b) the offence; and
- (c) the penalty imposed for the offence.

(2) The persons are—

- (a) the registered provider;
- (b) the registered manager;
- (c) the responsible individual (if one is nominated);
- (d) if the registered provider is an organisation, any director of that organisation;
- (e) if the registered provider is a partnership, any partner in that partnership.
