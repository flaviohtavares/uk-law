# The Children's Homes (England) Regulations 2015, section 37

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Other records

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Schedule 4 sets out the other information that the registered person must keep in relation to a children's home.

(2) The registered person must—

- (a) maintain in the home the records in Schedule 4;
- (b) ensure that the records are kept up to date; and
- (c) retain the records for at least 15 years from the date of the last entry.
