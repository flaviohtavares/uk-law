# The Children's Homes (England) Regulations 2015, section 1

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Children's Homes (England) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 1st April 2015.

(2) These Regulations apply—

- (a) only to children's homes in England;
- (b) to a person aged 18 or over, as they apply to children, if the person—
  - (i) is accommodated in a children's home; and
  - (ii) is not employed in, or in relation to, the home.
