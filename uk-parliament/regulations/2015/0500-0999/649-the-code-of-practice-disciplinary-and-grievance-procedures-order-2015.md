# The Code of Practice (Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 649

Terms And Conditions Of Employment

The Code of Practice (Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures) Order 2015

Made

10th March 2015

This Order is made in exercise of the power conferred by section 200(5) of the Trade Unions and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992[^f00001] (“the 1992 Act”).

Under section 199(1) and (4) of the 1992 Act, the Advisory, Conciliation and Arbitration Service (“ACAS”), may issue revised Codes of Practice containing such practical guidance as it thinks fit for the purpose of promoting the improvement of industrial relations.

In accordance with section 200(1) of the 1992 Act, ACAS prepared and published in draft a revised Code of Practice on disciplinary and grievance procedures (“the draft revised Code”), considered representations made to it about the draft revised Code, and modified it accordingly.

In accordance with section 200(2) of the 1992 Act, ACAS transmitted the draft revised Code to the Secretary of State.

In accordance with section 200(2) (a) of the 1992 Act, the Secretary of State approved the draft revised Code and laid it before both Houses of Parliament, and 40 days, beginning on the day on which the draft revised Code was laid before Parliament, have elapsed.

In accordance with section 200(4) of the 1992 Act, neither House of Parliament having resolved within the period of 40 days that no further proceedings should be taken on the draft Code, ACAS has issued the Code of Practice on disciplinary and grievance procedures[^f00002] in the form of the draft revised Code.

In accordance with section 200(5) the Secretary of State makes the following Order.

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Code of Practice (Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures) Order 2015.

## 2. Appointed day for the Code of Practice

The revised Code of Practice on disciplinary and grievance procedures, issued by ACAS on 10th March 2015 comes into effect on 11th March 2015.

Jo Swinson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations and Consumer Affairs

Department for Business, Innovation and Skills

10th March 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 11th March 2015 as the day upon which the revised Code of Practice on disciplinary and grievance procedures, which has been issued by ACAS under section 199 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, will come into effect.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this Order as the revised Code of Practice does not create any rights or obligations and it does not create any additional burdens on business.
