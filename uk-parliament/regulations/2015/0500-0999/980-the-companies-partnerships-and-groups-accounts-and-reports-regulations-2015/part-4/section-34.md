# The Companies, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Reports) Regulations 2015, section 34

> Text as at 2015-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Amendment of Part 1 (general rules and formats) of Schedule 3 to the Large and Medium-sized Companies Accounts Regulations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Part 1 of Schedule 3 to the Large and Medium-sized Companies Accounts Regulations is amended as follows.

(2) After paragraph 8, insert—

> **8A.**
>
> Where an asset or liability relates to more than one item in the balance sheet, the relationship of such asset or liability to the relevant items must be disclosed either under those items or in the notes to the accounts.
