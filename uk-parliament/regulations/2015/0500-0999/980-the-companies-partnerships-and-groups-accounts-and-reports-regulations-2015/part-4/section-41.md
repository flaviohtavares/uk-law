# The Companies, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Reports) Regulations 2015, section 41

> Text as at 2015-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Amendment of Schedule 10 (general interpretation) to the Large and Medium-sized Companies Accounts Regulations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Paragraph 3(1), substitute—

> (1) Save in Schedule 2 to these Regulations, the expressions listed in sub-paragraph (2) have the same meaning as they have in Directive [2013/34/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0034) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on the annual financial statements etc of certain types of undertakings and Council Directive [91/674/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0674) of 19 December 1991 on the annual accounts and consolidated accounts of insurance undertakings (for banking companies, see the definition in paragraph 96 of Schedule 2 to these Regulations).
