# The Companies, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Reports) Regulations 2015, section 10

> Text as at 2015-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Amendment of Chapter 1 of Part 16 (audit: requirement for audited accounts)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Chapter 1 of Part 16 of the Act is amended as follows.

(2) For sub-paragraph (i) of subsection (2)(c) of section 479A (subsidiary companies: conditions for exemption from audit) [^c22494521], substitute—

> - (i) the provisions of Directive 2013/34/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on the annual financial statements, consolidated statements and related reports of certain types of undertakings, or

.

(3) In section 479B (companies excluded from the subsidiary companies audit exemption) [^c22494531], for paragraph (a) substitute—

> - (a) a traded company as defined in section 474(1),

(4) In section 481 (companies excluded from dormant companies exemption), before paragraph (a), insert—

> - (za) is a traded company as defined in section 474(1),

.
