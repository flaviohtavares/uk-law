# The Companies, Partnerships and Groups (Accounts and Reports) Regulations 2015, section 25

> Text as at 2015-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Amendment of Schedule 8 (general interpretation) to the Small Companies Accounts Regulations

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 8 to the Small Companies Accounts Regulations is amended as follows.

(2) For paragraph 2(1), substitute—

> (1) The expressions listed in sub-paragraph (2) have the same meaning as they have in Directive 2013/34/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on the annual financial statements etc of certain types of undertakings.

.

(3) In paragraph 8(5) [^c22494561], omit the words “and Part 1 of Schedule 4”.

(4) In paragraph 11(3) [^c22494571], for the words “Formats 2 and 4” substitute “ Format 2 ”.
