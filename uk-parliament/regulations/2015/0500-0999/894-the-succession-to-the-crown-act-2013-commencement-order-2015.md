# The Succession to the Crown Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 894 (C. 56)

Succession

The Succession to the Crown Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015

Made

24th March 2015

The Lord President of the Council, in exercise of the power conferred by section 5(2) of the Succession to the Crown Act 2013[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Succession to the Crown Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of the Succession to the Crown Act 2013, so far as it is not already in force, is 26th March 2015.

Nick Clegg

Lord President of the Council

24th March 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on 26th March 2015, the remaining provisions of the Succession to the Crown Act 2013.
