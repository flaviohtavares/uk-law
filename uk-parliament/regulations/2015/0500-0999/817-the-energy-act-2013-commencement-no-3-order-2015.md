# The Energy Act 2013 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 817 (C. 49)

Energy

The Energy Act 2013 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2015

Made

15th March 2015

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 156(1) of the Energy Act 2013[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Energy Act 2013 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2015.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 30th April 2015

The following provisions of the Act come into force on 30th April 2015—

- (a) sections 119 to 130;
- (b) Schedule 13.

Philip Dunne

Minister for Defence Equipment, Support and Technology

Ministry of Defence

15th March 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the third commencement order made under the Energy Act [2013 (c.32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/32) (“the Act”). It brings Part 4 of and Schedule 13 to the Act, which relate to the government pipe-line and storage system, into force on 30th April 2015.
