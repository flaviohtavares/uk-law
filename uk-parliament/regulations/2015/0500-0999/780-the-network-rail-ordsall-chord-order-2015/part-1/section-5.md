# The Network Rail (Ordsall Chord) Order 2015, section 5

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Application of local railway enactments[^key-7a0da53770e5263a9e8a7131cd2c57e2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The following enactments cease to have effect on the transfer under the powers conferred by this Order to Network Rail of any land within the Order limits to which they relate or, if the land is transferred before the date on which the Order comes into force, on the date that the Order comes into force—

- (a) The Liverpool and Manchester Railway Act 1829[^f00018];
- (b) The London and North Western Railway (New Works and Additional Powers) Act 1867[^f00019]; and
- (c) The Manchester Liverpool Road (Castlefield Properties Limited) Light Railway Order 1993[^f00020].
