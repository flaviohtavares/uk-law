# The Network Rail (Ordsall Chord) Order 2015, section 8

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 8. Power to deviate[^key-cb249261a4900dc07c6072328614718d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In constructing or maintaining any of the scheduled works, Network Rail may—

- (a) deviate laterally from the lines or situations shown on the works plans to the extent of the limits of deviation for that work; and
- (b) deviate vertically from the levels shown on the sections—
  - (i) to any extent upwards not exceeding 0.5 metres in relation to Works Nos. 1 to 3 and 3 metres in relation to Works Nos. 4 and 5; or
  - (ii) to any extent downwards as may be found to be necessary or convenient.
