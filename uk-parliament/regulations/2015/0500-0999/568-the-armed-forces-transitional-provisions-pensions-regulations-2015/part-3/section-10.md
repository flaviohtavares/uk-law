# The Armed Forces (Transitional Provisions) Pensions Regulations 2015, section 10

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Insertion of new article 5 into the AFPS Order 2005[^key-fca9721df81de8f2721d367472553276]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Armed Forces Pension Scheme Order 2005[^f00014] (“the AFPS Order 2005”) is amended by inserting after article 4 the following new article—

> **5. Closure of Scheme and transitional provision**
>
> (1) Schedule 2 (Transitional Provision) has effect.
>
> (2) After 31st March 2015, the Scheme is closed to new members except where provided for in Schedule 2.

.
