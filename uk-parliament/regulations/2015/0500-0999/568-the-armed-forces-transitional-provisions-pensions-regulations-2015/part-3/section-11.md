# The Armed Forces (Transitional Provisions) Pensions Regulations 2015, section 11

> Text as at 2015-04-01.

## 11. Amendment of rule A.1 in Schedule 1 to the AFPS Order 2005[^key-f6372ad68e127191f1bac305e8e843f8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Rule A.1(interpretation: general) in Schedule 1 to the AFPS Order 2005 is amended by inserting at the appropriate place—

> - “AFPS 2015” means the scheme established by the Armed Forces Pension Regulations 2014[^f00015];

;
