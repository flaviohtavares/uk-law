# The Armed Forces (Transitional Provisions) Pensions Regulations 2015, section 28

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Insertion of new regulation 3A[^key-93fdb1bc1a8b826ef8e632e8412f7a28]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Reserve Forces Non Regular Permanent Staff (Pension and Attributable Benefits Scheme) Regulations 2011[^f00024] (“the NRPS Regulations 2011”) are amended by inserting, after regulation 3, the following new regulation—

> **3A. Transitional provision**
>
> (1) Schedule 3 (Transitional Provision) has effect.

.
