# The Armed Forces (Transitional Provisions) Pensions Regulations 2015, section 31

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Insertion of new article 5 into the Reserve Forces Pension Scheme Regulations 2005[^c-89ee7dc75954f8336992120b39379143]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Reserve Forces Pension Scheme Regulations 2005(“the RFPS Regulations 2005”) [^f00026] are amended by inserting after regulation 2 the following new regulation—

> **3. Closure of Scheme and transitional provision**
>
> (1) Schedule 3 (Transitional Provision) has effect.
>
> (2) After 31st March 2015, the Scheme is closed to new members except where provided for in Schedule 3.

.
