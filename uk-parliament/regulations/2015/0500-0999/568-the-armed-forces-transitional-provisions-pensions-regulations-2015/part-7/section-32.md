# The Armed Forces (Transitional Provisions) Pensions Regulations 2015, section 32

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Amendment of rule A.1 in Schedule 1 to the RFPS Regulations 2005[^key-c6cabd2fbcada3020b7ffa338c1b701c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Rule A.1(interpretation: general) in Schedule 1 to the RFPS Regulations 2005 is amended by inserting at the appropriate place—

> - “AFPS 2015” means the scheme established by the Armed Forces Pension Regulations 2014[^f00027];

.
