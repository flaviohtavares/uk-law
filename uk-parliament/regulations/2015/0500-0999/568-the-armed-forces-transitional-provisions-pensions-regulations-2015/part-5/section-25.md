# The Armed Forces (Transitional Provisions) Pensions Regulations 2015, section 25

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Insertion of new regulation 3 into the FTRS Regulations[^c-d9f33fd8d44e0a48a23bfd0f25cb3983]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The FTRS Regulations are amended by inserting, after regulation 2, the following new regulation—

> **3. Transitional provision**
>
> Schedule 2 (Transitional Provision) has effect.
