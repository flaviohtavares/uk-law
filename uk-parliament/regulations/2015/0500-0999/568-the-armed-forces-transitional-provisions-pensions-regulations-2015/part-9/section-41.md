# The Armed Forces (Transitional Provisions) Pensions Regulations 2015, section 41

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Amendment of the Armed Forces Pension Regulations 2014[^key-c15629c9deffefc19cbc34580e33b0f3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Armed Forces Pension Regulations 2014[^f00030] are amended by inserting into regulation 2 (interpretation) at the appropriate place—

> - “AFPS 2015” means the scheme established by the Armed Forces Pension Regulations 2014.
