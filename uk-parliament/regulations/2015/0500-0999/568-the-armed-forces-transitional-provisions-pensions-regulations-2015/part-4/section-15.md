# The Armed Forces (Transitional Provisions) Pensions Regulations 2015, section 15

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Amendment of article 3 (general interpretation) of the EDP Order 2005[^key-d9de447a1e0583d3cac8a39864adacfb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Article 3 (general interpretation) of the Armed Forces Early Departure Payments Scheme Order 2005[^f00018] (“the EDP Order 2005”) is amended by inserting at the appropriate places—

- (a) ““AFPS 2015” means the scheme established by the Armed Forces Pension Regulations 2014 (“the AFP Regulations 2014”);”,
- (b) ““EDP 2015” means the scheme established by the Armed Forces Early Departure Payments Scheme Regulations 2014;”,
- (c) ““transition date” in relation to a person with dual entitlement means 1st April 2015 or, if later, the day on which a person ceased to be a full protection member of the AFPS 2005;”, and
- (d) ““full protection member” has the meaning given in Part 2 of Schedule 2 to the Armed Forces Pension Scheme Order 2005[^f00019];”.
