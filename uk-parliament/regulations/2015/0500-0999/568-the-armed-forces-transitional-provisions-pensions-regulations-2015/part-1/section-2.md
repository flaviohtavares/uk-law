# The Armed Forces (Transitional Provisions) Pensions Regulations 2015, section 2

> Text as at 2015-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-0700ab5173aaa5788af54e6e0dbaccc9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations—

- “AFPS 2015” means the scheme established by the Armed Forces Pension Regulations 2014[^f00005];
- “EDP 2015” means the scheme established by the Armed Forces Early Departure Payments Scheme Regulations 2014[^f00006].
