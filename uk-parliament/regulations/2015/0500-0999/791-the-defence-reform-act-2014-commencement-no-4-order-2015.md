# The Defence Reform Act 2014 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 791 (C. 45)

Defence

The Defence Reform Act 2014 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2015

Made

19th March 2015

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 50(1) and (2) of the Defence Reform Act 2014[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Order may be cited as the Defence Reform Act 2014 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2015.

**2.** In this Order “the Act” means the Defence Reform Act 2014.

## 3. Commencement

Paragraphs 19(b), 20(b) and 21(b) of Schedule 4 come into force on 31st March 2015.

Philip Dunne

Minister for Defence Equipment, Support and Technology

Ministry of Defence

19th March 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the remaining provisions of Part 2 of the Defence Reform Act [2014 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/20). These provisions remove previous restrictions relating to the Review Board for Government Contracts, which has now been wound-up. Paragraphs 2(5) and 6(4) of Schedule 5 to the Act have been superseded and will not be commenced.
