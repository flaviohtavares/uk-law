# The National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015, section 13

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Change in the publication of the serial numbers of winning bonds[^key-151eb4226ce71d22389f4d8955ac5a69]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Despite any requirement in the terms and conditions applicable to bonds to publish the serial numbers of winning bonds in the London Gazette, the Director of Savings may instead publish the numbers on the National Savings and Investments website.
