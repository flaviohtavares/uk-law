# The National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015, section 14

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Notification of winners[^key-7183fcca396051a5ac8f3e3f90365f60]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The holders of winning bonds must be notified—

- (a) by post at the last address given to the Director of Savings; or
- (b) by any other means of communication as are provided for in the terms and conditions applicable to their bonds, or as the Director of Savings may direct.
