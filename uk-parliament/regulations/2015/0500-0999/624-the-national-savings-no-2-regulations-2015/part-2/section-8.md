> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Payments to minors under sixteen years of age[^key-965203246cd6733b5c1ac90c55c086ef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a bond is held by a minor under the age of sixteen years, no payment may be made to the minor in relation to the bond.

(2) A payment of the amount repayable, or any other amount payable, in relation to a bond held by a minor under the age of sixteen years must be made to, and on the application of, a person who the Director of Savings is satisfied is a proper person to receive payment.[^key-a04382af46a9b019e5dda69e5fd1e80e]
