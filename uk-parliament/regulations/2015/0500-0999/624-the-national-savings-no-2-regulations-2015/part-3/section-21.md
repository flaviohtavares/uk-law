# The National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015, section 21

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Conversion[^key-07ca015420827516cd2a7d37bcfddba4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Stock may be converted into or exchanged for stock of another description under the terms and conditions applying to that stock, or under a direction of the Treasury, and the Director of Savings may require for those purposes the delivery of any document relating to that stock.
