# The National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015, section 96

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Law applicable to crossed warrants[^c-a6d726bb3f9dc1c6d1dbcac196455202]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 76(1), (3), (4) and (5) (so far as it relates to crossed cheques) and sections 77(6) and 78 to 81 of the Bills of Exchange Act 1882[^f00038] (which relate to crossed cheques), and sections 3 and 4 of the Cheques Act 1957[^f00039] (which relate to unendorsed cheques as evidence of payment and to the protection of collecting bankers), apply to a crossed warrant issued under these Regulations as if it were a cheque drawn on the Director of Savings by the officer issuing the crossed warrant, but nothing in these Regulations makes a crossed warrant negotiable.
