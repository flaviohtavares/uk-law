# The National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015, section 77

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Saving for rights of joint holders, etc.[^key-5f72652cedb8805bb5e1b2b15a2effd8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Nothing in these Regulations relating to joint holders of stock or certificates shall affect the mutual rights of joint holders.

(2) Nothing in these Regulations relating to a trustee shall, as between trustees or as between a trustee and a beneficiary under a trust, be deemed to authorise a trustee to act otherwise than in accordance with legal requirements applying to the trust and the terms of the instrument (if any) constituting the trust.
