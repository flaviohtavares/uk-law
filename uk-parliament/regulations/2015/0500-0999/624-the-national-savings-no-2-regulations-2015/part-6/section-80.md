# The National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015, section 80

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Persons under disability[^key-7913d1020ffd5993fe7b0ab9f7d98059]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a person who—

- (a) holds a bond or holds stock;
- (b) holds, or has an interest in, a certificate; or
- (c) holds a children’s certificate,

is a person who lacks capacity or is under legal disability for any reason other than age, anything which under these Regulations is required or authorised to be done by or to that person is to be done by or to a deputy or other person having power in law to administer the estate.
