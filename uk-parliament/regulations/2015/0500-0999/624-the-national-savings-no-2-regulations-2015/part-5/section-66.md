# The National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015, section 66

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Payments in the case of holders under sixteen years of age[^key-138204378b988a4f3d11da5d46f9d846]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) and to regulation 67, no repayment may be made in relation to a children’s certificate held by a person under the age of sixteen years.

(2) Where a person holding a children’s certificate is a person under the age of sixteen years, other than a person who lacks capacity, the Director of Savings may pay the whole or part of the amount repayable to a person—

- (a) who has made an application for payment; and
- (b) appears to the Director of Savings to be a proper person to receive payment.
