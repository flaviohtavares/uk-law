# The National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015, section 58

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Maximum holding of certificates[^c-27eacb8c4d9ecf0555c47f294b4fbd24]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person may not purchase a children’s certificate if the aggregate purchase price of the children’s certificates of that description which would in that event be held by the person on whose behalf it is to be purchased would exceed the maximum set by the terms and conditions applying to that description of children’s certificate, and the aggregate purchase price must be calculated in accordance with those terms and conditions.

(2) No person may hold a children’s certificate purchased in contravention of paragraph (1).
