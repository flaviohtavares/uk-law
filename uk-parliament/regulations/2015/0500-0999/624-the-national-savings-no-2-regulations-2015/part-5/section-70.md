# The National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015, section 70

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Prohibition on transfers[^c-ee90604a582ef104c4cf49ab87ef725b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No transfer may be made of a children’s certificate either during the life, or on the death, of the holder of that certificate.
