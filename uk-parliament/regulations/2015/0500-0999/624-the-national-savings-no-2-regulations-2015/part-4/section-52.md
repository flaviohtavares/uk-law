# The National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015, section 52

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Payments in the case of bankrupts[^c-594f003b21f390e87931d4d1a7f44f4c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where it appears to the Director of Savings that—

- (a) a bankruptcy order has been made against a person who is the sole holder of a certificate;
- (b) a trustee has been appointed in the bankruptcy of a sole holder; or
- (c) an order for the administration in bankruptcy of the estate of a deceased sole holder has been made,

the Director of Savings may pay to the Official Receiver or trustee (on their application) the amount repayable in relation to the certificate.
