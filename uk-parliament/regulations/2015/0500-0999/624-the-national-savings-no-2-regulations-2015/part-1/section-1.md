# The National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015, section 1

> Text as at 2025-03-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-9958470ad1eb1af46b8b79cf0724cb3f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 6th April 2015.

(2) These Regulations extend to Great Britain and Northern Ireland, the Isle of Man and the Channel Islands.
