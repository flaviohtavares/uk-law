# The Dogger Bank Creyke Beck Offshore Wind Farm Order 2015, section 5

> Text as at 2025-11-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Operation of generating stations[^key-76e1e0c1152d56bb6aaa89456a3c9d7d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The undertaker is authorised to operate the generating stations[^f00018] comprised in the authorised development.

(2) This article does not relieve the undertaker of any requirements to obtain a permit or licence under any other legislation that may be required from time to time to authorise the operation of a generating station.
