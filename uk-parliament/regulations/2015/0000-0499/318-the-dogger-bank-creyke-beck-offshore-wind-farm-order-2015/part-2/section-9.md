# The Dogger Bank Creyke Beck Offshore Wind Farm Order 2015, section 9

> Text as at 2025-11-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9. Bizco 1 and Bizco 4 may enter certain land for purpose of construction[^c-008e6ef3745cdfad28bf1eb734a11ce9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purpose of constructing Works No. 2A, 3A, 4A, 5A, 6A, 8A and 9A, Bizco 1 may enter the land shown on the works plans within the Order limits for Works No. 2B, 2BA or 2BC, 3B, 4B, 5B, 6B, 8B and 9B that has been acquired for the purpose of Project B.

(2) For the purpose of constructing Works No. 2B, 2BA or 2BC, 3B, 4B, 5B, 6B, 8B and 9B, Bizco 4 may enter the land shown on the works plans within the Order limits for Works No. 2A, 3A, 4A, 5A, 6A, 8A and 9A that has been acquired for the purpose of Project A.

(3) For the purpose of constructing Works No. 7, 10A, 10B, 10C, 10D, 10E and 10F, Bizco 1 (in common with Bizco 4), and Bizco 4 (in common with Bizco 1), may enter the land required for those works.
