> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 22. Compulsory acquisition of land: minerals[^key-5370bf69ba5209df55d30a3cda8e0bfc]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Part 2 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 (minerals) is incorporated in this Order, subject to the modification that for “acquiring authority” there is substituted “undertaker”.
