# The Dogger Bank Creyke Beck Offshore Wind Farm Order 2015, section 39

> Text as at 2025-11-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 39. Deemed licences under Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^c-542d59c0af89a476abb55945dade97c2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The following marine licences set out in Schedules 8 to 11 are deemed to have been issued under Part 4 of the 2009 Act (marine licensing) for the licensed activities specified in Part A of each licence and subject to the conditions specified in Part B of each licence—

- (a) Marine Licence 1 (set out in Schedule 8);
- (b) Marine Licence 2 (set out in Schedule 9);
- (c) Marine Licence 3 (set out in Schedule 10);
- (d) Marine Licence 4 (set out in Schedule 11).
