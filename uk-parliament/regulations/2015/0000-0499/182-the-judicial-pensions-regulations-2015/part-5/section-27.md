> Text as at 2024-12-17.

## 27. Descriptions of full retirement pension[^c-0784facb13040f01e5019ac4560027cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purpose of these Regulations, the descriptions of full retirement pension are—

- (a) full retirement standard earned pension;
- (b) full retirement earned pension attributable to an effective pension age option;
- (c) full retirement added (self only) pension; and
- (d) full retirement added (all beneficiaries) pension.
