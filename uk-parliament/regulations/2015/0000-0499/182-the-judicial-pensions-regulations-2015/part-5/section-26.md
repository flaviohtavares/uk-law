## 26. Descriptions of accrued pension[^c-5b4866ba5c576e32e505ba5a88f9aa03]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purpose of these Regulations, the descriptions of accrued pension are—

- (a) accrued earned pension;
- (b) accrued standard earned pension;
- (c) accrued earned pension attributable to an effective pension age option;
- (d) accrued added pension;
- (e) accrued added (all beneficiaries) pension; and
- (f) accrued added (self only) pension.
