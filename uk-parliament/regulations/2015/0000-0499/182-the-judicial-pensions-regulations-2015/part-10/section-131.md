# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 131

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Application of Part[^key-11c1a1b21c860f9470920fab24b92922]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part—

- (a) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 4 of Part 4 (transfer values) of PSA 1993 and is without prejudice to that Chapter; and
- (b) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 5 of that Part[^f00047] (early leavers: cash transfer sums and contribution refunds) and is without prejudice to that Chapter.
