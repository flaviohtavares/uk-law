# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 138

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. Effect of transfers-out[^key-fdd5bbdd96f38c11f4942f2a43e0e0e5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a transfer value payment is made under this Chapter in respect of a member’s accrued rights to benefits under this scheme, those rights are extinguished.
