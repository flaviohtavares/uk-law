# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 1

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-215d2ed5b1af9f9bd18f5c8ff85f367e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015.

(2) Part 1, Part 3, regulations 20, 128 and 166, Schedule 1 and Schedule 2 come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

(3) Paragraph 8 of Schedule 3 comes into force on the same day as paragraph 38 of Schedule 13[^c-33ddd4f8e3341478e431cd9ba265f3df] to the Pensions Act 2014[^f00005] comes into force, and on that day paragraph 7 of Schedule 3 ceases to have effect.

(4) The remaining provisions come into force on 1st April 2015.
