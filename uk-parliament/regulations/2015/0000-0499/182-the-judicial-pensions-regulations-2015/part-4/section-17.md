# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 17

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Enrolment[^c-b53154878dead8b342c6f1f75643f9e4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person (P) begins pensionable service under this scheme in relation to a qualifying judicial office on the first day on which P becomes an eligible person in relation to service in that office unless—

- (a) regulation 21 applies; or
- (b) P is a transition member with continuity of service and paragraph (2) applies.

(2) This paragraph applies if—

- (a) the first day on which P becomes an eligible person in relation to service in a qualifying judicial office is the day after P’s closing date; and
- (b) on P’s closing date—
  - (i) P was in service in the same office; and
  - (ii) P has opted out of a relevant[^c-441519eab34a1407e23e82d70e752e88] scheme in relation to that service.
