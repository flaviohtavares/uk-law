# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 13

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Qualifying judicial office[^key-551fecf68fa34b4ae0975cecdc6edba9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purpose of these Regulations, a person who is appointed to an office specified in an order made under paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 (judiciary) to the Act is in qualifying judicial office.
