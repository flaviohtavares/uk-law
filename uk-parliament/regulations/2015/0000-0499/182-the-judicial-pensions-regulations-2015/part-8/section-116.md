# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 116

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Pension protection lump sum death benefit[^c-b1f56ce2e35e83ab91e2033e455391c4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A lump sum death benefit is treated for the purposes of FA 2004 as a pension protection lump sum death benefit if and to the extent that—

- (a) the member has given written notice to the scheme manager that the lump sum death benefit is to be so treated; and
- (b) any other conditions that are required to be met for the lump sum death benefit to be so treated are met (see paragraph 14 of Part 2 (lump sum death benefit rule) of Schedule 29 to FA 2004[^f00041]).

(2) Tax may be deducted from the lump sum death benefit if the scheme manager is liable for tax under section 206 (special lump sum death benefits charge) of FA 2004[^f00042] in respect of a pension protection lump sum death benefit.
