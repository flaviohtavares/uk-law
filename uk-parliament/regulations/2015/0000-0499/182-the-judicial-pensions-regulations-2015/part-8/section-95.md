# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 95

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Meaning of dependant’s ill-health pension[^key-0dfc1fcb34199f0d43616576c2d18a2f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A dependant’s ill-health pension is a pension payable on the death of a member of this scheme if the member was entitled to the immediate payment of an ill-health pension as at the date of the member’s death.
