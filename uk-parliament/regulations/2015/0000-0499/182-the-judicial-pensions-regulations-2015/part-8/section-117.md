# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 117

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117. Recovery of payments[^key-70c855fd5e5d3804882ae06743db60ee]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The scheme manager may recover a lump sum death benefit paid to any person if the person’s nomination is subsequently found to be invalid.
