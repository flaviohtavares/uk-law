# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 102

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Meaning of “eligible child’s pension”[^c-d06a72fb641e015c8d7f4bb8ccd010e9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In these Regulations, “eligible child’s pension” means any of the following pensions payable under this Part—

- (a) a child’s earned pension;
- (b) a child’s ill-health pension;
- (c) a child’s added pension.
