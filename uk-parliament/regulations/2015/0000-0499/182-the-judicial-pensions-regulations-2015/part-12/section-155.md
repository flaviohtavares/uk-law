# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 155

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 155. Scheme manager to be scheme administrator for the purposes of Part 4 of Finance Act 2004[^c-8b02709622ec8b19763e399d84f535a6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The scheme manager is appointed to be responsible for all functions that are functions conferred or imposed on the scheme administrator under Part 4 (pension schemes etc.) of FA 2004.
