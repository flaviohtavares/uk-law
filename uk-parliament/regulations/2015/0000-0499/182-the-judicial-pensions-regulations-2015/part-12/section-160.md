# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 160

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 160. General prohibition on unauthorised payments[^key-1b7f677e8221b10d9748c5298880c0d9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations requires or authorises the making of any payment, which, if made, would be an unauthorised payment for the purposes of Part 4 (pension schemes etc.) of FA 2004 (see section 160(5) (payments by registered pension schemes) of that Act).
