# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 148

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 148. Late payment of retirement index adjustment[^c-7f015eb46c1301863ef8312955bcf991]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations requires any part of a pension attributable to a retirement index adjustment to be paid before the end of the last active scheme year.
