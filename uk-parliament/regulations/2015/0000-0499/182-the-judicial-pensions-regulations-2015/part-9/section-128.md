# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 128

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128. Deduction of payments for extra pension under Schedule 1[^c-d18aed773ba4d25469a4c44912e9f6b5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 1 has effect for the purpose of enabling deductions of payments for—

- (a) added pension; and
- (b) an effective pension age.
