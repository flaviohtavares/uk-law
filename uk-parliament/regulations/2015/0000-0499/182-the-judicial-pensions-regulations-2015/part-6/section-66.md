# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 66

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Meaning of “permanent breakdown in health”[^c-9f8d2fa96da78a86746defc0e94d7d31]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Chapter, a member’s breakdown in health is “permanent” if it is likely to continue until the member reaches prospective normal pension age.
