# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 61

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Partial retirement option notice[^c-f959156c192538bc020b860f1ded0479]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A partial retirement option may only be exercised—

- (a) by notice to the scheme manager in a form required by the scheme manager (“option notice”); and
- (b)[^c-b2a9a9d5b96b6a0132cf06806cc1b526] in the 3 months after P’s pensionable earnings are reduced, on a date agreed by the member and the scheme manager.

(2) An option notice must specify—

- (a) whether P claims payment of—
  - (i) the whole of P’s accrued pensions to which the office relates[^c-19209f5d0872bf6fc8ccc5a1a22bb697]; or
  - (ii) part only of P’s accrued pensions to which the office relates[^c-54ca94379a0bb09db7c0a27de56272b9]; and
- (b) if P claims payment of part only—
  - (i) the descriptions of accrued pension for which payment is claimed; and
  - (ii) the proportion of each description of accrued pension for which payment is claimed (“option proportion”), which must comply with regulation 62 , and[^c-8bbba5f6d6692ffb52b6b631b0239238]
- (d) if P holds more than one office, the office or offices in respect of which P is exercising the partial retirement option.
