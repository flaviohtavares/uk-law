# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 82

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Adjustment of allocated benefit (members who have reached the age of 75)[^c-13f228e90d6e3b94b574c9f226194d18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The amount of allocated pension payable to the beneficiary of an allocation election may be adjusted in a manner determined by the scheme manager if—

- (a) the member who made the allocation election dies after reaching the age of 75; and
- (b) on the death of the member, the amount of allocated pension payable to the beneficiary does not qualify as a dependants’ scheme pension under section 167 (pension benefit rules) of FA 2004[^f00037].

(2) In this regulation, “allocated pension” has the meaning given in regulation 81(1)(b).
