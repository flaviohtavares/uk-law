# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 87

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Reduction in pension debit member’s benefits[^c-4a6ce550c133798f7cb03f9fd131ebec]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under this Part are subject to the reduction to be made under section 31 (reduction of benefit) of WRPA 1999.
