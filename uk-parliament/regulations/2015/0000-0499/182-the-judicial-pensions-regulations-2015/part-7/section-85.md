# The Judicial Pensions Regulations 2015, section 85

> Text as at 2024-12-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Annual rate of pension credit member’s pension[^c-d99c5deebc4571f83577a381f378745e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The annual rate of a pension credit member’s pension is calculated by—

- (a) taking the amount of credited pension specified in the pension credit member’s account;
- (b) subtracting the early payment reduction (if any) specified in that account in relation to that amount; and
- (c) subtracting the commutation amount (if any) specified in that account in relation to that amount.

(2) If a member opts to buy out the early payment reduction, as provided for by regulation 86, the annual rate of pension is calculated without subtracting the early payment reduction.
