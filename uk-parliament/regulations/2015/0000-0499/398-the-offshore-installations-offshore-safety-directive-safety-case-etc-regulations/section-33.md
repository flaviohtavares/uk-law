# The Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations 2015, section 33

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Notification of major accident etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The operator, well operator or, if appropriate, the owner must notify the competent authority without delay of—

- (a) a major accident; or
- (b) a situation where there is an immediate risk of a major accident.

(2) The notification must describe the circumstances, including, where possible, the origin, the potential impacts on the environment and the potential major consequences.
