# The Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations 2015, section 40

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Penalties

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The maximum penalty for an offence consisting of a contravention of a requirement or prohibition imposed by or under these Regulations is—

- (a) on summary conviction—
  - (i) in England and Wales, imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or a fine, or both;
  - (ii) in Scotland, imprisonment for a term not exceeding twelve months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both; and
- (b) on conviction on indictment, imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or a fine or both.
