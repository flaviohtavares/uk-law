# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 171

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 171. Payment of member contributions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Member contributions for each pay period are to be deducted by the member's employer in respect of the member's pensionable earnings for that pay period.

(2) A member's employer may make contributions on the member's behalf in circumstances determined by the scheme manager.
