# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 152

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 152. Nominations for lump sum death grants

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A member of this scheme may nominate an individual to receive a lump sum death grant.

(2) A nomination may only be made by giving a signed notice to the scheme manager in a form the scheme manager requires.

(3) A member may revoke or alter a nomination by giving a further signed notice to the scheme manager in a form the scheme manager requires.

(4) On receipt of a notice under this regulation, the scheme manager must send the member a written notification of its receipt.
