# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 15

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Police Pension Boards: procedure

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A Police Pension Board may determine its own procedures, subject to scheme manager approval.

(2) If there is a tied vote on any issue, the chair has a casting vote.
