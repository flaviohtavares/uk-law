# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 127

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Claim for payment of pension credit member's pension

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The claim for payment of a pension credit member's pension may only be made by written notice to the scheme manager.

(2) The notice must specify the date from which payment of the pension is claimed (“claim date”).

(3) The notice must be given at least one month before the claim date.
