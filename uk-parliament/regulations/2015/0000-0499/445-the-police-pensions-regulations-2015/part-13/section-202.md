# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 202

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 202. Late payment of retirement index adjustment or retirement PIA index adjustment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in these Regulations requires any part of a pension attributable to a retirement index adjustment or retirement PIA index adjustment to be paid before the end of the last active scheme year.
