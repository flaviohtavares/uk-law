# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 218

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 218. Scheme manager to be scheme administrator for purposes of Part 4 of Finance Act 2004

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The scheme manager is appointed to be responsible for all functions that are functions conferred or imposed on the scheme administrator by or under Part 4 of FA 2004 (pension schemes etc).
