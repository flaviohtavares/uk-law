# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 44

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Descriptions of accrued pension

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purpose of these Regulations, the descriptions of accrued pension are—

- (a) accrued standard earned pension;
- (b) accrued club transfer earned pension;
- (c) accrued added (all beneficiaries) pension; and
- (d) accrued added (self only) pension.
