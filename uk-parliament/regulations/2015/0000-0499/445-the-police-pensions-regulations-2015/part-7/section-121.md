# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 121

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 121. Exercising an option under this Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A member may exercise an option under this Chapter by written notice to the scheme manager.

(2) In this Chapter, “**option notice**” means a notice by which a member exercises an option under this Chapter.
