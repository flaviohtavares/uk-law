# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 33

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Pensionable earnings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-a034a7149390327fa790e2925c3039f4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-7ecd24c365a52d30d20c146cd519ba3b] A member’s pensionable earnings for any period means pay to which the member is entitled in that period, account being taken of any retrospective increase in that pay.

(3) For a person in service as a member of a home police force, the [^c-0bd4f7a1ea95227c8071fdfda16987f7]...pay to which the member is entitled is the [^c-0bd4f7a1ea95227c8071fdfda16987f7]... pay to which the member is entitled as such a member.

(4) For a person in service as a member of a police force in England and Wales other than a home police force, the [^c-1343034c0fb83a4e9de39d89dfff3ef6]... pay to which the member is entitled is the [^c-1343034c0fb83a4e9de39d89dfff3ef6]... pay to which the member is entitled under the member's terms and conditions of employment.
