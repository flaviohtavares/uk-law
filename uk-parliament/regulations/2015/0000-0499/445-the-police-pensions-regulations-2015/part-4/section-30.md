# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 30

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Opting out of this scheme

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person (P) opts out of this scheme in relation to eligible service if P opts not to be an active member of this scheme in relation to that service.

(2) P may only exercise the option under paragraph (1) by written notice to the scheme manager (“opt-out notice”).

(3) The option is taken to be exercised on the date on which the opt-out notice is received by the scheme manager.
