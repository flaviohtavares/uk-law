# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 25

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Interpretation of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**automatic re-enrolment date**”, in relation to a person in eligible service, means a date determined under regulation 12 of the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations 2010 [^c22402451];
- “**continuous period of eligible service**” means a period of eligible service disregarding any period not exceeding 5 years in which the person is not in eligible service [^c22402461]; and
- “**first day of eligible service**” means the first day of a continuous period of eligible service.

## Annotations

[^c22402451]: Marginal citation: [S.I. 2010/772](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/772); [regulation 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/445/regulation/12) was amended by [S.I. 2012/215](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/215).

[^c22402461]: Marginal citation: See Chapter 1 of this Part. For the purpose of these Regulations, a member of a police force on a period of unpaid leave from that police force remains in eligible service.
