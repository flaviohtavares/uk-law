# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 26

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Automatic enrolment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person (P) begins pensionable service under this scheme on P's first day of eligible service unless—

- (a) regulation 31 (opting out before the end of the initial period of eligible service) applies; or
- (b) P is a transition member with continuity of service and paragraph (2) applies.

(2) This paragraph applies if—

- (a) P's first day of eligible service is the day after P's closing date; and
- (b) as at P's closing date, P was opted out of the existing police pension scheme.

(3) Paragraph (4) applies in relation to a person (P) in service as a member of a police force in England and Wales (“the original police force”) who becomes a member of another police force in England and Wales ( “ the new police force ”).

(4) A person in pensionable service under this scheme in relation to the original police force continues in pensionable service under this scheme when P becomes a member of the new police force.

(5) Paragraph (6) applies in relation to a person (P) who is a member of the police service in Northern Ireland or the Police Service of Scotland who becomes a member of a police force in England and Wales ( “ the new police force ”).

(6) A person in pensionable service under the 2015 NI police pension scheme or the 2015 Scotland police pension scheme begins pensionable service under this scheme when P becomes a member of the new police force.
