# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 79

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Application of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter applies in relation to a member of a police force (“the member”)—

- (a) who is an active member of this scheme; or
- (b) who, for the purpose of determining entitlement to payment of benefits to or in respect of the member, is taken to be an active member of this scheme [^c22402551].

## Annotations

[^c22402551]: Marginal citation: See Chapter 1 of Part 4 (active membership) for when a member of a police force is taken to be an active member of this scheme.
