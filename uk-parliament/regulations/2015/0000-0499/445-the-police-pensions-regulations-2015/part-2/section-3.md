# The Police Pensions Regulations 2015, section 3

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Establishment of scheme

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

These Regulations establish a scheme for the payment of pensions and other benefits to or in respect of members of a police force [^c22402211] in England and Wales other than special constables or police cadets [^c22402221].

## Annotations

[^c22402211]: Marginal citation: Under paragraph 7 of Schedule 1 to the Act, “**members of a police force**” in relation to England and Wales includes special constables and police cadets. See section 101 of PA 1996 for the meaning of “police force”.

[^c22402221]: Marginal citation: [See the Special Constables (Pensions) Regulations 1973 (S.I. 1973/431) and the Police Cadets (Pensions) Regulations 1973 (S.I. 1973/430)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1973/430).
