# The Traffic Management (Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council) Permit Scheme Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 34

Highways, England

The Traffic Management (Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council) Permit Scheme Order 2015

Made

7th January 2015

Coming into force

2nd March 2015

The Secretary of State for Transport having approved the Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme under section 34(2) of the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001] makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 34(4) and (5) and 39(2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Traffic Management (Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council) Permit Scheme Order 2015 and comes into force on 2nd March 2015.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme” means the permit scheme set out in the Schedule to this Order which was prepared and submitted to the Secretary of State by Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council and has been approved by the Secretary of State; and
- “specified streets” has the meaning given by regulation 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007[^f00002].

## 3. Commencement of Permit Scheme

The Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme comes into effect on 2nd March 2015.

## 4. Application of Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007

Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007 shall apply to the specified streets within the Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order gives effect to the Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme.

The Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme is designed to control the carrying out of works in specified streets in Knowsley by imposing an obligation to obtain a permit from Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council before undertaking the works.

*Article 3* gives effect to the permit scheme, which will come into effect on 2nd March 2015. The permit scheme referred to in this article is set out in the Schedule to the Order.

*Article 4* applies Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007 (which makes provision for the disapplication and modification of enactments in relation to specified works in specified streets) to the specified works and specified streets within the Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme.

A copy of the Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council Permit Scheme is deposited and can be inspected at Municipal Buildings, Huyton, Knowsley, Merseyside L36 9YU and is available to inspect on the Council’s website http://www.knowsley.gov.uk/residents/roads-and-transport/transport-planning.
