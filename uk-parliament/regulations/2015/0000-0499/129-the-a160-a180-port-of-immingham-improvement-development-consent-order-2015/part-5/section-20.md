# The A160/A180 (Port of Immingham Improvement) Development Consent Order 2015, section 20

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Compulsory acquisition of land – incorporation of the mineral code[^c-d30dc9d086402d49b8efc06b860112c6]

*Extent: E+W*

Part 2 of Schedule 2 (minerals) to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^f00031] is incorporated in this Order subject to the modification that for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the Secretary of State”.
