# The A160/A180 (Port of Immingham Improvement) Development Consent Order 2015, section 8

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8. Application and modification of legislative provisions[^c-872d2043bf31ca34c942f0a8ba4db190]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in the following legislative provisions, including any requirements for consent, applies to the authorised development—

- (a) Humber Commercial Railway and Dock Act 1904[^f00016]; and
- (b) Barton and Immingham Light Railway Order 1908.
