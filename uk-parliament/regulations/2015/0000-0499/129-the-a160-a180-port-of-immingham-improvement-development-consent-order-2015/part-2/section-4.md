# The A160/A180 (Port of Immingham Improvement) Development Consent Order 2015, section 4

> Text as at 2017-12-28.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Maintenance of authorised development[^key-dbd7597f9975dba8eb6739d2c08e9551]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State may at any time maintain the authorised development, except to the extent that this Order, or an agreement made under this Order, provides otherwise.
