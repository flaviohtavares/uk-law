# The Local Audit and Accountability Act 2014 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 223 (C. 15)

Local Government, England And Wales

The Local Audit and Accountability Act 2014 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2015

Made

11th February 2015

The Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 49(1) and (5) of the Local Audit and Accountability Act 2014[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Local Audit and Accountability Act 2014 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2015.

## 2. Appointed day

The day after the day on which this Order is made is the day appointed for the coming into force of paragraphs 37 and 43(1) and (4) of Schedule 12 to the Local Audit and Accountability Act 2014 for the purpose of the Secretary of State making regulations under section 32 of that Act, and section 45 of that Act so far as it relates to those paragraphs and that purpose.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Kris Hopkins

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Communities and Local Government

11th February 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on the day after the day the Order is made amendments in the Local Audit and Accountability Act 2014 (“the 2014 Act”) to section 134(6) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999, for the purpose of the Secretary of State making regulations under section 32 of the 2014 Act.
