The Secretary of State is a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the regulation of specifications, construction, placing on the market and use of equipment intended for weighing, measuring or testing or for ancillary purposes.

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and the powers conferred by sections 10(1), 11(1) and (4), 15(1) and (2), 86(1) and 94(1)[^f00003] of the Weights and Measures Act 1985[^f00004].

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-6f01bd28cf63af8348ae2913fcb07cb9]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Weights and Measures (Revocations) Regulations 2015.

(2) These Regulations come into force—

- (a) insofar as is necessary to give effect to Part 1 of the Schedule, on 25th March 2015; and
- (b) for all remaining purposes, on 1st December 2015.

## 2. Revocations[^c-bd0a0fc18c7550c969fe6204fc1ad5a7][^c-086e183fd3604bc7a6517d5cea68cb38]

The Schedule to these Regulations (Revocations, Amendments and Savings Provisions) has effect.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make a number of revocations of instruments relating to weights and measures.

Part 1 of the Schedule to the Regulations, which is made in reliance on the powers cited under the Weights and Measures 1985 (and in the case of the revocation of paragraph (19) of Part 2 of Schedule 1 to The Weights and Measures (Packaged Goods) Regulations 2006, section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972), revoke the Capacity Serving Measures (Intoxicating Liquor) Regulations 1988 and amending regulations, as these have effectively become redundant, having been superseded by the requirements of the Measuring Instruments (Capacity Serving Measures) Regulations 2006. It also makes necessary consequential amendments to other secondary legislation.

Part 2 of the Schedule implements Directive 2011/17/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9th March 2011 repealing Council Directives [71/347/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0347), [71/349/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0349), [74/148/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0148), [75/33/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0033), [76/765/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0765), [76/766/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0766) and [86/217/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1986/0217) regarding metrology (OJ L 71 18.3.2011 p 1) (with the exception of the repeal of Council [Directive 71/349/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0349), which was implemented by the Calibration of Tanks of Vessels (EEC Requirements)(Revocation) Regulations [S.I.2011/1439](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1439)). Part 2 of the Schedule, which is made in reliance on section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, revokes provisions which gave effect to those Directives in the United Kingdom. These provisions relate to measurement of cereals, alcoholmeters and alcohol hydrometers, certain types of weights and tyre pressure gauges. Part 2 of the Schedule also revokes a number of other redundant provisions which had been kept in place in connection with the transitional arrangements under other EU legislation in particular [Directive 2004/22/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0022) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 31st March 2004 on measuring instruments (OJ No. L 135,, 30.4.2004, p1). These include provisions relating to material measures of length, cold water meters and gas meters. The revocations are subject to saving provisions.

No impact assessment has been prepared in relation to this instrument as no impact on business, charities, voluntary bodies or the public sector is foreseen. An explanatory memorandum is available alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
