# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 62

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Record of disciplinary proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0bef358505300faddff2082aea422704][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The chief constable must cause a record to be kept for seven years of disciplinary proceedings and special case proceedings brought under these Regulations, together with the finding and decision on disciplinary action and any decision made at an appeal hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where the officer concerned is the chief constable, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] MOD *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Police Committee must cause such a record to be kept. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
