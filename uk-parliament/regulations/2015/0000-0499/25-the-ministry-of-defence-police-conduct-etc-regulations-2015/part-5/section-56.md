# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 56

> Text as at 2020-11-02.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Attendance of others at special case hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ba9f573585814d8290070f49c729c7cd][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] and any direction to the contrary given under regulation 24A[^key-a7dd16480f8a46c20f6513d735ba860c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3], a special case hearing must be held in public. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The person conducting or chairing the special case hearing may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) exclude from the whole or a part of the hearing any person who would otherwise be entitled to attend the hearing by virtue of paragraph (1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] so far as that person considers necessary—[^key-453b9e8bc09d47b18557ae40bf452966] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) in the interests of justice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] to protect the Convention rights of any person; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] where the proceedings involve confidential information and publicity would damage that confidentiality; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) in order to facilitate the proper conduct of the hearing, impose conditions relating to the attendance at the hearing of any person who is entitled to attend the hearing by virtue of paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
