# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 60

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Notification of outcome (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-93a1c46db2f714770770740a54cd1534][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The relevant authority must inform the officer concerned in writing of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the finding of the person or persons conducting the special case hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the reasons for that finding; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) in a case other than one to which regulation 58 (senior officers: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] further hearing[^c-f041b812c4172a428631fab19c2aab4d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] of MOD Police Committee to consider disciplinary action) applies, any disciplinary action imposed under regulation 59(1) (outcome of special case hearing) or any action taken under regulation 59(6), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

as soon as reasonably practicable and in any event before the end of 5 working days beginning with the first working day after the conclusion of the special case hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) In a case to which regulation 58 applies *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] [^c-93197571fc8630220b62ddfe31210cc0]..., the relevant authority must inform the officer concerned in writing of — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) any disciplinary action imposed under regulation 59(2) or any action taken under regulation 59(7), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the reasons for such action, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any event before the end of 5 working days beginning with the first working day after the conclusion of the further hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) A notice under paragraph (1) or (2) must include notice of the right of the officer concerned to an appeal hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) The relevant authority must send a copy of any notice under paragraph (1) or (2) to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^c-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or the Ombudsman, in any case in which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or the Ombudsman was entitled to attend the special case hearing to make representations (regulation 54); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) to the complainant and any interested person, in any case *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] which has been investigated under external procedures[^c-67b17c2012f27733aac9f6d6667ddfbc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3].

(5)[^c-95e8cedaa9ebf50250d9560c79a6ed04] If the disciplinary action imposed as a result of the special case hearing or the further hearing under regulation 58(3) is dismissal (whether with or without notice), the relevant authority must send a copy of the written information provided to the officer concerned in accordance with paragraph (1) or (2) to the College of Policing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
