# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 59

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Outcome of special case hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f3e42a9007ae3853781b18b65f5f7756][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the person or persons conducting a special case hearing in the case of an officer other than a senior officer find that the conduct of that officer amounts to gross misconduct, they must impose disciplinary action, which may be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), a final written warning; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) extension of a final written warning in accordance with paragraph (3); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) dismissal without notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) At a further hearing for a senior officer, if the report submitted under regulation 58(1) contained a finding that the conduct of the officer amounts to gross misconduct, the MOD Police Committee must impose disciplinary action, which may be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), a final written warning; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) extension of a final written warning in accordance with paragraph (3); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) dismissal without notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Where, on the assessment date, the officer concerned had a final written warning in force— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) a final written warning shall not be given; but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) subject to paragraph (5), in exceptional circumstances, the final written warning may be extended. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Where a final written warning is extended under paragraph (3), that warning remains in force for a period of 18 months from the date on which it would otherwise expire. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) A final written warning may be extended on one occasion only. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) Where the person or persons conducting the special case hearing in the case of an officer other than a senior officer find that the conduct of the officer concerned does not amount to gross misconduct, they may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) dismiss the case; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) return the case to the relevant authority to deal with in accordance with Part 4 at a misconduct meeting or, if the officer concerned had a final written warning in force at the assessment date, at a misconduct hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) At a further hearing for a senior officer, if the report under regulation 58(1) contained a finding that the conduct of the officer concerned does not amount to gross misconduct, the MOD Police Committee may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) dismiss the case; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) deal with the case in accordance with Part 4 at a misconduct meeting or, if the officer concerned had a final written warning in force at the assessment date, at a misconduct hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(8) Where the case is returned to the relevant authority under paragraph (6)(b) or dealt with by the MOD Police Committee under paragraph (7)(b), the relevant authority must proceed in accordance with Part 4, subject to regulation 23(1) being read as if the following are omitted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the words “regulation 45 (referral of case to special *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] case[^c-60f83cf03b2633d60f4f55e0e717caaf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] hearing) and” and “on receipt of”; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) sub-paragraphs (a), (b) and (c). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(9) Except in the case of extending a final written warning, the disciplinary action has effect from the date on which it is notified to the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(10) Where the question of disciplinary action is being considered, the person or persons considering it— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) must have regard to the record of police service of the officer concerned as shown on the officer’s personal record; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) may consider such documentary evidence as would, in their opinion, assist them in determining the question; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) must give— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the officer concerned, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the officer’s police friend or the relevant lawyer, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

  an opportunity to make oral or written representations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
