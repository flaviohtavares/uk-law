# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 51

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Persons conducting special case hearing: senior officers (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-08fe90750e151f8ee5d1d09ac46da5fa][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the officer concerned is a senior officer, the special case hearing shall be conducted by a panel of persons specified in paragraph (2), appointed by the relevant authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Those persons are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a)[^c-f911cc17bbdf569561cac582eb4787a0] a chair selected by the relevant authority from a list of persons who satisfy the judicial-appointment eligibility condition on a 7-year basis and have been nominated by the Secretary of State for the purposes of these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the Inspector of Constabulary; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) a person selected by the relevant authority from a list of candidates maintained by MOD Police Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
