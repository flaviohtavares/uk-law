# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 47

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Remission of case (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-14c4fcef550be28b83fa35a540768feb][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (4) and (5), at any time after the case has been referred to a special case hearing but before the beginning of that hearing the relevant authority may direct that the case be dealt with under Part 4 if it considers that the special conditions are no longer satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where a direction is made under paragraph (1), the relevant authority must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) proceed in accordance with Part 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) notify the officer concerned in writing before the end of 3 working days beginning with the first working day after that direction is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Where the relevant authority is to proceed in accordance with Part 4, regulation 23 shall be read as if for paragraph (1) there were substituted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

> (1) Subject to paragraph (7), the relevant authority must, as soon as practicable, determine whether the officer concerned has a case to answer in respect of misconduct or gross misconduct or whether there is no case to answer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) In any case where a statement under regulation 45(1)(b) has been made by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^key-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3], the relevant authority must consult with the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] before making a direction under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) In any case where a statement under regulation 45(1)(b) has been made by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the Ombudsman *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3], the relevant authority must consult with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the Ombudsman *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] before making a direction under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
