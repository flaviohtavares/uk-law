# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 45

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Referral of case to special case hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9057690d7378e7a61a3e29fdf38388de][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On receipt of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) a statement made under regulation 22(3) (investigator’s belief that special conditions satisfied), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) a statement from the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^c-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or Ombudsman to the effect that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) a case is being, or has been, investigated under external procedures; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] in the view of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or Ombudsman (as the case may be), the special conditions are satisfied, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

the relevant authority must determine whether the special conditions are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) In any case where special case proceedings have been delayed by virtue of regulation 13(3), the relevant authority must, as soon as practicable after it considers that such proceedings would no longer prejudice any criminal proceedings, make a further determination as to whether the special conditions are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) In any case where disciplinary proceedings have been delayed by virtue of regulation 13(3), the relevant authority may, as soon as practicable after it considers that such proceedings would no longer prejudice any criminal proceedings, determine whether the special conditions are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Where the relevant authority determines that the special conditions are satisfied, unless it considers that the circumstances are such as to make it inappropriate to do so, it must certify the case as a special case and refer it to a special case hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) This paragraph applies where the relevant authority determines— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) that the special conditions are not satisfied; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) that, although those conditions are satisfied, the circumstances are such that it is inappropriate to certify the case as a special case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) Where paragraph (5) applies, the relevant authority must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) if the determination was made on receipt of a statement made under regulation 22(3)(a), return the case to the investigator to complete the investigation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) if the determination was made on receipt of a statement made under paragraph (1)(b), and the investigation has not been completed, return the case to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the Ombudsman *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] (as the case may be) for the investigation to be completed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) in any other case, proceed in accordance with Part 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) Where the relevant authority is to proceed in accordance with Part 4, regulation 23 shall be read as if for paragraph (1) there were substituted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

> (1) Subject to paragraph (7), the relevant authority must, as soon as practicable, determine whether the officer concerned has a case to answer in respect of misconduct or gross misconduct or whether there is no case to answer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

.

(8) All referrals to a special case hearing under this regulation are subject to regulation 13(3) (outstanding or possible criminal proceedings). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
