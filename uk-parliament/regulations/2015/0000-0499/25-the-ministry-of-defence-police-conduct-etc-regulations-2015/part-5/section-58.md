# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 58

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Senior officers: (affected by repeal, see note) further hearing (affected by repeal, see note) of MOD Police Committee to consider disciplinary action (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5aaaad8c31e2e6e18614706258883e53][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-3ac8751ef319aa782ab73a10d7aa9548][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-d8dd0d01775a7df79a36a6d5f48bb409] Where the officer concerned is a senior officer, the persons conducting the special case hearing must, as soon as reasonably practicable after the hearing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) submit to the MOD Police Committee a report setting out— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the finding of the persons conducting the hearing (regulation 57(11)); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the reasons for that finding; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] in the case of a finding that the conduct of the officer concerned amounted to gross misconduct, a recommendation as to any disciplinary action which in their opinion should be imposed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] any other matter arising out of the hearing which they desire to bring to the notice of the relevant authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) provide to the MOD Police Committee a copy of the direction (if any) given under regulation 45A(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where a report is submitted under paragraph (1), the persons conducting the special case hearing must send a copy to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the officer concerned; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) if the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^key-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or Ombudsman was entitled to attend to make representations (regulation 54(1) or (2)), to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or Ombudsman. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) On receiving a report under paragraph (1) the MOD Police Committee must *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] hold a further hearing[^key-9f63fd12ee08566803cb711da63e62c4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] for the purpose of considering what disciplinary action (if any) should be imposed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) The provisions of these Regulations specified in paragraph (5) apply, with the modifications specified in paragraph (6), to a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] further hearing[^key-90cab811945436d5e96ed1f368a5f5ac] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] under paragraph (3) as if it was a special case hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4A)[^key-5b889264f9b8720a691d0aeedb9c2ffa] The MOD Police Committee may appoint a person (who may be a relevant lawyer) to advise it at the further hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) The provisions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) regulation 10 (police friend and legal representation); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) regulation 12 (provision of notices or documents); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) regulation 13 (outstanding or possible criminal proceedings); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (ca (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-1e3c9c525c3d983d691f07414e35df5d][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] 45A[^key-362a158215b04981f1782bac20db4c05] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] (national security: power to give directions in relation to special case hearings); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] 48[^key-428c16dccfb25ba973b4abdda21ac37b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] (notice of special case hearing); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (da (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-06cf977ef49a499dd67dee6d8a489e55][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] regulation 52A (notification of special case hearings); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (e) regulation 53 (attendance of officer concerned at special case hearing); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (f) regulation 54 (participation of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or Ombudsman and investigator at special case hearing); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (g)[^key-d9f35968563a420f5292b8d9fe6d1411] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h) regulation 56 (attendance of others at special case hearing); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (i) regulation 57(1) to (10) (procedure at special case hearing); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (j) regulation 61 (record of special case hearing). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) The modifications are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) each reference to the person conducting or chairing the special case hearing has effect as a reference to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] [^key-3b6d5dabe55bdbbcc20a7ba2e231832c]... the MOD Police Committee; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] [^key-e444bf059fc9c6dcd8be525e54e82778]...
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c36003526908e7b0da7d21d435b437b3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] in regulation 45A, in paragraphs (3) and (4), “conducting or” is omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) in regulation 48(1) the reference to the date on which notice was given under regulation 46 has effect as a reference to the date on which the report is submitted under paragraph (1) of this Regulation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c)[^key-6022e326ec0a7e7fef4e834925b11b7c] in regulation 52A— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) paragraph (2)(b) is omitted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] in paragraph (3), “, having been informed of the specified date,” is omitted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] paragraph (5)(b) is omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
