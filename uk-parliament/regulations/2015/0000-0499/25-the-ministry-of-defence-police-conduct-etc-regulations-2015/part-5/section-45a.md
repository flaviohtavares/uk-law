# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 45A

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45A. National security: power to give directions in relation to special case hearings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6177ea195ab77e65541a449ec9b4fdc7][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the Secretary of State considers it expedient in the interests of national security, the Secretary of State may give a direction (“the direction”), in writing, in relation to a special case hearing, relating to one or more of the following matters— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) that all or part of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] special case[^c-eb07f46d89c123c0df48c5f5871d3329] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] hearing must be conducted in private; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) that a specified person must be excluded from all or part of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] special case[^c-2dacde1e4c242ddf5a42f87902da6103] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) that steps must be taken to conceal the identity of a witness; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) that specified information must be excluded from any notice published under regulation 52A (notification of special case hearings) or 60(A) (publication of information in relation to outcome). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The Secretary of State must provide the direction (or a copy of it) to the relevant authority as soon as possible. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Following receipt of the direction, the relevant authority must supply a copy of it to the person conducting or chairing the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] special case[^c-532a1b30f01210aedb25eaf127e3f7ba] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] hearing as soon as possible. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) The person conducting or chairing the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] special case[^c-50634b3bcf93044baa7120b14cf2ea2a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] hearing must comply with the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
