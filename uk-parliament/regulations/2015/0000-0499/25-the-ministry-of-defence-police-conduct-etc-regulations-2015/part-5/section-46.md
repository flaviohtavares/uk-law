# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 46

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Notice of referral to special case hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bbdd5e77049724b96d0d12c7b91e909c][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a case is certified as a special case and referred to a special case hearing (regulation 45(4)), the relevant authority must, before the end of 3 working days beginning with the first working day after the case is so certified— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) give the officer concerned written notice of the certification and referral which describes the conduct which is the subject matter of the case and how that conduct is alleged to amount to gross misconduct; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) supply the officer with a copy of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the certificate issued under regulation 45(4); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] any statement the officer may have made to the investigator during the course of the investigation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] subject to the harm test— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the investigator’s report or such parts of that report as relate to the officer (together with any document attached to or referred to in that report as relates to the officer); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] any other relevant document gathered during the investigation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) For the purposes of this regulation, “relevant document” means a document which, in the opinion of the relevant authority, is relevant to the case of the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
