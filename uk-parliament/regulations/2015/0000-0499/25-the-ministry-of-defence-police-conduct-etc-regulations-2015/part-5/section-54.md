# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 54

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Participation of (affected by repeal, see note) Director General (affected by repeal, see note) or the Ombudsman and investigator at special case hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4c6f007fe167f69d7ba9e6ecd4671cec][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In any case where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] has made a statement under regulation 45(1)(b) (view that special conditions satisfied), the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] may attend the special case hearing to make representations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) In any case where the Ombudsman has made a statement under regulation 45(1)(b) (view that special conditions satisfied), the Ombudsman may attend the special case hearing to make representations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or Ombudsman intends to attend the special case hearing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the Director General or Ombudsman (as the case may be)[^key-cc9989e1d24ddb10cf8f2e4b135e0e47] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] must notify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the complainant or any interested person, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the person conducting or chairing the hearing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

  prior to the hearing; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the person conducting or chairing the hearing must notify the officer concerned prior to the hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or Ombudsman attends a misconduct hearing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the Director General or Ombudsman (as the case may be)[^key-bfedf2e83264f2e491f09f0a6711581f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] may *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] be represented by a relevant lawyer[^key-b1957d233b2dc1a24c1bc7c41c9937f1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3].

(5) The investigator or a nominated person must attend the special case hearing on the request of the person conducting or chairing the hearing to answer questions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) In this regulation, a “nominated person” means a person who, in the opinion of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the relevant authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) in a case where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] has made a statement under regulation 45(1)(b), the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3], or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) in a case where the Ombudsman has made a statement under regulation 45(1)(b), the Ombudsman investigated the case, the Ombudsman, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

has sufficient knowledge of the investigation of the case to be able to assist the person or persons conducting the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
