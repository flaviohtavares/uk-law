# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 50

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Person conducting special case hearing: officers other than senior officers (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1cc1198389adfd9cf7f4e1c7a8d05040][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where the officer concerned is an officer other than a senior officer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Subject to paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] (3)[^key-e552214676bbc3bd8043e0520282876d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3], the special case hearing must be conducted by the chief constable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Where the chief constable is an interested party or is unavailable, the special case hearing must be conducted by the chief officer of police of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] a relevant force[^key-340f916dc48bacc6598cd46765c7e376] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3].
