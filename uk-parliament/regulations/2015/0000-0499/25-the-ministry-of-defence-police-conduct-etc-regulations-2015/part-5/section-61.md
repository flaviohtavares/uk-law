# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 61

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Record of special case hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1fd242ce6347607098f3115232c9a110][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The relevant authority must ensure that a verbatim record of the proceedings at the special case hearing is taken. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The relevant authority must, if the officer concerned so requests, give the officer concerned a copy of the record of the proceedings at the conclusion of the special case hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
