# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 49

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Procedure on receipt of notice of special case hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a3eee93ed0bb2e4e855e56edbb528629][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before the end of 7 working days beginning with the first working day after the written notice given to the officer concerned under regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] 46(1)[^key-8ef19e8a9c9c1583e7fa51178f3c8b68] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3], the officer concerned must provide to the relevant authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) written notice of whether or not the officer accepts that the conduct amounts to gross misconduct; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) where the officer accepts that the conduct amounts to gross misconduct, any written submission the officer wishes to make in mitigation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) where the officer does not accept that the conduct amounts to gross misconduct, or the officer disputes part of the case, written notice of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the allegations the officer disputes and the officer’s account of the relevant events; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] any arguments on points of law the officer wishes to be considered by the person or persons conducting the special case hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Within the same time period, the officer concerned must give the relevant authority and the person conducting or chairing the special case hearing a copy of any document the officer intends to rely on at the hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
