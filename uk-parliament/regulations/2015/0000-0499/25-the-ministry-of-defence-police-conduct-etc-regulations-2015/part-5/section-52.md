# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 52

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Documents to be supplied (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-43ee70fb60a70592b27c8893bc840f51][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Prior to the special case hearing the relevant authority must give the person or persons conducting the special case hearing a copy of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] and other documents[^c-a364de977aa814685b017bf391ca4d88] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] given to the officer concerned under regulation 46 (notice of referral to special case hearing); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b)[^c-49d301fbee272808e5a86c71dd7f7e45] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) the documents provided by the officer concerned under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) regulation 49 (procedure on receipt of notice of special case hearing); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] where paragraph (2) applies, regulation 26(3) and (4) (procedure on receipt of notice); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) where the officer concerned does not accept that the conduct amounts to gross misconduct, any other document that, in the opinion of the relevant authority, should be considered at the hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) This paragraph applies where the case was certified as a special case following a determination made under regulation 45(3) (delayed disciplinary proceedings). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Prior to the hearing the relevant authority must give the officer concerned a list of the documents given under paragraph (1) and a copy of any document given under paragraph (1)(d). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
