# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 48

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Notice of special case hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-dce251cf03bd587e6bc1c63c1195b865][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The relevant authority must specify a date for the special case hearing to commence, which must be not fewer than 10 and not more than 15 working days beginning with the first working day after the date on which notice was given under regulation 46. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2)[^key-c4d2a00c085d4ec1521a29f2fb4ab877] The relevant authority must immediately notify the officer concerned of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the date, time and place of that hearing; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the effect of regulation 10 (police friend and legal representation). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^key-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or the Ombudsman is entitled to attend the special case hearing to make representations (regulation 54) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] [^key-936f496845c12891f1f081936b1cfaf4]... the relevant authority must notify the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or the Ombudsman of the date, time and place of the hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4)[^key-81a48b82a5cd46c9ebd65c104c865a6f] In any special case hearing arising from a case which has been investigated under external procedures, the relevant authority must notify the complainant and all interested persons of the date, time and place of the special case hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
