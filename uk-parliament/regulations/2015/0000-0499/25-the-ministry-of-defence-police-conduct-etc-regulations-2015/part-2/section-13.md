# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 13

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Outstanding or possible criminal proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9f564cec4a0b6fc7517eb6150e026e0b][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to this regulation, proceedings under these Regulations must proceed without delay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Before referring a case to misconduct proceedings or a special case hearing, the relevant authority must decide whether misconduct proceedings or special case proceedings would prejudice any criminal proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) For any period during which the relevant authority considers any misconduct proceedings or special case proceedings would prejudice any such criminal proceedings— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) no such misconduct or special case proceedings may take place; but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the relevant authority must preserve any relevant evidence in its possession. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Where a witness who is or may be a witness in any criminal proceedings is to be or may be asked to attend misconduct proceedings, the relevant authority must consult the relevant prosecutor (and when doing so must inform the prosecutor of the names and addresses of all such witnesses) before making its decision under paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) In this regulation “relevant prosecutor” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) in relation to England and Wales, the Director of Public Prosecutions or any other person who has or is likely to have responsibility for the criminal proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) in relation to Scotland, the Lord Advocate or any other person who has or is likely to have responsibility for the criminal proceedings; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) in relation to Northern Ireland, the Director of Public Prosecutions or any other person who has or is likely to have responsibility for the criminal proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
