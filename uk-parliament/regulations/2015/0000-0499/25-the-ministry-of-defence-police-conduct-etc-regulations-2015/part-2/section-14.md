# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 14

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Suspension (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9e8fc377ab60b9745981ad36d4cafe85][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The relevant authority may suspend the officer concerned from membership of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] if the following conditions (“the suspension conditions”) are satisfied— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) having considered temporary redeployment to alternative duties or to an alternative location as an alternative to suspension, the relevant authority has determined that such deployment is not appropriate in all the circumstances of the case; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) it appears to the relevant authority that either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the effective investigation of the case may be prejudiced unless the officer concerned is suspended; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] having regard to the nature of the allegation and any other relevant considerations, the public interest requires that the officer should be suspended. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) An officer who is suspended under this regulation remains an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officer for the purposes of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) The relevant authority may suspend the officer concerned at any time from the date on which these Regulations first apply to the officer concerned (regulation 9) until— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the relevant authority decides that the conduct of the officer concerned shall not be referred to misconduct proceedings or a special case hearing; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) such proceedings have concluded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) A suspension takes effect from the date and time of notification to the officer concerned, which must be given either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) in writing with a summary of the reasons; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) orally, in which case the relevant authority must confirm the suspension in writing with a summary of the reasons before the end of 3 working days beginning with the first working day after the suspension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) The officer concerned (or the officer’s police friend) may make representations against the suspension to the relevant authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) before the end of 7 working days beginning with the first working day after the suspension takes effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) at any time during the suspension if the officer reasonably believes that circumstances relevant to the suspension conditions have changed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) The relevant authority must review the suspension conditions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) on receipt of any representations under paragraph (5); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) on being notified that circumstances relevant to the suspension conditions may have changed (whether by means of representations made under paragraph (5) or otherwise); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) if there has been no previous review, before the end of 4 weeks beginning with the first working day after the suspension; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) if there has been no review in the intervening period, before the end of 4 weeks beginning with the day after the previous review. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) Following any review under paragraph (6), the relevant authority must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) decide whether the suspension conditions remain satisfied and whether the suspension should continue; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) before the end of 3 working days beginning with the day after the review, notify the officer concerned in writing of the decision made and a summary of the reasons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(8) An officer who is suspended remains so suspended until the earlier of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the suspension conditions are no longer satisfied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the events mentioned in paragraph (3)(a) or (subject to paragraph (10)) (3)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(9) Where an officer who is suspended is dismissed with notice under regulation 39(3)(b)(iv) the officer remains suspended until the end of the notice period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(10) In a case which will be investigated under external procedures, the relevant authority must consult with the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^c-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the Ombudsman *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] (as the case may be)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) in deciding whether or not to suspend the officer concerned; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) before a suspension under this regulation is brought to an end by virtue of paragraph (7)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
