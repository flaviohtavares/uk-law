# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 10

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Police friend and legal representation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bee7b5d2339bec6867db5bbf2ecb2013][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The officer concerned may choose— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) an MDP officer; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) an officer from *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] a relevant force[^c-d3d3412473c43302823733cdb3badf9d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3];
- (c) a staff member; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) a person nominated by a staff association, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

who is not otherwise involved in the matter, to act as their police friend. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) A police friend may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) advise the officer concerned throughout the proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) unless the officer concerned has the right to be legally represented and chooses to be so represented, represent the officer concerned at the misconduct proceedings or special case hearing or appeal meeting; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) make representations to the relevant authority concerning any aspect of the proceedings; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) accompany, and take notes for the officer concerned at any interview, meeting or hearing which forms part of any proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Where a police friend is an MDP officer or a staff member, that person may use a reasonable amount of duty time for the purposes referred to in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) The officer concerned has the right to be legally represented, by a relevant lawyer of the officer’s choice, at a misconduct hearing or a special case hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) If the officer concerned chooses not to be legally represented at such a hearing the officer may be dismissed or receive any other outcome under regulation 39 or 59 without being represented. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
