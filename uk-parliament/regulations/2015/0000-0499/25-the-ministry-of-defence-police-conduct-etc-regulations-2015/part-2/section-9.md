# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 9

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Application (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e9d3b8e7bf4e45a8aeab506a7905337f][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) These Regulations apply where an allegation comes to the attention of a relevant authority which indicates that the conduct of an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] [^c-67435a5a7d10306d85f4fc9be4a80262]... may amount to misconduct or gross misconduct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2)[^c-fb4ddc16c2d69bf136ff0fb0fad874ed] Except as set out in paragraph (6), these Regulations also apply, with the modifications set out in Schedule 1A, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) an allegation comes to the attention of a relevant person, or came to the attention of such a person before 1st December 2018 but after 22nd February 2015, which indicates that the conduct of a person who at the time of the alleged conduct was an MDP officer (“P”) may amount to gross misconduct; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) Condition A, B or C is satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Condition A is that P ceased to be an MDP officer after the allegation first came to the attention of a relevant person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Condition B is that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) P ceased to be an MDP officer before the allegation first came to the attention of a relevant person; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the period between the date P ceased to be an MDP officer and the date the allegation first came to the attention of the relevant person did not exceed 12 months. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) Condition C is that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) P ceased to be an MDP officer before the allegation first came to the attention of a relevant person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the period between the date P ceased to be an MDP officer and the date the allegation first came to the attention of the relevant person exceeded 12 months; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) the case to which the allegation relates has been investigated under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) external procedures; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] procedures— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] established with the Commissioner in accordance with an agreement under article 4 of the Police, Public Order and Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2006 (Consequential Provisions and Modifications) Order 2007; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] established with the Commissioner in accordance with an agreement under article 3(5) of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 (Consequential Provisions and Modifications) Order 2013; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] which apply where the Commissioner has been directed to investigate by an appropriate prosecutor, and for this purpose “appropriate prosecutor” has the meaning given by section 47 of the Police, Public Order and Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2006 (Consequential Provisions and Modifications) Order 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) Paragraph (2) does not apply in relation to a person who ceased to be an MDP officer before 1st December 2018. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7)[^c-f7ba5179ee8a9676fdccf531f0dba8e5] Where a relevant authority is considering more than one allegation in relation to the same *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or person in relation to whose alleged conduct these Regulations apply by virtue of paragraph (2)[^c-888915eea5f1d90f5acaec72fa34bd49] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3], the allegations may be taken together and treated as a single allegation for the purpose of any provision of these Regulations which requires a person to make an assessment, finding, determination or decision in connection with conduct which is the subject matter of an allegation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(8)[^c-62fbc39a3cf47332e7643de436631c78] In this regulation, “relevant person” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) a relevant authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the Director General; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) the Ombudsman; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) the Commissioner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
