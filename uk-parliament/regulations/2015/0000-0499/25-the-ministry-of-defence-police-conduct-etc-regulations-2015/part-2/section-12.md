# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 12

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Provision of notices or documents (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e856f6b831b18e53a936f2f09504f917][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where any written notice or document is to be given or supplied to the officer concerned, it shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) given to the officer concerned in person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) left with any person at, or sent by recorded delivery to, the officer’s last known address; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) in respect of a written notice under regulation 19(1), given to the officer concerned in person by that officer’s police friend where the police friend has agreed with the relevant authority to deliver the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where any written notice or document is given or supplied under paragraph (1), delivery is effective on the date on which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) it is given to the officer concerned, under paragraph (1)(a) or (c); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) it is left with any person at the officer’s last known address, under paragraph (1)(b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) receipt was recorded, if sent by recorded delivery to the officer’s last known address under paragraph (1)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
