# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 11

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Legal and other representation for the relevant authority (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-92c61845306a0f9f1a8addbf10cb2a42][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The relevant authority may be represented at *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] disciplinary proceedings[^key-723872909280f38c277e4162acdac7c2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) an MDP officer; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) at a misconduct hearing or a special case hearing only, a relevant lawyer (whether or not the officer concerned chooses to be legally represented). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the relevant authority may appoint a person to advise the person or persons conducting the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] disciplinary proceedings[^key-1fbbb0928199242af4ac905be6dfb69c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3].

(3) At a misconduct meeting or an appeal meeting, the person appointed under paragraph (2) shall not be a relevant lawyer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
