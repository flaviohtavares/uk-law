# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 7

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Written warnings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-907f126135f2f8a327e44d5a75bcc36b][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of these Regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) a written warning remains in force for a period of 12 months beginning on the day after it was given; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) subject to regulations 39(7)(b) and 59(3)(b), a final written warning remains in force for a period of 18 months beginning on the on the day after it was given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The reference to the period of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) 12 months in paragraph (1)(a), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) 18 months in paragraph (1)(b) and regulations 39(8) and 59(4), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

does not include any time when the officer concerned is taking *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] extended special unpaid leave[^key-54dfc3caba16ca3d6bab5fa971c0af84] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3].

(3)[^key-045311c42c5a7daeecfa2f93939419cc] In paragraph (2), “extended special unpaid leave” means a period of more than 3 months’ unpaid leave which the Secretary of State has agreed may be taken by the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
