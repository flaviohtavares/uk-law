# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 8

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Delegation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c29e4a699b7ddc1175ac495400c1236f][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The relevant authority may delegate any of its functions under these Regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) subject to paragraph (2), where it is the chief constable, to an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officer of at least the rank of chief inspector; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) where it is the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] MOD *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Police Committee, to a sub-committee of no fewer than three persons appointed by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] MOD *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Police Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where a function under regulation 14 or 45 has been delegated under paragraph (1)(a), any decision taken under those regulations shall be authorised by a senior officer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
