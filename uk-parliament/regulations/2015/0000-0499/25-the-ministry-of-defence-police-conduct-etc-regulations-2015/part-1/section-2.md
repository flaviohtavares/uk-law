# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 2

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Revocation and transitional provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-cabb3e0e79e938d531cefb63cc2f8e53][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] 2009 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] are revoked. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where any steps have been taken under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] 2009 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] before 23rd February 2015 in relation to an allegation in respect of conduct of an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officer, nothing in Parts 1 to 7 of these Regulations applies in relation to that allegation and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] 2009 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] continue to have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) The amendments in Schedule 2 to these Regulations (amendments of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Appeal Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]) do not apply to an appeal against a decision made under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] 2009 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3].

(4) The amendments in Schedule 3 to these Regulations (amendments of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Performance Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]) do not apply in any case where any steps have been taken in relation to unsatisfactory performance or attendance by a police officer before 23rd February 2015. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
