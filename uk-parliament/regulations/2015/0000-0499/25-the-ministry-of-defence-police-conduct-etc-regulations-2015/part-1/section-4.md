# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 4

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. The harm test (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5b2bf37d552e425386a9b0fc1e723700][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Information in documents which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] is[^key-95ccdaa76645214bbd18eac1433aaf8b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] stated to be subject to the harm test may not be supplied to the officer concerned in so far as the relevant authority considers that preventing disclosure is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) necessary for the purpose of preventing the premature or inappropriate disclosure of information that is relevant to, or may be used in, any criminal proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) necessary in the interests of national security; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c)[^key-f05b72544ed5b849b8ff5b576c76e57a] necessary for the purpose of the prevention or detection of crime, or the apprehension or prosecution of offenders; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d)[^key-004ab68805128619ffa2c11900ae36d0] necessary for the purpose of the prevention or detection of misconduct by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) other MDP officers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] persons under the direction and control of the chief constable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] persons under the direction and control of a chief officer of a relevant force, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

  or their apprehension for such matters; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (e) justified on the grounds that providing the information would involve disproportionate effort in comparison to the seriousness of the allegations against the officer concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (f) necessary and proportionate for the protection of the welfare and safety of any informant or witness; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (g) otherwise in the public interest. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where the harm test is satisfied in relation to any information, the relevant authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) may not supply that information to the officer concerned; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) must inform the person conducting or chairing *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] disciplinary[^key-ac88efa6fa86ffab736332f0bcc634f7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] proceedings that the harm test is satisfied in relation to that information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
