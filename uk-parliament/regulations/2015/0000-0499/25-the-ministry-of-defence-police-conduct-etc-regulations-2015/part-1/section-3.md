(1) In these Regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- “the 1987 Act” means the Ministry of Defence Police Act 1987;[^c-cbece797a91a446d6dbd98e29631a060] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “the 1998 Act” means the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^f00002];
- “the 2002 Act” means the Police Reform Act 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^f00003];
- “the 2009 Regulations” means the Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct) Regulations 2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^f00004];
- [^c-291a9afaa246e288a144081d3880598a]...
- “allegation” means an allegation relating to a complaint or conduct matter; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- [^c-55277439f88a81347a4d917294a5ebb2]...
- “appeal hearing” means an appeal to the police appeals tribunal in accordance with the Ministry of Defence Police Appeals Tribunals Regulations 2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^f00007];
- “appeal meeting” means a meeting held in accordance with regulation 43 following a misconduct meeting; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “the assessment date” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (a) if Part 3 applies to the case, the date of the assessment of the conduct of the officer concerned under regulation 16(1); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (b) if the case has been investigated under external procedures, the date that the case was referred for such investigation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- [^c-afa8006b76b48adba347d377e25931bf]...
- “the Commissioner” means the officer known as the Police Investigations and Review Commissioner, established under section 33 of the Police, Public Order and Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2006;[^c-f3d7056f0d5e241ed2a2f7ad885e1257] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “chief constable” means the chief constable of the MDP; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “complainant” means a person who makes a complaint, or on behalf of whom a complaint is made, where that complaint is being or has been investigated under external procedures;[^c-56aa6a73c117e6b719444eaf3a10f1e6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “complaint” means an expression of dissatisfaction about the conduct of an MDP officer;[^c-1f8c70b99cba48e50bc4cd353f0554b9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “conduct matter” has the meaning given to it by regulation 5; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “[^c-ec15479d6a96da56f7757f45e860c1e4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]**Convention rights *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]**” has the meaning given to it in section 1 of the Human Rights Act 1998; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “criminal proceedings” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (a) any prospective criminal proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (b) all criminal proceedings brought which have not been brought to a conclusion (apart from the bringing and determinations of any appeal other than an appeal against conviction); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “Director General” means the Director General of the Independent Office for Police Conduct;[^c-9c5aca5f198e69a31b68631314799efd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “disciplinary action” has the meaning given to it by regulation 6; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “disciplinary proceedings” means any proceedings under these Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] [^c-f33867318d59b5e8bcd3580295347969]...;
- “document” means anything in which information of any description is recorded and includes any recording of a visual image *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] [^c-587f83141302796c1e48e65e6f08c90f]...;
- “external procedures” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (a) procedures established with the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^c-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] an agreement made under section 26(1) of the 2002 Act[^c-7b14559433401ce30d178772ee6645e3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (b) procedures established with the Ombudsman in accordance with an agreement made under section 60(1) of the 1998 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] [^c-cda69bed9598dc3b836f9708d02c4567]...
  - (c) [^c-9c7a483f1f36000c3b2f75588d0b3a9e]...
- “gross misconduct” means a breach of the Standards of Professional Behaviour so serious that dismissal would be justified; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “the harm test” has the meaning given to it by regulation 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “the Inspector of Constabulary” means; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (a) in relation to England and Wales and Northern Ireland, Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Constabulary appointed under section 54(1) of the Police Act 1996 (appointment and functions of inspectors of constabulary) or an inspector of constabulary nominated by the Chief Inspector of Constabulary; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (b) in relation to Scotland, one of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Constabulary appointed under section 71 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^f00008] (inspectors of constabulary); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “informant” means a person who provides information to an investigation on the basis that the person’s identity is not disclosed during the course of the disciplinary proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “interested party” means a person whose appointment could reasonably give rise to a concern as to whether that person could act impartially under these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “interested person” means any person who the[^c-df561b5649b499b4953f3451d93165d3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or a relevant authority consider to have an interest in being kept informed about the handling of a complaint under external procedures, where that person has given consent to being kept so informed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “investigator” means a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (a) appointed under regulation 17; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (b) appointed as an investigator under external procedures; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “management action” means action intended to improve the conduct of the officer concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “management advice” means disciplinary action imposed following misconduct proceedings or an appeal meeting; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “MDP” means Ministry of Defence Police; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “MDP officer” means a member of the MDP; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “MOD Police Committee” means the committee appointed by the Secretary of State under section 1(5) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] 1987 Act[^c-c8c485d644c4a3247bde5e976f8cfbd0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3];
- “misconduct” means a breach of the Standards of Professional Behaviour, but which does not amount to gross misconduct; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “misconduct hearing” means a hearing to which the officer concerned is referred under regulation 23 and at which the officer may be dealt with by disciplinary action up to and including dismissal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “misconduct meeting” means a meeting to which the officer concerned is referred under regulation 23 and at which the officer may be dealt with by disciplinary action up to and including a final written warning; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “misconduct proceedings” means a misconduct meeting or misconduct hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “the officer concerned” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] means the person in relation to whose conduct as an MDP officer there has been an allegation[^c-e92ddfef86209e8906ee0ccae5a102d4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3];
- “the Ombudsman” means the Police Ombudsman for Northern Ireland established under section 51 of the 1998 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “the Performance Regulations” means the Ministry of Defence Police (Performance) Regulations 2012 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^f00009];
- “police friend” means a person chosen by the officer concerned in accordance with regulation 10; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “proposed witness” means a witness whose attendance at the misconduct proceedings the officer concerned or the relevant authority wishes to request of the person conducting or chairing those proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “relevant authority” has the meaning given in section 4(4) of the 1987 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “relevant force” has the meaning given in section 2B(3) of the 1987 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “relevant lawyer” has the meaning given in section 4(4) of the 1987 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “special case hearing” means a hearing to which the officer concerned is referred under regulation 45 after the case has been certified as a special case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “special case proceedings” means the referral of a case to a special case hearing and any proceedings at or in connection with such a referral; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “the special conditions” has the meaning given to it by regulation 22(4); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “staff association” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (a) in relation to an MDP officer other than a senior officer, the Defence Police Federation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (b) in relation to a senior officer, the Chief Police Officers’ Staff Association; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “staff member” means any person other than an MDP officer who is employed by the Secretary of State for Defence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “Standards of Professional Behaviour” means the standards of professional behaviour contained in Schedule 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- “working day” means any day other than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (a) a Saturday or Sunday; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (b) a day which is a bank holiday in accordance with section 1 of the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^f00010] in England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (c) a day which is a public holiday in England, Wales, Scotland or in Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) In these Regulations a reference to a copy of a statement shall, where the statement was not made in writing, be construed as a reference to a copy of an account of that statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3)[^c-730ea9250252311442f42d2953f3ae51] For the purposes of these Regulations, the making by an MDP officer of a protected disclosure is not a breach of the Standards of Professional Behaviour, and for this purpose “protected disclosure” has the meaning given by section 43A of the Employment Rights Act 1996. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
