# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 6

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Disciplinary action (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-64e07fbae703fe9291b3546e8a545740][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Disciplinary action means, in order of seriousness starting with the least serious action— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) management advice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) a written warning; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) a final written warning; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) an extension to a final written warning; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (e) dismissal with notice; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (f) dismissal without notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
