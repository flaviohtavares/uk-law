> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 (affected by repeal, see note) — Standards of Professional Behaviour (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2c85919f41ccff18827c3f0660c07935][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulation 3(1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

[^c-2c85919f41ccff18827c3f0660c07935]

- **Honesty and Integrity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]**

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers are honest, act with integrity and do not compromise or abuse their position. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- **Authority, Respect and Courtesy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]**

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers act with self-control and tolerance, treating members of the public and colleagues with respect and courtesy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers do not abuse their powers or authority and they respect the rights of all individuals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- **Equality and Diversity *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]**

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers act with fairness and impartiality. They do not discriminate unlawfully or unfairly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- **Use of Force *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]**

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers only use force to the extent that it is necessary, proportionate and reasonable in all the circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- **Orders and Instructions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]**

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers only give and carry out lawful orders and instructions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers abide by police regulations, force policies and lawful orders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- **Duties and Responsibilities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]**

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers are diligent in the exercise of their duties and responsibilities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- **Confidentiality *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]**

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers treat information with respect and access or disclose it only in the proper course of police duties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- **Fitness for Duty *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]**

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers when on duty or presenting themselves for duty are fit to carry out their responsibilities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- **Discreditable Conduct *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]**

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers behave in a manner which does not discredit the police service or undermine public confidence in it, whether on or off duty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers report any action taken against them for a criminal offence, any conditions imposed on them by a court or the receipt of any penalty notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- **Challenging and Reporting Improper Conduct *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]**

  MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officers report, challenge or take action against the conduct of colleagues which has fallen below the Standards of Professional Behaviour. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
