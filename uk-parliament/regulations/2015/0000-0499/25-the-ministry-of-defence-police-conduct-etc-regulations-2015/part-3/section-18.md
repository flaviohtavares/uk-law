# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 18

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Investigation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-dad4de5dbaa8178a96b3d4faed4395d5][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The purpose of an investigation under this Part is to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) gather evidence to establish the facts and circumstances of the alleged misconduct or gross misconduct; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) assist the relevant authority to establish whether there is a case to answer in respect of misconduct or gross misconduct or whether there is no case to answer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
