# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 17

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Appointment of investigator (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-36d01fb977e0b43e591e541bf17e6e18][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a matter is to be investigated in accordance with regulation 16. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The relevant authority must appoint a person to investigate the matter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) If the officer concerned is the chief constable, the relevant authority must notify the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) No person shall be appointed to investigate the matter who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) does not have an appropriate level of knowledge, skills and experience to plan and manage the investigation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) is an interested party; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) works, directly or indirectly, under the management of the officer concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) in a case where the officer concerned is a senior officer, is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the chief constable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] another *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
