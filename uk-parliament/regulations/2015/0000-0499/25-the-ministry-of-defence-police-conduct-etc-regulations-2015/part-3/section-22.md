# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 22

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Report of investigation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-604b69d49ff963da544b068b30bce929][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On completion of an investigation the investigator must as soon as practicable submit a written report on the investigation to the relevant authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The written report must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) provide a summary of the evidence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) attach or refer to any relevant documents; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) indicate the investigator’s opinion as to whether there is a case to answer in respect of misconduct or gross misconduct or whether there is no case to answer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) If at any time during the investigation the investigator believes that the relevant authority would, on consideration of the matter, be likely to determine that the special conditions are satisfied, the investigator must either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) if the investigation is not yet complete, submit to the relevant authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) a statement of the belief and the grounds for it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] a written report on the investigation to that point; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) if the investigation is complete, include in the written report required by paragraph (2) a statement of the belief and the grounds for it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) In this regulation, “the special conditions” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) there is sufficient evidence, in the form of written statements or other documents, without the need for further evidence, whether written or oral, to establish on the balance of probabilities that the conduct of the officer concerned constitutes gross misconduct; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) it is in the public interest for the officer concerned to cease to be a police officer without delay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
