# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 16

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Assessment of conduct (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e4bc5dbf2d279ef1235dd1fb8172d148][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The relevant authority must assess whether the conduct of the officer concerned which is the subject matter of the allegation, if proved, would amount to misconduct or gross misconduct or neither. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where the relevant authority assesses that the conduct, if proved, would amount to neither misconduct nor gross misconduct, it may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) take no action; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) take management action against the officer concerned; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) refer the matter to be dealt with under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Performance Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3].

(3) Where the relevant authority assesses that the conduct, if proved, would amount to misconduct, it must determine whether or not it is necessary for the matter to be investigated and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) if so, the matter must be investigated and the relevant authority shall further assess whether, if the matter were to be referred to misconduct proceedings, those would be likely to be a misconduct meeting or a misconduct hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) if not, the relevant authority may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) take no action; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] take management action against the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Where the relevant authority assesses that the conduct, if proved, would amount to gross misconduct, the matter must be investigated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) At any time before the start of any misconduct proceedings, the relevant authority may revise its assessment of the conduct under paragraph (1) if it considers it appropriate to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) Where the relevant authority decides to take no action, take management action or to refer the matter to be dealt with under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Performance Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3], it must notify the officer concerned in writing of that decision as soon as practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
