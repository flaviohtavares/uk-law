# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 20

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Representations to the investigator (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7bfff662e01dcc2eddc9411864bcbe48][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before the end of 10 working days starting with the first working day after the notice is given under regulation 19(1) (unless this period is extended by the investigator)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the officer concerned may provide a written or oral statement relating to any matter under investigation to the investigator; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the officer concerned or the police friend may provide any relevant documents or other evidence to the investigator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The investigator must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) as part of the investigation, consider any such statement, document or other evidence; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) make a record of having received it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) In this regulation “relevant document”— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) means a document relating to any matter under investigation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) includes such a document containing suggestions as to lines of inquiry to be pursued or witnesses to be interviewed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
