# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 21

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Interviews during investigation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9be58f40996a2a9926d90c3eb1d45fbf][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where an investigator wishes to interview the officer concerned as part of the investigation, the investigator must, if reasonably practicable, agree a date and time for the interview with the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where no date and time can be agreed under paragraph (1), the investigator may specify a date and time for the interview. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Where a date and time is specified under paragraph (2) and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the officer concerned or the police friend will not be available at that date and time; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the officer concerned proposes an alternative date and time which satisfies paragraph (4), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

the interview must be postponed to the time proposed by the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) An alternative time must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) be reasonable; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) fall before the end of the period of 5 working days beginning with the first working day after the day specified by the investigator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) The investigator must give the officer concerned written notice of the date, time and place of the interview. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) The investigator must, in advance of the interview, provide the officer concerned with such information as the investigator considers appropriate in the circumstances of the case to enable the officer concerned to prepare for the interview. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) A police friend may not answer any questions asked of the officer concerned during the interview. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
