# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 24A

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24A. National security: power to give directions in relation to misconduct hearings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b684b1d8a4bb609ec75274d0418712fc][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the Secretary of State considers it expedient in the interests of national security, the Secretary of State may give a direction (“the direction”), in writing, in relation to a misconduct hearing, relating to one or more of the following matters— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) that all or part of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] misconduct[^key-b300f67ed9c76e7054dc6a23e4cfb2a3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] hearing must be conducted in private; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) that a specified person must be excluded from all or part of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] misconduct[^key-1261a7cc7d92c34576c94c2b8309bf0f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) that steps must be taken to conceal the identity of a witness; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) that specified information must be excluded from any notice published under regulation 30A (notification of misconduct hearings) or 40A (publication of information in relation to outcome). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The Secretary of State must provide the direction (or a copy of it) to the relevant authority as soon as possible. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Following receipt of the direction, the relevant authority must supply a copy of it to the person conducting or chairing the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] misconduct[^key-6b82384ec125911e288fc80744ceea24] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] hearing as soon as possible. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) The person conducting or chairing the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] misconduct[^key-f9d53640986e84f7101f54652d942a9b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] hearing must comply with the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
