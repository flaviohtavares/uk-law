# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 41

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Record of misconduct proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-41b3db9cd724b2ab82bd75d4d5eb395c][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The relevant authority must ensure that a record of the misconduct proceedings is taken, and in the case of a misconduct hearing that record must be verbatim. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The relevant authority must, if the officer concerned so requests, give the officer concerned a copy of the record of the proceedings as soon as reasonably practicable after the conclusion of the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
