# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 38

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Senior officers: (affected by repeal, see note) further meeting or further hearing (affected by repeal, see note) of MOD Police Committee to consider disciplinary action (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0b7d07ec5aad0f2c4e68e5a8a4b92270][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-d491b42fdab8a01557f4e4a250f0868a][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-c7806206ee48b997f6209212e4490b65] Where the officer concerned is a senior officer, the persons conducting the misconduct proceedings must, as soon as reasonably practicable after the meeting or hearing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) submit to the MOD Police Committee a report setting out— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the finding of the persons conducting the proceedings (regulation 37(12)); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the reasons for that finding; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] in the case of a finding that the conduct of the officer concerned amounted to misconduct or gross misconduct, a recommendation as to any disciplinary action which in their opinion should be imposed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] any other matter arising out of the proceedings which they desire to bring to the notice of the relevant authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) provide to the MOD Police Committee a copy of the direction (if any) given under regulation 24A(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where a report is submitted under paragraph (1), the persons conducting the misconduct proceedings must send a copy to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the officer concerned; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) if the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^key-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or the Ombudsman was entitled to attend to make representations (regulation 33(1) or (2)), to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or the Ombudsman. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3)[^key-3f9a23e5b1f609e8edde00e40e6cc0e4] On receiving a report under paragraph (1), the MOD Police Committee must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) in a case where the report was submitted following a misconduct meeting, hold a further meeting; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) in a case where the report was submitted following a misconduct hearing, hold a further hearing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

for the purpose of considering what disciplinary action (if any) should be imposed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4)[^key-49305fc5c7ed2a29ac9263bb576b6308] The provisions of these Regulations specified in paragraph (6) apply, with the modifications specified in paragraph (7)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) where those provisions relate to misconduct meetings, to a further meeting held under paragraph (3)(a) as if it were a misconduct meeting; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) where those provisions relate to misconduct hearings, to a further hearing held under paragraph (3)(b) as if it were a misconduct hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) The MOD Police Committee may appoint a person (who may be a relevant lawyer) to advise it at the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] further meeting or further hearing[^key-eaad68dadc1168093c68e76faeef46ff] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3].

(6) The provisions are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) regulation 10 (police friend and legal representation); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) regulation 12 (provision of notices or documents); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) regulation 13 (outstanding or possible criminal proceedings); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (ca (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b9f7b215914c729ed0641009b825e205][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] 24A[^key-0112d509c86fc44ff7ec0b1f6b01eefa] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] (national security: power to give directions in relation to misconduct hearings); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) regulation 26(5) and (6) (procedure on receipt of notice); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (e) regulation 27 (witnesses); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (f) regulation 28 (timing and notice of misconduct proceedings); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (fa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c9cf82157431052cbb40b9f1fa2881d2][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] regulation 30A (notification of misconduct hearings); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (g) regulation 32 (attendance of officer concerned at misconduct proceedings); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (h) regulation 33 (participation of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or Ombudsman and investigator at misconduct proceedings); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (i) regulation 34 (attendance of complainant or interested person at misconduct proceedings); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (j) regulation 35 (attendance of others at misconduct proceedings); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (k) regulation 36 (exclusion from misconduct proceedings); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (l) regulation 37(1) to (11) (procedure at misconduct proceedings); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (m) regulation 41 (record of misconduct proceedings). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) The modifications are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) each reference to the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings has effect as a reference to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] [^key-115522b83d7dbca4394683fdfef4ede5]... the MOD Police Committee; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-1f016c18f770ae6df7fd6f2182d5f131][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] in regulation 24A, in paragraphs (3) and (4), “conducting or” is omitted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) in regulation 26(5), the reference to the date on which the officer concerned has complied with paragraph (3) has effect as a reference to the date on which the report is submitted under paragraph (1) of this regulation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] [^key-2fd8b5d7c9d737235d64a0749cfa9c2a]...
- (c) in regulation 28— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the reference in paragraph (1) to the first working day after the documents have been supplied to the officer concerned has effect as a reference to the first working day after the report is submitted under paragraph (1) of this regulation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the references in paragraph (3) to the relevant authority are omitted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d)[^key-bd3e07cb53fed1127f3cdd0b0518a440] in regulation 30A— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) paragraph (2)(b) is omitted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] in paragraph (3), “, having been informed of the specified date,” is omitted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] paragraph (5)(b) is omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
