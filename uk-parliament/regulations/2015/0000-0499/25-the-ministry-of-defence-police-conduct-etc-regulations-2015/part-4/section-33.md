# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 33

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Participation of (affected by repeal, see note) Director General (affected by repeal, see note) or Ombudsman and investigator at misconduct proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d627ff319e28201e10c682fb4622a4d5][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] may attend the misconduct proceedings to make representations in any case where under external procedures— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] has managed the investigation or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] [^c-6915d45df932157dc99868973833512a]... carried out the investigation; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] has instructed the relevant authority to carry out the investigation on its own behalf or under the supervision of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3], and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]—
  - (i) recommended that misconduct proceedings should be brought; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] directed that the relevant authority must give effect to such a recommendation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The Ombudsman may attend the misconduct proceedings to make representations in any case where under external procedures— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the case was investigated by the Ombudsman or on the Ombudsman’s behalf; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the Ombudsman— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) recommended that misconduct proceedings should be brought; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] directed that the relevant authority must give effect to such a recommendation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or Ombudsman intends to attend the misconduct proceedings— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the Director General or Ombudsman (as the case may be)[^c-2b8832a830be8c2c2fe3e12926a5a791] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] must notify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the complainant or any interested person, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

  prior to those proceedings; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings must notify the officer concerned prior to the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or Ombudsman attends a misconduct hearing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the Director General or Ombudsman (as the case may be)[^c-1113cd13e55df63413498da6092bb3ab] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] may *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] be represented by a relevant lawyer[^c-d844d968e5b9f02ba6e7ed17e8a4a3e8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3].

(5) The investigator or any nominated person must attend the misconduct proceedings on the request of the person conducting or chairing those proceedings to answer questions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) Where more than one allegation is considered in the same misconduct proceedings (regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] 9(7)[^c-dd0623376af6781f203db6382da02c1f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]), this regulation applies to the whole of the proceedings and accordingly the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or Ombudsman may make representations in respect of any allegation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) In this regulation, “nominated person” means a person who, in the opinion of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the relevant authority, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) in a case where under external procedures the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] managed the investigation or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] [^c-933ba64749d9ed3e1402ac66cbbed6f3]... carried out the investigation, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3], or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) in a case where under external procedures the Ombudsman investigated the case, the Ombudsman, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

has sufficient knowledge of the investigation of the case to be able to assist the person or persons conducting the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
