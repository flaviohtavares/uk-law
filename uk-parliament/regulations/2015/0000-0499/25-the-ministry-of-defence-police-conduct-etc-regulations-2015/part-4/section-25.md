# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 25

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Notice of referral to misconduct proceedings and panel membership (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-dff5f4c610594d8993f565d17673b790][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a case is referred to misconduct proceedings, the relevant authority must as soon as practicable give the officer concerned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) written notice of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the referral; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the conduct that is the subject matter of the case and how that conduct is alleged to amount to misconduct or gross misconduct; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the name of the person appointed to (in the case of a misconduct meeting for an officer other than a senior officer) conduct or (in any other case) chair the misconduct proceedings and of the effect of paragraphs (3) to (6) of this regulation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the effect of regulation 10 (police friend and legal representation); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) a copy of any written statement, or transcript or note of any oral statement, the officer may have made to the investigator during the course of the investigation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) subject to the harm test, a copy of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the investigator’s report or such parts of that report which relate to the officer (together with any document attached to or referred to in that report which relates to the officer); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] any other relevant document gathered during the course of the investigation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) As soon as practicable after— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) any person has been appointed under regulation 11(2) to advise the person or persons conducting the misconduct proceedings; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) where the matter has been referred to misconduct proceedings conducted by a panel, the persons comprising the panel (other than the chair) have been determined, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

the relevant authority must give the officer concerned written notice of the names of such persons and of the effect of paragraphs (3) to (6) of this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) The officer concerned may object to any person described in paragraph (1)(a)(iii) or (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Any such objection must be made in writing to the relevant authority before the end of 3 working days beginning with the first working day after the officer concerned is given notice of the person’s name and must set out the grounds of objection of the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) The relevant authority must notify the officer concerned in writing as soon as reasonably practicable whether it upholds or rejects an objection made under paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) If the relevant authority upholds the objection, the person to whom the officer concerned objects must be replaced (in accordance with regulations 11(2), 29 or 30 as appropriate). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) As soon as reasonably practicable after any such new appointment, the relevant authority must notify in writing the officer concerned of the name of the person appointed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(8) The officer concerned may object to a new appointment under paragraph (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(9) Any such objection must be made in accordance with paragraph (4), provided that it must be made before the end of 3 working days beginning with the first working day after receipt of the notification referred to in paragraph (7); and the relevant authority must comply with paragraphs (5) to (7) in relation to that objection, but paragraph (8) shall not apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
