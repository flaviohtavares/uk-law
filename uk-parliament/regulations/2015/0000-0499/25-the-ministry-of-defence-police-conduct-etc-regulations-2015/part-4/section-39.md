# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 39

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Outcome of misconduct proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-098241777a9351c5531b8e6c0a772541][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The person or persons conducting misconduct proceedings in the case of an officer other than a senior officer may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) impose any of the disciplinary actions in paragraph (3)(a) or (b) as appropriate; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) where they find the conduct amounts to misconduct but not gross misconduct following a misconduct meeting or hearing, record a finding of misconduct but take no further action. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) At a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] further meeting or further hearing[^c-ecac621d4690a7d8850c2d56e72829a2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] held under regulation 38(3) the MOD Police Committee may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) impose any of the disciplinary actions in paragraph (3)(a) or (b) as appropriate; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) where the finding set out in the report is that the conduct amounted to misconduct but not gross misconduct, record a finding of misconduct but take no further action. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) The disciplinary action is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) at a misconduct meeting or, in the case of a senior officer, a further meeting[^c-f0ed5219e3726516ea0be8e9f39525ab] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]—
  - (i) management advice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] written warning; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] final written warning; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) at a misconduct hearing or, in the case of a senior officer, a further hearing[^c-5a54e8a03a115d6f06b4b96232f54cbc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]—
  - (i) management advice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] written warning; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] final written warning; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] dismissal with notice; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (v) dismissal without notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) The disciplinary action referred to in paragraph (3) has effect from the date on which it is notified to the officer concerned and, in the case of dismissal with notice, the person or persons imposing the disciplinary action must decide the period of notice to be given, subject to a minimum period of 28 days. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) Where there is a finding that the conduct of the officer concerned amounts to misconduct but not gross misconduct, the officer may not be dismissed (whether with or without notice) unless a final written warning was in force on the assessment date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) Where the officer concerned had a written warning in force on the assessment date, a written warning may not be given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) Where, on the assessment date, the officer concerned had a final written warning in force— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) neither a written warning nor a final written warning may be given; but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) subject to paragraph (9), in exceptional circumstances, the final written warning may be extended. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(8) Where a final written warning is extended under paragraph (7)(b), that warning remains in force for a period of 18 months from the date on which it would otherwise expire. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(9) A final written warning may be extended on one occasion only. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(10) Where there is a finding of gross misconduct and the person or persons considering the question of disciplinary action decides that the officer concerned shall be dismissed, the dismissal shall be without notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(11) Where the question of disciplinary action is being considered, the person or persons considering it— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) must have regard to the record of police service of the officer concerned as shown on the officer’s personal record; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) may receive evidence from any witness whose evidence would, in their opinion, assist them in determining the question; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) must give— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the officer concerned, the police friend or, at a misconduct hearing, the relevant lawyer, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] in the case of an officer other than a senior officer, the relevant authority or any person appointed to advise the relevant authority (regulation 11(2)), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

  an opportunity to make oral or written representations before any such question is determined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
