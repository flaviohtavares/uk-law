# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 42

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Appeal from misconduct meeting: officers other than senior officers (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6c2374be1dfffecfa2b89d46ca4444e3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the officer concerned is an officer, other than a senior officer, whose case was decided at a misconduct meeting, the officer may, subject to the provisions of this regulation, appeal— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) if the officer admitted that the conduct amounted to misconduct, against any disciplinary action imposed under regulation 39; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) if (after the officer denied misconduct) the person conducting the misconduct meeting found that the officer’s conduct amounted to misconduct, against that finding or any disciplinary action imposed under regulation 39. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The only grounds of appeal under this regulation are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the finding or disciplinary action imposed was unreasonable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) there is evidence that could not reasonably have been considered at the misconduct meeting which could have materially affected the finding or decision on disciplinary action; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) there was a serious breach of the procedures set out in these Regulations or other unfairness which could have materially affected the finding or decision on disciplinary action. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) An appeal under this regulation may be commenced by the officer concerned giving written notice of appeal to the relevant authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) subject to paragraph (4), before the end of 7 working days beginning with the first working day after the written notice and summary of reasons is given under regulation 40; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) stating the grounds of appeal and whether a meeting is requested. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) The relevant authority may extend the time period specified in paragraph (3)(a) where it considers that it would be in the interests of justice to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) An appeal under this regulation shall be determined— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) where the person who conducted the misconduct meeting was a member of a police force, by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) a member of a police force of at least one rank higher than that person; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] unless the case substantially involves operational policing matters, a staff member who, in the opinion of the relevant authority, is more senior than that person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) where the person who conducted the misconduct meeting was a staff member, by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) a member of a police force who, in the opinion of the relevant authority is more senior than that person; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] a more senior staff member, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

who is not an interested party, appointed by the relevant authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) The relevant authority must as soon as reasonably practicable give the officer concerned written notice of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the name of the person appointed to determine the appeal under paragraph (5); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the name of any person appointed under regulation 11(2) to advise the person determining the appeal; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) the effect of paragraphs (7) to (10) of this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) Once notified the officer concerned may object to any person who is to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) determine the appeal; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) advise the person determining the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(8) Any such objection must be made in writing to the relevant authority before the end of 3 working days beginning with the first working day after the officer concerned is given notice of the person’s name and must set out the grounds of objection of the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(9) The relevant authority must notify the officer concerned in writing whether it upholds or rejects an objection made under paragraph (7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(10) If the relevant authority upholds the objection, the person to whom the officer concerned objects shall be replaced (in accordance with regulation 11(2) or paragraph (5) as appropriate). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(11) As soon as reasonably practicable after any such new appointment, the relevant authority shall notify in writing the officer concerned of the name of the new person appointed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(12) The officer concerned may object to the appointment of a person appointed under paragraph (10). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(13) Any such objection must be made in accordance with paragraph (8), provided that it must be made before the end of 3 working days beginning with the first working day after the officer concerned is given the notice referred to in paragraph (11); and the relevant authority must comply with paragraphs (9) to (11) in relation to that objection, but paragraph (12) shall not apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
