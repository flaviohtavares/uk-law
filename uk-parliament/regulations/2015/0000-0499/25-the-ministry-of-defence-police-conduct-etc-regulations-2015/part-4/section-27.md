# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 27

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Witnesses (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4d8912b92e2abea739da5e6365c17782][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) As soon as practicable after any list of proposed witnesses has been— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) agreed under regulation 26(6); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) where there is no agreement under regulation 26(6), given under regulation 26(5)(a), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

the relevant authority must give a copy of that list or lists to the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) consider the list or lists of proposed witnesses; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) subject to paragraph (3), determine which, if any, witnesses should attend the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) No witness may give evidence at misconduct proceedings unless the person conducting or chairing those proceedings reasonably believes that it is necessary for the witness to do so in the interests of justice, in which case the person conducting or chairing those proceedings must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) where the witness is an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] MDP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] officer, cause that person to be ordered to attend the misconduct proceedings; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) in any other case, cause the witness to be given notice that their attendance is requested and of the date, time and place of the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
