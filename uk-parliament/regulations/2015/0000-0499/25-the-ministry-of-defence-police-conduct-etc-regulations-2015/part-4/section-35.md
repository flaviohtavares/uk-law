# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 35

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Attendance of others at misconduct proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ddc51f4a51c95129f1cf7e7c25839ead][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) [^c-4b562661fdb471337032cdb3416c22fe]... A misconduct meeting must be held in private. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Subject to paragraphs (6) and (7) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] , regulation 36 and any direction to the contrary given under regulation 24A[^c-f3b1b3bec223a4dc3da81d0a0a5c4cf5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3], a misconduct hearing must be held in public. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) A person nominated by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^c-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or the Ombudsman may attend a misconduct meeting which arises from a case which has been investigated under external procedures as an observer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Unless expressly authorised by the person conducting or chairing a misconduct meeting, a witness, other than a witness who is also a complainant, an interested person or the officer concerned, may only attend the meeting for the purpose of giving evidence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) The person conducting or chairing a misconduct meeting may permit a witness in the meeting to be accompanied by one other person at that meeting. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) The person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) exclude from the whole or a part of the proceedings any person who would otherwise be entitled to attend the proceedings by virtue of regulation 34 or this regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] so far as that person considers necessary—[^c-040b011e56075a49bbe44f26489e4a40] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) in the interests of justice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] to protect the Convention rights of any person; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] where the proceedings involve confidential information and publicity would damage that confidentiality *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) in order to facilitate the proper conduct of those proceedings, impose conditions relating to the attendance at the proceedings of any person who is entitled to attend the proceedings by virtue of regulation 34 or this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) If a person is to give evidence as a witness in misconduct proceedings, the witness, and any person accompanying the witness, must not attend the proceedings before the witness is called to give evidence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
