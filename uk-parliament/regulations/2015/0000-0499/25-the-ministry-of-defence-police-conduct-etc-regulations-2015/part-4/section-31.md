# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 31

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Documents to be supplied (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bf34c4cb29fdabf66f1ea250e8d30882][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Prior to the misconduct proceedings the relevant authority must give the person or persons conducting the misconduct proceedings a copy of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the documents given to the officer concerned under regulation 25(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the documents given by the officer concerned under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) regulation 26(3) and (4); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] where paragraph (2) applies, regulation 49; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) where the officer concerned does not accept that their conduct amounts to misconduct or gross misconduct, or disputes any part of the case, any other documents that, in the opinion of the relevant authority, should be considered at the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) This paragraph applies where the relevant authority has directed, in accordance with regulation 47(1), that the case be dealt with under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Prior to the misconduct proceedings the relevant authority must give the officer concerned a list of the documents given under paragraph (1) and a copy of any such document if the officer has not already been supplied with a copy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
