# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 37

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Procedure at misconduct proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-03d385bc6b71d6d25e2042a96045f5f2][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to these Regulations, the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings may determine the procedure at those proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The misconduct proceedings may not commence unless the officer concerned has been notified of the effect of regulation 10 (police friend and legal representation). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings may from time to time adjourn the proceedings if it appears to be necessary or expedient to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) The misconduct proceedings may not, except in exceptional circumstances, be adjourned solely to allow the complainant or any witness or interested person to attend. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) The person representing the officer concerned may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) address the proceedings in order to do any or all of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) put the case of the officer concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] sum up that case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] respond on behalf of the officer concerned to any view expressed at the proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] make representations concerning any aspect of the proceedings; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (v) subject to paragraph (8), ask questions of any witnesses; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) confer with the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) Where the person representing the officer concerned is a relevant lawyer, the police friend of the officer concerned may also confer with the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) The police friend or relevant lawyer of the officer concerned may not answer any questions asked of the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(8) Whether any question should or should not be put to a witness shall be determined by the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(9) The person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings may allow any document to be considered at those proceedings notwithstanding that a copy of it has not been supplied— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) by the officer concerned to the relevant authority in accordance with regulation 26(3); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) to the officer concerned in accordance with regulation 25(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(10) This paragraph applies where evidence is given at the misconduct proceedings that the officer concerned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) was given written notice to attend an interview with an investigator (whether under regulation 21(5) or any equivalent notice given under external procedures) but failed to attend that interview; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) on being questioned by an investigator at any time after the officer was given written notice of investigation (whether under regulation 19(1) or any equivalent notice given under external procedures) failed to mention any fact relied on at the misconduct proceedings, being a fact which in the circumstances existing at the time, the officer concerned could reasonably have been expected to mention when so questioned; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) in submitting any information or by not submitting any information at all under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) regulation 20(1) (representations to the investigator), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] regulation 26(3) or (4) (procedure on receipt of notice), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] where a direction has been made under regulation 47 (remission of case), regulation 49 (procedure on receipt of notice), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] any equivalent opportunities to submit information under external procedures, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

  failed to mention any fact relied on at the misconduct proceedings, being a fact which in the circumstances existing at the time, the officer concerned could reasonably have been expected to mention when providing such information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(11) Where paragraph (10) applies, the person or persons conducting the misconduct proceedings may draw such inferences from the failure as appear proper. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(12) The person or persons conducting the misconduct proceedings must review the facts of the case and decide whether the conduct of the officer concerned amounts— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) in the case of a misconduct meeting, to misconduct or not; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) in the case of a misconduct hearing, to misconduct, gross misconduct or neither. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(13) The person or persons conducting the misconduct proceedings may not find that the conduct of the officer concerned amounts to misconduct or gross misconduct unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) they are satisfied on the balance of probabilities that this is the case; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the officer concerned admits it is the case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(14) The decision of the panel at a misconduct hearing may be based on a majority (with the chair having the casting vote if necessary). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
