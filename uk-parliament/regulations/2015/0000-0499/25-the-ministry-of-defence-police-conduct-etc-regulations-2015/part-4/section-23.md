# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 23

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Referral of case to misconduct proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-260814700a455392e855cf331951755b][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to regulation 45 (referral of case to special case hearing) and paragraph (7), on receipt of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) a written report which is made under regulation 22(2) (report of investigation), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) an equivalent report made under external procedures, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

the relevant authority must, as soon as practicable, determine whether the officer concerned has a case to answer in respect of misconduct or gross misconduct or whether there is no case to answer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) All referrals to misconduct proceedings under this regulation are subject to regulation 13(3) (outstanding or possible criminal proceedings). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Subject to regulation 45(3) and paragraph (7), in a case where the disciplinary proceedings have been delayed by virtue of regulation 13(3), as soon as practicable after the relevant authority considers that such proceedings would no longer prejudice any criminal proceedings, it must make a further determination as to whether the officer concerned has a case to answer in respect of misconduct or gross misconduct or whether there is no case to answer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Where the relevant authority determines there is no case to answer, it may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) take no further action against the officer concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) take management action against the officer concerned; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) refer the matter to be dealt with under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Performance Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3].

(5) Where the relevant authority determines that there is a case to answer in respect of gross misconduct, it must refer the case to a misconduct hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) Where the relevant authority determines that there is a case to answer in respect of misconduct— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) if the officer concerned had a final written warning in force at the assessment date, it must refer the case to a misconduct hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) in all other cases, it may either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) refer the case to a misconduct meeting; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] take management action against the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) Where the relevant authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) accepts a recommendation made under external procedures that proceedings should be brought at a misconduct meeting or a misconduct hearing, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) is directed under such procedures to give effect to such a recommendation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

it must refer the case to such a meeting or hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(8) If the relevant authority fails to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) make the determination referred to in paragraph (1), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) where appropriate, decide what action to take under paragraph (6), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

before the end of 15 working days beginning with the first working day after receipt of the written report, it must notify the officer concerned in writing of the reason for this. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(9) Where under paragraph (6) the relevant authority determines to take management action, it must give the officer concerned written notice of this as soon as practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
