# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 30

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Persons conducting misconduct proceedings: senior officers (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ac0371d724a7c4d61924d9cf8e3345c8][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where the officer concerned is a senior officer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Whether the case is referred to a misconduct meeting or a misconduct hearing, that meeting or hearing must be conducted by a panel of persons specified in paragraph (3), appointed by the relevant authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Those persons are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a)[^key-fcbdcc2c1996b93866fd9460e151998d] a chair selected by the relevant authority from a list of persons who satisfy the judicial-appointment eligibility condition on a 7-year basis and have been nominated by the Secretary of State for the purposes of these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the Inspector of Constabulary; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) a person selected from a list maintained by the MOD Police Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
