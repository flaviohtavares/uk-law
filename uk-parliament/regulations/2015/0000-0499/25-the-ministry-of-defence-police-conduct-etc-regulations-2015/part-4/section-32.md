# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 32

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Attendance of officer concerned at misconduct proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c39c180811911115261094156562475d][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the officer concerned must attend the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where the officer concerned informs the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings in advance that the officer is unable to attend, on grounds which the person conducting or chairing those proceedings considers reasonable, that person may allow the officer concerned to participate in the proceedings by video link or other means. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Where the officer concerned participates in the misconduct proceedings by video link or other means, or fails to attend the misconduct proceedings— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the officer may nonetheless be represented at those proceedings by the— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) police friend; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] in the case of a misconduct hearing, the relevant lawyer (in which case the police friend may also attend); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the proceedings may proceed and be concluded in the absence of the officer concerned whether or not the officer is so represented. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Where the officer concerned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) attends the proceedings by video link or other means, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) is represented in accordance with paragraph (3)(a), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

the relevant lawyer or the police friend, or both, may participate using the video link or other means as are used by the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
