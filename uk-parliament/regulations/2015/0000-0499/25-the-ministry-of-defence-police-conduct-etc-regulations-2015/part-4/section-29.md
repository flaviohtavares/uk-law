# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 29

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Persons conducting misconduct proceedings: officers other than senior officers (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-15988fd1784e9ffa773f9a9b72a395df][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where the officer concerned is an officer other than a senior officer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where the case is referred to a misconduct meeting, that meeting must be conducted by a person appointed by the relevant authority who is not an interested party and who satisfies paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) The person must be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) an MDP officer of at least one rank higher than the officer concerned; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) unless the case substantially involves operational policing matters, a staff member who, in the opinion of the relevant authority, is more senior than the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Where the case is referred to a misconduct hearing, that hearing must be conducted by a panel of three persons appointed by the relevant authority, comprising— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a)[^key-0718e1c9a37fa36731286d911ee2e642] a chair selected by the relevant authority from a list of persons who satisfy the judicial-appointment eligibility condition on a 5-year basis and have been nominated by the Secretary of State for the purposes of these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) an MDP officer of the rank of superintendent or above, who is of at least one rank above the officer concerned; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) a person selected by the relevant authority from a list of candidates maintained by the MOD Police Committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
