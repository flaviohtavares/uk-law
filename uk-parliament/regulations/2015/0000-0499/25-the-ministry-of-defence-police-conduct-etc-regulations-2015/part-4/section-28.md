# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 28

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Timing and notice of misconduct proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-717283b92f662437b378fd28a27b5432][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (6), the misconduct proceedings must commence— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) in the case of a misconduct meeting, before the end of 20 working days; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) in the case of a misconduct hearing, before the end of 30 working days, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

beginning with the first working day after the documents have been given to the officer concerned under regulation 25(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings may extend the period specified in paragraph (1) where they consider that it would be in the interests of justice to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Where the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings decides to extend the period under paragraph (2), or decides not to do so following representations from the officer concerned or the relevant authority, that person must provide in writing the reasons for that decision to the relevant authority and the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) The person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings must, if reasonably practicable, agree a date and time for the misconduct proceedings with the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) Where no date and time is agreed under paragraph (4), the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings must specify a date and time for those proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) Where a date and time is specified under paragraph (5) and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the officer concerned or the police friend will not be available at that date and time; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the officer concerned proposes an alternative date and time which satisfies paragraph (7), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

the misconduct proceedings must be postponed to the date and time proposed by the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) An alternative date and time must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) be reasonable; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) fall before the end of 5 working days beginning with the first working day after the day specified by the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(8) The person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings must give the officer concerned written notice of the date, time and place of the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(9) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^c-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the Ombudsman *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] is entitled to attend the misconduct proceedings to make representations (regulation 33) or to nominate a person to attend the proceedings as an observer (regulation 35), the person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings must give the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] the Ombudsman *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] written notice of the date, time and place of the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
