# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 43

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Appeal meeting (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-55e594ef57b94d54ecc6a3d299335c78][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where the officer concerned requests a meeting in the written notice of appeal (regulation 42(3)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The person determining the appeal must determine whether the notice of appeal sets out arguable grounds of appeal and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) if so, the person determining the appeal must hold an appeal meeting with the officer concerned, subject to paragraphs (3) and (5), before the end of 5 working days beginning with the first working day after that determination; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) if not, that person must dismiss the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) The person determining the appeal may extend the time period specified in paragraph (2)(a) where that person considers that it would be in the interests of justice to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) The person determining the appeal must give written notice of the date, time and place for the appeal meeting to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the officer concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^key-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or the Ombudsman was entitled to attend the misconduct meeting to make representations (regulation 33(1) or (2)), or to nominate a person to attend the meeting as an observer (regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] 35(3)[^key-025bb7d31c34c7f093878a29910138dd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]), the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or the Ombudsman; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) where the complainant or an interested person was entitled to attend the misconduct meeting (regulation 34), the complainant or interested person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the officer concerned or the police friend will not be available at that time; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the officer concerned proposes an alternative time which satisfies paragraph (6), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

the appeal meeting must be postponed to the time proposed by the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) An alternative time must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) be reasonable; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) fall before the end of 5 working days beginning with the first working day after the day specified by the person determining the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) Prior to the appeal meeting the relevant authority must give the person determining the appeal a copy of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the documents given to the person who held the misconduct meeting as specified in regulation 31(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the notice of appeal given by the officer concerned under regulation 42(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) the record of the misconduct meeting taken under regulation 41(1); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) any evidence of a kind referred to in regulation 42(2)(b) that the officer concerned wishes to submit in support of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
