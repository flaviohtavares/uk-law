# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 24

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Withdrawal of case (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5d3e4d20e208076c44df601250ecf613][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies to cases which have been or are being investigated under Part 3. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) At any time before the beginning of misconduct proceedings, the relevant authority may direct that the case be withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Where a direction is given under paragraph (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the relevant authority may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) take no further action against the officer concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] take management action against the officer concerned; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] refer the matter to be dealt with under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Performance Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the relevant authority must as soon as reasonably practicable give the officer concerned written notice of the direction, indicating what action will be taken under paragraph (3)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) a direction is given under paragraph (2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the investigation has been completed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) the officer concerned so requests, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) the harm test is not satisfied, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

the relevant authority must as soon as reasonably practicable after that request give the officer concerned a copy of the investigator’s report or such parts of that report as relate to the officer concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
