# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 26

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Procedure on receipt of notice (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-374f23b67601eff5dbb27c52038b72c6][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the officer concerned must comply with paragraphs (3) and (4) before the end of 14 working days beginning with the first working day after the documents have been supplied to the officer concerned under regulation 25(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) The person conducting or chairing the misconduct proceedings may extend the period described in paragraph (1) where to do so is in the interests of justice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) The officer concerned must provide to the relevant authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) written notice of whether or not the officer accepts that the conduct amounts to misconduct or gross misconduct; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) where the officer accepts that the conduct amounts to misconduct or gross misconduct, any written submission the officer wishes to make in mitigation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) where the officer does not accept that the conduct amounts to misconduct or gross misconduct, or the officer disputes part of the case, written notice of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) the allegations the officer disputes and the officer’s account of the relevant events; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] any arguments on points of law the officer wishes to be considered by the person or persons conducting the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) The officer concerned must give the relevant authority a copy of any document that the officer intends to rely on at the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) Before the end of 3 working days beginning with the first working day after the date on which the officer concerned has complied with paragraph (3), the relevant authority and the officer concerned must each give to the other either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) a list of proposed witnesses, including brief details of the evidence that each witness is able to adduce; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) give notice that they do not have any proposed witnesses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) Where there are proposed witnesses, the officer concerned and the relevant authority must, if reasonably practicable, agree a list of proposed witnesses, including brief details of the evidence that each witness is able to adduce. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
