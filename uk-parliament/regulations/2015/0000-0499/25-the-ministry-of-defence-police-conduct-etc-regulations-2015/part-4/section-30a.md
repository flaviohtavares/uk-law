# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 30A

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30A. Notification of misconduct hearings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8e5822625d9a0e443a011141e9124c8d][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The person (“the chair”) chairing a misconduct hearing may require notice of the hearing to be published which contains information relating to one or more of the matters in paragraph (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Before requiring notice to be published under paragraph (1), the chair must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) specify the date (“the specified date”) by which written representations under paragraph (4) must be provided; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) inform the relevant authority of the specified date; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) consider any representations made under paragraph (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) The relevant authority, having been informed of the specified date, must without delay inform the persons in paragraph (5)(a) and (c) to (f) of the specified date in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) A person mentioned in paragraph (5) may make written representations to the chair in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) whether, and (if so) the extent to which, the chair should exclude any person from the whole or part of the hearing under regulation 35(6)(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) whether the chair should impose any conditions under regulation 35(6)(b); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) whether the chair should require notice to be published under paragraph (1) and, if so, which information relating to the matters in paragraph (6) should be included in any such notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5) The persons are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the officer concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the relevant authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) the complainant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) any interested person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (e) any witness; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (f) if the case was investigated under external procedures, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^c-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or the Ombudsman. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(6) The matters referred to in paragraphs (1) and (4)(c) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the name of the officer concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the date of the hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) the time of the hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (d) the place at which the hearing will take place; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (e) the conduct that is the subject matter of the case and how that conduct is alleged to amount to misconduct or gross misconduct, as set out in the notice given in accordance with regulation 25(1)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(7) If the chair requires notice to be published in accordance with paragraph (1), the relevant authority must publish the notice on its website at least 5 working days before the day on which the hearing is to begin and, once published, it must remain there until the conclusion of the hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
