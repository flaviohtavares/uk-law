# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 36

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Exclusion from misconduct proceedings (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-009bc8cf3ad3f39f740bd41c35fb53cc][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This paragraph applies where it appears to the person conducting or chairing misconduct proceedings that any person may, in giving evidence, disclose information the disclosure of which should be prevented for any of the reasons set out in paragraphs (1)(a) to (g) of regulation 4 (the harm test). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) Where paragraph (1) applies, the person conducting or chairing misconduct proceedings must require any person to withdraw while the evidence is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
