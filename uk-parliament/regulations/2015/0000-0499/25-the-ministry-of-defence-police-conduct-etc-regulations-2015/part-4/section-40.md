# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc. ) Regulations 2015, section 40

> Text as at 2020-11-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Notification of outcome (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1e3dc7aee5cc50e360e3f15a978fe8a0][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3][^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The relevant authority must inform the officer concerned in writing of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the finding of the person or persons conducting the misconduct proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) the reasons for that finding; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) in a case other than one to which regulation 38 (senior officers: meeting of MOD Police Committee to consider disciplinary action) applies, any disciplinary action imposed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

as soon as reasonably practicable and in any event before the end of 5 working days beginning with the first working day after the conclusion of the misconduct proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(2) In a case to which regulation 38 applies, the relevant authority must inform the officer concerned in writing of any disciplinary action imposed and the reasons for that action, as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any event before the end of 5 working days beginning with the first working day after the conclusion of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] further meeting or further hearing[^c-0efe28181d42bad2a274de23aaa16ab2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] held under regulation 38(3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(3) Where there was a finding of misconduct or gross misconduct a written notice under paragraph (1) must include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) where the officer concerned is an officer other than a senior officer— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (i) if the case was decided at a misconduct meeting, notice of the officer’s right of appeal under regulation 42; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] if the case was decided at a misconduct hearing, notice of the officer’s right of appeal to a police appeals tribunal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) where the officer concerned is a senior officer, notice of the officer’s right of appeal to a police appeals tribunal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (c) the name and address of the person to whom an appeal should be sent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(4) The relevant authority must send a copy of any written notice under this regulation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

- (a) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General[^c-f49dab00943ed1a8f437ad856b2448a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or the Ombudsman, in any case in which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] Director General *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3] or the Ombudsman was entitled to attend to make representations under regulation 33(1) or (2); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
- (b) to the complainant and any interested person, in any case to which regulation 34 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]

(5)[^c-00214903d612d713b7436d83b2ab13f0] If the disciplinary action imposed as a result of the misconduct proceedings is dismissal (whether with or without notice), the relevant authority must send a copy of the written information provided to the officer concerned in accordance with paragraph (1) or (2) to the College of Policing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9405d597f6e4f834f9fa2291f22278c3]
