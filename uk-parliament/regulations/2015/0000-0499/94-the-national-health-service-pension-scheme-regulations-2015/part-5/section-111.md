> Text as at 2026-03-28.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 111. Reduction in pension debit member's benefits

*Extent: E+W*

The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under this Part are subject to reduction pursuant to section 31 of the 1999 Act.
