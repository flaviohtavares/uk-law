> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 75. Late payment of pension with actuarial increase

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where a member becomes entitled to the immediate payment of an age retirement pension on ceasing pensionable employment after reaching normal pension age.

(2) An increase amount (“the late payment actuarial increase”) must be calculated in accordance with paragraph 2 of Schedule 13.
