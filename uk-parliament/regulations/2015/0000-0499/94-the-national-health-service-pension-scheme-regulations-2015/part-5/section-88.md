# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2015, section 88

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 88. Payment of pension after abatement

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies if—

- (a) a member's (M) pension is abated pursuant to regulation 86(3); and
- (b) M's pensionable earnings are not reduced in consequence of M again becoming subject to a relevant reduction as mentioned in regulation 87(2).

(2) M's pension is payable by the scheme manager—

- (a) when M partially retires or retires again from pensionable employment; or
- (b) M attains the age of 75.

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2), the scheme manager must—

- (a) have regard to any pension already paid, including any lump sum paid as a result of M making an application under regulation 77 (conversion of part of pension into lump sum); and
- (b) obtain advice from the scheme actuary.
