# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2015, section 56

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Annual rate of additional (self only) pension

*Extent: E+W*

The annual rate of additional (self only) pension specified in an additional pension election must be a whole number multiple of—

- (a) £250; or
- (b) such other amount as is determined by the scheme manager.
