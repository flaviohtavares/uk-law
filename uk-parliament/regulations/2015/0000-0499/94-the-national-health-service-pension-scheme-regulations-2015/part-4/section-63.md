# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2015, section 63

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 63. Repayment of lump sum contribution

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies if a member (M) has paid a lump sum contribution for additional pension.

(2)[^c-a8ed062369f0f3a38758ab09b7610a56] If M dies before the end of the refund period—

- (a) the amount of the lump sum paid by M must be repaid to M’s personal[^c-d154db83528f69bfdd43b2cea0ac991b] representatives;
- (b) the amount of the lump sum paid by M’s employing authority pursuant to regulation 57 must be repaid to the authority; and
- (c) the additional[^c-20a41b87647f299dfb8e00a5bbf1a579] pension account must be closed.

(3) If an ill-health pension becomes payable to M in consequence of a claim made[^c-8dc3c0d57bb7ad0b5cff18f214e036b5] before the end of the refund period—

- (a) the amount of the lump sum paid by M must be repaid to M;
- (b) the amount of the lump sum paid by M’s employing authority pursuant to regulation 57 must be repaid to the authority; and
- (c) the additional[^c-7638a52a0be65ce90bcde716d370d20c] pension account must be closed.
