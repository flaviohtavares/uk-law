# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2015, section 58

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Making an additional pension election

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An additional pension election by a member (M) must be made by written notice to the scheme manager stating whether additional pension contributions are to be paid—

- (a) as a lump sum; or
- (b) by periodic payments.

(2) An additional pension election by M's employing authority must be made by written notice to the scheme manager.

(3) A notice under this regulation must be accompanied by a declaration by M that M is in good health.

(4) An additional pension election must not be made at any time when M is absent from work for any reason.

(5)[^c-29d372fb6701b7374fc029ff00b689e4] For the purposes of this Chapter—

- (a) a member is treated as having made an additional pension election on the date on which the employing authority receives the member’s notice under paragraph (1); and
- (b) an employing authority is treated as having made an additional pension election on the date on which the scheme manager receives the authority’s notice under paragraph (2).
