# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2015, section 140

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 140. Effect of transfer-out

*Extent: E+W*

If a transfer value payment is made under this Section in respect of a person's rights under the scheme, those rights are extinguished.
