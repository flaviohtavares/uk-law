# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2015, section 23

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Effect of break in pensionable service

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the following table a break in a period of pensionable service of a member (M) in circumstances set out in column 1 has the effect described in column 2.

Table

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><th><em>Column 1</em></th><th><em>Column 2</em></th></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2"><strong>Part 1</strong><br>M leaves active membership, is neither qualified for a retirement pension pursuant to regulation 72 nor is a pensioner member because of rights resulting from that membership, then returns to active service</td></tr>
<tr><td>1 The break—<br>(a) does not exceed one month; or<br>(b) is due to a trade dispute (within the meaning of section 35(1) of the Jobseekers Act 1995 ).<br>2 No transfer payment is made to another scheme in respect of pensionable service before the break.<br>3 It is immaterial if a refund of contributions is paid to M in respect of the period before the break.</td><td>The period of pensionable service before the break is treated as continuous with the period after the break.</td></tr>
<tr><td>1 The break does not exceed 5 years.<br>2 No transfer payment is made to another scheme in respect of M's pensionable service before the break.<br>3 If a refund of contributions in respect of the period before the break is paid to M, it is repaid before the end of the period of 6 months starting on the first day of pensionable service after the break.</td><td>The period of pensionable service before the break is treated as continuous with the period after the break.</td></tr>
<tr><td>1 The break exceeds 5 years.<br>2 M may claim a refund of contributions in respect of pensionable service before the break.</td><td>The period of pensionable service before the break is not treated as continuous with the period after the break and does not count towards qualifying service.</td></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2"><strong>Part 2</strong><br>M leaves active membership, is qualified for a retirement pension pursuant to regulation 72 but is not a pensioner member because of rights resulting from that membership, then returns to active service</td></tr>
<tr><td>1 The break does not exceed 5 years.<br>2 No transfer payment is made to another scheme in respect of M's pensionable service before the break.</td><td>The period of pensionable service before the break is treated as continuous with the period after the break.</td></tr>
<tr><td>1 The break exceeds 5 years.<br>2 No transfer payment is made to another scheme is respect of M's pensionable service before the break.</td><td>The period of pensionable service before the break is not treated as continuous with the period after the break, but does count as qualifying service.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

[^c22361361]

(2) In paragraph (1) a reference to the repayment of a refund of contributions includes the repayment of any interest on the contributions.
