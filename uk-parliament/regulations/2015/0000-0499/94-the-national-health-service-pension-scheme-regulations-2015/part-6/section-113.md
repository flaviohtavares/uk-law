# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2015, section 113

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 113. Surviving adult dependant pension

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If an active member, a deferred member or a pensioner member dies leaving a surviving adult dependant, the surviving adult dependant is entitled to a pension payable for life.

(2) In this Part, “**surviving adult dependant**” means, in relation to a deceased member or former member—

- (a) the surviving spouse;
- (b) the surviving civil partner; or
- (c)[^c-74ec6078ecdda1563531d9b3dfeb5044] a surviving scheme partner.

(3) For the rate at which the pension is payable, see regulations 115 to 119.
