# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2015, section 118

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 118. Recent leavers

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies if—

- (a) a recent leaver dies leaving a surviving spouse or civil partner who has a guaranteed minimum under section 17 of the 1993 Act [^c22361621] in relation to benefits in respect of the recent leaver under this scheme; and
- (b) the leaver has died before reaching the normal pension age.

(2) The surviving spouse or civil partner is entitled to a pension payable for life of an amount equal to the surviving spouse’s or surviving civil partner’s[^c-d37d5215f6a62778152ce74b932100d4] guaranteed minimum pension (disregarding any additional pension).

(3)[^c-a6caecd2de36f69c5318b53369e40ec8] Paragraph (2) does not apply if—

- (a) the Secretary of State’s liability to provide a guaranteed minimum pension in respect of the surviving spouse or civil partner is discharged by the payment of a contributions equivalent premium under section 55[^c-74d9bbc989cb64393a7ec353bac92c09] of the 1993 Act or article 3 of the 2016 Order[^c-2b4eb77174d8b2fed0de7a09b760657f], or
- (b) a surviving widow, widower or surviving civil partner has been convicted of an offence specified in paragraph 12 of Schedule 3 and the Secretary of State directed, as a consequence of that conviction, that the person’s rights to a payment in respect of the member’s death is forfeit.

(4) In this Part, “**recent leaver**” means a person—

- (a) who left pensionable service less than 12 months before the date of death;
- (b) who is neither qualified for a retirement pension pursuant to regulation 72 nor is a pensioner member because of rights resulting from that employment; and
- (c) in respect of whom no transfer value or refund of contributions has been paid in respect of that employment.
