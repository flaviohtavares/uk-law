# The National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2015, section 112

> Text as at 2026-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 112. Lump sum on death

*Extent: E+W*

A lump sum is payable in accordance with Schedule 14 in respect of the death of—

- (a) an active member;
- (b) a deferred member;
- (c) a pensioner member;
- (d) a recent leaver (within the meaning of that Schedule);
- (e) a re-employed pensioner (within the meaning of that Schedule);
- (f) a partial retirement pensioner (within the meaning of that Schedule); or
- (g) a pension credit member.
