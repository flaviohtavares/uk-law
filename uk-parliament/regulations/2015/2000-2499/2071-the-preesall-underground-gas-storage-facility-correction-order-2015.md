# The Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility (Correction) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 2071

Infrastructure Planning

The Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility (Correction) Order 2015

Made

21st December 2015

Coming into force

22nd December 2015

The Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility Order 2015[^f00001] (the “Order”), which granted development consent within the meaning of the Planning Act 2008[^f00002] (the “Act”), contained correctable errors within the meaning of the Act.

In accordance with paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to the Act, the Secretary of State received, before the end of the relevant period referred to in paragraph 1(6)(a) of that Schedule, a written request from Halite Energy Group Limited to correct errors and omissions in the Order. In accordance with paragraph 1(7) of that Schedule, the Secretary of State informed Lancashire County Council and Wyre Borough Council that the request had been received.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers in paragraph 1(4) and (8) of that Schedule, makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility (Correction) Order 2015 and comes into force on 22nd December 2015.

## 2. Corrections

The Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility Order 2015 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;
- (b) column 2 sets out provisions or text to be omitted; and
- (c) column 3 sets out any provisions or text to be substituted in its place.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy and Climate Change

Giles Scott

Head of National Infrastructure Consents and Coal Liabilities

Department of Energy and Climate Change

21st December 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order corrects errors and omissions in the Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility Order 2015, which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a request from Halite Energy Group Limited under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
