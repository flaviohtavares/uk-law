# The Port of London Authority (Constitution) Harbour Revision Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 2003

Harbours, Docks, Piers And Ferries

The Port of London Authority (Constitution) Harbour Revision Order 2015

Made

9th December 2015

Coming into force

31st December 2015

The Secretary of State is satisfied that a harbour revision order ought to be made for the purpose of achieving, in relation to the Port of London Authority, the objects mentioned in section 15(1) of the Harbours Act 1964 (“the Act”)[^f00001].

The Secretary of State, being the appropriate minister under section 15(3) of the Act and being satisfied as mentioned in section 14(2)(b) of the Act, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 15(1) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Port of London Authority (Constitution) Harbour Revision Order 2015 and comes into force on 31st December 2015.

## 2. Amendment of the Port of London Act 1968

Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Port of London Act 1968[^f00002] is amended as follows—

- (a) omit paragraph 7; and
- (b) omit paragraph 10.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Linda Willson

Director, Maritime Commerce and Industry

Department for Transport

9th December 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Port of London Act 1968 (c.xxxii) (“the Act”).

Paragraph 7 of Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Act prevents a person aged 64 or over from being appointed a member of the Port Authority. Paragraph 10 requires a member of the Port Authority to retire at the end of the calendar year in which they reach the age of 70. Both these provisions will be repealed by the Order.

A full Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument because it has no impact on the costs of business, charities or voluntary bodies.
