# The London Underground (Bank Station Capacity Upgrade) Order 2015, section 49

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 49. No double recovery[^key-059aedf261982bb8ce3c174697204989]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Compensation is not payable in respect of the same matter both under this Order and under any other enactment, any contract or any rule of law.
