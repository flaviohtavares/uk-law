# The London Underground (Bank Station Capacity Upgrade) Order 2015, section 47

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. Certification of plans etc.[^c-67eb9eb3766e7f0282a7ef5a81564a7a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Company must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the book of reference, the sections, the land plan and the works plans, and the access to works and permanent stopping up of streets plan to the Secretary of State for certification that they are, respectively, true copies of the book of reference, sections and plans referred to in this Order; and a document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
