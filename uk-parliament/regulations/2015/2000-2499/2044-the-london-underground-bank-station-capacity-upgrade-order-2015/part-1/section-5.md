# The London Underground (Bank Station Capacity Upgrade) Order 2015, section 5

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 5. Disapplication and modification of legislative provisions[^c-180e5f83ea5c644a47556a3299112d80]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 6 of the Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction Measure 1963[^f00024], section 7 of the Faculty Jurisdiction Measure 1964[^f00025] and Part 3 of the Care of Churches and Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction Measure 1991[^f00026] do not apply to anything done under the powers conferred by this Order in relation to the lands numbered 108, 109 and 110 shown on the land plan.
