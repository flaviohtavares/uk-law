# Trading with the Enemy (Enemy Territory Cessation) (Albania) Order 1948

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1948 No. 1177

TRADING WITH THE ENEMY

The Trading With the Enemy (Enemy the Trading With the Enemy Territory Cessation) (Albania) Order, 1948

Made

31st May 1948

The Board of Trade in pursuance of the powers conferred upon them by Section 1 of the Trading with the Enemy Act, 1939 (hereinafter called “**the Act**”), Regulation 6 of the Defence (Trading with the Enemy) Regulations, 1940[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby Order as follows:—

**1.** Albania shall for the purposes of Sections 3A 4, 5, and 7 of the Act and for the purposes of any Order made under Section 7 cease to be treated as if it were enemy territory as from the date of this Order.

**2.** Notwithstanding anything contained in Section 1 of the Act, any person may trade within Albania with any individual or body of persons (whether corporate or unincorporate) resident or carrying on business therein.

**3.** This Order may be cited as the Trading with the Enemy (Enemy Territory Cessation) (Albania) Order, 1948

H. S. Gregory

An Under-Secretary of the Board of Trade

Dated this 31st day of May, 1948

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The provisions of the Trading with the Enemy (Custodian) Order, 1939, which is an Order made under Section 7 of the Act, continue to apply to the property in the United Kingdom of persons in areas which have ceased to be in the occupation of the enemy until it is otherwise decided.

Paragraph 1of the present Order provides for the removal of this control in respect of the money and property of persons resident or carrying on business in Albania except of those who still remain within the definition of “enemy”under the Act, e.g. by reason of German incorporation.

Paragraph 2 of the present Order removes any restriction so far as Section 1 of the Trading with the Enemy Act, 1939, is concerned on trading within Albania with any person carrying on business therein.
