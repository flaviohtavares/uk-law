# The South of England Virtual Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 554

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The South of England Virtual Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2003

Made

3rd March 2003

Laid before Parliament

12th March2003

Coming into force

3rd April 2003

Whereas an application for the purpose of extending an Education Action Zone has been made to the Secretary of State with the consent of the governing bodies of every school participating therein.

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(2) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 [^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the South of England Virtual Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 3rd April 2003.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In this Order–

- “South of England Virtual Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the South of England Virtual Education Action Zone (No. 2) Order 2000 [^f00002].

## 3. The extension of the South of England Virtual Education Action Zone

The South of England Virtual Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 3<sup>rd</sup> April 2003.

David Miliband

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

3rd March 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the South of England Virtual Education Action Zone for a further period of two years.
