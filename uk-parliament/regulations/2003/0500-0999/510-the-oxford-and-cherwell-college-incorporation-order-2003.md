# The Oxford and Cherwell College (Incorporation) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 510

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Oxford and Cherwell College (Incorporation) Order 2003

Made

3rd March 2003

Laid before Parliament

10th March 2003

Coming into force

1st April 2003

Whereas the Secretary of State for Education and Skills has received a proposal from the Learning and Skills Council for England, made in accordance with section 51 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001] (“the Act”), for the establishment of a body corporate under section 16(1)(a) of the Act;

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16(1) and 17 of the Act and for the purpose of giving effect to that proposal, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Oxford and Cherwell College (Incorporation) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2003 (“the commencement date”).

**2.** There shall be established as from the commencement date a body corporate called “Oxford and Cherwell College”, for the purpose of establishing and conducting as from the operative date a new educational institution of that name.

**3.** The operative date in relation to the further education corporation established by this Order shall be 1<sup>st</sup> August 2003, and the corporation shall conduct Oxford and Cherwell College from that date.

Margaret Hodge

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

3rd March 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes a further education corporation called Oxford and Cherwell College under section 16(1) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 for the purpose of conducting the educational institution called by the same name. It appoints 1st August 2003 as the operative date for the purposes of Part 1 of that Act.
