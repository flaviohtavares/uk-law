# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2003, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. The Prescribed Sum for Jobseeker’s Allowance

In regulation 172 of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations[^f00066] (trade disputes: prescribed sum) for “£29.00” substitute “£29.50 ”.
