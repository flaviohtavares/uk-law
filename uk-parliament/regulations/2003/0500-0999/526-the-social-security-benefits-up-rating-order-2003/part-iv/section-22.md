# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2003, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Increase in age-related amounts of contribution-based Jobseeker’s Allowance

In regulation 79(1) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (weekly amounts of contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£32.50” substitute “£32.90 ”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£42.70” substitute “£43.25 ”; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) for “£53.95” substitute “£54.65 ”.
