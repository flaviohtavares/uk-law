# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2003, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Increase in rates of transitional invalidity allowance in long-term incapacity benefit cases

In regulation 18(2) of the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) (Transitional) Regulations 1995[^f00036] (rate of long-term incapacity benefit in transitional cases)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£14.90” substitute “£15.15 ”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£9.50” substitute “£9.70 ”; and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (c) for “£4.75” substitute “£4.85 ”.
