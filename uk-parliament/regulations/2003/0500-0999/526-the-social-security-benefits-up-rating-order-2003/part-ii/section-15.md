# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2003, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Increase in rates of age addition to long-term incapacity benefit

In regulation 10(2) of the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) Regulations 1994[^f00035] (increase in rate of incapacity benefit where beneficiary is under prescribed age on the qualifying date)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£14.90” substitute “£15.15 ”; and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£7.45” substitute “£7.60 ”.
