# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2003, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Increase in rates of Disability Living Allowance

In regulation 4 of the Social Security (Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 1991[^f00033] (rate of benefit)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a) for “£56.25” substitute “£57.20 ”;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(b) for “£37.65” substitute “£38.30 ”;
- (c) in paragraph (1)(c) for “£14.90” substitute “£15.15 ”;
- (d) in paragraph (2)(a) for “£39.30” substitute “£39.95 ”; and
- (e) in paragraph (2)(b) for “£14.90” substitute “£15.15 ”.
