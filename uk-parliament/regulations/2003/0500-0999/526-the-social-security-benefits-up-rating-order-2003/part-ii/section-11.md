# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2003, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Statutory Paternity Pay and Statutory Adoption Pay

In the Statutory Paternity Pay and Statutory Adoption Pay (Weekly Rates) Regulations 2002[^f00029]—

- (a) in regulation 2 (weekly rate of payment of statutory paternity pay)—
  - (i) in paragraph (1) “£75.00” remains unchanged[^f00030], and
  - (ii) in paragraph (2)(a) “£100.00” remains unchanged; an
- (b) in regulation 3(a) (weekly rate of payment of statutory adoption pay) “ £100.00” remains unchanged.

## Annotations

[^f00029]: Footnote: [S.I. 2002/2818](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2818).

[^f00030]: Footnote: The rate stated is the rate applicable where the paternity pay period begins before 6th April 2003.
