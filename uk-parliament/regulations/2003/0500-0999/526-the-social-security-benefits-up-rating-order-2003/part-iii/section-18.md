# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2003, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Income Support Transitional Protection

It is hereby directed[^f00042] that the sums which are special transitional additions to income support payable in accordance with regulation 15 of the Income Support (Transitional) Regulations 1987[^f00043] (special transitional addition) shall be increased by 1.3 per cent. of their amount apart from this Order.
