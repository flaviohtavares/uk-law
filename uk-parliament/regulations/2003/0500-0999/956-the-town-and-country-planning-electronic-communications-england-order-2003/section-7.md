# The Town and Country Planning (Electronic Communications) (England) Order 2003, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Use of electronic communications for appeals against listed building enforcement notices

In section 39 of the Listed Buildings Act[^f00008] (appeal against listed building enforcement notices), in subsection (2) repeal “either” and at the end add—

> ; or
>
> - (c) by sending such notice to him using electronic communications at such time that, in the ordinary course of transmission, it would be delivered to him before that date.

.

## Annotations

[^f00008]: Footnote: Section 39 was amended, and in part repealed, by the Planning and Compensation Act 1991, sections 25, 84(6), and Schedule 3.
