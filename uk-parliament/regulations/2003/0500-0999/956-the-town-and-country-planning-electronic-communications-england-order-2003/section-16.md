# The Town and Country Planning (Electronic Communications) (England) Order 2003, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Amendment of regulations and rules relating to enforcement appeals

(1) The Town and Country Planning (Enforcement Notices and Appeals) (England) Regulations 2002[^f00021] are amended in accordance with Schedule 9 to this Order.

(2) The Town and Country Planning (Enforcement) (Inquiries Procedure) (England) Rules 2002[^f00022] are amended in accordance with Schedule 10 to this Order.

(3) The Town and Country Planning (Enforcement) (Determination by Inspectors) (Inquiries Procedure) (England) Rules 2002[^f00023] are amended in accordance with Schedule 11 to this Order.

(4) The Town and Country Planning (Enforcement) (Hearings Procedure) (England) Rules 2002[^f00024] are amended in accordance with Schedule 12 to this Order.

(5) The Town and Country Planning (Enforcement) (Written Representations Procedure) (England) Regulations 2002[^f00025] are amended in accordance with Schedule 13 to this Order.
