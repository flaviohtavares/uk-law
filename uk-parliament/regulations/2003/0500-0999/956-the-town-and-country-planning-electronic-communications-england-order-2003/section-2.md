# The Town and Country Planning (Electronic Communications) (England) Order 2003, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the 1990 Act” means the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00002];
- “the Listed Buildings Act” means the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990[^f00003].
