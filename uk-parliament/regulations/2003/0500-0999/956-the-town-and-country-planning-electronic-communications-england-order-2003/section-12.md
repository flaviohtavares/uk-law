# The Town and Country Planning (Electronic Communications) (England) Order 2003, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Amendment of certain rules relating to planning inquiries

(1) The Town and Country Planning (Inquiries Procedure) (England) Rules 2000[^f00014] are amended in accordance with Schedule 2 to this Order.

(2) The Town and Country Planning Appeals (Determination by Inspectors) (Inquiries Procedure) (England) Rules 2000[^f00015] are amended in accordance with Schedule 3 to this Order.

(3) The Town and Country Planning (Hearings Procedure) (England) Rules 2000[^f00016] are amended in accordance with Schedule 4 to this Order.
