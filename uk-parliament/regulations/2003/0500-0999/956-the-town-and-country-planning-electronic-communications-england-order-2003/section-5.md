# The Town and Country Planning (Electronic Communications) (England) Order 2003, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Postal addresses to be provided in response to certain notices requiring information

In the following provisions of the 1990 Act—

- (a) paragraphs (c) and (e) of subsection (3) of section 171C[^f00007] (power to require information about activities on land), and
- (b) paragraphs (b) and (e) of subsection (2) of section 330 (power to require information as to interests in land),

after “name and”, insert “postal”.

## Annotations

[^f00007]: Footnote: Section 171C was inserted by the Planning and Compensation Act [1991 (c. 34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/34), section 1.
