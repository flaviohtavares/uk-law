# The Town and Country Planning (Electronic Communications) (England) Order 2003, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Use of electronic communications for appealing against enforcement notices

In section 174 of the 1990 Act[^f00004] (appeal against enforcement notice), in subsection (3) repeal “either” and at the end add

> ; or
>
> - (c) by sending such notice to him using electronic communications at such time that, in the ordinary course of transmission, it would be delivered to him before that date.

.
