# The Town and Country Planning (Electronic Communications) (England) Order 2003, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Amendment of order and regulations relating to planning applications

(1) The Town and Country Planning (Applications) Regulations 1988[^f00017] are amended in accordance with Schedule 5 to this Order.

(2) The Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) Order 1995[^f00018] is amended in accordance with Schedule 6 to this Order.

## Annotations

[^f00017]: Footnote: S.I. [1988/1812](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1812). By virtue of the Planning (Consequential Provisions) Act 1990, these regulations now have effect as if made under sections 62 and 333 of the 1990 Act.

[^f00018]: Footnote: S.I. [1995/418](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/418), to which there are amendments not relevant to this Order. Schedule 2 to the Order is amended by the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (Amendment) (England) Order 2001 (S.I.
