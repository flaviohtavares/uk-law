# The Education Act 2002 (Academies) (Consequential Amendments) (England) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 537

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education Act 2002 (Academies) (Consequential Amendments) (England) Regulations 2003

Made

3rd March 2003

Laid before Parliament

10th March 2003

Coming into force

1st April 2003

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 214(1) of the Education Act 2002 [^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education Act 2002 (Academies) (Consequential Amendments) (England) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2003.

## 2. Amendment to the Teacher Training Agency (Additional Functions) (No. 2) Order 2000

(1) The Teacher Training Agency (Additional Functions) (No. 2) Order 2000 [^f00002] is amended as follows —

(2) In article 4(d) for the words “city academy” shall be substituted “Academy”.

## 3. Amendment to the Education (Special Educational Needs) (England) (Consolidation) Regulations 2001

(1) The Education (Special Educational Needs) (England) (Consolidation) Regulations 2001 [^f00003] are amended as follows —

(2) in regulation 18(2)(e) for the words “a City Academy” shall be substituted “an Academy”.

## 4. Amendment to the Education (School Performance Information) (England) Regulations 2001

(1) The Education (School Performance Information) (England) Regulations 2001 [^f00004] are amended as follows —

(2) in regulation 2(1) in the definition of “CTC”, the words “or a city academy” shall be omitted.

David Miliband

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

3rd March 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These regulations amend three sets of regulations which refer to city academies. Section 67 of the Education Act 2002 provides that schools which had, prior to the commencement of that section, been known as city academies, will now be known as Academies. As a consequence of “city academies” becoming “Academies” three sets of regulations have been amended to refer to “Academies” rather than “city academies”. The four sets of regulations amended by these regulations are:

The Teacher Training Agency (Additional Functions) (No. 2) Order 2000;

The Education (Special Educational Needs) (England) (Consolidation) Regulations 2001; and

The Education (School Performance Information) (England) Regulations 2001.
