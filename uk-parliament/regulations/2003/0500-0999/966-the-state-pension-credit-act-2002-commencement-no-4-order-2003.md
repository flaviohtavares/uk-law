# The State Pension Credit Act 2002 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been reprinted to correct an error in the layout of article 2 and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients.

2003 No. 966 (C.52 )

SOCIAL SECURITY

The State Pension Credit Act 2002 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2003

Made

1st April 2003

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 22(3) of the State Pension Credit Act 2002[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the State Pension Credit Act 2002 (Commencement No.4) Order 2003.

(2) In this Order, references to sections and Schedules are, unless the context otherwise requires, to sections of, and Schedules to, the State Pension Credit Act 2002.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of—

- (a) section 11 (in so far as it is not already in force) in relation to paragraphs 1 to 7, 10 and 12 of Schedule 1; and
- (b) section 21 and Schedule 3 to the extent that they repeal the provisions specified in the Schedule to this Order,

is 7<sup>th</sup> April 2003.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Ian McCartney

Minister of State,

Department for Work and Pensions

1st April 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 7<sup>th</sup> April 2003 as the day for the coming into force of section 11 of the State Pension Credit Act 2002 (in so far as it is not already in force) in relation to paragraphs 1 to 7, 10 and 12 of Schedule 1 to that Act (amendments to the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5) and to the Social Security Act [1998 (c. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/14) (“the 1998 Act”) which make provision for claims for, and decisions relating to, state pension credit). It also appoints that day for the coming into force of section 21 of, and Schedule 3 to, that Act in so far as it relates to certain repeals which are consequential on the coming into force of the amendments to the 1998 Act.

This Order does not impose any costs on business.

(This note is not part of the Order)
