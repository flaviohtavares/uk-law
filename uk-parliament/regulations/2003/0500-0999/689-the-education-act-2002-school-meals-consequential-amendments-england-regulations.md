# The Education Act 2002 (School Meals) (Consequential Amendments) (England) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 689

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education Act 2002 (School Meals) (Consequential Amendments) (England) Regulations 2003

Made

12th March 2003

Laid before Parliament

18th March 2003

Coming into force

11th April 2003

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 214 of the Education Act 2002[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education Act 2002 (School Meals) (Consequential Amendments) (England) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 11<sup>th</sup> April 2003.

## 2. Amendment of the Education (Transfer of Functions Concerning School Lunches etc) (England) (No. 2) Order 1999

The Education (Transfer of Functions Concerning School Lunches etc) (England) (No. 2) Order 1999[^f00002] is amended as follows –

- (a) in article 2(2)(a), for “section 512(1A) and (1B)” there is substituted “section 512(3) and (4)”;
- (b) in article 2(2)(b), for “section 512(3)” there is substituted “section 512ZB(1)”;
- (c) article 2(3) is revoked;
- (d) in article 3(1), for “section 512(3)(b)” there is substituted “section 512ZB(3)”; and
- (e) in article 4(1) and (2), for “section 512(2)(b)” there is substituted “section 512ZA(2)”.

## 3. Amendment of the Education (Information About Individual Pupils) (England) Regulations 2001

In paragraph 6 of Part 1 of the Schedule to the Education (Information About Individual Pupils) (England) Regulations 2001[^f00003], for “section 512(3) and (3A)” there is substituted “sections 512(3) and 512ZB”.

David Miliband

Minister of State

Department for Education and Skills

12th March 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make consequential amendments in connection with the bringing into force on 6<sup>th</sup> April 2003 of section 201 of the Education Act 2002, concerning the provision of school meals.

Regulation 2 makes amendments to the Education (Transfer of Functions Concerning School Lunches etc) (England) (No. 2) Order 1999.

Regulation 3 makes amendments to the Education (Information About Individual Pupils) (England) Regulations 2001.
