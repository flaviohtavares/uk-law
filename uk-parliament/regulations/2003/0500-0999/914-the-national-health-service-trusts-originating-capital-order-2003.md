# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 914

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2003

Made

26th March 2003

Coming into force

31st March 2003

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 9(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement, interpretation and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 31st March 2003.

(2) In this Order “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order.

(3) This Order applies to England only.

## 2. Originating capital of NHS trusts

The originating capital of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule shall be of the amount specified opposite it in column 2 of the Schedule.

Alan Milburn

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Department of Health

24th March 2003

We consent,

Nick Ainger

John Heppell

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

26th March 2003
