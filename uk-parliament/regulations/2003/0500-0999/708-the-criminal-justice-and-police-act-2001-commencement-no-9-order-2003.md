# The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 708 (C.34)

CRIMINAL LAW

POLICE

The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2003

Made

13th March 2003

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 138(2) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2003.

**2.** The following provisions of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 shall come into force on 1st April 2003—

- (a) sections 50 to 63;
- (b) section 64(1) save for the words “subject to subsection (2),”;
- (c) sections 65 to 70;
- (d) section 73;
- (e) section 74;
- (f) section 78(2);
- (g) section 79;
- (h) section 80(1);
- (i) section 137 in so far as it relates to the entries in Schedule 7 referred to in paragraph (m) below;
- (j) Schedule 1 save for paragraphs 35, 42 and 67;
- (k) Schedule 2 to the extent not already in force;
- (l) Schedule 5; and
- (m) Schedule 7, Part 2 to the extent not already in force.

John Denham

Minister of State

Home Office

13th March 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 2003 the provisions set out in article 2.
