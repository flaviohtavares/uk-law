# The Wireless Telegraphy (Public Fixed Wireless Access Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2002/1911](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1911) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2003 No. 397

TELEGRAPHS

The Wireless Telegraphy (Public Fixed Wireless Access Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 2003

Made

25th February 2003

Laid before Parliament

26th February 2003

Coming into force

27th February 2003

Whereas the Secretary of State published in the London, Edinburgh and Belfast Gazettes the notice required by section 6(2) of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1998 (“the 1998 Act”)[^f00001], in respect of the Wireless Telegraphy (Public Fixed Wireless Access Licences) Regulations 2002 (“the 2002 Regulations”)[^f00002] and no representations were made to her within the time specified in that notice;

And whereas the following regulations only amend a defect in the 2002 Regulations and do not alter the general effect set out in the said notice;

Therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 3 and 6 of the 1998 Act[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Public Fixed Wireless Access Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 27th February 2003.

## 2. Amendment

In Part 1 of the Schedule to the 2002 Regulations the sector “GU24” shall be omitted from the second column in the row entitled “Southern”.

Stephen Timms,

Minister of State for E-Commerce and Competitiveness,

Department of Trade and Industry

25th February 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations remove a defect in the Schedule to the Wireless Telegraphy (Public Fixed Wireless Access Licences) Regulations 2002, namely the inclusion of “GU24” in the row entitled “Southern”.
