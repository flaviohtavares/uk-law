# The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (England) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 28

ANIMALS, ENGLAND

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (England) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2003

Made

9th January 2003

Coming into force

31st January 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by sections 1 and 8(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] makes the following Order:

## 1. Title, extent and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (England) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2003; it extends to England only and comes into force on 31st January 2003.

## 2. Amendment of Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) England (No. 2) Order 2002

In the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) England (No. 2) Order 2002[^f00002], in article 1(2), for “1st February 2003” there shall be substituted “1st November 2003”.

Whitty

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department of Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

9th January 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which applies to England only, amends the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (England) (No. 2) Order 2002 (S.I. [2002/2154](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2154)), (“the principal Order”).

The only change made to the principal Order by this Order is to amend the date that the principal Order ceases to have effect (from 1st February 2003 to 1st November 2003) (article 2).

A regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this Order.
