# The Commission for Patient and Public Involvement in Health (Functions) Amendment Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct errors in S.I. [2002/3007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/3007) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2003 No. 497

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Commission for Patient and Public Involvement in Health (Functions) Amendment Regulations 2003

Made

4th March 2003

Laid before Parliament

7th March 2003

Coming into force

28th March 2003

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 20(2)(b) and (c), 20(9) and (10) and 38(5) to (7) of the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement, application and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Commission for Patient and Public Involvement in Health (Functions) Amendment Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 28th March 2003.

(2) These Regulations apply to England only.

(3) In these Regulations—

- “the principal Regulations” means the Commission for Patient and Public Involvement in Health (Functions) Regulations 2002[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of regulation 4 of the principal Regulations

In regulation 4 of the principal Regulations, in paragraph (d), after “Commissioner” insert “for England”.

## 3. Amendment of regulation 5 of the principal Regulations

In regulation 5 of the principal Regulations, in paragraph (d), for “Commissioners” substitute “Commissioner for England”.

## 4. Amendment of regulation 8 of the principal Regulations

In regulation 8 of the principal Regulations, in paragraph (1), for “regulation 8” substitute “regulation 9”.

## 5. Amendment of regulation 9 of the principal Regulations

In regulation 9 of the principal Regulations, in paragraph (1), for “regulation 7(1)” substitute “regulation 8(1)”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

David Lammy

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Health

Department of Health

4th March 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend The Commission for Patient and Public Involvement in Health (Functions) Regulations 2002 to correct errors in regulations 4(d), 5(d), 8(1) and 9(1) of those Regulations.
