# Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 385

MAGISTRATES' COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations 2003

Made

24th February 2003

Laid before Parliament

26th February 2003

Coming into force

1st April 2003

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 30B(1) of the Justices of the Peace Act 1997[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

## 2. Amendment to the Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Constitution) Regulations 1999[^f00002]

In regulation 15(3)(b) of the Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Constitution) Regulations 1999 for “£750” substitute “£812.50”.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Yvette Cooper

Parliamentary Secretary

Lord Chancellor’s Department

24th February 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority (Constitution) Regulations 1999 to increase the payment rate made to those who are members of the Greater London Magistrates' Courts Authority.

The payment rate has increased by £62.50 from £750 to £812.50 per quarter and takes effect from 1st April 2003.
