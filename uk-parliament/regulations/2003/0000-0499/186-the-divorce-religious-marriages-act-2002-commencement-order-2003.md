# The Divorce (Religious Marriages) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 186 (C. 10)

MATRIMONIAL CAUSES, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Divorce (Religious Marriages) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2003

Made

30th January 2003

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2(2) of the Divorce (Religious Marriages) Act 2002[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Divorce (Religious Marriages) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2003.

**2.** The Divorce (Religious Marriages) Act 2002 shall come into force on 24th February 2003.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Rosie Winterton

Parliamentary Secretary

Lord Chancellor’s Department

Dated 30th January 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Divorce (Religious Marriages) Act 2002 into force on 24th February 2003.
