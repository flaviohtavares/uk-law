# The Health and Social Care Act 2001 (Commencement No. 11) (England) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 53 (C. 3)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Health and Social Care Act 2001 (Commencement No. 11) (England) Order 2002

Made

30th December 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 64(6) and 70(2) of the Health and Social Care Act 2001[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, interpretation and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Care Act 2001 (Commencement No. 11) (England) Order 2002.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Health and Social Care Act 2001.

(3) This Order applies to England only.

## 2. Appointed day for provisions relating to local pharmaceutical services

1st January 2003 is the day appointed for the coming into force of—

- (a) paragraphs 1, 4, 5(17), 7, 9[^f00002], 11(2)(a) and 14 of Schedule 5 to the Act;
- (b) section 67(1) of the Act, in so far as it relates to those paragraphs; and
- (c) paragraphs 5(1) and 11(1) of Schedule 5 to the Act, in so far as they relate to paragraphs 5(17) and 11(2)(a) respectively of that Schedule.

## 3. Appointed day for provisions relating to local authority scrutiny of health service provision and public involvement and consultation

1st January 2003 is the day appointed for the coming into force of—

- (a) sections 7 to 11[^f00003] of the Act;
- (b) Schedule 1[^f00004] to the Act; and
- (c) paragraph 13 of Schedule 5 to the Act, and section 67(1) of the Act in so far as it relates to that paragraph.

## 4. Appointed day for provisions relating to pharmaceutical services

1st February 2003 is the day appointed for the coming into force of—

- (a) subsection (6) of section 20[^f00005] of the Act, and subsection (1) of that section in so far as it relates to subsection (6);
- (b) section 25 of the Act, in so far as that section is not already in force[^f00006];
- (c) section 67(2) of the Act, in so far as it relates to the entry in Schedule 6 to the Act repealing the “and” at the end of section 42(3)(d) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00007]; and
- (d) sub-paragraph (14) of paragraph 5 of Schedule 5 to the Act, in so far as that sub-paragraph is not already in force[^f00008], and section 67(1) of, and paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 5 to, the Act, in so far as they relate to that sub-paragraph.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

David Lammy

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Health

Department of Health

30th December 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the 11th Commencement Order made under the Health and Social Care Act 2001 (“the Act”), and applies to England only.

Article 2 brings into force, on 1st January 2003, certain provisions in connection with the introduction of pilot schemes for the provision of local pharmaceutical services under section 28 of the Act.

Article 3 brings into force, on 1st January 2003, sections 7 to 11 of the Act (overview and scrutiny committees, and public involvement and consultation), Schedule 1 to the Act (exempt information relating to health services), and paragraph 13 of Schedule 5 to the Act, which relates to the power to make regulations in respect of review and scrutiny committees.

Article 4 brings into force, on 1st February 2003, section 20(6) of the Act, which amends section 42 of the National Health Service Act 1977 concerning the provision of pharmaceutical services under Part II of that Act. It also brings fully into force section 25 of the Act, which had not hitherto been brought into force in relation to the provision of pharmaceutical services. Finally it brings into force as respects pharmaceutical services paragraph 5(14) of Schedule 5 to the Act.
