# The Payments to the Churches Conservation Trust Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 472

ECCLESIASTICAL LAW, ENGLAND

The Payments to the Churches Conservation Trust Order 2003

Made (Approved by the General Synod)

26th February 2003

Laid before Parliament

10th March 2003

Coming into force

1st April 2003

We, the Church Commissioners for England, in exercise of the powers conferred upon us by section 53(1) of the Pastoral Measure 1983 [^f00001] hereby make the following Order:

**1.** The amount to be paid to the Churches Conservation Trust (“the Trust”) by the Church Commissioners during the three year period 1<sup>st</sup> April 2003 to 31<sup>st</sup> March 2006, hereby determined as the funding period for the purposes of Part III of the 1983 Measure, shall not exceed £3,857,142.

**2.** The maximum amount of the payments which may be made to the Trust under section 52(1)(a) of the 1983 Measure out of the one-third of the net proceeds of sale and premiums shall not exceed £300,000 and such payments shall be applied towards the £3,857,142 referred to in paragraph 1.

**3.** Subject to paragraph 4 hereof, the balance of the said amount of £3,857,142 shall be paid in such amounts as the Church Commissioners shall determine, either under section 53(1)(c) of the 1983 Measure from any remaining balance of the said one-third of the net proceeds of sale and premiums, or by way of grants made to the Trust by the Church Commissioners under section 44(10) of the 1983 Measure out of their General Fund.

**4.** A payment on account of the said £3,857,142 shall be made to the Trust only when the Church Commissioners have determined that the payment is an appropriate proportion of a specific sum of money needed by the Trust and are satisfied the balance of that sum will be paid to the Trust out of moneys provided by Parliament.

**5.** This Order may be cited as The Payments to the Churches Conservation Trust Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> April 2003.

The COMMON SEAL of the CHURCH COMMISSIONERS was hereunto affixed this 23rd day of January Two thousand and three

Howell Harris Hughes

Secretary

APPROVED by the General Synod of the Church of England the 26th day of February 2003

William Fittall

Secretary General

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that for the period 1<sup>st</sup> April 2003 to 31<sup>st</sup> March 2006 sums up to a total of £3,857,142 may be paid to the Churches Conservation Trust, either out of the net proceeds of sale of redundant churches or by way of grant from the Church Commissioners.
