# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 2003, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and revocation

(1) This Order may be cited as the National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 2003 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(2) This Order revokes the National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 1999[^f00003] and the National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) (Amendment) Order 2002 [^f00004].
