# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 2003, section 143

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 143. Sending of applications and notices

Any application or notice which is required by this Order to be made to a registration officer or a returning officer shall be made in writing and sent by post or delivered to his office or to the address specified by him for the purpose.
