# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 2003, section 138

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 138. Advertisements

The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisement) Regulations 1992[^f00038] shall have effect in relation to the display on any site in Wales relating specifically to an Assembly election or Assembly elections as they have effect in relation to the display of an advertisement relating specifically to a parliamentary election.
