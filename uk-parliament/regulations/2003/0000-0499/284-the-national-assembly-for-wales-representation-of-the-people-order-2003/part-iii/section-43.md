# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 2003, section 43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 43. Expenses incurred otherwise than for election purposes

(1) Articles 41, 47 and 48 shall not apply to election expenses —

- (a) which are incurred by or on behalf of the candidate otherwise than for the purposes of the candidate’s election, but
- (b) which by virtue of article 61(1) fall to be regarded as election expenses by reason of the property, services or facilities in respect of which they are incurred being used for the purposes of the candidate’s election.

(2) The candidate’s election agent shall make a declaration of the amount (determined in accordance with article 62) of any election expenses falling within paragraph (1).

(3) In this article “for the purposes of the candidate’s election” has the same meaning as in articles 61 to 63.
