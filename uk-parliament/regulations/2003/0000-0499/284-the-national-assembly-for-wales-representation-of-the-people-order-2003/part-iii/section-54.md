# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 2003, section 54

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 54. Penalty for failure as respects return of declarations

Subject to the provisions of article 56, if any candidate or election agent fails to comply with the requirements of article 50 or 51 he shall be guilty of an illegal practice.
