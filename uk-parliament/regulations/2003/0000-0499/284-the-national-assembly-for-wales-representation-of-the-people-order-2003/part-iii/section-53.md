# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 2003, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Circumstances in which no return or declaration is required

Notwithstanding anything in article 50 or 51, no return or declaration as to election expenses shall be required in the case of a person—

- (a) who is a candidate at an Assembly election, but is so only because he has been declared by others to be a candidate, and
- (b) who has not consented to the declaration or taken any part as a candidate at the election.
