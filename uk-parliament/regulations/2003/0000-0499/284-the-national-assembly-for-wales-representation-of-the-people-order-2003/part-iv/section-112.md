# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 2003, section 112

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 112. Justice of the peace

Where a justice of the peace is reported by an election court to have been guilty of any corrupt practice in relation to an Assembly election the court shall report the case to the Lord Chancellor with such evidence as may have been given of the corrupt practice.
