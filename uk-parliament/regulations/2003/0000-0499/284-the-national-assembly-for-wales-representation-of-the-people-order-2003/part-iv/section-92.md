# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 2003, section 92

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 92. Petition at issue

(1) The Assembly election petition shall be at issue as from the relevant time, as defined by paragraph (2).

(2) In this article “the relevant time” means—

- (a) where the petitioner gives the security for costs required by article 91 by a deposit of money equal to the amount of the security required, the time when the security is given; and
- (b) in any other case, the time when—
  - (i) the time prescribed for the making of objections under article 91(4) expires, or
  - (ii) if such an objection is made, that objection is disallowed or removed,

  whichever happens later.
