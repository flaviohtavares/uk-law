# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 2003, section 97

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 97. Expenses of witnesses

(1) The reasonable expenses incurred by any person in appearing to give evidence at the trial of an Assembly election petition, according to the scale allowed to witnesses on the trial of civil actions, may be allowed to him by a certificate of the election court or of the prescribed officer.

(2) If the witness was called and examined by virtue of article 95(2), the expenses referred to in paragraph (1) shall be deemed part of the expenses of providing a court, but otherwise they shall be deemed costs of the petition.
