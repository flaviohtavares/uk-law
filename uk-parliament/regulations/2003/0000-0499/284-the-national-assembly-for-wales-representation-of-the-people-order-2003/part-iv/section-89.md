# The National Assembly for Wales (Representation of the People) Order 2003, section 89

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 89. Judges' expenses and reception

In relation to the trial of an Assembly election petition, the travelling and other expenses of the judges and all expenses properly incurred in providing them with necessary accommodation and with a proper court shall be defrayed by the Secretary of State out of money provided by Parliament.
