# Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations Order of Council 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 219

VETERINARY SURGEONS

Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations Order of Council 2003

Made

17th January 2003

Coming into force

1st April 2003

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 17th January 2003

By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

The Council of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons having made the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners Registration (Amendment) Regulations 2002 set out in the Schedule to this Order under section 11 of the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966[^f00001] and such Regulations not having effect unless approved by the Privy Council under section 25(1) of the Act, Their Lordships, having considered the Regulations, order as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations Order of Council 2003 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

**2.** The Regulations set out in the Schedule are approved.

**3.** The Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations Order of Council 2001[^f00002] is revoked.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE — THE VETERINARY SURGEONS AND VETERINARY PRACTITIONERS REGISTRATION (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2002

The Council of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons in exercise of its powers under section 11 of the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966, hereby make the following Regulations.

These Regulations may be cited as the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners Registration (Amendment) Regulations 2002.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Regulations approved by this Order amend the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners Registration (Amendment) Regulations 2001. They increase, from 1st April 2003, the fees payable for the registration and annual retention of names on the Registers of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons.
