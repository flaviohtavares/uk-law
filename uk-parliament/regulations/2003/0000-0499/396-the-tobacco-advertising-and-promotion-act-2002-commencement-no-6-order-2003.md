# The Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 396 (C. 26)

CONSUMER PROTECTION

The Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2003

Made

25th February 2003

The Secretary of State for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 22(1) of the Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002[^f00001] and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation, interpretation and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002 (Commencement No.6) Order 2003.

(2) In this Order “section” means a section of the Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002.

(3) This Order applies to England, Wales and Northern Ireland only.

## 2. Appointed day

26th February 2003 is the appointed day for the coming into force of—

- (a) section 4(3) and (4),
- (b) section 11(1) to (3), and
- (c) section 20

so far as not already in force.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hazel Blears

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

25th February 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 4(3) and (4), 11(1) to (3) and 20 of the Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002 so far as not already in force.

This Order does not apply to Scotland.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The following provisions of the Act have been or will be brought into force by a commencement order made before the date of this Order.
