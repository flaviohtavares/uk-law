# The Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 51

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 2003

Made

8th January 2003

Laid before Parliament

21st January 2003

Coming into force

15th February 2003

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 50 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 15<sup>th</sup> February 2003.

## 2. Revocation

The Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) Regulations 1995[^f00002], the Education (Further Education Institutions Information (England) (Amendment) Regulations 1997[^f00003], the Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 1998[^f00004] and the Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 1999[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

Ivan Lewis

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Education and Skills

8th January 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) Regulations 1995 as amended.
