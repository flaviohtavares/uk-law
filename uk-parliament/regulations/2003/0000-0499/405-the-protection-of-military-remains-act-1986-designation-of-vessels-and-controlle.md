# The Protection of Military Remains Act 1986 (Designation of Vessels and Controlled Sites) (Amendment) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed in consequence of an error in [SI2002/1761](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1761) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients

2003 No.405

DEFENCE

The Protection of Military Remains Act 1986 (Designation of Vessels and Controlled Sites) (Amendment) Order 2003

Made

26th February 2003

Coming into force

21st March 2003

The Secretary of State for Defence, (“the Secretary of State”), in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 1(2) of the Protection of Military Remains Act 1986[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement, and interpretation

(1) This order may be cited as the Protection of Military Remains Act 1986 (Designation of Vessels and Controlled Sites) (Amendment) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 21<sup>st</sup> March 2003.

(2) In this Order any reference to a point is a reference to a point given by co-ordinates based on the World Geodetic System 1984.

## 2. Amendment of the Protection of Military Remains Act 1986 (Designation of Vessels and Controlled Sites) Order 2002

Article 3(c)(ii) of the Protection of Military Remains Act 1986 (Designation of Vessels and Controlled Sites) Order 2002 [^f00002] shall be amended by substituting the word “East”, which appears at the end of the co-ordinates, for the word “West”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Defence

Dr Lewis Moonie

Date 26th February 2003

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Defence

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects an error in the Protection of Military Remains Act 1986 (Designation of Vessels and Controlled Sites) Order 2002, article 3(c)(ii) of which gives the wrong co-ordinates for an area that it designates as a controlled site. Article 3(c)(ii) gives the co-ordinates of the area to be designated as lying at longitude East. This Order corrects that co-ordinate to show that the area designated as a controlled site lies at longitude West.

This Order is being made available free of charge to all those who can show that they have already bought or who subsequently buy the Protection of Military Remains Act 1986 (Designation of Vessels and Controlled Sites) Order 2002.

## Annotations

[^f00001]: Footnote: [1986 c. 35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/35). The power to designate any land as, or as part of, a controlled site is exercisable in relation to Crown land as it is exercisable in relation to other land: see section 1(7). The terms “controlled site” and “remains” are defined in section 9(1). Section 9(2) states when a vessel is to be regarded as having been in military service.
