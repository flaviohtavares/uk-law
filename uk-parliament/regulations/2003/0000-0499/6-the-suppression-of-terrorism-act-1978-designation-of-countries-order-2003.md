# The Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Designation of Countries) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 6

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF TERRORISM

The Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Designation of Countries) Order 2003

Made

7th January 2003

Laid before Parliament

9th January 2003

Coming into force

10th January 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by the definition of “convention country” in section 8(1) of the Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Designation of Countries) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 10th January 2003.

**2.** (1) The countries set out in paragraph (2) are hereby designated as parties to the European Convention on the Suppression of Terrorism signed at Strasbourg on 27th January 1977.

(2) Those countries are—

- Albania;
- Bulgaria;
- Estonia;
- Georgia;
- Hungary;
- Latvia;
- Lithuania;
- Malta;
- Moldova;
- Poland;
- Romania;
- Russian Federation;
- San Marino;
- Slovenia;
- Ukraine.

Bob Ainsworth

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

7th January 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the countries listed in article 2(2) as countries which are for the time being parties to the Convention referred to in article 2(1).
