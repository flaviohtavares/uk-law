# The Local Authorities (Goods and Services) (Public Bodies) (England) (No. 2) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1018

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND

The Local Authorities (Goods and Services) (Public Bodies) (England) (No. 2) Order 2003

Made

7th April 2003

Laid before Parliament

7th April 2003

Coming into force

28th April 2003

The First Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 1(5) and (6) of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) (Public Bodies) (England) (No. 2) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 28th April 2003.

## 2. Designation as a public body

East Leake Schools Limited[^f00002], being a person appearing to the Secretary of State to be exercising a function of a public nature, shall be a public body for the purposes of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970, in its application to England[^f00003].

## 3. Restrictions on agreements—East Leake Schools Limited

Agreements which may be entered into by East Leake Schools Limited by virtue of this Order shall be subject to the following restrictions—

- (a) that any such agreements are for the provision of catering, cleaning or grounds maintenance services by Nottinghamshire County Council; and
- (b) that any such agreements are made in connection with the provision of services in the area of Nottinghamshire County Council.

Signed by authority of the First Secretary of State

Christopher Leslie

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Office of the Deputy Prime Minister

7th April 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order designates East Leake Schools Limited as a public body for the purposes of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970 (“the 1970 Act”).

The effect of the designation is to allow Nottinghamshire County Council to provide catering, cleaning or grounds maintenance services to East Leake Schools Limited by way of an agreement for any purposes prescribed by section 1(1) of the 1970 Act.

Article 3 places restrictions on the agreements that may be entered into.
