# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1256

SPORTS GROUNDS AND SPORTING EVENTS

The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2003

Made

29th April 2003

Laid before Parliament

8th May 2003

Coming into force

31st May 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 1(1) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00001], and after such consultation as is mentioned in section 18(4) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 31st May 2003.

## 2. Designation

The sports grounds specified in the Schedule to this Order (being sports grounds in England at which association football matches are played, which are occupied by clubs which are members of the Football League Limited or the Football Association Premier League Limited, and which in the opinion of the Secretary of State have accommodation for more than 5,000 spectators) are hereby each designated as a sports ground requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00002].

Tessa Jowell

Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport

29th April 2003

This Order designates the Neasham Road stadium in Darlington and the National Hockey Stadium and Conference Centre in Central Milton Keynes as sports grounds requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Ground Act 1975.
