# The Education Act 2002 (Commencement No.5 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No.1115 (C.56)

EDUCATION, ENGLAND

The Education Act 2002 (Commencement No.5 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2003

Made

14th April 2003

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 216(2), (4) and (5) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Education Act 2002 (Commencement No. 5 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2003.

(2) In this Order—

- “the 1998 Act” means the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 [^f00002],
- “the 2002 Act” means the Education Act 2002.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1<sup>st</sup> June 2003

The following provisions of the 2002 Act shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> June 2003:

- section 70;
- section 71;
- section 73;
- Schedule 8.

**3.** The following provisions of the 2002 Act shall come into force except in relation to Wales on 1<sup>st</sup> June 2003:

- section 74(1);
- section 75 to the extent that it is not already in force;
- sections 142 to 144;
- section 146 to the extent that it repeals section 218(6), (6ZA), (6A), (6B) and section 218A of the Education Reform Act 1988;
- section 154(1) and (2);
- Schedule 10 to the extent that it is not already in force;
- section 215 to the extent that it relates to the provisions of Schedules 21 and 22 specified below;
- in Schedule 21–
  - paragraph 9,

  - in the Education Reform Act 1998, section 218(6), (6ZA), (6A), (6B) and section 218A,
  - in the Education Act 1997, section 49,
  - in the Police Act, in section 113, in subsection (3A), paragraph (a)(ii) and (iii), and in subsection (3B), paragraph (c) and the words from “and the reference “ to the end, and section 115(6A),(a)(ii) and (iii),
  - in the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998, in Schedule 2, paragraph 1(5),
  - in the School Standards and Framework Act, in Schedule 6 –
    - in paragraph 3(2), the word “or” at the end at the end of paragraph (b),

    - section 5,

    - in section 1292) the definition of “the 1998 Act”,
  - in the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000, section 35(5), and in Schedule 7, paragraph 83.

## 4. Transitional and saving provisions

(1) Despite the coming into force of section 154(1) and (2) of the 2002 Act, where a local education authority in England has before 1<sup>st</sup> June 2003 taken a decision to establish a new maintained nursery school, section 28(1)(aa) of the 1998 Act shall not apply to the proposal.

(2) An authority’s decision to establish a maintained nursery school shall only be treated as such if it conforms with its arrangements for the discharge of its functions in accordance with its executive arrangements as defined by section 10(1) of the Local Government Act 2000[^f00003].

**5.** Despite the coming into force of paragraphs 2, 3(2), 4(2), 8 and 10(b) of Schedule 10 to the 2002 Act, the amendments to paragraphs 2, 3(1)(a)(i) and 4(1)(b) of Schedule 6 to, and paragraphs 7 and 9(1)(c) of Schedule 7 to, the 1998 Act shall not have effect in relation to any proposals published before 1<sup>st</sup> June 2003.

**6.** Despite the coming into force of paragraph 97 of Schedule 21 to the 2002 Act, where a local education authority has before 1<sup>st</sup> June 2003 commenced consultation, in accordance with section 28(5) of the 1998 Act, on the establishment of a community or foundation school which is an additional school within the meaning of section 70 of the 2002 Act, section 28(1)(a) of the 1998 Act shall apply to the proposal.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force provisions of the Education Act 2002 on 1<sup>st</sup> June 2003.

Article 2 commences sections 70, 71, 73 and Schedule 8 (proposals for additional secondary schools and proposals by governing bodies of community schools).

Article 3 commences sections 74(1) (the ability of a new school to be opened as a federated school), 75 and Schedule 10 to the extent that they are not already in force, (provisions consequential on sections 70, 71 and 73), and sections 142 to 144 (misconduct etc).

The provision listed in article 2 is brought into force for all purposes in accordance with the Secretary of State’s power in section 216(2). The provisions listed in article 3 are brought into force for England only, in accordance with the Secretary of State’s power in section 216(4).

The Order also makes transitional and saving provision in respect of the commencement of the school reorganisation changes.
