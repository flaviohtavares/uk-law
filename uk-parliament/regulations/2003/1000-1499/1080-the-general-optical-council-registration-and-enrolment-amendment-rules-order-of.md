# The General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules) Order of Council 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1080

OPTICIANS

The General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules) Order of Council 2003

Made

19th March 2003

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 19th day of March 2003

By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Whereas in pursuance of section 10 of the Opticians Act 1989[^f00001] the General Optical Council have made the Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules 2003 as set out in the Schedule to this Order:

And whereas by section 34 of the Act such rules shall not come into force until approved by Order of the Privy Council:

And whereas upon rule 2 of these rules coming into force the revocation mentioned therein will render wholly spent the General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules) Order of Council 2002[^f00002]:

Now, therefore, Their Lordships, having taken these rules into consideration, are hereby pleased to approve them with effect from 31st March 2003.

**1.** This Order may be cited as the General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules) Order of Council 2003.

**2.** The General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules) Order of Council 2002[^f00002] is hereby revoked with effect from 31st March 2003.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

The rules approved by this Order increase with effect from 31st March 2003 the fees payable to the General Optical Council by ophthalmic and dispensing opticians and bodies corporate carrying on business as opticians for registration, enrolment or retention in, restoration to and transfer within the register of opticians. The fees were last fixed with effect from 1st April 2002 and have been increased by 9.5 per cent.

This Order also revokes a previous Order of Council which becomes spent on the revocation of rules which are scheduled to it.
