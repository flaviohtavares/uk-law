# The Land Registration Rules 2003, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2024-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/1417
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 61. Documents executed by attorney

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If any document executed by an attorney is delivered to the land registry, there must be produced to the registrar—

- (a) the instrument creating the power, or
- (b) a copy of the power by means of which its contents may be proved under section 3 of the Powers of Attorney Act 1971 [^c10312071], or
- (c)[^c-dc974d65d497aac294d9beecc2bb7393] a document which under section 4 of the Evidence and Powers of Attorney Act 1940, paragraph 16 of Part 2 of Schedule 1, or paragraph 15(3) of Part 5 of Schedule 4 to the Mental Capacity Act [2005 (c.9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9) is sufficient evidence of the contents of the power, or
- (d) a certificate by a conveyancer in Form 1.

(2)[^c-f3be879ce14ee3fd97c0a005bb2cea5e] If an order or direction under section 22 or 23 of, or paragraph 16 of Part 5 of Schedule 4 to, the Mental Capacity Act 2005 has been made with respect to a power or the donor of the power or the attorney appointed under it, the order or direction must be produced to the registrar.

(3) In this rule, “**power**” means the power of attorney.
