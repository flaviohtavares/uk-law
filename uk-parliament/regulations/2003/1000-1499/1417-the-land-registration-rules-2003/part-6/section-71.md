# The Land Registration Rules 2003, section 71

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71. Note as to inclusion of mines or minerals in the registered estate[^key-dbee8e531c7b6f6225e61d54dd8f6dbf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application for a note to be entered that a registered estate includes the mines or minerals, or specified mines or minerals, must be accompanied by evidence to satisfy the registrar that those mines or minerals are included in the registered estate.

(2) If the registrar is satisfied that those mines or minerals are included in the registered estate, he must enter the appropriate note.
