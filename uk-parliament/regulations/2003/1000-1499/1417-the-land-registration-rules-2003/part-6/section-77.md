> Extent: England, Wales.

## 77. No entry in the register of a right of entry in certain leases[^key-47c67de2199623ff4354b6c0d6d222e3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This rule applies to a right of entry created in a grant of a term of years absolute, the right being exercisable over or in respect of that term of years.

(2) Where the grant is completed by registration, the disposition which consists of the creation of the right of entry is also completed by registration, without any specific entry relating to it being made in the register.
