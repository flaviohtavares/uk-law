## 58. Form of transfer of registered estates

*Extent: E+W*

A transfer of a registered estate must be in Form TP1, TP2, [^key-4561572b28bf404ead997ba3217525ad]... TR1, TR2, TR5, AS1 or AS3, as appropriate unless it is effected by an electronic document to which section 91 of the Act applies.[^key-107c7dbe8ba043825be002da0c260800]
