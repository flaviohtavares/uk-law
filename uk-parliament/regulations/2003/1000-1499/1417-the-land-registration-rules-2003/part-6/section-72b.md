# The Land Registration Rules 2003, section 72B

## 72B. Entries in the tenant’s registered title in respect of notices in the landlord’s registered title[^key-b05e2286317fa4a6632ef7cb949ab5e6]

*Extent: E+W*

On completion of a lease within section 27(2)(b) or (c) of the Act by registration, the registrar must enter a notice or make another entry, as appropriate, in the individual register of the registered lease in respect of any interest which—

- (a) at the time of registration, is the subject of a notice in the individual register of the registered estate out of which the lease is granted, and
- (b) the registrar considers may affect the registered lease.
