# The Land Registration Rules 2003, section 207A

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 207A. Amendment of certain Schedule 1 forms to provide for explanatory information to be altered[^c-73b53db5f862df10b9d2072e1cafff10]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In order to assist applicants in completing a form or in making an application in relation to a form, the registrar may remove, add to, or alter any explanatory information outside the panels of a Schedule 1 form.

(2) Any amendment under paragraph (1) must not alter the name and description of the form at the top of the first page or instructions as to what must be entered in the form.

(3) Where a form has been amended under paragraph (1) a person may use the form for the purposes of these rules as amended or as unamended.
