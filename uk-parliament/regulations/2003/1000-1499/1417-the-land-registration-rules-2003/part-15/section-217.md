# The Land Registration Rules 2003, section 217

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2024-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/1417
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 217. General Interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-a64e8a5daf16d912d0ad57fac6ad7d9d] In these rules—

- “**the Act**” means the Land Registration Act 2002 [^c10313671],
- “adjudicator” means an adjudicator appointed by the Secretary of State under section 398A (appointment[^key-4b3ab2cf5e8c84c996840eeacd604b60] etc of adjudicators and assistants) of the Insolvency Act 1986,
- “**affecting franchise**” means a franchise which relates to a defined area of land and is an adverse right affecting, or capable of affecting, the title to an estate or charge,
- “[^key-a40d64ccfc915d3e0edec75a33b13aee]**business day**” means a day when the land registry is open for business under rule 216,
- “**caution plan**” has the meaning given by rule 41(4),
- “**caution title number**” has the meaning given by rule 41(1),
- “**certified copy**” means a copy of a document which a conveyancer, or such other person as the registrar may permit, has certified on its face to be a true copy of the original and endorsed with his name and address, and the reference to a conveyancer includes where the document is one referred to in—
  - (a) rule 168(2)(a) or 168(3), the bankrupt's trustee in bankruptcy or the official receiver,
  - (b) rule 184(2), the company's administrator,
  - (c) rule 184(5), the company's liquidator,
- “**charges register**” is the register so named in rule 4 the contents of which are described in rule 9,
- “**charity**” and “**charity trustees**” have the same meaning as in sections 96 and 97(1) of the Charities Act 1993 [^c10313681] respectively,
- “Companies Acts” means—[^key-aa6503da00ce87b44a8713f639aae030]
  - (a) the Companies Act 2006 and any Act amending or replacing that Act,
  - (b) the provisions of the Companies Act 1985, the Companies Consolidation (Consequential Provisions) Act 1985, Part 2 of the Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) Act 2004 and the Companies (N.I.) Order 1986 that remain in force, and
  - (c) any former enactment relating to companies,
- “**control**” in relation to a document of which a person has control means physical possession, or the right to possession, or right to take copies of the document,
- “conveyancer” has the meaning given by rule 217A,[^key-2f28bd80717a6e134fb0b3823e4c1266]
- “**day list**” has the same meaning given by rule 12,
- **[^key-44cdc55b728a1bfbf634d936ac3f71bd]...**
- “**exempt charity**” has the same meaning as in section 96 of the Charities Act 1993 and “**non-exempt charity**” means a charity which is not an exempt charity,
- “home rights notice” means a notice registered under section 31(10)(a) or section 32 of, and paragraph 4(3)(a) or 4(3)(b) of Schedule 4 to, the Family Law Act 1996, or section 2(8) or section 5(3)(b) of the Matrimonial Homes Act 1983, or section 2(7) or section 5(3)(b) of the Matrimonial Homes Act 1967,[^key-bf09350c5abb7ae34a99c2e849a6c2f8]
- “**index map**” has the meaning given by rule 10(1)(a),
- “**index of proprietors' names**” has the meaning given by rule 11(1),
- “**index of relating franchises and manors**” is the index described in rule 10(1)(b),
- “**individual caution register**” is the register so named in rule 41(1) the arrangement of which is described in rule 41(2),
- “**individual register**” is the register so named in rule 2 the contents and arrangement of which are described in rules 3 and 4,
- “**inheritance tax notice**” means a notice in respect of an Inland Revenue charge arising under Part III of the Finance Act 1975 [^c10313711] or section 237 of the Inheritance Tax Act 1984 [^c10313721],
- “**matrimonial home rights caution**” means a caution registered under the Matrimonial Homes Act 1967 [^c10313731] before 14 February 1983,
- [^key-21c4b233be4eacc542794062c3ee61fb]...
- “**official custodian**” means the official custodian for charities,
- “**old tenancy**” means a tenancy as defined in section 28 of the Landlord and Tenant (Covenants) Act 1995 [^c10313761] which is not a new tenancy as defined in section 1 of that Act,
- “**overseas company**” means a company incorporated outside the United Kingdom[^key-ea15050942eedd079d2ece252cb30775],
- “**property register**” is the register so named in rule 4 the contents of which are described in rules 5, 6 and 7,
- “**proprietorship register**” is the register so named in rule 4 the contents of which are described in rule 8,
- “**registered title**” means an individual register and any title plan referred to in that register,
- “**relating franchise**” means a franchise which is not an affecting franchise,
- “**Schedule 1 form**” means a form in Schedule 1,
- “**Schedule 3 form**” means a form in Schedule 3,
- “**scheduled form**” means a Schedule 1 form or a Schedule 3 form,
- “**section 33(5) order**” means an order made under section 33(5) of the Family Law Act 1996,
- “statement of truth” has the meaning given by rule 215A,[^key-c15f9b38972a2279bb7d817237bd5cd3]
- “**statutory declaration**” includes affidavit,
- “**title number**” has the meaning given by rule 4,
- “**title plan**” has the meaning given by rule 5,
- “**trust corporation**” has the same meaning as in the Settled Land Act 1925 [^c10313771],
- “**trusts**” in relation to a charity has the same meaning as in section 97(1) of the Charities Act 1993,
- “**unregistered company**” means a body corporate to which section 718(1) of the Companies Act 1985 [^c10313781] applies,
- “working day” means any day from Monday to Friday (inclusive) which is not Christmas Day, Good Friday or any other day either specified or declared by proclamation under section 1 of the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 or appointed by the[^key-1918e47a4f4bb141a8d824a74aac506e] Secretary of State[^key-5f34447553377281510f5a0d065461f1] or, subject to rule 216A(5), certified as an interrupted working day under rule 216A(1).[^key-bb0ac59042b75fe56467b4ba5b5ea40a]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), a reference in these rules to a form by letter, or by number, or by a combination of both is to a scheduled form.

(3) A reference in these rules to Forms A to Y and Forms AA to[^key-193f28e7d5e739c00fb11560af99044f] SS[^key-c890c2a978de3a04c07a3b7fff928411] (in each case inclusive) is to the standard form of restriction bearing that letter in Schedule 4.
