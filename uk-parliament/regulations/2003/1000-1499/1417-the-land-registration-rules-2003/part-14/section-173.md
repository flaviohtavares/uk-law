# The Land Registration Rules 2003, section 173

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2024-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/1417
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 173. Escheat etc

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a registered freehold estate in land has determined, the registrar may enter a note of that fact in the property register and in the property register of any inferior affected registered title.

(2) Where the registrar considers that there is doubt as to whether a registered freehold estate in land has determined, the entry under paragraph (1) must be modified by a statement to that effect.
