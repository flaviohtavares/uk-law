## 182. Registration of trustees of charitable, ecclesiastical or public trust

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2)[^c-61631111cf7752bf3ce9c021a5fe33fc], where a corporation or body of trustees holding on charitable, ecclesiastical or public trusts applies to be registered as proprietor of a registered estate or registered charge, the application must be accompanied by the document creating the trust.

(2)[^c-5d4ecb1c811843dd71f450ddf7b44005] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-5d4ecb1c811843dd71f450ddf7b44005] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-73c4a7db1a4afc975eeed793aea508d9] Paragraph (1) of this rule does not apply in the case of a registered estate or a registered charge held by or in trust for a non-exempt charity.
