## 158. Application for official search for the purpose of the Family Law Act 1996 by a mortgagee

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A mortgagee of land comprised in a registered title that consists of or includes all or part of a dwelling-house may apply for an official search certificate of the result of a search of the relevant individual register for the purpose of section 56(3) of the Family Law Act 1996 [^c10312431].

(2) Subject to rule 132(1), an application under paragraph (1) must be made in Form HR3[^c-a8adc66ad9fc6b9e022104bf9f0de744] .
