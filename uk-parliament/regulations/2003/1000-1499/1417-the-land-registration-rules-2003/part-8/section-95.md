# The Land Registration Rules 2003, section 95

## 95. Form of obligatory restrictions

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The form of any restriction that the registrar is obliged to enter under any enactment shall be—

- (a) as specified in these rules,
- (b) as required by the relevant enactment, or
- (c) in other cases, such form as the registrar may direct having regard to the provisions of the relevant enactment.

(2) The form of the restriction required under—

- (a) section 44(1) of the Act is Form A,
- (b) section 37(5A) of the Housing Act 1985 [^c10312251] is Form U,
- (c) section 157(7) of the Housing Act 1985 is Form V,
- (d)[^key-553b4fb900656801709b0ac688104d27] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) section 133 of the Housing Act 1988 is Form X,
- (f) paragraph 4 of Schedule 9A to the Housing Act 1985 is Form W,
- (g) section 173(9) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 [^c10312271] is Form X, and
- (h) section 13(5) of the Housing Act 1996 [^c10312281] is Form Y.
