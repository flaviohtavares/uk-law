# The Land Registration Rules 2003, section 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2024-03-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/1417
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 82. Application for a ... home rights notice or its renewal[^c-287e86b677092749fa9004e834ef3a1b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application under section 31(10)(a) or section 32 of, and paragraph 4(3)(b) of Schedule 4 to, the Family Law Act 1996 [^c10312171] for the entry of an agreed notice in the register must be in Form HR1[^c-0856b5c8c2b38ccb4bbeb00291155c38] .

(2) An application to renew the registration of a [^c-8af2ac70d18694cb6062f13baa4669f8]... home rights notice or a matrimonial home rights caution under section 32 of, and paragraph 4(3)(a) of Schedule 4 to, the Family Law Act 1996 must be in Form HR2[^c-fd765ede3ecfd3da06561d11aff7015c] .

(3) An application in Form HR1[^c-edbff7b88cd5e2ea2877af32952a76a3] , where the application is made under section 32 of, and paragraph 4(3)(b) of Schedule 4 to, the Family Law Act 1996, or in Form HR2 must be accompanied by—

- (a) an office copy of the section 33(5) order, or
- (b) a conveyancer's certificate that he holds an office copy of the section 33(5) order.
