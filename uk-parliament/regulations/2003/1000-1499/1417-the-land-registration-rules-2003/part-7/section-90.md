## 90. Application for entry of a notice under paragraph 5(2) or, in certain cases, paragraph 7(2)(a) of Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Act

*Extent: E+W*

An application to meet the registration requirements under—

- (a) paragraph 5(2) of Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Act, or
- (b) paragraph 7(2)(a) of that Part, where the interest is created for the benefit of an unregistered estate,

must be made in Form AP1 or Form AN1[^c-6a18153f38aad0160f83bf9d8a63fbc1].
