## 51. Alteration of the cautions register—alteration of cautioner

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person who claims that the whole of the relevant interest described in an individual caution register is vested in him by operation of law as successor to the cautioner may apply for the register to be altered under section 21(1) of the Act to substitute him for the[^c-4697ce4e5260862f0133c4ec39e9b54d] cautioner in the cautioner's register [^c-5bbfa21306087a4d3d0c28ae241de7a4]....

(2) If the registrar does not serve notice under rule 50(2) or if the cautioner does not object within the time specified in the notice, the registrar must give effect to the application.
