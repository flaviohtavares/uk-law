# The Land Registration Rules 2003, section 111

## 111. Certificate of registration of company charges[^c-b6d359c47c3cecfd15d060f678d32946]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) When making an application for the registration of a charge created by a company registered under the Companies Acts or a limited liability partnership incorporated under the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000 [^c10312301] or the Limited Liability Partnership Act (Northern Ireland) 2002 the applicant must produce to the registrar the [^c-cfb7a74fadb1cfd2a7eb82ca752e2fa6]... certificate issued under section 859I[^c-aed11dbd3f25c6e19f9b72166d246aed] of the Companies Act 2006 that the charge has been registered under section 859A[^c-78ac8ebe7782679dbe02490ae49d3c10] of that Act.

(2) If the applicant does not produce the certificate required by paragraph (1) with the application for registration of the charge, the registrar must enter a note in the register stating that no evidence of registration of the charge in accordance with section 859A[^c-d1e417be7b9b78af6bd77d5886a24426] of the Companies Act 2006 [^c-766d463f31c2c579f2c4d9159ccb2864]... has been lodged.
