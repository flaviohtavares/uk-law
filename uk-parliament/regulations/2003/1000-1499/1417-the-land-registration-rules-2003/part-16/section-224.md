## 224. Registered rentcharges held in trust under section 75(1) of the 1925 Act on commencement

*Extent: E+W*

Where a rentcharge is held in trust under section 75(1) of the Land Registration Act 1925 immediately before the coming into force of section 97 of the Act, the beneficiary of the trust may apply—

- (a) to be registered as proprietor of the rentcharge, or
- (b) for the registration of the rentcharge to be cancelled.
