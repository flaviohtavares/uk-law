## 218. Definitions

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**the 1925 Act**” means the Land Registration Act 1925 [^c10313791],
- “**caution**” means a caution entered in the register of title under section 54 of the 1925 Act,
- “**cautioner**” includes his personal representative,
- “**the notice period**” is the period ending at 12 noon on the fifteenth working[^c-848b7a61f78352bd7407b9d7f3d2156b] day, or ending at 12 noon on such later working day as the registrar may allow, after the date of issue of the notice.
