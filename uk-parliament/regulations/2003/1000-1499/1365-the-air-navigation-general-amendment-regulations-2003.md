# The Air Navigation (General) (Amendment) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1365

CIVIL AVIATION

The Air Navigation (General) (Amendment) Regulations 2003

Made

20th May 2003

Coming into force

10th June 2003

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by article 47(1) of the Air Navigation Order 2000[^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (General) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 10th June 2003.

**2.** The Air Navigation (General) Regulations 1993[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

**3.** In regulation 18(2) for “N2700.00 W02500.00” substitute the following:

> - N2700.00 W04000.00
> - N2218.00 W04000.00
> - N1700.00 W03730.00
> - N2400.00 W02500.00

.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Spellar

Minister of State,

Department for Transport

20th May 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Air Navigation (General) Regulations 1993.

The airspace prescribed pursuant to article 47(1) of the Air Navigation Order 2000 as North Atlantic Minimum Navigation Performance Specification Airspace is amended. With this amendment it covers a greater area. In general terms the Southeast boundary is extended southwards some 600 miles from 27 degrees North to 17 degrees North.
