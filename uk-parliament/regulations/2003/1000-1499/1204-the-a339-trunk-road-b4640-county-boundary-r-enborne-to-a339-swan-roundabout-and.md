# The A339 Trunk Road (B4640 County Boundary (R. Enborne) to A339 Swan Roundabout and A339 Swan Roundabout to A34/A339 Junction) (Detrunking) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1204

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The A339 Trunk Road (B4640 County Boundary (R. Enborne) to A339 Swan Roundabout and A339 Swan Roundabout to A34/A339 Junction) (Detrunking) Order 2003

Made

29th April 2003

Coming into force

1st June 2003

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A339 Trunk Road (B4640 County Boundary (R. Enborne) to A339 Swan Roundabout and A339 Swan Roundabout to A34/A339 Junction) (Detrunking) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st June 2003.

**2.** In this Order—

- (i) “principal road” as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is a principal road for the purposes of enactments and instruments which refer to highways classified as principal roads and is also classified for the purpose of every other enactment and instrument which refers to highways classified by the Secretary of State; and
- (ii) “the trunk road” means the A339 Trunk Road (A339).

**3.** The length of the trunk road described in the Schedule to this Order shall cease to be a trunk road and shall be classified as a principal road from the date on which the Secretary of State notifies West Berkshire District Council that they will become the highway authority responsible for that length of road.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Mr W. S. C. Wadrup

(A Divisional Director) Highways Agency

29th April 2003

## THE SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF THE TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE A TRUNK ROAD

The length of trunk road ceasing to be a trunk road is situated from the B4640 County Boundary (R. Enborne) to its junction with the A339 Swan Roundabout and the A339 Swan Roundabout to points at the back of the nosings on the northbound entry slip road and the southbound exit slip roads from the A34 Newbury Bypass including:

- the A339/B4640 Swan roundabout;
- the A339/A343 St John’s Road roundabout;
- the A339/B3241 Kings Road roundabout;
- the A339/A4 Robin Hood roundabout;
- the A339/Vodafone roundabout;
- the B4640 from West Berkshire/Hampshire County Boundary to the A339 Swan roundabout;
- all parallel lay-bys and cycle tracks;
- all structures

and is shown by broad black dashes on the plan numbered HA 10/OD/400 marked “The A339 (B4640 County Boundary (River Enborne) to A339 Swan Roundabout to A34/A339 Junction) (Detrunking) Order 2003.” signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport and deposited at DfT Records Management Branch, Floor 13 (IMD), Ashdown House, St Leonards on Sea, Hastings, East Sussex TN37 7GA.
