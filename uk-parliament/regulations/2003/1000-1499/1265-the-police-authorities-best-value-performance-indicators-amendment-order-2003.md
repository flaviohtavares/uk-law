# The Police Authorities (Best Value) Performance Indicators (Amendment) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made to correct errors in [S.I. 2003/519](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/519) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2003 No. 1265

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, ENGLAND AND WALES

POLICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Police Authorities (Best Value) Performance Indicators (Amendment) Order 2003

Made

8th May 2003

Laid before Parliament

9th May 2003

Coming into force

2nd June 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 4(1)(a) of the Local Government Act 1999[^f00001] (having regard to the definition in section 1(1)(d)), and having consulted, in accordance with section 4(3) of that Act, persons appearing to him to represent police authorities and such other persons as he thought fit, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Police Authorities (Best Value) Performance Indicators (Amendment) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 2nd June 2003.

## 2. Amendment of performance indicators

(1) Schedule 3 (investigating crime) to the Police Authorities (Best Value) Performance Indicators Order 2003[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (a) of indicator number PBV7 the figure “10,000” is substituted for the figure “1,000”.

(3) In paragraph (b) of indicator number PBV8 the words “notifiable/recorded” is substituted for the word “notifiable”.

Bob Ainsworth

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

8th May 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes the amendments set out in article 2 to the performance indicators which are applicable to police authorities.
