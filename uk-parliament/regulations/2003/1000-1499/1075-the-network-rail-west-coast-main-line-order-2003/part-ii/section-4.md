# The Network Rail (West Coast Main Line) Order 2003, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Power to construct and maintain certain works

(1) Network Rail may construct and maintain the scheduled works.

(2) Subject to article 6, the scheduled works shall be constructed in the lines or situations shown on the deposited plans and in accordance with the levels shown on the deposited sections.

(3) Network Rail may on the land specified in columns (1) and (2) of Parts I and II of Schedule 2 to this Order carry out and maintain any works specified in relation to that land in column (3) of those Parts of that Schedule with all necessary works and conveniences in connection therewith.
