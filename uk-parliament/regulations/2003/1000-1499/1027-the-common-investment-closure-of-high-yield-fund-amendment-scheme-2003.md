# The Common Investment (Closure of High Yield Fund) (Amendment) Scheme 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made as a consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2003/778](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/778) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2003 No. 1027

SUPREME COURT OF ENGLAND AND WALES

COUNTY COURTS, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Common Investment (Closure of High Yield Fund) (Amendment) Scheme 2003

Made

5th April 2003

Laid before Parliament

7th April 2003

Coming into force

9th April 2003

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 42(1) and (16) of the Administration of Justice Act 1982[^f00001], hereby makes the following Scheme:

**1.** This Scheme may be cited as the Common Investment (Closure of High Yield Fund) (Amendment) Scheme 2003 and shall come into force on 9th April 2003.

**2.** In paragraph 2(1) of the Common Investment (Closure of High Yield Fund) Scheme 2003[^f00002], in the definition of “the effective date”, for “5th April 2003” substitute “10th April 2003”.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Rosie Winterton

Parliamentary Secretary,

Lord Chancellor’s Department

Dated 5th April 2003

(This note is not part of the Scheme)

The Common Investment (Closure of High Yield Fund) Scheme 2003 ([S.I. 2003/778](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/778)) erroneously defined the “effective date” as being 5th April 2003. This instrument substitutes the correct effective date, 10th April 2003.
