# The Land Registration Act 2002 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1028 (C. 53)

LAND REGISTRATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Land Registration Act 2002 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2003

Made

2nd April 2003

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 136(2) of the Land Registration Act 2002[^f00001], makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Land Registration Act 2002 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2003.

**2.** The following provisions of the Land Registration Act 2002 shall come into force on 28th April 2003—

- (a) section 107 and Schedule 9; and
- (b) paragraph 28 of Schedule 11 and, so far as it relates to that paragraph, section 133.

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Scotland of Asthal, QC

Parliamentary Secretary,

Lord Chancellor’s Department

2nd April 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 28th April 2003 the following provisions of the Land Registration Act 2002—

- section 107, which contains provisions relating to the appointment and qualification of the Adjudicator to Her Majesty’s Land Registry and gives effect to Schedule 9;
- Schedule 9, which makes further provision about the Adjudicator; and
- Schedule 11, paragraph 28, which adds the Adjudicator to Her Majesty’s Land Registry to the list of office holders in Schedule 5 to the Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993 subject to compulsory retirement at 70 with the possibility of annual extension up to 75.

(This note is not part of the Order)
