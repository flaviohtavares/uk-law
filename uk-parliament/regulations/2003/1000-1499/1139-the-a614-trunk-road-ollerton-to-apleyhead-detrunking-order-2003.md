# The A614 Trunk Road (Ollerton to Apleyhead) (Detrunking) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1139

HIGHWAYS, ENGLAND

The A614 Trunk Road (Ollerton to Apleyhead) (Detrunking) Order 2003

Made

22nd April 2003

Coming into force

2nd July 2003

The Secretary of State for Transport has made this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A614 Trunk Road (Ollerton to Apleyhead) (Detrunking) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 2nd July 2003.

**2.** In this Order—

- (i) “the principal road” as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is a principal road for the purposes of enactments and instruments which refer to highways classified as principal roads and is also classified for the purpose of every other enactment and instrument which refers to highways classified by the Secretary of State;
- (ii) “the trunk road” means the A614 Trunk Road; and
- (iii) “the plan” means the plan, numbered HA 10/OD/478, marked “The A614 Trunk Road (Ollerton to Apleyhead) (Detrunking) Order 2003”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport and deposited at DFT Records Management Branch, Ashdown House, Sedlescombe Road North, St Leonards-on-Sea, East Sussex, TN37 7GA.

**3.** The length of the trunk road described in the Schedule to this Order shall cease to be a trunk road and shall be classified as a principal road from the date on which the Order comes into force.

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Graham Bowskill

A Divisional Director in the Highways Agency

22nd April 2003

## THE SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF THE TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE A TRUNK ROAD

The length of the trunk road ceasing to be a trunk road is situated:

- (i) between the junction with the A616 and A6075 at Ollerton roundabout (marked “A” on the plan) and the A1 Trunk Road at Apleyhead roundabout (marked “B” on the plan), a distance of approximately 9.9km.

all in the County of Nottinghamshire. It is shown by broad black dashes on the plan.
