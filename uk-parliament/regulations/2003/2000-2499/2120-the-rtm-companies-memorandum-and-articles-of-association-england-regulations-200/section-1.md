# The RTM Companies (Memorandum and Articles of Association) (England) Regulations 2003, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the RTM Companies (Memorandum and Articles of Association) (England) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 30th September 2003.

(2) These Regulations apply to RTM companies[^f00002] in relation to premises[^f00003] in England.

## Annotations

[^f00002]: Footnote: For the definition of “RTM company” *see* sections 71(1) and 73 of the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002.

[^f00003]: Footnote: For the premises relevant to RTM companies, *see* section 72 of, and Schedule 6 to, the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002.
