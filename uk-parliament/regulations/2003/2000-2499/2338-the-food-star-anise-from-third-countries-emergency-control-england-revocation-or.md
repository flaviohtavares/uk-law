# The Food (Star Anise from Third Countries) (Emergency Control) (England) (Revocation) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2338

FOOD, ENGLAND

The Food (Star Anise from Third Countries) (Emergency Control) (England) (Revocation) Order 2003

Made

9th September 2003

Laid before Parliament

10th September 2003

Coming into force

12th September 2003

Whereas it appears to the Secretary of State that the import of certain Star Anise consigned from countries which are not Member States of the European Community no longer involves imminent risk of injury to health;

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 13(1) and 48(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001], having had regard in accordance with section 48(4A) of that Act to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency, makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food (Star Anise from Third Countries) (Emergency Control) (England) (Revocation) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 12th September 2003.

## 2. Revocation

The Food (Star Anise from Third Countries) (Emergency Control) (England) Order 2002[^f00002] is revoked.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Melanie Johnson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

9th September 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Food (Star Anise from Third Countries) (Emergency Control) (England) Order 2002 (S.I. [2002/334](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/334)), which prohibited the import of Star Anise and Japanese Star Anise into England unless certain conditions were complied with. The Food (Star Anise from Third Countries) (Emergency Control) (England) Order 2002 implemented Commission Decision [2002/75/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0075) laying down special conditions on the import from third countries of Star Anise (OJ No. L33, 2.2.2002, p. 31). That Decision was repealed by Commission Decision [2003/602/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0602) (OJ No. L204, 13.8.2003, p. 60).

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to this Order.
