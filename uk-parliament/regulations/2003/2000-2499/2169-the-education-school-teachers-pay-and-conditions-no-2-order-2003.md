# The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 2) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2169

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 2) Order 2003

Made

21st August 2003

Laid before Parliament

28th August 2003

Coming into force

1st September 2003

Whereas the review body established under section 1 of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991[^f00001] have made a report following the reference to them of certain matters;

And whereas the Secretary of State has consulted, in accordance with section 126 of the Education Act 2002[^f00002] (“the Act”), such associations of local education authorities, such local education authorities, such bodies representing the interests of governing bodies of schools and such bodies representing the interests of teachers as appeared to him to be appropriate having regard to the contents of this Order;

Now, therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 122(1), 123, 124 and 210 of the Act hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and revocation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 2) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st September 2003.

(2) The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 2) Order 2002[^f00003], the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 2003[^f00004] and the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (Amendment) Regulations 2003[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

## 2. Application

This Order applies to school teachers within the meaning of section 122 of the Act in England and Wales[^f00006].

## 3. School Teachers' Pay and Conditions

It is hereby directed that the provisions set out in section 2 of the document published by The Stationery Office Limited and entitled “School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2003 and Guidance on School Teachers' Pay and Conditions”[^f00007] shall have effect from 1st September 2003, save for the provisions set out in part 1 of Annex 6 which shall have effect from 1st September 2004 and for those set out in part 2 of that Annex which shall have effect from 1st September 2005.

David Miliband

Minister of State,

Department for Education and Skills

21st August 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order which is made under sections 122(1), 123 and 124 of the Education Act 2002 (“the Act”) refers to a document entitled “School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2003 and Guidance on School Teachers' Pay and Conditions” published by The Stationery Office Limited, which is obtainable from Government book shops and through booksellers. It can also be found on the internet at http://www.teachernet.gov.uk/management/payandperformance/pay/strb2003/

It revokes the Orders giving effect to and amending the previous School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2002.

The Order directs that the provisions set out in section 2 of the document shall have effect from 1st September 2003 save for those provisions set out in part 1 of Annex 6 which shall have effect from 1st September 2004 and those set out in part 2 of Annex 6 which shall have effect from 1st September 2005.

The Order applies to all school teachers within the meaning of section 122 of the Act in England and Wales. By virtue of section 122(2)(a) of the Act, the remuneration of school teachers to whom the Order applies is to be determined and paid to them by local education authorities in accordance with the pay scales and other provisions of section 2 of the document. By virtue of section 124(3)(b) of the Act, the provisions set out in section 2 of the document relating to the statutory conditions of employment have effect as terms of the contracts of employment of school teachers to whom the Order applies.
