# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment)(No. 4) Regulations 2003, section 3

> Text as at 2007-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Amendment of regulation 24 of the principal Regulations (persons by whom practical and unitary tests may be conducted) and consequential amendments to regulations 35 and 48 of the principal Regulations

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Regulation 24 [^c5211131] of the principal Regulations is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4) as follows.

(2) For paragraph (1)(c) substitute—

> - (c) In England and Wales, a person appointed by the chief officer of any fire brigade maintained in pursuance of the Fire Services Act 1947 [^c5211141] or, in Scotland, by the firemaster of such a brigade, for the purpose of testing members of any fire brigade maintained in pursuance of that Act.

.

(3) Omit paragraph (1)(e).

(4) In each of paragraphs (3) and (5) for “(b), (c), (d), (e) or (f)” substitute “ (b), (c), (d) or (f) ”.

(5) In regulation 35(1) of the principal Regulations for “24(1)(b), (c), (d), (e) or (f) or (2)(b)” substitute “ 24(1)(b), (c), (d) or (f) or (2)(b) ”.

(6) In regulation 48(3) [^c5211151] of the principal Regulations for “(b), (c), (d), (e), or (f)” substitute “ (b), (c), (d) or (f) ”.
