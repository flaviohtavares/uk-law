# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment)(No. 4) Regulations 2003, section 2

> Text as at 2007-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2. Amendment of regulation 23 of the principal Regulations (persons by whom theory tests may be conducted)

*Extent: E+W+S*

For regulation 23(1)(d) [^c5211111] of the principal Regulations substitute—

> - (d) In England and Wales, a person appointed by the chief officer of any fire brigade maintained in pursuance of the Fire Services Act 1947 [^c5211121] or, in Scotland, by the firemaster of such a brigade, for the purpose of testing members of any fire brigade maintained in pursuance of that Act.

.
