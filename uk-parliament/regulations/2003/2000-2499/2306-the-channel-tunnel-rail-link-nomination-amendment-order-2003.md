# The Channel Tunnel Rail Link (Nomination) (Amendment) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2306

TRANSPORT

RAILWAYS

The Channel Tunnel Rail Link (Nomination) (Amendment) Order 2003

Made

4th September 2003

Coming into force

28th September 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 34(1) and (4) of the Channel Tunnel Rail Link Act 1996[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Channel Tunnel Rail Link (Nomination) (Amendment) Order 2003, and shall come into force on 28th September 2003.

## 2. Amendment of nominated undertaker appointment

The Channel Tunnel Rail Link (Nomination) Order 1999[^f00002] is amended by, in articles 3(2)(b) and 3(2)(c) after “section 14”, inserting “(1)(b)”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Tony McNulty

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Transport

4th September 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Channel Tunnel Rail Link (Nomination) Order 1999 to further specify Union Railways (South) Limited as the nominated undertaker for the purpose of providing infrastructure services in relation to the part of the rail link between Fawkham Junction, Kent and the Channel Tunnel.
