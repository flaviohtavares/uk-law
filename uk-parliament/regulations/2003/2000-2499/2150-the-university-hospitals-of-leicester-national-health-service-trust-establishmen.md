# The University Hospitals of Leicester National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2150

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The University Hospitals of Leicester National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2003

Made

13th August 2003

Coming into force

3rd September 2003

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 3 of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the University Hospitals of Leicester National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2003 and shall come into force on 3rd September 2003.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Establishment Order” means the University Hospitals of Leicester National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999[^f00003]; and
- “the trust” means the National Health Service Trust established by the Establishment Order.

## 2. Increase in the number of non-executive directors of the trust

In article 4 of the Establishment Order (directors of the trust) “6 non-executive directors” shall be replaced by “7 non-executive directors”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Rosie Winterton

Minister of State,

Department of Health

13th August 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the University Hospitals of Leicester National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999, which established the University Hospitals of Leicester National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 of the Order increases the number of non-executive directors of the trust from 6 to 7.
