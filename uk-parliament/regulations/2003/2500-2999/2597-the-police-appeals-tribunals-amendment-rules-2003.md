# The Police Appeals Tribunals (Amendment) Rules 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2597

POLICE, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Police Appeals Tribunals (Amendment) Rules 2003

Made

14th October 2003

Laid before Parliament

16th October 2003

Coming into force

15th November 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 85 of the Police Act 1996[^f00001], and after consultation with the Council on Tribunals in accordance with section 8 of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00002], hereby makes the following Rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Police Appeals Tribunals (Amendment) Rules 2003 and shall come into force on 15th November 2003.

## 2. Amendment to the Police Appeals Tribunals Rules 1999

(1) Rule 5 of the Police Appeals Tribunals Rules 1999[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1) after the words “section 50(3) of the Act” there is inserted “or section 81 of the Police Act 1997”.

Caroline Flint

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

14th October 2003

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Police Appeals Tribunals Rules 1999 in consequence of the National Crime Squad being able to directly recruit officers following the bringing into force, to the extent they were not already in force, of sections 87 and 91 of the Police Reform Act [2002 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32).
