# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 14BA

## 14BA. Determination of code in respect of winter fuel payment charge[^c-d83be38daf4531a2fad96f207cefa2f7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

HMRC may determine a code, if and to the extent that the payee does not object, to secure that—

- (a) income tax payable for a tax year by the payee by virtue of section 681I of ITEPA (winter fuel payment charge) is deducted from PAYE income of the payee paid during that year, and
- (b) repayments are made in a tax year in respect of any amounts overpaid on account of income tax under that section for that tax year.
