# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 67BC

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/2682
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67BC. Regulations 67BA and 67BB: supplementary[^c-e59ddd91bfac9e0b72467dbbfe5638d9][^c-08b6e8f9f8eba33d4316dc76fb9ec396]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where regulation 67BA or 67BB applies, the information required by regulation 67B(1) in respect of the relevant payment may be included in a return with the information for any other relevant payment.
