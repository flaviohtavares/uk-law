## 97LG. Withdrawal of recovery notices[^key-a427e956584b4f42d2502d13f9285bee]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A recovery notice is withdrawn if the tribunal quashes it.

(2) An officer of Revenue and Customs may withdraw a recovery notice if the officer considers it appropriate to do so.

(3) If a recovery notice is withdrawn in accordance with paragraph (2), HMRC must give notice of that fact to the person to whom the notice was given.
