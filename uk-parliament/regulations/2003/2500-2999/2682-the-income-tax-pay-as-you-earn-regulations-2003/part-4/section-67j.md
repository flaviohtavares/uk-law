## 67J. Penalty: initial period[^c-bd34a6c50e9054748fb9f29bb73ba145]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of paragraph 6C(3), (4) and (5) of Schedule 55 to the Finance Act 2009 (initial period), the duration of the initial period is thirty days.
