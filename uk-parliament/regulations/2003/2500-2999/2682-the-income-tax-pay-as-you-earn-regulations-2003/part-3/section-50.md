# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/2682
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. No Form P45: code treated as issued by HMRC[^c-c94fdbf74c42c63311acd3566b9fdb09]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Code 0T, the[^c-311a0e75822657889900cb85978de4e2] emergency code or the basic rate code used by the employer in accordance with regulations 46[^c-94ff0b6e69182d994f84e7d056f2268a] to 49E[^c-fc2a2451765a7f7cf2dac9a6bd421b99] is treated, for the purposes of Parts 2 to 4 (codes; deduction and repayment of tax; payments, returns and information) as having been issued by the Inland Revenue as the code for use in respect of the employee.

(2) This does not apply for the purposes of regulation 18 (objections and appeals) and regulations 46 to 49E and 51 to 53 (... late presentation of Form P45).
