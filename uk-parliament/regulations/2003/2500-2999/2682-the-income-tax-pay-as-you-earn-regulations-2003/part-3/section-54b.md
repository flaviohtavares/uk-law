## 54B. Procedure in regulation 54A cases: code treated as issued by HMRC[^c-f15c7cdb504e62fa62da235bdebb33f1][^c-1c973cd25cce99ca5cc7124ba75f5637]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The 0T code used by the pension payer in accordance with regulation 54A is treated, for the purposes of Parts 2 to 4 (codes; deduction and repayment of tax; payments, returns and information), as having been issued by HMRC as the code for use in respect of the pensioner.

(2) This does not apply for the purposes of regulation 18 (objections and appeals) and regulations 58, 60 and 61 ... late presentation of Form P45 etc).
