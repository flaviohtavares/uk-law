# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 61B

## 61B. PAYE: benefits in kind[^c-ecb4e687e0271e10013cc735d05beac4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies where during a tax year an authorised employer provides a specified benefit to a specified employee.

(2) Where this Chapter applies—

- (a) the specified benefit is to be treated as a payment of PAYE income for the purposes of these Regulations; and
- (b) any reference (howsoever expressed) in these Regulations to relevant payment includes an amount in respect of the provision of a specified benefit, such amount to be determined in accordance with regulations 61D, 61H, 61I, 61J, 61K, 61L and 61LA[^c-d48edec8a663dd13ff861790a6e6fc60], as the case may be;

but this is subject to paragraph (3).

(3) An amount determined in accordance with regulation 61D, 61H, 61I, 61J, 61K, 61L or 61LA[^c-c73673acc4ec51ade622aec4217b6c4a], as the case may be, is not to be included as a relevant payment for the purpose of calculating whether the deduction of tax would exceed the overriding limit.
