## 41. Scope of Chapter 2

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter sets out the procedure to be followed for deductions and repayments (Form P45 and P46 procedure) in cases to which Chapter 3 (new pensioners: Forms P45 and P46) does not apply (see regulation 54).
