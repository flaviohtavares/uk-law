# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 22

> Text as at 2026-11-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. The cumulative basis

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An employer must deduct or repay tax on the cumulative basis, unless these Regulations provide otherwise.
