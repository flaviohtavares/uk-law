# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 49A

## 49A. Procedure where employee fails to assist with completion of new employee fields in returns under regulations 67B and 67D[^key-0f2cf395a51e4d45d1d8795988f879a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If, despite the requirements of regulation 40A(1) and (2) (duty of employee to assist with completion of new employee fields in returns under regulations 67B and 67D) and regulations 67B (real time returns of information about relevant payments) and 67D (exceptions to regulation 67B), a return is sent to HMRC under those regulations without the new employee fields being completed in respect of the employee, the employer must deduct tax on the non-cumulative basis using code 0T.

(2) In paragraph (1), “the new employee fields” has the same meaning as in regulation 40A.
