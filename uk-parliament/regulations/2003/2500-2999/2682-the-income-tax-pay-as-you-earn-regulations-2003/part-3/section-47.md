## 47. Procedure in Form P46 cases: (a) seconded expatriate is national of EEA state or Commonwealth citizen, or (b) employee is not seconded expatriate and Statement A applies[^c-278f6ae35d0bee012e288bbe63004ba7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-b515e4b658f85dc1d639cf6fa0393421] This regulation applies in the case of an employee (not a seconded expatriate)[^c-7ac18720886bf2742dea9b55a72424f0] who indicates that Statement A applies.

It also applies to a seconded expatriate who confirms being a national of an EEA state ... (see regulation 46(1B)).

(2) On making the first relevant payment which equals or exceeds[^c-866430bf2f5e8058bbe23b9ba8293795] either[^c-57e183f56f2e1cd38b69eb2617b70a3f] the lower earnings limit or the secondary threshold, whichever is the lesser amount,[^c-a2af4fdc55d723e1c9cf4f5671f28cfd] to the employee, the employer must—

- (a)[^c-a3863fbdf82245b4764b1019c3a1f981] send the Form P46 to Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs,
- (b) prepare a deductions working sheet and enter the total payments to date, and
- (c) deduct tax on the cumulative basis using the emergency code.

(2A)[^c-5c75aae39e1817fd8a36596a88b6e357] To comply with paragraph (2)(a)—

- (a) the employer must send the Form P46 to Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs even if the employee has not provided all of the information required by regulation 46, and
- (b) the employer must provide any of the information required by regulation 46(1B) that the employee has not provided.

(3) On making any subsequent relevant payment before the Inland Revenue issue a code for use in respect of the employee, the employer must continue to deduct or repay tax on the cumulative basis using the emergency code.
