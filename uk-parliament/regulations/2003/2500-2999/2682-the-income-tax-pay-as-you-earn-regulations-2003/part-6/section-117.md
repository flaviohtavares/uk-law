## 117. Retention of PSA records[^key-9633df3c069b3dde5fb8c3d33b7aafe9][^key-1d3c711e2993841ca01fecb077ab957e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-94726553192e80cd5ce800768fef1ce9] An employer must keep PSA records for not less than 3 years after the end of the most recent tax year to which they relate.

(2) “PSA records” means all books, documents and other records relating to—

- (a) the qualifying general earnings comprised in the PSA,
- (b) the calculation of amounts for which the employer is accountable to HMRC[^key-952ecd1c7024b21065c2d0a9dc3ced75] in accordance with the PSA, and
- (c) the payment of those amounts to HMRC[^key-0bf10dde621b14e8a9faaacf521d2cde].
