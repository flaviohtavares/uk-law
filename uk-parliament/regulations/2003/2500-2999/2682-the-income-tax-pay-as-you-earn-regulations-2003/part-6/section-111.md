# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 111

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/2682
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 111. Form of PSA[^c-07ca9e85e7d43d342a4e85f4fea74bde]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A PSA must be—

- (a) in writing, and
- (b) signed and dated by the employer and HMRC[^c-fd9161784dc94e43548db32e52979b19].

(2) A PSA must incorporate, whether by specification or indirect reference—

- (a) the qualifying general earnings included in the PSA,
- (b) the method of calculation, determined in accordance with regulation 108, of the amount of income tax for which the employer is to be accountable in respect of those qualifying general earnings, and
- (c) the due date by which, in accordance with regulation 109, income tax in respect of those qualifying general earnings is due and payable.

(3)[^c-e68bc36b0dc4d1b361d5ec1c56bf6b75] In paragraph (1) “in writing” includes electronic communications and “signed” includes electronic signatures.
