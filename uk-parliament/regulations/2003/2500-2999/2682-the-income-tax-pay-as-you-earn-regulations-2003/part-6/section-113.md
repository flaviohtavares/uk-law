# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/2682

## 113. Variation of PSA[^key-a28f3dd9ababeba48ee4ae326a213919]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) HMRC[^key-a1feda74bd989e9de1904dfca6093003] and the employer may, by agreement and consistently with the provisions of this Part, vary the terms of a PSA entered into by them.

(2) The agreement must be—

- (a) in writing, and
- (b) signed and dated by the employer and by HMRC[^key-b038528645a14e99e1d28d45a58986f5].

(3) The last date for variation of a PSA is 6th July following the end of the first tax year to which the variation relates[^key-133310152faacf6163c9e024d4b655ac] [^key-6a6782050d57bac6d53f696948d723b6]....

(4)[^key-03ce80fe70521284e27743591e452372] In paragraph (2) “in writing” includes electronic communications and “signed” includes electronic signatures.
