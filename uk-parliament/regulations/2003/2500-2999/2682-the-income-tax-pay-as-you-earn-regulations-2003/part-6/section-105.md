# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 105

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 105. HMRC and employer may make PSA[^key-cc10ef816b16d4d44a54d84f107b1ab6][^key-610a8055061a99be564bc20f8c10b60a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) HMRC[^key-ba80b5101523ab2c5278f3ee9e34fe03] and an employer may agree that paragraph (2) applies in respect of income tax on qualifying general earnings of the employer’s employees for a tax year and for any subsequent tax years[^key-5c51dbe6bce26d9c92a650b7f5a3031d].

(2) In relation to qualifying general earnings included in the agreement, the employer is—

- (a) accountable to HMRC[^key-fc5a1115617cd1cafff9b879fa8e41f1] in accordance with the terms of the agreement and this Part, and
- (b) not accountable in accordance with the rest of these Regulations.

(3) Such an agreement is referred to as a PAYE settlement agreement (“PSA”).

(4)[^key-e3eaf28a81309d09ab239f1b5b58c807] A PSA will continue to have effect until cancelled.
