## 147J. Recovery of overpaid apprenticeship levy by an employer[^c-937f54e360ea7aaf085cc7de2b061d0b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies where a person has overpaid apprenticeship levy in a tax year.

(2) The person must set off any overpaid apprenticeship levy against any amount which that person is liable to pay under these Regulations before making a claim to HMRC for a refund of the overpaid levy.
