## 147B. Interpretation of Part 7A[^c-937f54e360ea7aaf085cc7de2b061d0b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “monthly pay bill” means the total amount of earnings paid by a person in a tax month on which Class 1 secondary contributions would be payable[^c-00f7d7de39cc364a3b43f051a0cf0932] but for the condition in section 6(1)(b) of the Contributions and Benefits Act (liability for Class 1 contributions).
