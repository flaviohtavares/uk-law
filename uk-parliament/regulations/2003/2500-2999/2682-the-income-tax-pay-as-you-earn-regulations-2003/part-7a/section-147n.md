# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 147N

## 147N. Retention by employer of apprenticeship levy records[^key-937f54e360ea7aaf085cc7de2b061d0b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An employer must keep and preserve for not less than three years after the end of the tax year to which they relate all apprenticeship levy records which are not required to be sent to HMRC under regulation 147D.

(2) The duty under paragraph (1) to preserve apprenticeship levy records may be discharged by preserving them in any form or by any means.

(3) In this regulation, “apprenticeship levy records” means documents relating to the calculation of the amount of apprenticeship levy payable by the employer.
