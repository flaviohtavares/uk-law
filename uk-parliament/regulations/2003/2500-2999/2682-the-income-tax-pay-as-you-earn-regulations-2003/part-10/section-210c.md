## 210C. Appeals and interest[^key-142cf3b5a2a60c9616f10046cd34cb11]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An employer is not liable to a penalty if the employer had a reasonable excuse for failing to comply with regulation 205 ... which had not ceased at the time the ... specified information was delivered.

(2) A notice of appeal against a determination under section 100 of TMA of a penalty can only be on the grounds that—

- (a) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (aa)[^key-e16cef74c03fffe2dba51918e59bea17] the employer is not regarded as an employer the purposes of this Chapter
- (b) the employer did comply with regulation 205 ...,
- (c) the amount of the penalty is incorrect, or
- (d) paragraph (1) applies.

(3) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Section 103A of TMA (interest on penalties) applies to this penalty.
