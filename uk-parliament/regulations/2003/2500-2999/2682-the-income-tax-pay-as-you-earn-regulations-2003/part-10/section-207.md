## 207. Specified information

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-da024f0fe6dfa7085989152e745cb85d] In this Chapter, “specified information” means—

- (a) Part 1 of Form P45;
- (b) Part 3 of Form P45;
- (c) Form P46;
- (d) Form P46(Pen);
- (e)[^c-df3b1a622507daa6a6be921588105743] the return and accompanying information required by regulation 73 (annual return of relevant payments liable to deduction of tax (Forms P35 and P14)).
- (f)[^c-741e3049f493166546c136bcf6e81fb7] the return and accompanying information required by regulation 85 (employers: annual return of other earnings (Form P11D)).
- (e) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^c-a097e2b0b5f1bf4cb18fd2dd979504f2] But the information described in paragraph (1)(f) is not “**specified information**” in relation to an employer who has ceased to carry on business.

(1B) In paragraph (1A), “**business**” includes any trade, concern or undertaking.
