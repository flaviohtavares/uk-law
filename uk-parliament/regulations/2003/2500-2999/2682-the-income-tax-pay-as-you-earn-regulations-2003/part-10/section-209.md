## 209. Standards of accuracy and completeness[^key-904414869b79d306017e9b5e146d6769]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any ...specified information delivered by a method of electronic communications must meet the standards of accuracy and completeness set by specific or general directions given by the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs.

(2) Any ... specified information which fails to meet those standards must be treated as not having been delivered.
