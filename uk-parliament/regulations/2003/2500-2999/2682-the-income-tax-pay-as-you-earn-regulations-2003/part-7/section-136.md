# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 136

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/2682
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 136. Deduction of tax

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On making any payment of holiday pay to a recipient, a fund must deduct income tax at the basic rate in force at the time the payment is made.
