# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 133B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/2682
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133B. Application of other Parts[^c-b938ba14786892f1be33cb618079d081]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Parts 2 (codes) and 3 (deduction and repayment of tax) do not apply to lump sums.

(2) The rest of these Regulations apply as if Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs had issued a code in respect of a social security pension lump sum at either—

- (a) the rate notified to the Department by the recipient pursuant to regulation 133D, or
- (b) 20%[^c-48a4e2e9c55b1a54a630804af6db7895], if the recipient has not notified the Department of a rate.
