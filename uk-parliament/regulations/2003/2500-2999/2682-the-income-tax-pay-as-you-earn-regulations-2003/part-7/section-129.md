# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 129

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/2682
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Certificate of tax deducted

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On making any payment of reserve pay from which tax is deducted, the Ministry may, and if the reservist so requires must, give the reservist a certificate showing the following particulars.

(2) The particulars are—

- (a) the reservist’s name,
- (b) the reservist’s national insurance number, if known,
- (c) the date of the payment,
- (d) the amount of the payment, and
- (e) the amount of tax deducted.
