## 133C. Determination of Rate of Deduction[^c-b938ba14786892f1be33cb618079d081]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On making any payment of a lump sum to a recipient during a tax year, the Department must deduct income tax at the [^c-9b8f730b61e0e44fdcf4978f3195ffbf]... rate calculated in accordance with sub-paragraph (2).

(2) For the purposes of this chapter the [^c-356914777daf52eb5c50942dbf279f84]... rate is either—

- (a) the rate notified to the Department by the recipient in accordance with regulation 133D, or
- (b) in default of any such notification, 20%[^c-259606d5732822abcaab34f8edc08d24].
