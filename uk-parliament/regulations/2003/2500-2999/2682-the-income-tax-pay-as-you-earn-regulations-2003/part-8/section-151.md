# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 151

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/2682

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 151. Obtaining the claimant’s Form P45

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A claimant who has Parts 2 and 3 of Form P45 must deliver them to the Department on making a claim for a jobseeker’s allowance which includes taxable jobseeker’s allowance.

(2) If, on making a claim, the claimant declares that the claimant’s last employer did not provide Parts 2 and 3 of Form P45, the Department may require the employer to deliver them to a specified office of the Department.
