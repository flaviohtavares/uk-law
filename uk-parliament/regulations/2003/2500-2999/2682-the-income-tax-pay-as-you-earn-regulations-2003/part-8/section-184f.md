## 184F. No Form P45: deductions working sheet and return[^c-c08c73bf913dc1fcb9d8153ea8ca5d9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In a case falling within regulation 184D(3) (no Form P45), the Department must—

- (a) prepare the deductions working sheet within 14 days of the award of a taxable employment and support allowance, and
- (b) record the emergency code as the claimant’s code.
