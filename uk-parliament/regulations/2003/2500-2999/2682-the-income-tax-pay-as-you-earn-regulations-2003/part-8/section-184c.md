# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 184C

## 184C. Obtaining the claimant’s Form P45[^c-c08c73bf913dc1fcb9d8153ea8ca5d9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A claimant who has Parts 2 and 3 of Form P45 must deliver them to the Department on making a claim for employment and support allowance which includes taxable employment and support allowance.

(2) If on making a claim, the claimant declares that the claimant’s last employer did not provide Parts 2 and 3 of Form P45, the Department may require the employer to deliver them to a specified officer of the Department.
