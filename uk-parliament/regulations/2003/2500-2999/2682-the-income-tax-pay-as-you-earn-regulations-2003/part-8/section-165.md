## 165. Scope of Chapter 2

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies only to Chapter 2 claimants (as defined by regulation 148).

(2) Except for regulation 148 (interpretation), Chapter 1 does not apply to Chapter 2 claimants.
