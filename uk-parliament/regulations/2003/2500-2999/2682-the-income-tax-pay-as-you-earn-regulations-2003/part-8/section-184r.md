## 184R. Recording the amount of taxable employment and support allowance[^c-c08c73bf913dc1fcb9d8153ea8ca5d9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Whenever a payment of employment and support allowance is made, the Department must record the taxable employment and support allowance included in the payment.
