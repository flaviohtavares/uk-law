# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 184P

## 184P. Claimant’s code etc to be used for calculations[^key-c08c73bf913dc1fcb9d8153ea8ca5d9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if, in respect of a claimant, the Department receives notification from HMRC of—

- (a) a code or amended code,
- (b) total payments to date, or
- (c) total net tax deducted.

(2) The Department must record that notification in substitution for any previous record and use it for the purpose of all calculations required under this Chapter.
