# The Income Tax (Pay As You Earn) Regulations 2003, section 166

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-07-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2003/2682
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 166. Jobseeker’s allowance paid directly to claimant

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This regulation applies if the Department makes a payment of taxable jobseeker’s allowance directly to a Chapter 2 claimant.

(2) The Department must—

- (a) record the amount, and
- (b) pay the full sum without any deduction or repayment of income tax.
