# European Convention on Cinematographic Co-production (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2630

CINEMAS AND FILMS

European Convention on Cinematographic Co-production (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2003

Made

8th October 2003

Coming into force

1st November 2003

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 8th day of October 2003

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by paragraph 4(7) of Schedule 1 to the Films Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the European Convention on Cinematographic Co-production (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2003.

(2) This Order shall come into force on 1st November 2003.

**2.** In this Order, “the Schedule” means the Schedule to the European Convention on Cinematographic Co-production Order 1994[^f00002].

**3.** The Schedule shall be amended by inserting at the appropriate place in alphabetical order the words “Former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia”.

A K Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

The European Convention on Cinematographic Co-production Order 1994 (“the Principal Order”) provides that films made in accordance with the European Convention on Cinematographic Co-production (Cm 2495) are to be treated as British films for the purposes of Schedule 1 to the Films Act 1985. For the Convention to apply where there are only two co-producers one must be established in the United Kingdom and the other in one of the countries set out in the Schedule to the Principal Order. For the Convention to apply where there are three or more co-producers one must be established in the United Kingdom and at least two others in different countries set out in the Schedule to the Principal Order.

This Order amends the Schedule to the Principal Order by adding the Former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia to the countries set out in it.

Certain tax benefits may accrue in respect of a film which is a British film.
