# The Pensions Increase (Pension Schemes for David Calvert-Smith) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2623

PENSIONS

The Pensions Increase (Pension Schemes for David Calvert-Smith) Regulations 2003

Made

9th October 2003

Laid before Parliament

9th October 2003

Coming into force

1st November 2003

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(2) of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002], hereby make the following Regulations—

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Pensions Increase (Pension Schemes for David Calvert-Smith) Regulations 2003, and shall come into force on 1st November 2003.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “the 1971 Act” means the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971;
- “pension” means a pension within the meaning of the 1971 Act.

## 3. Pensions to which the 1971 Act will apply

The 1971 Act shall have effect in relation to any pensions payable under the Pension Schemes for Mr David Calvert-Smith (being schemes made under section 1 of the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00003], as if they were pensions specified in Part I of Schedule 2 to the 1971 Act.

Jim Murphy

Derek Twigg

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

9th October 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations apply the provisions of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 to any pensions payable for the Director of Public Prosecutions, Mr David Calvert-Smith, under schemes made under section 1 of the Superannuation Act 1972.
