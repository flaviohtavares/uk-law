# The Advanced Television Services (Amendment) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2003/1901](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/1901) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2003 No. 2750

ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS

The Advanced Television Services (Amendment) Regulations 2003

Made

27th October 2003

Laid before Parliament

28th October 2003

Coming into force

17th November 2003

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to advanced television services, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by that section, hereby makes the following Regulations—

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Advanced Television Services (Amendment) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 17th November 2003.

**2.** In the definition of “wide-screen television service” in regulation 3 of the Advanced Television Services Regulations 2003[^f00003], for “height-width ratio” there shall be substituted “width-height ratio”.

Stephen Timms,

Minister of State for Energy, E-Commerce and Postal Services,

Department of Trade and Industry

27th October 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Regulation 3 of the Advanced Television Services Regulations 2003 incorrectly defines “wide-screen television service” by reference to a height-width ratio of 16:9. These Regulations correct this so as to refer to a width-height ratio of 16:9.
