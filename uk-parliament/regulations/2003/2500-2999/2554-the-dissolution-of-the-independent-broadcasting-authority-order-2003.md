# The Dissolution of the Independent Broadcasting Authority Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2554

BROADCASTING

The Dissolution of the Independent Broadcasting Authority Order 2003

Made

2nd October 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 127(3) and (6) of the Broadcasting Act 1990 [^f00001], and having consulted the Independent Broadcasting Authority, the Independent Television Commission, the Radio Authority and National Transcommunications Limited, hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Dissolution of the Independent Broadcasting Authority Order 2003.

**2.** The Independent Broadcasting Authority are hereby dissolved with effect from 2nd October 2003.

Andrew McIntosh

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

2nd October 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

The property, rights and liabilities of the Independent Broadcasting Authority were divided between the Independent Television Commission, the Radio Authority and National Transcommunications Limited on 1 January 1991. The Secretary of State having satisfied herself, after consulting the Independent Broadcasting Authority, the Independent Television Commission, the Radio Authority and National Transcommunications Limited, that nothing further remains to be done by the Independent Broadcasting Authority by virtue of Schedule 9 to the Broadcasting Act 1990, dissolves the Independent Broadcasting Authority by means of this Order.
