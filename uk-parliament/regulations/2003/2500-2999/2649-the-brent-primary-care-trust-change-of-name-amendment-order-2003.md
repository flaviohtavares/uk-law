# The Brent Primary Care Trust (Change of Name) Amendment Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2649

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The Brent Primary Care Trust (Change of Name) Amendment Order 2003

Made

7th October 2003

Coming into force

21st October 2003

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 16A(1) and (2) and 126(3) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 5A to, the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Brent Primary Care Trust (Change of Name) Amendment Order 2003 and shall come into force on 21st October 2003.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Establishment Order” means the Brent Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2002[^f00002]; and
- “the trust” means the Primary Care Trust established by the Establishment Order.

## 2. Change of name of the trust

The trust shall be called the Brent Teaching Primary Care Trust instead of the Brent Primary Care Trust, and accordingly—

- (a) in article 1(2) of the Establishment Order (citation, commencement and interpretation), in the definition of “the trust”; and
- (b) in article 2(1) of the Establishment Order (establishment, operational date and name of the Primary Care Trust),

- “Brent” shall be replaced by “Brent Teaching”.

**3.** The change of name effected by article 2 of this Order shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person; or
- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before, on, or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to the trust by its previous name,

and all instruments and other documents which refer to the trust under its previous name shall be construed as referring to it under its new name.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

John Hutton

Minister of State,

Department of Health

7th October 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Brent Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2002, which established the Brent Primary Care Trust.

Article 2 of the Order changes the name of the trust from the Brent Primary Care Trust to the Brent Teaching Primary Care Trust, and makes consequential amendments to the Establishment Order.
