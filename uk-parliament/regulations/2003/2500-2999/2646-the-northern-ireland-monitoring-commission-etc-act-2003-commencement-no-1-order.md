# The Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2646 (c 101 )

NORTHERN IRELAND

CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

The Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2003

Made

10th October 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 12(2) of the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2003.

**2.** Section 1 of the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003 shall come into force on 13th October 2003.

Paul Murphy

One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

Northern Ireland Office

10th October 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 1 of the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act [2003(c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/25) on 13th October 2003.
