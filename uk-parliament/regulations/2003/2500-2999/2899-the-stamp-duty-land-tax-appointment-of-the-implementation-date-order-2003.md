# The Stamp Duty Land Tax (Appointment of the Implementation Date) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2899 (C. 106)

TAXES

The Stamp Duty Land Tax (Appointment of the Implementation Date) Order 2003

Made

12th November 2003

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 19 to the Finance Act 2003[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Stamp Duty Land Tax (Appointment of the Implementation Date) Order 2003.

## 2. Implementation date

The date appointed as the implementation date for the purposes of stamp duty land tax is 1st December 2003.

Nick Ainger

Derek Twigg

Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

12th November 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st December 2003 as the implementation date for the purposes of stamp duty land tax, introduced by Part 4 of the Finance Act 2003.
