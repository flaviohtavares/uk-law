# The Transport Act 2000 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1694 (C.71)

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Transport Act 2000 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2003

Made

7th July 2003

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 275(1) of the Transport Act 2000[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Transport Act 2000 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2003.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 29th July 2003

Section 228 of the Transport Act 2000 shall come into force on 29th July 2003.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Kim Howells

Minister of State,

Department for Transport

7th July 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 29th July 2003 section 228 of the Transport Act 2000 which provides for the amendment of certain provisions of sections 76 and 77 of the Railways Act 1993, which set out the general duties of the Rail Passengers’ Council and the Rail Passengers’ Committees respectively.

(This note is not part of the Order)
