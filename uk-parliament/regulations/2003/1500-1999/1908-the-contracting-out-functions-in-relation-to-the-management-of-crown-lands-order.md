# The Contracting Out (Functions in relation to the Management of Crown Lands) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1908

CONTRACTING OUT

The Contracting Out (Functions in relation to the Management of Crown Lands) Order 2003

Made

15th July 2003

Coming into force

16th July 2003

Whereas a draft of the Order has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament pursuant to section 77(2) of the Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994[^f00001];

Now therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 69 of that Act hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Contracting Out (Functions in relation to the Management of Crown Lands) Order 2003 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Contracting out of functions

The functions now vested in the Secretary of State under section 22 of the Crown Lands Act 1851[^f00002] in relation to Hampton Court Gardens, Green, and Road and Hampton Court Park and Kensington Gardens may be exercised by, or by employees of, such person (if any) as may be authorised in that behalf by the Secretary of State.

Andrew McIntosh

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

15th July 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order enables the Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport to authorise another person (or that person’s employees) to perform those management functions, in relation to Hampton Court Gardens, Green and Road, and Hampton Court Park and Kensington Gardens which are properties owned by the Sovereign in right of the Crown, and which are vested in the Secretary of State by section 22 of the Crown Lands Act 1851.
