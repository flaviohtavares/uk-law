# The Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1647

EDUCATION, ENGLAND AND WALES

The Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2003

Made

24th June 2003

Laid before Parliament

2nd July 2003

Coming into force

1st August 2003

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(2) and (7) of and paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Education (Student Loans) Act 1990[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:–

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> August 2003.

(2) These Regulations do not apply in or as regards Scotland [^f00002].

## 2. Amendments

(1) The Education (Student Loans) Regulations 1998[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 6(1) to (4) for each of the figures in the first column below wherever it appears there shall be substituted the corresponding figure in the second column below:

| ***First column*** | ***Second Column*** |
| --- | --- |
| 1,075 | 1,100 |
| 1,725 | 1,765 |
| 1,465 | 1,500 |
| 2,365 | 2,420 |
| 1,400 | 1,430 |
| 1,915 | 1,960 |

Alan Johnson

Minister of State,

Department of Education and Skills

24th June 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations which come into force on 1<sup>st</sup> August 2003 amend the Education (Student Loans) Regulations 1998 which govern mortgage style repayment loans made under the Education (Student Loans) Act 1990, to students who, for the most part, began their courses before 1<sup>st</sup> August 1998.

These Regulations increase the maximum amounts which may be lent to students in relation to an academic year in line with inflation.
