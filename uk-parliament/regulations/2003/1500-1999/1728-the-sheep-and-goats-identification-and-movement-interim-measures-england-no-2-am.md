# The Sheep and Goats Identification and Movement (Interim Measures) (England) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 3) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1728

ANIMALS, ENGLAND

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Sheep and Goats Identification and Movement (Interim Measures) (England) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 3) Order 2003

Made

10th July 2003

Coming into force

1st August 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by sections 1 and 8(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Sheep and Goats Identification and Movement (Interim Measures) (England) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 3) Order 2003 and comes into force on 1st August 2003.

## 2. Amendment to the Sheep and Goats Identification and Movement (Interim Measures) (England) (No. 2) Order 2002

The Sheep and Goats Identification and Movement (Interim Measures) (England) (No. 2) Order 2002[^f00002] is amended by deleting the words “and shall cease to have effect on 1st August 2003” in article 1(2).

Whitty

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

10th July 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Sheep and Goats Identification and Movement (Interim Measures) (England) (No. 2) Order 2002 by deleting the provision whereby that Order ceased to have effect on 1st August 2003.
