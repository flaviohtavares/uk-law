# The Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Modification) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1890

NORTHERN IRELAND

SOCIAL SECURITY

The Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Modification) Order 2003

Made

17th July 2003

Laid before Parliament

29th July 2003

Coming into force

19th August 2003

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 17th day of July 2003

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

WHEREAS Her Majesty deems it expedient to modify the Northern Ireland Act 1998[^f00001] and to do so by Order in Council in pursuance of section 87(7) and (11) of that Act:

NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by virtue and in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 87(7) and (11) of the said Act and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

## 1. Citation and Commencement

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Modification) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 19th August 2003.

## 2. Modification of the Northern Ireland Act 1998

In subsection (6) of section 87 of the Northern Ireland Act 1998 at the end there shall be added the following paragraph—

> - (q) the State Pension Credit Act 2002[^f00002] and the State Pension Credit Act (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00003]

.

A.K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds the State Pension Credit Act [2002 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/16) and the State Pension Credit Act (Northern Ireland) [2002 (c. 14 (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2002/14)) to the legislation listed in section 87(6) of the Northern Ireland Act [1998 (c. 47)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/47). The effect of this amendment will be to render those Acts subject to the provisions in that section concerning consultation and co-ordination between the Secretary of State and the Northern Ireland Minister having responsibility for social security.

This Order does not impose any charge on business.
