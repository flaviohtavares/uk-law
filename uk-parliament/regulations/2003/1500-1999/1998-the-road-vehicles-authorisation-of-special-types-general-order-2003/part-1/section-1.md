# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 1

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-e48ec60c4f6b01ccf387804cdd741c3f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003.

(2) Except as stated in paragraph (3), this Order comes into force on 25th August 2003.

(3) Paragraphs 15 to 18 of Schedule 2 come into force on 1st December 2004.
