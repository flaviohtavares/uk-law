# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 8

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 8. Application of this Order[^key-81f734c1aca42a03acb96adc3e928260]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Order applies only to motor vehicles or trailers—

- (a) that do not comply in all respects with the standard construction and use requirements; and
- (b) that fall within a recognised category of special vehicles.

(2) In this Order “recognised category of special vehicles” means a description of vehicles that is stated by a provision of this Order to be a recognised category of special vehicles.

(3) In paragraph (1), “standard construction and use requirements”, in relation to a motor vehicle or trailer, means the requirements of such of the regulations made under section 41 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00020] as would, apart from this Order, apply to that motor vehicle or trailer.
