# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 2

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2. Revocation[^c-90bfc3c5877baaf11dd18a1645e8d703]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The following instruments are revoked—

- (a) Motor Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) General Order 1979[^f00002];
- (b) Motor Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (Amendment) Order 1984[^f00003];
- (c) Motor Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (Amendment) Order 1986[^f00004];
- (d) Motor Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (Amendment) Order 1987[^f00005];
- (e) Motor Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (Amendment) (No 2) Order 1987[^f00006];
- (f) Motor Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (Amendment) Order 1989[^f00007];
- (g) Motor Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (Amendment) Order 1995[^f00008];
- (h) Motor Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (Amendment) Order 1998[^f00009];
- (i) Motor Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (Amendment) (No 2) Order 1998[^f00010].
