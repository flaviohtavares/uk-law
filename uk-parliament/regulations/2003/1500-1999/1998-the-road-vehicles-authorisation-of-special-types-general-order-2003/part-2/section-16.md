# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 16

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 16. Visibility and marking of forward, rearward and lateral projections of loads etc[^key-4c6627a145200a877a0a426cb58b3242]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 8 (which makes provision as to the visibility and marking of projections exceeding a certain length or width) applies in relation to loads carried on a Part 2 vehicle or Part 2 vehicle-combination.
