# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 14

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14. Forward and rearward projections: attendants[^c-8a2d2cee2cb129216cc28a79f4370dfe]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If paragraph (2) or (3) applies, the user of a Part 2 vehicle or Part 2 vehicle-combination must ensure that the vehicle or vehicle-combination is accompanied during the journey by one or more attendants employed in accordance with Schedule 6.

(2) This paragraph applies where a Part 2 vehicle or Part 2 vehicle-combination is carrying a load and the length of any forward projection of the load exceeds 2 metres.

(3) This paragraph applies where a Part 2 vehicle or Part 2 vehicle-combination is carrying a load and the length of any rearward projection of the load exceeds 3.05 metres.
