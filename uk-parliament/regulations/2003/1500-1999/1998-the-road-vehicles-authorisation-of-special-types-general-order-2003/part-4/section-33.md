## 33. Local excavation vehicles: authorisation requirements[^key-bfd5053afb6df8bf34c76f7a001a73c0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The authorisation requirements applicable to local excavation vehicles are—

- (a) the requirements specified in articles 34 and 35; and
- (b) the requirements specified in Schedule 10.
