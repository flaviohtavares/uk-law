## 32. Local excavation vehicles: recognised category[^c-cb0fd42e82a68d10cb74488e1b11bfa8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Local excavation vehicles are a recognised category of special vehicles.

(2) Local excavation vehicles are defined in paragraph 1 of Schedule 10.
