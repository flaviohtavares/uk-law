# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 29

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29. Motor vehicles or trailers carrying loads of exceptional width: authorisation requirements[^key-fc956ba08366a6d47d79fedbccaa7256]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The authorisation requirements applicable to vehicles falling within the recognised category of special vehicles mentioned in article 28(1) are—

- (a) the requirements specified in articles 30 and 31;
- (b) the Construction and Use Regulations, apart from regulation 82(1) and (2);
- (c) the Authorised Weight Regulations; and
- (d) the Lighting Regulations.
