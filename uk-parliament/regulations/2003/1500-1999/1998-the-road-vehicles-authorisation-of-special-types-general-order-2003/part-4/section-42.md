# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 42

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 42. Track-laying vehicles: authorisation requirements[^c-a667c308d5e2ddca20d6da03f23fce88]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The authorisation requirements applicable to vehicles falling within the recognised category of special vehicles mentioned in article 41(1) are—

- (a) the requirements specified in articles 43 and 44;
- (b) regulation 100 of the Construction and Use Regulations (maintenance and use so as not to be a danger);
- (c) the Authorised Weight Regulations; and
- (d) the Lighting Regulations.
