# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 41

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 41. Track-laying vehicles: recognised category[^key-9324bb99fc4c9d685d59f262976e79e1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Track-laying motor vehicles or trailers are a recognised category of special vehicles.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not include any track-laying vehicle that falls within any other recognised category of special vehicles.
