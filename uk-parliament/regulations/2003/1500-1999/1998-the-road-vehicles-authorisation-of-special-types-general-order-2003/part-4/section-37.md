# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 37

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. Vehicles for tests, trials or non-UK use etc: authorisation requirements[^key-ff85043e074455675f584cd988cb3c9e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The authorisation requirements applicable to vehicles falling within any of the recognised categories of special vehicles mentioned in article 36(1) are—

- (a) the requirements specified in articles 38 to 40; and
- (b) the requirements specified in Schedule 11.
