# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 58

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 58. Longer semi-trailers – legislation which applies as authorisation requirements[^c-bd9073478252496c0301552d6f639c81]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This article specifies the legislation that applies to longer semi-trailers as authorisation requirements.

(2) The legislation is—

- (a) the Construction and Use Regulations, apart from regulation 7 (length) and regulation 13A(4) and 13B(6);
- (b) the Authorised Weight Regulations;
- (c) the Lighting Regulations.
