# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 56

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 56. Vehicles propelled by natural gas[^key-108700554efded524f682704c898bb55]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Vehicles propelled by compressed natural gas are a recognised category of special vehicles.

(2) The authorisation requirements applicable to vehicles falling within the recognised category of special vehicles mentioned in paragraph (1) are the requirements specified in Schedule 12.
