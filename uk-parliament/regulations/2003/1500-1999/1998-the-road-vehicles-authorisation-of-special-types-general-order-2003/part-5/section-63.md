# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 63

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 63. Longer semi-trailers - application of section 53(1) and (2) of the 1988 Act[^key-bd9073478252496c0301552d6f639c81]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The provisions of section 53(1) and (2) of the 1988 Act apply to a longer semi-trailer authorised for use on roads by this order, except where the longer semi-trailer is used for a purpose specified by regulation 44(1)(a) to (d) or (f) to (h) of the Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) Regulations 1988;
