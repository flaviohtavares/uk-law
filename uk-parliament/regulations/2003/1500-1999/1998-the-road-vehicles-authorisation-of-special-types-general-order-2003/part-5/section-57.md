# The Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) (General) Order 2003, section 57

> Text as at 2023-05-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 57. Longer semi-trailers[^key-bd9073478252496c0301552d6f639c81]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Longer semi-trailers are a recognised category of special vehicles.

(2) The authorisation requirements applicable to longer semi-trailers are the requirements specified in articles 58 to 62.
