# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (No.2) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1637

SPORTS GROUNDS AND SPORTING EVENTS

The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (No.2) Order 2003

Made

24th June 2003

Laid before Parliament

25th June 2003

Coming into force

19th July 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 1(1) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00001], and after such consultation as is mentioned in section 18(4) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (No.2) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 19th July 2003.

## 2. Designation

The sports ground specified in the Schedule to this Order (being a sports ground in England at which association football matches are played, which is occupied by a club which is a member of the Football League Limited or the Football Association Premier League Limited, and which in the opinion of the Secretary of State has accommodation for more than 5,000 spectators) is hereby designated as a sports ground requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00002].

Tessa Jowell

Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport

24th June 2003
