# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed in substitution of the SI of the same number which was printed with the incorrect title and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument

2003 No. 1866

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2003

Made

17th July 2003

Laid before Parliament

18th July 2003

Coming into force

10th August 2003

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 54 and 61 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 10th August 2003.

## 2. Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) Regulations 1982

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) Regulations 1982[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following paragraphs—

- (a) in regulation 2 (general interpretation)—
  - (i) in paragraph (1)—
    - (aa) before the definition of “the Construction and Use Regulations” there shall be inserted—

      > - “Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156)” means Council Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156) of 6th February 1970 on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to the type approval of motor vehicles and their trailers[^f00003];
      > - “Directive [70/157/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0157)” means Council Directive [70/157/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0157) of 6th February 1970 on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to the permissible sound level and the exhaust system of motor vehicles[^f00004];
      > - “Directive [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220)” means Council Directive [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220) of 20th March 1970 on the approximation of the laws of the Member States on measures to be taken against air pollution by emissions from motor vehicles[^f00005];
      > - “Directive [88/77/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0077)” means Council Directive [88/77/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0077) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to the measures to be taken against the emission of gaseous pollutants for diesel engines for use in vehicles[^f00006];

      ;
    - (bb) in the definition of “emissions or noise item” for ““exhaust emissions” or “noise and silencers”” there shall be substituted ““emissions”, “on-board diagnostic system”, “diesel emissions” or “sound level””;
    - (cc) in the definition of “Framework Directive vehicle” for “Council Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156)” and the remainder of the definition there shall be substituted “Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156)”;
    - (dd) after the definition of “recovery vehicle” there shall be inserted—

      > - “relevant speed” has the meaning given in regulation 36B(15) of the Construction and Use Regulations;
      > - “road sweeper” means a vehicle constructed or adapted for the purpose of road cleansing, the collection or disposal of refuse or the collection or disposal of the contents of gullies and which—
      >   - (i) is incapable by reason of its construction of a speed of more than 20 miles per hour on the level under its own power; and
      >   - (ii) has an inside track width of less than 810 millimetres;

      ;
  - (ii) after paragraph (2) there shall be inserted—

    > (2A) Any reference in these Regulations to a Community instrument is a reference to that instrument as amended on the date the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 are made.

    ; and
  - (iii) after paragraph (5) there shall be inserted—

    > (6) In Part I of Schedule 1 to these Regulations—
    >
    > - (a) a reference to a vehicle of category N, N1, N2 or N3 (whether of a specified class or otherwise) is a reference to a vehicle of category N, N1, N2 or N3 as appropriate, within the meaning of Annex II to Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156); and
    > - (b) a reference to a vehicle of class I, II or III of any category referred to in sub-paragraph (a) is a reference to a vehicle of class I, II or III, as appropriate, of that category within the meaning of the said Annex II.

    ;
- (b) in regulation 3 (application of regulations)—
  - (i) in paragraph (1)(a)(i) for “three” there shall be substituted “four”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (2) “or” after sub-paragraph (t) shall be omitted and after sub-paragraph (u) there shall be inserted—

    > ; or
    >
    > - (v) a road sweeper.

    ;
- (c) in regulation 4 (application of type approval requirements)—
  - (i) in paragraph (1) for sub-paragraph (b)(i) there shall be substituted—

    > - (b)
    >   - (i) on and after the date these Regulations are amended by the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 or any later date specified in column (6) of Part I of Schedule 1, and

    ;
  - (ii) in paragraph (5) there shall be omitted “; and for the purposes” and the remainder of the paragraph;
  - (iii) paragraphs (8A), (9) and (12) and the second paragraph (13) shall be omitted; and
  - (iv) in paragraph (14) for “schedules 1B and” there shall be substituted “schedule”;
- (d) the content of the Schedule to these Regulations shall be substituted for Schedule 1;
- (e) Schedules 1A and 1B shall be omitted; and
- (f) in Schedule 1C, in Part I, in paragraph 1(1) “(other than item 2L)” shall be omitted.

## 3. Saving provision

(1) The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) Regulations 1982 shall continue to apply in relation to a relevant vehicle or a relevant part as if these Regulations had not been made.

(2) For the purposes of this regulation—

- (a) “relevant vehicle” means a vehicle in relation to which a certificate was issued under section 47 of the Road Traffic Act 1972[^f00007] or under section 55 or 58 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 before the date on which these Regulations come into force;
- (b) “relevant part” means a vehicle part in relation to which a certificate was issued under section 47 of the Road Traffic Act 1972 or under section 55 or 58 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 before the date on which these Regulations come into force.

> **PART II — REQUIREMENTS REFERRED TO IN PART I**
>
> **1. Sound level (Item 4A in Part I of this Schedule)**
>
> The sound levels when measured in accordance with the requirements specified in paragraphs 5.2 and 5.3 of Annex I to Directive 70/157 shall not exceed—
>
> - (a) for vehicles with an engine power of less than 75 kilowatts, 84 decibels (A); and
> - (b) for vehicles with an engine power of not less than 75 kilowatts, 86 decibels (A).
>
> **2. Speed Limiters (Item 9 in Part I of this Schedule)**
>
> The vehicle must have a maximum speed of less than 60 miles per hour.
>
> **3. Interpretation**
>
> In this Part of this Schedule, “maximum speed” has the meaning given in regulation 3(2) of the Construction and Use Regulations.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) Regulations 1982 (“the principal Regulations”).

These Regulations—

- (a) replace Schedule 1 to the principal Regulations with a Schedule of type approval requirements which are applicable from the date the principal Regulations are amended by these Regulations and make amendments in consequence thereof (*regulations 2(a), (c), (d), (e) and (f)*);
- (b) exclude road sweepers and vehicles having less than four wheels from the application of the principal Regulations (*regulation 2(a)(i)(dd) and (b)*); and
- (c) provide, in relation to a vehicle or part in respect of which a type approval certificate or Minister’s approval certificate was issued before these Regulations come into force, that the principal Regulations shall continue to have effect as if these Regulations had not been made (*regulation 3*).

In substituting the Schedule of type approval requirements referred to in sub-paragraph (a) above these Regulations also transpose into UK law the provisions of Council Directive [92/23/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0023) of 31st March 1992 relating to tyres for motor vehicles and their trailers and to their fitting (as amended by Directive [2001/43/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0043)) and Directive [2000/40/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0040) of 26th June 2000 on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to the front underrun protection of motor vehicles and amending Council Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156) in so far as these Directives prohibit the granting of national type approval if their requirements are not met.

The transposition details set out in the above paragraph are provided in place of a separate Transposition Note.

Copies of the above Directives and of the others referred to in the Regulations may be obtained from the Stationery Office.
