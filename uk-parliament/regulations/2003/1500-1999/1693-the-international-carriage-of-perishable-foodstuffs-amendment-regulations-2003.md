# The International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1693

ROAD TRAFFIC

The International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 2003

Made

4th July 2003

Laid before Parliament

8th July 2003

Coming into force

1st August 2003

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 3(1) and 4(1) of the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1976[^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 1st August 2003.

**2.** The International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Regulations 1985 (“ the 1985 Regulations”)[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4 below.

**3.** In regulation 25 of the 1985 Regulations—

- (a) for “£5” there shall be substituted “£40”, and
- (b) for “or” there shall be substituted “and the fee of £5 is to be paid for the issue of”.

**4.** (1) The Schedule to the 1985 Regulations shall be amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1 of Part I, for “£150” there shall be substituted “ £165”.

(3) In paragraph 2 of Part I, for “£405” there shall be substituted “ £450”.

(4) In paragraph 3 of Part I, for “£50” there shall be substituted “ £60”.

(5) In paragraph 1 of Part II, for “£850” there shall be substituted “ £925”.

(6) Paragraph 3 of Part II shall be deleted.

(7) In paragraph 1 of Part III, for “£80” there shall be substituted “ £90”.

(8) In paragraph 2 of Part III, for “£240” there shall be substituted “ £350”.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

David Jamieson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Transport

4th July 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations change some fees payable under the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Regulations 1985.

The fee for certification of a type vehicle (formerly £245) is cancelled.
