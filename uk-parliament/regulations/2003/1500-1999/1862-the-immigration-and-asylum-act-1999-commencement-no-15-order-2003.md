# The Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (Commencement No. 15) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 1862 (C.76)

IMMIGRATION

The Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (Commencement No. 15) Order 2003

Made

16th July 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 170(4) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (Commencement No. 15) Order 2003.

**2.** So far as it is not already in force[^f00002], section 165 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 shall come into force on 1st August 2003.

Beverley Hughes

Minister of State

Home Office

16th July 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st August 2003 section 165 (procedural requirements as to applications) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, so far as that provision is not already in force.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The following provisions of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 have been brought into force on the dates shown by commencement orders before the date of this order.
