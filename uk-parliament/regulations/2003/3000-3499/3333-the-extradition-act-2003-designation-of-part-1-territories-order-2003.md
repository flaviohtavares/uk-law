# The Extradition Act 2003 (Designation of Part 1 Territories) Order 2003

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

2003 No. 3333

EXTRADITION

The Extradition Act 2003 (Designation of Part 1 Territories) Order 2003

Made

18th December 2003

Coming into force

1st January 2004

Whereas a draft of this Order has been approved by each House of Parliament;

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1 of the Extradition Act 2003 [^c5112181], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1.

This Order may be cited as the Extradition Act 2003 (Designation of Part 1 Territories) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st January 2004.

**1A..**[^key-f2fdcea98b09efc82a4866e93161c5ae] The following territories are designated for the purposes of Part 1 of the Extradition Act 2003—

- Austria,
- Belgium,
- Bulgaria,
- Croatia,
- Cyprus,
- Czech Republic,
- Denmark,
- Estonia,
- Finland,
- France,
- Germany,
- Greece,
- Hungary,
- Ireland,
- Italy,
- Latvia,
- Lithuania,
- Luxembourg,
- Malta,
- The Netherlands,
- Poland,
- Portugal,
- Romania,
- Slovakia,
- Slovenia,
- Spain,
- Sweden.

**2.**[^key-397c66994342f45f53086e09b964507b] Gibraltar is designated for the purposes of Part 1 of the Extradition Act 2003.

Caroline Flint

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State

Home Office

*(This note is not part of the Order)*

Part 1 of the Extradition Act 2003 introduces new extradition procedures. These procedures include a new statutory appeal process and a reduced involvement of the Secretary of State. Article 2 designates those territories with which the United Kingdom will be operating the new extradition procedures.
