# The European Qualifications (Health Care Professions) Regulations 2003, section 15

> Text as at 2007-03-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. The General Osteopathic Council (Registration) Rules Order of Council 1998

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The General Osteopathic Council (Registration) Rules Order of Council 1998 [^c5104321] is amended in accordance with this regulation.

(2) In the Schedule to the Order (the General Osteopathic Council (Registration) Rules 1998), in rule 3 (the form of the register), in paragraph (4), in the definition of—

- (a) “European Economic Area State”, after “Agreement”, add “ or Switzerland [^c5104331] ”;
- (b) “European Economic Area Agreement”, after “1993”, add “ and as amended by Decisions of the EEA Joint Committee Nos. 25/94 of 2nd December 1994 [^c5104341], 6/98 of 30th January 1998 [^c5104351], 87/2000 of 27th October 2000 [^c5104361], and 84/2002 of 25th June 2002 [^c5104371] ”.
