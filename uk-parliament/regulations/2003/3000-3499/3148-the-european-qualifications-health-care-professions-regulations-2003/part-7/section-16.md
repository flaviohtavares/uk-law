# The European Qualifications (Health Care Professions) Regulations 2003, section 16

> Text as at 2007-03-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. The General Osteopathic Council (Application for Registration and Fees) Rules Order of Council 2000

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The General Osteopathic Council (Application for Registration and Fees) Rules Order of Council 2000 [^c5104381] is amended in accordance with this regulation.

(2) In the Schedule to the Order (the General Osteopathic Council (Application for Registration and Fees) Rules 2000), in rule 2 (interpretation), in paragraph (1), in the definition of—

- (a) “European Economic Area State”, after “Agreement”, add “ or Switzerland ”;
- (b) “European Economic Area Agreement”, after “1993”, add “ and as amended by Decisions of the EEA Joint Committee Nos. 25/94 of 2nd December 1994, 6/98 of 30th January 1998, 87/2000 of 27th October 2000, and 84/2002 of 25th June 2002 [^c5104391] ”.
