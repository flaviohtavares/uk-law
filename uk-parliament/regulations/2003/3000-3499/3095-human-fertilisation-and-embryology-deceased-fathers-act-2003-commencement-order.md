# Human Fertilisation and Embryology (Deceased Fathers) Act 2003 (Commencement) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 3095 (C. 121)

HUMAN FERTILISATION AND EMBRYOLOGY

Human Fertilisation and Embryology (Deceased Fathers) Act 2003 (Commencement) Order 2003

Made

28th November 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4(2) of the Human Fertilisation and Embryology (Deceased Fathers) Act 2003[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Human Fertilisation and Embryology (Deceased Fathers) Act 2003 (Commencement) Order 2003.

## 2. Commencement

The Human Fertilisation and Embryology (Deceased Fathers) Act 2003, so far as not already in force, shall come into force on 1 December 2003.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Melanie Johnson

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department of Health

28th November 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1 December 2003 all the provisions of the Human Fertilisation and Embryology (Deceased Fathers) Act 2003 except section 4 which came into force on Royal Assent.
