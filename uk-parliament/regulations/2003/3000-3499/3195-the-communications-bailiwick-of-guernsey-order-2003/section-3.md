# The Communications (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Order 2003, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**3.** Article 2(b) of the Wireless Telegraphy (Guernsey) Order 1998[^f00008] (which inserted in section 3 of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1998 as extended to the Bailiwick of Guernsey a new subsection (5A)) is revoked.
