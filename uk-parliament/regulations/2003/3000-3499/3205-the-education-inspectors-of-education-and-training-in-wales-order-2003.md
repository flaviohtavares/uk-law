# The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 3205

EDUCATION, WALES

The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2003

Made

10th December 2003

Coming into force

11th December 2003

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of December 2003

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001] is pleased, by, and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 11th December 2003.

**2.** The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales or Arolgwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru with effect from 11th December 2003.

A. K. Galloway

Clerk of the Privy Council

## SCHEDULE

- Bernard William O'Reilly
- Ann Jones
- Gerard Edward Kerslake
- Rosemary Lloyd Lait
- John Frederick Thomas

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints the persons named in the Schedule Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales, or in Welsh Arolgwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru.
