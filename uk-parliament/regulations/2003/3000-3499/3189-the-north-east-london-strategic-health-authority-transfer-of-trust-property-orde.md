# The North East London Strategic Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 3189

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, ENGLAND

The North East London Strategic Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2003

Made

8th December 2003

Laid before Parliament

15th December 2003

Coming into force

5th January 2004

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 92(1) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to changes in the area and functions of the North East London Strategic Health Authority and having consulted that Authority and the trustees for the Barts and The London National Health Service Trust[^f00002] in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the North East London Strategic Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 5th January 2004.

(2) In this Order—

- “the old Trust” means the North East London Strategic Health Authority[^f00003];
- “the new Trust” means the trustees for the Barts and The London National Health Service Trust;
- “the trust property” means the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the old Trust and the new Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 20th March 2003 and on behalf of the new Trust on 21st March 2003.

## 2. Transfer of trust property

(1) On 5th January 2004 the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property[^f00004], shall be transferred from the old Trust to the new Trust.

(2) In any trust instrument relating to the trust property, any reference to the name by which the trustees of the trust property are to be known shall be construed as a reference to the new Trust and any reference to the old Trust in the name by which the charitable trust governing the trust property is to be known shall be construed as a reference to the new Trust.

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Hutton

Minister of State,

Department of Health

8th December 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the trustees for the Barts and The London National Health Service Trust, trust property held by the North East London Strategic Health Authority and makes related provision. A copy of the schedule of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
