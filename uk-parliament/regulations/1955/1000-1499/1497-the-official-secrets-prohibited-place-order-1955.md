# The Official Secrets (Prohibited Place) Order 1955

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1955 No. 1497 (S. 136)

OVERSEAS RESOURCES DEVELOPMENT

The Official Secrets (Prohibited Place) Order, 1955

Made

23rd September 1955

Coming into Operation

1st October 1955

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 of the Official Secrets Act, 1911, as amended by . section 10 of the Official Secrets Act, 1920 and as read with . section 6 of the Atomic Energy Authority Act, 1954 I hereby make the following Order:—

**1.** This Order may be cited as the Official Secrets (Prohibited Place) Order, 1955, and shall come into operation on the first day of October, 1955.

**2.** The place specified in the Schedule to this Order, being a place belonging to or used for the purposes of the United Kingdom Atomic Energy Authority, is declared to be a prohibited place for the purposes of the Official Secrets Act, 1911, on the ground that information with respect thereto, or damage thereto, would be useful to an enemy.

Dated this 23rd day of September, 1955.

James Stuart

One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State

St. Andrew's House

Edinburgh, 1

## SCHEDULE

The Works and Offices of the United Kingdom Atomic Energy Authority at Dounreay in the County of Caithness.
